# ITIS Collembola and Protura authority sidecar

This collection adds a frozen, CC0, exact-only ITIS `2026-08-26` nomenclatural crosswalk for the two COL26.8 roots that remain outside the existing Crustacea, Insecta and Myriapoda ITIS collections: Collembola (`KZS5W`) and Protura (`8NKDZ`). It deliberately does not cover Aves, which already has an independent AviList collection, or Myxozoa, which is inside the existing Cnidaria ITIS collection.

The crosswalk has 9,668 strict accepted COL species: 2,075 exact current-name matches, 25 official ITIS synonym redirects, four ambiguous names and 7,564 unmatched rows. It separately retains 411 current ITIS-only species. The only matching transformations are the release-wide exact authorship suffix handling, Unicode NFC/whitespace normalization and one parenthesized subgenus token; no fuzzy or taxon-substitution matching is permitted.

Pages publishes descriptor metadata, counts and the canonical SHA-256 inventory only. Android and iOS native-full delivery retains all two COL-ID range shards and the separate ITIS-only shard byte-for-byte. This is nomenclatural linkage, not a checklist, species-concept equivalence claim, biological dossier, phylogeny, final classification or scientific review.
