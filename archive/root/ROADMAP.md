# Evo Atlas roadmap

Published content should explain its sources, scope, uncertainty and reproducible context.

## Current product

- A bilingual reader for taxa, events, stories, geological time, places, traits and references.
- An Explorer for source-bound time windows, coordinates, fossil samples, maps, trees and evidence links.
- A Research workspace for queries, local data imports, SQL, comparisons and reproducible exports.
- A pinned Catalogue of Life registry with explicit resource ownership and separate source-specific records.
- Web delivery for the complete catalogue, a selected Pages reading edition and a shared Capacitor client for the native core content.
- Basic engineering checks and browser tests for supported reading, querying, importing and exporting flows. Archive replay tests remain available for source changes.

## Content work

1. Extend biological reader pages using recorded primary sources and concrete locators.
2. Explain specimen, regional, temporal and model boundaries beside the relevant statements.
3. Keep missing sources, unknown identities and incomplete evidence explicit.
4. Add content to the selected reading editions only when its sources and runtime delivery are available.

## Product boundaries

- Catalogue naming coverage does not imply complete biological dossiers.
- Bounded fossil occurrences do not establish biological absence, richness or global first appearances.
- Navigation trees and map models do not constitute direct observations.
- Accounts, remote annotation and embedded AI services are outside the current product.
