# Evo Atlas roadmap

The product goal is not to cover the whole tree of life. It is to make each published entry clear about what is known, why, what remains uncertain, how it was reviewed and how it can be reproduced or corrected.

## Now — v0.12 public-foundation release

- Publish bilingual static Taxa, Event, Story, Methods and Dataset pages alongside the hash-based Explorer.
- Make scientific maturity, human review and query completeness visible and machine-validated.
- Keep Perissodactyla at `curator-draft` until an external domain reviewer records a real decision.
- Provide contribution, correction, package-authoring and review workflows.
- Maintain Chromium coverage and add focused Firefox/WebKit smoke plus visual-layout contracts.

## Next — reviewed Perissodactyla

- Obtain a named human review of all 17 entities and the ten rich profiles.
- Resolve the remaining Palaeotherium taxon-concept question.
- Replace every pending source locator needed by the reviewed scope with a concrete locator.
- Record accept, changes-requested or accepted-with-reservations decisions against the exact dataset version.
- Promote only the accepted scope to `expert-reviewed`; do not promote the package wholesale if the review is partial.

## Then — small vertical slices

1. Tetrapod Transition, to test transition narratives and bounded fossil windows.
2. Angiospermae, to test whether the model generalizes beyond vertebrates.
3. Dinosauria, after the review workflow is proven at smaller scale.

## Deliberately deferred

- A backend, login, account or online collaboration database.
- Thousands of shallow generated entries.
- Paleogeographic geometry without complete model, license, processing and checksum provenance.
- Richness claims derived from raw occurrence counts.
- Any expert-reviewed badge created by automation or an anonymous decision.
