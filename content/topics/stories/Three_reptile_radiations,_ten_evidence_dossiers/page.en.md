# Three reptile radiations, ten evidence dossiers

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Three reptile radiations, ten evidence dossiers
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Keep ichthyosaurs, plesiosaurs and pterosaurs as three independent radiations, and compare an older uncertain Bobosaurus candidate with diagnostic Rhaeticosaurus without turning either into a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## A short-snouted ichthyosauromorph holotype
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
AGM I-1 preserves a small short-snouted body with large flippers and dense limb and rib bone. Amphibious habits and suction feeding are functional interpretations, not recorded behaviour or proof of direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Pregnancy is stronger evidence than reconstructed birth history
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
Maternal Chaohusaurus specimen AGM I-1 contains two embryos and an adjacent neonate. Their association supports pregnancy, whereas head-first delivery and a terrestrial origin of viviparity remain comparative inferences.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Skin chemistry from one Jurassic ichthyosaur
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
MH 432 preserves skin layers, a blubber-like subcutaneous layer and eumelanin-bearing structures. Countershading and thermoregulation are interpretations from distribution and living comparisons, not directly measured physiology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## Older does not mean diagnostically plesiosaurian
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
Early Carnian Bobosaurus combines nothosaurian and plesiosaurian features, but alternative placements remain viable. It is an older near-lineage candidate, not evidence that diagnostic Plesiosauria already extended into the Carnian.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## A Rhaetian plesiosaur skeleton and histology
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
LWL-MFN P 64047 supplies articulated anatomy and vascularized long-bone tissue from the Rhaetian. Basal pliosaurid placement and physiological implications remain dependent on phylogenetic and histological interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## A fetus documents plesiosaur viviparity
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
Adult Polycotylus LACM 129639 contains one large articulated fetus identified as the same taxon. Pregnancy and viviparity are specimen evidence; parental care, sociality and K-selection are broader analogical hypotheses.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/title -->
## Four flippers tested in a water channel
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/text -->
A physical model measured thrust and efficiency at prescribed fore- and hind-flipper phase offsets. Vortex interaction supports mechanical plausibility, not a filmed extinct gait or one stroke shared by every plesiosaur.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/title -->
## Branched structures and melanosomes on one skull
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/text -->
MCT.R.1884 preserves monofilaments, branched feather-like structures and melanosomes around a Tupandactylus skull and crest. The evidence does not directly recover exact colours or a universal pterosaur covering.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/title -->
## Hundreds of eggs, sixteen with embryos
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/text -->
A Hamipterus block exposes 215 eggs and may contain about 300; sixteen eggs in the wider assemblage preserve embryonic remains across multiple levels. Nesting, transport and hatchling behaviour remain interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/title -->
## Quadrupedal launch is a feasibility model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/text -->
Scaling, bone mechanics and aerodynamic calculations make powered flight and forelimb-assisted launch feasible for sampled giant-pterosaur reconstructions. The model is not direct observation of an extinct takeoff.
<!-- /evo:text -->
