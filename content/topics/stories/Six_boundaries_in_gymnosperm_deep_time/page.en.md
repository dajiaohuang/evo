## One Jurassic wood specimen
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
PB22285 preserves a Ginkgo-like combination of wood characters in 153–165 Ma strata, but an isolated organ cannot identify a complete plant or set the first appearance of Ginkgophyta.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## A crown age moves with calibration
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
A plastome clock combining fossil and tectonic constraints places the living Cycadaceae crown much older than a Miocene-only story, while alternative schemes reveal wide model sensitivity.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Fossils change the map
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
Adding fossil leaves to a species-level model expands inferred past cycad latitudes and multiplies inferred local extirpations, demonstrating that living distributions alone erase history.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## A famous young-radiation model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
The 2011 estimate that most sampled living cycad species are no older than roughly 12 Ma remains historically important, but later fossil-integrated work prevents it from becoming the age of Cycadales.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## A relative hemispheric pattern
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
Southern Hemisphere conifer lineages in one living-species time tree have older median nodes than sampled northern lineages, but those medians are neither family origins nor fossil ranges.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## Topology is not a timeline
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
A broad nuclear dataset recovers cycads plus Ginkgo and a Gnetales–Pinaceae relationship among living families, while gene-tree conflict and extinct lineages remain visible limits.
<!-- /evo:text -->
