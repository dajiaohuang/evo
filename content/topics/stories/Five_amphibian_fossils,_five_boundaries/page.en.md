# Five amphibian fossils, five boundaries

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Five amphibian fossils, five boundaries
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Follow named specimens from a Permian stem batrachian to a provisional Oligocene crown-caecilian record without merging total groups, crowns, direct ancestry or global first appearances.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## A stem batrachian mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
One Gerobatrachus holotype combines frog-like and salamander-like characters in the original tree, but it is not crown Batrachia or a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Hidden anatomy in a split nodule
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
μCT reveals caudal vertebrae and a long trunk in the unique Triadobatrachus holotype, supporting stem Salientia rather than crown Anura or specialized jumping.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Many individuals, isolated elements
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
At least 76 Funcusvermis individuals from a dated bonebed support Gymnophionomorpha while remaining outside crown Gymnophiona.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## Articulated salamandroids below a dated flow
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
PKUP V0601–V0606 support an Oxfordian salamandroid record; the zircon age comes from overlying rock and neoteny is inferred.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## A provisional living-family assignment
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
DGM 1462-R supports an Oligocene caecilian compared with living Typhlonectidae, but formal phylogenetic testing and an unambiguous family synapomorphy are absent.
