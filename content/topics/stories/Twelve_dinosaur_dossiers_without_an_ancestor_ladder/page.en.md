# Twelve dinosaur dossiers without an ancestor ladder

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Twelve dinosaur dossiers without an ancestor ladder
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Compare named specimens, morphology matrices, histology, taphonomy and functional models while preserving every observation–interpretation boundary and handing Avialae/flight evidence to the crocodylomorphs-birds package.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## One matrix does not settle the root
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
Baron et al. recovered Ornithoscelida from 457 characters; Langer et al. recovered the traditional split after rescoring and added taxa. Both are topology tests.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Teeth bound an ancestral-diet model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
ULBRA-PVT280 preserves recurved serrated teeth. Faunivory near the sauropodomorph root is an ancestral-state model, not a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## A partial skeleton tests an early branch
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
SAM-PK-K8025 is a bounded partial skeleton. Matrix placement and formation age do not establish a global ornithischian first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## One species changes stance while growing
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
Mussaurus digital models combine several growth stages. The bipedal-to-quadrupedal shift is ontogenetic and modelled, not an evolutionary ladder.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## Gigantism precedes columnar limbs
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
BP/1/7120 supports an adult giant with flexed limbs. Twelve tonnes and habitual quadrupedality are comparative estimates from a partial skeleton.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## Armour is anatomy; placement is analysis
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
NHMUK R1111 preserves associated osteoderms. Lost armour, inferred keratin and stem-ankylosaur placement remain separate from observation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/title -->
## A complete skeleton tests a character mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/text -->
IVPP V14530 preserves a nearly complete early ceratopsian skeleton. Its topology is a matrix result, not a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/title -->
## Skin and damage constrain taphonomy
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/text -->
NDGS 2000 preserves skin and feeding traces. Scavenging-assisted desiccation is a pathway inferred for this carcass, not a universal rule.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/title -->
## Filaments do not require a flight story
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

Three Yutyrannus skeletons preserve filamentous integument. Function, complete coverage and Avialae flight are separate questions.
<!-- /evo:text -->
