# Three early-fish fossils, three different boundaries

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Three early-fish fossils, three different boundaries
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Move from an articulated stem gnathostome to lamprey and hagfish total-group fossils without turning specimen occurrences into crown origins or PBDB first appearances.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## An articulated jawed stem vertebrate
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
IVPP V300001 and twenty referred Xiushanosteus specimens expose an approximately 436 Ma placoderm-grade body and mosaic skull roof. Abundance in one Lagerstätte does not make the animal a direct ancestor or a global first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## A lamprey-like oral apparatus
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
Holotype AM5750 preserves a large oral disc, fourteen circumoral teeth and a branchial basket at roughly 360 Ma. These structures diagnose total-group lamprey anatomy, not parasitism, migration or an ammocoete lifecycle.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## A stem hagfish is not crown Myxini
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
FMNH PF15373 and PF8472 preserve a roughly 310–307 Ma cyclostome–hagfish mosaic, but different preservation and equivocal structures require a stem or putative total-group reading. The separate Myxini navigation node therefore does not inherit this age.
<!-- /evo:text -->
