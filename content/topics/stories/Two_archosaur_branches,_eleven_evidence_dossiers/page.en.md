# Two archosaur branches, eleven evidence dossiers

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Two archosaur branches, eleven evidence dossiers
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Keep named specimens, feather chemistry, wind-tunnel measurements, phylogenetic topology, molecular clocks and COL26.8 living-name coverage separate while comparing crocodile-line and bird-line evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## A suchian can resemble a theropod
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
AMNH FR 30587 records an edentulous, gracile suchian. Its ornithomimid-like proportions are a convergence diagnosis, not evidence that the two branches share an immediate ancestor or identical ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## A partial holotype, a bounded predator inference
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
NCSM 21558 documents a large Carnian crocodylomorph. Size and predator tier come from proxies and assemblage context, not preserved feeding behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Skull consolidation appears as a mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
IVPP V14010 combines a consolidated, crocodylian-like skull with a gracile terrestrial postcranium. It is one anatomical mosaic rather than a compulsory stage in a ladder.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## Living gharials meet fossils in a tip-dated model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
A combined morphology, molecular and age model groups Gavialis with Tomistoma while moving sampled thoracosaurs outside the crown. Other analyses differ.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## Archaeopteryx preserves an anatomical mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
WDC CSG 100 exposes theropod-like pedal and palate features alongside avialan anatomy. The mosaic supports affinity without turning one specimen into the unique first bird.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## Microscopy informs a colour reconstruction
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
Melanosomes in BMNHC PH828 support a statistical map of plumage regions. Preservation, reference samples and a tail borrowed from another specimen constrain the image.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/title -->
## Four feathered surfaces are preserved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/text -->
IVPP V13352 preserves long pennaceous feathers on forelimbs, hindlimbs and tail. The fossil records surfaces, not a flight trajectory.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/title -->
## A wind tunnel tests feasibility
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/text -->
Measured forces on a scaled model support stable high-drag gliding across tested configurations. That experiment is functional evidence, not footage of extinct behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/title -->
## One skull, two nearby crown placements
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/text -->
CT anatomy anchors NHMM 2013 008 near Galloanserae, but parsimony and tip-dated Bayesian analyses place it on different branches.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/title -->
## Whole genomes still retain difficult branches
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/text -->
Forty-eight representatives across neoavian orders recover major groupings and expose gene-tree conflict. This is sampled extant topology, not a complete species tree.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/10/title -->
## A calibrated time tree is a model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/10/text -->
Clock-like exons, broader RAG sampling and fossils infer a Cretaceous root and boundary-era radiation. The dates follow calibrations, topology and priors.
<!-- /evo:text -->
