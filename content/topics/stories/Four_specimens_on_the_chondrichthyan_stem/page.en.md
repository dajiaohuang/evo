# Four specimens on the chondrichthyan stem

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Four specimens on the chondrichthyan stem
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Compare isolated teeth, a disarticulated dermoskeleton, one armoured articulated body and a later compressed skeleton while keeping stem placement separate from crown sharks and global FAD claims.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## Direct teeth without a body
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
Twenty-three Qianodus tooth whorls at roughly 439 Ma provide direct evidence of jawed-vertebrate dentition. No articulated jaw or body is known, and nearby scales and spines cannot be assigned by proximity alone.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## A shoulder assembled and remodelled
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
Fanjingshania dermal elements from the same approximate age show a spine–plate complex that grew through multiple centres and cyclical tissue resorption. Disarticulation prevents a complete-body or associated-tooth claim.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## One articulated mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
Shenacanthus combines minute scales and a chondrichthyan-like posterior branchial region with large thoracic armour at roughly 436 Ma. A single toothless holotype and method-sensitive placement block a crown-shark reading.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## A later stem taxon remains unfamiliar
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
At roughly 385 Ma, UMZC 2000.32 preserves jaws, gill arches, cartilage, scales and teeth in a surprising character mosaic. Compression, a missing tail and inferred feeding ecology remain visible limitations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
