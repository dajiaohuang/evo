## story / dek

Move from named fossils and three-dimensional anatomy to functional and genomic models without turning separate samples into an ancestor ladder.

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## A model tests the abrupt trilobite appearance
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
Parsimony and Bayesian analyses of sampled early artiopodans recover a short interval between inferred trilobite origin and the oldest widespread body fossils.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Ash preserves a complete ventral view
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
X-ray microtomography resolves antennae, four post-antennal head-limb pairs, a labrum, a slit-like mouth and digestive tissues in articulated specimens.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## A limb branch is tested as a gill
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
Triarthrus filaments have a narrow central region and inflated margins, while Olenoides preserves a partial body-wall attachment comparable to lamellate respiratory branches.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## One gut preserves one last meal
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
Synchrotron microtomography maps ostracod valves, hyolith material and stylophoran plates through inventory specimen 8 at 11.35-micrometre voxel size.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## Adult soft anatomy tests the agnostid problem
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
Adult Peronopsis and Ptychagnostus preserve biramous appendages and enrolment anatomy scored in an explicit arthropod character matrix.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## Urokodia bridges appendage configurations
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
Urokodia preserves pincer-like short great appendages, overlapping exite flaps and a seven-segmented head in three dimensions.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/title -->
## Unequivocal chelicerae enter the Cambrian record
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/text -->
KUMIP 314091a,b preserves massive chelicerae, five post-cheliceral prosomal limb pairs and lamella-bearing opisthosomal appendages.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/title -->
## Nerves and appendages carry conflicting signals
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

MCZ 1811 and USNM 305093 preserve optic nerves, a possible compact cephalic synganglion and a segmental ventral nerve cord.

## A giant claw does not equal a complete body
<!-- /evo:text -->
