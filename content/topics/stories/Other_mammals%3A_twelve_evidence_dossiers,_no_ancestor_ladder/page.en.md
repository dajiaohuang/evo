# Other mammals: twelve evidence dossiers, no ancestor ladder

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## Other mammals: twelve evidence dossiers, no ancestor ladder
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Compare named fossils, a living genome, morphology matrices, bone-density measurements and molecular trees while keeping specimen facts, function, topology, clocks and COL26.8 name routing separate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## Steropodon jaw and monotreme dental evidence
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
AM F66763 preserves a right dentary fragment and three molars whose morphology was compared with monotreme and tribosphenic conditions.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Platypus draft genome and comparative mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
Approximately 26.9 million reads produced a 1.84-gigabase draft at about sixfold coverage, with 437 megabases ordered on 20 chromosomes and 18,527 predicted protein-coding genes.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Eomaia skeleton, topology and locomotor inference
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
CAGS 01-IG-1a,b documents the skull, dentition and much of the postcranium; the original matrix and limb comparisons supported a basal eutherian and scansorial interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## Juramaia specimen and conditional Jurassic signal
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
BMNH PM1343B preserves skull, dentition and postcranium; the original study attributed it to approximately 160-million-year-old strata and recovered a basal eutherian position.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## Ambolestes and a revised therian boundary
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
STM33-5 preserves ectotympanic, malleus and hyoid anatomy; the published matrix recovers Ambolestes and Sinodelphys in Eutheria.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/title -->
## Placental phenomic matrix and K–Pg model
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/text -->
The study scored 4,541 phenomic characters for 86 living and fossil species, supported by more than 12,000 annotated images, and combined them with 27 nuclear genes.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/title -->
## Placental four-clade molecular topology
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/text -->
The 16,397-base-pair matrix combines 19 nuclear and 3 mitochondrial genes; Bayesian and maximum-likelihood analyses recover four major placental clades.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/title -->

Holotype MNHN PM69 preserves a rostrum and maxillary dentition; fourteen additional specimens extend the sampled upper and lower teeth and skull anatomy.
