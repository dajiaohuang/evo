# Other mammals: twelve evidence dossiers, no ancestor ladder

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/annotation -->
Dental homology and affinity are interpretations; one jaw cannot establish a direct ancestor, a complete body plan or an immutable global first occurrence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/annotation -->
Assembly gaps, one-individual sampling, annotation and comparative models prevent direct reconstruction of an ancestral genome or single-gene explanations for organismal traits.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/annotation -->
Later combined matrices can move Eomaia outside Theria; topology and scansoriality are analytical outputs, not a directly observed ancestor or behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/annotation -->
Commercial collection provenance, stratigraphic attribution and matrices condition the result; unconstrained tip dating yields a younger morphological age signal rather than directly redating the rock.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/annotation -->
Reassignment and the resulting metatherian ghost lineage depend on sampled characters and taxa; the analysis does not observe the placental–marsupial split.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/annotation -->
The post-K–Pg chronology and reconstructed phenotype depend on parsimony, fossil ages, ghost lineages and ancestral-state optimization; no ancestor was directly observed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/annotation -->
The Afrotheria root, dates and Gondwanan scenario are model-conditioned, later studies test alternatives, and sampled taxa from other packages remain cross-boundary inputs.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/annotation -->
Stem placement and estimated 3–8 kilogram body mass derive from a character matrix and dental allometry, not observed ancestry or body mass.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/annotation -->
Aquatic adaptation, hydrostatic control and marine grazing are functional interpretations; species succession is not a directly observed ancestor chain.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/annotation -->
A 4.5-kilogram estimate, cursoriality and tapiroid-like niche are regression and ecomorphology inferences; the taxon is not a direct lagomorph ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/10/annotation -->
Powered flight and absence of specialized laryngeal echolocation are functional inferences, not observed behaviour, and one specimen is not a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/11/annotation -->
Multiple origins or losses of laryngeal echolocation are alternative historical reconstructions; the topology does not directly observe the first flight or sonar event. COL26.8 routes 5,099 accepted living species to other-mammals after excluding Cetartiodactyla, Perissodactyla, Primates and Carnivora; that nomenclatural snapshot is not 5,099 dossiers and does not resolve fossil topology.
