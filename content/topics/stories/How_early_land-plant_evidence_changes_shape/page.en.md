# How early land-plant evidence changes shape

<!-- evo:text /records/story/title -->
## How early land-plant evidence changes shape
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / dek

<!-- evo:text /records/story/dek -->
Move from a modelled crown divergence to dispersed cryptospores, spore-bearing fragments and anatomically informative Devonian bodies without collapsing them into one origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/title -->
## A modelled crown interval
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/text -->
A fossil-calibrated relaxed clock places crown Embryophyta in a broad middle Cambrian–Early Ordovician interval, but this is a model result rather than a fossil occurrence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/title -->
## Dispersed spores on emerged land
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/text -->
A Dapingian assemblage in Argentina contains five cryptospore genera, but dispersed propagules do not reveal a complete parent plant or a crown-bryophyte identity.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/title -->
## Spores linked to plant fragments
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/text -->
Late Ordovician Oman fragments preserve dense spore masses and probable sporangial covering, strengthening the land-plant interpretation while leaving liverwort crown affinity tentative.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/title -->
## A three-axis rooting system
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/text -->
Three-dimensional fossil reconstruction shows how Asteroxylon combined leafy shoots, root-bearing axes and rooting axes through an extinct developmental pathway.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/title -->
## A liverwort body-fossil record
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / text

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/text -->
Givetian Metzgeriothallus preserves thalloid gametophytes and an associated sporophyte capsule, adding body-plan information without turning one occurrence into a phylum origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->
