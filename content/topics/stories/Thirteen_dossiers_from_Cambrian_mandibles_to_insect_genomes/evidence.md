# Thirteen dossiers from Cambrian mandibles to insect genomes

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/0/annotation -->
Stem-mandibulate placement is a matrix result; Tokummia is neither a demonstrated direct ancestor nor a crown crustacean, insect or myriapod.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/1/annotation -->
Pancrustacean placement and active swimming predation are topology and functional interpretations, not stomach contents, observed behaviour or a direct ancestor claim.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/2/annotation -->
Eucrustacean crown placement and epipodite homology depend on character interpretation and must not be converted into an exact crown-origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/3/annotation -->
Ostracod monophyly and several deep nodes change when fast genes, distant outgroups or data partitions change; no displayed branch is a fossil observation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/4/annotation -->
Copepod, cephalocarid and other deep positions remain method- and sampling-sensitive, so the tree is a tested hypothesis rather than a fixed ancestor ladder.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/5/annotation -->
Rejected and recovered clades differ among sampled matrices and models; divergence estimates use thirteen vetted calibrations but remain clock outputs.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/6/annotation -->
This sampled occurrence supports collembolan affinity but does not by itself date hexapod terrestrialization, global origin or ecological dominance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/7/annotation -->
Three-dimensional reanalysis in 2017 found the structures more compatible with a myriapod, possibly a scutigeromorph centipede; insect and flight calibrations are therefore contested.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/8/annotation -->
A favoured marine route and inferred dates are model outputs conditioned on topology, calibrations and ancestral-state coding, not observed migrations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/9/annotation -->
Archaeorthopteran affinity follows incomplete venation, and one fragment cannot date the acquisition of powered flight or the global Pterygota first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/10/annotation -->
Developmental correspondence supports a homology model but does not preserve the first wing, its ecology or one uniquely identified fossil precursor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/11/annotation -->
Topology and node ages depend on sampling, orthology, sequence models, fossil assignments and clock priors; they are not observed fossil first appearances.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## story / steps / annotation

<!-- evo:text /records/story/steps/12/annotation -->
The specimens do not demonstrate synchronous origins, exact crown-order first appearances or a single climatic cause for later diversification. COL26.8 routes 1,049,133 accepted living species names through crustaceans-insects; this is nomenclatural coverage, not 1,049,133 evidence dossiers or a phylogenomic consensus.
<!-- /evo:text -->
