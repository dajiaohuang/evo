# Amphimedon queenslandica

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A living Great Barrier Reef demosponge supplied a draft genome for comparisons of animal gene families; this derived sampled species is neither a literal ancestral animal nor a proxy for all sponges.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The published assembly and comparative analyses directly report sequence content. Inferences about ancestral genomic toolkits are comparative and do not reconstruct a Precambrian individual, a species origin date or all Porifera.
