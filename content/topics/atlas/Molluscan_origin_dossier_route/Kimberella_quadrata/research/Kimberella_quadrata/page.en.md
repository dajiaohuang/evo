# Kimberella quadrata

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
More than 35 White Sea fossils document a bilateral benthic animal with a foot-like region and non-mineralized dorsal cover; molluscan affinity remains bounded.
