# primates_Research_examples

## Research_examples / examples / description

<!-- evo:text /records/Research_examples/examples/0/description/en -->
Inspect a Wisconsin National Primate Research Center study of 24 experienced fathers and 62 offspring. Offspring of fathers responsive to infant distress calls had higher survival past one month (p = 0.002) and greater mean weights at weeks 28 and 30. These are findings from one captive colony.
