# Ichthyophis nokrekensis — CaecilianTraits

## The deposited caecilian record

<!-- evo:text /records/caecilian-traits-reader/sections/0/text/en -->
CaecilianTraits version 4 records Ichthyophis nokrekensis under Ichthyophis, Ichthyophiidae. Its source species row is 140. The compilation retains individual, population and species data separately; equivalence with a current COL26.8 concept remains to be reviewed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Compiled habitat categories

<!-- evo:text /records/caecilian-traits-reader/sections/1/text/en -->
The source classifies its broad habitat as Terrestrial|Freshwater (=Inland waters) and its microhabitat as Fossorial. These categorical labels summarize the compilation and do not specify the frequency of habitat use or a complete geographic range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Compiled reproduction category

<!-- evo:text /records/caecilian-traits-reader/sections/2/text/en -->
The deposited categorical field records oviparous. The accompanying metadata uses a presumed, probable or expected interpretation; this is retained as a qualified compilation statement.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Water-dependent larval development

<!-- evo:text /records/caecilian-traits-reader/sections/3/text/en -->
The deposited aquatic-larvae field records presence of a water-dependent larval stage. This is the compilation category described in the associated data paper, not a statement that adults always inhabit water. The accompanying reproductive metadata and original reference must be consulted for observational scope and qualifications.
