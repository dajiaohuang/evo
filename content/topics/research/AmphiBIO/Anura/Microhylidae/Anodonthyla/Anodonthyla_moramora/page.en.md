# Anodonthyla moramora — AmphiBIO v1

## The source concept

AmphiBIO v1 records Anodonthyla moramora as row Anf4127, within Anodonthyla, Microhylidae, Anura. Its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The following traits describe that deposited source record; correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Adult food categories

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibio-source-reader/sections/1/text/en -->
The source flags arthropods as present categories. The dataset aggregates adult diet categories from literature reports of expert judgement, observation or stomach contents. A listed category is not a complete diet or a measured diet proportion.

## Compiled adult dimensions

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibio-source-reader/sections/2/text/en -->
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 16.5 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
