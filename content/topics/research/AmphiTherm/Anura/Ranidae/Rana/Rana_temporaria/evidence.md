Normoxia (158 mmHg). Experiment lasted for 40h but figure with results only shows observations for 6h; sample size was 6 individuals but figure 1 shows number of total observations (in 6h), knowing there were 15 observations per hour, there were 540 (6*6*15) observations shown in figure 1; two treatments: normoxia (158mmHg) and hypoxia (25mmHg) but with the same individuals (started in normoxia [figure 1B] then were subjected to hypoxia [figure 1C]); individuals from figure 1A were actually in a constant temperature setting.
