## amphitherm-source-reader / originalRows / values

<!-- evo:text /records/amphitherm-source-reader/originalRows/3/values/28 -->
Animals were sampled from 19°15'02"N to 19°34'04"N (latitude) and 96°59'44"W to 96°49'13"W (longitude). The midpoint of these coordinates was taken here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## amphitherm-source-reader / originalRows / values

<!-- evo:text /records/amphitherm-source-reader/originalRows/3/values/63 -->
The endpoint was the complete immobility and rigidity of the body of the animals. The starting temperature was different for each inidividual depending on the temperature from where they were collected (reported here the averages). SVL was taken as the mean of the range presented in the main text.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## amphitherm-source-reader / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/amphitherm-source-reader/limitations/en -->
Partial source-compilation reader. Critical, lethal and preferred-temperature metrics, experimental media, acclimation conditions and life stages are distinct. Study reanalysis, current identity and remaining scientific facets are incomplete;.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## amphitherm-source-reader / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/amphitherm-source-reader/limitations/zh -->
部分来源汇编读本。临界、致死与偏好温度指标、实验介质、温度适应条件及发育阶段分别处理。研究重新分析、当前身份与其余科学主题尚未完成。
