## Sex-specific measurement material

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibian-morphology-reader/sections/1/text/en -->
The compilation retains 2 rows and 14 populated trait columns for this source concept. Recorded sex codes are M, F: F and M denote the source female and male categories; J denotes juvenile material, and NA leaves the category unspecified. These are compiled rows rather than unique specimen counts. Values from separate sexes, life stages and publications must not be pooled into a single diagnostic size. Missing cells do not establish absence of an anatomical structure.
