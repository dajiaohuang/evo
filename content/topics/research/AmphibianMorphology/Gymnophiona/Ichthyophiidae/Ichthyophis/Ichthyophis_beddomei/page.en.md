# Ichthyophis beddomei — morphology source

## The deposited morphology record

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibian-morphology-reader/sections/0/text/en -->
The version 3 deposit records Ichthyophis beddomei within Ichthyophis, Ichthyophiidae, Gymnophiona. Its source concept is retained independently of current COL26.8 species. Original trait cells, sex codes and bibliographic rows accompany this record.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Sex-specific measurement material

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibian-morphology-reader/sections/1/text/en -->
The compilation retains 1 rows and 13 populated trait columns for this source concept. Recorded sex codes are NA: F and M denote the source female and male categories; J denotes juvenile material, and NA leaves the category unspecified. These are compiled rows rather than unique specimen counts. Values from separate sexes, life stages and publications must not be pooled into a single diagnostic size. Missing cells do not establish absence of an anatomical structure.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Deposited annulus counts

<!-- evo:text /records/amphibian-morphology-reader/sections/2/text/en -->
The deposited rows report row 70 (NA): annuli counted dorsally 275, annuli behind the vent 7. F/M denote recorded female/male categories, J juvenile material and NA unspecified sex. These compiled row values can include summaries and do not establish fixed diagnostic counts for every individual. Dorsal, ventral and post-vent counts remain distinct; missing fields are not zero.
