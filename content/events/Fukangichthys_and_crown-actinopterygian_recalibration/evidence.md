# Fukangichthys and crown-actinopterygian recalibration

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
Analyses of Fukangichthys specimens IVPP V4096.6 and IVPP V4096.13, compared with ropefish BMNH 2016.9.22.3, recovered scanilepiforms as stem polypterids and shifted the minimum calibration for crown Actinopterygii about 45 million years younger.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A 2017 CT-based analysis recovered scanilepiforms including Fukangichthys as stem polypterids, shifting many Palaeozoic ray-fins onto the actinopterygian stem and moving the study's crown minimum about 45 million years younger; this topology-dependent recalibration is not the crown's birth time.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimens, CT anatomy, phylogenetic matrices and revised calibrations are published with stability analyses. Medium confidence retains sensitivity to character and taxon sampling, distinguishes the 2017 placement from the 2014 neopterygian hypothesis, and avoids converting a fossil minimum into an origin time.
<!-- /evo:text -->
