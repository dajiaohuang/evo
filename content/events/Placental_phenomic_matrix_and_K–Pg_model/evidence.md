## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A matrix of 4,541 phenomic characters for 86 living and fossil species, combined with 27 nuclear genes, produced one parsimony tree with post-K–Pg crown-placental diversification and a reconstructed ancestral phenotype; both chronology and phenotype are model outputs rather than observations of an ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The public MorphoBank-backed matrix and annotated images make inputs inspectable, while tree search, fossil ages, ghost lineages and ancestral-state optimization control the resulting dates and reconstruction.
<!-- /evo:text -->
