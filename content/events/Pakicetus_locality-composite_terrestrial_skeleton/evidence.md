## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The published Pakicetus reconstruction combines skull H-GSP 96231 with separately catalogued vertebral and limb elements from one locality and supports terrestrial locomotion; it is not a single associated skeleton or a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The individual elements and locality are explicit and their anatomy strongly supports weight bearing, while taxonomic association and the whole-body reconstruction combine multiple individuals.
<!-- /evo:text -->
