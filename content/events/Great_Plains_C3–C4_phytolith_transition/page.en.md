## Great Plains C3–C4 phytolith transition
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Fourteen well-preserved palaeosol phytolith assemblages record a regional rise of chloridoid and other potentially C4 grasses within heterogeneous mixed C3–C4 habitats.
