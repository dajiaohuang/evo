## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Fourteen selected palaeosol phytolith assemblages from Nebraska and Kansas record an 8–5.5 Ma increase in chloridoid and other potentially C4 PACMAD grass short cells, reaching about 50–60% of the grass component by 5.5 Ma within heterogeneous mixed C3–C4 habitats.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The assemblage analysis directly quantifies a regional phytolith transition, but the sample is restricted to the central Great Plains and subject to phytolith production and preservation biases. The percentage applies within grasses, and not every PACMAD-type morphology diagnoses C4 photosynthesis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
组合分析直接量化了区域植硅体转变，但样本仅限中部大平原，并受植硅体产生与保存偏差影响。该百分比只适用于禾草内部，且并非所有 PACMAD 型形态都能诊断 C4 光合途径。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-statements.zh/0 -->
内布拉斯加州和堪萨斯州 14 个精选古土壤植硅体组合记录了 8–5.5 Ma 间虎尾草亚科型及其他潜在 C4 PACMAD 禾草短细胞的增加；到约 5.5 Ma，它们在异质 C3–C4 混合生境的禾草组分中达到约 50–60%。
