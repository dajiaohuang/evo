## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CRISPR knockout and expression comparisons in Parhyale hawaiensis and insects support serial homology between proximal leg segments, lateral body wall and wing-related structures, but do not directly observe the first insect wing or identify one fossil transition.
<!-- /evo:text -->
