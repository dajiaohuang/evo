## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Mature Burgess Shale Peronopsis and Ptychagnostus specimens preserve soft appendages used in a character matrix that recovers agnostinids as sister to polymeroid trilobites; the topology and nektobenthic ecology are analytical interpretations, not intrinsic labels or direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Adult soft anatomy expands the direct sample, while agnostid placement and ecology remain sensitive to character homology and taxon sampling.
<!-- /evo:text -->
