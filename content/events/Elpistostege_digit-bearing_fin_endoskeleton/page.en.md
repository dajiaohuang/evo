# Elpistostege digit-bearing fin endoskeleton

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Elpistostege digit-bearing fin endoskeleton
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Computed tomography of one articulated specimen reveals four proximodistal radial rows and two distal rows interpreted as digits or putative digits inside a pectoral fin that still bears fin rays.
