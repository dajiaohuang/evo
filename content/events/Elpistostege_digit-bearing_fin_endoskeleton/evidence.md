## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Computed tomography of articulated Elpistostege specimen MHNM 06-2067 reveals four proximodistal radial rows, branched carpals and two distal rows interpreted as digits or putative digits, while lepidotrichia show that the appendage remained a fin.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
A single exceptionally complete specimen and CT reconstruction directly document the internal skeleton and retained fin rays. Medium confidence preserves the interpretive status of digit homology and the analysis-dependent potential sister position of Elpistostege rather than treating either as permanent consensus.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
一件异常完整的标本和 CT 重建直接记录了鳍内骨骼及保留的鳍条。中置信度保留指同源性的解释属性，以及 Elpistostege 潜在姐妹群位置对分析方法的依赖，不把二者当成永久共识。
<!-- /evo:text -->
