## Modelled crown-Embryophyta divergence
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
A fossil-calibrated molecular clock estimates a middle Cambrian–Early Ordovician origin for crown land plants; it does not directly date fossil first appearances or terrestrial ecosystem expansion.
