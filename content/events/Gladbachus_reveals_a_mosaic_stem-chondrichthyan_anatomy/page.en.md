# Gladbachus reveals a mosaic stem-chondrichthyan anatomy

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Gladbachus reveals a mosaic stem-chondrichthyan anatomy
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
CT and histology of the single compressed specimen UMZC 2000.32 reveal a roughly 385 Ma stem chondrichthyan combining placoderm-like, osteichthyan-like and chondrichthyan features, demonstrating disparity without supplying a crown-shark ancestor.
