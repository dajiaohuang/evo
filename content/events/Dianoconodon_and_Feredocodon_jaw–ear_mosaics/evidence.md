## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Jaw and postdentary anatomy in Dianoconodon IVPP V4257 and Feredocodon IMMNH-PV01925 supports a comparative reduction of articular–quadrate load-bearing before complete middle-ear detachment, but function is inferred and the specimens are not an ancestor–descendant pair.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named fossils, detailed anatomy, MorphoBank matrix and archived analyses support comparison. Medium confidence separates anatomical observation from biomechanical and auditory interpretation and rejects a linear transition assembled from separate lineages and horizons.
<!-- /evo:text -->
