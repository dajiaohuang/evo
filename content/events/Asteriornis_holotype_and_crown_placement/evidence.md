## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CT-imaged holotype NHMM 2013 008 preserves a latest Maastrichtian skull and postcrania with galloanseran character combinations; parsimony and tip-dated Bayesian analyses place it differently near the crown, so membership is analysis-dependent rather than a directly observed global first modern bird.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Specimen anatomy and locality are explicit, but the fossil is fragmentary and alternative analyses recover different crown positions. Medium confidence keeps occurrence separate from topology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
