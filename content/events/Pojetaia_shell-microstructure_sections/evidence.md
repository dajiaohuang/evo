## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
New Pojetaia shell sections preserve a laminar inner microstructure comparable to foliated aragonite; the study does not identify undisputed Cambrian nacre and instead tests independent later origins of nacre across molluscan classes.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Shell fabrics are imaged and compared directly, while mineral identity, precursor relationships and repeated origins of nacre are comparative inferences affected by preservation.
<!-- /evo:text -->
