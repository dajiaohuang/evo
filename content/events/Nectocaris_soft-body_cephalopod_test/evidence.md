## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Ninety newly prepared Nectocaris specimens reveal paired eyes, two tentacles, lateral fins and a ventral funnel-like structure that support a cephalopod interpretation in the cited study, but absent diagnostic shell, siphuncle, beak and radula evidence leaves that placement contested.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The expanded sample strongly supports the described soft anatomy, but cephalopod affinity has unusual character combinations and lacks several diagnostic structures, so the claim is explicitly topology-limited.
<!-- /evo:text -->
