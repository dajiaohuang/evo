## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CT-informed comparison of holotype DGM 1462-R supports an approximately 25 Ma aquatic caecilian assigned to Typhlonectidae, but damage, absent unambiguous family synapomorphies and pending formal phylogenetic testing make the crown placement provisional.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
