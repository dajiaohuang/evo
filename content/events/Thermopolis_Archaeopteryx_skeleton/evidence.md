## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The nearly complete Thermopolis Archaeopteryx specimen, later catalogued WDC CSG 100, preserves a hyperextendible second toe, a tetraradiate palatine and a hallux unlike a fully reversed perching toe; this anatomical mosaic supports theropod affinity without proving direct ancestry or a unique first bird.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Articulated anatomy is directly documented and the specimen resolves features hidden in other skeletons. Medium confidence preserves uncertainty in posture, function and character-based topology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
