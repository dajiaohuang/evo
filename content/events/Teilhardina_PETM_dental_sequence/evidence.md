## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Correlated PETM sections and separately catalogued Teilhardina dental samples support an Asia-to-Europe-to-North-America appearance sequence within a short earliest-Eocene interval.
<!-- /evo:text -->
