## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Peregocetus holotype MUSM 3580 anchors a 42.6 Ma Peruvian protocetid occurrence and preserves weight-bearing and aquatic features; locomotor capability and a westward South Atlantic dispersal route remain phylogenetic and functional interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
