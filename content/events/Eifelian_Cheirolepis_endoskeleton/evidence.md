## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CT-scanned Cheirolepis trailli specimens from late Eifelian Achanarras Limestone preserve a mosaic of early ray-fin characters, including an imperforate propterygium and hyomandibular shaft plus peg-and-socket scales; the specimens neither define the global actinopterygian first appearance nor represent a demonstrated direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named museum specimens, scan methods and the relevant structures are directly described and illustrated. Medium confidence preserves deformation and incomplete mineralization, the study's 2012-timescale numerical conversion, and the distinction between a sampled stem taxon and the origin or first appearance of Actinopterygii.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
具名馆藏标本、扫描方法与相关结构均有直接描述和图示。中等置信度保留了压扁变形与矿化不完整、研究采用 2012 年时间标尺的数值换算，并区分已取样干群分类单元与辐鳍鱼起源或首现。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-statements.zh/0 -->
艾菲尔期晚期 Achanarras 石灰岩的 Cheirolepis trailli CT 标本保存了早期辐鳍鱼性状的镶嵌组合，包括无孔的前鳍骨与舌颌骨干以及榫槽式关节鳞片；这些标本既不能定义全球辐鳍鱼首现，也不是已经证明的直接祖先。
