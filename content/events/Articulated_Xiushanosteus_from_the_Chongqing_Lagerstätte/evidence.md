## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Xiushanosteus mirabilis holotype IVPP V300001 and referred specimens IVPP V300002–V300021 from the middle-to-late Telychian Huixingshao Formation at approximately 436 Ma preserve an approximately 30 mm placoderm-grade stem-gnathostome body and a mosaic skull roof; this is articulated specimen evidence, not a direct ancestor, global dominance estimate or global jawed-vertebrate FAD.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple articulated specimens directly preserve the small body and cranial armor, and the primary study provides a specimen list, horizon and detailed figures. The high confidence applies only to the documented material and character mosaic; placoderm-grade relationships remain poorly resolved and are not converted into ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
多件关节相连标本直接保存小型躯体与颅甲，一手研究给出了标本清单、层位和详细图版。高置信度只适用于已记录材料及性状镶嵌；盾皮鱼等级的关系仍解析不足，不能转化为祖先关系。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh
