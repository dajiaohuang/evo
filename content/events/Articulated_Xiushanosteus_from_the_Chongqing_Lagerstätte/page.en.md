# Articulated Xiushanosteus from the Chongqing Lagerstätte

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Articulated Xiushanosteus from the Chongqing Lagerstätte
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Holotype IVPP V300001 and twenty referred specimens preserve an approximately 30 mm, near-complete placoderm-grade stem gnathostome at about 436 Ma, directly exposing a mosaic skull roof and body plan without identifying an ancestor of crown jawed vertebrates.
