## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The 4.2 cm Priscomyzon riniensis holotype AM5750 from the Famennian Witpoort Formation at Waterloo Farm, approximately 360 Ma, preserves a large oral disc, fourteen circumoral teeth and a branchial basket; it is total-group lamprey evidence and does not directly establish a crown-Petromyzontida FAD, parasitic feeding, migration or an ammocoete lifecycle.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype anatomy, locality and Famennian assignment are described and illustrated in the primary study. Medium confidence preserves the single-specimen boundary, the source's approximate numerical age and the absence of preserved lifecycle or behavioural stages.
<!-- /evo:text -->
