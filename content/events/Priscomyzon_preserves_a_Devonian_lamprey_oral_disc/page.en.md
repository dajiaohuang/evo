# Priscomyzon preserves a Devonian lamprey oral disc

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Priscomyzon preserves a Devonian lamprey oral disc
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
The 4.2 cm holotype AM5750 from Famennian strata preserves a large oral disc, fourteen circumoral teeth and a branchial basket, establishing a specimen-level total-group lamprey record without demonstrating a modern lifecycle or crown-group FAD.
