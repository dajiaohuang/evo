## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Three-dimensional holotype PVSJ 698 supplies CT-resolved cranial characters recovered as an early-diverging stem lepidosauromorph across the cited analyses; that placement does not make the individual a direct ancestor or establish whole-body anatomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Preservation and CT observations are strong, and placement was tested under several analytical settings. The claim remains medium because only a skull is known and topology and ancestral-state reconstruction remain model dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->
