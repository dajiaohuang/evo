## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
At least 76 individuals represented by isolated three-dimensional Funcusvermis elements occur in a U–Pb-bracketed Norian bonebed and support a caecilian total-group record, but not crown Gymnophiona, one articulated animal or a global first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple named holotype, paratype and referred elements plus the dated section support the bounded occurrence; medium confidence retains isolated association and topology limits.
