# Living experiments on Meckel's cartilage breakdown

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
c-Fos mutant mice retained and ossified Meckel's cartilage, while pharmacological inhibition of clast cells in Monodelphis domestica likewise delayed cartilage breakdown and produced a persistent jaw–ear connection phenotype.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
c-Fos mutant mice and clast-cell inhibition in Monodelphis show that blocking cartilage resorption can retain or ossify Meckel's cartilage and preserve a jaw–ear connection, but these living perturbations do not directly reveal the mechanism in any fossil.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Two living-mammal experimental systems reproduce related persistence phenotypes and identify a clast-cell role. Medium confidence excludes literal recapitulation, acknowledges additional remodelling pathways and leaves ancient cellular mechanisms unobserved.
<!-- /evo:text -->
