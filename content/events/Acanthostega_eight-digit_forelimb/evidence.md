## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Upper Devonian East Greenland limb material documents eight digits in the Acanthostega forelimb, showing that five digits were not the fixed primitive condition and that digit-bearing limbs need not imply terrestrial walking.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Digit count is a direct observation from described limb specimens, and comparison with seven-digit Ichthyostega and six-digit Tulerpeton robustly rejects a universal primitive pentadactyl condition. The ecological implication is kept as a supported suggestion rather than direct behavioural observation.
<!-- /evo:text -->
