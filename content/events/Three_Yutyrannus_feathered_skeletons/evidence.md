# Three Yutyrannus feathered skeletons

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
Holotype ZCDM V5000 and referred specimens ZCDM V5001 and ELDM V1001 preserve filamentous integument in association with large tyrannosauroid skeletons; feather distribution and structure are observed, but insulation, display and whole-body coverage are functional reconstructions.

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
