## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
MUZM 60 and MUZM 80 preserve separate early Miocene shoulder and femoral anatomies used to reconstruct a mixed arboreal locomotor repertoire; they are not one individual or direct crown-hominoid ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->
