# Mussaurus ontogenetic 3-D stance models

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
Digital models built from the hatchling, yearling PVL 4587 and adult Mussaurus sample move the estimated centre of mass forward and support a bipedal-to-quadrupedal ontogenetic stance shift; the stance is a model result assembled from several individuals, not a preserved gait or evolutionary stage.
