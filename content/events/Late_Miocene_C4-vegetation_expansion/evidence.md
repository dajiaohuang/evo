## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Fossil tooth-enamel carbon-isotope records document a widespread increase in C4-plant biomass between 8 and 6 Ma across Asia, Africa, North America and South America, with earlier increases at lower latitudes.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
High confidence follows from directly measured enamel isotope shifts across multiple continental sequences. The inference concerns C4 biomass and regional timing; enamel δ13C is an animal-diet and local-habitat proxy that cannot identify the contributing C4 plant lineages or by itself demonstrate open grassland, vegetation cover, dental adaptation or faunal turnover.
<!-- /evo:text -->
