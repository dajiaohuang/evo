## Late Miocene C4-vegetation expansion
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Fossil tooth-enamel isotopes record a widespread, regionally asynchronous increase in C4-plant biomass; they do not alone demonstrate open grassland or faunal adaptation.
