## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Maternal specimen AGM I-1 directly documents pregnancy in Chaohusaurus through two embryos and an adjacent neonate, while head-first delivery and a terrestrial origin of viviparity remain orientation- and topology-based interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Embryonic association and skeletal maturity criteria are described for a named specimen. Medium confidence separates those observations from broader reconstruction of birth mode and the ancestral setting of viviparity.
