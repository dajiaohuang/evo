## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
SGS-UM 2009-002 records a 29–28 Ma catarrhine with a character combination recovered on the catarrhine stem in the cited matrix, not a crown catarrhine or direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
