## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Eritherium azzouzorum is represented by a 15-specimen hypodigm including holotype skull MNHN PM69 from the lower bone bed of Sidi Chennane; dental and cranial characters support a stem-proboscidean placement, while body mass and exact phylogenetic position are comparative estimates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype, hypodigm, locality and teeth are directly described. Proboscidean affinity, relationships and 3–8 kg mass use a character matrix and dental allometry.
<!-- /evo:text -->
