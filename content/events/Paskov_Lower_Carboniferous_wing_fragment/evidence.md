## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A crushed forewing part and counterpart from the lowermost Namurian Paskov drill core preserve cross-veins and main-vein branches attributed to Archaeorthoptera at about 324 Ma; the fragment does not date the origin of insect flight or all Pterygota.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The wing venation and locality are direct observations, but higher placement and global evolutionary timing are necessarily bounded.
<!-- /evo:text -->
