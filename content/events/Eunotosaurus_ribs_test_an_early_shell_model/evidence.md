## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Eunotosaurus specimens including sectioned rib NHM PV R 4949 document broadened ribs formed by successive bone deposition, but turtle-stem placement, shell homology and functional consequences are analytical rather than direct ancestral observations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named specimens, gross anatomy and histological sections are directly documented. Medium confidence separates preserved structures from character homology, matrix placement and reconstructed respiration or shell assembly.
<!-- /evo:text -->
