# Hamipterus egg and embryo assemblage

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
A 3.28-square-metre sandstone block exposes 215 Hamipterus eggs, possibly about 300 in the block, and the broader sample includes 16 eggs with embryonic bones across at least four stratigraphic levels.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The Hamipterus site preserves hundreds of three-dimensional eggs, including 215 exposed in one block and 16 containing embryonic remains across at least four levels; storm transport, colonial nesting, hatchling flight ability and parental care remain interpretations of context and development.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Egg counts, embryo-bearing eggs, computed tomography and stratigraphic repetition are directly documented. Medium confidence preserves uncertainty in taphonomic reconstruction, embryo stage and behavioural extrapolation.
<!-- /evo:text -->
