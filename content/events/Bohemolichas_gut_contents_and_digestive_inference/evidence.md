## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Synchrotron microtomography of Bohemolichas incola inventory specimen 8 maps ostracod, hyolith and stylophoran remains through the gut; prey identity is direct fossil content, while feeding rate, selectivity and neutral-to-alkaline chemistry are bounded inferences from one meal.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The itemized gut fill is directly imaged, but physiology and habitual diet cannot be generalized from one taphonomically filtered specimen.
<!-- /evo:text -->
