## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The sole Gerobatrachus hottoni holotype USNM 489135 combines frog-like and salamander-like characters and was recovered as a stem batrachian in the original analysis; it is neither crown Batrachia nor a demonstrated direct ancestor or global first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
A nearly complete articulated holotype and published character matrix support the morphology and proposed placement; medium confidence keeps the topology analysis-sensitive.
