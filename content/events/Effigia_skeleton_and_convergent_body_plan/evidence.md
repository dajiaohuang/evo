## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
AMNH FR 30587 preserves a nearly complete edentulous, gracile archosaur skeleton that the published character analysis places within Suchia; resemblance to ornithomimid theropods is interpreted as convergence, not shared ancestry or identical ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named skeleton and scored anatomy are direct evidence. Medium confidence keeps the matrix placement and convergence diagnosis distinct from unmeasured locomotion, diet and ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->
