## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Eifelian bedding surfaces at Zachełmie preserve quadrupedal trackways and isolated footprints with digit impressions attributed to an unidentified limbed tetrapod trackmaker; the traces do not identify a named body taxon or direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The prints, trackway geometry, digit impressions and stratigraphic context are directly documented, but trace-maker identity must be inferred from traces and the environmental interpretation has been debated. Medium confidence preserves the distinction between a digit-bearing trackmaker and a complete body-fossil or ancestor assignment.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
足印、行迹几何、趾印与地层背景均有直接记录，但造迹者身份必须由遗迹推断，古环境解释也曾存争议。中置信度保留了“具趾造迹者”与“完整身体化石或直系祖先”之间的边界。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-statements.zh/0 -->
Zachełmie 的艾菲尔期层面保存了四足行迹和带趾印的孤立足印，归因于一种身份未明的具肢四足动物造迹者；这些遗迹不能识别命名的身体分类单元或直系祖先。
