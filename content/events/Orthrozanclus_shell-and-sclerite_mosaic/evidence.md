## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Orthrozanclus reburrus combines a prominent anterior shell, three sclerite zones and long marginal spines in one articulated body; Halwaxiida and its position near early molluscan or broader lophotrochozoan branches are matrix-based hypotheses rather than observed ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->
