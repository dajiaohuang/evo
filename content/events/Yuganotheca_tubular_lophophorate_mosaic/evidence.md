## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The referred 710-specimen Yuganotheca elegans sample collectively supports a reconstruction combining paired agglutinated valves, a horseshoe-shaped lophophore, a bipartite tubular attachment structure and a long pedicle; no individual fossil is asserted to preserve this entire suite, and the mosaic supports comparison near the brachiopod–phoronid stem region without resolving exact ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The character combination is reconstructed collectively from referred Chengjiang specimens, while homology of the tube and valves and its exact lophophorate position remain topology-dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->
