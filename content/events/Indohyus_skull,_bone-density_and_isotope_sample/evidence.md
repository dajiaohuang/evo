## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Indohyus skulls RR 207 and RR 208, separate dense limb bones and a tooth-isotope sample support aquatic wading and a raoellid sister placement to Cetacea in the cited analysis, but not crown-cetacean membership or direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple anatomical, histological and isotope observations converge, but they come from a disarticulated bone-bed assemblage and the behavioural and topological conclusions remain interpretations of that sampled material and matrix.
<!-- /evo:text -->
