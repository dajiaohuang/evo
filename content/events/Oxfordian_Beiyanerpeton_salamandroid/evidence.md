## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Holotype PKUP V0601 and referred specimens PKUP V0602–V0606 preserve an Oxfordian salamandroid with larval structures interpreted as neoteny; the 157 ± 3 Ma zircon date is from overlying rock and the placement is sample-dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Articulated specimens and an explicit phylogenetic analysis support the dossier; medium confidence preserves the indirect age constraint and inferred developmental state.
