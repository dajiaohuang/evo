# Givetian Metzgeriothallus body fossils

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Givetian Metzgeriothallus body fossils
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Givetian shale and siltstone lenses preserve thalloid Metzgeriothallus sharonae gametophytes and an associated sporophyte capsule, a liverwort body-fossil record rather than a crown-origin date.
