## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Metzgeriothallus sharonae specimens from Givetian strata of eastern New York provide a Middle Devonian liverwort body-fossil record; this occurrence is not a crown-Marchantiophyta divergence date or a phylum-wide first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimens, type localities and Givetian strata are described directly, and the study assigns the material to a liverwort. Medium confidence preserves the distinction between a well-supported fossil occurrence, its finer systematic placement and the unknown completeness of the earlier liverwort record.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Metzgeriothallus sharonae is represented by well-preserved thalloid gametophytes with a medial costa and bifurcation, plus an associated sporophyte capsule; these observations diagnose the fossil material but do not describe all Marchantiophyta.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The thalli and associated capsule are illustrated and described in the primary study, but association is not the same as an anatomically connected whole plant and similar simple thalloid morphologies occur in more than one liverwort lineage.
<!-- /evo:text -->
