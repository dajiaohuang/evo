## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Onychonycteris finneyi holotype ROM 55351A preserves a nearly complete skeleton with wing proportions compatible with powered flight and a relatively small cochlea interpreted as lacking specialized laryngeal echolocation; neither behaviour was observed and the specimen is not a direct ancestor of living bats.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The skeleton and measurements are direct evidence, whereas flight performance, echolocation capacity and stem placement rely on functional proxies and a morphology matrix.
<!-- /evo:text -->
