## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Siliceous spicules from the basal-Cambrian Soltanieh Formation are the oldest reliable sponge remains under the cited candidate-by-candidate diagnostic reassessment.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The material is directly re-described with stratigraphic context, but isolated spicules do not identify all crown relationships or exclude future older finds.
<!-- /evo:text -->
