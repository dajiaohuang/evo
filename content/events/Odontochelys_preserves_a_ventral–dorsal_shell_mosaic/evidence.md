## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Odontochelys specimens IVPP V 15639, V 13240 and V 15653 preserve a developed plastron with expanded ribs and neural plates but no complete carapace; plastron-first assembly, basal topology and habitat remain interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The ventral and dorsal elements are figured in multiple named specimens. Medium confidence retains uncertainty in comparative homology, matrix placement, depositional ecology and extrapolation to a universal shell sequence.
<!-- /evo:text -->
