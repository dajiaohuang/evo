## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Holotype IVPP V13352 preserves long pennaceous feathers attached to forelimbs, hindlimbs and tail; these surfaces document a four-winged anatomical configuration, while flight path, aerodynamic performance and phylogenetic position remain inferred.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Feather placement on a named articulated specimen is direct evidence. Medium confidence separates the preserved surfaces from aerodynamic use, locomotor behaviour and a sampled tree.
<!-- /evo:text -->
