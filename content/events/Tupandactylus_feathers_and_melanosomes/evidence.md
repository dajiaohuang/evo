## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Tupandactylus specimen MCT.R.1884 preserves monofilaments, branched feather-like structures and distinct melanosome geometries in cranial soft tissues; exact colours, signalling roles and a universal pterosaur covering are not directly observed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple structures are documented in a named specimen using microscopy and chemical comparisons. Medium confidence keeps taphonomic, homology and phylogenetic-model uncertainty separate from the preserved morphology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
