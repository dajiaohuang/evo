# Bellairsia reveals a Jurassic anatomical mosaic

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
NMS G.2022.1.1 was imaged at ESRF beamline ID19 and preserves most of the skeleton; the documented mosaic includes a fused astragalus–calcaneum and squamate-like suspensorial traits alongside notochordal vertebrae with presacral intercentra and a pterygoid–vomer contact.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Synchrotron-scanned NMS G.2022.1.1 preserves a Jurassic mosaic of squamate-like and retained skeletal states and is recovered on the squamate stem in cited matrices; proposed kinesis and crown timing are not directly observed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
A near-complete named skeleton, specified ESRF scans and referred comparison material support the anatomical record. Functional mechanics and topology remain conditional on reconstruction, character coding and molecular-backbone constraints.
