## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The directly dated Ust’-Ishim 1 femur yielded genome PRJEB6622 with long Neanderthal-derived segments; the inferred 7–13 ka pre-individual admixture interval is a recombination model, not a directly observed contact event.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
High confidence reflects replicated direct dating, high-coverage sequence data and identifiable ancestry segments while retaining timing, geography and number of contacts as model-dependent.
