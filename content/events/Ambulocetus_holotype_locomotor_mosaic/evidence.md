## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Ambulocetus holotype H-GSP 18507 preserves a partial skeleton with an amphibious locomotor mosaic; otter-like swimming and sea-lion-like movement on land are functional hypotheses rather than preserved behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named skeleton directly preserves relevant vertebral and limb anatomy, but stroke pattern and terrestrial gait are reconstructed from comparative mechanics and incomplete material.
<!-- /evo:text -->
