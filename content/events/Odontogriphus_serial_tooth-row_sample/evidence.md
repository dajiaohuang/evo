## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The 189-specimen Odontogriphus sample repeatedly preserves paired transverse tooth rows on a membrane with posterior replacement, together with a broad foot and mantle-groove structures; calling this apparatus a molluscan radula is strongly argued but remains a homology interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
