## Cosmoselachus reveals an operculate symmoriiform
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
CT reconstruction of the partial holotype AMNH FF 20509 documents elongate fused branchial rays forming a corrugated operculate structure over posterior gill arches, adding a bounded Carboniferous body-plan sample to the early chondrichthyan record.
