## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The partial holotype AMNH FF 20509 of Cosmoselachus mehlingi from the Late Mississippian Fayetteville Shale preserves elongate rays from the hyoid and branchial arches fused into a corrugated operculum covering at least two posterior branchial arches.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary description and CT reconstruction directly document the accessioned partial specimen and operculate anatomy, while its damage and incomplete body limit broader functional inference.
