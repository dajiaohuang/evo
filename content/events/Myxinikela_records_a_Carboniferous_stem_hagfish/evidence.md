## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Myxinikela siroka holotype FMNH PF15373 and paratype FMNH PF8472 from the Moscovian Francis Creek Shale, approximately 310–307 Ma, preserve a cyclostome–hagfish anatomical mosaic and support a stem or putative total-group hagfish interpretation; they do not set the crown-Myxini range or demonstrate modern hagfish habitat, slime production or ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The revision compares both accessioned specimens, reports measurements, locality and anatomy, and explicitly recovers a stem-hagfish mosaic. Medium confidence reflects different taphonomic histories, equivocal structures including cartilage in the paratype and the substantial correction of the original modernized reconstruction.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
修订研究比较了两件有编号标本，报告了尺寸、地点和解剖，并明确恢复出盲鳗干群镶嵌。中等置信度反映两件标本的埋藏史不同、副模软骨等结构存在歧义，以及原先过度现代化复原受到大幅修正。
<!-- /evo:text -->
