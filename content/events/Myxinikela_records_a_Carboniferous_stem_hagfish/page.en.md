# Myxinikela records a Carboniferous stem hagfish

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Myxinikela records a Carboniferous stem hagfish
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Holotype FMNH PF15373 and paratype FMNH PF8472 from Moscovian Francis Creek Shale preserve a cyclostome–hagfish mosaic at roughly 310–307 Ma; the revised material is stem-hagfish evidence and is excluded from the crown-Myxini range.
