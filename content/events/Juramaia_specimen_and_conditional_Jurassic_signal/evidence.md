## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
BMNH PM1343B preserves a partial articulated skeleton whose original study attributed it to approximately 160-million-year-old Tiaojishan strata and recovered a basal eutherian position; provenance, age use and topology remain conditional, and an unconstrained tip-dating analysis instead estimated an Early Cretaceous morphological age signal.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen anatomy is direct, but collection provenance, stratigraphic attribution, character scores and tip-dating priors condition the competing age and topology interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
