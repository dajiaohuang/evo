## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The isolated wood specimen PB22285 from the 153–165 Ma Tiaojishan Formation of western Liaoning was described as Ginkgoxylon liaoningense from a suite of wood-anatomical characters, providing a well-dated Middle–Late Jurassic Ginkgo-like wood record without establishing the first appearance of Ginkgophyta or an association with a particular leaf species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen, locality, formation, numerical interval and diagnostic anatomy are directly described and illustrated. Medium confidence preserves the organ-taxon and single-specimen boundary, including the authors' statement that the wood cannot safely be linked to any one co-occurring foliage species.
