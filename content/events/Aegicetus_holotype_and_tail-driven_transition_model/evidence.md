## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Aegicetus holotype CGM 60584 records loss of a weight-bearing sacroiliac joint, posterior vertebral elongation and limb reduction; a shift toward mid-body-and-tail propulsion is inferred, not directly observed, and a fluke is not preserved.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The associated skeleton provides unusually extensive anatomy, but swimming mode is reconstructed and the paper discloses conflicting evidence for placement immediately around the Bartonian–Priabonian boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->
