## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
AM F66763 is a right dentary fragment with three tribosphenic molars from the Griman Creek Formation; its monotreme affinity rests on published dental comparison and does not make Steropodon a direct ancestor or establish a universal monotreme first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named jaw and teeth are direct specimen evidence, whereas molar homology, phylogenetic affinity and record priority depend on comparison and sampling.
<!-- /evo:text -->
