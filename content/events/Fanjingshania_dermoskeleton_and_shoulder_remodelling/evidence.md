## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Fanjingshania renovata from the late Aeronian Rongxi Formation at approximately 439 Ma is represented by holotype IVPP V27433.1 and more than one thousand isolated dermal elements; tomography of IVPP V27433.8–12 and V27434.2 shows a stem-chondrichthyan shoulder spine–plate complex formed through multiple growth centres and cyclical bone-and-dentine resorption, without an articulated body or associated dentition.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The original description fixes the material and horizon, and the later open-access tomography study directly images cement lines, resorption and tissue replacement across several referred elements. Medium confidence retains the disarticulated association, analogical full-body reconstruction and the prohibition on synonymizing co-occurring Qianodus teeth.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

## claim-statements.zh

约 439 Ma 晚埃隆期融溪组的 Fanjingshania renovata 由正模 IVPP V27433.1 和一千余件孤立皮骨骼元件代表；IVPP V27433.8–12 与 V27434.2 的断层成像显示，其软骨鱼干群肩部棘—板复合体由多个生长中心以及骨和牙本质的周期性吸收形成，但尚无关节相连躯体或伴生齿列。
