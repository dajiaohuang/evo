# Fanjingshania dermoskeleton and shoulder remodelling

## Fanjingshania dermoskeleton and shoulder remodelling
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Holotype IVPP V27433.1 and more than one thousand isolated elements document a roughly 439 Ma stem-chondrichthyan dermoskeleton; later tomography of six referred shoulder elements shows cyclical bone-and-dentine resorption without supplying an articulated body or associated teeth.
