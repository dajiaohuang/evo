# Riograndia and Brasilodon break a jaw-joint ladder

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Riograndia and Brasilodon break a jaw-joint ladder
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
A 26-specimen μCT sample found a dentary–squamosal contact in Riograndia but not Brasilodon, supporting iterative acquisition rather than a single ancestor-to-descendant jaw-joint ladder.
