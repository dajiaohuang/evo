# Riograndia and Brasilodon break a jaw-joint ladder

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
μCT reconstructions of Riograndia specimens MPDC-1B1, UFRGS-PV-596-T, UFRGS-PV-833-T and UFRGS-PV-1319-T show dentary–squamosal contact, whereas the nine-specimen Brasilodon sample, including UFRGS-PV-929-T, -1030-T and -1043-T, lacks that articulation.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
In a 26-specimen μCT sample, Riograndia preserves dentary–squamosal contact whereas nine Brasilodon specimens do not, supporting homoplastic acquisition of the contact rather than a single progressive ancestor-to-descendant jaw-joint sequence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named multi-specimen CT sample, segmented models and phylogenetic matrices are archived. Medium confidence limits the claim to sampled anatomy, distinguishes contact from a fully formed mammaliaform joint and avoids assigning crown status or direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->
