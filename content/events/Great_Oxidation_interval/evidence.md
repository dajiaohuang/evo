## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Sulfur-isotope and redox records support a prolonged oxygenation transition; U–Pb baddeleyite ages place its onset between about 2,460 and 2,426 Ma rather than at one instantaneous boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The proxy transition is supported across a broad synthesis and the onset bracket comes from directly dated igneous units. High confidence applies to that bounded onset interval, not to a single universal oxygen threshold or trigger.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
一手 U–Pb 测年把大氧化事件起始约束在约 24.60–24.26 亿年前，综合代用指标则说明变化是分阶段的；高置信度不等于存在单一全球氧阈值或唯一触发机制。
