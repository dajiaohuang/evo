## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Five Moscovian-to-Gzhelian insect fossils document a stem coleopterid, a holometabolous larva, a stem hymenopterid, early Hemiptera and Psocodea, expanding sampled Pennsylvanian Eumetabola without proving synchronous origins or later crown-order first appearances.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The fossils and diagnostic characters are directly described, while exact crown placements and diversification causes remain phylogenetic or palaeoecological interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
