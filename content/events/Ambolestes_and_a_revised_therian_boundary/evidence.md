## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Near-complete holotype STM33-5 preserves ear and hyoid anatomy used in a morphology matrix that recovered Ambolestes and Sinodelphys within Eutheria, shifting the oldest sampled metatherian record to younger North American fossils; this is a dataset-dependent topology, not an observed placental–marsupial split or direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named anatomy and matrix are explicit, but the reassignment of Sinodelphys, ghost-lineage length and therian boundary depend on sampled characters and taxa.
<!-- /evo:text -->
