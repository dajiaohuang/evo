## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Auroralumina preserves two bifurcating polyps in a rigid organic skeleton with tentacle impressions; a morphology analysis places it within crown Cnidaria.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
