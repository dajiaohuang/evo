## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Mimolagus aurorae holotype right M3 IVPP V20115 and referred teeth and tarsals document a large middle-Eocene mimotonid; estimated body mass and convergence on small tapiroid ecomorphology derive from astragalar regression and functional comparison, not direct ecology or ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named specimens, locality and dental and tarsal anatomy are directly documented; body mass, cursoriality, niche and mimotonid relationships remain comparative inferences.
<!-- /evo:text -->
