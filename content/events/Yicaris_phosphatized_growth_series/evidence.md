<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Phosphatized Yicaris dianensis individuals from several growth stages preserve leaf-shaped exites and eucrustacean-like limb organization; crown-eucrustacean placement and epipodite homology are character interpretations rather than an observed crown origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->
