# Early Cretaceous Leefructus eudicot

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Early Cretaceous Leefructus eudicot
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
A whole-plant fossil preserves deeply trilobate leaves and a reproductive axis with five partly united carpels, supporting an Early Cretaceous eudicot assignment while leaving family affinity provisional.
