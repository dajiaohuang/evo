# Qianodus tooth whorls in the late Aeronian

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Qianodus tooth whorls in the late Aeronian
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Twenty-three isolated tooth whorls, including holotype IVPP V26641, provide approximately 439 Ma direct evidence of jawed-vertebrate dentition while remaining disarticulated stem-chondrichthyan material rather than a crown-shark body or a global first-appearance datum.
