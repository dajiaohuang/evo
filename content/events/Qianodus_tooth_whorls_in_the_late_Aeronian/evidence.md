## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Twenty-three isolated Qianodus duplicis tooth whorls, including holotype IVPP V26641, occur in the late Aeronian Ozarkodina guizhouensis biozone of the Rongxi Formation at approximately 439 Ma and provide direct evidence of jawed-vertebrate dentition; they support a stem-chondrichthyan occurrence, not a crown-shark body, the origin of jaws or a global FAD.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary description directly documents the accessioned tooth whorls, histology, developmental sequence and conodont-zone age. Medium confidence preserves the absence of an articulated body or jaw, the restricted dental-character phylogeny and the inability to assign co-occurring scales and spines to this taxon.
<!-- /evo:text -->
