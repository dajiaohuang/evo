## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Scaled physical-model wind-tunnel tests found stable, high-drag gliding at high lift coefficients and limited performance differences among tested hindlimb configurations; these are model outputs, not observed Microraptor behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Measured forces from a disclosed physical model provide reproducible functional evidence. Fossil distortion, reconstructed geometry, scaling and absent living behaviour limit extrapolation.
<!-- /evo:text -->
