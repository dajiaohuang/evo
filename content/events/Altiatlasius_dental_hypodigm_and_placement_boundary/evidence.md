## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Altiatlasius is directly represented by a small Thanetian isolated-tooth hypodigm including holotype THR 141; tooth-locus assignments and its position near or outside crown Primates remain disputed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Low confidence applies to broad placement, not to the existence of the dental specimens: the sample is small, isolated and internally heterogeneous, and subsequent analyses have tested incompatible positions.
<!-- /evo:text -->
