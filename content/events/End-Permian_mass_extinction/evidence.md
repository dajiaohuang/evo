## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CA–ID–TIMS U–Pb ages from the Meishan section place the main end-Permian extinction interval between 251.941 ± 0.037 and 251.880 ± 0.031 Ma, lasting about 60 ± 48 kyr in that calibrated succession.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The interval is bounded by high-precision dated ash beds and a measured fossil succession. High confidence applies to the Meishan calibrated interval, while global ecological expression and causal mechanism require additional records.
<!-- /evo:text -->
