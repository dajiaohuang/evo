## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Holotype ROM 63823 and paratypes of Tokummia katalepsis preserve paired mandibles, maxillipeds and subdivided basipods; their recovery as stem mandibulates is a morphology-matrix result, not evidence that Tokummia is a direct ancestor of crustaceans, insects or myriapods.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named specimens directly preserve the scored appendages, while the statement separately bounds the matrix-derived topology and ancestry claim.
