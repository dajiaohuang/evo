## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Rhaetian holotype LWL-MFN P 64047 preserves plesiosaur anatomy and highly vascularized long-bone tissue; its basal pliosaurid placement and implications for growth rate or metabolism remain topology- and histology-based inferences.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen, horizon, anatomy and sampled histological sections are explicit. Medium confidence retains incomplete preservation, phylogenetic sensitivity and the distinction between tissue pattern and unmeasured physiology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
