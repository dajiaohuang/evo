## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Microtomographic reconstructions of ash-entombed Tatelt Formation Protolenus and Gigoutella specimens directly preserve four post-antennal cephalic limb pairs, a labrum, mouth and digestive tissues; feeding mechanics and group-wide homologies remain comparative interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Three-dimensional articulated anatomy is directly imaged in multiple specimens, but functional and group-wide extrapolations remain inferential.
