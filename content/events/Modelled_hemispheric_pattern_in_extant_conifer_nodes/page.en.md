## Modelled hemispheric pattern in extant conifer nodes
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
A 489-species time tree found older combined median node ages in predominantly Southern Hemisphere conifer lineages than in sampled Northern Hemisphere lineages, a relative living-lineage pattern rather than a fossil range.
