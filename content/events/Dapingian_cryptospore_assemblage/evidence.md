## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Palynological samples from the Zanjón Formation in northwest Argentina contain five genera of naked and envelope-enclosed cryptospore monads and tetrads dated by the source to approximately 473–471 Ma (Dapingian), representing evidence of embryophytes on emerged land rather than a crown-bryophyte fossil.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study reports duplicate laboratory processing, biostratigraphic control and illustrated monads and tetrads from a named horizon. Confidence applies to the dated cryptospore assemblage and its embryophyte interpretation, not to the authors' more speculative inference about the geographic center or still earlier timing of terrestrialization.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

一手研究报告了双实验室处理、明确的生物地层控制以及同一层位中有图示的单体和四分体。高置信度仅适用于有年代约束的隐孢子组合及其胚胎植物解释，不扩展到作者对陆生化中心或更早起源时间的推测。
