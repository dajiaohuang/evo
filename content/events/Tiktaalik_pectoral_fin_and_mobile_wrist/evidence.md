## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The Tiktaalik pectoral-fin endoskeleton contains a robust humerus, radius and ulna, wrist-like joints and distal radials capable of transmitting load while retaining lepidotrichial fin rays; it is a fin, not a free-digit limb or a recorded walking trace.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The articulated fin skeleton and joint surfaces are directly described and illustrated in multiple specimens. High confidence is confined to preserved anatomy; load transmission is a functional inference, and the retained fin rays prevent the evidence from being rewritten as a digit-bearing terrestrial limb.
<!-- /evo:text -->
