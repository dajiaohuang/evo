## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
μCT of the unique split-nodule holotype MNHN.F.MAE.126 reveals a stem-salientian body with fifteen presacrals, caudal vertebrae and possible mentomeckelians; it records an early frog-lineage mosaic, not crown Anura or specialized jumping.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The unique holotype was scanned and redescribed in detail; medium confidence preserves uncertain provenance, damaged anatomy and character interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
