## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The Willwerath Jaekelopterus rhenaniae fixed ramus preserves 36.4 cm; its complete length was estimated at 45.5 cm. The approximately 2.5-metre body length is a separate estimate based on related pterygotid proportions, with possible positive claw allometry remaining a source of uncertainty.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Claw dimensions are direct and reproducible; whole-body gigantism depends on extrapolation from related taxa and scaling assumptions.
<!-- /evo:text -->
