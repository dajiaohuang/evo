## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CT anatomy and dental wear from MCZ7/95A–B and MCZ10/G95 informed a rescored matrix that placed Late Triassic haramiyids outside crown Mammalia, while the postdentary trough supports rather than directly preserves a mandibular middle ear.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named specimens, tomographic observations, wear reconstruction and MorphoBank Project 2292 are available. Medium confidence keeps crown placement conditional on this character matrix and separates preserved jaw surfaces from inferred auditory anatomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->
