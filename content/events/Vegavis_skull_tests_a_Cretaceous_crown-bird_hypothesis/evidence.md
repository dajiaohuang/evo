## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The 69.2–68.4 Ma skull AMNH FARB 30899 supports placement of Vegavis among crown waterfowl in the 2025 combined analysis, but earlier analyses recovered stem-anatid, stem-anseriform or outside-Aves positions, so crown membership remains a scoped hypothesis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The age and nearly complete skull are direct observations, and the new matrix supplies explicit support for waterfowl affinity. Medium confidence retains the published history of conflicting placements and does not turn one topology into observed ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->
