# Neopterygian caudal-fin developmental mosaic

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
Adults and developmental series of stickleback, zebrafish, spotted gar, bowfin, paddlefish and bichir show a gar–teleost hypural-diastema complex and that the earliest caudal lepidotrichia contribute to external fin symmetry.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Comparative anatomy and development show a hypural-diastema complex in gars and teleosts and an early role for caudal fin rays in external symmetry, so the complete complex cannot be treated as an unconditional teleost synapomorphy; descent, loss and latent parallelism remain competing histories.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Adult specimens and developmental series span major sampled actinopterygian lineages and directly document skeletal and soft-tissue components. Medium confidence preserves incomplete species sampling, the paper's hypothesis-level preference for latent homology, and the poor fossil visibility of connective tissue and vasculature.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-statements.zh/0 -->
比较解剖与发育资料显示雀鳝类和真骨鱼类都具有尾下骨间隙复合体，尾鳍鳍条还早期参与外部对称性的建立；因此完整复合体不能被无条件视为真骨鱼共有衍征，继承、丢失与潜在平行演化仍是相互竞争的历史解释。
