## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Burgessomedusa specimens preserve a large bell, more than ninety marginal tentacles and internal radial structures consistent with a free-swimming medusozoan by the middle Cambrian.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple specimens preserve diagnostic gross anatomy; exact placement within Acraspeda and swimming performance remain comparative interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
