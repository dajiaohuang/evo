## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
CAGS 01-IG-1a,b preserves an articulated skeleton with dentition, feet and claws; the original matrix placed Eomaia near the eutherian root and inferred scansoriality, while later combined matrices can place it outside Theria, so neither topology nor locomotor habit is a directly observed ancestral state.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen and anatomical figures are direct evidence. Eutherian placement and scansoriality depend on character coding and functional comparison, and a later primary matrix supplies an explicit alternative.
<!-- /evo:text -->
