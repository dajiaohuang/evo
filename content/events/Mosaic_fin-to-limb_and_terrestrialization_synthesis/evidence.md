## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A synthesis of Devonian and Early Carboniferous evidence separates the acquisition of digit-bearing limbs, changes in locomotion and full terrestriality; these did not form one simultaneous fish-to-land event or a documented chain of direct ancestors.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The open review integrates multiple body fossils, anatomical systems and environments, but it is a 2009 synthesis rather than a primary description of each specimen and predates important later finds. Medium confidence therefore applies to the mosaic framing, not to every historical age, topology or functional interpretation in the review.
<!-- /evo:text -->
