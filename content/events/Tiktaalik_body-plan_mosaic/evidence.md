## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Tiktaalik roseae material from the Fram Formation combines scales, fin rays and fish-like jaws with a shortened skull roof, mobile neck, modified ear region, robust ribs and a functional wrist joint; this mosaic does not demonstrate habitual terrestrial walking or direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype and referred material directly preserve the stated anatomical character combination and the paper illustrates the specimens and phylogenetic analysis. Functional and habitat implications remain hypotheses, so the claim is limited to anatomy and explicitly excludes witnessed behaviour and ancestor status.
<!-- /evo:text -->
