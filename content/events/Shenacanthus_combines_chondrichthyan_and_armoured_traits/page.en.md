## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
The sole near-complete holotype IVPP V300000 records a roughly 436 Ma total-group chondrichthyan body with minute scales and a chondrichthyan-like branchial region but unexpectedly large thoracic armour plates; the exact stem position remains analysis-sensitive.
