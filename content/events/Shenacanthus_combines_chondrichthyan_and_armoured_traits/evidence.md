## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The sole near-complete Shenacanthus vermiformis holotype IVPP V300000 from the approximately 436 Ma Huixingshao Formation combines minute scales, absence of facial dermal bones and a chondrichthyan-like posterior branchial region with large thoracic armour plates; it is total-group chondrichthyan evidence, not a crown-shark diagnosis or a global Chondrichthyes FAD.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The body-plan mosaic is directly illustrated in an articulated holotype, but only one specimen is known and no teeth are preserved. Maximum-parsimony and Bayesian analyses differ in the details of its position, so the atlas retains total-group placement without overstating a precise stem node.
<!-- /evo:text -->
