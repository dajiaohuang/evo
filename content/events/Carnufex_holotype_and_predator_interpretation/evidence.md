## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Partial, skeletally immature holotype NCSM 21558 documents a large-bodied Carnian crocodylomorph; skull and femur scaling plus assemblage context support a top-tier-predator interpretation, not observed feeding, a global first occurrence or direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Specimen identity, preserved elements and locality are explicit. Body size, trophic tier and phylogenetic position rely on proxies, missing anatomy and a character matrix, so confidence remains medium.
<!-- /evo:text -->
