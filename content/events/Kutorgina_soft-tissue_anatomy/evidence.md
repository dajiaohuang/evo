## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Kutorgina chengjiangensis material from Chengjiang preserves paired valves together with an early spirolophe-stage lophophore, digestive tract and pedicle, directly documenting soft anatomy in an early calcareous-shelled rhynchonelliformean.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple anatomical regions are directly described from named fossil material; homology and broader ancestral-state implications remain separate from the observation.
