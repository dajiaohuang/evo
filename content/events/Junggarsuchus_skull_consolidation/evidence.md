## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Articulated specimen IVPP V14010 preserves a nearly complete Middle Jurassic skull with several crocodylian-like consolidations alongside a gracile terrestrial postcranium; functional and phylogenetic interpretations do not make it a directly observed crown ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The articulated skull and anterior skeleton provide strong anatomical evidence. Medium confidence separates those observations from cursorial function and the sampled topology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
