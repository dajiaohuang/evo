## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Cretophasmomima melanogramma preserves a wing-colour pattern that the describing study interpreted as possible leaf mimicry in the Early Cretaceous Jehol setting; the proposed mechanism remains an anatomical inference.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary paper directly documents the colour pattern and plant comparison, but a fossil cannot directly observe crypsis performance, predator response or feeding behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->
