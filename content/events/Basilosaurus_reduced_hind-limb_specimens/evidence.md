## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Egyptian Basilosaurus isis material preserves a tiny jointed pelvic limb and foot, and named specimen UM 93231 supplies measured femoral microstructure; terrestrial locomotion is excluded while a copulatory function remains inferred.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Hind-limb anatomy and the UM 93231 femur measurement are direct specimen evidence. Soft-tissue position, copulatory use and whole-animal swimming effects are functional extrapolations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
