## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The early Carnian Bobosaurus holotype combines nothosaurian and plesiosaurian features and was interpreted near the plesiosaur lineage, but it is an older uncertain candidate rather than diagnostic Plesiosauria; the articulated Rhaetian Rhaeticosaurus provides the older secure comparison used here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Bobosaurus is a named articulated specimen, but its placement was presented with alternatives. Rhaeticosaurus preserves a diagnostic articulated skeleton and was tested phylogenetically, allowing evidence age and assignment confidence to remain separate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-rationales.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-rationales.zh/0 -->
Bobosaurus 是具名关节标本，但原研究同时保留替代位置；Rhaeticosaurus 保存诊断性骨架并接受系统分析，因此可把“更老”与“归属更可靠”分开。
<!-- /evo:text -->
