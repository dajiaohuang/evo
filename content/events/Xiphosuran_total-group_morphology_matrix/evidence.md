## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
An expanded fossil-and-living morphology matrix resolves traditional synziphosurines across several euchelicerate stems rather than as one simple horseshoe-crab lineage; the result revises a classification hypothesis and does not demonstrate morphological stasis or a single habitat history.
<!-- /evo:text -->
