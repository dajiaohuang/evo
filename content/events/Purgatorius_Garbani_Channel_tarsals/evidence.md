## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Isolated UCMP 197509, UCMP 197517 and related late Puercan tarsals preserve arboreal euarchontan ankle morphology and are attributed to Purgatorius in the cited comparative analyses, without demonstrating crown-Primate status.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Medium confidence separates directly preserved ankle anatomy from an attribution based on size, abundance and morphology and from functional and phylogenetic interpretations.
