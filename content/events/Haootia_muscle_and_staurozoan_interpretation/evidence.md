## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Haootia preserves repeated aligned impressions in a tetraradial body, interpreted as muscle bundles and compared with Staurozoa, but fibre identity and detailed homology have competing primary assessments.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype impression is direct evidence; muscle tissue, orientation and crown-staurozoan placement are interpretations with published criticism and later reassessment.
<!-- /evo:text -->
