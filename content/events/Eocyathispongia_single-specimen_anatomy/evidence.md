## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
One phosphatized Eocyathispongia specimen preserves a millimetre-scale, multicellular, three-tube body with funnel and osculum-like openings interpreted as sponge-grade anatomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Cellular geometry is directly imaged by SEM and tomography, but poriferan affinity rests on one specimen without spicules or an independently replicated canal system.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

## claim-statements.zh

<!-- evo:text /records/claim-statements.zh/0 -->
唯一一件磷酸盐化的 Eocyathispongia 标本保存了毫米尺度、多细胞、由三条管组成的躯体，并具漏斗状和类似出水孔的开口，被解释为海绵级构造。
