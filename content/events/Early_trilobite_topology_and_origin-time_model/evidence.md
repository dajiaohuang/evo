## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A reassessed early-arthropod morphology matrix supports a relatively short cryptic interval before the approximately 521 Ma appearance of widespread trilobites; topology and origin time are analytical outputs rather than a Precambrian absence test or a direct fossil date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The published matrix and model comparison are inspectable, while fossil incompleteness, character coding and time priors limit the inferred origin interval.
<!-- /evo:text -->
