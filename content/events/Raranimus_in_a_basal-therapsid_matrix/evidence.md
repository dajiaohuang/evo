# Raranimus in a basal-therapsid matrix

## event / evidenceItems / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/event/evidenceItems/0/statement -->
IVPP V15424 is a roughly 100 mm partial, slightly crushed snout with a combination of cranial and dental characters; four shortest PAUP trees from the published 71-character matrix placed Raranimus immediately outside the remaining sampled Therapsida.

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
A 71-character morphology analysis of partial-snouted holotype IVPP V15424 recovered Raranimus as sister to the other sampled therapsids; the result is a matrix-dependent topology, not observed ancestry or a precise therapsid first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype, characters and four shortest trees are published, but the specimen is fragmentary, topology depends on the sampled matrix, and the authors report inadequate radiometric control for the Dashankou sequence.
<!-- /evo:text -->
