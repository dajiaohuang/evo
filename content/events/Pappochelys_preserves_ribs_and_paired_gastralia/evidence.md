## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The Pappochelys sample preserves broadened ribs and robust paired gastralia without a fused plastron or complete carapace; proposed gastral fusion and its position among sampled stem turtles are homology and topology hypotheses, not a linear ancestral stage.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Several named Vellberg specimens preserve complementary anatomy, but the taxon is reconstructed from multiple individuals and evolutionary sequencing depends on character interpretation and sampled matrices.
<!-- /evo:text -->
