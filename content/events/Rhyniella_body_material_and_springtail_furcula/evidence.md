## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Four Rhyniella praecursor head capsules and roughly ten additional Rhynie-chert body specimens, including an abdomen with a springtail-like furcula, support collembolan affinity without establishing the origin, global first appearance or ecology of Hexapoda.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The material and furcula are described directly, whereas absolute global priority and ecology exceed the specimen sample.
