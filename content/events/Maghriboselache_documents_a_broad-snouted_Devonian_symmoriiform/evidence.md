## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Several partly articulated Maghriboselache mohamezanei skeletons from the Famennian Thylacocephalan Layer of Morocco preserve three-dimensional neurocrania with a broad cartilage-enclosed snout, widely separated nasal capsules, a high-aspect-ratio tail and large pectoral fins; this is a specimen-level sensory and body-plan record, not a global crown-chondrichthyan origin.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study documents multiple named specimens, their Famennian layer and cranial and fin anatomy, while missing caudal regions, compression and matrix-sensitive placement limit organism-wide and lineage-wide inference.
<!-- /evo:text -->
