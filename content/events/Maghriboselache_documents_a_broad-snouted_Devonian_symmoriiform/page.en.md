# Maghriboselache documents a broad-snouted Devonian symmoriiform

<!-- evo:text /records/event/title -->
## Maghriboselache documents a broad-snouted Devonian symmoriiform
<!-- /evo:text -->

## event / summary

<!-- evo:text /records/event/summary -->
Several partly articulated Famennian specimens preserve three-dimensional neurocrania, a broad cartilage-enclosed snout, widely separated nasal capsules, a high-aspect-ratio tail and large pectoral fins; the sample records sensory and body-plan disparity without dating a global crown origin.
