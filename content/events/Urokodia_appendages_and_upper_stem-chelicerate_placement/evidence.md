## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Micro-CT reconstructions of Chengjiang Urokodia aequalis reveal pincer-like short great appendages, overlapping exite flaps and a seven-segmented head; their interpretation as intermediates toward chelicerae and book gills and the upper stem-chelicerate placement are morphology-matrix results.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Three-dimensional appendage anatomy is directly imaged, whereas segmental homology and branch position depend on reconstruction and scoring.
<!-- /evo:text -->
