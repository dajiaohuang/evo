## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Microstructure and carbon, oxygen and nitrogen isotope patterns in approximately 212 Ma Turkish coral skeletons are consistent with photosymbiosis in the sampled Late-Triassic taxa.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple independent measurements converge in the sampled skeletons, while algal cells and symbiont identities are not directly preserved.
<!-- /evo:text -->
