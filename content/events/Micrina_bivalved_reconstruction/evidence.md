## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Mitral and sellate Micrina sclerites can be reconstructed as a near-bilaterally symmetrical sessile bivalved organism: sellate sclerites preserve paired muscle scars, while mitral apophyses are interpreted as muscle platforms. Valve homology and stem-brachiopod placement remain hypotheses rather than preserved articulation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Sellate muscle scars and mitral apophyses are observed, while the muscle-platform interpretation, whole-body reconstruction, valve homology and stem position are tested comparative hypotheses.
