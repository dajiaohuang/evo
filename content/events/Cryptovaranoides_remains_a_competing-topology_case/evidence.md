## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Cryptovaranoides holotype NHMUK PV R36822 and disputed referred bones currently support incompatible published placements because element identity, referral and character scoring differ; this package does not treat the taxon as a secure crown-squamate first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Both research teams publish specimen-based anatomical arguments and explicit matrices, but repeated reanalysis has not converged on character identity, referability or topology. A contested label is more faithful than selecting one tree as fact.
<!-- /evo:text -->
