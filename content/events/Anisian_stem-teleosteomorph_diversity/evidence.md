## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Named specimens of Pseudopholidoctenus, Barschichthys and Ruedersdorfia from the approximately 244 Ma Rüdersdorf locality document taxonomic, anatomical and ecological disparity on the teleosteomorph stem; they do not establish a global fossil first appearance of crown Teleostei or a synchronous worldwide radiation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Holotypes, referred specimens, diagnostic characters and locality context are directly figured and tabulated. Medium confidence retains the source's stem Teleosteomorpha/Pan-Teleostei placement, the approximate locality age, one-locality sampling and the difference between small adult size and demonstrated developmental miniaturization.
<!-- /evo:text -->
