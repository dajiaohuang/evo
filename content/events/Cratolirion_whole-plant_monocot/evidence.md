## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Cratolirion bognerianum from the approximately 115 Ma Crato Formation is anatomically preserved from roots to reproductive organs and was recovered as a crown monocot; it is a specimen-scoped occurrence, not the origin of monocots or an uncontested living-family assignment.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Whole-plant preservation and anatomical imaging connect vegetative and reproductive characters in the same fossil, supporting a crown-monocot interpretation. Medium confidence keeps the approximate formation age and phylogenetic analysis visible and rejects popularized claims that the fossil is literally a modern lily or the first monocot.
<!-- /evo:text -->
