# Pterobranchia

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Yunotubus is a five-specimen early Cambrian fossil colony tested within Pterobranchia; its tubes, zooids and stolon are kept separate from the scored topology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Suspension feeding directly observed in Rhabdopleura normani; fossil diets are not generalized
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Tube-dwelling marine colony in the sampled living Rhabdopleura normani
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Zooids remain in secreted tubes while feeding; colony-level locomotion was not observed
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Millimetre-scale zooids in the sampled living Rhabdopleura; not a class-wide size bound
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Suspension feeder in the living species directly studied
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
Secreted tubular housing with fusellar construction in diagnostic fossil and living graptolithines
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Colonial zooids interconnected by a stolon in Yunotubus and living rhabdopleurids
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Tentaculate, ciliated feeding arms in directly observed living Rhabdopleura
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The approximately 516 Ma Yunotubus colony preserves diagnostic tube construction and a stolon; its pterobranch placement is morphology-matrix bounded and does not make every Cambrian tube a pterobranch.
<!-- /evo:text -->
