# Pojetaia runnegari

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
More than fifty Pojetaia runnegari shell specimens from probably Botomian Ajax Limestone and lowermost Botomian Erkeket Formation preserve a laminar inner fabric comparable to foliated aragonite; the paper does not identify undisputed Cambrian nacre.
<!-- /evo:text -->
