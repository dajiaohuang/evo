## life-history

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Colonies contain queens, workers and drones. Workers collect pollen and nectar, produce wax and care for brood, with tasks changing with age. Swarming can establish new colonies.

## range

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Native populations occur in Europe, Africa and the Middle East, while beekeeping has spread the species widely beyond that range. Managed or feral records do not establish local native status.
