## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This order includes ants, bees, wasps and sawflies. Winged adults couple their wings with small hooks and development includes a pupa. Wingless workers still belong here; broad-waisted sawflies show why a narrow waist is not universal.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Flower visiting, herbivory, predation and parasitoid life histories coexist. Parasitoid larvae develop at a host’s expense and ultimately kill it. Ant and honey-bee colonies do not represent all wasp and bee lifestyles.
