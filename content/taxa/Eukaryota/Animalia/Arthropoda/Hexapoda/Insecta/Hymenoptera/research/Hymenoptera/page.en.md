# Hymenoptera

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Hymenoptera is represented here by a transcriptomic topology, a fossil-calibrated history and a systematic review of early fossils; these are complementary samples rather than a complete order census.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Parasitoid, predatory and pollinating contexts occur among sampled lineages; no order-wide diet is assigned
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
The cited fossil and genomic studies do not provide a complete habitat distribution

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
