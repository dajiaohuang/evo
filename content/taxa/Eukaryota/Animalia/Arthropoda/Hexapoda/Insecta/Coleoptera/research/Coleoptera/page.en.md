# Coleoptera

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Coleoptera combines a fossil disparity record built from elytra with a broad but sampled genomic analysis of living beetles; neither source is a complete order history.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Plant associations and herbivory are discussed for sampled lineages; order-wide diet is not assigned
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Permian–Triassic vegetation settings and living samples provide context, not a complete habitat map
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Elytra protect the hindwings; flight performance is not measured by the cited disparity and genomic studies
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Not assigned as a complete comparative body-size dataset
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Vegetation-associated ecological shifts are inferred in the fossil sample; no universal guild is assigned
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Zhao et al. document elytral disparity through the end-Permian vegetation transition, while McKenna et al. resolve a sampled genomic history; fossil ecology, topology and range remain bounded by each study.
