## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Many lady-beetle larvae and adults prey on aphids, scale insects and other small animals, but plant-feeding members also occur. Similar appearance does not establish species identity or a predatory diet.
