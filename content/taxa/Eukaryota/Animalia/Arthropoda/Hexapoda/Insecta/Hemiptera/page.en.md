## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Hemiptera includes cicadas, aphids, scale insects and true bugs. Feeding uses piercing-sucking mouthparts. A hardened wing base and membranous tip characterize many true bugs, not the entire order.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Development is generally incomplete metamorphosis, with nymphs molting into adults. Many feed on plant fluids; others are predators or blood feeders. Some true bugs live on or within fresh water.
