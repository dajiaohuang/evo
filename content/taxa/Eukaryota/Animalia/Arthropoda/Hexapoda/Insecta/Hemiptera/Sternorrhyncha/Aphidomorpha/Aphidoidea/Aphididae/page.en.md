## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Common aphids have soft bodies and slender mouthparts for extracting plant fluids; many bear paired cornicles near the abdomen’s rear. A species may produce winged and wingless forms, and color alone is not a species diagnosis.

## development

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Many species reproduce parthenogenetically; some produce sexual generations seasonally. Observations from mild climates do not establish a fixed life cycle for all aphids everywhere.
