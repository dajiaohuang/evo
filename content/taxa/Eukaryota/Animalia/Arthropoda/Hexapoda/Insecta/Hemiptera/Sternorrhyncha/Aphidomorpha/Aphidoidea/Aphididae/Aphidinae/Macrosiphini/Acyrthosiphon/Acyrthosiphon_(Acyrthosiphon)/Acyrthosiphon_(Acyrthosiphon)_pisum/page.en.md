## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Green and pink forms occur, with long antennae, legs and cornicles. The source uses narrow dark bands on antennal segments to help distinguish the similar blue alfalfa aphid; color alone is insufficient.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Observations in California alfalfa describe sap feeding, honeydew and predation by lady beetles and other natural enemies. This agricultural setting is not a complete host or distribution inventory.
