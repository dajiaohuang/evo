## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
In the study's combined dual-port olfactometer analysis, each of the six F2–F4 colonies originating from Thailand, Cameroon, Florida, New York, Maryland, and Virginia was more likely to choose guinea-pig odor than human odor. This is a laboratory host-choice result for the tested colonies and assay, not a measure of field blood meals or a range-wide species preference.
