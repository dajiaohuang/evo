## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Many adults resemble bees or wasps but are flies. Some larvae eat aphids, while others use decaying plant material. The family name must not be interpreted as an aphid-feeding rule for every larva.
