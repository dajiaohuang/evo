# Cretophasmomima melanogramma

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
One female and two male Yixian specimens preserve a wing shoulder pad and female abdominal operculum used to identify a stem-Phasmatodea insect, with dark wing venation interpreted as a possible leaf-mimicry signal.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Plant association and leaf mimicry suggest a phytophagous context; feeding is not directly observed
<!-- /evo:text -->
