# Cretophasmomima melanogramma

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Cretophasmomima melanogramma was described as a stem-Phasmatodea insect on the basis of one female and two male Yixian specimens with diagnostic operculum and wing characters.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study names the species and explicitly uses the female abdominal operculum and forewing shoulder pad for its stem-Phasmatodea assignment; broader phasmatodean relationships are not inferred here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
The profiled Cretophasmomima material is from the Yixian Formation of the Jehol biota in Inner Mongolia, China; this records the sampled locality rather than a complete species distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The primary description gives the formation, Jehol biota context and Inner Mongolia locality for the three specimens. High confidence is confined to those records.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Dark longitudinal wing veins were interpreted as possible leaf mimicry associated with a contemporaneous plant organ; the described specimens measure approximately 47–56 mm, suggesting a plant-associated crypsis context without directly observing diet or behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The paper explicitly proposes crypsis from wing coloration and plant comparison, but the fossil does not directly record feeding, behaviour or a population-level ecological guild.
<!-- /evo:text -->
