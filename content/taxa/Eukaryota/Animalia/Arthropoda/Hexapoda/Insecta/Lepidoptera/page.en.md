## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Adult moths and butterflies usually bear scales on their bodies and wings. Many sip liquids through a coiled proboscis, while some groups retain chewing mouthparts or have reduced mouthparts.

## development

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Caterpillars commonly chew plant tissue before pupation and adult emergence. Day versus night activity alone cannot distinguish butterflies from moths: some moths are active by day.
