# Strudiella

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The single, nearly complete Strud specimen was described as a Late Devonian insect, but a subsequent re-investigation rejected that identification; the profile preserves both the named material and the unresolved arthropod affinity.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
An omnivorous orthopteroid-mandible interpretation was proposed; feeding is not directly observed
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Garrouste et al. identified the specimen as an insect, whereas Hörnschemeyer et al. found that interpretation untenable after re-examination; the atlas therefore treats Strudiella as a contested arthropod sample rather than a secure insect calibration.
