# Mollisonia symmetrica

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Carbonaceous films in two specimens preserve optic nerves, a possible cephalic synganglion and a segmental ventral nerve cord in a contested stem-chelicerate context.
<!-- /evo:text -->
