# Eurypterida

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Eurypterida is represented by an early Ordovician Fezouata occurrence, a reviewed late Permian record and morphology matrices; the combined history is a sampled fossil envelope rather than a complete origin-to-extinction sequence.
<!-- /evo:text -->
