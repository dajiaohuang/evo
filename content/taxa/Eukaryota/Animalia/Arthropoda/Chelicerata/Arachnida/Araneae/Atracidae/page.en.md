## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Spinnerets and the male second leg help distinguish Australian funnel-webs, but the traits must be compared within the family. The Australian Museum describes a conspicuous mating spur on the second-leg tibia as characteristic of Atrax; males in the other funnel-web genera differ in this feature.
