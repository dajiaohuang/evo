## taxonomy_story

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Unusually large Newcastle males prompted a re-examination of species boundaries in Sydney-region funnel-webs. By comparing morphology and DNA from newly collected and historical museum specimens, researchers distinguished the Sydney funnel-web Atrax robustus, reinstated A. montanus and newly described A. christenseni. This result concerns that regional species complex, not a phylogenetic reconstruction of the whole genus.
