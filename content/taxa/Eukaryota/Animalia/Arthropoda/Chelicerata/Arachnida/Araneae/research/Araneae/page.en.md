# Araneae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Araneae is represented by a fossil-origin review, a Carboniferous arachnid comparison and an extensive living-spider target-gene phylogeny; the combined record remains sampled and revisable.
<!-- /evo:text -->
