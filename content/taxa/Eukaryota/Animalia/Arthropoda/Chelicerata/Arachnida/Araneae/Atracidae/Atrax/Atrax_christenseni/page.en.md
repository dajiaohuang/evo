## taxonomy_story

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Unusually large males from Newcastle prompted a reassessment, after which the research team named that population—formerly included in A. robustus—as the new species A. christenseni. Males can be distinguished from similar species by the longer palpal tibia and embolus; size alone is unreliable.

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Records reported by the Australian Museum are concentrated within 25 km of Newcastle, and precise localities are withheld; biological information remains limited. A narrow known-record area is not proof that the full distribution has been measured.
