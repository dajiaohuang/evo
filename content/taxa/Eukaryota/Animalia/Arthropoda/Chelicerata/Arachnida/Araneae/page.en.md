## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Spiders are arachnids, not insects: the cephalothorax and abdomen are joined by a narrow pedicel, and the four pairs of walking legs, pedipalps and spinnerets have distinct roles. Silk is not synonymous with a web; it can capture prey, wrap it, protect eggs, line retreats and serve as a safety line.

## life_history

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The Australian Museum describes silk beyond prey capture: females wrap eggs in silk, and spiderlings may disperse by walking, bridging between nearby foliage on a line, or letting fine silk catch the breeze. These are different strategies reported across spiders, not behaviors attributed to every species.
