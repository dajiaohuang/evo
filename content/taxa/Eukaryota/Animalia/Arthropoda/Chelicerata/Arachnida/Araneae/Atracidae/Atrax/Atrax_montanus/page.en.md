## taxonomy_story

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Atrax montanus was once treated within the variation of the Sydney funnel-web; a 2025 morphological and molecular analysis of the regional complex restored the name published in 1914. The Museum notes that it resembles A. robustus and overlaps its range, showing why an old name or general appearance alone is insufficient for identification.

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The Australian Museum records this species in New South Wales from the Watagans south to Bowral and west to the upper Blue Mountains; its more southerly and westerly range overlaps the Sydney funnel-web. These are regional records, not a range for all of Australia.
