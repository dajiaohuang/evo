## evolution / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/evolution/claims/0/text -->
Using draft genome and silk-gland transcriptome assemblies for Caerostris darwini, the study reported at least 31 putative spidroin genes and expansions of several spidroin gene classes relative to Trichonephila clavipes. It also detected sex- and gland-specific expression patterns in the sampled tissues. The proposed connection between these patterns and exceptional dragline-silk toughness is presented by the authors as a hypothesis, not as a demonstrated causal result.
<!-- /evo:text -->
