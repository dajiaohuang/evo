# Megachelicerax cousteaui

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Holotype KUMIP 314091a,b preserves massive three-segmented chelicerae and a pseudobiramous body plan in a middle Cambrian stem-chelicerate analysis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Predatory feeding is inferred from massive chelicerae; prey is not preserved
<!-- /evo:text -->
