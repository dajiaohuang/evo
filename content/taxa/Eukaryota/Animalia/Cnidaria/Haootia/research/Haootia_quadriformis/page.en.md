# Haootia quadriformis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The holotype NFM F-994, documented together with plastotype OUM ÁT.424/p, preserves a quadrate Ediacaran impression with repeated aligned structures interpreted as muscular tissue, while later studies dispute the orientation and strength of its staurozoan comparison.
<!-- /evo:text -->
