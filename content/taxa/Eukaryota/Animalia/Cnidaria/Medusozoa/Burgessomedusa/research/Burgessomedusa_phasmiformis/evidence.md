# Burgessomedusa phasmiformis

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Raymond Quarry Burgessomedusa specimens preserve a bell, marginal tentacles and radial structures consistent with a swimming medusozoan; this middle-Cambrian sample is not the first appearance of all medusae.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Multiple specimens support the described gross anatomy, while broader medusozoan placement and first-occurrence claims remain outside the sampled quarry evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Burgessomedusa is displayed at 506–504 Ma only as the Raymond Quarry specimen interval described for the genus; it is not a crown-medusozoan FAD, direct ancestor or global continuous duration.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
Moon et al. (2023) directly documents the Raymond Quarry specimens, diagnosis and medusozoan comparison. Medium confidence is bounded to that sampled occurrence and does not extrapolate a higher-clade range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Moon et al. describe Burgessomedusa phasmiformis from Raymond Quarry and recover a medusozoan affinity; exact placement among stem cubozoans, stem Acraspeda and alternatives remains model-dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The named material and matrix result are directly reported, but alternative coding lowers placement resolution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
The Raymond Quarry sample includes 182 specimens, with a cuboidal umbrella up to about 20 cm high, more than 90 short marginal tentacles and preserved internal features.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
These are observations from described specimens; three-dimensional shape remains affected by compression.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The described Burgessomedusa material is from Raymond Quarry in the Burgess Shale of British Columbia, Canada; this is a locality claim, not a global distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
The source identifies the quarry and collection; it does not establish wider occurrence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/5/statement -->
Bell shape and specimen associations provide indirect support for free swimming and a possible nektobenthic predatory habit, not observed feeding or measured swimming performance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/5/confidenceRationale -->
The authors explicitly condition prey-capture inference on preservation and comparison with living medusozoans.
<!-- /evo:text -->
