# Burgessomedusa phasmiformis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Raymond Quarry sample of 182 compressed middle-Cambrian fossils preserves a large bell and numerous marginal tentacles; the authors recover a medusozoan affinity, while its exact placement is model-dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->
