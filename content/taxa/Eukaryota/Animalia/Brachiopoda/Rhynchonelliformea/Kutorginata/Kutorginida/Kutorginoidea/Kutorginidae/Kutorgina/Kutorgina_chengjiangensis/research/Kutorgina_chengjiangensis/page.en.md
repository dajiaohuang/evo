# Kutorgina chengjiangensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Articulated Kutorgina chengjiangensis specimens from Chengjiang preserve valves together with an early spirolophe lophophore, digestive tract and pedicle; these associated soft tissues document a sampled brachiopod anatomy, not a complete lineage or direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->
