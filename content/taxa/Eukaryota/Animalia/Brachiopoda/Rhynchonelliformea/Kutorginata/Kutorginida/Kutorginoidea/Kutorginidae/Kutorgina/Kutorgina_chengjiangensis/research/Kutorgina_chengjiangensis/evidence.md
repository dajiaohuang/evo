# Kutorgina chengjiangensis

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Chengjiang Kutorgina specimens preserve paired valves with lophophore, digestive tract and pedicle anatomy; this formation-scale sample documents one early rhynchonelliformean, not the global origin of brachiopods.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named material directly preserves multiple anatomical regions, while broader ancestral-state and first-appearance interpretations remain separate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Zhang et al. establish Kutorgina chengjiangensis from Chengjiang material and describe its articulated brachiopod anatomy; the species-level diagnosis is distinct from any claim that it is a direct brachiopod ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study provides a named species diagnosis and illustrated material; ancestral-state implications remain comparative and are not promoted into the taxonomic observation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
The Kutorgina chengjiangensis occurrence documented here is restricted to the Chengjiang Lagerstätte in Yunnan, South China; it does not establish the complete geographic range of the species or brachiopods.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The locality and stratigraphic setting are explicitly reported for the named material, while wider distribution is outside this occurrence study.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
The preserved lophophore and pedicle support a suspension-feeding, attached functional interpretation for the sampled Kutorgina chengjiangensis anatomy; feeding behaviour and habitat use remain inferred rather than directly observed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The anatomical regions are directly described, but functional ecology is an interpretation of fossil anatomy and preservation rather than an observation of behaviour.

## claims / statement

The Kutorgina chengjiangensis material in the Chengjiang Lagerstätte supports a 520–518 Ma Early Cambrian formation-scale study window; this bounded occurrence is not a global brachiopod FAD, LAD or continuous lineage range.
