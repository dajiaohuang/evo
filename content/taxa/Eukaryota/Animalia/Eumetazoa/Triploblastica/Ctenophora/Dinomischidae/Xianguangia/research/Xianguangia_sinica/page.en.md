# Xianguangia sinica

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
An expanded Chengjiang sample of 85 new specimens plus type material supports a tentaculate, suspension-feeding reconstruction. Later anatomical work reinterprets the column as tentacle-sheath complexes; its main Bayesian analysis recovers Dinomischidae on the ctenophore stem, while sensitivity analyses include a polytomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Ou et al. directly document the expanded specimen sample and recover a stem-cnidarian hypothesis; Zhao et al. revise the column interpretation and recover Dinomischidae on the ctenophore stem in the main Bayesian analysis, with a polytomy under alternative coding. Placement remains model-dependent rather than definitively resolved.
