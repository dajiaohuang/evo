# Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A small lower Anisian stem teleosteomorph from the Rüdersdorf Muschelkalk, documented by a skull roof, an almost complete but incomplete-body specimen and two additional skull roofs.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Feeding mode and prey are unresolved for this species in the cited study

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
MB.f.18641, MB.f.19904–19906 and the associated diagnosis document a distinctive small-bodied stem teleosteomorph; its Rüdersdorf occurrence is a local sample, not a global teleost origin or first appearance.
