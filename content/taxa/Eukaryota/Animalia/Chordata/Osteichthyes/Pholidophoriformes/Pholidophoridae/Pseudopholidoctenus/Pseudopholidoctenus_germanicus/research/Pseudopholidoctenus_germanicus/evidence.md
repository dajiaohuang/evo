# Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus is a named stem teleosteomorph established from the Rüdersdorf type material, including holotype MB.f.18641 and paratypes MB.f.19904–19906.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary description provides a new genus and species diagnosis, type material and comparison with other stem teleosts. Confidence remains medium because the profile preserves the source's stem placement and does not infer direct ancestry or universal consensus.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
The Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus material comes from the Rüdersdorf opencast mine in the Central European (Germanic) Basin, Germany; this locality does not establish a global distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study gives the mine, regional basin and German locality directly. Confidence is medium because a single fossil locality cannot establish absence, endemism or a global geographic range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
The cited description records an aquatic fish from a marine restricted-basin setting, but it does not establish a species-specific diet, locomotor performance or trophic guild for Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The source discusses the marine setting and compares jaw and body form, while explicitly limiting feeding inferences across early teleosteomorphs. Medium confidence applies to this bounded evidence statement, not to a reconstructed ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus has a fused, ornamented skull roof, large square-like scutes before the epaxial caudal lobe, fewer than 18 principal caudal rays and no hypaxial procurrent rays in the diagnosis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The diagnosis and Figures 2–6 directly document the named skeletal, scale and fin characters. Confidence is medium because the material is incomplete and the diagnosis concerns a limited type sample rather than population-wide variation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The displayed approximately 244 Ma point bounds the lower Anisian Rüdersdorf sample of Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus and is not a global teleosteomorph or teleost first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->
