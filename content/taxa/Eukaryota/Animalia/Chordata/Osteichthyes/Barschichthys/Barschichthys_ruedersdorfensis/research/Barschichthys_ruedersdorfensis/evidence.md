# Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis is a named stem teleosteomorph and type species of Barschichthys, based on holotype MB.f.19907 and paratypes MB.f.19908–19909 from Rüdersdorf.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary description supplies a genus and species diagnosis, type material and explicit placement among stem teleosts. Confidence remains medium because the partial referred specimen and stem placement retain the source's sampling limits.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
The Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis type and referred specimens come from the Rüdersdorf opencast mine in the Central European (Germanic) Basin, Germany; no global distribution is inferred.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study reports the named mine and basin directly for the type material. Confidence is medium because the one locality and small collection cannot establish absence, endemism or a global geographic range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis has a broad lobated skull-roof margin lacking the triangular nasal region, a fused ornamented cranial plate, ganoid scales and a long lower jaw with small conical teeth.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The family and species diagnosis plus Figures 7–10 directly document the skull roof, jaw, scale and ornamentation characters. Confidence is medium because preservation is incomplete and the referred trunk specimen is limited.
<!-- /evo:text -->
