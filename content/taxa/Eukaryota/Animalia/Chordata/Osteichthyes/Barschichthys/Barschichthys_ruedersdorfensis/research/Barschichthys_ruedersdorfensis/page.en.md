# Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A small lower Anisian stem teleosteomorph known from two skull roofs and a partial head and trunk that preserve its characteristic ornamented cranial and scale anatomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->
