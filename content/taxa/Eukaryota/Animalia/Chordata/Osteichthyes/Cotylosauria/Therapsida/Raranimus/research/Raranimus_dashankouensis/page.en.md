# Raranimus dashankouensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Holotype IVPP V15424 is a fragmentary Middle Permian snout tested in a 71-character morphology matrix; its basal-therapsid position is an analytical result, not direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Carnivorous diet inferred from cranial and dental morphology
<!-- /evo:text -->
