# Haramiyavia clemmenseni

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
CT scans and occlusal reconstruction of named Haramiyavia jaws document a Late Triassic haramiyidan whose placement outside crown Mammalia is specific to the cited character matrix.
<!-- /evo:text -->
