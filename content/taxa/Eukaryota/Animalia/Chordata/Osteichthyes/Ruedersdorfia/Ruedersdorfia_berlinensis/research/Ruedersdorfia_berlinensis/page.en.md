# Ruedersdorfia berlinensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A lower Anisian stem teleosteomorph represented by three ornamented skull roofs from the Rüdersdorf Muschelkalk, with no preserved body outline.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Holotype MB.f.19910 and paratypes MB.f.14888 and MB.f.19911 document a diagnostic skull-roof combination and an incertae-sedis position within Teleosteomorpha; body anatomy, ecology and global range remain unavailable.
