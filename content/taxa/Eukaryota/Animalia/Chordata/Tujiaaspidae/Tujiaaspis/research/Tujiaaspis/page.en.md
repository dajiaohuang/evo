# Tujiaaspis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Telychian galeaspid genus anchored here by articulated Tujiaaspis vividus material from the Huixingshao Formation, including the holotype and two figured paratypes from Chongqing Municipality and Hunan.
