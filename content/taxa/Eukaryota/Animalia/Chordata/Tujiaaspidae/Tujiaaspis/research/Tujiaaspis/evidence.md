# Tujiaaspis

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Tujiaaspis vividus is a galeaspid represented by articulated Huixingshao Formation material; its placement is a published phylogenetic interpretation and is not presented as direct ancestry to jawed vertebrates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Tujiaaspis vividus: articulated specimens and a published phylogenetic analysis support this limited identification. Confidence remains medium because the placement is model-dependent and the historical Ostracodermi navigation grade is not a natural clade.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
The figured Tujiaaspis material comes from the Huixingshao Formation at Lianghe village, Yongdong town, Xiushan County, Chongqing Municipality, and Kapeng Reservoir in Baojing County, Xiangxi, Hunan, South China; these are sampled localities, not a global distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
Tujiaaspis vividus: the primary study maps the localities and identifies the fish-bearing horizon. A single formation sample cannot establish absence, endemism or global geographic extent.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
The study reconstructs continuous ventrolateral fins and models their passive lift, but diet, life habitat, trophic guild and measured swimming performance remain unresolved.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
Tujiaaspis vividus: the model result is explicitly bounded to reconstructed morphology and CFD assumptions. Medium confidence applies to that boundary, not to a full ecological reconstruction.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Holotype IVPP V26668 and paratypes IVPP V27410–V27411 preserve three unpaired dorsal fins, an approximately symmetrical hypochordal tail and continuous branchial-to-caudal ventrolateral fins.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
Tujiaaspis vividus: the named specimens and figures directly support the recorded postcranial characters. Confidence is medium because preservation and reconstruction limit character completeness and variation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The displayed 438.6–432.9 Ma interval is the Telychian time-scale envelope for the Huixingshao Formation Tujiaaspis sample and is not a global genus range, FAD, LAD or divergence date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

Tujiaaspis vividus: the primary source and concrete geological locator support the source horizon. Confidence is medium because the numeric envelope is locality- and stage-scoped rather than a global first or last appearance.
