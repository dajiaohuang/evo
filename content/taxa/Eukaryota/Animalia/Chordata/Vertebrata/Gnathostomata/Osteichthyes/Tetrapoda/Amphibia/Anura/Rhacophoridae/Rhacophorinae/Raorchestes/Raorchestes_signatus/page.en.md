## Variable markings complicate comparison with thodai
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7936570-4rjb3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The 2021 remarks noted that dorsal colour and markings, and the presence or absence of radiating iris lines, can vary among signatus individuals within a population. Those traits overlapped with the published thodai diagnosis and motivated a proposed synonymy. This is evidence that the cited colour characters alone were insufficient for the comparison, not a population-wide measurement of each morph’s frequency or an independently certified merger. The original thodai locality and specimens remain attributed to that source concept; they are not newly added signatus occurrences. Additional specimen-linked evidence is needed to reconcile the catalogue and the paper’s interpretation.
