# Buriolestes

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Buriolestes is anchored by holotype ULBRA-PVT280 from the approximately 233.2 Ma Late Triassic assemblage; it is a sampled occurrence, not a global genus or dinosaur first appearance and not a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype, assemblage and radiometric context are directly reported. High confidence is limited to the named sample and age context.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Buriolestes schultzi is based on articulated holotype ULBRA-PVT280, and the study recovers it among sauropodomorphs in its sampled morphology matrix; the matrix placement is not evidence of direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The holotype is directly documented, while phylogenetic placement depends on character coding, taxon sampling and analysis settings.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Holotype ULBRA-PVT280 includes a partial skull, axial skeleton, forelimb and nearly complete left hind limb, and preserves recurved, serrated teeth; these observations are limited to the named specimen.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

## claims / statement
