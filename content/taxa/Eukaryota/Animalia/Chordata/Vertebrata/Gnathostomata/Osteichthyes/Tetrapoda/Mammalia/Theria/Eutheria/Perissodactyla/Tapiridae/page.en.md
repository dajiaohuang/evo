## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A mobile short proboscis formed by the nose and upper lip grasps vegetation. Forefeet have four digits and hindfeet three; cheek teeth bear transverse ridges. Juvenile stripes and spots should not be used as adult identification characters.
