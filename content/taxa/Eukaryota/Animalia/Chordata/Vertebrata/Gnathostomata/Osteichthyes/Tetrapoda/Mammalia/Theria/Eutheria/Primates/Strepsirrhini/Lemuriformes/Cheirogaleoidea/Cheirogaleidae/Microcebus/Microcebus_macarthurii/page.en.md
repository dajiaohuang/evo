## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Schüßler and colleagues studied mouse-lemur assemblages in a small northeastern region of Madagascar in 2020 and described MacArthur's mouse lemur and a close unnamed lineage as apparently restricted to lowland forest. This conclusion belongs to the paper's regional sites and comparisons; it should not be extended into a complete modern range or species-wide habitat preference.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In a morphometric comparison with its closest unnamed lineage, the paper reported differences in five of 13 parameters and described MacArthur's mouse lemur as smaller-bodied with a longer tail. This result comes from particular samples and measurements; the study also reports some misclassification, so one trait is not a sufficient diagnosis for every individual.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This account is limited to one northeastern regional study and its comparison with an unnamed lineage; it does not assess current full distribution, natural history or conservation. MDD currently displays Anjiahely Mouse Lemur; this page retains the MacArthur mouse lemur name used in its cited literature. The Chinese common name is editorial; the page is not externally expert-reviewed.
