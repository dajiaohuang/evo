## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A transect study in Menabe Central, western Madagascar, found a significant negative within-transect association between Madame Berthe's mouse lemur and the gray mouse lemur (Microcebus murinus) in non-degraded forest during the dry season (ISIA T = −0.13, p = 0.013). Surveys ran from 2003–2007 on 34 one-kilometre transects, with 786 mouse-lemur sightings, including 92 M. berthae. This local habitat-season pattern does not prove competition or represent other seasons and sites.
