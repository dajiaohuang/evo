## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A microbiome study compared fecal samples from two wild populations and one captive population, each represented by multiple groups. Microbial communities varied somewhat between wild populations, but differences between wild and captive sifakas were larger; captive samples also lacked some microbes observed in wild animals. This work connects feeding environment and gut microbial ecology, but it is not a direct field-diet or population study.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The study compares gut microbiomes across two wild and one captive population; it does not by itself establish the cause of differences or describe the species’ natural history. This profile is not externally expert-reviewed.
