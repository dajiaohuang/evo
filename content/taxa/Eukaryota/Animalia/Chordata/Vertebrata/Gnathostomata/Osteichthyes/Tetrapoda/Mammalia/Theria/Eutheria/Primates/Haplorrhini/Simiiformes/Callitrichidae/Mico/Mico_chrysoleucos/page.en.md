## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2018 multiyear survey reported 14 records from 44 survey days and extended the then-described southern distribution about 100 km to the Maracanã River. A later programme based on 2015–2018 fieldwork reported 12 additional records from Apuí, Maués, Novo Aripuanã and Urucurituba; it described an eastern extension onto Ilha Tupinambarana of about 160 km and a record-based distribution estimate of 46,800 km². These are study-era delineations, not abundance estimates or a complete current census.

## habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2018 study searched trails, roads and river margins in a 3,919 km² middle-Madeira study area across several seasons. The 2023 note drew on ten playback-focused expeditions across southern Amazonia and documented habitat use at recorded sites. The papers have different sampling windows and methods and should not be collapsed into one continuous survey.
