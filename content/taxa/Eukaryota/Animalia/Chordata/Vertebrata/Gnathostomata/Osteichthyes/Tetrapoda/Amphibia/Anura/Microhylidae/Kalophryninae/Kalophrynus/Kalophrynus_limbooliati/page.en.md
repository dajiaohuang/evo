Table 1 records 4 Kalophrynus limbooliati encounters at Gunung Belumut Amenity Forest in Johor, Peninsular Malaysia, or 2.3% of the 172 reported amphibian records. Sampling combined drift-fenced pitfall traps with searches along streams and forest trails in parts of 2022 and February 2023. Most captured animals were released and selected vouchers retained; the article does not document marking that would certify every encounter as a different individual. This is a local survey count, not population density, an elevational limit or complete distribution. The forest’s 1,010 m summit elevation is not assigned as the elevation of every frog record.
