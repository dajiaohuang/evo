## Marmorata is the sampled sister of the Song Hinh lineage
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10415765-qmtb3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
In the sixty-five-specimen combined mitochondrial–BDNF dataset, marmorata was recovered sister to the new albopunctata lineage, within a clade also including annamensis. The reported 16S uncorrected distance to albopunctata is 5.3%. This is a sampled reference relationship, not newly sampled complete marmorata population coverage or a divergence date. The source’s central/northern Vietnam distribution statement is comparison background rather than a new comprehensive range survey.

## Relative head and skin traits come from a cited comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10415765-qmtb3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The account contrasts marmorata’s moderately stocky body, bluntly rounded snout and smoother or weakly pustular dorsum with albopunctata. It describes marmorata’s head as approximately as wide as long, with a relatively longer foot. These are comparative published traits, not a new measured marmorata series or a performance test. The albopunctata side of the head-ratio and webbing comparison contains inconsistencies, so the pair must not be presented as a fully validated numerical identification key.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## A cited pulse comparison does not resolve source unit conflicts
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10415765-qmtb3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The paper cites earlier marmorata call work and prints nine to thirteen pulses per call in comparison with albopunctata. The latter side prints two to six, but elsewhere two to six counts calls in a series and successive pulse counts differ. The cited marmorata figure is not a new recording series in this study, and the inconsistent comparison cannot certify a complete recognition rule. Call, pulse and series remain distinct evidence units.
