## Eastern Chinese records under the monograph’s treatment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-mr77
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The monograph reported localities in Fujian, Jiangxi, Zhejiang and Anhui at 200–1400 m under its B. boettgeri treatment. Its examined material included several mountain localities in those provinces. The remarks retained controversy over Huangshan and Wuyuan lineages: earlier molecular work suggested a possible cryptic Wuyuan species, while one delimitation analysis grouped the lineages. The geographic summary is therefore retained within this dated taxonomic treatment, without certifying that every historical population has the same present species identity.
