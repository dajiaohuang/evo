# Geokichla camaronensis

## Tracing the historical Zoothera name

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 organizes this page at Geokichla camaronensis (3FPZ6). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 8546 and eBird 2021 row 4307 share the name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-DB5CE93A; BirdTree row 4288 gives Zoothera camaronensis, with match type 1BL to 1BT. Read COL synonym 5D7ND is spelled Zoothera cameronensis and points to this ID, whereas the crosswalk spells camaronensis. The discrepancy is retained without silently renaming the source or declaring an exact synonym confirmed. These links locate data without establishing identical species boundaries across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Measured birds and beak/wing values

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 6 birds: 1 female, 4 male and 1 unsexed; 4 birds have complete measurement sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 11.2 mm, tarsus 27.9 mm, unflattened wing 97.3 mm and hand-wing index 25.2 are summaries in this historical row. Inference=NO does not establish equal counts across fields; complete-set birds do not supply every field’s denominator. The index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed, migration distance or a range-wide performance test.

## Food categories and feeding observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
This row has Trophic.Niche=Invertivore and Trophic.Level=Carnivore. Invertivore primarily uses terrestrial invertebrate resources and has a different definition from the separate Carnivore/Omnivore trophic-level field; neither replaces the other. These are compiled categories, whose thresholds are not locally measured seasonal diet percentages. Prey species, individual feeding observations, digestion and resource availability are absent here, leaving detailed prey lists and seasonal proportions unsupported.

## Lifestyle, vegetation density and migration

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Primary.Lifestyle=Terrestrial: most time is spent on the ground, with walking or hopping to obtain food; this does not imply inability to fly. Habitat=Forest and density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per square metre; these fields retain separate meanings. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility. Historical map summaries are not interpreted, wing index is not used to verify the category, and these labels do not establish current range-wide behaviour or occupancy.
