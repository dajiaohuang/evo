# Hypsipetes nicobariensis


## What one ecological field can tell us

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Hypsipetes nicobariensis is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Pycnonotidae. This page compares the same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record through one ecological field group with source information. The source follows earlier bird or MSW3 mammal taxonomy; a name match does not resolve modern splits or synonym disputes.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Foraging-stratum weights flagged as species-level in the archive are understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m 40%; trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy 40%; ground 20%. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page interprets one supported ecological field group; it does not fill the remaining diet, activity or stratum fields. This is a reading guide to historical same-name, same-family foraging records in EltonTraits 1.0, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical species concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized by the authors from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes can also refer to a congener judged ecologically similar by the compilers; this page does not claim direct observations of this species for every field. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing individual review do not fill definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread, and historical citations do not constitute external expert review. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation still require other sources.
This page withholds diet (source code D1) because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits. Specific reasons and original values remain in the source archive; they do not fill definite ecological conclusions.
