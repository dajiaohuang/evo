## Forward limb reach and male cloacal morphology
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13463510-v5n3n
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
In the named comparison, daloushanensis fingertips reached midway between the eye and nostril when the forelimbs were extended forward, and males had cloacal genital papillae. The paper contrasted these with sheni fingertips beyond the snout and absent male papillae. Reach depends on the specified limb position; it is not a limb-length measurement in millimetres or a locomotor-performance assay.
