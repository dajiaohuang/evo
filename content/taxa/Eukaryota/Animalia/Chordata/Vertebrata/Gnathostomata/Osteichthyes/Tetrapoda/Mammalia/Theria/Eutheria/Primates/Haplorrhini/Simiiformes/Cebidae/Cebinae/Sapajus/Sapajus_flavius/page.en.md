## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Lins and Ferreira studied a group of 133 blond capuchins in a 270-ha Atlantic Forest fragment in northeastern Brazil. During the study year, fruit was preferred while sugarcane served as a staple fallback food, accounting for about half of the group’s consumed food. Contrary to fallback-food model predictions, direct competition peaked while the monkeys were feeding on sugarcane. This result comes from one large group and one forest fragment, so it is not a species-wide feeding rule.
