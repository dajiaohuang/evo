# Sapajus cay

## Feeding research

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Smith and colleagues compared food records from three groups in degraded and near-pristine fragments of the Upper Paraná Atlantic Forest in eastern Paraguay. After accounting for age, sex and season, their models still indicated dietary flexibility. The monkeys opportunistically used crop plants, particularly seeds in slash-pine plantations when perceived risks were lower. This describes local feeding in three groups; it does not show that all agricultural areas are safe or that the species uses the same foods everywhere.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Source-linked regional feeding study; not externally expert-reviewed. It compares three groups in eastern Paraguay and does not establish range-wide diet or current conservation status.
