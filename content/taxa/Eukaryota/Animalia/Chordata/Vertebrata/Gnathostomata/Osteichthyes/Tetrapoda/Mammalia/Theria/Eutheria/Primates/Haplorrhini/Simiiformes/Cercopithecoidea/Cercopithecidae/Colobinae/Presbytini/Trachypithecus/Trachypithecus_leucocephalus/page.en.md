# Trachypithecus leucocephalus

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The current MDD page uses the English name White-headed Langur. Li and Rogers studied socioecology in Fusui Precious Animal Reserve, Guangxi, China, comparing habitats with different levels of human disturbance. Their study site does not represent the full species range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Ecological evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study was conducted in 1997–1998 and classified habitat quality by human disturbance. Higher-quality habitat had greater plant diversity and more important food plants; home ranges covered 28–48 ha, and area per individual declined as habitat quality increased. Small one-male, multi-female groups defended ranges weakly, while larger groups defended them more intensively; only harem males were observed defending groups. These are local associations and behaviours at Fusui, not species-wide rules.
