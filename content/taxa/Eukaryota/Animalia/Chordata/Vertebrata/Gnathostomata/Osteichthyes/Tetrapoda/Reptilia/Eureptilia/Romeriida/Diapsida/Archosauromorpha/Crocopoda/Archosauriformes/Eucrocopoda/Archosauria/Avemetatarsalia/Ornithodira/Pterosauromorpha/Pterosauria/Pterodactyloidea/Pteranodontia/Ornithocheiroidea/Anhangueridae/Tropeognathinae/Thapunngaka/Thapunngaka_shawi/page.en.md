# Thapunngaka shawi

## The holotype preserves part of a mandible

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Holotype KKF494 in the 2021 description is the front of a crested mandible, with alveoli but no in-place teeth. It is housed at Kronosaurus Korner in Richmond.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Formation assignment and exact horizon

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is on Wanamara Country northwest of Richmond, in the upper Albian Toolebuc Formation. Its exact position within the formation remains unclear.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Why a large span is not a direct measurement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2024 Haliskia description revisits Thapunngaka size estimates. Large span values are extrapolated from partial mandibular material, with outcomes sensitive to comparator choice and symphysis-to-skull proportions. Its authors argue that the evidence then available does not support the claim of Australia’s largest known pterosaur. This account retains that dated criticism without supplying unpreserved complete wings or turning a disputed estimate into typical or maximum species size. The earlier reconstruction should be read with its assumptions; the criticism does not adjudicate every subsequently reported specimen.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## What the second specimen preserves

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The later paper describes KKF600, also written KKF0600: two incomplete upper-jaw rostral pieces from Cambridge Quarry northwest of Richmond, in the upper Albian Toolebuc Formation. These are additional upper-jaw material, not a complete skull or wings of the holotype individual.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Referral evidence and the later objection

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Richards and colleagues refer KKF600 to Thapunngaka shawi using alveolar morphology and arrangement and complementary occlusal surfaces, while acknowledging no anatomical overlap. The 2024 Haliskia study questions the referral because of that absence, at least three pterosaur taxa in the Toolebuc Formation, and anhanguerian diversity elsewhere. Both arguments remain explicit; referred material is not treated as undisputed species evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Mythungini as a phylogenetic hypothesis

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The later study’s strict consensus places Thapunngaka, Mythunga and Ferrodraco in an unresolved trichotomy named Mythungini. This records that analysis without converting it into a settled immediate relationship in the storage tree.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page covers the holotype, horizon, size-estimation debate and second-specimen referral dispute. The 2021 description was read through its public author transcription without visual inspection of its measurement tables. For the later study, relevant description, comparison and analysis sections of the accepted pre-proof PDF were read, with the referral passage visually inspected. This is not a complete review of the final published version; measurement-table values are not expanded. Diet, reproduction, behavior, complete range and abundance remain insufficient. Neither a complete species monograph.
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 276-item pterosaur working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Pterosauria storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
