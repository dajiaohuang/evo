## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
From August to December 2020, researchers surveyed 64.1 km along 21 transects in Laos’s Phou Hin Poun National Protected Area. An 18-person survey team worked in the field for 37 days and recorded 35 langurs in 9 groups. Buamlou had the highest encounter rate, at 1 group per km; the other three sectors were lower. The authors said these preliminary data were insufficient to fully evaluate local population status. These are records from one protected area and survey period, not a species-wide abundance or trend estimate.
