# Mosasauridae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Bell and Polcyn's sampled Mosasauridae includes both plesiopedal Dallasaurus and more derived hydropedal members; limb grade alone is not treated as a family diagnosis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Marine carnivore; diet varied among sampled taxa
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The Dallasaurus skeletons, a 144-character phylogenetic analysis, a five-taxon isotope sample and a latest-Maastrichtian skull separately constrain morphology, limb-grade evolution, sampled ecology and the displayed occurrence envelope.
