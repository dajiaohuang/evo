## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A body-mass study of wild black-and-white snub-nosed monkeys at Baimaxueshan National Nature Reserve, Yunnan, reported a mean of 21.8±2.2 kg for eight-year-old males (n=35) and 11.8±1.2 kg for females (n=116), a sexual-dimorphism ratio of 1.85. The methods counted repeated annual records of the same monkey separately, so these sample counts are not unique individuals. The result is limited to the monitored population and measurement design.
