## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This reading path connects the six Papio species entries in the fixed COL26.8 roster: olive, yellow, hamadryas, Kinda, Guinea and chacma baboons. Compare the yellow baboon study of yearling-female diet nutrients with activity and ranging in a naturally foraging chacma group; then read the nine-year Kinda baboon social-affiliation study alongside observations of Guinea baboon parties splitting and fusing as they crossed an open area. Finish with GPS movement records for hamadryas baboons and a small Alu-insertion comparison panel that included olive baboons. The cases differ in place, sample, method and question; this page is a reading route, not a genus-wide average, ecological law or single social model.
