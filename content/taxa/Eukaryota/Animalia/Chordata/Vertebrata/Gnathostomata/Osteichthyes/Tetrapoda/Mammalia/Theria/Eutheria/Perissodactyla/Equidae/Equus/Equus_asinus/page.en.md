## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD treats the domestic donkey as a domestic derivative of the African wild ass. Its separate accepted name is retained here to distinguish domestic, feral and wild material. Feral occurrence is not restoration of an original wild population.
