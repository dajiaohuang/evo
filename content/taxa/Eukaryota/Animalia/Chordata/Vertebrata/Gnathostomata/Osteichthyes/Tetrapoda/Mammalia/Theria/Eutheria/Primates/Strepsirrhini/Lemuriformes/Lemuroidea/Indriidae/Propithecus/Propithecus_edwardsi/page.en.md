## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Ranomafana, seven groups were followed in logged and unlogged rainforest from December 2002 to November 2003. Groups in logged forest traveled 7–13% less per day despite a larger median annual home range (46.12 ha versus 23.52 ha); core-use areas also shifted seasonally. The researchers found no density difference between the two sites and called for multiyear work because food availability varies among years.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
