# Alligator


## From skin structures to local sensory recordings

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
American alligators provide one juvenile skin-sensation study for Alligator. The 2012 work links the distribution of jaw integumentary sensory organs (ISOs), trigeminal receptive fields and aquarium orienting behavior. Limb touch remained despite the absence of the same postcranial ISO spots. Rapid and slow adaptation classify recorded unit responses to maintained stimulation, not two kinds of individual. Chinese alligators were not sampled in that study, and the genus’s other life-history accounts do not thereby acquire the same neural thresholds.
