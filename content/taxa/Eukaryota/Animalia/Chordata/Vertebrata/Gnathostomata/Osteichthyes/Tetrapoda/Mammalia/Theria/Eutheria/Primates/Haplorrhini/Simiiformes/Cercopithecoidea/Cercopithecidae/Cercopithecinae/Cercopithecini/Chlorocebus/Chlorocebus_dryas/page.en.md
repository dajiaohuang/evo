## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Lomami National Park and its buffer zone, local knowledge helped direct camera-trap surveys at Bafundo Forest and Camp Bartho. Across 55 cameras placed from ground level to canopy, the study recorded 32 Dryas monkey events in the understory, one in the canopy and none at ground level. Understory detections were more frequent near fallow-field edges in Bafundo than in mature forest at Camp Bartho.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The paper treats the Dryas monkey as Chlorocebus dryas and accepts Cercopithecus salongo as its junior synonym, while noting that further specimen-based analysis is needed. This page keeps the pinned COL26.8 name and reports the authors' treatment without resolving broader generic placement.
