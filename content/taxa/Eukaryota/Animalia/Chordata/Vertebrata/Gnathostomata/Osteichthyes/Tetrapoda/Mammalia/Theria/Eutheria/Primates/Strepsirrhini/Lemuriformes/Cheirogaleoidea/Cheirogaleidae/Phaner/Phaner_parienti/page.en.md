## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A survey across six Madagascan transects covered 20 km and recorded 52 animals across three fork-marked lemur taxa; it reported an encounter rate of 2.4 *P. parienti* per km. This is a transect encounter rate, not a detection-corrected density.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Night surveys in cacao shade plantations near Ambanja from November 2016 to March 2017 also recorded the species, at 0.4 per km. The study covered several lemur taxa; this local record shows occurrence at plantation sites, not a general habitat preference.

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
