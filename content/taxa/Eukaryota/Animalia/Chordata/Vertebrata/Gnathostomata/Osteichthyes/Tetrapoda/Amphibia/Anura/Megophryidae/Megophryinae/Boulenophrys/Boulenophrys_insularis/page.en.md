## Nan’ao Island locality knowledge in the monograph
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9k7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The male holotype SYS a002169 was from Nan’ao Island, Guangdong, at 425 m. The monograph’s cited distribution account knew the species only from its island type locality at 50–500 m. These records do not demonstrate occurrence throughout that elevation interval, absence on other islands or the complete present range.

## Five males and one female in the examined type material
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9k7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported five adult males with snout–vent lengths of 36.8–41.2 mm and one female at 47.1 mm. Selected features included a weak tongue notch, distinct rounded tympanum, strong vomerine ridges with teeth, granular and tuberculate dorsal skin, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. The single female supplies one measurement rather than female size variation; these selected traits are not a complete identification key.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-lx9k7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys insularis as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
