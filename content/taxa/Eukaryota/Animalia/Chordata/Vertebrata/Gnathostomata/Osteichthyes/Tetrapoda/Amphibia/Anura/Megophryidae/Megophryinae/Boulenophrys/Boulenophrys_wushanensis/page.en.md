## Wushan and Daba mountain locality knowledge
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-mr7f
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The monograph listed male holotype CIB 98012, formerly CIB 571991, from Miaotang, now Wulipo Nature Reserve, Wushan, at 1200 m, retaining the historical Sichuan attribution and current Chongqing administration. It examined five males from Wenshui Forest Station, Shennongjia, Hubei, at about 1490 m. Its broader summary included Wushan and Daba mountain localities along Hubei, Chongqing, Sichuan, Shaanxi and Gansu borders at 900–1500 m; this is a locality summary, not a census or continuous-range demonstration.

## Toe-fringe differences require more females
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-mr7f
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
Earlier descriptions reported rudimentary basal webbing and relatively wide toe fringes in males but neither character in females. The monograph noted that only one female, the allotype, supported the female comparison and treated that observation as variation rather than a diagnosis, requesting further female specimens. Its male diagnosis retained brown nuptial spines on fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. The one-female comparison does not establish a general sex-specific toe difference.
<!-- /evo:text -->
