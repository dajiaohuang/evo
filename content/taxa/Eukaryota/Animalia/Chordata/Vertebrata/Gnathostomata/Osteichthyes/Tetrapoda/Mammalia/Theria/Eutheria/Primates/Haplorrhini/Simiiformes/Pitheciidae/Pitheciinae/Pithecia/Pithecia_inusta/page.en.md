## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh's 2014 revision applied Spix's illustrated name to a set of saki populations; the original mounted specimen is lost, and the publication did not identify its country or locality. Serrano-Villavicencio and Terra Garbino reread Spix's French description in 2024 and placed the type locality at Tonantins forest near Tabatinga on the Solimões. They therefore questioned applying the name to Ucayali and upper Juruá populations, suggested treating those populations as P. monachus pending evidence, and argued that P. inusta is a synonym of P. hirsuta. COL26.8 and the current MDD still list the species name as accepted; this page preserves the disagreement.
