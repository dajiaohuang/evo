# Pithecia isabela

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh described Isabel's saki as a new species in 2014, using museum material including the type specimen and pelage comparisons. The holotype is an adult male from Santa Elena on Peru's Río Samiria, Loreto, at 130 m; paratypes also came from sites in the Río Samiria region of Pacaya-Samiria. These localities and specimens document the name's original basis, not the full present-day range.
<!-- /evo:text -->
