## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
In a 2009–2010 field study, 608 fruit-foraging focal follows from 49 habituated wild Cebus capucinus in three groups at Pacuare Nature Reserve were used in the return-rate analysis. Adults had higher return rates than younger age classes for fruits ranked difficult to acquire; the paper reports no significant age-class differences for easy-to-acquire fruits. These are results from this study sample and its ordinal food-difficulty categories, not a species-wide diet estimate.
<!-- /evo:text -->
