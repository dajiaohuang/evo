# Saguinus

## Choose a reading path by research question

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID 7BP7 now links selected Saguinus pages for S. bicolor (4TZBJ), S. martinsi (4TZBW), S. midas (4TZBY), S. niger (4TZC2), and S. ursula (4TZC8). Read Gregorin and de Vivo's (2013) taxonomic comparison of ursula east and niger west of the Tocantins River, then consult the eight historical Saguinus rows in Supplementary Table S1 of Mourthé et al. (2022). The latter is a regional range-map overlay; its old row names are not mapped one by one to current accepted species and do not represent a complete genus distribution or abundance assessment.
