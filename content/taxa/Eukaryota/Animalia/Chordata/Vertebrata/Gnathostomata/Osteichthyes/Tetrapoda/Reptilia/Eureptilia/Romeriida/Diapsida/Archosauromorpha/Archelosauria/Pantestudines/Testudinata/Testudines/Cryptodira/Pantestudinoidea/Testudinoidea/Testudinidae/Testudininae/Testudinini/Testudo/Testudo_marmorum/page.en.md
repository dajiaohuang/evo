# Testudo marmorum


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Testudo marmorum is accepted in the fixed PBDB pantestudines working roster, with database parent Testudo. This guide uses 6 eligible species-level occurrence records from 6 collections and 5 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.
<!-- /evo:text -->

This is not a complete species distribution list, and presence in one region does not prove absence elsewhere.

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 15.98–5.09 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 1150-item pantestudines working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Pantestudines storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
