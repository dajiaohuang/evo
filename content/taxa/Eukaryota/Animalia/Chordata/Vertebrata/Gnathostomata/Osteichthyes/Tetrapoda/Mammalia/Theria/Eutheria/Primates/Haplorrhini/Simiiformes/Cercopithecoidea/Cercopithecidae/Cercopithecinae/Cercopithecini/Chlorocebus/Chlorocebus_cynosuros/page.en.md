## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the Liuwa–Mussuma landscape, an institutional scoping report records Malbrouck monkeys in camera traps at Mussuma, Angola (121 events; relative encounter index 1.54) and the Upper West Zambezi Game Management Area, Zambia (9 events; index 0.15). These are results for the surveyed camera-trap locations, not direct counts of monkeys.

## evidence-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The report combines camera trapping, interviews and spatial analysis across a proposed transboundary conservation landscape. The two event totals cannot be read as a census or a species-wide abundance comparison, and the report is not a peer-reviewed population estimate.
