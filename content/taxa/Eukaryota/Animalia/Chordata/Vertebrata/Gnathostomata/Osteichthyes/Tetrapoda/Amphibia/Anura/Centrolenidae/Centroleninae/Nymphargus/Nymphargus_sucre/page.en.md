## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13061190-6sd2l

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Sucre is compared as having translucent esophageal/stomach peritonea and a brownish-yellow dorsum with yellow dots. Those differ from the white peritonea and uniformly green dorsum of dajomesae. The phylogenetic illustration also names a sucre voucher QCAZ-A68099; its caption is not a newly inspected specimen or independent color photograph analysis.
