## field ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Brief visits to Sangihe in December 1995–January 1996 and July 1997 documented six captured animals from six sleeping sites. The author also reported tarsiers in secondary forest, mixed tree gardens, coconut groves, mangrove and villages; the team did not randomize its search and did not estimate species-specific population density.

## evidence_scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2009 conservation paper's potential population figures use GIS habitat classes and density values borrowed from related tarsiers, not direct counts of T. sangirensis. The 2013 observations are brief and non-random; neither source is a current census.
