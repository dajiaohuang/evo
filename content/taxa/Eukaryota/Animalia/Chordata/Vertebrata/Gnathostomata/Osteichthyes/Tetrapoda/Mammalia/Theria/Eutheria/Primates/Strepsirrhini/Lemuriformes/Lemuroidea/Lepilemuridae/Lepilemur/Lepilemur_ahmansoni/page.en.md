## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2006 description characterized Ahmanson's sportive lemur as a small form (about 0.61 kg), mainly dark gray with diffuse reddish-brown on the limbs and a reddish-brown tail. The authors contrasted it with L. aeeclis, including the latter's more prominent dorsal stripe. These traits describe the sampled animals, not the full range of variation.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Tsiombikibo Classified Forest, west of the Mahavavy River in northwestern Madagascar. The paper knew the species only from this region and left its southern limit unresolved. Although geographically near L. aeeclis and L. edwardsi, the authors placed it closer to the northwestern L. sahamalazensis and L. dorsalis in their analyses. Field surveys were still needed to define its range.
