# Crocodylidae


## From skin structures to local sensory recordings

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Two Nile-crocodile studies provide the named sensory entry for Crocodylidae. Integumentary sensory organs (ISOs) occur on jaws and body scales. Dense organs near teeth, neural receptive fields, local thermal/pH responses and incubated embryonic timing address different questions. Comparisons with alligatorid material clarify anatomical contrasts without supplying equivalent functional measurements for Mecistops, Osteolaemus or every Crocodylus species. Electrical recordings from living juveniles do not reconstruct skin sensation in fossil branches.
<!-- /evo:text -->
