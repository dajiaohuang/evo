## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Meier and colleagues described Hapalemur aureus in 1987. MDD lists the type locality in primary rainforest near the village of Ranomafana in southeastern Madagascar. This anchors the name and type material; it does not define the species’ full range.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A comparative study at Ranomafana tested 102 food, urine and fecal samples from three sympatric bamboo-lemur species during two pre-dry seasons in 2006 and 2007. It detected cyanide in bamboo foods and urine from H. aureus. The study also found that the lemurs consumed different bamboo species and plant parts; H. griseus had lower but still detectable results. The authors noted that how the animals avoid poisoning remained unknown.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The chemical tests cover limited samples and two periods at Ranomafana; they do not establish a physiological mechanism, every population’s diet or the current range. The Chinese common name is editorial. These pages are not externally expert-reviewed.
