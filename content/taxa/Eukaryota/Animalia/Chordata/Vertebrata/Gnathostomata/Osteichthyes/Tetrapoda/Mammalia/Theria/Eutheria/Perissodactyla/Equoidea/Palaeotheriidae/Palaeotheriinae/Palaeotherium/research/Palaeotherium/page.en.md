# Palaeotherium

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A western European palaeothere represented here by dental-wear evidence from Palaeotherium medium and regional terminal records near the Grande Coupure.
<!-- /evo:text -->
