## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The paper notes an earlier record of Spix's howler occurring with the paper's Alouatta puruensis in the Paranaíta area and discusses a possible contact zone near the middle Juruena River. The authors propose a potential hybrid zone but do not confirm gene flow. COL26.8 currently has no accepted species usage for A. puruensis; this page preserves the paper's name rather than mapping it to another accepted species.
