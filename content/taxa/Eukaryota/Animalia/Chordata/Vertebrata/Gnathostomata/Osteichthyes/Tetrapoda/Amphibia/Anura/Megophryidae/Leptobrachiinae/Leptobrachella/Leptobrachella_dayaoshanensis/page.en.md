## Calling observations in March and April
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Males called beside rocky streams between 19:00 and midnight during the survey, with some calls also heard by day. Calls were audible from early March through late April. Calling months and times remain observation records, rather than proof of a complete breeding season, egg-laying period or larval development schedule.
