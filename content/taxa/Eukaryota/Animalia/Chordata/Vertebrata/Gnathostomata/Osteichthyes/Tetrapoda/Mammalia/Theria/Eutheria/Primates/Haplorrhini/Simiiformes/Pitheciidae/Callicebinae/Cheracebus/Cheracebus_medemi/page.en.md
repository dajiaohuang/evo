## taxonomyAndTypeLocality

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Cheracebus medemi (TXQM) as a species. Hershkovitz originally described the taxon as Callicebus torquatus medemi. MDD records the holotype as FMNH Mamm 70699, from near the mouth of the Río Mecaya on the right bank of the Río Caquetá, Putumayo, Colombia, at about 180 m. A type locality anchors a name; it is not a complete modern range map.

## regionalFieldEvidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Palacios and Peres (2005) surveyed three lowland terra firme forests in southeastern Colombia and reported densities under the broader name Callicebus torquatus: 6.8 individuals/km² at Ayo, 4.8 at Caño Pintadillo, and 9.6 at Caparú. The transects were run in 2001–2002 in the lower Caquetá and Apaporis basins. This study predates the current species-level treatment; the figures are regional evidence for the historically broader complex and cannot be assigned to C. medemi as standalone population estimates without specimen-based re-evaluation.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Available material includes the original type record, MDD summary fields and one regional transect study published under the older name Callicebus torquatus. The older data have not been re-evaluated to separate them across the current C. medemi species boundary, and no current species-level abundance trend is established here.
