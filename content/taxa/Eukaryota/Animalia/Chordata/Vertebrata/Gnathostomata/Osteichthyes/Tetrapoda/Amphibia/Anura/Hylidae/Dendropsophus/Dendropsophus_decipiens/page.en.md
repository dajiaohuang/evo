## Ten labelled lineages are not ten certified species
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/11/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8279364-34s53
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/11/text/en -->
The study added two structured lineages labelled decipiens to eight previously reported ones, calling them IX from Santos Dumont and X from Cananéia. Reported 16S distances across decipiens-labelled lineages ranged from about 2% to 11%, with 6% between two sympatric Aurelino Leal lineages. Haddadi remained embedded in the broader decipiens complex. These results identify unresolved diversity among source-labelled samples; they do not independently describe ten accepted species, demonstrate reproductive barriers or place all sequence labels in one certified contemporary concept. The authors emphasized further morphology and acoustic review, especially because published decipiens calls were not from the type locality.
