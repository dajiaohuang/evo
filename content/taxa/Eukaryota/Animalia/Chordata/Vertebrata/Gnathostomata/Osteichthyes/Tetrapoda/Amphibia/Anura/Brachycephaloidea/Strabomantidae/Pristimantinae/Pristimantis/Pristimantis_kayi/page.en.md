## Several Ecuadorian mountain sites

primary-pmc10838188-scf76

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->

## Low vegetation in primary and disturbed forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10838188-scf76
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Frogs were found at night on the forest floor or vegetation below 60 cm, in both primary and disturbed Andean forest. Males were heard by day and night. These observations document habitat use and calling; they do not experimentally establish disturbance tolerance or identify egg and larval sites.
