## population estimate

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The survey covered 18 transects and 192 km, recording 285 detections across four primates, including 79 Siberut macaque detections. The authors estimated about 5.55 individuals/km² and 9,250 in the park; transects overrepresented suitable habitat, possibly slightly inflating the park total, and the study could not establish a time trend.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a park estimate based on 2011 transects, not a current island-wide total; the concentration of transects in suitable habitat may cause slight overestimation. The Chinese label is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
