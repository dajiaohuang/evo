## The highest-ranked desert species and a historical synonymy
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
Fronteriensis and philippii share the highest printed index, 2.50; halli and chusmisensis are also high priority. Reserve expansion, water protection and mining-habitat restoration are proposed actions rather than completed outcomes. Halli rescue records are discussed under a cited synonymy of dankoi and vilamensis, not independently certified current inventories. Culeus appears as a Peru/Bolivia comparison and has no Chilean row or index. Table 4 assigns pefauri and peruvianus an inconsistent family label; this does not replace the pinned classification.
