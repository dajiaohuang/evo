## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Silent Valley rainforest, direct feeding observations from 1993 to 1996 identified 89 plant species eaten by Nilgiri langurs. Most were characteristic evergreen-forest plants, and the frequency of feeding observations on different foods varied by month and season.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The feeding account is based on observations in one rainforest park during 1993–1996; it is not a current range-wide dietary survey. The older paper's generic combination is retained as published. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
