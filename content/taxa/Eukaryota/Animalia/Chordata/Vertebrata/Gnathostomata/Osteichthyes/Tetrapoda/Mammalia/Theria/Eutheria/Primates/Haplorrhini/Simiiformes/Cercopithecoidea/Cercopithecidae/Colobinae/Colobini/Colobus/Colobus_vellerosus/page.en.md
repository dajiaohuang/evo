## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2019 ground survey at Boabeng-Fiema Monkey Sanctuary, Ghana, recorded 34 groups and 602 white-thighed colobus across six forest patches. The survey used 17 transects and covered 71.05 km on foot; this is a site-level count, not a global population estimate.

## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The same article reports a 70% increase in estimated density at Boabeng-Fiema between 2007 and 2019 (5.8% per year), while noting that survey personnel and methods differed. The comparison is not a methodologically uniform or range-wide population trend.
