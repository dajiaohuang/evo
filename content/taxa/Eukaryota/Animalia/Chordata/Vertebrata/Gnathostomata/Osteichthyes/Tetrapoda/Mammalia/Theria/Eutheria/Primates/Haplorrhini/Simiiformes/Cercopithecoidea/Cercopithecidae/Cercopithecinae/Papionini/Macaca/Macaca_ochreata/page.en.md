## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Riley et al. ran transect surveys at Matano and Kasintuwu in Faruhumpenai Nature Reserve during April–July 2006. They reported booted macaque group densities of 0.97–1.56 groups/km² at Matano and 1.45–2.00 groups/km² at Kasintuwu. They also documented the species in and around the reserve and concluded that its range extended farther north and northwest than previously recognized.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The transects, densities and distribution finding are limited to two reserve sites in 2006; groups/km² is not individuals/km² or an island-wide population estimate. The English common-name label follows MDD; the Chinese label is an editorial translation. This guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
