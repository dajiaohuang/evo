# Teleoceras

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A North American rhinoceros with locality-dependent isotope evidence: principally terrestrial Florida populations contrast with limited-mobility, wet-habitat T. major at Ashfall.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Grazing or mixed feeding depending on locality
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Open Florida settings and the Ashfall watering-hole landscape
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion
