## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Passamani and Rylands followed one Geoffroy’s tufted-ear marmoset group in a southeastern Brazilian Atlantic Forest fragment from February 1993 to January 1994. Gums made up 68.6% of feeding records, fruits 15%, invertebrates 14.6% and vertebrates 0.8%. Fruit appeared more often in the diet during the wet season, while gums and insects were eaten year-round. The authors described the group as using resources available in secondary and disturbed forest. One group, fragment and year do not establish diet proportions for every habitat or the full species range.
