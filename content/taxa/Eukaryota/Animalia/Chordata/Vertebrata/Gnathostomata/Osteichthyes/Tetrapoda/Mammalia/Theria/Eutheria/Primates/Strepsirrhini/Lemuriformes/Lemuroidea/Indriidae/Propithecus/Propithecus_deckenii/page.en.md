## behaviour

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2017–2018 focal-sampling study monitored eight groups across Tsimembo-Manambolomaty and Mandrozo, with groups in intact and disturbed habitat at both protected areas. The paper reports that scent-marking frequency differed by habitat and sex, while estimated home range, core area and daily path length varied among groups. These are local results from a small set of monitored groups, not a species-wide test of habitat tolerance.
