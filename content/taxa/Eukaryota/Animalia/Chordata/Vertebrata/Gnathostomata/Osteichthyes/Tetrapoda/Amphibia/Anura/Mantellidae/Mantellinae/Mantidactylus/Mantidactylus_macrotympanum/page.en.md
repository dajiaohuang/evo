## Only males provide measured adult size in this description
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-qmctd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Six males were measured at 57.9–62.1 mm snout–vent length; the diagnosis rounds their range to 57–62 mm. Females were unknown, and the proposed female size above 62 mm is speculation rather than a measured range. The diagnostic combination includes dark brown colour, a granular posterior body, no inguinal streak, a large tympanum at least 7.6 mm, tibiotarsal articulation beyond the snout and a reported sum of free toe phalanges of 3.75. Belly spots and flank white markings vary among the sampled males; no fixed female appearance is established.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Male encounters span quiet streams and rocks in fast rivers
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-qmctd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The source reports males in rainforest around quiet streams and rocks in fast rivers, with a plantation record at Manongarivo. Encounters span 12:00–20:00 and are described as more nocturnal. At night frogs used branches 10–200 cm high, whereas daytime encounters were on rocks. All observed individuals in this account were males. One plantation record does not establish disturbance tolerance, successful breeding in plantations or female habitat use; encounter hours and substrates do not constitute a controlled preference study.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Named northern localities carry different elevation information
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-qmctd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The distribution account lists relict forest at Ambalafary, Manongarivo Reserve at 250 m, and the Ramena area of Tsaratanana at 180–800 m. The 250 m record and the Ramena interval retain their locality associations; no elevation is invented for Ambalafary. These scattered records do not establish continuous occupancy between forest remnants or a complete northern Madagascan range. Local habitat descriptions should not be interpreted as measured population abundance or a demonstrated dependence on every listed forest type.

## Three genetic vouchers do not resolve broader subgenus membership
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-qmctd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
Genetic references comprise AMNH A167589 / RAX 2715, UMMZ 201416 / RAN 39170 and UMMZ 213447 / RAN 39125. The discussion places majori as the molecular sister of macrotympanum with posterior support 0.91, while excluding majori from Ochthomantis on morphological grounds and recommending broader sampling. That unresolved tension is retained. A supported sister relationship does not by itself settle subgenus limits or justify moving catalogue taxa. The three listed samples are not evidence of range-wide multilocus population coverage.
