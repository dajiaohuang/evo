## Accepted name and placement

COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys congjiangensis (Luo, Wang, Wang, Lu, Wang, Deng & Zhou, 2021) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Two locality elevations that differ from the summary
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5dg7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The monograph listed the male holotype from Yueliangshan, Congjiang, Guizhou, at about 730 m and examined three males and one female from Jiuwan Mountain, Guangxi, at about 1280 m. Its separate distribution summary gave 1100–1300 m for Congjiang and Jiuwan. The 730 m type locality falls below that summary; both statements remain explicit, without creating a silently corrected elevation range or treating either as physiological limits.

## Examined measurements and attributed type-series corrections
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5dg7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The newly examined males measured 31.7–33.9 mm snout–vent length (three specimens); the single female measured 39.0 mm. The monograph separately reported type-series measurements and corrected the original designation to 14 male paratypes, explaining that the printed count of 15 conflicted with the listed vouchers. It also corrected three female voucher numbers to GZNU 20200707004–006. These corrections are the monograph’s documented interpretation, while the new measured sample remains distinct from the historical type series.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v5dg7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys congjiangensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
