## Contrasts cited in the group redefinition
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9985417-4mjjj
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
Discussion contrasts the formerly added verecundus/jubatus branches with the narrower myersi group by more expanded digital discs and a discoidal fold. It additionally states that celator andjubatus lack vocal slits. These are source-level comparative statements based partly on earlier descriptions, not newly checked measurements of every Pristimantis mutabilis specimen. Lack of vocal slits is not automatically lack of calling, and a cited arboreal-versus-ground-use contrast is not an experimental test of climbing performance or a species-wide habitat survey.
