## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In a master’s study, Fernandes followed one group living in an urban forest fragment at the Mello Leitão Biology Museum park in Santa Teresa, Espírito Santo. Observations covered September and November 2011 and February–July 2012. The group spent about half its time resting, 20% moving, 17% feeding and 13% in social activity. Fruit made up about 70% of its diet, from at least eight plant families.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Instantaneous scans at five-minute intervals mapped one group’s space use across 46 sampling days, covering about 3.94 ha of the urban forest fragment. The observed food trees, schedule and activity budget are local data, not species-wide averages.
