## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2019 survey documented 22 occurrence points for the Benin subspecies, C. e. erythrogaster, across four previously prospected southern forests: Lama, Lokoli, Gnanhouizounmè and Togbota. A MaxEnt model projected suitable habitat across a 1,997 km² study landscape. Under the study's threshold, 3.62% of the landscape was classed as suitable; 57.46% of that predicted area fell within protected areas, mainly the central Lama Forest Reserve. These are model outputs, not confirmed current presence or a complete range. The authors noted that occurrence data were sparse and the model extended beyond surveyed forests.
