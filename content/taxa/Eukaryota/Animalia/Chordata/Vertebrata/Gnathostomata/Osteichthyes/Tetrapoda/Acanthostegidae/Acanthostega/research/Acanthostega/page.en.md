# Acanthostega

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Devonian tetrapod represented by East Greenland limb material that documents an eight-digit forelimb and preserves the distinction between digit-bearing limbs and evidence for terrestrial walking.
