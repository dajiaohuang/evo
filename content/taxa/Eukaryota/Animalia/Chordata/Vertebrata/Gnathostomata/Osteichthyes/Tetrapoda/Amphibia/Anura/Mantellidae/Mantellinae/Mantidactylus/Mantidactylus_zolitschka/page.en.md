## The male reference specimen is smaller than the printed diagnosis range
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-3xwfz
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
The diagnosis gives male snout–vent length of 29.6–30.6 mm and female length of 37.6–37.7 mm, yet the next paragraph describes adult male UADBA 6966 at 27.65 mm. Both statements are retained; the reported diagnostic interval is not a complete adult male limit. Tibiotarsal reach is at least to the nostril, terminal disc width/base width is 1.78, and reduced webbing has a sum of free phalanges above nine, 9.75 in the reference specimen. Absence of a snout-to-groin lateral stripe distinguishes it from ambony in the comparison, without resolving the body-size discrepancy.

## Preserved colour and glands describe one reference male
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10563080-3xwfz
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
UADBA 6966 is described as slender, with a relatively smooth upper surface, slightly granular flanks and smooth ventral surface. In preservative its grey-brown dorsum has irregular light and dark marbling, the tympanic region is dark brown and the lower lip alternates light and dark spots. Two longitudinal throat markings merge into a Y-like pattern. Distinct femoral glands contact the cloacal area and show granular, crater-like patterns. These are reference-male and preservation observations, not a measured live-colour series, universal female pattern or histological test of gland function.
