# Cheirogaleus andysabini

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Dwarf lemurs around Montagne d'Ambre were sampled during a 2005 expedition, and the team obtained additional specimens in February 2015. Combining genetic and morphological evidence, the authors described the northern Madagascar material as an independent lineage, Montagne d'Ambre dwarf lemur. This supports the identification of studied samples, not a survey of every northern forest.
