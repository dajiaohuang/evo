# Lepilemur betsileo

## Morphology and field descriptions

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The paper described Betsileo's sportive lemur at about 1.11 kg, with mixed gray and reddish-brown fur that is darker dorsally; the front of the jaw is white and the tail is black, contrasting with the body. These are identification cues in that revision based on limited specimens, not a claim that every animal has identical coloration.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Known localities and evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors placed the known records around Fandriana, between the Mangoro and Namorona rivers, while stating that the northern and southern limits were unknown. The locality records did not define a complete range, and the paper called for further surveys. This historical summary is not presented as a current distribution map.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is based on the 2006 description, limited specimens, and mitochondrial fragments; the source did not establish population boundaries or present-day status. The COL26.8 accepted name is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial, and the guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
