## A seven-specimen description from Mt. Ailao
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10351143-v5bsz
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Tang and colleagues described Amolops ailao in2023 using seven specimens from Mt.Ailao,Xinping,Yunnan. Holotype GXNUYU000004 is an adult male collected12May2019 at23°56′58.20″N,101°29′51.66″E and2043m. Six paratypes include five males andone female KIZ2022041 collected22June2022. The paper proposes Ailao cascade frog and哀牢湍蛙 as common names. These reported type records anchor the source description without independently authenticating the museum specimens or every historical catalogue concept.

## Glandular folds are distinct from true dorsolateral folds
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10351143-v5bsz
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The diagnosis andholotype distinguish absence of true dorsolateral folds from the presence of conspicuous folds made of interrupted gland series. Selected characters include absent vomerine teeth,distinct tympanum underhalf eye diameter,absent first-finger-disc circummarginal groove,smooth dorsum without white spines,paired large cloacal-side tubercles andabsence of a vocal sac. These anatomical observations do not imply inability to call or a completed acoustic survey. They are selected diagnosis features rather than a complete identification key.

Nuptial pads in allmale types andeggs in thefemale motivate a possibleMay–July breeding window; this is an inference from limited observations,not a fullannual breeding survey. ## Seven type specimens
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/sourceIds/0 -->
tang-2023-ailao
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/text/en -->
The 2023 description examined six adult males and one adult female from the type locality on Mt. Ailao, Yunnan. Male snout–vent length was 33.0–35.1 mm; the single female measured 41.3 mm. The diagnosis includes absent vomerine teeth and dorsolateral lines formed by glands rather than true folds. These measurements describe this small type series, not population-wide size limits.
