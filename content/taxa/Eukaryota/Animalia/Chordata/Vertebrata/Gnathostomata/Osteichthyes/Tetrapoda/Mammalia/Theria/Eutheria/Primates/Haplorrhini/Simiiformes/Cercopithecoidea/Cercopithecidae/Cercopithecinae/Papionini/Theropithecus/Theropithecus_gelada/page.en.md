## feedingEcology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At Guassa, Ethiopia, Fashing et al. followed feeding ecology for seven years. The reported annual diet was 56.8% graminoid parts, 37.8% forb parts, 2.8% invertebrates and 2.6% other items; the team recorded at least 56 plant species and 20 invertebrate species and noted monthly variation. These observations describe the Guassa study system, not a species-wide dietary census.

## populationSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2007 direct census reported 4,264 geladas in and around Simien Mountains National Park and estimated about 2,460 within the park boundary then in use. A 2023 habitat-based survey combined direct counts with village interviews and recorded 223 geladas across three selected Tigray mountain clusters. The years, sites and methods differ; neither value is a current national or global total, and they cannot be added.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## humanModifiedLandscape

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
A separate August 2008–March 2009 study in the unprotected Wonchit Valley used a total count and sampled the behavior of one band; it reported 1,525 geladas in its study area and documented crop-related conflict through interviews. Its count is local and dated, while the behavioral sample does not represent every group or habitat.
