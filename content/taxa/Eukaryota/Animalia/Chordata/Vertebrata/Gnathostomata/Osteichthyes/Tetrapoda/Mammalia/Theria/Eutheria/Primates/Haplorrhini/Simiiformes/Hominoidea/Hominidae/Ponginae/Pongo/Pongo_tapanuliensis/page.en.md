## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A historical synthesis estimated orangutan extents of occurrence at about 40,796 km² in the 1890s and 21,313 km² in the 1940s, compared with an approximately 1,023 km² current range for the Tapanuli orangutan. Attribution of historical records to P. tapanuliensis depends on the authors' interpretation of populations south and southeast of its present range. Northern records may instead be P. abelii, and cited specimens from outside the current range had not been genetically identified. The proportions are therefore conditional historical estimates, not a verified complete range history.
