## Overlapping male size ranges and diagnostic combinations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13463510-7dblv
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The comparison reported liuyangensis SVL of 64.2–82.0 mm in males and 80.8–88.0 mm in females, versus sheni male SVL 60.9–70.1 mm. The male ranges overlap, so the paper’s larger-body description is not an identification threshold for every specimen. It also attributed indistinct middorsal nodules, adpressed limbs not meeting, forward fingertips reaching eye level, finger order II > III > I > IV and a high dorsal tail fin fold. These characters form the cited combination; no new universal size limit, sex-specific sample count or measured functional performance is inferred.

Table 1 records external-head/osteological counts for Tylototriton liuyangensis as 1/0, literature image. External heads include soft tissues; skulls use bony landmarks. These dataset counts are not additive unique animals, and external images are not all micro-CT scans. The study mainly used males, except the female E. maxiquadratus holotype photograph. Lateral and dorsal configurations, individual and species-mean analyses must remain distinct. Allometry was removed from shape analyses; centroid size measures landmark configurations, not snout–vent length, mass or a growth curve. This sample does not characterize the full species-wide geographic, age or sexual variation.
