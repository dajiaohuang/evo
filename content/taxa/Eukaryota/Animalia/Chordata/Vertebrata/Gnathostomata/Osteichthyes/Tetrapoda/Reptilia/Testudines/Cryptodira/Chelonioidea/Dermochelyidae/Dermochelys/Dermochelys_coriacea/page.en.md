# Dermochelys coriacea

## The accepted record and the research question

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/topic -->
overview
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Dermochelys coriacea (Vandelli, 1761), COL 34XB9, is the only strict accepted species under Dermochelys (COL 62PMJ) in pinned COL26.8. This reader page uses a morphological study to explain shell-character observations and their coding. The classification list establishes identity; it does not supply an extant flag or equate this record with a fossil concept of the same name.

## Scutes and the shape of a comparison

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/topic -->
morphology
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Joyce (2007) reports absent carapacial and plastral scutes in D. coriacea (characters 60 and 89). This concerns the shell's keratinous covering, not absence of the entire shell.

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
For taxa without plastral scutes, Joyce treats further plastral-scute characters as inapplicable. Appendix 3 distinguishes unknown entries (?) from inapplicable entries (–). Keeping them separate avoids turning an unscorable question into an observed character state.
