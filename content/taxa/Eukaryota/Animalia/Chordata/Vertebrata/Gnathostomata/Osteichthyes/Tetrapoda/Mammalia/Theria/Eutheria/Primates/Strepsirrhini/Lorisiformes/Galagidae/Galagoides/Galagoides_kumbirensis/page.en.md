# Galagoides kumbirensis

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID 3F2DY. The local exact MDD name match supplies the display label Angolan Dwarf Galago; the Chinese name is editorial. The species was described in 2017 from material in western Angola.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Research observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original description reports field surveys in three forest habitats along western Angola's escarpment: Kumbira Forest, the Bimbe area and Northern Scarp Forest. It began with recordings of an unidentified galago and compared field calls with museum material from around the world. These sites frame the discovery and description; they are not a complete mapped range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The authors used call structure and craniodental comparisons in the species diagnosis, including twelve linear skull-and-tooth measurements, and described pelage, facial markings and larger body size. Its loud crescendo call differs from those of close relatives. The paper discusses call function in comparative context but does not establish this species' specific social behavior in the wild.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Interpreting the evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The original description shows how calls, specimen comparison and field surveys can reveal cryptic diversity among dwarf galagos. Its evidence centers on discovery in western Angola and comparative specimens, and does not estimate population size, long-term activity patterns or current conservation status.
<!-- /evo:text -->
