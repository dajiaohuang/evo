## Rock shelters in shrubland and páramo records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6687670-scc7n
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The revision reported Azuay, Loja and Morona Santiago localities at 2460–3525 m in inter-Andean shrubland and páramo. Most specimens were beneath rocks in páramo or pasture near roads, during day or night. These sheltered encounters retain the sampled landscape, without showing that roads are necessary habitat or that every population occupies pasture.

## Two April pairs and one October caller
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6687670-scc7n
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Two amplectant pairs were found beneath rocks at night in April. A calling male was found in low vegetation at 19:00 on 16 October 2013. These separate observations document mating behavior and calling, without locating deposited eggs or establishing uninterrupted reproduction between April and October.
