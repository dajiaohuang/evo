## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A four-month transect study in Kayan Mentarang National Park compared four forest types. Recorded groups were generally single-male units of 7–8 animals, with group densities of 0.8–2.3 groups per km²; the authors also reported associations with vegetation features such as tree height. These figures describe the surveyed sites and period, not current species-wide density.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the same study, biomass ranged from 92 kg per km² in primary hill forest inside the reserve to 38 kg in old secondary forest and 31 kg in young secondary forest near villages. The author discussed disturbance and hunting as explanatory factors, but noted that studies reported inconsistent density and biomass responses. These values are a local comparison, not a universal pattern.
