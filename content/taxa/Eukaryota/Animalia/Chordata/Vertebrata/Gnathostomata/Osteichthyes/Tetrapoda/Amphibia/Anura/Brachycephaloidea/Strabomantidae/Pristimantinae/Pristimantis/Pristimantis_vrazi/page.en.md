## Two buffer-zone records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11621819-tzq3s
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The account knew the species from two sites in the northwestern buffer zone of Pui Pui Protected Forest, Peru, at 1550–1730 m. The type locality lies in the Río Huatziroqui valley. These records do not establish continuous occupancy across the buffer zone or a complete range.

## Low vegetation in a disturbed forest mosaic
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11621819-tzq3s
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Type specimens were collected in disturbed forest mixed with small coffee plots, houses and paths. All were found at night on vegetation no higher than 80 cm; the holotype was observed at 20:55. These collection records document perch and activity observations without establishing an exclusive habitat or the effects of coffee cultivation.
