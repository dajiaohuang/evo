## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists seven accepted species in Colobus: C. angolensis, C. caudatus, C. congoensis, C. guereza, C. polykomos, C. satanas and C. vellerosus. English common names for C. polykomos and C. satanas follow their locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 records; the other species retain their pinned COL identities.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with the description of Likweli from the Lomami–Lualaba interfluve, then read it alongside the single-group Cameroon feeding study of C. satanas. Although the species are compared, their sampling designs and geographic questions differ. Next read the historical forest-density estimate for black colobus at Lopé; then the rapid surveys of the Mount Kilimanjaro guereza at two Kenyan forests, noting the source’s subspecies rank. Finish with the Tiwai comparison of high forest and regrowth for C. polykomos, then connect to the existing C. angolensis, C. guereza and C. vellerosus pages.

## classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The path keeps a new-species description, single-group ecology, a historical reserve estimate, rapid distribution records and local habitat selection separate. These studies do not form a systematic review of the genus. Their taxonomic ranks, dates, sites and methods differ and cannot be combined into one range, population or ecological conclusion.
