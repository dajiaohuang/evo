## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2009 North Pacific status assessment reported that more than 99% of Laysan albatrosses nested in the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands at the time. It described foraging across the North Pacific and breeding on oceanic islands from Mexico to Japan. The proportion and range summary reflect assessment evidence through 2005, not a current global atlas.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The assessment described Laysan albatrosses as open-ocean birds that come ashore to breed and identified commercial-fishery bycatch as an important human-caused source of post-fledging mortality during its review period. This is a historical threat summary, not a current mortality rate.
