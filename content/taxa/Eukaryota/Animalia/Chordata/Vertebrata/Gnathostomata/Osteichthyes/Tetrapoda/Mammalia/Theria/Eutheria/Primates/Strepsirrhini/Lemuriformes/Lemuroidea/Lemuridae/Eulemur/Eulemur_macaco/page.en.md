## behaviour

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 15-month socioecological study at Ambato Massif followed the population then named Eulemur macaco macaco. The paper described year-round cathemeral activity and reported more nocturnal group movement and vocalizations around waxing and full moons. Because the nighttime data were inferred indirectly, the author treated them as preliminary.
