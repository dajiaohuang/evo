## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
During a 12-month study at Tsinjoarivo, two groups in continuous forest showed greater dietary diversity and ate more fleshy fruit than two groups in fragments. In the dry season, continuous-forest groups relied more on mistletoe; fragment groups used mistletoe throughout the year, where several fruiting trees found in continuous forest were absent. The contrast links diet to these four groups and this landscape, rather than proving one universal response to fragmentation.
