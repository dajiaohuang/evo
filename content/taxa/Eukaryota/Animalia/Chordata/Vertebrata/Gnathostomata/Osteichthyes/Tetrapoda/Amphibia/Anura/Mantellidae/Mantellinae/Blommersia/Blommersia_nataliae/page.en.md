## Two surveyed Mayotte mountains
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7686220-m6296
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description recorded frogs in forest and bamboo on Mont M’Sapere at 235–409 m and on Mont Bénara at a 317 m site in Mayotte. These are dated survey records from 2012, 2014 and 2019; unsuccessful searches elsewhere do not prove complete absence from every other locality.

## Eggs above water in cut bamboo
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7686220-m6296
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Three clutches counted at Mont M’Sapere in November 2012 contained 42, 43 and 22 eggs, placed on inner bamboo walls above water. Only some appeared fertilized. Eggs and tadpoles were observed during wet periods in November–December 2012 and April 2014. Suggested provisioning of unfertilized eggs to tadpoles remained a hypothesis needing further work. Failure to hear calls during the observations does not establish that the species never calls.
