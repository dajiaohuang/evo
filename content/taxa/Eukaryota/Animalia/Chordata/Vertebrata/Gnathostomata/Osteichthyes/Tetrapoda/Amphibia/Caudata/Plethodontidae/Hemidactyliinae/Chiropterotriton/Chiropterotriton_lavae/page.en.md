## A historical holotype and a separate nineteen-animal revision sample
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-5y6m3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
The account retained historical holotype EHT-HMS 28937, now FMNH 100118, with type locality two miles west of La Joya, Veracruz. The 2020 examined series comprised ten males and nine females from La Joya. Male mean SVL was 32.4 mm, range 31.1–33.8 mm; female mean was 31.6 mm, range 27.9–34.9 mm. Mean tail/SVL was 1.19 and 1.02 respectively, and mean maxillary teeth 7.0 and 20.8. Male adpressed limbs overlapped or approached closely, averaging −0.60 costal folds; females averaged 0.6. The historical holotype is not conflated with this later sampled series, and a negative fold interval denotes overlap rather than negative limb length.

## Variable live bands and separate preserved colours
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-5y6m3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The live account described dark brown backgrounds with dorsal bands varying from yellow to reddish-, orange- or pale brown. Bands could be continuous, broken into blotches or streaks, or absent; irises were golden-brown and venters dark to pale grey, sometimes white-speckled. Preserved animals ranged from pale to dark brown, often with a paler broad dorsal band and tan-to-pale-brown venter. Variation and preservation state remain explicit; no single band colour is elevated to an invariant identification test or demonstrated adaptive function.

## Cloud-forest bromeliads near La Joya
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-5y6m3
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
The 2020 account knew the species from forested areas between Toxtlacoaya and La Joya along the Perote–Xalapa road, Veracruz, at 2000–2200 m. It reported occurrence in cloud-forest bromeliads and encounters in somewhat disturbed habitat in and around La Joya. These records identify sampled habitat and a dated narrow known range. They do not demonstrate obligate bromeliad breeding, road dependence, a full disturbance-tolerance experiment or the absence of populations outside the surveyed localities.
