# Geokichla


## One bird and twenty-three differ in coverage

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Fourteen historical guides linked to older Zoothera names are added here, without complete genus natural histories. [Geokichla oberlaenderi](Geokichla_oberlaenderi/page.en.md), [Geokichla dumasi](Geokichla_dumasi/page.en.md), [Geokichla mendeni](Geokichla_mendeni/page.en.md), [Geokichla joiceyi](Geokichla_joiceyi/page.en.md) each have one morphology bird and one complete set; [Geokichla erythronota](Geokichla_erythronota/page.en.md) has 23 birds but four complete sets and 19 unsexed birds; [Geokichla gurneyi](Geokichla_gurneyi/page.en.md) has 14 birds and nine complete sets. Totals do not supply every field’s denominator or establish equally precise mean estimates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Food and primary lifestyle vary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
All fourteen rows are Forest, but [Geokichla wardii](Geokichla_wardii/page.en.md) and [Geokichla mendeni](Geokichla_mendeni/page.en.md) have density code 2 while other selected rows have 1. [Geokichla guttata](Geokichla_guttata/page.en.md) has Omnivore niche and the other thirteen Invertivore; trophic level is Omnivore for cinerea, guttata, sibirica and wardii, and Carnivore in the other selected rows. Lifestyle is Generalist for cinerea and wardii, and Terrestrial elsewhere in this selection; these differences do not establish a uniform ground-dwelling insect diet for the genus.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Migration codes are not replaced by wing index

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected rows comprise five sedentary code-1, five partially migratory code-2 and four migratory code-3 records. Read code definitions before comparing hand-wing index 18.1 in [Geokichla joiceyi](Geokichla_joiceyi/page.en.md), 21.3 in [Geokichla dumasi](Geokichla_dumasi/page.en.md) and 35.9 in [Geokichla sibirica](Geokichla_sibirica/page.en.md); the first two have single-bird samples, and an index does not supply individual migration distance. Compiled mass is 49.49 g for both dumasi/joiceyi and 56.32 g for mendeni/erythronota. The original book has not been examined to explain these equalities, which do not establish identical individual distributions.
<!-- /evo:text -->
