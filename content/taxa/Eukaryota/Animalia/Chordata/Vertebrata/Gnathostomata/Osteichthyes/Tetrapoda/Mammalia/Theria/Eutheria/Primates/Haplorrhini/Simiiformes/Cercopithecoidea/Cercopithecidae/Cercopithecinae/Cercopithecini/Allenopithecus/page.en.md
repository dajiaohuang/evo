## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID S4R connects accepted species page Allenopithecus nigroviridis (BTKJ). Read Gautier’s 1985 preliminary hunter-interview and short-observation account alongside the local northwestern Congo records reported by Maisels and colleagues in 2006. The studies differ in date, location and evidence type; their combined reading path does not establish a continuous range, dispersal route or genus-wide ecological synthesis.
