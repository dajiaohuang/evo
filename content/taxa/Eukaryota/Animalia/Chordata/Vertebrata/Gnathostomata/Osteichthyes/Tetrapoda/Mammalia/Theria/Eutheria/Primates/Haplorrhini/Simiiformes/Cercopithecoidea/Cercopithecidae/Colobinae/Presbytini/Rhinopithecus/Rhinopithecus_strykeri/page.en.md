## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows the COL26.8 accepted name Rhinopithecus strykeri. The English common name comes from the locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 record; the Chinese rendering is editorial. COL26.8 gives the authorship year as 2010, while the exact-matched MDD v2.5 record gives 2011. The original description appeared online in 2010 and in a 2011 journal issue, so the page preserves the pinned checklist year and records the publication-date difference.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original species description was based on skins and skulls of four specimens obtained from local hunters. This material supports the morphological description and taxonomic naming, but it was not a census of the wild population.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
From 2019 to 2021, a 26-month survey in the Pianma border region accumulated 212 forest-search workdays, followed monkeys for 46 days and recorded 1,002 locations. A crossing census in November 2020 counted 149 animals; the authors estimated about 155–160 in the local transboundary group. This is not a species-wide count.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The survey interpreted observations in Pianma as one cross-border group rather than several stable, separate groups, while noting that nearby areas and the Myanmar side still require investigation. This does not define the full species boundary or complete a transboundary census.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The four specimens used in the 2010 description and the 2019–2021 Pianma survey address different questions. The 155–160 estimate concerns one local transboundary group; adjacent areas and groups on the Myanmar side remain incompletely surveyed. Not externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
