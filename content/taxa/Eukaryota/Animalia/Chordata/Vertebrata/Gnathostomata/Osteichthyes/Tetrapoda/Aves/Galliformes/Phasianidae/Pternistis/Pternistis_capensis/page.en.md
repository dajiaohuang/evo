# Pternistis capensis

## Historical genus names and species concepts

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 organizes this page as Pternistis capensis (4PV6L). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 2510 and eBird 2021 agree in name, with Avibase ID AVIBASE-6405CD29; BirdTree gives Francolinus capensis, with match type 1BL to 1BT. Read COL older-combination record 6JLDZ points to accepted ID 4PV6L. These names lead back to historical rows without proving all species boundaries identical across dates.

## Measured samples and inference limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 4 birds (2 female, 2 male); 4 have complete measurement sets. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 18.3 mm, tarsus 62.4 mm, unflattened wing 205.8 mm and hand-wing index 21.6. These are species-row averages; the index is defined by Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not individual bounds or flight speed.

## Ground feeding, habitat and sedentary category

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Habitat is Shrubland; density code 2 means semi-open habitats, not birds per unit area. Omnivore niche means use of multiple resources without one dominant niche. Trophic level is separately recorded as Herbivore; niche and level are different fields whose labels are not substituted for each other. Terrestrial means mainly feeding while walking or hopping on the ground; Migration=1 is sedentary. Neither supplies individual routes, flight capacity or annual diet percentages.
