## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2006 eastern-Avahi paper described the Manombo population as A. meridionalis ramanantsoavani, using a type series of eleven tissue and DNA samples from the reserve. The 2007 genus revision raised that form to species rank. This page follows the pinned COL26.8 species name; it preserves the earlier subspecies treatment as part of the taxonomic history.
