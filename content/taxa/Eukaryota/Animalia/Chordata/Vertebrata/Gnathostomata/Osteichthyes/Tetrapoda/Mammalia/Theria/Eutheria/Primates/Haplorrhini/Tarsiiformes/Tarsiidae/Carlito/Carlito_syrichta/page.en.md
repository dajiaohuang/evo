## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At the Mt. Bontoc forest fragment in Hindang, Leyte, the team opportunistically recorded five tarsiers in August 2021 and six records from direct sightings and camera traps in January–February 2022. The authors could not establish whether records represented different individuals and said population viability was unknown; these are not a total count or population estimate.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Around five tarsier sighting sites at Mt. Bontoc, researchers measured 612 woody stems in plots; mean diameter at breast height was 2.5 cm and mean height was 5.0 m. They described the site as regenerating secondary forest on limestone. This vegetation sample from one forest fragment does not represent all habitats used by Philippine tarsiers.
