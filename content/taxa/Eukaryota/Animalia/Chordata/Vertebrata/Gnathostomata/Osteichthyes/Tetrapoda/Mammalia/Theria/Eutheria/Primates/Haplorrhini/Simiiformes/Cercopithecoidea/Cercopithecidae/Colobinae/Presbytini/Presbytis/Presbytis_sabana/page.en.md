## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A long-running North Borneo taxonomic and ecological study compared calls, crania, pelage and molecular data in Hose's langur and related forms. Ramlee reported marked differences between the male loud calls of P. hosei and P. sabana and argued for species-level distinction; COL26.8 also lists P. sabana as accepted.

## limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The doctoral study spanned 18 months, but its field ecology chiefly concerned P. hosei. This page's account of P. sabana is limited to comparative calls and taxonomy; it is not a Sabah population survey or a complete species diet or abundance account.
