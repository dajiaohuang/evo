## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A single horn and pronounced skin folds are characteristic. This species uses alluvial grasslands, wetlands and adjacent woodland. Habitat use depends on water, food and disturbance; historical range is not equivalent to current occupancy.
