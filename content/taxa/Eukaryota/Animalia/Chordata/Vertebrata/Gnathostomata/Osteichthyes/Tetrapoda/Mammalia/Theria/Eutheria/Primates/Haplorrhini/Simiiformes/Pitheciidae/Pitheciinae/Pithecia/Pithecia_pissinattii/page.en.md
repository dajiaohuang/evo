## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh described this name as a new species in 2014 based on pelage; the holotype is an adult specimen at the Natural History Museum, labelled from Paraná do Jacaré, Canabouça, Amazonas. Serrano-Villavicencio and colleagues compared the type with other museum material in the group in 2019. They recognized diagnosable pelage groups only for P. irrorata and P. vanzolinii and treated P. pissinattii as a synonym of P. irrorata. COL26.8 still lists P. pissinattii as accepted, while the current MDD places it under P. irrorata; this page does not resolve the disagreement.
