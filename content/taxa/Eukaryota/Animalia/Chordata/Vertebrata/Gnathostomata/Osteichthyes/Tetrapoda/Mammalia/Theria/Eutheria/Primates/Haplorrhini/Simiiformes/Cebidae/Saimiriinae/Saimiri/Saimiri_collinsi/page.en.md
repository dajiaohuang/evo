## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Field expeditions in southern Amazonia, Brazil, recorded Collins' squirrel monkey on the left bank of the Jamanxim River and on the right banks of the upper Tapajós and lower Teles Pires rivers. From these occurrence points, the authors estimated a westward range extension of about 600 km. This updates a regional range account; it is not a complete range survey or population estimate.
