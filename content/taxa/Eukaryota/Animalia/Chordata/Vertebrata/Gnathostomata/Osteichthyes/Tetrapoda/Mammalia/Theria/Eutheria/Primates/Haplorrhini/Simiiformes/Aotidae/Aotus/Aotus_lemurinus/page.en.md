## study-sites

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2018–2019 study followed three groups in two Colombian Andean forests: El Colegio at 2,350–2,550 m and Pijao at 1,750–1,800 m. The sites differed in forest size and landscape setting, so group-level behavior and space use should be read in that context.

## feeding-and-activity

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Across the four study groups of A. lemurinus and A. griseimembra, fruits were the main food and about 48% of the night was spent resting. The highland A. lemurinus groups also frequently ate plants in Melastomataceae and Rubiaceae. These are observations from three groups over roughly a year, not a complete species-wide diet.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
