## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Hotaling and colleagues used mitochondrial gene trees, nuclear population structure and Bayesian species-delimitation tests to support separation of Manitatra mouse lemur from western M. murinus samples. They interpreted it as a disjunct southeastern lineage; the result is limited to the populations compared in the paper.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The paper designated an adult female collected in 2007 as the holotype and linked the name to a southeastern extension disjunct from western M. murinus. Type material is not a substitute for a modern range-wide survey.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The name manitatra comes from Malagasy for “range expansion.” It records the authors' naming rationale for the taxon's geographic pattern, not a demonstrated expansion process or migration route. Current MDD treatment includes this name within *M. murinus*.
<!-- /evo:text -->
