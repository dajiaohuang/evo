## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Andriamahaihavana and colleagues surveyed lemurs along transects in Andranobe, Masoala National Park, from 2017 to 2022, covering 110.42 km of diurnal and 86.93 km of nocturnal transects across Cyclone Eliakim in 2018. They reported no post-cyclone decline in white-fronted brown lemur density. Large diurnal lemurs as a group were more often associated with canopy gaps larger than 150 m²; that size-and-activity-group result is not a species-specific gap preference for E. albifrons. These transects describe Andranobe, not a range-wide population trend or conservation assessment.
