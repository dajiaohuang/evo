## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The description estimated Seal's sportive lemur at about 0.95 kg, with long, dense fur that is light chocolate to reddish brown, a paler underside, and a uniformly brown-gray tail. The authors also compared it with southeastern relatives using three mitochondrial sequence fragments.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Anjanaharibe-Sud Special Reserve. The paper then placed known records north of the Antainambalana River and left the southern and northern limits unresolved. The authors provisionally assigned a Mananara-Nord population to this species while noting that preliminary molecular data might indicate a separate taxon; this was not a current taxonomic conclusion.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The distribution and assignment of the Mananara-Nord population were hypotheses in the 2006 paper. This guide does not present a possible split as current taxonomy. The COL26.8 accepted name is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial, and the page is not externally expert-reviewed.
