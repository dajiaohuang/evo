# Carnufex

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Carnufex is anchored by partial, immature holotype NCSM 21558 from the Carnian Pekin Formation of North Carolina; that one individual does not define the genus-wide first or last appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen number, formation and age are directly reported. High confidence is intentionally specimen-local and does not depend on predator reconstruction.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Zanno et al. named Carnufex carolinensis from holotype NCSM 21558 and recovered it at the base of Crocodylomorpha in an unresolved polytomy in their 79-taxon, 413-character analysis; this is a sampled topology, not direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The name, type material and matrix result are explicit in the primary study. Confidence remains medium because the basal placement is analysis-dependent and the holotype is skeletally immature and incomplete.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
The Carnufex type and referred material are recorded from the approximately 231 Ma Pekin Formation in Chatham County, North Carolina; this single formation sample does not establish a genus-wide distribution or absence elsewhere.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The locality, formation and age are directly reported for the named material. High confidence is limited to that occurrence and does not infer a complete geographic range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
NCSM 21558 preserves partial cranial and postcranial material, including a reconstructed slender, ornamented skull longer than 50 cm and elongate, serrated, slightly recurved premaxillary teeth; these observations are limited to the immature holotype.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The preserved elements, three-dimensional reconstruction and tooth morphology are described and figured directly. High confidence does not extend the reconstruction to unknown individuals or ontogenetic stages.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The study interprets Carnufex as an approximately 3 m terrestrial faunivore and top-tier predator in the sampled Pekin Formation assemblage using serrated teeth, reconstructed skull length and an immature femur-length estimate of 354–441 mm; feeding, locomotion and guild are not direct behavioural observations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
Dentition and preserved anatomy are specimen observations, whereas total length, femur proxy and predator-guild assignment are reconstructions bounded to the study and assemblage.
<!-- /evo:text -->
