## Lengths in the diagnostic material
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10208806-pfw73
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported male snout–vent lengths of 32.2–33.1 mm and female lengths of 37.6–39.3 mm. It described smooth dorsal skin, absent vomerine teeth and male nuptial pads on the first finger. These observations describe the diagnostic material and are insufficient alone for identifying every population.

## Night-time karst-forest collections
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10208806-pfw73
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The species was reported from Dien Bien and Son La in northwestern Vietnam. Specimens were collected between 19:00 and 23:00 on limestone cliffs and leaves about 0.5–1.2 m above ground in secondary karst forest. Survey air temperatures of 13–18 °C and relative humidity of 65–80% are collection conditions, not species-wide physiological limits.
