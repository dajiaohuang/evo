## Four sexed specimens and a combined diagnostic pattern
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-77whb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2022 account reported two females, DHMECN 14423–14424, with body sizes of 21.33–22.40 mm and two males, DHMECN 14421–14422, at 15.58–16.73 mm. Its diagnostic summary combined irregular white markings on a black venter, conical upper-eyelid tubercles, one interorbital tubercle and a row of ulnar and tarsal tubercles. These small local samples are not population size limits or a statistical demonstration of sexual dimorphism.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Low leaf records in the Machay montane forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-77whb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The four specimens were found on bush, palm and cyclanth leaves 20–50 cm above ground in Machay Reserve montane forest at 2290 m. This documents the sampled encounter substrates and height; it does not establish obligatory use of these plants, an elevational tolerance interval, an egg site or a complete daily activity pattern.
