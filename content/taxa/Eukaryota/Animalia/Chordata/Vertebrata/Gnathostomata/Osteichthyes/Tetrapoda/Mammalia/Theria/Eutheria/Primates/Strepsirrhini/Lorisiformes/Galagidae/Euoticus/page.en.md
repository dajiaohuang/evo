## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID 4G62 links the accepted E. elegantulus (3CHNL) and E. pallidus (3CHNM) pages. Start with the Cameroon transect survey to compare the reported encounter rates for the two species, then read the vegetation study for pooled genus-level context: its 14 Cameroon quadrats were selected after nocturnal Euoticus observations and showed a difference in tree density between sampled primary and secondary forests. The encounter rates come from selected sites and are not densities; the vegetation article does not distinguish habitat responses between the species. This is a path through selected evidence, not a complete genus roster or ecological synthesis.
