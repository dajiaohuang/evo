# Panolax sanctaefidei


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Panolax sanctaefidei is accepted in the fixed PBDB mammalia working roster, with database parent Panolax. This guide uses 2 eligible species-level occurrence records from 2 collections and 2 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 16.3–9.4 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is Santa Cruz, occurrence occ:192451 (16.3–12.5 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Avawatz Mountains, occurrence occ:190993 (12.5–9.4 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.
