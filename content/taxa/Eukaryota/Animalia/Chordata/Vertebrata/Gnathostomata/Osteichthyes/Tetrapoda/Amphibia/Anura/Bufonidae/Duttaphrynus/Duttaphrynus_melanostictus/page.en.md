## Four sampled toads were excluded from the diet comparisons
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6173161-6dq3t
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/text/en -->
The Andaman study captured four toads labelled Duttaphrynus melanostictus but excluded them from the comparative diet analysis because sample size was inadequate. The methods explicitly called their taxonomic and geographic status uncertain. Accordingly, tigerinus–Limnonectes or tigerinus–Fejervarya overlap values and prey electivity are not melanostictus results. This records limited source-labelled sampling rather than a certified contemporary concept, a complete toad diet, a proven native or invasive status, or an absence of trophic interaction. Sparse sampling cannot establish that the toad has no local ecological role.

## Five sampled populations have different measurement denominators
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/17/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10451166-6dq3t
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/17/text/en -->
The study collected 153 animals during April–August 2018–2020 from five sites in Yunnan, Guizhou and Hunan. Site counts were 45 Midu, 50 Mouding, 16 Pingjiang, 18 Pingbian and 24 Yuanling. Organ data were reported for 153 animals, brain data for 147; Pingbian had only one male and was excluded from the testis population analysis. These denominators do not mean every animal supplied every reproductive measurement, and the collection methods allow both mature animals and subadults. The sample is not a species-wide census.

## Preserved dry mass, tract length and estimated volume are distinct
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/18/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10451166-6dq3t
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/18/text/en -->
Specimens were formalin-fixed and stored for two weeks to two months before organ processing. Most organ sizes were dry masses after 24 h at 60 °C; digestive tract length was measured from photographs. Brain volume was estimated from photographed length, width and height using the study’s geometric formula, with paired structures derived from one hemisphere. These are different assay units, not living wet masses or directly measured three-dimensional volumes, and they do not quantify digestive efficiency or cognition.

## Relative organ size is a model residual
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/19/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10451166-6dq3t
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/19/text/en -->
The authors define relative organ size as the residual of log10 organ mass, length or volume against log10 snout–vent length. Their body-condition index is a separate body-mass/SVL regression residual. It is not a simple organ/body percentage. Body condition was associated with seven relative organ measures but not tract length or gallbladder size in the reported tests. Different raw units and model-derived residuals should not be merged into one biological percentage.

## Mitochondrial sampling and phylogenetic limits
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/29/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc4740822-6dq3t
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/29/text/en -->
The molecular comparison concatenates partial12S and16S mitochondrial regions and uses model-based maximum-likelihood and Bayesian inference. The focal sequences are reported as417bp and551bp, respectively; alignment lengths and raw data were not independently verified. The paper reports43 species across21 genera in Table3, with a36-species broad analysis and selected Asian subtrees. Its closest-to-focal Bufoides placement in the broad tree had relatively low support and changes in restricted sampling must retain their context. Two mitochondrial regions are not independent nuclear evidence of ancestry or reproductive isolation. The paper calls distances “uncorrected k2p”; that wording is retained as a source ambiguity, not silently interpreted as an uncorrected p-distance or a universal genus-delimitation threshold.
