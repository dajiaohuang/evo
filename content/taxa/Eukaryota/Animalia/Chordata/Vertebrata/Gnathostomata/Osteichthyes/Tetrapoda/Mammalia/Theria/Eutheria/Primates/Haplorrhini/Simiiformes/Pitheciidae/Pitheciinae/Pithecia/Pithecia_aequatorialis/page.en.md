## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh's 2014 revision examined 20 skins and skulls and gives the type locality as Santa Luisa on Peru's Río Nanay, Loreto, at 160 m. Tirira's 2023 account assembled seven Ecuadorian records: five museum specimens and two field photographs from the preceding decade. They place records in Pastaza at 180–405 m, more than 200 km west of the previously known Peruvian records. The paper still calls for field work on the southern and western limits.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
