# Dimetrodon

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Named Early Permian Dimetrodon and cf. Dimetrodon teeth document diverse carina and cusp anatomy in a sphenacodontid matrix; neither the sample nor its topology identifies a direct mammalian ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Predatory feeding inferred from sphenacodontid tooth anatomy; individual prey is not preserved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Fully terrestrial apex-predator interpretation for the sampled sphenacodontids; habitat breadth is unresolved
