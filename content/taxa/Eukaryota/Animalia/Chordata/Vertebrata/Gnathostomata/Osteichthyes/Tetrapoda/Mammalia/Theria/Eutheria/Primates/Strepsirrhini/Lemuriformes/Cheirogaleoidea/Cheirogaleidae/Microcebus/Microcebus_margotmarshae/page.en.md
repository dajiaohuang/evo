## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Louis et al. (2008) described Margot Marsh's mouse lemur in a revision of northern and northwestern mouse lemurs, using material from Antafondro Classified Forest. MDD's primary display name is Margot Marsh's Mouse Lemur and it also lists the locality-based name Antafondro Mouse Lemur.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The MDD record places holotype TAFO6.1 in Antafondro Classified Forest at about 134 m. This type locality locates the specimen used in the description; it does not by itself establish the full range or current forest condition.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Evidence is limited to the 2008 revision, its descriptive material and the type locality; the type point is not treated as a present-day range survey, and no population trend or conservation category is inferred. The Chinese common name is editorial and the page has not received external expert review. The page is not externally expert-reviewed.
