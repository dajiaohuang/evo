## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At the Pearl River and Ross Barnett Reservoir in Mississippi, researchers tracked 17 adult male alligators in 2012–2013 and collected 1,145 locations. Habitat selection differed between home-range and within-range patch scales; some individuals selected patches with floating or emergent vegetation.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In this sample of 17 males, body size was not associated with home-range size or resource selection. The result comes from adult males in one river–reservoir system and does not establish patterns for females, juveniles or other regions.
