## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Using genetic differences among samples from Arunachal Pradesh, Ghosh et al. (2022) described the Sela macaque as a distinct phylogenetic species from the Arunachal macaque and estimated their allopatric divergence at about 1.96 million years ago in relation to the Sela mountain pass. The abstract also reports high within-species genetic variation and at least two conservation units; these are genetic and taxonomic findings.
