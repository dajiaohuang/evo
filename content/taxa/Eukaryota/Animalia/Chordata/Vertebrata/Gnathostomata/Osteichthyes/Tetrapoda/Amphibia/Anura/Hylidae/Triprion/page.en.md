## Named records in a community-conserved cloud forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The 2021 Santa Cruz Tepetotutla checklist includes Triprion spinosus. These selected Triprion records come from the 2018–2019 survey in the Chinantla region of Oaxaca. Four observers made eleven five-day trips across wet and dry seasons, in pristine and managed sites, and photographed and released encountered animals. The recorded elevations and CNAR photographic catalogue numbers remain species-specific. They establish source-reported presence, not an exhaustive genus inventory, authenticated museum vouchers, global range limits or population trends.
