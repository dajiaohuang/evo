## Accepted name and placement

COL26.8 accepts Nanorana xuelinensis Liu, Zhang & Rao, 2021 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Nanorana (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Chest and broader body spines come from a cited comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10649098-qn2d5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The 2023 account distinguishes laojunshanensis by paired brown chest spines from the cited xuelinensis pattern of black spines on the chest, belly and lateral body. The genus discussion also notes first finger longer than second and an outer fifth-toe fringe in xuelinensis. These are comparison statements based on earlier taxonomic work, not a fresh series measuring population variation, seasonality or spine function.
