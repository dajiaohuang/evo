## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Allenopithecus nigroviridis (Pocock, 1907) as species ID BTKJ under genus ID S4R. The MDD v2.5 page uses Allen's Swamp Monkey. Its header lists northwestern Democratic Republic of the Congo and northeastern Angola, while the country list separately names the Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, and Cameroon?; these fields do not define one continuous range. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## earlyFieldEvidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Gautier’s preliminary 1985 account combines a hunter interview survey in Zaire with nine days of observation in June 1984 at a site where the monkey was abundant. It reports local records around Mbandaka, Gemena, Mondombé and Lisala, and extends the historical southern record to Kasongo-Lunda on the basis of a hunter’s reports. These are collection and interview records assembled in a short preliminary study, not a standardized range survey.

## northwestRecords

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Maisels and colleagues (2006) reported records in northern Republic of the Congo at least 100 km northwest of the previously known range, west of Nouabalé-Ndoki National Park. They noted apparent absence from much of the park interior and proposed movement along the Sangha and Ndoki rivers as an explanation. The article presents local observations and a biogeographic interpretation; it does not measure dispersal or estimate abundance.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The two field sources are a short 1984 preliminary study and later local observations in northwestern Congo. The latter’s river-corridor explanation is an interpretation. MDD header and country-list distribution fields differ in scope. COL26.8 ID BTKJ fixes page identity; the Chinese display name is editorial. No dossier or external expert review was added. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
