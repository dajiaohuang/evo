The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Placement and an older name combination
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6404656-486j9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The2019 study formally places the older combination Centrolenella balionota in Nymphargus and includes balionotus in its mitochondrial tree. This is the authors’ taxonomic action, while the page retains the frozen accepted Nymphargus balionotus name. The paper describes green dorsal skin with black andsometimes yellow-to-cinnamon blotches; it does not transfer manduriacu’s false-ocellus diagnosis to this species.
