## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This guide links selected evidence types rather than covering every species in the genus. Begin with diet of Delacour's langurs at Van Long, habitat and range use of white-headed langurs at Fusui, karst-slope use by Cat Ba langurs, golden-langur transects in western Assam, and seasonal diet records from one Sunda silvery langur group in West Sumatra. Then compare the mitochondrial study of the silvered-langur group: *T. auratus*, *T. germaini*, *T. margarita* and *T. mauritius* were among five sampled lineages; the same study named the Malay Peninsula form *T. cristatus selangorensis* at subspecies rank, while COL26.8 retains *T. selangorensis* as an accepted species. Read the single-zoo-specimen description of *T. barbei* alongside the museum-based mitochondrial analysis of *T. phayrei* and the type-specimen description of *T. popa*, keeping each paper's taxonomic proposals distinct from the pinned catalogue. Finish with strata use by *T. crepusculus* at Mt. Wuliang and the 2019–2020 fragment-occupancy survey of *T. pileatus* in Assam. Different sites, samples, years and methods do not combine into a genus-wide ecological rule or complete phylogeny. Finish by comparing four local studies: protected-area transects for *T. laotum* in Laos, feeding records for *T. obscurus* in Malaysia, fecal-barcode diet evidence for *T. hatinhensis* in Vietnam, and interviews plus field surveys for *T. shortridgei* in Yunnan. Then read the name history and sparse records for *T. ebenus*, and the type-based correspondence between COL26.8 *T. melamerus* and MDD *T. melamera*. Different sites, dates, sample units and name treatments do not combine into a genus-wide range, population status or uniform diet pattern.
