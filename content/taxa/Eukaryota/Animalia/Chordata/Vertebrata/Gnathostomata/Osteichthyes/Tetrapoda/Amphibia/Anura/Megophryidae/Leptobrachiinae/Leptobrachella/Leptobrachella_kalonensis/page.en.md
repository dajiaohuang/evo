The 2026 description of L. deocaensis cites a pale brownish-pink belly with white speckling, distinct dorsolateral markings, black flank spots and a gold iris with a copper-orange upper third for Leptobrachella kalonensis. These traits come from the literature comparison, not newly measured animals of this species in the Deo Ca survey. The comparison contrasts it with the newly described population and is not a complete identification key.
