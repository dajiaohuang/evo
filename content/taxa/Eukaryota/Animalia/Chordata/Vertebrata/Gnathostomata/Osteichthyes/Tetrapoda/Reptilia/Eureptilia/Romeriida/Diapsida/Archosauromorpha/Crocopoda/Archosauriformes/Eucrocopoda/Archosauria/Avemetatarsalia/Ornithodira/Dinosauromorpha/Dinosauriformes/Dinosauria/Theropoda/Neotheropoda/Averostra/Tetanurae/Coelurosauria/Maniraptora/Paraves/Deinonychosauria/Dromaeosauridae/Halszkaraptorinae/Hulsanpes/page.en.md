# Hulsanpes


## From a small set of foot bones to diagnosis and affinities

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
[Read the H. perlei redescription guide](Hulsanpes_perlei/page.en.md), beginning with preserved material, then the centimetre-scale braincase, metatarsal overlap and immaturity inference before relationships and function. Missing foot ends and the full body are not supplied by group reconstructions. Estimated and measured bone lengths remain distinct.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Different material and functional arguments within the subfamily

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The [Halszkaraptor genus guide](../Halszkaraptor/page.en.md) follows ecological arguments about snout, teeth and forelimbs. The 2018 Hulsanpes authors compare foot morphology with that genus to propose possible ecological differentiation. A partial braincase and right foot do not justify importing another genus’s neck, snout or swimming interpretation into this species.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route connects one fragmentary holotype redescription with fixed occurrences, without assigning identical anatomy, ecology or preserved material to relatives. Specimen size, immaturity and locomotor interpretations have distinct limits. A complete body is not preserved, and subsequent studies are not fully assembled. Counts and broad ages do not measure ancient abundance or death dates.
