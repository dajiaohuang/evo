## feedingBiomechanics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
An adult bite-force study covering 23 living crocodilian species grouped the Chinese alligator with the broad-snouted caiman as a blunt-snouted, blunt-toothed type associated with hard-shelled prey. The similarly sized species still spanned much of the molariform tooth-pressure range among crocodilians of comparable size, and snout shape alone did not reliably predict bite force. The feeding description synthesizes earlier literature; the experiment measured biting performance in adult research, conservation and display specimens.
