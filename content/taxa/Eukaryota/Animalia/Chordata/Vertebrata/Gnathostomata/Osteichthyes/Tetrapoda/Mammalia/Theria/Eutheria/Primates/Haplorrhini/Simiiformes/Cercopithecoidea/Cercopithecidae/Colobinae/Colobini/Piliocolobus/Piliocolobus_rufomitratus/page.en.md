## habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A study of Tana River forest fragments related the probability of red colobus occurrence to measured habitat features, including the relative amount of edge and canopy-tree composition. Local density variation was associated with measurements of food trees. These are site-level correlations from a historical fragment study, not proof of causation or a current range-wide estimate.
