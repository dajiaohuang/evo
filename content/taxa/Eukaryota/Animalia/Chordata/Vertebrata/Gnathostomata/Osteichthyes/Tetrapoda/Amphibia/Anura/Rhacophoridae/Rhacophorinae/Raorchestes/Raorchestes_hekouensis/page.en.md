## Yunnan and northern Vietnamese records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10905625-slx5r
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description reported Hekou County in Yunnan, China, and Bac Pan in Tuyen Quang, Vietnam. At the Yunnan site, frogs occupied shrubs and herbs beside a small roadside stream at about 1200 m. These are reported localities and a surveyed microhabitat, without establishing continuous occupancy between countries.

## March and April survey contrast
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10905625-slx5r
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Surveys on 25 March 2019 and 4 April 2023 found no calling male or egg in late March but calling males in April. The authors inferred an April breeding-season onset. The observations came from different years and did not include an egg record, so they do not establish a fixed annual onset or the complete reproductive season.
<!-- /evo:text -->
