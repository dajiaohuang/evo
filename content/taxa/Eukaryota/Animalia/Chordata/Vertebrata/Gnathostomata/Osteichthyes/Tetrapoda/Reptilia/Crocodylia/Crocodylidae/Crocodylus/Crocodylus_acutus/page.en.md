## populationGenetics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A study spanning the Americas and Greater Antilles combined mitochondrial control-region data with 11 nuclear microsatellite loci and found strong genetic structure among sampled localities. Differentiation was especially pronounced between continental and Greater Antillean samples, with additional separation between Cuba and Jamaica; the Venezuelan samples yielded three newly reported haplotypes. The results show why American-crocodile genetic relationships should be interpreted by region and sampling locality.
