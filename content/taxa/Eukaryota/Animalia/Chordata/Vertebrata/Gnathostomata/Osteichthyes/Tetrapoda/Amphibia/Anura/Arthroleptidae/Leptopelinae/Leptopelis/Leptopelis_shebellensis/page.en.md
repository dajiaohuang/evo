## Didda Plateau and northern Bale meadows
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9836736-qfbkc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The original account reported grassy meadows of Ethiopia’s Didda Plateau and northern Bale Mountains at 2429–3296 m, including localities around Assela, Huruta, Dinsho, Adaba, Dodola, Goba and Chole. These records describe the published sample without implying continuous occupancy across that elevation interval.

## Calling in both recorded seasons
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9836736-qfbkc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Males were heard calling in dry-season April and June and rainy-season July and August, by day and night. Calling sites included stream banks and side pools, often ground holes or sites under low vegetation. These calling observations do not demonstrate uninterrupted year-round reproduction or establish the timing of egg laying and larval development.
