## readingPath

Start with Hadi and colleagues’ three-group account of social structure, then compare the northern Siberut study of canopy use and diet, and finish with the two-year Pungut birth record. Each study covers a small local sample; this is a route through those records, not a genus-wide ecological synthesis.
