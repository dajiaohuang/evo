## An island holotype and three adult paratypes
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7830202-q37lt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Adult holotype CGRB15863, field ID mms3149, was collected at Geoje-myeon on Geoje Island on 6 April 2011. Three adult paratypes were from the same site or Mundong-dong, collected in April 2011 or March 2012. Their sex is not supplied by these treatment paragraphs. The material is tied to dated vouchers, not a complete present-day island census; no body dimensions are inferred from photographs or locality alone.

## A contextual size comparison with ambiguous identification thresholds

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->

The account described geojeensis as confined to Geoje Island and sympatric with notialis in its northern range. It called geojeensis generally broader and longer than notialis, yet its identification sentence used upper cutoffs such as SVL below 45.59 mm for the sympatric area. The broader analysis found overlapping measurements and no universal nonoverlapping variable. ## Natural history pooled across three species
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7830202-q37lt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The authors explicitly pooled natural history for H. notialis, H. geojeensis and H. perplicatus because separate breeding ecology was poorly known. They described still-water reproduction in roadside or field ditches and fallow rice paddies, earliest breeding in late January, a late-February to early-March peak and some late-March spawning. Females generally deposited two curved sacs, sometimes looped or nearly straight, and attachment varied with the site. Larvae developed paired balancers and metamorphosed before the following winter; larval overwintering was not known. These pooled observations are not independently measured schedules, clutch sizes or developmental durations for each species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## An island-area proxy and a misplaced species name in the assessment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7830202-q37lt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The conservation paragraph estimated geojeensis extent of occurrence below 500 km² and area of occupancy below 100 km² using island area as a proxy, but its concluding Critically Endangered sentence printed the name notialis. The discussion separately identified geojeensis as the species needing strong protection and described no more than five known localities. The name mismatch is explicit; the paragraph is not silently corrected or used to assign a current category. Island area is not directly surveyed occupancy, and the dated concern does not quantify population size.
