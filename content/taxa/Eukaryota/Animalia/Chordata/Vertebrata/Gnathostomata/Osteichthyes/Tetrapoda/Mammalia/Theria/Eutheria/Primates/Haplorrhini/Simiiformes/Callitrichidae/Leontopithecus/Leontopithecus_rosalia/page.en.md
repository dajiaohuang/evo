## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A lure-assisted point-transect survey across seven management units estimated 3,706 golden lion tamarins in 2014 (95% CI 1,863–5,383). The authors estimated about 59% came from remnant wild populations, 34% were descendants of captive-born reintroduced animals and 7% descendants of translocated wild groups. These are survey-based historical estimates, not direct counts or current abundance.
