# Dormaalocyon latouri

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
About 280 teeth, jaws and ankle bones document an early Eocene carnivoraform sample and replace the historical two-tooth picture of “Miacis” latouri.
