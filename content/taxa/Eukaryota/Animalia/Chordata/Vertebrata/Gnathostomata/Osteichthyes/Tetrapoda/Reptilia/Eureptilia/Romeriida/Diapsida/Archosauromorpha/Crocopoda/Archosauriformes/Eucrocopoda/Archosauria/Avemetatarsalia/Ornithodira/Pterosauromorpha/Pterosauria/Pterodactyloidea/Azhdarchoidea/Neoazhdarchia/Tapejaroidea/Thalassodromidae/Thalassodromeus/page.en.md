## Stratigraphic names and relationships have dated frameworks

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2002 paper records the type horizon as the Romualdo Member of the Santana Formation, Albian; the 2018 paper uses Romualdo Formation within the Santana Group. It explicitly notes disputed relationships and uses a framework with Thalassodrominae and Tapejarinae as sisters. The fixed PBDB collection row below retains 119.57–113.2 Ma, Late Aptian and the Thalassodromidae parent chain. Stratigraphic terminology, dating and classification differences remain distinct, without replacing database scope from one older paper or claiming a unified latest consensus.
