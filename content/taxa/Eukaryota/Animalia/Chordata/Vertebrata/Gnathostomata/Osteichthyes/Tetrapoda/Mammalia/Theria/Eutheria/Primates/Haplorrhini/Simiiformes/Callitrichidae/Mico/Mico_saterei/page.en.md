## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2025 Brazilian ICMBio assessment card describes the species as endemic to Amazonas and gives river-based limits from the original account: Abacaxis to the east, Canumã and Sucundurí to the west, and Paraná Urariá to the south. It notes that additional sampling is needed near the southern limit around Igarapé do Arreganhado. The card reports a minimum-convex-polygon extent of occurrence of 1,571.2 km² and a separate habitat-distribution estimate of 19,569.7 km²; the measures are not interchangeable.

## habitat_and_conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The card reports use of primary and secondary terra firme forest and igapó, and cites 0.05% habitat-area loss for 2002–2018. It records a Brazilian Least Concern category dated 27 September 2019, published in the 2025 card, while population trend is unknown. Mining-related habitat impacts are identified; ecology, population-size and threat-impact studies remain research needs.
