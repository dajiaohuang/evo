## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The study recorded a Lake Baptista titi within an area then attributed to Hoffmann's titi and noted earlier records from the same area. The authors proposed that the two forms might represent clinal pelage variation within one species, while emphasizing that their taxonomy and distribution were poorly studied and that they had not been compared in a molecular phylogenetic analysis.
