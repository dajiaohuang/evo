## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 places accepted species pages 75DLX and 75DM9 under genus ID 63HY8. Start with Nash and Harcourt's radio-tracking and mark-recapture study of O. garnettii at two Kenyan coastal sites, then compare Long et al.'s seasonal food and hormone measurements for O. crassicaudatus at Lajuma, South Africa. Harcourt's South African diet study and the Kenyan substrate-use comparison add targeted follow-up. The studies use different places, periods and methods; this path is not a genus-wide ecological synthesis.
