## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2000 geographic-variation study compared wild animals and museum skins and argued that western Avahi contained several geographically distinct pelage forms; it described A. unicolor as one of those forms. A later 2006 comparison included five A. occidentalis samples from Ampijoroa and Ankarafantsika. These records reflect the material available to those studies, not a present-day distribution survey.
