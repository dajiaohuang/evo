## Stream edges and trails in semi-evergreen forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7180169-slxbb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description recorded frogs in northeastern Bangladesh’s semi-evergreen primary and secondary forests, mainly beside streams and human-made trails. Individuals perched on small-tree leaves and branches or bamboo trunks; calling individuals were 1–1.5 m above the forest floor. These observations describe occupied substrates at the surveyed sites, without showing dependence on trails or tolerance of all disturbed habitat.

## Rain-season calling observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7180169-slxbb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Calling activity accompanied the April onset of rains, usually becoming audible just after sunset, around 18:15 in April, and starting earlier during rain. Researchers did not hear calls after August. These observations delimit recorded calling activity, without proving absence of reproduction outside that window or specifying egg sites and development.
