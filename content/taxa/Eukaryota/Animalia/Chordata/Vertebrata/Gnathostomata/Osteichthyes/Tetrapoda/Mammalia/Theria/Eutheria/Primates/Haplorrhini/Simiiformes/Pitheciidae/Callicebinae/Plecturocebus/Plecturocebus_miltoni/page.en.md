# Plecturocebus miltoni

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The supplement records Milton's titi at Apuí and Novo Aripuanã in Amazonas. These are local records from the survey period, not a complete range map or abundance assessment.
