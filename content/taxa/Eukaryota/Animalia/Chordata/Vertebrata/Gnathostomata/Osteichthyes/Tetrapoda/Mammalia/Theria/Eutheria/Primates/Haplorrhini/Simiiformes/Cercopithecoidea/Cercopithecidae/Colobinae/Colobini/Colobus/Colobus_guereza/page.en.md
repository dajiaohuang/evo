## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Kalinzu Forest in western Uganda, researchers observed one habituated family group for 4,308 hours. Young leaves accounted for 87.0% of feeding time and fruit for 9.8%, with the remainder including flowers, bark and soil. In fine-grained analyses of leaves that were eaten, protein, digestibility and toughness were associated with choices; those measures did not explain the broader eaten-versus-not-eaten pattern. The results concern this group and period.
