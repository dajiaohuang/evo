## Reported adult measurements and toe fringes
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v57nh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported 26 adult males at 26.4–32.4 mm snout–vent length and two adult females at 29.5–34.7 mm. It described neither vomerine ridges nor teeth, rudimentary basal toe webbing with wide lateral fringes, and male nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II, with a subgular vocal sac. These diagnostic sample counts and characters do not establish population-wide size limits or reproductive timing.

## Adult evidence added to an earlier tadpole comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v57nh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The remarks reported earlier possible cryptic-species labels M. sp15 for Sanming and M. sp16 for Chaozhou, although ABGD grouped them together. The 2021 description included Chaozhou in the distribution but had only two Chaozhou tadpoles available. The monograph added Chaozhou adults and found no distinguishing morphology from Sanming, Longyan and Fuzhou material, treating these populations as conspecific. This is the authors’ evidence-based interpretation, not an independent reproductive-compatibility test.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v57nh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys sanmingensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
