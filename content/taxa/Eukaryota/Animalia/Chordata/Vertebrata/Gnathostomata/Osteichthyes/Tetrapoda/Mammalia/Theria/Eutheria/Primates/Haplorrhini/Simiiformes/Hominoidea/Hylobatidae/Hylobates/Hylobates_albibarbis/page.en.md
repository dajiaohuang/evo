## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Cheyne and colleagues surveyed the Sabangau catchment from July to December 2005 and combined auditory sampling with detailed behavioral observations of six habituated groups. They cautioned that sound records alone do not necessarily give an accurate picture of population size, density or distribution unless surveys span enough time to account for seasonal variation in calling. This page describes that survey design; it does not turn the 2005 result into a present-day estimate.
