## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The fixed COL26.8 roster and MDD v2.5 list red bald uacari as Cacajao rubicundus at species rank. A 2021 IUCN assessment used the earlier combination C. calvus rubicundus and stated that field-ecology data were unavailable at that time; the 2022 molecular systematics paper likewise labels relevant samples with the older combination. The current species-level roster and older assessment do not use the same rank, so the latter’s mapped region or inferred habitat should not be expanded into a complete ecological account of the current species.

## molecular sampling

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the 2022 systematic study, the cytochrome-b dataset included eight sequences under the historical label C. calvus rubicundus, and the smaller ddRAD dataset included six. These are genetic samples assigned under the paper's earlier subspecies taxonomy, not a population count or a direct estimate of current Cacajao rubicundus abundance. The study tests relationships among bald-uakari lineages using molecular data and museum pelage comparisons; it does not supply a field-ecology account for the species as currently delimited by COL26.8.
