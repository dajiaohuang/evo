## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The fixed COL26.8 roster and MDD v2.5 list red bald uacari as Cacajao rubicundus at species rank. A 2021 IUCN assessment used the earlier combination C. calvus rubicundus and stated that field-ecology data were unavailable at that time; the 2022 molecular systematics paper likewise labels relevant samples with the older combination. The current species-level roster and older assessment do not use the same rank, so the latter’s mapped region or inferred habitat should not be expanded into a complete ecological account of the current species.
