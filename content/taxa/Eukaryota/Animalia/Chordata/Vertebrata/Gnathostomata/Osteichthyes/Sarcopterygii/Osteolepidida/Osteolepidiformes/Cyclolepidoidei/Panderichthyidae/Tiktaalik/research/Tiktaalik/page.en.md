# Tiktaalik

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Frasnian elpistostegalian known from articulated material that combines fish-like features with a mobile neck, robust ribs and a fin containing wrist-like joints.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Body, life and relationships
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/0/text/en -->
Tiktaalik retains fin rays alongside wrist-like joints, a mobile neck and robust ribs. How does this combination connect fins and limbs? The fossil records anatomy in one body; movement capabilities and everyday behaviour require separate interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/1/title/en -->
## Scales, fin rays and wrist-like joints
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/1/text/en -->
Tiktaalik retains scales and fin rays, alongside a mobile neck, robust ribs and a pectoral fin with wrist-like joints. Features we often discuss separately occur together in the same body.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/2/title/en -->
## A mosaic of features in one body
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/2/text/en -->
Researchers compare the shape, position and connections of bones. A mosaic of features is a combination of traits associated with different groups. It informs the study of anatomical change without identifying a fossil as a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/3/title/en -->
## How anatomy constrains interpretations of movement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/3/text/en -->
Anatomy constrains interpretations of movement, but a structure and an everyday behaviour are different kinds of evidence. These descriptions support the anatomical combination; they do not establish habitual walking on land. Distinguish what is preserved from what is inferred.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / readerLimitations

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerLimitations/en -->
Anatomical observations, interpretations of behaviour and modelled branching times answer different questions. The selected material does not identify a direct ancestor or establish a complete distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->
