# Macaca leucogenys

## Taxonomic research and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Li, Zhao and Fan (2015) described Macaca leucogenys from 738 photographs and distinguished it from the M. sinica species group. They recorded tropical forest (1,395 m), evergreen broad-leaved forest (2,000 m), and mixed broadleaf–conifer forest (2,700 m); proposed range extension to nearby areas remains tentative.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
One species description is not a range-wide survey, population estimate or trend analysis; extension to nearby areas is tentative. The Chinese label is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
