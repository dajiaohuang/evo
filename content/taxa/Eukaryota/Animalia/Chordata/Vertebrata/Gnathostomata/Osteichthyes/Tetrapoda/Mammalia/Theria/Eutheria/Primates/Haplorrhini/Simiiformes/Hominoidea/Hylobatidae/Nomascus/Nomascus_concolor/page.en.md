## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Wuliang Mountain in Yunnan, China, long-term observations from March 2003 to June 2009 found two breeding females in each of a focal group and two neighbouring groups. Researchers also made detailed social observations of one habituated group for nine months. Adult members of the focal group showed little aggression and the females remained close to one another. These records document local multifemale groups; the sample and site do not establish social organization across the species.
