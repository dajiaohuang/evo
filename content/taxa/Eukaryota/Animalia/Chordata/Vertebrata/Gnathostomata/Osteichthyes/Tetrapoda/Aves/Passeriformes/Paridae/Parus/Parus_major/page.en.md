## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
From 1997 to 2000, researchers compared 2-hectare nest-box plots where great tits bred with matched control plots in 12 commercial Dutch apple orchards: six under integrated pest management and six organic. In the integrated-management orchards, caterpillar damage affected 2.9% of apples in plots with breeding tits versus 5.8% in controls; the study did not show the same reduction in organic orchards.
