# Neornithes

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The crown group defined by the common ancestor of living birds and all descendants; this boundary excludes Archaeopteryx and keeps Cretaceous fossil membership tied to explicit phylogenetic analyses.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Highly diverse; the highlighted Vegavis skull supports underwater prey capture for that fossil only
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Global across marine, freshwater and terrestrial environments today
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Predominantly volant, with multiple independently flight-reduced or flightless lineages
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Extremely diverse
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
No single crown-wide ecological guild
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
Crown membership is defined relationally, not by one universally diagnostic fossil character
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Vegavis skull anatomy combines an avian beak and crown-like neuroanatomy with a specialized feeding apparatus
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Asteriornis preserves a different late Maastrichtian galloanseran character combination
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
A 2025 skull analysis strengthens crown-waterfowl placement for older Vegavis, but the study explicitly records its earlier contested placements; younger Asteriornis also shifts among near-crown positions under different analyses.
<!-- /evo:text -->
