## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Garbino, Silva and Davis reported two additional records under the historical name Callibella humilis. One was observed and photographed in campinarana on the west bank of the Rio Madeirinha, extending the reported distribution about 270 km south to the west bank of the Rio Roosevelt. The second was a collected individual from the Rio Roosevelt mouth, less than 10 km from the type locality of Mico marcai. Two records do not provide an abundance estimate or a complete range survey.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Source-linked introduction; not externally expert-reviewed. This page follows COL26.8 as Mico humilis, while the field article used Callibella humilis. MDD records Callibella as a subgenus and notes differing generic treatments. The range statement comes from two records, not a current full-range assessment.
