## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Using specimens with locality data, the 2000 revision summarized records from southwestern Madagascar: from Lamboharana south to the Toliara region and southeast to Beza Mahafaly. The authors left open the possibility of records farther south and east. These are located specimen records, not a continuous survey or a complete range.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Specimens around Beza Mahafaly had gray-based dorsal fur with reddish markings, and the revision also used external, cranial and dental measurements to distinguish similar species. The paper's specimens came from subarid thorn scrub in the Ihazoara Valley near the reserve boundary; this describes the sampled locality, not habitat composition throughout the species' range.
