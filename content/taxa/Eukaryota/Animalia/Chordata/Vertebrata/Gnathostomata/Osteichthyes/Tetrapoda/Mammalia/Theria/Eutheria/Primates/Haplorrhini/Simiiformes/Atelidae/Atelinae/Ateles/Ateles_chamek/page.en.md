# Ateles chamek

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Field expeditions during 2015–2018 recorded two black-pelaged spider monkeys without the white forehead and cheek hairs characteristic of the white-cheeked spider monkey on the right bank of the middle Teles Pires River. The authors identified them as Chamek spider monkeys; the points fall within the mapped range of the white-cheeked species, and one overlaps its record. River islands may facilitate crossings, but ongoing gene flow remains to be tested with more records and genetic samples.
