## breedingSuccess

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
McLatchie et al. analysed camera images from 450 Adélie penguin nests at five Windmill Islands sites across ten breeding seasons (2011/12–2020/21). Mean breeding success was 0.96 ± 0.76 SD chicks reaching crèche per nest. The yearly site samples ranged from 0.68 chicks per nest in 2014/15 to 1.3 in 2017/18 and 2020/21. These are local monitored nests, not a species-wide productivity estimate.

## nestBuilding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the same study, earlier nest occupation was associated with larger nests, and nest structure was positively associated with chick survival, especially where ground moisture and snow cover were high. The authors could not tell whether earlier arrival simply allowed more time to build or reflected other differences among birds. These relationships describe the Windmill Islands study and do not establish that the same factors dominate at every colony.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This profile draws on one ten-season study from one East Antarctic region. Some camera data were unavailable in certain years, and the monitored sites do not represent the whole circumpolar range. The study does not establish current global abundance, diet or migration patterns. The page is not externally expert-reviewed.
