## One male defines the reported size observation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9583859-scf8q
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Kunam was described from one adult male with SVL 14.66 mm, with female size unknown. Its diagnostic combination included a tuberculate dorsum with large conical tubercles, an absent tympanic membrane but present annulus, no basal toe webbing, three conical ulnar tubercles, and absent vocal slits and nuptial pads. The single male value is not a species range, mean from multiple animals or juvenile growth trajectory. Structural absence does not directly establish sensory performance or the species’ complete behaviour.

## A single fern-leaf encounter in primary forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9583859-scf8q
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The only known kunam individual in the study was collected at night on a fern leaf 130 cm above ground at one locality on Sangay volcano’s eastern foothills. The ecosystem description spans Eastern Montane Forest at 1,300–3,600 m, which is not a measured kunam elevational range. One encounter does not establish obligatory fern use, exclusive primary-forest occupation, abundance or a complete activity schedule. No female, clutch or larval observations are supplied by this site record.

## Unknown population status after possible volcanic impacts
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9583859-scf8q
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The description recommended Data Deficient and noted that Sangay’s eruptions since May 2019 might have affected the type locality, while population status was unknown. It cited only 0.12% deforestation within a five-kilometre radius using a 2018 map. Possible eruption effects are not measured mortality, and low mapped deforestation is not proof of safety from other threats. The inferred low tolerance of habitat change from a single primary-forest record was not experimentally established. The proposal and maps remain dated evidence, without a verified current census.
