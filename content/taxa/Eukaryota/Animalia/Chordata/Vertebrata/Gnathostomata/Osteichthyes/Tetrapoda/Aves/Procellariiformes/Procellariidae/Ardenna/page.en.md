## Three lifestyles within a shared marine food niche

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
All seven have Marine/open-environment 3/migration 3/Aquatic predator/Carnivore. The 60% aquatic-animal niche and 70% live-animal level thresholds define categories, not measured species diets. creatopus/tenuirostris/grisea/pacifica have Generalist, without a primary lifestyle; gravis/carneipes Aerial, defined as much time flying and foraging predominantly on the wing; bulleri Aquatic, defined as much time afloat and feeding while afloat or diving. Categories specify no capture actions, dive depth or time budgets. An aquatic food niche does not automatically imply Aquatic lifestyle. Migration 3 describes most individuals making long-distance movements without routes or each individual’s movements; hand-wing index is not converted to speed or mileage.
