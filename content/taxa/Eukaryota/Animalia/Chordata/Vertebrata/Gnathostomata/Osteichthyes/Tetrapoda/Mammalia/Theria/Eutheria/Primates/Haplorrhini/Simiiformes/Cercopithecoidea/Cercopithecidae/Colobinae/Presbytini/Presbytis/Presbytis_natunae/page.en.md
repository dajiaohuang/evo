## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists the Natuna Islands Langur as an accepted species. Lammertink, Nijman and Setiorini surveyed Bunguran for two months in 2003; their records confined the langur to forest and found it more often in primary lowland forest patches.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Groups averaged about 3.5 individuals in that survey, with an estimated density of 2.3 groups per km² in sampled areas. The authors extrapolated an island total, but this was a 2003 survey estimate, not a current count. The forest-patch preference is likewise limited to the transect data then collected.
