## Rainy-season specimens in one temporary pond
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8626410-tnjf9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description found all its specimens around 14:30 under leaf litter and among arrowroot stems in a small temporary pond surrounded by hill evergreen forest. The clear pond had a litter-covered bottom and measured approximately 5.2 m by 2.7 m, with a maximum depth of 17 cm. Fish were not observed during this visit. Afternoon refuge records do not establish a general daily activity rhythm, and the authors’ reference to the genus’s rainy-season breeding does not by itself document this species’s eggs or mating.
