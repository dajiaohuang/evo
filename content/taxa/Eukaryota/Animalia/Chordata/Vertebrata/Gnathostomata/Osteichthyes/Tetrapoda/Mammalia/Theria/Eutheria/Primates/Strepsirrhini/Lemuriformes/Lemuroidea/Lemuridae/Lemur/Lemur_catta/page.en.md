## evolution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A study analyzed 106 adult ring-tailed lemurs from nine sites in Madagascar and found moderate genetic differentiation among sampled localities. Its multilocus results suggested northern, southern, western and possibly central clusters. This is population structure inferred from selected markers and sites, not a complete species tree or evolutionary history.
