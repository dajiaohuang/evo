## Yingjiang locality records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Male holotype SYS a006524 came from Xiaolangsu Village, Tongbiguan, Yingjiang, Yunnan, at 700 m. Additional examined material included Xiaolangsu specimens and seven males from Nabang at about 760 m. Citing Yang and colleagues (2018), the monograph summarized known distribution as the vicinity of the Yingjiang type locality at 700–1200 m. These are recorded localities and a cited summary, not a complete range survey or physiological elevation limits.

## Adult measurements exclude the listed juvenile
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5pjx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported twelve adult males at 24.2–28.1 mm snout–vent length and two females at 28.8–38.9 mm. A separately examined juvenile is not included in those adult denominators. Diagnostic characters included absence of vomerine ridges, vomerine teeth and subarticular tubercles, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with moderate lateral fringes. Breeding males had a subgular vocal sac without nuptial pads or spines. These sampled anatomical observations do not establish population-wide size bounds or a breeding calendar.
