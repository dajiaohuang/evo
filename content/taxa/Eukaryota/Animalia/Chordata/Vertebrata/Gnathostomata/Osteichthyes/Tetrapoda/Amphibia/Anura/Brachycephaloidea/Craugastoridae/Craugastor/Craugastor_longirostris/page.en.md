## A mixed-source urban and periurban checklist record
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13149743-6bcf8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The 2026 Guayaquil checklist lists Craugastor longirostris in conservation areas; unprotected remnant native vegetation. The source combines January 2008–February 2025 field surveys with museum, database, literature and critically screened secondary records. Its focus includes remnant forests and revegetated parks in the urban/periurban footprint, with selected near-boundary areas retained; it is not a uniform present-day census across all 4,113.89 km² of Guayaquil. Table 3’s presence categories are not individual counts, breeding records, occupancy probabilities or independently checked voucher identifications. The city-center elevation cannot be assigned as every specimen’s collection height.

## Global and Ecuadorian risk categories preserve different scales
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13149743-6bcf8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
Table 3 assigns Craugastor longirostris IUCN LC (Least Concern) and Ecuadorian Red List LC (Least Concern). The Methods refer to IUCN 2025 and the national amphibian assessment published in 2021; these are source-reported historical assignments, not independently verified current categories or equivalent local extinction probabilities. LC does not establish urban safety, NE is not demonstrated absence of threat, and DD denotes an information gap. Differences between geographic assessment scales do not alone prove an error or quantify local population decline. The source’s 63 mixed-herpetofauna conservation totals must not be treated as nineteen-amphibian-only counts.
