## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows the COL26.8 accepted name Rhinopithecus brelichi. The English common name comes from the locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 record; the Chinese rendering is editorial. The selected studies concern social organization, elevational activity and genetic samples at Fanjingshan, Guizhou; they are not a synchronized multi-site population survey.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A field project begun in 1979 included ten months of intensive observation in 1991. The study described family groups typically containing 5–10 monkeys centered on one adult male; several groups could travel in large, loose bands and occasionally aggregate into groups of more than 400. These are early Fanjingshan observations, not current social structure or abundance.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
From September 2011 to August 2013, researchers at Fanjingshan National Nature Reserve recorded activity between 1,432 and 2,100 m and documented feeding on 104 plant species. Autumn and winter use spanned a broader elevational band, while activity from April to September was more concentrated at 1,700–1,900 m. These seasonal patterns apply to that site and observation period.

## genetics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
A mitochondrial control-region study analyzed 141 fecal samples collected from wild monkeys and reported five haplotypes. This describes one genetic marker and sample set; it cannot by itself establish current abundance, population health or adaptive capacity.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The selected behavioral and elevational studies are from one reserve, and the genetic result comes from one sample set and one mitochondrial marker. Early findings are not generalized to range-wide patterns, current abundance or a complete conservation assessment. Not externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
