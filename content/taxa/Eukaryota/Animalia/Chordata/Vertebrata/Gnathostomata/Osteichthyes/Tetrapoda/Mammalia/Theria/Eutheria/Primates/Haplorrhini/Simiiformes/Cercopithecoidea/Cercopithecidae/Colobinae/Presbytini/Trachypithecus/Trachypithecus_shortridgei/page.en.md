## distribution_and_survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Community interviews in 2010–2012 and field surveys in 2012–2013 covered the Dulong and Nu river valleys of Yunnan, China. Interviews identified about 19 groups in the Dulong Valley; field teams repeatedly observed two groups directly at two sites. Interviews west of the Nu River in the Gaoligong Mountains reported 12 solitary individuals, but field surveys in that region produced no direct records. The authors estimated about 250–370 langurs in China from the study’s interviews and observations. That estimate is bounded by the survey dates, interview evidence and coverage; it is not a current census.
