## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID S7B links the accepted Allocebus trichotis page (BVDB). Start with the 2023 northeastern Madagascar survey to separate direct sightings from presence-only habitat models, then read the two Analamazaotra studies for radio-tracked home ranges and tree-hole use. Schüßler and colleagues describe Allocebus as a monotypic genus; the linked page is a species reading route, not a genus-wide ecological synthesis.
