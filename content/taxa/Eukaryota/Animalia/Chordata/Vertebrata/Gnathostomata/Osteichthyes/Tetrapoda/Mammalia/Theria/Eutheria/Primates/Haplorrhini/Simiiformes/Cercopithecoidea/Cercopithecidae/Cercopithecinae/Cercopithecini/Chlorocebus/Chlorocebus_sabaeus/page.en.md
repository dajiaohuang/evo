## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Surveys in southern Guinea-Bissau and the Bijagós Archipelago recorded 18 green-monkey groups; 76 fecal samples from 11 locations were separately identified using molecular methods. Direct observations covered mangroves, forests, woodland and herbaceous savanna. These records add occurrence and mitochondrial-diversity information for the country, but are not a complete range or habitat survey.
