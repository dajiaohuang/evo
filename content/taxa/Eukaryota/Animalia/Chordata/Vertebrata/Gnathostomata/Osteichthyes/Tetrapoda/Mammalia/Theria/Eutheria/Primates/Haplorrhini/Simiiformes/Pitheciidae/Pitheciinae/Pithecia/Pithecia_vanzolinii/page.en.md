## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh elevated Vanzolini's bald-faced saki from a subspecies of P. irrorata in 2014. Serrano-Villavicencio and colleagues re-examined pelage in 2019 using 54 skins from nine museums across the group's range. They recognized two morphologically diagnosable, geographically isolated groups corresponding to P. irrorata and P. vanzolinii, while placing three other named forms within P. irrorata. This is a museum-based taxonomic comparison, not a field test of isolation or gene flow.
