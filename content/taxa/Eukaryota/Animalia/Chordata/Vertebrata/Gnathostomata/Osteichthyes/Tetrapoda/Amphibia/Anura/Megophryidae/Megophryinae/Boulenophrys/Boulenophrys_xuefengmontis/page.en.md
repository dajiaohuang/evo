## The 2023 description and Xuefeng mountain localities
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9r8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The monograph described this species as new in 2023. Male holotype SYS a007226 was collected on 15 June 2018 from Xuefeng Mountain, Hongjiang, Hunan, at about 1280 m; the paratypes shared its collection data. The name refers to that type locality. The distribution statement included Xuefeng and Yunshan Mountain, Wugang, at 1200–1300 m in western Hunan. One June collection date is not a breeding-season calendar, and the named localities do not establish continuous occupancy.

## Eight type adults with unwebbed toes
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9r8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported six adult males at 37.0–38.3 mm snout–vent length and two females at 45.3–48.9 mm. It described no vomerine ridges or teeth and no toe webbing or lateral fringes. Breeding males bore dense black nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. These are diagnostic observations of the small type sample, not universal size bounds, a population sex ratio or evidence for the functional effects of the toe or finger structures.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-lx9r8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys xuefengmontis as without a vomerine ridge. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
