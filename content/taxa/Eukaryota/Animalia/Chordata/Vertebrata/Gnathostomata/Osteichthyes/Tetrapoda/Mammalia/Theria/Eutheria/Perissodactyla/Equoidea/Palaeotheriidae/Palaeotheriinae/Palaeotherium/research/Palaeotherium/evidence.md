# Palaeotherium

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Palaeotherium medium is sampled in the late Eocene at La Débruge, and separate British range data place regional Palaeotherium loss near the Grande Coupure without dating a global extinction instant.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Two primary regional records independently constrain late Eocene occurrence and disappearance near the turnover. High confidence remains regional and does not convert a sampled last appearance into a global extinction instant.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
The profile uses Palaeotherium at genus rank within Palaeotheriidae, while ecological detail is explicitly restricted to the sampled species P. medium.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
Primary species attribution and the regional range study support the genus context without collapsing all species into P. medium.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
In the sampled Palaeotherium medium, molar wear facets, high occlusal relief and mixed sharp and rounded cusps provide the morphological basis for dietary inference; limb proportions are not claimed here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study measures dental form and wear directly. Removing unsupported limb analogies keeps morphology at the observed specimen level.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
The La Débruge Palaeotherium medium sample is consistent with leaf browsing plus a smaller fruit or abrasive-seed component; habitat and locomotor behavior are not inferred from tooth wear alone.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
High confidence applies to the replicated wear proxies, while the explicit non-inferences prevent dental data from carrying unrelated ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
This profile represents western European study records from La Débruge and the Hampshire Basin rather than a complete genus-wide occurrence distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
Two primary regional studies directly establish the French and British contexts; broader geography is intentionally not asserted.
