# Eritherium azzouzorum

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Holotype skull MNHN PM69 and fourteen referred specimens from Sidi Chennane test primitive proboscidean dental and cranial anatomy in a source-bounded morphology matrix.
<!-- /evo:text -->
