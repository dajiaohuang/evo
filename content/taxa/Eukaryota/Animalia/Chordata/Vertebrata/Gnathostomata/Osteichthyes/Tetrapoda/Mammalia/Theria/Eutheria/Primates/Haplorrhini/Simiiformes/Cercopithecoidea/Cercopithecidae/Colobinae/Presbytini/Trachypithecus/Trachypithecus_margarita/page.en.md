## phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the 2008 study of the silvered-langur group, sampled *T. margarita* formed one of five main monophyletic mitochondrial clades. The authors sequenced a 573-bp cytochrome-b fragment from 115 animals, 68 of known origin. They proposed treating the five clades as separate species but did not resolve their branching order. This page reports the study's genetic grouping and taxonomic proposal; one mitochondrial fragment cannot reconstruct the species' full history or range.
