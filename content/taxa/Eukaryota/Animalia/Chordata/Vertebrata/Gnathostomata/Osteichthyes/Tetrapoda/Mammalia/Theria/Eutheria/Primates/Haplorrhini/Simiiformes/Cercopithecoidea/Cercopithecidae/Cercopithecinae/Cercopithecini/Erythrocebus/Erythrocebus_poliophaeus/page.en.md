## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID 3BDH6; MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Blue Nile Patas Monkey. Gippoliti’s 2017 review revived E. poliophaeus from synonymy using geographic separation, pelage and historical type material. The proposal was morphological and historical, not a molecular delimitation study; the MDD-cited 2020 assessment classified the taxon as Data Deficient because its range was poorly known.

## fieldRecords

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Yirga et al. reported two groups during a preliminary 28-hour survey at Anbesa Chaka in western Ethiopia in 2010; reported counts were 33 and 23. Their paper called the animals E. patas and suggested a possible distinctive subspecies. Gippoliti later treated the photographs and localities as important evidence for the southern limit of E. poliophaeus, while describing the taxon as effectively unstudied in its native range. The original survey is not a population estimate or definitive species identification.

## rangeEvidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Gippoliti’s review places documented records in the Blue Nile basin of western Ethiopia and adjoining Sudan, but emphasizes their scarcity and calls for field surveys. It discusses the type region and a few Ethiopian sites; it does not provide a modern range-wide survey, population count or genetic test of the proposed boundaries.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 2010 group records were published under the broad name E. patas; their later placement with E. poliophaeus is Gippoliti’s interpretation, not the original authors’ species identification. The 2017 proposal relies on morphology, photographs and sparse locality history, and explicitly calls for more fieldwork. The 2019 IUCN evidence was Data Deficient. The Chinese common name is editorial; this page has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
