## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Marsh treated Rylands' bald-faced saki as a new species in 2014 based on pelage differences. Orsini and colleagues reported three observations in the same semideciduous forest fragment in Mato Grosso: one adult male in 2013 and two females in 2014, adding a southern record. Serrano-Villavicencio and colleagues compared 54 skins from nine museum collections across the group in 2019; they recognized two diagnosable pelage groups corresponding to geographically isolated populations and treated P. rylandsi as a synonym of P. irrorata. COL26.8 still lists it as accepted, while the current MDD lists it as a synonym; this page presents both treatments.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Three local observations and a comparative museum-pelage study address different questions; neither is a population trend or modern range survey. COL and MDD taxonomic treatments differ; this page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
