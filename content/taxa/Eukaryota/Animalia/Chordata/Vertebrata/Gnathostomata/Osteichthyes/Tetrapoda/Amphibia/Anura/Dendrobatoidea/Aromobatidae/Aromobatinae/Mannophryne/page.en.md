## Reproductive function and ancestry remain hypotheses
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->

It did not establish an adhesive function or a causal effect on ovulation or oviposition in these genus members. Inferences of origins and losses use a fixed, pruned earlier tree and a limited ingroup sample; alternative optimizations remain possible. Type II–IV similarities also leave questions about developmental relationships and gland homology open. These interpretations should not be converted into experimentally verified genus-wide biology.
