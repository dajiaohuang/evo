## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Amboseli National Park in Kenya, Altmann studied foraging by yearling female yellow baboons and compared individual diet nutrients with energy-maximizing optimal diets. Every studied diet fell short of its modeled energy optimum, yet a combination of protein intake above requirements and closeness to the modeled energy target predicted reproductive lifespan, numbers of infants and juveniles produced, and offspring survival to adulthood. The paper links dietary quality with lifetime reproductive outcomes in a local cohort; it is not a species-wide menu or a causal feeding experiment.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

Source-linked local study introduction; not externally expert-reviewed.
