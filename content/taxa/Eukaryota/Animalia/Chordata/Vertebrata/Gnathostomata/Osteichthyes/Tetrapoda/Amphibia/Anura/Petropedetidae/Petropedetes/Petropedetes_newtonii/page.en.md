## Accepted name and placement

The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Records reconsidered under a revised name
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5999683-776cx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The revision supported P. newtonii on Bioko and the southern Cameroonian coast, while distinguishing older continental records assigned to P. vulpiae. Its historical locality discussion incorporates earlier collections and genetic studies; old labels alone do not assign all mainland Petropedetes to the revised species.

## The 2003 Bioko field observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5999683-776cx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Field notes from late November to early December 2003 described mostly dusk collections near shallow streams, with frogs on rocks, leaves or branches. Three juveniles were found by day, including one away from immediate water. No calls, amplexus, egg laying or offspring care were observed. These records preserve differences among individuals and do not establish a universal stream-distance or activity rule.
