## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
During a 17-month study in northern Lopé Reserve, Gabon, one group of C. cephus lived in a 9-ha forest patch surrounded by savanna. A young male C. nictitans was in permanent association with the group. The study compared primates using the fragment with conspecific groups in nearby continuous forest; its findings concern a particular fragment community and do not estimate C. cephus abundance or range.
