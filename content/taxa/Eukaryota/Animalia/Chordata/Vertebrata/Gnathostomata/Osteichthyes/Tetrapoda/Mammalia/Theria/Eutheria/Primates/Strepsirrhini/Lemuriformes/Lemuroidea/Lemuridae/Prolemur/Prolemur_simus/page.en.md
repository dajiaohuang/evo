## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 places this species in the separate genus Prolemur. The 2002 mitochondrial study and the 2010 cyanide comparison used Hapalemur simus or H. (Prolemur) simus. Those historical combinations remain attached to their respective studies and do not replace the pinned checklist identity.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A survey designed to improve distribution knowledge visited six sites in eastern Madagascar. It found characteristic feeding signs at five and a direct sighting at one of those sites. One site extended 45 km north of the then-known extant range; two others lay in a forest corridor between previously known northern and southern populations. Signs and local sightings support further survey work but do not alone establish resident populations at every site or a complete continuous range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2013 bamboo study documented the distribution and field identification of large-culmed bamboos in Madagascar as context for a major food resource of the greater bamboo lemur. Its review of field studies describes diets dominated by bamboo, especially large-culmed Cathariostachys and Valiha; the plant survey is not a range-wide dietary census of the lemur.
