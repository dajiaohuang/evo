## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
African wild asses should be distinguished from domestic donkeys. This biological source concerns Somali wild asses, including striped legs and feeding in arid habitats; subspecies traits are not automatically generalized to all African wild asses.
