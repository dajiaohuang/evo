## Tiantai and neighboring Xikou records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5ghc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The male holotype was listed from Huading on Tiantai Mountain at about 680 m. The monograph examined the type series and five additional males from Xikou, Ningbo, at about 570 m; its cited distribution summary covered those eastern Zhejiang localities at 500–700 m. This sampled geography does not establish continuous occupancy between the towns or the present full range.

## A measured sample with selected external characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5ghc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The diagnosis gave adult male snout–vent lengths of 30.7–33.6 mm for nine specimens and 37.5–41.4 mm for two females. It described an unnotched tongue, visible tympanum, no vomerine ridges or teeth, granular and tuberculate dorsal skin, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow fringes. The measurements retain sex and sample size; these selected features alone are not a complete key or proof of uniformity across populations.
