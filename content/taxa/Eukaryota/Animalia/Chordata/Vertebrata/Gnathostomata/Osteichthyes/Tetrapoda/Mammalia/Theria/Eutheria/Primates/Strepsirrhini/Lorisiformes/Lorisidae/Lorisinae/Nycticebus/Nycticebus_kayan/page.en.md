# Nycticebus kayan

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD currently displays Kayan Slow Loris and records holotype AMNH M-106012 from Peleben, East Kalimantan, Indonesia. Munds and colleagues described N. kayan in the associated taxonomic study. The type locality and holotype anchor the name but do not identify every animal reported elsewhere.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Munds and colleagues found that facial-marking combinations clustered geographically and used this evidence to support N. kayan among Bornean animals previously grouped under N. menagensis. They called for genetic testing and refinement. A 2023 study found that several pelage-named samples did not align one-to-one with molecular lineages, so this page does not present the diagnosis as a final resolution.
<!-- /evo:text -->
