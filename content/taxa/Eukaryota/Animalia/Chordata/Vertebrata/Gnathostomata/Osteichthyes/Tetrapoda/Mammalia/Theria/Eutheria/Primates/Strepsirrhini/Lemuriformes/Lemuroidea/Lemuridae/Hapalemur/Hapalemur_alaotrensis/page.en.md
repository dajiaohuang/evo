## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Hapalemur alaotrensis. The current MDD display name is Lac Alaotra Bamboo Lemur, with reed-beds around Lake Alaotra in Madagascar given as its distribution. Mutschler and colleagues used the then-current combination H. griseus alaotrensis for their 1999 survey. This profile keeps the checklist identity separate from the older study name and does not treat either summary as a complete distribution survey.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Mutschler and colleagues surveyed in February–March 1994 and 1999 using direct observation from canoes. The same team used the same methods and compared group encounter rates by site as an index of relative density. Rates at most sites were at least half those recorded in 1994. After accounting for unusually low water during the 1999 drought, the authors estimated an approximately 30% decline over those five years. This was a historical estimate based on encounter rates, not a current population count.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page summarizes historical surveys around Alaotra only. The survey used group encounter rates rather than a direct lake-wide count, and the paper used an older subspecies combination; the Chinese common name is editorial. These pages are not externally expert-reviewed.
