# Trachypithecus auratus

## Phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In a mitochondrial study of the silvered-langur group, the authors sequenced a 573-bp cytochrome-b fragment from 115 animals, 68 of known origin. The sampled *T. auratus* formed one of five main monophyletic clades. The authors estimated a near-radiation among those clades about 0.95–1.25 million years ago and proposed treating them as separate species. That estimate concerns the modeled separation among clades, not the origin of this species or present-day population structure.
<!-- /evo:text -->
