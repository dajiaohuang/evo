## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2026 study tracked 24 collared dwarf lemurs in the Andasivodihazo forest fragment at Tsinjoarivo: 13 Crossley's and 11 Sibree's dwarf lemurs. Observations before and after hibernation in 2011–2014 found smaller home ranges, shorter nightly movements, and more use of forest edges and degraded habitat by Crossley's dwarf lemurs at this site; researchers also recorded a more diverse diet.
