# Presbytis canicrus

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In 2011, Lhota et al. recorded Miller's Langur at two mineral springs 4.2 km apart in Wehea Forest, East Kalimantan, using concealed observation and camera traps. The paper used the older combination P. hosei canicrus and reported that these observations extended known records westward to Wehea.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Ecological evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The mineral springs provided opportunities for direct observations; the paper noted group composition and used cameras to supplement evidence of visits. Observations concentrated at two sites cannot be converted into forest-wide density and did not resolve the species' wider range boundaries.
<!-- /evo:text -->
