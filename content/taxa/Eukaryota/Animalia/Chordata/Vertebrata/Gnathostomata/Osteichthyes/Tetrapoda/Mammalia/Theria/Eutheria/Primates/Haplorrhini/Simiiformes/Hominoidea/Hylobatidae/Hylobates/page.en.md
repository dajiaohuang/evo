# Hylobates

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Read these Hylobates pages as a route through unlike field methods, not as a standardized genus comparison. Begin by contrasting the local, pooled-opinion estimate for Abbott’s gray gibbon at Gunung Niut with the 2005 auditory and behavioral survey of Bornean white-bearded gibbons at Sabangau. Then compare daily activity records for East Bornean gray gibbons at Danum Valley with two studies of vocal behavior: known Kloss’s gibbon groups on Siberut and location-specific male-song playbacks in H. muelleri. Finish with a model-based Javan gibbon estimate from the Dieng Mountains and the older protected-area survey of pileated gibbons in Thailand. These studies differ in date, scale, and method; their estimates are not directly comparable, and this path is not a complete review of Hylobates.
