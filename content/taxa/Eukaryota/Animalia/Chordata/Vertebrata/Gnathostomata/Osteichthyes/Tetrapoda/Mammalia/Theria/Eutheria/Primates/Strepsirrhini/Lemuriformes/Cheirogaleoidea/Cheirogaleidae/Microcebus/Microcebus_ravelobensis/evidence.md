## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
In the same wild Mariarano sample, captured M. ravelobensis were weighed and age-classified; adults were defined using body mass of at least 41 g together with other morphometric parameters. A logistic model estimated a 390-m depth of edge influence for local body-mass variation, but the overall edge-versus-interior body-mass difference was not significant; the female-only contrast was a statistical trend (p=0.052), with no corresponding male trend. This local response does not establish a species-wide body-mass range, diagnostic morphology, or sexual dimorphism.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
At Mariarano Classified Forest, four 1-km transects perpendicular to forest edges were sampled in 2017-2018 with 2,424 trap-nights, yielding 226 captured M. ravelobensis. A logistic model estimated a 340-m depth of edge influence for capture-based local abundance, but the overall edge-versus-interior abundance difference was not significant; the male-only difference was a statistical trend (p=0.082), and the female comparison was not significant (p=0.843). Within these transects, abundance was negatively associated with measured densities of large trees, small trees, and saplings. These results describe one local sample and do not establish a causal edge effect, total population size, or a species-wide pattern.
<!-- /evo:text -->
