## phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2006 study's tree placed the white-footed sportive lemur and L. ruficaudatus in one major lineage, while mitochondrial and cytogenetic evidence supported their separate species status. This is a relationship under that study's sampling and analysis, not a complete account of variation across the species' population.
