## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Read these Propithecus profiles by evidence type and region, not as a genus-wide ranking. Start with the dated northern surveys for Perrier’s and crowned sifakas, then compare Daraina diet sampling and Decken’s local territorial observations in the west. Continue to Marojejy sleeping-tree choice and Ranomafana space use, then compare the Tsinjoarivo feeding study with the wild-versus-captive gut microbiome work on Coquerel’s sifaka. The studies use different years, methods and sample sizes; their measurements cannot be treated as a standardized comparison. Several profiles are single-study notes, not complete species accounts, and this path is not an exhaustive genus review.
