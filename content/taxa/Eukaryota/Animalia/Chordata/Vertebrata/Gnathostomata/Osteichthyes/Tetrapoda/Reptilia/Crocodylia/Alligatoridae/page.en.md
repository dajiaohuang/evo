# Alligatoridae


## From skin structures to local sensory recordings

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Alligatoridae route compares American-alligator tactile recordings from 2012 with spectacled-caiman multi-stimulus recordings from 2013. Integumentary sensory organs (ISOs) are concentrated on the head in this material, while postcranial skin can retain sensory nerves; no visible spots does not mean no touch. Methods, juvenile samples and stimuli differed, so numerical results are not pooled into a family mean sensitivity. Other alligators, caimans and black caimans retain their own sources rather than borrowing these two species’ electrode results.
