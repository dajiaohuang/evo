# Lepilemur scottorum

## Morphology and field descriptions

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Masoala description reported a body mass of about 0.88 kg, mostly reddish-brown fur, and a long, dense coat. A diffuse black dorsal stripe ended around mid-body. The face was whitish gray, with white cheeks and eyebrows; the tail darkened toward a black tip. These traits describe the sample, not the full range of variation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Known localities and evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is in Masoala National Park in northeastern Madagascar. The paper then knew the species only from Masiaposa Forest inside the park. Surveys were still needed to establish eastern and northern limits; this local record is not a current range map or population estimate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Mass, pelage, and range reflect the 2008 description sample; no current population estimate or complete ecological range is inferred. The Chinese name is editorial, and this guide is.
