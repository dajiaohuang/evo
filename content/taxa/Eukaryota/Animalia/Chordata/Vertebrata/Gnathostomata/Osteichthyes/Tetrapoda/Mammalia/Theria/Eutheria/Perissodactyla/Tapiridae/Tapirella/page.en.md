## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This catalogue places Baird’s tapir in this genus. The biological account uses Tapirus bairdii. Its proboscis and leaf-feeding observations concern that species; the older genus usage is not imposed on the entire family.
