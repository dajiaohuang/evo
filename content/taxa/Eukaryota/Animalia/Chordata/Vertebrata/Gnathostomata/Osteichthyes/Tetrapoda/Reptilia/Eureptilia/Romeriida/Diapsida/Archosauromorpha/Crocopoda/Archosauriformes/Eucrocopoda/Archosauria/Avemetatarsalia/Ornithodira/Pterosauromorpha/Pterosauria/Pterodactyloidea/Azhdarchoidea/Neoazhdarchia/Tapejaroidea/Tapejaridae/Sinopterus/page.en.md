# Sinopterus


## Read two catalogue entries alongside one revision

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The fixed catalogue offers S. dongi and S. gui entries. The 2023 revision treats the latter as a synonym of the former and names the other morphotype Huaxiadraco corollatus. Entries are database work records and the revision is a named scientific interpretation; entry counts do not establish confirmed biological-species counts.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Check skeletal growth before names and flight subjects

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Begin with the immature holotype, anatomical combination and methods in [S. dongi](Sinopterus_dongi/page.en.md), then preservation and synonymy in [S. gui](Sinopterus_gui/page.en.md). Small juvenile crests do not automatically indicate females, morphological clustering is not genealogy, and the very young Nemicolopterus specimen cannot be confidently assigned to either species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route combines occurrence guides for two fixed catalogue entries with the 2023 revision, without supplying a complete descendant catalogue or common ecological synthesis. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. The 2023 front matter, Introduction, Methods through nomenclatural acts, two holotype accounts, statistical results, species interpretations and Tables 4–5, crest sections, Nemicolopterus comments, phylogenetic results, systematic accounts and Conclusions were read. Other specimen accounts, detailed anatomical-variation discussion and part of Methods remain unread. Images, specimens, supplementary measurements and matrices were not independently inspected or recalculated. Original 2003 descriptions, complete life history, diet remain incomplete; synonymy, juvenile size and identification retain their sources.
