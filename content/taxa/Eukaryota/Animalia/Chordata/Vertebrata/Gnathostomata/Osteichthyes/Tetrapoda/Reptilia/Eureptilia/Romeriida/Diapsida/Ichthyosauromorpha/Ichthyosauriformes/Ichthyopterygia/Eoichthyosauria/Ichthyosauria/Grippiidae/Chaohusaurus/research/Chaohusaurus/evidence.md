# Chaohusaurus

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Chaohusaurus is anchored by maternal specimen AGM I-1 from the Procolumbites Zone at Majiashan; the locality record does not establish a global genus range, an exact first appearance or ancestral status.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Specimen association and geological setting are directly documented. The claim deliberately omits genus-wide endpoints and reproductive-behavior extrapolation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Chaohusaurus is assigned to the Early Triassic ichthyopterygians in Motani et al.'s study using delayed ossification of carpals and tarsals, dental morphology, vertebral count and hypophalangeal flippers; species-level taxonomy may require revision as more specimens are prepared.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The paper states the diagnostic character combination and assignment procedure directly; the species-level caveat is retained rather than treating the genus assignment as an immutable taxonomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
The profiled Chaohusaurus record is limited to south Majiashan Quarry at Chaohu, Anhui, China, where the Spathian Nanlinghu Formation is exposed; this named collection does not establish a complete geographic distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The primary paper names the quarry, region and exposed formation in its specimen methods. The claim is explicitly a locality boundary and does not infer a global range from one collection.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Maternal specimen AGM I-1 preserves an articulated Chaohusaurus skeleton with two embryos in the body and a neonate adjacent to the pelvis, while the genus assignment uses its documented dental, vertebral and flipper characters.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The specimen association and preserved elements are illustrated and described in the results. High confidence is restricted to the named slab and diagnostic character set, not to a universal body plan.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
AGM I-1 directly documents pregnancy in an Early Triassic ichthyosauromorph; the head-first embryo orientation and a proposed terrestrial origin of viviparity are comparative interpretations, not directly observed behaviour or an ancestral state for all ichthyosaurs.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
Embryo association is direct specimen evidence, whereas delivery posture and reproductive-history conclusions depend on orientation, phylogeny and comparative reasoning; the claim keeps those evidence levels separate.
<!-- /evo:text -->
