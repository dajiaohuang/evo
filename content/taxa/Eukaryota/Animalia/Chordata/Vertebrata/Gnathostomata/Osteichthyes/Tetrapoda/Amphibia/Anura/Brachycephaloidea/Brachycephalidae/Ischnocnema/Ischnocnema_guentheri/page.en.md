## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-3q4rq
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Ischnocnema guentheri as direct-developing and its oviposition as terrestrial. In the article, direct development means no free-living larval stage, with a froglet hatching rather than a free-living tadpole; it does not mean that embryonic development is absent or that humidity is unnecessary. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.
