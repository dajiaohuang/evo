## A karst stream with contrasting forest cover
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6536485-3tk97
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2019 description knew this species only from Qingshan Village in Zhaozishan Nature Reserve, Bijie, Guizhou. It found frogs along a clear rocky stream at 1670–1750 m elevation, approximately 2 m wide and 20–30 cm deep. Broad-leaved forest surrounded the stream below 1700 m and coniferous forest above it. This describes the study’s locality and stream, without establishing the present total range or a strict forest-type requirement.
