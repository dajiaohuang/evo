# Eudromia elegans


## The material behind this genome

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Sackton and colleagues (2019) added a genome for Eudromia elegans. NCBI master record PTEX00000000 belongs to PRJNA433110 and records sex as male; voucher ROM:ORN:44215; tissue blood. These fields identify assembly material, not species-wide or wild-population statistics. Missing tissue or locality fields are not inferred here. The main and companion phylogenetic papers use the same genome set, not two independent population samples.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Flighted tinamous are nested within palaeognaths

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
This species is one of four tinamous in the 2019 analyses. Sequence analyses recover tinamous as sister to little bush moa, nested among lineages traditionally called ratites. Flightless palaeognaths therefore do not form a natural group excluding tinamous. Their flighted condition informs comparisons of flight-loss history; the genome tree does not measure this species’ flight distance, frequency or field behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## One sequence in a chicken forelimb reporter assay

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Sackton and colleagues select candidate region mCE967994 in a TEAD1 intron, place bird sequences in a GFP reporter system and electroporate chicken embryos at HH19–20, examining them about 24 hours later. The Eudromia elegans sequence gives 6 strong, 3 partial and 1 absent-expression outcomes in 10 assays; the chicken sequence gives 11 strong, 4 partial and 1 absent in 16 assays. These are experiments on one candidate region in a chicken host, not wild individuals of this species. Nine positive outcomes include both strong and partial expression. This result neither recreates flight nor excludes other regulatory effects in the species’ natural context.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Relative weights of food categories

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/text/en -->
The archive’s nonzero food weights are seeds, nuts and spores included in this category 40%; invertebrates 30%; other plant material (the source category also includes fungi) 30%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/14/text/en -->

They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
This batch uses historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are reported compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the presence of same-name sparse rows support the association here; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components from the reported summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed here, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed.
