## A group definition is not an individual measurement

primary-pmc9985417-788fp

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
The revised group is described by a short snout, robust body, FingerI shorter thanII, ToeV longer thanIII and absent cranial crests, with body-size ranges pooled at group level. The paper states that no unambiguous phenotypic synapomorphies are known. Its female15.8–34.6mm and male12.6–20.5mm SVL ranges are not newly measured ranges for Pristimantis gladiator. Digital discs are generally narrow in this definition, with floridus explicitly exceptional; its phylogenetic position remains untested.
