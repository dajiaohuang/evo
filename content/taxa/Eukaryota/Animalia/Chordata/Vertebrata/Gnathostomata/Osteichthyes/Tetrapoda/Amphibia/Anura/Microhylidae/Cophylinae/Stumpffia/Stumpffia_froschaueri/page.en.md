## Three northwestern forest blocks with different evidence
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7285848-t8vcw
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description attributed the species to Anketsakely, Ankarafa and Angorony in northwestern Madagascar, across reported elevations of 100–340 m. Angorony was assigned using deposited DNA sequences from specimens not examined by the authors. That sequence attribution remains distinct from their field observations, and the three blocks do not establish continuous occupancy through the degraded surrounding habitat.

## Daytime leaf-litter activity
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7285848-t8vcw
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
At Anketsakely and Ankarafa, active individuals and quietly calling males were found by day in forest-floor litter, in relatively undisturbed forest. These observations identify surveyed activity and substrates, without establishing an exclusive daily schedule or the species’ absence from every disturbed forest.
