## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID 4LR5 connects the new G. gallarum (3F2D4) and G. matschiei (6JW34) pages with existing G. moholi (3F2DD) and G. senegalensis (3F2DM) pages. For G. gallarum, compare the 2004 Meru/Kora encounter records with the 2011 north-coast Kenya observations, keeping the latter’s tentative Agarbul identifications distinct. For G. matschiei, read the 2025 museum-specimen and range-boundary reassessment alongside the historical Uganda forest records it summarizes. The studies differ in sites, dates and methods; this path is not a complete genus roster or genus-wide ecological synthesis.
