## taxonomy_and_evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists *T. ebenus* as an accepted species. MDD gives the English common name Black Langur; its name history shows that Brandon-Jones first described the name in a subspecies combination in 1995. Later works differ in how they place it among populations close to *T. francoisi*, *T. hatinhensis* and *T. laotum*. A review of the Lao forms also describes the group’s names and species boundaries as unstable, with few explicit records and some older records that are difficult to identify. The available material supports a page about nomenclature and evidence gaps, but not a separate account of current range, ecology or abundance.

## catalogue-profile / limitations
