## Two supported Taiwanese sites
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11886882-v2m5p
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2025 account knew the species from Lienhuachih at about 670 m and Xiangshan near Sun Moon Lake at about 800 m. It left a historical Jiaoxi report unconfirmed after repeated searches. The supported sites and the unresolved historical report are kept separate, without assuming that two known sites establish the entire distribution.

## Mud nests and a small reported egg sample
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11886882-v2m5p
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The natural-history account described males excavating mud nests close to water and calling inside them. It reported clustered eggs in six nests, ranging from 31 to 51 per nest. The account combines its species description with cited earlier observations; these reported counts are not a species-wide clutch-size rule. It described tadpoles remaining in nests until rain or rising water allowed them to enter adjacent water bodies, without establishing one fixed schedule for all nests.
