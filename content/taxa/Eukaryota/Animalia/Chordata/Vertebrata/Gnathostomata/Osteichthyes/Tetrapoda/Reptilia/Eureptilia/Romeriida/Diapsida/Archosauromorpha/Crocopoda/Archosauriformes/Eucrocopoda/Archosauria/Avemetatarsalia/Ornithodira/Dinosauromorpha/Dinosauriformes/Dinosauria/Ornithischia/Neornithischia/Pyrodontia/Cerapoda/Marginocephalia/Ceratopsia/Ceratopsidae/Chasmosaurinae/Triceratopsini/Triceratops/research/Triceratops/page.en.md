# Triceratops

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A latest Cretaceous ceratopsid represented here by a stratigraphically controlled Hell Creek skull sample; species boundaries, ontogeny and evolutionary mode remain competing interpretations rather than a single resolved lineage.
