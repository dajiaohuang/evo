## Dapan sample measurements and anatomical characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v59h9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The examined Dapan series contained four adult males with snout–vent lengths of 33.0–34.8 mm and two females at 42.8–45.3 mm. The diagnosis described a slightly concealed tympanum, vomerine ridges without teeth, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow lateral fringes. Breeding males had black nuptial spines on finger I and II pads and a subgular vocal sac. These measurements concern the examined sample rather than all separately listed types or every population.

## Missing measurement does not remove a designated paratype
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v59h9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The remarks noted that the original designation listed seven male paratypes, but its measurement tables included only six. The monograph retained CIB 20180514007 as a paratype because it was explicitly designated, despite missing measurement data. It also reported that an arc-shaped swelling above the anal region, mentioned for the holotype, was not observed in its examined material and suggested individual variation. These are attributed nomenclatural and morphological interpretations; the omitted measurement is not reconstructed and the swelling is not presented as a universal diagnostic character.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v59h9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys xianjuensis as with heels overlapping when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
