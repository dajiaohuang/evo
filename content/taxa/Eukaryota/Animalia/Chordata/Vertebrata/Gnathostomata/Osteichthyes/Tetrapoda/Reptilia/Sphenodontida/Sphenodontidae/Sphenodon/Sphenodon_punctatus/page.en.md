## island habitat and feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/topic -->
island habitat and feeding
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/sourceIds/0 -->
docNaturalHistory
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The undated Department of Conservation account places naturally wild populations on islands off the northeastern North Island and in the Marlborough Sounds. It describes seabird-colony habitats and mainly invertebrate prey, alongside lizards and seabird eggs or chicks. Rats threaten eggs and young. These are account-level descriptions, without species-wide diet proportions or current abundance.

Gemmell et al. (2020) reported a 4.3-Gb reference assembly and 17,448 annotated genes. Figure 3d compares nine Little Barrier, nine Stephens and ten North Brother Island animals, finding population structure. Separate management for North Brother is supported; a species split or current head count is not established.

## Limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This source-linked guide combines an official account with one genome study. It has no species dossier and no external expert review. A complete dated range, reliable current abundance, island-specific quantitative diets and reproductive parameters remain source-insufficient in this batch. The official account is undated; 2020 study data are not a 2026 census. Collection dates for the island samples are not documented in this guide. The Chinese name is editorial.
