## research

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Field evidence for Wolf's monkey is sparse in the material used here. A Lomako Forest census study included it in a six-monkey comparison of habitat use and mixed-species associations; the authors described its group sizes as similar to those reported from other sites. A later ground-camera survey in Lomami recorded one independent C. wolfi event among a broader survey of arboreal monkeys. One camera event is only a detection, not an abundance measure or behavioural profile. These studies support a local research note, while species-specific diet, demography and range limits remain poorly resolved here.
