## Páramo shelters and nighttime vegetation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6687670-scc8y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The revision reported two eastern-Andean páramo localities in Tungurahua, Ecuador. Daytime individuals were beneath rocks, among moss or bunch grasses; at night they were active on low vegetation up to 80 cm high. Additional populations in largely unexplored Llanganates National Park were considered possible, without being confirmed. The records distinguish shelter and active encounters rather than a complete activity budget or range.
