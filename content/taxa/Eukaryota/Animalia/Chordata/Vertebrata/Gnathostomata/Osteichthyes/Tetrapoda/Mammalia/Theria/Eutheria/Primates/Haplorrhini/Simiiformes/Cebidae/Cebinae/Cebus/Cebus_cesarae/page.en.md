## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts RYZ7 as Cebus cesarae Hershkovitz, 1949. MDD v2.5 lists the 2013 combination C. cesarae as a synonym of C. albifrons. This page records that disagreement and does not automatically treat regional evidence under older names as evidence for a modern, stable species boundary.

## classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Ruiz-García et al. (2010) sequenced 696 bp of mitochondrial COII from 118 white-fronted capuchins under the older taxonomy and identified northern Colombian malitiosus, versicolor-pleei-cesarae and leucocephalus groups, but did not resolve relationships among the main groups. A larger 2019 mitochondrial and nuclear-microsatellite analysis proposed further complexity among older names including cesarae and versicolor. These papers document taxonomic history; they do not provide independent ecology or a current population estimate for this page.
