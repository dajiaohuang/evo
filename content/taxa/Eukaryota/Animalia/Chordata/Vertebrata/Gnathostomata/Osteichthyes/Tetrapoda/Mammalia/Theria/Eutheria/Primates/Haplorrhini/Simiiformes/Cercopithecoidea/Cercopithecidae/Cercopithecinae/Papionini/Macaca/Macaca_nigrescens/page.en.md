## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the 1987–1988 survey, reported M. nigrescens densities were 15.5 and 16.4 monkeys/km² in central and western Dumoga-Bone, and 8.15 monkeys/km² in peripheral areas. From that survey, the authors estimated fewer than 34,000 animals in Dumoga-Bone. These are historical site values and an estimate, not a current population census.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The authors' density and population estimate came from the 1987–1988 Dumoga-Bone survey; it cannot be treated as a present reserve population or a range-wide estimate. The English common-name label follows MDD; the Chinese label is an editorial translation. This guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
