# Sporophila crassirostris

## Separate species and subspecies targets through older genera

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 organizes this page at Sporophila crassirostris (9G8LP). BirdLife 2020 row 8207 and eBird 2021 row 9841 share the name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-2824A1D9, with type 1BL to 1eBird; BirdTree row 9782 gives Oryzoborus crassirostris, with type 1BL to 1BT. Exact catalogue synonym 6SZGL points to this accepted species. Name relationships locate records without establishing identical species boundaries across dates.

## Separate total birds from complete sets

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology comprises 13 birds: 6 female, 5 male and 2 unsexed; 3 complete sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 10.2 mm, tarsus 18.6 mm, unflattened wing 66.9 mm, tail 59.6 mm and hand-wing index 16.2 are historical row means. Total birds exceed complete sets; not every bird has a full set of fields. Inference=NO does not replace each field’s sampling denominator. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed; beak length supplies neither bite force nor the ability to process particular foods.

## Read seed niche and trophic level separately

These are separate category definitions, not measured proportions for this species. Underlying diet papers were not read; seed species, seasonal diets, shelling actions and beak bite force remain unsupported.

## Diet does not replace primary lifestyle

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->

Habitat=Shrubland; density code 2 denotes semi-open vegetation, not bird abundance. Migration=2 is partially migratory, which can include a minority moving long distances, most birds making short-distance movements, nomadism or distinct altitudinal migration; the row does not specify which applies. A shared food niche does not establish shared foraging strata, social activity or seasonal movements. Historical map fields are uninterpreted here and do not supply current range-wide distributions or movement routes.
