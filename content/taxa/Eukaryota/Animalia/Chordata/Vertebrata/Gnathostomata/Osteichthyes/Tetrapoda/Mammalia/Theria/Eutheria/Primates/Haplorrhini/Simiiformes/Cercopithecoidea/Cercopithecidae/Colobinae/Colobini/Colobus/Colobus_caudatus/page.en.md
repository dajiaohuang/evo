# Colobus caudatus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists Colobus caudatus as an accepted species. The cited field paper uses the subspecies combination C. guereza caudatus, so this page treats its evidence as historical and local records for that named taxon, not as evidence about the broader C. guereza.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Research observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Butynski and de Jong made rapid primate surveys at Kitobo and Loitokitok forests in Kenya on 27–28 September 2014. At Kitobo they encountered three groups of at least 6, 6 and 8 animals; night and early-morning choruses led them to infer at least six groups there. On the next day at Loitokitok they observed one group of two adult males at 1,835 m.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The same 2015 paper compiled earlier literature, specimens and author records to report an elevational span of about 660–3,050 m and an estimated range of about 4,040 km². These are the paper’s data compilation and mapping results at that time. Its new Kenyan records are limited to two forest reserves, not a systematic survey of the species’ present distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The English name follows the source’s Mount Kilimanjaro Guereza, and the Chinese common name is an editorial rendering; the accepted species identity follows COL26.8.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Core field records come from short rapid surveys at two Kenyan forests in 2014; the range map depends on historical records compiled by that paper. The source uses subspecies rank, while this page follows the pinned COL26.8 species identity and retains that rank difference. No current abundance or conservation category is asserted..
