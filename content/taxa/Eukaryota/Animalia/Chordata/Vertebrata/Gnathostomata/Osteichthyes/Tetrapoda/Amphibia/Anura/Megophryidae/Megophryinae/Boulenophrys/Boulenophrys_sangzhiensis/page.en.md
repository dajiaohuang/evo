Male holotype CIB 200078 came from Tianping Mountain, Sangzhi, Hunan, at 1300 m. The monograph examined additional Tianping adults at about 1380 m and summarized known Tianping and Huping localities in northwestern Hunan at 1300–1400 m. These reported localities and sample elevations are not a complete range survey or evidence of absence elsewhere.

## Six examined adults with finger-specific tubercles
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5gx2
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported two adult males at 55.7–62.2 mm snout–vent length and four females at 62.2–76.3 mm. It described weak vomerine ridges without teeth, distinct subarticular tubercles at fingers I and II but absent on the other fingers, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without fringes. Breeding males had sparse tiny black nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. These sampled characters and measurements are not universal adult size bounds or measured functional performance.
