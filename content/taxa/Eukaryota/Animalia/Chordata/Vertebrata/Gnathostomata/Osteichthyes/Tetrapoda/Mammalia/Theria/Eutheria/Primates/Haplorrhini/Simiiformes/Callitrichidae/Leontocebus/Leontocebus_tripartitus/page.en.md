# Leontocebus tripartitus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts *Leontocebus tripartitus*. Molecular taxonomy and regional records support treating it as a distinct saddle-back taxon; De la Torre (2017) recorded it at multiple Ecuadorian sites south of the Napo River, not as a continuous range boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Research observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2017 paper assembled one- to two-day surveys at 23 Ecuadorian Amazon sites from 1989 to 2014. Group and density records came from two Yasuní areas: four groups in about 100 ha at Tiputini over 30 sampling days, and two groups in about 50 ha at Tambococha over six days. Both estimates were 20 individuals per km² with mean groups of about five; the sites and sampling effort differed, so equal estimates do not imply identical ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Site and group-density data come from local Ecuadorian surveys, and most of the 23 distribution sites were surveyed for only one or two days. They do not estimate range-wide abundance, trend or conservation status. MDD v2.5 uses a Saguinus subgeneric combination; the COL26.8 ID remains fixed. Not externally reviewed.
