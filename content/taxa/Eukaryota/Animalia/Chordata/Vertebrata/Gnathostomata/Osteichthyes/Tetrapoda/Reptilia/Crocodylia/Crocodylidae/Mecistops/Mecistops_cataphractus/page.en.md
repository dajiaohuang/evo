## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A Côte d'Ivoire study conducted 195 repeated surveys at 38 sites from 2014 to 2019 and examined both the West African slender-snouted crocodile and the West African crocodile. The model estimated a mean detection probability of 0.75 for M. cataphractus; under the study's modeled conditions, at least two surveys were needed to infer absence at 90% confidence. Fishing-net encounter rate was significantly negatively associated with detection of M. cataphractus.
