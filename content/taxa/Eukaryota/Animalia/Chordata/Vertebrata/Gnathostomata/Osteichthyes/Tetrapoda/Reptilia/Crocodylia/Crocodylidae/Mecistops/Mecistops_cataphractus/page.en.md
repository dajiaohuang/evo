## Read the study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Why does a survey that finds no crocodile still need a detection model? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record 3YKJB and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. A Côte d'Ivoire study conducted 195 repeated surveys at 38 sites from 2014 to 2019 and examined both the West African slender-snouted crocodile and the West African crocodile. The model estimated a mean detection probability of 0.75 for M. cataphractus; under the study's modeled conditions, at least two surveys were needed to infer absence at 90% confidence. Fishing-net encounter rate was significantly negatively associated with detection of M. cataphractus.

Continue with Mecistops leptorhynchus (COL 8GPZP) to compare monitoring evidence with a skull-based diagnosis in its congener. The Crocodylia group guide provides the wider reading route; the neighboring page retains its own sources and limits.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Source-linked local monitoring guide, not externally expert-reviewed. Detection probability is not an abundance estimate. The fishing-net association comes from surveys in Côte d'Ivoire and should not be generalized unconditionally across the range or interpreted as a causal effect.
This batch uses historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are reported compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found. They are not direct measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the presence of same-name sparse rows support the association here; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components from the reported summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed here, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources; no complete monograph or external expert review is claimed.

The compilation contains gestation_d, outside the class scopes declared in Supplemental Table 9. Original values and reference labels remain in the source archive; without underlying-source review they are not interpreted as the corresponding reproductive or developmental events for this species.

## How to read reproductive records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
the reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 17.75. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Limits of these trait data

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
