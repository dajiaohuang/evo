## July observations beside swift streams
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12981243-v2gjs
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2025 description knew the species only from Baihualing, Longyang, Yunnan. During July surveys between 21:00 and midnight, frogs were observed on large stream rocks or banks beside wide, swift mountain streams bordered by broadleaf and bamboo forest. Disturbed individuals jumped into water. These observations describe the sampled sites and nights, without establishing an exclusive habitat requirement.

## An inferred July–August season

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
