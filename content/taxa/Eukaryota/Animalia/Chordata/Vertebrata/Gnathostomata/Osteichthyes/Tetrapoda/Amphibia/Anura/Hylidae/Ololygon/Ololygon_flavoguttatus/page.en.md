## Organization of the recorded vocalization
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-4992v
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Ololygon flavoguttata as follows: A harmonic click-like sequence and a non-harmonic fused-pulse note, emitted separately or combined with the first type preceding the second. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.

## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-4992v
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is Below 1,400 and above 6,000. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness. Raven measurements use 90% energy bandwidth, 5% and 95% energy frequencies, and peak frequency for the dominant component; these are not interchangeable with absolute frequency extrema or the fundamental. The Methods print FFT1024, 50% overlap, hop256 samples and grid93.8 Hz; figure captions describe a different display setting. No raw-audio reanalysis or silent correction of those settings is claimed.
