# Tamarinus labiatus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists the red-bellied tamarin as *Tamarinus labiatus*. Buchanan-Smith (1990) conducted a five-month field study in Pando Department, northern Bolivia, and published the animals as *Saguinus labiatus*. That study provides this page’s local group and foraging evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Social behavior and organization

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study combined observation and radio-location to record group size, ranging, daily activity and intergroup interactions. During the study, red-bellied tamarins formed stable mixed-species groups with saddle-back tamarins and jointly used and defended home ranges. The authors also described differences in foraging, forest height and travel. These are Pando study-site observations, not species-wide group parameters.
<!-- /evo:text -->
