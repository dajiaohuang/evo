## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2005 description designated a live male captured and released at Bemaraha on 3 October 1991 as the holotype, and gave a type locality near Ambalarano. It distinguished the form by facial and forehead pelage characters. The description documents the basis for the name; it is not a modern population assessment.

## behaviour

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A 2006 field report summarized telemetry and indirect observations of one local group in October 1991. During 85 hours 10 minutes of indirect observation, researchers recorded 105 loud “vou-hy” calls; the report also described whistles and growls. It listed detections at two localities within Tsingy de Bemaraha. These historical observations do not establish current occupancy.
