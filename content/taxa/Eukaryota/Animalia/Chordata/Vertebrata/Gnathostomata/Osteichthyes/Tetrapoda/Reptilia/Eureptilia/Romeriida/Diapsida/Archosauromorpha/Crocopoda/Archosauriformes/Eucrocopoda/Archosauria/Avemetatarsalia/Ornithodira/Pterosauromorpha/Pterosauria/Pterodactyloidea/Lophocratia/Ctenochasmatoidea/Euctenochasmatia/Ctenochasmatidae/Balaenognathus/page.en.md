# Balaenognathus


## A distinctive dentition known from one specimen

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The genus was described in 2023 from the B. maeuseri holotype NKMB P2011-633. A toothless front edge of the spatulate snout, at least 480 slender teeth and hooked/nodular crown tips lead the authors to a filtering interpretation. Many teeth belong to one animal, not many individuals or a population count. Proposed buccal/gular pumping and passive suspension feeding are not two confirmed ecological types within the genus.
