## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The eastern mouse-lemur revision proposed Jolly's mouse lemur as a taxon based on mitochondrial sequences and sample comparisons. Its morphometric table lists only three Jolly's mouse lemur individuals, so the taxonomic evidence should be read together with that small sample.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2006 paper gives Kianjavato as the type locality. This eastern site is part of the original taxonomic record; it does not mean all southeastern coastal forests were surveyed or define a current continuous range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
This page follows the fixed COL26.8 accepted ID `42SBP` and the study's Jolly's label. The current MDD page also lists Marohita and notes later treatment of M. marohita; read this alongside the COL accepted status of `42SBV`.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Limited to the 2006 eastern samples, three morphometric individuals and the type locality; no current range, abundance or conservation status is inferred. MDD and COL26.8 name treatments are shown side by side; the page is not externally expert-reviewed.
