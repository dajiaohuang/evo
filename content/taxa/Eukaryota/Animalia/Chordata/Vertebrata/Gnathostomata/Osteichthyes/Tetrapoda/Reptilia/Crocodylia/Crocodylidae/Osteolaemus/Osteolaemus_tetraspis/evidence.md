# Osteolaemus tetraspis

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/0/usage/scope/zh -->
加蓬 Abanda 洞穴与周边森林矮鳄的食性和体况局地比较；沿用原文分类名称，不独立解决洞穴种群的谱系地位。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/0/usage/scope/en -->
Local comparison of diet and body condition in dwarf crocodiles from Gabon's Abanda caves and nearby forest; follows the paper's taxonomic usage and does not independently resolve the cave population's lineage status.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/1/usage/scope/zh -->
对多个已知地点样本的线粒体与核基因及有限形态比较，支持三个地理谱系并定位 O. tetraspis 模式产地流域；不直接确定后来的 Abanda 洞穴采样个体归属。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/1/usage/scope/en -->
Mitochondrial and nuclear markers and limited morphology from multiple known localities support three geographic lineages and identify the basin of the O. tetraspis type locality; the study does not directly assign later Abanda cave samples.
<!-- /evo:text -->
