# Crithagra

## Enter through older Serinus combinations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
All twenty-four selected BirdLife historical rows have same-name eBird and one-to-one BirdTree links. Read COL cr/se shards confirm twenty-three exact older Serinus combinations, including ending changes such as rufobrunneus/rufobrunnea and sulphuratus/sulphurata. Serinus hypostictus at [Crithagra hyposticta](Crithagra_hyposticta/page.en.md) was not confirmed as an exact catalogue synonym in this reading. Links locate historical rows without proving concepts identical across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Seeds, multiple niches and primary lifestyles

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Niche categories are fifteen Granivore and nine Omnivore, while all twenty-four trophic levels are Herbivore. Seeds/nuts at 60% and all plants at 70% are different definition thresholds, not observed diet percentages. Lifestyles comprise twelve Insessorial, ten Generalist and two Terrestrial; habitats fourteen Shrubland, three Woodland, four Forest and three Grassland; density counts two code-1, fifteen code-2 and seven code-3. All migration codes are 1 without proving lifelong immobility. Shared diet categories do not establish a shared lifestyle.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, same-name eBird links, older Serinus BirdTree links, relevant Metadata definitions and this reading of COL cr/se shards. Catalogue synonyms and crosswalks do not prove complete equivalence of historical concepts to COL26.8; the exact older hypostictus combination was not confirmed in these shards. Morphology birds, complete sets and Dunning compiled mass retain separate denominators. Individual specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Current distributions, conservation, plumage, calls, breeding, seed lists, bite forces, observed diets and movement routes remain unsupported. No expert review or complete natural-history monograph is counted.
