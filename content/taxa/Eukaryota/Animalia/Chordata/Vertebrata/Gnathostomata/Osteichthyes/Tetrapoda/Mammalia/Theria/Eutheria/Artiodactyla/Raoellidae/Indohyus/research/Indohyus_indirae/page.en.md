# Indohyus indirae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Separately catalogued skulls, limb bones and teeth support an aquatic-wading interpretation and a raoellid sister placement to Cetacea in the published analysis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Diet unresolved; stable isotopes constrain water use more directly than food choice
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
