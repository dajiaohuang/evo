## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Cebus leucocephalus Gray, 1865. MDD v2.5 lists C. leucocephalus Gray, 1866 as a synonym of C. albifrons and gives its restricted type locality as Santander, Colombia. This page preserves the difference in authorship year and rank rather than presenting one treatment as uncontested.

## classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
An older COII study of 118 white-fronted capuchins recognized a leucocephalus group, but could not resolve relationships among the main genetic groups; the samples and names belonged to a broader C. albifrons research framework. A 2025 Colombian assessment says local biological and population information for the species is lacking. This evidence does not support turning the older name-group result into a natural history of an independently delimited species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2020 Colombian atlas gives modeled surfaces of 38,085 km² for potential range and 11,597 km² for remnant range. A 2025 national assessment reports no GBIF species records and very little biological or population information. Range models and occurrence records answer different questions; the former are not confirmation of current occupancy.
