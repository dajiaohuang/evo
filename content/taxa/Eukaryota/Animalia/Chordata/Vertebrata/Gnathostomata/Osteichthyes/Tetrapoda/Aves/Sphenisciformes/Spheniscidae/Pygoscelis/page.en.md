## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus 75VG, Pygoscelis, contains three accepted species: P. adeliae, P. antarcticus and P. papua. The Adélie page follows nest success across ten breeding seasons at Windmill Islands camera sites; the Chinstrap page summarizes a mixed-method global colony assessment; the Gentoo page describes winter diet at Bird Island in one unusually warm year. The Chinstrap article’s P. antarctica spelling is retained as source usage while COL26.8 accepts P. antarcticus. The studies differ in place, period and method; together they are a selected reading path, not a complete genus account or a uniform population trend.
