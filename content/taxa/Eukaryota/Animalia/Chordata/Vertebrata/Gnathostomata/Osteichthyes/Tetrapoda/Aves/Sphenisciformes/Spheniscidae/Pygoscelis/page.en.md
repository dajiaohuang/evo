## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus 75VG, Pygoscelis, contains the three accepted species P. adeliae, P. antarcticus and P. papua. This path currently links study pages for Chinstrap and Gentoo penguins; no Adélie species reader profile is linked here, so that third species remains outside this batch. Read the Chinstrap global breeding-site compilation alongside the Gentoo winter diet study at Bird Island. The Chinstrap article’s P. antarctica spelling is retained as a source usage while COL26.8 accepts P. antarcticus. These studies differ in place, period and method; they do not form a complete genus account or a uniform population trend.
