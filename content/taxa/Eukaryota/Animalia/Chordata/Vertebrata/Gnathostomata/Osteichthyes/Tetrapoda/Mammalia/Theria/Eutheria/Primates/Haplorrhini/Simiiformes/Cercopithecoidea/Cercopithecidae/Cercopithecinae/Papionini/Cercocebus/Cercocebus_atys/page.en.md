## communication

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In a Taï National Park group, researchers coded 226 interactions among 21 adult females from observations collected across three field seasons between 2022 and 2024. The study included affiliative, agonistic, feeding, grooming and sexual contexts. Interaction structure and signal-exchange timing differed by context; age was associated with gap duration only in affiliative exchanges, while rank and measured relationship strength showed no detectable effect. The authors caution that one group of adult females cannot establish species-wide patterns.
