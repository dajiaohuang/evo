# Ceoptera evansae


## Three adjoining blocks preserve the holotype

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Holotype NHMUK PV R37110 is a partial skeleton in three adjoining blocks, with shoulder, wing and hindlimb material. Two of seven vertebrae remain uncertain. Reconstructions do not supply preserved evidence for missing bones.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Scanning reveals bones still embedded in matrix

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Micro-CT segmentation reveals embedded bones; digital preparation does not supply missing anatomy.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Diagnosis focuses on shoulder and pelvis

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The diagnosis identifies a wavy coracoid flange and a posterior iliac depression divided by a low ridge. These do not determine diet.
<!-- /evo:text -->
