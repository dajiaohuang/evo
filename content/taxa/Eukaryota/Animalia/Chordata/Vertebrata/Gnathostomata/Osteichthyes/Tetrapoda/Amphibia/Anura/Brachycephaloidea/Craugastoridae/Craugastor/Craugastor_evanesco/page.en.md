<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Historical source concept: Craugastor evanesco, under the compilation's Frost 2011 classification. Equivalence with the current species concept remains unverified. AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 64.3 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
