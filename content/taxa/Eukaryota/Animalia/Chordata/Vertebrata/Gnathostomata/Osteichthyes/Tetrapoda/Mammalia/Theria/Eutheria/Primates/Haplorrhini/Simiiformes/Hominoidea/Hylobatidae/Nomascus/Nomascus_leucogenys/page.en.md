## lifeHistory

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Recordings from nine captive northern white-cheeked gibbons were classified into three intragroup call types—grunt, hoo and long hoo—across social, feeding, stress and attention contexts. The sample was captive; these categories are not a complete wild call repertoire and do not establish age-specific behavior in wild animals.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A fecal 16S study of 56 captive northern white-cheeked gibbons at two Chinese zoos found differences in gut-microbiome composition and diversity among age groups and between adult groups under different captive conditions. It did not sample wild populations or isolate the effect of any single husbandry factor.
