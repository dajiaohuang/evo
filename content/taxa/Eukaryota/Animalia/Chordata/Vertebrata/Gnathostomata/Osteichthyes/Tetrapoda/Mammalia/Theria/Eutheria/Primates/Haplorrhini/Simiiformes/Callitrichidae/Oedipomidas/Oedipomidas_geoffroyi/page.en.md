## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A three-month field study in Panama compared presence and relative abundance across sites. The authors reported broad occurrence in eastern Panama but substantial local variation; densities were higher at sites where human access was strictly limited than at less protected sites. This was not a national population census and does not by itself quantify the causal effect of protection.

## habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A canopy study published in 2010 observed 27 tamarins for 32.25 hours at Agua Clara, Panama, and 14 tamarins for 29.6 hours on Barro Colorado Island. Plots used by tamarins had shorter distances between neighboring canopies and between canopies and the ground; the authors discussed canopy runways as a possible influence on movement. The sites and observation hours were limited and do not establish a fixed species-wide habitat preference.

## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
At Cerro Ancón Nature Reserve, a study surveyed a 1.75-km transect two days per week, in morning and afternoon periods, from August 2019 to March 2020. Researchers counted 54 individuals in nine groups and estimated a density of 4.5 individuals per km². This describes one urban reserve and a particular survey design, not Panama as a whole.
