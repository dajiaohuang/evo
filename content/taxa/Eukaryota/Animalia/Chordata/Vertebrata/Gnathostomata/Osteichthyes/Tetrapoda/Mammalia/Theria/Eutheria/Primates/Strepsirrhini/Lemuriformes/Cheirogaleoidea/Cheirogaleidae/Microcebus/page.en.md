## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Read the mouse lemur profiles by study region and evidence type: begin with the 12-locality western revision and northern/northwestern taxonomic samples, continue through eastern descriptions and lowland studies, then compare multilocus species-delimitation work with the five-site northeastern lowland-forest study. Linked profiles cover Peters's, gray-brown, Sambirano, northern rufous, Bongolava and Danfoss's mouse lemurs in western/northwestern studies; Jolly's, Goodman's, Manitatra, rufous, Simmons's, Boraha, Marohita, Anosy and Gerp's mouse lemurs in eastern studies; the Montagne d'Ambre and Margot Marsh's mouse lemurs in northern descriptions; and MacArthur's and Jonah's mouse lemurs in northeastern work. The Ranomafana rufous mouse lemur page is a separate single-site mark-recapture example. This sequence follows studies, not evolutionary relationships, and is not a complete species checklist. Current MDD entries include Marohita under Jolly's, Boraha under Simmons's, and ganzhorni and manitatra under the gray mouse lemur; pinned COL26.8 retains the corresponding IDs separately, so the pages show both source systems. Local records do not define the genus-wide range, shared ecology or current status.
