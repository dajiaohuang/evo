## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Noronha and colleagues used a 2004 field trip to update the distribution account and reported eight new occurrence records: seven direct sightings and one interview with a local informant. The survey covered the headwaters of the Maués, Abacaxis and Sucundurí rivers. These records refine a historical locality picture; they are not a present-day census or proof of continuous occupancy.

## habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors obtained records in terra firme primary forest, secondary growth and igapó. Identification used diagnostic characters from the description and comparisons with museum specimens. The observations apply to sampled sites only and do not establish a current trend.
