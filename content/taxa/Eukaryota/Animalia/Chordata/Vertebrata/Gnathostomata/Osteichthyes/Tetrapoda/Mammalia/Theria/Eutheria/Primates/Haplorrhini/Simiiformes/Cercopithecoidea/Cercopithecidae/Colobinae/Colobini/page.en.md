# Colobini


## Count names, animals and preserved parts separately

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Berlin’s red-colobus entries distinguish three relationships: the lulindicus holotype and a same-locality male excluded from paratype status; original names such as kabambarei, likualae and umbrinus versus the catalogue’s later valid names; and multiple skull, skin or skeleton numbers for one animal. The preussi account lists a skin, while gordonorum survives as an incomplete skin. Part numbers do not increase animal counts, and a historical name’s type does not automatically become the holotype of a later valid name’s original description. Museum material does not directly represent modern population ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
