## Two recorded localities
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
varela-2025-maycu
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The account reported the type locality in Morona Santiago and another locality in Zamora Chinchipe, Ecuador, approximately 32 km apart, at 882–1183 m elevation. Frogs were associated with ravines and streams in primary and secondary forest. These observations establish the article’s recorded sample without implying continuous occupation between localities.

## A tadpole and a metamorph near streams
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
varela-2025-maycu
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The authors recorded a tadpole, QCAZ 67083, in a pool beside a stream in February and a metamorph on a rock island within a stream in March.
