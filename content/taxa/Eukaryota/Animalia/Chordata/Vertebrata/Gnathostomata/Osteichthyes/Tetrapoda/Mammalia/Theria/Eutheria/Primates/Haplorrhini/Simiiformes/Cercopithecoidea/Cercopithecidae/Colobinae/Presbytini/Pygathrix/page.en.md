## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists three accepted species in Pygathrix: P. cinerea, P. nemaeus and P. nigripes. This reading path connects studies from central and southern Vietnam and Son Tra in Da Nang. The pages use English common names from their locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 records and editorial Chinese renderings; their dates, sites and methods differ.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with the multi-year feeding records for P. nemaeus at Son Tra, then read the home-range and daily-path study of one population at the same reserve; the two studies have different questions and samples. Continue with wet- and dry-season feeding comparisons for P. nigripes at Nui Chua and Phuoc Binh. Finish by reading the 2007–2008 P. cinerea diet study at Kon Ka Kinh alongside the historical 2004/2006 surveys of two protected forests in Gia Lai.

## interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Together these studies connect feeding, phenology, movement and local surveys, but diet proportions, home ranges and sighting counts cannot be pooled: sites, years, observation methods and sample units differ. This path is not a genus-wide ecological review or complete distribution account.
