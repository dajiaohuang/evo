# Piliocolobus bouvieri


## Holotype and paratypes under the name likualae

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2024 catalogue places the original name Colobus (Tropicolobus) likualae Matschie, 1914 under Piliocolobus bouvieri (Rochebrune, 1887). The likualae holotype is ZMB 17977/17978 (old number A 108.14.186), an adult female skull and skin collected by Escherich in 1913 on the Sangha opposite the Likouala estuary, in present-day Republic of the Congo. It anchors the original name likualae, not the original bouvieri 1887 name. The historical locality does not create precise coordinates or a contemporary range boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## The paratypes are two juvenile females

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The catalogue lists two paratype animals: ZMB 17979/17980 is one juvenile female skull and skin, and ZMB 17974–17976 another juvenile female represented by skull, skin and partial skeleton. Five part numbers are not five paratype animals. An adult holotype and two juvenile paratypes do not establish a growth curve, adult-size statistics or field sex ratio. The 1914 description, original labels and physical specimens were not freshly inspected here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2016 large-mammal survey in the Lefini and Lesio-Louna reserves of the Republic of Congo recorded one group while observers travelled along a river through a remote part of Lesio-Louna. The paper notes that earlier surveys had not recorded the species after the early 1970s. This single encounter documents a local record, not a population estimate or a mapped range.
<!-- /evo:text -->
