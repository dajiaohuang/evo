## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Using the source name Lagothrix cana cana, the supplement lists records at Urucurituba and Apuí in Amazonas and Vale do Anari and Ji-Paraná in Rondônia. COL26.8 links that source usage as a synonym to the accepted species Lagothrix lagothricha and accepts the subspecies L. l. cana; the mapping is explicit here, while the records remain limited to surveyed localities.
