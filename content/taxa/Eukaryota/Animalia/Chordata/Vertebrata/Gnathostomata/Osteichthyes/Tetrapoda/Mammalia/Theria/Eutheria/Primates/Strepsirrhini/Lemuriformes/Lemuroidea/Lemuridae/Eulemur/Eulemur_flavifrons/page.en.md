# Eulemur flavifrons

## Survey records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Researchers used a total-count method with marked individuals and known groups in part of Ankarafa Forest from September 2007 to February 2008. They counted 228 lemurs in 29 groups; group sizes ranged from four to eleven. The result is a bounded estimate for the surveyed forest area, not a current count for the whole species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Counts and density apply to one part of Ankarafa Forest and the 2007–2008 survey window. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
