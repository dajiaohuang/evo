# Ambulocetus natans

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Partial skeleton H-GSP 18507 combines large weight-bearing limbs with vertebral and hind-limb anatomy used to model amphibious swimming.
