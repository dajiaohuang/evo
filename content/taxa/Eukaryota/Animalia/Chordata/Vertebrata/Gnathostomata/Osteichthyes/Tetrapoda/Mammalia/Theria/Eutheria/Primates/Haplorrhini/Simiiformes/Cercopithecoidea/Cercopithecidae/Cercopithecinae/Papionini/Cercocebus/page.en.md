## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This path links all seven Cercocebus species in the fixed COL26.8 primate roster and compares how evidence was collected. Begin with the multi-patch lower-Tana census and single-forest diet comparison for C. galeritus, then the two-forest acoustic estimate for C. sanjei. Read C. agilis beside C. torquatus to compare an early northeastern-Gabon account published under the older subspecies name with a fruit-linked party study from South Loango. Continue with the Taï group communication study of C. atys, then the discontinuous regional surveys for C. chrysogaster and the camera-confirmed site record for C. lunulatus. The dates, methods and taxonomic labels differ; these pages do not combine into a genus-wide range, abundance estimate, diet or status.
