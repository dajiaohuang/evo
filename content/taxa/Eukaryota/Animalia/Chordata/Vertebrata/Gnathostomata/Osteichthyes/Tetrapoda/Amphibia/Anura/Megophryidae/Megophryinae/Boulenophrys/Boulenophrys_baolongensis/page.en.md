## The cited male sample and selected external characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-mr75
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
Drawing on prior descriptions, the diagnosis reported five adult males with snout–vent lengths of 41.8–45.0 mm. Selected features included a distinct tympanum, a weak posterior tongue notch, absent vomerine ridges and teeth, mostly smooth dorsal skin with scattered spiny granules, and toes without webbing or lateral fringes. These describe the cited material; the male-only measurements do not provide female dimensions or biological limits for every population.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison

The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys baolongensis as without a vomerine ridge. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
