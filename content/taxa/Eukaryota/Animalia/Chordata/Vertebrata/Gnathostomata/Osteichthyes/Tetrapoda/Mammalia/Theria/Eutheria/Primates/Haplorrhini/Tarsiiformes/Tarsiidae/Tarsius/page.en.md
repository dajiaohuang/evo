## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Use this path through all twelve fixed COL26.8 Tarsius species IDs as a set of unlike evidence, not a complete genus review. Start with local field studies: T. fuscus sleeping nests in South Sulawesi, the dated Peleng–Banggai survey for T. pelengensis, and brief non-random observations of T. sangirensis. Keep T. tarsier as a taxonomy and attribution note because an older Selayar-plus-mainland study pooled its observations. Continue with the high-elevation, single-group study of T. pumilus and the land-use comparison for T. dentatus (published as T. dianae); then read the diagnostic calls and limited records for T. lariang and T. wallacei. Finish with island and northern forms described mainly through specimens and vocal evidence: T. tumpara, T. niemitzi, T. spectrumgurskyae and T. supriatnai. Dates, sites and methods differ and are not interchangeable.
