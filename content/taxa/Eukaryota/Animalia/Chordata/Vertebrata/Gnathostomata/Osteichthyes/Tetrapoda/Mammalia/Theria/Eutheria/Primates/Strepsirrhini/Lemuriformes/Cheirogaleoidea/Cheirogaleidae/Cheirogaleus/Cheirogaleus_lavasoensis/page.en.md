## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Dwarf lemurs from the Lavasoa Mountains were initially assigned provisionally to Crossley's dwarf lemur because comparative genetic data were limited. Thiele et al. added 51 dwarf lemurs from nine southern Madagascar sites and analysed mitochondrial and three nuclear loci; the study delineated genetic subgroups and formally described Lavasoa dwarf lemur. Its genetic conclusions concern sampled individuals, not a complete field map of the genus.
