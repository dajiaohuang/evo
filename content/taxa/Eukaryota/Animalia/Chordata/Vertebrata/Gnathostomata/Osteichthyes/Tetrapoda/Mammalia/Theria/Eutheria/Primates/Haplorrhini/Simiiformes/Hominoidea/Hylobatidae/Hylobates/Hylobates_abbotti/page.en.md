## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2019 study in Gunung Niut Nature Reserve, West Kalimantan, used pooled local expert opinion (PLEO) alongside point-transect work for selected species. Fifty-eight community members and patrol staff contributed information across ten 5–20 km² sample units covering 167 km², about 18% of the reserve. The paper reports a local PLEO estimate of 1.70 individuals per km² for H. abbotti (CV 0.16; 95% interval 1.13–2.26). It is a knowledge-based estimate for one landscape, not a direct species-wide census or a current abundance figure.
