## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Aquino and colleagues walked 610 km of transects at four sites on both banks of Peru's Río Curaray in November–December 2012. They followed Marsh's 2014 taxonomy and recorded 16 P. napensis sightings, only north of the river. Six encounters had complete counts; observed groups ranged from 2 to 7 animals, with a mean of 4.5. This short transect survey in one basin is not a species-wide range or population estimate.
