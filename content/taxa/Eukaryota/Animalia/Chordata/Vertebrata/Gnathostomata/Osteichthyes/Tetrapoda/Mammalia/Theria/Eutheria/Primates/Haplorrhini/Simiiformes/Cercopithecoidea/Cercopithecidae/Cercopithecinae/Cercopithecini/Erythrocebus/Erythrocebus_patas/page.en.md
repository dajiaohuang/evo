## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At Murchison Falls National Park, Hall reported 640 observation hours across three main study periods. Observed group sizes ranged from 9 to 31 animals, with a mean of 15; one group had a reported home range of 5,200 ha. These are historical measurements from that Ugandan site, not species-wide group-size or range estimates.

## feedingEcology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A separate 17-month study in Laikipia, Kenya, reported that E. patas pyrrhonotus fed mainly on gum from Acacia drepanolobium and arthropods, including those associated with the trees’ swollen thorns. The source names the eastern subspecies; these observations remain at that taxonomic and geographic scale.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Hall’s 1966 paper predates later species splits and describes animals at one Ugandan site; the Laikipia paper explicitly uses E. patas pyrrhonotus. Neither is a range-wide account. MDD v2.5 writes the authority as “von Schreber, 1774,” while pinned COL26.8 gives “Schreber, 1774”; the COL identity is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial; this page has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
