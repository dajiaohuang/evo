## population and elevation survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The survey recorded nine groups totaling 123 Assamese macaques at 250–856 m elevation; the authors calculated ecological density of 4.7 individuals/km² for a local occurrence area of about 26.3 km². The abstract reports about 67% of recorded individuals at 400–600 m.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Results are limited to the Dampa Tiger Reserve core area; local ecological density is not a range-wide census or current estimate. The paper's 2015 conservation assessment is not presented as current. The Chinese label is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
