# Psilopogon


## Seventeen records with unequal sample coverage

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This batch adds 17 historical guides linked to older Megalaima names and retains the earlier [Psilopogon pyrolophus](Psilopogon_pyrolophus/page.en.md) route; complete genus coverage is not claimed. [Psilopogon haemacephalus](Psilopogon_haemacephalus/page.en.md) has 11 birds but eight complete sets; [Psilopogon lineatus](Psilopogon_lineatus/page.en.md) ten birds and eight complete sets; [Psilopogon virens](Psilopogon_virens/page.en.md) eight birds and four complete sets. Most other rows have four to six birds; complete sets and unsexed counts remain separate, and totals do not supply every field’s denominator.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Shared fruit labels with different levels and habitats

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
All 17 rows are Frugivore and Insessorial, but [Psilopogon haemacephalus](Psilopogon_haemacephalus/page.en.md) is Woodland/semi-open while the other selected rows are Forest/dense vegetation. Trophic level is Omnivore for haemacephalus, rafflesii, mystacophanos, chrysopogon and javensis, and Herbivore for the other selected rows; the fruit niche does not replace that field. [Psilopogon faber](Psilopogon_faber/page.en.md) and [Psilopogon nuchalis](Psilopogon_nuchalis/page.en.md) share compiled mass 87.69 g; the original book has not been examined to explain the equality, which does not establish identical individual distributions.
<!-- /evo:text -->
