## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The East Sumatran Banded Langur appeared in earlier studies as the subspecies P. f. percura. In 2020, Ang et al. reconstructed the first mitogenomes for this taxon from eastern Sumatran faecal samples; one of eight samples had insufficient coverage and was excluded from further analysis.

## genetics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Across several delimitation methods used by the authors, P. f. percura and P. f. femoralis formed separate mitochondrial operational units; the paper reported a minimum whole-mitogenome distance of 5.3%. This is evidence from a maternally inherited marker, and the authors noted the need for broader population sampling to clarify boundaries.
