## Females carrying eggs and juveniles
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10210337-t474s
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
An active female carried ten eggs at night, while another uncollected female carried nine juveniles in a bromeliad. By day, females carrying two and four near-term juveniles sheltered inside Brocchinia reducta tubes, and one carrying nine juveniles rested under a rock. These individual observations document maternal carriage and shelter use, without providing a population clutch-size distribution or development duration.

## Active night encounters and daytime refuges

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
