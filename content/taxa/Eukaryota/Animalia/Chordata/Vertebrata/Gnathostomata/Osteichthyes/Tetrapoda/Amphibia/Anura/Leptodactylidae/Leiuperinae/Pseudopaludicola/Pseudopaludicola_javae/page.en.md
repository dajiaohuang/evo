## Twelve encounters at a wet-soil ecotone
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12654886-s6t46
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The study found 12 individuals in March 2022 at Conceição do Araguaia, 217 m, on soaked soil between a forest patch and seasonally flooded open vegetation. Several were located by calls. Its cited background describes evening and night ground activity and occurrence in grass-dominated flooded areas and disturbed settings. The study’s encounters do not establish a fixed density, exclusive wet-soil use or successful reproduction in every pasture or road margin.

## Three calls from one male retain their sampling units
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12654886-s6t46
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Calls of male MPEG 44093 were recorded on 17 March 2022 at 16:34. Three calls contained 11–18 pulsed notes, emitted at 51.3–55.0 notes per minute. Eighteen measured notes lasted 297–339 ms, with 10–12 pulses per note at 32.2–36.5 pulses per second; dominant frequency was 4.91–5.21 kHz and corresponded to the fundamental. These are one male’s acoustic measurements, not three males or 18 independent animals. ## Acoustic and genetic specimens supporting the first Pará record
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12654886-s6t46
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The authors reported acoustic agreement with the original description and a 16S distance of 1.3% between separate specimen MPEG 44094 and cited type-series sequences OR509824–OR509828. They interpreted the locality as the first Pará and northernmost known record, about 183 km north of the Tocantins type locality, Fazenda Escondida. The genetic specimen is not confused with the recorded male MPEG 44093. This dated range addition does not establish continuous occupancy, a universal genetic threshold or recent northward movement.
