## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The description gave a mass of about 0.80 kg and a mainly gray coat. The fur is lighter over the eyelids, a diffuse stripe runs from the forehead to roughly the middle of the back, and the tail changes from reddish gray near its base to darker gray. The authors compared these traits with other southeastern sportive lemurs.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is the Manangotry rainforest parcel of Andohahela National Park in southern Madagascar. The paper placed the known forest between the Mandrare River to the west and the Mananara River to the north, but said the range needed confirmation. This study locality and its proposed river limits are not a complete ecological survey.
