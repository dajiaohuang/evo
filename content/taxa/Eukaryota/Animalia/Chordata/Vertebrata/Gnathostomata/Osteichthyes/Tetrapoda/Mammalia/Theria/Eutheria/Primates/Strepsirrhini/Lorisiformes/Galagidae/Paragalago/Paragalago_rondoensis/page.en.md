## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Published field evidence for the Rondo Dwarf Galago consists mainly of surveys in patches of Tanzania’s coastal forests. Studies from different years report local occurrence, nocturnal encounters or rough density estimates; they do not combine into a current census of the species.

## survey-records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A February 2003 biodiversity survey at Pande Game Reserve covered two survey areas and used nocturnal observations, call recordings and several trapping methods. The author estimated 3–6 Rondo galagos per hectare in about 6 km² of coastal thicket and forest, yielding a rough extrapolation of 180–260 animals. This was not a direct census.

## field_observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The Tanzania Forest Conservation Group selected six sites in Rondo Reserve for surveys in 2012 and 2013. The mammal checklist records the species at Site 1; the primate table lists one dusk/night vocal record and no visual sighting. The authors treated this as evidence that the species persisted in the reserve during the survey, but the record cannot estimate abundance.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The two direct reports are local surveys at individual reserves and are historical. The Pande number was extrapolated from a rough density estimate, while the Rondo Reserve survey recorded one call. This page does not present these materials as current status or a complete range. Source-linked bilingual draft; not externally expert-reviewed.
