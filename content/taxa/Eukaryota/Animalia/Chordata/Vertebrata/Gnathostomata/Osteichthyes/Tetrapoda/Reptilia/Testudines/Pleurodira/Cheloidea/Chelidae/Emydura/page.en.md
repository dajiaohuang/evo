## A hatchling chondrocranium study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Paluh and Sheil (2013) describe the mature chondrocranium of Emydura subglobosa using hatchling specimens and cleared, double-stained preparations. The paper reports a prominent taenia marginalis and a greatly reduced, nearly absent pila antotica in those hatchlings. These are developmental anatomy observations from that study, not a genus-wide adult skull account.
<!-- /evo:text -->
