## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Inogwabini and Thompson compiled three intermittent, discontinuous surveys from 1994–2007 across forest blocks in and around Salonga National Park and west of Lake Maindombe. Their 2013 analysis mapped an estimated occupied area of about 70,000 km² and reported no detections across more than 37% of the presumed historical range. Because the surveys did not cover a continuous landscape or a single time window, those mapped limits should not be read as a present-day range boundary.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
