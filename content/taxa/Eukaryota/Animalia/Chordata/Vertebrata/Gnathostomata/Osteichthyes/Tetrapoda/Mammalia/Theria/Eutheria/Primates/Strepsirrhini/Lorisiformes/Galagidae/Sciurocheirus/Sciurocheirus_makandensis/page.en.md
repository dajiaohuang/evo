## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID 4VNCK. The MDD v2.5 English display name is Makande Squirrel Galago. The species name was published by Ambrose in 2013; the MDD account records Makandé, Forêt des Abeilles, Gabon, south of the Ogooué River, as the type locality.

## bioacoustics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Ambrose’s 2003 comparison identified a Makandé-region loud-call profile south of the Ogooué, distinct from the Bioko/southwestern Cameroon and northern Gabon profiles. The 2013 species account names this form as S. makandensis. The evidence concerns acoustic and geographic differentiation; the sources used here do not provide a species-specific diet, home range or population estimate.

## limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2003 paper studied galagos under the then-broad name Galago alleni and predicted three species-level forms; the 2013 account formalized the Makandé form. This page does not expand the type locality or acoustic records into a continuous range or a complete natural-history account.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Evidence is centered on the acoustic comparison and short species account; it does not establish abundance, trend or full natural history. The acoustic paper used the historical broad name Galago alleni, and this guide preserves its proposed interpretation rather than presenting it as a range-wide field survey. The Chinese name is editorial; this page has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
