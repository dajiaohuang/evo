## sleeping and foraging

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The study recorded 50 sleeping sites, mostly on cliffs, for one Huangshan group. Distance comparisons across 76 full-day observations supported a tendency toward multiple-central-place foraging, and sleeping-site use was positively associated with nearby foraging patches.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The study covers one group at Huangshan for one year; multiple-central-place foraging is an interpretation of local behavioral data, not a species-wide result. The Chinese label is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
