## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/3/usage/scope/en -->
Mitochondrial cytochrome-b and D-loop analysis, using mainly fecal samples from wild Peruvian tamarins; supports species-level hypotheses for several nigricollis-group lineages but does not alone settle genus-level naming.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/4/usage/scope/zh -->
结合遗传、骨骼、毛色与发声资料提出 Callitrichinae 分类方案；该方案把 Leontocebus 置于 Saguinus 的亚属层级，代表与 COL26.8 不同的属级处理。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/4/usage/scope/en -->
Combines genetic, osteological, pelage and vocal evidence in a Callitrichinae classification that treats Leontocebus as a subgenus of Saguinus, a genus-level treatment differing from pinned COL26.8.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/5/usage/scope/en -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/6/usage/scope/en -->
A systematic survey at one Peruvian Panguana reserve; L. leucogenys observations relate to a 35.7-ha observation area, its gaps and local groups, not the full range.

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

2011 年秘鲁东北部 Quebrada Blanco 两群比较，一群可进入十年生次生林、另一群只用原生林；猎物捕获结论受地点、季节和两群样本限制。

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/7/usage/scope/en -->
A 2011 comparison of two groups at Quebrada Blanco in northeastern Peru: one had access to ten-year-old secondary forest and one used primary forest; prey-capture findings are limited to those groups, site and season.

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/8/usage/scope/en -->
One- to two-day surveys at 23 Ecuadorian Amazon sites from 1989 to 2014; population estimates came from only eight sites and are local records, not a full-range census.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/9/usage/scope/en -->
Observations and interviews in the Morona basin from 2019 to 2024; direct L. lagonotus observations were on the southeast bank, while interview reports from the opposite bank were not field-confirmed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/10/usage/scope/zh -->
厄瓜多尔亚马逊两个野生群体、两个月的习惯化观察；记录了观察者在场时行为与空间使用变化，不代表全种固定行为。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/10/usage/scope/en -->
Two wild groups in the Ecuadorian Amazon followed for two months during habituation; documents behavior and space-use changes with observer exposure, not fixed species-wide behavior.
<!-- /evo:text -->
