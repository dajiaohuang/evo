## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The paper estimated Wright's sportive lemur at about 0.95 kg, with a reddish- to gray-brown back and a paler underside. In a sample of only five animals, the authors observed uniformly gray heads in females but less contrast in males, and suggested possible sexual dichromatism. The small sample makes this a hypothesis to test, not a settled genus-wide pattern.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Befarara in Kalambatritra Special Reserve. The paper then knew the species from the reserve area west of the Mananara River and north of the Mandrare River; it called for additional range surveys and verification of the coat-color difference. These local records do not provide a current population estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The sexual-coloration observation came from five animals, and the distribution reflects the 2006 sample. This guide retains those uncertainties and does not generalize the claim. The COL26.8 accepted name is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
