## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Valohoaka in Ranomafana National Park, a three-month 2018 study followed two habituated groups to record fruit feeding, movement and seed dispersal. The teams logged 590 observation hours, 431 fruit-feeding events at 210 plants and 346 dispersal events. These observations and the resulting spatial models describe those groups and the study site.
