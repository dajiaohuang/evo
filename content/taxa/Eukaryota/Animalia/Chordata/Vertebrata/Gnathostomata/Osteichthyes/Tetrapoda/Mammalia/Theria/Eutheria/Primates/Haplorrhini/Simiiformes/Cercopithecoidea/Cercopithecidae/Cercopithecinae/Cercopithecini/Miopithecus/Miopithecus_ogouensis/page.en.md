# Miopithecus ogouensis

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Miopithecus ogouensis as species ID 43L7Y under genus ID 5TJ3. MDD v2.5 supplies “Northern Talapoin Monkey” as the English display name. The field account from Makokou was published in 1971 as “the talapoin of Gabon,” when the taxonomic placement of talapoins was still debated; this page keeps that historical name visible and treats the observations as locality-bounded context for the current Gabon taxon.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Field research in Makokou

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Gautier-Hion reported 20 months of observations around Makokou, including extended stays near several troops and detailed ecological work on three groups close to the research station. Day observations were supplemented by night work, about one quarter of the field hours. The study also surveyed forest routes over a 30–40 km radius. These methods describe a local field programme, not a modern abundance estimate or a range-wide account.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Survey methods

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2015–2017 survey around ten villages in northeastern Gabon combined 60 two-kilometre transects with visual, camera-trap and dung records. It logged 828 visual talapoin observations and 93 camera detections; these are method-specific detections, not 921 unique animals. A separate Batéké Plateau camera study sampled 40 locations for 5,902 camera-days and recorded the species in a transitional forest–savannah landscape. The studies use different sites and protocols, so their counts should not be pooled.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The ecological evidence is local to Makokou, northeastern Gabon and the Batéké Plateau, and includes repeated detections rather than individual population counts. The 1971 paper predates the current species name and explicitly describes unresolved taxonomy. These studies do not establish a modern range, population trend or complete natural history. The Chinese display name is editorial. No dossier or external expert review was added. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
