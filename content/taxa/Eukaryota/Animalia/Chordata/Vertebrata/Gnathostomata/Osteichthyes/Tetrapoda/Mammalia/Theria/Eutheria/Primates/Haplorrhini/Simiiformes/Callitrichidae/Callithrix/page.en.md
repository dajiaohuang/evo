## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This path connects all six species IDs in the fixed COL26.8 Callithrix roster, including existing pages for C. aurita and C. jacchus. Start with C. kuhlii isotope samples across a forest-cover gradient, then compare the local C. flaviceps transect survey with the one-group C. geoffroyi feeding study. Read the C. penicillata Cerrado observations last, alongside the older C. kuhli exudate-and-ranging account. Sites, methods and sample sizes differ; these papers are examples for reading, not a genus-wide ecological profile or complete range review.
