## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Goffard, Arroyo-Rodríguez and Aguirre surveyed four sites in Santa Cruz, Bolivia: the 172-ha Ocorutú Refuge and three cattle ranches with different levels of human pressure. They sampled 619 ha of tropical forest and recorded 143 individuals in 23 troops, plus two solitary adult males; total density across the sampled area was 23.1 individuals per km². Counts and density were highest in the protected refuge (51 individuals in eight troops; 44 per km²), while densities at the ranch sites ranged from 10 to 33 per km². The comparison describes these four sites and cannot by itself separate the effects of protection, habitat, poaching and other site differences.
