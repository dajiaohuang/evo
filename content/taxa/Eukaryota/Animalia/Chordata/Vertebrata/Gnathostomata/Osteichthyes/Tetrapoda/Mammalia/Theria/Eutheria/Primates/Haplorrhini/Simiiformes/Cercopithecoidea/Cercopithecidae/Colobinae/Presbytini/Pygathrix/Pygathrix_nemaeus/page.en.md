## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The Son Tra study assembled 11 years of group-follow data and monthly phenology from 160 trees representing 60 species in 16 vegetation plots. The authors listed at least 226 food plants. Buds and young leaves were the main foods, supplemented by flowers, fruit, seeds and bark. Plant species varied by month while the number of food resources used remained relatively stable across months and seasons.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A separate Son Tra study collected 259 hours of behavioral data and estimated a 36.00-ha home range, an 8.02-ha core area and a mean daily path length of 509.11 ± 244.87 m for the study population. Daily path length was correlated with precipitation. The authors interpreted a defendability index of 0.75 as inconsistent with daily travel far enough to actively defend territorial borders.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Both studies are limited to Son Tra in Da Nang. Ranging data concern one study population, and the food list and phenology plots describe that reserve. The results are not generalized to the full range, a species-wide home range or current conservation status. Not externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
