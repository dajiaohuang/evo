## field ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
From October 2020 to January 2021, researchers located 13 sleeping nests across Bantimurung Bulusaraung National Park and Hasanuddin University Educational Forest. Nest substrates included gaps in Ficus trees, karst rock shelters, forest pandanus and fallen tree debris; most nests were 2–7 m above ground, with recorded elevations spanning 68–947 m.

## evidence_scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The observations cover two nearby South Sulawesi sites and 13 nests, not the full range or a population trend. Morning vocalizations and visual encounters were used to locate nests; site and detection limits constrain comparisons between forest types.
