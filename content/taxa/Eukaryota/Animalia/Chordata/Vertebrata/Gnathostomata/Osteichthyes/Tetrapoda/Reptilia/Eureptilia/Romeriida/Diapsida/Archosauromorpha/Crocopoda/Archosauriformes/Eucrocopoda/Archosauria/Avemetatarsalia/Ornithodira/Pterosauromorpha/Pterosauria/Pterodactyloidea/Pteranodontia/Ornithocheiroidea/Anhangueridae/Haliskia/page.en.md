# Haliskia

## Read Haliskia through its type species

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Haliskia has H. peterseni as its type species, described in 2024 from partial skeleton KK F1426. This guide leads to the account supported by that material. Genus guides are counted separately from the PBDB accepted fossil-species list and do not increase the species denominator. The catalogue places the genus in Anhangueridae, whereas the paper compares multiple phylogenetic matrices and retains uncertainty in finer relationships.

## From preservation to functional inference

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Begin with the specimen and stratigraphy on the Haliskia peterseni page, then compare teeth and hyoid measurements, followed by wingspan estimates, phylogenetic analyses and feeding hypotheses. Bone lengths are observations, complete span is a comparative reconstruction, and prey control or niche partitioning remain interpretations. Return to the Pterosauria guide for eggs, integument and flight models to compare evidence types; those taxa’s reproductive, integumentary or flight conclusions cannot simply be transferred to Haliskia.
