## seasonalFeeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In Dja Reserve, researchers used 15-minute scan samples from February to December 1998 to record feeding, activity and habitat use. The mangabeys fed on fruits, seeds, leaves and flowers from 132 plant species, while five plant species accounted for 45% of feeding records. During fruit-lean periods, observations shifted toward seeds, flowers and young leaves, alongside more use of swamp habitat and greater dietary diversity. These patterns describe the studied Dja population and period.
