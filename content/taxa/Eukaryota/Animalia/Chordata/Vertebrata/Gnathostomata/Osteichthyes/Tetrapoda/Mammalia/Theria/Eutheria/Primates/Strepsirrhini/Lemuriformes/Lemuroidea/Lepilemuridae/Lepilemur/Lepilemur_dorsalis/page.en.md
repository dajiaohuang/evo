## population-level taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The study reported 5.18–5.88% mitochondrial divergence between Gray's sportive lemur populations from Ambanja/Nosy Be and the Sahamalaza Peninsula. In a separate comparison, L. ankaranensis differed by 2.90–3.60% from Ambanja/Nosy Be samples and by 4.56–5.35% from Sahamalaza samples. The authors questioned the monophyly of traditional L. dorsalis and proposed a further split; COL26.8 still accepts this entry as L. dorsalis.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The population result comes from one 2006 mitochondrial marker and cytogenetic comparisons, and the authors called for more evidence. This page does not present the proposed split as current taxonomy. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
