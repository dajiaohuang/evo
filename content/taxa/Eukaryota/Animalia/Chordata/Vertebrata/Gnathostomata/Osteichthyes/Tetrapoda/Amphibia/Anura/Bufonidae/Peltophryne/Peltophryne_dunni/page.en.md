## What the bibliographic survey can establish

The review searched BioOne, ISI, Jstor, PubMed, Scielo, Scopus, Web of Science and monographs for regional molineid literature from 1819–2025. It excluded incomplete records, unpublished theses or meeting data and parasite records not identified to species. The authors also sought species-level molecular records in the literature and GenBank. Search strings and individual database search dates are not documented in the inspected methods, so omission from this list is not a reproducible negative finding.
