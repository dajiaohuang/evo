## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies

The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is No filter. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness. Raven measurements use 90% energy bandwidth, 5% and 95% energy frequencies, and peak frequency for the dominant component; these are not interchangeable with absolute frequency extrema or the fundamental.
