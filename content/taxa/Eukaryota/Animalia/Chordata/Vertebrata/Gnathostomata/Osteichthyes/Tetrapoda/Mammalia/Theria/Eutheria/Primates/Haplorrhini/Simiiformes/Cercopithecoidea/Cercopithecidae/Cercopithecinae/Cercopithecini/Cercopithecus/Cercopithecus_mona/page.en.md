## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Okomu National Park, Nigeria, feeding observations were collected over 37 days during dry and wet seasons in 2011–2013. The preliminary study recorded 31 plant species from 21 families; fruits accounted for 64% of recorded plant parts, with seeds and nuts also eaten. About 21% of the observed diet came from outside the park, and the list included introduced or cultivated plants. Monkeys fled quickly from observers, so group composition and density were not reliably measured. The page describes one Okomu sample, not a complete diet.
