## Rocky headwaters at night
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12163431-v3tm5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Specimens were found between 19:00 and 23:00 in headwater stream water or on banks at 300–750 m, surrounded by secondary forest. Survey air temperature was 20.1–25.7 °C and humidity 83–95%. No calls or tadpoles were recorded. These conditions describe collection, not thermal tolerance or confirmed larval habitats.
