## Reference labels and small distances do not resolve every species concept
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The paper reuses listed Dryophytes mitogenomes and earlier RAD reads for suweonensis, immaculatus and flaviventris to reconstruct mitochondrial fragments. A reported maximum COX1 comparison of 1.9% between suweonensis and flaviventris is not a universal threshold or a reason to merge names. The source’s Hyla japonica label is retained alongside fixed Dryophytes japonicus. These are accession-bounded references, not new population surveys or independent voucher-wide concept certification.

## Repeated notes and recording cohorts define the comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The 2023 study analyses 2,858 notes from fifty-six callers at five Korean sites. Eumseong was recorded in 2012, whereas the other sites date to 2021 and/or 2022; individuals were not captured. Geographic north/south labels were used within suweonensis sensu lato. Species pages distinguish standard errors from ranges, note duration from whole-call duration and maximum-power frequency from the high-frequency boundary. Note-level ANOVA degrees of freedom do not establish that repeated callers were modelled as independent individuals.

## Endemism and risk categories retain their original evidence boundaries
<!-- /evo:text -->
