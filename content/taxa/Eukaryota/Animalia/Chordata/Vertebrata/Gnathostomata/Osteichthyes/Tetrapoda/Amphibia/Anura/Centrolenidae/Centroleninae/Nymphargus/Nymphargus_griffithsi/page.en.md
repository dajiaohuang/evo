## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13061190-486jl
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
Griffithsi and lasgralarias are compared as having translucent esophageal/stomach peritonea, green digits and a golden-yellow or whitish-cream iris with dark punctuations, against dajomesae’s white peritonea, yellow digits and tan reticulated iris. Some griffithsi individuals have dark flecks; this is variable rather than a compulsory mark in every individual.

## A sampled biogeographic hypothesis

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/text/en -->
The Discussion reports griffithsi/lasgralarias as geographic neighbors andsometimes sympatric under60% described-genus sampling. The authors propose Andean linearity anddry transverse valleys as possible isolation mechanisms, but explicitly note no studies designed to test those valleys’ diversification effects. This selected mitochondrial/geographic association is not direct gene-flow measurement, a new divergence clock or a settled relationship for all unsampled populations.
