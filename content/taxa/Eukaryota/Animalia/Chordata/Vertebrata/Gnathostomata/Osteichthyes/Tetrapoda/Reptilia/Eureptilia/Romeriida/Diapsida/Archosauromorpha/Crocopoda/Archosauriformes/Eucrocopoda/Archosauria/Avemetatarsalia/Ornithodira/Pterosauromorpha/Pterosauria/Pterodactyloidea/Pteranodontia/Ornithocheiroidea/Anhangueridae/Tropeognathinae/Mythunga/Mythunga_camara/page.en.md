# Mythunga camara

## Start with the holotype and locality

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Holotype QM F18896 preserves an incomplete snout with an adherent mandible. Phillip Gilmore found it in 1991 at Dunluce Station near Hughenden, within a calcareous nodule from the Toolebuc Formation, assigned to the late Albian in the 2007 paper.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Internal chambers do not supply a complete head

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2007 authors describe internal chambers, thin bone walls and supports in the snout. Missing anterior and dorsal portions constrain reconstruction of the complete head and any crest.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## The preserved anterior teeth were reinterpreted

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The public reappraisal abstract identifies the anterior preserved structures as a maxillary tooth root and its replacement, rather than contralateral teeth. They therefore cannot establish the anteriormost tooth pair. It also identifies the bone in the nasoantorbital fenestra as the right splenial. These are abstract-supported revisions; the complete article was not obtained in this batch.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Relationships depend on material and matrices

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The reappraisal abstract places Mythunga in Anhangueria with much of that clade unresolved. The second-specimen Thapunngaka study groups Mythunga, Thapunngaka and Ferrodraco in an unresolved trichotomy named Mythungini. One analysis in the 2024 Haliskia study identifies Mythunga and Thapunngaka as unstable and excludes them a posteriori. These results are recorded separately; no agreed nearest sister group is assigned, and database storage placement does not adjudicate the debate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page uses the original description, the public reappraisal abstract, and comparison and analysis passages read in later studies. The original article pages were extracted from the scanned journal, with holotype and locality pages visually inspected; measurement tables are not expanded, and PDF or figures are not redistributed. The complete reappraisal remains unavailable, so its full revised diagnosis is not claimed reviewed. Original span, maturity and diet interpretations are not treated as direct measurements or established life history. Reproduction, behavior, complete range and abundance remain insufficient;
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 276-item pterosaur working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Pterosauria storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
