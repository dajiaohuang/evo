## feedingBiomechanics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A comparative study of adult crocodilians reported a measured bite force of 16,414 N for one saltwater crocodile, the highest individual value in that study; its taxon-representative value at the rear molariform tooth position was 8,983 N. These are different statistics, and the study also found body mass to be the main explanation for bite-force variation among crocodilians.

## movementStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A satellite-tracking study in northern Australia moved five adult male saltwater crocodiles 100–320 km from their capture sites. Their sea movements were generally directed toward the original sites, but none completed the return around Cobourg Peninsula. One control animal later made a roughly 900 km journey over six months before returning. The result shows that translocation outcomes depend on geographic barriers and individual variation; it is not a fixed movement rule for all saltwater crocodiles.
