## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Playback surveys recorded buffy-tufted-ear marmosets in 15 of 45 Atlantic Forest remnants around Alfenas, Minas Gerais, Brazil, during 2010–2013. Candidate models considered patch area, functional isolation, visibility between remnants and surrounding land-use composition. Patch area was the leading variable explaining occurrence, and the best model had a reported p-value of 0.023. The authors then tested candidate models in 20 fragments near Poço Fundo; the three models reported as validated concerned the other two primate species, not this marmoset. That means this study did not validate a marmoset model at the second site. It does not mean the species was absent there or establish ecology across its range.
