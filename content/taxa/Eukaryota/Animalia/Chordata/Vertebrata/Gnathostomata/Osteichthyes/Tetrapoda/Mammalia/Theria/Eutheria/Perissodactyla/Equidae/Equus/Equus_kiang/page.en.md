## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Kiangs are associated with the Tibetan Plateau and nearby high-elevation regions and eat grasses and other low-growing plants. Darker upperparts and pale underparts aid comparison; activity and group size require seasonal and observational context.
