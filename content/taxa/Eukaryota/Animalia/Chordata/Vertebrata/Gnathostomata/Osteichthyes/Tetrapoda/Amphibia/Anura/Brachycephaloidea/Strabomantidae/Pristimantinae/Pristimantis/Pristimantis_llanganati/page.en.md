## Three nearby Llanganates records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc4926633-4mjgx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The description reported three Napo Province localities in Ecuador’s Llanganates National Park along the Salcedo–Tena road, at 2253–2883 m. Their maximum straight-line separation was 6.5 km. These locality records are not a complete range survey, and continuous forest mapped at the sites does not establish continuous frog occupancy.

## Night vegetation encounters in two forest settings
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc4926633-4mjgx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
All specimens were collected at night on vegetation: four in a flooded forest-edge area and two in primary forest. This identifies the sampled encounter settings, without demonstrating breeding in the flooded area, preference for forest edges or absence of daytime activity. Population data were unavailable in the description.
