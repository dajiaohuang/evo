A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. ## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-34rlc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/text/en -->
The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is blank, not explicitly no filter. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness. Raven measurements use 90% energy bandwidth, 5% and 95% energy frequencies, and peak frequency for the dominant component; these are not interchangeable with absolute frequency extrema or the fundamental. The Methods print FFT1024, 50% overlap, hop256 samples and grid93.8 Hz; figure captions describe a different display setting. No raw-audio reanalysis or silent correction of those settings is claimed.
