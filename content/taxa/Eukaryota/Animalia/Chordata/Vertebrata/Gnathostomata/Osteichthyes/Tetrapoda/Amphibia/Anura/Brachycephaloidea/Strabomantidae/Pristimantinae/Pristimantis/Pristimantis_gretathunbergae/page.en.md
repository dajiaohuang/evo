## Accepted name and placement

COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis gretathunbergae Mebert, González-Pinzón, Miranda, Griffith, Veselý, Schmid & Batista, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Night perches and daytime shelter
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763812-scdv8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Records ranged from 718 to 1439 m in montane forest. At night, frogs were seen 0.5–3 m above ground on bark and bromeliads; by day they hid between bromeliad leaves. These observations describe the study material and do not establish all activity periods or exclusive microhabitat requirements. Diet was explicitly unknown; the authors’ comparison with other Pristimantis is not treated as a measured diet.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Three egg-guarding females
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763812-scdv8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Three females were observed guarding egg clutches for at least four nights in bromeliads or on moss-covered branches. Males also called during rainy-season observations. The guarding duration is a minimum observation interval, not total care duration, and the records do not establish every population’s reproductive season or offspring survival.
