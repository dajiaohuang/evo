## form

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The account identifies “true foxes” by a combination of relatively small size, flattened skull, pointed muzzle and large bushy tail, then contrasts fennec foxes in deserts with Arctic foxes in tundra. These genus-level cues do not replace species-level identification.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Habitats described across the genus extend from desert to Arctic conditions and include mountains and plains; food, shelter and human settlement affect where foxes occur. This is a genus-level contrast, not a claim that every fox species occupies all of these habitats.
