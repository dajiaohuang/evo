## Named records in a community-conserved cloud forest
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The 2021 Santa Cruz Tepetotutla checklist includes Quilticohyla acrochorda. These selected Quilticohyla records come from the 2018–2019 survey in the Chinantla region of Oaxaca. Four observers made eleven five-day trips across wet and dry seasons, in pristine and managed sites, and photographed and released encountered animals. The recorded elevations and CNAR photographic catalogue numbers remain species-specific. They establish source-reported presence, not an exhaustive genus inventory, authenticated museum vouchers, global range limits or population trends.

## Conservation evidence has temporal and sampling limits

These are different area descriptions, not interchangeable estimates. The nineteen amphibian species and2,640effective survey hours refer to the overall checklist, not this genus alone. Historical IUCN, EVS and NOM-059 entries retain their distinct frameworks and are not current rechecked assessments. The accumulation curve did not reach an asymptote, and presence data do not quantify a causal management benefit, detection probability or absence outside sampled sites.
