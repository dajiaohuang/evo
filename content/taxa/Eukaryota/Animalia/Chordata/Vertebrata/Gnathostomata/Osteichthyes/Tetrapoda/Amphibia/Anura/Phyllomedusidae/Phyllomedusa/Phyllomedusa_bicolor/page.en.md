## Published reproductive observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
venancio-2010
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
In Acre, Brazil, a study observed three mating pairs in 2008–2009. At Catuaba, eggs were enclosed in a three-leaf chamber above a pond; larvae emerged after 14 days, but were not followed to metamorphosis. A separate São Jorge clutch contained 1,202 eggs. These observations concern the sampled sites and nests, not fixed species-wide clutch size or developmental duration.
