## Reproductive function and ancestry remain hypotheses

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/12/text/en -->

It did not establish an adhesive function or a causal effect on ovulation or oviposition in these genus members. Inferences of origins and losses use a fixed, pruned earlier tree and a limited ingroup sample; alternative optimizations remain possible. ## Recorded vocal structures in selected members
