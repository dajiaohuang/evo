primary-pmc13109738-73h3c

## Diet overlap does not measure competitive pressure
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/17/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13109738-73h3c
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/17/text/en -->
Pianka’s dietary overlap was reported as 97% between heymonsi and mukhlesuri, compared with 49% between heymonsi and lingnanica. ANOSIM found no significant heymonsi–mukhlesuri difference in prey abundance or volume matrices, while comparisons with lingnanica were significant. These analyses compare sampled prey assemblages; they do not establish absence of all ecological differences, resource scarcity, competitive exclusion or a causal mechanism maintaining coexistence. Percent overlap is an index reported on a percentage scale, not the percentage of meals containing the same prey.

These analyses compare sampled prey assemblages; they do not establish absence of all ecological differences, resource scarcity, competitive exclusion or a causal mechanism maintaining coexistence.
