# Ornithomimidae


## Read group evidence through bonebed formation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Sinornithomimus mud-trapped bonebed provides a specific behavioral case within Ornithomimidae. Postures of partially articulated skeletons, sedimentary structures and age sampling support an immature group becoming trapped together. Three histologically sampled animals and thirteen newly excavated skeletons at this locality do not define maturity ages, group sizes or diets for the whole family. Begin with formation processes before comparing life histories.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route introduces occurrence records for species authored in this batch, not a complete descendant catalogue or a common ecological summary. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes.
New text uses the 2008 published article’s scientific text (pp. 567–576), two tables and five figure captions. Table 1 on p. 570, Figure 3 on p. 571 and Figure 4 on p. 573 were also visually inspected; other images, actual specimens and original thin sections were not examined, and dating/orientation analyses were not rerun. Fourteen earlier individuals, thirteen new skeletons and three histological samples remain separate denominators. The 2003 original description and other works cited by this article were not freshly reviewed individually here. Annual assumptions/minimum ages, mass mortality interpretations and breeding hypotheses remain distinct.
