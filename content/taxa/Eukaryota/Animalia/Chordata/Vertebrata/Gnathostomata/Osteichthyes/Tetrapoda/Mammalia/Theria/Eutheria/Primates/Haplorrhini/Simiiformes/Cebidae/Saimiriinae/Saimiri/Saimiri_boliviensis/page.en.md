## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Two social groups in a captive open-diffusion experiment contained 23 Bolivian squirrel monkeys. Of 21 observers, 12 solved the apparatus task at least once, and solvers more often used the response direction demonstrated by a model. This was one artificial task experiment; it does not establish social learning in the wild or a species-wide cognitive capacity.
