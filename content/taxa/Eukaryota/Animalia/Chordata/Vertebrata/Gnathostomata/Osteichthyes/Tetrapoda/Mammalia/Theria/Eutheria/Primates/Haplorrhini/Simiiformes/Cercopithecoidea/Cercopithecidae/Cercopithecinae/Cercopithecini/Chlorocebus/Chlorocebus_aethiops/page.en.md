## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Batiero Church Forest in Tigray, researchers recorded grivet feeding from September 2012 to February 2013, sampling eight consecutive days per month. Seeds and leaves dominated early-dry-season records; as the dry season advanced, the recorded foods varied and included roots, flowers, bark and animal matter. The authors also reported feeding on crops and crop residues outside the forest.

## evidence-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
This is a seasonal study of opportunistically encountered groups in one small church forest. Its feeding observations describe that site and period; they do not estimate the species' range, population size or typical diet across its range.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
