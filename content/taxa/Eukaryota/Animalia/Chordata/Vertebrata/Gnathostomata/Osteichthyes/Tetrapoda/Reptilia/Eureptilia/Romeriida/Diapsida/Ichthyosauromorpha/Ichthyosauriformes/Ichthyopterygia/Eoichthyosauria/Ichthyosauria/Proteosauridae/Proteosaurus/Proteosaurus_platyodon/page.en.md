# Proteosaurus platyodon


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Proteosaurus platyodon is accepted in the fixed PBDB ichthyosauriformes working roster, with database parent Proteosaurus. This guide uses 6 eligible species-level occurrence records from 6 collections and 3 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts. In 5 records, the original identification or combination is connected to the database accepted name. ## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 182-item ichthyosauriformes working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Ichthyosauriformes storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled; external domain expert review has not occurred.
