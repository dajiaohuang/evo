## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A broad, roughly square mouth suits feeding on grasses near ground level, and the neck and shoulders form a prominent hump. A species overview does not erase taxonomic debate or differing conservation circumstances of northern and southern populations.
