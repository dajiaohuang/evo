## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Begin with Hending’s 2021 literature-based genus analysis for broad context: its Mirza density models found positive associations with temperature and precipitation, but not with measured vegetation-quality, human-footprint or elevation variables. Then read the coquereli page for population genetics and a Zombitse canopy survey, and the zaza page for Sahamalaza nest sharing and local density. The genus-level correlations are not causal or species-specific, and the local estimates are not directly interchangeable.
