# Chalicotherium

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Southern German chalicotheriine material identified as Chalicotherium goldfussi and the historical combination Chalicotherium (Anisodon) grande preserves large ungual phalanges and shortened hind limbs, while claw use and upright feeding posture remain functional reconstructions.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study reviews the relevant postcranial anatomy and clearly labels its historical taxonomy. Medium confidence preserves the distinction between observed bones and reconstructed posture or behavior.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Chalicotherium is retained at genus rank, but the evidence profile distinguishes C. goldfussi from material published under the historical combination Chalicotherium (Anisodon) grande.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The primary study’s own specimen labels prevent obsolete combinations from being silently treated as current genus membership.
<!-- /evo:text -->
