## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A direct census at the HPPB research forest of Andalas University in West Sumatra from June to November 2012 recorded 13 animals in three groups. Within the 0.75 km² study area, the reported density was 17.3 animals per km² and mean home range 0.13 km². The researchers also recorded nine food plants. These are small-site observations, not species-wide constants.
