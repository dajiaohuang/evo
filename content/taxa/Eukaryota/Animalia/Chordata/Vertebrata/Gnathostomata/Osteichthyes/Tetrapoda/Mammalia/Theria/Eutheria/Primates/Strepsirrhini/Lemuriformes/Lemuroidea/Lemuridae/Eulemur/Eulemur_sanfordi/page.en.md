## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The same preliminary Ankarana line census reported troop-size and density estimates for Sanford’s brown lemurs across primary, secondary and degraded forest during the 2012 dry season. The paper describes its estimates as among the first for this species in the Ankarana region, and warns that old estimates came from different areas. They are not current regional totals.
