## species delimitation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
When comparing the northern sportive lemur with L. ankaranensis, the study found only one chromosomal rearrangement difference, but mitochondrial relationships and genetic distances still supported treating them as separate species. Chromosomal similarity alone did not determine the paper's taxonomic conclusion.
