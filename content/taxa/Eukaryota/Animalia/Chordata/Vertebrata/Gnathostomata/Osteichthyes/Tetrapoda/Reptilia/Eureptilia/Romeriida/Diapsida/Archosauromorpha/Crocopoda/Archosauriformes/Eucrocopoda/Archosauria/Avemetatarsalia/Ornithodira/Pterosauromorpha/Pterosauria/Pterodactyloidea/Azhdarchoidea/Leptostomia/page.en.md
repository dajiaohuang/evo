# Leptostomia


## Read from two jaws to the probing hypothesis

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Start with the holotype and paratype in [L. begaaensis](Leptostomia_begaaensis/page.en.md): distinguish preserved fragments from estimated missing tips, then compare the upper-jaw ridge, lower-jaw groove, cross sections and foramina. Continue to maturity, relationships and the probing hypothesis. Missing bill tips leave remote touch open to testing. Two fragments referred to one species are not one associated individual or a complete animal reconstruction.

## Read horizons, hypotheses and database counts separately

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original description records Aferdou N’Chaft, Morocco, upper Ifezouane Formation, retaining ?Albian–lower Cenomanian. The 2023 account likewise retains an uncertain Albian–Cenomanian formulation. The 100.5–93.9 Ma bracket below belongs to the fixed database collection row rather than precise dating of the type. Read the fluvial setting separately from the probing hypothesis; one database occurrence is neither the two type specimens described in the paper nor a population count.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page adds the original-description author manuscript and the 2023 Leptostomia account. Figures 2 and 7 and table 1 were inspected; other images, supplemental matrices, raw XCT and specimens remain incomplete. Upper/lower-jaw species referral, maturity, relationships, probing and sensory ability retain their separate inference limits; missing lengths are not measurements of complete jaws. Database counts, ages, parent chains and review states remain, without remeasurement, independent reruns. Complete biology and reader delivery remain pending.
