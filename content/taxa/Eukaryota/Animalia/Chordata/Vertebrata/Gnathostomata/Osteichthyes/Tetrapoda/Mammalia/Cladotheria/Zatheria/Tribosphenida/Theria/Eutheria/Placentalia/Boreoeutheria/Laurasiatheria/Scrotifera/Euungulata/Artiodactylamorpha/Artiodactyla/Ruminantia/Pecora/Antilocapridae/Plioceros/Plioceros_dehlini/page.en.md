# Plioceros dehlini


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Plioceros dehlini is accepted in the fixed PBDB mammalia working roster, with database parent Plioceros. This guide uses 2 eligible species-level occurrence records from 2 collections and 2 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.
<!-- /evo:text -->


## Begin with named collections and strata

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Collection East of Garner Bridge (Nebraska), region code US; database age bracket 16.3–12.5 Ma, early interval Barstovian. Collection Plioceros Site (Nebraska), region code US, stratum name Ash Hollow; database age bracket 12.5–9.4 Ma, early interval Clarendonian. Locality names, region codes and stratigraphic names retain the source wording. This is not a complete species distribution list, and presence in one region does not prove absence elsewhere.

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 16.3–9.4 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is East of Garner Bridge, occurrence occ:184997 (16.3–12.5 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Plioceros Site, occurrence occ:186587 (12.5–9.4 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.

## Evidence limits

Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts.
