## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2004 study noted that *T. barbei* had been known mainly from museum specimens and field observations. The authors located a zoo animal and presented it as the first confirmed evidence of the species' continued existence since 1967. They described previously undocumented cranial, pelage and coloration features, provided the first molecular evidence used to distinguish the species, and mapped compiled known localities. A zoo specimen and historical locality map do not substitute for a current wild-population survey.
