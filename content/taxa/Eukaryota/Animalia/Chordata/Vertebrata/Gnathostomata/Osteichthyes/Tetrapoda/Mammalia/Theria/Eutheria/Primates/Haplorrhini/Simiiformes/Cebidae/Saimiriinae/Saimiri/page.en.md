## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The pinned COL26.8 primate roster lists eight accepted species in Saimiri: S. boliviensis, S. cassiquiarensis, S. collinsi, S. macrodon, S. oerstedii, S. sciureus, S. ustus and S. vanzolinii. This guide follows that version; sample names and species-level treatments differ among studies.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with the local Iquitos ecology study and Peruvian-territory distance sampling for S. macrodon, then read seasonal feeding and five-year Mamirauá monitoring for S. vanzolinii. Continue with Costa Rican habitat, vocalization and distribution studies of S. oerstedii. Existing pages for S. boliviensis, S. cassiquiarensis, S. collinsi, S. sciureus and S. ustus complete the set. Sites, designs and source taxonomic combinations differ, so density and behavior values should not be pooled.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Genus-level phylogenetic studies sample broad regions, but marker choice and specimen assignments produce differing relationship hypotheses. This reading path connects pinned accepted names to local primary studies; it does not resolve Saimiri taxonomy or automatically rewrite older source combinations as COL accepted species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The genus guide follows the pinned COL26.8 roster and does not imply that all taxonomic treatments use the same species boundaries. It connects studies from different sites and methods without comparing population density or social behavior across the genus. The Chinese genus name is editorial; the page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
