## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A May 2004 survey in Similipal Tiger Reserve, Odisha, used line transects over four consecutive days and covered 122 km. The researchers recorded 144 troops and 1,505 Hanuman langurs, with a mean of 9.6 ± 1.19 animals per troop. The paper identified the langurs as S. entellus entellus.

## evidence-boundary
