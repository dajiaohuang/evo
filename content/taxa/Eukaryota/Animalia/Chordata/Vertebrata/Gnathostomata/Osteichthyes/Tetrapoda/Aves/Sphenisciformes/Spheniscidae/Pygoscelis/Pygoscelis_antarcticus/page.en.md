## globalBreedingAssessment

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Strycker et al. (2020) combined ground counts with drone and satellite imagery and assembled records for 398 breeding sites. They estimated 3.42 million breeding pairs across 375 extant colonies. About 35% of colonies could not be compared with an adequate historical benchmark; estimates also differed in age and precision. This is a dated, mixed-method compilation rather than a single synchronized census or a present-day 2026 count.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 2020 compilation combines field, drone, satellite and historical data with uneven dates and precision; many colony trends remained unassessable. Its estimate should not be presented as a synchronized census or current 2026 abundance. The study uses the spelling P. antarctica while this page follows COL26.8 P. antarcticus. The page is not externally expert-reviewed.
