## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD summarizes the Golden Langur's distribution as lying between the Sankosh and Manas rivers on both sides of the India–Bhutan border. Srivastava's field surveys covered Manas National Park and several reserve forests in western Assam, India. That historical survey footprint describes the study design, not a current range assessment.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
This multi-stage study used a 1997 survey as its baseline, revisited the same transects in September–October 2001, and added areas missed earlier during 2002–2004. Four to seven observers used stratified-random transects across more than 100,000 ha in Manas National Park and the Ripu, Chirang and Manas reserve forests. Intensive work at two Manas and Chirang sites used monthly transects to follow 19 focal groups. This is a historical regional survey design, not a current species-wide population estimate.
