## Minute adults measured to the posterior vent angle
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5119241-56ktb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Female holotype MVZ 185344 was collected north of San Miguel Suchixtepec, Oaxaca, on 16 July 1976 at 2700 m. Its standard length, defined as snout to posterior angle of the vent, was 25.5 mm; length to the anterior angle was separately 23.6 mm, and tail length 22.9 mm. The variation sample comprised eight males, mean SL 25.7 mm and range 23.5–29.6 mm, and nine females, mean 26.5 mm and range 25.5–28.2 mm. Maxillary teeth were absent in the examined sample; mean vomerine counts were 4.8 in males and 5.7 in females. Posterior-vent SL is not silently equated with an anterior-vent measurement or a differently defined dataset length.

## Dated live notes and fading in preserved specimens
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5119241-56ktb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Live-colour notes from July 1976 and January 2001 described black flanks with fine white speckling and a broad brassy-copper dorsal band with thin black lines, occasional dark blotches and no conspicuous chevrons. The venter was pale and lightly speckled. Older preserved specimens had lightened, with a more obscure dorsal band and sometimes a herringbone pattern; the band was more prominent in life. The distinct observations are retained by preservation state rather than turning either band pattern into a universal identification criterion or demonstrated camouflage.

## Moist log refuges and conflicting elevation summaries
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5119241-56ktb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The distribution paragraph reported localities near San Miguel Suchixtepec and eastward in the Sierra Madre del Sur, with elevations 2490–2700 m. Its paratype list also contained eastward localities at 2870–2995 m and 3000 m; those entries remain explicit rather than being forced into the lower summary interval. July 1976 collections were under charred logs and nearby litter. In January 2001, three specimens occupied moist spaces beneath logs, loose bark or stump bark in an otherwise dry, logged pine-dominated slope. These are encounters, not proof of dependence on fire or logging, or of breeding under bark.

## A 2016 Critically Endangered recommendation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5119241-56ktb
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The authors recommended Critically Endangered in 2016, reporting drastic declines at the few known localities over 30–40 years, thought likely to exceed 80%, a known extent of occurrence much below 100 km² and continuing loss of montane-forest extent and quality. They said the decline was unexplained and further population and range surveys were urgently needed. The estimated decline is not a directly counted species-wide mortality rate; extent of occurrence is not occupied area, and this dated recommendation is not a verified current Red List assessment.
