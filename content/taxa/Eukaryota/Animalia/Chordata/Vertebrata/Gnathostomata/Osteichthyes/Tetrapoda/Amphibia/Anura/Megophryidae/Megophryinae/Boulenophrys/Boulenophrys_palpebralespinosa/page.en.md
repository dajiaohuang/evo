## A corrected two-male Hekou diagnosis
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9mf
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The diagnosis relied on earlier descriptions of Bozhujing, Hekou, material and reported two adult males at 36.2–38.0 mm snout–vent length. The remarks attributed revisions of the earlier one-male/one-female interpretation to two males, and of paired vocal sacs to a subgular vocal sac, to Fei and colleagues (2009). Anatomy included vomerine ridges and teeth, rough tuberculate skin, several prominent eyelid tubercles, half-webbed toes with wide fringes, and dense brown nuptial spines on fingers I and II. The grey-brown dorsal colour was explicitly described in preservative, not certified as live colour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Guangdong record rejected by voucher-image interpretation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9mf
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The monograph reviewed a published Tianjing Mountain, Guangdong, record far from the main regional distribution. Interpreting the earlier voucher figure, its authors reassigned that record to B. shimentaina and removed Guangdong from their B. palpebralespinosa account. This is an attributed identification decision based on the published image, not a newly examined Guangdong specimen or proof that B. palpebralespinosa cannot occur there.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-lx9mf
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys palpebralespinosa as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
