## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Kalatop-Khajjiar Wildlife Sanctuary in Chamba, two groups were followed for an autumn diet study from September to November 2020. The researchers collected 71 scan samples and recorded 20 plant species from 15 families. Leaves made up 84.32% of recorded feeding, with Hedera nepalensis, Quercus oblongata and Ilex dipyrena among the main foods.

## evidence-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Diet composition differed between the two study groups; the authors suggested that their different elevation distributions might help explain the contrast. Because sampling covered two groups and one autumn season at one sanctuary, it does not describe the species' full diet or current range.
