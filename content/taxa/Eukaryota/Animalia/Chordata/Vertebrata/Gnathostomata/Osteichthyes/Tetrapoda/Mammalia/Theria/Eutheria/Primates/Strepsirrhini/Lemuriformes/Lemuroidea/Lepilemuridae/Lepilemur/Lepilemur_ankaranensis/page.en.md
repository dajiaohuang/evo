## species delimitation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2006 molecular-phylogenetic and cytogenetic study included the Ankarana sportive lemur among eight distinguishable species. It reported only one chromosomal rearrangement between L. ankaranensis and L. septentrionalis, while mitochondrial relationships and genetic distances still supported treating them separately; this is the study's interpretation.
