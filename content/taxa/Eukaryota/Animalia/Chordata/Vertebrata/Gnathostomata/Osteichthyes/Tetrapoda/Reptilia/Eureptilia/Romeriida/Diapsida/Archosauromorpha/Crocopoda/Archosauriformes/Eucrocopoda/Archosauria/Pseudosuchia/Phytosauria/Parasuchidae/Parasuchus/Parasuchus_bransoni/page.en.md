## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 179-item other-archosauria working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Archosauria storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled; external domain expert review has not occurred.
This batch covers only the other-archosauria partition of the fixed PBDB Amniota working list, using external OIDs to exclude entries assigned to Dinosauria, Pterosauria and Crocodylomorpha partitions. The partition organizes dated database records; other phylogenetic schemes may place taxa differently, and membership is not an expert adjudication.
