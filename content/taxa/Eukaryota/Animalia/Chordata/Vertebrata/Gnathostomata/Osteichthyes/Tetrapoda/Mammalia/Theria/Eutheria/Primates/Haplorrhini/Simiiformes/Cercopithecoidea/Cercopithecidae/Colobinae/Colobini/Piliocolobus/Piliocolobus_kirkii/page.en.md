## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A systematic assessment on Unguja reported 4,725 survey hours and estimated 342 groups comprising 5,862 individuals, with an estimated area of occupancy of 376 km². The survey also estimated that 4,042 individuals were inside protected areas. These figures are the authors’ results for that survey, not present-day counts.
