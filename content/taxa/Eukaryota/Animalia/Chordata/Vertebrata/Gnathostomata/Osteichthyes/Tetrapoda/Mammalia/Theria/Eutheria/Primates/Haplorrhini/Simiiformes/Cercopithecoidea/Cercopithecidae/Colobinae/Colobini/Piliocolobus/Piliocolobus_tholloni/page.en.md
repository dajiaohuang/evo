## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Botsima in Salonga National Park, Maisels and colleagues followed one habituated group of more than 60 red colobus across a 250-ha study area. Over 12 months of feeding observations, the paper reported a diet of 60.7% leaves, 30.8% seeds, 7.1% ripe fruit and 1.4% flowers. The article used the name Colobus badius tholloni. Its figures describe that group and site, not a genus-wide or fixed species diet.
