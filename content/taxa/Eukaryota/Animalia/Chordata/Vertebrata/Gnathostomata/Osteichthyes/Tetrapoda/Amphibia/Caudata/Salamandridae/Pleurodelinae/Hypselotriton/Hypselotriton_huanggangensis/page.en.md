## Total length and the unresolved sample count
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11336389-ppwff
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported total length from snout tip to tail tip of 73.0–79.1 mm in adult males and 79.6–89.4 mm in adult females. The type-material account lists seven males and two females, while the measurement methods say seven specimens were measured. The difference remains unresolved; neither denominator is silently substituted for the other.

## A shaded waterhole at the type locality
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11336389-ppwff
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The description knew the species only from the western side of the Wuyishan Mountains in northeastern Jiangxi. Newts were found in a small shaded waterhole among mountain shrubs at 84 m elevation, with observations in July, September and February. These records establish the observed microhabitat and months, without proving breeding timing, complete seasonal activity or a universal habitat requirement.
