# Tamarinus kulina

## Name combination and publication year

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Lopes et al. described this species as *Saguinus kulina* in a 2023 PeerJ paper, with the type locality in the Baixo Juruá Extractive Reserve on the right bank of the Andirá River, Amazonas, Brazil. COL26.8 retains the combination *Tamarinus kulina* and lists the authorship year as 2022. The current MDD page accepts *S. kulina* and lists *Tamarinus kulina* as a name combination for the same taxon; the older local MDD v2.5 sidecar predates the species. This cross-reference resolves the name link without changing the pinned COL name or authorship year.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Pelage diagnosis and alternative genus names

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original description combined ddRAD genomic analyses, pelage characters and geographic evidence to support a distinct evolutionary lineage. The shoulder mantle and forelimbs have a yellow subterminal band on dark brown-black hairs, unlike the orange band of the close relative *T. mystax*; the underparts are darker. A separate 2023 genomic-systematics study treated the recognized Tamarinus species and subspecies.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Specimen localities and known river boundaries

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The type and referred specimens in the original description came from the Juruá–Tefé interfluvial region of Amazonas, Brazil; multiple collection localities from 2018–2019 were recorded in terra firme forest. The paper bounds the known range between the right bank of the Juruá River and the left bank of the Tefé River. These are specimen records in a taxonomic study, not proof that the species is restricted to terra firme forest and not a population census. The current MDD page lists its IUCN status as Not Evaluated.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The original description provides diagnostic pelage, river-bounded range and specimen localities, but no account of this species' diet, group behavior, field density or population trend. The current MDD links *Tamarinus kulina* as a name combination under *Saguinus kulina*, resolving the name link missing from the older local sidecar; the pinned COL26.8 authorship year is preserved. The page meets the first-round species-introduction standard, but is not a complete natural history or population assessment. It.

## Genomic and morphological samples have different scopes

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The description examined 38 specimens morphologically and used 25 mystax-group samples plus three S. inustus outgroups for ddRAD analyses. Filtering retained 556 loci and 196,771 nucleotides. This samples genomic regions rather than whole genomes; the 28 samples do not represent a census of kulina. The taxonomic argument combines lineage, coat and locality evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Retain the conflict between type assignment and a figure caption

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The type account assigns adult male IDSM03594 (BJ015), collected on 18 July 2018, as the kulina holotype. Figure 4 instead labels that collection number S. mystax. The article alone does not resolve this labeling conflict; the caption does not replace the type designation here. Publication was in 2023, while the species heading itself gives 2022; nomenclatural availability is not independently adjudicated.
