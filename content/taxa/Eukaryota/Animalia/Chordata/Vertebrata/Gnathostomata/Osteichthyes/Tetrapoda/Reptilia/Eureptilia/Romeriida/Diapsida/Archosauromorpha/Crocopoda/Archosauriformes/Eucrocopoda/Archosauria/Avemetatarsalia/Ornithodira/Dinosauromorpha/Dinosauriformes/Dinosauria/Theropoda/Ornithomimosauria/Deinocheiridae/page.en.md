# Deinocheiridae


## Research classifications of Beishanlong and Deinocheirus

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2014 Deinocheirus study included Beishanlong in its Deinocheiridae discussion, whereas the earlier Beishanlong description left relationships with Harpymimus and others incompletely resolved; current PBDB places Beishanlong outside this family path. Different material and matrices are compared without replacing the catalogue with either paper’s tree. Beishanlong growth lines do not establish Deinocheirus age, and Deinocheirus fish remains do not establish diet in every related lineage.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Compare maturity before size and diet

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with specimen association, fibular histology and the 626 kg estimate on [Beishanlong grandis](../Beishanlong/Beishanlong_grandis/page.en.md), then examine the composite reconstruction and gastrolith/fish association on [Deinocheirus mirificus](Deinocheirus/Deinocheirus_mirificus/page.en.md). Their material, histology and behavioral hypotheses remain separate: growth lines are not feeding evidence, and estimated mass is not weighing. Finally compare dated phylogenies with catalogue positions, retaining unresolved relationships rather than turning missing bones into known features.

## Catalogue and study members under the family name

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2014 Deinocheiridae analysis connected Deinocheirus, Garudimimus and Beishanlong, using non-cursorial limb proportions as comparative evidence. Membership in the current PBDB family path differs from that paper, so historical phylogeny and the fixed catalogue remain separate. Deinocheirus size, gastroliths and fish remains have named specimen support; they do not supply every family member’s mass, omnivorous diet or environment. Each member still requires its own descriptive material.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Start with specimens, then compare functional inference

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Start with the holotype, referred specimens and composite reconstruction on [Deinocheirus mirificus](Deinocheirus/Deinocheirus_mirificus/page.en.md), then examine what the broad beak, hindlimbs and in situ fish remains support. Compare named material, strata and research classification on [Garudimimus brevipes](Garudimimus/Garudimimus_brevipes/page.en.md) and [Beishanlong grandis](../Beishanlong/Beishanlong_grandis/page.en.md); existing occurrence guides do not establish reexamination of their morphological matrices. Returning to Deinocheirus, separate skeletal structures, estimated mass, suction feeding, tail plumage and phylogeny, without using one species to complete the whole group.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route introduces occurrence records for species authored in this batch, not a complete descendant catalogue or a common ecological summary. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes.
