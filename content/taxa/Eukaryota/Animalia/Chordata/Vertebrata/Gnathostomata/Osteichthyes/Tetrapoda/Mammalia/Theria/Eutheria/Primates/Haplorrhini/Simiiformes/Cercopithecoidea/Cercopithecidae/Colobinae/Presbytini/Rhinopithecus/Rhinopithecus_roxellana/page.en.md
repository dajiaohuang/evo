## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A Sichuan distribution study combined field surveys, protected-area records and historical records, defining 2001–2023 as its current period. The authors reported that the species' distribution in Sichuan was confined to elevations of about 1,000–4,000 m in the Qionglai and Minshan ranges, with no current presence recorded in four northeastern Sichuan areas below 1,000 m. This is a regional assessment for Sichuan, not the complete distribution across China.
