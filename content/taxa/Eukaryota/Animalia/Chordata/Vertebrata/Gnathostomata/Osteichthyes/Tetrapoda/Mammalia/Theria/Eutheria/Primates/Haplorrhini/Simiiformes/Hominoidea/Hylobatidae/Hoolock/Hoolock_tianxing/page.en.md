## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Acoustic surveys at six sites in Kachin State and three in Shan State, Myanmar, recorded 23 and 21 gibbon groups, respectively. Genetic identification from skin and saliva matched Hoolock tianxing reference sequences at 99.54–100% identity. The results confirm occurrence at surveyed sites, not a range-wide population estimate; Kayah and Kayin sites were inaccessible.
