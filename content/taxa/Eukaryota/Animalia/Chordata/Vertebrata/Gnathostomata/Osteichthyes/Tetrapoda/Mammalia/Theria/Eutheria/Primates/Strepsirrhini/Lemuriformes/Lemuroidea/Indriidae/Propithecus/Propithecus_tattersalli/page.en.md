## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Quéméré and colleagues analyzed dry-season feces from 96 individuals sampled across the fragmented range in Daraina using plant-DNA metabarcoding. They detected at least 130 plant species from 80 genera and 49 families. Habitat type and openness were associated with diet composition, and feces from forest-edge groups included cultivated or naturalized plants. The result is a range-wide sample for one season, not a complete year-round menu.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Fecal DNA data were collected in the dry season; plant detections do not measure consumed biomass or establish year-round diet. This profile is not externally expert-reviewed.
