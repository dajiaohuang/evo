# Ichthyostega

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Devonian tetrapod whose three-dimensional limb reconstruction provides a bounded test of joint mobility and the mechanical requirements of a typical lateral-sequence walk.
<!-- /evo:text -->
