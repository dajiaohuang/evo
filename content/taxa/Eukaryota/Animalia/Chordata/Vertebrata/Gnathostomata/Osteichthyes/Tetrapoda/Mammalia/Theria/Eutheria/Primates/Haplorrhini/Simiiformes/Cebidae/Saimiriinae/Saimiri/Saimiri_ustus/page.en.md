## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The supplement lists bare-eared squirrel monkey observations and vocalizations in Amazonas, Rondônia, and Mato Grosso, including Urucurituba, Apuí, Humaitá, Porto Velho, Itapuã do Oeste, and Cotriguaçu. The records cover selected survey sites, not the full species range or abundance.
