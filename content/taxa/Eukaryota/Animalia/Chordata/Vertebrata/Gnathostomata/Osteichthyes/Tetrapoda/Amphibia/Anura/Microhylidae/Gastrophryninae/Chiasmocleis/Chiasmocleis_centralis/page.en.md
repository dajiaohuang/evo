The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A single juvenile with a molecularly supported assignment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12654886-tz22
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The 2025 study collected juvenile MPEG 44026 at Conceição do Araguaia, southeastern Pará, at 220 m and measured snout–vent length as 16.3 mm. Its 16S rRNA fragment had uncorrected pairwise distances of 0.2–0.6% from cited comparisons, including 0.2% to MPEG 29414 and 0.6% to CHUNB 51830. The authors interpreted this as supporting assignment to C. centralis. This one juvenile is not an adult size endpoint, a population sample or an independent certification of all current taxonomic boundaries.
