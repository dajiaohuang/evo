## northeasternSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Schüßler and colleagues searched 109 nocturnal transects totaling 303.73 km across northeastern Madagascar during three periods in 2019, 2021 and 2022. They reported 23 direct observation occasions and compiled 31 locality records from earlier literature. These are sightings and mapped occurrence records, not counts of unique animals.

## habitatObservations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The survey recorded sightings in undisturbed, selectively logged and heavily logged primary forest, as well as in woody fallow and vanilla agroforestry; none was recorded in grass- or shrub-dominated fallows. The authors noted the small sample and did not statistically test the visually different vegetation structure. The records show where this survey found the lemur, not a general tolerance threshold for habitat disturbance.

## analamazaotraHomeRange

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Biebouw radiotracked animals at Analamazaotra Special Reserve in 2007. One complete sleeping group of four adults was followed for eight months and one partial group of two females for three months. Their group ranges were 35.5 and 16.0 ha by 100% minimum-convex-polygon estimates, or 15.2 and 7.1 ha by the 95% kernel method. These small-sample, site-specific estimates should be read with the method and group coverage attached.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## modelBoundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2023 paper fitted separate ecological-niche models to literature records, new observations and both combined; predicted suitability differed with the input set. Its mapped areas are model outputs based on occurrence and environmental layers, not direct surveys of every predicted locality or a census. The separate Analamazaotra tree-hole study offers a site-specific follow-up on sleeping-site use.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 2023 survey concerns northeastern Madagascar and 23 observation occasions; its niche models use presence records and environmental layers rather than animal counts. The 2007 home-range evidence is limited to two sleeping groups at Analamazaotra and varies by estimator. These sources do not establish current population size, trend or a complete confirmed range. The Chinese display name is editorial. No dossier or external expert review was added. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
