## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2023 study compiled surveys of the five social groups at Bawangling, Hainan, from 2002 to 2020, covering 72 months, 1,464 observation hours and 274 GPS location records. The combined range of the five groups was calculated as 14.89 km². Remote sensing and habitat-suitability modelling for 2000–2020 estimated 26.9 km² of highly and moderately suitable habitat, with low connectivity among group ranges. These results are specific to Bawangling and the study period and model; they are not a current population census.
