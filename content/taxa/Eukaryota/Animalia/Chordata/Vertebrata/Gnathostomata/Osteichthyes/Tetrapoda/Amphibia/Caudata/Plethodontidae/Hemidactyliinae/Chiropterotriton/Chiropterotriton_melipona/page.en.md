## Type material and a separately counted adult sample
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-mfq4y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Male holotype MVZ 200726 was collected near Xicotepec de Juárez, Puebla, on 8 December 1983 at 1080 m; the estimated location carried a maximum error distance of 1.0 km. Seven paratypes comprised four males and three females, with two further referred specimens from Cuetzalan. The diagnosis summarized four adult males, mean SVL 29.2 mm and range 26.4–31.4 mm, and three females, mean 28.5 mm and range 27.1–29.8 mm. Its four-male diagnostic count is retained separately from the holotype plus four male paratypes. These sample ranges are not species-wide size limits.

## Long tails and a comparison-text conflict
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-mfq4y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The diagnosis reported complete tails longer than SVL, with mean tail/SVL 1.16 in males and 1.11 in females. Adpressed limbs remained separated, averaging 2.3 and 1.8 costal folds respectively; digits were slender with small subterminal pads and little or no webbing. Several comparison paragraphs call the tail shorter than those of C. ceronorum, C. perotensis or C. orculus while printing larger ratios for C. melipona. The numerical diagnosis is recorded with this wording conflict; the comparison adjectives are not silently corrected or presented as a resolved diagnosis.

## A photographed referred animal and faded preserved types
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-mfq4y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
No live-colour notes were available for the type series. The account instead used a photograph of referred specimen IBH 30112, describing a dark brown head with pale grey specks, an orange-brown dorsal band continuing along the tail, and a coppery iris. The faded preserved holotype was yellowish tan with a bright yellow dorsal stripe and very pale venter. These are different specimens and preservation states; yellow preserved colours are not substituted for the unrecorded live holotype colour.

## One CT specimen with an explicit maturity caveat
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-mfq4y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The osteological account examined the anterior skeleton of male MVZ 178706, SVL 28.5 mm, and warned that it might be sexually immature and not represent adult conditions. Its delicate skull had thin roofing bones and a relatively large frontoparietal fontanel. The hand phalangeal formula was 1–2–3–2 on each side; mesopodial cartilages were not mineralized. These observations belong to this scanned specimen. They do not establish invariant adult skull architecture, developmental timing or measured mechanical weakness throughout the species.

## Northern Puebla plant encounters and a dated conservation proposal
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7258950-mfq4y
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The 2020 account reported Sierra Norte localities near Cuetzalan, Xocoyolo and Xicotepec de Juárez at 690–1420 m, with collections from banana plants and bromeliads and sympatry with Aquiloeurycea quetzalanensis. Occurrence between known localities was a prediction. The authors reported mature-forest loss and recent encounters in small forest patches, secondary vegetation and coffee plantations, recommending Endangered under B1ab(iii). This is their dated proposal, not a verified current Red List category. The observations do not establish an occupied continuous range, a diet linked to bananas or breeding in bromeliads.
