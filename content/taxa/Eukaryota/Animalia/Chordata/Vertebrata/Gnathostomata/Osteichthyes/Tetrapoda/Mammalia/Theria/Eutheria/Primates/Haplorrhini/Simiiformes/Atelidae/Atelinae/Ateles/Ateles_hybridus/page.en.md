# Ateles hybridus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID J8P7. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Variegated Spider Monkey, but lists the authority year as 1828 while COL26.8 gives 1829; the page retains the COL26.8 name record. The field studies summarized here concern separate Colombian groups and sites.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Feeding research

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At Serranía de Las Quinchas, researchers followed one habituated group for 2.5 years and recorded 847 hours of behavior. The group fed on at least 123 plant species; ripe fruit accounted for 92% of feeding time. Monthly fruit availability did not predict the monthly share of fruit in this group's diet. These are observations from one group and site.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Social-network research

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A separate study at Hacienda San Juan de Carare used observations of one group in a 65-ha seasonally flooded rainforest fragment to compare social-network contact with direct physical contact in parasite-transmission models. It examined contact structure; it did not measure parasite prevalence or demonstrate transmission in the group.
<!-- /evo:text -->

Network-based transmission modeling is not evidence of infection or measured parasite prevalence.
