# Dicerorhinus

## Dicerorhinus in the pinned catalogue

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The pinned COL26.8 catalogue places one accepted species in Dicerorhinus: the Sumatran rhinoceros. Its hair is conspicuous among living rhinoceroses, it has two horns, and calf and adult coats differ. The catalogue supports its name and placement; the existing species account supports morphology. One accepted species refers to this catalogue release, rather than a fossil history or a list of every historical name.
