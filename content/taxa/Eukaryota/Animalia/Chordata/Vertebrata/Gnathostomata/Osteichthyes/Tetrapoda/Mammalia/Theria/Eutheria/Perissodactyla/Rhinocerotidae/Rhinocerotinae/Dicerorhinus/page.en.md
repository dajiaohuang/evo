## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This catalogue includes the Sumatran rhinoceros in this genus. Hair is more conspicuous than in other living rhinos, and two horns are present. Juvenile and adult coats must be distinguished.
