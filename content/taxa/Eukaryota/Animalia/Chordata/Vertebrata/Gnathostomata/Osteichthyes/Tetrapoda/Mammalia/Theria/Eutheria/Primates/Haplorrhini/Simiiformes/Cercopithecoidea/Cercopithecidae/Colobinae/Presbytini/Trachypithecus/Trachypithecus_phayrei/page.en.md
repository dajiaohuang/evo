## taxonomy_story

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Roos and colleagues analyzed 41 mitochondrial genomes from georeferenced fecal samples and museum specimens, including two holotypes. In material then assigned to *T. phayrei*, they identified West, Central and East geographic clades. The Central boundary remained uncertain, and samples at one monastery included both Central and East haplotypes. The paper proposed revised names and species limits using mitochondrial, morphological and ecological differences. This page retains the fixed COL26.8 identity and does not present that proposal as the only classification.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Geographic clades are based on limited museum and fecal samples; the Central boundary and mixed monastery haplotypes remain uncertain. The paper's taxonomic revisions are kept distinct from the pinned catalogue identity. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation; the page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
