## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2000 revision limited the then-known records to Ankarana Reserve and listed several sample sites within it. The authors also described a mix of natural forest, savanna edge and woodland affected by grazing and fire. These are site descriptions, not a summary of all habitat used by the species.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the study's comparison, six Ankarana specimens had skull lengths of 31.8–33.6 mm, while the Ankarafantsika sample of golden-brown mouse lemurs measured 33.1–36.1 mm. The authors noted similarities in pelage and body proportions but used cranial, dental and genetic comparisons to distinguish them; these small samples do not identify every individual by size alone.
