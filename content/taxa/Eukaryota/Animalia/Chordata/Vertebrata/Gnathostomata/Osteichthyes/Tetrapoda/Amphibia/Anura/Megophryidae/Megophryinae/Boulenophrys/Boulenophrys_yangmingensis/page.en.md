## Yangming type locality without an invented upper elevation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5hr5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Male holotype SYS a002887 came from Yangming Mountain, Shuangpai, Hunan, at approximately 1360 m. Citing Lyu and colleagues (2020), the monograph knew this species only from the type locality above 1300 m. That statement supplies no upper elevation bound and is retained without inventing one; it is not proof of absence outside the mountain.

## Seven male types and one female
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5hr5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The examined type material comprised seven adult males at 33.2–37.1 mm snout–vent length and one adult female at 45.2 mm. The diagnosis described weak vomerine ridges without teeth and rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow lateral fringes. Breeding males had villiform black nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. The female measurement remains one observation rather than a size range, and these anatomical characters do not determine reproductive timing.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v5hr5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys yangmingensis as with heels overlapping when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
