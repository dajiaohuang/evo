# Plecturocebus cinerascens

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The supplement lists ashy titi records at Apuí, Amazonas, and Cotriguaçu, Mato Grosso, including vocalization records. These occurrences at surveyed localities do not represent the species-wide range or abundance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This guide summarizes records from selected sites; it is not a complete species account, range survey, population estimate, or conservation assessment. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation. This guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
