# Trachypithecus germaini

## Phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Roos, Nadler and Walter's 2008 analysis placed sampled *T. germaini* in one of five monophyletic lineages. The study used a 573-bp mitochondrial cytochrome-b fragment from 115 langurs, 68 with known collection origins, and proposed that the five lineages represented separate species. The authors also reported that relationships among the lineages were not well resolved. The result describes grouping at this marker, not a settled species tree or range-wide population boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->
