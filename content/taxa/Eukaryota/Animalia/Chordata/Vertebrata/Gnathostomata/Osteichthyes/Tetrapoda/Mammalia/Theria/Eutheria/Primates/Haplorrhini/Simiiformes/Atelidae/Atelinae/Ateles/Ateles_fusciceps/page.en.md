## distributionSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Cervera and Griffith surveyed a 1,500-km² landscape in Manabí, about 100 km southwest of the then-known range. After an initial encounter in 2014, they interviewed local residents and used call playback in six forest fragments between March and July 2015. Groups of one to seven were seen in four fragments, and 16 individuals were visually confirmed. The landscape was reported as 89% deforested, with most fragments under 300 ha.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 16 visually confirmed animals are not a census of the wider species or even a complete count of the Manabí population; interviews and playback detections do not demonstrate movement among fragments. The source names the Ecuadorian subspecies A. f. fusciceps. The Chinese common name is editorial; this page has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
