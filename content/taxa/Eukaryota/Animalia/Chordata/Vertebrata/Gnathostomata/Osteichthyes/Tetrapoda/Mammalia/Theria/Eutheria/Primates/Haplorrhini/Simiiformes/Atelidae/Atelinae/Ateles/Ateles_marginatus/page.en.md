## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
On the right bank of Brazil's middle Teles Pires River, one white-cheeked spider monkey locality overlaps a new record of the Chamek spider monkey. The study distinguished the two using black pelage and the presence or absence of white hairs on the forehead and cheeks, and proposed that river islands may provide crossing routes. It did not directly measure migration or gene flow; the authors call for more field records and genetic samples.
