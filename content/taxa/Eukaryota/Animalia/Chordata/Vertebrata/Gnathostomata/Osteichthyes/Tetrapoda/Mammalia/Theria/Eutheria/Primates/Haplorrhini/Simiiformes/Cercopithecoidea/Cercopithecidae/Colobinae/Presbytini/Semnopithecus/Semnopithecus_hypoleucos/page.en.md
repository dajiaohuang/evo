## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A South Indian comparison measured food items and activity budgets in S. hypoleucos and the Nilgiri langur, S. johnii. In the studied populations, S. johnii was more folivorous; S. hypoleucos showed a social organization similar to S. johnii, while its time spent in social activity was closer to the comparison literature for S. entellus.

## evidence-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors proposed that time spent resting to process leafy food could constrain social activity in S. johnii. That is an interpretation of their comparison, not a demonstrated mechanism for every group or a species-wide behavioral rule.
