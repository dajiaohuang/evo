## Accepted name and placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis tungurahua Reyes-Puig, Yánez-Muñoz, Cisneros-Heredia & Ramírez, 2011 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A female count that disagrees with the voucher list
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-4mjqq
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2022 paragraph says three females with body sizes of 20.15–22.87 mm but lists four voucher identifiers, DHMECN 14427, 14428, 14429 and 14433. It separately gives two males, DHMECN 14430–14431, at 15.87–16.77 mm and juvenile DHMECN 14432 at 14.02 mm. The female-count discrepancy is unresolved, and the juvenile is not combined with adults. Salmon-red groin and ventral limb coloration plus dorsolateral folds form the account’s diagnostic summary; these samples do not establish population dimorphism.

## Low vegetation, ground sticks and an excluded candidate
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-4mjqq
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The named account describes collection on fern and shrub leaves 30–40 cm above ground and on dry sticks at ground level, with some specimens in pitfall traps, and reports an extended known elevational interval of 2400–2900 m. A separate Machay specimen, DHMECN 14445 at 2600 m, was labelled aff. tungurahua and differed in white rather than red ventral and groin markings. That candidate record is not merged into the accepted species’ confirmed specimen sample. Encounter heights and pitfall captures do not establish an exclusive substrate or a breeding site.

## Placement in the proposed Cryptomantis framework
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12717849-4mjqq
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 study includes Pristimantis tungurahua in the bicantus species group of its proposed subgenus Cryptomantis. The sampled major clade had bootstrap support 100 and its two group clades 99. Morphological synapomorphies were explicitly unknown. The dated placement does not certify a current catalogue subgenus, a complete range for this species or identity of every historical broadly identified voucher. Unconfirmed candidate lineages remain separate from accepted named species.

## Characters in the paganus comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12717849-4mjqq
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The paper distinguishes the new paganus population from Pristimantis tungurahua using a W-shaped marking evident in males but not females, low ulnar tubercles and subconical upper-eyelid tubercles. These are comparative taxonomic characters, not a newly sampled population-wide trait-frequency analysis. Sex and body-region wording are retained. A selected fold or tubercle alone is not a complete identification key, and these comparisons provide no new diet, clutch-size or developmental measurements for this species.
