## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Galago matschiei Lorenz, 1917 as species ID 6JW34 under genus ID 4LR5. The Mammal Diversity Database (MDD) uses Spectacled Lesser Galago, lists Burundi, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda and Uganda, and gives a Least Concern label. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## rangeAndHabitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Butynski and de Jong’s 2025 account describes G. matschiei from southern Uganda and the western Albertine Rift, from at least southern Rwanda (probably northern Burundi) north to west of Lake Albert in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. They report a known elevational range of 670–2,500 m and associate the species with moist mid-altitude and montane forests. These are the authors’ bounded range and habitat descriptions, not a complete census of every country in the MDD list.

## rangeBoundaryEvidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2025 study revisited an earlier claim that G. matschiei occurred on Mount Moroto, east of Uganda’s Lake Victoria–Nile River Basin. The authors compared four adult Moroto museum specimens collected in 1952 with two adult G. matschiei specimens from the Democratic Republic of the Congo and identified all four Moroto animals as G. senegalensis. The paper reports no evidence for G. matschiei east of the basin. It also summarizes Ambrose’s 2000–2001 surveys of 14 Ugandan forests (83 nights, 308 hours), which recorded 30 G. matschiei in Mabira, Mpanga and Zika forests west of the Nile; those are historical site records, not a population estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 2025 paper tests the proposed eastern occurrence and summarizes selected Uganda forest surveys; it is not a range-wide census or full ecological study. The 30 historical records are not a population estimate. MDD country records are coarse. COL26.8 ID 6JW34 fixes page identity; the Chinese display name is editorial.
