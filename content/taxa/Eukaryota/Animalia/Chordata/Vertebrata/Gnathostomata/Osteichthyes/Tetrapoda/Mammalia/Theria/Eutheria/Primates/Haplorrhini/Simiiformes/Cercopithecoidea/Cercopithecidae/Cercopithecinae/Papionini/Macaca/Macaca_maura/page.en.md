## local population and diet

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Murti et al. directly observed eight groups totaling 193 Moor macaques in Karaenta Forest; group sizes were 13–39, with local densities of 52–134 individuals/km² and a mean of 92. Most individuals were seen on the ground, and feeding records listed 27 plant species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The survey covered eight groups in Karaenta Forest for four months; the authors note incomplete seasonal coverage and that results do not represent the whole park. The Chinese label is an editorial translation; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
