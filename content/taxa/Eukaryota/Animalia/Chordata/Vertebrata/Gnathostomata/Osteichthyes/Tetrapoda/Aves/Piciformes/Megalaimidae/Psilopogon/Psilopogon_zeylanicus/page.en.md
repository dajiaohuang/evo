# Psilopogon zeylanicus

## Tracing the historical genus name

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 places this page at Psilopogon zeylanicus (7WP6S). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 9707 and eBird 2021 row 8674 use the same name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-15471F21; BirdTree row 8629 gives Megalaima zeylanica, with match type 1BL to 1BT. Read COL older-combination record 7VSWH points to this accepted ID. These associations locate historical data without establishing identical species boundaries across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Measured samples and beak/wing averages

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 5 birds: 2 female, 3 male and 0 unsexed; 4 have complete measurement sets. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 25.5 mm, beak width 12.2 mm, tarsus 28.5 mm, unflattened wing 108.8 mm and hand-wing index 18. Inference=NO does not establish an identical count for every field; complete-set birds and total samples remain separate. The index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed, and beak width is not a test of fruit swallowing or pecking performance.

## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Frugivore is the Metadata category for predominantly fruit resources; trophic level is separately Herbivore. These categories describe different levels of classification rather than individual feeding observations. Category thresholds are not measured diet percentages for a locality or season. Fruit species, food size, digestion and seed-dispersal experiments are absent here, leaving detailed diet and dispersal effects unsupported.

## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Insessorial means primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, without implying never descending to the ground. Habitat is Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is sedentary, without establishing lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted here, and categories do not establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This guide uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two name crosswalks, relevant Metadata definitions and local COL name shards. Morphometric means and compiled mass have separate denominators; one-to-one names and catalogue synonyms do not prove identical concepts across dates. Individual specimens, the whole workbook, Dunning’s book and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation status remain unsupported here. No expert review or complete natural-history monograph is counted.
