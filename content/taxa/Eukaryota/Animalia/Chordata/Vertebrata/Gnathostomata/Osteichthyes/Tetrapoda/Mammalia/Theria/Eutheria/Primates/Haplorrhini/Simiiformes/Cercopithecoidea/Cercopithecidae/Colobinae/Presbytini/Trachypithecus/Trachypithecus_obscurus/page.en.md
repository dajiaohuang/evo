## feeding_ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A Malaysian study from April 2014 to March 2015 compared study groups of dusky langurs and long-tailed macaques in a landscape of urban and agroforested areas and forest fragments. Researchers recorded 4,029 feeding records for the langurs, covering 130 plant species; leaves made up 51% of records and fruits 40%. Monthly fruit eating tracked fruit availability at the site, and dietary overlap between the primates was also related to fruit availability. These proportions and relationships describe the study site and groups only.
