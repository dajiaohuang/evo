## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The pointed, mobile upper lip gathers browse, unlike the white rhino’s broad mouth. Adhering soil affects apparent colour, so colour alone does not reliably distinguish black and white rhinoceroses.
