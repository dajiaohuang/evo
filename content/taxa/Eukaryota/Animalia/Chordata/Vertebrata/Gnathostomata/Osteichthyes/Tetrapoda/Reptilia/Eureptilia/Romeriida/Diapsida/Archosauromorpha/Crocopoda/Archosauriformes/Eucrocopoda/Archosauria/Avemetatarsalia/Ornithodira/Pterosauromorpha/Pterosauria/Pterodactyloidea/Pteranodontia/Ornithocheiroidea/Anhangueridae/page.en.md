# Anhangueridae


## Extend holotype reading to comparative bodies and isolated wing bones

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Alongside the existing Australian holotype route, read the [large comparative Tropeognathus specimen](Tropeognathinae/Tropeognathus/Tropeognathus_mesembrinus/page.en.md). MN 6594-V associates cranial and body material; MCT 1838-R is only a distal humerus and MPSC R 1395 a partial wing. Identification levels and completeness differ, with the latter two indeterminate at family level rather than three individuals of one species. Read material identity before comparing size and growth.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Reconstructions, histology and span estimates answer different questions

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the same study, cranial photographs show retained fragments and the outline reconstruction marks preserved regions in grey. Sampled wing-phalanx histology informs growth interpretation, while span estimates reconstruct missing segments using comparative skeletons. An intact outline does not establish an intact skull, near cessation of growth does not establish a species maximum, and estimated span does not directly supply diet or flight performance. This selected material route does not generalize specimen limits into traits shared by every family member.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Read taxonomic names with definitions and dates

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The supplementary PBDB directory places Haliskia and Ferrodraco within Anhangueridae, the latter through Tropeognathinae. The 2019 Ferrodraco description uses names including Ornithocheiridae, while the 2024 Haliskia study discusses different matrices and clade definitions. Names cannot be interchanged solely by spelling, nor can the papers and catalogue be merged into an undisputed tree. Directory ancestry supports page storage; publication results retain their own material and analytical scope.

## A route through two Australian holotypes

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
This guide selects two specimens: AODF 876 of Ferrodraco lentoni and KK F1426 of Haliskia peterseni. Compare preserved elements and horizons first, then dentition, maturity evidence and span reconstructions, followed by phylogenetic analyses. Haliskia’s hyoid and feeding hypotheses cannot become direct evidence for Ferrodraco, and depositional context does not supply a complete life history. These pages are selected taxon examples, not a complete accepted-species index for the family or a uniform dietary or behavioral account for all Anhangueridae.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a selected route through two holotype studies and large comparative material. Family membership, definitions and biological information remain incomplete; neither a complete family synthesis. PBDB and COL concepts remain separate.
This route introduces occurrence records for species authored in this batch, not a complete descendant catalogue or a common ecological summary. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. Figures 1–3 and their captions were inspected here, distinguishing preserved regions from reconstructed outlines; all images, the whole paper and specimens have not been audited.
