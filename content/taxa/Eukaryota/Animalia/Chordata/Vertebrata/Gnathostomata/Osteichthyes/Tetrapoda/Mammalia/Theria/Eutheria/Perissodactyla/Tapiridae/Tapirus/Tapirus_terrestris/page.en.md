## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A mobile proboscis and a short crest-like mane characterize the head. Lowland tapirs eat leaves, fruit and other plant material. Diet varies with place and season; fruit eating alone does not quantify seed-dispersal effects.
