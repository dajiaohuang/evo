## Accepted name and placement

The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10348306-m7yd

Boana geographica was consistently observed at ChP2 during the Tena monitoring effort. Its reported visual detection probability was 0.39, compared with 0.83 for eDNA. The authors attribute the contrast to molecular units detected at additional sampling sites, but read numbers are not frog counts and waterborne signals do not automatically locate living individuals. The estimates are specific to the six-site, six-visit wet-season design and its reference database; they do not define complete distribution or a species-wide monitoring success rate.
