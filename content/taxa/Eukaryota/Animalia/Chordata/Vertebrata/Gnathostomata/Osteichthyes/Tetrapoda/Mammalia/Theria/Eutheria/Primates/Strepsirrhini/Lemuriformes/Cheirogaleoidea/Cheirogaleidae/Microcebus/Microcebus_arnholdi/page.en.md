# Microcebus arnholdi

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Louis et al. (2008) described this mouse lemur in a revision of northern and northwestern Madagascar, as one of two new mouse lemur species associated with the Montagne d'Ambre and Antafondro forests. MDD displays the English name Montagne d'Ambre Mouse Lemur; this page uses it to retain the study's locality cue.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The MDD record places holotype AMB5.39 in Montagne d'Ambre National Park and Special Reserve at about 990 m. This type locality and elevation locate the name-bearing material; they do not map the species' full current range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The MDD page also lists Arnhold's Mouse Lemur as an English common name. This page follows its primary display name; the COL26.8 accepted identity remains keyed by `6RFN3`.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This account is limited to the 2008 northern revision, type material and locality names; it does not provide a current full range, population trend, ecological or conservation assessment. The Chinese name is editorial. The page is.
