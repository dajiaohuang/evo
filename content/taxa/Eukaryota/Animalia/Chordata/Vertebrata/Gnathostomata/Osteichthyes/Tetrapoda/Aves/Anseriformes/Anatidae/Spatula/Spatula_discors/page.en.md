# Spatula discors

## Historical Anas combination and the pinned name

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows pinned COL26.8 Spatula discors (9HKHF). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 398 and the eBird 2021 crosswalk use the same name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-90E2543E; BirdTree gives Anas discors, labelled 1BL to 1BT. Pinned COL synonym DGNG points to accepted ID 9HKHF. Name links do not verify every species boundary across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Beak, wing and compiled mass have different denominators

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 6 birds (2 female and 4 male), with 4 complete measurements. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 31.8 mm, beak width at the nostrils 15.6 mm, unflattened wing 178.3 mm and hand-wing index 53.8. These are species averages; the index is defined by Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed. Mass 359.44 g comes separately from Dunning’s literature compilation and cannot inherit the morphology sample size. Inference=NO retains the workbook’s non-inference flag without supplying individual bounds from unread raw material.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Read feeding niche and trophic level separately

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Habitat is Wetland and density code 3 means open habitats. Trophic.Niche=Herbivore aquatic denotes an aquatic-plant-oriented niche; Trophic.Level=Omnivore denotes a category with more balanced use across trophic levels. The columns have different definitions. Original labels are retained without rewriting either from the other or deriving seasonal diet percentages. Aquatic lifestyle means substantial time on water and feeding while afloat or diving; it does not specify dive depth, frequency or the species’ particular feeding action.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Movement category and the observations still needed

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Migration=3 classifies the species by long-distance migration in the majority of its population. The row contains no individual tracks, departure month or full route, and does not require every bird to move identically. Hand-wing index and habitat labels cannot replace behavioural observations. Locality, timing and explicit sources are needed to connect the category to particular populations’ life histories.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Scope is the selected historical AVONET v7 rows, relevant field definitions, two name crosswalks and local COL accepted/synonym records. The whole workbook, individual raw measurements, Dunning’s book and a new full methods-paper reading are excluded. Morphology counts are not literature-mass denominators, and ecological categories are not seasonal diet surveys. Plumage identification, breeding, lifespan, current range and conservation status need other sources; no expert review or complete monograph is claimed.
