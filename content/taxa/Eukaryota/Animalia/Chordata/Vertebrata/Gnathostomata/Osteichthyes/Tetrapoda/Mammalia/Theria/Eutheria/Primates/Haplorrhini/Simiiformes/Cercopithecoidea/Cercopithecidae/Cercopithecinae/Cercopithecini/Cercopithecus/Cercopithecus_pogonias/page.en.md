## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Published dietary comparisons distinguish three Gabon samples of crowned guenons: fruit formed about 80% of the Makokou sample and 69% at Lopé, while seeds were the largest category (50%) in the Makandé sample. These records come from separate sites and studies, not one continuous range-wide survey. A ten-year Lopé community study also recorded the species among the forest primates whose feeding shifted as fruit availability changed. Together they show why a single diet label hides local and seasonal differences.
