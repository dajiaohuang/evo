# Piliocolobus epieni

## Survey records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2013–2014 survey across the presumed range in Nigeria’s central Niger Delta reported observations concentrated in two adjoining forest clusters. The author described the combined area as about 7,896 ha and estimated, from cumulative observations, that only hundreds of animals might remain at that time. Those are dated field and landscape estimates; they should not be read as a current census.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
