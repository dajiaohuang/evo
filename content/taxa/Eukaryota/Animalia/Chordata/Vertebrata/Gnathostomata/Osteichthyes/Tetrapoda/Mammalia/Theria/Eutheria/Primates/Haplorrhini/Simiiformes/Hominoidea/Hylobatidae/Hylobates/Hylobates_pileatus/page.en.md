## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Phoonjampa and Brockelman surveyed protected areas in southeastern Thailand during 2004–2005, using auditory censuses in the largest forest areas and interviews or presence checks in smaller ones. Their paper estimated about 3,800 km² of suitable canopy forest and roughly 3,000 breeding groups, or about 12,000 individuals, across four of the largest forest areas. These are historical, method-dependent estimates; they are not a current census or a count for the entire species range.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The estimates describe selected Thai protected areas surveyed in 2004–2005 and depend on acoustic sampling and extrapolation; they are not current or range-wide totals. This profile is not externally expert-reviewed.
