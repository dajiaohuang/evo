# Carnufex carolinensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Carnufex carolinensis is documented by partial, skeletally immature holotype NCSM 21558 and referred humerus NCSM 21623. Its estimated size and placement among high-tier terrestrial predators are study reconstructions, not direct evidence of feeding behaviour or ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Faunivory is inferred from serrated, recurved teeth rather than directly observed feeding
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Terrestrial Pekin Formation assemblage in the study's reconstruction
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Treated as terrestrial in the cited comparison; locomotor performance was not directly tested
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Approximately 3 m in the study's reconstruction, with an immature femur-length estimate of 354–441 mm
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Top-tier terrestrial predator in the sampled Pekin Formation assemblage, as interpreted by the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
NCSM 21558 preserves parts of the skull, vertebral column, ribs and left humerus
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
The reconstructed skull is slender and ornamented, with an estimated length greater than 50 cm
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Premaxillary teeth are elongate, serrated and slightly recurved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The holotype anatomy, Chatham County horizon and approximately 231 Ma age are direct specimen and geological records; total length, body-mass proxy, guild assignment and basal crocodylomorph placement remain bounded analytical interpretations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
