## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The current MDD page uses the English name Cat Ba Langur and associates the species with Cat Ba Island, Vietnam. Hendershott, Behie and Rawson studied two reproductive groups on the island, observing their use of rocks, sparsely vegetated areas, slopes and cliffs in karst terrain. The local sample does not represent every group or habitat on the island.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study collected 180 days of observations from two reproductive groups of 7 and 10–13 animals, with estimated home ranges of about 22 and 50 ha. Both groups had smaller ranges in the dry season, but the study could not test seasonal variation statistically. Scans were often on rocks, sparsely vegetated areas, exposed slopes and steep cliffs; foraging occurred mainly on gradual slopes, especially in the dry season. The journal full text is restricted, so this page uses only results in the public abstract.
