## Species-group placement remained unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7936570-6wsrp
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The study explicitly left Raorchestes indigo outside its defined species groups because phylogenetic relationships were unresolved and clearly shared diagnostic characters were lacking. Its green dorsal colour and occasional blue groin, axillary or thigh colour resemble beddomii, but superficial similarity did not resolve its placement. Acoustic characters were not studied. Missing calls and superficial resemblance cannot be replaced with relatives’ durations, frequencies or a certified group assignment. This is an unresolved grouping within the accepted genus, not evidence that the species itself is invalid. The authors identified further acoustic, ecological, reproductive and behavioural evidence as potential ways to resolve the uncertainty.
