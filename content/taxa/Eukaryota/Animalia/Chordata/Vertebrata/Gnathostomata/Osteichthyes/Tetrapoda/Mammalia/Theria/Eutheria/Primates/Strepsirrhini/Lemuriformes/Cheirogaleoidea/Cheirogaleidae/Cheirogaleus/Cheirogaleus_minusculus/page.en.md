## evidence_limit

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The name Lesser Iron-gray Dwarf Lemur was based on a museum holotype from Ambositra; the Natural History Museum catalogue records the specimen as a juvenile. A multilocus taxonomic study explicitly found the available evidence insufficient to assess the species' status. This page preserves the catalogue identity without adding behaviour, ecology, or a complete range unsupported by field observations.
