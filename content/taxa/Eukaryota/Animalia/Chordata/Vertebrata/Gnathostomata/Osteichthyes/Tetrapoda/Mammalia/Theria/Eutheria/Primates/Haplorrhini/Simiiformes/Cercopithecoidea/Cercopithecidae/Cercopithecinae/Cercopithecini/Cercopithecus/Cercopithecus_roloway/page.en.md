## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Curtin studied feeding and ranging at Bia National Park in western Ghana in 1976–1977. The chapter reports 1,250 mapped feeding trees and more than 3,000 feeding visits, involving plant parts from over 130 tree, climber and epiphyte species. Mature fruit pulp, arthropods, oil-rich seeds and young leaves were prominent foods. The chapter was published under the combination Cercopithecus diana roloway; the pinned COL26.8 record used here is C. roloway (69KD3). The decades-old, single-site observations are not a current range survey.
