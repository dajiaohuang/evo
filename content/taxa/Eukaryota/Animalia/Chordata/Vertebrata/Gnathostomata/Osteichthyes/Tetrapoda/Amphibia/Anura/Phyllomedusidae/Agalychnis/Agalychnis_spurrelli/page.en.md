## Two males above a roadside puddle
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11156955-65jq8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
On 12 March 2023, the authors recorded two males at San Pablo, Cañar, Ecuador, at 420 m elevation. They perched on a branch 120–190 cm above a puddle beside a secondary road. Vouchers ZSFQ5182 and ZSFQ5183 measured 64 and 62 mm snout–vent length. The survey found them at that road edge rather than in the lightly disturbed forest it searched; this local contrast does not demonstrate a general preference for roads or an egg-laying site.

## A locality inside a reported information gap
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11156955-65jq8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The authors described the new Cañar record as filling a 328 km information gap along Ecuador’s western Andean foothills, between nearby records 189 km to the north and 159 km to the south. These distances describe gaps between known localities, not demonstrated continuous occupancy or a dispersal event. The broader Costa Rica-to-Ecuador range in the account came from cited sources.

## Non-systematic records have explicit timing and measurement limits
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11156955-65jq8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The article uses free, unrestricted visual searches and manual captures rather than equal-effort detection sampling. Methods describe January2014–December2023 expeditions, but the species accounts also contain a2008 alytolylax and February2024 muricatus record; those dates remain visible instead of being forced into the stated window. GPS elevations are encounter values, distinct from camp elevations. Small-animal SVLs are said to be rounded to0.1 mm, while many printed records contain two decimals; they are retained as reported without certifying finer precision. Tissue collection and deposited vouchers are provenance claims, not independently inspected specimens or newly reported genetic identifications.
