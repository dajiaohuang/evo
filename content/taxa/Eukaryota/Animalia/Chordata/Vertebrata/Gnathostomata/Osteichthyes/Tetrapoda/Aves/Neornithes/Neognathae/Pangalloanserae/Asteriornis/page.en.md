# Asteriornis

## A genus introduced through its type specimen

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/0/title/en -->
A genus introduced through its type specimen
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/0/text/en -->
This route introduces genus Asteriornis through NHMM 2013 008, the holotype of A. maastrichtensis. It comes from the Maastricht Formation at Belgium’s CBR-Romontbos quarry; the cited 2020 study constrains its horizon to 66.8–66.7 Ma. CT imaging reveals a nearly complete three-dimensional skull and associated postcranial bones. This named fossil is the starting material, rather than a complete sample of variation across the genus.

## Limits and open questions

<!-- evo:text /records/readerLimitations/en -->
This genus route is limited to a named holotype and its cited study, without a complete genus range, ecology or variation account. The route’s 66.8–66.7 Ma interval applies to the cited sample; a global species range and first and last appearances remain unresolved. Recorded database placement and atlas navigation do not independently resolve competing phylogenetic results. This bilingual guide has no external expert review.
