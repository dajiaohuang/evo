## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Ramnagar, Nepal, researchers recorded feeding time for 21 unprovisioned adults over a year using instantaneous samples, totaling 773 observation hours. Their geophagy study examined soil eating in a leaf-and-fruit-eating foregut fermenter, separating proposed mineral, detoxification and acidity-buffering explanations.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors found geophagy consistent with sodium acquisition rather than detoxification or prevention of acidosis. The result addresses the studied langurs and soils at Ramnagar; it does not establish one function for soil eating in every population.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page summarizes a one-year behavioral study of 21 adults at one Nepal locality. It does not establish the function of geophagy across the species, and it is not externally expert-reviewed.
