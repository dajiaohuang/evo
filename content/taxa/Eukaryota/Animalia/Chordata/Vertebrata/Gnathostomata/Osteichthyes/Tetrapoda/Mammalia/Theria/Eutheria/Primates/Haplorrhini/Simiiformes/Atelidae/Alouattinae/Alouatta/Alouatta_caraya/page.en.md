## evolution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A study across 22 sites in Argentina, Paraguay and Brazil analysed genetic samples from 256 black-and-gold howlers. Four sites were sampled before reservoir flooding; samples from those sites showed higher mitochondrial and microsatellite diversity. The findings apply to the sampled populations and markers.

## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study recorded 14 mitochondrial haplotypes at four sites that were later flooded; 13 were not found at the other sampled sites. The authors suggest that some local genetic variation may have been lost with inundation, while noting that sampling did not cover the species’ full range and cannot show that these variants are absent from other populations.
