## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Kibale National Park, Uganda, Struhsaker compared diet observations from six neighboring redtail groups and three hybrid monkeys at Ngogo over 12 years, with observations from one group at Kanyawara. Diet varied among months, years and neighboring groups. The hybrid monkeys differed in some comparisons, including greater invertebrate consumption. The paper used the name C. ascanius schmidti; the page follows the pinned COL26.8 species record C. ascanius (STDP) and does not generalize the local results to every population.
