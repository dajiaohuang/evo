## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Frasier et al. described Ankarana dwarf lemur from material in northern Madagascar's dry and transitional forests, using genetic divergence and morphological comparisons to place it in the C. medius group. The original account covered localities including Ankarana, Andrafiamena-Andavakoera, and Analamerana; its conclusions reflect the collected material and survey coverage at the time.

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Rapid biodiversity assessments in the SAVA region in 2017 recorded the species at 10 sites, nine not previously known; two Analamanara sites were about 20 km south of its then-known southern limit. The study added occurrence records, not a complete survey of continuous distribution.
