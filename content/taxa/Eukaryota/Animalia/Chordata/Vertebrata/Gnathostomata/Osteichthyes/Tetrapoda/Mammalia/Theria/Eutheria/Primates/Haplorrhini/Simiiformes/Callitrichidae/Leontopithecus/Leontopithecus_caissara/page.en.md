# Leontopithecus caissara

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID 3T6ZQ. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Black-faced Lion Tamarin. The studies summarized here examine small island and mainland groups in coastal southeastern Brazil; their estimates and habitat models are tied to those field samples.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Population surveys

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Line-transect surveys on Superagüi Island ran from November 2000 to January 2002. The team recorded perpendicular sighting distances and cluster sizes and estimated density at 1.79 individuals per km², with an island population estimate of 205. Combining this with a mainland extrapolation, the paper gave a total estimate of 392 (95% interval 218–1,026).
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Habitat use

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A separate study compared habitat use by four groups—two on Superagüi Island and two on the mainland—against vegetation availability in their home ranges. It reported different patterns between island and mainland sites and used those local habitat relationships to model carrying capacity. The model depends on the mapped areas, group home ranges and vegetation assumptions used by that study.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Scope of the evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The transect estimate is historical and based on a low encounter rate; the authors noted that the recommended sighting count would require an impractical survey effort. The habitat-capacity results are model outputs, not present-day counts. Neither study measures current population trend.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Both sources are tied to field samples and models from the early 2000s. The reported population estimate is not a current count, and carrying capacity is not evidence that habitat is occupied. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation. This page is.
