## Separate generalist lifestyle from omnivore level

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
All eight rows have Invertivore niche, defined by at least 60% of resources from terrestrial invertebrates; six have Carnivore level, defined by at least 70% from live invertebrate or vertebrate animals. [Argya fulva](Argya_fulva/page.en.md) and [Argya aylmeri](Argya_aylmeri/page.en.md) retain Omnivore level, for roughly equal resource proportions across trophic levels; without underlying diet evidence these codes are neither harmonized nor given an inferred explanation. Seven have Generalist lifestyle and rubiginosa has Terrestrial; Generalist describes activity classes rather than an omnivore diet. Six habitats are Shrubland and altirostris/earlei are Wetland; five density codes are 2 and squamiceps/fulva/earlei have code 3, for habitat rather than bird abundance. All eight have migration 1 without implying lifelong immobility; Wetland does not establish swimming, diving or wading behavior.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route uses eight selected historical AVONET v7 rows, same-name eBird and older Turdoides BirdTree links, relevant definitions and the read COL ar/tu shards. Eight catalogue synonyms supply name correspondence without proving identical historical and current species boundaries. Morphology birds, complete sets and Dunning compiled mass retain separate denominators. Original specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Invertivore niche and Carnivore/Omnivore level are not interchangeable. No measured diet, prey list, capture behavior, plumage, calls, breeding, current range or migration route is supplied. Source family Leiotrichidae and catalogue Leiothrichidae retain their spellings; Generalist lifestyle is not Omnivore diet. Complete natural history are not counted.
