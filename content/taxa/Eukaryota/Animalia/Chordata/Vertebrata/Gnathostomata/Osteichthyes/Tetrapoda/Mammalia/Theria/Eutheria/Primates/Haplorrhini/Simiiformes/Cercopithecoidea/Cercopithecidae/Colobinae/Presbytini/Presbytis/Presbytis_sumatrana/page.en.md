## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2024 bachelor's thesis compared local habitat use by the Black Sumatran Langur and silvered langur in forest near the Martabe mine area of South Tapanuli. The authors reported more than 50% home-range overlap and predominant use of the 6–20 m canopy; they found no clear evidence of interspecific competition.

## limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
This is a local study in forest near an industrial area. It does not establish home ranges, competition or abundance trends across Sumatra, and this page does not infer the mechanism of long-term coexistence.
