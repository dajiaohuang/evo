## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/14/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5683629-6mbtp
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/14/text/en -->
For Hoplobatrachus occipitalis, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.

## Species Index and site index have different meanings
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/18/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13072372-6mbtp
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/18/text/en -->
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI4 for Hoplobatrachus occipitalis is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS1/2/4/8, TBS0/1/2/4/8 and SBS0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.

## primaryStudyScopes / sectionEvidence / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/primaryStudyScopes/1/sectionEvidence/2/evidenceSummary/en -->
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI4 for Hoplobatrachus occipitalis is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS1/2/4/8, TBS0/1/2/4/8 and SBS0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
