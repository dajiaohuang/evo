## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts *Tamarinus subgrisescens*. Earlier ecological studies treated it as *Saguinus imperator subgrisescens*. Gregorin et al. (2023) compared pelage, cranial morphology, cytochrome-b and distribution and supported it as distinct from *T. imperator*. A contact zone and hybridization remain unknown.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A four-month primate survey in Pando recorded group size, habitat use and locomotion under the name *Saguinus imperator*, and found a more restricted local distribution than previously reported. Gregorin et al. assigned Bolivian Pando records to subgrisescens using locality and specimen evidence. This remains one historical regional survey, not a current population or species-wide habitat estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Local field work used the older subspecies combination. Taxonomic studies support species-level separation, while the contact zone and hybridization status remain unknown. Not every old *S. imperator* record can be reassigned to this species. This bilingual draft has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
