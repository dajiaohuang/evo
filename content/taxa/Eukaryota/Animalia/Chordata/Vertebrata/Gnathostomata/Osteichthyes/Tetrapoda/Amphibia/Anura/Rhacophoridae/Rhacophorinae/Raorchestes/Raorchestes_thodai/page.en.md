## A synonymy suggestion based on unresolved distinguishing characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7936570-4rjb8
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The remarks traced thodai to two original specimens from Ooty in the Nilgiris, reported at 1,980 m elevation. Subsequent collections and the authors’ surveys did not obtain new specimens they could confidently assign to it. Iris lines, flank/groin colour and dorsal markings overlapped with variation within signatus, leading the authors to suggest thodai was likely a junior subjective synonym of signatus. The pinned catalogue nonetheless retains thodai as accepted. This page records the disagreement rather than silently adopting the suggested synonymy. Non-detection does not prove extinction, while uncertain colour distinctions do not identify every unassigned Ooty frog as thodai or signatus.
