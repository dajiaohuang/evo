# Callithrix flaviceps

## Population evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinto and colleagues used repeated transects to survey five primate species, including the buffy-headed marmoset, in montane rainforest in eastern Brazil. They compared habitat, density and group size and reported higher primate densities in secondary vegetation in the surveyed area; that overall result is not specific to C. flaviceps. Group sizes and densities at Augusto Ruschi Biological Reserve were generally lower than published values from other Atlantic Forest sites, with hunting pressure and habitat carrying capacity suggested as possible explanations. This is a local survey plus literature comparison, not a current population estimate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Source-linked local transect survey;. The secondary-vegetation pattern is reported for the surveyed primate community, not as a species-specific result; cross-site density comparisons are not standardized.

## The name in the historical life-history record

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Callithrix flaviceps is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Callitrichidae. This section associates the same-name, same-family historical record in Ernest’s 2003 table. Its taxonomy is Wilson and Reeder 1993; matching names do not resolve later splits and lumps.
