## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinto and colleagues used repeated transects to survey five primate species, including the buffy-headed marmoset, in montane rainforest in eastern Brazil. They compared habitat, density and group size and reported higher primate densities in secondary vegetation in the surveyed area; that overall result is not specific to C. flaviceps. Group sizes and densities at Augusto Ruschi Biological Reserve were generally lower than published values from other Atlantic Forest sites, with hunting pressure and habitat carrying capacity suggested as possible explanations. This is a local survey plus literature comparison, not a current population estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
