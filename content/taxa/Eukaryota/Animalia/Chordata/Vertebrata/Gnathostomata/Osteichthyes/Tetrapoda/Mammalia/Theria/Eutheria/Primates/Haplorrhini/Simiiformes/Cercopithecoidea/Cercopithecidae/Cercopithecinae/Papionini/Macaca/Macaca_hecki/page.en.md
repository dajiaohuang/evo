## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Sugardjito et al. (1989) reported M. hecki at only Tangale and Panua reserves during their 1987–1988 northern Sulawesi survey, with site densities of 3.3–5.2 monkeys/km². The authors suggested that its range and abundance may have declined relative to Groves's earlier observations; this is an interpretation of a historical survey, not a measurement of the present trend.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
