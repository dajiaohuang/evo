## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts five species in Hapalemur: H. alaotrensis, H. aureus, H. griseus, H. meridionalis and H. occidentalis; it places the greater bamboo lemur separately in Prolemur. For the species pages in this batch, begin with the 2002 mitochondrial study spanning northern to southeastern Madagascar: the authors examined 115 captured animals from six surveys in 1997–2001, but treated several taxa now recognized as species within H. griseus. Next read the Ranomafana comparison of foods and excreta in three sympatric bamboo lemurs, then the winter feeding sample from Mandena. Finish by comparing the historical canoe surveys at Alaotra with small-fragment observations on the Masoala Peninsula, keeping the limits of encounter-rate indices and single-group data in view. This order follows evidence types, not evolutionary relationships, a current range map or a complete genus-level natural history; classification here follows COL26.8. For the new H. griseus page (6LC7M), continue with Overdorff et al.’s seasonal observations from one southeastern group and Grassi’s three-group Ranomafana comparison; Yamashita et al. provide a separate, semi-quantitative look at cyanide in bamboo foods and excreta. Keep each study tied to its own sites and methods.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This introduction uses a small set of regional studies as a reading path. Their dates, taxonomic labels, sampling methods and sites differ; do not combine them into a complete range, a shared diet or a final taxonomy. These pages are not externally expert-reviewed. The added H. griseus reading path is also limited to the stated southeastern Madagascar and Ranomafana samples.
