# Rhaeticosaurus mertensi

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A small-bodied Late Triassic plesiosaurian represented by holotype LWL-MFN P 64047; its articulated anatomy and phylogenetic analysis extend the sampled plesiosaurian record into the Rhaetian without making the specimen a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Diet and prey are not directly preserved by the partial holotype
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Marine mudstone depositional setting in the Rhaetian Exter Formation; habitual microhabitat remains unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Four similar flippers and a short tail support plesiosaurian hydrofoil and paraxial-locomotion comparisons, not a measured gait
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
The subadult holotype has an estimated total length of 237 cm; a genus-wide size range is not inferred
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Marine predatory ecology is a broad plesiosaurian interpretation, not a directly observed feeding record
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
Partial articulated skeleton preserving occiput, lower jaw, vertebral column, girdles and left limbs
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Ventrally concave V-shaped neurocentral sutures in anterior and middle cervical vertebrae
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Foreshortened zeugopodials and highly vascularized humeral and femoral bone tissue
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Wintrich et al. describe holotype LWL-MFN P 64047 from a defined bed in the Exter Formation at Bonenburg, 21 m below the Triassic–Jurassic boundary. Figures and supplementary sections document the skeleton and histology; basal pliosaurid placement, fast growth and physiological implications remain topology- and interpretation-dependent.
<!-- /evo:text -->
