## population-study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Aquino and Encarnación’s 1988 transect study in northeastern Peru reported estimated densities of about 46.3 individuals/km² in lowland forests and 24.2 individuals/km² in highland forests for A. nancymai. Their paper described the taxon south of the Amazon–Marañón rivers, with a northern enclave between the Tigre and Pastaza rivers. These are historical, site- and method-dependent estimates, not a present-day census or complete range assessment.
