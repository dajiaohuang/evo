# Kretzoiarctos beatrix

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A left P4, right M1 and partial mandible from two Spanish middle Miocene localities were analysed in a fossil-plus-living ursid matrix that recovers the sampled genus within Ailuropodinae.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
