# Nycticebus borneanus

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD currently displays Bornean Slow Loris and lists the type locality as the Sakaiam River in Sanggau District, West Kalimantan, Indonesia; the holotype is USNM:MAMM:142234. The type locality identifies the original name-bearing material, not a complete current range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In 2013, Munds and colleagues separated Bornean material previously treated under N. menagensis into regional facial-mask forms and supported N. borneanus as one of four species; they also called for genetic confirmation. A 2023 molecular study did not produce a clean match between those four pelage-based forms and the main genetic lineages. The page retains the COL26.8 name while leaving the relationship open to further comparison.
<!-- /evo:text -->
