## Species Index and site index have different meanings
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13072372-v5rfg
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI10 for Sclerophrys berghei is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS1/2/4/8, TBS0/1/2/4/8 and SBS0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
