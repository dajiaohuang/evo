# Coelodonta

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A rhinoceros genus with species- and region-specific paleoecology, from Tibetan and East Asian forms to the northern Eurasian woolly rhinoceros C. antiquitatis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Typical grazing in C. antiquitatis; mixed feeding in some C. nihowanensis samples
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Cold dry steppe for C. antiquitatis; other species and regions vary
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Terrestrial and graviportal
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Large; about 2900 kg reviewed for C. antiquitatis
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Cold-steppe grazer in C. antiquitatis, not a genus-wide constant
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
Long hair and dense underwool in C. antiquitatis
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Low head posture and long nasal horn in C. antiquitatis
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
High-crowned teeth associated with abrasive grazing
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Ma and colleagues synthesize isotopic and morphological evidence across species. The 14.4 ka genome constrains one late C. antiquitatis individual and is not used to support morphology, ecology or an exact global extinction boundary.
<!-- /evo:text -->
