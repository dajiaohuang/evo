## field_study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At a 25-ha lowland forest site in Mandena, southeastern Madagascar, Lahann used capture, radio-tracking, and focal observations during rainy seasons in 2002 and 2003–2004. The study identified two presumed family groups; body mass and tail circumference increased from November to February, consistent with pre-hibernation fattening. The author also noted that the local timing and duration of hibernation remained unresolved.
