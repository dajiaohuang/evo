## evolution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A Y-chromosome comparison of ten gibbon species included 26 captive males, three of them agile gibbons. The tree built from seven concatenated amplicons left H. agilis, the silvery gibbon and the Kloss's gibbon in an unresolved trichotomy; the authors noted weak support and insufficient Y-chromosome variation. This is a limited paternal-marker result, not a complete species tree.

## evolution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A separate gibbon opsin study collected blood from 37 agile gibbons at zoos in Indonesia and Thailand and from animals kept by local residents in Kalimantan; two samples were excluded from opsin genotyping. In male H. agilis samples, detected L/M opsin gene-conversion tracts were mostly in introns. The study analyzed gene sequences, not color-vision behavior, and does not establish species-wide prevalence.
