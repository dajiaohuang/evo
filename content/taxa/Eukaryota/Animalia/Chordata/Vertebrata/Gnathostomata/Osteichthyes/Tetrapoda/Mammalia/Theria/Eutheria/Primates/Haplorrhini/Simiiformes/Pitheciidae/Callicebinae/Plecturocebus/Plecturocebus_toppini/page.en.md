## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Using field observations, photographs, museum specimens and literature, Vermeer and Tello-Alvarado (2015) reassigned reddish titis from southern Peru, western Brazil and northwestern Bolivia to Toppin's titi. Their six-week 2013 survey around Atalaya covered 144 km of trails and 169 km by canoe; the authors still considered the northern limit unknown and river boundaries provisional.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2015 paper reinstated Callicebus toppini after its earlier synonym treatment and compared it with C. cupreus as then used. COL26.8 accepts the combination Plecturocebus toppini; this page does not turn the authors' proposed river limits into verified taxonomic boundaries.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 2015 range is a hypothesis based on limited observations, specimens and literature; the paper explicitly leaves the northern limit and some river boundaries unresolved. No current abundance is inferred. The Chinese name is editorial; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
