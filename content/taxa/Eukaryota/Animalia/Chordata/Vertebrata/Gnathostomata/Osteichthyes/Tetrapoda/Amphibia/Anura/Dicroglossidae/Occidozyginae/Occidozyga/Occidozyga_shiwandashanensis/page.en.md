## A Vietnamese record beyond the Chinese type locality
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10589760-qt6br
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The study reported the species in Bac Giang, Vietnam, about 180 km from its Guangxi type locality. Vietnamese specimens came from 300–400 m elevation, while the account cited earlier Chinese records at 550–650 m. These separate records do not establish continuous occupancy between the sites or physiological altitude limits.

## Ponds, streams and ground at night
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10589760-qt6br
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Specimens were found between 19:00 and 23:00 on the ground, in small ponds and in small streams surrounded by mixed secondary evergreen forest. Females contained yellowish-cream eggs with dark poles. These observations document adult sites and egg appearance in the examined females; they do not identify an egg-laying site or establish the full reproductive cycle.
