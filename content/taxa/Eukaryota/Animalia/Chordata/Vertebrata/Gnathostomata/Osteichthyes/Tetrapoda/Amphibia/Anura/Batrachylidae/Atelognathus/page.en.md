## Shape decoupling is a bounded comparative hypothesis
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The whole sample shows weak but significant larval–adult shape association (Mantel r 0.16, p 0.003), with family-specific disparity patterns. This supports the authors’ adaptive-decoupling interpretation without demonstrating complete genetic independence or a causal developmental mechanism. Phylogenetic comparisons retain only 74 of 83 sampled species; PC1 ancestral reconstructions and geological dates are model estimates rather than newly observed fossils. Lower adult K still has reported p 0.001, and family sample-size/statistic inconsistencies remain unresolved. Size and convergence effects cannot be assigned as universal diagnostic properties of each linked species.
