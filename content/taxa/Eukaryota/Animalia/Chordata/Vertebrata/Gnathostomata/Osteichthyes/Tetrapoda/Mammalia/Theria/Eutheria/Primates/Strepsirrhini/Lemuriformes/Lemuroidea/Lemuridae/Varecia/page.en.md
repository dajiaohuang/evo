# Varecia

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This genus guide links two COL26.8 species pages through studies that ask different questions: seasonal feeding at Mangevo for V. variegata, and fruit use and seed movement at a Masoala restoration site for V. rubra. The two local samples should be read on their own terms.

## Choose a reading path by research question

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->

Then read Martinez and Razafindratsima for a three-group study of diet and seed dispersal at a regenerating site. Compare the questions and sample designs, not the percentages as if they came from one experiment.
