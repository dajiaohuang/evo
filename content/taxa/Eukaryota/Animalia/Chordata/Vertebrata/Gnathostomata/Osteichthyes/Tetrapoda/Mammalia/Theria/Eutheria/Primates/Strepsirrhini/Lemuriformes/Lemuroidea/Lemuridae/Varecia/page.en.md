## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with Beeby and Baden to see how fruit availability corresponded with feeding, activity and canopy use across two observation periods. Then read Martinez and Razafindratsima for a three-group study of diet and seed dispersal at a regenerating site. Compare the questions and sample designs, not the percentages as if they came from one experiment.
