## Accepted name and placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis colodactylus (Lynch, 1979) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Five bromeliad encounters and retreat behavior
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mjbz
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
Only five specimens were encountered during2016–2020. Four were inside terrestrial or arboreal bromeliads; MUTPL675 was on a bromeliad leaf and retreated backwards into the plant’s water when approached. Short digits are proposed adaptations that may improve adhesion, not experimentally tested performance. No calling males were heard and the advertisement call remains unknown; absence of observed calling is not proof of inability to call.

A hyphen means no accession is supplied in that locus cell; it is not absence of the gene in the animal. The study concatenates mitochondrial12S/16S and nuclearRAG-1 data, but this donor-specific table determines which loci are represented. Repeated accession numbers across locus columns may refer to shared records and are retained rather than counted as independent assays. No GenBank record, raw sequence, alignment or chromatogram was independently authenticated here; inclusion does not measure gene flow, reproductive isolation or divergence time.
