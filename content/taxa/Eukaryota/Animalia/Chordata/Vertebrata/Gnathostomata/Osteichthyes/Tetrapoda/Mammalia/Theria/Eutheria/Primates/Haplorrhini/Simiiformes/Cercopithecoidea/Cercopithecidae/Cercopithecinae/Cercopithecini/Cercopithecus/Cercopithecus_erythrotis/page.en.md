## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
On Bioko Island, Equatorial Guinea, reconnaissance surveys compared primate abundance and hunting intensity across three areas of the Gran Caldera Scientific Reserve. The authors described C. erythrotis and C. nictitans as relatively tolerant of heavy hunting compared with other primates in that assessment. This is a relative result from those sites and methods, not a current population estimate, range-wide response or conservation-status update.
