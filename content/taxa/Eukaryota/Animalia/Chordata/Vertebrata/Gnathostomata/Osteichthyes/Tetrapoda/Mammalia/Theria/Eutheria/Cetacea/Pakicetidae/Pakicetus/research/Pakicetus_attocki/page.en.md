# Pakicetus attocki

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Thewissen et al. report a wolf-sized Pakicetus attocki reconstruction from separately catalogued early-Eocene material at one Pakistani site; cranial and postcranial anatomy supports a terrestrial locomotor interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Diet is unresolved from the described skeleton and teeth; the study does not attribute prey remains to the sample
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Terrestrial use is inferred from the locomotor skeleton; the locality and formation do not prove a shoreline microhabitat
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Running and terrestrial locomotion are inferred from limb proportions and osteology; performance was not observed
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Wolf-sized pakicetid in the authors' comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Terrestrial early-cetacean interpretation with ecological guild bounded to the named sample
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Weight-bearing limbs and running adaptations in separately catalogued postcrania
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

Locality-composite reconstruction combining multiple individuals
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Skull H-GSP 96231 and separately catalogued vertebrae and limb bones from H-GSP Locality 62 support a terrestrial body plan, but the reconstruction is not one associated skeleton and Pakicetus is not presented as a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->
