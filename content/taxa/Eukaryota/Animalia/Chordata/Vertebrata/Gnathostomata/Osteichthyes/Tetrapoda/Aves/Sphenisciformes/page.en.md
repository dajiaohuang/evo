## emperor penguin population connection

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Cristofari and colleagues analyzed RAD-SNP data from 110 emperor penguins sampled at six East Antarctic colonies in 2004–2012. Their coalescent model supported strong demographic coupling among those colonies, interpreted by the authors as one genetic population. The sampling and model do not provide a current census or instantaneous migration rates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## emperor penguin conservation assessment

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The emperor penguin page also summarizes BirdLife International’s assessment accepted for pre-publication in IUCN Red List 2026(2), dated 26 February 2026. It distinguishes satellite-derived population indices from a census and reports a model-based projection. These assessment results belong to Aptenodytes forsteri; they should not be generalized to the whole order.

## selected Pygoscelis studies

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The Pygoscelis route connects three distinct cases: a ten-season nest-survival study at the Windmill Islands, a mixed-method global colony assessment for chinstrap penguins, and one unusually warm winter diet study at Bird Island for gentoo penguins. Their places, periods and methods differ, so these papers do not form a uniform comparison or an order-wide population and ecology synthesis.
<!-- /evo:text -->
