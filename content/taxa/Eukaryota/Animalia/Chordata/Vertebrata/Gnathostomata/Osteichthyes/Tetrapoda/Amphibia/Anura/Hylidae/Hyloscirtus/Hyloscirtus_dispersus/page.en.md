## Calling from streamside vegetation
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
varela-2025-dispersus
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The account described nocturnal frogs in hillside forest, including secondary forest and artificial open areas, associated with running streams and ravines. Males called on riparian vegetation up to 2.5 m above ground. These observations document habitat use; they do not measure the consequences of disturbance or establish habitat preferences experimentally.
