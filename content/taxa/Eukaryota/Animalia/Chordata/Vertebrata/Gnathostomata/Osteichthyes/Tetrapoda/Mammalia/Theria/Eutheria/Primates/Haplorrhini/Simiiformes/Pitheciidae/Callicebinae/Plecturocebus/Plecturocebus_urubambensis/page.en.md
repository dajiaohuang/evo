## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2015 description combined a short survey around Atalaya, museum specimens and historical records. It documented localities in the Río Urubamba basin and proposed a hypothetical range across lowland forest. The authors cautioned that large rivers are not absolute barriers for titis, so the rivers mark a working distribution rather than a complete range survey.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original description distinguished it from a similar brown titi by its overall dark-brown coloration, darker face and black forehead band; specimens also varied in how much black occurred on the forearms, hands and feet. These characters reflect the description material and limited observations, not a diagnostic test of every population.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The species was described as Callicebus urubambensis in 2015; COL26.8 accepts Plecturocebus urubambensis. The accepted name follows the pinned catalogue, while biological statements remain limited to the localities and specimens covered by the description.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The original field survey was brief, and part of the range is a hypothesis based on sparse records; rivers are not treated as proven biogeographic barriers. The Chinese name is editorial; this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
