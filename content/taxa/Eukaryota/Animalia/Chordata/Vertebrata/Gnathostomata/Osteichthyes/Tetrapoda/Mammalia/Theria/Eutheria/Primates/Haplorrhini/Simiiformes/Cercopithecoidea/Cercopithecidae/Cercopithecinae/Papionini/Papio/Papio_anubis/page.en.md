## populationGenetics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Jordan et al. compared Alu insertion polymorphisms across six living Papio species, sampling two individuals per species. Under the authors' criterion for this 12-individual panel, 4,645 insertions occurred in both sampled olive baboons and were absent from the other sampled baboons, so they were classed as indicative of P. anubis. This count depends on a small comparative panel; it does not represent all genetic variation in olive baboons or resolve every reticulate relationship in Papio.
