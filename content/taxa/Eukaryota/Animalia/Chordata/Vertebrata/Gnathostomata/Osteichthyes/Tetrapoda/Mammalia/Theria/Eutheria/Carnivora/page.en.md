## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
“Carnivora” is not a diet list. Feeding habits vary within the order, and the carnassial teeth are modified in bears, raccoons and pinnipeds; mammals outside the order, including bats, marsupials and cetaceans, also prey on animals. Taxonomic membership and actual diet need to be kept separate.
