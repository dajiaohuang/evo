## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In their 2000 revision, Rasoloarison and colleagues restricted the then-known range to the area north of the Tsiribihina River and at least as far as the Soalala Peninsula. They noted that the old St. Augustin type-locality label was far south of this boundary and had an uncertain provenance, so it did not confirm the species south of the river. This is a boundary in the paper's specimen and sampling record, not a complete modern range map.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The revision distinguished western mouse lemurs using external, cranial and dental characters. In its table for adult Kirindy/CFPF specimens, Peters's mouse lemurs averaged 49.0 g, with a recorded range of 36.5–64 g and a sample size of 15. These are measurements from that study sample, not a species-wide body-mass range.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This guide focuses on locality limits and sample measurements in a 2000 taxonomic revision; it is not a current account of range, behaviour or conservation. COL26.8 and the MDD page differ in authorship year, and this page retains the fixed-denominator name. The Chinese common name is editorial; the page is not externally expert-reviewed.
