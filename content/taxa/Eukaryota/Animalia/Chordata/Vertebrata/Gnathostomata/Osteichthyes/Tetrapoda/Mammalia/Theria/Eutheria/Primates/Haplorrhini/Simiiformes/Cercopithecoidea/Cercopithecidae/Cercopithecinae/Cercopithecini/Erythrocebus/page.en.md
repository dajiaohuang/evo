## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This COL26.8 genus guide links three accepted species separated from older, broader uses of E. patas. Their sources differ: local field ecology in Uganda and Kenya, historical distribution reconstruction in Tanzania, and sparse morphology-and-locality evidence around the Blue Nile. The records remain attached to their names, dates and methods rather than being merged into a genus-wide natural-history account.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Read E. patas first for Murchison Falls observations and the separately named Laikipia subspecies study. Then compare E. baumstarki’s dated Tanzania distribution syntheses, keeping the 2009 pre-split name visible. Finish with E. poliophaeus: the 2010 Anbesa Chaka survey was published under broad E. patas, and the 2017 review later used it as locality evidence while stressing how little is known. Compare evidence type, geography and taxonomic certainty, not pooled abundance or shared ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->
