# Asteriornis maastrichtensis

## A specimen from a constrained Belgian horizon

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/0/title/en -->
A specimen from a constrained Belgian horizon
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/0/text/en -->
Holotype NHMM 2013 008 comes from the Maastricht Formation at the CBR-Romontbos quarry in Belgium. Field et al. (2020) constrain its source horizon to 66.8–66.7 Ma. This is a locality/horizon constraint, not the species’ complete duration or global first and last appearances. The collection number connects anatomy and phylogenetic interpretations to the same material; the locality does not identify the geographic origin of crown birds.

## What the fossil preserves

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/1/title/en -->
What the fossil preserves
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/1/text/en -->
The specimen preserves a nearly complete three-dimensional skull with associated postcranial elements, studied through CT imaging and digital segmentation. In the authors’ comparison, its cranium combines galliform-like and anseriform-like features. These observations belong to the preserved material and comparison; the combination does not make it a modern chicken or duck or reveal unpreserved soft tissues and behaviour.

## Two analyses, two nearby placements

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/2/title/en -->
Two analyses, two nearby placements
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/2/text/en -->
The study’s parsimony analysis recovered it as sister to crown Galloanserae. Its tip-dated Bayesian analysis instead placed it as the stemward-most member of Pangalliformes. Both concern its relationships near galloanserans, but they do not agree on the exact branch. This page reports the method-dependent results together; recorded database placement and atlas navigation do not independently resolve these competing results.

## A model estimate and a tentative habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/3/title/en -->
A model estimate and a tentative habitat
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSections/3/text/en -->
The reported mean body-mass estimate of 394 g is a skeletal-scaling model result, not a measured living animal. Small size and marine depositional context led the authors to suggest possible littoral ecology; burial setting does not directly establish habitat preference. The cited description does not resolve species-specific diet, locomotor performance or a feeding guild.

## Limits and open questions

<!-- evo:text /records/readerLimitations/en -->
These sections are limited to NHMM 2013 008 and the description, comparisons and models in Field et al. (2020). A global range and first and last appearances remain unresolved for lack of sufficient provenance. Diet, flight or other locomotor performance, soft tissues and population variation remain unsupported in this batch. The horizon age does not close the range gap, and competing placements do not establish direct ancestry. This bilingual guide has no external expert review.
