## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Hending et al. (2020) surveyed calls at 66 northern Madagascan forest sites and detected Montagne d’Ambre fork-marked lemurs at 22. Records were more frequent in dry-to-transitional forest, but the model found that spatial autocorrelation explained the pattern better than the measured environmental variables.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Call detections indicate occurrence at surveyed sites; they are not an individual census or an island-wide population estimate. The habitat associations apply to the northern sites and variables used in that study.
