## Rainy-night stream observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12246799-v44rt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
During an overcast rainy-night survey from 20:00 to 22:00, frogs were frequently found on stream stones surrounded by evergreen broadleaf forest. Survey air temperature was about 16 °C and relative humidity 85–95%; these are observation conditions, not physiological limits. No usable call recordings or tadpoles were obtained, so those facets remain unresolved.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v44rt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys daxuemontis as with heels overlapping when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
