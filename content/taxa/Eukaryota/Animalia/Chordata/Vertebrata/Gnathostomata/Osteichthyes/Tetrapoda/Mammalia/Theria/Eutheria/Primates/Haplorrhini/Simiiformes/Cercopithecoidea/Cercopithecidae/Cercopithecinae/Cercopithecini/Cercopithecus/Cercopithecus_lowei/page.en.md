## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At the Duasidan Monkey Sanctuary in Ghana, a 2017 study rotated observations among three groups of 8–15 monkeys and recorded 312 hours. The site is a roughly 1.8-ha riverine-forest patch beside a settlement. Feeding records included 13 plant species, invertebrates and discarded human food. Fruit was recorded more often in the wet season and buds in the dry season; the authors reported anthropogenic food contributions of 34.8% and 41.3%, respectively. These results describe three groups at one small site, not species-wide diet, population trend or health effects.
