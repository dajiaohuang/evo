# Plesiosauria

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A marine sauropterygian radiation with a diagnostic Rhaetian record; older Bobosaurus is displayed as an uncertain candidate and does not set the Plesiosauria range.
<!-- /evo:text -->
