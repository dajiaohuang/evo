# Testudines

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The turtle-line route is anchored by a shell mosaic: Odontochelys has a developed plastron and incomplete dorsal carapace, while Pappochelys retains broadened ribs and paired gastralia. These are sampled stem-turtle observations and comparative hypotheses, not a direct ancestor sequence or a crown-origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
No taxon-wide diet is established by the cited shell studies; this field remains unresolved beyond the named specimens.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Sample-specific only: the marine deposits yielding Odontochelys were interpreted as marginal sea or river-delta settings; this is not a global turtle habitat claim.
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Li et al. document three named Odontochelys specimens from southwestern China, while Schoch and Sues document complementary Vellberg Pappochelys anatomy. Their shell sequence and topology are retained as bounded interpretations with specimen, formation and analysis limits.
