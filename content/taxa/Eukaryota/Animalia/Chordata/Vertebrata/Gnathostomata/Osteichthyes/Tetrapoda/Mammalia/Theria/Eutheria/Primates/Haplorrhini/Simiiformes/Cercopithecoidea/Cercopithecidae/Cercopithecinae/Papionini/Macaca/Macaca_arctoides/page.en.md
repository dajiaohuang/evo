## foraging and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Stotrabhashyam et al. (2023) studied one troop, which spent about 73.2% of observed time foraging and feeding. In December–February it mainly used open degraded forest and ate Schizostachyum polymorphum shoots and Forrestia (also called Amischotolype) mollissima roots; in March–April it used more canopy-covered degraded forest and fed on the latter plant.
