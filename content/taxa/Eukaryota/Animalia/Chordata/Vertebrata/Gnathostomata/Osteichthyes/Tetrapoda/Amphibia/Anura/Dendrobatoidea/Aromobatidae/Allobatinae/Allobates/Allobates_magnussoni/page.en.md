## A reported distance to velocicantus

These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. ## A single-note arrangement in the reviewed comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7648453-5twz4
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The2020 description summarizes magnussoni calls as a single note emitted continuously or irregularly, contrasting them with velocicantus trills of66–138 pulsed notes. This information is attributed to prior acoustic studies cited there; their original recordings, individual counts and temperature conditions have not been reread here. “Single note” describes the reported call unit, not inability to repeat notes or produce other vocalizations.
