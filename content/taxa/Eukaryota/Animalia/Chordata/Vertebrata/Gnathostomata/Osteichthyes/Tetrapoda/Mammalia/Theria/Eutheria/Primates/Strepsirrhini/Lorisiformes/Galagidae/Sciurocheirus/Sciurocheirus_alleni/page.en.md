## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows COL26.8 species ID 4VNCG. MDD v2.5 uses the display name Bioko Squirrel Galago and notes that the name has included cameronensis, which some sources treat separately. Ambrose's 2003 study examined galagos then classified broadly as Galago alleni; its Bioko and southwestern Cameroon acoustic form is the evidence summarized here, not a claim that every later checklist uses the same boundary.

## bioacoustics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At ten locations in Gabon, Cameroon and Bioko, Ambrose compared morphology and loud calls among populations classified at the time as Galago alleni. The calls formed three regionally consistent profiles; the Bioko and southwestern Cameroon profile was identified as the Allen's form. The paper predicted distinct species from these profiles under its acoustic recognition framework.

## interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The study is a species-boundary and vocal-profile comparison, not a population census or a full account of diet, ranging or social life. Its broad starting name and the later checklist treatment of cameronensis should remain visible when connecting the paper to this COL ID.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The main evidence is one regional acoustic study using the historical broad name Galago alleni. It does not establish current abundance, population trend, range-wide ecology or a final taxonomic boundary for cameronensis.
