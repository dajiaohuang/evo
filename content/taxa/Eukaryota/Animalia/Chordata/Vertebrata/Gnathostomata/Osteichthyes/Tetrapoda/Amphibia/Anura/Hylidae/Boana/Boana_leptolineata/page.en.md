## Organization of the recorded vocalization
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-m7z6
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Boana leptolineata as follows: Two harmonic units: an A trill with 2–14 fused pulses and a B sequence with 4–50 separated click-like pulses, often combined. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.

## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies

The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is No filter. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness.
