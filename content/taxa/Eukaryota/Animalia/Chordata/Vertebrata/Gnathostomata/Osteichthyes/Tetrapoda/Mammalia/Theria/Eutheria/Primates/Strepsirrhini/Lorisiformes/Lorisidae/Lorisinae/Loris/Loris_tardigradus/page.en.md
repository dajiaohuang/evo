## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2001–2002 survey in Sri Lanka covered 31 sites across five ecological zones. About 766 km of survey effort crossed 17 areas with no loris sightings; another 192 km was walked or motored at 14 sites where 185 sightings were recorded. These route observations describe the survey record, not the number of lorises across the island.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Under the paper’s taxonomic usage, 69 sightings were assigned to the nominate form L. t. tardigradus and one to L. t. nycticeboides. The authors reported a sightings-based density range of 0.86–13 animals per kilometre for L. t. tardigradus. That range is limited to the surveyed routes and period, not a current species-wide density; one sighting cannot estimate density or present status for nycticeboides.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a local survey guide, not a comprehensive red slender loris account; it is not externally expert-reviewed. The survey dates to 2001–2002; sightings and density ranges do not describe current populations. The paper’s subspecific labels reflect its study-era taxonomy and are not projected onto present-day boundaries across the range. The publisher page gives different issue and first-online years, both of which are retained.
