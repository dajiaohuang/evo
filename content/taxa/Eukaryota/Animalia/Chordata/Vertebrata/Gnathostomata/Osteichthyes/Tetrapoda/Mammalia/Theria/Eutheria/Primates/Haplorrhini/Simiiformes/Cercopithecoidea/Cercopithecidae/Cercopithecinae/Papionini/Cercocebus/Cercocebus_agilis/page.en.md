## field_study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
René Quris’s 1975 study documented ecology and social organization of mangabeys in northeastern Gabon using the name Cercocebus galeritus agilis. The paper itself described field knowledge of the forest-dwelling group as sparse at the time. It is useful as a dated, place-specific account of the lineage now listed separately as C. agilis in COL26.8, rather than as a modern range or population assessment.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The source predates the current species treatment and uses a subspecies name. The profile preserves that historical framing; it does not infer that the Gabon study represents every population now assigned to C. agilis. No external expert review; Chinese common name is editorial. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
