## phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the study's mitochondrial tree, Milne-Edwards's sportive lemur grouped in one major lineage with L. microdon. The authors included L. edwardsi among the eight species-level taxa supported jointly by mitochondrial sequences and chromosome data. This reports the study's phylogenetic placement, not a full distribution or behavior account.
