## social_ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Sentier Nature forest in Gabon’s South Loango National Park, Dolado, Cooke and Beltran followed a population during the peak-fruiting season of 2014. They compared party composition and habitat use with fruit availability from four tree species and reported seasonal fission–fusion patterns associated with fruit availability. The result describes that population and study season; it does not establish a year-round pattern across the species.
