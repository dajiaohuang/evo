## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The description emphasized brown pelage, a black midline extending along most of the back, and whitish-gray markings from the jaw toward the ears. Compared with L. fleuretae and L. betsileo, the coat was shorter and appeared smoother. The type locality was Manombo Special Reserve.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors associated L. jamesi with one of the few remaining low-elevation coastal rainforests and said its northern and southern limits needed more survey. This is the habitat context in the 2006 description, not evidence of today's forest extent, threat level, or full-species ecology.
