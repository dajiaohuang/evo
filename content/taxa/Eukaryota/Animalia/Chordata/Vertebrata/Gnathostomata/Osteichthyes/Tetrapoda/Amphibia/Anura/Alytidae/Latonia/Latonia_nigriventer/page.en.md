## Ecology in the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The surveys describe L. nigriventer as localized and elusive; they detected Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis in the region and on sampled frogs but observed no signs of chytridiomycosis in this species.

Study scope: Hula Valley surveys summarized in the 2017 publication; the pathogen observation is restricted to that study's sampled place, time and frogs.

Study material: Wild survey observations; pathogen detection is not interpreted as clinical disease or a current population-wide condition.
