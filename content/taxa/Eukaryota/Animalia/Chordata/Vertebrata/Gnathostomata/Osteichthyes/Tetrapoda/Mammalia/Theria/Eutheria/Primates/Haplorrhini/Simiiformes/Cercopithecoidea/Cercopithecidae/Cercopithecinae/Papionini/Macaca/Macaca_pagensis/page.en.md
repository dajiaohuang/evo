## transect survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Yanuar and Supriatna (2018) surveyed 157.5 km on Sipora with 93 sightings and 185.6 km on the Pagai Islands with 109. The estimated area for M. pagensis on Sipora was 12.6 km². Among four primates surveyed on Pagai, this species had the highest group density.
