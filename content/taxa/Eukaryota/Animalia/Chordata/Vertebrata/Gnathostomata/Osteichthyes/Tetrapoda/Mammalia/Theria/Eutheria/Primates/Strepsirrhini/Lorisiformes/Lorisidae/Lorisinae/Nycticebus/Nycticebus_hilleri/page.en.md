# Nycticebus hilleri

## Distribution and habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD currently displays Hiller's Slow Loris, records the type locality as Batu Sankar in the Padang Highlands of western Sumatra, and lists holotype ANSP 6590; its distribution summary is northern Sumatra. MDD also flags the species identity of a pictured animal from southern Way Kambas as uncertain, so this page does not count it as a confirmed range record.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Stone and Rehn's 1902 paper placed hilleri under N. coucang; current MDD lists Nycticebus hilleri as an accepted species and retains Sumatran Slow Loris as another English name. The older name combination records taxonomic history, not field morphology or ecology. The evidence set used here contains no species-specific field-ecology study for this profile.
<!-- /evo:text -->
