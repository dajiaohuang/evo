## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At the Kasintuwu site in 2006, Riley et al. recorded groups of Tonkean macaques and mixed groups with M. ochreata. The article abstract gives no M. tonkeana-only group-density estimate; the observation is specific to that site and survey period and does not establish a range-wide community pattern or stable rate of mixed grouping.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The source reports observations at one survey site and period around Faruhumpenai and gives no M. tonkeana density. The English common-name label follows MDD; the Chinese label is an editorial translation. This guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
