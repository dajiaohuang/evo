## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The description gave Daraina's sportive lemur a mass of about 0.72 kg, with a reddish-brown back, grayish-white underside, gray-brown facial mask, and long, thick fur. The authors molecularly identified it in Andrafiamena Classified Forest, where L. ankaranensis was also recorded.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Andranotsimaty near Daraina. The paper then knew the species from the Daraina region south of the Loky River and reported only this species in its Daraina records. The authors called for surveys south of Andrafiamena and southwest of the Loky to clarify distribution, as well as population studies where it co-occurs with another species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
These findings reflect the 2006 sampling and mitochondrial analyses; the authors explicitly called for further range and population work. They do not establish present-day dynamics where the species co-occurs. The COL26.8 accepted name is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial, and the page is not externally expert-reviewed.
