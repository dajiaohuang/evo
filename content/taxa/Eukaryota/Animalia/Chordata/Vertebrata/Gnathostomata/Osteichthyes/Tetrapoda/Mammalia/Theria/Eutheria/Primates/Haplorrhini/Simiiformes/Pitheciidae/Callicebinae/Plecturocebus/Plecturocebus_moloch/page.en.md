## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
An occurrence-record study across interfluves in southern Amazonia proposed a revised regional range for the red-bellied titi and treated the recently described Plecturocebus grovesi and P. vieirai separately from areas previously assigned to P. moloch. The authors also note unresolved issues involving P. moloch's type locality, two historical synonyms, and titi taxonomy in the Jamanxim–Teles Pires interfluve.
