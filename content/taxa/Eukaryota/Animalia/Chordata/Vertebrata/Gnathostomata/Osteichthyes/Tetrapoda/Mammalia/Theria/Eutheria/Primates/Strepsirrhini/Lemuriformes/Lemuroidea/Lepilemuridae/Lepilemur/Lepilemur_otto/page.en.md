## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Ambodimahabibo description characterized the back, shoulders, and limbs as mostly gray-brown. A diffuse dark line ran from the crown down the back but did not continue onto the tail. The underside ranged from gray to cream; the face and forehead were gray, and the tail varied from gray-brown to dark brown, sometimes with a white tip. These traits come from a limited sample.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality and records known to the paper were limited to Ambodimahabibo in northwestern Madagascar, west of the Mahajamba River and south of the Sofia River. The original study called for surveys; local sampling does not establish full range or current occupancy. The guide separates the 2007 biological description from the 2017 correction.
