## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This catalogue includes the greater one-horned and Javan rhinoceroses. “One-horned” does not mean every individual bears a prominent horn: female Javan rhinos typically lack one. Skin folds also need species-level comparison.
