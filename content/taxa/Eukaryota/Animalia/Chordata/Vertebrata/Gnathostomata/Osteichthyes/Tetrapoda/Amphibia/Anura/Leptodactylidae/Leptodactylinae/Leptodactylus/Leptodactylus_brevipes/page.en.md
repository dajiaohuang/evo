## Published reproductive observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
carrillo-2022
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2022 report from the Brazilian Cerrado described a female guarding a tadpole school and performing pumping movements. Larvae repeatedly contacted her legs and back; no aggressive female behaviour was recorded. This documents maternal attendance in the reported observation, without establishing its frequency, a survival benefit or universal behaviour across populations.
