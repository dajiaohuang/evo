## communication

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A predator-model experiment tested 19 wild groups in Nouabalé-Ndoki National Park, Republic of the Congo, in 2019 and 2020. When a leopard model was shown to females, males approached the group in 13 of 17 analysed trials; when the male saw the model, females did not approach in nine trials. Once males saw the model, they switched to a call associated with leopard defence and mobbing. The experiment isolates responses to a simulated threat; it does not measure how often natural attacks occur or how groups fare in the wild.
