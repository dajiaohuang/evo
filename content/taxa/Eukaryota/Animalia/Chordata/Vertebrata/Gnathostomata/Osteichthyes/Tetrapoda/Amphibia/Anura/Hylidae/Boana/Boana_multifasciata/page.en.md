COL26.8 accepts Boana multifasciata (Günther, 1859) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Boana (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-m7zl
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Boana multifasciata as having larval development and its oviposition as aquatic. A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.

## Juvenile habitat and egg-site categories have defined scope
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-m7zl
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The same row codes juvenile habitat as aquatic and marks eggs directly in water or on submerged substrate among the seven oviposition-site categories. The positive category summarizes a broad trait; it does not establish a specific nest architecture, substrate species or oviposition height. A juvenile-habitat code is not automatically an adult habitat preference. The compiled record does not newly establish clutch size, egg dimensions, parental attendance, spawning season or offspring survival.

## Compiled lengths retain inference and measurement limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-m7zl

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The row does not carry a head-width or tibia-length inference flag. That absence is not an independent audit establishing how each original value was measured. Body length, head width and tibia length are retained numerically in the row-level evidence snapshot. ## Compiled habitat and threat labels are bounded descriptions
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12654886-m7zl
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
The Boana multifasciata row prints habitat F,O,Pa and IUCN LC. The source defines F as forest, O as open vegetation and Pa as perianthropic; these are compiled habitat categories, not measured habitat preference, breeding-site selection or demonstrated tolerance of pollution/mining. The printed F Pa spacing in some rows is retained without silently substituting a different classification. LC means the source’s Least Concern label, DD Data Deficient, and a dash remains an unassigned table status rather than a certified absence of threats. The table does not provide an assessment date for each species, and these labels are not a current IUCN refresh. They do not measure local population change or the effectiveness of protected areas.

## Unequal field, museum and genetic effort limits interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->

The other areas each have two expeditions, with19 days for Conceição,19 for Ourilândia/São Félix and23 for São Geraldo. These are aggregate effort summaries, not this species’ independent encounter counts. Museum, literature and genetic species totals overlap and must not be summed as unique richness. The regional barcode library covers only part of the amphibian inventory; inclusion in S3 Table does not by itself establish a barcode, recorded call or successful phylogenetic diagnosis for this row. Qualifiers cf., aff., gr. and sp. on other rows remain unresolved rather than being stripped to accepted species.
