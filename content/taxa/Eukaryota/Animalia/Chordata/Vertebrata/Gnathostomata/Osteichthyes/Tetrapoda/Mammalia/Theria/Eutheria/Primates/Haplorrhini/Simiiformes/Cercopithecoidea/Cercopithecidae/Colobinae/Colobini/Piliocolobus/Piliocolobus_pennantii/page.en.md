## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A reconnaissance study compared monkey abundance and gun-hunting evidence across three areas of Bioko’s Gran Caldera Scientific Reserve. In that study’s historical usage, Procolobus pennantii referred to the endemic Bioko population, which was reported as especially vulnerable and restricted to a small southwest area of the reserve. The finding is local to the survey and its taxonomic usage.
