# Mosasaurus hoffmannii


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Mosasaurus hoffmannii is accepted in the fixed PBDB squamata working roster, with database parent Mosasaurus. This guide uses 26 eligible species-level occurrence records from 25 collections and 21 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts. In 11 records, the original identification or combination is connected to the database accepted name. ## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 83.6–61.66 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is Big Brook (Wenonah/Mt. Laurel), occurrence occ:558113 (83.6–66 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Inversand Company pit / Edelman Fossil Park, Sewell (MFL), occurrence occ:1261001 (72.2–61.66 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival. For occ:1261001, the original stratigraphic comment describes a mixed Maastrichtian–Danian assemblage and proposes a Maastrichtian lag-deposit interpretation. This is a collection-level comment, not a specimen age reviewed here. Its broad bracket extends into the Early Paleocene and does not establish survival of this species into that interval. The original comment and identification Mosasaurus maximus remain in the source archive.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 1010-item squamata working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Squamata storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
