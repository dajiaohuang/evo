## Calling without observed eggs or tadpoles
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9836736-qfbmm
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Males called at night from the ground or vegetation up to 60 cm high, beside slow streams or in flooded forest clearings. The authors explicitly lacked reproductive information beyond calling and had encountered neither eggs nor tadpoles. These calling sites are retained without assigning an egg site or larval habitat.
