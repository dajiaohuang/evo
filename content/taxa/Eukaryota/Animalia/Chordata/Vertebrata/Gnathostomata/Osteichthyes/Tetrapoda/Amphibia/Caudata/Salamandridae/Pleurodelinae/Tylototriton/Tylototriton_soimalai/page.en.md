## Doi Soi Malai record and adjacent habitat prediction
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11496826-tz6yt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description reported Doi Soi Malai in Mae Tuen Wildlife Sanctuary, Tak, northwestern Thailand. The contiguous Doi Soi Malai-Mai Klay Pen Hin National Park was suggested as possible habitat. That expectation remains unconfirmed, without establishing an additional occupied locality or a complete contemporary range.

## Adults and two larvae in one muddy puddle
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc11496826-tz6yt
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
During rainy-season observations around noon, adult males surfaced and two larvae occupied a single isolated roadside mud puddle below Doi Soi Malai. It was about 10 m long, 5 m wide and at most 35 cm deep, with turbid water and muddy sediment; no fish were observed. These local observations do not establish exclusive daytime activity, obligatory roadside breeding, fish absence throughout the season or a complete reproductive calendar.
