## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A roadside survey along routes connecting Mysore, India, reported 889 bonnet macaques in 2003 and 407 in 2015, about 46% of the earlier count. The January–February 2015 resurvey covered 464 km, counting strips within 15 m of each side of the road centre. This describes change along specific roadside strips, not total species abundance, and does not by itself establish the cause of decline.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the Parambikulam landscape of Kerala, researchers detected 54 bonnet macaque groups in 33 of 64 grid cells. A single-season occupancy model estimated mean cell occupancy at 0.51±0.08 and detection probability at 0.25±0.05. These modelled values belong to one forest survey and its sampling design; they are not a range-wide distribution or abundance estimate.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A craniodental comparison of twelve macaque species and 711 museum specimens placed the bonnet macaque among near-equatorial species tending toward smaller overall craniodental size. The table lists 79 M. radiata specimens from South and West India; the sample includes subadults and a few captive or unprovenanced specimens. This interspecific comparison does not establish variation within the species or diagnostic traits.

## conservation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Using local records from roads, temples and tourist sites in southern India, a 2017 study proposed considering some vegetated hillocks and human-use locations as conservation reserves. This was a research recommendation, not evidence that statutory reserves were established, a current conservation category or a range-wide recovery plan.
