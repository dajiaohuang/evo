## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows COL26.8 in listing Sapajus cucullatus as an accepted species; the fixed MDD crosswalk has no corresponding entry, so both display names are editorial translations. Taxonomic treatments differ: the 2020 IUCN assessment placed it as the subspecies Sapajus nigritus cucullatus, whereas Rylands and Mittermeier’s 2024 review of Neotropical primates lists S. cucullatus as a monotypic species. This page records that history only; the available sources do not support an ecological account of a distinct population under this name.
