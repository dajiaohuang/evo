# Presbytis robinsoni

## Classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Ang et al.'s 2020 study used the older combination P. femoralis robinsoni. The authors noted that the only publicly available molecular record then was one mitochondrial sequence from Redang Panjang, Malaysia; all three delimitation methods in their comparison separated this lineage from P. f. femoralis and P. f. percura.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Scope of the evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
This is a taxonomic comparison using one existing sequence, not new sampling across multiple Robinson's Banded Langur populations. Mitochondrial results can indicate lineage differences but do not alone describe species-wide genetic variation, admixture or boundaries.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The taxonomic account rests on one public mitochondrial sequence and comparative analysis; new multi-population sampling is lacking. This page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
