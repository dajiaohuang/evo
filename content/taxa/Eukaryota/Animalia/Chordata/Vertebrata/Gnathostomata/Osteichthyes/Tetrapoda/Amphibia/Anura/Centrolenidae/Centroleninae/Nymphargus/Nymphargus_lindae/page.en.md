## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13061190-qqpg9
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The comparative diagnosis describes lindae with translucent esophageal/stomach peritonea and a green dorsum bearing black ocelli with yellow-to-orange centers. These differ from the white peritonea and unmarked green dorsum of dajomesae. The comparison is not a new size, call or larval dataset for lindae.
