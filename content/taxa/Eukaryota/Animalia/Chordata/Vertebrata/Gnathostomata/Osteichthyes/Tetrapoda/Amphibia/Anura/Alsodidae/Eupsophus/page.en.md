## Shape decoupling is a bounded comparative hypothesis

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->

This supports the authors’ adaptive-decoupling interpretation without demonstrating complete genetic independence or a causal developmental mechanism. Phylogenetic comparisons retain only 74 of 83 sampled species; PC1 ancestral reconstructions and geological dates are model estimates rather than newly observed fossils. Lower adult K still has reported p 0.001, and family sample-size/statistic inconsistencies remain unresolved. Size and convergence effects cannot be assigned as universal diagnostic properties of each linked species.

## Compilation scope and uneven evidence
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/14/text/en -->
The authors surveyed published regional literature from 1819–2025 across seven databases and monographs, excluding incomplete records, unpublished theses and meeting material, and parasites not identified to species. Database-specific query strings and search dates are not given in the inspected methods. Overall host totals and some account summaries disagree internally; no corrected host census is inferred here.
