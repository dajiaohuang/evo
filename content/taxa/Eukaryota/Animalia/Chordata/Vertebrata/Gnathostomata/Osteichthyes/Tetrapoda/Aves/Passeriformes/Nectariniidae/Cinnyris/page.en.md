# Cinnyris


## Older Nectarinia names and spelling differences

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Twenty-eight historical guides have same-name eBird links and one-to-one BirdTree crosswalks. Read COL ci/ne shards confirm twenty-two exact older combinations. Crosswalk Nectarinia neergardi differs from catalogue Nectarinia neergaardi (462P2) at [Cinnyris neergaardi](Cinnyris_neergaardi/page.en.md); crosswalk coccinigaster differs from catalogue coccinigastra (462N2) at [Cinnyris coccinigastrus](Cinnyris_coccinigastrus/page.en.md). [Cinnyris reichenowi](Cinnyris_reichenowi/page.en.md) links to crosswalk name Nectarinia preussi, which was not confirmed as an exact synonym in the read shards; older combinations for chalcomelas, fuelleborni and tsavoensis also remain unconfirmed there. Name links do not prove identical concepts across dates.

## Complete-set counts differ

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
[Cinnyris rufipennis](Cinnyris_rufipennis/page.en.md) has four birds but only one complete set; [Cinnyris reichenowi](Cinnyris_reichenowi/page.en.md) has fourteen birds and ten complete sets; [Cinnyris chloropygius](Cinnyris_chloropygius/page.en.md) has twelve birds and eleven complete sets, with one unsexed bird; [Cinnyris neergaardi](Cinnyris_neergaardi/page.en.md) has six birds and five complete sets, with two unsexed birds. Totals do not replace field denominators. Compiled mass has a separate source: bifasciatus/osea share 7.18 g, erythrocercus/rufipennis share 8.79 g and tsavoensis/reichenowi share 6.8 g. The original book has not been examined to explain the equalities; identical individual distributions are not established.
