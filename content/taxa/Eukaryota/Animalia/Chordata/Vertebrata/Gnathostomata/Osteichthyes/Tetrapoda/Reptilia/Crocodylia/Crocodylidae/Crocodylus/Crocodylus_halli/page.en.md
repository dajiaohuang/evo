## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Geometric morphometric comparisons of New Guinea crocodile skulls identified a morphologically distinct group south of the Central Highlands, which the authors described as Hall's New Guinea crocodile, Crocodylus halli. The paper discusses cranial and scale characters for comparison with the northern New Guinea crocodile. This is not a field key covering every population on the island or a complete ecological survey.
