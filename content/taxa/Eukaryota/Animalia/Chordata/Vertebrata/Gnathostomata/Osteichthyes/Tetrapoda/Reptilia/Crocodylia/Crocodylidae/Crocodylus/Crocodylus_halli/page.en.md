## Read the study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
What specimen comparisons underlie the description of Hall's New Guinea crocodile? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record 8GRXJ and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. Geometric morphometric comparisons of New Guinea crocodile skulls identified a morphologically distinct group south of the Central Highlands, which the authors described as Hall's New Guinea crocodile, Crocodylus halli. The paper discusses cranial and scale characters for comparison with the northern New Guinea crocodile. This is not a field key covering every population on the island or a complete ecological survey.

Continue with Crocodylus novaeguineae (COL ZKNM) to read the northern comparison material in the same morphological study. The Crocodylia group guide provides the wider reading route; the neighboring page retains its own sources and limits.
