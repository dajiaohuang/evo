## Read the study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
How did crocodile observations vary through one year at a Ugandan river delta? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record ZKNK and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. In the Victoria Nile delta of Lake Albert, western Uganda, researchers made monthly surveys along five transects from October 2017 to September 2018, recording 186 crocodile observations. Records were most frequent in January–February and April–May and least frequent in July–August and October–November. Crocodiles were encountered most often along woody north-shore riverbanks, followed by papyrus–Vossia habitat. Hatchlings had the narrowest habitat niche breadth and subadults the widest.
