# Aptenodytes


## Read coordination in a named colony

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Aptenodytes route can begin with a time-lapse study of one winter egg-carrying emperor-penguin colony: recurring small steps propagate through dense huddles, alongside group movement and slow edge turnover. Place, weather and breeding stage are explicit. This case is not transferred to king penguins or used to assign identical huddle densities, incubation seasons or coordination mechanisms to both members of the genus.

## Candidate genes and historical responses in large diving penguins

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The 2022 study discusses coagulation-related FIBB and ANO6 and size-related CREB3L1 and SMARCAD1 on the ancestral Aptenodytes branch. These are candidates based on sequence screening and known functions, without corresponding transcriptomic validation or independent experimental confirmation of each role. Emperor and king penguins differ in glacial effective-size patterns; candidate shared adaptations do not combine their ecology.
