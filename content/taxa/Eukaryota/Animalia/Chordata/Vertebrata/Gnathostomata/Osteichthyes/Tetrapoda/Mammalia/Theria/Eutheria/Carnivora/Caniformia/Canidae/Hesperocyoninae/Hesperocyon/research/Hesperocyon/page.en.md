# Hesperocyon

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Wang's monograph revises Hesperocyon within the extinct hesperocyonine canid radiation using named fossil material and a morphology matrix; its sampled placement is not presented as direct ancestry to later canids.
