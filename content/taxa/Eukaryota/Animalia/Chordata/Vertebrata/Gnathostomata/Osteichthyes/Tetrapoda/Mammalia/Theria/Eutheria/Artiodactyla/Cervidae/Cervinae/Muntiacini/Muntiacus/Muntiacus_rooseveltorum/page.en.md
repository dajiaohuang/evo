## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch uses historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are reported compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found. They are not direct measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the presence of same-name sparse rows support the association here; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components from the reported summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed here, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources; no complete monograph or external expert review is claimed.
This page interprets one supported ecological field group; it does not fill the remaining diet, activity or stratum fields. This is a reading guide to historical same-name, same-family foraging records in EltonTraits 1.0, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical species concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized by the authors from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes can also refer to a congener judged ecologically similar by the compilers; this page does not claim direct observations of this species for every field. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing individual review do not fill definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread, and historical citations do not constitute external expert review. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation still require other sources.
