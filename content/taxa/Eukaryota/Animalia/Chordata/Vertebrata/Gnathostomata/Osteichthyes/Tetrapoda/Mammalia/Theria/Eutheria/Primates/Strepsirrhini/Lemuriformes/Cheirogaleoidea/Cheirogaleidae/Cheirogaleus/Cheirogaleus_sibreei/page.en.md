## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2026 study tracked 11 Sibree's dwarf lemurs in the Andasivodihazo fragment at Tsinjoarivo and compared them with 13 Crossley's dwarf lemurs at the same site. During pre- and post-hibernation observations, Sibree's dwarf lemurs travelled farther at night and used the fragment's western area more often. Their mean home range was larger, but the full-sample difference was not statistically significant; the authors caution that the sample was small.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The taxonomic history and genetic evidence for Sibree's dwarf lemur are not fully congruent. A 2009 multilocus study placed its mitochondrial data within the C. medius lineage but could not clearly resolve its position morphologically; a 2010 Tsinjoarivo study used mitochondrial and nuclear markers to support a distinct genetic clade for local C. sibreei-like material.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The ecological comparison covers 24 individuals at one high-altitude Tsinjoarivo fragment, and earlier taxonomy work reported unresolved morphological placement. Local movement and home-range differences should not be generalized to the species. This page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
