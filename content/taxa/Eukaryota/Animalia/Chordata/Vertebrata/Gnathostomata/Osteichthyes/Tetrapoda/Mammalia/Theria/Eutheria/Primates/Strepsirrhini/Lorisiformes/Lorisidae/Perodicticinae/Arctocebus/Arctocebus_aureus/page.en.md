COL26.8 accepts Arctocebus aureus de Winton, 1902 under fixed species ID 5W3R4 and genus parent 32BW. The Mammal Diversity Database uses Golden Angwantibo and lists Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, and the Republic of the Congo; its Democratic Republic of the Congo record is marked uncertain. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## fieldEcology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Historical field work around Makokou in Gabon treated the taxon then called Arctocebus calabarensis aureus as a small nocturnal primate of the forest understory. Hladik’s 1979 synthesis reports a local diet estimate of about 85% animal prey and 14% fruit by fresh weight, drawing on stomach-content analysis and field observations attributed to Charles-Dominique’s work. These records describe an older, single-region evidence base, not a current range-wide diet estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The ecology material is historical and local to Gabon; later syntheses report diet proportions from stomach contents and field observations but do not provide a contemporary, range-wide sample. MDD gives a coarse country list and marks the Democratic Republic of the Congo record uncertain. COL26.8 ID 5W3R4 fixes the page identity; the Chinese display name is editorial.
