## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Reconnaissance teams covered 571.51 km and passive acoustic sensors recorded 12,347 hours in Makombe and Ndokbou forests during 2018–2019. The study found no verified visual encounter with Preuss's red colobus; one putative call was detected near Mt. Sinai in Ndokbou. The authors recommend another targeted search there. This is a detection record for two surveyed forests and a defined period, not proof of absence across the species' range.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The study did not visually verify the species in its two survey forests; its possible call is uncertain, and neither result establishes range-wide presence or absence. This source-linked draft is not externally expert-reviewed.
