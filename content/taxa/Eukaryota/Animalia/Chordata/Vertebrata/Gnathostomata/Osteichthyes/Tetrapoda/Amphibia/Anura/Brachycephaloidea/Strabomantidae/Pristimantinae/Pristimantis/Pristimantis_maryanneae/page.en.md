## Five individuals in the lower forest layer
<!-- /evo:text -->

The five known specimens were reported from low forest vegetation, with leaf perches 60–160 cm above ground. One was found in leaf litter by day; the others were found on fern leaves at night. ## Type-series labels and size endpoints with unresolved conflicts
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-scfpg
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2022 description identifies adult male holotype DHMECN 14454, collected at Naturetrek Vizcaya on 26 February 2018 at 2404 m, and four paratypes. The paratype heading says one female and three males, but its individual entries include two adult males and a juvenile. The diagnosis gives male snout–vent length 17.61–17.8 mm and one female at 21.06 mm, whereas the holotype paragraph gives 17.86 mm. These conflicts are retained; the juvenile is not silently promoted to an adult male, nor is the holotype excluded or rounded to fit the diagnostic interval. The natural-history account’s 2400-m locality is retained separately from the 2404-m collection entry.

## A skin-covered ear region is not a test of hearing
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-scfpg
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The diagnosis describes a hidden tympanic region with the annulus visible beneath skin and no male vocal slits or nuptial pads. The holotype account similarly describes an undifferentiated membrane but gives annulus diameter as 33.61% of eye diameter, versus 25% in the diagnosis. It also describes toe fringes and an inner tarsal fold, whereas the diagnosis lists both as absent. These disagreements remain source-level uncertainties. A hidden external tympanum or absent vocal slit does not establish deafness, silence or a measured reproductive behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Live browns and coppery eyes become a grey preserved pattern
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-scfpg
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The live male holotype was described as light to dark brown with dark markings outlined by golden reticulations, banded limbs and a coppery-yellow iris with black reticulation. In 70% ethanol the account described grey tones, dark bands and small black ventral points. Reported individuals also varied in dorsal browns and head-mask colour. These live and preserved states are kept separate; a single colour state is not treated as an invariant species trait or a demonstrated camouflage mechanism.
