# Mimolagus aurorae

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Mimolagus aurorae is bounded by holotype right M3 IVPP V20115 and referred teeth and tarsals from the lower Irdin Manha Formation within an approximately 47.8–41.2 Ma interval; this does not establish a global genus range or direct glires ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Named material and formation assignment are direct; body mass, ecomorphology and higher placement are comparative.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Right M3 IVPP V20115 and referred material are assigned to Mimolagus aurorae in the published comparison; mimotonid and lagomorph affinities are matrix- and character-dependent, not direct ancestry.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale
