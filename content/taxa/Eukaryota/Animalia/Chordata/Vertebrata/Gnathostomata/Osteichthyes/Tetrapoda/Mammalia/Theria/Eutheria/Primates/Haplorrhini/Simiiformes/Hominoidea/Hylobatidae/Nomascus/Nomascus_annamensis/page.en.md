## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Veun Sai–Siem Pang National Park in northeastern Cambodia, researchers followed the park's only fully habituated northern yellow-cheeked crested gibbon group during the 2017–2018 and 2018–2019 dry seasons and recorded calling behaviour on 165 days. During chainsaw activity, scan and focal samples showed more resting and less scanning; calls were more likely on mornings without chainsaws, but that difference was not statistically significant. The findings concern one group and dry-season observations, not a park-wide or species-wide noise effect.
