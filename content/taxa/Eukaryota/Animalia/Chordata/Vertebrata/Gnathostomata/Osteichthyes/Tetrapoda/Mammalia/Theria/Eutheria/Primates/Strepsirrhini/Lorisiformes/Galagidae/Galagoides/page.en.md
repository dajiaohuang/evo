The pinned COL26.8 primate roster lists demidoff, kumbirensis and thomasi as the three accepted Galagoides species. This guide uses their COL IDs as reader entry points; the genus and member list follow that release and do not resolve broader galagid classification.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with Ambrose and Perkin's nocturnal Moca survey on Bioko for local records of demidoff and thomasi. Compare Wickings et al.'s sympatric Gabon study with Masters and Couette's craniodental identification analysis. Then read Svensson et al.'s original description of the Angolan dwarf galago to see how calls, field surveys and museum specimens jointly support a species diagnosis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Keep locality records, museum identifications and full-range inference separate. The Bioko and Gabon studies address local occurrence and historical identification of demidoff/thomasi, while the Angolan paper centers on a separate discovery. Different sites and methods cannot be combined into one continuous range or a genus-wide life history.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a research reading path for three COL26.8 accepted species, not a genus monograph or complete species account. This batch does not systematically cover full ranges, life histories, population trends or current conservation status for all three species. The Chinese genus name is editorial; the guide has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
