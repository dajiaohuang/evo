## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Chiropotes utahicki Hershkovitz, 1985 as species ID 84HTH under genus ID 3NCR. MDD v2.5 uses Uta Hick's Bearded Saki and Brazil as the country record. The MDD note says utahickae, often used in literature, is an unjustified emendation of the original spelling utahicki. Older field papers used the name C. satanas utahicki; the page retains COL26.8 ID 84HTH as its identity anchor.

## habitatSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Bobadilla and Ferrari (1998) reported 1996 line-transect surveys at Ferreira Penna Scientific Station in Caxiuaná National Forest and at Fazenda Arataú in the Xingu–Tocantins interfluvium. The surveys covered 532.9 km across wet and dry-season months at the former site and 101.3 km in the late wet/early dry season at the latter. They recorded six and 21 sightings, respectively, with reported encounter rates of about 0.11 and 2.07 per 10 km. Because effort, season and site context differed, these rates are not directly comparable population densities and do not isolate a disturbance effect.

## islandDiet

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2002 thesis followed one 24-member group identified at the time as C. satanas utahicki on Germoplasma Island in the Tucuruí Reservoir. Its abstract reports 5,490 records for that group, with feeding the largest activity category, and 110 plant species in the diet; seed items made up 75.6% of the reported feeding records (n=2,721). A later chapter compares the same island group’s diet in 2001 and 2004, but both datasets remain local observations from one group and sampling periods.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 1998 transects used the historical trinomial C. satanas utahicki and had different effort and seasonal coverage at the two sites. The diet record concerns one island group and limited periods, not species-wide diet or behavior. MDD records only Brazil at country level. COL26.8 ID 84HTH fixes page identity; the Chinese display name is editorial. No dossier or external expert review was added. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
