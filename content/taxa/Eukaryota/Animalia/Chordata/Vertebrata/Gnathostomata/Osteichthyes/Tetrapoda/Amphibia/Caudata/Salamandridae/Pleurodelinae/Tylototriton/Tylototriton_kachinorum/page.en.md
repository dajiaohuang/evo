## Ten adult types from a dated forest-clearing collection
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Male holotype ZMMU A5953 was collected at 21:00 on 18 July 2018 in a swamp in a forest clearing on Ingyin Taung Mountain near Indawgyi Lake, Kachin, Myanmar, at 1000 m. Nine paratypes comprised seven males and two females from the same collection. The diagnosis gave male SVL 62.3–74.1 mm and female SVL 72.5–84.8 mm; the holotype measured SVL 67.2 mm and tail 77.3 mm. Two male paratypes had shortened regenerating tail tips, so their tails do not define intact-tail minima. These type-series ranges are not universal adult limits or a population-level dimorphism test.

## Head ridges and a conflict in labelled limb measurements
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The holotype had protruding broad supratemporal ridges, a weak sagittal ridge, a wide nonsegmented vertebral ridge and fourteen rib nodules on each side. Fingers and toes lacked webbing and the adpressed limbs overlapped. The body description labelled forelimb/SVL and hindlimb/SVL as 26.2% and 28.0%, but the holotype list repeated RFLL 26.2 and RHLL 28.0 under a heading saying all measurements were in millimetres. These incompatible unit presentations are explicit; the ratios are not silently converted into measured 26.2-mm and 28.0-mm limbs. Tooth counts of 93 upper-jaw, 110 lower-jaw and 95 vomerine teeth belong to this specimen.

## Dull live markings and six-month ethanol fading
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The live holotype had dark brown head and trunk, dull yellowish- to orange-brown ridges and nodules, lighter limbs and a brown iris with tiny bronze speckles. After six months in ethanol, yellow and orange tones faded to light brownish-grey. Other types varied from lighter yellow-orange to darker, duller brown. These observations retain specimen and preservation state; subtle nodules or dull markings are not demonstrated camouflage, toxicity or an invariant colour test for every population.

## Five ovarian eggs and observed July courtship

primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

Clutch size was explicitly unknown. Five ripe ovarian eggs from female paratype ZMMU A5956 had diameters 1.6–1.7 mm, mean 1.7 mm, with dark brown animal poles and cream-coloured remaining surfaces. These are eggs within one ovary-bearing female, not five mothers, deposited clutch counts or fertilized embryos. Male courtship was observed in July. ## One stage-40 larva with a developing fifth toe
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
Larval description used single voucher ZMMU A5957 at Grosse (2013) stage 40. It measured SVL 10.5 mm and tail 9.7 mm, with developed external gills, four developed fingers and four developed toes plus a fifth-toe nub. Its skin was smooth, unlike the granular adult skin, and no yolk remains were seen. Live background was ochre-golden with translucent pinkish ventral surfaces and darker tail pigmentation. A stage-specific specimen does not establish hatchling or metamorphic size, developmental duration, universal larval colour or an anuran Gosner stage.

## Direct aquatic encounters and separate local reports
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The 2019 account knew one Ingyin Taung locality at 900–1050 m and predicted possible occurrence on adjacent mountains. Adults were encountered after 19:00 in shallow slow streams and artificial cattle-watering ponds surrounded by secondary bamboo and mixed evergreen forest. Males moved on clay bottoms in 20–40 cm water; females and the larva were netted among vegetation in 60–100 cm water. Farmers separately reported animals far from water after rain and in wells. Direct observations and reported knowledge remain distinct; predicted mountains are not confirmed populations. A tentative Vulnerable proposal accompanied explicit unknown range, trends and threats, not a verified current assessment.

## Two mitochondrial markers and inconsistent distance labels
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6591163-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
The study placed kachinorum with sampled Myanmar species and Himalayan himalayanus using ND2 and 16S mitochondrial sequences. The opening result and diagnosis gave comparison minima of 5.3% for ND2 and 2.4% for 16S, while a later genealogy paragraph labelled a 5.3–14.6% range as 16S. The differing marker labels are retained as an unresolved source conflict rather than selecting one distance table interpretation without inspection. Marker genealogy and morphological concordance support the authors’ species hypothesis, not a complete nuclear genealogy or universal divergence threshold.

## A later comparative total length, distinct from SVL
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/11/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13084263-7dblh
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/11/text/en -->
The 2026 paper compared male kachinorum total length 144.5–170.3 mm with smaller vietnamirabilis. It also attributed thirteen or fourteen rib nodules, an indistinct crown glandular ridge, absent lateral tail grooves and a well-developed tail fin fold to kachinorum. The stated total-length interval is not substituted for SVL in the 2019 type description. This later attributed comparison does not establish intact-tail lengths for regenerated individuals or a new population measurement series.
