## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Monthly transect work at the Sainte Luce littoral forest covered four fragments from 2011 to 2018; S17 was used only to confirm presence, while trend analysis used the other three. Across 285 km of transects and 1,968 lemur observations, Thomas's dwarf lemurs showed no significant overall change across the monitored fragments, while detections increased in the most degraded fragment, S7.
