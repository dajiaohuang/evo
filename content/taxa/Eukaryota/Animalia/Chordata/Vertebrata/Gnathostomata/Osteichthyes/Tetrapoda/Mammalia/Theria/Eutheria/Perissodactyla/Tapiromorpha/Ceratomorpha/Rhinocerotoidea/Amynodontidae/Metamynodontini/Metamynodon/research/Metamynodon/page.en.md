# Metamynodon

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
An amynodontid represented by a comparative anatomical test of semi-aquatic locomotion in Metamynodon planifrons, not by a family-wide “swamp rhino” label.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Herbivorous; diet not tested in the locomotor study
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Associated with Orellan channel sandstones
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Semi-aquatic inference from comparative anatomy and deposits

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Wall and Heinbaugh compare orbital, axial and limb characters with other amynodontids. Anatomy and sedimentary association support but do not directly observe behavior.
