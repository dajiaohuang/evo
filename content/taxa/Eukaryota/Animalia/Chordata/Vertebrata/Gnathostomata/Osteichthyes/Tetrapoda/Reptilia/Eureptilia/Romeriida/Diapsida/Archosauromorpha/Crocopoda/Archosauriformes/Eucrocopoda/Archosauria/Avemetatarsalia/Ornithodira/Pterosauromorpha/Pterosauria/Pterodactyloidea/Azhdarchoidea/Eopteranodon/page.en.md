## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route combines occurrence guides with a named 2023 reassessment, without a complete descendant catalogue or shared ecology. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. No external expert review has occurred. The Eopteranodon account and textual captions and both phylogenetic-analysis sections were read here. Original 2005/2006 full papers, images, specimens and supplementary matrices were not independently inspected, and the entire 2023 paper has not been read. Source interpretations are not independent identification, measured performance or expert review; reader-facing delivery remains pending.
