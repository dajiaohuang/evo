## One Cutucú locality
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7683497-scfyx
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The description recorded the species at one locality in Cordillera del Cutucú, Morona Santiago, Ecuador, at 1364–1413 m. Frogs were found at night beside a clear creek in secondary forest, with different specimens on leaves or a terrestrial bromeliad. These records do not establish every occupied habitat or a complete range.
