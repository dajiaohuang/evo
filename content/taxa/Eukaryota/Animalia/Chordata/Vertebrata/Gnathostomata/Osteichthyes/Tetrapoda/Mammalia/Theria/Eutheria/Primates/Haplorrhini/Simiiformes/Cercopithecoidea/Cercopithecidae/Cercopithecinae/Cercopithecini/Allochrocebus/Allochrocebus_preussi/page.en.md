# Allochrocebus preussi

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID BVHH. The exact MDD v2.5 name record supplies the display label Preuss's Monkey; the Chinese name is editorial.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Local knowledge

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Nkengbeza et al. conducted semi-structured interviews with 262 households in 17 villages around Cameroon's Ebo Forest. Villages were selected partly for proximity to the forest and dependence on it for livelihoods. The study records local ecological knowledge and perceptions, not a random census of monkeys.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Local recognition and use

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Interviewees distinguished this monkey from other primates and described a black, hairy animal with a pale throat seen on the ground and in trees; the paper records the local name “Nten.” These are descriptions and a name reported in this study, not a universal community name or formal morphological diagnosis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Reported changes

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The paper compared respondents' uses with their judgments about abundance change and reported different perceptions of decline across use categories; it also recorded conservation support among some subsistence users. These are associations and attitudes in interview responses, not repeated field counts or causal tests.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Phylogeography

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Tosi's comparative study included representatives of preussi in the lhoesti group. A limited set of two Y-chromosomal genes and one X-chromosomal intergenic sequence grouped it with lhoesti, apart from solatus. The analysis concerns sampled sequences, not a modern range survey.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page guides readers through interviews around Ebo and limited-marker phylogenetic research; it is not a complete species account. Interviews do not estimate field density or trends, and the sources do not cover the full range, year-round ecology or a current conservation assessment. The Chinese name is editorial.
<!-- /evo:text -->
