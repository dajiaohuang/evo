## Border mountain localities in Jiangxi and Hunan
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5njd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The monograph listed male holotype SYS a001420 from Bamianshan on Jinggang Mountain, Jiangxi, at 1369 m. Its distribution account combined cited and study records from Jinggang and Nanfengmian in Jiangxi and Taoyuandong and Bamian Mountain in Hunan at 1100–1610 m. These named localities do not demonstrate continuous occupancy across the border mountains or an experimentally determined elevation range.

## Adult measurements within a series also containing a juvenile
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5njd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Examined type material comprised twenty adult males, five adult females and one juvenile. The diagnosis gave adult male snout–vent lengths of 34.1–39.7 mm and female lengths of 37.0–39.9 mm. The juvenile is not added to either adult measurement denominator, and these ranges do not describe growth rate or every population’s size distribution.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-v5njd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys lini as with wide lateral toe fringes. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
