## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Woolly lemurs are nocturnal Malagasy primates whose named diversity has changed as researchers combined mitochondrial sequences, cytogenetics, pelage, morphology, and calls. One eastern study compared 55 animals from 14 eastern and 3 western sites; a later revision analyzed approximately 3,000 bp of mitochondrial sequence with morphological and phenotypic evidence. A separate Masoala study described A. mooreorum. These are taxonomic samples from particular periods and places, not a current genus-wide range map.

Suggested reading path: read the 2006 eastern revision before the 2007 genus treatment to see how A. peyrierasi, A. meridionalis, and the Manombo form were interpreted, then compare the source-bounded pages for A. betsileo and A. mooreorum. For western forms, read the geographic and pelage study alongside later sequence comparisons; the latter report disagreement between fragments about the placement of A. cleesei. Finish with Harcourt’s single-male A. laniger follow and the short Bemaraha call study, keeping both tied to their local samples.
