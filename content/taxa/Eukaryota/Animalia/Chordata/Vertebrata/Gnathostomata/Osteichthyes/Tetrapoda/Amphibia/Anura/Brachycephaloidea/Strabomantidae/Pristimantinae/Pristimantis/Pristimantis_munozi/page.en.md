## Membership in a revised rainfrog group
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9985417-4mjjd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The2023 study retains Pristimantis munozi among16 named members of its narrower Pristimantis myersi group, for which it proposes Trachyphrynus as a formal subgenus name. It excludes several formerly included clades rather than treating every superficially similar frog as part of this group. This is a source-specific supraspecific hypothesis; frozen accepted species identity and a complete current classification are not independently certified by it. Unnamed candidate lineages are retained under their study labels and are not assigned to Pristimantis munozi.

## A group definition is not an individual measurement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9985417-4mjjd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The revised group is described by a short snout, robust body, FingerI shorter thanII, ToeV longer thanIII and absent cranial crests, with body-size ranges pooled at group level. The paper states that no unambiguous phenotypic synapomorphies are known. Its female15.8–34.6mm and male12.6–20.5mm SVL ranges are not newly measured ranges for Pristimantis munozi. Digital discs are generally narrow in this definition, with floridus explicitly exceptional; its phylogenetic position remains untested. Group-level low-perching/ground-use statements are not independent observations of every population or life stage.

## The named lineage keeps its specimen context

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc9985417-4mjjd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
The Results include type specimens from Verdecocha and an additional LaVictoria specimen, both on the western slope of Pichincha volcano. Munozi is the outermost lineage in subclade4, whose reported support is76% ultrafast bootstrap and0.61 Bayesian posterior probability. Those different support statistics are not interchangeable probabilities of the species being valid. The2.9–8.4% distances are reported across all subclade members, not a separate munozi-only range.
