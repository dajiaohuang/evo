# Mico intermedius

## Field observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Rylands' preliminary account describes observations at Dardanelos on the Rio Aripuanã from May through November 1978. Published under the historical name Callithrix humeralifer intermedius, it reports groups of 4–13 animals and use of dense primary forest, secondary forest and campinarana. These observations describe one locality and period, not the species' full range or present-day group size.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Source-linked introduction;. The study is a preliminary 1978 account at Dardanelos and predates the current Mico combination; it cannot establish current abundance, full distribution or genus-wide habitat preference.
