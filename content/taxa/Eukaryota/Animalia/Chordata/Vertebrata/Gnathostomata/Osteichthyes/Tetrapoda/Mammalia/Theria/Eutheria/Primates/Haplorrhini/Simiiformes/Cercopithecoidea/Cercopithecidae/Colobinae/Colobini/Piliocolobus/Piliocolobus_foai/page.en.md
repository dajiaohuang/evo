## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Hart, Ting and Maisels's 2020 assessment treats Foa's red colobus as a Democratic Republic of the Congo montane lineage and reports only two isolated locations in the evidence available to that assessment. The 2021 red-colobus action plan places its boundaries near other Central African forms and calls for monitoring and genetic work. These are dated syntheses of uneven survey evidence, not a new range survey or a present-day population count.
