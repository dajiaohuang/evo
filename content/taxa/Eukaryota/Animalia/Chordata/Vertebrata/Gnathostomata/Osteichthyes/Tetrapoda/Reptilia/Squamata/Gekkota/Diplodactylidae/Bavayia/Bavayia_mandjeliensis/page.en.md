# Bavayia mandjeliensis

## Discovery and specimen

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Named in 2022, the species is represented by adult male holotype MNHN-RA-2022.0061, collected on 4 January 1995 at a forest clearing, 540 m above sea level on Mt. Mandjelia, New Caledonia.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Morphology with specimen context

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Holotype snout–vent length is 66.8 mm. Described males have three precloacal-pore rows, totalling 26–37 pores; these sampled characters are not fixed measurements for every individual.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Night activity and forest records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The revision records night activity on small-tree trunks and branches in forest and clearing edges, above 500 m on Mt. Mandjelia. These observations do not establish current distribution or a complete activity cycle.
<!-- /evo:text -->
