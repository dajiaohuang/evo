## Morphology in the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the described larval chondrocranium at Gosner stage 27, Staurois parvus lacked a larval otic process; the author identifies this as a possible distinguishing feature whose taxonomic level needs investigation in other ranids.

Study scope: Vienna Zoo Schönbrunn breeding facility, Austria; one developmental series of 116 larvae across Gosner stages 19–27, including 11 stage-27 larvae. Article first published 2026-03-31; sample collection dates are not stated.

Study material: Larvae were provided by a zoo breeding facility. This is captive-facility material and does not describe wild-collected larvae or population-level variation.

## Ecology in the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At the studied fast-flowing stream, males used foot flagging among their conspicuous visual signals; acoustic advertisement calling preceded foot flagging in playback and observational results.

Study scope: One population at a rocky section of Sungai Mata Ikan, Ulu Temburong National Park, Brunei Darussalam; sampling periods were 2005–2007 and 2010.

Study material: The study describes free-ranging frogs at the named site; its result is not generalized to all populations.
