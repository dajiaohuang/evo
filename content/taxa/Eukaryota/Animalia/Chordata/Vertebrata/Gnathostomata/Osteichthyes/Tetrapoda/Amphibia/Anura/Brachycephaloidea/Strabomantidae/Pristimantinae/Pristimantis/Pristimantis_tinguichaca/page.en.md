## A north-Pastaza record at 2290 metres
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc8763828-4mjqg
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The specimen was collected on a palm leaf in Machay Reserve montane forest at 2290 m. In 2022 the authors called it the first record north of the Rio Pastaza and an extension below the previously reported 2470-m record in Tungurahua Province. These are dated record comparisons; they do not demonstrate dispersal across the river or define physiological elevation limits.
