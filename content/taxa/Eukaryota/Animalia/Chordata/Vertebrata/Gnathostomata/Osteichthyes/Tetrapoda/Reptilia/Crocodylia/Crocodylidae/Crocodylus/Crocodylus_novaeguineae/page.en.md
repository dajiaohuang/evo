## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Murray and colleagues compared skull morphology in crocodiles from north and south of New Guinea's Central Highlands and described the southern sample as Hall's New Guinea crocodile (Crocodylus halli). Their northern C. novaeguineae comparison material included specimens from the Sepik River and Hunstein. In the compared samples, the northern group had a relatively longer maxilla and smaller postcranial elements than the southern groups. This summarizes differences in a taxonomic diagnosis study, not a field key validated across the island.
