## Read the study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
How was northern New Guinea comparison material used in a species diagnosis? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record ZKNM and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. Murray and colleagues compared skull morphology in crocodiles from north and south of New Guinea's Central Highlands and described the southern sample as Hall's New Guinea crocodile (Crocodylus halli). Their northern C. novaeguineae comparison material included specimens from the Sepik River and Hunstein. In the compared samples, the northern group had a relatively longer maxilla and smaller postcranial elements than the southern groups. This summarizes differences in a taxonomic diagnosis study, not a field key validated across the island.

Continue with Crocodylus halli (COL 8GRXJ) to read the southern diagnosis alongside its northern comparison. The Crocodylia group guide provides the wider reading route; the neighboring page retains its own sources and limits.
