## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This guide links 18 selected COL26.8 species pages across Ghana, Côte d’Ivoire, Nigeria, Benin, Cameroon, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Rwanda, the Republic of the Congo, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya and Uganda. Begin with the Taï comparison of C. campbelli and C. petaurista; compare feeding notes from Bia, Okomu and three Gabon sites; then read the Nyungwe studies of C. denti and C. hamlyni. The Benin habitat model, Niger Delta occupancy survey and Kafa activity scans each answer a different local question. Finish with putty-nosed monkey alarm experiments and the limited Lomako/Lomami records for Wolf’s monkey. These are selected studies, not a complete genus account or a single comparable dataset.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
A reading path for 18 selected species pages, not a complete genus checklist, range summary or ecological synthesis. Sites, dates, methods and taxonomic concepts remain separate. One Wolf's monkey page is explicitly a sparse local research note; several other profiles cover a single study only; not externally expert-reviewed.
