## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Around Dharan in eastern Nepal, a 2018–2019 survey made direct observations at 10 sampling sites and 28 spots across an area reported as 192.6 km². The researchers counted 247 Tarai gray langurs in nine troops; the largest group they recorded contained 73 animals.

## evidence-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The counts describe the survey area and methods used by that study. They should not be read as a population estimate for Nepal or as a present-day species total.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The field account is confined to sites around Dharan and its 2018–2019 sampling period. It does not establish current status across the range and is not externally expert-reviewed.
