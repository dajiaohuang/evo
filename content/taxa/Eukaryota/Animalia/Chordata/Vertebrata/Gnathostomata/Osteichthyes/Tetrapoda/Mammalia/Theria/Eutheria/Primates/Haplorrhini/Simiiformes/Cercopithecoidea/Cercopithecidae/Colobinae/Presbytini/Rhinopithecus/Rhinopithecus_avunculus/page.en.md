## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This page follows the COL26.8 accepted name Rhinopithecus avunculus. The English common name comes from the locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 record; the Chinese rendering is editorial. The selected sources cover feeding and short-term ranging at Khau Ca, and a 2017–2018 habitat survey in Quan Ba forest, northern Vietnam.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A nutritional-ecology study at Khau Ca compared nutrient and anti-nutrient contents in plant parts that monkeys did and did not eat. The authors reported that selected leaves tended to have more water, lipid, protein and metabolizable energy, and less fiber, total phenol and tannin. This describes plant samples at that site, not a fixed species-wide diet.

## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A separate Khau Ca study recorded seven encounters and 26 contact hours during a 28-day field season in May–June 2008. It reported daily path lengths of 851.3 m and 673.5 m on two observed days. These short-term observations do not estimate an annual home range or a species-wide travel distance.

## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
A Quan Ba study used 2017–2018 data to identify forest used by the local population and estimated an area of about 5,000 ha. The authors cited local population estimates of 30–35 in 2007 and 15–21 in 2016. These are dated estimates for one area, not a current species-wide count.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Feeding and ranging data are from Khau Ca, while habitat evidence is from Quan Ba. Different sites, dates and methods are not pooled to infer a species-wide diet, home range, complete range or current population size. Not externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
