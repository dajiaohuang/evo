## movement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pyritz, Kappeler and Fichtel observed four red-fronted lemur groups at Kirindy from 2008 to 2010, accumulating 1,898 hours. Groups remained highly cohesive throughout the year, while daily travel and home-range use varied with ecological season; adult females were the most consistent leaders of group movements. The results describe movement coordination in four groups at one site over a bounded period, not a fixed social rule for every population.
