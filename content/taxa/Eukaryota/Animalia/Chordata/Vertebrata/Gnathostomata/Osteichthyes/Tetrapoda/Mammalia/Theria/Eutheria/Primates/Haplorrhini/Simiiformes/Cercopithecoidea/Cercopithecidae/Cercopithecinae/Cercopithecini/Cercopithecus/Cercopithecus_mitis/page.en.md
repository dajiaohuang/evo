## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Kakamega Forest, Kenya, researchers used 371 all-day focal follows of 24 adult females from three groups over nine months. They recorded 445 food items and analyzed more than 700 food and fecal samples. Although foods eaten varied with availability and among individuals, nutrient intake was more similar across subjects and groups. The study describes one rainforest population and its sampling period, not the full species or every age-sex class.
