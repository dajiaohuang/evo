# Psilopogon haemacephalus

## Tracing the historical genus name

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 places this page at Psilopogon haemacephalus (7WP68). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 9690 and eBird 2021 row 8657 use the same name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-2B7AC50E; BirdTree row 8612 gives Megalaima haemacephala, with match type 1BL to 1BT. Read COL older-combination record 7VSVX points to this accepted ID. These associations locate historical data without establishing identical species boundaries across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Measured samples and beak/wing averages

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 11 birds: 4 female, 5 male and 2 unsexed; 8 have complete measurement sets. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 14.8 mm, beak width 9.3 mm, tarsus 19.2 mm, unflattened wing 80.6 mm and hand-wing index 25.1. Inference=NO does not establish an identical count for every field; complete-set birds and total samples remain separate. The index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed, and beak width is not a test of fruit swallowing or pecking performance.

## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Frugivore is the Metadata category for predominantly fruit resources; trophic level is separately Omnivore. The level spans trophic resources while the fruit-niche label remains; neither field replaces the other. Category thresholds are not measured diet percentages for a locality or season. Fruit species, food size, digestion and seed-dispersal experiments are absent here, leaving detailed diet and dispersal effects unsupported.

## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Insessorial means primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, without implying never descending to the ground. Habitat is Woodland; density code 2 means semi-open habitats, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is sedentary, without establishing lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted here, and categories do not establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.
