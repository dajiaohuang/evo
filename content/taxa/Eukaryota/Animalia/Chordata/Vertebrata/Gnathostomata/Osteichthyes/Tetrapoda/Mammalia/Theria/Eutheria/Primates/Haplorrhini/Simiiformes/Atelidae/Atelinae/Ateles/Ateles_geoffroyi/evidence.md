## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
A comparative CT study found smaller nasal-turbinate surface area in 15 Ateles geoffroyi specimens than in eight Alouatta palliata specimens (Dunn post hoc p = 0.0001); the difference persisted after adjustment for skull size. The Ateles specimens' geographic provenance, collection dates and wild/captive status were not reported, so this sample does not establish species-wide anatomical variation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/1/text -->
Among trichromatic individuals in the comparative analysis of fruit-foraging sequences, Ateles geoffroyi had the highest fruit-sniffing rate of the three study species: A. geoffroyi, Cebus imitator and Alouatta palliata. Alouatta sampling combined Sector Santa Rosa observations with supplemental records from Isla Agaltepec; the result is limited to study samples and does not establish a species-wide rate.

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/2/text -->
For one subadult female Ateles geoffroyi at Barro Colorado Island, movement-step selection was positively associated with canopy height, distance from canopy gaps using an 8-m vegetation cutoff, and crown thickness. The spider-monkey model had a mean cross-validation Spearman correlation of 0.94; this is an individual-level model result, not a species-wide estimate.
