# Tarsius tumpara

## Morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The species was described from Siau Island using tail-tuft, pelage, skull and vocal characters. In comparison with T. sangirensis, the paper notes a shorter, sparser and lighter tail tuft, reduced tarsal fur, and differences in the morning duet.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Locality records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is at the southern end of Siau. The 2008 paper had one holotype and one earlier museum skull; it explicitly reported that genetic data for the newly described species were unavailable.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Scope of the evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Behavioral observations and conservation concerns in the description are historical and geographically limited. The paper does not provide a current island-wide population count.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The taxonomic account is based on very few specimens and localized observations; it is not a current population or island-wide survey. This page is.
<!-- /evo:text -->
