## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Carlos Botelho State Park in southeastern São Paulo, Chaves-Diaz et al. followed one brown-howler group from August 2023 to January 2024. They located the group on 37 follow days and completed full tracking on 22 days, with 344 hours of effective observation. Mean daily path length was 531 m (SD 172 m). In their observational model, the group travelled shorter distances on days with more resting and leaf feeding, and longer distances on days with encounters with other primates; daily distance showed no detectable relationship with fruit feeding, temperature or day length. The result connects movement to behaviour in one group over six months; it is not a species-wide movement average or a causal experiment.
