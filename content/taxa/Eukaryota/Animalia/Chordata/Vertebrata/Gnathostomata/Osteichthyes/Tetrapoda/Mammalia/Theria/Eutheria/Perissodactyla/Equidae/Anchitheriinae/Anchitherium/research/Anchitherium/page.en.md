# Anchitherium

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Miocene equid represented here by A. aurelianense at Sandelzhausen and the regional European MN3–MN9 record, not a single global genus ecology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Browse-dominated mixed feeding at Sandelzhausen
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Warm-temperate woodland context; one-locality dietary sample
<!-- /evo:text -->
