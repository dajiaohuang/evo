## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Cephalopachus bancanus (Horsfield, 1821) under fixed species ID SDLD and genus parent 3KFW. The Mammal Diversity Database uses the common name Western Tarsier and records the species from Borneo and several islands of Indonesia. Older literature often uses Tarsius bancanus; that historical name is not a different page identity here. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## habitatUse

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A 2021 undergraduate thesis studied the Belitung subspecies C. b. saltator at Bukit Peramun using concentration counts and vegetation analysis. It described use of secondary forest at 110–150 m elevation and identified local sleeping trees. This is a site-specific study of one named subspecies; it does not establish habitat use or abundance across C. bancanus as a whole.

## physiology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Welman, Tuen, and Lovegrove captured 13 adult tarsiers in secondary forest at Sama Jaya Nature Reserve, Borneo, in 2014–2015. Laboratory respirometry and temperature measurements found no clear evidence of adaptive heterothermy. Free-ranging core-temperature records came from only one male and one female after most implanted loggers failed, so the field result has a very small individual sample.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This source-linked guide joins one local study of the Belitung subspecies C. b. saltator with a separate thermoregulation study at one Sarawak reserve. Their taxonomic scope, sites, methods, and sample sizes remain distinct. They do not establish species-wide habitat preference, abundance, population trend, a complete range account, or current conservation assessment.
