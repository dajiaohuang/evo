## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Koshima Island in southern Japan, a habituated and provisioned macaque population took part in novel-object and novel-food tests during winter and spring 2013. The study reported weak, non-significant repeatability for the caution component of novel-object responses, but moderate, significant repeatability for consummatory and caution components of novel-food responses. These results apply to that population and protocol, not a species-wide behavioral average.
