## A newly described glassfrog outside the frozen catalogue
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The2026 study describes Nymphargus dajomesae from El Quimi, Ecuador, under ZooBank act ECC9AD28-F16A-4B58-8E13-CD6DA3A82A9E. The frozen COL26.8 accepted-species list has no exact entry for that name. Its source account is therefore presented here at genus level, separately from the accepted-species routes; no identifier or complete catalogue concept is invented. The epithet honors Ecuadorian Olympic weightlifter Neisi Dajomes. The proposed common names are Dajomes Glassfrog and Rana de cristal de Dajomes.

The2026 study describes Nymphargus dajomesae from El Quimi, Ecuador, under ZooBank act ECC9AD28-F16A-4B58-8E13-CD6DA3A82A9E. The frozen COL26.8 accepted-species list has no exact entry for that name. Its source account is therefore presented here at genus level, separately from the accepted-species routes; no identifier or complete catalogue concept is invented. The epithet honors Ecuadorian Olympic weightlifter Neisi Dajomes. The proposed common names are Dajomes Glassfrog and Rana de cristal de Dajomes.
