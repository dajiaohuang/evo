## feedingBiomechanics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In an adult bite-force experiment spanning 23 living crocodilian species, the Australian freshwater crocodile showed relatively high body-size-standardized pressure at its front caniniform teeth and comparatively high pressure at molariform teeth. The authors related its slender snout and pressure profile to feeding on small prey, while the experiment itself directly measured biting force and tooth pressure; the dietary interpretation also draws on earlier studies.
