## Calling and maturity did not coincide in every survey
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc13424835-lx9j7
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Calls were recorded in June–July 2023 while nuptial structures and eggs were still immature. Reproductive observations in early May 2025 and late March 2026 informed a proposed early-March to late-August season. The authors associated calling with heavy rain and its aftermath. This seasonal span remains their synthesis of available surveys; calling alone does not demonstrate mature breeding condition or year-wide reproductive timing.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys elongata as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
