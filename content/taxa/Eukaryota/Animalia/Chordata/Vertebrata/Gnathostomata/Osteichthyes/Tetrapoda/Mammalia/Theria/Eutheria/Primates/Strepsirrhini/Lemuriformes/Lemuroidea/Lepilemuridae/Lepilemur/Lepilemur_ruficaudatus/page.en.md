## geographic genetic variation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The study separated three geographic populations of the red-tailed sportive lemur—Kirindy, Andramasay and Anjahamena—into distinct mitochondrial clades, with 5.88–7.63% divergence; corresponding chromosomal differences were not detected. The authors proposed that these populations might represent different taxa, while COL26.8 continues to accept L. ruficaudatus as one species.
