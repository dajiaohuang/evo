# Elpistostege

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A late Devonian elpistostegalian represented by an exceptionally complete Miguasha specimen whose pectoral fin preserves a mosaic of fin-ray anatomy and wrist- or digit-like endoskeletal elements.
<!-- /evo:text -->
