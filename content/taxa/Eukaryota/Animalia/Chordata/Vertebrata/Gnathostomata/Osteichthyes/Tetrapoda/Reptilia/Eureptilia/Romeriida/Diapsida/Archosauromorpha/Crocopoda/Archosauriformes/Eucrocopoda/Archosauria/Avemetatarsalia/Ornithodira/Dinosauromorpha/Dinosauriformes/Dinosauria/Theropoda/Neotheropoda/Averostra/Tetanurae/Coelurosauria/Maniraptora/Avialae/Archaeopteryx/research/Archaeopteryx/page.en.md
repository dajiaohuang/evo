# Archaeopteryx

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Late Jurassic avialan known from a small Bavarian specimen series; it lies outside crown Neornithes and is not presented as the direct ancestor of living birds.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Not resolved by the cited flight and systematic studies
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Solnhofen-archipelago carbonate environments represented by Bavarian lithographic limestones
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Terrestrial and aerial capacities; flight style is inferred from bone geometry rather than observed behaviour
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Small-bodied paravian
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Unresolved; no single feeding guild is assigned from the cited evidence
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/0 -->
Asymmetrical flight feathers associated with skeletal specimens
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Toothed jaws and a long bony tail
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Pectoral geometry compatible with short-burst active flight in a biomechanical comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The oldest referred specimen extends the genus into the earliest Tithonian Painten Formation and documents substantial anatomical variation; synchrotron comparisons support active flight capacity but not modern crown-bird flight performance.
<!-- /evo:text -->
