# Tarsius

## referenceBindings / usage / title

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/0/usage/title -->
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 — Tarsius Storr, 1780
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/0/usage/scope/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 accepted name, authorship, rank and parent classification; not biological evidence.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/0/usage/scope/zh -->
固定 COL26.8 中的接受名、作者、等级与分类父链；不提供生物学证据。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/1/usage/scope/en -->
Four study plots across a human land-use gradient; the study captured 71 tarsiers and radio-tracked 30. Reported habitat, density, range-size and nightly-travel patterns are confined to those plots and the paper-era name T. dianae.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/1/usage/scope/zh -->
支持四个不同人类土地利用样区的比较；研究捕获 71 只并对 30 只进行无线电追踪。栖地、密度、活动范围和夜间移动结果仅限这些样区，原文使用当时的名称 T. dianae。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/scope/en -->
Supports the authors’ use of T. dentatus for the central-Sulawesi lineage often called T. dianae and the 2001–2006 transect study of 144 tarsiers between Kamarora and Peana; it is not a range-wide population survey.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/scope/zh -->
支持作者将常称为 T. dianae 的中苏拉威西谱系归入 T. dentatus，并记载 2001—2006 年 Kamarora 至 Peana 样线上的 144 只样本；这不是全分布区调查。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/3/usage/scope/en -->
Supports the 2001 Gimpu specimen series, comparisons of pelage, anatomy and proportions, duet-song description, and local records near Gimpu, Lempelero, Tomua and Marena. The authors’ proposed outer limits were explicitly tentative.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/3/usage/scope/zh -->
支持 2001 年 Gimpu 标本系列、毛色与解剖和比例比较、二重唱描述，以及 Gimpu、Lempelero、Tomua、Marena 附近的局地记录；作者提出的外围界限明确仍待确认。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/4/usage/scope/en -->
A two-month exploratory study in August–September 2008 at Mt. Rore Katimbu in Lore Lindu National Park located one group of at least four; three adults were captured. Grouping, body-proportion and behavior findings are preliminary and based on very small samples.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/4/usage/scope/zh -->
支持 2008 年 8—9 月在 Lore Lindu 国家公园 Rore Katimbu 山开展的两个月探索性研究：找到至少四只组成的一群，捕获三只成体。群体结构、身体比例和行为结论均为小样本初步发现。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/5/usage/scope/en -->
Supports the naming of the Manado form as T. spectrumgurskyae, with duet-call and specimen provenance central to identification; the type locality is Manado. The paper places this taxonomic treatment in a northern-Sulawesi biogeographic argument, not a new population census.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/5/usage/scope/zh -->
支持将 Manado 形态群命名为 T. spectrumgurskyae；辨识以二重唱和标本来源为重要依据，模式产地为 Manado。论文将该分类处理置于苏拉威西北部生物地理讨论中，并非新的种群普查。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/6/usage/scope/en -->
Acoustic recordings from Tangkoko National Park in July–August 2018; female duet phrases were assigned to individuals at 80% accuracy and male phrases at 64% using the paper’s classifier. These are results for sampled recordings, not a species-wide communication rate.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/6/usage/scope/zh -->
支持 2018 年 7—8 月 Tangkoko 国家公园的声学记录；论文分类器将雌性二重唱片段判给正确个体的准确率为 80%，雄性为 64%。这些是所采录音的结果，不代表全种交流率。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/7/usage/scope/en -->
Supports the description of the Gorontalo form as T. supriatnai, the Bumbulan type locality, and the diagnostic use of duet-call form and provenance in the authors’ northern-Sulawesi classification.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/7/usage/scope/zh -->
支持将 Gorontalo 形态群描述为 T. supriatnai、Bumbulan 模式产地，以及作者在苏拉威西北部分类中使用二重唱形式和来源地进行辨识。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/8/usage/scope/en -->
Local nest-tree observations compared agricultural areas and secondary forest in Popayato-Paguat, Gorontalo. The paper reports different commonly used nest-tree plants by habitat and 42.9% continued use among nest trees surveyed for site fidelity; these results do not establish species-wide preferences.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/8/usage/scope/zh -->
支持 Gorontalo 的 Popayato-Paguat 农业地与次生林巢树观察比较。论文报告不同生境中常见巢树植物不同，并记录后续忠实度调查仍使用 42.9% 的既有巢树；这些结果不构成全种偏好。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/10/usage/scope/en -->
