## primaryStudyScopes / sectionEvidence / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/primaryStudyScopes/0/sectionEvidence/1/evidenceSummary/en -->
BSSVS and selected DIVALIKE+j analyses favour mainland origin with one event for the eiffingeri–berylliniris–wangi ancestor and another for the idiootocus lineage. The discussion gives approximate dates of five and one million years ago, while acknowledging uncertainty from unresolved topology. Pure DIVALIKE instead reconstructs widespread ancestors and vicariance. Model choice therefore materially changes the history inferred; AICc weights of 0.59 or 0.63 for the selected model are comparative fit weights, not probabilities that an observed ocean crossing occurred.
