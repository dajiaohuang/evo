## feedingBiomechanics

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A bite-force study spanning 23 living crocodilian species found the gharial to be a statistical low-force outlier after accounting for body size, yet it generated high pressure at its front caniniform teeth. The authors linked this combination to its extremely slender snout and narrow teeth with very small contact areas: lower force does not mean lower local tooth pressure.
