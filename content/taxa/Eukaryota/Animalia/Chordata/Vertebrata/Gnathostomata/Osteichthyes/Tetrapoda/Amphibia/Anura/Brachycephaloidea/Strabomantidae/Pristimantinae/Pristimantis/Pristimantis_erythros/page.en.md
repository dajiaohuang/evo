## Accepted name and placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis erythros Sánchez-Nivicela, Celi-Piedra, Posse-Sarmiento, Urgilés, Yánez-Muñoz & Cisneros-Heredia, 2018 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Grassland and terrestrial bromeliad encounters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5923216-4mjdr
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The description reported the Cajas Massif type locality at 3450–3500 m, in páramo grassland and shrubs. Specimens were mainly collected in Puya hamata bromeliads and Neurolepis villosa grasses near small streams. These encounter substrates do not establish egg deposition in bromeliads or streams, or physiological elevation limits.

## Observed activity and unrecorded sounds
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5923216-4mjdr
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Vocalizations were heard but not recorded between 08:00 and 11:00 and between 17:00 and 19:00. Active individuals were observed from dusk to about 21:00, after which activity declined. The account supplies observed time windows, without a verified acoustic description or proof that no activity occurs outside the sampled hours.

## Survey non-detections beyond the type locality
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5923216-4mjdr
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
Surveys at 26 suitable Cajas Massif localities during 2014–2017 yielded no additional records. The authors therefore suggested a very restricted distribution and proposed Critically Endangered status. The non-detections retain their surveyed places and years; the dated proposal is not a verified current IUCN assessment or proof of absence from every unsurveyed site.
