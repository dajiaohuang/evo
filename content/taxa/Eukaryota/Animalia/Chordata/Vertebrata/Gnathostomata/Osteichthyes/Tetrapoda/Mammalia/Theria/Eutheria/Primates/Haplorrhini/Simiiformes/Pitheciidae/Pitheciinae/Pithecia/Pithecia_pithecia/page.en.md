## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Furuviksparken in Sweden, five captive-born adult white-faced sakis were offered all pairwise combinations of 15 foods from their usual managed diet, with each pair presented ten times. Group preference rankings correlated with total energy, and lipid content had the strongest positive relationship among carbohydrates, protein and lipids. The 5,250 choices were repeated presentations to the same five animals and do not represent wild feeding preferences.
