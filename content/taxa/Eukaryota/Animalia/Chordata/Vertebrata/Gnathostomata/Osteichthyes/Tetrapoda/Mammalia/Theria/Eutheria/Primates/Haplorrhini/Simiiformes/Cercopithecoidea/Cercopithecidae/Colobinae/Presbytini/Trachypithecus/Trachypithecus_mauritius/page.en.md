## phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In 573-bp mitochondrial cytochrome-b data from 115 silvered langurs, Roos, Nadler and Walter identified five main monophyletic lineages, including *T. mauritius*. They proposed treating each lineage as a separate species and estimated a near-radiation among them about 0.95–1.25 million years ago, while leaving their exact relationships unresolved. Their broader scenario of an origin on Java followed by expansion concerns the group and is not, by itself, a migration history for this species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
