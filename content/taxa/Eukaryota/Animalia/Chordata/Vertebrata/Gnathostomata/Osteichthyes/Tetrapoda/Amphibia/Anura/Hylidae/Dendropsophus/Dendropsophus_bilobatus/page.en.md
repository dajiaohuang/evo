## The upper Madeira east-bank records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7316805-n9x49
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The research team sampled more than 150 permanent sites around the upper Madeira and Purus–Madeira region but reported this species only from east-bank lowland open rainforest. The authors expected possible occurrence across the nearby Bolivian border; that expectation is not recorded here as a confirmed Bolivian locality.

## Wet-season choruses with unresolved endpoints
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7316805-n9x49
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The account observed frogs during the rainy season from early November to late March. Males called in choruses in river-connected flooded areas, perched from just above water to about 2 m high. Calling began around dusk and continued at least until midnight; its nightly endpoint was unknown. Amplexus was not observed, so these calling records do not establish mating or egg-laying behaviour.
<!-- /evo:text -->
