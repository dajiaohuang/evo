For the source name Vitreorana uranoscopa, juvenile habitat is coded aquatic. Positive egg-site flags indicate arboreal eggs attached to plants. Every positive category is retained, including multiple categories where present. Juvenile habitat is not an adult preference, and terrestrial oviposition is distinct from direct development. These broad categories do not establish clutch size, seasonal breeding, parental attendance, nest dimensions or offspring survival. Original references are indexed in the archived row; those upstream studies have not all been independently read.

## Workbook record Vitreorana uranoscopa: inference and length limits
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-7g5fd
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
Vitreorana uranoscopa carries explicit inference flags for head width and tibia length in the workbook. The flagged cells are phylogenetically inferred estimates, not new measurements of specimens. Numeric body/head/tibia cells remain in the evidence snapshot. Methods combine available means with otherwise maxima, while length headers do not explicitly state units and rows do not uniformly specify sex or developmental stage. Consequently these values are not restated as diagnostic millimetre ranges or measured population variation. Mapping a historical name does not resolve those measurement limitations.
