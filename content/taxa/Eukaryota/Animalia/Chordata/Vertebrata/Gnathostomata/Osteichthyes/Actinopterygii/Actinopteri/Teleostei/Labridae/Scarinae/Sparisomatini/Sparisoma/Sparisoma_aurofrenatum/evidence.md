## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
In the first-year caged experiment on new cinderblock substrates at Conch Reef in the Florida Keys (November 2003–August 2004), redband parrotfish-only treatments had algal cover and biomass similar to herbivore-exclosure controls and showed no significant effect on measured algal groups except Dictyota spp. This is a single-site, caged-treatment result for algal succession on new substrates; it is not a range-wide estimate of grazing by free-ranging fish.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / textZh

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/textZh -->
在佛罗里达礁岛群康奇礁的新混凝土砌块基质第一年围笼实验中（2003 年 11 月至 2004 年 8 月），仅放入红带鹦嘴鱼的处理组，其藻类覆盖度和生物量与草食动物排除对照相近；除 Dictyota 属外，对测量的藻类类群未显示显著影响。这是单一地点、围笼处理下针对新基质藻类演替的结果，不能作为自由活动鱼类摄食影响的全分布区估计。
