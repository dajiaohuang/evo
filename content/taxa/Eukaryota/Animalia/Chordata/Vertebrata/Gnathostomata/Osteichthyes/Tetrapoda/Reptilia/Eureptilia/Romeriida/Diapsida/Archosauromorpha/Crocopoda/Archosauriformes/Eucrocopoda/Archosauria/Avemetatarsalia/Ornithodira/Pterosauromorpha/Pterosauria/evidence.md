## readerSources / scope / en

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSources/1/scope/en -->
Detailed redescription and phylogenetic analysis of Norian pterosaur holotype MFSN 1797 and referred specimen MPUM 6009; this locality does not establish a global first appearance.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## readerSources / scope / zh

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSources/1/scope/zh -->
对诺利期翼龙正模 MFSN 1797 与归入标本 MPUM 6009 的详细再描述和系统分析；该地点不能确定全球最早出现时间。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## readerSources / scope / en

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSources/2/scope/en -->
Quantitative analysis of 108 pterosaur taxa; reports an every-continent record while emphasizing severe spatial and temporal patchiness and sampling-sensitive biogeographic inference.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## readerSources / scope / zh

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSources/2/scope/zh -->
对 108 个翼龙分类单元进行定量分析；报告各大洲均有记录，同时指出时空分布极不均匀，地理推断受采样影响。
<!-- /evo:text -->

## readerSources / scope / en

<!-- evo:text /records/readerSources/3/scope/en -->
Reports exposed Hamipterus eggs and embryo-bearing specimens from one fossil block; behavioral and hatchling-flight interpretations remain distinct from preserved evidence.

## readerSources / scope / zh

## readerSources / scope / en
