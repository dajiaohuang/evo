## Separate seed niche from plant trophic level

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Trophic.Niche=Granivore; Trophic.Level=Herbivore. The source defines Granivore as at least 60% of food resources coming from seeds or nuts. Herbivore level means at least 70% of food resources from plants. These fields and thresholds remain separate, not a measured seasonal diet percentage here; the seeds/nuts threshold differs from the all-plant threshold. No seed, nut or insect species list or individual feeding record is supplied, leaving detailed and breeding-season menus unsupported.
