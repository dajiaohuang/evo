This page retains that original row under its recorded catalogue link to Brachycephalus garbeanus; the biological traits remain source-concept observations rather than newly certified traits of every specimen assigned to the accepted name. Direct development means no free-living tadpole stage, not absence of embryonic development or humidity requirements. The compiled categories are not new breeding experiments.
