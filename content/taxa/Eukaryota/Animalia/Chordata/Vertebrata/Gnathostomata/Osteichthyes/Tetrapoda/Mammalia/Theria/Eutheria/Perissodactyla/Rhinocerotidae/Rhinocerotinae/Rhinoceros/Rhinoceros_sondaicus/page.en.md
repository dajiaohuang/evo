## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Skin folds and an extended upper lip are characteristic. Adult males typically bear one small horn, whereas females often lack a prominent horn; absence of a conspicuous horn alone does not exclude this species.
