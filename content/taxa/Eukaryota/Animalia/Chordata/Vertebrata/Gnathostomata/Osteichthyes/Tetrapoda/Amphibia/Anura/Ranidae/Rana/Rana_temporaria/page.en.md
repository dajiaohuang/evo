## Life history in the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the author's Skåne monitoring series, start of breeding among local Rana temporaria populations varied by as much as three weeks within a year. The observations span 1990–2015, with the number of monitored ponds varying from 29 to 84 and the most distant ponds separated by 60 kilometres; this is a local monitoring series, not a species-wide phenology estimate.

Study scope: Skåne province, southern Sweden; monitoring from 1990 through 2015; 29–84 ponds depending on year, maximum pond separation 60 km.

Study material: Wild breeding populations observed through their spawn at ponds.

## Ecology in the study
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
For 19 Skåne ponds with logger data in 2011–2015, earlier mean breeding time was associated with warmer average logged pond temperatures and larger average breeding congregations, estimated from spawn-clump counts; the reported temperature and congregation terms were statistically significant. Substantial residual pond variation remained, and this observational association does not identify a causal mechanism.

Study scope: 19 logger ponds in Skåne, southern Sweden; breeding and temperature subset from 2011–2015. Spawn-clump count was used as the breeding-congregation estimate.

Study material: Wild breeding populations; no captive frogs were studied.

## A genomic synteny comparator, not a new behaviour experiment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10548314-6wr99
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/13/text/en -->
Published genome assemblies of Rana temporaria provide a comparison for the SWS2–LWS region in Figure2. The authors locate an SWS2-containing block alongside other conserved genes, helping interpret the absence of recognizable SWS2 from two poison-frog genomes. These are comparative searches of existing genomic resources, not new individual sampling or measurements of colour discrimination, retinal cell type or full opsin function in Rana temporaria. Presence of a sequence does not alone establish its expression in every stage or tissue.

## Repeat annotations are computational observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12822217-6wr99
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/16/text/en -->
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Rana temporaria was performed here.
