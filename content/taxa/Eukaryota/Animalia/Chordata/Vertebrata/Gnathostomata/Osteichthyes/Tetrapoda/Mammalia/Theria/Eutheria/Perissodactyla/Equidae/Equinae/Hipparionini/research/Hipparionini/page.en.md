# Hipparionini

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A persistently tridactyl equine tribe whose Old World radiation comprised multiple genus-rank clades rather than a single broadly defined Hipparion.
<!-- /evo:text -->
