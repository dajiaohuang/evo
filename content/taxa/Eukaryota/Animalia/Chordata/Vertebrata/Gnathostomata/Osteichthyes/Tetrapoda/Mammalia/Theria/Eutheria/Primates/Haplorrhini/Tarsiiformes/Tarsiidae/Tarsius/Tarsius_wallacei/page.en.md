## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The paper mapped separated northern and southern populations in Central Sulawesi, with Palu Bay and the city of Palu between them. The southern population was confirmed only at the type locality Uwemanje; boundaries in the paper reflect the authors’ surveys.
