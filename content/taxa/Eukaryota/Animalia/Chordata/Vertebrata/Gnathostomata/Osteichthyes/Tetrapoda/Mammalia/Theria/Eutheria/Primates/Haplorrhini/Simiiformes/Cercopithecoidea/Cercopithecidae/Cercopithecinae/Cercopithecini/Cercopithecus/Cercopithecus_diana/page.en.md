## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Researchers played alarm calls from familiar and unfamiliar monkeys to wild Diana monkeys at Taï Forest in Côte d’Ivoire and Tiwai Island in Sierra Leone. The calls referred to leopard and eagle threats. Responses varied with social familiarity and habitat context. These playback results describe reactions to particular calls in the studied groups, not actual predation risk or a complete social network.
