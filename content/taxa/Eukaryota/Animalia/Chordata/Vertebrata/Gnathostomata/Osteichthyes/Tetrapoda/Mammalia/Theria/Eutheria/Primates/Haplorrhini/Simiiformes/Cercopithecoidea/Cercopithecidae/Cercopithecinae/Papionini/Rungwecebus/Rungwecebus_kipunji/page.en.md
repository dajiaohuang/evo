## populationSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The first census report combined presence surveys conducted from 2003 to 2006 with total counts in the Southern Highlands and Ndundulu (Udzungwa); it reported 1,117 animals across the known populations at that time. A 2022 complete count surveyed 1,428 km in three Southern Highlands forests and reported 1,866 individuals in 59 groups, with the authors comparing these results to their earlier Southern Highlands baseline. The 2022 count did not include a new census of Ndundulu.

## udzungwaSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2026 paper reported three standardized sweep censuses of the Ndundulu population between 2013 and 2024. Each census recorded four to six groups across a 10.42-km² range that includes opportunistic sightings; mean minimum group size was 17 ± 2, producing crude minimum-abundance values of 51 ± 6 to 102 ± 12. These are estimates for the Udzungwa population and survey periods, not for the species as a whole.
