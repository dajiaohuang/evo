## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Plains zebras use African grasslands and open woodland and feed mainly on grasses. Stripe patterns vary within the species. This entry concerns the entire species; extinction of the quagga must not be assigned to all Equus quagga.
