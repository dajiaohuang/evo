## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A preliminary 2012 dry-season line census in and near Ankarana National Park compared crowned lemurs across primary, secondary and degraded forest. The authors reported local density estimates for each forest type and noted that their comparison with earlier estimates was limited because the older surveys did not always sample the same areas. These values are historical site estimates.
