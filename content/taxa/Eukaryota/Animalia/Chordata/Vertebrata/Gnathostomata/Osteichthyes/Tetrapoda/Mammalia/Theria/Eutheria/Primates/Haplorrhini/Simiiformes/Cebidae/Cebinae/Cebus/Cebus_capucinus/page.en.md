## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2009–2010 field study at Pacuare Nature Reserve, Costa Rica, analyzed 608 fruit-foraging focal follows from 49 habituated white-faced capuchins in three groups. Adults had higher return rates than younger age classes for fruits ranked difficult to acquire; no significant age-class difference was detected for easy-to-acquire fruits. This is a foraging comparison within a particular sample and difficulty ranking, not a species-wide diet.

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
