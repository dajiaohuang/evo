## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Radespiel and colleagues surveyed Sahafina Forest, described as lowland rainforest at about 29–230 m near Mantadia National Park. The authors said the full distribution of the newly described species was unknown; the interpretation of a narrow, fragmented distribution is bounded by that survey's coverage and is not a range-wide census.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study combined phylogenetic analyses of three mitochondrial genes with morphometric comparisons and photographs to distinguish the Sahafina sample from then-described mouse lemurs and propose a new species. This page preserves that combination of evidence; a taxonomic study at one forest cannot by itself establish the full range, daily behaviour or current population condition.
