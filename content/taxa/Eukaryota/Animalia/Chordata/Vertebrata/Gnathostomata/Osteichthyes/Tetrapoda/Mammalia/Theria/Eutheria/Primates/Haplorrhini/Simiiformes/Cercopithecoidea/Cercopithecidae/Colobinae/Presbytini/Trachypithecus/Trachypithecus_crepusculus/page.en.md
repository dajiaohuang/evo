## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Researchers followed one group in primary evergreen forest on Mt. Wuliang in southwest China from June 2012 to January 2016. The group was typically arboreal, spending about 2.9% of observed time on the ground, mainly during geophagy. It preferred higher strata while resting and usually moved below 20 m. The paper also compared forest cover and terrestriality across six langur species. One Mt. Wuliang group does not represent all habitats or behavior of this species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
Strata and terrestriality results come from one Mt. Wuliang group; the interspecific association is comparative and does not show that forest cover caused individual behavior.
