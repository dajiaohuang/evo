## One skull in a comparative CT study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The skull MNHN.ZA.AC 1946-71 was included in Martín-Jiménez and Pérez-García's 2021 comparative neuroanatomy study. Their reconstruction describes a concave anterior dorsal cranial-cavity surface associated with the expanded olfactory region and a posteriorly directed cavity profile. These observations remain specific to the examined skull.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## How to read reproductive records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 22. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Limits of these trait data

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
This supplemental account uses limited trait material and does not count as complete morphology or ecology. This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
