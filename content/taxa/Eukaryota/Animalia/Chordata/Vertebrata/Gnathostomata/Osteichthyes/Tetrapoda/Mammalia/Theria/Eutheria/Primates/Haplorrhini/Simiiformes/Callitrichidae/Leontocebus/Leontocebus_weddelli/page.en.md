# Leontocebus weddelli

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The supplement records two observations at Candeias do Jamari and Itapuã do Oeste, Rondônia, under Leontocebus weddelli weddelli. COL26.8 places that subspecies under the accepted species L. weddelli; the records are not a range-wide survey.
<!-- /evo:text -->
