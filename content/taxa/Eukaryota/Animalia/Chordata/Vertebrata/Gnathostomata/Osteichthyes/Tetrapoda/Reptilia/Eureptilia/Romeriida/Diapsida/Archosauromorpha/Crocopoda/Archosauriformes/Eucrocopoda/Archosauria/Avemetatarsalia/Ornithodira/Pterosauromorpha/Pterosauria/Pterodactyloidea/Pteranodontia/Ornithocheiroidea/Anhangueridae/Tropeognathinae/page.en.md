## Keep specimen identity when comparing maturity and size

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Sampled histology of MN 6594-V supports ceased or nearly ceased growth despite incomplete fusion in some axial structures. The unfused wing-finger extensor process in MPSC material informs the authors’ discussion of immaturity. These concern different specimens, and the latter is not established as a member of this subfamily. Comparisons retain sampled elements, identities and reconstruction methods rather than forming a measured Tropeognathinae growth curve.
