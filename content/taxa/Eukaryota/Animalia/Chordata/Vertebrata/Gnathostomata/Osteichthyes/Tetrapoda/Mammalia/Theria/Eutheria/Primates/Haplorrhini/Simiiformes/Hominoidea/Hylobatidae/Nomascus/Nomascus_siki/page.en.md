## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2018–2019 survey in Vietnam's Truong Son Key Biodiversity Area recorded 149 southern white-cheeked gibbon groups across four study areas. Combined with a similar 2016 survey in Khe Nuoc Trong Forest, the authors identified at least 252 groups and estimated about 425 groups across the KBA; records were concentrated in western montane evergreen forest near the Vietnam–Laos border. This is a protected-landscape survey and estimate, not a global species total.
