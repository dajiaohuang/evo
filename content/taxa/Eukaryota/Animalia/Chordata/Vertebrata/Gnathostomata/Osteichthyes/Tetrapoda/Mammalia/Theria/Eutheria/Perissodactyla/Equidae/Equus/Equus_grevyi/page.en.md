## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Narrow stripes, large rounded ears and a largely white belly are characteristic. Semi-arid grasslands are important habitat, and water needs vary with reproductive state. Its social organization should not simply be copied from plains-zebra family groups.
