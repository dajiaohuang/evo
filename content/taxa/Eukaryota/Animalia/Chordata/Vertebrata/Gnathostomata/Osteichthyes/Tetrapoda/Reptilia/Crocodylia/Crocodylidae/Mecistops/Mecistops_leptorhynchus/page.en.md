## Read the study

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Which measured skull features helped distinguish two slender-snouted crocodiles? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record 8GPZP and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. A comparison of adult skulls from the two living slender-snouted crocodiles found cranial shape differences between the Central African slender-snouted crocodile (Mecistops leptorhynchus) and its West African congener. Nasal shape and the snout length-to-width ratio at the premaxillary notch were especially informative. The paper concerns cranial diagnosis; one ratio is not an absolute field character independent of age, individual variation or measurement method.

Continue with Mecistops cataphractus (COL 3YKJB) to read the congener's field-monitoring guide without treating skull measurements as a census. The Crocodylia group guide provides the wider reading route; the neighboring page retains its own sources and limits.
