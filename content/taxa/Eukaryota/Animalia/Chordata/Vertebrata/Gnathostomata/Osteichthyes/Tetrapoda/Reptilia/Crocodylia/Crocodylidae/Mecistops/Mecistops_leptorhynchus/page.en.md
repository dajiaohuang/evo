## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A comparison of adult skulls from the two living slender-snouted crocodiles found cranial shape differences between the Central African slender-snouted crocodile (Mecistops leptorhynchus) and its West African congener. Nasal shape and the snout length-to-width ratio at the premaxillary notch were especially informative. The paper concerns cranial diagnosis; one ratio is not an absolute field character independent of age, individual variation or measurement method.
