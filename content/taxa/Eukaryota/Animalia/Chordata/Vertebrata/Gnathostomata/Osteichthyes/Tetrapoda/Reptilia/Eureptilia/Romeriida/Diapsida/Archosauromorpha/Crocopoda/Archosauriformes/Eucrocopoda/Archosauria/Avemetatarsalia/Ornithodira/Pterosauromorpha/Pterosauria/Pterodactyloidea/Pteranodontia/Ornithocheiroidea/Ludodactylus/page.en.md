## Naming history and dated classification

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2003 authors explain the genus name through ludus, game or play, and dactylus, finger, referring to toy forms combining teeth with a posterior crest. The species name honors artist John Sibbick. The original description places the genus in its dated Ornithocheiridae framework. That historical attribution and the fixed database parent chain remain; all subsequent taxonomic revisions have not been inspected.
