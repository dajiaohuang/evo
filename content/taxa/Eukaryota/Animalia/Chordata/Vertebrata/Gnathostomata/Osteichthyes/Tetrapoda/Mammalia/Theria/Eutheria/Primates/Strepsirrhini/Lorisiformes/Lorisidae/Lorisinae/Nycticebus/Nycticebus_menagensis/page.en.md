## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
MDD currently uses Philippine Slow Loris and records Tawi-Tawi as the type locality, while noting that the type specimen is lost; its distribution summary also includes Borneo and Bangka and Belitung. Timm and Birney reviewed historical collecting records from Tawi-Tawi in 1992. Those historical records and the MDD summary are not a modern field census.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Timm and Birney examined the history of the name menagensis and its Tawi-Tawi material. Munds and colleagues used N. menagensis as part of their Bornean taxonomy in the 2013 study. Current MDD displays the same pinned COL26.8 name as Philippine Slow Loris; readers should distinguish the paper-era usage from the current checklist identity. A 2023 molecular study also reported limits in matching several Bornean pelage forms to genetic lineages.
<!-- /evo:text -->
