# Huaxiapterus


## Entries under the older genus receive different interpretations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The fixed Huaxiapterus directory offers benxiensis, corollatus and jii entries. The 2023 revision recombines corollatus into Huaxiadraco, treats benxiensis as its synonym and refers jii to S. dongi. The parent chain preserves record traceability rather than establishing these three names as a confirmed natural group.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route combines occurrence guides for three fixed entries with a named revision, without supplying a complete descendant catalogue or shared ecology. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. The added account reuses the 2023 revision and previously read passages, with complete ZMNH M813, BXGM V0011 and D2525 accounts read in P5-99 and the complete GMN-03-11-001 account and textual caption additionally read in P5-100; the original 2005 full description remains unavailable. Images, specimens, original 2006/2007 descriptions, other newly reported specimens, supplementary measurements, histology and diet remain incompletely read or independently checked. Referral and maturity are author interpretations; fixed parent chains, occurrence counts and ages remain intact. Complete monographs and reader-facing delivery remain unfinished.
