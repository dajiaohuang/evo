# Pithecia milleri

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Villalba, Páez and Palacios combined opportunistic field observations from 2007–2015 with museum and database records, assembling 77 records from western Amazonian Colombia. The study extended records eastward along Guaquirá Creek and raised the reported elevational limit to 1,100 m based on a field observation near 1,070 m. The authors identified the Caguán–Yarí interfluve and the contact area with P. hirsuta as survey gaps; these are compiled records, not continuous monitoring.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 77 records combine different years, collections and opportunistic observations; alone they do not establish present occupancy, abundance or a contact boundary with P. hirsuta. The Chinese name is editorial. This page is.
