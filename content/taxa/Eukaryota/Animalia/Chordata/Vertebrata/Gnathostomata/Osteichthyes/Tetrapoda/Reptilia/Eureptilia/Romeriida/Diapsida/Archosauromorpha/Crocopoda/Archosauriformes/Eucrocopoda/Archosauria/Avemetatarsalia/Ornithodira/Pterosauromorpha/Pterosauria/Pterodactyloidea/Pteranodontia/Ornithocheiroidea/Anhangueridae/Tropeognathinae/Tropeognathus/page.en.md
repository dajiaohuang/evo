# Tropeognathus


## Compare large body material with named species records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This Tropeognathus guide now pairs database-recorded named species material with the more extensive large body specimen described in 2013. MN 6594-V retains a cf. identification, an unknown exact site and a wingspan reconstructed with missing segments. These limits govern its anatomical and size interpretation. Other indeterminate anhanguerid wing bones in the study are not assigned to this genus.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Read specimen identity before wingspan and development

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Open [T. mesembrinus](Tropeognathus_mesembrinus/page.en.md) and follow MN 6594-V through comparative identification, concretion preservation, sampled histology and wingspan methods before returning to named occurrence records. Preserved bones, comparative estimates and functional hypotheses have distinct evidential scopes; retained stratigraphic brackets do not supply a precise age for a specimen with an unknown collecting site.

## Evidence limits
