## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Taï Forest in Côte d’Ivoire, a 13-month study compared two habituated groups each of Campbell's monkey, lesser spot-nosed monkey and Diana monkey. Campbell's monkeys ate mostly fruit along with substantial animal matter and used ground and low forest strata (below 5 m) more than the other two study species. Diet overlap with Diana monkeys was highest overall and declined in the low-fruit season. This is a comparison of six local groups, not a species-wide ecological rule or direct measure of competition.
