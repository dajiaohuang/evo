## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the Dieng Mountains of Central Java, Widyastuti and colleagues collected field data in 2018 and 2021. They combined triangulation and acoustic spatial capture–recapture to estimate group density, then used a habitat-suitability model to extrapolate a local population estimate of 1,092 gibbons. That number is a model-based estimate for the Dieng landscape and study period, not a current count for Java or the species as a whole.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The 1,092 estimate is local to the Dieng Mountains and uses habitat-suitability extrapolation from 2018 and 2021 fieldwork; it is not a current island-wide total. This profile is not externally expert-reviewed.
