## Accepted name and placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Allobates pacaas Melo-Sampaio, Prates, Peloso, Recoder, Vechio, Marques-Souza & Rodrigues, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Aromobatidae (family) → Allobates (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A reported distance to velocicantus
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7648453-lcnzp
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The2020 study’s Table2 reports 10.3% uncorrected p-distance and 11.2% Kimura2-parameter distance between the Allobates pacaas label and velocicantus, based on a678-bp16S fragment. These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. The table is a sampled comparison; original accessions, alignments and per-donor TableS1 entries remain independently unreviewed.

## Dark dorsal marks in the published comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7648453-lcnzp
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The velocicantus description places pacaas among compared Allobates with large dark dorsal patches or rhomboid,diamond,hourglass ortriangular marks. It contrasts this group with the focal species’ lack of large dark marks; it does not assign one particular shape to every listed species. This is the2020 author comparison, not a newly inspected photograph series, a full diagnosis or an invariant pattern in all life stages.
