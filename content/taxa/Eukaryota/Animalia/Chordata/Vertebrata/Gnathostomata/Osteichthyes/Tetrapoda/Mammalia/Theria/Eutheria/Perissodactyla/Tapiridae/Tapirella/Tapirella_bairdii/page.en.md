## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Leaves are prominent in the plant-based diet, alongside fruit, twigs and flowers. A short grasping proboscis assists feeding. The source uses Tapirus bairdii, while this release accepts Tapirella bairdii.
