## The cited comparison lacks finger nuptial spines
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10649098-45n7v
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
Nanorana taihangnica: The 2023 account describes this species as lacking finger nuptial spines in its comparison with laojunshanensis. The genus discussion additionally lists first finger longer than second and complete toe webbing for both quadranus and taihangnica. These statements come from comparative descriptions, not a newly examined absence in every season or all populations. Lack of finger spines does not establish absence of reproduction, a silent male or absence of spines elsewhere on the body.
