## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
From February 2019 to January 2020, Gogoi and colleagues surveyed 40 rainforest fragments in Assam's Upper Brahmaputra Valley and confirmed capped langurs in 11. Their occupancy models found the strongest association with food-tree diversity. The result describes fragment occupancy in one valley during one survey period; it is not a range-wide abundance census and does not by itself show that tree diversity caused the presence or absence pattern.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The survey covered 40 fragments in the Upper Brahmaputra Valley of Assam during 2019–2020; it does not represent the full species range or all current populations.
