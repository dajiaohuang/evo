## survey-records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A northeastern Peru survey reported A. miconax records from 1,200–3,100 m. Across all Aotus in the wider survey, the researchers compiled evidence from 44 localities using direct visual observations, calls and secondary signs; the abstract does not break those totals down by species. The elevation span is therefore a summary of surveyed records, not a complete altitudinal limit.

## model-boundary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The same paper combined field records with ecological-niche modelling to estimate probable historical distribution and discuss habitat change. Modelled historical suitability and the authors’ dated conservation recommendation are not current field confirmations or a new population census.
