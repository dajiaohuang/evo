## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10688756-4h7rn
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Phyllomedusa iheringii as having larval development and its oviposition as terrestrial. A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.

## Compiled lengths retain inference and measurement limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The row explicitly marks head width and tibia length as inferred, not an observed measurement from a new specimen. The article fills missing traits with a phylogenetic maximum-likelihood procedure; across the full pool, 139 head widths and 146 tibia lengths were inferred. Body length, head width and tibia length are retained numerically in the row-level evidence snapshot. ## Organization of the recorded vocalization
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-4h7rn
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->

## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc6746222-4h7rn
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is Below 450. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness. Raven measurements use 90% energy bandwidth, 5% and 95% energy frequencies, and peak frequency for the dominant component; these are not interchangeable with absolute frequency extrema or the fundamental. The Methods print FFT1024, 50% overlap, hop256 samples and grid93.8 Hz; figure captions describe a different display setting. No raw-audio reanalysis or silent correction of those settings is claimed.
