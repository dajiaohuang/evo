## A character cited in the gutu comparison

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys nankunensis as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species.
