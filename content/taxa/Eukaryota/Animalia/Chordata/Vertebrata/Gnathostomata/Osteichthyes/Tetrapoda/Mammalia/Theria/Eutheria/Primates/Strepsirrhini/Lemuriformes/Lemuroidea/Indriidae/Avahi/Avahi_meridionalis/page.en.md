## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2006 cytogenetic and mitochondrial revision distinguished a southern eastern-forest lineage and described A. meridionalis from material associated with Sainte Luce and Andohahela. The authors said that its boundaries with A. peyrierasi and the Manombo population required further study. In 2007, a genus-level revision treated A. meridionalis as a full species while noting that broader comparative sampling was still needed.
