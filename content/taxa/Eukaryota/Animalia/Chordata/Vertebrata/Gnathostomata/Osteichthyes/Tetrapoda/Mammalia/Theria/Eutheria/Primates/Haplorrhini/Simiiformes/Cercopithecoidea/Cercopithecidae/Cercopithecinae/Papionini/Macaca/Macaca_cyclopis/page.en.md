## field ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The thesis abstract reports 128 troop observations over 73 field days and 762 hours; arboreal activity accounted for 83.3% of forest-stratum use records and ground activity 16.7%. Fruits made up 58.3% and leaves 33.6% of recorded food parts, with seasonal dietary change.
