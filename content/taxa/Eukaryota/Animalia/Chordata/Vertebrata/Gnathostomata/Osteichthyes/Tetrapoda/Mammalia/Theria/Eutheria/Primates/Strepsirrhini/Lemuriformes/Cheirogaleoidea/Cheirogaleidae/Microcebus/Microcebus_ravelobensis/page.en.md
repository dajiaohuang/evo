## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In Mariarano Classified Forest in northwestern Madagascar, researchers sampled four 1-km transects perpendicular to forest edges, totaling 2,424 trap-nights in 2017–2018 and capturing 226 golden-brown mouse lemurs. A model estimated a 340-m depth of edge influence, but the overall edge-versus-interior abundance difference was not statistically significant. The result is limited to one forest and sampling design; it does not establish a causal edge effect or species-wide pattern.
