# Tarbosaurus bataar


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Tarbosaurus bataar is accepted in the fixed PBDB dinosaur working roster, with database parent Tarbosaurus. This guide uses 27 eligible species-level occurrence records from 26 collections and 14 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts. In one record, the original identification or combination is connected to the database accepted name. Original strings are retained; recombination does not become a new independent discovery.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 3538-item dinosaur working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Dinosauria storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
