## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Manombo forest, a feeding study recorded 54 plant species in the diet, with ripe and unripe fruit making up more than two-thirds of reported items. The authors also described opportunistic feeding on a shelf fungus from invasive trees and on non-native Aframomum fruit in open, disturbed habitat. The observations are specific to this recovering forest and study period.
