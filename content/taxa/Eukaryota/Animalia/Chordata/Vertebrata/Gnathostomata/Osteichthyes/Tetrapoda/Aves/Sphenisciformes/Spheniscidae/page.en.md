# Spheniscidae


## Read coordination in a named colony

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The Neumayer-area emperor-penguin time-lapse recording provides a penguin group-behavior case with explicit location, date and method. Four hours of trajectories and observed movement waves show how local huddles resume motion while densely packed. They do not establish shared huddling patterns, thermal benefits or social hierarchies in all penguins. This behavioral route sits alongside existing morphology, life-history and phylogenetic studies with each sampling frame retained.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Count genomes, fossils and living catalogue entries separately

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Cole et al. (2022) combine 27 living and recently extinct lineage genomes with stratigraphic information from 47 fossil entries and morphological data. The 27 include species, subspecies and regional lineages; 47 describes fossil study entries. These cannot be added to the pinned 18 species pages as one accepted-species count. With fossils included, the authors estimate crown-penguin origin at about 14 million years ago, distinct from the older stem history.
<!-- /evo:text -->
