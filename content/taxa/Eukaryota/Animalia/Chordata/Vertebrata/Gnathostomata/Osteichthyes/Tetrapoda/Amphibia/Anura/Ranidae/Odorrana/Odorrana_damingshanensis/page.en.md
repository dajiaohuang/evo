## Rock, leaf and branch observations beside slow streams
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10835719-r2zn5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
At Damingshan in Guangxi, China, frogs were found beside slow rocky streams in evergreen forest around 1200 m. Most sat on rocks, while individual specimens were recorded on a leaf and a dry branch. The April ambient reading of 20 °C describes survey conditions, not thermal tolerance, and these perching records do not define exclusive microhabitats.

## Egg-bearing females and indoor amplexus
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10835719-r2zn5
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Both examined females carried creamy-yellow eggs without dark poles; no advertisement calls were heard during the survey. Amplexus was observed when males met females indoors. The authors proposed April–May breeding, which remains their inference. Indoor behaviour is kept distinct from a wild mating observation or a complete natural breeding season.
