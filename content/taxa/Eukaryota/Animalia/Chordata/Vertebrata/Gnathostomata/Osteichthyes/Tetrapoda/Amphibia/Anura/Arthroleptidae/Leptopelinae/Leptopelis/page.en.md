SI sums those three inputs for a species. It is not a genus score, extinction probability or an experimentally measured tolerance. A site's RABI sums all detected species SI values without dividing by richness. Doubled category weights encode the authors' prioritization choices rather than measured doubling of biological risk. Threat scores are historical study inputs; the combined VU/DD score cannot by itself distinguish Vulnerable from Data Deficient.
