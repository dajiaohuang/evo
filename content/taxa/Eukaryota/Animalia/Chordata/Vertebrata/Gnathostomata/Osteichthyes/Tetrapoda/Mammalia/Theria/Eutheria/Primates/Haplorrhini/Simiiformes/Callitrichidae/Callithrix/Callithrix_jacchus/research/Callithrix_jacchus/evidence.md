## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
In one captive Wisconsin colony, offspring of 24 experienced common-marmoset fathers classified as responsive to infant distress calls had higher survival past one month (p = 0.002; 62 offspring) and greater mean weights at weeks 28 and 30; this study-level association does not establish a species-wide causal effect or wild behavior.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The paper directly reports the comparison and outcomes for its captive colony, but father responsiveness was not randomly assigned and the sample does not represent wild populations or the full species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
A Callithrix mitogenomic study combining 49 newly sequenced marmoset mitogenomes with published sequences estimated that Callithrix jacchus and the Callithrix penicillata Caatinga clade were the most recently diverged sampled sister lineages, at about 0.51 Ma. This is a mitochondrial-lineage estimate under the study sampling and model, not a nuclear species-tree date or definitive species-origin date.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
