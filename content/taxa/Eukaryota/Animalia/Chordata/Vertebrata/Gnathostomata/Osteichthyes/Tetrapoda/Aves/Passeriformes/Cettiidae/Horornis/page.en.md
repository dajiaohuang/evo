# Horornis

## Read seven historical rows through older Cettia names

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This entry organizes seven guides under COL26.8 Horornis (62V9J) and Cettiidae (7Y5), retaining source family Scotocercidae separately. BirdLife/eBird names and respective Avibase IDs match in seven one-to-one links; BirdTree uses Cettia throughout. Six exact older combinations have catalogue synonyms. The read shards give Horeites brunnescens at brunnescens, which is not substituted as a confirmed Cettia synonym. Correspondence does not establish identical species concepts across versions. The genus record has twelve immediate children; seven guides do not complete genus coverage or natural history.
<!-- /evo:text -->

acanthizoides has four birds, two female and two male, four complete sets; parens two female birds and two complete sets, without male comparison. seebohmi has four, one female and three male, three complete; fortipes five, two female and three male, four complete; ruficapilla four, two female, one male and one unsexed, four complete; diphone five, no female, two male and three unsexed, four complete, without an all-male sample; brunnescens five, two female and three male, four complete. Complete counts are not uniform field denominators or individual and sex variation distributions. acanthizoides/brunnescens both record Dunning 5.32 g without establishing identical actual mass; compiled mass remains separate from morphology sampling.

## Retain migration differences within a shared food niche

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
All seven have Invertivore/Carnivore and Generalist. The 60% niche and 70% level thresholds define categories, not measured species diets; Generalist does not mean omnivory. acanthizoides/brunnescens have Shrubland, density 2 and migration 2; fortipes Forest, density 1 and migration 2. parens/seebohmi/ruficapilla have Forest, density 1 and migration 1; diphone Shrubland, density 1 and migration 1. Density describes vegetation rather than bird numbers. Partial migration does not specify minority long-distance, majority short-distance, nomadic or altitudinal movements; sedentary does not mean lifelong immobility. Seasonal routes, calls, breeding and current distributions remain unsupported.
