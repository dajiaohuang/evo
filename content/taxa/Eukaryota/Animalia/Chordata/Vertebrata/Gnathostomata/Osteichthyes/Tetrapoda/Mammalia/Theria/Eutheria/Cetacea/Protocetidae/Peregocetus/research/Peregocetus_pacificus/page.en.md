# Peregocetus pacificus

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Holotype MUSM 3580 is a partial skeleton from the middle Eocene Yumaque Member of coastal Peru; its sacral attachment, robust limbs and hoof-like phalanges document a quadrupedal whale with both aquatic and weight-bearing anatomical signals.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Carnivory is inferred from cetacean dentition; prey and feeding behaviour are not preserved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Marine depositional context is documented, while amphibious habitat use is inferred from the skeleton
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Quadrupedal swimming and terrestrial weight bearing are functional reconstructions; no gait or swimming performance was observed
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
Approximately 4 m long according to the reported skeletal dimensions
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Amphibious quadrupedal cetacean interpretation with route and behaviour unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Two fused sacrals, defined femoral head and hoof-like distal phalanges
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Functional hind limbs alongside aquatic cetacean adaptations
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
MUSM 3580 fixes an approximately 42.6 Ma Peruvian protocetid occurrence and preserves anatomy consistent with weight bearing and aquatic locomotion; walking, swimming and westward dispersal remain analytical interpretations, not direct ancestry.
