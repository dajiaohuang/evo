## Internal-temperature assumptions and canopy definitions remain bounded
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
A nine-animal/four-species repeated skin–cloacal comparison is used to assume that dorsal skin represents internal temperature; nonsignificant differences do not independently certify equivalence in every sampled species and environment. Six hourly ground loggers cover three elevations and two broad canopy categories, but are not positioned at every frog’s substrate. Forest canopy above sixty percent and open canopy up to forty percent exclude intermediate canopy conditions from those definitions. The pooled analyses adjust for body size without a phylogenetic model, so species composition and habitat choice limit claims about evolutionary causation.
