## The mountain name, type locality and transported fossils

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Bakonydraco combines the Bakony mountain name with Latin draco; galaczi honors András Galácz. The 2005 type locality is the Csehbánya Formation at Iharkút, western Hungary, assigned to the Santonian from palynological work cited in the paper. The authors describe fluvial floodplain deposits and bones transported by flowing water into fossil pockets. The database bracket of 85.7–83.6 Ma and one collection record below retain their source scope. Depositional labels do not describe the entire living habitat, and occurrence counts are not the paper’s fragment or individual counts.
