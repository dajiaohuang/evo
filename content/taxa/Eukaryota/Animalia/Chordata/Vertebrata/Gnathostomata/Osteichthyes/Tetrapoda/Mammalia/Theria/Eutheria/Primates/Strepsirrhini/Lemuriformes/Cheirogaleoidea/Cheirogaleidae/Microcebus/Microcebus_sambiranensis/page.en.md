## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In their 2000 revision, Rasoloarison and colleagues reported the species as then known only from the Manongarivo Special Reserve; referred specimens listed in the paper came from Bekolosy Forest at about 360 m. The phrase “known only” describes the paper's specimen and record boundary, not a verified present-day full range.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The revision compared Manongarivo specimens with samples from Kirindy/CFPF and Beza Mahafaly: the former had a longer palate and a more elongated rostral profile. The authors used these cranial and dental differences in discussing western species boundaries; they are sample-based comparisons, not a single field identification trait.
