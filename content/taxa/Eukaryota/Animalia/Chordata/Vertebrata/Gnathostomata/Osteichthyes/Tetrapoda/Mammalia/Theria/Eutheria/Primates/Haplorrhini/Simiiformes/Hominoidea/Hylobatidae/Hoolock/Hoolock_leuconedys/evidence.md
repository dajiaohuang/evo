## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
In a short 2012 post-release study at Mehao Wildlife Sanctuary, feeding observations of three Eastern Hoolock Gibbon family groups recorded fruits and flowers at the Sally Lake groups; the 20 Kilo group also had leaves and animal matter recorded. These observations describe only those groups during the preliminary survey and do not establish the species' usual or range-wide diet.
<!-- /evo:text -->
