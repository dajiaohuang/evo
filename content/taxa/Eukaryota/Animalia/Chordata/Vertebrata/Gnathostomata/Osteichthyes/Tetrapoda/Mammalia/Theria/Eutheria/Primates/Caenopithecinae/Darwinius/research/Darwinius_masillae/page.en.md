## One animal preserved on two slabs
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/0/text/en -->
The Messel specimen known as Ida is a part-and-counterpart fossil: PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 are the opposing surfaces of one juvenile skeleton, not two individuals. The skeleton preserves an unusually continuous body outline, limbs, hands, feet and tail. Reading the two slabs together helps separate the animal’s preserved anatomy from the gaps and distortions introduced during burial and preparation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## A juvenile with unusual soft-tissue clues
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/1/text/en -->
The authors described a soft-tissue outline and plant material in the gut region, alongside the skeleton’s teeth and limbs. Those remains make the fossil unusually informative, but its juvenile age matters: adult size and some adult proportions cannot be read directly from this individual. The gut contents support a limited dietary interpretation for this animal, not a complete menu for every member of the species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Completeness does not settle placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerSections/2/text/en -->
The original description documents that part of the counterplate contains fabricated preparation material. The fossil’s combination of features has been used in debates about adapiforms and anthropoid relationships, but one striking skeleton cannot by itself settle a branching history or make Darwinius a demonstrated human ancestor. Specimen completeness, preparation history and phylogenetic placement are separate questions.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / readerLimitations

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/readerLimitations/en -->
This guide follows one juvenile holotype and its original description. It does not provide a population-level account, a direct adult body-size estimate, or a settled phylogenetic position; preparation details and interpretations should be read with the specimen record.
