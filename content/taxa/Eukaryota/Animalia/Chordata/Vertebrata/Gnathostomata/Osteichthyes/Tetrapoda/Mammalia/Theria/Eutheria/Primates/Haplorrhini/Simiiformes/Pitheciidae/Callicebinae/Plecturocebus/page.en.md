## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This guide uses the fixed COL26.8 genus ID 6SDJ. Byrne et al. (2016) combined molecular, morphological and biogeographic evidence to propose three titi-monkey genera: Plecturocebus, Callicebus and Cheracebus. Later work continues to revise species limits within the genus. The genus name is a navigation anchor, not a claim that every species treatment is settled.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with 4K5Y5, which places Yasuní field records under an older name beside the 2025 renaming of the white-browed titi. Next read 4K5YG, comparing the 2002 type-based description with the 2025 museum-pelage interpretation. Finish with 782LM for a two-landscape behavior and diet comparison in Colombia. These pages represent nomenclatural history, taxonomic diagnosis and local behavioral research; they do not combine into a genus-wide range or life history.

## limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
None of the three species has a dossier. COL26.8 and MDD v2.4 differ on the species limits or name treatment of discolor and stephennashi; their pages retain the fixed IDs but are not counted at the first-round content standard. The ornatus page is supported by a two-group study. Evidence is limited to Yasuní, Purus River museum material or Colombian Llanos sites, and none of the pages has received external expert review.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a local reading entry anchored to the fixed COL26.8 genus ID, not a complete genus review or accepted-species checklist. The three taxa differ in study site, method and taxonomic concept; two names conflict across lists, so their local measurements and taxonomic treatments cannot be combined into genus-wide ecological conclusions. This guide has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
