## taxonomy_story

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the 2008 study, *T. cristatus* samples fell into two main mitochondrial clades: one included specimens from Sumatra, Borneo and the Natuna archipelago, while the other contained only Malay Peninsula specimens. The authors named the peninsula form *T. cristatus selangorensis* at subspecies rank; this page follows the COL26.8 accepted name *T. selangorensis*. Because the analysis used a 573-bp mitochondrial fragment, the rank difference is recorded rather than presented as resolved by genome-wide evidence.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The page distinguishes the subspecies name used in the 2008 paper from the COL26.8 accepted species identity; evidence is a short mitochondrial fragment, not a genome-wide result or every taxonomic treatment. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation; the page has not received external expert review. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
