# Ammospiza caudacuta

## One historical record, two genus names

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Pinned COL26.8 organizes this page as Ammospiza caudacuta (9G43T). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 6373 uses the same name, as does its one-to-one eBird 2021 crosswalk; the BirdTree crosswalk gives Ammodramus caudacutus, labelled 1BL to 1BT. Pinned COL’s older-combination record CTKZ points to accepted ID 9G43T. These associations support reading the historical row without verifying all taxonomic boundaries across dates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Beak, wing and tail in a small sample

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Morphology covers 10 measured birds (4 female, 6 male), of which 9 have complete measurements. Species averages are 9 mm from the nostril’s anterior edge to the beak tip, 21.2 mm tarsus, 56.8 mm unflattened wing and 47.5 mm tail. These are not individual size bounds. Hand-wing index is 18.9, defined through Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, rather than flight distance or speed. Dividing rounded species-average lengths cannot replace the reported index.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Mean body mass comes from a separate compilation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Body mass is 19.19 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning, defined as a species average incorporating female and male mass. It is not field weighing of the 10 morphology birds or an annual or geographic minimum–maximum range. Inference=NO means the biometric row was not inferred from a relative; mass still comes from published material, and Dunning’s original book and individual denominator were not read here.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Compiled habitat and food categories

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The row records Wetland habitat, an Invertivore niche, Carnivore trophic level and Generalist lifestyle, meaning no single dominant lifestyle. Habitat-density code 3 means open habitats, not three birds per square metre. These compiled categories provide neither seasonal diet percentages nor independent stomach-content observations.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## What the migration code means

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
Migration=3 classifies the species as having long-distance migration in the majority of its population. The code supplies no departure month, specific route or tracked individual, and does not require every bird to migrate. Hand-wing index cannot replace migration observations.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This page interprets historical species averages and coarse ecology in AVONET v7. Selected rows, field definitions and name crosswalks were read; individual raw measurements, Dunning’s original book, independent field behaviour and expert review were not. Morphology counts are not mass-literature sample sizes, and Inference=NO does not establish concept identity with COL26.8. Plumage identification, breeding, lifespan, current range and conservation status remain unsupplied; this is not a complete monograph.
