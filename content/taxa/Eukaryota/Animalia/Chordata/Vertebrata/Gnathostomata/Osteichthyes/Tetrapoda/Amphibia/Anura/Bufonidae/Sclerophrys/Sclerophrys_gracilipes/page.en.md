## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc5683629-4vq8l
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/5/text/en -->
For Sclerophrys gracilipes, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.

## Mitochondrial sampling and phylogenetic limits
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc4740822-4vq8l
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/10/text/en -->
The molecular comparison concatenates partial12S and16S mitochondrial regions and uses model-based maximum-likelihood and Bayesian inference. The focal sequences are reported as417bp and551bp, respectively; alignment lengths and raw data were not independently verified. The paper reports43 species across21 genera in Table3, with a36-species broad analysis and selected Asian subtrees. Its closest-to-focal Bufoides placement in the broad tree had relatively low support and changes in restricted sampling must retain their context. Two mitochondrial regions are not independent nuclear evidence of ancestry or reproductive isolation. The paper calls distances “uncorrected k2p”; that wording is retained as a source ambiguity, not silently interpreted as an uncorrected p-distance or a universal genus-delimitation threshold.
