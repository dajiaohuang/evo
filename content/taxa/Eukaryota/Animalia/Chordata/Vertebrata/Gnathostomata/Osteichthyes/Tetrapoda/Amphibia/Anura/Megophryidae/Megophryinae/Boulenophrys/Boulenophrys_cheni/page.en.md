## Jinggang type material and the cited Taoyuandong range
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-v5b7l
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The monograph examined type material comprising 15 adult males and three adult females. The male holotype was from Jingzhushan on Jinggang Mountain, Jiangxi, at 1210 m. Its distribution summary cited earlier records from Jinggang and neighboring Taoyuandong Nature Reserve in eastern Hunan at 1200–1530 m. Those numbers describe the examined type series and cited localities, not population abundance, a complete present range or physiological limits.

The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys cheni as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
