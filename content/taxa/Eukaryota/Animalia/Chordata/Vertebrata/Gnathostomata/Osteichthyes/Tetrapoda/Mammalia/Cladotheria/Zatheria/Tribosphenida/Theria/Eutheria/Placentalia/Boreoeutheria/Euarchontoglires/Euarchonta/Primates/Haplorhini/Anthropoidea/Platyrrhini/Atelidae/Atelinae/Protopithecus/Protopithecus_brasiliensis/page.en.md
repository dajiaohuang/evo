# Protopithecus brasiliensis


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Protopithecus brasiliensis is accepted in the fixed PBDB mammalia working roster, with database parent Protopithecus. This guide uses 2 eligible species-level occurrence records from 2 collections and 2 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 2.58–0 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is Lapa do Periperi, occurrence occ:802755 (2.58–0.0117 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Toca da Boa Vista, occurrence occ:661932 (0.129–0 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.
Some selected records have a bracket ending at 0 Ma. Here, zero is the boundary of a broad database age bracket, not evidence of present survival or an exact death or extinction date.

## Evidence limits

Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts.
