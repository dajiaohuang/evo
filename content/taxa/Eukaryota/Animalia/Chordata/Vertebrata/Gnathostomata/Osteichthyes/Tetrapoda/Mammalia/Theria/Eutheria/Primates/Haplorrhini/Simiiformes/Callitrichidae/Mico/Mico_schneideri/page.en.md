## taxonomy_and_distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Costa-Araújo and colleagues tested Juruena–Teles Pires marmosets with pelage morphology, phylogenomics and locality records. Their 2015–2018 programme included ten field expeditions and museum specimens and tissues. They described Mico schneideri as a distinct, fully supported lineage; sampled records were confined to the Juruena–Teles Pires interfluve, with no documented overlap with other Mico in that study. The holotype was an adult female collected on 2 April 2016 in an urban forest fragment at Paranaíta, on the left bank of the Teles Pires River. This is the paper's sampled distribution and species-delimitation result, not a complete current range census.
