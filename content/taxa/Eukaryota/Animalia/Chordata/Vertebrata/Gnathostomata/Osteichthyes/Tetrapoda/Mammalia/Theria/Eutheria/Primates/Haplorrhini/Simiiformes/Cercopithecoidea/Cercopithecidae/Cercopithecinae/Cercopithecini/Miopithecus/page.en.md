## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 places accepted pages 43L7Y (*Miopithecus ogouensis*) and 43L7Z (*M. talapoin*) under genus ID 5TJ3. Read the northern species through the historical Makokou field study and later multi-method surveys in Gabon; read the southern species through Uíge hunting and market studies in Angola. The evidence differs sharply in question and method, so this path compares research records rather than presenting a unified ecology of the genus.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The reading path places Gabon ecological observations beside Angola hunting and market records; these are not comparable population measures. The 1971 Gabon paper predates the current species name and describes taxonomy as unsettled. This is a research guide, not a genus-wide ecological synthesis. The Chinese display name is editorial. No dossier or external expert review was added. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
