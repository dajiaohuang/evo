# Paraceratherium

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
An Oligocene Asian paraceratheriid genus whose 26.5 Ma Linxia skull and vertebrae refine species relationships and a Tibetan-region dispersal hypothesis.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Large terrestrial herbivore; feeding height inferred from neck and body form
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Oligocene Asian woodland–grassland mosaics; locality dependent
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion
