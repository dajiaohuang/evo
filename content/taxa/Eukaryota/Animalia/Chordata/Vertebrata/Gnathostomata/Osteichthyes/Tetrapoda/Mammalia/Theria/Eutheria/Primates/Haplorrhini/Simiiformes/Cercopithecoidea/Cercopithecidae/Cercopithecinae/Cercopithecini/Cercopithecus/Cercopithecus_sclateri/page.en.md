## survey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
An occupancy-survey study in Nigeria's Niger Delta detected Sclater's monkeys in 13 of 58 plots, across 17 of 125 surveys. Detection varied strongly by observer, showing why raw encounter counts can mislead for a vigilant forest monkey. Modelled occupancy was highest in plots with 20–40% farmland and secondary dry forest, but the authors cautioned that detections were few and plot boundaries complicated density estimates. This is a study of survey methods and one forest–farm landscape, not a current population estimate for the species.
