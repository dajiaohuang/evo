## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A throat dewlap and rump stripe pattern help comparison with other zebras. Mountain habitats and climbing ability are relevant to its ecology; all zebras should not be assigned a single plains-herding pattern.
