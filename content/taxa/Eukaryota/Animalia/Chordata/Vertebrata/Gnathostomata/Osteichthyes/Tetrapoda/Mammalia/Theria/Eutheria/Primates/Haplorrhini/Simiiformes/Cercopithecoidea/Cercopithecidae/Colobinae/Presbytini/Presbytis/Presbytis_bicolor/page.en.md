## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Records compiled by Iqbal et al. place the Black-and-white Langur between the Indragiri and Batanghari rivers in Sumatra and add sites within that area. The paper assembled published and unpublished observations from January 2016 to April 2022; this is a record-collection window, not continuous regional monitoring.

## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study confirmed feeding on rubber-tree leaves in rubber-plantation settings and recorded mango leaves and other plants. The authors inferred that the langur can persist in some degraded forest, but observations at particular sites do not show that all plantations are suitable habitat.
