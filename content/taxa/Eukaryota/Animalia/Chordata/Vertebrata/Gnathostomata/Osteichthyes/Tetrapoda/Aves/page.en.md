## Which measurements can be compared

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Beak length, width and depth use specified anatomical landmarks; wing length runs from the carpal joint to the longest primary tip on an unflattened wing. Values are source species averages rather than individual extremes. Mass has separate literature or weighing provenance and cannot inherit morphological sample counts. Uninferred morphology does not imply that every ecological field is available.
<!-- /evo:text -->
