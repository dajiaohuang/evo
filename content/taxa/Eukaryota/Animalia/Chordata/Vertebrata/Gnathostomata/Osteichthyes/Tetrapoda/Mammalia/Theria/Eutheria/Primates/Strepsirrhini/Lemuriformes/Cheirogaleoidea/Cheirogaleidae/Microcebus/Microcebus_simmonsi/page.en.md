## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Louis and colleagues treated Simmons's mouse lemur as a species in their 2006 revision of eastern Madagascar. In reviewing the region, Schüßler et al. (2020) cited records from Zahamena National Park, Betampona Special Nature Reserve and Tampolo, including a record around 956 m at Zahamena. These are published locality records, not a complete current range map.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The MDD record lists holotype PBZT HDZ 117 from Betampona Special Reserve at about 956 m. The type locality identifies the specimen behind the name; it does not replace a survey of habitats or range limits inside or outside the reserve.

## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The current MDD Simmons's mouse lemur page notes that it includes Boraha mouse lemur; the pinned COL26.8 still accepts *M. boraha* (`42SBH`) and *M. simmonsi* (`42SC4`) separately. Both treatments are retained without claiming the taxonomic disagreement is resolved.
