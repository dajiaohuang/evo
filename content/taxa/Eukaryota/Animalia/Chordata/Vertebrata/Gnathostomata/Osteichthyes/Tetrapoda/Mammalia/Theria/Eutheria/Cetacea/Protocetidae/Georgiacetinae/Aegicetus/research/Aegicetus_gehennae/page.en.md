# Aegicetus gehennae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Holotype CGM 60584 combines a long posterior trunk, unfused sacrals, no weight-bearing sacroiliac joint and reduced limbs in a model of increasing tail propulsion.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Marine predation inferred from cetacean dentition; prey not directly preserved
<!-- /evo:text -->
