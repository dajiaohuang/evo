## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A parasitology study examined 251 fecal samples from 25 social groups across four Udzungwa forest blocks. It identified five helminth taxa, and at least one nematode was found in 181 samples. These measurements show what the study’s sampling and laboratory methods detected; they are not a complete health profile for every group or season.
