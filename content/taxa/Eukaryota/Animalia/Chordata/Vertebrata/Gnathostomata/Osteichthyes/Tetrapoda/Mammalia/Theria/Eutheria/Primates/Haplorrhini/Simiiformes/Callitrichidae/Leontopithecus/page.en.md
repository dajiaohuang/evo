## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This genus guide connects four COL26.8 species pages: the existing golden lion tamarin and golden-headed lion tamarin entries, plus the new black-faced and black lion tamarin research pages. The selected field studies span coastal Superagüi and mainland habitats in São Paulo and Paraná, Brazil.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The guide draws on four site-specific studies and existing profile pages; it is not a complete genus account. Sampling periods and methods are not comparable enough to infer shared ecology or current population size. The Chinese genus name is editorial; this guide has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
