## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A stocky body and upright mane without the domestic horse’s long forelock are characteristic. It feeds on plants, and conservation includes breeding and reintroduction. Relationships with early human-managed horses require ancient-genome evidence.
