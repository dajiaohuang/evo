## evolution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Comparisons of mitochondrial and nuclear markers and karyotypes from African crocodile samples and Egyptian mummies supported a cryptic lineage corresponding to the West African crocodile, Crocodylus suchus, within the Nile crocodile complex. Mummy haplotypes also indicate that two Crocodylus lineages occurred historically along the Nile. These are phylogenetic and historical-specimen findings, not a current range boundary or abundance estimate.
