## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The description estimated Nosy Be sportive lemurs at about 0.88 kg. The coat is light brownish-gray with a lighter reddish-brown upper back, a pale gray-white underside, and a dark stripe from the head to mid-back; the gray face has a mask-like appearance. The authors used these traits with mitochondrial evidence to distinguish Nosy Be samples from nearby northern forms.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Lokobe National Park on Nosy Be Island. The paper then knew the species from the Lokobe/Nosy Be area. Although it was compared with nearby L. milanoii, the authors called for further surveys to define the range. These island and reserve records are not a complete survey of current distribution or status.
