# Xanthonycticebus

## Choose a reading path by research question

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 genus ID BSTB7 connects two selected species routes: X. intermedius (BV3HZ) links historical lineage work with a 2025 local field survey in Guangxi; the existing X. pygmaeus page (BTCRT) describes a captive-welfare study of females moved from solitary housing to group enclosures. In proposing Xanthonycticebus, Nekaris and Nijman (2022) combined morphological, behavioral, karyotypic, and genetic evidence. The field survey and captive study concern different species, settings, and questions; they do not combine into a genus-level natural history or account of wild social systems.
