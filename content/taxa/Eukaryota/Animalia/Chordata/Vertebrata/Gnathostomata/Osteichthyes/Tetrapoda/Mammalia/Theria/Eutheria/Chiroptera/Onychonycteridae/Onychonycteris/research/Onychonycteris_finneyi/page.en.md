# Onychonycteris finneyi

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Nearly complete holotype ROM 55351A preserves elongated wings and a relatively small cochlea used to test the sequence of flight and echolocation specializations.
<!-- /evo:text -->
