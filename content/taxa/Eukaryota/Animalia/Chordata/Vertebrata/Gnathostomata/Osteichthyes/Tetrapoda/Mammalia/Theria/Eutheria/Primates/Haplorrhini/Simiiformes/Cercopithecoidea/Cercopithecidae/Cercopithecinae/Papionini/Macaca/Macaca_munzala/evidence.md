## evolution / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/evolution/claims/0/text -->
In the study's approximate Bayesian computation analysis, decline Scenario 2 was the best fit for the tentative Upper Subansiri population. The model estimated effective population size fell from approximately 50,600 macaques (95% HPD 19,600-68,300) to approximately 1,700 (95% HPD 476-4,090), about a 30-fold decline, around 3,500 years before present (95% HPD 520-13,800). This is a model-based historical effective-population estimate for one sampled population, not a census or a species-wide reconstruction; the study could not rule out unsampled migration and did not establish whether climate or human activity caused the inferred decline.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## evolution / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/evolution/claims/1/text -->
The mitochondrial-DNA Bayesian skyline plot for the tentative Upper Subansiri population indicated expansion after the Last Glacial Maximum, around 15,000 years before present. This is a model-inferred trajectory for one geographically distinct population, based on 10 successfully sequenced individuals from 12 Upper Subansiri trophy-skin samples; the study could not rule out effects of local migrants. It does not estimate census size or establish a species-wide history. The same plot's small apparent reduction 5,000–7,000 years ago may fall within the credibility interval and was not treated as conclusive evidence of a demographic shift.
<!-- /evo:text -->
