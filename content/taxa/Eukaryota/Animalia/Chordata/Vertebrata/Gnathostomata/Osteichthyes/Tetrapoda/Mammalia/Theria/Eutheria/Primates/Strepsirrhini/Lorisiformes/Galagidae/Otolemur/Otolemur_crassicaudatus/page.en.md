## seasonalFieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
At the Lajuma Research Centre in the Soutpansberg Mountains, free-ranging galagos were sampled across June 2017–June 2018. Light traps captured 3,820 insects over 185 nights; catches were lowest in winter and highest in summer. Researchers observed gum feeding from Vachellia karroo. In the same site-specific sample, female faecal glucocorticoid metabolite levels were higher in summer than winter and autumn, and were higher during lactation than in two comparison reproductive periods. Male levels did not differ significantly by season. These hormone measures are study indicators, not a direct measure of every animal’s experience or a species-wide seasonal rule.
<!-- /evo:text -->
