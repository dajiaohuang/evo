## Accepted name and placement
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis andinognomus Lehr & Coloma, 2008 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Frequent calls under a qualified identification
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mj9b
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The authors describe the local population as common in higher Abra de Zamora and among the most frequently encountered frogs with versicolor. Male calls were heard every fieldwork night regardless of weather and sometimes by day. These are local observations under an unresolved taxonomic assignment, not an independently verified density or a weather-independence experiment. The species is not one of the five newly analyzed call datasets.

## Orestes-subgroup placement does not erase the population problem
