# Turdoides


## Retain one spelling difference among matching names

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The twelve Turdoides historical rows link by the same name to eBird. Eleven BirdTree rows also match; reinwardtii is written reinwardii, with one fewer t. The read tu catalogue shard retains twelve accepted names and four other ending variants, without confirming exact reinwardii correspondence. A name-table crosswalk is not turned into a catalogue synonym or equivalence of species concepts across dates. Source family Leiotrichidae and catalogue Leiothrichidae remain separate.

Complete sets, sex counts and literature mass denominators remain distinct without inferred individual variance.

## Two niches, two trophic levels and three lifestyles

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
There are seven Invertivore and five Omnivore niches, with six Carnivore and six Omnivore trophic levels. [Turdoides plebejus](Turdoides_plebejus/page.en.md) pairs Invertivore niche with Omnivore level without harmonizing them; the other six Invertivore rows have Carnivore level. Lifestyles comprise seven Generalist, three Terrestrial and two Insessorial. Wetland at [Turdoides hartlaubii](Turdoides_hartlaubii/page.en.md) retains Terrestrial lifestyle, whose definition may include wading with the body above water; it does not establish that this record actually wades, swims or dives. Other habitats comprise seven Shrubland and four Woodland; all twelve have density 2 and migration 1. Categories supply no cooperative breeding, division of foraging tasks or current range.
