## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Start with A. fusciceps to see how interviews, playback and visual confirmation documented a local population beyond the then-known range. Then read A. hybridus for feeding observations from Las Quinchas and a separate contact-network study at Hacienda San Juan de Carare. Compare study units and methods; do not combine the results into one genus-wide range, diet or population estimate.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This guide connects three local studies and is not a complete genus account. The records differ in locality, subspecific wording, period, sampling unit and outcome; no genus-wide ecological or abundance conclusion is inferred. The Chinese genus name is editorial; this guide has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
