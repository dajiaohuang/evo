## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Paddock, Bruford and McCabe used the distinctive “whoop-gobble” call to estimate abundance in the two known Udzungwa forest populations. Across 28 surveys, their model estimated 3,167 individuals (95% CI 2,181–4,596): 1,712 in Mwanihana and 1,455 in Uzungwa Scarp. Estimated group density was lower at Uzungwa Scarp. These are survey-era estimates with broad confidence intervals, not a current count.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The abundance estimates come from two forest blocks and a 2020 study. They do not establish a later population trend or a continuous distribution between the blocks. The page has no external expert review; the Chinese common name is editorial. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
