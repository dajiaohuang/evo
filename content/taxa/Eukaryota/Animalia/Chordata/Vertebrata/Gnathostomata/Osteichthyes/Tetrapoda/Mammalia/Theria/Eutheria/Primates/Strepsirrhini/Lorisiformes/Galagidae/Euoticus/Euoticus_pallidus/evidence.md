## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/scope/en -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/scope/zh -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope
