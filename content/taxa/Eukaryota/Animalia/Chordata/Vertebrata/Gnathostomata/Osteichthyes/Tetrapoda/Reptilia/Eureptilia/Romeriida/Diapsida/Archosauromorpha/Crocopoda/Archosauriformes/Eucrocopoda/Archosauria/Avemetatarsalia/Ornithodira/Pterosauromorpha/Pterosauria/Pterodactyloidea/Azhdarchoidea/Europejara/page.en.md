# Europejara

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route combines occurrence guides and an original description, without a complete descendant catalogue or shared ecology. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. No external expert review has occurred. All four scientific main sections and textual captions of the 2012 paper were read here. Images, specimens, the supplemental matrix and all later research have not been independently inspected or assembled. Wingspan, upper crest, diet and geographic origin retain their reconstruction or hypothesis conditions; expert review and reader-facing delivery remain unfinished.
