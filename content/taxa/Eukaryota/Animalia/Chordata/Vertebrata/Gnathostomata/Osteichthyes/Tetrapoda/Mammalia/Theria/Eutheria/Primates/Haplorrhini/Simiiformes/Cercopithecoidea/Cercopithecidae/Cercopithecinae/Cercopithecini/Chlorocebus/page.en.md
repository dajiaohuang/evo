## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This reading path follows all seven Chlorocebus species IDs in the fixed COL26.8 primate roster: six new study guides and the existing C. sabaeus page. Start with Bale monkeys' dietary comparison across continuous forest and fragments, then contrast the local feeding studies of grivets and Tantalus monkeys. Continue with the three-troop play study of vervets, the Liuwa–Mussuma camera-trap record for Malbrouck monkeys, and the Dryas monkey camera survey and taxonomy note. Close with the existing green-monkey occurrence guide from Guinea-Bissau. These studies differ in dates, methods and evidence types; the route is not a genus-wide ecological synthesis or a taxonomic revision.
