## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2024 Bintan study used the subspecies name P. siamensis rhionis. The authors surveyed mangroves, lowland secondary forest and mixed forest for four days and 12 hours in the island's northern tourism area in 2022, adding observations from 2021–2022. Records clustered in the better-surveyed northwest; this was not an island-wide census.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors compiled eight food plants from field observations and resident reports and estimated groups of about 4–8 individuals; mangrove records included feeding on Xylocarpus and Rhizophora. A separate 2020 study used only one Sumatran faecal sample labelled P. siamensis cf. cana; its mitochondrial result should not be transferred to Bintan rhionis.
