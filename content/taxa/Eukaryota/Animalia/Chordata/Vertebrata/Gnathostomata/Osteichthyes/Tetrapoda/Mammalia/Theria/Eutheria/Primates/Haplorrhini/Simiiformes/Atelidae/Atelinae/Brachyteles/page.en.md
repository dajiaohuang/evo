## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This COL26.8 genus guide connects the Southern Muriqui (B. arachnoides) and Northern Muriqui (B. hypoxanthus) pages. Its examples come from different Brazilian Atlantic Forest sites: a single-group feeding study in a semideciduous fragment and an individual-based demographic series from a protected forest.

## readingPath

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Read the southern muriqui page for the local composition and seasonality of feeding records. Then read the northern muriqui page for long-term population change and the way an abrupt demographic shift changed model projections. Compare their sampling unit, timescale and outcome rather than combining the results into one genus profile.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This is a reading path, not a complete genus account. The studies differ in site, period, sampling unit and question; they do not establish genus-wide diet, demography or abundance. The Chinese genus name is editorial; this guide has not been externally expert-reviewed. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
