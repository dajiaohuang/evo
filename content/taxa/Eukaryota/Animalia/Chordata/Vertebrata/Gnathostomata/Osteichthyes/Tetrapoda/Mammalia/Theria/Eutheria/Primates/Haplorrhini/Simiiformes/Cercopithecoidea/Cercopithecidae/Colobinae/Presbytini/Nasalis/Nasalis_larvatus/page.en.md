## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
In the Lower Kinabatangan Wildlife Sanctuary, Sabah, Malaysia, researchers combined boat-based feeding observations with trnL DNA metabarcoding of 155 fecal samples. They recorded at least 89 consumed plant taxa from 76 genera and 45 families. Sampling covered May–August 2015, January–June 2016 and November 2016–March 2017. These records describe sampled groups, sites and periods, not the complete diet of proboscis monkeys.
