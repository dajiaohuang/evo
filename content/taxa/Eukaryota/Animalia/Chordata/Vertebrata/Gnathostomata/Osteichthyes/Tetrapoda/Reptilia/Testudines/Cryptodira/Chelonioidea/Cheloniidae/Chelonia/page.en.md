# Chelonia

## The pinned genus and its species

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/topic -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Chelonia is the accepted genus COL 3MPC within Cheloniidae in the pinned COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026. Traversing every descendant in that release gives one accepted species, Chelonia mydas (COL TVGD). This is the complete accepted-species reading index for this dated genus concept. It is not a statement about every historical name or a current global taxonomic consensus; the snapshot has no extant flag.

## Two ways into Chelonia mydas

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/topic -->
overview
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Begin with the Chelonia mydas page's Rose Atoll nesting surveys: the result is a minimum count of confirmed nesting females, and its meaning depends on survey effort. Then read the Little Cayman seagrass study, which measured ecosystem metabolism using simulated clipping and a local natural-grazing comparison. These studies describe different places, periods and units of observation. Reading them together shows what a source can establish about nesting or ecological effects while keeping each conclusion within its own sample.
