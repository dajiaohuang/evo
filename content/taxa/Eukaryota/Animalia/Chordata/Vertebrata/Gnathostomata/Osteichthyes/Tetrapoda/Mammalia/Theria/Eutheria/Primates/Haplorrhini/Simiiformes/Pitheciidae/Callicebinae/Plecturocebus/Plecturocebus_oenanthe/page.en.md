## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2024 Mammalian Species account restricts this titi to Peru's northern San Martín region, chiefly the Alto Mayo and Huallaga valleys, usually at about 200–1,000 m elevation. It also notes local records at higher elevations and on both sides of rivers; mapped rivers and records are not a gap-free survey of the entire range.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The account describes family groups usually made up of a pair and their offspring. Fruit is the main food, with leaves, flowers and invertebrates also included. Group-size and activity observations come from local studies in differing settings and should not be collapsed into a single species-wide constant.

## taxonomy
