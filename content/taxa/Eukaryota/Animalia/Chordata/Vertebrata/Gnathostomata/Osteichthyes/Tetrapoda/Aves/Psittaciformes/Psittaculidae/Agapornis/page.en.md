# Agapornis

## Eight same-name links retain different family names

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Eight selected Agapornis historical rows have same-name links to eBird and BirdTree. Source Psittacidae and catalogue Psittaculidae remain separate. The read COL ag shard contains eight accepted names and four catalogue synonyms with different endings, while these crosswalks actually use accepted-name spellings. The four synonyms are not presented as older crosswalk combinations. Eight rows are not a complete accepted-descendant denominator.

## Separate generalist lifestyle from feeding niche

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
All eight have Herbivore level, defined by at least 70% of resources from plants; swindernianus/canus have Omnivore niche and the other six have Granivore niche, defined by at least 60% from seeds or nuts. Lifestyle comprises three Generalist (swindernianus, lilianae, taranta), three Terrestrial (personatus, pullarius, roseicollis) and two Insessorial (canus, fischeri). Generalist does not mean Omnivore, and beak form does not establish a particular diet or seed-processing ability. Habitats comprise three Forest, three Woodland and two Shrubland; one density code is 1, five are 2 and pullarius/fischeri have code 3; all eight have migration 1. Without underlying diet literature fields are neither harmonized nor given a causal explanation; social groups, pair bonds, breeding and current maps are not supplied.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route uses eight selected historical AVONET v7 rows, same-name eBird and BirdTree links, relevant definitions and the read COL ag shards. The two same-name crosswalks and catalogue accepted names supply name correspondence without proving identical historical and current species boundaries. Morphology birds, complete sets and Dunning compiled mass retain separate denominators. Original specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Granivore/Omnivore niche and Herbivore level are not interchangeable. No measured diet, prey list, capture behavior, plumage, calls, breeding, current range or migration route is supplied. Source Psittacidae and catalogue Psittaculidae, and Generalist lifestyle and Omnivore diet, remain separate. Complete natural history and external expert review are not counted.
