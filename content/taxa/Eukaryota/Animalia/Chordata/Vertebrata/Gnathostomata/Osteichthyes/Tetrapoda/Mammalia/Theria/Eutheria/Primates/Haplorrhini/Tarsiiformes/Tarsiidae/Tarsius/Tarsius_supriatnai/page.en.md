## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2017 description named the Gorontalo form Tarsius supriatnai; the authors used duet-call form and provenance to distinguish northern Sulawesi taxa. Bumbulan in Gorontalo is the type locality cited in the species description.

## habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A later Popayato-Paguat study compared nest trees in agricultural areas and secondary forest. Bamboo species were frequent nest trees in agricultural plots, while Schizostachyum lima and Calamus zollingeri were commonly used in the sampled secondary forest.

## site_fidelity

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The authors revisited nest trees first recorded in 2018 and reported continued use of 42.9% in their site-fidelity survey. This percentage describes that local follow-up sample; it is not a species-wide fidelity rate.
