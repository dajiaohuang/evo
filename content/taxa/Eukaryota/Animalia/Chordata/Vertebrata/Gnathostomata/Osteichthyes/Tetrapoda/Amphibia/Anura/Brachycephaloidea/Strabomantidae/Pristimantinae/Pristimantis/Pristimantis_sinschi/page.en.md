## Three night-time leaf observations
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7686219-sch4n
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
All three reported frogs were found at night on vegetation 0.8–2.5 m above ground, including a tree-fern leaf for the holotype. The type site had primary montane forest with ferns and abundant epiphytes. This small collection sample does not establish exclusive fern use or every daily activity period.
