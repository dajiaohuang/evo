## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A GPS study in southwestern Saudi Arabia compared minimum daily path lengths of male hamadryas baboon troop representatives in natural, semi-natural and urban settings. Ten adult males were collared; nine were successfully tracked, yielding 13,962 locations over an average of 256 days. Path lengths differed statistically among the three categories. The result describes the tracked males and does not prove a causal habitat effect or species-wide movement pattern.
