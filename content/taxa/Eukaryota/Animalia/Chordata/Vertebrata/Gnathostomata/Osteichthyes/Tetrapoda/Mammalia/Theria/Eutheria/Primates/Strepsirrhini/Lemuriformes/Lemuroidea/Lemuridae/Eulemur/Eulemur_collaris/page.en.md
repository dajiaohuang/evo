## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A study compared collared lemurs in Sainte Luce, a more intact littoral forest, with groups in the more degraded Mandena fragment. Fruit made up about 70% of recorded feeding time at the two sites during the study periods, but group-size medians were larger at Sainte Luce than at Mandena. The comparison shows local feeding flexibility under different forest conditions; it is not a range-wide diet or population estimate.
