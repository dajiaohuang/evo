# Tamarinus pileatus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts *Tamarinus pileatus*. Peres’s Upper Urucu River study used the older combination *Saguinus mystax pileatus*. Lopes et al. (2023) later reassessed the group using field and genomic evidence and supported pileatus as a distinct lineage. Older work can provide site-specific ecological observations, but not every historical *S. mystax* record can be reassigned to this species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Ecological evidence

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Peres (1993) studied a stable mixed-species group of moustached and saddle-back tamarins in terra firme forest along the Upper Urucu River in Amazonas, Brazil. The study reported differences in feeding and foraging: moustached tamarins identified at the time as *S. m. pileatus* searched for mobile prey mainly in the forest midstorey. This is one group at one site, not a species-wide diet or habitat estimate.
<!-- /evo:text -->
