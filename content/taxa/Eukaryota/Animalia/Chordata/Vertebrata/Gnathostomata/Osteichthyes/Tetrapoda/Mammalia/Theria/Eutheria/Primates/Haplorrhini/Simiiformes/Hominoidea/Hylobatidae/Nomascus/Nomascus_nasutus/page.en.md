## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2021 survey of the transboundary forest block spanning Cao Vit Gibbon Species and Habitat Conservation Area in Vietnam and Bangliang Nature Reserve in China combined vocal fingerprinting, field observations and long-term monitoring records. It identified 11 family groups and estimated 74 Cao Vit gibbons. The authors attributed a 38% difference from an older estimate mainly to duplicate group counts and reported no evidence of decline. The estimate excludes an unknown number of solitary dispersers and does not cover the former range.
