## feeding-and-activity

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Pothigaiadi, a rural village near the eastern edge of the reported range, researchers followed one group during 2019–2020 for 96 full-day observations. They recorded 95 food-plant species from 29 families. Leaves made up 62.90% of the group's reported diet, and the plant foods changed across the dry season and two monsoon periods.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The account covers one group near the reported eastern range boundary during 2019–2020. It should not be generalized into a species-wide activity budget, and it is not externally expert-reviewed.
