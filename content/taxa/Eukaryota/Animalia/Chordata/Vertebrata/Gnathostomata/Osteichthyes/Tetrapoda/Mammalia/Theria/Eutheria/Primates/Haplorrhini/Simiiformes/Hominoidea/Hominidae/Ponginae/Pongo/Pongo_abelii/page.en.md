## lifeHistory

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
At Ketambe in Sumatra, Hardus and colleagues recorded three slow-loris eating events involving one adult female Sumatran orangutan and her dependent daughter. The events occurred in 2007–2008. The mother often rejected the offspring's requests for meat, while the offspring initiated sharing. These observations describe one mother–daughter pair, not a species-wide feeding or family pattern.
