## Shimentai type-locality records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9rk
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Male holotype SYS a002099 came from Shimentai Nature Reserve, Yingde, Guangdong, at approximately 357 m. Citing Qi and colleagues (2021), the monograph knew the species only from this type locality at 300–400 m. This is a dated account of known localities, not proof of absence elsewhere or a physiological elevation tolerance.

## Nine adult types and oral diagnostic characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc10083232-lx9rk
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
Four adult males and five adult females formed the examined type material. Diagnostic snout–vent lengths were 33.2–35.3 mm in males and 36.3–45.8 mm in females. The diagnosis described prominent vomerine ridges with teeth, smooth dorsal skin with small granules, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. Breeding males had fine, dense black nuptial spines on fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. The small sample does not establish population-level sexual size differences, and the oral characters do not identify a diet.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12466624-lx9rk
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/4/text/en -->
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys yingdeensis as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
