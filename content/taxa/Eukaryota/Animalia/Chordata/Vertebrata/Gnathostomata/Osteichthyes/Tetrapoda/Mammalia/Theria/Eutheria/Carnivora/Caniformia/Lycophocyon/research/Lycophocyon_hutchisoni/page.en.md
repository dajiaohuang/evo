# Lycophocyon hutchisoni

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Named craniodental and postcranial material from southern California was tested in morphology matrices; the broad analysis leaves nearby carnivoramorphan relationships unresolved, while a Paleogene-only subset recovers a caniform position outside crown Canoidea.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
