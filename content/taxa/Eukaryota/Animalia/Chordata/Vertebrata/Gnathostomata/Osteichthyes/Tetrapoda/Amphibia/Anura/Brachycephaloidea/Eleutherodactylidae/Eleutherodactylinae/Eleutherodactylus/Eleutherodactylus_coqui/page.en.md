## Repeat annotations are computational observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/19/sourceIds/0 -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/19/text/en -->
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Eleutherodactylus coqui was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/20/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc12822217-3996f
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/20/text/en -->
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.
