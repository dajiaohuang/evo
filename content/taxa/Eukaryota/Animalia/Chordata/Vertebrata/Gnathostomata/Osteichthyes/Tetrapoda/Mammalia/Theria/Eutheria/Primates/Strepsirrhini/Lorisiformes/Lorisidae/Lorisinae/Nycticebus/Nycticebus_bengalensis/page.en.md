## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The current MDD page uses the English name Bengal Slow Loris and summarizes a range from northeastern India through mainland Southeast Asia to southwestern China. Swapna and colleagues studied feeding only at Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary in Tripura, northeast India; one reserve does not represent the full range.

## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The study covered winter from December to February and summer in March and April, with 177 hours of observations. Feeding made up 22.3±2.2% of the activity budget, with no seasonal difference in that measure. Exudates accounted for 94.3% of winter feeding time; in summer they accounted for 67.3%, with nectar from one plant species contributing 22.3%. These seasonal samples at one reserve show the importance of exudates there, not a species-wide diet.
