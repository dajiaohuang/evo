## seasonalForaging

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Keuroghlian and Passos followed one group of five to seven tamarins at Caetetus for 83 observation days spanning wet and dry seasons. In that group's records, animal prey made up 11.2% of the diet in the dry season and 1.9% in the wet season; prey-foraging occupied 19.8% and 12.8% of activity time, respectively. The study used 15-minute scan samples.

## cameraTraps

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
A 2026 study placed 27 arboreal cameras in two forest remnants and accumulated 9,872 camera-days from November 2020 to December 2024. It recorded 129 independent black lion tamarin events and followed three focal groups; camera records documented an infant and a juvenile developing over time. The observed activity pattern had a late-morning peak and a smaller late-afternoon rise.

## limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
The foraging study followed one group for 83 days. The camera study monitored three groups and placed cameras around selected resources; feeding stations were fruit-baited during part of the study. Detection totals are therefore not population estimates, and neither design establishes species-wide seasonal diet or habitat use.
