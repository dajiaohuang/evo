## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/scope/en -->
Mitochondrial cytochrome-b and D-loop analysis, using mainly fecal samples from wild Peruvian tamarins; supports species-level hypotheses for several nigricollis-group lineages but does not alone settle genus-level naming.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/3/usage/scope/zh -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

## referenceBindings / usage / scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sources/referenceBindings/3/usage/scope/en -->
Combines genetic, osteological, pelage and vocal evidence in a Callitrichinae classification that treats Leontocebus as a subgenus of Saguinus, a genus-level treatment differing from pinned COL26.8.
<!-- /evo:text -->
