## phylogenetic placement

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The study placed the small-toothed sportive lemur and L. edwardsi in one major mitochondrial lineage. Together with cytogenetic results, this supported including L. microdon among the paper's eight species-level taxa. The study used 1,140 bp of cytochrome-b sequence, not genome-wide comparison.
