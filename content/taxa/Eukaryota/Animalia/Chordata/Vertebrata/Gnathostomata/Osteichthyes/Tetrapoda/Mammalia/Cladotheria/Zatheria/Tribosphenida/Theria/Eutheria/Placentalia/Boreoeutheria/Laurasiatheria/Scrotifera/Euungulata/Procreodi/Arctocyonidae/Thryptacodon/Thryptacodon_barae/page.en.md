# Thryptacodon barae


## Which fossil records this name represents

Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 56–48.07 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is SC-121, occurrence occ:171668 (56–51.1 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is MP-38, occurrence occ:286950 (56–48.07 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.
