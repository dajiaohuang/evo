## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 and MDD v2.5 list Novaes’s bald uacari as a species in Cacajao; Hershkovitz proposed the taxon in 1987. In its 2022 systematic treatment of bald uakaris, Silva and colleagues still label relevant sequence samples with the older combination C. calvus novaesi. Rank usage therefore differs across sources and dates. This page keeps the fixed COL26.8 identity and uses the molecular paper as taxonomic-history evidence; the available sources do not provide a field-ecology study independently attributable to this species.

## molecular sample attribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The 2022 paper's cytochrome-b dataset lists five sequences under the historical label C. calvus novaesi. The authors also question two earlier assignments: voucher INPA5241 is described as an immature white uakari from the upper Rio Tarauacá and treated as Cacajao sp., while UFPA-Ccn1 has no available skin or skull and was assigned from a Rio Juruá locality more than 350 km north of the then-known novaesi range. The count is therefore a paper-era taxonomic grouping, not five independently confirmed wild individuals or a census. The authors' broader analyses combine genetic datasets and museum pelage comparisons; they do not measure this taxon's field ecology.
