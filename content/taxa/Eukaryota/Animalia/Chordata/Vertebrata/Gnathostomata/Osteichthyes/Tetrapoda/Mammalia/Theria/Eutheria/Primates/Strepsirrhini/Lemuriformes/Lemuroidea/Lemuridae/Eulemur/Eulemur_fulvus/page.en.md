## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A one-year study followed three wild brown lemur groups in Ankarafantsika National Park and analyzed non-invasive fecal DNA from 24 individuals. The study reported routine dichromacy in Eulemur fulvus and modeled how lemurs with different visual phenotypes could distinguish consumed fruits under daylight, twilight and moonlight. This combines field observations with visual models; it is not a direct test of every individual’s perception.
