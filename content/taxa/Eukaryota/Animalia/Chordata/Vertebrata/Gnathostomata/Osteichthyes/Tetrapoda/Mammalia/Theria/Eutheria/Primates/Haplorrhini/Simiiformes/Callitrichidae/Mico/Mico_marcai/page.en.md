## distribution

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Silva and colleagues surveyed the Marmelos–Aripuanã interfluve during 2012–2015 and reported Mico marcai at 13 localities. Their record-based extent-of-occurrence estimate was 31,073 km². This describes that study's mapped evidence, not a complete present-day range survey.

## population_and_risk

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Distance sampling estimated density at 8.31 individuals/km²; extrapolation gave 258,218 animals (95% confidence interval 150,705–441,860). A business-as-usual scenario projected loss of about 10,000 km², or 33% of the modeled range, over three generations (about 18 years). The authors recommended Vulnerable at publication. These dated estimates and scenario are not a current census or status reassessment.
