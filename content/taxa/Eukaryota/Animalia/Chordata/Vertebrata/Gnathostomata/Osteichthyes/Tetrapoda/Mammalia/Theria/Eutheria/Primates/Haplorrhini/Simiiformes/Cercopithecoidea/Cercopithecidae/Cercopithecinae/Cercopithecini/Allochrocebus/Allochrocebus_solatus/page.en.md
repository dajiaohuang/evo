## distributionSurvey

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Gautier et al.'s Gabon survey combined 38 weeks of field work between 1985 and 1989 with interviews of local hunters and information from villagers. The authors discussed the range then known and its ecological context; this mix of evidence is not a standardized present-day occupancy census.

## rangeRevision

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Coad et al. compiled all field observations then known in 2010. Records since 1999 extended the known range south, east and west, leading the authors to conclude that the extent of occurrence almost certainly included three national parks rather than one. This describes that synthesis of records available at the time, not a complete survey after 2010.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## phylogeography

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/3/text/en -->
In Tosi's limited sex-chromosome marker analysis of representatives of the three lhoesti-group taxa, solatus sequences were distinct from the lhoesti–preussi grouping. This describes relationships in sampled sequences; the 2008 marker study is not presented as a current, complete taxonomic revision.
