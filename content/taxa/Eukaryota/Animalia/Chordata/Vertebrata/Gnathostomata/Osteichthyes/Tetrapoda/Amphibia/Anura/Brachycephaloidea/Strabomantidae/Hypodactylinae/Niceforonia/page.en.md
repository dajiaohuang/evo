## Field temperature routes preserve locality and measurement type
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The selected Niceforonia routes retain 1 species and 17 reported adult records from nocturnal sampling in Tolima, Colombia, during September–December 2018. Species pages separate each site/elevation and forest/open row, with body, substrate and air temperatures in degrees Celsius as mean ± standard deviation and observed range. Body temperature means dorsal skin at the encounter; nearby air is measured twenty centimetres above the frog. These are observed temperatures, not critical limits, preferred temperatures or a complete physiological niche for the genus. The study does not establish all-season exposure or independently marked unique individuals.

## Internal-temperature assumptions and canopy definitions remain bounded
