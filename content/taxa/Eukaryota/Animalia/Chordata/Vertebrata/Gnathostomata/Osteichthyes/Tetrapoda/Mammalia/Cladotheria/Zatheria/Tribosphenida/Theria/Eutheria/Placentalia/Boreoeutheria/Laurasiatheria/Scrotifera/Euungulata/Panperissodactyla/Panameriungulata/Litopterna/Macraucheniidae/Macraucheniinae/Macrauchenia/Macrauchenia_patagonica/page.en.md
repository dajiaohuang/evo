# Macrauchenia patagonica


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Macrauchenia patagonica is accepted in the fixed PBDB mammalia working roster, with database parent Macrauchenia. This guide uses 26 eligible species-level occurrence records from 26 collections and 20 reference identifiers. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts. In 24 records, the original identification or combination is connected to the database accepted name. Original strings are retained; recombination does not become a new independent discovery.

## Age bounds of these records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 56–0.0117 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is San Julian-Cabo Curioso (section 2), occurrence occ:1515950 (56–5.333 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Arroyo Napostá Grande (Miembro Arenoso Medio), occurrence occ:597490 (0.4–0.0117 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 13958-item mammalia working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Mammalia storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled;
