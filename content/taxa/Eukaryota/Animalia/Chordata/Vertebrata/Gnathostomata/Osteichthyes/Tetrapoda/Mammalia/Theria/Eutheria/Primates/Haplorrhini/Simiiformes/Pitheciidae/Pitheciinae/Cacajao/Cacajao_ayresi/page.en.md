## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Boubli and colleagues reassessed black uakaris using surveys and collecting along Rio Negro tributaries, museum-specimen morphology and molecular analyses, and proposed three species. Material from the Rio Aracá, a left-bank tributary, was described as Cacajao ayresi, separating it from the broader earlier treatment. The study also named the northern Pico da Neblina form C. hosomi and treated C. ouakary as a junior synonym of C. melanocephalus. This account presents the taxonomic evidence and its limits; the type basin is not a complete range map or current population estimate.
