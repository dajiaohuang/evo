# Hipparionini

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
The Old World hipparionin review places first dispersal at 11.4–11.0 Ma and the latest regional records around or below 1 Ma; those dates are distinct from the broader North American tribe-level history.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
Two systematic reviews cover complementary North American tribe-level and Old World genus-rank ranges. Medium confidence retains regional and taxonomic heterogeneity instead of treating one endpoint as global.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Hipparionini is treated as a tribe of Equinae; Old World diversity is resolved into multiple genus-rank clades rather than a single broad genus Hipparion.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The two systematic reviews directly support tribe context and the multi-genus Old World revision.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Hipparionini remained tridactyl and many lineages were hypsodont, while body size and foot proportions varied substantially across the radiation.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The foot-evolution review and Old World synthesis support the traits without treating them as uniform species diagnoses.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Old World hipparionins ranged from browse-dominated and mixed feeders to grazers, with ecology varying by lineage, body size, region and time.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The integrated review maps dietary preference and body mass by genus-rank lineage; medium confidence preserves heterogeneity.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The represented history distinguishes a North American tribe-level radiation from an Old World dispersal at 11.4–11.0 Ma and later regional Eurasian and African records.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
Complementary reviews support the regional scopes and prevent Old World genus dates from being applied to the entire tribe.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/5/statement -->
Hipparionini retains four distinct displays: a heterogeneous 17–0.6 Ma navigation composite, a 17–2 Ma North American review envelope, an 11.4–11 Ma Old World dispersal window, and a 1–0.6 Ma selected terminal regional window.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/5/confidenceRationale -->
Two systematic reviews directly support the separate regional markers, but their heterogeneous taxa, continents and purposes preclude one global lineage-duration interpretation.
<!-- /evo:text -->
