## morphology and field description

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The type-series description gave Hubbard's sportive lemur a mass of about 0.99 kg and a reddish-brown, gray, and white coat. A lighter reddish-blond collar surrounds the neck; the back lightens toward the hips, the underside is white, and the tail is uniformly reddish blond. These are the original authors' observations of the type series.

## known locality and limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The type locality is Zombitse National Park in Madagascar. The authors summarized the then-known region as north of the Onilahy River and south of the Mangoky River. They did not estimate population size, and the boundaries required additional survey. This guide does not turn those river references into a modern mapped range.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This guide uses the 2006 species description, a limited specimen series, and mitochondrial fragments; it does not provide a current population or conservation assessment. The COL26.8 accepted name is retained. The Chinese common name is editorial, and this guide is not externally expert-reviewed.
