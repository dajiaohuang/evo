## life_history

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 12.5-year wild study recorded Thomas's Langur life history: the mean interval after a surviving infant was about 26.8 months, falling to about 22 months when births after non-surviving infants were included; mortality was highest in the first year. These are study-period estimates, not fixed parameters for every current population.
