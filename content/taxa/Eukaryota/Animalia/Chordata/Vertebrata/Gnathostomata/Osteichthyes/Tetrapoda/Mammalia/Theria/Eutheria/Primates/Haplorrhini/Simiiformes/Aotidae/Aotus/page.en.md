## Single markers and a tentative phylogeny

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The authors regarded MT-CO2 as unreliable for identifying closely related species because of its short length, relatively few informative sites and low interspecific distances. Datasets differed in support and topology; the conclusions retained a tentative reconstruction and an alternative with collapsed nodes, while questioning grey-neck/red-neck groupings. This study does not establish a final phylogeny of every pinned COL26.8 owl-monkey concept.
