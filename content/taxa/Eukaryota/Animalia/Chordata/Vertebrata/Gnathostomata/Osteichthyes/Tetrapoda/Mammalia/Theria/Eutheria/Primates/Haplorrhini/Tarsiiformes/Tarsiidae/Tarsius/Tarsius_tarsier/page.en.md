## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 accepts Tarsius tarsier (Erxleben, 1777); the current MDD name is Selayar Tarsier, and Groves and Shekelle restrict the taxon to Selayar Island. Earlier literature sometimes applied T. tarsier or T. spectrum to broader Sulawesi populations, so names must be checked against study locality.

## field ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
During an exploratory survey on 3–22 July 2006, the authors separately described observations from Selayar. At Punagaan they found three nests near maize, coconut and banana plots; each was reported to contain 3–6 tarsiers, with 200–500 m between nests. At Labau, 2–3 animals were seen at bamboo and riverside Ficus roosts, and the authors noted animals playing among bamboo by the roost until about 10 a.m. They described the local habitat as secondary forest and forest gardens, with bamboo frequently used for roosting.

## evidence_scope

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The paper also includes mainland Patunuang, but this page uses only observations explicitly located on Selayar; pooled totals, mixed-site plant tallies and combined elevation summaries are not assigned to the current taxon. The food samples were not collected, prey were not identified, and local reports that tarsiers ate young maize were left unverified by the authors.

## catalogue-profile / limitations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
These are brief, exploratory observations from selected Selayar sites in 2006, not a representative range-wide survey, population estimate or trend. The paper also includes mainland Patunuang; only its explicitly Selayar-located results are used here. Diet and reported crop feeding remain unresolved. This page is not externally expert-reviewed.
