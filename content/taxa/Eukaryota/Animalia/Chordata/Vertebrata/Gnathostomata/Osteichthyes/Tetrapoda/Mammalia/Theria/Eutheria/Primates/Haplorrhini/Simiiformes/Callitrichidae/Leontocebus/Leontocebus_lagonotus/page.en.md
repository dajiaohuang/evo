# Leontocebus lagonotus

## Overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
COL26.8 lists this species as *Leontocebus lagonotus*. A 2011 mitochondrial study treated lagonotus as a distinct saddle-back lineage; the field material summarized here comes from selected Ecuadorian Amazon sites and does not define a complete current range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Research observations

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
De la Torre (2017) assembled one- to two-day surveys at 23 sites from 1989 to 2014 and followed groups at two of them: 30 sampling days at Selva Viva and 15 at Yanacocha, each within a 50-ha area and each with two recorded groups. Estimated densities were 30 and 26 individuals per km², but the difference was not significant and the samples do not represent the whole species.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Habitat context

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
Fieldwork in the Morona basin from 2019 to 2024 recorded family groups, with all direct observations on the river’s southeast bank; interviewees described both banks, but the opposite-bank reports were not field-confirmed. A separate two-month habituation study of two groups found changes in resting, foraging and space use associated with observer exposure, so the observation process itself matters when interpreting behavior.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
The COL26.8 accepted name and ID are retained. The MDD v2.5 source row uses Saguinus with Leontocebus as a subgenus, and the local cross-snapshot join is unmatched. Bank-side observations, interviews and local density estimates remain tied to their sites, years and methods, not generalized to a full range or abundance. This bilingual page.
