# Morganucodonta

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Morganucodon watsoni from Wales and M. oehleri from China anchor a Lower Jurassic skull comparison with both a squamosal–dentary contact and retained articular–quadrate joint; the anatomy is not a linear transition or direct ancestry claim.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Tooth classes are functionally differentiated, but direct diet is not observed
<!-- /evo:text -->
