## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Olivieri and colleagues sampled eight inter-river systems and 25 sites in northern and northwestern Madagascar; samples ranged from 2 to 49 individuals per site and species. Three mitochondrial fragments totaling 1,296 bp and morphometrics supported three new reddish lineages, including the Bongolava mouse lemur. This is a taxonomic result from the sampled sites, not a current island-wide census.

## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The original study placed this lineage in a northwestern sampling frame and compared river, mountain and watershed explanations for isolation. Inter-river boundaries reflect the paper's sites and model, not a complete present-day range map.

## morphology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The study combined pelage categories, morphometrics and mitochondrial relationships to distinguish reddish lineages; dorsal color alone is not an individual diagnosis. This account preserves the paper's taxonomic evidence without extending it to behavior or ecology.
