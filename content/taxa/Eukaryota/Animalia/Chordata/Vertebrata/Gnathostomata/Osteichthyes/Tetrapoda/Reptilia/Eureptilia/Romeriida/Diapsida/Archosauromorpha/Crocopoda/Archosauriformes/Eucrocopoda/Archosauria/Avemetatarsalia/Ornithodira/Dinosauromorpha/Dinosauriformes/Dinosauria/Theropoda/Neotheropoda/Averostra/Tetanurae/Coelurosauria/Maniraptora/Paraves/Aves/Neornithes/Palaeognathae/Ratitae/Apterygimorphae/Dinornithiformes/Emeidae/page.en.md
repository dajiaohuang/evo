## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
This route introduces occurrence records for species authored in this batch, not a complete descendant catalogue or a common ecological summary. Record counts are not comparable measurements of specimens, individuals, populations or sampling intensity; outer age bounds are not actual origin and extinction times. PBDB classification paths organize the material while remaining distinct from COL and other phylogenetic schemes. No external expert review has occurred.
New genome text uses Sackton et al. (2019), published main text, four figure captions and figure 1 pixels; the read sampling, marker extraction, species-tree methods, selected results and discussion passages of Cloutier et al. (2019); and ten NCBI WGS master records. Companion papers share material rather than independent population replicates. Other figure pixels, supplements, raw sequences and analysis code were not independently examined or rerun; the companion paper was not reviewed paragraph by paragraph in full. Candidate regulatory acceleration, open chromatin and a single-region chicken reporter assay are distinct evidence levels, not functional validation across all species. Complete biology and external expert review remain unfinished.
