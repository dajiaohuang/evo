## primaryStudyScopes / sectionEvidence / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/primaryStudyScopes/1/sectionEvidence/2/evidenceSummary/en -->
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Pipa carvalhoi was performed here.

## primaryStudyScopes / sectionEvidence / evidenceSummary

The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression.
