# Brontotheriidae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
An Eocene perissodactyl family revised at species level, with shared cranial and molar traits but substantial variation in size and cranial appendages.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Herbivorous; folivory supported for sampled teeth
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Terrestrial Eocene localities; family-wide habitat not generalized
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Mihlbachler’s monograph supplies the taxonomy, morphology and distribution. Dispersal direction and deeper placement remain inferred, and neither giant size nor horns define every species.
