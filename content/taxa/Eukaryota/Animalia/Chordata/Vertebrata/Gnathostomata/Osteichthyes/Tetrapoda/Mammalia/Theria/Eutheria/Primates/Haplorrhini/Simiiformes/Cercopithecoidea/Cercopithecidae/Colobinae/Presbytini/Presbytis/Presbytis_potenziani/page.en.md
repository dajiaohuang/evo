## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Fuentes studied ten groups in primary forest on North Pagai Island. The langurs used the upper canopy chiefly for feeding, movement and rest; daily travel ranged from about 100 m to more than 1 km, and reported home ranges were no larger than 40 ha. These are site-specific study results.

## feeding

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
The studied groups fed mainly on climbers and dipterocarp trees, taking mostly leaves but also fruit and other plant parts. The observations recorded feeding peaks in early morning and mid-afternoon. Ten groups on one island provide a concrete behavior case, not a fixed diet for all Mentawai populations.
