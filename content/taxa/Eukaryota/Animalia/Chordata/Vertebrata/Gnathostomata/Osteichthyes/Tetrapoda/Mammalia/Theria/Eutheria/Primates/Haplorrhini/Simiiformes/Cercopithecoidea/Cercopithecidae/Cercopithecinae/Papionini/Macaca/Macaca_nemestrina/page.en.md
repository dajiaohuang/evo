## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Camera-trap occupancy models from Pasoh Forest Reserve and the Belum–Temengor Forest Complex in Peninsular Malaysia linked clear-cutting at Pasoh with higher local-extinction probability. At Belum–Temengor, the study detected no clear occupancy difference between selectively logged and undisturbed areas. These results belong to two study landscapes and their models, not a general response to every form of forestry.
