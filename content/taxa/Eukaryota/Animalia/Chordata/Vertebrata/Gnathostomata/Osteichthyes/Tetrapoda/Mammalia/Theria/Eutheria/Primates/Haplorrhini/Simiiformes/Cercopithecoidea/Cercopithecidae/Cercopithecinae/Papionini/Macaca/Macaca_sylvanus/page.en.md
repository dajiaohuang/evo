## ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
Two macaque groups in Gouraya National Park, Algeria, were observed from April 2007 through March 2008. The study reported higher annual dietary plant-species diversity for the peri-urban Les Oliviers group (13.8) than for the non-urban Cap Carbon group (10.4). The comparison covers these two groups and one annual cycle; it does not show that urbanization caused the difference or represent the species' diet as a whole.
