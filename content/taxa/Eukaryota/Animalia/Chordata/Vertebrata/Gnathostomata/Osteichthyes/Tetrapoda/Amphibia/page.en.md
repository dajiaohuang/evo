Recorded metrics: CTmax, 2756 rows across 563 source concepts; Tpref, 679 rows across 134 source concepts; CTmin, 908 rows across 274 source concepts; LT50_cold, 9 rows across 3 source concepts; LT50_hot, 49 rows across 12 source concepts. CTmax and CTmin concern critical limits, LT50_hot and LT50_cold lethal-limit assays, and Tpref thermal preference. These metrics describe different experiments and cannot be merged into one tolerance range. Overlapping metric counts do not sum to distinct species or animals.
