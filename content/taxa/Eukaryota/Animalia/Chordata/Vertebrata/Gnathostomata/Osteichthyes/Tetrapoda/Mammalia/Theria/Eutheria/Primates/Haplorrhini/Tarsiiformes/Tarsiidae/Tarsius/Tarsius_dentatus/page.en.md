## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The COL26.8 accepted name is Tarsius dentatus. A widely cited 2005 habitat study used T. dianae; a later Sulawesi study treated dianae as a junior synonym of dentatus. The names are not interchangeable without that taxonomic context.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## field ecology

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
In the Lore Lindu region, researchers compared four plots spanning different human land uses, captured 71 tarsiers and radio-tracked 30. In that sample, more disturbed settings were associated with lower density and longer travel, while the smallest ranges occurred in slightly disturbed forest.
