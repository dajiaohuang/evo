## Reproductive function and ancestry remain hypotheses
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/18/text/en -->
Protein-reactive hand glands motivated hypotheses about chemical courtship signals, but the study did not identify a pheromone, characterize secretion composition, test female responses or demonstrate olfactory versus transdermal delivery. It did not establish an adhesive function or a causal effect on ovulation or oviposition in these genus members. Inferences of origins and losses use a fixed, pruned earlier tree and a limited ingroup sample; alternative optimizations remain possible. Type II–IV similarities also leave questions about developmental relationships and gland homology open. These interpretations should not be converted into experimentally verified genus-wide biology.
