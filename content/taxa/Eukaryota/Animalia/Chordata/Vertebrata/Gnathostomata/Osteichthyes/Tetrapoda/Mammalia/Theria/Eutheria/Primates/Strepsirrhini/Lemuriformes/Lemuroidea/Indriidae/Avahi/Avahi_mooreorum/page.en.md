## taxonomy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
The 2008 Masoala study used roughly 3,000 base pairs of mitochondrial sequence, alongside morphological and phenotypic characters, to diagnose Moore’s woolly lemur from material at Masoala National Park. Its authors said the taxon was then known only from the park and called for additional surveys to establish its northern limits. The type-series diagnosis is evidence about sampled animals, not a complete range description.

## population

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
A later distance-sampling study walked 11 transects repeatedly, covering 33 km across four Masoala forest types. It estimated A. mooreorum density at 121.21 individuals/km² and reported a significant forest-type effect on abundance. The estimate belongs to that peninsula study and its sampling design.
