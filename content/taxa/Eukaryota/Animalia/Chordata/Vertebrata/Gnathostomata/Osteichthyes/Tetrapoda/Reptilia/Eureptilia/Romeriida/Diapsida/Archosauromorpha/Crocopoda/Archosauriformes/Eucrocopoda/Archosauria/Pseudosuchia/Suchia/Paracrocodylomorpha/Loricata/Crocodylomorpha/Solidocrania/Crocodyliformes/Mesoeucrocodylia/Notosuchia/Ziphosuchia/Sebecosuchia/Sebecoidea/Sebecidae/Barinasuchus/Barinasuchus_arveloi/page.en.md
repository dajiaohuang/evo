# Barinasuchus arveloi


## Which fossil records this name represents

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->

This guide uses 2 eligible species-level occurrence records from 2 collections and 1 reference identifier. Occurrences, collections and references are different units, not specimen or individual counts.

This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 672-item crocodylomorph working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Crocodylomorpha storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. Database accepted names can connect older combinations; that database correspondence does not decide specimen identifications or all taxonomic disputes and is not merged with same-name COL concepts. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation modes have distinct meanings. They do not directly establish complete range, origin or extinction time, fixed species ecology, biological abundance or the condition of every specimen. Original descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet and behavior remain incompletely assembled; external domain expert review has not occurred.
