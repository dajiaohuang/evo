## distribution_habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
A 2021 roaming survey in the Cantung watershed recorded 72 white-fronted langurs at 31 locations, at roughly 5–85 m elevation. The locations included plantations, dryland agriculture, mixed gardens and mining landscapes. The study assessed suitability using six local habitat components; its findings apply to this watershed and survey design. Sightings and counts are not a species-wide range map or population estimate.
