# Microcebus mamiratra

## Distribution records

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
During wet-season nocturnal distance sampling on Nosy Be in northwestern Madagascar, researchers recorded 92 visual detections along 46.5 km of 15 transects in 2023. Mean density was estimated at 125.1 animals per km² (95% CI 65.3–239.5). Extrapolation to forest within the mapped island range produced an estimate of about 4,700 animals; it is a model-based island estimate, not a census or an estimate for the full species range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## Evidence limits

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/limitations/en -->
