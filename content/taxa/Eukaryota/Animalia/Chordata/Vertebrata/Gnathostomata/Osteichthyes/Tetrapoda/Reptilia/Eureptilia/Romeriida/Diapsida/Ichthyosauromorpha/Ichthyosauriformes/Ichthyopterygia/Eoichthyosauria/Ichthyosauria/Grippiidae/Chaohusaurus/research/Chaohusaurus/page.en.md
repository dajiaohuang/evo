# Chaohusaurus

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
An Early Triassic ichthyosauromorph genus represented by maternal specimen AGM I-1; delayed ossification, dental morphology, vertebral count and hypophalangeal flippers support the assignment, while species-level taxonomy remains revisable.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Diet is not directly preserved by maternal specimen AGM I-1 and remains unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Marine ichthyosauromorph from the Majiashan depositional setting; habitual habitat is not directly resolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Paddle-like flippers are preserved, but gait and swimming performance were not measured
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
AGM I-1 records an adult with embryos; a genus-wide body-size envelope is not inferred
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

Predatory guild is not directly observed in the maternal specimen

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/1 -->
Delayed ossification of carpals and tarsals relative to metacarpals, metatarsals and phalanges
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Hypophalangeal flippers and the dental and vertebral characters used for genus assignment
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Primary study Motani et al. documents AGM I-1 from south Majiashan Quarry in the Spathian Nanlinghu Formation and records two embryos plus a neonate. Embryo orientation and a terrestrial origin of viviparity are comparative interpretations, not directly observed behaviour or an ancestral state for all ichthyosaurs.
<!-- /evo:text -->
