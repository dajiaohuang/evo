## overview

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/0/text/en -->
This reading path now connects all twelve species IDs in the fixed COL26.8 Eulemur roster. Begin with E. collaris in two littoral-forest settings and the Ankarana comparison of E. coronatus and E. sanfordi; continue through E. macaco activity, E. fulvus visual ecology, E. rubriventer seed dispersal and the Manombo observations for E. cinereiceps. The Ankarafa count for E. flavifrons shows how survey boundaries shape a local estimate. Then compare E. albifrons before and after Cyclone Eliakim, the dated E. mongoz surveys and habitat model, seasonal group movement in E. rufifrons, and the historical E. fulvus rufus records now relevant to E. rufus. Dates, sites and taxonomic usages differ, so these studies do not form a genus-wide ecological estimate or complete range account.
