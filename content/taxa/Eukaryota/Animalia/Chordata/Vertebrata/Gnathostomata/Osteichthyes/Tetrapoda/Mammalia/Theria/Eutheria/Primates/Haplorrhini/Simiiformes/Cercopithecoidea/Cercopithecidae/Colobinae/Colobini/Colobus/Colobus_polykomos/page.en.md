## fieldStudy

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/1/text/en -->
Dasilva compared high forest with secondary succession 5–12 years after farm abandonment at Tiwai. Relative to the availability of these two habitats in the study, C. polykomos was among the primates selecting high forest; C. campbelli, C. petaurista, Cercocebus atys and Pan troglodytes more often used successional habitat.

## interpretation

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/2/text/en -->
The study addresses relative use of two forest types at Tiwai. It did not test every successional age, other regions, or whether the species ever enters regrowth, so it does not show that the species lives only in primary forest.
