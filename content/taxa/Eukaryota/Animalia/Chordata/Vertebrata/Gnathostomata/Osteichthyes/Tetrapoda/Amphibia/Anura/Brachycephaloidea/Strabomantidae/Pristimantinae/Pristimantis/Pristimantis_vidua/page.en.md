## Males revise the historical all-female hypothesis
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mjrc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/6/text/en -->
The original description used15females and motivated a possible all-female interpretation reflected in the name. The revision found males with vocal slits and a subgular sac. More females were encountered, but different perching and hiding behavior could bias detection. Female-biased encounters therefore do not certify a population sex ratio, and neither the name nor the original sample establishes parthenogenesis.

## Measured sizes and life-visible diagnostic characters
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mjrc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/7/text/en -->
Adult female SVL is17.9–26.8mm (mean22.4±2.35SD,n16) and male16.1–19.5mm (17.6±1.28,n11). The diagnosis includes finely tuberculate dorsal skin, areolate ventral skin, dorsolateral and discoidal folds, no tympanic membrane but a partly visible annulus, and slightly expanded rounded digit discs. The revision adds small ulnar/heel tubercles and a long inner-tarsal fold; several features are clearer in life. These are measured-sample and treatment statements, not universal sizes or independent inspection of figure pixels.

## Two-note clicking calls from three males
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mjrc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/8/text/en -->
Recordings from one Abra de Zamora male in2017 and two Madrigal males in2018 yielded two-note clicking calls. Table2 reports mean call duration0.022±0.004s over117calls, rate291.3±36.817calls/min across three males, and dominant frequency3302.2±124.359Hz with273measurements. Calls, frequency measurements and males are different sampling units; none of these counts is a population census. Long continuous calling leaves whole-series duration unknown. Original recordings and per-male supplementary data remain independently unreviewed.

## Four confirmed localities and unconfirmed Azuay records
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/sourceIds/0 -->
primary-pmc7482940-4mjrc
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-profile/sections/9/text/en -->
The paper reports DNA-associated presence at Cajanuma, Reserva Madrigal del Podocarpus, Abra de Zamora and Bosque Protector Washapamba, with encounters2750–3000m in montane cloud forest and subpáramo. Azuay presence remains unconfirmed and considered unlikely by the authors. Females commonly perched20cm–1.5m above ground; males were more hidden or called from basal moss. These observations do not establish absence outside surveyed sites.
