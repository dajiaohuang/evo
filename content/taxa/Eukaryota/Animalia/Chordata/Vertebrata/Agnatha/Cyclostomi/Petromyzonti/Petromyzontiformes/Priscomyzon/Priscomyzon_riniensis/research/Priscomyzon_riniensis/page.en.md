# Priscomyzon riniensis

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Famennian total-group lamprey known from one 4.2 cm body fossil with a preserved oral disc and branchial basket.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Undetermined; the oral disc does not by itself demonstrate parasitism
<!-- /evo:text -->
