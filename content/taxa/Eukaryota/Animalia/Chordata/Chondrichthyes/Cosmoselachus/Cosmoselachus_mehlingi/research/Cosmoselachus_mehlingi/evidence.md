# Cosmoselachus mehlingi

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Cosmoselachus mehlingi is a new symmoriiform chondrichthyan from the Late Mississippian, represented by the partial holotype AMNH FF 20509 and recovered in the cited analysis as a falcatid within Symmoriiformes.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The paper provides a formal diagnosis and named specimen, while the higher placement is a sampled phylogenetic result rather than a universal topology.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Cosmoselachus mehlingi is recorded from the Fayetteville Shale near Cove Creek in Searcy County, Arkansas, United States; this holotype locality does not establish a global distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The type locality and formation are directly tied to AMNH FF 20509, but one partial specimen cannot establish geographic absence, endemism or a species-wide distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Cosmoselachus preserves teeth, branchial arches and partial pectoral fins in a marine Fayetteville Shale setting, but prey, habitat preference, locomotor performance, body size and trophic guild remain unresolved from the partial holotype.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The paper supplies anatomical and depositional observations but does not directly observe behaviour or provide a complete body for ecological or performance estimates.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Cosmoselachus has small cladodont teeth in widely spaced families, scalloped Meckel cartilages, elongate fused pharyngeal rays forming an operculum and approximately fifteen to eighteen distal fin radials.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
The diagnosis and CT figures directly support the named dental, jaw, branchial and fin features, while CT resolution and partial preservation limit some anatomical identifications.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The displayed 326 Ma point is the Late Mississippian Fayetteville Shale holotype sample for Cosmoselachus mehlingi and is not a global species FAD, LAD, symmoriiform endpoint or complete range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
The source fixes the Late Mississippian formation and holotype, but one partial specimen supports only a locality- and sample-scoped time point.
