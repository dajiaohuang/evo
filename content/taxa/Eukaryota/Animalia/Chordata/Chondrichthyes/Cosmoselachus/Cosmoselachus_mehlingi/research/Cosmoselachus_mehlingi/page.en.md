# Cosmoselachus mehlingi

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Late Mississippian symmoriiform known from the partial holotype AMNH FF 20509, whose CT-based reconstruction reveals elongate fused branchial rays forming an operculate structure.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Undetermined; tooth arrangement is preserved but prey use is not observed
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The single partial holotype AMNH FF 20509 preserves lower jaws, a partial upper jaw and basicranium, branchial elements, pectoral cartilage, fins and teeth; CT and a phylogenetic analysis support a falcatid symmoriiform interpretation while preserving the limitations of one damaged specimen.
