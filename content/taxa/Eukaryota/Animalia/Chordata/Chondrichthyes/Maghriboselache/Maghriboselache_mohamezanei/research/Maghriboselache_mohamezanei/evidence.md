# Maghriboselache mohamezanei

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/statement -->
Maghriboselache mohamezanei is a new symmoriiform chondrichthyan represented by several partly articulated skeletons, including three-dimensional cranial specimens AA.MEM.DS.12 and PIMUZ A/I 5159; the cited matrix places it with Cladoselache in an early symmoriiform grouping, while alternative analyses can recover symmoriiforms differently.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/0/confidenceRationale -->
The named taxon and specimen-based diagnosis are directly described, but the paper reports alternative placements for symmoriiforms and the atlas preserves that topology sensitivity.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/statement -->
Maghriboselache mohamezanei is documented from the Thylacocephalan Layer at localities in the southern Maïder and Tafilalt regions of the eastern Anti-Atlas, Morocco; this represented sample does not establish a global distribution.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/1/confidenceRationale -->
The source lists named localities and specimen accessions directly; a finite Moroccan sample cannot establish absence, endemism or a worldwide range.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/statement -->
Maghriboselache preserves a marine depositional context, digestive-tract and liver traces, a high-aspect-ratio tail and large pectoral fins; PIMUZ A/I 5155 contains a more or less articulated small actinopterygian stomach content approximately 40 mm long, recording one feeding event, while broader diet, habitat preference, locomotor performance and trophic guild remain unresolved.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/2/confidenceRationale -->
The paper reports a more or less articulated small actinopterygian stomach content approximately 40 mm long in PIMUZ A/I 5155 alongside preserved soft-tissue and fin evidence; one feeding event cannot establish broader diet, habitat preference, locomotor performance or trophic guild.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/statement -->
Maghriboselache has a broad cartilage-enclosed snout with large, widely separated nasal capsules, a high-aspect-ratio lunate tail and strap-like pectoral-fin radials; the holotype neurocranium and referred three-dimensional material document these features separately.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/3/confidenceRationale -->
Figures and descriptions directly document the named cranial and fin features, but the material is distributed across specimens and some regions are compressed or missing.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / statement

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/statement -->
The displayed 369.5–369 Ma interval is the late early to early middle Famennian Thylacocephalan Layer sample for Maghriboselache mohamezanei and is not a global species FAD, LAD or crown-chondrichthyan origin.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## claims / confidenceRationale

<!-- evo:text /records/claims/4/confidenceRationale -->
The primary source fixes the Famennian layer and named localities, but the interval is explicitly a study-sample envelope rather than a complete stratigraphic range.
<!-- /evo:text -->
