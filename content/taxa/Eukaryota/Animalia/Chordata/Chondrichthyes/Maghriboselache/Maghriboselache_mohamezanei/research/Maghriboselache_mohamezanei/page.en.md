# Maghriboselache mohamezanei

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A Famennian symmoriiform known from several near-complete, partly articulated skeletons, including three-dimensionally preserved neurocrania in AA.MEM.DS.12 and PIMUZ A/I 5159 with a broad rostrum and widely separated nasal capsules.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
PIMUZ A/I 5155 contains a more or less articulated small actinopterygian stomach content approximately 40 mm long, recording one feeding event; broader diet, habitat preference, locomotor performance and trophic guild remain unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Several Moroccan specimens, including AA.MEM.DS.12 and PIMUZ A/I 5159, preserve articulated bodies and three-dimensional cranial anatomy. Published matrices place the new genus among early symmoriiforms, with alternative stem-holocephalan versus stem-elasmobranch interpretations retained.
