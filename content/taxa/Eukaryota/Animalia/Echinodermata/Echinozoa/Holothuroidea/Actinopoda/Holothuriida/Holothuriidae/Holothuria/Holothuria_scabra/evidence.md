## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
At the first recovery measurement after releases of hatchery-cultured juvenile Holothuria scabra into 100 m² seagrass sea pens near Kavieng, Papua New Guinea, overall survival was 93.5% at Limanak 1, 84.6% at Limanak 2, and 52.7% at Enuk; no juveniles were found at the fourth site, Ungakum. These site-level results combine the study's free-release, one-day cage, and seven-day cage treatments and describe this short ranching experiment only.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / textZh

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/textZh -->
在巴布亚新几内亚卡维恩附近的 100 平方米海草围栏中放流人工孵化幼参后进行的首次回收测量显示，Limanak 1、Limanak 2 和 Enuk 的总体存活率分别为 93.5%、84.6% 和 52.7%；在第四个地点 Ungakum 未发现幼参。这些地点级结果合并了研究中的直接放流、笼护 1 天和笼护 7 天处理，仅描述这项短期海参牧场试验。
