# Echinoidea

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The atlas separates Darriwilian total-group plates from a much younger modelled crown origin, so fossil occurrence and molecular divergence are not conflated.
<!-- /evo:text -->
