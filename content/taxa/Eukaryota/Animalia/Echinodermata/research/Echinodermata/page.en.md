# Echinodermata

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
A marine deuterostome phylum whose earliest robust atlas endpoint uses differentiated articulated faunas rather than isolated stereom or a contested Fortunian assignment.
<!-- /evo:text -->
