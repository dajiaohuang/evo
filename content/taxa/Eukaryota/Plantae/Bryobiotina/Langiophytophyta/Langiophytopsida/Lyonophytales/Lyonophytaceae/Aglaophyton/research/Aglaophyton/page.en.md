# Aglaophyton

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Aglaophyton major is represented here by Edwards's re-examination of new and historical Rhynie-chert material, which established the genus and reconstructed its axes and conducting strand without assigning it to a living higher group.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Photosynthetic land-plant identity; carbon acquisition was not measured in the fossils
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Rhynie Chert depositional context; the cited redescription does not establish a genus-wide habitat
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Sessile growth reconstructed from decumbent and upright axes
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

Short plant reconstructed from sampled axes; no genus-wide population range

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
The 1986 redescription directly supports the named genus, sampled anatomy and reconstruction. The separate Rhynie U-Pb study supplies only a locality-age window, not a global genus duration, direct ancestor or settled higher placement.
