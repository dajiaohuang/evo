# Horneophyton

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
Horneophyton lignieri is represented by a 24-section museum sample, with three historical thin sections selected for confocal imaging that revised the interpretation of its conducting tissues without making the fossil a direct ancestor.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Photosynthetic land-plant identity; carbon acquisition was not measured in the fossils
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Rhynie Chert sinter-surface association reported in the study context; ecological breadth is unresolved
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

Sessile growth; locomotor performance does not apply

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Confocal microscopy and three-dimensional modelling directly document cell-wall form in named NHMUK slides. Transfer-cell resemblance and transport function remain interpretations, while the independent locality-age window is not a global range or origin date.
