# Lycopodiophyta

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The profile uses Asteroxylon mackiei as a specimen-level exemplar of early lycopsid rooting architecture and does not project that anatomy onto every lycophyte.
<!-- /evo:text -->
