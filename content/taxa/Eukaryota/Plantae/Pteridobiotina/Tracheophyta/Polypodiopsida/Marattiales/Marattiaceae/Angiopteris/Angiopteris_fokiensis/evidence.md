## evolution / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/evolution/claims/0/text -->
A genomic SSR survey analyzed 96 young-leaf samples from ten wild Angiopteris fokiensis populations in Guangdong Province using 15 markers. In the reported Mantel test, genetic distance was not significantly associated with geographic distance (r = -0.1287, p = 0.4033). This is a result for the sampled populations and markers, not evidence that isolation by distance is absent throughout the species.
<!-- /evo:text -->
