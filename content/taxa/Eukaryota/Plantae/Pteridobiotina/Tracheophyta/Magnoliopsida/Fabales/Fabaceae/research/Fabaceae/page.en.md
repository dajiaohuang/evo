# Fabaceae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The 65.35 Ma Corral Bluffs fruit and leaflets provide a source-bounded family record, while a taxonomically comprehensive phylogeny revises living subfamily classification. Neither evidence stream fixes crown-Fabaceae age, subfamily placement of the fossil or an origin centre.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Photosynthetic primary-producer identity inferred from family membership; the Corral Bluffs fruit and leaflets do not directly recover a family-wide ecology
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Lyson and colleagues document the dated fossil material, Koenen and colleagues discuss its status as the oldest unequivocal legume fossil, and LPWG supplies the sampled living-family classification. The profile does not infer direct ancestry or continuous global occupancy.
