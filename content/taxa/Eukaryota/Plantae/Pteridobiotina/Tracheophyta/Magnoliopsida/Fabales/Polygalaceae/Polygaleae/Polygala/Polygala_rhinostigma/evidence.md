## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Annual herb, up to 0.2 m high; tufted, not spinescent. Stems spreading, many, slightly branched, leafy; crisped pubescent. Leaves sessile or subsessile, overlapping; blade elliptic to narrowly ovate, 10-15 x 4-8 mm, apex acute, base rounded, softly hairy. Flowers: in terminal racemes; wings obovate, 6.5-7.0 x 3.5-4.0 mm, apex rounded, pink; petals pale magenta-pink; Oct.-Feb.
<!-- /evo:text -->
