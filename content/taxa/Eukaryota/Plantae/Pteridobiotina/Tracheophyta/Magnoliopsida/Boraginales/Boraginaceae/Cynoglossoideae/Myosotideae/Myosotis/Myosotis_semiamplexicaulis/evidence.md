## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Stem erect, nearly simple, patently, densely and minutely hirsute; leaves oblong, hirsute-pilose on both surfaces, the lower attenuate into a petiole, the upper semi-amplexicaul; pedicels as long as the calyx; calyx 5-lobed beyond the middle, hirsute-canescent, a few of the hairs almost hooked; corolla-tube as long as the calyx.
<!-- /evo:text -->
