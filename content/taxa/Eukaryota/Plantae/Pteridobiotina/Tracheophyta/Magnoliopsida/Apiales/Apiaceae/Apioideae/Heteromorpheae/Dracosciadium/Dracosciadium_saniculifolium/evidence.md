## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb. Flowering stems up to 0.45 m high; erect, tufted. Leaves simple, radical, petiolate; blade palmate, 50-90 mm in diameter, margins 7-lobed, lobes sharply toothed; petioles ± 150 mm long. Flowers: involucre present; cream-coloured; Dec.-Feb. Fruit on erect or drooping peduncles, ovoid or shortly oblong, laterally flattened.
<!-- /evo:text -->
