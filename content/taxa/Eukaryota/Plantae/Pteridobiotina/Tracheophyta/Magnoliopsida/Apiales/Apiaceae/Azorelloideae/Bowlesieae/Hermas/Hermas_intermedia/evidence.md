## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Tufted, evergreen perennial to 60 cm. Leaves in basal clusters, long-petiolate, lamina ovate, 65-120 mm long, both surfaces grey-felted, margins crenulate, base truncate to cordate. Flowers crowded in compound umbels on woolly peduncles, maroon. Fruit laterally compressed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Hermas intermedia shares the large, adaxially felty leaves with H. gigantea and H. lanata. It can be distinguished from H. gigantea by the densely felty or lanate involucral bracts, involucellar bracts and penduncle, as well as having only the sepals maroon (entire umbels maroon in H. gigantea). It can be distinguished from H. lanata by the maroon sepals (cream in H. lanata), less rigid, adaxially short lanate leaves (rigid, long lanate, matted and shaggy in H. lanata) with usually truncate bases (prominently cordate in H. lanata).
<!-- /evo:text -->
