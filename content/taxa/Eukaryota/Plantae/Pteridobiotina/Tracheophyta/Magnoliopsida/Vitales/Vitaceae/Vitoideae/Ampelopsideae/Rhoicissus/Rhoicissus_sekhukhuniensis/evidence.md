## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Shrub up to 3 m high, occasionally branches scandent, densely hairy with reddish brown or silvery white hairs when young, glabrous with age. Tendrils present, leaf-opposed. Bark greyish white. Leaves 3(5)-foliolate, petiolate; lamina with midrib and principal lateral veins prominently raised, margins coarsely and bluntly toothed, densely fringed with hairs; terminal leaflet obovate, 70-85(-160) x 50-75(-130)mm, apex rounded, base cuneate, petiolule up to 10 mm long; lateral leaflets ± ovate 60-75(-150) x 45-60(-110) mm, apex rounded to acute, base unequally sub-cuneate to rounded, sessile or with short petiolules up to 5 mm long; petiole up to 50 mm long. Stipules early deciduous. Inflorescences leaf-opposed ± condensed thyrsoid cymes, bracteate. Flowers regular, bisexual, pedicellate subglobose in bud, densely hairy. Calyx cup-shaped, 2 mm long, 5-lobed; lobes broadly ovate, densely hairy. Petals 53 mm long. Stamens 5, curved inwards over disc; anthers ± 0.8 mm long. Fruit a subglobose berry, ± 16 x 13 mm, with four seeds, ripening through red to black. Seeds smooth with a conspicuous longitudinal dorsal furrow.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Rhoicissus can be divided into two main groups: (a) leaves simple, and (b) leaves 3- or occasionally 5-foliolate. Rhoicissus sekhukhuniensis belongs to the latter group. It is easily distinguished from other compound-leaved species by its relatively large leaflets with prominently sinuate margins. It resembles R. tomentosa in the dense reddish brown indumentum of young growth and the coarsely toothed leaflet margins, but differs in having compound instead of simple leaves. It also differs in being essentially a scandent shrub and not a climber like R. tomentosa.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
Occurs in mountainous areas among boulders on dry, warm slopes descending towards non-perennial streams. It is found in dense to open savanna (Mixed Bushveld) dominated by the tree Kirkia wilmsii Engl. Rain occurs mainly in summer, and the average annual rainfall varies between 300 and 500 mm. Plants grow in clayey soils derived from ultramafic rocks (mainly norite, anorthosite and pyroxenite) of the Eastern Bushveld Igneous Complex (Rustenburg Layered Suite).
<!-- /evo:text -->
