## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants mat-forming and pendent from cliff faces with stems to 500 mm long forming loose clusters. Roots fibrous. Branches 1.8-2 mm in diameter, soft, brittle, terete, at first reddish green becoming grey-green; internodes 12-15(-30)mm apart, surface glabrous, or with scattered hairs when young. Leaves fleshy, dorsiventrally compressed, ovate, narrow ovate to linear ovate, occasionally elliptic to linear oblanceolate, slightly cymbiform and with an obscure keel, 25-35 x 7-12 mm, spreading to slightly recurved; surface shiny green occasionally hairy when young becoming glabrescent, the surface smooth green and shiny; margin entire to ciliate reddish toward apex; apex acute beset with green to reddish mucro. Flowers axillary, solitary to 20-25 mm in diameter on pedicles 8-10 mm long; petals magenta, mauve or yellow, persistent and becoming black with age. Receptacle cup-shaped 3 mm deep and 10 mm in diameter bearing two outer ovate sepals 7-8 x 4 mm and three smaller obovoid 3 x 2.5 mm with membranous margins and pointed apices, bearing scattered hairs. Petals in 2 series, outer linear lanceolate to 15 x 1.3-1.5 mm, the inner shorter and narrower 5 x 0.2 mm. Stamens in three series to 3-5 mm long at first overtopping stigmas. Filaments white to light mauve, hairy, anthers 0.7-1 mm, yellowish, ovary elevated to about 2 mm, 5-lobed. Stigmas 5, lanceolate, 2 mm long papillate; nectaries dark green 2 x 0.3 mm. Capsule top-shaped, 8-10 mm in diameter, grey, old petals persistent and becoming blackish; covering membranes lacking, valve wings present, closing bodies absent. Seed pale brown, pear-shaped 0.5 x 0.4 mm in diameter, tuberculate.

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
The habitat of our new species consists of narrow, shady to sunny kloofs (mainly E. S. and W. aspects) with a moderate subtropical climate. The plants grow pendent from vertical quartzitic sandstone cliffs, 400-800 m in altitude, often on ledges or crevices. The average daily maximum of 24øC has been recorded. The average daily minimum temperature for the region is 16øC. Winters are cooler but frost absent. Rainfall occurs mainly during summer but also occasionally during winter, ranging between 1000-1250 mm per annum. The habitat consists mainly of Northern Afro- temperate Forest and Pondoland-Ugu Sandstone Coastal sourveld. Associated cremnophytes include; Bulbine natalensis, Crassula orbicularis, Gasteria croucheri, Plectranthus ernstii, Cotyledon orbiculata, Crassula perforata, C. perfoliata and Aloe arborescens. The geology consists mainly of quartzitic sandstone (light-coloured and smooth-textured), Natal Formation (Cape Supergroup).
