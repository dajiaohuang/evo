## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Glabrum, primo compactum, deinde ramos floriferos adscendentes vel fere erectos, 9 cm. longos vel ultra, emittens, ramis asperulis, ad 2 mm. diam., internodiis infra cymam 1.5-2.5 cm. longis; folia pro sectione obtusissima, lat. visa superne leviter, inferne haud, vel levissime, angustata, supra plana vel superne convexula, dorso rotundata, viridia, punctis inter papillas minutas sat magnis et leviter prominentibus, ad 2.5 cm., vel saepius ad 2 cm., longa, ad 4 mm. lata diametroque; cyma ad 5 cm. diam., plus minusve regulariter 1-3-ternati, floribus superioribus tardissime evolutis itaque capsula matura alabastrisque novellissimis in cyma eodem tempore; pedunculi 0.3-0.7 mm. longi; flores diurni, 1.5-2 cm. diam., receptaculum prope apicem constrictum ibique ad 3 mm. diam., apice 4 mm., 3 mm. longum; sepala 5, brevia, ad 3-5 mm. longa, haud vel levissime accrescentia; petala 2-3-seriata, sat densa, interiora pauca, obtusa vel acuta, superne leviter ampliata, nivea, ad 7 mm. longa, ad 1.5 mm. lata; staminodia diu adpressa, apice patentia, ad 5 mm. longa, inferne parce papillato-ciliata; filamenta ad 3 mm. longa, alba, antheris pollineque aureis; glandulae distantes; ovarium leviter elevatum, lobis inconspicuis rotundatis; stigmata 5, subulata, longe tenuissimeque acuminata, 2 mm. longa; capsula ad 5 mm. diam., lobis per 1.5 mm. elevatis.
