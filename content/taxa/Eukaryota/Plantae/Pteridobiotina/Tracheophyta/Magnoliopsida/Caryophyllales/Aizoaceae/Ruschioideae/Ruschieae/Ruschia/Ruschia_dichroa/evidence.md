## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Leaf blades longer than connate sheath. Plants prostrate, sprawling. Decumbent shrub, with short branches to 10 cm. Leaves trigonous, glaucous, minutely dotted, margins denticulate, 30-60 mm long. Flowers subsessile, red with white centre, 35-40 mm diam. Fruits 6-locular.
