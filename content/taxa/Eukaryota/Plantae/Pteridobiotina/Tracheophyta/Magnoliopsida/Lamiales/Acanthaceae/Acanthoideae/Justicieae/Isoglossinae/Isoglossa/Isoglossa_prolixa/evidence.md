## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Monocarpic herb or suffrutex, upright or sometimes scandent, 0.1-3 m tall; stems either bifariously hairy or with long (more than 3 cells), glandular and eglandular trichomes on all surfaces, glandular trichomes generally longer than eglandular trichomes. Leaves ovate, elliptic, (16-)20-59(-97) x (6-)10-28(-41) mm; dark upper and lighter lower surface with sparse, short, eglandular trichomes, acuminate, sometimes acute; base rounded, obtuse to cuneate; lateral veins 3 to 6, sometimes 7. Petiole (2-)2.1-22(-48) mm long with few, short, eglandular trichomes. Inflorescence terminal and axillary; flowers usually 1 at a node; terminal inflorescence (7-)14-43(-85) x (6-) 7.3-12(-15) mm; leaf below inflorescence ovate, (4-)5-14(-23) x (3-)3.3-7.6(-13) mm; leaf below terminal inflorescence (4-)5-14 (-23) x (3-)3.3-7.6(-13) mm; bracts with rare short and long eglandular trichomes and dense glandular trichomes with 5 or more stalk cells on the outer surface and rare short and dense long eglandular trichomes and sparse glandular trichomes with 5 or more stalk cells on the margins; bracts at base of inflorescence narrowly elliptic, ovate to lanceolate, often folding along their length, tip slightly recurved, (4.5-)5.5-8.7(-11) x (0.5-)0.8-3(-5) mm, acute to acuminate; bracts next to open flower narrowly elliptic to broadly lanceolate, often folding along half their length, (4-)4.7-7.3(-9.5) x (0.6-)0.7-2.4(-4.6) mm; apex slightly recurved; lateral inflorescence (7-)11-30(-47) x (4-)6.1-11(-14) mm; leaf below inflorescence ovate, (6-)6.6-10 (-12) x (2.3-)2.7-6.6(-11) mm; bracts at base of inflorescence narrowly elliptic, ovate or lanceolate, often folding along half their length, (4-)5.1-8.3(-10) x (0.5-)0.6-2(-3.5) mm, apex slightly recurved; bracts next to open flower lanceolate, (4-)5.3-9.6(-14) x (0.4-) 0.6-1.6(-2.5) mm; apex slightly recurved. Sepals linear, (4.5-)5.3-7.6(-9) x (0.4-)0.6-1.1(-1.5) mm at anthesis, (7-)7.9-9.8(-11) x (0.5-)0.7-1.2(-1.5) mm at fruiting, outer surface with rare short eglandular and sparse glandular trichomes with up to 4 or with 5 or more stalk cells, margins with rare short and dense fine eglandular trichomes and rare glandular trichomes with up to 4 or with 5 or more stalk cells. Corolla tube (3.2-)5.1-6.6(-7) x (2.2-)2.8-3.8(-4) mm; upper lip (4.5-)5.6-7.4(-8.5) x (4.5-)4.8-6.5(-7.5) mm; lower lip (4.5-)5.4-7.3(-8.5) x (4.5-)5.8-8.3(-8.5) mm; ridges on palate 5 to 8, usually 6 or 7. Pollen grains 36.9-37.3 µm diam. Capsule (7.1-) 8.1-11(-12.5) x (1.4-)2.2-3.6(-4) mm. Seeds (1.6-)1.8-2.9(-3.2) x (0.7-)0.8-2.3(-3.4) mm.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
I. prolixa has inflorescences in which there is often only one flower at each node. In addition, the internodes of the inflorescence may be as long as or slightly longer than the calyx. The margins of the bracts and calyx lobes have glandular trichomes with stalks of five or more cells and these distinguish I. prolixa from other species of Isoglossa, where the glandular hairs on the margins of the bracts and calyx lobes have stalks with four or less cells. The leaves of I. prolixa have a slight greyish bloom.
<!-- /evo:text -->
