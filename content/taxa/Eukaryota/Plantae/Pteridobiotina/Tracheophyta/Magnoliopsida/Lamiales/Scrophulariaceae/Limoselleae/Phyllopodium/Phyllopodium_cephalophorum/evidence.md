## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
P. cephalophorum can be recognized by its shaggy white indumentum, narrow toothed leaves and masses of small round heads corymbosely arranged, the bracts characteristically glabrous to puberulous at the tips and becoming shaggy in the lower half. It is often confused with P. capitatum. Phyllopodium cephalophorum is closely allied to P. phyllopodioides and in areas where they are sympatric they may be difficult to distinguish.
<!-- /evo:text -->
