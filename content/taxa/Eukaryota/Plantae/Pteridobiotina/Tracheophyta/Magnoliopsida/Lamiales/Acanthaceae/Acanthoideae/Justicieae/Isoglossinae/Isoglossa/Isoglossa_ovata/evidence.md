## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Spreading perennial herb in grassland, tending towards a suffrutex at forest margins; branches stout and rigid, stems either tomentose on all sides, or bifariously hairy with curved, eglandular hairs from the third or fourth internode of the inflorescence downward. Leaves ovate, (23-)30-52(-68) x (9-)16-33(-45) mm, acute or acuminate; base rounded; lateral veins 4 to 5, sometimes 6. Petiole (1-)2.3-6.7(-11) mm long. Inflorescence terminal and lateral, more or less interrupted below; terminal inflorescence (30-)44-88(-124) x (8-)9.6-18(-27) mm; leaf mm, acuminate; lateral inflorescence (12-)20-50(-89) x (4-)7-15(-20) mm; leaf below inflorescence ovate to linear-lanceolate, (10-)11-15(-19) x (0.8-)3-6(-7) mm, acute to acuminate; bract at base of inflorescence linear-lanceolate, (6-)8-13(-19) x (0.5-)2-4(-6.5) mm, acuminate; bracts next to open flower linear-lanceolate, (5.5-)8-13(-18) x (1-)2-4(-4.6) mm, acuminate. Flowers 2 at a node. Sepals linear, (8-)9.2-12.6(-19) x (0.3-)0.3-0.5(-0.8) mm at anthesis; (9.5-)11-15(-19) x (0.3-)0.3-1.3(-2) mm at fruiting, outer surface with rare short and sparse long eglandular trichomes and rare short glandular trichomes, margins with sparse short and long eglandular trichomes. Corolla tube (3-)5.1-7.6(-10) x (1.5-)2.5-3.8(-4.6) mm; upper lip bifid, (4.5-)6-8(-8.5) x (5.8-)6-7.2(-8) mm; lower lip trifid, (5.5-)6-7.7(-8.5) x (5.9-)6-7.6 (-8.5) mm; ridges on palate 6 or 7. Pollen grains 38-42 µm diam. Capsule (7.1-)8.1-11 (-12) x (2.8-)2.9-3.2(-3.4) mm. Seeds (2.2-)2.3-2.8 (-3) x (1 -)1.3-1.9 (-2) mm.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The rounded leaf bases together with prominent midveins and lateral veins and long (30-118 mm), mostly terminal inflorescences are characteristic of I. ovata. The bracts have only eglandular trichomes. All other species of Isoglossa are found on forest floors, along forest edges or in savanna whereas I. ovata is mainly found in grassland.
<!-- /evo:text -->
