## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Dense, rounded shrublet, ± 0.4-1.5 m tall, pubescent on young parts with minutely stellate or branched hairs. Leaves tufted, elliptic to narrowly spathulate, thick, velvety. Flowers in narrow, loose panicles, mauve, calyx 5-lobed, velvety outside.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Selago beaniana resembles S. singularis in its velvety pubescence with a strong component of dendroid hairs but is at once distinguished by its distinctly petiolate leaves with much less markedly revolute margins, bracts oblong, not elliptic, and plane (not concave) on the inner face, and corolla tube longer (c.3-3.4 mm versus 1.7-2.8 mm). Also, the panicles are laxer, but this is difficult to quantify.
<!-- /evo:text -->
