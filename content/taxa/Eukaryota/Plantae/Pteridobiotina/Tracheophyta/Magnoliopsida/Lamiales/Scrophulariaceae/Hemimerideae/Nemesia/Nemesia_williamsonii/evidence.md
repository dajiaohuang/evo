## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Annual, up to 280 mm tall. Leaves lanceolate-ovate to ovate, apex acute, base rounded to cuneate. Flowers in lax racemes, calyx lobes lanceolate, acute, sparsely glandular pilose, margins entire to toothed, corolla 10-16 x 10-15 mm, upper lip orange, lower lip orange with strongly convex yellow palate in lower half, spur projecting backwards, mostly 3.1-4.2 mm long, not strongly recurved apically. Capsule oblong, 4.5-13 x 3.6-7 mm, bilobed.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Nemesia williamsonii is easily recognized by its bright orange and yellow flowers. It can be distinguished from the similar and related N. maxii by its colour (white vs violet), the difference in orientation of the spur, and the shape of the bracts. In N. williamsonii. the spur projects straight backwards or back and then downwards distally. whereas in N. maxii the spur projects straight downwards or down and forwards distally. The bracts of N. williamsonii are lanceolate with a truncate base, whereas those of N. maxii are triangular or cordate.
<!-- /evo:text -->
