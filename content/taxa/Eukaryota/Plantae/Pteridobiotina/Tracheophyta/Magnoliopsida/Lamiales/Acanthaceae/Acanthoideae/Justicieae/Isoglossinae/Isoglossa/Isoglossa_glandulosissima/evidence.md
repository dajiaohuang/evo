## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The bracts of I. glandulosissima are narrowly elliptic and acute. They are as long as or longer than the calyx, and are covered with dense glandular trichomes that have three long cells and a terminal short cell in the stalks. The compact inflorescence with opposite flowers separates I. glandulosissima from I. prolixa which has a single flower at most inflorescence nodes and usually a markedly interrupted inflorescence. The bracts of I. delicatula are similar in length, but are narrower (1.3 ±0.9 mm rather than 3.2 ±0.7 mm wide and linear or lanceolate rather than narrowly elliptic) and the inflorescence is interrupted. I. woodii may have a dense inflorescence like I. glandulosissima, but it has ovate to obovate bracts with a markedly acuminate tip.
<!-- /evo:text -->
