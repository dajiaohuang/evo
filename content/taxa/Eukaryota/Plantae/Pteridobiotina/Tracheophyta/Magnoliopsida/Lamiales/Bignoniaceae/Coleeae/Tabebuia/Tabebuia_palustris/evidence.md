## referenceBindings / usage / originalSourceText

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/sources/referenceBindings/2/usage/originalSourceText -->
Shrub or small tree, often twisted, to 4 m tall and 5 cm d.b.h., the bark smooth, pale gray; wood whitish, soft; twigs terete, lepidote, the nodes without interpetiolar glandular fields or pseudostipules.
