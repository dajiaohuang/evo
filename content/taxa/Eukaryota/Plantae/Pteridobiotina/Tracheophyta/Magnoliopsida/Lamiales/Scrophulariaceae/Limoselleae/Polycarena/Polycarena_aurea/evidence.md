## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
In the southern part of its range, the flowers are wholly yellow. Around Middelpost and south east of it (3120CC and 3220AB) the flowers may be either wholly yellow or the limb may be white with only the tips of the posticous lobes tipped with yellow. Polycarena aurea is easily distinguished from P. pubescens, with which it is partly sympatric, by its larger limb (6-9.5 mm versus 3-5 mm), upper lip glabrous or very nearly so (not well bearded) and shorter indumentum (hairs on stem, bract and calyx up to 0.25-0.4 mm, versus 0.5-1 mm). It is distinguished from P. formosa by its shorter indumentum (longer hairs 0.25-4 mm versus 0.6-1.2 mm), leaves only obscurely toothed, differently shaped bracts (elliptic versus linear to oblong) adnate roughly halfway up the calyx tube (not nearly to the top) and often shorter - corolla tube (3-5.5 mm versus 5-6.5 mm). The area of P. aurea lies mainly west and south of ' that of P. formosa; they appear to be sympatric only in the neighbourhood of Niewoudtville.
<!-- /evo:text -->
