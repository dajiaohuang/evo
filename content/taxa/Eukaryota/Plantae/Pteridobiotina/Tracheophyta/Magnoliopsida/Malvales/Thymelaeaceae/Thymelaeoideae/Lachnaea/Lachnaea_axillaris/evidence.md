## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Lachnaea axillaris is closely related to L. pudens, L. leipoldtii and L. pusilla. The upper portion of the hypanthium in these species is basally invaginated. L. axillaris is easily distinguished from L. pudens by the hypanthium being glabrous on the outside. In L. axillaris the upper portion of the hypanthium is cup-shaped and the stigma is capitate and papillate, whereas in L. leipoldtii and L. pusilla it is narrowly obconical and the stigma is conical and papillate and narrowly conical and papillate respectively.
<!-- /evo:text -->
