## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Undershrub, shrub or small tree up to 8 m high; branchlets with ± conspicuous lenticels. Leaf blade 7.5-17.2 x 2.5-7 cm, ovate to oblong, oblique and cordate at the base, muticous or mucronulate, entire to serrulate and often undulate when dry at the margin, pinnately nerved, glabrous above, glabrous or with hairs on the nerves beneath, sometimes puberulous in the axils of secondary nerves, petiole 3-8 mm long, glabrescent. Stipules united on the lower side of the branchlets, free on the upper side, 6-10(-13) mm long, glabrous or pubescent. Flowers 14-60 in partial, compound dichasia, the extremities tending to umbels, glabrous to densely pubescent; peduncle 1.6-4(-6.5) cm long, glabrous or pubescent; bracts 3-6 mm long, subglabrous to velutinous, caducous or persistent at anthesis; secondary branches 3-17(-27) mm long; pedicel of the primary flower up to 2 cm long, the others 6-12 mm long, glabrous to densely pubescent. Buds glabrous to velutinous, sometimes with a tuft of hairs at the apex. Sepals 2-3.5 x 1.5-2 mm, triangular to deltate, glabrous, puberulous or tomentulose, sometimes with longer hairs at the apex, spreading to reflexed at anthesis. Petals 1.7-3 x 0.5-1 mm, flat or slightly incurved at the margin in dried material, caducous. Stamens 1.5-2.2 mm long, erect at anthesis, caducous; anther 0.6-1 mm long, oblong. Disc 2-3 mm diam. Ovary glabrous, very rarely puberulous; style 1-2 mm long, divided from apex to 1/3-2/3 of length into 3 branches, erect to recurved at anthesis. Fruit 9-15 mm diam. Seeds 6-9 x 6-7 mm, brown.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Capuron in a revision of the genus in Madagascar drew attention wide variation found within L. pervillei, and the existence of several intermediates which did not allow the recognition of infraspecific taxa. In fact, although the gatherings from northern Madagascar do not disagree with the original concept of the species, the collections from the south of the island show a tendency towards pubescence and are similar to the specimens from Natal. These, as well as the collections from Mozambique, have been identified as L. mildbraedii. However, they differ from this species in several morphological characters including larger flowers and capsules, conspicuous disc, reflexed sepals, inflorescences tending to umbels, and usually broadly ovate leaves instead of elliptic or oblanceolate. They also occupy a different habitat.
<!-- /evo:text -->
