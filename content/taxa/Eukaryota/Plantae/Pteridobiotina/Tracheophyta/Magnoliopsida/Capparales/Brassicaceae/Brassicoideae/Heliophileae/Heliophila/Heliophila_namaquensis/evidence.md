## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Purplish tinged annual, 150-350 mm tall, stems and branches minutely hairy. Leaves pinnatifid, 30-100 mm long, 3-9-lobed. Flowers small, white. Fruit ± orbicular to obovate, 8-10 mm across, pendulous.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The plants are more strongly pigmented than in T. capensis, the whole plant usually having a purplish tinge. Differs from T. capensis in the smaller fruits and seeds, the lax fruiting racemes, and in the flowers which are smaller in all their parts.
