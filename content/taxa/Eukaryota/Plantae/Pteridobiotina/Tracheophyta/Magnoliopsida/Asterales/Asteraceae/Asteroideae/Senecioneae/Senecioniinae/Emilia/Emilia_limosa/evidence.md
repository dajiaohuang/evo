## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
This species appears to be very similar to or the same as E. integrifolia Baker which occurs in the DRC, Congo, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Malawi, and Madagascar. Emilia integrifolia has more capitula than E. limosa, as many as 14 in lax or congested terminal corymbs and the capitula are smaller. However, intermediate specimens exist, e.g. Fanshawe F7937 (SRGH) from Broken Hill, Zambia and Robinson 2645 (SRGH) from Mkushi, Zambia are like E. integrifolia with many capitula, but of the larger size seen in E. limosa. Similarly, Gilbert & Mesfin Tadesse 6455 (PRE) from Kenya has the larger capitula typical of E. limosa but more than 3 or 4 per corymb, whereas Robinson 3597 (SRGH) from Kalenda Plain in the Mwinilungu District of Zambia has slightly smaller capitula but few (2 or 3) per stem branch.
<!-- /evo:text -->
