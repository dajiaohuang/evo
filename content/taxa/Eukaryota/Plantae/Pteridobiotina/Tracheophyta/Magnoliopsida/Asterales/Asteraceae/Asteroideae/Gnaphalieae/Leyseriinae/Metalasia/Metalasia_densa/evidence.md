## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
An erect, white-woolly shrub to 2.5 m, sometimes 4 m, with often reflexed, lanceolate to ovate twisted leaves 2–15 mm long and axillary tufts; discoid heads are in terminal clusters, three- to five-flowered, with erect or rarely spreading bracts, outer ones brown below and inner petaloid bracts white, sometimes brown.
