## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
A decumbent slender shrublet, up to a few dm high (?), glabrous except for leaf-axils, repeatedly branching; branches decumbent-ascending, leafy towards the tips, lower down nude and clothed with linear-acuminate-subulate, persistent leaf-bases; cortex grey, cicatricose, somewhat sulcate. Leaves closely set, erecto-patent, narrowly linear, 1-2.5 cm long, 0.5-1 mm wide, many entire and some 2-3-forked from or above the middle (occasionally pinnatipartite with 2 pairs of lobes), flattened, coriaceous-subcarnose, indistinctly midveined, acute-subobtuse, acutely apiculate with a firm, whitish-brownish point; leaf-base dilated, almost half-clasping, arachnose-woolly in the axil. Peduncles lateral in the upper leaf-axils, several on each branch, ± erect, filiform and slender, 3.5-9 cm long, 0.2-0.4 mm thick, straw-coloured, somewhat sulcate. Involucre cupshaped-somewhat campanulate, 4-6 mm in diam. Involucral bracts 8-12, uniseriate, narrowly oblong-lanceolate, 2.5-3.5 mm long, 0.6-1.2 mm wide, connate to 1/4-1/3, herbaceous, distinctly 1-3-veined with thick resiniferous veins, somewhat acute with blunt puberulous tips, basally slightly gibbous. Receptacle somewhat convex, distinctly alveolate with raised pit-margins. Ray-florets 5-6, yellow. Tube 0.5-1 mm long, cylindrical. Lamina elliptic-oblong, 2.5-3 mm long, 0.8-1.4 mm wide, 4-veined. Style somewhat flattened with slightly swollen base; style branches 0.8-1 mm long, obtuse. Disc-florets c. 20-35. Corolla 2.5-3 mm long. Tube 0.7-1 mm long, cylindrical. Limb campanulate, 1.8-2 mm long; lobes deltoid, 0.5-0.7 mm long. Anthers 1.2-1.5 mm long incl. the oblong-ovate obtuse appendage. Style terete or flattened with slightly swollen base, fertile or sterile; stylophore short but distinct; style branches 0.1-0.5 mm long, truncate, with or without stigmatic surfaces. Pappus bristles few, 0.5-1 mm long, somewhat flexuous, white, densely barbellate. Achenes oblong-obovate, 2-2.5 mm long, 0.8-1 mm wide, densely hirsute with short, ± appressed, light brownish hairs, with c. 8 faint ribs under the indumentum.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
This new species is characterized, i.a., by the decumbent habit, the very slender peduncles, the small capitula, the short rays, and the sparse and short pappus. It has some resemblance to E. longipes, which is a species of south-western Cape with a more erect growth and different involucres with broader phyllaries, etc. E. gracilipes is only known from a single, recent collection. The inconspicuous habit together with the unusual flowering time (early autumn) may explain why the species has not been collected earlier.
<!-- /evo:text -->
