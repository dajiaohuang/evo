## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
A much-branched erect shrublet, forming dense sprawling cushions, glabrous except for leaf-axils; young branches closely leafy, becoming defoliated with age and clothed with imbricated, scale-like, triangular, acute or sometimes cuspidate (from the persistent midrib) leaf-bases. Leaves ± spreading, becoming reflexed before withering, linear, 7-15 mm long, 1.52 mm wide, entire, flattened, somewhat fleshy, pure green, obtuse, finely impressed-midveined on the adaxial side, mid-ribbed esp. towards the base on the abaxial side; leaf-base dilated, half-clasping, with thin margins, ciliolate along the margins, arachnose in the axil. Peduncles terminal, solitary, erect, 1-5 mm long, after anthesis elongating to c. 2 cm, c. 0.5 mm wide, terete, somewhat striate. Involucre campanulate, 5-7 mm wide, 4-6 mm high, coriaceous. Involucral bracts 8-10, uniseriate, connate to the middle; the free lobes narrowly ovate-lanceolate, 2.5-4 mm long, 1-2 mm wide, with 3 distinct resiniferous veins, obtuse-subacute, with puberulous margins and tips. Receptacle somewhat convex, distinctly alveolate. Ray-florets 8-10, yellow. Tube c. 0.5 mm long, cylindrical. Lamina narrowly elliptic-oblong, 7-9 mm long, 3-4 mm wide, 4-veined. Style branches c. 0.7 mm long, obtuse-rounded at the apex. Disc-florets c. 15. Corolla 3-4 mm long. Tube 0.5-1.5 mm long, cylindrical. Limb campanulate, c. 2.5 mm long; lobes narrowly ovate, c. 1 mm long and 0.5 mm wide, with subcucullate tips. Anthers c. 1.5 mm long incl. the narrowly oblong-ovate, acute or subacute appendage. Style terete or flattened with somewhat swollen base on a short stylophore; style branches 0.2-0.3 mm long, with papillate outsides and truncate-slightly convex tips, at least sometimes probably sterile. Pappus bristles fairly numerous, 2-3 mm long, ±erect, white. Achenes obovate, 3-3.5 mm long, c. 1.5 mm wide, glabrous, greenish -yellowish brown, with 8(-9) light brown ribs.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
This little-known species differs from its consectioners by its entire, obtuse leaves in combination with the comparatively long postfloral peduncles and the distinctly three-nerved involucral bracts, which are connate only for half their length. In foliage the species has some resemblance to E. annae, which in other respects is very different, however, and belongs to a different section (peduncles numerous and lateral, involucral bracts c. 5, achenes 5-ribbed, etc.). The closest ally may be E. montanus, which has likewise entire leaves and similar achenes. In the latter species, however, the leaves are distinctly narrowed towards the base, and the peduncles are much more reduced. The styles of the disc-florets appear to have poorly developed stig-matic surfaces, and probably they are at least sometimes sterile. The scanty material available did not permit investigation of a large number of florets. E. brevipes is only known from a single collection. Only a few flowering capitula are present in the material, and it is likely that the species has its main flowering period in summer.
<!-- /evo:text -->
