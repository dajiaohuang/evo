## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Tufted annual herb; stems up to 250 mm, erect or decumbent, closely leafy throughout. Leaves sessile, up to 20 x 3 mm, oblong or spathulate-oblong, apex and mucronate, margins entire, somewhat revolute, green above, grey- or white-woolly below. Heads oblong, c. 14 x 4 mm, solitary and terminal on very short leafy shoots, many crowded at the ends of the branches in a racemose inflorescence. Involucral bracts graded, innermost blotched purple towards the tips. Flowers hidden by the copious pappus, nondescript, hermaphrodite ones purple-tipped. Achenes 1.5 mm long. Pappus bristles fine, white, silky-plumose.
<!-- /evo:text -->
