## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Mat-forming perennial to 5 cm with creeping rhizome, roots thickened, fusiform. Leaves in a basal rosette, narrowly oblanceolate, margins involute, sinuate, densely white-felted below. Flower heads radiate, sessile, yellow.
