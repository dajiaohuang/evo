## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Shrublet. Leaves at branch tips and crowded on short-shoots, cuneate and apically closely 7-20-fid, 5-13 mm long, lobes with distinct, firm, white tips. Flowerheads solitary in upper axils on peduncles 30-60 mm long, radiate, yellow; involucre hemispherical, ± 10 mm diam., bracts 9-11, connate at base only. Cypselas shortly hairy, mucilaginous when wet.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
This rare and little-known species comes near to E. cuneatus and E. namaquensis but differs from both by the peculiar, many-lobed leaves. These are short and wide with much shorter and more numerous lobes than in E. namaquensis. The new species differs from E. cuneatus, apart from in number of leaf-lobes, by the more numerous, biseriate involucral bracts, the shorter (and in water mucilaginous) indumentum of the achenes, and the smaller pollen grains. The white-tipped leaf-lobes probably afford effective protection against insolation. The lobes stand close together, apparently forming a white, reflecting surface directed towards the sun rays and sheltering the green parts of the leaf. E. pleiodontus has only been collected twice by the Schlechter brothers. The locality 'I'us' has not been located but both localities are probably situated in the same area to the east of Steinkopf.
<!-- /evo:text -->
