## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Shrub, up to 2 m high. Leaves ovate, dentate, glandular and pubescent, shortly petiolate. Inflorescence corymbose, heads with 3 flowers. Inner pappus bristles barbellate. Achenes glandular and pilose. Flowers mauve.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Diagnosed by its 3-flowered capitula and ovate, coarsely serrate leaves with medifixed hairs. This species is superficially very similar to Gymnanthemum amygdalinum, but the latter has narrowly ovate to lanceolate leaves with sub-entire to serrulate-crispulate margins and a foliar indumentum of short-spurred hairs, and 5-flowered capitula.
<!-- /evo:text -->
