## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Well-branched rounded dwarf shrublet up to 200 mm high and 300 mm across, main stem very short, gnarled, woody, up to 5 mm diam., branches slender (±1 mm diam.), nude below and rooting, closely leafy above. Leaves closely imbricate, visible part suborbicular, ±5 x 5 mm, thick, greyish-white woolly, base oblong, slightly shorter and narrower than blade, membranous, nearly glabrous, stem-clasping. Heads homogamous, campanulate, 10-13 mm long, ±20 mm across the fully radiating bracts, solitary on terminal leafy peduncles, these leaves spathulate to elliptic, the uppermost sometimes with a scarious appendage. Involucral bracts in ±11 series, descending on the peduncle, graded, loosely imbricate, outer palest brown, inner exceeding the flowers, obtuse, opaque, white, all tinged crimson at the base, giving a rosy cast to the whole head. Receptacle nearly smooth. Flowers 45-81, yellow. Achenes 1.25 mm long, with long (up to 0.5 mm) duplex hairs. Pappus bristles ±12-14, about equalling corolla, tips shortly plumose, bases not cohering.
<!-- /evo:text -->
