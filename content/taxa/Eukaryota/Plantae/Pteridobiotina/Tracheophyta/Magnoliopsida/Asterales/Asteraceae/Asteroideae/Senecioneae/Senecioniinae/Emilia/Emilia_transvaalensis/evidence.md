## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Annual herb, up to 450 mm tall. Leaves oblong to elliptic, base broad, clasping, margins entire; slightly fleshy; thinly cobwebby at first, glabrescent. Heads solitary, discoid; involucral bracts 6 or 7, 5.0-6.5 mm long. Achenes with white clavate hairs on angles. Flowers pink to lilac.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Although described in Senecio, S. transvaalensis is better placed in the tropical genus Emilia, and Mkuze is about as far south as this genus occurs in eastern Africa. Emilia is distinguished from Senecio by its shortly tailed style branches. This plant may prove to be S. lambomboensis Muschler, but there are several discrepancies with his description and the type specimen (Wilms 826, Olifantsriver, in den Lambombobergen) has not been traced.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/2/text -->
This widespread species in the eastern regions of southern Africa is characterised by its grey-green, semi-succulent leaves and discoid capitula with pale pink or lilac florets. Its most southerly distribution is noted to be Mkuzi in KwaZulu-Natal. Bolus 12304 was selected as the lectotype because it has the most duplicates of the three syntypes, and the BOL specimen still has capitula attached whereas the PRE one does not.
<!-- /evo:text -->
