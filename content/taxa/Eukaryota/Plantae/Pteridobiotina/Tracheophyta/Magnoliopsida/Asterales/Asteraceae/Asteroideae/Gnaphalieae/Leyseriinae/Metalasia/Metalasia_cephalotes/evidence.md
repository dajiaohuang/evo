## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
A spreading, white-woolly shrub sometimes to 1 m, with reflexed, scarcely twisted leaves 3–20 mm long and axillary tufts; discoid heads are fused in dense terminal clusters, five-flowered, with petaloid, usually glandular bracts, the inner ones spreading and pink or white.
