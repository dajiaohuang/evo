## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
This rare and little known species possesses some unique features, such as the long and much exserted filaments and styles. It somewhat resembles W. elongata, but the spike is twice as broad, the flowers much larger, and the stamens often overtopping the perianth segments. The dark and thickish margins of the segments are reminiscent of W. spicata var. ustulata.
<!-- /evo:text -->
