## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Dwarf shrub, up to 500 mm tall. Branches, including leaf sheaths, long and slender, much longer than thick. Leaf blades glabrous or with only marginal trichomes; leaf sheaths not splitting, 30 mm long. Ovary trichomes appressed, 0.2-0.6 mm long. Flowers mauve.
