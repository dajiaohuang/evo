## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Robust perennial, short rhizomatous and tufted (erect), up to 1.4 m high. Leaf blades up to 800 mm long; 2-3(-6) mm wide. Culms 5-6 mm in diameter, unbranched, lower internodes woolly-hairy or glabrous. Spikelets 25-30 mm long (including awns); lower glume half the length of the upper, mucro or awn absent; lemma articulation absent; lemma narrowed into a beak or very short twisted column; awns 3, subequal; callus tip small, naked, swollen and rounded.
<!-- /evo:text -->
