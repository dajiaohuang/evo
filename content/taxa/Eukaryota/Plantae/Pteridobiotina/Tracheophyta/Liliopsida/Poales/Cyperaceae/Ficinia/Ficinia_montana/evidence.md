## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial, short rhizome to 10-30 mm diameter. Culms 25-200 mm tall, 0.9-1.2 mm thick, but ca. 2.0 mm thick across the rim of the leaf sheath, glabrous. Leaf sheath 7-58 mm long, glabrous, not papery, wine-red, not sticky, lacking ligule. Leaf blade 15-82 mm long and 0.4-1.5 mm wide, glabrous, canaliculate. Involucral bracts 3-5, leaf-like and erect, 5-29 mm long, glabrous. Inflorescence capitate, 5.0-8.0 mm diameter, each with over 10-15 spikelets. Spikelets 2.3-5.5 x 1.0-3.0 mm, terete, dark brown, florets bisexual. Glumes 2.1-5.0 mm long, boat-shaped with a mucro to 0.5 mm long; margins entire. Stamens 3, anthers crested. Style trifid. Nutlets 0.7-2.0 x 0.5-1.5 mm, dark brown, papillose; hypogynous disc to 0.2 mm long, cupular, 6-9-lobed.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Similar to Ficinia indica, differing in habit (mat-forming vs. erect), plant height (below 250 mm vs. to 900 mm), and restricted to mountains above 800 m elevation (vs. typically below 600 m). The epithet montana refers to the habitat of this species, on top of the mountains, which is unique among the species in the F. indica complex.
<!-- /evo:text -->
