## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Tufted perennial 300-600 mm high; old leaf sheaths firm, erect, fibrous. Leaf blade 80-200 x 2-4 mm. Inflorescence open, scabrid; pedicels and branches glabrous to rarely sparsely scabrid, at least some shorter to almost as long as spikelets. Spikelet 15-50 x 3-6 mm (including awns), linear, purplish, hairy; lower glume 1-3-nerved; upper glume 3-5-nerved; lemma 10-15 mm long; awn 3-6 mm long; palea ciliate on keels, minutely hairy between keels; callus scar circular; anthers 4-7 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
