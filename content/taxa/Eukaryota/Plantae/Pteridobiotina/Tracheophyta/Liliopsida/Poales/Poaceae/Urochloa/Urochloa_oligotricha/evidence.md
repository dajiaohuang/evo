## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial, rhizomatous (rhizomes stout, sometimes shortly creeping), up to 1 m high. Leaf blades 50-100 mm long, 6-12 mm wide. Basal sheaths very densely hairy, old sheaths splitting into fibres. Spikelets 3-5 mm long. Racemes 5-20, 30-100 mm long; spikelets lanceolate; lower glume 5-nerved; upper lemma with mucro 0.3-0.5 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
