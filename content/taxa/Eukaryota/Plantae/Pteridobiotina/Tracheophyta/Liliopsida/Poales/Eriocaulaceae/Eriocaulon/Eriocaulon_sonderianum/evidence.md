## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Mr R. D. Meikle of Kew suggested that E. sonderianum could be a variety of E. dregei, and the ample collections available now support this view. E. dregei appears to be a polyploid. E. dregei var. sonderianum is the common variety in the Transvaal; in Natal both varieties occur but in the Eastern Cape the larger var. dregei becomes common whereas var. sonderianum is rare. At high altitudes, where ice and snow occur in winter, the dwarfed plants of var. sonderianum are found.
<!-- /evo:text -->
