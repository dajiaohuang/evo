# Poaceae

## atlas-profile / overview

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/overview -->
The profile separates a sampled plastid topology from latest-Cretaceous Lameta phytolith evidence and a regional Neogene phytolith transition; none of these records supplies a single grass origin, universal C4 history or global grassland narrative.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / diet

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/diet -->
Photosynthetic primary producer; no family-wide ecological role is inferred from the fossil samples
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / habitat

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/habitat -->
Heterogeneous mixed C3–C4 habitats in the sampled central Great Plains palaeosols; no global habitat is assigned
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / locomotion

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/locomotion -->
Sessile growth
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / bodySize

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/bodySize -->
No family-wide body-size range is inferred from phytolith assemblages
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / ecology / guild

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/ecology/guild -->
Primary-producer context only; the evidence does not equate C4 biomass with open-grassland dominance
<!-- /evo:text -->

<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / traits

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/traits/2 -->
Multiple reconstructed C4 origins on the sampled plastid tree
<!-- /evo:text -->

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary

<!-- evo:text /records/atlas-profile/evidenceSummary -->
Prasad and colleagues supply a contested, locality-bounded phytolith record, Strömberg and McInerney quantify a later regional assemblage shift, and GPWG II supplies a sampled plastid topology. These evidence streams are kept separate from body-fossil first appearances, crown ages and global vegetation cover.
<!-- /evo:text -->
