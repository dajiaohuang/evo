## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Tufted perennial about 600 mm high; stolons horizontal; basal sheaths white, shiny, persistent; leaves basal; glands absent. Leaf blade to 300 x to 1.5 mm, rolled, rigid, glabrous; margins scabrid; sheath mouth with a ring of bristles. Inflorescence open, 60-110 x 50-80 mm, with ± 45-100 spikelets; pedicels glabrous; axils usually hairy. Spikelet 7.5-8.5 mm long; glumes acuminate, green, glabrous; lemma 3.5-5.0 mm long, sparsely hairy all over except apex usually glabrous; lateral awns 3-4 mm long, exserted from glumes; central awn 7-12 mm long, geniculate; palea hairy in upper third; anthers 2-3 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
