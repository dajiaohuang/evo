## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial, rhizomatous (rhizomes stout) or tufted (geniculate at base), up to 0.7 m high. Leaf blades up to 250 mm long, up to 4 mm wide. Basal sheaths glabrous at base. Spikelets 6-8(-15) mm long, 1.5-3.0 mm wide. Inflorescence ovate to orbicular, branches fairly rigid, spreading, pedicels 3 times the length of spikelets, spreading; spikelets grey-green, rhachilla persistent, becoming fragile in upper portion; lemma acuminate, lateral nerves distinct; palea oblanceolate, thick-textured, apex acute to subacute, keels entire, forming a prominent ridge, shortly ciliolate; anthers 3, 0.8-1.0 mm long. Caryopsis elliptic.
<!-- /evo:text -->
