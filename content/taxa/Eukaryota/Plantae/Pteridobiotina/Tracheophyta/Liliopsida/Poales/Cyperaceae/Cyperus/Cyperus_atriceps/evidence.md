## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Annual herb, up to 0.27 m high; nutlets narrowly ellipsoid, surface smooth. Flowers: spikes shortly cylindrical to almost spherical; spikelets > 3 mm long, abscising as entire units, except for basal sterile glumes; glumes few per spikelet, not winged, median longitudinal vein of glume more-or-less recurved, reddish black; Feb.-May.
