## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial, forming clumps to 300 mm diameter, base hardened, without obvious rhizome. Culm 50-160 mm tall, 0.3-0.6 mm thick, glabrous. Leaf sheath 7-19 x 1.5-2.2 mm, glabrous, not papery, wine-red, sticky. Leaf blade 26-55 x 0.3-0.9 mm, channelled, glabrous except for scabrid margins towards apex. Involucral bract 2 or 3, leaf-like, 10-29 x 0.4-0.6 mm, margin scabrid towards apex. Inflorescence capitate, 4-8 x 2.4-5.7 mm, with 3-6 spikelets. Spikelets 3.9-6.4 x 1.6-2.5 mm, terete, dark brown. Glumes 2.7-3.8 mm long, acute, with a mucro to 0.3 mm long; margins entire. Style trifid. Stamens 3, anthers crested. Nutlets 1.5-2.0 x 0.8-1.0 mm, brown, minutely papillose; hypogynous disc to 0.8 mm long, cupular, 3-lobed.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Ficinia overbergensis has morphological and ecological similarity to Ficinia quartzicola Muasya & N.E.Helme. Both taxa grow in quartz substrate but are geographically separated with F. quartzicola only known from the Knersvlakte, where it forms tufts of similar size. Morphologically, the species share sticky leaf sheaths, but F. quartzicola forms tough tufts (stuck together tightly) with rigid culms that lack leaf blades. The inflorescence of F. overbergensis is capitate with 2 or 3 leafy involucral bracts and 3-6 spikelets whereas F. quartzicola has a pseudolateral to capitate inflorescence with 5-10 spikelets and where the involucral bract is stem-like.
<!-- /evo:text -->
