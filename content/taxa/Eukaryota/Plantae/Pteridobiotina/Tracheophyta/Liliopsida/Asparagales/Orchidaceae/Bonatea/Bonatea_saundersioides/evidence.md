## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
B. saundersioides is found in shady habitats within riverine forest and savannah of the northeasterly parts of South Africa. It is closely allied to B. cassidea, both species sharing intermediately basal lower leaves, desiccated floral bracts at flowering, narrowly-cucullate median rostellum lobes, and curved spurs where the clavate apex comprises 1/2-2/3 of the total spur length. B. saundersioides has longer and narrower lower petal lobes ((13-) 16-24 x 0.5-2.0(-3.0) mm) than B. cassidea (8-15 (-19) x (1.5-)3.0-8.0 mm). This species also has narrower lip lateral lobes (0.5-2.0 mm vs (1.5)2.0-6.5 mm). Colour variation occurs in the perianth of B. saundersioides with either green or white lip lateral lobes.
<!-- /evo:text -->
