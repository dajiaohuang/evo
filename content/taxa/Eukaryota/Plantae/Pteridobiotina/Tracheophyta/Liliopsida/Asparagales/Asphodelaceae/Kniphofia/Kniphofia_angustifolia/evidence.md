## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants one-to several-stemmed. Leavei soft, erect at first, later arcuate, 400-700 x 2-1 mm, ± triangular in cross section with a narrov channel above, slightly keeled below; margin and keel smooth or remotely denticulate toward; apex. Scape 400-650 mm tall, overtopping arcu ate leaves. Raceme usually lax, rarely subdense oblong-cylindrical, 80-250 x 5-6 mm; bud cream-coloured or dull yellow to orange-red flowers pendulous, often somewhat arcuate, vary ing in colour from white to cream, yellow or coral-red. Bracts lanceolate to linear-lanceolate acute to acuminate, 6-10 mm long. Pedicels 2-3 mm long. Perianth subcylindrical, often slightly arcuate, 19-30 mm long; lobes ovate, 2.5 mm long, not or scarcely spreading. Stamens included or slightly exserted at anthesis, later withdrawn. Fruit subglobose to ovoid, ±6 mm long. Flowening time: end of November to early April with a peak from January to March.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The variation may occur within a small area and the situation resembles the hybrid swarms reported under K. fibrosa. Here again it is assumed that the longer perianth and reddish colours are contributed by K. triangularis, which has dense, concolorous racemes, orange-red to coral-red in colour, with flowers 24-35 m long. Specimens with flowers about 30 mm long may be confused with K. ichopensis var. ichopensi, but the two can usually be separated on the basis of leaf width which, in K. angustifolia, does not exceed 5 mm.
