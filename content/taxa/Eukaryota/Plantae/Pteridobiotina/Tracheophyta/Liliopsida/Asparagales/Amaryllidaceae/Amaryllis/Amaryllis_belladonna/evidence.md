## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The flowers show considerable natural variability, both in size and colour. The perianth varies from deep rose pink to nearly white and is 7-11 cm long. Some of the forms have been afforded varietal and even specific rank but field observations do not support this treatment. The peduncle of A. belladonna persists for a lengthy period after the ripening of the seeds. This contrasts with the collapse of the peduncle just above the bulb of species of Brunsvigia and Crossyne ['as Boophone'] for example, whose ripe inflorescences are rolled about the veld by wind during seed dispersal.
<!-- /evo:text -->
