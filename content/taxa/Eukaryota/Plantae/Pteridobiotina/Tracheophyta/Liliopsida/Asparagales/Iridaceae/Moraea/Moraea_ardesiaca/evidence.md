## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants 500-700 mm high, usually solitary. Corm ± 15 mm diam.; tunics of medium-textured, netted fibres. Stem sheathed below by light brown, broken cataphylls, unbranched, partly covered by sheathing leaves 2(3), ± 100 mm long, lower part often exposed. Foliage leaf linear, channelled, usually exceeding stem, 5-10(-25) mm wide. Rhipidial spathes grey-green with light brown tips, inner 90-140 mm long, outer ± 2/3 as long. Flowers long-lived, slate-blue to pale blue-mauve, outer tepal limbs with linear, pale yellow nectar guides at bases, half reflexed; outer tepals 65-75 x 25-35 mm, limb 45-50 mm long; inner tepals 50-60 mm long. Filaments 8-10 mm long, united in lower half; anthers 10-12 mm long, as long or longer than filaments. Ovary cylindric, up to 25 mm long; style branches ± 15 mm long, crests 15-20 mm long. Capsules cylindric-3-lobed, ± 20 mm long, shortly beaked. Seeds unknown.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The only blue-flowered member of subg. Grandiflorae in southern Africa, Moraea ardesiaca is immediately recognised by the large flowers either slate-blue to blue-mauve. Confusion with M. carnea is possible but this species has pale pink flowers with darker pink to purple style crests and smaller, narrower tepals 55-60 mm long vs. 65-70 mm in M. ardesiaca. Moraea carnea also occurs along streams on open slopes at relatively high elevations, usually above 2300 m, whereas M. ardesiaca is generally found at lower elevations along streams and drainage lines.
<!-- /evo:text -->
