## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, up to 100 mm tall, stem exposed below. Basal leaves short, linear, spreading, unifacial distally, upper leaves short, bifacial, broadly ovate, concave. Flowers yellowish with large brown spots and margins, tepal claws broad, forming a wide cup, nectaries prominent, anther lobes parallel. Capsule not beaked.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The parallel anthers, wide and shallow tepal cup, spindle-shaped ovary without a beak, and stigmas terminal on the style arms, place Ferraria ovata in sect. Ferraria where it is unusual in the few-branched or simple stem and strongly dimorphic leaves, the basal linear and the cauline bifacial, short and spoon-shaped. The relatively small flowers are strikingly coloured yellow and glossy dark brown. The nectaries are bilobed or paired well above the bases of the tepal claws. Ferraria ovata is one of three winter-flowering species in the genus, the others being the coastal species F. densepunctulata and F. ornata.
<!-- /evo:text -->
