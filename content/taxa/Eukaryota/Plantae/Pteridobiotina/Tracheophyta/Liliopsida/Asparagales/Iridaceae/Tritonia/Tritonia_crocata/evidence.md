## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Distinguished from the two other species with large, bowl-shaped flowers with spathulate tepals and diverging filaments by the broad, orange to red tepals with hyaline margins on the claws, the lower 3 tepals with a narrow median yellow stripe in the throat, sometimes raised as a low ridge. Orange-flowered Tritonia deusta lacks the hyaline margins to the tepals and has a distinct, large yellow star-like marking in the throat of the tube and often a large dark blotch on the outer tepals, and T. squalida has pink to mauve, often narrower tepals, also with hyaline margins on the claws. Hybrids between T. crocata and T. deusta have been recorded between Riversdale and Albertinia where the ranges of the two species overlap.
<!-- /evo:text -->
