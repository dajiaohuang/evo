## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants (40-) 60-150 mm; leaves 2, subbasal, subopposite, ovate, acute, 10-20 (-25) x 6-12 (-15) mm. Inflorescences 1 (-2)-flowered; bracts ovate, acuminate, 5-8 x 2.5-4 mm. Flowers whitish or tinged with pink, median sepal darker or olive, lateral sepals marked with a longitudinal pink streak; ovary ovoid, c. 4 mm long. Median sepal ovate, with an erect, tubular spur to 4-6 (-9) mm long; lateral sepals elliptic, acuminate, 5x2 mm, spurs obtuse, 1 mm long. Petals adnate to the median sepal, curved, falcate, dimidiately rotund, acuminate, 4.5 x 3 mm.
