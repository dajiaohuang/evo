## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants mostly 150-350 mm high. Corm bell-shaped with flat base; tunics dark brown, softly woody, outer layers irregularly broken, margins minutely toothed. Stem unbranched. Leaves 3, lower 2 basal, falcate, blades 50-100 x 2-6 mm, bright green to slightly glaucous, with thickened, raised main vein, margins translucent-hyaline, uppermost leaf inserted shortly above ground, sheathing stem for most of its length with short blade up to 10 mm long. Spike flexuose, 1- or 2(3)-flowered, outer bract ± 12 mm long, pale green flushed red to purple at tip, inner slightly shorter, with 2 green keels, narrowed distally. Flowers crepuscular, rotate, white without darker colouring on reverse, dull yellow-green in throat and tube, tepals remaining cupped when fully open, with sweet, rose-like scent; perianth tube narrowly funnel-shaped, 5-6 mm long; tepals subequal, elliptic, 15-17 x 4-5 mm. Filaments ± 3 mm long, inserted ± 2 mm above base of tepals; anthers held horizontally, 7-8 mm long, pale yellow; pollen yellow. Style dividing 2 mm below mouth of tube, branches ± 12 mm long, diverging.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
With a bell-shaped corm with a flat base, Hesperantha eremophila conforms to sect. Hesperantha, where it is distinguished by pure white flowers lacking dark colour on the reverse of the outer tepals, a funnel-shaped perianth tube 5-6 mm long, and tepals remaining cupped when fully open. The tepals, 15-17 mm long, are much longer than the perianth tube, and the long style branches, ± 12 mm long, are distinctive. The flowers are crepuscular, opening mid- to late afternoon and closing after midnight. Among the white-flowered species of the section and genus only H. dolomitica has similarly cupped tepals also without colouring on the outside of the tepals, which suggests a possible relationship with H. eremophila. Flowers of H. dolomitica are larger, with a perianth tube 12-14 mm long and tepals about the same length, and style branches ± 7 mm long, just over half as long as those of H. eremophila.
<!-- /evo:text -->
