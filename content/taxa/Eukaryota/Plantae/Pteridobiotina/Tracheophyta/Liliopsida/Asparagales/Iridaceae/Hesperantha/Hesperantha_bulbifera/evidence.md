## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous herb. Stems trailing, up to 300 mm long. Plants bearing a cormlet in axil of 1 to several stem nodes. Perianth tube 9-15 mm long, recurved, shorter than bracts and emerging between them. Flowers pale creamy white, tinged with lilac or pink.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Allied to the largely western southern African Hesperantha bachmannii, H. bulbifera has similar white flowers with curved perianth tube but these are somewhat larger, with a perianth tube 9-15 mm long and tepals 16-20 mm long vs. tube ± 10 mm long and tepals 14-20 mm long in H. bachmannii. In addition, H. bulbifera always has larger corms, 8-10 mm diam. vs. 6-8 mm, with a conspicuous cormlet in the lower leaf axils not present in H. bachmannii.
<!-- /evo:text -->
