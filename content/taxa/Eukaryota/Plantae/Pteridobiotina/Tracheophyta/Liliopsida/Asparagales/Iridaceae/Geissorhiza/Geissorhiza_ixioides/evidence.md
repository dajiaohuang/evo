## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants (120-)160-300 mm high. Corm ovoid, symmetric, 5-8 mm diam.; tunics dark brown to blackish, imbricate, outer layers regularly notched below into sections. Stem sparsely ciliate, simple or 1(2)-branched. Leaves 3, lower 2 basal, linear to sword-shaped, usually shorter than stem, 3-5 mm wide, apparently plane, but margins raised at right angles to blade into wings ciliate along edges, main vein also slightly raised and winged, sparsely ciliate along edges, uppermost cauline, sheathing for at least 1/2 its length. Spike flexed at base, 2- to 6-flowered, flexuose; bracts ± membranous, dry and pale brown above, outer 10-12 x 4-4 mm, inner slightly smaller. Flowers actinomorphic, shallowly cupped, pale red-purple, dark in throat; perianth tube funnel-shaped, 1.5-2.0 mm long; tepals narrowly obovate, (10-)12-16 x 5-7 mm. Filaments erect, equal, 3.5-4.5 mm long; anthers 5-7 mm long (4 mm after anthesis), light purple; pollen dull yellow. Style dividing opposite middle 1/3 of anthers, pale mauve (drying dark purple), branches 3-6 mm long. Capsules globose, ± 5.5 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Evidently allied to Geissorhiza leipoldtii and sharing with it a papillate to ciliate stem and three leaves, the lower two basal and the upper cauline and largely sheathing, G. ixioides is recognised by its mauve-pink flowers with a dark purple centre, relatively short, equal filaments, and short, purple style dividing opposite the middle third of the anthers. Geissorhiza leipoldtii has larger, pure white flowers with longer stamens with equal filaments 7-8 mm long and anthers 6.5-8.0 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
