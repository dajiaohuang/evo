## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 1.0-1.8 m high, stem solitary or tufted, robust; corm 30-40 mm in diam. Leaves linear, apiculate; radical leaves 500-900 x 4-11 mm; reduced sheathing leaves 4 or 5. Flowering stem 0.9-1.8 m high. Bracts brown, 23-34 x 10-12 mm, elliptic-lanceolate, acute, flecked. Inflorescence a raceme of spikes, with 4-8 branches. Perianth magenta-pink or purple-red, 35-54 mm long; tube mainly 10.0-15.5 mm long; tepals 23-39 x 10-19 mm. Anthers 8-11 mm long. Flowering time Nov.-Mar.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Occurring as solitary stems or small clumps with stems 900-1800 mm tall, leaves 4-11 mm wide, and pendulous inflorescences of moderately large, magenta to purple flowers with a rather closed perianth 35-54 mm long. The species is easily recognised in the living state by the characteristic flowers, with suberect tepals from a conical base. Dierama pendulum, with widely spreading tepals from a rounded base, has a more southern distribution. Forms with dark red flowers occur in areas of sympatry with dark-flowered Dierama atrum (Dohne, Stutterheim, Pirie and King William’s Town) and D. reynoldsii (Baziya-Engcobo), suggesting possible hybridisation. Hybridisation with D. igneum in the Hogsback area is also a possibility.
<!-- /evo:text -->
