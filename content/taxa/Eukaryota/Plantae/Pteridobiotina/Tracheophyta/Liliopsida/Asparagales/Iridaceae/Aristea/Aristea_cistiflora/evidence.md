## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Rhizomatous perennial, mostly 40-50 cm, forming small clumps, stems mostly unbranched. Leaves linear, tough and fibrous. Flowers pale pink to lilac, tepal bases deep lilac or violet, streaked dull brownish outside, outer tepals with a copper-brown mark in lower third. Capsules elongate.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Aristea cistiflora is evidently most closely related to A. teretifolia, which also has the outer tepals with dark markings, the markings extending to the middle of the tepals in A. teretifolia versus close to the base in A. cistiflora. Aristea teretifolia is additionally distinguished by the leaves sometimes terete or linear and up to 2 mm wide, the flowering stem bearing at most two lateral flower clusters (versus up to six in A. cistiflora), and by the slightly smaller flowers with ascending tepals, the outer ± 20 mm long and the inner 24-28 mm long, thus substantially longer than the outer. In A. cistiflora the inner tepals are only 1-2 mm longer than the outer.
<!-- /evo:text -->
