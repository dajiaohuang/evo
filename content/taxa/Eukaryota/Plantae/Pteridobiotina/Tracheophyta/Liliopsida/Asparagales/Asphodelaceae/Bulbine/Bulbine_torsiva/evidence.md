## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants deciduous herbs, 6-11 cm high, arising from dorsiventrally flattened pyramidal tubers 1-1.5 cm high and 2-3 cm broad, with spreading, horizontal roots. Leaves 2-3, light green, erect, subtended by up to 5 tight, papyraceous, white-spotted sheaths, 4 mm-1.5 cm long and 2-4 mm broad. Leaves subterete, acute, forming tight spirals, 5-8 cm long when flattened and 1.5-2 mm in diameter. Inflorescence erect, solitary; peduncle averaging 5 cm long, to 15 cm in robust specimens after un- seasonal late rains, ca. 1.5 mm thick; raceme 2.5-6 cm long. Flowers 6-12, all tepals tightly reflexed towards pedicel; bract narrowly triangular, membranous, 0.8-1 mm long, pedicels 8 mm long, deflexed at 45°, with flowers drooping, only opening after midday; tepals very light yellow with green to orange central vein, outer elliptic, subacute, 5-7 x 1.3-2 mm, inner elliptical, subacute, 5-7 x 2.5-4 mm. Filaments rather stout, dimorphic, bearded, ca. 5-6 mm long, outer filaments all recurved towards tepals, with single cushion of hairs projecting towards the anther, the inner laterally flattened, projecting forwards with a double cushion of hairs; hairs finely clavate with bulbous apex, 1.8 mm long; anthers oblong, 0.8 mm long, pollen brownish. Ovary globose to oblong, dark green to brown, 1.5 mm long, style yellow, very slender, 4 mm long, stigma very small, green. Capsule subglobose to oblong, ca. 2 mm long; seed black, pyramidal, with undulate ridges and granulate surface, 1-1.5 mm long.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Bulbine torsiva has a superficial resemblance to B. quartzicola but differs in possessing up to 4 or 5 basal leaf-sheaths and distinctly spiralled leaves, which are not simply curled towards the apex. The flowers are also very interesting in that they droop at approximately 45° and only open after midday.
