## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 0.9-2.5 m high; stems form large clumps; corm 35-40 mm in diam. Leaves linear, ± acute; radical leaves 550-1500 x 5-15 mm; reduced sheathing leaves 5. Flowering stem 1.1-2.7 m high. Bract white, sometimes with few brownish spots, 18-27 x 7-10 mm, elliptic-lanceolate, acute to acuminate. Inflorescence a raceme of terminal spikes, usually pendulous or suberect, with 6-11 branches. Perianth pink or rarely red, (20-)24-33 mm long; tube (5.0-)8.0-10.5 mm long; tepals 12-23 x 5-10 mm. Anthers (7-)8-10 mm long. Flowering time Oct-May.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Characterised by the conspicuously clumped habit, typically broad leaves, 5-15 mm wide, and pink or rarely dark wine-red flowers with white or faintly speckled bracts. Dark-flowered plants are sympatric with Dierama reynoldsii but that species grows as solitary stems or in small tufts of up to three stems, with narrower leaves 3-6 mm wide.
<!-- /evo:text -->
