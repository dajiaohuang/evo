## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants slow-growing, long-lived succulent perennials forming dense clusters (branched from the base) to about 400 mm in diameter and consisting of about 12-60 heads, mainly pendent from cliff-faces. Branches 50-200 mm long. Leaves ascending erect, in 5 spirally twisted rows densely imbricate in a central rosette (35-38 mm in diameter), glaucous-green, becoming reddish-green during the dry periods, in 5 ranks, ovate- triangular, firm, 22-25 x 12-15 mm with a distinct marginiform keel; margin cartilaginous, serrulate, apex acute to acuminate, minutely denticulate-tuberculate. Adaxial surface somewhat channeled, faintly striate; abaxial surface tuberculate; tubercles greenish-white. Inflorescence a simple raceme 120-460 mm long; scape 2-2.5 mm wide at the base, with raceme in upper half to one third. Bracts membranous; lower bracts to 5 mm long, triangular acuminate, whitish-translucent; floral bracts 2.5-3 mm translucent with darker median stripe. Flowers 3-10 mm apart, sub-secundly arranged, ascending spreading. Pedicel 3 mm long. Perianth 11 x 2.5 mm; lobes fused into a tube, slightly curved, free for 1.5 mm, grey- green, with white stripes; outer lobes spreading, apices truncate to emarginate; inner lobes obtuse, curved inwards. Stamens 8-9 mm long, anthers 5 mm long. Ovary 3.5 x 1.5-2 oblong, green, styles 3 mm long, stigma minute, white, capitate. Capsule linear ovoid 7 x 3 mm. Seeds not seen.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## ecology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/ecology/claims/0/text -->
Astroloba cremnophila grow on N, E, W, & S facing cliffs above the Buffels River (later becoming the Gouritz River) at an altitude of between 600 & 800 m. Its habitat consists of sunny to slightly shady exposed cliffs with mainly Gamka Thicket vegetation (Fynbos on the upper slopes). The plants grow on ledges in mineral-poor quartzitic sandstone soil (Peninsula Formation, Cape Supergroup) together with other cremnophytes such as Adromischus leucophyllus, Albuca buffelspoortensis, Aloemitriformis subsp. comptonii, Crassula orbicularis, Crassula atropurpurea, C. cultrata, C. mucosa, Cyrtanthus inaequalis, Rhadamanthus montanus, Senecio citriformis, Senecio ficoides and Trichodiadema densum.
