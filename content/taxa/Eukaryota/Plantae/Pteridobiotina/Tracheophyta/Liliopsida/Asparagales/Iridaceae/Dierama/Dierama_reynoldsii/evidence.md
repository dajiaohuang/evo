## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 0.8-2.0 m high; stem solitary or 2 or 3 tufted together; corm 30 mm in diam. Leaves linear, acute; venation pronounced; radical leaves 450-950 x (3-)4-6 mm; reduced sheathing leaves 5. Flowering stem 1-2 m high. Bracts white, slightly flecked, 18-32 x 7-10 mm, elliptic-lanceolate, acuminate. Inflorescence a raceme of spikes, pendulous, with 3-8 branches. Perianth dark red, 20-30 mm long; tube 6-12 mm long; tepals 13-20 x 5-9. Anthers 7.5-10.5 mm long. Flowering time Oct.-Feb.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Occurring as solitary stems or up to three in small tufts, with pure white or lightly flecked bracts appearing silvery white, and dark wine-red flowers with perianth 17-30 mm long. Dierama dubium has much smaller flowers usually shorter than the bracts, the perianth 14-18 mm long. Purple-flowered forms of D. latifolium are distinguished by the markedly clumped habit and often broader leaves, 5-15 mm.
<!-- /evo:text -->
