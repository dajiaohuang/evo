## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, 400-600 mm tall, with lateral flower clusters sessile. Leaves 2-4, linear, channelled, trailing above. Flowers enclosed by green to partly dry spathes, yellow, tepals forming a short tube, ovary shortly stalked, style with 6 filiform arms extending between stamens. Capsules enclosed in the spathes.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The yellow flowers with a short perianth tube only 1-2 mm long distinguish Moraea brevituba from close allies in sect. Hexaglottis, particularly the widespread M. virgata, which has similar vegetative morphology but flowers with a well-developed perianth tube some 4-7 mm long, and the ovary entirely included on short pedicels 4-6 mm long. The ovary of M. brevituba is often partly exserted and the pedicels are (4-)7-10 mm long. Like other species of the section, the flowers open in the middle of the afternoon and last just a few hours.
<!-- /evo:text -->
