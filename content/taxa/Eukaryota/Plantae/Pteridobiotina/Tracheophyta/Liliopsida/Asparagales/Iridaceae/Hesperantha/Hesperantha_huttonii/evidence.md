## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 0.25-0.60 m high; corm turbinate, tunics concentric, base truncate, lacerate, cormlets often produced in axils of lower leaves; stem simple, weak. Leaves 5(6), slightly falcate, acute to subobtuse, lowermost basal, others inserted on stem, uppermost reduced to sheathing bracts. Bracts 20-40 mm long. Inflorescence a 2-7-flowered spike, ± straight; flowers bright pink; perianth tube dilated in throat; tepals subequal, elliptic, obtuse to subacute. Stamens exserted; filaments 6.0-7.5 mm long; anthers 7.5-10.0 mm long. Flowering time Jan.-June. Capsule 13-20 x 4-5 mm.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Readily recognised by the pale pink flowers with an elongate perianth tube, typically 30-39 mm long, and large yellow anthers, 7-10 mm long. Plants have fairly broad, flaccid, drooping leaves, a suberect to drooping stem, and usually a cormlet in the axils of the lower leaves. The seeds of H. huttonii are distinctive, mostly 2.5-3.5 x ± 1.25 mm wide, with membranous wings at either end, the wings each ± half as long as the seed body which is up to 1.5 mm long. Plants from coastal northern Eastern Cape province, some 250 km to the east of the main range, best match the species, but one of the collections from there appears to lack axillary cormlets and has a perianth tube 23 mm long, while another collection has flowers with a tube only 15-17 mm long. The coastal plants are provisionally included in H. huttonii until more information becomes available.
<!-- /evo:text -->
