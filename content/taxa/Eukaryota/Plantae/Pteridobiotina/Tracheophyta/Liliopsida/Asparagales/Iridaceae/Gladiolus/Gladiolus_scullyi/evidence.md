## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, 200-600 mm tall, corm with gnarled woody tunics. Leaves sword-shaped to falcate, plane. Flowers 5-8 in a flexuose, inclined spike, bilabiate, brown to yellowish, lower tepals banded yellow and purple, windowed in profile, sweetly scented, dorsal tepal arching, lower tepals pinched together below.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Recognised by the sweetly scented, dull yellowish to brownish flowers with the upper tepals windowed in profile, ± erect leaves, unspotted upper cataphylls and woody corm tunics clawed at the base and orange when young. The parapatric Gladiolus venustus has abruptly deflexed lower tepals with the lower laterals strongly auriculate at the base of the limbs and suberect upper lateral tepals not forming windows with the dorsal in profile. The two species intergrade along the Vanrhynsdorp-Calvinia axis and discrimination between them may be difficult there.
<!-- /evo:text -->
