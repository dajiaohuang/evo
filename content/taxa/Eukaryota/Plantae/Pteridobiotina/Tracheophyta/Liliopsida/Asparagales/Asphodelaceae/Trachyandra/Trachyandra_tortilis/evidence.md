## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Similar to Trachyandra zebrina in the usually coiled leaves, large cataphylls (up to 30 mm long) and upcurved styles, but differs in that the cataphylls are not striped (transversely striped in T. zebrina), the leaves are transversely plicate (spirally coiled in T. zebrina), and the fruiting pedicels recurved.
<!-- /evo:text -->
