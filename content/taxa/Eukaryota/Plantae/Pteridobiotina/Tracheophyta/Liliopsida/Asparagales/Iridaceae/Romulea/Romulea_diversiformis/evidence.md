## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, 80-200 mm tall, corm rounded at base, with curved acuminate teeth. Leaves several, narrowly 4-grooved. Flowers yellow, tepals dimorphic, the outer broader; outer bract with membranous margins and tip and inner bract with wide membranous margins; fruiting peduncles curved.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Readily recognised by its bright yellow, unmarked flowers with relatively broad, obovate tepals, the inner notably broader than the outer, and the long style, exserted 3-6 mm beyond the anthers. The numerous leaves and the whitish or colourless membranous margins to the inner and outer bracts are also distinctive. The species has been confused with Romulea luteoflora, but that species has fewer leaves (2 to 6), brown-flecked membranous margins to the inner and outer bracts, and a shorter style not exserted beyond the stamens.
<!-- /evo:text -->
