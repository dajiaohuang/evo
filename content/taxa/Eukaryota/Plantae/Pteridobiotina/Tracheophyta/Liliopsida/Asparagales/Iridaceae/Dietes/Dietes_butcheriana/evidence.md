## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, 0.8-1.2 m high; stem thick, often flexuous. Leaves distichous, erect, ensiform, coriaceous, arranged in a fan-shaped manner. Bracts alternate, spathe-like, sheathing; apices brown. Spathe 45-60 mm long, robust, apex obtuse or emarginate. Inflorescence erect with slight zigzag curvature; perianth white with yellow nectar guides on outer tepals; tepal claws speckled orange, bearded. Stamens pressed against petaloid style; filaments filiform, 6 mm long; anthers 4 mm long. Ovary green, inferior, turbinate, 10 mm long; style 3 mm long, with white, acuminate branches. Flowering time Sept.-? Capsule subglobose, 25-35 x 20 mm, subpendulous, indehiscent, walls disintegrating when mature.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Recognised by its very broad leaves, mostly 30-50 mm wide, Dietes butcheriana has white flowers closely resembling those of D. iridioides but with white rather than blue-purple flushed style branches. The large capsules, up to 35 x 20 mm, are pendent and have a smooth surface and rounded apex quite different from the beaked and wrinkled capsules of D. iridioides and are indehiscent, the walls slowly decaying to release the seeds.
<!-- /evo:text -->
