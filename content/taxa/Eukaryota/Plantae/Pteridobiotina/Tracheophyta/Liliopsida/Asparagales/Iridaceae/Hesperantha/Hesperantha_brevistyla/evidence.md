## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, up to 160 mm high. Leaves 4 or 5, lower 3 or 4 basal; blade 2-4 mm wide, firm; midrib and margins slightly raised. Flowers: 1-4 in a spike; anthers 2.5-4.0 mm long; pollen white; style branches ± 6 mm long; perianth with tube 7-9 mm long, segments 7-8 mm long and pale pink, outer ones pale greenish towards base, with darker green longitudinal veins; Feb., Mar.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
With small, pale pink flowers, the outer tepals pale greenish pink on the outside and veined dark green, Hesperantha brevistyla has unusually small white anthers, 2.2-4.0 mm long and remarkably short, suberect style branches that reach only to the base or middle of the anthers, unlike the longer, spreading style branches typical of the genus Hesperantha brevistyla may be most closely allied to a second small-flowered Drakensberg species, H. exiliflora, which has pink flowers with a perianth tube 3.5-5.0 mm long, tepals 6-8 x ± 2.5 mm, yellow anthers 2.5-3.3 mm long, and style branches 3.0-3.5 mm long, reaching to between the middle of the anthers or just beyond their tips in the closed flower. The flowers of H. exiliflora dry purple whereas those of H. brevistyla dry pale pink.
<!-- /evo:text -->
