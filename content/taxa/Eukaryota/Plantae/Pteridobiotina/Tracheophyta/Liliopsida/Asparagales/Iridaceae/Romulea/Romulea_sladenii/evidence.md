## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/2/text -->
Recognised by the white flowers with orange-yellow cup, the outer tepals flushed green or pink on the reverse, and the bell-shaped corm. The presence of secondary veins in the leaf ribs appears to be unique in the section. In the field the species is easily confused with another Gifberg endemic, Romulea toximontana, unless excavated, when the latter is readily identified by its laterally compressed, oblique corm with wide basal ridge. The two species differ slightly in their flowers, with the bright yellow anthers in R. sladenii partially exserted from the cup on filaments 5-7 mm long and the cream-coloured anthers of R. toximontana included in the cup on shorter filaments 3-5 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
