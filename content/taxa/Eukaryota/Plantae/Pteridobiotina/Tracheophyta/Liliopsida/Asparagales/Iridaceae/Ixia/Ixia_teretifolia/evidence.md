## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants mostly 1.2-1.5 m high, sheathed below by prominent, pale, dry-membranous, often irregularly torn cataphylls with age accumulating in a dense mass. Corm 12-18 mm diam., with tunics of relatively fine fibres. Stem usually with 3 to 5 short branches up to 4 mm long, branches subtended by pale, dry, attenuate scale-like bracts and prophylls 2-4 mm long. Leaves 3, lower 2 terete, ± 1/2 as long as stem, mostly 1.5-2.0 mm diam., firm-textured without evident veins when alive; uppermost leaf entirely sheathing or almost so, reaching to spike base or to lowermost branches. Main spike (1)2(3)-flowered, lateral spikes 1- or 2(3)-flowered; bracts silvery membranous, translucent with narrow, dark purple veins, ± 7 mm long, outer 3-veined and 3-toothed or 3-lobed; inner 2-veined, shallowly forked. Flowers upright to half nodding, pale blue, pale green in throat, faintly rose-scented, with bright yellow anthers prominently displayed; perianth tube broadly funnel-shaped, ± 5 mm long, narrow part ± 2 mm long; tepals spreading, patent distally, ovate, ± 12 x 6.0-6.5 mm, outer slightly wider than inner. Filaments ± 6 mm long, exserted ± 4 mm from tube; anthers 4-5 mm long, ± parallel, yellow; pollen yellow. Style dividing opposite lower 1/3 of anthers, branches ± 3 mm long, slender, extending between anthers.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
The short, widely funnel-shaped perianth tube, ± 5 mm long, flowers with outspread tepals and partly exserted filaments with prominently displayed anthers, and the several short lateral branchlets place Ixia teretifolia close to I. marginifolia. The tall stature and habitat are shared in sect. Morphixia with I. alata but the several very short branches, each with 1 or 2(3) flowers differ from that species, and recall particularly I. linearifolia. The centric leaves lacking visible veins are unique in sect. Morphixia and unusual in the genus, almost all species of which have plane, isobilateral leaves. Except for their slightly smaller size, the flowers of I. teretifolia differ very little from those of I. marginifolia and its immediate allies, which have a short, funnel-shaped perianth tube, tepals spreading distally and fully exserted anthers.
<!-- /evo:text -->
