## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous herb, up to 350 mm tall. Filaments 6-7 mm long. Style branches about 4 mm long, crests linear, 2-3 mm long. Outer tepals about 20 mm long. Inner tepals upright with long lateral cusps obtuse and slender, acute central cusp curving outwards in a wide arc. Flowers white, innertepals, claws and nectar guides yellow with brown spots.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Recognised but the moderate-sized to small white, sometimes blue to mauve flushed flowers, the outer tepals only 16-30 mm long and with a spreading to half reflexed limb 9-20 mm long, and characteristic inner tepals with the limbs consisting of three linear, subequal lobes with the inner lobe spreading and arching upward distally. It is closely allied to the Mpumalanga and Eswatini M. pubiflora, with strikingly similar but substantially larger white flowers, the outer tepals 25-30 mm long and disproportionately long filaments with a column ± 13 mm long vs. 4 to 9 mm in M. brevistyla. Moraea pubiflora also has unusual scabrid or velvet outer surfaces of the large outer tepals, whereas in M. brevistyla the reverse of the outer tepal claws is smooth or minutely papillate. The ranges of the two overlap in the Wakkerstroom area of the Mpumalanga-KwaZulu-Natal Border, but the two species remain distinct morphologically. Moraea brevistyla is parapatric in the Sentinel area of the northern high Drakensberg with the very similar M. vigilans.
<!-- /evo:text -->
