Inflorescences dense, many-flowered; bracts lanceolate, acuminate, to 35 mm long, hispid along the margins and midrib; ovary curved, subªsessile, 11-15 mm long. Flowers green. Petals simple, very obliquely triangular-ovate, obtuse, as long as the median sepal and adªherent to it. Lip porrect, tripartite; midlobe narªrowly oblong, obtuse, 4-6 mm long; side lobes linear, acute, as long as or slightly exceeding the midlobe, 4.5-6 mm long; spur inflated apically, as long as or shorter than the ovary, c. 9 mm long.
