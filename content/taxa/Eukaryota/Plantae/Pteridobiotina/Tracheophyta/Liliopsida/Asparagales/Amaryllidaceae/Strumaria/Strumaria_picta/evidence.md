## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Florally, S. picta is recognised by its widely campanulate flowers; the broad brownish red median band on the abaxial surface of each tepal; the broadened filaments and ovoid style. Vegetatively, the species is differentiated by its spreading, lorate, glaucous leaves with the margins densely fringed with minute cilia. Strumaria picta is the only species in the genus in which one to three leaves with reduced leaf blades remain hidden in the bulb. These are considered not to be comparable to the cataphylls of other species as they do not occur in a regular sequence from year to year.
<!-- /evo:text -->
