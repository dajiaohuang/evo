## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 0.9-1.1 m high; stems form small clumps; corm 10-15 mm in diam. Leaves linear, ± acute; venation pronounced; radical leaves 300-4050 x 4.0-6.5 mm; reduced sheathing leaves 3 or 4. Flowering stem 0.90-1.05 m high. Bract light red-brown with broad white margins, lanceolate, tapering, 15-25 x 6-8 mm, ± heavily flecked towards base and along midline. Inflorescence a raceme of spikes; pendulous, with 2-6 branches. Perianth magenta-pink, 18-26 mm long; tube 6-10 mm long, tepals 11-16 x 4-6 mm. Anthers 4.50-5.75 mm long. Flowering time Nov.-Feb.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
A clump-forming species with pendulous spikes of magenta-pink flowers, characteristic bracts with 6 to 9 lateral veins each side of the main vein and with the intermediate veins strongly developed, the veins all coalescing toward the base into solid tissue. The primary spike has 7 to 11 flowers. Dierama dracomontanum has the veins well-spaced with thinner tissue between them, and mostly fewer flowers in the main spike, 4 to 7, with a shorter perianth tube 5-7 mm vs. 6-10 mm long. Occasional hybrids between the two species are known from the Zuurberg and Ngeli Mtns where they co-occur.
<!-- /evo:text -->
