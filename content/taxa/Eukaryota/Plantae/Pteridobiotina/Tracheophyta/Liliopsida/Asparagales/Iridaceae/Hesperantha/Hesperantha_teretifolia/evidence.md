## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Cormous geophyte, 300-600 mm tall, corm globose, tunics overlapping. Leaves 3, terete, hollow, with fine longitudinal grooves, scabrid on the edges. Flowers 2-5 per spike, cream, light mauve outside, tepals spreading, exceeding perianth tube, open in the late afternoon and evening, then sweet scented.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Distinctive in the centric, ± terete, minutely grooved, hollow leaves with the surface between the grooves densely covered by minute papillae or extremely short, cilia-like hairs (visible only at 10x magnification). The white flowers open late in the afternoon, mostly after 16:30, and are then sweetly scented. The flowers, typical of the genus in form, are creamy white with a perianth tube 9.5 mm long, slightly shorter than the elliptic tepals.
<!-- /evo:text -->
