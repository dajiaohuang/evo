## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Terrestrial herbaceous plants with erect stems. Stem: above-ground portion excluding inflorescence 80-135 mm tall, 3.4-6.0 mm in diameter at base, vinaceous. Leaves 2, borne close to ground, frequently wilted at anthesis, 21-38 x 20-36 mm, broadly ovate, obtuse, entire, with prominent striation on underside, glabrous, pale yellowish-green to green; transition between leaves and cauline leaf sheaths abrupt. Leaf sheaths 3-4, leaving a portion of stem exposed, 22-43 x 11-23 mm, amplexicaul cupuliform, sometimes filled with water, obtuse to attenuate, apex sometimes reflexed, yellowish-pale green with maroon spots, especially dense at base. Inflorescence 42-119 mm long, 27.3-34.5 mm wide, densely congested with 6-20 flowers; bracts reflexed or partly reflexed at anthesis, 13.5-24.0 mm x 6.7-13.4 mm, lanceolate, acute to attenuate, glabrous, entire to undulate, green with maroon spots, especially around the margins and apex, and more pronounced on abaxial side. Flowers non-resupinate, white, spur tips and base of sepals and lateral petals green; sweetly scented. Ovary 5.4-7.5 mm x 3.7-5.0 mm, longitudinally ridged, minutely papillate on ridges, maroon, standing away at an acute angle from rachis. Labellum galeate, globose, with a prominent dorsal ridge, margins crisped, reflexed from apex to 90° down on each side for 0.9-1.5 mm, side of labellum forming a loose tube with sepals and lateral petals, base of labellum glabrous to sparsely papillate inside; galea aperture 3.8-4.3 mm wide and 4.1-5.7 mm tall, forward facing. Spurs 17.4-18.2 mm long, parallel to ovary. Sepals and median petals fused for 2.9-3.7 mm. Free part of lateral sepals lanceolate, acute, entire, projecting forward for 4/5 of their length, then abruptly reflexed, 9.0-10.7 mm x 4.1-4.9 mm. Free part of median sepal oblong, acute, entire to undulate, projecting forward for 4/5 of its length, then abruptly reflexed or only weakly projecting downward, 9.0-9.2 mm x 2.3-3.3 mm. Free part of lateral petals oblanceolate, obtuse, crisped, concealed by lateral sepals for about half of their width, projecting forward for 4/5 of their length, then abruptly reflexed, 9.2 mm x 2.9-4.4 mm. Gynostemium filling back of galea. Column part slightly curved away from rachis, 5.4-6.8 mm x 1.0-1.1 mm, white. Stigma projecting slightly away from rachis, obovate, emarginate, 2.1-2.4 mm x 1.7-2.2 mm. Rostellum very weakly triangular, 1.6 mm x 1.5-1.8 mm; viscidia terminal, circular, plate like. Anther reflexed and aligned with column, 1.4-1.8 mm x 1.9-2.1 mm, connective broadly retuse, not extending beyond individual anther thecae. Lateral anther appendages placed at anther base, 0.7-1.2 mm in diameter.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Morphologically similar to S. candidum, but differs from this species by having lateral sepals that overlap with the outside surface of the labellum and project at a ninety-degree angle from the median sepal instead of projecting from the same plane, and lateral sepals and petals that are approximately equal in size instead of smaller lateral petals than lateral sepals, and a median sepal that is narrower and longer than the lateral sepals instead of more or less similarly-sized.
