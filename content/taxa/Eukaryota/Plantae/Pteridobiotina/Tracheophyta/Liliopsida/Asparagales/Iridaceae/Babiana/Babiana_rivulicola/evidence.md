## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants up to 300 mm high, excluding longer leaves, growing in dense clumps. Stem simple or 1- or 3-branched, slightly angled. Leaves mostly 6 or 7, linear, 250-450 x 5-10 mm, exceeding stem, firm but arching toward ground, shallowly pleated to almost flat, smooth. Spike flexed outward with flowers initially in two ranks, mostly 6- to 9-flowered; bracts green below, dry and light brown toward tips, outer mostly up to 35 mm long but lowermost much longer, attenuate, inner ± 2/3 as long as outer, 2-keeled, forked apically, tips attenuate. Flowers zygomorphic, pale blue to blue-mauve, paler in throat, lower 3 tepals with central, white, lanceolate blotch and darker violet marks near base, darkly lined on abaxial side of throat, lightly sweet-scented; perianth tube funnel-shaped, 42-44 mm long, lower part cylindric, upper ± 10 mm expanded into wide throat; tepals lanceolate, unequal, dorsal ± 30 x 8 mm, lower 3 tepals united with upper laterals for ± 3 mm, arching outward, 25 x 6 mm. Stamens unilateral; filaments straight, suberect, ± 15 mm long, included in floral cup; anthers ± 5 mm long, pale mauve; pollen white. Ovary smooth; style dividing opposite or slightly beyond anther tips, branches ± 5 mm long, recurved distally.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Babiana rivulicola most closely resembles a second, more widespread Namaqualand endemic, B. dregei, both species having an aerial stem, smooth leaves, stem and bracts, and flowers with a long perianth tube. The leaves of B. dregei are broadly lanceolate and rigid with sharply pungent tips and the short stem is concealed among the leaves, whereas the stem reaches up to 300 mm high in B. rivulicola and the linear leaves are longer and relatively softly textured, with acute but not pungent tips. The flowers of the two species are broadly similar, ± funnel-shaped with the lower two thirds of the perianth tube cylindric, but B. dregei has a ± cylindric perianth tube (45-)50-65(-70) mm long, curved just below the apex, unlike the shorter, funnel-shaped perianth tube of B. rivulicola, 42-44 mm long. Additional floral differences include the longer anthers, ± 8 mm long and shorter style branches, 3-4 mm long in B. dregei vs. anthers ± 5 mm long and style branches ± 5 mm long in B. rivulicola. Babiana dregei occurs on dry, stony slopes close to sites where B. rivulicola grows but is in fruit when B. rivulicola comes into flower.
<!-- /evo:text -->
