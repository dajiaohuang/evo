## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Plants 150-200 mm high. Corm ovoid, ± 8 mm diam.; tunics of fine netted fibres. Stem usually with 1 or 2 short branches. Leaves 2 or 3, lower 1 or 2 basal, narrowly sword-shaped, ± as long as stem, 3-7 mm wide, main vein and margins slightly thickened, pale when dry, upper leaf sheathing lower 1/3-1/2 of stem, with a short free blade. Main spike 3- to 6-flowered, lateral branches 1- to 3-flowered; bracts membranous, translucent, 3.5-5.0 mm long, outer with 3 prominent light brown veins, 3-toothed, teeth attenuate, inner 2-veined and 2-toothed. Flowers salver-shaped, pale to bright pink with yellow throat; perianth tube filiform, 12-15 mm long, flaring in upper 1-2 mm; tepals narrowly ovate, 11-12 x 4.0-4.5 mm, inner three slightly smaller than outer. Filaments inserted at mouth of tube, 3-5 mm long; anthers 4-5 mm long, yellow; pollen yellow. Style dividing between middle of filaments and base of anthers, branches ± 4 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Somewhat unusual in sect. Ixia, I. helmei has a relatively long perianth tube 12-15 mm long and slightly longer than the tepals, and is readily recognised by this feature alone. The presence of two or three leaves, the uppermost almost entirely sheathing, the relatively long perianth tube, and the late flowering time in mid- to late October, combine to confirm its taxonomic isolation. The relatively long style, dividing just below or opposite the bases of the anthers, places it in ser. Longistylae but its immediate relationships are uncertain.
<!-- /evo:text -->
