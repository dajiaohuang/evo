## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Perennial herb, geophyte, 0.25-0.80 m high; corm depressed-globose to ovoid, tunics of elongated reticulate fibres; stem simple or 1-3-branched, sometimes slightly flexuose. Leaves linear to linear-lanceolate, (sub)erect, acute to acuminate, with strong midline vein, turning darker when dry. Bracts membranous, golden-brown, margins papery; outer tridentate; inner bidentate. Inflorescence with red, pink or orange-red, zygomorphic flowers; perianth tube funnel-shaped; tepals elliptical, obtuse, spreading, subequal in length, lower with yellow blotch in throat. Stamens curved; filaments 7-12 mm long; anthers curved, pink or yellow. Flowering time Aug.-Apr. Capsule subglobose or obovoid.

## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Recognised by its lanceolate leaves (5-)8-12(-20) mm wide with a prominent submarginal vein and funnel-shaped, red, orange-red or pink flowers, sometimes darkly veined, lacking well-developed calluses. The lower tepals, especially the median, are marked at the base with a small yellow blotch outlined with red. It is closely allied to and largely sympatric with T. gladiolaris, which blooms earlier, mainly August to November (rarely into December), and has cream- to straw-coloured or pale yellow flowers, sometimes flushed pale apricot-coloured, usually with prominent dark veins. Plants with ± 3-toothed or irregularly toothed bracts, the lower bracts sometimes acute, were separated as subsp. rubrolucens from typical T. disticha with obtuse to acuminate or sometimes 3-toothed bracts, recorded mainly from the coastal Eastern Cape and KwaZulu-Natal.
<!-- /evo:text -->
