## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Cyrtanthus debilis is easily distinguished from other Cyrtanthus species by its pink, trumpet-shaped flowers and the characteristic length and position of the stamens. In particular, the filaments are 15-30 mm long, well exserted from the throat and tightly clustered together against the lower tepals. Although the species is distinctive, its affinities with other members of the genus are not so readily apparent.
<!-- /evo:text -->
