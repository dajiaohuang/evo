## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/1/text -->
Most remarkable for its small size, Moraea alpina stands no more than 120 mm high and is distinguished by the solitary, terete basal leaf, which is absent or emergent from a separate shoot at flowering, as in M. stricta. That species blooms early in the season before the new leaf has emerged, and the leaf attached to the flowering stem is dry or absent. Moraea alpina has deep blue to violet flowers somewhat larger than those of M. stricta and ± globose capsules ± 5 mm long, whereas capsules of M. stricta are club-shaped and up to 10 mm long.
<!-- /evo:text -->
