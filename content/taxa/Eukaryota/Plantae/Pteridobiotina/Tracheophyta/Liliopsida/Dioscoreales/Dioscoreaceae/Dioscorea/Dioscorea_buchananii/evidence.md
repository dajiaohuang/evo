## morphology / claims / text

<!-- evo:text /records/catalogue-dossier/facets/morphology/claims/0/text -->
Tuber perennial, woody, irregularly shaped, often with flattened sometimes horizontal lobes, up to 30 cm long. Plant glabrous. Twining stems up to 9 m. long, terete. Leaves alternate; petiole up to 40(-60) cm long; blade very variable in shape and size, heart-shaped or triangular in outline, entire or 2-6-lobed in the lower half, with a short acute acumen, up to 12 cm long and 15-5 cm wide, often proportionally much narrower. Inflorescences directed downwards, single in the leaf-axils. Male raceme dense, up to 5(-6) cm long, often much shorter; pedicels ±2 mm long; perianth cup-shaped, ±10(-13) mm across when flattened. Stamens 6. Female inflorescences spicate, up to 35 cm long; flowers directed downward or somewhat spreading; perianth star-shaped, ±8 mm in diameter. Ovary ±8 mm long. Capsule up to 3-5 cm long and 2 cm in diameter. Seeds with a broad wing at each end joined by a narrower wing, oblong, 16 mm long, 10 mm wide.
<!-- /evo:text -->
