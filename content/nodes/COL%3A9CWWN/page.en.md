## ## A deeply divided leaf and five-ridged flower bud {#s-2d05acad47}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records ovate stipules and leaves divided deeply into two spreading lobes; both leaf surfaces are hairless. The flower bud has five ridges, and the tubular floral cup is densely velvety inside. At opening, the calyx splits into two or three lobes; the row also records ten anthers. The pod opens elastically. This regional entry lists several alternative leaf-base and texture states, without assessing population-level variation.
