## Bulbinella calcicola: narrow leaves and orange-tipped flowers {#s-ca5546487d}
A South African regional account describes a rhizomatous geophyte up to 80 cm tall. Its linear leaves are 1.5–2.0 mm wide and dry at flowering. Yellow flowers with orange tips grow on 3–4 mm pedicels along a narrowly cylindrical raceme about 10 mm in diameter.

These characters and measurements come from a regional South African flora account cited to a 2012 publication. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish variation across the full range. It does not independently classify the material as native, naturalised, cultivated or domesticated.

The account records calcrete and limestone pavements as habitats.
