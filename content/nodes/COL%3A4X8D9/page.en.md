## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-f8eb2e92dd}
The erect stems are woolly with simple, star-shaped or rusty hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned leaves are lance-shaped or ovate, with heart-shaped bases, pointed or rounded tips and toothed margins. The upper surface becomes nearly hairless and bears star-shaped hairs. Flowers may be crowded in the leaf axils or at stem tips, or solitary in the axils. The sepals are pointed and the corolla yellow. The dry fruit splits into more than ten segments (mericarps); each may have an awn no longer than 1 mm or lack one. The segments are hairless.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-1e11ad9a9f}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
