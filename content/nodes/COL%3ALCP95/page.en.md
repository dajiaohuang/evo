## A type series from southwestern Brazilian Amazonia {#s-2594f00449}
Souza and colleagues described Allobates velocicantus in 2020. The holotype is male INPAH41342, field APL21398, accession MT446461, collected along highway AC405 in Mâncio Lima, Acre, on 13 January 2019 at 211 m. Eleven paratopotypes comprise eight males and three females; another male, INPAH41350, came from the Cruzeiro do Sul–Guajará road on 15 February at 181 m. These reported type records anchor the description; a frozen accepted-name match does not independently authenticate each museum specimen.

## Adult size and the limits of the female sample {#s-10d183495c}
The ten measured males had snout–vent lengths14.9–16.2 mm, mean15.4±0.3 mm; three females measured16.0–17.4 mm, mean16.7±0.7 mm. The holotype is15.3 mm. Females were larger on average in this sample, but the authors explicitly withheld statistical confirmation of sexual size dimorphism because only three females were available. These are examined-series measurements rather than complete geographic or age-dependent variation.

## Granular dorsum, stripes and digital characters {#s-3d8e67c613}
Selected diagnostic characters are a mostly light-brown granular dorsum without large dark marks, a dark-brown lateral stripe from snout to groin, an incomplete light ventrolateral stripe, an inconspicuous tympanum, and paired dorsal digital scutes. Finger I is slightly longer than II; finger fringes and hand webbing are absent, with basal toe webbing only between III and IV. The pale dorsolateral stripe can be absent or faint in life and more evident after preservation. These selected characters are not a complete identification key.

## Leaf oviposition and a single observed courtship {#s-e0c3ff2ffc}
Natural-history notes place calling frogs10–30 cm above the ground in leaf litter of primary and secondary lowland open ombrophilous terra firme forest, near or away from small streams. One observed pair used the upper surface of a shrub leaf about20 cm high: cephalic amplexus lasted2 minutes and oviposition40 minutes, leaving13 eggs. The Methods observation date is14 February2019, separate from January type-series collection. One pair and two observed clutches do not define a complete breeding season or clutch-size distribution.

## Male tadpole transport was observed directly {#s-0db434877b}
The second reported clutch contained nine tadpoles ready for transport. Male INPAH41347 entered it and moved in circles while tadpoles climbed onto his back; the loading event lasted5 minutes. This documents male transport behaviour at that locality without establishing its frequency in all populations or subsequent survival. Newly laid eggs had a dark-gray animal pole and white vegetal pole within cloudy jelly. The paper’s leaf-surface and pigment observations are distinct from its untested radiation-protection hypotheses.
