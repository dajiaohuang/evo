## Conflicting hypotheses for deep branches {#s-c73e5a64f3}
Centipede relationships remain contested in the cited evidence: analyses using seven genes and morphology produce conflicting hypotheses for deep branches. The atlas’s 418–0 Ma display begins with the oldest catalogued fossil, *Crussolum* from the Upper Silurian, and continues to living centipedes. That fossil is a record anchor, not an origin date.

The study combines seven genes with morphological data, but the two evidence streams do not yield one settled deep topology. The atlas preserves this disagreement rather than presenting a single tree as final or turning it into a direct-ancestor sequence.

## The oldest catalogued fossil {#s-bf7187981f}
The global fossil catalog identifies *Crussolum* at Ludford Lane in the Upper Silurian, about 418 Ma, as the oldest centipede record in that catalog. The displayed span reaches living centipedes, but the fossil occurrence does not establish when Chilopoda originated.
