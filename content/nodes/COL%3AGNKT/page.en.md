## Aristea grandis {#s-9a20502d82}
Aristea grandis is a robust plant that reaches about 950 mm, with leaves 10–16 mm wide and a flowering axis bearing as many as six large clusters. Each cluster may contain up to 16 deep-blue flowers. The account highlights its smooth-surfaced, angular seeds, unlike the pitted seeds described for most other eastern southern African Aristea. Grassland is the recorded habitat. These diagnostic details come from the cited regional account.
