## Reboulia hemisphaerica: two habitat labels {#s-7fae65c996}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this taxon:

“Rupícola” (rock-associated)

and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither provides a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
