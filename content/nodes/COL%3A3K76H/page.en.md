## Soft basal leaves and small brown-bracted heads {#s-ac5e129f8e}
The description records a perennial herb with soft, glandular-hairy leaves and a simple flowering stem up to about 75 cm. Many tiny heads form a very compact cluster with straw to pale-golden bracts. The source compares it with H. nudifolium and notes its softer leaves and smaller heads; it grows on moist, rocky grassy or scrubby slopes.
