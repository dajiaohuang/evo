## Leaf surfaces, sepals and capsule characters {#s-1f0352d514}
The source entry records velutinous branches and petioles. Leaves are chartaceous, tomentose on the adaxial surface and tomentose or velutinous on the abaxial surface; margins are not revolute, and leaves are not appressed to the stem. The panicle rachis is tomentose, with partial racemes that are capitate or congested. Pedicels are tomentose or puberulent. The outer sepal apex is rounded or dorsally mucronate; the inner sepal apex is dorsally mucronate. The keel is not clawed. The style is pubescent on the basal half and suberect; the ovary is pubescent or oblong. The fruit is a two valved, loculicidal capsule with a puberulent surface. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
