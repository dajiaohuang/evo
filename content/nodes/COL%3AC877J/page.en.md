## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ccf1b11371}
COL26.8 records Pseudemys floridana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Emydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-2c427b981e}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-fc2c084f7f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The diet field classifies the entry as plant-eating (Herbivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Lagueux et al. 1995. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Lagueux et al. 1995.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f6070d004e}
The row reports 2 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
