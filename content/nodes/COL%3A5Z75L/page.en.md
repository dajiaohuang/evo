## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-074cdd13f6}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Cinnyris superbus (Shaw, 1812) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Sample scope for beak, wing and tail {#s-303def5552}
Morphology covers 7 birds: 2 female, 5 male and 0 unsexed; 4 birds have complete measurement sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 26.9 mm, tarsus 19.7 mm, unflattened wing 72.4 mm, tail 45.6 mm and hand-wing index 19.5 are historical row means. Inference=NO does not establish 7 independent records in every field; complete sets do not supply each field’s denominator. Beak length alone does not establish adaptation to particular flower species, tongue structure or pollination effects. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed.

## Keep the compiled-mass denominator {#s-cb427a6866}
Mass=16.8 g and Mass.Source=Dunning denote a compiled species average; the 7 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book and individual weights were not read, leaving sex, season and locality minimum–maximum bounds unsupported.

## Omnivore is retained within a sunbird genus {#s-bb352af68e}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Omnivore and Trophic.Level=Herbivore. Omnivore niche means using multiple food niches in relatively equal proportions; Nectarivore in other genus rows does not replace this record. Herbivore level is defined by at least 70% of food resources coming from plants, a different threshold from nectar niche. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-0b45eec680}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility. These historical categories provide neither current range-wide occupancy nor individual movement radii or routes; historical map fields are not interpreted in this batch.

## Evidence limits {#s-842c1a94a5}
This page uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two crosswalks, relevant Metadata definitions and local COL ci/ne shards. Crosswalk names and older synonyms do not guarantee equivalence between historical concepts and COL26.8; source spellings such as preussi, neergardi and coccinigaster are retained. Morphology birds, complete sets and separately compiled mass keep different denominators. Individual specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Current maps, conservation, plumage, calls, breeding, tongue structure, pollination effects, flower or prey species, observed diets and movement routes are unsupported here.
