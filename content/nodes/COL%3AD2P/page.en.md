## form {#s-f422be7dac}
Winged adults typically fly with one pair of forewings; the hindwings form balancing organs called halteres. Mosquitoes, flies and midges belong here and develop through a pupal stage.

## ecology {#s-5cfe611146}
Larvae and adults can use different foods. Flower visitors, decomposers, predators and parasites occur in this order; blood feeding and pathogen transmission are not universal traits.
