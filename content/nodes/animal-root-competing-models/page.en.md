## Why genome-scale studies disagree about the animal root {#s-aa5b821507}
Two genome-scale studies asked which living animal lineage branches nearest the rest: sponges or comb jellies. An analysis of 1,719 genes from 97 species supported sponges in that position under site-heterogeneous models; a separate study adding 27 comb-jelly transcriptomes recovered comb jellies instead. Each result has support within its own dataset and model, but the disagreement shows that sampling and evolutionary assumptions affect the inferred root. Neither analysis directly observes the first animal.
