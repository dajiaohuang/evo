## Hemipogon irwinii {#s-eb8fd2fcad}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Hemipogon irwinii* as an accepted species of *Hemipogon*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry contains only a terrestrial habitat label. This regional field gives no locality or range and includes no morphology, so it supports no broader account of the species.
