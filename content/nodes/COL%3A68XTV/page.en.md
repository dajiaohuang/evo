## *Canna indica*: red and yellow petals on tall annual stems {#s-0100a6d1bd}
The South African regional account describes a perennial herb up to 1.2 m tall, with tall annual stems wrapped by their leaf sheaths. Its flowers have three upper red petals and two lower yellow petals tipped and flecked with red. The account records the plant at disturbed sites and roadsides.
