## Syagrus itacambirana {#s-4a554fd208}
Syagrus itacambirana is described with a solitary, clustered or underground stem and green pinnate leaves. Its rigid, grouped leaflets sit at varied angles; the upright panicle bears one-sided branches. Globose yellow-green fruits have a hairy skin and fibrous, juicy mesocarp. The dataset labels the habitat terrestrial.
