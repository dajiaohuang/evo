## Zhongdian samples are separate from the Laojun lineage {#s-8223747d44}
The 2023 study identifies a Zhongdian Plateau lineage as ventripunctata and distinguishes it from the newly described Mt. Laojun lineage. Table 1 includes sequenced ventripunctata material from Xiaozhongdian and Bitahai. These are study records, not the full geographic range or proof that all older Yunnan identifications have been reassessed. The paper measured eight ventripunctata specimens for morphology; molecular and morphometric sample counts should not be combined into extra animals.

## Direct comparisons retain specimen and method context {#s-f9ebdc60f7}
Relative to laojunshanensis, the account describes ventripunctata with clearer subarticular tubercles and dark blotches on a grey-white throat, belly or limb venter. The size-adjusted comparison of eight specimens separated the samples along PC2; laojunshanensis was interpreted as having narrower upper eyelids, larger eyes and greater nostril–eye distance. These are comparative examined traits, not a complete population diagnosis, absolute size limit or functional feeding experiment.
