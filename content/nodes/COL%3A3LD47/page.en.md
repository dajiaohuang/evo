## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Heteropterys rhopalifolia? {#s-c009b61283}
Opposite leaves range from papery to leathery and oval to obovate, with wedge-shaped bases and tapering or pointed tips. Glands may be conspicuous, sunken and near the base; lower-surface hairs range from sparse wool to nearly hairless. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts and stalked pedicels. Sepals may all bear glands or only the front one lack them; the glands can extend down the stalk, and the sepals have rusty hairs. Yellow petals are thin, clawed, unscented and ragged-edged; they may stand or spread. Anthers may be upright or turned over. The front styles are longer than the rear ones, sometimes hairy at the base and pointed at the tip. The fruit body has prominent side veins.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c71637a78f}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
