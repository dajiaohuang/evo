## Source classification and concept status {#s-b0205a4f44}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2968 records *Cruziohyla calcarifer* under the source classification Cruziohyla (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging category (arboreal) {#s-407aba2ad5}
AmphiBIO records an arboreal category for this row. It is a broad category, not a fine-scale habitat description. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-dd723c1726}
The row is marked for both daytime and night-time activity. These broad categories are not a continuous behavioural record; unlisted periods remain unspecified.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm) {#s-d437f084f5}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-13862cd36b}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 87 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-dfeb92a3c6}
The row lists 10–54 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 3.8–4 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size fields may refer to eggs.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-5fe2c5cabd}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
