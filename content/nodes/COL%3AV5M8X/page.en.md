## Leigong Mountain type-locality records {#s-e897140ae5}
The male holotype CIB LS20160610002 was listed from Leigong Mountain, Guizhou, at 1571 m. The monograph examined six males and one female from that mountain and cited a known type-locality distribution at 1200–1750 m. This dated locality knowledge does not establish absence elsewhere or physiological elevation tolerances.

## New measurements kept separate from type-series figures {#s-d034e37ac2}
The newly examined six males measured 30.9–34.7 mm snout–vent length, and one female measured 37.4 mm. The parenthetical type-series figures were 30.4–38.7 mm for ten males and a printed 42.3–42.3 mm for two females. The repeated female endpoints are retained as printed, without inventing a corrected value. These samples are separate and are not pooled into an unsupported population size range.
