## Umbel-like clusters of small yellow heads {#s-df24c7f134}
This record describes an upright, nearly hairless plant arising from a tuberous root or swollen underground stem. Opposite leaves have short stalks and toothed margins. Many small, disk-shaped heads form crowded, umbel-like clusters; each has a cylindrical involucre and yellow disk florets, with no ray florets. The bristly fruits bear a pappus of free, lance-shaped scales. The habitat field says terrestrial.
