## ## A terrestrial habitat entry {#s-a4a3f15257}
COL26.8 lists *Lecointea peruviana* as an accepted species. A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 habitat field records it as terrestrial, meaning it grows on land. This is a broad habitat category, not a locality or distribution map. The reviewed fields provide no morphological description, observation date or sampling frame, so they do not support a fuller account of its appearance or geographic range.
