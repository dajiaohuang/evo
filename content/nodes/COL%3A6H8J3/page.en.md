## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-6b4da1dd33}
E. sclateri belongs to the group declining early in the Last Glacial Period and recovering later, unlike the rise-then-fall pattern of E. robustus and E. pachyrhynchus. Refugia and later contact are discussed as historical explanations, without a present breeding-colony census or evidence of current growth. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
