## Helichrysum subglomeratum {#s-142751b6f5}
## Silvery leaves and dense flattened clusters

Herman and Retief's 1997 account describes a perennial herb 80–600 mm tall. Leaves form both a basal rosette and grow along the stems; they are tongue-shaped, oblong or elliptic, clasp the stem at the base, and have silvery, silky felt on both surfaces.

Many narrow, cylindrical flower heads crowd into small, flattened, spreading clusters. The flowers are yellow; the outer bracts are brown or straw-coloured, and the inner bracts end in bright canary-yellow tips. The account records the plant scattered in stony grassland or forming small mats over exposed rock sheets.
