## Molineid nematodes in a regional literature checklist {#s-26b189ac47}
The 2025 review lists Leptodactylus latrans as a host in its Molineidae checklist, associating the written host name with Oswaldocruzia lopesi, Oswaldocruzia mazzai, Oswaldocruzia proencai, Oswaldocruzia subauricularis, Schulzia travassosi. These are literature-compiled associations across different studies, rather than a newly examined sample or an infection measured in every member of this species. The entries designate this host as the original type host for Oswaldocruzia lopesi; this concerns the parasite's description, not the type specimen of the amphibian. The cited reports and archived specimens have not been independently reidentified here. The checklist does not supply this host's examined-animal denominator, prevalence, infection intensity, pathology or mortality.

It excluded incomplete records, unpublished theses or meeting data and parasite records not identified to species. The authors also sought species-level molecular records in the literature and GenBank. Search strings and individual database search dates are not documented in the inspected methods, so omission from this list is not a reproducible negative finding. ## Rejected historical parasite identifications remain separate {#s-1a2a855cc1}
The discussion rejects the 1925 O. subauricularis report from this historical host label after a later correction by Travassos, without rejecting every other subauricularis record listed for it. It also excludes unconfirmed South American O. filiformis reports, including this host, and does not treat an O. pipiens report identified only as Leptodactylus sp. as species-level evidence for L. latrans. Paraguayan material once called O. mazzai was later assigned to O. proencai. These record-specific corrections do not establish parasite absence or validate every remaining host identification.
