## Three EltonTraits field groups {#s-581609672a}
Otomys occidentalis is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. Its same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record provides three ecological field groups with source information. The archive follows earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals; a name match does not resolve modern splits or synonym disputes.

## Relative weights of reported food categories {#s-4f64341c16}
The nonzero weights are other plant material, a source category that also includes fungi, 80%; fruit 10%; and seeds, nuts and spores 10%. Code ABC combines high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates may borrow from an ecologically similar congener. They are not necessarily direct observations of this species. EltonTraits standardizes verbal descriptions, not measured diet composition; zero does not prove a food is never eaten. Broad categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO do not mean exclusive use of nectar, seeds or plants.

## Ground is a category, not a time budget {#s-b0b0946537}
The mammalian stratum assignment is G, the ground category, whose source definition includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Metadata attributes mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). This is not a percentage of time or proof of exclusive ground use.

## Several activity periods are marked {#s-8b45e04391}
The archive marks feeding activity at night, twilight and daytime as 1. Multiple periods can be recorded together; these values are not proportions that add to a daily time budget or schedules shared across every day, season and site. Activity certainty is ABC and may still rely on a congener considered ecologically similar. Genus or family typical activity values do not fill this record.

## Keep the field-specific source labels {#s-8a521bc169}
The ecological groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum and activity may draw on different material; the same Ref number in bird and mammal tables does not identify the same source. The archive retains original strings by file, row, field group and reference table. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so labels provide traceability rather than independent observations.

## Limits of the historical ecological record {#s-43fd257f0b}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between its historical concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized relative weights from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is necessarily a direct observation of this species. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite claims. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other evidence.
