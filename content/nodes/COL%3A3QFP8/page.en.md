## Pink flowers with a dark ring and white border {#s-8520ab36b3}
Ixia metelerkampiae reaches 70 cm and has crowded pink, lilac or mauve flowers. Each tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal) bears an irregular dark-red or purple mark surrounded by white; the narrow flower tube is 2–4 mm long. The account also records cormlets or thick, rhizomes (horizontal underground stems)-like stolons (creeping stems that extend from the plant).
