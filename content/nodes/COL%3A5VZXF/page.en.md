## Stems, leaves, and fruit {#s-1dc3384c50}
The source describes a clump-forming palm with stems up to 4 m tall and black spines as long as 7 cm. Its pinnate leaves have 21–49 pairs of oblanceolate leaflets—broad toward the tip and narrower at the base—arranged irregularly in groups of two to six and at differing angles. The branched inflorescence grows between the leaves and has a spiny bract on its stalk. The fruit is recorded as flattened, globose, or beaked; its colour is black or purple, and its outer layer is hairless.

The field records clump-forming stems up to 4 m tall with black spines up to 7 cm; pinnate leaves with 21–49 pairs of oblanceolate leaflets, irregularly grouped two to six at differing angles; a branched inflorescence between the leaves with a spiny stalk bract; and flattened, globose, or beaked fruit, black or purple, with a hairless outer layer.
