## A distinctive leaf attachment {#s-ef767e532f}
The regional description records ovate, membranous leaves with a sagittate base and a red midrib. The blade is peltate: its petiole (leaf stalk) attaches to the underside within the blade rather than at the edge. The spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) has a white blade and green tube; the source lists fruit as unknown.

## A soil-associated habitat label {#s-5ee585a6fc}
The habitat field in Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 uses “terrícola” for this species. The row does not include a collection locality or date, so it cannot locate an occurrence.
