## What leaf and flower traits identify Licania gracilipes? {#s-61605dfd12}
Its lance-shaped to oblong-lanceolate leaves have woolly hairs underneath; the leaf stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in panicles and have bell-shaped receptacles. Individual flowers are stalked, with three separate stamens whose filaments remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is described as powdery.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-38469466d8}
It is categorized as terrestrial; the record gives no specific locality.
