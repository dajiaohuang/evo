## Aponogeton stuhlmannii {#s-750caec307}
The SANBI regional account describes a tuberous rhizome and narrow floating leaves; submerged, awl-shaped leaves are noted as rare. Petioles reach 170 mm, and the simple spike bears a few small white flowers with small, deciduous perianth segments. The habitat field names small water-worn pools in sandstone ridges or granite. This South African account gives no population frequency or dated sampling frame.
