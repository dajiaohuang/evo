## What does the record say about Guatteria rigida? {#s-343d971ab7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes leathery, elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and sparsely hairy below. Solitary flowers grow in the leaf axils; the stalks have sparse hairs. The free sepals are broad and triangular, and the petals are egg-shaped. Ellipsoid fruit segments contain pitted seeds.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-b745156ec1}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
