## Linking the 2015 record {#s-28312ab4af}
COL26.8 lists *Butorides sundevalli* in Ardeidae. A 2015 life-history database has a historical record with the same name and family; six selected fields have cited species-level rows. The match supports linking the records, but the older compilation may have inherited data among historical species or subspecies.

## Adult mass and the missing hatchling value {#s-da079a12b6}
The compilation summarizes adult body mass as 202.5 g. This field may combine males, females and birds of unspecified sex, so it is not a sex-specific value or a range of individual masses. The page does not add a birth or hatching mass because no such value was selected here.

## Reproductive and developmental milestones {#s-341cd14dd7}
The database reports female age at first conception as 730.5 days, offspring per litter or eggs per clutch as 3, time from laying to hatching as 22 days, and age at flight capability as 34.5 days. Some records describe the last milestone as leaving the nest. Values are medians, or means of the middle pair when the number of records is even; they summarize distinct events, not one breeding schedule.

## What the longevity value represents {#s-cf522e76ce}
The archive’s maximum-longevity field is 11.6 years, the largest value in its collected material under the source method. It is not a typical or mean lifespan, nor a survival limit for every individual. The compilation does not consistently establish whether the underlying record was wild or captive.

## References behind the fields {#s-fec8295923}
Life-history rows are linked to sources including Dunning (1992), Szekely, Lislevand and Figuerola (2007), Bennett (1986), and Meyers and colleagues’ 2009 BC Birds database. The archive retains each field’s cited row and value. Rows are not counts of measured birds, and repeated citations do not constitute independent replication.

## Limits of the life-history compilation {#s-0cbbf8207e}
These are historical database summaries, not direct observations of one bird or current population estimates. The 2015 compilation reports medians or means of the middle pair, except that longevity is the largest collected value. It may carry data across historical species and subspecies; the matching name and family do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited material. Wild versus captive conditions are not uniformly known, and the underlying publications have not been individually reviewed here. Current ecology, distribution, conservation and other missing fields require additional sources.
