## Accepted name and placement {#s-1252ccd077}
COL26.8 records Pristimantis pahuma Hutter & Guayasamin, 2015 at species rank within the genus Pristimantis, family Strabomantidae, order Anura. This identifies the name and its place in the catalogue.

## What the classification tells us {#s-2efcd7d6aa}
The bound catalogue evidence establishes the accepted name, rank and recorded placement. It supplies no species-specific observations of form, habitat, range or life history, so those traits are not described here.
