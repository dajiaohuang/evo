## What one platypus genome can compare {#s-c9de38eda1}
The draft genome of one female platypus, Glennie, combined about 26.9 million reads into a 1.84-gigabase assembly at roughly sixfold coverage. Of the sequence, 437 megabases were ordered on 20 chromosomes, and 18,527 protein-coding genes were predicted. Assembly gaps, one-individual sampling and comparative annotation limit the result: it is a genomic sample, not a reconstruction of an ancestral monotreme genome or proof that single genes caused whole-animal traits.
