## Holotype KKF494 {#s-10270e27fe}
The 2021 description names KKF494 as the holotype: the front portion of a crested lower jaw, with tooth sockets but no teeth preserved in place. The specimen is held at Kronosaurus Korner in Richmond.

## Type locality and horizon {#s-0fbfde20a2}
The type locality is reported from Wanamara Country northwest of Richmond, in the upper Albian Toolebuc Formation. Its exact position within the formation is not known.

## Debate over size estimates {#s-add13c9e20}
A 2024 study describing Haliskia revisited estimates of Thapunngaka's size. The larger wingspans were extrapolated from partial jaw material, so the result depends on the comparison species and the proportions assumed between the jaw symphysis and skull. The authors argued that evidence available at that time did not support calling it Australia's largest known pterosaur. That dated criticism does not supply missing wings or decide every later specimen; the debated estimates are not a measured typical size or species maximum.

## A second upper-jaw specimen {#s-211cd67b0e}
The later paper describes KKF600, also written KKF0600: two incomplete pieces from the front of an upper jaw. They came from Cambridge Quarry, northwest of Richmond, in the upper Albian Toolebuc Formation. They add upper-jaw material, not a complete skull or wings belonging to the holotype individual.

## Competing views on the referral {#s-d916951da0}
Richards and colleagues referred KKF600 to Thapunngaka shawi based on the form and arrangement of its tooth sockets and matching biting surfaces. They also noted that the two specimens share no overlapping anatomical part. The 2024 Haliskia study questioned the referral, citing that gap, at least three pterosaur taxa in the Toolebuc Formation, and anhanguerian diversity at other sites. The referral remains disputed rather than an undisputed source of species-level traits.

## An unresolved phylogenetic result {#s-c2a1fc727e}
In the later study's strict consensus tree, Thapunngaka, Mythunga, and Ferrodraco form an unresolved three-way branch named Mythungini. The result records that analysis; it does not establish which of the three is the other's closest relative.

## PBDB name and record counts {#s-61e078b533}
The fixed PBDB pterosaur roster accepts Thapunngaka shawi under the parent name Thapunngaka. This guide includes two eligible species-level occurrence records, two collections, and two reference identifiers. Each count measures a different kind of database record, not specimens or animals.

## Selected collection records {#s-5078ed9d23}
Cambridge Quarry, Morungle Station (Queensland; region code AU) is recorded in the Toolebuc Formation with an age bracket of 113.2–100.5 Ma and an early-interval label of Albian. Free Fossil Hunting Site 1 (Queensland; AU) is also listed with Toolebuc, an age bracket of 106.3–100.5 Ma, and an early-interval label of Late Albian. The locality, region, and stratum names follow the database wording. These selected records do not establish the species' full distribution or absence from other regions.

## Age bounds in the selected records {#s-502071f268}
Together, the selected records have an outer age envelope of 113.2–100.5 Ma, where Ma means millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound; it is not a continuous fossil record or a measured species origin, extinction, or individual lifespan. Each collection keeps its own broader interval, and this envelope does not revise the original dating interpretations.

## What the records can show {#s-f2db8980b3}
The holotype is a partial lower jaw, the proposed upper-jaw referral is contested, and large size estimates depend on extrapolation. The phylogenetic result is unresolved. Two selected collections do not establish a continuous range or abundance; their age bounds are not exact specimen dates. The available records do not establish diet, reproduction, or behavior.
