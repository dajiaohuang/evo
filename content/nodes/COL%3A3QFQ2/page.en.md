## Pale flowers and coarse corm fibres {#s-c66b6c0892}
Ixia paucifolia has pale beige to creamy-white flowers with a cylindrical tube, usually 25–35 mm long, and narrow tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 11–15 mm long. Its coarse, wiry corm tunics (the fibrous outer layers around the underground corm, a swollen storage stem) are one feature that distinguishes it from the similar I. dolichosiphon.

## Stony mountain slopes {#s-3b17b02e9f}
The cited accounts describe its habitat differently: one diagnostic treatment associates it with clay and shale slopes, while a regional habitat entry lists stony middle to upper mountain slopes. Neither gives an elevation interval.
