## Stem surface {#s-e4d25d3ed8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes stem hairs as hirsute or tomentose (hairy or densely matted). Simple hairs are present and may point forward or backward while lying close to the surface. Unbranched bristles are thread-like; branched bristles are listed as star-shaped, scale-like, or feathery. Glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-b6cdf897ec}
Stipules are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries and projections between pairs of pinnae as absent. The inflorescence is cylindrical; floral bracts are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-7ee2401af6}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous, and its lobes are pappus-like, resembling a tuft of fine bristles or scales. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are glabrous. The androecium is isostemonous, with stamens equal in number to the petals; the filaments are yellow.

## Habitat category {#s-4c010c7e91}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
