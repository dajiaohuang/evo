## Historical names and sample {#s-2712af301e}
COL26.8 accepts Zosterops citrinella in Zosteropidae. The AVONET v7 entry uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 7 measured individuals, 6 with a complete morphological set. Trait-specific sample sizes may differ.

## Beak dimensions {#s-c1ba6fb1df}
The mean length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 7 mm; width and depth at that landmark average 2.8 mm and 3.4 mm. They are distinct dimensions at standardized positions, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-53c5820419}
Unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary averages 57.5 mm; tarsus length averages 17.4 mm. The compiled mean mass is 11.5 g, classed as Updated_literature. It comes from separate species-average information, not from weighing all 7 morphological specimens.

## Habitat and broad feeding category {#s-462fc38263}
The AVONET categories are Shrubland for habitat, Omnivore for trophic niche and Insessorial for primary lifestyle. Insessorial describes a chiefly perching way of life. These are broad comparison categories, not a claim that the bird uses only shrubland or eats all foods in equal amounts.

## Migration and historical range summary {#s-73a6523e1f}
The migration category is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints −16.76° to −6.63°. They do not give a full account of non-breeding movements or establish current occupancy throughout the mapped range. Geography and morphology come from separate compilations.

The EltonTraits entry assigns 50% relative weight to invertebrates and 50% to fruit. Its code A records high compiler confidence in the source and diet coding; it is not a direct measurement of what this bird ate. The percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition, and a zero in a category would not prove that the food is never eaten.

## Feeding positions in the vegetation {#s-7812ec4da8}
The species-level foraging record assigns 50% to the canopy or just above it, 20% to understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m, 20% to trees or tall bushes from 2 m up to the canopy, and 10% to the ground. These standardized weights estimate relative use of feeding positions; they are not nest sites, a full habitat description or a whole-day time budget. The categories are not elevation bands and cannot be used to calculate flight height or habitat area.

## Scope of these historical summaries {#s-f0d3f37ab2}
The AVONET means describe a sample and are not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation; the mass has a separate source. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, and the latitude summary covers historical breeding and resident maps. The EltonTraits percentages are standardized estimates from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of intake, time or energy. Same-name links and historical crosswalks associate these records with COL26.8 but do not prove equivalent species concepts. The entries do not establish local or seasonal variation, current distribution, population trends or conservation status.
