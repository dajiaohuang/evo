## Parabembras robinsoni {#s-ee3047d17b}
## A combination of diagnostic spines

The 2018 genus revision distinguishes *Parabembras robinsoni* by four features: 11 spines in the first dorsal fin; a distinct knob where the lower jaws meet at the midline; two spines before the eye; and one lachrymal spine, on the bone at the front of the eye. These are selected characters from the revision's examined and compared specimens, not a complete account of anatomy or variation.

## A regional range summary

The same revision summarizes *P. robinsoni* as occurring in the southwestern Indian Ocean from South Africa to Mozambique. This is a regional summary of cited records, not a dated, georeferenced inventory and not evidence of present occupancy at every locality.
