## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-12bdc5bc0c}
COL26.8 records Tricholaema at genus rank, with Lybiidae as its direct parent.

This part contains six Tricholaema species pages with AVONET measurements: T. diademata, T. frontata, T. hirsuta, T. lacrymosa, T. leucomelas and T. melanocephala. The route follows pinned COL26.8, while each page retains the historical AVONET name and its own sampling details.

## Differences between records {#s-4bead69007}
The lowest and highest compiled species-level mass values in these records are 20.45 g for T. melanocephala and 55.2 g for T. hirsuta. This is a comparison across species, not a range of individual weights. The AVONET rows include Forest, Woodland and Shrubland habitat labels and Frugivore, Omnivore and Invertivore niche labels. Check the individual pages before treating any category as characteristic of a particular species.

## Amniote Life-history archive member records {#s-38d54d76c2}
One historical life-history account is linked under Tricholaema in this part: T. leucomelas. It has one entry each for adult body mass, clutch size, incubation duration and fledging age. These counts describe the available archive fields, not a complete life-history profile; check each field's unit, event definition and source before comparing it.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-216c6f64d9}
The six measured pages are this part's authored records, not a complete list of accepted Tricholaema descendants. The mass endpoints compare compiled values between species and say nothing about within-species variation. The single life-history account does not establish a shared reproductive pattern across the genus. Historical tables may report medians or inherited species/subspecies values, and the original input studies have not all been reviewed here. Current range, biology and status require further evidence.
