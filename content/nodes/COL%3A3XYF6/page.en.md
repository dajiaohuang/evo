## *Maranta parvifolia*: a densely silky ovary {#s-00434155c2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes oval leaves with a hairy pulvinus, the joint between the leaf stalk and blade. Loosely arranged bracts surround the flowers. The sepals are longer than the straight corolla tube, and the ovary—the part that develops into the fruit—is densely covered with silky hairs.

Two calluses are recorded on a sterile stamen-like part called a staminode. The fruit is ellipsoid, and the sepals remain upright after flowering.
