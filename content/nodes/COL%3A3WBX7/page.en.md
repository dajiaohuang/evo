## Lucilia lycopodioides {#s-237fa5e308}
## Silver-gray leaves held against upright stems

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright stems bearing lance-shaped to triangular leaves pressed close to the stem. Both leaf surfaces are the same silver-gray color. The inner bracts around each head are recorded as white or brown. Hairs on the dry fruit are described as double or covering hairs. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional record gives no locality or complete range.
