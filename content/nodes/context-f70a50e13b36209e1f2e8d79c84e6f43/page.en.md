## Source classification and concept status {#s-bcbf7d3c1d}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3144 records *Hyperolius guttulatus* under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-5d75cf472a}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-791e55a91b}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-49055a5c80}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 37 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-9c5bf22d3b}
AmphiBIO reports both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch as 250, and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. The row does not report an offspring-size value. These counts are literature-compiled; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-fbe726ee64}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
