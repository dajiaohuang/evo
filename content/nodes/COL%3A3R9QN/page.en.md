## *Klotzschia rhizophylla*: a basal rosette with three to five shallow lobes {#s-b5d61b033e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character field describes smooth stems without star-shaped hairs. Its leaves are crowded in a basal rosette, with three to five inconspicuous lobes. The habitat label is terrestrial; this regional record gives no named locality or date.
