## A class name anchored by one named fossil sample {#s-91b685f1ca}
Ophiuroidea includes brittle stars and basket stars, but the fossil evidence summarized here is narrower: the systematic study identifies Stenaster material as an ophiuran at class level. A database occurrence independently places the sample in Utah's Wah Wah Formation. This supports the group's presence in that documented setting; it does not describe every branch of the class or identify a direct ancestor.

## The Floian age is a stage ceiling {#s-cd677e32bc}
The Wah Wah sample is assigned to the Floian, whose older boundary supplies the displayed 477.1 Ma ceiling. That boundary is not a point date on Stenaster or a measured first appearance of all brittle stars. The study does not establish class-wide diet, habitat preference, body size or locomotion, so ecology is kept at the level of the named marine formation rather than filled in from living relatives.
