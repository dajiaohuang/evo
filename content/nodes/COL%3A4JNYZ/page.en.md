## *Platycaulos galpinii*: flowers shorter than their bracts {#s-b04be31889}
Its wiry, wrinkled stems reach 20–50 cm. A spikelet is a compact cluster of flowers; male and female spikelets differ in number and size. The regional description compares it with *R. sejunctus*: *P. galpinii* has fewer, larger spikelets, larger bracts with unbroken edges, and flowers shorter than the bracts. Bracts are the small leaf-like structures around flowers.

The account places it on grassy basalt slopes and in montane grassland at 2,100–2,750 m, listing Drakensberg Grassland and Sub-Escarpment Grassland.
