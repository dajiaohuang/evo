## Growth marks in tyrannosaur bones, and the limits of a curve {#s-6439eae522}
Growth marks in tyrannosaurid bones provide the direct observations in this study's specimen series, which includes FMNH PR 2081. Researchers used estimated ages and body masses to fit growth curves; the reported peak Tyrannosaurus growth rate of about 2.1 kilograms per day is one such estimate, conditional on missing marks and body-mass equations. The specimens span tyrannosaurids from roughly 83 to 66 million years ago, not one population, and the fitted curve is not a universal growth schedule for dinosaurs.
