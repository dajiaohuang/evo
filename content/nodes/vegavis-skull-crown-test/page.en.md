## A new skull tests Vegavis's place among birds {#s-1627140074}
The nearly complete skull AMNH FARB 30899, dated to 69.2–68.4 Ma, provides direct anatomical evidence for Vegavis. A 2025 combined analysis supports placement among crown waterfowl, while earlier studies placed it among stem anatids, stem anseriforms or outside Aves; crown-group membership therefore remains a topology-dependent hypothesis.
