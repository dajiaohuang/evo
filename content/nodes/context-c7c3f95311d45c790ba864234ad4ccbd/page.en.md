## The source concept {#s-d20fb3c8cf}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Frostius erythrophthalmus* in row Anf0604 under Frostius, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-efbba2e02d}
For Anf0604, the compilation tags terrestrial as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-ca89e5f92f}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-00364e742d}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 25.6 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
