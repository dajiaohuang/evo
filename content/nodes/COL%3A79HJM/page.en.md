## *Sanchezia tigrina* {#s-0f12f10747}
The field describes oblong leaf blades with tapered bases and long-pointed tips. The inflorescence branches near the base, with three or four flowers per node along one side of the main axis. Outer bracts are ovate and at least 12 mm long; inner bracts are oblong. The calyx is 15–19 mm long with oblong, blunt-tipped lobes; the corolla is red to orange. These are source-recorded characters.
