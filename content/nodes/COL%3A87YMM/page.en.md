## Begonia hoehneana {#s-1be2af8703}
## Simple hairs on both surfaces of a creeping begonia

Begonia hoehneana has prostrate stems and persistent stipules. Simple hairs occur on its petioles and on both surfaces of the slightly asymmetric, toothed kidney-shaped leaves. The male and female flower segments are hairless; female margins are entire and bracteoles are present. Capsule wings are nearly equal, and the seed tips rounded. The source labels the habitat rocky.
