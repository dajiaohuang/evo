## What range of leaf and seed traits is recorded for Turnera melochioides? {#s-4e93a9def0}
The branches bear simple hairs and stalked glandular hairs with rounded heads. Stipules may be reduced to glands; petioles are present, and extrafloral nectaries may occur or be absent. Leaf shapes range from ovate and lance-shaped to elliptic or linear; margins vary from toothed to nearly entire. The upper surface may be sparsely hairy or bristly, the lower woolly or densely bristly. Inflorescences may be racemes or single flowers borne on leaves; peduncles and bracteoles are present, but pedicels are absent. The corolla is yellow, without a corona; filaments join the floral tube at the base. Styles are heterostylous. The fruit exocarp is smooth. Seeds are net-veined, with smooth or papillose outer layers and blunt or slightly raised chalazae.
