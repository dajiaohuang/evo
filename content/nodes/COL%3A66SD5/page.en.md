## What variation does the record give for Annona tomentosa? {#s-c82b50c412}
The field describes a smooth-barked tree with entire leaves; leaf tips range from pointed to rounded. Flowers may grow above or below a leaf axil or at branch ends. They have six yellow or cream petals, and the sepals are slightly joined at the base. The aggregate fruit is syncarpous; seeds are brown and smooth.
