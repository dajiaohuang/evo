## Mikania anethifolia {#s-8f0abeab7b}
## Whorled leaves on an upright stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright, smooth branches with shallow lengthwise lines and solid pith. Its lance-shaped leaves grow in whorls and are leathery, with narrowing bases, blunt tips, and smooth upper and lower surfaces. The margin is recorded as entire or deeply divided into lobes that are themselves divided; the veins are pinnate and loop toward the edge.

Loose panicles carry stalked heads. Long, narrow bracts occur near the top of each head stalk; the bracts around a head are elliptic to oblong, smooth, and recorded with both tapered and blunt tips. The bell-shaped corolla widens beyond its tube; its triangular lobes are tapered and glandular. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. A separate habitat field says terrestrial; this regional record gives no locality or complete range.
