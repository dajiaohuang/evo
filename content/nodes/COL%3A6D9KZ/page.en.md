## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-8dea8afe9a}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records hairless leaves that turn black when dry. They are more than 5 mm wide, have petioles up to 2.5 mm long, blunt bases and flat to slightly rolled-under margins. Secondary veins fade before reaching the edge; at least two occur on each side, with no small hair tufts in their axils. The floral prophyll (a small bract below the flower) reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. The epicalyx segments, an extra sepal-like ring below the calyx, are either 0–1.5 mm or 2–11 mm long, shorter than the sepals, and bend downward or spread outward. The corolla is 1–3 cm across and has 12 stamens. These alternatives are listed in a regional database field without frequencies.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-dc761c0c6f}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat, with no locality or collection date.
