## How does this perennial spread and flower? {#s-dd99290729}
Nemesia umbonata is described as a tufted perennial up to 40 cm tall that spreads through underground runners. Its narrow leaves grow in a dense terminal cluster. The flowers are lilac-pink, with a yellow palate and orange-yellow patches at the mouth of the spur.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-804fdbe8f2}
The habitat field lists grassland, without a more specific locality.
