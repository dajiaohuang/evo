## Limits of the historical life-history and ecology records {#s-ce17adc926}
The life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 archive. Except for maximum longevity, its summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity uses the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespan or a current population survey. The compilation may share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the same-name sparse rows support this association, but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove every inherited component. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and the underlying publications were not individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields need other evidence.
