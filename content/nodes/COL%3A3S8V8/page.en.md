## Narrow tepals and a spiny corm rim {#s-e0ca5382aa}
Lapeirousia tenuis grows 70–250 mm tall and has blackish corm tunics (fibrous outer layers around the swollen underground storage stem) with horizontal spines along the rim. Its lilac to mauve flowers have narrow tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) that spread from the tube; flowering is recorded from July to early September.
