## Prostrate stems and finely divided leaves {#s-9abeeea786}
This account describes prostrate or reclining stems that are woody near the base and herbaceous above. Smooth leaves occur along the stems and are divided into narrow, comb-like lobes. Yellow radiate heads form corymb-like clusters; the ribbed fruits are silky or softly hairy and bear a persistent white pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
