## What stem, leaf and flower traits does the regional field describe? {#s-a5c5bd2504}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root and erect, four-angled branches densely covered with short glandular hairs. Opposite leaves are arranged in crossing pairs; they are ovate with pointed tips, abruptly narrowed bases, a thick texture, and scalloped, hair-fringed margins. Three main veins arise from the leaf base. Terminal panicles bear flowers with four sepals and four lilac petals. Each stamen has two awn-like appendages. The fruit is a three-chambered capsule, and the seeds are coiled. These are the field's regional traits, not a global account.
