## Stature and flowers {#s-9f57262c82}
Codd's 2005 regional account describes plants growing singly or in small clusters. The rigid leaves stand upright or curve slightly like a sickle and measure 150–280 × 4–12 mm, with a shallow keel. The flowering stalk rises above the leaves and is 250–650 mm tall. Its dense, cylindrical raceme measures 40–90 × 10–15 mm. Brownish buds stand upright; the flowers range from upright to spreading, are dull yellow to brownish, dry purplish brown, and are slightly scented. The ovate to ovate-deltoid, pointed bracts are 4–5 mm long, with finely eroded, toothed edges. Pedicels reach 0.5 mm. The bell-shaped to nearly top-shaped perianth is 4–5 mm long; its ovate lobes are 1.0–1.5 mm long and do not spread. At flowering, the stamens project 2 mm beyond it. The fruit is globose, but mature fruits were not seen in the account.

These measurements and observations come from a regional species account published in 2005; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Comparisons in the account {#s-dcf1705794}
Codd compares the species with *K. typhoides*, describing *K. brachystachya* as smaller, with shorter and narrower leaves and somewhat smaller flowers, 4–5 mm long. Its bracts are ovate and pointed, while those of *K. typhoides* have rounded tips. The account names *K. buchananii* as the only other species with flowers this small; that species has white flowers, lance-shaped, tapering bracts, and grass-like leaves measuring 400–600 × 2.5–4.0 mm.

These comparisons come from the same regional species account published in 2005; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
