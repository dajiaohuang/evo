## Lagarosiphon major: submerged leaves and recorded waters {#s-42b3843a6b}
A 1997 South African regional account describes Lagarosiphon major as a submerged aquatic herb. Its leaves are thick and opaque, 2–3 mm wide, with small, somewhat papillose-rhomboid cells. Leaf teeth are minute, blunt and ascending.

These traits come from a regional species account cited to 1997. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish the full range of variation.
