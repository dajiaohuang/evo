## Renqingbeng type-locality knowledge {#s-c2744a769b}
Male holotype CIB MT171053 was listed from the vicinity of Renqingbeng Temple, Medog, Xizang, at 2003 m. Citing Shi and colleagues (2020), the monograph recognized the species only from this type locality at approximately 2000 m. The reported locality and elevation do not establish a broader surveyed range, physiological limits or absence elsewhere.

## A diagnosis based on three original adults {#s-6b189a89fb}
Based on the original description, the diagnosis reported one adult male at 23.0 mm snout–vent length and two adult females at 23.5–23.9 mm. It described a weak vomerine ridge without teeth, no subarticular tubercles, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow lateral fringes. The breeding male had a subgular vocal sac without nuptial pads or spines. The single male is not a male size range, and these three adults do not support a population-wide estimate of sexual size differences.
