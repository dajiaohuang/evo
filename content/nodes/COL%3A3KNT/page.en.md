## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-319b9bc8f5}
COL26.8 records Cerastes at genus rank, with Viperinae as its direct parent.

Pinned COL26.8 places Cerastes in Viperidae. ReptTraits v1-2 includes three same-name, same-family species records under this genus: Cerastes cerastes, C. gasperettii and C. vipera. The workbook retains its 2023 name framework; these three records describe this source set, not the genus's accepted-species count.

## What do the reported lengths measure? {#s-3485537e92}
Among the cited size entries, the reported maximum snout-to-vent length (SVL) is 214.6 mm for Cerastes vipera and 547 mm for C. gasperettii. The values come from different species; they do not describe variation within one species or the actual lower and upper bounds for the genus. Other traits need their own measurement definitions and sources.

## What does the historical life-history archive contain? {#s-834ba44553}
The August 2015 life-history archive has two same-name entries, for Cerastes cerastes and C. gasperettii. Both include records for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, oviposition-to-hatching time, and the compilation's longest-lifespan record. Two entries describe this archive's coverage, not all accepted Cerastes species. Check each event, unit and source method before comparing values.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-3917f97f54}
The trait route includes entries with at least four source-supported focal traits plus a size or reproductive value; the historical archive route includes records with at least three supported focal fields. These filters describe the material summarized here, not a complete list of Cerastes species. Maximum lengths are compiled reports, not averages or identification thresholds. Historical records can inherit taxonomic data, and the name match does not prove equivalence with the pinned COL26.8 concept. This page does not provide current conservation or population status, and the records do not establish one reproductive mode or life history for every member of the genus.
