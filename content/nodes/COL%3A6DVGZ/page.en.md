## Narrow leaves with variable margins {#s-5a06c56391}
The morphology record allows for cylindrical or flattened stems, with or without branches, and silky hairs. Linear-lanceolate leaves are thin, pointed and taper toward the base; their edges may be entire or prickly. Flower-head clusters divide along a single axis, and the heads may be on short or long stalks. The involucre has awl-shaped bracts, while the heads themselves become nearly smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
