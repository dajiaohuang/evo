## Rhynchosia minima {#s-208bf6419e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Rhynchosia minima* as an accepted species of *Rhynchosia* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama archive retrieved by WFO MBG on 2026-09-08 describes *Rhynchosia minima* as an herbaceous to subshrub vine with three-part leaves; its leaflets are 2.0–3.8 cm long and 1.0–3.0 cm wide. Yellow flowers with purple stripes are 5–7 mm long, and the pods 0.9–1.5 cm. The distribution field calls it nearly cosmopolitan, from Mexico and Central America south to Argentina.
