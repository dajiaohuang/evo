## How do the male and female structures differ? {#s-367efbf3c8}
A 2012 regional account describes *Staberoha cernua* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 60 cm, with simple culms (stems). Its female flowers have finely cut, wing-like edges; the male spikelets (small flower clusters) are numerous and 5–7 mm wide. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Which sandstone slopes drain well? {#s-b9a8ff7dca}
The account lists sandstone slopes that are usually well drained. This regional habitat description does not establish a complete range.
