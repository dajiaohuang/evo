## How does its description follow the plant from leaves to seed? {#s-51248b731c}
Hyptis recurvata is described as a slender annual herb, lightly hairy and up to 1 m tall. Its ovate-to-elliptic leaves reach 3 × 2 cm, and the roughly 1 cm flower heads sit on slender stalks. The tubular calyx curves back above its midpoint; the white corolla and protruding stamens and style are also noted. The description records four pale-brown, oblong-ovoid nutlets. It gives no sample size or estimate of variation among populations.

## What regional range was recorded? {#s-81ad8870bc}
The 2013 Flora of Panama field reports parts of Central America and northern South America. This is a historical regional statement in the source version retrieved on 8 September 2026, not a current range survey or global inventory.
