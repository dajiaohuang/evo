## Eriocephalus scariosus {#s-de45b206ea}
## Variable leaf hairs and aromatic foliage

This slender shrub is described as 0.5–1.5 m tall, with alternate, narrow leaves that are slightly succulent and silvery to bright green. The leaf hairs vary from dense and silky to nearly absent; a SANBI account says plants in far-northern Namibia are more sparsely hairy or nearly glabrous. Radiate heads are solitary or racemose, with white ray florets and woolly marginal paleae. One source calls it probably the most aromatic Eriocephalus. Its habitat is rocky hillsides.
