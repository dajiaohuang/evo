## Haemanthus amarylloides: leaves and flowers {#s-04633df7a5}
A 2012 regional account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 25 cm. It records two leaves, dry at flowering, ranging from erect to prostrate; they are strap-shaped, hairless, flat and pinkish toward the base. The spreading flowers range from pale to dark pink. There are 4–9 thin-textured pink bracts.
