## Platynereis dumerilii {#s-b678c2ec8c}
## Reproduction in controlled culture

Under the study's controlled culture system, mature *Platynereis dumerilii* spawned and produced viable embryos. The authors used those embryos in experiments and to replenish the cultures. This demonstrates reproduction in the reported laboratory conditions; it does not measure reproductive timing or rates in wild populations.

## Platynereis dumerilii {#s-d6b72ac603}
## Culture sites and seawater conditions

The cultures were maintained at the Marine Biological Laboratory in Woods Hole, Massachusetts, and experiments were also conducted at Max Perutz Labs in Vienna. The reported animals were held in full-strength natural filtered seawater: salinity was 32–34 ppt at Woods Hole and 34–35 ppt for the Vienna experiments. These settings describe the study's laboratory system, not the species' wild habitat.
