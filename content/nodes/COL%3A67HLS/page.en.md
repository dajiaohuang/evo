## Form described in the flora record {#s-3ccf1d7ca5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a twining stem, with cataphylls (scale leaves) and prophylls (the first leaves on a shoot) entire along the stem. Petioles (leaf stalks) are smooth and channelled, with sharp margins above and rounded margins below. Leaf blades are entire (undivided, with unbroken margins), flat, and ovate, with an acute tip; the base is recorded as obtuse, rounded, or cordate. Glandular dots are absent. No primary-vein character is recorded; secondary veins are obscure above, while a collecting vein (running along the leaf margin) is present along the full upper edge.

The inflorescence is smaller than half the leaf size. Its peduncle (inflorescence stalk) is recorded as cylindrical or keeled. The field does not characterize the spathe (floral bract) surface, shape, or angle to the peduncle. A stipe (supporting stalk) is recorded as shorter than the spadix (fleshy flower-bearing axis) or absent. Berries are green or orange.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.

This is a habitat category, not a collection locality; the field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.
