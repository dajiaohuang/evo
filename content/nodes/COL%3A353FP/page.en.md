## ## Climbing stems and obovate fruit segments {#s-d8f4362a9d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a climbing plant with cylindrical branches bearing hooked hairs and no xylopodium. The alternate leaves have three lanceolate to oblong leaflets. Pseudoracemes bear lilac flowers, and the stamens are partly joined in a pseudo-monadelphous androecium. The fruit is a loment, a segmented legume; its obovate articles have a central constriction, do not twist and carry hooked hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
