## Peltogyne purpurea {#s-1ccac09588}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Peltogyne purpurea* as an accepted species of *Peltogyne* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama account in a WFO MBG archive retrieved 2026-09-08 describes a tall forest tree, often in places with a dry season, with two leaflets per leaf, each 5–7 cm long and 2–3 cm wide. It explicitly says the flowers were not described or examined. The distribution field reports southern Darién, Panama, and marks Costa Rica with a question mark.
