## Cocoon traits under controlled conditions {#s-efff399d2e}
A controlled study tested spinning Bombyx mori larvae from one four-way hybrid cross. Ninety larvae were assigned to nine temperature and humidity groups. Temperature treatments affected cocoon morphology, stiffness and strength; relative humidity affected cocoon colouration. The experiment used domesticated silkworms reared in the laboratory. Its measured outcomes apply to those groups, not to wild populations or every B. mori strain. The cited methods do not give the experiment’s location or dates.
