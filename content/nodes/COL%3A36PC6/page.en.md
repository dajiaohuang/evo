## What leaf and floral characters does the flora list? {#s-b414c3f4b3}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records hairless leaves that do not blacken when dry. They are sessile, more than 5 mm wide, with a notched or stem-clasping base and flat margins. Several main veins converge toward the leaf tip; at least two lateral veins occur on each side, without hair tufts in the vein axils. A small bract below the flower reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 7–47 mm long. Epicalyx segments—the extra sepal-like ring below the calyx—are 2–11 mm long, shorter than the sepals, and spread outward. The corolla is 3.5–7 cm across; the stamen count is listed as 18 or 19–55. These are regional database alternatives, not measurements from one specimen.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-e905afae7b}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; no locality or collection date is given.
