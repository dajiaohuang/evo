## Six historical ecological entries {#s-e2f4ce5ebd}
The Myospalacinae route includes 6 historical ecological entries. Five named examples are Eospalax fontanierii, E. rothschildi, E. smithii, Myospalax psilurus and M. aspalax. Food-category weights, foraging strata and mammalian activity periods are each available in 6 entries. These counts describe the historical set, not all accepted species or ecology shared across the group; compare sources, coarse weights and categorical or binary codes separately.

## Scope of the historical ecological route {#s-9c0d88ccd3}
The route covers same-name, same-family historical entries with at least two usable ecological field groups; it is not a complete descendant catalogue. Food types, estimated weights and activity patterns in descendant records are not thereby traits of the whole group. Semiquantitative conversion, historical taxonomy and possible congeneric borrowing in ABC fields limit interpretation of the individual records.

## One PanTHERIA account {#s-f2877d216f}
The local PanTHERIA route includes one usable data account: [Myospalax myospalax](Myospalax/Myospalax_myospalax/page.en.md). It retains its historical MSW05 name, variable-specific conditions and missing data. Navigation follows pinned COL26.8; a historical table row is not the current accepted-species denominator.

## Limits of the PanTHERIA comparison {#s-574e474f32}
This guide covers represented entries with at least one focal field and a same-name, same-family, one-to-one historical association; it does not cover every accepted descendant. Comparison endpoints are consolidated historical values, not individual extremes, and fields may use different source conditions. These values do not establish current species concepts, distribution or conservation.
