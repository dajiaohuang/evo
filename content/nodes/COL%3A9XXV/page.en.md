## Adelonema erythropus: a regional morphology record {#s-bfa6a8862f}
The 2020 Brazilian Flora entry records short stem internodes. Leaves are described as membranous, uniformly colored and oval to oval-elliptic, with cuneate bases; lobes are marked not applicable, and petioles are sparsely prickly. The inflorescence has a straight apex and is described as greenish to brown; its spathe is under 10 cm. The flower field codes the locules as “4/3” without explaining whether those are alternatives or a combined value.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.
