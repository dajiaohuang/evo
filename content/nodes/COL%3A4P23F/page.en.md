## *Pseudolycopodiella meridionalis*: prostrate stems near defined strobili {#s-f730354835}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 character record, the stem lies prostrate and branches unevenly. Leaves near the strobili—the cone-like structures that carry sporangia—are close together, and the foliage has more than one shape. Leaves bearing sporangia differ from the ordinary leaves.

The strobili are clearly defined, and the spores have rough surfaces. The source lists both rock and soil as substrates but provides no named locality or date; these regional fields do not establish a full range.
