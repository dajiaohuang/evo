## Unequal leaf pairs on a compact succulent shrub {#s-0bbd5754bc}
This account describes a densely branched succulent shrub up to 15 by 20 cm, rising from a short trunk about 1 cm across. The paired leaves differ in shape and bear papillae; the second pair is joined for about half its length. A cup-shaped structure sits beneath the calyx, which has four sepals. Petals and thread-like staminodes are white. The fruit has five to seven chambers. It grows in shallow soils, often with quartz pebbles.
