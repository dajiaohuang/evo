## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Mascagnia affinis? {#s-d92b4cea97}
The flat, papery leaves are lance-shaped or ovate, with heart-shaped bases, pointed tips and sparse woolly hairs below. The main flowering branch bears a solitary corymb; its peduncle is 1.5–8 mm long at flowering, and bracteoles attach at its tip. Petals are lilac or pink, and the style tip is blunt. The fruit has both a dorsal wing and lateral wings, all developed.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-2a1f3525fb}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
