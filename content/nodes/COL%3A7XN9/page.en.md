## Collective elicitation and scope of correspondence {#s-59a1e1db9e}
Thirteen native isiZulu-speaking volunteers from five Zululand areas participated in two2016 workshop groups. Photograph-assisted discussions yielded six broad folk categories; later species naming combined those categories, published names and guidelines with assistance from Bongani Mkhize. Additional broad terms in the paper came from earlier publication or borrowing.
