## Pouteria bapeba T.D.Penn. {#s-85863a9e8f}
## Velvety fruit with one or two large-scarred seeds

Pouteria bapeba has alternate, spiral leaves with brochidodromous veins and a channelled petiole. Its axillary flower clusters bear five-parted flowers with a cup-shaped corolla and ragged-edged lobes. The fruit is smooth beneath a velvety covering and matures vinaceous to blackish. It contains one or two smooth seeds with a broad scar. The source lists alternative hair states for young branches.
