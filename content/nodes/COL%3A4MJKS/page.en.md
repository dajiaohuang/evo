## A juvenile on an anthurium leaf {#s-89255d30f3}
Juvenile DHMECN 14425 had a reported body size of 15.05 mm and was collected on an anthurium leaf 160 cm above ground in Naturetrek-Vizcaya Reserve montane forest at 2100 m. It is a single juvenile encounter, not an adult size endpoint or evidence of exclusive use of anthuriums.

## A range-extension interpretation relative to earlier records {#s-9e5ec1a37e}
The 2022 authors interpreted this voucher as extending the known distribution beyond the few previously documented localities near Tungurahua Volcano. They summarized diagnostic traits as a rostral papilla, a dorsally subacuminate snout and absence of pungent eyelid tubercles. The extension is relative to the knowledge cited in that paper; it does not certify the full contemporary range or continuous occupancy between sites.
