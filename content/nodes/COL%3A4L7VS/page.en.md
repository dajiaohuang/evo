## Polygala meridionalis {#s-28ea463384}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala meridionalis* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a leafy shrublet to 40 cm with ascending, linear leaves that are sparsely hairy below. Short terminal racemes carry purple flowers; the side petals are blunt at the tip. The account records coastal sandy and limestone slopes and flats as habitat. It does not supply a complete distribution or sampling frame.
