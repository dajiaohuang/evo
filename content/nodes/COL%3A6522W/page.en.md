## What flower and fruit traits are listed for Actinostemon echinatus? {#s-a47c0b0377}
The two leaf sizes are not markedly different. Lower and upper cataphylls—small scale-like leaves—fall early, and the foliage is alternate; veins in the cataphylls are unknown. The inflorescence is an unbranched raceme-like cyme and is not subtended by bracts; the pistillate cymules lack bracts. Female sepals lack glands, the female flower has no perianth, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit bears thin, pointed appendages.
