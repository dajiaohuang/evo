## *Xyris savanensis*: rough blades on two-ranked leaves {#s-25353b7e46}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description places the leaves in two ranks. Their flat blades have wrinkled or rough surfaces and pointed to blunt tips; the leaf sheaths are reddish-brown or pale and scarcely widened at the base. The regional description lists several possible states rather than measuring their frequency in a population.

The flowering stalk is cylindrical, 5–50 cm long, and carries one or two ridges that may be rough. Its ovoid spike is surrounded by four sterile bracts. The flower count is given as either up to 10 or 10–25, and seeds as globose to nearly globose. The habitat entry says only “terrestrial”; no collection locality or survey date accompanies the regional record.
