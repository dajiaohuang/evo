## An articulated Ludlow specimen {#s-af503bdf60}
The atlas anchors its Sarcopterygii route with an articulated *Guiyu oneiros* specimen from the Ludlow, about 419 Ma, and extends the route to living sarcopterygians. The fossil provides a sampled record, not a known direct ancestor or proof of the group’s exact global first appearance.

The connected *Guiyu oneiros* material gives the study an evidence-linked older anchor for the route. Its age is approximate and tied to the sampled Ludlow occurrence. The fossil’s articulated state does not by itself show that it gave rise directly to living members.
