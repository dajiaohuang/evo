## *Goeppertia burle-marxii*: wavy bract margins and a cleft corolla tube {#s-5a357862dc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes spiral leaves with stalks, attached on stem or basal shoots. All flowering bracts are fertile and spiral, and their margins curve back or wave; their recorded color is neither red nor orange. Each small flower cluster has two flowers and one membranous bracteole, a small leaf-like structure directly beneath a flower.

Its corolla tube is described as cleft, and the flower has one outer staminode with a non-petal-like callus at its tip. These are the morphology fields available in this regional record; no habitat or locality is given here.
