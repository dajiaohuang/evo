## Cleistes rosea {#s-f65684c644}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description gives Cleistes rosea one to three ovate leaves with blunt tips and pink to brown flowers. Its lip is only lightly three-lobed: the narrow side lobes run down toward rounded tips, while the middle lobe is deltoid. The callus is smooth at its base and scaly farther out; the habitat category is terrestrial.
