## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the regional description {#s-63805cff6a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records branches with flattened, triangular prickles and linear stipules. The leaves are shallowly divided into two lobes and papery in texture; the lobes are oblong to egg-shaped, with a heart-shaped base and blunt tip. The upper surface is hairless; the lower is villous (covered in long, soft hairs) and has abundant glands. Flower buds are tubular; their tips are described as club-shaped or tubular, blunt and without appendages. The hypanthium (floral cup) is tubular, and the calyx splits into two or three lobes when the flower opens. Petals are obovate-spatulate. The source records five anthers and describes five short filaments alternating with five long ones. The staminal column bears internal, tongue-shaped appendages and is described as bearded to hairy; the ovary is stalked. The legume is recorded as opening elastically. This regional description does not assess species-wide variation or distribution.
