## Graphiurus (Aethoglis) nagtglasii: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Graphiurus (Aethoglis) nagtglasii* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Jentink, 1888.
