## Reported plant and flower characters {#s-3ef4a36a5f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives plant size as 30 cm/45 cm and the number of leaves at flowering as 4/5; it also describes a resinous bulb. Because the source separates these values with slashes, they are retained as recorded rather than assigned to specimens or treated as ranges. Flowers are predominantly bluish or purplish. The inner tepals are described as lacking black marks at the tip, no longer than half the outer tepals, erect, and free. The fruit is recorded as inserted or partly inserted relative to the rhipidium bracts.

This is one morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset. Its completeness, variation, and diagnostic scope have not been assessed; it does not establish a global description or sampling coverage.

The dataset labels the growth form “terrestrial” (Portuguese: *terrícola*).
