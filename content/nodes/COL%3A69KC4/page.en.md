## Ecological evidence {#s-fce093ea14}
The first systematic field study of Dent's monkey followed one population for three months in 2017 in the Cyamudongo Forest Fragment, Nyungwe National Park, Rwanda. Leaves made up about 58% of the recorded diet and fruit about 18%. Monkeys spent most observed time 10–20 m above ground in secondary forest and open areas. This short study documents flexible feeding at one fragment; it does not establish a species-wide diet, habitat preference or response to fragmentation.
