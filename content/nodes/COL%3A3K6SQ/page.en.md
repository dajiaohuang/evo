## Helichrysum homilochrysum {#s-6ee9c7b11d}
## Woolly leaves and pale straw-colored bracts

The SANBI record describes an erect or decumbent shrub up to 450 mm tall. Ovate to elliptic-ovate leaves measure 25–50 by 10–35 mm and narrow abruptly into a broad, flat leaf-stalk-like base. The base is ear-shaped; the upper surface is thinly woolly and the lower more densely so.

Many bell-shaped heads form compact corymb-like clusters. Their glossy involucral bracts are pale straw-yellow, surrounding yellow flowers. The habitat field places the plant on cliff faces and edges; it does not establish a complete range.
