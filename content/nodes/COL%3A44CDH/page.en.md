## Tiny yellow flowers with reduced style branches {#s-ac399fadf9}
Moraea vlokii grows 15–20 cm tall and usually has one foliage leaf. Its pale-yellow flowers have short tepal claws (the narrowed bases of tepals, flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) and vestigial style branches concealed by the anthers; flowers close at about 13:00.

## Arid fynbos sandstone slopes {#s-9fbe977d98}
The source records rocky sandstone slopes in arid fynbos, a Cape shrubland vegetation type.
