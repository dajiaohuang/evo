## The source tadpole concept {#s-420f415bf5}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Julianus camposseabrai. The source classification lists Julianus as the genus and Hylidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Scinax camposseabrai. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
