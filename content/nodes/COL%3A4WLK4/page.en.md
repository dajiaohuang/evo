## Bare lower stems and narrow yellow-flowered heads {#s-02bc60cfc4}
The account describes a perennial with a woody base and herbaceous upper growth. The lower stem is leafless, while narrow, smooth leaves occur farther up; their margins may be entire or toothed. Yellow radiate heads form corymb-like clusters, and the smooth fruits bear a white pappus. The habitat field classifies it as terrestrial.
