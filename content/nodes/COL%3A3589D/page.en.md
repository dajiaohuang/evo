## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-665bcb232e}
COL26.8 records Diadophis punctatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-bcc0fca501}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 459.3 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Parker & Brown 1974. The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 870 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Parker & Brown 1974, Seymor 1987. This row records TL and SVL in separate fields; the captured definitions provide no conversion between them.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ba8585573a}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-8e16fb4d77}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-77649ae0dd}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
