## AmphiTherm and the recorded species concept {#s-293f90c372}
What does AmphiTherm record for Hylorina sylvatica? Its pinned dataset places the source concept in family Batrachylidae, order Anura, using the nomenclature of Jetz and Pyron (2018). It contains 2 thermal-estimate rows linked to 1 study-reference label. Rows are estimates, not counts of distinct animals; repeated cohorts, life stages and treatments remain separate. Equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
