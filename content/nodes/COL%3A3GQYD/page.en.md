## Goeppertia straminea {#s-070e03239c}
## A floral record with several unreported details

The source lists the leaves as spiral or solitary, basal, and ornamented when young, with sheaths and petioles present. Its basal flower stalk is recorded at a node different from the leaves. All bracts are fertile, arranged spirally, and described as neither red nor orange; each small flower cluster has two flowers. The bracteole and three flower structures are marked unknown, so those details remain open.
