## What is recorded for Hirtella tenuifolia? {#s-56c651635c}
Young branches are sparsely hairy, and the leaves are lance-shaped. The record notes no ant-associated cavities in the leaves. Flowers grow in racemes; both the bracts and the smaller bracteoles have glands without stalks. Each flower has five stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-aad4413dc1}
The dataset records it as terrestrial; no specific site is given.
