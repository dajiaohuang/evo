## Nuptial spines restricted to finger I distinguish the cited material {#s-a3f889e951}
Nanorana kangxianensis: The account cites nuptial spines on finger I in kangxianensis, contrasting with fingers I and II in laojunshanensis. The Discussion also lists first finger longer than second, complete toe webbing and absence of a tarsal fold in kangxianensis. These cited characters are not a fresh population series, direct performance measurements or a verified year-round reproductive state. They illustrate that the older Allopaa diagnosis contains traits shared with Nanorana.

## Published placement within Chaparana {#s-4af1505337}
The 2023 combined-locus analysis placed Nanorana kangxianensis with known members of Chaparana; the authors therefore suggest allocating it to that subgenus.
