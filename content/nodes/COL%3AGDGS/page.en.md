## Ardisia nervosissima Lundell {#s-c5c17b0015}
## Long leaves and small clusters at branch tips

Ardisia nervosissima is a shrub with stout branchlets that are initially scaly and become smoother with age. Its long, thin leaves are entire and oblanceolate, with a rounded, partly clasping base and 18–30 pairs of lateral veins. Terminal, twice-branched panicles carry small, sessile flower clusters; the buds are enclosed by bracts. The dry fruit is nearly spherical, 5–6 mm across and dotted with small black glands. The species is native to Panama.
