COL26.8 lists Leptopelis parbocagii Poynton & Broadley, 1987 as an accepted species in the order Anura, family Arthroleptidae and genus Leptopelis. This name and placement describe the catalogue release published on 20 August 2026; other records need separate identity evidence before their species concepts can be treated as equivalent.

## What one AmphiBIO row records {#s-db74ea2b80}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0244 uses the name Leptopelis parbocagii under its Frost 2011 concept, which has not been matched to COL26.8. It flags fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic foraging categories as Fos=1, Ter=1 and Aqu=1. These are compiled categories, not measures of frequency or duration, and they do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision notice covered these microhabitat fields, but this row has not been checked field by field. The row reports a maximum adult body size of 64 mm; for anurans, AmphiBIO defines this as snout–vent length. It also records Reproductive_output_y=1, interpreted by the compilation as at most one reproductive event per year, and Lar=1 for a larval stage. The row does not identify the population, sex, date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind the reproductive value, so it is not a schedule for every population. Other fields marked NA remain missing data.
