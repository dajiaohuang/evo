## Hairless leaves with a raised or channelled midrib {#s-f2bc9f8a2d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field allows either monopodial or sympodial stem growth. It records no hairs, and lists the upper-surface midrib as raised or channelled. Leaves may be elliptic, lanceolate, or ovate; their undersides are glabrous. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below leaf nodes are absent. These are alternatives in one regional dataset field, with no frequencies reported.

## A cap-opening flower and a globose fruit {#s-c8a5a839c8}
The rounded inflorescence branchlets have no bracteoles beneath the flowers. One inflorescence is listed per axillary shoot; it may be axillary or terminal, in an alternate pyramidal arrangement. The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is obconic, or inversely cone-shaped, and glabrous. Sepals are fully fused, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx is recorded as opening by a calyptra. On the globose fruit, the calyx lobes wither but remain attached (marcescent). The field reports no frequencies for its listed states.
