## *Parachimarrhis breviloba* {#s-a0cb728988}
COL26.8 accepts *Parachimarrhis breviloba* Ducke as a species of *Parachimarrhis* in tribe Dialypetalantheae, subfamily Dialypetalanthoideae, within Rubiaceae. A Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives one habitat category, “Terrícola” (terrestrial). That field supplies no collection locality or survey date, does not establish the species’ full range, and does not say whether the record concerns wild or cultivated plants.
