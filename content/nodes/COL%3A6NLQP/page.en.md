## Leaves and flowers {#s-f55ceab971}
Codd's 2005 regional account describes plants with a few to several stems. The limp, sickle-shaped leaves are yellow-green, 150–300 mm long at flowering and eventually lengthen to 600 mm; they are 15–30 mm wide, V-shaped in cross-section, and tend to fold along the midrib. The margins and keel have fine teeth. The flowering stalk rises above the recurved leaves and reaches 250–500 mm tall. The nearly globe-shaped raceme measures 50–70 × 50–60 mm, is often loose at the tip and denser below. Spreading buds are greenish yellow, often washed with dull red; the pendulous flowers are lemon-yellow. Papery, broadly oblong bracts with rounded to blunt tips are 5–6 mm long, with margins that are nearly entire to somewhat eroded. Pedicels are 3.5–5.0 mm long. The nearly cylindrical perianth is 35–40 mm long, with spreading, broadly ovate lobes 3 mm long. At flowering, the stamens extend up to 5 mm beyond the perianth, then retract. The ovoid, three-angled fruit is 7–9 mm long.

These measurements and observations come from a regional species account published in 2005; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## A possible herbarium confusion {#s-8a86fb113f}
Codd notes that herbarium specimens may be confused with *K. baurii*, which has somewhat narrower, glaucous leaves, slightly shorter and greener flowers, and a more inland distribution.
