## Its relationship to the reconstructed island lineages {#s-6c9cd52e4d}
Idiootocus belongs to clade I alongside lenquanensis, silvaenaias, inexpectatus and raoi. The selected analyses infer an island-colonization event for its lineage around one million years ago, separate from the older clade-II event. BSSVS and the selected DIVALIKE+j model support mainland origin, whereas pure DIVALIKE gives a widespread-ancestor vicariance scenario. This contrast makes the inference model-dependent; it is not direct observation of an ocean crossing or proof of the route taken.
