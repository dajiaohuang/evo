## White flowers with a yellow throat {#s-964b6aec5d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records inflorescences in both axillary and terminal positions. It describes the calyx as lacking hairs and the corolla as white with a yellow throat. The fruit is also recorded as hairless, with a smooth surface.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-6564d93b91}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Mostuea surinamensis* as terrestrial but supplies no collection locality or further environmental detail.
