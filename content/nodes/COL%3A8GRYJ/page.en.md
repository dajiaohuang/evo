## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-383bf43eab}
COL26.8 records Emydura gunaleni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-7b6ec6a7a2}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to The Reptile Database 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a084d78383}
The row reports 2.5 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
