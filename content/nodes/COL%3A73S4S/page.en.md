## A flattened midrib and woolly leaf underside {#s-231e2b7d80}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field records a monopodial stem, in which growth continues along one main axis, and simple hairs. Leaves are elliptic or ovate, with a fully flattened midrib on the upper surface and a woolly (tomentose) underside. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below the leaf nodes are absent. These are the states listed in one regional dataset field; it gives no frequencies.

## Compressed flower clusters and an enlarging fruit calyx {#s-1fec4cc51e}
The inflorescence branchlets are compressed, with rounded or deltoid bracteoles beneath the flowers. The field lists one inflorescence at each axillary bud, in a subterminal or terminal position, and describes pyramidal forms with alternate or opposite arrangements. It records free sepals, a two-chambered ovary, and a pubescent flower cup (hypanthium), while the cup's shape and the anther locules are unknown. Hairs are recorded at the base of the style on the floral disc. The calyx is described as not opening by tearing; its lobes enlarge on the globose fruit.
