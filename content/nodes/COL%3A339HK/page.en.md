## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2153c5dade}
COL26.8 records Cynisca schaeferi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-dafaa967a3}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 197 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-e512490cb5}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f5c52f8d00}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
