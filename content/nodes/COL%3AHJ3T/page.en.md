## Aspilia itabaianenis {#s-5fc5bcbf61}
## Solitary yellow heads on papery, cross-paired leaves

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes herbaceous, hairy stems and petiolate leaves arranged in opposite pairs at right angles. The papery blades range from ovate to oblong-lanceolate or oblong-elliptic. Flower heads are solitary and radiate, with a bell-shaped involucre of overlapping bracts and a flat receptacle bearing paleae, or narrow chaff scales. The flowers are yellow; outer ligulate florets are neutral and the central tubular florets bisexual. Central anthers form a tube; disc florets are fertile, while ray florets have rudimentary female parts. The dry fruits have an obovate attachment base and a pappus with two awns.
