## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-c8e76a8e15}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Scinax juncae* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Scinax (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.
