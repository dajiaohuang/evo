## Form and flowers {#s-f84135a06d}
Bayer's 2012 regional account describes *H. venosa* as a succulent that is usually stemless but may sometimes have a stem. Its short, spreading leaves are dark green with a net-like pattern; the flowers are white.

This is a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-31ea19404e}
The account records the species on rocky slopes among grasses in renosterveld.

This habitat note comes from a regional species account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
