## Source classification and concept status {#s-b2f2f21f0f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3119 records Hyperolius bocagei under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-bf29720df5}
The compilation records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-35c43d97aa}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 27 mm for this row. AmphiBIO's Body_size_mm field records maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
