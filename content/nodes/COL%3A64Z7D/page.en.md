## Pendulous flowers with very long spurs {#s-442233b1f2}
Aerangis kirkii is an epiphytic orchid (one that grows on another plant for support) with two to seven fleshy leaves, each up to 150 mm long. Pendulous inflorescences carry two to six white flowers with pink tinges in the spur (a slender projection from the flower); each slender spur is 60–75 mm long.
