## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-7b1b9c0028}
COL26.8 records Suta gaikhorstorum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-20656cfa7c}
The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015.
