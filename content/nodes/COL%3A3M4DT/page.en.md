## How does Hirtella parviunguis appear in this record? {#s-b331697700}
Young branches are sparsely hairy or become nearly smooth. The leaves are narrowly oblong to oblong, without the ant-associated cavities assessed in the record. Flowers grow in racemes, and neither bracts nor bracteoles are recorded with glands. Each flower has seven stamens.
