## What leaf and flower traits are noted for Croton viridulus? {#s-11846b8312}
Young branches have star-shaped or outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked oval or oval-lanceolate leaves have star-shaped hairs above and star-shaped or porrect hairs beneath, looping side veins, and entire margins. Nectar glands are absent, as are leaf-like stipules at young branch tips. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are shortly stalked; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate and unequal, the style has four lobes, and petals are absent. Bud parts overlap in an imbricate pattern; the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
