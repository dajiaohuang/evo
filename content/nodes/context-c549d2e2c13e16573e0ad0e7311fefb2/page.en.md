## Source classification and concept status {#s-1f6e87590a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2796 records *Litoria coplandi* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-8734755ff4}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 1.34 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 43 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura.
