## Two April pairs and one October caller {#s-346469f521}
Two amplectant pairs were found beneath rocks at night in April. A calling male was found in low vegetation at 19:00 on 16 October 2013. These separate observations document mating behavior and calling, without locating deposited eggs or establishing uninterrupted reproduction between April and October.
