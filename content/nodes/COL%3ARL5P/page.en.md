## What does the record say about Casselia confertiflora? {#s-6e5c03d982}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright herb with a woody base and bristly stems. Its short-stalked, elliptic leaves have toothed edges and pointed tips. The crowded flower clusters contain several flowers. The calyx, the outer floral cup, has nearly linear teeth and does not enlarge to cover the fruit. The mature fruit is recorded as dry drupelets, the small segments of a divided fruit.
