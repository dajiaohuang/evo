## Leaves {#s-e7221a3e94}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record allows stipules to be deciduous or absent and describes the leaf rachis as not winged. Leaves are even-pinnate, with one or two pairs of leaflets. The elliptic leaflets are listed with asymmetrical, acute bases and with acute, rounded, or truncate tips. Their lower surface is recorded as lacking punctations.

## Flowers and what was not observed {#s-2d3c0c0db2}
The inflorescence is a raceme with a glabrous axis. Flowers have a hairless hypanthium and four free sepals. Petals are described as clawed, ear-shaped, or obovate. The record lists three stamens with hairy filaments and a glabrous ovary. Fruit and seeds are marked “not observed” in this source; that records a limit of observation, not their absence.

## Habitat category {#s-e7b763a952}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
