## Pouteria sagotiana (Baill.) Eyma {#s-c5373c5086}
## One-chambered ovary; fruit characters unknown

Pouteria sagotiana has young branches that become smooth with age and bear lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have channelled petioles and veins that fade before the margin. Axillary or branch-borne clusters carry five-parted flowers with a cup-shaped corolla and rounded or blunt lobes. The ovary has one chamber. The source records one smooth seed with a narrow scar, but leaves fruit characters unknown.
