COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys hungtai Wang, Zeng, Lyu, Xiao & Wang, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). ## Two reported eastern Guangdong localities {#s-76a41e4122}
The monograph cited two localities in Jiedong District and Jiexi County at 500–1000 m. The male holotype SYS a007578 was listed from Liwangzhang Mountain, Jiexi, at about 990 m. Two reported localities do not establish continuous occupancy between them, the complete present range or a physiological elevation interval.

## Thirteen males and selected diagnostic characters {#s-0c8b1f5575}
The examined type material comprised thirteen adult males, with snout–vent lengths of 25.8–33.3 mm. Selected diagnostic features included a visible tympanum, unnotched tongue, absent vomerine ridges and teeth, rough granular dorsal skin with conical tubercles, and toes without webbing or lateral fringes. This male-only series supplies neither female size variation nor a measured population-wide size distribution.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-a5c61cd278}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys hungtai as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
