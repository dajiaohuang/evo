## Lejeunea oligoclada: two habitat labels {#s-846406cf59}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this species:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Epixila” (wood-associated).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
