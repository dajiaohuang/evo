## Couratari tenuicarpa A.C.Sm. {#s-052451d588}
## Pink flowers and a ridged, three-sided fruit

Couratari tenuicarpa has smooth, leathery leaves that are absent when the plant flowers; the leaf margin is entire or crenate. Flowers occur in racemes or panicles and have a finely hairy hypanthium and pale pink to reddish-pink petals. The androecial hood is wrinkled and dark pink. The fruit is conical, with a triangular cross-section and a striated outer surface; its stalk persists. The operculum tip may be convex or concave, and the seeds have circular wings. The source does not report bark characters.
