## Substrates named in the flora record {#s-b0206f4517}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record uses *Corticícola*, meaning growth on bark.

It also lists *Epixila*, growth on decaying wood.

These are substrate categories, not collection localities. The field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Scope of the record {#s-3a062059e4}
The habitat field does not say whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

These habitat labels do not describe life cycle or reproduction; those were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

This regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
