## Chamaecrista andromedea {#s-3679c78577}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista andromedea* records one pair of sessile obovate leaflets, glandular hairs, yellow unequal petals, twisted mature pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
