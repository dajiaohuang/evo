## A tight leaf fan and narrow floral cup {#s-2e6bb20278}
Ferraria brevifolia is a small corm-bearing geophyte (a plant with a swollen underground storage stem as its renewal organ), 70–200 mm tall. Its short, blunt leaf blades sit in a two-ranked fan and are much shorter than their 40–80 mm sheaths. The pale-yellow flowers have a narrow cup and faint, sweet scent; their tapering outer tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal) tips coil loosely.
