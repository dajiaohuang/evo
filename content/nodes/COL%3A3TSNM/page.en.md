## Highland meadow records under the revised concept {#s-825d1f2ead}
The revised account recorded the species in Ethiopian highland meadows north of the Great Rift Valley at 2339–3337 m. A historical specimen attributed to the Arussi Plateau east of the valley depended on the accuracy of its locality label. ## Calling at wet and temporarily dry sites {#s-5658acf6c3}
Males called from ground sites, cavities or beneath rocks, generally near streams or flooded areas, at night and sometimes by day. Some called beside a completely dried stream. The authors could not determine whether breeding extended into the dry season or whether calling persisted only through shorter dry periods; the observation is not converted into a confirmed dry-season breeding claim.
