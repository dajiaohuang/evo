## A white-hairy rosette with narrow upright pods {#s-c8d912165d}
This account describes an annual or overwintering herb up to 1 m tall, with lower stems densely covered in backward-pointing hairs. Large, white-hairy basal leaves form a rosette and are divided into short side lobes and a broad terminal lobe; the upper leaves are smaller and simple. Narrow, upright pods are about 0.7–1.5 cm long. The habitat record lists well-drained loam in full sun and several South African vegetation types, including grassland, thicket, fynbos-renosterveld, forest and savanna.
