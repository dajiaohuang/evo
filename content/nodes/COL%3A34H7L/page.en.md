## Smooth green branches and white petals {#s-977735fcd2}
The Latin description records smooth, bright-green stems and branchlets, sometimes with a pale bluish cast. Linear-lanceolate leaves may stand upright, spread or curve backward; they range from long and pointed to shorter and blunt-tipped. White petals form two dense rows, and the recurved staminodes are up to 7.5 mm long. The account records flats and hills on shale-rich Karoo sediments, in scrub and thicket settings and several named South African vegetation types.
