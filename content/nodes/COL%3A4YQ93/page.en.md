## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2c5ca4c3f7}
COL26.8 records Sphaerodactylus ocoae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Sphaerodactylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
