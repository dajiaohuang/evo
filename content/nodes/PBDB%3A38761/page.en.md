## Skull variation and the Anatotitan name {#s-3a020f1cd0}
In Campione and Evans’s cranial sample, the long, low skulls historically assigned to *Anatotitan copei* fall within or near the variation of *Edmontosaurus annectens*. The authors explain the proportions through size-related allometry—how body proportions change as an animal grows—and individual variation; crushing may have accentuated the shape.

The skull sample supports a reduced set of latest Cretaceous North American edmontosaur species, but it does not assign every hadrosaurid skull or set exact genus boundaries. The displayed 73–66 Ma interval is the stratigraphic envelope of the sampled skulls, not a global genus duration, first or last appearance, divergence date, or claim of continuous occupancy.
