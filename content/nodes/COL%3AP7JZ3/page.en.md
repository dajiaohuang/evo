## Rolled leaf edges and fused cotyledons {#s-f238b49dc6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or obovate leaves with acute bases and tips that taper to a point. The leaves are papery; their margins roll under or are wavy, without a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with two veins near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles; bract status is unknown. Smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth and hairless; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-826ffd85e1}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This category adds no distribution claim.
