## Research overview {#s-d3f2dc7f94}

Eriaster marks the body-fossil boundary used here, but one occurrence does not establish a global first appearance, class-wide ecology or direct ancestry.

## Body fossils and disputed traces {#s-21d2a230f7}
The study describes the body-fossil anatomy of *Eriaster ibexensis*.

Asteroid body fossils are treated separately from trace fossils whose attribution remains uncertain.

## One marine occurrence, bounded ecology {#s-46f738ba57}
Eriaster comes from a source-bounded marine depositional context. One occurrence does not establish class-wide diet, locomotion, body-size range or ecological guild.
