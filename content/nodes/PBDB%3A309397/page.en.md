## Recorded parent of Potamarchinae {#s-b8ba119ce5}
Potamarchinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Dinomyidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
