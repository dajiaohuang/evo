## Brunsvigia elandsmontana {#s-12c250c9bd}
Brunsvigia elandsmontana is a low geophyte with bright-pink, radially symmetrical flowers in a compact cluster. The regional account distinguishes it from the scarlet B. marginata by its shorter perianth tube and stamens that do not extend beyond the tepals. Both are reported from fire-prone fynbos, but B. elandsmontana is associated with lowlands around 100 m, while B. marginata is recorded at 450–1,200 m. Pebbly, well-drained flats are its habitat.
