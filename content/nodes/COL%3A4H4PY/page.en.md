## What leaf and flower traits distinguish this entry? {#s-39f48441dc}
The record describes simple stems and petiolate leaves that are alternate or arranged in two ranks, with elliptic to broadly elliptic blades. Flowers may be solitary or arranged in umbel-like clusters. Male flowers have five white sepals, a segmented nectar disc and two stamens joined together. Female flowers have six white sepals and a segmented nectar disc. The fruit is a spherical capsule, and the seeds are smooth and unornamented.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-1160b5db8f}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
