## Crowded leaves on a hairy perennial {#s-2d6650124c}
This account describes a coarse perennial herb or subshrub up to 1 m tall, with branches mostly from the base. Its crowded opposite leaves are narrow, 4–12 cm long, and densely hairy with tiny glands. Pale-red to lavender flower heads form branched clusters; each head has 21–25 florets, and the fruits carry about 25 slender bristles.
