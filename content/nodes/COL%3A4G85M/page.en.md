## Philodendron inaequilaterum {#s-d29d93272d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists entire leaves with ovate, elliptic, or oblong forms, a cuspidate tip, and an asymmetric base. It also records cylindrical berries and numerous seeds. The habitat category is hemiepiphytic; this regional description is not a complete range or population account.
