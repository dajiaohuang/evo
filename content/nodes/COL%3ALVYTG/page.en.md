## Ayapana triplinervis {#s-41bf49fb1c}
## Opposite leaves and loose white-flowered panicles

The source describes a cylindrical stem with opposite, nearly sessile leaves. Blades are oblong or lance-shaped, entire and glabrous. Loose panicle-like inflorescences have a bell-shaped involucre with partly overlapping lanceolate phyllaries. The white florets have funnel-shaped corollas; the fruit is obpyramidal and hairy, with a conspicuous carpopodium at its base and rough bristles in the pappus. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
