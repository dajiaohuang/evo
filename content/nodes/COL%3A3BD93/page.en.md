## Erythrina chiriquensis {#s-a12826b418}

The Flora of Panama describes an armed tree with flattened, triangular spines to 1 cm long and leaflets 6–20 × 4–12 cm. Its pods reach 13 cm, and the seeds are dull or bright scarlet, about 1.2 × 0.6 cm; this account reports it only from upland Chiriquí, Panama.
