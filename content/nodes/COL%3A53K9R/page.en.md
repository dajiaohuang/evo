## Syagrus × campos-portoana: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-a414b7a75c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a smooth, solitary, aerial stem. Leaves are green and pinnate. The sheath is described as deciduous, spirally arranged in a regular series, and edged with toothed fibres. The petiole also has toothed fibrous margins, and the rachis is arched. Pinnae are leathery, grouped or set at different angles, with acute, rounded or asymmetrically notched tips; they are dark green and slightly discolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate or pendulous, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved or pruinose; its rachis is glabrous and its rachillae are arranged helically. Fruits are ovate or elliptic. The epicarp is green or yellow-green, with indumentum at the tip. The mesocarp is fibrous or succulent, the endocarp woody or oval, and the endosperm slightly irregular and gibbous.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Source alternatives are retained without resolving them. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
