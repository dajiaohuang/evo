## Several leaf outlines and a fused embryo {#s-2d2f247202}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists elliptic, lance-shaped, narrowly elliptic or oblanceolate leaves; the last form is widest nearer the tip. The base may be acute or wedge-shaped, and the tip tapers to a point. The leaves are papery, with flat or rolled-under margins and no hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved; one vein lies near the edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles. Their supporting bracts fall away, while the smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo.
