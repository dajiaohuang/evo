## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-50f1bc9aff}
COL26.8 records Bothrops asper as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-aac173a046}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 2500 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-24214f874c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Leenders 2019. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bowler 1977, Sasa et al. 2009, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-e9f983e95f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 29.9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bowler 1977, Sasa et al. 2009, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-31c2120eff}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
