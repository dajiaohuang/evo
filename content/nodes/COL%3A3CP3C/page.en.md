## What stem and flower structures are recorded for Euphorbia gymnoclada? {#s-b995e30e31}
The cylindrical stems are hairless. Leaves are alternate, entire, and lack stipules; their side veins are inconspicuous. Green bracts surround solitary or paired cyathia at the upper stem nodes. The involucre is smooth and top-shaped, with four or more transversely elliptic glands whose finger-like appendages stand upright. Female flowers lack a pseudoperianth; the ovary is hairless and the styles are undivided. The fruit is hairless, and seeds lack a caruncle.
