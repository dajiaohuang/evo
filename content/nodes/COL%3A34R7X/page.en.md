## *Dendropanax trilobus*: long-tapering leaves and five-chambered ovaries {#s-77e4c2fb09}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field lists elliptic, ovate or lance-shaped leaves with long tapering tips; their papery undersides are dotted, and a pair of veins rises above the base. Umbrella-shaped flower clusters are carried in a raceme, with four to eight secondary branches and up to 30 flowers per umbel.

The ovary is recorded as five-chambered. Fruits are as long as or slightly longer than their stalks, and the styles join into a column longer than 1 mm. These are regional character states, not a population-wide measure.
