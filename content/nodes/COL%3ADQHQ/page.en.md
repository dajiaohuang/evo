## Hairy leaves and flower traits {#s-1d6d91baff}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a hairy lower leaf surface and a smooth upper surface, with two to six pairs of leaflets. It notes terminal flower clusters, reddish-brown hairs on the calyx, pink to purple petals, and a hairless ovary. The fruit is described with green or brown outer tissue and a woody, fibrous or granular interior; the source lists three seeds and alternative states for several features. This is a regional description, not a study of variation across populations.
