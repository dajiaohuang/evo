## Evolutionary evidence {#s-e6b8e0bbd3}
A squirrel-monkey marker study tested 382 Alu insertion loci in a 32-individual panel; 110 loci were polymorphic for insertion presence or absence, and 51 showed allele-frequency distributions informative between the authors’ S. sciureus and S. boliviensis groups. The authors also noted that two specimens labeled S. sciureus clustered closer to the S. boliviensis group. These findings are limited to that marker panel and specimen assignments and do not independently resolve species boundaries.

These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-73095e8191}
Same-name sparse rows carry source labels including Jones, K., Bielby, J., Cardillo, M. et al., 2009; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; Ernest et al., 2003; Ricklefs, 2010b, among others. These labels identify material used by the compilation. Row counts are not animal counts, and repeated citations need not represent independent studies.

## Evidence limits {#s-3cea5cc716}
The Alu panel and uncertain specimen assignments constrain taxonomic interpretation and are not genome-wide evidence.
Amniote LifeHistory 2015 reports historical summaries, generally medians (or averages of the two middle records) and maximum longevity as the largest recorded value. Values may be shared across historical species and subspecies; a name match does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept, and wild or captive conditions are not identified consistently.
