## Dense or solitary flowers and a heart-shaped leaf base {#s-2eac139d6c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or oval, papery leaves with heart-shaped or blunt bases and tips that taper to a point. Their flat margins lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers may grow in tight clusters called glomerules or occur singly; bract status is unknown. The smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. Seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial habitat label without a locality {#s-9a65864a3d}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This is a habitat category, not a distribution statement.
