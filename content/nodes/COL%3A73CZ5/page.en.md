## A tall shrub with keeled, twisted leaves {#s-1951754c6f}
The regional account describes an erect, white-woolly shrub up to 3 m tall. Its leaves are twisted and keeled; the outer bracts are brown and the inner petal-like bracts white. Flowering is recorded from September to December, on rocky slopes across the listed range from Klein Swartberg to Cathcart.
