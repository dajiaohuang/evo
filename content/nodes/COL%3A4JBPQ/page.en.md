## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-302cb93716}
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Pithecopus rusticus as follows: Short notes with two or three pulses and long notes with 50–80 pulses, either isolated or combined. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.
