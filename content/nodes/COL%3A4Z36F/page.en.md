## *Spigelia olfersiana* {#s-ffdbcafa05}
## Woolly stems and a funnel-shaped flower

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records internodes longer than the leaf blades and cylindrical stems with woolly or star-shaped hairs. Nearly stalkless leaves are lance-shaped, with a nearly heart-shaped or cut-off base and an acute tip; their surfaces also carry woolly or star-shaped hairs, and their veins are pinnate. Long flower stalks bear more than 4 flowers. The linear sepals and ovary are woolly; the funnel-shaped corolla is white with pink or purple spots and is recorded as hairless. Stamens remain inside it. The fruit is downy and the seeds nearly spherical. The listed alternatives do not quantify how often each occurs.
