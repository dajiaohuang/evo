## Leaves and flowers {#s-4946aac0e4}
The account describes a bulbous herb up to 450 mm tall. It has two or three leaves appearing with the flowers; they are 500–600 mm long and 25–40 mm wide. The perianth is 35–45 mm long, with lobes 10–15 mm long. Flower colour ranges from orange-red to dark red.
