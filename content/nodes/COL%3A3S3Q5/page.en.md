## Lampranthus glomeratus {#s-ef148636c7}
## A stiffly branched shrublet with sickle-shaped leaves

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes the plant as erect and rarely rooting at the nodes. The species account gives a stiffly branched shrublet up to 20 cm tall; the broader description gives dwarf shrubs 10–25 cm high.

The smooth, slender leaves stand upright and are glaucous, greenish and somewhat sickle-shaped; they are 10–15 mm long. Flower clusters bear one to three pink or purplish flowers about 28 mm across. The fruits are about 6 mm in diameter. The habitat account records sandy, stony slopes.
