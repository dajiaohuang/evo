## A variable yellow-flowered herb {#s-0c7fac8a1a}
The regional account describes S. zeyheri as a slender herb up to 500 mm tall, with leaves generally along the stem. Its orchid lip—a specialized petal—has three deep lobes and three fleshy raised pads; the flowers are bright yellow. The same source calls the species very variable and reports a putative hybrid with S. flexuosus. “Putative” means the hybrid interpretation is proposed rather than confirmed in this record.
