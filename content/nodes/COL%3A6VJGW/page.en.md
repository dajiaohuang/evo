## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-75d55d4826}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Phytotriades auratus* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Phytotriades (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.

## Where adults were reported foraging {#s-21d05f44d0}
The AmphiBIO compilation uses the historical name Phytotriades auratus under a Frost 2011 classification. That source concept has not been shown to match the current COL26.8 species concept exactly. The compilation records arboreal as present categories. These are broad foraging categories; the compilation does not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries copied from earlier compilations. The affected row is not identified in this record, so no replacement value can be assigned. This caveat does not show that every earlier entry is wrong.
