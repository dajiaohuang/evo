## ## Two habitat categories in one dossier {#s-110939ff3b}
COL26.8 places *Arachis diogoi* in *Arachis*, within Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 dossier lists both “aquatic” and “terrestrial” as habitat categories. It does not attach either label to a locality, collection date or population, so the two entries are retained without inferring where or under what conditions the species grows.
