## Read the definition before comparing {#s-32c8cf8b71}
Among size records in this batch, Pseudoacontias madagascariensis has a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 200 mm and Pseudoacontias unicolor 227.8 mm. These are endpoints of records for different species, not individual variation within one species or the smallest/largest actual individuals of the whole group. Retain field-specific sources when comparing activity, feeding and reproduction.
