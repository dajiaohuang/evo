## ## Wavy leaflets and variable flower-color entries {#s-4b93a2c60f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records oblanceolate leaflets and lists leaves as unifoliolate, trifoliolate or digitately arranged. The leaflets bear hairs on both surfaces and have wavy margins. The free distal portion of the stipules is recorded as 3.3–56 mm long. Inflorescence bracts are shed and longer than 10 mm; the listed flower colors are purple, pink and blue. This regional dataset entry preserves those character states but does not measure variation across the species.
