## A grooved midrib that flattens toward the tip {#s-3594df868b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth, with two-armed hairs on the stem. Leaves are elliptic, ovate, or obovate. The upper midrib is channelled toward the stalk and flattens distally; the underside is hairless or finely short-haired. Bracts or cataphylls beneath leaf nodes fall away. These are listed states from a regional field, without frequencies.

## Many axillary clusters and a round fruit {#s-a3dccd51bf}
Small flowering branches are compressed, and the bracteoles below the flowers fall away. Per axillary shoot, the field lists one, two, three, four, or more than four inflorescences; their position is axillary and their arrangement is described as pyramidal and opposite. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal. The floral disc is hairless; the flower cup is top-shaped and smooth. Sepals are separate, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. The fruit is globose; its calyx lobes fall away.
