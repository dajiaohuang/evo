## Record identity and classification {#s-25c70f7315}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf4090 records Pseudocallulops eurydactylus under Pseudocallulops, Microhylidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Adult food categories {#s-446416764c}
The row lists arthropods as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-3acf76906c}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 33 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.
