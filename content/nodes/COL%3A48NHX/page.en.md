## Odontadenia markgrafiana {#s-d8797e30d6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Odontadenia markgrafiana* as an accepted species of *Odontadenia*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records terrestrial habitat and nothing more about ecology, locality, or range. No morphology claim is included in that regional source record.
