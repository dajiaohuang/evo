## Moquiniastrum barrosoae {#s-32dacc998d}
## Star-shaped hairs line the branch and both leaf faces

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes striate branches with star-shaped hairs. Alternate, stalked leaves differ in colour between their surfaces; the blades are papery to leathery, heart-based, entire-edged and pointed. A woolly covering and star-shaped hairs are recorded on both faces.

Terminal panicles bear bell-shaped flower heads. Their involucres—the bracts surrounding each head—have 4–6 series of bracts, with the outer ones ovate, pointed and silky. Each head has 18–26 cream tubular florets, recorded as bisexual or female. The cylindrical fruits have five ribs and a silky covering. Their straw-coloured pappus consists of unequal, rough to barbed bristles in two or three rows.
