## Hong Kong and Shenzhen records with an explicit identity decision {#s-5b8bf91dde}
The monograph recognized Hong Kong and neighboring Shenzhen records at 30–580 m. Examined material included Tai Tam and Lantau in Hong Kong and Yangtai, Sanzhoutian and Qiniang mountains in Shenzhen. Earlier molecular work had suggested the Shenzhen population might be a distinct cryptic species. The monograph found no distinguishing morphological characters and treated the populations as conspecific. That is the authors’ taxonomic decision, not independent proof that all genetic or reproductive differences are absent.
