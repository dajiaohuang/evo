## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9afd2b442e}
COL26.8 records Thamnophis eques as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-2dd3b366f3}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 2000 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-34f43b9589}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0e89da5d2f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 12.58. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bronikowski and Vleck 2010, Harding 1997, Wechsler 2001, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-d4d11ea119}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
