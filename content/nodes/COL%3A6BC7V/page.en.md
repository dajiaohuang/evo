## Accepted name and placement {#s-214b2e7131}
COL26.8 records Craugastor rugulosus (Cope, 1870) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; historical source concepts are described separately below.

## Reading route {#s-bfdb9dd9ad}
The catalogue entry and the Frost 2011 AmphiBIO row are separate taxonomic records. Several deposited values below carry explicit unit or revision limits.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-4d7a7c115b}
AmphiBIO codes terrestrial and aquatic foraging for its historical Craugastor rugulosus concept. The categories are broad and may overlap; a later revision reports changes to some inherited microhabitat and mass values, but this row has not been checked against it.

## Adult food categories — AmphiBIO source {#s-78500cd562}
Arthropods appear among the row's adult food categories. The compilation combines literature reports based on expert judgment, observation or stomach contents; the category is not a complete diet or a food proportion.

## Activity periods — AmphiBIO source {#s-09db83bb59}
The source records both daytime and nighttime activity. These are broad overall codes, not a continuous activity series or proof that individuals are active at all hours.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-5865469b5c}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 75 g for this historical row. A later TetrapodTraits revision specifically reports correcting some inherited amphibian body-mass entries, and this row-level value has not been checked. Treat 75 g as a deposited historical value requiring review, not a verified modern measurement; no replacement value is inferred.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-286ffa37b7}
The row lists both a minimum and maximum clutch count of 64 offspring or eggs and a maximum of one reproductive event per year. The compiled field does not resolve whether the count refers to eggs or offspring, or specify sex, population, date or wild/captive conditions. The identical minimum and maximum are retained as reported, not interpreted as a universal clutch size.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-8023d3050f}
Direct development is marked for the source concept. It does not by itself specify care, breeding location or season, and the historical taxonomy has not been reconciled with the catalogue entry.
