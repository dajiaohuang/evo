## Arcticodactylus: PBDB classification route {#s-b3449fc4ea}
PBDB records *Arcticodactylus* at genus rank. This route links it to the one species represented in the selected occurrence set, *Arcticodactylus cromptonellus*. The database's recorded parent chain organizes records; it is not a phylogenetic tree, and a one-species roster does not measure evolutionary importance. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a genus-wide account of anatomy or ecology.

## Limits of the selected Arcticodactylus records {#s-845ad06340}
This route covers one selected species and one recorded occurrence; it is not a full descendant catalogue or an ecological synthesis. PBDB row counts depend on record coverage and grouping, and are not comparable measures of fossils, individuals, ancient populations or collecting intensity. The database's broad 227.3–205.7 Ma first- and last-occurrence bounds are not direct specimen dates or estimates of evolutionary origin and extinction. PBDB's parent chain is a cataloguing path, separate from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
