## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e7797a824e}
COL26.8 records Pelodiscus axenaria as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trionychidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-ad7203a543}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 128.7 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-2db4e901aa}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to The Reptile Database 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-716d89c601}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
