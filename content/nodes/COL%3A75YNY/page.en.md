## Pelekium muricatulum: four recorded substrate categories {#s-c0ab937994}
Brazilian Flora 2020 includes a “corticícola” (bark-associated) entry for *Pelekium muricatulum*.

A separate entry uses “epixila,” the wood-associated category.

Another lists “rupícola,” the rock-associated category.

The fourth is “terrícola,” the terrestrial category.

These are regional habitat or substrate labels, not a measure of how common each setting is. The dataset gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish a complete range.
