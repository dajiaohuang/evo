## A cushion with purple-pink, white-lined petals {#s-758eaf5132}
This account describes a cushion-shaped succulent shrub up to 9 cm tall, with reclining branches and densely papillate, subcylindrical leaves. The purplish-pink petals have a broad white line at the base. The five-chambered fruit has a bell-shaped base and nearly rectangular valve wings. It grows in loam with some quartz pebbles.
