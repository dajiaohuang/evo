## Source classification and concept status {#s-cc1dbe1670}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2888 records *Litoria oenicolen* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-494f042713}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-a383e5067b}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 40.3 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
