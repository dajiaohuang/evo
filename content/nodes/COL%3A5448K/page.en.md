## Syspira tigrina {#s-397f63277c}
## Three stripes on a dark carapace

The 2025 treatment describes a dark carapace—the spider's upper body shield—with three stripes, and sand-colored living specimens. These are diagnosis-level characters from that treatment, not a survey of variation across populations or life stages. Its diagnosis and description differ on two structures of the male palp, a reproductive appendage: the embolus and cymbial groove. Those characters are left out because the two passages disagree.

The authors describe *S. tigrina* as mainly active at night. In pitfall-trap samples from the reported study sites, it accounted for up to 50% of the cursorial spiders collected—spiders moving along the ground. That percentage describes those places and that capture method; it is not an abundance estimate for the species as a whole.
