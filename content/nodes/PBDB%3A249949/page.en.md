## A branching system preserved in three dimensions {#s-de4c27464e}
Asteroxylon mackiei from the Rhynie chert preserves leafy shoots, axes bearing roots and separate rooting axes. The three-dimensional reconstruction makes this one extinct lycopsid's branching architecture visible. It is an anatomical case study, not a direct ancestor of living lycophytes or a body plan shared by the whole lineage.
