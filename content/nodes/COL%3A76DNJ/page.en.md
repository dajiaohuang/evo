## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-2894321e9f}
The status of a xylopodium, a woody underground swelling, is unknown in the record. The leaves are simple and entire. Terminal racemes carry leafy bracts and shield-shaped bracteoles. The stamens are united into a hairy column; ten hairy styles are recorded. The corolla is pink with a wine-colored spot at the base. The fruit separates into hairy segments (mericarps) that split open, each with a small pointed tip and no lateral wings. The seeds are hairless and lack spots.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-da85f5da90}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
