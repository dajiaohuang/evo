## Twelve Ordovician fragments preserve spore masses {#s-51864c325c}
Twelve fragments from the Ghaba-1 core in northern Oman preserve permanent spore tetrads or dyads, with some showing a presumed sporangial covering. The source calls the deposits Caradoc; the atlas therefore keeps a broad Sandbian–Katian display interval rather than converting that legacy regional stage into a precise date. Layered spore walls suggest liverwort affinity, but missing elaters and unresolved early-plant relationships prevent a crown-group identification.
