## When ostracod relationships change with data {#s-7878e0ca39}
Nine new 454 transcriptomes joined morphology, fossils and several sequence datasets in analyses of Pancrustacea. Support for major groupings is conditional: ostracod monophyly and several deep nodes shift when fast genes, outgroups or data partitions change.

## A broad data combination {#s-68e81321a7}
The analysis added nine new ostracod transcriptomes to morphology, fossils, expressed-sequence tags, genomes, mitochondrial proteins and ribosomal data. A transcriptome is the set of RNA sequences measured from sampled tissue. Combining these sources supports some major pancrustacean groupings in selected analyses.

## Read the sensitivity results too {#s-b962ca52e6}
Ostracod monophyly and several deeper relationships change when fast-evolving genes, distant outgroups or data partitions are treated differently. These branches are outputs of phylogenetic analyses, not fossil observations. The study therefore presents conditional support and sensitivity rather than one settled tree for every deep pancrustacean node.
