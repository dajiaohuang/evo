## Leaves and flower heads in the morphology record {#s-a09d511daf}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field lists a xylopodium at the root. It describes crenate leaf margins, petioles as present or absent, a flat blade margin, and leathery blade texture; it gives both margin descriptions without explaining their relation. Mature flower heads (capitula) are nearly globose and have a peduncle, or stalk. The flower field records flattened appendages on the calyx lobes and no stylopodium. This single regional field does not establish completeness, variation, or diagnostic scope.
