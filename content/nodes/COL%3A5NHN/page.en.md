## Recorded vocal structures in selected members {#s-a5003e456d}
The 2019 analysis describes archived calls from three species. Melanophryniscus alipioi has two harmonic-note sequences: an introduction with longer notes and gaps, followed by a faster main segment. M. moreirae has a regularly repeated non-harmonic note series and separate non-pulsed harmonic notes, which the authors propose may serve advertisement and aggressive roles. One analyzed M. vilavelhensis call contains 546 non-pulsed, non-harmonic notes in introductory and main segments. These patterns describe the recordings studied; the limited male samples do not establish a genus-wide repertoire or diagnostic cutoffs for identifying species.
