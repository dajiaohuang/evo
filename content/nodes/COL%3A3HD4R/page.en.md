## What shrub, bract and flower traits are recorded? {#s-3b1fceab0e}
The regional account describes a shrub about 1 m tall. Its bracts are three-cleft. The petal blade is about 2 mm long and short, triangular in shape. The flowers are white.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-6b112499f4}
The regional account reports dry woodland on Kalahari sand. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
