## Accepted name and classification {#s-8543c69088}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Amolops siju* Saikia, Sinha, Shabnam & Dinesh, 2023 as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Amolops (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## Traits in the published group comparison {#s-ab7fee981f}
The 2023 description of *Amolops ailao* discusses *A. siju* in its *marmoratus* species-group comparison. Earlier descriptions associate *A. siju* with a circummarginal groove (a groove around the edge) on the first-finger disc and with vomerine teeth (teeth on the vomer bones in the roof of the mouth); the vocal-sac comparison explicitly treats it as an exception. These contrasts come from earlier literature, not a newly measured *A. siju* sample. Placement in a historical species group does not by itself settle current species limits.
