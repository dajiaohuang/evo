## Silvery leaves on a rooting mat {#s-724ba1db8a}
The source describes a perennial herb that spreads into a mat: its prostrate stems root and produce many crowded leaf rosettes. Narrow leaves have a silvery-white underside, while the flower heads form a dense cluster with yellow-tipped bracts. The fruit bears hairs that become mucilaginous when wet and yellow pappus bristles with short feathery tips. It grows on steep, stony mountain slopes and summits.
