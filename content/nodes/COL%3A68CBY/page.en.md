## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Banisteriopsis parviflora? {#s-24fa0d8ee9}
Leaves are opposite or in threes, with elliptic blades, stalkless petiole glands, and stalked glands at the blade base. The upper surface is hairless; the lower is hairless or sparsely silky-haired. Axillary racemes each bear four umbels. Bracts persist, and the stalkless pedicels are hairless. The calyx bears an oil gland; petals are white or pale yellow. Anther chambers are hairless. The three styles differ: the front style is slightly longer and broader than the two behind it. The fruit has no lateral projections.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c08b93103e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
