## Form in the flora record {#s-7bdb434198}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists clasping or feeding roots, an erect stem, and a deciduous prophyll. The sheath is inconspicuous. Petioles are conspicuous, smooth, striate, subcylindrical, green, flattened on the upper side, and rounded below.

The blade is entire and sagittate, with an acuminate tip. Primary lateral veins are recorded on both sides of the blade; the field lists values of 3–5 and 6–9. The margin is entire.

The inflorescence is sympodial, with counts of 2, 3, or 4. The peduncle is cylindrical, green or yellowish, and short—shorter than the spathe. The spathe is slightly constricted; its tube is light green on the outer surface, while the blade's inner surface may be wine-colored, red, light green, or cream. The spadix is sessile, with female, sterile male, fertile male, and apical sterile male zones.

Flowers have 4, 5, or 6 short stamens; staminodes are short. The gynoecium is cylindrical and the ovary subcylindrical. Ten locules and 2 or 3 ovules are recorded; placentation is basal and the stigma capitate. Berry and seed characters are marked unknown.

The regional dataset row cites Sakuragui and colleagues' 2020 Philodendron treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.
