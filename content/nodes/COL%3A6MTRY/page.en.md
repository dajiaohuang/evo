## A putative specimen is not a confirmed concept match {#s-94454c4ea4}
The 2016 account labelled specimen KU 319433 from Mt. Magdiwata only putatively I. mindanaoensis and stated that it appeared to be a juvenile male. The authors considered the taxonomy and range uncertain, suggesting that some Mt. Malindang records might instead be I. glandulosus and calling for a re-examination of diagnostic variation. This historical candidate is recorded with that uncertainty, not certified as matching the pinned accepted species or converted into a verified occurrence.

## One candidate found in dry soil beneath a log {#s-c4d86bfa2b}
The putatively identified specimen came from Barangay Bayugan II, San Francisco, Mt. Magdiwata, in dry soil beneath a log several hundred metres from a small stream. That single collection documents the candidate’s encounter substrate, not an obligatory dry-soil preference, an absence of aquatic life stages or the species’ full daily activity. The account cited Data Deficient in 2016 because taxonomic and distribution uncertainty remained; this dated category is not a current official assessment.
