## Accepted name and placement {#s-8423302a53}
COL26.8 accepts Tylototriton panwaensis Grismer, Wood, Quah, Thura, Espinoza & Murdoch, 2019 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Salamandridae (family) → Tylototriton (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A 2026 comparison of tooth-series contact and ridges {#s-252debd9cb}
The vietnamirabilis description characterized panwaensis by vomerine tooth series not contacting the choanae, absence of a gular fold, an unsegmented vertebral ridge and a male tail shorter than SVL. These are attributed comparator states from the 2026 paper, not a full panwaensis redescription or a verified invariant state for every individual. The comparison does not provide measured feeding mechanics, reproductive function or a new range record.

## The lowest sampled divergence does not settle a sister relationship {#s-5b11313587}
The paper reported vietnamirabilis’s lowest genetic divergence with panwaensis at 5.33–5.35% despite an estimated 930-km geographic separation, but its morphological-results text explicitly said no clear sister taxon was recovered. The reported distance and geographic gap are therefore not promoted to a resolved sister relationship, travelled dispersal distance or a universal species threshold. Its two-comparator morphology analysis covered panwaensis and anguliceps, not every congener.
