## What hangs from its flower stalk? {#s-7cdac9793c}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records *Heliconia pendula* with a thick, short rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) and a hanging flower cluster. Its petiolate leaves are listed as oblong, lance-shaped, or inversely lance-shaped. The bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) may be boat-shaped, and several bract and flower features remain alternatives in the source field.
