## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-caebc96a6c}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Ischnocnema abdita as direct-developing and its oviposition as terrestrial. In the article, direct development means no free-living larval stage, with a froglet hatching rather than a free-living tadpole; it does not mean that embryonic development is absent or that humidity is unnecessary. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.

## Juvenile habitat and egg-site categories have defined scope {#s-43f425d0b6}
The same row codes juvenile habitat as terrestrial and marks eggs on the ground, rocks or in burrows among the seven oviposition-site categories. The positive category summarizes a broad trait; it does not establish a specific nest architecture, substrate species or oviposition height. A juvenile-habitat code is not automatically an adult habitat preference. The compiled record does not newly establish clutch size, egg dimensions, parental attendance, spawning season or offspring survival.
