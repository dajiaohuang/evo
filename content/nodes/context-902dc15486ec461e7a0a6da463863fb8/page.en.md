## AmphiBIO species record {#s-8b89147a3c}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Bufo tuberospinius? Row Anf0553 places it in genus Bufo, family Bufonidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
