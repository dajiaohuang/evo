## The deposited caecilian record {#s-c921ae4a6a}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis multicolor in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-496f873e1f}
The compilation lists 14 measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of 14 distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-6204743598}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-d246a619e0}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. The accompanying metadata describes this as presumed, probable or expected, so it remains a qualified compilation statement rather than an unqualified observation.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-8355f5da9e}
The species-level record gives 364.428571428571 for dorsal annuli (body rings), 4 for post-vent annuli, 6.28571428571429 annuli interrupted by the vent, and 128.857142857143 vertebrae. These compiled values may summarize records rather than describe one animal; they do not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic counts.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-4aa10a42e6}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
