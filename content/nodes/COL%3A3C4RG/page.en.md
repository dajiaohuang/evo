## A grooved midrib and unknown seed fields {#s-51157241c3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or obovate leaves with acute or gradually narrowed bases and tips that are acute, rounded or tapering to a point. The leaves are papery; their flat margins lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis. Bract status is unknown, while smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth. Fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial label without range information {#s-e089661343}
The habitat field says “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category does not supply distribution information.
