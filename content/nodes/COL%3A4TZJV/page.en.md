## Distribution records {#s-ccfba23c62}
Mourthé et al. (2022) overlaid IUCN range maps on ten major Brazilian Amazon interfluves. Supplementary Table S1 lists Saimiri cassiquiarensis (Lesson, 1840) in 4 areas: Jaú, Napo, Imeri, Pantepui-Duida. Here, overlap is a region-level map result; it does not confirm animals at every site and is not a population count or complete range assessment.

## Evidence limits {#s-46ecd5a5e7}
The abstract and methods describe 80 species, while main-text Table 2 occupancy classes sum to 81 and report the 46 single-interfluve species as 57%, unlike the 58% stated in the text. Names and counts here come from Supplementary Table S1; they are range-map overlaps, not point-level field confirmations, population counts or complete range assessments. Chinese common names are editorial translations.
