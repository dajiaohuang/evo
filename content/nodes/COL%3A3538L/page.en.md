## Desmodium infractum {#s-30a441affd}

The Flora of Panama account describes a sprawling vine with three leaflets and hooked yellowish hairs on the stem; the leaflet undersides are paler and softly hairy. Its flowers range from magenta to white, and the jointed fruit bears 2–3 sections; the kidney-shaped seeds are about 3.5 × 2.5 mm. The account lists scattered southern Mexican localities (Oaxaca, Nayarit, Michoacán or Guerrero, Chiapas and Veracruz), specimens from every Central American country, Panama’s Veraguas, Los Santos and Canal Zone, and records in Colombia, Venezuela and Ecuador.
