## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a96bbcb321}
COL26.8 records Atractus microrhynchus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-d2e6f5fa2f}

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1bfff22776}
