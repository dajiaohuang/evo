## Recorded parent of Glyptosaurini {#s-a7827c98ef}
Glyptosaurini is listed at rank tribe in the PBDB classification route, with Glyptosaurinae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
