## A small, sweet-scented Freesia {#s-6c81df8917}
The regional flora describes Freesia caryophyllacea as a corm-bearing geophyte (a plant whose renewal organ is a swollen underground storage stem), 5–10 cm tall, with blunt, sword-shaped leaves and sweetly scented white flowers marked with yellow.

## Flowering season and Cape habitats {#s-aaaaa7718f}
Flowering is reported from April to June. The flora records clay and limestone soils, renosterveld (a Cape shrubland vegetation type) and coastal bush, with a regional range from Villiersdorp to Swellendam and Bredasdorp. This is a regional account, not a global range assessment.
