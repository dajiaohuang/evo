## Platycaulos acutus {#s-e10f974a61}
## A solitary female spikelet in marsh habitat

Platycaulos acutus is described as a dioecious perennial up to 1 m tall, growing in tufts or tangled mats, with branched, flattened stems. Female spikelets are solitary; the ovary has one chamber, and the female flower is 5–6 mm long. The regional account records montane marshes as habitat.
