## *Felicia hirsuta*: hairy leaves and a variable growth form {#s-20952dd4c0}
The regional account describes a rounded, roughly hairy shrublet to about 50 cm, with narrow gray-haired leaves often clustered in tufts. Its solitary flower heads have yellow centers and blue to mauve rays. A second passage emphasizes strong variation in growth form, leaf shape, and leaf thickness: plants may be compact and low or taller with long shoots, and leaves range from long and narrow to short, thick, and nearly round. The account records dry flats and slopes as habitat.
