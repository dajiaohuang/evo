## What traits are recorded for Annona senegalensis? {#s-c42cdb7df0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a smooth-barked tree with an upright trunk. Leaves have entire edges, curved veins, and blunt tips. Yellow or cream flowers grow in the leaf axils and have six petals; the sepals are slightly joined at the base. The aggregate fruit is syncarpous, meaning its carpels are fused. Seed color and surface are not specified.
