## Two reported maxima, two species {#s-e8c9d85c37}
The reported maximum snout–vent length is 87.4 mm for P. gularis and 91.3 mm for P. collicristatus. Snout–vent length runs from the snout to the vent, excluding the tail. The figures belong to different species; they do not describe variation within either species or the smallest and largest individuals across the genus.
