## Leaflet positions and tendril forms in the entry {#s-ab8b9913f8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists leaflets as alternate, opposite or subopposite. Tendrils are simple, bifid or trifid. Inflorescences have 1–5 flowers, and racemes are described as pedunculate or subsessile. Calyx lobes are unequal. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields retain alternatives and do not establish variation or a complete range.
