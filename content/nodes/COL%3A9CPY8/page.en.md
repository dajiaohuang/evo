## An undivided, long-pointed leaf {#s-6bfdc060d7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description distinguishes Bauhinia dubia by an undivided, oval-to-lance-shaped leaf with a base that ranges from slightly heart-shaped to notched and a long, tapering tip. The upper surface is smooth or nearly so; the lower may have fine hairs pressed against it. The flowers have a tubular or urn-shaped base and a calyx that splits into lobes as the flower opens. Its pod is recorded as opening elastically. Alternative states in the dataset describe source variation, not a single fixed combination.
