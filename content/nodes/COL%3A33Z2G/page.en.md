## ## Notched leaflets and clustered stamens {#s-52ef5dc5a4}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 13–25 pairs of oblong leaflets, with an upper value of 29; their tips are notched and their bases blunt. Panicles carry flowers with ten stamens whose filaments are joined in two bundles. Corolla color is listed as unknown, while the anthers open lengthwise. The oblong to oblong-elliptic fruit has a uniformly veined surface. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
