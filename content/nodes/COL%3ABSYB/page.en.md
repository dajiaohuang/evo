## Allagoptera caudescens: fronds, flowers and fruit {#s-939be8de6b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records adventitious roots and an aerial stem. The leaf sheath is fibrous and tomentose on its underside; the petiole is tomentose, the pinnae rigid and the leaf rachis glabrous.

The inflorescence is interfoliar and spike-like, with a long, glabrous rachis. The spathe is subwoody and fissured externally, and the peduncle is glabrous. Male flowers are cream-colored and trimerous; female flowers are cream-colored, sessile and trimerous. The fruit is greenish and obovoid. The field lists the endocarp as rigid or thin, the epicarp as glabrous and the mesocarp as fibrous. The seed has a basal embryo and ruminate endosperm.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The endocarp alternatives are retained as listed. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
