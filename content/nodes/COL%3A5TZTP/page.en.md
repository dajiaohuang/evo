## The name and this data account {#s-c705f0b6e6}
Amazona viridigenalis is an accepted Psittacidae species in COL26.8. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 5 measured birds, 4 with a complete morphological set. Usable counts may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-42c6f3252f}
Mean beak length is 26.6 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip. At that edge, mean width is 16.4 mm and depth is 27.5 mm. These are separate measurements at defined landmarks, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-b94d64d802}
Mean unflattened wing length from carpal joint to longest primary is 199 mm; mean tarsus length is 20.1 mm. A separate compilation gives mean mass as 316 g (Dunning category), which does not mean all measured birds were weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-2895d61956}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, Omnivore trophic niche and Insessorial lifestyle. These broad categories support comparison across species; they do not mean this bird uses only one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-0851d51bd6}
The migration field marks the record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps reach latitude endpoints of 21.21° and 25.68°. They do not give a full account of non-breeding movements or current global occupancy. The geographic and morphological data were compiled separately.

This page interprets historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages for the listed samples, not identification limits or individual variation ranges. Mass comes from separate literature or weighing sources, so morphology sample counts do not apply to it.
