## Norantea guianensis Aubl. {#s-5e5ce70485}
## Many-flowered clusters with orange to red nectaries

Norantea guianensis has leaves described as elliptic or obovate, with tips that may be acute or blunt and bases that may be wedge-shaped or acute. Its inflorescences are many-flowered, and the nectaries are orange or reddish. The habitat labels include both hemi-epiphytic growth, partly on other plants, and terrestrial growth; the source does not choose between them.
