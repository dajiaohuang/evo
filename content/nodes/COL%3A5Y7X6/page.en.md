## Seed records in forest edge and grassland {#s-cd1899667e}
At Ngel Nyaki Forest Reserve, three semi-habituated groups used forest edge and grassland. Researchers collected 164 fecal samples and recorded seed deposits in both habitats; many samples contained intact seeds. From December 2009 to September 2010, they walked six transects totaling 4.69 km on 18 occasions and logged more than 2,000 scans across 378 hours of troop time-use observations. These records describe three groups at one reserve, not a species-wide estimate of seed dispersal.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-ee7117233a}
The paper's seed samples came from one Nigerian montane reserve, and collection was concentrated in the dry season because rain washed deposits away. Population-level seed-dispersal rates also use local density and defecation rates measured from three captive monkeys, so they are estimates for the study landscape rather than a species-wide rate.
