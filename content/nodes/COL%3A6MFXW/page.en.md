## What branch, leaf and fruit traits mark Humiriastrum mussunungense? {#s-766ff3222c}
The terminal branches are hairless. Leaves have pointed, tapering tips, hairless blades, and stalks 3–10 mm long. The inflorescence has deciduous bracts, and the fruit is ellipsoid.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-268e7684ad}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
