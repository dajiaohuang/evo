## *Soliva sessilis*: low stems and axillary flower heads {#s-551bc2bc0e}
Brazilian Flora 2020 describes prostrate or trailing, hairy stems and alternate, stalked, hairy leaves. The small flower heads are sessile in the leaf axils; “sessile” means attached without a stalk. The bracts around each head are broadly egg-shaped to lance-shaped, and the receptacle beneath the florets is conical to convex.

The field records 4–6 sterile male tubular florets in the disc and 10–12 female marginal florets without a corolla. Its fruit is described as glabrous and ovate-lanceolate, with an entire wing and a prominent hairy tip. These are morphology fields in a regional Brazilian dataset, not a complete species-wide account.
