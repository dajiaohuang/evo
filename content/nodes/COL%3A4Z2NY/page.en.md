AVONET v7 lists *Sphyrapicus ruber* under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The record includes 8 measured individuals, 5 in a complete morphological set; trait means may use different sample sizes.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-e2d03db3b8}
Mean beak length is 21.5 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width at that landmark is 8 mm and depth 7.1 mm. These standardized dimensions are not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-dd864bbc3a}
Mean unflattened wing length (carpal joint to longest primary) is 126.6 mm; mean tarsus length is 21.6 mm. The separate AVONET mass mean is 54.79 g in the Dunning category; it is not a weight for each morphological specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-2605b91200}
AVONET codes habitat Woodland, trophic niche Invertivore and lifestyle Insessorial (perching-associated). These broad categories do not imply exclusive use of woodland or invertebrate food.

## Evidence limits {#s-3d7c1f6b65}
This account combines historical AVONET, 2015 life-history and EltonTraits records. AVONET morphometrics are sample-based species averages, mass comes from a separate source, and map endpoints concern historical breeding/resident geography. Life-history values are archive summaries, with possible taxonomic inheritance; a name and family match does not prove equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. EltonTraits percentages are literature-based relative weights rather than direct measurements. The underlying works have not all been reviewed; current range, population, conservation and complete biology require other sources.
