## Branches and leaves {#s-eab46782e6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives “10/20 m” in its plant-height field and describes the branches as striated; it does not explain whether the two height values are alternatives or endpoints. Leaves are compound, with leaflets described as oblong, obovate or oval-lanceolate. The leaflet-count text is “7.0 9.0 à 11,” too unclear to normalize as a range. Venation is called eucamptodromous: the side veins curve toward the edge and fade before reaching it, linked by cross-veins without conspicuous loops.

## Flowers {#s-d17f53ba37}
The inflorescences are described as crowded. Small bracteoles (bracts near a flower) fall away and sit at the base of the calyx. The calyx is cup-shaped with at least three lobes; the petals are unspotted and recorded as pink-lilac or reddish.

## Pod and seeds {#s-2bd3ad60e0}
The pod is described as woody to leathery and constricted between seeds, with a samaroid (samara-like) form. The vexillary, or banner-facing, margin is veined. The seeds are reniform (kidney-shaped).

## Habitat category {#s-dc7b8d13ff}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
