## Inflated bracts and a spiny corm base {#s-41d3e74bc6}
Lapeirousia dolomitica is up to 100 mm tall. Its dark corm tunics (fibrous outer layers around a swollen underground storage stem) bear prominent basal spines, and the inflated floral bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) have weak keels. The pale-blue to lilac flowers have a tube mostly 25–35 mm long.

## Stony, often shale soils {#s-5de52375e9}
The cited flora records stony soils, often shale.
