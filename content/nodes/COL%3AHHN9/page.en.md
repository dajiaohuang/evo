## White latex and smooth fruit in the flora record {#s-69ba6b65e1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records white latex, cylindrical branches with no thickened bark, and spaced leaves arranged alternately, one at a time along the stems. The leaf bases are flattened. Its flower clusters are broad-topped, branching groups at the branch tips or just below them. Sepals, the flower's outer leaf-like parts, are similar in length; petal lobes, the separated segments at the edge of the fused petals, are shorter than or as long as the tube formed by fused petals, and the flower's ovary, the part that later develops into fruit, is woolly-haired. The smooth fruit has visible ribs, a stalk, and distinct lenticels (small openings in its surface). This regional database entry gives no sampling frame or account of variation.
