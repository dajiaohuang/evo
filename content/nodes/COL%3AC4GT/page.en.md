## Alomiella hatschbachii {#s-ed176fc36f}
## Three-veined leaves and white tubular florets

The source records cylindrical or slightly striated stems with stalked glandular hairs. Leaves are alternate, though basal leaves may be opposite; the rhomboid blades have acute tips, cuneate bases, serrated margins and three veins arising near the base. Loose cymose secondary inflorescences carry about 30 phyllaries in three unequal, partly overlapping rows. Each head has 35–40 white, tubular-funnel-shaped florets. The obpyramidal cypselae have five ribs, a symmetric carpopodium and short deciduous pappus bristles.
