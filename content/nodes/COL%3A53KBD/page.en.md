## Stem, leaves, and inflorescence {#s-1676edb217}
The source describes a solitary underground stem with leaf-sheath remnants. Green pinnate leaves have persistent sheaths that wrap helically around the stem and have fibrous margins; the petiole margin is unarmed and the leaf axis slightly curved. Leaflets are leathery, rigid, uniformly arranged at one angle, with tips recorded as acute, rounded, or notched and asymmetric. Their surfaces are green and the same colour on both sides. The inflorescence is erect and spike-like, with a grooved waxy-coated spadix, a hairless main axis, and no rachillae. The fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves.

## Fruit {#s-74a0e8bd53}
Fruit is ovoid or elliptic, with a green-yellow woolly outer layer, fibrous succulent middle layer, and fusiform inner layer. The endosperm is homogeneous.
