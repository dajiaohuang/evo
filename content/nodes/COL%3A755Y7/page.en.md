## Accepted name and placement {#s-ddaafa4c4b}
COL26.8 lists Oreophrynella seegobini Kok, 2009 as a species in Anura, Bufonidae and Oreophrynella. The name and placement follow the release dated 20 August 2026; a record in another checklist or fossil database may use a different species concept.
