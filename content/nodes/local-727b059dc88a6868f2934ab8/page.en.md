## The source tadpole concept {#s-931aad3794}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Bufo torrenticola, in the genus Bufo and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Bufo torrenticola. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.
