## Recorded parent of Archosauriformes {#s-f8130b6a45}
Archosauriformes is listed at rank unranked clade in the PBDB classification route, with Crocopoda as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
