## A creeping growth form {#s-7e4f4d0c28}
The regional description records a creeping stem and oblong to obovate leaf blades with a bullate (blistered) surface and heart-shaped bases. Secondary veins are impressed into the blade; the spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) is oblong or linear, and the berry is green. These are listed taxonomic characters, not a sampled measure of variation.

## Soil-associated in the flora dataset {#s-301d25a278}
The habitat field in Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 uses “terrícola” for this species. That regional category has no attached locality or date and does not define the species’ range.
