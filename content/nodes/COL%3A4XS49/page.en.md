## Sisymbrium officinale {#s-5d0abb49dd}
## Basal leaf rosettes sit below long stems and short pods

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records stems up to 150 cm long and basal leaves arranged in a rosette. The conical fruits are up to 2 cm long and lie close against the flower stalk. Their stalks are 1–3 mm long, and the style at the fruit tip is indistinct. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
