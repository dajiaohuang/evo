## ## Many paired pinnae and rounded flower heads {#s-f8b9b078d5}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 leaf description records six to eleven pairs of pinnae, the primary divisions of a compound leaf, and eleven to twenty-seven pairs of smaller leaflets on each pinna. The stipules are described as alike in form. Round nectaries, or nectar-producing glands, on the leaf stalk are placed at the lowest pinna pair or just below it. The leaf axis carries rust-colored, dense woolly hairs. Flower clusters are recorded as globose, head-shaped racemes; the corolla, all petals together, has fine hairs. The fruit is an oblong, sickle-shaped follicle, a dry fruit that opens along one seam; seeds are round or elliptical. A separate habitat field says terrestrial but gives no locality or survey frame, so these regional rows do not establish full range or variation.
