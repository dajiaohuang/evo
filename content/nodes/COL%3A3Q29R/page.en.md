## Basal branching and white flowers {#s-3c2a1adfb3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records stems branching from the base. Leaves are spaced along the branches; the blades are membranous and lanceolate or elliptic, with conspicuous secondary veins. The inflorescence has leaf-like bracts. Flower buds are described as blunt or rounded, and the corolla is white. The source field also notes a deciduous corolla in its fruit entry, without further detail on that observation.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-e7ea4adae3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels *Irlbachia poeppigii* terrestrial; it gives no collection locality or more specific substrate.
