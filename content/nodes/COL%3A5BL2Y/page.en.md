## What does the description record about the tree and its leaves? {#s-c9ebfcc66f}
Vitex cooperi is described as a tree reaching 26 m, with a fluted, buttressed trunk up to 75 cm in diameter. Its leaves have three leaflets: the central one is broadly elliptic and 4.5–22 cm long, while the side leaflets are smaller. The record also notes axillary, many-flowered clusters and fragrant blue or lavender flowers. These are source-description traits, not a population survey or range estimate.

## What range and habitat did the historical flora report? {#s-6eb65c81d8}
The 2013 Flora of Panama account reports Vitex cooperi from Guatemala to Panama. It describes wet thickets from sea level to 600 m and says the species was fairly common on hills bordering Gatun Lake on the Atlantic watershed. These are historical regional statements, not current range or frequency estimates; the source version was retrieved on 8 September 2026.
