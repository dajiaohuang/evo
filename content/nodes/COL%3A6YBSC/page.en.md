## Selaginella salinoi {#s-35edfb019a}
## Prostrate stems and coloured spores

The source describes prostrate, smooth, unjointed stems that are greenish or straw-coloured, with rhizophores at axillary or dorsal positions. The small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are ovate, rounded at the base, and have a rounded or blunt tip; idioblast cells are present. Dorsal leaves range from nearly round to broadly ovate, with asymmetric bases and acute or blunt tips; idioblasts are also recorded there. Axillary leaves are ovate with rounded bases and rounded or blunt tips. Megaspores are cream-coloured and microspores yellow.
