## Ruschia cupulata {#s-632b001d30}
## Rigid grey branches and fine bumps along leaf ridges

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a shrublet that may lie down or grow upright, with erect forms reaching 20 cm. Its rigid, grey branches ascend; short shoots arise from the leaf axils. The upright leaves are three-angled in cross-section, 15–30 mm long and 3–4 mm wide, with fine bumps along their raised keels.

Flowers grow in clusters and reach up to 28 mm across. The five-chambered fruit has a funnel-shaped base. The habitat account records coastal areas.
