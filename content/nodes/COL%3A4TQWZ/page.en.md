## Ruschia diversifolia {#s-8a743cfdda}
## Long leaves with firm, lightly toothed edges

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a prostrate, sprawling shrublet up to 14 cm tall, with stiff trailing branches up to 25 cm long that change from reddish to grey. The leaves are three-angled in cross-section, with firm, cartilage-like edges bearing small teeth. On long shoots they reach 70 mm; leaves on short shoots are shorter. The leaf blade extends beyond the fused basal sheath.

The flower clusters are purplish and reach 28 mm across. The account records the fruits as unknown and leaves the habitat uncertain.
