## A high-climbing vine with large leaflets {#s-a1c1c657ac}
The Flora of Panama describes Canavalia oxyphylla as a vine that twines on shrubs and climbs high. Its leaves have three paper-like leaflets, usually 10 to 12 cm long, with sparse hairs on both surfaces. Axillary flower clusters bear white, pink or lavender flowers. The oblong pod has ridges along its seams and one additional ridge on each valve; its tan seeds are compressed and oval.

## A historical regional range {#s-211def2edf}
The same Flora of Panama source gives the range as southern Mexico to northern South America. This is not presented as a current global inventory.
