## Distribution and habitat {#s-50065942b3}
The 2013 description linked the known specimens to Forêt de Marohita in eastern Madagascar, within the broader Forêt de Marolambo area; the wider distribution was then unknown. On a later visit, the authors reported that the forest had become substantially degraded since the original 2003 collection. This records the paper's place and time, not a current habitat survey.

## Morphology {#s-3ff26e72ad}
The description treated the Marolambo sample as an independently evolving mouse-lemur lineage and noted its comparatively large body mass in the data then available. The authors also stated that few specimens had been collected and that field studies had not covered its natural, social or life history. Individual weights and one pregnant female are not population statistics.

## Classification {#s-c9f4ca492d}
The catalogues differ: COL26.8 retains *Microcebus marohita* as accepted species 42SBV, whereas the current MDD page treats marohita as a synonym of *M. jollyae*.

## Evidence limits {#s-cf99651475}
COL26.8 accepts this name, while the current MDD page lists *M. marohita* as a synonym of *M. jollyae*. The biological account is based on the small 2013 sample and study locality. The Chinese translation and English common label Marohita Mouse Lemur are editorial.
