## A tiny wing fragment from the Paskov core {#s-8a7d8d817a}
A roughly 10 mm crushed forewing and counterpart from the lowermost Namurian Paskov drill core preserve cross-veins and branches of major veins. Dated to about 324 Ma, the specimen was assigned to Archaeorthoptera from incomplete venation. It records an early winged insect, but cannot date powered-flight origins or the first appearance of all Pterygota.
