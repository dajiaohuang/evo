## A sampled sister relationship links petrigena and perparva {#s-e3b85ec321}
The combined mitochondrial–BDNF analysis recovered petrigena and perparva as sisters with strong reported support within Group I. This uses the study’s reference dataset, not two newly surveyed full populations. It does not measure contemporary gene flow, date their separation or establish identical habitat or breeding biology. Strong support for one pair does not resolve the deeper Microhyla–Nanohyla–Glyphoglossus relationships, which the same study found weakly resolved.
