## Dense foliage and slender capsule horns {#s-a97eecaf05}
The source description records a closely leafy, hispid subshrub to 40 cm tall, erect or spreading. Leaves are sessile, fascicled and linear lanceolate, hispid below and along the margins, with spine tipped apices. Flowers are sessile, axillary, and pink or white. Capsules are hispid with slender, elongated horns. The ecology field gives low sandy or clay slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
