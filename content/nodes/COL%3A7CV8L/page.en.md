## Triplaris americana {#s-e1b0cb461a}
## Panicles carry flowers above smooth stems

Triplaris americana is recorded with hairless stems and oblong or oblong-elliptic leaves. The leaf-base ochrea is shed; the grooved petiole is smooth and not jointed to it. Panicle-like thyrses have a hairy, whitish axis and narrow, hairy bracteoles. Male flowers have a bell-shaped hypanthium. The fruiting perianth bears spatulate or obovate wings; its tube is ovoid to globose and pubescent, while the three-angled nut has no beak. The habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
