## What flower details are recorded for Chiropetalum foliosum? {#s-cf1c3b76ab}
The ovate leaves have toothed edges and simple or Malpighian hairs. Racemes carry seven or fewer flowers, with one female flower at the base. Male flowers have three-lobed petals and five stamens. The styles and floral nectaries are hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d7ac76ede4}
The dataset lists it as terrestrial and gives no site-specific detail.
