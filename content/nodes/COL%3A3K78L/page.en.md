## A cushion of grey leaves in sandstone crevices {#s-7e4a206272}
This account describes a gnarled shrublet only about 5 cm tall, growing as a dense cushion. Its small, overlapping leaves are grey-felted, and single whitish flower heads sit at the branch tips. The source distinguishes it from H. sessile and H. archeri by its shorter, broader leaves and blunter bracts; it records the plant in sandstone crevices at 1,500–2,000 m.
