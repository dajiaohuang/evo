## *Didymoglossum ovale* {#s-221ab1bfe2}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — creeping; leaf-related structures — pinnate (side divisions arranged along a central axis); spores (reproductive cells) — with a three-rayed mark. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
