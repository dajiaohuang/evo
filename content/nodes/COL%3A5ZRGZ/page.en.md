## What stem, leaf and flower traits does the flora field record? {#s-0927d8bf3a}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field records a taproot and cylindrical stems bearing scale-like hairs on branches, leaves and flowers. Simple, opposite leaves are elliptic, short-stalked and papery to somewhat leathery, with 4–8 pairs of secondary veins and scattered scale-like hairs on the upper surface. Terminal racemes bear radially symmetric, four-part flowers. Four petals sit at the opening of the upper floral cup, and there are eight stamens. The nectar-producing disk is well developed and hairy. The floral cup narrows like a funnel at its base and becomes cup-shaped above; the ovary is below the other floral parts, has two carpels and one chamber, and is recorded with 2–6 ovules. The fruit is a drupe with four wings. This is the database's regional description, not a global account.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ad00b96c63}
The same Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and supplies no locality or collection date.
