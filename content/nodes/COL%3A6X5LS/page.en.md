## A dated multicriteria conservation priority {#s-0fbaec78c9}
The 2024 Chilean assessment assigns Rhinella atacamensis a printed conservation-priority index of 1.13 and places it in the low category in Table 4. The study reviews 58 selected species using literature, citizen-science records and government plans, rather than newly censusing every population. Its index combines distribution, threat, population trend, taxonomic uniqueness and body-size scores with a protected-area term. The value is a management ranking under this model, not an extinction probability, a current IUCN assessment or a direct measurement of population size. “Non-priority” and “low” do not mean biologically safe or no need for conservation.
