## Limits attached to these records {#s-49304902fa}
This page joins only this batch’s two measured records, two historical life-history entries and two EltonTraits entries for Eudocimus albus and E. ruber. The counts are route coverage, not a complete descendant denominator or a genus-level synthesis. AVONET mass values are compiled species means. Life-history summaries retain historical inheritance and may combine source rows; EltonTraits percentages standardize literature descriptions. Same-name links do not prove current taxonomic concept equivalence. Individual and seasonal variation, current range, populations, conservation and unrepresented descendants need separate evidence.
