## Five-veined leaves and hairy stems {#s-a3ffb6f138}
The description records a woody underground base and hairy, glandular stems. Opposite leaves range from lance-shaped to round and have five main veins; the surfaces may be smooth, rough-haired or dotted with glands. Bell-shaped flower heads grow in compact or corymb-like clusters, and the dry fruits are cone-shaped.
