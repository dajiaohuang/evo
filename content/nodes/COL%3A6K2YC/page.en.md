## Substrates recorded in the flora dataset {#s-b2e6ee8928}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record labels the species *Corticícola*, or growing on bark.

This is a substrate category, not a collection locality; the field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

It also records *Rupícola*, meaning that it grows on rock.

This second category is not a mapped collection site and does not supply a survey date, range, or sampling-completeness measure.

## Limits of this record {#s-39d2baec06}
No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

These substrate labels do not describe the life cycle or reproduction, which were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
