## Macleania pentaptera Hoerold {#s-4667ba6874}
## Winged flower base and scarlet tubular corolla

Macleania pentaptera is an epiphytic shrub, often with a swollen, tuber-like root. Its leathery ovate leaves have strongly heart-shaped bases. Short axillary racemes bear three to eight flowers. The flower base has five fleshy wings that rise above the calyx rim and form a short, leathery spur. The tubular corolla is scarlet to orange-red with a green tip; ten stamens are about half as long as the corolla. The source says the known range was recently extended to eastern Panama; otherwise it was known from Colombia and Ecuador.
