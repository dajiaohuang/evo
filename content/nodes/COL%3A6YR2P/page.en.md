## A regional description of leaves and flowers {#s-4d88d87136}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes the leaves as lanceolate, elliptic or oval and notes Malpighian-type trichomes (a named form of plant hair) on the stem. Its flower description lists four stamens, purple anthers with a purple rostrum (a beak-like tip), petals without a basal claw (the narrow, stalk-like base of a petal), and no staminodes, the sterile structures resembling stamens. It also records the hypanthium—the cup-like part around the ovary—as shorter than, equal to, or slightly longer than the capsule. These alternatives come from one regional data field; the record does not establish how common each form is, how much populations vary, or which life stage was observed.

## A single habitat category {#s-3a236e9e2b}
The same dataset labels the habitat “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial or land-growing. It gives no collecting locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not say whether the record concerns wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This is one regional habitat label, not a full account of the species’ ecology.
