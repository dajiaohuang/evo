## Cattleya luteola {#s-e9584dc7c3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records Cattleya luteola with flattened, clavate pseudobulbs and one elliptic leaf. Its greenish-yellow to ochre flowers have a slightly three-lobed lip, with pale-yellow middle and side lobes; red veins are noted on the inner side lobes. The habitat field lists it as epiphytic.
