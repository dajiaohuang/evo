## Older genus characters were compared rather than uniquely validated {#s-aff31ff7be}
The discussion reviews the earlier Allopaa diagnosis: first finger longer than second, blunt digit tips, no tarsal fold, full webbing, a limited outer fifth-toe fringe, an internal vocal sac and male nuptial spines. It argues that these cited characters are not unique because they vary among Nanorana. These are historical comparative diagnostic traits, not measurements from a new hazarensis series; lack of a unique generic character does not make every individual identical to another species.
