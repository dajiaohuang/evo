## ## Winged leaf stalks and hirsute fruit {#s-8977c270bb}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with long, stiff hairs and lenticels. The persistent stipules are sickle-shaped or ovate; the petiole and main leaf axis are winged. Leaves have 3–7 pairs of leaflets and stalked, cylindrical nectaries. The spike-like inflorescence bears stalkless flowers with tubular calyces. The flat fruit has papery valves and long, stiff hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
