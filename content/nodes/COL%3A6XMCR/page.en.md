## *Saranthe klotzschiana*: a tall rosette above an elongated rhizome {#s-497c017b1f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description records an elongated underground rhizome and aerial shoots that form a rosette or stand upright, reaching 40–100 cm. The oblong leaves taper to a long point; their stalks and sheaths are described as hairless. The flowering structure is loose, with one to three flower clusters at a node and 18–32 persistent, papery bracts that do not overlap.

Each bract encloses a two-flowered cluster; bracteoles are absent in the recorded profile. The flowers include two nearly equal, petal-like outer staminodes. The habitat field says terrestrial only and gives no named locality or date, so the account remains within the regional source description.
