## Dearc’s three-dimensional Skye skeleton {#s-c2abe1da41}
A three-dimensionally preserved, associated skeleton is the holotype (name-bearing specimen) of Dearc. A 2025 study expanded its skeletal description and used bone landmarks to discuss muscle anatomy and function. This account centers on one specimen; preservation gaps limit reconstruction and do not establish genus-wide ecology.

## Limits of the selected Dearc records {#s-2865f9e487}
One three-dimensionally preserved holotype anchors the anatomical account. Occurrence rows and broad-age brackets are separate from specimen counts and do not measure a lifespan. Proposed muscle and hand-claw functions are interpretations of preserved anatomy.
