## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9e7a359c9f}
COL26.8 records Sonora palarostris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-940b62fad2}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 430 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-70e8f47ec4}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d735ffdeba}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Slavens and Slavens 1999. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2015.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-6c058a5194}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
