## Astroloba congesta {#s-e66c9a97a6}
Astroloba congesta is a low succulent with a leafy stem to about 25 cm and leaves arranged in five straight rows. Its loose upright inflorescence bears green flowers tinged with cream. The regional account reports flowering throughout the year, with peaks in January and September, and records karroid flats across several grassland and thicket vegetation types.
