## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-be0da95a7d}
COL26.8 records Mesoclemmys perplexa as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-aea271fa23}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 193.7 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013. The row reports a female maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 193.7 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Powell et al. 2016. These overall and sex-specific maxima cite separate studies, but the row gives no matched sample ranges, sample sizes, or localities; they do not establish population-level sexual dimorphism.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-fd6dc0cc52}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-dc5a4434b1}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-e9646e7a29}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
