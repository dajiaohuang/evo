## Cyclanthera quinquelobata (Vell.) Cogn. {#s-f393bd837c}
## A densely spiny, unevenly swollen fruit

Cyclanthera quinquelobata has simple leaves with lobes or smaller lobules, up to 10 cm long; nectar glands occur at the base of the blade. Male flowers grow in axillary racemes longer than the nearby leaf stalk, and female flowers have stalks under 10 mm long. The fruit is gibbous, meaning unevenly swollen, and densely covered in prickles.
