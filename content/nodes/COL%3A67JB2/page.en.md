## An elliptic-to-obovate blade {#s-7e41e7c8e5}
The regional entry describes blades as elliptic or obovate (egg-shaped with the broader end toward the tip), with tips that may taper or be blunt and bases described as obtuse, truncate, or heart-shaped. Secondary veins are prominent; berries are wine-colored. The alternatives are listed descriptors, not a quantified variation study.

## Epiphytic in the habitat field {#s-45d11a2e27}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 habitat label is “epífita,” meaning growth on another plant. The entry names no host or locality and does not define a complete range.
