## The source concept {#s-0d200abd72}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Physalaemus caete in row Anf3288, under the genus Physalaemus, family Leiuperidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page identifies the source row; it does not establish that the row matches a current COL26.8 species concept.
