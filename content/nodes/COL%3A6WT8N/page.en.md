## *Raulinoreitzia tremula*: cream flowers and a persistent bristly pappus {#s-775d0bba84}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records a hairless, cylindrical stem and linear-lance-shaped leaves with long-tapered tips and bases. The flower heads are cylindrical; the overlapping bracts are listed in several shapes. The tubular corolla is cream-coloured, five-lobed, and glandular. Its four- or five-ribbed fruitlet carries a single row of about 30 persistent pappus bristles. These are regional morphology fields, not a complete species-wide description.
