## Overview {#s-c692a27c96}
The original description used nine live animals captured at Andasibe in March 2005 (six males and three females); the authors reported external measurements and sample masses. It is a taxonomic sample near the type locality, not an island-wide body-size survey.

## Morphology {#s-1b43fe4d37}
The authors described it as small-bodied, with bright maroon-orange dorsal fur and a creamy-white underside; this description supported diagnosis of the sampled animals. Variation among individuals and populations elsewhere is not exhausted by nine animals.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-578bfbf3e7}
The paper lists Andasibe in eastern Madagascar as the type locality. This page does not turn one type locality into a modern range boundary; differences among lowland and highland sites require their own survey evidence.

## Evidence limits {#s-80a58044db}
