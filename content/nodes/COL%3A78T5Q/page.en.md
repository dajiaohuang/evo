## Raputia megalantha {#s-2cf0be34e9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records three auriculate-based leaflets, a corolla about 30 mm long and finely strigose fruit segments.

## Three leaflets and a long corolla {#s-47b00b803a}
The record describes three leaflets with auriculate bases and petioles bearing fine strigulose hairs. Inflorescences are 2.5–6 cm long, and the corolla is about 30 mm. Fruit mericarps have strigulose indument. A separate habitat field classifies the setting as “Terrícola” (terrestrial); it gives no locality or complete distribution.
