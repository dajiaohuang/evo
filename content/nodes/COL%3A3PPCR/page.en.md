## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-c2841f2019}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with fine, very short hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Leaves have ovate stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) that fall away; the leaf stalk (petiole) is recorded as margined or winged, while the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) is winged. Each leaf has four or five leaflet pairs. Stalked nectar glands (nectaries) are cup- or funnel-shaped. The inflorescence is spike-like; the outer floral cup (calyx) is funnel-shaped and each flower lacks a separate stalk. The pod walls (valves) are leathery. The fruit shape is recorded as constricted, sinuous, or curved, and its surface is hairless. This is a single regional dataset field, so it does not establish the traits' variation or how widely they occur.

## Habitat category {#s-a567069376}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat field “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial: growing on land. This is the category recorded in one dataset row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
