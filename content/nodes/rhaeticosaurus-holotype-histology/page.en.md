## What Rhaeticosaurus bone tissue can tell us {#s-5b96b2b885}
The articulated Rhaetian holotype LWL-MFN P 64047 preserves plesiosaur anatomy; sampled humeral and femoral sections show rapidly deposited, highly vascularized bone tissue. Its basal pliosaurid placement comes from phylogenetic analysis. Growth rate and metabolism are inferred from histology rather than measured in the animal, so direct observations and interpretations remain distinct.
