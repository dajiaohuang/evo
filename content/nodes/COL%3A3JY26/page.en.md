## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Hebepetalum roraimense? {#s-31e21cdb76}
The cylindrical branches are hairless. Leaves are alternate and spiral, with blades that may be elliptic or obovate, hairless, and marked by looping veins and a submarginal vein. The blade edge may be rounded-toothed or carry bristle-like teeth; tips vary from notched or rounded to pointed forms. Axillary or terminal panicles bear flowers with unequal sepals; the sepals may be leathery or thin, with entire, hairy margins. The elliptic petals are hairy along the edges and inner surface. There are ten stamens with flattened filaments, four ovary chambers and four styles. The elliptic drupe has four ridges and a hard, peeling outer layer; the seed is elliptic and flattened.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-571e2e8ec6}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
