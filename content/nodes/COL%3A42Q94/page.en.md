## What leaf and flower details are recorded for Micrandra rossiana? {#s-50da463646}
The branches are pubescent. Leaves are elliptic-oblong or obovate, with a basal, dish-shaped gland and side veins that loop near the margin. The underside may have hairs along the veins or tufts between major veins; the upper surface is hairless or sparsely hairy along the veins. Bisexual panicles bear male flowers with sepals fused halfway, eight stamens, and a pistillode. Female flowers have a hairy ovary, a sessile style, a head-shaped stigma, and staminodes. The fruit is pubescent, and the seed has a caruncle.
