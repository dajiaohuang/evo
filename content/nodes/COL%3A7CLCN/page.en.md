## How to read reproductive records {#s-707cbee868}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, temperature-dependent sex determination (TSD), and a consolidated mean of 12 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch. The summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily the arithmetic average of observed clutches. The mean is not a fixed count for each clutch. These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
