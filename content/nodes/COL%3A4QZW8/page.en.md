## Stems and flowers in the 2013 flora account {#s-2ffbdfb902}
Bruyns’s 2013 South African flora account compares *Q. pallens* with *Q. parviflora* and notes mottled stems. It records pedicels—the stalks of individual flowers—3–5 mm long and corollas (the petals collectively) 4–8 mm across; the corolla interior is pale green, without markings, hairs, or cilia, and the corona, a crown-like floral structure, is pale green to white. These are descriptions in a regional account, not population-wide measurements.

## Variation and floral comparison in the 2005 treatment {#s-6fce42609b}
Bruyns’s 2005 diagnostic account says specimens are often scandent among bushes: in that condition stems reach 300 mm and are slightly stouter. Some more exposed specimens were more slender than usual and darker. Flowers occur in small numbers, face partly upward on fairly long pedicels, and have small corollas with narrow lobes that spread or bend back; the lobe tips are pale green, becoming white toward the centre, with no dark spots or bars, hairs, or marginal cilia. The nearly white corona has slender, deeply split outer lobes; its inner lobes lack dorsal projections and mostly cover the anthers, the pollen-bearing tips of the stamens. The account interprets this combination as a close affinity with *Q. parviflora* subsp. *parviflora*; it also says that comparator retains purple bars, sometimes faint, and fine cilia along the lobe margins. This is an affinity interpretation, not a claim of direct ancestry.

## Habitat in the regional account {#s-4d5786da5f}
The South African flora account lists rocky slopes inside bushes. It gives no observation locality, date, or population frequency, so this habitat entry does not define the species’ full range.
