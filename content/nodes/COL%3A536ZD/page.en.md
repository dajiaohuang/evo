## Terminal clusters and a leathery fruit wall {#s-a9f8945899}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists tendrils as present or solitary and spines as absent. Leaves have petioles about 7 mm long and three to five veins; the lower vein axils on the underside are not bearded. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are described as corymbiform. The corolla is tubular or salver-shaped, with a tube longer than its lobes; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 2–4 cm in diameter, with a leathery fruit wall (pericarp) 0.5–1 mm thick. The seeds are recorded as three to five, with a crust-like seed coat. The source row leaves more than one state for the tendrils.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-0a3bf8446c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Strychnos araguaensis* as terrestrial but names no collection locality.
