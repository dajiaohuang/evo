## Confirmed locality kept separate from an expected range {#s-54319ab53e}
The monograph listed male holotype ZSI A11605 from Cona County, Shannan, Xizang, at 1110 m. It recognized the species only from the type locality at approximately 1100 m, while citing an expectation that it might occur at neighbouring southeastern Xizang localities. Those expected localities are not presented as confirmed occurrences or evidence for a wider occupied range.

## Original-description male diagnosis {#s-01441cd75d}
Based on Mahony and colleagues (2013), the monograph reported four adult males at 27.5–30.6 mm snout–vent length. The separately listed types were one male holotype and two male paratypes; the four-male diagnostic denominator is not silently equated with that three-type list. The diagnosis described no vomerine ridges, teeth or subarticular tubercles, rudimentary basal toe webbing with wide fringes, and a subgular vocal sac without nuptial pads or spines in breeding males. No female measurement was supplied in this diagnosis.
