## Two habitat categories in the flora record {#s-b289530df5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists *Rupícola*, meaning growth on rock.

It also lists *Terrícola*, the dataset's terrestrial category.

These categories describe habitat or substrate, not collection localities. The field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Limits of this record {#s-06b82e6e1d}
The field does not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat categories do not describe life cycle or reproduction; those were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
