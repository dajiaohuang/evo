## Bifrenaria mellicolor {#s-6ddf8e050b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row describes a tufted orchid with pseudobulbs that are not laterally compressed and a short inflorescence bearing one to three flowers. The sepals and petals range from brown to green without spots; the lip is pink to purple, and its conspicuous spur bends strongly. The same regional record lists it as epiphytic.
