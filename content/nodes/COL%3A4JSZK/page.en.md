## Leaf, inflorescence and fruit characters in the entry {#s-69a5917edf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records stipules with a narrow-triangular/triangular form; the leaflet undersides and leaf rachis are glabrous. Distal leaflets are listed as 2–6.5 cm or 4–11 (–25) cm long. The inflorescence is a raceme, described as erect and dense or pendulous and dense; it is glabrous and congested, with 20–100 flowers per raceme. Bracts are oblong, 2–3 mm long and 1–2 mm or 0.5–1 mm wide, and are recorded as early-deciduous. Bracteoles are oval, 1–2 mm long and 1–2 mm wide, and are early-deciduous or persist after anthesis. Flowers are at least 10 mm long, with short claws on the wing and keel petals. The samara is oblong. No habitat field is included, so this description does not establish habitat or range.
