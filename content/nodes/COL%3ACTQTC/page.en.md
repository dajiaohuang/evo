## form {#s-2a8780bbf4}
In Australian funnel-web spiders, the abdominal spinnerets and the male's second pair of legs help distinguish members. The Australian Museum describes a prominent mating spur on the tibia of that leg as characteristic of Atrax; males in the other funnel-web genera differ in this feature.

## Scope of this account {#s-63c5d9f25d}
This account describes Australian funnel-web spiders. Genus and species counts change as classifications are revised, so no current inventory is given here.
