## What flower and seed traits are recorded for Clusia spiritu-sanctensis? {#s-7bc756b3aa}
The plant has a taproot and additional roots, with stalked, leathery, obovate leaves whose secretory channels are inconspicuous. Upright flower clusters bear four sepals and five petals; the white corolla has a red center. Male flowers have 31 stamens or more than 40, according to the alternatives in the record; the stamens are separate, resin-bearing, and the pistillode is reduced. The ovary has five chambers. Sepals, petals, and staminodes are not recorded as persisting on mature fruit. Seeds have an aril.
