## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-6260513666}
COL26.8 records Emydocephalus orarius as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-088c81c06f}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ea72aa3630}
