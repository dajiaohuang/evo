## Leaves, extrafloral nectaries and a two winged samara {#s-5410a3a4b8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a simple root without a xylopodium. The plant is subwoody and erect, with eglandular trichomes; extrafloral nectaries near the leaf bases are absent. Leaf blades are linear or lanceolate, with petioles over 1.5 mm and membranaceous texture; tips are acute, acuminate or apiculate. The fruit is a two winged samara with one seed. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields retain alternatives and do not establish variation or a complete range.
