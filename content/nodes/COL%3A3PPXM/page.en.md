## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-1a5b18c015}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with velvety hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Ovate stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) fall away. The leaf stalk (petiole) and central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are cylindrical; the field lists three, four or five leaflet pairs. A stalkless, saucer-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. The inflorescence is listed as spike-like or racemose (flowers arranged along a main axis); the calyx (outer floral cup) is bell-shaped, and flowers are described as stalkless or nearly so. The pod walls (valves) are woody; the fruit is flat and velvety-haired. This single regional field preserves the listed alternatives but does not establish how traits vary or how widely they occur.

## Habitat category {#s-c97bb05c26}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
