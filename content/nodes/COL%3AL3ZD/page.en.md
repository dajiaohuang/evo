## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the flora description {#s-65dc64d5e5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records unarmed branches and lance-shaped stipules. The leaf blade is divided into two nearly round lobes, with a heart-shaped base and rounded tip. Its upper surface is hairless with sunken veins; the lower surface has short, fine hairs that lie flat, prominent secondary veins and forked glandular hairs. Flower buds are tubular and five-ribbed, with five notches at the tip. The hypanthium (floral cup) is tubular, and the calyx splits into two to five lobes when the flower opens. Petals are linear; ten anthers are recorded. The staminal column has long, soft hairs on its inner face, while the stalked ovary is covered with dense rusty-coloured hairs. The legume is recorded as opening elastically. This is a regional dataset description and does not assess variation or distribution across the species.
