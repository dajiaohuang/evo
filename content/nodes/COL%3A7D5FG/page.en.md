## Long leaves and yellow flowers {#s-44403043c3}
Flora do Brasil records Trimezia steyermarkii with flat, linear to sword-shaped leaves up to 80 cm long and yellow flowers. Up to eight leaves may be present at flowering; the single bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower) can reach 40 cm, and the inner tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal) tips are reflexed.
