## Notobubon laevigatum {#s-1ca04ec6ba}
## Entire leaf segments and variable shrub size

One flora description calls this a yellow-flowered shrub up to 1 m tall, with pinnate to more divided leaves and entire leaflets. A taxonomic discussion notes wide variation: plants are usually larger, reaching 1.5 m, but may stay smaller in drier sites. The ultimate leaf segments are usually entire and narrow, though some on one plant may be two- or three-lobed. The discussion distinguishes it by leaves over 20 mm long and tiny sepals under 0.2 mm, and groups it with two congeners that lack the fuller fruit oil-duct pattern seen in other species. The fruit is blunt at both ends; the habitat record says grassland.
