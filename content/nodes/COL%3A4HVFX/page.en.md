## Pilosocereus pentaedrophorus {#s-5bfe27eaca}
## The source retains two rib-count ranges

The stems branch above the base and have isolated areoles with conspicuous central spines. The morphology record gives two rib-count ranges—four to six or six to ten—rather than one value. No cephalium is recorded; the flower tube is green and curved, the fruit scar is deeply inset, and the seeds are smooth. This entry supplies no habitat or locality.
