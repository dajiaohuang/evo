## Two habitat categories in a regional record {#s-355e07528c}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Breutelia grandis (Hampe) Kindb. at species rank. The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data for this accepted name.

One Brazilian Flora category is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

A separate entry is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. These labels record habitat or substrate categories, not specific collection sites or a full account of the moss's ecology.

Both categories are regional dataset fields; they have no collection location or survey date attached here.

The first source field gives no collection place, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

The second source field likewise gives no collection place, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

The fields do not specify wild or managed status.

No distinct morphology field was included.

The source habit categories are not life-history evidence; life cycle and reproduction were not assessed.

The two source categories do not describe interactions, variation, locality or sampling coverage.

No species-level phylogenetic or comparative evidence is included.

No locality or range evidence is included; this regional flora record does not establish a complete distribution.

No species-level fossil evidence was assessed.

No conservation assessment was reviewed.
