## atlas-profile / overview {#s-c5ae006ec4}
Phosphatized fossils from the Lower Cambrian Chiungchussu Formation in Yunnan preserve *Yicaris dianensis* at several growth stages. Its limbs include leaf-shaped exites, or outer lobes, and resemble those of eucrustaceans. A character analysis placed *Yicaris* near crown Eucrustacea, but that is a phylogenetic interpretation, not evidence that it was a direct ancestor or a date for the group's origin.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-57da17a949}
Multiple growth stages preserve appendage structure in phosphatized fossils. The 521–514 Ma interval is a broad formation context, not a species duration; placement near crown Eucrustacea and the homology of the exites remain interpretations of anatomical characters.
