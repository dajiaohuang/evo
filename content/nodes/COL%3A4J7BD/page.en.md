## Piptolepis leptospermoides {#s-812e47a156}
## Tiny leaves with rolled-under margins

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes oblanceolate leaves under 1 cm long and less than 5 mm wide. They are not glaucous, their margins roll under, and the upper face is silky. Flower heads are solitary and each has fewer than 14 florets. The dry fruits have hairy ribs and a two-row pappus whose rows differ in length. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
