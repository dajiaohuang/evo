## Behavioral records {#s-d13833cedf}
At Danum Valley Conservation Area, Inoue, Sinun and Okanoya documented activity budgets, travel distance, sleeping time, activity height and travel order for wild East Bornean grey gibbons. The study offers a way to read daily behavior through measured variables rather than a general species template. Its observations belong to that conservation area and study period, not every population across Borneo.
