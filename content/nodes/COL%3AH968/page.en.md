## Linear leaves and sepal characters in the entry {#s-9796283249}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records fleshy roots and somewhat tufted branching, with pubescent branches. Leaves are linear, with acute bases and tips; petioles are 1–3 mm long. The inflorescence has oval, deciduous bracts and a somewhat lax raceme. The ovary is oblong; pedicels are puberulous and erect. Outer sepals are ciliate, with glands on the margin; inner sepals are asymmetric and obovate. Seeds are oblong with a horny caruncle. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or range.
