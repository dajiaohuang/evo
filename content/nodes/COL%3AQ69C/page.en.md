## Leaves and flowers {#s-3f08d76e1a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes underground bulbs and leaf blades with keel-like ridges. The inflorescence is recorded as pseudolateral, with terminal bracts present and a sessile rhipidium. The outer bracts of the rhipidium are 5–17 mm long and the inner bracts 10–26 mm. Flowers are lilac or bluish, without glandular hairs. The anthers are about 4–5 mm long; the style is three-forked, with its entire portion measuring 0.9–2.5 mm.

This is a morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, or population variation.

## Recorded growth form {#s-506f299a82}
The dataset labels the growth form “terrestrial” (Portuguese: *terrícola*).

This is a habitat or substrate category from the Brazilian Flora dataset row; it supplies no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
