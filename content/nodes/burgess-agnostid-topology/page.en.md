## What soft parts reveal about Burgess Shale agnostids {#s-08a787b679}
Adult fossils of Peronopsis and Ptychagnostus from the Burgess Shale preserve soft appendages as well as the body posture called enrolment, in which the animal curled up. Researchers scored these features in an arthropod character matrix; their analysis places agnostinids as close relatives of polymeroid trilobites. That placement depends on which taxa and anatomical features are compared. A swimming-near-the-seafloor way of life is an ecological interpretation, not something the fossils directly show.
