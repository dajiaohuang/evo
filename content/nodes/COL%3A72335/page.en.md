## Linear leaves and stalked flower heads {#s-2eb57fc92e}
This record leaves the root form unknown and describes few-branched stems with leaves along them. The narrow, leathery leaves are smooth on both sides and have entire edges. Stalked flower heads sit singly or in small clusters, with 4–5 rows of bracts and about 40–60 florets. The top-shaped fruits may be silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
