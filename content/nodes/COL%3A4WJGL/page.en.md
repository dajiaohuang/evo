## Senecio albus {#s-280a372c1e}
## White wool covers the leaves; ray florets are absent

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes *Senecio albus* as an upright, herbaceous perennial. Its leaves cluster near the base and become smaller higher on the stem. They clasp the stem or lack a stalk, and their lance-shaped to elliptic blades have ear-like basal lobes and scalloped or toothed edges. Dense white woolly hairs cover both surfaces.

Flower heads form a branched, corymb-like cluster. Each head is disc-shaped, with 5–15 involucral bracts around it; ray florets are absent. The flowers are yellow, tubular and bisexual. Style branches end in a blunt tip with a spreading crown of hairs. The cylindrical, ribbed dry fruits are hairless and carry a white pappus—a crown of bristles that falls away. The source's habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
