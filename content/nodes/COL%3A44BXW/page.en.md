## Moquiniastrum hatschbachii {#s-240d9ed1c2}
## Hairless branches and a terminal spray of cream heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes hairless branches. Alternate, stalked leaves are the same colour on both faces, papery to leathery, elliptic to narrowly elliptic, and blunt or rounded at the tip. Their edges are entire and their bases taper; the upper surface is glossy and hairless, while the lower one becomes hairless. Malpighian-type hairs are also noted.

Terminal panicles bear nearly stalkless, bell-shaped flower heads; the source notes that stalks may sometimes carry bracts. Each head has 4–6 series of bracts, with outer ones ovate and pointed. Cream tubular florets number 10–14 per head and are recorded as bisexual or female. Five-ribbed cylindrical fruits are silky. Their straw-coloured pappus has unequal, rough to barbed bristles.
