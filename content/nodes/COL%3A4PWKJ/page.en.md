## Relative weights of food categories {#s-85b86d5724}
The nonzero food weights are invertebrates 50% and fish 50%. Source code B indicates reasonable confidence in the source but possible uncertainty in the relative proportions. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove that an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and do not imply a diet limited to nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-366a63a855}
The archive's species-level foraging-stratum weights are 80% on or just below the water surface and 20% below it. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevation ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-a290cf52a5}
This is a guide to historical same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 records, not a complete species account. Matching names and classifications do not establish equivalence between historical concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar by the compilers; this page does not claim direct observation of this species for every field. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite conclusions. The underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation still require other sources.
