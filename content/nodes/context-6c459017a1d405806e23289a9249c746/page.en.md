## Record identity and classification {#s-d4a77bbb93}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf4438 records Nyctibatrachus minimus under Nyctibatrachus, Nyctibatrachidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-8434dc7906}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 14.8 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.

## Developmental category {#s-d4211bb63e}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists larval stage as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
