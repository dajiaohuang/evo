## What is recorded about Siparuna petasiformis? {#s-dc2d6e6d82}
The field describes opposite, obovate leaves with smooth upper surfaces and fine hairs below. The plant is monoecious. Male and female flowers have cup-shaped receptacles; their tepals fuse into a wheel-like rim, and the styles are joined. The fruiting receptacle is smooth, but the source leaves its opening behavior and the drupelet’s form unspecified.
