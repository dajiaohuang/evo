## Centropogon congestus {#s-785f909f35}
## Tall stems carry curved rose-purple flowers

The historical Flora of Panama account describes an upright, nearly unbranched herb with partly woody growth, usually 1–2 m tall; it leaves open whether plants may reach 3 m. The middle stem leaves are usually 15–30 cm long and 6–12 cm wide, elliptic to obovate, with double-toothed margins. Their upper surfaces are hairless, while the undersides bear short hairs, especially along the veins.

The usually dense flower clusters carry 8–40 flowers. Each flower is 3.5–4.8 cm long; its rose-purple to reddish-pink fused petals curve in the upper third and have short hairs outside but no hairs inside. The account describes thin-walled, nearly spherical, slightly angular berries and dark-brown, lens-shaped seeds with a pitted, net-like surface, about 0.5 mm long.
