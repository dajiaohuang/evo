## Tachigali leiocalyx {#s-ea9afb5867}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali leiocalyx* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records no stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) or domatia (small cavities) and up to 10 leaflet pairs. Flowers are no more than 10 mm long, with petals under 1 mm wide and no hairs; each has 10 stamens. The fruit is elliptic to oblong.
