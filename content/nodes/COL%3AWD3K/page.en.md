## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Clusia diamantina? {#s-7cc497b155}
The record describes a taproot with additional roots and stalked, leathery, oblanceolate leaves with conspicuous secretory channels. Pendulous thyrses, or branched flower clusters, bear flowers with six sepals and five petals; the corolla is wine-colored to very dark purple. Male flowers have 13–30 separate stamens, resin-bearing stamens or staminodes, and a pistillode. The ovary has five chambers. Sepals, petals, and staminodes are all recorded as absent from the mature fruit; seeds bear an aril.
