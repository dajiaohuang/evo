## A smooth leaf surface and ridged flower cup {#s-15ea2dc906}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes cylindrical or nearly cylindrical stems without short shoots. The stalked leaves are broadly oval; their lower surface has glandular hairs, while the upper surface is smooth. The bell-shaped flower cup has ridges and is either hairless or glandular-haired. Calyx segments have smooth, untoothed margins and tapering tips. Petals are mostly red to orange with yellow at the base. Ten stamens are nearly equal; their filaments are hairless, and the tissue joining the anther sacs is unextended and hairless. Glandular hairs occur at the ovary's tip, and the ovary has three chambers. The habitat field separately records “terrícola,” or terrestrial. The source lists these traits but does not quantify their variation.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-1ffbe41eb3}
The separate habitat field labels the setting “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It includes no locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not say whether the material is wild, cultivated, managed or fossil. This is a limited category rather than a complete ecology.
