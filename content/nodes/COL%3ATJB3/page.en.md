## A finely divided leaf and yellow flowers {#s-f24a7d4895}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes spiral leaves with 12–38 pairs of leaflets, the small blades attached along each leaf. It notes glandular hairs and terminal flower clusters with bracts. The yellow petals differ in shape, and ten stamens are counted. The fruit and seed fields are recorded as unknown. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
