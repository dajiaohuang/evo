## Bilobed stipules and twisted keel petals {#s-0bfdfcfb51}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records twining growth. Stipules extend below their point of insertion and have bilobed bases; leaflets are lanceolate or ovate. The calyx has five laciniate segments. The corolla is white or purple to lilac and asymmetric, with keel petals twisted laterally. The style does not extend beyond the stigma insertion, and the stigma is terminal or subterminal. The legume is linear and hirsute; seeds are brown. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or range.
