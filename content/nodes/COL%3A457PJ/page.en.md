## Glabrous leaf undersides and deciduous flower bracteoles {#s-682c262d68}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and allows stem hairs to be absent or simple. Leaves are elliptic or obovate, with an upper-surface midrib that may be raised or fully flattened; the underside is glabrous. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below leaf nodes are absent. Compressed inflorescence branchlets carry bracteoles beneath the flowers that fall away. One or two inflorescences are listed per axillary shoot, in subterminal positions with a mixed terminal placement; their pyramidal arrangement may be alternate or opposite. The field gives these alternatives without frequencies.

## A two-chambered ovary and an enlarging fruit calyx {#s-eebc2b9732}
The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is obconic, or inversely cone-shaped, and glabrous. Sepals are free, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx is listed as not opening by tearing. Its lobes enlarge on the globose fruit. These states are listed in one regional dataset field, with no frequencies reported.

## A terrestrial label, without site details {#s-eed60f5ab3}
The same regional dataset classifies the species as terrestrial (terrícola), but gives no locality or more specific habitat description.
