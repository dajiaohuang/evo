## Curved flower spikes beside rivers and floodplains {#s-7aa7ebe39d}
This account describes an upright annual or short-lived perennial herb up to 1.5 m tall, with large toothed leaves and long, curved flower spikes. The flowers are described as white or, less often, mauve or blue in one account; another lists blue or violet, rarely white. The fruit splits into four outward-spreading nutlets. It is recorded from sandy or muddy riverbanks and floodplains, always near water, and as a roadside weed widely distributed in Panama and the American tropics.
