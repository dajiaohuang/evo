## Oenocarpus distichus: crown and inflorescence notes {#s-1a9684083d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records a solitary stem. Leaf sheaths lack long, thick, black fibres; leaves are arranged distichously in the crown and described as plumose. The inflorescence peduncle measures 8–11 cm. The field gives a rachilla value of 50–160 without stating its unit or whether it is a length or count. The fruit entry notes homogeneous endosperm.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The 50–160 rachilla value has no unit or measurement label in the field. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.
