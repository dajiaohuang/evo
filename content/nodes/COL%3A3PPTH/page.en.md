## ## Spike-like clusters and a sinuous fruit {#s-2260a5950f}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with lenticels and surfaces ranging from glabrous to woolly. The persistent stipules are lanceolate or oblong; leaves have two or five pairs of leaflets and stalkless, saucer-shaped nectaries. The inflorescence is spike-like or umbel-shaped. Tubular calyces accompany flowers that may be stalkless or stalked. The fruit is constricted or sinuous, with leathery valves that range from glabrous to finely hairy. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
