## Tongguzhang type-locality knowledge in 2023 {#s-a42b7afc15}
The male holotype SYS a004744 was listed from Tongguzhang Mountain, Fengshun, Guangdong, at about 1500 m. Citing the 2022 description, the monograph knew the species only from that type locality at 800–1500 m. This is the treatment’s dated geographic knowledge, without a survey demonstrating absence elsewhere, continuous occupation of that elevation interval or physiological tolerances.

## Male and female measurements in the type material {#s-e371a59936}
The examined type material comprised six adult males and two females. The diagnosis gave snout–vent lengths of 34.3–39.4 mm for males and 42.5–44.9 mm for females. It described a distinct tympanum, unnotched tongue, vomerine ridges and teeth, rough granular dorsal skin with sparse tubercles, and rudimentary toe webbing without lateral fringes. These retain the measured sex and sample size; selected traits are not a complete identification key or universal size bounds.
