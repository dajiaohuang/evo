## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Heliothraupis oneilli Lane, Aponte, Rheindt, Rosenberg, Schmitt & Terrill, 2021 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
