## *Diplusodon incanus* {#s-5fe4c9b9bc}
The field records hairy leaves that do not turn black when dry, petioles 0–2.5 mm long, blades over 5 mm wide, and pinnate venation with at least two lateral veins on each side; domatia are present on the lateral veins. Flowers have 12 stamens and a corolla 1–3 cm across. The epicalyx segments (small bracts below the calyx) are deflexed and longer than the sepals; the field gives two segment-length bands, 0–1.5 mm and 2–11 mm. These are the recorded descriptors.
