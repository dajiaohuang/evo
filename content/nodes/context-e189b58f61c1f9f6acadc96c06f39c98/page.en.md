## Source classification and concept status {#s-12a650d392}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2483 records Osteocephalus castaneicola under the source classification Osteocephalus (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.
