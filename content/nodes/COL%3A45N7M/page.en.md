## Accepted name and placement {#s-9bdbe3d73a}
COL26.8 accepts Nanorana rarica (Dubois, Matsui & Ohler, 2001) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Nanorana (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A cited dorsolateral fold distinguishes this comparison {#s-052843ec9d}
Nanorana rarica: The 2023 account includes this species among Nanorana reported to have a dorsolateral fold, contrasting with the absent fold in laojunshanensis. That is a cited taxonomic comparison based on earlier descriptions, not a newly measured series for this species. It does not state a population-wide frequency, fold dimensions or a functional role. A single shared trait cannot identify all species; the new-species diagnosis uses a character combination rather than treating fold presence as unique.

## Published placement within Paa {#s-66a853d115}
The 2023 combined-locus analysis placed Nanorana rarica with known members of Paa; the authors therefore suggest allocating it to that subgenus. This is an explicit sampled phylogenetic proposal, not a change to the fixed species ID or independent certification that a rank is biologically required.
