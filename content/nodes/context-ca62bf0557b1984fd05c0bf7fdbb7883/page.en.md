## Source classification and concept status {#s-27f8cfad2e}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3092 records *Chrysobatrachus cupreonitens* under the source classification Chrysobatrachus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-bdf2a46f45}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and aquatic as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-c176707255}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 34 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
