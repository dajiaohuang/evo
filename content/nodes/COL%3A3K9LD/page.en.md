## What flower arrangement and fruit forms are recorded? {#s-c833db931d}
The stipules persist. Each one-flowered scorpioid cyme (a curved, coiled flower cluster) lacks an extrafloral nectary; its prophylls, the first small bracts below the flower cluster, are awl-shaped or linear. Flowers are straight rather than bent at a joint. Their petals are nearly equal, with claws four to eight times shorter than the calyx. There are fewer than ten free stamens. The densely hairy capsule may be straight or spiraled.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-69001f91c5}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
