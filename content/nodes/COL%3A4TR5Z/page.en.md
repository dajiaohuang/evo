## Ruschia rigida {#s-ab5d3fdf2a}
## White flowers above rough-keeled leaves

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a rounded shrublet up to 40 cm tall that may lie down or grow upright. The three-angled leaves taper to a sharp point; each has a 5–10 mm sheath, and the free portion is usually slightly shorter. The keels are rough.

The white flowers reach 10 mm across. The account records the fruits as unknown and leaves the habitat uncertain.
