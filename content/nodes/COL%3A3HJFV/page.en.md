## Guarea purusana {#s-0048afa354}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record combines opposite leaflets and axillary flowers with variable leaflet tips and a smooth or hairy fruit.

## Opposite leaflets and a fruit with alternative surface states {#s-48d77567f6}
The leaf rachis is recorded as grooved or square in cross-section, with a terminal bud. Opposite leaflets are oblong or oblanceolate; their tips may be acuminate, acute, rounded, cuspidate or obtuse. Venation is eucamptodromous and the lower surface hairy. Axillary inflorescences carry flowers whose outer calyx may be hairy or strigose; the corolla is valvate in bud and its outer surface strigose or silky. The staminal tube is externally glabrous, and the ovary pubescent or strigose, with two ovules per locule. Fruits are botuliform or obovoid, with rounded or slightly notched tips; their surface is smooth and the indument may be nearly glabrous or hairy. The separate ecology field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
