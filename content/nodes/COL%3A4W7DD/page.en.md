## Dense flower clusters on a small erect herb {#s-4eab3c6324}
The SANBI account describes an erect, much-branched herb 4–15 cm high. The larger leaves are 6–14 mm long and 3–4 mm wide, ovate-lanceolate or narrowly elliptic with acute tips; lower leaves are reduced and often scale-like. The many-flowered inflorescence is dense or sub-dense, with well-developed bracts. Flowers are yellow. The source gives the corolla tube as 7–11 mm long and its lobes as 4.5–8 mm; the style and stigma together measure 5.5–7.5 mm, with a swelling below the midpoint and a two-lobed stigma. For both leaves and corolla lobes, the account lists more than one shape.

## Grassland habitat descriptions {#s-3c6e4bac1f}
The SANBI habitat row lists Dry Grassland and Dwarf Karroid Shrubland, and also gives “Grassland, disturbed places.” These are habitat labels in the regional record; it does not identify a collection site or the frequency of each setting.
