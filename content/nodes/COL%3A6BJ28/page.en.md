## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton imbricatus? {#s-2430b33a2c}
Young branches and both surfaces of the short-stalked oval, elliptic, or obovate leaves have scale-like hairs. The leaves have looping side veins and entire margins, with no nectar glands; leafy stipules are absent at young branch tips. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are shortly stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are nearly sessile, with separate sepals, a two-lobed style, and equal calyx lobes. Female petals are absent. The bud parts overlap in an imbricate pattern, and the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
