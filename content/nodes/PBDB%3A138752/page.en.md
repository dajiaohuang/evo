## Eopteranodon: PBDB classification route {#s-00cb98e56a}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) lists Eopteranodon as a genus and links E. lii beneath it. That hierarchy organizes the occurrence record; it does not establish the genus phylogenetic position.

## Member material linked to Eopteranodon {#s-c12dc4cb38}
