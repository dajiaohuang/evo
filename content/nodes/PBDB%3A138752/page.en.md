## Connect Yixian material with Jiufotang comparisons {#s-bfbbe7a9a7}
The genus account compares the fixed E. lii record with the 2023 reassessment of BPV 078 and the Sinopterus complex. Earlier Yixian material informs the anatomy and relationship discussion, but it does not establish a genus-wide ecology or complete growth sequence.

## Identify skull remains before comparisons and hypotheses {#s-678f392e6b}
The [Eopteranodon lii](Eopteranodon_lii/page.en.md) account discusses jaw identification and proportions; the [S. dongi](../Neoazhdarchia/Tapejaroidea/Tapejaridae/Sinopterus/Sinopterus_dongi/page.en.md) account treats growth and taxonomy separately. Their comparison does not demonstrate direct ancestry, whether the names mark successive portions of one evolutionary lineage, or a shared diet. Occurrence records are separate from the anatomical and taxonomic arguments.

## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-00cb98e56a}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) lists Eopteranodon as a genus and links E. lii beneath it. That hierarchy organizes the occurrence record; it does not establish the genus phylogenetic position.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-c12dc4cb38}
The selected E. lii entry contains one occurrence from one collection in the Yixian Formation. This is a locality record, not a count of one fossil individual or a population estimate.

## Evidence limits {#s-2b2eb144ec}
The cited anatomy and 2023 reassessment compare Eopteranodon and Sinopterus material. They do not demonstrate direct ancestry, that the names represent successive stages of one evolving lineage, a shared diet or the complete growth history and ecology of Eopteranodon.
