## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-bf8930f836}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Psilopogon javensis (Horsfield, 1821) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Measured samples and beak/wing averages {#s-300493ab6c}
The AVONET sample has five birds (two female, three male, none unsexed); four have complete measurement sets. Recorded means are 28.7 mm from nostril to bill tip, 13.9 mm beak width, 29.7 mm tarsus and 109.4 mm unflattened wing. The hand-wing index is 18.6. Inference=NO does not mean every field has the same sample count. The index is Kipp's distance divided by wing length, ×100—not flight speed—and beak width is not a test of feeding performance.

## Compiled mass has a separate denominator {#s-c8203afeb1}
AVONET lists mass as 115 g and Mass.Source=Dunning. This is a compiled species average with a separate source basis; the five morphology birds are not its denominator. Individual weights and Dunning's book were not examined, so sex-, season- or locality-specific minima and maxima cannot be supplied.

## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields {#s-f9b42c757d}
Metadata defines Frugivore as a niche centered on fruit resources; this row separately records trophic level Omnivore. The two labels describe different fields and are not contradictory. They are categories, not measured diet percentages for a place or season. The record gives no fruit species, food sizes, digestion data or seed-dispersal experiments, so those details cannot be inferred.

## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category {#s-94c9707932}
The AVONET lifestyle category Insessorial refers to perching above ground on branches or other substrates; it does not mean the bird never comes to the ground. Habitat is Forest, and density code 1 denotes dense vegetation rather than birds per area. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, not proof of lifelong immobility. No historical map field is interpreted here, so the categories do not establish present occupancy, nest sites or daily activity height.

## Evidence limits {#s-19ebfb4bd8}
This account draws on selected AVONET v7 rows, two name crosswalks, workbook Metadata definitions and local COL name records. Morphometric means and compiled mass have separate source bases; one-to-one name links and catalogue synonyms do not prove concept equivalence across dates. Individual specimens, the full workbook, Dunning's book and a fresh full reading of the methods paper were not examined. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation status are outside the evidence used here.
