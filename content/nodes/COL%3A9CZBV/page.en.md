## A single trunk and curved pods {#s-3c4dc61ace}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a tree with one upright trunk. Its leathery leaves have two or three pairs of oval leaflets, with small dots on their surfaces. Loose branching flower clusters are about as long as, or up to roughly twice as long as, adjacent leaves. The slightly sickle-shaped pod is 2–3.5 cm long; the seed covering, called an aril, is yellow. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
