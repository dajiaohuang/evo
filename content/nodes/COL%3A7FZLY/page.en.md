## How does its woolly foliage frame the flower spike? {#s-80e1e53627}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with dark, broadened sheaths and flat, ridged blades covered in hairs. The flower stalk is 20–50 cm long, with irregular ridges; hairs occur along the ridges. Its ellipsoid spike has up to ten flowers. The entry lists alternatives for bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) and other features.
