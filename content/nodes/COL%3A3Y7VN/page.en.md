## Marlothistella uniondalensis {#s-9c53e526fd}
## Thick branching roots and sharply pointed leaves

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora compares this species with M. stenophylla. Its thick taproots branch toward their ends, and the leaves are cylindrical at the base before narrowing to a sharp point above. They reach 45 mm long, about 5 mm wide and about 3 mm in diameter.

The habitat account records grasslands or karroid vegetation.
