## Growth and leaves {#s-1f2b5592d1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists plants as upright or twining. Leaves have three leaflets, which may be unlobed or lobed.

## Flowers and fruit {#s-ca4c6719e5}
The inflorescence entry records fascicles of first-order bracts as absent. Flowers have red corollas. A raised callus at the base of the banner petal’s blade extends down to its claw; prominent papillae are absent around the callus. The keel ends in a hooked beak. The legume is recorded as longer than 6 cm.

## Habitat category {#s-b0bc49bad9}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
