## Alternanthera micrantha {#s-424d9e0054}
## Decumbent stems and stalked flower clusters

Brazilian Flora 2020 records secondary roots and decumbent stems. The leaves have petioles and elliptic blades; the source lists acute or blunt tips, with the apex recorded as entire, mucronate or minutely mucronulate. Leaf hairs are present.

The flower cluster is stalked, with second- or third-order side branches. Individual flowers lack stalks. Each has five three-veined tepals and a white, chaff-like perianth; the androecium has five stamens, short pseudostaminodes, a hairy stigma and a short style. The fruit wall is smooth or has texture at the apex; the seeds lack lateral projections. The dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial.
