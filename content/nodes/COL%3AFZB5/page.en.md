## Three substrate labels in a regional source {#s-e4a501373c}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Aptychopsis pungifolia (Hampe) Broth. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its entries are habitat or substrate categories.

One entry is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A second is “Epífita,” or epiphytic, recorded as growing on another plant as a support.

A third, separate label is “Saxícola,” meaning rock-dwelling. These are categories in the source, not collection locations.

The first field gives no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

The second field likewise gives no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

The third field also gives no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

These three source-labelled categories are the only biological content in this regional record; they do not make a full account of the species.
