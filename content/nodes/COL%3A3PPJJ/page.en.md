## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-0372aeef57}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records angular branches with hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) are lance-shaped or oblong. Both the leaf stalk (petiole) and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; the dataset lists three or five leaflet pairs. Stalked nectar glands (nectaries) are cup-shaped. The inflorescence is spike-like, the outer floral cup (calyx) is tubular, and flowers are described as stalkless or nearly stalkless. The pod walls (valves) are leathery. The fruit shape is listed as flat or curved, and its surface is bristly. This is one regional dataset field; it does not establish variation or the frequency of these traits.

## Habitat category {#s-0dc1d6963c}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
