## A molecular-clock estimate for the land-plant crown {#s-cab2c67d23}
Using 37 fossil calibrations, a Bayesian relaxed clock places crown Embryophyta—the common ancestor of living land plants and its descendants—in a middle Cambrian–Early Ordovician interval, earlier than the estimated crown of Tracheophyta (vascular plants) in the Late Ordovician–Silurian. These divergence times depend on clock and calibration choices; they are model estimates, not direct fossil first appearances or dates for ecosystem expansion.
