## Classification {#s-0670d09f65}
This page follows the COL26.8 accepted species name Presbytis mitrata. A 2018 riparian study in the Merangin Geopark used the older combination Presbytis melalophos ssp. mitrata. The two usages remain tied to their sources; that study offers local evidence associated with this taxon but does not establish modern species boundaries by itself.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-4cef3c981f}
The study surveyed three riparian transects in the Merangin Geopark area of West Sumatra for about three months. Repeated daily strip-transect counts recorded 29 animals, and 31 tree species were documented in the site; the authors reported that transects sampled about 0.48% of the study area. These results describe a limited riparian sample, not total population size or range.

## Evidence limits {#s-232fff102c}
Biological evidence is limited to a riparian site in the Merangin Geopark. The paper used the older combination Presbytis melalophos ssp. mitrata and did not test taxonomic boundaries. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation.
