## Creamy flowers on a small corm-bearing plant {#s-2f7824d3c3}
Freesia occidentalis is described as a 9–50 cm corm-bearing geophyte (a plant whose renewal organ is a swollen underground storage stem), with blunt, sword-shaped leaves and faintly scented creamy-white and yellow flowers.

## Flowering season and western range {#s-63b90d67c0}
The account reports flowering from July to September and records stony soils. Its regional range is given from the Cedarberg to Touws River and the western Karoo; this is not a complete global range survey.
