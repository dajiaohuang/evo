## Social behavior and organization {#s-e823c234ed}
At Niokolo Koba National Park in southeastern Senegal, Patzelt et al. studied a troop and used a fixed observation point to record party composition as animals crossed an open area; this paper reports three months of observations spanning dry and wet seasons. Party size and membership varied daily and seasonally, and 45.9% of arriving parties changed composition through fission or fusion while crossing. The authors found that this sample fit neither a typical hamadryas-like multilevel structure nor a stable medium-sized savannah-style multimale group. These crossing observations at one site do not by themselves settle the species’ full social organization.
