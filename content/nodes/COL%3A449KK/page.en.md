## *Monotagma smaragdinum*: green expanded bracts and a dark midrib {#s-f771ecb06b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes symmetric leaves with a black midrib and hairs on the underside. There is no ring at the leaf joint, and the profile records neither root tubers nor overlapping leaf sheaths. A terminal flower cluster sits on a stalk longer than 2.5 cm and contains two to nine nodes.

The green bracts expand at flowering, and the outer staminode—a sterile stamen-like part—is spatula-shaped. The linked habitat field says only “terrestrial”; it gives no collection locality or date.
