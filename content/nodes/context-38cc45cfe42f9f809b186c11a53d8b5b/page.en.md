## The source concept {#s-2efbe4521a}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Brachycephalus pulex in row Anf0280, with genus Brachycephalus, family Brachycephalidae and order Anura. The source says its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a link to a current COL26.8 concept has not been established. The traits below describe this archived row, not a new species assessment.

## Foraging strata {#s-60d50f6e5f}
The row marks Ter=1 in its foraging-stratum field. This is a recorded category, not a measure of how often or how long an individual used that stratum; the row does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. Later TetrapodTraits revisions reported corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries, but this row has not been compared field by field. Treat this archived flag as awaiting row-level review.

## Adult food categories {#s-0acbf6c5c1}
The row marks Arthro=1, recording arthropods as an adult food category in AmphiBIO. A flagged category is not a complete diet or a measured proportion of food intake.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-b80ad33510}
AmphiBIO reports Body_size_mm=8.4 mm as a maximum adult body size. For Anura, this field denotes snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a sample distribution or a claim that every adult reaches this size.
