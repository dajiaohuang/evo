## Gene expression in two cultivated sample types {#s-6e41cfa6ee}
A transcriptome comparison—an analysis of RNA-derived gene activity—contrasted artificially cultivated mycelium with mature fruiting bodies. It identified 2,712 differentially expressed genes under the study’s thresholds: 2,113 were reported as higher in the mycelium sample and 599 in the fruiting-body sample. The mycelium was grown on PDA for 18 days at 22°C in darkness; fruiting bodies were grown on rice medium through dark and 16:8-hour light:dark phases.

## Evidence limits {#s-9a265f4e62}
These counts compare artificial-culture samples under different media and growth conditions. The record does not identify the strain’s wild provenance or give cultivation site and dates; this is not a species-wide developmental rule.
