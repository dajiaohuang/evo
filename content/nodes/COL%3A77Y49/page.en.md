## Behavioral records {#s-8ed576fa14}
Mills and colleagues followed one wild group in Marojejy National Park for ten months and recorded 828 sleeping trees from about 35 genera. Nearly 95% were food trees, and Weinmannia accounted for 29% of sleeping trees despite low abundance in the study area. The group slept at an average height of 16 m and did not reuse a tree on consecutive nights. The authors discuss food access, temperature and predator avoidance as possible influences on site choice.
