## Bryopteris filicina: three habitat labels {#s-5b920a491d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists three habitat categories for this taxon:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated),

“Epixila” (wood-associated),

and “Rupícola” (rock-associated).

These are source labels from three regional dataset rows; none gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
