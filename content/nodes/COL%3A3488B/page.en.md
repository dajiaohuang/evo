## Dasycondylus resinosus {#s-6ade9ef52e}
## Hirsute stems and a persistent bristly pappus

The Brazilian Flora record describes cylindrical, striated stems with stiff hairs. Opposite, petiolate ovate leaves have cuneate bases, acute tips and serrated margins. Heads are shortly pedicellate in flat-topped panicles; the receptacle is conical and pitted. White funnel-shaped corollas sit above a five-ribbed prismatic dry fruit (cypsela) with a distinct basal attachment area (carpopodium). The persistent pappus, a crown of bristles on the fruit, has 30–40 rough bristles in one row. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
