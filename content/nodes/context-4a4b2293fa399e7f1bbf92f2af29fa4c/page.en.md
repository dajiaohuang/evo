## Source classification and concept status {#s-7c7b20ef73}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2791 records Litoria christianbergmanni under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging category (arboreal) {#s-fdc97947de}
The compilation records arboreal as a foraging category for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Activity periods (night and twilight) {#s-2ba62c95d1}
The row lists night and twilight as activity-period categories. These are broad summaries, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-9350332730}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 31.2 mm for this row. The source field Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
