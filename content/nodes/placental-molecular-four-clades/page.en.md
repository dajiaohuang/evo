## Four major branches in one placental-mammal analysis {#s-16c8b4ad04}
One study analysed 16,397 DNA base pairs from 42 placental mammals and two marsupial outgroups, using 19 nuclear and three mitochondrial genes. Bayesian and maximum-likelihood analyses recovered four major placental clades: Afrotheria, Xenarthra, Laurasiatheria and Euarchontoglires, with Afrotheria at the root in that study. The root placement, its estimated age of about 103 million years and the proposed Gondwanan scenario depend on the sample, calibrations and models; later analyses test alternative relationships.
