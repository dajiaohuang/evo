## Distribution records {#s-abd43bbaa8}
The 2006 description drew on observations, photographs, video and specimens from Madidi in northwestern Bolivia. Preliminary surveys placed the known range west of the Río Beni, in Andean foothills and adjacent lowland forest. Line transects covered four sites, so they are not a complete range survey or a current population census.

## Classification {#s-4738691eda}
COL26.8 lists Plecturocebus aureipalatii as an accepted species, while the current MDD lists it as a synonym of P. toppini and includes Madidi Titi among P. toppini's common names. A 2015 paper also said their boundary required further study. This page follows the pinned COL26.8 identity and records the disagreement; its English label reflects the historical concept, not agreement between the lists.

## Evidence limits {#s-46898ed2f7}
The original range work was limited; the 2015 paper preserved a taxonomic question with P. toppini, and this page does not judge whether later work resolved it. The Chinese name is editorial.
