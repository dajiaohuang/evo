## What does the record say about Unonopsis guatterioides? {#s-73b9a6e110}
The field describes papery, narrow elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves with tapering tips and variable bases, from pointed to heart-shaped. Flowers grow on branches or at their ends. The fruit ranges from globose to ellipsoid.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-5bc9b5a8bd}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
