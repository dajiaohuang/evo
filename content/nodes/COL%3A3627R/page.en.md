## Long lateral stalks and wine-red flowers {#s-325513336c}
Dierama mobile grows as a solitary plant or small tuft and can reach 1.5 m. Its lateral flower spikes are carried on conspicuously long peduncles, usually 90–230 mm; the flowers are magenta-pink to deep wine-red, 16–20 mm long, and the bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) have 4 or 5 veins on each side of the main vein. The account says it may hybridise with D. pictum in Eswatini.

## Rocky grassland {#s-f19f7e6806}
The regional flora records rocky grassland as habitat; it does not establish how frequently the species occurs there.
