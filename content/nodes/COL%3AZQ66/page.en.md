## What leaf and flower traits mark Croton gracilipes? {#s-88bca5a181}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. Long-stalked oval or heart-shaped leaves also have star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and toothed margins. A sessile nectar gland occurs at the leaf base; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are nearly sessile, with separate sepals, a two-lobed style, and equal calyx lobes. Female petals are reduced to glands, the bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
