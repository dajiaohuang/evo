## Lessingianthus subcarduoides {#s-ed970232a4}
## Leaf edges may roll under; fruit shape is unspecified

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record does not specify the root type. Stems carry leaves along their length and are unbranched or sparsely branched. Oblong to lance-shaped leaves are leathery and the same colour on both faces, with acute tips and bases. The upper face is hairless; the lower may be hairless or pilose. Edges are entire and may roll under or remain flat.

Solitary heads have stalks and bell-shaped involucres with 5–6 rows of bracts. The source records 20 florets per head. Fruit shape is unspecified; its covering is silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
