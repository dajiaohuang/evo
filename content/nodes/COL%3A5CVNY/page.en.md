## Zamia amazonum {#s-53da261169}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records a subterranean stem and oval-to-elliptic leaves with prickles on the petioles. Leaf texture is listed as papery or chartaceous, and the habitat field records soil. These are regional source fields, not a global description or account of population variation; wild or cultivated status is not specified.
