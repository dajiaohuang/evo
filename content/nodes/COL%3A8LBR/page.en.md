## What does the record say about its two leaf forms? {#s-dced60004c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records dimorphic, linear leaves with flattened blades in *Abolboda americana*. It describes a simple flower stalk and spikes with two to four flowers. For some traits, including spike shape and leaf tips, the field lists alternatives rather than selecting one state; it does not explain how the two leaf forms differ.

## How does the flora classify its habitat? {#s-bccf208e2d}
The same regional data row classifies it as aquatic. It supplies no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
