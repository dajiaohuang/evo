## Survey records {#s-9a3a775169}
Between May and July 2008, researchers used line transects at three localities in Panama’s Chagres National Park. They observed 33 individuals in 16 groups at two sites and none at the third. Estimated density was 19.7 individuals/km² at Campo Chagres and 14.3 individuals/km² at Cerro Azul. The authors treated this as a pilot study and called for longer-term work before using the estimates for conservation decisions.

## Evidence limits {#s-b2f4db0fa6}
The survey lasted three months and covered three sites in one park; its local abundance estimates are not a current range-wide population estimate.
