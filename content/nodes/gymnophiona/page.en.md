## A caecilian fossil assignment remains provisional {#s-4dedf4032f}
The holotype of *Ymboirana acrux*, about 25 Ma old, was assigned to the living family Typhlonectidae through comparative anatomy. That assignment provisionally supports a crown-Gymnophiona fossil record, but the cited work reports neither an unambiguous family synapomorphy nor a formal phylogenetic test. The 25–0 Ma display therefore records a bounded group-level context, not a demonstrated global first appearance.
