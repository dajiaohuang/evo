## The verecundus clade and its limits {#s-607e84c3b8}
The paper treats celator as one of three named members of the verecundus clade, with mutabilis and verecundus, alongside nine unnamed, unconfirmed candidate lineages. Its named celator sample is placed in Carchi, Ecuador. The 2.6–16.3% inter-lineage distance range refers to the whole clade, not celator alone. The narrower myersi definition excludes this branch, but its unnamed candidates are not transferred to the accepted celator page.
