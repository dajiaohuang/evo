## Leaves, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-d79bf51178}
The morphology field records an underground bulb and pseudopetiolate leaves with elliptic or ovate blades, tapering bases, acute tips, and no ornamentation. The scape is described as solid and entire; its spathe-like bract is free.

The flowers are unscented. Their unspotted perianth is bell-shaped and lilac with a white base. The hypanthium tube is in the source’s “short” category, at 0.1–1.0 cm long. The record lists six stamens, with filaments shorter than the sepals and petals, grey anthers, and basal placentation. Fruit shape is recorded as globose or obovoid.
