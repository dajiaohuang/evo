## Asparagus exuvialis: stems and flowers {#s-9dc63c71ad}
A South African regional account describes an erect or scrambling shrub, 0.5–2 m tall. Its stems have white, papery bark and may bear minute spines. The threadlike cladodes grow in bundles. Two to six flowers occur in the leaf axils, with tepals and filaments spreading outward.
