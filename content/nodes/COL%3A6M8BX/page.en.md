## What details are recorded for Hirtella barrosoi? {#s-7e5fae1544}
Young branches are hairy at first, then become nearly smooth. The leaves are lance-shaped, and the record notes no ant-associated cavities in them. Flowers grow in panicles; the bracts and smaller bracteoles lack glands. Each flower has three stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-4eaa747719}
The dataset classifies it as terrestrial and gives no specific locality.
