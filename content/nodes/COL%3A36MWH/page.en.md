## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Diplopterys caduciflora? {#s-bf03644031}
Leaves persist during flowering. Their elliptic blades have blunt or rounded bases, are subleathery to leathery, and are hairless below; two to four gland pairs lie between the midrib and edge. Petioles range from under 3.5 mm to 4–24 mm, and there are six to nine pairs of side veins. Branched thyrses carry triangular bracts held upright or close to the flower stalk. The sepals overlap in the bud and bear oil glands; the petals are hairless or sparsely silky below, with ragged edges. The stamens are joined halfway up, and the front style is nearly as long as the others. The side wings of the fruit segments are unknown.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-dababbbde5}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
