## Overview {#s-6d0dea8261}
COL26.8 accepts *Leontocebus illigeri*. Earlier field work used *Saguinus fuscicollis illigeri*; Matauschek et al. (2011) included illigeri among nigricollis-group taxa supported at species level. Later classifications still differ in genus-level treatment.

## Research observations {#s-3f97c39445}
Soini (1987) followed one troop intermittently for five years at Río Pacaya in northeastern Peru, with quantitative data mainly from 1979 to 1982. Modal troop size was six (observed range 2–10), and the troop used a 15.7–16.5 ha home range; activity records emphasized insect foraging and lower forest strata. This is single-troop natural history, not a modern multi-site species average.

## Evidence limits {#s-c1d9c9614e}
The main ecological source is an older study of one troop under a former genus combination; troop size, home range and feeding proportions are not generalized to the whole species.
