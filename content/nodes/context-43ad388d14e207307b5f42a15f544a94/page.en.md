## The source concept {#s-412c64a2bb}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Brachycephalus pitanga* as row Anf0278, in the genus Brachycephalus, family Brachycephalidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-f7acb05a1a}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) as a broad foraging category. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Adult food categories {#s-1639f7be57}
The row records arthropods (Arthro = 1) as an adult food category. AmphiBIO compiles these from literature reports based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; listed categories are not a complete diet or measured food proportions.

## Activity periods {#s-3fa4a8dd44}
The row records daytime (Diu = 1) as an activity period. These are broad categories, not a continuous record of behaviour through the day.

## Seasonal activity {#s-bb434e1a69}
The row records wet and warm (Wet_warm = 1) as a seasonal activity category. AmphiBIO relates activity conditions to the local annual climate, so these labels do not correspond to fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-79135b1ed3}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 14 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
