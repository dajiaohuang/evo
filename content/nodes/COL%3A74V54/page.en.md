## ## Red-and-black seeds and woody valves {#s-10217d7444}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records opposite to subopposite leaflets, with counts listed as five to 13 or more than 13. Their 9–20 pairs of secondary veins run straight and parallel, while tertiary veins form an inconspicuous network; the leaflet surfaces are sparsely pubescent or silky. The papilionaceous flowers exceed 13 mm and have pink or lilac petals and a lateral, bilobed stigma. The dehiscent, flattened fruit has woody valves 2–5 mm thick and is generally 2–4 cm wide. Seeds are listed as red and black, up to three per fruit, compressed or ellipsoid, 10–15 mm long and up to 15 mm wide; the hilum is 2–5 mm long. The habitat field says “terrestrial”; this regional entry does not establish the species’ full variation or range.
