## The source concept {#s-86481cd3bc}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis sylvaticus as row Anf0150 under Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); no current-concept match has been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-fdbb9fd2ca}
The row flags terrestrial foraging. Other strata are missing, not shown to be absent. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the deposited category is retained without treating it as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2fb222323f}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 21.5 mm. For anurans, this field means snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a range for the species.

## Compiled life history {#s-0ef203727a}
The row reports a maximum of one reproductive event per year. It does not supply clutch-size, maturity, or offspring-size values.

## Developmental strategy {#s-4a3f2da986}
The entry flags direct development; larval-stage and viviparity categories are not flagged. No breeding-site or parental-care detail is given.
