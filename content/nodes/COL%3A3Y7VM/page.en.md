## Purple flowers in a tuft of narrow leaves {#s-b0061acb93}
This tufted perennial has a subterranean tuber-like base. Its smooth, sharp, three-sided leaves reach 50 mm long and are 2–3 mm wide and thick. Solitary purplish flowers are 20–25 mm across, and the fruit has five chambers with a large closing body. Grassland is the listed habitat.
