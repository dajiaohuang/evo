## Mimosa uliginosa {#s-b0cb095d92}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa uliginosa* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry lists the stem as hairy or bristly, the stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) as persistent, and the flower clusters as ellipsoid. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and bristly petal lobes; the entry records a terrestrial setting.
