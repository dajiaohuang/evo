## ## Opposite pinnae and cream-colored flowers {#s-72cae2e8c3}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaves with six or seven pairs of opposite pinnae—the side axes that carry the leaflets. The oblong to lanceolate leaflets have rounded or wedge-shaped bases and pointed tips; the lower surface is rusty-hairy, while the upper surface is glabrous. The flowers are cream-colored; the spindle-shaped ovary is bristly. Mature fruits are leathery, elongated, straight or slightly curved, and dark brown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
