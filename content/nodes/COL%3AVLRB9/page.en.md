## ## Many leaflets and white to cream flowers {#s-15d7d099e1}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 13–25 pairs of obovate to oblong leaflets, with an upper value of 29; the tips are notched and the bases rounded. Panicles bear cream to white flowers with ten stamens whose filaments are united into one group. Anther opening is listed as unknown. The oblong to oblong-elliptic fruit has a surface-vein pattern recorded as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
