## Accepted name and placement {#s-b5041b0b8b}
COL26.8 accepts Oreobates zongoensis (Reichle & Köhler, 1997) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Oreobates (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
