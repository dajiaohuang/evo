## Overview {#s-25e289c7f6}
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID 5WRMR. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Southern Muriqui. The field study summarized here followed one group in a semideciduous forest fragment in southeastern Brazil, so its measurements describe that group and site.

## Feeding research {#s-aea192acea}
Behavioral feeding records from the study group covered 47 plant species: leaves accounted for 55.3% of records, flowers 16.1%, fruit 12.1% and seeds 16.5%. Leaf feeding was significantly higher in the dry season; the study found no evident seasonal difference for flowers, fruit or seeds. Only 11 plant taxa overlapped with groups studied in evergreen forests.
