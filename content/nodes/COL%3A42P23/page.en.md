## Web-like leaf hairs and a tubular flower cup {#s-4a7e1f184b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records no hairs on young stems, but dense web-like hairs on the lower leaf surface. Leaves are obovate (egg-shaped, widest toward the tip) or oblong, with gradually tapering tips, wedge-shaped bases and entire margins without fringe-like hairs. Five main veins begin above the base. Flowers form dense clusters. The flower cup is tubular; its calyx falls away and has distinct lobes. Each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the anther's pollen sacs is prolonged and bears two lower-side lobes. Lilac anthers have one small pore, while the ovary is hairless. These are the traits in the source field; it does not specify how their variation is distributed.
