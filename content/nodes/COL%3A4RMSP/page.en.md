## Ravenia infelix {#s-a149e95326}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes unequal leaf sizes, a 15–22 mm white corolla and sepals glabrous on both surfaces.

## Unequal leaves and a white corolla {#s-25f0567665}
The morphology field records anisophylly, meaning leaves differ in size. It lists no basal anther appendages, a white corolla 15–22 mm long, and sepals glabrous on both surfaces. The separate ecology category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or full range.
