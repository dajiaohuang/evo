## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Callaeum psilophyllum? {#s-f99aa74536}
The stem nodes lack raised nodules. Glands occur along the leaf margins, and the bracteoles are hairy. The rear petal has a glandular spot. The fruit segment has a fully developed wing.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-e707ef423d}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
