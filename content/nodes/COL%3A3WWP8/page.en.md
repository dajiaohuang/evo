## Did two macaque groups differ in dietary variety? {#s-bf1863e3fb}
From April 2007 to March 2008, researchers observed two free-ranging groups in Gouraya National Park, eastern Algeria. The peri-urban Les Oliviers group was reported as 31–34 individuals and the non-urban Cap Carbon group as 40–46; unweaned infants were excluded from the diet analysis. The study reported higher annual dietary plant-species diversity for Les Oliviers (13.8) than for Cap Carbon (10.4; F=5.51, df=1, P<0.05). This result describes two groups at one site over one annual cycle. It does not show that urbanization caused the difference or describe the species' diet as a whole.
