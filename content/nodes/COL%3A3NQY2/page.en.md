## Workshop vocabulary and later species naming {#s-7c29b2e8f0}
The underlying workshop used an opportunity sample of 13 volunteer native isiZulu speakers, aged18–55, from five Zululand areas: ten participants on28November and three on1December2016 at Tembe Elephant Park. Participants viewed frog photographs and discussed names collectively, resolving disagreements by discussion. The six broad names elicited during the workshop did not individually distinguish all58 species. Species-level names were assigned afterwards with Bongani Mkhize, combining this vocabulary with earlier published names and naming guidelines. These group discussions do not supply independent per-person frequencies, dialect-wide agreement, or a test of recognition accuracy for Hyperolius pusillus.
