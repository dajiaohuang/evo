## *Diplusodon xiphodon* {#s-e04a265d68}
The field records hairy leaves that darken when dry, petioles 0–2.5 mm long, and blades over 5 mm wide. Venation is pinnate or acrodromous; the base is blunt or notched, the margin rolled under, and domatia occur on lateral veins. Flowers have 12 stamens and a 1–3 cm corolla. Epicalyx segments are 2–11 mm long, shorter than the sepals, and spreading; bracteoles are no longer than half the floral tube. These are source-field alternatives and ranges.
