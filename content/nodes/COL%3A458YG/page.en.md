## *Myriophyllum aquaticum* {#s-374924ddfc}
Compared with *Myriophyllum spicatum*, the account describes emergent leaves densely covered with papillae and bearing deciduous, awl-shaped, stipule-like outgrowths. Flowers are solitary in the leaf axils. These are the stated comparative characters.

The source calls it a weed in rivers and ponds. The statement does not quantify abundance or geographic extent.
