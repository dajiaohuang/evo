## Ormosia holerythra {#s-a55c694457}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ormosia holerythra* as an accepted species of *Ormosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes 5–13 leaflets, usually with up to eight pairs of arching secondary veins. The leaflets are hairless; flowers are vinaceous and up to 13 mm long. Flattened pods are usually 2–4 cm wide, with leathery valves, and the red or orange seeds are up to 15 mm wide.
