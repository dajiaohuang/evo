## Recorded parent of Anguiformes {#s-4dcc9216b7}
Anguiformes is listed at rank unranked clade in the PBDB classification route, with Anguimorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
