## Crocosmia fucata {#s-118eb35b78}
Crocosmia fucata is a tall cormous plant, 1.5–2 m high, with sword-shaped leaves and a widely branched flowering stem. Its spreading flower spike carries scarlet, trumpet-shaped flowers; the regional description notes a narrow lower tube that widens above and a prominent horizontal upper tepal. It is recorded along streams in light bush, with flowering reported mainly in November. The account distinguishes it from similarly long-tubed Crocosmia by its flatter rather than strongly pleated leaves.
