## What changed during the Great Ordovician Biodiversification? {#s-ad77558d98}
Across the Ordovician, reviews document marine diversification in several dimensions: genus and family richness, reef and tiering structures, and broad geographic recurrence. Regional pulses were asynchronous, and measured richness depends partly on which rocks and collections are available. The evidence supports multi-clade taxonomic, ecological and biogeographic restructuring, not a single cause or one precise global rate.
