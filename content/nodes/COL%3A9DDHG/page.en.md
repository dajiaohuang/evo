## Moldenhawera longipedicellata {#s-eeab182f6c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Moldenhawera longipedicellata* as an accepted species of *Moldenhawera* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records reddish or translucent Malpighiaceae hairs on young branches and several flower parts, including petals and sepals. Its leaves are pinnate with elliptic to oblong leaflets; the flowers are pentamerous and yellow, with nine staminodes (sterile stamens), and the oblong legume has no upper wing.
