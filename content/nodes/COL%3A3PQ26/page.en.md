## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-50a3abd4f5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes angled branches with hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent lance-shaped stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) remain attached. The leaf stalk (petiole) and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; the field lists three or four leaflet pairs. A stalkless, saucer-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. The flower cluster is spike-like, the calyx (outer floral cup) funnel-shaped, and the flowers lack separate stalks. The leathery pod is described with alternative shapes: flat, linear, or curved; its surface is hairless. This is a single regional field; it preserves those alternatives without establishing variation or their frequency.

## Habitat category {#s-674f385603}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial (growing on land). That is the category in one row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of distribution.
