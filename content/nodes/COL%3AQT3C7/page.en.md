## Rice fields and still pools in northern Guangxi {#s-bc105a9367}
The study described frogs in northern Guangxi rice paddies and still pools at 300–1300 m. Advertisement calls were heard during April–July surveys. These records retain the sampled environmental and seasonal scope, without establishing all occupied habitats or a complete breeding calendar.

## Open nests made from rice stems {#s-d1d6cfd478}
The authors observed nest construction and eggs in uncovered nests made from rice stems, with nest diameters of 20–30 cm. Females carried creamy-yellow eggs with dark poles. These observations do not establish clutch-size distributions, parental-care duration or the survival advantage of nesting.
