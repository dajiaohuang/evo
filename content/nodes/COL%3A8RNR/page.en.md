## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Acalypha acuminata? {#s-ec6d2184bf}
The record describes an upright woody plant with simple, non-glandular hairs on the stem. Leaves have pinnate veins and serrated edges, and lack stipels, the small appendages at the base of leaflets. Axillary and terminal flower clusters are recorded: terminal clusters may be female or bisexual, while axillary ones are male. Female flowers are sessile or nearly so, with three calyx parts and upright, toothed bracts. Their separate styles are irregularly divided into narrow lobes. The fruit is smooth and may be hairless or hairy; seeds lack a caruncle and may be smooth or pitted.
