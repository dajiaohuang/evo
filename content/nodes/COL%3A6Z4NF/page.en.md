## Reduced leaves and a yellow flower {#s-e601da5d70}
Flora do Brasil describes Sisyrinchium subnudum with a conspicuous rhizomes (horizontal underground stems) and absent or rudimentary foliage leaves. Its terete (round in cross-section) flowering stalk bears terminal bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) and flat yellow flowers; the staminal column has glandular hairs near its base. Fruit characters are unknown in this record.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-aca2432875}
The source classifies the species as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant).
