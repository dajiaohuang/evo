## ## Compact panicles and a long compressed fruit {#s-810ce6538d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records short, straight hairs on the branches, leaves, inflorescences and calyx; they are rusty and silky or pubescent. The leaflets are papery to chartaceous, with an upper surface that is glabrous or rarely sparsely hairy. Compact panicles are shorter than the adjacent leaf, and flowers reach 15 mm. The samara-like fruit is laterally compressed, with the seed near its center; it is papery to chartaceous and longer than 7 cm. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
