## Aspilia caudata {#s-661229dc84}
## Opposite leaves and yellow radiate heads

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes woody, hairy stems and petiolate leaves arranged in opposite pairs, with each pair at right angles to the next. The papery leaves range from lance-shaped to ovate or ovate-lance-shaped. Several flower heads may form a corymb; each has a cylindrical involucre of overlapping bracts and a flat receptacle bearing paleae, or narrow chaff scales. The flowers are yellow: outer ligulate florets are neutral, while the central tubular florets are bisexual. Their anthers form a tube; disc florets are fertile, while the ray florets have rudimentary female parts. The dry fruits have an oblong attachment base (carpopodium) and no awned pappus.
