## A tall woolly shrub with twisted leaves {#s-14a9c80e30}
The account describes an erect, white-woolly shrub usually up to 2.5 m tall, sometimes reaching 4 m. Its short, twisted leaves are often bent backward and have small hair tufts in their axils. Three- to five-flowered heads form terminal clusters; the inner petal-like bracts are white and can sometimes be brown. Flowering is recorded mainly from June to October, on sandy or stony flats and slopes across the listed regional range.
