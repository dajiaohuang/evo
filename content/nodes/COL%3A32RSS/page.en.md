## Cybianthus potiaei (Mez) G.Agostini {#s-95a21599b2}
## Smooth shoots and axillary panicles

Cybianthus potiaei has smooth, cylindrical branches more than 3 mm in diameter, without swollen nodes. Its elliptic leaves are papery to leathery and hairless, with acute tips and bases; curved secondary veins are visible beneath. Axillary panicles bear four-parted flowers with rounded corolla lobes. The source does not describe the staminate flowers or their stamens; pistillate flowers are stalked. The immature fruit wall is globose and dotted.
