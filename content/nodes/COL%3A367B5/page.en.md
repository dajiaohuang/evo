## ## Membranous leaflets and yellow-green flowers {#s-9fc215cfae}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaves with three or four pairs of opposite pinnae—the side axes that carry the leaflets. The lanceolate or oval leaflets have rounded bases and pointed or gradually tapering tips; they are membranous, with fine hairs below and a glabrous upper surface. The flowers are yellow to green; the fusiform ovary is glabrous. The mature fruit is woody, elongated, straight or slightly curved, and reddish brown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
