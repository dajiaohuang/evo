## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-cc9796ad59}
COL26.8 records Cryptoblepharus ahli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.
