## Two mammaliaform fossils trace a jaw–ear shift {#s-112e6b5e0f}
The Dianoconodon holotype (IVPP V4257) and a Feredocodon paratype (IMMNH-PV01925) preserve jaw-joint and postdentary bones used to compare load-bearing before the middle ear became fully detached from the jaw. Reduced loading is an anatomical inference, not a measurement of bite force or hearing. Their combined 201.4–161.5 Ma range spans separate fossils and formations, not a continuous lineage.
