## Two veins beside leathery leaves {#s-a3d8d03d32}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves with a gradually narrowing base and a tip that tapers to a point. The leaves are leathery, with flat margins and no hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface; two additional veins run near the edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis. The field gives no bract status. Smaller leaf-like parts beside flowers, called bracteoles, persist and are inconspicuous. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx, the outer ring of flower parts, is open in bud, with four sepals (outer parts) and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth. The source marks fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo as unknown.

## A terrestrial label without a locality {#s-7447e7d38d}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It gives no collection locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This is a habitat category rather than a distribution account.
