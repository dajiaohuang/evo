Archived PBDB field scm (source-language text; not translated or interpreted):
The shallow-marine deposits of the Kababish Formation are correlated with shallow-marine Campanian–Maastrichtian sediments in the Dakhla and Asyut Basins in Egypt (Barazi 1985; Wycisk 1994). Similar fossil plant communities occur in both the Kababish Formation and the Campanian–Maastrichtian sediments of the Western Desert of Egypt. For example, plant fossils such as Credneria, Ficophyllum, Magnoliaephyllum, and Typhaephyllum, which occur in the Kababish Formation (Barazi 1985), are also found in the Kiseiba Formation (Campanian–Maastrichtian) in southern Egypt near the border with Sudan, as well as in the Quseir Formation (Campanian–Maastrichtian) and the Mut Formation (Campanian) in the vicinity of the Dakhla and Kharga oases (Klitzsch and Hermina 1989). Lejalia leaves have been found in both the Kababish Formation (Coiffard and Mohr 2015) and the Mut Formation (Campanian) near the Dakhla Oasis (Coiffard and El Atfy 2023).
