## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Maprounea amazonica? {#s-02c08873e3}
The oval leaves have blunt to rounded tips. Glands are scattered along the secondary veins but absent at the leaf base. The inflorescence has one small female flower. The fruit is recorded as 8–10 mm long.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-cae7922d04}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
