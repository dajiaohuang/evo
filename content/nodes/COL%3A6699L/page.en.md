## Selected hand and colour contrasts {#s-fd6e83c184}
The2020 author comparison reports transverse limb bars, non-uniform fingerIII phalangeal width and a translucent-white throat for grillisimilis, contrasting those selected characters with velocicantus. These are attributed comparative descriptions, not new measurements or a complete key. Similar preserved ventral colours and male size can make superficial identification difficult; the paper does not authenticate all historical museum labels through these traits.
