## Armed leaf stalks and oblong fruit {#s-7730d6ed32}
The morphology record describes leaves with three to five leaflets; both the leaf stalks and blades bear prickles, and the edges are finely toothed and fringed with hairs. The flowers have lance-shaped sepals and oblong petals. The ovary is oblong to inversely lance-shaped, with a rounded stigma. The fruit is oblong to inversely lance-shaped.
