## Solitary flowers and fruit fields left unknown {#s-8d5d37f74e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves with gradually narrowed or wedge-shaped bases and tips that are acute or rounded. The leaves are papery; their margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is described as grooved or flat, with one or two veins near the leaf edge. Flowers are solitary, with persistent supporting bracts and small, inconspicuous bracteoles beside the flower. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. Fruit shape and surface, fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial label without a locality {#s-50115b99f3}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category is not a full ecological account.
