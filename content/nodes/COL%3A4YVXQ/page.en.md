## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-08136dc4f1}
COL26.8 records Sphenomorphus transversus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e4b69c09d8}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 68 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-cd26070bee}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as concealed (Cryptic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
