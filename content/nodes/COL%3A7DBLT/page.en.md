## Comparative results retain their measurement limits {#s-dd7423a416}
Although assigned to Yaotriton in the paper, T. panhai had a dorsal external-head mean within the Tylototriton subgenus morphospace. This exception prevents treating subgenus separation as universal across sampled species and views. An external-head mean outside its group’s main region does not reassign the species, establish hybrid ancestry or demonstrate a breeding mode. External and osteological datasets have different distributions.
