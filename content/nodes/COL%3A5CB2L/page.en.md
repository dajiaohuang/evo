## Examined Medog localities and the wider cited account {#s-592e2abb4b}
Male holotype CIB 24350, formerly CIB 73II0015, was listed from Medog, Xizang, at 1000 m. The monograph examined adults from Beibeng at 870 m, Xirang at about 750 m and the Medog urban neighbourhood at 907 m. Its wider cited distribution account covered multiple Medog localities at 600–2200 m. These newly examined sites and the wider account remain separate; the word common in the summary is not a quantified density or population trend.

## Eight measured adults are not the large historical type series {#s-1e57676b6c}
The examined diagnosis reported six adult males at 56.1–62.4 mm snout–vent length and two females at 73.8–79.6 mm. The separately listed historical paratypes included 160 adult males, a juvenile and tadpoles without supplied voucher numbers; none is silently added to those measured adult denominators. Diagnostic anatomy included vomerine ridges and teeth, rudimentary basal toe webbing without fringes, and dense black nuptial spines on fingers I and II in breeding males with a subgular vocal sac. These descriptions do not establish diet or breeding-season timing.
