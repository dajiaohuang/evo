## Aloe verecunda: two-ranked leaves and pink-red flowers {#s-cbe4cf9149}
A South African regional account describes a short-stemmed succulent herb up to 450 mm tall. Its leaves are non-fibrous, arranged in two ranks and 160–400 × 7–15 mm; the margins usually have minute teeth. The bracts are pointed and shorter than the pedicels. Flowers range from scarlet to magenta.

The diagnostic account says large *A. verecunda* plants can resemble *A. cooperi*, but lack its keeled leaves. *A. verecunda* has fewer leaves per head and smaller inflorescences in all parts. Differences from *A. vossii* are referred to that species’ account.
