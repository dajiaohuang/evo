## Plagiochila disticha: two habitat labels {#s-7d0bb5dea9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this taxon:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Rupícola” (rock-associated).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
