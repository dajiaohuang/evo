## Shenacanthus pairs shark-like traits with heavy armour {#s-e950481201}
The near-complete holotype IVPP V300000 from China’s roughly 436-million-year-old Huixingshao Formation combines minute scales, a chondrichthyan-like rear branchial region and large thoracic armour plates. Its body plan places familiar fish features beside a substantial armoured covering, a combination documented in one specimen.

Only this specimen is known, and it preserves no teeth. Maximum-parsimony and Bayesian analyses differ over its precise position within the chondrichthyan total group. The mosaic is not a crown-shark diagnosis and does not establish the global first appearance of Chondrichthyes.
