## Distinguish major genetic groups from boundaries within them {#s-fd9585282f}
The 2014 study expands geographic coverage with 91 samples and compares mitochondrial data with three nuclear loci. Nuclear analyses support four major dwarf-lemur groups without making every within-group locality a distinct clade: the authors report allele sharing across populations more than 900 km apart, with subdivided Medius A as an exception. Deep mitochondrial divergence does not replace nuclear results or independent evidence of reproductive isolation.

The authors discuss ancient introgression, incomplete lineage sorting and insufficient nuclear data as possible causes of nuclear/mitochondrial incongruence, without selecting one established mechanism. ## Dwarf-lemur research questions and reading routes {#s-e4d9a95321}
Dwarf lemurs are nocturnal primates endemic to Madagascar. Fat storage in the tail and seasonal hibernation are recurring research themes, but hibernation sites, timing, and local ecology differ. Species boundaries in the genus have long been disputed: a 2009 multilocus analysis supported fewer principal genetic lineages, while a 2014 revision that added geographic samples from 91 individuals proposed additional species and candidate species; Lavasoa, Montagne d'Ambre, Ankarana, and Groves's dwarf lemurs were formally described later. Page identities here follow pinned COL26.8 and do not turn its accepted count into a biological consensus.

Suggested path: compare the different taxonomic interpretations of Groeneveld et al. and Lei et al.; then read the locality-bounded descriptions by Thiele, Lei, Frasier, and McLain. Next, use Dausmann's hibernation study of the fat-tailed dwarf lemur, Lahann's local study of the greater dwarf lemur, and Blanco et al.'s same-site comparison of Crossley's and Sibree's dwarf lemurs to explore ecological differences. Hending et al. (2023) compiled records from 1964–2020 but modelled only three of nine catalogue species because the other six had too few occurrence points; a species not modelled is not a species shown to be absent.
