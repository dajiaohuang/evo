## What marks Duguetia sessilis? {#s-1caefbdabf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and smooth or sparsely scaled underneath. Flower clusters may be whip-like or grow directly from older stems. The flowers are red or pink, with pink stamens. Pink fruit ranges from flattened-elliptic to spherical and is densely covered with star-shaped or scale-like hairs; it lacks a basal collar.

## What broad habitat is given? {#s-10c8a40b02}
The source records it as terrestrial, without a named site.
