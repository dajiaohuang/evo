## Seven focal fields from the 2015 archive {#s-b4117a42ab}
Rattus tiomanicus is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. Seven focal life-history fields are compared with cited species-level sparse-table rows matching the name and family. The archive’s historical summaries may inherit older taxonomic data; matching names do not rule that out.

## Mass values for adult and newborn stages {#s-5e763be1c7}
The life-history archive gives adult body mass of 142.68 g and birth or hatching mass of 4.54 g. Adult mass can combine males, females, and records with unspecified sex; the newborn value concerns another stage. These are not repeated measurements of the same animals or a full range of individual masses.

## Four measures across reproduction and weaning {#s-b327efaa46}
The reported values are female maturity, defined as age at first conception, 102.65 d; offspring or eggs per litter or clutch, 5.88; conception or fertilization to birth, 22.26 d; and age of dependence on food other than maternal milk, 26.09 d. Here d means days. Litter/clutch is a combined average, not a fractional event, and each timing describes a different stage rather than one uniform cycle.

## The largest longevity value {#s-63d4f691d3}
maximum_longevity_y is 4.2 years, the largest collected record under the archive’s method. It does not estimate typical or mean wild lifespan, set a limit for every individual, or establish a currently repeatable record.

## References and rows are not independent samples {#s-b01749beac}
Source labels include Jones, K., Bielby, J., Cardillo, M. et al., 2009; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; Ernest et al., 2003; and Ricklefs, 2010b, among others. Field-specific row numbers, values, labels, and full reference notes remain in the archive. A label can point to a paper, book, or database. Row counts are not counts of measured animals, and repeated references do not establish independent replication.

## EltonTraits supplies a separate ecological record {#s-90cfbdaa82}
Rattus tiomanicus is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. Three ecological field groups are drawn from its same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record. That archive follows earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals, so the correspondence does not resolve later splits or synonyms.

## Relative diet weights from descriptions {#s-7e33a16d84}
The archive assigns 40% to seeds, nuts, and spores, 30% to invertebrates, 20% to fruit, and 10% to other plant material, including fungi. The percentages standardize verbal accounts, not measured intake. ABC pools high, reasonable, and partial confidence; some estimates may use a congener judged ecologically similar, so direct observation of this species is not assured. Zero weight is not proof of never eating an item; Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broad source categories.

## Ground is a category, not a time share {#s-ac180b11d0}
The mammalian entry is G, the ground category, whose source definition includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment; metadata traces mammalian stratum values collectively to Nowak (1999). This does not quantify time across strata or imply exclusive use.

## Night activity is a coded period {#s-784074d6da}
The archive marks feeding activity at night with 1. Other periods can also be marked 1, so the code is not a proportion of a daily time budget and does not describe every day, season, or location. Activity certainty is ABC and may depend on an ecologically similar congener; genus/family typical activity values are not used to fill this field.

## Source labels stay with their field groups {#s-55c5bdaa09}
The groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum, and activity may come from different sources; Ref numbers repeated across bird and mammal tables need not denote the same work. The archive preserves original strings by file, row, group, and reference table. Underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so labels do not add independent observations.
