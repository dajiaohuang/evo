## Saranthe madagascariensis {#s-ac523a50c2}
## Rosette stems reaching 250 cm

The Brazilian Flora description records threadlike roots, an underground elongated rhizome, and upright or rosette-forming aerial stems 100–250 cm tall. Its narrow elliptic leaves taper at both ends; the midrib is sparsely silky-haired, and the petiole and sheath carry the listed hair and margin textures. The compressed inflorescence arises from the rhizome and bears two-flowered clusters; bracteoles are recorded absent. Flowers have unequal pedicels and a short, broad, three-lobed tube. The ovary is cylindrical and glabrous, with three chambers but only one fertile chamber. Capsules and seeds are marked not observed in this source, which does not establish that they are absent. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
