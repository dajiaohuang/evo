## Symphyopappus reitzii {#s-827618b7e8}
## Glandular leaves in terminal corymbs

The source records upright or decumbent, glabrous stems. Elliptic leaves are petiolate, serrated and glandular; their arrangement may be opposite, alternate or densely spiral. Terminal corymbs have a glabrous or pitted receptacle and five tubular florets per head. The cypselae have five ribs and sparse bristly hairs, with a two-row pappus. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
