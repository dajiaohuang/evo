## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Lacunaria grandifolia? {#s-e6d928d641}
The leathery elliptic leaves are whorled, with entire or rolled-under margins and 20–40 pairs of side veins. The leaf-size value is given as 30–60, but the claim does not supply a unit. Axillary flowers have four sepals, eight petals and 115–133 stamens; outer sepals are 5–6 mm, inner sepals 8 mm, and petals 10–11 mm. There are 10–14 styles. The subglobose fruit is 4–5 cm, with a rounded tip.
