## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-7add23b90e}
COL26.8 records Trachyphonus at genus rank, with Lybiidae as its direct parent.

This Trachyphonus route includes 3 measured species records: Trachyphonus erythrocephalus, Trachyphonus margaritatus and Trachyphonus vaillantii. The hierarchy is pinned to COL26.8; AVONET v7 values keep their historical names and sampling scope.

## Examples for comparison {#s-2cac4b116d}
Compiled mean mass spans 63.5 g for Trachyphonus erythrocephalus to 69.4 g for Trachyphonus vaillantii. The records represent Shrubland and Woodland habitat categories, and an Omnivore trophic niche. These examples guide comparison among pages, not shared genus traits or a complete descendant list.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-eaecbbf33d}
This guide covers this batch’s measured entries with one-to-one historical crosswalks and matching catalogue name and family, not all accepted descendants. Mass endpoints compare compiled species means, not an individual’s minimum and maximum mass. Each species account carries its own taxonomic, sampling and other biological limits.
