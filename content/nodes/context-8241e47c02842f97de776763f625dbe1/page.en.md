## The source concept {#s-2bcf90c6b6}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Atelopus patazensis* in row Anf0508 under Atelopus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-f0fffbcff7}
For Anf0508, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-3710211a2d}
The row carries a daytime activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-8393745879}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 48.8 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-f2f89295ad}
The row records 348 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch and 2 mm as both the minimum and maximum offspring or egg size. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every individual, clutch or offspring.
