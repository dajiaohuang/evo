## The Great Oxidation was a prolonged transition {#s-65c29a9d1c}
Sulfur-isotope and redox records point to a prolonged rise in oxygen, marked by the loss of mass-independent sulfur-isotope signals, widespread oxidized continental deposits and changes in carbon and sulfur cycles. U–Pb dates on baddeleyite bracket the onset at about 2,460–2,426 Ma. The records indicate episodes and different local ocean histories, not one instantaneous global threshold or trigger.
