## Oxypetalum coccineum {#s-4cefc21529}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum coccineum* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records the habitat category as terrestrial. The source gives no locality or complete distribution and includes no morphology, so this regional label is the extent of the supported account.
