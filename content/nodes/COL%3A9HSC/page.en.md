## A measured record of leaflets, fruit and seed

The available morphology record for Acosmium diffusissimum is limited to a few counts and measurements. Leaves have 11–29 leaflets, whose bases are rounded or blunt. The calyx is 1.7–2.3 mm long, the fruit 0.9–1.3 cm wide, and the seeds 4–6 mm wide.
