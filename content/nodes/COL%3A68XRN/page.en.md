## A pink-flowered vine with compressed pods {#s-9297ea624f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes twining branches that range from nearly smooth to hairy, and elliptic leaflets with tapering tips. Pink flowers grow in axillary inflorescences. The pod is laterally compressed, hairy and opens at maturity; the record notes a raised ridge along its middle. Its seeds are brown and ellipsoid. These are the row-level traits recorded for this species.
