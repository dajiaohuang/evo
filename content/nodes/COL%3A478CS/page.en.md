## A historical feeding record, with gaps {#s-842a22a640}
COL26.8 accepts Nesoromys ceramicus in the family Muridae. EltonTraits 1.0 provides one interpretable field group here: historical food-category weights. The same-name, same-family link follows an older taxonomy and does not resolve later splits or synonym disputes.

## Weights assigned to food descriptions {#s-4c7cbb95d3}
EltonTraits assigns 40% to invertebrates, 30% to seeds, nuts and spores, and 30% to other plant material; the last source category also includes fungi. These weights standardize written descriptions, not measured food intake. The ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and a field may use a related species in the same genus judged ecologically similar. The weights are therefore not all direct observations of Nesoromys ceramicus. A zero weight would not prove that a food is never eaten.

## Evidence limits {#s-61e4b28bdb}
This page interprets only the historical food-weight field. EltonTraits withholds the foraging-stratum field under code B because its category is uncertain or unavailable, and activity under code D2 because of uncertainty, interpolation or source-link limits. Those withheld fields do not support claims about where or when the animal feeds. The available weights summarize old literature descriptions and can include material from a related species in the same genus; the underlying sources have not been individually read. The historical name match does not prove equivalence with COL26.8 or provide a complete account of the species, its current range or conservation.
