## What underground, leaf and flower features are recorded? {#s-d1048193f0}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field records a rhizome and Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves have cuneate (wedge-shaped) bases, linear to elliptic blades and flat margins. The inflorescence is bracted; flower stalks are listed as 0.1–6 mm long, and bracteoles are absent. The 3–10 mm floral tube bears stiff, closely pressed or Malpighian-type hairs outside. Its blunt spur points horizontally. Petals may be white, purple or pink and are absent by the fruit stage; stamens attach around the middle third of the tube. No vesicle is recorded. A nectar gland at the ovary base points upward. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin blunt. The row's alternatives are not tied to individual specimens or frequencies.
