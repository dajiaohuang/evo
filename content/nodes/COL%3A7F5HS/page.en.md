## How are its leaves, bladder traps and yellow flowers described? {#s-2ae6327304}
The source records simple oval-to-elliptic leaves with a single main vein. Its ovoid bladder trap opens at the base and bears a simple, straight appendage on the back. The yellow corolla has a three-lobed lower lip and a curved, cone-shaped spur shorter than that lip, with a blunt tip. The fruit opens through a ventral pore and the seeds are globose. This is a morphological account, not a survey of trait variation.
