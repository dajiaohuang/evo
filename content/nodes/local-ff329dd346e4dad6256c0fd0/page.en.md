## The deposited caecilian record {#s-5d3fb13771}
The fourth version of CaecilianTraits lists Dermophis gracilior in the genus Dermophis and family Dermophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. Whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept has not been established.

## Measurement material {#s-2b8804c30e}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-1f95006297}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-da70c6899b}
The compilation classifies the species as viviparous, meaning that it bears live young. The accompanying metadata describes this as presumed, probable or expected, so it remains a qualified compilation statement rather than an unqualified observation.

## Compiled clutch counts {#s-2c5817fa7c}
The source species record lists a minimum clutch size of 2 and a maximum of 16. These counts come from cited reproductive accounts; they describe the compiled source material, not universal limits or annual reproductive output. The associated reproductive metadata remains part of their interpretation.
