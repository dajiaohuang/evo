## Hippia hutchinsonii {#s-1e3c94ebe8}
## A low shrublet with three-part leaves

The flora description records a slender-stemmed shrublet up to 25 cm tall. Its leaves divide into three narrow, linear lobes. Yellow disc-shaped heads are borne in sparse clusters at the stem tips, and the achenes are ellipsoid. The habitat field places it on sandstone slopes.
