## Oxypetalum schottii {#s-229adafca9}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum schottii* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry is limited to the habitat category terrestrial. It does not identify a collection site, describe morphology, or establish the species’ full distribution.
