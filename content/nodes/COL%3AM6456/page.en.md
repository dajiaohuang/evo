## Accepted name and placement {#s-d2c5df645a}
COL26.8 accepts Breviceps ombelanonga Nielsen, Conradie, Ceríaco, Bauer, Heinicke, Stanley & Blackburn, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brevicipitidae (family) → Breviceps (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Three genetically supported Angolan areas {#s-26a776e94e}
The study confirmed records from Kissama National Park in western Angola, central Bié and river-source areas in Moxico. Reported elevations ranged from near sea level to above 1400 m. Other Angolan Breviceps records were left uncertain pending additional sampling and genetics; they are not automatically assigned to this species.

## Rain-associated collections and food records {#s-fafda66796}
The type series was collected after gentle rain in habitats spanning sandy savanna and wetter miombo woodland. The holotype was observed eating small unidentified ants. Remains were also reported from two snake stomachs. These local records identify observed feeding and predation events, not the complete diet; egg sites and clutch sizes were unknown in the description.
