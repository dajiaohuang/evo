## Life-history evidence limits {#s-e5b3d5c4c2}
The values come from a historical 2015 compilation. They are reported medians or means of two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespan or a current population survey. The archive may carry information between historical species and subspecies; matching name, family and source rows support the association but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited data. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range and conservation require other sources.
