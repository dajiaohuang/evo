## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-242c01a72b}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts Strix nivicolum in Strigidae. The 2015 life-history archive has a same-name entry with seven usable fields, each linked to cited species-level rows in the same family. The summaries may include data inherited across historical species or subspecies; a matching name does not remove that possibility.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-4128bfa211}
The archive reports adult body mass of 496.5 g and birth or hatching mass of 28 g. Adult mass may combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex. The two values describe different life stages; they are not measurements tracking the same individuals or a range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-ade733ce3d}
The archive reports female maturity at 364.23 d, defined by its source as age at first conception; 3.1 offspring per litter or eggs per clutch; 29.7 d from oviposition to hatching; and age at flight capability of 31.2 d. In some source material, flight capability is represented by leaving the nest. Here, d means days. The litter/clutch field combines young and egg counts, so a fractional summary does not mean one event produced part of an individual. These time fields describe different events, not one complete reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-f505e0368f}
The archive's maximum_longevity_y field is 22.42 years, the largest record in its collected material under the source method. It is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, a limit for every individual, or a record shown to be repeatable today.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-149401079f}
The matching sparse rows cite Dunning, 1992; Szekely, Lislevand, and Figuerola, 2007; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; and Bennett, 1986. The source archive keeps each field's row number, value and label, with full reference explanations. A label may point to a paper, book or database. Rows are not measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experiments.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-e5b3d5c4c2}
The values come from a historical 2015 compilation. They are reported medians or means of two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespan or a current population survey. The archive may carry information between historical species and subspecies; matching name, family and source rows support the association but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited data. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range and conservation require other sources.
