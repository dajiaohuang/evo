## Slender plants with smaller salmon-pink flowers {#s-0ef596dadf}
Romulea gracillima is more slender than R. hirsuta and has smaller, paler pink to salmon flowers, with tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 12–18 × 3–5 mm. The source says it may represent a small montane form of R. hirsuta rather than a distinct species.

## Sandstone slopes {#s-ae3d36ec1b}
The regional account records sandstone slopes as habitat.
