## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Jatropha grossidentata? {#s-8444844198}
The gray, hairless stem bears petiole scars and clear latex. Long-stalked leaves are glabrous and divided into three or five lobes with serrated edges. The inflorescences are capitate cymes at the shoot tips. The corolla is recorded as red, wheel-shaped, and hairless. Male flowers have eight stamens joined into one bundle and arranged in one row; female flowers have three styles. The fruit is a three-chambered capsule.
