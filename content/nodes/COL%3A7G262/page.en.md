## Leathery leaves and smooth fruits {#s-a275f04409}
The record describes upright stems that may be smooth, nearly smooth or hairy, but lack glands. Stalked, leathery leaves range from lance-shaped to oval or oblong; their tips are pointed, bases narrow and surfaces may be smooth or hairy. The heads contain 8–22 florets with smooth, glandless corollas. The fruits are smooth and glandless, with flattened outer pappus bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
