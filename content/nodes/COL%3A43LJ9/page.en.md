## Mirabilis rotundifolia {#s-efd16638c9}
## Branches fork above the leafy lower half

The cited Flora of North America account describes stems that are erect or ascending and 20–30 cm long. Leaves are concentrated mainly on the lower half of each stem, while the upper part forks openly. This excerpt supplies a limited stem-and-leaf outline only; it gives no further ecology or locality.
