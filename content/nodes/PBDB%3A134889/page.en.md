## Stratigraphic names and relationships have dated frameworks {#s-ec7cf9e040}
The 2002 paper records the type horizon as the Romualdo Member of the Santana Formation, Albian; the 2018 paper uses Romualdo Formation within the Santana Group. It explicitly notes disputed relationships and uses a framework with Thalassodrominae and Tapejarinae as sisters. ## Evidence limits {#s-b35ea7a213}
Published crest, feeding and flight models offer functional hypotheses rather than direct observations of behavior. Their interpretation depends on specimen attribution, preserved geometry and model assumptions. The selected PBDB occurrences do not establish a complete distribution or species history.
