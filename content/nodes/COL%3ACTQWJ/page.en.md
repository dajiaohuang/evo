## taxonomy_story {#s-6db22c480e}
Reports of unusually large males from Newcastle prompted renewed comparison of funnel-web spiders from the Sydney region. Researchers combined morphology and DNA from new collections with historical museum specimens. In that regional species complex, the 2025 analysis distinguished the Sydney funnel-web *Atrax robustus*, reinstated *A. montanus*, and newly described *A. christenseni*. The result applies to those samples and that species-delimitation analysis; it does not resolve relationships across the whole genus.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-2b4c168e5b}
The 2025 species delimitations cover material from the Sydney region. A pinned checklist helps identify names, but it does not settle species concepts outside the study's samples and analysis.
