## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-da7c487b20}
The lance-shaped stipules are entire or nearly so. Leaves may be oval, elliptic or oblanceolate, with pointed or blunt tips and toothed to slightly toothed margins; their surfaces range from finely hairy to woolly. The flowers are yellow or bluish. They have robust stalks, nearly equal lance-shaped sepals, oblong upper petals and spatula-shaped side petals. A reddish-brown membranous appendage is present, as is a hairless nectar appendage on the filament, described as cone- or globe-shaped. The style is straight and hairless, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is a leathery, ovoid capsule.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-e0853e9a0a}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
