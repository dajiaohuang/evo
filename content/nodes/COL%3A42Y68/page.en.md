## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-c5f22d0e6c}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands or long-stalked glandular hairs on the stem. Leaves may be stalked or sessile, differ in color between their two faces, and bear spherical glands or long-stalked glandular hairs; one or three veins are listed. Flowers are solitary. The floral cup is cylindrical and may bear either type of glandular hair. Sepals are awl-shaped, glandular and shorter than or equal to the cup. The corolla is pink. Anthers are two-colored and have four pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. This regional database field lists alternatives without frequencies.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-376be4ed4d}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat, without a locality or collection date.
