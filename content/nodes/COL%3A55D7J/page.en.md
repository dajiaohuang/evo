## Tephrosia vogelii {#s-1dbf6c801f}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tephrosia vogelii* as an accepted species of *Tephrosia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes pinnate leaves with more than three leaflets and terminal flower clusters; the petals are pink, and the woolly pods contain oval, marbled seeds. Its habitat field says terrestrial. These regional data fields do not give a complete account of worldwide variation.
