## The species in a pinned catalogue {#s-df369696a8}
COL26.8 recognizes *Gammarus pulex* (Linnaeus, 1758) as an accepted species. The research described here concerns one laboratory comparison of this species with two *Dikerogammarus* species; it does not represent a complete account of the species or variation among its wild populations.

## Leaf processing changed with the experiment's temperature {#s-036076bf3f}
In laboratory microcosms—small, controlled experimental systems—researchers compared leaf detritus processing by U.K.-native *Gammarus pulex* and two invasive *Dikerogammarus* species. They varied temperature (8, 14 and 20 °C) and supplied oak, sycamore or alder leaves. At the lower temperature treatments, *G. pulex* processed the leaves faster than either tested *Dikerogammarus* species; the differences between species became smaller as treatment temperature increased. The result belongs to these experimental animals, diets and temperatures. It does not measure leaf breakdown in rivers, natural population dynamics or responses across the species' full environmental range.
