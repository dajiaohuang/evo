## Serrated leaves and separate flower-head forms {#s-0010a1fdee}
The morphology record describes developed, nearly stalkless leaves with pinnate veins and serrated margins; the blades are obovate or oblong. Flower heads are grouped in panicles or corymbs. The record separates heads made only of tubular male flowers from those made only of slender female flowers, and lists both rocky and terrestrial substrates.
