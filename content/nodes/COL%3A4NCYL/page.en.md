## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-1043e09b43}
COL26.8 records Psammophis sudanensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Psammophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-ae4a7343ad}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1655 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Shine et al. 2006.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-551126367d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-baa5184920}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 8.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Broadley 1983, Marais 2004, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-2d69cfae48}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
