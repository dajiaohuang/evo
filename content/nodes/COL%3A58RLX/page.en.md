## What leaf, flower and capsule traits are recorded? {#s-0d8f3c94bc}
The stems are decumbent. Opposite, ovate leaves have persistent, divided stipules, glandular blades and yellowish undersides. The inflorescence is a panicle; the bracts and sepals are not glandular. One petal type is round, swollen and glandular outside; another is spatula-shaped and hairy. The flower has six stamens, four staminodes (sterile stamens) and glands recorded with two, three or more deeply divided lobes; the ovary has three chambers. The capsule retains a central framework (replum) and may split lengthwise either from tip to base or from base to tip. It is lanceolate or elliptic with a tapering tip. Its outer wall may be hairy or net-patterned, and the inner wall is hairy. The valves may be entire at the tip or attached to the framework at the base; the framework may be free and threadlike, and the valve backs are angular. Seeds have long, curled hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-22c986af0b}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
