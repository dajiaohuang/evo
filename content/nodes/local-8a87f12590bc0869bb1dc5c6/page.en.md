## The deposited caecilian record {#s-7a68bfdda6}
The fourth version of CaecilianTraits lists Gymnopis multiplicata in the genus Gymnopis and family Dermophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. Whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept has not been established.

## Measurement material {#s-cd26dac544}
The compilation lists 33 measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of 33 distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-e2d024c635}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-3e0ee4d743}
The compilation classifies the species as viviparous, meaning that it bears live young. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as absent. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults never use water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
