## What leaf and flower traits are noted for Pausandra martini? {#s-8eef2775a8}
The branches are hairless. Leaves have tapering tips and are papery to slightly leathery; their surfaces may be pubescent or become nearly smooth, and the leaf stalks do not darken. Flowers grow in spikes of small clusters and are sessile or nearly so. Male flowers are hairy and have six stamens. In female flowers, the petals are joined above their midpoint. The fruit is hairy, and the seed surface is mottled.
