PanTHERIA 1.0 links 52 usable historical accounts to Feliformia. Examples include Atilax paludinosus, Genetta genetta, Galidia elegans, Galidictis fasciata, and Bdeogale crassicauda. Entries retain MSW05 names and field-specific notes or gaps; the 52 accounts describe this source route, not the current accepted-species count in COL26.8.

The available sex-adjusted historical adult-mass medians range from 281.83 g for Helogale parvula to 161,914.66 g for Panthera tigris. They summarize separate source records, not individual extremes or the complete body-size variation of Feliformia.

## Life-history fields in Ernest’s 77 accounts {#s-6b7890589e}
Ernest’s historical life-history route contains 77 Feliformia accounts, including Cryptoprocta ferox, Eupleres goudotii, Fossa fossana, Galidia elegans, and Galidictis fasciata. All 77 record adult mass and a maximum-lifespan value; 76 include offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, and 59 include conception or fertilization to birth. Counts describe this compilation’s field coverage, not a complete list of living feliforms.

## Food, strata, and activity fields in EltonTraits {#s-b57ab732e1}
The EltonTraits route contains 100 historical Feliformia entries, including Acinonyx jubatus, Caracal caracal, Catopuma badia, C. temminckii, and Felis catus. Food-category weights are available in 98 entries, foraging strata in 100, and activity periods in 99. Food weights standardize literature descriptions; strata and activity use separate categories or codes rather than direct measurements of an individual’s diet or daily time.

## One EltonTraits entry has only a food field {#s-b793e95b00}
A separate EltonTraits subset contains one record, Leopardus tigrinus. It has a food-category weight but no foraging-stratum or activity-period value in this selection. Those blank fields describe the subset, not the species’ complete ecology.

## Limits across the historical compilations {#s-4a967bfa34}
These pages cover separate historical samples with their own name crosswalks, selection rules, field definitions and source conditions; none covers every COL26.8 descendant. The life-history subset uses one-to-one same-name and same-family matches with at least three supported fields. EltonTraits separates entries with at least two usable field groups from those with exactly one; standardized food weights, activity codes and possible congeneric borrowing remain record-level limits, not evidence of shared Feliformia ecology or current distribution.

## Gestation, first reproduction, and maximum lifespan {#s-ee8b196e49}
Gestation appears in 30 accounts, female age at first reproduction in 24, and maximum lifespan in 29. First reproduction is distinct from first birth and sexual maturity; a maximum-lifespan entry is not an average. Source conditions vary, and missing values are not filled from related species.

## How to interpret Ernest’s historical values {#s-6b66aaca79}
These accounts use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 same-name crosswalk; this does not prove equivalence to current species concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally give literature sources equal weight regardless of sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies can be reused. Maximum lifespan is the longest record found, not typical lifespan. Adult mass is not interpreted in this Ernest route; wild, captive, and locality conditions were not established for every source.
