## PanTHERIA’s Feliformia records {#s-0ac545d7f7}
PanTHERIA 1.0 links 52 usable historical accounts to Feliformia, including Atilax paludinosus, Genetta genetta, Galidia elegans, Galidictis fasciata, and Bdeogale crassicauda. The records retain their MSW05 names and notes about field-specific conditions and gaps. COL26.8 supplies the catalogue navigation; 52 is the size of this source route, not the current accepted-species count.

## Adult-mass summaries in this set {#s-b8c3869db7}
The sex-adjusted historical adult-mass medians available here range from 281.83 g for Helogale parvula to 161,914.66 g for Panthera tigris. These values summarize different source records; they are not individual extremes or a complete account of body-size variation across Feliformia.

## Which life-history fields were recorded {#s-6b7890589e}
The life-history route contains 77 historical accounts. All 77 report adult mass and a maximum-lifespan record; 76 include offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, and 59 include conception or fertilization to birth. Examples include Cryptoprocta ferox, Eupleres goudotii, Fossa fossana, Galidia elegans, and Galidictis fasciata. These counts describe this compilation’s coverage, not a complete list of living feliforms; each field has its own event definition, unit, and sources.

## Food and activity records in EltonTraits {#s-b57ab732e1}
The EltonTraits route includes 100 historical Feliformia entries. Food-category weights are available for 98, foraging strata for 100, and activity periods for 99. Examples include Acinonyx jubatus, Caracal caracal, Catopuma badia, C. temminckii, and Felis catus. Food weights standardize literature descriptions; stratum and activity fields use separate categories or codes, not direct measures of each animal’s diet or daily time budget.

## One record with a food-category field {#s-b793e95b00}
A separate EltonTraits subset contains one entry, Leopardus tigrinus. It has a food-category weight but no foraging-stratum or activity-period record in this subset. That single field is not a complete ecological profile of the species.

## Scope of the historical comparisons {#s-4a967bfa34}
The pages summarize several historical compilations with different name crosswalks, selection rules, field definitions, and source conditions. They do not cover every COL26.8 descendant, and matching an older name does not establish that the historical and current species concepts are identical.

Adult-mass and life-history values summarize separate source records; they are not individual ranges or current population measurements. A maximum-lifespan value is a record, not an average, and a blank field does not show that the trait is absent.

EltonTraits food weights standardize literature descriptions, while mammalian ABC entries can include inference from a congener. Feeding strata and activity codes are categorical source summaries, not direct measurements of daily time budgets. Current distributions and conservation status require other evidence.

## What the PanTHERIA route represents {#s-39f4a42dc5}
This route includes historical accounts with at least one focal field, linked by matching names and families and a one-to-one historical correspondence. It does not cover every COL26.8 descendant. The adult-mass values are summaries from separate entries; current species concepts, distributions, and conservation status require other evidence.

## Different events, different summaries {#s-ee8b196e49}
Gestation appears in 30 accounts, female age at first reproduction in 24, and maximum lifespan in 29. First reproduction is distinct from first birth and sexual maturity; a maximum-lifespan record is not an average. Source conditions vary, and missing values are not filled from relatives.

## How to interpret these historical values {#s-6b66aaca79}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 name crosswalk; this does not prove equivalence with current species concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight, regardless of sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies can be reused. Maximum lifespan is the longest record found, not a typical lifespan. Adult mass is not interpreted in this Ernest route, and wild, captive, and locality conditions were not established for every source.
