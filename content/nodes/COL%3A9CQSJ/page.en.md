## Copaifera oblongifolia {#s-7ebbcd0e37}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Copaifera oblongifolia* records 5–8 oblong leaflet pairs with surface dots, legumes 2–3.5 cm long, yellow-orange seed covering. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
