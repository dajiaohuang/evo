## The captured thermal evidence {#s-d7714bdcc4}

CTmax and CTmin are the upper and lower critical thermal limits; LT50_hot and LT50_cold are temperatures associated with 50% mortality in hot and cold assays; Tpref is preferred temperature. These metrics describe different experiments and cannot be merged into one tolerance range. Overlapping metric counts do not sum to distinct species or animals.
