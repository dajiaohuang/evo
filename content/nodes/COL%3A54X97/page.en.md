## Five unarmed leaflets and a cylindrical fruit {#s-b70bb1f855}
The record describes five leaflets without prickles on either the stalk or blades. Their edges are fringed and may be entire or finely toothed. The flowers have ovate-lanceolate sepals, oblong-elliptic petals and a conical nectary; the stigma is rounded, and the style is not elongated. The fruit is cylindrical.
