## What the material shows {#s-4380c3bc7e}
Early Triassic maternal specimen AGM I-1 from south Majiashan Quarry preserves two embryos and an adjacent neonate. Its delayed ossification, dental and vertebral characters, and hypophalangeal flippers support assignment to Chaohusaurus, though species-level taxonomy can change.
