## ## Two leaflet pairs and small axillary clusters {#s-20e115f779}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two pairs of leaflets on alternate leaves arranged in two ranks; the xylopodium is listed as unknown. Leaflets range from narrowly oblong and oblong to obovate, and are rarely curved or sickle-shaped. Extrafloral nectar glands occur on the petiole, and colleters are recorded between the bud and stipule in the meristematic region. Axillary racemes are reduced to a few flowers or a fascicle. The ten-stamen flowers have yellow petals, sometimes with reddish bases. The oblong pods twist at maturity and range from 2–6.5 cm long; their surfaces may be silky-haired, pubescent or glabrous. Seeds are depressed, from rhomboid to obovoid. The source gives variable character states rather than a population frequency.
