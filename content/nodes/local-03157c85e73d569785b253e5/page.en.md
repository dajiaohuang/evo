## The source tadpole concept {#s-1711550e37}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Cardioglossa melanogaster, in the genus Cardioglossa and family Arthroleptidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Cardioglossa melanogaster. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-044582b53f}
The compilation has 11 records transcribed from literature and one measured from an image. Supplied stage labels are 24, 26, 27, 34, 35, 31, 36, 25 and 38; the last is marked “Judging based on the picture.” These labels belong to source records, not a single tadpole’s timeline. The compilation preserves uncertainty and identifies picture-based judgements instead of presenting them as directly documented stages. Record counts do not give the number of unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.

## Selected image-derived proportions {#s-afd8b99cc8}
Image-data row 225 gives five proportions at stage 38, labeled “Judging based on the picture”: body length / total length = 0.264; tail length / total length = 0.737; maximum body height / total length = 0.129; maximum tail height / total length = 0.111; and tail-muscle height / total length = 0.079. Values are rounded to three decimals and have no units. The source image may be a drawing or photograph, so these are not direct measurements of a representative wild population. Fields that do not agree with their same-row numerator and total length are left out.
