## Distribution and habitat {#s-91f8bbd25f}
The 2013 description limited known records to two sampled sites, Manantantely and Ivorona, in the Anosy region of southeastern Madagascar; the authors explicitly said the species was known only from those two sampled sites at the time. The records do not form a continuous range or a complete survey of nearby forest.

## Classification {#s-cd6d18f40d}
Ten samples from the two sites formed a distinct lineage in the mitochondrial data and nuclear fga analysis used by the paper, leading the authors to describe a new species. This supports genetic differentiation and a taxonomic interpretation under that sampling design; the paper also noted that field research on life history and social behaviour was lacking.

## Evidence limits {#s-59fccb7c51}
This is a taxonomic guide based on ten samples and two sites, not a complete range or behavioural review. The Chinese common name is editorial.
