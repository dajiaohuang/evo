## Distribution records {#s-92dc205122}
Mourthé et al. (2022) overlaid IUCN range maps on ten major Brazilian Amazon interfluves. Supplementary Table S1 lists Leontocebus fuscicollis as overlapping 1 area: Inambari. This is a regional map result; it does not confirm animals at every locality and is not an abundance estimate or a complete range assessment.
