## Saadanius and the catarrhine stem {#s-b55165a708}
The partial holotype SGS-UM 2009-002 preserves much of the face, palate and dentition, along with a tubular ectotympanic. Its age of 29–28 Ma is biochronological, and the published stem-catarrhine placement comes from a matrix of 36 characters and 19 taxa. The fossil documents a character combination within that analysis, not the catarrhine crown split’s direct date or a known ancestor.
