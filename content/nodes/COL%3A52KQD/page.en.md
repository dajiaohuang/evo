## Large smooth leaves around rounded flower heads {#s-1aa33c297f}
The morphology record describes young bark as smooth and leafy branches as nearly hairless. The oblong leaves are 10–11 cm long, smooth-edged and pointed, with short stalks. Panicles carry disk-shaped heads in bell-shaped involucres, each with about 20 smooth bracts. The fruits are smooth, cylindrical and topped with white bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
