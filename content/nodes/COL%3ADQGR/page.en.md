## ## Leaf surfaces, flowers and a three-seeded fruit {#s-2e3e3475fa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes leaf undersides as becoming nearly hairless and upper surfaces as hairless; it lists two to nine pairs of leaflets. Terminal inflorescences have reddish-brown hairs, and the petals are recorded as pink or purple. The fruit description notes a green outer layer, a woody fibrous inner layer and a granular, woody middle layer; three seeds are recorded. Its habitat category is “rupicolous,” meaning associated with rock. This is one regional record and gives no locality or complete range.
