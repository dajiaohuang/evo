## The name in the limited ecology record {#s-c6717b99e4}
Otus siaoensis is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Strigidae. This page links it to the same-name, same-family historical AVONET v7 record through two one-to-one crosswalks. Those links support comparison but do not resolve species concepts after later splits or lumps.

## Habitat, feeding and activity position {#s-ee19019fdc}
The recorded habitat category is tall-tree vegetation with a relatively closed canopy. The recorded primary lifestyle involves much time perched above ground on vegetation, rocks or other substrates. These coarse record categories do not replace food lists, behavioural observations or local microhabitat descriptions.

## The scope of the migration category {#s-7fb40f0b11}
The recorded migration category is sedentary. This is the source’s sedentary category; regional seasonal movements are not separately listed. This entry does not supply current routes, schedules or movement distances for every population.

## Evidence limits {#s-5a4554c733}
This account provides limited historical ecology and geography, not a complete species monograph. AVONET1_BirdLife uses HBW–BirdLife 5.0 names from December 2020. Matching names and families, together with one-to-one BirdLife/eBird/BirdTree crosswalks, establish an association rather than equivalence to current COL26.8 species concepts. Ecological fields are coarse literature-derived categories, and the underlying species literature was not individually reviewed here. Original morphology can involve inference from relatives; mass is from Dunning's 2008 compilation. Neither is interpreted as a direct measurement of this species. Historical maps cover breeding and resident ranges and do not assess current population, occupancy or conservation.
