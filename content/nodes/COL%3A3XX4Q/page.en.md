## What does the detailed account describe? {#s-0fbe083e4f}
Manulea bellidifolia is described as an annual 15–45 cm tall, often with several stems arising from a woody taproot. Leaves cluster mostly near the base, and simple racemes carry many flowers. The corolla is white, cream, or dull yellow with orange-yellow around the mouth and throat; stems, calyx, and corolla tube bear distinctive balloon-tipped hairs. Flowering is mainly May–October.

## Which traits help distinguish it? {#s-a0b0b076a3}
The source emphasizes a woody taproot, several loosely arranged stems, basal leaves, simple racemes, and balloon-tipped hairs on the corolla tube, calyx, and stems. It says these features help separate it from M. plurirosulata, while the annual habit, loose leaf arrangement, and simple racemes distinguish it from M. crassifolia.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-6b7fd25fea}
The account places it in sandy places, often along riverbanks and also on hillsides. It gives no complete range statement.
