## A hemispheric pattern in living-conifer time trees {#s-293596ac73}
A fossil-calibrated time tree sampling 489 living conifer species gives a combined median extant-node age of 8.7 Ma for predominantly Southern Hemisphere lineages, including Araucariaceae, versus 5.2 Ma for sampled Northern Hemisphere Pinaceae and Cupressoideae. These are modelled ages of living-lineage nodes, not fossil ranges or family-origin dates.
