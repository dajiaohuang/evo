## What leaf surfaces and flower parts are recorded? {#s-6047ce752b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes Chionanthus fluminensis with simple, opposite, oval leaves. Their leathery blades have entire edges, lack hairs underneath, and bear domatia—small structures at leaf-vein junctions. The source also records a calyx and corolla and four stamens. It does not provide measurements or a population survey.
