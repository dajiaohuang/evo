## How do the male and female flowers differ in Virola koschnyi? {#s-361c67dcaa}
The source describes dioecious trees up to 40 m tall, with large leathery leaves whose undersides carry ochre, stalked star-shaped hairs. Male flowers occur in branched panicles; female flowers form racemes of three to seven. The fruits are woody and split lengthwise into two valves, each 18–30 mm long, with an ellipsoid seed and a fringed aril.
