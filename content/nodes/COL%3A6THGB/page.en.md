## Oxypetalum tubatum {#s-5f386fb38d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum tubatum* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 source row records terrestrial habitat only; no locality, range, or morphological description accompanies it. This is a regional category, not a complete ecological profile.
