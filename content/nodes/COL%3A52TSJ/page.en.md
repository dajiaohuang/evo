## *Stomatanthes reticulatus*: branched clusters and seven-ribbed fruits {#s-b85493ccac}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records a woody underground swelling (xylopodium), alternate leaves with pinnate veins, and branched flower clusters. The style is spoon-shaped, and the fruitlets have seven ribs. The broad substrate field is “terrestrial”; no locality or fuller habitat description is supplied.
