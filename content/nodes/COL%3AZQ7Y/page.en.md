## What distinguishes Croton hemiargyreus in the record? {#s-9dddf486d8}
Young branches have scale-like hairs. The long-stalked oval or heart-shaped leaves have sparse or scale-like hairs above and scale-like hairs beneath, looping side veins, and toothed margins. A stalked nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and bell-shaped; female flowers are shortly stalked, with fused sepals, a four-part style, and equal calyx lobes. Female petals are reduced to glands, with a quincuncial overlap pattern in the bud. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
