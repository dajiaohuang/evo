## Phlegmariurus pungentifolius {#s-6033764195}
## Linear-lanceolate leaves and pitted spores

The source records a green-to-brown basal stem, upright or prostrate growth, and a decumbent base. The leaves may be uniform or spaced apart; their linear-lanceolate blades are attached in positions described as running down the stem, spreading, recurved or appressed. The leaf base is short and green. Spore-bearing leaves match the vegetative leaves. Two equal valves form each kidney-shaped sporangium; the spore surface is recorded as pitted or grooved.
