## Aloe komaggasensis {#s-3ba48fce0d}
Aloe komaggasensis has a short or absent stem and broad, boat-shaped grey-white leaves that are usually spineless. Its yellow, sometimes orange flowers hang in branched, rounded clusters. The regional account notes that a few leaves may have faint spots, a trait it compares with A. buhrii. Rocky mountain slopes are the recorded habitat.
