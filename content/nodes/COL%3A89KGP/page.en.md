## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5152da3bac}
COL26.8 records Nephrurus cinctus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Carphodactylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-9b19f4ba4d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-769471d976}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
