## One laboratory breeding event {#s-01d72fa4d4}
A laboratory male carried nine tadpoles on 7 June 2023. After transfer to water, the tadpoles were free by the following day; the first metamorph appeared on 21 August and the last on 10 September. The paper reports 74–94 elapsed days under those captive conditions, not a duration from fertilization or a wild developmental rate. Other breeding and territorial behaviour remained uncorroborated in the field.
