## ## Red exudate, divided leaves and a many-stamened flower {#s-0e0d0885d1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records red stem exudate and alternate compound leaves with one to five leaflets; leaflet length is listed as 0.5–1 (2) cm. Rust-colored, silky panicles may be terminal or axillary. The flowers are radially symmetric, and the description records 60 stamens; the fruit is a non-woody drupe with trichomes and two seeds. The habitat field says “terrestrial.” These are observations from one regional flora row, not a global description or account of population variation.
