## A pale-patterned window on a two-lobed succulent {#s-f3150f0bda}
This dwarf, cone-shaped succulent usually has two to four heads. The paired leaves are separated by a fairly deep cleft; viewed from above, they range from elliptical to kidney-shaped, with flat or slightly humped upper surfaces. Their windows may be open or partly obscured, with pinkish beige, light grey or pale green-grey markings. The yellow flower has a white centre and is about 20–25 mm across. It grows on gravelly flats among dark pebbles or in shallow limestone pockets.
