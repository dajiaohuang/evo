## Pradosia grisebachii (Pierre) T.D.Penn. {#s-e13fded4c2}
## Branch-borne flowers and red to vinaceous corollas

Pradosia grisebachii has no lenticels. Leaves may be alternate or whorled, papery to leathery, with grooved petioles and no intersecondary veins; the primary vein is depressed above. Small stipels are present. Flowers grow on the branches, and the corolla is red or vinaceous. The fruit surface is smooth, with an outer layer that may be hairless, nearly hairless or minutely hairy.
