## Gonolobus parviflorus {#s-e1f06868cc}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Gonolobus parviflorus* as an accepted species of *Gonolobus*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record contains one habitat label, terrestrial. The source does not specify a collection locality or range and includes no morphology claim; the label should be read only as a regional habitat category.
