## atlas-profile / overview {#s-ec7256b5b1}
Two Carboniferous Myxinikela siroka fossils preserve an elongated, scaleless body and soft-tissue traces. Their revised anatomy combines general cyclostome features with specializations compared with living hagfishes.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-3374e9d644}
FMNH PF15373 and PF8472 support a stem or possible total-group hagfish interpretation. Some structures remain uncertain, and the specimens are outside the crown-Myxini fossil range.

## Two specimens and a preserved body outline {#s-9ae4ab3c9d}
The holotype and paratype, FMNH PF15373 and PF8472, measure about 62.5–73.3 mm in the represented material. They preserve an elongated, scaleless body outline and soft-tissue traces from the Francis Creek Shale.

## A hagfish-like pattern with open questions {#s-4f22b548d6}
The revised description compares the fossils with cyclostomes—lampreys and hagfishes—and identifies specializations resembling hagfish anatomy. The authors place Myxinikela on the stem, or possibly within the total group, of hagfishes. Some features remain equivocal, so this is not a crown-hagfish record.
