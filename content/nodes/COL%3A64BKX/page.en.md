## What traits distinguish Acalypha klotzschii? {#s-39d00fd490}
The record describes an upright woody stem with simple, non-glandular hairs. Leaves have pinnate veins, serrated margins, and no stipels. The axillary inflorescences are bisexual. Female flowers are sessile or nearly so, with a three-part calyx and spreading, ear-shaped bracts; their separate styles are regularly divided into narrow lobes. The fruit is hairy and covered with small blunt projections. Seeds are pitted and lack a caruncle.
