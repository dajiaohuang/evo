## Pauridia alticola {#s-58454bbb3c}
Pauridia alticola bears one flower close to the ground; a long beak on the underground ovary lifts the white, pink-backed tepals above the surface. The regional account distinguishes it from P. verna by its fibrous corm covering and hairy seeds. It records seasonally wet summit plateaus in doleritic soils on the Hantamsberg and Roggeveld, shale bands in the Cederberg, and former clay-loam valley habitat in the Kouebokkeveld, much of which has since become dams and orchards.
