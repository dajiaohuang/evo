## Fagopyrum esculentum {#s-7b0f35045a}
## Triangular leaves and sharply three-angled nuts

Fagopyrum esculentum is an annual herb up to 0.6 m tall. Its triangular, arrow-shaped leaves have heart-shaped bases. The flowers are white or cream. As the nut develops, it becomes pale brown, matte and sharply three-angled. The source lists disturbed places as habitat but gives no locality or survey coverage.
