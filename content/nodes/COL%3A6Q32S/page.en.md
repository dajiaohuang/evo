## Lessingianthus macrocephalus {#s-3c0c09ad18}
## Brown wool on leaves along the stem or in a rosette

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record does not specify the root type. Leaves may grow along the stem or form a rosette; stems are unbranched or sparsely branched. Subleathery elliptic to lance-shaped leaves have pointed tips and bases, scalloped edges and different-coloured faces. The upper face is hairless and the lower brown-woolly; the margins do not roll under.

Stalked heads may be solitary or arranged in corymb-shaped clusters. Their bell-shaped involucres have 5–8 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 50 or 60 florets. Cylindrical fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
