## Stem and leaves {#s-346e0797f0}
The source records a solitary or underground stem with leaf-sheath remnants on its surface. Green pinnate leaves have persistent sheaths arranged helically around the stem, with fibrous margins. The petiole margin is unarmed, and the leaf axis is erect or slightly curved. Leaflets are leathery, grouped at one angle and arranged uniformly; they are rigid with asymmetric pointed tips, and are green or slightly differently coloured.

## Inflorescence and fruit {#s-e73fa7d958}
The inflorescence is an erect, spike-like structure, and the fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves. The grooved spadix and its main axis are hairless; the field records no rachillae. Fruit is elliptic, with a green-yellow woolly outer layer, fibrous succulent middle layer, and woody fusiform or oval inner layer. The endosperm is homogeneous.
