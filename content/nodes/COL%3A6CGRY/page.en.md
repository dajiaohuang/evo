## *Deianira cyathifolia* {#s-e41b2a2271}
## A leaf base clasping the stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description calls the leaves leathery, with a perfoliate base: the stem appears to pass through the leaf. It records no auricles at that base and says the secondary veins are conspicuous. The flower stalks are also conspicuous. The calyx is longer than the corolla tube, but its lobes are shorter than the calyx tube; the corolla is recorded as pink or white. These traits come from a single regional flora field, not a survey of the species’ full range or variation.
