## Tiny woolly heads in seasonal wet places {#s-75b4e9723c}
The account describes a tiny annual that forms dense mats, with prostrate, branching stems and small white-woolly leaves. Flower heads sit in clusters at branch tips, surrounded by wool and leaves; the florets are yellow, sometimes pink-tipped. The habitat note names seasonally damp streambanks, valley bottoms and depressions, while allowing that the plants may persist when these places dry.
