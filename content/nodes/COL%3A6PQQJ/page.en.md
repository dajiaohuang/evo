## Lessingianthus sancti-pauli {#s-8f19fbd78e}
## Brown wool lines the lower face of narrow leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and stems with leaves along their length, unbranched or sparsely branched. Narrow, linear to lance-shaped leaves are leathery to subleathery, with acute tips and bases. The upper face is finely hairy and the underside brown-woolly; their margins are entire and do not roll under.

Heads may be sessile or stalked, solitary, with bell-shaped involucres and 6–7 rows of bracts. The source lists 15, 20 or 25 florets. Top-shaped fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
