## Lithospermum papillosum {#s-3a42607f42}
## White flower clusters rise above close-set leaves

The Greater Cape flora describes a closely leafy perennial up to 25 cm tall. Its pressed-down, lance-shaped leaves have hairs only along the underside midrib. White flowers grow in leafy clusters at stem tips and in leaf axils. Each flower has a ring at the base and five gland-lined pits in the throat. The small dry fruits, or nutlets, are smooth. The habitat record is mountain slopes.
