## Plant and flower description {#s-188bb3f0de}
The SANBI account describes a tufted perennial geophyte 0.80–0.95 m tall, with a corm 30 mm in diameter. The linear, pointed leaves have slightly pronounced veins; the radical leaves measure 450–650 × 4.5–7.0 mm, and there are four reduced sheathing leaves. Flowering stems are 0.80–0.95 m tall. The inflorescence is a pendulous raceme of spikes with 3–5 loose branches. Bracts measure 15–19 × 8–11 mm; they are broadly obovate, pointed, flecked, and have broad white margins, with some flecking along the midline. The pale-mauve perianth is 25–28 mm long, with a 6–9 mm tube and tepals 18–23 × 8–12 mm; anthers are 6.0–8.5 mm long. Flowering is recorded for September and October.

## Comparison with *Dierama dracomontanum* {#s-788101947c}
A separate SANBI account describes *D. jucundum* as a clump-forming species with pendulous spikes and moderately large, widely funnel-shaped pale-lilac flowers. It gives the perianth as 25–28 mm long and the broad tepals as 8–12 mm wide. By comparison, *Dierama dracomontanum* is described as having slightly smaller pink to almost red flowers, with a 19–25 mm perianth and tepals 5–8 mm wide.
