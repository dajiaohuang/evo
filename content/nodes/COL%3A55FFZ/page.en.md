## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a04ab4da82}
COL26.8 records Teretrurus travancoricus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Uropeltidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-8413302f58}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as concealed (Cryptic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f4fe966d1f}
The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015.
