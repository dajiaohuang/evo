## Source classification and concept status {#s-27a378c325}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3124 records *Hyperolius castaneus* under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-91091e3fcf}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-8dd1a5d397}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; daytime activity is unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-a377fc37c9}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 36 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-a8559abe94}
AmphiBIO reports 100–150 offspring or eggs per clutch and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. The row does not report an offspring-size value. These counts are literature-compiled; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-e9450e3cab}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
