## Accepted name and placement {#s-8f78c9e6f2}
COL26.8 records Craugastor aurilegulus (Savage, McCranie & Wilson, 1988) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; they do not establish equivalence with historical source concepts.

## Reading route {#s-13028311a0}
The accepted record provides the current catalogue placement. The biological values below come from AmphiBIO's older Frost 2011 classification and should be read within that source's scope.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-55199c8014}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging categories for its historical Craugastor aurilegulus row. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries, but this row has not been checked against the revision and current-concept equivalence remains unresolved.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-bc42eb0bf5}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 87 mm. For this anuran, body length means snout–vent length. This is a literature-compiled maximum for the historical row, not a range or a value measured from every individual.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-721e954c17}
AmphiBIO records at most one reproductive event per year. Its literature-derived count does not specify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions for each observation; fields for offspring size may refer to eggs.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-5460cf47f7}
The row flags direct development. This source code does not by itself establish parental care, a breeding site or seasonal timing, and its taxonomy has not been reconciled with the current catalogue concept.
