## Changing of the herbivore guild {#s-6787f9803c}
Follow three moments in changing herbivore communities, keeping regional succession and diet separate from evolutionary ancestry.

## Brontotheres diversify through the Eocene {#s-fe471a6f82}
Eocene brontotheres ranged from small early forms to rhinoceros-sized species in North America and Asia. This was an extinct herbivore radiation, not a modern animal community recreated in the past.

## Ice growth and mammal turnover near the boundary {#s-6fc334ce14}
Near the Eocene–Oligocene boundary, steps in marine ice growth can be aligned with the regional Hampshire mammal sequence. But faunal turnover, gaps in the rock record, and immigration are separate observations; their timing alone does not make them one event.

## Miocene feeding niches overlap {#s-ace484851d}
In the Miocene, Anchitherium, hipparionins, and Teleoceras occupied different dietary niches. Evidence varies among lineages and localities, and the change in herbivore guilds unfolded over time rather than in a single replacement.
