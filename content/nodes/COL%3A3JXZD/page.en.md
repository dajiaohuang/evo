## What features distinguish Hebenstretia paarlensis? {#s-4ad290c36c}
Hebenstretia paarlensis is described as a small shrub up to 40 cm tall, with upright branches crowded with narrow leaves. The leaves bend downward and have fine teeth. White flowers with orange markings form elongated spikes. The ellipsoid fruit has an upper segment shaped like a small shield.

## Where does it grow? {#s-6bc558eaa7}
The habitat field places it on rocky sandstone slopes. It does not identify a particular collecting site.
