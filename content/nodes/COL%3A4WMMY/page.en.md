## Senecio promatensis {#s-844eb1ffcc}
## Deeply divided leaves rise above a leafless base

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes stems that are woody at the base and herbaceous toward their tips. They are leafless near the base, with sessile leaves higher up. The oval-lance-shaped blades are deeply divided almost to the midrib and hairless on both surfaces.

Corymb-shaped clusters bear yellow radiate heads with 5–15 involucral bracts. The yellow strap-shaped ray florets are female; the yellow tubular disc florets are bisexual. The dry fruits are cylindrical, ribbed and silky to softly hairy. Their white pappus (bristle crown) persists. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
