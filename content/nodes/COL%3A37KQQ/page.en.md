## A non-fenestrated blade {#s-ff41fc0072}
The flora field records petioles 10–30 cm long and a non-fenestrated, subleathery blade; its front division is lanceolate to oblong, while the rear division reaches 3 cm. The flower-cluster stalk (peduncle) is listed at 12–15 cm and the spathe (bract around the cluster) at 5.5–6.5 cm; flowers have one or two locules (internal chambers). These are regional taxonomic measurements, with no sampling frame stated.

## The listed substrate category {#s-0ee4e49ece}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records “terrícola,” or soil-associated, as the habitat category. The row has no locality or date and cannot be used as a range map.
