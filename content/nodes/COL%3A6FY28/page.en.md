## Larval feeding and adult flower visits {#s-de9320a6c0}
Larvae feed on aphids found on trees, shrubs and herbs, while adults regularly visit flowers. The two life stages therefore have different feeding habits and occupy different roles in food webs.

## What British records show {#s-446352a5de}
British records include individuals arriving from continental Europe as well as adults that overwinter. Counts from Britain can vary from year to year; those local records do not by themselves measure change in the species' worldwide population.

## Scope of these records {#s-83f331c7a0}
These notes describe larval feeding, adult flower visits and the interpretation of British records. Regional record counts alone do not establish a change in the worldwide population.
