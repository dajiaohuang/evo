## The source tadpole concept {#s-321913bd55}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Allobates grillisimilis, in the genus Allobates and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying work is also Allobates grillisimilis. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-46f226fc1a}
The compilation has four records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Their supplied stage labels are 25, 26–28, 35–37 and 38–40. These are labels attached to source records, not a single tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves their reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; four records also do not mean four unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
