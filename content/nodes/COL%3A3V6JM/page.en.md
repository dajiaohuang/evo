## Which record these facts describe {#s-245d986fdb}
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts Liolaemus montanezi in the family Liolaemidae. The traits below come from its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which follows the Reptile Database's 2023 name framework and cites sources by trait. That row describes the compilation, not every population as if it were identical.

## What the length numbers measure {#s-cd389098ae}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 64 mm and a female maximum of 61 mm. SVL measures from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. These compiled maxima are neither typical sizes nor biological ceilings. The two sex-specific values may come from different studies or places; by themselves they do not measure how much males and females differ within one population.

## Microhabitat and time of activity {#s-d8e230c229}
The row labels its microhabitat terrestrial, a ground-associated category, and its activity diurnal, or active by day. It supplies no diet value, prey list, or detailed habitat description. The one-row summary does not show whether microhabitat use or activity varies among places, seasons, or life stages.

## Limits of this account {#s-179689adf5}
This page paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and keeps its 2023 name framework and trait-specific citations. A single row compresses differences in sex, age, locality, and population; the same name and family do not prove that a historical record matches every later taxonomic concept. The reported maximum is not an absolute size limit. ReptTraits also includes mass estimates derived from length equations; they are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions, so they are not used as conclusions. This page does not update conservation status, venom, current distribution, or population trends. The citation labels retain the dataset's source chain; they do not mean this project independently rechecked every original study.
