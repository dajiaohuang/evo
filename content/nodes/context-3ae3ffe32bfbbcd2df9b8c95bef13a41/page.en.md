## Source concept and record identity {#s-f1df218a38}
AmphiBIO v1 names Ceratophrys joazeirensis in row Anf1159, listing genus Ceratophrys, family Ceratophryidae and order Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging-stratum flags: fossorial and terrestrial {#s-4b3a5dabb5}
The row records Fos=1 (fossorial); Ter=1 (terrestrial). These are broad source categories: 1 marks a listed category, 0 is the stored code, and NA remains missing data. The fields do not describe seasonal or developmental changes.

A later TetrapodTraits revision notice (3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) covers selected body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. This row has not been compared at row level, so its original entries remain and no replacement is inferred; the notice does not establish that every original value was wrong.

## Maximum adult body length {#s-497d1ab9ad}
The AmphiBIO row records maximum adult body length 105 mm. For Anura, Body_size_mm is snout–vent length, measured from the snout tip to the vent; the reported length is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental code: larval stage {#s-c5da77edb7}
The row records Lar=1 (larval stage). Here 1 marks a listed source category, 0 is the stored code and NA is missing data. A larval stage is a phase before metamorphosis. These codes do not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
