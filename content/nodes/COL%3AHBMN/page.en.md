## Asparagus transvaalensis {#s-2135c7db49}
Asparagus transvaalensis is a much-branched shrub to 1 m with erect, threadlike cladodes and spines below the nodes and cladode clusters. The regional account distinguishes it from A. krebsianus by its longer upright cladodes and more loosely arranged flowers. It records bushveld, rocky places, hillsides and cliffs as habitat.
