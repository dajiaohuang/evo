The archive’s nonzero food weights are invertebrates 100%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. ## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-bbca422155}
Foraging-stratum weights flagged as species-level in the archive are ground 60%; on or just below the water surface 40%. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.
