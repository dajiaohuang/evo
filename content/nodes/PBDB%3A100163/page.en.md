## Recorded parent of Rhomaleosauridae {#s-122d8af9e5}
Rhomaleosauridae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Plesiosauria as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
