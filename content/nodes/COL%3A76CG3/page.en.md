## Quiet calls from shrubs near flowing water {#s-7035f987db}
The 2016 account described this small frog calling softly from shrubs and understorey trees near running water. The authors also reported observations on ornamental plants around buildings at forest edges and interpreted the species as tolerating moderate disturbance. These are attributed encounter observations, not a measured tolerance threshold, a complete daily calling schedule or evidence that breeding succeeds around every building.

## A disjunct historical concept with a proposed future revision {#s-70181e6257}
The account included Mindanao and Basilan, with Zamboanga as type locality, alongside parts of the Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, Mentawai and Natuna islands. The authors suspected that Philippine populations might eventually be distinguished from populations abroad. That suggestion remains a 2016 taxonomic hypothesis; those geographically separated records are not certified as one contemporary concept. The cited Least Concern category likewise retains its publication context rather than serving as a current official assessment.
