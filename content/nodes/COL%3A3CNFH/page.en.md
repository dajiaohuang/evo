## What form and flower features define Euphorbia crassipes? {#s-413cfafd05}
This spineless, hairless succulent can reach 300 × 300 mm. A stout, upside-down-cone-shaped main stem supports a rosette of ascending cylindrical branches, 8–10 mm thick, with low tubercles and persistent woody peduncles. The small ovate leaves fall early. Numerous cyathia cluster near branch tips, each 6–7 mm across on a sometimes persistent stalk; five brown glands have five to seven finger-like processes each. The sessile capsule is smooth and hairless, 5–6 mm across.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-8312487b75}
The source lists gravelly flats as its habitat.
