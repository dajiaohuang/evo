## Fissidens flaccidus: three habitat entries {#s-7de031ed8c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records three habitat categories for this moss:

“Epixila,”

“Rupícola,”

and “Terrícola.”

These are source labels from three regional dataset rows; none gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
