## *Felicia microsperma*: blue rays and tiny fruits {#s-d6a91528a1}
The regional account describes a roughly hairy annual up to 50 cm tall, with linear leaves and solitary radiate flower heads: blue rays surround yellow disc florets. A comparison passage distinguishes it from *Felicia australis* by a more branched inflorescence, hairier leaves, more numerous bracts around the heads, and exceptionally small fruits. The account records sandy soil near water as habitat.
