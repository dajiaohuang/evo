The life-history fields draw on historical same-name records in the 2015 archive. Summaries are medians or the mean of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. These figures are not measurements of a single individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit values across historical species and subspecies. A matching name and family, together with same-name sparse rows, supports the association here but does not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove inherited components from the summaries. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually.
