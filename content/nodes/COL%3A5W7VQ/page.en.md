## Asterostigma luschnathianum: floral structures and terrestrial habitat {#s-53cff82585}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an oblong-elliptic spathe, with the female zone of the spadix partly attached to it. It records the connective as pink or white and the synandrium as boat-shaped. The staminodes are connate and described as the same length as the ovary. Stigmatic lobes run parallel to the inflorescence axis; they are oblong with entire tips.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
