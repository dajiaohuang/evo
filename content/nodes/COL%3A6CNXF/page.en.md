## Leaf spines and fruit {#s-7d75b6d5d5}
The source records straight leaf spines longer than 1 cm and no spines on the cirri, the tendril-like structures associated with the leaves. The upper surface at the base of each pinna is hairless. In the inflorescence, the main axis is thicker than its smaller branches and is straight. The fruit has an irregular surface with many fibers; its undivided corolla is less than one-quarter the fruit's size, and the endocarp is ellipsoid.

The field records straight leaf spines longer than 1 cm; no spines on the cirri; a hairless upper surface at the pinna base; a straight inflorescence rachis thicker than the rachillae; fruit with an irregular surface and many fibers; a corolla less than one-quarter the fruit's size and not divided; and an ellipsoid endocarp.
