## A giant claw, and the assumptions behind its body-length estimate {#s-be22536e24}
At Willwerath, a fossil of Jaekelopterus rhenaniae preserves 36.4 cm of the fixed branch of a chelicera—the front appendage used for feeding in this group of eurypterids. Its complete length was estimated at 45.5 cm. The much-cited body length of about 2.5 m is a separate extrapolation from proportions in related pterygotids, not a measurement from a complete skeleton; possible differences in claw-to-body proportions add uncertainty.
