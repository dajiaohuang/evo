## Branches and leaves {#s-419bfd70b8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives “5.0/10 m” in its height field and describes the branches as smooth; it does not explain how to interpret the two height values. The leaves are compound, with deciduous stipules and oval, obovate or elliptic leaflets. The leaflet-count text is “7.0 9.0 à 11,” which is too unclear to normalize as a range. Their venation is called brochidodromous: neighboring side veins curve and join near the leaf edge, forming loops.

## Flowers {#s-0e81da6178}
The inflorescences are described as open rather than crowded. Persistent bracteoles (small bracts near a flower) occur at the base of the calyx. The calyx is recorded as both truncate and cup-shaped; the flowers are violet, and the petals are dotted.

## Pod and seeds {#s-044c34a960}
The pod is described as papery to chartaceous (firm and paper-like), with a samaroid (samara-like) form and a winged vexillary, or banner-facing, margin. The source lists constrictions between seeds as both “yes” and “no,” so that feature is unresolved in this record. The seeds are reniform (kidney-shaped).
