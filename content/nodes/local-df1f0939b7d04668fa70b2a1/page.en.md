## Name and classification in the source {#s-1ffdca5643}
Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Anaxyrus houstonensis* under genus *Anaxyrus*, family Bufonidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.
