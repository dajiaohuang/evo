## What root, leaf and flower traits does the regional field record? {#s-f9eff0c718}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records a xylopodium, a woody underground storage organ, and Malpighian hairs (a named hair type) on the stem. Leaves are opposite or whorled, linear, acute at the base and rolled under along their edges. The flower clusters may be leafy or bracted; the stalks are recorded in two size groups, 0.1–6 mm or 7–20 mm, and small bracteoles are present. The floral tube is 3–10 mm long, with long or closely pressed stiff hairs outside; its blunt spur points horizontally. Petals are recorded as white, lilac or pink, inserted around the middle third of the tube; they are absent by the fruit stage. A nectar gland at the ovary base bends downward, no vesicle is recorded, the fruit is tubular and the seed margin blunt. These are alternatives in one regional database row, not measurements from a single specimen.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-93780608ea}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies this plant as terrestrial, without a collection locality or survey date.
