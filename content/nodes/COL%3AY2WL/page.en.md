## ## Grouped trunks and a white seed covering {#s-0d84c7d451}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two or more trunks growing together. The leaves have 3–5 pairs of leathery, hairless, elliptic leaflets; distal leaflets are larger than proximal ones, and the tips are blunt or notched. Loose panicles are about as long as the adjacent leaf. The sepals and ovary are glabrous. The slightly sickle-shaped pod is 2–3.5 cm long, and the seed bears a white aril, a fleshy covering. This is a regional character record, not a population survey.
