## *Sida cabraliana* {#s-d68f59fa94}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, linear, saw-toothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem); flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
