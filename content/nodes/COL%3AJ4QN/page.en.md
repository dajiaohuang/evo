## Astronium ulei {#s-bd1aa1dcda}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 account records leaflet alternatives, floral details and a fusiform fruit; its separate ecology field says terrestrial.

## Leaflet alternatives and fusiform fruit {#s-a38b49f3c7}
The morphology field describes lenticellate bark and lateral leaflets that are ovate, obovate or elliptic, with entire or sinuate margins and conspicuous tertiary veins. It records no pistillode and subapical ovule placentation. Sepals on pistillate flowers are glabrous or ciliate and 1–2.9 mm long. The fruit is fusiform; the fruit-related sepal field gives 0.8–4.5 cm and describes the sepals as subequal to the fruit. The separate ecology category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial); it gives no locality or complete distribution.
