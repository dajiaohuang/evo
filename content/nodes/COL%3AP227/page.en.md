## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima correifolia? {#s-e563c03c80}
The lower leaf surface is woolly-haired. Flower clusters bear one or two flowers on stalkless pedicels. The petals may be white or pink, and the anthers have silky hairs. The fruit is hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f5c6dff505}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
