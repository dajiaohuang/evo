## Three yellow tepals and a puzzling placement {#s-6d3fc31132}
Moraea cooperi is recognised by yellow, finely veined flowers with only three tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals), joined at the base into a short tube. The source describes its relationships as puzzling: morphology and chromosome count suggest one placement, while available plastid DNA (DNA from cell structures such as chloroplasts) analyses place it apart from the sampled relatives.
