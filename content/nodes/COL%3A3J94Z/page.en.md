## The 55 mm figure leaves out the tail {#s-9afd05ecf3}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 55 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent, excluding the tail. This compiled maximum is not a typical body size or an absolute growth limit.

## Three habitat categories and nocturnal activity {#s-7396dc6f1c}
The entry lists arboreal, saxicolous (rock-associated) and terrestrial microhabitats, alongside a carnivorous diet and nocturnal activity. The combined habitat categories indicate more than one recorded setting; they do not define a complete habitat range or a fixed routine. “Carnivorous” does not identify prey, and the row cannot show how use varies by locality, season or life stage.
