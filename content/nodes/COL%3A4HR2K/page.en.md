## Evidence limits {#s-ad1a0ac497}
AVONET v7 measurements are sample averages rather than identification diagnoses or individual ranges; the 13.29 g mass is separately sourced and is not based on those morphological sample counts. Habitat, feeding and migration are broad categories, and the latitude endpoints describe historical breeding and resident maps. The 2015 life-history archive reports compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. These summaries are not one individual's measurements, ranges of variation or mean wild lifespans. The historical compilation may inherit data across species or subspecies; matching names and family do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited material. Wild versus captive conditions are not uniformly known, and the underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. EltonTraits gives 100% invertebrates for food and equal understory and mid/high stratum weights; these are standardized literature summaries, not direct measurements of intake or time use. Ref_7 is a citation locator, not independent confirmation. Current range, population and conservation require other sources.
