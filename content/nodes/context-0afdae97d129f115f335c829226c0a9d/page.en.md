## Source classification and concept status {#s-aa32f2e325}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3048 records *Megaelosia bocainensis* under the source classification Megaelosia (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-9260b47551}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-96aa47c0a3}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; night-time activity is unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-13baeed39c}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 66.7 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
