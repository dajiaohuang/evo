## AVONET sample and taxonomic label {#s-53f8d55ed3}
COL26.8 accepts Egretta ardesiaca in Ardeidae. AVONET v7 reports measurements under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name: 5 individuals were measured, and 4 had a complete morphological set. The usable sample size can differ by trait.

## Beak dimensions at stated landmarks {#s-e2e6b6b01b}
Mean beak length from the anterior edge of the nostril to the tip is 57.6 mm; width is 8.4 mm and depth at that same landmark is 10.3 mm. These are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and compiled mass {#s-0623206375}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 245.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 80.4 mm. The compiled mean mass is 324 g, in source category Dunning. Mass is a separate species-average record, so the measurement sample does not imply that every individual was weighed.

## Three broad ecology categories {#s-6d55bcfe6b}
AVONET classifies habitat as Wetland, trophic niche as Aquatic predator and primary lifestyle as Terrestrial. These are broad categories for comparison across species, not claims of exclusive habitat or food use.

## Migration and mapped latitude {#s-9397992117}
The migration field classifies the record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -31.66° to 17.8°. These endpoints are not a current-location or global-occupancy estimate and do not describe all non-breeding movements. The geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## EltonTraits diet and foraging fields {#s-d0b6181a56}
EltonTraits 1.0 links a historical record for Egretta ardesiaca under the same name and family, with two field groups for diet and foraging position. The historical classification does not settle later splits or synonymies.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-57b2f0cbfd}
EltonTraits assigns relative food weights of 80% to fish and 20% to invertebrates. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These weights standardize verbal descriptions; they are not direct measurements of diet, and a zero category would not prove that a food is never eaten.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-70e466bb9f}
The species-level foraging entry assigns 100% of its weight to the water surface or just below it. This estimates relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, complete habitat use or a whole-day time budget. It does not provide water depth, flight height or habitat area.

## Trace each field group to its source labels {#s-bb288f762e}
Both EltonTraits field groups carry the Ref_1 label. It links the diet and foraging summaries to their recorded source trail; the label is not a separate observation, and the underlying work was not individually read for this page.

## Evidence limits {#s-8801a6c428}
AVONET measurements are species averages for the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from separate cited literature or weighing records, so the morphological sample count does not apply to it. Ecology fields are broad categories, and maps cover historical breeding and resident ranges. The name links and historical crosswalks associate records but do not prove equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps.
EltonTraits standardizes descriptions into broad food categories and relative weights, not direct measures of intake, energy or observation time. Code A records compiler confidence, not direct observation. The name match does not establish equivalence with the current species concept. Local and seasonal variation, current distribution, populations and conservation are not described by these historical entries.
