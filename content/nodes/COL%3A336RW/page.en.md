## Nearly stalkless yellow flower heads {#s-43f0b3d4e6}
The flora description records a twiggy shrublet up to 60 cm tall, with narrow leaves that often have three to five lobes and hooked tips. Yellow flower heads with white rays sit on short terminal stalks and often nod. The source notes substantial variation in leaf shape and says the heads appear nearly stalkless; it records clay slopes and renosterveld as habitats.
