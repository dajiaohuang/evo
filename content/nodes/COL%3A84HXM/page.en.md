## Description samples {#s-8e89b11fef}
The 2020 formal description linked this species to historical specimens and newly sampled material from central Myanmar's dry zone. Its holotype is an adult male skin and skull collected on Mount Popa in September 1913; the study sequenced its mitochondrial genome and compared its morphology with *T. phayrei* and the paper's *T. melamera*. The authors placed the proposed range between the Ayeyarwaddy and Thanlwin rivers but left the northeastern boundary unresolved. The specimens support the description; population figures in the paper are not a current census.
