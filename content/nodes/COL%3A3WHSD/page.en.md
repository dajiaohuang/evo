## How to read these measurements {#s-0000de86d3}
COL26.8 accepts Lybius guifsobalito in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife name from 2020 and reports six measured birds, four with a complete morphological set. Trait means may draw on fewer individuals than that total.

## Beak dimensions at standard landmarks {#s-52d3bf7c83}
Mean length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 15.6 mm; width and depth at the nostril edge average 9.5 and 11.5 mm. The three values describe distinct axes at standard landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, leg and mass records {#s-fa281ac244}
The unflattened wing, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, averages 86.3 mm; the tarsus averages 20.8 mm. A separate compilation reports mean mass of 42.2 g (source category Dunning), which does not imply these morphology specimens were weighed.

## Woodland, fruit and perching {#s-674afa987d}
AVONET places the record in the Woodland habitat class, identifies its trophic niche as Frugivore and its lifestyle as Insessorial, meaning primarily perched above ground. These are broad dominant categories, not an exclusive habitat or diet.

## Movement and historical mapped extent {#s-ea3269c356}
The migration field labels the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps run from 2.77° south to 16.65° north. They do not represent all non-breeding movement or current occupancy, and the map and morphology were assembled separately.

## Limits of this account {#s-f0e317c24a}
The measurements are means from historical AVONET samples, not diagnostic limits or estimates of individual variation. Mass has separate provenance and must not inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges. The name crosswalk links this record with the current catalogue name but does not prove unchanged species concepts after taxonomic splits or lumps. Reproduction, present range, populations and conservation require other sources.
