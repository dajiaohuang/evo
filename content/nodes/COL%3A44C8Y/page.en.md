## One trailing terete leaf {#s-77207c5988}
Moraea monticola grows to 40 cm and is distinguished by its single, curved to trailing leaf with a terete (round in cross-section) blade. Blue or white flowers have undivided, threadlike style branches that extend between the anthers.

## Rocky lower slopes {#s-abfb24904d}
The source records rocky lower slopes as habitat.
