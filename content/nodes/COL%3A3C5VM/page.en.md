## *Eugenia pittieri* {#s-bed2642110}
The cited English description characterizes the plant as a slender shrub 1–4.5 m tall, with densely fine-haired young twigs. Leaves are nearly sessile, oblong to oblanceolate, 4–6 × 1–2 cm, thin-textured and densely gland-dotted; small flowers occur in few-flowered clusters in leaf axils or along branches. The berry is red, nearly spherical, 5–7 mm long, and retains the sepals. This summary follows that account's described material.

The source reports the species from low elevations in Panama and Costa Rica. This is the account's stated range, not a claim of complete occurrence coverage.
