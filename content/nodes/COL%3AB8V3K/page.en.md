## What the *Micrina* fossils preserve {#s-69822667fa}
Brachiopods, often called lamp shells, are represented here by a broad history from the Early Cambrian to living species. The older anchor is a reconstruction of the stem brachiopod *Micrina*. Its shell-bearing parts are fossil observations; the animal’s bivalved form and its place on the brachiopod stem are comparative interpretations.

Early Cambrian *Micrina* material includes mitral and sellate sclerites with muscle scars. These structures are observed in the fossils. Their arrangement supports a bivalved stem-brachiopod reconstruction, but the full-body reconstruction and proposed valve homologies are hypotheses based on comparison with other animals.

## A sampled range, not a crown-group date {#s-c63bb09d20}
The node displays an approximate 520–0 Ma span, from the Early Cambrian sample of *Micrina* to living brachiopods. The older bound is rounded from a sampled interval and does not date the origin of the group. In particular, it is not a first appearance for crown brachiopods.
