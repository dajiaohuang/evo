## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-aa9c3a4b8e}
The structured field records an underground bulb and no rhizome. Leaves are strap-shaped with blunt tips. The inflorescence bears two to four flowers with a funnel-shaped perianth. Because the field uses slash-separated entries, its colour and shape values are best read as listed options rather than as one description applying all at once.

Tepal-apex colour options include white, white to cream, greenish white, red tessellation, and dark-pink tessellation. Base entries include yellowish white, pale green to yellowish, vinaceous, or diffuse colouring at the base. The hypanthium tube is categorized as long, with lengths listed from 6.5 cm to greater than 12 cm. Sepals are slightly wider than petals; sepal-shape entries include obovate, narrowly obovate, and broadly elliptic. Lower lateral sepals are described as little different from the upper sepal. Petals are narrowly elliptic or narrowly obovate, with the lower petals slightly smaller than the upper ones.

The field records no corona and fasciculate filaments shorter than the sepals and petals. Anthers are yellow or cream. The stigma is capitate and trilobed, with triangular lobes. The capsule is brown inside; seeds are numerous, flattened, and disc-shaped.
