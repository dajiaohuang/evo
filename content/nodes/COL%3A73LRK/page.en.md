## Mimosa rondoniana {#s-9130b72b44}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa rondoniana* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes silky stem hairs, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and projections between pairs of leaf divisions. Its flower clusters are ellipsoid, and the petal lobes are hairy; the entry labels it terrestrial.
