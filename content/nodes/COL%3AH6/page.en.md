## A Paskov wing anchors the insect route {#s-5d0a7d1163}
The Insecta root uses a 324–0 Ma sampled navigation envelope. Its older anchor is a lowermost Namurian wing part and counterpart from Paskov, whose preserved venation is attributed to Archaeorthoptera; living insects provide the present endpoint. This replaces the unsupported 410 Ma value, but it does not date the origin of insects or flight.
