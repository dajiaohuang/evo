## What marks Lippia pedunculosa? {#s-ae127527b1}
The record describes smooth stems and opposite, stalked, lance-shaped leaves. The upper leaf surface bears stiff hairs, while the lower has glands and stiff hairs. Flower heads may be rounded or cylindrical, with leathery bracts arranged in a spiral. The bisexual flowers are pink to lilac and have salver-shaped corollas.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-405d15b229}
The dataset records it as terrestrial, without identifying a particular site.
