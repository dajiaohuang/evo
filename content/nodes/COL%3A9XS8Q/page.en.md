## Bredemeyera bracteata {#s-e3aeac0d63}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Bredemeyera bracteata* as an accepted species of *Bredemeyera*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes velvety branches and petioles, with the lower leaf surface also velvety; the upper surface is mostly smooth apart from hairs on the midrib. Its flowers are carried in panicles, and the fruit is a two-valved capsule. The record gives a regional morphological description but no habitat claim.
