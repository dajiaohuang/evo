## Cylindrophyllum tugwelliae {#s-67500ad188}
## Old glaucous leaves persist between the new ones

The Extra Cape flora describes a dense cushion-shaped succulent shrub up to 250 mm tall and 600 mm across. Its cylindrical leaves taper to a point and are 50–80 mm long, up to 10 mm wide and thick. They are glaucous, with older leaves turning blackish green and remaining between new leaves.

Solitary flowers have white to salmon or peach-coloured petals and numerous filament-like sterile stamens and fertile stamens. The five-chambered fruit has broad valve wings and small closing bodies. The habitat record is flats with fine-grained soils.
