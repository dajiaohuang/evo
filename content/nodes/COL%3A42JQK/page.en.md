## Metrodorea nigra {#s-7342ea05d3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records several leaf-tip and leaflet-count alternatives, long inflorescences and cream-to-purple petals.

## Variable leaves and long inflorescences {#s-95d4e5bdab}
Young branchlets may be hairy, become less hairy or glabrous. Leaf tips are listed as acuminate, obtuse, retuse or emarginate; blades measure 5–25 cm. Stipules are partly free from the petioles, and the lower blade surface may be glabrous or sparsely pubescent at the base. The leaflet field records one, rarely two or three, and also separately lists three; petioles are 4–60 mm. Inflorescences measure 15–35 cm. Petals are cream, vinaceous or purple, the ovary is larger than the disc, and fruit apophyses are present. The ecology field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
