## Six DNA regions test the living carnivoran tree {#s-f9dc20867e}
Researchers combined six nuclear and mitochondrial DNA regions—TR-i-I, TBG, IRBP, ND2, CYTB and 12S rRNA—from 76 living carnivoran species into a 6,243-base-pair analysis. The main tree strongly separated sampled Feliformia and Caniformia, the cat-like and dog-like branches. It describes the living species and models in this study; it does not establish fossil relationships, first appearances or divergence dates, and finer branching depends on the sample and analyses.
