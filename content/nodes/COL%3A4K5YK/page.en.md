## Distribution and habitat {#s-d31451b051}
A Brazilian ICMBio species assessment records this titi in Mato Grosso and Pará, in the Xingu–Iriri interfluve and forests east of the Teles Pires River. Described habitats include humid forest, riparian forest and the Amazon–Cerrado transition; these regional summaries are not a continuous, gap-free field survey.

## Feeding research {#s-2721865a4f}
The assessment lists only one diet study, of a group in an urban forest fragment; the animals were observed eating fruit, flowers, leaves and invertebrates. This documents observed food types, not species-wide dietary proportions or seasonal change.

## Evidence limits {#s-d256d62346}
