## AmphiBIO species record {#s-91bb8f4049}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Odorrana graminea? Row Anf4933 places it in genus Odorrana, family Ranidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-5fe8f188db}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 100 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.

## Developmental category in the record {#s-84709797c6}
The AmphiBIO record assigns this source concept the developmental category “a larval stage”. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
