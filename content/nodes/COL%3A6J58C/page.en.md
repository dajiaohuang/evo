## Fissidens adianthoides: three recorded habitat labels {#s-a86cd427e4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists this moss under three habitat categories:

“Corticícola,”

“Epífita,”

and “Rupícola.”

These are categories from three regional dataset rows, not a mapped range; the fields give no collection locality, survey date or sampling completeness.
