## Forearm length in three adults {#milleri-forearm-sample}
The 2025 genus-wide study included 299 adult specimens from ten Molossus species, including three M. milleri. Their mean forearm length was 36.76 mm, which the authors placed in their small-size band. This is one quantitative measure from an adult sample, not a complete morphological description or an estimate of sex, age, or geographic variation.

## A low-support mitochondrial placement {#milleri-mitochondrial-placement}
In the study tree inferred from mitochondrial COI and cytochrome b, M. milleri was recovered as sister to M. verrilli, but branch support was low. The authors estimated the divergence in the Pliocene. This is one sampled mitochondrial tree, not a population-level history or a synthesis of nuclear and mitochondrial evidence.
