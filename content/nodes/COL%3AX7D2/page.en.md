## A wood-associated habitat category {#s-7f3b3f7711}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Cololejeunea paucifolia (Spruce) Bernecker & Pócs as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with one source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Epixila,” recorded as a wood-associated substrate category.

It is not accompanied by a collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

This single category is the only biological content included from the regional record; it is not a full species account.
