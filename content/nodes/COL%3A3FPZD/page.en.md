AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 8553 and eBird 2021 row 4314 share the name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-45C55FDE; BirdTree row 4295 gives Zoothera gurneyi, with match type 1BL to 1BT. ## Measured birds and beak/wing values {#s-25f0b037ba}
Morphology covers 14 birds: 4 female, 5 male and 5 unsexed; 9 birds have complete measurement sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 13.3 mm, tarsus 33.9 mm, unflattened wing 108 mm and hand-wing index 24.1 are summaries in this historical row. Inference=NO does not establish equal counts across fields; complete-set birds do not supply every field’s denominator. The index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed, migration distance or a range-wide performance test.

## Food categories and feeding observations {#s-18351ed1ff}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Invertivore and Trophic.Level=Carnivore. Invertivore primarily uses terrestrial invertebrate resources and has a different definition from the separate Carnivore/Omnivore trophic-level field; neither replaces the other. These are compiled categories, whose thresholds are not locally measured seasonal diet percentages. Prey species, individual feeding observations, digestion and resource availability are absent here, leaving detailed prey lists and seasonal proportions unsupported.

## Lifestyle, vegetation density and migration {#s-44813067c9}
Primary.Lifestyle=Terrestrial: most time is spent on the ground, with walking or hopping to obtain food; this does not imply inability to fly. Habitat=Forest and density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per square metre; these fields retain separate meanings. Migration=2 is partial migration, which can include a minority making long-distance movements or most birds making short-distance, nomadic or distinct altitudinal movements; this row does not distinguish these alternatives. Historical map summaries are not interpreted, wing index is not used to verify the category, and these labels do not establish current range-wide behaviour or occupancy.
