## A scattered range with unresolved border populations {#s-169bc86926}
The 2019 review synthesized scattered records mainly in the Middle East at 0–2000 m, including the Levant, parts of Turkey, Greek islands Limnos, Lesbos and Kos, the southern Caucasus and southern Caspian shores in Iran. It said central Anatolian distribution was poorly delineated. Some southeastern Bulgarian and European Turkish populations might instead belong to P. balcanicus, pending molecular identification. Suggested loss from western Jordan was uncertain. Those qualifications are retained; neither the disputed records nor possible local loss are asserted as resolved.
