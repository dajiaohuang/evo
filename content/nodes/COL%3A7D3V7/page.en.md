## A tufted marsh perennial {#s-ab2923778e}
The account describes a tufted, rhizomatous perennial 15–90 cm tall, thinly fibrous at the base. Its terete leaves are 1–2 mm in diameter. Dense racemes carry 10–100 green flowers. The fruits are often secund, narrowly ovoid, and 5–9 mm long.

Tufted, rhizomatous perennial, 15–90 cm, thinly fibrous at base. Leaves terete, 1–2 mm diam. Flowers 10–100 in dense racemes, green. Fruits often secund, narrowly ovoid, 5–9 mm long.
