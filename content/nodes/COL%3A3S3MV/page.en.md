## Lampranthus borealis {#s-8ceef6ce64}
## Blunt, three-angled leaves and large pale-rose flowers

Klak’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes an erect, smooth-skinned succulent shrub with stems 90–180 mm long. Its leaves are separated near the base, blunt at the tip and three-angled in cross-section; they grow to 25 mm long and are 3–5 mm thick.

The flowers number 1–3 and are pale rose, 40–50 mm across. They lack thread-like staminodes, or sterile stamens. The fruit has five chambers, a funnel-shaped base and diverging keels. The habitat account places the plant on rocky gneiss slopes.
