## *Restio similis*: paired or solitary spikelets {#s-325795be54}
This tufted perennial can reach 60 cm. Its stems are slender and either unbranched or sparsely branched; the leaf sheaths end in awns up to 5 mm long. Spikelets occur singly or in pairs.

The account records it on well-drained slopes up to 1,500 m.
