## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-8f201a51b9}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Physalaemus fernandezae (Müller, 1926) as a species in the genus Physalaemus, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.
