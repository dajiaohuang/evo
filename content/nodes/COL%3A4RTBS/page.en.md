## Restio brachiatus {#s-fa9599478f}
## The female spikelet carries several flowers

Restio brachiatus is a dioecious, tufted perennial that can reach 2 m. The regional account places female spikelets in a branched raceme and notes bracts with translucent margins; it distinguishes the species from C. cymosus by the 3–5 flowers in each female spikelet. Its habitat is seasonally damp slopes above 1,000 m.
