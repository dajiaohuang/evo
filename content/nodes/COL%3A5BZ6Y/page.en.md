## Willdenowia humilis {#s-1883fb9a04}
## Female spikelets pressed against the stalk

The regional account describes Willdenowia humilis as a tufted or rhizomatous perennial reaching 1 m, with simple or sparsely branched stems. Its female spikelets lie tightly against the flower stalk. The nut is greenish, cylindrical, and covered by pointed surface lobes. Seasonally damp sands are the recorded habitat.
