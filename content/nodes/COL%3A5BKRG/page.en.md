## What traits are known for Vismia lateriflora, and which remain unknown? {#s-c48f96b4f2}
The stems exude orange sap, and the terminal branches bear hairs. Leaves are stalked, oval or elliptic, with bases ranging from rounded to heart-shaped and tips pointed or tapering. Small black glands dot the lower surface. Axillary panicles bear flowers whose sexual form—homostylous or heterostylous—is not known. Each flower has five sepals and five petals with linear, glandular markings. The stamen bundles persist; each has many stamens, and their column bears sparse hairs. Each ovary chamber has many ovules but no glands. The ovoid fruit lacks the black dots or lines, and the sepals point upward around it.
