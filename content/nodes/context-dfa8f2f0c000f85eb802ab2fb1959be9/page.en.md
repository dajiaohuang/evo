## Source classification and concept status {#s-214822b47b}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3028 records *Hylodes dactylocinus* under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-579866ec32}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and aquatic as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime activity {#s-cc4db5e36d}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Seasonal activity (dry and warm) {#s-082107957d}
The row carries the seasonal category dry and warm. It relates activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-ead21e820d}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Clutch size {#s-e0f108fef8}
The row lists 40–60 offspring or eggs per clutch. These are literature-compiled values; the record does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions. No annual reproductive-output or offspring-or-egg-size value is listed.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-631d1de252}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
