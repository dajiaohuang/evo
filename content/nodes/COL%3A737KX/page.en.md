## What leaf, flower-cluster and fruit traits distinguish Mascagnia divaricata? {#s-50ff7d79e2}
The flat, papery ovate leaves have rounded bases, pointed tips and lower surfaces that become hairless or have sparse silky hairs. The inflorescence is a thyrse: its main flowering branch bears one to many side clusters. The flowering peduncle is at most 1 mm long, with bracteoles at its tip. Petals are lilac or pink, the style tip is hooked, and the fruit has developed dorsal and lateral wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-bd8bfce6ca}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
