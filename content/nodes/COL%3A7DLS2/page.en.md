## What is recorded about Unonopsis stipitata? {#s-63f55bc09e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves, papery or leathery, with pointed or blunt bases and tapering tips. Flowers grow on branches or directly from the trunk. The fruit is ellipsoid.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-b3cf7d996a}
The dataset records it as terrestrial, with no specific locality.
