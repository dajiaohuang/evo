## PanTHERIA retains its historical bat names {#s-4c52a13518}
The PanTHERIA route preserves MSW05 names and variable-specific conditions for entries such as *Balantiopteryx io*, *B. plicata*, *Balionycteris maculata*, *Chaerephon ansorgei* and *C. major*. COL26.8 supplies current navigation; the archive’s historical entries do not constitute a complete modern species list.

## Compare body size and life history {#s-3ddd4e660e}
Among available consolidated adult-mass values in this batch, Craseonycteris thonglongyai records 1.96 g and Pteropus neohibernicus records 1017.37 g. These are endpoints of historical medians adjusted for sex. Continue to fetal development, weaning, activity and group fields after reading their definitions; body-size differences alone do not establish a shared lifestyle for all descendants.

## Life-history fields have different coverage and meanings {#s-116e120078}
The life-history archive includes entries for *Balantiopteryx plicata*, *Coleura afra*, *Emballonura monticola*, *Rhynchonycteris naso* and *Saccopteryx bilineata*. Adult mass, offspring per litter or clutch, gestation and the age when young begin using non-maternal food are recorded as separate fields. Their units, events and source conditions need to be checked individually.

## Foraging archives standardize different fields {#s-4134de6119}
The EltonTraits route includes entries such as *Acerodon celebensis*, *A. humilis*, *A. jubatus*, *A. leucotis* and *Aethalops aequalis*. Food weights translate literature descriptions into coarse categories; foraging strata and activity periods use separate codes. These summaries do not describe one ecology shared by all bats.

## Some horseshoe-bat entries retain activity only {#s-3a532430c3}
For *Rhinolophus maendeleo*, *R. ziama*, *R. siamensis*, *R. madurensis* and *R. montanus*, this EltonTraits selection records activity periods but has no food-weight or foraging-stratum values. The distinction is about which fields the archive contains, not evidence that these bats lack those behaviors.

## Keep the four archive routes separate {#s-bc80a97616}
The PanTHERIA selections use historical name links and different focal-field rules; the life-history route has its own historical taxonomy and summaries; EltonTraits standardizes diet, foraging and activity fields. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. Read each species page with its own definitions and source limits.

## An early bat occurrence is not a crown origin {#early-fossil}
The Chiroptera route is anchored by the Green River holotype of *Onychonycteris* (ROM 55351A) together with living bats. The specimen documents an early bat occurrence; it does not date crown-bat origin, establish a direct ancestor or provide a global first-appearance datum.
