## What flower and capsule structures are described? {#s-703b5d3773}
The main axis is erect, and the leaves are pinnatifid, with deep divisions along each blade. The flower sheath (spathella) is hairless. Flowers are radially symmetrical, with triangular tepals reaching the base of the ovary. Five to eight stamens are arranged laterally; no gynophore, the stalk-like structure below the ovary, is recorded. The capsule has ribs, with three running from base to tip on each valve.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-1d3bfde878}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as aquatic and gives no locality.
