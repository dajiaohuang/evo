## A single dense spike {#s-b02fb91152}
The southern African account describes submerged, spindle-shaped leaves 5–10 cm long and 3.5–4.5 mm wide, tapering narrowly at both ends. A threadlike scape may reach 2 m and bears one very short, densely flowered spike, about 5 mm long and 25 mm across, recalling a buttercup flower. Lower flowers are sterile; upper flowers have one or no perianth segments. Three follicles bear two or three seeds each; seeds are 1.5–1.75 mm long.

## Pools in Cave Sandstone {#s-a03e9c0ed2}
The cited account places the plant in pools in Cave Sandstone formations, at depths up to 7 m and about 2,600 m elevation. It describes the species as vulnerable. That category is the account’s statement, not an independently verified current assessment in this record.

## Evidence limits {#s-ec0af20989}
This regional flora account is cited to Cook (2004); it provides no dated sampling frame or population frequency. The vulnerable label is reported as stated in that account.
