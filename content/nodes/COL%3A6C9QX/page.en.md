## Cyrtopodium gigas {#s-91c4fc3c92}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes a green fusiform pseudobulb longer than 20 cm, with leaves still small or developing at flowering and shed when mature. Its panicle bears yellow sepals marked with red; the side lobes of the lip are red with yellow at the base. Habitat records include epiphytic and rupicolous categories.
