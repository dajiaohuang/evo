## Morphology and field descriptions {#s-3e1cede3a8}
The paper described Grewcock's sportive lemur as medium-sized, with a type-series mass of about 0.78 kg and mainly gray fur. The mandible and upper snout are whitish-pink; a dark head stripe may continue onto the back but varies among individuals. Its entirely gray tail contrasts with the consistently white-tipped tail described for L. edwardsi.

## Known localities and evidence limits {#s-ee99095ad9}
The paper then knew the species from the Anjiamangirana region, south of the Mahajamba River and north of the Maevarano and Sofia rivers; the southern limit still needed survey. Sampling in this one region does not establish current range-wide occupancy or abundance.
