## What stem and flower traits identify Euphorbia mauritanica? {#s-670215333f}
This monoecious, much-branched succulent shrub reaches 2 m. Its bright-green, round stems are 3–6 mm thick. The sessile, lance-shaped leaves are 8–15 × 3–5 mm and fall seasonally. Flower clusters usually form at branch tips: a nearly stalkless male cyathium is surrounded by five to seven bisexual cyathia, together spanning 7–15 mm. Their yellow glands have entire margins.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-80db89f5f8}
The source lists flats and stony slopes.
