## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3c8bcbe788}
COL26.8 records Cryptoblepharus keiensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-7126c9297c}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 42.7 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d5646508bd}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
