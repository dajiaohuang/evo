## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima putumayensis? {#s-b8365c6cb5}
The lower leaf surface has silky hairs. Each cluster has one flower; its stalk may be stalked or sessile. Petals are yellow, and the anthers are hairy. Fruit hairs were not observed, so the record does not establish whether the fruit is hairy.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-685c604965}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
