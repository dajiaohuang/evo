## Aplacophora is retained as a historical grouping {#s-c44ecb5027}
Phylogenomic analyses test relationships among sampled solenogasters and caudofoveates, but Aplacophora is retained here as a historical navigation grouping rather than treated as one globally bounded natural clade. The former 485 Ma–present display is withdrawn because the living sample does not define a coherent temporal range for this grouping; schema zeroes are placeholders.
