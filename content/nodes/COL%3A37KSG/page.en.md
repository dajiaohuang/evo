## Dracontium spruceanum {#s-975ed3440a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description lists a tuber and leaf blades that may be three-part, arrow-shaped, or lobed. The flower stalk is 30–250 cm; the spathe (bract) exceeds 20 cm, while most of the 2–6 cm spadix (flower-bearing axis) remains hidden inside it. The flower field gives 4 or 5 tepals (undifferentiated perianth segments) and 6 or more stamens; berries range from red to yellowish. The habitat field records soil.
