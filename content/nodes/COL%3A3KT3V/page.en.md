## Accepted name and placement {#s-d4ff997011}
COL26.8 accepts Hemisus guineensis Cope, 1865 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Hemisotidae (family) → Hemisus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Barcode-supported identification retains provisional boundaries {#s-e423f569bc}
The Congo records occupy a COI BIN/ABGD group and16S clade distinct from the Gabon perreti group. The authors apply guineensis using a published morphological description and note unresolved boundaries around marmoratus/sudanensis. The identification is their integrative working assignment, not a new type-based taxonomic revision independently verified here.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-eff30f67c6}
For Hemisus guineensis, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
