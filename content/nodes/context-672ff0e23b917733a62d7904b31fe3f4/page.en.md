## The source concept {#s-040b5d674d}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Proceratophrys rondonae* as row Anf1406, in the genus Proceratophrys, family Cycloramphidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-959b69a648}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 62.9 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
