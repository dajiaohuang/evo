## A woolly rosette beneath a crowded flower spike {#s-69d56e8fc8}
This record describes an upright plant with woolly, hollow stems and a basal rosette of sessile, leathery leaves. The leaves are folded lengthwise; the upper surface is silky or softly hairy, while the lower surface is woolly. Crowded heads form a spike on long stalks, with woolly bracts and a flat receptacle. A head may contain up to 55 florets. The fruits are slightly grooved and bear outer flattened pappus scales and inner bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
