COL26.8 accepts Brachycephalus coloratus Ribeiro, Blackburn, Stanley, Pie & Bornschein, 2017 as a species. ## Nine type adults with sex-specific size summaries {#s-a1e2d68486}
The 2017 description reported five adult males with snout–vent lengths of 10.3–10.6 mm, mean ± standard deviation 10.4 ± 0.1 mm, and four females at 12.2–13.3 mm, mean ± standard deviation 12.9 ± 0.5 mm. Male holotype MHNCI 10273 measured 10.3 mm. The paper’s broader adult diagnostic interval of 10.3–13.3 mm combines these sex samples; it does not establish universal size limits or a population-level estimate of sexual dimorphism.

## Three main live colours change after preservation {#s-015d6c08e1}
The live holotype had a pale-red dorsum from head to pelvic region, yellowish-green body sides and dorsal thighs, yellow chin and ventral limbs, and a black iris. In preservative the reddish region became caramel and the greenish regions became dark brown. The diagnosis combined live coloration with a smooth dorsum and absence of osteoderms. These are the description’s observed diagnostic traits, not interchangeable live and preserved colours or a stand-alone identification test for every population.

## Type-locality leaf litter and neighbouring nondetections {#s-b01d5a25d4}
The male holotype came from Estância Hidroclimática Recreio da Serra in Serra da Baitaca, Piraquara, Paraná, Brazil, at 1144 m. The account knew the species from that locality, on the ground beneath leaf litter in high-elevation forest with montane elements below about 1180 m. Visits to neighbouring Pão de Ló, 4.96 km away, in October 2015 and January 2016 found no evidence of the species. Those nondetections belong to the reported visits and do not establish permanent absence or a complete present-day range.

## Road-development concern and a dated assessment proposal {#s-6aa4eb3706}
The authors raised concern about a planned road in the type-locality development, while reporting a resident’s statement that land above 1000 m would not be occupied. A frog record 130 m from the Serra da Baitaca State Park boundary motivated a recommendation to search the park. They proposed Data Deficient status because available information was limited. These are the 2017 paper’s development information and assessment proposal, not a verified current road plan, a confirmed park occurrence or an official current IUCN listing.

## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-4381d7feb6}
Reddish central dorsum contrasts with greener sides and mixed yellow-green ventral colour; preserved material loses this live contrast. These are the authors’ comparative observations rather than a standardized colour-chart measurement. Colour and specimen counts vary by trait; similar colours recur among species, so a single hue is not a complete identification rule. Preserved appearance should not be substituted for live appearance.
