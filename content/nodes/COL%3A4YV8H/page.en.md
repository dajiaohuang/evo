## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-11dad429d1}
The authors group S. mendiculus with the FAL gentoo lineage and E. chrysocome: their model curves decline across roughly the last 250,000 years. A shared pattern does not establish identical local conditions or numbers of birds. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
