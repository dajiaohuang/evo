## How is this species described in comparison with Lachnaea grandiflora? {#s-d91c1c3f83}
The cited regional account compares this species with L. grandiflora: plants reach 45 cm, may have one or several stems from the base, and have flexuous (bending back and forth) branches and narrowly elliptic leaves. Flowers are pink, sometimes white. Their silky outer surface has blunt rather than needle-like hairs on the lower floral cup (hypanthium). The stigma, the pollen-receiving tip of the flower, is narrowly cone-shaped to ellipsoid and bears long papillae, tiny surface projections.

## Where does the regional account place it? {#s-78791a0264}
The 2012 regional account records sandy flats and rocky sandstone slopes from 15 to 860 m elevation. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
