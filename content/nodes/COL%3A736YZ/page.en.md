## Matelea palustris {#s-a4184f8c69}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Matelea palustris* as an accepted species of *Matelea*. The only ecological information in Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 is the habitat category “terrestrial.” It provides no collection site or complete range and includes no morphology description.
