## Othonna quercifolia {#s-50b5bdf811}
## Brittle stems rise from a fleshy base

Manning and Goldblatt describe a succulent shrublet up to 40 cm tall, with brittle stems arising from a fleshy caudex (swollen stem base). Oblanceolate leaves range from toothed to lobed and are woolly in the axils.

Yellow radiate flower heads grow singly or a few together in loose corymbs on long stalks. The pappus (bristle crown on the fruit) of ray florets lengthens markedly as the fruit develops. The account records sandstone and granite outcrops as habitat.
