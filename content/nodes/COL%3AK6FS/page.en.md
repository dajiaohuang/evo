## Babiana rivulicola {#s-d7ea970fa7}
Babiana rivulicola grows in dense clumps, with a stem up to 300 mm tall and long, relatively soft linear leaves. Its pale-blue to blue-mauve flowers have a 42–44 mm funnel-shaped tube. The regional account distinguishes it from the similar Namaqualand species B. dregei by the taller stem, softer leaves and shorter, funnel-shaped tube. It records the plants in cracks in granite bedrock along streams. These are source-scoped diagnostic and habitat observations.
