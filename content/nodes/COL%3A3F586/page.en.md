## Galipea revoluta {#s-102822ccd4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records one, sometimes two or three, leaflets, partial inflorescences along the rachis and a 10–14 mm fruit.

## Variable leaflet number and a long corolla tube {#s-9146eb126f}
Leaves bear one leaflet, sometimes two or three; blades are elliptic or obovate, and terminal leaflets are larger than lateral ones. The leaflet tip may be emarginate, acute or without an acumen. Domatia are absent from the lower surface, while the upper surface is glossy and its veins raised. Partial inflorescences occur along the rachis and are no longer than the leaves. The calyx is slightly ribbed or flat, with conspicuous lobes and sparse hairs outside. The corolla tube exceeds 10 mm; anthers are joined and have basal appendages, while the ovary is glabrous. Fruit length is recorded as 10–14 mm. The ecology field gives “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or full range.
