## Recorded parent of Rhinocerotinae {#s-280650a25d}
Rhinocerotinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
