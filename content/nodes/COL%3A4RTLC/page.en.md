## How does its single spikelet sit among tangled stems? {#s-000f852ac8}
A regional account describes *Restio scaber* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants), stoloniferous perennial reaching 40 cm. Its tangled culms (stems) are sparsely branched and covered with coarse bumps; the account notes a solitary spikelet (a small flower cluster). The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## At what elevations are stream seepages listed? {#s-2e78099839}
The regional account lists seepage areas along streams at 500–800 m. It does not provide a survey date or a complete range.
