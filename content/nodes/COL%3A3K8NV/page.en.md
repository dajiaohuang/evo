## *Heliconia farinosa*: an upright flower cluster with boat-shaped bracts {#s-92da766f89}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description records a rhizome and petioled, elliptic to oblong leaves with pointed or tapering tips. Its upright flower cluster carries persistent, boat-shaped bracts arranged in two ranks. The bracts may be smooth or hairy, and the perianth—the floral envelope around the reproductive parts—is recorded as glabrous or hairy.

The fruit is a rounded or angular drupe, a fleshy fruit with a hard inner layer. The associated habitat field labels the plant terrestrial but gives no named locality or date. These are regional character descriptions; the source does not quantify variation across populations.
