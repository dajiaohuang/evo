## ## Leathery leaves and a flower that splits open {#s-2f8b54762d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes unarmed branches and entire, elliptical leaves with a leathery to somewhat leathery texture. Their tips taper to a point; the upper surface may be nearly hairless or hairless, while the lower surface carries short, appressed hairs. The tubular flower opens with its calyx splitting into two to five lobes, and the row records ten anthers. Its legume is described as opening elastically. This is a regional morphology record, not a complete account of variation.
