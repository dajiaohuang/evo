## Isoetes organensis {#s-34fcddadd1}
## Reticulate megaspores and basal leaf wings

Its source profile records forked roots and a two-lobed stem described as globose or short and vertically elongated. The upright or ascending linear leaves have a circular to semicircular cross-section near the tip; their wings occur only at the base. A partial velum covers the basal sporangia, which are described as hyaline or light brown. The megaspores are trilete and reticulate on both surfaces; the microspores are monolete, with a finely spiny distal outer layer.
