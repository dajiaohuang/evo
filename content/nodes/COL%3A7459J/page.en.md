## Myrsine rubra M.F.Freitas & Kin.-Gouv. {#s-14d002b06c}
## Elliptic leaves and oblong drupes

Myrsine rubra has slender, smooth terminal branches and alternate, papery leaves spaced along the stems. The blades are ovate to elliptic, with acute to acuminate tips and acute bases; short lines of resin cavities occur beneath. Umbel-like inflorescences bear five-parted flowers. The fruit is an elliptic drupe on a short stalk.
