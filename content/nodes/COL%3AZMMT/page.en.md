## The name and this data account {#s-50e7eaa8c2}

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-a5ba776e24}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 32.6 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 17.9 mm in width and 16.3 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-eb60e15c81}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 282 mm; mean tarsus length is 83.3 mm. Compiled mean mass is 2075.25 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Evidence limits {#s-67c982719d}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
