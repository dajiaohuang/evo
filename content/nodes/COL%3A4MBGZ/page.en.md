## Axillary cymes with five nectaries and apically joined follicles {#s-9f0081ab1b}
The source description records inconspicuous intrapetiolar colleters on a glabrous stem. Leaves lack blade colleters and are coriaceous to subcoriaceous and glabrous. Axillary monochasial cymes have glabrescent to pubescent indumentum and inconspicuous scarious bracts. The floral throat ring is evident; anthers are included, the corolla and sepals glabrous, corolla lobes present, throat lobes with exserted tips, and the nectary divided into five. Follicles are recorded as free but joined at the apex or free and spreading apart. Seed shape and indument are unknown. The habitat field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial). This single morphology field does not establish variation or full diagnostic scope; no locality or range evidence was assessed.
