## A molecular estimate sets the older navigation bound {#s-fc5dc3a81c}
A placental analysis of 16,397 base pairs recovered Afrotheria in its sampled topology and estimated a split at about 103 Ma under the study’s models. The atlas rounds this to a 100 Ma navigation ceiling and carries the route to the present through living descendants. The estimate is model context, not a fossil occurrence, an exact crown-group origin, or evidence that the ancestor lived in Africa.
