## Afrotheria’s older limit comes from a molecular model {#s-fc5dc3a81c}
A 16,397-base-pair placental matrix recovers Afrotheria in its sampled topology and estimates a split at about 103 Ma under the study’s models. The atlas’s rounded 100 Ma navigation ceiling is model context, not a fossil occurrence, exact crown origin or evidence for an African ancestral location; living descendants extend the route to the present.
