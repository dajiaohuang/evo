## Ledothamnus guyanensis Meisn. {#s-aee8f86e33}
## Overlapping leaves and variable pink to red flowers

Ledothamnus guyanensis has upright, slender shoots and small, papery leaves that may overlap along the stem or occur in whorls of four. The source lists several possible leaf shapes and hair patterns, with a short gland-tipped bristle at the leaf tip. Axillary flowers grow near the shoot tips. Their corollas are described as bell-shaped and pink or red, while the capsules may be globose or ovoid. The record lists rock-dwelling habitat. Several hair and shape alternatives remain unresolved in the source fields.
