## Aldama grandiflora {#s-67ef41a8c1}
## One firm shoot aboveground and awns free from the pappus scales

Flora do Brasil 2020 records an underground stem that produces one firm aerial branch; root thickening is unknown. The leaves are alternate and sessile, ranging from elliptic or lance-shaped to oblong or obovate, without a terminal point. Radiate flower heads have involucral bracts with acute to long-tapering tips; receptacle paleae also taper to points. The ray corolla forms one whorl, tubular florets have minute stiff hairs, and disc florets are glandular.

The narrowly obovoid dry fruits bear bristles. Their pappus awns are separate from the scales and lack a keel.
