## How a broad reed frog concept was split {#s-adcc89d67a}
The 2022 revision tested the broad Afrixalus laevis concept using 1,554 base pairs of 16S and RAG1 from 34 A. laevis samples and one sympatric A. orophilus, together with previously sequenced GenBank samples. It combined phylogenetic and species-delimitation analyses with measurements of 11 characters. The authors concluded that Albertine Rift and nearby forest populations represented two new species, A. lacustris and A. phantasma, distinct from western Central African A. laevis and sister to A. dorsimaculatus. The abstract does not give species-specific diagnostic measurements or localities.
