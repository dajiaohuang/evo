## What visible traits are recorded? {#s-a7fcb8ccc6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes four-angled stems and opposite leaves ranging from elliptic to round, with scalloped edges. Leaves may be stalkless or stalked, and both surfaces carry hairs. The terminal spikes have hairy, fringed bracts and a four-toothed, hairy calyx. Blue, salver-shaped flowers are followed by dry fruits that split into two one-seeded segments.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-aa27155a30}
The dataset records it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
