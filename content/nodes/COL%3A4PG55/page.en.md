## Psiguria ternata (M.Roem.) C.Jeffrey {#s-770a793334}
## Three leathery leaflets and pink, symmetrical flowers

Psiguria ternata has compound leaves with three leathery leaflets. The source describes its flowering arrangement as pyramidal and lists the plant as either monoecious or dioecious, without resolving which applies. Its pink flowers are stalked and bilaterally symmetrical.
