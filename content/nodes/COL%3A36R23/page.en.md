## Calyx and fruit {#s-d6d2fe2c19}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records 6–14 leaflets. The calyx is membranous and pink, with wart-like markings; its size is given as 15–20, but the field states no unit. The fruit is ovoid and has a fleshy wall. This is one regional row, not a complete or global description.

A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only; no locality, range or sampling coverage is supplied.
