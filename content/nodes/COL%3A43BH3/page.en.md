## Mikania paniculata {#s-26050f6d73}
## Smooth, three-veined leaves and clustered heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, smooth branches; stem pith was not observed. Opposite, ovate leaves have entire margins, rounded bases, short tapered tips and smooth surfaces. Three main veins radiate from the base; the leathery blades have no interpetiolar stipules.

The panicle has spike-like branches and stalkless or nearly stalkless heads. Short, linear bracts occur below the heads; the oblong bracts around them are smooth and blunt-tipped. The tubular corolla has triangular, tapered lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional account gives no locality or complete range.
