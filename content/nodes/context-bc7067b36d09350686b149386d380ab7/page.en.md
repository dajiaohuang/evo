## Source classification and concept status {#s-7e78ff8862}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3016 records *Crossodactylus dantei* under the source classification Crossodactylus (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-98f329334a}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime activity {#s-028a3335db}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-026ad0d34f}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 30 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-12fe5730ae}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
