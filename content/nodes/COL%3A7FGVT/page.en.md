## A leafless climber with long capsules {#s-d3a75359f8}
The regional description portrays V. roscheri as a climbing plant with a fleshy, cylindrical stem up to 20 mm across. Its leaves are reduced to small brown, membranous remnants. White flowers have a pink throat, and the hanging capsules can reach 175 mm long.

## Coastal scrub and mangroves {#s-67e993b72f}
The same account records coastal bushland, mangrove swamps and open evergreen scrub as habitats. It does not tie those settings to particular collection localities.
