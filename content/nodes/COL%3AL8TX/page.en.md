## Begonia wasshauseniana {#s-ca32dd31ac}
## Scaly hairs beneath the ovate leaves

Begonia wasshauseniana has erect stems and deciduous stipules. Its transversely ovate basal leaves are slightly asymmetric, with entire margins; the upper surface is smooth and the underside bears scale-shaped hairs. Similar scales occur on the petiole and both male and female flower segments. Female margins are entire and bracteoles are absent. Capsule wings are nearly equal, and seed tips are rounded.
