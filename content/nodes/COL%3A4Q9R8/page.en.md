## Workshop vocabulary and later species naming {#s-05a9430cb4}
The underlying workshop used an opportunity sample of 13 volunteer native isiZulu speakers, aged18–55, from five Zululand areas: ten participants on28November and three on1December 2016 at Tembe Elephant Park. The six broad names elicited during the workshop did not individually distinguish all58 species. Species-level names were assigned afterwards with Bongani Mkhize, combining this vocabulary with earlier published names and naming guidelines. ## A species score for a specified conservation framework {#s-8fb579711f}
Table A4 reports DBS 4, TBS 0, SBS 4 and total SI 8 for Ptychadena oxyrhynchus. DBS weights the number of represented Rwanda ecozones and national endemism; TBS uses national red-list proposals reported by earlier research, rather than a fresh global IUCN assessment. Its score 0 corresponds to LC in Table 1. SBS 4 corresponds to undisturbed habitats only. These are categorical inputs to this Rwanda index, not experimentally measured physiological tolerances, demonstrated responses to every land use or a newly assigned current threat status. A combined VU/DD score does not distinguish those two categories by itself.

## Species Index and site index have different meanings {#s-6ac260535f}
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI 8 for Ptychadena oxyrhynchus is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS 1/2/4/8, TBS 0/1/2/4/8 and SBS 0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
