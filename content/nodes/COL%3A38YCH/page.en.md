## Elaeodendron transvaalense {#s-e5c24aae3f}
## Three-part flowers in open woodland

Elaeodendron transvaalense is described as a shrub or bushy tree up to 10 m tall. Its alternate or clustered leaves are narrowly elliptic to oblong, with entire or toothed margins. Small inflorescences bear cream-colored flowers with parts in threes; the fruit is a white drupe. The recorded habitat is open woodland.
