## Pink flowers on a leafy cactus {#s-ac34df9f6d}
The Portuguese record describes straight spines at the stem areoles and leaves with blunt or notched tips. Leaf shapes range from obdeltate and oval to obovate; lateral veins meet the midrib at 40–60 degrees. Three to seven flowers form a corymb. The flowers have no bristles on the receptacle areoles or staminodes, and the pink perianth is wheel-shaped. The turbinate fruit contains more than five seeds. Terrestrial habitat is recorded.
