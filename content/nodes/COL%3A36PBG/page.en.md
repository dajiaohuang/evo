## What leaves and flowers are described? {#s-0d4366f9cc}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes hairy leaves that do not blacken when dry. They are sessile (without petioles), more than 5 mm wide, heart-shaped at the base and flat-edged. Several main veins run from the base toward the tip; at least two lateral veins occur on each side, with no small hair tufts in their axils. A small bract below the flower (prophyll) reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 7–47 mm long. The extra sepal-like lobes outside the calyx (epicalyx segments) are 2–11 mm long, equal to or shorter than the sepals, and erect. The corolla is 3.5–7 cm across, with 12 stamens. These are regional database states, not a population survey.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-4a7ef73d04}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without locality or collection date.
