## Cream flowers with purple markings {#s-2db062e797}
This account describes a shrublet 30–60 cm tall, with sessile, hairy, linear-lanceolate leaves. Cream-colored, wheel-shaped flowers with purple markings form dense, head-like clusters. The outer surface of the flowers is smooth; triangular staminal scales have side lobes, and the stamens extend beyond the corolla. Shale slopes and flats are recorded as habitat.
