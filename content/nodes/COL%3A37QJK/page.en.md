## Three to five flowers in compact clusters {#s-2ac87b2611}
The record describes an erect shrublet up to 15 cm tall, with young stems that spread at right angles and are covered with papillae. The rounded leaves are blunt-tipped and papillate. Three to five flowers form a cyme; after the side flowers fall, their stalks remain as short spines. The fruits have five chambers. Dwyka tillite is listed as habitat.
