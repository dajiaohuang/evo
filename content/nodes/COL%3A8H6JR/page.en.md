## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0fc90ddd7e}
COL26.8 records Phymaturus loboi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Liolaemidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-83a4810f35}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-51799f521d}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
