## Phlegmariurus wilsonii {#s-7b0b2d580d}
## Hanging stems and reddish leaf bases

Its stems are recorded as hanging, upright or arching, with the basal stem colour listed as reddish or green to brown. The small leaves have a consistent form, from linear-lanceolate to needle-like or awl-shaped, and attach by spreading or running down the stem. Their short bases are red to reddish. Spore-bearing leaves resemble the vegetative leaves; the kidney-shaped sporangia have two equal valves. The spores are pitted or grooved. The habitat field labels the species rock-dwelling.
