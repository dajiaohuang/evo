## Helanthium bolivianum {#s-97d375b294}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes adventitious roots and a cylindrical rhizome (an underground stem) with stolons. It distinguishes emergent, petioled leaves with lanceolate to oblong-elliptic blades and 3–7 veins from submerged, linear leaves. The umbel-shaped inflorescence has 1–2 whorls; the flower field records 9 stamens and 12–18 carpels. A separate habitat field labels the plant aquatic. These are regional source fields, not a full range or population survey.
