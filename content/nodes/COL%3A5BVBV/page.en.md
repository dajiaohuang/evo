## Early flowers on a sometimes leafless stem {#s-3fbb45f3e5}
Watsonia mtamvunae grows 0.30–0.45 m tall. Its narrow leaves, 3–4 mm wide, are often dry and broken by flowering; the small pale-pink flowers have tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 14–16 mm long. Flowering is reported from August to October.

## Well-drained grassy or stony slopes {#s-46d4ae22bb}
The species is reported from well-drained grassy or stony slopes around the Mtamvuna River Gorge, within the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt.
