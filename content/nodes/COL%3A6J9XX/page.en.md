## A bark-associated habitat category {#s-6a30f94d9e}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Floribundaria flaccida (Mitt.) Broth. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. This page summarizes a source-labelled habitat field.

The field uses “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling. It records a substrate category rather than a collection location.

The field provides no locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

This single category is the biological information carried by the regional record; it is not a complete account of the moss.
