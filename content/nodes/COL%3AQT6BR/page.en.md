## Accepted name and placement {#s-257ce7e864}
COL26.8 accepts Occidozyga shiwandashanensis Chen, Peng, Liu, Huang, Liao & Mo, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Occidozyga (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## How to read this profile {#s-3bbf584fbe}
COL26.8 supplies the accepted name and placement above. The study-based sections below summarize a Vietnamese distribution record and field observations; they are limited to the material reported in that study.

## A Vietnamese record beyond the Chinese type locality {#s-6183ac170c}
A study extended the reported record of Occidozyga shiwandashanensis to Bac Giang, Vietnam, about 180 km from its type locality in Guangxi. The Vietnamese specimens came from 300–400 m elevation; the account also cited earlier Chinese records at 550–650 m. These are separate documented localities, not evidence of continuous occupancy between them, and the sampled elevations do not define physiological limits. The study's species assignment has not been independently reconciled with COL26.8.

## Ponds, streams and ground at night {#s-ff5a08f66e}
During the study, specimens were encountered from 19:00 to 23:00 on the ground, at small ponds and in small streams amid mixed secondary evergreen forest. Examined females contained yellowish-cream eggs with dark poles. These observations describe the sampled adults and eggs; they do not identify an egg-laying site or establish a full reproductive cycle. The study sample is limited, and its species assignment has not been independently reconciled with COL26.8.
