## Name and this data account {#s-1bdc1f5124}
Cercosaura hypnoides is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Gymnophthalmidae. This account uses its ReptTraits v1-2 record, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and trait-specific sources. The dataset consolidates information into one row per species; this describes the cited record rather than a uniform state of all populations.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-fb301e4c00}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 36.8 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent and differs from total length including the tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Where and how it is active {#s-c8c259a656}
In the entry, microhabitat is Terrestrial; active timing is Diurnal. These categories summarize activity and resource use rather than an exhaustive habitat list or fixed schedule. Positions such as arboreal and terrestrial can be combined. A carnivorous category does not specify prey, and populations, seasons and life stages may differ.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-8aff4f5121}
The ReptTraits entry labels the reproductive mode as oviparous and gives a consolidated mean of 2 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch. The summary may use the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint between reported extrema; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed litters or clutches. The listed low and high values are endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed smallest or largest observed litters or clutches. These reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or a uniform pattern across populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-6873cdcd58}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
