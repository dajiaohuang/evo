## Accepted name and placement {#s-64711737af}

## Comparative material does not supply every trait {#s-8e48970b5b}
The study combines focal-toad field observations, preserved adults and larvae, osteology of selected specimens, earlier literature and mitochondrial sequence comparisons. Only material explicitly annotated as cleared-and-stained or radiographed carries that particular examination label; an unannotated voucher is not inferred to have undergone it. Generic contrasts in the paper cannot automatically become species-specific measurements for every Bufoides kempi specimen. Historical genus size, distribution and trait summaries belong to the2016 literature context. No present-day population survey, full species diagnosis or new conservation assessment of Bufoides kempi is established by its inclusion in this comparison.
