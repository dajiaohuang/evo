## Large crowded leaves on a tuberous plant {#s-7b5349d1d9}
The description records a short-stemmed perennial with a turnip-shaped tuber and large, crowded, stalkless leaves. The leaves are oval to elliptic and may have tiny sparse teeth or coarser, jagged lobes. Yellow radiate flower heads form loose, branched clusters. Their hairy, ribbed fruits bear a pappus. The habitat is recorded as stony slopes.
