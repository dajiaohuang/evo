## How are the flattened stems, flowers and seeds described? {#s-7cbb8a8296}
The record describes flattened cladodes—flattened stem structures—and few leaves; when leaves are present, they are alternate and narrowly lance-shaped. Flowers occur in small unisexual clusters or singly. Male flowers have six cream sepals, a segmented nectar disc and three stamens joined into a column. Female flowers also have six cream sepals, with an unsegmented, saucer-shaped nectar disc. This description supplies no fruit or seed characters.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-85d7a7aca1}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
