## Ruschia phylicoides {#s-a8d141fa28}
## Persistent old leaves and dull-white flowers

Buys’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a shrub about 15 cm tall. Its small, smooth, three-sided leaves measure 5–10 by 1.5 mm, and old leaves remain on the plant.

Clusters carry three to five flowers with dull-white petals only 0.75 mm wide. Thread-like sterile stamens (staminodes) are present, with white filaments. The five-chambered fruit has a funnel-shaped base and a semi-globose top. Its covering membranes are convex and the closing ledge is distinct; there is no closing body, and the expanding keels touch at the base. The habitat account records coastal sand in fynbos.
