## A species score for a specified conservation framework {#s-87d113f6bd}
The study’s Species Index (SI) for *P. acutirostris* is 10: distribution score 4, threat score 2 and habitat score 4. SI is an input to this Rwanda prioritization framework, not an extinction probability.

## Species Index and site index have different meanings {#s-72b9f9c98c}
Here SI adds the three component scores for one species. The site index RABI instead adds the SI values of all recorded species at a site. Thus this species’ SI of 10 is not a site’s RABI. The authors’ doubled category weights are choices in the index design, not measured doubling of risk or sensitivity.

## Sampling and transfer limits {#s-ecb8122074}
The 51 sites were unevenly spread across five ecozones, and the species-accumulation curve had not levelled off, so the inventory remained incomplete.
