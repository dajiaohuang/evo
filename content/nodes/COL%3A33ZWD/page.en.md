## What distinguishes Dalechampia hastata in this record? {#s-f85dbc1459}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium. It has simple, lance-shaped leaves, but no lobed or compound leaves are recorded. The surrounding bracts are linear-lanceolate. Resin glands are absent; female flowers have lance-shaped sepals and a cylindrical tip to the style column. The fruit has silky hairs, and the seeds are smooth or spherical.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-57a29cfad0}
The source record lists it as terrestrial, with no named locality.
