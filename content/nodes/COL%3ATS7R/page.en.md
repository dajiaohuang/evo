## A bark-associated source field {#s-d499bb4113}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Cheilolejeunea xanthocarpa (Lehm. & Lindenb.) Malombe as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is a regional entry with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

It does not provide a collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

The habitat category is the only biological information included from this regional entry; it is not a complete account of the species.
