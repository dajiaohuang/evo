## *Hydrocotyle sibthorpioides*: creeping stems and kidney-shaped leaves {#s-04e2d16846}
The cited South African regional account describes a delicate creeping herb that roots at its stem nodes. Its threadlike stems are whitish to brownish. Kidney-shaped leaves sit on 3–40 mm stalks, singly or in groups of two or three; each blade has a narrow notch at the base and five to seven rounded lobes with triangular teeth. The upper surface is hairless, while the lower surface usually has scattered curled hairs, especially on young leaves.

A compact, head-like flower cluster is shorter than the leaf above it and bears three to ten flowers on a 2–9 mm stalk. The broadly ellipsoid fruit is about 1.5 × 1.0 mm, flattened side to side, deeply notched at the tip and strongly ribbed. The account marks habitat as uncertain, gives 100–2,600 m, and names Drakensberg Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Grassland and Sub-Escarpment Savanna.
