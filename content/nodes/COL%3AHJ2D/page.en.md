## Lance-shaped leaves and paired flower heads {#s-478ebdf620}
The morphology record describes a hairy, woody-stemmed plant with stalked leaves arranged opposite one another in two ranks. The lance-shaped leaves are papery rather than leathery. Radiate flower heads occur singly or in pairs; the yellow ray florets are sterile and the central tubular florets bisexual and fertile. The fruit is recorded without an awn-like pappus.
