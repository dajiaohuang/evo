## The source concept {#s-90ca497e10}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Cycloramphus cedrensis as source row Anf1428, under genus Cycloramphus, family Cycloramphidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

## Foraging strata {#s-87c3c78c5f}
The source row marks terrestrial (on land) and aquatic (in water) as broad foraging settings. These labels do not pinpoint feeding sites or show seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat (small-scale habitat) entries carried forward from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so the listed categories remain historical AmphiBIO values; no replacements are inferred. The notice does not mean every original entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-1cfa953299}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 50 mm. For Anura, the compilation measures body length as snout–vent length (from snout to vent); it uses total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a maximum compiled from literature, not a measurement for every individual.
