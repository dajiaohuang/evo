## *Renealmia acreana*: pink bracts on a prostrate flowering stalk {#s-56bae8801e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character record describes striated leaf sheaths and a terminal flower cluster carried on a branch without leaves. Its flowering stalk lies prostrate. Each thin, membranous bract is red or pink and bears one flower; the calyx is tubular.

The mature capsule is ellipsoid. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial but supplies no collection locality or survey date. These characters come from a regional record, not a range-wide description.
