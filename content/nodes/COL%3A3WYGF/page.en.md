## Branches and leaflets {#s-b1712bfeea}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists branches as either unarmed or armed (spiny), with cylindrical cross-sections; the stipules are leaf-like. Leaflets are elliptic or ovate. Their tips are listed as acuminate, acute, obtuse or retuse, and their bases as acute, rounded or attenuate. The source calls the venation “compound brochidodromous”; neighboring side veins link near the leaf edge to form loops. The upper surface is glabrous. The lower surface is described as glabrescent, hairy, or hairy only along the primary veins.

## Inflorescences {#s-4d4d17c4ca}
The inflorescences are listed as racemes, panicles or fascicles (compact flower clusters), positioned in leaf axils or at shoot ends. Their bracts are leaf-like.

## Flowers {#s-60855cf6a4}
The calyx is bell-shaped. Petals are listed as white or greenish. Hairs are present on the standard, or banner petal, and the stamens are monadelphous, united into one bundle.

## Fruit {#s-8c9fee7ea3}
The fruit is described as a samara, or winged dry fruit. Its seed-bearing core is listed as smooth.

## Habitat category {#s-bcce68f930}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
