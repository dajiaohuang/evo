## Arrow-shaped leaves and separate flower whorls {#s-febc0a3e16}
The flora description records a cylindrical rhizome (a stem that grows sideways) and emergent leaves whose blades include sagittate, or arrow-shaped, forms. In the flower cluster (inflorescence), lower whorls (rings around its axis) bear female flowers and upper whorls male flowers; the petals are white. The fruit is described as a cluster of flattened, winged achenes (small dry fruits). These are regional taxonomic descriptors, with no sampling frame for population variation.

## An aquatic habitat category {#s-b8a9016623}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 also classifies the habitat as “aquática” (aquatic). That row gives no locality or survey date, so it does not identify where the plant was recorded or define its range.
