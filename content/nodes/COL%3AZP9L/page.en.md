## Crotalaria vitellina {#s-b53755ca4b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Crotalaria vitellina* records plant up to 1.5 m tall, erect or supported growth, three-part leaves, yellow-orange flowers, pubescent brown pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
