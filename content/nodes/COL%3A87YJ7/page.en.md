## Begonia fluminensis {#s-0dfe1b5b93}
## A creeping stem carries ciliate, heart-shaped leaves

Begonia fluminensis has prostrate stems and persistent stipules. The petioles and lower leaf surfaces bear star-shaped hairs; the upper surfaces are smooth. The slightly asymmetric blades are heart-shaped or transversely ovate, with ciliate margins. Male and female flower segments are hairless; the female margins are entire. Bracteoles were not observed. Capsule wings are unequal, and the seed tip is blunt or rounded. The habitat label is rocky.
