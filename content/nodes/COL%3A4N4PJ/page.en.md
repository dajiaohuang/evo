## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-50d85fc247}
COL26.8 records Protobothrops kaulbacki as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5367ecde94}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records AMB (ambush or sit-and-wait foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2007.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-ab0aae0974}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 19. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993.
