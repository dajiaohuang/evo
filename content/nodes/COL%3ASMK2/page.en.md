## What details mark its nuts and sheaths? {#s-140c8b39fa}
A 2012 regional account describes *Ceratocaryum caespitosum* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1.2 m. The leaf sheaths have a hair-fringed upper edge. Its smooth nuts are 7–9 mm long, lack an elaiosome, a fleshy seed appendage, and bear a warty cap. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## At what elevations are sandstone slopes listed? {#s-f6c7a8c112}
The regional account lists sandstone slopes from 100 to 1,000 m elevation. It does not provide a dated survey frame or a complete range.
