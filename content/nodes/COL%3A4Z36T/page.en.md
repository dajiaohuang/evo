## Linear leaves and spotted flowers {#s-87c30c9340}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes glabrous stems with internodes longer than the leaf blades; the stems are cylindrical or grooved. Leaves are sessile or nearly sessile, linear, hairless, and single-veined, with attenuate bases and acute tips. Long peduncles carry more than four flowers. The tubular corolla is white with pink or purple spots; the ovary and sepals are hairless, and the stamens project beyond the corolla. The fruit is hairless, and the seeds are subglobose.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-986096bec8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Spigelia pulchella* as terrestrial but does not name a collection locality.
