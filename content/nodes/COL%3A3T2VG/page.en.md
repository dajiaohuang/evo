## Persistent old leaves and a large flowering cluster {#s-7c467c2c69}
This succulent shrub may be erect or decumbent. Its finger-shaped leaves are fused at the base into a sheath, exceed 70 mm long, and remain on the plant after drying. The source lists a cyme measuring 76–127 mm and flowers up to 35–50 mm across; the 12-chambered fruit has large closing bodies. Coastal plain is the recorded habitat.
