## A bristly vine with three-veined leaves {#s-f3ca8a6e41}
The record describes a twining, bristly vine with opposite ovate leaves and three main veins from the base. The leaves may have smooth or finely toothed margins; their upper surface is bristly and the underside has stiff hairs. Loose panicles carry flower heads, and the dry fruits are sparsely hairy.
