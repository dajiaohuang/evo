## Phiambolia similis {#s-4f24e63021}
## A more delicate relative with small leaves and few flowers

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora compares P. similis with P. mentiens and describes it as more delicate overall. Its leaves measure 5–14 by 3–5 mm, and one to three flowers are recorded.

The habitat account places the plant on sandstone rocks at elevations of 850–950 m.
