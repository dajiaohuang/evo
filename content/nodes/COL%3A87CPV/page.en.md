## A curated route through trait records {#s-8925b2517b}
COL26.8 places Viperinae within Viperidae. This ReptTraits v1-2 route links 65 species records matched by name and family, including *Atheris acuminata*, *A. anisolepis*, *A. barbouri*, *A. broadleyi* and *A. ceratophora*. The records retain the workbook's Reptile Database 2023 name framework while navigation follows COL26.8. The 65 linked records are not a count of all accepted species in the subfamily.

## Read the definition before comparing {#s-f24cee271a}
Among the linked size records, *Bitis schneideri* has a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 198.7 mm and *Montivipera xanthina* one of 942 mm. These compiled values belong to different species; they are not individual variation within one species or the actual smallest and largest individuals across Viperinae. Activity, feeding and reproductive fields require their own definitions and sources before comparison.

## Choose comparison pages by life-history fields {#s-3229fce675}
The separate 2015 life-history archive has 30 Viperinae accounts, including *Bitis arietans*, *B. gabonica*, *B. rhinoceros*, *Causus rhombeatus* and *Cerastes cerastes*. The archive reports a longest-lifespan record in 30 accounts, a litter-or-clutch value in 28, adult body mass in 26, and oviposition-to-hatching time in 14. These are counts of archive entries, not measured animals or a complete species denominator; the fields refer to different events and units.

## Evidence limits {#s-57b6178f6d}
This route covers selected records, not every descendant of Viperinae. The 2015 entries can carry historical data between species or subspecies, and their summaries are not individual measurements. Some records were retained even when a source-field scope caution reduced the number of traits that could be interpreted. A group page does not imply that all descendants share a reproductive mode or life history. These records do not provide a complete account of Viperinae or its current conservation status.

## Citation gaps in some descendant accounts {#s-1ec4a405f8}
Two species accounts carry a citation-scope caution for a field. Those values are not used as definite trait conclusions; the species pages retain the exact fields and citation labels. Counts on this route describe linked records, not complete trait coverage.

## Choose comparisons by available fields {#s-a321e71bdc}
Each species page interprets only its own cited fields. Compare size, activity, diet or reproductive values only when both pages support the same measure or category. A missing value is not supplied by a related species, and a turtle's straight carapace length (SCL) is not interchangeable with snout–vent length (SVL).

## Limits of these trait data {#s-7f2b62ec98}
The linked species pages are a selection with at least one sourced focal trait and a COL name-and-family match; they are not an exhaustive descendant list. Maximum lengths are compiled records, not individual averages or identification thresholds. Historical one-row summaries do not resolve every taxonomic revision or population difference, so comparison stays within each field's source and definition.
