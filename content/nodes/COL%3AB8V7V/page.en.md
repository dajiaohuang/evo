## A Paleozoic clock estimate differs from the skeletal record {#s-0105cc6126}
A four-locus relaxed-clock analysis places sampled deep scleractinian splits in the Paleozoic, substantially before the known skeletal record. This is a model-dependent posterior estimate, not a fossil occurrence or exact Scleractinia origin. Oliver’s review-bounded fossil-to-living record runs from the Middle Triassic to the Holocene, displayed as 247.2–0 Ma; that range is distinct from the clock estimate and from individual symbiosis-event windows.
