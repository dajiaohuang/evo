## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-4032fd7aa5}
COL26.8 records Mesalina ercolinii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Lacertidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-f689a962f7}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 66 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-046431fa8e}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
