## Small spring flowers and a late leaf {#s-4672a57383}
Moraea marionae is inconspicuous even when flowering. Its small white to pale-blue flowers have three-lobed inner tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) with a narrow central cusp; the terete (round in cross-section) leaf may be absent or only just emerging during flowering, reaching full length later in summer.
