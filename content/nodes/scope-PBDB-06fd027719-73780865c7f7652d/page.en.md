## Eritherium in the PBDB source path {#s-bf2efc4ec0}
PBDB records *Eritherium* at genus rank in this route. This route has occurrence guides for one species, *Eritherium azzouzorum*. Recorded database parents organize these records; they are not a unique phylogenetic scheme or a measure of evolutionary importance. This route lists non-extant fossil-species records only and does not establish that the entire group is extinct.
