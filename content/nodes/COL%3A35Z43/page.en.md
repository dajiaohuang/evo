## *Didymopanax gardneri*: five to eight leaflets in a terminal whorl {#s-a077c42061}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character field describes a single whorl of five to eight finger-like leaflets at the top of the leaf stalk. Their elliptic to oblong blades have rounded bases, unbroken margins and long tapering or tail-like tips; the undersides may retain silky hairs. The terminal flower cluster has five to ten or more primary branches, each bearing umbrella-like groups of flowers.

Petals are yellowish or greenish, and the ovary is listed as two- or three-chambered. When dry, the fruit is laterally compressed or three-lobed. These are regional description states, not a measured range of variation.
