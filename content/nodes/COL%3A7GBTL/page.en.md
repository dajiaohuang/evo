## Life-history evidence limits {#s-9d9bafbed3}
The life-history values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 archive. They are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical taxa may share or inherit data; matching names, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been independently reviewed.
