## One occurrence at the holotype locality {#s-6decbcc4f7}
The fixed Paleobiology Database (PBDB) roster accepts Hatzegopteryx thambema beneath Hatzegopteryx. This guide uses one eligible species-level occurrence linked to one collection and one reference identifier. Those count database records and citations, not fossils or individual animals.

## Valioara and the Densuș-Ciula unit {#s-fc13f292f5}
The selected collection is Valioara, Hatzegopteryx holotype site, in Hunedoara (region code RO). Its recorded unit is Densuș-Ciula and its database age bracket is 72.2–66 Ma, with the older bound in the Early Maastrichtian. This one locality does not map the species’ full range.

## The broad age bracket {#s-b93fbbfe1b}
The selected occurrence has a database bracket of 72.2–66 Ma (Ma means million years ago). It bounds the recorded age; it does not imply continuous fossils through the interval or directly date the species’ origin, extinction or an individual’s lifespan. The range is not a precise date for the holotype.

## A channel label describes the collection setting {#s-c30a0ba71d}
The Valioara collection carries the database environment label “channel” and preservation-mode label “body.” These are collection-level categories. A channel setting does not establish Hatzegopteryx’s living habitat or diet, and the preservation label need not describe every specimen individually.

## The citation attached to the collection {#s-c4aa652ff5}
The database links Valioara, Hatzegopteryx holotype site to Buffetaut et al. (2002), reference 30139. This is the citation attached to the collection record; the database link alone does not establish the full history of study for the species.

## Evidence limits {#s-fe823be30c}
This guide selects one species-level record from the fixed 276-name PBDB pterosaur roster. Database acceptance and parentage organize that record; they do not settle every specimen identification or taxonomic dispute, and remain separate from same-name Catalogue of Life concepts. The holotype locality, broad age bracket and collection labels do not establish the species’ full range, exact origin or extinction, abundance, habitat or diet. The occurrence record is not a complete anatomical or ecological account.
