## Accepted name and placement {#s-a5de93cb12}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus aporostegus Schwartz, 1965 at species rank in Eleutherodactylus, within Eleutherodactylinae, order Eleutherodactylidae. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026; it is distinct from the historical source concepts below.

## Reading route {#s-5b7f4256b1}
The accepted name locates this record in Eleutherodactylus. The biological entries are older AmphiBIO codes under Frost 2011 and may not share the present catalogue concept.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-0668cbcd0d}
AmphiBIO marks both fossorial and terrestrial foraging in its historical row. These are broad categories, not a detailed account of where or how often the frog feeds. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against that notice.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-da94885b0d}
The source gives a maximum adult body length of 48 mm; for an anuran this is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a range across individuals.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-9ce0b71da7}
Minimum and maximum clutch entries are both 18 individuals or eggs. The source does not resolve whether the count refers to eggs or offspring, nor specify sex, population, date or wild/captive conditions. The identical extrema are retained as reported, not generalized to every clutch.
