## Compact clumps and woody capsules {#s-c46a3a7c44}
This dwarf perennial forms compact clumps and reaches 10 cm; its fibrous roots are thickened. Glaucous, three-sided leaves form short sheaths and have cartilaginous margins, toothed tips and dots. Solitary bright pink flowers are recorded from September to April. Woody capsules fall away, leaving a star-shaped stalk; they have five chambers, no valve wings, and complete covering membranes. It grows on rocky dolerite outcrops and weathered Karoo sediments.
