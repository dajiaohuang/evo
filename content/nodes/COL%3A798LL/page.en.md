## Yellow flowers and a long projecting style {#s-9e9f135294}
Romulea diversiformis is 80–200 mm tall and has bright-yellow flowers. The inner tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are broader than the outer, and the long style projects 3–6 mm beyond the anthers; numerous leaves and translucent bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower) margins are also diagnostic.

## Wet dolerite and clay {#s-b6eb422890}
The cited flora records moist or waterlogged dolerite and clay.
