## A segmented chelicera in a Cambrian fossil {#s-21ad624e76}
The Cambrian holotype of *Megachelicerax cousteaui* from Utah preserves a large, three-segmented chelicera—the first pair of appendages near the mouth—five pairs of limbs behind it, and rear-body appendages with thin plate-like lamellae. These are observations from one specimen. Predation is a functional interpretation, and the proposed position linking habeliids with synziphosurines comes from a scored phylogenetic matrix; neither follows from the anatomy alone.
