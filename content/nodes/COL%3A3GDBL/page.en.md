## Glossarion bilabiatum {#s-c13f4a4532}
## A single cylindrical head with two-lipped florets

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a woolly stem and alternate, oblong leaves with entire edges. The upper leaf face is hairless and the underside woolly; veins branch and reconnect in a net. The inflorescence usually has one head. Its cylindrical involucre has overlapping, lance-shaped bracts.

The florets are bilabiate (with two distinct lips), and the outer lip stands upright. The dry fruits are prism-shaped, hairless and have ten ribs. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
