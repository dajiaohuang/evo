## Unequal leaflets and a sickle-shaped petal {#s-649e4921cc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes three to six pairs of unequal leaflets, ranging from ovate to broadly elliptic, on alternate, spirally arranged leaves. The leaflets are 0.7 to 2.7 cm long and 0.5 to 2.1 cm wide. Yellow flowers have four obovate petals and one longer, sickle-shaped petal; the linear-oblong pods are 3.5 to 4.5 cm long and bear yellowish, sticky hairs. Seed form is marked unknown in the source.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-4a71c801b1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrícola, or terrestrial. The field gives no collection site or full range.
