## Branches, leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-6cc918501e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists armed branches, with cylindrical or flattened cross-sections, and spiny stipules. Leaflets are oblong or obovate, with notched (emarginate) or blunt tips and wedge-shaped (cuneate) or oblique bases. The source describes their venation as cladodromous: side veins branch toward the margin rather than forming loops. The upper surface is described as becoming glabrous or as hairy; the lower surface is hairy. Inflorescences are racemes or panicles, axillary or terminal, with leaf-like bracts. Flowers have an urn-shaped calyx, lilac or purple petals, a hairy standard (banner petal), and stamens united into one bundle (monadelphous). The fruit is a samara, a dry winged fruit, with a warty seed-bearing core. These are source-listed descriptions and alternatives, not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-219d04dd31}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
