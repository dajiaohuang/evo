## The deposited caecilian record {#s-9574259ef8}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis pseudangularis in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-2b3889eefe}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-4b32689d72}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-9a1777ed4b}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-ae0b35955a}
At species level, the compilation gives a post-vent annulus (body-ring) count of 5. This value may summarize records rather than describe one animal; it does not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic count.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-5edf71fae2}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
