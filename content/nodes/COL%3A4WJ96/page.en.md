## *Senaea janeirensis* {#s-f9566e57bf}
## Narrow leaves and a lilac-blue corolla

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records narrow elliptic leaves with acute tips. The calyx is green or purplish, while the corolla is lilac or blue and has ovate lobes. This is the content of a single regional morphology field; it does not provide a broader account of range or variation.
