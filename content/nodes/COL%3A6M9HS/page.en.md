## A flower-lip difference from H. villosa {#s-ca1ff12634}
The SANBI regional account describes a slender, tuberous plant up to 29 cm tall, with two basal oval leaves that become nearly hairless. Green flowers sit along a dense raceme, and the lip is entire and short-spurred. The lip also separates this species from H. villosa: in H. exilis its side lobes are absent or no longer than 0.4 of the middle lobe; in both H. villosa varieties they measure 0.6 to 1 times the middle lobe. These are regional descriptions, not population measurements.

## A coastal habitat record {#s-e19e564e88}
The same regional account places H. exilis in coastal regions. It gives no locality, survey date or population frequency, so this habitat note does not define the species’ full range.
