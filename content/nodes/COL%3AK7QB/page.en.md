## *Baccharis stylosa*: three-veined leaves and toothed margins {#s-b0f11e5615}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records developed leaf stalks and three-veined leaves. Their listed outlines range from lance-shaped to egg-shaped, elliptic, or oblong, with serrated margins. Flower heads are grouped in panicles. The field describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered; these are alternatives rather than a combined description. Rock-associated and terrestrial substrate labels are listed without localities.
