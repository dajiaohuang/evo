## Recorded parent of Halcyornithidae {#s-445abd8f5f}
Halcyornithidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Psittaciformes as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
