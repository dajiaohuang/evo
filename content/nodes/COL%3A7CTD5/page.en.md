## Papery leaves and yellow flowers {#s-01d6f9e6ef}
Flora do Brasil describes Trimezia martinicensis with papery, flat leaves up to 60 cm long and yellow flowers. Flowering stems may carry one to seven rhipidia (compact flower clusters); the single bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower) can reach 35 cm. The anthers are dark brown to blackish, and the style branches have two crests.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-04284d436e}
The source classifies the species as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant).
