## Astroloba herrei: waxy leaves and long flower stalks {#s-8c94e34f69}
A South African regional account describes a stemmed succulent up to 20 cm tall. The light-green leaves are not pungent, and are smooth with fine striations. Erect flowers grow on loose racemes; they are white with yellow tepals and pale-green midribs. The outer tepals are sometimes slightly swollen below.
