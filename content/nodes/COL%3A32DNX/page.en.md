## Cullumia selago {#s-474a07f7a3}
## Pungent bristles point inward on a compact shrublet

The account describes a prickly, densely leafy shrublet up to 20 cm tall. Its elliptic leaves curve inward and carry two rows of sharp bristles that also bend inward. Radiate flower heads are yellow. The cited habitat field records stony slopes.
