## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the flora description {#s-df7f00cfc8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records unarmed branches. Leaf blades range from entire to two-lobed, with the lobes diverging in an arch. The base is described as rounded or flat-cut to slightly heart-shaped, and the tip as long-pointed. The upper surface is hairless or becoming hairless; the lower has fine hairs lying flat or short, bristly hairs. Flower buds are tubular with a sharp point. The hypanthium (floral cup) is tubular and hairless inside. The calyx splits into two or three lobes when the flower opens; petals are linear. The source records ten anthers and a woolly inner surface on the staminal column, with no tongue-shaped appendages. The ovary is stalked. The legume is recorded as opening elastically. These are regional dataset observations, not a complete account of variation or distribution.
