## Could giant pterosaurs fly and launch quadrupedally? {#s-96ee0e00cc}
Calculations using reconstructed mass, wing shape, bone strength and launch kinematics find powered flight and a four-limbed launch mechanically feasible for the sampled giant-pterosaur reconstructions. This tests whether model inputs can work together; it does not observe launch or directly measure extinct animals’ mass, muscle power or movement. Feasibility in these reconstructions is not a universal account of pterosaur behaviour.
