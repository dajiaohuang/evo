## Mikania glabra {#s-fba0cb405c}
## Serrated leaves and rounded head bracts

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright stems with solid pith. Opposite leaves are elliptic to lance-shaped, leathery, and smooth on both sides; their edges are serrated, bases rounded or blunt, and tips acute. The veins are pinnate and loop near the margin.

Corymb-like clusters carry stalked heads. Small, scale-like bracts sit at the top of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around a head are smooth and rounded at the tip. The bell-shaped corolla has lobes as long as its tube, with hairs on the lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional entry gives no locality or complete range.
