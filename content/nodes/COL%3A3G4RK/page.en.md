## Whiplike leaves and long cream flowers {#s-77bed941e5}
Gladiolus leptosiphon has slender, whiplike leaves and long-tubed cream to pale-yellow flowers. The tube is 45–50 mm long, more than twice the bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters); the lower lateral tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are smaller and carry a purple median streak.

## Dry, stony sandstone slopes {#s-52e551e8f6}
The regional flora records dry, stony sandstone slopes.
