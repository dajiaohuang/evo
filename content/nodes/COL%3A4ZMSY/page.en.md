## Limits of the historical life-history and ecological records {#s-f6a02797a6}
Life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 archive. Except for maximum longevity, summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespan or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names and families and same-name sparse rows support the association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
