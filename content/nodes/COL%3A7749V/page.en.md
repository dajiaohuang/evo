## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Pera anisotricha? {#s-0b318cd27c}
The branches are pubescent with star-shaped, scale-like hairs. Alternate leaves have sparse scaly hairs and domatia—small structures on the lower surface—and saucer-shaped glands. Axillary clusters of unisexual flower units have two basal bracts; their involucre opens fully at flowering. Each group has three stalkless male flowers with one perianth whorl and a developed sterile pistil, plus four stalked female flowers without a perianth. The flask-shaped ovary is hairy; the stigma is umbrella-shaped, and filiform staminodes arise from sepals. The fruit capsule splits into three segments; its nearly spherical, hairy seed-bearing portions have smooth inner tissue. Seeds are ovate and carunculate.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-7366f54869}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
