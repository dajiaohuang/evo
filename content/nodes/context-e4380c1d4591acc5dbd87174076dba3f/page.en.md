## The source concept {#s-850a13bd12}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Plectrohyla charadricola as row Anf2532, classified in the genus Plectrohyla, family Hylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); whether this record matches a species concept in COL26.8 has not been established. The traits below describe the deposited record.

## Activity periods {#s-9b617859c2}
The source assigns both daytime and night activity categories. These labels describe overall activity periods, not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Seasonal activity {#s-2541c816ab}
The row is assigned to the wet-and-warm seasonal activity category. These categories compare activity conditions with the local annual climate, so they do not correspond to the same calendar months everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-6d13d25e46}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 50.9 mm. For frogs, this measure is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-374eb8834a}
The row reports a maximum of one reproductive event per year. These values are extrema or counts compiled from literature; the row does not specify the sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive setting for each value. Size fields may describe eggs or offspring.
