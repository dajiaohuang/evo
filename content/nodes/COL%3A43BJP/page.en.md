## Mikania psilostachya {#s-d4d0f0881e}
## A woody vine with gland-dotted leaves

The supplied description presents *Mikania psilostachya* as a sparsely branched woody vine. Its brown to reddish stems are round to slightly angular, finely lined and densely covered with short hairs; the pith is not hollow. Opposite leaves may reach 15 by 8 cm. They are ovate to elliptic, with widely spaced small teeth, sharply pointed tips, rough upper surfaces and lower surfaces that are hairy and densely dotted with glands. Short petioles are about 1 cm, reaching 3 cm on the largest leaves.

Panicles have elongated, spike-like or raceme-like branches. Heads are 7–9 mm high. The white corolla is 4–5 mm long, with a narrow tube and a narrow bell-shaped to tubular upper part. The dry fruits (achenes) are 2.5–3 mm long, have four or five ribs, and bear glands and scattered hairs. Each pappus has about 40 slender bristles, mostly in one row, with gradually thickened tips. This description supplies morphology but no habitat or locality, so it does not establish a complete range.
