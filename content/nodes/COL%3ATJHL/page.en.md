## ## Sticky hairs and compound racemes {#s-67b5b13b37}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium and 2–4 pairs of oval or elliptic-ovate leaflets. Stems and leaves bear sticky, villous hairs, often with stiff bristles; terminal and axillary compound racemes are also hairy. The yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. The oblong pods are 3–4 cm long, do not twist at maturity, and bear glandular, bristly or villous hairs. The seed is depressed-obovoid. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
