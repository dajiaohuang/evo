## Pteronia sordida {#s-e8f8e5422e}
## Small ericoid leaves and villous fruits

The description records a shrub about 35 cm tall, with rough, splitting bark and branches that are hairless or become so with age. Its opposite, narrow leaves are blunt-tipped and nearly joined at the base; they are flat above, rounded beneath and 2–6 mm long. Compact heads have about eight florets. The oblong achenes are densely villous, and the pale straw-coloured pappus is 8–9 mm long, about as long as the corolla. Habitat is listed as Dwarf Karroid Shrubland on sandy soil or stony flats.
