## Seventeen records with unequal sample coverage {#s-f30eb2da32}
This route brings together the earlier strict same-name Psilopogon pyrolophus profile and 17 additional guides linked through older Megalaima names. Those are selected records, not complete coverage of the genus. The samples differ: Psilopogon haemacephalus has 11 measured birds, eight with complete sets; Psilopogon lineatus has 10 and eight; Psilopogon virens has eight and four. Most other selected rows contain four to six measured birds. A complete-set count is distinct from the total, and neither gives every trait's denominator.

## Shared fruit labels with different levels and habitats {#s-92d60aea38}
Across the 17 older-name rows, AVONET assigns the same Frugivore niche and Insessorial lifestyle. Habitat differs: Psilopogon haemacephalus is coded Woodland/semi-open, while the other selected rows are Forest/dense vegetation. Trophic level is a separate field: haemacephalus, rafflesii, mystacophanos, chrysopogon and javensis are Omnivore; the other rows are Herbivore. Psilopogon faber and Psilopogon nuchalis each carry a compiled mass of 87.69 g. The underlying book was not checked to explain the matching figures, which do not establish matching individual-weight distributions.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-f6ed3bd391}
The route covers one strict same-name AVONET profile and 17 additional guides linked through historical names; it is not a complete catalogue of accepted Psilopogon descendants. Compiled masses summarize source records rather than the lightest and heaviest individuals. The historical-name guides keep their own crosswalk, sample and category limits, and the original book behind the matching faber/nuchalis mass figures was not reviewed. These pages are comparison routes, not genus monographs or complete species accounts.
