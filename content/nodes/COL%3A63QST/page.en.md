## Seventeen records with unequal sample coverage {#s-f30eb2da32}
This route brings together the earlier strict same-name [Psilopogon pyrolophus](Psilopogon_pyrolophus/page.en.md) profile and 17 additional guides linked through older Megalaima names. Those are selected records, not complete coverage of the genus. The samples differ: [Psilopogon haemacephalus](Psilopogon_haemacephalus/page.en.md) has 11 measured birds, eight with complete sets; [Psilopogon lineatus](Psilopogon_lineatus/page.en.md) has 10 and eight; [Psilopogon virens](Psilopogon_virens/page.en.md) has eight and four. Most other selected rows contain four to six measured birds. A complete-set count is distinct from the total, and neither gives every trait's denominator.

## Shared fruit labels with different levels and habitats {#s-92d60aea38}
Across the 17 older-name rows, AVONET assigns the same Frugivore niche and Insessorial lifestyle. Habitat differs: [Psilopogon haemacephalus](Psilopogon_haemacephalus/page.en.md) is coded Woodland/semi-open, while the other selected rows are Forest/dense vegetation. Trophic level is a separate field: haemacephalus, rafflesii, mystacophanos, chrysopogon and javensis are Omnivore; the other rows are Herbivore. [Psilopogon faber](Psilopogon_faber/page.en.md) and [Psilopogon nuchalis](Psilopogon_nuchalis/page.en.md) each carry a compiled mass of 87.69 g. The underlying book was not checked to explain the matching figures, which do not establish matching individual-weight distributions.
