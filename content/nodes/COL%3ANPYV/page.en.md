## Bulbine diphylla: a tuber and two or three stem leaves {#s-6c2cde2e46}
A South African regional account describes a geophyte recorded at 10–15 cm, with a tuberous rootstock. It has two or three unequal, fleshy, ovoid leaves that arise clearly along the stem. Yellow flowers grow on a loose raceme.

These characters and measurements come from a regional South African flora account cited to a 2012 publication. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish variation across the full range. It does not independently classify the material as native, naturalised, cultivated or domesticated.

The account records sandstone and granite outcrops as habitats.
