## Ecological evidence {#s-dc3dc2b2a3}
Two Bornean studies show how diet depends on site and study design. Over 25 months in Sabah’s Danum Valley, feeding time was spent mainly on young leaves and seeds. A separate year of records at Central Kalimantan’s Sabangau involved a group of three adult females, with seeds making up most recorded foods. A multi-site study also detected terrestrial behavior at six of eight sites; ground use was more common in disturbed forest, though the authors called for more data to confirm the association.
