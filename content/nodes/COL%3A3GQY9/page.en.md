## *Goeppertia squarrosa*: spiral bracts around paired flowers {#s-70c0777e29}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description places leaves spirally on stem or basal shoots and records unornamented young leaves. The flowering stalk is stem-borne; all bracts are fertile, spiral and not red or orange, with upright margins. Each small flower cluster bears two flowers and one membranous bracteole, a small leaf-like structure beneath a flower.

The corolla tube is entire. One outer staminode is present, with a non-petal-like callus at its tip. This account reflects the listed regional morphology characters; it does not add a habitat or range claim.
