## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-d45e8c7c67}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) accepts Tupandactylus imperator under the recorded parent Tupandactylus. The two selected species-level occurrences come from Nova Olinda quarries and Mina Triunfo in Ceará, Brazil, and carry separate references. One older identification, “Tapejara imperator n. sp.,” is linked to the accepted name; that recombination records a name history, not a separate discovery.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-bb4688753d}
Nova Olinda quarries and Mina Triunfo are both recorded from the Crato unit in Ceará (region code BR). PBDB assigns each the same broad bracket, 119.57–113.2 Ma, with Late Aptian as its early interval. These two collections are the selected entries here, not a complete map of the species’ distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-e31de1e4bc}
The two selected records together span a database age envelope of 119.57–113.2 Ma (Ma means millions of years ago); each collection has that same bracket. The interval does not imply continuous presence through every part of that span, directly date a specimen, or establish the species’ origin or extinction.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-ef8aec7738}
PBDB labels Nova Olinda quarries “lacustrine - large,” with preservation modes “body, replaced with pyrite, replaced with limonite.” Mina Triunfo is labeled “lacustrine indet.” and “body, soft parts.” These are collection-level descriptions; they do not establish this species’ habitat or diet, or describe every fossil individually.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-ab00ed179a}
The Nova Olinda quarries record links to Campos and Kellner (1997), ref:32111; Mina Triunfo links to Pinheiro et al. (2011), ref:34733. These database attributions do not by themselves identify the original description or provide complete author lists. The distinct links accompany two separate collection records; they are not a specimen-level reassessment.

## Evidence limits {#s-4ff9a85d56}
This page summarizes selected PBDB records available on 2026-10-02. It belongs to a fixed 276-entry pterosaur roster and includes species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. PBDB’s accepted name and recorded parent chain organize the entry; they do not resolve specimen identifications or every taxonomic dispute, and same-name Catalogue of Life concepts remain separate. Collection sites, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and preservation labels describe different things. They do not establish the species’ complete range, exact origin or extinction, ecology, abundance or the condition of every specimen. The selected records do not form a full review of descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet or behavior.
