## What range of leaf, flower and fruit traits is recorded for Heteropterys trigoniifolia? {#s-57e2544871}
Opposite leaves range from membranous or papery and elliptic, oval, lance-shaped or oblong. Bases are blunt or rounded; tips range from pointed to rounded or small-pointed. Hairiness and glands vary, but the margins roll under. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts and stalked pedicels. Sepals have flat tips and rusty hairs, and may be glandless or glandular. Petals may be tiny or larger, white with a pink mark on the rear petal; they may be clawed or keeled, with ragged edges. Anthers may be upright or turned over. Styles range from equal in length to unequal, and from straight to distally diverging, with pointed or blunt tips. The seed-bearing fruit body may be rough or have lateral ridges that are faint or conspicuous.
