## What traits are recorded for Clusia rosea? {#s-f3553784b5}
It has a taproot and additional roots, and stalked, leathery, obovate leaves with conspicuous secretory channels. Pendulous flower clusters bear four sepals and six to eight petals; the corolla may be white, cream, or pink. In male flowers, the stamens, their fusion, and the pistillode were not observed in this record; resin is present in the stamens or staminodes. The ovary has six to ten chambers. Sepals and staminodes remain on mature fruit while petals fall, and the seeds have an aril.
