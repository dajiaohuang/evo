## What marks Stachytarpheta macedoi? {#s-3953ce0e3f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records smooth, upright, cylindrical stems and opposite, narrow leaves without stalks. Small lilac, funnel-shaped flowers form terminal spikes. Each flower has two fertile stamens and two reduced sterile stamens. The dry fruit splits into two segments, each containing one seed; the segments are recorded as dark brown.

## What habitat category is given? {#s-d780423661}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, with no collection locality specified.
