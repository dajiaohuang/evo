## What leaf and flower traits distinguish Croton urticifolius? {#s-2648ac9c15}
Young branches and short-stalked oval or heart-shaped leaves have star-shaped or outward-radiating star hairs. The leaves have radiating main veins, finely toothed margins, and no nectar glands; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are shortly stalked; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate, the style is two-lobed, and the calyx lobes are alike. Female petals are absent, the bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
