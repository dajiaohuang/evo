## Large white flowers and smooth globose fruit {#s-5c2533d10d}
The record describes nearly smooth stems and elliptic to obovate leaves with tapering or blunt bases and pointed tips. The upper leaf surface is smooth or nearly so, while the lower is bristly. White bisexual flowers have a smooth tubular-bell-shaped calyx over 1 cm long and a funnel-shaped corolla over 2 cm. The ovary and globose white fruit are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
