## Classification {#s-fa8a822714}
The 2007 revision described A. betsileo from Bemosary Classified Forest in the Fandriana District. Its type series comprised three adults sampled on 13 and 15 July 2002, represented by curated blood, measurements and field photographs. The authors reported distinctive pelage and mitochondrial sequence attributes, but described the regional limits as tentative and called for surveys north and south of the type locality.
