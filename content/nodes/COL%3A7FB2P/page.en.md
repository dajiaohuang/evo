## Valeriana glechomifolia F.G.Mey. {#s-aa771ba6f7}
## A low herb with round to kidney-shaped leaves

Valeriana glechomifolia has prostrate or decumbent herbaceous stems. Its petiolate leaves are round to kidney-shaped and slightly different in color between their two surfaces. The flowers form a head-like inflorescence and are pubescent. The species is gynodioecious: some plants bear bisexual flowers, while others bear female flowers. The fruit is hairy and three- or five-angled, with five ribs, a blunt tip and a calyx recorded as toothed or ring-shaped.
