## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f64e23491d}
COL26.8 records Hypsicalotes kinabaluensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Agamidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework.
