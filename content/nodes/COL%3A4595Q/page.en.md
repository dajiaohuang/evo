## Hairy leaves and four-lobed white ripe fruits {#s-32ae77c5bd}
The morphology record describes alternate, stalked leaves that are woolly and may bear hairs swollen at the base. The leaves are lance-shaped to oblong, with pointed tips, tapering bases and entire edges. Dense terminal clusters carry hairy, five-parted flowers whose corollas may be green, white, yellow, purple or bluish. The fruit is a drupe: green when unripe, white with black spots when ripe, hairy and strongly four-lobed.
