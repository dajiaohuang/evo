## Source classification and concept status {#s-9fbd541b4a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3283 records Physalaemus angrensis under the source classification Physalaemus (Leiuperidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-76684d380d}
The compilation records terrestrial and aquatic as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Activity periods (night and twilight) {#s-029d78dd0a}
The row lists night and twilight as activity-period categories. These are broad summaries, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm) {#s-1a0791b094}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-d4cdf87da2}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 20 mm for this row. AmphiBIO's Body_size_mm field records maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-7a438dcef5}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
