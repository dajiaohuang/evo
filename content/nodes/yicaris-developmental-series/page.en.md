## Growth stages preserved in phosphate {#s-daf592f5eb}
Phosphatized Yicaris dianensis fossils preserve individuals at several growth stages, including an eight-segment thorax reconstructed from soft-part anatomy. The series shows leaf-shaped outgrowths on the limbs and an organization resembling that of eucrustaceans. These are anatomical observations from the fossils; identifying the outgrowths as epipodites and placing Yicaris within crown Eucrustacea depend on character interpretation. The sample therefore does not provide an exact date for the origin of the crown group.
