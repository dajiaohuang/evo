## The source concept {#s-44f75a4a29}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Breviceps poweri* in row Anf0328 under Breviceps, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-c3eef4ace4}
For Anf0328, the compilation tags fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-16607fa8d1}
The adult food field lists arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; this tag is not a complete diet and gives no share of food items.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-bcbbe6ca8f}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 50 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-52ae3fa3c2}
The row records a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report.

## Developmental strategy {#s-3561ddc8a9}
The deposited row classifies development as direct. That label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
