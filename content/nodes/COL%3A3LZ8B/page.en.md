## Hippeastrum vittatum: flowers and seeds {#s-2557685128}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb and no rhizome. Leaves are strap-shaped with blunt tips, and the inflorescence bears 5–8 flowers. The funnel-shaped perianth has white tips and a base ranging from green to white, with reticulate margins recorded in red to violet. The hypanthium tube is of medium length, 2.5–6 cm. Sepals are slightly wider than the obovate petals and are broadly obovate; the lower lateral sepals differ little from the upper ones. The lower petals are equal to the upper petals. The corona is fringed and partly fused. The filaments are fascicled and much shorter than the sepals and petals; the anthers are yellow or cream, and the stigma is three-cleft.

The capsule is brown inside. Seeds are numerous, flattened and disc-shaped.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
