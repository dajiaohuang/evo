## Wedelia bahiensis {#s-b2f63e6812}
## Ribbed, stiff-haired branches and variable leaf margins

Flora do Brasil 2020 records erect, cylindrical, ribbed branches with stiff, appressed hairs. Leaves may be petiolate or sessile; the blades range from lance-shaped to ovate, with acute tips and tapered or wedge-shaped bases. Their margins are recorded as entire, toothed, flat or rolled under, and both surfaces bear stiff hairs.

Flower heads occur singly or in paired-branched clusters (dichasia); the stalks also have stiff hairs, and ray florets are present. The fruits have smooth surfaces. The entry leaves xylopodium presence unknown and classifies the plant as terrestrial.
