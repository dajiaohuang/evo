## Tridax procumbens {#s-25fdaf4c0e}
## Trailing stems carry solitary heads and two kinds of fruit

The Flora of Panama account describes a trailing perennial herb 14–40 cm tall. Its branched stems have longitudinal ridges, woody bases and often a reddish colour; stiff, multicellular hairs are thickest at the nodes. Leaves are ovate to lance-shaped, often lobed near the base, and measure 1–6 by 0.5–4 cm. Their tips are acute, bases wedge-shaped to tapered, and wavy margins range from nearly entire to toothed. Both surfaces are hairy, especially along the veins. Petioles are 0.2–2 cm long and clasp the stem at their bases.

Solitary bell-shaped flower heads sit on ridged, hairy stalks 6–18.4 cm long. Heads measure 0.6–2.2 cm across the rays and 0.6–1.4 cm high. Two or three rows of bracts surround a short-conical to nearly flat receptacle. The hairy outer bracts are ovate to oblong, with tips from acute to tapered and margins often purple; the inner ovate to obovate bracts have thin, purple margins and pointed tips. Persistent lance-shaped paleae (scales on the receptacle) have finely fringed purple margins; inner paleae are narrower.

Each head has 3–6 fertile ray florets with pale-yellow to white, oblong ligules, each divided into two or three lobes. The ligules are hairy along their main veins. Numerous fertile disc florets are yellow, often tinged purple; their narrow tubular corollas have five lance-shaped lobes, short hairs near the tips and a ring-like thickening near the base of the tube. The ray-floret style branches curve backward; disc-floret style branches are slender, flattened and rolled back, ending in narrow points.

The two floret types also have different dry fruits. Ray-floret fruits are brown-black, narrowly obconical, 1.5–2.5 mm long, densely covered with upward-pointing hairs, and have a distinct attachment base (carpopodium) plus feathered pappus bristles 0.7–2.5 mm long. Disc-floret fruits are similarly dark and narrow, with a small ring at the tip; they bear 18–20 feathered bristles of alternating lengths, 3.5–6 mm long. The account gives chromosome number n = 18.
