## What flower and fruit traits identify Pterandra pyroidea? {#s-56b771a0a7}
Its stipules are silky-haired, and the record notes Malpighian hairs (distinctive branched hairs) on the plant. Flower clusters have four, five or six units. The corolla is pink. The fruit is densely hairy, with hairs ranging from coarse to woolly.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f2dd9dbe13}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
