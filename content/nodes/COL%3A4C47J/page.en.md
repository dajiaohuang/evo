## Read the study {#s-1f96577959}
How was bite force measured for Paleosuchus palpebrosus in a standardized adult sample? This guide follows the accepted COL26.8 record 4C47J and reads the study at its stated sample and geographic scale. In Erickson et al.'s 2012 comparison, 3 sexually mature Cuvier's dwarf caimans were tested, with a mean sample body mass of about 13 kg. At the prominent molariform tooth near the back of the upper jaw, the study reported a taxon-representative bite force of 900 N; the sampled values ranged from 667–1,125 N. Researchers used the highest of 3–5 bites per animal. Across species, body mass was the main predictor of bite force, while rostral proportion explained little additional variation. This is a standardized measurement from a limited institutional sample, not a field-performance constant or population range.
