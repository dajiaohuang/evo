## How to read reproductive records {#s-6843752916}
the reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 13.3; lowest reported clutch/litter mean 10; highest reported clutch/litter mean 25; consolidated litters/clutches per year 1; egg length 70 mm. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Bite force in three adult Cuvier's dwarf caimans {#s-1f96577959}
In Erickson et al.'s 2012 comparison, 3 sexually mature Cuvier's dwarf caimans were tested, with a mean sample body mass of about 13 kg. At the prominent molariform tooth near the back of the upper jaw, the study reported a taxon-representative bite force of 900 N; the sampled values ranged from 667–1,125 N. Researchers used the highest of 3–5 bites per animal. Across species, body mass was the main predictor of bite force, while rostral proportion explained little additional variation. This is a standardized measurement from a limited institutional sample, not a field-performance constant or population range.

Continue with Paleosuchus trigonatus (COL 4C47K) to compare the same experimental measure while keeping sample size and body mass in view. The Crocodylia group guide provides the wider reading route; the neighboring page retains its own sources and limits.
