## Citronella mucronata {#s-04a4de9583}
## Mucronate leaves top a branched flower axis

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes oblong to ovate leaves 4–6.5 cm long, with petioles 0.5–1 cm long. Their bases are acute or blunt; their tips are acute or end in a tiny abrupt point. The leaves have 5–6 pairs of secondary veins.

The main flower axis is 4–10 cm long, with hairy bracts about 2 cm long. The ovary is hairless; the petals are hairless and lack appendages, while the sepals are hairy. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
