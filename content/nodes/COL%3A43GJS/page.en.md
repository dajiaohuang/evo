## Mimosa miranda {#s-1e9813af65}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa miranda* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes a bristly stem, stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) that fall away, and a projection between pairs of leaf divisions. Its flower clusters are cylindrical; the flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairy petal lobes. The same regional record describes it as terrestrial.
