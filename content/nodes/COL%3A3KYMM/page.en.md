## Yellow flowers above thick, sickle-shaped leaves {#s-cce15f4bf3}
This account describes a dense succulent shrub up to 12 cm tall and 30 cm across. Its sickle-shaped leaves are thick and slightly rough. Flowers are solitary or arranged in elongated, one-sided clusters; the bracts join to form a cup below each flower. The petals are yellow and red on the outside. The five-chambered fruits have narrow valve wings and no closing bodies. It grows on slopes with quartzite rock.
