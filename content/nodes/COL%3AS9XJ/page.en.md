## A source-bound comparison with the La Enramada types {#s-52c381890a}
The 2023 paper cites a truncated dorsal snout, half-visible tympanic annulus, pale dorsal spots and separate enamelled limb tubercles rather than continuous folds for Centrolene altitudinale.
