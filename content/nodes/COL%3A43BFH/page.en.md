## *Mikania nana*: hairy leaves in corymb-like clusters {#s-1c701e397a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, papery leaves that are elliptic, lance-shaped, or egg-shaped. Both surfaces are hairy, and three strong veins arise from the base. The margins are toothed, and the base may be tapered or wedge-shaped. Erect or trailing, woolly branches carry corymb-like clusters of stalked heads. The corolla limb is bell-shaped, with triangular, pointed lobes; the fruitlets are sparsely hairy. These are alternative morphology entries in a regional dataset.
