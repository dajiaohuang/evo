## Distinguish the measured length {#s-3ed0eecf60}
The compiled maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 295 mm; the female maximum is also 295 mm and the male maximum is 235 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. These are maxima in the reported material, not theoretical limits or typical individual sizes. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and cannot alone quantify sexual dimorphism across a population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-4038d3c372}
The row classifies the microhabitat as Aquatic/Terrestrial, diet as Carnivorous and foraging mode as active (ACT). These are broad categories, not a complete habitat or food inventory or a fixed schedule. The diet label does not identify particular prey, and the one-row summary cannot show differences among populations, seasons or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-b3898e0f07}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, a mean of 3.5 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, low and high reported means of 4 and 9, egg length of 31.8 mm and egg width of 30.8 mm. A clutch/litter summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The fractional mean is a dataset summary, not a count from one event. The low and high fields are endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed individual-clutch limits. These fields do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
