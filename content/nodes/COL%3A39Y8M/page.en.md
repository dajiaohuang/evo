## ## Head-like flower clusters and a pleurogrammed seed {#s-db626da034}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records petiolar nectar glands and bipinnate leaves with 2–6 pairs of pinnae, each bearing 5–12 pairs of leaflets. The flowers are grouped in head-like racemes and are similar in form; the calyx and corolla are hairy, while the ovary is glabrous. The rounded or curved fruit has a smooth outer wall. The elliptic seed bears a pleurogram, a distinct line-like mark on its surface. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
