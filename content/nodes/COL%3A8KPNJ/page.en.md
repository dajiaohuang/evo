## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e631b64eee}
COL26.8 records Antaresia papuensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pythonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-159b2a8651}

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-c70c9d0bad}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
