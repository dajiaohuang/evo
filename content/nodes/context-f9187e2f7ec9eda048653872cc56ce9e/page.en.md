## The source concept {#s-7c5edf9f68}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0116 records Mannophryne vulcano in genus Mannophryne, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-a764bf5c31}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The fields Fos=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this does not show that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-51bcc93f0c}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity). Noc=NA remains missing, not evidence of absence. These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Seasonal activity fields: Wet_warm=1, Dry_warm=1 {#s-c2e77e75f9}
The row records Wet_warm=1 (activity in warm, wet conditions), Dry_warm=1 (activity in dry, warm conditions). Seasonal categories are compared with local annual climate and do not map to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=23.1 mm {#s-eb74d52c5a}
Body_size_mm=23.1 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
