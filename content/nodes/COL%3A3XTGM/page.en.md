## Mandevilla subsagittata {#s-1c97dbe530}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mandevilla subsagittata* as an accepted species. A historical Flora of Panama account describes a liana, mostly hairless, with opposite leaves and yellow flowers whose throat is red. The corolla tube is recorded as 25–30 mm long; follicles were unknown in that account. Its distribution field gives Mexico, Central America, and northern South America as a historical regional statement, not a current or global range.
