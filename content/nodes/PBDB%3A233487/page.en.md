## Moganopterinae: PBDB classification route {#s-eb34bcbef7}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Moganopterinae as a subfamily. This fixed reading route links to one species page: Moganopterus zhuiana. Its database parent path helps readers find records; it is not a claim that every study uses the same classification. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a group-wide account of shared anatomy or ecology.

## Member material linked to Moganopterinae {#s-a445744993}
The linked species guide records one eligible occurrence from one collection, with Yixian as the named stratum. Its age bracket and reference help describe that database record. One occurrence and one collection are not counts of individual animals or fossils, and this single locality does not map the subfamily’s full range.

## Limits of the selected Moganopterinae records {#s-840640443f}
This route covers one species page and a selected occurrence record, not a complete account of Moganopterinae’s anatomy, ecology or geographic range. Record counts and broad database ages do not measure population size or directly date the subfamily’s origin and extinction. PBDB parentage is kept as a catalogue path, separate from Catalogue of Life concepts and phylogenetic hypotheses.
