## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-2ee39afef8}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-dde7d0eea8}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 4. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. ReptTraits combines separately cited traits in a species-level row. The 2015 life-history archive can transfer records between historical species and subspecies. The two tables have separate source histories; their values need not describe one shared specimen or population.
