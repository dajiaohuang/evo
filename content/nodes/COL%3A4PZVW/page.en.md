## Pteronia empetrifolia {#s-d97304a29d}
## Thick opposite leaves and purple or yellow discs

The source describes a sticky shrub 0.2–0.6 m tall, with crowded opposite leaves that are linear and thick. Solitary discoid heads sit at branch tips; their involucres are somewhat top-shaped, with yellowish blunt bracts whose margins are narrowly fringed. Disc florets are recorded as purple or yellow, and the cypselae are shortly hairy. Habitat includes mountain slopes, rocky areas and sandy flats.
