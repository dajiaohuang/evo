## atlas-profile / overview {#s-36579125bf}
Crinoidea—the crinoids, including sea lilies and feather stars—is a class of echinoderms. The cited systematic study describes named early crinoid taxa and identifies the middle Tremadocian as the start of the unproblematic fossil record. It treats Middle Cambrian Echmatocrinus as a problematic assignment, so this profile does not use it to move the class’s secure record earlier.

The cited systematic study supports a middle Tremadocian unproblematic record and describes early crinoid anatomy. The time-scale interval for the middle Tremadocian is 486.85–477.1 Ma, but the source does not supply a point-dated global first appearance.
