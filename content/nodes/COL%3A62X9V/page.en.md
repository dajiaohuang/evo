## Short-marker support is bounded by the included references {#s-2670ec6c60}
The reported 16S dataset comprises 112 new and reference sequences across 534 positions, with four newly collected samples. The deocaensis lineage was placed within the applebyi group with reported Bayesian/ML support 1.00/98. The closest reported compared distance was 6.34% to macrops. Neither high support nor this distance establishes a universal threshold, complete genomic isolation or a divergence date. The paper’s association of genetic separation with coastal conditions remains an interpretation rather than an experimental causal result.

## Coastal observations and earlier mountain records remain separate {#s-38989ef5de}
The newly described population was observed along a rocky stream at 50–150 m in coastal secondary evergreen forest. Macrops and duyenae elevation and proximity comparisons come from earlier mountain records. Recorded air temperature and humidity describe observation sites, not physiological limits. Adjacent survey nondetections do not prove absence; sympatry with other frogs does not establish food or competition. No complete genus diet, breeding calendar or larval biology follows from this localized survey.
