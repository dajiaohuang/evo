## *Fleischmannia hatschbachii*: open branches and white flowers {#s-4eaf08eae7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records open-spreading branches and ovate leaves with non-glandular hairs. Broad, panicle-shaped flower clusters carry heads on hairless stalks. The corollas are white, and the fruit-tips bear pappus bristles with cylindrical bases. These are the specific morphology fields recorded for the species in this regional dataset.
