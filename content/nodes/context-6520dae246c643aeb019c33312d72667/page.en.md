## The source concept {#s-92e8466b10}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Ameerega ignipedis as source row Anf1464, under genus Ameerega, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-8f4a0c0ba2}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 24.2 mm. For Anura, the compilation measures body length as snout–vent length (from snout to vent); it uses total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a maximum compiled from literature, not a measurement for every individual.
