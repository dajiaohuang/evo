## Form and flowers {#s-e1a1111635}
The regional account describes a cormous geophyte 8–18 cm tall, with woody, overlapping corm tunics and ribbed leaves. Flowers are violet with a red centre and cupped tepals; the stigmas are broad and villous.

## Diagnostic comparisons {#s-759273597b}
The SANBI account notes that *Geissorhiza mathewsii* has been confused with *G. eurystigma*. Both are described as having similarly coloured deep-blue flowers with red centres and broad style branches, as well as leaves with many ribs, but their flower structures differ. In *G. mathewsii*, the perianth tube is 2–3 mm long; the anthers are articulated on the filaments, lie almost horizontally, and are held above the broad, flat style branches. *G. eurystigma* has a 6–9 mm perianth tube and a long style whose broad branches are held above upright anthers. Its style branches are conspicuously hairy, whereas those of *G. mathewsii* appear smooth to the naked eye.

The account says *G. eurystigma*'s distinctive flower colouring also occurs in *G. radians*. It distinguishes *G. radians* by pitted tepal markings, narrower leaves up to 1.5 mm wide with two-grooved blades, and a longer perianth tube 6–8 mm long.
