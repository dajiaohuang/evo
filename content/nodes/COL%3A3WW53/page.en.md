## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Mabea pulcherrima? {#s-8383f98150}
The elliptic leaves have tapering tips and blunt or rounded bases. Their margins are entire. The lower surface may be hairless or hairy only in its central area, and has glands just inside the margin; the upper surface is hairless. Simple flower clusters have umbrella-shaped subclusters with three male flowers each. The bracts bear hairless glands on short stalks. Female flowers have six sepals longer than the ovary. The smooth fruit is over 15 mm long, and the style column is over 10 mm.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3d17fa038b}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
