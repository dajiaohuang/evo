## Accepted name and placement {#s-68a426a014}
COL26.8 records Craugastor montanus (Taylor, 1942) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The placement refers to the release dated 20 August 2026; it is separate from the historical AmphiBIO classification.
