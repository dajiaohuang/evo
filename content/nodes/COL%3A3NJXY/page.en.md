## Hymenolepis indivisa {#s-7dce8c8123}
## Entire leaves and compact yellow flower clusters

SANBI describes a slender, few-branched shrub to 1.3 m tall, with threadlike leaves whose margins roll under. Small yellow disc-shaped heads are crowded in rounded clusters. A taxonomic comparison notes that it shares hairless heads and open, twice-compound clusters with H. crithmifolia, but differs in having entire leaves and smaller clusters under 70 mm across. The habitat record says sandstone slopes.
