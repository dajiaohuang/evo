## Detectability depends on rain, call recognition and low leaves {#s-f0badff346}
The authors described a rattling advertisement call with decreasing pulse rate and advised searches on nights after heavy rain among small leaf perches, including leaf undersides, 0.3–1.0 m above the forest floor.
