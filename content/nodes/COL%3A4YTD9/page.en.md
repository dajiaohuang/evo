## A terrestrial label in a regional source {#s-e50b83e76f}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Sphagnum sparsum Hampe as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is labelled “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

No locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage is given with this category.

This source category is the biological information included from the regional record; it does not describe the moss in full.
