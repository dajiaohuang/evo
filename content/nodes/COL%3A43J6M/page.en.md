## Minaria harleyi {#s-161b1dad79}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Minaria harleyi* as an accepted species of *Minaria*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records smooth, hairless branches and opposite oval leaves with rolled-under margins. The inflorescences are corymb-like, with 1–3 flowers, and the corolla is bell-shaped. This record contains morphology only; it provides no habitat claim.
