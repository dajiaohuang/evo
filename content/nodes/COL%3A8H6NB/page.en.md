## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-05fc7fc4d8}
COL26.8 records Flexiseps decaryi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-0ae7a9a939}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 43 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
