## A floating-leaved plant {#s-3b4472f789}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records adventitious roots (roots arising from non-root tissues), a globose rhizome (rounded underground stem), and stolons, or creeping shoots. Its leaves float on petioles (leaf stalks) and have rounded to ovate blades with heart-shaped bases. The flower description records bisexual flowers, 20–25 stamens, and pale-yellow petals marked yellow at the base. These regional descriptors do not provide a sampling frame for variation.
