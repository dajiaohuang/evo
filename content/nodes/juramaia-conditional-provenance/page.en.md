## Juramaia and a conditional Jurassic date {#s-d62ab0e733}
The partial articulated specimen BMNH PM1343B preserves skull, teeth and postcranial bones. Its original study assigned it to the roughly 160-million-year-old Tiaojishan Formation and recovered a basal eutherian position. The specimen came through commercial collection, so provenance, stratigraphic attribution and matrix choice condition that result. An unconstrained tip-dating analysis estimated an Early Cretaceous morphological age signal; that model does not redetermine the rock.
