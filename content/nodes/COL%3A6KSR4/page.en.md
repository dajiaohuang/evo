## Yellow ray flowers and a bristly fruit tuft {#s-8fd1be3333}
The morphology field describes a woody, branched plant with smooth stems and sessile, elliptic to obovate leaves whose margins are toothed. Each solitary flower head has yellow, three-lobed ray florets around a center of tubular flowers. Its dry fruit is hairless and prism-shaped, with a deciduous tuft of bristles.
