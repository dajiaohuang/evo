## Souroubea gilgii V.A.Richt. {#s-a14932e1b7}
## Long tubular nectaries beside dense racemes

Souroubea gilgii is a climbing epiphytic shrub or liana. Its large, often asymmetric leaves have small glands beneath. Dense racemes grow on axes 10–20 cm long, alongside tubular nectaries 7–15 cm long. The flowers are small, with five stamens; the corolla tube splits at flowering and its lobes reflex. The fruit was not seen in the cited account. The species is reported from low-altitude rainforests of Atlantic Central America, from British Honduras to Panama.
