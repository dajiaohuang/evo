## *Xyris natalensis*: shiny brown heads above wiry leaves {#s-34a2c37da4}
This tufted perennial reaches 0.85 m. Its leaves are narrow and wiry, up to 50 cm long, while the shiny, mid-brown flower heads are cone-like to oval and 10–28 mm long. The lateral sepals have a prominent ridge lined with hairs, especially toward the tip. The account says it flowers in summer.

It is common in swamps, on river banks and along lakes, according to the regional habitat entry.
