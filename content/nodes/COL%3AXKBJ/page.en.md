## Conchocarpus elegans {#s-76355fb365}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records acute leaf tips, sepals that form a ring after falling, and coherent pubescent anthers.

## Deciduous sepals and coherent anthers {#s-ca4d4c6a6d}
Leaves are alternate and simple or one-foliolate, with slightly expanded petiole tips and acute blade tips. The source records solitary inflorescences generally over 7 cm long between leafy branches, no evergreen leaf-like bracts, and unbranched pseudoracemose growth. Sepals fall away and form a ring. Flowers have two fertile stamens and a radially symmetric cream-white corolla; anthers are coherent and pubescent. The ovary is pubescent and the style connate. The fruit apex is rounded. The separate ecology field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
