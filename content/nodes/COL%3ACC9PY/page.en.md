## Five habitat categories kept distinct {#s-3d0e0280b3}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Thuidium tomentosum Schimp. ex Besch. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Each listed term is a habitat or substrate category from that source.

One row is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A separate row, “Epixila,” denotes a wood-associated substrate category.

Another row is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The source separately lists “Saxícola,” also a rock-associated category; both source labels are retained here.

Its fifth category is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. These labels are not collection sites or a complete range.

The underlying rows do not supply collection localities, survey dates, a global range or sampling completeness.

The five source labels are the only biological content in this regional record; they do not make a complete account of the moss.
