## The source tadpole concept {#s-15dc4a5a34}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Ameerega altamazonica. The source classification lists Ameerega as the genus and Dendrobatidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Ameerega altamazonica. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
