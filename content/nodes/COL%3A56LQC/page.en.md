## Evidence limits {#s-b149d1388f}
AVONET morphology values are sample averages, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from separately cited literature or weighing sources; the 15 measured birds must not be assumed to be the mass sample. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, and the geographic summary covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. Historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name support association but do not prove that species concepts remained equivalent through later splits or lumps.
The life-history values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 compilation. They are medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest reported record. They are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies; name and family matches support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly known, and cited publications have not been individually reviewed. Other sources are needed for diet, behavior, current populations and conservation status.
