## Wedelia regis {#s-a936cf5678}
## Hairy branches and toothed leaves

Flora do Brasil 2020 records a xylopodium (a woody underground swelling) and tufted or erect, cylindrical, ribbed branches with hairs ranging from shaggy to felt-like. Leaves may be sessile or petiolate and vary from elliptic or lance-shaped to ovate. Their tips may be acute or blunt, margins toothed or rounded-toothed, and bases blunt; both surfaces bear stiff hairs or felt-like hairs.

Flower heads form thyrsoid or paired-branched clusters on hairy to felted stalks. Ray florets are present, and the fruits have smooth surfaces. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
