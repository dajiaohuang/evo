## What traits are recorded for this tall, evergreen tree? {#s-0c7876a852}
The regional dataset lists no buttress roots or xylopodium. The stem exceeds 10 m; the leaves are evergreen, with more than five scale-hairy leaflets. Flowers are up to 5 cm long, with oblong buds and a bell-shaped, rusty-woolly calyx whose edge is lobed. No nectary is recorded on the receptacle. The obovate petals are not rolled. The staminal tube is cylindrical, constricted and up to 1.5 cm long; there are 45–170 stamens. The fruit contains abundant brown, silky fibers.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-55f0c892d3}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
