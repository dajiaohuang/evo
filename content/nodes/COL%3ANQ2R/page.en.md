## Bulbine navicularifolia: boat-shaped leaves {#s-beb2e23cf7}
A South African regional account describes a plant similar to *B. succulenta*, 250–500 mm tall. It has three extremely succulent, boat-shaped leaves that clasp the stem in their lower half and sheath toward the base. The lower leaf margins are fringed, and firm, straight bristles cluster around the base. Light-yellow flowers grow in a dense raceme. The ovoid capsule is about 5 mm long.

These characters and measurements come from a regional South African flora account cited to a 2013 publication. It does not independently classify the material as native, naturalised, cultivated or domesticated.

The account records gentle slopes with stony clay as habitat.
