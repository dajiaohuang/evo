## Urokodia’s appendages and its proposed chelicerate position {#s-c181923999}
Micro-CT reconstructions of Chengjiang Urokodia aequalis show pincer-like short great appendages, overlapping exite flaps and a head with seven segments. The study interprets this combination as a possible bridge toward chelicerae and book gills and places Urokodia high on the chelicerate stem. Appendage homology and that position depend on reconstruction and phylogenetic scoring, so they remain hypotheses rather than a settled ancestor sequence.
