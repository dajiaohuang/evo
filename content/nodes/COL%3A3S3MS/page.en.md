## Yellow petals with a scarlet or copper reverse {#s-cbb0aad4f8}
The stiffly branched shrublet grows upright to 30 cm and rarely roots at its nodes. Its rough leaves are shortly fused at the base and 12–25 mm long. One to three flowers have yellow petals with a scarlet or copper-colored reverse. Sandy flats and slopes at 10–1,100 m are listed as habitat.
