## Restio sporadicus {#s-e67cd59615}
## A low-growing plant of seasonally wet coastal flats

The regional account describes Restio sporadicus as a dioecious perennial—male and female flowers occur on separate plants—up to 25 cm tall. It spreads by stolons, with branched, bending stems that bear fine tubercles. Sandy coastal flats, often seasonally wet, are the recorded habitat.
