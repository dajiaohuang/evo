## Bonatea boltonii {#s-0b8cd3177a}
The SANBI account describes Bonatea boltonii as a terrestrial orchid with green-and-white flowers. Its identification rests on floral details: the lower petal lobes spread horizontally while the lateral lobes of the lip descend. Those features help distinguish it from B. antennifera and B. volkensiana in the account. It is recorded from grassland, savannah and dunes.
