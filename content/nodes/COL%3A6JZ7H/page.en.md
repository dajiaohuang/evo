## Gaylussacia cinerea Taub. {#s-1b9173b6da}
## Strongly rolled leaf edges and woolly surfaces

Gaylussacia cinerea is described with upright or columnar shoots, sometimes arising from a prostrate base. Its leathery leaves do not overlap; they may be narrowly elliptic, obovate or oblong. The margins are strongly rolled under, sometimes nearly enclosing the blade, and both surfaces may be woolly or velvety. Axillary racemes bear a few flowers with white, tubular to bell-shaped corollas. The fruit may be ovoid or flattened and globose, with ribs and a hairy to woolly surface. The source lists alternatives for several features without selecting one.
