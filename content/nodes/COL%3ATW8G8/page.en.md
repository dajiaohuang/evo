## An erect stem and two leaflets in the source record {#s-27bc7a0491}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a woody root without a xylopodium and an erect stem. Leaves are bifoliolate, the inflorescence spike like, and flowers papilionaceous. Loment articles are listed as with or without prickles; seeds are brown. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields preserve alternatives and do not establish variation or a complete range.
