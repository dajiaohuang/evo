## Securidaca diversifolia {#s-b49e3ecfd7}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Securidaca diversifolia* as an accepted species. A historical Flora of Panama account describes a climbing shrub or woody vine with leaves that vary in shape and size; its flowers are pink or purple. The samara (winged fruit) is noted as not seen at maturity. The distribution field gives a historical range statement from Mexico to Peru and the Lesser Antilles, not a current or global inventory.
