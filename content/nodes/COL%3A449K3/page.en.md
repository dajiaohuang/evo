## Monotagma juruanum {#s-e1c66480e9}
## Green bracts that do not expand at flowering

The flora record notes no root tubers and symmetric leaves with a green midrib; the lower leaf surface is hairy, and the pulvinus has no rings. Terminal flowering is recorded, with a peduncle longer than 2.5 cm and two to nine nodes in the synflorescence, the combined flowering axis. The bracts are green but are recorded as not expanded at anthesis. The outer staminode is obovate, and the habitat is labelled terrestrial.
