## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a4bd49197d}
COL26.8 records Ctenotus aphrodite as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-360a7034ab}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 72 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4b62db6a62}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-87c20f3204}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
