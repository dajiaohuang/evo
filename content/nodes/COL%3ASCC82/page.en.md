## Andean grass and vegetation encounters {#s-18f88bc81a}
The revision reported Azuay and Cañar localities in Ecuador at 2895–4100 m, spanning páramo, inter-Andean shrubland and western and eastern montane forest. Nighttime specimens occurred in bunch grasses, pasture and low vegetation up to 80 cm high; daytime specimens occurred in pasture or beneath rocks. These collection settings do not establish physiological elevation limits or identical activity across all populations.

## Calling in three sampled months {#s-e1291aec57}
Calling males were found at night on bunch grasses or low vegetation in February, July and December. The scattered monthly observations document recorded calling activity rather than continuous year-round breeding, and supply no observed egg sites or clutch-size estimates.
