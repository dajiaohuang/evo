## How does Caperonia regnellii differ in this record? {#s-abc87260ff}
It has rhizomes and a solid stem that may be hairy or prickly. Its lance-shaped or elliptic leaves have pinnate veins and either fewer than nine or more than ten side veins; simple or gland-tipped hairs are recorded. Inflorescences are bisexual, with one to five female flowers. Female flowers have five sepals; male-flower petals are alike, and the outer sepal surfaces are hairless.
