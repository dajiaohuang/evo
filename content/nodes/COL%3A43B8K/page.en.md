## Mikania duckei {#s-43af990a20}
## Twining branches and three basal veins

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, lengthwise-lined branches; their pith is recorded as either hollow or solid. Opposite, elliptic leaves have entire margins, wedge-shaped bases and acute tips. The upper surface is smooth and the lower surface shaggy; three main veins radiate from the base, and the blades are somewhat leathery.

Raceme-like flowering clusters bear stalked heads. Lance-shaped bracts occur at the top of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around the heads have either acute or blunt tips. The corolla lobes are smooth, tapered and as long as the tube. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
