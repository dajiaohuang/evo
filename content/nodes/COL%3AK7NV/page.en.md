## Baccharis scopulorum {#s-0bdec06cd7}
## Winged branches and terminal or axillary heads

The source lists winged branches and leaves that may be reduced to scales or fully developed. The leaves are sessile or nearly so, with three main veins; blades range from obovate to round and have entire margins. Flower heads may be solitary in the leaf axils or at shoot tips. The florets are described as either all tubular and male or all threadlike and female.
