## Myrsine cipoensis M.F.Freitas & Kin.-Gouv. {#s-8993379d53}
## Leathery leaves crowded near branch tips

Myrsine cipoensis has slender, smooth terminal branches. Its alternate, leathery leaves cluster near the branch tips and are nearly sessile; the blades are ovate or elliptic, with pointed tips and rounded bases. Resin cavities beneath the leaves are globose or arranged in short lines. Umbel-like inflorescences bear five-parted flowers, and the globose drupes sit on short stalks.
