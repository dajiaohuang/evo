## A reddish tuft on the fruit {#s-a04ddb3242}
The description gives a small shrub about 20–30 cm high, with opposite leaves and grey, woolly hairs on young stems. Its solitary flower heads have about 34 florets and a reddish-brown pappus, the bristly tuft on the fruit. The source marks habitat as unknown, while recording an elevation of about 855 m and vegetation zones.
