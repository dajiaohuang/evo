## Senecio riograndensis {#s-b647a9a74a}
## White wool covers divided leaves on the herbaceous stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an erect herb with leaves along the stem. Sessile blades range from ovate-lanceolate to lance-shaped, with ear-like basal lobes and deep divisions. Woolly hairs cover both surfaces; the underside is specifically described as white-tomentose.

Corymb-shaped clusters carry radiate heads with 16–24 involucral bracts. Yellow, strap-shaped female rays surround yellow tubular bisexual disc florets. The style branches end bluntly in a spreading crown of hairs. The ribbed dry fruits range from cylindrical to inversely cone-shaped and are softly hairy. Their white pappus (bristle crown) persists. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
