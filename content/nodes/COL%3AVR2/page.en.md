## Assay and sampling differences constrain comparison {#s-992f6f2144}
This Andinobates subset contains 6 GC-MS animals and 0 UHPLC-HESI-MS/MS animals. GC-MS uses full skins from the2014 collection series and relative integrated signals; LC-MS uses separately processed dorsal/ventral skins from the2022 Panama or2024 Texas series and reports qualitative detection rather than alkaloid quantities. The methods differ in sensitivity, retention-time resolution and structural annotation, so their compound counts and signal outputs are not directly comparable. ## Chemical detection does not establish a sequestration mechanism {#s-53d45075db}
The authors propose that dietary toxin exposure, resistance and delayed elimination may precede specialised transport/storage mechanisms of chemical sequestration. The sampled Andinobates records help compare chemical occurrence but do not measure elimination rates, trace each molecule to prey or reconstruct ancestral transport pathways. A skin detection alone cannot distinguish temporary passive retention from derived sequestration, and a non-detection cannot exclude every defence chemical. The proposed ancestral state and sequence remain hypotheses requiring tissue/time-series experiments and wider lineage sampling; they are not certified biological properties of every species in this genus.

## Reproductive function and ancestry remain hypotheses {#s-4c00fed4ef}
Protein-reactive hand glands motivated hypotheses about chemical courtship signals, but the study did not identify a pheromone, characterize secretion composition, test female responses or demonstrate olfactory versus transdermal delivery. It did not establish an adhesive function or a causal effect on ovulation or oviposition in these genus members. Inferences of origins and losses use a fixed, pruned earlier tree and a limited ingroup sample; alternative optimizations remain possible. Type II–IV similarities also leave questions about developmental relationships and gland homology open. These interpretations should not be converted into experimentally verified genus-wide biology.
