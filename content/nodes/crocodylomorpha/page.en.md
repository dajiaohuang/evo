## Carnufex anchors a Crocodylomorpha route {#s-d838be7d46}
The Crocodylomorpha route uses the Carnian *Carnufex* holotype as an early sampled occurrence and living crocodylian descendants as its navigation endpoint. Its 231–0 Ma display is a represented navigation composite: neither edge establishes an exact clade origin, a global first appearance or a direct ancestor chain.
