## ## A woody climber with purple flowers {#s-78ac5d3137}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes *Clitoria selloi* as a woody twining vine with pinnate leaves of three leaflets. Its pseudoracemes arise in the leaf axils or directly from the stem. The flowers are lilac to purple; cleistogamous flowers are recorded as absent. The androecium is pseudo-monadelphous, with the stamens partly joined. The pod is flattened, with woody, unornamented valves; seed form is listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
