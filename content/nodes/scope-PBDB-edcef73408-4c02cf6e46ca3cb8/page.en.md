## Mimolagus in the PBDB source path {#s-4bb05e7b3e}
PBDB records *Mimolagus* at genus rank in this route. The fixed working roster has occurrence guides for two species: *Mimolagus rodens* and *Mimolagus aurorae*. Recorded database parents organize these records; they are not a unique phylogenetic scheme or a measure of evolutionary importance. This batch lists non-extant fossil-species records only and does not establish that the entire group is extinct.

## What this occurrence guide does and does not cover {#s-6e4b4d7ac3}
These pages guide through the fossil-species occurrence records authored in this batch; they are not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological account. Record counts do not measure specimens, individuals, populations or sampling effort, and age envelopes are not origin or extinction dates. PBDB paths organize these records and remain distinct from COL classifications and phylogenetic hypotheses.
