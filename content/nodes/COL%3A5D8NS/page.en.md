## What one ecological field can tell us {#s-56b97f003a}
COL26.8 places *Zosterops poliogastrus* in Zosteropidae. EltonTraits 1.0 contains an older same-name, same-family record for comparison. Its taxonomy predates COL26.8, so the shared name does not settle later species splits or synonymy.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-cee9660209}
EltonTraits assigns relative food weights of 40% to invertebrates, 40% to fruit, and 20% to nectar, pollen and plant exudates. Its code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. The percentages standardize written descriptions; they are not measurements of what an individual ate. A category with no listed weight would not prove that the bird never eats those foods, and the plant-food group covers more than a single everyday label.

## Evidence limits {#s-930866f113}
Diet is the only EltonTraits field group interpreted here; foraging height and other ecological fields are not filled in. The food percentages are literature-derived weights, not direct measurements of intake, and the source work has not been reread here. The historical name match does not establish that EltonTraits and COL26.8 use identical species concepts. This account also lacks evidence for local or seasonal diet variation, current distribution, population size or conservation status.
