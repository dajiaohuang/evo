COL26.8 records Bryophryne bustamantei (Chaparro, De la Riva, Padial, Ochoa & Lehr, 2007) at species rank in Bryophryne (Holoadeninae, Strabomantidae), in the release dated 20 August 2026.

## Seasonal activity — AmphiBIO source {#s-6a282aa806}
AmphiBIO marks both wet-and-warm and dry-and-warm seasonal conditions. These categories compare activity with the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-b25cf2a036}
The compilation reports a maximum adult length of 23.4 mm. For an anuran, length means snout–vent length; the value is not a complete size range.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-8164184983}
