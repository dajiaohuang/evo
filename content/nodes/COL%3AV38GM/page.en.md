## Accepted name and placement {#s-4969147e52}
COL26.8 accepts Ranitomeya aquamarina Mônico, Koch, Dayrell, Moravec & Lima, 2025 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dendrobatidae (family) → Ranitomeya (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Daytime activity around water-holding vegetation {#s-eefb1ffff4}
The description recorded the species at one of four sampled sites near the Eiru River in Amazonas, Brazil. Most observations were in Phenakospermum guyannense clumps, with another record in an understory plant-held water cavity about 3 m above ground. Activity was greatest in early morning and late afternoon, extending through rainy days. These observations concern the surveyed locality rather than every population.

## Eggs and larvae in vegetation cavities {#s-9b46ba1818}
Researchers found eggs and tadpoles in plant axils and small tree holes containing water. Different larval stages and metamorphosing individuals in the same area suggested prolonged rainy-season reproduction, without confirming its full calendar. Eight observed adult pairs suggested monogamy, and male responses to playback suggested territoriality; neither hypothesis was established by a long-term mating or territory study.
