## A bifid palmar tubercle in an eight-male diagnosis {#s-0a40aedccc}
The diagnosis measured eight males at 25.1–27.6 mm snout–vent length, mean 26 mm. It combines a half-arm axillary membrane, basal finger webbing, toe webbing, a bifid palmar tubercle and moderate pectoral patches. Thin pale dorsolateral bands, rounded shank marks and lateral sacral stripes accompany a brown to greyish-brown dorsum; no reticulated dorsal pattern was reported in the diagnosis. This is a sampled male combination, not a measured female range or proof that an unobserved colour form can never occur. Calls measured for other complex members are not vraemi acoustic observations.

## Live holotype colours and preserved variation are separate {#s-b9dd229394}
The live holotype had yellowish head undersides, vocal sac and belly, orange limb undersides, webbing and discs, and a dull-bronze iris. The variation account chiefly concerns preserved specimens: brown to greyish-brown background, thin creamy-white or light-brown bands, and commonly two or four unconnected sacral stripes. Two rounded shank spots were described, near the heel and mid-shank. These accounts cannot be pooled into frequencies for a live population, and the diagnostic grey-bronze versus holotype dull-bronze terminology should remain descriptive rather than treated as distinct species states. Colour alone is not a certified identification criterion.

## One recorded locality beside an Apurímac tributary {#s-16e87bf566}
At publication, vraemi was known from La Mar, Ayacucho, Peru, at 694 m elevation. Individuals were collected at night on pasture vegetation about 50 cm above ground by a small stream flowing into the Apurímac River, near San Antonio. The regional vegetation category was Peruvian Yungas. A single locality and an observation in pasture do not establish a full geographic limit, preference for altered habitat or population tolerance of land-use change. The stream context and low perches do not identify a natural egg substrate, quantify larval development or prove that the frog is restricted to flowing-water breeding.

## Data Deficient was the authors’ dated recommendation {#s-d455c6578c}
Given the limited information, the 2017 authors suggested Data Deficient using the IUCN 2001 criteria. This is a published recommendation, not independent confirmation of a current Red List entry. The single locality, eight measured males and absence of a detailed acoustic account constrain available knowledge but do not measure extinction probability, abundance or trend. Nearby forest and other river valleys should not be added as confirmed populations from the species name or apparent habitat suitability. Wider surveys and correctly identified material are needed to evaluate range and conservation status.
