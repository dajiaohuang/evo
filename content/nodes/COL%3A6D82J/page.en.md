## Divided leaves, flowers and woody fruit {#s-917ed35f07}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records 5–10 pairs of pinnae, the side divisions of a compound leaf; these may alternate, nearly oppose one another or stand opposite. Leaflets along them are oblong to oval, with heart-shaped bases and rounded or notched tips. They are somewhat leathery, hairy underneath and nearly hairless above; the midrib is conspicuous on the lower side. The flowers are yellow, with hairless calyx and corolla surfaces; the ovary is spindle-shaped and hairless. The fruit is woody and elongated, straight or slightly curved, and dark brown when mature. This is one regional flora row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-00a1b17a94}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It supplies a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
