## Limits on the historical ecology fields {#s-930c07d879}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family reading guide, not a complete species monograph; the historical concept is not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized literature weights, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may borrow from a congener, so each value is not necessarily direct observation of this species. Genus/family typical values, avian strata without species-level support, and split records needing review do not support definite conclusions. Underlying references have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation require other evidence.
