## The source tadpole concept {#s-0dd51c4f80}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Astylosternus occidentalis. The source classification lists Astylosternus as the genus and Arthroleptidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Astylosternus occidentalis. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
