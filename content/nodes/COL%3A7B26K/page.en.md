## Stylogyne nigricans (A.DC.) Mez {#s-fa54cc548c}
## Papery leaves and papillose panicles

Stylogyne nigricans has alternate, simple leaves with entire, lance-shaped blades that are papery and smooth. Its panicles have a papillose surface. Five-parted flowers are borne on 3–4 mm stalks; the sepals are papillose along their margins, and the lance-shaped petals are punctate. The anthers split open lengthwise.
