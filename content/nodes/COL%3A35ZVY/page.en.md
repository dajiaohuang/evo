## Dieffenbachia paludicola {#s-e46735e2cf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field calls the plant acaulescent (without an above-ground stem) and gives leaf blades 50–100 cm long, over 10 but under 20 cm wide, with a length-to-width ratio of 5–7. Petiole length is listed both as up to 23 cm and generally over 23 cm, so both source entries are retained. The regional account does not establish population variation or full range.
