## The source concept {#s-7cc32e1bd5}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Cycloramphus acangatan as source row Anf1420, under genus Cycloramphus, family Cycloramphidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-486da5195f}
