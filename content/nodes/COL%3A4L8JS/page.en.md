## A tall shrub with dark purple terminal racemes {#s-7a8be0e31d}
The regional account describes a slender-stemmed shrub sometimes reaching 2 m, with leaves only on the upper stems. Most leaves are lanceolate-elliptic. Many uniformly dark purple flowers form terminal racemes; the side petals are entire and the pedicels hairy. The habitat entry records sandstone, clay or limestone slopes, often at forest margins. These account fields do not establish the full range.
