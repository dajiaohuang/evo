## What do its leaves and calyx look like in the regional description? {#s-3a706f828a}
Physocalyx major is recorded with oval, elliptic or nearly round leaves 2.8–7.2 cm long, without a hair covering. Its calyx is cylindrical and also lacks hairs on the outside. These are traits in one Brazilian Flora 2020 field, not a population-wide account.

## How is its parasitic status classified? {#s-bfa4c622e9}
The habitat field classifies it as hemiparasitic, a category for plants that partly depend on a host while remaining green and photosynthetic. The field does not name a host or collection site.

## What substrate category is also listed? {#s-a09dc09be1}
A separate record labels it rupicolous, or associated with rock. It gives no particular locality.
