## Adult tapeworm associations in a historical North American checklist {#s-27688dc3cf}
The 2023 review includes Amphiuma tridactylum among amphibian hosts with records of adult intestinal cestodes. Its named parasite associations are Ophiotaenia alternans, Ophiotaenia amphiumae. These describe compiled host–parasite records, not parasites measured in every member of the species. The scope is adult worms in wild continental North American herpetofauna; larval cestode records are excluded. An intestinal association alone does not establish disease severity, host mortality, zoonotic transmission or the species’ role in completing every parasite life cycle. Prevalence, intensity, examined-host denominators and experimental susceptibility are not supplied by this host checklist.
