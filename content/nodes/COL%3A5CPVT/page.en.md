## What distinguishes its smooth cylindrical leaves? {#s-430544303a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with dark sheaths that widen at the base. Their cylindrical blades are smooth, hairless and pointed. The flower stalk is listed in two classes, 20–50 cm and 50–70 cm, with one ridge. Its ovoid or obovoid spike bears 10–25 flowers.
