## Accepted name and placement {#s-7bccfeb996}
COL26.8 accepts Limnonectes fastigatus Stuart, Schoen, Nelson, Maher, Neang, Rowley & McLeod, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Limnonectes (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A Gia Lai provincial record {#s-e2e5251b13}
The 2025 paper reported a first Gia Lai Province record in Vietnam and cited earlier Kon Tum and Cambodian records. The new provincial observation is retained as the paper’s contribution, without treating its cited older range as independently re-examined here or assuming continuous occupancy.

## Rocky-stream observations at night {#s-66e4639925}
Frogs were found between 19:00 and 23:00 in rocky-stream water or on the ground, surrounded by mixed secondary evergreen forest. Survey air temperature was 18–28 °C and humidity 76–86%. These describe collection conditions and habitat use; they do not measure thermal tolerance or define all suitable habitats.
