## What the regional record describes {#s-d2ab0f2726}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes alternating, elliptic leaflets with a leathery texture and smooth upper and lower surfaces. It also notes a raceme, and pea-family flowers with an obovate standard and wings, a lance-shaped keel, and fine hairs on all three petal types. The fruit is recorded as straight. These are traits in a regional dataset row, not a survey of variation across the species.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-2ebbaa40d5}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 record labels the species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. The field gives a substrate category, not a collection locality or a complete range.
