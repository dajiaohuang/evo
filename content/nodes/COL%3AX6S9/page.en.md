## Group counts in six Boabeng-Fiema patches {#s-a4f603193b}
A 2019 ground survey at Boabeng-Fiema Monkey Sanctuary, Ghana, recorded 34 Colobus vellerosus groups and 602 animals across 6 forest patches. The team walked 17 transects for 71.05 km over two survey rounds. This is a site-level count from that survey design, not a global population estimate or a map of the species' full range.

## Comparing the 2007 and 2019 density estimates {#s-f2d467e226}
The article reports that estimated density at Boabeng-Fiema increased between 2007 and 2019 by 70%, or 5.8% per year. It also notes that survey personnel and methods differed between the two estimates. The comparison is therefore not a methodologically uniform time series and cannot establish a range-wide population trend.
