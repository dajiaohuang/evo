## Salacia crassifolia {#s-d83d48596f}
## Alternate leathery leaves above axillary flower clusters

Salacia crassifolia has erect stems and subcylindrical young branches. Its alternate, leathery leaves are elliptic or oblong with entire margins; the lower surface turns brown when dry, and its secondary veins are impressed. Small, hairless flower clusters arise in the leaf axils. The flowers are bell-shaped, with two-loculed anthers and a three-lobed stigma. The fruit is spherical. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
