## Survey records {#s-7d1804da1b}
Nolan and colleagues used camera traps in Cape Three Points Forest Reserve, Ghana, from January to April 2018. Four independent camera events at four locations confirmed the species’ presence during the survey; the cameras were deployed across 21 locations and recorded 778 camera-trap days. This is a site-level confirmation, not an abundance estimate or evidence that the reserve is connected to another population.
