## The source concept {#s-a3e52a56b8}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Arthroleptis tuberosus as row Anf0154 within Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. Its source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a current-concept match has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-93e3aa77bc}
The row flags both terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. The compilation does not specify their settings or seasonal use. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the deposited values are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-810de9cf99}
The compilation reports a maximum adult body length of 28.2 mm. For anurans, this field uses snout–vent length. The value is a compiled maximum rather than a range across individuals.

## Developmental strategy {#s-93724aa553}
The record flags direct development; larval-stage and viviparity categories are not flagged. These categories alone do not describe breeding location or care.
