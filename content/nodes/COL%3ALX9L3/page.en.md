## Accepted name and placement {#s-0cc210e845}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys lishuiensis (Wang, Liu & Jiang in Wang, Liu, Jiang, Jin, Xu & Wu, 2017) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Fengyang Forest Station records in Liandu {#s-d7f052bb4c}
The male holotype CIB 106669, formerly WYF00164, was listed from Fengyang Forest Station, Liandu, Zhejiang, at 1100 m. The monograph examined nine males and one female from that station and summarized known records from the type locality at 900–1200 m. This locality knowledge does not prove absence elsewhere, continuous occupation of the elevation interval or physiological tolerances.

## Nine males and one female in the measured sample {#s-cb99daad0b}
The diagnosis reported nine adult males with snout–vent lengths of 30.9–33.9 mm and one female at 38.3 mm. The female value is a single observation, not a range. Sex and sample size remain explicit, and the measurements are not universal limits or an estimate of population abundance.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-1bf2e39488}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys lishuiensis as without a vomerine ridge. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
