## Limits of the evidence {#s-71beef50c9}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical data guide, not a complete species account. Food and foraging percentages are weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of diet, intake or feeding time. Source labels have not been individually traced to the underlying works, and some mammal entries may refer to a congener. Local and seasonal variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
