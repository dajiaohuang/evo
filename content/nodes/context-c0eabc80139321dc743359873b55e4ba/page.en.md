## The source concept {#s-b1adfe48b5}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0016 records Allobates algorei in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat field: Ter=1 {#s-91cd6708df}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). The fields Fos=NA, Aqu=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-d0a93e6d47}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity). Noc=NA remains missing, not evidence of absence. These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Body_size_mm: 19.5 mm maximum adult body length {#s-a9325596c5}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=19.5 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
