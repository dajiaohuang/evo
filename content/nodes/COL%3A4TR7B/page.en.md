## Ruschia staminodiosa {#s-2087c36a77}
## Glaucous leaves and a recurved tip; habitat remains uncertain

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a shrublet that may grow prostrate or upright, with erect, much-branched forms reaching 15 cm. The three-angled leaves have recurved tips, a glaucous surface and measure up to 15 by 5 mm.

The flowers are solitary, purplish and about 25 mm across. The fruit has five chambers and a closing body. The same treatment marks the habitat as uncertain.
