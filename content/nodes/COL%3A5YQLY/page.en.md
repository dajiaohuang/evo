## Chrysophyllum flexuosum Mart. {#s-5e5753c938}
## Two-ranked leaves with a hairy underside

Chrysophyllum flexuosum has alternate leaves arranged in two ranks. The entire blades are oblong, oblanceolate or elliptic; the upper surface is smooth and the underside hairy. The leaves range from papery to leathery. Flowers are bisexual, with a tubular corolla and four or five floral parts; the stamens are attached along the middle of the corolla tube. The ellipsoid fruit lacks an enlarged, persistent calyx. The source records the seed as not laterally compressed.
