## Basal leaves and a dark-tinged flower spike {#s-fb9ad73a7d}
The regional description records S. lupulinum as a tuberous geophyte up to 30 cm tall. It has one to three basal leaves, often purple on the underside. Its rather dense raceme (flowers arranged along a central stalk) bears 12–20 dull yellow-green flowers, usually tinged dark purple; the spurs are 15–18 mm long.

## Moist, sandy ground {#s-88a860559c}
The regional habitat entry records moist, sandy slopes or flats. It names these habitat settings but does not supply a collection locality.
