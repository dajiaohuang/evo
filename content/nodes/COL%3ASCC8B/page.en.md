## Multiple substrates in Yacuri records {#s-d4bcee04c9}
The revision reported Yacuri National Park sites in Loja and Zamora Chinchipe at 3186–3515 m, in páramo and eastern montane forest. Late-afternoon and nighttime encounters included moss, mat-forming plants, tussock grasses, rocks, shrub branches and bromeliads. Daytime specimens were inside bromeliads. These varied encounters do not establish an obligatory bromeliad life cycle or equivalent use of every substrate.

## Three months of reproductive observations {#s-fc556e9d4c}
Calling males and gravid females were found in December, April and May; males called from low shrub branches. These records identify observed reproductive activity in those months, without showing continuous reproduction between them, egg-deposition sites or clutch sizes.
