## A bark-dwelling substrate label {#s-0913c01a38}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Cheilolejeunea adnata (Kunze ex Lehm.) Grolle as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a source-labelled habitat row.

That row is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

The field gives no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

The habitat category is the only biological content included from this regional row; it is not a full species account.
