## Chromolaena ferruginea {#s-a29febd421}
## Velvety leaves and a three-veined pattern

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Stems range from densely woolly to finely hairy. Opposite leaves may be lance-shaped, ovate or diamond-shaped, with entire, scalloped or serrated margins and three main veins. Both surfaces are recorded as gland-dotted and as woolly or velvety.

Corymb-like clusters or panicle-like groups bear cylindrical heads, either stalked or nearly stalkless. The bracts lie close against the head and are smooth. Corolla lobes bear glands; the dry fruits (cypselae) are obconical with short bristles, and their pappus bristles are rough. The habitat field says terrestrial; this regional record gives no locality or full range.
