## Three leaflets and a flower spur {#s-2ac5f0545a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records three leaflets, with oval to elliptic blades that are hairy. Their texture is listed as papery or leathery. The flowers are pink and have a spur, a narrow projection from the flower; the straight fruit has hairy valves. The source lists alternatives for texture and leaflet shape.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-dc62ef54bc}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 entry labels the habitat terrícola, meaning terrestrial. It does not identify a collection site or complete range.
