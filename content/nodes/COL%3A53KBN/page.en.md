## Syagrus pleiocladoides {#s-208509d445}
Syagrus pleiocladoides is recorded with a solitary, short or underground stem and green pinnate leaves. Its leaflets are evenly arranged along one axis and bend downward; the erect inflorescence may be spike-like or branched. Ovoid fruit has a hairy green to yellow-green skin; the seed cavity is reported as either present or absent in the dataset field.
