## The accepted name in COL26.8 {#s-9a6fcb0d74}
COL26.8 lists *Ceuthomantis cavernibardus* (Myers & Donnelly, 1997) as an accepted species in the 20 August 2026 release. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Ceuthomantidae (family) → Ceuthomantis (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## How to use this profile {#s-81e5ce52ef}
This profile brings together the accepted name, historical AmphiBIO traits and one row from a morphology table. They come from separate sources and do not amount to a complete account of the species or its current populations.

## A historical foraging category {#s-c16a7e4bc2}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1228 follows the compilation's Frost 2011 species concept and marks Ter=1 (terrestrial) as a broad foraging category. It gives no season or life stage for that label. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried forward from older compilations; the source material cited here gives no replacement value for this row. Treat Ter as a historical category rather than a current ecological observation. The revision notice alone does not show that this row is wrong.

## A broad daytime-activity label {#s-8ed2865255}
The same AmphiBIO row marks Diu=1, meaning activity during the day, under the Frost 2011 source concept. This is a broad period category, not a continuous record of the frog's behavior. Its match to the current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## AmphiBIO's compiled maximum {#s-ff0becca43}
For its Frost 2011 source concept of *Ceuthomantis cavernibardus*, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 32 mm. For frogs and toads in Anura, this field means snout–vent length. It is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual, and the historical name concept has not been shown equivalent to COL26.8's current one.

## One row in the morphology table {#s-a420c9789d}
Version 3 of the amphibian morphology database contains one row (Anura.csv row 1053) in the male category. Its family field says Brachycephaloidea, while the COL26.8 path above places the accepted name in Ceuthomantidae; the source table has not been mapped to the current concept. It lists snout–vent length (SVL) 28.8, head length (HL) 10.8 and head width (HW) 10.9. Other populated codes are ED, TD, IOD, IND, UEW, EN, FL, TL, Fin3DW and Toe4DW. The row is one measurement entry, not a count of unique specimens. Units are unconfirmed, so these values do not establish comparable body sizes or a sex-based diagnosis. ## Profile scope {#s-a39921df6c}
The Frost 2011 AmphiBIO concept and the v3 morphology table have not been reconciled with the current COL26.8 concept. The measurement row has unconfirmed units and does not identify a unique specimen; this is not a complete species dossier.
