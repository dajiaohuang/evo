## White flowers on upright annual shoots {#s-5f39ffa8be}
This account describes a small succulent perennial, 9–15 cm tall, with upright annual shoots bearing tiny papillae. The spreading leaves range from linear to nearly cylindrical and are minutely papillate. Flower clusters near the branch tips have white petals, sometimes washed with purple; the staminodes and filaments are white. It grows in grassland and disturbed places in the listed vegetation types.
