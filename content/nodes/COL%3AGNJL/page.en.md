## Aristea anceps {#s-646570d12b}
Aristea anceps is a low rhizomatous perennial with a flattened, two-winged stem and sword-shaped leaves. Its blue flowers sit among dry, rusty spathes that tear with age. The regional account separates it from the broadly similar A. abyssinica by its sturdier stem and longer, firmer spathes and bracts, about 15–20 mm long. It is recorded mainly on coastal slopes and flats; the source does not provide a broader sampling frame.
