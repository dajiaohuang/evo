## Source classification and concept status {#s-fddab39308}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3084 records *Afrixalus weidholzi* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-6cd8004a4d}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-e3b5f8abb6}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 25 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-fd5e7c3821}
AmphiBIO reports 80–180 offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring or egg sizes of 1–1.7 mm. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. Its size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-173dab8e0c}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
