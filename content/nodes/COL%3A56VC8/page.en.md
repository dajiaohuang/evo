## Three veins and a tuft of anther hairs {#s-9d97b45770}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field marks stem branching as absent and its cross-section as cylindrical; it separately describes scale-like covering on branches. The entry records three leaf veins and a conspicuous petiole (leaf stalk) longer than 5 mm. Oval bracteoles, small leaf-like structures beside flowers, are recorded as free rather than fused; the stamen filaments, which carry the anthers, bear hairs. A tuft of trichomes (plant hairs) sits on the connective, the tissue joining pollen sacs in an anther. These are descriptors from one regional source field; it does not establish their variation, life stage or diagnostic scope.
