## What features are recorded for Duguetia lucida? {#s-b7f02ceccc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic to egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and sparsely scaled underneath. Greenish flowers grow in branched clusters; the stamens are red. The fruit is pink, orange, or green and ranges from spherical to nearly spherical, with a basal collar and a smooth surface.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ba4918ce45}
The source classifies it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
