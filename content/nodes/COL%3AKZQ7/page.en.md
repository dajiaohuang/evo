## Accepted name and placement {#s-e984f4ccd2}

COL26.8 accepts Batrachoseps altasierrae Jockusch, Martínez-Solano, Hansen & Wake, 2012 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Plethodontidae (family) → Batrachoseps (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## How a regional reassessment separated nearby populations {#s-b13893c237}

The 2012 abstract describes a taxonomic reassessment of slender salamanders in the southern Sierra Nevada. The authors conclude that Breckenridge Mountain populations belong to Batrachoseps relictus, while northern Kern River populations previously treated under that name represent an undescribed species. They name B. altasierrae for southern Sierra Nevada populations previously referred to B. relictus.

## What the abstract says about the evidence {#research}

The authors report that a comprehensive morphological dataset showed the distinctiveness of populations from the Upper Kern River Canyon. They then state that allozyme and mitochondrial DNA sequence data support the distinctiveness of the newly described species. This is the study’s abstract-level summary; it does not give the measurements, specimen-by-specimen localities or support values needed to reconstruct those analyses.
