## Epidendrum apuahuense {#s-27b7deb2c1}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row, Epidendrum apuahuense has a pseudobulb and oblong to elliptic leaves near the stem tip. The extended flower stalk bears beige, ochre or brown flowers; the three-lobed lip has an entire margin and a lengthwise keel. No habitat claim is recorded in this entry.
