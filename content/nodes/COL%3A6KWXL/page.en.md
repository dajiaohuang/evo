## Habenaria tysonii {#s-dc17dd7689}
The South African account describes Habenaria tysonii as a terrestrial herb up to 300 mm tall, with numerous green flowers and a spur 12–14 mm long. It may be confused with the more widespread H. lithophila; the account distinguishes it by horizontally held flowers, a reflexed narrow median sepal exposing the gynostemium, twisted upper petal lobes, and longer lower petal and lip side lobes. Rocky hillsides are the recorded habitat.
