## Monnina xalapensis {#s-244c2da72a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Monnina xalapensis* as an accepted species. The historical Flora of Panama account describes plants from herbs to small trees up to 6 m, with leaves 3–10 cm long and purple or blue flowers in short racemes. The fruit is described as a glabrous, wrinkled-reticulate drupe. Its distribution field says higher-elevation localities from Mexico to Panama; this is a historical regional statement, not a current or global range.
