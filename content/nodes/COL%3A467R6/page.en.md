## What does the description record? {#s-ad5999e22f}
Nemesia fourcadei is described as an annual herb reaching 74 cm. Opposite, sharply toothed leaves grow on short stalks; flowers form short clusters that lengthen as fruit develops. The flowers are white with yellow-orange markings and a hairy raised palate. Their spurs are mostly 3–4.5 mm long.

## How does it differ from Nemesia lucida? {#s-1d1b63c6a4}
The source distinguishes it from the similar N. lucida by larger flowers (7.6–13.2 mm versus under 7.5 mm), longer spurs (mostly 3–4.5 mm versus under 1.5 mm), and broader capsules relative to their length. These measurements are the source’s diagnostic comparison.

## What setting does it occupy? {#s-11326ecb3e}
The habitat field lists grassy forest margins and sandy-loam soils. It gives no specific collection site.
