## Reading extinction through dates, rocks and surviving branches {#s-69ab1d3b4a}
At Meishan, dated ash beds bracket the main end-Permian extinction interval. At K–Pg, iridium, impact ejecta and radiometric ages link an impact to boundary turnover; a dinosaur tree analysis answers a different question about branching.

## End-Ordovician: ice, sea level and marine loss {#s-0112a56bb3}
Glaciation and falling sea level coincide with a major loss of marine diversity near the end of the Ordovician. Their timing makes them part of the explanation under study, but the fossil record alone does not reduce the event to one cause.

## Late Devonian: several crises over time {#s-ae058ba936}
Reef systems and other marine communities were affected by multiple crises across an extended interval. The record describes a sequence of losses, rather than one brief event at a single boundary.

## Meishan ash beds bound a short extinction interval {#s-4eac2a79f0}
At Meishan, CA–ID–TIMS U–Pb dates on ash beds bracket the main end-Permian extinction between 251.941 ± 0.037 and 251.880 ± 0.031 Ma—about 60 ± 48 thousand years in this calibrated section. The precise sequence anchors one locality; it does not make every regional collapse synchronous or settle the interacting causes on land and at sea.

## End-Triassic: turnover before Jurassic dominance {#s-cb31f91efd}
Marine and terrestrial ecosystems both show turnover across the end-Triassic interval. The change precedes the Jurassic dominance of dinosaurs; that sequence in time does not by itself describe every local cause or ecological response.

## Boundary clay and ejecta link impact to turnover {#s-eb6717ec14}
Iridium-rich boundary clay and impact ejecta support an extraterrestrial impact; high-precision 40Ar/39Ar dating places K–Pg at 66.043 ± 0.043 Ma and resolves impact and extinction within about 32,000 years. Chicxulub is identified as the source crater. This boundary-scale link does not mean every species disappeared together: survival and recovery varied among regions and groups, with Deccan volcanism contributing background stress.
