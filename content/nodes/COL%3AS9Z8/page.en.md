## A compared morphology and geographically distant reference {#s-8ebb31f2b1}
The ericsmithi comparison cites a sloping lateral snout, green dorsum with yellow-green spots, a pale lip stripe continuing along the flanks and strongly protruding nostrils for sabini. Discussion refers to earlier Kosñipata, Peru records, not a new sabini range survey. Its reference groups with an unnamed Ecuadorian lineage; geographic distance and a 12S comparison do not directly measure reproductive barriers or certify identities of all populations.
