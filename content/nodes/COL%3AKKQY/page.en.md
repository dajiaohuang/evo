## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Banisteriopsis acerosa? {#s-271099d260}
Opposite, narrowly lance-shaped leaves have no glands on their stalks, but bear stalked glands at the blade base. The upper surface is hairless and the lower becomes nearly hairless. Terminal umbels have two or three flowers; bracts fall by flowering or fruiting. Their sessile flower stalks are sparsely velvety. The calyx bears a red or brown oil gland, and the petals are white. Anther chambers are densely hairy; the three styles are equal and flattened from side to side. The fruit has no lateral wings, and its seed-bearing center is rough or cross-braced.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-579bdc1a75}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
