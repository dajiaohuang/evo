## Which leaf and stalk forms are listed? {#s-94b6343e12}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records two-ranked or nearly two-ranked leaves, with sheaths that widen markedly and may be velvety or fringed with hairs. The blades are listed as cylindrical, nearly cylindrical, or angular, with a smooth or cross-wrinkled surface. The stalk is listed as 5–10 cm or 10–35 cm long; its ovoid or ellipsoid spike bears 10–25 flowers. The source retains these alternatives rather than selecting one state.

The regional data row labels the species terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
