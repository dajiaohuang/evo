## Didymopanax macrocarpus {#s-4fc4b7ac60}
## Terminal umbels and hairy leaflet undersides

Its compound leaves bear three to nine elliptic or oblong leaflets with entire margins and rounded tips. The leaflet undersides are villous, or covered with long soft hairs. The terminal inflorescence has two to eight primary branches, with partial umbels. Flowers are yellowish or greenish; the ovary is bilocular or trilocular. The dry fruit is laterally flattened or three-lobed.
