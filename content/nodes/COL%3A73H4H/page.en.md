## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-fc14b166e2}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Micromitrium tenerum (Bruch & Schimp.) Crosby as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is a regional data entry with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial; it records a habitat category, not a collection site.

No collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage is supplied with the field.

The source-labelled habitat category is the only biological content included here, so this is not a complete account of the species.
