## Accepted name and placement {#s-95449197f7}
COL26.8 accepts Xenophrys dehongensis Lyu & Wang, 2023 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Xenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Dehong type localities and a conflicting summary {#s-efbba215f2}
The monograph described this species as new in 2023, with male holotype SYS a003860 collected at Nabang, Yingjiang, Yunnan, on 23 May 2015 at about 760 m. Listed paratypes included Hongbenghe at about 300 m, Tongbiguan at about 700 m and Qingping, Longchuan, at about 1470 m. Its distribution summary nevertheless stated Yingjiang and Longchuan at 300–800 m. The 1470 m paratype locality is retained as a source discrepancy with that summary. Neighbouring northern Myanmar localities were only expected, not confirmed records in this treatment.

## Seven adults in the new-species diagnosis {#s-0467765cca}
The diagnosis reported five adult males at 34.8–36.7 mm snout–vent length and two adult females at 45.7–46.8 mm. It described vomerine ridges and teeth, absent subarticular tubercles, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. Breeding males had dense black nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. These small type-sample measurements are not universal adult size bounds, and the May, June and August collection dates do not establish a breeding-season interval.
