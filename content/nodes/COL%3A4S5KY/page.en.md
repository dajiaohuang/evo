## Rhea placement differs between analytical methods {#s-6c508b6dc9}
Both rhea species enter the comparison. Two summary species-tree methods in Cloutier and colleagues place rheas sister to the kiwi–emu/cassowary clade, whereas concatenation can place them outside the remaining nonostrich palaeognaths. Marker comparisons and retroelements support the former interpretation, but high support alone cannot remove model differences. The discussion concerns lineage sorting during ancient divergences, not an extant rhea being another species’ ancestor.

## One sequence in a chicken forelimb reporter assay {#s-84cc4aa8cd}
Sackton and colleagues select candidate region mCE967994 in a TEAD1 intron, place bird sequences in a GFP reporter system and electroporate chicken embryos at HH19–20, examining them about 24 hours later. The Rhea americana sequence gives 2 strong, 1 partial and 10 absent-expression outcomes in 13 assays; the chicken sequence gives 11 strong, 4 partial and 1 absent in 16 assays. These are experiments on one candidate region in a chicken host, not wild individuals of this species. Reduced reporter expression neither recreates flight nor excludes other regulatory effects in the species’ natural context.
