## What does its leaf rosette and flower cluster look like? {#s-073aa21cf8}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes *Ctenanthe glabra* with an underground rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) and a basal rosette of oblong, uniformly green leaves. Its branched flower cluster is panicle-like, with one to four inflorescences recorded at a node. The entry describes tubular white flowers and a capsule that is green when young and brown when mature; the seed bears a yellow, plate-like aril (a seed appendage).
