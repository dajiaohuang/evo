## Baccharis jocheniana {#s-cd39fed88c}
## Scale-like leaves on winged branches

The species record lists winged branches and no woody underground base (xylopodium). The leaves are reduced to scales, with one vein and entire margins. Flower heads may be arranged in panicles, spikes or dense clusters; their florets are recorded as either all tubular and male or all threadlike and female. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
