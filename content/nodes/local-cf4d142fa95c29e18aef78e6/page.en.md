## The deposited caecilian record {#s-e91f9bac40}
In CaecilianTraits version 4, Caecilia marcusi is listed in the genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. The compilation keeps individual, population and species records separate. It has not yet been established whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept.

## Measurement material {#s-114f10c1e7}
The compilation lists nine measurement records for this source concept: six for individuals and three for populations, with none at species level. These are database rows, not a population estimate or proof of nine distinct specimens. Each row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-37616aa16d}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are recorded categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-736947e1a4}
At species level, the compilation gives 150.1 for its primary-fold count and 36.1 for its secondary-fold count. The decimal values may summarize records rather than describe one animal, so they are not literal fractional folds or evidence that every individual has the same diagnostic counts.
