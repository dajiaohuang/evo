## Eulophia speciosa {#s-508dca0f9f}
The South African account describes Eulophia speciosa as a terrestrial herb up to 0.9 m, with pale-green sepals and petals and a yellow lip. Its spur is very short, under 1 mm. The account distinguishes it from E. streptopetala by the lip’s flatter middle region; the recorded settings include bushveld, thorny lowveld scrub and mountain grassland.
