## Symphyopappus brasiliensis {#s-5b6dbab9aa}
## Elliptic leaves and terminal corymbs

The Brazilian Flora fields describe upright stems that may be glabrous or glandular. Elliptic leaves are petiolate, serrated and may be opposite, alternate or arranged in two ranks. Terminal corymbs have a glabrous or pitted receptacle and five tubular florets per head. The cypselae have five ribs and sparse bristly hairs; the pappus has two rows. Habitat labels include rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
