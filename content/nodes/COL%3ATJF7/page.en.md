## ## Many leaflet pairs and twisted pods {#s-054524938d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium and 14–40 pairs of oblong to oblong-elliptic or oblong-ovate leaflets, with an occasional value of 44 pairs. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stem, leaves and inflorescences. Terminal racemes are leafy or bracteose. The yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. The oblong pod valves twist at maturity and bear glandular and non-glandular hairs; the seed is depressed-obovoid. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
