## Crinum jagus {#s-c65b54f01d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records Crinum jagus as having a globose bulb, lance-shaped leaves and nodding or declined white flowers with a bell-shaped upper perianth. The flowers are nearly sessile, with pedicels under 3 mm. The dataset lists both aquatic and terrestrial habitat categories, without a locality or explanation of the two labels.
