## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-0c37168a12}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes angled branches with very short, fine hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent elliptical stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) remain attached. The leaf stalk (petiole) and central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; each leaf has two leaflet pairs. A stalkless, cup-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. Flower clusters are spike-like, the calyx (outer floral cup) is bell-shaped, and flowers are nearly stalkless. The fruit is described as flat, while the dataset leaves pod-wall texture and surface hair covering unknown. This is one regional field and does not establish variation or how widely the traits occur.

## Habitat category {#s-4719a21c89}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
