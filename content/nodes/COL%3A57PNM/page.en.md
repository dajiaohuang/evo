## Growth and flowers {#s-0d65830752}
Snijman's 2013 regional account describes a rhizomatous perennial reaching 400 mm tall, with many hard, spreading roots. Its numerous linear, succulent leaves usually curl loosely toward their tips; the membranous leaf sheaths have irregular fringes. White flowers, yellow-spotted at the base, grow in a widely branched panicle. The erect capsules are ellipsoidal and reach about 8 mm long.

These traits come from a regional species account published in 2013; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
