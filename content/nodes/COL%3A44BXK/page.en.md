## White-woolly leaf undersides and cream flowers {#s-3d2a017624}
The morphology record describes alternate, stalked leaves with contrasting surfaces: the lower side is woolly and the upper side smooth. The oval to narrow-elliptic leaves taper toward the base, and the branches are grooved and woolly when young, becoming nearly smooth. Terminal corymb-like clusters carry stalked, bell-shaped heads with cream-colored female or bisexual florets. The ribbed fruits are silky-haired and bear bristles in two or three rows.
