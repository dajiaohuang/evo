Ferrodraco lentoni is the type species, based on partial skeleton AODF 876. The genus name refers to ironstone preservation, while the species name honors an individual; neither name establishes diet or behavior. The recorded PBDB hierarchy places Ferrodraco within Tropeognathinae.

## Ferrodraco and Haliskia: preserved material and span estimates {#s-28916cb7bd}
A wingspan of about four metres is reconstructed for Ferrodraco lentoni; the fossil does not preserve a complete wing for direct measurement. Haliskia peterseni has a different set of preserved bones, and its span is also reconstructed, so the two estimates do not rank complete body size. The 2019 and 2024 phylogenetic analyses use different matrices and taxon definitions. Their results do not establish direct ancestry, and one species' hyoid anatomy or feeding hypotheses cannot be assigned to the other.
