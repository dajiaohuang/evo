## Cereus pierre-braunianus {#s-4155f7a829}
## Prominent ribs frame a long, smooth flower tube

The source describes non-succulent roots and a trunk that branches above ground. Its upright, green branches carry five to seven prominent ribs; the wood is recorded as white to brown. Flowers measure 8.5–13.5 cm and have smooth tubes. The greenish-to-pink fruit opens along one slit and sheds the remaining perianth. The habitat is labeled rock-associated, without a collection locality.
