## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-8581e06e95}
COL26.8 records Nessia didactyla as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-47847a9e22}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 86 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-dd1ba68c3a}
The active-time field classifies activity as active across day and night (Cathemeral); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
