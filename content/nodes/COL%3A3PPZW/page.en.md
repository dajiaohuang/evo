## ## Angular branches and finely hairy pods {#s-0abc3db37c}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records angular, softly hairy branches with lenticels—small openings in the bark. The stipules, small appendages at the leaf base, fall early and are listed as lance-shaped or elliptic. The leaf stalk (petiole) is edged or winged, while the central leaf axis (rachis) is winged; the field records four or five pairs of leaflets. It also lists sessile (stalkless), dish-shaped nectaries (nectar-producing glands). The spike-like flower cluster has a tubular calyx (the outer floral cup), and the flowers lack individual stalks. The fruit is described as having woody pod walls (valves), a linear form and a fine, short-hair covering. A separate habitat field labels it terrestrial, without a locality or sampling frame. The regional description does not establish full range or variation.
