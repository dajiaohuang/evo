## *Xyris brevifolia*: four bracts around a small spike {#s-4d233b6846}
The leaves are arranged in two ranks and have broad, sometimes ear-lobed bases. A small ligule—the thin flap where blade meets sheath—is present but inconspicuous. The stalk is 5–50 cm long; its nearly spherical spike has four sterile bracts and up to ten flowers.

The source categorizes it as terrestrial.
