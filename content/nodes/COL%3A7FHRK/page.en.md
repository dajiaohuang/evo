## Overview {#s-7a3fe1d3a5}
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID 7FHRK. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Black-and-white Ruffed Lemur. The field evidence summarized here comes from Mangevo, a primary rainforest site in southeastern Madagascar, and concerns two adjacent communities studied at particular times.

## Seasonal foraging in neighboring groups {#s-7a0f8ad12c}
Beeby and Baden collected 29 months of behavioral observations from two neighboring groups across two periods: 2007–2008 and 2017–2018. They compared feeding, activity and canopy use between fruit-abundant and fruit-lean seasons. The results describe these groups and periods, not the species across its range.

## Seasonal feeding {#s-bd03c10503}
Fruit made up 74% of the reported diet, and feeding occupied about 30% of time year-round, mostly in the middle canopy at 11–20 m. During fruit-lean months, fruit consumption fell and leaf consumption rose; the communities also travelled less, rested more and used higher canopy strata. Dietary diversity was reported across 98 plant taxa in abundant seasons and 70 in lean seasons.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-c12833eccf}
These measurements describe two neighbouring communities at one primary-rainforest site, with observed interannual variation. They do not establish a species-wide diet, current abundance, population trend or conditions at other sites.
