## Name and trait overview {#s-574a500065}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts *Myuchelys bellii* in Chelidae. ReptTraits v1-2 compiles reported traits for this species under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and retains citations by trait. The chapters below describe the compiled record; they do not imply that every population, sex or life stage shares the same characteristics.

## What the length record measures {#s-79ff89dd62}
The row reports maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 300 mm; female maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 300 mm; male maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 227 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding head, neck and tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Activity and diet labels {#s-318e8d44bc}
The row records microhabitat as Aquatic; active time as Crepuscular (around dawn and dusk). These are broad literature-derived labels; they indicate reported patterns, not a complete habitat, diet or year-round activity account, and a species-level row cannot show differences among localities, seasons or life stages.

## Reproductive fields and summary values {#s-c90ba82cf5}
The row records oviparous reproduction (egg-laying); sex determination as genotypic (XX/XY); consolidated mean of offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 15.5. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-88a9d4c7b0}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 15.5 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 69 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-46da0f5d2d}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 26.5 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.
