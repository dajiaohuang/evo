## Form in the flora record {#s-b62e4c807a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a basal stem with no branches or only a few, and an erect aerial habit usually below 1 m. Petioles are up to 23 cm long; their length is one quarter of or less than half the blade length. The sheath-to-petiole ratio is below half or above half. Petioles are uniformly green and smooth.

The blade is elliptic or lanceolate, about 15 cm or 20–40 cm long, and 3.5–4 cm, 7–15 cm, or under 10 cm wide. Its length-to-width ratio is 3.5–4.5. The upper surface is uniformly or dark green; the base is acute or blunt, and secondary veins meet the midrib at less than 80°.

Several inflorescences are reported per sympodium. The peduncle is usually under 8 cm long. The spathe is usually under 10 cm long, with a spathe-to-peduncle length ratio above 1.0; when mature, it is pale green to green.

The regional dataset row cites Mayo's 2020 Dieffenbachia treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
