## Tiktaalik’s wrist belongs to a wider anatomical mosaic {#s-f2e1213ecd}
Fram Formation material of Tiktaalik roseae combines scales, fin rays and fish-like jaws with a shortened skull roof, a mobile neck, a modified ear region, robust overlapping ribs and a functional wrist joint. The holotype and referred material together document a body plan in which aquatic fish features coexist with changes in the head and paired fins.

A functional wrist joint is an anatomical observation; what it meant for support or movement is an interpretation. The mosaic and depositional setting support hypotheses about shallow marginal habitats, but do not show habitual walking on land. Intermediate character combinations also do not make Tiktaalik a direct ancestor of later tetrapods.
