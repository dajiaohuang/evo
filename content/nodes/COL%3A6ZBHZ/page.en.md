## White leaf undersides and blue-violet florets {#s-db721aaad6}
This account describes an erect or partly reclining herb, with silky stems that may be purplish where exposed. Elliptic leaves have sharply pointed, toothed edges, a silky upper surface and a conspicuous white-woolly underside. Flower clusters contain many small groups of one-flowered heads, each with a blue-violet corolla. The smooth, resin-dotted fruits bear a two-row pappus of translucent scales. The recorded range extends from Belize to Venezuela and Panama.
