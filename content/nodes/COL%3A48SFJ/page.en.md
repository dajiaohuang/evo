## Accepted name and classification {#s-a400e47448}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Odorrana geminata* Bain, Stuart, Nguyen, Che & Rao, 2009 as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Odorrana (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## Measurements retained by sex in Amphibian morphology {#s-ab31f97da3}
The Amphibian morphology dataset files the record under *Odorrana geminata*. The matching name offers a route to comparison, but the source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to COL26.8. The compilation retains 2 rows and 12 populated trait columns for this source concept. Recorded sex codes are F, M: F and M denote the source female and male categories; J denotes juvenile material, and NA leaves the category unspecified. These are compiled rows rather than unique specimen counts. Values from separate sexes, life stages and publications must not be pooled into a single diagnostic size. Missing cells do not establish absence of an anatomical structure.
