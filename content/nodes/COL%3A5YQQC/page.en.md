## Chrysophyllum splendens Spreng. {#s-25135a9f2b}
## Membranous leaves and cup-shaped flowers

Chrysophyllum splendens has alternate, two-ranked leaves with entire blades that are lanceolate, oblanceolate or elliptic. The upper surface is smooth and the underside hairy; the leaves are membranous. Its bisexual flowers have five sepals, five corolla lobes and five stamens attached along the middle of the corolla tube. The corolla is cup-shaped. The ellipsoid fruit lacks an enlarged, persistent calyx, and the seed is not laterally compressed.
