## Setose young growth and short horned capsules {#s-cceb024d12}
The source description records a low subshrub to 20 cm tall, setose on young parts. Leaves are fascicled, linear and rigid, with spine tipped apices. Flowers are sessile, axillary and pink. Capsules are hispid and shortly horned. The ecology field gives rocky slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
