## A mixed field-and-literature inventory in northern Sichuan {#s-710010d5e2}
In the 2025 paper, the name *Amolops lifanensis* appears in the Tangjiahe National Nature Reserve inventory in northern Sichuan, China. The full list contains 25 species: 20 from field surveys and 5 from literature. The field survey searched for adults on rain-free nights from May to August 2024 along 39 transects, each 200–500 m long and 5 m wide, with five trained observers. ## Regional gradients and survey gains do not certify recovery {#s-4ca8f7f0c8}
The reserve description spans 1,150–3,830 m, while sampling methods cover 800–2,600 m and the statistical analysis uses amphibian observations from 800–2,500 m. These study-wide ranges should not be read as the elevation range of *Amolops lifanensis*. Species richness peaks in two regional elevation bands, but its linear association with elevation was not significant (R² 0.114, p 0.412). The reported increase in recorded species over time was also not significant (Mann–Kendall p 0.148). These results do not show whether populations remained stable or grew, or isolate a management effect. The paper’s discussion makes stronger claims about fragmentation, conservation success, and large populations than these tests establish; the underlying observations and comparability of historical sampling remain unresolved.
