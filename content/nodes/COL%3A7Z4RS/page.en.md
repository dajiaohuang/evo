## Spondias expeditionaria {#s-fad180010b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description focuses on leaflet alternatives and records a globose to depressed-globose fruit with a lenticellate outer layer.

## Leaflet forms and a lenticellate fruit surface {#s-9c29795c41}
The regional description records non-tuberous roots and fissured bark. Its leaf rachis is densely pubescent; lateral leaflets are lanceolate, oblong-lanceolate or oblanceolate. Their margins are serrate or crenate, and their bases cordate or truncate, with symmetry recorded as either symmetric or asymmetric. The margin is revolute, the lower midrib prominent, and glandular hairs are present on the blade; basal leaflet insertion is symmetric, and intersecondary veins run perpendicular to secondary veins. The fruit has an endocarp not compressed from the sides, a lenticellate exocarp, and a globose or depressed-globose outline. This single Brazilian Flora field does not establish population variation or a global description.
