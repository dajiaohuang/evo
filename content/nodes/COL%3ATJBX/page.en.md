## Leaflet size and a pear-shaped seed {#s-f3c9f06a29}
The flora description records eight to 18 pairs of oblong to oblong-elliptic leaflets, each 0.9–3 cm long and 0.3–0.9 cm wide. Yellow flowers appear in clusters at branch tips and beside the leaves. Four larger, fan-shaped petals contrast with a smaller inner petal that encloses the stamens; ten stamens are listed. The mature pod is narrow and oblong, with twisting sides, while the flattened pear-shaped seeds are glossy brown to very dark brown. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
