## Xyris malmeana {#s-670f1e7286}
## Raised ridges on the flower stalk

The flora record describes flat, smooth leaf blades and a flowering stalk 20–70 cm long with more than two raised ridges. Small papillae cover those ridges. The egg-shaped to ellipsoid spike has four sterile bracts and up to ten flowers; the habitat is labelled terrestrial.
