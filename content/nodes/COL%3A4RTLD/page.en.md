## Restio scaberulus {#s-768978bb29}
## Rough stems in moist Fynbos slopes

The regional account describes Restio scaberulus as a tufted perennial up to 2 m, with densely tuberculate stems and branches arranged in whorls. Its spikelets may be solitary or paired. It is recorded from moist slopes with sandy soils, including stream margins in riverine forest, at 150–550 m in the Eastern Fynbos–Renosterveld region.
