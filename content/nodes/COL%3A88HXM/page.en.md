## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-10156c6d57}
COL26.8 records Leiopython fredparkeri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pythonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-17b1c3aba8}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1760 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Seymor 1987.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ea6651aa85}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a12ba3b699}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-5d687f45c4}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
