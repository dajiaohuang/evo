## Kniphofia ritualis {#s-e9ae8ead55}
Kniphofia ritualis has soft, deeply keeled leaves and a dense raceme of pendulous greenish-yellow flowers. Coral or salmon buds turn dull orange-red; the regional account distinguishes it from K. sarmentosa by its regularly serrulate leaves and longer flowers. It is recorded in shallow sandstone soils, rock cracks, dry watercourses and grassy slopes.
