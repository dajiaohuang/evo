## Five calyx lobes and a stalked ovary in the entry {#s-bea3a77fe5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records deciduous stipules and bilobed leaf blades on flowering or fruiting branches. Young buds have an unwinged calyx tube and are fusiform. At anthesis the calyx is deeply divided into five lobes, each with a minute apiculum. The upper petal is obovate to oblong, its outer surface is silky, and the anthers are oblong to linear. The ovary is stipitate and the fruit is dehiscent. No habitat field is included, so the description does not establish habitat or range.
