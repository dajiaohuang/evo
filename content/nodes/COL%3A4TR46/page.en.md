## Ruschia obtusa {#s-ab9cf563aa}
## Broad grey-green leaves and hook-shaped fruit closures

Buys’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a shrub about 40 cm tall, with blackish-brown internodes. Its smooth, pale grey-green leaves have a raised keel and measure 20–45 mm by 5–8 mm.

One to three magenta flowers grow in each cluster, about 18 mm across. Small bract-like leaves (bracteoles) form a pouch around the flower base; the petals are about 1.5 mm wide, and thread-like sterile stamens (staminodes) are absent. The six-chambered fruit has a short funnel-shaped base, a raised top, low rims and a hook-shaped closing body. The habitat account records rocky places with shallow soils.
