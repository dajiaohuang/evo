## Strelitzia juncea {#s-a6e8057112}
## Orange sepals and blue petals

The SANBI species entry describes a perennial herb up to 2 m tall with an underground stem. Its oblong-lanceolate leaves measure about 220 × 76 mm and have slender petioles. The green spathe has red edges; the sepals are bright orange and about 100 mm long, while the petals are blue. The entry gives a July–November flowering period and places the plant on gentle hillsides in Albany Thicket and Eastern Fynbos-Renosterveld. These are the source’s regional habitat descriptions, not a complete range statement.
