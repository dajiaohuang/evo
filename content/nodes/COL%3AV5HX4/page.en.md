## Accepted name and classification {#s-6219bd84c8}
In the 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release, Phyllonastes arutam (Brito-Zapata, Chávez-Reyes, Pallo-Robles, Carrión-Olmedo, Cisneros-Heredia & Reyes-Puig, 2024) is an accepted species. Its recorded classification path is Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Phyllonastes (genus).

## Reading the source records {#s-a736190559}
The catalogue name is accompanied by selected observations or compiled values from named sources. Each result remains tied to the source concept and any stated sample, place, period, and method; together they do not make a complete account of the species.

## The original name and fixed catalogue combination {#s-50f004d01f}
The 2024 original description names the Río Blanco lineage Noblella arutam and designates female ZSFQ 1882 as the holotype, the single name-bearing specimen. COL26.8 records the accepted combination Phyllonastes arutam. The paper places the lineage in its northern clade and treats it in Noblella; these are the source’s taxonomic and phylogenetic conclusions.

## Fourteen types have separate sex and measurement scopes {#s-9189533d9d}
The type series comprises one female holotype, the single name-bearing specimen, and thirteen paratypes, additional specimens included in the original description: five females and eight males. The diagnosis reports male snout–vent length (distance from the snout tip to the vent) of 13.8–15.1 mm (n=8) and female length of 17.3–20.3 mm (n=6). It separately gives the holotype's measurement as 20.34 mm; the diagnosis reports the female upper value rounded to 20.3 mm. These measurements describe the type series rather than population-wide size limits. The paper also reports nineteen animals collected and additional encounters released after capture; these counts have different sample scopes.

## Variable live patterns change after preservation {#s-63433467aa}
Living backs range from light brown through grey-brown to dark brown, with inverted-V or irregular darker markings; a faint pale mid-dorsal stripe may occur. The holotype has a dark lateral band, yellow-to-orange groin and yellow underside with white and brown dots. Descriptions of specimens preserved in ethanol separately record paler cream ventral areas. Some tubercles and folds become less apparent after preservation. The paper describes colour in life and after preservation as separate observations.

## One cleared male supports the skeletal description {#s-04754853dd}
The osteological description used adult male ZSFQ 1886, prepared by clearing and staining: soft tissues were made translucent and the skeleton stained for examination. The paper reports finger phalangeal counts of 2-2-3-3, toe counts of 2-2-3-4-3, eight presacral vertebrae (vertebrae before the sacrum) and terminal phalangeal knobs. The diagnosis describes strongly T-shaped terminal phalanges. Cartilage structures were omitted from the osteological description, and the poorly ossified prepollex was difficult to see. This description is based on one prepared male; it does not report frequencies of skeletal variation, female anatomy or a developmental sequence.

## Leaf-litter activity has local observation limits {#s-808a80b4ff}
The account records Río Blanco and nearby sites at 1720–1890 m in humid montane evergreen forest. Most animals were active at night on the forest floor; three were found in morning leaf litter and four paratypes in pitfall traps. These observations do not yield a complete daily activity budget. No calls were heard during expeditions even though the diagnostic males have vocal slits and an external sac; nondetection does not prove silence.

## A single stomach documents thirteen prey items {#s-0c82de6ee3}
The stomach of male ZSFQ 1886 contained three ant-mimicking rove beetles classified as Staphylinidae: Pselaphinae, two aquatic fly larvae labelled Diptera: Nematocera, and eight ants classified as Formicidae: Myrmicinae. The thirteen items describe one stomach, not a species-wide fixed diet, preference for ants or feeding rate. Their presence does not establish where each prey item was captured or how prey availability varies among seasons.

## A dated risk proposal separates occupancy from range extent {#s-d0fcec2987}
