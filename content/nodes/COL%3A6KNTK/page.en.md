## *Goeppertia widgrenii*: a petal-like callus on the outer staminode {#s-7df2c81e94}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes basal leaves with spiral arrangement; young leaves may be ornamented or plain. All flowering bracts are fertile and spiral, with upright margins, and each small cluster has two flowers and one club-shaped bracteole, a small leaf-like structure directly beneath a flower.

The corolla tube is cleft. One outer staminode, a sterile stamen-like floral part, bears a petal-like callus at its tip. The source is a regional morphology field and does not estimate how often either juvenile leaf state occurs.
