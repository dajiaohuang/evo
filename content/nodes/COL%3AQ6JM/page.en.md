## A bark-associated category in a regional record {#s-328c19e2f2}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Calymperes lonchophyllum Schwägr. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat row is a source category, not a collection location.

The row is labelled “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

The field gives no locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

This single source label is the biological information included in the regional record; it does not make a complete account of the moss.
