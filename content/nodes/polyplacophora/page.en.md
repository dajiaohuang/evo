## Thirteen new genomes expand the chiton sample {#s-b70a46c2ce}
A mitogenomic analysis adds 13 chiton genomes to comparisons within Polyplacophora. The added sequences let researchers test relationships among sampled lineages, while the mitochondrial tree represents one inherited genomic system.

## Deep branches need caution {#s-8918818ead}
Mitochondrial inheritance and uncertainty in calibration affect the deepest relationships and timing. This result expands the data available for comparison, but does not make every deep branch or divergence date secure.
