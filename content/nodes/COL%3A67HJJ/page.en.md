## A papery blade and a prickled petiole {#s-954c6f6722}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description gives short internodes (stem sections between nodes) and papery, oval to broadly oval leaves; their bases are recorded as cordate, sagittate, or subcordate. The leaf stalks (petioles) may be sparsely or densely prickled. The field describes the inflorescence tip as curved and reddish to brown and records five floral locules (internal chambers); it does not provide a sampling frame for how these forms vary.
