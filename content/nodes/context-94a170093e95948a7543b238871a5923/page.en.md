## Source classification and concept status {#s-066e295a44}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2912 records *Litoria revelata* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-87744f437e}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Night-time activity {#s-c95512bd33}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-38ab8b51b9}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 0.93 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 35 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size and annual reproductive output) {#s-bd72f3be2f}
The row lists 200–1,000 offspring or eggs per clutch and up to 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; this record gives no offspring-or-egg size, and does not specify the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind the counts.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-0a5d810276}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
