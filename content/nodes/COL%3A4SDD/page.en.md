## Selected isiZulu names in the regional compilation {#s-dabaa4bf22}
In its 2019 Zululand compilation, the study lists the isiZulu (Zulu-language) name Isele lasempophomeni for Hadromophryne natalensis. Species-level names were assigned after the workshop; they were not all elicited from participants as pre-existing names for individual species. The linked species page records whether its form was newly proposed or modified from Tarrant 2016. This is a regional name record, not a global naming authority or a biological diagnosis of the genus.

## Collective elicitation and scope of correspondence {#s-d0a3fea5af}
Thirteen native isiZulu-speaking volunteers from five Zululand areas took part in two workshop groups in 2016. Picture-assisted discussions identified six broad folk categories—everyday groupings that do not always match scientific taxa. Later, with assistance from Bongani Mkhize, the study assigned species names using those categories, published names and naming guidelines. Some broad terms came from earlier publications or borrowing. Because a folk category can span multiple genera or families, a name need not identify one scientific genus or species. These selected entries do not establish community-wide name frequencies, independent recognition accuracy, current local occupancy or ancestral habitat and call traits for Hadromophryne. The study documents language and naming, not measured biology.
