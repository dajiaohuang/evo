## Conchocarpus marginatus {#s-f53229a2d4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description pairs acute or obtuse leaf tips with a laxly branched inflorescence and yellow flowers.

## Lax branches and yellow flowers {#s-138d11e609}
Leaves are alternate and simple or one-foliolate, with slightly expanded petiole tips and acute or obtuse blade tips. The solitary inflorescence is generally over 7 cm long and positioned between leafy branches; evergreen leaf-like bracts are absent, and branching is lax. Sepals persist. Flowers have two fertile stamens and a radially symmetric yellow corolla. Anthers are free and pubescent; the ovary is pubescent and the style connate. The fruit apex is rounded. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a collection locality or complete range.
