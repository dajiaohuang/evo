## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-8fe5dd362a}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Psilopogon chrysopogon (Temminck, 1824) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Measured samples and beak/wing averages {#s-5797c297a8}
Morphology covers 5 birds: 1 female, 3 male and 1 unsexed; 4 have complete measurement sets. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 37.6 mm, beak width 15.8 mm, tarsus 31.5 mm, unflattened wing 124.7 mm and hand-wing index 18.8. Inference=NO does not establish an identical count for every field; complete-set birds and total samples remain separate. The index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed, and beak width is not a test of fruit swallowing or pecking performance.

## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields {#s-c999006a62}
Frugivore is the Metadata category for predominantly fruit resources; trophic level is separately Omnivore. The level spans trophic resources while the fruit-niche label remains; neither field replaces the other. Category thresholds are not measured diet percentages for a locality or season. Fruit species, food size, digestion and seed-dispersal experiments are absent here, leaving detailed diet and dispersal effects unsupported.

## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category {#s-d7ba5ad61a}
Insessorial means primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, without implying never descending to the ground. Habitat is Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is sedentary, without establishing lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted here, and categories do not establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.
