## Spathiphyllum cuspidatum: proportional and floral traits {#s-ac2507499b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records petioles in length categories of less than 35 cm, more than 35 cm and more than 20 cm. Petiole length is less than 1.2 times blade length. Blades are more than 3 times and less than 6 times as long as wide, and have more than 9 secondary veins per side.

The peduncle is less than twice the petiole length and less than 1.9 times the blade length. The spathe persists at maturity. The spadix is cylindrical, longer than 1 cm, and its length is less than 5 times its stipe length. The style does not extend above the perianth and is truncate or nearly truncate; tepals are free at the apex or completely free.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Hemiepífita,” meaning hemiepiphytic.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
