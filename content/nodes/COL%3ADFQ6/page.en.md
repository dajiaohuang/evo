## Anaphyllopsis cururuana {#s-1f23c3a261}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records three-part, pinnately cut leaf blades with rounded lobes, an aquatic habitat, and ovoid berries. The spadix—a flower-bearing spike—is listed as up to 5 cm long. These are fields from a regional dataset, not a complete species-wide description or distribution.
