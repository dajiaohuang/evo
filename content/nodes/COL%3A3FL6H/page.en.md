## Male and female flower records with fruit measurements {#s-e7b9a86fd8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records opposite leaves with entire or lobed margins. It describes terminal male inflorescences as cymose (flower clusters with branching axes) and the female inflorescence as one-flowered. The calyx is listed as campanulate, toothed, and glabrous; the corolla is funnel-shaped. Anther length is recorded as 2.0–2.5 cm. The fruit is a globose berry, 5.5–8.5 cm in diameter. Seeds are compressed and recorded as round, ellipsoid, or three-angled with rounded edges. These characters and alternatives come from one regional description field.

## Two habitat categories in the dataset {#s-59acc3a3a0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat rows list both rupicolous (rock-associated) and terrestrial categories for *Genipa infundibuliformis*. They provide no collection locality or further detail about these labels.
