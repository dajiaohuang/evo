## Coastal hills of eastern Fujian {#s-40c98c951d}
The description knew the species from the coastal hills of Ningde, eastern Fujian, China, at 150–510 m elevation. This recorded range reflects the study’s sites, rather than a complete contemporary distribution or altitude tolerance.

## Seeps, litter and calling crevices {#s-6add70056e}
Frogs inhabited flowing mountain seeps and nearby forest floor and leaf litter, surrounded by secondary forest mixed with bamboo. Males were heard calling in rock crevices from February to May. Those calling observations do not establish egg-laying dates, larval development or the full annual reproductive cycle.
