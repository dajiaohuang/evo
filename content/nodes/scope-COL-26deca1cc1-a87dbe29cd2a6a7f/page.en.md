## atlas-profile / overview {#s-74d938cdca}
The order Perissodactyla includes living horses, tapirs and rhinoceroses alongside many extinct branches. Fossils place its early radiation during the PETM, but the sampled record and analyses do not resolve the order’s exact origin.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-930fc79579}
The PETM occurrence study and broader fossil synthesis trace a branching history across northern continents. They do not date the order’s exact origin or describe one way of life for every branch.

## Different bodies on separate branches {#s-3f75ff2541}
Horses, tapirs and rhinoceroses look very different today. Early fossils can be distinguished by finer features of their teeth and skulls, while many extinct branches add still more variation. Together they form a branching record rather than a sequence leading only to the living forms.

## How early relationships are reconstructed {#s-3d7cb3ed91}
Small differences in teeth and skulls help researchers compare early branches. The study of earliest occurrences analyzes 71 terminals and 102 morphological characters; alternative basal placements show why the order’s first fossils do not settle its origin.

## Each lineage has its own history {#s-b918ae4686}
The order includes many extinct groups. Teeth, skulls and limbs changed along different branches, which later included horses, tapirs and rhinoceroses. A list of fossils arranged as successive ancestors would hide this branching pattern.

## Dispersal during the PETM, origin unresolved {#s-63d78c4df5}
The earliest sampled perissodactyl records fall around 56–54 Ma, during the Paleocene–Eocene Thermal Maximum. Their distribution supports rapid dispersal among North America, Europe and Asia, but does not identify the order’s exact place of origin.

## atlas-profile / readerLimitations {#s-27a0e8bb60}
The early radiation is reconstructed from sampled fossils and comparative anatomy. Basal placements depend on analysis, and the selected records do not identify direct ancestors, a precise origin date or a complete distribution.
