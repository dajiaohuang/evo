## How does Nemesia linearis grow and flower? {#s-3cb5ad82a3}
The source describes an annual 10–60 cm tall, with upright, branched stems and narrow leaves. Flower clusters are dense and rounded, becoming longer as fruit develops. Flowers may be white, pink, or mauve with purple veins; the yellow-orange palate and short, curved spur add contrasting markings. The entry gives an August–December flowering period and capsule dimensions of 4–14 mm long.

## What is the stated basis of the description? {#s-7451637027}
The source says the account draws on Ventenat’s 1804 Latin diagnosis and specimens identified by A. L. Grant as “N. capensis var. linearis” in an unpublished treatment. It points readers to the comparison under N. foetens; that source note does not add a separate field observation.

## What growing conditions are recorded? {#s-6dcef52abf}
The habitat field places it in dwarf Karroid shrubland, on rocky or sandy soil, often in streambeds and in full sun. This describes the listed habitat, not a complete range.
