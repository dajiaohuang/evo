## A stout plant with two ground-level leaves {#s-a14f61f0eb}
The regional diagnosis describes S. bicorne as a stout, tuberous geophyte up to 60 cm tall, with a stem wrapped in conspicuous tubular sheaths. It usually has two prostrate, oval to rounded leaves. A raceme (flowers arranged along a central stalk) bears 4–40 pale greenish-yellow flowers, with spurs 10–22 mm long.

## A scrub habitat record {#s-b289ff26aa}
The regional habitat field labels the setting simply as “scrub.” It does not give a precise locality or further describe the vegetation.
