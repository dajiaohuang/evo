## Yellow disk flowers in corymbs {#s-4aec7620a2}
The record describes an upright, bristly-stemmed plant with opposite, short-stalked, elliptic leaves. The leaves are pointed, toothed and rough to the touch. Crowded corymbs carry disk-shaped heads with yellow florets and no ray florets. The fruits may be smooth or bristly, and the pappus consists of separate, lance-shaped scales. The habitat field says terrestrial.
