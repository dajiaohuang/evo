## What traits identify Clusia nigrolineata in the record? {#s-f353366dd7}
It has a taproot and additional roots, with stalked, leathery, obovate leaves and conspicuous secretory channels. Pendulous flower clusters bear flowers with four to six sepals and five petals; the corolla is white with a red center. Male flowers have seven to ten separate stamens, resin-bearing stamens or staminodes, and a pistillode. The ovary has five chambers. The sepals, petals, and staminodes are not recorded as persisting on mature fruit; the seeds have an aril.
