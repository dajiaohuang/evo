## Sampled localities and voucher forms remain distinct {#s-3c9b481505}
The account retains LMR specimen USNM558530, initially called Leptopelis cf.notatus, and Republic of Congo specimen USNM576025. These are records from the2017 study’s sampled material. LMR denotes the Loubomo–Mougagara Road corridor in Nyanga, Gabon; the Republic of Congo is distinguished from the Democratic Republic of Congo. Museum reference samples are not automatically observations at LMR. Survey non-detection is bounded by effort and seasons, not regional absence or a complete species range. Original vouchers, contemporary occurrence and taxonomic identity have not been independently inspected here.

## Barcode-supported identification retains provisional boundaries {#s-8f65c2a4bc}
The two occur in separate COI BINs and ABGD groups. They are sister in16S to references labelled brevirostris, one of which is now the crystallinoron type ZFMK73139 and another lacks voucher information. The studied frogs’ conspicuous tympana contrast with the cited crystallinoron description, so the authors retain brevirostris. Two other Congo frogs initially assigned brevirostris remain Leptopelis sp.A and are not added to this species’ sample.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-f52b44ac8c}
For Leptopelis brevirostris, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
