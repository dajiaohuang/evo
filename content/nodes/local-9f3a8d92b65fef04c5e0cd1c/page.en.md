## How the source names this tadpole {#s-67334082c7}
TadpoleTraits version 4 groups these records under Alsodes verrucosus. The dataset authors use Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024) for its placement in Alsodes and Alsodidae; the underlying publications also use the name Alsodes verrucosus. This source grouping does not establish that the records match a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Stage records in the dataset {#s-eb8084305b}
The compilation contains one row from published literature and two rows measured from images. The listed stage records are 25–38, 38 and 36. Stage labels and any uncertainty are kept as supplied; a stage estimated from an image is not a direct developmental observation. Rows do not count distinct tadpoles, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.

## Proportions measured from images {#s-ad099a339c}
The image records give these proportions of total length: maximum tail height, about 0.177 (source row 35, stage 38); oral-disc width, about 0.168 (row 35, stage 38); tail length, about 0.617 (row 36, stage 36); maximum body height, about 0.195 (row 36, stage 36); and maximum tail height, about 0.178 (row 36, stage 36). Each compares one length with total length, so it has no unit; values are rounded to three decimals. The source images may be drawings or photographs, and these proportions are not direct measurements of a representative wild population. Ratios that conflict with the numerator and total length recorded on the same row are omitted here.
