## Mimosa pachycarpoides {#s-adb5f9ccd9}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa pachycarpoides* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes a hairy stem with curved hairs, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and globose flower clusters. It leaves most floral characters unspecified, so the entry supports a short account of the stem and cluster form rather than a fuller floral description.
