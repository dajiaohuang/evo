## Source classification and concept status {#s-55485021f3}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3131 records Hyperolius diaphanus under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-dacbc1afd3}
The compilation records terrestrial and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-488c88107f}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 32 mm for this row. AmphiBIO's Body_size_mm field records maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-6104a277a0}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
