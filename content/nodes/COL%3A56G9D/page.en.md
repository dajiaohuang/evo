## Thibaudia cupatensis Huber {#s-041de86c2c}
## Small flower clusters and grayish hairs

Thibaudia cupatensis has branchlets that may be smooth or hairy. Its ovate to elliptic leaves have blunt, rounded or tapering tips, an obtuse base, and margins that may be flat or slightly rolled under. The underside is described as smooth and without punctate glands. Flowers occur in fascicles of two to six on stalks 3–17 mm long; no involucre is present. The calyx is tubular to bell-shaped, and the corolla bears grayish hairs.
