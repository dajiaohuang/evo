## Cayaponia amazonica (Poepp. & Endl.) Cogn. {#s-9a98549698}
## Few large fruits borne in racemes

Cayaponia amazonica has simple tendrils and non-prostrate branches. Its alternate leaves are membranous and have three to seven leaflets. The flowers are solitary and bell-shaped. Few globose fruits are reported, with one or two noted; they are at least 25 mm long and arranged in racemes. Mature fruit color is recorded as yellow to red, and the surface ranges from becoming hairless to hairless; the fruit stalk is hairless. Each fruit has one to three dark, ovate-oblong to nearly round seeds with a fibrous aril.
