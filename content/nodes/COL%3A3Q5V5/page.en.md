## *Ischnosiphon ovatus*: a rosette of symmetrical ovate leaves {#s-00da291d8e}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, the plant grows as an unbranched rosette with symmetrical, ovate leaves. Each small flower cluster has one bracteole, a small leaf-like piece immediately beneath a flower. The petals are recorded as white or pink, and the outer staminode—a sterile part derived from a stamen—is white.

The habitat field labels the species terrestrial but gives no locality or survey date. These traits belong to a regional morphology entry, not a measured survey of population variation.
