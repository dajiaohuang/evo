## Trachyandra arenicola {#s-55c394a666}
Trachyandra arenicola is a rhizomatous perennial with thickened roots and leathery, falcate leaves. Its slender, loosely branched raceme bears white flowers with hairy bracts. The regional account separates it from the more robust T. falcata by its sandy-flat habitat and laxer inflorescence. Sandy coastal flats are recorded.
