## What range of leaf and flower traits is recorded for Heteropterys escalloniifolia? {#s-08dcfd161d}
The leafy branches may become hairless. Opposite petioles may be hairless, woolly or glandless. The leathery blades are elliptic or oblanceolate, with wedge-shaped or blunt bases and pointed or blunt tips. Hair is confined to the lower surface, where it may thin out or remain silky. Marginal glands may be absent or sunken just inside the edge, which is rolled under. Upright inflorescences have persistent elliptic bracts and stalked pedicels. Sepals may have glands across all sepals or only some, rusty hairs and tips that curl back. Yellow petals are thin, clawed and unscented, with edges ranging from smooth to ragged; they may reflex or stand upright. Anthers are upright and hairless. The front styles are longer than the others; style tips vary. The seed-bearing fruit body has conspicuous side veins.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-679416a5b8}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
