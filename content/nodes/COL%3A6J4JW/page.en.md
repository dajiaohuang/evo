## Three habitat categories in a regional entry {#s-71d8b8c695}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens flabellatus Hornsch. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat terms are source categories, not collection locations.

One row is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A second is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The third is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The source does not give the collection localities, survey dates or a complete range.

These categories have no locality, date, global range or sampling-coverage details attached.

The three habitat labels are the only biological content included from this regional record; they do not provide a full account of the species.
