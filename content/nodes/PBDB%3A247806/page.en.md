## Marsupialia in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-5d8928bbb8}
PBDB records Marsupialia at infraclass rank. This selected guide links three fossil-species entries; that count describes the page selection, not all descendants. A route through extinct fossil records does not mean the entire group is extinct, and a database parent path is not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Five selected occurrences and five collections {#s-af96458056}
The selected pages begin with Wirunodon chanku, Pilkipildra taylori, and Djilgaringa gillespiei. Together they link five occurrence records to five collections; Yahuarango, Etadunna, and Ettadunna are example stratum names. Each collection keeps its own age interval and identification qualifications, so comparisons belong at record level. Occurrence and collection counts are not counts of specimens or populations.

## What the selected fossil records leave open {#s-1d2fda792c}
This subset is neither a complete descendant catalogue nor a shared ecological profile. Occurrences and collections do not measure specimens, individuals, populations, or sampling effort; pooled outer age bounds do not date the group’s origin or extinction. The PBDB parent path organizes records separately from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
