A Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives a partial description of Philodendron hatschbachii. It links clasping and feeding roots, short stem internodes, elliptic to oblong leaves, and an axillary flowering structure. These are traits in one regional dataset field, not a complete account of the species.

## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-8c3849cb60}
The source field describes clasping and feeding roots and short internodes. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the petiole is conspicuous, cylindrical, and green. The entire leaf blade is recorded as elliptic or oblong with a rounded base. The same field gives “3–5” after those blade descriptors but does not make clear which feature the number qualifies, so it is left unassigned here. The axillary, sympodial inflorescence has a short cylindrical peduncle shorter than the spathe; the slightly constricted spathe is pale green, and the spadix is nearly unstalked (subsessile).

## Flowers and recorded alternatives {#s-b8ec892abe}
The recorded flowers have short stamens and staminodes. The gynoecium is described as ovoid to cylindrical and the ovary as ovoid to subcylindrical; it has 10 locules (ovary chambers), 4 ovules, and subbasal placentation. Stigma, berry, and seed traits are marked unknown in this field.
