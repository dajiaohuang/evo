## Evolutionary evidence {#s-e133df93c3}
A study compared about 348 base pairs of mitochondrial D-loop sequence, including six newly collected fecal samples from Peninsular Malaysia and published database sequences. Its trees recovered separate Peninsular Malaysian and Sumatran siamang clades, with maximum-likelihood bootstrap support of 98% and 83%, respectively. This is a regional result from one mitochondrial marker, not genome-wide evidence or a range-wide population-structure analysis.
