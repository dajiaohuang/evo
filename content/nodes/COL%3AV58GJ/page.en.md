## Accepted name and placement {#s-d2660f0b64}
COL26.8 accepts Tylototriton maolanensis Li, Wei, Cheng, Zhang & Wang, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Salamandridae (family) → Tylototriton (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Sex-specific comparison samples and tail proportions {#s-32eba0a6cb}
The 2026 sheni description reported maolanensis comparison SVL of 76.8–91.8 mm in seven males and 76.3–87.4 mm in two females, with TAL/SVL of 1.00–1.14 and 0.88–0.96 respectively. It described strongly overlapping adpressed limbs and male cloacal genital papillae. These are sex-specific comparison observations, not total-length ranges or all-population limits. The female ratio is below one, so an unqualified statement that every maolanensis tail is longer than SVL would misrepresent the data. Its larger size is relative to the Huangyan sample, not to every congener.
