## Myroxylon peruiferum {#s-074b6f39ef}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Myroxylon peruiferum* as an accepted species of *Myroxylon* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records 5–15 pinnate leaflets, each 2.9–10.5 cm long and 1.7–3.3 cm wide, with conspicuous glands and veins. Flowers are 7–9 mm long; the fruit is a samara (a winged fruit) with an elliptical seed-bearing area, and the seed coat is wrinkled. The source labels it terrestrial.
