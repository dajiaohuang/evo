## How to read these historical values {#s-7acdd708e7}
The life-history fields come from same-name entries in the 2015 archive. Values are compilation medians or averages of two middle records, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. They are not individual measurements, variation ranges, mean wild lifespans, or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share inherited data. Name, family, and sparse-row matches support association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited value. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently known, and publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, present range, conservation, and missing fields require other sources.
