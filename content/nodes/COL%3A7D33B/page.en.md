## Trichilia surumuensis {#s-55219776e1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description notes vestigial basal leaflets, a bilocular ovary and oblong or obovoid fruit.

## Vestigial basal leaflets and a two-locular ovary {#s-139c9f7511}
The stem-bud field records no cataphylls and simple hairs. Leaves may be odd- or even-pinnate, with an unwinged rachis and alternate or opposite leaflets; basal leaflets are vestigial. Leaflet bases are acute or cuneate and the lower surface glabrous. Petals are free and imbricate; staminal filaments are fully united, and a nectary is present. The ovary is glabrous or puberulous, bilocular and has two collateral ovules per locule. Fruit is oblong or obovoid, with strigose or puberulous indument. The separate ecology field classifies the setting as “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
