## AmphiBIO species record {#s-09e0a71b29}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Pseudophilautus hypomelas? Row Anf5201 places it in genus Pseudophilautus, family Rhacophoridae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts.
