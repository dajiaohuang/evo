## Classification concept in the record {#s-63af2286cc}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates fratisenescus* in row Anf0026, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura, following the concept in *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011). A COL26.8 name match remains only a candidate; equivalence to this source concept has not been established. The sections below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded foraging setting {#s-07632c330e}
AmphiBIO lists terrestrial foraging. This broad category does not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some microhabitat entries propagated from earlier amphibian compilations, but a row-level comparison for this record is unavailable. The source category is retained as a historical value; no replacement is inferred and the original is not treated as proven wrong.

## Reported adult maximum length {#s-23e8f067b2}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult length of 21.2 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This is a literature-compilation maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
