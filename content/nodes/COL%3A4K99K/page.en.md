## Three leaf veins and stamen details {#s-b847c0a62c}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record, the leaf is described as petiolate, meaning it has a stalk that joins the blade to the stem, and the blade is marked by three veins. The flower entry lists two bracts and distinguishes hairless stamen filaments from glandular hairs on the connective, the tissue joining the pollen sacs in an anther.
