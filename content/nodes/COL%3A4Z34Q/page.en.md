## *Spigelia cremnophila* {#s-5b848fd9fc}
## One or three main veins in the leaf

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical stems whose internodes are longer than the leaf blades; stem surfaces may be hairless or rough. Leaf stalks are nearly absent or well developed, and the ovate to rhombic leaf blades taper toward the base, have acute or blunt tips, and a rough surface. Their venation is recorded as one- or three-veined. Long flower stalks bear more than 4 flowers. The linear sepals and funnel-shaped corolla are hairless; the corolla is white or yellow. The ovary is hairless, stamens remain inside the corolla, the fruit is hairless, and the seeds are nearly spherical. The regional field keeps these alternatives without giving their frequency.
