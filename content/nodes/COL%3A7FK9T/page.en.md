## Inflorescence hairs and a winged samara {#s-06c6e98b55}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records grey tomentose indument on the inflorescence. Bracteoles are inserted on the pedicel, the calyx is funnel shaped and the androecium has 10 stamens. The samara has a glossy surface; its seed bearing body carries lateral wings that do not extend to the stipe. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
