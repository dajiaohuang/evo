## ## Dense terminal racemes and cylindrical pods {#s-ab4fa7f5fb}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a plant up to 1.5 m tall, with erect, pubescent branches. The leaves have three elliptic to oblong-elliptic leaflets on long petioles; the linear, threadlike stipules are deciduous. Dense terminal racemes bear many flowers on persistent linear bracts. The flowers are yellow, with a nearly even or truncate calyx. The cylindrical pods are black or brown and glabrous; the seeds are yellowish. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
