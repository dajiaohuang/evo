## An urn-shaped flower cup and two inner veins {#s-4766633fb6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes star-shaped hairs on young stems and ovate leaves with acute or gradually tapering tips. The base is heart-shaped or rounded; the margin is entire or toothed and lacks fringe-like hairs. Five main veins arise at the base, with two faint veins just inside the edge. The underside is densely covered by star-shaped hairs attached directly to it. Flowers form compact clusters. The urn-shaped flower cup bears a persistent calyx with flat-ended lobes; each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the pollen sacs is prolonged and bears two lower-side lobes. White anthers have one small pore, and the ovary is hairless. The field describes this combination without establishing how it varies among plants.

## A terrestrial category with no locality {#s-0cd1b77e02}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no collection locality, survey date or range coverage and does not distinguish wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category alone cannot describe the species' full ecology.
