## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-166b58fe39}
The regional description records an underground, rhizome-like stem and a pseudostem. It describes fewer than ten glaucous (blue-grey) leaves, with pinnae regularly arranged in one plane and inserted at less than 45° to the leaf axis. The mature inflorescence is yellow and shorter than 40 cm; its spathe is woody, hairy, and unarmed. Flowers are stalkless. Fruits are under 2.5 cm across, with a yellowish or greenish outer layer and yellow, orange, or whitish flesh.

The source field records an underground, rhizome-like stem and a pseudostem; fewer than ten glaucous leaves with regularly arranged pinnae in one plane at an angle under 45° to the leaf axis; mature yellow inflorescences under 40 cm with a woody, hairy, unarmed spathe; stalkless flowers; and fruits under 2.5 cm across with yellowish or greenish epicarp and yellow, orange, or whitish mesocarp.
