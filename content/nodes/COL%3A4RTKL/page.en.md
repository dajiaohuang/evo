## *Restio pulvinatus*: a cushion on south-facing ledges {#s-ab360f6e3f}
This tufted, tangled perennial spreads by stolons and grows to about 30 cm. Its stems branch and carry fine wrinkles; the flower-bearing spikelets are solitary or paired.

The account places it on south-facing rock ledges above 1,000 m.
