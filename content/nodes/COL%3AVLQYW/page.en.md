## One leaflet pair and small trapezoidal seeds {#s-e667a39112}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records one pair of leaflets arranged in two ranks and axillary flower clusters with 10 stamens and yellow petals. The pod valves twist at maturity. Seeds are described as trapezoidal or slightly compressed and measure 3.5–5.0 mm long. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. These regional fields give no sampling frame, locality, observation date, or wild/cultivated status, so the measurements should not be read as a population range.
