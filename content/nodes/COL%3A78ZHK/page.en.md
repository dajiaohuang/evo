## Salacia solimoesensis {#s-b22076882f}
## Opposite leaves and flower clusters along stems

Salacia solimoesensis has erect stems with young branches that may be cylindrical or flattened. Its opposite, leathery elliptic leaves have entire margins; the lower veins range from flat to slightly raised and turn brown when dry. Hairless flower clusters may grow on branches or stems. The flowers are wheel-shaped, with oblong two-loculed anthers and a cushion- or ring-shaped disc; the stigma is unlobed. The fruit is spherical, and habitat is labeled terrestrial.
