## ## A resprouting shrub in subalpine fynbos {#s-e9813a776d}
The 2012 SANBI regional flora account describes *Cyclopia aurescens* as a sturdy, erect shrub that resprouts and reaches 70 cm. Its leaves have three linear leaflets with strongly rolled-under margins, and the flowers are yellow. The account places it in subalpine mountain fynbos above 1,800 m and gives the Klein Swartberg as its regional range summary. These statements describe that flora account, not a complete environmental envelope or a dated locality survey.
