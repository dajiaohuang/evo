## The source's rock-associated label {#s-533e4871b9}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Molendoa sendtneriana (Bruch & Schimp.) Limpr. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is a regional entry; its biological field is a habitat category.

It is labelled “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

No collection locality, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage comes with this category.

The habitat label is the only biological content included from the regional entry, not a complete description of the moss.
