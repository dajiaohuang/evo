## Dark-backed white flowers {#s-11b47fc99b}
Hesperantha rupestris is a robust plant, sometimes 450–500 mm high. Its white to creamy-white flowers have outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) flushed dark pink to red on the reverse; the straight tube is about 7 mm long and the tepals about 14 mm.

## Marshy grassland {#s-8fd106be25}
