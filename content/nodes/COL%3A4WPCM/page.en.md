## Senna pinheiroi {#s-2ec9e155a5}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna pinheiroi* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes two pairs of leaflets and a sessile extra-floral gland between each pair. Its flowers have seven fertile stamens; the hanging, cylindrical pods do not split open, and the seeds are arranged in one row. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
