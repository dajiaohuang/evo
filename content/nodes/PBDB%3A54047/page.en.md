## Recorded parent of Hathlyacininae {#s-e2de38f50b}
Hathlyacininae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Sparassodonta as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
