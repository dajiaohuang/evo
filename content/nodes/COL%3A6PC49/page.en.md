## Morphology and field descriptions {#s-90f6b958dc}
The 2017 correction compared the Andramasay type-locality sample with related species. The authors reported a slightly smaller body than L. aeeclis and L. ruficaudatus, a narrower but somewhat longer head, with the difference more pronounced in males, and hind feet similar to L. aeeclis. These are sample comparisons, not the full range of variation.

## Known localities and evidence limits {#s-c8ab736a12}
The study then confirmed records only from the Andramasay type locality and Bemaraha. The Tsiribihina River was a possible southern limit; the northern limit remained unresolved, with the Manambaho or Mahavavy du Sud rivers only candidate barriers. Further fieldwork was needed, so these are not confirmed boundaries.
