## An upright, basal-branching cactus with a white cephalium {#s-f84eba2908}
The Portuguese morphology record describes an upright, cylindrical cactus branching at the base, with a white cephalium. Basal areoles lack secondary growth; straight central spines may project outward or curve back. Flowers open in the evening and are white or yellow on the outside. The habitat is listed as rock-associated.
