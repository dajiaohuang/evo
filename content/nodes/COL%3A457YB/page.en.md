## An elliptic leaf and a small flower record {#s-c09a201985}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field describes elliptic leaves with tips that may be acute, rounded, or retuse (shallowly notched). Flowers are in dichasial clusters, branching flower groups with three to seven flowers. The flower cup (hypanthium) is listed as hairless or pubescent (bearing short hairs). The field gives four or five sepals and 15–20 ovules in each ovary chamber. It does not quantify these alternatives or describe the species’ full variation.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-8de5c398c8}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date, range coverage, or wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil status. This is a habitat label, not a geographic account.
