## Small choruses at streamside rock pools {#s-c380224de8}
The 2016 Mindanao account reported tightly clustered choruses of about 10–25 individuals around streamside pools in rock impressions, which the authors described as preferred breeding habitat. Calls were described qualitatively as quiet, high-frequency peeps. The chorus count is not a density estimate, and the account provides no measured frequency interval, egg-site observation or complete annual breeding calendar in this paragraph.
