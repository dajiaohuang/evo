## What is known about Virola officinalis? {#s-8643d755a0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery, oblong leaves with 20–35 pairs of side veins. The underside has fine hairs and star-shaped scales; the petiole is slightly grooved. Flowers grow in racemes and have three fused anthers, with the floral column as long as or longer than the anthers. The perianth is split to its base. Fruit, seed, and aril traits are not specified in this record.

## What broad habitat category is listed? {#s-5c5070d983}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
