## Hippeastrum angustifolium: flowers and seeds {#s-ae07dd8193}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb and a rhizome. Leaves are strap-shaped with acute tips. The inflorescence bears 5–8 flowers. Their funnel-shaped perianths have dark-red tips and green coloration diffusing from the base; the hypanthium tube is short, up to 2 cm. The sepals are narrowly oval and slightly wider than the petals; the lower lateral sepals fold around the filaments. The petals are narrowly elliptic, with the lower ones much smaller than the upper ones. The corona is fringed and partly fused. The filaments are grouped and longer than the sepals and petals; the anthers are purple or dark purple, and the stigma is three-lobed.

The capsule is brown inside. The seeds are numerous, flattened and disc-shaped.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Aquática,” meaning aquatic.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
