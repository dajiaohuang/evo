## Slow pulsed calls high in the canopy {#s-7d7496c689}
The 2016 account described relatively large body size, widely expanded finger discs and a slowly repeated pulsed call. Frogs usually called high in the forest canopy, which the authors suggested could explain infrequent collection and scarcity in museum material. ## Canopy dependence as dated assessment reasoning {#s-ea39e4b1af}
The account cited records from Bohol, Leyte, Samar and Mindanao, while reporting fewer encounters and lower local apparent abundance than P. guentheri. The authors interpreted the frog as forest-canopy dependent and considered the previously assigned Vulnerable category appropriate.
