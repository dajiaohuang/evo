## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-48c1435cb0}
COL26.8 records Oligodon booliati as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-b5bacd12cf}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 510 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-8d5cc0884d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.
