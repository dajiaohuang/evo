## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0c222ec21d}
COL26.8 records Amphisbaena anaemariae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-f188a36b9a}

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-bbee39f039}
