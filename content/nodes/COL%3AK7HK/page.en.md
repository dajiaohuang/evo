## Baccharis pauciflosculosa {#s-395f38fac1}
## Axillary heads and crenate leaves

Its developed leaves are sessile and oblong, with three main veins and crenate edges. The branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated; no woody underground base (xylopodium) is recorded. The flower heads grow singly in the leaf axils. Their florets are listed as either all tubular and male or all threadlike and female. Habitat labels include rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
