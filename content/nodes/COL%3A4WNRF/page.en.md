## A disk-shaped head without ray florets {#s-7c486ac281}
This record describes an upright annual or biennial herb with sessile leaves that may clasp the stem or have winged stalks. The leaves are deeply divided and may be smooth, woolly or glandular on different surfaces. Yellow tubular florets form disk-shaped heads without ray florets. The fruits are hairy and bear a persistent white pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
