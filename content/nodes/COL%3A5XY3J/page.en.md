## Molecular phylogeny of the manakins (Aves: Passeriformes: Pipridae), with a new classification and the description of a new genus {#study-account}
Ohlson, Fjeldså and Ericson studied manakin relationships using three nuclear introns and one mitochondrial gene. The publisher's indexed passage names Chloropipo flavicapilla among taxa with incomplete ND2 sequence. The abstract reports that Chloropipo was polyphyletic as then circumscribed, but does not give this sample's placement. The passage preserves a study label and sampling limit.
