## Globose leaf glands and head like inflorescences {#s-7b4349e19b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical branches with irregularly arranged, recurved prickles. Linear stipules accompany one sessile, globose petiolar nectary on the upper third of the petiole; a rachis nectary is also present. Leaves have 5–8 pairs of pinnae, each with 17–25 pairs of oblong leaflets. The leaflets are listed as glabrous on both surfaces or sericeous on both, with oblique bases and obtuse tips; the primary vein is subcentral and has hairs at its base. The head like inflorescence is 22–28 mm in diameter. Flowers are sessile; the tubular calyx is glabrescent, the tubular corolla is glabrous, anther tips lack glands and the ovary is glabrous. The fruit is a legume with elliptic seeds. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
