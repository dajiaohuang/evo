## Living experiments on Meckel’s cartilage {#s-e8e419e2a4}
Experiments in c-Fos mutant mice and in the opossum Monodelphis domestica inhibited clast cells, which resorb cartilage and bone. Meckel’s cartilage then persisted or ossified, and a jaw–ear connection remained. These are developmental perturbations in living mammals: they identify a role for clast cells but do not show that the same cellular mechanism produced any particular fossil condition or operates identically across Mammalia.
