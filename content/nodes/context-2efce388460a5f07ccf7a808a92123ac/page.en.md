## Source classification and concept status {#s-693e718dfe}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2928 records *Litoria spenceri* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-a07bd4387b}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and aquatic as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-c943eff866}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-0baa588281}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 13 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 55 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (maturity, lifespan, clutch size and annual reproductive output) {#s-2af29a7e2a}
The row lists sexual maturity at 2–6 years and 23–40 mm, a maximum lifespan of 11 years, 200–1,000 offspring or eggs per clutch, and up to 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled ranges and maxima; this row does not specify the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind each value. It gives no offspring-or-egg size.
