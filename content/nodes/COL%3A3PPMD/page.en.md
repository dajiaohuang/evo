## Inga goldmanii {#s-8b178a1649}

The Flora of Panama describes a medium-sized tree with mostly eight leaflets per leaf; the leaflets may reach 25 × 15 cm. Its flowers have stamens about 6 cm long, and the flat, densely hairy pod is about 18 cm long and roughly 4 cm wide, with the source marking the width uncertain. The recorded range is Panama and Costa Rica.
