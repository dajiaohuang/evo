## Zephyranthes purpurella {#s-28eaf05eeb}
Zephyranthes purpurella is recorded with declined, narrow leaves and a single flower. The pink to purple perianth is 1.1–2.0 cm long, with a 2–5 mm nectar tube; the six stamens are erect or arranged in two series, and the style exceeds them. The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field says terrestrial.
