## What are the repeated lines on Haootia? {#s-139836068b}
The holotype of *Haootia quadriformis* from Newfoundland's Fermeuse Formation preserves a four-part body pattern and repeated, aligned impressions around a central region and stalk-like structure. The 2014 paper interpreted the lines as muscle bundles; a 2015 reassessment disputed their orientation and comparison, and a 2024 redescription proposed a crown-Staurozoa relationship. The fossil preserves an impression, not tissue histology, so whether the lines are muscle and how closely *Haootia* belongs to Staurozoa remain contested.
