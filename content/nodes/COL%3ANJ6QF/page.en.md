## Larval guild and adult ecomorph are separate source categories {#s-c4307d12d1}
The row codes Cycloramphus parvulus as larval END (endotrophic) and adult TER (terrestrial). The two stages are retained separately; the adult code cannot be assigned automatically to tadpoles, or the reverse. Lentic/lotic and torrential labels summarize broad habitat associations rather than quantified flow tolerance, while saxicolous or terrestrial codes do not define an entire geographic range. An endotrophic tadpole category is distinct from direct development and does not remove the larval stage. The authors compiled functional assignments from species accounts rather than experimentally measuring every species’ feeding, hydrodynamics, locomotion or habitat choice.

## Paired-stage comparisons do not establish complete developmental independence {#s-e1218012b4}
Across the sampled species, larval–adult shape dissimilarities show a weak but significant Mantel association (r 0.16, p 0.003), while disparity correlations differ among families. The authors interpret the overall pattern as support for adaptive decoupling; it does not demonstrate zero correlation, genetic independence, an experimentally selected trait or this species’ developmental trajectory. Phylogenetic analyses use 74 of the 83 sampled species, and ancestral shape/time patterns are model reconstructions from extant data rather than newly measured fossil morphology. Adult K 0.44 with p 0.001 describes less close-relative similarity than the reference expectation; the paper’s “not phylogenetically structured” wording cannot mean that the test is nonsignificant. Supplement S3 repeats family N values summing to 112 for both stages, so adult individual denominators and imputed cells remain uncertified without raw matrices.

## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-3bea7f9260}
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Zachaenus parvulus as follows: Two or three similar pulsed notes, usually with the second note strongest. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.

## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies {#s-220a8dfcc1}
The files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution, then individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak. Table1 filter specification is No filter. Normalized amplitudes are not calibrated source-level loudness. Raven measurements use 90% energy bandwidth, 5% and 95% energy frequencies, and peak frequency for the dominant component; these are not interchangeable with absolute frequency extrema or the fundamental. The Methods print FFT1024, 50% overlap, hop256 samples and grid93.8 Hz; figure captions describe a different display setting. No raw-audio reanalysis or silent correction of those settings is claimed.
