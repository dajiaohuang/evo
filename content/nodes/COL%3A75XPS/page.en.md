## What leaf, flower and gland traits are recorded for Peixotoa spinensis? {#s-dcea5aeb52}
Inflorescence stipules are pubescent and 9–10 mm long. Leaf stalks are 6–9 mm; ovate blades have a blistered upper surface, stalked glands and velvety hairs below, with margins rolled under. Four-flowered umbels form part of a dichasium. Pedicels reach 10 mm. The anther sacs are recorded as always open; glands on sterile stamens have toothed surfaces, and styles are unequal in length.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d79bd2ef1c}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
