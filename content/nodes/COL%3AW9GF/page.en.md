## Clitoria densiflora {#s-a2bec4f62d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Clitoria densiflora* records upright semiwoody or herbaceous habit, palmate three-leaflet leaves, closed cleistogamous flowers, ridged leathery pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
