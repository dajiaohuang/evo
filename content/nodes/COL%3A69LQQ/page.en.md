## Tuberous roots and drooping branches {#s-9e1ab235c3}
The Portuguese record describes tuberous roots, no trunk, and decumbent branches arising above ground. The glaucous stem has three to five low ribs; its woody tissue is listed as white to brown. Flowers are 5–8 cm long and the floral tube bears areoles. The indehiscent fruit is greenish to pink and retains the remains of the perianth. Terrestrial habitat is recorded.
