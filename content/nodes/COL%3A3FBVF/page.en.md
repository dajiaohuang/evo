## Blue rays and glandular leaves {#s-952fcac7b7}
This record describes an aromatic perennial herb or low shrub, 0.3–1.2 m tall, with sticky, glandular hairs. Its alternate leaves clasp the stem and are deeply cut; flower heads have blue to violet ray florets around yellow central florets. The habitat notes riverbanks, hillsides and rocky or stony ground; the source distinguishes it from G. latifolium by the number of bract rows.
