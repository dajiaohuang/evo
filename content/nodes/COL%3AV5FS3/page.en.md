## A taxonomic revision names six new species {#s-0792773bf4}
In its 2023 revision of the Spotted and Northern Dusky Salamander complexes, Pyron and Beamer evaluated 11 candidate species previously circumscribed from the two complexes and concluded that six represented new species, including Desmognathus lycos sp. nov. The abstract identifies the evidence as genetic, geographic, and morphological. It also notes that additional cryptic diversity may remain within the re-delimited species and elsewhere in Desmognathus. This is the revision’s abstract-level account of the name D. lycos; it supplies no species-specific locality, diagnosis, or natural-history description.
