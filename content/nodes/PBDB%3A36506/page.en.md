## The fossil behind the record {#s-24bc13bf7f}
Specimen LACM 129639 makes this genus page more than a list of names: it preserves a gravid Polycotylus from the Late Cretaceous Pierre Shale in Kansas, dated to about 78 million years ago. This is a local observation of a reproductive fossil, not the genus’s first or last appearance.

## Read the occurrence in context {#s-0403df4c5f}
The batch’s PBDB guide links selected occurrences to named Polycotylus species. Its nine selected occurrences come from nine collections. For each record, compare the identification, collection, rock unit and age bracket; a database occurrence documents a reported fossil, not an estimate of animal abundance.

## Evidence limits {#s-9e692d4377}
The gravid specimen records one reproductive association at one locality. It does not by itself describe the biology of every Polycotylus species or set the genus’s full time range. The PBDB route is a reading guide to selected records, not a complete account of the group.
