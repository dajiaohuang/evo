## What leaf and flower structures are recorded for Croton bresolinii? {#s-fd617ff13e}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. The short-stalked oval or elliptic leaves also bear star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, and their margins are entire. The record describes hyphodromous venation, with lateral veins inconspicuous, and a sessile nectar gland at the leaf base. Young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and bell-shaped; female flowers have short stalks and separate sepals. The style is two-lobed, while petal details are unknown. In the bud, the calyx parts meet edge to edge in a valvate arrangement, and the fruit columella has only weakly distinct apical lobes.
