## Stromanthe stromanthoides {#s-14b43beb6c}
## A panicle with orange bracts

The flora description records a thickened underground rhizome and an above-ground stem with an elongated first internode. Its oblong leaves have rounded bases, oblique tips and a papery blade; the central vein is softly hairy. The loose panicle has two or three nodes, with two to five inflorescences per node. Orange, papery bracts subtend the flower clusters. Flowers have three persistent sepals, white corolla lobes and one white, spatulate outer staminode. The yellow, smooth capsule is oblong and angled; the single brown seed is wrinkled and bears a lamellate aril. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
