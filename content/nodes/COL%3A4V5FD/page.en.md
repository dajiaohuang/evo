## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-75fbecfc82}
COL26.8 records Scelotes mossambicus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b57a900c07}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The lowest value among reported litter or clutch means is 2; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The highest value among reported litter or clutch means is 3; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-ff7c7d3d85}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
