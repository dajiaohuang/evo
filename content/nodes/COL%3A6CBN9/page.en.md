## Dalbergia cearensis {#s-0c495a5d69}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Dalbergia cearensis* records usually 5–9 leaflets, source range 3–13; oblong pod veins concentrated over the seed region. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
