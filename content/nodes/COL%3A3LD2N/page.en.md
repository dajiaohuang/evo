## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Heteropterys murcapiresii? {#s-3997e5260c}
Opposite, leathery elliptic leaves have blunt bases, rounded tips and hairless surfaces; the margins are flat. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts without glands and stalked pedicels. Sepals have curled tips and rusty hairs; only the front sepal may lack glands. Yellow clawed petals are thin, keeled? No: the source records no keel; their margins are ragged. Anthers are hairless. Styles are equal, bent at the base and blunt-tipped. The seed-bearing fruit body is rough.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3d0d06c7d8}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
