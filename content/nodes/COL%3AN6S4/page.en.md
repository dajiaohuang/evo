## A three-character diagnosis {#summary}
What marks Branchiostoma longirostrum among the lancelets described from the Gulf of Mexico and Florida? Boschung reported means of 237 dorsal fin-ray chambers, 61.3 total myotomes and a rostrum 2.91% of total length.

## The character combination used in the description {#structure}
Boschung diagnosed the species by a combination of relatively few dorsal fin-ray chambers (mean 237), a high total myotome count (mean 61.3), and a comparatively large rostrum averaging 2.91% of total length. These are reported sample means; the abstract does not give sample size or ranges.

## What the abstract does not report about life {#lifestyle}
The abstract reports where the described specimens came from, but gives no observation of feeding, burrowing, substrate use, depth or daily activity. It cannot support a species-level lifestyle account.

## Comparisons named by the author {#relationships}
Boschung compared the new species with Branchiostoma virginiae and B. bennetti, which the author called its probable nearest relatives, and also with B. floridae and B. bermudae. This is the author's comparative statement, not evidence here for direct ancestry or a tested phylogenetic tree.

## Specimens and the limits of the accessible account {#research}
The description was based on specimens reported from the northern and eastern Gulf of Mexico and Florida's east coast. The accessible publisher abstract identifies the diagnostic character combination and comparisons; it does not expose specimen counts, individual measurements, collection dates or detailed localities.
