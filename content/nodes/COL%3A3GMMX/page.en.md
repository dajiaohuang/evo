## What leaves and flower clusters are described? {#s-74104f20c6}
This lax, silvery shrub grows to 2 m. Opposite oblong-oval leaves are densely silky-haired. Two to four cream-colored flowers grow at branch tips; the flower tube is densely silky-haired and bears four petal-like scales that are membranous to slightly fleshy.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-c440e9e347}
The regional account reports mountain slopes. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
