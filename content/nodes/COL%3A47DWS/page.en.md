## What leaf, flower-stalk and fruit-wing traits distinguish Niedenzuella glabra? {#s-e22e2578d2}
The elliptic leaves are hairless below or have a dull, silky surface; their margins lack glands. Two glands sit near the tip of each petiole. Flower stalks are longer than 1 mm, and each fruit segment has four lateral wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a0bb07ff68}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
