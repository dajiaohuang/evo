## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-c63403955d}
COL26.8 accepts Chrysocolaptes stricklandi in Picidae. The historical life-history archive includes a same-name entry; this page compares 4 focal fields, each supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows matching the name and family. The summaries may retain older taxonomic inheritance, which a matching row name alone cannot rule out.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-79c6eb1b2c}
The historical summary lists adult body mass as 170.92 g. Adult values may combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex, so this is not a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-a840715868}
The historical compilation reports offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 3.2 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 14.75 d; age at flight capability (leaving the nest in some source material) 25 d. Here, d means days. The litter/clutch field merges offspring or egg counts; a fractional summary is not a fraction of an individual in one event. Each timing field refers to its own event; the values do not form one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-c7b1466986}
Cited sparse rows under the same name carry source labels including Dunning, 1992; Boback and Guyer, 2003; Bennett, 1986; Boyer, A.G., Cartron, J-L.E., and J.H. Brown, 2009, among others. Field-level provenance retains the raw row number, value and source label; the reference table provides the full citation explanations. A label may point to a primary paper, a book or another database. Citation-row counts do not count measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replications.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-5dee64b07b}
The life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 historical archive. The archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. These summaries are not direct measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The historical compilation may share or inherit values across species and subspecies. A matching name and family, together with same-name sparse rows, supports the association; it does not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove every inherited component from the summaries. The source does not consistently establish wild or captive conditions, and the cited publications are not individually summarized here.
