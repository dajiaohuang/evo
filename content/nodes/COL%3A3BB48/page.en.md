## Eryngium regnellii {#s-bd7f323ad5}
## Erect inflorescences and scaly fruit segments

The source records sessile leaves with parallel venation, an upright inflorescence and sessile flowers. The fruit is sessile and dehiscent, opening at maturity. Its mericarps—the halves of the split fruit—are described as bare, partly scaly or scaly on the back. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
