## Survey records {#s-7e24755523}
Surveys in 2009–2010 checked 70 forest fragments and 12 corridors around 19 sites in northwestern Madagascar, then used line transects in six localities. The presence–absence work recorded 331 social groups and 1,234 individuals; 220 km of transects produced 444 sightings for local density estimation. The authors warned that extrapolating from the surveyed area to the full range was difficult, so these figures remain tied to their survey design and date.
