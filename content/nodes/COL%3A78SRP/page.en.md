The article’s kadalarensis species row describes a non-pulsatile Type 1 call with duration 15.6 ms and dominant frequency 3.4 kHz. Type 1 has one pulse. Pulsatile Type 2 of 52.6 ms, three pulses and 3.4 kHz follows it in fixed-order call groups. These are source summary values, not a complete range or a universal constant. Type numbers label the study’s call ordering or frequency of delivery, rather than independently demonstrated functions such as alarm or aggression.
