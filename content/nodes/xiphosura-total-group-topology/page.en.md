## A revised morphology matrix for horseshoe crabs {#s-529c3fe5ca}
An expanded matrix of fossil and living xiphosurans, with rescored euchelicerates, places traditional synziphosurines across several euchelicerate stems rather than in one simple lineage. The topology depends on character coding, fossil completeness and taxon sampling.

## More fossils and revised character scores {#s-1ff5d0a849}
The analysis compares living and fossil xiphosurans—horseshoe crabs and their fossil relatives—and rescored euchelicerate taxa in an expanded morphological character matrix. The matrix is the evidence used to test how these fossils fit among chelicerate lineages.

## A classification result that remains testable {#s-173cadaadc}
The resulting tree separates traditional synziphosurines among several euchelicerate stem groups, challenging their treatment as one natural group. Many fossils preserve incomplete anatomy, and missing observations or different character coding can shift short internal branches. The result revises a phylogenetic hypothesis; it does not demonstrate evolutionary stasis or one shared habitat history.
