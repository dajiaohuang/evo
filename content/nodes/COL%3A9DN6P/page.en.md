## Stylosanthes longiseta {#s-b518a233b5}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Stylosanthes longiseta* as an accepted species of *Stylosanthes* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records golden hairs on the bracts and a one-segment fruit with a rudimentary beak shorter than 3.5 mm. A marginal vein is present on the leaf; the seeds are black and the plant is recorded as terrestrial.
