## Overview {#s-b63fab4c07}
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID 6K834. The local exact MDD name match supplies the display label Thomas's Dwarf Galago; the Chinese name is editorial.

## Research observations {#s-fb1a9ca4d3}
Ambrose and Perkin surveyed Moca in southern Bioko for 60.5 nocturnal hours from 26 February to 8 March 1997. Their paper reported the first identification of this species on the island and listed it alongside demidoff, Allen's galago and the pale needle-clawed galago. The survey focused on one montane-forest locality and did not estimate island-wide occupancy.

## Morphology {#s-36c36eb86a}
A craniodental study compared 290 museum Galagoides skulls. Samples carrying the original thomasi and demidoff labels could be separated about 70% of the time; this is not an error-free identification rule. The Moca survey records calls and nocturnal observations, and the museum result is not a count of animals at that site.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-fe89c34dc2}
A study from Gabon's Haut-Ogooué region treated thomasi and demidoff as sympatric populations. The first Bioko record and the Gabon study are local evidence from different places; they support a reading path and occurrence records, not a complete range map or population assessment.

## Evidence limits {#s-a0806634c8}
This is a guide to a first record from one Bioko locality and a sympatric study in Gabon, not a complete species account. Available sources do not support a full range, range-wide ecology or current population status; neighboring-population research is not extrapolated to the island as a whole. This page is.
