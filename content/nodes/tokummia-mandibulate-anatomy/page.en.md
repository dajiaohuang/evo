## Tokummia preserves mandibles and segmented limb bases {#s-a36f525119}
The holotype ROM 63823 and paratype ROM 63824 preserve the appendage combinations scored in the published analysis: paired mandibles, maxillipeds and subdivided basipods. A basipod is the basal part of an arthropod limb; the study compared its structures alongside the jaws to test early mandibulate anatomy. The matrix recovers Tokummia katalepsis on the mandibulate stem. That placement is an analytical result, not evidence that Tokummia directly gave rise to crustaceans, insects or myriapods.
