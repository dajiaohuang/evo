## What leaf and pollen details mark Kielmeyera rizziniana? {#s-c764706439}
Its leaves are described as leathery to slightly leathery, elliptic, oblong, or obovate, with rounded or shallowly notched tips and a narrowing base. The record notes elongated glands on the leaves. Flower clusters carry more than three flowers; the white petals are strongly asymmetric and the inner and outer green sepals differ in size. The upright anthers have conspicuous apical glands. Pollen occurs in tetrads, groups of four grains, and the ovary and style are hairless.
