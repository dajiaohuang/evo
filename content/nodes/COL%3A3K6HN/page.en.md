## White bracts over a low mat {#s-a51f8d0f78}
The record describes a prostrate, mat-forming perennial with narrow, linear leaves. Solitary or small groups of flower heads rise at stem tips; the flowers are yellow and the glossy bracts white, sometimes faintly brownish outside. Leaf length and woolliness vary, and the source notes that rock-grown plants may have shorter leaves than nearby grassland plants.
