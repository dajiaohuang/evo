## Drosanthemum delicatulum {#s-2bd5472f91}
## Pale-pink flowers sit on a compact, stiffly branched shrublet

The Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 10 cm tall, with stiff branching and red stems bearing sparse papillae. The nearly spherical leaves are papillate. Solitary flowers are almost stalkless, pale pink, and up to 10 mm across. The five-chambered fruits have low rims. The habitat record is shale slopes.
