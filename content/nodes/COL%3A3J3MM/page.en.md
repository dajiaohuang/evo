## Habenaria arenaria {#s-3e467ea39b}
The South African account describes Habenaria arenaria as a terrestrial herb up to 400 mm tall, with two or three basal leaves that are faintly mottled. A loose inflorescence bears many green flowers; the petals are undivided and the lip has three equal lobes. The account records seasonally dry, shaded habitats.
