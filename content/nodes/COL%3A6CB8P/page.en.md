## Food changes between life stages {#s-5c9add1eb1}
Monarch caterpillars feed on milkweeds in the genus Asclepias. They pupate before adults emerge, and adult flower resources differ from the larval host plants. The two stages therefore rely on different kinds of food resources.

## Migration varies among populations {#s-0df059634f}
Some North American populations make seasonal migrations, and resident populations also occur. A migration pattern described for one population cannot stand for every monarch or establish a species-wide abundance trend or level of threat.

## A population-specific view {#s-7fc6a441d8}
The migration examples apply to particular populations. They do not describe every population or establish a trend or threat level for the species as a whole.
