## Record identity and classification {#s-799f5a1c6f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3998 records Cophixalus tridactylus under Cophixalus, Microhylidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Adult food categories {#s-dc0897a28f}
The row lists arthropods as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Developmental category {#s-6b2b1838b2}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists direct development as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
