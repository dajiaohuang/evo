## Barcode-supported identification retains provisional boundaries {#s-e076aac3a9}
The specimen was placed in its own COI BIN and ABGD group, but the authors could not obtain its16S sequence. A missing16S result is a marker-data gap, not proof of no mitochondrial gene, and this individual cannot be described as independently concordant across both loci.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-286a1a34c4}
For Cardioglossa gratiosa, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
