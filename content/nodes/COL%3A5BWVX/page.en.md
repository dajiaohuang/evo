## Three habitat categories in a regional record {#s-a99e225fbf}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Weissia controversa Hedw. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora entry is regional data. Its labels are habitat or substrate categories, not named collection sites.

One row is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A second row is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The third is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. These rows do not give collection localities, survey dates or a complete range.

The source categories have no locality, date, range or sampling-coverage details attached.

These three habitat categories are the biological content included from the regional record; they do not make a full account of the species.
