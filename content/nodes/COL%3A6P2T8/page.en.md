## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-889078b2b1}
COL26.8 records Laticauda crockeri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-214a628490}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 885 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-2ffbf46843}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-3a4fc395d7}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3.7. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015.
