## Chamaecrista myrophenges {#s-c2a7d8bbbc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista myrophenges* records 2–4 leaflet pairs, bulbous glandular hairs, yellow unequal petals, twisted mature pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
