## Woolly leaf undersides and clustered heads {#s-db8f7bf6f5}
This description records woolly branches and small, narrow leaves with contrasting surfaces: the upper side is hairless, the lower side densely felted. Around 20 flower heads form a corymb-like cluster. The dry fruits bear glandular hairs and a tuft of 25–30 bristles; the habitat field says terrestrial.
