## Selaginella anceps {#s-e05646c8af}
## Upright stems with three distinct leaf positions

The record describes upright, straw-coloured, smooth stems without joints and with ventral rhizophores, the root-bearing structures. The small leaves are dimorphic by position: lateral leaves are oblong-lanceolate, while dorsal leaves are ovate-elliptic and end in an acuminate or short-awned tip. Axillary leaves are also oblong-lanceolate. The field records asymmetric bases for lateral and dorsal leaves, no idioblasts in either, and terrestrial habitat.
