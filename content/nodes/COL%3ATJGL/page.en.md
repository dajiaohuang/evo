## ## Stiff hairs and twisted pod valves {#s-05f7b93491}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 8–13 pairs of oblong to ovate leaflets with asymmetrical, heart-shaped bases. Stiff hairs occur on stems, leaves and inflorescences. The yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. At maturity, the pod valves twist and carry glandular hairs. The xylopodium and seed characters are listed as unknown; the dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
