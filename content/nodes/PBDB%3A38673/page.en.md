## Evidence limits {#s-b78324a925}
The character comparison supports separating the two genera; it does not establish the first or last appearance of the entire family, nor describe every brachiosaurid. The 154.8–145 Ma interval belongs to the reviewed type-material comparison, not a complete family range.

## Taylor’s type-material comparison {#taylor-literature-context}
Taylor (2009) compares Brachiosaurus altithorax with Giraffatitan brancai, including Morrison and Tendaguru type material. The character comparison supports separating the two genera.

The associated 154.8–145 Ma Late Jurassic interval describes this type-material sample; it does not define the complete Brachiosauridae range.
