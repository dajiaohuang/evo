## Source classification and concept status {#s-f12ae9d4b3}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3080 records *Afrixalus uluguruensis* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (aquatic and arboreal) {#s-ae5c820821}
AmphiBIO records aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-b4a49756f9}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-a9c2906ee5}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; night-time activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-6150dabd90}
AmphiBIO records activity in dry and cold conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-8a7e88d967}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-444d3f305a}
AmphiBIO reports 10–16 offspring or eggs per clutch and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. The row does not report an offspring-size value. These counts are literature-compiled; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-4fb58d7ba5}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
