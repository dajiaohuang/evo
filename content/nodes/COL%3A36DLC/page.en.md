## Diosma meyeriana {#s-6973ec92a0}
Regional accounts describe a finely leafy aromatic shrub with linear-lanceolate leaves and small white flowers, while differing in their notes on its branching form.

## Aromatic foliage and persistent flowers {#s-90a2de8479}
A 2012 flora describes a finely leafy aromatic shrub up to 1 m, sometimes branched from the base. Its sessile, ascending leaves are linear-lanceolate and sharply mucronate; small white flowers occur in terminal clusters on short racemes. A 1982 diagnostic account instead describes a shrub from one main stem and entirely glabrous. It records alternate, erect-adpressed leaves with a small straight point, and small crowded subsessile flowers with glabrous petals that close again after flowering and persist. Fruits are described as not oily, with matte seeds. The account compares it with *D. hirsuta*, which has petiolate leaves, and *D. acmaeophylla*, whose petals are pubescent. The habitat field says rocky sandstone slopes. These descriptions come from separate regional accounts; neither supplies a dated sampling frame or population-frequency estimate.
