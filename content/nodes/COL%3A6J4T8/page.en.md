## Fischeria polytricha {#s-9fb99396be}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Fischeria polytricha* as an accepted species of *Fischeria*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records only its terrestrial habitat category. The regional field gives no locality, range, or morphology description, so it supports no further characterization of the plant.
