## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a44e4d45cf}
COL26.8 records Aipysurus pooleorum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5e1bf63971}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018.
