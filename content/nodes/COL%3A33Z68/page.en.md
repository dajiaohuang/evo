## A variable leaflet count and rounded fruit {#s-af3bdcc2e0}
The flora entry records leaves usually with five to nine oval or oblong leaflets, while three or up to 13 are also listed. The tips are pointed and the bases rounded. Flowers may be in simple clusters, branched clusters, or small bundles. The flower has nine stamens, with filaments recorded as joined into one or three bundles. The fruit is rounded, with a uniform surface-vein pattern. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
