## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-c4c3b4bb20}
COL26.8 records Bothrops erythromelas as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-a06fc4e1f5}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 850 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a9b1413f97}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015. The foraging-mode field records AMB (ambush or sit-and-wait foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Martins et al. 2002.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-7f40bf237b}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-590d71daa8}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
