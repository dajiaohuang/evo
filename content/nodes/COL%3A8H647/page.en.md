## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-4449132d8f}
COL26.8 records Kinosternon vogti as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Kinosternidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-fd8b91cf39}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 102 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to López-Luna et al. 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-615b329921}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-4138d15f23}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
