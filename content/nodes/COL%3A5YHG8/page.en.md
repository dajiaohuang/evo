## Chromolaena alvimii {#s-0d32f2a56a}
## A woody underground structure and three-veined leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes a xylopodium, a woody underground structure. Stems are listed as smooth or covered in short, stiff hairs. Opposite leaves range from lance-shaped to ovate, with serrated edges and three main veins; the upper surface is stiffly hairy, while the lower may be smooth, gland-dotted or stiffly hairy.

Corymb-like clusters of heads have stalks and bell-shaped involucres. Outer bracts spread outward and have glands; inner bracts stand upright and also have glands. The corolla lobes are papillose, with minute raised projections. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth, obconical and topped by smooth pappus bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or complete range is supplied.
