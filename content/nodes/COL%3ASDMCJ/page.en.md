## Ngoc Linh records and a Lao sequence attribution {#s-1fe649707f}
The description reported Ngoc Linh Mountain in Vietnam’s Central Highlands and attributed a Xekong, Laos, record using deposited genetic data. The Vietnamese collections and the sequence attribution are kept separate, without treating them as a complete contemporary range.

## High-elevation headwater observations {#s-6a2f5acd83}
Specimens were found between 19:00 and 23:00 above 1500 m, in rocky-stream water or on banks within secondary forest. Air temperature was 18.5–22.5 °C and humidity 68–85% during surveys. Calls and tadpoles were unrecorded; these collection observations do not establish reproductive timing or physiological limits.
