## Leathery leaves with a woolly grey underside {#s-bf0c6a28aa}
This record describes a swollen underground stem and leaves along the stem. The lance-shaped leaves are leathery, smooth above and greyish or brown-woolly beneath; their margins may be flat or rolled under. Sessile flower heads form cymose clusters, with 6–8 rows of bracts and 50 or more florets. The top-shaped fruits are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
