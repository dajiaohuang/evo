## Limits of the historical records {#s-2b0dd3baac}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are sample averages, not diagnoses or individual-variation ranges. Mass comes from separate sources, so the morphology sample count cannot be assigned to it. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography covers historical breeding and resident ranges. Historical crosswalks and the matching catalogue name support an association, not equivalence after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation require other sources.

The 2015 life-history archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. These are not direct measurements of one animal, individual ranges, average wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching name and family and same-name rows support the association but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications were not individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields need other sources.
