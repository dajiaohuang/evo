## Trachyandra gerrardii {#s-d64acf50e7}
Trachyandra gerrardii is a robust perennial reaching about 0.6 m, with hairy leaves and branched racemes bearing white flowers. The regional account describes the mature capsule as about 10 mm across with a tuberculate surface. Grassy slopes and the edges of vleis are recorded habitat; the South African account does not establish global variation.
