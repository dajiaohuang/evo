## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-cb9b55cc8a}
COL26.8 records Calamaria arcana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-656cdad6e6}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2010.
