## A species score for a specified conservation framework {#s-32af8bd99f}
Table A4 reports DBS 1, TBS 0, SBS 0 and total SI 1 for Xenopus victorianus. DBS weights the number of represented Rwanda ecozones and national endemism; TBS uses national red-list proposals reported by earlier research, rather than a fresh global IUCN assessment. Its score 0 corresponds to LC in Table 1. SBS 0 corresponds to the broadest tolerance category, including multiple microhabitats in disturbed areas. These are categorical inputs to this Rwanda index, not experimentally measured physiological tolerances, demonstrated responses to every land use or a newly assigned current threat status. A combined VU/DD score does not distinguish those two categories by itself.

## Species Index and site index have different meanings {#s-ed6533ceb4}
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI 1 for Xenopus victorianus is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS 1/2/4/8, TBS 0/1/2/4/8 and SBS 0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
