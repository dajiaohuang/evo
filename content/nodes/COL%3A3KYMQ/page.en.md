## Hereroa fimbriata {#s-32e1065733}
## Long-stalked fruits distinguish it from two similar species

The Extra Cape flora compares this species with H. crassa and H. joubertii. Hereroa fimbriata is described as a shrub up to 350 mm tall, with leaves extending along the full length of the erect flowering shoots. Its fruits are large, 10–12 mm across, and carried on long stalks. The habitat record is rocky, shaly places.
