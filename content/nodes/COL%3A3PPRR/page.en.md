## ## Winged leaf axes and a woody fruit {#s-29a3277b15}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical, villous branches with lenticels. The deciduous stipules are ovate; the petiole may be cylindrical or winged, and the main leaf axis is winged. Leaves have two or three pairs of leaflets and stalkless, saucer-shaped nectaries. Spike-like clusters bear stalkless flowers with tubular calyces. The flat fruit has woody valves that range from glabrous to densely woolly. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
