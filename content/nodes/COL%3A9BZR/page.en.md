## What fruit and flower structures are recorded for Acioa edulis? {#s-7defa3610e}
The record describes oblong leaves and a cone-shaped floral receptacle, the structure that bears the flower parts. Each flower has 17–20 separate stamens arranged in a semicircle. The outer fruit wall is recorded as hairless with a warty surface.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-54eee3bc29}
The dataset classifies it as terrestrial, without specifying a locality.
