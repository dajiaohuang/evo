## What flower arrangement is recorded for Bernardia multicaulis? {#s-397e948a59}
The record identifies it as monoecious, with male and female flowers on the same plant. Leaves are thin, toothed, and hairy beneath, with immersed glands. Male flowers grow in spikes bearing two to ten cymes beneath cup-shaped bracts; the stalks are 5 mm to 3 cm long. Female flowers form rounded clusters of three to ten, usually sessile or nearly so. Male flowers have four to six stamens and stalks over 2 mm; female flowers are sessile with undivided styles. The fruit is sessile.
