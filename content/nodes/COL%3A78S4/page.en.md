## Overview {#s-afe36e26bf}
COL26.8 lists five accepted Rhinopithecus species: R. bieti, R. roxellana, R. avunculus, R. brelichi and R. strykeri. This reading path adds bilingual pages for the latter three and links the existing pages for the first two. The studies span northern Vietnam, Guizhou and the China–Myanmar border, with markedly different sites, dates, sample units and questions.

## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-f2ae1624db}
Start with R. avunculus: keep the Khau Ca leaf-chemistry comparison separate from short-term ranging observations, then read the local habitat survey from Quan Ba. Continue with R. brelichi by pairing the 1991 Fanjingshan social observations with the 2011–2013 elevational and food-plant records, and treat the mitochondrial sample study as a separate line of evidence. Next read the description of R. strykeri, followed by the 2019–2021 Pianma transboundary-group study and its single census. Finish with the repeated-record caveat on body mass in the existing R. bieti page and the Sichuan-only distribution study in the R. roxellana page. Do not pool measurements from different dates, samples and places.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-6cbfab540d}
This is a reading path linking five checklist-accepted species to selected local studies, not a genus-wide ecological review or a phylogenetic conclusion. Population, ranging and habitat results remain tied to their stated sites and periods; the new species pages.
