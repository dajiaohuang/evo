## Source classification and concept status {#s-1ded71f5b1}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3128 records *Hyperolius cinnamomeoventris* under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-0c37313242}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-08282091ca}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-4f6ee25c31}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; daytime activity is unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-e967bbf599}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 33 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-a4f376ca04}
AmphiBIO reports 114–146 offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and both minimum and maximum offspring or egg size as 1.5 mm. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. Its size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-bac20b371f}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
