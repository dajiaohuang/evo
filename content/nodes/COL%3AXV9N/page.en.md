## Constantia microscopica {#s-f238c3ab71}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes Constantia microscopica with a short creeping rhizome, dark-green ovoid pseudobulbs and two light-green, warty leaves. One flower rises above the leaves on an elongated inflorescence; the sepals and petals are white to pink, and the white lip has a green centre. The habitat category is rupicolous.
