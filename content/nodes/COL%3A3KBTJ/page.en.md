## Heliophila eximia {#s-863e4064d4}
## Tooth-tipped leaves top a low, woody-based herb

The 2013 Extra Cape flora describes a straggling perennial about 300 mm tall, woody at the base. Its elongate, wedge-shaped leaves are 45–65 mm long and 10–20 mm wide, with 3–7 teeth near the tip. Flowers range from white to blue, and the ovules number about six.

The fruit is teardrop-shaped to elliptic, about 15 × 5.5 mm, with evenly curved margins. The habitat record places it on stony slopes and quartzite cliffs.
