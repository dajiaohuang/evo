## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-459898e84c}
The 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release accepts Boulenophrys palpebralespinosa (Bourret, 1937) as a species. Its recorded placement is Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). This identifies the catalogue name and classification; biological observations below retain the scope of their own sources.

## Border-region records and Vietnamese syntypes {#s-46d449a8f1}
The monograph listed syntypes (the name-bearing specimens when no single holotype was designated) MNHN 1948.114–116 from Chapa, now Sa Pa, Lao Cai, northern Vietnam, at 1500 m. Its cited distribution included Hekou and Lyuchun in Yunnan, Jingxi in Guangxi, northern Vietnam and northeastern Laos at 1100–2070 m. This is the treatment’s regional locality account. The description of relatively rare Chinese records is not a quantified abundance estimate or a current population trend.

## A corrected two-male Hekou diagnosis {#s-cefe9f86de}
The diagnosis relied on earlier descriptions of Bozhujing, Hekou, material and reported two adult males at 36.2–38.0 mm snout–vent length (measured from the tip of the snout to the vent). The remarks attributed revisions of the earlier one-male/one-female interpretation to two males, and of paired vocal sac (a throat pouch used when calling)s to a subgular vocal sac, to Fei and colleagues (2009). Anatomy included vomerine ridges (ridges on bones in the roof of the mouth) and teeth, rough tuberculate skin, several prominent eyelid tubercles, half-webbed toes with wide fringes, and dense brown nuptial spines on fingers I and II. The grey-brown dorsal colour was explicitly described in preservative, not certified as live colour.

## Guangdong record rejected by voucher-image interpretation {#s-dce01c8445}
The monograph reviewed a published Tianjing Mountain, Guangdong, record far from the main regional distribution. Interpreting the earlier voucher figure, its authors reassigned that record to B. shimentaina and removed Guangdong from their B. palpebralespinosa account. This is an attributed identification decision based on the published image, not a newly examined Guangdong specimen or proof that B. palpebralespinosa cannot occur there.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-f3b5acf943}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys palpebralespinosa as with vomerine teeth (teeth on bones in the roof of the mouth). That statement belongs to the paper’s comparison; it is not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. This single comparison does not show how often the character occurs across the species or provide a complete diagnosis, diet or developmental account.
