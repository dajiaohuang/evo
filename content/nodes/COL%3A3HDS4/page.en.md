## Griffinia nocturna {#s-66cf4efc8b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Griffinia nocturna with a subterranean bulb and narrow elliptic to oblanceolate leaves. Its fragrant, white, funnel-shaped flowers have a tube longer than 5 cm and six stamens; the fruit is ellipsoid. The habitat label is terrestrial, with no locality or field context supplied.
