## Spathiphyllum tenerum: proportional and floral traits {#s-522149f09a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records petioles in length categories of less than 35 cm, less than 20 cm and more than 20 cm. Petiole length is more than 1.2 times blade length. Blades are more than 3 times and less than 6 times as long as wide, and have fewer than 9 secondary veins per side.

The peduncle is less than twice the petiole length and more than 1.9 times the blade length. The spathe persists at maturity. The spadix is cylindrical, longer than 1 cm, and more than 5 times as long as its stipe. The style does not extend above the perianth and is truncate or nearly truncate; tepals are free at the apex or completely free.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
