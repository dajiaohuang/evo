## A gill-cover structure reconstructed from CT {#s-2316febf1b}
AMNH FF 20509 preserves jaws, part of the skull base, gill supports, pectoral cartilage, fins and teeth. CT imaging shows elongated, fused branchial rays forming an operculum over the gill region.

## A shark relative known from one partial fossil {#s-a1e34d4af5}
A phylogenetic analysis supports placing the fossil among falcatid symmoriiforms. The sample does not reveal prey or measured swimming performance, and its damaged state limits what can be said about the whole animal.
