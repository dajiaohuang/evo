## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded? {#s-8eb1aa7a08}
The leaves may be elliptic or obovate, with pointed tips and bases that range from nearly heart-shaped to rounded or asymmetric. Their hairs range from nearly absent to woolly or cobweb-like, including star-shaped hairs. Flower clusters may grow in leaf axils or at shoot tips. Each flower has seven or eight epicalyx bracts no longer than the calyx, lance-shaped sepals and white obovate petals no longer than the calyx. The filaments are free, and the staminodes are shorter than the stamens. The ovary is globose and grooved; the style base is woolly. The obovate to oblong fruit has a rounded tip and wedge-shaped base. Its valves may be rounded or flat, lack a central vein and open partway along a sharp, uncrested line. The fruit is woolly. Seed wings are less than three times the seed’s length.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-a128f3e545}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
