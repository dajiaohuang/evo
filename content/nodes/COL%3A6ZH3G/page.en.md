## What features distinguish Stachytarpheta discolor? {#s-a1d9efc5ec}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes nearly smooth, cylindrical stems and opposite, leathery, stalkless leaves with toothed edges. Blue, funnel-shaped flowers form terminal spikes; each calyx has four teeth. The dry fruit splits into two segments, each with one seed.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-1398770391}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial and gives no more specific locality.
