## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f82805930d}
COL26.8 records Amphisbaena mitchelli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-5d90038dff}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 165 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-724eb93169}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
