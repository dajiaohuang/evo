## Narrow leaves in a regional comparison {#s-5edc9a923c}
The regional flora compares *Ectadium virgatum* with *E. latifolium*: its leaves are linear, meaning long and narrow, and measure 60–80 × 3–4 mm. The same account records its habitat as riverbeds and gravelly flats. This short comparison is the source's species-level description; it provides no wider account of growth, reproduction, or variation.
