## Leaves, flower, and fruit {#s-e6c74d683d}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte and gives a 4–10 cm measurement without specifying what it measures. It records seven to eighteen leaves, dry at flowering and held partly upright. The linear leaves range from loosely to tightly coiled; both surfaces bear sparse simple hairs. The flower has spreading tepals and is white to pale pink. It has six stamens, and the style curves to one side. The fleshy berry is yellow to orange.
