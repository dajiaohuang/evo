## Litogyne gariepina {#s-0ffe9fd4e5}
## Winged stems and small purple heads

Herman and Retief's 1997 account describes a perennial herb or shrub up to 1 m tall, with winged stems. The sessile, gland-dotted leaves are lance-shaped to oblong, with smooth or sparsely toothed margins.

A few small, purple flower heads form almost umbel-shaped clusters at the ends of side branches. The account records sandy grassland, open woodland and roadside weed sites as habitat.
