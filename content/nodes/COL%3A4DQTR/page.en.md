## Parodia scopa {#s-469c8d8265}
## Black seeds have a strongly tuberculate coat

The source describes medium-sized clustered stems, globose or cylindrical, with continuous or slightly tuberculate ribs. Their areoles are distinct; spines may be fine and white or golden, and the short central spines stand erect. Flowers form below the tip with abundant golden hairs. The yellow petals have rounded tips and irregularly notched margins; the stigma is red or vinaceous. Dry, light-brown fruits contain black seeds with strongly tuberculate coats. The habitat is labeled rocky.
