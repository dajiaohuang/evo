## An effort-bounded Gunung Belumut survey record {#s-70b2d963b5}
Table 1 records 2 Polypedates discantus encounters at Gunung Belumut Amenity Forest in Johor, Peninsular Malaysia, or 1.2% of the 172 reported amphibian records. Sampling combined drift-fenced pitfall traps with searches along streams and forest trails in parts of 2022 and February 2023. Most captured animals were released and selected vouchers retained; the article does not document marking that would certify every encounter as a different individual. This is a local survey count, not population density, an elevational limit or complete distribution. The forest’s 1,010 m summit elevation is not assigned as the elevation of every frog record.

## A unique checklist entry is not geographic endemism {#s-f552d4bd0d}
The paper reports Polypedates discantus only in Gunung Belumut among its selected comparison checklists. That distinction concerns the particular published lists and their unequal survey histories, not proof that the species is endemic to this forest or absent from the other regions.
