## Tamarindus indica {#s-2f83ab3241}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tamarindus indica* as an accepted species of *Tamarindus* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama fields in a WFO MBG archive retrieved 2026-09-08 describe *Tamarindus indica* as a naturalized tree. Its leaves have 6–18 leaflet pairs, flowers are yellow striped with red, and the pod is 5–15 cm long with acidic pulp around the seeds. The distribution field reports it across the world’s tropics.
