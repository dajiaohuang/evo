## Roots, leaves, and flowers {#s-24cd7c71d1}
Snijman's 2013 regional account describes a rhizomatous perennial 100–350 mm tall, with a few bulbous roots joined to a short rhizome. The roots stain purple when pressed. There are 4–20 thread-like, rough leaves, curled toward their tips. Pinkish-white flowers with spots at the base grow in a panicle with several nearly upright branches. The globose capsules are about 3 mm in diameter.

These measurements and traits come from a regional species account published in 2013; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Comparison with *Trachyandra thyrsoidea* {#s-ba8c260179}
Boatwright and Manning's 2010 comparison says the two species share membranous bracts and upcurved styles, but differ in their roots. *T. sanguinorhiza* has bulbous roots with red inner tissue; *T. thyrsoidea* has white, finger-shaped roots swollen toward their ends.
