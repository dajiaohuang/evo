## Potamogeton sclerocarpus {#s-55213f08ef}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records linear-to-elliptic submerged leaves without petioles, plus ovate floating leaves with petioles. The submerged leaves have acute tips and truncate bases; floating leaves have acuminate tips and wedge-shaped bases. The fruit is obovate and lacks a dorsal keel, a raised lengthwise ridge along its back. The habitat field says aquatic.
