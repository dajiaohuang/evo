## Jointed coastal stems and small pale flowers {#s-98d0050748}
This account describes a succulent shrub up to 40 cm tall that may recline or stand upright, and becomes prostrate in sandy soils. The leaves are nearly cylindrical, slightly flattened above and briefly joined to their opposite partners at the base. Small flowers form in branched clusters, usually white to pale pink or dark reddish-purple. The fruit has five chambers. It is recorded mainly from coastal saline habitats, in sand or loam and between granite outcrops.
