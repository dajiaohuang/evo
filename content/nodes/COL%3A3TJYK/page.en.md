## What flower traits are recorded for Leptobalanus maguirei? {#s-1a6d8246ea}
The record describes hairless leaf undersides and glands on the leaf stalks. Flowers grow in panicles and are sessile, meaning they attach without individual stalks. Each flower has 14 stamens with filaments extending beyond the floral tube, and a cup-shaped receptacle. The fruit’s outer wall is not described.
