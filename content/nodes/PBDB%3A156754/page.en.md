## The mountain name, type locality and transported fossils {#s-21fdff5516}
Bakonydraco combines the Bakony Mountains with Latin draco; galaczi honours András Galácz. The type locality is the Csehbánya Formation at Iharkút in western Hungary. The 2005 paper assigns it to the Santonian using cited palynological work and describes river floodplain deposits where flowing water carried bones into fossil pockets. PBDB gives one collection an age bracket of 85.7–83.6 Ma. These depositional clues describe how remains were buried, not the animal’s entire habitat; the database count is not a count of fragments or individuals.

## Evidence limits {#s-bb1a65131e}
The 2005 description and 2011 account add jaws and updated type-number links, while the referred postcranial bones remain distinct material. The exact keratinous covering is unknown; wingspan and feeding are comparative estimates or functional hypotheses. PBDB’s one collection and age bracket do not establish a complete range. The papers’ Azhdarchidae placement is retained here without claiming to settle later revisions or reproduce their analyses.
