## What leaf and floral structures are recorded for Dalechampia ficifolia? {#s-f93cc21428}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium, a woody underground root structure. Its simple leaves are three-lobed; the record does not list unlobed simple leaves or compound leaves. The flower cluster has slightly three-lobed surrounding bracts and resin glands. Female flowers have pinnately divided sepals, and the style-column tip is lobed or disk-shaped. Fruits are hairy, and the seeds are smooth.
