## *Restio nodosus*: black, curled bract tips {#s-f3d6d1ced8}
The stems of this tufted or runner-forming perennial are branched and finely to coarsely warty. Its solitary, rounded spikelets may reach 10 mm; the bract tips are black and crisped, or tightly curled. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

The account records it on shallow rocky soils above 1,200 m, often in places that are seasonally wet.
