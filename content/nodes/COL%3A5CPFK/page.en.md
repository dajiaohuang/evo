## Xyris cipoensis {#s-46ffc9554d}
## A globose head with many flowers

The flora record describes Xyris cipoensis with a rounded to nearly spherical spike containing more than 25 flowers and more than 25 sterile bracts. Its flowering stalk may range from 20 cm to over 70 cm and has one or two raised ridges. The habitat field is terrestrial.
