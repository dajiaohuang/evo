## A deeply lobed oak recorded as a street tree {#s-494a49e66e}
The regional description records a tree up to 25 m tall, with deciduous, elliptic leaves that are very deeply lobed and bristle-tipped. The leaf base is broadly tapered and the apex acute; the upper surface is green and shining, the lower paler. Acorns are nearly hemispherical, 12–17 mm in diameter, with the base enclosed by a thin, saucer-like cup. A separate account field says it is cultivated for street trees; this does not establish a wild habitat or native range.
