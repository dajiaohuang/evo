## *Restio saxatilis*: tangled cushions and membranous sheaths {#s-8634ca4f56}
Only about 30 cm tall, this much-branched perennial forms small tangled cushions. Its stems bend in a winding course, and the leaf sheaths rise into conspicuous membranous shoulders. The account records it on rocky slopes and at seeps.

The source describes both exposed rock and local wet spots, not a single moisture condition throughout the species’ range.
