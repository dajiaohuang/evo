## Single-leaflet leaves and a compact raceme {#s-3404a01e0c}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records a woody root with a xylopodium, an erect stem, and one leaflet per leaf. The racemes are congested and bear few flowers. The fruit is described as very hairy, though the row gives no further detail about the hairs; seed colour is listed as black or brown. These are traits from one regional entry, not a complete or global description.

A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives only a habitat category, without a locality, range or sampling coverage.
