## A name and a comparatively detailed trait row {#s-2b8ffc4c27}
COL26.8 accepts Cyrtodactylus macrotuberculatus in Gekkonidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 entry links size, microhabitat, active time and reproductive fields to separate citations within the workbook’s Reptile Database 2023 framework. These are consolidated reports, not a single specimen’s measurements or a population-wide survey.

## The two SVL maxima exclude the tail {#s-af74b3c1eb}
The overall maximum snout–vent length is 120.1 mm; the female maximum is 113 mm. SVL ends at the vent, so neither value includes the tail. They are compiled maxima, not typical sizes, and need not represent animals from the same place or study; their difference alone is not a population-level test of sexual dimorphism.

## Three microhabitats share a nocturnal record {#s-eff0d13f45}
The entry groups arboreal, saxicolous (rock-associated) and terrestrial microhabitats, with activity classified as nocturnal. These labels do not quantify use of each setting or give a fixed schedule. Diet is not supplied in this account, so no prey or feeding behavior can be inferred from the activity category.

## A mean of 1.75, with reported means from 1 to 2 {#s-82c3bec558}
The reproductive mode is oviparous. The combined litter/clutch field gives a mean of 1.75, with the lowest and highest reported means recorded as 1 and 2. The protocol may use midpoints of reported means or, when those are unavailable, midpoints of reported extremes; 1.75 therefore need not be an arithmetic mean of observed clutches, and it does not describe a fractional clutch.

## What the compilation leaves uncertain {#s-3797fa5f6b}
These values belong to the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and its 2023 name framework; the name match does not establish equivalence with pinned COL26.8. One row compresses differences by sex, age, locality and population. The reported maximum is not a species ceiling, and reproductive summaries do not fix every clutch; the account does not infer mass, juvenile length, venom, conservation, current range or trends.
