## Lobelia organensis {#s-a81ae4776e}
## Downy leaf undersides distinguish the foliage

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes hollow stems and alternate lance-shaped leaves. The leaves taper or continue down at the base; their margins are entire to toothed, and their tips acute to long-pointed. The upper surface is hairless, while the lower surface is hairy. A terminal raceme bears bilaterally symmetrical flowers with downward-bent, lance-shaped bracts. The semi-globose flower base supports a tubular corolla with a dorsal split; the source records it as purple.

The stamens join after flowering, the stigma has two lobes, and the fruit is an ovoid capsule with lens-shaped, winged seeds. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
