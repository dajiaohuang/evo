## Stryphnodendron polyphyllum {#s-c586d6989e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Stryphnodendron polyphyllum* as an accepted species of *Stryphnodendron* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description lists 10–19 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), 1–4 wart-shaped glands along the leaf axis, and flowers that are pink or vinaceous. The bell-shaped calyx (the outer layer formed by sepals) and corolla (the petal layer) are hairless, while the ovary is short-haired; the leathery legume is described as glabrescent or powdery. The plant is terrestrial.
