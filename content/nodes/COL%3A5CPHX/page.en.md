## *Xyris fallax*: smooth ridged stalks below an oval head {#s-597e6564a0}
Its red leaf sheaths are not strongly widened, and the flat blades have smooth or striated surfaces. The cylindrical flower stalk is smooth but has several ridges. An egg-shaped to ellipsoid head carries four sterile bracts and up to 25 flowers; the sepals are free.

The evidence categorizes this species as terrestrial.
