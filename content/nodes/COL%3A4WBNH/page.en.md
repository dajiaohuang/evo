## *Selaginella decomposita*: upright stems with leaves that differ by position {#s-b7f2ccb035}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright, green to pale stems without hairs or joints. Root-bearing rhizophores are listed on the lower or upper side of the stem. The small leaves differ by position: side leaves are ovate-lanceolate with blunt bases and pointed or tapering tips; upper leaves are elliptic, with rounded or wedge-shaped bases and long tapering or bristle-like tips. Leaves in the axils are lance-shaped to ovate-lanceolate.

An idioblast, a specialized cell type, is recorded in the side leaves but not the upper leaves. The habitat field says terrestrial. These are regional character states, not measurements of their frequency across populations.
