## Form described in the flora record {#s-467b8e93fc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem with fibers at the base and entire cataphylls and prophylls at the stem tip. Petioles are smooth and recorded as flattened above and rounded below, or channelled with blunt upper margins and a rounded lower side. Leaf blades are entire, flat, and elliptic or ovate, with an apiculate or acute tip and a truncate or heart-shaped base. Glandular dots are absent. Primary veins are obtuse on both sides; secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is smaller than half the leaf size. The peduncle is cylindrical or has one keel. The spathe is flat and elliptic or ovate, with an acute angle to the peduncle. A stipe is recorded as shorter than the spadix or absent. The berries have a green apex and white base.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
