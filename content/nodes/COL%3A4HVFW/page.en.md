## A small cactus recorded without central spines {#s-d83c6d0dcf}
This upright cactus branches only at the base and has more than ten ribs, isolated areoles and no central spines. The source allows the flowering zone to be absent or superficial. Its funnel-shaped flower tube is vinaceous, the fruit scar is deeply inset and the seed coat is warty. Rock-associated habitat is recorded.
