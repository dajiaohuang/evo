## What is recorded about Hornschuchia alba? {#s-fbebd30051}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes oval to lance-shaped leaves with variable tips and wedge-shaped or decurrent bases. The inflorescences occur on leafy branches. The fruit segments range from elliptic to globose, as do the seeds.

The source categorizes it as terrestrial, with no locality named.
