## Pinna counts and fruit measurements in the entry {#s-992e9cdd40}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records 10–13 pinnae. The calyx is described as pinkish white, and translucent glands are present. The legume’s wing is listed as larger than its seed bearing core, and fruit length is recorded as 5–6 cm. The habitat field assigns the terrestrial category. These regional data fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
