## Growth and general form {#s-894a288b44}
The regional account describes a clump-forming, rhizomatous perennial 20–40 cm tall. Its stems are unbranched, and the lateral flower clusters are sessile. The leaves are narrow and linear; flowers range from lilac to cream with a dark centre, and the capsules are elongated.

These traits come from a regional South African account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Flower and fruit details {#s-1457819f92}
A 2020 regional diagnostic account describes a cylindrical ovary and elongated capsules 50–60 mm long, sometimes reaching 75 mm—the longest in its subgenus. Flowers are pale lilac, mauve, or whitish, with a dark greenish-black central eye 6–7 mm across that grades to turquoise at the edges. The filaments and style are also dark greenish black. Tepals measure 22–26 × 12–15 mm, with the outer three slightly smaller than the inner three. The anthers are about 5.5 mm long and bear orange pollen.

These diagnostic details come from a regional South African account published in 2020; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-a77e9ef263}
The regional flora records the species on clay and granite slopes in fynbos or renosterveld.

This habitat description comes from a regional South African account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
