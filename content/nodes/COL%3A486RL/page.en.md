## Nymphoides thunbergiana {#s-ff30befa8c}
## Yellow flower clusters grow in still or slow water

Herman and Retief’s 1997 South African flora guide describes a perennial water plant arising from a short, upright rhizome. Its flower clusters usually bear additional roots. Many yellow flowers grow in each cluster, set some distance from the leaf axil—the angle where a leaf joins the stem. The capsule is oblong-ovoid with a blunt tip. The habitat account records still to slow-flowing water in pans, rivers, or streams.
