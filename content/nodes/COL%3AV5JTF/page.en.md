## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-2a73af97db}
The 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release accepts Boulenophrys jiulianensis (J. Wang, Zeng, Lyu & Y. Y. Wang in Wang et al., 2019) as a species. Its recorded placement is Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). This identifies the catalogue name and classification; biological observations below retain the scope of their own sources.

## Jiulian and Nankun records in the cited account {#s-84b8ae5caf}
The monograph listed male holotype (the name-bearing specimen designated in the original description) SYS a002112 from Daqiutian Protection Station, Jiulian Mountain, Jiangxi, at 560 m. Its cited distribution paragraph described Jiulian and Nankun Mountain, Guangdong, at 500–800 m and characterized the frog as common. That qualitative wording is not a standardized abundance estimate, and these locality records do not establish continuous occupancy or complete present distribution.

## Measurements of the examined type series {#s-e361e59c7e}
The examined type material contained ten adult males and two females. Diagnostic snout–vent length (measured from the tip of the snout to the vent)s were 30.4–33.9 mm in males and 34.1–37.5 mm in females. These measurements retain their sex and sample size and do not establish population-wide size bounds or growth trajectories.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-3df0418002}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys jiulianensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. That statement belongs to the paper’s comparison; it is not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. This single comparison does not show how often the character occurs across the species or provide a complete diagnosis, diet or developmental account.
