## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora contracta? {#s-a3365068b2}
The woody stem is cylindrical. Entire leaves have smooth edges, no ocelli and a velvety surface; petiole glands are absent. Stipules and bracts are narrow, from threadlike to lance-shaped. The inflorescence is a raceme. Flowers lack sepal awns and have a bell-shaped floral cup with two rows of corona filaments. The operculum is divided into lobes, and the limen is cup-shaped. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless; oblong seeds have crosswise ridges or pits.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-32ee634c6c}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
