## A slightly larger relative of R. minutiflora {#s-c1fdf796c1}
Romulea sinispinosensis has white flowers 15–20 mm long with a yellow cup and slightly larger floral parts than R. minutiflora. The cited treatment regards it as polyploid (having more than two sets of chromosomes) and raises a possible hybrid origin, but does not resolve that hypothesis.
