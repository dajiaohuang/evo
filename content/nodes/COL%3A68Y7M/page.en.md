## ## Leaflet arrangement and floral parts {#s-64e3d0099a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records seven to thirteen leaflets per leaf, arranged opposite or nearly opposite on the rachis. The leaflets are oblong to lance-shaped, papery and long-tailed at the tip; their lower surfaces range from finely hairy to nearly hairless. Flowers are radially symmetric, with a tubular or cup-shaped calyx and fifteen stamens. The anthers are hairless on the upper face and hairy below. This is one regional morphology row, not a full account of variation.
