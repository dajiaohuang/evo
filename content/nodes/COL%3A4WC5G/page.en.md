## Selaginella mucugensis {#s-dc9ea9e224}
## Rounded lateral leaves and varied tips

Its stems are upright or ascending, pale straw-coloured, smooth and without joints; rhizophores are listed at axillary or dorsal positions. The small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are ovate with rounded bases and tips recorded as blunt or acute. Dorsal leaves are ovate or elliptic, with asymmetric bases and shortly acuminate tips; axillary leaves are ovate with rounded bases and acute or blunt tips. The entry records idioblasts as absent from the lateral and dorsal leaves.
