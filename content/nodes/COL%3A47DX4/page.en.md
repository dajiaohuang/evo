## What leaf, flower-stalk and fruit-wing traits distinguish Niedenzuella stannea? {#s-2328203058}
The ovate leaves have a dull, silky-haired lower surface and margins without glands. Two glands sit near the tip of each petiole. Flower stalks are at most 1 mm long, and each fruit segment has two lateral wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-89ac7f2052}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
