## The name and this data account {#s-f2f160f329}
AVONET v7 lists *Andigena laminirostris* under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The sample contains 6 measured individuals, 4 in a complete morphological set; individual trait means may use different sample sizes.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-cc30fdec05}
Mean beak length is 76.6 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width at that landmark is 26 mm and depth 28.8 mm. These standardized measurements are not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-90a42a740a}
Mean unflattened wing length (carpal joint to longest primary) is 169.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 41.7 mm. AVONET separately lists mean mass of 311 g in the Dunning category, not a weight for every specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-d79d4da0f2}
AVONET codes habitat Forest, trophic niche Frugivore and lifestyle Insessorial (perching-associated). These broad comparison labels do not mean the species uses only forest or eats only fruit.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-9824841edf}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span −2.19° to 1.49° latitude. They do not describe all non-breeding movement or current global occupancy.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-508b671399}
Non-zero diet weights are fruit 80%, invertebrates 10%, and mammals and birds 10%. Confidence code A indicates high compiler confidence. These percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than measure diet composition or identify prey species.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-a174b64f9b}
Species-level foraging weights are trees or tall bushes from 2 m to below the canopy 90%, and understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m 10%. They describe feeding positions, not nesting, all habitat or daily time use; strata are not elevations or flight heights.

## Evidence limits {#s-2338750a3c}
AVONET morphometrics are sample-based species averages, while mass comes from a separate source. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map endpoints describe historical breeding/resident geography. EltonTraits converts literature descriptions into relative diet and foraging weights, not direct measurements. The historical name association does not establish equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Underlying works have not all been reviewed here; current range, population, conservation and complete biology require separate sources.
