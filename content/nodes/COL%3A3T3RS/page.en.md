## Three substrate categories in the flora record {#s-d3045fe1cb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists *Corticícola* (growing on bark).

It also lists *Epífila*, meaning growth on leaves.

The third category is *Rupícola* (growing on rock).

These source labels describe substrates, not collection localities; they provide no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Scope of the record {#s-23974fe51c}
The habitat field does not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat labels do not describe life cycle or reproduction; neither was assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

This regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
