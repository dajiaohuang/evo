## Golden bristles and a curved pod {#s-1216e91728}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, spiral leaves carry two pairs of elliptic to oblong leaflets, each 0.5–1.7 cm long. Short golden bristles are recorded on the stems and around the flower clusters. The yellow flowers have ten stamens. Its brown pod is curved and described as 3–5 cm long, with bristly hairs on the surface. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
