## *Itasina filifolia*: simple leaves and sporadic spiny sepals {#s-7c152c9607}
The cited South African regional account describes a tufted perennial to 45 cm. Its linear to threadlike leaves are sometimes spatula-shaped and are usually dead by flowering. White flowers form compound umbels on branched stalks; the narrow elliptic fruit is ribbed, and its narrow sepals may end in spines.

Both seedling and mature leaves are described as simple, meaning undivided. The account notes variation in plant size, leaf size and calyx shape, with spine-tipped calyx lobes occurring only sporadically. It places the species on sandstone and limestone flats; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequencies.
