## A woody base and variable flower clusters {#s-23d29f846a}
The record notes a woody underground base and narrow, nearly stalkless leaves, with shapes ranging from lance-like to rounded and margins from smooth to toothed. Flower heads may form racemes or corymbs, or appear singly in leaf axils. Separate heads contain either tubular male florets or slender female florets; rock-associated and terrestrial substrates are both recorded.
