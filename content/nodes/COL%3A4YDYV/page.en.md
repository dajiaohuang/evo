## Limits of the historical life-history and ecology records {#s-d1fd19aeb9}
Life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 archive. Except for maximum longevity, summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespan or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names and families and same-name rows support the association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and publications have not been individually reviewed.

EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete species monograph; matching names do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized relative weights from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation need other evidence.
