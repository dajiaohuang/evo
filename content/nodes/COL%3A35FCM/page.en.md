## Small blue flowers on a slender annual {#s-eadef1e4a8}
This account describes an annual herb usually 5–50 cm tall, sometimes reaching 75 cm. The leaves range from lance-shaped to ovate and may be sessile or stalked, with toothed or jagged edges. The flower clusters may carry a few to about 30 small flowers, usually in shades of dark blue, purple or lilac, rarely white. The narrowly ellipsoid capsules are 3–8 mm long. The recorded range extends from central Mexico through the Central American highlands and the Andes from Colombia to Peru and Bolivia.
