## Cream flowers in clustered heads {#s-bbf76081c8}
The morphology record describes an upright plant with a swollen underground base. The upper and lower leaf surfaces differ in their covering: below, the leaves are woolly to shaggy; above, they are cobwebby, woolly or becoming nearly hairless. Flower heads occur in clusters rather than being scattered throughout the inflorescence, and the corollas are cream-colored. The habitat field says terrestrial.
