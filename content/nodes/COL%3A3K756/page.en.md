## Helichrysum plebeium {#s-93f78399f8}
## Strongly rolled leaf margins and a smooth receptacle

Manning and Goldblatt's account compares this species with *H. hebelepis*. It distinguishes *H. plebeium* by its consistently linear to linear-oblong leaves with strongly rolled-under margins, and by a smooth receptacle rather than a honeycombed or fringed one. The source habitat field lists sandstone slopes; this comparison does not define a complete range.
