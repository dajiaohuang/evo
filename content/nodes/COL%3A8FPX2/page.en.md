## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f1499f5625}
COL26.8 records Trachylepis keroanensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-b2b4435f31}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 61.5 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-e06f5d9c1b}
The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-e49082695b}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 4.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
