## Narrow pink petals on a small rigid plant {#s-e4fe7f27fc}
The Latin description records a rigid, smooth plant about 30 cm tall with a woody root and pale, upright branches. The succulent leaves are narrow and rounded in cross-section; their surfaces have small, rounded papillae. Two to five flowers occur loosely at branch tips. The flowers lack staminodes; their very narrow petals are white below and pink above. The habitat field records the term “koppie.”
