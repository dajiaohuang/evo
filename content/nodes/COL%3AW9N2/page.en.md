## Clivia caulescens: leaves, flowers and comparisons {#s-e9dd9fd6a6}
A 1997 regional account describes an evergreen perennial herb reaching 0.65 m in height. It records several dark-green, strap-shaped leaves arranged in two ranks, each 300–400 × 30 mm. The peduncle is 300 mm long, and the umbel bears 15–20 flowers. The perianth is about 40 mm long; the flowers are deep red, green-tipped and yellow-margined.
