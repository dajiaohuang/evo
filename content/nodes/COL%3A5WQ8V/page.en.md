## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-af37600e5b}
Variable dark green, brown and orange dorsal/lateral patterns with an orange belly; preserved animals are darker with cream ventral areas. These are the authors’ comparative observations rather than a standardized colour-chart measurement. Colour and specimen counts vary by trait; similar colours recur among species, so a single hue is not a complete identification rule. Preserved appearance should not be substituted for live appearance.
