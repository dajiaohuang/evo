## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3d1e48c806}
COL26.8 records Ramphotyphlops lorenzi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Typhlopidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-67e1512e60}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 337 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-d8f9ed6422}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b54ad6719c}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
