## A spring-head record {#s-bfd2901efd}
The 2025 treatment places the type locality at Bouboukas spring, near the road to Maneli in Achaia/Kalavryta, north-west Peloponnese, Greece (38°00′53″N, 21°58′31″E; 703 m). It identifies the holotype as NHMW-MO-113908, collected on 12 September 2007, and says the species was known only from this type locality at the time of the account. The record describes spring-head habitat; it does not map unsampled sites.

## What the COI sequence can show {#s-ed8b5abf92}
The study assigns sampled material to mitochondrial operational unit mOTU F and reports COI sequence accession KC011744. COI is a mitochondrial marker; the paper cautions that these data do not resolve deeper phylogenetic relationships. The mOTU label is a study-specific sequence grouping, not a species-tree placement.

## The treatment’s conservation assessment {#s-66ec286cb7}
The authors assess the species as Endangered (EN). That is the treatment’s assessment; an independent current Red List record was not verified in this dossier.

## Evidence limits {#s-5d4e05568b}
This page follows one species account published in 2025. Its locality statement reflects the authors’ reported material and study period, not a complete range survey. The EN category is attributed to the authors and is not presented as an independently verified current listing.
