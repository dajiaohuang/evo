## Begonia collaris {#s-ed0caf976e}
## A ring-like collar sits at the petiole tip

Begonia collaris has erect stems and deciduous stipules. Its hairless petiole ends in a thin, ring-shaped membrane. The strongly asymmetric basal leaf blades are transversely elliptic and toothed; the upper surface is hairless, while the underside bears scale-shaped hairs. Male flower segments are hairless; female segments may be smooth or glandular, with toothed or crenulate margins. Bracteoles are present. The capsule wings are unequal and the seed tip blunt.
