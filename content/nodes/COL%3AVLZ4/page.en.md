## What does Citharexylum cooperi look like? {#s-2c2ede4b95}
This shrub or small tree reaches 8 m, with a spreading crown and a trunk up to 15 cm across. Its opposite leaves are leathery, oval to elliptic, and up to 20 cm long. Dense, branched racemes carry fragrant white flowers. The orange, rounded fruit is about 8 mm long; the calyx hardens and thickens around its base as the fruit develops.

## Where is it reported? {#s-96ba37d5f3}
The source reports the species from clearings and plains in Costa Rica and Panama. It does not state that these records define its complete range.
