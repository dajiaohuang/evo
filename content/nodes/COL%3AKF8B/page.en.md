## Name and AVONET sample {#s-2ca7085b9c}
COL26.8 accepts Balaeniceps rex within Balaenicipitidae. AVONET v7 associates its data with the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Six individuals were measured and four had a complete morphological set; usable samples may differ among traits.

## Beak dimensions {#s-160678dec0}
Mean beak length is 174 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 88.9 mm and depth 87.2 mm at that edge. These separate measurements use standardized landmarks and are not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-b42378f4e6}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 642.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 243.8 mm. AVONET’s compiled mean mass is 5941.01 g, sourced to Dunning. This is separate species-average information, not necessarily the mass of the measured specimens.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-0c6d65607f}
AVONET records Wetland habitat, an Aquatic predator trophic niche and a Terrestrial primary lifestyle. These broad labels support comparison; they do not describe every habitat or food used.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-7305fe86dd}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude −12.61° to 10.32°. These endpoints do not describe all non-breeding movements, a current position or occupancy throughout the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## Limits across these historical sources {#s-a4bd6a8913}
This page combines AVONET v7, the August 2015 life-history archive and EltonTraits 1.0; it is not a complete species account. AVONET morphology values are sample means and its mass has separate provenance; ecology categories are broad and maps are historical. The 2015 archive reports compilation summaries rather than individual measurements and may retain taxonomic inheritance. Source conditions are not consistently known and underlying papers were not individually reviewed. EltonTraits weights come from literature descriptions, not direct measurements. Same-name, same-family associations do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Current ecology, range, population and conservation require other sources.
