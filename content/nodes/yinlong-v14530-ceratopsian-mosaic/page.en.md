## What Yinlong’s articulated skeleton says about ceratopsian traits {#s-fc20c8665a}
Holotype IVPP V14530 from China’s Upper Shishugou Formation preserves a nearly complete articulated skeleton with a skull about 18 centimetres long. Its recorded 161.2–155.7 Ma interval is formation-level, not a direct date on the specimen. The character combination informed matrix-based discussion of basal ceratopsian and marginocephalian relationships; coding, outgroups and sampled taxa affect that topology. The mosaic helps diagnose the fossil but does not establish direct ancestry to later ceratopsians.
