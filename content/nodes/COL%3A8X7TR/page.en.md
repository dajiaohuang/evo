## Classification and source scope {#s-d8a0ad7cee}
COL26.8 lists *Syagrus cataphracta* as an accepted species in *Syagrus*, within the palm family Arecaceae. The details below come from one regional Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field; its alternative forms remain separate.

## Stem and leaves {#s-17c39c6ced}
The stem is recorded as solitary or clustered, aerial and very short; its surface may retain leaf sheaths or show conspicuous scars. The pinnate leaves are glaucous, a blue-grey or waxy-looking colour. Their persistent sheaths wrap helically around the stem and have fibrous margins; the petiole margin is unarmed, and the leaf axis is slightly curved. Leaflets may be leathery or membranous, grouped and set at differing angles. They are flexible, with asymmetric pointed tips; their surfaces are described as glaucous, green, differently coloured, or silver on the underside.

## Inflorescence and fruit {#s-59b99791dc}
The inflorescence may be an erect or hanging panicle, and the fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves. The grooved main axis is sparsely woolly; its smaller branches are arranged on one side. Fruit is ovoid or elliptic, with a green-yellow woolly outer layer, fibrous succulent middle layer, and woody fusiform inner layer. The seed's endosperm is homogeneous.

The field records a solitary or clustered aerial stem, very short, with leaf-sheath remnants or conspicuous scars; glaucous pinnate leaves with persistent helically arranged, fibrous-margined sheaths, unarmed petiole margins, and a slightly curved rachis; leathery or membranous pinnae inserted at differing angles, grouped and flexible, with asymmetric acuminate tips and glaucous, green, discoloured, or silver lower surfaces; an erect or pendulous panicle and exposed interfoliar infructescence; a grooved spadix with a sparsely tomentose rachis and one-sided rachillae; ovoid or elliptic fruit with a green-yellow tomentose epicarp, fibrous succulent mesocarp, woody fusiform endocarp, and homogeneous endosperm.
