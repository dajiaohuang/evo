## Mikania microdonta {#s-030db2e5ba}
## Five-veined leaves with long-tapered tips

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, hairy branches with solid pith. Opposite, ovate leaves have toothed edges, heart-shaped bases and long-tapered, sometimes tail-like tips. The upper surface becomes nearly smooth, while the lower is hairy; five main veins radiate from the base. The leathery blades have no interpetiolar stipules.

Loose panicles or corymb-like clusters carry stalked heads. Long, linear bracts occur at the base of each head stalk; the elliptic bracts around a head are fringed and acute. The bell-shaped corolla widens beyond its tube and has triangular, smooth lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional record supplies no locality or full range.
