## *Selaginella amazonica*: differently shaped leaves above ventral rhizophores {#s-8063f85781}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional profile describes prostrate, pale, hairless stems without joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise on the lower side of the stem. The small leaves differ by position: those at the sides have blunt bases and pointed tips, while upper leaves have ear-shaped or slanted bases and tapering tips. Leaf-like structures in the axils are ovate-deltate and pointed.

The source records an idioblast, a specialized cell type, in upper leaves but not in the lateral ones. The associated habitat label is terrestrial; no locality or survey date is supplied.
