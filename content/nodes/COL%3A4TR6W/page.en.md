## Velvety leaves and pitcher-shaped floral filaments {#s-b8fe0874f5}
This shrub has branches about 300 mm long and blackish grey internodes. Its sea-green leaves are round to club-shaped, blunt-tipped and covered in long papillae. Solitary flowers are 26–30 mm across with six sepals; magenta petals are about 1 mm wide. Staminodes and filaments form a pitcher-like arrangement, purple at the tips and pink below. The fruit has six chambers; granitic-derived soils are listed.
