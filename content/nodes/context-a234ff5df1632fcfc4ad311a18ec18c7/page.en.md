## The source concept {#s-d037745f25}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Xanthophryne koynayensis* in row Anf0904 under Xanthophryne, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-fd642197ce}
For Anf0904, the compilation tags terrestrial as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-7921f15ab0}
The row carries a twilight activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Seasonal activity {#s-759b98bb9e}
The seasonal fields tag wet and warm. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-6e2d4d81b4}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 31.8 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-d04e72e62b}
The row records 35 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every individual, clutch or offspring.
