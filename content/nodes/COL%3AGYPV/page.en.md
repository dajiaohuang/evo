## Species score and site score are different {#s-e1456e47ec}
For one species, SI is the sum of DBS, TBS and SBS. A site's RABI is the sum of SI scores for every recorded species; it is not divided by the number of species. So SI 6 for Arthroleptis adolfifriederici is not the score of a site where it was found, an extinction probability or an effect size. The study assigns stepwise weights of 1, 2, 4 or 8 for DBS; 0, 1, 2, 4 or 8 for TBS; and 0, 1, 2 or 4 for SBS. These weights define the authors' ranking system; they do not show that risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles at each step. The reported positive correlations among sub-indices also mean their inputs are not independent in this sample.
