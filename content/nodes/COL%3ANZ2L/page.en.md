## Stem, leaf base, and fruit {#s-a5b68e8cc8}
The source records a solitary aerial stem and a toothed pseudopetiole, the stalk-like portion at the base of a leaf. The spathe is woody and hairless, and the inflorescence is branched. Both the endocarp (the fruit's inner layer) and the fruit itself are described as ovoid.

The field records a solitary aerial stem; a toothed pseudopetiole; a branched inflorescence with a woody, hairless spathe; and ovoid endocarp and fruit.
