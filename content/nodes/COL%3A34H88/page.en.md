## Delosperma calitzdorpense {#s-4943c9a803}
## White flowers rise above spreading, keeled leaves

The Greater Cape flora account describes spreading leaves with a central keel and one to three white flowers. It includes both a general note that shrublets exceed 10 cm and a species description of a densely branched, grey-stemmed shrublet up to 10 cm tall. The fruits are unknown, and the habitat entry is uncertain.
