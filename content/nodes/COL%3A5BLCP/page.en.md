## What does the record say about its leaflets and flower clusters? {#s-be3234e405}
The central leaflet of Vitex sellowiana is recorded as elliptic or ovate, with a tapering tip and pointed base; its upper and lower surfaces differ in color. The description notes more than three leaflets and domatia, small structures at leaf-vein junctions. Flower clusters bear more than seven flowers, with stalks longer than the calyx; the clusters may be loose or congested. These are alternatives in a regional flora description, not a measure of population variation.

The habitat field classifies Vitex sellowiana as terrestrial. It gives no collection locality or complete range.
