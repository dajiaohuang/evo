## A fragment from a drill core {#s-da0206a0d4}
This single fossil is a forewing part and counterpart from the Leonard IV horizon in the Paskov Mine drill core, Czech Republic. Part of its venation is preserved. The 325–323 Ma envelope dates this one sample; it is not the global range of winged insects or a date for the origin of flight.

The Paskov specimen preserves a forewing in two matching pieces from the Upper Silesian Basin. Its vein pattern provides the anatomical evidence used in the Archaeorthoptera study. The record concerns this fragment and its horizon, not a whole insect body.

## A local age, not a global timeline {#s-2de3860b05}
The rounded lowermost Namurian envelope for the Leonard IV horizon is 325–323 Ma. It locates the studied wing in time but cannot establish when winged insects or powered flight first evolved.
