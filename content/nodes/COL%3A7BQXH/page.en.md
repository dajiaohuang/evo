## Tetracera willdenowiana Steud. {#s-0547844887}
## Coriaceous leaves with several recorded tip forms

Tetracera willdenowiana has alternate, simple, leathery leaves with grooved petioles. The blade is lance-shaped to elliptic or ovate, and the source records several possible tip forms: rounded, blunt, notched, sharply pointed or squared off. Its base is rounded or blunt; the edge may be entire or finely toothed, with 9–15 lateral veins that loop near the margin. Branched flower clusters occur at branch tips or along the sides. Flowers have five round sepals, three or four obovate to oblong petals, and three carpels. Each follicle bears one seed with a fringed aril.
