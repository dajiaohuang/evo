## What leaf and flower traits distinguish this entry? {#s-b3563917b6}
The alternate, elliptic leaves have tapered tips, serrated margins and no hairs; domatia are absent. The inflorescence is a pseudo-raceme. Petals are densely woolly above and hairy below. The filaments are free, the connective is covered with whitish woolly hairs, and the ovary has golden woolly hairs. Each carpel has one ovule. The symmetrical fruit is under 1.2 cm long, with one seed per valve. Seed shape is unknown, but the seed surface is hairless.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-ab1938cfaf}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
