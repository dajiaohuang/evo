## AmphiBIO species record {#s-3265115fd6}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Eupsophus septentrionalis? Row Anf1373 places it in genus Eupsophus, family Cycloramphidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Developmental category in the record {#s-e002de3363}
The AmphiBIO record assigns this source concept the developmental category “a larval stage”. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
