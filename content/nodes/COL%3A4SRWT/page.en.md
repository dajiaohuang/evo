## What leaf, flower and fruit structures are described? {#s-3f96eaf3ab}
The stem is thalloid or irregular, short or inconspicuous, and unbranched. The leaf stalk has an expanded base and is flattened sideways or winged. Thick, leathery blades fork repeatedly, ending in lance-shaped to triangular lobes. Numerous flowers occur in clusters. The mature flower sheath (spathella) is funnel-shaped; the cylindrical flower stalk has no inversely cone-shaped tip. Anthers have blunt or notched tips. The ovary is ovoid or ellipsoid, and the parallel, awl-shaped stigmas are fully hardened. The fruit has only slight wings and inconspicuous ribs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-bac407867e}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as aquatic and gives no locality.
