## An attributed 1974 historical Bd detection {#s-f56c63e649}
The authors cited a Bd-positive chico collected in 1974 at El Chico National Park, Hidalgo, as the nearest early detection to their magnipes study area, about 130 km south of its 1969 positive sample. This is a cited historical detection, not a newly sampled chico population or a verified contemporary infection rate. Geographic spacing and date order do not demonstrate a transmission route or confirm that the historical fungus was Bd-GPL; the paper requested successful historical genotyping to resolve such interpretations.
