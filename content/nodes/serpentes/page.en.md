## A combined tip-dating analysis {#s-aa6416fd89}
A combined genomic, phenomic, and fossil analysis estimates Pan-Serpentes near 128.5 Ma and places crown snakes about 20 million years later. The 128.5 Ma–present display is model-dependent. Reconstructed early ecology and ancestral states are analysis results, not observed ancestors or an exact date for the origin of snakes.

The study integrates sampled snake genomes, phenomic data, and fossils. Its tip-dating model estimates the pan-serpent lineage near 128.5 Ma, with crown Serpentes inferred later. Those dates depend on the model and the included evidence; they are not fossil first-appearance dates.

## Reconstructed states are hypotheses {#s-a921ee096b}
The study also reconstructs early ecological and ancestral states. These are model outputs inferred from the sampled tree, not observations of a known ancestor. The displayed route to the present summarizes that analysis rather than a complete fossil range.
