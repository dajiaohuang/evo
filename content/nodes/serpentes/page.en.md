## A model-dependent total-group and crown estimate {#s-aa6416fd89}
A combined genomic, phenomic and fossil analysis estimates Pan-Serpentes near 128.5 Ma and crown snakes near 110.3 Ma. These are model-based divergence estimates from the sampled tree, not fossil first appearances or precise observed splitting dates.

## Reconstructed states are hypotheses {#s-a921ee096b}
The study reconstructs early ecological and ancestral states from its sampled phylogeny. These are model outputs, not observations of a known ancestor. Read them with the analysis assumptions and sampling limits; the results do not establish one exact early snake ecology.
