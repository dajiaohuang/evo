## Coccoloba acuminata {#s-3a714381c1}
## A shrub or tree with large leaves and three-angled fruit

The source describes Coccoloba acuminata as a shrub 1–2 m tall or a tree reaching 8 m. Its lance-shaped leaves are 11–24 cm long; the lower surface and veins vary in hairiness, while the upper surface is glossy. Flowering sprays are 15–29 cm long. The subglobose fruit is strongly three-angled, 6–8 mm wide, and surrounded by a fleshy red perianth when fresh that turns black on drying. The shiny achene is chestnut to pale tan and also three-angled. The recorded range includes Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela, Guyana and Brazil.
