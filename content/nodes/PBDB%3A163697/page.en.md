## Read the preserved skull before the leaf interpretation {#s-82fde51f6b}
Start with SMNK PAL 3828 in [L. sibbicki](Ludodactylus_sibbicki/page.en.md), separating skull and mandible, compression and crest cutting, provenance and morphology. Continue to leaf position, botanical analogies and the accidental-ingestion hypothesis, then the 2018 hyoid comparison. These different lines of evidence do not combine into a settled complete life history. The database collection record and the type specimen also retain their different counting units.

## Naming history and dated classification {#s-beba365a7a}
The 2003 authors explain the genus name through ludus, game or play, and dactylus, finger, referring to toy forms combining teeth with a posterior crest. The species name honors artist John Sibbick. The original description places the genus in its dated Ornithocheiridae framework, a historical classification that later proposals may revise.

## Evidence limits {#s-697d61550a}
The 2003 description is available here as OCR-transcribed text, which cannot support checking figure detail, page layout or specimen condition directly. The 2018 hyoid comparison does not represent a complete preserved tongue. The identity of the leaf, presence of a gular pouch, ingestion and cause of death remain interpretations rather than directly preserved evidence; later classification and the referral of additional fossils also remain uncertain.
