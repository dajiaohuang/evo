## Chamaecrista punctata {#s-17c46b764b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista punctata* records 2–4 elliptic to oblanceolate leaflet pairs, yellow unequal petals, twisted oblong mature legumes. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
