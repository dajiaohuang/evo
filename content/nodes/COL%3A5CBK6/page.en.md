## Prostrate leaves and long-tubed white flowers {#s-38d4a00d21}
Xenoscapa fistulosa is a small corm-bearing plant (its corm is a swollen underground storage stem), 3–20 cm tall, with prostrate ovate leaves and small white flowers with long tubes. The flora reports flowering from August to October.

## Damp clay and a western regional range {#s-35c1035f2f}
The account records damp clay, sometimes over granite, and gives a regional range from southern Namibia and the western Karoo to Montagu, Vredenburg and the Cape Peninsula. This is a regional flora record, not an exhaustive global range.
