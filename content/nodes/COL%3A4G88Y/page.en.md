The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field for Philodendron meridionale describes an erect stem, cordate or ovate leaves, and a spadix with several flower zones. Its ecology field supplies no concrete habitat category, so this description stays with the anatomy actually recorded.

## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-5cecc1fd3a}
The field records clasping and feeding roots. The stem is erect, with a deciduous wine-coloured prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the smooth, cylindrical petiole is grooved above and rounded below. The entire blade is cordate or ovate, with 3–5 primary lateral veins on both sides. The axillary sympodial inflorescence is listed with 1 or 2 units; its peduncle is shorter than the nonconstricted spathe. The spathe tube is light green outside and pinkish inside with reddish resin; the outer and inner surfaces are light green, while the outer blade is whitish. The stipitate spadix is divided into female, sterile-male, fertile-male, and apical sterile-male zones.

## Flowers and recorded alternatives {#s-cac0a39f28}
The flowers have short stamens and staminodes. The gynoecium is ovoid and the ovary subcylindrical; the field lists 6–10 locules and 3–5 ovules, basal placentation, and a capitate stigma. Berry and seed characters are unknown in the field.
