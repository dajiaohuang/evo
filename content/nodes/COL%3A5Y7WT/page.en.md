## Scope of the evidence {#s-a5c88c2869}
The report combines camera trapping, interviews and spatial analysis across a proposed transboundary conservation landscape. The two event totals cannot be read as a census or a species-wide abundance comparison, and the report is not a peer-reviewed population estimate.
