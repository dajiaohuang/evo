## A four-angled stem and winged calyx {#s-24cdfdd87d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a four-angled stem and membranous leaves; leaf venation is listed as acrodromous–brochidodromous. The flowers are yellow. Their calyx is winged, with acute ovate to lance-shaped lobes, and the corolla is funnel-shaped with obovate lobes that also end acutely. The filaments have crenulate membranous wings, and the seed coat is pitted.

## Terrestrial habitat category {#s-40a24b33ca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Schultesia piresiana* as terrestrial but supplies no collection locality.
