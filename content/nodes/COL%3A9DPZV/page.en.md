## Leaflet, flower and seed characters in the entry {#s-b293ae9a8c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records branches as slightly pubescent when young and glabrescent when mature. Leaflets are elliptic to obovate, with rounded basal pairs and the terminal leaflet widest. The rachis is slightly pubescent and sometimes grooved on the adaxial surface; petiole status is unknown, and petiolules are pubescent. Axillary inflorescences have a simple axis or occur in pairs; the axis is slightly pubescent, pedicels sparsely strigose, and deltoid bracts persist or fall by fruiting. The calyx is glabrous inside and slightly, sparsely pubescent outside. Corolla petals are absent; the androecium has 8–10 stamens, and the glabrous style persists in fruit. Fruits are strigose to pubescent. Seeds are ellipsoid and red or dark brown to black when dry. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
