## Leaflets, flowers and segmented fruit {#s-cb9b151841}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records petioles up to 1.5 cm and more than 15 leaflets per leaf, each under 3 cm long. The erect leaflets are boat-shaped, with veins that loop and join near the edge; shield-shaped glands are also recorded. Racemes are shorter than the leaves that subtend them. Their bracts are not leaflike, have squared-off bases and are under 1 cm; gray hairs occur on the inflorescence axis, bracts and calyx. Flowers are asymmetrical, resupinate (turned from the usual orientation) and under 2 cm long, with a two-lipped calyx, reddish to orange petals and two forms of anther. The fruit is flattened side-to-side and divided by crosswise partitions; the entry records up to ten seeds. These traits come from one regional row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-ed08981371}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It supplies only a habitat category; there is no locality, range or sampling coverage.
