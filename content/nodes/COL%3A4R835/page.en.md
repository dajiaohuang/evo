## White-dotted leaves and golden flowers {#s-052e0ae733}
This perennial succulent herb grows to about 4 cm high. Its widely spreading leaves have broad bases, taper to their tips and reach 55 by 10 mm; white dots mark the surface. Golden petals form flowers 29–34 mm across, with filaments white below and pale yellow at the tips. Flowering is recorded from October to February; the fruit has seven to ten chambers. Dwarf Karroid Shrubland, pans and rock crevices are given as habitat.
