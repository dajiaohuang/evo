## How to read reproductive records {#s-3985669a96}
The entry labels its reproductive mode oviparous and reports a consolidated mean of 4.78 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch and 1 litter/clutch per year. A clutch or litter summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily the arithmetic mean of observed clutches. A fractional summary does not mean a litter contained a fraction of an offspring. The lowest and highest fields are endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed individual-clutch limits. These reproductive labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
