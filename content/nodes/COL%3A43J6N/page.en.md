## Whorled narrow leaves and umbelliform cymes {#s-b30b1480a2}
The source morphology field records indumentum over the full surface of the branches. Leaves are whorled, narrowly lanceolate and flat-margined. Umbelliform cymes bear one to three flowers. The sepals are glabrous on the abaxial surface and at the margins. The corolla is bell-shaped, with lanceolate or oval lobes papillose to barbellate on the adaxial surface; the throat is barbellate. Ovate-lanceolate outer corona lobes are longer than the sessile or subsessile gynostegium. The ovate retinaculum is smaller than the pollinia; caudicles are horizontal and not geniculate, and the pollinia are elliptic. The habitat label is “Rupícola” (rupicolous, associated with rock). This single field does not establish variation or full diagnostic scope, and no locality or range was assessed.
