## Wolffiella neotropica {#s-7456e92131}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records free-floating fronds (leaf-like plant bodies) without roots, 2–8 mm long and 1.6–3.9 mm wide; two fronds may join at a vegetative pocket. The flower cavity is angled 100–120 degrees, and each frond bears one male and one female flower; the fruit is an utricle (a small, thin-walled fruit). The habitat field says aquatic. This is a regional description, not a population survey.
