## Portulaca oleracea {#s-6e3c82d664}
## A spreading herb with black, finely tubercled seeds

Portulaca oleracea is described as a smooth, fibrous-rooted herb that lies flat or rises slightly. Its alternate leaves are elliptic to obovoid, 1–3 cm long, and flowers are crowded, nearly sessile and yellow. The capsule is 6–9 mm long and splits around its circumference just below the middle. Nearly black seeds are about 0.5–0.75 mm across, with a finely tubercled seed coat. In Panama the source records the name verdolaga.
