## Recorded parent of Pepesuchinae {#s-26a1ae6be7}
Pepesuchinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Peirosauridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
