## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-666e29cc5f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical branches with a dense woolly covering and no stem lenticels (small pores). Deciduous stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) are listed as thread-like or triangular. The leaf stalk (petiole) is cylindrical, while the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) is winged; the field lists four, five or seven leaflet pairs. A stalkless, cup-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. Flower clusters are spike-like, the calyx (outer floral cup) tubular, and flowers lack separate stalks. The leathery pod is flat and woolly. This one regional field retains the listed leaflet-count alternatives; it does not establish their frequency or the full variation of the traits.

## Habitat category {#s-4bfda90a3d}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is a category recorded in one row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
