## Recorded parent of Omphalosauridae {#s-0625d288d6}
Omphalosauridae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Ichthyosauriformes as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
