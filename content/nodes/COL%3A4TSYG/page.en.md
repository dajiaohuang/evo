## *Rustia schunkeana* {#s-fa8feb50cc}
COL26.8 accepts the name *Rustia schunkeana* Delprete within *Rustia*, tribe Dialypetalantheae and subfamily Dialypetalanthoideae. Brazilian Flora 2020 contributes a single habitat entry, “Terrícola” (terrestrial). It identifies a category rather than a place: the field gives no locality, survey date, complete range, or wild-versus-cultivated status.
