## Rounded leaflets and twisting pods {#s-83b8015209}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with usually five to nine pairs of leaflets, and also notes a rarer three-pair form; the leaflets range from nearly round to oval with a triangular outline. It describes flower clusters at branch tips and beside the leaves. Yellow petals differ in shape, ten stamens are listed, and mature pod sides twist. The seeds are flattened and obovoid. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
