## A Carboniferous fossil at the hagfish stem {#s-fb03201c56}
Two Moscovian Francis Creek Shale fossils—holotype FMNH PF15373 (73.3 mm) and paratype FMNH PF8472 (62.5 mm)—preserve body outlines and soft-tissue traces combining cyclostome features with hagfish specializations. They support a stem or possible total-group hagfish interpretation, not crown Myxini. The material does not establish deep-water habitat, slime production or modern hagfish ecology, and some structures remain equivocal.
