## Bingling and nearby Wawu material {#s-06448bf0b8}
Male holotype CIB 950263 came from Bingling, Wawushan, Hongya, Sichuan, at 1480 m. The monograph examined one male and one female from Gufuping on Wawu Mountain at about 1560 m and summarized known distribution as the vicinity of the Hongya type locality at 1480–1560 m. These localities are not evidence of absence elsewhere or a measured elevation tolerance.

## Two newly measured adults and an unresolved printed unit {#s-7e8613eb90}
The newly examined male measured 44.8 mm snout–vent length and the female 59.2 mm. The same diagnostic sentence cited an earlier three-male value of 45.1–51.0 without printing its unit beside those endpoints; this page does not silently certify the missing unit. Diagnostic anatomy included weak vomerine ridges without teeth, a distinct subarticular tubercle at finger I but absent on other fingers, rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow fringes, and tiny dense black nuptial spines on fingers I and II in breeding males, with a subgular vocal sac.

## Corrected locality transliteration preserves the species epithet {#s-9721bd9b4a}
The remarks identified inconsistent Binling and Bingling spellings for the type locality in the original description. The monograph treated Bingling as the correct pinyin transliteration and revised the English common name accordingly, while preserving the originally published epithet binlingensis under its cited nomenclatural rules. This is an attributed spelling treatment, not a change of type locality or evidence of another species.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-65397f3119}
