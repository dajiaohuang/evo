## The source concept {#s-df69fde445}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Pelophryne saravacensis* in row Anf0748 under Pelophryne, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-c4e48cab53}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 22 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
