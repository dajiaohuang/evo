## Restio nudiflorus {#s-fde5542a12}
## Flowers extend beyond the bracts

Restio nudiflorus is described as a small, tufted perennial up to 20 cm, with tangled, branched stems covered in coarse tubercles. Its flowers extend beyond the bracts, and the sepals have blunt tips. Damp ledges and seeps are the recorded habitat.
