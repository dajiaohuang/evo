## What leaf and fruit traits mark Stachytarpheta cassiae? {#s-b160b37388}
The record describes upright, four-angled stems and opposite, round to oval leaves on stalks. Their edges are scalloped and may roll under; the upper surface has hairs and glands, while the lower is hairless. Blue, salver-shaped flowers have a four-toothed calyx. The dry fruit splits into two one-seeded segments, recorded as dark brown.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-46dc11fe01}
The dataset categorizes the species as terrestrial, without a collection locality.
