## Recorded parent of Lambeosaurinae {#s-d795b21ec7}
Lambeosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Hadrosauridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
