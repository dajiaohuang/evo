## Silky leaf undersides and compressed flower branches {#s-a4d4f692ba}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth with simple stem hairs. Leaves are elliptic or ovate; the upper midrib is grooved toward the stalk and flattens toward the tip, while the underside is silky-haired. Bracts or cataphylls below leaf nodes are absent. Small flowering branches are compressed; bracteoles beneath flowers are listed as falling away or unknown.

## A hairy flower disc and an urn-shaped fruit cup {#s-7452127601}
The field lists one or two inflorescences per axillary shoot, near the shoot tip or mixed with terminal clusters, in a pyramidal, opposite arrangement. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal, and hairs cover the floral disc. The flower cup is top-shaped and silky-haired. Sepals are separate, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. Fruit shape and fruit calyx lobes are unknown.

## A terrestrial label without a named place {#s-40ec803d6d}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The row gives no locality, survey date, or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material.
