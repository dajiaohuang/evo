## Recorded morphological characters {#s-f00b9f38e5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records young branches as cylindrical to subcylindrical. Leaves have deciduous stipules and membranous blades. In the flower description, the filaments are glabrous (without hairs), and the anthers are dorsifixed (attached on the back). The fruit retains its calyx. These characters are listed in one regional description field; the source does not assess their variation or diagnostic value.

## Recorded habitat category {#s-ee460f35ce}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels *Bathysa sylvestrae* terrestrial, but provides no collection locality.
