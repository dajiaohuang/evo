## Relative weights of food categories {#s-e97aa94a07}
EltonTraits assigns its only nonzero food weight to invertebrates: 100%. The A code signals high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. This percentage is a standardized reading of written descriptions, not a measured share of food; a zero does not establish that a category is never eaten. Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader labels than their everyday meanings and do not imply an exclusive diet of nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-2499a3b5cf}
The archive marks the species-level feeding-position estimate as ground 100%. It describes relative use of a feeding position, not nesting, the full range of habitat or time allocation across a day. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial positions have separate source definitions; they are not elevation bands and cannot provide flight heights or habitat area.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-8a6b718831}
This page guides readers through a historical EltonTraits 1.0 record matched by name and family; it is not a complete species monograph. A matching name and classification do not establish that the older species concept equals COL26.8. Food and bird feeding-position percentages are coarse relative weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy use or observation time. Bird diet codes A/B preserve source and proportional uncertainty. Mammal ABC diet or activity codes may also draw on a congener the compilers considered ecologically similar, so this page does not claim that every field is a direct observation of *C. rupicola*. Genus- or family-level typical values, bird strata not marked species-level and split records awaiting individual review were not used as definite conclusions. The underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Other sources are needed for individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation.
