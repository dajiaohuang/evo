## Pouteria polysepala T.D.Penn. {#s-349e909ba4}
## A flower calyx with seven to eleven sepals

Pouteria polysepala has alternate, spiral leaves and no stem lenticels; young-branch hairs vary in the source record. Branch-borne clusters carry flowers with an unusual seven to eleven sepals and a five-lobed tubular corolla. The ovary has five chambers. Fruits are smooth and hairless or becoming hairless; the source lists one, two or more than two seeds, with a broad seed scar. Fruit color is not recorded.
