## ## Lenticels and head-like flower clusters {#s-4d2c1f148b}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical, glabrous branches with lenticels, the small corky pores visible on young stems. The leaves have two pairs of leaflets, cylindrical petioles and leaf axes (rachises), and deciduous, awl-shaped stipules; nectaries are absent. Head-like inflorescences bear flowers on stalks, with bell-shaped calyces. Fruit shape, surface and valve texture are listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
