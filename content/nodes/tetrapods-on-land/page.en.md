## From fins to limbs and land: a mosaic of transitions {#s-869dec6cf8}
Across the 385–350 Ma display window, the review follows several anatomical changes rather than a single “fish to land” event. Fins, girdles, joints and digits appear in different combinations as researchers ask three separate questions: when did digit-bearing limbs arise, how did locomotion change, and when were vertebrates fully terrestrial? The chapter’s mosaic framing is a 2009 synthesis, not a new date for one event or a ladder of proven direct ancestors, and it predates later fossil discoveries.
