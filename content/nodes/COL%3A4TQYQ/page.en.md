## Ruschia hamata {#s-a67e340ed1}
## Strongly recurved leaf tips and seasonal pink-purple flowers

The 2017 Free State flora describes a non-spiny dwarf shrub up to 45 cm tall, with erect stems and rigid branches. Its leaves taper to a prominent, strongly recurved tip. The epidermis is raised over idioblasts, specialized cells that often lie in rows along the leaf keel and margins.

Flowers arise singly, but several may form during one season’s growth, giving the shoot a branched, panicle-like appearance. The flowers are about 16 mm across; petals range from pink to purple, and the purple staminodes (sterile stamens) have white tips. Flowering is recorded for December through February. The fruit has five chambers.

The guide lists the plant in Vredefort Dome Savanna, Kalahari Deciduous Acacia-wooded Grassland, Dry Grassland, Mesic Grassland and Dwarf Karroid Shrubland, and also records grassland habitat.
