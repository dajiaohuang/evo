## Trixis pruskii {#s-64f2ee461d}
## Yellow racemes with cream-coloured pappus

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes stems with solid interiors and no winged branches. Leaf surfaces are not varnished. Racemes bear yellow flowers whose inner and outer corolla surfaces are hairless. The pappus is cream-coloured. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
