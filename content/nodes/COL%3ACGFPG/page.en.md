## Soriculus beibengensis: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Soriculus beibengensis* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority X. Pei, Z. Chen, Q. Li, X. Li, C. Pu, K. Luo, J. Luo, M. Pu, H. Wang, L. Khanal, & X. Jiang, 2024. This node currently contains a classification record but no biological observation. The record therefore does not support a description of the animal’s form, habitat, range, or behavior.
