## Restio verrucosus {#s-a5c4f52e96}
## Uneven tubercles on a branched plant

The account describes Restio verrucosus as a tangled, tufted perennial up to 40 cm, with slender stems that branch heavily. Their tubercles are coarse and uneven. Seeps along shale bands between 400 and 600 m are the recorded habitat.
