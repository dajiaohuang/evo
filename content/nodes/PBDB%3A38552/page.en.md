## Recorded parent of Therizinosauridae {#s-06fb14cb0a}
Therizinosauridae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Therizinosauroidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
