## *Vittetia orbiculata*: opposite leaves and funnel-shaped corollas {#s-c198f20f32}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records a much-branched stem and opposite leaves. The overlapping bracts around each flower head are closely set but not fully overlapping. The corolla is funnel-shaped, and the fruitlets bear two kinds of hairs. Its substrate category is the broad label “terrestrial,” without a locality.
