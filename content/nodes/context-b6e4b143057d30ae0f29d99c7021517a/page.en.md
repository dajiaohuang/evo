## Source classification and concept status {#s-5c67c81361}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3004 records *Phyllomedusa rohdei* under the source classification Phyllomedusa (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-4b9ae0cc1f}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-cf3e59291c}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 36 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Annual reproductive output {#s-882d5ccffa}
The row lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO gives no clutch-size or offspring-or-egg-size values for this record; these fields are missing, not zero. The row does not give the population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind the annual value.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-fab36bef80}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
