## Life-history evidence limits {#s-a130f37aa2}
The 2015 archive reports compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. These are compiled summaries, not one individual's measurements, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies can share inherited data, so the same name, family and species-level rows do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently established, and the cited publications have not been individually reviewed here. EltonTraits estimates food as invertebrates 90% and fish 10%; species-level foraging stratum is on or just below the water surface at 100%. These percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than measure intake or time use. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the cited source and diet information; Ref_1 locates the citation but does not independently confirm it.
