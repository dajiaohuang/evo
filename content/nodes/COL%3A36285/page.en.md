## Large flowers with a conical base {#s-2f4e41b410}
Dierama pulcherrimum is described as a robust plant 0.9–1.8 m tall, with pendulous flowers 35–54 mm long in magenta-pink or purple-red. A diagnostic account notes the suberect tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) arising from a conical base, a feature that helps separate it from D. pendulum, whose tepals spread from a rounded base. Flowering is recorded from November to March.

## Grassland and forest habitats {#s-64aa3f1c09}
The regional account lists grasslands and forests, including Sub-Escarpment and Drakensberg Grassland, Albany Thicket, Sub-Escarpment Savanna and Indigenous Forests. This is the account’s habitat list, not a complete range survey.
