## Habitat labels in the record {#s-58b5b04e7a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists these habitat labels for Scaphyglottis stellata: epiphytic, rupicolous. epiphytic means a plant attached to another plant for physical support; rupicolous means a plant growing on rock or rocky surfaces. The field gives no collection locality or account of how each label applies across the species.
