## What is recorded for Croton muellerianus? {#s-a9acf1cabf}
Young branches have scale-like hairs. The short-stalked oval or elliptic leaves are hairless above and scale-haired beneath, with looping side veins and entire margins. Nectar glands and stipules at young shoot tips are absent. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are nearly sessile; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female flowers have separate sepals, equal calyx lobes, and a two-lobed style; female petals are absent. The bud arrangement is valvate. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
