## Selected Mexican survey localities, 2017–2018 {#s-abde82459b}
Figure 1 associates source-labelled Nyctanolis pernix with Cueva Margaritas. Researchers searched 26 localities in five Mexican states from March 2017 to November 2018; salamanders were encountered at sixteen. Identification used external size, tail proportions, feet and color pattern. These sites are observed study links, not the species’ complete distribution or proof of present occupancy. No individual voucher or molecular re-identification has been independently checked. The additional Aquiloeurycea sp. is left unnamed and its observations are not redistributed among accepted species.

## Skin swabs describe a filtered bacterial community {#s-8bbf185399}
For Nyctanolis pernix and the other sampled hosts, the study changed gloves between animals, rinsed each with 100 ml sterile water and swabbed the whole body for thirty seconds. A separate ventral-body/tail/leg swab was used for Bd testing. The V4 16S bacterial assay retained forward reads, filtered very rare variants and rarefied to 17,536 reads per sample. It is not a complete skin metagenome, cultured viable-cell census or fungal community survey. Eight environmental swabs represented cave walls; each cave supplied only one wall sample, with no matched forest or karst environmental sampling. Negative bacterial controls did not amplify and were not sequenced, which is not independent proof of absence of contamination.

## Bd detection is not a test of resistance or disease survival {#s-edbd0806d3}
The Results report 2/3 (66.7%) Bd-positive Nyctanolis pernix samples. This is a small, dated sampled proportion, not regional prevalence, an experimental infection challenge or proof of resistance. No obvious disease signs or dead/dying animals were observed during the sampling visits; absence of observed signs does not establish later survival or population safety. Bd was quantified as ITS copies with synthetic DNA standards, not directly counted zoospores. ITS copy number differs among strains, so copy values cannot be equated to a cited fatal-zoospore threshold. Aggregate Results 31/86 differs from Methods microbiome comparison groups 30 positive/57 negative; skin-sequencing and diagnostic denominators remain unresolved without the individual supplement.

## Antifungal capacity is inferred rather than demonstrated in this host {#s-b447aca166}
The study matches bacterial marker sequences to a reference database of previously tested Bd-inhibitory isolates and predicts functions with PICRUSt2. It does not culture this Nyctanolis pernix sample’s bacteria to demonstrate inhibition, measure antibiotic production or test a probiotic treatment. Cave and forest samples did not differ significantly in abundance/diversity of potentially inhibitory bacteria; this does not establish equivalent protection. Pooled microbial co-occurrence edges are statistical associations, not observed cooperation or competition, and the networks were not estimated for each host species. The authors warn that reference-genome predictions may miss real functions or infer absent ones in poorly represented cave communities.
