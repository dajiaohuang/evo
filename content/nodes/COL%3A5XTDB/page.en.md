## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2287a758af}
COL26.8 records Chioninia spinalis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-3e00f68432}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 87 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-353feb37db}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
