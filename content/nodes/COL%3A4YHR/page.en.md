## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-bc81ea4b81}
Start with H. hoolock for two feeding studies: one follows two groups at Hollongapar for a year, while another follows a five-member group at Lawachara through a seasonal cycle. Continue to H. leuconedys for a short post-release activity study at Mehao, then H. tianxing for acoustic and genetic records from accessible Myanmar sites. The studies differ in population context, methods and questions; compare their evidence boundaries rather than treating them as a shared genus profile.
