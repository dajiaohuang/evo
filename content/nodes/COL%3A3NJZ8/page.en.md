## Hymenolobium modestum {#s-9eb67102c9}

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records buttress roots and pubescent to silky hairs on branches and leaves. The leaflets are up to 5 cm long, and the rounded panicles carry flowers up to 15 mm long; the laterally compressed samara (a winged fruit) has one oblong seed. The habitat field is marked terrestrial.
