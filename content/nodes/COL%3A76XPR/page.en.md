## Philodendron angustialatum: leaf and floral characters {#s-72253f473f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field pairs clasping and feeding-root descriptors. Stem internodes are short. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous, and the petiole is spongy or somewhat spongy. The blade is entire and oblong with an acuminate tip and entire margins; the field also contains “3–5” without clarifying which character it measures.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium. Its cylindrical peduncle is short and shorter than the spathe; the spathe is slightly constricted, with a light-green outer tube and greenish-cream inner surface. The spadix is subsessile. Floral descriptors include short, free stamens and short staminodes, a cylindrical gynoecium and subcylindrical ovary, 8, 9 or 10 locules, and 2, 3 or 4 ovules; placentation is subbasal. The stigma, fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The unassigned “3–5” value and alternative counts remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
