## Megachirella anchors a stem route {#megachirella-stem}
Megachirella from PZO 628 provides a roughly 240 Ma Middle Triassic stem anchor in the cited combined analysis. The placement supports a Pan-Squamata route that includes sampled stem and crown forms; it does not make Megachirella a direct ancestor or a crown-Squamata first appearance. The route is not a complete duration for the clade.
