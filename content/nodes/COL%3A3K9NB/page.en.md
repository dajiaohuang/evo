## What distinguishes the flower stalks and fruit? {#s-4a9d302005}
The stipules persist, and the inflorescence has two-flowered scorpioid cymes without extrafloral nectaries. The prophylls are threadlike or deeply divided. Flowers have a bend at a joint; their petals are nearly equal, with claws one to three times shorter than the calyx. Ten stamens are joined at the base. The densely hairy capsule is spiraled.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-591966b6cf}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
