## What features does the regional morphology field record? {#s-440597124e}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes leaves as obovate or oblong-obovate, with rounded tips and wedge-shaped bases. They are papery, with pinnate loops of secondary veins; the surface may be pubescent (covered in soft hairs) or become nearly hairless. Small tufts of hairs occur in some vein axils beneath the leaf, and glands are absent. Axillary, head-shaped spikes bear bisexual flowers. The lower floral cup is hairy and the upper part cup-shaped; the calyx lobes are inconspicuous. Ten stamens occur in two whorls. Style hairs were not observed in the field, which is not the same as a confirmed hairless style. The fruit is recorded without wings. This regional dataset field gives no global or sampling claim.
