## Recorded parent of Procolophonoidea {#s-08e999e50b}
Procolophonoidea is listed at rank superfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Parareptilia as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
