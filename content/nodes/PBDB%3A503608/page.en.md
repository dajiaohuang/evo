## Ventral fins and a passive-lift model {#s-b047312eb6}
Articulated Tujiaaspis vividus specimens from the Huixingshao Formation preserve three unpaired dorsal fins, a near-symmetrical hypochordal tail and ventrolateral fins extending from the branchial region toward the tail. A model tests passive lift from those fins, but does not measure swimming performance or establish the fish’s life habitat.

## The Huixingshao sample and its age window {#s-8bbedd83e8}
Holotype IVPP V26668 and paratypes IVPP V27410–V27411 anchor the postcranial observations. The 438.6–432.9 Ma interval belongs to the sampled Huixingshao Formation horizon; it is not a global range, direct ancestry date or universal fin-evolution sequence.
