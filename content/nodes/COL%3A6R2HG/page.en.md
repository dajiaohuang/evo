## Branches and stipules {#s-e82754f93c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists the branches as either unarmed or armed (spiny), with flattened cross-sections. It likewise gives stipules as either spiny or leaf-like, leaving both traits as alternatives.

## Leaflets {#s-9c6443753f}
Leaflets are described as oblong or obovate, with acute or obtuse tips and acute or wedge-shaped (cuneate) bases. Their venation is brochidodromous: neighboring side veins curve and join near the leaf edge, forming loops. The upper surface is glabrous; the lower surface bears hairs.

## Inflorescences {#s-5f3ac4b33c}
The inflorescences are panicles, positioned in leaf axils or at shoot ends. Their bracts are described as leaf-like.

## Flowers {#s-c2c2162850}
The calyx is bell-shaped and the petals are white. Hairs are present on the standard, or banner petal. The stamens are monadelphous, united into one bundle.

## Fruit {#s-30b54eddb2}
The fruit is described as a samara, or winged dry fruit. The record marks the seed-bearing core as unknown, so it provides no further description of that part.

## Habitat category {#s-b937665b7c}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
