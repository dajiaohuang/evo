## Very broad leaves and long red flowers {#s-d64a7f6673}
Watsonia vanderspuyae is a robust plant 1–2 m tall, with leaves 33–85 mm wide and strongly thickened margins. Its red flowers have a long tube up to 50 mm; the floral bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) are dry and torn.
