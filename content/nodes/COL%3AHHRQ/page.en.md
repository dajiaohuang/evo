## Flowers on the branches and a stalkless fruit {#s-cd71b37384}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records white latex and alternate, loosely spaced leaves on cylindrical branches with unthickened bark. The inflorescence is described as ramiflorous and axillary and is classified as a corymb-like cyme. The ovary is hairless, and the corolla lobes are listed as longer than or equal to the tube. The smooth fruit has evident ribs and conspicuous lenticels but no stalk. This regional morphology entry does not provide a sampling locality or population-level range.

## Terrestrial habitat category {#s-23590a9321}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Aspidosperma ramiflorum* as terrestrial. It gives no collection locality or more specific substrate.
