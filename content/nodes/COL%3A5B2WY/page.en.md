## What traits distinguish Verbena sagittalis? {#s-56a78781c6}
The field describes erect, smooth plants with elliptic to egg-shaped leaves and rounded bases. Leaf edges may be smooth or toothed mainly toward the tip. Dense, sessile flower clusters carry bracts; side clusters are shorter than the terminal one. The calyx has five short triangular teeth, and the corolla tube is under 7 mm.
