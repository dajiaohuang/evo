## Stem, leaves, and inflorescence {#s-23cac942b4}
The source records a solitary, underground, or prostrate stem with leaf-sheath remnants. Green pinnate leaves have persistent sheaths arranged helically around the stem and edged with fibers; the petiole margin is unarmed and the leaf axis slightly curved. The leaflets are leathery, grouped at differing angles, rigid, and have tips described as acute, rounded, or notched and asymmetric. Their surfaces are dark green and the same colour on both sides. The panicle is exposed between the leaves. Its spadix is grooved, hairless, or waxy-coated; the main axis is sparsely woolly, and the smaller branches spiral around it.

## Fruit {#s-2c2df0e0c3}
The fruit is ovoid, with a green-yellow woolly outer layer, fibrous succulent middle layer, and woody oval inner layer. The endosperm is homogeneous.
