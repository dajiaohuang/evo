## Ischnosiphon killipii {#s-60c8a270a5}
## A climbing stem and lance-shaped leaves

The regional flora entry records a climbing stem that branches, with lance-shaped, asymmetric leaves. Each flower cluster is reported to have two bracteoles. Petals are recorded as yellow, while the outer staminode colour field lists purple, pink and yellow; the dataset does not explain whether that list reflects variation. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
