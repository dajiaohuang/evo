## ## Winged leaves and a constricted fruit {#s-c638350257}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical, glabrous branches with lenticels, or small corky pores. The deciduous stipules are ovate; both the petiole and main leaf axis are winged. Each leaf has one pair of leaflets and stalkless, cup-shaped nectaries. Racemes—flower clusters arranged along a central stalk—bear nearly stalkless flowers with bell-shaped calyces. The fruit is constricted and curved, with glabrous, leathery valves. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
