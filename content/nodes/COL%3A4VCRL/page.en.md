## How often did one survey find this yeast in honey? {#s-1f19f7913b}
In a study covering honey samples from 43 countries, researchers isolated *Schizosaccharomyces pombe* from 49 of 386 samples (12.7%). The authors discuss honeybee honey as a likely primary habitat, but the observed frequency varied among countries and depends on the study’s sampling and enrichment methods. This is a result for that sample set, not a worldwide prevalence estimate or proof that honey is the species’ only habitat.
