## Conchocarpus toxicarius {#s-3907b1f4bf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description distinguishes this record by its three-to-seven leaflets and five fertile stamens.

## Three to seven leaflets and five fertile stamens {#s-487b243fc1}
Leaves are alternate and divided into three to seven leaflets; the petiole tip is slightly expanded and leaflet tips are acuminate. Inflorescences are solitary, generally over 7 cm long and between leafy branches, without evergreen leaf-like bracts; branching is absent (pseudoracemose). Sepals persist. Flowers have five fertile stamens and a radially symmetric cream-white corolla. Anthers are free and glabrous, the ovary glabrous, and the style connate. The fruit apex is rounded. The ecology category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a collection locality or complete range.
