## Flat leaves and yellow flowers {#s-fd17f55da6}
Flora do Brasil records Sisyrinchium glaziovii with fibrous roots, flat basal leaves and a winged flowering stalk. Its yellow flowers have a flat perianth and globose to nearly globose fruit; several floral characters are unreported in this entry.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-9e05356073}
The source classifies the species as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant).
