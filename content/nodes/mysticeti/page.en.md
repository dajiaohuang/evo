## Baleen whales emerge through a mosaic {#s-e28098e198}
The review treats Mysticeti as the baleen-whale branch within sampled Neoceti. Toothed stem forms and later baleen-bearing lineages preserve a mix of traits, so the record is better read as a sequence of changes than as one sudden transformation.

## A stem-to-living sample is not an origin date {#s-055b81a844}
The displayed 34–0 million-year envelope spans the review’s sampled fossil evidence through living mysticetes. It does not identify an exact origin for the whole clade or show that every form was a direct ancestor of the next.
