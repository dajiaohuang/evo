## Distribution and habitat {#s-70f700ccbf}
The current MDD page lists Delacour's Langur and gives Hoi Xuan, Vietnam, as the type locality. Workman's diet study was conducted at Van Long Wetland Nature Reserve. A type locality and one research site anchor the name and local study; they are not a complete range map.

## Ecological evidence {#s-3490e24b94}
Workman recorded feeding at Van Long from August 2007 to July 2008. Feeding made up 29% of the activity budget; foliage accounted for nearly 79% of the annual diet, with young leaves making up nearly 60%. Of 145 available plant species, the langurs ate 42; five plants comprised more than 60% of the diet and 16 more than 93%. The important foods were not limestone-endemic plants, so this local diet study alone does not explain the species' association with karst.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-024f0998b7}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 7325 g; mass at birth or hatching 457 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 1460 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 1.5 offspring/eggs; age of dependence on food other than maternal milk 398 d; d denotes days.
