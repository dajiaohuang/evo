## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are described? {#s-04b478b08c}
The stem has sparse hairs: short simple hairs under 1 mm, plus star-shaped and gland-tipped hairs. Ovate to narrowly ovate leaves have toothed margins, pointed or blunt tips and six to eight veins radiating from the base. The calyx is 5–10 mm long, with a pointed tip; its central vein is not prominent outside. Petals are 12–20 mm long, dark yellow with a wine-colored basal mark. The staminal tube is 3–5 mm. The fruit is a cone-shaped to spherical schizocarp that is not enclosed by the calyx. It has eight to ten pointed segments (mericarps). Each segment has a developed internal tongue-like structure that is membranous and fibrous, strongly curved and toothed, helping retain the seed; it bears six to eight pairs of glandular-hairy teeth. The seed surface has star-shaped hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-56110f2d9c}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
