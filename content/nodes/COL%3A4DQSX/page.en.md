## Clustered stems and red succulent fruit {#s-f60afe69a5}
This small cactus grows in clusters and has globose to cylindrical stems with low-profile ribs and distinct areoles. Fine white spines include short, upright central spines. Yellow flowers open at the stem tip; white hairs may be abundant or sparse, and the petals have pointed tips and entire edges. The stigma is white or yellowish. The succulent fruit is red, while seed characters are unknown. Rock-associated habitat is recorded.
