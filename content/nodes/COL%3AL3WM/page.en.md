## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the regional description {#s-ea58b96d7b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records flattened, triangular prickles on the branches. The leaf blade is membranous and divided into two lobes, described as triangular or blunt. Its base ranges from heart-shaped to notched; the narrow tip is described as blunt or slightly pointed. Both surfaces are recorded as hairless or becoming hairless. Flower buds are described as smooth, tubular, club-shaped and five-ridged, with a five-horned tip. The floral cup (hypanthium) is tubular, and the calyx splits into two or three lobes as the flower opens. Petals are described as linear above the midpoint and narrow-elliptic. The field records five fertile anthers alternating with five sterile stamens (staminodes), a staminal column ranging from bearded to hairy, and a stalked ovary. The fruit is a legume recorded as opening elastically. This regional description does not assess variation or distribution across the species.
