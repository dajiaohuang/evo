## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-db9cba814b}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Crithagra ankoberensis (Ash, 1979) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## The scope of one measured bird {#s-430e10bed9}
Morphology comprises 1 bird: 0 female, 1 male and 0 unsexed; 1 complete set. Nostril-to-tip beak length 6.8 mm, tarsus 14.4 mm, unflattened wing 73 mm, tail 55 mm and hand-wing index 25.5 are historical row means. One bird cannot establish sex differences or a population distribution. Inference=NO does not replace each field’s sampling denominator. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed; beak length supplies neither bite force nor the ability to process particular seeds.

## Mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-a37ee695c6}
Mass=14.9 g with Mass.Source=Dunning is a compiled species-average body mass incorporating both sexes; the 1 morphology bird is not a verified independent weighing denominator. A single morphology bird does not turn the compiled mass into that bird’s weight. The original book, weighed individuals, localities and dates were not read, leaving sex, seasonal and regional mass ranges unsupported. Mass and beak length do not establish feeding mechanics.

## Separate seed niche from plant trophic level {#s-02fcd8d04e}
Trophic.Niche=Granivore; Trophic.Level=Herbivore. The source defines Granivore as at least 60% of food resources coming from seeds or nuts. Herbivore level means at least 70% of food resources from plants. These fields and thresholds remain separate, not a measured seasonal diet percentage here; the seeds/nuts threshold differs from the all-plant threshold. No seed, nut or insect species list or individual feeding record is supplied, leaving detailed and breeding-season menus unsupported.

## Diet does not replace primary lifestyle {#s-a9128fd6de}
Primary.Lifestyle=Terrestrial means most time on the ground, obtaining food while walking or hopping in the source definition. Habitat=Grassland; density code 3 denotes open vegetation, not bird abundance. Migration=1 is the sedentary category without implying lifelong immobility. A shared food niche does not establish shared foraging strata, social activity or seasonal movements. Historical map fields are uninterpreted here and do not supply current range-wide distributions or movement routes.

## Evidence limits {#s-a71ae9b924}
This page uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, same-name eBird links, older Serinus BirdTree links, relevant Metadata definitions and this reading of COL cr/se shards. Catalogue synonyms and crosswalks do not prove complete equivalence of historical concepts to COL26.8; the exact older hypostictus combination was not confirmed in these shards. Morphology birds, complete sets and Dunning compiled mass retain separate denominators. Individual specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Current distributions, conservation, plumage, calls, breeding, seed lists, bite forces, observed diets and movement routes remain unsupported.
