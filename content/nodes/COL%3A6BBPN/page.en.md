## Axillary clusters and a smooth pod {#s-22ecdb0399}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes leaves on long stalks, bearing either one or three leaflets with smooth edges. Short flower clusters grow beside the leaves; the yellow flowers have a short, bulging outer floral cup with triangular lobes. The fruit is stalkless and rounded or sickle-shaped, with smooth pod sides. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
