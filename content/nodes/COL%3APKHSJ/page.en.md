## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Hypocnemis rondoni Whitney, M. L. Isler, Bravo, Aristizábal, Schunck, Silveira, Piacentini, Cohn-Haft et al., 2013 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
