## Marmosops (Sciophanes) ojastii: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Marmosops (Sciophanes) ojastii* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority García, Sánchez-Hernández & Semedo, 2014.
