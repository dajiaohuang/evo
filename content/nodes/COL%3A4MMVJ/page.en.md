## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-c03bd765c2}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Proceratophrys subguttata as having larval development and its oviposition as aquatic. A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.

## Juvenile habitat and egg-site categories have defined scope {#s-c4bf46a811}
The same row codes juvenile habitat as aquatic and marks eggs directly in water or on submerged substrate among the seven oviposition-site categories. The positive category summarizes a broad trait; it does not establish a specific nest architecture, substrate species or oviposition height. A juvenile-habitat code is not automatically an adult habitat preference. The compiled record does not newly establish clutch size, egg dimensions, parental attendance, spawning season or offspring survival.

## Compiled lengths retain inference and measurement limits {#s-8c3f95b93c}
The row does not carry a head-width or tibia-length inference flag. That absence is not an independent audit establishing how each original value was measured. Body length, head width and tibia length are retained numerically in the row-level evidence snapshot. The Methods use means when available and otherwise maxima, while the workbook length headers do not explicitly state units and do not consistently identify sex or stage. These cells are therefore not presented as new diagnostic millimetre measurements, a sex-specific range or measured species-wide variation. Upstream reference entries are preserved, but their original measurements have not been independently checked.
