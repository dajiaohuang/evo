## Accepted name and classification {#s-14a98105a5}
In the 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release, Phrynopus valquii Chávez, Santa Cruz, Rodríguez & Lehr, 2015 is an accepted species. Its recorded classification path is Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Phrynopus (genus).

## Twenty specimens from the high Andes {#s-8044f95b42}
Phrynopus valquii was described from the high Andes of La Libertad, Peru, from a series of 20 specimens. The diagnosis combines skin texture and body characters, including the absence of tympanum, vomerine dentigerous processes, vocal slits and nuptial pads. The paper's distribution section places the known records in the eastern Andes near the southwestern boundary of Río Abiseo National Park.
