## Senna tropica {#s-4c1947d59e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna tropica* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 2–4 pairs of leaflets and extra-floral glands between them. Its flowers have seven fertile stamens; the hanging, indehiscent (not splitting open at maturity) pods are cylindrical or four-angled and have seeds in two rows. The plant is listed as terrestrial.
