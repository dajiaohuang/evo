## ## Straight or hooked prickles and a slowly opening pod {#s-24eaad50f8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records straight or hook-shaped prickles on the branches. Its undivided, elliptical leaves have rounded bases and pointed to tapering tips; the upper surface is hairless and the lower bears short, appressed hairs. The flower bud is tubular and may reach about 15 mm; ten anthers are listed. Unlike some related entries that describe elastic opening, this row says the legume dehisces late. This is a regional description, and it does not assess the species’ full variation.
