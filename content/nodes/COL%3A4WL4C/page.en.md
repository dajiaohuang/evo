## Senecio harleyi {#s-e2fe9ecad5}
## A leafless base leads to a leafy upper stem

In the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record, the lower stem is woody and its upper part herbaceous. The base is leafless, with sessile leaves higher up. Their blades are lance-shaped to elliptic, taper toward the base and have smooth edges; both surfaces are hairless.

The branched, corymb-like panicle bears disc-shaped heads with 5–15 involucral bracts. Ray florets are absent; the white tubular florets in the centre are bisexual. Style branches end bluntly with a spreading crown of hairs. The cylindrical, ribbed dry fruits have a finely papillose surface, and their white pappus (bristle crown) persists. The source habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
