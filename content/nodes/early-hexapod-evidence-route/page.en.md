## A browsing route joins separate early-hexapod evidence {#s-dad786f202}
The Early Hexapoda entry is a navigation route, not a biological taxon or ancestor sequence. Its 411–0 Ma envelope joins two Rhynie specimens with a fossil-calibrated terrestrialization model—a model of the transition onto land whose timing is anchored by fossils—and living molecular data. The route does not establish the origin of Hexapoda or the timing of land colonization, nor does it claim global first and last appearances.
