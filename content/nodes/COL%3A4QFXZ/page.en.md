## The name and this data account {#s-58a088d69c}
Puffinus opisthomelas is accepted in COL26.8 within Procellariidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-0a18500c97}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 28.8 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 6.6 mm in width and 8.4 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-7b77e0be0e}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 233 mm; mean tarsus length is 43.7 mm. Compiled mean mass is 408 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.
