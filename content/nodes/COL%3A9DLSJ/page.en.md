## Schnella alata {#s-51d3f8af06}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Schnella alata* as an accepted species of *Schnella* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) that fall away and short bilobed leaf blades on flowering or fruiting branches. Young flower buds have a winged calyx tube (the tube formed by the outer sepals); at opening, the calyx is urn-shaped. The fruit splits open at maturity, releasing its seeds.
