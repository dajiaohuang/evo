## ## Buttress roots and a winged fruit {#s-17e7e142ea}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records buttress roots and deciduous, linear to lanceolate stipules. The leaflets are ovate to elliptic, longer than 5 cm, with glossy upper surfaces and blunt to acute tips; small appendages at their bases (stipels) are over 1.5 mm long. The globose panicles bear flowers usually longer than 20 mm. The oblong samara—a flattened, winged fruit with a central seed chamber—has a round wing and contains one or two oblong seeds. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
