## Overview {#s-2334cb309c}
Hotaling and colleagues separated the Île Sainte-Marie sample from the earlier Simmons's mouse-lemur hypothesis and named it the Boraha mouse lemur. Their treatment combined mitochondrial and nuclear population structure, species-delimitation tests and morphology; it is a taxonomic conclusion about sampled material, not an island-wide population census.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-d4f248a5cb}
The paper gives Ikalalao Forest on Île Sainte-Marie, at about 68 m, as the type locality; the type series came from this island forest. This supports the study's island-sample scope, not all island habitats or the current range.

## Morphology {#s-bb3d0a2aa2}
The 2016 description used an adult-female holotype with associated skin, skull, skeleton and tissue; the authors recorded a holotype mass of 56.5 g and described reddish dorsal pelage. A measurement from one type specimen is not a species-wide body-size range.

## Classification {#s-e82d630f92}
The current MDD Simmons mouse lemur entry includes Boraha; COL26.8 retains two accepted species.

## Evidence limits {#s-bf7b289721}
COL26.8 accepts this species, while the local MDD exact-name crosswalk has no matching name. This page follows the pinned COL identity and does not treat that as a resolution of global taxonomic disagreement. Current range, population trend and conservation status are not assessed; the common-name label is an editorial translation.
