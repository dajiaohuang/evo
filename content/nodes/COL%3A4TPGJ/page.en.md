## Rumex acetosa {#s-9afc81e9bf}
## Arrow-based leaves and a creeping rhizome

Rumex acetosa has a creeping rhizome and smooth, solid stems. Its lance-shaped leaves have arrow-shaped bases and a thin, translucent ochrea at the leaf base. Loose, branched flower clusters include leaf-like bracts. The female flowers have smooth, entire oval tepals. Fruiting valves carry oval, hairless wings with entire margins and basal callosities. The source gives a broad terrestrial habitat label.
