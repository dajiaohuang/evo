## A compact subshrub with mauve flower clusters {#s-c33adb0323}
The regional flora describes a few-branched, closely leafy subshrub to 60 cm tall. Its elliptic leaves have translucent margins and a prominent midrib beneath. Crowded terminal, subumbellate racemes bear mauve flowers with entire, obtuse side petals. The habitat entry gives rocky sandstone slopes; these regional account fields do not establish the species’ full distribution.
