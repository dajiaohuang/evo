## The source tadpole concept {#s-f92d4e61ae}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Aromobates orostoma, in the genus Aromobates and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying work prints Nepbelobates orostoma, which the compilation retains as its own source name. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-aec94348a9}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images; its supplied developmental-stage label is 37. That label belongs to a source record, not a full developmental timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
