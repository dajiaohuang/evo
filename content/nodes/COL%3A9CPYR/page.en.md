## ## Bilobed leaves and two kinds of stamen {#s-1110cc840d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes flattened, triangular prickles and leaves split into two blunt lobes, with a heart-shaped base. The lower leaf surface has hairs along the main vein. Its flowers have five fertile stamens alternating with five staminodes—sterile stamens—and the calyx splits into two or three lobes as the flower opens. The legume is recorded as opening elastically. These traits are from one regional row and do not establish the species’ full variation.
