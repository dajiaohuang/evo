## Name and placement in COL26.8 {#s-1c279a8ac4}
COL26.8 lists Atelopus balios Peters, 1973 as an accepted species. In the release dated 20 August 2026, its recorded path runs through Anura (order), Bufonidae (family) and Atelopus (genus). This placement belongs to that checklist; the same name in another source needs its own identity evidence.

## Maximum adult body length in the compilation {#s-c4f5f6c1c4}
Under AmphiBIO's historical Frost 2011 classification, maximum adult body length is recorded as 37.3 mm. For Anura, body length means snout-to-vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona it means total length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual. Its source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to COL26.8.
