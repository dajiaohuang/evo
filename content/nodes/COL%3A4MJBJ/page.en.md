Counts are cumulative records, not an independently certified number of distinct marked animals, population density or a current abundance estimate. Surveys combine daytime/night-time visual and photographic encounters, ten 8 × 8 m quadrats, four 100 × 20 m transects and fifty pitfall traps across six habitats. ## Species-labelled rows conflict with a genus-only identification statement {#s-1bc056a565}
Methods state that Pristimantis individuals were conservatively identified only to genus, without molecular analyses. Table 2 nevertheless prints Pristimantis carvalhoi and other named Pristimantis species alongside five separate Pristimantis sp. rows. This conflict remains unresolved: the named row is presented as an attributed source record, not independent certification of species identity. The five unnamed rows are not assigned to this species or pooled into its 12 records. Original diagnostic photographs, vouchers or author clarification are needed before treating the names as verified occurrence evidence.
