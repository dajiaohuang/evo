## Bellairsia: a Jurassic skeleton seen in fine detail {#s-7e94dfe137}
Synchrotron scans of NMS G.2022.1.1 from Skye resolve most of a Middle Jurassic skeleton, including squamate-like ankle and skull features alongside more retained skeletal states. The study’s matrices place Bellairsia on the squamate stem, but that position depends on character coding and analytical constraints. Proposed skull mobility is an interpretation; the fossil does not directly date the crown group or represent an ancestor of living geckos.
