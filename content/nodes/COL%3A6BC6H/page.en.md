## Accepted name and placement {#s-5fde085167}
COL26.8 records Craugastor rayo (Savage & DeWeese, 1979) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; historical source concepts are described separately below.

## Reading route {#s-8bf598290b}
The catalogue placement is distinct from AmphiBIO's older Frost 2011 classification, which supplies the trait codes below.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-f6a22af0f4}
The historical row marks terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. These broad categories can overlap; they are not measures of frequency or seasonal change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries; this row's status has not been checked, and its match to the current concept remains unknown.

## Activity periods — AmphiBIO source {#s-3964132ed1}
AmphiBIO codes night activity for its historical source concept. This is an overall time-of-activity category, not a continuous record.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-0d173d7511}
The source lists maximum adult length as 71 mm. For frogs this means snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a range of every individual.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-74c083718e}
The row flags direct development. The code does not establish parental care, breeding location or timing.
