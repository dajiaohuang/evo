## A living-primate tree supports this branch {#s-477e782ae2}
A supermatrix of living primates strongly supports the branch labelled Simiiformes in the study. This atlas uses the corresponding Anthropoidea route to guide readers through those sampled relationships.

## Living samples do not settle fossil membership {#s-f96c686384}
The name correspondence applies to the study’s living-primate sample and its tree. It does not decide which contested fossils belong within Anthropoidea, nor does it supply a fossil history for the route.
