## A living-primate tree supports sampled Simiiformes {#s-477e782ae2}
A supermatrix of living primates strongly supports the branch labelled Simiiformes in the study. This atlas maps that sampled branch to the Anthropoidea navigation route, giving readers a way to follow the relationships represented in the living-primate sample. The mapping connects two labels for this route; it is not a new classification or a fossil-placement result.

## What the living sample leaves unresolved {#s-f96c686384}
The tree’s topology applies to the study’s living-primate sample. It does not decide which contested fossils belong within Anthropoidea, and it supplies no complete fossil history. The numerical range therefore remains withheld; a first-appearance date cannot be inferred from this living tree.
