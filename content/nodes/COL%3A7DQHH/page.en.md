## What traits are recorded for Urbanodendron bahiense? {#s-6559ad821f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical, grooved branches with silky hairs that become less dense with age. Alternate, papery leaves are lance-shaped to elliptic, with a wedge-shaped base and pointed tip; their underside has a dense net of veins. Axillary flower clusters also carry silky hairs. The similar-looking tepals are hairy, and the stamens have glands near their bases. The ellipsoid fruit is partly enclosed in a rough, cone-shaped cupule with a double rim; the tepals fall away.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-91d0c9611a}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a particular locality.
