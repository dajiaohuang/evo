## What leaf, flower and capsule traits are recorded? {#s-b0fdeac527}
The stems grow along the ground. The opposite elliptic to obovate leaves have entire stipules, no leaf glands and a yellowish underside. The inflorescence may be a raceme or a double raceme; its bracts and sepals are not glandular. One petal type is elongated and swollen, hairy on the outside but not glandular; another is spatula-shaped and hairy. The flower has seven stamens, four staminodes (sterile stamens), two bilobed glands and two trilobed glands; the ovary has three chambers. The elliptic capsule has a small pointed tip and splits lengthwise from tip toward base into three chambers around a persistent central framework (replum). Its outer wall may be raised, hairless or hairy; the inner wall is hairless. The valves are rounded on the back, entire at the tip and attached to the framework at the base. Seeds bear long, curled hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-61b2745ee3}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
