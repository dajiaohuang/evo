## *Vanhouttea hilariana* {#s-fc19b55f66}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — covered with fine hairs; leaf-related structures — elliptic, egg-shaped, saw-toothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem); fruits — hairless. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as rock-dwelling (Portuguese: Rupícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
