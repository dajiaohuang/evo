## *Goeppertia fasciata*: basal leaves and two-flowered clusters {#s-2cebdb5df2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character record places the leaves at the base of the plant and notes ornamentation on young leaves. Its flowering stalk rises from a different node; all recorded bracts are fertile, arranged spirally, and not red or orange. Each small flower cluster has two flowers, with zero or one club-shaped bracteole, a smaller leaf-like piece directly beneath a flower.

The corolla tube is recorded as entire, and one outer staminode bears a petal-like callus at its tip. These details describe the regional flora character record; they do not supply a measured range of variation.
