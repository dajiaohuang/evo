## Stem surface {#s-20b52305c9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as pubescent, or covered with hairs. Hair direction is given as patent (spreading outward) or recurved (curving back), without resolving between those descriptions. Simple hairs and capitate glandular hairs (with small head-like tips) are present; branched and unbranched bristles are absent. Scattered retrorse prickles point back along the stem, and short lateral shoots called brachyblasts are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-25d5692787}
Stipules, small appendages at leaf bases, persist, while extrafloral nectaries (glands outside flowers) are absent. The record lists projections between pinna pairs as both absent and present, leaving that character unresolved. The flower clusters are globose, and before the flowers open their floral bracts are shorter than the flowers.

## Flowers {#s-4f45557e70}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as three-part. The calyx is membranous, with lobes described as triangular or lanceolate. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are listed as glabrous or pubescent. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Substrate {#s-b1a023922a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, meaning it grows on land. This field does not provide a more specific habitat or collection locality.
