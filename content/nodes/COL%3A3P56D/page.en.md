## What traits mark the leaves and flower heads? {#s-dd85a065b5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records no xylopodium. Its leaves have crenate or serrate margins, no petioles, and flat, leathery blades; mature flower heads are spherical or nearly spherical and lack a recorded stalk. A stylopodium is present, but the calyx lobes lack flattened appendages. These are traits in the regional description, not a measure of population variation.

## What broad habitat label is recorded? {#s-00c74b69da}
The same source lists the habitat as terrestrial. It names no collection locality or complete range, so this category is not a distribution map.
