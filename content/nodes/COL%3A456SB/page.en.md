## Gland-dotted leaves with several hair colors {#s-9166d5aef4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes smooth cork on the stem. Leaves may be elliptic, lance-shaped, or ovate, with acuminate or acute tips and acute, gradually narrowed, or wedge-shaped bases. Their texture is leathery or membranous, and the edge is flat. The upper midrib is grooved; two marginal veins are recorded. The hair covering is described through alternatives that include two-armed hairs and white, grey, rust-coloured, ochre, or reddish hairs. Leaf stalks are grooved. The field does not give frequencies for these alternatives.

## Closed buds and fruit colour alternatives {#s-36a260ce4a}
Inflorescences may be axillary, paired, or solitary; bract status is unknown. The flower cup is inverted-cone-shaped or hairy. Bracteoles are lance-shaped or oval, sepals ovate or triangular, and petals round; flower buds are closed. Fruit bracteoles may persist or fall away. Fruits are listed as ellipsoid and orange, purple, or red. Seeds are deltoid (triangular in outline).

## A terrestrial label without range coverage {#s-5a5f81c78a}
The habitat field says “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It provides no locality, survey date, or range coverage and does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil.
