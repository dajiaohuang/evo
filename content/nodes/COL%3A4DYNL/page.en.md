## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora menispermifolia? {#s-d7f2f257a3}
The stem is cylindrical or angled and partly woody. Leaves may be entire or three-lobed, with smooth margins, coarse hairs and stalked petiole glands; stipules are kidney-shaped. Solitary flowers have lance-shaped bracts, awned sepals and a dish-shaped floral cup. The corona has several rows; the operculum ends in a membrane or threadlike tip, and the limen is cup-shaped. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The berry is hairy, with obovate, pitted seeds.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-9168b961f1}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
