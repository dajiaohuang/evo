In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1959 is the entry for Eleutherodactylus rubrimaculatus, placed by the source in Eleutherodactylus (Eleutherodactylidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-a544ac16a8}
Its maximum adult body length is 24 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
