## How the source names this tadpole {#s-f51e968290}
TadpoleTraits version 4 groups these records under Allobates albiventris. The dataset authors use Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024) for its placement in Allobates and Aromobatidae; the underlying publications also use the name Allobates albiventris. This source grouping does not establish that the records match a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Stage records in the dataset {#s-4eb4cc4e70}
The compilation contains four rows from published literature and no image-derived rows. The listed source stages are 35, 34, 37 and 36. Stage labels and any uncertainty are kept as supplied; a stage estimated from an image would not count as a direct developmental observation. Rows do not count distinct tadpoles, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
