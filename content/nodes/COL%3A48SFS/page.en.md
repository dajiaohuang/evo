## An effort-bounded Gunung Belumut survey record {#s-4a3c6df238}
Table 1 records 18 Odorrana hosii encounters at Gunung Belumut Amenity Forest in Johor, Peninsular Malaysia, or 10.5% of the 172 reported amphibian records. Sampling combined drift-fenced pitfall traps with searches along streams and forest trails in parts of 2022 and February 2023.
