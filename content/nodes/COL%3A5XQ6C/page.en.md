## Chamaecrista echinocarpa {#s-fabbd0a0e5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista echinocarpa* records one pair of narrow obovate leaflets with bulbous glandular hairs, yellow unequal petals, twisted pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
