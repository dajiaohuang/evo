## *Sida marabaensis* {#s-333d6ab2f5}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — covered with fine hairs; leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, linear, saw-toothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal; flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
