## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Scytalopus frankeae K. V. Rosenberg, T. J. Davis, G. H. Rosenberg, Hosner, Robbins, Valqui & Lane, 2020 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
