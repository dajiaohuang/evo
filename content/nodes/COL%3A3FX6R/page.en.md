## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-01b18960aa}
COL26.8 records Gerrhopilus andamanensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gerrhopilidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-0ccb339b62}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 160 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-11d00ebec3}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
