## Fast streams in the Dushan sample {#s-ef643525f4}
The description recorded frogs in Dushan County, Guizhou, China, at 1000–1200 m, beneath stones in fast mountain streams surrounded by evergreen broadleaf forest. This observed microhabitat and locality do not establish a complete range or an exclusive habitat requirement.

## Late March remained a hypothesis {#s-a424363f76}
The authors found no eggs, tadpoles or females and proposed that breeding probably occurred in late March based on their surveys. The proposed timing is kept as an inference; the missing life stages do not demonstrate their absence from the species or establish larval requirements.
