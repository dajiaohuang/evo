## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2562328f17}
COL26.8 records Eunectes beniensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Boidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-21067a9879}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 4000 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b999d0bb68}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
