## *Manihot cezarii* {#s-3ff1604986}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — hairless; leaf-related structures — entire, untoothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis), axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
