## Name and this data account {#s-711857795a}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts Kinosternon acutum in Kinosternidae. This account follows its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which retains the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and sources for individual traits. Because the dataset has one row per species, its categories describe that record rather than every population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-c56f57ba3e}
The ReptTraits row labels microhabitat Aquatic/Terrestrial, diet Carnivorous and activity Nocturnal. These broad categories do not give a complete habitat list or a fixed daily schedule. Carnivorous does not identify particular prey, and the one-row summary cannot show differences among populations, seasons or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-1f7ee87379}
The row records the reproductive mode as oviparous (egg-laying) and sex determination as temperature-dependent sex determination (TSD). These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or show that every population has the same pattern. Clutch and litter counts are not interpreted in this account.

## Evidence limits {#s-0fe3a277a1}
This is a limited trait account, not a complete treatment of morphology or ecology. It paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 framework and trait-specific source chain. A single row compresses variation in sex, age, locality and population; a matching name and family do not guarantee the same concept in later taxonomies. A reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and a reproductive mean is not a fixed count for each clutch. Many mass values are estimated from length equations, so they are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions and are not used in the conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated; missing fields remain gaps. Citation labels preserve the dataset's source chain and do not mean that this project independently reviewed every original work.

## Traits not interpreted here {#s-ce5a0ec2b4}
Five traits have field-specific source labels and are interpreted here. Uninterpreted fields are maximum length (SVL in mm, or SCL in mm for turtles), maximum female and male length using those measures, maximum total length (TL in mm), mean offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, minimum and maximum clutch size, annual litter or clutch count, egg length and width, and foraging mode. This account does not establish a supported species value and source for those fields; relatives and blank entries cannot supply the missing facts.
