## What leaf and flower traits are noted for Croton polygonoides? {#s-5612306363}
The short-stalked elliptic leaves are hairless above and star-haired beneath, with fine teeth along the margin. A sessile nectar gland sits at the leaf base; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and shallowly bell-shaped; female flowers are sessile, with fused sepals, equal calyx lobes, and a two-lobed style. Female petals are reduced to glands, the bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
