## Leaves {#s-93937bb6f2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record allows stipules to be deciduous or absent and marks the leaf rachis as absent. It describes an even-pinnate leaf with one pair of leaflets; the source fields are retained as recorded. Leaflets are listed as slightly to strongly sickle-shaped or elliptic, with asymmetrical, acute bases and acuminate or acute tips.

## Flowers, fruit and seeds {#s-f6f7e660c4}
The inflorescence is a raceme with a hairy axis. Flowers have a hairless hypanthium and five free sepals; petals are obovate. The record lists three stamens and describes the ovary as hairless, hairy, or hairy along its margin. The fruit is a legume, and the seeds are nearly round (orbicular). These are source-listed descriptions and alternatives, not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-768430cee5}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
