## *Strelitzia alba*: white flowers on a large, single-stemmed tree {#s-a6877708d8}
The cited South African regional species account describes *Strelitzia alba* as a single-stemmed tree reaching 10 m, with large, oblong leaves. Its flowers are white, and the petals have a spear-shaped base.

The same regional account places it in forests. These statements describe that source's South African species concept; they do not establish a complete global range or population-wide height distribution.
