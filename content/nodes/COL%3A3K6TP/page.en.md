## Helichrysum intricatum {#s-fc0d3380b4}
## Gray wool and tiny yellow heads

The *Plants of the Greater Cape Floristic Region* account describes a wiry shrublet or subshrub up to 60 cm tall, covered in gray wool. Oblanceolate leaves have slightly rolled-under edges; the upper surface becomes nearly smooth but remains rough-haired, while the lower is woolly.

Many small, bell-shaped heads crowd into terminal corymbs. Each head is about 4 by 2.5 mm, canary-yellow, and has 8–15 florets. The ovary is recorded as smooth or covered in papilla-like hairs. The source habitat field lists seeps on slopes; it does not define a complete range.
