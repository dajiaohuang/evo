## How Ernest’s historical values were assembled {#s-436d22beaf}
These records do not cover all accepted Felidae or provide complete family biology. Species entries use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 same-name table; equivalence to current concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight regardless of sample size; inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and sources can be reused. Maximum lifespan is the longest record, not typical lifespan. Adult mass is not interpreted here, and relatives do not fill missing fields. Original reference numbers remain, but the underlying species literature and every wild, captive, or locality condition were not independently established.
