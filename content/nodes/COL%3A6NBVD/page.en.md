## Stolons ending in cormlets {#s-f9db3d73e2}
Ixia flagellaris is 150–330 mm tall and produces slender stolons (creeping stems that extend from the plant) up to 50 mm long, each ending in a relatively large cormlet. Its small pink flowers have yellow throats; the account notes that the damp-site habitat and the exact status of some collections remain uncertain.

## A damp habitat still being established {#s-d02b43a475}
The source suggests damp flats, probably peaty sandstone, but explicitly says that this habitat still needs to be established. A type collection was made the season after a fire; that does not define a fire interval.
