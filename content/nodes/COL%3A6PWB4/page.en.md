## What leaf and fruit details are recorded for Licania parvifructa? {#s-76fb9e21b5}
The leaves are oblong or ovate, with a floury, powder-like covering beneath; their stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in panicles, with bell-shaped receptacles and individual stalks. Each flower has three separate stamens whose filaments remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is also described as powdery.
