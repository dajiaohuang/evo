## Vocalization research {#s-e099509dde}
A comparison of two Bolivian endemic titis found more frequent calling by groups in more continuous forest; P. modestus gave more territorial calls in the dry season. Call rates varied with habitat and season, so counts of calls should not be converted directly into group density without accounting for these factors.

## Survey methods {#s-3d3f30fa7d}
The study examined daily, seasonal and habitat-related variation relevant to using territorial calls to locate groups in surveys. Its results provide context for acoustic monitoring, not an estimate of total abundance or long-term trend.

## Evidence limits {#s-8614d798e2}
