## Herniaria schlechteri {#s-2168a1c1cd}
## A prostrate annual of coastal limestone

The account describes a prostrate annual with a slender taproot and short, backward-pointing hairs. Opposite elliptic leaves are finely hairy, and the stipules are papery. Greenish flowers occur in axillary clusters; their anthers are about 0.2 mm long. Coastal limestone is the stated habitat, but the account does not name a collection site or survey extent.
