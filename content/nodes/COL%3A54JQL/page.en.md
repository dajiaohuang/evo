## Tagetes erecta {#s-1601553337}
## Solitary heads top an upright, finely divided herb

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a taprooted, smooth herbaceous annual, 0.3–1.5 m tall, with upright branches. Its leaves may be simple or deeply divided, have an elliptical outline, sit directly on the stem and bear serrated edges. Elongated, dotted oil glands are recorded on the leaves.

Each stalked, radiate flower head grows singly. Its bell-shaped involucre has 5–8 bracts. Five to eight strap-shaped ray florets surround more than 12 tubular bisexual disc florets. The dry fruits are dark, compressed and spindle-shaped, hairless or becoming hairless. Their pappus has one long awn and a shorter, scale-like awn. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
