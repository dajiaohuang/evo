## Anthurium plowmanii: morphology and epiphytic habit {#s-e5a38605df}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem with cataphylls and prophylls recorded as fibrous along it. Petioles are marked smooth; their compound shape field combines channelled or grooved forms, acute upper margins and keeled lower sides, so the geometry remains unresolved. Leaf blades are entire and flat, elliptic or obovate, with acuminate or acute tips and acute or obtuse bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is obtuse on both surfaces, secondary veins are prominent above, and a collecting vein is absent along the margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as less than half a leaf, with a cylindrical or one-keeled peduncle and a flat, ovate spathe at an acute angle to it. The entry gives conflicting states for the spathe stipe (present and shorter than the spadix, or absent). Berries are described as reddish.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Compound petiole and stipe states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
