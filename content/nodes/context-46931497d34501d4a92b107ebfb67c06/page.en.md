## The source concept {#s-321e1d3b9b}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Breviceps verrucosus* in row Anf0332 under Breviceps, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-b75d3548ad}
For Anf0332, the compilation tags fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-2b07046a0f}
The adult food field lists arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; this tag is not a complete diet and gives no share of food items.

## Seasonal activity {#s-2885e0319c}
The seasonal field is tagged wet and cold. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-bcd7327830}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 53 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-002bcd31d7}
The row records 35 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year and 5 mm as both the minimum and maximum offspring or egg size. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every clutch or offspring.

## Developmental strategy {#s-3dd65bd290}
The deposited row classifies development as direct. That label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
