## Armed stems and flattened legumes {#s-a82eb371e7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records gray-brown armed stems and alternate, twice-divided leaves. Their leaflets can be opposite or alternate and are sometimes asymmetric. Terminal or axillary panicles carry yellow or white flowers; the pods are flattened, hairless and dehiscent, with leathery valves. The source lists alternative states for several traits, which should not be treated as one fixed description of every plant.
