## The southwestern European range synthesized in 2019 {#s-25911312ce}
The 2019 treatment synthesized a southwestern European range at 0–1770 m, principally the Iberian Peninsula south of the Cantabrian Mountains and Pyrenees, while reporting absence from Spain’s southeastern tip. In France it described Atlantic coastal records between Landes and the Loire, Mediterranean records from the Spanish border to Var, an extension toward Valence in the Rhône valley and some southwestern isolates. These are the review’s cited geographic records, without new verification of every locality, continuous occupancy or physiological elevation limits.

## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-968318ff63}
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Pelobates cultripes was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-8e3aa1bdf7}
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.
