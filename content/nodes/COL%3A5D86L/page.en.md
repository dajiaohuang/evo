## Zornia pardina {#s-a4b63e6851}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zornia pardina* as an accepted species of *Zornia*. The available Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records its habitat category as terrestrial. It does not identify where specimens were collected or describe the species’ broader distribution, so the label should be read as one regional habitat record.
