## Small elliptic leaves in loose flower clusters {#s-6af653e114}
This entry records branched stems with fine hairs and stalked glands. The alternate, leathery leaves are elliptic with pointed tips and smooth edges; the lower leaves may be opposite. Loose clusters carry 25–30 florets per head, and the fruits have long stalks and a 2–3 mm bristly tuft. The habitat field says terrestrial.
