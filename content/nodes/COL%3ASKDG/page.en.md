## Cerastium mollissimum {#s-26a34d5aef}
## Woolly leaves and loose axillary cymes

Cerastium mollissimum has stems that range from prostrate to upright, with basal indumentum ranging from woolly to nearly hairless. Its sessile, lance-shaped leaves are thin and woolly. Loose, two-branched cymes grow from the leaf axils, and the flowers have a gynophore, a short stalk supporting the ovary. The source gives only a broad terrestrial habitat label.
