## Achyrocline anabelae {#s-ab06e7001d}
## Dense woolly leaves and yellow head clusters

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records branched, usually much-branched roots that are woody or partly woody. Stems may be herbaceous or woody at the base and are aromatic; dense woolly hairs conceal glandular hairs beneath. Alternate leaves are densely hairy on both sides, lance-shaped to elliptic or obovate, with narrowing bases and blunt or rounded tips that may have a small point. Margins are usually entire but can be eroded or rolled under.

Numerous yellow heads form compact clusters. The narrow involucre is cylindrical to spindle-shaped, with translucent, blunt bracts. Disk and marginal florets differ in corolla and style details; both are described as glandular-hairy, and the white pappus has slightly club-shaped tips. The dry fruits are oblong to elliptic, slightly compressed and angled, with a papilla-textured surface. Seeds are oblong or elliptic and curved, straw-colored or greenish. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; no locality or complete range is given.
