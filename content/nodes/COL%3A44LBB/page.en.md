## Needle like leaves and sandstone slopes {#s-da4cb53149}
The source description records a sprawling subshrub up to 25 cm tall, pubescent to scabrid. Leaves are sessile, fascicled, rigid and needle like, with spine tipped and often apically recurved tips. Flowers are sessile, axillary and pink. Capsule shape is marked uncertain as “?horned.” The ecology field gives sandstone slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
