## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora oerstedii? {#s-5cc4730b8f}
The herb has cylindrical to slightly angled stems and entire or three-lobed, hairy leaves. The margins are smooth; stalked glands occur on the petiole, and stipules are kidney-shaped. A solitary flower has lance-shaped or ovate bracts, awned sepals and a bell-shaped floral cup. The corona has several rows; the operculum ends in threadlike filaments, and the limen is cup-shaped. The reproductive stalk is at most 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless; narrow obovate seeds have net-like surfaces.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b96cf491de}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
