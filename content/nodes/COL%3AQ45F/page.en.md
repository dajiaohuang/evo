## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-4bc670290e}
COL26.8 records Calotes medogensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Agamidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-21cb56c6b0}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 76 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-333215ec62}
The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-c2f0956791}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 7. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
