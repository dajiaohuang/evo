## Myotis (Myotis) longicaudatus: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Myotis (Myotis) longicaudatus* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Ognev, 1927.
