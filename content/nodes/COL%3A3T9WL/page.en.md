## Lepidaploa persicifolia {#s-67c0d5df5b}
## Sessile lance-shaped leaves and lilac florets

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright stem that gradually loses its hairs and lacks branch glands. Alternate leaves sit in two vertical rows and have no stalk. Their lance-shaped blades have long-pointed tips and blunt bases; the upper face is hairless and the lower face becomes hairless. Leaf-surface glands are absent.

Flower heads grow on leafy stems. Bell-shaped involucres have overlapping bracts, with pointed inner bracts. Each head has 35–45 lilac florets. The pappus is white, and the fruits have no glands. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
