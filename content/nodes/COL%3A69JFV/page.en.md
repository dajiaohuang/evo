## Glandular stems and stalkless leaves {#s-ca69e7565e}
The Portuguese morphology entry describes ascending to erect stems with glandular hairs. The leaves have no petioles; their membranous blades are spatulate to oblanceolate and glabrous. It records no cymose inflorescence and no gynophore, the stalk-like structure below an ovary. The ecology entry says terrestrial.
