## Habitat labels in the regional flora record {#s-2e5fa41819}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20; ChecklistBank dataset 316115) lists *Plagiothecium novogranatense* (Hampe) Spruce as an accepted species in Plagiotheciaceae M. Fleisch.. The linked World Flora Online Plant List 2026-06 matches the accepted name, and the Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record carries that WFO name link. Its habitat field records the Portuguese category labels “Corticícola” (bark-associated) and “Rupícola” (rock-associated). These are source-specific habitat or substrate categories; the original field does not define them further. It supplies no collection locality, survey date, complete range, or sampling coverage, and does not state whether the material was wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil. The exact-name crosswalk is nomenclatural and does not establish full concept equivalence.
