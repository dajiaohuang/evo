## Wolffiella welwitschii: floating fronds and flowers {#s-1674c56cb0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes 2 rootless fronds joined by vegetative pouches and floating freely. The fronds are thin, flattened and elongated, ranging from orbicular to ovate; they measure 3.2–6.5 mm long and 2–5.7 mm wide. The vegetative cavities contain a median line of transparent elongated cells and have an angle of 90–120 degrees. In dry fronds, the epidermal cells are brown.

The inflorescence has 2 flowers, with one inflorescence on each side of the frond’s midline. One flower is male and one female. The male flower has 1 stamen with a unilocular, basifixed anther. The female flower has 1 basal, orthotropous ovule and a circular stigma. The fruit is a utricle; its size is given as “0.37 to 0.2 mm” in that order in the source field.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil; the fruit measurement order is preserved as recorded.

The dataset labels the habitat “Aquática,” or aquatic.
