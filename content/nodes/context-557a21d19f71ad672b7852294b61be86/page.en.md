## Source classification and concept status {#s-9ebed293cb}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2932 records *Litoria subglandulosa* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-ac257a1a57}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-33b1ce732d}
The row is marked for both daytime and night-time activity. These broad categories are not a continuous behavioural record; unlisted periods remain unspecified.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm) {#s-1db3c7f5ff}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-440f9c63df}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 5.68 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 50 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-95d8333271}
The row lists 292–425 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 2.1–2.1 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size field may refer to eggs.
