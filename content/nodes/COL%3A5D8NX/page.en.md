## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-084ea16c49}
The species-level foraging-stratum estimate assigns 100% to feeding in or just above the canopy. It describes relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a whole-day time budget. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevation bands and cannot be used to calculate flight height or habitat area.
