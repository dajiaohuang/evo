## *Abolboda abbreviata*: several short-flowered spikes per rosette {#s-802df010f3}
Its narrow, flat leaves have smooth surfaces, pointed tips and thickened edges. A rosette is a cluster of leaves around the base; each may carry more than one elliptical spike, each with two to four flowers. The lower bracts are longer than the upper ones, and the lateral sepals extend beyond them.

The source categorizes it as terrestrial.
