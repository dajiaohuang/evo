## Recorded parent of Elasmotheriina {#s-b26aba6679}
Elasmotheriina is listed at rank subtribe in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
