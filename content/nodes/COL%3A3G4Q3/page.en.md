## Succulent narrow leaves and early white flowers {#s-4c87d8ad03}
Gladiolus inandensis is 0.20–0.45 m tall, with four to six narrow, fleshy leaves 1.5–2.0 mm wide. Its white to cream flowers have a short tube, 9–12 mm long; the regional account records flowering from October to January.

## Rocky sandstone grassland {#s-fb058e27cc}
The source records rocky grassland on sandstone hillsides, within Sub-Escarpment Grassland. This is the cited habitat entry rather than a complete range.
