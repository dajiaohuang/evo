## What leaf, flower and fruit details are listed? {#s-d962d6e894}
The alternate elliptic to obovate leaves have 5–10 mm petioles and blades 16–20 cm long by 4–6 cm wide, tapering at the base. The inflorescences may be terminal or axillary racemes, often crowded; the recorded size is about 2 cm. Flowers are 10–15 mm long and may be yellow, white, pink or orange. The narrow ovoid buds are 4–6 mm wide. The sepals range from oval-lanceolate to oval, but their length is not known. The staminal tube is 3–4 mm long. The fruit is a capsule, and the seeds are globose or ovoid.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-07e7cf102f}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
