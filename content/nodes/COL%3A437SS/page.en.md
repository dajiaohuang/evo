## Microtea maypurensis {#s-0a880d17fa}
## Terminal spikes rise from slender, angular stems

Microtea maypurensis has an axial underground root and erect, hairless aerial stems with angled branches. Its thin leaves alternate or cluster, with pointed tips and tapered bases; the source lists several possible blade shapes. Indeterminate spikes terminate the stems. Flowers have lance-shaped bracts and equal stamens, while the rounded ovary leads to a muricate or hooked achene—a small dry fruit—and tiny lenticellate seeds. The entry gives no ecology or locality.
