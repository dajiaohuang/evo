## Bite traces and a bone-crushing model for Tyrannosaurus {#s-fb5beea563}
Bite traces on MOR 799, coprolite SMNH P2609.1 and measurements from seven adult skulls constrain a model of Tyrannosaurus bite force, estimated at 8,526–34,522 N. The force range is modeled; the traces and coprolite are separate evidence and do not identify every prey item or feeding event.
