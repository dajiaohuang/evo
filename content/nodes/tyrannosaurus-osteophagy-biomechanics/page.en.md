## Bite traces and a bone-crushing model for Tyrannosaurus {#s-fb5beea563}
About eighty tooth traces on the Triceratops ilium MOR 799 record biting, while bone in coprolite SMNH P2609.1 records ingestion. Separately, a muscle-and-lever model using BHI 3033 and six other adult skulls estimates Tyrannosaurus bite force at 8,526–34,522 N. That range tests mechanical capacity; the marks and coprolite are separate evidence, not one reconstructed meal or every prey item.
