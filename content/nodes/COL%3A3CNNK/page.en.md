## What growth form is recorded for Euphorbia duseimata? {#s-b5fcedfca6}
This dwarf succulent reaches about 60 mm tall. A tuberous main root supports one or two underground stems; each sends six to nine branches from its tip at ground level. The surface tubercles are not prominent. Flower stalks are deciduous, and the flowers are whitish green.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-4d35736430}
The source lists grassland as its habitat.
