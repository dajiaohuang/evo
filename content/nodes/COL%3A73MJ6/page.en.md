## What leaf and ovary traits are recorded? {#s-5921ebb825}
The elliptic to ovate leaves have short tapering tips, wedge-shaped bases and entire margins. Scale-like hairs cover both surfaces, and the tertiary veins are conspicuous beneath. The ovary also has scale-like hairs. This record supplies no fruit or seed description; those fields are unreported, not evidence that fruits or seeds are absent.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-0a2228f951}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
