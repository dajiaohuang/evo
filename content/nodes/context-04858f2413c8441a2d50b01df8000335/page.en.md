AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6714 records Typhlonectes compressicauda under Typhlonectes, Caeciliidae, and Gymnophiona, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

The row lists arthropods as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-287ff860b0}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body mass of 600 g and a maximum adult body length of 800 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian body-mass entries; this remains the AmphiBIO v1 value, not a revised species-level estimate.
