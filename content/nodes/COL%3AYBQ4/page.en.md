## Cordia sagotii {#s-aacecfacbe}
## Stiff hairs cover the upper leaf face and stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stems with stiff, appressed hairs. Elliptic to lance-shaped leaves have rounded to blunt bases and long-pointed tips. Their upper surfaces carry stiff, appressed hairs; the undersides have longer, coarse hairs. The bisexual flowers are white, with smooth, tubular calyces and corollas, each under 1 cm long. The anthers have glands, the stigma is head-shaped, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is yellow, elliptic, and hairless. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
