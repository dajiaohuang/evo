## Overview {#s-b3809c4085}
The sampled record spans about 479–250 Ma, from unequivocal Fezouata eurypterids to a reviewed late Permian occurrence. It combines local fossil records with morphology matrices; these endpoints describe the material assembled here, not the order’s exact origin and extinction.

## Diet {#s-529b5635ef}
The cited studies do not establish one feeding mode for the order; predation is not assigned as a shared observation.

## Habitat {#s-4371155d6a}
Fezouata and Russian deposits provide settings for particular samples, not a complete habitat map.

## Locomotion {#s-89a832c503}
Appendages are described in sampled fossils, but locomotor performance is not measured.

## Body size {#s-deef2f0c0e}
The cited material is not a complete comparative body-size dataset.

## Ecological role {#s-1d0148f9fd}
These occurrence and morphology studies do not support an order-wide ecological guild.

## Evidence and scope {#s-4d98b8971b}
Van Roy and colleagues document an unequivocal Fezouata occurrence and score sampled morphology; Tetlie reviews later distribution. The combined 479–250 Ma envelope is a reviewed fossil record, not an exact biological lifespan or a complete tree.

## Limits of this account {#s-6e527cf4a0}
The anatomical and geographic claims apply to the cited samples. The evidence does not provide an order-wide diet, habitat map, locomotor-performance dataset or complete body-size comparison.
