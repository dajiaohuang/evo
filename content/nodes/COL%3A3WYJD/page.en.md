## Branches, leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-f8abcf0cb8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset describes unarmed branches with cylindrical cross-sections and leaf-like stipules. Leaflets are elliptic or elliptic-oblong, with acuminate tips and bases listed as acute or obtuse. Their compound brochidodromous venation has side veins joining into loops near the margin. The upper surface is glabrous; hairs on the lower surface are limited to the primary veins. Inflorescences are listed as racemes, panicles or fascicles, in leaf axils or at shoot ends, with leaf-like bracts. Flowers have bell-shaped calyces, white or purple petals, a hairy standard (banner petal) and stamens united into two bundles (diadelphous). The fruit is a samara, a dry winged fruit, with a striate seed-bearing core. These are source-listed descriptions and alternatives, not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-2d8158f555}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
