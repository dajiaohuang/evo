## Fanjingshania anchors a historical grade {#s-c97a11ac8b}
The historical Acanthodii route starts with an approximately 439 Ma disarticulated dermoskeletal sample of *Fanjingshania*, which the cited analysis places on the chondrichthyan stem. A stem placement describes a position outside the living crown group; it does not make the specimen a direct ancestor. The 252 Ma younger edge is a rounded historical composite, not proof of a monophyletic Acanthodii or a global first occurrence.
