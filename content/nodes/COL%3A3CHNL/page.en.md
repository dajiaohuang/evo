## Overview {#s-654f0503d2}
COL26.8 accepts Euoticus elegantulus (LeConte, 1857) as species ID 3CHNL under genus ID 4G62. The Mammal Diversity Database (MDD) uses Southern Needle-clawed Galago and assesses it as Least Concern. Its distribution text names Gabon, the Republic of the Congo, Rio Muni and Cameroon south of the Sanaga River; its separate coarse country summary also lists the Democratic Republic of the Congo and a possible Central African Republic record. These are database-level country records, not a continuous range map. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## Survey records {#s-62c0daf96c}
Forbanka’s Cameroon survey used three transects totalling 8.5 km and recorded 56 encounters of E. elegantulus and E. pallidus combined. The paper reports an encounter rate of 9.9 individuals per kilometre for E. elegantulus. Transects were deliberately placed at sites where the animals had been seen or heard on earlier survey nights, so this is a detection result from selected locations, not a density estimate or a range-wide population measure.

## Habitat context {#s-44a7fe0b69}
A separate Cameroon vegetation study sampled 14 quadrats in forest areas selected after nocturnal observations of Euoticus. Tree density differed between its sampled primary and secondary forests. The authors described some flexibility across those forest types while associating Euoticus with tall, large-diameter trees. Because the study treated the two needle-clawed species together, it does not establish a species-specific habitat response for E. elegantulus.
