## ## Three leaflets, lilac flowers and a floral spur {#s-05bb68f35d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a conspicuous leaf rachis and three leaflets, ranging from oval through lanceolate and elliptical to linear. The leaves are membranous to papery, and their surfaces range from nearly hairless to sparsely or more clearly hairy. The lilac flowers bear a spur. The straight fruit has finely hairy valves. This is a regional character list; it does not state how broadly these traits were sampled.
