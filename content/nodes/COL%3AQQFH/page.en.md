## Name, classification and sample {#s-9387a146ee}
COL26.8 accepts Capito aurovirens in Capitonidae. AVONET v7 records 4 measured individuals under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name; all 4 have a complete morphological set. Trait-specific sample counts may still vary.

## Beak dimensions {#s-2183dc9a82}
Mean beak length from the anterior nostril edge to the tip is 15.3 mm; width is 6.6 mm and depth at that edge is 9.6 mm. These dimensions use standardized landmarks and are not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-4378d27f34}
Mean unflattened wing length, from carpal joint to longest primary, is 82.8 mm; mean tarsus is 22.1 mm. A separate source category, Dunning, supplies the compiled mean mass of 56.2 g; it does not show that the measured birds were weighed.

## Ecological categories {#s-84e85292ff}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, Frugivore trophic niche and Insessorial lifestyle. These dominant categories support comparison; they do not claim that the bird uses only forest or eats only fruit.

## Migration and historical map {#s-5754b7e848}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude -8.51° to 2.06°. This is not a complete non-breeding movement record or a current/global occupancy estimate; the geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Limits of these records {#s-53742540d9}
AVONET v7 presents historical species averages for the available samples, not individual ranges or diagnostic limits. The Dunning mass estimate has a separate source and cannot use the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; mapped latitudes describe historical breeding and resident ranges only. Name matches and crosswalks do not prove that historical species concepts equal COL26.8. Reproduction, current populations and conservation require other sources.
