## Axillary flowers and a dark round fruit {#s-8a704f80b8}
The SANBI account describes an evergreen shrub or small tree up to 6 m tall, with straw-coloured bark on older stems. Its leathery, glossy leaves are elliptic and reach 80 × 40 mm; the midrib is raised beneath the blade. Axillary cymes bear one to five white or pink flowers. The corolla tube is 4–6 mm long, hairless outside and densely curled-hairy at the throat; its reflexed lobes reach 6 × 3 mm. Flowering is listed for September–December. The spherical fruit is purplish black and 7–8 mm in diameter.

## Coastal forest and dune-bush habitat {#s-14c935c7bf}
The linked SANBI habitat record lists coastal forest and dune bush, within the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt and Indigenous Forests vegetation categories. These are regional habitat descriptions, not individual collection sites.
