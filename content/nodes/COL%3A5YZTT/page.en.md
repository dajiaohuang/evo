## Toothed leaves and darkening flower-head bracts {#s-337f0387a5}
The record describes cylindrical branches with stiff, bristly hairs. Stalked leaves range from lance-shaped to broadly oval, with toothed edges, a long-pointed tip and rough hairs on both surfaces. Small heads gather in branched clusters; their bracts darken as the fruits mature. The account distinguishes the tubular female and male florets and notes that the fruit is hairy at its tip. The habitat field says terrestrial.
