## Three Yutyrannus skeletons with filamentous coverings {#s-77ff1067ac}
Holotype ZCDM V5000 and referred specimens ZCDM V5001 and ELDM V1001 associate filamentous integument with large tyrannosauroid skeletons. The distribution and structure are observed; insulation, display and complete body coverage are functional reconstructions, and the evidence concerns a non-avian theropod.
