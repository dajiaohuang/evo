## Leaflets, flowers and fruit {#s-bdbb9ab06d}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry describes scaly bark and leathery, broadly elliptic to ovate leaflets. Their surfaces are recorded as glossy and hairy on both sides, or hairy below with the upper side glabrous or becoming hairless. Veins are conspicuous underneath and impressed above. The inflorescences are described as dichasial cymes (branching flower clusters) or corymbs (flat-topped clusters). Bud colours are listed as golden, yellow or brown; the sepals do not bend back and are hairy on the inner surface. Petals are white and hairy, and the ovary is hairy throughout. Fruits are yellow, with an outer skin recorded as slightly warty or dull; each contains 3–7 dark-brown seeds. These alternatives come from one regional row, which does not establish the species' full variation or global form.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-77758121ef}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
