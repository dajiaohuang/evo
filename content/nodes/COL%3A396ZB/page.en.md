## How are its female flowers arranged? {#s-5e4e10bf1c}
A 2012 regional account describes *Elegia macrocarpa* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1.5 m, with branched culms (stems) and a long rhizome (a horizontal underground stem). Its leaf sheaths fall away. Each female spikelet, a compact flower cluster, has one flower, 4–8 mm long. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## Which sandstone settings are listed? {#s-bb35224c0d}
The regional account lists sandstone slopes and flats. It does not provide a dated survey frame or a complete distribution.
