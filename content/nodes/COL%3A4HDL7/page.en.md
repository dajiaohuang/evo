## Phymaspermum scoparium {#s-5b6b17456a}
## Spine-tipped branches and axillary flower heads

The source describes a sparsely leafy shrublet whose height is unreported. Its erect or spreading, spine-tipped branches carry tiny, fleshy leaves densely covered in silvery hairs. Small discoid heads sit almost without stalks in the leaf axils, and the yellow florets have a hairy corolla. Flowering is recorded from November to March. The species is reported to favour seasonally moist sites.
