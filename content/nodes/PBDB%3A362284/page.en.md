## Recorded parent of Anteosaurinae {#s-8e99baac3c}
Anteosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Anteosauridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
