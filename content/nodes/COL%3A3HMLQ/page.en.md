## Gurania brevipedunculata Cogn. {#s-a91e1d81e4}
## Hairless, lobed leaves and clustered racemes

Gurania brevipedunculata has simple tendrils. Its hairless leaves are palmately divided into three to five lobes, with a notched base and a long-pointed tip. Racemes carry flowers grouped near the tip. The species is dioecious, and its flowers are nearly stalkless. The tubular hypanthium has minute hairs; the narrow, triangular sepals stand upright or spread outward and taper to long points. Staminate flowers have two stalkless, linear anthers, each with an appendage and two pollen-bearing compartments.
