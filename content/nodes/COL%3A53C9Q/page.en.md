## Styrax rotundatus (Perkins) P.W.Fritsch {#s-0003da7cec}
## White flowers and star-haired fruit

Styrax rotundatus has woolly branches with star-shaped hairs. Its leaves range from papery to leathery and from elliptic or ovate to nearly round; the margins may be flat or slightly rolled under. The upper surface may be nearly smooth or woolly, while the underside is woolly to densely hairy. Star hairs range from pale greenish white to gray or rusty. White petals meet edge-to-edge in the bud, and each flower has ten stamens. The fruit is a woolly drupe, globose to flattened-globose.
