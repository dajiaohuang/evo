## Mikania campos-portoana {#s-c1e80ca736}
## Velvety leaves and a hollow stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, velvety branches with hollow pith. Opposite leaves are triangular and thin, with a squared-off base, a short tapered tip, and margins recorded as entire or toothed. The upper surface is hairy and the lower surface velvety; three main veins radiate from the base.

The thyrsoid flowering cluster is branched and bears stalked heads. Short, narrow bracts sit at the base of each head stalk; the lance-shaped bracts around the heads are shaggy and have acute tips. The tubular corolla has triangular, smooth lobes as long as the tube. The dry fruits (cypselae) are sparsely hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial; this regional dataset does not establish a locality or full range.
