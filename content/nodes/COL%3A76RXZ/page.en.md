## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Pera furfuracea? {#s-8e0dbfa19c}
The branches have star-shaped scale hairs. Alternate leaves have sparse scaly hairs below and saucer-shaped glands. Axillary clusters of unisexual flower units have two basal bracts; the involucre opens fully at flowering. The source records three male flowers, but their perianth and sterile pistil details are unknown. Four female flowers lack a perianth and are nearly stalkless. Their flask-shaped ovary is densely woolly-haired; the stigma is umbrella-shaped, and sepal-derived staminodes are present. The fruit capsule splits into three segments; globose, hairy seed-bearing portions have smooth inner tissue. Caruncle presence is unknown.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a8423cd7dd}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
