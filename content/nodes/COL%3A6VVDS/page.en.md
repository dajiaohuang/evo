## Pouteria subrotata Cronquist {#s-22be5e8554}
## Reddening fruit with a seed that fills the interior

Pouteria subrotata is a 10–15 m tree with erect limbs. Its obovate leaves have prominent lateral veins and may be smooth above and smooth or sparsely bristly beneath. Small flowers cluster in leaf axils and at nodes where leaves have fallen. The corolla is white and nearly wheel-shaped. The oblong-ellipsoid fruit changes from green or yellow-green to red and contains one large seed that nearly fills the fruit; the seed scar runs along its length.
