## *Mikania longipes*: long-tipped leaves and loose panicles {#s-7d3a4f113b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, elliptic leaves with tapered bases and long, narrow tips. Their surfaces are hairless, with pinnate veins that loop toward the leaf edge; the texture is somewhat leathery. The branches may twine or be hairless. Loose panicles carry stalked heads with blunt-tipped bracts. The corolla tube and its tubular limb are about equal in length, and the fruitlets are glandular.
