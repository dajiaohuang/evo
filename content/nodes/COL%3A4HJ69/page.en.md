## How does the calyx change from flowering to fruiting? {#s-19b3ff0055}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records a 3–8 mm stem base and a broadly inflated internode near the stem tip. It describes both the leaf blade and calyx as membranous. The calyx measures 1.8–2 mm at flowering and 5–6 mm in fruit, when it is broadly bell-shaped. These stage-specific measurements are from the source entry; it reports no sample size or variation.
