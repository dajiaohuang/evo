## Two habitat labels in the flora record {#s-71be02dc4e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists *Corticícola* (growing on bark).

It also lists *Epífila*, meaning growth on leaves.

These are source-labelled substrate categories, not collection localities. The fields give no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Scope of the available record {#s-07d196f624}
The habitat fields do not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and they do not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat labels do not describe life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
