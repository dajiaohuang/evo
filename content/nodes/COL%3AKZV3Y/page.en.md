## A fine-haired climbing vine {#s-921892d093}
The Flora of Panama describes a slender vine with three leaflets per leaf. The leaflets are mostly 2 to 4 cm long, with fine hairs pressed against the underside; clusters of two to five flowers arise from the leaf axils. Flowers are white, pink or purple, and the narrow, nearly straight fruit is 2 to 3 cm long. These details come from a historical regional flora description.

## A historical range statement {#s-c0d5b3f136}
The Flora of Panama field gives a range from southern Mexico to northern South America. It is a historical source statement, not a current map or a complete global inventory.
