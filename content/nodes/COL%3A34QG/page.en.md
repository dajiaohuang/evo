## Working barcode identifications retain unresolved labels {#s-494a321a7c}
In the selected 2017 account, six *Astylosternus batesi*-labelled specimens from the Republic of the Congo form one COI Barcode Index Number (BIN) and one Automatic Barcode Gap Discovery (ABGD) group. Their 16S sequences also cluster with references labelled *batesi*, *schioetzi* and *diadematus*. The authors flag inconsistent labels and a possible species complex, and call for verification; they do not formally synonymize *schioetzi* or *diadematus* with *batesi* or confirm every reference label as a local record at LMR.

## Mitochondrial markers provide no nuclear comparison {#s-7886f3c79d}
The selected 2017 material compares barcode groups based on mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase subunit I (COI) with mitochondrial 16S references, morphology and type-locality context. Because both markers are mitochondrial, they do not provide an independent nuclear comparison or test reproductive isolation. The authors use neighbor joining to group similar sequences, not to reconstruct deep evolutionary history. Not every GenBank voucher could be examined, and many species had samples from only one or two localities. The source distinguishes missing markers, inconsistent labels, and photo- or tissue-only support from intact vouchers. Original traces, sequence assignments, type specimens and current species concepts have not been independently authenticated in this review.
