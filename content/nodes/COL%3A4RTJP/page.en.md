## Restio paniculatus {#s-53316fbc9e}
## A branched flower cluster along streams

Restio paniculatus is a dioecious perennial up to 2 m, with long rhizomes and branched stems that are round to semicircular in cross-section. Its flowers form a compound cluster bearing many spikelets on the main branches; the ovary does not split open at maturity. The account places it mainly along streams.
