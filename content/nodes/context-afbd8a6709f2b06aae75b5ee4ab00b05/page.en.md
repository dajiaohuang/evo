## Source classification and concept status {#s-a4514261ea}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2815 records Litoria eurynastes under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-ff7a6796ab}
The compilation records terrestrial and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-d00a2278c7}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 33 mm for this row. The source field Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
