## How can the annual speedwell be recognized? {#s-6845327f80}
The cited flora describes a low annual herb that usually spreads along the ground, though it can rise upward. Stems branch near the base and may root where they touch the soil. Opposite leaves are broadly egg-shaped or elliptical, with conspicuous teeth over much of their edge. Blue, wheel-shaped flowers with a white center appear singly in upper leaf axils; each has two stamens. The capsule is hairy across its surface.
