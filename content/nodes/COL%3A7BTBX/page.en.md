## How long are its female spikelets? {#s-af127153d2}
A 2012 regional account describes *Thamnochortus glaber* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1.2 m. It grows from a rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) or in tufts; its smooth culms (stems) are simple, and female spikelets (small flower clusters) reach 30 mm. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Which coastal soils are listed? {#s-b3dd858d62}
The account lists coastal slopes on sand and granite-derived loam. This regional habitat note does not establish a complete range.
