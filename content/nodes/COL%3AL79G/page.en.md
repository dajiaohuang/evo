## Begonia grisea {#s-8ae8c9ccd3}
## Star-shaped hairs cover the kidney-shaped leaves

Begonia grisea has erect stems and stipules that fall away. Its slightly asymmetric basal leaves are kidney-shaped, with star-shaped hairs on both surfaces; the margins may be entire or finely toothed. The petioles and male and female flower segments also bear star-shaped hairs. The capsule wings are unequal and seeds have rounded tips. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings.
