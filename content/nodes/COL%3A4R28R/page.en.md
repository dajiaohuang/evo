## A defensin sequence match does not establish shared activity {#s-98314f7b6f}
The study reports 64.15% sequence identity between australis MA-BD2 and a Quasipaa spinosa beta-defensin, the highest match among the candidates it compared. A sequence match does not show equal antimicrobial activity or identical expression, and the paper did not assay the spinosa peptide's activity. Weak support in the defensin gene tree also limits evolutionary conclusions; this comparison does not by itself establish a species-level sister relationship.
