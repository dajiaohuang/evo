## Which classification concept does this record use? {#s-d733ca8ac7}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates juanii* in row Anf0034, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura, following the source concept in *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011). Its correspondence to the current COL26.8 species concept has not been established; the sections below describe this deposited row.

## Recorded foraging setting {#s-262b120618}
AmphiBIO lists terrestrial foraging. This broad category does not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some microhabitat entries propagated from earlier amphibian compilations; a row-level comparison for this record is unavailable. The recorded category remains a historical value, with no replacement inferred and no claim that the original is proven wrong.

## Time of activity {#s-0734443b3d}
AmphiBIO lists daytime as the activity period. This is a broad category, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Reported adult maximum length {#s-bd3adc5dd0}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult length of 24.3 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This is a literature-compilation maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Annual reproductive frequency {#s-c94494431a}
This row records at most one reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO compiles literature counts, and the entry does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for this value.

## Larval stage {#s-6be4294f2c}
AmphiBIO marks this record as having a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
