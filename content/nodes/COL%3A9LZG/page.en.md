## Acrocomia glaucescens {#s-e58b818618}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records fewer than 10 glaucous leaves and a mature yellow inflorescence shorter than 40 cm. The same coded field leaves stem length as either over 6 or under 1, without a unit, and lists a woody, hairy spathe with or without spines. Fruit diameter is over 2.5 cm; its outer layer is yellowish or green. The habitat field records soil.
