## Micrina: rebuilding an animal from isolated plates {#s-1951c02059}
Micrina is known from isolated sclerites—small hard skeletal plates—not an articulated whole animal. Sellate plates preserve paired muscle scars; protrusions on mitral plates have been interpreted as muscle platforms. Bringing those observations together yields a testable reconstruction of a nearly bilaterally symmetrical, attached animal with two valves. The plate-to-valve homology and a position on the brachiopod stem remain hypotheses; the fossil record does not preserve the proposed articulation directly.
