## What alternatives does the description preserve in leaf and flower form? {#s-d3eb751909}
For Vitex orinocensis, the central leaflet is recorded as elliptic, oblong or obovate, with a tapering or sharply pointed tip and a base that extends down the stalk. The record notes more than three leaflets, contrasting upper and lower surfaces, and no domatia (small structures at leaf-vein junctions). Flower clusters are compact and many-flowered; the blade-to-petiole length ratio is recorded as either below or above three. These alternatives are retained in the source field and should not be collapsed into one form.

## How does the regional record classify its habitat? {#s-234e112387}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field lists Vitex orinocensis as terrestrial. It supplies no collection site or complete distribution.
