## Prismatocarpus cordifolius {#s-bb3a1b327f}
## Sparse leaf hairs and toothed bracts separate it from P. nitidus

Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 Cape flora account distinguishes this species from P. nitidus by its sparsely hairy leaves and clearly toothed bracts. The fruit is about 10 mm long. The same account places the plant in sheltered sandstone crevices.
