## *Mikania obovata*: blunt leaves and a funnel-shaped corolla {#s-f2329f0519}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, papery leaves that are egg-shaped or inversely egg-shaped, with blunt or rounded tips and hairless surfaces. Three strong veins arise from the base. Branches may twine; the stem pith is recorded as hollow or solid. Loose panicles carry stalked heads with oblong, blunt-tipped bracts. The funnel-shaped corolla limb is longer than its tube. The fruitlets are listed as hairless, glandular, or woolly, reflecting alternatives in the field.
