## Prickles, paired leaflets and a late-opening pod {#s-f57ba4016a}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 description records straight or curved conical prickles on the branches. The leaf blade has two leaflets, with a rounded base and blunt tip; the lower surface bears fine, appressed hairs. The flower has five fertile stamens alternating with five sterile stamens, and the linear petals sit around a tubular floral base. Its legume is recorded as opening late or remaining closed. These are the source row’s morphological states, not a population study.
