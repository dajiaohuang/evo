## Leaf venation and fruit rostrum in the entry {#s-0507da3ea5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records no xylopodium, eucamptodromous leaf venation and no marginal vein. Hairs on the bracts are ochraceous. One inner bracteole is recorded, and the rudimentary axis is absent. The fruit has one article; its helicoid rostrum is under 3.5 mm. The seed is brown. The habitat category is rupicolous. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
