## A species name anchored to a cultured strain {#s-be19129d11}
COL26.8 lists *Streptomyces shinuiensis* Lim et al., 2026 as an accepted species. The cited description centers on type strain HK10T, the reference culture used in naming the species. Its observations give a focused view of one strain and its laboratory characterization, rather than a survey of variation across the species.

## Filaments and growth conditions {#s-b30910dc25}
The species account describes HK10T as aerobic, Gram-stain-positive and filamentous. It reports branched substrate mycelium and aerial hyphae bearing spore chains. In culture on ISP2 medium, growth was reported from 25–40 °C, pH 7–9 and 0–9% NaCl, with optima of 30 °C, pH 8 and 3% NaCl. These are the reported culture conditions for the named strain, not environmental limits measured across wild populations.

## A saltern sediment isolate and salternamides {#s-f951ef751c}
HK10T was isolated from saltern sediment on Shinui Island, Republic of Korea. The study identifies the strain as a producer of salternamides, drawing on the production context reported in earlier isolation work. This links the named cultured isolate to a particular collection setting and reported compound production; it does not establish the strain's ecological role in the saltern sediment.

## How the study distinguished the species {#s-4ec69923ed}
The authors place HK10T within *Streptomyces* using 16S rRNA and phylogenomic evidence. They also report average nucleotide identity (ANI) and digital DNA–DNA hybridization (dDDH), two measures of genome similarity, below the species-delineation thresholds cited in their study for the comparator type strains. This is the authors' inference from that strain and comparison set; it is not a broader analysis of diversity within *Streptomyces*.
