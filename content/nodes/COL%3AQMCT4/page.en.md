## Accepted name and placement {#s-7ccb81cb44}
COL26.8 accepts Mantidactylus ambony Scherz, Rasolonjatovo, Köhler, Rancilhac, Rakotoarison, Raselimanana, Ohler, Preick et al., 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Mantellidae (family) → Mantidactylus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Small adult size and a pale lateral stripe form the diagnosis {#s-639ee30e15}
The revision gives male snout–vent length of 30.0–31.8 mm and female length of 34.0–37.9 mm. A sharply defined white-to-yellow lateral stripe runs from snout to groin along head and body. The species is distinguished from ambreensis by smaller size and from other compared species by the stripe. These are sample-based diagnostic combinations, not universal size thresholds or proof that striped individuals outside genetically confirmed localities have independently certified identity.

## New encounters and cited perch observations remain separate {#s-23308679a2}
The account reports semi-aquatic forest encounters by quiet rocky rivers at 11:00–20:00 and near irrigation canals at Station les Roussettes. It cites the earlier description for flow-associated ecology, frequent terrestrial daytime positions on ground, rocks, wood or lichen, concealment under rocks, and night perches above water, once at two metres. The quiet-river encounters and cited rheophilous description are not averaged into a measured flow preference. A cited maximum perch observation does not determine a height limit, and these substrates do not establish oviposition sites.

## Broader morphological records contrast with the type-locality statement {#s-bd7159c563}
The distribution paragraph reports northwestern and northern forests at 300–1,150 m, from relict deciduous forest south of Maevatanana to humid Montagne d’Ambre. The comments also say the species occurs only at Montagne d’Ambre when citing the earlier work, then explain that outside records in this revision rely on morphology only. The broader distribution is therefore not presented as uniformly genetically confirmed. Both source scopes are retained rather than silently restricting every record to the type area or certifying all outside records by sequence.

## The holotype connection does not sequence-certify outside specimens {#s-d7a0ccab44}
The authors group their morphologically and molecularly attributed ambony material with the earlier holotype ZSM 2078/2007, field number FGZC 1039. They explicitly distinguish morphology-only records outside Montagne d’Ambre from their molecular evidence. The type connection is an attributed taxonomic argument; it cannot make unsequenced outside vouchers genetically confirmed. No new population sample count or range-wide genomic equivalence is inferred from the paragraph’s wording about a single molecular result.
