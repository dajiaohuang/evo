## Vigna lasiocarpa {#s-1d07ce5467}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Vigna lasiocarpa* as an accepted species of *Vigna*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records climbing stems, stipules (small appendages at a leaf base) that extend below their point of attachment, and yellow flowers with laterally twisted keel petals. Its pods are linear and woolly, and its seeds are brown. No habitat claim is included in this source record.
