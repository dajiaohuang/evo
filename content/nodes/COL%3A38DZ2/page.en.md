## Ecclinusa guianensis Eyma {#s-3fced171b9}
## Raised primary veins on flat leaf surfaces

The morphology record for Ecclinusa guianensis is limited. It notes stipules with hairs on the lower surface and a smooth upper surface, and lenticels on the stem. The leaf blade is flat, with the primary vein grooved and the tertiary veins running obliquely in roughly parallel lines. The flower has five sepals.
