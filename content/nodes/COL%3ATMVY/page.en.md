## A local cloud-forest occurrence and its elevation {#s-6ac021dd66}
The 2021 Santa Cruz Tepetotutla checklist reports Charadrahyla nephila at 800–2200 m above sea level during its 2018–2019 survey. Table 1 compares this with an earlier reported elevation of 680–2256 (Mendelson and Campbell 1999) and lists photographic catalogue number IBH-RF 596. This is a reported local occurrence, not a surveyed global range, independent verification of the archived photograph, or evidence of occupancy at every intermediate elevation. ## Interpret the record within the survey design {#s-3e5106c6b4}
Four observers surveyed pristine and managed cloud-forest sites on eleven five-day trips in 2018–2019, in wet and dry seasons. Three daily windows were 09:00–13:00, 15:00–19:00 and 21:00–01:00; the paper reports 2,640 effective field hours for the whole herpetofaunal inventory. Animals were photographed and released, and GPS locations had a reported 5 m error range. The checklist's Charadrahyla nephila row establishes presence in that sampling context. Total effort and the nineteen amphibian species are not species-specific detection probabilities, population estimates or trends. The accumulation curve had not reached an asymptote, so a missing record cannot establish local absence.
