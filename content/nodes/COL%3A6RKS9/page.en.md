## *Mikania filgueirasii*: hairy leaves and long-tipped bracts {#s-3e1274cf48}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records climbing or hairy branches and opposite, elliptic or egg-shaped leaves. The leaves taper at the base, end in a long point, have entire margins, and are hairy on both surfaces; three strong veins arise at the base. Loose panicles bear stalked heads with blunt-tipped bracts. The tubular corolla has triangular, hairless lobes, and the fruitlets are hairless. These are morphology fields in a regional dataset.
