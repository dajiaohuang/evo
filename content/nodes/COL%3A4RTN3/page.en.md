## Restio tenuissimus {#s-5b495d90d4}
## Tiny spikelets on slender branches

Restio tenuissimus is recorded as a tangled, tufted, or stolon-forming perennial no taller than 30 cm. Its slender stems are rough and branched; the usually solitary spikelets are just 2–5 mm long. The regional account lists seeps as its habitat.
