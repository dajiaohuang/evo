## Aloe neilcrouchii {#s-e97784d98a}
Aloe neilcrouchii has long stems that sprawl or rise from the base and form offshoots along their length. Its broad, white-spotted leaves and tall salmon-pink flower stalks distinguish it in the regional account from its closest cited ally, A. boylei. It grows on southeast-facing rocky grassland slopes in Drakensberg foothill vegetation and Ngongoni Veld.
