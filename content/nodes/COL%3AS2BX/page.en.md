## What leaf and flower traits are listed? {#s-4c5c43b778}
The stems bear prickles. Leaves have three jointed leaflets with tiny pointed tips and entire margins. Petals are at least 48 mm long and reflex outward at flowering; their inner surface is white to cream and their obovate outer surface is hairy to silky. Appendages on the staminal tube are hairy. Each anther has two pollen-bearing compartments, and the anthers are separate. The fruit is up to 15 cm long.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-67243dc1f0}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
