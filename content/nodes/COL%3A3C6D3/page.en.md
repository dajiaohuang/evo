## Several leaf-tip forms and alternative flower cups {#s-79003f3285}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or obovate, leathery leaves with bases that may narrow gradually, be wedge-shaped or rounded. Their tips are listed as acute, shallowly notched, notched, ending in a short abrupt point, or tapering to a point. The margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme or a raceme labeled “auxotélico”; the field does not explain that qualifier. Bracts fall away; bracteole status is unknown. The flower cup is smooth and either hairy or hairless. Its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. Fruit is round or ellipsoid and smooth; fruit bracteole status is unknown. One seed is recorded, with free, not fused cotyledons in the embryo.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-75886c546b}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This category adds no range statement.
