Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Cinnyris coccinigastrus (Latham, 1802) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Keep the compiled-mass denominator {#s-fc13ab8ca8}
Mass=14.3 g and Mass.Source=Dunning denote a compiled species average; the 8 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book and individual weights were not read, leaving sex, season and locality minimum–maximum bounds unsupported.

## Omnivore is retained within a sunbird genus {#s-03301b1b16}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Omnivore and Trophic.Level=Herbivore. Omnivore niche means using multiple food niches in relatively equal proportions; Nectarivore in other genus rows does not replace this record. Herbivore level is defined by at least 70% of food resources coming from plants, a different threshold from nectar niche. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-1826333646}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Shrubland; density code 2 means semi-open vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility. These historical categories provide neither current range-wide occupancy nor individual movement radii or routes; historical map fields are not interpreted in this batch.
