## Taxonomic evidence {#s-2cb2db11ee}
The name history of this species has overlapped with that of the Kenya coastal galago. Butynski et al. (2006) noted that many early behavior and distribution studies published as *Galago zanzibaricus* at Diani and Gedi actually concerned the northern coastal form now called *P. cocos*; those records therefore do not support behavioral or distribution claims for the Tanzania Coast species. Kiwia (2006) recorded the older combination *Galagoides zanzibaricus* at Zaraninge Forest in Bagamoyo, Tanzania; this is local species-level evidence without a subspecies assignment.

## Species delimitation {#s-bba3985576}
Pozzi et al. (2020) combined mitochondrial and nuclear data with multiple species-delimitation methods and supported three independent lineages in the *P. zanzibaricus* complex: *P. cocos*, *P. zanzibaricus* and *P. granti*. This is evidence about classification and evolutionary relationships, not a study of this species’ diet, reproduction or population size.

## Field ecology {#s-a60696d0d4}
Kiwia (2006) conducted nocturnal transect surveys in Zaraninge’s dry evergreen coastal forest in Bagamoyo District, Tanzania. The study reports 58 galago counts over 223.8 km; its table lists 73 records and 88 individuals for Zanzibar galago (*Galagoides zanzibaricus*) and gives a local density estimate of 12.9 ± 10.5 individuals/km². This is a single-forest estimate with a large standard error.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-452a381670}
Zaraninge is one historical study site; its estimate does not establish current range-wide abundance, a population trend, complete habitat preferences or diet. Kiwia used the older combination *G. zanzibaricus* without specifying a subspecies. Older Diani and Gedi records from Kenya remain assigned to *P. cocos* and are not combined here.

## Evidence limits {#s-eaab44c5f1}
A historical line-transect estimate is reported for one Tanzanian coastal forest. It remains a single-site account, not a range-wide natural history, abundance change or current trend.
