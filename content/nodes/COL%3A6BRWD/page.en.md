## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-74f86b0a3b}
COL26.8 records Cycloderma aubryi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trionychidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-93b44f96f3}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Chirio et al. 2017. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Chirio et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1445fdcbd8}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
