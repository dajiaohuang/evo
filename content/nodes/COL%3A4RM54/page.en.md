## An EltonTraits entry linked by name {#s-4f26ca2978}
Rattus hoogerwerfi is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. This page follows three ecological field groups in the same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record. Because the archive uses older bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals, the name match cannot resolve later splits or synonym questions.

## Food weights summarize written accounts {#s-bed05187e7}
The nonzero weights are 40% seeds, nuts, and spores, 30% invertebrates, 20% fruit, and 10% other plant material, which includes fungi. EltonTraits standardizes verbal diet descriptions; these values are not measured intake. ABC combines high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and a congener considered ecologically similar may inform an estimate. The proportions therefore do not guarantee direct observation of R. hoogerwerfi. Zero is not proof of never eating an item, while labels such as Nect, Seed, and PlantO have broad definitions.

## Where the mammalian stratum code points {#s-1b3d7afb75}
The entry uses G for the ground stratum; the source definition also counts some aquatic foraging. Its A code describes compiler confidence in the source and classification. Metadata attributes mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). It is a category assignment, not a percentage of time or a claim that the species uses only ground level.

## How to read the activity code {#s-436933fadc}
Feeding activity at night is marked 1. Since multiple periods may each receive 1, the record is not a daily time budget or a fixed schedule across days, seasons, and locations. Activity confidence is ABC and can involve a similar congener; genus- and family-level typical values do not replace missing species-level evidence.

## References attach to separate field groups {#s-e016f2d883}
The groups use Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum, and activity may draw on distinct source material, and identical Ref numbers in bird and mammal reference tables are not necessarily the same citation. The archive preserves source strings by file, row, field group, and reference table. The underlying publications have not been read individually; labels are traceability information, not extra observations.

## What these ecological fields can support {#s-fc1a37bf03}
EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family reading guide rather than a monograph; historical species concepts do not automatically match COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized weights from literature, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may be assigned using an ecologically similar congener, so not every value is a direct observation of R. hoogerwerfi. Genus/family typical values, avian strata without species-level support, and split records awaiting individual review do not establish a definite claim. Underlying works have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation need additional sources.
