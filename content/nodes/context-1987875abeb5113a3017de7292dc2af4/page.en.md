## AmphiBIO species record {#s-feb77a2c07}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Hylarana eschatia? Row Anf4793 places it in genus Hylarana, family Ranidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
