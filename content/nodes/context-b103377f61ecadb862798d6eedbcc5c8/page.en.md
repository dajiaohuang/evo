## AmphiBIO species record {#s-d63946c4d4}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Litoria rheocola? Row Anf2913 places it in genus Litoria, family Hylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-29626708d6}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body mass: 1.65 g; maximum adult body length: 50 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass values carried over from earlier compilations, but does not say whether this row was affected; no replacement measurement is inferred.
