## Small flowers and a study-specific history of spread {#s-012964f940}
Kew describes this annual's small flowers as having four white spoon-shaped petals and six stamens; the central four stamens are shorter than the two lateral ones. Its UK cultivation guidance says autumn-sown plants flower the following March to June, then die and usually self-sow; those dates apply to UK cultivation. Kew's UK profile records it in rocky ground, dunes, open sandy and calcareous sites, as well as disturbed places such as gardens and railway ballast. A 2016 study of 1,135 natural inbred lines reported relict populations mainly in Iberia and inferred that another lineage spread north from an unknown glacial refugium. These are sample-based findings from that study, not a complete species history.
