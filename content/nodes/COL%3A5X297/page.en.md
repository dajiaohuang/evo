## Direct development still includes embryonic development {#s-46fe8893a0}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Brachycephalus alipioi as direct-developing, with terrestrial oviposition. Here, direct development means the frog has no free-living larval stage: a froglet hatches instead of a free-living tadpole. It does not mean that embryonic development is absent or that humidity is unnecessary. These are categories compiled from literature, not a newly observed spawning event or a developmental experiment on this species. The exact match between the source taxonomy and the currently accepted species concept remains uncertified.
