## What leaf, flower and seed traits distinguish Passiflora eichleriana? {#s-f77e367b92}
The cylindrical stems may be somewhat woody or herbaceous. Hairless leaves have three lobes and smooth margins; stalked glands occur on the petiole, and stipules are kidney-shaped. Flowers are solitary, with ovate to ovate-lanceolate bracts and awned sepals. The floral cup is bell-shaped; the corona has several rows, the operculum is membranous at the tip, and the ring-shaped limen is present. The reproductive stalk is at most 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless, with oval seeds whose surface is alveolate.
