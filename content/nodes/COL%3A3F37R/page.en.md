## The name and this data account {#s-031bed347b}
Galbula cyanescens is accepted in COL26.8 within Galbulidae. AVONET v7 links its record to the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and reports 8 measured individuals, but only 1 has a complete morphological set. Trait-specific sample sizes can differ, so each mean should be read with its own sampling scope.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-3bcda6686f}
Mean beak length is 38 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width and depth at that edge average 5 mm and 6 mm. These are distinct standardized dimensions, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-d956daaded}
Mean unflattened wing length is 76.5 mm from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 13.5 mm. The compiled mean mass is 25.3 g, labelled Dunning. It is a separate species-average source, not a weight assigned to all eight measured individuals.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-4554fa5cbf}
AVONET labels habitat Forest, trophic niche Invertivore and lifestyle Insessorial. These categories describe the record for comparison, not an exclusive habitat or a complete feeding profile.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-b66fd51e94}
The movement field is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps have latitude endpoints −13.6° and −3.31°; these do not show all non-breeding movements or current occupancy. Geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Read this historical record through food and foraging position {#s-40aa2ddb39}
EltonTraits 1.0 links a historical same-name, same-family record and retains both diet and foraging-position groups. The older taxonomy does not settle modern splits or synonyms.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-6073303d8c}
Invertebrates account for all nonzero food weight (100%), but source code B indicates that relative proportions may be uncertain despite reasonable confidence in the source. The standardized weight is not a measured share of consumed food.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-275b041651}
The species-level foraging record assigns 100% to trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy. This is a source-defined position category, not an exact height measurement, nest-site description or whole-day time budget.

Both field groups carry Ref_7, recorded as Handbook of the Birds of the World volume 7. The archive preserves the link; the underlying book has not been independently reviewed here, and the reference label is not another observation.

## Evidence limits {#s-6e9dc70e9d}
AVONET v7 reports means under a historical name, but only one of eight measured individuals has a complete morphological set; the separate Dunning mass mean has its own source scope. Forest, Invertivore, Insessorial and Sedentary are broad categories; historical map endpoints are not current occupancy. A name match does not establish equivalence to COL26.8. EltonTraits code B retains uncertainty in relative diet proportions, and its foraging category is not an exact height. Ref_7 remains a source label, not independently reviewed evidence.
