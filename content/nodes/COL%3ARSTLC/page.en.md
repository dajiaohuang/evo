## Rain-associated observations of the chloeae subspecies {#s-cfa7e4ab93}
The 2019 treatment explicitly said the ecology of P. balcanicus chloeae had not been studied separately and inferred similarity to the nominal subspecies. It also reported one observer’s count of more than 70 individuals, mostly juveniles, during a 15-minute search at the type locality in late October 2018. The same observer recorded activity around 13–20 °C and as low as 7 °C. These observations concern that subspecies and locality; encounter temperatures are not experimental thermal limits, and the timed count is not a standardized density estimate.
