## Accepted name and placement {#s-a3ff352811}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis diadematus (Jiménez de la Espada, 1875) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Six local records are not a current provincial census {#s-0085457f30}
Table 2 of the 2026 paper attributes 6 records to source-labelled Pristimantis diadematus in Fátima parish, Pastaza, Ecuador, between September 2013 and September 2018. These are cumulative records, not a certified count of distinct marked animals, population density, or current abundance. The surveys combined daytime and night-time visual and photographic encounters, ten 8 × 8 m quadrats, four 100 × 20 m transects, and 50 pitfall traps across six habitats. The site description’s 900–1,100 m gradient and a later 2025 weather-summary temperature are not this species’ measured elevation limits or thermal tolerance. Province-wide occurrence and all source identifications remain uncertified.

## Species-labelled rows conflict with a genus-only identification statement {#s-7c4ba607ea}
Methods state that Pristimantis individuals were conservatively identified only to genus, without molecular analyses. Table 2 nevertheless prints Pristimantis diadematus and other named Pristimantis species alongside five separate Pristimantis sp. rows. This conflict remains unresolved: the named row is presented as an attributed source record, not independent certification of species identity. The five unnamed rows are not assigned to this species or pooled into its 6 records. Original diagnostic photographs, vouchers or author clarification are needed before treating the names as verified occurrence evidence.
