## Searsia refracta {#s-58578925f6}
Two regional accounts describe a dioecious shrub or small tree and note leaf-form distinctions, a fruit-based diagnosis and a suspected hybrid zone with *Searsia crenata*.

## Leaf forms and fruit distinguish the regional descriptions {#s-b2220d5af9}
A 2012 account describes a dioecious evergreen shrub or small tree to 4 m, with three-part leaves and sessile obovate leaflets, pale-yellow flowers, and round smooth drupes whose stones bear lateral processes. The 1993 account distinguishes a leafy form from a glabrous, less revolute inland form; without fruit, it says the inland form is difficult to separate from *R. pentheri*. Its diagnosis emphasizes squarrose spines, somewhat rugose, revolute, hairy leaflets and pruinose bluish-brown drupes with a laterally ridged stone. Collections between Alexandria Forest and the Great Fish River show features of both this species and co-occurring *R. crenata*; the account suspects they are fertile natural hybrids. A separate habitat entry says “scrub.” These are regional statements, with no dated sampling frame or population-frequency estimate.
