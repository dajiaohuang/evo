## Restio arcuatus {#s-6b03d36448}
## Curving branches on steep southern slopes

Restio arcuatus is described as a tangled perennial to 50 cm, with branches that bend and leaf sheaths loosely wrapped around the stems. Its spikelets are solitary and about 5 mm long. The account records steep, south-facing slopes below 1,200 m as its habitat.
