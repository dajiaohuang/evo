## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-10c453caeb}

## Evidence limits {#s-01b520663e}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical data guide, not a complete species account. This page interprets one supported ecological field group and leaves diet and activity unresolved. Food and foraging percentages are weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of diet, intake or feeding time. Source labels have not been individually traced to the underlying works, and some mammal entries may refer to a congener. Local and seasonal variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.

## Habitat, feeding and activity position {#s-654ea465bf}
The record places habitat in tall-tree vegetation with a relatively closed canopy and classifies its feeding niche around terrestrial vertebrates. It assigns no single primary lifestyle. The source uses a threshold of at least 60% of food resources to define a dominant feeding niche. These are broad record categories, not a new measurement of diet proportions or evidence that the owl eats only one kind of prey. They do not replace food lists, behavior observations or local microhabitat descriptions.

## The scope of the migration category {#s-778ac23a31}
The source classifies the record as sedentary and does not list regional seasonal movements separately. It provides no current routes, schedules or movement distances for every population.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-eb8397d91d}
These are limited historical ecology and geography records, not a complete species monograph. AVONET1_BirdLife uses HBW–BirdLife 5.0 names from December 2020. Matching names and families, plus the one-to-one BirdLife, eBird and BirdTree crosswalks, support an association but do not prove equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Ecological fields are broad categories derived from literature, which was not individually reviewed here. The original morphology may include inference from relatives, and mass may come from genus averages or models; neither is presented as a direct measurement of this owl. The maps cover historical breeding and resident ranges and provide no current population, occupancy or conservation assessment.
