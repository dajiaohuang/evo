## AmphiBIO species record {#s-ee747e5deb}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Hyloxalus italoi? Row Anf1597 places it in genus Hyloxalus, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
