## The source concept {#s-60a429014e}
AmphiBIO v1 records Hyla tsinlingensis in row Anf2332 under Hyla, Hylidae and Anura, using the species concept in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The measurements and categories below are values deposited in that compilation. This row has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept, so read it as a source record rather than a confirmed modern taxon profile.

## Foraging strata {#s-9fcffe09a5}
AmphiBIO marks terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. These broad labels do not give seasonal or life-stage detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; the affected row-level comparison has not been captured here. Treat these historical labels as needing review. No replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show that every original value is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a0af081f29}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 44.1 mm. For Anura, its body-length field means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-c86c52a1be}
This row reports at most one reproductive event per year and an offspring-or-egg size of 1.3 mm. AmphiBIO compiles these values from literature; the row does not specify the sex, population, date, or wild versus captive conditions behind each value. The size field may describe an offspring or an egg.
