## Several leaf shapes and a smooth fruit {#s-090136bc87}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists elliptic, oval or oblong leaves with acute or gradually narrowed bases and tips that are acute or tapering. The leaves are papery; their flat margins lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis; bract status is unknown. Smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth. Fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-fe4e3556b7}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The label does not add a distribution statement.
