## Staberoha distachyos {#s-a36a23c406}
## Large bracts around the solitary female spikelet

Staberoha distachyos is a dioecious, rhizomatous perennial reaching 75 cm, with simple stems. The female spikelets are solitary and about half as wide as they are long; their bracts measure 11–16 mm. The account records well-drained sandy flats and plateaus as habitat.
