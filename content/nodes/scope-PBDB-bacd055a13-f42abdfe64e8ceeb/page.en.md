## What the material shows {#s-32b570a510}
Holotype NUFV 405 preserves about 65% of one skeleton. Its weight-bearing limbs occur with traits used to infer freshwater swimming, a combination relevant to hypotheses about pinnipedimorph transitions.

## Feeding {#s-8fb98f28cc}
Dentition supports a carnivorous diet; prey are not directly preserved.

## Setting {#s-81bb8a6156}
The fossil occurs in a freshwater-lake deposit. A habitual freshwater habitat is inferred rather than observed.

## Movement {#s-63d0b85dee}
Limb and body proportions support both terrestrial and semiaquatic capability as interpretations, not measured performance.

## Size {#s-e9c607786e}
The animal is described as medium musteloid-sized; the available account does not give a complete population range.

## Ecological role {#s-d94918001e}
A semiaquatic predatory role is inferred from anatomy and setting.

## Preserved features {#s-0aa6cd69b7}
The holotype includes substantial cranial and postcranial material.

## Preserved features {#s-87e4a5d193}
Its limbs could bear weight and also show proportions used to infer swimming.

## Preserved features {#s-25da390a03}
A long tail and freshwater depositional context contribute to the semiaquatic interpretation.

## How to read the evidence {#s-d9d492309d}
The associated skeleton supports an anatomical mosaic. Semiaquatic locomotion, pinnipedimorph placement and a freshwater transition are interpretations, not an ancestor sequence.
