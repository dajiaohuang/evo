## Three habitat labels in a regional record {#s-468e652fec}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Leucomium strumosum (Hornsch.) Mitt. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; each label below is a source category rather than a collection locality.

One entry is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

A second entry is separately labelled “Saxícola,” also indicating a rock-associated substrate category.

A third entry is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The record gives no collection locality, survey date or complete range.

The first habitat field supplies no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

The second habitat field supplies no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

The third habitat field likewise supplies no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

These three source-labelled habitat categories are the only biological content in this regional record; they do not make a complete account of the species.
