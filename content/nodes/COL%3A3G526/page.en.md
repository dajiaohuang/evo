## Nodding scarlet flowers and reduced stem leaves {#s-9bf8798164}
Gladiolus stokoei is 30–45 cm tall. Its flowering stem carries reduced, softly hairy, bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower)-like leaves, while nodding scarlet flowers with cupped tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) make it distinctive.

## Marshy sandstone slopes {#s-d832ef13e0}
The regional account records marshy sandstone slopes at 500–1,000 m.
