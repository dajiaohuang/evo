## Lithops otzeniana {#s-6bb6fd35c8}
## Heart-shaped paired leaves with scalloped windows

Hammer’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a dwarf, somewhat heart-shaped succulent. Its leaf pair gapes slightly and is elliptic from above, with convex upper surfaces that range from smooth to slightly wrinkled. Bold scalloped markings edge the fairly large leaf windows, which are light grey and usually tinged with pale pink, cream, green or blue.

The flower is yellow with a white centre and 20–30 mm across. The habitat account records decomposed granite, often beneath shrubs.
