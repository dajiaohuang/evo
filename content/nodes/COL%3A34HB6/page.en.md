## Copper to magenta flowers on marooning stems {#s-111a41b572}
The record describes a succulent dwarf shrub up to 40 cm tall, with many upright branches; older internodes become deep maroon. Its narrow linear leaves spread or curve backward. Flowers are 16–20 mm across, with petals ranging from bright copper to magenta-purple, white staminodes and pale-yellow anthers. Flowering is recorded from October to February. The habitat is listed as karroid shrubland.
