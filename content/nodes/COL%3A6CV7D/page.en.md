## Clumped stems and fine bract veins {#s-843ddc83bd}
Regional descriptions place this clump-forming corm-bearing herb (a corm is a swollen underground storage stem) at 0.3–0.75 m. Its pale to deep lilac-pink or magenta flowers and short bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) with only 3–4 lateral veins on each side of the midvein help distinguish it. One account also notes that cormlets are absent from the leaf sheaths at the flowering-stem base.

## Open grassy places {#s-175c4e137d}
The cited regional account records open grassy places. It gives no dated survey frame or population frequency, so this entry describes a recorded habitat rather than the species’ full ecology.
