## Retroposon evidence for a whale–hippo sister relationship {#s-b0d93f31bf}
Retroposons are DNA insertions whose shared presence can mark common ancestry. In sampled living cetartiodactyls, the study isolated ten additional loci, characterized sixteen independent insertion events and analysed twenty informative events; four loci support a whale–hippo clade. This is a topology test across living genomes: it supports Hippopotamidae as the extant sister group of Cetacea, but does not date their split, place every fossil cetartiodactyl, or identify a direct ancestor.
