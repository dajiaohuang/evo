## Branched glandular hairs {#s-906fea40b4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records two to five pairs of elliptic to ovate leaflets, with six pairs occurring rarely. Branched, tree-like glandular hairs are noted on the stems and flower clusters. Yellow flowers have ten stamens, and the pod sides twist as the fruit matures. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
