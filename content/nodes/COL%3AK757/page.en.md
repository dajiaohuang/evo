## Baccharis coronata {#s-744ccfd74d}
## Crenate leaves and racemes of flower heads

The Brazilian Flora fields record no woody underground base (xylopodium). Branches may be smooth and unwinged or striated. The developed leaves have pinnate veins, distinct petioles, lance-shaped or elliptic blades and crenate edges. Flower heads form racemes; the sex fields describe either heads with only tubular male florets or heads with only threadlike female florets. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial, with no further distinction.
