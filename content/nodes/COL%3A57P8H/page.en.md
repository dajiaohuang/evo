## Name and trait overview {#s-d09238c4e7}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts *Trachemys grayi* in Emydidae. ReptTraits v1-2 compiles reported traits for this species under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and retains citations by trait. The chapters below describe the compiled record; they do not imply that every population, sex or life stage shares the same characteristics.

## Activity and diet labels {#s-2ca228c5c9}
The row records microhabitat as Aquatic. These are broad literature-derived labels; they indicate reported patterns, not a complete habitat, diet or year-round activity account, and a species-level row cannot show differences among localities, seasons or life stages.

## Reproductive fields and summary values {#s-8c2dbd0518}
The reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean of offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 9.35. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Limits of these trait data {#s-b4199f654c}
This supplemental account uses limited trait material and does not count as complete morphology or ecology. This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-a1db8a78b0}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 1854 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-dd0c5bcb21}
Retained compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 2920 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 9.35 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 65.43 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-f33d93885c}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 75 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.
