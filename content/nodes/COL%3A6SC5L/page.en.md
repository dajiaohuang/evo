## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-42298860ef}
Nothura chacoensis is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Tinamidae. This page reads the same-name historical life-history record through 7 focal fields. Each field is supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows with matching name and family, although the summaries may still include inherited historical taxonomy.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-c702b5b75b}
The archive reports adult body mass of 250.13 g and birth or hatching mass of 18.6 g. Adult mass may combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; the second value refers to a different life stage. These values do not follow the same individuals through development or give the species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-87e35d2365}
The available fields report female maturity, defined by the source as age at first conception, at 57 d; 5.5 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch; 17 d from oviposition to hatching; and age at flight capability at 28 d. Some sources use leaving the nest for the last field, and d means days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring and egg counts, so a fractional summary does not mean one event produced a fraction of an individual. Each timing value describes a different event, not one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-cf551cb846}
The archive's maximum_longevity_y value is 4 years, the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current record that can be repeatedly attained.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-8392b82e20}
Same-name sparse rows carry source labels including Dunning, 1992; Davies, S.J.J.F., 2002; and BC Birds - Bump, G., Bump, J.W., 1969. The batch archive retains raw row numbers, values and labels for each field, along with the final table's full reference explanations. A label may identify a primary paper, book or another database. Row counts do not count measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-3135e0f308}
These values come from same-name records in the 2015 life-history archive. Except for maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found, values are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values. They are not direct measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit information across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family, and the presence of same-name sparse rows, support this page's association; they do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been reviewed individually. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
