## Begonia elianeae {#s-321bb294e4}
## Several hair types appear across the leaves

Begonia elianeae has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its strongly asymmetric leaves are obovate or oblong, with entire margins; simple, glandular and star-shaped hairs are recorded on the petioles and both leaf surfaces. Glandular hairs also occur on the male and female flower segments. The female segments have entire margins, and bracteoles are present. The capsule wings are strongly unequal; the seed tip is blunt.
