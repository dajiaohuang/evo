## Delosperma velutinum {#s-c37fc64392}
## Fine hair-like papillae cover a compact plant

Bolus’s 1929 description portrays a compact plant up to 5 cm tall. Its pale-green, thick leaves are ovate to broadly ovate, narrow toward the base, and rise from densely leafed flowering shoots. Fine hair-like papillae give the herbaceous parts a velvety surface.

Solitary daytime flowers are about 14 mm across. White petals barely exceed the sepals; pale-pink sterile stamens and pale filaments gather in a cone, with yellow pollen. The expanded capsule reaches 8 mm across. A separate 2019 flora lists shady, humus-rich places and the vegetation types Sub-Escarpment Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Savanna, and Indian Ocean Coastal Belt.
