## How is Kielmeyera bifaria described? {#s-2cf877bd22}
The taxonomic record describes an upright plant with leathery, elliptic to oblong leaves. Leaf tips range from blunt to shallowly notched, and bases from rounded to tapered or wedge-shaped; the blades lack reddish spots and glands, and their margins are not rolled under. Flower clusters have falling bracts and more than three flowers. The white petals are moderately asymmetric, while the green inner and outer sepals differ in size. The upright anthers have apical glands and the pollen is grouped in polyads, clusters of grains. The ovary and style are recorded as hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3d8a9adc5c}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no collection locality or date.
