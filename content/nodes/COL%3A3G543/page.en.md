## A long tube on a fynbos Gladiolus {#s-495c6833d9}
Gladiolus uitenhagensis resembles G. permeabilis but has a longer perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts), 22–35 mm, and grey-blue to white flowers. The cited account records it in sandstone fynbos (a Cape shrubland vegetation type), flowering after fire; the taxon is described as poorly collected.

## Sandstone fynbos after fire {#s-6be0cfd1d2}
The cited account records rocky sandstone slopes in fynbos and notes flowering after fire. It gives no full range or fire interval.
