## AmphiBIO species record {#s-57ce4c1dff}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Ameerega altamazonica? Row Anf1453 places it in genus Ameerega, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging categories in this record {#s-c989a5fd9e}
The AmphiBIO row lists these broad foraging categories: terrestrial (land). They do not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat categories carried over from earlier compilations, but does not identify whether this row was affected. These remain the AmphiBIO labels; no replacement category is inferred.

## Activity periods in the compilation {#s-11c230b707}
The AmphiBIO row classifies activity as twilight. These are broad time-of-day categories, not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-e1abb4629d}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 25 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-833df821fd}
For this source concept, the literature compilation records these life-history values: minimum number of offspring or eggs per clutch: 14 individuals/eggs; maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch: 26 individuals/eggs. These are literature-reported minima, maxima and counts; the row does not tie each value to a sex, population, date or wild/captive setting. The source notes that offspring-size values may refer to offspring or eggs.
