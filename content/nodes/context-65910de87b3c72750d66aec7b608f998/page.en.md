## The source concept {#s-a385bfd055}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Mertensophryne nairobiensis* as row Anf0690, in the genus Mertensophryne, family Bufonidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Developmental strategy {#s-6745169b9e}
The row records larval stage (Lar = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
