## What is distinctive about its female flowers? {#s-76cfbab6ae}
A regional account describes *Restio cincinnatus* as a rhizomatous (with a horizontal underground stem) or tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 40 cm. Its culms (stems) are much branched, knobbly and flexuous. Each female flower has six tepals, the flower segments, and a single-chambered ovary.
