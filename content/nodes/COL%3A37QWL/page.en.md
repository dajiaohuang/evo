## Drosera slackii {#s-6b00aedfda}
## Leaf hairs differ between the upper and lower surfaces

The account distinguishes Drosera slackii from D. aliciae by its leaves: the upper surface is hairless across the lower half, while the underside bears only a few fleshy hairs. The habitat is described as damp sandstone soil. No collecting locality or broader sampling frame is provided in this entry.
