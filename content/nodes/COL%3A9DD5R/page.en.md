## Stem surface {#s-25a0e84aee}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as hirsute, covered with coarse hairs that spread outward. It lists simple hairs, thread-like unbranched bristles, and capitate glandular hairs whose tips form small heads. Branched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-2c8227b7bb}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are caducous and fall early. Extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) are absent, while projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) are present. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; its floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-343ead9c53}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, has triangular lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and no hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.
