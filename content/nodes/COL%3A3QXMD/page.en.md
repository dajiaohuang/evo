## Juttadinteria albata {#s-9de7fc0d25}
## Broad, nearly toothless leaves distinguish this plant

Klak’s 2013 Greater Cape flora account compares this species with J. attenuata. It describes J. albata as procumbent to erect, with pale, smooth leaves that are nearly toothless. The leaves are at least 12 mm wide at the base, usually much wider, and narrow toward the tip.

The habitat account lists sandy soil on rocky slopes, south-facing ground, gravel and river terraces.
