## Indoor captive samples delimit the microbiome evidence {#s-8e8a39fb96}
The 2023 synthesis included 28 samples labelled Eurycea waterlooensis from indoor captive individuals. The authors explicitly stated that these indoor environments did not represent the native habitat. Captive samples contributed to bacterial relative-abundance and diversity estimates; the separate statement that only two host species were represented exclusively by captive animals places waterlooensis in that category. These sample counts are not established counts of distinct marked individuals, and laboratory-associated bacterial communities do not describe the complete wild microbiome, native water chemistry, breeding ecology or pathogen resistance. Sampling-habitat categories in this synthesis refer to sample provenance rather than whole-species life history.
