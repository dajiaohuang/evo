## Guayania bulbosa {#s-d785148eb7}
## A perennial herb with tuberous roots

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a perennial herb with tuberous roots. Its thin leaves are elliptic to ovate, with scalloped-toothed margins. The flowers are recorded in groups of 29–36. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; the entry gives no locality or complete range.
