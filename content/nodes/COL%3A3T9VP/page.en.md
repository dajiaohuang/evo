## Sessile, narrow leaves and lilac florets {#s-2413869a34}
The morphology record describes an upright, bristly stem without glands. Narrow, linear leaves are sessile and arranged in two ranks; the upper surface is silky-haired, the lower bristly, and both have glands. Bell-shaped heads have partly overlapping bracts and 10–15 lilac florets. The pappus is white, and the fruits are recorded without glands. The habitat field says terrestrial.
