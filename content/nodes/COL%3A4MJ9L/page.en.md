## Accepted name and placement {#s-601d18a280}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis appendiculatus (Werner, 1894) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Compiled maximum adult length {#s-601b69428d}
AmphiBIO reports 35 mm as maximum adult body length for its Frost 2011 source concept. In anurans this measure is snout–vent length, from the snout to the cloaca. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every adult, and has not been reconciled with the current species concept.
