## Habitat context {#s-fb8c683b8e}
A two-month exploratory study in 2008 found a group of at least four at Mt. Rore Katimbu, in the mossy cloud forest of Lore Lindu National Park. The site was around 2,100 m elevation; the paper contrasts this highland setting with lowland Sulawesi tarsier studies.

## Behavioral records {#s-e4b28ec7d9}
Researchers captured three adults from the group. During limited observations, the animals returned to the same sleeping tree and were heard vocalizing or scent-marking infrequently; the paper presents these as preliminary contrasts, not fixed species-wide rules.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-618fb3c878}
The study was explicitly exploratory, with observations of one group and three captured animals. Proposed links between high-altitude resources and behavior remain hypotheses requiring broader samples.
