## What leaf, flower, and fruit traits are recorded for Parinari cardiophylla? {#s-07eded0a54}
The record describes aerial stems and oblong leaves with shallowly heart-shaped bases, flat margins, and a 9–12 mm tapering tip. Stipules fall early and are less than 2 cm long. Extrafloral nectaries occur midway along the leaf stalk, and are often present along the lower leaf margin; these glands lie outside the flowers. Side veins are spaced 3–5.5 mm apart. Flowers arise along the sides of branches, The hypanthium, the floral cup at the base of the flower, is under 6 mm long. The fruit is elliptic.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-fbd9366f00}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
