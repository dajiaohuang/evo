## Two Cameroon species illustrate marker-specific evidence {#s-29ee992df1}
The 2020 descriptions of arcanus and mbabo combine morphology, mitochondrial sequences and nuclear RAG1 evidence. Their distance is 1.2% in a short 510-base 16S barcode but 5.0% in the longer 12S–16S fragment. Those values answer different marker comparisons; inferred nuclear haplotypes are allelic units rather than additional frogs. Adult sizes overlap other small puddle frogs, and juvenile steindachneri can resemble the new species superficially, so a genus name or size alone cannot assign old records. These two mountain species exemplify the studied Cameroon radiation, not a biological synthesis of all Phrynobatrachus.
