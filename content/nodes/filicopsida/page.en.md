## A living-tree result, not a fossil timeline {#s-498c29feb2}
A four-gene supermatrix resolves many relationships among the sampled ferns in Filicopsida, while some branches remain poorly supported. The tree describes the sampled living-heavy data, not the complete history of the group.

## Why no single age is shown {#s-5828b9ac68}
The historical class name does not map cleanly onto one crown or total group. The review questions Devonian fern assignments but recognizes firmer Lower Carboniferous filicalean evidence, so the former 360–0 million-year display is withheld rather than presented as a settled range.
