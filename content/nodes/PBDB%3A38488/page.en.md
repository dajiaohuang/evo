## Evidence limits {#s-bf4ae20419}
The reading path draws on selected text, tables and captions from studies published in 2008, 2021 and 2023. The eleven MIC-V55 histology sections are samples from one specimen. Claims about tooth attachment, replacement generations, diet and sexual maturity remain interpretations with stated limits; the 2021 flight models depend on their published assumptions and correction. This page does not present independent specimen or slide examination, a rerun of the models, or a complete review of original descriptions and later literature. The PBDB occurrence counts, ages and parent chain describe database entries and remain separate from the anatomical studies.
