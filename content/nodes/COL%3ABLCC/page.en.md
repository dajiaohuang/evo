## Lance-shaped leaves and silky fruits {#s-f1deda2ec0}
The morphology record describes a firm aerial shoot from an unthickened underground base. Alternate, stalked leaves range from lance-shaped to oval-lanceolate and end in a tiny point. Radiate flower heads have pointed bracts and a single series of ray florets. The obovoid fruits are silky-haired; their awns are free from the pappus scales.
