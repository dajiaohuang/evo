## Accepted name and placement {#s-3cb96aea85}
COL26.8 lists Bombina orientalis (Boulenger, 1890) as a species in Anura, family Bombinatoridae, genus Bombina. This is the classification in the 20 August 2026 release. It identifies the catalogue entry; it does not by itself establish which older research records below describe the same species concept.

## What a historical compilation reports {#s-90aa4a75e7}

That family label differs from Bombinatoridae in COL26.8, and no mapping establishes that the old row and the current catalogue species are equivalent. The values below describe this historical compilation row, not a validated profile of every current B. orientalis population.
