## Genetic connections among six emperor colonies {#s-7ec0be867a}
Cristofari and colleagues analyzed RAD-SNP data from 110 emperor penguins sampled at six East Antarctic colonies in 2004–2012. Their coalescent model supported strong demographic coupling among those colonies, interpreted by the authors as one genetic population. The sampling and model do not provide a current census or instantaneous migration rates.
