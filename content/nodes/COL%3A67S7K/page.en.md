## A maximum measured to the vent {#s-5172a712e2}
The compiled maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 63.5 mm; the female maximum is 43.8 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent, excluding the tail. These are reported maxima rather than typical sizes or growth limits. The sex-specific records may come from different places or studies, so their difference alone cannot establish size dimorphism within one population.

## Carnivorous and cathemeral in the source {#s-59a645720f}
The entry classifies the diet as carnivorous and activity as cathemeral, a category spanning daytime and nighttime activity. It does not identify prey or provide a timetable, and it gives no microhabitat. These broad labels cannot describe every season, locality or life stage.

## Egg-laying is recorded without a clutch count {#s-36b6f66c13}
The source labels the reproductive mode oviparous, or egg-laying, but gives no clutch or litter count. That absence leaves the number of eggs per event unresolved. The mode label alone does not establish embryo nutrition or show that reproduction is uniform among populations.
