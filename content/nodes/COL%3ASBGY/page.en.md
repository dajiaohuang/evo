## ## Five or seven leaflets and sickle-shaped pods {#s-e3b252ca60}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character list describes papery, odd-pinnate leaves with a conspicuous rachis and five or seven obovate, hairy leaflets. The purple flowers have a spur. The pod is sickle-shaped, and its valves are finely pubescent. The regional dataset does not provide a population sampling frame.
