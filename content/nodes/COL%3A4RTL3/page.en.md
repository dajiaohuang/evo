## *Restio rigidus*: bracts that hide the flowers {#s-5d4500e97f}
This rhizomatous perennial reaches 50 cm and sends out trailing, branched stems. Its spathes extend beyond the spikelets, then turn grey and ragged. The flowers are partly hidden by firm, pointed bracts. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

It is recorded on dry, stony slopes and summit ridges.
