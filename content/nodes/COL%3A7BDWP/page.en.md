## Tachigali alba {#s-296d961516}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali alba* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records vestigial stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), up to 10 leaflet pairs, and smooth-backed leaflets. Flowers are radial, with 10 stamens, and the fruit is elliptic to oblong; the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
