## Ferrichrome in a lichen experiment {#s-1a80861859}
A 2026 study detected ferrichrome in Xanthoria parietina thalli and in culture of the fungal partner (mycobiont) isolate L2379. Adding ferrichrome promoted growth of the tested Trebouxia decolorans photobiont strain MT603980. The Austrian field thalli and cultured fungal isolate were not from the same thallus, so the assay establishes neither a natural pairing nor a response shared by all populations and lichen partnerships.

## Evidence limits {#s-03ee004b8d}
This is a laboratory result from specified thalli, one cultured mycobiont isolate, and one photobiont strain. It does not establish how all X. parietina populations or lichen symbioses respond.
