## Rabiea difformis {#s-5a406ebf73}
## Dark green tannin cells mark its tapered leaves

The 2019 Eastern Cape flora describes a perennial, tuft-forming dwarf shrublet up to 3 cm high, with thickened, tapering roots. Its leaves narrow gradually from a broad base to a pointed tip. They are not keeled, and the epidermis contains dark green tannin cells.

Flowers are solitary and yellow; the flora lists flowering from May through August. The capsules are reddish brown and woody, with seven to ten chambers, valve wings and covering membranes, but no closing bodies. The habitat account records rocky outcrops of Karoo sediments.
