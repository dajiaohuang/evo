## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-14cd0a475f}
COL26.8 records Tropidophorus iniquus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-93f7721df6}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 96 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4aebaa6513}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f8d8d74045}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 4.6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
