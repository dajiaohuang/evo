## A 45–75 cm tufted perennial {#s-e7c097659b}
The regional flora describes *Tetraria flexuosa* as a tufted perennial 45–75 cm tall, with dark-brown spikelets.

## From flats to middle slopes {#s-37c8ef9910}
The SANBI habitat entry records flats to middle slopes. It provides no dated sampling frame or population frequency, so it is a regional habitat note rather than a full-range survey.
