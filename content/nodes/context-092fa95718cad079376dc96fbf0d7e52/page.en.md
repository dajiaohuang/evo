## The source concept {#s-10c2f74c57}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Hyloxalus sauli* as row Anf1618, in the genus Hyloxalus, family Dendrobatidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-f92757a405}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) and aquatic (Aqu = 1) as broad foraging categories. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Adult food categories {#s-505cb211d4}
The row records arthropods (Arthro = 1) as an adult food category. AmphiBIO compiles these from literature reports based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; listed categories are not a complete diet or measured food proportions.

## Seasonal activity {#s-fdb4d77318}
The row records wet and warm (Wet_warm = 1) and dry and warm (Dry_warm = 1) as seasonal activity categories. AmphiBIO relates activity conditions to the local annual climate, so these labels do not correspond to fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a0eac2d029}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 28.7 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-5c00da008f}
The row records 6 as the minimum and 14 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch. The row records minimum and maximum offspring or egg sizes of 1.5 and 2.9 mm. The maximum reproductive output listed is 4 events per year. These are literature-compiled extrema or counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-249c3747ef}
The row records larval stage (Lar = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
