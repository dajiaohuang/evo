## Names used in the historical records {#s-10bfe23df1}
The pinned COL26.8 entry is Psilopogon mystacophanos (7WP6J). AVONET v7's BirdLife 2020 row 9698 and eBird's 2021 row 8665 use that name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-85DECE79. BirdTree row 8620 instead uses Megalaima mystacophanos, matched as 1BL to 1BT; the read COL older-combination record 7VSW6 points to the accepted entry. These crosswalks locate historical rows but do not prove that all sources used the same species boundary.

## What the measured birds contribute {#s-405ae7eef6}
Six birds enter the morphology record: two females, three males and one of unrecorded sex; four have complete measurement sets. Means are 30 mm for nostril-to-tip beak length, 14.9 mm for beak width, 25.2 mm for tarsus and 95.2 mm for unflattened wing. The hand-wing index is 15.8, calculated as Kipp's distance divided by wing length and multiplied by 100. It is a wing proportion, not flight speed. Inference=NO does not give every field the same sample count, and beak width alone does not test fruit handling or pecking performance.

## A separately compiled mass value {#s-d7d71cd97a}
The compiled mean mass is 77.4 g and the source field is Dunning. It has a separate denominator from the six birds measured for morphology; they are not thereby a weighed sample. Individual weights and Dunning's original book were not examined, so sex-, season- and locality-specific ranges are unsupported.

## Fruit niche and trophic level answer different questions {#s-5204d13df8}
Metadata classifies the niche as Frugivore, with fruit as the predominant resource, while the separate trophic-level field is Omnivore. One field describes the primary resource category and the other a broader trophic classification; neither is a record of individual meals. Their thresholds are not measured percentages for a place or season. Fruit species, food size, digestion and seed-dispersal experiments are absent, so detailed diet and dispersal effects cannot be inferred.

## Activity and habitat classes {#s-837fef2a1e}
Insessorial describes primarily perching on branches or other substrates above ground; it does not mean the bird never descends. The habitat class is Forest. Density code 1 means dense vegetation, not a count of birds per area. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, not evidence of lifelong immobility or inability to fly. This account does not interpret the historical map fields, and these categories do not establish present occupancy, nest sites or daily activity height.

## What these records leave unresolved {#s-0357d21e76}
This account uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two name crosswalks, relevant Metadata definitions and local COL name records. Morphology means and compiled mass have separate sample bases; matching names or catalogue synonyms do not prove identical concepts across time. Specimens, the complete workbook, Dunning's book and a full methods-paper review were not examined. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation status remain outside these records.
