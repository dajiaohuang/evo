## Allophylus pauciflorus {#s-082ca10158}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes three-leaflet leaves with domatia, an androgynophore and a four-lobed unilateral nectar disc; dried pericarp colour is listed as blackish or reddish.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-0040426728}
Leaves are trifoliolate. Lateral leaflets are smaller than the central one and sessile; the central leaflet is petiolulate or sessile. Both central and lateral blades are described as symmetrical and chartaceous (papery), concolorous and not blackened when dry. Lateral margins are serrate from the middle toward the apex. The lower surface is glabrous to nearly glabrous; venation is mixed, with secondary veins straight and not prominent on the upper surface. Domatia are present. The inflorescence is unbranched. The androgynophore is present; the nectar disc is four-lobed and unilateral. Sepal undersides are glabrous to nearly glabrous. The pericarp is recorded as blackish or reddish when dry.

## Habitat field {#s-01a6197996}
The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality, survey date, global range or sampling-completeness information.
