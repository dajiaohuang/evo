## Narrow leaves and a few pale flowers {#s-94707b0366}
Ixia capillaris grows 20–45 cm tall and has linear leaves no more than 2 mm wide. Its main spike usually carries one to three pale-blue or occasionally creamy-yellow flowers; a 4–7 mm tube helps distinguish it from I. exiliflora, whose tube is 8–10 mm.

## Mainly clay slopes {#s-72b6c8ebdb}
The cited flora records mainly clay slopes as habitat.
