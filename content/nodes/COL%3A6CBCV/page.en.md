## What is recorded for Dalechampia burchellii? {#s-3f187ba325}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium. Its simple leaves are elliptic or lance-shaped, and it also has three-leaflet compound leaves. The flower cluster is surrounded by three-lobed bracts and has resin glands. Female flowers have deeply divided sepals; the style-column tip is crater-shaped. Fruit covering and seed traits were not observed in the source record.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-6fae1c280d}
The dataset lists it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
