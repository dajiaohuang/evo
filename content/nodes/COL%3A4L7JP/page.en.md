## Polygala fruticosa {#s-e552c213cd}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala fruticosa* as an accepted species. Its regional account describes a shrub to 2 m with opposite, nearly stalkless leaves, lance-shaped to ovate and heart-shaped at the base. Short racemes at branch tips bear purple flowers; the inner sepals are often green, and the two side petals are deeply divided into unequal lobes. The habitat field records rocky sandstone and clay slopes; this account is regional, not a full range survey.
