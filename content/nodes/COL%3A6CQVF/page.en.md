## Accepted name and placement {#s-8eb1d6a47b}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts *Diasporus vocator* (Taylor, 1955) as a species in Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family) and *Diasporus* (genus). This records the name, rank and placement used by the pinned catalogue.

## Separate records, separate scopes {#s-b5fb73caba}
This page brings together a current catalogue entry, an AmphiBIO v1 row using the Frost 2011 source concept, one morphology v3 record and a role in a mitochondrial 16S analysis of *Pristimantis*. The historical and deposited source concepts have not been matched to the current COL26.8 concept, so each record is described within its own scope.

## Land and vegetation foraging categories {#s-8c1dd2a029}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1809 lists terrestrial and arboreal foraging for its Frost 2011 *Diasporus vocator* record. These broad categories do not describe changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations, but this row has not been compared field by field. The categories remain historical deposited values; no replacement is inferred, and the revision notice does not show that this row is wrong.

## Arthropods in the compiled diet {#s-a978392d19}
For its Frost 2011 record, AmphiBIO lists arthropods as an adult food category. Arthropods are invertebrates with jointed limbs. The compilation draws such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observations or stomach contents; a listed category is neither a complete diet nor a measured share of food. Its equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept is unresolved.

## Activity recorded at night {#s-071d535247}
The AmphiBIO row classifies the Frost 2011 source concept as nocturnal. This is a broad time-of-day label, not a continuous record of behaviour, and it has not been verified as a current species-wide description.

## A literature-compiled adult maximum {#s-bc1c811517}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 20 mm for this source concept. For frogs in Anura, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length (SVL), from the snout tip to the vent. This is a literature-derived maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every individual; its Frost 2011 concept remains unmatched to the current catalogue record.

## Clutch counts and yearly reproduction {#s-3124f6a768}
The AmphiBIO row gives both the minimum and maximum number per clutch as 5 offspring or eggs, and the maximum number of reproductive events per year as 1. These are literature-compiled counts and extrema. The row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive setting for each value; its offspring-size fields may refer to young or eggs.

## A direct-development label {#s-6302432e0c}
AmphiBIO assigns direct development to its Frost 2011 source concept. The label does not by itself describe parental care, a breeding site or seasonal timing, and its match to the current COL26.8 species concept is unresolved.

## Used as an outgroup in a frog analysis {#s-a4741c7e98}
A *Pristimantis* phylogenetic study lists *Diasporus vocator* among ten Craugastoridae outgroup labels; those sequences came from previously available GenBank material. An outgroup helps orient the sampled tree. Its use here is not evidence that *D. vocator* is a direct ancestor of the ingroup, nor is it a new field study of this frog.

## What the single-marker analysis cannot show {#s-1de47c98f5}
The relevant analysis uses mitochondrial 16S with model-based maximum-likelihood and Bayesian inference. Its main text reports 760/219 as sampling totals and 742/215 as analyzed totals; these are retained as separate reported counts, not combined into one sample size. This marker alone does not establish nuclear ancestry, reproductive isolation or a molecular-clock date for *D. vocator*. The recorded source review covered the main XML, all 231 appendix donor-row cells, all 9 distance-table terminal labels and 67 selected uncorrected 16S percentage cells. Other matrix cells, raw alignments, GenBank records, museum vouchers, figure pixels and deposited datasets were not reviewed; this is not full-article or whole-species concept certification.
