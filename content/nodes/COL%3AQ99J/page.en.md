## Clustered short shoots and red-orange petals {#s-5e4347be08}
In a Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field, the cylindrical branches carry clusters of short shoots (brachyblasts). The nearly stalkless leaves are linear or narrowly oblong. The lower surface has branching hairs near or on veins described as winged, while the upper surface is smooth. The bell-shaped flower cup is ridged and hairless. Its calyx segments have smooth, untoothed edges and taper to points. Petals are mostly red to orange with yellow at the base. Ten stamens—the pollen-bearing parts—are nearly equal in size; their filaments bear glandular hairs. The tissue joining the anther sacs is neither prolonged nor hairy. The ovary has glandular hairs and five chambers. These are the source's recorded structural details; no habitat field accompanies them.
