## What leaf and flower features identify Croton ericoides? {#s-4206ae9f5e}
Young branches and both surfaces of the short-stalked oval-lanceolate to lance-shaped leaves have scale-like hairs. The leaves have inconspicuous lateral veins, entire margins, no nectar glands, and no leaf-like stipules at young branch tips. Terminal thyrses bear unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are nearly sessile; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate and unequal, the style is two-lobed, and petals are absent. The calyx parts meet edge to edge in the bud. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ffd3b9f881}
It is listed as terrestrial, without a specific site.
