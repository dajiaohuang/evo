## Accepted name and placement {#s-b6fcc4433c}
COL26.8 records Microkayla chacaltaya (De la Riva, Padial & Cortéz in De la Riva, 2007) at species rank in Microkayla (Holoadeninae, Strabomantidae), in the release dated 20 August 2026.
