## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-82560d2304}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis lapemoides as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-d29a37849b}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1050 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ef233b323b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d325f9cc7f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-db53ee02b7}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
