## A leaf that appears after flowering {#s-ad4ac28a47}
The regional description records N. lilacea as an upright terrestrial herb up to 80 mm tall. It has one heart-shaped leaf that appears after flowering; the erect flowering stalk bears one small white to faint-pink flower with cerise markings.

## A plantation habitat note {#s-599ee176c0}
The same account records N. lilacea in plantations of exotic trees. It gives no collection locality and does not describe the plant’s relationship to the planted trees.
