## *Bonyunia minor* {#s-7e0aed79e1}
## A small leaf and a long corolla tube

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes hairless branchlets and leaves up to 4 cm long, with obovate or nearly round blades and a tiny point at the tip. The calyx is hairless, and the corolla tube is at least three times the calyx length. The fruit is described as becoming less hairy (glabrescent), and the seeds have regular wings. These traits come from one regional morphology entry, not a survey of their frequency or variation.
