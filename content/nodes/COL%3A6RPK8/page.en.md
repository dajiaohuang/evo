## Pale flowers with a long inner-tepal cusp {#s-3279cca159}
Moraea rivulicola grows 300–600 mm tall and has one channelled leaf. Its pale cream, greenish or beige flowers have three-lobed inner tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) with a long, obliquely coiled central cusp; the account contrasts them with the smaller flowers of M. unguiculata.

## Granitic slopes near streams {#s-49b594e873}
