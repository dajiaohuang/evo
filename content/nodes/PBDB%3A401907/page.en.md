## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-d0f4c57293}
The PBDB path records Mimodactylidae as an unranked clade and links two species guides: Mimodactylus libanensis and Haopterus gracilis. This parent route organizes the records; it is not a complete catalogue or a phylogenetic test.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-84a2071e16}
The two selected guides cover two occurrence rows from two collections; the listed strata are Sannine Limestone and Yixian. Compare each record's own age bracket, locality, and identification. Two database rows do not measure the number of animals that lived in the past.

## Evidence limits {#s-9266d2843d}
These pages summarize selected PBDB records, not all members of the clade or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence rows and collections are not specimen counts, population estimates, or measures of sampling effort. Database age brackets do not directly date biological origin or extinction, and the parent path is distinct from phylogenetic hypotheses.
