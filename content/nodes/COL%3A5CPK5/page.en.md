## *Xyris globosa*: rounded heads with fringed bracts {#s-0858df82e8}
The flower heads are rounded to nearly spherical, with 10–25 bracts and a similar number of flowers. Their bract margins may be torn and ciliate, or edged with fine hairs. Leaves spiral around the stem, and the stalk is 20–50 cm long.

The source categorizes the species as terrestrial.
