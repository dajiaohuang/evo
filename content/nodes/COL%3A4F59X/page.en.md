## Peritassa hatschbachii {#s-bfaef5760c}
## A climbing plant with spherical fruit

Peritassa hatschbachii is recorded as a climber with opposite elliptic leaves; their margins range from entire to faintly crenulate or toothed. The panicle-like flower cluster has a prominent, smooth central axis and cylindrical branches. Its bell-shaped flowers have a wavy disc, oblong anthers that open obliquely, and an unlobed stigma. Each ovary chamber has two to four ovules. The fruit is spheroid; habitat is labeled terrestrial.
