## Eulophia meleagris {#s-f753b10817}
The South African account describes Eulophia meleagris with underground, bead-like rhizomes, thin leaves and a flowering stem 200–800 mm tall. Its loose inflorescence bears three to 30 flowers; purple-to-white petals and lip have dark bluish-purple margins or veins, and the lip crests form three to five wavy lamellae. It is recorded near upland-forest margins, often among dense herbs.
