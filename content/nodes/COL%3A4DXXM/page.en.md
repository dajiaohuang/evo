## ecology {#s-e9f4ecf062}
A long-term island population study examined how spring temperature and population size related to natal dispersal in house sparrows, comparing islands that differed in habitat quality. On lower-quality islands without farms, warmer springs and larger local populations were associated with higher natal dispersal. On higher-quality islands with farms, where food and shelter were available throughout the year, dispersal showed no clear relationship with those two factors. The findings indicate that these associations varied across the studied habitats, rather than showing one uniform climate or density effect across all sites.
