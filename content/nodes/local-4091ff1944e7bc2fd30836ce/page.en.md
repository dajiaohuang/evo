## The deposited caecilian record {#s-27addd6697}
The fourth version of CaecilianTraits lists Gegeneophis tejaswini in the genus Gegeneophis and family Grandisoniidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. Whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept has not been established.

## Measurement material {#s-f0be54e389}
The compilation lists eight measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of eight distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-74b707e922}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-c6ede3bbc5}
At species level, the compilation gives a primary-fold count of 127.625 and a secondary-fold count of 22. The decimal value may summarize records rather than describe one animal; these entries do not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic counts.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-b6eea1256e}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as absent. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults never use water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
