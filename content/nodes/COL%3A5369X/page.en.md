## Strumaria aestivalis {#s-06d486f88e}
Strumaria aestivalis has two or three recurved, strap-shaped leaves covered in long silky hairs, and white, widely funnel-shaped flowers with pale-pink midstripes. The regional account highlights broad nectar wells formed where inner filaments meet the style. It records slopes and ledges among shale chips as habitat; the anatomical comparison is part of that source’s interpretation.
