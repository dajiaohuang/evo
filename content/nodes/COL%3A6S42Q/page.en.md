## Three EltonTraits field groups {#s-31bd48f8f4}
Myomimus setzeri is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Gliridae. Its same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record provides three ecological field groups with source information. The archive follows earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals; matching names do not settle modern splits or synonym disputes.

## Relative weights across reported foods {#s-4c4e5f017e}
The nonzero weights are fruit 30%; seeds, nuts and spores 30%; mammals and birds 20%; invertebrates 10%; and unspecified vertebrates 10%. Code ABC combines high, reasonable and partial confidence; estimates may borrow from an ecologically similar congener, so they are not necessarily direct observations of this species. EltonTraits standardizes verbal descriptions rather than measures diet composition; zero does not prove a food is never eaten. Broad categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO do not imply exclusive foods.

## Ground-stratum category {#s-027940f899}
The mammalian assignment is G, the ground category, whose source definition includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Metadata attributes mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a category, not a time proportion or proof of exclusive ground use.

## Nighttime feeding is recorded {#s-cad22cad93}
The archive marks nighttime feeding activity as 1. Activity fields can be recorded together; this is not a proportion of a daily time budget or a fixed schedule across days, seasons and sites. The used groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum and activity may draw on different material; the same Ref number in bird and mammal tables does not identify the same source. Underlying books and papers have not been individually read; labels support traceability rather than add independent observations.

## Limits of the historical ecological record {#s-65929201f1}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete species monograph; matching names do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized relative weights from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite claims. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other evidence.
