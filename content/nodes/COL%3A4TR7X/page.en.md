## Slender branches and small white flowers {#s-3984dd7701}
This much-branched shrublet reaches 30 cm and may be decumbent or erect. Its slender leaves are fused below; the free parts are 6–12 mm long, sharply three-sided and recurved. One or two small white flowers are produced, and the fruit has five chambers. Clayey, gravelly soils among renosterveld are listed.
