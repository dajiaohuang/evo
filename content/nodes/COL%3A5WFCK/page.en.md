## Begonia maynensis {#s-028eed85e7}
## Glandular hairs and three recorded habitat types

Begonia maynensis has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its slightly asymmetric basal leaves are obovate or elliptic, with serrated margins; the upper surface is smooth and the underside may be smooth or bear faint glandular hairs. Glandular hairs are recorded on male flower segments, while the female indumentum was not observed. Bracteoles are absent, capsule wings are nearly equal, and the seed tip is rounded. Habitat labels include epiphytic, rocky and terrestrial settings, without a named locality.
