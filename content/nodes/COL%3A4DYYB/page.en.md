## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora suberosa? {#s-c0458d2e05}
The cylindrical stem is corky. Leaves may be entire or three-lobed, hairless, and have smooth margins; small eye-like marks (ocelli) may be present or absent. Stalked glands occur on the petiole, and stipules are narrow and bristle-like. Flowers are paired, with narrow bracts and sepals without awns. The floral cup is dish-shaped; the corona has two rows, and the operculum is fringed. A ring-shaped limen is present, and the reproductive stalk is at most 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless, with obovate, pitted seeds.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-fd205b0742}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
