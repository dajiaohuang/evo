## Pleurochaete luteola: three habitat labels {#s-e5b08e3a63}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records three habitat categories for this moss:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated),

“Rupícola” (rock-associated),

and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These are labels from three regional dataset rows; none gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
