## What changes in the small calyx from flower to fruit? {#s-27eda1fff4}
Scutellaria racemosa is described as a weak-stemmed perennial with rhizomes and stems 0.15–0.3 m long. Its narrow leaves have entire edges, often rolled inward; the corolla is 4.5–6 mm long and ranges from violet to red or white with purple spots. The calyx lengthens from 1.5–2 mm at flowering to about 3 mm in fruit. The source also records pale-brown, finely tuberculate nutlets, but gives no population sample.

## What wet settings are reported? {#s-e91a62ca50}
The habitat entry describes Scutellaria racemosa as naturalized along riverbanks and in moist places. It gives no collection coordinates or survey dates, so those settings are not a complete range.
