## Selaginella tenella {#s-169295bf68}
## Variable stem posture and two habitat labels

The record lists stems as upright, prostrate, partly upright or ascending, greenish or straw-coloured, smooth and without joints. Rhizophores are axillary or dorsal. Lateral leaves are oblong or ovate-oblong with rounded or slightly heart-shaped bases; dorsal leaves have several listed forms, oblique or wedge-shaped bases, and long awns. Idioblasts are recorded in both lateral and dorsal leaves. The source supplies two habitat labels—rock-dwelling and terrestrial—without reconciling them.
