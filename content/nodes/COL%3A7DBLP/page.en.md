## Comparative results retain their measurement limits {#s-43aa39e8a8}
T. taliangensis differed from several congeners, yet the discussion did not identify a separate morphospace supporting a separate subgenus. Its lateral cranial centroid size differed from T. uyenoi and T. yangi, without establishing a whole-body size ranking. Historical Liangshantriton proposals are discussed alongside phylogenetic concerns, not adopted as a new classification. Separation of species means must not be transferred to every individual.
