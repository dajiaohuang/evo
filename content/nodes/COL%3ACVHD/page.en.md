## One historical record, two genus names {#s-3e109f3413}
Pinned COL26.8 organizes this page as Ammospiza maritima (CVHD). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 6375 uses the same name, as does its one-to-one eBird 2021 crosswalk; the BirdTree crosswalk gives Ammodramus maritimus, labelled 1BL to 1BT. Pinned COL’s older-combination record CTL5 points to accepted ID CVHD. These associations support reading the historical row without verifying all taxonomic boundaries across dates.

## Beak, wing and tail in a small sample {#s-8ab673415b}
Morphology covers 9 measured birds (4 female, 5 male), of which 8 have complete measurements. Species averages are 11.1 mm from the nostril’s anterior edge to the beak tip, 22.9 mm tarsus, 60.6 mm unflattened wing and 54.8 mm tail. These are not individual size bounds. Hand-wing index is 14.8, defined through Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, rather than flight distance or speed. Dividing rounded species-average lengths cannot replace the reported index.

## Mean body mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-d252409b81}
Body mass is 22.16 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning, defined as a species average incorporating female and male mass. It is not field weighing of the 9 morphology birds or an annual or geographic minimum–maximum range. Inference=NO means the biometric row was not inferred from a relative; mass still comes from published material, and Dunning’s original book and individual denominator were not read here.

## Compiled habitat and food categories {#s-baa3c4b26a}
The row records Wetland habitat, an Invertivore niche and Carnivore trophic level. Terrestrial lifestyle means predominantly obtaining food while walking or hopping on the ground, including wading with the body above water. Habitat-density code 3 means open habitats, not three birds per square metre. These compiled categories provide neither seasonal diet percentages nor independent stomach-content observations.

## What the migration code means {#s-79a19c6553}
Migration=2 is partially migratory: the definition can include a minority moving long distances or widespread short-distance, nomadic or distinct altitudinal movements. The code alone does not identify which pattern applies, route length or residence in each population. Locality, timing and individual tracking are needed to distinguish them.

## Evidence limits {#s-b79ceb8633}
This page interprets historical species averages and coarse ecology in AVONET v7. Selected rows, field definitions and name crosswalks were read; individual raw measurements, Dunning’s original book, independent field behaviour were not. Morphology counts are not mass-literature sample sizes, and Inference=NO does not establish concept identity with COL26.8. Plumage identification, breeding, lifespan, current range and conservation status remain unsupplied; this is not a complete monograph.
