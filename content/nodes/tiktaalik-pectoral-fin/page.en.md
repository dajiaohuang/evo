## Tiktaalik's wrist-like joints inside a fin {#s-30657c0b22}
In *Tiktaalik roseae*, the pectoral fin's internal skeleton includes a humerus, radius and ulna, wrist-like joints and small distal radials, yet the fin still carries fin rays (lepidotrichia). These structures are directly observed in multiple specimens. Researchers infer from the joint geometry that the fin could transmit load, but that is a functional interpretation: no free digits or walking trace are preserved, so the fossil does not show that *Tiktaalik* walked on land.
