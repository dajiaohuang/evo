## Leaf and floral bud characters in the description {#s-ac829615b9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records deciduous stipules and entire leaf blades on flowering or fruiting branches. Young floral buds have an unwinged calyx tube and are oblong to ovoid. At anthesis, the calyx is campanulate with denticulate lobes. The upper petal is narrowly oblong, the outer petal surface is silky, anthers are ovate and the ovary is sessile. The fruit is dehiscent. No habitat field is included, so the description does not establish habitat or geographic range.
