## One historical AVONET record {#s-8249a9adb1}
COL26.8 accepts Zosterops sanctaecrucis in Zosteropidae. AVONET v7 contains a historical record under the same name and family. The name and its one-to-one eBird and BirdTree crosswalks link the records; they do not settle species concepts after later splits or lumps.

## Canopy habitat and perching lifestyle {#s-23d8e12375}
The AVONET entry classifies habitat as tall-tree vegetation with a relatively closed canopy. Its trophic-niche field indicates resources from multiple feeding niches. The primary-lifestyle category is insessorial: much time perched above ground on vegetation, rocks or other substrates. These broad categories do not give food proportions or local behavior, and do not imply the same resource use in every season, region or individual.

## Migration category {#s-f347bbbbfc}
The recorded migration category is sedentary. Regional seasonal movements are not listed separately, so this entry does not provide routes, schedules or distances for each population.

## Latitude in historical maps {#s-b865b31908}
Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -10.89° to -10.67°. The source worksheet links these fields to BirdLife/HBW 2019.1 maps accessed on 27 March 2020. Endpoints summarize a mapped range; they are not a locality list or evidence of occupancy throughout it, and omit a complete account of non-breeding movements. They do not establish current distribution or conservation status.

## What the ecology record does not measure {#s-5ae3ac81d3}
The AVONET record contains all four ecology categories: habitat, trophic niche, primary lifestyle and migration. Its biometric fields are not flagged as inferred, but mass is sourced from an EltonTraits mass–length model. This account withholds interpretation of the dimensions and mass; missing fields do not show that the species lacks a trait.
