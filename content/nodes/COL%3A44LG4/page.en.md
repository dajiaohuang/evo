## Hairy foliage and short horned capsules {#s-ea654ac395}
The source description records a softly hairy, spreading shrublet to 20 cm tall. Leaves are loosely fascicled, linear lanceolate, hairy and mucronate. Flowers are axillary, pale pink and shortly pedicellate. Capsules are hispid and shortly horned. The ecology field gives rocky slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
