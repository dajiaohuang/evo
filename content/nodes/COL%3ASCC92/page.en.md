## Bromeliads and low vegetation in Loja records {#s-9e690e2f01}
The revision reported surroundings of Celica, Guachanamá and La Tolera in western-Andean Loja, at 2101–2833 m. Most nighttime specimens occurred in terrestrial or arboreal bromeliads up to 3 m high within native-vegetation patches, or on low vegetation up to 60 cm high. Daytime encounters were only inside bromeliads. These sample-specific observations do not establish obligatory bromeliad breeding or an exclusive daily activity pattern.

## A February calling record {#s-d4a4c726eb}
Calling males were found in February on low vegetation about 30 cm above ground. This documents recorded calling sites and timing, without locating eggs, specifying development mode or establishing the entire annual reproductive season.
