## Accepted name in COL26.8 {#s-7f78b054ce}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists Leptopelis boulengeri (Werner, 1898) as an accepted species in Anura, Arthroleptidae and Leptopelis. This is the name and placement recorded in that release. Other checklists or fossil databases may use different names or species concepts and need to be checked separately.

The 2017 study reports one Republic of Congo specimen, USNM 584082, examined molecularly. LMR is the Loubomo–Mougagara Road corridor in Nyanga, Gabon; the Republic of Congo is separate from both that corridor and the Democratic Republic of Congo. A museum reference specimen is not automatically an LMR observation. Survey non-detections are limited by where and when researchers looked; they do not establish regional absence or the full range. ## Mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-690e379569}
The study combined COI BIN/ABGD clustering, comparison with 16S references, and morphology and type-locality context. COI and 16S are both mitochondrial markers, so they are not independent nuclear confirmation and do not test reproductive isolation. The authors used neighbor joining to group similar sequences and explicitly limited its use for inferring evolutionary history. Most species had material from only one or two localities, and not every GenBank voucher could be checked morphologically. Reference labels and missing sequences remain limitations. Fieldwork was in May–June 2012 and March–April 2013; GenBank/BOLD comparisons used a 2016 reference-library snapshot, not a current database audit.

## Study totals are not counts for this species {#s-901532446f}
Across its multispecies reference library, the study reports 540 sequenced specimens, 524 COI sequences and 528 16S sequences, assigned to 94 BINs and 85 ABGD groups. These totals cover all taxa, not Leptopelis boulengeri. The methods list 80 LMR specimens from the first season and 97 from the second, while Table 1 lists 79 and 97 sequenced or retained entries. The methods also report 275 Republic of Congo reference specimens, but Table 1 locality rows sum to 274. The source gives no reason for these collection-versus-analysis differences, so they remain unresolved. The group totals are not a verified census of currently accepted species. Raw traces, BOLD assignments and GenBank accessions were not reanalysed here.
