## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0ed741eb74}
COL26.8 records Advenus montisilvestris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Diploglossidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-80e35fb4cc}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bars-Closel et al. 2017.
