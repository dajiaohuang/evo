## What did researchers detect in the Hula Valley? {#s-9dd3888d7b}
The surveys described Latonia nigriventer as localized and difficult to detect. They found the fungus Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis in the region and on sampled frogs, but reported no signs of chytridiomycosis in this species. Detection in the study samples is not a diagnosis of disease or a current population-wide finding.
