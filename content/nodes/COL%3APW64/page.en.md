## An EltonTraits entry under a historical name {#s-c850c7aae0}
COL26.8 accepts Callosciurus phayrei in Sciuridae. EltonTraits 1.0 contains a same-name, same-family record with three ecological field groups. Its taxonomic framework follows older bird or MSW3 mammal classifications, so the name match alone cannot settle later splits or synonymy.

## Standardized food weights {#s-66941cc16b}
Reported nonzero weights are other plant material, including fungi in the source category (30%); invertebrates (20%); fruit (20%); seeds, nuts and spores (20%); and unspecified vertebrates (10%). ABC combines high, reasonable and partial confidence and can include estimates borrowed from an ecologically similar congener. The weights standardize literature descriptions; they are not measured diet proportions, and a zero is not proof of never eating a category. Source labels such as Nect, Seed and PlantO have broader definitions than their everyday wording.

## One assigned foraging stratum {#s-d33fa62da8}
The record assigns Ar, arboreal, with confidence code A. Metadata groups these mammalian stratum assignments under Nowak (1999). This is a categorical assignment rather than time spent in each stratum, and it does not mean the animal forages only in trees.

## Limits of the historical foraging account {#s-0aeb975821}
This is a guide to a same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 entry, not a full species account. The historical species concept is not proven equivalent to COL26.8. Food weights are coarse standardizations of literature descriptions, not direct measures of intake, stomach contents, energy or observation time. ABC diet and activity codes may include congeneric evidence, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Underlying publications have not been reread; this entry does not establish individual or seasonal variation, current distribution, population size or conservation status.
