## Accepted name and placement {#s-8546576a0c}
COL26.8 records Craugastor emcelae (Lynch, 1985) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; they do not establish equivalence with historical source concepts.
