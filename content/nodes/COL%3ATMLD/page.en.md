## Chaptalia hermogenis {#s-2b54c706e2}
## Heart-shaped leaves and beaked, ribbed fruits

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes heart-shaped leaves with toothed edges and a flat surface. Their veins form loops near the margin. The flowering stalk has no bracts. Outer disc florets are present in the heads. The fruits are beaked, papillose and ribbed, with a cream-coloured pappus.
