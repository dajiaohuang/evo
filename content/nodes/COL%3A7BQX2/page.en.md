## Tetracera forzzae Fraga & Aymard {#s-5e73c3ba0d}
## Elliptic leaves with 14–18 lateral veins

Tetracera forzzae has alternate, simple, papery leaves with grooved petioles. The elliptic blade has a blunt or tiny-pointed tip and a base ranging from blunt to wedge-shaped; its margin may be entire or finely toothed. Fourteen to eighteen lateral veins reach the margin. Branched flower clusters (thyrses) arise laterally. Flowers have seven sepals, five petals and one carpel. Each follicle bears one seed with a fringed aril.
