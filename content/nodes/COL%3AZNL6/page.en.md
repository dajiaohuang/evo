## ## Velvety branches and yellow flowers {#s-2da9ec1154}
Brazilian Flora 2020 describes a plant up to 1.5 m tall, with erect branches that are silky to velvety and lack wings between the nodes. The leaves have three elliptic leaflets on long petioles and persistent, linear to threadlike or lanceolate stipules. Loose racemes opposite the leaves bear many flowers, with persistent linear bracts. The yellow flowers have a nearly even or bell-shaped calyx. The oblong pods are brown and velvety to silky; the seeds are ochre-brown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
