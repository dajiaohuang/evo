## Gerbilliscus (Taterona) nigricaudus: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Gerbilliscus (Taterona) nigricaudus* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority (Peters, 1878).
