## Leaflet surfaces and calyx texture in the entry {#s-f70a104bcb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records alternate leaves with glabrous leaflets that are oval lanceolate. The adaxial surface of the leaflet venation is inconspicuous, while the abaxial surface is prominent; leaflet tips are acute or acuminate. The calyx is described as membranaceous. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish character variation or a complete range.
