## *Baccharis orbiculata*: round, entire-margined leaves {#s-68962d5361}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records nearly stalkless, round leaves with pinnate veins and smooth margins. A single flower head sits in each leaf axil. The field describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered, which are alternative entries rather than one combined state. Rock-associated and terrestrial substrate labels are listed without a collection locality.
