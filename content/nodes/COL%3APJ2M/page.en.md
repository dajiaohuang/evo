## Calea papposa {#s-de4ef8dfbb}
## Silky fruits beneath yellow disc florets

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes an erect plant with a simple taproot and pubescent stems. Its opposite ovate leaves are sessile. Umbel-like clusters bear discoid heads with bell-shaped involucres of two distinct bract forms; the receptacle carries linear paleae.

Ray florets are absent, and the disc florets are yellow. The dry fruits are silky-haired; the pappus consists of separate, lance-shaped scales. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
