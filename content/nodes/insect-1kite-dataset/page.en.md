## A broad molecular sample {#s-fb9bf86bee}
The 1KITE entry is a research dataset made from living insect samples, not an insect lineage or fossil timeline. Its published matrix includes 144 species and 1,478 protein-coding genes. The 0–0 marker denotes the present-day dataset; estimated node ages and fossil calibrations have separate dates.

The dataset draws on sampled insect transcriptomes associated with BioProject PRJNA183205. Its matrix combines 144 species and 1,478 protein-coding genes for phylogenomic analysis. Those numbers describe the study’s sample and data matrix, not every insect species or gene.

## Dataset date versus evolutionary dates {#s-04b65057ab}
The present-day 0–0 display identifies when the sampled organisms are living; it does not express the age of Insecta. Fossil calibrations and modeled divergences remain distinct evidence and should be read from their own records.
