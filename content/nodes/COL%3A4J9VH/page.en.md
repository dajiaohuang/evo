## Floating leaves and flower bracts {#s-7b012db646}
The regional account describes an aquatic, floating, stoloniferous (producing runners) herb without a stem, with a tuft of fibrous roots. Its leaves sit directly on the plant (sessile) in a rosette and are cuneate-obovate, with fan-shaped veins raised on the lower surface. Green spathes—bracts enclosing the flower cluster—arise in leaf axils on very short peduncles.
