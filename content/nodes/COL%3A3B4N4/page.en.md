## Eriogonum elegans {#s-c6873541e9}
## Small basal leaves with softly hairy petioles

The Flora of North America account describes basal leaves with petioles 0.3–3 cm long and thinly covered in woolly hairs. The leaf blades are oblong, rounded or nearly heart-shaped, measuring 0.3–1.5 cm in each dimension. This excerpt gives a focused leaf description only; it supplies no habitat or locality.
