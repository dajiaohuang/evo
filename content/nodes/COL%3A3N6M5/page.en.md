## Hydrocotyle langsdorffii {#s-f9916a7a33}
## Peltate leaves and diamond-section fruit halves

The stems are glabrous. Leaves attach by their petioles near the centre of the blade, a peltate form; the round or palm-shaped blades have crenate margins and five to seven primary veins. The source allows the petiole and both blade surfaces to be glabrous or pubescent. Umbel-like flower clusters are borne on stalks that are likewise glabrous or pubescent, and the flowers are pedicellate. The fruit halves have a rhombic cross-section and a hairless surface.
