## Encounter habitats with vegetation remaining {#s-79835875af}
The 2016 authors reported encounters in agricultural areas next to second growth, tree nurseries, lowland riparian forest and mountain sites up to 1500 m. Their account described naturally low abundance where some vegetation persisted. This qualitative statement is not a standardized density estimate, and the upper surveyed elevation does not define a physiological limit or demonstrate that every agricultural setting supports reproduction.

## Reported island limits remain dated records {#s-97e7c8a2e4}
The account cited occurrence across Mindanao and Basilan and no occurrence on Dinagat, Siargao, Leyte, Samar or Bohol. The authors assigned Least Concern using the criteria available to them. These statements retain the 2016 paper’s range interpretation and assessment proposal; unrecorded islands are not proven permanently unoccupied, and the proposal does not substitute for an independently verified current listing.
