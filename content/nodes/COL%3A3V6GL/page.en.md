## Which record these facts describe {#s-017e61861b}
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts Liolaemus hermannunezi in the family Liolaemidae. The traits below come from its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which follows the Reptile Database's 2023 name framework and cites sources by trait. That row describes the compilation, not every population as if it were identical.

## What the length number measures {#s-06779001b4}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 71.4 mm. SVL measures from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. This compiled maximum is neither a typical size nor a biological ceiling. No sex-specific maximum is interpreted here, so the record cannot show how lengths differ between sexes.

## What the clutch figure means {#s-5af517de70}
The source labels the reproductive mode oviparous, or egg-laying, and gives a consolidated mean count of 5 in its litter-or-clutch field. ReptTraits may calculate a summary from the midpoint of published means, or use the midpoint of reported extremes when means are missing; the value is not necessarily an arithmetic average of directly counted clutches. This row does not provide low and high clutch values. The labels do not describe how egg counts vary among populations or establish that all populations share one pattern.

## Limits of this account {#s-a0905e943b}
This page paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and keeps its 2023 name framework and trait-specific citations. A single row compresses differences in sex, age, locality, and population; the same name and family do not prove that a historical record matches every later taxonomic concept. The reported maximum is not an absolute size limit, and a compiled mean is not a fixed clutch count. ReptTraits also includes mass estimates derived from length equations; they are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions, so they are not used as conclusions. This page does not update conservation status, venom, current distribution, or population trends. The citation labels retain the dataset's source chain; they do not mean this project independently rechecked every original study.
