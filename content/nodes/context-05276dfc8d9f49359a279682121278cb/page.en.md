## The source concept {#s-d303a76b70}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Oophaga lehmanni as source row Anf1528, under genus Oophaga, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.
