## Two substrate categories in a regional record {#s-139c05a4b1}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Calyptothecium duplicatum as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its two entries are habitat or substrate categories.

One entry is “Corticícola,” the category for a bark-associated substrate.

The other is “Epixila,” recorded as a wood-associated substrate. The source gives no collection locality, survey date or complete range.

The first field does not supply a locality, date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

The second field likewise does not supply a locality, date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

These two source categories are the only biological content included for the species in this regional record.
