## Food and foraging-height estimates {#s-51b7f55bad}
COL26.8 lists *Ardeola speciosa* in Ardeidae. EltonTraits 1.0 contains an older same-name, same-family record for comparison. Its taxonomy predates the current catalogue, so a matching name does not resolve later species splits or synonymy.

## Foods in the compiled diet {#s-d49d40074c}
The EltonTraits record assigns relative weights of 40% to invertebrates, 30% to reptiles and amphibians, and 30% to fish. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions; they are not measurements of food intake, and an unlisted category would not prove the bird never eats it.

## Feeding at the water and on the ground {#s-624a922e09}
The archive marks this estimate as species-level: 80% of the recorded feeding position is on or just below the water surface, and 20% is on the ground. The weights describe relative use of positions in the source account, not nest sites, all habitat or a full-day time budget. They are not elevation bands and cannot establish flight height or habitat area.

## Source behind the food and height estimates {#s-f6f4f1633c}
Both estimates are linked in the archive to volume 1 of *Handbook of the Birds of the World*. The book has not been independently checked for this account. The percentages remain EltonTraits’ standardized summaries of literature descriptions rather than new observations.

## Limits on the EltonTraits estimates {#s-042b3bd979}
Only the food and foraging-position groups are interpreted here; they do not provide a complete ecology account. EltonTraits converts written descriptions into relative weights, not direct measurements of intake or time spent feeding. Its older same-name record does not prove identity with the current COL26.8 concept, and the underlying literature was not individually reread for this page.

## Freshwater habitat and feeding mode {#s-a2dcbe5c8a}
AVONET categorizes the habitat as freshwater wetlands, including lakes, marshes or reedbeds. It classifies the main feeding niche as aquatic animals and the primary lifestyle as spending much time on the ground, walking, hopping or wading while feeding. These are broad categories, not a list of foods or local habitat observations. The feeding-niche scheme uses a threshold of at least 60% of food resources for a dominant category.

## What partial migration means here {#s-03a0cfd8c8}
The source classifies *A. speciosa* as partially migratory. In its definition, that label can cover long-distance travel by a minority, shorter movements by most populations, nomadic movement, or distinct altitudinal migration. This record does not identify which pattern applies in each population or give current routes, timing or distances.
