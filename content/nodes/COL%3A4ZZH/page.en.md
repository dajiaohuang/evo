## The captured thermal evidence {#s-d58bbd3dd0}
In the archived AmphiTherm dataset, the source classification associates the name Hylarana with 3 deposited species concepts and 4 estimate rows. The recorded metric is CTmax, with 4 rows across 3 source concepts. CTmax and CTmin are the upper and lower critical thermal limits; LT50_hot and LT50_cold are temperatures associated with 50% mortality in hot and cold assays; Tpref is preferred temperature. These metrics come from different experiments and cannot be combined into a single tolerance range. Their source concepts overlap, so counts cannot be added as distinct species or animals. The archived life-stage codes are larvae and adults. This dated dataset describes study coverage, not the physiology of every current member; its 2018 classification is not the same as the current group boundary.

## Reference evidence does not substitute for type or nuclear data {#s-49e278ccba}
The selected species accounts combine COI (mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase subunit I) Barcode Index Number (BIN) and Automatic Barcode Gap Discovery (ABGD) groupings with 16S mitochondrial ribosomal RNA references, morphology and type-locality evidence. COI and 16S are mitochondrial markers, so the groups are not independent nuclear confirmation and do not test reproductive isolation. Sparse localities and vouchers that were not morphologically examined limit inference. These working identifications retain the study's stated alternatives.
