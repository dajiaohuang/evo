## Accepted name and placement {#s-112726bba0}
COL26.8 records Diasporus hylaeformis (Cope, 1875) at species rank in Diasporus, within Eleutherodactylinae, order Eleutherodactylidae. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026, separate from the historical source classification.
