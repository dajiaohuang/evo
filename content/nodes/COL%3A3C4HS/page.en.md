## Rolled leaf edges and a globose or ellipsoid fruit {#s-2e224aefe2}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves with acute bases and tips. The leaves are papery; their margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles. Their supporting bracts and the smaller bracteoles beside flowers persist; the bracteoles are inconspicuous. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs. Its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is listed as globose or ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, and its embryo has fused cotyledons (the seed leaves).

## A terrestrial label, not a distribution record {#s-7145ba0e9b}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. It is a habitat category rather than a range description.
