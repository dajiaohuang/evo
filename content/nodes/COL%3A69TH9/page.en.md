## *Chionolaena capitata*: narrow leaves and umbel-shaped heads {#s-644d20897e}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records linear leaves more than seven times as long as wide. Fewer than 30 florets occur in heterogamous heads arranged in umbels; the field records the male florets as more numerous. The fruitlets bear hairs with blunt tips. The broad substrate category is “terrestrial,” with no collection locality.
