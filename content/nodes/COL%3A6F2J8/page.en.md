## How are its stems arranged along the rhizome? {#s-df70b7c465}
The 2012 regional account describes *Elegia deusta* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial. Its simple culms, or upright stems, reach 70 cm and cluster along a long rhizome, a horizontal underground stem. The leaf sheaths persist, with one noted per culm. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## Which slopes does the account list? {#s-3911d594c5}
The regional flora account lists mainly south-facing slopes. It gives no survey date or complete distribution.
