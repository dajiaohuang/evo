## Bobosaurus: an older plesiosaur-line candidate? {#s-0a3810a27b}
The articulated holotype MFSN 27285 comes from the lower Carnian Rio del Lago Formation and combines nothosaurian and plesiosaurian features. Its original study considered alternative placements, including a position nearer Plesiosauria, so it is an older but uncertain candidate, not evidence extending diagnostic Plesiosauria; it is compared with the more securely identified Rhaeticosaurus.
