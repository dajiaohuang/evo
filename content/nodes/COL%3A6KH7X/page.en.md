## A crowded spike of scarlet flowers {#s-7f5d2ff2d3}
Gladiolus nerineoides is 30–40 cm tall and has reduced foliage. Its crowded spike bears scarlet to reddish-orange, narrowly funnel-shaped flowers; the tube is 25–31 mm long and the tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 19–22 mm.

## Mountain sandstone slopes and cliffs {#s-b4e9c9bc79}
The regional account records rocky sandstone slopes and cliffs at 500–1,500 m.
