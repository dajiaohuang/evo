## Poiretia bahiana {#s-4d3727c575}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Poiretia bahiana* as an accepted species of *Poiretia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes an upright stem, four-leaflet leaves, and branched flower clusters. The flowers are stalked on short pedicels, and the fruit is a lomento with few segments.
