## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-3ba85022a4}
The diagnosis compares colomai and chancas as having pale dorsal flecks/dots on a tan, green or brown background and a white iris with a dark horizontal stripe. The focal new species instead has an unmarked green dorsum and finely reticulated tan iris. These traits concern the authors’ comparison, not an independently authenticated complete diagnosis of all colomai populations.

## The sampled relationship retains support and label limits {#s-fdb2102c4a}
The new tree places chancas in a clade with balionotus, colomai, grandisonae, manduriacu and mariae, rather than as sister to the rest of Nymphargus as in an earlier analysis. The paper associates such disagreements with weakly supported nodes. Membership in this reported clade does not settle the exact branching order or supply a new species-specific divergence time.
