## Soft-haired leaves and tiny pink-purple flowers {#s-48646ae276}
This description records a low, weakly branched plant with soft, spreading hairs. Its nearly upright leaves cluster at branch tips and overlap; the tips may be blunt or pointed. One to three sessile or nearly sessile flowers are only 6–8 mm across, with rose-purple petals and white staminodes that extend beyond the stamens. The source notes pointed anthers as a distinguishing feature. It grows on shale cliffs inland or near the coast.
