## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f3ca418366}
COL26.8 records Amplorhinus multimaculatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pseudoxyrhophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-bd9818571b}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 630 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a3f13536a3}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1e62bdf066}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-868cad217d}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
