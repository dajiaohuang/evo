## *Biophytum dormiens* {#s-37ee371db3}
The field records unbranched stems and a leaf rachis up to 10 cm long, with 10–18 opposite pairs of narrowly oblong leaflets. Bracts are narrow-triangular and no more than 1 mm long; the peduncle is short, while the flower stalks are elongated. Veins on the calyx lobes are conspicuous. These are source-field descriptors.
