## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b6eb63b6bd}
COL26.8 records Cyrtodactylus wetariensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-5a029e5b25}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 70 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-8e8fc0979e}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
