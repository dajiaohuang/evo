## Yunotubus anchors the older graptolite sample {#s-eb30e997f4}
The 516–0 Ma Graptolithina route begins with diagnostic *Yunotubus* material from the approximately 516 Ma Hongjingshao Formation and continues to living rhabdopleurids. Five studied specimens preserve tubes, zooids (individual feeding units) and a connecting stolon, the tissue that links them; the specimens were included in an analysis of sampled pterobranchs. This is a sample envelope, not a graptolite origin date.
