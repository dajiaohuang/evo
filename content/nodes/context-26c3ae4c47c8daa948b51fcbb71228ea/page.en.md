## Anf1491 and the recorded concept {#s-88aa58f9c9}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1491 is the entry for Colostethus fugax, placed by the source in Colostethus (Dendrobatidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-f144d00b04}
For Colostethus fugax, row Anf1491 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-d29808b2d8}
Its maximum adult body length is 20.1 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-8f912c2016}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
