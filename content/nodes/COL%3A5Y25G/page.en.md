## Two leaflet pairs and a twisting pod {#s-7692efd44f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records two pairs of elliptic-to-obovate leaflets and bulbous glandular hairs. Its yellow flowers have 10 stamens; the mature pod valves twist, and seeds are described as depressed obovoid. The xylopodium, a woody root swelling, is marked unknown in the source and is not inferred here. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. This regional entry gives no sampling frame, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status.
