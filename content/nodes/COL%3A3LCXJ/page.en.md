## What leaf, flower and style traits are recorded for Heteropterys capixaba? {#s-bf738dcc75}
The leafy branches and opposite leaves are woolly-haired. The strongly two-colored, leathery blades are oval or lance-shaped, heart-shaped at the base and tipped by a small point; their lower surface has rusty woolly hairs. The surface is blistered and the margins roll under. The inflorescence hangs downward; its oval bracts lack glands, and the pedicels are stalkless. Sepals are hairless and lack glands, with flat tips. Yellow clawed petals have entire margins and a keel. The anthers are hairless. Styles are equal in length, curved and hairless, ending bluntly. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a1163c7245}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
