## Living monoplacophoran trees conflict across matrices {#s-683b1833a7}
The cited phylogenomic analysis of living taxa recovers Monoplacophora with Cephalopoda in its principal analyses, but other matrices conflict with this result. It is a study-specific topology, not universal consensus or a fossil range.
