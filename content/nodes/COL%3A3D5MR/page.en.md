## Long-stalked yellow heads on a low shrublet {#s-5ecabea219}
This account describes a spreading shrublet up to 20 cm tall, with linear leaves that are sometimes divided into one or two lobes. Yellow radiate flower heads stand singly on long terminal stalks. Although its overall form can resemble some members of another Euryops group, the source places it in section Euryops based on features of the involucre, disk florets and fruits. It grows on steep, moist sandstone slopes above 1,200 m.
