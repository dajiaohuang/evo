## Source classification and concept status {#s-9bbd4c285a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2703 records Scinax tripui under the source classification Scinax (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.
