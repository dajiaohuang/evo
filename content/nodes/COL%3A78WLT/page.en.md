## Pointed corms and membranous bracts {#s-cb03ea3608}
Romulea membranacea grows to 12 cm and has a corm (a swollen underground storage stem) pointed at the base. Its bright-yellow flowers have darker throat veins, and both inner and outer bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) are largely membranous.
