## Overview {#s-e198e5fccf}
At the Lemos Maia study area in southeastern Bahia, Heiduck followed a group of four titi monkeys for one year. Its 22-ha home range comprised 58% undisturbed forest, 31% selectively logged forest and 11% regrowth after clear-cutting. The paper used the subspecies combination *Callicebus personatus melanochir*; this page follows the COL26.8 accepted species name *C. melanochir*.

## Research observations {#s-f40f175a94}
Use of the forest types was not simply proportional to their area within the home range. Undisturbed forest held the most potential food per unit area and regrowth the least; the observed use aligned with food availability, suggesting that the quality of disturbed forest matters. The result is limited to one group and this forest.
