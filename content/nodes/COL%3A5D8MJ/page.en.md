## Linking the catalogue name to an older record {#s-a187c7bc6a}
COL26.8 lists *Zosterops gibbsi* in Zosteropidae. AVONET v7 contains a same-name record under the older HBW–BirdLife taxonomy, with matching family and crosswalk entries. These links connect records for comparison; they do not show that later taxonomic splits or lumps left the species concepts unchanged.

## Habitat, feeding and activity position {#s-4eae5e8100}
AVONET classifies the main feeding niche as terrestrial invertebrates and the primary lifestyle as perching above ground on vegetation, rocks or other supports. Its dominant-niche categories use a threshold of at least 60% of food resources. They are broad literature-derived labels, not a new measurement of this species’ diet or evidence that it feeds only on invertebrates. The source also has a habitat category, which this account does not interpret.

## The scope of the migration category {#s-1068320b76}
The migration field is classified as sedentary. The record does not separately describe regional or seasonal movements, so this label alone cannot specify routes, timing or distances for every population.

## Latitude in historical breeding and resident maps {#s-1d557420fa}
The historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints from −11.73° to −11.59°. The source worksheet attributes these values to BirdLife/HBW 2019.1 maps accessed on 27 March 2020. They summarize mapped range limits, not a list of occupied localities or proof of continuous occupancy; they also do not describe the full non-breeding movements or establish the species’ current range.

## What still needs other sources {#s-450f21c7cb}
Three of the four ecological categories in this record are interpreted here; the habitat category remains undescribed. The AVONET source does not flag biometric inference, but its mass value comes from an EltonTraits genus average. Because neither those biometric values nor their species-level basis is assessed in this account, no body measurements or mass are presented as observations of *Z. gibbsi*. Reproduction, growth and conservation status also require other sources; their omission is not evidence that the species lacks those traits.

## Evidence limits {#s-efefe6417f}
This is a limited account of historical ecology and mapped geography. AVONET’s BirdLife table uses HBW–BirdLife 5.0 names from December 2020. The matching name, family and one-to-one BirdLife, eBird and BirdTree crosswalks support an association with the current COL26.8 entry, not proof that their species concepts are identical. The ecological categories summarize literature, whose underlying species studies were not individually reread for this account. Historical breeding and resident maps do not assess present-day occupancy, population size or conservation status. Morphology and mass are left uninterpreted because this account cannot establish their species-specific basis; the recorded mass, in particular, is a genus average.
