## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-07348e4548}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Pleurodema cordobae Valetti, Salas & Martino, 2009 as a species in the genus Pleurodema, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.
