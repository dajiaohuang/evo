## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-07e80d24b0}
COL26.8 records Homalophis doriae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Homalopsidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-c35191edc4}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 797 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-1aaa004e0c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1861c99e55}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
