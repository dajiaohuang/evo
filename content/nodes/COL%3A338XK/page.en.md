## Cynanchum mossambicense {#s-f6c358b26b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Cynanchum mossambicense* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a twining perennial herb with heart-shaped leaf bases and white flowers. Its corolla lobes are 4–6 mm long. The habitat field places it in thickets near rivers or dams. This is a source-specific regional account, not evidence of a complete range or population pattern.
