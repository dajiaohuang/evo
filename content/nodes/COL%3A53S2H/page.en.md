## Symplocos pustulosa Aranha {#s-2c9face4ac}
## Pustule-like hairs and persistent leaf glands

Symplocos pustulosa is dioecious; its branches range from becoming smooth to hairy and bear pustule-like hairs. The leathery leaves are elliptic, obovate or spatulate, with smooth edges or teeth near the tip; glands persist at the tooth tips. The upper surface is smooth and the underside may be smooth or hairy. Inflorescences may be cymes, racemes or panicles, with deciduous bracts. Unisexual flowers have three or four corolla lobes, and the filaments are smooth. The ellipsoid or ovoid drupe retains a calyx that may sit below the disk or extend beyond it.
