## Hoodia gordonii {#s-f3abf265d2}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Hoodia gordonii* as an accepted species. A regional account describes a spiny, cactus-like, leafless succulent to 1 m, with stems bearing 11–17 angles and clear sap. Its flesh-coloured flowers are 40–100 mm across and somewhat foul-smelling. The habitat record gives dry stony slopes and flats. These are regional source statements, not a complete range assessment.
