## Baccharis suberectifolia {#s-5be5e317f6}
## A woody underground base and racemose heads

The Brazilian Flora fields record a woody underground base (xylopodium) present. Stems are unwinged and either smooth or striated. The leaves are sessile, linear, one-veined and entire. Flower heads form racemes; their florets are recorded as either all tubular and male or all threadlike and female. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
