## Zachełmie’s Eifelian footprints and the tetrapod question {#s-8461fd037e}
Bedding surfaces at Zachełmie preserve quadrupedal trackways and isolated prints with digit impressions from the Eifelian, a Middle Devonian interval. They indicate an unidentified limbed tetrapod trackmaker, but traces do not name its body taxon or make it a direct ancestor. The displayed 393.47–387.95 Ma span is the atlas’s stage window, not an error range for one footprint.
