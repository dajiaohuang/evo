## Accepted name and placement {#s-89f66131b7}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys tongboensis (Wang & Lyu in Lyu, Zeng, Wang, Liu, Huang, Li & Wang, 2021) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Pingxi type-locality knowledge {#s-a89a00fb12}
Male holotype SYS a003228 came from Pingxi on Tongbo Mountain, Guangfeng, Jiangxi, at approximately 1100 m. Citing Lyu and colleagues (2021), the monograph knew this species only from its type locality at 1100–1115 m. That narrow reported interval is not a measured physiological limit or evidence that other elevations lack the species.

## Five males with unwebbed toes {#s-4bc1ca7e5b}
The examined type series contained five adult males with diagnostic snout–vent lengths of 26.5–31.5 mm. The diagnosis described a visible tympanum with an indistinct anterior edge, vomerine ridges and teeth, and toes without webbing or lateral fringes. Collected males had a subgular vocal sac but no nuptial pads or spines. No female measurement was supplied in this diagnosis; the male sample does not establish female morphology or population-wide size limits.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-6f85aeb35b}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys tongboensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
