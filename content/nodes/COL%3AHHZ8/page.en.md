## Yellow ray florets on solitary flower heads {#s-455b3ba496}
The record describes a hairy herb with sessile, opposite leaves ranging from oblong to lance-shaped and a leathery texture. Solitary, radiate flower heads have a bell-shaped involucre and a flat, chaffy receptacle. The yellow ray florets are sterile, while the central tubular florets are bisexual and fertile. The cypsela fruit has an oblong attachment base and no awn-like pappus.
