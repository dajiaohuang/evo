## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-eafcfab2d7}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 24 mm. For frogs, this measure is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental strategy {#s-afbf83ddb2}
