## Beak, wing and tail in a small sample {#s-f06552a789}
Morphology covers 10 measured birds (4 female, 6 male), of which 9 have complete measurements. Species averages are 9 mm from the nostril’s anterior edge to the beak tip, 21.2 mm tarsus, 56.8 mm unflattened wing and 47.5 mm tail. These are not individual size bounds. Hand-wing index is 18.9, defined through Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, rather than flight distance or speed. Dividing rounded species-average lengths cannot replace the reported index.

## Mean body mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-9ecb81f183}
Body mass is 19.19 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning, defined as a species average incorporating female and male mass. It is not field weighing of the 10 morphology birds or an annual or geographic minimum–maximum range. Inference=NO means the biometric row was not inferred from a relative; mass still comes from published material, and Dunning’s original book and individual denominator were not read here.

## Compiled habitat and food categories {#s-b4831225ab}
The row records Wetland habitat, an Invertivore niche, Carnivore trophic level and Generalist lifestyle, meaning no single dominant lifestyle. Habitat-density code 3 means open habitats, not three birds per square metre. These compiled categories provide neither seasonal diet percentages nor independent stomach-content observations.

## What the migration code means {#s-1c10e5bc75}
Migration=3 classifies the species as having long-distance migration in the majority of its population. The code supplies no departure month, specific route or tracked individual, and does not require every bird to migrate. Hand-wing index cannot replace migration observations.

## Evidence limits {#s-6f3432a255}
This page interprets historical species averages and coarse ecology in AVONET v7. Selected rows, field definitions and name crosswalks were read; individual raw measurements, Dunning’s original book, independent field behaviour were not. Morphology counts are not mass-literature sample sizes, and Inference=NO does not establish concept identity with COL26.8.
