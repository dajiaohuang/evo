## A spurred greenish-yellow flower {#s-024ad501c4}
The Flora of Panama describes *Halenia euryphylla* as an herb to about 50 cm tall, while marking its perennial status with a question mark. Stems are procumbent, round in cross-section, and faintly striate. The membranous leaves have three veins, conspicuous vein connections, and blades 2–6 cm long; inflorescences are loose, few-flowered cymes, usually terminal and rarely axillary. Flowers are greenish-yellow, with a spreading spur 5–8 mm long. The ellipsoid capsules are 1.2–1.5 cm long; the smooth, light-tan seeds are 1–1.3 mm long. The source leaves its perennial status uncertain.

## Known record from Cerro Copete {#s-05f74d5f4a}
The Flora of Panama distribution field says the species is known only from Cerro Copete, at 2,300–3,000 m in Chiriquí Province, Panama. This is the source's reported range; it is not a set of individual collection coordinates.
