## Muellera tubicalyx {#s-03ca691991}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muellera tubicalyx* as an accepted species of *Muellera* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record gives a 4–5 m height and 9–11 leaflets per leaf. It describes loose flower clusters and flowers that may be sessile or stalked, with pink petals; the fruit is a papery-to-leathery winged legume and the seeds are kidney-shaped.
