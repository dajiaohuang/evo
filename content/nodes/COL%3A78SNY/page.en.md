## *Restio egregius*: pendulous male flower clusters {#s-7a044b7134}
This tufted perennial may reach 2.4 m, taller than many low restios in the same flora. Its male flower clusters form drooping panicles, while the female spikelets are solitary and 20–40 mm long. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

The recorded habitat is rocky sandstone slopes.
