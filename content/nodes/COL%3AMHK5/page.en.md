## What traits are recorded for Bonnetia sessilis? {#s-62a86a2e95}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes unstalked leaves longer than 2 cm, clustered near branch tips with curved, looping veins. Dense flower clusters carry white corollas with pink tips. The sepals are rounded, and the style divides at its tip.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-1efdf015ab}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a site-specific description.
