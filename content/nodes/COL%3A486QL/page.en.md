## Floating leaves and fringed white or yellow flowers {#s-483aa99352}
This account describes a rhizomatous aquatic perennial with rounded, heart-shaped floating leaves. Two or more flowers grow in clusters at the nodes, and their petals have fringed edges. Flowers may be entirely yellow or white, or have a yellow tube and white lobes. Seed surfaces range from smooth to densely covered with tiny bumps; the source notes that both extremes may grow side by side. It lives in permanent pools.
