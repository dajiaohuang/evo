## How large was Ledumahadi? {#s-652d13e4fc}
The partial postcranial holotype BP/1/7120 preserves limb bones used for histology and circumference-based scaling. The study identifies an adult and estimates a mass of about 12 tonnes, with habitual quadrupedality despite flexed, non-columnar limbs; both mass and posture are model-based conclusions.

## The holotype and its measurements {#s-d17dede423}
BP/1/7120 is a partial postcranial skeleton. Limb-bone histology—the microscopic record of bone tissue and growth marks—was used to assess maturity, while bone circumference fed a comparative mass estimate of about 12 tonnes. The specimen is the concrete anchor; the number is an output of scaling against other animals.

## A quadrupedal model with limits {#s-0a2f18519e}
The authors inferred habitual quadrupedality even though the limbs were flexed rather than column-like. That conclusion comes from comparative limb metrics, not a preserved footprint sequence or observed gait. The displayed Early Jurassic interval is formation-bounded and does not set a universal threshold for sauropod gigantism or identify Ledumahadi as an ancestor.
