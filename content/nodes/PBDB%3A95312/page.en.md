## Three scans, three places on the lepidosaur tree {#s-6769d247ec}
Three imaged fossils anchor this route through early lepidosaur history. Megachirella PZO 628 contributes CT-resolved skull, wrist and shoulder characters; Taytalura PVSJ 698 preserves a three-dimensionally exposed skull examined by CT; Bellairsia NMS G.2022.1.1 was studied with synchrotron tomography. These are complementary specimens, not one continuous lineage.

The cited analyses place Megachirella and Bellairsia on the squamate stem and Taytalura among early-diverging stem lepidosauromorphs. A stem placement means a fossil lies on the branch leading toward a living group, outside that group’s crown; it does not identify a direct ancestor. The route records anatomy and matrix-dependent relationships, not a shared diet or habitat for Lepidosauria.
