## Tarenaya diffusa {#s-bd28c3a687}
## Three to five leaflets frame differently shaped flowers

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records leaves with 3–5 leaflets. Their stalks and blades lack spines; the margins range from entire to finely toothed and have hairs. Bract stalks may have a spine at the base or may lack one.

The sepals are linear to triangular, while the petals range from inversely lance-shaped to spoon-shaped. A disc-shaped nectary sits near the spindle- to ellipsoid-shaped ovary. The style is short and the stigma head-shaped. The fruit is spindle-shaped to oblong.
