## From articulated ribs to a swimming hypothesis {#s-5e0580f019}
The MPC-D 102/114 holotype preserves a nearly complete skull and articulated dorsal ribs. The 2022 authors compare the ribs with birds and interpret the material as supporting a semiaquatic form. Propulsion, fish eating and whole-body shape remain separate inferences. Missing characters affect the phylogenetic analysis, which is not presented here as later consensus.

## Evidence limits {#s-62bd1ff3a0}
Occurrence and collection counts do not measure ancient population size. Complete reconstructions, propulsion, and diet are interpretations rather than direct observations; the PBDB path remains distinct from phylogenetic hypotheses.
