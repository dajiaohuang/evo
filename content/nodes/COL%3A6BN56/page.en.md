## *Ctenanthe compressa*: paired flowers above a rhizome {#s-85336a0174}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, aerial shoots form a leaf rosette above a subterranean rhizome, an underground stem. The elliptic green blades sit on sheaths that may be hairless or sparsely woolly. A compressed, panicle-shaped flowering structure carries one to four flower clusters at each node; each small cluster has two flowers.

The flowers are tubular and yellow. Two lilac, petal-like staminodes—sterile stamens—surround the single fertile stamen. After flowering, a papery capsule changes from green to brown as it matures and contains one rough, ellipsoid seed with a yellow aril, a fleshy seed attachment. The regional habitat field records terrestrial substrate only, without a locality or date.
