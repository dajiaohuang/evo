## Climbing stems and heart-shaped leaves {#s-ca97e70d7f}
The morphology field describes climbing, grooved stems with hairs and opposite ovate leaves. Their bases are heart-shaped, and the three main veins begin near the base; the lower surfaces are hairy. Thyrse-like flower clusters carry heads with funnel-shaped florets, and the dry fruits are hairless or sparsely hairy.
