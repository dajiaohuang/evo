## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-a1b4d69314}
The description records clustered stems up to 8 m tall and 8–14 leaves. Each side of the leaf axis has 85–115 pinnae, arranged regularly in the same plane. The inflorescence is upright and emerges between the leaves; one female flower is recorded at the base of each small flower-bearing branch. Fruits range from inverted-cone or egg-shaped to nearly spherical, with an outer covering described as bristly, brown-haired, or bearing small spines. They do not split open at maturity.
