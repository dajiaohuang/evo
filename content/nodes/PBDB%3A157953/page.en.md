## A family route to three species pages {#s-c7ca8ac091}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Chaoyangopteridae as a family. This route links to occurrence guides for three species in the fixed pterosaur roster. The database parent path helps readers find the records; it does not represent a universal phylogenetic arrangement.

## Compare four selected occurrences {#s-1072e5129a}
The three linked species are Shenzhoupterus chaoyangensis, Apatorhamphus gyrostega and Lacusovagus magnificens. Their pages cover four selected occurrences from four collections, with recorded units including Jiufotang, Ifezouane and Crato. Compare each locality, age bracket and identification note separately. Occurrence and collection counts are not counts of fossils, animals or ancient populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-6676af2180}
This route covers selected records for three species, not a complete descendant catalogue or an ecological summary of Chaoyangopteridae. Record counts do not measure specimens, individuals, population size or sampling effort, and broad database ages do not directly date the family’s origin or extinction. PBDB parentage is used for navigation and remains separate from Catalogue of Life concepts and phylogenetic hypotheses.
