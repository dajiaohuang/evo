## Different leaf forms along the stem {#s-e6eafcf1c4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry distinguishes leaf position: lower leaves have two to four pairs of leaflets, with one pair also recorded, while upper leaves have seven to 16 pairs. The leaflets are described as narrow, from oblanceolate to oblong or linear. Flower, fruit, and seed traits are marked unknown in this profile. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
