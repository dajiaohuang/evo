## Ciliate wine-colored sepals and a stalked fruit {#s-b78be9253a}
The morphology record notes leaves with straight tips and flowers borne singly or in small clusters. The sepals are wine-colored and fringed with hairs; the petals are equal in length, while the anthers are unequal. The fruit is stalked and obovoid. The habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
