## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-8fc774ca98}
Diomedea antipodensis is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Diomedeidae. The 2015 life-history archive has a record under the same name, with seven focal fields supported by cited species-level rows from the same family. Those rows connect the records but do not remove older species or subspecies data that may have been carried into the summaries.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-d5ecae1365}
The archive reports an adult body-mass summary of 7,790 g and a birth-or-hatching mass summary of 357.9 g. Adult values may combine males, females and records with unspecified sex; the second value concerns a different life stage. They are compiled summaries, not measurements that follow the same birds or define the species’ range of individual mass.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-d537a5c182}
Four timing and reproduction summaries are available: female age at first conception 3,670.76 days; one offspring or egg per litter or clutch; 79 days from laying to hatching; and 277.7 days to flight capability. Some source material defines the last event as leaving the nest. The archive combines offspring and egg counts in one field, so its summary does not describe a fraction of an individual. These values mark distinct events, not one interchangeable reproductive interval.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-9266d1a199}
The archive reports a maximum-longevity value of 60 years, selected as the largest record in its collected material. This is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, a limit that applies to every bird, or a claim that the record is currently repeatable.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-1d84ecc7cc}
The cited rows include Dunning (1992), Szekely, Lislevand and Figuerola (2007), de Magalhaes and Costa (2009), and Bennett (1986), among other sources. The archive retains a separate row number, value and source label for each field, alongside its reference explanations. A label may point to a paper, book or another database; rows are not counts of measured birds, and repeated citations are not independent experiments.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-ab4d99415d}
The values come from a historical 2015 life-history compilation. They are reported as medians or as the average of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. None is a direct measurement of one individual, an individual-variation range, a mean wild lifespan or a current population survey. The archive may carry information across historical species and subspecies. A matching name and family support the record association but do not prove equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept or remove inherited data. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently identified, and the original publications have not been reviewed individually. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and unreported traits require other sources.
