## Name and family {#s-62b7c21a8b}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 accepts *Holocalyx balansae* as a species name and places it in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat record {#s-62ae6d772c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset classifies its habitat as terrestrial. The field supplies no collection locality, range or sampling coverage.
