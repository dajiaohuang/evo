## Age bounds of these records {#s-c22349f075}
Selected occurrences have an outer age envelope of 5–0.0117 Ma, where Ma denotes millions of years ago. It combines the oldest early bound and youngest late bound among selected records, rather than a continuous record at every time or measured species origin, extinction or individual lifespan. Each collection retains its own age bracket. Broad intervals are not narrowed to precise specimen ages, and original dating interpretations are not revised here.
One record supplying the older envelope bound is Farola (FL1), occurrence occ:1180272 (5–3.8 Ma); one supplying the younger bound is Bajada Grande, Huayquerías del Este, occurrence occ:1639629 (3.6–0.0117 Ma). These are collection-age bounds, not endpoints of species survival.

## Evidence limits {#s-74633233de}
This batch is a guide to PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02, not a complete species monograph. Entries belong to the fixed 13958-item mammalia working roster, retain the current same name and a complete parent chain to the recorded Mammalia storage anchor, and have usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers.
