## Herreria montevidensis: leaves and flowers in a simple raceme {#s-6fc41233dd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical, hairless branches without prickles. Leaves are papery and narrowly linear or narrowly obovate. The inflorescence axis is hairless and bears a simple raceme. Flower stalks are hairless; the buds are ellipsoid and described as greenish white. The yellow tepals may be oblong, lanceolate or oblanceolate. Filaments are flattened and awl-shaped, the anthers oblong, and the ovary ellipsoid with three angles.

This is one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. It does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat category “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.
