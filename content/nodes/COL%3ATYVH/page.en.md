## Chevreulia revoluta {#s-bf122e7db3}
## Prostrate stems and narrow leaves along the stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes prostrate stems with linear leaves attached along them. The leaf tips taper to a point, and the upper surfaces are smooth. Bracts around each head have rounded or blunt tips. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional record supplies no locality or complete range.
