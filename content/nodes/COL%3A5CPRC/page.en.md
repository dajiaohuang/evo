## *Xyris pranceana*: spiral leaves and an inconspicuous ligule {#s-8b8bf558bf}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, leaves spiral around the stem and have narrow blades that are round or nearly round in cross-section. A ligule—the small flap where blade and leaf sheath meet—is present but inconspicuous. The blade surface may be striated or cross-wrinkled, and tapers to a slender tip; the sheath base is scarcely widened.

The cylindrical flowering stalk is listed at 5–50 cm, with a single ridge. Its ellipsoid spike has up to 10 flowers, surrounded by four sterile bracts; seeds are described as ellipsoid to egg-shaped. The regional habitat field records only a terrestrial substrate, without a named locality or date. These are regional flora characters, not a documented range-wide measure of variation.
