## What distinguishes Kielmeyera gracilis in the record? {#s-3f80a67f3b}
Its leaves are papery rather than leathery, elliptic to nearly round, and may have blunt or shallowly notched tips; the base narrows toward the stalk. The record notes flower clusters with more than three flowers, green sepals of unequal size, and strongly asymmetric white petals. Upright anthers bear conspicuous apical glands. Pollen is described as tetrads, groups of four grains, and the ovary and style are hairless.
