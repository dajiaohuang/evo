## What does Lantana velutina look and smell like? {#s-68f0d0edc3}
This diffusely branched shrub reaches 2 m and has a rhubarb-like odor. Its opposite leaves are softly hairy and often wrinkled; prominent membranous bracts overlap around each flower head. The fragrant, usually white flowers often have a yellow throat. The fleshy pink fruits are described as sweet and edible.

## What range and settings does the source report? {#s-186314e3dd}
The source places it in waste ground, mesas, foothill slopes, alluvial plains, sandy bottoms, and llanos from southern Texas and northern Mexico to Venezuela. It also lists Socorro Island and occasional cultivation. These are the locations and settings in the cited account, not a current complete-range assessment.
