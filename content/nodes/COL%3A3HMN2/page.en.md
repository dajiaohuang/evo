## Gurania malacophylla Barb.Rodr. {#s-f6c51f69b0}
## Shaggy leaves and silvery flower tubes

Gurania malacophylla has smooth stems and simple tendrils. Its leaves have three to five palm-shaped lobes, a heart-shaped base and a tapering tip. The underside is densely shaggy; the upper surface is also hairy, from villous to bristly. The source lists hairs in two length classes, under 5 mm or 5–15 mm. Racemes gather flowers near their tips; the species is dioecious and the flowers are sessile. The tubular to ovoid hypanthium has silvery hairs, and its linear sepals bend backward. Staminate flowers have two nearly round anthers with appendages; the anther bases are folded back.
