## Adults near rapids, with some rain-period records away from water {#s-25ce613ead}
The Mindanao account typically collected adults in splash zones beside rapids or waterfalls, but also found some away from water, especially after rain. In suitable habitat the authors described many observations, without giving a standardized density here. Their explanation of patchy mountain-associated distribution relied on cited torrent-larval habitat information; the adult observations do not alone test that causal explanation.

## Structured lineages awaiting taxonomic resolution {#s-86e4241fce}
The authors cited a study identifying five geographically structured haplotype groups and reported two of those proposed conservation units in their own material. They observed differing morphotypes between Hilong-hilong/Magdiwata and Balatukan/Lumot. They treated possible cryptic species as requiring further taxonomic work. These are dated lineage and morphology observations, not five established species or a current conservation assessment.
