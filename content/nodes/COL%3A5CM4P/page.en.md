## What are the leaves, flowers, and fruits like? {#s-dabd4000c3}
The field describes young branches with woolly hairs. Leaves are 3–8 cm long, narrow elliptic to oblong, with 10–15 pairs of side veins; the underside is hairy or silky. Two to four flowers, usually two, grow in leaf axils on short stalks. The fruits have six to eight club-shaped segments, 9–12 mm long, with slight wrinkles and fine hairs; seeds are 6–7 mm long.
