## What growth, leaf and flower traits are described for Schlechterina mitostemmatoides? {#s-efcfe6a879}
This small liana or leaning shrublet reaches 3 m and grows from a perennial rootstock. Older stems become corky; tendrils are 3–15 cm long. Leaves vary from elliptic to linear, with entire or toothed edges, or may be pinnately lobed; juvenile shoots often have narrow, deeply lobed leaves. Blades measure 2.5–13 × 0.7–4.5 cm, while juvenile forms may reach 10–30 × 0.2–2 cm. Petioles are 0–12 mm; glands may be absent or occur in one or two pairs at the tip. Small marginal glands sit mostly at tooth tips. Inflorescences bear one to three flowers. The shallow floral cup is 3–4.5 mm wide; flowers have three or four sepals and two to four petals. A single-row corona of threadlike segments rises 5–8 mm; six to eight stamens are joined at the base, and the ovary has three or four angles. The fruit is an ellipsoid capsule with three or four valves, about 4.5–5 cm long, with a few seeds about 8 mm long.

## What habitats and soils are recorded? {#s-86100b4fa2}
The source records coastal scrub and forest, including sandy soils and old coral reefs.
