## Three narrow leaves and pale fragrant flowers {#s-40e1b54985}
Gladiolus exilis is 25–45 cm tall, with three narrow, short-bladed leaves and fragrant pale-blue to whitish flowers. The funnel-shaped flower tube is shorter than the dorsal tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal); the leaves lack a thickened midrib and margin.

## Clay loam in fynbos {#s-dabc7fc06b}
The regional record places it in clay loam in fynbos (a Cape shrubland vegetation type). It does not establish how broadly the species occupies this habitat.
