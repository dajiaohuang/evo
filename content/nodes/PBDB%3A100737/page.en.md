## Recorded parent of Poposauroidea {#s-2842ea625f}
Poposauroidea is listed at rank superfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Pseudosuchia as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
