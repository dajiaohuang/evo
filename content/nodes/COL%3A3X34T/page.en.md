## A record that preserves several forms {#s-10054c7937}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field keeps several alternatives rather than selecting one form. It lists the stem as round or slightly quadrangular; leaves may be sessile or shortly petiolate, papery or leathery, and elliptic, ovate, or obovate. Their bases are recorded as rounded or cuneate and symmetric or oblique, and the blade is hairy on both surfaces. The field likewise gives alternatives for bract attachment and shape, calyx-lobe shape and tip, and calyx indumentum. Corolla colour is cream-green or white-green, with a funnel-shaped form. The data row does not resolve why these alternatives are grouped together.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-65c80c2f96}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Macrocarpaea obtusifolia* as terrestrial but names no collection locality.
