## A basal rosette beneath green, wine-edged bracts {#s-7848a59890}
The record describes a basal rosette of smooth or nearly smooth leaves, ranging from obovate to spoon-shaped. A single rounded cluster of heads rises at the stem tip; its green bracts have wine-colored edges. The lilac flowers have a white ring near the top of the tube and at the base of the lobes. The pappus persists on the fruits. The habitat field says terrestrial.
