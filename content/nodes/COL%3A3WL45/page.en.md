## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ea7c3c2fd9}
COL26.8 records Lycodon semicarinatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-bcd1cb04dc}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 2080 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-0d82057725}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0bf790dec9}
The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
