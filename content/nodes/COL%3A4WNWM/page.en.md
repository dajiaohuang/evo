## Leaf glands, pinna counts and flower traits {#s-8ea7a0d90d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical branches with irregularly arranged, recurved prickles. Stipules are lanceolate. One sessile petiolar nectary is warty and lies on the lower third of the petiole; a rachis nectary is present. Leaves have 7–19 pairs of pinnae, each with 4–18 pairs of oblong leaflets. The leaflets are sericeous on both surfaces, with oblique bases, mucronate tips and a listed width of 4–4.5 mm. The primary vein is subcentral, with no hairs at its base. Spike like inflorescences are 15–20 cm long. Flowers are sessile; the tubular calyx is sericeous or puberulent, the tubular corolla is sericeous, anther tips bear glands and the ovary is villous. The fruit is a legume; the seed condition is listed as immature or absent. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
