## Distinguish the measured length {#s-7bd2e67b9e}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 808 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding head, neck and tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.
