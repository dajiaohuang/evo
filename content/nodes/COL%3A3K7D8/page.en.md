## Helichrysum truncatum {#s-28006bce3b}
## Silvery leaves in wet grassland and rock seeps

Herman and Retief's 1997 account describes a mat-forming perennial herb up to 150 mm tall. Its leaves first form a rosette, then are described as oblanceolate to lance-shaped, about 50 by 5–7 mm, with silky silvery hairs over both surfaces.

The cylindrical flower heads grow in dense, flat-topped clusters, with heads also recorded singly at branch tips. The outer bracts are golden-brown above. The account records marshy grassland and sedge mats over wet rock seepage areas as habitat.
