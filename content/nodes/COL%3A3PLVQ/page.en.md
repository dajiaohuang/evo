## ## Rusty hairs and curved pods {#s-a484f3fa79}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium, a woody underground organ, and striate stems with rough, rusty-colored hairs. The alternate leaves are odd-pinnate, ending in a single leaflet, and have obovate to elliptic leaflets without glands. Congested racemes bear red to wine-colored petals. The pod is curved. These are regional character fields; the source does not provide a population sampling frame.
