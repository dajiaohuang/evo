## *Restio fragilis*: tangled stems in damp high ground {#s-c684acd6c6}
This low perennial, to 40 cm, spreads by runners and forms tangled clumps. Its slender stems are branched and finely wrinkled. The account locates it at seeps and on damp, south-facing slopes above 900 m.

These records describe wet patches on elevated slopes; they do not establish the species’ distribution beyond the regional account.
