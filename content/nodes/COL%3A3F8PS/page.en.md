## Gamochaeta camaquaensis {#s-685020aeaa}
## Rounded spatulate leaves and a continuous flower spike

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes basal branching, with both basal and stem leaves. The blades are spatulate and the same color on both sides; their upper surfaces are woolly or softly hairy. Leaf tips are rounded and lack a small terminal point. Flower clusters form a continuous spike of compact heads, whose inner bracts have acute tips. A separate habitat field says terrestrial; this regional entry gives no locality or full range.
