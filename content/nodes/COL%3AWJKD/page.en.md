## What leaf and flower traits identify Cnidoscolus populifolius? {#s-f7ceb87593}
The leaves are undivided and not lobed, with one or two persistent, fleshy glands on the leaf stalk. They have uniform color and irregularly toothed margins, with no glands along the edge. The androecium has four or more whorls of stamens. Male flowers have salver-shaped perianths, with a long tube opening into a flattened limb; female perianth parts are separate. The fruit is a globose capsule, and the seed has a caruncle.
