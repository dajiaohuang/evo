CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis youngorum in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-5b69b1e86a}
The compilation lists 14 measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.
