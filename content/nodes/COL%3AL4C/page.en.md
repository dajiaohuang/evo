Gestation and female age at first reproduction appear in eight accounts each; maximum lifespan appears in five. First reproduction differs from first birth and sexual maturity. ## Limits of Ernest’s historical values {#s-d49255bfb8}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 table; equivalence with current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources equally, without sample-size weighting. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies can overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted in this route; Reference numbers remain; wild, captive, and locality conditions are not consistently specified across sources.
