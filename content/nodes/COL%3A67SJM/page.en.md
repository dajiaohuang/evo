## Arrojadoa rhodantha {#s-df47db80f2}
## A cephalium rises above the ribbed stem

Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical stems with fewer than 20 ribs and no root tuber. The cactus areoles—the small growth points—are hairless. A cephalium, the compact flowering region, is present. The inner perianth segments are pink with blunt tips, and the source records the segments as uniformly colored. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings, without a named locality.
