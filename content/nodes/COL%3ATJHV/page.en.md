## One pair of narrow leaflets {#s-a9ef671869}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records one pair of oblanceolate leaflets, meaning each blade is wider toward its tip than its base. Bulb-tipped glandular hairs are noted, as are compound flower clusters at branch tips and beside the leaves. Yellow flowers have ten stamens. Fruit and seed form are marked unknown, and the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
