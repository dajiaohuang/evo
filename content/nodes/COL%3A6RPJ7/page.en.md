## White-woolly leaves beneath cream flower heads {#s-6188e60efd}
The record describes upright or reclining branches that are woolly when young and become nearly smooth. Alternate, short-stalked leaves are broadly elliptic, with smooth upper surfaces and woolly undersides. Cream-colored tubular florets form terminal, branched clusters that rise above the leaves. The ribbed fruits are silky-haired and topped with rough, straw-colored bristles.
