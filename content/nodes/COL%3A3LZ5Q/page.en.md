## Hippeastrum calyptratum: flowers and seeds {#s-011f155824}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records an exposed bulb and no rhizome. The leaves are sickle-shaped with acute tips, and the inflorescence bears two flowers. The funnel-shaped perianth has green bases and green tips, with or without reddish streaks at the tips. The hypanthium tube is of medium length, 2.5–6 cm. The sepals are obovate and narrower than the elliptic petals; the lower lateral sepals differ little from the upper sepals, and the lower petals are like the upper ones. The corona is plate-like. The filaments are not fascicled and exceed the sepals and petals in length; the anthers are purple or dark purple, and the stigma is three-cleft.

The capsule is brown inside. Seeds are numerous, flattened and disc-shaped.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
