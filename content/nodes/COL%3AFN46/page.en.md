## life-history {#s-af5ac68c97}
A honey bee colony contains queens, workers, and drones. Workers gather pollen and nectar, make wax, and care for brood; their tasks change with age. A swarm can establish another colony.

## range {#s-f782f60f16}
The species is described as native to Europe, Africa, and the Middle East. Beekeeping has carried it far beyond that reported native range. A managed hive or a feral record alone does not show that a population is native at a particular locality.
