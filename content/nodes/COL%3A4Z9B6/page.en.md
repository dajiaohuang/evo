## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Spizocorys starki (Shelley, 1902) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Regularly drinking desert birds have greater evaporative cooling capacity and higher heat tolerance limits than non-drinking species {#study-account}
A physiological study sampled ten birds identified as Spizocorys starki at Black Mountain Mine in South Africa between December 2018 and February 2019. The authors list mean mass of 17.9 ± 1.7 g and classify the birds as seed-eating regular drinkers. The measurements describe one arid-zone site and sample, not every population.
