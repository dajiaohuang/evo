## Small evening-scented white flowers {#s-65258c2e6f}
Hesperantha spicata grows 12–35 cm tall and has small white flowers with a cinnamon-like scent, especially in the evening. Three named subspecies differ in leaf form, but the cited account says recent observations make their separation uncertain.

## Clay and sandy soils {#s-08f3795910}
The regional record lists clay and sandy soils. It provides no full ecological survey.
