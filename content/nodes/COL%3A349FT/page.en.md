## Paired straight spines on reclining branches {#s-ab3ac3be17}
The morphology record describes reclining branches with paired, straight spines. Alternate, stalked leaves are papery and elliptic to broadly elliptic, with a pointed, spine-like tip. Branched clusters carry bell-shaped heads with brownish or green bracts; the flowers have short hairs on their lobes. The habitat field says terrestrial.
