## A five-species bone-density study {#bone-density-series}
Amson et al. compared bone compactness across five named Thalassocnus species sampled from successive horizons in Peru’s Pisco Formation, spanning an approximately 8–3 Ma study window. The series supports comparison of bone-density change across those samples. Functional adaptation and continuity of one lineage are interpretations; the sample window is not a global genus duration or a direct-ancestor sequence.
