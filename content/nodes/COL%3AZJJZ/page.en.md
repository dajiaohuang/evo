## A tall shrub with narrow leaves {#s-57b1a2a50d}
The morphology account describes a shrub reaching 8 m, with opposite, narrowly elliptic leaves up to 23 cm long and finely toothed margins. Its flower heads contain five white florets and are grouped in large, branched clusters. The dry fruits are about 2.5 mm long and carry a tuft of 25–30 slender bristles.
