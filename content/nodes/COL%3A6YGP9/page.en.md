## Senegalia kuhlmannii {#s-d47c87ea8b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senegalia kuhlmannii* as an accepted species of *Senegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 14–18 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), with 37–55 pairs of leaflets on each pinna. The inflorescences are spike-like, and the pods are 2.2–3.3 cm wide. Seed shape was not observed in the source record; the habitat field describes the plant as terrestrial.
