## A stem with a central strand {#s-45a3d2a004}
The Flora of North America account describes a sparsely branched stem with a central strand and no axillary hyaline nodules (clear nodules at leaf axils). Its leaves occur in as many as 20 pairs and are described as obovate to lanceolate—broadest toward the tip or shaped like a lance. This regional taxonomic excerpt gives no sampling frame.
