## Fragmentation and a dated Vulnerable proposal {#s-f53bb53dcb}
The 2021 paper estimated unisacculus extent of occurrence below 2500 km² and area of occupancy below 2000 km² from landscape and usable habitat. It described fragmentation among islands, peninsula and mainland and by urban areas and highways, and proposed Vulnerable under B1ab(i,ii,iii). These estimated areas remain distinct from directly measured occupied habitat. Unknown population sizes and unquantified dynamics mean the proposal is not a demographic extinction model or an independently verified current assessment.
