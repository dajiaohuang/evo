## Pustulate skin and historical measurements {#s-d4c4a609d7}
The diagnosis describes dorsal/flank skin with large flat pustules, a differentiated tympanum, broad rounded digit discs and one large plus smaller rounded heel tubercles. It reports female SVL30.1–38.2mm (33.6±4.15SD,n3) and one male29.8mm; Table3 marks some measurements as included from original descriptions. Lip orange stripes were not present in the three newly encountered specimens, despite the historical name explanation. The absence in this sample does not invalidate the species identification.

## A local encounter history with low detection {#s-967f53afa4}
