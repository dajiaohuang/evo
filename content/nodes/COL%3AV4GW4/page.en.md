## Montane forest litter and leaf records {#s-4e13fd68d5}
The account described a terrestrial frog in tropical montane forest, at about 1500 m in Crocker Range Park and 1650 m on Mount Mulu, sheltering beneath moist forest-floor litter or perching on leaves. ## Two measured adults and their distinct reproductive characters {#s-a1a63a160b}
The 2025 Crocker Range account described one female at 43.0 mm snout–vent length with enlarged unpigmented ova and one male at 51.0 mm with a black nuptial pad on finger I. Both lacked vomerine teeth, had one conical upper-eyelid projection without a rostral projection, and a longitudinal dorsolateral fold. The male’s head was wider than its trunk, differing from the cited original descriptors. Two individual measurements do not establish a population-level size difference between sexes, and ova or a finger pad do not define a complete breeding season.

## A first Sabah record claimed by the 2025 account {#s-5b0cf0193b}
The authors presented their Crocker Range Park occurrence as the first record for both the park and Sabah, contrasting it with earlier Mount Mulu, Sarawak, records.
