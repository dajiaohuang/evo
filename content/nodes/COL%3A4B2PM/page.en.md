## A thorny shrublet with small fleshy leaves {#s-4186752ac4}
This description records a much-branched shrublet up to 1 m tall, with twigs ending in thorns. Small, fleshy, oval leaves occur singly along the branches and in axillary tufts. Yellow radiate heads are solitary in these tufts, on very short or nearly absent stalks. Stony slopes are listed as habitat.
