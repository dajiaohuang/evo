## How are its parasitic habit and flower dimensions recorded? {#s-b51bc6d703}
Harveya pauciflora is described as an erect, semiparasitic herb up to 350 mm tall, with small ovate leaves. Its calyx is 10–12 mm long, and the corolla tube reaches 18–24 mm, widening to 7–10 mm at the top. The flower tube is cream or yellow, while the expanded limb is pinkish white or pink. These measurements belong to the source description; no sample size is given.

## What landscape setting is listed? {#s-ae8b2f742a}
The habitat field places Harveya pauciflora in rocky grassland. It does not identify a collection locality or full range.
