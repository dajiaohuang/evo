## Silky leaf undersides and mixed-sex flower heads {#s-cc127ccf6a}
The record describes branches with fine Malpighian hairs and alternate, stalked, leathery leaves. The oval leaves have smooth upper surfaces and silky, hairy undersides; their tips range from rounded to slightly notched or mucronate. Terminal or axillary clusters carry bell-shaped heads with cream tubular florets, which may be bisexual or female. The ribbed fruits are silky-haired and topped with straw-colored bristles.
