## How are Hirtella davisii branches, leaves, and flowers described? {#s-30ba252e82}
Young branches are described as sparsely hairy or becoming nearly smooth. Leaves are oblong or elliptic, without the ant-associated cavities assessed in this record. Flowers grow in panicles. The bracts bear glands without stalks, while the smaller bracteoles have stalked glands. Each flower has three stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-74f716112b}
It is categorized as terrestrial, with no named site.
