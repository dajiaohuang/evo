## The source concept {#s-3054292865}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Fejervarya parambikulamana* as row Anf1658, in the genus Fejervarya, family Dicroglossidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-82cabb3fa7}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1), aquatic (Aqu = 1), and arboreal (Arb = 1) as broad foraging categories. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-564d3a5473}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 39 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental strategy {#s-764e5f28c5}
The row records larval stage (Lar = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
