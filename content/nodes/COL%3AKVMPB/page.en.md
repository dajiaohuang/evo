## Fin and head characters in diagnosis {#brief}
Parabembras is a deep-water flathead genus whose 2018 review separated three recognized species through combinations of head spines, dorsal-fin spines, lateral-line scales and pectoral-fin reach. The comparison makes clear why species identification depends on several characters together.

## A character combination separates the three species {#spine-scale-differences}
Kai and Fricke recognized P. curta, P. robinsoni and P. multisquamata. P. robinsoni is distinguished by eleven first-dorsal-fin spines, a lower-jaw symphyseal knob, two preocular spines and one lachrymal spine. P. multisquamata and P. curta both lack the knob and share two lachrymal and one preocular spine, but P. multisquamata usually has ten first-dorsal spines, 9–11 supraocular spines, 40–44 pored lateral-line scales, and a pectoral fin extending past the anus; P. curta has eight or nine, 6–8, 34–39, and a pectoral fin that does not reach the anus. These are diagnostic ranges reported in the taxonomic review.
