## A sagittate leaf and a cream spadix {#s-9f5eeaf06f}
The accepted name follows Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage 4G8FB and a matched World Flora Online usage wfo-0000269019. The biological details below come from the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional morphology field.

COL26.8 accepted species ID 4G8FB and exact name/authorship map through exactly one accepted WFO usage wfo-0000269019; the Brazilian Flora source record carries the same WFO ID.

A Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Philodendron spiritus-sancti through its sagittate or hastate leaves, short gray internodes, and axillary inflorescence. Vein and ovary counts remain alternatives in the source record.

## Morphology and flowers {#s-ff1fd41782}
The field records feeding roots, short internodes described as bright gray, and a pale-green prophyll (a protective leaf around a developing shoot). The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole conspicuous and green. The entire blade is listed as sagittate, hastate, or triangular, with a sagittate base; the source gives 3–6 primary lateral veins on both sides. The axillary inflorescence (arising where a leaf joins the stem) is sympodial (its axis continues through successive side branches). Its short peduncle is shorter than the spathe and recorded as green or wine-coloured. The slightly constricted spathe has a bright-green outer surface with reddish spots. The subsessile spadix (a fleshy flower spike) is recorded as light green or cream.

The record notes short stamens and staminodes narrowed at the base. The gynoecium and ovary are each recorded as ovoid or subcylindrical. The ovary has 7, 8, or 9 locules (chambers), 3 or 4 ovules, and subbasal placentation. Stigma structure is unknown. The berry is cream-yellow and the seed is described as numerous.

## Habitat recorded in the flora {#s-758c80bf70}
The field records “Hemiepífita” (hemiepiphytic); it gives no locality or complete range.
