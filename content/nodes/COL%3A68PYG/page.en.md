## Accepted name and placement {#s-f5405ff5ab}
COL26.8 lists Brachycephalus toby Haddad, Alves, Clemente-Carvalho & Reis, 2010 at species rank within Brachycephalus, in family Brachycephalidae and order Anura. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026; it does not confirm that other databases use the same species concept.

## Adult food categories — AmphiBIO source {#s-f151fef81f}
The historical row lists arthropods among adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiled these literature-derived categories from expert judgment, observation or stomach contents; the code is neither a complete diet nor a measure of food proportions.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-6cab10152d}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 13.9 mm for its historical anuran row. For frogs this means snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not the range of all individuals, and the source concept is not certified as equivalent to the present catalogue record.
