## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-ce7a1ef5bf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with a dense woolly hair covering and stem lenticels (small pores). Lance-shaped stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) fall away. The leaf stalk (petiole) is recorded as cylindrical or winged, and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) is winged. Each leaf has three leaflet pairs. A stalkless, cylindrical nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is noted. Flower clusters are spike-like; the calyx (outer floral cup) is recorded as bell-shaped or tubular, and flowers lack separate stalks. The leathery pod is flat; its surface may be hairless or hairy. This single regional field preserves the alternatives and does not establish variation or how widely these traits occur.

## Habitat category {#s-46bccb2921}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is a category recorded in one row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
