## *Piqueria trinervia*: fragrant white heads without a pappus {#s-1697795181}
The Flora of Panama account, retrieved on 8 September 2026, describes a short-lived perennial herb up to 1.5 m tall. Its opposite leaves have rounded bases with three main veins, coarse serrations, and short stalks. Loose to fairly dense panicles bear fragrant heads of three or four florets, with white corollas. The fruitlets are hairless and lack a pappus. The account’s historical distribution statement lists Mexico, Central America, and Hispaniola; this is not a current range inventory.
