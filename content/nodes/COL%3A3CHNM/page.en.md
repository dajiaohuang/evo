## Overview {#s-01bb82b08a}
COL26.8 accepts Euoticus pallidus (Gray, 1863) as species ID 3CHNM under genus ID 4G62. The Mammal Diversity Database (MDD) uses Northern Needle-clawed Galago, lists Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Nigeria, and assesses it as Near Threatened. The Chinese display name is editorial.

## Survey records {#s-04b132c0cc}
Forbanka’s Cameroon survey used three transects totalling 8.5 km and recorded 56 encounters of E. elegantulus and E. pallidus combined. The paper reports an encounter rate of 8.3 individuals per kilometre for E. pallidus from the Mount Kupe study site. Transects were deliberately placed at sites where the animals had been seen or heard on earlier survey nights, so this is a selected-site detection result, not a density estimate or a range-wide population measure.

## Habitat context {#s-ac2c947784}
A separate Cameroon vegetation study sampled 14 quadrats in forest areas selected after nocturnal observations of Euoticus. Tree density differed between its sampled primary and secondary forests. The authors described some flexibility across those forest types while associating Euoticus with tall, large-diameter trees. Because the study treated the two needle-clawed species together, it does not establish a species-specific habitat response for E. pallidus.
