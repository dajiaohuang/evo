## An older combination leads into the Huaxiadraco revision {#s-72416dcd35}
The 2026-10-02 PBDB snapshot lists Huaxiapterus corollatus for the selected occurrence record. The 2023 study assigns holotype ZMNH M813 to a new genus as Huaxiadraco corollatus and treats H. benxiensis as its junior synonym. Compare anatomy, growth evidence and diagnosis through the holotype and referred material; retaining the older database name preserves the occurrence-record link, not a second biological grouping.

## A nearly complete holotype still has damaged and obscured regions {#s-b67a52145c}
ZMNH M813 is nearly complete, but the posterior skull, post-cervical vertebrae and feet are especially damaged. Forearm bones cover cervicals four and five, preventing a direct length comparison; the posterior extent and proportions of the nasoantorbital fenestra partly depend on reconstruction from the jaw joint. Near-completeness does not make every character measurable, and the reconstructed fenestra dimensions remain estimates.

## The skull combination includes a downturned rostrum and trapezoidal crest {#s-e5fae91c8d}
The holotype account describes a trapezoidal premaxillary crest, shallow mandibular crest, more strongly downturned rostrum and shorter nasoantorbital fenestra, with a gap between closed jaws. Roughly 21° rostral deflection and a fenestra length/height ratio near 2.2 describe this specimen with some reconstruction, rather than invariant values for every individual. Diagnosis combines further skeletal traits; crest size or shape alone does not establish sex, food or a separate species.

## A short first metacarpal and terminal wing phalanx inform diagnosis {#s-8e93d092dd}
The holotype’s first and second metacarpals do not reach the wrist and extend roughly one-third the length of metacarpal four; wing phalanx four is about one-fifth the length of the first. This differs from the longer first metacarpal and terminal wing phalanx in S. dongi, helping support a separate morphotype. Skeletal proportions do not reconstruct a complete wing membrane or directly determine maximum speed or flapping style.

## Partial fusion supports a subadult interpretation {#s-987579ff3c}
Posterior skull bones, scapula/coracoid and the first wing-phalanx extensor tendon process remain unfused, while the right tibiotarsus is fused and carpals appear to form proximal and distal fused units. Poor preservation prevents assessment of humeral epiphyses. The authors infer a subadult with an estimated wingspan near 1.56 m: relative maturity within the group rather than exact age, sex or maximum adult wingspan.

## Skull-less D2525 is referred through limb combinations {#s-fd0d755cc9}
D2525 lacks its skull and parts of the vertebral column and was originally reported as S. dongi. The 2023 revision refers it to H. corollatus using its short first metacarpal, short fourth wing phalanx and metatarsal proportions. An approximately two-metre wingspan and extensive fusion support greater maturity. Skull-less material cannot independently confirm crest shape, referral does not combine it with the holotype into one complete skeleton, and its estimate is not a species maximum.

## Compare the two older holotypes before the other morphotype {#s-f12bab891e}
Compare the damaged-humerus reassessment and more advanced fusion in [H. benxiensis](../Huaxiapterus_benxiensis/page.en.md), then anatomy, growth methods and crests in [Sinopterus dongi](../../Sinopterus/Sinopterus_dongi/page.en.md). Possible temporal coexistence does not establish shared localities or dietary partitioning, and the published comparisons do not establish a diet for either species.

## Evidence limits {#s-aa529b86a4}
This guide combines selected PBDB occurrence records dated 2026-10-02 with the published 2023 revision and specimen comparisons. The database retains the older name Huaxiapterus corollatus while the revision refers holotype ZMNH M813 to Huaxiadraco corollatus; database name links do not settle specimen identification or every taxonomic dispute. Preservation gaps limit diagnosis and comparison: parts of ZMNH M813 are damaged or obscured, D2525 lacks its skull, and maturity estimates do not determine exact age or full adult size. Collection sites, broad stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation labels describe different things; none alone establishes complete range, origin or extinction time, ecology, abundance or the condition of every specimen.
