## *Xyris aurea*: a rounded head on a tall stalk {#s-8006ae2f55}
Its dark-brown leaf bases are strongly widened and may form ear-like lobes. A flower stalk rises 50–70 cm and carries a nearly spherical head with 10–25 bracts and a similar number of flowers. The evidence records free sepals and elliptical seeds.

The species is categorized as terrestrial.
