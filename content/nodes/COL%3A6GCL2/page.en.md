## A rounded cluster of many joined flower heads {#s-69fc477e65}
The record describes reddish-brown woolly stems and leathery, dark-green leaves that are sessile or shortly stalked. More than ten flower heads join into a rounded cluster, with some fusion among the heads. Each small, cone-shaped involucre has purple-tipped bracts and a single floret. The pubescent fruits retain a pappus arranged in three to five rows, with flattened outer scales. The habitat field says terrestrial.
