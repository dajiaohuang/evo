## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-73d0994c45}
The Hamada du Guir pterosaurs (BSPG) collection and Lamaaka collection are both recorded in Drâa-Tafilalet, Morocco (region code MA). PBDB gives Hamada du Guir an age bracket of 100.5–93.9 Ma, with Early Cenomanian as its early interval. Lamaaka spans 106.3–93.9 Ma, from Late Albian to Early Cenomanian. These two named collections are selected database records, not a complete map of the species’ distribution.
