## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-cef4279410}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) accepts Afrotapejara zouhri under the recorded parent Afrotapejara. This page follows two eligible species-level occurrence records, one from Hamada du Guir and one from Lamaaka. They represent two collections and share one reference identifier; those counts describe database records, not fossils or individual animals.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-73d0994c45}
The Hamada du Guir pterosaurs (BSPG) collection and Lamaaka collection are both recorded in Drâa-Tafilalet, Morocco (region code MA). PBDB gives Hamada du Guir an age bracket of 100.5–93.9 Ma, with Early Cenomanian as its early interval. Lamaaka spans 106.3–93.9 Ma, from Late Albian to Early Cenomanian. These two named collections are selected database records, not a complete map of the species’ distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-cdd7eed25e}
Across the two selected records, the outer age envelope is 106.3–93.9 Ma (Ma means millions of years ago). This envelope joins the oldest bound from Lamaaka to the youngest bound shared by the records; it does not show continuous presence throughout that span or directly date the species’ origin, extinction or any animal’s life. Each collection keeps its own bracket, and no more precise specimen age is supplied.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-ea43bf4905}
PBDB labels both collections “fluvial indet.” for environment and “body” for preservation mode. These are collection-level database descriptions. They do not by themselves establish where Afrotapejara lived or what it ate, nor do they describe the condition of every fossil in either collection.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-c7a099fea5}
Both collection records cite Martill et al. (2020) as database reference ref:72064. This shared attribution is a source link, not a claim that the cited work is the original species description or a complete author list. The database preserves separate collection and identification records; this page does not provide a specimen-by-specimen reassessment.

## Evidence limits {#s-7b001f6840}
This page summarizes the selected PBDB records available on 2026-10-02. It belongs to a fixed 276-entry pterosaur roster and includes species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. PBDB’s accepted name and recorded parent chain organize the entry; they do not resolve specimen identifications or every taxonomic dispute, and same-name Catalogue of Life concepts are kept separate. Localities, stratigraphic intervals, depositional settings and collection-level preservation labels describe different things. They do not establish the species’ complete range, exact origin or extinction, ecology, abundance or the condition of every specimen. The selected records do not form a full review of descriptions, revisions, morphology, diet or behavior.
