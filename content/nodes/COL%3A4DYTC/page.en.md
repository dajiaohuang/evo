## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Passiflora plumosa? {#s-f90e0ba4b1}
The somewhat woody stem has angles. Entire, hairless leaves have smooth, glandular margins and sessile petiole glands; the stipules are linear. Flowers may be solitary or racemose, with deltate bracts. Sepals lack awns, and the floral cup ranges from funnel-shaped to cylindrical. The corona has several rows; the operculum has a scalloped tip, and the limen is absent. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. Fruit and seed traits are recorded as unknown.
