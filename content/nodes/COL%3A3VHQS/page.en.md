## Accepted name and classification {#s-6ef12b4f4d}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Lithobates pustulosus* (Boulenger, 1883) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Lithobates (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## A source-listed native regional occurrence {#s-e29a4e58e6}
The 2025 checklist lists Rana pustulosa as native in the Sierra Madre Occidental biogeographic province of western Mexico. The authors compile and check state lists, museum-based information, VertNet and GBIF rather than newly surveying every species. Their regional scope crosses parts of Sonora, Chihuahua, Sinaloa, Durango, Zacatecas, Nayarit and Jalisco; it does not imply occurrence throughout all seven states or every habitat in the mountains. The record is attributed to the source taxonomy, not an independently examined voucher, density estimate or complete current range.

## Three conservation systems retain distinct dates and meanings {#s-d1b29a60d8}
The source records IUCN LC (Least Concern), with the full status/trend cell “LC (=)”; Environmental Vulnerability Score “L (9)”; and Mexican category Pr (subject to special protection). IUCN entries use version 2024-2 accessed in March 2025, while the authors describe the national list as a static 2010 source and EVS as expert-based vulnerability scoring. These are not newly verified current categories, measured local population trends or directly comparable risk probabilities.
