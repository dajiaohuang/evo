## Haworthia lockwoodii {#s-9bb95a5d79}
Haworthia lockwoodii has a compact rosette whose leaf tips dry and become papery during the resting season, withdrawing toward the ground. The regional account distinguishes this from its close relative H. mucronata by that response to stress. It records firm quartzite soils between large stones or under Karoo scrub, in an area receiving about 125–250 mm rain annually.
