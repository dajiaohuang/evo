## The name and this data account {#s-8dfb680c33}
Cacatua haematuropygia is accepted in COL26.8 within Cacatuidae. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 6 measured birds; 4 have a complete morphological set. Trait-specific sample sizes may still differ.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-7e28915add}
The mean beak length is 24.5 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip. At that edge, mean width is 17 mm and depth is 28 mm. These are separate measurements at defined landmarks, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-a33204e107}
Mean unflattened wing length from carpal joint to longest primary is 214.5 mm, and mean tarsus length is 23 mm. A separate source compilation gives mean mass as 288 g (Dunning category); the morphological specimens were not necessarily weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-13383e942c}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, Granivore trophic niche and Insessorial lifestyle. These are broad categories for comparison across species, not claims that the bird uses only one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-97147dad42}
The migration field classifies the record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from 4.61° to 15.06° latitude. They do not fully describe non-breeding movements or current global occupancy. The geographic and morphological data come from separate compilation steps.

## Evidence limits {#s-88af4e3cf7}
This page guides readers through historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages for the listed samples, not identification limits or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from separate literature or weighing sources, so the morphology sample counts do not apply to it. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories; the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges. A one-to-one historical crosswalk and matching catalogue name support the association but do not show that later taxonomic concepts are equivalent. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status need other sources.
