## Two habitat categories in the flora record {#s-005fbe9792}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record includes *Rupícola*, meaning growth on rock.

It also includes *Terrícola*, the dataset's terrestrial category.

These categories do not identify collection localities; the source field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## What the record does not show {#s-7e8355fa93}
The field does not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

These habitat categories do not describe life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
