## Astrocaryum campestre {#s-0d7de28a75}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records a stemless (acaulescent) plant with 5–15 leaves and about 20 leaflets per side, arranged in roughly the same plane. The upright inflorescence emerges between the leaves; 2–4 female flowers sit at the base of each flower branch. Fruits are obovoid to nearly spherical, smooth, and indehiscent. The habitat field records soil.
