## Stem surface {#s-aac449336c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record calls the stem glabrous, or hairless, and reports no simple hairs or hair direction. It nevertheless lists capitate glandular hairs, whose tips form small heads, without explaining how these descriptors relate. Unbranched bristles are thread-like; branched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-b32f00890f}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are caducous and fall early. Extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) are absent, while projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) are present. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; its floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-845430058f}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, is marked as lacking lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and are listed as hairless or pubescent, with short soft hairs; the source does not select one description. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-dd1a7cb5c9}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not identify a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
