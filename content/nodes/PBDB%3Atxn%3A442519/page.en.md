## Limits of the selected Tropeognathinae records {#s-e1cd7074a5}
This route presents selected PBDB occurrence guides, not every descendant or one ecological summary. Occurrences, collections and references are counted at separate database levels, not as animals, populations or sampling effort; an age envelope is not an origin or extinction date. PBDB parent paths organize the catalogue and differ from COL nomenclature and phylogenetic hypotheses. Figure captions separate preserved regions from reconstructions, but the records do not establish every anatomical detail or a subfamily-wide growth curve.
