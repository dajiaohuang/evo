## Could Ichthyostega use a walking gait? {#s-ad58f1a49e}
Micro-CT reconstructions of Ichthyostega’s shoulder and hip joints were compared with five living tetrapod analogues. The measured joint ranges lacked the limb long-axis rotations required for a typical lateral-sequence walk. This constrains possible movement but does not record actual gait; the result may reflect restricted early mobility or a specialization of Ichthyostega.
