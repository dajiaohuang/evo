## Molluscan phylogenomics: Monoplacophora with Cephalopoda {#s-7032ea8512}
Smith et al. added transcriptomes for 15 species to a matrix representing all major living molluscan groups. Their principal analyses placed Monoplacophora with Cephalopoda, challenging the Serialia grouping. Other matrices and studies recover different deep conchiferan relationships, so this is a result of that dataset rather than a settled molluscan tree.
