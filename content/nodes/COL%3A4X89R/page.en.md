## *Sida parva* {#s-a9e9dde381}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — covered with fine hairs; leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, saw-toothed margins, pointed tips; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem); flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
