## Hairy sepals and a rough fruit {#s-445882f2fc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records flower clusters at shoot tips. The corolla, meaning all the petals taken together, is white; hairs are present on the sepals, the flower's outer leaf-like parts, and on the fruit. The fruit surface bears small pointed bumps (muricate). This is a brief regional database description; it does not give sampling coverage or variation.
