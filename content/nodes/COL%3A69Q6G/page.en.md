## Nectar glands and an untwisted pod {#s-76f650f571}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records 12 to 16 pairs of oblong to linear leaflets on leaves arranged in two rows. Nectar glands occur outside the flowers on the leaf stalk. Yellow flowers have ten stamens and form small clusters beside the leaves. Unlike several related-looking profiles, this entry says the linear-oblong pod does not twist when mature; it measures 3–6.7 cm long and is hairy. Seed shape is marked unknown.
