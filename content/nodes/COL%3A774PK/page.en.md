## Periandra heterophylla {#s-4053758a73}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Periandra heterophylla* as an accepted species of *Periandra* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes stems with fine, silky, or velvety hairs; oblong, papery leaves without petioles; and red, short-haired flowers. The fruit is listed as straight, and the plant as terrestrial.
