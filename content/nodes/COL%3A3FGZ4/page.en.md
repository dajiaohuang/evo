## A single nodding blue flower per branch {#s-4309c5d14a}
Geissorhiza monticola is a 10–16 cm branched plant. Each branch bears one large, nodding blue flower, with exserted stamens leaning to one side; its perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts) is about 3 mm long and tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are 14–18 mm.

## Peaty pockets on sandstone {#s-681e31b61c}
The regional flora records sandstone slopes with pockets of peaty sand. No broader ecological survey is described.
