## What leaf, flower and fruit traits distinguish Mascagnia velutina? {#s-3e8d3f2a8a}
The elliptic, papery leaves are blistered, heart-shaped at the base and velvety below. The main flowering branch bears a solitary corymb; the peduncle is 1.5–8 mm long at flowering, and bracteoles attach at its tip. Petals are yellow, the style tip is hooked, and the fruit has developed dorsal and lateral wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-edcb47591b}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
