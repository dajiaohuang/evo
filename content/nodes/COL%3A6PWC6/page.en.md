## What traits are recorded for Licania savannarum? {#s-3fa114cc35}
Its elliptic to oblong-lanceolate leaves have a woolly, flour-like covering underneath, and the leaf stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in panicles; the receptacle is urn-shaped and the flowers are sessile. Each flower has three separate stamens that remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-766fa31155}
It is categorized as terrestrial; no specific site is named.
