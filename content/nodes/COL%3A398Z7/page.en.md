## Accepted name and placement {#s-8ca27e0cd5}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts *Eleutherodactylus alticola* Lynn, 1937 as a species in Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family) and *Eleutherodactylus* (genus). This is the name, rank and placement recorded in the pinned catalogue.

## Two source records, distinct concepts {#s-b8d113a19b}
Alongside the current catalogue entry, this page reports an AmphiBIO v1 row under the historical Frost 2011 source concept and one morphology v3 measurement row. The shared name does not establish that either deposited concept is equivalent to COL26.8; each trait below remains attached to its own source.

## A terrestrial foraging category {#s-3183acab03}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1816 records terrestrial foraging for its Frost 2011 *Eleutherodactylus alticola* concept. The broad category does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations, but this row has not been checked field by field. Keep the archived value provisional; no replacement is inferred, and the revision notice does not show that this row is wrong.

## Maximum adult length in AmphiBIO {#s-f631c03d0e}
The same AmphiBIO row gives a maximum adult body length of 26 mm. For Anura, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length (SVL), measured from the snout tip to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every individual; its Frost 2011 concept remains unmatched to COL26.8.

## Clutch and offspring entries {#s-f43fde2a3e}
AmphiBIO lists 41 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and 8 mm as both the minimum and maximum offspring-or-egg size. These are literature-compiled counts and extrema. The row does not tie each value to a sex, population, date or wild versus captive setting, and the size fields may refer to young or eggs.

## A direct-development label {#s-cd6d802538}
AmphiBIO assigns direct development to its Frost 2011 source concept. This category does not by itself describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing, and the historical concept has not been matched to COL26.8.

## What the morphology row contains {#s-a002d04bf4}
The morphology v3 archive links one measurement row, Anura.csv row 1640, to this name. Its family field is Brachycephaloidea, its sex field is NA, and four trait fields contain values: SVL 21, HW 9, ED 3 and HLL 29. Its family label and source concept remain separate from COL26.8 unless independently reconciled.
