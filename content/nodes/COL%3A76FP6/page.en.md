## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-767cc20333}
COL26.8 records Pelusios upembae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pelomedusidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-b6605fb601}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-e538445707}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 34.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
