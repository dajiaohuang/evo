## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Myzomela prawiradilagae Irham, Ashari, Suparno, Trainor, Verbelen, M. Y. Wu & Rheindt, 2019 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
