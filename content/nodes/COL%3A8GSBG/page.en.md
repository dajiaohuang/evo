## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a60d2881a5}
COL26.8 records Tupinambis zuliensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Teiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
