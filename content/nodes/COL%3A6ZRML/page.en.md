## Stevia camporum {#s-714b3fe1c6}
## Axillary heads and a crown-shaped pappus

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes opposite leaves with petioles. Dense, stalked heads are grouped in corymb-like clusters in the leaf axils. The dry fruits are described as both bristly and glandular; their pappus forms a small crown. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial. These regional fields do not provide a locality or complete range.
