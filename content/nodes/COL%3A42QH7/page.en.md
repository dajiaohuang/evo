## Micranthocereus albicephalus {#s-02e25a2351}
## A deeply set cephalium with golden bristles

The green stems bear erect branches in clusters, and their basal areoles lack secondary growth. The cephalium is recessed and lined with golden bristles. Flowers open in the afternoon; their outer surface is green and the perianth segments share one color. The fruit is fleshy, with brown remnants of the perianth, and the seed is spoon-shaped. The habitat is labeled rock-associated, not tied to a locality.
