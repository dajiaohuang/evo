## Form described in the regional flora account {#s-cdef2e92c7}
The SANBI regional species entry describes *Aponogeton desertorum* as a freshwater aquatic plant. Its rhizome (underground stem) is tuberous, with a globose (round) tuber 10–20 mm in diameter. The leaf blade is oblong-lanceolate with a heart-shaped base, and the flower stalk (scape) is swollen above. Flowers grow along long, cylindrical, tapered spikes that fork into two branches; flowers are arranged around the axis and are yellow.
