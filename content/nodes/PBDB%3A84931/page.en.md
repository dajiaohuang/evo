## An Early Jurassic Hildoceratidae matrix is an ammonitid case study {#s-f4e27573a5}
An integrated conch-character analysis resolves relationships within sampled Hildoceratidae, not an order-wide Ammonitida tree. Bardin and colleagues analysed a 105-taxon Early Jurassic matrix, supporting a 201.4–174.7 Ma period envelope for that sampled family. It does not represent the complete Ammonitida range or establish a Cretaceous extinction endpoint.
