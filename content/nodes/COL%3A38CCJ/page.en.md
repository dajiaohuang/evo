## A shrub that becomes cushion-shaped with age {#s-9f6f164ec6}
The regional SANBI account describes a shrub up to 300 mm tall that becomes cushion-shaped with age and is spiny because fruits abort or are shed. Its three-sided leaves are papillate, with small surface projections. The flowers lack petals and have numerous white, filamentous staminodes (sterile, stamen-like structures); the stamens are also white. The fruit has five chambers (five-locular), is 8–12 mm in diameter, and has hook-shaped closing bodies and small closing rodlets; its valve wings are very narrow. This regional account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequencies.

## The habitat field names a coastal setting {#s-eb5ec6070b}
The SANBI/WFO habitat field records “Coastal regions.” It supplies no observation localities, seasonality, or interactions, and does not establish the species’ full range.
