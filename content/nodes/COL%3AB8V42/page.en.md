## A family-rich molecular sample leaves gaps {#s-8a63dd03de}
An 18S–28S analysis included all 19 scyphozoan families recognized at the time. It supports several clades, but leaves other families or suborders unresolved or non-monophyletic, showing that some parts of the jellyfish tree remain difficult to place.

## Why the age display was removed {#s-21fd8650fc}
A phylogeny of living taxa does not establish that a diagnostic crown scyphozoan lived 505 million years ago. The former 505–0 Ma scalar range is therefore withheld; living relationships alone do not provide that fossil boundary.
