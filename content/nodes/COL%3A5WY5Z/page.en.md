## Yellow petals on an unbranched flower stalk

The flora record notes a long, creeping rhizome—an underground stem—and an unbranched flower stalk. Its yellow petals are under 4 cm long and reflex at the margins, and the flower has more than one staminode, a sterile stamen-like structure. The habitat field labels it terrestrial.
