## How these historical life-history values were assembled {#s-2b081b523e}
These authored records do not cover every accepted descendant or provide complete group biology. Species pages use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 same-name table; equivalence to current species concepts has not been established. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally give literature sources equal weight, regardless of sample size; inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and studies may be reused. Maximum lifespan is the longest record, not a typical value. This selection does not interpret adult mass or fill gaps from relatives. Original reference numbers remain attached, but the underlying species literature and every wild, captive, and locality condition were not independently established here.
