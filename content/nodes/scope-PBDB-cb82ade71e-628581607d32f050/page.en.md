## Three dental elements anchor the bear account {#s-df1aab6f6c}
Kretzoiarctos beatrix is described from left fourth premolar MNCN-CSIC NV-2-42, right first molar NV-2-40 and partial mandible IPS 46473 from two Spanish middle Miocene localities. A fossil-and-living bear matrix places the genus within Ailuropodinae; that placement is an analytical result.

## A bear represented by teeth and a partial jaw {#s-bf5e6c8742}
The account centers on holotype left P4 MNCN-CSIC NV-2-42, paratype right M1 NV-2-40 and partial mandible IPS 46473 from two Spanish localities. These named teeth and jaw define the described anatomical sample.

## A comparative placement, not a panda ancestor {#s-d0b747ab86}
A matrix combining living and fossil bears places Kretzoiarctos within Ailuropodinae. Dental comparisons suggest a more herbivorous tendency, but feeding was not observed. The sample does not identify a direct ancestor of the giant panda or prove identical ecology.
