## atlas-profile / overview {#s-df1aab6f6c}
Kretzoiarctos beatrix is described from a left fourth premolar, a right first molar and a partial mandible from two Spanish middle Miocene localities. A fossil-and-living bear matrix places the genus within Ailuropodinae.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-da829cad0a}
The named dental specimens and localities are direct records. Subfamily placement and a more herbivorous tendency come from comparative analyses; they do not establish direct ancestry, an origin centre or a global first appearance.

## A bear known from a small dental sample {#s-bf5e6c8742}
The account centers on holotype left P4 MNCN-CSIC NV-2-42, paratype right M1 NV-2-40 and partial mandible IPS 46473 from two Spanish localities. These teeth and jaw form the anatomical sample.

## A comparative placement, not an ancestor {#s-d0b747ab86}
A matrix combining living and fossil bears places Kretzoiarctos within Ailuropodinae. Dental comparisons suggest a more herbivorous tendency, but feeding was not observed. The sample does not identify a direct ancestor of the giant panda or prove identical ecology.
