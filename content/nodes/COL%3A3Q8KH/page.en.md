## *Isoetes amazonica*: basal wings and warted megaspores {#s-762c0dd964}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a three-lobed, globose stem and upright, needle-shaped leaves. In cross-section, the distal leaf is round or semicircular; a wing is present only at its base. A sporangium, the spore case, sits at the leaf base and has a rudimentary or absent velum, a thin covering.

The profile distinguishes large megaspores with three-rayed scars and wart-like surfaces from smaller microspores with a single scar; the microspore's outer coat bears spines on its distal surface. The habitat field says aquatic, but gives no named locality or date. These are regional morphology characters, not a distribution survey.
