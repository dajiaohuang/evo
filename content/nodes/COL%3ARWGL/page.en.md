## Cattleya nobilior {#s-4958592eb5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row describes Cattleya nobilior with clavate pseudobulbs, two elliptic to oblong leaves and one to three flowers in shades of pink and lilac. Its three-lobed lip has a long narrow middle section; the short side lobes do not wrap around the column. Habitat records include both epiphytic and rupicolous categories.
