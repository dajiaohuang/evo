## Accepted name and placement {#s-d3f9937468}
COL26.8 accepts Centrolene ericsmithi Cisneros-Heredia, Yánez-Muñoz, Sánchez-Nivicela & Ron, 2023 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Centrolenidae (family) → Centrolene (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A very small male type sample {#s-0e77ce5d5c}
The description rests on adult male holotype DHMECN 11406, collected at La Enramada on 31 March 2015, with snout–vent length 27.3 mm. Females are unknown in this account. The diagnosis combines absent dentigerous vomerine processes, a rounded snout in both views, dorsal spicules, a male humeral spine and low enamelled limb folds. A single male does not establish a size range, population variation, female morphology or a complete identification key.

## Living and ethanol colours remain distinct {#s-6c75b758ca}
The live holotype has a bright uniform green back, a thin yellowish lip stripe followed by a row of white tubercles towards the arm, and a yellowish line from the arm insertion to the groin. Its bones are green and iris has a flesh-coloured background with fine brown reticulation. In ethanol the back is lavender. These observations belong to one specimen and do not establish invariant colours or female patterns.

## Heard calls were not recorded or described {#s-5ebdb76e85}
Both new species vocalized during March 2015 sampling, but no recordings were taken. The diagnoses place calling males on the upper surface of leaves while explicitly leaving advertisement-call properties, fighting, eggs, parental care and tadpoles unknown. Humeral spines and nuptial structures do not fill those missing observations. Neither one month of calling nor the absence of recordings establishes a complete breeding season.

## A single stream site and later nondetections {#s-43a8c35c30}
The type locality is La Enramada in Azuay, southwestern Ecuador, with the type account at 2950 m and the forest/fieldwork account at 2900 m. These source statements remain separate, not a certified continuous elevation range. The two species occurred in the same steep creek in montane evergreen forest. Only the March 2015 expedition collected them; April 2019 and December 2022 surveys did not. Those nondetections do not prove extinction, absence in other seasons or a complete range boundary.

## Different relatives despite a shared site {#s-6cdde54fef}
The study places ericsmithi sister to a clade containing sabini and an unnamed Ecuadorian lineage, MRy 547. Reported 12S distances are 2.9% to sabini (standard error 0.549%) and 3.7% to the unnamed lineage (standard error 0.709%). Standard error is not standard deviation. The geographically distant relationship and proposed reproductive barriers are interpretations of the sampled topology, not direct measurements of mating or gene flow.

## The 2023 Data Deficient proposal {#s-c34b9b0269}
The authors propose Data Deficient for both new species after limited records and later survey nondetections. They discuss regional forest loss, degradation and fragmentation as concerns. This is a dated author proposal, not a newly verified official IUCN category or measured decline in these species. Three type specimens across two species do not constitute a population census or sufficient monitoring of trends.
