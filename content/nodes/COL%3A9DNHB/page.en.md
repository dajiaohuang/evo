## Tachigali odoratissima {#s-c37205e531}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali odoratissima* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records leaf-like stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), cylindrical domatia (small cavities) on the petiole, and up to 10 leaflet pairs. Its flowers exceed 10 mm and have densely hairy petals; each flower has 10 stamens. The habitat field lists the plant as terrestrial.
