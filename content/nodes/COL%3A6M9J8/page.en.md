## A hairy, many-flowered spike {#s-98adbb5a27}
In the SANBI regional account, H. villosa is a slender, tuberous plant up to 36 cm tall. Its flowering stalk is silky-hairy; two hairy, oval leaves grow at the base. Many green flowers form a dense raceme, and the lip is spurred and divided into three to five lobes. These details describe the regional flora account rather than the full range of variation in the species.

## Rocky sandstone and granite ground {#s-d4f4a03e58}
The same account records H. villosa on sandstone and granite slopes and pavements. It does not give localities or establish how broadly this habitat description applies.
