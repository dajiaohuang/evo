## Flowers {#s-3556e48c2b}
The SANBI account describes a corm-bearing herb up to 0.6 m tall. The lance-shaped perianth segments form a cup and are more than half as long as the 30–50 mm tube. The source excerpt also records “Flowers 4–6 cm a lax spike”; because the relation of this measurement to the flower or spike is unclear, the value is retained without assigning it to either. Flowers are described as bright scarlet.
