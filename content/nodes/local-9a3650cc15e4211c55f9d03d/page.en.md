## The source tadpole concept {#s-fc6cb7b04b}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Mannophryne herminae, in the genus Mannophryne and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying work is also Mannophryne herminae. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-c51449593d}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images; its supplied developmental-stage label is 44. That label belongs to a source record, not a full developmental timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
