## A fossil sample and a living topology {#evidence-bridge}
The Whippomorpha route combines two different records: an approximately 48.6 Ma *Indohyus* sample from Sindkhatudi, with morphology and isotope evidence, and retroposon evidence for relationships among living whales and hippos. The rounded 55 Ma ceiling is a navigation bound; neither source dates the Whippomorpha crown or a continuous lineage duration.
