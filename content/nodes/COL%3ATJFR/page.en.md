## ## Two pairs of notched leaflets {#s-5a2bc70f3e}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium and two pairs of obovate leaflets. Their rounded tips are notched and end in a short mucro, while the bases are asymmetrical. The yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. At maturity, the fruit valves twist and bear bristly or villous hairs. Seed shape is listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
