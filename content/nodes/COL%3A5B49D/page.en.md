## Leathery leaves and glandular fruits {#s-c7f0b69082}
The record describes upright stems that may be smooth or hairy and bear glands. Leathery, stalked, elliptic leaves have pointed or mucronate tips and narrowing bases; their surfaces vary in hair cover and carry glands. The heads contain 8–22 florets with smooth, glandless corollas. The fruits are hairy and glandular, and the pappus has flattened outer bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
