## The deposited caecilian record {#s-0e030cd1a3}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis nokrekensis in species-level row 140, under genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. Individual, population and species data are kept separate in the compilation. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-dd4788aca0}
For this source concept, CaecilianTraits records broad habitat as Terrestrial|Freshwater (=Inland waters) and microhabitat as Fossorial. These categorical labels summarize the compilation and do not specify the frequency of habitat use or a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-9338328239}
The deposited reproduction field lists the category as oviparous. The accompanying metadata uses a presumed, probable or expected interpretation; this is retained as a qualified compilation statement.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-cec8900ec9}
In the deposited record, the aquatic-larvae field notes presence of a water-dependent larval stage. This is the compilation category described in the associated data paper, not a statement that adults always inhabit water. The accompanying reproductive metadata and original reference must be consulted for observational scope and qualifications.
