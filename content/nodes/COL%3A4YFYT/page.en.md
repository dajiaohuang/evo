## Measurements and flower structure {#s-4bbf4023a8}
The leaf petiole (stalk) is shorter than 20 cm and less than 1.2 times the blade length; the blade is more than 6 times as long as it is wide. The field records fewer than 9 secondary veins on each side. It gives the peduncle-to-petiole comparison as both “less than 2 times” and “more than 2 times,” without specifying which applies to which material; separately, peduncle length is less than 1.9 times the blade length. The spathe remains attached when mature. The cylindrical spadix is longer than 1 cm, and its length is less than 5 times the stipe length.

The style does not extend above the perianth (the floral envelope around the reproductive parts) and is truncate or nearly truncate at its tip. The tepals, the undifferentiated perianth segments, are free at the tip or entirely free.
