## Form in the flora record {#s-a5870f39ff}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a boat-shaped spathe. The female part of the spadix is partly fused to the spathe. The connective is red, and the synandrium is boat-shaped. The staminodes are fused together and are short, reaching at most half the ovary length. The triangular stigma lobes lie parallel to the inflorescence axis and have entire tips.

The regional dataset row cites Calazans and Valadares' 2020 Asterostigma treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
