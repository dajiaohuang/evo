## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Stigmaphyllon matogrossense? {#s-73e05547ab}
Smooth branches bear leaf stalks 12.5–60 mm long, with a stalkless disk gland near the tip. The blades are undivided, densely woolly below, and have entire, glandless margins. Flower clusters form umbels arranged in a dichasium and carry 15 to many flowers. The petals are yellow with fringed margins; anthers are hairless. The styles are hairy, with front styles shorter than the others and expanded at the tip; the stigma points toward the flower center. The fruit has a developed dorsal wing and one pair of lateral winglets.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b51c809366}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
