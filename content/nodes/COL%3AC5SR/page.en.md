## What traits are recorded for Aloysia oblanceolata? {#s-d69901ce52}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes unstalked leaves arranged in whorls around the stem, with smooth, un-toothed edges. Flowers occur only in the leaf axils. The calyx has four teeth at its tip.

## What habitat category is given? {#s-146f46053a}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial; no particular locality is named.
