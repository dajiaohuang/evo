## Leaf surfaces, keel length and seed appendage {#s-bcc6b176b2}
The source entry lists petioles as 2–4 mm or longer than 4 mm. Leaves are described as membranaceous or erect; both adaxial and abaxial blade surfaces are glabrous or sparsely puberulous. Extrafloral nectaries are present. Floral buds have rounded apices; the keel is 6–10 mm long and flowers are white. The seed appendage, a caruncle, is not swollen, and the seed is subtriangular. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or range.
