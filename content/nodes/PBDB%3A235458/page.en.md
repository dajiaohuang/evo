I visited this area in 1971 and many of them are still recognizable and a few are currently being worked. Unfortunately neither Rutimeyer (1873) nor Bram (1965) have used quarry numbers to indicate specimen localities and I have been unable to find information of this sort in the Solothurn Museum catalogue. Rutimeyer and Bram both said, however, that many of the specimens were found by quarry workmen who brought the specimens to the museum, and it is quite possible that the specific quarry data was not retained.
