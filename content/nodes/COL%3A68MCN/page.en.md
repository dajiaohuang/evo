## Five veins and a berry-like fruit {#s-a14b682ac8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes petiolate leaves with obovate to nearly round blades. Their tips range from rounded to abruptly tapering, the bases are rounded, and the margins are entire or wavy. Five main veins arise above the base and run in arching paths toward the tip. Flowers grow in leaf axils, with two pairs of bracts set at right angles. The flower cup is bell-shaped; triangular calyx lobes taper to points. Petals are obovate. The anthers are described as equal-sized, oval to nearly round, rounded at the tip and purple. The ovary is fully joined to the flower cup; the same source field adds “6 loculicida” without defining that term. Fruit is recorded as berry-like and the seeds as egg-shaped. These details are limited to the listed source field.
