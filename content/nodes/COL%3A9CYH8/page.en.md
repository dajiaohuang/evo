## ## A single leaflet pair and nectar glands {#s-bb12c57f03}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium and one pair of leaflets on alternate leaves arranged in two ranks. Extrafloral nectar glands occur on the petiole and inflorescence. The inflorescences are described as corymb-like or axillary racemes, usually with more than eight flowers. Yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. The fruit valves twist at maturity; the oblong fruit is glabrous, and the depressed seed is quadrangular to rhomboid. This regional character list does not describe population coverage.
