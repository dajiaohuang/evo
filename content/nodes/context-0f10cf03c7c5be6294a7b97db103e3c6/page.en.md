## The source concept {#s-9f249a79f7}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Leiopelma archeyi in row Anf3264, under the genus Leiopelma, family Leiopelmatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; they do not establish that it matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-6df68a91f5}
This row marks terrestrial and aquatic foraging as present categories. These labels are broad and do not describe changes across seasons or life stages. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not yet been checked against those changes. Treat its categories as provisional historical values: no replacement is supplied here, and the revision does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-1a7ab5a644}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines adult diet categories reported from expert judgement, observations, or stomach contents. A listed category does not give a complete diet or a measured share of food.

## Activity periods {#s-245d37a531}
The row records night as an activity category. This summarizes a reported period of activity; it is not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-517d98d0ef}
The compiled maximum adult body length is 38 mm. For Anura, AmphiBIO measures body length from the tip of the snout to the vent (snout–vent length); this is a reported maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-624873af85}
The row reports sexual maturity at 3–4 years and a maximum lifespan of 17 years. It gives 4–13 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 4.5–8 mm. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts; the row does not specify the sex, population, date, or wild versus captive conditions behind each value. Size entries may describe eggs or offspring.

## Developmental strategy {#s-551113cf28}
The deposited row records direct development. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or the timing of reproduction.
