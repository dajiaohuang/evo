## *Austroeupatorium petrophilum*: opposite leaves and white funnel-shaped flowers {#s-c3122a82e1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records cylindrical, hairy stems and opposite, stalked, egg-shaped leaves with a thin, papery texture. Bell-shaped heads form corymb-like clusters. The bracts around each head are not overlapping; the corolla is white and funnel-shaped. Its fruitlet is prism-shaped and glandular, with a distinct ring-shaped attachment base. These are field entries in a regional dataset, not a full species-wide account.
