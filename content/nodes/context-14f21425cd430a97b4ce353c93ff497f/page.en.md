## AmphiBIO species record {#s-cd6f27c3db}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Pristimantis pharangobates? Row Anf5793 places it in genus Pristimantis, family Strabomantidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging categories in this record {#s-ea0fac6ed8}
The AmphiBIO row lists these broad foraging categories: terrestrial (land) and arboreal (trees or other vegetation). They do not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat categories carried over from earlier compilations, but does not identify whether this row was affected. These remain the AmphiBIO labels; no replacement category is inferred.

## Activity periods in the compilation {#s-2d21bf78d8}
The AmphiBIO row classifies activity as night. These are broad time-of-day categories, not a continuous record of behaviour.
