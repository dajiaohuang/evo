## Stolons, cleistogamy and flower characters {#s-92f816863f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records stoloniferous stems and digitately trifoliolate leaves. Bracts are present, and flower counts are listed as up to 25 or more than 20. Cleistogamy is recorded as present. Flowers are pink or red; the calyx is not accrescent or urceolate, and the standard is smooth. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields retain both flower count descriptions and do not establish variation or a complete range.
