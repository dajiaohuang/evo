## What body and fruit traits are listed? {#s-409ca70b43}
The entry lists roots as absent and describes prostrate, branched stems. It allows that tepals may be present or absent and records two stamens. The fruit surface is described as smooth or ribbed. These are the alternatives recorded in this regional dataset row.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-ab1e66e2ba}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as aquatic and gives no locality.
