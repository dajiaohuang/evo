## *Notobubon galbanum*: a many-rayed yellow umbel and toothed leaflets {#s-06d056ed87}
The cited South African regional account describes a shrub, from slender to robust, with few branches and a height up to 2.5 m. Its twice-divided leaves are paler or bluer underneath; the leaflets are toothed, rhombus- to egg-shaped, and sometimes three-lobed. More than 30 rays carry yellow flowers in a large, rounded compound umbel, an umbrella-like branching flower cluster.

The elliptic to rounded fruit is compressed from front to back and narrowly winged at its margins. The account distinguishes this plant from *N. galbaniopse* by its very large many-rayed primary umbel and regularly toothed leaflets; its leaf margins are not rolled under. Rocky sandstone slopes in forests and bush are the cited habitat, not a complete range survey.
