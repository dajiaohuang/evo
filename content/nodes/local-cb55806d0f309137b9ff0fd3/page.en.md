## The deposited caecilian record {#s-e639e41764}
The fourth version of CaecilianTraits lists Boulengerula taitana in the genus Boulengerula and family Herpelidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. Whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept has not been established.

## Measurement material {#s-6ab3b73e6e}
The compilation lists four measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of four distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-2bc026ddf5}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-7ba3a5c33c}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-549d1409a7}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as absent. The associated source description calls development direct and says reproduction does not depend on water bodies; the dataset category still does not mean adults never use water. The reproductive metadata and original reference provide the scope and qualifications for these statements.

## Compiled clutch counts {#s-81caf74201}
The source species record lists a minimum clutch size of 2, a maximum of 9 and a mean of 5. These values come from cited reproductive accounts and describe the compiled source material, not universal limits or annual reproductive output. The associated reproductive metadata remains part of their interpretation.
