## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-b5b5915328}
Rhamphocephalus bucklandi is the accepted species name in these PBDB records, with Rhamphocephalus as the recorded parent. Five selected occurrences come from two collections and carry three reference identifiers. Two records connect original identifications or combinations to the accepted name; a recombination is not a new discovery.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-9c85fe2758}
Shotover Hill, Oxford Clay (UK) is bracketed at 161.5–154.8 Ma (Oxfordian); Stonesfield Slate quarries (UK) at 168.2–165.3 Ma (Middle Bathonian). The recorded strata are Oxford Clay and Taynton Limestone, respectively. The two collection histories remain separate; these records are not a complete species distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-2d178fa0f3}
The selected records span an outer envelope of 168.2–154.8 Ma, formed from their oldest and youngest age bounds. This does not mean continuous presence through the interval or establish species origin, extinction or an individual lifespan.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-dc621c3825}
Shotover Hill is labelled “marine indet.” and Stonesfield Slate quarries “marginal marine indet.”; both collections carry the preservation label “body.” These are collection-level database descriptors, not direct reconstructions of diet or living habitat.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-9d4be7ec1a}
The database attributes Shotover Hill to Phillips (1871), ref:23773, and Stonesfield Slate quarries to Owen (1874), ref:30979. These abbreviated attributions do not necessarily name the original species descriptions.
