## The deposited caecilian record {#s-1b7854c3ad}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Microcaecilia iwokramae in species-level row 192, under genus Microcaecilia and family Siphonopidae. Individual, population and species data are kept separate in the compilation. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Measurement material {#s-01b491077a}
This source concept has 1 measurement row: 1 individual-level row, 0 population-level rows and 0 species-level rows. These counts describe table rows; they are not population estimates or counts of unique specimens. Specimen identifiers, sex codes and references remain attached to their rows. Comparisons of body dimensions need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-2d39ec7e0f}
For this source concept, CaecilianTraits records broad habitat as Terrestrial and microhabitat as Fossorial. These categorical labels summarize the compilation and do not specify the frequency of habitat use or a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-b53457c5e6}
The deposited reproduction field lists the category as oviparous. This category does not specify parental care, the timing of reproduction or population variation.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-3a5150fa12}
The species-level row lists primary fold count: 102; secondary fold count: 9. These are deposited species-level values, which may be summaries rather than integer measurements of one animal; they do not establish invariant diagnostic counts for every individual.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-f8fba075bd}
In the deposited record, the aquatic-larvae field notes absence of a water-dependent larval stage. This is the compilation category described in the associated data paper, not a statement that adults never use water. The accompanying reproductive metadata and original reference must be consulted for observational scope and qualifications.
