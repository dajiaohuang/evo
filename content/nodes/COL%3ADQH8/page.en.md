## ## White petals and a yellow fruit exterior {#s-8a2e3394ea}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 entry, the lower leaflet surface is hairy while the upper surface is glabrous; the row lists two to five leaflet pairs. Axillary or terminal inflorescences have reddish-brown hairs, and the flowers are recorded with white petals and a hairy ovary. The fruit has a yellow outer layer and a woody, fibrous inner layer; one or two seeds are listed. The habitat category is “terrestrial,” without a linked locality or survey date, so it does not establish a geographic range.
