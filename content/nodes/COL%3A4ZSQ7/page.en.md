## How are the stems, leaves, and flowers arranged? {#s-49f9467c7b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright stems and unstalked leaves whose bases clasp the stem. Flowers form slender, spike-like clusters in the leaf axils. Their flower stalks reach about 2 mm; small bracteoles are present. The flowers are not twisted, and the capsule opens along its internal partitions or walls.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-ba130181e8}
The same dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a specific site.
