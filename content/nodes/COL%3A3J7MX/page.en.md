## Leaves and flowers {#s-636b319378}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte and gives a 5–20 cm measurement without specifying the dimension. It records two leaves, dry at flowering, held partly upright, narrowly strap-shaped, channelled, and twisted. Red bars occur near the base on the underside. The flowers spread slightly and range from pink to white. Four to six thin-textured bracts range from pale to dark pink.
