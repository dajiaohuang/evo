## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton lechleri? {#s-a43d8227d5}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. The long-stalked leaves are oval-lanceolate or heart-shaped, with star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and entire margins. A sessile or nearly sessile nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; leafy stipules are present at young shoot tips. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and shallowly bell-shaped; female flowers are stalked, with separate sepals and a two-lobed style. The calyx lobes are alike, female petals are absent, and the bud arrangement is valvate. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
