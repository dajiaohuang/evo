## Long, loose inflorescences {#s-1670551369}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records a twining growth form. Its leaflets are described as membranous, papery or leathery, with tips ranging from sharply pointed to gradually tapering into a longer point (acute to acuminate). Leaflets on the same plant are alike in shape, while their covering is described as glabrous to hairy. The inflorescence axis is longer than 4.5 cm and the inflorescence is lax; flowers exceed 2 cm. The dataset does not assess how these traits vary across the species.
