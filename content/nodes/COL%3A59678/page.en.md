## Trixis forzzae {#s-9ce0fe6200}
## Yellow corymbs above non-waxy leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes stems with solid rather than hollow interiors and no winged branches. Leaf surfaces are not varnished. Corymbs bear yellow flowers with hairless corolla surfaces. The pappus is recorded as cream or white; no habitat category is supplied in the cited fields.
