## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima euryphylla? {#s-22097a90f8}
The lower leaf surface is hairless. Each flower cluster has one flower. Its flower stalk may be stalked or sessile (without a stalk), and the petals are pink or red. Anthers are hairless. Fruit hairs were not observed, so the record does not establish whether the fruit is hairy.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f8b13a0de9}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
