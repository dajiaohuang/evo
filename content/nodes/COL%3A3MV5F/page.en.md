## The flower colors and loose inflorescence {#s-1308b37ce4}
The regional description records erect plants 150–400 mm tall, with spaced leaves: the lower blades measure 60–120 by 45–70 mm and the upper leaves are smaller. A loose inflorescence bears 6–20 flowers. The sepals are lime-green and reflexed; the petals are pale mauve, while the pendent lip is transversely oval. These are characters from a South African regional account; the excerpt does not report population-level variation.
