## Callulops fojaensis: a voice from a burrow {#primary-intro}
Two adult males from wet montane forest in the Foja Mountains formed the original sample of Callulops fojaensis. Their short limbs, slightly widened digit tips and barking calls linked an anatomical description with observations at forest-floor burrows.

## Short limbs and widened digit tips {#primary-body}
The two described males measured 51.9 and 53.0 mm in snout–vent length, from snout to cloacal opening. Their finger and toe tips were slightly expanded into discs; toe discs were wider than finger discs. The holotype had a robust, pear-shaped body and short limbs. This sample provides no measurements for females or juveniles.

## Calling from forest-floor burrows {#primary-habitat}
Both males were located calling from burrows in the forest floor in late afternoon, in wet, mossy forest at approximately 1600 m elevation. The original account knew the species only from this Foja Mountains site. Two encounters cannot establish population abundance.

## Comparing a brown-bodied frog {#primary-comparison}
The authors considered it most similar to the nominal taxon C. robustus, but distinguished it from topotypic material using smaller male size and smoother skin. This treatment’s comparison and taxonomic usage do not establish a molecular phylogeny.

## Two calls from one male {#primary-study}
The holotype, MZB Amph. 11958, supplied two complete recorded calls: six notes in 3.3 seconds and seven in 3.4 seconds. Figure 3B identifies a recording temperature of 19 °C. These barking sequences describe one male’s recordings, not a species-wide range of vocal behaviour.

## Accepted name and placement {#s-ea5f8f7dc7}
COL26.8 accepts Callulops fojaensis Oliver, Richards & Tjaturadi, 2012 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Callulops (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Scope of this partial account {#s-ad9dcb720f}
This partial account combines the primary description with the reported observations of two adult males and recordings from the holotype. Those measurements, calling observations, and call sequences remain bounded to the described specimens and Foja locality. The treatment’s comparison with nominal C. robustus is not a sampled sister relationship; females, juveniles, abundance, and a molecular phylogeny are not established here.
