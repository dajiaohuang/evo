## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-1e7b30737d}
Turdoides bicolor is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Leiothrichidae. This page compares the same-name historical life-history entry using 4 focal fields, each supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows with matching name and family. Reported summaries can still include historical taxonomic inheritance; a matching row name does not remove that influence.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-28bf830e7e}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 74.35 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-90e26e0e21}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 3.25 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 16 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-478dadc88d}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 16.5 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-a690ceafe6}
Same-name sparse rows carry source labels including Dunning, 1992; Szekely, Lislevand, and Figuerola, 2007; Bennett, 1986; Jetz, Sekercioglu, and Bohning-Gaese, 2008, among others. This batch’s archive retains field-specific raw row numbers, values and labels, plus the final table’s complete reference explanations. A label may represent a primary paper, book or other database. Row counts are not numbers of measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-304cd8c279}
This batch uses historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are reported compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found. They are not direct measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the presence of same-name sparse rows support the association here; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components from the reported summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed here, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed.
