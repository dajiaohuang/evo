## ## Yellow petals and red stamens {#s-95db6e5da2}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records alternate, bipinnate leaves, in which leaflets are arranged along paired side axes. Terminal racemes bear five-part, bilaterally symmetric flowers arranged around a bell-shaped floral cup. The petals are yellow and overlap one another. Ten free stamens are red and longer than the petals; their anthers attach on the back. The fruit is a compressed legume that opens at maturity, with woody, glandular valves. These are regional character fields, not a population survey.
