## Melocactus longicarpus {#s-a4419a4e17}
## Long lower radial spines and elongated fruits

Brazilian Flora 2020 records a conical stem with white pith, dark-green skin and angular ribs. The straight spines number up to five; central spines exceed 1 cm, and the lower radial spines exceed 4 cm. The cephalium is longer than wide, with conspicuous bristles but no brown tuft. Flowers are reddish, with up to 23 perianth segments. The magenta-and-white fruit is more than twice as long as its diameter; seeds are at most 1.5 mm with a convex coat. The habitat label is rocky, without a named locality.
