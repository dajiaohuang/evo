## Mimosa setuligera {#s-4b9eec1c65}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa setuligera* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes a hairy to velvety stem, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and globose flower clusters. Flowers are recorded with three perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairy petal lobes; the entry also identifies the plant as terrestrial.
