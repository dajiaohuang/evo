## *Eryngium ekmanii*: papillose fruit backs in an upright flower cluster {#s-280943fd59}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field describes stalked leaves with net-like veins. The flower cluster stands upright, while individual flowers attach directly to its axis without stalks. The fruit is likewise stalkless and does not split open at maturity; the back of each mericarp, a fruit segment, is covered in small raised papillae.

The habitat field records terrestrial substrate only, without a locality or date. These are regional character descriptions, not population-level measurements.
