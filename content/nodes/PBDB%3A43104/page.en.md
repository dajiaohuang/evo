## Tapiroidea in the PBDB record path {#s-14ad5b2941}
PBDB records Tapiroidea at superfamily rank and this selected route links to 75 species occurrence guides. Its parent chain organizes database records; it does not establish relationships or evolutionary importance. The route contains selected non-extant fossil records, not a complete account of the group.

## Occurrences from 373 named collections {#s-42eea3ed9d}
Selected record pages include Irdinolophus tuiensis, Bahinolophus birmanicus, Deperetella cristata, D. depereti, and Hesperaletes borineyi. The guide contains 406 occurrences from 373 collections; Kholboldchi, Hedi, Heti, and Pondaung are example stratum names. Each occurrence has its own age bracket and identification qualifications. The totals describe database records, not specimens or populations.

## What the occurrence route does not establish {#s-bf0f037312}
This PBDB selection is not a complete descendant catalogue or ecological summary. Occurrence and collection totals do not measure specimens, individuals, populations, or sampling intensity, and pooled outer ages do not give the group’s actual origin or extinction. The database classification path remains separate from COL and phylogenetic hypotheses.

## Tapiroidea topology differs between analyses {#s-9c6d88e4a6}
The study’s most-parsimonious trees recover sampled Tapiroidea as monophyletic after excluding paraphyletic “isectolophids,” while Bayesian resolution differs. This is a bounded result about sampled relationships, not a universal membership list or first appearance. The cited study does not support a global older bound of 55 Ma, so no numerical range is shown.
