## How to read reproductive records {#s-523313db39}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, a consolidated mean of 15 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, and 3 litters or clutches per year. A clutch/litter summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The annual count is a dataset summary, not a guarantee for every individual or population. These fields do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
