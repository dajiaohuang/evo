## Overall and female maximum SVL {#s-8d34f99a78}
The maximum snout–vent length is 98 mm; the female maximum is 86.4 mm. SVL measures from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. These are compiled maxima, not typical sizes, and the two sex-specific values may come from different samples or sites rather than one matched comparison.

## The activity category is nocturnal {#s-0737306773}
The source records active time as nocturnal. It does not give a daily timetable or show how activity varies by season or locality. No microhabitat or diet value is interpreted here, so the record does not identify where the gecko is active or what it eats.

## Oviparity is reported; clutch size remains open {#s-c063af5f5a}
The reproductive mode is labelled oviparous, but there is no supported clutch-size mean or range in this account. Egg number and annual clutch frequency therefore remain unspecified; neither can be inferred from the mode label.
