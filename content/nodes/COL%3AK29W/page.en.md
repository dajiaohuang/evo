## Classification {#s-d0bfa292c4}
The 2000 western-Avahi study examined geographic and pelage variation in wild subjects and museum skins and described A. unicolor from the Ampasindava region. A later genus-level comparison included western Avahi in mitochondrial and morphological analyses; the inferred sister relationship of A. cleesei varied between sequence fragments. The data support a study history, not a complete current range or a final resolution of all western species boundaries.

## Read this historical record through food and foraging position {#s-cfb4a628fe}
Avahi unicolor is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Indriidae. The EltonTraits 1.0 record is linked by historical name and family under earlier bird or MSW3 mammal taxonomy. That association does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept or resolve later splits and synonymies.
