## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-8377a141f5}
COL26.8 records Panaspis namibiana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-4d3281e57f}
The row reports 5 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015.
