## How the food percentages should be read {#s-00a9931d58}
The archive assigns 50% to other plant material, 30% to seeds, nuts and spores, and 20% to invertebrates; the broad plant category includes fungi. These percentages standardize descriptions in the literature rather than measure a diet sample. ABC combines high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and an estimate may draw on a congener considered ecologically similar. It therefore does not establish direct observation of this species. Zero weight is not proof of never eating an item, and labels such as Nect, Seed, and PlantO have broader definitions than their everyday wording.

## Limits of the ecological record {#s-46986af28c}
EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete species account; its historical concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse weights standardized from published descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity entries may use a congener, and this page does not claim that every field is a direct observation of P. chapmani. Genus/family typical values, avian strata lacking species-level support, and split records needing individual review do not fill the account. Source works have not been reread; local, seasonal, and individual variation, current range, population, and conservation require other evidence.
