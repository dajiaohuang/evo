## The deposited caecilian record {#s-84c312779e}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Caecilia inca on its species-level data sheet (row 21), under genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. Its mapping to a current COL26.8 concept remains unresolved.

## Measurement material {#s-48e9f861b8}
The source lists 1 measurement row for this concept: 1 at individual level, 0 at population level and 0 at species level. These are spreadsheet rows, not counts of distinct animals. Identifiers, sex codes and references remain attached where supplied. The deposited column definitions do not establish what each measurement field represents or its units; check them before comparing values.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-31d53b0cd6}
The species row assigns the broad habitat to terrestrial and the microhabitat to fossorial (burrowing-associated). These are compilation categories; they do not measure how often the species uses a habitat or define its full geographic range.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-e4affe59f1}
The species-level row lists primary fold count: 158; no secondary fold count is recorded in this row. These are compiled species-row values and may be summaries rather than counts from one animal; they do not establish a fixed value for every individual.
