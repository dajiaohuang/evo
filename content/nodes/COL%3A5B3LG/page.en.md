## Verdoornianthus griffinii: two habitat labels {#s-92f1730e5f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this taxon:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Epífila” (leaf-associated).

These labels are from two regional dataset rows, with no collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage supplied.
