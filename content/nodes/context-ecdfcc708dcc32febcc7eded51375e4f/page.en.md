## The source concept {#s-09535c64dd}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Amietophrynus danielae* as row Anf0362, in the genus Amietophrynus, family Bufonidae, and order Anura. The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Developmental strategy {#s-422a159906}
