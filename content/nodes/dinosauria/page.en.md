## A route assembled from different samples {#s-58ff2d6cf7}
This atlas route links distinct fossil samples to living birds. A Late Triassic skeleton preserves parts of a skull and limbs; three Early Cretaceous Yutyrannus skeletons preserve filamentous integument. Those records answer different anatomical questions, while the route’s displayed 233.2–0 Ma span is a navigation composite rather than a continuous occurrence record.

## The Brazilian skeleton at about 233.2 Ma {#s-1094661cd5}
Holotype ULBRA-PVT280 comes from an approximately 233.2 Ma assemblage in the Candelária Sequence of southern Brazil. It preserves a partial skull, axial skeleton, forelimb and nearly complete left hind limb. This local sample establishes what was present at that site and time; it does not fix a global first appearance or make Buriolestes a direct ancestor of later dinosaurs.

## Two analyses recover competing early trees {#s-88a47c6a21}
One analysis of 74 taxa and 457 characters grouped Theropoda with Ornithischia as Ornithoscelida. A published reanalysis changed character scores and added taxa, recovering the traditional ornithischian–saurischian split. These are outcomes of different matrices: coding and sampling change the inferred early branches, and neither analysis directly observes ancestry.

## Filaments in three Yutyrannus skeletons {#s-96acff462b}
Three nearly complete Yutyrannus skeletons from the Lower Cretaceous Yixian Formation preserve long filamentous integument. They document those structures in this tyrannosauroid sample only; they do not establish full-body coverage, the filaments’ function, or the integument of every dinosaur lineage.
