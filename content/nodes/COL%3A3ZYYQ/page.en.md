## Mesembryanthemum neofoliosum {#s-3ed23c829f}
## White flowers top densely opposite branches

Bolus’s 1929 description depicts a densely branched plant 30–90 cm tall. Branches and branchlets grow in opposite pairs; flowering side shoots are usually 5–7 cm long and carry one to five flowers near their tips. The yellow-green leaves spread or rise, are blunt-tipped, and measure 6–9 mm long by 1–1.5 mm wide.

The flowers have five slightly unequal sepals and acute white petals about 5 mm long. The expanded capsule is about 7 mm across. The habitat wording records northern slopes.
