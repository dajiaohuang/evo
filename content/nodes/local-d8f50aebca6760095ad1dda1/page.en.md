## AmphiTherm and the recorded species concept {#s-ca38211b42}
What does AmphiTherm record for Epipedobates tricolor? Its pinned dataset places the source concept in family Dendrobatidae, order Anura, using the nomenclature of Jetz and Pyron (2018). It contains 3 thermal-estimate rows linked to 3 study-reference labels. Rows are estimates, not counts of distinct animals; repeated cohorts, life stages and treatments remain separate. Equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
