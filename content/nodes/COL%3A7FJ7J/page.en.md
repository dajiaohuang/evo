## Rough leaves and hairy flower clusters {#s-b97cd8d016}
The record describes bristly to shaggy stems and stalked leaves that vary from ovate to triangular-ovate. Their bases taper or narrow, and both surfaces are hairy, especially the woolly underside. Bristly, head-like flower clusters form at the tips or between leaf nodes. The flowers have a bell-shaped calyx with thread-like lobes and a funnel-shaped corolla over 2 cm long. The ovoid fruit is smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
