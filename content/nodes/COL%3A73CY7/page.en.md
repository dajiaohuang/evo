## Metalasia brevifolia {#s-53480f8e6e}
## Short, backward-curving leaves on a woolly shrub

The regional account describes an erect shrub with white woolly hairs, reaching 1 m but usually shorter. Its leaves are only 2–8 mm long, twisted and bent backward, with small tufts in their axils. Disc-shaped flower heads form clusters at the ends of branches; the bracts stand upright, and the inner, petal-like bracts are white. The account records sandstone flats and slopes as habitat.
