## *Lomatozona inaequale*: deeply lobed, glandular leaves {#s-c2b44c9274}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records glandular stems and leaves; the leaves are deeply lobed. The bracts around each head have sharp tips, and the receptacle beneath the florets lacks paleae, the small scales found in some flower heads. Each head is recorded with 15–20 bilaterally symmetrical florets. The substrate label is rock-associated, without a collection locality.
