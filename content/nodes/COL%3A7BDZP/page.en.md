## Tachigali poeppigiana {#s-9d58f3be08}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali poeppigiana* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes leaf-like stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), cylindrical domatia (small cavities) on the leaf axis, and up to 10 or more leaflet pairs. Flowers are up to 10 mm long, with hairless petals and 10 stamens; the fruit is elliptic to oblong. The entry lists the plant as terrestrial.
