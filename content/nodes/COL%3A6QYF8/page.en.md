## Evidence limits {#s-f34f19d543}
AVONET's measurements are species-level summaries from a small sample, not individual ranges or diagnostic limits; Dunning is the separately recorded source for the mass value. Habitat, niche and migration are broad coded fields, while the latitude endpoints refer only to the dataset's historical breeding/resident geography. EltonTraits turns literature descriptions into semiquantitative relative weights; its 100% diet assignment and 50/50 stratum split should not be read as direct measurements of intake or time spent. The record's name-based links do not settle changes in species concepts. Current distribution, population, breeding biology and conservation need other evidence.
