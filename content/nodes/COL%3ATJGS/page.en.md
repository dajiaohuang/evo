## Two pairs of leaflets and terminal flower clusters {#s-32f1af9dd5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes spiral leaves with two pairs of obovate to elliptic leaflets; they are rarely almost round. Bulb-tipped glandular hairs are recorded on the plant. Yellow flowers grow in bracted clusters at branch tips, and ten stamens are listed. The mature oblong pod sides twist. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
