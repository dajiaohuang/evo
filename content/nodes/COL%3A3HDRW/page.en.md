## Griffinia intermedia: leaves and flowers {#s-12ae3f905e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb. Leaves have pseudopetioles and elliptic or oblong blades with attenuate bases and acute or acuminate tips. Blade markings are usually absent; a central white longitudinal stripe is recorded rarely in young leaves. The solid scape bears fused, spathe-like bracts. Flowers are unscented and campanulate. The perianth is spotted and lilac with a white base, or rarely white. The short hypanthium tube is 0.1–1.0 cm long. There are six stamens; their filaments are as long as, or slightly shorter than, the sepals and petals. The anthers are ash-grey, placentation is basal, and the fruit is obovoid.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
