## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3fe9e4cc06}
COL26.8 records Trachemys terrapen as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Emydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5c5a4e6c4e}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The diet field classifies the entry as plant- and animal-eating (Omnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2011.

ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
