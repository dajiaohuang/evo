## Six tanks tested one probiotic treatment {#s-0577f066a6}
Juvenile Penaeus vannamei came from an aquaculture farm in Pingtan, Fujian, China. Researchers stocked 30 shrimp in each tank and compared two groups of three replicate tanks at about 13‰ salinity. The treatment group received Bacillus licheniformis strain FS051 in the water every five days, at 5 × 10^4 CFU/mL. The 30-day trial dates were not reported. This design describes cultured juveniles from one source lot under one set of tank conditions.

## What changed during the trial {#s-532c6a47b0}
Compared with controls, shrimp receiving FS051 had significantly higher reported length, weight, survival and yield, and a lower feed-conversion ratio (p < 0.05). The result is tied to this strain, dose, source stock and 30-day tank experiment. Three replicate tanks per group do not establish the same outcome across farms or environments, and the study does not describe wild shrimp.
