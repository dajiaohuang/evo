## The name and this data account {#s-c3766bd500}
Zosterops luteus is accepted in COL26.8 within Zosteropidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 7 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-0ba798ac59}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 7.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 2.8 mm in width and 3.2 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-bda3cf385b}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 56.1 mm; mean tarsus length is 17.3 mm. Compiled mean mass is 9.7 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.
