## Historical PanTHERIA entries {#s-a85c4dc907}
The local PanTHERIA route includes eight usable Macaca accounts, with examples such as M. assamensis, M. cyclopis, M. fascicularis, M. mulatta, and M. radiata. Each entry retains its historical MSW05 name, variable-specific conditions, and missing fields. The selection follows pinned COL26.8 navigation; eight is archive coverage, not the current accepted-species denominator.

## Selected adult-mass medians {#s-e6a86c1477}
The selected sex-adjusted historical adult-mass medians are 4,569.32 g for Macaca fascicularis and 11,471.53 g for M. sylvanus. They summarize separate archive entries, not individual extremes or a complete body-size range for the genus. Other life-history fields require their own definitions and source conditions.

## Fields in the Amniote archive {#s-d09ea577f5}
The August 2015 Amniote archive contributes 20 historical Macaca accounts on this route. Adult mass, conception or fertilization-to-birth duration, and maximum-lifespan records appear in all 20; the source-defined female-maturity field—age at first conception—appears in 19. Examples include M. arctoides, M. cyclopis, M. fascicularis, M. fuscata, and M. hecki. These field counts describe the selected archive, not all accepted species or a shared reproductive pattern.

## Food weights, feeding strata, and activity codes {#s-cae438fc68}
EltonTraits includes 21 historical entries on this route. Food-category weights are available in 18; foraging strata and mammalian activity periods in all 21. Examples include Macaca leonina, M. maura, M. mulatta, M. nemestrina, and M. nigra. Food weights standardize literature descriptions; strata and activity are categorical or binary codes, not direct measurements of every macaque’s diet or daily schedule.

## Limits across the separate routes {#s-482c9d4649}
PanTHERIA, the Amniote archive, EltonTraits, and Ernest’s compilation use different historical records, selection rules, fields, and taxonomic crosswalks. Their counts do not represent the current Macaca roster. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. EltonTraits weights and codes translate literature categories, and some mammalian ABC values may borrow from a congener.

## Life-history accounts in Ernest’s compilation {#s-efa1600d36}
Ernest’s 2003 compilation contributes seven historical Macaca accounts on this route. Examples include Macaca sylvanus, M. sinica, M. silenus, M. mulatta, and M. cyclopis. The entries preserve the compilation’s name matching under pinned COL26.8 navigation; they are a selected historical set, not a complete accepted-species list.

## Gestation, first reproduction, and longevity {#s-11a6106e8e}
Gestation and female age at first reproduction appear in six accounts each; maximum lifespan appears in three. First reproduction is distinct from first birth and sexual maturity. Maximum lifespan is the longest record, not an average. Source conditions differ among traits, and missing entries are not filled from relatives.

## Limits of Ernest’s historical values {#s-2e434fb675}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 table; equivalence with current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources equally, regardless of sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted here; original reference numbers remain on the pages, but source papers and all wild, captive, and locality conditions were not individually reviewed.
