## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Quiina pteridophylla? {#s-f589d3cfc0}
Leaves are whorled, with petioles over 3 cm and one interpetiolar stipule. The blades are about 30 cm long, elliptic, obovate or oblong, hairless, with pointed tips and tapered or wedge-shaped bases; 21–30 pairs of raised side veins are recorded. The inflorescence bears 51–100 male flowers and 30 bisexual flowers. Flowers have five sepals and five petals, two styles and a two-chambered ovary. The ellipsoid-to-obovoid fruit is 1–2.5 cm.
