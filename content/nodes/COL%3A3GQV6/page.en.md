## Which bracts are sterile in the record? {#s-8fd258d522}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral, basal leaves and a flower stalk arising at the same node. The bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are spiral; the apical bracts are identified as sterile, while the source marks some flower-tube details as unknown. The entry does not resolve all bract states into a single form.
