## AmphiTherm and the recorded species concept {#s-8d383222fe}
What does AmphiTherm record for Rhinella marina? Its pinned dataset places the source concept in family Bufonidae, order Anura, using the nomenclature of Jetz and Pyron (2018). It contains 239 thermal-estimate rows linked to 28 study-reference labels. Rows are estimates, not counts of distinct animals; repeated cohorts, life stages and treatments remain separate. Equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
