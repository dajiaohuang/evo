## Trachyandra chlamydophylla {#s-4f6ee0279a}
Trachyandra chlamydophylla is a clumping rhizomatous perennial to about 70 cm. Its linear leaves grow in groups, and the dense, unbranched raceme bears white flowers. The fruits hang from unusually long, downward-bent stalks. Clay flats and renosterveld slopes are the recorded habitat.
