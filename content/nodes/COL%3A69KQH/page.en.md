## Population evidence {#s-0a0426583c}
Paddock, Bruford and McCabe used the distinctive “whoop-gobble” call to estimate abundance in the two known Udzungwa forest populations. Across 28 surveys, their model estimated 3,167 individuals (95% CI 2,181–4,596): 1,712 in Mwanihana and 1,455 in Uzungwa Scarp. Estimated group density was lower at Uzungwa Scarp. These are survey-era estimates with broad confidence intervals, not a current count.
