## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-7fdb7dc249}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) accepts Germanodactylus rhamphastinus under the recorded parent Germanodactylus. The two selected species-level occurrences come from Daiting and Solnhofen and share one reference identifier. One record links an earlier name or identification to the accepted name; it is a record of name history, not an additional discovery.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-0689f9d222}
Daiting (BSPG collection) and Solnhofen (MCZ coll) are recorded in Bavaria, Germany (region code DE), in the Mörnsheim and Solnhofen units, respectively. Both have the same PBDB bracket, 149.2–145.06 Ma, with Early Tithonian as the early interval. These two selected collections do not define the species’ full distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-a5705c5790}
Together, the selected records have an outer database age envelope of 149.2–145.06 Ma (Ma means millions of years ago); each collection carries the same bracket. This does not indicate continuous presence throughout the interval or directly date a specimen, the species’ origin or its extinction.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-982b5a407b}
PBDB labels both collections “lagoonal/restricted shallow subtidal” for environment. Daiting has preservation mode “body,” while Solnhofen is “adpression.” These collection-level labels do not establish the species’ habitat or diet and do not describe every fossil individually.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-4b41c2ad56}
Both collection records link to Bennett (2006), database reference ref:61049. This shared citation is a database attribution, not proof that the cited work is the original description or a specimen-by-specimen reassessment.

This page summarizes selected PBDB records available on 2026-10-02. It belongs to a fixed 276-entry pterosaur roster and includes species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. PBDB’s accepted name and parent chain organize the entry; they do not resolve specimen identifications or every taxonomic dispute, and same-name Catalogue of Life concepts remain separate. Localities, broad ages, depositional settings and collection-level preservation labels do not establish the species’ complete range, exact origin or extinction, ecology, abundance or the condition of every specimen.
