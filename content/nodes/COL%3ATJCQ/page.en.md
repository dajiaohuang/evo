## ## Narrow leaflets and yellow petals {#s-79d1eaa364}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records three or four pairs of linear-elliptic leaflets, 1–3 cm long and 0.2–0.65 cm wide, with blunt, mucronulate tips and decurrent bases. Glandular hairs on the leaves and inflorescences are described as blistered or wart-like. Terminal, corymb-like racemes bear yellow flowers with ten stamens and petals of different forms. The source marks the xylopodium, fruit and seed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
