## Nidorella resedifolia {#s-70c3bcdd0d}
## Rough-haired leaves and dense yellow heads

The source describes a stout annual herb up to 90 cm tall, with rough hairs. Its obovate leaves partly clasp the stem at the base and may be lobed. Yellow discoid heads are packed into dense corymbs. The habitat entry lists roadsides and grassland.
