## Philodendron splitgerberi: recorded morphology and habitat {#s-a33eb82258}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records feeding roots and a stem prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous petiole is flattened on the upper side and rounded below. Blades are entire, heart-shaped or ovate, with acuminate tips, truncated bases and entire margins. The field also gives “6 to 9” without specifying what is counted.

The inflorescence is axillary and sympodial, with a green peduncle; the spathe entry describes a tube, and the spadix is subsessile. The field lists axial, basal, subbasal or biseriate placentation. It marks the stamens, staminodes, gynoecium, ovary, locules, ovules, stigma, berries and seeds as unknown.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Hemiepífita,” meaning hemiepiphytic.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
