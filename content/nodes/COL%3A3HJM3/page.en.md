## What leaf, flower, and seed traits are recorded? {#s-6fa183ea7b}
The field describes papery, narrow elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves with sparse hairs above and denser hairs beneath. Flowers grow in leaf axils or on leafless stems, usually one or two together; stalks are densely hairy. The free sepals are broadly triangular, and the petals are egg-shaped. Ellipsoid fruit segments contain seeds with lengthwise or crosswise grooves, or a wrinkled surface.
