## How does the record describe Clusia penduliflora? {#s-f0ad83619e}
The record lists a taproot and additional roots, and allows the stem surface either to peel or not peel in rings. Its stalked, leathery leaves are obovate or elliptic, with secretory channels ranging from conspicuous to faint. Pendulous flower clusters carry five or six sepals and five petals; corollas are white, cream, or yellow. Male flowers have 31–40 stamens joined at the base, without resin, and an absent or greatly reduced pistillode. The ovary has five chambers. Sepals, petals, and staminodes are recorded as persisting on mature fruit, and seeds bear an aril.

## Which habitat categories are listed? {#s-60da8ab0f9}
The dataset lists hemiepiphytic growth—partly on other plants—as well as terrestrial growth, without naming a locality.
