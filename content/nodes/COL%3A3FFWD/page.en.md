## Gaylussacia pseudociliosa Sleumer {#s-6021381a09}
## Nearly smooth leaves and globose, ribbed fruit

Gaylussacia pseudociliosa has upright, closely branched shoots, sometimes with a prostrate base. Its leathery to very stiff leaves are elliptic or ovate; the margins may be flat or slightly rolled and finely scalloped near the tip. The upper surface may be hairy only at the base, while the underside may be smooth or hairy there. Axillary racemes carry a few flowers; the corolla is white or pink and may have hairs in its angles. The fruit is globose, ribbed and hairy. Several hair states remain alternatives in the source record.
