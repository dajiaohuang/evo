## Eupelycosauria — A Re-Description of Mycterosaurus smithae and its Impact on the Phylogeny of Pelycosaurian-Grade Synapsids {#body}
A parsimony analysis places Caseasauria as sister to the remaining synapsids, which it labels Eupelycosauria. Adding Eocasea and postcranial data changed an earlier result. This is the analysis result, not a complete anatomy or lifestyle account of every member.


## In brief {#preview}
A revised analysis changes where Caseasauria sits among early synapsids, illustrating how new postcranial evidence can reshape the named clade.

## Limits of this evidence {#limits}
Analysis-specific result; not a comprehensive biological description of the PBDB suborder.
