## A small tufted perennial {#s-3550a353bf}
The regional flora describes a tufted perennial with a rhizome (horizontal underground stem), 9–30 cm tall, and a few long, pale fibres. Its cylindrical leaves are 1–2 mm in diameter. Three to 17 green flowers form a loose raceme; the narrowly ovoid fruits are 4–8 mm long.
