## Magenta flowers and conspicuous bract tips {#s-4ebb213a64}
Romulea vlokii is distinguished by magenta flowers with a yellow cup and outer bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) with conspicuous membranous tips. Its pointed corm (a swollen underground storage stem) base helps separate it from the similar R. atrandra.
