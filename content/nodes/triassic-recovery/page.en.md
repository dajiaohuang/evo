## Triassic ecosystems recovered along different paths {#s-f599cb10ce}
After the end-Permian extinction, recovery unfolded unevenly across food webs, marine reefs and pelagic communities, and reptile groups. The sources gathered here describe different regions, ecological measures, and stages; some Early Triassic communities also faced renewed environmental stress. “Recovery” therefore does not name one synchronous event with a single duration or global trajectory.
