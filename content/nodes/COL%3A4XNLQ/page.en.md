## Siphocampylus baccae {#s-92cb2ab756}
## Red solitary flowers rise from decumbent stems

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records decumbent stems, which lie along the ground, and alternate, membranous oval leaves. Leaf bases are heart-shaped or rounded, and the margins are toothed. Flowers are solitary; their calyx lobes are inconspicuous or no more than 2 mm long. The inverted-cone-shaped flower base carries a red corolla, whose lobes are also recorded as red.
