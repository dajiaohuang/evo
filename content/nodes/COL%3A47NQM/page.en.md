## Ecological evidence {#s-762f43d007}
At Cát Tiên National Park in Vietnam, researchers followed one southern yellow-cheeked gibbon group—an adult male, an adult female and an offspring—for a year, recording 1,391 hours across 168 days. The group's annual home-range use was 45.5 ha and differed between the rainy and dry seasons; daily travel varied by month and was associated with food resources and temperature. These are baseline results for one group at one site, not a species-wide account.
