## The name links COL26.8 with WFO {#s-ef55244ae3}
The pinned COL26.8 catalogue lists Andreaea subulata as an accepted species in genus Andreaea and family Andreaeaceae. Its exact name is crosswalked to an accepted WFO 2026 usage. This link supports the name and recorded classification; it does not mean that every aspect of the two catalogues’ species concepts has been compared.
