## Source classification and concept status {#s-067fadb545}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3088 records *Alexteroon obstetricans* under the source classification Alexteroon (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-093f49ccc7}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-0dfb55da2f}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-844557d1c4}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for this row. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-2f2baf7ec9}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
