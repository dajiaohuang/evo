## One selected CTmax estimate {#s-947f4b815f}
The pinned AmphiTherm snapshot has one estimate row for this name and study label, not one value for the whole species. Row 3311 (ID 3629; Meza-Parral et al., 2020) gives adults a CTmax estimate of 33.61493213 °C. CTmax is the dataset's critical upper thermal-limit metric; its endpoint field is “other,” and the attached note describes complete immobility and rigidity. The estimate was compiled by digitizing individual points from Figure 6; the study has not been independently reanalysed here.

The row records wild adults, sex unknown, test medium “ambient,” a reported mean starting temperature of 21.7 °C, and a ramp rate of 0.5 °C/min. Animals are marked “field-fresh,” while acclimation temperature and time are not reported. The source lists n_trait = 13 and a standard deviation of 0.807014431 °C. Do not treat n_trait as an animal count across repeated cohorts or the standard deviation as interchangeable with another uncertainty measure. Chemical, hormone, infection, and pathogen fields are NA, meaning unreported rather than confirmed absent.

This is one selected compiled estimate, not a species-wide tolerance boundary, environmental temperature range, or husbandry recommendation. The AmphiTherm source concept remains unmatched to the current catalogue species concept.
