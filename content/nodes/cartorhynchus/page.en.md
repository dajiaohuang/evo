## The Cartorhynchus record rests on one holotype {#s-46aaae42a5}
*Cartorhynchus* is anchored here by holotype AGM I-1 from the Lower Triassic Nanlinghu Formation. The paper reports an approximate age near 248 Ma; the 248–248 Ma display reflects that stratigraphic placement, not a specimen-level radiometric date. This one named specimen does not define genus-wide first or last appearance or an ichthyosauriform origin.
