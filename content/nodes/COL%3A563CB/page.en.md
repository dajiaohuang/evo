## Thamnochortus gracilis {#s-368c4aa9d3}
## Transparent bract margins on small female spikelets

Thamnochortus gracilis is recorded as a tufted perennial up to 30 cm, with slender, spreading fertile stems that branch only sparsely. Female spikelets reach 10 mm; their pointed bracts have transparent upper margins. The account says it usually grows on well-drained sandy soils.
