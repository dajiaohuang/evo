COL26.8 records Ophiodes luciae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Diploglossidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.
