## Historical local survey records are distinct from a current provincial census {#s-2cc4cacd5f}

Counts are cumulative records, not an independently certified number of distinct marked animals, population density or a current abundance estimate. Surveys combine daytime/night-time visual and photographic encounters, ten 8 × 8 m quadrats, four 100 × 20 m transects and fifty pitfall traps across six habitats. ## Species-labelled rows conflict with a genus-only identification statement {#s-3fd1a7e5d3}

Table 2 nevertheless prints Pristimantis croceoinguinis and other named Pristimantis species alongside five separate Pristimantis sp. rows. This conflict remains unresolved: the named row is presented as an attributed source record, not independent certification of species identity.
