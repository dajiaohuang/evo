## ## Gray hairs and straight pods {#s-d7cd3bcb51}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium, a woody underground organ, and striate stems with rough, gray hairs. The odd-pinnate leaves have opposite, oblong to elliptic leaflets without glands; stipels are present at the leaflet bases. Congested racemes bear red to wine-colored petals. The pod is straight. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
