## Philodendron venezuelense: two recorded habitat categories {#s-44618e91af}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat row labels the species “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

A second row labels it “Terrícola,” or terrestrial. The dataset preserves both categories; it does not explain their relationship.

These are habitat categories in regional dataset rows. They give no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage and do not specify whether the records concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.
