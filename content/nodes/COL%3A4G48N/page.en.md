## Long flower stalks above recurved leaves {#s-a551017a93}
This erect shrublet reaches 25 cm and has slender, stiffly upright branches. Its recurved leaves are up to 25 mm long. Many-flowered dichasial clusters sit on 25–40 mm stalks and bear purplish flowers up to 14 mm across. The fruits are about 5 mm across; sandy and stony slopes are listed as habitat.
