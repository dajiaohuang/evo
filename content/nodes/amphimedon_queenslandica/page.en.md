## A living sponge genome, not a preserved ancestor {#s-ae92c6a0f8}
Amphimedon queenslandica is a living demosponge sampled from the Great Barrier Reef for a draft genome. It offers comparisons among animal gene families; it is one living species, not a literal ancestral animal or a stand-in for every sponge.
