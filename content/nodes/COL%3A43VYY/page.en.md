## What traits are recorded for Mollinedia luizae? {#s-88ceb0cbfd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes corky bark and opposite, papery, elliptic leaves. The leaf edges may be entire or toothed, while both surfaces are hairless. Male flowers occur in three-flowered clusters and have a flat floral cup. The fruit’s shape, hair covering, and surface are not specified in this record.

## What habitat category is given? {#s-93bca94341}
The source records it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
