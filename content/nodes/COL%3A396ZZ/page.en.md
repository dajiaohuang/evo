## Elegia recta {#s-85a42a114c}
## Slender underground stems on damp flats

The regional flora describes Elegia recta as a mat-forming perennial reaching 60 cm, with long, slender rhizomes (underground stems), slender stems, and leaf sheaths (the basal parts of leaves around a stem) that fall away. Its habitat is recorded as damp flats, often on clay and occasionally on silcrete (a hard layer cemented by silica).
