## Eptesicus (Eptesicus) taddeii: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Eptesicus (Eptesicus) taddeii* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Miranda, Bernardi & Passos, 2006.
