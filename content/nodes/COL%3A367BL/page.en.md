## ## Many pairs of leaf-bearing pinnae {#s-a074380f3e}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaves with seven to nine pairs of opposite or nearly opposite pinnae, the side axes that carry the leaflets. The oblong to lanceolate leaflets are papery, with rounded or blunt bases and pointed or gradually tapering tips; the lower surface is glabrous and the upper surface pubescent. The flowers are cream-colored, and the fusiform ovary is pubescent. The mature fruit is woody, elongated and straight, with a reddish-brown color. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
