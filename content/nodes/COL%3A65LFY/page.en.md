## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-41175d653e}
COL26.8 records Agama sankaranica as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Agamidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-b1e380d211}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bars-Closel et al. 2017. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-552d3c3bdb}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-65e9dc362f}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
