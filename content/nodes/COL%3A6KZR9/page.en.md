## The name and this data account {#s-2272f63324}
COL26.8 accepts *Halobaena caerulea* in Procellariidae. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 4 measured individuals, 3 with a complete set of morphological measurements. Sample sizes can differ by trait.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-ae169872ee}
The recorded beak means are 22.1 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip, 7 mm wide and 7 mm deep at the nostril edge. These are separate dimensions at defined landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-1bf7d32d5c}
Mean unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 206.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 31.6 mm. The compiled mean mass is 202 g, from source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information, so the morphology records do not show that every bird was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-b8f517c8d2}
The dataset's dominant categories are Marine for habitat, Aquatic predator for trophic niche and Generalist for lifestyle. They support cross-species comparison, but do not mean the bird uses only marine habitat or eats only aquatic prey.

The migration field classifies the record as partially migratory. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitudes -67.7° to -31.39°. They do not describe all non-breeding movements or show current positions or global occupancy. The geographic and morphological data come from separate compilation steps.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-5a3fed5077}
The compilation reports 1 offspring or egg per litter/clutch, 46 d from oviposition to hatching and 58 d to flight capability (some sources describe leaving the nest); d means days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring and egg counts. Each timing value describes a distinct event, not one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-970d1ac6d7}
The nonzero food weights are invertebrates 80% and fish 20%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and do not imply a diet limited to nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-e5c7039502}
The archive's species-level foraging-stratum weights are 80% on or just below the water surface and 20% below it. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevation ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.
