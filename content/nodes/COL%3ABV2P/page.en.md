## Accepted name and placement {#s-65e5b24c8a}
COL26.8 accepts Allobates tinae Melo-Sampaio, Oliveira & Prates, 2018 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Aromobatidae (family) → Allobates (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A reported distance to velocicantus {#s-6b571ea5da}
The2020 study’s Table2 reports 9.4% uncorrected p-distance and 10.1% Kimura2-parameter distance between the Allobates tinae label and velocicantus, based on a678-bp16S fragment. These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. The table is a sampled comparison; original accessions, alignments and per-donor TableS1 entries remain independently unreviewed.

## Duration and spacing in cited acoustic comparisons {#s-79200be9bb}
The2020 paper attributes to previous studies a maximum call duration of 3.7 s, maximum 13 notes and inter-note interval 100–410 ms for tinae. These cited summaries preserve their units and do not describe the same sampling protocol or temperatures as the eight focal velocicantus males. Original source recordings have not been independently reanalysed, and no new mate-recognition experiment follows from this numerical contrast.
