## *Tocoyena sellowiana* {#s-0a58948621}
Brazilian Flora 2020 describes stems that are round in cross-section or angular, with striated or lenticellate surfaces (bearing small corky pores); the indumentum may be absent or becoming sparse. The petiolate, elliptic leaves are listed as spaced along the internodes; their blades may be smooth or bullate (blistered), brownish or black, and hairless, with domatia (leaf pockets) recorded. Upright inflorescences arise laterally on the stem and may be congested or more developed; their form is recorded as a dichasium or thyrse (branched flower clusters), with triangular or short-triangular bracts. The description also notes cup-shaped calyces, pedicellate flowers, obtuse corolla lobes and an ellipsoid ovary. Fruits are globose and may be hairless or pilose (hairy). A separate habitat field in regional record 83076 reads “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a category without a locality or survey date. These regional fields do not establish a global description, population variation, sex or life stage, or wild-versus-cultivated status.
