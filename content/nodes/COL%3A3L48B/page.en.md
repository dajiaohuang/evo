## Long pink tubes and joined bracts {#s-a6fb6e1734}
Hesperantha elsiae grows 25–30 cm tall. Its deep-pink flowers have a tube up to 21 mm long; the stamens sit within its middle and the long style branches reach the tube mouth. The outer bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) join near the base around the spike axis.
