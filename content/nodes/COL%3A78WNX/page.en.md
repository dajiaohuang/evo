## Pink to copper-salmon flowers {#s-264c6e9894}
Romulea namaquensis grows to 200 mm and varies in flower size. Its flowers range from pink to coppery salmon, with little yellow at the base; dark maroon filaments are inserted around or above the middle of the long tube.
