## What does the record report for Clusia insignis? {#s-3611b3ca38}
The plant has a taproot and additional roots, with stalked, leathery leaves that are obovate, elliptic, or oblong; secretory channels may be conspicuous or faint. Pendulous flower clusters or solitary flowers are recorded. Flowers have four sepals and eight petals, with corollas ranging from white or cream to red. Male flowers have more than 40 stamens joined at the base, resin-bearing stamens or staminodes, and a pistillode. The ovary has 11–20 chambers. Sepals and staminodes remain on the mature fruit, while petals fall; seeds have an aril.
