## Accepted name and placement {#s-8e73dc7403}
In the frozen COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026, Leptodactylus jolyi Sazima & Bokermann, 1978 is accepted at species rank. Its recorded placement is Anura (order), Leptodactylidae (family) and Leptodactylus (genus). This is the classification in that release; other catalogues need their own evidence for name identity.
