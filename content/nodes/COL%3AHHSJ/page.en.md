## Crowded foliage and a hairy ovary {#s-7414661ba7}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description, cylindrical branches have bark recorded as either unthickened or slightly thickened. The leaves are alternate and crowded, with white latex noted in the leaf entry. The inflorescences are terminal, corymb-like cymes. The source describes a hair-covered ovary and corolla lobes shorter than the tube; the sepals are nearly equal. The fruit is smooth, with evident ribs, a stalk, and conspicuous lenticels. This regional morphology entry does not give a collection locality or describe variation across the species.
