## Active gestation and first reproduction {#s-0c1d7fb862}
The compiled averages are active gestation 0.71 months and female age at first reproduction 1.6 months. Gestation covers active fetal development and excludes delayed implantation. First-reproduction age is when a female first breeds and conception is possible; if a source gives first-birth age, the method subtracts gestation. It is distinct from sexual maturity and first-birth age. These are cross-source summaries, not a schedule for every animal.
