## Nodal signalling and opposite shell coiling in two snails {#s-a2b8aff66d}
Why do these two snails coil their shells in opposite directions? In embryos of the dextral limpet Lottia gigantea and the sinistral snail Biomphalaria glabrata, nodal and Pitx expression appears on opposite sides, matching each species’ adult chirality. Inhibiting the Nodal pathway disrupts Pitx expression and shell coiling in the tested animals. This experiment connects a developmental signal to handedness in two living gastropods; it does not directly explain torsion, fossil coiling or every molluscan asymmetry.
