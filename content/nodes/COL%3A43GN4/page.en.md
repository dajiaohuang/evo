## Mimosa petraea {#s-70be5d46d6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa petraea* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records forward-pressed, short rough hairs on the stem and stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) that fall away. Its flower clusters are globose, and the flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) with hairless petal lobes. The entry labels it terrestrial.
