## Cyclopia alpina {#s-b5b4135b10}
The 2012 South African account describes *Cyclopia alpina* as a sprawling, resprouting shrub up to 30 cm tall, with three-foliolate leaves and yellow flowers from November to December. It reports sandstone slopes at 1,170–2,070 m in the Hex River, Hottentots Holland, and Kammanassie mountains; this is the account’s regional range, not a complete environmental envelope.
