## Record identity and classification {#s-bf59357856}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf5026 records Rana maoershanensis under Rana, Ranidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-aaf2fa76fc}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 55 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.
