## Philodendron cipoense: leaf and floral characters {#s-91f6c12a41}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and an erect stem. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole conspicuous; the blade is entire, cordate or ovate, with an entire margin. One inflorescence is listed per sympodium. Its peduncle is green, the spathe green, light green or cream, and the spadix stipitate; female, sterile-male and fertile-male zones are listed.

The stamens and staminodes are short. The gynoecium and ovary are ovoid; locule counts are listed as 6 or 7 and ovule counts as 3 or 4. Placentation is subbasal and the stigma capitate. The mature berry is obovoid and cream-colored; seeds are numerous.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative colors and counts are retained as reported.
