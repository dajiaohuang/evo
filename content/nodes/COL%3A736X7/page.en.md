## Matayba stenodictya {#s-d5e9f1dc81}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 record, *Matayba stenodictya* has leaves distributed along the branches, variable leaflet texture and folding, and globose capsules with warty outer walls. The same record labels its habitat “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

## A leaf record with several listed states {#s-78a942311f}
The field places leaves along the whole branch. It lists the rachis as flat or subcylindrical, including a subcylindrical form with two channels. Leaflet texture ranges among papery, somewhat leathery, and leathery states; the blades may be conduplicate (folded lengthwise) or recurved (curved backward). They are discolorous, with different-colored upper and lower surfaces. Dry leaflets are recorded as green, brown, grey, or blackish. Their margins are entire, and the lower surface is hairless to nearly hairless. The source describes brochidodromous venation, closed areoles, straight secondary veins, and no domatia. It gives upper-surface venation as prominent or inconspicuous; both alternatives are retained rather than choosing one.

## Sepal attachment and fruit surfaces {#s-5d752178b2}
Sepals are joined either at the base or around the middle, with sparse hairs or a pubescent covering on the lower surface. Capsules are globose. Their outer wall is warty and ranges from sparsely hairy to pubescent; the inner wall is villous, with long soft hairs. The seed aril is positioned in the middle part of the seed. These are the source field's alternatives and observations; it does not provide a full account of the species' reproductive cycle.

## “Terrícola” is a habitat category {#s-7a03dffe74}
The habitat value is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It does not give a collection locality or range. COL26.8 species ID 736X7 is matched through an exact accepted-name and authorship crosswalk to one WFO usage, which the Brazilian Flora record also carries. That identity link does not make this regional morphology and habitat record a complete or current account of the species.
