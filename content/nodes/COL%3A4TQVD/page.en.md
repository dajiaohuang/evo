## Ruschia cedarbergensis {#s-8adc526c3e}
## U-shaped leaf pairs and magenta flower clusters

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes plants that range from decumbent to erect; the shrublet reaches 25 cm in its erect form. The paired leaves nearly form a U, with a 4–6 mm line separating them at the sheath. They are almost cylindrical in cross-section and slightly rough.

Many magenta flowers grow together in clusters. The five-chambered fruit has a low top, a funnel-shaped base and small closing bodies. The habitat account is marked uncertain.
