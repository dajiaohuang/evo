## Twining stems and trifoliolate leaves in the entry {#s-c36e073696}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records twining stems and trifoliolate leaves. Inflorescences are racemose and smaller than the leaves. Calyx lobes are listed as larger than or smaller than the corolla; the fruit is recorded as constricted and the seed as concolorous. The habitat field gives the category terrestrial. The entry does not explain the alternative calyx comparisons, and these regional fields do not establish a complete range.
