## Eryngium coronatum {#s-4bcbe99250}
## Parallel midveins and a papillate fruit surface

The source records leaves as either petiolate or sessile. Their central veins are parallel, while the marginal veins form a netted pattern. The inflorescence is upright and the flowers sessile. The fruit is sessile and indehiscent; the back of each mericarp, one half of the fruit, is papillate or covered with small rounded projections.
