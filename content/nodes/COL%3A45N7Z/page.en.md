## Accepted name and placement {#s-9b7005ad1c}
COL26.8 accepts Nanorana vicina (Stoliczka, 1872) as a species in Anura, Dicroglossidae, and Nanorana. This is the classification recorded in the release of 20 August 2026; a 2023 study's proposed placement within Paa is a separate phylogenetic interpretation.

## Published placement within Paa {#s-d2d5fa2b47}
In a 2023 combined-locus analysis, Nanorana vicina grouped with sampled members of Paa, leading the authors to suggest placing it in that subgenus.
