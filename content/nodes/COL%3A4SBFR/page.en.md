## Woolly flower areoles on cylindrical segments {#s-f9c6afa546}
The mature areoles are spineless, and the cylindrical segments end in smooth or crenate tips. Lateral flowers arise from woolly areoles; the wheel-shaped corolla has white filaments, and the flower base is immersed and shorter than the perianth segments. The globose or flattened fruit may be white, pink or magenta. The record lists both epiphytic and rock-associated settings.
