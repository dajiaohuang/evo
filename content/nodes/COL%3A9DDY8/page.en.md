## Nissolia brasiliensis {#s-8cf79a2e50}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Nissolia brasiliensis* as an accepted species of *Nissolia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 5–8 mm stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) and leaves with 5, 7, 9, or 11 leaflets. It lists flowers in clustered, racemose, or solitary arrangements, and a cylindrical, stalked, wingless lomento fruit. A lomento is a segmented pod that separates into seed-bearing sections.
