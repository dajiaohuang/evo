## White, long-tubed flowers above rough leaf undersides {#s-a873fab920}
The morphology record describes bristly stems and elliptic-lanceolate leaves that taper at the base and end in a long point. The upper surface is smooth and the lower surface bristly. Bisexual white flowers have a ribbed, tubular calyx under 1 cm long and a bell-shaped tubular corolla over 2 cm long. The ovary and oval white fruit are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
