## The source concept {#s-9640be8b23}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria mystax as row Anf2876 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-5a55ef6fcc}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 30 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental strategy {#s-0148b16c85}
The record indicates a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or reproductive timing.
