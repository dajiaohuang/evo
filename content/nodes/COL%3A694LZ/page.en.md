## Scarlet flowers on a small tree {#s-400e971da6}
The archived Flora of Panama description portrays Brownea rosa-de-monte as a small tree. Its elliptic leaflets (the lowest on a leaf are often ovate) reach 20 cm or more in length and 2–9 cm in width; the conspicuous scarlet flowers have five petals, about 4 cm long and 1–1.5 cm wide. A separate historical distribution field places the record from Costa Rica to northern South America and Trinidad. The flora gives no population sampling frame or observation date, so this is not a current or complete range account.
