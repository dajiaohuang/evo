## ## Pendulous inflorescences and pale flowers {#s-6f7b471fca}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records pendulous inflorescences without stipitate glandular hairs. The standard petal reaches 25 mm long; the petals are white to pale lilac. Flowers are not resupinate—that is, they are not twisted upside down—and the fruit is also described as non-resupinate, with a straight margin. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial; this regional character list gives no locality or population coverage.
