## Accepted name and placement {#s-feaf56c59f}
COL26.8 accepts Eleutherodactylus simulans Díaz & Fong, 2001 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Eleutherodactylidae (family) → Eleutherodactylus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
