## Cosmos caudatus {#s-41467f94bf}
## Finely divided leaves and white-to-violet rays

The Brazilian Flora fields record no rhizome. The leaf blades are divided two or three times in a pinnate pattern. Flower heads measure 2–4 cm across and have white-to-violet ray florets. The cypselae are 10–30 mm long and bear two or three awns. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
