## *Restio subverticillatus*: fertile branches in whorls {#s-3ecb41e6f4}
This species may reach 2.4 m and bears numerous branches arranged in whorls around the stem. Most of those branches are fertile, and the flower bracts range from blunt-tipped to pointed. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

Its recorded habitat is stream banks and river-edge scrub.
