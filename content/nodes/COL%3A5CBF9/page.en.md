## Name and classification {#s-3e743e2ddc}
COL26.8 lists Xenorhina similis (Zweifel, 1956) as a species of Xenorhina in Microhylidae, within Anura, the order of frogs and toads. This placement follows the catalogue release dated 20 August 2026.

It is a compiled maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every individual; the historical source concept has not been matched to the current catalogue entry.
