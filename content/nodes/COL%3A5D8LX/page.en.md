## How to read these life-history values {#s-8425c0f74a}
The August 2015 archive reports compilation medians (or the mean of the two middle values); maximum longevity instead uses the largest collected record. These summaries are not individual measurements, variation ranges, wild-population averages or current surveys. Historical species and subspecies records can share inherited data, and a matching name, family and source row do not prove identity with the COL26.8 concept or remove every inherited component. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently established, and the underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behaviour, current distribution, conservation and other unlisted fields require separate sources.
