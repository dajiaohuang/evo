## Leaves and flowers {#s-5e73bd3428}
The regional account describes a bulbous geophyte and gives its size as 20–40 cm. It records four to six leaves, sometimes as many as eight; the leaves lie flat and are tongue-shaped, with red or pale-red margins, and are dry when flowering. Twenty to forty flowers form a dense hemispherical head. Their tepals (the flower segments) spread evenly or flare upward; the floral tube is well developed, and the flowers are pink to crimson. The capsules have three angles, a rounded upper end, and pronounced ribs.

The field records a bulbous geophyte, 20–40 cm; four to six leaves, sometimes eight, dry at flowering, prostrate and tongue-shaped with red or pale-red margins; 20–40 flowers in a dense hemispherical head, with tepals evenly spreading or flaring upward, a well-developed tube, and pink-to-crimson colour; and three-angled capsules rounded above and heavily ribbed.
This regional flora account was published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
The regional account does not specify native, naturalised, cultivated, or domesticated status beyond its explicit statements.
