## Stems, leaves and flowers {#s-3937eac420}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records abundant branching, with branches lacking wings. Semi-sagittate stipules (small leaf-base appendages shaped like half an arrowhead) accompany subulate, or awl-shaped, leaflets whose veins are not prominent. Tendrils (slender curling structures) are bristle-like. Racemes (flower clusters along a central axis) bear up to six flowers, colored bluish to purple. The stigma (pollen-receiving tip) is split in two. This single regional field does not establish the full variation of these traits.

## Habitat category {#s-1bc1f3688c}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is a category recorded in one row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of distribution.
