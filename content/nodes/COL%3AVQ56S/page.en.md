## One strain described under culture conditions {#s-8590325880}
The species description centers on one cultured type strain, ES5-4T. A type strain is the reference culture used to anchor a bacterial species name. The authors describe its cells as Gram-positive, motile, aerobic rods. After three days on tryptic soy agar (TSA) at 28 °C, its colonies were milky, circular, moist and smooth. These observations describe this strain under the stated laboratory conditions.

## A plant-root isolate and a laboratory test {#s-5335c9bedb}
The paper traces ES5-4T to the rhizosphere of Galinsoga parviflora in a selenium-rich ore area near Enshi, Hubei Province, China. It also reports laboratory tolerance to selenite up to 5000 mg/L. That value comes from an in-vitro assay: it does not establish a field exposure threshold, a role in selenium cycling, or the species’ range. The locality is the reported collection site of one strain.

## How the authors compared close strains {#s-0fe0edbc36}
The authors used 16S rRNA and a bac120 phylogenomic tree to place ES5-4T within Paenibacillus. Their average nucleotide identity (ANI) and digital DNA–DNA hybridization (dDDH) comparisons with close type strains fell below the species-level cutoffs they applied. This is the taxonomic evidence in the 2025 description; it is not a comprehensive or independently reviewed species tree.
