## Broad leaves with variable hairs and toothed edges {#s-ad43e97a1d}
This detailed record describes a plant without a swollen underground stem, with branches that range from nearly smooth to bristly or shaggy. The oval to lance-shaped leaves vary in their hair cover and in how deeply their edges are toothed. Small flower heads form clusters; the inner bracts are leaf-like at their tips, and the white flowers include six staminate florets. The oval to round fruits are bristly, with hairs at the tip. The habitat field says terrestrial.
