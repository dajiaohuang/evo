## What traits describe Stachytarpheta pohliana? {#s-6031742329}
The record describes hairy, four-angled stems and opposite, stalked oval leaves with toothed edges and hairs on both surfaces. Hairy, fringed bracts line the terminal spikes. The blue, funnel-shaped flowers have a five-toothed calyx, two fertile stamens, and two sterile ones. The dry fruit separates into two dark-brown, one-seeded segments.
