## Choose comparisons by available fields {#s-9422a2c20f}
These descendant pages interpret only their own sourced fields. Inspect what each page lacks, then compare jointly supported size, activity, diet or reproductive fields. Counts do not establish trait completeness, and missing values are not filled from relatives. Turtle SCL and other taxa’s SVL remain different measurements.

## Evidence limits {#s-d879f31bfd}
This guide links entries with at least one sourced focal trait and matching COL name and family; each descendant page stays within its own source scope. It is not an exhaustive descendant list. Maximum sizes are reported compiled records, not individual averages or identification thresholds. Turtle carapace length and snout–vent length in other groups are different measures. Consult sources and aggregation rules by field; historical one-row records do not resolve every taxonomic revision or population difference.
