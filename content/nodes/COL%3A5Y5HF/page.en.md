## Chionolaena jeffreyi {#s-6b228f908e}
## Usually solitary heads with two kinds of florets

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes linear to elliptic leaves whose length is less than seven times their width. Heads are usually solitary, rarely paired, and contain fewer than 30 florets. The heads are heterogamous: they contain different floret types, with staminate florets more numerous. Hairs on the dry fruit are blunt-tipped. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or complete range is supplied.
