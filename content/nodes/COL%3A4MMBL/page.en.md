## Accepted name and placement {#s-fbced55daa}
COL26.8 accepts Probreviceps durirostris Loader, Channing, Menegon & Davenport, 2006 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brevicipitidae (family) → Probreviceps (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading the separate source records {#s-52a434ff35}
AmphiBIO and the morphology deposit use their own historical classifications. Neither record has been shown to match the current COL26.8 concept in every respect, so the traits and measurements below stay attached to their source rows.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-2f663186b4}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 concept is tagged terrestrial in the foraging field. This broad category does not describe season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision (version 3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) reports corrections to selected inherited microhabitat entries, but the row-level status here is unresolved; the recorded label is retained without a replacement.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-db3d6e0194}
The AmphiBIO row reports a maximum adult body length of 35 mm. For Anura the measure is snout–vent length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual or a current species-wide range.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-cc1a371a6f}
The same source concept records at most 1 reproductive event per year. The row does not link the value to a sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive setting, so it is a compiled report rather than a complete breeding history.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-d4bf6e23c1}
AmphiBIO labels development as direct, a source category for development without a free-living larval phase. It does not specify parental care, breeding location or timing.
