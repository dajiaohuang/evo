## Rolled leaf edges and pale grey undersides {#s-48106aa296}
This account describes a thick underground root or swollen stem and few-branched shoots with leaves along their length. The linear, leathery leaves have smooth upper surfaces, pale grey undersides and edges rolled under. Stalked flower heads occur singly or in clusters and contain about 20–30 florets. The cylindrical fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
