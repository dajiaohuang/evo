## What narrow-leaf and flower measurements are recorded? {#s-34590293fc}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes hairy leaves that do not blacken when dry. Leaves are sessile and either 0.5–4 mm or more than 5 mm wide, with heart-shaped or notched bases and rolled-under margins. Only the midvein is apparent; the field lists 0–1 lateral veins on each side and no hair tufts in the vein axils. A small bract below the flower is shorter than, or reaches halfway up, the floral tube. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. The epicalyx segments—an extra sepal-like ring below the calyx—are 2–11 mm long, shorter than the sepals and erect. The corolla is 1–3 cm across and has 12 stamens. The regional row lists alternatives without their frequency.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-9e6e7ce7dc}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; no locality or collection date is supplied.
