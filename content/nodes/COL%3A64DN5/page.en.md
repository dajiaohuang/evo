## Spiny branches and small flowers in the entry {#s-5bf9ecd626}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiny branches and opposite leaves under 10 cm long. The inflorescence is a congested raceme with a rachis up to 1 mm. Flowers are white with a blue border; rudimentary petals are listed as 1 mm or 1.5 mm long. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or geographic range.
