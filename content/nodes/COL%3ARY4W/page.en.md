## Cavendishia santafeensis Luteyn {#s-25f454ee8c}
## Large pale bracts around white flowers

Cavendishia santafeensis is a glabrous epiphytic shrub. Its elliptic to lance-shaped leaves are 8–19 cm long and have three to five main veins. Racemes carry nine to twelve flowers and unusually large, pale reddish-violet bracts, about 3.3–4.5 cm long. The white corolla is about 4.25 cm long, with purple lobes. Fruit was not seen in the cited description. The evidence labels the species endemic but does not name the endemic region.
