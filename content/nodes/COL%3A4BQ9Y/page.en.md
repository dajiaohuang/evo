## Pachycarpus plicatus {#s-3ac5bf9b59}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Pachycarpus plicatus* as an accepted species. A regional account describes terminal umbels of 2–4 flowers, with a spreading, cup-shaped corolla marked dark violet and yellow. Longitudinal folds in the keels of its flower’s corona (a crown-like central structure), together with anther wings set diagonally rather than vertically, are used to distinguish the species. The account records occasional plants in grassland and rocky areas at 760–1100 m. These are regional account details.
