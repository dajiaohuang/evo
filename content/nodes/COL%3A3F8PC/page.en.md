## *Gamochaeta americana*: basal rosettes and clustered heads {#s-3b975f2f54}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records an unbranched stem and basal rosettes of spoon-shaped leaves. The upper surface is hairless or nearly so, and the two surfaces differ in colour. Leaf tips are blunt or rounded, without a small hard point. Flower-head clusters form a continuous or conspicuously interrupted spike. The inner bracts around each head have pointed tips, sometimes with a short terminal point. The broad substrate category is “terrestrial.”
