## Source concept and record identity {#s-8fb070008b}
AmphiBIO v1 names Rulyrana adiazeta in row Anf1003, listing genus Rulyrana, family Centrolenidae and order Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging-stratum flags: terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal {#s-170570e9c2}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial); Aqu=1 (aquatic); Arb=1 (arboreal). These are broad source categories: 1 marks a listed category, 0 is the stored code, and NA remains missing data. The fields do not describe seasonal or developmental changes.

A later TetrapodTraits revision notice (3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) covers selected body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. This row has not been compared at row level, so its original entries remain and no replacement is inferred; the notice does not establish that every original value was wrong.

## Maximum adult body length {#s-0bd4040007}
The AmphiBIO row records maximum adult body length 28.8 mm. For Anura, Body_size_mm is snout–vent length, measured from the snout tip to the vent; the reported length is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
