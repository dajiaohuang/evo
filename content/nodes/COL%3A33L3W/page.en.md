## Cyrtanthus stenanthus: flower form and scent note {#s-cb5e7ca2b5}
A 1997 regional account describes a bulbous herb up to 300 mm tall, with a bulb about 20 mm in diameter. The perianth is 35–45 mm long and slightly constricted at the throat. Its tube is very slender, and the lobes become recurved. The flowers are yellow.
