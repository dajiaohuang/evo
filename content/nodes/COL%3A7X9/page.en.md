## How the separate datasets are bounded {#s-515e985633}
PanTHERIA, the Amniote archive, EltonTraits, and Ernest’s compilation keep distinct name crosswalks, variable definitions, selection rules, and source conditions. Their records do not cover every COL26.8 descendant, and a historical name match does not establish equivalence with a current species concept. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. EltonTraits categories summarize literature, and some mammalian ABC entries may infer from a congener.

## What this small selection represents {#s-387b264303}

## Interpreting the historical life-history values {#s-01fe150c54}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 crosswalk; equivalence with current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, fields generally average literature values equally, without sample-size weighting. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints when data are sparse, and underlying studies may overlap.
