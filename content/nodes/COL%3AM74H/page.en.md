## Leaf-attached egg masses and excluded large counts {#s-b7578bef25}
The maintained blommersae attached most eggs to the underside of live leaves. The study described substantial within-species egg-mass variability but excluded two masses containing 208 and 222 eggs because they appeared to combine clutches from different females. These excluded values should not be reported as accepted single-female clutch maxima. The authors warned that blommersae and sarotra look superficially similar and had identified founders only morphologically. The observations remain attributed to the maintained blommersae label rather than a genetically certified population, and captive leaf use does not establish every wild egg substrate.

## Externally feeding tadpoles in nonflowing-water care {#s-4bcb3ce6c0}
Blommersae tadpoles were classified as exotrophic and received prepared external foods. As a lentic-breeding species in the account, they were raised in groups in bare-bottom plastic containers with nonflowing water. The container sizes, feeding mixture and water-change routine describe husbandry rather than demonstrated natural requirements or safe thresholds. The founders came from Analamazaotra Forest Station, initially in 2011 with an additional collection in 2016, but that origin does not certify a full natural range.
