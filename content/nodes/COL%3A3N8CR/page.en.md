## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b7f7c712ae}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis zweifeli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-bf2eaecf1b}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 800 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-23498da602}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cogger 2018.
