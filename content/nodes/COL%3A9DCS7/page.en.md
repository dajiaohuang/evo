## Stem surface {#s-e88b69518f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists the stem surface as glabrous or strigose (bearing stiff, slanting hairs). It also records simple hairs and thread-like, unbranched bristles; branched bristles and glandular hairs are absent. These fields are retained as alternatives rather than reconciled. The simple hairs point backward and lie close to the surface (retrorse-appressed). Prickles are scattered and spread outward; short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-1505d4f49b}
Stipules are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries and projections between pairs of pinnae as absent. The inflorescence is globose; floral bracts are shorter than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-89a6ef1af6}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous and has no lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are glabrous. The androecium is isostemonous, with stamens equal in number to the petals; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-4503f45284}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
