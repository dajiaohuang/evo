## Recorded localities and elevations {#s-c2594d123f}
The description reports seven localities around Tungurahua and nearby reserves in Ecuador’s southern Pastaza drainage, at 2,950–3,800 m. The sites span high Andean forest and grass-and-shrub páramo. These are sampled places, not evidence of continuous occupancy between them or physiological elevation limits for the species.
