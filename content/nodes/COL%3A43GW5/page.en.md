## Mimosa velloziana {#s-c792de9dde}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa velloziana* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists several stem-hair states and recurved prickles in rows, as well as projections between leaf divisions and globose flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), with petal lobes listed as hairless or hairy; the entry calls the plant terrestrial.
