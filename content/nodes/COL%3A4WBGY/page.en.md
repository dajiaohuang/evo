## *Selaginella brevifolia*: leaves differ by position {#s-7c78473a65}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile records prostrate, pale, hairless stems without joints; root-bearing rhizophores arise from the leaf axils. Its small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are triangular or ovate-triangular with rounded to shallowly heart-shaped bases, while upper leaves are ovate-elliptic with oblique bases and long tapering tips. Axillary leaves are broadly ovate or triangular.

The record also notes an idioblast, a specialized cell type, in lateral leaves but not upper leaves. The associated habitat field says terrestrial, without a named locality or survey date; these are regional characters rather than a full-range description.
