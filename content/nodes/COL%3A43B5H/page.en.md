## *Mikania cacerensis*: heart-shaped leaves and a loose panicle {#s-eee7bfc78b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, thin leaves that are egg-shaped or heart-shaped, with a heart-shaped base and either entire or toothed edges. The upper surface is hairless and the lower hairy; three or five strong veins radiate from the base. The branches are listed as twining or hairy. Loose panicles carry stalked heads with blunt-tipped bracts; the flower lobes are triangular and hairless, and the fruitlets are hairless. These listed options are not a complete species-wide account.
