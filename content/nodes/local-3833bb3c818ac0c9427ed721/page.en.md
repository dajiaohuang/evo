## The deposited caecilian record {#s-a71efd49a8}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis billitonensis in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-c56faa1dd8}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-6a150e8310}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” The accompanying metadata treats part of this description as presumed or possible, so these categories are not confirmed observations or a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-6c7a509280}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. The accompanying metadata describes this as presumed, probable or expected, so it remains a qualified compilation statement rather than an unqualified observation.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-3450460c04}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
