## Compressed, curved leaves and a range of flower colors {#s-8c9199bd16}
This account describes an upright shrublet 20–60 cm tall, with spreading, somewhat curved leaves that are strongly compressed and 6–9 mm long. One to three flowers occur in a branched inflorescence; they may be white, pale pink or magenta and about 32 mm across. It is recorded on stony sandstone slopes, often after fires, at 100–900 m.
