## Escallonia ledifolia Sleumer {#s-8ce47a89e3}
## Red petals and narrow, sessile leaves

Escallonia ledifolia has woolly hairs on the stem. Its sessile leaves are oblanceolate, with serrated margins and tips recorded as acute, acuminate or blunt. The inflorescence and hypanthium bear short soft hairs to longer pubescence. The style is longer than 10 mm, and the petals are reddish.
