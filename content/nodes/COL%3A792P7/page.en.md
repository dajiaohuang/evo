## Overview {#s-b6f1894029}
This page follows the COL26.8 accepted name Pygathrix cinerea. Its English common name comes from the locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 record; the Chinese rendering is editorial. Available field sources include one year of feeding observations at Kon Ka Kinh National Park and historical surveys in two protected forests in Gia Lai.

## Research observations {#s-af366b622b}
Feeding observations at Kon Ka Kinh ran from August 2007 to July 2008 and covered 480 hours, 7,390 observations and 880 feeding records; 166 food-plant species were identified. The study reported that young leaves dominated the dry-season diet, while the proportion of fruit rose and young leaves fell in the wet season. Food composition also differed significantly among months.

## Distribution records {#s-3648c00928}
Surveys in 2004 and 2006 covered 37 transects and trails totalling about 185 km in Gia Lai’s Kon Cha Rang Nature Reserve and Kon Ka Kinh National Park. They observed 10 groups, estimated at about 139–150 animals; most observations were in montane evergreen forest at 900–1,300 m. This describes the two survey areas at that time, not present-day species-wide abundance.
