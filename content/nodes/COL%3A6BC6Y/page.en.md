## Accepted name and placement {#s-5c1c3cfd0a}
COL26.8 records Craugastor opimus (Savage & Myers, 2002) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The placement refers to the release dated 20 August 2026; it is separate from the historical AmphiBIO classification.
