## Stem surface {#s-f43ec848a2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a silky stem covering, with hairs that curve inward. It lists simple hairs and thread-like, unbranched bristles; branched and glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-5850e1a794}
The stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are caducous and fall early. The record marks extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) and projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) as absent. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are larger than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-7923c5d1c6}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, is marked as lacking lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and a tomentose covering of dense, matted hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-a6cc9bd095}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not identify a collection locality or the species’ full range.
