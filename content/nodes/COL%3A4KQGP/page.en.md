## Podocoma blanchetiana {#s-092212fca9}
## Woolly stems and beaked fruits

The Brazilian Flora record describes stems with loose woolly hairs and alternate leaves forming a loose basal rosette. Leaf blades range from oblanceolate to obovate or broadly obovate, with a central midrib and lateral veins arising above the base. The cypselae, or dry one-seeded fruits, have long beaks. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
