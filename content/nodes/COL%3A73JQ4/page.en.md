## What features characterize Microstachys revoluta? {#s-54aacdb490}
The species lacks a xylopodium. Its erect, woody branches bear stiff, appressed hairs. The same-colored, papery leaves are ovate, with blunt bases, pointed tips and entire margins rolled under; a few glands occur at the blade base and others along the edge. Ovate stipules accompany them. Spiral flower clusters have a hairless axis; their torn-edged bracts carry stalkless glands. Female flowers have no glands, rounded sepals, joined styles and a hairless ovary. The round fruit bears many conspicuous pyramid-shaped projections on each segment, and the stigma persists. The seed lacks a coat layer (testa).
