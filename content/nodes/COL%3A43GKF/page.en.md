## Mimosa multiplex {#s-aa6dcd8ef0}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa multiplex* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes a hairless stem and stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) that fall away. Its flower clusters are globose; flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), 1–3 veins in the petal lobes, short-haired lobes, and pink stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers). The entry also records a terrestrial setting.
