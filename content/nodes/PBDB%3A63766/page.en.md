## What these records can show {#s-c48262dc3e}
This is a guide to 36 selected PBDB occurrence rows in a snapshot dated 2026-10-02, not a species monograph. A locality and an age interval describe their database records; they do not establish a complete geographic range, continuous presence through time, ecology, behavior, or population size. Collection IDs, occurrence IDs, specimen notes, and any reported abundance units remain separate. Locality-coordinate fields and paleocoordinate/model fields are retained as distinct source fields. The listed reference attributions have not been treated as a full-text review.
