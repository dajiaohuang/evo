## Cyrtanthus brachyscyphus {#s-517a308a74}
Cyrtanthus brachyscyphus is a small-flowered bulbous geophyte with bright-red flowers in an umbel. Its slender perianth tube widens near the throat, while the short lobes do not spread; flowering is recorded mainly from September to November, sometimes later. Shaded stream banks among rocks in indigenous forest are the cited habitat.
