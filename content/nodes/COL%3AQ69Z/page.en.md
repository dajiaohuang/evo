## Calydorea riograndensis {#s-3a13b2edc4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records an underground bulb and keeled leaf blades for Calydorea riograndensis, with a terminal inflorescence bearing stalked rhipidia. The flowers are described as white, lilac or bluish and without glandular hairs. Anthers are about 4–4.5 mm long; unlike the undivided style reported for C. charruana, this account records a three-branched style whose undivided portion is about 3.2–5 mm. The measurements and colour terms belong to this regional dataset entry.
