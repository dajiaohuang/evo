## Gethyllis campanulata: leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-fe296162f8}
A 2012 regional account describes a bulbous geophyte up to 15 cm tall that usually forms clumps. It records 9–23 leaves, dry at flowering, nearly upright and linear, with little twisting. The leaf surfaces are smooth or slightly hairy, with short hairs. The large, cup-shaped flowers are cream-coloured and have 12 anthers arranged in six pairs. The berry is fleshy and yellow.
