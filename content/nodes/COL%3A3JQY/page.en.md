## Which Causus species have trait records? {#s-2b62c4649c}
Pinned COL26.8 groups 3 ReptTraits v1-2 species records under Causus: Causus defilippii, C. resimus and C. rhombeatus. Each record keeps its trait citations attached to its own species row. This set offers a reading route into the genus, not its full accepted-species count.

## What do the body-length values measure? {#s-bc3e3b7644}
Among the available size records, Causus defilippii has a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 317 mm and C. rhombeatus 630 mm. SVL runs from the snout to the vent. These compiled maxima belong to different species; they are not a within-species range or the actual extremes for the genus. Activity, diet and reproductive fields each need to be read with their own sources.

## What does the life-history record cover? {#s-27df4967d6}
The historical life-history route for Causus has 1 record, for Causus rhombeatus. Adult body mass, offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, time from egg laying to hatching, and the compilation's longest-lifespan record each appear once. These counts describe source coverage, not the number of species in the genus. Check the event, unit and source behind each field before comparing values.

## What these records can support {#s-bec36254d7}
These are selected historical records, not a complete Causus species list. The ReptTraits route requires at least four sourced focal traits plus a size or reproductive value and a name-and-family match in COL; the life-history route requires at least three sourced focal fields and the same name-and-family match. Reported body lengths are compiled maxima, not averages or identification thresholds. Medians, longest-lifespan records and inherited historical taxonomy retain the definitions of their source fields; they do not settle later taxonomic revisions or population differences. Current population and conservation status are not covered here, and a genus-level route does not imply that every member shares one reproductive mode or life history.

## Which traits can be compared across species? {#s-ac5b1c7b13}
Each species page reports only fields with its own source. Size, activity, diet or reproductive traits can be compared when both records use the same field definition; a missing value cannot be supplied from a relative. Turtle straight carapace length (SCL) and another taxon's snout–vent length (SVL) measure different things.

## Coverage and limits of comparison {#s-8ece8959cf}
These pages cover only entries matching COL by name and family and carrying at least one sourced focal trait; they are not a complete list of descendants. Reported maximum body lengths are compiled records, not averages or identification thresholds. Read each field with its own source and aggregation rule. Historical one-row records cannot settle every later taxonomic revision or population difference.
