## Neea robusta {#s-3b43e6c0f8}
## Erect terminal cymes above smooth leaves

Neea robusta has opposite, stalked leaves that are hairless on both surfaces. The source lists elliptic, oblanceolate or oblong-elliptic blades, with entire margins, pointed or blunt tips and acute bases. Erect, many-flowered cymes terminate the branches. The male and female perianths are not described, but an intrastaminal floral disc is absent. The fleshy anthocarp has closed cupule lobes. Habitat is labeled terrestrial.
