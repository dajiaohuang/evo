## *Ischnosiphon grandibracteatus*: branching stems and asymmetrical leaves {#s-6df53690a4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional profile describes upright, branching stems and asymmetrical ovate leaves. Each small flower cluster has two bracteoles, the smaller leaf-like structures immediately beneath the flowers. The petals are recorded as white, while the outer staminode—a sterile, stamen-derived floral part—is yellow to cream.

The accompanying habitat field says only “terrestrial,” without a named locality or date. The entry supplies floral and leaf characters, but no population-level measurements.
