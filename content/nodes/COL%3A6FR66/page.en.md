## Three fields in the source row {#s-d95e6affca}
Pinned COL26.8 places Epictia subcrotilla in Leptotyphlopidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 row reports maximum total length, microhabitat and reproductive mode, each with a field-specific source. The workbook names follow The Reptile Database (2023); the accepted-name match does not establish full equivalence between historical and current taxon concepts.

## A reported total length {#s-c60ee8de50}
The row gives a maximum total length (TL) of 184 mm, attributed to Feldman et al. (2016). TL includes the tail; snout–vent length (SVL) ends at the vent and is a different measurement. This compiled maximum is not a typical individual size or an absolute limit for the species.

## Microhabitat {#s-f56ba1fee3}
The microhabitat field is “fossorial,” sourced to Feldman (2015). The workbook treats this as one category among arboreal, cryptic, saxicolous, semi-aquatic and terrestrial labels, which may also be combined. It is not a detailed habitat or observation-site description.

## Reproductive mode {#s-ce15077ae8}
The row records “oviparous,” citing Zimin et al. (2022). The record gives no clutch size, annual clutch count or egg dimensions; those details cannot be inferred from its reproductive-mode label.

## Limits of the compiled record {#s-8586569782}
The row combines one compiled length and two categories, not a full account of morphology, ecology or reproduction. It provides no sex-specific lengths or population-level comparison. Source labels preserve the workbook’s citation chain, not an independent rereading of every cited study.

## Other fields not established {#s-1d5f289f53}
The row has no reader-grounded SVL or sex-specific length, reproductive counts, egg dimensions, diet, active time, foraging mode or sex-determining category. These fields remain open; values from relatives or blank cells cannot complete the species record.
