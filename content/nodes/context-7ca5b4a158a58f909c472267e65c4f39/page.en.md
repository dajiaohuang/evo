## The source concept {#s-950d31549b}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Amietophrynus mauritanicus* in row Anf0376 under Amietophrynus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-e1555a698c}
For Anf0376, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-91bc371657}
The adult food fields list arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; these tags do not make a complete diet or give the share of food items.

## Activity periods {#s-cc5d39ea07}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Seasonal activity {#s-d8d6b2791f}
The seasonal fields tag wet and warm and dry and warm. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-99ef54905d}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 150 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-2ed05fd57e}
The row records 10000 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, a minimum offspring or egg size of 1.5 mm and a maximum offspring or egg size of 1.7 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every individual, clutch or offspring.
