## Monotagma tomentosum {#s-c54bcdb988}
## Green bracts that expand at flowering

This source describes no root tubers, a green midrib and hairy undersides of the leaves. The pulvinus has rings, and the leaf blades are asymmetric. Terminal flowering is present; its peduncle exceeds 2.5 cm and the synflorescence has two to nine nodes. Green bracts expand at anthesis. The outer staminode is spatulate, and the habitat field says terrestrial.
