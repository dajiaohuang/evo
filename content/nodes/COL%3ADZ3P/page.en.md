## What features are recorded for Angelonia crassifolia? {#s-1e469a47a2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes pubescent stems and opposite leaves that narrow toward the base, with blades shaped between inversely lance-shaped and inversely egg-shaped. Flowers occur singly in the leaf axils. The record also notes a linear floral appendage with a split tip; it does not specify further function in this field.

## What habitat category is given? {#s-df1f19276d}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a particular locality.
