## Leaf, flower and fruit characters in the source record {#s-6b2211bace}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records stems that are sparsely pubescent or strigulose. Stipules are subulate or lanceolate; the petiole is under 5 cm, while rachis length is listed as under 5 cm or 11–20 cm. Extrafloral nectaries are recorded as absent, including their position and form. Leaves are listed with 4 pairs, 5 pairs or 6–10 pairs of lanceolate leaflets with acuminate tips. The raceme has obovate bracts. Flowers are asymmetric, with an obovate central adaxial petal and seven fertile stamens. The fruit is a dehiscent legume, erect and flat, with external depressions between seeds; seeds are arranged in one series. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
