## Anf1779 and its source comment {#s-591e9f0ae9}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf1779 names Ingerana alpina and records Ingerana, Dicroglossidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

The AmphiBIO OBS field preserves the source comment “Considered as Limnonectes alpinus by Frost 2014”. It is presented as a historical note, not as an independently verified current taxonomic or conservation conclusion.
