## Broad leaflets and fewer stamens {#s-75dafb45c3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records six to 12 pairs of oblong-elliptic to broadly elliptic leaflets on spiral leaves. Yellow petals differ in shape, and the source records fewer than ten stamens without giving an exact count. The seed is flattened, from diamond-like to inverted-egg-shaped. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; fruit form is marked unknown.
