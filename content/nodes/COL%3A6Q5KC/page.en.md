## Life-history evidence limits {#s-de05cab206}
This account uses historical same-name rows from the August 2015 life-history archive. Its summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. They are not direct measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The archive may share or inherit data among historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family supports this association but does not prove equivalence with the COL26.8 concept or remove every inherited component. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually. Diet, behavior, present-day range, conservation and the missing life-history fields need other sources.
