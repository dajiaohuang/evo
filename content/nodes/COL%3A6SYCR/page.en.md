## Orthosia selloana {#s-07c13c5b08}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Orthosia selloana* as an accepted species of *Orthosia*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry says terrestrial in the habitat field. That category does not identify a collection locality or define a complete range; the entry has no morphology claim.
