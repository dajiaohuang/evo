## Lychnophora diamantinana {#s-5593a560ad}
## Pale outer pappus scales beyond the florets

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes fascicled roots and stem leaves. Leaf veins loop near the margin, and tips are acute or end in a small mucro. Each head has at least five florets. An outer pappus row is present and consists of pale, scale-like structures. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
