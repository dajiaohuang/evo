## How many short female spikelets are listed? {#s-7ddbbbfc33}
A 2012 regional account describes *Thamnochortus muirii* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1.2 m, with simple culms (stems). It bears several female spikelets (small flower clusters), each up to 15 mm long. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## What is its coastal sand usually associated with? {#s-5a6af0948f}
The regional account lists coastal sands, usually associated with limestone. It does not provide a survey date or complete range.
