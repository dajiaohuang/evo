## Epidendrum fulgens {#s-2d759e70d9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description gives Epidendrum fulgens unbranched stems, scattered oblong leaves and a corymb-like raceme. Its sepals and petals are red to orange and may be spotted; the three-lobed lip is orange or yellow with spots and a toothed edge. The habitat fields include rupicolous and terrestrial categories.
