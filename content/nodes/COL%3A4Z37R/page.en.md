## Long-acuminate leaves and a warty fruit tip {#s-0866ff1fb6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records glabrous, cylindrical stems with internodes shorter than the leaf blades. Leaves are linear or lanceolate, with tapered bases, long-acuminate tips, and hairs along the margins. Developed peduncles carry more than four flowers. The linear sepals are hairless; the tubular corolla is white or white with pink or purple spots. The ovary is hairless and the stamens remain within the corolla. The fruit is hairless except for a muricate, wart-like apex; seeds are subglobose or kidney-shaped.
