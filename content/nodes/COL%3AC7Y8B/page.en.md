## Distinguish the measured length {#s-0f0cc878d7}
The recorded maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 84 mm; the female maximum is also 84 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. These are maxima in the compiled material, not theoretical limits or typical individual sizes. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and do not alone measure sexual dimorphism across a population.
