## What root, leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-eed309e842}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves are linear or oval, with blunt, acute or squared-off bases and flat to slightly rolled-under margins. The inflorescence is bracted. Flower stalks are 0.1–6 mm long, and small bracteoles are present. The floral tube is 3–10 mm long and hairy, with long or soft hairs; its blunt spur points horizontally or downward. Petals are purple or red and are absent by the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube. No vesicle is recorded; a nectar gland at the ovary base may point downward or horizontally. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin thickened or blunt. These are alternatives in a regional database row, not a single measured specimen.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-71c4157cff}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no collection locality or date.
