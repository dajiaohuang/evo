## What branch, leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Gymnanthes discolor? {#s-45c8b69e85}
Its branches are stiff and lack spines. Alternate leaves have hairless scale-like leaves at the shoot base and veins that loop near the blade edge. Small inflorescences occur singly at branch tips or in leaf axils. Male flowers have three sepals partly joined at the base. Female sepals lack glands, and the smooth ovary and fruit have no appendages. The seed has a caruncle, a small attachment at one end.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5a6cea052a}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
