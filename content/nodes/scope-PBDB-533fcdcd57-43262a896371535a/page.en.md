## Mammaliaformes depends on definitions and matrices {#s-54d9124a36}
A sampled morphological analysis distinguishes Mammaliaformes from the narrower crown-based Mammalia and places several early Mesozoic groups around that boundary. Membership depends on definitions and character matrices; the analysis is not a continuous ancestor sequence. The 225–0 Ma display is the review’s definition-dependent sample envelope, not a crown-Mammalia origin date or complete global range.
