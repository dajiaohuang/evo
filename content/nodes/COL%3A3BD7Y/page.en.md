## Bark, leaves and flowers {#s-d3d197262f}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry describes a dark-brown trunk with whitish streaks; the bark surface is recorded as scaly or smooth, and prickles are present. The petioles are armed, while the leaf blades are unarmed and alike in colour on both sides. Leaflets are ovate or rhombic with entire margins. Terminal, upright pseudo-racemes bear pink, tubular standard petals; the calyx is tubular, truncate and fleshy. The free keel petals are sickle-shaped. The stamens are arranged in two bundles, with anthers attached at their bases. The fruit is a hairless, oblong, bead-like legume; seeds are kidney-shaped and red and black. These traits come from one regional row, not a complete or global account.
