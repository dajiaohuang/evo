## Recorded parent of Glyptodontidae {#s-b4dcc6bb40}
Glyptodontidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Glyptodontoidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
