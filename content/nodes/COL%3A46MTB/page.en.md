## *Neomirandea croatii*: epiphytic shrubs in a single-collection record {#s-b8f91d201f}
The Flora of Panama account, retrieved on 8 September 2026, describes a slightly fleshy epiphytic shrub to 1 m tall, with densely hairy stems and opposite, thick leaves. Its branching flower clusters may reach 15 cm wide; the small heads usually contain five lavender or purple florets. The fruitlets are 2.5–3 mm long, with a pappus of about 50 bristles. The account says the species was known only from its type collection. That statement reflects this historical flora record, not a current survey.
