## Taytalura’s skull preserves a mosaic of early diapsid and lepidosaur traits {#s-f82ef362aa}
Micro-CT reveals cranial characters inside the three-dimensionally preserved holotype PVSJ 698. Its skull combines early diapsid and lepidosaur-like states—the anatomical evidence behind the study’s phylogenetic test.

Across tested data partitions and optimality criteria, the analyses recover Taytalura as an early-diverging stem lepidosauromorph, outside crown Lepidosauria. This is a position inferred from one skull; it neither makes Taytalura a direct ancestor nor provides a whole-body record.
