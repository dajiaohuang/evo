## Taytalura’s three-dimensional skull and its uncertain branch {#s-f82ef362aa}
Micro-CT reveals cranial characters in the three-dimensionally preserved holotype PVSJ 698. Across the cited analyses it is recovered as an early-diverging stem lepidosauromorph outside crown Lepidosauria. The placement was tested under multiple settings, but remains a phylogenetic result from one skull; it does not identify Taytalura as a direct ancestor or establish whole-body anatomy.
