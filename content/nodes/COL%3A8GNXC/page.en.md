## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d18670a082}
COL26.8 records Ahaetulla travancorica as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-7f0db369e0}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Deepak et al. 2022. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-47d60142c8}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
