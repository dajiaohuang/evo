## What leaf and axillary flower traits are recorded? {#s-9407d70766}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes erect branches and sessile, oval leaves with one vein. The lower leaf surface may have glandular hairs or simple hairs without glands; the upper surface may have simple non-glandular hairs. Margins are flat or not rolled under. Flowers are solitary and axillary. The inner tip of the floral cup is hairless. Petals are pink, and anthers are straight or slightly curved. Toothed appendages between the sepals are absent. The capsule is about as long as the floral cup, with many seeds in each chamber. These are regional database alternatives.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a134744904}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; it gives no locality or collection date.
