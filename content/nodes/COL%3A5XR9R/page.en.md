## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f745d2c51a}
COL26.8 records Chelodina burrungandjii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-fb74ad1553}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 316 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Thomson et al. 2000.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-e1ebb92d7c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Thomson et al. 2000.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-9f7cef8668}
The row reports 2 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-390bd07a15}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
