## Source classification and concept status {#s-141b5046f0}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3000 records *Phyllomedusa nordestina* under the source classification Phyllomedusa (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging category (arboreal) {#s-6c6a177836}
AmphiBIO records an arboreal category for this row. It is a broad category, not a fine-scale habitat description. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-c27a100972}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Night-time activity {#s-f569066783}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-a2c43b29dc}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 43.7 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-e8b98db2cc}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
