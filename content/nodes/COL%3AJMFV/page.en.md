## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-ab7fe9732d}
The source describes the stem as underground or very short and gives a diameter below 25 cm. Leaf sheaths are fibrous; petioles exceed 50 cm. The pinnae are evenly arranged at one angle along the slightly curved leaf axis, whose surface bears whitish hairs. In bisexual inflorescences, female flowers pair with male flowers in the basal part of each small branch, while only male flowers occur farther out. Male flowers have six stamens; the female flowers have three stigmas. The fruit has a narrow middle layer and a smooth, non-striate inner wall, with one or more cavities and inconspicuous internal fibres.

The source records an underground or very short stem with a stated diameter below 25 cm; fibrous leaf sheaths; petioles over 50 cm; evenly arranged pinnae inserted at one angle on a slightly curved, whitish-hairy rachis; bisexual inflorescences with pistillate flowers paired with staminate flowers in basal rachilla portions and only staminate flowers distally; six stamens and three stigmas; and a narrow mesocarp with homogeneous, non-striate endocarp, one or more cavities, and inconspicuous internal fibres.
