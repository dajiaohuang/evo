## Distribution records {#s-9e81d7ea7b}
The supplement records two observations at Candeias do Jamari and Itapuã do Oeste, Rondônia, under Leontocebus weddelli weddelli. COL26.8 places that subspecies under the accepted species L. weddelli; the records are not a range-wide survey.

## Evidence limits {#s-23379ff471}
This guide summarizes records from selected sites; it is not a complete species account, range survey, population estimate, or conservation assessment. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation.
