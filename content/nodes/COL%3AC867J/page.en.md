## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-70ba0efccb}
COL26.8 records Psammophis trivirgatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Psammophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-6e4e4ead1a}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cottone and Bauer 2009.
