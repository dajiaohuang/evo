## What does the description record about its leaves and tubular flowers? {#s-1072f22406}
Torenia thouarsii is described as an ascending herb up to 20 cm tall. Opposite leaves are lance-shaped to ovate, about 1.5–2.5 cm long, with roughly six toothed notches on each edge. One to three flowers arise in a leaf axil. Their tubular calyx lengthens slightly in fruit, and the bluish, purplish or whitish corolla is about 10 mm long with five lobes. The capsule is narrowly ovoid-conical and splits along its internal partitions; the source gives no sample size or range.

## Where does the historical flora report it as native or naturalized? {#s-bfc320dc64}
The 2013 Flora of Panama field describes Torenia thouarsii as native to the Indian Ocean region and naturalized in the New World tropics. It lists Trinidad and a range from Peru to Costa Rica, plus the lower Orinoco in Venezuela. These are the source's historical regional statements, not a current global inventory; the source version was retrieved on 8 September 2026.
