## The named clade includes unresolved historical labels {#s-be56da8e07}
The paper uses the ginesi clade for related summit species. An undescribed Stefania sp.4, sister to upuigmae in cited earlier molecular work, had previously been confused with ginesi. It remains unnamed here and is not a new accepted ginesi record. Larger tubercles and a greyish-blue iris appear in the new-species comparison, but external similarities and conserved skeletons make historical assignments uncertain. The cited Pliocene divergence near4million years is not a new molecular-clock estimate in this description.
