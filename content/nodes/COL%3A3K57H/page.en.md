## Heliamphora hispida Wistuba & Nerz {#s-0ba250d93a}
## Funnel-shaped pitchers with an expanded opening

Heliamphora hispida forms a stemless rosette with pitchers 10–20 cm tall. The pitchers narrow toward the middle and widen again at the opening. Their rounded, hood-like lids may be sessile or stalked. Sparse, stiff hairs 2–4 mm long occur in the upper third of the inner surface. The flower stalk is smooth, but the individual flower stalks are hairy. The flowers have up to 15 stamens; the anthers are 6–9 mm long and the tepals 3–5 cm long.
