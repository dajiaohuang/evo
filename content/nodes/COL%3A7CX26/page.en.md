## Name and this data account {#s-dae9ac1eaa}
COL26.8 accepts Tropidolaemus laticinctus in Viperidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 entry follows the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and retains trait-specific citations. The single species row summarizes compiled records, not one uniform condition across populations.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-a95f9b39de}
The reported maximum total length (TL), including the tail, is 1,300 mm. Snout–vent length (SVL) ends at the vent and measures a different dimension. This compiled maximum describes the source records, not typical size or an absolute growth limit. Values for different sexes may come from separate places or studies and do not establish size differences within a population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-d6ab267bf3}
The entry records arboreal microhabitat, a carnivorous diet, and nocturnal activity. These categories do not identify prey or describe every habitat or activity period. The compiled row does not establish whether populations, seasons, or life stages share these patterns.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-246b911f01}
The entry labels reproductive mode as viviparous and reports a consolidated mean of 15 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch. Such summaries may be midpoints of reported means, or of reported extremes when means are unavailable; they are not necessarily averages of observed clutches. A fractional summary would not represent part of an individual. The dataset defines lowest and highest values as endpoints of reported means, not the smallest and largest observed clutches. These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-89f710354f}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
