## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-299a0f1a46}
COL26.8 records Eryx vittatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Boidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-3c50b26a4e}
