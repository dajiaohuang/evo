## Sponge-like anatomy in a single fossil {#s-391834a052}
One phosphatized Eocyathispongia specimen preserves a millimetre-scale, multicellular body made of three tubes, with funnel-like and osculum-like openings. Electron microscopy and tomography reveal its cellular geometry, but the sponge-grade interpretation rests on this single specimen, without spicules or an independently repeated canal system.
