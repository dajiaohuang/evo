## Monstera spruceana: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-20e4f292fa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes juvenile leaves as appressed. The petiole sheath is marcescent or deciduous. Leaf blades are regularly pinnately lobed, with tertiary veins parallel to the secondary veins; the pinna bases are not narrowed and their divisions are deep.

The upper surface of the spathe is listed as light green or white, and the peduncle is shorter than the spadix. The style-region apex is truncate, stigmas are linear, and the berry is white.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative sheath and spathe-color states are retained as reported.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
