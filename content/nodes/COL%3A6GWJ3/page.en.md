## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-499c2f3402}
The royal entry is grouped with early Last Glacial Period decline followed by late recovery, while E. c. chrysolophus declines strongly across the period. Different curves of close lineages cannot be averaged into one trend or directly determine present species boundaries or abundance. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
