## What hides its female flowers? {#s-6f702f4152}
A 2012 regional account describes *Elegia rigida* as a neatly tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 50 cm. Its female spathes, the bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) around the flowers, are 1–2 cm long and overlap; their tips bend backward and completely obscure the flowers. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## Which seasonally wet landscape is named? {#s-b10f4a0b4b}
The regional flora account lists seasonally wet plateaus. This habitat line is not a complete range or a dated survey record.
