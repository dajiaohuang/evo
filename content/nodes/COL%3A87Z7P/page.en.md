## Begonia sanguinea {#s-022e3b1a5e}
## A hair collar marks the petiole tip

Begonia sanguinea has erect stems, persistent stipules and hairless petioles with a collar of hairs at the tip. Its strongly asymmetric, transversely ovate basal leaves have entire margins and smooth surfaces. Male flower segments are glandular; female segments may be smooth or glandular, with entire margins. Bracteoles are present. The capsule wings are unequal and the seed tip blunt. The habitat label is rocky.
