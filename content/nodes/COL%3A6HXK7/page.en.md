## *Ficus duartei* {#s-e123201aa0}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — hairless; leaf-related structures — oblong, tapering tips. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as hemiepiphytic (growing on another plant; Portuguese: Hemiepífita). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
