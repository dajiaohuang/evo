## Reusing an existing genome rather than adding a field sample {#s-c2865b8fbc}
The 2019 study compares an existing genome of Struthio camelus with eleven added palaeognath genomes. Existing material retains its own provenance: vouchers for the ten newly sequenced living birds are not assigned to this species. Sequence counts describe marker coverage, not multiple independent individuals of this species; the two companion papers share the comparison material.

## The ostrich split provides a comparison {#s-dc394f740a}
The 2019 comparison reuses an existing genome of this species. The ostrich lineage is recovered as the earliest split among sampled palaeognaths, providing a reference for relationships among other lineages. Living ostriches have continued evolving: an early split does not imply an unchanged ancestral state or substitute for other Struthio species absent from this genome set. Flight-loss counts depend on ancestral-state and relationship models rather than a fixed tally of living genera.
