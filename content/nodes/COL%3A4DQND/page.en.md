## Parodia crassigibba {#s-f566fd1cb3}
## White-haired flowers sit below the stem tip

The account lists several alternatives: stems may be solitary or clustered, disk-shaped or globose, with continuous or slightly tuberculate ribs. The areoles are distinct; spines range from fine and inconspicuous to bristly, and central spines are absent. Flowers arise just below the stem tip, with abundant white hairs; petals may be yellow, pale or pink, with rounded tips and smooth margins. The stigma is red or vinaceous. Fruit and seed traits are marked unknown. The habitat label is rocky.
