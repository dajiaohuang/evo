## Accepted name and placement {#s-1fa6956576}

## Yunkaishan type-locality knowledge {#s-5d5ea6e0ec}
Male holotype SYS a004637 came from Yunkaishan Nature Reserve, Xinyi, Guangdong, at approximately 950 m. The monograph cited Qi and colleagues (2021) for records only from the type locality at 900–1400 m. These known collection elevations are not a demonstrated physiological range or evidence that the species is absent outside the reserve.

## Nine adults with sampled spiny skin characters {#s-70aecbcfd0}
The examined type series contained six adult males at 35.3–40.0 mm snout–vent length and three adult females at 45.3–46.1 mm. The diagnosis described rough dorsal skin, dense tubercles on the ventral thighs, spiny tubercles around the cloaca, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. Adult males were described with tiny spines on the upper lip, upper eyelid and loreal and temporal regions, excluding the tympanum. Breeding males also had fine, dense black nuptial spines on finger I and II pads and a subgular vocal sac. These sampled structures do not establish their functional effects or breeding-season timing.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-705f93e990}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys yunkaiensis as with wide lateral toe fringes. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
