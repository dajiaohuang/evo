## PBDB classification and 22 selected species pages {#s-3574418528}
PBDB lists Tyrannosauridae at family rank, and this guide links 22 selected fossil species pages. The recorded parent chain is a database classification; it does not establish phylogeny or complete family membership.

## 153 occurrences across 149 collections {#s-e9828424a3}
Five linked pages—Nanuqsaurus hoglundi, Thanatotheristes degrootorum, Daspletosaurus horneri, D. wilsoni and D. torosus—offer a starting set for comparing the records and cited literature. The selected guide contains 153 occurrences from 149 collections. Prince Creek, Foremost, Two Medicine and Dinosaur Park appear as example stratum names. Compare each occurrence’s age and identification fields with its source; these counts describe database entries, not animals or populations.

## Brusatte et al.’s sampled family window {#s-329b664fcd}
Brusatte et al.’s 2010 review supports a sampled Tyrannosauridae window of 84–66 Ma, not exact global origin or extinction dates. The review synthesizes anatomy, growth and biomechanics. Its family-wide patterns remain bounded by that sample; behavior is inferential, and Tyrannosaurus is not a proxy for every member.
