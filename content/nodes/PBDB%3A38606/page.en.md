## Tyrannosauridae in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-3574418528}
PBDB lists Tyrannosauridae at family rank. This guide follows selected fossil-species entries as a route into the records, not as a complete family catalogue. The database parent chain records classification; it does not establish phylogeny.

## Selected tyrannosaurid species and named strata {#s-e9828424a3}
The selected records link Nanuqsaurus hoglundi, Thanatotheristes degrootorum and three Daspletosaurus species. Prince Creek, Foremost, Two Medicine and Dinosaur Park are named strata in this set. Read each occurrence with its own identification and age fields; these database records do not count animals or populations.

## Tyrannosaurid anatomy, growth and biomechanics {#s-329b664fcd}
Brusatte et al.’s 2010 review synthesizes sampled tyrannosaurid anatomy, growth and biomechanics. It cautions against treating Tyrannosaurus as a proxy for every member. Behavior and family-wide generalization remain inferences bounded by the reviewed sample; the synthesis does not set exact global range endpoints.

## Tyrannosauridae in the review’s 84–66 Ma sample window {#s-review-window-8466-en}
The 84–66 Ma interval is the sampled Tyrannosauridae record described in Brusatte et al.’s 2010 review. It is a source-bounded window, not a claim about exact global origination or extinction, a global FAD or LAD, continuous occupancy, divergence, or direct ancestry.
