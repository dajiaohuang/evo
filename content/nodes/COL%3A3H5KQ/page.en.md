## *Graffenrieda moaensis* {#s-8d1d178c52}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — elliptic, entire, untoothed margins, tapering tips; flower clusters (inflorescences) — terminal; flowers — white. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as terrestrial (Portuguese: Terrícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
