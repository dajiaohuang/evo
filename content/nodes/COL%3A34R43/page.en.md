## Many flowers in a simple umbrella-shaped cluster {#s-10eab04101}
The record describes thin, elliptic to slightly obovate leaves, with seven or more pairs of side veins. Small dots mark the underside, and the margins may be smooth or finely toothed near the tip. Flowers gather in simple umbels—clusters whose stalks radiate from one point—with more than 30 flowers recorded per umbel.
