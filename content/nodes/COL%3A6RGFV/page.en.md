## A scaly underside and a one-sided flower cluster {#s-24da671c53}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes scale-like hairs on young stems and dense scale-like covering on the leaf underside. The leaves are elliptic or obovate (egg-shaped, widest toward the tip), with pointed or gradually tapering tips and narrowed or rounded bases. Their margins are entire and lack fringe-like hairs. Three main veins arise at or above the base, with two faint veins just inside the edge. Flowers are secund, arranged along one side of the cluster axis. The bell-shaped flower cup bears a persistent calyx with flat-ended lobes; each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the pollen sacs is not prolonged; the appendage is listed as two lower-side lobes or one back-facing tooth. White anthers have one small pore, and the ovary bears hairs. The dossier supplies no habitat field, so it does not support a habitat description.
