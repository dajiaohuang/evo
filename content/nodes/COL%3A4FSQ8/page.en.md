## A one-night call survey gave a local density estimate {#s-3113a3f61f}
At Kirindy, researchers used 12 playback experiments to assess call-detection distance and placed four automated recorders along one fixed transect; they manually annotated 10,380 Phaner calls from one complete dry-season night. The model estimated about 38.6 animals per km² within the truncated 150-m-radius detection area of roughly 0.07 km².

## What a separate playback study showed {#s-97ca9989f6}
That value is a model estimate for a local acoustic detection area, not a density for all Kirindy Forest or the species’ range. A separate playback experiment reported approaches only to conspecific calls; the result is bounded by its recordings and playback setting.
