## Leaflets, flower heads and pods {#s-3bbb0b8ede}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row records 10 to 20 pairs of pinnae and 30 to 50 or more pairs of pinnules, the small divisions of a twice-divided leaf. It places oblong nectaries near the middle of the leaf stalk and describes clustered, head-like racemes. The fruit is listed as straight or curved with wavy to constricted margins and a warty or scaly surface; the rounded seeds lack wings. The field offers alternatives for several traits and is not a population survey.
