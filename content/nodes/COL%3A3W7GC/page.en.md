## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}

Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Lorius domicella (Linnaeus, 1758) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## A field study in context {#seram-field-records}

During fieldwork on Seram in February 2012, Reeve and colleagues reported Purple-naped Lories as fairly common in primary forest at 800-1,000 m above Kanikeh. From 6-11 February they recorded 5-15 birds daily, with the largest groups numbering two or three. The authors said these observations raised the known upper altitudinal limit from 900 m; that is a result of this short, site-specific survey, not a full range estimate.
