Zosterops novaeguineae is accepted in COL26.8 within Zosteropidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-f43dabaf3d}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 6.8 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 2.8 mm in width and 3 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-cb06455d61}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 54.6 mm; mean tarsus length is 15.2 mm. Compiled mean mass is 12.5 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.
