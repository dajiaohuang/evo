## Mimosa spixiana {#s-3d79c42e8c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa spixiana* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists the stem as hairless or short-haired and the flower clusters as cylindrical. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), silky to woolly petal lobes, and two rings of stamens. The habitat field records a terrestrial setting.
