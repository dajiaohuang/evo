## A twining vine description from the regional flora {#s-e71ff5d0f0}
The Flora of Panama description calls *Teramnus uncinatus* a long twining vine, mostly growing on shrubs and herbs. Stems are brownish to greyish and sericeous, with long and short hairs; the shorter hairs are reflexed, and the surface becomes glabrescent. This morphology description supplies no separate locality or habitat category and does not define a complete range.
