## Begonia rossmanniae {#s-5a3e111ecc}
## Trailing stems and smooth, toothed leaves

Begonia rossmanniae has trailing stems and deciduous stipules. Its slightly asymmetric leaves are obovate or elliptic, toothed and hairless on both surfaces; petioles are also smooth. Male and female flower segments are hairless, with entire margins on the female flowers. Bracteoles are present and the placenta is divided. Capsule wings are strongly unequal. The seed tip was not observed.
