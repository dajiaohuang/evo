## The workbook codes direct development and terrestrial eggs {#s-5450f9fef8}
The 2023 Atlantic Forest workbook's row 211 codes direct development=1 and terrestrial oviposition=1 for Ischnocnema_parva. In the paper, direct development means there is no free-living larval stage: a froglet hatches rather than a free-living tadpole. It does not mean embryos do not develop or that humidity is unnecessary. These are literature-compiled categories, not a new observation of this species spawning or a developmental experiment; the row's name has not been certified against the current COL26.8 concept.
