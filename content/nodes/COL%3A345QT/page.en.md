## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-3f29a2102f}
The branches grow monopodially. Alternate leaves are elliptic or oblanceolate, with blunt to rounded tips and wedge-shaped to rounded bases. Simple hairs occur on the blades; the upper surface is hairless, while the lower may be woolly or sparsely hairy. Axillary male-flower stalks reach up to 8 cm. Petal-like scales and staminodes are absent. The fruit is recorded as white.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-c722e5de37}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
