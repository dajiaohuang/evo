## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-8b7e21d43f}
For Hymenochirus curtipes, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits.
