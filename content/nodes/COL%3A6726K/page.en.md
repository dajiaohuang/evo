## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-50520035bd}
COL26.8 records Anilios centralis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Typhlopidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-00ac8b297a}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 320 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-1057bace56}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cogger 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-7ce15c7738}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-6a008f7cae}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
