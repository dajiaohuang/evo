## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-d3ba09819e}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Trachycephalus quadrangulum* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Trachycephalus (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.

## A mixed-source urban and periurban checklist record {#s-0ee6188b1b}
The 2026 Guayaquil checklist lists Trachycephalus quadrangulum in conservation areas; unprotected remnant native vegetation. The source combines January 2008–February 2025 field surveys with museum, database, literature and critically screened secondary records. Its focus includes remnant forests and revegetated parks in the urban/periurban footprint, with selected near-boundary areas retained; it is not a uniform present-day census across all 4,113.89 km² of Guayaquil.
