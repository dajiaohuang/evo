## Snout–vent length excludes the tail {#s-2cb0039e88}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 70.7 mm, with a female maximum of 59.8 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent and does not include the tail. These are compiled maxima, not typical sizes or growth limits. The sex-specific figures may come from different studies or places, so their difference alone cannot quantify size dimorphism in one population.

## An arboreal, nocturnal entry {#s-c82e97fa43}
The entry records an arboreal microhabitat, a carnivorous diet and nocturnal activity. These broad labels summarize the cited record rather than every habitat or activity period. “Carnivorous” does not identify prey, and the row cannot describe differences by locality, season or life stage.

## A clutch average between one and two {#s-c4bd692b40}
The source classifies reproduction as oviparous, or egg-laying. Its consolidated offspring/egg summary is 1.5, with reported mean endpoints of 1 and 2. A midpoint may be used when combining reports, so 1.5 is a statistical summary rather than a fractional clutch. The endpoints refer to published means, not guaranteed smallest and largest clutches. These labels do not establish embryo nutrition or uniformity among populations.
