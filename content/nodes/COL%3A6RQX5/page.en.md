## Mucuna bennettii {#s-96cd38a815}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mucuna bennettii* as an accepted species of *Mucuna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record identifies trifoliolate leaves, asymmetrical lateral leaflets, and a diadelphous androecium, meaning the stamens are grouped into two bundles. Many other fields in the source description are blank, so they do not support a fuller account of the flowers, fruit, or seeds.
