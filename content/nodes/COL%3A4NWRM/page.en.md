## Wild-population skin bacterial sampling in a comparative study {#s-e78dc3c16f}
Pseudoeurycea nigromaculata was one of six Mexican plethodontid species contributing 111 newly generated skin-microbiome samples to the 2023 comparative study. The methods identify these six species as sampled from wild populations; 111 is their pooled total, not a count for this species. The study used 16S rRNA amplicons to characterize bacterial communities. Its aquatic or terrestrial habitat labels refer to where and when an animal was sampled, rather than a complete species life-history classification. The methods subsequently refer to “five species” despite listing six plethodontids plus the axolotl; this inconsistent scope is retained and the rinse and swab protocol is not independently certified for every listed species. The pooled diversity and habitat associations are not species-specific infection resistance, a natural-history census or a demonstrated probiotic treatment.
