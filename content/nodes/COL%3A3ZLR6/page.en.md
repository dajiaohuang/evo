## A wide cephalium and spines curved toward it {#s-00428b6905}
This entry allows globose, hemispherical or cylindrical stems, with a white pith and angular ribs. It records nine or more spines, some curved toward the stem or cephalium, and a cephalium wider than tall with conspicuous bristles but no brown tuft. Flowers are reddish; fruits are white to pinkish, and the seed coat is flat. Rock-associated habitat is listed.
