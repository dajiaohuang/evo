## What color are its smooth nuts? {#s-bd4b804836}
A 2012 regional account describes *Willdenowia glomerata* as a rhizomatous (with a horizontal underground stem), dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1.5 m, with smooth, branched culms (stems) and translucent sheath tips. Its flower segments, or tepals, are tiny or absent; the styles are white. The nuts are smooth and black. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Which stony slopes drain well? {#s-8eeacab0da}
The regional account lists well-drained, stony slopes. It does not provide a survey date or a complete range.
