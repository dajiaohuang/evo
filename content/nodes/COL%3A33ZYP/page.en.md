## What floral and seed traits are recorded for Dalechampia purpurata? {#s-6474f8abb2}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium. The record lists three-leaflet compound leaves, with no simple leaves. Its flower cluster has three-lobed surrounding bracts and resin glands. Female sepals are pinnately divided, and the style-column tip is lobed. Fruits bear silky hairs, while seeds are smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-4b100a8397}
It is categorized as terrestrial; the dataset gives no locality.
