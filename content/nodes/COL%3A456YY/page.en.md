## Elliptic leaves and hairy floral parts {#s-0d894d1ccd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and simple stem hairs. Leaves are elliptic or lance-shaped; the upper midrib has a continuous groove, and the underside is hairy. Bracts or cataphylls below leaf nodes are absent. Small flowering branches are rounded, with lance-shaped bracteoles beneath the flowers. The field lists one inflorescence per axillary shoot, axillary or terminal position, and a pyramidal, opposite arrangement.

## A bristly flower cup and an enlarging fruit calyx {#s-6993158322}
The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal, and hairs cover the floral disc. The flower cup is top-shaped and bristly. Sepals are separate, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. On the fruit, the calyx lobes enlarge. The fruit is ellipsoid or cylindrical.
