## The source concept {#s-91f982eaaa}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Mertensophryne schmidti* in row Anf0692 under Mertensophryne, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Developmental strategy {#s-85e055a17f}
The deposited row marks a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
