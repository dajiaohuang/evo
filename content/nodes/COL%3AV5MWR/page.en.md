## The Libo type-locality sample {#s-81c883d304}
The original description recorded the species at its type locality in Libo County, Guizhou, China, at 1100–1200 m elevation. These type-locality records do not establish the full geographic range or an altitude limit.

## Calling beneath stream rocks {#s-f335c5659d}
The described habitat was a mountain stream beneath evergreen broadleaf forest, with little surface water. Males were heard calling beneath rocks at night, and females were frequently found near large rocks. These observations document local habitat use and calling sites, without establishing egg deposition or tadpole requirements.
