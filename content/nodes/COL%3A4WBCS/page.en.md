## Creeping stems with two leaf forms {#s-2e6840c2ed}
The morphology record describes pale, hairy stems that creep along the surface, with root-bearing rhizophores arising from the upper side. The small leaves differ in shape around the stem: side leaves are ovate, while the upper leaves are elliptic to oblong. The record marks the spores yellow and the substrate rupicolous (rock-associated).
