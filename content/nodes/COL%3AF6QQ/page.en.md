## Distinguish the measured length {#s-476d0f604e}
The compiled maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 356 mm; the female maximum is also 356 mm and the male maximum is 178 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. These are maxima in the reported material, not theoretical limits or typical individual sizes. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and cannot alone quantify sexual dimorphism across a population.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-7041c9bf76}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, a mean of 12.2 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, low and high reported means of 3 and 28, and 2.5 litters or clutches per year. A clutch/litter summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. Fractional values are dataset summaries, not counts from one event. The low and high fields are endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed individual-clutch limits. These fields do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-1a6982f506}
This is a limited trait account based on the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 framework and individual trait sources. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure later taxonomic concepts are equivalent. Many dataset mass values are estimated from length equations and are not treated here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions and are not used in the conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated; missing values remain gaps. Citation labels preserve the source chain but do not imply independent review here of every original work.
