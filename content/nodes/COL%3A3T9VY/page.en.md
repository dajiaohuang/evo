## Lepidaploa lilacina {#s-3cef05a6ba}
## Woolly leaves surround white to lilac heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright, woolly stems without branch glands. Alternate leaves sit in two vertical rows on stalks. Elliptic to lance-shaped blades have pointed tips and bases, and both faces are long-hairy; leaf-surface glands are present.

Flower heads grow on leafy stems. Their bell-shaped involucres have overlapping bracts, with the inner bracts tipped by a small point. Each head has 20–30 florets, white or lilac. The pappus is brown, and the fruits have glands. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
