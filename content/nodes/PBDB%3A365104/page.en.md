## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-fc8fbd335f}
In the Paleobiology Database (PBDB) snapshot, Pterodactylus spectabilis is listed under Pterodactylus. It has 1 species-level fossil occurrence record across 1 collection. Each occurrence record connects the taxon with a named collection and age bracket; the sample links to 1 reference ID. These are database and citation counts, not fossil specimens or individual animals.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-da604c90ab}
The selected records span a combined outer age envelope of 149.2–145.06 Ma. The envelope joins the oldest and youngest bounds among these collections, so it does not show continuous fossil presence or directly date the species’ origin, extinction or an individual lifespan. Each collection keeps its own age bracket.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-296472f706}
Eichstätt, Solnhofen (MfN collection) has a recorded collection-environment label lagoonal/restricted shallow subtidal; preservation-mode label mold/impression,adpression,soft parts. These are collection-level labels and assemblage summaries, not descriptions of every fossil. A collection setting alone cannot establish the species’ diet or habitat or show how every specimen was preserved.

## Evidence limits {#s-d07854ee3e}
This guide uses the fixed 276-name pterosaur working list and a PBDB snapshot dated 2026-10-02; it shows selected occurrence records, not a full species monograph. PBDB names and parent paths organize these entries but do not settle specimen identifications or every taxonomic question.
