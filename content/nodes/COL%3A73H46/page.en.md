## What stem, leaf and flower variation does the regional field list? {#s-930ec4c39e}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field allows hairless stems or stems with spherical glands or long-stalked glandular hairs. Petiolate leaves differ in color between the two faces, bear long-stalked glandular hairs, and have three or five veins. Flowers are solitary. The floral cup ranges from oblong to oblong-bell-shaped and bears spherical glands. Triangular sepals have bristles or spherical glands and are shorter than or equal to the cup. Petals may be pink, magenta or purple. Anthers are two-colored and have multiple pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. These are alternatives in a regional dataset field, not a description of one specimen.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-24c0bca10c}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and provides no locality or collection date.
