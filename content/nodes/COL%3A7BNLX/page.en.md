## Accepted name and classification {#s-a0065f3aad}
COL26.8, released 20 August 2026, accepts Taudactylus rheophilus Liem & Hosmer, 1973 as a species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Myobatrachidae (family) → Taudactylus (genus).
