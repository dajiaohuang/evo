## Form in the flora record {#s-8a1b6409c1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem, with cataphylls and prophylls entire at the tip and fibrous at the stem base. Petioles are smooth and flattened, with rounded upper and lower sides. The flat, entire leaf blade is elliptic, with an acute tip and base. Glandular dots are absent. The primary veins are blunt on both surfaces, the secondary veins are indistinct on the upper surface, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is longer than half the leaf length and has a cylindrical peduncle. The flat, elliptic spathe meets the peduncle at an obtuse angle. A stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. The berry is green at the tip and white or green at the base.

The regional dataset row cites Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
