## How the study identified specimens {#s-8da8fc1dce}
For S. gabonicus, the study combines COI (mitochondrial cytochrome-c-oxidase I) barcode groups, mitochondrial 16S comparisons, morphology, and type-locality context. Both markers are mitochondrial, so they provide neither independent nuclear confirmation nor a test of reproductive isolation. The neighbor-joining tree groups sequences by similarity; the authors do not use it to infer evolutionary history. Most species were represented from only one or two localities, and not every GenBank voucher could be checked morphologically. Fieldwork took place in May–June 2012 and March–April 2013; database comparisons used the 2016 GenBank/BOLD snapshot, not a current audit.
