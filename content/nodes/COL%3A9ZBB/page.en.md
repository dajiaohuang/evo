## *Adenocalymma cinereum* {#s-2e35e60e78}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — hairless; leaf-related structures — three-leaflet; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis); flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
