## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-382ee1bd64}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body size of 51.6 mm for this row. For frogs and toads (Anura), the dataset defines body size as snout–vent length: the distance from the snout tip to the vent. This is a compiled maximum, not a typical measurement for every individual.
