## Start with the holotype and locality {#s-cab951f4ea}
Holotype QM F18896 preserves an incomplete snout with an adherent mandible. Phillip Gilmore found it in 1991 at Dunluce Station near Hughenden, within a calcareous nodule from the Toolebuc Formation, assigned to the late Albian in the 2007 paper.

## Internal chambers do not supply a complete head {#s-935b8e2429}
The 2007 authors describe internal chambers, thin bone walls and supports in the snout. Missing anterior and dorsal portions constrain reconstruction of the complete head and any crest.

## The preserved anterior teeth were reinterpreted {#s-a132fea067}
The public reappraisal abstract identifies the anterior preserved structures as a maxillary tooth root and its replacement, rather than contralateral teeth. They therefore cannot establish the anteriormost tooth pair. It also identifies the bone in the nasoantorbital fenestra as the right splenial. These are abstract-supported revisions; the complete article was not obtained in this batch.

## Relationships depend on material and matrices {#s-4fb1848178}
The reappraisal abstract places Mythunga in Anhangueria with much of that clade unresolved. The second-specimen Thapunngaka study groups Mythunga, Thapunngaka and Ferrodraco in an unresolved trichotomy named Mythungini. One analysis in the 2024 Haliskia study identifies Mythunga and Thapunngaka as unstable and excludes them a posteriori. These results are recorded separately; no agreed nearest sister group is assigned, and database storage placement does not adjudicate the debate.
