## *Trichogonia rhodotricha*: broad flower clusters and 20 florets {#s-9593c00cf9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records branched stems with fine hairs and sessile glands. The petiolate leaves are ovate, three-veined, blunt-tipped, and broadly scalloped, with a pale lower surface. The field lists opposite, alternate, or looser leaf arrangements. Flower heads on stalks form open corymb-like panicles; each has about 20 florets and a flat receptacle. Fruits have a ring-shaped attachment base and a 3–4 mm pappus. These are source-field alternatives, not a single combined account.
