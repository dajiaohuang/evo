## Leaf surfaces, perianth and seed characters {#s-c509124de4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes leaves with revolute margins, subcoriaceous texture and narrow oblanceolate to narrowly oblong blades. The surfaces are subtly wrinkled; the apex is acute or acuminate, the base cuneate, and the blade glabrous. Venation is described as brochidodromous or inconspicuous, with secondary veins grooved on both surfaces. Inflorescence indument is glabrescent, and the rachis is labelled monadic in the source field. The perianth is tubular and thick, with slightly zygomorphic lobes; the calyx is fused through the upper half, its lobes reflexed and margins not thickened. The androecium is monadelphous with eight stamens; filaments are fully united into an open sheath, whose cap is free. The fruit has a thick, granular pericarp with a woody transverse section. Seeds are glabrous and have a grooved raphe. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
