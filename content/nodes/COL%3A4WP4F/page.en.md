## Petiolar nectaries and leaf counts in the entry {#s-61e4f4aebb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical branches with irregularly arranged, recurved prickles. Linear stipules accompany one sessile, patelliform petiolar nectary on the middle portion of the petiole; a rachis nectary is present. Leaves are listed with 10–25 pinnae and 35–80 leaflets. The leaflets are linear and glabrous on both surfaces, with oblique or truncate bases and acute tips. The primary vein is subcentral, with no hairs at its base. The inflorescence is head like. Flowers are sessile; the calyx is campanulate or tubular and puberulent, the tubular corolla is puberulent, anther tips bear glands and the ovary is pubescent. The fruit is a legume with elliptic seeds. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
