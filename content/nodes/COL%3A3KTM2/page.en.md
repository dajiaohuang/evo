## What Herre recorded in the described specimen {#type-material}

Herre’s 1923 description identifies its type specimen as a pregnant female, 955 mm long overall, obtained at Dumaguete, Oriental Negros, in March 1922. It contained twelve young, which Herre described as nearly ready for delivery. This is a record for the described material, not an estimate of reproduction across the species.

On the same printed page, Herre separately reports six females (202–218 mm) and six males (five 200–208 mm and one 220 mm), noting differences from the mother. Keep these comparative specimens distinct from the twelve young reported inside the pregnant female; the excerpt does not justify a population-level reproductive estimate or a precise mapping of each comparative specimen to the type.
