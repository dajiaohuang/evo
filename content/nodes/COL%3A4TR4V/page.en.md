## Grey leaves merging into the stems {#s-c8fa0e6665}
The source leaves the shrub's height uncertain and notes that its internodes turn white with age. The grey, three-sided leaves merge into the stems; their blades have two to four teeth along the keel and margins. Solitary flowers have pale purple petals and filament-like staminodes. The fruit has five chambers, and shale soils are recorded.
