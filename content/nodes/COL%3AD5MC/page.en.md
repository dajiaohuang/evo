## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-534cff2558}
COL26.8 records Amphisbaena xera as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-36b82a83e5}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 245 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-2c2390cd6a}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
