## One comparative skull study {#s-d47ece345e}
Martín-Jiménez and Pérez-García (2021) include Hydromedusa tectifera in a comparative CT-based neuroanatomical study of pleurodiran turtles. The sampled skull is MNHN.ZA.AC 1870-9. The paper describes a long, narrow olfactory nerve and weak lateral expansion of the cerebral hemispheres in this reconstruction; these observations remain tied to the individual skull and study methods.
