## Family name and placement {#s-0ad0980798}
COL26.8 records Aphididae at family rank. Its recorded path places the family within Aphidoidea, Aphidomorpha, Sternorrhyncha and Hemiptera.

## The scope of a family entry {#s-61c560fb93}
This node identifies the family within that hierarchy. Family membership alone does not establish a biological trait for every descendant species.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-a7760a40be}
This entry records the name and classification position of Aphididae. It does not provide evidence for a family-wide account of morphology, ecology or life history.
