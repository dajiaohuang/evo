## Jobinia longicoronata {#s-ba99ade039}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Jobinia longicoronata* as an accepted species of *Jobinia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 labels its habitat terrestrial. No locality, survey date, range, or morphology is supplied in that source row, so this remains a single regional habitat category.
