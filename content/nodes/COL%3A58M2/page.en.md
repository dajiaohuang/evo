## Distinguish averages from records {#s-368a9abb5e}
Gestation and female age at first reproduction each appear in 4 of the 5 accounts; maximum lifespan is reported in 3. First reproduction means age when conception is possible, not first birth or sexual maturity. A maximum-lifespan value is a record, not a typical lifespan.
