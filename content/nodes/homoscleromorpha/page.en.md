## Revised family diagnoses contrast spicules {#s-d594888017}
Gazave and colleagues distinguish homoscleromorph families with small skeletal spicules (Plakinidae) from those without them (Oscarellidae). Their diagnoses also describe different aquiferous systems, the canal networks through which water passes; they do not claim that every member of Homoscleromorpha has a mineral skeleton.

Molecular and morphological evidence supports treating Homoscleromorpha as a fourth sponge class rather than placing it within Demospongiae. This taxonomic revision does not date the class. The cited diagnosis supplies no fossil boundary, so the former 0–0 placeholder is withheld instead of being shown as a temporal range.
