## Green coloration and expanded finger discs {#s-646e2c9160}
The authors cite bright green dorsal coloration, a green iris, an acuminate snout, flared lips, serrated cranial crests especially in females, a conical upper-eyelid tubercle and greatly expanded truncate discs on FingersII–IV. These are local comparative identification characters drawn from earlier work. Expanded discs alone do not quantify climbing performance, and the account does not newly measure sex differences or chemical coloration mechanisms.
