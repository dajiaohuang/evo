## Muraltia pottebergensis {#s-7c6e11e494}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia pottebergensis* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a shrub to 80 cm, with loosely branched, hairy young shoots and clustered, narrow leaves that are woolly beneath and tipped by a spine. Pink flowers are sessile in the leaf axils; the hairy capsules have slender horns. The account records sandstone slopes as habitat, not a complete distribution.
