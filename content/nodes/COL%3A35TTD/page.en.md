## Dicrocaulon grandiflorum {#s-1cdaef56b6}
## Large flowers distinguish it from a similar relative

The Extra Cape flora compares this species with D. ramulosum. D. grandiflorum is described as a shrub up to 500 mm tall, with leaves on the second leaf pair reaching 55 mm and flowers reaching 40 mm across, white or pink. The source says the two taxa are separated by chromosome numbers and geographic distribution. The habitat record is rock crevices on quartzitic reefs.
