## A resurrected name for western Panamanian populations {#s-1737d377d9}
The 2019 study describes high phenotypic variation within the Craugastor podiciferus group and combines mitochondrial DNA sequences with morphometric analysis. Its authors restrict C. podiciferus to specified Costa Rican and western Panamanian populations, then resurrect C. blairi for populations in the Cordillera de Talamanca and Cordillera Central of western Panama. The conclusion reflects the study’s taxonomic assessment and sampling.

Its authors restricted C. podiciferus to specified Costa Rican and western Panamanian populations and restored C. blairi for populations in western Panama’s Talamanca and Central Cordilleras.
