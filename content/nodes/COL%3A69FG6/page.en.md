## Cattleya granulosa {#s-d42dfeb495}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row records a rhizomatous Cattleya with cylindrical pseudobulbs carrying two leaves and one to four flowers. Sepals and petals range from green to brown or ochre; the three-lobed lip is white, with a yellow base and purple warts on its middle lobe. The habitat category is epiphytic.
