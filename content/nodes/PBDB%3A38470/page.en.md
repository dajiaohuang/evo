## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-beb73798bc}
Rhamphorhynchidae is a family in the selected PBDB parent hierarchy. This guide links 19 roster species, including Bellubrunnus rothgaengeri, Qinglongopterus guoi, Dearc sgiathanach and two Rhamphorhynchus species. The path organizes database names; it is not a new phylogenetic analysis.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-a4c48f767f}
The guide contains 73 selected occurrences from 64 collections. Tiaojishan, Lealt Shale, Solnhofen and Solnhofener Plattenkalk are among the recorded strata. Linked species pages provide their own locality, age bracket and source attribution; counts of records, collections and species are different units, not population estimates.

## Evidence limits {#s-f7fba8f8d8}
This route summarizes selected PBDB occurrence entries for 19 roster species, not every rhamphorhynchid or a shared ecology. The database hierarchy is not a phylogenetic result. Collection age brackets and database occurrence counts do not establish a continuous history, full distribution, abundance, origin or extinction.
