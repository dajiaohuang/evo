## Silky stems and purple flower heads {#s-86df64633c}
This account describes an upright, silky-haired stem without glands. Alternate, stalked leaves in two ranks range from oval to oblong, with pointed tips and rounded bases; the upper surface is softly hairy and the lower silky. Bell-shaped heads have overlapping bracts and 17–20 purple florets. The fruits are recorded without glands and bear a white pappus.
