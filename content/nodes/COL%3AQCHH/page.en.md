## Limits on interpretation {#s-f510cb3d29}
AVONET measurements are means from historical samples, not diagnostic limits or individual ranges. Mass has separate provenance and does not share the morphology denominator. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map summarizes historical breeding and resident ranges. The name crosswalk links this record to the current catalogue entry but does not prove unchanged concepts after later taxonomic splits or lumps. Reproduction, current distribution, population and conservation require other evidence.

EltonTraits supplies a historical ecological comparison, not a complete species account. Diet and stratum percentages standardize literature descriptions; they are not direct measurements of intake, energy or time. Code A records compiler confidence in the diet source but the weights remain estimates. The selected avian foraging group is species-level, though the Handbook was not independently reread here. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation are outside these records.
