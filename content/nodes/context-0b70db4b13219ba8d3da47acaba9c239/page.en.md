## Anf1735 and the recorded concept {#s-8e0305b43b}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf1735 names Nanorana aenea and records Nanorana, Dicroglossidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

## Seasonal activity — dry and warm {#s-044bc934be}
The source row marks dry and warm in its seasonal-activity fields. These labels compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-5c7a1aa530}
Its maximum adult body length is 77.4 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-b3c0f639d3}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
