## How do the leaves and calyx differ from Hermannia rudis? {#s-d4ebf36c15}
A southern African account distinguishes this species from H. rudis by its oblanceolate, wedge-based leaves and calyx lobes that are hairless except along their margins.

## What flower features does the revision use to distinguish it? {#s-22cb13fc99}
A 1980 revision describes a parchment-like calyx with five raised angles running from the notches to its base and concave surfaces between them. The calyx is broad at the base; its lobes do not spread or reflex. The slightly protruding petals are strongly twisted and lemon-yellow, shading to orange-red. Each petal has a broad oblong blade several times longer than its basal claw.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-014014aa5a}
The regional account reports dry, stony slopes. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
