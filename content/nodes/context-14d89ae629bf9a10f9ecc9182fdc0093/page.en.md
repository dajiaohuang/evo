## The source concept {#s-61e9aeacb9}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Brachycephalus vertebralis* as row Anf0282, in the genus Brachycephalus, family Brachycephalidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-704e646b73}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) and aquatic (Aqu = 1) as broad foraging categories. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Adult food categories {#s-f2ab8bf4fa}
The row records arthropods (Arthro = 1) as an adult food category. AmphiBIO compiles these from literature reports based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; listed categories are not a complete diet or measured food proportions.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-db8531bc52}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 12 g and maximum adult body length of 15.1 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass or microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not been checked against that revision.

## Developmental strategy {#s-7693302451}
The row records direct development (Dir = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
