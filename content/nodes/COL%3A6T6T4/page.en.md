## Ormosia amazonica {#s-a323624b9d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ormosia amazonica* as an accepted species of *Ormosia* in Fabaceae. COL26.8 lists *Ormosia amazonica* as an accepted species; the linked Flora of Panama record describes a tree about 20 m tall, with leaves of 7–11 leaflets and purplish flowers 15–17 mm long. Its woody fruits contain 1–3 seeds, red with a black spot. The distribution field says it is known in Panama and otherwise only from Brazil’s and Peru’s Amazon Basin; this wording reflects that source record.
