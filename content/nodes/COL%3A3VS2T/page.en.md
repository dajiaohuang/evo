## Solitary flowers among smooth oval leaves {#s-48081aab32}
The record describes dense stems with alternate, smooth, toothed oval leaves; their bases may taper or be heart-shaped. Flowers grow singly in the leaf axils without bracts. The flowers are bilaterally symmetrical, with tubular corollas split along the upper side; colors may be white, pink or blue. The stamens join after flowering, and the stigma has two lobes. The fruit is a rounded capsule with three-angled seeds. The habitat field says terrestrial.
