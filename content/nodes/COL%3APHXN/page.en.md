## Opposite leaves and yellow radiate heads {#s-71002f5856}
The morphology record describes an upright, bristly plant with a simple taproot and opposite, stalkless leaves ranging from linear to elliptic. Radiate heads occur in paired branching clusters; the flowers in both the rays and disk are yellow. The fruits are smooth and glabrous, with a pappus of separate, lance-shaped scales. The habitat field classifies it as terrestrial.
