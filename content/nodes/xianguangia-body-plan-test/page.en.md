## How new Xianguangia fossils reshape a body-plan debate {#s-05b1c08d0f}
A study of 85 new specimens plus type material treats Xianguangia, Chengjiangopenna and Galeaplumosus as one form, preserving a holdfast, internal cavities and pinnule-bearing appendages. One analysis reconstructs a suspension-feeding stem-cnidarian body plan; another places Dinomischidae on the ctenophore stem, while alternative coding yields a polytomy. The larger sample sharpens comparison, but anatomy and placement remain disputed.
