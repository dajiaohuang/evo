## Mandevilla krukovii {#s-f70767d53b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mandevilla krukovii* as an accepted species of *Mandevilla*. In Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147, the habitat field labels it terrestrial. This is a regional category rather than a collection locality or full range, and the source record does not describe its morphology.
