## Tephrosia egregia {#s-9cf96685d4}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tephrosia egregia* as an accepted species of *Tephrosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records a gray, rough-haired stem and pinnate leaves (leaflets arranged along a central stalk) with more than three leaflets. Pink flowers are borne at the shoot tips; the fruit is a gray, hairy legume, and the marbled seeds have smooth coats. The source labels the plant terrestrial.
