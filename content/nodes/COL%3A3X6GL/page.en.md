## Two regional habitat categories {#s-78d3557218}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Macromitrium microstomum as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is a regional source entry. This page summarizes its two habitat fields only.

One category is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A separate category is “Epífita,” or epiphytic: recorded as growing on another plant as a support. These are source categories, not a complete description of the moss's ecology.

The first field gives no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

The second field likewise gives no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

Neither field specifies whether the occurrence is wild, cultivated or managed.

The second category also leaves wild or managed status unspecified.

No distinct Portuguese morphology field was included.

The source habit fields are not life-history evidence; life cycle and reproduction were not assessed.

The two categories do not describe interactions, variation, locality or sampling coverage.

No species-level phylogenetic or comparative evidence is included.

No locality or range evidence is included; this regional record does not establish a complete distribution.

No species-level fossil evidence was assessed.

No conservation assessment was reviewed.
