## Dracosciadium italae {#s-af1c61a16a}
## A rock-sheet plant with shield-shaped leaves

The SANBI description records smooth stems up to 60 cm tall, widely branching from a dark-brown stem base (caudex) that lies horizontally at or just below the soil surface. Most leaves are basal, with long petioles and shield-shaped blades 70–140 mm across. Each blade is divided into seven narrow segments near its base; the segments are cut almost to the midrib and have coarse, irregular sharp teeth. The terminal flower umbel is bisexual and larger than the usually male lateral umbels. The plant grows on and around rock sheets in shallow soil, with an underground rootstock that turns horizontal and spreads vegetatively.
