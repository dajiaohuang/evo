## Ardisia tysonii Lundell {#s-c0d61deedc}
## Stiff, scaly leaves on a small shrub

Ardisia tysonii is a shrub about 3 m tall. Its short, thick branchlets are covered with closely pressed brown scales. The small, rigidly leathery leaves are elliptic to ovate-elliptic, 5–8.5 cm long, paler beneath and entire along the margin; young leaves are conspicuously dotted with black glands. Terminal, three-times-branched panicles bear compact clusters of five-parted flowers. The cited morphology describes the flowers and ovary but provides no fruit description. The species is native to Panama.
