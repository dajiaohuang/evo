## Mimosa rava {#s-c63ae4bf52}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa rava* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes a bristly stem, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and spherical flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), short-haired petal lobes, and pink stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers); the same entry describes the plant as terrestrial.
