## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-fae33a2d0c}
COL26.8 records Prosymna stuhlmanni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Prosymnidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Fossorial/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bates et al. 2014.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0cc781722f}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
