## Cyrtopodium caiapoense {#s-4e6fe34e4a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description places the small, whitish, ovoid pseudobulb below or partly below ground. Leaves are only partly developed at flowering and are recorded as glabrous and shed at maturity; the inflorescence is a panicle. The sepals and petals are greenish or brown with dense reddish-brown spots. The habitat category is terrestrial.
