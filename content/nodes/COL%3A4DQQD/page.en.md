## Golden hairs around yellow flowers {#s-8cc384496a}
This record describes a large, globose cactus, either clustered or solitary, with continuous ribs and distinct areoles. Fine white spines include a curved central spine. Subapical flowers have abundant golden hairs, yellow petals with spatulate tips, erose margins and a red or vinaceous stigma. The fruit is succulent and cream-colored; seed characters are recorded as unknown. Rock-associated habitat is listed.
