## Conflicting gene trees at the Neoaves root {#early-branches}
Neoaves is a navigation route through living birds. In the reviewed phylogenomic analysis, gene trees conflict around its earliest branches, and the root is treated as a hard-polytomy hypothesis: the available signal may not resolve the branching order. This is not a settled ancestor sequence or a fossil divergence date. The cited study samples living lineages but supplies no dated fossil start for a numerical range.
