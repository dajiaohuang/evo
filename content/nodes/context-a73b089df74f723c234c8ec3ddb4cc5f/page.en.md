## The source concept {#s-bd67e2bbff}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Adenomus kelaartii* in row Anf0352 under Adenomus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-054b4bf502}
For Anf0352, the compilation tags terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-0aff2242f1}
The row carries both daytime and night-time activity tags. The compilation does not explain whether they refer to different individuals, seasons or conditions; they are broad categories, not a continuous schedule.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-b0bf62773e}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 50 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-5af8038858}
The row records 1000 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; it does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every clutch, age or offspring.

## Developmental strategy {#s-2e6e5dc06b}
The deposited row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
