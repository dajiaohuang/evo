## Poecilanthe fluminensis {#s-b548d0f990}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Poecilanthe fluminensis* as an accepted species of *Poecilanthe* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records a single leaflet per leaf, longer than 9.5 cm, and a straight central vein. Its flowers are under 2 cm long, with pedicels up to 2 mm and purple-to-vinaceous petals; the upper edge of the fruit is not extended.
