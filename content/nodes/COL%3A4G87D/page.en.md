## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-e62adc3a1d}
The field records clasping roots and a stem described as flagelliform (threadlike) and erect. The sheath is inconspicuous; the short, dark-green petiole is rounded below. The entire oblanceolate blade has a cuspidate tip and cuneate base. Its lateral veins are obscure when fresh, but striped and slender when dry. The inflorescence is recorded as sympodial, terminal or axillary, with “1/2” in the source sequence; the text does not clarify how that count maps to the two positions. The peduncle is cylindrical. The spathe is constricted with a ventricose (swollen) tube and cream outer blade. The stalked spadix has a cream, subcylindrical female zone, a subcylindrical sterile-male zone, and a white fertile-male zone.

## Flowers and recorded alternatives {#s-cbe599febd}
The flowers are recorded with 3 or 4 stamens narrowed at the base and short staminodes. The gynoecium is prismatic and the ovary ovoid; the field gives 4 or 6 locules and 2 or 4 hemianatropous (partly inverted) ovules, with axile placentation. The stigma is discoid. An immature infructescence is noted; seed characters are unknown.
