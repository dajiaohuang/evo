## The name used by AmphiBIO {#s-0d7bb2e854}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Eleutherodactylus melatrigonum* in Anura, family Eleutherodactylidae and genus *Eleutherodactylus* (row Anf1918). Its taxonomy follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This is the name concept used in the dataset; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established. Other fields marked NA are missing data, not evidence that a trait is absent.

## A maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-3f560417b1}
For body size, the row gives a maximum adult length of 20.3 mm. For frogs and toads (Anura), the compilation measures length from the tip of the snout to the vent, rather than total length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every animal; the row does not specify a sample size or population for the value.
