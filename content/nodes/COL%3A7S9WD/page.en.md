## Clutch size and two developmental intervals {#s-f5193ca7da}
The archive gives 3.2 for its combined litter-or-clutch field, 14.75 days for incubation, and 25 days for fledging age. The first field may represent offspring per litter or eggs per clutch; its fractional summary is not a count from one breeding event. Fledging age is defined as age at flight capability, though some underlying material describes leaving the nest. Incubation and fledging refer to distinct events, not interchangeable measures of one reproductive interval.

## Limits of the historical record {#s-a118684904}
These are historical summaries from the August 2015 archive. Its methods report medians, averaging the two middle values when there is an even number of records, and use the largest available record for maximum longevity. The values here are not direct measurements of one bird, individual-variation ranges, or current population estimates. The compilation may share or inherit data among historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family plus same-name source rows support this page's association, but do not prove concept equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited data. Conditions are not consistently identified as wild or captive, and the cited publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current distribution, conservation status, and fields absent from this record need other sources.
