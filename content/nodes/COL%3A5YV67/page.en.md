## Omnivore is retained within a sunbird genus {#s-8207a7b079}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Omnivore and Trophic.Level=Herbivore. Omnivore niche means using multiple food niches in relatively equal proportions; Nectarivore in other genus rows does not replace this record. Herbivore level is defined by at least 70% of food resources coming from plants, a different threshold from nectar niche. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-8c368d4fbf}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Shrubland; density code 2 means semi-open vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility.
