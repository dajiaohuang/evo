## The isolates described {#s-0a8a8eb913}
The species treatment describes isolates KXB24, KXB25 and KXB45 as Gram-negative bacteria. These are the named cultured isolates and reported observations; they do not describe variation across the full species.

## From symptomatic sweet corn {#s-bdc8a49ce2}
The paper reports recovering KXB24, KXB25 and KXB45 from symptomatic sweet corn in the Everglades Agricultural Area of Florida, USA. This is the crop and locality reported for the samples; their association with symptomatic plants does not establish causation or prevalence.

## How the species was proposed {#s-942ba26ffe}
The authors used polyphasic taxonomic characterization, including genomic comparisons, to propose Pantoea graminicola for the studied isolates. This taxonomic inference concerns those isolates and the comparator taxa analyzed in the treatment.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-01e0f3b4c8}
This account draws on one peer-reviewed species treatment. Its observations are limited to the named isolates and reported conditions; the study did not assess population-wide variation, reproductive or developmental biology, behavior, lifespan, fossils or conservation status.
