## Stem surface {#s-8f6ebb45fe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as pubescent or velutinous: covered with short soft hairs or a dense, velvet-like coat. It separately reports hairs that spread outward and simple hairs; the record does not assign those surface descriptions to different specimens. Branched and unbranched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent. Capitate glandular hairs, with small head-shaped tips, are present.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-e1bf3748be}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are persistent. Extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) and projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) are absent. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-f67e50cfb8}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having three parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, has triangular lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and no hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-bd86026daa}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not identify a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
