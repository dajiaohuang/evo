## Transparent bands on tiny leaves {#s-8ef16cd878}
Moraea fenestralis is one of the smallest species in its subgenus, reaching about 25 mm tall. Its curved, cylindrical leaves have a transparent band on the upper surface; the plant flowers early in the season, mostly in June and also in July.
