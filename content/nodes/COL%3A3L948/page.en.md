## Heterocypsela andersonii {#s-40713290ff}
## Sixty to seventy florets; outer fruits are winged

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record notes yellow-brown stems. The outer involucral bracts are linear to threadlike, and each flower's corolla tube is hairy. A head contains 60–70 florets. Outer dry fruits are winged. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
