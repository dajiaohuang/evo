## Satyria cerander (Dunal) A.C.Sm. {#s-f48a22d57d}
## Short racemes of bell-calyxed flowers

Satyria cerander has smooth, brown or gray, striated branchlets. Its leathery leaves are ovate to oblong-elliptic, with pointed or tiny-tipped apices and rounded bases; the secondary veins arise close to the base. Short racemes carry four to nine flowers, with a 5–15 mm axis. The calyx is bell-shaped and the corolla cylindrical, 8–11 mm long. The habitat labels include both hemi-epiphytic and terrestrial settings.
