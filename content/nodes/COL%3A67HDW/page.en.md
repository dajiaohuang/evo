## Form described in the flora record {#s-aa9dc6708c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem with fibers at the base and entire cataphylls and prophylls at the stem tip. Petioles are smooth and channelled, with sharp margins above and rounded margins below. Leaf blades are entire, flat, and recorded as linear, oblong, or elliptic; the tip is acuminate or acute and the base obtuse or rounded. Glandular dots are absent. Primary veins are obtuse on both sides; secondary veins are obscure above, and the field does not characterize a collecting vein.

The inflorescence is larger than half the leaf size, with a cylindrical peduncle. The spathe is flat and oblong or elliptic; its angle to the peduncle is not characterized. The stipe is absent. The berries are reddish.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.

## Two habitat labels {#s-f1d4153dc3}
The dataset records *Epífita*, its epiphytic habitat category.

This is a habitat category, not a collection locality; the field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

It also records *Terrícola*, the dataset's terrestrial category.

This second category is also not a collection locality and supplies no survey date, range, or sampling-completeness measure.

## Limits of this record {#s-517d81514b}
Only one source-labelled morphology field is included; its completeness, variation, and diagnostic scope have not been assessed.

The source habit field is not evidence about life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
