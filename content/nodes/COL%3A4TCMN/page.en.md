## Roridula gorgonias Planch. {#s-54a596bd30}
## Tentacle-edged leaves on wet sandstone

Roridula gorgonias is a shrublet up to 60 cm tall. Its narrow, lance-shaped leaves crowd the branch tips and bear tentacles along their margins. Short-stalked pink flowers grow in terminal racemes; the petals fall early, and the style is longer than the ovary and is described as capitate. It grows on damp sandstone slopes and seeps at 100–900 m elevation.
