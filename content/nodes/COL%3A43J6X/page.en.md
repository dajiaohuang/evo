## Three to five flowers per cyme; outer corona lobes shorter {#s-32d22c3ff5}
The source morphology field records indumentum over the full surface of the branches. Leaves are opposite, oblong or elliptic, with flat margins. Umbelliform cymes bear three to five flowers. The sepals are hairy on the abaxial surface and glabrous at the margins. The corolla is bell-shaped, with oval or triangular lobes finely hairy to papillose on the adaxial surface; the throat is finely hairy. Ovate-lanceolate outer corona lobes are shorter than the sessile or subsessile gynostegium. The ovate retinaculum is smaller than the pollinia; caudicles ascend and are not geniculate, and the pollinia are oblong. The habitat label is “Rupícola” (rupicolous, associated with rock). This single field does not establish variation or full diagnostic scope, and no locality or range was assessed.
