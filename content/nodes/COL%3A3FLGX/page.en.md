## How do its yellow flowers and seed capsule compare in the description? {#s-1a78d3fbd7}
Genlisea pygmaea is recorded with rosette-forming stems, no tubers and thin, spatula-shaped leaves. The yellow or yellowish-white corolla is 3–7 mm long without the spur; the straight spur runs parallel to the flower stalk. Its capsule opens around the circumference, rather than lengthwise, and the stalk is erect. The source describes angular-prismatic seeds with a net-like surface pattern; it gives no population sample.
