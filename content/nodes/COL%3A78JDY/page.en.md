## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-12e843e30d}
The field describes leathery leaves ranging from narrow oblong-elliptic to inversely egg-shaped, with pointed or blunt bases and tapering tips. Flowers may grow in leaf axils or directly from branches. The bracts are depressed-oval in shape.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-0cd245a54e}
The dataset categorizes Pseudoxandra leiophylla as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
