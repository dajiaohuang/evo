## Mesembryanthemum fastigiatum {#s-1ac650cfd6}
## Bladder cells mark the surface of cylindrical leaves

The Extra Cape flora describes a succulent annual up to 100 mm tall, growing upright or leaning but prostrate when flowering. Its linear, cylindrical leaves are up to 40 mm long and 7 mm across; conspicuous bladder cells usually mark the leaf surface. White flowers are 10–15 mm across, with relatively few petals, sterile stamens, and fertile stamens.

The fruit has five chambers. Its valve wings bend backward and join in pairs. The habitat record is disturbed open ground.
