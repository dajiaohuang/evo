## *Symphyopappus lymansmithii*: toothed leaves and a terminal corymb {#s-74d96cd88a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records erect, hairless stems and opposite or two-ranked leaves. The ovate to elliptic leaves have stalks, toothed margins, and prominent veins radiating from the base. A terminal corymb carries five-flowered heads; the receptacle beneath the florets is described as bristly or pitted. The corolla is tubular, five-lobed, and glandular. The five-ribbed fruitlets are glandular and bear a single row of pappus bristles. The broad substrate category is “terrestrial.”
