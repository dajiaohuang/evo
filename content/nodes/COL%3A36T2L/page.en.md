## Discocactus placentiformis {#s-d6442aff0b}
## A solitary disk-shaped stem bears wavy ribs

The recorded stem is solitary and ranges from disk-shaped to hemispherical. Its ribs are wavy, the areoles dense, and the spines thick and recurved. These traits describe the source's morphology account; the accompanying habitat labels are rocky and terrestrial, with no locality or survey frame.
