## Short leaves fused to grey branches {#s-34251512cb}
This lax shrublet reaches 30 cm and has stiff, spreading grey branches. Leaves are fused to the stem for the lower 5 mm; the free parts are only 2–4 mm long and end in one or two small teeth. Solitary, nearly stalkless pale pink flowers are about 14 mm across. The five-chambered fruits have high rims; shale flats are listed.
