## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e2a25b5036}
COL26.8 records Pararhadinaea melanogaster as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pseudoxyrhophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-8a3a56ecca}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 215.6 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013. The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 251.7 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016. This row records TL and SVL in separate fields; the captured definitions provide no conversion between them.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-cd389a3bfe}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-96e169b70e}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-5ebbc740aa}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
