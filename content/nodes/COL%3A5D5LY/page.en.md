## Radiate heads with red-orange ray florets {#s-5b85a8cb8d}
This account describes an annual herb up to 42 cm tall, with stems that may stand upright or lie partly along the ground. Sessile leaves are narrow-ovate, resin-dotted and hairy only along their edges. Flower heads are radiate, with red-orange rays that are yellow-green beneath and yellow disk florets; cultivated forms can have more ray florets. The fruits have no pappus. The distribution record identifies Mexico as its native region.
