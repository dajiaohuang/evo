## The source concept {#s-a7801cc250}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Boophis calcaratus in row Anf3512, under the genus Boophis, family Mantellidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; they do not establish that it matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-12e025e54a}
This row marks arboreal foraging as a present category. It is a broad label, not a detailed account of where individuals forage across seasons or life stages. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not yet been checked against those changes. Keep the deposited category provisional: no replacement value is available here, and the revision does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-54c5cffb0b}
The row records night as an activity category. This summarizes a reported activity period; it is not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-921a44afff}
The compiled maximum adult body length is 39.5 mm. For Anura, AmphiBIO measures body length from the snout to the vent (snout–vent length). This is a reported maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
