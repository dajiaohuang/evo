## Mimosa pseudoradula {#s-f0a4d90c35}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa pseudoradula* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists several stem-hair forms, including hairs pressed forward, and globose flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts); the petal lobes are listed as hairless or short-haired. This regional description preserves alternatives rather than resolving them into one form.
