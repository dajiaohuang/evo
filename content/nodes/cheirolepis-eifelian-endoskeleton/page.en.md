## Cheirolepis: what CT scans reveal about an early ray-finned fish {#s-4167584c52}
CT scans of named Cheirolepis trailli specimens from the late Eifelian Achanarras Limestone show parts of the braincase, jaw suspension, pectoral-fin skeleton, and scales. The sample combines early ray-finned-fish traits with other character states. The often displayed 390.4–388.1 Ma range is a numerical conversion of a biostratigraphic assignment on an older timescale, not a direct date on these specimens or a global first appearance for ray-finned fishes.
