## Accepted name and classification {#s-ce4ed01119}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Hylarana temporalis* (Günther, 1864) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Hylarana (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.
