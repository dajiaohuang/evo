## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-f43c0ddde3}

Dajomesae has white peritonea, yellow digits and a tan to greyish-tan iris with fine dark reticulations and punctuations. No numerical wileyi call series was measured in this diagnosis.
