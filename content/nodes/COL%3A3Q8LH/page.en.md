## Isoetes bradei {#s-2cfab5b48f}
## Tuberculate megaspores and a reduced velum

The source describes a three-lobed, globose stem and upright linear leaves with wings running from base to tip. Sporangia sit at the leaf bases; their velum, a covering over the spore case, is rudimentary or absent. The megaspores have a three-rayed trilete mark and tuberculate surfaces on both sides; the microspores have a single monolete mark and a spiny outer layer on the distal surface. The habitat field labels the species aquatic.
