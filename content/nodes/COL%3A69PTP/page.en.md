## Small flower groups and a narrow pod {#s-21072706c4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records two to 11 pairs of oblong to lance-shaped leaflets on leaves arranged in two rows. Nectar glands occur outside the flowers on the leaf stalk. Small groups beside the leaves usually have one or two flowers, though three to six are also recorded. The yellow flowers have five to nine stamens. The narrow pod is 1.7–5 cm long, slightly curved or straight, and brown to blackish; the seed is trapezoidal.
