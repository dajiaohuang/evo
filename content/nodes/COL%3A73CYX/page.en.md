## Interlocking flower clusters on a low shrublet {#s-22ae17d03e}
The account describes a slender, sprawling, white-woolly shrublet up to 30 cm tall. Its short leaves are reflexed and twisted, with small tufts in the leaf axils. Discoid heads form tightly interlocking clusters at the branch tips; the inner petal-like bracts are pink, sometimes white. Flowering is recorded from October to January on sandstone slopes in the listed northwestern range.
