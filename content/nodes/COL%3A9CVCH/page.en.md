## Aldina microphylla {#s-abda223b4a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Aldina microphylla* records alternate compound leaves with 1–5 opposite leaflets, each 1.5–2 cm long, red stem exudate, 60 stamens, one-seeded nonwoody drupes. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
