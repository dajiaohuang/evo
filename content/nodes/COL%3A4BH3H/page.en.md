## Oxypetalum macrolepis {#s-dacec9cf62}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum macrolepis* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry marks the habitat terrestrial, but supplies no locality, range, or morphology. The category is therefore a source-specific regional record rather than a complete ecological profile.
