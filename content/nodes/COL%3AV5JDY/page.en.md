## Traits in the published group comparison {#s-8201f99a17}
The 2023 description of *Amolops ailao* places *A. wangyufani* in its *viridimaculatus* species-group comparison. Its review of earlier descriptions records vomerine teeth (teeth on the vomer bones in the roof of the mouth) and no dorsolateral gland series (rows of glands along the back’s sides) in the compared species. These are literature-derived contrasts, not new measurements of *A. wangyufani*; historical group placement does not settle current taxonomic boundaries or establish that every individual shares these traits.
