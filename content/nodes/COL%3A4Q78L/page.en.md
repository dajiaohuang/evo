## A tuberous orchid with a cross-shaped flower appendage {#s-b914d0b290}
The regional description calls P. cruciferum a tuberous geophyte—a plant with an underground tuber—reaching 40 cm tall. It has two or three leaves, from linear to elliptic. A loose raceme (a flower cluster arranged along a central stalk) bears a few yellow-green flowers; the lip has a cross-shaped appendage.
