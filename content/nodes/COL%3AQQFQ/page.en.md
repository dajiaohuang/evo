## The name and this data account {#s-e1c39abdca}
Capito squamatus is accepted in COL26.8 within Capitonidae. AVONET v7 links its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name to 4 measured individuals, all with complete morphological sets. Trait-specific sample sizes may still vary, and this historical naming link does not establish taxonomic concept equivalence.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-0887c6fc07}
The mean beak length is 16.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; mean width and depth at that edge are 8.3 mm and 10.4 mm. They are separate standardized measurements rather than identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-721e13d4fc}
Mean unflattened wing length is 81.9 mm from carpal joint to longest primary; mean tarsus length is 22.4 mm. Compiled mean mass is 59.26 g, labelled Dunning. That mass summary is separately sourced and is not a record that each morphology specimen was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-403374a1fd}
The source classifies habitat as Forest, trophic niche as Frugivore and lifestyle as Insessorial. These labels describe the AVONET row; they do not establish that the species uses only forest or eats only fruit.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-56781f6925}
The movement field is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude −3.58° to 2.06°. The endpoints do not show non-breeding movement or current occupancy, and the map is separate from the morphological measurements.
