## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-73a72edf09}
COL26.8 records Opisthotropis kuatunensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-6c71e85ac1}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2011.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-51a4a8fe56}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-af7be33edc}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
