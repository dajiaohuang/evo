## Blade form and venation {#s-d066e9ea0e}
The flora field lists obovate blades, tapering or small-pointed tips, acute bases, and prominent secondary veins; it also records the absence of a collecting vein (a vein running near the leaf edge). The spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) is ovate and the berry wine-colored. These are regional taxonomic descriptors, not a measure of variation.

## Epiphytic and rock-associated categories {#s-dd1ad9ec45}
Separate Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 rows classify the habitat as “epífita” (growing on another plant) and “rupícola” (rock-associated). Neither row names a host, site, or date, so they do not locate the records.
