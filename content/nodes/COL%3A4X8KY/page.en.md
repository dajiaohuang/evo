## What leaves, flowers and fruit details are recorded? {#s-3ac201e55a}
The erect stems are woolly, with simple, long, star-shaped or translucent hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned, symmetrical leaves are heart-shaped or ovate, with heart-shaped bases, pointed tips and toothed margins. The upper surface is bristly with star-shaped hairs. Flowers may grow singly in leaf axils or in terminal panicles. The sepals have pointed tips and the corolla is yellow. The dry fruit splits into five segments (mericarps) without awns; the segments bear simple hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-8b6feeb475}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
