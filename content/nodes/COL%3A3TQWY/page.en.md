## Selected identification characters {#s-b9888ffa12}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes this as a woody plant with imparipinnate leaves: leaflets arranged along an axis with a terminal leaflet. Flower bracts are longer than 1.2 mm. The ovary is hairy, ranging from short-haired to densely woolly. The adaxial petal (the one facing the flower's axis) is recorded as undifferentiated. For the seed, the embryo's hypocotyl–radicle axis (the line between its early stem and root portions) is listed as parallel or oblique. These are selected characters from one regional dataset field, not a complete description of the species.

## Habitat category {#s-7814e9612b}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of distribution.
