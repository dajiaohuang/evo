## Source classification and concept status {#s-028a8f91f0}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2892 records *Litoria pallida* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-19db5a1ddc}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Night-time activity {#s-06e0b8e7cc}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-5e1350e5a2}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 1.72 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 41 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-5528c2ef29}
The row lists 50–350 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 1.2–1.4 mm. These are literature-compiled values. The row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size fields may refer to eggs.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-6ec4f3f3a7}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
