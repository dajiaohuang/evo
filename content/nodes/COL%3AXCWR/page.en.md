## Colura cylindrica: two habitat labels {#s-7bba59608a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this species:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Epífila” (leaf-associated).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither specifies a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
