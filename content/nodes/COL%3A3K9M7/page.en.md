## What traits distinguish its flowers and stipules? {#s-aee7158d01}
The stipules fall away. The inflorescence has two-flowered scorpioid cymes, no extrafloral nectaries and awl-shaped or linear prophylls. Flowers are straight rather than bent at a joint; the petals are nearly equal, and their claws are four to eight times shorter than the calyx. There are ten free stamens. The densely hairy capsule may be straight or spiraled.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-a65efcb370}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
