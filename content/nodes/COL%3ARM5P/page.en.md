## Leaflet dimensions and yellow-to-reddish petals {#s-bd8f1a25d8}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes Cassia spruceana with 3–5 pairs of leaflets; the terminal pair is broadly ovate or ovate-elliptic, 9–12 cm long and 4–5.5 cm wide. Petals are recorded as oblong-elliptic or oblanceolate, 1.3–1.5 cm long and 0.5–0.8 cm wide, and yellow or reddish; the field also lists 10 stamens. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. The regional data fields specify no sampling frame, sex, life stage, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status.
