## Ground feeding, habitat and sedentary category {#s-668850cd7c}
Habitat is Forest; density code 1 means dense habitats, not birds per unit area. The label Herbivore terrestrial is read with Metadata’s definition for plant material in non-aquatic systems, including leaf and bud categories. Trophic level is separately recorded as Herbivore; niche and level are different fields whose labels are not substituted for each other. Terrestrial means mainly feeding while walking or hopping on the ground; Migration=1 is sedentary. Neither supplies individual routes, flight capacity or annual diet percentages.
