## Phytolacca dioica {#s-7b1944a49a}
## A massive soft trunk and separate male and female trees

The account describes Phytolacca dioica as a cultivated ornamental tree that may reach 25 m. Its enormous trunk is succulent, soft-wooded and buttressed. Bright-green ovate leaves have pinkish petioles; racemes are about 100 mm long. Male and female flowers occur on separate trees, and the small flowers are creamy yellow. Flowering is recorded from September to April. Pendulous berry-like fruits are succulent, green before turning black. The cited origin is northern Argentina, southern Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay; an invasive-category 3 status is recorded for several named South African vegetation regions.
