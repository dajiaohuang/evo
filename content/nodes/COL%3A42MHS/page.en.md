## Distribution records {#s-001969e1aa}
A 2023 south-central Amazonia account used two previously reported localities and three new ones to delineate the reported range of the Acarí marmoset between the right bank of the Acarí River, the left bank of the Sucundurí River and areas south of BR-230, about 17,480 km². This is a regional inference from occurrence records, not a complete survey or current abundance estimate.

## Evolutionary evidence {#s-b7a9cfcda1}
The same paper proposed from locality records and regional geomorphology that the Acarí River headwaters might be a contact zone for the Acarí marmoset, Mico chrysoleucos and M. melanurus, where hybridization could occur. It did not report observed hybrids or gene flow; the authors called for further surveys, specimens and samples to establish whether the contact zone exists.
