## The examined material {#s-dd90e34f9d}
Tschopp (2016) examined MDHC 193 as part of a museum skeletal comparison. Vertebral structures were inspected under a light microscope. The material comes from the MDHC collection at the University of Turin; no additional animals were collected for this study.

## What the vertebrae show {#s-f2e3aeda4d}
This skink supplies another comparison outside Lacertidae. Its cervical vertebrae have laterally expanded synapophyses with a distinct connection to the centrum. An anterior centrosynapophyseal lamina and a prezygosynapophyseal lamina are recorded there, showing why the new vocabulary must accommodate differences among lizard lineages.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-563774f833}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 159.8 mm; female maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 109.4 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent and differs from total length including the tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-f20809c324}
the reproductive-mode field uses the original label viviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 6.5; lowest reported clutch/litter mean 1; highest reported clutch/litter mean 25; consolidated litters/clutches per year 1.5. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Limits of these trait data {#s-86bc51fe7c}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
