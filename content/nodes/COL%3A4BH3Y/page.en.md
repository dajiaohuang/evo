## Oxypetalum montanum {#s-4b5af08f99}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum montanum* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 row records only a terrestrial habitat category. It provides no collection locality, complete range, or morphology description, so the label remains a regional record.
