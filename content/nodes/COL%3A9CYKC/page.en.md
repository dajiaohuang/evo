## Many leaflets and small axillary clusters {#s-e1085d4468}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes leaves arranged in two rows, with seven to 19 pairs of obovate to elliptic leaflets. Nectar glands occur on the leaf stalk, and the flowers grow singly or in small groups beside the leaves. Yellow flowers have ten stamens. Mature pod sides twist; seed form is marked unknown.
