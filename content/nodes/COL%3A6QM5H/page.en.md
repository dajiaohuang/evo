## Blue flowers in a terminal raceme {#s-26c2908541}
The morphology record notes dense stems and alternate smooth leaves ranging from oval to linear, with toothed edges and bases that run down the stem. A terminal raceme carries bilaterally symmetrical, blue tubular flowers with a split along the upper side. The stamens join after flowering, and the stigma has two lobes. The fruit is an obconic capsule with ellipsoid seeds. The habitat field says terrestrial.
