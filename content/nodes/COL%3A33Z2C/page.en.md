## Dalbergia glandulosa {#s-f180484159}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Dalbergia glandulosa* records 13–25 leaflets, sometimes 29, panicles, purple-to-cream flowers with 10 stamens, oblong pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
