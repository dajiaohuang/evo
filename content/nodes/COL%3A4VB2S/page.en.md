## Schizoglossum hilliardiae {#s-500622f0dc}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Schizoglossum hilliardiae* as an accepted species. The regional description records stems 9–34 cm tall, branched only at the base, and 3–9-flowered inflorescences. The corolla is green with brown or purple markings on one surface and white with brown-purple veins on the other; the corona is cream to yellow-green. A fruit about 6.5 cm long is recorded, but seeds were not seen. The stated habitat is subalpine grassland.
