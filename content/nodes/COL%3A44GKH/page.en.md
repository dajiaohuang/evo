## How does the record describe its growth and flower structures? {#s-2e8171f426}
The stem is flattened in two sides and lies flat against the substrate. The leaf petiole is inconspicuous; the thin blade is pinnatifid and has no spines. The unbranched, spike-like inflorescence rises on a long stalk distinct from the main axis, with bracteoles. The tubular flower sheath is as long as the bracteoles. Tepals and filaments are free; the filaments are unbranched and round to narrowly cone-shaped. No gynophore is recorded. The ovary is oblong to ellipsoid, and the stigmas are narrowly triangular.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-c1f029b690}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as aquatic and gives no locality.
