## Leaves and a branched flower cluster {#s-80404a8841}
The regional account describes a perennial aquatic herb, stoloniferous (producing horizontal shoots), and up to 1 m tall. Its ovate leaf blades have rounded to heart-shaped bases, with petioles reaching 400 mm. The much-branched flower cluster forms a pyramid; petals are larger than sepals, and flowers are white or pink.
