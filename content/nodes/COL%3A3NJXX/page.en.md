## Yellow flower heads on an upright shrub {#s-25eccee9d8}
The description records an upright shrub 30–90 cm tall, with leaves divided into narrow, rolled-edge segments. Small yellow flower heads crowd together in rounded clusters, and their surrounding bracts are velvety. In the source comparison with H. speciosa, H. incisa is characterized by smaller, more weakly divided leaves, usually with five lobes.
