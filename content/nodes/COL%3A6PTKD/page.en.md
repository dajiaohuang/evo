## Life-history evidence limits {#s-de3df565c8}
The values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 life-history archive. They are reported as compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, average wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited components from the summaries. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
