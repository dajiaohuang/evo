## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-7c0dca3b8c}
Leucocarbo verrucosus is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Phalacrocoracidae. This page compares the same-name historical life-history entry using 5 focal fields, each supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows with matching name and family. Reported summaries can still include historical taxonomic inheritance; a matching row name does not remove that influence.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-c41a937f68}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 2430 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-687c630b55}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 2.5 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 29 d; age at flight capability (leaving the nest in some source material) 56 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-72a38a4614}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 10.92 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-61223183eb}
The life-history rows carry labels including Dunning, 1992, and Bennett, 1986. A label may identify a paper, book or another database. The row count is not a count of measured individuals, and repeated citations do not amount to independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-de3df565c8}
The values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 life-history archive. They are reported as compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, average wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited components from the summaries. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
