## Dendrophorbium glaziovii {#s-2c2c1bd356}
## Yellow radiate heads above pale felted leaf undersides

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes erect, woody stems. Alternate leaves are elliptic, ovate or ovate-lance-shaped; the blades are less than four times as long as wide, with rounded or tapered bases and finely toothed margins. Petioles lack ear-like appendages, and the leaf undersides have white or yellow felted hairs.

Panicles bear radiate flower heads with bell-shaped involucres and an outer ring of small bracts; each involucre has 6–8 bracts. Yellow ray florets are ligulate and female, with short 2–3.5 mm limbs; the yellow disc florets are tubular and bisexual. The style branches are convex and carry a divergent tuft of hairs at the tip.

The cylindrical dry fruits are hairless and ribbed. Their persistent pappus (hair-like crown at the fruit tip) is yellow. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
