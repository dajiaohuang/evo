## Prismatocarpus pilosus {#s-625f8c76cc}
## Fine hairs on the ovary and calyx mark the comparison with P. diffusus

Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 Cape flora account compares this species with P. diffusus: its ovary and calyx are finely hairy, and the bracts are about as long as the ovary, roughly 5–10 mm. The habitat entry places it on sandstone slopes.
