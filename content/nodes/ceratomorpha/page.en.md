## Ceratomorpha trees differ in basal resolution {#s-8d52d5d3dd}
A 2020 parsimony analysis recovers sampled Ceratomorpha as Tapiroidea plus Rhinocerotoidea. Its Bayesian tip-dating tree is less resolved at several basal positions, so the study supports a sampled topology rather than one fixed placement or ancestor sequence. The former 56 Ma older edge is not established by this analysis; no numerical range is exposed.
