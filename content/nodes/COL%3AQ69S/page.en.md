## Leaves and flowering structure {#s-c3c2105bad}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes an underground bulb and leaf blades with a keel-like projection. It records a terminal synflorescence, no terminal bracts, and a stalked rhipidium. The outer bracts of that structure measure 14–20 mm and the inner bracts 20–28 mm. Flowers are described as whitish, white, or bluish, without glandular hairs. Anthers are about 4–5 mm long; the style is three-forked, with its entire portion about 3.5–7.5 mm long.

These details come from one morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset. Its completeness, variation, and diagnostic scope have not been assessed; it does not establish a global description or sampling coverage.
