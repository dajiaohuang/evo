## Lessingianthus cephalotes {#s-56d2dbad5b}
## A basal rosette sends up stalked heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium, a woody underground stem, and sparse or absent branching. Leaves form a rosette. Their obovate to oblong blades are thin and papery, differ in colour between faces and have scalloped, non-rolled edges. Tips range from blunt to rounded; bases are blunt or tapered. The upper face is hairless or finely hairy, while the lower has a grey-white felt of hairs.

Stalked heads occur singly or in corymb-shaped clusters. Their bell-shaped involucres have 2–5 rows of bracts. The source lists 30, 35 or 40 florets per head. Top-shaped dry fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
