## The name and this data account {#s-518fb6c46d}
Celeus spectabilis is accepted in COL26.8 within Picidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 8 measured individuals, 5 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-7a43d283ef}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 22.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 11 mm in width and 8.6 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-76c1594d0f}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 146.1 mm; mean tarsus length is 25.4 mm. Compiled mean mass is 114 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-261b16a979}
Foraging-stratum weights flagged as species-level in the archive are trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy 100%. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-2491dcb403}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only.
