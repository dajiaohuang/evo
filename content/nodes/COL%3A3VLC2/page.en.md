## Leaves change from broad to narrow up the stem {#s-3f1b66a38d}
This description records an upright, branched herb with finely rough, close-lying hairs on the stem. The lowest leaves are oval-oblong and taper into long stalks; middle leaves are lance-shaped, while the uppermost are narrow and sessile, with rolled-under edges. Leafy racemes rise at the stem tips, and the corolla is about twice as long as the calyx. The smooth, shiny fruits are ovoid. It is recorded in sheltered places among rocks in the Upper Karoo and Dry Highveld Grassland.
