## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3d86db4823}
COL26.8 records Trachylepis wahlbergii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-27dbd77a47}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-7323481ed5}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records ovoviviparous; this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
