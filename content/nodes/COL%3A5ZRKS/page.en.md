## Conchocarpus adenantherus {#s-d046004527}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record combines obtuse leaf tips and a long, solitary inflorescence with a cream-white, two-stamen flower.

## A long inflorescence and two fertile stamens {#s-5c21b80275}
Leaves are alternate and simple or one-foliolate, with slightly expanded petiole tips and obtuse blade tips. The source describes solitary inflorescences, generally over 7 cm long, between leafy branches; evergreen leaf-like bracts are absent. Inflorescence branching is recorded as absent (pseudoracemose) or lax. Sepals persist. Flowers have two fertile stamens and a radially symmetric cream-white corolla; the anthers are free and glabrous. The ovary is pubescent and the style connate. The fruit apex is rounded. The ecology category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or range.
