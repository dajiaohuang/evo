## What flower and fruit traits distinguish Byrsonima lanulosa? {#s-c5f0de89ca}
The lower leaf surface is woolly-haired. Each cluster has one flower on a stalkless pedicel. The petals are yellow, the anthers are hairless, and the fruit is densely woolly.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ca2a2d63b9}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
