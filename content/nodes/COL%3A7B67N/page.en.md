## Leaves and hanging inflorescence {#s-72a5fb9639}
The source records a solitary aerial stem with leaf-sheath remnants on its surface. Leaves are green and pinnate; the persistent sheaths wrap helically around the stem and have fibrous margins. The petiole margin is unarmed and the leaf axis is curved. The leaflets may be leathery or membranous, grouped and set at different angles; they are flexible, with drooping, asymmetrically pointed tips, and are green on both surfaces. The inflorescence is a hanging panicle. Its fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves; the grooved spadix and its main axis are hairless. Smaller branches lie along one side near the base, then spiral around the axis farther up.

## Fruit {#s-80bee1c25b}
The fruit is ovoid. The green or yellow-green outer layer is hairless or has hairs at the tip; the middle layer is fibrous and succulent. The inner layer is woody and oval, and the seed endosperm is homogeneous.

The field records a solitary aerial stem with leaf-sheath remnants on its surface; green pinnate leaves with persistent sheaths arranged helically on the stem and fibrous margins; unarmed petiole margins and a curved leaf rachis; leathery or membranous pinnae inserted at differing angles, grouped and flexible, with drooping asymmetrically acuminate tips and a concolorous green surface; a pendulous panicle and exposed interfoliar infructescence; a grooved, hairless spadix and hairless rachis; rachillae arranged on one side along the initial rachis portion and helically along the middle and terminal portions; ovoid fruit with a green or yellow-green epicarp, hairless or with indument at the apex, fibrous succulent mesocarp, woody oval endocarp, and homogeneous endosperm.
