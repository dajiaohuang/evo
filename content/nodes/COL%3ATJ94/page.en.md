## Nectar glands on the leaf stalk {#s-d0a2ba09d4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes leaves arranged in two rows, usually with one or two pairs of leaflets and only rarely three. Nectar glands sit on the leaf stalk and its central axis. The yellow flowers have ten stamens. At maturity, the hairless pod sides twist; the seeds are flattened, with shapes described from roughly quadrangular to rhomboid.

## A rocky-habitat label {#s-cb0071f02e}
The flora entry labels its habitat rupicolous, meaning associated with rocky surfaces. The label supplies a habitat type, not a precise collection locality.
