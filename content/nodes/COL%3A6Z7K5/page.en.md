## Axillary cormlets and pale flowers {#s-69c6088cac}
Sparaxis bulbifera is 15–45 cm tall and forms cormlets in the leaf axils after flowering. Its nearly radially symmetrical flowers are white to cream, often with purple on the reverse of the outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) and a faint sweet scent.
