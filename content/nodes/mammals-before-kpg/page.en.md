## Mammals did not begin at K–Pg {#s-0833368525}
The K–Pg boundary changed opportunities for surviving mammals. Their evolutionary history had begun far earlier.

## A much older branch {#s-e7f117e5ed}
The synapsid lineage begins hundreds of millions of years before crown mammals.

## Mesozoic mammal diversity {#s-a9f3dee429}
Small body size does not mean ecological uniformity: Mesozoic mammals include climbers, diggers, swimmers and gliders.

## Radiation after survival {#s-cdf3712b16}
After 66 Ma, surviving mammal lineages expanded in body size and ecological roles.
