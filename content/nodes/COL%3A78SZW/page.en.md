## Rhipsalis campos-portoana {#s-15a2516928}
## Cylindrical stem segments end in smooth tips

The stems are composed of cylindrical segments whose mature areoles lack spines. Distal segments remain terminal, and growth of each segment is limited to its smooth tip. Hairless flower-bearing areoles produce distal flowers with a bell-shaped corolla and white filaments. The exposed pericarpel—the flower's ovary-bearing base—is shorter than the perianth segments. The globose-to-ovoid yellow fruit lacks areoles. The habitat is labeled epiphytic, without a named host or locality.
