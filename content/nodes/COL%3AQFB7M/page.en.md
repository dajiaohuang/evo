## Name and placement {#s-157ba9f0cc}
COL26.8 lists Leptopelis diffidens Tiutenko & Zinenko, 2021 as an accepted species. In that catalogue it sits within order Anura, family Arthroleptidae and genus Leptopelis. These details describe the record in the release published on 20 August 2026; a similarly named entry elsewhere still needs its own evidence of identity.

## Observations in Harenna Forest {#s-5d8b3f84bb}
The field account places Leptopelis diffidens in Ethiopia’s Harenna Forest at 1,400–2,300 m. It highlights natural glades that are temporarily flooded by seasonal rain. The frogs were described as ground-dwelling and sometimes burrowing; dry-season observations were made at night. Breeding sites included standing or slow-moving water. The authors proposed that particular glade vegetation and water conditions may matter, but this remains their interpretation of the sites studied.

## Life stages seen together {#s-bc0efeb8cd}
At one site on the same day, researchers found an egg-bearing female and individuals near the end of metamorphosis; they also reported metamorphs in June. From these observations, they proposed that breeding might occur more than once a year. The co-occurrence of life stages does not show that one female bred repeatedly or reveal the species’ full annual breeding schedule.
