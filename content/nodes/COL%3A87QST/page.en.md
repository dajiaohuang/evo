## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-da3ac1c2f4}
COL26.8 records Aspidura ravanai as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-0eb7025a99}
The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2017.
