## A cited Palawan distinction with remaining island questions {#s-c9568062a7}
The 2016 account cited a 2011 study distinguishing Palawan-region frogs, written as “Staurois nubilis” in its paragraph, from Mindanao-region S. natator. That cited name is retained as printed without independently certifying its current spelling or accepted status. The authors still considered Basilan populations and the distinctive Samar and Leyte colour patterns unresolved. Those remaining questions are not silently converted into accepted species splits or certified contemporary population assignments.

## Qualitative regional commonness and museum lots {#s-a2c855c5fa}
The authors described S. natator as commonly observed in the Mindanao faunal region and cited Least Concern in 2016. Their voucher list included KU 333775–801 at AN 13 and KU 333710–74 at MO 2. These lots support traceability of historical assignments, not a new museum reidentification or a numerical island-wide density.
