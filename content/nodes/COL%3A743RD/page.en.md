## Leaf shapes, edges, and hair alternatives {#s-607c52ba2d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes smooth cork on the stem. Leaves are listed as elliptic, lance-shaped, obovate or ovate, or oblanceolate (widest above the middle); their tips are acute or rounded and their bases acute or wedge-shaped. The blades are leathery, with wavy, flat, or rolled-under edges. The upper midrib is grooved; a marginal vein may be absent or visible. The hair covering is described with alternatives: two-armed hairs, becoming hairless, hairy, or densely woolly. Leaf stalks are grooved. The field does not say how common these states are.

## Paired or solitary clusters and an unknown seed form {#s-df9403c5aa}
Inflorescences are listed as paired or solitary, with persistent bracts. The flower cup is inverted-cone-shaped; bracteoles are oval, sepals and petals are oblong or oval, and the flower bud is open. The fruit is globose, with persistent bracteoles. Seed form is marked unknown in the field.

## A terrestrial label without locality detail {#s-b4254f3bbf}
The habitat field says “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date, range coverage, or life-status information. The category does not establish a distribution.
