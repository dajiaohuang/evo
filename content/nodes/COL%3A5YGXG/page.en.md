## Chromolaena pedunculosa {#s-0e58056d34}
## Three-veined leaves with hairy surfaces

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Stems are recorded as glandular and ranging from finely hairy to shaggy. Opposite, lance-shaped to ovate leaves have three main veins and margins listed as entire, scalloped or serrated. The upper surface is stiffly hairy; the lower may be gland-dotted and woolly or stiffly hairy.

Corymb-like or panicle-like clusters bear heads on stalks or nearly stalkless. Their cylindrical involucres have smooth bracts pressed close to the head. Corolla lobes are recorded as glandular, finely hairy or smooth. The dry fruits (cypselae) are obconical, smooth or bearing short bristles; the pappus bristles are rough. The habitat field says terrestrial, with no locality or complete range.
