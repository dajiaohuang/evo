## What do the feeding and activity entries mean? {#s-5d37f2221c}
The EltonTraits 1.0 entry has no food-category weights (0), one mammalian foraging-stratum assignment (1), and one activity-period entry (1). Food weights standardize descriptions from sources; they are not direct measurements of what an animal ate. Stratum and activity entries are categories, not percentages of time. This single historical entry does not establish a shared ecology for the whole genus.
