## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Caperonia cordata? {#s-fe34804cda}
The plant has rhizomes and a solid stem that may be hairy or prickly. Leaves are heart-shaped or ovate, with palm-like veins and fewer than nine side veins; they bear simple or gland-tipped hairs. The inflorescences are unisexual. Female clusters may carry one to five or more than five flowers, according to the record. Female flowers have five sepals, while petals of male flowers are alike; the outer surfaces of their sepals are hairy.

The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial and gives no specific site.
