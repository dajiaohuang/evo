## Ceratocaryum decipiens {#s-9fffada4f6}
## Dark female spathes enclose the spikelets

The regional account describes a tufted perennial to 1.5 m that spreads by rhizomes. Its leaf sheaths have a fringe of hairs along the upper edge; female spikelets reach 15 mm and sit within shiny, dark-brown spathes longer than 5 cm. The smooth nuts bear an elaiosome, a fleshy attachment. Sandstone slopes are the recorded habitat.
