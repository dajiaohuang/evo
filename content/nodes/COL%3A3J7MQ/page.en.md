## Haemanthus humilis: flowering season and habitat {#s-21366e9c0d}
A 2019 regional account describes a perennial geophyte 50–350 mm tall. Leaves emerge at flowering; they are somewhat spreading, rarely prostrate, somewhat elliptic and 13–150 mm wide. They are somewhat hairy, though rarely only fringed. Many flowers form a somewhat loose cluster, with pale-pink surrounding bracts. The perianth is 7–25 mm long and pale to rose-pink, rarely whitish; the stamens are at least as long as the perianth. The account gives the flowering period as November–February.
