## Evidence limits {#s-a43964932d}
This page guides readers through historical AVONET v7 data. Morphology values are averages for the listed samples, not diagnoses or individual-variation ranges. Mass comes from separate sources, so the morphological sample count cannot be assigned to either mass figure. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography covers historical breeding and resident ranges. Historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name support the association, not equivalence of concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation need other sources.

The 2015 life-history archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. These are not direct measurements of one animal, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family plus same-name source rows support the association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild versus captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications were not individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and absent fields need other sources.
