## Scarlet flowers with white chevrons {#s-4f9c1a5e94}
Gladiolus cruentus grows on trailing, arching stems 500–700 mm long. Its large scarlet flowers have white chevrons near the bases of the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) and a tube about 28 mm long; the filaments extend roughly 35 mm beyond the tube.

## Damp sandstone cliffs {#s-aac5d944d2}
The regional account records damp sandstone cliffs. It does not describe the frequency or full extent of this habitat.
