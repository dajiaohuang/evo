## What textures are recorded on its leaves? {#s-a8ced70395}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records leaves in two or nearly two ranks, with flat blades that are cross-wrinkled. Their edges are described as hairy or rough. The flower stalk is listed in two length classes, 20–50 cm and 50–70 cm; the ovoid or nearly spherical spike bears 10–25 flowers. These options are preserved as listed, not resolved into one form.
