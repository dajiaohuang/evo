## How are its leaves and flower heads described? {#s-c652fe8256}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a woody underground storage organ called a xylopodium and leaves with rounded-toothed edges but no petioles; the flat blades are membranous. Mature flower heads are nearly spherical and borne on stalks. It also notes a stylopodium—the base of the flower’s style—while flattened appendages on the calyx, the flower’s outer whorl, are absent. This is the flora entry’s description, not a survey of variation across populations.

## What habitat category accompanies the description? {#s-5ebf314a64}
The same regional archive labels its habitat as terrestrial. That field does not identify a collection locality or establish the species’ full geographic range.
