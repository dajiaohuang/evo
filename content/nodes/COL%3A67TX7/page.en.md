## Askidiosperma capitatum {#s-6b200d07cb}
## Female flowers in black clusters shaped like heads

The regional flora describes Askidiosperma capitatum as a tufted perennial reaching 80 cm. It is dioecious (female and male flowers grow on separate plants). Female flowers are 7–9 mm long, and the spikelets (small flower clusters) are grouped into black clusters shaped like heads. The account also lists stony slopes and sandy flats as habitat.
