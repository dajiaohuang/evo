## Oblong leaves on a moderately branched plant {#s-9e0d65281d}
The record describes a swollen underground stem and moderately branched shoots with oblong, leathery leaves. The leaf bases are heart-shaped; the upper surfaces are smooth and the lower surfaces pubescent. Sessile heads form grouped cymes, with cylindrical involucres in 4–6 rows. The top-shaped fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
