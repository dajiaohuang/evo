## The accepted name and record counts {#s-ac662921fa}
PBDB accepts Pterodactylus antiquus under the database parent Pterodactylus. The selected data contain 1 species-level occurrence record from 1 collection and 1 reference identifier. These are database counts, not numbers of specimens or animals. One record links an original identification or combination to this accepted name; the database link is not an additional occurrence.

## Scope of this record card {#s-fc9a35e05c}
This card summarizes selected PBDB occurrences queried on 2026-10-02; it is not a species monograph. Accepted names and database parent paths organize records but do not settle every specimen identification or taxonomic dispute. Occurrence, collection and reference counts are different units. Collection age brackets and environment or preservation labels do not establish a complete range, exact origin or extinction time, species-wide ecology, abundance or the condition of every specimen.
