## Several recorded leaflet and pod forms {#s-54bee120bf}
The flora entry describes an upright, single-trunk tree with three to five pairs of elliptic to oval leaflets. It lists alternative states for leaflet hairs, flower-cluster size, and pod length: pods are recorded as either 1–2 cm or 2–3.5 cm long, and the seed covering as yellow or orange. The pod is slightly sickle-shaped. These alternatives are retained as recorded rather than treated as one fixed measurement. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
