## *Phylica stokoei* {#s-be6a757f7d}
Compared with *Phylica excelsa*, the source describes stalked flowers with appressed, straight golden hairs and slightly longer sepals, about 2 mm.

A second account distinguishes it by the direction of hairs on the calyx tube and ovary, and by longer sepals. These are the stated comparative characters.

The source lists high rocky slopes as habitat; no locality or date is supplied.
