## What does the record describe for Tapura guianensis? {#s-35d0278de6}
Young branches are hairless or become nearly so. The leaves range from elliptic to lance-shaped, with pointed tips, flat margins, and smooth or nearly smooth undersides. Their upper midrib is indented; the record gives up to ten or more than ten pairs of side veins. Flowers arise without a stalk at leaf-stalk tips, and the individual flowers are nearly sessile. The calyx underside is finely hairy, with unequal lobes; the corolla also has unequal lobes and forms a tube longer than 5 mm. Each flower has three fertile stamens, and the ovary is hairy. The fruit’s color is unknown.
