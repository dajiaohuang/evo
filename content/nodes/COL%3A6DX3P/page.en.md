## Comparative results retain their measurement limits {#s-0257d81afe}
Okinawa and Amami were separate analytical units based on earlier divergence studies, not newly accepted species. External-head plots showed no clear population separation; the paper reports no significant population difference in cranial shape or size. Lateral skull means placed Okinawa closer to Yaotriton and Amami within the Tylototriton subgenus space. These mean positions do not override the population comparison or identify individuals. The smaller Amami body size described by earlier sources was not reproduced as a cranial-size pattern, with small sample size noted.
