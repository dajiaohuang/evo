## ## Many leaflets and a lilac, pea-shaped flower {#s-5c0e39d951}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records alternate, odd-pinnate leaves—leaves that end in a single leaflet—with 14–20 leathery leaflets. The stipules fall away, and the side veins reach the leaf margins. Terminal panicles bear lilac, pea-shaped flowers with a bell-shaped, five-part calyx and free stamens. The ovary is stalkless and contains one ovule. The fruit is laterally compressed, ellipsoid and dehiscent, meaning it opens at maturity. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
