## Hippeastrum morelianum {#s-4c6b27f90f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Hippeastrum morelianum with a subterranean bulb and strap-shaped leaves. Its two to four bell-shaped flowers have red-patterned tepals and a partly united, fringed corona; the anthers are yellow or cream and the stigma is three-lobed. The dataset records both rock-dwelling and terrestrial habitats, without giving a locality or explaining the two categories.
