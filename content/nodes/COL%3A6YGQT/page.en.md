## Senna hirsuta {#s-4e4dc1399c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna hirsuta* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists four or five pairs of leaflets and an extra-floral gland on the leaf stalk. Its flowers have seven fertile stamens; the hanging, linear pods split open and have seeds in one row. The stem is recorded as softly or coarsely hairy.
