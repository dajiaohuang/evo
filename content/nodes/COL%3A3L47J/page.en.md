## Short plants with rose-backed white flowers {#s-a3adc2b5ad}
Hesperantha alborosea stands 70–110 mm high. Its white flowers have outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) flushed rose-pink on the reverse and a short tube, 6–9 mm long; the firm leaves have thickened margins and midribs.

## Wet rock flushes and gravel {#s-704a72628e}
The regional source records wet sites such as rock flushes and wet gravel.
