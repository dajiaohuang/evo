## Leaf spines, inflorescence, and fruit {#s-c0fbc7686b}
The field describes recurved leaf spines shorter than 1 cm. Spines occur near the base of the cirri (tendril-like leaf structures). The upper surface at the pinna base is described as bearing minute spines and/or dense woolly hairs. The inflorescence rachis is thicker than its smaller branches and wavy. Fruit has an irregular surface with many fibers; the corolla is less than one-quarter the fruit's size and is irregularly divided into three lobes. The endocarp ranges from globose to obovoid.

The field records recurved leaf spines shorter than 1 cm; spines near the base of the cirri; a pinna base whose upper surface bears minute spines and/or dense tomentum; a wavy inflorescence rachis thicker than the rachillae; fruit with an irregular, densely fibrous surface; a corolla less than one-quarter the fruit's size and irregularly divided into three lobes; and a globose to obovoid endocarp.
