## Eriocephalus karooicus {#s-c0055bbd8d}
## Aromatic, spinescent shrubs with tiny silver leaves

The SANBI description gives a much-branched, spreading shrub 150–450 mm tall, with spinescent shoot tips and strongly hairy silver- to green-grey leaves only 1–4 mm long. Small radiate heads, 2–4.5 mm long, are solitary or clustered along shoots and partly hidden among leaves. The two or three white ray florets surround four to ten disc florets, whose corollas range from white or yellow to red-purple at the lobes. The source records flowering in December–March in summer and July–September in winter. Listed veld types include False Karoo and Cymbopogon-Themeda transitions, mainly on heavy soils.
