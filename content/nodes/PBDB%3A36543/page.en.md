## Recorded parent of Ichthyosauria {#s-07bf90949e}
Ichthyosauria is listed at rank unranked clade in the PBDB classification route, with Eoichthyosauria as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
