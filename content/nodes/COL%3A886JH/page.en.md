COL26.8 records Elaphe moellendorffi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-35720e7928}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2007.

The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. ## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-96cef91b57}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
