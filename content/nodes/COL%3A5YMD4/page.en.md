## What root, flower, and fruit traits are recorded for Chrysochlamys ulei? {#s-3b5358c5a0}
The record notes conspicuous stilt roots and white latex. Leaves are simple and opposite, with elliptic to obovate blades; their thin texture and side-vein counts are given as alternatives. Flowers arise from leaf axils. Each has five sepals, five petals, and an ovary described as five-carpellate. The fruit may be spherical or egg-shaped, with a green to wine-colored outer wall and five valves. Mesocarp color and aril color were not observed in the source record.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-faf2a3684e}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a named locality.
