## Maranta humilis {#s-cdceff3cbd}
## A straight flower tube; fruit shape unreported

Its leaf blades are recorded as elliptic or ovate, with sparse hairs on the upper side of the pulvinus. The bracts are loosely arranged. The straight corolla tube is shorter than the sepals; the ovary is densely silky-haired but smooth at the surface. The source leaves the staminode callus and fruit shape unknown, while noting that the sepals persist and remain upright. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
