## Woolly leaves and white or purple florets {#s-6d7944691a}
The morphology record describes an upright, woolly stem with glands. Alternate leaves in two ranks have stalks, elliptic to lance-shaped blades and pointed ends; both surfaces are woolly and glandular. Bell-shaped heads have overlapping bracts, with a small point at the tip of the inner bracts, and 10–15 florets that may be white or purple. The pappus is white, and the habitat field says terrestrial.
