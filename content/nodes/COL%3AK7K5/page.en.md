## Baccharis polyphylla {#s-74627cb5a2}
## Needle-like leaves and solitary axillary heads

The record describes developed, sessile, needle-like leaves with a single main vein and entire margins. Branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated; the woody underground base (xylopodium) is absent. Flower heads are solitary in the leaf axils. The source lists either heads of tubular male florets only or heads of threadlike female florets only. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
