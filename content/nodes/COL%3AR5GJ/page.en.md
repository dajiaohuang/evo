## Accepted name and catalogue placement {#s-5dd071ee82}
COL26.8 records Cardioglossa melanogaster Amiet, 1972 as an accepted species under Anura, Arthroleptidae and Cardioglossa. This is its position in the pinned catalogue; it does not make the linked developmental or image records equivalent to the current species concept.

## Two records, not a species synthesis {#s-e70df1536f}
This node has links to AmphiBIO v1 and TadpoleTraits v4, but their recorded source concepts have not been verified as the COL26.8 species. The source data are therefore presented as bounded records below, not combined into a single account of this frog.

## Stage labels and their scope {#s-5f37a65660}
The TadpoleTraits compilation retains 11 literature-derived rows and one image-derived row for its Cardioglossa melanogaster source concept. Supplied stage labels are 24, 26, 27, 34, 35, 31, 36, 25 and 38 (Judging based on the picture). The last label explicitly records a picture-based judgment, not a directly documented developmental observation. These are row and label counts, not counts of unique animals; the source concept is not yet matched to the currently accepted species.
