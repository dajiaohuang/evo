## Buriolestes teeth and a reconstructed ancestral diet {#s-a15df72390}
Holotype ULBRA-PVT280 preserves a slender early sauropodomorph skeleton with recurved, serrated teeth. Researchers mapped feeding characters across sampled dinosaurs and placed faunivory near the sauropodomorph root. That ancestral-state result depends on the taxa, character coding and tree used; it is not a meal preserved in this individual or a linear record of the transition to herbivory, and the specimen is not established as a direct ancestor of later sauropods.
