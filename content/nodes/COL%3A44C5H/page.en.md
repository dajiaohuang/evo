## Early flowers after seasonal rains {#s-0a32bde72f}
Moraea flexicaulis grows to 150 mm and has branching stems, several leaves and a sturdy column formed by united filaments. It flowers in late June and July, three to four weeks after the first soaking rains; size varies with rainfall, and a robust Richtersveld form remains uncertain.
