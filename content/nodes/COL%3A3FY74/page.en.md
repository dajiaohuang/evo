## Gethyllis marginata {#s-ef593fa7a8}
The regional account describes Gethyllis marginata as a small bulbous geophyte with one to four dry-at-flowering leaves. The leaves are suberect and elliptic, with thickened, pale, finely wavy margins; its white flower has widely spreading tepals and a sideways-curved style. Habitat is marked uncertain in the source.
