## Two windows onto salamander history {#s-4cb7f66c8c}
Two kinds of evidence anchor this profile. Shen et al. used 102 nuclear markers to test relationships among sampled living salamanders; Gao and Shubin described articulated Jurassic skeletons of Beiyanerpeton from Liaoning. Together they illuminate parts of salamander history, not a complete tree or ancestor sequence.

## Diet is not established here {#s-509e488c31}
The cited marker and fossil studies do not establish what these salamanders ate.

## Fossil locality and modern sampling {#s-06519c56a5}
The Tiaojishan Formation provides the geological setting for Beiyanerpeton, while the molecular study samples living lineages. These are research contexts, not a full map of salamander habitats.

## Anatomy without a performance measure {#s-7475fadcb4}
The fossil paper describes bones, but the cited studies do not measure how salamanders moved.

## No order-wide size range {#s-6527cc0933}
These studies do not provide a complete comparative body-size dataset for Caudata.

## Ecological role remains open {#s-7f7640652a}
The cited evidence is not enough to assign a single ecological guild to the order.

## A sampled molecular tree {#s-56b9123e58}
Shen et al. tested higher-level relationships with 102 nuclear markers and the living salamander taxa sampled in that study.

## Articulated Jurassic skeletons {#s-142c6710d0}
The Beiyanerpeton sample includes articulated skulls and skeletons behind the skull, preserving anatomy in place.

## Bony structures beside the cheek {#s-1a4ecd6e15}
Gao and Shubin describe bony gill structures near the cheek in the Beiyanerpeton specimens.

## A sample, not a complete history {#s-6036865a7b}
The marker analysis tests relationships among its sampled living salamanders; the fossil account describes six Beiyanerpeton specimens from western Liaoning. Their overlying volcanic unit is dated to 157 ± 3 million years ago, a geological constraint rather than a direct date on the skeletons. Neither line of evidence establishes the full Caudata range, ecology or ancestry.
