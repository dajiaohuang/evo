## The source concept {#s-3eeb30b31f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0020 records Allobates caribe in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-1b0ab778c0}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The fields Fos=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Seasonal activity field: Wet_warm=1 {#s-613026ffc4}
The row records Wet_warm=1 (activity in warm, wet conditions). Seasonal categories are compared with local annual climate and do not map to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Body_size_mm: 20.8 mm maximum adult body length {#s-91d7c013da}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=20.8 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
