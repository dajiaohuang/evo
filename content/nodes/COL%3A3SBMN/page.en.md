## *Larryleachia picta* {#s-84b3c8c0d1}
Compared with *Larryleachia cactiformis*, the source describes yellow corollas spotted red, a distinctly five-angled tube, and inner corona lobes that rise above the anthers. The comparison highlights the longer inner corona lobes as a useful distinction.

The detailed account describes numerous evenly spaced purple-brown spots on the corolla, a tube that is usually five-angled, and rounded papillae inside. The corona varies: outer-lobe horns may spread or remain closer together, while the relatively long inner lobes rise into a slender column above the flower centre and can sometimes form a cage over the anthers. Vegetative and floral features can resemble *L. cactiformis*; the account presents the long inner lobes as the clearest floral distinction.

The source records stony slopes as habitat, without a locality or date.
