## The source concept {#s-0990a11310}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria aurifera as row Anf2768 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-75298744fc}
The row lists terrestrial and aquatic foraging. These categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. Its row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-4abada7051}
The entry lists both daytime and night activity. These broad categories are not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-45f08c9d58}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 22 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-0bc0c604c3}
The row gives 60–111 offspring or eggs per clutch and offspring-or-egg sizes of 0.8–1 mm. These are literature-derived counts and extrema. The entry does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions; size may refer to offspring or eggs.
