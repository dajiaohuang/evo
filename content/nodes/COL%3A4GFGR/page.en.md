## Phlegmariurus christii {#s-3aa22ce1a3}
## Recurved leaf tips and distinct spore-bearing leaves

The basal stem is recorded as green to brown; stems may be upright or prostrate, with a decumbent base. The small leaves are described as uniform in form, linear-lanceolate and recurved near their tips. Their bases are short and green, and the recorded attachments range from running down the stem to reflexed or pressed close against it. Spore-bearing leaves differ from the vegetative leaves. Each sporangium has two equal valves and is kidney-shaped or globose; the spores are described as pitted.
