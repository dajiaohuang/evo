## What leaf and flower traits identify Tragia giardelliae? {#s-44f4686cd2}
The stem twines. Broadly ovate leaves have heart-shaped bases, tapering tips, and toothed edges. Racemes bear glandular or stinging hairs. Male flowers have three nearly round sepals and two stamens; each female cluster has one flower with six broadly elliptic sepals. The female sepals lack glands at their tips, and the stigma surface is smooth. The fruit stalk lengthens as the fruit develops; seeds have faint papillae.
