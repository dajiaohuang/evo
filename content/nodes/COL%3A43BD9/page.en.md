## Mikania leptotricha {#s-44d13468ed}
## Hairy stems and a hairy leaf underside

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, hairy branches; stem pith was not observed. Opposite, elliptic leaves have wedge-shaped bases and short, tapered tips. Their margins are recorded as entire or finely toothed, the upper face becomes nearly smooth, and the lower face is hairy. The blades are leathery, with pinnate veins that loop near the edge.

Loose panicles, sometimes with spike-like side branches, bear shortly stalked heads. Short, linear bracts sit at the top of each head stalk; the elliptic bracts around a head are woolly and acute. The bell-shaped corolla has lobes as long as the tube, with smooth triangular lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth. A separate habitat field says terrestrial; this regional entry gives no locality or full range.
