## The examined adult specimens {#s-4a6909434b}
Lyu and colleagues measured snout–vent lengths of 24.2–27.5 mm in seven adult males and 31.5–32.2 mm in three adult females. These describe the examined sample, not universal size limits.

## Wetland observations in the taxonomic account {#s-79304c3988}
The account describes lowland ponds and rice fields, calling at the water surface or bank vegetation, and April–August breeding. This dated synthesis combines the authors’ observations and cited literature; it does not establish the schedule of every population.

## Voucher evidence and unresolved records {#s-6fac1dd852}
The study’s vouchers support Guangdong and Guangxi localities. Its authors required further evidence for Yunnan records formerly called O. lima. Their proposed Endangered listing is a 2022 recommendation, not a verified current IUCN assessment.
