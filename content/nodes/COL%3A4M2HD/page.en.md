## Portulacaria afra {#s-59d8adac45}
## A succulent shrub with dense pink flower sprays

Portulacaria afra is described as a succulent shrub or small tree up to 5 m tall. Its fleshy, obovate leaves are sessile. Small pink-to-mauve flowers are produced abundantly in dense sprays at the ends of short side branches. The cited habitat is rocky hillsides; no locality or dated survey is supplied.
