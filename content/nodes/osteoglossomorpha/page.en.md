## Relationships among living families {#s-4a24def39d}
A phylogenomic study sampled 546 exons across all six living osteoglossomorph families and recovered detailed relationships among the living groups. A separate clock estimate places the sampled Elopomorpha–Osteoglossomorpha split between 239.12 and 213.05 Ma. This is a model interval for the represented crown route, not a fossil first appearance.

The 546-exon dataset spans all six extant osteoglossomorph families. It supports a detailed topology for those sampled living lineages, but an extant-only family sample does not define the full fossil range or directly date the group’s origin.

## A molecular split estimate {#s-121737bfb6}
The concatenated ohnologue clock model estimates the Elopomorpha–Osteoglossomorpha split within 239.12–213.05 Ma. The atlas uses this model interval for the represented crown route; it does not treat it as a fossil occurrence or a complete range for all members.
