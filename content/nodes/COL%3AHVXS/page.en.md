## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Astraea macroura? {#s-3402748089}
The record notes no xylopodium. The stem is hairy to pubescent, with star-shaped hairs. Leaves are papery, with narrow awl-shaped stipules; blades may be undivided or split into two or three parts. The upper surface may have simple hairs under 1 mm or star-shaped hairs, including porrect forms. Male flower clusters contain four to six flowers. Female flower stalks are up to 2.5 mm long, and the outer calyx lobes have short, simple hairs. The fruit is glossy; the seed is four-angled in cross-section.
