## What traits are noted for Hirtella lancifolia? {#s-7d19070d32}
Young branches are shaggy to densely hairy. The leaves are ovate or oblong, and the record notes no ant-associated cavities. Flowers grow in racemes; the bracts lack glands, while the smaller bracteoles have stalked glands. Each flower has four stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5a75ea6f10}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without specifying a locality.
