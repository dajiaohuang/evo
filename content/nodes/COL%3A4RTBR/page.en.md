## What is packed into its female spikelets? {#s-dda8526947}
A regional account describes *Restio bolusii* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial up to 1 m tall, with sparsely branched, finely warty culms (stems). Its female spikelets (small flower clusters) are 10–20 mm long, elliptical and pointed, and each contains fewer than six flowers. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
