## Bulb, leaves, and flowers {#s-95fd371c29}
Baker's description calls the bulb small and globose, with a neck 1–1.5 inches long. It records one lanceolate leaf appearing with the flower, 1.5–2 inches long, moderately firm, and crisped at the edge. The perianth tube is about 1 inch long; its whitish limb is 0.75–1 inch long, with acute lanceolate segments about 0.125 inch wide. The anthers are about 0.167 inch long. These measurements remain in the source's units.
