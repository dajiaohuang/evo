## Monstera adansonii: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-419c993f0d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes juvenile leaves as spreading and the petiole sheath as persistent and entire. Leaf blades are recorded as perforated or entire; tertiary veins run parallel to the secondary veins. Pinna-base and pinna-division characters are not supplied. The upper surface of the spathe is listed as cream, pale yellow or white, and the peduncle is about as long as or longer than the spadix.

The style-region apex is described as truncate or prominently obtusely conical; stigmas are elliptic or linear. The berry is white.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative states are retained as reported.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
