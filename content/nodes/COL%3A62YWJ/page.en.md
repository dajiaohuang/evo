## A mitochondrial analysis and its limits {#s-f11e0ef549}
The study concatenates partial mitochondrial 12S and 16S sequences. Its broad tree includes 36 species drawn from 43 source labels, with selected Asian subtrees. In the broad analysis, Bufoides is the nearest sampled placement to the focal toad but has low support, so the tree does not establish a sister relationship. Two mitochondrial regions do not provide nuclear evidence or test reproductive isolation. Clearing and staining or radiography are reported only for explicitly named specimens; those preparations cannot be assumed for other vouchers. The paper calls its distance measure “uncorrected k2p”; that wording is ambiguous and does not by itself establish a genus boundary.
