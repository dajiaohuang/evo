## A route through armoured dinosaurs {#s-4a239a0181}
This atlas route uses the revised Scelidosaurus lectotype as one sampled anchor for studying Thyreophora, the armoured-dinosaur branch. Its anatomy was re-evaluated in a character analysis that tests where it fits relative to ankylosaurs and stegosaurs.

## Placement is a result to compare {#s-dcb728779e}
The lectotype provides extensive anatomical evidence, but its position depends on the characters and taxa included in the analysis. This route is a way into one phylogenetic question; it does not make Scelidosaurus a direct ancestor or define the full range of Thyreophora.
