## Small, narrow white flowers {#s-1245a96b35}
Moraea cedarmontana is usually a slender plant up to 300 mm tall. Its white flowers are relatively small, with outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 24–27 mm long and narrow inner tepals 20–25 mm long.

## Sandy streambanks {#s-4fec8e797c}
The flora records streambanks with sandy soils.
