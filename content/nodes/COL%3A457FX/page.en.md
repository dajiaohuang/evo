## A hairless stem and a variable midrib {#s-ae51035e05}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and no hairs on the stem. Leaves are lanceolate or ovate; their upper-surface midrib is listed as either channelled or fully flattened, and the underside is glabrous. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below the leaf nodes are absent. The field gives these alternatives without frequencies.

## A three-chambered ovary; fruit details unrecorded {#s-739ad460ee}
The inflorescence branchlets are compressed, with bracteoles beneath the flowers that fall away. One or two inflorescences are listed per axillary shoot, in the leaf axil and in an alternate pyramidal arrangement. The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is obconic, or inversely cone-shaped, and glabrous or puberulent (covered with fine, short hairs). Sepals are free, the ovary has three chambers, and the calyx is listed as not opening by tearing. The source marks the fruit's calyx lobes and shape as unknown, so this field does not describe the fruit.
