## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-799039fb67}
PBDB places Rhamphorhynchinae at subfamily rank in the recorded classification path. This page links to 13 species-level occurrence guides. The database parent chain organizes records; it does not by itself resolve evolutionary relationships.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-2f41550c55}
The linked species pages include Qinglongopterus guoi, Dearc sgiathanach, Rhamphorhynchus muensteri, Rhamphorhynchus etchesi, Angustinaripterus longicephalus. Together, they cover 66 selected occurrence records from 59 collections, with recorded strata including Tiaojishan, Lealt Shale, Solnhofen, Solnhofener Plattenkalk. Read each collection with its own age interval and citation; these counts do not estimate ancient population size.
