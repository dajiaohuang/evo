## Long orange flowers and thick leaf margins {#s-f85b26afed}
Watsonia schlechteri grows 40–100 cm tall and has long-tubed orange flowers. The lower and upper parts of the tube are 17–20 mm and 22–30 mm long, respectively; the filaments are 35–45 mm. The source notes that specimens from the Langeberg transition zone can be difficult to identify confidently.

## Rocky sandstone fynbos {#s-18421650db}
The regional flora records rocky sandstone slopes in fynbos, a Cape shrubland vegetation type.
