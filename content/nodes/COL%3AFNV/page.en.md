## Shared traits among living rhinos {#s-b1672e1912}
Living rhinoceroses have robust limbs, normally with three digits on each foot. Their horns are made of keratin. Feeding ranges from browsing to grazing; one species does not represent the ecology of the entire family.

## Three different measures of reproduction and lifespan {#s-c33b8585b5}
All five accounts record gestation, female age at first reproduction, and maximum lifespan. First reproduction is not the same as first birth or sexual maturity; maximum lifespan is not average lifespan. Source conditions differ, missing fields require other evidence, and same-name matching does not resolve historical species concepts.

## How to read these historical life-history values {#s-bffa101141}
These five accounts are not the family’s complete biology. They use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 same-name table; equivalence to current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight regardless of sample size; inputs can be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and studies may overlap. Maximum lifespan is the longest available record, not typical lifespan. Adult mass is not interpreted here, and relatives do not fill missing values. Original reference numbers remain on the pages, but the underlying species literature and every wild, captive, or locality condition were not independently established.
