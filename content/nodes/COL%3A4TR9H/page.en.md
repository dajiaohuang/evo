## Ruschiella argentea {#s-9b7ba28c3a}
## Upright glaucous leaves and a cone of sterile stamens

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a stiffly erect shrublet up to 30 cm tall. Its three-angled leaves are glaucous and reach 20 mm long by 5 mm wide.

The flowers may be solitary or occur in groups of three; they are pinkish, with sterile stamens (staminodes) gathered into a central cone. The fruit is bell-shaped below and has broad, rectangular valve wings. The habitat account says the plant occurs mostly on sandstone slopes.
