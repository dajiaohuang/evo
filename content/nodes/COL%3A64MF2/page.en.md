## What does the plant account describe? {#s-5d5a91ac1c}
The plant account describes *Acorus calamus* as a perennial herb with strongly pungent aromatic oils in all parts. Its sword-shaped leaves are 0.8–2.0 m long. The account describes a lateral green spadix (a flowering spike) beside a leaf-shaped green spathe (a leaflike bract).

## Where does the account record it growing? {#s-fb620b1014}
The habitat entry places the plant in shallow, slow-flowing or still water along stream edges and in swamp areas. This describes the habitats named in that account; it does not provide a geographic range or an estimate of how commonly the plant occurs there.
