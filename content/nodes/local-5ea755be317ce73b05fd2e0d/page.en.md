## The source tadpole concept {#s-9cfee5df20}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Sclerophrys poweri, in the genus Sclerophrys and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying work prints Amietophrynus poweri, which the compilation retains as its own source name. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-f11f049f0b}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Its stage field is NA, so no developmental stage is supplied. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
