## Merciera azurea {#s-399a437455}
## Blue flowers rise among stiff, closely packed leaves

Manning and Goldblatt's Greater Cape flora account describes a rigid shrublet up to 30 cm tall. Its densely packed leaves overlap, are stiff and linear, and end in a sharp tip. Short hairs cover the leaves; their margins roll slightly under and are rough with cilia.

Blue to purple flowers are nearly stalkless in the upper leaf axils. Their narrowly funnel-shaped tubes are 10–25 mm long, with elliptic-lanceolate petals. The cited habitat field records sandstone slopes.
