Mesowear and microwear in the studied southern German Miocene samples support browse-dominated feeding with mixed food textures; bark or twigs are among the proposed abrasive foods. This is a dietary inference from wear, not a directly observed meal.

## Claw use and posture are reconstructions {#s-2758c64069}
The sampled animals are large chalicotheriines. Samples identified as Chalicotherium goldfussi and the historical combination Chalicotherium (Anisodon) grande preserve large terminal toe bones interpreted as claws and shortened hind limbs. Upright posture and claw use remain functional reconstructions.
