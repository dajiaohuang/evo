## Wear supports browse-dominated mixed feeding {#s-6a9dd4b804}
Mesowear and microwear in the studied southern German Miocene samples support browse-dominated feeding with mixed food textures. Bark or twigs are among the proposed abrasive foods. This is an inference from tooth wear, not a directly observed meal.

## Claws and posture are functional reconstructions {#s-2758c64069}
The studied large chalicotheriines preserve large terminal toe bones interpreted as claws and shortened hind limbs. Upright posture and claw use are reconstructions from anatomy, not observed behavior.
