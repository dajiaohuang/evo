## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-eed3ee8694}
COL26.8 records Pristurus guweirensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Sphaerodactylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-de2359a7e8}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
