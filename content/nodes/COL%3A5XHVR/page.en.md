## Orange scales beneath the leaflets {#s-9ef6de9c39}
The archived Flora of Panama description portrays Centrolobium yavizanum as a tree with 9–21 leaflets per leaf. The oblong to ovate-oblong leaflets are 5–13 cm long and about 6 cm wide; their undersides carry dense orange scale-like coverings, and terminal flower clusters can reach 50 cm. A separate historical distribution field says the species was known only from Darién Province, Panama. The archive gives no population sampling frame or observation date, so that regional statement is not a verified current global range.
