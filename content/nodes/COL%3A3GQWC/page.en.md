## How is its flower stalk separated from the leaves? {#s-6a69de517b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists the leaves as solitary and basal, with petioles present and sheaths absent. The flower stalk arises at the base from a different node than the leaves. Its fertile bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are spiral and recorded as not red or orange; each small flower cluster has two flowers.
