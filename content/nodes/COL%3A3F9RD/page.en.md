## What fruit and leaf traits are recorded for Garcinia brasiliensis? {#s-085c5bae49}
The record describes an upright, branching plant with a taproot. Its stalked, elliptic or ovate leaves measure 5–23 cm long and 2–11.5 cm wide; tips may be blunt, sharp, or tapering, while bases may be rounded or narrowed. Axillary clusters carry 20 or 30 stamens. The fruit has a smooth outer wall and no beak; the smooth seed is elliptic.
