## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-b18cfc6a61}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field describes an erect stem with sparse to dense glandular hairs, bristles or long soft hairs. Opposite leaves have a flat upper surface, similar hairs, a heart-shaped or lobed base and scalloped, toothed or hair-fringed margins; their main veins arise from the base. The inflorescence is a curved, scorpioid cyme or a dichasium, a cluster branching in pairs. The floral cup may be cup-shaped, glandular, bristly or softly hairy. The calyx is membranous, with lobes that may be single-lobed, glandular, bristly, softly hairy or fringed. Pink petals spread outward. The ovary has three chambers and a hairless style. Fruit placentas may be smooth or wrinkled; the fruit is obtriangular and the seed has a beak-like tip. These are alternative states in the regional field.

## What growth substrate is listed first? {#s-5245fb80ba}
One Brazilian Flora row records epiphytic growth, meaning growth on another plant. It gives no locality or collection date.

## Is growth on rock also recorded? {#s-0e22e95d81}
A second row records rupicolous growth on rock surfaces, without a locality or date.

## Is terrestrial growth also recorded? {#s-57eafa1a8e}
A third row lists terrestrial habitat. These three categories are database entries; their sites or relative frequency are not supplied.
