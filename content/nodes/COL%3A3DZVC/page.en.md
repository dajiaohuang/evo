## Tufted growth and small pale flowers {#s-c299889d23}
Ferraria macrochlamys reaches 100–150 mm and is recognised by its tufted growth and small, pale-yellow flowers. The flowers have a narrow cup and widely diverging anther lobes. Leaf form varies among the named subspecies, so the broad species description should not be read as one uniform leaf shape.
