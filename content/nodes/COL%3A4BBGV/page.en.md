## A description limited by dried specimens {#s-f3aaf3877e}
The source describes a herb up to 400 mm tall, with a contractile root on the bulb, smooth bulb tunics and no rhizome runners. It cautions that the account is based only on dried specimens and that the name may cover a polymorphic group; bulbs are almost always missing from herbarium specimens, limiting assessment of root-system characters.
