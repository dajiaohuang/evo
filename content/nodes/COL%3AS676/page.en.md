## Solitary heads of tubular florets {#s-10b2c4a351}
The morphology record describes a plant with stalked leaves that range from obovate to oblong, with edges that may be toothed, lobed or deeply divided. Flower heads are solitary and contain tubular florets without ray florets. The oblong to obovoid fruits are smooth, ribbed and topped by two rows of bristles.
