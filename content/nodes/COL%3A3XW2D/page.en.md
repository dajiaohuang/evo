## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5b92689c86}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as active around dawn or dusk (Crepuscular); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-5d13dc22c8}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 39. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Werner 2010. The highest value among reported litter or clutch means is 50; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to Emer 2007. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
