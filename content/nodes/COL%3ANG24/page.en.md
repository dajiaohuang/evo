## Brunsvigia pulchra {#s-7f4a2f587a}
Brunsvigia pulchra is a 20–30 cm bulbous geophyte with a compact, brush-like head of ruby-red to rose-pink flowers. The account notes erect perianth segments as a distinguishing feature; the leaves are prostrate and dry at flowering. It grows on stony slopes in gravel and granite-derived soils. These traits summarize the regional species account.
