## Selaginella magnafornensis {#s-82e74fce62}
## Ascending stems and coloured spores

The record gives ascending, straw-coloured stems without joints and with smooth surfaces. Rhizophores are reported at ventral, axillary or dorsal positions. Its small leaves differ by position: lateral leaves are ovate-oblong or ovate, while the dorsal leaves are ovate-elliptic with oblique bases and awned tips. Idioblasts, distinctive cells, are recorded in lateral but not dorsal leaves. The spore fields give yellow megaspores and orange microspores.
