## Original-concept characters in a later comparison {#s-17ce6e84b2}
The arutam description for the original concept named Noblella naturetrekii reports two phalanges (finger bones) on finger IV, a granular belly, nuptial pads, and toe tips without papillae. It cites adult lengths of 11.2–12.1 mm for males and 13.1–14.4 mm for females. COL26.8 uses the name Phyllonastes naturetrekii, so the source combination and limited comparison scope are retained. These characters are not a population-wide frequency study or a complete identification key. The authors compare lochites and myrmecoides only through their original descriptions because broader historical labels may include different lineages. The comparison does not establish diet, breeding timing or the species’ full range.
