## The source tadpole concept {#s-e8f8112bd1}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Bombina maxima, in the genus Bombina and family Bombinatoridae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Bombina maxima. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-75cd9cb0ee}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images; its supplied stage label is 26–27. This is the label attached to the source record, not a full developmental timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
