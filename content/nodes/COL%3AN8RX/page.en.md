## Leaf surfaces and keel petal form {#s-f6a2527fcc}
The source entry records branches as pubescent or puberulous. Petioles are puberulent; leaves are chartaceous, not appressed to the stem, and have nonrevolute margins. The adaxial surface is glabrous except for a pubescent midrib; the abaxial surface is glabrous to puberulous. The panicle rachis is tomentose or pubescent, with partial racemes that are umbelliform or lax. Pedicels are puberulent or glabrous. The outer sepal apex is rounded and not dorsally mucronate; the inner sepal apex is dorsally mucronate. The keel is clawed. The style is pubescent on its basal half or arched; the ovary is glabrous or orbicular. The fruit is a glabrous, two valved, loculicidal capsule. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or range.
