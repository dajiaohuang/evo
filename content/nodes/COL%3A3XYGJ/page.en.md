## *Maranta ruiziana*: oval leaves and persistent upright sepals {#s-9a1c33a3e2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character field describes oval leaves with a hairy pulvinus, the joint between leaf stalk and blade. Flower bracts are loosely arranged. The sepals may be as long as or longer than the straight corolla tube; the ovary, which later forms the fruit, is smooth and hairless.

Two calluses are recorded on a staminode, a sterile stamen-like floral part. The ellipsoid fruit retains upright sepals. Its habitat row says “terrestrial” only, with no collection locality or date; this regional description does not quantify how often the alternative sepal lengths occur.
