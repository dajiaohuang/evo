## Panphalea cardaminifolia {#s-598e0fdb8d}
## Winged leaf stalks hold deeply divided blades

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record says the roots are not thickened. Leaves do not change form along the plant; their stalks are winged, and the blades are pinnatifid, with deep divisions along either side of a central midrib. Involucral bracts have pointed or blunt tips, each with a short mucro. No habitat category is supplied in the cited fields.
