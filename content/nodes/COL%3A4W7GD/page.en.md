## *Sebaea scabra* {#s-22d04600fe}
## Rough-veined calyx wings

The SANBI/WFO field describes *S. scabra* as an annual reaching 20 cm, with ovate leaves and yellow flowers divided into five lobes. The calyx lobes carry strongly veined, rough-textured wings. Stamens attach in the gaps between the corolla lobes, and the style has a swelling below its midpoint. This concise morphology entry is attributed to Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 account in *Plants of the Greater Cape Floristic Region*.
