## Mitochondrial sampling and phylogenetic limits {#s-a352327475}
The study compares partial mitochondrial 12S and 16S sequences using maximum-likelihood and Bayesian methods. It reports focal sequences of 417 bp and 551 bp. Table 3 lists 43 species in 21 genera; the broader analysis includes 36 species, alongside selected Asian subtrees. In the broad tree, the closest placement to the focal taxon is Bufoides, with relatively low support; restricted samples change the comparison context. Because both regions are mitochondrial, they are not independent nuclear evidence for ancestry or reproductive isolation. The paper’s phrase “uncorrected k2p” is ambiguous and does not establish a universal distance measure or genus threshold.
