## A comparative series in the 2023 muisca description {#s-d8a543511e}
The B. muisca description compared 26 B. adspersa specimens with SVL range 30.7–66.3 mm and mean 45.0 mm. It described moderate webbing, but less than in its sampled B. muisca, with digit tips extending beyond the web margin, and a relatively thin, long tail. ## Bogotá samples as the sister lineage of muisca {#s-0d9468823c}
In the study’s multilocus tree, B. adspersa samples from Bogotá, its type locality, and surroundings formed the sister lineage of B. muisca with ultrafast bootstrap 98 and posterior probability 0.99. Reported uncorrected mitochondrial distances between the two lineages were 2.1% for 16S and 7.0% for cyt b. These quantities refer to sampled markers and lineages, not genome-wide divergence, elapsed speciation time or measured reproductive isolation. The supported separation does not resolve every historical population previously called adspersa.
