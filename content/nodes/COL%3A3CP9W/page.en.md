## What stem and flower features identify Euphorbia insulana? {#s-331cabbd4b}
The cylindrical stems are hairless. Alternate, entire leaves have pinnate veins and lateral glandular stipules. Green bracts surround loose dichasial clusters of cyathia at the upper nodes. The involucre is top-shaped and hairy, with two, rarely three, bilipped glands whose fleshy appendages stand upright. Female flowers lack a pseudoperianth; the ovary and fruit are hairy, and the style is two-lobed. Seeds lack a caruncle.
