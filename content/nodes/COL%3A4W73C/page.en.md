## Three-part leaves and a laterally marked stone {#s-91a27d6685}
The cited South African account describes a dioecious evergreen shrub or small tree up to 4 m tall. Its leaves have three sessile, obovate leaflets with blunt, crenate tips. Flowers are cream-coloured. The drupes are round and dark blue or brown, and their stones have lateral processes. A separate habitat entry places the species on sandy coastal flats. These are statements from a regional account, without a dated sampling frame or population-frequency estimate.
