## Pfaffia velutina {#s-32c59f8c44}
## Erect hairy stems carry oval leaves and spike-shaped axes

Brazilian Flora 2020 describes erect, hairy stems with leafy branches. The leaves are recorded as elliptic, oblong or oval, with short petioles; the partial flowering axes are spike-shaped. The habitat field says terrestrial, but supplies no locality or sampling coverage, so it does not establish the species' full range.
