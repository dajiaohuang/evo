## Recorded parent of Hippopotamoidea {#s-3f8b3e69cd}
Hippopotamoidea is listed at rank superfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Whippomorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
