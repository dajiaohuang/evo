## Source classification and concept status {#s-a0619688c7}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2691 records Scinax rogerioi under the source classification Scinax (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 35.6 mm for this row. Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
