## *Renealmia matogrossensis*: green bracts on an upright flowering stalk {#s-f022fbf606}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile records striated leaf sheaths and a terminal flower cluster on a branch without leaves. The flowering stalk is upright. Green, membranous bracts carry two or more flowers each, and the calyx is tubular.

The mature capsule is described as globose. The habitat field gives the broad label “terrestrial” but no named locality or date; the description is limited to the regional flora record.
