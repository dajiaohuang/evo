## Stem surface {#s-8b63fa7991}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a hairy stem. The simple hairs may point toward or away from the tip while lying close to the surface (antrorse- or retrorse-appressed). Unbranched bristles are thread-like; branched bristles are listed as feathery or star-shaped. Glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-d03ae28365}
Stipules are deciduous. The record marks extrafloral nectaries and projections between pairs of pinnae as absent. The inflorescence is globose; floral bracts are shorter than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-0c58add384}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous and has no lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and bear stiff, slanting hairs (strigose). The androecium is isostemonous, with stamens equal in number to the petals; the filaments are yellow.

## Habitat category {#s-d20ef9f367}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
