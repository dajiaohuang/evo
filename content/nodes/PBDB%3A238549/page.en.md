There is some confusion over the provenance of MG33 because one of the labels associated with the specimen contradicts the others. Two detailed labels are considered correct and state that MG33 originated from “600 m a N30E du pys. dans Alhadas” and “mélange du lias moyen avec q.q. fossiles du Toarcien”. This is corroborated by Sauvage (1897–1898:2) who identified the skull as “d’anciennes récoltes faites à 1500 métres O.N.O. du village d’Alhadas, et près de Murtede, présentent un mélange de Charmouthien et de Toarcien” (= collected at 1500 m WNW from the village of Alhadas, near Murtede, which yield a mix of Charmouthian [equivalent to Pliensbachian] and Toarcian fossils). An erroneous label gives the location of MG33 as São Pedro de Moel.
