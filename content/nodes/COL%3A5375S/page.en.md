## *Strychnos eugeniifolia* {#s-938a04938f}
## A terminal fork and a large woody fruit

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records a solitary tendril and no spines. Leaf stalks are 2–3 mm long, the leaves have three veins, and the underside has no beard of hairs where the basal veins meet. Terminal flower clusters are dichasial—a repeated fork of side branches—and corymb-shaped. The corolla is tubular or salver-shaped, with a tube longer than its lobes; the ovary and style are hairless.

The fruit is 7–8 cm across, with a woody pericarp 2–4 mm thick and a fruit stalk 11–15 mm across. Seeds are recorded as 5–10 or more, with a crust-like coat. These figures and alternatives are from one regional morphology field, not a population-level estimate.
