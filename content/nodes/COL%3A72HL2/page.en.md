## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-eb0d026832}
COL26.8 records Liolaemus multiformis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Liolaemidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d0e8a5e6ed}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-4a925c71f3}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
