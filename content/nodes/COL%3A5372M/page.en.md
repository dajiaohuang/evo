## *Strychnos brasiliensis* {#s-668408b201}
## Spines in place of tendrils

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records no tendrils and the presence of spines. Leaf stalks are 0.5–4 mm long, and the leaves have 3–5 veins; the underside has no beard of hairs where the basal veins meet. Terminal flower clusters form corymbs or panicles. The corolla is wheel-shaped or shortly funnel-shaped, with a tube shorter than its lobes. The ovary and style are hairless.

Fruits are about 2 cm across, with a fleshy to somewhat leathery pericarp up to 0.5 mm thick; the fruit stalk is about 2 mm across. Seeds are usually 1, sometimes 2, and have a cartilage-like coat. These measurements and alternatives follow one regional dataset field.
