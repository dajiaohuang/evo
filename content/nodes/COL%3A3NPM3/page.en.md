## How does the record characterize Hypericum gentianoides? {#s-a7a8fc190e}
The stems have side branches from base to tip. Opposite leaf pairs cross at right angles; their blades are separate and flat, with unthickened edges and mainly lengthwise veins. Terminal or lateral flower clusters may be branched, or lateral flowers may be solitary; the bracts are leaf-like. Flowers are under 6 mm wide. Sepals are equal or nearly equal, with flat edges; petals may be symmetric or asymmetric. Stamens are separate, and the styles number three or four. The capsule is spindle-shaped. Seeds are yellow or brown, with ridges on the seed coat.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-bae96e385f}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
