## Night vegetation and one tree-hole caller {#s-e67798e1a0}
Specimens with ecological data were collected at night on vegetation 1.5–3 m above ground. At Juyuintza, one male called from a partly flooded tree hole 1.5 m high in terra firme forest, with two other callers nearby. No amplectant pairs, clutches or tadpoles were observed. Calling from a water-filled cavity is not presented as confirmed egg deposition or larval development.
