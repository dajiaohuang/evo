## Macrosamanea froesii {#s-5b98bfc618}

Brazilian Flora 2020 records 3–7 pairs of pinnae, with 12–18 leaflet pairs per pinna; the proximal and distal leaflets differ in shape. The flowers have a white corolla 20–31 mm long, and the compressed oblong follicle opens along its lower suture. The habitat field is marked terrestrial.
