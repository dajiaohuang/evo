## What traits does the Flora do Brasil field record? {#s-a62f03aeb6}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional dataset records star-shaped scales on the stem. Leaves have tapered bases, are elliptic, obovate or oblong, and have petioles 0.8–2.5 cm long; 5–11 pairs of secondary veins are listed. The ovary is one-chambered. Petals are joined into a tube and have pointed tips; the sepals are ovate or triangular. This is the dataset's regional morphology field, not a global description, and it gives no sampling frame or variation information.
