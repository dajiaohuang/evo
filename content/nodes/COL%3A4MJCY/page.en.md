## Accepted name and placement {#s-99d6a68971}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis dendrobatoides Means & Savage, 2007 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-d50be20c1b}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 record for Pristimantis dendrobatoides codes terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal foraging, night activity, direct development, maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm, and up to 1 reproductive event per year.

## Three foraging categories in an older record {#s-45e178c2ac}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging categories under its Frost 2011 source concept. These broad labels do not report how often each applies or how use varies with life stage or season. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian microhabitat entries, but this row has not been checked against those corrections. The source concept is not verified as the current COL26.8 species.

## Night activity category {#s-8d60441bdf}
For the historical source concept, AmphiBIO marks night activity. This is an overall category, not a continuous daily record or a measure of how activity changes during the night. Its equivalence to the current species concept remains unverified.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-ec861c2202}
AmphiBIO reports 31 mm as maximum adult body length under the Frost 2011 source concept. For an anuran, the measure is snout–vent length, from snout to cloaca. The compiled maximum is not a measurement of every adult and has not been reconciled with the current accepted species boundary.

## A yearly reproductive count {#s-3a098b3ef7}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for the historical source concept. The compilation gathers literature counts and extrema; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild/captive conditions. Offspring-size values in the dataset may refer to offspring or eggs.

## A developmental category {#s-d9125d24f8}
The archived AmphiBIO row is coded as direct development. It supplies no further account of parental care, breeding site or seasonal timing, and its older Frost 2011 concept has not been verified against the current accepted species.
