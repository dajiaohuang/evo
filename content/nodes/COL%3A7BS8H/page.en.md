## *Terminalia pulcherrima* {#s-4dcedc5664}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — hairless; leaf-related structures — elliptic, pointed tips; flower clusters (inflorescences) — spikes, axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
