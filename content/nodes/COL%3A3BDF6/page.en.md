## Leaves, flowers and pods {#s-451664cb5b}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry describes a smooth trunk, gray to dark brown with whitish streaks; prickles are present. Petioles may be armed or unarmed. Leaf blades are unarmed and the same colour on both sides; the triangular leaflets have gently wavy margins. Paired round stipels (small appendages at leaflet bases) are recorded. Upright terminal pseudo-racemes (raceme-like flower clusters) bear flowers with a spathe-shaped, papery calyx. The resupinate standard (banner petal, turned from its usual orientation) is orange, less often white; wing petals have ear-like lobes and no claw, and the free keel petals are sickle-shaped. Stamens form two bundles, and anthers attach on their backs. Fruits are oblong, hairy and slightly pinched between seeds. Kidney-shaped seeds are recorded as black, red or red-and-black. These traits come from one regional row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-0dc5211c3f}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It supplies only a habitat category, without locality, range or sampling coverage.
