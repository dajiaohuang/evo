## Groups and fecal samples from different periods {#s-d1d6164323}
From 2015-2022, field teams directly observed 18 green-monkey groups in southern mainland Guinea-Bissau and the Bijagós Archipelago: 3 in Cufada Lagoons Natural Park, 3 in Dulombi National Park, and 12 in Boé National Park. Observed settings included mangroves, primary and secondary forest, woodland, and herbaceous savanna. A separate molecular sample comprised 76 fecal specimens from 11 locations collected during 2008-2022. The group observations and genetic samples document selected sites; they do not provide a national census, a complete range, or a habitat-comparative occupancy analysis.
