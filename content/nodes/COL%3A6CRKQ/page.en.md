## Diaphananthe fragrantissima {#s-820b8dbb5c}
The regional account describes Diaphananthe fragrantissima with several hanging racemes and many small greenish or yellowish flowers, arranged opposite one another or in whorls of four. Its strap-shaped leaves are deeply unequal at the tip, and the lip has a fringed edge and a short point. The account places it in hot coastal bush, gallery forest and dry woodland.
