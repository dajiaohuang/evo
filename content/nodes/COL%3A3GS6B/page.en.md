## Gomesa imperatoris-maximiliani {#s-0c04934989}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes Gomesa imperatoris-maximiliani with smooth, laterally compressed pseudobulbs and oblong-ovate leaves. Its brown flowers lack spots, and the petals are much larger than the sepals. The lip is brown with a contrasting centre and reduced side lobes; its five-lobed callus is warty. The habitat field lists it as epiphytic.
