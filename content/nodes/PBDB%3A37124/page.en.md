## PBDB’s recorded fossil classification {#s-3afa01a2ae}
PBDB’s 2 October 2026 snapshot records Trematosauridae Watson 1919 at family rank under Trematosauroidea. This page follows that supplementary fossil classification; it does not establish that a similarly named COL entry represents the same taxon.

## What the captured fossil records show {#s-4938aecb47}
In the captured PBDB hierarchy, 24 accepted fossil-species concepts are placed under Trematosauridae. The occurrence snapshot contains 46 records with exact taxon-ID matches; 0 of the 24 concepts have no matching record in this capture. Across these 46 dated collection intervals, the outer age bounds run from 259.857 to 227.3 million years ago. These pooled collection bounds do not date the taxon’s origin or extinction and do not show continuous presence through the full span. PBDB’s placement is supplementary and does not establish equivalence with a similarly named COL group.

## What the fossil records cannot tell us {#s-515d34f82d}
This section makes no claims about morphology, life history, ecology, evolution, geographic range, fossil interpretation, or conservation. Collected reference metadata and indexes are discovery aids, not verified biological evidence. The PBDB summary above reports only its recorded classification and occurrence data.
