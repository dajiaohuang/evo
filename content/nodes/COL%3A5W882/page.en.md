## The inflorescence and its flowers {#s-ca02978c04}
The Brazilian Flora field describes a boat-shaped spathe (the bract around the flower cluster), with part of the female zone of the spadix (flower-bearing axis) attached to it. The fused stamens form an urn-shaped synandrium (a structure of joined stamens); the sterile (non-pollen-producing) stamens are joined and shorter than half the ovary length. These specialized floral descriptors come from a regional entry, which gives no sampling frame for variation.

## The soil-associated category {#s-8bcccf22fe}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records “terrícola” (soil-associated) for this species. The row supplies no locality or date, so it does not map an occurrence.
