## What leaf, fruit and seed traits identify Dilkea exilis? {#s-a633d4549f}
Growth is intermittent and rhythmic. Alternate leaves have petioles at least 1 cm long, narrowed above the joint-like pulvinus. The narrow oblanceolate blades are up to 5 cm wide and membranous to papery. The spherical fruit is up to 5 cm long. Seeds are ovate, not flattened from side to side, and longer than 1.5 cm.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-8bd0d8fe8d}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
