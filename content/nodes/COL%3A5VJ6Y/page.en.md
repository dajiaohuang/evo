## Palmately divided blades {#s-48817d3a93}
The regional flora describes a creeping stem and palmately compound leaves (their divisions radiate from one point like fingers from a palm), whose forms are listed as linear, elliptic, or ovate. The spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) is ovate and the berries are wine-colored. These descriptors do not establish how common the listed forms are in the species.

## A hemiepiphytic category {#s-a7afb0dd13}
The habitat field in Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 uses “hemiepífita,” the category for a growth form with an epiphytic phase. The row gives no locality or date and does not describe the stages or circumstances of growth.
