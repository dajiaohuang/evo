## Phoradendron poeppigii {#s-ac8b5b77f7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 account records a hemiparasitic plant with scale-like leaves and an inflorescence that may be axillary or terminal.

## Scale-like leaves and ovoid fruit {#s-e87c28bbea}
One source description gives stems that are monopodial or sympodial and dichotomously branched, circular in cross-section and unwinged. Sheathing cataphylls occur at every internode; one pair is recorded and fertile cataphylls are absent. Leaves are sessile and scale-like, with pinnate venation, an obtuse base and an acute tip. Inflorescences may be axillary or terminal; the source records two pairs of sterile basal bracts, bisexual flowers, three or four cylindrical fertile articles with flowers in two rows, and staminate flowers at the article tips. Fruits are ovoid, smooth, and red or orange, with erect petals. The same regional record labels the habit “Hemiparasita”; it gives no host or complete range.
