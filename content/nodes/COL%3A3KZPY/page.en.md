## What does the regional account describe about the shrublet? {#s-08acefbcbd}
The account describes a roughly hairy shrublet up to 1.5 m tall. Its oblong leaves have three teeth near the tip. Solitary flowers grow in the upper leaf axils along horizontal racemes, all facing one side. The flowers are bell-shaped and mauve with dark veins.

## What additional flower and fruit features does the revision note? {#s-76623f2228}
A 1980 revision describes a woody, much-branched shrub with linear leaves, usually three-toothed near the tip. Drooping, bell-shaped flowers face one side near branchlet tips; their petals fold together to give the flower a flat-looking end. The threadlike styles may project slightly. Short, broad, five-lobed capsules remain partly covered by the persistent flower envelope; each lobe tip bears a pair of horizontal, hairy horns.

## What substrate is reported? {#s-c5224dca81}
The regional account reports stony soils. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
