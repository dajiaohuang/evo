## Syspira longipes {#s-abf285809f}
## Markings described in the taxonomic account

The 2025 account describes net-like markings on the carapace and light-grey coloration in living *Syspira longipes*. It gives descriptions and measurements for male and female specimens and reports variation within the examined material. These are observations from that treatment, not an estimate of variation across all populations.

## Syspira longipes {#s-2f87a80d29}
## Reported microhabitats in Baja California

The authors record the species from dry (xeric) parts of the Baja California Peninsula. Their account lists bare soil, fallen wood, leaf litter, gravel and cobbles, vegetation above the ground, beach areas, and sites near oases. These are literature-based habitat reports from the cited treatment, not a complete or exclusive niche description.
