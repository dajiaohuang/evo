## Accepted name and placement {#s-6552b3ba8a}
COL26.8 accepts Ansonia platysoma Inger, 1960 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Bufonidae (family) → Ansonia (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-a4bc85b2bf}
The same compilation gives a maximum adult snout–vent length of 26 mm. This is a literature-derived maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every frog.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-d70d278c75}
The historical row records at most one reproductive event per year. It does not link this count to a sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive setting; its offspring-size field may refer to eggs.
