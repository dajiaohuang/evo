## Tianping material and a cited neighboring reserve {#s-880f42d34e}
The account listed the male holotype from Tianping Mountain, Hunan, at 1600 m and one examined male from the same mountain at about 1380 m. Its cited distribution summary included Tianping in northwestern Hunan and neighboring Houhe Nature Reserve in southwestern Hubei at 1100–1600 m. Examined material and the broader cited summary are distinct evidence; neither demonstrates continuous occupancy between sites or physiological elevation limits.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-9ede285826}
