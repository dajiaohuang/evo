## What one regional flora field records {#s-7f6a8814c2}
The COL26.8 species usage for Pinnatella minuta (Mitt.) Broth. matches one accepted World Flora Online name and authorship record.

Its Brazilian Flora entry is regional data, and the habitat row is attributed at dataset level.

The row is labelled “Corticícola,” a bark-dwelling substrate category.

No collection locality, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage is supplied with the category.

The regional row supplies a substrate label only, so this page does not describe the species' full biology.
