## Polygala krumanina {#s-4e71388f36}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala krumanina* as an accepted species. A regional account describes it as a perennial herb with eight fertile stamens. Its flattened capsule is winged and ranges from nearly spherical to obovoid; the seeds have a caruncle (a small seed appendage) and dense hairs. The source also records steppe habitat. These features and the habitat label belong to a regional account and do not establish a global range or variation pattern.
