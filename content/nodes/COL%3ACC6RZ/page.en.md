## A fungus described from two Yunnan collections {#s-d1fcfce5d4}
Tatraea yunnanensis C.J.Y. Li & Q. Zhao is the accepted species in Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage CC6RZ.

The name crosswalk includes Species Fungorum Plus and Index Fungorum record 901180; this source is used for identity, not biological claims.

The 2024 description measured ten dry apothecia, or fruiting bodies: 3.8–5.0 mm wide and 2.5–4.1 mm high, with means of 4.8 ± 0.8 mm by 3.7 ± 0.8 mm. The sampled bodies were scattered, superficial when fresh, short-stalked and without hairs; the receptacles were brown and the discs pastel green to light green. These measurements describe the collected material, not the full range of variation in the species.

The authors describe Tatraea yunnanensis as saprobic on decayed wood; both reported collections were made from decayed wood.

In the paper's combined LSU and ITS phylogeny, Tatraea yunnanensis was placed as sister to T. macrospora, with 98% maximum-likelihood bootstrap support and a Bayesian posterior probability of 1.0. The reported Φw statistic for this pair was 1.0. This is a relationship in that study's sampled tree, not a claim that one species is the direct ancestor of the other.

The paper reports two collections in Yunnan, China: the type from Jingdong County, Puer City (1,455 m; 23 August 2022) and a paratype from Tengchong City (1,714 m; 16 August 2022). These are the documented study localities, not a complete geographic range.

## Limits of the account {#s-d2373ba66f}
The description covers measured collections; it does not systematically establish a genus-wide diagnosis, additional populations, developmental stages or within-species variation.

The paper describes substrate and saprobic habit, but not interaction mechanisms, the identity of the wood where it was not identified, seasonality or population ecology.

The phylogenetic placement is specific to the study's sampled taxa and loci. Broader phylogenomic evidence, alternative topologies and divergence times were not assessed; the authors regard genus placement as provisional because the type species lacks genetic data.

The account documents only the named Yunnan collections; it does not establish a global range, a georeferenced boundary or native versus introduced status.

Life history, fossils and conservation are not assessed here. The account is bounded to one original taxonomic paper and is not a comprehensive species review.

The Index Fungorum name link matches this COL usage, but broader reconciliation of species concepts has not been completed.
