The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Eipper 2012, Feldman et al. 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-dae8b22117}
