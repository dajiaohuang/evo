## Manilkara salzmannii (A.DC.) H.J.Lam {#s-201dff4a88}
## Ten-locular ovary and variable flower clusters

Manilkara salzmannii has stem lenticels and stipules. Its leathery leaves have channelled petioles and smooth or hairy undersides; leaf tips range from rounded or blunt to retuse or notched. Inflorescences may have up to five flowers or more than five. The ovary is smooth and has ten chambers, while the sterile stamens have two-parted tips. The source records one or two seeds.
