## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-32448508b3}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Physalaemus bokermanni Cardoso & Haddad, 1985 as a species in the genus Physalaemus, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.

## What the classification can tell us {#s-e60912031a}
The accepted record places this species within Physalaemus, Leptodactylidae and Anura. That classification gives a route to related catalogue entries, but it does not by itself establish the species' full morphology, life history, distribution, evolutionary history or conservation status.

## A compilation codes aquatic larval development and egg laying {#s-69deddd841}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Physalaemus bokermanni as having a larval stage and laying eggs in an aquatic setting. These are categories compiled from literature, not a new observation of spawning or a developmental experiment. The codes do not give larval duration, every larval habitat, or how strongly development depends on standing water. The compilation's taxonomic concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to the current COL26.8 concept.

## Juvenile habitat and egg-site records are broad categories {#s-89badf900a}
The same row codes juvenile habitat as aquatic and records both bubble nests on still water and foam nests among seven egg-site categories.
