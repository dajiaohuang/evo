## The source concept {#s-f97bc0fa7a}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Callulina stanleyi* in row Anf0340 under Callulina, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-e4b99419e1}
For Anf0340, the compilation tags arboreal as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-ea987b918a}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-cd3cb289e0}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 42.5 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
