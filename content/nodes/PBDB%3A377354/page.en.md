## The fossil behind the record {#s-bb410fd71e}
Ledumahadi is known here through partial postcranial holotype BP/1/7120 from South Africa’s earliest Jurassic Elliot Formation. The specimen anchors the account to a named fossil and formation; it is not, by itself, evidence for the genus’s full range or for when quadrupedal posture evolved.

## Read the occurrence in context {#s-6b633ebdce}
The PBDB route lists selected records for the named Ledumahadi species. Check the specimen identification, collection and formation together when reading its age. This source-bounded occurrence helps locate the material, while the anatomical study supplies the specific holotype context.

## Evidence limits {#s-6c30b4c0d7}
The partial holotype supports a record from the earliest Jurassic Elliot Formation. It does not define a global genus range or establish the origin of quadrupedal sauropodomorphs. The occurrence and the broader evolutionary question are different claims.
