## Two measurements of length {#s-75c22007c4}
The listed maximum snout–vent length is 77 mm, and the female maximum is 73.6 mm. Snout–vent length measures from the snout to the body opening and excludes the tail. These compiled maxima are not typical sizes or growth limits; sex-specific values may come from different samples and do not alone show the size difference within a population.
