## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0d66a7d729}
COL26.8 records Podarcis lusitanicus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Lacertidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5723c72a7d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-9c6caa2345}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 4. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
