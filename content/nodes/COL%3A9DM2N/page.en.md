## Senna biglandularis {#s-ff2a4ab205}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna biglandularis* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry lists four pairs or 6–10 pairs of leaflets, with extra-floral glands either between leaflet pairs or on the flower stalk. It records seven fertile stamens and hanging pods that are linear or four-angled. These alternatives are retained as the source reports them.
