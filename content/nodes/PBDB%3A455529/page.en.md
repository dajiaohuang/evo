## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-99323940be}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Pachagnathus at genus rank under Raeticodactylidae. This placement organizes the taxon record; it is not, by itself, a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-720910681c}
The linked occurrence guide includes Pachagnathus benitoi and a collection recorded from Quebrada del Barro. Read the age and identification with that collection: one listed locality does not establish the genus’s complete distribution.

## Evidence limits {#s-bcc088f7b6}
This page follows selected PBDB occurrences, not a complete range or an ecological account. Database parentage and age bounds describe the record set; they do not establish direct ancestry or exact origin and extinction dates.
