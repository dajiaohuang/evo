## ## Palmate leaves and closed flowers {#s-0f6402fc7d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes an upright, semi-woody or herbaceous plant with palmately arranged leaves made up of three leaflets. Axillary pseudoracemes bear lilac to purple flowers, and cleistogamous flowers—flowers that remain closed—are also recorded. The androecium is described as pseudo-monadelphous, with the stamens partly joined. The oblong, ribbed fruit has leathery valves, and the seeds are globose. These regional fields do not specify the frequency of the closed flowers.
