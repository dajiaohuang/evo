## What underground, leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Stillingia salpingadenia? {#s-8f1e3055f8}
A xylopodium, a woody underground structure, is present. The branches are not hollow, varnished or marked by lenticels (small gas-exchange openings). Leaves are spirally arranged, with stalks and narrow elliptic, papery blades. The blade base is pointed; the thickened edge is toothed, and the veins loop toward the margin. Glands are absent at the blade base. Floral bracts have a tiny point at the tip and tubular glands. Female sepals are elliptic with a bristle-like tip. The capsule has three lobes, and the seed coat is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-01ce08a2d4}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
