## White flowers with the style inside the tube {#s-b0a857c14f}
Geissorhiza esterhuyseniae is a tiny plant, 7–8 cm tall, with white flowers. The diagnostic account notes that both stamens and style remain inside a relatively long perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts), up to 16 mm. It also says that corms have not been documented, despite the geophyte description in the regional morphology record.

## Sheltered south-facing sandstone {#s-8123d4a404}
The cited flora places it on rocky, south-facing sandstone slopes at about 1,200 m. This is a habitat observation, not a measured range boundary.
