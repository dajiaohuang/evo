## Stylogyne cauliflora (Mart. & Miq.) Mez {#s-c53674278a}
## Entire leaves and mixed flower clusters

Stylogyne cauliflora has simple, alternate, stalked leaves with elliptic to oblong, glabrous blades and entire margins. The inflorescence is described as an indeterminate, corymb-like cluster. Flowers have four or five parts, with elliptic buds and oblong, punctate petals. The anthers open by a short slit and by pores at the tip.
