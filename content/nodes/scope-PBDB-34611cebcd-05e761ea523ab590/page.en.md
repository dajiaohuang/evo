## Neoceti’s timing combines fossils and models {#s-5974058555}
In the cited synthesis, Neoceti is the sampled crown-whale branch joining Mysticeti and Odontoceti. Its Eocene–Oligocene timing combines fossil and model evidence rather than directly observing the crown split. The 36–0 Ma display is a synthesis-bounded navigation envelope, not an exact global endpoint pair or direct date of that split.
