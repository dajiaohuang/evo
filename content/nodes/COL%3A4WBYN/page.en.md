## *Selaginella lechleri*: ventral rhizophores and unequal leaves {#s-a87ec6fe4d}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, pale, hairless stems are decumbent or upright and lack joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise on the lower side of the stem. Side leaves are oblong-lanceolate with asymmetrical bases and pointed tips; upper leaves are ovate-elliptic with slanted bases and pointed or tapering tips. Axillary leaves are lance-shaped, with blunt bases and sharp tips.

The source records an idioblast, a specialized cell type, in side leaves but not upper leaves. Its habitat field gives the broad category “terrestrial,” with no named locality or date.
