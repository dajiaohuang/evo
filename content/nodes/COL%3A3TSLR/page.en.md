## Barcode-supported identification retains provisional boundaries {#s-5dea20a4e9}
They occupy one COI BIN and ABGD group and form a16S clade sister to a Cameroon millsoni-labelled reference KF888342, with greater than4.5% divergence. Sister placement does not turn the aubryioides records into millsoni, and the two mitochondrial markers do not independently test reproductive isolation.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-76c897b2fb}
For Leptopelis aubryioides, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
