## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-5a91e415b4}
COL26.8 lists Ischnocnema melanopygia Targino, Costa & Carvalho-e-Silva, 2009 as a species in Anura, family Brachycephalidae, genus Ischnocnema. This is the classification in the release dated 20 August 2026; it does not reconcile names used by other databases.

## The workbook codes direct development and terrestrial eggs {#s-9660e4d5de}
The 2023 Atlantic Forest workbook's row 206 codes direct development=1 and terrestrial oviposition=1 for Ischnocnema_melanopygia. In the paper, direct development means there is no free-living larval stage: a froglet hatches rather than a free-living tadpole. It does not mean embryos do not develop or that humidity is unnecessary. These are compiled literature categories, not a new observation of this species spawning or a developmental experiment; the row's name has not been certified against the current COL26.8 concept.
