## Accepted name in the catalogue {#s-5a12b6e7d4}
The 20 August 2026 release of Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) accepts Eleutherodactylus dennisi (Lynch, 1970) as a species. Its recorded path is Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family), then Eleutherodactylus (genus). This is the name and placement in that release; another checklist or fossil database needs separate identity evidence.

In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs, but total length for salamanders and caecilians. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a value for every individual; its equivalence to the current catalogue concept has not been established.
