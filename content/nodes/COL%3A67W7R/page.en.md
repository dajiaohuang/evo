## Asterostigma cubense {#s-977ee5fe61}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes a boat-shaped spathe (a bract around the flower-bearing axis), with the female zone of the spadix (flower-bearing axis) partly attached to it. Staminodes (sterile stamens) are fused and no longer than half the ovary; the triangular stigma lobes sit at about 45 degrees to the inflorescence axis. The habitat field records soil. These are regional observations, not a population-wide diagnosis.
