## How fossil calibrations change the angiosperm clock {#s-250cce9353}
A 2026 molecular-clock analysis combined 25,685 non-pollen fossil occurrences with 110 calibrations for a tree of 644 species and 83 genes. Its preferred hard-bound run estimated crown Angiospermae at 152.99–151.46 Ma; soft-bound and skew-T treatments gave 172.53–158.45 Ma and 261.26–212.30 Ma. These are calibration-dependent divergence estimates, not observed Jurassic flowers or fossil first appearances.
