## What curves around its single female spikelet? {#s-3e67269843}
A regional account describes *Restio curvibracteatus* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 20 cm, with simple culms (stems) and sheaths wrapped tightly around them. It bears a single female spikelet (a small flower cluster). The floral bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) taper to a point and curve backward. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
