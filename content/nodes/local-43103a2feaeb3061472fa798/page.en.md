## Ornithischians with theropods, or Saurischia? {#s-f118a6f8e9}
Baron et al. (2017) analyzed a 74-taxon, 457-character morphological matrix and recovered Ornithoscelida, joining Theropoda with Ornithischia. Their compact projection places Sauropodomorpha and Herrerasauridae as sampled terminals within Saurischia sensu Baron. Langer et al. (2017) rescored the characters and expanded the taxon sample, recovering Ornithischia opposite a monophyletic Saurischia containing Sauropodomorpha and Theropoda. These are alternative sampled tree roots, not the atlas classification tree or a claim that one group is the direct ancestor of another.

## What changes between the analyses {#s-efe663e85f}
Both trees are compact projections. The Baron projection does not show all 74 sampled taxa or all 457 characters; this record describes the Langer analysis as rescored and taxon-expanded but gives no sample total. Character rescoring, outgroup choice and taxon sampling change the root. Branch lengths and displayed temporal ranges are not support values or divergence times. Both trees stop at three sampled major lineages and do not include Avialae or crown birds.
