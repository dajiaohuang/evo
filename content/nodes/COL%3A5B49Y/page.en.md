## Vernonanthura lucida {#s-7852439a87}
## Gland-dotted leaves on a hairless stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, hairless stem without glandular dots. Sessile, subleathery leaves are lance-shaped with acute tips and rounded bases. Their faces are recorded as becoming hairless and bearing glands. Venation is described as hyphodromous, with lateral veins absent or inconspicuous.

Involucral bract tips may be acute or blunt. Each head contains 8–22 florets; the corolla is hairless and lacks glands. The pubescent dry fruits have no glands, and the outer pappus bristles are cylindrical. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
