## Source classification and concept status {#s-72ccbac18d}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2467 records Lysapsus caraya under the source classification Lysapsus (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (fossorial and aquatic) {#s-cb22a8c582}
The compilation records fossorial and aquatic as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. ## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-8ad1d6d601}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 16.5 mm for this row. The source field Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
