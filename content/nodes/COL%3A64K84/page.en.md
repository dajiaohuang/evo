## What petal, stamen and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-0dfc0a478f}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records petiolate leaves and five-part flowers. Each flower has ten stamens of two forms and no staminodes (sterile stamens). The anthers curve and end in a slightly beak-like tip; petals are pink. The ovary is hairless and three-chambered. The fruit is a capsule, and the seeds are coiled. This is a regional database description and gives no wider sampling information.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-cfe5c85834}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial, with no locality or collection date.
