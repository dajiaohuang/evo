## A bag-cultivation substrate trial {#s-27a023996f}
In one managed cultivation experiment, the Hangxiu 1 strain was grown in substrate bags. Treatment A3 replaced 20% of cottonseed shell with camellia shell and yielded 407.83 ± 24.93 g per bag; biological efficiency was 72.83 ± 4.45% (mean ± SD, n = 3). The article found no significant yield or biological-efficiency differences among tested 0–20% replacement treatments.

## Evidence limits {#s-657e25faca}
This result belongs to one bag-cultivation trial with strain Hangxiu 1. Trial location and dates are not stated, and wild populations were not studied.
