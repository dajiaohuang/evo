## Why the inherited range was removed {#s-ed9676d97b}
The atlas withholds a Scorpiones fossil range because its former Silurian anchor, *Parioscorpio*, was re-examined and rejected as a scorpion by Anderson and colleagues in 2021. Until independent scorpion evidence supports a revised interval, this entry does not assign an origin date.

The previous older endpoint relied on the Silurian occurrence of *Parioscorpio*. The 2021 redescription rejected that identification, so the fossil cannot establish the global range of Scorpiones. The atlas leaves the interval withheld rather than retaining an unsupported boundary.

## No date is implied by the placeholder {#s-e5b4c0abac}
The zero endpoints in the range record are withheld placeholders, not ages for scorpion origin or survival. A new interval requires independently supported scorpion fossil evidence.
