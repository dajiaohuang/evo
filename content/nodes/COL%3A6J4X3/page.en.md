## Catalogue placement {#s-c84628479f}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 lists *Fissicalyx fendleri* as an accepted species in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-afd81382f5}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrestrial. This is a habitat category only; the field gives no locality, range or sampling coverage.
