## Pteronia lucilioides {#s-e27a861056}
## Straw-coloured pappus on glandular fruits

The source describes a shrub or dwarf shrub up to 1 m tall, with small opposite leaves that are densely white-scabrid or papillose. Solitary heads are terminal; the buds are sharply pointed and become oblong-cylindrical at flowering. Yellow disc florets mature into cypselae densely covered with glandular hairs; the pappus is straw-coloured. A taxonomic note describes size variation between taller plants near Aus and dwarf forms nearer the coast, and reports evidence separating P. quadrifaria from P. lucilioides by leaf arrangement, phyllary form and pappus colour. The habitat field places P. lucilioides on rocky mountain slopes and outcrops in sandy or stony soil.
