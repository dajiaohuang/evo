## Haemanthus nortieri {#s-fb43351c28}
Haemanthus nortieri has a usually solitary, erect, leathery leaf that feels rough and slightly sticky; sand may adhere to it. The compact flower head is deep red, with five to eight pointed, leathery bracts. The account records seasonal washes as habitat. These features distinguish the source’s regional description without establishing a full range.
