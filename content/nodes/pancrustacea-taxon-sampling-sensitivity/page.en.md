## Change the sample, change the pancrustacean tree {#s-58cf5d31d0}
One analysis compared two pancrustacean matrices built with the same ortholog-selection procedure: a 98-taxon set and a 105-taxon set containing 90 transcriptomes, 15 genomes and 576 protein-coding genes. Changing the sampled taxa altered relationships among deep branches, even though the gene-selection pipeline was shared.

The result makes a methodological point: a tree can be sensitive to who is included. The study also estimated divergence times using thirteen vetted calibrations, but those dates remain clock-model outputs. Neither the topology nor its dates should be treated as a final, universal crustacean tree.
