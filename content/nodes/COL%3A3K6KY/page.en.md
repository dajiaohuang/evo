## Helichrysum dasymallum {#s-ff59691c4e}
## Woolly leaves and many yellow-flowered heads

The SANBI account describes a perennial herb up to 400 mm tall. Leaves reach 130 by 50 mm and become smaller up the stem. Lower leaves are obovate to elliptic, with broad bases that partly clasp the stem; upper leaves are obovate to oblanceolate and densely covered in gray-white wool.

Many turbinate-to-bell-shaped heads, about 9 mm long, form small corymb-like panicles. Their involucral bracts are yellow. The account compares the species with *H. oreophilum*, distinguishing it by its larger heads and woolly covering. The habitat field lists grassland; no complete range is established here.
