Archived PBDB field scm (source-language text; not translated or interpreted):
Kitching (1977, "Distribution of the Karroo Vertebrate Fauna") assigns Knofloksfontein to the Tapinocephalus Zone which was split into a lower part (Tapinocephalus or Deinocephalian Assemblage Zone) and an upper part (Pristerognathus Assemblage Zone) by later authors. The presence of Glanosuchus and Bradysaurus suggests an assignment to the Tapinocephalus AZ and thus, the Abrahamskraal Fm. (cf. fig. 3 in Rubidge et al., 1995, SACS Biostrat. Ser. 1). The Tapinocephalus AZ is entirely within the Capitanian stage (see fig. 25 in Catuneanu et al., 2005, J. Afr. Earth Sci. 43(1-3)).
Formation assignment questionable.

Archived PBDB field ggc (source-language text; not translated or interpreted):
"Knoflok's Fontein, near Van der Byl's Kraal in the Gouph." (Broom, 1904). No further details are given. There is a farm named "Knoflok Fontein" in the former Laingsburg District. The name "The Gouph", however, refers to the plains in the surroundings of Beaufort West. There is a farm and a river named "Van der Byls Kraal" near Merweville in the Beaufort West Local Municipality and Kitching (1977) lists a locality "Knofloksfontein, Beaufort West" which, given the fossils found there, well may be the one mentioned by Broom (1904).
