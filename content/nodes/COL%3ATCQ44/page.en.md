## Accepted name and placement {#s-23e1f7758d}
COL26.8 accepts Theloderma hekouense Du, Wang, Liu & Yu, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Rhacophoridae (family) → Theloderma (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Hekou specimens and a Vietnamese molecular attribution {#s-ca55ca9e64}
Besides the Hekou type locality in Yunnan, China, the description attributed Ha Giang samples from northern Vietnam to the new species through molecular analysis. This separates the Chinese type-locality evidence from a reassignment of Vietnamese samples, without implying a complete range survey.

## Water-filled cavities and artificial traps {#s-cd758da87b}
The account described shrub use and breeding in water-filled tree hollows. All Yunnan specimens were found in artificial water-bottle breeding traps used to survey amphibian diversity. That trapping context is retained: it is not rewritten as direct observation of every specimen breeding in a natural tree hollow, and provides no clutch-size or larval-development estimate.
