## Anf1371 and the recorded concept {#s-df7a6987fb}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1371 is the entry for Eupsophus queulensis, placed by the source in Eupsophus (Cycloramphidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-e264f21427}
For Eupsophus queulensis, row Anf1371 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-725b5c3c72}
Its maximum adult body length is 47.3 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
