## Recorded parent of Geosaurinae {#s-82aa756de1}
Geosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Metriorhynchidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
