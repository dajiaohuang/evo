## Source classification and concept status {#s-c7b58fca4d}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3423 records Leptodactylus rhodomerus under the source classification Leptodactylus (Leptodactylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.
