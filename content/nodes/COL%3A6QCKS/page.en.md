## Limnophyton obtusifolium {#s-943dc968fe}
The South African SANBI regional account describes an annual aquatic herb up to 1 m tall, with erect arrow-shaped leaves on long petioles that are triangular in cross-section. Its pyramidal panicle (a branched flower cluster) bears white flowers in 3–7 whorls. The habitat field simply says “Pans”; the account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
