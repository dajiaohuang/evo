## Ecological evidence {#s-9b0be840fd}
A year-long study of one Rwenzori Angolan colobus (Colobus angolensis ruwenzorii) band near Lake Nabugabo, Uganda, identified 12 core units among 135 individuals. Some core units associated non-randomly in two clans, supporting three observed levels: core unit, clan and band. This describes one studied population of one subspecies and should not be generalized to every Colobus angolensis population.
