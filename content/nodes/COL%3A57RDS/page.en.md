## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-dae8f26708}
COL26.8 records Trachylepis chimbana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ac54877c7c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f8976f2a34}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-f05c684916}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
