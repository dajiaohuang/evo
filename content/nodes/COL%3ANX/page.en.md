## A family sample within a larger order {#s-8a6e0f729f}
A comprehensive comparative-skeletal monograph samples living and fossil Amiidae and presents explicit phylogenetic hypotheses for that family. The atlas withholds an order-wide Amiiformes range because this family-focused sample does not validate the former 251.9 Ma–present interval for every member of the order.

The monograph compares living and fossil amiids and tests their relationships using skeletal characters. That broad family sample is useful for Amiidae, but it does not by itself establish the full stratigraphic span or origin of Amiiformes as an order.

## Why no order-wide interval is shown {#s-0fdc50eb94}
The previous 251.9 Ma–present display is not directly supported across the order-level concept. The atlas therefore withholds the interval rather than extrapolating a family record to all Amiiformes.
