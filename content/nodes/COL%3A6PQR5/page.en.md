## Lessingianthus floccosus {#s-45119c088f}
## Villous leaf undersides and solitary sessile heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and stems with leaves along their length, unbranched or sparsely branched. Leathery leaves are elliptic, ovate, lance-shaped or oblanceolate, with acute tips and rounded, heart-shaped or squared-off bases. The upper face is hairless or brown-woolly; the underside is villous. Margins may roll under and range from entire to wavy.

Solitary, sessile heads have bell-shaped involucres with 5–6 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 10, 15, 20 or 25 florets. Cylindrical dry fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
