## atlas-profile / overview {#s-8551dff403}

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-e6f38e2dd7}
The approximately 516 Ma colony preserves diagnostic tube construction and a stolon. Its pterobranch placement comes from morphology analysis; suspension feeding is directly observed only in living Rhabdopleura normani.

## Tubes, zooids and a connecting stolon {#s-a8d19ca7eb}
Five Yunotubus specimens preserve a colony of zooids, tubular housing and a stolon linking the units. The study describes the tube as fusellar, built from repeated ring-like sections. These structures together, rather than a tube alone, support comparison with pterobranchs.

## A fossil placement and a living feeding sample {#s-03899b7301}
A morphology matrix places Yunotubus within the pterobranch comparison, but this does not make every Cambrian tube a pterobranch. Suspension feeding by ciliated tentacles is directly described for living Rhabdopleura normani; the fossil colony’s diet and movement were not observed in the same way.
