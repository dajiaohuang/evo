## Name and AVONET sample {#s-1898d84000}
COL26.8 accepts Pelecanus philippensis within Pelecanidae. AVONET v7 links its data to the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Six individuals were measured, with 4 complete morphological sets; usable samples may differ by trait.

## Beak dimensions {#s-24fc16a2f7}
Mean beak length is 312.2 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 51.9 mm and depth 45.1 mm at that edge. These are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-d760b0a9f2}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 562.8 mm; mean tarsus length is 86.9 mm. AVONET’s compiled mean mass is 5010.98 g, sourced to Dunning. This is separate species-average information, not necessarily a measurement from the morphology sample.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-f525e69940}
AVONET assigns Wetland habitat, an Aquatic predator trophic niche and an Aquatic primary lifestyle. These are broad comparison labels; they do not describe every habitat or feeding behavior.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-e3124ee8d3}
The migration category is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude −5.36° to 28.64°. The endpoints do not capture non-breeding movements, current position or occupancy throughout the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## The August 2015 life-history entry {#s-0766cf41d8}
The 2015 archive provides 6 focal fields in cited same-name, same-family, species-level rows. This supports historical association, but its summaries may retain inherited taxonomy; name matching does not remove that influence.

## Adult mass in the life-history archive {#s-4cbae42e5d}
The archive reports adult body mass of 4900 g. Adult records may combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; this is not a range of individual masses. No birth or hatching mass is added.

## Reproduction and development {#s-8566227c59}
The source-defined female maturity field (age at first conception) is 913.2 d; litter or clutch size is 3 offspring or eggs; oviposition-to-hatching time is 30 d; and age at flight capability is 90 d (some sources define it as leaving the nest). Here, d means days. These are summaries of distinct events, not one reproductive-cycle measure.

## A maximum record, not typical lifespan {#s-0c9e7b19cb}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 31 years, selected as the largest record under its method. It is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, an individual survival ceiling or a current record known to be repeatedly attainable.

## Limits across these historical sources {#s-ee3ce7989d}
This page combines AVONET v7, the August 2015 life-history archive and EltonTraits 1.0; it is not a complete species account. AVONET morphology values are sample means, its mass has separate provenance, and its ecology fields are broad categories; the map is historical. The 2015 archive reports compiled summaries rather than measurements of one individual and may retain inherited taxonomy. Conditions are not consistently known, and source papers were not individually reviewed. EltonTraits weights come from literature descriptions, not direct measurement; code B retains uncertainty in proportions. Same-name and same-family matching does not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Current range, ecology, population and conservation need other sources.
