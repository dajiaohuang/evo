## The source tadpole concept {#s-a092bf2b7d}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Andinobates daleswansoni. The source classification lists Andinobates as the genus and Dendrobatidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Andinobates daleswansoni. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
