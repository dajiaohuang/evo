## Jurassic skeletons test haramiyidan placement {#s-7e4c5a2900}
Three Jurassic euharamiyidan skeletons add cranial and postcranial traits to a mammaliaform analysis. In that matrix, haramiyidans group with multituberculates, while other matrices and older tooth-only fossils leave the broader placement of Haramiyida open to revision.

## A time envelope combining teeth and skeletons {#s-bf0a61b247}
The displayed 205.7–157.3 million-year envelope combines the contested tooth record with Jurassic skeletons discussed in the study. It is a source-bounded sample interval, not a stable first and last appearance for the whole group.
