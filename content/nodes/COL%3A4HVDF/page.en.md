## Upright basal branches without a cephalium {#s-e0955c0473}
This Portuguese morphology entry describes an upright cactus that branches only at the base, with four to six ribs and conspicuous central spines. A cephalium is absent. The flower tube is green and straight; the fruit has a deeply set tube scar, and the seed coat is rough. The ecology record includes rock-associated and terrestrial settings.
