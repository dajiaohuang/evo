## The source concept {#s-aa302bdc88}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Centrolene daidaleum* in row Anf0920 under Centrolene, Centrolenidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-f09d6076a6}
For Anf0920, the compilation tags terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-e9482fc4c9}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-7eacae63b9}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 25.5 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
