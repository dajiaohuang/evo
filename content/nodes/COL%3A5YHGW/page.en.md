## Chromolaena campestris {#s-eaed11720d}
## Three-veined leaves and a cylindrical involucre

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes a xylopodium, a woody underground structure, and smooth stems. Alternate, elliptic leaves are smooth on both sides, with three main veins; their margins are described as entire or serrated.

Corymb-like clusters bear nearly stalkless heads with cylindrical involucres. The outer and inner bracts stand upright and are smooth. The corolla lobes have fine short hairs. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth and obconical to oblong, with rough pappus bristles. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; no locality or full range is given.
