## Contrasting concealed-surface markings {#s-77f389e3dd}
The local identification account describes a small frog with whitish or yellowish coloration and pale brown stripes, a yellowish-orange iris and low ridges on the dorsal skin. Axilla, groin and concealed limb surfaces have black-and-white markings, sometimes with yellow or orange. These characters distinguish it within the reported local assemblage; they are not new measured body-size distributions or proof that each color variant represents a separate lineage.

## A supported subgroup in the source phylogeny {#s-8aa0cba35b}
The discussion places atratus in a strongly supported phoxocephalus-group subgroup with percultus, chomskyi, andinogigas and multicolor. That relationship is based on the paper’s concatenated mitochondrial12S/16S and nuclearRAG-1 dataset and sampled taxa. It does not independently settle every current species boundary or the suspected chomskyi/andinogigas problem, and it supplies no dated divergence for atratus.
