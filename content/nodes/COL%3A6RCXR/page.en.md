## Metalasia divergens {#s-365ff76298}
## A low sprawling shrub with five-flowered heads

The regional account describes a sprawling shrublet with white woolly hairs, up to 70 cm tall. Its leaves bend backward, are slightly twisted and measure 2–12 mm; small tufts occur in their axils. Disc-shaped flower heads grow in dense clusters at branch ends, with five flowers per head. Most petal-like bracts are glandular. The account records sandstone slopes as habitat.
