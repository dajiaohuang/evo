## Cyrtanthus fergusoniae: leaves and flower structure {#s-21112fb607}
A 2012 regional account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 35 cm. It records 1–3 linear, thick leaves, often dry at flowering, with ribs minutely papillate on the underside. There are 4–8 spreading, tubular red flowers. Their segments are about half as long as the tube: three upper segments project forward, two lateral segments spread outward, and the lower segment bends downward. The style is subcapitate and arches upward.
