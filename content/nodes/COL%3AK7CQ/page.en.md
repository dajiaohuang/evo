## *Baccharis longiattenuata*: toothed leaves and corymbs of heads {#s-a0522100a3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records nearly stalkless, inversely egg-shaped leaves with toothed margins and pinnate veins. Flower heads are arranged in a corymb, a cluster in which lower stalks are longer so the heads form a flatter top. The field describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered; those are alternative entries, not one combined description. It supplies no substrate category or collection locality.
