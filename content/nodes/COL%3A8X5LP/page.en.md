## Dense tufts and pale spikelets {#s-d8fcbdfd73}
The regional description records *Schoenus graminifolius* as a densely tufted perennial reaching 40 cm, with light-brown spikelets.

## A lower-slope habitat note {#s-59e78ce65c}
The SANBI habitat entry lists lower slopes. It supplies no dated sampling frame or population frequency, so it does not show the extent or prevalence of this habitat across the full range.
