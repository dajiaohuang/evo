## A tree account and a historical range entry {#s-201a5effe2}
The historical Flora of Panama account describes *Himatanthus articulatus* as a tree up to 10 m; its note about whether the plant is hairless is marked uncertain. Leaves are obovate, widest above the middle, up to 25 × 9 cm, hairless, firm but thin and membrane-like, glossy above and dull below. Regularly branched flower clusters grow at shoot tips. The fused-petal tube, formed by the joined lower parts of the petals, is about 18–20 mm long and its lobes 22–25 mm. The follicles, dry fruits that open along one side, are about 20 cm long; the seeds are about 7 cm. A separate field in that same historical regional source lists Panama and northeastern South America. This is not a current global range or a statement that all listed occurrences are native.
