## Cheiloclinium pedunculatum {#s-6ae1b80913}
## Trunk-borne yellow-green flowers; fruit unknown

Cheiloclinium pedunculatum has a climbing growth form and no scale-like cataphylls on young branches. Its compound dichasial flower clusters grow on the trunk and are hairless; the flowers are bell-shaped and yellow to green, with smooth petals. The source records three-part male and female floral structures and more than two ovules per chamber. Fruit shape is unknown.
