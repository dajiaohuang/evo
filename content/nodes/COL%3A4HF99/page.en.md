## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-62156c7c5b}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Physalaemus crombiei Heyer & Wolf, 1989 as a species in the genus Physalaemus, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.

## What the classification can tell us {#s-e7a487aa2f}
The accepted record places this species within Physalaemus, Leptodactylidae and Anura. That classification gives a route to related catalogue entries, but it does not by itself establish the species' full morphology, life history, distribution, evolutionary history or conservation status.

## One compiled estimate of larval heat response {#s-ff82241081}
AmphiTherm row 3106 (record ID 3423; Madalozzo_2018) lists a larval critical thermal maximum (CTmax) of 42.1 °C, the temperature at which the source reports no response to prodding. The recorded acclimation temperature was 20 °C, with a heating or cooling rate of 0.05 °C per minute; the test medium was water, the sex was unknown, and the animals were wild. The source's n_trait is 5, and its uncertainty field is a standard deviation of 0.49 °C. These values describe one selected compiled estimate, not a species-wide tolerance boundary, environmental range or husbandry recommendation. The underlying assay has not been independently reanalysed. Chemical, hormone, infection and pathogen treatment fields are marked NA, meaning unreported rather than confirmed absent. The source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to the current COL26.8 concept.

The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Physalaemus crombiei as having a larval stage and laying eggs in an aquatic setting. The codes do not give larval duration, every larval habitat, or how strongly development depends on standing water.
