## Research overview {#s-f75500ad4d}
The Early Cretaceous fossil Cratolirion bognerianum preserves anatomy from roots to flowers at about 115 Ma. A morphology-matrix analysis places it within crown monocots, but does not assign it to a living family or identify the origin of the whole group.

## What this evidence can establish {#s-7ecc525ffa}
Cratolirion preserves extensive anatomy at a bounded fossil occurrence. Its crown placement is an analytical result, not a date for monocot origins or evidence that it belongs to one modern family.

## From roots to flowers in one fossil {#s-479b18915a}
