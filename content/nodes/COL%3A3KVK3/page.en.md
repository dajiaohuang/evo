## What variation in leaves and flowers does the regional field record? {#s-d7bb8147ae}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes roots as axial or non-axial and branches as semi-cylindrical. Petiolate leaves may be elliptic, narrow-obovate or narrow-lance-shaped to linear; tips range from acuminate to acute-acuminate or rounded-acuminate, and bases from wedge-shaped or acute to slightly tapered or rounded. Margins may be entire with hairs or scalloped. The record lists either five main veins, including basal outer and inner pairs, or three main veins with one or two basal outer pairs. Flowers occur in clusters. The floral cup is oblong; inner calyx lobes are ovate, while outer lobes may be narrow-triangular, nearly absent, or toothed with a small point. Petals are white to lilac, obovate and pointed. Anthers may be purple or white to lilac, lance-shaped, blunt or awl-shaped, with a terminal pore and paired side appendages. The ovary is fused to the floral cup, tubular and recorded as six-chambered with chambers opening along their walls. The fruit is globose or berry-like; seeds are pear-shaped or oblong. These alternatives come from a regional database field and are not assigned to individual specimens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f07cdcfafd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and provides no locality, collection date or sampling-completeness information.
