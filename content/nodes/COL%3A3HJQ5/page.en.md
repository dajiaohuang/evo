## What leaf and flower variation is recorded? {#s-cf188f6a0b}
The field describes Guatteria guianensis leaves ranging from narrow elliptic to broadly elliptic, with a warty surface. The upper side is hairless; the underside has sparse hairs. Flowers may grow in leaf axils or on leafless stems, singly or in clusters of several. Their stalks vary from sparsely to densely hairy. The free sepals are broad and triangular, and the petals are egg-shaped. Seeds are wrinkled.
