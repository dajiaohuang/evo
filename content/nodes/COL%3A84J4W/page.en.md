## Description samples {#s-e65c5ec653}
The 2019 description is anchored by a single adult-male holotype collected at Desa Benteng on Togean Island in 2009. The authors’ diagnosis draws on the island population’s provenance, duet call and 12S mitochondrial sequence evidence.

## Vocalization research {#s-31eb8d1f47}
The paper describes a comparatively simple duet structure: a female call followed by two or three male calls, and reports the Togean form as a monophyletic group in its 12S analysis. The genetic result concerns the sampled sequences, not a complete population-genetic survey.

## Distribution records {#s-bae8ba082b}
The authors proposed occurrence across the Togean Islands except Una Una. Because the available hypodigm was a single specimen, the proposed distribution should be read as a literature-based taxonomic range statement, not a systematic census of every island.

## Evidence limits {#s-8070ecfe11}
The description had one available type specimen; genetic and vocal evidence rely partly on previously published material, and the mapped island distribution is not a census. This page is.
