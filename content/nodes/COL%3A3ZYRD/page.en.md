## Mesembryanthemum hypertrophicum {#s-d8bb3a5938}
## Large pale flowers spread over a prostrate succulent

The Extra Cape flora describes a succulent annual up to 250 mm tall but spreading to about 1 m across. It grows upright or leaning before lying flat when it flowers. Cylindrical leaves reach 70 mm long and 10 mm across; their surface bladder cells are usually inconspicuous.

The flowers are white to pale yellow and can reach 60 mm across, with many petals and stamens and sterile stamens between them. The five-chambered fruit has valve wings that bend backward and fuse in pairs. The habitat record is disturbed places.
