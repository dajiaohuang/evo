## What leaf, flower, and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-3d7f000caa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes furrowed bark and entire-margined leaves ranging from elliptic to lance-shaped, with pointed to tapering tips. Dried leaves are olive green. Flowers are white or greenish, with a cup-shaped receptacle where the flower parts attach. The fruit is a dark purple to black, stalkless drupelet, and the receptacle forms a projection around it.
