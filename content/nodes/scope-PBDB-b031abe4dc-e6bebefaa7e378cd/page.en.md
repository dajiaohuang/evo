## Three connected tubes in one holotype {#s-9d76104828}
The phosphatized holotype is millimetre-scale and preserves three connected tubes; openings were compared with a funnel and an osculum. SEM and tomography document its shape and internal cellular organization, but all of these observations come from one specimen.

## The sponge interpretation has clear limits {#s-4dc7cb19f5}
The authors propose sponge-grade anatomy, but the specimen lacks spicules, a replicated canal system and corroborating fossils. Sessile life and suspension feeding are inferences from that proposed body plan, not directly observed states.

## atlas-profile / readerLimitations / en {#s-6b26438493}
The Weng’an Doushantuo occurrence is one fossil in marine phosphorite. Its depositional setting does not establish living position, and one specimen cannot define a global range or directly reveal feeding or locomotion.
