## ## Fissured bark and a spiny fruit {#s-4d3df22b76}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records fissured bark and lanceolate stipules up to 5 mm wide. The leaflets are described as ranging from nearly hairless to pubescent, and the bracts persist. Flowers are longer than 12 mm and have a bell-shaped calyx. The fruit reaches up to 12 cm long, with spines up to 2.5 cm. The dataset also records the plant as terrestrial; it does not provide a locality or survey coverage in this field.
