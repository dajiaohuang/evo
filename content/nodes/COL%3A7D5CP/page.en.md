## Flat leaves and yellow flowers {#s-b647c55582}
Flora do Brasil records Trimezia brevicaulis with linear, sword-shaped leaves up to 50 cm long and yellow flowers. Flowering stems carry one to three rhipidia (compact flower clusters), each subtended by a single bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower) about 5 cm long; the outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) spread distally.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-32f5dcb404}
The source classifies the species as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant).
