## What is in the dataset {#s-95644a2f5e}
This entry describes a research dataset, not a biological taxon. It combines nine new 454 transcriptomes with existing sequence and morphology partitions, alongside a comparative fossil matrix. Its present-day 0–0 marker identifies the dataset’s living-taxon sample; fossil calibrations and modeled node ages keep their own dates.

The study added nine transcriptomes generated with 454 sequencing and analyzed them with earlier sequence data and morphological partitions. The linked fossil matrix contributes to the comparative analysis, but this route groups research materials rather than naming a natural lineage.

## How to read the time marker {#s-499b57f2a8}
The 0–0 display marks a present-day dataset. It is not the age range of ostracods or of a single evolutionary lineage. Fossil calibrations and estimated tree nodes are separate evidence with their own ages.
