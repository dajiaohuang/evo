## Leaves {#s-47d4c65d04}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes unifoliolate leaves, each with one leaflet. The leaflet is elliptic, oval or oblong-elliptic, hairy on both surfaces, and has an entire margin. Stipules are recorded as absent.

## Flower clusters and color {#s-f8e827cacb}
The inflorescence bracts are described as deciduous and up to 10 mm long. Flower color is recorded as purple, lilac, blue or violet.

## Habitat category {#s-02fefe4194}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
