## What the row bridge does not establish {#wave11-bounded-context}
Wegener et al. (2013) treated *X. mocquardi* as a junior synonym of *X. grandidieri* and described a monotypic genus and family under that treatment; that paper also said the evidence was fragmentary and insufficient for a thorough revision. The captured current Reptile Database species record lists Xenotyphlopidae as the higher taxon, but also cites a 2024 *X. mocquardi* item. The pinned COL26.8 `HY5` record has no member list or external link. This single row does not establish current monotypy, complete membership or historical/current concept identity.
