## Drosanthemum opacum {#s-b5db9113f2}
## Pink flowers rise above blunt, papillate leaves

The Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrub up to 30 cm tall, with decumbent branches that may reach 50 cm. Young branches bear papillae. The leaves are cylindrical, blunt-tipped, and densely papillate. Pink flowers grow on short shoots and reach 22 mm across; the fruits have five chambers. The habitat record is west-facing shale slopes.
