## *Restio cedarbergensis*: finer stems than *R. ocreatus* {#s-a47c6b3e82}
The species account describes a tufted plant 0.3–0.6 m tall. Compared with *R. ocreatus*, it has finer stems and smaller spikelets and forms a lower, more tangled plant; *R. ocreatus* is described as stouter, with larger spikelets. A spikelet is a compact flower cluster.

Collections and field observations cited in the account place it on well-drained, often rocky soils, including shale bands and dry soils where sandstone meets shale. The authors suggest it favors hot, dry sites and report that plants appear to be killed by fire and regenerate from seed; these are qualified observations, not a measured fire-response rate.
