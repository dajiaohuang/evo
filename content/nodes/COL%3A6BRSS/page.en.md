## What leaf, flower and seed details are in the regional field? {#s-4c5ee00876}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves are oblong or elliptic, with tapered to acute bases and flat margins. The inflorescence is leafy. Flower stalks fall into two recorded groups, 0.1–6 mm or 7–20 mm; bracteoles are present. The 13–30 mm floral tube is densely hairy, and its blunt spur points horizontally. Red petals persist into the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube; no vesicle is recorded, and the ovary-base nectar gland bends downward. The fruit is tubular, while the seed margin is listed as unknown. These are regional database entries, not a complete global description.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-7b67457c14}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial; it gives no locality or collection date.
