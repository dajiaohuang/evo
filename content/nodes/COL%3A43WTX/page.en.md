## Opposite leaves and a salver-shaped corolla {#s-f3892482d2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes white latex and glabrous branches. Leaves are opposite, and their venation is recorded as brochidodromous. The inflorescence is a terminal, corymb-like cyme. The source describes a white, salver-shaped corolla with acute lobes, equal-sized sepals, a fusiform style head with a thickened basal ring, and a bilocular ovary. It also records a follicular fruit and naked seeds. For anther and ovary characters, the field lists alternatives without explaining their relationship. This is one regional dataset description rather than a full account of the species.
