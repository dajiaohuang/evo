## Hooked closing bodies on a five-chambered fruit {#s-ac70b32a3c}
This shrub reaches about 300 mm. Its three-sided leaves are smooth and lack raised markings. Most cymes carry three flowers, with magenta petals about 1 mm wide and numerous filament-like staminodes that are paler at the base. The fruit chambers separate at maturity; the long funnel-shaped base, high rims and hooked closing body are also recorded. Mudstone soils are listed.
