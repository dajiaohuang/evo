## *Baccharis uleana*: sessile leaves and panicles {#s-187618410f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records leaves without stalks, with three main veins, entire margins, and lance-shaped or egg-shaped blades. Flower heads are arranged in panicles. It records heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered. The substrate fields include both aquatic and terrestrial categories; they provide no collection locality, so they do not define a complete habitat or range.
