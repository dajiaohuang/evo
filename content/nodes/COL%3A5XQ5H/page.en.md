## ## Blister-like hairs and axillary flowers {#s-f3d30573ac}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 2–5 pairs of oval to oval-lanceolate leaflets. Blister-like glandular hairs occur on the stem, leaves, inflorescence and outer sepal surface. Axillary flowers form leafy inflorescences; the yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. The pod valves twist at maturity, and the seed is depressed-obovoid. The xylopodium is listed as unknown; the dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
