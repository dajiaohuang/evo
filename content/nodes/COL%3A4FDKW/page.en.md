## What traits are recorded for Petrea brevicalyx? {#s-a419d3eb19}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records opposite leaves with a net-like vein pattern underneath. Terminal flower clusters have one bract per flower. The flowers are lilac, and the calyx is fused for more than half its length.

## What broad habitat is given? {#s-af6ed8cf4c}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
