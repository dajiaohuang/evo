## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Moronobea ptaritepuiana? {#s-087effaec6}
The leaves are spatulate or obovate, with rounded tips, stalks longer than 3 mm, and visible dots on the underside. Petals are white or greenish yellow. Flowers have three or four stamens and sepals longer than 0.5 cm. Fruit surface and size were not observed in the source record.
