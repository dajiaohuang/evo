## An ancient spicule anchors a cautious route {#s-e357b37c17}
The 536–0 million-year Hexactinellida route links basal Cambrian spicules from the Soltanieh material with living glass sponges. Those spicules provide a cautious fossil anchor; their interpretation does not establish an exact first appearance for the crown group.

## The route is not a continuous record {#s-55b8f3b548}
A fossil anchor and living members make a useful navigation path, but the evidence does not show uninterrupted occupancy from the Cambrian to today. The age label is not a definitive crown-Hexactinellida range.
