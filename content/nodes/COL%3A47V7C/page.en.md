## A tall shrub with silvery leaf undersides {#s-495ce432ad}
This record describes a large shrub or small tree reaching 5 m, with dark-green leaf tops and silvery, glaucous undersides. Compared with N. sonderi, it is much taller; compared with N. tenuifolium, it has larger elliptic fruits (5.5–8.5 mm) and broader, more crowded leaflet lobes. It is recorded from stream beds along forest margins.
