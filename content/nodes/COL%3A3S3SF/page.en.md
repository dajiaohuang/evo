## Lampranthus peacockiae {#s-1845869ad6}
## Trailing stems root at nodes and bear fine-papillate leaves

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes this plant as prostrate and trailing, with stems often rooting at the nodes. Its flowers may be pink, magenta or white.

In comparison with L. filicaulis, the leaves are much shorter and more slender, with minute papillae on their surface. The habitat account records clayey hillsides or flats.
