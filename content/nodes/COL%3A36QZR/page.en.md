## ## Pink, dotted calyces and fleshy fruit {#s-e06c2c3a3d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaves with 4–8 leaflets. The calyx is membranous, pink and 6–15 mm long, with a warty surface. The fruit is ovoid to oblong, with a fleshy pericarp—the fruit wall. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial; this regional character list does not specify population coverage.
