The studied material was first partially collected in March of 1940 and in between April and May of 1941 by Rodolfo Maldonado Bruzzone who was the chief of the terrain subcommittee of the National (Argentinean) Committee for the Measurement of the Meridian Arch (Comisi on para la Medici on de un Arco de Meridiano) directed by Frenguelli (1939).
