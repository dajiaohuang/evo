## Anacardium microsepalum {#s-ba5aff4163}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 description records leaf, flower and fruiting details for this species and classifies its growth setting as terrestrial.

## Small floral measurements and an incomplete fruit field {#s-11a4565b5e}
The source describes an aerial trunk and petiolate leaves with obtuse or wedge-shaped bases and leathery or papery texture. Proximal bracts are greenish cream. Sepals are recorded as under 3 mm and the staminal tube under 2 mm; the corolla is bell-shaped and the petals rolled. The smaller anther thecae are marked absent. The fruit-form field is also marked absent, so it does not show whether fruit is absent; the fruiting pedicel is recorded as not enlarging. The ecology entry is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or range.
