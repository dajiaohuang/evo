## The deposited caecilian record {#s-c210986b63}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis orthoplicatus in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-9c9f9c5357}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-811ff303aa}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” Its accompanying species note lists evergreen forests, rubber and tea plantations, paddy fields, rural gardens and farms, wetlands, and cattle pastureland. These source descriptions do not show how often each setting is used or define a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-858d263291}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-ab74dbf144}
At species level, the compilation gives a dorsal annulus (body-ring) count of 291. This value may summarize records rather than describe one animal; it does not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic count.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-6c9a996b9d}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
