## Evidence limits {#s-96d74da37c}
This account brings together historical AVONET v7, life-history and EltonTraits 1.0 records. AVONET means summarize the listed measurement samples, not identification diagnoses or individual ranges; mass is separately sourced and is not covered by morphology sample counts. Habitat, trophic niche, lifestyle and migration are coarse categories, while the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. The life-history archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity is the largest collected record. These are not direct measurements of one individual, individual ranges, average wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies; matching names, family and sparse rows support an association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently confirmed. EltonTraits food and stratum percentages are standardized from literature descriptions rather than direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty; mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may refer to a congener considered ecologically similar. The underlying books and papers have not been independently reviewed, so the records do not establish direct observation of this species for every field. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records requiring individual review cannot resolve those limits. Individual, seasonal and local variation, diet and behavior beyond these categories, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
