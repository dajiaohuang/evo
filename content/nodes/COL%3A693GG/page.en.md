## Bredemeyera martiana {#s-c213f0a81c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Bredemeyera martiana* as an accepted species of *Bredemeyera*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes velvety branches and leaf surfaces, racemes arranged in panicles, and a two-valved capsule. Its habitat field says terrestrial. The source is a regional species description; it does not assess the full range of variation.
