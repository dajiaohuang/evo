## Which details mark its leaves and flower stalk? {#s-03ff91addb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records *Xyris glochidiata* with spiral or nearly two-ranked leaves. Their reddish-brown sheaths widen at the base, sometimes into ear-like lobes; the blades are nearly cylindrical and cross-wrinkled. Its 20–50 cm flower stalk has more than two ridges, and the obovoid spike carries 10–25 flowers. Several leaf and bract (a leaf-like structure around a flower) states are listed as alternatives.
