## Felicia josephinae {#s-38ff4b4192}
## Broad cream rays around a purple disc

The SANBI description records a branched annual to 15 cm tall, with solitary radiate heads on long stalks. The broad ray florets are white to cream and lack pappus bristles; the central disc is deep purple. A taxonomic account contrasts these broader rays and shorter stalks with F. heterophylla. Another source describes the heads as purple-blue with cream-coloured rays, so the colour wording differs between the records.
