## Syagrus × matafome {#s-9dc41aafcc}
The entry for Syagrus × matafome describes a solitary aerial stem and pinnate leaves with stiff, grouped leaflets set at differing angles. Its hanging branched inflorescence and exposed fruiting structure sit between the leaves. Fusiform to ellipsoid fruit has a green to yellow-green skin and fibrous, juicy mesocarp. No parentage or locality is given.
