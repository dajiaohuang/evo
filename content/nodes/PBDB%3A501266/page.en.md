Archived PBDB field scm (source-language text; not translated or interpreted):
Based on the regional stratigraphy and palynological assemblages, the Ebelsberg Formation has been considered as Upper Oligocene (Hochuli, 1978, Roetzel, 1983, Harzhauser and Mandic, 2002). Recent investigations of the herein described section near Pucking, however, proved an Aquitanian age (zone lower NN2) based on a nannoplankton assemblage with Reticulofenestra minuta, R. pseudoumbilica and R. haqii (Gregorova et al., 2009). The Ebelsberg Formation has been dated as Aquitanian based onthepresence of the nannoplankton taxa Helicosphaera scissura and Helicosphaera sellii and the absence of Helicosphaera ampliaperta (lower nannoplankton Zone NN2, Martini, 1971; ca. 22.5-20.44 Ma, Gregorova et al., 2009; Raffi et al., 2020). This dating is corroborated by the sympatric presence of the dinoflagellates Deflandrea phosphoritica and Deflandrea apenninicum, both of which are indicative of the earliest Miocene (23.04-22 Ma; Grunert et al., 2010; Raffi et al., 2020). Accordingly, the occurrence overlap of age-diagnostic nannoplankton and dinoflagellate taxa suggests an absolute age of between 22.5 and 22 Ma. According to the regional stage system for the Central Paratethys, the deposits are part of the upper Egerian stage (Piller et al., 2007). Early papers described the locality as the "Älteren Schlier," but Wagner (1996) subdivided the "Älteren Schlier" into four formations: the Zupfing Formation (Kiscellium), Puchkirchen Formation (Egerian), Eferding Formation (Egerian) and Ebelsberg Formation (Egerian).
