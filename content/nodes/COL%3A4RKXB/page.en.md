## Rapistrum rugosum {#s-4fc712e9bd}
## Pale-yellow flowers top a tall annual

Herman and Retief’s 1997 South African flora guide describes an annual herb up to 0.8 m tall, with pale-yellow flowers. The upper segment of the fruit is globose to ovoid and ends in a beak formed by the persistent style. A separate South African account says local plants approach most closely to subsp. orientale and subsp. rugosum, and also records pale-yellow flowers. The habitat entry is grassland.
