## Catalogue name and classification {#s-7de2f6f578}
COL26.8 lists Barbourula busuangensis Taylor & Noble, 1924 as an accepted species in the order Anura, family Bombinatoridae and genus Barbourula. This name and placement describe the release published on 20 August 2026; other records need separate identity evidence.

## How to read this record {#s-0caba3ed36}
The genus, family and order pages provide routes to related catalogue entries. The linked AmphiBIO row follows the Frost 2011 concept and uses Bombinatorina as its family; it has not been matched to the current COL26.8 concept.

## What the AmphiBIO row reports {#s-fd80a29b61}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0258 follows Frost 2011 and lists the family as Bombinatorina, unlike the current COL26.8 family Bombinatoridae; the source concepts have not been matched. The row flags fossorial (burrowing) as a broad foraging category and arthropods as an adult-food category. Its literature compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 85.3 mm (snout–vent length in frogs), minimum and maximum clutch counts of 78 and 78 offspring or eggs, minimum and maximum offspring-or-egg sizes of 5.9 and 5.9 mm, and a maximum reproductive output of one event per year. These values are not a full diet or a schedule for every population; the row does not identify sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions. A later TetrapodTraits revision covers some microhabitat fields but does not establish whether this row was affected.
