## Spotting, membranes and live coloration {#s-4d3a38155b}
The diagnosis gives greyish-to-olive green dorsal skin with yellow spots, sometimes surrounded by poorly defined black rings called false ocelli. Live bones are green; the iris is light grey with thin dark reticulations and pale yellow around the pupil. Males lack a projecting humeral spine and have dorsal microspicules; the reported female lacks them. The white parietal peritoneum covers roughly one-third to one-half, the pericardium is white and other visceral peritonea are clear. These distinguish the source’s character combination, not an independently tested pigment mechanism.
