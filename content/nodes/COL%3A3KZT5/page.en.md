## Grey-felted leaves and maroon flowers {#s-7b48d45d52}
The flora description records an evergreen perennial forming a tuft up to 60 cm high. Its long-stalked, grey-felted leaves sit in basal clusters; the crowded flower heads grow on woolly stalks, with maroon sepals. It is distinguished from H. lanata in the record by its less rigid leaves and usually truncate rather than strongly heart-shaped bases. The habitat is high, rocky sandstone slopes.
