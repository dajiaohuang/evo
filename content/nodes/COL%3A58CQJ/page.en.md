## Aromatic leaves with velvety undersides {#s-9faa8d2150}
The morphology record describes sticky, aromatic stems with glandular hairs. Its stalked leaves are lance-shaped to triangular, with toothed margins, hairless upper surfaces and velvety undersides. Loose corymb-like clusters carry 18–25 florets per head; the dry fruits have glandular hairs and a 3–4 mm bristly tuft.
