## Limits of the 2015 archive {#s-866ffee54b}
This page summarizes an August 2015 compilation, not a complete species account or a set of measurements from one individual. Values are medians or averages of the two middle records, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. Data may be shared or inherited across historical species and subspecies; same-name rows do not prove equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the source papers have not been individually reviewed.
