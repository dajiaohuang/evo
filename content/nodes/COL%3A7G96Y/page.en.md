## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2fe7f6d4a3}
COL26.8 records Xenochrophis cerasogaster as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-b869dbf767}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 667.5 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Shine et al. 2006a. The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 970 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016. This row records TL and SVL in separate fields; the captured definitions provide no conversion between them.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-16887cce08}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1dd0d128f3}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 30. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Slavens and Slavens, 1999, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-8d001f1629}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
