## Neomirandea gracilis {#s-82e6581de2}
## A small epiphytic shrub with lavender heads

The supplied description portrays *Neomirandea gracilis* as a small shrub growing on other plants, up to 1 m tall. Its round stems have fine hairs that disappear lower down. Opposite, papery leaves are oblong-elliptic, mostly 4–8 by 1.5–3 cm, with short wedge-shaped bases and widely spaced fine teeth. The upper surface has sparse dots; the lower is finely hairy and densely dotted with glands. Short petioles measure 1–2 mm.

Small corymb-like panicles are about 4.5 cm wide and 3.5 cm high. Heads are about 6 mm high, 1.5 mm wide and contain 8–9 florets. Roughly 12 narrow bracts form two loose rows. Lavender, funnel-shaped corollas are about 4 mm long, hairy and glandular outside. The dry fruits are about 1.7 mm long, finely hairy and glandular; their pappus has about 32–35 rough bristles with pointed tips. This description contains no locality or habitat range beyond the epiphytic habit.
