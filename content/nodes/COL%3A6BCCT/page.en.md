## The name in COL26.8 {#s-240b1b7733}
COL26.8 accepts *Craugastor crassidigitus* (Taylor, 1952) as a species in its 20 August 2026 release. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Craugastoridae (family) → Craugastor (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## How to read this profile {#s-ef0e02fb07}
This page brings together a historical AmphiBIO trait record and one AmphiTherm thermal estimate. Each belongs to its own source concept; neither name match alone establishes that it describes the current COL26.8 concept.

## Two historical foraging categories {#s-e27b89f89a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1253 follows the compilation's Frost 2011 species concept and marks Ter=1 (terrestrial) and Aqu=1 (aquatic) as broad foraging categories. It does not specify season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries but does not say whether this row was affected. Keep the two flags as historical categories; no replacement values are supplied, and the revision notice alone does not show that this row is wrong.

## AmphiBIO's night-activity category {#s-b580faebb3}
The same Frost 2011 source concept is marked Noc=1 for activity at night. This is a broad time-of-day category, not a continuous record of behavior, and it has not been reconciled with the current COL26.8 concept.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-f9bec19352}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 48 mm under its Frost 2011 source concept. For Anura, this field means snout–vent length. It is a literature-derived maximum, not a measurement of every individual, and the historical concept has not been shown equivalent to the current COL26.8 record.

## Clutch and annual-output values in AmphiBIO {#s-6b38227ee8}
The same historical compilation lists 36 as the minimum and 45 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, and 1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. These are literature-compiled limits and counts; the row does not tie each value to a sex, population, date or wild/captive setting. Its offspring-size fields may refer to eggs.

## A developmental category, not a breeding account {#s-f7dd5b0b10}
AmphiBIO records Dir=1, or direct development, for the Frost 2011 source concept. This category does not establish parental care, breeding location or season; equivalence with the current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## One compiled critical thermal maximum estimate {#s-f50640a9f6}
AmphiTherm row 1671 (ID 1854; reference label `Frishkoff_et_al_2015`) records an adult critical thermal maximum (`CTmax`) of 35.562 °C, using loss of righting response (`LRR`) as the endpoint. This is one estimate row: `n_trait` is 11 and `error_trait` is 0.229 °C (`se`, standard error). It is not a species-wide tolerance boundary, and sample sizes should not be added across repeated cohorts. The underlying study has not been independently reanalysed.

The row describes wild adults tested in water; sex is unknown. Acclimation is recorded for adults at 22.65 °C, with a companion temperature field of 1.95 °C. An attached note says 20.7–24.6 °C was represented as 22.65 ±1.95 °C. The dataset stores `acclimation_time` = 6.5; its note says this is the mean of a 4–9 day range, although the field itself has no unit. Testing started at 30 °C and ramped at 0.2 °C/min. The row labels the population `Craugastor_crassidigitus_pop1` and cohort `Craugastor_crassidigitus_pop1_UTL_co1`.

Its location fields give 8.784597744, −82.95828595 and 1,209 m; the sampling window is April–May 2013. A note says the coordinates were estimated using Google Maps, so treat them as dataset location metadata rather than a precise site record. Chemical, hormone, infection and pathogen fields are NA: the deposit has no value for them, which does not prove that treatments were absent. The estimate's source concept has not been matched to the current COL26.8 species concept.
