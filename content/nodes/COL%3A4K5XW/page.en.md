## Distribution records {#s-3c23b3ef2e}
Fieldwork recorded a Lake Baptista titi within the range traditionally attributed to Hoffmann's titi. The paper notes that the forms are poorly studied and had not been included together in a molecular phylogenetic analysis. It raises continuous pelage variation, rather than a sharp interspecific boundary, as an explanation to test—not as a taxonomic revision established by this study.

## Evidence limits {#s-0a46beaf4a}
One record does not delimit the full range; pelage continuity and species boundaries require more specimens, samples, and phylogenetic evidence.
