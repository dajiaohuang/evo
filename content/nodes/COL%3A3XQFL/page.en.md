## Malouetia sessilis {#s-e8a7a73c81}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Malouetia sessilis* as an accepted species of *Malouetia*. In the Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record, the sole ecological entry is “terrestrial.” It is a habitat category rather than a locality or range, and no morphology is included in this source record.
