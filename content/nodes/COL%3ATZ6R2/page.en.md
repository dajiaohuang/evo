## Accepted name and placement {#s-309b756f29}
COL26.8 accepts Boophis archeri Vences, Köhler, Hutter, Preick, Petzold, Rakotoarison, Ratsoavina, Glaw & Scherz, 2024 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Mantellidae (family) → Boophis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Eight lineages in a treefrog revision {#s-8d48d5def6}
Boophis archeri was named in an integrative revision of the B. marojezensis complex. The authors used DNA from the name-bearing type together with mitochondrial and nuclear sequences, morphology and calls to resolve eight species-level lineages, formally describing seven new species. The paper's complex-wide conclusions should not be read as a complete range account for B. archeri.
