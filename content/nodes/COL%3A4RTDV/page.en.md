## *Restio distans*: a runner-forming plant on high ledges {#s-0e471938ac}
At up to 30 cm, this perennial spreads by stolons, or horizontal runners, and has much-branched stems that are smooth or finely wrinkled. The account records four to eight spikelets, each no more than 5 mm long.

Its documented sites are slopes and ledges above 900 m.
