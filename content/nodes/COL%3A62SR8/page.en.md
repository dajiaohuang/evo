## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-68361aff63}
COL26.8 records Forpus at genus rank, with Psittacidae as its direct parent.

AVONET v7 measurements are represented for six Forpus species under historical source names: F. coelestis, F. conspicillatus, F. cyanopygius, F. modestus and F. passerinus are examples. The page hierarchy follows pinned COL26.8, while measurements retain the source names and sampling scope.

## Differences between records {#s-c29e65c539}
Among the represented records, compiled mean mass ranges from 23 g in Forpus passerinus to 35 g in F. xanthops. The data include Shrubland, Woodland and Forest habitat categories, and Omnivore, Frugivore and Granivore niches. These are examples from this set, not shared genus traits or a complete descendant list.

## Amniote Life-history archive member records {#s-d07fffda70}
Five historical life-history accounts are represented: Forpus coelestis, F. cyanopygius, F. passerinus, F. xanthops and F. xanthopterygius. All five include adult mass, offspring-per-litter or eggs-per-clutch, and maximum recorded lifespan; female maturity (defined by the source as age at first conception) appears in two. These counts describe the available accounts, not the full accepted-species total. Check each field’s event, unit and source before comparison.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-95b2c98181}
The measurement set summarized here includes six species, not every accepted Forpus. Mass endpoints compare compiled species means rather than the lightest and heaviest individuals; species pages retain their taxonomic, sampling and biological limits.
Five historical same-name, same-family life-history accounts have at least three source-supported fields. Compilation summaries and historical taxonomic inheritance retain their source limits; this route does not imply a shared reproductive mode or life history.
