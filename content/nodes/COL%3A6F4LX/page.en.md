## Accepted name and placement {#s-087902950d}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts *Eleutherodactylus armstrongi* Noble & Hassler, 1933 as a species in Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family) and *Eleutherodactylus* (genus). This is the name, rank and placement recorded in the pinned catalogue.

## Two source records, distinct concepts {#s-2cfdf76d8a}
Alongside the current catalogue entry, this page reports an AmphiBIO v1 row under the historical Frost 2011 source concept and one morphology v3 measurement row. The shared name does not establish that either deposited concept is equivalent to COL26.8; each trait below remains attached to its own source.

## Land and vegetation foraging categories {#s-da6c9fe28a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1824 lists terrestrial and arboreal foraging for its Frost 2011 *Eleutherodactylus armstrongi* concept. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations, but this row has not been checked field by field. Keep the archived categories provisional; no replacement is inferred, and the revision notice does not show that this row is wrong.

## Maximum adult length in AmphiBIO {#s-76225063f2}
The same AmphiBIO row gives a maximum adult body length of 43 mm. For Anura, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length (SVL), measured from the snout tip to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every individual; its Frost 2011 concept remains unmatched to COL26.8.

## Clutch counts in the literature compilation {#s-d2669b04b9}
AmphiBIO lists 31–49 offspring or eggs per clutch and up to 1 reproductive event per year for its source concept. These are literature-compiled counts and extrema. The row does not tie each value to a sex, population, date or wild versus captive setting. No offspring-size value is listed for this row; more generally, AmphiBIO offspring-size fields may refer to young or eggs.

## A direct-development label {#s-99b89dce51}
AmphiBIO assigns direct development to its Frost 2011 source concept. This category does not by itself describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing, and the historical concept has not been matched to COL26.8.

## What the morphology row contains {#s-d3c0c68fcd}
The morphology v3 archive links one measurement row, Anura.csv row 1649, to this name; its citation is listed at Reference.csv row 2214. The source fields place it in Brachycephaloidea, mark sex as F (female category), and give three values: SVL 35, HW 12 and HLL 51. The row does not confirm units or identify a unique specimen. Treat these as deposited fields, not a comparable size range or a diagnostic measurement. Its classification and concept remain separate from COL26.8 unless independently reconciled.
