## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-803420eb1e}
COL26.8 records Dasypeltis arabica as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b94a8c41b9}
The row reports 2 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015.
