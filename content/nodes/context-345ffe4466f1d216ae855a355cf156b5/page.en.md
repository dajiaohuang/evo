## Anf1975 and the recorded concept {#s-ca8bd2cb79}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf1975 names Eleutherodactylus teretistes and records Eleutherodactylus, Eleutherodactylidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-f9d28e6a15}
For Eleutherodactylus teretistes, row Anf1975 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.
