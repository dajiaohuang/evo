## Form in the flora record {#s-e4358ea98d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a regular tuber. Petioles are variegated; the leaf blade is three-parted, sagittate, or lobed, with irregular lobes.

The emergent peduncle is 30–250 cm long and striped or spotted. The spathe is not differentiated into a tube and blade, is longer than 20 cm, and has a translucent basal region. The spadix is mostly hidden by the spathe and only partly exposed; it is 2–6 cm long and lacks an apical appendix. Flowers have 4, 5, or 6 tepals and 7 or more stamens. The berry color is unknown. The seeds are kidney-shaped, with one seed recorded.

The regional dataset row cites Calazans and Soares' 2020 Dracontium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
