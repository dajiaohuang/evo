## Oxypetalum arachnoideum {#s-a489cb8333}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum arachnoideum* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. The available Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records terrestrial habitat only. It supplies no collection locality, range, or morphology description, so the label is a limited regional record.
