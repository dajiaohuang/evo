## Which record these facts describe {#s-90108b9ee8}
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts Liolaemus robustus in the family Liolaemidae. The traits below come from its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which follows the Reptile Database's 2023 name framework and cites sources by trait. That row describes the compilation, not every population as if it were identical.

## What the length number measures {#s-a829173cd3}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 85 mm. SVL measures from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. This compiled maximum is neither a typical size nor a biological ceiling. No sex-specific maximum is interpreted here, so the record cannot show how lengths differ between sexes.

## What the reproductive record means {#s-5bc32786ba}
The source labels the reproductive mode viviparous, or live-bearing, and gives a consolidated litter-or-clutch mean of 3. ReptTraits may calculate a summary from the midpoint of published means, or use the midpoint of reported extremes when means are missing; 3 is not necessarily an arithmetic average of directly counted litters. This row gives no low or high values for individual litters. The mode and mean do not show how litter size varies among populations.

## Other traits not described by this row {#s-5070cb3fa9}
The row supports three details here: a maximum SVL, a live-bearing label, and a compiled litter-or-clutch mean. It supplies no interpreted values for sex-specific or total length, low or high clutch size, annual litter count, egg dimensions, microhabitat, diet, activity, foraging, or sex determination. These gaps prevent this record alone from describing the species' full body or ecology; relatives and blank fields cannot fill them.

## Limits of this account {#s-f61c10ec97}
This page paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and keeps its 2023 name framework and trait-specific citations. A single row compresses differences in sex, age, locality, and population; the same name and family do not prove that a historical record matches every later taxonomic concept. The reported maximum is not an absolute size limit, and the compiled mean is not a fixed litter count. ReptTraits also includes mass estimates derived from length equations; they are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions, so they are not used as conclusions. This page does not update conservation status, venom, current distribution, or population trends. The citation labels retain the dataset's source chain; they do not mean this project independently rechecked every original study.
