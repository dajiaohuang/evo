## Anf1263 and the recorded concept {#s-ae387281d8}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1263 is the entry for Craugastor evanesco, placed by the source in Craugastor (Craugastoridae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-6b779eeb89}
Its maximum adult body length is 64.3 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
