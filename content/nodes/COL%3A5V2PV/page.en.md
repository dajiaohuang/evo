## Hairy leaves, flowers, and follicles {#s-af396141e9}
The Flora of Panama description presents *Anechites nerium* as a slender liana with sparsely hairy stems. The leaf outline is marked uncertain in the source: it is possibly elliptic, reaches 12 × 6 cm, and has an acute to acuminate tip and an obtuse to rounded base. The leaf surface is papery and hairy; hairs on top are stiff and have multicellular bases, while the underside is softly hairy at least along the midrib. Axillary racemes carry a few to several flowers on peduncles about 9–12 cm long. The corolla is sparsely and softly hairy outside, with a tube about 4 mm long and obliquely obovate lobes about 6–7 mm long. The fruit is a narrow, cylindrical follicle described as one-seeded by abortion, ending in a tapering sterile beak with rust-coloured bristly hairs.

## Regional range in the Panama flora {#s-4bcb715874}
The Flora of Panama distribution field lists parts of the West Indies, Panama, Colombia, and Ecuador. These are broad regional labels; the field does not give collection localities within them.
