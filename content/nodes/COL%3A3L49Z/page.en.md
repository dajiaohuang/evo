## Hairy leaves and two flower-opening patterns {#s-e401a8ab0f}
Hesperantha pilosa is 10–30 cm tall and has hairy leaves. The cited account distinguishes two subspecies: subsp. pilosa has mostly white flowers that open around 18:00, while subsp. bracteolata has blue or deep-pink flowers that open in the morning and usually close in early afternoon.

## Sandstone and clay slopes {#s-45d1bc12d9}
The regional account records sandstone and clay slopes; it does not give a complete distribution map.
