## Name and classification in AmphiBIO {#s-db1bf295c9}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Kaloula aureata as row Anf4264. The compilation assigns it to the genus Kaloula, family Microhylidae, and order Anura, using Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. These traits describe the deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Arthropods in adult food records {#s-9193f913ea}
The row lists arthropods among its adult food categories (Arthro = 1). The compilation groups diet categories from literature reports based on expert judgment, observation, or stomach contents. A listed category is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Maximum adult body length: 65 mm {#s-ba2c99aca8}
The compilation lists a maximum adult body length of 65 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Larval stage recorded {#s-ff3d656b19}
The row marks a larval stage (Lar = 1). This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
