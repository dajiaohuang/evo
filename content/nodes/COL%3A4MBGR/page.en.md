## Prestonia lagoensis {#s-a2c905b849}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Prestonia lagoensis* as an accepted species of *Prestonia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes thin, membranous leaves and glabrous, axillary inflorescences arranged as one-sided cymes, in which each branch ends in a flower. The follicles are recorded as fully joined, and the seeds as truncate; seed hairs are unspecified. The habitat field says terrestrial, within a regional dataset record.
