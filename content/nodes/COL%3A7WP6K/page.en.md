## Following the older genus name {#s-c4a85e0506}
Pinned COL26.8 places this page at Psilopogon nuchalis (7WP6K). AVONET v7 BirdLife 2020 row 9699 and eBird 2021 row 8666 use that name and Avibase ID AVIBASE-244D5336. BirdTree row 8621 lists Megalaima nuchalis, with match type 1BL to 1BT. The older combination was not found in the related records in the read meg/psi shards. This source crosswalk links historical data; it does not create a catalogue synonym or establish unchanged species boundaries across dates.

## Morphology samples and averages {#s-963dc5deaf}
Five birds contribute morphology: 2 female, 3 male and 0 unsexed; 4 have complete measurement sets. Mean nostril-to-tip beak length is 17.6 mm, beak width 8.4 mm, tarsus 25.8 mm, unflattened wing 100 mm and hand-wing index 17.8. Inference=NO does not mean every field has the same sample count. The index is Kipp’s distance divided by wing length ×100, not flight speed; beak width does not test fruit swallowing or pecking performance.

## Mass is compiled separately {#s-b93b309e0d}
The reported mass is 87.69 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning. It is a compiled species average, not the mass of the 5 morphology birds. Individual masses and the original book were not read, leaving sex, season and locality-specific minimum–maximum bounds unsupported. [Psilopogon faber](../Psilopogon_faber/page.en.md) also reports 87.69 g; that equality does not show identical mass distributions.

## Fruit niche and trophic level {#s-3ee3388770}
Frugivore is the Metadata category for predominantly fruit resources; Herbivore is a separate trophic-level value. The fields classify different things and are not individual feeding observations. Category thresholds are not measured diet percentages for a particular place or season. Fruit species, food size, digestion and seed-dispersal experiments are absent, so detailed diet and dispersal effects are unsupported.

## Perching, vegetation and migration {#s-ef237f27fd}
Insessorial describes primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, not never descending to the ground. Habitat is Forest. Density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is the sedentary category; it does not establish lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted here, and the categories do not establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.

## Evidence limits {#s-28853b3835}
This account draws on selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two name crosswalks, Metadata definitions and local COL name shards. Morphological means and compiled mass use separate denominators. One-to-one links and catalogue synonyms do not prove unchanged concepts across dates. Individual specimens, the entire workbook, Dunning’s book and a new full reading of the methods paper were not examined. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation are unsupported here.
