## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-4eb9e57351}
The field records feeding roots, short stem internodes, and a pale-green prophyll (the protective leaf around a developing shoot). The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous dark-green petiole is slightly channelled on its upper side. The entire blade is listed as sagittate, hastate, ovate, or triangular, with an acute to cuspidate tip and sagittate base. The sequence “3–6 / 1 / 2” and a single basiscopic vein are also recorded, but the structured wording does not clearly map each number to a feature. The axillary sympodial inflorescence has a short green cylindrical peduncle shorter than the slightly constricted spathe. The outer spathe is green with reddish spots; its tube is wine-coloured outside and inside, while the inner blade is green. The spadix is subsessile.

## Flowers and recorded alternatives {#s-45259a135c}
The field describes short stamens and staminodes, an ovoid-to-cylindrical gynoecium, and an ovoid-to-subcylindrical ovary. It lists 5, 6, or 7 locules; 4 or 5 ovules; subbasal placentation; and a globose stigma. Berry and seed characters are marked unknown.
