## Narrow leaves and a very long tube {#s-74dad6a4ae}
Gladiolus dolichosiphon has six or seven narrow leaves and creamy-pink to pale salmon flowers. Their slender tube is 30–55 mm long and exceeds the tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals); this contrasts with G. bilineatus, which has three leaves and a tube 50–70 mm long.
