## Evidence limits {#s-d065adb168}
The route covers records authored in this part, not every accepted Pogoniulus descendant. The mass endpoints compare different species' compiled values and do not describe within-species variation. Each page retains its own historical name, sampling basis and source limits. The single linked life-history account likewise does not establish a shared reproductive pattern across the genus. Historical tables can contain summaries or inherited species/subspecies values; the original studies have not all been reviewed here. Current distribution, biology and status require additional evidence.
