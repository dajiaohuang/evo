## Cyclodictyon varians: three recorded substrate categories {#s-d68a02481b}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records “corticícola,” the bark-associated category, for *Cyclodictyon varians*.

A second entry uses “epixila,” the wood-associated category.

A third lists “rupícola,” the rock-associated category.

These are habitat or substrate labels in a regional flora dataset, not collection locations. The fields provide no survey date or complete range.
