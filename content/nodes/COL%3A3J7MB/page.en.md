## Leaves, flower head, and fruit {#s-7c1c9eb074}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte and gives a 6–30 cm measurement without naming the dimension. It records two to six leaves that are green at flowering and range from spreading to lying flat. They are tongue-shaped, smooth or pubescent, and unmarked. White flowers form a compact head. The four to eight bracts are thin-textured, white, and veined green. The berry is recorded as white, orange, or red.
