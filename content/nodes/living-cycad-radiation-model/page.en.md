## A recent radiation estimate for living cycads {#s-be12e80f0d}
A 2011 fossil-calibrated molecular model placed much of the diversity among sampled living cycads within roughly the previous 12 million years. Later studies that add fossil evidence recover older deep branches and weaken a single, globally synchronous radiation. The estimate concerns sampled living species; it does not date the origin of Cycadales or its first fossil appearance.
