## What leaves and fruit are described? {#s-d2fb370c33}
The taproot supports erect, velvety stems with simple, long, star-shaped or translucent hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. The uniformly colored, symmetrical oblong leaves have subcordate or truncate bases, blunt tips and toothed margins; the upper surface is silky-hairy. Crowded flower clusters grow in the leaf axils. The sepals are pointed and the corolla is pink. The dry fruit splits into six to ten segments (mericarps), without awns; the segments have simple hairs.
