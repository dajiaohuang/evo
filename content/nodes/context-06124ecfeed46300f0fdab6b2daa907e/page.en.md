## The source concept {#s-2a8124bdb7}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Breviceps bagginsi* in row Anf0320 under Breviceps, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-79c5486aef}
For Anf0320, the compilation tags fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-26c34900b9}
The adult food field lists arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; this tag is not a complete diet and gives no share of food items.

## Activity periods {#s-496006e58b}
The row carries a daytime activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Seasonal activity {#s-0a87248d61}
The seasonal field is tagged wet and warm. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
