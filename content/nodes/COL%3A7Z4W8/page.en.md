## *Stigmaphyllon mikanifolium* {#s-cf9136eedc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes smooth branches and petioles 34–60 mm long, with stalked, cup-shaped glands near the petiole tips. Leaf blades are undivided and hairless beneath, with lobed margins bearing sessile glands or thread-like hairs. A solitary umbel has 3–12 flowers. The field also records red marks on the rear or side petals, fringed petal margins, mostly hairless anthers and styles, differences among the styles, and a developed dorsal fruit wing with no lateral areoles. These are selected database characters.
