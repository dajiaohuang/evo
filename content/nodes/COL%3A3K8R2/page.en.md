## *Heliconia marginata*: a hanging flower cluster with spiral bracts {#s-1ccb029f54}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, leaves with stalks have elliptic, lance-shaped or inversely lance-shaped blades; their bases range from tapered to heart-shaped or rounded. The flower cluster hangs down, and its persistent, boat-shaped bracts are arranged in a spiral. The record allows both smooth and hairy bracts, so it does not narrow the plant to one surface texture.

The floral envelope may likewise be glabrous or hairy, while the fruit is an angular drupe. The linked habitat field uses only the broad category “terrestrial” and supplies no collection locality or date. The morphology is a regional Flora description, not a population-wide estimate of variation.
