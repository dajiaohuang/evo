## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-c53ebfa4e1}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Chalcites ruficollis (Salvadori, 1876) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Separate sex counts from complete sets {#s-9186b118e6}
Morphology comprises 1 bird: 1 female, 0 male and 0 unsexed; 1 complete set. Nostril-to-tip beak length 11 mm, tarsus 17.9 mm, unflattened wing 95 mm, tail 62 mm and hand-wing index 38.1 are historical row means. Complete and total counts agree without supplying individual variation bounds. One female cannot establish sex differences or a population distribution. Inference=NO does not replace each field’s sampling denominator. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100; the index is not flight speed, and wing length is not measured flight ability.

## Invertebrate niche and carnivorous trophic level {#s-a30c3f933e}
Trophic.Niche=Invertivore is defined as at least 60% of food resources from invertebrates in terrestrial systems; Trophic.Level=Carnivore means at least 70% from live invertebrate or vertebrate animals. These thresholds define categories rather than measured proportions for this species. Underlying diet papers were not read; insect species, other prey, seasonal diet and capture actions remain unsupported. Carnivore does not mean exclusive vertebrate feeding.

## Separate perching from multiple lifestyles {#s-a424d4a10c}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time perching on branches, vegetation or other raised substrates. Habitat=Forest; density 1 denotes dense vegetation, not bird abundance. Forest does not automatically mean dense vegetation. Migration=1 is sedentary without implying lifelong immobility. Shared food codes do not establish shared lifestyles, exact strata, brood parasitism, breeding or current distributions; historical map fields are uninterpreted here.
