## Differences between records {#s-33f4cc3635}
Across the represented species, compiled mean mass ranges from 23 g in Forpus passerinus to 1331 g in Anodorhynchus hyacinthinus. The records include Shrubland, Forest, Woodland and Grassland habitat categories and Frugivore, Omnivore and Granivore niches. These are examples in the dataset, not shared family traits or a complete list of descendants.

## Evidence limits {#s-459a297285}
The AVONET measurement set summarized here has 114 species; it is not a complete list of accepted Psittacidae. Mass endpoints compare compiled species means, not the lightest and heaviest individuals. Each species page retains its taxonomic, sampling and other biological limits.
The life-history route has 80 historical same-name, same-family accounts with at least three source-supported focal fields. It is not a complete descendant catalogue. Compilation summaries, maximum-lifespan records and historical taxonomic inheritance retain their source limits; a family route does not imply shared reproduction or life history.
The two-field ecology group contains 111 historical entries with food and foraging-stratum data. The one-field group contains 15 entries, 14 with food data and one with stratum data. Neither group is a complete descendant catalogue or evidence of family-wide traits. Semiquantitative conversion, historical taxonomy and possible congeneric borrowing retain their per-page limits.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-157700fd14}
These 13 accounts summarize limited historical ecology records, not every accepted descendant. COL26.8 navigation, same-name same-family association and two one-to-one same-name crosswalks do not remove later differences in species concepts. Coarse categories are not independent behavioral observations, and historical maps are not current occupancy or conservation assessments. Original morphological inferences and mass sources are retained without being interpreted as direct measurements of the species.
