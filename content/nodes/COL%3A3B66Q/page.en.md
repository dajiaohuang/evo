## Single-leaflet leaves and few-flowered racemes {#s-3f096f9c03}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records a woody root with a xylopodium, an erect stem, and unifoliolate leaves (each leaf has one leaflet). The racemes are loose and bear few flowers. Fruit hairiness is listed as either very heavy or slight, without the row resolving which; seed colour is listed as black or brown. These details come from one regional entry, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-d948d7f00e}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
