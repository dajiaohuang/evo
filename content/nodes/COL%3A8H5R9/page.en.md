## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b65d2a2875}
COL26.8 records Sceloporus hondurensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Phrynosomatidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-212a562502}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
