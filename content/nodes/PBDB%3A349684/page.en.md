## A living lineage and a much older stem fossil {#s-2d397c5539}
Phylogenomic analyses recover Medusozoa as a branch among the sampled lineages and test relationships within it. The atlas’s 562–0 million-year route also includes Auroralumina as a cautious, matrix-dependent Ediacaran stem-medusozoan anchor alongside living members.

## Do not read the route as a crown-group range {#s-0cdb3e7324}
Auroralumina is not established here as a direct ancestor or as the first member of crown Medusozoa. The route combines a debated stem-fossil interpretation with living taxa for navigation; it does not claim continuous global occupancy.
