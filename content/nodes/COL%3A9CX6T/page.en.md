## ## A rocky or terrestrial habitat entry {#s-e9c09ead7a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records simple leaves with absent or very short stalks; the blade underside is described as woolly or velvety. Its inflorescence is about the same size as, or slightly larger than, an adjacent leaf, and the calyx is pubescent. The dossier lists both “rupicolous” (rock-associated) and “terrestrial” habitat categories, without attaching either to a locality or survey date. These entries do not define a range.
