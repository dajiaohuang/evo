## One juvenile skeleton in two Messel slabs {#s-dc08af4639}
PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 are the part and counterpart of one juvenile *Darwinius masillae* skeleton from Messel. Together they preserve articulated limbs, hands, the right foot, a complete tail, a soft-tissue outline and gut contents. The individual was juvenile, and fabricated preparation material was disclosed on part of counterplate B; anatomical completeness does not establish a settled tree position or direct ancestry.

## What the holotype does and does not show {#s-5322a0e3a0}
Franzen and colleagues describe the juvenile Messel holotype PMO 214.214 and counterpart WDC-MG-210 as part and counterpart of one skeleton. The gut material preserves dietary evidence but does not establish a complete diet. Preparation concentrated on counterplate B, which limits what is exposed. The specimen does not establish direct ancestry.

## PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 are counterpart slabs {#s-0a8fe8e0ca}
At Messel, PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 represent the two sides of one juvenile skeleton. Together they preserve a near-continuous outline with articulated limbs, hands, feet and tail. The counterpart surfaces help distinguish preserved anatomy from gaps or distortion associated with burial and preparation.

## Gut material belongs to one juvenile {#s-fdbe210b0a}
The description records plant material in the gut region alongside a soft-tissue outline, teeth and limbs. These observations support a limited dietary interpretation for this individual. Because the skeleton is juvenile, it does not directly establish adult size or proportions, and its gut contents are not a species-wide diet.

## Preparation history and phylogenetic placement are separate {#s-e8acd1664d}
The original description discloses fabricated preparation material on part of counterplate B. Researchers have discussed the anatomical combination in relation to adapiform and anthropoid relationships, but this one skeleton cannot settle branching history or demonstrate a human ancestor. Preparation history, preserved anatomy and phylogenetic placement are distinct questions.

## Scope of the Messel holotype account {#s-f2649deddd}
This account follows one juvenile holotype and its original description. It does not provide population-level variation, a direct adult body-size estimate or a settled phylogenetic position. Preparation disclosures and interpretations remain tied to the specimen record.
