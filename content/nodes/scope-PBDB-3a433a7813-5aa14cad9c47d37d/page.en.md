## Two slabs, one juvenile skeleton {#s-0a8fe8e0ca}
At Messel, PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 are the two sides of one juvenile skeleton, not two animals. Together the slabs preserve a continuous outline with limbs, hands, feet and tail. Comparing the counterpart surfaces helps distinguish the animal’s anatomy from gaps and distortions introduced during burial or preparation.
