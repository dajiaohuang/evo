## Forest and pasture encounter records {#s-3ece8fd9a0}
The revision reported western montane forest records in Bolívar, Chimborazo and Cotopaxi at 2258–3200 m, including primary forest, secondary forest and pasture. Nighttime individuals occurred on low vegetation up to 1.6 m high or inside bromeliads up to 3 m high; daytime encounters were only inside bromeliads. Those survey patterns do not establish habitat preference or obligatory bromeliad breeding.

## Night callers in three months {#s-394e472304}
Calling males were found at night on low vegetation in October, December and January. These records document sampled calling activity, without establishing continuous reproduction between the observations, clutch sizes or natural egg sites.
