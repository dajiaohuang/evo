## Hydrocleys mattogrossensis: an aquatic species in a regional flora {#s-06a24e45f6}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists adventitious roots, a globose rhizome and stolons as commonly present. It describes the leaves as floating and petiolate, ovate with acrodromous venation, cordate bases and rounded tips; the petioles are cylindrical and septate, with a basal sheath. The cymose inflorescence is described with a decumbent, floating, septate peduncle and propagule production. Its lanceolate bracts are shorter than the pedicels, longer than wide and acuminate.

The flower entry calls the flowers bisexual, with cylindrical pedicels, green ovate concave sepals lacking prominent veins, and yellow petals. It describes cylindrical, linear-lanceolate carpels that narrow toward the upper part, with a curved style papillose at the tip. The fruit is a globose cluster of follicles; the follicles are lanceolate and membranous, without dorsal striations. Seeds are obovate and glabrescent, with hairs that do not cover the whole surface; glandular hairs are also listed. The source combines an unclear petal-length comparison with mixed stamen and staminode count categories, so those are not resolved here.

These are descriptors in one regional dataset field. It does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, variation, sex or life stage; ambiguous compound terms are left unresolved.

The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat row classifies the species as “Aquática,” or aquatic.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
