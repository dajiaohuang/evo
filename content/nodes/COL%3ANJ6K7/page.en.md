## Larval guild and adult ecomorph are separate source categories {#s-cd5329b64a}
The study codes Cycloramphus carvalhoi as larval END (endotrophic) and adult TER (terrestrial). These categories describe separate life stages. Lentic, lotic and torrential labels summarize habitat associations rather than measured flow tolerance; saxicolous and terrestrial labels do not define a full geographic range. Endotrophic tadpoles still have a larval stage, unlike direct developers. The authors compiled functional assignments from species accounts rather than experimentally measuring each species' feeding, hydrodynamics, locomotion or habitat choice.

## Paired-stage comparisons do not establish complete developmental independence {#s-1cfbcd8f9b}
Across sampled species, larval–adult shape dissimilarities have a weak but significant Mantel association (r = 0.16, p = 0.003), while disparity correlations differ among families. The authors interpret the overall pattern as support for adaptive decoupling; it does not show zero correlation, genetic independence, experimental selection or this species' developmental trajectory. Phylogenetic analyses include 74 of 83 sampled species. Reconstructed ancestral shape and timing patterns are model estimates from extant data, not fossil measurements. Adult K = 0.44 (p = 0.001) indicates less close-relative similarity than the reference expectation. Supplement S3 reports family N values totaling 112 for both stages, so these values do not establish separate adult and larval specimen counts.
