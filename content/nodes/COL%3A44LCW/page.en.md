## Muraltia crassifolia {#s-4d54139feb}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia crassifolia* as an accepted species. Its regional description gives a low subshrub to 15 cm, erect or sprawling, with angled branches that are often gummy. The sessile leaves are oblong to nearly round and have a small terminal point; one or a few pale-pink or cream flowers appear in the leaf axils. The cited account records rocky sandstone slopes as habitat, not a complete range.
