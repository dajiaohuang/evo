## Accepted name in the catalogue {#s-abf172bdd4}
The 20 August 2026 release of Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) accepts Eleutherodactylus fowleri Schwartz, 1973 as a species. Its recorded path is Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family), then Eleutherodactylus (genus). This is the name and placement in that release; another checklist or fossil database needs separate identity evidence.

## Where this record fits {#s-82ff6e9abf}

## Historical foraging categories {#s-4f2134dc65}
AmphiBIO v1, under its Frost 2011 classification, flags terrestrial and arboreal foraging for Eleutherodactylus fowleri. These are broad historical categories, not a description of seasonal or life-stage variation, and their equivalence to the current catalogue concept is unresolved. A later TetrapodTraits revision (version 3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) reports changes to some inherited body-mass and microhabitat entries, including Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu and Arb. The notice does not identify whether this row is affected, so the archived values need row-level review; no replacement is inferred.

## Activity period in the compilation {#s-fdc720a4a6}
AmphiBIO v1 records night activity for its Frost 2011 E. fowleri concept. This is a broad category, not a continuous observation of when individuals are active; the source concept has not been reconciled with the current catalogue record.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-ba964d3b5e}
AmphiBIO v1 reports a maximum adult body length of 33 mm for its Frost 2011 concept of E. fowleri. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs, but total length for salamanders and caecilians. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement for every individual, and its current-concept match is unresolved.

## Clutch and offspring values in the compilation {#s-a79fe9adf2}
For its Frost 2011 E. fowleri concept, AmphiBIO v1 records 12 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 7.5–8.5 mm. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts; the row does not specify sex, population, date or wild/captive conditions for each value. Its size field may refer to an offspring or an egg, and the source concept has not been reconciled with the current catalogue record.

## Developmental strategy in the compilation {#s-91de4a77d9}
AmphiBIO v1 marks direct development for its Frost 2011 E. fowleri concept. That category does not by itself specify parental care, egg-laying site or seasonal timing, and its equivalence to the current catalogue concept remains unresolved.
