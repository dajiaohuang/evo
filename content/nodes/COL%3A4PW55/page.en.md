## The name and this data account {#s-06c44d2a85}
Pterocles orientalis is accepted in COL26.8 within Pteroclidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-c5385bce2d}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 9.9 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 5.7 mm in width and 7 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-fe6e6f9b89}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 228.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 25.7 mm. Compiled mean mass is 404.87 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-fcccfa9fb4}
The dataset assigns habitat Grassland, trophic niche Granivore and lifestyle Terrestrial. These are dominant categories for cross-species comparisons; they do not imply exclusive use of one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-1177f0a05b}
The migration field classifies this record as partially migratory. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints 26.09° to 48.57°. This excludes a complete account of non-breeding movements and is not a current-position or global occupancy estimate. Geography and morphological measurements come from different compilation steps.

## Adult and hatchling mass fields {#s-e25e183382}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 405.5 g; mass at birth or hatching 18.5 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-2e691c8fec}
Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 363.47 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 2.76 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 25.5 d; age at flight capability (leaving the nest in some source material) 25 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. ## Evidence limits {#s-aec4572064}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
