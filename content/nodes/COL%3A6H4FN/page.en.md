## Eryngium leptophyllum {#s-4ff160003c}
## Parallel and netted veins, bladder-like fruit segments

Leaves are recorded as petiolate, with parallel veins through the centre and a netted pattern between them. The inflorescence is upright and the flowers sessile. The fruit is sessile and dehiscent, meaning it opens at maturity; each mericarp, or fruit half, has a vesiculose, bladder-like back. The habitat field labels the species terrestrial.
