## A local cloud-forest occurrence and its elevation {#s-5ad0c59c03}
The 2021 Santa Cruz Tepetotutla checklist reports Craugastor pygmaeus at 1550 m above sea level during its 2018–2019 survey. Table 1 compares this with an earlier reported elevation of 0–2145 (Ahumada-Carrillo 2013) and lists photographic catalogue number IBH-RF 599. The locality is in the Chinantla region of Oaxaca, Mexico. This is a reported local occurrence, not a surveyed global range, independent verification of the archived photograph, or evidence of occupancy at every intermediate elevation. ## Interpret the record within the survey design {#s-1d91c41fe5}
Four observers surveyed pristine and managed cloud-forest sites on eleven five-day trips in 2018–2019, in wet and dry seasons. Three daily windows were 09:00–13:00, 15:00–19:00 and 21:00–01:00; the paper reports 2,640 effective field hours for the whole herpetofaunal inventory. Animals were photographed and released, and GPS locations had a reported 5 m error range. The checklist's Craugastor pygmaeus row establishes presence in that sampling context. Total effort and the nineteen amphibian species are not species-specific detection probabilities, population estimates or trends. The accumulation curve had not reached an asymptote, so a missing record cannot establish local absence.
