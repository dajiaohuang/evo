## *Thamnochortus pulcher*: different branches on fertile stems {#s-2533d288c5}
This tufted perennial reaches 60 cm. Fertile stems are simple, while sterile stems branch from the base. Male spikelets are large, grey-brown and elliptical; female spikelets reach 20 mm and have slender, tapering bracts. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

It is recorded on well-drained stony slopes, usually below 800 m.
