## ## Two leaflet pairs and an unknown petal color {#s-15b8c4ceec}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two pairs of elliptic to elliptic-obovate leaflets and bulbous glandular hairs on the stem, leaves, inflorescences and outer sepal surfaces. The ten-stamen flowers have petals of different forms, but their color is listed as unknown. The oblong pod valves twist at maturity and carry glandular and non-glandular hairs. Both the xylopodium and seed characters are listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
