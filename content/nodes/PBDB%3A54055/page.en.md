## Recorded parent of Prothylacyninae {#s-5502eeee2b}
Prothylacyninae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Borhyaenidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
