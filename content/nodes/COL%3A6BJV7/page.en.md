## What flower and leaf traits distinguish Croton pullei? {#s-20637096ca}
Young branches and both surfaces of the elliptic to oblong leaves have star-shaped scale hairs. The leaves have looping side veins and entire margins; a stalked nectar gland occurs at the base, and leafy stipules are present at young shoot tips. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are stalked; male flowers are shallowly bell-shaped. Female sepals are fused and unequal, the style has many lobes, and petals are reduced to glands. The bud parts have a reduplicate-valvate arrangement. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3e76fdce17}
It is listed as terrestrial, with no named locality.
