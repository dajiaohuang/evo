## Desmoncus polyacanthos {#s-aa2eea8473}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field notes short, recurved spines on the leaf cirri of Desmoncus polyacanthos, including near their base. Fruits are smooth, with a small, irregularly three-lobed corolla and globose to obovoid endocarp. Habitat is listed as terrestrial, without locality or survey details.
