## Zanthoxylum nemorale {#s-9b8e1279ba}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes persistent paripinnate leaves, axillary-to-lateral pyramidal panicles and sessile flowers; the habitat field records terrestrial growth.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-c3b138f47c}
The source describes thin, non-exfoliating periderm on the branchlets. Leaves are persistent and paripinnate (even-pinnate); prickles are absent, leaflet bases are stalked, and domatia are absent. The leaflet margin is crenate or crenulate, and the lower surface is glabrous. Pyramidal, non-corymb-shaped panicles are axillary to lateral; their stalk and axis are neither corky nor reticulate. Flowers have three carpels, rarely two, a disc-shaped stigma and four or five parts; they are recorded as sessile. Fruit segments (mericarps) are separate and bear raised glands, while the seed hilum is linear.
