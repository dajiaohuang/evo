## Accepted name and placement {#s-de04771bcd}
In the frozen COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026, Leptodactylus didymus Heyer, García-Lopez & Cardoso, 1996 is accepted at species rank. Its recorded placement is Anura (order), Leptodactylidae (family) and Leptodactylus (genus). This is the classification in that release; other catalogues require their own evidence for name identity.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-8762baad34}
AmphiBIO reports adult maxima of 16.5 g body mass and 58.6 mm body length; for anurans, body length is snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical adult measurements. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited body-mass entries, and the 16.5 g value has not been compared with that update.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-e30a356a3b}
The compilation lists 192–460 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most one reproductive event a year, and offspring-or-egg size of 2 mm at both minimum and maximum. Its row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild/captive setting for each value; the size field may refer to eggs or offspring.

A larval stage is listed in the source record. This label alone does not specify parental care, breeding site or timing.
