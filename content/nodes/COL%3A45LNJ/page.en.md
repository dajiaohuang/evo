## Nananthus gerstneri {#s-62ec654c38}
## Red-lined yellow petals on a tiny succulent herb

The 2017 Free State flora describes a perennial succulent herb only 2 cm high. Its spreading leaves are broadest at the middle of the blade and measure about 35 by 5–6 mm.

The yellow petals carry red longitudinal lines. Flowering is recorded for December through February. The fruit has six to ten chambers. The habitat account places the species in Dwarf Karroid Shrubland, on dolerite slopes and in fine soil in washes.
