## Freziera carinata A.L.Weitzman {#s-aac156cdda}
## Male and female flower forms on a rock-growing shrub

Freziera carinata has alternate leaves arranged in two ranks, with terminal leaves sometimes folded together. The source describes ribbed veins on the underside and several alternative leaf-hair states. Axillary clusters or short racemes bear male and female flowers on a gynodioecious plant: male flowers have 15 stamens, while female flowers have 15 sterile stamens and a three-chambered ovary. The fruit may be globose or cord-shaped and retains the style. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings.
