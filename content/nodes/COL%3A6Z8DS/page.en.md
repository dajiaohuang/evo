## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-bfdd26edc1}
COL26.8 records Sphaerodactylus armstrongi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Sphaerodactylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e09e4e9fa6}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 31 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-8b31724f1b}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
