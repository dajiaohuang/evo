## What does the record say about Annona deminuta? {#s-da51f388d3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes smooth bark and leaves with entire edges, curved veins, and sharply tapering tips. Yellow flowers grow at branch ends and have six petals; the sepals are slightly joined at the base. The fruit is recorded as a fused aggregate, while seed color and surface are unspecified.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-546529521f}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial; it does not identify a particular site.
