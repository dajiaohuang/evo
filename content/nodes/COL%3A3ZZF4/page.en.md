## Mesembryanthemum vanrensburgii {#s-00f0a7ff4f}
## A single complete plant anchors this detailed description

Bolus’s 1954 account says that only one complete plant had been seen. It describes a richly branched plant with creeping lower stems bearing old leaf remains and flowering branches up to 24 cm long. Leaves spread or ascend; they are opposite, sometimes alternate on flowering growth, and reach 5 cm long. In the account’s warm-chamber observation, flowers opened day and night and expanded to 4 cm over several days.

The flowers have five sepals and several rows of narrow white petals up to 2 cm long. Five slender stigmas reach 5–7 mm. The habitat record is coastal strand with fine shingle.
