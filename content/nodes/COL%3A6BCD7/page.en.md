## Accepted name and placement {#s-408b084d10}
COL26.8 records Craugastor chrysozetetes (McCranie, Savage & Wilson, 1989) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; they do not establish equivalence with historical source concepts.

## Reading route {#s-9dffb42cbf}
Read the catalogue placement first, then treat the following traits as historical AmphiBIO entries. Their Frost 2011 classification is not certified as the same concept used by the current catalogue.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-1134459fc7}
The historical row marks terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. The categories can overlap and do not describe frequency, season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited microhabitat and mass entries, but the affected row has not been verified here.

## Activity periods — AmphiBIO source {#s-7b8308a9c8}
AmphiBIO codes daytime activity for its historical source concept. This is a broad category, not a continuous record of daily or seasonal behaviour.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-106994c53e}
The compilation reports maximum adult body length of 46 mm; for anurans this is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not the range of all individuals.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-3defad0094}
The source lists no more than one reproductive event per year. This literature-derived count is not separated by sex, population or conditions, and offspring-size fields may refer to eggs.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-bdaa8357b1}
Direct development is flagged in the AmphiBIO row. The code alone does not establish parental care, breeding site or timing.
