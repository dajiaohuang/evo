## Branched stems and apple-green leaves {#s-a06e3c9ed5}
Watsonia borbonica is a robust cormous plant 0.5–2 m tall, with bright apple-green leaves and branched stems. Its pink flowers have a funnel-shaped tube 20–40 mm long; filament orientation varies from arched to downward-leaning.

## Rocky slopes across several vegetation zones {#s-2b0eed3a10}
The regional account records mainly rocky sandstone slopes, also granite and clay, at 100–1,500 m. This is the source’s habitat range, not a complete global distribution.
