## Leaves and flowers {#s-551ecdf8b0}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 23 cm, with two leaves that are dry at flowering, spreading, linear, and shallowly channelled. Star-shaped flowers form a dense, spreading cluster. Their crisped tepals are pink with a reddish centre. The stamens are about two-thirds as long as the tepals.
