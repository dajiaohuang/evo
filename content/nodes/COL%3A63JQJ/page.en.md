## Overview {#s-95c3efa30e}
COL26.8 lists five accepted Paragalago species: *P. cocos*, *P. granti*, *P. orinus*, *P. rondoensis* and *P. zanzibaricus*. Masters et al. (2017) proposed Paragalago for the eastern group based on genetic evidence that eastern and western “dwarf galagos” are not one clade; a 2020 molecular study supported three cryptic lineages within the *P. zanzibaricus* complex. Read the local field studies for Kenya and South Africa first, followed by historical surveys in Rondo forest. Then compare the low-elevation Mkungwe record and climate-suitability model for *P. orinus* with the still-qualified Mufindi record, before reading the name and taxonomic boundary around the Tanzania Coast species. Small nocturnal galagos from different places should not be treated as one survey target.

## Classification {#s-a941e890b3}
The genus-level classification and species-level field evidence answer different questions: genetic and comparative evidence explains the recognition of Paragalago, while each species page retains its own sites, identification confidence and observation dates.
