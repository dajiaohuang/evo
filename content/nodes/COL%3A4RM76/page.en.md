## Adult and newborn mass refer to different stages {#s-38f7e75132}
The archive reports adult body mass of 200 g and birth or hatching mass of 4.55 g. Adult values can combine males, females, and records with no sex stated; the newborn value concerns another stage. These are not a longitudinal series for the same animals or a range of individual masses.

## Four timing and reproduction fields {#s-eb9b514e0a}
The compilation gives female maturity, defined by the source as age at first conception, as 102.65 d; offspring-or-egg count as 5.95; conception or fertilization to birth as 22.26 d; and age of dependence on food other than maternal milk as 26.09 d. Here d means days. The combined litter/clutch field is an average, not a fractional event; each time value concerns a different event and they do not form one interchangeable cycle.

## Largest longevity record in the archive {#s-a1d82b0a9d}
maximum_longevity_y is 4.2 years, the largest value found under the source method. It is not typical or mean wild lifespan, a guaranteed ceiling for every individual, or a current record known to recur.

## The ground stratum includes some aquatic foraging {#s-e7217571d9}
EltonTraits assigns G, the ground category; its source definition includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. ## Night activity is a code, not a schedule {#s-a8b248d3d5}
Feeding activity at night is marked 1. Multiple periods can be marked 1, so the values do not form a daily time budget or describe every day, season, and site. Activity confidence is ABC and may rely on a congener; genus/family typical activity does not fill missing species-level evidence.
