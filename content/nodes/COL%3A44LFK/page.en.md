## Muraltia origanoides {#s-233691cca8}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia origanoides* as an accepted species. A regional account describes a densely branched shrub to 20 cm, hairy on young parts, with branches that become spiny. Its obovate to elliptic leaves have thickened, hairy margins and a reflexed spine-like tip; pink flowers sit in the leaf axils, and the capsules are hairy with short horns. The recorded habitat is gravel slopes, not a complete range statement.
