## What growth form is recorded for Euphorbia pseudoglobosa? {#s-763cdbcb8a}
This dioecious dwarf succulent has a main stem underground and a rosette of slender branches just below the surface. The branches are up to 15 cm long, globose to cylindrical, with six to eight faint angles and a width of 12–25 mm. Leaves are deciduous and 1–5 × 1 mm. Solitary 3–4 mm cyathia have entire glands ranging from green to reddish brown.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-3af28f6ef7}
The source lists gravelly flats or slopes on shale, often among quartz patches.
