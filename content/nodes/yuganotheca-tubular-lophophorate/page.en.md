## A mosaic reconstruction of Yuganotheca {#s-f48fb07fbb}
The study draws on 710 referred Yuganotheca elegans specimens to reconstruct paired agglutinated valves, a horseshoe-shaped lophophore, a two-part tube and a long pedicle. This anatomy is assembled across the sample; no single fossil is said to preserve the entire combination. The mosaic invites comparison near the brachiopod–phoronid stem region, but exact placement and the homology of valves and attachment structures remain unresolved.
