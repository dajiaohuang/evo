## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-24537569c4}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Physalaemus albifrons (Spix, 1824) as a species in the genus Physalaemus, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.

## What the classification can tell us {#s-06cf03fe66}
The accepted record places this species within Physalaemus, Leptodactylidae and Anura. That classification gives a route to related catalogue entries, but it does not by itself establish the species' full morphology, life history, distribution, evolutionary history or conservation status.
