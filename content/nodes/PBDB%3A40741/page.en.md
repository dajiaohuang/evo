## Recorded parent of Carpolestidae {#s-a225adbaaf}
Carpolestidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Plesiadapoidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
