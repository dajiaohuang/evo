## Name and AVONET sample {#s-6615e97397}
COL26.8 accepts Ardea sumatrana within Ardeidae. AVONET v7 reports it under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Eight individuals were measured; four had a complete morphological set. The usable sample may differ among traits.

## Beak dimensions {#s-e44ab3272b}
Mean beak length is 123.6 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 18.4 mm and depth 27 mm at that edge. These are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-c971eded01}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 453.5 mm; mean tarsus length is 156.8 mm. The compiled mean mass is 2024 g, sourced to Dunning. This mass is from separate species-average information, not necessarily the measured specimens.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-5bf60b8c00}
AVONET assigns Coastal habitat, an Aquatic predator trophic niche and a Terrestrial primary lifestyle. These broad labels support comparison; they do not imply exclusive use of a habitat or food.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-43d1e9123d}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude −21.02° to 21.23°. The endpoints summarize mapped geography, not non-breeding movements, a current position or occupancy throughout the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## A second ecology compilation {#s-2e384ecd73}
EltonTraits 1.0 contains a same-name, same-family historical entry with two usable groups: diet and foraging stratum. The older taxonomy and matching name do not resolve later species splits or synonym disputes.

## Food weights and confidence {#s-6ec1661098}
The standardized diet weights are fish 60% and invertebrates 40%. Code B means the compilers had reasonable confidence in the source, while relative proportions may be uncertain. The percentages translate descriptions, not measured diet shares; zero does not prove a food is never taken. Source categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names.

## Feeding positions at the surface and ground {#s-78512869f1}
The species-level foraging weights are 90% on or just below the water surface and 10% on the ground. They describe relative feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or time budgets. Source classes for water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial use have separate meanings; they are not elevation bands or flight-height measurements.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-dbbaf41ce2}
This is a reading guide to historical AVONET v7 and EltonTraits 1.0 data, not a complete species account. AVONET morphology values are sample means and its mass is separately sourced; habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, while geography covers historical breeding and resident maps only. EltonTraits weights are standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements; code B retains uncertainty in relative proportions. Same-name, same-family matching supports association but not equivalence to COL26.8. Current ecology, range, population and conservation require other sources.
