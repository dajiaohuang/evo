## Living genes reveal repeated convergence {#s-df50448dbf}
A multigene phylogeny of living cephalopods supports repeated convergence associated with shifts in habitat. Similar adaptations can arise independently, so the tree helps distinguish shared ancestry from repeated change within the sampled living lineages.

## Extinct forms and ancestral settings remain open {#s-3ece7b8056}
The analysis does not directly sample extinct cephalopods, and inferred ancestral habitats are model reconstructions rather than observations. Its result should be read as a hypothesis about the sampled living tree.
