## Cannomois virgata {#s-9c1ab49154}
## Female flower clusters and a shale mountain habitat

The regional flora describes Cannomois virgata as a perennial with underground stems that forms mats and reaches 1.5 m. Female spikelets (small flower clusters) are usually solitary and sometimes paired; their tips are pointed, and they are up to 30 mm long. The tepals (flower parts not divided into sepals and petals) are as long as the 9–12 mm nut. The account places it usually on shale in high-rainfall mountains and on south-facing slopes, sometimes in dense stands.
