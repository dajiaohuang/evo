## Babiana villosa {#s-c44326c5f8}
Babiana villosa is recognised by its radially symmetrical flowers and large dark anthers with broad, arrow-like connectives. The regional account records flower colours from scarlet through deep purple to pink, with the pink-flowered plants near Paardeberg having the longest reported tubes. It grows on clay flats and slopes in renosterveld. Colour and tube length vary among the cited regional populations; the account does not establish how often each form occurs across the full range.
