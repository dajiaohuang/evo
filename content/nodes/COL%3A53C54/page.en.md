## Styrax ferrugineus Nees & Mart. {#s-2c0e1b7183}
## Rusty star hairs beneath pale flowers

Styrax ferrugineus has branches that range from becoming smooth to woolly, with star-shaped hairs. Its elliptic, narrow elliptic or ovate leaves are papery to somewhat leathery, with entire margins. The upper surface becomes smoother with age, while the underside is woolly; the star hairs may be gray, rusty or brown. The white to cream petals meet edge-to-edge in the bud. Flowers have ten stamens, and the fruit is a globose or nearly globose, woolly drupe.
