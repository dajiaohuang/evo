## Philodendron hederaceum: leaf and floral characters {#s-b3fb60efe2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a light-green prophyll. The leaf sheath is conspicuous. The green petiole is smooth, slightly flattened above and rounded below. Leaf blades are entire, cordate or ovate, with entire margins; the basal midrib is thickened. The field also gives “3–5” without clarifying which character it measures.

One axillary inflorescence is listed per sympodium. Its short green peduncle is shorter than the slightly constricted spathe, which has a light-green outer tube and vinaceous inner blade. The subsessile spadix is also described as cream-colored. Stamen state is unknown; staminodes are narrow at the base. The gynoecium and ovary are ovoid, with 6 or 7 locules and 20 ovules; placentation is axial and the stigma capitate. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The unassigned “3–5” value and alternative locule counts remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
