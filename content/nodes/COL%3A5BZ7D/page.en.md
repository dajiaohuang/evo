## What do its grey flower spikes and nuts look like? {#s-75ddd1f068}
A 2012 regional account describes *Willdenowia teres* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants), rhizomatous (with a horizontal underground stem) perennial reaching 1 m. Its branched stems are smooth or finely wrinkled, and the spikelets (small flower clusters) are greyish. The flower segments, or tepals, widen toward their base; the nut is smooth or sparsely pitted. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Which sandy places drain well? {#s-b042c62614}
The regional account lists well-drained sandy flats and slopes. This habitat description does not establish a complete range.
