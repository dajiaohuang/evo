## What traits are recorded for Guatteria modesta? {#s-a986d2c5fc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic to egg-shaped leaves with a warty surface. The upper surface is hairless; the underside may have sparse or dense hairs. One or two flowers grow in the leaf axils or on leafless stems. The free sepals are triangular, the petals oblong-elliptic, and the fruit segments ellipsoid. Seeds are pitted.

## What broad habitat is recorded? {#s-e7d0edc024}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without further habitat detail.
