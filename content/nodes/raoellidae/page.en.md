## Raoellids as sampled fossil relatives {#close-fossil-relatives}
The whale-origin synthesis uses sampled raoellids as close fossil relatives for testing the land-to-water transition. Their relationship to Cetacea is a phylogenetic inference; the cited evidence does not identify a raoellid specimen as a direct whale ancestor.

## A review sample, not a family-wide range {#sample-window}
The 48–47 Ma display marks the raoellid interval sampled in the whale-origin review. It is not a global first or last appearance, a direct-ancestor interval or a measure of the family’s complete duration.
