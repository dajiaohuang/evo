## Muraltia elsieae {#s-7f54ae5152}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia elsieae* as an accepted species. A regional account describes a closely leafy, single-stemmed shrub to 1.5 m. Its short-stalked, linear-lanceolate leaves end in a small point, and solitary purple flowers grow in the leaf axils. The account records rocky sandstone slopes as habitat; it does not report a sampling frame or population frequency.
