## Distribution records {#s-c65919117b}
How many *Microcebus mamiratra* were recorded on Nosy Be? In 2023, researchers surveyed at night during the wet season, using line-transect distance sampling—a method that estimates density from sightings along a route and their distance from it. Across 15 transects totaling 46.5 km, they recorded 92 visual detections and estimated mean density at 125.1 animals/km² (95% confidence interval: 65.3–239.5). Extrapolating the study-area estimate to forest within the species' mapped extent of occurrence on Nosy Be yielded about 4,700 animals. This is a model-based island estimate, not a census or a population estimate for the entire species range; the study did not measure population trend or seasonal variation.

## Evidence limits {#s-3223f5e3f1}
This focused island survey does not establish abundance across the species' entire range, population trend or seasonal variation.
