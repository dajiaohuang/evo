## Coccoloba conduplicata {#s-056c8ad7e1}
## Climbing branches and persistent leaf-base sheaths

Coccoloba conduplicata is recorded with climbing branches and leaves that range from oval to lance-shaped or elliptic. The leaf-base sheath, or ochrea, persists and joins the petiole; the lower blade surface may be hairless, finely hairy or becoming hairless. Long racemose flower clusters have a smooth or finely hairy axis and leathery bracts. Bell-shaped, membranous bracteoles sit beneath the flowers. The fruiting perianth is ovoid and leathery, and does not enlarge; the nut is ovoid with a pointed tip. Habitat is labeled terrestrial.
