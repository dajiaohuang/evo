## What leaf and flower traits mark Croton stenotrichus? {#s-98392c5a82}
Young branches have scale-like hairs. Short-stalked lance-shaped leaves have star-shaped hairs above and scale-like hairs beneath, with looping side veins and entire margins. A sessile nectar gland occurs at the base; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked, though their shape is unknown. Female flowers are nearly sessile, with fused sepals, a two-lobed style, and unequal calyx lobes; female petals are absent. The bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
