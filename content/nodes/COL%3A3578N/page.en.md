## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Diacidia glaucifolia? {#s-22af42b5c1}
The stipules are joined at their tips. The lower leaf surface has a pale, waxy bloom and silky hairs. The flower clusters are arranged in a branched thyrse and have four to ten units. Flowers bear eight or nine fertile stamens. The sepals enlarge greatly as the fruit develops.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5c0cb95027}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
