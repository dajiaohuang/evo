## *Isoetes serracarajensis*: triangular leaves and basal wings {#s-21a2ede1b5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional profile describes a three-lobed, globose stem and linear to triangular leaves. The distal leaf cross-section is triangular, and wings extend from the base toward the tip. Sporangia, the spore cases, sit at the leaf bases and are elliptical, dark brown to reddish brown; their velum is rudimentary or absent.

Large megaspores have wart-like surfaces on both sides, while the smaller microspores have a spiny outer coat on the distal surface. The morphology field lists these states but provides no habitat or locality claim.
