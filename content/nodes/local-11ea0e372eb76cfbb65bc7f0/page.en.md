## Name and classification in the source {#s-5f582be006}
Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Boana calcarata* under genus *Boana*, family Hylidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.
