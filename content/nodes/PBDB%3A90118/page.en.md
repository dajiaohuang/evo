IVPP V14530 is nearly complete, although the end of its tail is missing. The description records a broad skull behind the eye, a shallower region in front of the eye, a frontal midline hollow and a trapezoidal quadratojugal. The reported total length of about 120 cm is an estimate for this individual, not a measured species-wide size range.

The cited character matrix places Yinlong among basal ceratopsians; that is a result of the sampled characters and taxa, not a direct-ancestor claim. The study does not resolve diet or a preferred habitat from this skeleton.
