## ## Winged leaf stalks and saucer-shaped nectaries {#s-9d632f5976}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical, pubescent branches with lenticels. The persistent stipules are elliptic; both the petiole and main leaf axis (rachis) are winged. The leaves have two or three pairs of leaflets and saucer-shaped nectaries without stalks. Umbels—flower stalks radiating from one point—bear flowers on stalks, with funnel-shaped calyces. The fruit is flat and glabrous, though the source lists the valve texture as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
