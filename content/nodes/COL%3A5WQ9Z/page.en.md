## Accepted name and placement {#s-0be34dbb11}
COL26.8 accepts Brachycephalus fuscolineatus Pie, Bornschein, Firkowski, Belmonte-Lopes & Ribeiro, 2015 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brachycephalidae (family) → Brachycephalus (genus). Dorsal skin is densely rough (n = 12) and lateral skin densely rough (n = 12). These examined ranges are not whole-species maxima, a statistical proof of population sexual dimorphism or a growth series. Smooth skin in this study can include small, inconspicuous warts; external digit absence does not alone establish skeletal absence.

## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-2c697bea2e}
Orange and green dorsal patterns vary from a greener midline to a broadly greenish back, with orange sides and belly. Preserved material is cream and dark. These are the authors’ comparative observations rather than a standardized colour-chart measurement. Colour and specimen counts vary by trait; similar colours recur among species, so a single hue is not a complete identification rule. Preserved appearance should not be substituted for live appearance.
