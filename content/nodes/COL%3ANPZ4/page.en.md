## Bulbine fallax {#s-600dd020a6}
Bulbine fallax has 12–20 leaves that lie close to the ground and develop broad, pale-bordered rectangular windows. The yellowish flowers are followed by oblong-ellipsoid capsules about 10 mm long. It is recorded from hard clay near patches of quartz pebbles. These are regional diagnostic features, not a range-wide variation study.
