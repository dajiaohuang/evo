## *Myrcia mucugensis* {#s-9f86c86e71}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — egg-shaped; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as terrestrial (Portuguese: Terrícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
