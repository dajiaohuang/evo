## The name and this data account {#s-c9ca189352}
COL26.8 accepts *Poliocephalus poliocephalus* in Podicipedidae. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 4 measured individuals, 3 of them with a complete set of morphological measurements. Sample sizes can vary by trait.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-829ad9c415}
The recorded beak means are 10.8 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip, 3.9 mm wide and 6.4 mm deep at the nostril edge. These are separate dimensions at defined landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-b606555b35}
Mean unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 110 mm; mean tarsus length is 34.5 mm. The compiled mean mass is 239.86 g, from source category Dunning. It comes from separate species-average information, so the morphological specimens were not necessarily weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-37aba1d3d6}
The dataset's dominant categories are Wetland for habitat, Aquatic predator for trophic niche and Aquatic for lifestyle. They support comparisons across species, but do not imply exclusive use of one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-76933910b1}
The migration field classifies the record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitudes -43.64° to -25.91°. They do not describe all non-breeding movements or show current position or global occupancy. The geographic and morphological data come from separate compilation steps.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-4bed74fc15}
The nonzero food weights are invertebrates 90% and fish 10%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and do not imply a diet limited to nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-af48af48a7}
The archive's species-level foraging-stratum weights are 70% below the water surface and 30% on or just below it. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevation ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

This page guides readers through historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from separate literature or weighing sources; the morphological sample counts do not apply to mass.
