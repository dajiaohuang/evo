## *Schnella erythrantha* {#s-f3cac0d043}
The field records deciduous stipules and entire leaf blades on flowering or fruiting branches. Buds are ovoid; their calyx tube is not winged. At flowering, the calyx is bell-shaped or deeply divided into five toothed lobes. The upper petal is obovate and woolly, the anthers oblong to linear, the ovary stalked, and the fruit dehiscent (opening at maturity). These are database characters, not a complete description.
