## *Restio unispicatus*: a blue, finely tubercled mat {#s-27c8b842f6}
This mat-forming perennial spreads by stolons and reaches 40 cm. Its branched stems are bluish and densely marked with fine tubercles. The account notes that female spikelets are solitary.

It records the plant on seasonally dry sandy plateaus, called *suurvlaktes* in the source.
