## Leaves and stipules {#s-61bb428ce5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes oblanceolate leaflets, hairy on the underside and with entire margins. It lists their arrangement as “trifoliolate/digitate,” retaining alternatives rather than selecting one: trifoliolate means three leaflets, while digitate means arranged like fingers. The free distal portion of the stipules is reported as 3.3–56 mm long; the record does not explain this wide span.

## Flower clusters {#s-e2ab391e2b}
The inflorescence bracts are described as deciduous and longer than 10 mm.

## Flower color {#s-8f1cac5b33}
Flower color is recorded as blue.

## Habitat category {#s-833f228b3d}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
