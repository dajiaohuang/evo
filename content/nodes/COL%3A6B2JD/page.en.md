## Cordia kingstoniana {#s-c0eb5f5512}
## Long-haired stems and yellow, hairy fruits

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes hairy stems and elliptic-lance-shaped leaves. Leaf bases are wedge-shaped to rounded, and tips are acute. The upper surface is hairless, while the underside has short, stiff hairs. The bisexual white flowers have a ribbed, tubular calyx under 1 cm long and a tubular corolla also under 1 cm. The anthers lack glands; the stigma is head-shaped, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is yellow, elliptic, and covered with short, stiff appressed hairs. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
