## Maranta burchellii {#s-c022082d90}
## A swollen corolla tube and ellipsoid fruit

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes elliptic leaf blades with hairy pulvini, the small joints between blade and stalk. Bracts are loosely arranged. The corolla tube is gibbous, or swollen; the ovary is densely silky-haired with a smooth surface, and the sepals are longer than the tube. The record gives one callus on the staminode, an ellipsoid fruit and persistent, upright sepals. Its habitat field says terrestrial.
