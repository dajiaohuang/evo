## Pouteria lucumifolia (Reissek ex Maxim.) T.D.Penn. {#s-710b7a190b}
## Channelled petioles and yellow-orange fruit

Pouteria lucumifolia has smooth young branches with lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have channelled petioles; the side veins loop near the margin toward the tip. Axillary or branch-borne clusters carry flowers with four or five sepals and corolla lobes. The corolla is tubular, and its lobes are rounded or blunt. The ovary has two chambers. The fruit is hairless and yellow to orange, with a smooth or finely warty surface; it contains one seed with a wrinkled coat and narrow scar.
