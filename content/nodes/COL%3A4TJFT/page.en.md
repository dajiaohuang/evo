## A difficult species complex {#s-6a646286fe}
The source describes a scandent shrub with 5–7 leaflets that are slightly discoloured, hairy and prominently veined below; flowers are pink or mauve. It calls this species intermediate between *R. pinnatus* and *R. exsuccus* and describes the three as a taxonomically difficult complex with confusion over boundaries and names. Habitat is forest margins and disturbed places.
