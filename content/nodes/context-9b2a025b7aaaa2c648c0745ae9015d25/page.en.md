## The source concept {#s-227a523296}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria havina as row Anf2832 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-158afb82ef}
The row lists terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-7cbf1c08a1}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 38 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-5a45ff89e9}
AmphiBIO lists 3–36 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to one reproductive event per year, and offspring or egg sizes of 2.5 mm. These are compiled literature counts and extrema; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
