## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2bb0889e42}
COL26.8 records Achalinus zugorum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Xenodermidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-832354758a}
The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018.
