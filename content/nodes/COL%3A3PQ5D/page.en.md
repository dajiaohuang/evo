COL26.8 accepts Ingerana borealis (Annandale, 1912) as a species in Anura, Dicroglossidae, and Ingerana. This is the classification recorded in the release of 20 August 2026.

## Maximum adult body length in AmphiBIO {#s-1633598167}
Under its historical Frost 2011 concept, AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 38 mm for Ingerana borealis; equivalence to the species concept used here has not been established. For frogs, body length is measured from snout to vent. This compiled maximum is not a measurement expected for every individual.
