## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-358d05304a}
COL26.8 records Simalia nauta as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pythonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-68a37e5c0f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Carey and Judge 2000, Slavens and Slavens 1999, Feldman et al. 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-4fd1ced3d2}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
