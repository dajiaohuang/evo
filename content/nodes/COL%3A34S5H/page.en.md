## Morphological records and mitochondrial identities do not all agree {#s-bf01f5e632}
The tree recovered haddadi terminals, including topotypic material, among structured decipiens lineages. The authors also found Alagoas sequences labelled haddadi closer to decipiens lineage VII than to topotypic haddadi and questioned several northeastern morphology-only records. This is evidence of unresolved source concepts, not a completed synonymy or permission to assign all those populations to tapacurensis. Specimen labels, topotypic comparisons and proposed reassignment remain separate. The article requested additional morphology, calls and sequence sampling to resolve species limits; this page does not pool every historical record into one contemporary haddadi concept.

## The comparison cites earlier haddadi call measurements {#s-1926d1c064}
The tapacurensis comparison cited haddadi dominant frequencies of 4,312–4,875 Hz, series of one to three notes and note durations of 4–59 ms. These were earlier-source values, not a uniform new acoustic survey of all haddadi populations. The discussion warns that the published haddadi call came from outside the type locality and that morphology-only identification can be uncertain. Consequently the ranges are retained as attributed measurements with a concept limitation, not as universal diagnostic bounds or evidence that every northeastern population has those calls. Reported male size also overlaps tapacurensis, so a single size or frequency value cannot settle identity.
