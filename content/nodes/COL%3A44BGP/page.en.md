## Name and dataset row {#s-1cdb6e293d}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts *Montivipera wagneri* in Viperidae. The ReptTraits v1-2 row follows the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and has supported fields for length, microhabitat and reproduction.

## Reported maximum total length {#s-8bdfdda274}
The record reports maximum total length (TL) of 950 mm. Total length includes the tail, unlike snout–vent length (SVL). This compiled maximum is not a typical individual size or a theoretical growth limit.

## Microhabitat label {#s-fdc6974324}
The ReptTraits row labels the microhabitat “Terrestrial.” This broad label is not a detailed description of habitat use and does not specify daily activity, prey or differences among places, seasons and life stages.

## Live-bearing and the compiled mean {#s-8405e99f32}
The row labels reproductive mode “viviparous” (live-bearing) and reports 11 in the combined field “offspring per litter or eggs per clutch.” The field joins two kinds of counts; 11 is a dataset summary, not a count for every litter. Under the workbook protocol, summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are absent, extrema, so this value need not be an arithmetic mean of litters observed in one population.

## Evidence limits {#s-3f52c1ff61}
This account is limited to the cited ReptTraits row. A compiled maximum is not an absolute species limit, and the reproductive summary is not a fixed litter size. Matching name and family do not prove equivalence among later taxonomic concepts; the row also does not describe variation among individuals or populations. Conservation, venom and current distribution are outside the fields presented here. Source labels preserve the citation trail but do not mean that every underlying work was independently reviewed.
