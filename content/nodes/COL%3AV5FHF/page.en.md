## Northern Guizhou and southern Chongqing localities {#s-ac4175827b}
Male holotype CIB KKS20180722006 came from Kuankuoshui Nature Reserve, Suiyang, Guizhou, at 1521 m. The monograph examined males from Taiyangshan in that reserve at about 1600 m and Jinfo Mountain, Chongqing, at about 1420 m. Its broader summary covered hilly localities along northern Guizhou and southern Chongqing at 1270–1704 m. The examined sites and the wider cited summary remain distinct rather than demonstrating continuous occupancy.

## Historical and examined adult samples {#s-075a3c7579}
The diagnosis cited Liu and colleagues (2020) for nine adult males at 34.4–39.2 mm snout–vent length and two females at 39.5–40.4 mm. The monograph’s own eight males measured 34.5–38.6 mm. These samples are not pooled into one uniformly measured series. Diagnostic anatomy included no vomerine ridges or teeth, toes without webbing or lateral fringes, and tiny, dense black nuptial spines on finger I and II pads in breeding males, with a subgular vocal sac. These observations do not determine reproductive timing or universal size limits.
