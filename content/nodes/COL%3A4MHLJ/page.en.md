## Pungent leaves with prickly edges {#s-e9769df0fa}
This account describes a stiffly upright shrublet up to 1 m tall, with short hairs on the stems. Linear-lanceolate leaves are often twisted, with rolled-under, prickly edges and a sharp smell. Broad, deeply divided bracts also have rolled-under margins. White to lilac funnel-shaped flowers cluster in the leaf axils, and the fruits are about 10 mm long. It grows on stony slopes, often over shale.
