## Name, taxon and sample {#s-11bf45ca18}
COL26.8 accepts Zosterops nehrkorni within Zosteropidae. AVONET v7 reports this name under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 taxonomy. The dataset has one measured individual and one complete morphological set; usable sample counts can still vary by trait.

## Beak measurements {#s-a8be930e31}
The mean beak length is 7.8 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 2.9 mm and depth 3 mm at that edge. These are separate measurements at defined landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-87e4f65fd4}
Mean unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 56.4 mm; mean tarsus length is 16.9 mm. The separately compiled mean mass is 10 g, sourced to Dunning. It is not assigned from the morphology sample.

## Broad ecological categories {#s-3fe0a07a3d}
AVONET records Forest habitat, an Omnivore trophic niche and an Insessorial primary lifestyle. These broad categories support comparison among species; they do not mean that every habitat or food is used in the same way.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-7fe8d2235d}
The migration category is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude 3.48° to 3.54°. The endpoints describe a mapped range, not non-breeding movements, present location or confirmed occupancy at every point. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## Limits of this historical account {#s-9e5d4b5d11}
This is a narrow account of historical AVONET v7 data. The morphology sample is a single complete set, so the reported means do not describe individual variation or identify the species. Mass comes from a separate source. Ecological fields are broad categories, and the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. Historical crosswalks and matching names support association, not equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Reproduction, current distribution, population and conservation require other sources.
