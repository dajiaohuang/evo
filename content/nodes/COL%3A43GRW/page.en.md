## ## Branched setae and yellow filaments {#s-e5075e6fe3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records pubescent or hispid stems with hairs appressed forward. Simple hairs are absent; branched setae are listed as barbellate, plumose, star-shaped or wart-like, while unbranched setae, glandular hairs, prickles and short shoots are absent. The stipules are shed, and extrafloral nectaries and projections between pinna pairs are absent. The inflorescences are globose, with bracts shorter than the flowers before opening. The flowers have four perianth parts, a membranous calyx with triangular lobes and hairy corolla lobes with one to three veins. The androecium is isostemonous, with yellow filaments. The habitat field says “terrestrial” but gives no locality or finer habitat description.
