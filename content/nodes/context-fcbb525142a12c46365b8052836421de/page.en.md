## The source concept {#s-2e1e0778f9}
In AmphiBIO v1, Platymantis acrochordus appears in row Anf1072, classified in the genus Platymantis, family Ceratobatrachidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited record; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Developmental strategy {#s-0a0125ca7d}
The deposited record marks direct development. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
