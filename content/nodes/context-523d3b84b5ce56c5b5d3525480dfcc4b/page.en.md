## Foraging strata {#s-bbee71f06a}

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-d1acb3f300}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 76 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
