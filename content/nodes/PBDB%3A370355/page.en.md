## Recorded parent of Perognathinae {#s-1d0295b7f5}
Perognathinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Heteromyidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
