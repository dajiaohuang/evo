## Asparagus declinatus: growth and flowers {#s-ac288679e9}
A regional account describes a much-branched scrambling plant up to 1 m tall. Its linear cladodes occur in groups of three. Flowers hang downward, grow singly in the leaf axils, and have tepals joined at the base and straight filaments.

These characters come from a South African regional flora account cited to a 2012 publication. The account records mostly rocky outcrops, fynbos and coastal scrub as habitats.
