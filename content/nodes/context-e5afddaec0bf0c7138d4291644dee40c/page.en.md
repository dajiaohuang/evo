## The source concept {#s-d8720e4064}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Breviceps macrops* in row Anf0324 under Breviceps, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-44d896bdc1}
For Anf0324, the compilation tags fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-5efa52aa22}
The adult food field lists arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; this tag is not a complete diet and gives no share of food items.

## Activity periods {#s-a925ce34b2}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Seasonal activity {#s-bd6ddec432}
The seasonal field is tagged wet and warm. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-0cf9d1295b}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 50 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-f71aad264a}
The row records 43 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every clutch or offspring.

## Developmental strategy {#s-40245894d3}
The deposited row classifies development as direct. That label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
