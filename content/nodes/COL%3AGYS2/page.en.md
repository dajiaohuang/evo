## What the AmphiBIO v1 row reports {#s-20003e19e0}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis poecilonotus as row Anf0143 and assigns it to Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina and Anura. Its species concepts follow Frost 2011's *Amphibian Species of the World*; equivalence with the COL26.8 record is unresolved.

The historical row flags terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging strata and arthropods in the adult diet. It reports maximum adult mass of 2.69 g and maximum adult body length of 33.4 mm. For Anura, this length is snout–vent length (SVL, from snout to vent). The diet categories compile literature reports based on expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; a listed category is not a complete diet or a measured proportion. The broad foraging categories do not describe seasonal or developmental changes.

The row also reports sexual-maturity ages of 0.3–0.4 years and size at maturity of 20 mm; maximum lifespan of 0.3 years; 10–30 offspring or eggs per clutch; up to 2 reproductive events per year; and offspring or egg size of 2.9 mm. It flags direct development. These are literature-derived compiled values. The row does not identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions for each value, and the 2.9 mm field may refer to offspring or eggs. TetrapodTraits v2.0.1 reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries, but the affected-row match has not been checked. Treat the 2.69 g value and foraging flags as unverified historical entries; no replacement is inferred.
