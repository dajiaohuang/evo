## Vigna unguiculata {#s-60d3e2c302}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Vigna unguiculata* as an accepted species of *Vigna*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes climbing growth and records a purple-lilac to pink corolla with bilateral symmetry. The linear pod is hairless; the source lists brown, blackish, or white seeds. The same regional record calls its habitat terrestrial, but does not give a collection locality or a complete range.
