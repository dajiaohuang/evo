## Overview {#s-03a197272a}
Barschichthys ruedersdorfensis is a lower Anisian stem teleosteomorph known from skull-roof specimens and a partial head and trunk from Rüdersdorf, around 244 Ma. The remains preserve a broad lobed skull-roof margin, cranial ornament, ganoid scales and a long lower jaw with small conical teeth. A partial trunk is about 50 mm standard length; the diagnosis estimates about 80 mm total length.

## Diet {#s-29f812a845}
The paper discusses feeding comparisons among Triassic stem teleosteomorphs but does not identify this species’ prey.

## Habitat {#s-06a603463a}
The type material comes from a marine, restricted-basin setting in the Central European (Germanic) Basin; no broader habitat preference is established.

## Locomotion {#s-e3e7739f4e}
It was an aquatic fish; the study does not measure swimming performance.

## Body size {#s-2d68f1d6da}
The diagnosis gives about 80 mm total length; the partial trunk specimen is about 50 mm standard length.

## Ecological role {#s-2ebdd76efe}

## Preserved features {#s-fda922ffdd}

## Evidence and scope {#s-3c17c8ce26}
MB.f.19907–19909 support a distinct species diagnosis at Rüdersdorf, including the approximately 50 mm standard-length partial specimen. The roughly 244 Ma point is a local sample; it does not establish a global range, direct ancestry or a feeding guild.

## Limits of this account {#s-6b39f1a945}
The locality and marine basin setting describe the known sample, not a global range. Feeding comparisons do not identify this species’ prey, and aquatic anatomy is not a measurement of swimming performance or proof of a trophic guild.
