## Morphology, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-702327a4f9}
The field records feeding roots and a dark-green, deciduous prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole cylindrical to subcylindrical. The blade is hastate and pedatisect (divided in a hand-like pattern), with a cuspidate tip, an oblong sinus, and the source count “6–7”; the counted feature is not made explicit in the structured text. The sympodial inflorescence is listed with 1 or 2 units. Its peduncle is green. The subsessile spadix accompanies a spathe whose inner tube is reddish purple, outer tube light green, and outer blade light green.

The source marks the stamens, staminodes, gynoecium, ovary, locule count, ovule count, and stigma as unknown. It lists placentation as axial, basal, subbasal, or biseriate (in two rows), without selecting one state. Berry and seed characters are also unknown.
