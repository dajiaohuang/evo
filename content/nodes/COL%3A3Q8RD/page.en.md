## *Isoetes naipiana*: a partial velum over the basal sporangium {#s-f273962535}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, the globose stem has two lobes, while the needle-shaped leaves ascend or curve backward. A wing occurs only near the leaf base. The sporangium—the spore case—sits at the base of a leaf and is partly covered by a transparent velum, a thin membrane.

The large megaspores have net-like surfaces on both sides; the smaller microspores have a single scar and a smooth outer coat on the distal side. The regional morphology field lists these characters but gives no habitat or locality claim.
