## Sticky stems and simple style branches {#s-5320f3134a}
Moraea simplex grows 30–40 cm tall and has sticky stems and a single trailing leaf. Its pale-yellow flowers have simple, threadlike style branches extending between the anthers, an unusual combination in the subgenus.
