## *Restio corneolus*: awned bracts on a tall tuft {#s-40cb9418ba}
This tufted perennial can reach 90 cm and has sparsely branched, mostly straight stems. Its spikelets range from one to several, and the flower bracts bear an awn—a slender, bristle-like tip.

The flora records it on slopes above 300 m, sometimes in seepage areas where water moves through the soil.
