## *Thamnochortus lucens*: smooth stems and firm bracts {#s-6458a476d8}
This tufted perennial reaches 60 cm. Its simple stems are smooth, though some plants also have sterile, much-branched stems. Female spikelets are 10–20 mm long and bear cartilaginous bracts, firm and flexible like cartilage.

The recorded habitat is dry mountain slopes.
