## A small perennial herb with reticulate venation {#s-17d9ca7b81}
The source description records a perennial herb up to 90 mm tall. Leaves are elliptic, 9–11 × 6 mm, acute, coriaceous and conspicuously reticulate veined on both surfaces. Flowers are solitary in the leaf axils and blue or lavender. The ecology field gives grassland and bushveld. These source fields do not define a complete range.
