## A rhizome with a basal rosette of smooth leaves {#s-f475c72f1a}
The record describes a rhizome and a basal rosette of smooth, leathery leaves with entire edges. The blades range from inversely lance-shaped to lance-shaped and have rounded bases. A single stalked head has a bell-shaped involucre in four rows; it contains about 20–30 florets. The cylindrical fruits may be silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
