## Winter flowers on drying grooved leaves {#s-cf0181b9c9}
Gladiolus vernus reaches 0.8 m. Its linear, grooved leaves dry by flowering, when the plant bears pale magenta-pink, dirty-pink or mauve flowers; the account describes it as winter-flowering.
