## ## Stalked nectaries and a hairy pod {#s-66eeeb6468}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes cylindrical branches with coarse, stiff hairs; lenticels are listed as absent. The deciduous stipules, small appendages at the leaf base that fall early, are oval or oblong. The leaf stalk (petiole) and central leaf axis (rachis) are winged; the field lists three or five pairs of leaflets and stalked, head-shaped nectaries (nectar-producing glands). It records a spike-like flower cluster, a bell-shaped calyx (the outer floral cup) and flowers without individual stalks. The fruit is linear, with leathery pod walls (valves) and coarse, stiff hairs. The regional morphology row gives no habitat or locality, and its two leaflet-pair counts are not explained as variation or as a range.
