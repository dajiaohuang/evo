## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-153ceb461b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Das and Singh 2009. The foraging-mode field records AMB (ambush or sit-and-wait foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Das and Singh 2009.
