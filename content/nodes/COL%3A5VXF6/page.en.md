## Austroeupatorium rosmarinaceum {#s-396264b30f}
## White corollas and glandular fruit

The source records cylindrical, pubescent stems and opposite, sessile, linear papery leaves. The flower heads form flat-topped panicles with a bell-shaped involucre and partly overlapping bracts. Corollas are white, with cylindrical anthers and threadlike, papillose styles. The prismatic dry fruits (cypselae) are glandular and have a distinct, elongated, top-shaped basal attachment area (carpopodium). Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
