## Notharctus’s second toe, compared with grooming claws {#s-f9c7da9e33}
AMNH 143612 and its rejoining block AMNH 143640 preserve a semi-articulated foot from Grizzly Buttes, Bridger B. An associated mandible, AMNH 143611, supports attribution to Notharctus; within the foot, distal phalanx AMNH 143612-03 can be assigned to digit two and compared with grooming-claw phalanges.

The shape supports a comparative grooming-claw interpretation, but bone form alone does not show behavior. Any broader effect on primate relationships comes from the study’s character analysis, not from the toe bone as a direct record of ancestry.
