## A digit-two grooming-claw bone in Notharctus {#s-f9c7da9e33}
The semi-articulated Notharctus foot AMNH 143612/143640 lets researchers assign phalanx AMNH 143612-03 to digit two and compare its shape with grooming phalanges. The preserved association and anatomy support that comparison, while behaviour and broader anthropoid implications remain interpretations.
