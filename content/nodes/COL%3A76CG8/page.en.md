## One leaf perch on a montane forest trail {#s-a617cd6a08}
The Crocker Range Park survey found a specimen on a leaf about 0.5–1.0 m above ground along a montane forest trail at 1216 m elevation. The account explicitly noted that little was known of the species’ natural history. This encounter supports that local perch and elevation record, without establishing preferred perch height, physiological elevation limits, abundance or breeding behavior.

## A single male diagnostic comparison {#s-be2f6b83a3}
The account described a 23.6 mm adult male with a vocal-sac opening on the right side of the mouth floor, a weak finger-I nuptial pad with densely packed colourless spinules, and no mandibular spinules. Expanded, truncate outer fingertips and a tympanum smaller than half the eye diameter were also reported. The authors compared size and mandibular characters with three expanded-fingertip Pelophryne species and assigned the specimen to P. rhopophilia. This is a specimen-level morphological identification, not a newly genetically verified population or a female diagnosis.
