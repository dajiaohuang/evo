## Aponogeton angustifolius {#s-a88a8e2b20}
In a South African regional account archived by SANBI/WFO, this aquatic plant has a rhizome (an underground stem), small floating leaves that range from elliptic to lanceolate, and white flowers in two ranks. Each flower is recorded with 2 tepals (parts not distinguished as sepals or petals) and usually 6 stamens. The habitat line says pools and ditches; the account is regional, not a complete range or population-frequency survey.
