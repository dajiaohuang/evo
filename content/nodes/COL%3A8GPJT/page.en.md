## Name and the traits supported here {#s-a1a5d04b12}
COL26.8 lists Cyrtodactylus equestris as an accepted species in Gekkonidae. The linked ReptTraits v1-2 row records maximum length, a female maximum, oviparity and a clutch/litter summary; the workbook retains its Reptile Database 2023 name framework and field-specific citations. These entries report the cited record, not every population’s state.

## Overall and female maximum: 139 mm SVL {#s-8f4ab7682d}
Both the overall maximum and the female maximum are reported as 139 mm snout–vent length (SVL). SVL measures from snout to vent, excluding the tail. The matching figures are two fields in a compiled dataset, not proof that the underlying measurements came from the same specimen or locality, and they do not establish a typical size or population-level sexual dimorphism.

## Oviparity and a mean count of two {#s-864228b464}
The reproductive mode is labelled oviparous, while the consolidated offspring-per-litter or eggs-per-clutch field gives a mean of 2. The protocol can summarize reported means by their midpoint, or use the midpoint of reported extremes when means are unavailable, so this is not necessarily an arithmetic mean of observed clutches or a count fixed for every clutch.

## The scope of these records {#s-65e7a1f414}
These traits come from the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and its 2023 name framework; matching names do not establish equivalence with later taxonomic concepts. The compilation compresses differences in sex, age, locality and population, and the identical SVL figures do not remove that limitation. Missing fields do not support claims about mass, juvenile length, venom, conservation, current range or trends; the citations retain the source chain without implying a fresh review of every original study.
