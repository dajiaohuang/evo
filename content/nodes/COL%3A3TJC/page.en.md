## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-6bdc7d4b36}
COL26.8 records Contomastix at genus rank, with Teiidae as its direct parent.

COL26.8 accepts Contomastix as a genus in Teiidae. This page points readers to two fuller species records, Contomastix lacertoides and Contomastix vacariensis, and four more limited entries linked below. Those are the authored records represented here, not a count of all accepted species in the genus.

## Two maxima from different species {#s-1ed0ee2ad2}
The cited size records give a maximum snout–vent length of 76.9 mm for Contomastix lacertoides and 81.8 mm for C. vacariensis. Snout–vent length stops at the body opening and excludes the tail. These values compare two species’ compiled maxima; they are neither a within-species range nor the smallest and largest actual animals.

## Four additional pages keep their own scope {#s-104bb2adcf}
The limited records for Contomastix celata, Contomastix leachei, Contomastix serrana, and Contomastix vittata carry field-specific citations. Each page should be read for the traits it actually supports; missing data in one record are not filled from another species.

## A second pair of size records {#s-e13cddc62b}
The reported maximum snout–vent lengths are 63.3 mm for Contomastix serrana and 76 mm for C. vittata. These are records for two named species, not a body-size range for Contomastix as a genus. Comparisons of activity, feeding and reproduction also depend on matching fields and their sources.
