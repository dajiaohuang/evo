## Guarea guentheri {#s-8835a0165f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records alternate leaflets, axillary, branch-borne or trunk-borne inflorescences, and hairy ribbed or pear-shaped fruits.

## Alternate leaflets and several inflorescence positions {#s-bc33570382}
The regional description records a flattened or cylindrical leaf rachis and a terminal bud. Leaflets are alternate, elliptic, oblong or oblanceolate, with acuminate or cuspidate tips and eucamptodromous venation; the lower surface is strigose or pubescent. Inflorescences may be axillary, borne on branches or borne on the trunk. The outer calyx and corolla are strigose; corolla parts meet edge-to-edge in bud. The staminal tube is externally glabrous, while the ovary is pubescent or strigose and each locule has two ovules. The fruit is botuliform (sausage-shaped) or pear-shaped, with an acute apex, ribs and hairs. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial) without a locality or complete range.
