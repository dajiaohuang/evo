## Overview {#s-8f7bd74b04}
COL26.8 accepts Lagothrix flavicauda (Humboldt, 1812) as species ID 6NTWY under genus ID 63837; MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Yellow-tailed Woolly Monkey. The population and distribution records below come from separate montane-forest studies in northern Peru.

## Population trends {#s-3e5e08e9e3}
Shanee & Shanee compared transect surveys at the same approximately 700-ha La Esperanza site in 2008/09 and 2012/13. Their estimates showed group and individual densities increasing by 18.8% and 35.9%, respectively, but neither overall change was significant on its own; increases in infant and juvenile densities were significant. The authors interpreted the age-class pattern as mostly natural population growth, but the result remains a two-period comparison at one site, not a range-wide trend or a causal test of conservation action.

## Survey records {#s-edfcf78f08}
A 2014 Huánuco inventory walked 618 km of transects and recorded 47 primate groups across five species. It recorded 13 groups of L. flavicauda only in the Río Chontayacu microbasin and estimated 20.8 individuals/km² there. This is a site-level survey result, not a species-wide density.

## Range records {#s-2de8a9b5c3}
In 2016, Aquino et al. walked 213 km over 532 hours and recorded five groups between Monopampa and Libertad on the eastern side of the Río Huallaga. They described this as a first record east of the river and a southeastern extension of the then-known range toward the Pasco boundary; the paper also notes that further surveying was needed. The five groups are not a total count.

## Evidence limits {#s-7da92d4c24}
Density values and groups are tied to individual survey areas and methods. The La Esperanza comparison covers two periods at one site; Huánuco detections and the 2016 distribution record do not establish a current range-wide population. The Chinese display name is editorial. This page is.
