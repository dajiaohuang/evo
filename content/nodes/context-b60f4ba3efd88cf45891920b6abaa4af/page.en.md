## Source classification and concept status {#s-f0fe9028db}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3024 records *Hylodes asper* under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-3057db382f}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime activity {#s-caff634f11}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-1f6f55ea6c}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 44.9 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-1cec0c4756}
The row lists 57–114 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 2.5–4 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size fields may refer to eggs.
