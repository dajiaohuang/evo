## Hairy, grooved stems and toothed leaves {#s-f8ea2d816c}
The record describes erect, flattened, grooved branches with hairs, and stalked, ovate leaves that taper to a point and have toothed edges. The leaf base extends down along the stem; the upper and lower surfaces bear different kinds of hairs. Flowering branches divide in pairs, and the rays are present. The fruit surface is recorded as smooth or rough, and the habitat class is terrestrial.
