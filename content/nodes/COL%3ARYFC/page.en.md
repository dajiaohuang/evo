## Cayaponia citrullifolia (Griseb.) Cogn. ex Griseb. {#s-d1972eed41}
## Branched tendrils and small clustered fruits

Cayaponia citrullifolia has branched tendrils and non-prostrate branches. Its alternate leaves may be thin or leathery, and the blade extends down along the petiole. Racemes bear bell-shaped flowers. The fruits are arranged in racemes and number from three to many; they are ellipsoid or oblong to obovoid, under 23 mm long, and yellow to red when mature. Their surfaces range from becoming hairless to hairless, while the fruit stalks are woolly. Seeds are more than three per fruit, pale or spotted, ovate-oblong to nearly round, and enclosed in a fibrous aril.
