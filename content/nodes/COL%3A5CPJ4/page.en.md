## Xyris fibrosa {#s-694550e646}
## A cylindrical spike with a dozen or more flowers

The flora record describes Xyris fibrosa with nearly cylindrical leaves and a strongly widened leaf-sheath base. Its cylindrical spike carries 10–25 sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers on a flowering stalk 20–70 cm long. The recorded habitat is terrestrial.
