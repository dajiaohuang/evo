## The source concept {#s-45653cf074}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Scinax tigrinus as row Anf2700 under genus Scinax, family Hylidae, and order Anura. Its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-45108be39f}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 30.8 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.
