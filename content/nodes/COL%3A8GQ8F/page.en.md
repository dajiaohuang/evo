## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e6e7ff7274}
COL26.8 records Psammophis afroccidentalis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Psammophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-8be3c60532}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
