## Why the classification remains disputed {#s-e3e0fbe220}
The repeated preservation of soft anatomy does not resolve the placement. The cited study reports no diagnostic shell, siphuncle, beak or radula, so cephalopod affinity remains contested.


## What the soft-bodied specimens preserve {#s-288f328d63}
The study describes 90 specimens with paired eyes, two tentacles, lateral fins and a ventral funnel-like structure. The authors interpret the anatomy as that of a swimming, predatory cephalopod.
