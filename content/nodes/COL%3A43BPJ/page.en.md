## Mikania trinervis {#s-4e6830c1f8}
## Three basal veins and two above them

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, smooth, striate branches with solid pith. Opposite leaves range from lance-shaped to ovate, with entire margins, wedge-shaped bases and short tapered tips. Three main veins start at the base, with two more beginning above it. The smooth blades are described as membranous to leathery.

Loose panicles or thyrsoid clusters bear stalked heads. Short, linear bracts sit near the top of each head stalk; the lance-shaped bracts around a head are smooth or finely hairy, ciliate, and acute or blunt at the tip. The tubular corolla has triangular, tapered, smooth lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are recorded as smooth, glandular or sparsely hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or complete range is supplied.
