## A name in a pinned catalogue {#s-2eae992175}
The pinned Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 accepts Aprasia haroldi in Pygopodidae, the family of legless lizards. The profile uses its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which follows the workbook’s Reptile Database 2023 name framework and cites sources for individual traits. A species row condenses reports; it should not be taken as a uniform description of every population.

## Maximum length excludes the tail {#s-eb77e7945b}
The largest reported snout–vent length is 110 mm; the largest female value is 96.5 mm. Snout–vent length (SVL) measures from the snout to the vent, not to the tail tip. These compiled maxima are not typical sizes or theoretical limits on growth. Since sex-specific reports may come from different studies or places, their difference alone cannot measure population-level size dimorphism.

## How the source classifies habitat and activity {#s-0f7d43789f}
The record combines fossorial and terrestrial settings, classifies the diet as carnivorous and activity as nocturnal. “Fossorial” means adapted to digging or living underground. The categories summarize broad settings and timing; they are not a complete habitat account or a fixed schedule. Carnivorous does not identify prey, and reports may not cover every season, population or life stage.

## Egg-laying and clutch summaries {#s-19d0b344f4}
The source labels the reproductive mode oviparous, or egg-laying, and gives 2 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch as its consolidated figure. The lowest and highest reported clutch or litter means are also 2. The summary may use midpoints of reported means, or of reported extremes when means are unavailable. These values are not guaranteed counts for every clutch; the low and high fields mark endpoints of reported means rather than necessarily the smallest or largest nests observed. The labels do not establish embryo nutrition or uniformity across populations.
