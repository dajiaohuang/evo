## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-bc9f44c9cc}
The four selected guides cover four occurrence rows from two collections; Romualdo is among the listed strata. Read the locality, age bracket, and identification attached to each collection before comparing entries. Those records do not measure the number of animals that lived in the past.

## Evidence limits {#s-c654de6489}
These pages guide readers through the selected PBDB records, not every descendant or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence and collection counts are not specimen, individual, population, or sampling-intensity estimates. Database age bounds do not directly date a species' origin or extinction, and the recorded hierarchy is distinct from other taxonomic and phylogenetic treatments.
