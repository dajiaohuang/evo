## Silky leaves and flower heads in corymbs {#s-b1cfd1b4d6}
This record describes a woody plant with silky-haired, stalked leaves arranged in opposite pairs at right angles. The leaves are elliptic to oblong-elliptic and leathery. Radiate flower heads occur in corymbs, with yellow sterile ray florets and bisexual central florets. The fruit bears a pappus with two awns.
