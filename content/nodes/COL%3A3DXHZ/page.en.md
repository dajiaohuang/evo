## Creeping growth with upright flower shoots {#s-a85d050c1d}
The German description records a perennial herb with creeping stems; only its flowering shoots rise upright, to about 15 cm. The narrow leaves have long hairs along their edges, and each upright stem bears one blue-violet flower head with a yellow center. The habitat record is short grass at 2,000–2,538 m in the Drakensberg Grassland.
