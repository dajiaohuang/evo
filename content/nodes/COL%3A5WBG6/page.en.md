## Bahiella infundibuliflora {#s-188f947642}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Bahiella infundibuliflora* as an accepted species of *Bahiella*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records opposite leaves, milky latex, and cymose inflorescences with funnel-shaped corollas. The fruit is described as follicular. The habitat field says terrestrial. These are regional source fields; they do not provide a locality or a complete account of variation.
