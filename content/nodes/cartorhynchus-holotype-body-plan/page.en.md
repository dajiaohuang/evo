## Cartorhynchus: a mosaic preserved in one skeleton {#s-936120d06c}
The articulated holotype AGM I-1 is about 40 centimetres long. It combines a short snout and robust trunk with large flippers and thick cortical bone in the ribs and limbs. Those are observations from one specimen; amphibious habits and suction feeding are interpretations drawn from anatomy and comparison. A published corrigendum corrected character descriptions and tree statistics while retaining the reported topology. The fossil is not shown to be a direct ancestor or a global first appearance.
