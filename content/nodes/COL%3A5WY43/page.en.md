## Lilac flowers and a beaked keel petal {#s-29d72216ca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records twining stems, ovate-to-obovate leaflets, and axillary flower clusters. The corolla is lilac; the wing petals are coded as spiralled, while the keel, the paired lower petals, is beaked and spirals at the tip. The pod is laterally compressed, silky-haired, and dehiscent, meaning it opens at maturity; the seeds are brown and ellipsoid. A separate habitat field labels it terrestrial. The regional fields give no sampling frame, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status.
