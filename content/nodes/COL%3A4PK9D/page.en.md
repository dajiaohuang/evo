## *Psittacanthus truncatus* {#s-f6de8cae50}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — opposite arrangement; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis), axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
