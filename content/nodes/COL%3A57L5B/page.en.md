## What traits distinguish Tovomita spruceana in the record? {#s-1628485d28}
It has conspicuous stilt roots and yellow exudate. Its stalked, elliptic to oblong leaves may dry green or coppery, and their side veins are not arched. Male plants have pleiochasial clusters and female plants dichasial clusters. Flowers have two sepals and four petals, with white, cylindrical stamens or staminodes. The female structures have five chambers and five styles. Pear-shaped fruits have five valves and a smooth, unribbed outer wall; the inner flesh is red. Seeds have an orange, vein-marked aril.
