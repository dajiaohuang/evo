## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-619db24970}
COL26.8 records Emydocephalus annulatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e0e16269d1}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-694c8d9c77}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cogger 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-00397d894e}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3.7. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The row reports 1.5 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-9d13605580}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
