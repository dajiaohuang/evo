## A solitary disc-shaped cactus with yellow flowers {#s-3dfc6a5eb1}
The Portuguese description calls this a small, solitary cactus with a disc-shaped stem, slightly tuberculate ribs and distinct areoles. Spines range from fine, pale and inconspicuous to bristly; a curved central spine is present. Flowers arise at or near the stem tip. They are yellow, with pointed petals and a red or vinaceous stigma; the dry, light-brown fruit contains black seeds with strongly tuberculate coats. Rock-associated habitat is recorded.
