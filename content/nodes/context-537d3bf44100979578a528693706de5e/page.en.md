## The source concept {#s-acb4ea093e}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Rhinella centralis* as row Anf0818, in the genus Rhinella, family Bufonidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a75cf7cca5}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 30 g. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass or microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not been checked against that revision.
