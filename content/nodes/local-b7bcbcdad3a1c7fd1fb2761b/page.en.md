## The source tadpole concept {#s-66882af84d}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Telmatobufo bullocki, in the genus Telmatobufo and family Calyptocephalellidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Telmatobufo bullocki. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-e66291e08e}
The compilation has three records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 26 and 28. These are labels attached to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; three records do not necessarily represent three unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
