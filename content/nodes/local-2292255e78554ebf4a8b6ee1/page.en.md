## The deposited caecilian record {#s-db4329cd20}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Caecilia macrodonta in species-level row 24, under genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. Individual, population and species data are kept separate in the compilation. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Measurement material {#s-97b163e5b7}
This source concept has 2 measurement rows: 2 individual-level rows, 0 population-level rows and 0 species-level rows. These counts describe table rows; they are not population estimates or counts of unique specimens. Specimen identifiers, sex codes and references remain attached to their rows. Comparisons of body dimensions need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-7f1004f54a}
The species-level row lists primary fold count: 127; secondary fold count: 0. These are deposited species-level values, which may be summaries rather than integer measurements of one animal; they do not establish invariant diagnostic counts for every individual.
