## Lessingianthus bishopii {#s-5d0d7c6b3a}
## Brown wool lines the lower leaf surface

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and stems that are unbranched or sparsely branched, with leaves along their length. Oblong, leathery leaves differ in colour between faces. Their edges are scalloped or toothed; the upper face may be hairless or brown-woolly, while the lower is brown-woolly. Tips range from acute to blunt or rounded; bases are blunt or rounded.

Solitary heads may be sessile or stalked, with bell-shaped involucres and 4–6 rows of bracts. The source lists 60, 70 or 80 florets. Cylindrical dry fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
