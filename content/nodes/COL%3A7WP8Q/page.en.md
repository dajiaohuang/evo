Mean beak length is 62.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width at that point averages 20.9 mm and depth 26 mm. These standardized measurements are not identification cutoffs.

Mean unflattened wing length (carpal joint to longest primary) is 129 mm; mean tarsus is 33 mm. AVONET separately lists mean mass of 146 g in the Dunning category, not a weight for every measured bird.

## Evidence limits {#s-f6607dca43}
AVONET measurements are historical sample-based species averages; mass is separately sourced. Habitat and migration are broad codes, while the latitude endpoints describe historical breeding/resident maps. EltonTraits reports literature-based relative diet and foraging weights, not direct measurements or identified prey.
