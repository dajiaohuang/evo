## Accepted name and placement {#s-13781d47d0}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus guttilatus (Cope, 1879) at species rank in Eleutherodactylus (Eleutherodactylinae, Eleutherodactylidae), in the release dated 20 August 2026.

## Reading route {#s-5a0001adde}
This page brings together an ITIS label and a historical AmphiBIO row. Treat both as source-specific records; the AmphiBIO concept has not been reconciled with the current catalogue entry.

## Recorded geographic labels {#s-0f38680509}
The pinned ITIS record for TSN 550239 lists “Middle America” and “North America.” These are textual labels rather than mapped range limits or dated observations. ITIS leaves distribution status unspecified, so the labels do not establish origin status.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-78bf99ebbc}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 row codes fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic foraging. The categories are broad and may describe different settings; a later TetrapodTraits notice reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries. Whether this row is affected has not been verified.

## Adult food categories — AmphiBIO source {#s-a0589d0659}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines literature records based on expert judgment, observation or stomach contents; the category does not give a complete diet or measured proportions.

## Activity periods — AmphiBIO source {#s-fa33d1619e}
Night is the row’s activity category. This broad code is not a continuous record of activity across seasons or days.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-3a699af3b3}
The historical compilation reports maximum adult mass of 2 g and maximum length of 32 mm; in an anuran, length means snout–vent length. A later TetrapodTraits notice reports corrections to some inherited body-mass entries, so the 2 g value requires row-level review before reuse. Both values are compiled maxima, not ranges for all individuals.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-284a8872cc}
The row gives maturity-size limits of 20–25 mm, 5–12 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most one reproductive event per year, and a 4 mm minimum and maximum offspring-or-egg size. The source does not resolve whether clutch counts or size refer to eggs or offspring, nor give sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions. These are compiled extrema, not universal values.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-5cf4c20f30}
The archived source marks direct development. The category alone does not specify parental care, breeding site or timing.
