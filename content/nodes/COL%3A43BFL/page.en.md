## Mikania neblinensis {#s-68666d30d7}
## Silky undersides and smooth fruits

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records twining, silky branches; stem pith was not observed. Opposite, ovate leaves have entire margins, rounded bases and acute tips. The upper surface is smooth, while the lower is silky; the somewhat leathery leaves have pinnate veins that loop near the edge.

Loose panicles carry stalked heads. Small, lance-shaped bracts sit at the top of each head stalk; the oblong to oblanceolate bracts around a head are finely hairy and acute. The bell-shaped corolla has smooth, triangular lobes as long as the tube. The dry fruits (cypselae) are sparsely hairy. No habitat field is recorded here, and the description gives no locality or complete range.
