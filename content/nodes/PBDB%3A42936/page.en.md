## Basilosaurids form a sampled late Eocene radiation {#s-698070baff}
Uhen’s synthesis describes a sampled late Eocene radiation of fully aquatic archaeocetes, including several distinct basilosaurid genera. The 41–34 Ma interval bounds the reviewed family sample, not exact global first and last appearances. Family membership does not place every basilosaurid on the direct ancestry of Neoceti.
