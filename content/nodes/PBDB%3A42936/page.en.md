## How PBDB organizes fossil occurrences {#s-bc93607b37}
PBDB groups the selected fossil-occurrence records for *Basilosauridae* under its recorded classification path and links them to collections, strata, age brackets, and references. This hierarchy organizes database records; it remains distinct from COL taxonomy and is not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Named basilosaurid occurrences and collections {#s-0c46a90eb8}
The route links records for taxa such as *Bartoncetus wanklyni*, *Perucetus colossus*, *Saghacetus osiris* and *Supayacetus muizoni* with their named collections, strata and age brackets. Read the qualification and source for each record; occurrence and collection units do not estimate ancient population size.

## How to interpret the displayed age brackets {#s-4dd65bbf4c}
These PBDB age brackets bound the registered occurrences shown here. They do not by themselves establish a taxon’s global first or last appearance or a clade’s origin or extinction.

## Basilosaurids form a sampled late Eocene radiation {#s-698070baff}
Uhen’s synthesis describes a sampled late Eocene radiation of fully aquatic archaeocetes, including several distinct basilosaurid genera. The 41–34 Ma interval bounds the reviewed family sample, not exact global first and last appearances. Family membership does not place every basilosaurid on the direct ancestry of Neoceti.
