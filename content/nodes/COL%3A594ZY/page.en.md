## Red and pink flowers with a yellow throat mark {#s-db89cb03cd}
Tritonia disticha is 0.25–0.80 m tall, with zygomorphic red, pink or orange-red flowers. The lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals), especially the median one, have a small yellow blotch outlined in red; the flora reports flowering from August to April.

## Montane and forest grasslands {#s-1e537f3055}
The regional account records montane and forest grasslands and lists Sub-Escarpment Grassland, Drakensberg Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Savanna, Indigenous Forests, Indian Ocean Coastal Belt and Albany Thicket.
