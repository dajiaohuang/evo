## Bobartia parva {#s-8362396d33}
The 2012 southern African flora account describes Bobartia parva as a slender rhizomatous perennial, 15–40 cm tall, with cylindrical leaves and a small yellow inflorescence made up of several cymes. It records flowering from November through February and moist mountain slopes as habitat. Its reported range is the Langeberg area from Swellendam to Lemoenshoek. These statements summarize that regional account; they do not define a current global range or population-level flowering pattern.
