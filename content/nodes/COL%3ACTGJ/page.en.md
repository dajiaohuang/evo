## Ammocharis longifolia {#s-a14bbeb91a}
Ammocharis longifolia differs from A. coranica in the regional account by reaching 40 cm and by fruits that are six-ribbed and spindle-shaped rather than smooth and nearly spherical. As the fruit ripens, its stalks stiffen and lengthen; the detached fruiting head can tumble in wind, releasing the large seeds through the papery fruit walls. Flowers open in succession and change from white or pink to deeper pink as they age. Sandy or stony flats and slopes are recorded habitat.
