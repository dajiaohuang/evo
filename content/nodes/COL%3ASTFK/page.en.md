## Feeding research {#s-06ccb8f591}
A June 2022–May 2023 study compared six groups, one in each management zone of Bonga and Saja forests in Ethiopia's Kafa Biosphere Reserve. Instantaneous scans recorded 34 food-plant species across the two sites (27 at Bonga, 24 at Saja, with 17 shared). Fruit-bearing trees accounted for 83.7% of feeding scans. Feeding and movement proportions differed among the buffer and transition zones. This is a comparison of six groups at two forests, not a range-wide test of disturbance effects.
