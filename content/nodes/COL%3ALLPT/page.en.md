## What distinguishes Bernardia spartioides in the record? {#s-e13b26c3ec}
This species is recorded as dioecious. Its papery leaves are narrow and lance-shaped or triangular, with smooth edges, embedded glands, and stiff hairs beneath. Male flowers form clusters of one to three cymes under boat-shaped bracts; female flowers form rounded clusters of three to seven. The flower-cluster stalks are sessile or nearly so. Male flowers have six to fifteen stamens and stalks over 1 mm; female flowers are sessile, with undivided styles. The fruit is sessile.
