## A two-lobed leaf and an opening calyx {#s-322466e247}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes a two-lobed leaf with a heart-shaped base and a rounded to nearly flat tip; the underside has fine hairs pressed close to the surface. As the flower opens, its calyx splits into two to five lobes. The record also describes linear petals and a legume that opens elastically. Some flower traits are listed as alternatives in the source, so the row is not a full account of within-species variation.
