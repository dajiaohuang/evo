## Pterocarpus dubius {#s-93bdaf45d4}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Pterocarpus dubius* as an accepted species of *Pterocarpus* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists a single-leaflet leaf and simple axillary racemes (flower clusters arranged along a stalk). Its fruit is described as a drupe, with the source listing either remaining wing tissue or water dispersal; those alternatives are retained as reported.
