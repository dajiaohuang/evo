## A tiny terrestrial frog known from Maharira {#s-f1765c55d5}
The 2019 description knew this terrestrial frog only from Maharira in Ranomafana National Park, Madagascar. Its single known specimen was an adult male collected on 25 January 2004 at a campsite at about 1248 m elevation and measured 11.3 mm snout–vent length. That measurement describes one male, not a sampled adult size range. The authors explicitly reported very little natural-history information and recommended Data Deficient while more range and ecological data were needed.

## A tentative assignment of morning leaf-litter calls {#s-650bc5431e}
Calls recorded at 08:45 on 26 January 2004 in primary-rainforest litter at Maharira were tentatively assigned to this species. The holotype had been found where similar calls were heard and had an apparently partly inflated vocal sac, but the recorded caller was unidentified. The calls followed cyclonic rainfall and retreating flooding. The authors emphasized similarity to Stumpffia miery and the need for confirmation; these recordings therefore are not presented as securely identified species calls or evidence of a fixed rain-triggered behavior.
