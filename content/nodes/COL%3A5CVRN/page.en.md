## Zamia poeppigiana: morphology and habitat in a regional flora {#s-7578fa8d3d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology entry describes an aerial stem, prickles on the petioles and no petiolules. It records the leaf texture as subcoriaceous/coriaceous (subleathery/leathery) and the shape as lanceolate.

These are the statements in one regional dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The same dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
