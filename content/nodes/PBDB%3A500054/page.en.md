## atlas-profile / overview {#s-3c2af394ce}
Xiushanosteus mirabilis is anchored by an articulated holotype and more than twenty referred bodies from the Chongqing Lagerstätte. Its small, armoured body and mosaic skull roof contribute to a Telychian record of early jawed vertebrates.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-a49f86d6c6}
IVPP V300001 and referred IVPP V300002–V300021 preserve articulated anatomy. The sample does not establish a global first appearance, direct ancestry or a complete history of placoderms.

## Armour preserved in articulated fossils {#s-6069f99fd9}
Holotype IVPP V300001 and referred specimens IVPP V300002–V300021 preserve articulated bodies about 30 mm long, including cranial armour. Their skull roofs combine character states described as a mosaic in the study.

## A window onto an early jawed-vertebrate sample {#s-f66aec9a55}
The fossils come from the Telychian Huixingshao Formation and are interpreted as a jawed stem gnathostome. A Lagerstätte can preserve many individuals from one setting; that concentration does not make Xiushanosteus a direct ancestor or the first jawed vertebrate worldwide. The formation’s depositional setting is not a direct measure of preferred habitat.
