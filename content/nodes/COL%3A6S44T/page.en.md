## Two-armed hairs and a finely hairy leaf underside {#s-f1f7a01d84}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records sympodial growth, where later growth continues from side shoots, and two-armed stem hairs. Leaves are elliptic or oblong; the upper midrib is channelled toward the stalk and flattened toward the tip. The underside is puberulent, with very fine short hairs. Bracts or cataphylls below leaf nodes are deciduous. These states are listed in a regional field without frequencies.

## An urn-shaped flower cup and a marcescent fruit calyx {#s-8085b74ff4}
Small flowering branches are compressed, with bracteoles beneath flowers that fall away. The field lists two or three axillary inflorescences per shoot, arranged pyramidal and opposite. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal; the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup is urn-shaped and silky-haired. Sepals are partly fused, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens partly by tearing. In fruit, the calyx lobes wither but remain attached (marcescent); the fruit is globose.
