## Recorded parent of Nesodontinae {#s-a8ac83c1b5}
Nesodontinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Toxodontidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
