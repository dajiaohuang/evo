## Hyphessobrycon peugeotorum {#s-d611ba8811}
## Fin counts and varied collection sites

This page uses the accepted name *H. peugeotorum*; the 2013 treatment uses *H. peugeoti*, which the catalogue links to the same accepted usage. In that treatment, mature males had an elongated, threadlike dorsal fin, and living fish were described as red, compared with golden *H. loweae*. The diagnosis reports five horizontal scale rows from the dorsal-fin origin to the lateral line and 21–24 branched anal-fin rays (most often 22); *H. loweae* had six to seven rows and 17–21 rays (most often 20). These are comparative counts from examined material, not population-wide averages. The morphometric table covers 24 specimens; scale and fin-ray counts have character-specific sample sizes.

The treatment reports collections in water less than one metre deep during the early dry season, but the sites differed. The type locality was a swampy, deforested roadside area beside preserved forest; MNRJ 29609 came from below a recently breached dam; the single MNRJ 29503 specimen came from a clear, flowing stream with a sandy bottom and sparse aquatic vegetation; and named paratypes (other specimens in the original type series) came from a flooded area beside the rio Juruena. These records describe collection settings, not one habitat preferred across the species.
