## One basal leaf and pale flowers {#s-e273a54c43}
Romulea rupestris grows 150–300 mm tall and has a single basal leaf. Its white to pale-mauve flowers have a pale-yellow cup; the membranous bract (a modified, leaf-like structure associated with a flower) margins are flecked with brown.

## Rocky mountain slopes {#s-e6d6d6c52b}
The source records rocky mountain slopes as habitat.
