## Philodendron micranthum: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-3073f7e32e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a prophyll on the stem. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole conspicuous. Leaf blades are elliptic or oblanceolate, with acuminate tips and cuneate bases; lateral veins are obscure when the blade is green, and the margin is entire.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium. Its short peduncle is shorter than the slightly constricted, oblong spathe. The outer tube is cream when young, the inner tube greenish cream, and the outer blade whitish. The spadix is subsessile. The entry records 3 stamens, an unknown staminode state, a prismatic gynoecium, an ovoid ovary, 5 or 6 locules and 1 ovule; placentation is basal, while stigma, fruit and seed are unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The alternative locule counts and unknown source states are retained.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
