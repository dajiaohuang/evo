## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-542de6296d}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Sporophila funerea (P. L. Sclater, 1860) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Separate total birds from complete sets {#s-edeb9c0b0e}
Morphology comprises 33 birds: 15 female, 15 male and 3 unsexed; 4 complete sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 9.3 mm, tarsus 17.4 mm, unflattened wing 55 mm, tail 48.7 mm and hand-wing index 15.4 are historical row means. Total birds exceed complete sets; not every bird has a full set of fields. Inference=NO does not replace each field’s sampling denominator. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed; beak length supplies neither bite force nor the ability to process particular foods.

## Mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-5d4b120d4c}
Mass=13 g with Mass.Source=Dunning is a compiled species-average body mass incorporating both sexes; the 33 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book, weighed individuals, localities and dates were not read, leaving sex, seasonal and regional mass ranges unsupported. Mass and beak length do not establish feeding mechanics.

## Read seed niche and trophic level separately {#s-042d376cbc}
Trophic.Niche=Granivore is defined as at least 60% of food resources from seeds or nuts. Trophic.Level=Herbivore means at least 70% from plants. These are separate category definitions, not measured proportions for this species. Underlying diet papers were not read; seed species, seasonal diets, shelling actions and beak bite force remain unsupported.

## Diet does not replace primary lifestyle {#s-96ac564fa4}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time perching on branches, vegetation or other raised substrates in the source definition. Habitat=Human Modified; density code 2 denotes semi-open vegetation, not bird abundance. Migration=2 is partially migratory, which can include a minority moving long distances, most birds making short-distance movements, nomadism or distinct altitudinal migration; the row does not specify which applies. A shared food niche does not establish shared foraging strata, social activity or seasonal movements. Historical map fields are uninterpreted here and do not supply current range-wide distributions or movement routes.
