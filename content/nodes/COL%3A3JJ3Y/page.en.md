## Hammeria cedarbergensis {#s-138ec96a09}
## Two leaf pairs spread and rise from a tiny succulent

The Greater Cape flora describes a dwarf succulent up to 6 cm tall. The outer leaf pair spreads outward, while the second pair stands erect with the leaves pressed together. Each three-sided leaf reaches 18 mm long and 3–4 mm wide and thick.

Solitary pale-pink flowers are 20–27 mm across, with sterile stamens gathered into a central cone. The fruit has five chambers and a flat top. The habitat record is arid fynbos at 850 m.
