## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ec778125d3}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis major as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-0a1d348690}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1300 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-af67cc450c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Mirtschin et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a9f98fa806}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-6d16e98fba}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
