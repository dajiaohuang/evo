## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded for Sauvagesia capillaris? {#s-e351751609}
The woody stem does not branch from the base. Whorled leaves on short shoots are 5–20 mm long, oblanceolate, and crenulate; round, stalkless glands line their margins. Secondary veins are conspicuous. Terminal pyramidal panicles bear long lateral racemes. Flowers have upright pedicels and sepals without marginal glands. The pink, star-shaped corolla has open petals that do not overlap. Outer staminodes are absent; inner staminodes are fused and pink with white bases. The two anthers have small points.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f759b8e2b4}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
