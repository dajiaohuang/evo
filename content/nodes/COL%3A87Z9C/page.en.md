## Begonia solitudinis {#s-e8a0d60801}
## Scale-haired petioles on a creeping plant

Begonia solitudinis has prostrate stems and persistent stipules. Scale-shaped hairs cover the petioles and lower leaf surfaces, while the upper surfaces are smooth. The strongly asymmetric, lobed basal leaves are transversely ovate, with ciliate or wavy margins. Male flower segments bear simple hairs; female segments are hairless. Capsule wings are reduced to rudiments, and the seed tip is blunt. The habitat is rocky.
