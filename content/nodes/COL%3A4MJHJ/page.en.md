## A high elevation frog with a tentative group placement {#s-4f68606f22}
The authors described Pristimantis mariaelenae from humid puna at 3,596 m in Cañaris, northwestern Peru. They tentatively assigned the new species to the P. orestes Group. The group placement is presented as provisional in the abstract, so it should not be read as a settled classification.
