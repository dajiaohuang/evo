## Accepted name and placement {#s-e090877fe0}
COL26.8 accepts Brachycephalus olivaceus Bornschein, Morato, Firkowski, Ribeiro & Pie, 2015 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brachycephalidae (family) → Brachycephalus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Sex-specific measurements retain variable-specific sample counts {#s-bf96c652da}
In the 2025 comparative table, male snout–vent length for Brachycephalus olivaceus is 8.9–10.0 (N = 15) mm and female length is 11.0–12.6 (N = 4) mm, with N retained separately for each sex. Dorsal skin is densely rough (n = 19) and lateral skin densely rough (n = 19). N refers to animals observed for each variable, so the size, skin and colour denominators cannot be pooled into extra specimens. These examined ranges are not whole-species maxima, a statistical proof of population sexual dimorphism or a growth series. Smooth skin in this study can include small, inconspicuous warts; external digit absence does not alone establish skeletal absence.

## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-dfef97151d}
Green with orange on the chin and central belly or scattered ventral orange dots; preserved areas are dark and cream. These are the authors’ comparative observations rather than a standardized colour-chart measurement. Colour and specimen counts vary by trait; similar colours recur among species, so a single hue is not a complete identification rule. Preserved appearance should not be substituted for live appearance.
