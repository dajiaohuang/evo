## Evidence limits {#s-e37a794ba6}
AVONET morphology values are sample averages, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from a separately cited literature or weighing source; the four measured birds must not be assumed to be the mass sample. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, and the geographic summary covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. Historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name support association but do not prove that species concepts remained equivalent through later splits or lumps. Other sources are needed for reproduction, current populations and conservation status.
