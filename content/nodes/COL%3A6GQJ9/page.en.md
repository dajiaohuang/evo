## Limits of these trait data {#s-b9e282fc1c}
This is a supplemental trait account, not a complete morphology or ecology treatment. ReptTraits compresses sex, age, locality and population variation into one row under its 2023 framework. Reported maxima are not absolute size limits, and the clutch mean is not a fixed count. Estimated mass is not presented as direct measurement. Unreported traits remain gaps; conservation, venom, current range and population trends require other sources. Workbook citations preserve the source chain but do not imply independent review of every underlying paper.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-51d73ae543}
ReptTraits records oviparity and a consolidated mean of 22 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch. The count summarizes source data, not every clutch; oviparity does not establish embryo nutrition.

## One skull in a comparative CT study {#s-1a5d17a4b7}
Skull MNHN.ZA.AC 1946-71 was included in Martín-Jiménez and Pérez-García's (2021) comparative neuroanatomy study. Their reconstruction describes a concave anterior dorsal cranial-cavity surface associated with expansion of the olfactory region, with the cavity extending posteriorly. These observations concern the examined skull.
