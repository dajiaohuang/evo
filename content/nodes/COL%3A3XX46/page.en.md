## What does the record say about Manulea acutiloba? {#s-11a1c4224d}
This account describes a small annual, 3–11 cm tall, with glandular hairs and elliptic leaves near the base. Its flowers form racemes. The corolla tube is 4–5.5 mm long, and the lobes split deeply at their tips. The flowers are usually white, though the source marks blue or mauve as uncertain; a yellow, star-shaped patch surrounds the mouth and extends down the tube.

## How does it differ from Manulea silenoides? {#s-cdb0436721}
The source distinguishes it by narrower calyx lobes, hairs concentrated near their edges, and more deeply cut corolla-lobe tips. The hair pattern on two stamen filaments can vary in M. acutiloba, from absent to dense; in M. silenoides those filaments are consistently bearded. The account notes that two plants with hairless filaments were deliberately chosen as the type specimen.

## What is known about its ecology? {#s-a9b9af7fa8}
The source explicitly says the species’ ecology is not known. No habitat is inferred here.
