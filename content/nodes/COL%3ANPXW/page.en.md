## Bulbine asphodeloides: basal rosette and yellow flowers {#s-2c1a78beb3}
A South African regional account describes a succulent perennial herb, stemless or nearly so, with a rhizome at the base. Dark-green leaves form a basal rosette; each blade is half-round in cross-section and up to 0.57 m long. Several inflorescences rise upright or curve. Their racemes are loose to somewhat compact. White bracts have threadlike tails at the tips, slightly ear-shaped bases and deeply divided margins, with hairs toward the base. When flowers are absent, the pedicels curve back or coil. The perianth is yellow. The recorded flowering period spans January through December. The capsule is not inflated.
