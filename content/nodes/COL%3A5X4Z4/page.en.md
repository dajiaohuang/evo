## Flowers, leaf divisions and seed pods {#s-23903b88f4}
The Flora of Panama describes Caesalpinia pulcherrima as a lightly armed shrub or small tree. Its twice-pinnate leaves can carry eight or more pairs of pinnae, with about ten pairs of leaflets on each; the terminal flower clusters bear red to yellow blossoms. The flattened pods can reach 12 cm and open elastically. This description comes from a historical regional flora record.

## Cultivation and probable native range {#s-aa4ce9b23b}
The same Flora of Panama source says the species is widely cultivated or escaped across tropical regions worldwide and is probably native to northern Central America. The source itself presents native status as probable; the statement should not be turned into a confirmed native range or a current global inventory.
