## What flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-5835fbb98a}
The leaves are symmetrical and ovate. Flowers may grow singly or in clusters. The calyx enlarges after flowering and becomes net-veined and papery. Each fruit has five smooth, unornamented segments (mericarps).

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-03c0fe82ba}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
