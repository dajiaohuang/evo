## Start with the type species {#s-a35de863ca}
Haliskia is based on its type species, H. peterseni, described in 2024 from partial skeleton KK F1426. The catalogue places the genus in Anhangueridae, while the paper compares several phylogenetic matrices and leaves finer relationships uncertain. A genus account and a species entry are separate from the PBDB accepted-species count.

## Separate measurements from reconstruction {#s-f4c7dea09c}
The linked H. peterseni account begins with the specimen and its stratigraphy, then considers tooth and hyoid measurements, estimated wingspan, tree analyses and feeding proposals. Bone lengths are measured observations; a complete wingspan is reconstructed from comparisons, and prey control or niche partitioning are hypotheses. Results about eggs, integument or flight in other pterosaurs provide comparison, not direct evidence about Haliskia.
