## Ponds and seasonal water holes in the island survey {#s-1b5e71d2fb}
On Ko Pha-ngan, surveyors sometimes encountered Hoplobatrachus chinensis in or beside small ponds and seasonal water holes used by buffalo in marshy fallow fields. They detected frogs in 2 of 8 settlement survey areas, 2 of 13 disturbed-forest areas, and 2 of 11 national-park forest areas. The account also notes island farming and the species’ presence in food markets, but it does not determine whether each frog found in a field was wild-origin or came from farming.
