## Name and this data account {#s-6e7b5b3247}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts Mauremys annamensis in Geoemydidae. This account follows its ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and trait-specific sources. Because the dataset has one row per species, its categories describe that record rather than every population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-80bca7713b}
The row classifies the microhabitat as Aquatic and activity as Nocturnal. These are broad categories, not a complete habitat inventory or a fixed schedule; the one-row summary cannot show variation among populations, seasons or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-280f33514b}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode and temperature-dependent sex determination (TSD). These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or show that every population shares the same pattern. Clutch and litter counts remain uninterpreted here.

## Evidence limits {#s-7c50a7d88c}
This is a small supplemental trait account, not a complete treatment of morphology or ecology. It paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 framework and individual trait sources. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; a matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later taxonomic concepts. Reported maxima are not absolute length limits. Many dataset mass values are estimated from length equations and are not treated here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions and are not used in the conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated; missing fields remain gaps. Citation labels preserve the source chain but do not imply independent review here of every original work.

## Traits not interpreted here {#s-ade2b86d1a}
Four traits have field-specific source labels and are interpreted here.
