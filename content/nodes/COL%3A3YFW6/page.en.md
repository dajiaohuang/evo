## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-6e7b5b3247}
COL26.8 records Mauremys annamensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Geoemydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-80bca7713b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-280f33514b}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
