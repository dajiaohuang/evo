## Accepted name and placement {#s-d1d138e82c}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus johnstonei Barbour, 1914 at species rank in Eleutherodactylus (Eleutherodactylinae, Eleutherodactylidae), in the release dated 20 August 2026.

## Reading route {#s-e88c701511}
This page combines historical AmphiBIO categories with two selected thermal estimates from AmphiTherm. The sources retain separate concepts and methods; the estimates are not a species-wide temperature profile.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-ea61efb58c}
The Frost 2011 AmphiBIO row codes terrestrial and arboreal foraging. These broad categories do not quantify habitat use. A later TetrapodTraits notice reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; the row-level effect is unverified.

## Adult food categories — AmphiBIO source {#s-b679e0f72e}
Arthropods are listed as an adult food category. AmphiBIO compiles literature classifications based on expert judgment, observation or stomach contents, so this does not describe a complete diet or its proportions.

## Activity periods — AmphiBIO source {#s-252b745d24}
The source classifies activity as nocturnal. This is a broad category rather than a time series.

## Seasonal activity — AmphiBIO source {#s-0f5cc65534}
The source marks wet and warm seasonal conditions. These categories compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-220e83c96b}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult mass of 24 g and maximum length of 35 mm; for an anuran, length is snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima. A later correction notice covers some inherited body-mass entries, but this row has not been checked against it; the 24 g value needs review before reuse.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-49ef0c40fb}
The historical row gives maturity-size limits of 18–24 mm, 10–30 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most four reproductive events per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 2.7–3 mm. The compilation does not identify whether clutch counts and size values refer to eggs or offspring, or specify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-465d76cd18}
The source marks direct development. This code alone does not specify parental care, breeding place or timing.

## Selected critical upper thermal limit estimate — AmphiTherm source {#s-78c0f14907}
AmphiTherm row 3711 (ID 4052; Pough et al. 1977) reports an adult CTmax of 39.2 °C. CTmax means critical thermal maximum; the source labels this an “other reported endpoint.” The recorded acclimation temperature is 20 °C and the heating or cooling rate is 0.75 °C/min. Its n_trait field is 10 and its uncertainty is 0.47 °C standard error. The test medium is recorded as water; sex is unknown and origin is wild. Chemical, hormone, infection and pathogen treatment fields are NA, meaning unreported rather than confirmed absent.

## Selected thermal preference estimate — AmphiTherm source {#s-599acabbed}
AmphiTherm row 3717 (ID 4058; Pough et al. 1977) reports an adult Tpref of 25.7 °C. Tpref denotes the source’s temperature-preference measure, but its endpoint is unspecified. Acclimation is recorded at 20 °C; n_trait is 49 and uncertainty is 0.67 °C standard error. The test medium is recorded as ambient; sex is unknown and origin is wild. Chemical, hormone, infection and pathogen treatment fields are NA, meaning unreported rather than confirmed absent.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-575ef338cb}
The AmphiBIO Frost 2011 concept and both AmphiTherm source estimates are not reconciled with the current catalogue concept; the Pough et al. 1977 studies have not been independently reanalysed. The n_trait fields belong to separate rows and must not be summed across repeated cohorts. NA treatment fields mean unreported. CTmax and Tpref are selected source estimates, not species-wide limits or husbandry guidance.
