## An annual with rhombic leaves {#s-4c131eca04}
The description records an upright annual herb up to 1 m tall, with sparse branches and opposite, papery rhombic leaves. The leaves have small scallops above their widest point, and their veins arise prominently from the base. Lavender flower heads contain about 25 florets; mature black fruits carry around 25 slender bristles.
