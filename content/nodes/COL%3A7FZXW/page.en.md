## Which traits appear in its smooth leaves and round spike? {#s-0549d51ce4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records *Xyris cachimbensis* with leaves in two ranks or nearly so. The cylindrical leaf blades are smooth or dotted and hairless. Its flower stalk is 20–50 cm long, cylindrical and without prominent ridges; the spike is nearly spherical and contains 10–25 flowers. The entry lists alternatives for the number and margins of sterile bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers).
