## What features define this small shrub? {#s-d52c09ba78}
Gosela eckloniana is described as a shrublet up to 80 cm tall, with slender branches that angle upward. Narrow leaves grow in tufts. Compact flower spikes bear flowers with long, slender, light-brown corolla tubes.

## Where does it grow? {#s-b86bf7bbe1}
The habitat field names sandstone slopes, but gives no collection locality.
