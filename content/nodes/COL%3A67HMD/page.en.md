## Anthurium bocainense: a regional morphology record {#s-2014c1fbc7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at the stem tip and a fibrous stem base. Petioles are marked smooth, though the entry's compound petiole-shape phrase is difficult to parse. Leaf blades are entire and flat, ovate, with acuminate or acute tips and cordate bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is coded as acute above and obtuse below; secondary veins are prominent above, and a collecting vein is present all along the margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle. Its boat-shaped, elliptic spathe forms an acute angle with the peduncle; a stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. The berries are described as green.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The compound petiole-shape phrase remains unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
