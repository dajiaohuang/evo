## Heliophila variabilis {#s-126039dd87}
## A spreading annual with divided leaves and narrow fruits

The Greater Cape flora account describes a bright-green, finely hairy annual that spreads across the ground and reaches 35 cm. Its stipulate leaves are deeply divided along the midrib. The flowers are white, and the ovules number 16–26.

The fruits are narrow, partly bead-like, and 20–40 mm long. The habitat record is dry sandy slopes.
