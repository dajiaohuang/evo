## Calydorea charruana {#s-dc0f623a99}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description, Calydorea charruana is characterized by an underground bulb, keeled leaf blades and a terminal inflorescence with stalked rhipidia. The external and internal bracts are reported as 19–24 mm and 24–34 mm long, respectively. Its bluish-violet flowers lack glandular hairs; the anthers are about 4–5 mm long and the style is undivided, with an 8–10 mm undivided portion. These are the dataset’s regional diagnostic fields, not a global or population-wide survey.
