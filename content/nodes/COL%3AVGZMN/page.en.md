## Color is not enough for identification {#s-3a301f0699}
Green and pink forms are recorded, with long antennae, legs, and cornicles. The source account points to narrow dark bands on antennal segments as a clue for distinguishing the similar blue alfalfa aphid. Color by itself is not enough to identify the species.

## Observations from California alfalfa {#s-5f7c482af1}
In California alfalfa, observations describe sap feeding and honeydew, along with predation by lady beetles and other natural enemies. This agricultural setting offers examples of the aphid's interactions, not a complete host-plant list or range.
