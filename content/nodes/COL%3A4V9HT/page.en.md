## What young-branch and flower-bract traits identify Schistostemon retusum? {#s-7d9dcb91db}
Young branches are hairless. The leaves are 4.5–10 cm wide and have extremely short stalks, 0–1 mm long. Inflorescences are arranged alternately, and their bracts are oval or oval-oblong.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5991729cb7}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
