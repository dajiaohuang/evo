## Accepted name and placement {#s-009442381b}
COL26.8 records Craugastor brocchi (Boulenger in Brocchi, 1882) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; they do not establish equivalence with historical source concepts.

## Reading route {#s-284659af63}
The catalogue placement identifies the species entry. AmphiBIO supplies a historical set of broad traits, not a current, independently verified account.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-a4d2d72593}
The Frost 2011 AmphiBIO row marks terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. These are broad categories rather than measures of habitat use, and several may be coded for one source record. A later revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian microhabitat and body-mass entries; this row-level effect has not been checked, and the historical concept may not match the current species.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-283d8fb271}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult length of 83.3 mm for its anuran row, meaning snout–vent length. This is a compiled maximum, not the range of individuals or a fresh specimen measurement.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-4cd0195206}
The row records a maximum of one reproductive event per year. The compilation does not attach the count to a specified sex, population, date or wild/captive condition; offspring-size fields may include eggs.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-835286031c}
Direct development is flagged for the historical source concept. That code does not tell us the breeding location, care of offspring or seasonal schedule.
