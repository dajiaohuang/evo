## What branch, leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-5c42353e13}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records erect or prostrate branches. Leaves are petiolate, oval-lance-shaped or oblong, with glandular hairs on both faces, flat margins and three to five veins. Flowers are solitary and axillary. The inner tip of the floral cup is hairless. Petals are pink, anthers are straight, and toothed appendages between the sepals are absent. The capsule is shorter than the floral cup, and each chamber contains many seeds. These alternatives come from a regional dataset field.

## What rocky habitat is listed? {#s-c4c12daebd}
One Brazilian Flora row records rupicolous growth on rock surfaces, without a locality or date.

## Is terrestrial habitat also listed? {#s-4c87aa6c08}
A second row in the same dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial. The two entries provide categories, not their relative frequency.
