## A dwarf blue-flowered species {#s-4f8bd4bb2b}
Moraea nubigena is only 3–5 cm tall, with a single leaf and fleeting blue-mauve flowers. The source notes its chromosome number, 2n = 20, and an isolated position in molecular studies; its placement remains provisional.
