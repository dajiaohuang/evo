## Begonia polyandra {#s-dda7a75627}
## Hairless upper leaves and unequal capsule wings

Begonia polyandra has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its slightly asymmetric basal leaves are lance-shaped and serrated; the upper surface is hairless, while simple hairs occur beneath and on the petiole. Male flower segments bear simple hairs, whereas female segments are glandular and crenulate at the margin. Bracteoles are present. Capsule wings are strongly unequal and the seed tip is blunt. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
