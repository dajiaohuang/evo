## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-e14c671bd1}
The archive marks the species-level foraging-position estimates as 80% in or just above the canopy and 20% in trees or tall bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy. These percentages estimate relative feeding-position use. They do not describe nesting, all habitat or a full day's activity. The source defines surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial positions separately; they are not elevation ranges and cannot give flight heights or habitat area.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-95ba76fa66}
This is a reader's guide to a historical EltonTraits 1.0 record matched by name and family, not a full account of the species. The match does not prove that the older species concept equals COL26.8. Food and bird feeding-position percentages are coarse relative weights standardized from written literature, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy use or observation time. Bird diet codes A/B retain uncertainty in source and proportion. Mammal ABC diet or activity codes may also use a congener considered ecologically similar by the compilers; this page does not claim every field is a direct observation of *M. tristis*. Genus- or family-level typical values, bird strata not identified as species-level and split records needing review do not support definite conclusions. The underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Other sources are needed for individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation.
