## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Cnidoscolus hamosus? {#s-62865cdb74}
The leaves are undivided, not lobed, with uniform color, entire margins, and no glands on the leaf stalk or edge. The androecium has four or more whorls of stamens. Male flowers have bell-shaped perianths; in female flowers, the perianth parts are separate. The fruit is an ellipsoid capsule, and the seed bears a caruncle, a small fleshy outgrowth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-0b6cfcf7dc}
It is categorized as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
