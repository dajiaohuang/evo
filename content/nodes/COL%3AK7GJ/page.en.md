## Baccharis oreophila {#s-28a08a4ee0}
## Three-veined leaves and clustered heads

The flora fields describe sessile, lance-shaped leaves with three main veins and serrated margins. Branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated; no xylopodium, a woody underground base, is recorded. Flower heads form racemes or dense clusters. The source lists heads with either tubular male florets only or threadlike female florets only. Habitat labels include rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
