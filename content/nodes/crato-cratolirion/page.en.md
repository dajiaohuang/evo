## Cratolirion preserved from roots to reproductive organs {#s-58ae2506c8}
A Cratolirion bognerianum fossil from Brazil’s Crato Formation, about 115 Ma, preserves roots, narrow parallel-veined leaves, vascular anatomy and three-part reproductive organs in one plant. The combined anatomy supports a crown-monocot placement; it does not date the origin of monocots or establish membership in a living lily family.
