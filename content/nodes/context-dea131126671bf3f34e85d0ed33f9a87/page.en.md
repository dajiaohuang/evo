## The source concept {#s-4271a27602}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Alsodes monticola as row Anf1356, classified in Alsodes, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-564f77f70a}
The row marks terrestrial and aquatic foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage variation in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against those revisions; verify the historical categories before reuse. No replacement values are inferred, and the notice does not imply that every original entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a5241da919}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 49.1 mm for Alsodes monticola. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
