## *Dendropanax nebulosus*: narrow leaves and compound umbels {#s-e537540288}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description lists narrow elliptic to lance-shaped leaves with visible speckling underneath. Their margins are entire or finely toothed near the tip, and a pair of veins rises above the base. The compound umbels have four to seven secondary branches and up to 30 flowers each.

The ovary is recorded as having four or five chambers, with flower stalks up to 7 mm. The habitat field says terrestrial. These character alternatives belong to a regional flora record and are not a measurement of population variation.
