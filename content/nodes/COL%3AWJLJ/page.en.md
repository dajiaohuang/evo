## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Cnidoscolus urentissimus? {#s-3bff36d950}
The leaves have one or two persistent, blade-like glands on the stalk. Blades are lobed, with flat sinuses and toothed margins; glands are absent from the edges. The androecium has two whorls of stamens. Male flowers have salver-shaped perianths, while female perianth parts are separate. The fruit is an ellipsoid capsule, and the seed bears a caruncle.
