## ## Pinnate leaves and a tiny floral bract {#s-d06d3c1b0d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes *Leptolobium multijugum* as woody. Its leaves are recorded as odd-pinnate or even-pinnate: leaflets sit along a central axis, which may end in a single leaflet or in a pair. The flower bracts, small leaf-like structures beside flowers, measure up to 1.2 mm. The ovary, the flower part that can develop into fruit, bears hairs ranging from fine to dense and woolly. The petal on the axis-facing side is described as slightly distinct. The seed entry records the embryo axis between the hypocotyl and radicle as parallel or oblique. A separate habitat field says terrestrial but gives no locality or survey frame. These regional fields do not establish full range or population variation.
