## Aloe suprafoliata: two-ranked young leaves {#s-23d01330b0}
A South African regional account describes a stemless or short-stemmed succulent herb up to 400 mm tall, excluding the inflorescence. Young leaves, through flowering size, are arranged in two ranks and have smooth surfaces. The inflorescence is 0.6–2.0 m high; the racemes are shorter than 300 mm. The outer perianth segments are free to the base, and the flowers are brilliant pink.

A diagnostic account compares the species with its nearest ally, *A. thorncroftii*. It says young *A. suprafoliata* leaves are always two-ranked, with smooth, glaucous surfaces. Its flowers are described as long and showy, but shorter and less conspicuous than those of *A. thorncroftii*.
