## Leaf and inflorescence {#s-7e675d2c76}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the petiole (leaf stalk) as channelled and the leaflet venation as strongly reticulate, or net-like. It reports inflorescence length as “1.8–4 (–5.3)”; the field gives no unit and does not explain the parenthetical endpoint.

## Fruit form {#s-838e52d61d}
The fruit is described as obovate (broader toward its tip), up to 8 cm long, with inconspicuous surface venation and one to two seeds. This description comes from one regional dataset record and does not report how these traits vary across the species.
