## The source concept {#s-abca62fc91}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Osteopilus dominicensis as row Anf2504, classified in the genus Osteopilus, family Hylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); whether this record matches a species concept in COL26.8 has not been established. The traits below describe the deposited record.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-0ae136c30c}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 99 mm. For frogs, this measure is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-c997b4b6f9}
The row gives both the minimum and maximum clutch count as 436 eggs or offspring, and reports a maximum of one reproductive event per year. These are values compiled from literature; the row does not specify the sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive setting for each one. The clutch count may refer to eggs or offspring.
