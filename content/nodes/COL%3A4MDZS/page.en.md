## Large leaves and nodding flower heads {#s-a37cceb5d9}
The German account describes a perennial herb usually with one 40–60 cm stem and several nodding flower heads. Its broad, hairy leaves reach 20 cm long; the flower heads are pale green, and the dry fruit carries a tuft of long bristles. The species is recorded from moist grassland and grassy mountain slopes, often sheltered by rock outcrops.
