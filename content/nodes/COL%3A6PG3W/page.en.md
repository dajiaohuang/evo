## Two reported localities beside karst streams {#s-43f3acb5c9}
The 2019 description reported Jinjiazhai Village in Wujing Nature Reserve and Baimashan Forest Station in Zhaozishan Nature Reserve, both in Bijie, Guizhou. Frogs occurred along a clear rocky stream approximately 3 m wide and 10–20 cm deep, surrounded by broad-leaved forest in karst terrain at 1600–1900 m. These are the published study records, not proof that the species occurs nowhere else.

## July males calling on leaves and rocks {#s-d08769bfa0}
During 2–4 July 2018 between 21:00 and 23:50, many males called on plant leaves, while some called on or beneath streamside rocks. The account does not quantify male abundance or demonstrate an annual breeding window, egg placement or tadpole habitat.
