## The length stops at the vent {#s-4d2964f686}
The entry gives a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 74 mm and a female maximum of 47.7 mm. SVL is measured from the snout to the vent, so it leaves out the tail. These are compiled maxima, not typical sizes or proof of a growth ceiling. Because the male and female records need not come from the same place or study, the two figures alone do not measure sexual size differences in a population.

## Two eggs in a compiled clutch summary {#s-cfa2f989c8}
The source calls the reproductive mode oviparous, or egg-laying. Its consolidated clutch/litter field is 2, and both the lowest and highest reported means are also 2.
