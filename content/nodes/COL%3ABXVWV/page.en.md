## form {#s-c903c01835}
Hemiptera includes cicadas, aphids, scale insects and true bugs. Its members feed with piercing-sucking mouthparts. In many true bugs, the forewing is hardened at the base and membranous at the tip; that pattern does not describe the whole order.

## ecology {#s-d6b68d3c3b}
Most hemipterans develop through incomplete metamorphosis: nymphs molt as they grow into adults without a pupal stage. Many feed on plant fluids, while other species prey on animals or feed on blood. Some true bugs live on freshwater surfaces or within the water.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-d5ca1c3719}
Feeding and habitat vary across the order, so a pattern found in one family or subgroup cannot be assigned to every hemipteran. The available account does not map the whole order's range or quantitatively compare its traits, and it does not resolve the group's evolutionary tree or current conservation status.
