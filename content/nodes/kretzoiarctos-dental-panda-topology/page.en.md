## How a few teeth place Kretzoiarctos near pandas {#s-c5657caa8b}
Kretzoiarctos is represented by named dental material from Nombrevilla 2 and Abocador de Can Mata in Spain. The holotype, MNCN-CSIC NV-2-42, is a left fourth upper premolar; NV-2-40 is a right first molar, while IPS 46473 is a partial mandible with a broader tooth series.

A character matrix places the sample within Ailuropodinae, the panda subfamily, and dental comparisons suggest a herbivorous tendency. Those results do not show that Kretzoiarctos had the same ecology as the living giant panda, was its direct ancestor, or marks a secure centre of panda origins. The fossils are evidence for a sampled tooth pattern and a conditional placement.
