## Acrocomia intumescens: stem, leaves and fruit {#s-8052fe91e7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records an aerial stem, an unarmed, swollen stipe and no pseudostem. It says the stem is “longer than 6” but gives no unit. The leaves are green, emerge above the soil, and number more than 20; pinnae are regularly arranged, their insertion angle to the rachis is greater than 45 degrees, and their attachments lie in different planes. Remnants of leaf sheaths are absent from the stipe.

The inflorescence is longer than 50 cm and yellow at maturity. The spathe is woody and tomentose, with or without spines. Flowers lack pedicels. The fruit’s exocarp is yellowish or greenish, the mesocarp yellow, and fruit diameter exceeds 2.5 cm. The source gives an endosperm number of 1–4.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The stem threshold has no unit in the field, and its value is not expanded here. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
