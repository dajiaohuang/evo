## Cattleya coccinea {#s-44822c028d}
For Cattleya coccinea, the Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology entry describes one small, cylindrical pseudobulb and a single oblong leaf. The one or two flowers have red sepals and petals; the slightly three-lobed lip has a red margin and a yellow to orange-yellow base. The habitat data list both epiphytic and rupicolous categories.
