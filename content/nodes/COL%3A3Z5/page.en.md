## Limits of the Ernest summaries {#s-b0c8e28e7a}
The Ernest accounts use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 mapping; equivalence to current species concepts is unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature inputs with equal weight, which may be published means, individual values, or range midpoints. Sample sizes do not set weights and source studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted in this route. Original reference numbers remain on species pages, but underlying papers and wild, captive, and locality conditions were not reviewed consistently.
