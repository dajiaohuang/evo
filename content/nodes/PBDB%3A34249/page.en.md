## The *Xiushanosteus* sample {#s-5abc5728f1}
This atlas treats Placodermi as a historical grade and links its older display edge to *Xiushanosteus* from the Huixingshao Formation, at about 436 Ma. The younger 358.9 Ma edge is a rounded composite boundary. Neither end is presented as a global first or last appearance, and the interval is not a direct-ancestor sequence.

Material from the Huixingshao Formation provides the evidence-linked older anchor for this navigation route. The study supports a record around 436 million years old, but the sample does not establish that placoderms first appeared then or that it represents an ancestor of later groups.

## Why the younger edge is different {#s-32967470ef}
The 358.9 Ma endpoint remains a rounded composite boundary rather than a specific last fossil occurrence. The two bounds therefore describe the atlas’s historical-grade display, not a measured global lifespan for Placodermi.
