## Drosanthemum globosum {#s-16d517a71f}
## Globose leaves and magenta flowers mark the branching shrublet

The Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 45 cm tall, with many branches bearing fine brown papillae. The leaves are globose and densely papillate. Flowers reach 17 mm across and are magenta. The five-chambered fruit is almost star-shaped. The habitat record is loamy soils.
