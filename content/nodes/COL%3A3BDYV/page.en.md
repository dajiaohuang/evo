## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-6a10fc75cf}
COL26.8 records Erythrolamprus miliaris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-d3f196d38d}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-cc0b2a1d3e}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Stuginski et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b018d7852a}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-6482532753}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
