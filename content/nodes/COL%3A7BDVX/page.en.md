## Winged calyx lobes and a narrow funnel-shaped corolla {#s-6f8b90e37e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical stems and membranous leaves, with blades elliptic or oblong. The calyx lobes are joined above the midpoint of the calyx, ovate, and acute; their lower face is winged. The corolla is narrowly funnel-shaped, with ovate lobes. The source field provides these floral and leaf characters without a separate locality description.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-7233ee1c39}
The associated Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Tachia occidentalis* as terrestrial, without naming a collection site.
