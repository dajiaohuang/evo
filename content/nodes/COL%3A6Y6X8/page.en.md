## Scapteromys meridionalis: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Scapteromys meridionalis* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Quintela, Gonçalves, Althoff, Sbalqueiro, Oliveira & Freitas, 2014.
