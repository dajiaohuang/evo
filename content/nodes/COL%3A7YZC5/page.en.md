## Selaginella germinans {#s-8f76ec3ee6}
## Dorsal rhizophores and asymmetric lateral leaves

The species record describes prostrate or decumbent, straw-coloured stems with joints and dorsal rhizophores, the root-bearing structures. Its small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are ovate, asymmetric at the base and acute at the tip; the source records distinctive idioblast cells in them. Dorsal leaves are ovate-elliptic or ovate-lanceolate, with an eared or asymmetric base and an awned tip. Axillary leaves are ovate with two earlike basal lobes and an acute tip.
