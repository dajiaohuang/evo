## The source concept {#s-e3d4751cc7}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Callulina laphami* as row Anf0338, in the genus Callulina, family Brevicipitidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-18fe055e34}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) as a broad foraging category. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Activity periods {#s-ff979c5636}
The row records daytime (Diu = 1) and night (Noc = 1) as activity periods. These are broad categories, not a continuous record of behaviour through the day.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-90cf5d6b67}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 45.4 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
