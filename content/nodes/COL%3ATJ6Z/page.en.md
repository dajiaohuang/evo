## Chamaecrista auris-zerdae {#s-e646a70244}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista auris-zerdae* records woody underground base, 3–4 leaflet pairs, yellow unequal petals, twisted mature pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
