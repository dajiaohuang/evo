## Elegia fistulosa {#s-a0b80f6e30}
## Hollow stems and a flower cluster nearly square in outline

The South African regional account describes Elegia fistulosa as a dioecious perennial (female and male flowers grow on separate plants) that forms tufts and reaches 1.1 m. Its hollow stems often spread through surrounding vegetation; its leaf sheaths (the basal parts of leaves around a stem) fall away. The inflorescence (the full flower cluster) is nearly square in outline, and the styles (flower parts between the ovary and stigma) are red. Seeps are listed as habitat.
