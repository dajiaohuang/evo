## Chamaecrista fulgida {#s-bd5a44c1ec}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista fulgida* records 4–5 leaflet pairs, elliptic to lance-shaped leaflets, yellow unequal petals. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
