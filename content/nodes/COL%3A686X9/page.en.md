## *Asplenium zamiifolium* {#s-2297478df3}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, oblong, pinnate (side divisions arranged along a central axis). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as rock-dwelling (Portuguese: Rupícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
