## Blepharodon costae {#s-e3a4c9ddbc}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Blepharodon costae* as an accepted species of *Blepharodon*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record supplies a terrestrial habitat label only. No collection locality, complete range, or morphology is provided, so the entry supports this regional category and no broader description.
