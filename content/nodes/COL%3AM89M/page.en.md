## Which features identify this plant? {#s-b617364cbd}
The regional account describes *Bobartia gladiata* as a rhizomatous perennial 20–80 cm tall. A rhizome is an underground stem. The plant usually has flattened stems, linear leaves and a flattened cluster of yellow cymes (flower clusters).

## When does the account record flowering? {#s-34366ba2da}
The account gives a flowering period of September through December. This is the regional flora’s observation, not a full account of the plant’s life cycle.

## Which habitats are listed? {#s-f098e20d7a}
The regional account records mountain slopes and coastal flats in fynbos as habitats. It does not establish that the plant occurs in every site of those habitat types.

## How far does the regional range account extend? {#s-2e31ba9ee2}
The source gives a southwestern range, from the Cape Peninsula and Bain’s Kloof to Hermanus. This is the range stated in that regional account, not a complete global range assessment.
