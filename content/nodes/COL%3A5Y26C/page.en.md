## ## Bulbous glandular hairs and twisted fruit valves {#s-eafbbca18e}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two pairs of oblong to ovate leaflets, with bulbous glandular hairs on the stems, leaves and inflorescences. The ten-stamen flowers have yellow petals of different forms. The pod valves twist at maturity, and the seeds are depressed-obovoid. The record lists the xylopodium as unknown and classifies the plant as terrestrial.
