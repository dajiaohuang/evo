## Trichogonia spathulifolia {#s-a30262a54e}
## Spoon-shaped leaves and gray woolly bracts

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes gray, finely hairy branches. Leaves are usually alternate and loosely spaced, with lower leaves sometimes opposite. Their short stalks measure 0.8–1 cm. The somewhat leathery, spoon-shaped blades have rounded tips and entire edges; the upper surface is finely hairy, while the lower is gray-woolly with sessile glands.

Loose corymb-like panicles carry stalked heads. Two rows of gray-woolly bracts surround each head: outer bracts are oblong-spatulate, inner ones oblong-rhombic and blunt. The receptacle is flat and heads have about 30 florets. The dry fruits are stalked and finely bristly along their ribs; a regular pappus measures 3.5–4 mm. No habitat field or locality is recorded here.
