## Tachigali formicarum {#s-1681eddcca}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali formicarum* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes leaf-like stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) and triangular or ribbed domatia (small cavities) on the petiole or leaf axis. It lists up to 10 leaflet pairs, flowers up to 10 mm with sparse petal hairs, and 10 stamens; the fruit is elliptic to nearly round. The plant is terrestrial.
