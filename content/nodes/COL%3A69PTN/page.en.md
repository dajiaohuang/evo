## A sticky surface and darkening pod {#s-9c2c2d0d03}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, spiral leaves carry three or four pairs of elliptic to oblanceolate leaflets, 2–4.4 cm long. The source records warty, sticky glandular hairs and terminal or leaf-axil flower clusters. Yellow petals differ in shape, with ten stamens. The oblong pod is about 3 cm long; its sticky surface darkens as it matures and the pod sides twist. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
