## Drosanthemum vandermerwei {#s-12482dafc1}
## Pink flowers top sprawling reddish branches

The Greater Cape flora describes a prostrate to sprawling shrublet with reddish branches up to 40 cm long. Its leaves are subcylindrical to cylindrical, blunt-tipped, and the surface is papillate. Flowers reach 27 mm across and are pink; the fruit has five chambers. The habitat record is explicitly uncertain.
