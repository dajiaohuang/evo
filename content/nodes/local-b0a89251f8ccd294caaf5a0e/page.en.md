## How the source names this tadpole {#s-ed30492c65}
TadpoleTraits version 4 groups these records under Allobates carajas. The dataset authors use Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024) for its placement in Allobates and Aromobatidae; the underlying publications also use the name Allobates carajas. This source grouping does not establish that the records match a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.
