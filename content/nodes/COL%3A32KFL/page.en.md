## Curio crassulifolius {#s-ae9d8734ed}
## Spindle-shaped leaves crown an erect or sprawling succulent

Manning and Goldblatt describe a succulent perennial up to 30 cm tall, growing erect or sprawling. The leaves cluster at stem tips and are spindle-shaped, with a round cross-section.

One or a few disc-shaped flower heads grow at the ends of elongated stalks that bear only a few small bracts. Heads are white, sometimes yellow, and have about eight involucral bracts. Rocky outcrops and ledges are recorded as habitat.
