## Philodendron callosum: leaf and floral descriptors {#s-c006217824}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and short stem internodes. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous, and the petiole is purple at the tip. Leaf blades are entire, elliptic or oblong, with a cuneate base and entire margins; lateral veins are obscure when the blade is green.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium. Its short peduncle is shorter than the spathe. The spathe is constricted, with a green outer surface marked red, a greenish-cream inner tube and an outer blade described as whitish or cream. The spadix is subsessile. Stamen and staminode states are unknown; the gynoecium is cylindrical and the ovary subcylindrical. The ovary has 2 locules and 10 ovules, with axial placentation; stigma, fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Unknown source states are retained.

The dataset records the habitat category “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
