## What leaf and reproductive traits are recorded for Sapium ciliatum? {#s-bc0f9b6f0b}
Its leaves are alternate in a spiral arrangement, with persistent stipules and grooved stalks. A cylindrical gland sits near the tip of each stalk. The blade is narrowly elliptic or oblanceolate, with a wedge-shaped base and a tapering or tail-like tip. The source allows variation in texture and edge: leaves may be leathery or papery, and margins nearly entire or toothed and fringed with hairs. Veins curve toward the margin. The solitary inflorescence is terminal. Male flowers have two stamens and partly joined sepals; the female calyx is two-lobed. The capsule has two or three chambers and a pericarp about 0.7 mm thick. Seeds are ellipsoid, rounded at the base and blunt at the tip.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-51ead418ec}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
