## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-2c67b1c101}
COL26.8 records Crithagra Swainson, 1827 at genus rank, with Fringillidae as its direct parent.

All twenty-four selected BirdLife historical rows have same-name eBird and one-to-one BirdTree links. Read COL cr/se shards confirm twenty-three exact older Serinus combinations, including ending changes such as rufobrunneus/rufobrunnea and sulphuratus/sulphurata. Serinus hypostictus at Crithagra hyposticta was not confirmed as an exact catalogue synonym in this reading. Links locate historical rows without proving concepts identical across dates.

## One bird and larger incomplete samples {#s-b2e0cf3029}
Crithagra xantholaema has one female bird and Crithagra ankoberensis one male, each with one complete set; neither provides a sex comparison. Crithagra citrinelloides has fifteen birds but four complete sets; Crithagra mozambica seventeen but ten complete, with three unsexed; Crithagra dorsostriata nine but six complete, with one unsexed. Crithagra citrinipectus has seven birds and seven complete sets but three unsexed; Crithagra leucoptera five birds and five complete sets but three unsexed. Dunning mass has a separate literature denominator that cannot be merged with these counts.

## Seeds, multiple niches and primary lifestyles {#s-13d48f7f5d}
Niche categories are fifteen Granivore and nine Omnivore, while all twenty-four trophic levels are Herbivore. Seeds/nuts at 60% and all plants at 70% are different definition thresholds, not observed diet percentages. Lifestyles comprise twelve Insessorial, ten Generalist and two Terrestrial; habitats fourteen Shrubland, three Woodland, four Forest and three Grassland; density counts two code-1, fifteen code-2 and seven code-3. All migration codes are 1 without proving lifelong immobility. Shared diet categories do not establish a shared lifestyle.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-c508902f34}
This page uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, same-name eBird links, older Serinus BirdTree links, relevant Metadata definitions and this reading of COL cr/se shards. Catalogue synonyms and crosswalks do not prove complete equivalence of historical concepts to COL26.8; the exact older hypostictus combination was not confirmed in these shards. Morphology birds, complete sets and Dunning compiled mass retain separate denominators. Individual specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Current distributions, conservation, plumage, calls, breeding, seed lists, bite forces, observed diets and movement routes remain unsupported.
