## Recorded parent of Paenungulata {#s-88ed6d6bc4}
Paenungulata is listed at rank superorder in the PBDB classification route, with Paenungulatomorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
