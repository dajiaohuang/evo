## What vine, leaf and flower traits are described for Passiflora caerulea? {#s-6747b532c2}
This climbing vine has conspicuous, leaf-like bracts with smooth margins. Kidney-shaped stipules are 10–20 mm long and curved, with widely spaced teeth. Leaves have three to five, sometimes nine, lobes, cut nearly to the base. Flowers are white or pinkish; the corona—the rows of filaments around the floral center—is purple at the base, white in the middle and blue at the tip.

## How is it used? {#s-60d3aa0659}
The source lists it as a garden ornamental.
