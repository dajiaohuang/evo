## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Clusia macropoda? {#s-41d4a122ab}
The record describes a single taproot and stalked, leathery, obovate leaves with inconspicuous secretory channels. Upright flower clusters bear flowers with six sepals and six petals; the corolla may be white, cream, or pink. Male flowers have more than 40 separate stamens, resin-bearing stamens or staminodes, and an absent or greatly reduced pistillode. The ovary has six to ten chambers. Sepals and staminodes remain on the mature fruit while petals fall; seeds have an aril.
