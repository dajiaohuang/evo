## What the captured fossil records show {#s-a29caedd06}
In the captured PBDB hierarchy, 7 accepted fossil-species concepts are placed under Trimerorhachidae. Across these 27 dated collection intervals, the outer age bounds run from 303.7 to 251.902 million years ago. These pooled collection bounds do not date the taxon’s origin or extinction and do not show continuous presence through the full span. PBDB’s placement is supplementary and does not establish equivalence with a similarly named COL group.
