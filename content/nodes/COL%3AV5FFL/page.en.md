## Accepted name and placement {#s-70403d1c4c}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys jinggangensis (Wang in Wang, Zhang, Zhao, Sung, Yang, Pang & Zhang, 2012) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Jiangxi–Hunan mountain records in the study {#s-ceb31c0984}
The monograph reported records across the Wanyang, Jiuling and Mufu mountains near the Jiangxi–Hunan border at 400–1200 m. Examined material included Jinggang, Wugong, Meiling, Jiuling and other mountain localities; the female holotype SYS a001430 came from Jinggang at 845 m. The geographic summary concerns that study’s material and taxonomic treatment, without proving continuous occupancy or a present population-density pattern.

## Reported diagnostic measurements with sex and sample size {#s-e1a592d35e}
The diagnosis reported 49 adult males at 33.9–48.9 mm snout–vent length and 21 females at 38.4–52.6 mm. It described an unnotched tongue, distinct tympanum, weak vomerine ridges with teeth, dorsal granules and tubercles with short irregular ridges, and rudimentary toe webbing with narrow fringes. These are the printed diagnostic sample totals, not an independently recounted voucher census or population-wide size limits.

## A conspecificity decision after competing lineage interpretations {#s-8d0438a36d}
The authors discussed earlier molecular suggestions that Jiuling and Meiling populations could be cryptic species distinct from Jinggang topotypes. The cited ABGD analysis instead grouped the three populations. The monograph found no distinguishing morphological characters across its examined localities and treated them as B. jinggangensis. This is an attributed taxonomic conclusion under that study, without independent certification of current concept equivalence or absence of reproductive isolation.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-622f8c30f6}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys jinggangensis as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
