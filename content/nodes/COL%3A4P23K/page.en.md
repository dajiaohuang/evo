## Pseudolycopodiella tatei {#s-2a344542a4}
## Unequal branches with defined strobili

The flora entry describes flattened, prostrate stems with unequal branching. The leaves are heteromorphic, meaning their forms differ, and the spore-bearing leaves differ from the vegetative ones. Strobili—the defined spore-bearing cones—are set apart or nearly set apart along branches. The spores are recorded as rugose, or wrinkled, and the habitat is labelled terrestrial.
