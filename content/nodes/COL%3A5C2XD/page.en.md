## Wolffia arrhiza {#s-a4652a588a}
The South African SANBI regional account describes this aquatic herb as having globoid to ovoid fronds (leaf-like plant bodies), 1.0–1.5 times as deep as wide, with their greatest width below the water surface. The upper side is intensely green, and the account records 10–100 stomata (microscopic surface pores). Habitat entries are dams and pools; the excerpt gives no population frequency or dated sampling frame.
