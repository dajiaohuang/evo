## How do the flattened stems, leaves and flowers vary? {#s-c2d59be672}
Flattened cladodes are recorded, with leaves absent or mostly absent; young leaves may be present. When present, the leaves are alternate and oval. Male flowers have six cream or greenish sepals, a segmented nectar disc and three or four free stamens. Female flowers have six cream or yellow sepals and an entire nectar disc. The fruit is a spherical capsule, and the seeds are smooth.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-21bcdaf201}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
