## The source concept {#s-81d3999edd}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0044 records Allobates olfersioides in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat field: Ter=1 {#s-46e60f4520}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). The fields Fos=NA, Aqu=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-4fee886bd5}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity). Noc=NA remains missing, not evidence of absence. These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Body_size_mm: 19 mm maximum adult body length {#s-c0a388e4af}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=19 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Clutch, reproductive-output and offspring-size values {#s-e2c87249ed}
The row lists Litter_size_min_n=11 as the minimum number of offspring or eggs per clutch; Litter_size_max_n=11 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch; Reproductive_output_y=3 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year; Offspring_size_min_mm=1.5 as the lower endpoint for offspring or egg size (mm); Offspring_size_max_mm=1.5 as the upper endpoint for offspring or egg size (mm). The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-bd5329f203}
Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
