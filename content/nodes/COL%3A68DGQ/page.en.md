## The study’s first Pará occurrence {#s-d76bc0da62}
The authors reported their material as the first B. ternetzi occurrence in Pará. Two specimens, MPEG 44016 and 44017, had 16S sequences 0.27% and 0.6% different from a cited sequence originating nearest the type locality among available comparisons, versus 1% and 1.5% from a southern Minas Gerais comparison. These particular genetic comparisons support the reported identification; they are not a comprehensive population-genetic survey or evidence of a recent range expansion.

## Habitat background and incompletely established regional lineages {#s-a4fe8611aa}
The account described primarily forested habitats with some occurrence in open vegetation and cited older karyotypic evidence for north–south differentiation. These hypotheses remain separate from the study’s own 16S comparisons of MPEG 44016 and 44017. ## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-bcaa7660ca}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Barycholos ternetzi as direct-developing and its oviposition as terrestrial. In the article, direct development means no free-living larval stage, with a froglet hatching rather than a free-living tadpole; it does not mean that embryonic development is absent or that humidity is unnecessary. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. Exact correspondence between the source taxonomy and the current accepted concept remains uncertified.
