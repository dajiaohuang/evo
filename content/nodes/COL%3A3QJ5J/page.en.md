## Spiny stems and compound leaves {#s-0ca416d218}
The morphology record describes a tree with spines and no tuberous root; reported height classes range from 6–12 m to above 20 m. The smooth leaflets are obovate to oblong, with pointed or tail-like tips and entire edges; leaves have three or five leaflets. Panicles bear greenish flowers, and the ovoid fruits have five ribs. The habitat field says terrestrial.
