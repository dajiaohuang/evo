## Parioscorpio is not accepted as a scorpion {#s-b51c573d30}
*Parioscorpio venator* is recorded from Wisconsin’s Silurian Waukesha Lagerstätte, an exceptionally fossil-preserving deposit; the dossier names holotype UWGM 2162 and paratype UWGM 2163. A 2021 redescription rejected the scorpion interpretation but left its exact arthropod affinity unresolved. The 437.5–436.5 Ma interval is conodont-bounded locality age, not a direct date on either specimen or proof of habitat.
