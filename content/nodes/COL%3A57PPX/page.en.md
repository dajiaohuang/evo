## Leaves {#s-2c29e88944}
Manning and Goldblatt's 2012 regional account compares the species with *T. revoluta*. It describes *T. muricata* as having a few to several lance-shaped leaves, each not enclosed separately by a cataphyll.

This comparison comes from a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-3bd7153405}
The account records the species on stony clay slopes in Karroid scrub and renosterveld.

This habitat note comes from a regional species account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
