## *Monotagma aurantiispathum*: orange bracts that stay unexpanded {#s-6ed418ca13}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional profile records symmetric leaf blades, a green midrib and hairs on the underside. It describes a terminal flower cluster on a stalk longer than 2.5 cm, with two to nine nodes. The orange bracts do not expand at flowering; the outer staminode, a sterile stamen-like part, is elliptical.

The profile also records no root tubers and no overlapping leaf sheaths. Its associated habitat field says only “terrestrial,” without a named locality or date. These characters belong to a regional description, not a survey of all populations.
