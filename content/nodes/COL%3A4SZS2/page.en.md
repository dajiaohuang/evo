## Richterago elegans {#s-f041ada537}
## Grey woolly leaves frame white radiate heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, woody flowering stalk with alternate, leathery leaves. Their elliptic blades are flat or folded lengthwise, pointed, finely toothed and tapered at the base. A grey woolly covering and prominent veins mark the leaf surfaces; the short leaf stalk widens at its sides.

Radiate heads grow in corymb-shaped clusters on branching, bracted stalks. Each cylindrical to top-shaped involucre has 10–15 rows of bracts: the inner ones are lance-shaped, while the outer ones are ovate, pointed and grey-woolly, later losing some hairs. The 10–15 bilabiate female rays surround 20–40 white bisexual disc florets. Cylindrical, silky fruits carry a single row of 25–42 finely toothed pappus bristles, joined at the base by a fleshy ring.
