## Acritopappus longifolius {#s-ee15703608}
## Lance-shaped leaves and a cylindrical involucre

The Brazilian Flora record describes hairy stems that are not viscous. Lance-shaped leaves have petioles, serrated margins and a caudate or apiculate tip; both surfaces are hairy, with the lower side also glandular. The panicles bear heads with cylindrical involucres and a flat receptacle. The florets are glandular, and the fruit has an awned pappus. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
