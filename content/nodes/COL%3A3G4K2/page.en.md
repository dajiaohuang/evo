## Reduced leaves and carmine autumn flowers {#s-e11d9ddd24}
Gladiolus carmineus reaches 35 cm and is recognised by reduced foliage and funnel-shaped carmine flowers. The flower tube is 30–35 mm long; the account identifies it as an autumn-flowering species.
