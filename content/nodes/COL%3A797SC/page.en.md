## Leaf and flower characters in the source entry {#s-00611ffe96}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records an erect stem and trifoliolate leaves. The inflorescences are smaller than the leaves and fasciculate. Calyx lobes are shorter than the corolla; the fruit is recorded as not constricted, and the seed as concolorous. No habitat field is included, so this record does not establish habitat or geographic range.
