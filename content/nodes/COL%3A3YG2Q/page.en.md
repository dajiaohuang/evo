## Mauritiella armata {#s-88d8b73d10}
Mauritiella armata is recorded with 1–7 stems, 2–20 m tall and 8–30 cm in diameter. Plants bear 4–10 leaves with stiff blades divided into 60–100 segments; marginal spines may be present or absent. Female inflorescences have 12–34 branches, and fruits are 2.5–3.5 cm long. Habitat is listed as terrestrial.
