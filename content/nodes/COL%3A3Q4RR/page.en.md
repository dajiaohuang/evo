## The workbook codes direct development and terrestrial eggs {#s-61be368f6d}
The 2023 workbook's row 199 codes direct development=1 and terrestrial oviposition=1 for Ischnocnema_henselii. In the paper, direct development means there is no free-living larval stage: a froglet hatches rather than a free-living tadpole. It does not mean embryos do not develop or that humidity is unnecessary.
