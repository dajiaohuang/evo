## What one sponge genome can tell us {#s-9852fbfa2a}
The draft genome of the living demosponge Amphimedon queenslandica contains many gene families associated with animal signalling, transcription, adhesion and cell cycling, although the adult sponge lacks eumetazoan organs.

## A molecular toolkit in a simple body {#s-b11486f665}
The published draft assembly and gene annotations report developmental-signalling, transcription-factor, adhesion and cell-cycle families in Amphimedon. Comparisons with sampled non-metazoan outgroups identify repertoires present in this sponge and not detected in those comparison samples. The result separates the presence of molecular machinery from the later assembly of organs and cell types.

## One living lineage is not an ancestor {#s-118fc632e9}
Amphimedon queenslandica is one living demosponge, and its genome is a draft assembly interpreted through comparative annotation. Gene presence, absence and family size can inform evolutionary hypotheses, but they cannot reconstruct a literal Precambrian animal or represent every member of Porifera. The study concerns the sampled genome and comparison set.
