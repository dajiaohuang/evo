## Campylocentrum robustum {#s-7925d3c96d}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology entry, the oblong, bilobed leaves are arranged in two ranks. Unlike the three-lobed lips recorded for several related entries, this description gives Campylocentrum robustum an entire, lanceolate lip and a curved cylindrical spur. Its fruit is smooth and ellipsoid; the habitat field says epiphytic.
