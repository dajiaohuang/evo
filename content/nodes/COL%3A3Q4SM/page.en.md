## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-1bfbc6d1ce}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Ischnocnema spanios as direct-developing and its oviposition as terrestrial. In the article, direct development means no free-living larval stage, with a froglet hatching rather than a free-living tadpole; it does not mean that embryonic development is absent or that humidity is unnecessary. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species.
