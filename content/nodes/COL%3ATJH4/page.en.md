## Small flower groups and golden-coated seeds {#s-fb296ee9a3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes four or five pairs of leaflets, with three pairs also recorded; the tip leaflet is sickle-shaped. A nectar gland sits on the leaf stalk, and small groups beside the leaves usually carry one or two flowers. The yellow flowers have three to five stamens. Brown pods are 4–13 mm long and 2–3 mm wide, with dense hairs; the irregularly diamond-shaped seeds have a golden coat. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
