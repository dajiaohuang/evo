## Cyrtanthus pondoensis {#s-b40866ff2d}
Cyrtanthus pondoensis spreads by stolons that form new bulbs away from the parent. Its regional description gives a 45 mm funnel-shaped flower, with lobes that close at dusk, and distinguishes it from C. brachysiphon by this stoloniferous growth and floral form. It is recorded on rocky, quartzitic sandstone banks of the Msikaba River.
