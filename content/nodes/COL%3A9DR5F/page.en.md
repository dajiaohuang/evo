## Zygia racemosa {#s-e3a1ec7d77}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zygia racemosa* as an accepted species of *Zygia*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description notes bipinnate leaves with 4–7 pairs of pinnae and 27, 29, 31, 33, or 35 leaflets per pinna. The inflorescences are head-shaped, and the flat legumes have finely hairy valves. The source labels the habitat terrestrial; it does not establish global variation or a complete range.
