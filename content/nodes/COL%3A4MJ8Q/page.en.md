## Accepted name and placement {#s-bd8275401b}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis actites (Lynch, 1979) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-52cb7cacf8}
AmphiBIO’s historical record for Pristimantis actites includes terrestrial foraging, daytime activity, wet- and cold-season labels, maximum adult snout–vent length of 64.2 mm, maturity at 1 year, and up to 1 reproductive event per year; it also codes direct development. AmphiTherm separately reports adult CTmax 33.25 °C and CTmin 4.9 °C using “no response to prodding” endpoints.

## The older habitat record {#s-99bb8d0451}
AmphiBIO assigns the Frost 2011 source concept a terrestrial-foraging category. It is a broad label, without seasonal or life-stage detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian microhabitat entries, but this row has not been checked against that revision. Read the category as historical evidence; equivalence to the current COL26.8 species concept is unverified.

## Daytime activity category {#s-670d88b5bb}
For the historical source concept, AmphiBIO records “daytime” activity. This is a broad category, not a continuous record over a full day or across seasons. The entry remains tied to the Frost 2011 concept rather than a verified match to the current accepted species.

## Seasonal activity categories {#s-1ef67c13b9}
AmphiBIO also records “wet” and “cold” activity-season categories for the historical source concept. The dataset compares activity conditions with the local annual climate, so these words do not mean fixed calendar seasons. They are not a year-round behavioural series or a newly verified observation of the current species concept.

## Maximum adult body length {#s-67578062fb}
The historical AmphiBIO row lists 64.2 mm as maximum adult body length. Because this record is an anuran, the measure is snout–vent length, from snout to cloaca. It is a compiled maximum rather than a measurement of all adults, and the source concept has not been reconciled with the current species boundary.

## Reproductive counts and age at maturity {#s-31b1ce392c}
For this historical source concept, AmphiBIO lists minimum and maximum ages at sexual maturity of 1 year each, plus a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value; offspring-size entries elsewhere may refer to offspring or eggs.

## A developmental category {#s-cf4f618bbb}
The archived AmphiBIO record is coded as direct development. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing, and the historical Frost 2011 source concept has not been shown to match the current COL26.8 species concept.
