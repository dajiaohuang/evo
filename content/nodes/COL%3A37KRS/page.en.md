## Dracontium margaretae: inflorescence and seed measurements {#s-a1ac9797b3}
The 2020 Brazilian Flora entry records regular or irregular tubers, light-green petioles and a leaf blade divided in a trichotomous pattern, with linear lobes. The peduncle is subterranean or very short and smooth. The spathe is not differentiated into a tube and blade, reaches 20 cm, and lacks a translucent region at its base. The spadix is fully exposed, 2–6 cm long, with an apical appendage.

The flowers are listed with 5 or 6 tepals and 6 stamens. Berries range from red to yellow. Seeds are reniform or subglobose, with the number listed as 2 or 3.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative counts and forms are retained as reported.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
