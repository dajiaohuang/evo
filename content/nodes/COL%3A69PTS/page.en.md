## Chamaecrista obtecta {#s-c4bd4ac9c2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista obtecta* records two leaflet pairs: first nearly round to kidney-shaped, second ovate; yellow unequal petals, twisted mature pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
