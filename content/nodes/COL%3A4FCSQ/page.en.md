## Petalostelma cearense {#s-f5abd73013}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Petalostelma cearense* as an accepted species of *Petalostelma*. The only habitat information in its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 row is “terrestrial.” The regional field names no collection site or broader range, and it includes no morphology description.
