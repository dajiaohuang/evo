## The name and this data account {#s-e83d692132}
Monasa flavirostris is accepted in COL26.8 within Bucconidae. AVONET v7 links the record to the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and reports 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set. Usable sample sizes may differ by trait; the historical name match does not prove concept equivalence.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-7086c14ac8}
Mean beak length is 21.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width and depth at that edge average 8 mm and 9.3 mm. They are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-f1ff48afd1}
Mean unflattened wing length is 107.6 mm, from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 16.7 mm. The separate compiled mass mean is 62.8 g, labelled Dunning. It is not a weight measured on each of the five morphology specimens.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-94417d52f9}
AVONET labels the habitat Forest, trophic niche Invertivore and lifestyle Insessorial. These are broad comparison categories, not exclusive habitat or a complete account of feeding and behavior.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-7ef48345b4}
The migration field is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude −14.24° to 5.22°; they do not describe all non-breeding movement or current occupancy. The map and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## What one ecological field can tell us {#s-0d767d498a}
EltonTraits 1.0 retains one usable field group for this same-name, same-family record: foraging position. The diet group is withheld with code D1, so no food profile is inferred from it. The historical taxonomy does not resolve modern splits or synonyms.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-1036df3ffc}
The species-level foraging weights are 40% in air well above vegetation or other structures, 30% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy, and 30% in or just above the canopy. These are relative use weights for the source’s distinct position categories, not heights or a whole-day time budget.

AVONET v7 reports sample-based morphology and a separately sourced Dunning mass mean under a historical HBW–BirdLife name. Forest, Invertivore, Insessorial and Sedentary are broad labels; historical latitude endpoints are not current occupancy. Matching names do not establish equivalence to COL26.8.
