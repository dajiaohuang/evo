## What leaf, flower, and seed traits are recorded? {#s-16bdee8263}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic leaves, smooth on both surfaces. One or two flowers grow in the leaf axils on smooth stalks. The sepals are free, and the petals are egg-shaped. Ellipsoid fruit segments contain seeds with pits or lengthwise grooves.
