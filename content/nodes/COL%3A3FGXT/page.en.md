## Form and flowers {#s-d61193ca4b}
The regional account describes a cormous geophyte 8–20 cm tall, with woody, concentric corm tunics and usually branched stems. The leaves are short, lance-shaped, and soft. Flowers range from lilac to mauve-pink, with white anthers.

## Diagnostic comparison {#s-dd3c141920}
A separate SANBI account distinguishes it by 5–7 characteristically short, lance-shaped leaves and relatively large pink-to-purple flowers. Tepals measure 13–17 mm and the perianth tube about 5 mm. The account says it may be confused with *Geissorhiza inconspicua*, which has smaller violet-to-purple or white flowers, tepals mostly 8–11 mm long, and longer leaves that reach at least to the base of the spike.
