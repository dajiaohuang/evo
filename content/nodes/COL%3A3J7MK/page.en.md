## Haemanthus crispus {#s-6e0eb997bb}
Haemanthus crispus is a small bulbous geophyte with two narrow, wavy-edged leaves that are dry at flowering. Its compact flower head is red, rarely pink, and surrounded by waxy bracts. The regional account highlights layered, horizontally arranged bulb tunics as unusual within the genus. Stony lower slopes are the recorded habitat.
