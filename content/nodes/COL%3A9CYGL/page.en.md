## ## Rounded leaflets and nectar glands {#s-0497608d51}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character list describes alternate leaves arranged in two ranks, with two or three pairs of rounded leaflets. Extrafloral nectar glands occur on the petiole and inflorescence. The yellow flowers have ten stamens; the mature fruit valves are twisted and glabrous. The seed is depressed and ranges in shape from quadrangular to rhomboid. This regional record does not specify population coverage.
