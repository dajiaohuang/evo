## Tufted growth and dark, unspotted flowers {#s-66638e3709}
Flora do Brasil describes Bifrenaria atropurpurea as tufted, with pseudobulbs (swollen stem structures) that are not laterally compressed. Its inflorescence is longer than the pseudobulb and bears at least five purple-brown flowers without spots; the purple-brown lip has a slightly curved spur (a slender projection from the flower) and a fleshy distal callus.

## Epiphytic and terrestrial records {#s-bcb905c266}
The source records both epiphytic and terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant) habits, without distinguishing their conditions or relative frequency.
