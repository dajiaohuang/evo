## ## Large leaflets and a dense flower cluster {#s-ee05363a14}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records six to ten leaflets per leaf, rarely fifteen; the distal leaflets are oblong or lance-shaped and 17–26.5 cm long. The leaf rachis measures 19–30 cm. Inflorescences are terminal or cauliflorous, meaning they may arise from the trunk or older woody stems; each is recorded with 90–100 flowers and pedicels 0.9–1.2 cm long. The row also labels the habitat “terrestrial,” without giving a locality or survey date.
