## Pachyrhizus erosus {#s-f1fca2c09a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Pachyrhizus erosus* as an accepted species of *Pachyrhizus* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama fields in a WFO MBG archive retrieved 2026-09-08 describe an herbaceous vine with a tuberous rootstock, three-part leaves, blue flowers, and compressed pods about 10 cm long. Its distribution field reports a range through the Neotropics and introduced, naturalized populations in parts of the Old World.
