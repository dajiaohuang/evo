## Rigid, spiny leaf tips on sandstone slopes {#s-cce19d7639}
The regional account distinguishes this species from C. setosa by its stiff, leathery leaves and straight, non-reflexed spine at the tip. Plants are reported to exceed 1 m tall and to flower from September through December. The listed habitat is sandstone slopes up to about 1,500 m; the entry names the Kamiesberg, Bokkeveld Mountains and Little Karoo within its regional units.
