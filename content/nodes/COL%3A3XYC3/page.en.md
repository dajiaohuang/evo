## *Maranta foliosa*: a hairy leaf joint and two staminode calluses {#s-ae84694c1e}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, the leaf blade is elliptical and its pulvinus—the joint at the base of the leaf stalk—is densely hairy. The flower cluster bears loosely arranged bracts. Its corolla tube is straight; the sepals are longer than the tube, and the ovary is smooth and hairless.

The profile records two calluses on a staminode, a sterile stamen-like floral part, and upright sepals that persist after flowering. The habitat field labels it terrestrial but provides no named locality or date. These are regional character notes, not an estimate of within-species variation.
