## Scaly rhizomes and variable flowers {#s-8abdd61f7f}
Radinosiphon leptostachya ranges from about 80 to 500 mm tall, occasionally reaching 750 mm. It has scaly rhizomes (horizontal underground stems), sometimes with cormlets, and pink flowers marked with dark-pink streaks; the tube is usually 15–30 mm. The cited account notes local variation in flower dimensions and distinguishes smaller-flowered R. leptostachya from R. lomatensis.

## A seasonal life cycle and eastern African range {#s-3dd0f17815}
The source describes a deciduous perennial: aerial growth dies back in winter, leaves return in September–October, and flowering occurs December–February. It records visits by the long-proboscid fly Prosoeca ganglbaueri and a range across South Africa, Eswatini, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Malawi, Tanzania and Zambia; this is a regional flora account, not a current global survey.
