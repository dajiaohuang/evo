## Demonstrator-biased choices in a captive task {#s-b04039e1b3}
At the UT MD Anderson Cancer Center Keeling Center in Bastrop, Texas, an open-diffusion experiment used two social groups of captive Bolivian squirrel monkeys (23 animals total). Of 21 observers, 12 solved the apparatus task at least once; those solvers preferentially chose the response direction modeled by a demonstrator. The cited methods and results do not specify trial dates. This result is limited to a captive task and does not establish wild social learning or a species-wide cognitive capacity.
