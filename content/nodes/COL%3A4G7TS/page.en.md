## Form in the flora record {#s-ea3da51cef}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists clasping or feeding roots, an erect stem with short internodes, and an inconspicuous sheath. The petiole is conspicuous, smooth, and flattened on the upper side. The cordate blade has a cuspidate tip and an entire margin.

Inflorescences are described as sympodial, with counts of 1, 2, or 3. The peduncle is short and shorter than the spathe. The spathe is not constricted and may be red, green, light green, or cream. The spadix is sessile and decurrent; the field lists female, sterile male, fertile male, and apical sterile male zones.

Flowers have 4 or 5 free stamens narrowed at the base. Staminodes are short and narrowed at the base. The gynoecium and ovary are cylindrical or subcylindrical; 7, 8, 9, or 10 locules and 2 or 3 ovules are recorded. Placentation is subbasal, and the stigma is discoid or lobed. The berries are cylindrical or obovoid, and the seeds are numerous.

The regional dataset row cites Sakuragui and colleagues' 2020 Philodendron treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
