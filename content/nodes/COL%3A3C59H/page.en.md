## A grooved midrib and fruit traits left unknown {#s-4dfb6d448c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic, obovate or oblong leaves with gradually narrowed bases and tips that taper to a point. The leaves are papery, with flat margins and no hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with two veins near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis; bract status is unknown. The smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The field leaves fruit shape and surface, fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo unknown.

## A terrestrial label, without further range detail {#s-e6c0cb31af}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category does not extend the limited fruit and seed record.
