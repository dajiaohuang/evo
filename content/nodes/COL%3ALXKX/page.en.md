## What do the reported lengths measure? {#s-01cdf7bca3}
The reported maximum snout-to-vent length (SVL) is 839.5 mm, and the reported maximum total length (TL) is 1850 mm. SVL ends at the vent; total length also includes the tail. These compiled maxima describe reported material, not a theoretical growth limit or typical individual size. Female and male values may come from different localities or studies, so they cannot by themselves measure sexual dimorphism within one population.

The ReptTraits values follow its Reptile Database 2023 name framework.
