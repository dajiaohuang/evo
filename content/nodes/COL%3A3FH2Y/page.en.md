## One flower on each branch {#s-1c9a53fde2}
Geissorhiza uliginosa has branched stems, each branch carrying a single pink, bilaterally symmetrical flower. Its leaves trail along the ground, and the short flower tube is 9–13 mm long.

## Waterfalls and wet cliffs {#s-5d39c11822}
The regional account records waterfalls and wet cliffs. It does not provide a fuller survey of habitat use.
