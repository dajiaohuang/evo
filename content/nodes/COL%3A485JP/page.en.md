## Distribution and habitat {#s-d37a91835e}
The current MDD page uses Javan Slow Loris and gives Java as the type locality. Arismayanti, Perwitasari and Winarti observed two adult females in natural forest at Mount Halimun-Salak National Park; the sample describes space use at that study site only.

## Ecological evidence {#s-71c06f742e}
The study estimated home ranges with a kernel method, sampled habitat with the Point Centered Quarter method, and recorded behaviour using five-minute instantaneous focal-animal sampling. Contiguous home ranges for the two adult females were estimated at 5.43 and 5.58 ha. Daily activity mainly involved travel, feeding, other active behaviour and foraging in the lower canopy; sleeping usually occurred in the middle canopy. Two animals do not represent the species-wide distribution of home-range sizes.
