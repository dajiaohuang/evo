## Babiana curviscapa {#s-26d574e5b2}
Babiana curviscapa is a Namaqualand geophyte with a long, sharply bent flower tube and cherry-red to violet flowers. The regional account says it flowers mainly in September, later at higher elevations, and contrasts its long-tubed flowers, associated with long-proboscid flies, with the shorter-tubed, bee-pollinated flowers of B. attenuata. Their flowering periods may overlap at some sites, and the account notes that this can complicate identification. It is recorded on sandy and stony flats and granite outcrops.
