## Large basal leaves and pale flower clusters {#s-df6403558d}
The morphology record describes a perennial herb up to 65 cm tall, with large basal leaves on long, flattened stalk-like bases. The upper surfaces are rough-haired and the undersides grey-felted; many bell-shaped heads form compact clusters with white to pale-yellow flowers. Leaf shape shifts with elevation, and the source notes difficult identifications where H. oxyphyllum occurs alongside it.
