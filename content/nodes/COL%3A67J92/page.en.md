## Leaf base and inflorescence {#s-705ad64ae0}
The regional entry records ovate blades with small-pointed tips and bases described as acute or obtuse. Secondary veins are impressed; the ovate spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) meets its stalk at a right or obtuse angle, and the berries are green. These descriptors are not tied to a quantified sample of variation.

## The soil-associated record {#s-45eef1f326}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 labels this species “terrícola” (soil-associated). The row has no locality or survey date, so it cannot map where the species occurs.
