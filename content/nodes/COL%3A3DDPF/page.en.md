## What stands out about Euthystachys abbreviata? {#s-5490135889}
This resprouting shrublet grows up to 80 cm and is densely leafy. Four narrow leaves form each whorl, with two grooves underneath. Tiny white flowers hang in compact, nearly rounded spikes and are mostly hidden by long, leaf-like bracts. The flowers have a two-lipped calyx and hairs inside the corolla throat.

## Where does it grow? {#s-3cb1f275f4}
The habitat field records high-elevation sandstone slopes, without a more exact locality.
