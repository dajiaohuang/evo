## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Mabea klugii? {#s-e20276160b}
Leaves are oblong or elliptic, with tapering tips and blunt or rounded bases. The lower surface has red hairs and a waxy bloom only between the veins; glands occur along the margin. The simple flower cluster has umbrella-shaped subclusters with three male flowers each. Its bracts bear hairless, stalkless glands. Female flowers have six sepals longer than the ovary. The smooth fruit exceeds 15 mm; the style column may be under or over 10 mm.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-01631fc10b}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
