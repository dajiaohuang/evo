## Five-flowered heads on a white-woolly shrublet {#s-6633970f5e}
The regional account describes a white-woolly shrublet up to 20 cm tall. Its short leaves may be straight or twisted. Flower heads occur near the branch tips and contain five florets; the innermost white, petal-like bracts have recurved tips. The account records flowering from September to November and sandstone slopes in the Caledon Swartberg area.
