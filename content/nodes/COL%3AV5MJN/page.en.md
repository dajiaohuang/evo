## Examined mountain material and the broader summary {#s-f57584708c}
The male holotype SYS a006391 came from Mufu Mountain, Hunan, at 1300 m. Beyond the type material, the monograph examined a male and female from Dawei at about 800 m and two females from Jiuling at about 700 m. Its separate summary included Mufu, Jiuling and Guanshan Nature Reserve at 300–1300 m. The examined localities and the broader cited summary remain distinct; they do not demonstrate continuous occupancy across that interval.

## Reported measurements from eight examined adults {#s-f31ce4bc58}
The diagnosis reported three adult males at 29.9–30.8 mm snout–vent length and five females at 36.3–38.8 mm. These are diagnostic sample measurements rather than population abundance or universal adult size bounds, and they do not alone establish how size varies among mountain populations.

## Geographic isolation proposed for mitochondrial differences {#s-cb7080eb26}
The authors described relatively large mitochondrial differences among populations using three mitochondrial segments. They found no distinguishing morphological characters and treated the populations as conspecific, proposing geographic isolation as a possible explanation for the genetic differences. The conspecificity decision and isolation explanation are attributed interpretations; neither is independent evidence that reproductive isolation is absent or that geographic separation caused the observed divergence.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-08271dec01}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys mufumontana as with heels overlapping when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
