## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-965acaf766}
COL26.8 records Oligosoma robinsoni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-39d24c0ba2}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-2c11dcb51a}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
