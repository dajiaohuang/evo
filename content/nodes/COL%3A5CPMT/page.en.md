## Xyris lomatophylla {#s-f8252333ad}
## A ciliate leaf margin and many bracts

The record describes Xyris lomatophylla with flat, smooth leaf blades edged by fine hairs. Its flowering stalk is 50–70 cm long; the egg-shaped to cylindrical spike has 10–25 sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers. The habitat label is terrestrial.
