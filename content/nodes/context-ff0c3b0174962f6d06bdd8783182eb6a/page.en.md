## Name and classification in AmphiBIO {#s-b8ee386aad}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Petropedetes vulpiae as row Anf4468. The compilation assigns it to genus Petropedetes, family Petropedetidae, and order Anura, using Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. These traits describe the deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Terrestrial and aquatic foraging categories {#s-a106ace61a}
The row records these foraging categories: terrestrial and aquatic (Ter = 1; Aqu = 1). They do not detail seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries from earlier compilations; this row-level comparison is not captured here. Check the historical categories before reuse. No replacement is inferred, and the notice does not make every original entry erroneous.

## Twilight activity category {#s-d3020625fb}
The row lists twilight activity periods (Crepu = 1). These broad labels are not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Maximum adult body length: 46.5 mm {#s-b09707e8df}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length of 46.5 mm. For Anura, this means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, total length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.
