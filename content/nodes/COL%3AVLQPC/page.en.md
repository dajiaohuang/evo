## Branches and leaves {#s-3091b1ffa0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes glabrous, unarmed, cylindrical branches. Leaves are alternate and twice divided (bipinnate), with one or two pairs of pinnae. Stipules are listed as deciduous and lanceolate, with indistinct venation. Each pinna bears 14–29 pairs of leaflets: proximal ones are elliptic or lanceolate, distal ones lanceolate. Leaflet tips are blunt, notched, or end in a tiny point (mucronulate), and are not S-shaped; the two surfaces differ in color, and the blades are papery. The primary vein is straight and central or just off-center; secondary veins form loops near the margin (brochidodromous), and venation is conspicuous on at least one surface. Nectar glands are recorded on the petiole between pinna pairs and between leaflets; their shapes are listed as elliptic, circular, oval, or saucer-shaped, and concave.

## Inflorescences and flowers {#s-0cff136133}
Inflorescences are simple, axillary racemes shaped like heads, with a globose receptacle. Bracts and bracteoles are alike; the source lists them as persistent or deciduous and notes bracts with nectaries. Flowers are alike in form and sessile or nearly so. The calyx is cylindrical to bell-shaped, not inflated, green to greenish-white, finely hairy or glabrous, and 8–16 mm long. The corolla is funnel-shaped, white, 21–32 mm long, and distinctly veined; it is described as finely hairy or glabrous. The staminal tube is attached to the corolla; stamens are numerous, with filaments white and pink toward their tips. The flower has one carpel; its style is linear, slightly curved, and projects beyond the flower, while the stigma is not enlarged.

## Fruit and seed {#s-0a3abcfbfb}
The fruit is described as a simple, compressed, oblong follicle, straight or curved and opening along its ventral suture; the calyx persists. The seed stalk (funicle) is nearly thread-like. Seeds are elliptic to oblong or obovate, and dark brown. The seed coat is described as dry and papery-membranous, with no pleurogram. Cotyledons are plane-concave. The plumule (embryonic shoot) is developed and differentiated into pinna-like segments bearing rusty hairs.

## Habitat category {#s-f539eacf6b}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
