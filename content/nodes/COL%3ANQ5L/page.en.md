## Bulbinella triquetra: toothed leaves and yellow flowers {#s-2c11b4abce}
A South African regional account describes a rhizomatous geophyte up to 35 cm tall. Its threadlike leaves have fine teeth. Yellow flowers grow in a raceme ranging from nearly corymb-shaped to narrowly conical.

The diagnostic account compares *B. triquetra* with the similarly sized *B. divaginata* and *B. trinervis*, which also have narrow leaves. Their leaves are almost cylindrical and lack teeth, unlike the three-sided, finely toothed leaves of *B. triquetra*. The two comparison species flower in autumn, when their leaves are still developing; *B. triquetra* flowers in spring to early summer, after its leaves have finished developing. *B. trinervis* has white flowers, while *B. divaginata* and *B. triquetra* have yellow flowers. The latter two are distinguished by their leaf bases: *B. triquetra* has sheathing bases, whereas *B. divaginata* forms a fibrous sheath from separate cataphylls.
