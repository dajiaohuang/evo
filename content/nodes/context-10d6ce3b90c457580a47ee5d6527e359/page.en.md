## The source concept {#s-d4410989f9}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis bioko as row Anf0122 under Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); its correspondence to a current species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-6d2f7223bf}
The row flags terrestrial foraging. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the deposited category is retained without treating it as confirmed or necessarily wrong. Other stratum fields are missing, so their absence cannot be inferred.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-720207517a}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 32.7 mm. For anurans, this means snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
