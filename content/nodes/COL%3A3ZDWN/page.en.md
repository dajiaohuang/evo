## What flower-stalk and flower traits are recorded? {#s-ea04b0eab8}
A regional flora describes this branched herb as up to 0.9 m tall. Its epicalyx bracts are lance-shaped. Mature flower stalks are 10–40 mm long, as long as or longer than the leaf petioles, and bear one to three yellow flowers.

## Why did the revision question some historical names and collection localities? {#s-aee52c1f7c}
A 1981 revision found no basis for separating the Transvaal form from M. burchellii, despite differences in range and soil: near Potgietersrus and Pietersburg it grew in heavy gravelly loam, unlike the loose sands reported in the northwestern Cape. The revision suggested that a specimen named as M. albicans and attributed to Pilgrims Rest may instead have been collected near Eersteling, where its collector had stayed before traveling to Pilgrims Rest. It also questioned a “Zuloo Country” label on a Miss Owen specimen, since the collection may have been made after she left Zululand; Kuruman or Zeerust were suggested as alternatives. A Zeyher “Aapies River” locality was also considered doubtful because the species had not been found near Pretoria. The revision had not seen the syntypes of M. dinteri, but judged its description to fit this taxon best. These are the revision’s historical interpretations, not newly verified collection records.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-621bbb0dcb}
A regional flora records bushveld. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
