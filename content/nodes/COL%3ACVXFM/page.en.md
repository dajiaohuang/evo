## Naming Atrax christenseni {#s-16303e060a}
The large Newcastle males described in the study led researchers to reassess spiders previously assigned to A. robustus. They named the Newcastle population A. christenseni. In males, a longer palpal tibia and embolus distinguish it from similar species; size alone is not reliable.

## Known records near Newcastle {#s-a75d5dc01d}
The Australian Museum records cited in the account fall within 25 km of Newcastle, while precise localities are withheld. Biological information remains limited, so this concentration of known records is not a complete distribution map.
