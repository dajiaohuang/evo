## Hippeastrum leucobasis: flowers and seeds {#s-7163d14d2e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb and no rhizome. Leaves are strap-shaped with acute tips, and the inflorescence bears 2–4 flowers. The campanulate perianth has dark-red tips; its base is white, yellowish white, or light green to yellow. The short hypanthium tube is up to 2 cm. Sepals are slightly wider than the petals and are obovate or broadly elliptic; the lower lateral sepals differ little from the upper ones. The petals are elliptic, with the lower ones slightly smaller than or equal to the upper ones. The corona is free and fringed. Filaments are not fascicled and exceed the sepals and petals in length; the anthers are yellow or cream, and the stigma is globose-headed or headed with triangular lobes.

The capsule is brown inside. Seeds are numerous, flattened and disc-shaped.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
