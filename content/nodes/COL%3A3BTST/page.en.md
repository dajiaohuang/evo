## Euclea crispa (Thunb.) Gürke {#s-c65b80555d}
## Fragrant cream flowers on rusty, hairy clusters

Euclea crispa is a dioecious shrub or tree reaching about 8 m. Young growth has a rusty, granular covering, and the obovate to lance-shaped leaves may be hairy. Axillary racemes carry fragrant, cream-colored flowers with a deeply divided corolla; the ovary is bristly. The cited habitat is forest margins and wooded ravines.
