## Magenta flowers on a bristled cephalium {#s-079f023621}
The Portuguese description gives a globose to hemispherical stem with a white pith and no internal mucilage. It lists five to eight straight spines and a cephalium taller than wide, with conspicuous bristles but no brown tuft. Magenta flowers have perianth segments up to 23 mm; fruits may be red and white or magenta and white. The convex seed coat is noted. The habitat record includes rock-associated and terrestrial settings.
