## *Curtia verticillaris* {#s-05935a3c9a}
## Four to seven leaves at a node

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes upright stems, green or wine-colored to purple. Each node bears a whorl of 4–7 stalkless leaves; their recorded shapes range from narrow to broad elliptic, lance-shaped, or narrow ovate, and their colors from green to wine-colored or purple. The corolla may be white, blue, pink, lilac, or purple, and bell-shaped or tubular. Its inner tube is hairless. The calyx lobes are lance-shaped or narrow ovate and taper to a point; the stigma is head-shaped, without separate stigma lobes. The style persists on the fruit, whose capsule is elliptic. These alternatives are reported by one regional dataset field and do not describe their frequency or variation.
