## The deposited caecilian record {#s-2426a65bcc}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis longicephalus in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-e013678476}
The compilation lists two measurement records for this source concept, both at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of two distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-186f2c9afc}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-8ec9f548ce}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-ba63ed7ccf}
The species-level record gives a primary-fold count of 347.5 and a post-vent annulus (body-ring) count of 8.5. These decimal values may summarize records rather than describe one animal; they do not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic counts.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-8cb6b5a41c}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
