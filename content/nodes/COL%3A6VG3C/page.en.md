## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-f1c12bb10a}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Phyllomedusa burmeisteri as having larval development and its oviposition as terrestrial. A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. ## Molineid nematodes in a regional literature checklist {#s-aecd0ac021}
The 2025 review lists Phyllomedusa burmeisteri as a host in its Molineidae checklist, associating the written host name with Oswaldocruzia subauricularis. These are literature-compiled associations across different studies, rather than a newly examined sample or an infection measured in every member of this species. This host appears in the additional-host entries of the associated parasite accounts. The cited reports and archived specimens have not been independently reidentified here.
