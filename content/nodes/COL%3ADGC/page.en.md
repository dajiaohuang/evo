## form {#s-57f32cc3b9}
Many adult brush-footed butterflies hold their reduced forelegs against the body instead of using them for walking, so they can appear to stand on four legs. This is a family-level visual clue; identifying a species takes additional characters.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-3de8d9f91a}
This account describes a common adult foreleg form. It does not establish the characters of every descendant, a complete range, measured traits, a phylogeny or a current conservation status; each requires its own evidence.
