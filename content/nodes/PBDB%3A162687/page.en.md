## A database branch for finding species records {#s-4f2d5772d9}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) labels Novialoidea an “unranked clade”: a named group without a formal rank such as family or order. This route links to occurrence guides for 42 species in the fixed pterosaur roster. The database’s parent path helps organize those records; it is a catalogue hierarchy, not a verdict that all researchers use the same phylogenetic arrangement.

## Compare each collection on its own terms {#s-db367338ef}
To begin, compare the pages for Anurognathus ammoni, Batrachognathus volans, Dendrorhynchoides mutoudengensis, Dendrorhynchoides curvidentatus and Jeholopterus ningchengensis. Across the 42 species represented here, the selected guides cover 100 occurrences from 75 collections. Their recorded strata include Solnhofener Plattenkalk, Karabastau, Tiaojishan and Yixian. Check each collection’s age bracket and identification notes before comparing places. Species, occurrence and collection totals count different things; none gives the size of an ancient population.

## Evidence limits {#s-7374f0ab94}
This route covers selected occurrence guides for 42 species; it is neither a complete list of descendants nor a shared ecological profile for the group. Record counts do not measure specimens, individuals, population size or sampling effort, and broad age bounds do not directly date the group’s origin or extinction. PBDB’s classification path is retained as a record-navigation structure, separate from COL and from phylogenetic hypotheses.
