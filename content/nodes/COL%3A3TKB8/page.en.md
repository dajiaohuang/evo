## A strongly supported sister comparison uses one short marker {#s-6b7be4626a}
The newly sampled kungfu lineage was recovered sister to the yunkaiensis reference with reported bootstrap 99 and Bayesian posterior probability 1.00, at mean 16S p-distance 4.9%. The complete matrix was 512 base pairs and contained 110 new and reference samples, including six new kungfu individuals and two outgroups. These are not 110 newly sampled yunkaiensis frogs. The result does not certify nuclear-genome isolation, historical dispersal paths or a universal species threshold.
