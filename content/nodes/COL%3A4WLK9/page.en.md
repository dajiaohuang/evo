## Senecio lanifer {#s-0ece421239}
## Woolly leaves line the upright stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, herbaceous perennial with leaves along the full stem. The sessile leaves are lance-shaped to oblanceolate, with a squared-off base. Woolly hairs cover the upper face and fine woolly hairs the lower face.

Corymb-shaped clusters carry radiate heads ringed by 16–24 involucral bracts. The strap-shaped ray florets and tubular central florets are both yellow. The dry fruits are cylindrical to inversely cone-shaped, ribbed and hairless. Their white pappus—a crown of bristles at the fruit tip—persists. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
