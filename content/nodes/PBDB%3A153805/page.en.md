## How the 2023 revision treats the name {#s-e161ce990b}
The 2023 revision treats holotype GMN-03-11-001 as indistinguishable from Sinopterus dongi and regards Huaxiapterus jii as its junior synonym. PBDB still lists the older name. That database entry does not represent a second confirmed biological species.

## What the holotype preserves {#s-914c4f6f58}
The holotype is nearly complete, but the back of the skull is damaged and displaced. Only two separate neck vertebrae are visible, so the full cervical length sequence cannot be compared. The foot bones are also disarticulated, preventing a reliable comparison of metatarsal proportions. The estimated nasoantorbital opening is about three times as long as high, based on a partly preserved jugal rather than an intact rim.

## Crests and skull proportions {#s-2c41cb620c}
The snout bends down by about 14° relative to the rear palate. Its premaxillary crest has the same parabolic outline as the Sinopterus dongi holotype but is larger; the lower-jaw crest is larger too. A partly preserved left jugal has three branches. The original diagnosis emphasized crest depth without a precise measurement, so crest size alone does not establish a separate species, sex or diet.

## Comparing the wing bones {#s-e2df6ec188}
The first metacarpal, a hand bone in the wing, is at least 90% as long as the fourth and reaches the wrist. The fourth wing phalanx is about 0.30 the length of the first. The revision treats both proportions as shared with Sinopterus dongi; corollatus and benxiensis material has shorter first metacarpals and terminal wing phalanges. These comparisons do not reconstruct the whole wing membrane or measure flight speed.

## Bone fusion and growth stage {#s-e5a8539b61}
Several bones remain unfused: parts of the rear skull, shoulder girdle, humeral ends, wrist, the tendon process on the first wing phalanx and the lower leg. Some back vertebrae have partly fused centra and arches, with sutures still visible; this is more developed than some fully unfused, disarticulated vertebrae in the Sinopterus dongi holotype. The authors interpret both specimens as juveniles, with jii slightly further developed. The evidence does not give an exact age, sex or complete adult form.

## The reported size of the holotype {#s-fd9e0ed4e0}
The 2023 account reports a 79 mm humerus and a 1,602 mm wingspan for GMN-03-11-001, larger than the Sinopterus dongi holotype. Both specimens are immature, so the measurements must be read alongside their bone-fusion states. They are the authors’ reported values, not independent remeasurements, and the roughly 1.6 m span is not a maximum adult wingspan for the species.

## Comparing the named forms {#s-201828ac8d}
The revision compares the holotype and growth interpretation with Sinopterus dongi, then contrasts its longer first metacarpal and wing phalanges with Huaxiapterus corollatus. These are the specimen comparisons behind the proposed synonymy; incomplete preservation and inferred maturity limit what the comparisons can establish.
