## Evidence limits {#s-03f21de98f}
This account brings together historical AVONET v7, life-history and EltonTraits 1.0 records. AVONET means summarize the listed measurement samples, not identification diagnoses or individual ranges; mass is separately sourced and is not covered by morphology sample counts. Habitat, trophic niche, lifestyle and migration are coarse categories, while the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. The life-history archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity is the largest collected record. These are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data; matching names, family and sparse rows support an association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. EltonTraits food and foraging-stratum percentages are standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty; mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may refer to a congener considered ecologically similar by the compilers. The records do not establish direct observation of this species for every field. Individual, seasonal and local variation, behavior beyond these categories, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
