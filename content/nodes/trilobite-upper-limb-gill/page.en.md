## Did trilobite upper limbs act as gills? {#s-13939a3e41}
Electron and X-ray imaging of Triarthrus eatoni filaments records a narrow centre with expanded margins; Olenoides serratus preserves a partial body-wall attachment. These forms resemble respiratory branches in living arthropods, supporting a gill-function hypothesis for the trilobite upper limb branch. Fossils do not measure gas exchange or haemolymph flow, and comparisons across two taxa do not settle limb-branch homology.
