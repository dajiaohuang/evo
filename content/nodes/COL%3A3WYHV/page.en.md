## ## Leaflet form and two possible fruit surfaces {#s-5fd3696a4e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists branches as armed or unarmed and cylindrical or flattened. The leaves have leaf-like stipules; their leaflets are elliptic, obovate or ovate, with tips ranging from tapered or cuspidate to blunt and bases rounded or tapering. The upper surfaces may be becoming hairless or remain hairy, while the lower surfaces are hairy. Panicles or fascicles occur in the leaf axils; bract form is marked as unknown. The flowers have a bell-shaped calyx, white or greenish petals and a hairy standard; the stamens form one bundle. The fruit is a samara, and its seed-bearing region is listed as smooth or warty. A habitat field says “terrestrial”; it gives no locality or more detailed habitat information.
