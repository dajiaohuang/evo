## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-9c1d5d0804}
Mariae is contrasted by vomerine teeth, flat dorsal warts, translucent esophageal/stomach peritonea, yellow dorsal spots or reticulation and adult-male SVL25.4–31.7mm. Dajomesae lacks vomerine teeth, has shagreen skin, white peritonea, uniform green and male SVL20.8–27.2mm. The ranges belong to the cited male comparison, not females or tadpoles.

## The sampled relationship retains support and label limits {#s-831611513a}
The new tree places chancas in a clade with balionotus, colomai, grandisonae, manduriacu and mariae, rather than as sister to the rest of Nymphargus as in an earlier analysis. The paper associates such disagreements with weakly supported nodes. Membership in this reported clade does not settle the exact branching order or supply a new species-specific divergence time.
