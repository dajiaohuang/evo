## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-67bc186548}
COL26.8 records Chitra chitra as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trionychidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-49440a270b}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 100. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-c1b8eaf434}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
