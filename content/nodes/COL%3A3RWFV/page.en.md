## Lagenaria siceraria {#s-2345c9f03a}
## White flowers on a cultivated annual vine

Lagenaria siceraria is described as an annual herb that sprawls or climbs. Male and female flowers occur on the same plant, and the male flowers are solitary and white. Glands near the leaf-stalk tip are faint and curve backward. The source notes cultivation for its fruits and occurrence along rivers, but gives no specific locality.
