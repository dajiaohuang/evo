## The tail is outside the length measure {#s-6eeb1c4828}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 57 mm, with a female maximum of 47.8 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent and excludes the tail. These compiled maxima are not typical sizes or theoretical growth limits. Since the sex-specific records may come from different studies or places, their difference alone cannot establish a population’s size dimorphism.
