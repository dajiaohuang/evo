## Flowers and floral measurements {#s-4fdaec5061}
The 2012 regional account compares *B. tubiflora* with *B. tubulosa*: *B. tubiflora* has uniformly creamy-white flowers or red marks on the lower tepals, a tube with a short, inconspicuous gullet, filaments 13–16 mm long, and style branches 3–4 mm long.

This comparison comes from a regional South African account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Distinguishing Babiana tubulosa {#s-c6475f8b4b}
A 2020 diagnostic account describes *B. tubiflora* as usually small, with relatively small white to creamy-white flowers, often marked with red or pink at the base of the lower tepals, and often narrow, linear leaves. The perianth tube is elongated, uniformly slender or only gradually expanded near the tip. It is usually 60–80 mm long, but can be shorter or longer. The filaments are 13–16 mm long, and the 3–4 mm style branches often do not reach the tips of the anthers.

The account says *B. tubiflora* is closely related to, and often confused with, *B. tubulosa*. The latter is described as a local endemic of the Atlantic Coast and nearby interior of Western Cape, with creamy-beige to pink flowers usually flushed pink outside and prominent red markings on the lower tepals. Its perianth tube is 65–105 mm long, widening near the tip into a broader gullet 5–12 mm long. Its longer filaments measure 20–22 mm; the style divides opposite or just beyond the anther tips, with branches about 6 mm long.

These diagnostic comparisons come from a regional South African account published in 2020; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-312c5ec5c2}
The regional flora records the species on sandy flats, lower slopes, and dunes.

This habitat description comes from a regional South African account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
