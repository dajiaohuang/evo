## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ab7d6f72ce}
COL26.8 records Raclitia indica as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Homalopsidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-dbc19e7678}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 470 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-db1660ebf4}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0566c4a2ee}
The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-356200412f}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
