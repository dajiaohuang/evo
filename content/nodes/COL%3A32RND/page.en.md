## Cybianthus barrosoanus G.Agostini {#s-5ddf5b0087}
## Smooth, leathery leaves; flower details unknown

Cybianthus barrosoanus has cylindrical terminal branches more than 3 mm in diameter and leaves without swollen nodes. The alternate leaves are elliptic to oblanceolate, leathery and smooth, with acute to acuminate tips and acute bases; the secondary veins are not evident. Panicles may arise at terminal or axillary nodes. The corolla is wheel-shaped with rounded lobes. The source marks the male and female flowers, stamens and immature fruit wall as unknown.
