## *Staberoha banksii*: long female spikelets {#s-69b1fc8cbd}
This tufted perennial reaches 1 m and has simple stems. Its female spikelets measure 2.5–5 cm, with bracts 1.2–1.5 cm long, forming a notably elongated flower cluster. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

It is recorded on sandstone slopes and flats, usually below 800 m.
