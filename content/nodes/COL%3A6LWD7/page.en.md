## What does the record report for Hirtella radamii? {#s-26b39b4e2b}
Young branches bear a sparse covering of stiff hairs. The lance-shaped to oblong leaves lack the ant-associated cavities assessed in the record. Flowers grow in racemes; the bracts carry glands without stalks, and the smaller bracteoles may have stalked or unstalked glands. Each flower has six stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-db9013622e}
The dataset classifies it as terrestrial, without a named locality.
