## How can Stachytarpheta alata be recognized? {#s-a0d1bcd7cd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes upright, four-angled stems and opposite, oval leaves on stalks. The leaf edges range from shallowly scalloped to smooth, and both surfaces have hairs. Dense terminal spikes bear blue, salver-shaped flowers, with two fertile stamens and two reduced sterile stamens. Each dry fruit splits into two one-seeded segments.
