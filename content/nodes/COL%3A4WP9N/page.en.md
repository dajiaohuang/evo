## Leaf glands, leaflet forms and pods in the record {#s-7475152057}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records pubescent stems and elliptic stipules. Petioles are listed as under 5 cm or 6–10 cm, and the rachis as over 21 cm. Extrafloral nectaries occur between all leaflet pairs; they are listed as sessile, subsessile or stalked, with lanceolate or ovate forms. Leaflet counts are 3 pairs, 5 pairs or 11–15 pairs; leaflets are oblong or lanceolate with obtuse or emarginate tips. The raceme has ovate or cymbiform bracts. Flowers are asymmetric, with a central adaxial petal that is obovate or oblong and seven fertile stamens. The legume is dehiscent and erect, described as flat or linear and externally depressed between seeds. Seeds are arranged in one series. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
