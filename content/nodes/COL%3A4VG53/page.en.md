## *Schultesia pachyphylla* {#s-0c891384b9}
## A purple flower with a salver-shaped corolla

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a cylindrical stem and papery leaves. It records the same acrodromous–brochidodromous venation label used for veins that run toward the leaf tip and join in loops near the edge. The flower is purple and its calyx is winged, with ovate lobes ending in acute tips. The corolla is hypocrateriform, or salver-shaped: a narrow tube opens into a spreading rim. Those oblong lobes have blunt tips. The filaments are simple, and the seed coat is ruminate, with an irregularly folded or grooved surface. The description is one regional dataset record, not a survey of variation across the species.
