## Accepted name and placement {#s-c640f73c29}
COL26.8 records Pristimantis ecuadorensis Guayasamin, Hutter, Tapia, Culebras, Peñafiel, Pyron, Morochz, Funk & Arteaga-Navarro, 2017 as an accepted species. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). This is the classification in the release issued on 20 August 2026; it establishes catalogue placement, not a biological account or identity match to another checklist or fossil record.

## Three sampled Andean localities {#s-e193df3f02}
The study separates P. ecuadorensis from lowland P. ornatissimus using congruent genetic and morphological evidence. It records P. ecuadorensis at three nearby western-Andean localities in Cotopaxi and Pichincha, 1,450–1,480 m; P. ornatissimus sensu stricto was below 1,100 m in the study. At the type locality, the frog was found in primary forest among bromeliad, Heliconia and palm leaf axils, and at native-forest edges with banana and sugar-cane plantings.
