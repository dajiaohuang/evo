## Richterago stenophylla {#s-13ba16947e}
## Narrow rosette leaves rise on a woolly scape

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a plant with a flowering scape emerging from ground-level leaves. The linear leaves are leathery, hairless, with prominent veins, sharp tips and entire, rolled-under margins. Their short stalks widen at the sides and have a rust-coloured velvety covering.

Radiate heads grow in corymb-shaped clusters on branching, bracted stalks. Each cylindrical to top-shaped involucre has 5–9 rows of bracts. Ten white, bilabiate female rays—some with a wine-coloured stripe beneath—surround 30 white bisexual disc florets. The dry fruits are cylindrical and silky. Their one-row pappus has 25–42 finely toothed bristles joined at the base by a fleshy ring.
