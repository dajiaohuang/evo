## Three-forked leaves and short flower stalks {#s-62a6a0d6f1}
The record describes mostly three-forked, smooth leaves with narrow, linear lobes and relatively short flower stalks. Its fruits have smooth ridges. These traits distinguish it from E. brachypodus, whose leaf lobes are more pointed and whose leaf axils are woolly. It is recorded on rocky slopes in fynbos and at forest margins.
