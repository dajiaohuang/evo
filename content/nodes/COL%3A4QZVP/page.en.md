## Stem form in two regional descriptions {#s-00647eecd2}
A 2013 South African flora account compares *Quaqua armata* with *Q. mammillaris*. It describes *Q. armata* as mat-forming, with stems rooting on branches and measuring 20–30 mm in diameter; it also notes fewer, blunter teeth, purple mottling, and flowers often brown to yellowish. The comparison is relative: the excerpt does not provide a full description of *Q. mammillaris*.

## Stem form in two regional descriptions {#s-bb54c97b11}
A 2005 diagnostic treatment calls *Q. armata* and *Q. arenicola* similar and says both form clumps, with side stems that root readily at their bases. The passage then turns to features specifically described for *Q. arenicola*; those details cannot be assigned to *Q. armata*. The two excerpts describe stem rooting in different terms, and the available text does not reconcile them.

## Habitat in the South African account {#s-10a1e07801}
The regional flora lists gravelly and sandy flats and slopes as habitat for *Q. armata*. This is a habitat description in that account, not a mapped locality or a complete range: the excerpt supplies no observation date, site, or population frequency.
