## What is recorded about its central leaflet and flower clusters? {#s-e2a59a90c8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes one central leaflet with an elliptic blade, pointed tip and base, and differently colored upper and lower surfaces. The petiole is conspicuous, but the leaflet stalk is not; no domatia—small structures at leaf-vein junctions—are recorded. Flower clusters are compact and branch as a simple dichasium. The source allows either up to seven or more than seven flowers per cluster, so it does not give one fixed count.

## What habitat category accompanies the morphology? {#s-495591a57a}
The habitat field classifies Vitex gardneriana as terrestrial. It gives no collection locality or full range.
