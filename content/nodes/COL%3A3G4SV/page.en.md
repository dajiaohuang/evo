## A late-season leaf on a separate shoot {#s-391dda1823}
Gladiolus martleyi flowers on a 20–35 cm plant with reduced leaves on the flowering stem. Later, one terete (round in cross-section), four-grooved leaf develops from a separate shoot. The pink to mauve funnel-shaped flowers have yellow bands or red-edged spear-shaped marks on the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals).

## Sandy and rocky low slopes {#s-6e5a3e3611}
The cited flora records sandy and rocky flats and lower slopes up to 200 m.
