## A fragmentary account of leaves, sepals and flowers {#s-7115986003}
The morphology field begins with the fragment “below, apical spine strongly recurved” but does not identify what “below” refers to. It also records glabrous sepals with ciliate margins and straight spine tips, hispid leaves and young branches, and mauve flowers. The ecology field gives montane grassland. The incomplete diagnostic text does not define a complete range.
