## Distinguish the measured length {#s-557cf2205c}
The record lists maximum snout–vent length (SVL) as 232.1 mm and maximum total length (TL) as 269 mm. SVL ends at the vent; TL includes the tail, so these measures are not interchangeable. Both are compiled maxima, not typical size or absolute limits. Sex-specific records may come from different places or studies.

## Where and how it is active {#s-34d76e31b8}
The entry records a Fossorial microhabitat and a Carnivorous diet category. Fossorial indicates an underground or burrowing association; Carnivorous does not identify particular prey. Neither field provides a complete account of habitat or feeding.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-0ab67308b0}
The row classifies the species as oviparous and gives a mean of 3 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch. This summary can be the midpoint of reported means, or of reported extremes when means are unavailable; it is not necessarily an arithmetic mean of observed clutches. A fractional value does not mean a fraction of an offspring. The lowest and highest fields refer to reported means, not guaranteed individual clutch limits.
