## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a2609ab24b}
COL26.8 records Myriopholis macrorhyncha as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Leptotyphlopidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-34d76e31b8}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0ab67308b0}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-0b02d37a3e}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
