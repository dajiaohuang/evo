## *Felicia quinquenervia*: blue flowers and five-veined leaves {#s-3a8903d168}
The regional account describes a perennial herb up to 0.6 m tall. Leaves form a basal rosette and also occur along the stem; their blades are inversely egg-shaped to elliptic, up to 30 mm wide, with three to five veins starting at the base. The leaves may be hairy or hairless. Blue flowers sit within an involucre, the cup of overlapping bracts around a flower head; its bracts are equal in length and arranged in three rows. The account records grassland habitat.
