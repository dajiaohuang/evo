## *Chromolaena tecta*: narrow leaves and stalked heads {#s-a8a22f5dc2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, linear or lance-shaped leaves with three main veins. The upper surface is hairless; the lower may have glandular dots, woolly hairs, or short stiff hairs. The edges are entire or serrated. Stalked heads form corymb-like clusters inside cylindrical bract cups. The bracts lie close against the cup and are hairless. The inversely cone-shaped fruitlets are bristly, with rough pappus bristles. These are alternatives in a regional morphology field.
