## Recorded parent of Desmatosuchia {#s-ca1f4c7f2d}
Desmatosuchia is listed at rank suborder in the PBDB classification route, with Stagonolepididae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
