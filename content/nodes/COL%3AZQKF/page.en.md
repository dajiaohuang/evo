## What leaf and flower structures mark Croton megalocalyx? {#s-b8818b21b7}
Young branches bear outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked oval, oval-lanceolate, or elliptic leaves have the same hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and toothed margins. A stalked nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are shortly stalked, with fused sepals, unequal calyx lobes, and a two-lobed style. Female petals are threadlike, the bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
