## Trichilia pallens {#s-21911c83fc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives this species several leaflet, petal-arrangement and ovule-count alternatives, plus globose or ovoid fruit.

## Three-part or odd-pinnate leaves and papilose fruit {#s-2b15be3792}
The stem-bud field records no cataphylls and simple hairs. Leaves may be three-foliolate or odd-pinnate, with an unwinged rachis and opposite leaflets; basal leaflets are not heteromorphic. Their bases are acute, attenuate or decurrent, and the lower surface is hairy. Petals are free and arranged imbricately or quincuncially; staminal filaments are fully united. The nectary may be absent or present, and the ovary is glabrous and three-locular with one or two collateral ovules per locule. Fruits are globose or ovoid and papilose. The ecology entry is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), not a mapped locality or complete range.
