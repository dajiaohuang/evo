## The source concept {#s-f7a358811f}
AmphiBIO v1 records Hyla zhaopingensis in row Anf2336 under Hyla, Hylidae and Anura, using the species concept in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The measurements and categories below are values deposited in that compilation. This row has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept, so read it as a source record rather than a confirmed modern taxon profile.

## Foraging strata {#s-74c88cdb3a}
AmphiBIO marks terrestrial and aquatic as foraging categories for this row. These broad labels do not give seasonal or life-stage detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; the affected row-level comparison has not been captured here. Treat these historical labels as needing review. No replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show that every original value is wrong.

## Developmental strategy {#s-c37934552f}
The deposited row flags a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
