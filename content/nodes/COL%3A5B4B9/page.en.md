## Vernonanthura westiniana {#s-319947a528}
## Gland-dotted woolly leaves below apiculate bracts

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes erect, woolly stems with glandular dots. Papery, petiolate leaves are lance-shaped to oblong, with acute tips and bases. Both faces bear glands; the upper face has stiff hairs and the underside is woolly or bristly. Secondary veins arch toward the margin and fade before reaching it.

Involucral bracts end in a small point. Each head contains 8–22 florets; the corolla is hairless and lacks glands. The bristly dry fruits are glandular, and the outer pappus bristles are flattened. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
