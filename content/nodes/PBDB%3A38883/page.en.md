## Pelycosauria is retained as a historical grade {#s-0aeeee4772}
Brocklehurst and colleagues analyse “pelycosaurian-grade” synapsids as an early, non-crown sampling grade. The atlas therefore retains Pelycosauria as a historical grade, not as a monophyletic ancestor taxon. The 308–260 Ma interval follows the review’s sampled grade and does not assert exact endpoints, continuous occupancy or a single ancestral lineage.
