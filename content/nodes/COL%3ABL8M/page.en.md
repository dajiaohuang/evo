## Aldama amphichlora {#s-f08d96a697}
## Tufted shoots with spiny pappus awns

Flora do Brasil 2020 records underground stems with firm, tufted aerial branches; the degree of root thickening is unknown. Leaves are sessile, arranged in mixed patterns, and lance-shaped with a small terminal point. Radiate flower heads have acute involucral bracts and blunt-tipped paleae on the receptacle. The ray corolla forms one whorl; tubular florets are minutely bristly, and disc florets lack glands.

The broadly obovoid dry fruits are covered in stiff hairs. Their pappus awns are joined to the scales and lack a keel.
