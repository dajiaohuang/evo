## Pleated leaves and bell-shaped flowers {#s-019fa54f23}
Flora do Brasil records underground bulbs and pleated leaf blades 4–5 mm wide. The blue to pale sky-blue or violet flowers have a free, bell-shaped perianth; the style is about as long as the stamens, and the capsule (a seed-bearing fruit) is long and oblong-elliptic.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-28e0dcfc4a}
The source classifies Gelasine rigida as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant). This record does not supply a more detailed habitat description.
