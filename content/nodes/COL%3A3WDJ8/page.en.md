## ## Leaflet surfaces and winged fruit {#s-593ac5f897}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records leaves with three to nine leaflets. It describes leaflet outlines from broadly elliptic to ovate or nearly round, usually finely toothed margins, and blunt, acute or rounded tips that may be notched. The leaflets and leaf rachis are densely woolly or downy, and each leaflet pulvinus is under 6 mm long. The erect panicles have oval to elliptic bracteoles; the inflorescence axis and calyx bear tawny to dark-brown silky hairs. Flowers are over 13 mm long, with white and pink petals, an obovate standard and oblong wings and keel. The fruit has lateral wings beside the seed-bearing region. The same regional dataset labels the habitat “terrestrial”; it supplies no locality or broader habitat account.
