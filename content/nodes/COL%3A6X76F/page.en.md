## A protruding flower base and magenta fruit {#s-189399de08}
Its flattened or three-winged stem segments end in crenate tips, with new growth at the segment ends. Lateral flowers have glabrous areoles, a wheel-shaped corolla and white filaments. The flower base projects beyond the stem and is longer than the perianth segments. The flattened globose fruit is magenta and lacks areoles. Epiphytic and rock-associated habitats are both listed.
