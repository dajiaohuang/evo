## Leaflets and upright flower clusters {#s-b09803c703}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records leaflike stipules positioned laterally and 5–6 pairs of leaflets, which are not sickle-shaped. Its inflorescences are short and upright. Sepals differ in size and have no nectar glands; the floral cup is regular in form. The staminal tube is short and regular, with ten fertile stamens. The stigma is blunt to head-shaped. These traits come from one regional row and do not establish complete variation or a global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-48cc3fa71e}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It supplies only a habitat category, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
