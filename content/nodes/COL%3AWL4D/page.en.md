## Coccoloba rosea {#s-d36d4c8e95}
## Persistent leaf-base sheaths and variable fruit coverings

Coccoloba rosea has erect branches and leaves that range from oval or obovate to heart-shaped or rounded. Their persistent leaf-base sheaths, called ochreae, join the petioles; the lower surfaces and sheaths may be finely hairy or pubescent. Elongated, raceme-like flower clusters bear bell-shaped, two-lobed bracteoles. The fruiting perianth may be ovoid or globose and leathery or succulent; its lobes do not enlarge. The nut is ovoid to nearly spherical with a pointed tip. The habitat label is terrestrial.
