## Form and flowers {#s-002fde0524}
The regional account describes a cormous geophyte 150–300 mm tall, with woody, overlapping corm tunics and a velvety stem. Sword-shaped leaves have slightly thickened margins and midribs. A few flowers grow on each spike; they are blue-violet, with white at the tepal bases and in the tube. The perianth tube is 18–25 mm long, slightly longer than the tepals. Tepals spread outward, and the stamens are usually slightly unequal.

## Diagnostic comparison {#s-190c6ae16d}
A separate SANBI account distinguishes the deep-blue flowers, paler in the throat, and the elongated perianth tube, 18–25 mm long and slightly longer than the long floral bracts. The style is relatively short and divides opposite the lower third of the anthers. Stems and leaves have dense, pale, almost velvety hairs. *Geissorhiza namaquensis* and *G. namaquamontana* are described as similar in vegetative form, with short-haired stems and leaves with raised, winged margins; their flowers are paler blue to blue-mauve and their perianth tubes shorter, only 6–11 mm.
