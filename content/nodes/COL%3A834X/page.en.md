## Eleven species entries anchor this route {#s-cdb8077034}
The ReptTraits route assembled here contains 11 species entries placed under Tropiocolotes by the pinned COL26.8 ancestry; each retains citations for its individual traits and the workbook’s 2023 naming framework. Five starting points are Tropiocolotes algericus, T. bisharicus, T. hormozganensis, T. nattereri and T. naybandensis. The route is a selection of sourced records, not a count of every accepted species in the genus.

## Two length records are not a genus range {#s-2f6ddf5ebb}
Among the entries, Tropiocolotes somalicus has a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 28 mm, while T. tripolitanus has 37 mm. Each figure belongs to a different species. Together they are not a lower and upper boundary for the genus, nor a measure of variation within either species. Other traits need their own sources and definitions before they can be compared.

## The 2015 archive covers two named records {#s-8e21dd4dfe}
The separate 2015 life-history archive contributes records for Tropiocolotes nattereri and T. steudneri. Both have entries for adult body mass, offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, time from oviposition to hatching, and the archive’s longest-lifespan field. These counts describe this route’s coverage only. Before comparing numbers, check what event each field measures, its unit and the historical source attached to it.

## Compare only traits that both records support {#s-e3e04f9c5c}
Each species page should be read for the fields it actually documents. Compare size, activity, diet or reproduction only where both entries have supporting sources; a missing value is not supplied from a relative. Measurements also need to match: straight carapace length (SCL) in turtles and snout–vent length (SVL) in other reptiles are not interchangeable.

## A route through records, not a complete genus account {#s-541e19dfd1}
This page links selected descendant entries with sourced focal traits and matching COL names and families. It does not cover every Tropiocolotes species or all known biology. Reported maxima are compiled records, not average sizes or identification cutoffs. Historical one-row data can hide population variation and do not settle every taxonomic revision; read each field with its source and aggregation rule.

## What the route leaves open {#s-c312821e28}
The trait route and the historical life-history route use different inclusion rules, and neither is a complete descendant catalogue. The 2015 figures retain the archive’s median or midpoint summaries, its maximum-record definition for longevity, and possible inheritance across historical species and subspecies. A group page cannot imply that all descendants share one reproductive mode or life history. Full biology and current conservation status are outside these records.
