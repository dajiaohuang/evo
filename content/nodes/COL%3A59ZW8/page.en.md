## The name and this data account {#s-7888fc1c14}
COL26.8 accepts Tyto soumagnei in Tytonidae. AVONET v7 uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 4 measured individuals, 3 with a complete set of measurements. A trait may still have a smaller usable sample.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-2aff8a47f2}
Mean beak length is 14.8 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; mean width is 7 mm and depth is 12.2 mm at the nostril edge. These are separate measurements at defined landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-cc16164a25}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 210.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 55 mm. The compiled mean mass is 284 g and carries source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information, so the AVONET measurement count does not mean those birds were weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-91788ea4f4}
The dataset codes habitat as Forest, trophic niche as Vertivore and lifestyle as Generalist. Its categories summarize the record for comparison; they do not mean the owl uses only forest, eats only vertebrates or has one fixed activity pattern.

The only nonzero food weight is mammals and birds 100%. Source code A marks high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. This percentage standardizes a literature description; it is not a direct measurement of diet, and unlisted foods are not proven absent.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-13d64030a1}
