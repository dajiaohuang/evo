COL26.8 records Aipysurus duboisii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-53e77cd071}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Greer 1997. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
