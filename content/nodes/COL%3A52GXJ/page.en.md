## Stevia lucida {#s-4507ee190f}
## A gland-dotted shrub with a low scale crown

The supplied description records a shrub up to 2 m tall, with stems, leaves, flowering branches and involucral bracts that appear varnished. Opposite leaves may reach 16 by 5 cm. They range from oblong-ovate to lance-shaped, are somewhat folded lengthwise, and have toothed margins; both surfaces are densely dotted with glands. The lower surface is paler and fleshier, and leaf stalks reach 2 cm.

The flat-topped flowering cluster is corymb-shaped. Heads are mostly 9–11 mm high; their narrow bracts are 5–6 mm long and gland-dotted. Pink corollas are 5–6 mm long, with equal, smooth lobes. The dry fruits are bristly. Their pappus is a low crown of joined or separate toothed scales, about 0.2 mm high, without awns. The description gives no habitat or locality, so a complete range is not established.
