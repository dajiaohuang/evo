## Southeastern Xizang and northwestern Yunnan records {#s-af4f622b8d}
Male holotype BNHS 6055 came from Medog, Xizang, at 450 m; the type list also included Cona material at 1110 m. The monograph cited Mahony and colleagues (2018) and Shi and colleagues (2020) for distribution in Cona, Medog and Gongshan, Yunnan, at 260–2236 m. These recorded localities and cited elevation summary do not demonstrate continuous occupancy or physiological tolerances across the interval.

## Original-description adult measurements {#s-ac8a0d85fd}
The diagnosis based on the original description reported twelve adult males at 71.3–93.8 mm snout–vent length and one adult female at 112.0 mm. The cited diagnostic denominator is not treated as a new measurement series in this monograph. Characters included vomerine ridges and teeth, small lower-jaw asperities, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with absent or indistinct narrow fringes. Breeding males had black or brown nuptial spines on finger I and II pads and a subgular vocal sac. The female value is one observation, not a female range or a population sex-size estimate.
