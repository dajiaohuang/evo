## What is known about Anaxagorea manausensis? {#s-9a596ef128}
The field describes narrow elliptic leaves with long-tapering tips; the upper surface is hairless, while the lower may be hairless or sparsely hairy. Flowers are solitary and may arise in leaf axils, on branches, or directly from older stems. The fruit’s color, hair covering, and number of segments are not specified in this record.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-2b22444b9d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field categorizes it as terrestrial. No specific habitat or locality is given.
