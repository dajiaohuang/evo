## Pouteria decussata (Ducke) Baehni {#s-cce96602d6}
## Distichous leaves and unknown fruit characters

Pouteria decussata has young branches that are smooth or becoming hairless, with lenticels present. Its alternate, two-ranked leaves have cylindrical petioles; the side veins curve upward near the base and loop toward the margin near the tip. Axillary or branch-borne clusters carry flowers with four or five sepals and a cup-shaped corolla with four or five lobes. The lobes have entire margins and pointed tips; the ovary has three chambers. Fruit and seed traits are recorded as unknown.
