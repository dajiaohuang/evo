## Chaohusaurus: pregnancy preserved in a fossil {#s-e99a848414}
The Early Triassic specimen AGM I-1 preserves a maternal skeleton, two embryos inside the body, and a neonate beside the pelvis. One embryo lies head-first near the pelvic region. This association directly records pregnancy in Chaohusaurus; the proposed birth orientation and the idea that live birth began on land are interpretations of position and evolutionary context, not observations in the fossil.
