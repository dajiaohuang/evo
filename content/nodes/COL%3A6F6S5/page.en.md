## Red tubular flowers in corymbs {#s-5c0947e9a6}
This morphology record describes leaves that may have stalks or clasp the stem, with entire or toothed edges. Peduncled, disk-shaped heads form corymb-like clusters, and their tubular bisexual flowers are red. The cylindrical fruits have ten ribs and hairs; the white pappus persists. The habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
