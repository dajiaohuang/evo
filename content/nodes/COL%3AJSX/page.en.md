## Scope of the historical route summaries {#s-7117b51f34}
The PanTHERIA comparison covers represented same-name, same-family entries with at least three focal fields and one-to-one historical correspondence; it does not cover every accepted descendant. Comparison endpoints are consolidated historical values rather than individual extremes, and source conditions can differ by variable.

The life-history route covers same-name, same-family entries with at least three source-supported focal fields. Compilation medians, maximum-lifespan records and historical taxonomic inheritance retain their limits; the route does not show that descendants share one reproductive mode or life history.

The ecological summaries describe separate historical sets: one has at least two usable field groups, and another has exactly one. They are not complete descendant catalogues, and food types, estimated weights or activity patterns in individual records are not traits of the whole subfamily. Semiquantitative conversion, historical taxonomy and possible congeneric borrowing in ABC fields limit interpretation.
