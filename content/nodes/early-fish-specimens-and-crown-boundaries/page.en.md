## Three early-fish fossils, three different boundaries {#s-6e4cef4f5a}
Three early vertebrate fossils preserve different evidence and sit on different branches. Their local ages do not define global origins for the living groups.

## An articulated jawed stem vertebrate {#s-72262a4115}
IVPP V300001 and twenty referred Xiushanosteus specimens preserve a roughly 436 Ma placoderm-grade body and a mosaic skull roof. Abundance in one Lagerstätte does not make the animal a direct ancestor or global first appearance.

## A lamprey-like oral apparatus {#s-8278b5a293}
Holotype AM5750 preserves a large oral disc, fourteen circumoral teeth and a branchial basket at roughly 360 Ma. These structures diagnose total-group lamprey anatomy, not parasitism, migration or an ammocoete lifecycle.

## A stem hagfish is not crown Myxini {#s-b53aba2fc6}
FMNH PF15373 and PF8472 preserve a roughly 310–307 Ma cyclostome–hagfish mosaic, but different preservation and equivocal structures require a stem or putative total-group reading. The separate Myxini navigation node therefore does not inherit this age.
