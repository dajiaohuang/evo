## Four substrate categories in a regional record {#s-463928977c}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Pelekium minutulum (Hedw.) Touw as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its entries are source categories, not named collection sites.

One row is labelled “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A second row is “Epixila,” a wood-associated substrate category.

A third is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The fourth is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The rows do not give localities, survey dates, a complete range or sampling coverage.

These habitat fields supply categories but no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.

The four source labels are the only biological content in this regional record; they do not make a complete account of the species.
