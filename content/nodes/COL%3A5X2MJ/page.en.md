## A voucher-based restriction of the historical range {#s-2960967b23}
The monograph recognized Wuyi and Yangjifeng mountains near the Fujian–Jiangxi border at 1200–1400 m, based on examined vouchers and molecular evidence. It noted that many older southern Chinese records lacked available vouchers and could not be reidentified, and recommended excluding those records from the accepted range. This is an attributed evidence-based restriction, not proof that every excluded locality lacks the species or an independent reexamination of missing specimens.

## Thirteen males and a single measured female {#s-14f89424a3}
The diagnosis reported thirteen adult males with snout–vent lengths of 27.5–33.6 mm and one adult female at 32.3 mm. These are the examined diagnostic measurements; one female does not define female size variation, and the sample does not establish universal adult size limits.
