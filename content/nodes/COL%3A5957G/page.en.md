## Long, scented flowers pollinated by hawk moths {#s-8923dfd3eb}
Tritoniopsis nervosa grows 500–1,100 mm tall and has creamy-white to pale-yellow flowers. The slender tube is 30–40 mm long, with shorter tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 9–13 mm; the sweet scent and flower form are associated with sphinx-moth pollination in the source.
