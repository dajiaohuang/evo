## What leaf and flower traits are listed? {#s-3afc836acd}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes hairy leaves that blacken when dry. They are sessile, more than 5 mm wide, shallowly heart-shaped at the base and rolled under at the margin. Several main veins converge toward the tip; at least two lateral veins occur on each side, with no hair tufts in their axils. A small bract below the flower reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. Epicalyx segments, an extra sepal-like ring below the calyx, are 2–11 mm long, as long as the sepals, and spread outward. The corolla is listed as 1–3 cm or 3.5–7 cm across, with 12 stamens. The regional field does not give specimen-level frequencies.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-9890eb7562}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat, with no locality or collection date.
