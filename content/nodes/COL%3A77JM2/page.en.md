## Many ribs and a funnel-shaped flower tube {#s-3b02414952}
The Portuguese description records erect branches that fork only at the base, with more than ten ribs and conspicuous central spines. Areoles are isolated along the stem. The flowering zone may be superficial or deeply set; flowers have a green to pinkish funnel-shaped tube. The fruit's tube scar is deeply inset, while the seed coat is smooth.
