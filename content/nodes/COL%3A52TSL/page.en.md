## Stomatanthes trigonus {#s-f26404dba0}
## A xylopodium and ribbed fruit

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records roots with a xylopodium, a woody underground structure. Leaves are alternate and have pinnate veins. The inflorescence is branched; the flowers have spatulate styles, and the dry fruit is recorded with five or seven ribs. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial. These regional fields do not give a locality or establish a complete range.
