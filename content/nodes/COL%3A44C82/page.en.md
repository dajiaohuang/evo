## Fringed style crests on small blue flowers {#s-cd93596aac}
Moraea lugubris is 6–16 cm tall and has small violet to pale-blue flowers. Its feathery or fringed style crests are distinctive in the genus; coarse fibres loosely surround its corm (a swollen underground storage stem).

## Mostly damp sandstone soils {#s-ec4d754871}
The flora records mostly damp sandstone soils.
