## Funcusvermis in a Norian bonebed {#s-bb34b2a4e4}
At least 76 individuals are represented by isolated, three-dimensionally preserved jaws, vertebrae and femora from a U–Pb-bracketed Norian bonebed in Arizona. The fossils support a caecilian total-group occurrence outside crown Gymnophiona; they do not form one articulated animal, and this locality is not a global first-appearance date.
