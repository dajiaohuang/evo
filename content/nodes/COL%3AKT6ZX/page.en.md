## Monodelphis (Monodelphis) arlindoi: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Monodelphis (Monodelphis) arlindoi* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Pavan, Rossi & Schneider, 2012.
