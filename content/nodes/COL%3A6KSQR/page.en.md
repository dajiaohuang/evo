## A rock-associated category in a regional entry {#s-3891d892b1}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Grimmia longirostris Hook. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

It is not tied to a collection locality or survey date and does not establish a complete range or sampling coverage.

This habitat category is the only biological information included from the regional record; it is not a full species account.
