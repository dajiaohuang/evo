## Diagnosis and mixed-concept size data {#s-318b6668a3}
The diagnosis describes granular-looking shagreen dorsal skin, a low mid-dorsal fold, smooth ventral skin, a distinct tympanum and narrow unexpanded digit tips without circumferential grooves or toe webbing. MUTPL342 lacked discoidal and thoracic folds in life and preservative, unlike nearby candidate populations. Published female34.6–44.2mm (n31) and male24.0–35.2mm (n27) SVL ranges pool several localities and may include cryptic species; they are not certified size limits of the restricted concept.
