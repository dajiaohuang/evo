## Distribution records {#s-49c5001e2b}
The study overlaid IUCN range maps on ten major Brazilian Amazon interfluves and listed Cebuella pygmaea (Spix, 1823) in 2 areas: Jaú, Napo. Here, overlap means a region-level map result; it does not confirm animals at every site in those areas and is not a population count or complete range assessment.

## Evidence limits {#s-1ce1aba605}
The abstract and methods describe 80 species, while main-text Table 2 occupancy classes sum to 81 and report the 46 single-interfluve species as 57%, unlike the 58% stated in the text. Names and counts here come from Supplementary Table S1; they are range-map overlaps, not point-level field confirmations, population counts or complete range assessments.
