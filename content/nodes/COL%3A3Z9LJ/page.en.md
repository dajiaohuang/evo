## A proposed reproductive role remains untested {#s-7ae0139ff4}
The authors suggest that protein-containing specialized hand glands could mediate chemical courtship signals during contact with a female's head. They did not characterize the secretions, identify a pheromone, test female responses or establish whether delivery is olfactory or transdermal. Hand location and stain reactions therefore cannot certify that Melanophryniscus cambaraensis uses a particular chemical mating mechanism, causes ovulation or oviposition, or secretes an adhesive. A lack of these gland types likewise does not prove a species lacks every form of chemical communication.
