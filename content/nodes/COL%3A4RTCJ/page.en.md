## Restio clandestinus {#s-426cda0f99}
## Rough stems on marshy coastal slopes

The regional description gives Restio clandestinus a tangled or tufted habit up to 40 cm, with densely tuberculate stems and leaf sheaths ending in long, translucent tips. The flower-covering perianth is hairless, and the spikelet segments are elongated. Marshy coastal slopes at 300–900 m are the recorded habitat.
