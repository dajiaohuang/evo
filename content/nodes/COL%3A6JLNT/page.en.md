## Frailea mammifera {#s-c1b10f8fcd}
## Interlacing spines surround purple-based tubercles

The source records fibrous roots and a globose stem with indistinct ribs. Conspicuous tubercles have purplish bases; the spines interlace, light-colored hairs occur on the areoles, and the radial spines are yellow. The flowers are pale yellow with pointed perianth segments. The habitat label is rocky, without a collection locality.
