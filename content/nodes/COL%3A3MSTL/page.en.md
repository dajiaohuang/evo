## Tuberculate stems and papillate, maroon-marked flowers {#s-118f315ff2}
The regional account describes a small clumping succulent, with clumps reported up to about 0.5 m across. Decumbent, grey-green stems are 20–120 mm long and 10–20 mm thick excluding the teeth, sometimes mottled purple. Spreading, laterally flattened tubercles are 3–8 mm long, join into four or five fairly sharp stem angles, and taper abruptly to small acute teeth. Stem angles are described as usually sharp and clearly defined; stems may be loosely grouped depending on shade.

Inflorescences bear 1–3 flowers that open in succession from a short peduncle; attenuate bracts reach 6 mm. Pedicels are 8–10 mm, occasionally 18 mm, and about 2 mm thick, spreading so the flower faces horizontally. Narrowly ovate-acuminate sepals are 8–10 mm long and about 2 mm wide at the base. The tubular-campanulate corolla is 20–28 mm long and 20–40 mm across. Its smooth outer surface is cream to pink, with 3–5 raised longitudinal veins on each lobe. Inside, a cream background bears fine red-brown or maroon spots and concentric markings in the lower tube. Papillae are absent below the tube thickening, reach up to 1.5 mm, are densest at the mouth and decrease onto the lobes; each cream papilla is maroon-spotted and ends in a bristle up to 0.75 mm. The tube reaches 20 mm, widens gradually toward the mouth and is not pentagonal. Lobes are 8–10 mm long and 8–10 mm broad at the base, erect to spreading, deltoid and acuminate.

The corona is 5–6 mm high and about 8 mm broad, without a basal stipe. Its outer lobes spread at the tube base, partly fuse to it, and divide into five broad, roughly truncate or notched dark-maroon lobes. Inner lobes are 3–4 mm long, cream with pale-maroon spots and darker tips; they lie against the anther backs near the base, then stand together before diverging above. They are flattened front to back, with a swollen dorsal base and a distinctly swollen, bristly tip. The habitat account says plants often grow in Botswana on sandy flats beneath bushes or trees, while farther south they are mostly found on stony flats. These regional descriptions do not establish population-level variation or a complete range.
