## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Nok hualon (I. A. Woxvold, J. W. Duckworth & R. J. Timmins, 2009) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
