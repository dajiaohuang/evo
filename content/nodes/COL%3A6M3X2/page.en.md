## Heteropsis spruceana {#s-63fd0990be}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic, lanceolate, or ovate leaves with sinuate margins, and a yellow spathe (bract) around a green to greenish-cream spadix (flower-bearing axis). Berries are listed as cream or orange. The habitat label is hemiepiphytic, a growth mode recorded as partly epiphytic; the source gives no complete range or population account.
