## A few-flowered spring spike {#s-f5e0cf0c42}
Hesperantha modesta is 0.15–0.25 m tall and usually has three leaves. Its lax spike bears two to four bright mauve-pink flowers; flowering is reported from August to October.

## Marshes, vleis and streambanks {#s-d5a04060f3}
The regional record lists marshy grassland, vlei (a seasonally wet depression or wetland) edges and streambanks, within the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt.
