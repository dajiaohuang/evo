## *Acritopappus subtomentosus*: hairy leaves and flat receptacles {#s-3bf002a076}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records a hairy, non-sticky stem and stalked, flat, egg-shaped leaves with long-tapered tips and serrated margins. Both leaf surfaces are hairy. Flower heads form corymb-like clusters, with bell-shaped bract cups and flat receptacles beneath the florets. The flower parts are glandular, and the fruit-tip has a crown-shaped pappus. The substrate field is the broad category “terrestrial.”
