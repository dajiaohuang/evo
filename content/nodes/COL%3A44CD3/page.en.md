## Broad leaves and large blue-violet flowers {#s-140749d6d8}
Moraea venenata is 200–300 mm tall, with blue-violet flowers and several leaves up to 30 mm wide. The source distinguishes it from M. polystachya by its more robust form, wider leaves and translucent margins on the outer spathe (a bract that surrounds or encloses a flower cluster).

## Dry stony ground, sometimes limestone {#s-b77e3f4745}
The regional account records dry stony ground, sometimes over limestone.
