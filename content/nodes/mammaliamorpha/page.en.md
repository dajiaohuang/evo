## A matrix definition of Mammaliamorpha {#mammaliamorph-topology}
In the cited character analysis, Mammaliamorpha names a sampled branch that includes tritylodontids and mammaliaforms. Alternative definitions and character matrices move some basal groups, so the label describes this study’s concept rather than a single settled ancestor lineage.

## A definition-dependent sample envelope {#mammaliamorph-range}
The displayed 225–0 Ma interval follows the review’s sampled envelope and living continuation. It is not an observed origin date, an exact clade range, or a direct-ancestor sequence. The boundary depends on the review’s taxon definitions.
