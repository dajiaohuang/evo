## A leaf without a stalk and a five-veined blade {#s-06ecd9d75b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes sessile leaves—attached without a petiole—with orbicular blades, rounded or blunt tips and tapered bases. Their margins are entire, and five basal acrodromous veins run upward from near the base. A terminal corymb-like thyrse bears leafy bracts. Flowers are five-parted, with a tubular hypanthium; the calyx has irregularly valvate lobes and no outer lobes. The oblanceolate petals have rounded tips. Stamens are similar in size and form; their slightly curved, awl-like anthers have rough pollen sacs and ventral pores near the tip. The connective does not extend below the pollen sacs, and the field lists a narrow triangular spur or an upward-curved, linear, awl-like spur. The ovary is attached to the hypanthium at its base, oblong, unribbed and truncate at the tip. The fruit-type label is velatídio and is described as slightly ribbed; seed shape is recorded as unknown. A separate field labels the habitat “terrícola,” or terrestrial, without a locality or range estimate. These are source-record descriptors, not a measure of variation or a complete diagnosis.

## A habitat category without a locality {#s-da23c83512}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record labels the habitat “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not specify whether the material is wild, cultivated, managed or fossil. The label is narrower than a full ecological account.
