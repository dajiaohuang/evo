## A specimen from a constrained Belgian horizon {#s-8ea0b1d7a7}
Holotype NHMM 2013 008 comes from the Maastricht Formation at the CBR-Romontbos quarry in Belgium. Field et al. (2020) constrain its source horizon to 66.8–66.7 Ma. This is a locality/horizon constraint, not the species’ complete duration or global first and last appearances. The collection number connects anatomy and phylogenetic interpretations to the same material; the locality does not identify the geographic origin of crown birds.

## What the fossil preserves {#s-1211ecc1f1}
The specimen preserves a nearly complete three-dimensional skull with associated postcranial elements, studied through CT imaging and digital segmentation. In the authors’ comparison, its cranium combines galliform-like and anseriform-like features. These observations belong to the preserved material and comparison; the combination does not make it a modern chicken or duck or reveal unpreserved soft tissues and behaviour.

## Two analyses, two nearby placements {#s-4219554863}
The study’s parsimony analysis recovered it as sister to crown Galloanserae. Its tip-dated Bayesian analysis instead placed it as the stemward-most member of Pangalliformes. Both concern its relationships near galloanserans, but they do not agree on the exact branch. This page reports the method-dependent results together; recorded database placement and atlas navigation do not independently resolve these competing results.
