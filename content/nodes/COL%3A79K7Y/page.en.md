## Feeding research {#s-636c7aa4bb}
Teixeira and colleagues combined feeding observations and fecal sampling for seven months in Brasília National Park. Their paper reports 33 plant species from 21 families, with fruits accounting for 55% of observed feeding time. This describes a local diet in one protected Cerrado site; the study period does not represent every season or the full species range.

## Ecological evidence {#s-6c6d7b72eb}
The authors also compared germination trials using seeds collected from trees with seeds recovered from capuchin feces, to study how gut passage may affect seed dispersal. The study design shows how germination differences were tested; this page does not extrapolate dispersal distance or plant-regeneration effects to other landscapes.

## Evidence limits {#s-303b218663}
Source-linked local feeding and seed-dispersal study;. Sampling lasted seven months at one protected Cerrado site and does not establish a complete annual or range-wide diet.
