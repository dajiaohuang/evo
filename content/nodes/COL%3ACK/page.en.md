## A topology from living chimaeras {#s-bf62f52eae}
A mitochondrial-genome study sampled eight living holocephalans across all three extant families. Its clock model estimates the lineage leading to modern holocephalans at 421 Ma, with a 95% credible interval of 410–447 Ma. This is a model-to-present window, not a fossil first appearance or the full total-group range.

The sampled mitogenomes represent all three living chimaeriform families and support a topology among the included taxa. Because the study samples living species, those relationships do not directly date the fossil origin of Holocephali.

## A clock estimate with uncertainty {#s-ea51ff8608}
The molecular clock places the lineage leading to modern holocephalans at an estimated mean of 421 Ma. Its 410–447 Ma credible interval reflects calibration and model uncertainty. The display carries that estimate forward to living members; it does not supply fossil endpoints for the total group.
