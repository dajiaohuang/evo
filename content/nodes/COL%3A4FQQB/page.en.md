## Measured beak dimensions {#s-0e1246a2be}
The mean beak length is 59 mm, measured from the front edge of the nostril to the tip. Mean width is 13.9 mm and depth is 19.1 mm, both measured at the nostril edge. These are separate dimensions taken at defined landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-4a77423259}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 314.5 mm; mean tarsus length is 63.8 mm. A separate species-average source gives mass as 2801.78 g and is labelled Dunning. The 4 measured birds should not be read as 4 weighed birds.

EltonTraits assigns the nonzero food weight to fish (100%). Its source code A means the compilers rated the source and diet information highly. ## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-08cb435049}
The species-level foraging record assigns 100% of its weight to feeding below the water surface. This estimates the relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or time spent there over a day. The archive's categories describe different positions; they are not elevation bands and cannot be used to infer flight height or habitat area.
