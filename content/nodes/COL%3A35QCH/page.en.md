## What distinguishes this slender annual herb? {#s-e56d8ed47d}
The source describes a delicate annual herb 5–12 inches tall, with a four-angled stem and opposite, thin leaves. A few flowers sit in a loose terminal raceme. Each flower has four stamens, a two-lipped corolla with orange marks on its raised palate, and a short conical spur projecting from the flower base. The capsule is pale and broadly cut at its tip; its seeds have broad, ribbed wings.

## Where was it found? {#s-4cd188b898}
The source records Diclis stellarioides among shrubs in a deep valley. It gives no named locality in this habitat statement.
