## Five field studies, five local questions {#s-dadaacd1ee}
The pinned COL26.8 roster connects six Callithrix species IDs, while these field studies ask different local questions. Soto da Costa et al. sampled hair from 107 individuals in 30 groups across 14 municipalities in southern Bahia in 2023–2024; mixing models used dietary-resource baselines from six sites. Rylands observed two groups for three months each in a coastal Atlantic Forest remnant in 1980, using the spelling C. kuhli. Pinto et al. estimated five species, including C. flaviceps, along repeated transects in an eastern Brazilian montane rainforest reserve. Passamani and Rylands followed one C. geoffroyi group in a southeastern Atlantic Forest fragment from February 1993 to January 1994. de Miranda and de Faria observed three C. penicillata groups weekly from March to December 1996 in one cerradão and two dense-cerrado patches in Brazil’s Federal District; group sizes were 4–11. These distinct designs support local readings, not a standardized genus-wide profile.

## Keep the study settings separate {#s-f853f70788}
The isotope samples, reserve transects, and observations of individual groups differ in population, place, period, and method. Diet proportions and seasonal patterns belong to the group and year studied; density estimates belong to the surveyed reserve; isotope models use the six sites with resource baselines. The historical spelling C. kuhli is retained as used by Rylands (1989), without asserting that it matches a current species concept. The six COL26.8 IDs describe the catalogue route, not complete ecological coverage or a genus-wide range review.

## Two historical life-history records {#s-3c7eea76e2}
The separate 2003 compilation route links Callithrix flaviceps and Callithrix jacchus under the pinned COL26.8 genus. One account contains each of the listed gestation, female age at first reproduction, and maximum-lifespan fields; litter size appears in both. These are historical name-matched entries, not a complete species roster or a present-day genus synthesis.

## Read reproductive fields by definition {#s-cd7a263cf9}
Gestation is pregnancy duration. Female age at first reproduction is not necessarily age at sexual maturity or age at first birth, and maximum lifespan is the longest recorded value rather than an average. The fields can draw on different source conditions. An empty field is not evidence that a species lacks the trait, and the historical name matching does not resolve changes in species concepts.

## Limits of the historical life-history route {#s-b09f56cd9f}
The compilation links species entries by family and matching names, with a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 crosswalk; equivalence to current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources equally rather than weighting by sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints when other data are unavailable, and underlying studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted here, missing fields are not filled from relatives, and original source numbers remain on the species pages. Wild, captive, and locality conditions were not consistently established
