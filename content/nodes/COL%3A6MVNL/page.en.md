## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-1656e6a5dc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical, hairless branches with stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent spatulate stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) remain attached. The leaf stalk (petiole) is cylindrical, and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) is grooved. Each leaf has two or three leaflet pairs. A stalkless, saucer-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. Flowers are arranged in umbels (clusters radiating from a common point); the calyx (outer floral cup) is tubular, and flowers are nearly stalkless or stalked. The leathery pod walls surround a cylindrical, hairless fruit. This single regional field preserves alternatives and does not establish trait variation or frequency.

## Habitat category {#s-36eb41bfc1}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
