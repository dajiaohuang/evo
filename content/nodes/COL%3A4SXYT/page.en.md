## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0e514d41fa}
COL26.8 records Riama labionis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gymnophthalmidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-39c2d5af4c}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 60 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-ecdc6851bf}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
