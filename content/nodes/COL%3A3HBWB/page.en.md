## Grazielia dimorpholepis {#s-fa88d9cd07}
## Ovate leaves and a turbinate cypsela

The Brazilian Flora record describes stems with glandular or appressed bristly hairs. Ovate leaves have rounded bases and acute tips. The dry one-seeded fruit (cypsela) is top-shaped, and its basal carpopodium—the attachment area between fruit and stalk—is recorded absent. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
