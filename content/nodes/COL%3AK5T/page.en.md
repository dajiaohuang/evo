For an initial comparison, use Blythipicus pyrrhotis, Blythipicus rubiginosus, Campephilus gayaquilensis, Campephilus guatemalensis or Campephilus imperialis. Each AVONET v7 profile keeps its historical name and sample limits; this route does not claim complete subfamily coverage.

## Differences between records {#s-2ad8412148}
The compiled mass values at the two ends of these records are 34.94 g for Melanerpes cactorum and 700 g for Campephilus imperialis. They are species-level summaries, not individual minimum and maximum weights. Represented habitat labels are Forest, Woodland, Shrubland, Rock and Grassland; trophic-niche labels include Invertivore, Omnivore, Frugivore and Granivore. The range of labels helps compare records but does not define traits shared by every picine.

## Evidence limits {#s-3cc520de1d}
The route brings together separate selected sets: 94 measured profiles, 43 life-history accounts, 120 EltonTraits entries with diet and foraging fields, 21 entries with one usable ecological group, and five ecology-only accounts. None is a complete accepted-descendant catalogue. Mass endpoints compare compiled species values rather than individual extremes; life-history summaries retain their median, maximum-record and historical taxonomic limits. EltonTraits weights are semiquantitative, and some ABC fields may use a congener. Read each page's provenance and scope before comparing it with another record; this route is not a set of complete species monographs or a current status assessment.

## What is readable and what remains uninterpreted {#s-9663c6ecd6}
Each of the five ecology-only pages has a habitat, trophic-niche, primary-lifestyle and migration category. Read those fields by their original definitions: they are coded summaries, distinct from historical maps and individual observations. A repeated label does not establish a behavior for every descendant. Beak, wing, tarsus, tail and mass fields are retained but not interpreted here, so these pages do not support a group body-size comparison.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-5a189f1c33}
These five limited accounts describe selected historical ecology and geography records, not all accepted Picinae descendants. COL26.8 navigation, same-name/same-family association and the two one-to-one name crosswalks do not resolve later concept changes. Categorical fields are not independent behavior observations, and historical maps are not current occupancy or conservation assessments. Original morphology-inference and mass-source labels remain attached to their records; they are not presented here as direct measurements of the species.
