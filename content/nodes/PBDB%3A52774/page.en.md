## Recorded parent of Neoceratopsia {#s-0fa71a568b}
Neoceratopsia is listed at rank infraorder in the PBDB classification route, with Ceratopsia as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
