## ## One field trial reports a yield comparison {#s-a5be713599}
A field experiment in Goiás, Brazil, during the 2019/2020 summer crop reported grain productivity of 3,440.63 ± 30.80 kg/ha for the control and 4,007.29 ± 99.71 kg/ha for the SAF9 rhizobacteria treatment (root-associated bacteria). These are the reported means and variation from Field 2, that season and those treatments. The result does not establish yield effects across cultivars, soils, regions or seasons.
