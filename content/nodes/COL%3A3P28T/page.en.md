## Leaves and flowering season {#s-c9ed3ea659}
The regional account describes *Hypoxis zeyheri* as a perennial geophyte, similar to *H. villosa*. Its leaves are almost hairless: the inconspicuous hairs are approximately evenly spaced along the margins and the lower-surface midrib. They are white or pale brown and fall off as the leaves age. Flowering is recorded from October to April.

These traits and the flowering period are reported in a regional South African species account published in 2019; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-2eaf15e75e}
The same account lists open grass patches and the Sub-Escarpment Grassland, Eastern Fynbos-Renosterveld, and Albany Thicket.

This is a regional South African habitat account published in 2019; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
