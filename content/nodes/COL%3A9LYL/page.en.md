## overview {#s-a1f203ee51}
Adults have a conspicuous pale saddle across the middle of the body with a darker head and hind limbs; young have stripes and spots. The source uses Tapirus indicus, whereas this release uses Acrocodia indica.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-1c792cc5d1}
Source-linked introductory account;. Quantitative traits, complete ranges, divergence dates and current conservation assessments require separate evidence. Catalogue counts are not counts of living wild biological species.
