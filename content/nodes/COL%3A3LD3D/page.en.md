## What leaf, flower and style traits are recorded for Heteropterys pannosa? {#s-1f1196ed97}
The leafy branches and petioles are woolly-haired. Opposite, leathery lance-shaped leaves have rounded bases, pointed tips and golden-brown woolly hairs below; glands are absent. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts without glands and stalked pedicels. Sepals have curled tips and rusty hairs; the front sepal may alone lack glands. Yellow clawed petals are thin, without a keel, and have toothed margins. Anthers are hairless. The front styles are shorter than the rear ones, curve at the base and end in hooked tips. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b92727c2c0}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
