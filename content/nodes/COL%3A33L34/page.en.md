## Cyrtanthus guthrieae {#s-41dd3dc649}
Cyrtanthus guthrieae is described as a small bulbous geophyte with one or two linear leaves that are dry at flowering. Its large, bright-red flowers are usually solitary, upright and widely funnel-shaped, with stamens projecting beyond the throat. The account records lower sandstone slopes as habitat and notes a historical comparison with Vallota, now placed in Cyrtanthus.
