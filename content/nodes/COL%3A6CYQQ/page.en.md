## Disa rungweensis {#s-1135881f4a}
The account describes Disa rungweensis as a terrestrial herb 5–35 cm tall, with narrow leaves along the stem and small greenish-white, pink, lilac or purple flowers. Its short spur measures 2.5–3.5 mm. The record places it on steep rocky slopes among lava or pumice; it separately describes subsp. rhodesiaca as larger, with broader leaves and many lilac or purple flowers.
