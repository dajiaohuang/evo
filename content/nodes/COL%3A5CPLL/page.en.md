## *Xyris jataiana*: narrow leaves and a long, ribbed stalk {#s-740b06af1f}
The leaves spiral or nearly form two ranks, and their blades are narrow, angular or nearly cylindrical. The tip may be beak-like or asymmetrical. The stalk is at least 50 cm long and has several ridges; the spike ranges from egg-shaped to cylindrical or ellipsoid, with 10–25 bracts and flowers. The species is categorized as terrestrial.
