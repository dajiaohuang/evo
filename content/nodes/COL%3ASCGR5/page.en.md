## Three Cañar localities {#s-59ac893d31}
The description reported Guangras, Rivera and Llavircay in Ecuador’s Cañar Province, at 2500–2800 m in eastern-Andean evergreen high montane forest. These reported sites do not establish continuous occupancy between them, a complete current range or physiological elevation limits.

## Night litter and low-branch encounters {#s-8b3de0c18c}
All study specimens were encountered at night among small shrubs and litter; some were on small branches from ground level to 25 cm high. Other Pristimantis were recorded nearby. The encounters document sampled substrates and co-occurrence, without proving exclusive nocturnality, breeding in litter or direct interactions with the other frogs.
