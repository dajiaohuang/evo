## Notched leaflet tips and grouped stamens {#s-802c0ec3d2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile records 13–25 pairs of oval to obovate leaflets, sometimes up to 29. Their tips may be notched or shallowly blunt, and their bases rounded or heart-shaped. Cream-yellow flowers have ten stamens: nine are joined in one group and the remaining one is free. Fruit shape and surface-vein details are marked unknown. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
