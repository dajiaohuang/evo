## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5212fd5694}
COL26.8 records Liotyphlops caissara as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Anomalepididae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a017e97073}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
