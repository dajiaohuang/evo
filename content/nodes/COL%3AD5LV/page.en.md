## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5ec96a2bf2}
COL26.8 records Amphisbaena talisiae as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-14563f2f5d}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 130 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
