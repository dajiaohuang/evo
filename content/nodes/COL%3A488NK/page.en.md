## What one ecological field can tell us {#s-12f84adbf7}
The pinned COL26.8 record accepts Nystalus striolatus in Bucconidae. EltonTraits 1.0 has a historical record with the same name and family, and this page uses its foraging-stratum field group only. Matching names link the records for comparison but do not establish equivalent species concepts after later splits or synonym changes.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-602696664d}
The archive flags the foraging-stratum estimates as species-level: 50% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy, and 50% in or just above the canopy. These are estimated shares of foraging use across categories, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. They do not describe elevation, flight height or habitat area.

## Trace each field group to its source labels {#s-87c565570d}
The used foraging field group carries source label Ref_7, identified in the archive as *Handbook of the Birds of the World*, volume 7 (1992–2011). A matching Ref number in a bird and a mammal reference table need not mean the same source. The archive retains the source string with its file, row and field group; the underlying book has not been individually reread, so its label is provenance rather than a new observation.

## Evidence limits {#s-86dcaec7c3}
The EltonTraits account covers only the supported foraging-stratum field group, not a full species monograph or the remaining diet and activity fields. Its percentages are estimated category use from literature, not measurements of nest sites, habitat area or whole-day time budgets. The source label and species-level flag do not replace reading the underlying book. The diet group is withheld: the archive marks it D1 because of uncertainty/interpolation, unresolved source labels or unusable category values, so this page draws no diet conclusion.
