## Graphistylis cuneifolia {#s-391748b99c}
## Radiate heads in corymb-like clusters

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes oblanceolate leaves with serrated margins and the same colour on both surfaces. Leaves are sessile or have a petiole no longer than 2 mm; the underside is hairless.

Corymb-like clusters bear radiate flower heads surrounded by 11–13 involucral bracts. Each head has three to eight ray florets and 15–28 disc florets; disc corollas are 4–9 mm long. The style branches are truncate and have a central tuft of hairs. Cylindrical dry fruits are hairless, and their pappus (hair-like crown at the fruit tip) persists. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
