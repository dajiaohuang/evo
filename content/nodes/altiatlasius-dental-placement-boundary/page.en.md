## Altiatlasius: teeth and an uncertain place {#s-4aa9fdd72e}
What can a handful of teeth tell us about early primate relationships? Altiatlasius is known from about ten isolated teeth from Adrar Mgorn 1, including holotype THR 141 (whose molar position is uncertain, ?M1 or M2) and lower molar THR 135. The Thanetian sample is real; its placement near or outside crown Primates is not settled. The teeth therefore do not establish a secure crown-primate or anthropoid position, let alone a direct ancestor.
