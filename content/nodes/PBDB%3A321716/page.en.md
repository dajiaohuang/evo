## What the partial skeleton preserves {#s-92844659f8}
The holotype NCSM 21558 preserves parts of the skull, vertebral column, ribs and left humerus; a separate referred humerus is NCSM 21623. The reconstructed skull is slender and ornamented, with an estimated length greater than 50 cm. Its premaxillary teeth are long, serrated and slightly recurved. Because the holotype was still growing and is incomplete, these observations do not describe every Carnufex individual or adult body proportions.

## How the study inferred a large predator {#s-f76f9234e3}
The cited analysis estimated a body length of about 3 m using the reconstructed skull and an immature femur-length estimate of 354–441 mm. It placed Carnufex near the base of Crocodylomorpha in an unresolved polytomy in a matrix of 79 taxa and 413 characters. The same study interpreted it as a top-tier terrestrial predator in the Pekin Formation community. Length, branch position and ecological role are model-based conclusions from the sampled material, not direct evidence of adult size, behaviour or ancestry.
