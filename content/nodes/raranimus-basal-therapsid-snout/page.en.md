## Raranimus at the base of one sampled therapsid tree {#s-a06b3a042f}
The holotype IVPP V15424 is a slightly crushed snout about 100 mm long. In a published matrix of 71 morphological characters, four shortest trees placed Raranimus just outside the other sampled therapsids. That is a result of one taxon-and-character sample, not direct ancestry. The displayed 274.4–264.28 Ma envelope is a broad stage-based translation; the source reports no reliable radiometric date for the Dashankou sequence.
