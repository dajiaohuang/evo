## Shell, sites, and genetic evidence {#s-f78a688811}
COL26.8 lists *Radomaniola testavariabilis* Grego, Beran & Falniowski, 2025 as a species in Hydrobiidae; the exact COL identifier was joined to the archived MolluscaBase record by ID, not by a name-only match. That nomenclatural match does not establish full biological-concept equivalence. The 2025 treatment describes the shell and soft-part anatomy and gives a diagnostic account. Its type locality—the collection place of the holotype—is Vrelo Vrioštica spring, Vitina, Ljubuški, Bosnia and Herzegovina (43°14′15″N, 17°29′09″E; 93 m). The holotype NHMW-MO-113904 was collected 2018-04-02. The treatment lists six localities, its numbers 15–20: three in Croatia and three in Bosnia and Herzegovina, including springs, the Neretva River, and interstitial water. The study groups the sampled mitochondrial COI sequences (accessions PX113128–PX113141) in operational unit mOTU A; the authors caution that COI does not resolve deeper relationships, and this grouping is not a species-tree placement. The authors assess the species as Vulnerable (V), but that is not a verified current IUCN or national Red List listing.
