## Hippeastrum psittacinum: flowers and seeds {#s-303c4ce7c2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb and no rhizome. Leaves are sickle-shaped with acute tips, and the inflorescence bears 2–4 flowers. The perianth is funnel-shaped. Tepal tips are dark red or red-tessellated; the bases range from green to white, with reticulate margins recorded in red, violet or green. The hypanthium tube is of medium length, 2.5–6 cm. The sepals are broadly elliptic and much wider than the petals. The lower lateral sepals are described as slightly asymmetric with descending tips, or as smaller than the upper sepals. The petals are elliptic, with the lower ones much smaller or slightly smaller than the upper ones. The corona is absent or described as irregular, fringed callosities. The filaments are fascicled and shorter than the sepals and petals; the anthers are purple or dark purple, and the stigma is three-cleft.

The capsule is brown inside. Seeds are numerous, flattened and disc-shaped.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.
