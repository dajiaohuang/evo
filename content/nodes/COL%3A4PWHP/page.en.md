## Pterodon emarginatus {#s-86b17eb532}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Pterodon emarginatus* as an accepted species of *Pterodon* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 5–10 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), dark pink to violet calyces, and fruits 4–5 cm long. The fruit’s wing is larger than its seed-bearing center; the source labels the plant terrestrial.
