## Concealed black surfaces and measured adult sizes {#s-d5371abbc1}
The diagnosis includes no tympanic membrane but an evident oval annulus, broadly expanded truncate digit discs, a large conical heel tubercle and black axilla/groin or concealed limb surfaces edged in white, yellow or orange. Adult female SVL is28.9–45.7mm (35.3±9.06SD,n3), male25.6–31.2mm (n2). The female/male samples are small, and the source’s descriptive larger-female statement is not an independently performed significance test here. Variation in edging color does not define a separate lineage.

## Five reported DNA-associated localities {#s-ffc9aa8b01}
The authors report DNA-associated presence at Tapichalaca, Cajanuma, Abra de Zamora, Huacapamba and Washapamba, with recent encounters2480–2960m in cloud forest, upper montane forest and subpáramo. Specimens were encountered at night on vegetation30cm–2m above ground, sometimes near streams, including disturbed habitats. Local tolerance of disturbance is not proof that deforestation has no population cost or that stream proximity is an obligatory breeding requirement.

## Different support at group and nearest-relative levels {#s-29971f3c58}
Huicundomantis monophyly receives bootstrap98.7% and posterior1 in the paper, but the cryptomelas group has strong Bayesian and moderate maximum-likelihood support. With added cryptomelas sequences, its relationship relative to spinosus becomes weak in Bayesian and moderate in ML analysis. These hierarchical support statements are distinct; broad monophyly does not certify the nearest branching order.

## Unknown call and a dated Near Threatened interpretation {#s-6910041c90}
No calling males were heard and the advertisement call is unknown in the revision. Lack of vocal sac/slits does not prove inability to vocalize. The2020 authors retain a cited Near Threatened category, discussing five localities, fragmented populations and habitat-quality decline from grazing and deforestation. That interpretation is not a newly verified current assessment or a quantitative decline rate.
