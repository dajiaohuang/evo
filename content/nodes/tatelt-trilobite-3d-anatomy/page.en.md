## Three-dimensional trilobites reveal hidden mouth anatomy {#s-fd2042bd6a}
Micro-CT reconstructions of ash-entombed Tatelt Formation trilobites, including Protolenus and Gigoutella, resolve four pairs of appendages behind the antennae, a labrum, mouth structures, and digestive tissues. These rare three-dimensional fossils let researchers examine anatomy obscured in flattened specimens. Feeding mechanics and body-part homologies remain comparative interpretations; the sample does not establish behavior or anatomy for every trilobite.
