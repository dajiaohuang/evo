## Research overview {#s-e2dc0826e2}
The nearly complete holotype IVPP V300000 combines a scale-covered body and a chondrichthyan-like rear branchial region with large plates of thoracic armour. That unusual character mix makes its evolutionary placement an open question.

## What this evidence can establish {#s-d05e7edc19}
One holotype provides an unusually detailed body plan, but it cannot show the species’ population variation. Its exact position among early jawed vertebrates and its ecology remain uncertain.

## A character mix in one holotype {#s-2404aeb203}
The described specimen has minute scales across its body and large armour plates over its thorax. Its posterior branchial region—the rear of the gill region—has chondrichthyan-like features.

## What one holotype can and cannot show {#s-da4b7c6210}
The Huixingshao Formation provides depositional context, not a resolved habitat preference. Its preserved aquatic body form does not measure locomotion, and a small, nearly complete holotype cannot define population-wide body size, diet or ecological role.
