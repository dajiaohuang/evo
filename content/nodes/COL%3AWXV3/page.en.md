## Coleocephalocereus goebelianus {#s-71ed7eb77e}
## A rusty cephalium and white afternoon flowers

Brazilian Flora 2020 describes solitary, upright cylindrical stems. Basal areoles lack secondary growth; the spines are curved, with central spines erect or spreading. A rusty-colored cephalium is present. The flowers open in the afternoon and are white on the outside. The habitat field includes rocky and terrestrial labels but no locality.
