## Helichrysum evansii {#s-58b9ac009e}
## A woolly mat with yellow heads

The SANBI account describes a mat-forming perennial with many dwarf branches. Lateral flowering stems reach 100 mm and young parts are densely gray-white and silky-woolly. Crowded basal rosettes bear spatulate leaves up to 20–30 by 10 mm, rounded at the tip and broad at the base; both surfaces are densely felted with gray-white wool.

Many small, cylindrical heads form a terminal, nearly spherical cluster; wool mats their bases together. Four loose rows of glossy bright-yellow bracts spread at the top but do not radiate. Each head has 4–6 yellow disk florets. The dry fruits are smooth; numerous flattened pappus bristles have slightly feathered tips and bases that lightly cohere. The source habitat fields list mesic grassland, cliff faces, rock platforms and stony ground.
