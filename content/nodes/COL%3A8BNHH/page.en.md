## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e6454d7972}
COL26.8 records Platyceps saharicus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-40b99980d7}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1410 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-b1f36820c6}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
