## One Orthrozanclus fossil combines a shell, plates and spines {#s-97bc9d8c09}
An articulated Orthrozanclus reburrus body preserves an anterior shell, three zones of overlapping sclerites (small protective plates), and long marginal spines. This combination tests proposed relationships among halwaxiids, molluscs and other lophotrochozoans. The anatomy is observed in one fossil; group placement depends on character comparison and coding, and does not make Orthrozanclus a crown mollusc or direct ancestor.
