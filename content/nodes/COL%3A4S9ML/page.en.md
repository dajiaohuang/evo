## Body mass at age eight {#s-b23693f29f}
At Baimaxueshan National Nature Reserve in Yunnan, a study of wild black-and-white snub-nosed monkeys reported a body-mass dimorphism ratio of 1.85 among eight-year-olds. Males averaged 21.8±2.2 kg (n=35) and females 11.8±1.2 kg (n=116; p<0.001). The counts include repeated annual records of some monkeys, so they are not numbers of distinct animals. This comparison describes one monitored population and measurement design.
