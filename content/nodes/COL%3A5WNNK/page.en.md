## What a reference genome assembly measures {#s-09f16fcf9a}
A study published online in December 2025 and in a 2026 volume lists Bombina variegata assembly GCA_029574335.1. Its assembly summary gives a genome size of 9.37 Gbp and a contig N50 of 1,741.7 Kbp; N50 describes the length at which half of the assembled sequence lies in contigs that long or longer. These figures describe this particular assembly and its continuity, not DNA content per cell, a fixed genome size for every member of the species, or demonstrated ploidy. ## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-9f2aaed3a3}
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Bombina variegata was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-d773fb1a24}
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.
