## The source concept {#s-b3773bd924}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Arthroleptis loveridgei in row Anf0134 under Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. Its taxonomy follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the row has not been reconciled to a current species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-55947117bd}
The row flags terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. These broad categories do not specify where or when each is used. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; its deposited values are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Developmental strategy {#s-656cda5aea}
The record flags direct development; it does not flag larval development or viviparity. No breeding-site or care details accompany these coded fields.
