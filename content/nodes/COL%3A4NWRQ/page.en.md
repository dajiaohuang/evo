Table 1 compares this with an earlier reported elevation of 800–1390 (Brodie et al. 2002) and lists photographic catalogue number IBH-RF 616. ## Interpret the record within the survey design {#s-b655bb09d6}
Four observers surveyed pristine and managed cloud-forest sites on eleven five-day trips in 2018–2019, in wet and dry seasons. Three daily windows were 09:00–13:00, 15:00–19:00 and 21:00–01:00; the paper reports 2,640 effective field hours for the whole herpetofaunal inventory. Animals were photographed and released, and GPS locations had a reported 5 m error range. The checklist's Pseudoeurycea orchileucos row establishes presence in that sampling context. Total effort and the nineteen amphibian species are not species-specific detection probabilities, population estimates or trends. The accumulation curve had not reached an asymptote, so a missing record cannot establish local absence.
