## *Baccharis pluricapitulata*: linear leaves and head panicles {#s-f648fc8a05}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records linear, stalkless leaves with one main vein and entire margins. Flower heads are arranged in panicles, branched clusters. It describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered; those are alternative dataset entries, not a combined flower-head description.
