## Vietnamese type locality and Chinese border records {#s-c791da048c}
Male holotype VNMN 2017.002 came from Sa Pa, Lao Cai, Vietnam, at 1708 m, with male paratypes from nearby sites at 1400–1722 m. The monograph’s cited account also included Maandi, Jinping, Yunnan, and neighbouring northern Vietnam. These type elevations and the wider cited locality account do not establish a physiological range or continuous cross-border occupancy.

## Seven original males and red-orange thigh markings {#s-1b1dca8381}
The diagnosis based on Tapley and colleagues (2017) reported seven adult males at 26.7–30.5 mm snout–vent length. It described dark-spined tubercles on the dorsum, flanks and dorsal limbs, unwebbed toes with narrow lateral fringes, and red-orange colour on the posterior flank and anterior thigh near the groin. Breeding males had dense nuptial spines on the dorsal base of finger II and a subgular vocal sac. No female measurement was supplied in this diagnosis; the male finger-II character is not expanded to finger I or a breeding calendar.
