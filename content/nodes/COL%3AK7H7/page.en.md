## *Baccharis parvidentata*: axillary heads and toothed leaves {#s-99237b94ba}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records leaves without stalks, with one main vein and toothed margins; blade shapes listed are egg-shaped, inversely egg-shaped, elliptic, or oblong. Flower heads are solitary in the leaf axils. The field describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered, and lists rock-associated and terrestrial substrate categories without giving localities.
