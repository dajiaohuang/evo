## A species score for a specified conservation framework {#s-eba11243c2}
Table A4 reports a distribution-based score (DBS) of 4, threat-based score (TBS) of 2, sensitivity-based score (SBS) of 4, and species index (SI) of 10 for Cardioglossa cyaneospila. DBS weights the number of represented Rwanda ecozones and national endemism. TBS draws on national red-list proposals reported by earlier research, not a new global IUCN assessment. Table 1 groups Vulnerable and Data Deficient as VU/DD at score 2, so this score does not distinguish the two categories. SBS 4 is the category for undisturbed habitats alone. These are inputs to the Rwanda index, not measured physiological tolerances, demonstrated responses to every land use, or a current threat assessment.

## Species Index and site index have different meanings {#s-57ea0acacc}
The species index is DBS + TBS + SBS for one species. At a site, RABI sums the SI values of all recorded species without dividing by species richness. Thus this species' SI of 10 is not the RABI of a site where it was recorded, an extinction probability or an effect size. The categories use weights of 1/2/4/8 for DBS, 0/1/2/4/8 for TBS, and 0/1/2/4 for SBS. These steps express the authors' prioritization scheme; they do not show that risk or ecological sensitivity doubles in nature between categories. The study also reports positive correlations among the sub-indices, so their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
