## Lejeunea controversa: three habitat labels {#s-c8c00f1b34}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records three habitat categories for this species:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated),

“Rupícola” (rock-associated),

and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These labels come from three regional dataset rows; none provides a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
