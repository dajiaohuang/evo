## 这个名称对应哪些化石记录 {#s-c90aab8ce1}
Mesosaurus tenuidens 在固定 PBDB 羊膜动物剩余化石条目工作名录中为接受种，数据库父级为 Mesosaurus。本页导读 21 条可用种级出现记录，涉及 20 个采集记录和 12 个引文编号。出现、采集和引文数量是三个层次，不等同于标本或个体数量。

PBDB 出现记录补充:
Mesosaurus tenuidens 在 PBDB 快照中对应分类条目 txn:149291。这份存档导读包含 21 条选取的出现记录，关联 20 个不同的 PBDB 采集记录编号和 12 个不同的 PBDB 引文编号。出现记录与采集记录是不同单位；这两个数量都不等于标本数或个体数。此页聚焦一个具体问题：这些记录各自连到哪些地点、年代界、材料说明和引文？

PBDB 存档字段 scm 原文（未翻译或解释）：
Cycle 2 is at the top of Unit 5 in Sections 2, 3, 4, and interfingers with the sandstones of Unit 6 in section 5. underlies the Gai-as Formation (=depositional unit 7). Unit 5/6 is correlated with the Irati Shale Fm in the Parana Basin, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, and with the upper Prince Albert Fm and overlying Whitehill Fm in the Karoo Basin, Eastern Cape Province, South Africa (Horsthemke et al., 1990).
The description above along with absolute datings of ash beds in the Gai-as Fm. (272-263 Ma; see Warren et al., 2001, J. Vert. Paleont.), a bentonite bed in the Irati Fm. (278.5 Ma; see Santos et al., 2006, Gondwana Res. 9) and a tuff bed in the Whitehill Fm. of southern Namibia (280.5 Ma; Werner, 2006, PhD thesis, http://www.opus-bayern.de/uni-wuerzburg/volltexte/2007/2175/pdf/00-Complete_thesis-STD.pdf) implies an Artinskian age (cf. time scales in Gradstein et al. (2004) and Ogg et al. (2008)) for the beds in question (referred to as Huab Formation in Warren et al., 2001) (TL).
According to the facts given above the original maximum time interval entry >>Rotliegendes<< is replaced by >>Artinskian<<, the group name >>Karoo<< is replaced by >>"Ecca"<< (due to the fact that "Karoo" refers to a superordinate unit and the Huab Fm. is part of a series equivalent to the Ecca Group of the Main Karoo), and formation name >>Huab<< is added (TL).
