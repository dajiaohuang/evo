## Caribemys gives turtles a minimum, not a birth date {#s-991c255e42}
Holotype MNHNCu P-3209 of Caribemys oxfordiensis comes from Cuba’s Oxfordian Jagua Formation. The cited calibration study treated it as a pleurodire within crown Testudines and used the fossil to set a 155.6-million-year hard minimum: under that calibration scheme, the crown must be at least this old.

The same analysis estimated a much older crown age, with a 194.90–231.41 Ma posterior interval and a 212.33 Ma mean. Those are outputs of a relaxed-clock model under chosen topology, molecular data and priors—not dates measured from the fossil. The authors did not resolve whether the turtle crown originated in the Triassic or Jurassic.
