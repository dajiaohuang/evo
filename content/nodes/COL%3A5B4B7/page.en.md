## Glandular, woolly leaves and stems {#s-863a663dc3}
This morphology record describes upright, woolly stems with glands. The sessile leaves are subleathery, lance-shaped to oblong, pointed and glandular; both surfaces are woolly or bristly. The corollas are smooth but have glands and contain 8–22 florets. The bristly fruits also have glands, and the pappus has flattened outer bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
