## New sampled haplotypes and an incomplete western range {#s-d6949cb1d1}
The study reported newly detected H. iwami haplotypes at sampled sites in Oda and Gotsu, Shimane, and described a parapatric boundary with H. kunibiki. Its discussion explicitly said the western Shimane distribution of iwami was not resolved in detail and required further research. These sampled detections and the interpreted boundary do not provide complete occupancy, an experimentally demonstrated barrier to reproduction or a measured migration rate. Historical place assignments remain tied to the study’s evidence.
