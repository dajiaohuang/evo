## A large fossil series, one anatomical pattern {#s-a76fc38923}
More than 1,800 Waptia specimens let researchers compare a recurring body plan rather than rely on a single compressed fossil. The preserved combination includes a two-valved carapace, mandibles and repeated trunk appendages. A bivalved carapace has two shell-like halves; the mandibles are paired feeding appendages, while the serial limbs show how much of the trunk is represented in the sample.

These repeated structures support anatomical comparison and the study’s mandibulate interpretation. The number of specimens strengthens the record of this form, but it does not by itself settle every branch of the early pancrustacean tree or make Waptia a direct ancestor of living crustaceans.
