## Calypogeia miquelii: two habitat labels {#s-3f71dd5230}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this liverwort:

“Epixila” (wood-associated)

and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither specifies a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
