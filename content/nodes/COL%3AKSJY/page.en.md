## *Barrosoa organensis*: tapered leaf bases and pointed bracts {#s-84f6e485d5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records stems with soft or glandular hairs. Its lance-shaped leaves taper toward the base and have scalloped margins. The bracts around the flower heads end in sharp tips. The dataset gives only the broad substrate category “terrestrial,” without a locality or fuller habitat description.
