## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-071f6a27ed}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 1 offspring/eggs; age of dependence on food other than maternal milk 234 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

The archive’s nonzero food weights are invertebrates 20%; fruit 20%; seeds, nuts and spores included in this category 20%; other plant material (the source category also includes fungi) 20%; unspecified vertebrates 10%; nectar, pollen and plant exudates 10%. ## Evidence limits {#s-715b81576a}
This guide summarizes records from selected sites; it is not a complete species account, range survey, population estimate, or conservation assessment. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation.
The 2015 archive compiles historical species- and subspecies-level life-history records: non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians, while maximum_longevity_y is the largest compiled record. Historical taxonomic inheritance means a same-name match does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept; these records do not yield a uniform wild-only or captive-only estimate. EltonTraits 1.0 converts literature descriptions into diet weights and categorical activity/foraging fields; mammalian ABC fields may draw on congeners, so they are not direct species-level observations in every field. Neither archive establishes current distribution or conservation status.
