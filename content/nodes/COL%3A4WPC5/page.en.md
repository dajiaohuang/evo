## Senna organensis {#s-6f1375a700}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna organensis* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists leaves with three, four, or five leaflet pairs and extra-floral glands either between all pairs or between basal pairs. The flowers have seven fertile stamens; the erect, flat pods split open and carry seeds in one row.
