## *Restio andreaeanus*: small flower clusters on a tufted plant {#s-7b8000aa11}
The regional description calls this a tufted perennial reaching 60 cm, with branched stems and tightly rolled leaf sheaths. Its flowers are arranged in spikelets 8–12 mm long. In restios, male and female flowers occur on separate plants; this condition is called dioecy.

The recorded habitat is sandstone slopes.
