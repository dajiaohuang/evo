## Source classification and concept status {#s-ec02332abc}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2916 records *Litoria robinsonae* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging category (arboreal) {#s-dc58decd78}
AmphiBIO records an arboreal category for this row. It is a broad category, not a fine-scale habitat description. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-b4b43c9dfa}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 28.7 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
