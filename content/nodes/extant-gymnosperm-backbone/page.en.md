## A tree of living gymnosperms {#s-062416995a}
A nuclear phylogenomic study sampled all 13 living gymnosperm families and up to 1,308 loci. It recovered cycads plus Ginkgo as sister to the other living gymnosperms, and Gnetales as sister to Pinaceae; gene-tree conflict and subset analyses keep this a hypothesis about living lineages.

## The sampled living-family backbone {#s-8c02190aa6}
Phylogenomics compares many genes to infer relationships. In this study, nuclear loci sampled all 13 living gymnosperm families, with analyses using up to 1,308 loci. The recovered topology places cycads and Ginkgo together as sister to the remaining living gymnosperms, and Gnetales as sister to Pinaceae.

## A hypothesis about living plants {#s-ecda40ec2d}
The study also reports conflict among gene trees and alternative placements in subsets of the data. The Gnepine arrangement is therefore a published topology supported by a large sample, not permanent consensus. Its tree concerns sampled living lineages; it does not supply fossil ranges or relationships among extinct seed plants.
