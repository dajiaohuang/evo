## What the relative diet weights represent {#s-5b202abc76}
EltonTraits assigns 50% to other plant material, 30% to seeds, nuts, and spores, and 20% to invertebrates; the broad plant category also includes fungi. The percentages standardize verbal diet descriptions rather than measure food intake. ABC pools high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and some estimates may borrow from an ecologically similar congener. They are not necessarily direct observations of P. patrius. Zero does not prove an item is never eaten; labels such as Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broader than their everyday wording.
