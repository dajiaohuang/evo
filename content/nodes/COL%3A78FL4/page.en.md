## An EltonTraits record with an older taxonomy {#s-c769deff5f}
Pseudomys patrius is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. The page follows three ecological field groups in the same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record. Its classification follows earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals; a historical name match cannot settle modern splits or synonymy.

## What the relative diet weights represent {#s-5b202abc76}
EltonTraits assigns 50% to other plant material, 30% to seeds, nuts, and spores, and 20% to invertebrates; the broad plant category also includes fungi. The percentages standardize verbal diet descriptions rather than measure food intake. ABC pools high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and some estimates may borrow from an ecologically similar congener. They are not necessarily direct observations of P. patrius. Zero does not prove an item is never eaten; labels such as Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broader than their everyday wording.

## The ground category includes some aquatic foraging {#s-5401b769fe}
The mammalian stratum is G, the ground category, whose source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Metadata attributes these mammalian assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a categorical placement, not a time share or a claim of exclusive ground use.

## Night is one coded activity period {#s-e91421bd41}
The archive records feeding activity at night with value 1. Several periods can be marked 1 together, so the values do not add up to a daily time budget or describe every day, season, and site. Activity confidence is ABC and may draw on an ecologically similar congener; genus- or family-level typical values are not used to fill gaps.

## Field groups retain their source labels {#s-a33702ca19}
The included groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum, and activity can come from different materials, and an identical Ref number in bird and mammal tables does not identify the same source. Original strings remain traceable by file, row, field group, and reference table. Underlying books and papers have not been individually read; the labels do not count as independent observations.

## Limits of the ecological evidence {#s-f67cbd2cf3}
This page guides the reader through historical same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 records; it is not a full species monograph, and those historical concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity entries may rely on an ecologically similar congener, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus/family typical values, avian strata without species-level support, and split records requiring individual review do not establish a definite result. Underlying works have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation require other evidence.
