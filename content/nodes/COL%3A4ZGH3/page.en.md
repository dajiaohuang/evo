## Samples and measurement scope {#s-6c499aee46}
COL26.8 accepts Stactolaema whytii in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 uses its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records five measured birds, four with a complete morphological set. A trait mean may use fewer individuals than that total.

## Beak dimensions at standard landmarks {#s-385ee8b693}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 12.5 mm; width and depth at that edge average 8.8 and 9.6 mm. They describe distinct dimensions at standardized points, not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-2e9dcbe1fb}
The unflattened wing, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, averages 91.4 mm; the tarsus averages 20.4 mm. A separate compilation reports mean mass of 55.6 g (source category Dunning). It does not establish that the morphology specimens were weighed.

## Habitat and broad feeding category {#s-64b2df3c68}
AVONET assigns the habitat class Woodland, trophic niche Frugivore and lifestyle Insessorial (primarily perched above ground). These broad labels describe dominant tendencies, not exclusive habitat use or diet.

## Migration and historical map extent {#s-009e56f56a}
The migration field labels the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span 20.07° to 7.4° south. They do not show a full non-breeding itinerary or current occupancy; geographic and morphological data were compiled separately.

## Limits of the historical profile {#s-017c602cf9}
The morphology values are species means from historical AVONET samples, not diagnostic limits or individual ranges. Mass has a separate source and must not inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. The name crosswalk associates this record with the current catalogue entry but does not prove unchanged species concepts after taxonomic splits or lumps. Reproduction, present distribution, populations and conservation require other sources.
