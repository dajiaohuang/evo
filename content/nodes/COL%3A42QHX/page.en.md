## Narrow leaves and pale-mauve to blue flowers {#s-d77286a6ca}
Micranthus filifolius is described as 180–300 mm tall, with four to six narrow, solid leaves and pale-mauve to blue flowers. The account reports flowering from mid-November to late February.

## Clay or loam, often after fire {#s-9c0df04540}
The archived habitat note records mostly clay or loam and says plants can be especially abundant after fire. The regional flora gives a range from Steenbras and Cape Hangklip east to Elim and the lower Caledon Swartberg; it is not a current global range inventory.
