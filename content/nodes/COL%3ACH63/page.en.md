## Amaranthus retroflexus {#s-dddf388147}
## Hairy young branches and five-part female flowers

Brazilian Flora 2020 records hairs on young branches and no spines at the petiole bases. Petioles are about half as long as the leaf blades. The blades are recorded as ovate to rhombic-ovate; their tips may be acute, slightly notched or blunt, and bear a small terminal point (mucro).

In the pistillate flowers, bracts are longer than the five perianth segments (tepals) and taper to a point. The tepal tips are recorded as notched or blunt, also with a small mucro. The stigma is three-lobed, and the style is erect or slightly spreading.

The small dry fruit (utricle) is ellipsoid or obovoid, with a smooth to slightly wrinkled pericarp (fruit wall) that opens regularly. The source reports the fruit wall as either equal in length to the tepals or subtended by them. The dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial.
