## Long-stalked leaves in a marshland habitat {#s-f9980d4aed}
This account describes a stemless perennial herb with stiff, entire, lance-shaped leaves that continue down along their long stalks. The leaves are smooth, with barely visible veins. Yellow or white ray florets, rarely tinged mauve, surround a disk that may be purplish, red, mauve or yellow. The species is recorded from marshes or vleis; the source suggests its smooth leaves may be an adaptation to these moist habitats.
