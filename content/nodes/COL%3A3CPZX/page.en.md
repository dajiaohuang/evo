## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Euphorbia oerstediana? {#s-3a5171ebe5}
The herb has round stems, sparsely branched and erect to about 9 inches; they are hairless except near the tips and not fully jointed. Alternate leaves may become opposite near stem tips. Their petioles are about 1 cm long, and the thin ovate-lanceolate blades measure 3.5–6 × 1–2 cm, with scattered short hairs, entire margins, and blunt to tiny-pointed tips. Small, bell-shaped cyathia form in terminal groups and are about 1 mm across. Two glands have green appendages with entire margins; the appendages may be upright or absent. The broadly ovoid capsule is about 3 mm wide and densely hairy, especially when young. Ovoid seeds are 2.5 mm long, papillose, and have a minute caruncle.
