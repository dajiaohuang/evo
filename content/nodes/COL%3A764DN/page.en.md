## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded for Passiflora cerradensis? {#s-20c4cc500e}
The stem is cylindrical and somewhat woody. Leaves are entire, hairless and have smooth, glandular margins; petiole glands are sessile. Stipules and bracts are linear to triangular. Flowers may be solitary or paired. Their sepals lack awns, and the floral cup is cylindrical. The corona has several rows; the operculum has a torn edge, and the limen is absent. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The berry is hairy, while seed shape and surface are recorded as unknown.
