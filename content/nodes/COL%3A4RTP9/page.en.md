## *Restio wallichii*: spikelets in stream-flood crevices {#s-730dfd0c45}
This perennial may form a tuft or spread by stolons and reaches 70 cm. Its stems are simple or sparsely branched and finely spotted. The leaf sheaths are setaceous—narrow and bristle-like—and the spikelets taper from awl-shaped to cylindrical.

The account records it in rock crevices along stream flood lines.
