## Indigofera suffruticosa {#s-51abbccb9e}

The Flora of Panama describes a perennial herb or shrub up to 3 m tall, with 9–17 leaflets and rust-red flowers. Its curved pods reach 15 mm and contain 3–7 seeds. The account lists it from South Carolina, Florida and Texas southward to Bolivia and Argentina, and also from Bermuda and the West Indies. It records the species as naturalized in tropical Africa, Asia and Australia.
