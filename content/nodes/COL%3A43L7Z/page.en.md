## Overview {#s-766ef49603}
COL26.8 accepts Miopithecus talapoin as species ID 43L7Z under genus ID 5TJ3. MDD v2.5 supplies “Southern Talapoin Monkey” as the English display name. The current field sources in this page concern hunting records and market observations in Angola; they provide little direct information on the species’ daily ecology or social behaviour.

## Hunting in northern Angola {#s-6a2919e0b0}
In 2019, researchers visited 27 localities across six municipalities in Uíge Province, accompanied 23 hunters along snare lines and interviewed hunters and other residents about captured mammals. Miopithecus talapoin was among the species documented in the hunting study. These interview and harvest records show a local human-use context; they are not a systematic wildlife census, and reported capture rates cannot be read as population abundance.

## Market research in Uíge {#s-92b36e3930}
A separate study visited Uíge’s central market on 38 non-consecutive days in January–March 2022. Across 16 recorded species, the researchers documented 1,524 animals; southern talapoins were among the taxa sold as fresh whole carcasses. Market availability reflects trade and sampling at that market, not the species’ abundance or trend in the province. The study also interviewed nine vendors and surveyed 204 consumers, but those wider social results are not species-level population evidence.
