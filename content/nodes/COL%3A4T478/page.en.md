## What leaf, flower and seed details are recorded? {#s-f82f20640e}
The alternate, hairless leaves are obovate, with tapering tips, nearly entire margins and symmetrical bases. Small domatia—structures on the leaf underside—are present. The inflorescence is a thyrse, a branched cluster with a main axis. Petals are finely hairy; the filaments are fused, and the ovary is hairy near its tip. Each carpel has one ovule. Fruit symmetry and size, as well as seed shape and surface, are not known in this record; it lists one seed per fruit valve.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-2c18c0da89}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
