## Chloroleucon tortum {#s-5418c64b44}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chloroleucon tortum* records more than five pinnae, 10–20 oblong to obovate leaflets, fleshy pods that twist or coil. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
