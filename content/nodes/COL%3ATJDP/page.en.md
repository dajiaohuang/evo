## Two habitat labels in one record {#s-f2e26cd82a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes four to 13 pairs of elliptic to ovate leaflets, the smaller blades attached along a leaf. Yellow flowers have ten stamens, and the mature pod sides twist; the seeds are flattened and obovoid. The same profile assigns two habitat labels: rupicolous, associated with rocky surfaces, and terrestrial. It does not reconcile the two labels.
