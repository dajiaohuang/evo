## Winged stems and pinkish-purple flowers {#s-550c20b653}
The account describes an herb up to 2 m tall with winged stems. Its glandular leaves are oblong to elliptic or obovate, with coarsely toothed edges. The flower stalks arch over, and the flowers are pinkish purple. The habitat record lists woodland and savanna, and also notes it as a weed in cultivated fields or disturbed places.
