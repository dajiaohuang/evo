## The source concept {#s-e7c8f161cb}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Pelophryne linanitensis* in row Anf0744 under Pelophryne, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-f709045642}
For Anf0744, the compilation tags terrestrial and arboreal as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-43b3ac368d}
The row carries a night-time activity tag. It is a broad period category, not a record of exact hours or a continuous behavioural series.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-4bbf778736}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 18.6 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
