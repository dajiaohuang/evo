## What is recorded about its broad sheaths and ribbed stalk? {#s-148b5367ec}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records two-ranked leaves with dark-brown to blackish sheaths that widen markedly at the base and can form ear-like lobes. The flower stalk has more than two ridges; the entry lists two length classes, 20–50 cm and 50–70 cm. Its ellipsoid spike has 10–25 flowers. These alternatives are retained as recorded, rather than combined into one measured range.

## What broad habitat label is recorded? {#s-bfe3482351}
The regional data row labels the species terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
