## A bark-associated source category {#s-a85cad0aeb}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Cryphaea filiformis (Hedw.) Brid. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat field is a source category, not a collection location.

The record labels the substrate “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

The field gives no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

This one habitat label is the only biological content in the regional record; it does not make a complete account of the species.
