## Renealmia aromatica {#s-6fe648095d}
## Green bracts and globose capsules

The Brazilian Flora record describes striated leaf sheaths and an erect flowering stalk. Its terminal inflorescence grows on a branch without leaves and has green, membranous bracts, each carrying two or more flowers. The calyx is turbinate, or top-shaped, and the mature capsule is globose. The habitat field labels the species terrestrial.
