## White, star-shaped flowers and a long spur {#s-0da1174d74}
The regional account describes R. muscicola growing on other plants or on rock. Its short, stout stems carry fleshy to leathery leaves, and the white, star-shaped flowers are described as sweetly scented. The flower spur (a narrow projection) is 50–90 mm long.

## Often recorded on sandstone {#s-583c3a6ea9}
The same regional source says it is often recorded as a lithophyte, or rock-growing plant, on sandstone. The wording does not provide a collection locality or show how broadly the observation applies.
