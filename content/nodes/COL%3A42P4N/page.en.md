## Three veins and a one-toothed anther {#s-d875723ad4}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes star-shaped hairs on young stems. Leaves may be elliptic, ovate or lance-shaped, with acute, gradually tapering or tail-like tips and narrowed or blunt bases. The margin is entire, toothed or without fringe-like hairs. Three main veins arise at the base; one alternative adds two faint veins just inside the edge. The lower surface is recorded as hairless. Flowers branch in paired units. The bell-shaped flower cup bears a persistent calyx with distinct lobes; each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the anther's pollen sacs is prolonged and has one back-facing tooth. White anthers have one small pore, and the ovary bears hairs. A separate field labels the habitat “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. These alternatives are not a quantified account of variation.

## A terrestrial source label {#s-0abe89f86f}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It does not identify where or when observations were made, establish range coverage, or specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The label should be read within that limited scope.
