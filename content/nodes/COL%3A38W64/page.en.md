## *Eitenia praxeloides*: violet florets and three-ribbed fruits {#s-64f2aaa5fb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records hairy stems and opposite, stalked leaves with toothed margins. The leaves are egg-shaped, hairy on both sides, and have veins that loop toward the edge. Flower heads form panicles within bell-shaped bract cups; the receptacle beneath the florets is convex and hairless. The flowers are violet. Its fruitlets have three ribs, bristles, and an asymmetric attachment base; a single row of pappus bristles is recorded.
