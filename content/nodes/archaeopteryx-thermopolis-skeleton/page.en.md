## The Thermopolis Archaeopteryx skeleton {#s-01207f19a9}
The nearly complete Thermopolis skeleton, later catalogued WDC CSG 100, preserves a hyperextendible second toe, a tetraradiate palatine and a hallux unlike a fully reversed perching toe. This combination adds anatomical detail to Archaeopteryx’s theropod affinity. Joint posture, perching ability and phylogenetic placement remain interpretations; one skeleton is neither a direct ancestor nor proof of a unique first bird.
