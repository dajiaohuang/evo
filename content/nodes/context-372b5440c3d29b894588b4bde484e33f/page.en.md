## Record identity and classification {#s-e6984c62b7}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6034 records Hynobius kimurae under Hynobius, Hynobiidae, and Caudata, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-b53046a1a2}
This AmphiBIO row marks terrestrial; aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-7e0faf09ed}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body mass of 14.9 g and a maximum adult body length of 184 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian body-mass entries; this remains the AmphiBIO v1 value, not a revised species-level estimate.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-35f80f5bf5}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists: minimum age at sexual maturity: 5 years; maximum age at sexual maturity: 7 years; maximum lifespan: 27 years; maximum reproductive events per year: 1. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
