## Leaves and flowers {#s-b959679600}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records underground bulbs and flat leaf blades that are linear to sword-shaped and pleated. It notes that the inflorescence lacks a terminal bract and gives the spathe length as 3.0–5.5 cm. Flowers are lilac to purple, about 5 cm across, with unequal tepals.

This is a morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, or population variation.

## Recorded growth form {#s-55393c2eab}
The dataset labels the growth form “terrestrial” (Portuguese: *terrícola*).

This is a habitat or substrate category from the Brazilian Flora dataset row; it supplies no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
