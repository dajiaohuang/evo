## Foraging height in the historical record {#s-d38c97ae88}
COL26.8 places *Zosterops wallacei* in Zosteropidae. EltonTraits 1.0 contains an older same-name, same-family record for comparison. Its taxonomy predates COL26.8, so the name match does not settle later species splits or synonymy.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-a8be710a33}
The archive marks its estimate as species-level: half of the recorded foraging is assigned to trees or tall bushes from 2 m up to the canopy, and half to the canopy or just above it. These weights describe relative use of feeding positions in the source account. They do not describe nest sites, all habitat or a full day of activity; the categories are not elevation bands and cannot establish flight height or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-596216bf37}
The foraging-height estimate is the only EltonTraits field group interpreted here. The diet entry is withheld because its source has uncertainty, interpolation or unresolved links; this page does not infer a diet from that missing field. The archived percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than measure feeding time or food intake. The older same-name record does not prove equivalence with COL26.8, and its underlying source has not been reread here. Current distribution, local and seasonal variation, population size and conservation status need other evidence.
