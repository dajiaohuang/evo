## *Chamaecrista depauperata* {#s-9719bd0150}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — pointed tips; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis), axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem); flowers — yellow; fruits — hairless. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
