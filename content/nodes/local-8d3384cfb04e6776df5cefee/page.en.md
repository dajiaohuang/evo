## The source tadpole concept {#s-9b90da64cb}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Atelopus exiguus, in the genus Atelopus and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Atelopus exiguus. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-af52a0929d}
The compilation has nine records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 31, 34, 35 and 36. These are labels attached to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; nine records do not necessarily represent nine unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
