## Traits cited in the Deo Ca comparison {#s-a0a68c5c49}
The 2026 description of L. deocaensis cites roughly granular dorsal skin with larger tubercles, black flank spots, rudimentary toe webbing and an orange-gold iris with a greenish lower half and fine dark reticulation for Leptobrachella macrops. These traits come from the literature comparison, not newly measured animals of this species in the Deo Ca survey. The comparison contrasts it with the newly described population and is not a complete identification key.
