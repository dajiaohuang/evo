COL26.8 accepts Sphenodon punctatus (Gray, 1842), species 4YVHH, under genus Sphenodon (63SJ6), family Sphenodontidae (GHG) and suborder Sphenodontida (8FV6K). ## island habitat and feeding {#s-429bbe5c70}
The undated Department of Conservation account places naturally wild populations on islands off the northeastern North Island and in the Marlborough Sounds. It describes seabird-colony habitats and mainly invertebrate prey, alongside lizards and seabird eggs or chicks. Rats threaten eggs and young. These are account-level descriptions, without species-wide diet proportions or current abundance.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-96885a6b27}
the reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; sex determination is recorded as TSD. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Limits of these trait data {#s-7da9294a81}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
