## Maireana brevifolia {#s-dd1432e41b}
## Papery wings form around the fruiting perianth

A 2013 regional flora account describes Maireana brevifolia as a shrub up to 1 m tall. Its longitudinally streaked stems are sparsely woolly, while the short, fleshy, obovoid leaves are hairless. One flower occurs in each leaf axil, without bracteoles. As the perianth enters the fruiting stage, it expands into five papery, fan-shaped wings with radiating brown veins around thick, fleshy lobes.

The account lists roadsides and disturbed places as habitat, but gives no dated survey or precise locality.
