## Zanthoxylum djalma-batistae {#s-c0f235776f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records persistent, imparipinnate leaves and terminal panicles, alongside alternatives in leaflet hairiness and flower pedicel presence. The habitat field records terrestrial growth.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-37ce54a6ef}
The description records thin, non-exfoliating periderm on the branchlets. Leaves are persistent and imparipinnate; prickles on the petiole, rachis or midrib are absent, leaflet bases are stalked, and domatia are absent. Leaflet margins are crenate or crenulate, with the lower surface recorded as sparsely or densely pubescent. Terminal panicles are pyramidal rather than corymb-shaped, and their stalk and axis are neither corky nor reticulate. Flowers have three carpels, rarely two, and a disc-shaped stigma; the parts are pentamerous. The source lists both sessile and pedicellate flowers. Fruit segments (mericarps) are separate and have raised glands; the seed hilum is linear.

## Habitat field {#s-131cb84939}
The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial). Its scope text gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
