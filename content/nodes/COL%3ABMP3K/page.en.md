## Classification {#s-4170ceedda}
Silva and colleagues described the Kanamari white uacari in a 2022 systematic study of bald uakaris; the populations had previously been assigned to Cacajao calvus. The type locality is Lago Itucumã in Amazonas, Brazil, in seasonally flooded forest on the right bank of the lower Rio Tarauacá. This locality records the basis of the name, not a complete mapped range; the account focuses on systematics and type provenance rather than a species-wide ecological profile.
