## Foraging in or above the canopy {#s-2224692ff4}
The species-level EltonTraits entry assigns 100% of its foraging-stratum weight to the canopy or just above it. The weight estimates relative use of feeding positions; it does not describe nest sites, all habitat or a whole-day time budget. It is not an elevation range and gives no flight height or habitat area.
