## Leaf branches and compact flower heads {#s-089e65cacf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records pinnate leaves: each leaf has up to five or more than five side branches, with 10–20 or more oblong, obovate, or linear leaflets along them. A stalkless nectar gland sits directly on the leaf stalk. The flowers form compact, rounded clusters of differing forms. The linear pods are described as non-fleshy, and the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
