## Dotted leaves in a detailed Latin description {#s-59afcf6d72}
The Latin description records decumbent or rooting branches and ascending, glaucous green leaves 1–1.2 cm long, with conspicuous dots. The three-sided leaves have keeled, faintly toothed backs and short sheaths. Pink-purple petals occur in three rows, and the pink staminodes curve backward. The capsule lobes are strongly compressed and raised. Plains and hills on Karoo sediments are listed.
