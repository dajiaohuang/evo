## What underground, leaf and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-be09c9f854}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records a xylopodium, a woody underground storage organ, and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves are narrow or lance-shaped, with blunt bases and flat margins. The inflorescence is leafy. Flower stalks are listed in two groups, 0.1–6 mm or 7–20 mm, and bracteoles are present. The 3–10 mm floral tube bears soft hairs; its sharp spur points horizontally. Purple petals are absent by the fruit stage, and stamens attach in the upper third of the tube. A vesicle is present, while the ovary-base nectar gland bends downward. The fruit is cylindrical and the seed margin thickened or blunt. The database field lists alternatives but no frequency or sampling frame.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-9b02481871}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without a collection locality or date.
