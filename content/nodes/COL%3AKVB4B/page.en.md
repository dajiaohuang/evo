## Sampling across eight genera {#brief}
A phylogenetic study used the name Gasterosteoidei for a suborder containing eight genera and sampled nine species, with at least one representative from each genus. Its value for this broad node is the design and limits of that comparison: the sequence tree is a sample-based hypothesis, not a complete portrait of every named lineage.

## How the study sampled Gasterosteoidei {#suborder-sampling}
Kawahara and colleagues included at least one species from each of the eight genera assigned to Gasterosteoidei in a 2008 mitogenome-based classification. Their nine-species sample combined 14,807 base pairs of mitochondrial genome sequence with 11 nuclear genes (8,703 base pairs). The paper’s principal inference is framed around Gasterosteidae; this suborder page therefore uses the study as evidence about sampled representatives and does not infer a complete set of suborder relationships from the family-level conclusion.
