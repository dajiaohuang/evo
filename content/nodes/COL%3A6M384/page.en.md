## Hessea cinnamomea {#s-555a702d7e}
Hessea cinnamomea has widely funnel-shaped flowers with crisped tepals and a claret-coloured centre. Its regional account records seasonally waterlogged peaty lowlands below 60 m. At the cited reserve and Modderdam sites, many bulbs flowered after fires in 1986 and 1989; flowering declined as shrub cover recovered over the following one to three years. These observations describe those populations and dates.
