## Cells of strain 5S69T {#s-63182c4fa0}
The paper's wild-origin type strain (the reference strain used in the species description), 5S69T, was Gram-negative in the standard staining classification, strictly anaerobic (growing only without oxygen), and non-spore-forming. Its cells were straight or slightly curved rods measuring 0.35–0.45 × 1.0–2.0 μm, with a single flagellum at one end for movement. These observations describe this strain; they do not characterize variation across populations, growth phases, or environments.

## A sulfate-reducing strain from injection water {#s-0c40c4cb25}
The authors isolated strain 5S69T from injection water collected at the Vostochno-Anzirskoe oil field in Tatarstan, Russia, and characterized it as sulfate-reducing. The study does not measure this organism's activity or role across the oil field.

## The reported locality {#s-f0151f705b}
The paper reports one source for the type strain: injection water from the Vostochno-Anzirskoe oil field in Tatarstan, Russia. This identifies the collection site, not a wider range; additional localities, a range boundary, and sampling completeness were not assessed.

## How the authors placed the species {#s-14761f4e30}
To assess the strain's position, the authors compared its 16S ribosomal RNA (rRNA) sequence, 120 conserved single-copy proteins, and genomic relatedness indices. They concluded that 5S69T represents a distinct species in *Pseudodesulfovibrio*. This is the study's taxonomic conclusion from its sampled taxa and analyses; alternative taxon sampling and independent phylogenomic review were not assessed.
