## What the mitochondrial tree can show {#s-d5c56f1aaf}
The study joins partial mitochondrial 12S and 16S sequences. It reports a broad tree of 36 species and selected Asian subtrees based on 43 source species labels. Any placement depends on which taxa were sampled and how strongly branches were supported; the reported position of Bufoides nearest the focal toad has low support and does not establish a sister relationship. These two mitochondrial regions do not provide an independent nuclear history or evidence of reproductive isolation. Cleared-and-stained specimens and radiographs are described only where the paper explicitly labels them. The paper's phrase “uncorrected k2p” and its historical accession strings are retained as reported.
