## Aeschynomene selloi {#s-c01049d1bc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Aeschynomene selloi* records upright stems, pinnate leaves with elliptic to oblong leaflets, yellow flowers, straight pods, glossy kidney-shaped brown seeds. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
