## Trachyandra thyrsoidea {#s-26372c6d8c}
Trachyandra thyrsoidea has several spreading roots, sometimes swollen toward their tips, and narrow leaves usually covered with long tawny hairs. Pale-pink flowers are arranged in a compact, many-branched panicle with conspicuous white bracts. Stony flats and slopes are the regional account’s recorded habitat.
