## Large size and head shape in the local assemblage {#s-dd8d10190f}
The revision identifies testudinea by its large size, robust body, wide head, flat top of the head and characteristic advertisement call. The comparison is to other species recorded in Abra de Zamora and cites earlier descriptive work. No new sex-specific body-length sample or numerical diagnostic cutoff is given in this account.

## Eastern-slope lower montane forest {#s-57e5261ea1}
It is reported as uncommon near El Tambo in eastern-slope evergreen lower montane forest below2200m, and as the only Gastrotheca recorded on that slope in the account. This is local survey coverage, not evidence that other marsupial frogs could never occur there. The elevation is not treated as a global species maximum.

## Audible males and difficult visual detection {#s-c2233e3941}
Individuals were difficult to find in dense vegetation, whereas characteristic male calls could be heard frequently, especially on rainy days. The revision therefore distinguishes a low rate of visual encounters from audible activity. It does not supply a measured population density, a rain-manipulation experiment or numerical acoustic measurements for testudinea in its five-species call analysis.
