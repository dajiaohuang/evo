## Asparagus pearsonii {#s-26ece61a5d}
Asparagus pearsonii is a pale yellow-green, low, hard shrub with lax, spreading branches and very few or weak spines. Its small cream flowers appear before or with the fine cladodes; berries are blue to black. The regional account records sandy flats, boulder cracks and watercourse margins, with flowering reported after late-summer rains.
