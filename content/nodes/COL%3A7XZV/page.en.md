These are source classifications, not individual observations or evidence of traits shared by every descendant. Beak, wing, tarsus, tail and mass fields remain uninterpreted here and do not establish a genus-level size comparison.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-beabf1111f}
The two limited accounts summarize historical ecology, not every accepted descendant. COL26.8 navigation, same-name same-family association and two one-to-one same-name crosswalks do not remove later differences in species concepts. Coarse categories are not independent behavioral observations, and historical maps are not current occupancy or conservation assessments. Morphological values may be inferred from relatives and mass may come from related species; they are not interpreted here as direct measurements of Touit species.
