## Syagrus cearensis: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-364bed339d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes the stem surface as smooth and lists solitary, clumping and aerial forms. Leaves are green and pinnate, with deciduous, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is arched. Pinnae are leathery, rigid and grouped, inserted at different angles, with asymmetrically acuminate tips. They are dark green and slightly discolorous.

The inflorescence is described as paniculate or pendulous, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved or pruinose; its rachis is sparsely tomentose, and the rachillae are arranged helically along it. Fruits are globose. The epicarp is yellowish and tomentose; the mesocarp is fibrous and succulent. The source describes the endocarp as woody and globose or oval, and the endosperm as homogeneous or having an internal cavity.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
