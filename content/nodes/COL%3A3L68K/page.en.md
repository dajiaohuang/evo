## Hessea stellaris {#s-2701522048}
Hessea stellaris bears pale to deep-pink star-shaped flowers and narrow leaves, typically 1–7 mm wide. The regional account distinguishes it from H. breviflora by the proportions of its short floral tube and the fact that its lower leaf sheath does not emerge above ground. Sandy or clay flats are the recorded habitat; these comparisons belong to the cited regional treatment.
