## Symplocos insignis Brand {#s-6a6c396360}
## Separate male and female flowers

Symplocos insignis is dioecious. Its leaves range from membranous to leathery and from elliptic to oblong; the margins are toothed near the tip, with glands that may persist at the teeth. Inflorescences may be cymes, racemes or panicles, and their peduncles are not hidden by the deciduous bracts. Flowers are unisexual, male or female, with three or four corolla lobes. The stamens occur in alternating groups; the attached disk may be exposed or covered by calyx lobes. The ellipsoid or ovoid drupe retains a calyx that extends beyond the disk.
