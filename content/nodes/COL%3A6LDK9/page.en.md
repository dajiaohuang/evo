## Inflorescence, flowers, and fruit {#s-8d40266837}
The peduncle bract is reduced, and each secondary branch bears one flower. The calyx bracteoles are ovate-deltoid with conspicuous veins. Flowers are erect; at anthesis the perianth is open, white to cream inside and green to olive outside. The stamens converge toward the gynoecium; their filiform filaments are white with a brown spot, and the anthers are ovoid. The fruit is a capsule whose style is completely divided into three.
