## Stems, leaves and flowers {#s-21f1dc7356}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records an unbranched stem and says that branches lack wings. Semi-sagittate stipules (small leaf-base appendages shaped like half an arrowhead) accompany elliptic or linear leaflets with prominent veins. Tendrils (slender curling structures) are bristle-like. The raceme (flower cluster along a central axis) has more than eight flowers; their color ranges from bluish to purple. The stigma (pollen-receiving tip) is split in two. This single regional field does not establish the full variation of these traits.

## Habitat category {#s-5490225cb1}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is a category recorded in one row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of distribution.
