## Leaf glands and paired leaflets {#s-2be253032c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes leaves arranged in two rows, each with two pairs of leaflets—the smaller blades attached to a leaf stalk. Nectar glands are recorded on the leaf stalk and its central axis, and also among the flower clusters. Those clusters generally carry more than eight flowers; the yellow petals differ in shape, and the entry counts ten stamens, the pollen-bearing parts. Mature oblong pods are hairless, and their sides twist as the fruit ripens.
