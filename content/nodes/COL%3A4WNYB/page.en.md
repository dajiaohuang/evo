## Senegalia giganticarpa {#s-bc1f50d2cc}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senegalia giganticarpa* as an accepted species of *Senegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records 4–6 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf) and 6–14 pairs of leaflets per pinna. The leaflets are oblong to falcate, silky on both faces, and 10–13 mm wide; the inflorescences are rounded heads and the pods 5–6.5 cm wide. The habitat field calls the plant terrestrial.
