## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-382ca69b95}
COL26.8 records Carphophis amoenus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-82d0facebd}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 350 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-bc40724553}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gray 2014.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-6cc4bc52ec}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5.1. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gray 2014. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-7118d08d54}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
