## Low stems and smooth or cobwebby leaves {#s-71254e45cd}
The record describes a perennial with prostrate or reclining stems and sessile leaves that range from elliptic to spoon-shaped. Leaf bases may narrow or clasp the stem, and the edges may be entire or toothed. The upper and lower surfaces may be smooth or cobwebby. Yellow radiate heads form corymb-like clusters; the smooth fruits bear a white pappus that falls away. The habitat field says terrestrial.
