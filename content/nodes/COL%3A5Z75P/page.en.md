## Nectarivore is not a flower-visiting record {#s-ee5e4a30a8}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Nectarivore and Trophic.Level=Omnivore. The source defines Nectarivore as at least 60% of food resources from nectar; that threshold is not a locally measured seasonal nectar percentage here. Omnivore level means obtaining resources from multiple trophic levels in roughly equal proportions, separately from the niche definition. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-86022c164e}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Shrubland; density code 3 means open vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility. These historical categories provide neither current range-wide occupancy nor individual movement radii or routes; historical map fields are not interpreted in this batch.
