## What leaf and flower measurements are recorded? {#s-b9816a3174}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field describes hairless leaves that do not turn black when dry. They are sessile (without a petiole), more than 5 mm wide, with flat, blunt, notched, shallowly heart-shaped or stem-clasping bases. The vein pattern is described as either with secondary veins fading before the edge or with only the main vein apparent; there are at least two lateral veins on each side and no small hair tufts in their axils. The floral prophyll, a small bract below the flower, reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm; the extra sepal-like lobes below the calyx (epicalyx segments) are 2–11 mm long and shorter than the sepals, spreading outward or standing erect. The corolla is 3.5–7 cm across and has 19–55 stamens. These are regional database states, not specimen-level measurements.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a6cf4f5fea}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
