## Drosera capillaris {#s-bd3bb9a716}
## A conspicuous flower stalk rises above the leaves

The morphology account describes a stemless plant with spatulate, hairless leaves and rectangular stipules. Its upright flower stalk, or scape, is conspicuous and taller than the leaves; the stalk and sepals are hairless. The corolla is pink, with three bifurcate styles. Ovoid seeds have a papillose seed coat. The broad habitat label is terrestrial.
