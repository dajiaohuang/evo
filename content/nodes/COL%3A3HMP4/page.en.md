## Gurania sinuata (Benth.) Cogn. {#s-ab971105f6}
## Rough leaves may be entire or three-lobed

Gurania sinuata has tendrils described as simple or four-angled. Its rough leaves may be entire or three-lobed, with a heart-shaped or notched base, a blunt tip and hairs shorter than 5 mm. Racemes bear stalked flowers; the species is dioecious. The tubular to ovoid hypanthium is densely woolly, and its upright sepals are linear-lanceolate with pointed tips. Staminate flowers have two stalkless, linear anthers, each with an appendage and two pollen-bearing compartments.
