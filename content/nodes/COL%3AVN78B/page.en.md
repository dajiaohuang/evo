## The name and this data account {#s-ac6c7d86de}
Plegadis ridgwayi is accepted in COL26.8 within Threskiornithidae. AVONET v7 links its record to the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and reports 4 measured individuals, each with a complete morphological set. Trait-level usable samples can still differ; the historical name is not proof of modern concept equivalence.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-ee7a78ca31}
Mean beak length is 89.7 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width and depth at that edge average 10.7 mm and 14.1 mm. Each is a separate standardized dimension, not an identification threshold.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-5f8e221015}
Mean unflattened wing length is 277.8 mm, from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 74.2 mm. The separately compiled mass mean is 579.41 g, labelled Dunning. It is not a weight measurement for each of the four morphology records.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-14dc5af1a4}
AVONET assigns this record the categories Wetland, Aquatic predator and Terrestrial for habitat, trophic niche and lifestyle. They are broad comparative labels, not a complete account of the species’ feeding or habitat use.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-0946031a60}
The migration field is partially migratory. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude −24.64° to −7.13°; these limits do not cover every seasonal movement and are not a current range map. The map and morphological measurements come from separate source steps.

## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-ee86c30a1e}
Three focal fields are available in the 2015 historical life-history record, linked by the same name and family. Because the archive can inherit information across older species and subspecies, that link does not show that every summarized value belongs to the current COL26.8 concept alone.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-8322cd8352}
The archived adult body-mass summary is 584 g. It may combine adults of different or unspecified sex and is not an individual range; no birth or hatching mass is included in this account.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-f684d75f5e}
The clutch or litter field summarizes 2 eggs or offspring. The archive combines those event types in one field, so the count should be read with that definition. Reproductive durations are not supplied here.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-d393adc398}
The maximum_longevity_y field is 18 years, the largest record retained under the archive method. It does not describe typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan or a survival limit for every bird.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-246c289422}
The same-name source labels include Dunning (1992), de Magalhaes and Costa (2009), and Jetz, Sekercioglu, and Bohning-Gaese (2008). Field-level rows, values and source labels are retained in the archive; row counts are not bird counts, and repeated citations do not create independent observations.

## What one ecological field can tell us {#s-a82c0bb722}
EltonTraits 1.0 retains one same-name, same-family ecological field group for this historical record: foraging position. Diet is withheld because its source code is C. The older taxonomy and source limitations do not resolve modern splits or synonyms.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-9d8a362d5b}
The retained species-level position weights are 80% at the water surface or just below and 20% on the ground. They standardize literature descriptions of feeding positions; they are not time budgets, nest-site data or evidence that the bird uses only these places.

## Trace each field group to its source labels {#s-2e34853a29}
The foraging field carries Ref_1, recorded as Handbook of the Birds of the World volume 1. The archive preserves that association; the underlying book has not been independently reviewed here, and the label is not a second observation.

## Evidence limits {#s-f41e681bd4}
AVONET v7 reports means under the historical HBW–BirdLife name: four measured individuals had complete morphological sets, while mass is a separate Dunning compilation. Wetland, Aquatic predator, Terrestrial and partially migratory are broad categories; historical latitude limits are not current occupancy. The name match does not prove equivalence to COL26.8.

The life-history values retain the 2015 archive’s historical inheritance and field definitions; the longevity value is a maximum record. EltonTraits preserves one foraging-position group, with diet code C withheld because of uncertainty, interpolation or source-link limits. Its percentages standardize literature descriptions; Ref_1 remains an archive label, not an independently reviewed observation. Current range, population and conservation need other sources.
