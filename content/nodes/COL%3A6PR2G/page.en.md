## Linear leaves with rolled-under edges {#s-2f198b48bb}
This record notes a thick underground root and leaves that may occur along the stem or only near the base or top. The linear, subleathery leaves have smooth upper surfaces, greyish undersides and edges rolled under. Flower heads may be stalkless or stalked and solitary; the involucre ranges from bell-shaped to cylindrical. Each head contains about 10–15 florets, and the cylindrical fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
