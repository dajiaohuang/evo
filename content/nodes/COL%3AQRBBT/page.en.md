## Accepted name and placement {#s-58241287a1}
COL26.8 accepts Micryletta hekouensis Liu, Hou, Mo & Rao, 2021 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Micryletta (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Night-time streamside observations {#s-a9dbe85ba4}
In the Ha Nam survey, specimens were found on the ground beside a small stream between 19:30 and 21:30, surrounded by mixed secondary forest. Males called at the site. These records document local adult activity and do not establish a complete daily activity pattern or exclusive habitat requirements.

## Tadpoles in temporary puddles {#s-2db4b784b0}
The species account reported tadpoles in temporary water puddles. That observation is retained at the survey’s scope; it does not establish egg deposition, the complete larval-development sequence or whether every population uses temporary puddles.
