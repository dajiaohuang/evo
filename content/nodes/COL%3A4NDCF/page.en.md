## Psammotropha mucronata {#s-73cf65c289}
## Translucent leaves and slender sprays distinguish this variable species

Psammotropha mucronata forms low cushions or patches. Its slender stems are 2–10 cm long and are not widened at the tip. The thin, partly translucent leaves narrow toward the base and end in a point or short bristle; their midrib is often invisible. Erect or spreading inflorescences reach 3–25 cm, with small flowers commonly grouped on one side. The source notes substantial variation and distinguishes the species from P. myriantha by its slimmer stems, undepressed leaf tufts, narrowed leaves and less crowded flowers. It records damp rocky mountain and grassveld habitats.
