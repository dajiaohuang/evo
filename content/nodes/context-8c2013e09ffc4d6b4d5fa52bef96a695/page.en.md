## The source concept {#s-1fcc68fee2}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Odontophrynus cultripes as row Anf1384, classified in Odontophrynus, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-a7991f15e5}
The row marks fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage variation in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against those revisions; verify the historical categories before reuse. No replacement values are inferred, and the notice does not imply that every original entry is wrong.

## Seasonal activity {#s-a54c51f985}
The row places activity in wet and warm and dry and warm conditions. In this dataset, seasonal categories compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-da196f27dd}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 70 mm for Odontophrynus cultripes. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-fc73810348}
The row reports up to one reproductive event per year. These literature-derived extrema and counts do not separately identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions. Size values may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-e26984eb9b}
The deposited record marks a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
