## Record identity and classification {#s-13e6c32122}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf5066 records Chiromantis cherrapunjiae under Chiromantis, Rhacophoridae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-df13fb90c1}
This AmphiBIO row marks arboreal among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Seasonal activity categories {#s-41c12ffde7}
AmphiBIO records wet and cold for seasonal activity in this row. The categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not map to fixed calendar months across regions.

## Developmental category {#s-8676d86627}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists larval stage as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
