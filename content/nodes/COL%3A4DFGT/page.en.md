## Parathesis glabra Donn.Sm. {#s-d603b16f96}
## Star-scaled leaves and dark-dotted fruit

Parathesis glabra is a shrub or slender tree up to 15 m tall. Its elliptic leaves have long, tapering tips and dark punctate marks; the underside is initially covered with tiny, pressed star-shaped scales, which fall away early. Axillary panicles usually shorter than the leaves bear small flowers with narrow petals. The depressed-globose fruit dries to about 8 mm across. The species is reported from Costa Rica and Panama.
