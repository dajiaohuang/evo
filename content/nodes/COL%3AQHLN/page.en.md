## Rock and terrestrial categories in a regional record {#s-499166cc5a}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Campylopus cryptopodioides Broth. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its two entries are habitat categories, not collection locations.

One category is “Saxícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The other is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The source rows do not give a locality, survey date or complete range.

Neither habitat field supplies collection locality, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

These two source labels are the only biological content in the regional record; they do not make a full species account.
