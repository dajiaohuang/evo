## The name in COL26.8 {#s-b14e0c9a48}
COL26.8 lists *Craugastor batrachylus* (Taylor, 1940) as an accepted species in its 20 August 2026 release. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Craugastoridae (family) → Craugastor (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## How to read this profile {#s-5eaa8adb41}
This page reports two trait categories from AmphiBIO v1 and identifies the limits of that historical record. The compilation does not provide a complete account of the species or its current populations.

## A historical terrestrial-foraging category {#s-948b0a0144}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1241 follows the compilation's Frost 2011 species concept and marks Ter=1 (terrestrial) as a broad foraging category. It does not specify season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat categories but does not say whether this row was affected. No replacement value is supplied, so Ter remains a historical record rather than a verified account of current ecology; the revision notice alone does not establish that this entry is wrong.

## AmphiBIO's developmental category {#s-d3be15cfef}
The same row assigns Dir=1, or direct development, under the Frost 2011 source concept. This label does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing, and its match to the current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.
