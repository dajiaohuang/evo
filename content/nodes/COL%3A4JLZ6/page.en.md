## How is the plant built? {#s-2d83572c2c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records a thickened main root and an elongated caudex, the short stem base from which leaves and shoots arise. An aerial stem is present. The leaves are narrow and pointed; the upright flowering stalk bears flat hairs with no stated direction. The flowers have four stamens and a smooth corolla tube, and the fruit is recorded with three smooth, oval seeds.

## Where does it grow? {#s-0756d55913}
The dataset categorizes Plantago trinitatis as rupicolous, meaning associated with rocky substrates. It does not name a collection site.
