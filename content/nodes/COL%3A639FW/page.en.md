## A living frog in an otherwise fossil lineage {#s-3c3d58a740}
The study combines 2,503 base pairs from three nuclear and three mitochondrial genes with high-resolution scans of four Latonia nigriventer individuals collected in 1955 and 2011. Its morphological analysis scores 22 osteological characters. The authors identify L. nigriventer as the only living representative of Latonia and explicitly hypothesize that the lineage reached the Hula Basin in the Miocene. The colonization date is a hypothesis, not a directly observed event.
