Thirteen native isiZulu-speaking volunteers from 5 Zululand areas took part in two workshop groups in 2016. Photograph-assisted discussions yielded 6 broad folk categories. Later species names combined those categories, published names and guidelines, with help from Bongani Mkhize; other broad terms in the paper came from earlier publications or borrowing. Folk categories can cross scientific genera and families, so one vernacular term need not identify one scientific genus or species. ## Three species scores use different inputs {#s-7b85b28239}
Table A4 gives Arthroleptis adolfifriederici DBS2/TBS0/SBS4, SI6; A. nyungwensis DBS2/TBS1/SBS4, SI7; and A. schubotzi DBS2/TBS0/SBS1, SI3. DBS reflects represented Rwanda ecozones and national endemism; TBS uses earlier national red-list proposals; SBS is a categorical input for sensitivity to habitat disturbance. SI sums the three inputs for each species. It is not a genus score, extinction probability or experimentally measured tolerance. A site’s RABI adds the SI values of detected species without dividing by richness. Doubled category weights reflect the authors’ prioritization choices, not a measured doubling of biological risk. The threat scores are historical inputs, and the combined VU/DD score cannot distinguish Vulnerable from Data Deficient on its own.
