## A short flower tube and sticky leaves {#s-f2cab68a58}
Geissorhiza brevituba grows 12–20 cm tall and has deep-pink flowers with a relatively short perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts), about 8 mm long. Its sword-shaped leaves have thickened, sticky margins and midribs; the flowers are bilaterally symmetrical, with the stamens and style leaning to one side.
