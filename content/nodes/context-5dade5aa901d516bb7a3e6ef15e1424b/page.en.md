## The source concept {#s-4090b0e1a7}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis nguruensis as row Anf0136, within Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, Anura. Its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The following traits describe that deposited source record; correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Recorded habitat fields {#s-22dc0327fe}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). Fos=NA, Aqu=NA and Arb=NA are missing values; they do not show that those categories are absent. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or developmental changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries; its affected fields include Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu and Arb. No row-level comparison for this species is captured here, no corrected value is substituted, and the revision notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-29cdb9b6b6}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=58 mm as maximum adult body length. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
