## What branch, flower and seed traits are recorded for Amanoa cupatensis? {#s-31f28fb30a}
The branches are hairless, and the petioles are black. Stipules are inconspicuous. The inflorescence is a bisexual raceme. Flowers lack an androphore and styles; pedicels may be present or absent. The pericarp is thin. Seeds have a pitted surface.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5fa2fdfc9c}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
