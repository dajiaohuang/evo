## Four wild individuals represented by different material {#s-91ed21e165}
The 2024 study generates whole-genome data for four Cat Ba langurs spanning Hang Cai, Sanctuary and Cua Dong sub-populations. Blood came from two females translocated in the wild in 2012; tissues came from deceased infants found in the wild in 2015 and 2018. This is not a same-day random sample of four adults. Mean sequencing coverage of 32.7× describes read coverage rather than surveys. No close relatedness was detected among the four, but their genomes do not constitute an island census or a representative sample of every age class.

## Nuclear genomes support three northern species {#s-c35b737c72}
Nuclear-genome analyses place poliocephalus as sister to the joint francoisi–leucocephalus lineage, differing from treatments that combine poliocephalus and leucocephalus based partly on similar pelage. Genetic clusters and gene-flow analyses also support treating the northern three separately as species. The estimated poliocephalus separation at about 1.0 Ma, with a 0.8–1.3 Ma interval, is a model result rather than an exact origin date. Failure to detect extensive secondary gene flow does not establish absence of exchange at every time or resolve all boundaries in the genus.

## Low overall diversity with regional genomic differences {#s-f08caf8674}
The four genomes show low overall diversity and long runs of homozygosity, while protein-coding regions and the gene-rich human chromosome 19 ortholog retain relatively more diversity. ROHs inform inbreeding history; variation remaining in some regions does not erase risks elsewhere. Reference genomes and ROH settings differ with analytical purpose, and distinct metrics are not interchangeable percentages. These are genetic comparisons of four samples, without a current island population count, individual health assessment or future reproductive-success estimate.

## Candidate variants in a high-calcium environment {#s-fbdc051eca}
The paper discusses high calcium in karst food and water and identifies non-synonymous variants in calcium-related genes: DNA differences that can alter protein amino acids. Some of the 92 related genes occur in multiple functional categories, so category counts cannot be summed as independent genes. The authors distinguish sample-private variants from changes present at low frequency in other langurs. These offer adaptation candidates rather than functional experiments on every variant, and Cat Ba results are not calcium-tolerance measurements of an ebenus individual.

## Saltwater adaptation remains a preliminary interpretation {#s-88899f130b}
The authors connect previously observed brackish-water licking/drinking with sodium-related variants: 30 non-synonymous changes in 22 genes, plus a premature stop codon in CDH26 in all four samples that may affect its intracellular component. They explicitly regard saltwater-adaptation interpretations as preliminary. No safe intake salinity was measured here, transporter function independently retested or supplementary video watched. A shared variant in four samples is not automatically species-wide fixation, and a candidate mechanism does not replace existing local habitat and behavioral studies.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-00b7932538}
The current MDD page uses the English name Cat Ba Langur and associates the species with Cat Ba Island, Vietnam. Hendershott, Behie and Rawson studied two reproductive groups on the island, observing their use of rocks, sparsely vegetated areas, slopes and cliffs in karst terrain. The local sample does not represent every group or habitat on the island.

## Ecological evidence {#s-6ef733258d}
The study collected 180 days of observations from two reproductive groups of 7 and 10–13 animals, with estimated home ranges of about 22 and 50 ha. Both groups had smaller ranges in the dry season, but the study could not test seasonal variation statistically. Scans were often on rocks, sparsely vegetated areas, exposed slopes and steep cliffs; foraging occurred mainly on gradual slopes, especially in the dry season. The journal full text is restricted, so this page uses only results in the public abstract.
