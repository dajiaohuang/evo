## Asparagus nodulosus: swollen spine bases {#s-cf0cae6a23}
A South African regional account describes a scandent plant with a hard, woody rhizome and cylindrical roots. The stems have very short upright white hairs and swollen dark knobs below; these knobs are old, enlarged spine bases, about 4 mm across. Younger spines protrude from the stem and are short and sharp. Numerous dull-green, hairy branches spread and grow upright, with many short, hairless branchlets. Fine, thin cladodes, about 5 mm long, form dense semicircular bundles. Flowers and berries were not seen in the material described.

The account says the plant usually occurs in woodland or bush, often near rivers.
