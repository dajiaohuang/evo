The historical summaries report an adult body mass of 5036.54 g and a birth-or-hatching mass of 100.94 g. ## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-6f13de665e}
The archive reports female maturity at 307.83 d (the source defines this as age at first conception), 3.69 offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, 64.72 d from conception or fertilization to birth, and 81.51 d until dependence on food other than maternal milk. Here, d means days. The litter-or-clutch field combines counts of offspring or eggs, so its fractional summary does not mean one event produced part of an individual. The timing values describe different events, not one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-61b18ed7dc}
The life-history values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 archive. They are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans, or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data among historical species and subspecies. A matching name and family, together with same-name source rows, supports this comparison but does not prove equivalence to a COL26.8 concept or remove every inherited value. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually. Other sources are needed for diet, behavior, current range, conservation, and fields absent here.
