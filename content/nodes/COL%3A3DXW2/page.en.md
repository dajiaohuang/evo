These are compilation summaries rather than measurements of one animal. Reported non-longevity values are database medians or interpolated medians. The female-maturity field is defined as age at first conception, not first birth. Gestation is the source-defined interval from conception or fertilization to birth. The litter/clutch field can combine offspring and egg counts; a decimal is a compilation summary, not a fractional reproductive event. Maximum longevity is the longest record found, not a mean or typical lifespan. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly established in the archive, and historical inheritance can remain in a same-name row; the association does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept for *Felis silvestris*.

These life-history values are compilation summaries that may inherit older species or subspecies records. Source conditions are not uniform, and a historical same-name match does not establish equivalence with the pinned COL26.8 concept.
