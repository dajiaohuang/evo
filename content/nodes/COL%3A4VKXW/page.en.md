## *Sciodaphyllum sprucei*: long stipules and arching lateral veins {#s-c4e029a611}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field describes nine or ten leaflets on each leaf, with strap-shaped stipules—appendages at the leaf base—longer than 5 cm. The elliptic to oblanceolate blades taper at the base and end in a long point. Their upper surfaces are hairless; lateral veins curve and join toward the margin, and the small stalk of each leaflet is shorter than half its blade.

The lower leaf surfaces also bear hairs that become sparse. The terminal flower structure has one to three primary branches, with secondary but no tertiary branches; its partial clusters are heads. The dry fruit is four- or five-lobed. These characters come from a regional flora field, not a population survey.
