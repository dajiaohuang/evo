## The original genus description sampled three male vouchers {#s-4e0ec72ab7}
The 2018 study described inexpectata, orlovi and occidentalis and proposed Vietnamophryne as a distinct Asterophryinae lineage, sister to Siamophryne in its mitochondrial genealogy. The 12S–16S fragment did not fully resolve all subfamily lineages. Each original species had one preserved adult male, with snout–vent lengths 14.2, 15.4 and 20.5 mm respectively; those values are not population ranges or female measurements. Comparative finger reduction, dorsal texture, proportions and live ventral colour help distinguish the examined material. The three original species are selected historical examples, not all six members of the pinned genus. Later names need their own evidence rather than inheriting the original males’ measurements.

## Concealed encounters leave much of the life history unknown {#s-9e24fdceb9}
The original inexpectata was found under a rotten log in wet soil, orlovi near fern roots underground, and occidentalis in litter after rain, with an additional uncollected under-log encounter. These observations support tentative concealed or semi-fossorial interpretations, not a measured underground activity budget or universal burrowing requirement. No calls were recorded, but missing recordings do not prove silence. The confirmed Kon Chu Rang, Phia Oac and Doi Tung localities remain separate from predicted other Vietnamese mountains, Laos or Myanmar. The paper’s Data Deficient recommendations are dated author proposals. Species pages preserve plant-name differences, a misplaced species name and the separation of one voucher from an uncollected encounter.
