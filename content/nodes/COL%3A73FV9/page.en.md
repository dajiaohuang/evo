## Cobweb-like hairs and five lengthwise veins {#s-9441b96cfa}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes cobweb-like (arachnoid) hairs on young branches. Leaves are elliptic or ovate, with acuminate tips (long-tapering points), blunt bases, and entire margins without a fringe of hairs. Their veins arise above the base, and five lengthwise veins are listed. The underside has a dense hair covering with cobweb-like hairs. The field gives this regional description without frequencies or a full account of variation.

## Six petals and alternative ovary hair states {#s-b5a0d702b6}
Flowers occur in dichasial clusters, a branching arrangement. The flower cup (hypanthium) is bell-shaped. The calyx falls away and has truncate lobes. Each flower has six petals. The connective, the central tissue in an anther, is not extended; its appendage has two lower (ventral) lobes and one upper (dorsal) tooth. Anthers are white with one small pollen opening. The ovary is listed as either hairless or hairy.

## A terrestrial label without range coverage {#s-6cd6f6aeb1}
