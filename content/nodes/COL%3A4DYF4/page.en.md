## What flower, fruit and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora fuchsiiflora? {#s-b8c2d126eb}
The woody stem is cylindrical and bears entire, hairless leaves with smooth margins. Sessile glands sit on the petioles. The raceme has linear, bristle-tipped bracts; stipules are unknown. Flowers lack sepal awns and have a cylindrical floral cup with a three-row corona. The operculum is fringed, the limen is absent, and the reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. Fruit, seed and stipule traits recorded as unknown remain unresolved in this account.

The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
