## Right M3 IVPP V20115 and referred tarsals {#s-2e9e569df6}
The holotype of *Mimolagus aurorae* is a right upper third molar (M3), IVPP V20115, from the Middle Eocene lower Irdin Manha Formation. Other teeth and tarsals are referred to the sample. These remains support comparative study of mimotonid anatomy and relationships, not a complete-body account.

## Observed material and comparative claims {#s-5b714ed83d}
The named teeth and tarsals preserve anatomy and document an Erlian Basin occurrence. The estimated mass, inferred herbivory and locomotion, and proposed relationship to lagomorphs come from comparisons; they do not make *Mimolagus* a direct ancestor.

## Teeth and ankle bones in comparative analyses {#s-bec441c4ef}
The holotype is right upper third molar IVPP V20115. Other teeth and tarsals are referred to the species. Dental and tarsal characters were included in comparative analyses of *Mimolagus*.

## Diet, movement and mass are comparative inferences {#s-2a4627056b}
Herbivory is inferred from dental form, with no direct food remains assigned. Tarsal comparisons inform locomotor interpretation; comparative regressions estimate a mass of about 4.5 kg rather than measure it. The lower Irdin Manha depositional setting does not resolve habitual microhabitat, and the proposed large herbivorous duplicidentate guild is an ecomorphological inference.
