## Helichrysum chrysargyrum {#s-90e15a64e0}
## Silvery leaves and crowded golden heads

The SANBI record describes a perennial herb up to 400 mm tall. Narrowly lance-shaped leaves reach 75–90 by 5–6 mm, taper to long points and carry a silvery, silky covering. Broad bell-shaped heads crowd into terminal corymb-like panicles. Inner bracts are yellow; outer bracts are pale golden-brown or straw-colored. Flowering is recorded from February to May and again from August to September. The habitat field says mountain grassland; it does not establish a complete range.
