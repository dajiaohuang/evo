## How do its kidney-shaped leaves and traps relate to the flower? {#s-d1434ef647}
Utricularia nephrophylla is described with kidney-shaped leaves and globose bladder traps that open at the base; each trap bears a simple, recurved appendage on its back. The white corolla has a three-lobed lower lip and a curved, cylindrical spur shorter than that lip. Its throat is bulging and yellow or orange. The capsule opens lengthwise and the seeds are globose; the source does not describe how these traits vary.
