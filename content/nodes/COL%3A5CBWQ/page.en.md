## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-129a04b577}
COL26.8 records Xenotyphlops grandidieri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Xenotyphlopidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e0da7cb3d6}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 284 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-8d53501e43}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015.
