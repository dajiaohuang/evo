## Crotalaria hilariana {#s-fb1723cd5a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Crotalaria hilariana* records plant 0.3–0.5 m tall with prostrate to ascending branches and simple leaves; yellow flowers, black hairless pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
