## Record identity and classification {#s-2ccd30df6a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6402 records Plethodon dorsalis under Plethodon, Plethodontidae, and Caudata, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-1a423dc11d}
This AmphiBIO row marks terrestrial; aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Adult food categories {#s-b765dd58f4}
The row lists arthropods as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-4ae11c36d8}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 111 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-d68574b39a}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists: minimum size at sexual maturity: 50 mm; maximum size at sexual maturity: 60 mm; minimum clutch count (offspring or eggs): 3; maximum clutch count (offspring or eggs): 9; maximum reproductive events per year: 1; minimum offspring or egg size: 4 mm; maximum offspring or egg size: 5 mm. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value. A size value may refer to an offspring or an egg.

## Developmental category {#s-cff251d7bb}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists direct development as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
