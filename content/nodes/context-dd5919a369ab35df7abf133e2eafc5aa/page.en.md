## Source classification and concept status {#s-3daf9acc3f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2880 records *Litoria nasuta* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-2d2550a2dd}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-36a4957bc5}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 10 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 50 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-5d05144d7f}
The row lists 20–100 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 1–1.3 mm. These are literature-compiled values; this row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and its size fields may refer to eggs.

AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
