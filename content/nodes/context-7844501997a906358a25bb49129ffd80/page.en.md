## The source concept {#s-b8d9ccdfda}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis kidogo as row Anf0130 under Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The source uses the species concept in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); alignment with a current concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-74ac319ab6}
The row flags terrestrial foraging. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the deposited category is retained without treating it as confirmed or necessarily wrong. Other strata are missing, not shown to be absent.

## Activity periods {#s-7be2f0b6cc}
The activity field flags diurnal activity. The row gives no seasonal or population-specific detail.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-6ae48707a9}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 15 mm. For anurans, the field represents snout–vent length; it is a compiled maximum rather than a description of every individual.
