## Fissidens bryoides: two habitat entries {#s-5469bc456b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists two habitat categories for this moss:

“Rupícola”

and “Terrícola.”

These are source labels from two regional dataset rows; neither gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
