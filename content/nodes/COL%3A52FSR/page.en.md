This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Separate histories in the two archives {#s-18e42c96e3}
ReptTraits combines separately cited traits in a species-level row. The 2015 life-history archive can transfer records between historical species and subspecies. The two tables have separate source histories; their values need not describe one shared specimen or population.

## Why one trait label is withheld {#s-86e00ea628}
Foraging mode is withheld for Sternotherus carinatus because its citation scope is unresolved: Harrington et al. (2018) studied snakes, while Rhodin et al. (2011) concerns turtles. Original values and labels remain in the source record; the mismatch does not show the value is false, and no substitute is inferred.
