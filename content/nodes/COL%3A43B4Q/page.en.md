## Mikania biformis {#s-7a671b14f5}
## Twining stems and opposite leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, smooth branches with shallow lengthwise lines and solid pith. Opposite leaves are elliptic to ovate, with entire margins rolled under, an arrow- or spear-shaped base, and a short, tapered tip. The upper surface is smooth and the lower surface hairy. Their veins are pinnate and loop near the edge; the thin to somewhat leathery blades have no interpetiolar stipules.

Loose panicles bear stalked heads. Bracts surround each head in oblong, smooth pieces with either acute or blunt tips. The bell-shaped corolla has triangular, smooth lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are recorded as smooth or sparsely hairy. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; it supplies no locality or complete range.
