## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Bunchosia itacarensis? {#s-43338f7bbf}
The smooth leaf blades have a blistered surface and alternate side veins. The flower stalk is short. Flowers sit opposite one another, with separate styles and petals whose margins lack glands. The orange drupe—a fleshy fruit with a hard inner layer—has two chambers.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f2d668955a}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
