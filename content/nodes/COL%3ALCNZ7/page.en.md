## Male throat colour in a diagnostic comparison {#s-becccfe10c}
The2020 comparison describes male nunciatus throats as violaceous, in contrast to the white centre/yellow edges reported for velocicantus. Sex and comparison scope matter: this paragraph does not establish female throat colour, a complete preservation-dependent colour series or population-wide variation. ## Duration and spacing in cited acoustic comparisons {#s-9410bdbb3c}
The2020 paper attributes to previous studies a maximum call duration of 357 ms, maximum 10 notes and inter-note interval 29–85 ms for nunciatus. These cited summaries preserve their units and do not describe the same sampling protocol or temperatures as the eight focal velocicantus males. Original source recordings have not been independently reanalysed, and no new mate-recognition experiment follows from this numerical contrast.
