## Ecological evidence {#s-fbdd223f6f}
A preliminary study at Bukit Semujan in Danau Sentarum National Park recorded feeding on 27 plant species during July and August 2021. Leaves made up the largest share of recorded foods, followed by fruit and seeds. Most activity occurred in canopy stratum C, then stratum B. The paper used the name P. chrysomelas ssp. cruciger, so these data describe a short local study under that taxonomic usage, not the entire COL accepted species.

## Classification {#s-6b891bd3a1}
“Three-colored langur” is the common name used by the local study; this entry follows the COL26.8 identity Presbytis chrysomelas. Other research has studied the black morph chrysomelas and cruciger separately as subspecies. Local observations illustrate variation among forms but do not define a complete species range.

## Evidence limits {#s-f8bf08d4be}
Ecological evidence is limited to two months of observations at one Danau Sentarum site in 2021; the paper used the subspecific combination P. chrysomelas ssp. cruciger. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation. This account presents historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are reported compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found.
