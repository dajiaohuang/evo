## Restio fuscidulus {#s-5cdc9cd89e}
## Large female spikelets on dry rocky slopes

The account describes Restio fuscidulus as a rhizomatous or tufted perennial up to 50 cm, with simple or sparsely branched stems and spreading sheaths. Its female spikelets are nearly spherical and 11–25 mm long. Dry rocky slopes are the recorded habitat.
