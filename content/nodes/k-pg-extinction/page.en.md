## The K–Pg impact and the abrupt fossil turnover {#s-bb0eb00612}
Iridium-rich boundary clay and impact ejecta link the K–Pg crisis to an extraterrestrial impact, with Chicxulub identified as the source crater. High-precision 40Ar/39Ar dates place the boundary at 66.043 ± 0.043 Ma and resolve impact and extinction as synchronous within about 32,000 years. That event-level link does not mean every species vanished at once: Deccan volcanism contributed background stress, and survival and recovery varied among regions and groups.
