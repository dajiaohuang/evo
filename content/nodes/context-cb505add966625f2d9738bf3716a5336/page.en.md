## The source concept {#s-0acdcfdc5a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0048 records Allobates pittieri in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat field: Ter=1 {#s-c41003a152}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). The fields Fos=NA, Aqu=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this does not show that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Adult diet field: Arthro=1 {#s-42319e1784}
The row records Arthro=1 (arthropods). Vert=NA remains missing. AmphiBIO compiles adult-diet categories from literature reports, including expert judgement, observations or stomach contents. The listed categories do not describe a complete diet or measured proportions.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=18.8 mm {#s-994d1902f2}
Body_size_mm=18.8 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Reproductive_output_y: maximum 1 event/year {#s-67837f6b18}
The row lists Reproductive_output_y=1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-fa2675ee5a}
Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
