## Potamogeton trichoides: submerged leaves and diagnostic traits {#s-35d9392d09}
A 1997 South African regional account describes Potamogeton trichoides as a small, wiry herb up to 0.75 m high. All leaves are submerged and usually 1 mm wide. The lacunar system bordering the midrib is absent or very narrow. The stipule is folded. The fruit is usually tuberculate and has a basal tooth.

A 2004 diagnostic account distinguishes it among other linear-leaved species by mentioning open and tightly inrolled stipules; the midrib occupies 30–70% of leaf width at the base.
