## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-4fbe5f607d}
The source records an aerial stem, fibrous leaf sheaths, and absent or very short petioles. The leaflets are grouped and evenly arranged along the leaf axis, attached at one angle, with smooth margins. In bisexual inflorescences, female flowers occur in pairs with male flowers near the base of each small branch, while only male flowers occur farther out; the field also describes male inflorescences. Male flowers have six stamens. The fruit has a thick middle layer and a strongly fibrous inner wall attached to it, with one or more seeds.
