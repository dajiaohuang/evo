## A small forest hedgehog in eastern China {#field-study}
Shi and colleagues described Mesechinus orientalis from seven specimens collected in Anhui and Zhejiang between 2018 and 2023. The type series includes an adult male holotype from Xikou Town, Xuancheng, collected in May 2023, plus six adult paratypes; the specimens are deposited at Anhui Normal University. The species is small-bodied relative to other Mesechinus, with reported head–body length averaging 188.83 mm and spines 18–20 mm long. In the sampled localities it occurred in scrubland and subtropical broadleaf evergreen forest from 30 to 700 m elevation.

The diagnosis combines short spines with cranial and tooth characters: the second upper premolar (P2) has two roots that are not fully fused, while the third premolar (P3) is small with a vestigial protocone. Molecular analyses place M. orientalis as sister to the lineage containing M. hughi and M. wangi. The authors estimate that split at about 1.10 million years ago, with a 95% interval of 0.78–1.47 Ma; this is a model-based divergence estimate, not the age of a fossil or the first appearance of the species.
