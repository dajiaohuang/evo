## Teilhardina teeth across the PETM {#s-6b555853ca}
Correlated sections record separate Teilhardina dental samples from Asia, Europe and North America across the Paleocene–Eocene Thermal Maximum. Their proposed order is a regional appearance sequence, not a single migrating population or an ancestor chain.

## Different species, different sections {#s-57313c2fab}
The comparison includes T. asiatica specimen IVPP V12357, European T. belgica specimen IRSNB M64, and North American T. brandti material including USNM 493913 and USNM 493914. Figure 2 places these dental samples in correlated PETM sections; they are not parts of one animal or a single locality assemblage.

## A sequence built from correlation {#s-b6835eeedd}
The study proposes an Asia-to-Europe-to-North-America appearance order within a short earliest-Eocene interval. That order depends on dental species assignments and carbon-isotope correlation between sections. It does not identify one migrating population, a direct ancestor series or the origin date of crown Primates.
