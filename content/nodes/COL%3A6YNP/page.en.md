## Three broad morphotypes, separate evidence sets {#s-04ec1167fd}
Zaher and colleagues examined 322 morphological specimens; their separate DNA dataset contained 34 Pseudalsophis individuals (two from mainland Peru and 32 from the Galápagos). The paper describes three broadly defined morphotypes. Some species or populations within morphotype clusters overlap morphometrically. The analyses supported Pseudalsophis monophyly and placed mainland P. elegans as sister to the Galápagos radiation.

## A modeled colonization interval {#s-decbbf5d9c}
The time-calibrated analyses estimate a single oceanic colonization between 6.9 and 4.4 million years ago; this is a model-based interval, not an observed arrival date. The 2008 expedition visited 14 islands and islets in 15 days. The itinerary is not evidence that every island or islet population was sampled.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-29de32fef5}
This guide covers only this batch’s entries with one or more sourced focal traits, each interpreted within its own scope, and matching COL name and family. It is not an exhaustive descendant list. Maximum sizes are reported compiled records, not individual averages or identification thresholds. Turtle carapace length and snout–vent length in other groups are different measures. Consult sources and aggregation rules by field; historical one-row records do not resolve every taxonomic revision or population difference.

## Three broad forms in Pseudalsophis {#card}
A study examined 322 morphological specimens and a separate DNA dataset of 34 Pseudalsophis individuals, including mainland Peru and Galápagos samples. It described three broad morphotypes, with some overlap in measurements within clusters. These are study-level groupings from the sampled material, not a claim that every population fits a sharply separate form.
