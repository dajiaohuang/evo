## Spines and a thick fruit wall {#s-a03cc2c671}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records tendrils in a single pair and spines present. Leaves have petioles about 5 mm long and three veins; the underside vein axils are sometimes bearded. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are corymbiform. The corolla is rotate or short-funnel-shaped, with lobes longer than the tube; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 2–4 cm in diameter, with a woody fruit wall 4–6 mm thick. Seeds are usually three to five and have a fibrous coat.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-98532b7d98}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field lists *Strychnos cerradoensis* as terrestrial but supplies no collection locality.
