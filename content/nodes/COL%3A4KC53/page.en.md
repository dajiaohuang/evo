## What leaf and flower measurements are recorded? {#s-ff31b453e3}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes blunt-tipped leaves 0.7–1.5 mm wide, with flat margins. The flower tube is up to 7 mm long; its dorsal petals (those on the upper side of the flower) are lilac to white and up to 2 mm wide. Epicalyx segments, the extra sepal-like ring below the calyx, are present. The lower chamber of the ovary is sterile, meaning it contains no functioning ovules in this record. This regional field gives no broader sampling or variation information.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f42f1f3e1a}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and supplies no locality or collection date.
