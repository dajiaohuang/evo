## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-b90bc63117}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 7046.7 g; mass at birth or hatching 357.9 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-8a7116687f}
Foraging-stratum weights flagged as species-level in the archive are on or just below the water surface 100%. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.
