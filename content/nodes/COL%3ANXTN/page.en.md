## Inflated magenta berries in wet mountain forests {#s-2fbe1f5933}
This account describes a slender, smooth, epiphytic herb with hollow stems up to 70 cm long. Its lance-shaped leaves taper to long, often curved tips. Solitary flowers hang from the upper leaf axils on long stalks; the corolla may be greenish, purple or russet-red. The inflated magenta berries reach several centimetres in length. The species is recorded from wet montane forests in Chiriquí Province, Panama.
