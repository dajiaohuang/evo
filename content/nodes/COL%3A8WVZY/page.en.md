## Leaves, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-e787b13f4f}
The field records an underground bulb and pseudopetiolate leaves. Their blades are linear, taper toward the base, and have acute tips; blade ornamentation is marked unknown. The scape is recorded as entire and solid, with a fused, spathe-like bract.

The flowers are recorded as unscented, with an unspotted, funnel-shaped perianth described as lilac with a white base. The hypanthium tube falls in the source’s “short” category and measures 0.1–1.0 cm long. The field lists six stamens; the filaments are equal to or slightly shorter than the sepals and petals. Anthers are grey, and placentation is basal. Fruit shape is marked unknown.
