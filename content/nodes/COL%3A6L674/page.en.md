## What does the record say about Guatteria pastazae? {#s-638d0385c3}
The field describes narrow oblong-elliptic leaves with a warty surface, smooth above and sparsely hairy below. Flowers grow in leaf axils, singly or in larger clusters; their stalks vary from sparsely to densely hairy. The sepals are free or joined at the base, and the petals vary from elliptic to obovate or oblong-elliptic. The ellipsoid fruit segments contain seeds that may be pitted or grooved across the surface.
