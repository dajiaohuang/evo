## Campuloclinium hirsutum {#s-5a8ec45e33}
## Pink flower heads on hairy, striated stems

The Brazilian Flora record describes woody, striated stems with long stiff hairs and glandular hairs; the xylopodium status is unknown. Opposite leaves become smaller and sometimes nearly opposite near the stem tips. Their ovate, papery blades have acute tips, toothed margins and sparse multicellular and glandular hairs. The panicle-like or corymb-like heads contain 35–47 pink florets. The elongated, five-ribbed cypselae are hairy over the surface and bear a single row of equal barbellate pappus bristles.
