## How the Scelidosaurus lectotype constrains its armour {#s-76e853b3d9}
The lectotype—the specimen designated as the name-bearing reference—NHMUK R1111 preserves the skull, jaws, much of the skeleton and associated osteoderms, or bony plates in the skin. Where plates remain associated with the bones, they constrain armour arrangement; missing pieces and preparation history leave gaps. A keratinous covering and the proposed stem-ankylosaur placement are interpretations rather than direct observations.
