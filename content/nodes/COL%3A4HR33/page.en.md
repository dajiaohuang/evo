## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-f63fa56fca}
The archive summarizes adult body mass as 137 g. It may combine male, female and sex-unspecified records; this is a compiled value, not a range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-bd5c5146f7}
Reported reproductive summaries are 7.5 eggs or offspring per clutch or litter, 15.5 days from laying to hatching, and 25 days to flight capability. Some source material defines the last as leaving the nest. The fractional clutch value summarizes records rather than describing a single brood.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-71c156db39}
Maximum longevity is 7.7 years, the largest record found for that field. It is not typical lifespan, an average for wild birds or a survival limit for every individual.
