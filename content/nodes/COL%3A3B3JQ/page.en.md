## *Eriocephalus punctulatus*: flower heads on slender stalks {#s-3929d4601a}
The regional account compares this species with *Eriocephalus brevifolius*: its stated distinction is that the flower heads sit on longer, threadlike stalks. The account records sandstone slopes as its habitat. It supplies no further description here, so those are the specific traits documented in this dossier.
