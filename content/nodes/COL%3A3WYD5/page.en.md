## ## Axillary panicles and samaras {#s-1aca99b778}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes unarmed, cylindrical branches and leaves with leaf-like stipules. The leaflets are elliptic, lance-shaped or oblong-lanceolate, with acute or tapered tips and rounded bases. Their upper faces are hairless; the lower faces become hairless or retain hairs only along the main vein. The recorded venation is brochidodromous, with secondary veins looping near the margin. Panicles occur in leaf axils or at shoot tips and bear leaf-like bracts. The flowers have a bell-shaped calyx, white to cream petals and a hairy standard; the stamens are recorded as united in one bundle. The fruit is a samara, with a warty seed-bearing region. This is a regional flora description, not a survey of species-wide variation.
