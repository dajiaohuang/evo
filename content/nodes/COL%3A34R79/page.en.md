## Dendropanax praestans {#s-dc5a40477b}
## Juvenile and mature leaves take different forms

Young leaves are nearly round, up to 40 cm across, and deeply divided into five to seven toothed lobes. Mature leaves may be entire or deeply three-lobed, with irregular, blunt teeth. The inflorescence has five to ten umbels grouped in an axillary compound umbel. The distribution entry lists Costa Rica and western Panama at 1,700–2,300 m elevation.
