## Pink flowers on a low succulent shrub {#s-1236aafed2}
This account describes a decumbent succulent shrub with branches up to 20 cm long. Its subcylindrical leaves are densely covered with rounded papillae. Pink flowers grow singly on short, upright side shoots and are 22–27 mm across; filament-like staminodes are absent. The five-chambered fruit has a placental tip ending in two tiny white knobs. Fine-grained soils are listed as habitat.
