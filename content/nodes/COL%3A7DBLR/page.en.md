## Comparative results retain their measurement limits {#s-0c5dff316d}
The paper treats shanjing and verrucosus as sister taxa and reports pairwise morphological differences, especially in lateral skull view, contrasting with an earlier analysis finding no morphometric difference. Low genetic divergence and disputed status are discussed without resolving synonymy through this dataset alone. Pulcherrimus had no study data. Older captive observations labelled verrucosus might involve shanjing, so those uncertain identities cannot automatically certify reproductive traits of either pinned species.
