## Drosanthemum calycinum {#s-e288d77128}
## Hair-like bladder cells rise from the stems

The Greater Cape flora describes a sprawling, papillate shrublet up to 50 cm tall. Its cylindrical, blunt-tipped leaves have shiny papillae. White flowers grow in cymes. Filament-like sterile stamens gather in a cone and later curve backward. The fruit has five chambers and a false partition. The habitat record is stony slopes.
