## Two matrices, two early dinosaur trees {#s-760adbfa24}
A 74-taxon, 457-character analysis grouped theropods with ornithischians as Ornithoscelida. A published reanalysis that rescored characters and added taxa recovered the traditional ornithischian–saurischian split, showing how the result depends on matrix construction.

## What the first matrix recovered {#s-f0bb559836}
The 2017 analysis coded 457 morphological characters for 74 taxa and recovered Theropoda together with Ornithischia as Ornithoscelida. This was a branching hypothesis inferred from the chosen fossil sample and character scores. It was not a direct observation of which dinosaur gave rise to another.

## Why a second analysis matters {#s-80cc4a234b}
A reanalysis rescored characters and added taxa, then recovered the traditional split between Ornithischia and Saurischia. These are competing results from explicit matrices, and coding, outgroups and taxon sampling affect the short deep branches. The atlas interval of 233–228 Ma is a display context for early dinosaur fossils, not an age dated by either tree.
