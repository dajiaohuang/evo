## A living-taxon matrix recovers Aculifera {#s-5daaadacea}
Transcriptome and genome matrices recover Polyplacophora together with Aplacophora as Aculifera in the sampled analysis. The result describes how these living lineages group under the included data and methods.

## The placement depends on the sample {#s-081f4ab2ef}
Monoplacophora is omitted, and alternative matrices can produce different relationships. This is a study-specific topology for sampled living taxa, not a settled full molluscan tree.
