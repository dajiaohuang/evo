## A brittle submerged plant {#s-42bc4b981a}
The regional account describes a submerged annual herb that is brittle and can form dense mats resembling fine grass. Its thin, erect stems reach 500 mm; leaves are arranged in threes or opposite pairs. The inflorescence is umbel-like, and the flowers are green.
