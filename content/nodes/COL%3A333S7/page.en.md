## Cream flowers above persistent dark old leaves {#s-72bccb3cfe}
The record describes a succulent shrub up to 10 cm tall and 20 cm across. Its cylindrical leaves are 6–7.5 cm long; old leaves turn black and remain on the stems. Solitary cream flowers may reach 6.5 cm across, with petals that merge into filament-like staminodes. The fruit has five chambers. Shale slopes are listed as habitat.
