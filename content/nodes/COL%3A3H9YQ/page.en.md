## Grey-woolly leaf undersides beneath flower clusters {#s-f858298279}
The record describes stalked, elliptic leaves with toothed edges and contrasting surfaces: the underside is grey-woolly. Branched clusters carry radiate heads with ten involucral bracts, 5–8 ray florets and 15–28 disk florets. The cylindrical fruits are smooth and retain their pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
