## Persistent leaves with skeleton-like veins {#s-6a511daa5f}
The record describes a prostrate to sprawling perennial with broad, flat leaves whose dry remains persist as a vein-like skeleton. The leaf tips curve backward, and the surface bladder cells are small. Stalked yellow flowers are about 40 mm across; thread-like staminodes hide the reproductive parts. The five-chambered fruits have valve wings. Stony slopes are recorded as habitat.
