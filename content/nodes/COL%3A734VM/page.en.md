## Malouetia calva {#s-6389895885}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Malouetia calva* as an accepted species of *Malouetia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records its habitat category as terrestrial. The field does not name a collection site, give a range, or describe morphology, so the available evidence supports only this regional habitat label.
