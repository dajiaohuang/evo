## Praxelis asperulacea {#s-74cd747c79}
## Three-part head clusters and a bristly pappus

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Upright, cylindrical stems are grooved, hairy and branch in pairs. Opposite, linear leaves are thin, sessile, serrated and mostly smooth; each has one main vein. Heads occur in three-part cymes on short, hairy stalks. Their cylindrical involucres have overlapping, deciduous bracts and a smooth, conical receptacle.

The lilac florets are bisexual, with five fused anthers and two-lobed styles. The ribbed dry fruits (cypselae) have an asymmetric attachment area, paired hairs along two to four ribs, and a single row of barbed pappus bristles with pointed tips. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; it supplies no locality or complete range.
