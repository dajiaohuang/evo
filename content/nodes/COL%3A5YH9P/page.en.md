## Chromolaena revoluta {#s-866e2a8c64}
## Revolute bracts and variable head stalks

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes a xylopodium, a woody underground structure. Stems are glandular and range from finely hairy to stiffly hairy. Leaves may be opposite or alternate, lance-shaped to elliptic, toothed or serrated, and have three main veins. The upper surface is smooth; the lower is gland-dotted and may be nearly smooth or stiffly hairy.

Corymb-like clusters have cylindrical heads, either stalkless or stalked. The outer bracts are smooth or glandular and stand upright or curve backward; the inner bracts stand upright and are smooth. Corolla lobes are smooth or finely hairy. The dry fruits (cypselae) are obconical, smooth or bearing short bristles, with rough pappus bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or complete range is given.
