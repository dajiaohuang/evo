## Pouteria amapaensis Pires & T.D.Penn. {#s-d0a7fbd36d}
## Axillary clusters and four-chambered flowers

Pouteria amapaensis has young branchlets with soft hairs and no lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have a cylindrical petiole and eucamptodromous venation, in which the side veins fade before reaching the margin. Axillary clusters, sometimes borne on branches, carry flowers with four sepals and a four-lobed tubular corolla. The rounded corolla lobes have fringed edges; the filaments are smooth, and the ovary has four chambers. Fruit and seed characters are recorded as unknown.
