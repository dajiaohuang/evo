## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-93031f7239}
The regional flora field notes short internodes (stem sections between nodes) and papery leaf blades, with cordate, sagittate, or nearly heart-shaped bases; the color is recorded as variegated or uniform. The leaf stalks (petioles) may bear sparse or dense prickles. The inflorescence tip is described as curved and reddish to brown, and the floral field records five locules (internal chambers). These are listed descriptors, not a quantified survey of variation.
