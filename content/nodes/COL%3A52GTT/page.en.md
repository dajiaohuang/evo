## Stevia decussata {#s-cbb86846b0}
## Sessile leaves and an awned pappus

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes opposite leaves without petioles. Dense, stalked heads form corymb-like clusters in the leaf axils. The dry fruits are bristly, and the pappus consists of scales with awn-like tips. A separate habitat field says terrestrial; the regional entry gives no locality or complete range.
