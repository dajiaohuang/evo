## Elegia ebracteata {#s-4fb85277e5}
## One or two female flowers per spikelet

The regional description gives Elegia ebracteata a tufted or rhizomatous perennial habit and a height of up to 80 cm. Its sheaths fall away, and each female spikelet bears one or two flowers measuring 3–4 mm. Sandstone slopes are the documented habitat.
