## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-41ce09f3f9}
COL26.8 records Macrovipera razii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-efff00a65d}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 7.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Carey and Judge 2000, Slavens and Slavens 1999, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
