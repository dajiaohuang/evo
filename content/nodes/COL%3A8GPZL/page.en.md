## Egg laying and a compiled mean {#s-efff00a65d}
ReptTraits labels the reproductive mode “oviparous” and reports 7.5 for its combined field “offspring per litter or eggs per clutch.” That field joins two kinds of counts, and 7.5 is a compiled summary, not a count for every clutch. Under the dataset protocol, a summary may use midpoints of reported means or, when means are absent, extrema; it is not necessarily the arithmetic mean of clutches measured in one population.
