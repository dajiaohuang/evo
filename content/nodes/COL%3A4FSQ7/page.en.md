## Overview {#s-20727ceacc}
Across four survey periods in 2017–2022, Schüßler et al. covered 109 transects totalling 303.73 km at 22 unprotected northeastern Madagascan sites, with no visual or call detections. Interviews in 22 villages involved 243 people; only four reported rare sightings. The authors discuss a possible disjunct distribution, but these surveys do not prove absence across northeastern Madagascar or the island.

## Research observations {#s-bb3720e005}
The central result is a bounded non-detection: it describes particular sites, years and methods. Interview reports are a separate evidence type from direct sightings or call detections.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-1862764b32}
The archive’s nonzero food weights are nectar, pollen and plant exudates 100%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. The northeastern surveys yielded no direct detections and few interview reports. Results apply only to the surveyed sites, periods and methods; they do not establish extinction or the full range.
