## *Hymenolepis gnidioides*: a woolly shrub with yellow heads {#s-7c34146941}
The regional account describes a slender, white-woolly shrub, reported as 1–3 m tall, with oblong leaves that are warty beneath. Its yellow flower heads have silky bracts, the small modified leaves around a head. Another diagnostic passage describes crowded, flat-topped head clusters with narrow, leaflike bracts, and fruit with smooth ribs and a prominent pappus, the bristly crown that remains on the fruit. The account records sandstone slopes and ridges as habitat.
