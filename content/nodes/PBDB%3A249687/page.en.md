## What does the age interval mean? {#s-1e748be67f}
The selected records have an outer age interval of 168.2–152.21 Ma; Ma means millions of years ago. This span combines the oldest early bound with the youngest late bound among the chosen records. It does not show continuous presence through every intervening time or directly date the species origin, extinction or an individual lifespan. Each collection keeps its own bracket; this summary does not narrow it to a specimen age or revise the source dating interpretation.
