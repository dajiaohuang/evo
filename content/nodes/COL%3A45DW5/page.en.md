## Myrsine laetevirens (Mez) Arechav. {#s-b70fb9800c}
## Spatulate leaves and short racemes

Myrsine laetevirens has slender, smooth terminal branches. Its alternate, papery leaves are spaced along the stems and carried on short petioles. The blades are spatulate, with rounded tips and acute bases; short lines of resin cavities occur beneath. Short racemes carry five-parted flowers, followed by globose drupes on short stalks.
