## ## A xylopodium and a few-flowered raceme {#s-5fc87a4293}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium, a woody underground organ, and 1–3 pairs of leaflets on alternate leaves arranged in two ranks. The oblong to obovate leaflets are 26–45 mm long and 2–14 mm wide, with oblique bases and mucronate tips. Axillary racemes bear only a few flowers or sometimes one. The yellow flowers have ten stamens. The brown, pubescent pod is 6–6.7 cm long and contains 6–7 wine-red, trapezoidal seeds. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
