The Upper Member of the Rhaetian Formation outcropping at Pégairolles was first dated as Rhaetian by Dieulafait (1868) on the basis of the presence of the bivalve species Rhaetavicula contorta (Portlock 1843). Lopez (1992) confirmed this age based on the stratigraphy of the layers above and below the formation. The top of the Upper Member is covered by the Hettangian “faciès de Parlatges”, which yielded the typical lower Hettangian ammonite species Psiloceras planorbis (Sowerby 1825). The Rhaetian Formation is bounded at its base by marine transitional facies that Lopez (1992) assigned to the Norian. The Rhaetian age was subsequently confirmed for the Lower Member of the Rhaetian Formation on the basis of palynomorphs assemblages densely sampled along the whole sequence (Courtinat and Piriou 2002).
