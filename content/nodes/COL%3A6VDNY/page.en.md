## Species Index and site index have different meanings {#s-0c297753eb}
SI is the sum DBS+TBS+SBS for one species. Site RABI sums the SI of every recorded species, without dividing by species richness. Consequently, SI 10 for Phrynobatrachus scheffleri is not the RABI of a site containing it, nor a probability of extinction or an effect size. Successive input categories use doubled weights: DBS 1/2/4/8, TBS 0/1/2/4/8 and SBS 0/1/2/4. Those weights express the authors' prioritization design; they are not evidence that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The positive correlations reported among sub-indices also mean that their inputs are not empirically independent in this sample.
