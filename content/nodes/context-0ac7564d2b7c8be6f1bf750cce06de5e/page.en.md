## The source concept {#s-1ae226187f}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria booroolongensis as row Anf2776 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-2af70fc47c}
The row lists terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal foraging. These categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. Its row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-66941c69ce}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 45 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-a2ec32918b}
The row gives 1,236 offspring or eggs per clutch and up to one reproductive event per year. These literature-derived counts do not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-e149c2c236}
The record indicates a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or reproductive timing.
