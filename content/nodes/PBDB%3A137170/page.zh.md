Several lines of evidence indicate that the Bermont Fm was deposited during the middle Pleistocene, most likely between 1.6 and 1.0 Ma (summarized by Morgan and Hulbert 1995). The majority of direct chronological constraints on the Bermont Fm derive from exposures at the Leisey Shell Pits in Hillsborough County, Florida, which includes marine sand and shell deposits containing Bermont index gastropods (Portell et al. 1995). One such Bermont-equivalent marine unit at Leisey is bounded above and below by beds with dense vertebrate-fossil assemblages. Hulbert and Morgan (1989) apply vertebrate biochronology to these bounding beds to bracket the age of the Bermont Fm gastropods to between 1.7 and 1.0 Ma. Webb et al.
