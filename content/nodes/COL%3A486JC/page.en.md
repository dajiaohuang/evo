## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-74ff757d2e}
Cariticommatus is described in the comparison as having a snout rounded dorsally and bluntly rounded laterally, green dorsal coloration with small pale flecks, and a translucent stomach peritoneum. Dajomesae is diagnosed with a truncate snout, unmarked green dorsum and white stomach peritoneum. The new species’ dorsal-snout diagnosis differs from its own holotype text and is not silently corrected.
