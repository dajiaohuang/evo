## Record identity and classification {#s-1e35498672}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6646 records Gegeneophis madhavai under Gegeneophis, Caeciliidae, and Gymnophiona, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-246434cd0d}
This AmphiBIO row marks fossorial; terrestrial; aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Developmental category {#s-b28e242b16}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists direct development as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
