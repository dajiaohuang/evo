## Cynodont branches in a skeletal-character matrix {#s-7d8103a46a}
An enlarged skeletal-character analysis samples all main Late Permian–Early Jurassic cynodont clades and recovers basal cynodonts and epicynodonts as successive grades relative to Eucynodontia; the topology is a matrix hypothesis, not observed ancestry.

## Temporal coverage of the cynodont matrix {#s-9650e92265}
Cynodontia is displayed at 259.5–174.7 Ma only for this matrix sample’s temporal coverage, not as a continuous global range or direct-ancestor chain.
