## Philodendron fragrantissimum: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-b47df25159}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists clasping or feeding roots and flagelliform or erect stems, with a persistent fibrous prophyll. The conspicuous leaf sheath surrounds a green petiole that is flattened and grooved above, rounded below, with erect, winged margins. Leaf blades are cordate or sagittate, with acuminate tips and entire margins. Primary lateral veins are recorded on both surfaces, with 6–9 listed; there is 1 acroscopic vein and no basioscopic vein.

The inflorescence count per sympodium is given as “2/5,” without clarifying whether it represents alternatives or a range. The short, cylindrical green peduncle is shorter than the spathe. The constricted spathe has a cream or carmine outer tube, a whitish outer blade and a cream inner blade. The spadix is subsessile and includes female and fertile-male zones; the fertile-male zone is cylindrical.

The entry lists 3 or 5 stamens, short staminodes, a subcylindrical gynoecium and ovary, 7 or 9 locules, and 50 or 91 hemianatropous ovules; placentation is axial and the stigma discoid. The berry is cylindrical or globose and reddish; seeds are subglobose.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The “2/5” value and alternative counts remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
