## Begonia angularis {#s-0a8719f691}
## Strongly asymmetric leaves with toothed edges

Begonia angularis has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its basal leaves are strongly asymmetric, transversely elliptic or ovate, with margins that may be serrate, toothed, wavy or crenulate. The upper surface is hairless; simple hairs occur beneath. Petioles may be hairless or glandular-hairy. Female flower segments have crenulate margins and bracteoles are present. The capsule wings are strongly unequal; the seed tip is blunt.
