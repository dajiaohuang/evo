## Euterpe edulis {#s-be3a3e9cd6}
The regional description gives Euterpe edulis a 5–12 m stem, 8–15 leaves and 65–72 leaflets arranged in one plane along the leaf axis. Its inflorescence bears 49–110 branches; globose fruits are 1–1.4 cm across, with homogeneous endosperm. The dataset records terrestrial habitat only.
