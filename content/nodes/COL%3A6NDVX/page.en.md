## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Jatropha palmatipartita? {#s-08a02950ac}
The green stem is hairy and has petiole scars and clear latex. Long-stalked leaves are glabrous and divided into five or seven lobes, with entire margins. The inflorescences are capitate cymes at shoot tips. The tubular corolla is red or purple with yellow tips. Male flowers have six stamens joined into one bundle and arranged in one row; female flowers have a hairless ovary and two styles. The fruit is a capsule.
