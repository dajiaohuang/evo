## A reported distance to velocicantus {#s-ea7642f6b6}
The2020 study’s Table2 reports 10.8% uncorrected p-distance and 11.8% Kimura2-parameter distance between the Allobates masniger label and velocicantus, based on a678-bp16S fragment. These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. ## Male throat colour in a diagnostic comparison {#s-2c47ec6ad9}
The2020 comparison describes male masniger throats as dark gray, in contrast to the white centre/yellow edges reported for velocicantus. Sex and comparison scope matter: this paragraph does not establish female throat colour, a complete preservation-dependent colour series or population-wide variation. Original descriptions cited by the paper remain separately unreviewed.

## A single-note arrangement in the reviewed comparison {#s-5d7986fe37}
The2020 description summarizes masniger calls as a single note emitted continuously or irregularly, contrasting them with velocicantus trills of66–138 pulsed notes. This information is attributed to prior acoustic studies cited there; their original recordings, individual counts and temperature conditions have not been reread here. “Single note” describes the reported call unit, not inability to repeat notes or produce other vocalizations.
