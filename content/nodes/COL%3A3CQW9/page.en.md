## *Euphorbia susannae* {#s-c7c2c20b1d}
Compared with *Euphorbia pseudoglobosa*, this account describes a mostly subterranean plant whose branch tips reach the surface. Branches are nearly spherical, sometimes flattened at the tip, with 12–16 angles and spreading, narrowly triangular tubercles. The account presents a comparison, not a complete description.

The source records gentle slopes in karroid scrub, often among quartz patches. It does not claim this is the complete habitat range.
