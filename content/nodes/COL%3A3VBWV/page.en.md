## What form do the leaves and flower clusters take? {#s-00ad41bbf9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records hairy, glandular stems and stalked, papery leaves. The elliptic to egg-shaped blades have rough upper surfaces and hairy, glandular undersides. Flower clusters are cylindrical or nearly spike-shaped, with spirally arranged papery bracts. The bisexual flowers have a tubular calyx and a salver-shaped corolla, white, pink, or lilac.
