## Ternstroemia pungens Gleason {#s-d95bce0ca2}
## Leaves crowded near branch tips

Ternstroemia pungens has alternate or spiral leaves that cluster near branch tips and may fall from lower stem sections. The leaves are leathery, usually hairless, and may have a rounded, blunt or notched tip. Axillary inflorescences consist of a single flower. The source gives several possible forms for the flower parts and describes the smooth, nearly spherical fruit as either indehiscent or opening by valves. The seeds are elliptic and curved at the tip. These alternatives are retained without choosing one.
