## ## A long-tapering leaf and elastically opening pod {#s-e0def0df82}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes unarmed branches and undivided, somewhat leathery leaves whose tips taper to a long point. The upper leaf surface is recorded as nearly hairless or hairless; the lower surface may also become nearly hairless, with a raised vein along the margin. The row lists ten anthers and a legume that opens elastically. These are regional description fields, and their alternative states do not establish the species’ full variation.
