## What leaf and flower details are recorded for Croton glutinosus? {#s-cc503b00bd}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. The long-stalked oval leaves are hairless, with looping side veins, scalloped or toothed margins, and nectar glands along the edge; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are stalked; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate, the style may be two- or many-lobed, and the calyx lobes are alike. Female petals are absent, and the bud arrangement is valvate. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
