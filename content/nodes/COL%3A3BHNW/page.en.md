## Esenbeckia hieronymi {#s-fb72c7782e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records opposite three-leaflet leaves and a nearly spherical, muricate-to-spiny fruit.

## Three leaflets and an ornamented fruit {#s-40fca0d4e7}
Leaves are compound with three opposite leaflets on an unwinged petiole. Leaflet bases are attenuate, tips acuminate, and the lower surface pubescent. Inflorescences are terminal. Sepals are deltoid and pubescent on both surfaces; petals are cream or white, chartaceous and pubescent below. Basal filament appendages are absent, and the disc and ovary are glabrous. The fruit is subglobose, has subapical apophyses and a surface ranging from muricate to echinate. The ecology category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a collection locality or complete range.
