## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Heteropterys banksiifolia? {#s-dad5c151ad}
Leafy branches bear silky hairs. Opposite leaves are leathery and lance-shaped, with blunt or rounded bases and pointed tips. The lower surface has silky hairs; dark, stalkless marginal glands make the edge toothed. Petioles may have glands at the tip. Upright inflorescences have persistent elliptic bracts without glands and stalked pedicels. Sepals have flat tips, rusty hairs and glands that do not run down the surface; the front sepal alone may lack glands. Yellow, clawed petals have entire edges, and the anthers are hairless. The front styles are shorter than the rear ones and end in a flattened tip. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-9549c3b99e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
