## Which traits are recorded for this species? {#s-45e9f86072}
The dataset lists no buttress roots or xylopodium, and a stem no more than 10 m long. The evergreen leaves have up to five hairless leaflets. Flowers are up to 5 cm long with oblong buds and a cup-shaped calyx covered in rusty wool; its edge is lobed or tipped. A nectary is present on the receptacle. The obovate petals are not rolled. The cylindrical staminal tube is constricted and up to 1.5 cm long, with 45–170 stamens. The fruit contains abundant brown, silky fibers.
