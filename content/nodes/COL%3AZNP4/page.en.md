## A black cylindrical pod {#s-e5c628e459}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect plant up to 1.5 m tall, with three leaflets per leaf and long leaf stalks. Dense flower clusters grow at the branch tips; their oval-lanceolate bracts persist. Yellow flowers are followed by a cylindrical black pod with short hairs. The seeds are yellowish, and the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
