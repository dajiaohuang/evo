## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Euphorbia tettensis? {#s-e2dcb9abc9}
This hairy herb may grow upright or along the ground, reaching 300 mm. Its conspicuously opposite, ovate leaves are up to 15 × 9 mm. Small flower units grow in leaf axils, and the flowers are white.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-570469d798}
The source lists bushveld.
