## Temperature studies represented in AmphiTherm {#s-1efb4a3d32}
The pinned AmphiTherm dataset uses a 2018 source classification. In that scheme, Odontophrynus matches 3 deposited species concepts and 23 estimate rows: CTmax, 11 rows across 3 concepts; CTmin, 5 across 1; and Tpref, 7 across 1. CTmax and CTmin are critical temperature-limit measures; LT50_hot and LT50_cold label hot and cold lethal-limit assays; Tpref records thermal preference. These measures come from different experiments and cannot be combined into one tolerance range. Counts overlap and do not represent distinct species or animals. Source life-stage codes are larvae and adults. The rows show study coverage under that classification, not the physiology of every currently accepted member of the genus.
