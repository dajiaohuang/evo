## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-72ca1ca7aa}
COL26.8 records Crotalus campbelli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-da324a3ffe}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 569 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-f7cd96ffc2}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021.
