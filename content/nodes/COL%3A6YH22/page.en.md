## Stipules, nectaries and late-dehiscent fruit {#s-d522319636}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records stems as pubescent or strigulose. Stipules are elliptic, ovate or falcate; the petiole and rachis are each under 5 cm. Sessile or stalked, fusiform or ovate extrafloral nectaries occur between all leaflet pairs. Leaves have two pairs of elliptic, ovate or lanceolate leaflets, with tips listed as acuminate, obtuse or cuspidate. The raceme has elliptic, ovate or lanceolate bracts. Flowers are zygomorphic, with an obovate central adaxial petal and seven fertile stamens. The legume is late dehiscent and pendulous, subcylindrical and externally depressed between seeds; seeds are arranged in two series. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
