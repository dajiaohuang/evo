## A cushion of leaves with raised flowering branches {#s-5a0d10d450}
This description records a densely leafy, cushion-shaped succulent shrub up to 10 cm tall. Its compressed, sickle-shaped leaves are slightly rough, and the flowering branches rise above the shrub-like foliage. One to three flowers form terminal clusters; the petals are yellow. The five-chambered fruits lack closing bodies. It grows on flats with broken shale.
