## Overview {#s-3b904fcd77}
Printes et al. conducted five surveys in northeastern Brazil in 2004–2005, totalling 130 field days across Bahia, Sergipe and Alagoas. They surveyed forest fragments, interviewed residents and played calls, reporting 37 new localities, mainly in dense arboreal Caatinga and highland coastal rainforest at 241–908 m.

## Research observations {#s-4d7d1d4a2d}
A later study repeated playback counts at 34 sites over nine consecutive days in a 221-km² subregion and used N-mixture models to account for imperfect detection. It estimated 91 groups there, or about 273 adults when assuming three adults per group, with a local density near 2.3 animals/km². This applies to the study area and group-size assumption, not the full range.
