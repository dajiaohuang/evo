## Varronia leucomalloides {#s-6a914b3eed}
## Dense leaf hairs frame small flower heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stems with long, coarse hairs. Petiolate, ovate-elliptic leaves have acute bases and acute to blunt tips. The upper surface is densely woolly; the underside has long, coarse or loose, tufted hairs.

Short-stiff hairs cover the flower clusters, which are head-shaped and arise at the stem tip or in leaf axils. The calyx is bell-shaped with long-pointed lobes; the bell-shaped corolla is under 1 cm long. The fruit is globose and hairless. The habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
