## Triangular leaves on a twining stem {#s-e5f9088689}
The morphology record describes a twining, shortly hairy stem with a solid center. Its opposite, triangular leaves have toothed edges and a heart-shaped base, with three main veins arising near the base. The branched flowering clusters bear small heads; the corolla lobes are shorter than the tube. The habitat field classifies it as terrestrial.
