## Moquiniastrum sordidum {#s-91285defbc}
## Glossy upper leaf faces contrast with silky undersides

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes pubescent stems with Malpighian-type hairs. Alternate, stalked leaves have papery to leathery blades, elliptic to lance-shaped, with pointed tips and tapered bases. The upper face is glossy and hairless; the lower is silky, with conspicuous veins and Malpighian-type hairs.

Flower heads form corymb-like clusters at stem tips or in leaf axils; the axillary clusters are shorter than the leaves. Bell-shaped involucres have 3–4 series of ovate, pointed, silky bracts. The source lists the heads as homogamous or heterogamous and the florets as bisexual or female. Cream to white tubular florets number 8–12. The cylindrical fruits have five ribs and a silky covering; their straw-coloured pappus has unequal, rough to barbed bristles in two or three rows.
