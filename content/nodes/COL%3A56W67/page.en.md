## Name and AVONET sample {#s-50f36bde6b}
COL26.8 accepts Tigrisoma lineatum within Ardeidae. AVONET v7 associates the data with its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Seven individuals were measured, with 4 complete morphological sets; usable sample counts can vary by trait.

## Beak dimensions {#s-3ed31cc3d6}
Mean beak length is 81.8 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 12.4 mm and depth 20.4 mm at that edge. These are standardized, separate measurements, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-d6946093d7}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 306.7 mm; mean tarsus length is 98.8 mm. AVONET’s compiled mean mass is 812.99 g, sourced to Dunning. This mass is separate species-average information, not necessarily a measurement from these specimens.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-20f57b1b05}
AVONET records Wetland habitat, an Aquatic predator trophic niche and a Terrestrial primary lifestyle. These are broad comparison categories, not a complete description of habitat or diet.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-c9cd388e1c}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude −36.04° to 15.16°. These endpoints do not describe all non-breeding movements, current position or occupancy throughout the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

The source-defined female maturity field (age at first conception) is 1090.4 d; litter or clutch size is 2 offspring or eggs; and oviposition-to-hatching time is 32.5 d. Here, d means days. These are compilation summaries for different events, not a single reproductive-cycle measure.

## Limits across these historical sources {#s-cd99c5d0d0}
This page combines AVONET v7, the August 2015 life-history archive and EltonTraits 1.0; it is not a complete species monograph. AVONET morphology values are sample means and its mass has separate provenance; its ecological categories are broad and its maps are historical. The 2015 archive reports compiled summaries, not measurements from one individual, and may retain data inherited across historical taxa. Source conditions are not uniformly known and underlying papers were not individually reviewed. EltonTraits weights are literature-derived estimates, not direct measurements. Same-name and same-family matching supports association, not equivalence to COL26.8. Current ecology, range, population and conservation need other sources.
