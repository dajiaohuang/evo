## A single copper-red flower above creeping stems {#s-f3b3385647}
The Latin description records a low, spreading succulent with creeping or reclining branches up to 30 cm long. Its green, keeled leaves spread or curve backward; their margins may have small hairs. A single flower sits on a stalk up to 1.2 cm long, with small leaf-like bracts near its base. The petals are copper-red above and reddish purple below. The species is recorded among shrubs in several named coastal and sub-escarpment vegetation types.
