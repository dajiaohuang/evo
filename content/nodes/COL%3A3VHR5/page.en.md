## Accepted name and classification {#s-7bdb00ca56}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Lithobates vaillanti* (Brocchi, 1877) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Lithobates (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.
