## Leaf surfaces and flowers {#s-190b9764a5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes leaves whose lower surface is hairy and upper surface smooth, with a grooved midrib on the upper side. The listed leaflet count ranges from two to five pairs. Its flowers have white petals; the row lists reddish-brown hairs on the inflorescence and calyx, and a hairy ovary. Fruit layers are described as woody or granular, with one or two seeds; several fruit and vein traits have alternative states in the source.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-d5aa8ef2e0}
This Brazilian Flora 2020 entry labels Andira cujabensis terrícola, or terrestrial. It supplies a habitat or substrate category, but no collection locality or complete distribution.
