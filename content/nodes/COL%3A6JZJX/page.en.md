## Gaultheria gracilis Small {#s-b47953ea37}
## Urn-shaped flowers on a small epiphytic shrub

Gaultheria gracilis is a 0.4–1 m shrub that may grow on other plants or on the ground. Its leathery leaves are elliptic to lance-shaped, finely toothed and bristly, especially beneath. Racemes rise from the upper leaf axils, with persistent, papery bracts. The flowers are urn- to cone-shaped and range from greenish white to deep rose; each has ten stamens whose anthers end in two short, outward-bent awns. The fruit is a five-celled capsule, 2.5–4 mm high. The species is reported from Costa Rica and western Panama.
