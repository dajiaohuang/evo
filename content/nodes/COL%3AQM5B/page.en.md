## What is recorded about its red flowers and rhizome? {#s-dbf53ca6cf}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes *Canna paniculata* with a long, creeping rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) and an unbranched inflorescence (the whole flower cluster). Its flowers are larger than 4 cm and have red petals. One staminode, a sterile stamen-like flower part, is recorded. The entry does not give a dated sampling frame or population variation.
