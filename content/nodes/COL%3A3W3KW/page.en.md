## Lopholaena dregeana {#s-7dd873d51d}
## Abundant pappus with a yellow base and snow-white tip

De Candolle's 1837 Latin description gives the leaves as 0.5–3 *poll.* long and 5–10 *lin.* wide; these historical unit abbreviations are retained as printed. It describes the pappus as abundant, yellowish at the base and snow-white at the tip. A 2019 Eastern Cape flora records colonies in coastal grassland within the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt.
