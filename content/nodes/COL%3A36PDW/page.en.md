## What leaf, flower and vein-axil details are recorded? {#s-95d5729e5e}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes hairy leaves that do not blacken when dry. Leaves are 0.5–4 mm wide and sessile or on petioles up to 2.5 mm long; their bases are blunt and margins rolled under. Several main veins converge toward the tip, and at least two lateral veins occur on each side; small hair tufts are present in some vein axils. A small bract below the flower is shorter than, or reaches halfway up, the floral tube. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. Epicalyx segments—the extra sepal-like ring below the calyx—are 0–1.5 mm long, shorter than the sepals and spreading outward. The corolla is listed as 1–3 cm or 3.5–7 cm across; there are 18 stamens. The regional row gives alternatives without a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-94fda62f86}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; it gives no locality or collection date.
