## The name in COL26.8 {#s-8ce19b1699}
COL26.8 lists *Craugastor amniscola* (Campbell & Savage, 2000) as an accepted species in its 20 August 2026 release. The recorded path is Anura (order) → Craugastoridae (family) → Craugastor (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## How to read this profile {#s-7fb6ec24fb}
This page combines the accepted name with two separate kinds of source record: historical AmphiBIO trait categories and a later morphology table. They describe what those datasets contain; neither by itself supplies a complete account of current populations.

## Two historical foraging categories {#s-7ca6f4b6b0}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1233 records Ter=1 (terrestrial) and Aqu=1 (aquatic) as broad foraging categories under the compilation's Frost 2011 species concept. The row does not distinguish season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat categories inherited from earlier datasets but does not identify whether Anf1233 was affected. Keep these as historical values; no replacement categories are supplied, and the revision notice alone does not show that this row is wrong.

## AmphiBIO's developmental category {#s-e4633b20ee}
Row Anf1233 assigns Dir=1, or direct development, to the Frost 2011 source concept. This category does not establish parental care, breeding location or season. Its equivalence to the current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## One male-coded morphology row {#s-7f870f1c50}
The version 3 morphology table contains one entry (Anura.csv row 1351) with sex code M. Its Family field says Brachycephaloidea, while COL26.8 places the accepted name in Craugastoridae; the source record has not been mapped to the current species concept. It lists snout–vent length (SVL) 38.8, head length (HL) 15 and head width (HW) 14; nine other measurement fields are also populated, for 12 in total. Units are unconfirmed, and the row does not identify unique specimens. These are deposited values, not confirmed absolute dimensions or a sex-based diagnosis. ## Profile scope {#s-1999c40f5a}
The AmphiBIO values belong to a Frost 2011 source concept, while the morphology table's family placement differs from COL26.8 and its units are unconfirmed. Neither source has been reconciled with the current concept; this is not a complete species dossier.
