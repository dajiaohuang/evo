## Where the Atillo observations were made {#s-3fec10b6c2}
The revision reports records from Lagunas de Atillo and nearby Sangay National Park localities at 3,185–3,730 m. Most nighttime captures were active on vegetation up to 1 m above ground; one was on a bromeliad at 2.5 m. Daytime specimens were found beneath rocks. Most collections were roadside, so these encounters do not show that the frogs depend on roads or impose a universal perch-height limit. Calling males were recorded in March, but the account does not define the full breeding season.
