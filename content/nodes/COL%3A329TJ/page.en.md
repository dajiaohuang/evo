## Cucumis maderaspatanus {#s-b69f071ccb}
## Red berries, with a conflicting size field

Cucumis maderaspatanus is a prostrate or climbing herb with simple tendrils. Its rough-haired leaves range from unlobed to palmately three- or five-lobed, with a heart-, spear- or arrow-shaped base. Flowers are yellow. One description gives bright-red, spherical fruit 6–11 mm across in axillary clusters, while another field lists a conflicting fruit length; this dossier therefore does not support a single settled size. The cited habitats are floodplain and valley grasslands, river margins and wetter woodland.
