## What the microhabitat label means {#s-7d180593a7}
The entry records a fossorial microhabitat: associated with burrowing or life underground. This broad category summarizes activity or resource use; it is not a complete habitat list or a fixed daily schedule. The workbook allows positions such as arboreal and terrestrial to be combined, and activity may vary among populations, seasons and life stages.
