## *Pteronia viscosa*: yellow heads on a twiggy shrub {#s-3aa4d37336}
The regional account describes a twiggy shrublet 30–100 cm tall, with whitish, hairless branchlets. Its oblong-lanceolate leaves are leathery, keeled, and edged with fine bristles. Solitary, yellow flower heads sit at branch tips; each is about 20–25 mm long and around 15 mm wide. The bracts around the heads are rough, with margins that are only faintly fringed. The account records rocky slopes as habitat.
