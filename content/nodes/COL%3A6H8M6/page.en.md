## A raised midrib and fused seed leaves {#s-4781540725}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes papery elliptic or oblong leaves with blunt bases. Their tips may be acute, blunt or tapering. The margins roll under and have no cartilage-like edge; the upper midrib is raised, with two veins near the leaf margin. Flowers form compact clusters, and their supporting bracts persist. The small bracteoles also remain; the smooth flower cup bears hairs. The calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One or two seeds are recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo. A separate habitat field labels the setting “terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## A terrestrial label with no range information {#s-79e52e6943}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. It is a category, not a distribution statement.
