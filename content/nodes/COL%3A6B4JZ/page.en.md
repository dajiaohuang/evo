## Accepted name and placement {#s-6318368dd1}
COL26.8 accepts Cornufer citrinospilus (Brown, Richards & Broadhead, 2013) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Ceratobatrachidae (family) → Cornufer (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A shrub frog described from one mountain site {#s-b4bae8eb86}
The 2013 paper describes the species under the name Platymantis citrinospilus. Its abstract reports four males measuring 29.5–32.2 mm, expanded finger and toe discs, prominent supraocular and tarsal tubercles, yellow flank markings, and a distinctive advertisement call. The specimens came from Tompoi Camp at 1,700 m. The account is based on material from that reported site.
