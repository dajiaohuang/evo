## Anf1755 and the recorded concept {#s-84a18fc538}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1755 is the entry for Nanorana rarica, placed by the source in Nanorana (Dicroglossidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-5051751fe4}
Its maximum adult body length is 45.6 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-9e0fa370b0}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
