## How are its female spikelets arranged on each branch? {#s-33d32915ae}
A regional account describes *Restio distichus* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 50 cm and spreading by stolons, horizontal stems. Its branched culms (stems) are smooth or finely wrinkled, and each branch carries several female spikelets (small flower clusters). Their firm, narrow, lance-shaped bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) have translucent tips; the female flowers have a keel or wing-like edges.
