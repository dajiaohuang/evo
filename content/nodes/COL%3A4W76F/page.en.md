## Searsia tridactyla {#s-2c71d3907f}
Regional descriptions distinguish this species by its strictly linear, two-surface-stomata leaflets and discoid drupes; its soil association is stated tentatively.

## Linear leaflets and discoid drupes {#s-6053425aae}
A 1997 account describes a shrub up to 4 m with three-part, petiolate leaves. The sessile leaflets are olive-green, glabrous and amphistomatous, with linear blades, rounded mucronulate tips and entire margins; terminal leaflets are recorded as (11-)29(-50) × (1.0-)1.7(-2.1) mm. Panicles are lax. Flowers are greenish yellow, and the drupes rhombic to discoid, shiny, glabrous, light to dark brown and about ±5.8 × 2.0 mm. A 1993 account says *R. tridactyla* is often confused with co-occurring *R. ciliata* because of its wiry habit and superficially similar leaflets, but distinguishes it by the strictly linear amphistomatous leaflets, rounded tips and discoid drupes. Its habitat is said to be “apparently” limited to calcareous soils. The regional excerpts do not give a dated survey or population-frequency estimate.
