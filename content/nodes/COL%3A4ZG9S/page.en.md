## What leaf and flower features are noted? {#s-5698b1dbd2}
This field describes four-angled, hairy stems and opposite, stalked, leathery leaves. The oval blades have toothed edges that may roll under. Hairy, fringed bracts surround terminal spikes of blue, salver-shaped flowers. The persistent calyx has four teeth; the dry fruit splits into two light-brown segments, each with one seed.
