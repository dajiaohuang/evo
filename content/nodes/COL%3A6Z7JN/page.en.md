## What leaf, flower, and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-9ea745851c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery leaves on stalks 0.9–3.2 cm long. The blades are obovate-lanceolate to obovate-elliptic, with rounded or blunt bases, pointed tips, and slightly wavy edges. Grey, woolly bracts accompany branching flower clusters. The flowers are bisexual and radially arranged, with four or five similar-looking tepals and the same number of stamens. The fruit is an ellipsoid drupe with five to seven ribs.
