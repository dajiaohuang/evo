## Effigia: similar outlines do not mean close kinship {#s-fb65b8fce0}
The nearly complete specimen AMNH FR 30587 preserves an edentulous skull and much of a gracile skeleton. In the published character analysis, Effigia falls within Suchia, a group on the crocodile-line archosaur branch, rather than among theropod dinosaurs. Its resemblance to ornithomimid theropods is treated as convergence: similar form evolved in separate lineages. The fossil does not directly measure gait or diet, and the resemblance does not establish identical ecology or ancestry.
