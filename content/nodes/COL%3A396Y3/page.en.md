## Elegia asperiflora {#s-45de66810e}
## Flower bracts meet the flowers

Elegia asperiflora is described as a rhizomatous perennial up to 50 cm tall, with slightly flattened culms. Its sheaths persist or fall late, while the floral bracts reach the height of the flowers and have somewhat ragged edges. The regional account places it in seeps and other damp areas.
