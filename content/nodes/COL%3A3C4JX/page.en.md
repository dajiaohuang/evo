## A variable leaf texture and one seed {#s-16957a773e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves whose bases may be acute or blunt and whose tips may be acute or tapering to a point. Their texture is listed as papery or leathery. The margin is flat and lacks a hard, cartilage-like edge; the grooved upper midrib, or main leaf vein, lies beside two veins near the edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles. Their supporting bracts and the smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles persist. The smooth flower cup bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is globose and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-21ae195bf4}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It provides no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category adds no further distribution detail.
