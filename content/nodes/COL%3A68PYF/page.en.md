## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-f84e5bca35}
COL26.8 lists Brachycephalus mariaeterezae Bornschein, Morato, Firkowski, Ribeiro & Pie, 2015 as a species in Anura, family Brachycephalidae, genus Brachycephalus. This is the classification in the release dated 20 August 2026; it does not reconcile names used by other taxonomic or fossil databases.

## The sex ranges come from separate, small samples {#s-e0a58e7696}
In comparative Table 2 of the 2025 study, male snout–vent length (SVL) is 10.4–11.2 mm (N = 4), and female SVL is 10.7–13.4 mm (N = 3). The authors describe dorsal skin as moderately rough and lateral skin as densely rough, each with n = 10. These counts belong to separate variables and cannot be added to make one sample size. The ranges describe examined animals, not species-wide maxima or a statistical test of sexual dimorphism. The authors note that smooth skin may include small, inconspicuous warts; externally absent digits alone do not establish that bones are absent.

## Colour differs between living and preserved specimens {#s-e8242059c0}
