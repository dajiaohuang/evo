## Distinguish the measured length {#s-efe1c377e9}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 139 mm; female maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 110.5 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent and differs from total length including the tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Evidence limits {#s-0124e3c3ee}
The finite element models standardize volume and bite loading and use homogeneous isotropic bone properties with shared muscle-attachment assumptions to compare shape. Model strain is not measured live bite force. Single-skull samples, age differences and model restraints limit generalization; they do not establish whole-species rankings, conservation status or complete life histories.
