## Gamochaeta purpurea {#s-969ee03084}
## An interrupted spike of flower clusters

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes basal leaves with upper surfaces that are smooth or becoming smooth, and stem leaves as well. The blades are obovate; their two surfaces differ in color, and their tips are blunt without a small terminal point. Branching is absent or begins at the base.

The conspicuous inflorescence is a discontinuous spike of compact flower clusters. Inner bracts have blunt tips. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; the regional entry supplies no locality or complete range.
