## What stem and fruit traits are recorded for Euphorbia exilis? {#s-9d1dc61c26}
This spineless, hairless succulent shrub reaches 0.6 m and branches mainly from the base. Its brittle, smooth, green branches are round in cross-section and 1–3 mm thick, occasionally 5 mm. Tiny, soon-falling spatulate leaves press against the lower half of the branch and sit beside shiny brown stipules. Numerous 3–4 mm cyathia occur in groups of one to three at some branch tips, with five green glands. The sessile capsule is glossy and hairless, 3–5 mm across, occasionally 7 mm; red stripes mark its angles and grooves.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-e9211380c0}
The source lists stony to sandy flats and gentle slopes.
