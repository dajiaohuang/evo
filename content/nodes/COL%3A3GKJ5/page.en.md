## Gnaphalium pauciflorum {#s-e643430a69}
## A small woolly annual with few heads

The *Plants of the Greater Cape Floristic Region* account describes a white-woolly annual up to 10 cm tall, either erect or spreading. Its leaves are linear. One to a few disk-shaped heads sit in small terminal clusters; the surrounding bracts are straw-colored or golden-brown, sometimes tinged purple. The source habitat field lists flats and slopes; it does not establish a complete range.
