## Large flowers in a basal tuft {#s-0477130043}
Lapeirousia montana is a stemless plant 50–100 mm tall, with a basal tuft of leaves and flowers. Its sweetly scented blue to whitish flowers have a tube mostly 32–45 mm long, longer than the flowers of the related L. plicata.

## Clay and loam soils {#s-fc5fb625a9}
The regional account records clay and loam soils as habitat.
