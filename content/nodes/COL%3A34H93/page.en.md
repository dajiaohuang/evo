## Rose-purple flowers in loose clusters {#s-beb0bd9310}
The record describes a dwarf succulent shrub up to 25 cm tall, with densely branched stems and pale-ochre internodes. Its bright-green, semi-cylindrical leaves have pointed tips and are sparsely dotted with elongated bladder cells. Loose clusters carry rose-purple flowers up to 30 mm across, with pink staminodes and filaments. Flowering is recorded from October to February. It grows in shady places with damp black soil.
