## Physiology {#s-28159a32af}
A field study in Madagascar documented fat-tailed dwarf lemurs hibernating in tree holes for about seven months. In poorly insulated holes, body temperature fluctuated with ambient temperature; in better-insulated holes it remained more stable, with periodic arousals. The study shows that hibernaculum conditions shape thermal patterns, so the word “hibernation” alone does not imply one uniform process.

## Classification {#s-c7f156bb6f}
A multilocus study placed several historically named or morphologically distinct dwarf lemurs within fewer genetic lineages; it treated C. adipicaudatus as part of the fat-tailed dwarf lemur but considered evidence insufficient for C. sibreei and C. minusculus. A 2014 revision used broader sampling to propose different species and candidate-species boundaries, illustrating continuing disagreement over how evidence should be interpreted in the genus.
