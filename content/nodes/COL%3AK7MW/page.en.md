## *Baccharis rufidula*: three-veined leaves and branched heads {#s-a29309d243}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records developed leaf stalks and blades with three main veins; listed outlines are lance-shaped, egg-shaped, or elliptic, with entire margins. Flower heads form branched panicles. The field records heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered. These are alternatives in the source field, which also carries rock-associated and terrestrial substrate labels without naming localities.
