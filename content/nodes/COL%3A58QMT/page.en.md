## Triglochin scilloides: recorded root, leaf and reproductive traits {#s-eb85362919}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes the roots as rhizomatous and the leaves as sessile. It marks the stem as “aquiferous: yes”; that source term is retained without further interpretation. The inflorescence is listed as unisexual or bisexual. The flower has 1 pistil. The fruit is listed as a follicle or nutlet, and the seed is always solitary.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.
