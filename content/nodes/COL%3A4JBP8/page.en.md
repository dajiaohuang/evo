## Eight reported specimens at three southeastern Pará localities {#s-fa3d3e11a2}
The study encountered eight individuals assigned to P. araguaius in Conceição do Araguaia, São Félix do Xingu and São Geraldo do Araguaia, listing MPEG 43621, 44075, 43973–43976, 43978 and 43913. These sites fell within the range of an earlier cited clade treatment. The authors interpreted their evidence as confirming presence in southeastern Pará. This local sample is not an island or state-wide census, a newly observed expansion or proof of continuous occupancy among the three sites.

## Different distances to a paratopotype and a Pará comparison {#s-82f8c8983a}
The reported 16S divergence was 3.46–4.2% from Mato Grosso paratopotype ZUEC 21659, sequence MF926298.1, but 0.25–1.23% from Marabá specimen MZUSP 140079, sequence OQ916232.1. The authors nevertheless assigned their material to the previously delimited araguaius clade and cited its sister relationship to P. hypochondrialis. These particular distances and the cited clade interpretation remain explicit; they are not silently flattened to one uniform divergence or treated as an independently completed species-delimitation analysis.
