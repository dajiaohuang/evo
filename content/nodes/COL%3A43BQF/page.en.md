## Mikania virgata {#s-8c7f4b7d94}
## Whorled leaves with gland-dotted undersides

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright branches with solid pith; their surfaces are listed as smooth or bristly. Leaves grow in whorls and are elliptic to ovate, with narrowing bases and acute or blunt tips. Margins are entire or scalloped. The upper surface is smooth, the lower dotted with glands, and three main veins radiate from the base. The blades are somewhat leathery.

Raceme-like clusters carry stalkless or nearly stalkless heads. Short, linear bracts lie below the heads; the oblong bracts around them are fringed, glandular and acute. The bell-shaped corolla has triangular, rounded, glandular lobes as long as the tube. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. The habitat field says terrestrial; the regional entry gives no locality or full range.
