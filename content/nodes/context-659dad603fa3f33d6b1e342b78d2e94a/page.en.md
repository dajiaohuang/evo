## Anf1719 and the recorded concept {#s-66a812a73e}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1719 is the entry for Limnonectes parvus, placed by the source in Limnonectes (Dicroglossidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-b8f3cb1a03}
For Limnonectes parvus, row Anf1719 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-31bff20575}
Its maximum adult body length is 30 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Compiled clutch counts, yearly reproductive output, and offspring or egg size {#s-04c0a6756d}
For reproduction, offspring-or-egg counts per clutch (one reproductive batch) range from 7 to 12 and maximum reproductive output is 1 events per year.

The offspring-or-egg size ranges from 2 to 2.3 mm. The source field may refer to offspring or to eggs.

AmphiBIO compiles extrema and counts from literature; this row does not separately identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
