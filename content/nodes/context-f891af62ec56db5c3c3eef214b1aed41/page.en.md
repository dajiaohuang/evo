## The source concept {#s-b6ffbfad5a}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Arthroleptis schubotzi as row Anf0146 within Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The source uses Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); correspondence with a current species concept is unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-b48575c7bd}
The row flags terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. These are broad compiled categories without details about setting, season, or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the saved values are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-760b8372f4}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 21 mm. For anurans, this is snout–vent length; the maximum is not a description of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-39e0532fad}
The row reports a maximum of one reproductive event per year. It supplies no clutch-size, maturity, or offspring-size values for this record.

## Developmental strategy {#s-fa7a1a4b44}
The entry flags direct development; larval and viviparity categories are not flagged. No breeding-site or parental-care description is provided in these fields.
