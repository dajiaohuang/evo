## How do its red bracts frame the flower? {#s-bd09bf4b1c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an unbranched stem and elliptical or obovate leaves. The bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) have leafy appendages, are red on both sides and have a leathery texture. Its corolla is yellow or orange, the flower lip is yellow, and the corolla is hairless. The entry lists alternative leaf and corolla states rather than a single form.
