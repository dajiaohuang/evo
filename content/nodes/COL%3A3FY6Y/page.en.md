## Gethyllis lata: leaves and flower {#s-c295273263}
A 2013 regional account describes a solitary bulbous geophyte 30–40 mm tall. It records 2–6 leaves that are dry at flowering, prostrate, broad and flat. The leaves are glabrous, with a raised, pinkish or straw-coloured cartilaginous margin. The flower is salver-shaped and white to pale pink.
