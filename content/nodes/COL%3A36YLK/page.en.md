## Disynaphia littoralis {#s-1ca7590f29}
## Linear leaves and five-flowered heads

The source records a branched woody plant with linear leaves that become nearly hairless on both surfaces. The inner bracts around the flower head are oblong with blunt tips, and each head has five florets. The dry one-seeded fruit (cypsela) is glabrous. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial, without further detail.
