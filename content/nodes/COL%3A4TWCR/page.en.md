## Cell area changed around death signals in two strains {#s-cb8423929e}
A time-lapse culture study measured cell-area change around the onset of PI-positive death in two laboratory strains: mean area fell by 27.2% in W303-1A and 27.0% in BY4741. With phloxine B staining, the corresponding reductions were 24.8% and 27.9%. W303-1A reached stationary phase after two days and BY4741 after one; both were then imaged hourly for 30 hours at about 24°C. These values measure cell area in two strains under this protocol, not volume, baseline morphology or species-wide variation.

## Source categories for 103 strains are not habitat frequencies {#s-4aed002ba1}
The 2009 study analyzed 103 strains with recorded origins that included clinics; soils in Pennsylvania and North Carolina; vineyards in North Carolina and Australia; fruits; a brewery; commercial Saccharomyces boulardii preparations; and insect guts. This is a list of sample-source categories, not the frequency of habitats or a complete ecological account of the species.

## Five nuclear loci describe structure within the sampled panel {#s-fc562df541}
Among strains with complete sequence data at five nuclear loci, the authors report three population-genetic groups associated with different ecological and geographic origins. In this panel, recombination was not detected at those loci in soil isolates. These results are limited to the strains and loci in the 2009 study; they are not a species phylogeny, a complete evolutionary history or a description of present-day wild-population dynamics.
