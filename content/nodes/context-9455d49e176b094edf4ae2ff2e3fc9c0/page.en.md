## The source concept {#s-4ae77e96a6}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Thoropa megatympanum as row Anf1412, classified in Thoropa, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-bbfac794b8}
The row marks terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage variation in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against those revisions; verify the historical categories before reuse. No replacement values are inferred, and the notice does not imply that every original entry is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-6af3752f42}
AmphiBIO lists arthropods among the adult food categories. These categories compile literature reports based on expert assessments, observations or stomach contents; they are not a complete diet or measured diet proportions.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-64ad9ff042}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 53 mm for Thoropa megatympanum. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-c0edf7a2e8}
The row reports minimum and maximum clutch counts of 40 and 95 offspring or eggs, up to one reproductive event per year and minimum and maximum offspring or egg sizes of 3.4 and 3.4 mm. These literature-derived extrema and counts do not separately identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions. Size values may refer to offspring or eggs.
