## Matelea fontellana {#s-1efe45b901}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Matelea fontellana* as an accepted species of *Matelea*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 gives terrestrial as its habitat category, with no locality, survey date, or range. The source record contains no morphology description, so the evidence here is limited to that regional label.
