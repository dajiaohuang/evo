## Pilosocereus aureispinus {#s-87c631aaab}
## More than ten ribs and no central spines

Brazilian Flora 2020 records erect stems that branch only at the base, with more than ten ribs. The areoles are separate and lack central spines. A cephalium is absent or shallow. Flowers have straight tubes colored green or vinaceous. The scar left by the floral tube is deeply inset in the fruit, and the seeds have a warty coat. The habitat label is rocky, with no named locality.
