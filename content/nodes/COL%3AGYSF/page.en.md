## Accepted name and classification {#s-bb51a46221}
COL26.8 accepts *Arthroleptis schubotzi* Nieden, 1911 as a species in the genus *Arthroleptis*, family Arthroleptidae and order Anura. This name and placement follow the release dated 20 August 2026.

## Scores in the Rwanda prioritization index {#s-1cbf72323f}
Table A4 reports DBS 2, TBS 0 and SBS 1, for species index SI 3. DBS weights the number of represented Rwanda ecozones and whether the species is nationally endemic. TBS uses national Red List proposals reported by earlier studies, not a new global IUCN assessment; score 0 corresponds to LC (Least Concern) in Table 1. SBS 1 represents a few microhabitats in disturbed areas. These are categories in this Rwanda index, not measured physiological tolerances, demonstrated responses to every land use or an updated threat status. A combined VU/DD result cannot distinguish Vulnerable from Data Deficient by itself.

## Species score and site score are different {#s-faf72eacb2}
SI adds DBS, TBS and SBS for one species. Site RABI adds the SI scores of all recorded species and does not divide by species richness. Thus SI 3 is not a site’s RABI, an extinction probability or an effect size. The index uses stepped weights—DBS 1/2/4/8, TBS 0/1/2/4/8 and SBS 0/1/2/4. These weights express the authors’ ranking design; they do not show that extinction risk or ecological sensitivity actually doubles between categories. The reported positive correlations among sub-indices also mean their inputs were not empirically independent in this sample.

## Sampling limits and inconsistent totals {#s-258782d0f8}
The 51 sites are unevenly distributed among the study’s ecozones: EZ1 has 6, EZ2 21, EZ3 8, EZ4 2 and EZ5 14. The accumulation curve did not level off, so regional detections are incomplete and missing taxa may change scores. The index uses assembled occurrences and literature-informed categories, not a controlled habitat experiment or a newly reanalysed time series. Applying it elsewhere would require local ecozones, inventories and national threat information, with locally appropriate categories. The source also gives inconsistent totals: Results use 54 taxa while Table 4 says 55; the maximum EZ3 RABI is 38 in Results and Table A1 but 33 in Table 7. This page does not reanalyse field data, inspect figure pixels or verify current legal protection status.
