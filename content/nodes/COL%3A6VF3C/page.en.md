## What female-flower and seed details are listed? {#s-b1407de2ba}
The record describes simple stems and petiolate, alternate elliptic or oval leaves. It lists no male-flower traits; that is a gap in this entry, not evidence that the plant lacks male flowers. Female flowers have six sepals and a shallow dish-shaped nectar disc, but sepal color is not reported. The fruit is a spherical capsule, and the seeds are smooth and unornamented.
