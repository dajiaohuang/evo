## Chamaecrista feliciana {#s-6e2758f997}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista feliciana* records 8–13 pairs of broad ovate leaflets, four large fan-shaped yellow petals plus a smaller inner petal, legumes 3.5–4 cm long. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
