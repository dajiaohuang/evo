## Accepted name and placement {#s-5a74cac8b0}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts *Eleutherodactylus casparii* Dunn, 1926 as a species in Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family) and *Eleutherodactylus* (genus). This is the name, rank and placement recorded in the pinned catalogue.

## Land and water foraging categories {#s-b411f45185}
The AmphiBIO row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) and aquatic (Aqu = 1) foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations, but this row has not been checked against that revision. The flags remain historical AmphiBIO values, not verified present-day ecology.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-707713e80e}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 21 mm for this source concept. For Anura, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length (SVL), from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a typical size or a measurement of every individual; the historical concept remains unmatched to COL26.8.

## One maximum reproductive event per year {#s-a7d0fd3979}
The maximum reproductive output listed in AmphiBIO is 1 event per year. The row does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions behind that value, and it does not by itself establish an annual schedule for every population.
