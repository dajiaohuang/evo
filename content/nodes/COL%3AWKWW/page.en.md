## Coccoloba glaziovii {#s-24f01d220b}
## Persistent ochreae frame cordate leaf bases

Coccoloba glaziovii has erect branches and persistent ochreae—the sheaths at the leaf bases. Its oval or elliptic leaves have cordate to nearly cordate bases; the lower surface may be hairless or variably hairy. Elongated racemose flower clusters carry cylindrical, two-lobed bracteoles. The fruiting perianth is ovoid, leathery or thick, and hairless; its lobes do not enlarge. The pointed, ovoid nut and terrestrial habitat are recorded, but no locality is supplied.
