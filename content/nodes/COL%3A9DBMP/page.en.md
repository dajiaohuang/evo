## Leaves {#s-5cdefb8815}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset describes persistent stipules and a winged leaf rachis. Leaves are even-pinnate, with 7–10 pairs of oblong leaflets. Their bases are asymmetrical and their tips notched (emarginate); the lower surface is recorded as lacking punctations.

## Flowers, fruit and seeds {#s-8ae9bd4f7b}
The inflorescence entry identifies a raceme with a glabrous axis. Flowers have a hairless hypanthium and four free sepals. Petals are described as clawed, ear-shaped, or obovate. The record lists three stamens with hairy filaments and a hairy ovary. The fruit is a legume; seeds are ovate. These are source-listed descriptions, not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-dbc2a60848}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
