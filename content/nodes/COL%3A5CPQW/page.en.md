## *Xyris picea*: two-ranked leaves above a broad sheath {#s-1a8117ffb9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description places the leaves in two ranks. Their chestnut-based sheaths widen markedly, sometimes forming ear-like lobes; the flat blades can be smooth, ridged or finely bumpy, with pointed or uneven tips. Some margins are ciliate, meaning fringed with short hairs, while others are rough.

The cylindrical flowering stalk carries one or two ridges and can reach 50–70 cm. Its ridges alone may be rough. The ovoid flower spike has 10–25 flowers and four outer sterile bracts, the small leaf-like structures around the flowers. The accompanying habitat field says only “terrestrial,” without a collection locality or date; the regional description does not report how widely these traits vary among populations.
