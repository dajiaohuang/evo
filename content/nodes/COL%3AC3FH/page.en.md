## Aloe braamvanwykii {#s-71efdb060f}
Aloe braamvanwykii is a slow-growing, long-lived aloe that usually forms dense clumps, sometimes with dozens of heads. Its relatively small, intense-red flowers and short, early-season flowering period (December–February) distinguish it in the regional account. It is associated with open grassy patches in Klerksdorp Thornveld, often on red sandy loam. The account describes these regional habitat and phenology observations.
