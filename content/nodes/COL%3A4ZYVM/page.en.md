## A sac-shaped flower spur {#s-8b07500613}
The regional account describes S. inandensis flowers as pink to lilac and similar in form to those of S. longifolia, but with a saccate, or sac-shaped, spur. This is the diagnostic comparison provided in the source.

## Soil pockets on sandstone {#s-a4ad2a4c90}
The habitat record places the plant in pockets of soil on sandstone. It gives no more specific locality.
