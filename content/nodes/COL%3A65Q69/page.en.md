## Agarista nummularia (Cham. & Schltdl.) G.Don {#s-0a944a7bd3}
## Rounded leaves and several recorded surface states

Agarista nummularia has upright stems. Its leaves range from elliptic or ovate to nearly round, with bases that may be rounded, squared off or heart-shaped. The margins may be entire, wavy or rolled under. The source records both smooth and hairy leaf surfaces, including gland-tipped hairs beneath, rather than resolving one consistent surface state. Racemes bear urn- to cylinder-shaped white flowers. The fruit is a depressed, globose capsule; its surface is also described with alternative hair states.
