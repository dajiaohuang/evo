## ## One pair of pinnae and bell-shaped calyces {#s-6992665c23}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaves with one pair of pinnae—the side axes that carry the leaflets. Leaflet-pair counts are given as 3–10 and 5–10 in the source fields. In central flowers, the calyx is tubular to bell-shaped and ranges from glabrous to finely hairy. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
