## One thermal estimate in the source dataset {#s-abeff77c69}
The curated AmphiTherm dataset contains one adult estimate row under one source species concept matched to Bombinatoridae in its 2018 classification. The row reports CTmax, a critical high-temperature limit. CTmax and CTmin are critical thermal limits; LT50_hot and LT50_cold come from lethal-limit assays, while Tpref records thermal preference. These are different experiments and cannot be combined into one tolerance range. Counts can overlap and do not equal distinct species or animals. The source classification and its single estimate do not describe every current member of the family.
