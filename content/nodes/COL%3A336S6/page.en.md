## What does the description say about Cymbopetalum brasiliense? {#s-80bd67596e}
This small tree has large, papery leaves and long flower stalks. Its petals are hairy on both sides; the inner petals are thicker than the outer ones. Numerous curved, cylinder-shaped fruit segments can reach 3.5 cm long, and each may contain up to six seeds, usually fewer.

## What range does the source report? {#s-b3d67eebbf}
The cited account reports it in eastern South America from Trinidad and Venezuela south to Rio de Janeiro, and in western South America from Panama to southern Colombia. This is the range stated in that source.
