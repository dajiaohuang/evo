## Accepted name and placement {#s-a21e26bbf2}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis bicumulus (Peters, 1863) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-63a6d4f5fa}
AmphiBIO’s historical record for Pristimantis bicumulus codes terrestrial foraging and direct development, lists maximum adult snout–vent length of 36 mm, and gives up to 1 reproductive event per year under its Frost 2011 concept.

## The older ground-foraging label {#s-7ecf2130f6}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 source concept is coded as terrestrial foraging. This broad label does not report frequency or seasonal and life-stage variation. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian microhabitat entries, but this row has not been checked against the revision. Its equivalence to the current COL26.8 species concept is unverified.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-22c1b59600}
AmphiBIO gives 36 mm as maximum adult body length for the Frost 2011 source concept. For an anuran, body length is snout–vent length, from the snout to the cloaca. This literature-compiled maximum is not a measurement of every adult or a confirmed value for the current species concept.

## A yearly reproductive count {#s-4aea6863a1}
The same historical row lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO compiles literature counts and extrema, but does not identify the sex, population, observation date or wild/captive conditions behind each value. Offspring-size fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## A developmental category {#s-f9c70f4fdc}
The archived AmphiBIO record is coded as direct development. It does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing, and its Frost 2011 source concept has not been reconciled with the current accepted name.
