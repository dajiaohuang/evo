## What does the description say about Xylopia crinita? {#s-8936714381}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records young branches with velvety or woolly hairs. Its elliptic-lance-shaped leaves are 2–10 cm long, with 12–19 pairs of side veins and a hairy underside. One or two flowers grow in the leaf axils or on older branches; the sepals are fused for more than half their length. The plant bears more than 20 kidney-shaped fruit segments, each 11–45 mm long and woolly. Seed length and the dried fruit surface are not specified.

The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
