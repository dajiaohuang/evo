## Gaylussacia rigida Casar. {#s-661c8a41a2}
## Hairless upper leaf surfaces and ribbed fruit

Gaylussacia rigida is described as an upright shrub with a rounded crown. Its papery leaves are obovate to oblanceolate, with margins that may be flat or finely scalloped near the tip. The upper surface is smooth; the underside may also be smooth or have hairs along the midvein or base. Axillary racemes carry a few white, tubular or urn-shaped flowers with smooth corollas. The fruit is globose, ribbed and hairless. The source leaves several details, including the lower-surface glands, as alternatives.
