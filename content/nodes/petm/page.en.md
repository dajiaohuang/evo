## The PETM: warming and biological responses {#s-5ecd29d5b6}
During the Paleocene–Eocene Thermal Maximum (PETM), a rapid negative carbon-isotope excursion coincided with warming, deep-sea benthic extinctions and poleward shifts in organism ranges. These records establish a close timing relationship; the carbon source and release pace remain debated, and estimated onset and recovery durations depend on the resolution of each archive.
