## Two linked fronds and a floral cavity {#s-e45b3d711d}
The accepted species name follows Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage 7G7JL and matched World Flora Online usage wfo-0000334487. The biological details below come from a Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record.

COL26.8 accepted species ID 7G7JL and exact name/authorship map through exactly one accepted WFO usage wfo-0000334487; the Brazilian Flora source record carries the same WFO ID.

A Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Wolffiella oblonga as free-floating and rootless, with two vegetative fronds sometimes joined by a pouch. It records a small two-flower inflorescence within a cavity on the upper surface.

## Fronds and structure {#s-115c33bd02}
The source records two free-floating fronds without roots, joined by a vegetative pouch; some form a star-shaped colony. Fronds are slender and flattened, ranging in outline from round or ovate to tongue-shaped and sometimes sickle-shaped. They measure 3.2–5.4 mm long and 1–1.5 mm wide. Vegetative cavities have a line of elongated, transparent cells along the margin and an angle of 60–75°. Brown-coloured epidermal cells are noted on dried fronds.

One two-flower inflorescence occurs per frond, without a prophyll, in a floral cavity on the upper surface near the frond base and to one side of the midline. It has one male and one female flower. The male flower has one stamen with a single-chambered, basifixed anther (attached at its base). The female flower has one basal, orthotropous (straight) ovule and a circular stigma. The fruit is asymmetric and one-seeded; the field gives a persistent style or utricle, a small one-seeded fruit.
