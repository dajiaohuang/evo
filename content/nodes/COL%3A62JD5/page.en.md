## From skin structures to local sensory recordings {#s-9b86926b14}
Spectacled caimans provide one local multi-stimulus study for Caiman. In 2013, electrodes near juvenile jaw integumentary sensory organs (ISOs) recorded responses to brushing, temperature and pH, but no corresponding response to the tested salt solutions. Head organs, postcranial skin without the same organs and similar tongue structures require separate interpretation. Broad-snouted and yacare caimans were not part of this experiment; membership in the genus does not supply identical sensory measurements for every species.
