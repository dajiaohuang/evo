## Leaves, flowers and dark fruit {#s-832cdfe0be}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry describes scaly bark and membranous, broadly elliptic leaflets. Their surfaces are dull and glabrous or becoming hairless; veins are conspicuous underneath. Inflorescences are described as dichasial cymes (branching flower clusters) or corymbs (flat-topped clusters). Buds are gray. The sepals do not bend back and are hairless on their inner surface; petals are white and hairless, and the ovary is glabrous. Fruits are dark brown, with an outer skin recorded as slightly warty or dull. Each fruit contains 3–7 dark-brown seeds. This is one regional row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-afc0b8b0c3}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
