## Trichocline catharinensis {#s-137299ff75}
## Woolly scapes carry yellow to orange rays

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record allows several leaf forms: spoon-shaped, oblanceolate, wavy-edged or linear. The undersides are woolly. Scapes may be under or over 30 cm long and either upright or lying along the ground; they are woolly and have bracts at their nodes.

The involucre has two distinct bract shapes, lance-shaped to inversely egg-shaped. Ray florets are yellow or orange. The pappus bristles are straight at the tip. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
