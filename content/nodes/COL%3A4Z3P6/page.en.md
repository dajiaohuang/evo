## Mass values from two life stages {#s-bf2a22271f}
The historical summaries are 583.25 g for adult body mass and 12.29 g for mass at birth or hatching. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex. The two values describe different life stages, not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a range of individual masses across the species.

## Reproductive timing and source definitions {#s-b1e07b30e9}
The archive reports 196.96 d for the female maturity field, defined as age at first conception; 5 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch; 32.99 d from conception or fertilization to birth; and 50 d to dependence on food other than maternal milk. Here d means days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring and egg counts. Each interval refers to a different event, not one general reproductive cycle.

## Maximum longevity is a recorded maximum {#s-1364df7dc2}
The archive reports 10.5 years for maximum longevity, using the largest record in its collected material under the source method. This is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current record known to be repeatable.

## Estimated weights of food categories {#s-67bd085651}
The reported nonzero weights are mammals and birds 40%, invertebrates 30%, and other plant material 30% (a source category that also includes fungi). The ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates may draw on a congener judged ecologically similar. These are standardized estimates from verbal descriptions, not direct measurements of this species’ diet. A zero does not show that a food is never eaten. Source classes such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday labels and do not mean an exclusive diet of nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging stratum in the mammal record {#s-681d715140}
The mammal record assigns G, the ground category; the source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. The metadata attributes mammalian foraging-stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a category, not a share of time spent in each stratum, and it does not mean the species forages only there.

## Activity-period code {#s-0ea5a9158c}
The record marks night feeding activity as 1. Several periods can be marked together, so the codes are not shares that add to a daily time budget or a schedule that applies at every site and season. Activity confidence is ABC and may include evidence from an ecologically similar congener; genus- or family-level typical values are not used to fill missing fields here.

The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so these labels are not additional independent observations.

## What these records establish {#s-30938d9806}
The 2015 life-history values are historical same-name records. They are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. The summaries are not measurements of one animal, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies; matching names and families do not remove that possibility or prove equivalence to COL26.8. Wild or captive conditions are not confirmed uniformly, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually.

The EltonTraits 1.0 entry is a historical feeding-ecology record, not a complete species account. Its food and avian foraging-stratum percentages are coarse weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain uncertainty in sources and proportions. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may include a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus- and family-level values, non-species avian strata and records needing review do not establish definite species-level conclusions. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation status require other sources.
