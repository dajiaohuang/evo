## Stem surface {#s-3239d1b99c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes velvety stem hairs that spread outward; simple hairs are present. Branched and unbranched bristles, glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are recorded as absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-b8436f061d}
Stipules are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries and projections between pairs of pinnae as absent. The inflorescence is globose; floral bracts are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-f58272133d}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous and has no lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and bear silky hairs (sericeous). The androecium is isostemonous, with stamens equal in number to the petals; the filaments are pink.
