## Small stagnant waters with an inferred egg substrate {#s-3434d85c6d}
The 2016 account described the Philippine frogs assigned to this name as associated with swamps, ephemeral pools and stagnant water. It stated that eggs were most likely laid in mud and that tadpoles were unknown. The mud statement is therefore an inference, not an observed oviposition event, and the unknown tadpoles are not interpreted as absence of a larval stage. No species-wide breeding calendar or measured reproductive success is supplied.

## Three historical Philippine lineages remained unresolved {#s-7b4a72fd6c}
The account cited a 2014 phylogeographic study as suggesting several independently evolving Philippine lineages and described three major lineages as taxonomically unresolved. It cited a Data Deficient recommendation for these Philippine populations and listed records along the eastern island arc, with an intervening Bicol gap. These statements retain the 2016 regional concept and cited evidence; they do not certify all historical populations as matching the current accepted taxon, name three new species or prove permanent absence in Bicol.
