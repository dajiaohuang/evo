## What do sightings tell us about population size? {#s-8fae7befd9}
At the Mt. Bontoc forest fragment in Hindang, Leyte, researchers opportunistically recorded five tarsiers in August 2021. Direct sightings and camera traps added six records in January–February 2022. The authors could not tell whether the records represented different individuals and said population viability was unknown, so these figures are neither a total count nor a population estimate.

## Ecological evidence {#s-9ca98a01ea}
Around five tarsier sighting sites at Mt. Bontoc, researchers measured 612 woody stems in plots; mean diameter at breast height was 2.5 cm and mean height was 5.0 m. They described the site as regenerating secondary forest on limestone. This vegetation sample from one forest fragment does not represent all habitats used by Philippine tarsiers.

## Evidence limits {#s-1ebbd02691}
Source-linked introduction;. Evidence comes from a short study of one forest fragment on Leyte; individual counts could not be confirmed, viability is unknown, and findings do not generalize across the species' range.
