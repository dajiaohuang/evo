## Mittenothamnium reptans: three habitat categories in the dataset {#s-fffdb820bc}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 record labels the substrate “corticícola,” or bark-associated.

Another uses “rupícola,” the rock-associated category.

A third lists “terrícola,” the terrestrial category, for *Mittenothamnium reptans*.

These are habitat labels, not descriptions of how common each setting is. The records give no collection locality or survey date and do not establish a complete range.
