## Chaohusaurus anchors a Triassic ichthyosaur sample {#s-99f9352efe}
The Ichthyosauria navigation envelope begins with a *Chaohusaurus* specimen assigned to the Procolumbites Zone, at approximately 248 Ma, and retains 90 Ma as a rounded younger composite edge. The specimen provides a study anchor, not an exact global first appearance, complete lineage duration or direct ancestor.
