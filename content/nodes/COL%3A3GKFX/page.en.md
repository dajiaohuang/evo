## White-woolly leaves around clustered heads {#s-200174ca9a}
This record describes an erect or decumbent perennial 4–20 cm tall, with narrow, pointed, white-woolly leaves. Congested clusters of flower heads are closely surrounded by leaves; the flowers are white or dull white. It is recorded from seasonally wet places, and the account uses its small pale bract tips to distinguish it from G. vestitum.
