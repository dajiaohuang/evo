## Dieffenbachia aglaonematifolia {#s-f06147dbb5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 coded description lists an erect, sparsely branched stem and petioles up to 23 cm long; leaf blades are under 60 cm and include elliptic, oblong, ovate, or lanceolate forms. Stem height appears in two conflicting entries—usually under 1.0 m and usually over 1.0 m—so the source does not settle that measure. The habitat field records soil.
