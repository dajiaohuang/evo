## Low estimated detection reflects a restricted survey record {#s-3b920c1d3b}
Oreobates quixensis was detected by eDNA only at river site TrP2 and observed twice at the same site by visual surveys. The reported fitted detection probabilities were 0.29 for eDNA and 0.056 for visual surveys. These values summarize the paper’s presence–absence matrices and model; they do not demonstrate that the species is globally rare or that either method has this sensitivity everywhere. Nor does restriction to one detected site prove absence from every other unsampled part of the basin.
