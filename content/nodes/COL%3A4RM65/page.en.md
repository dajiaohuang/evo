## The EltonTraits name and its limits {#s-b144bcf34f}
Rattus niobe is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. Three field groups are drawn from its same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record. The archive retains earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals, so matching names do not settle current species boundaries or synonym disputes.

## Three relative categories in the diet record {#s-407e60c76b}
EltonTraits assigns 40% to invertebrates, 30% to seeds, nuts, and spores, and 30% to other plant material, including fungi. These percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than directly measure diet. ABC combines high, reasonable, and partial confidence; some estimates may draw on an ecologically similar congener, not direct observation of R. niobe. Zero weight does not show an item is never eaten. Nect, Seed, and PlantO also have broader meanings than their everyday labels.

## A ground-category placement {#s-4995187855}
The mammalian foraging stratum is G, defined as ground while including some aquatic foraging. Code A marks compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Metadata links mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a broad category, not a time share or exclusive-use claim.

## Night activity is one of several possible codes {#s-c6c2314d43}
The archive marks feeding at night with 1. Multiple periods can be marked 1 at once, so the entries are not percentages summing to a daily schedule or a guarantee across sites and seasons. Activity confidence is ABC and can rely on congeneric evidence; genus/family typical values do not fill species-level gaps.

## Source labels identify the field group {#s-6d762cb4c3}
Included field groups have Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum, and activity may have separate sources; a Ref number shared by bird and mammal tables is not necessarily the same reference. Original strings are retained by file, row, field group, and reference table. Underlying books and papers have not been individually read; the labels do not increase the number of observations.

## Evidence limits for this record {#s-7d992da18d}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family reading guide, not a monograph, and historical species concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized relative weights from literature, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportion uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet and activity entries may use a congener judged ecologically similar, so the page does not claim every field is a direct observation of R. niobe. Genus/family typical values, avian strata lacking species-level support, and split records needing individual review do not establish definite conclusions. Source works have not been reread. Local, seasonal, and individual variation, current range, population, and conservation require further evidence.
