## Cussonia gamtoosensis {#s-24bcb41cd7}
## Slender trees with digitately divided leaves

SANBI describes slender trees with several stems, up to 4 m tall. The leaves divide twice in a hand-like pattern, with the leaflets mostly divided once more in the same way. The inflorescence consists of flower spikes arranged like an umbel, and the flowers are greenish yellow. Its recorded habitat is dry, rocky slopes.
