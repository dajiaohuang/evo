## Two specimens and a topology-dependent crown calibration {#s-be83c3ae55}
Two named Middle Triassic specimens of Fukangichthys longidorsalis preserve cranial, scale and CT-visible skeletal characters. Their analysis places the scanilepiform on the polypterid stem, changing one estimate of the ray-fin crown minimum by 45 million years; that shift depends on the tested topology, not a directly observed origin.

## Kelamayi ages and the 45-million-year shift {#s-83aaa68aee}
Holotypes IVPP V4096.6 and V4096.13 anchor the anatomy and topology. Their 247–241.464 Ma Kelamayi sample interval is distinct from the topology-dependent calibration shift and is not a global range or direct ancestry date.
