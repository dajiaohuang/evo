## What distinctive leaf shape and flower traits are recorded? {#s-b1a2ea962c}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes erect branches and petiolate leaves that are triangular in cross-section. The leaves are hairless on both faces, have flat margins and one vein. Flowers are solitary and terminal. The inside tip of the floral cup is hairless. Petals are pink, the anthers are straight, and toothed appendages occur between the sepals. The capsule is longer than the floral cup, with many seeds in each chamber. These are the regional dataset's recorded traits.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c050834af3}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without a locality or collection date.
