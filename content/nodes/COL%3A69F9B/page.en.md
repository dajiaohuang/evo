## Name and trait record {#s-e3939e88c0}
COL26.8 places *Causus defilippii* in Viperidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 row uses the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and cites its length, microhabitat and reproductive-mode fields separately. These are compiled reports, not a uniform account of every population.

## Two length measures {#s-f457c70f93}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 317 mm and the maximum total length (TL) is 420 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent; TL includes the tail. These compiled maxima are the largest cited values, not growth limits or typical sizes. They need not describe the same individual, and sex-specific records from different places or studies do not by themselves measure sexual dimorphism within a population.

## Microhabitat {#s-8fc1d7c367}
The row records a Terrestrial microhabitat, or ground-associated setting. This category is not a complete habitat account, and the field does not describe variation among populations, seasons or life stages.

## Reproductive mode {#s-7133fed70e}
The reproductive-mode field is labelled oviparous, or egg-laying. The row gives no clutch-size summary, so this label does not establish a typical number of eggs or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-60fce3673a}
The row may combine reports that differ by sex, age, locality or population. Its Reptile Database 2023 name framework does not by itself prove equivalence to the COL26.8 concept. The reported lengths are not absolute species limits. Mass fields may be estimated from length equations and juvenile-length definitions differ; neither is presented here as a measurement. This record does not update venom, conservation status, current distribution or population trends. Trait citations preserve the source chain but do not mean that every underlying work was independently checked for this account.
