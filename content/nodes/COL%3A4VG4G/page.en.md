## Winged calyx and variable floral description {#s-baf65ecaa0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a four-angled stem and membranous leaves. It lists leaf venation as acrodromous–brochidodromous, flower colour as yellow or white, and corolla-lobe tips as obtuse or acute; the record does not distinguish those alternatives. The calyx is winged, with acute linear to lance-shaped lobes. The corolla is funnel-shaped with obovate lobes. Its filaments bear toothed membranous wings, and the seed coat is pitted.

## Terrestrial habitat category {#s-0483d62816}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field lists *Schultesia benthamiana* as terrestrial but does not give a collection locality.
