## Reusing an existing genome rather than adding a field sample {#s-d5c3d45af6}
The 2019 study compares an existing genome of Apteryx mantelli with eleven added palaeognath genomes. Existing material retains its own provenance: vouchers for the ten newly sequenced living birds are not assigned to this species. Sequence counts describe marker coverage, not multiple independent individuals of this species; the two companion papers share the comparison material.

## Kiwi relationships and the difficulty of short branches {#s-1f6c05dd13}
This species enters the 2019 palaeognath genome comparison. Cloutier and colleagues’ MP-EST and ASTRAL species trees associate kiwi with emu/cassowary, with rheas sister to that combined clade; concatenation differs for some relationships. The authors interpret gene-tree disagreement across short internal branches through incomplete lineage sorting, comparing marker types and retroelements. The result does not mean every gene supports one tree or include unsampled kiwi species in this species’ sample.

## Evidence limits {#s-5760ad601b}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
