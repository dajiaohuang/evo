## *Cuscuta gerrardii* {#s-af20a3bd7e}
The account describes slender to medium yellow stems and small flowers, about 2 mm long, in loose clusters. The bell-shaped corolla has upright to slightly spreading lobes; the capsule is spherical to flattened-spherical and contains one or two black, finely roughened seeds. Calyx and corolla proportions, lobe shape, and other floral details vary in the source description, so this summary does not treat one state as universal.

The source says *Cuscuta gerrardii* has been found on several unidentified herbs and small shrubs. The hosts were not identified, so this is not a complete host list.
