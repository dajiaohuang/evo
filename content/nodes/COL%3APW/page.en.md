## Historical foraging codes in AmphiBIO {#s-f1670aa8c6}
AmphiBIO v1 retains 5,971 records under Anura in the Frost 2011 source classification. Positive foraging codes appear in 397 records for fossorial (burrowing), 4,987 for terrestrial, 3,287 for aquatic and 2,326 for arboreal categories. A record can carry more than one code, and an unreported value remains unknown. These totals describe coded historical records, not the share of species in the current order with each trait.

## Developmental codes in the same historical subset {#s-0446cb4eef}

These are coded source records, not independent population samples; a missing code does not show that a category is absent. The counts retain the historical compilation's scope and are not a complete classification of current Anura.

## What the thermal records cover {#s-4007888803}
In the pinned, curated AmphiTherm classification, Anura matches 519 deposited species concepts and 3,545 estimate rows. The metric counts are CTmax, 2,237 rows across 485 source concepts; Tpref, 447 across 86; CTmin, 803 across 248; LT50_cold, 9 across 3; and LT50_hot, 49 across 12. CTmax and CTmin record critical thermal limits, LT50_hot and LT50_cold come from lethal-limit assays, and Tpref records thermal preference. These are different measurements, not one combined tolerance range. A concept may have records for multiple metrics, so the counts do not represent distinct species or animals. Source life-stage codes include larvae, metamorphs, adults and juveniles. The dataset describes study coverage, not the physiology of every current member; its 2018 source classification remains separate from current group boundaries.
