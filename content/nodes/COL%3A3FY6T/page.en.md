## Gethyllis gregoriana {#s-a738692a0a}
Gethyllis gregoriana is a clumping bulbous geophyte with one or two grey, strap-shaped leaves that dry during flowering. Its white flowers have spreading tepals and a central upright style; the leathery berry is cream to olive-green with maroon speckles. The regional account records upland sand or clay as habitat.
