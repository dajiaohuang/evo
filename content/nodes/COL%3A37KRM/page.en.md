## Dracontium laetum {#s-a7b4f1df9f}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records a regular tuber, a three-part leaf with irregular lobes, and a spathe (bract) up to 20 cm long that conceals most of the spadix (flower-bearing axis). The exposed flower spike is listed at 2–6 cm; flowers have 4 tepals (undifferentiated perianth segments) and 5 or 6 stamens. The habitat field records soil. These regional fields do not give a full-range description.
