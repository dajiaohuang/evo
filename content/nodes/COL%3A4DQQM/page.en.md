## Parodia linkii {#s-3bba34d5ec}
## Golden hairs frame yellow flowers

This account describes small-to-medium clustered stems, globose or cylindrical, with continuous ribs and distinct areoles. The spines may be fine or thick and golden; central spines may curve or be absent. Flowers form at the stem tip amid abundant golden hairs. Their petals are yellow with spatulate tips and irregularly notched margins; stigma color is listed as white or yellow, or red to vinaceous. The fruit is succulent, but its color and the seed details are unknown. The habitat label is rocky.
