## Chamaecrista paraunana {#s-e6e39583b1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista paraunana* records two leaflet pairs, yellow unequal petals, oblong legumes with valves twisting at maturity. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
