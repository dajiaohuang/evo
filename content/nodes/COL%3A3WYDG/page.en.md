## Branches and leaflets {#s-913ee315d0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes armed branches with flattened cross-sections and spiny stipules. Leaflets are oblong or elliptic-lanceolate, with acuminate tips and bases described as acute or obtuse. Their secondary veins are cladodromous: they branch freely toward the edge and fade before reaching it. Hairs are present on the upper leaflet surface; the lower surface is glabrous.

## Inflorescences {#s-980a16bedb}
The inflorescences are panicles, positioned in leaf axils or at shoot ends. Their bracts are described as leaf-like.

## Flowers {#s-3659c6a130}
The calyx is bell-shaped and the petals are yellow. Hairs are present on the standard, or banner petal. The stamens are diadelphous, arranged in two groups.

## Fruit {#s-7b1a3d692e}
The fruit is described as a samara, a winged dry fruit. Its seed-bearing core is verrucose, or warty.

## Habitat category {#s-c7d3ae1ba3}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
