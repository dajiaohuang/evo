## The source concept {#s-1bace7d622}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Leptodactylus engelsi in row Anf3376, under the genus Leptodactylus, family Leptodactylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page identifies the source row; it does not establish that the row matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Activity periods {#s-bde98a6197}
The row records daytime as an activity category. This summarizes a reported period of activity; it is not a continuous record of behaviour.
