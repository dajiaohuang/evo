## Forearm length in six adult specimens {#plos-morphology}
For 6 adult Molossus fluminensis specimens, the comparative study reports a mean forearm length of 50.66 mm and places it in the study's large size band. This is a small adult sample and one linear measurement; the assigned evidence does not establish sex- or age-related variation or a complete species morphology.

## Mitochondrial clade and skull-size pattern {#plos-mitochondrial-clade}
The study's COI plus cytochrome-b tree places M. fluminensis in a strongly supported clade with M. bondae, M. aztecus, M. sinaloae, M. rufus, M. pretiosus, and M. currentium. The authors also report increasing skull centroid size along the part of this clade containing M. rufus, M. pretiosus, and M. fluminensis. These are results for the sampled taxa and analyses, not a complete species phylogeny or a demonstrated cause of size change.
