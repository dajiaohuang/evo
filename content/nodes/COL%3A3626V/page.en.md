## Form, bracts, and flowers {#s-4019d24087}
One SANBI account describes a corm-bearing herb up to 1.2 m tall, with 5–7 coflorescences. Its bracts measure 10–18 × 4–6 mm and are usually very lightly flecked across the surface, or more heavily flecked at the base and along the midline. The tip of each bract does not reach, or just reaches, the base of the second bract above it. Flowers range from light mauve-pink to bright magenta-pink and are rarely white.

## Diagnostic comparison {#s-e143d47379}
A separate SANBI account describes *D. adelphicum* as solitary-stemmed or tufted, with stems up to 1.2 m, leaves mostly 3–5 mm wide, and terminal spikes bearing 8–10 flowers and 5–7 lateral spikes. Its flowers are described as mauve to magenta, with a perianth 15–21 mm long. The account compares it with *Dierama gracile*: that species is described as clump-forming, with generally shorter stems 300–750 mm tall, 1–5 lateral spikes per stem, and delicately veined bracts bearing only 3 or 4 lateral veins on each side of the main vein.
