## Edmontosaurus NDGS 2000: skin and preservation {#s-332e76aa61}
NDGS 2000 preserves skin draped over bones, punctures and hook-shaped scores, as well as manus anatomy visible in CT scans. Sediment measurements and these traces let researchers propose a history of scavenging-assisted deflation, drying and burial for this single carcass. The preserved skin and damage are observations; the sequence connecting them is a taphonomic explanation, not a universal recipe for dinosaur “mummies.” The specimen does not by itself reveal original colour or physiology.
