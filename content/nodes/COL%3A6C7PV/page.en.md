## Cypella crenata {#s-d1e0180ec5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes Cypella crenata as a bulbous terrestrial plant that may reach 1 m or more. Its keeled leaves are no more than 10 mm wide, and the terminal inflorescence has up to ten branches with stalked rhipidia, each bearing one flower. Flowers are yellow; the entry also gives measurements and colour characters for the filaments, anthers, connective and style crests. The habitat field says only “terrestrial,” so it supplies no locality, survey date or broader ecological account.
