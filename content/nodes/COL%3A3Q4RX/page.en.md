An Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes this source row as direct-developing, with terrestrial oviposition. Direct development means a froglet hatches without a free-living tadpole stage; it does not mean there is no embryonic development or no need for moisture. These are literature-compiled categories, not a new observation or experiment for this species, and their taxonomy has not been certified as identical to the current catalogue concept.
