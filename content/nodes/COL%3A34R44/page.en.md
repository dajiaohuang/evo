## Dendropanax bahiensis {#s-414bc7d5f6}
## Dotted leaf undersides and compact flower clusters

The leaf blades range from elliptic to narrowly elliptic or slightly obovate, with bases and margins recorded in several forms. They are membranous, dotted on the lower surface, and have a conspicuous pair of veins arising above the base; the source lists seven to eleven or more secondary-vein pairs. Flower clusters are compound umbels or umbels arranged in racemes, with up to 30 flowers per umbel. The ovary has five chambers. Fruit length is recorded as equal to or slightly greater than the pedicel, and the styles may remain free or join into a short column. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
