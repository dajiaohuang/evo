## Ruschia aggregata {#s-1082aabe54}
## Tiny magenta petals and a hook-shaped fruit closure

Buys’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a shrub with branches about 360 mm long. The smooth leaves range from round to club-shaped and have a short sheath.

Many flowers grow together in branched clusters, with small bract-like leaves at their base. The magenta petals are only 0.5–1 mm wide; thread-like sterile stamens (staminodes) are absent, and the anthers and pollen are yellow-brown. The five-chambered fruit has a funnel-shaped base, a raised top and low rims. Its covering membranes end in small rods, its closing body is hook-shaped, and the expanding keels nearly meet at the base. The habitat account records gneiss.
