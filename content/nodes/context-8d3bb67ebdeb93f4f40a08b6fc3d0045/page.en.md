## The source concept {#s-3b0737c849}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Cochranella adenocheira* in row Anf0948 under Cochranella, Centrolenidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-3b45074aed}
For Anf0948, the compilation tags aquatic and arboreal as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-13c537c191}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 27.7 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
