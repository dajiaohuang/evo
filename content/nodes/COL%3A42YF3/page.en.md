## Microloma poicilanthum {#s-c2d0669d7a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Microloma poicilanthum* as an accepted species. A regional account describes it either as a climber to 1 m or a shrub to 150 mm. The small, grey-green leaves are finely hairy; clusters carry 1–8 flowers. The bell-shaped corolla is white, with yellow hair patches inside, and fades to yellow and brown. The account records gravelly flats, often with spiny *Ruschia* species, as habitat; it does not establish a complete range.
