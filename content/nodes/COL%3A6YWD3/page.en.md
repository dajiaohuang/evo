## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b248165d0c}
COL26.8 records Sphaerodactylus schwartzi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Sphaerodactylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-0a4de8154d}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 20 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-e0e65f3e13}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-c6eb427706}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
