## What does the regional description record? {#s-21e784651e}
A 1997 regional account describes a shrub up to 30 cm tall, with hairy stems and flower stalks. Its leaves are linear and hairless. The five-part flowers are yellow, lack petals, and occur in stalked flower heads at branch tips.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-3e30aba06d}
The same regional account lists grassland as the habitat; it does not give a locality or survey date.
