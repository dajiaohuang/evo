## Disa conferta {#s-5a53e32fd3}
The South African account characterizes Disa conferta by a slender, beetroot-red plant and minute flowers in a dense raceme. The morphology text calls the spur obsolete, while its diagnostic sentence describes the spurs as sac-like. Flowers are lime-green at opening and the sepals soon turn deep red; the recorded habitat is sandy fynbos slopes.
