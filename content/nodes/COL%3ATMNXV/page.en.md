## Type elevation and the separate distribution summary {#s-fb32bd9678}
The monograph listed male holotype BNHS 6050 from Cona County, Shannan, Xizang, at 370 m. Its distribution summary, citing Mahony and colleagues (2018), included Cona and Medog at 375–410 m. The 370 m holotype value is retained separately from the slightly narrower summary rather than silently corrected. These locality elevations are not physiological limits or proof of continuous occupancy.

## The attributed correction from seven males to six {#s-228810a8f1}
Based on the original description, the diagnosis reported six adult males at 68.0–73.5 mm snout–vent length and one female at 83.9 mm. The remarks explained that one holotype, four paratypes and one referred male agreed with six measurement entries, while a comparison paragraph had stated seven males. The monograph followed the designation and measurements and used n=6. This is its documented resolution of inconsistent source counts; the female remains one measurement, not a range.

## Oral, toe and breeding-male characters {#s-c3c4e8591c}
The cited diagnosis described vomerine ridges and teeth, a mostly smooth dorsum, and small black or white dermal asperities on the lower jaw. Subarticular tubercles were absent; toes had rudimentary basal webbing without lateral fringes. Breeding males had finger I and II nuptial pads with black spines and a subgular vocal sac. These are anatomical observations rather than measured feeding or locomotor performance, and they do not identify a breeding season.
