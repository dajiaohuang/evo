## Manilkara dardanoi Ducke {#s-034ef878e8}
## Seven-locular ovary and one seed

Manilkara dardanoi has stem lenticels but no stipules. Its leathery leaves have a channelled petiole and a grooved midrib above; the upper surface is smooth and the underside hairy. Leaf tips are recorded as sharp, rounded, retuse or notched. Flowers are solitary. The ovary is smooth and has seven chambers; sterile stamens have tips that may be shallowly or deeply two-parted, or irregularly divided. One seed is recorded.
