## Deep arachnid relationships remain contested {#s-035c15e53f}
Phylogenomic studies sample the major arachnid orders but find systematic conflict among data partitions at deep nodes. Dense sampling also recovers Arachnida as paraphyletic in a tested topology, meaning the sampled orders do not form one exclusive branch there. The former 485 Ma older limit is withheld; these results establish neither one settled tree nor a direct ancestor or temporal origin.
