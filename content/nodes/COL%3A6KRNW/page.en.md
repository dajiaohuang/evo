## White-woolly leaf undersides below flower clusters {#s-83ac3ddf23}
The record describes stalked, lance-shaped to oblong-lanceolate leaves with toothed edges and contrasting surfaces: the underside is densely white-woolly. Corymb-like clusters carry radiate heads with 5–8 ray florets and 15–28 disk florets. The smooth, cylindrical fruits retain their pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
