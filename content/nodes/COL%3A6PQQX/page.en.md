## Lessingianthus ammophilus {#s-cfb44214cf}
## Solitary sessile heads rise above leathery leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium, a woody underground stem, and stems that are unbranched or sparsely branched. Leathery, oblanceolate leaves grow along the stem. They have acute tips, heart-shaped bases and entire or toothed, non-rolled edges; the upper face is hairless and the lower is hairless or pubescent.

Solitary, sessile heads have bell-shaped involucres with 5–7 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 or 60 florets. Top-shaped dry fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
