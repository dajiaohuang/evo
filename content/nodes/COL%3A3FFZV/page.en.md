## Woolly leaf undersides on gravelly flats {#s-4b0d7c7588}
The account describes an annual, sometimes up to 30 cm tall, with inversely lance-shaped leaves. Their undersides are woolly, the upper surfaces nearly smooth, and the edges slightly rolled under. Yellow, sometimes orange, radiate heads have a smooth involucre with a collar-like base and pointed inner bracts. Gravelly and sandy flats are listed as habitat.
