## *Pseudobrickellia brasiliensis*: gland-dotted leaves and fringed bracts {#s-5296ff5c56}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records linear leaves with a hairless upper surface and a silky, gland-dotted lower surface. The flower-head stalks are also gland-dotted, and the bracts have sharp tips with finely fringed margins. The anther bases are arrow-shaped, the style branches club-shaped, and the fruitlets have ten ribs. The dataset gives only the broad substrate category “terrestrial.”
