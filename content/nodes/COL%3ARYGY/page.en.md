## Cayaponia ophthalmica R.E.Schult. {#s-b5b679d9e5}
## Branched tendrils and large clustered fruit

Cayaponia ophthalmica has branched tendrils and non-prostrate branches. Its alternate leaves are leathery, and the leaf blade does not run down along the petiole. Bell-shaped flowers are grouped in fascicles. The fruits are also fascicled, yellow to red when mature, and ellipsoid or oblong to obovoid; they measure at least 25 mm long and range from becoming hairless to hairless. Fruit stalks are hairless. Each fruit contains one to three pale or spotted, elliptical to obovate seeds with a fibrous aril.
