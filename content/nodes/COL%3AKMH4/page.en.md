## A woody vine in the Flora of Panama {#s-a4155283d8}
The Flora of Panama describes Barbieria pinnata as a woody vine whose branches may twine, trail, or stand partly upright. Its alternate leaves have 9 to 23 elliptic leaflets; the leaf underside is densely hairy, while the upper surface ranges from smooth to sparsely hairy. Racemes can reach 25 cm, and the flowers are red and up to 5.5 cm long. This is a historical regional description with no stated population sampling frame.

## The source range statement {#s-50035aff4b}
The Flora of Panama distribution field says Barbieria pinnata occurs from Mexico through South America and in the West Indies. This is the wording of that historical source record, not a current range map or a complete global inventory.
