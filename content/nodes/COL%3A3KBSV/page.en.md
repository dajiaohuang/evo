## Heliophila cornuta {#s-c97dd62639}
## Beaded fruits hang from a succulent shrublet

The Greater Cape flora account describes a shrublet up to 1.5 m tall, sometimes minutely hairy below. Its fleshy leaves range from threadlike to linear-oblanceolate, are nearly round in cross-section, and lack stipules. Flowers may be white, mauve, or blue; their petals have appendages at the base, and the ovules number 14–38.

The fruits form a bead-like chain and are 30–100 mm long. The habitat record is stony flats and slopes.
