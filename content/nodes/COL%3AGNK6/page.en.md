## Aristea cuspidata {#s-d7412c45c4}
Aristea cuspidata has a slender, usually unbranched stem with several flower clusters; lower clusters may sometimes have short stalks. The blue flowers are accompanied by spathes that begin green and turn rust-brown, with rough, finely scabrid surfaces. These details distinguish it in the regional account from other Aristea descriptions. It is recorded on grassy and stony slopes. The account characterizes the cited regional material and gives no population frequency.
