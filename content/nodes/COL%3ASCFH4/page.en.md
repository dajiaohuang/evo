## Streamside secondary forest and rainy-night calling {#s-d65dc3802c}
Most frogs were encountered at night on vegetation 0.1–3 m above ground, often near small streams in secondary forest. Calling males were encountered year-round, more frequently on rainy nights. Surveys did not detect frogs where native streamside vegetation had been destroyed. These findings document encounter patterns; they do not establish continuous breeding or a causal habitat-loss response experimentally.
