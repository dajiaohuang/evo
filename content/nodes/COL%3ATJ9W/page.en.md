## A single leaflet pair and yellow flowers {#s-c5adeaff19}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records one pair of oblong-to-ovate leaflets and axillary flower clusters reduced to a few flowers or sometimes one. A nectar gland outside the flower is listed on the petiole. The petals are yellow and the flower has 10 stamens; mature pod valves twist, and the seed is coded as trapezoidal. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial. The regional fields give no sampling frame, locality, observation date, or wild/cultivated status.
