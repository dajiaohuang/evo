## A bent petiole and a flower cluster with distinct zones {#s-7cd9ee2729}
The accepted species name follows Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage 4G8BQ and a matched World Flora Online usage wfo-0000268896. The biological details below come from the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional morphology field.

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Philodendron platypodum with a geniculate (knee-like) petiole and a spadix divided into female, sterile-male, and fertile-male zones. Its ovule and flower counts are recorded as alternatives, while the berry is described as cylindrical and cream-yellow.

## Morphology, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-59586be48c}
The field records clasping roots and a flagelliform, erect stem with short internodes. The leaf sheath is conspicuous; the green petiole is geniculate (bent like a knee). The entire blade has an apiculate tip, a truncate base, and primary lateral veins on both sides. The terminal sympodial inflorescence is listed as one unit; its short peduncle is striate and shorter than the slightly constricted spathe. The spathe tube is light green on both surfaces. The subsessile, decurrent spadix has an oblong female zone, cylindrical sterile- and fertile-male zones, and an obtuse apex.

The record gives 4 or 6 free, long stamens and long staminodes. The gynoecium and ovary are subcylindrical. It lists 4 or 6 locules and 14 or 15 hemianatropous (partly inverted) ovules; hairs are absent from the funicle, the stalk attaching an ovule. Placentation is axial and the stigma discoid. An immature infructescence is noted; its berries are cylindrical and cream-yellow. Seed traits are unknown.
