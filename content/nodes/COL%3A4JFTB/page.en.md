## Four substrates recorded in the flora source {#s-30d1033980}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record includes *Corticícola*, meaning growth on bark.

It also lists *Epixila*, growth on decaying wood.

Another category is *Rupícola*, meaning growth on rock.

The fourth is *Terrícola*, the dataset's terrestrial category.

These are habitat or substrate labels rather than collection localities. The source field gives no survey date, range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Limits of the available record {#s-62e21f479d}
The field does not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

These habitat labels do not describe life cycle or reproduction; those were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
