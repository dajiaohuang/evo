## Pauridia gracilipes {#s-baa4543efd}
The regional flora account describes Pauridia gracilipes as a cormous geophyte 3–22 cm tall, with twisted roots covering the corm and three to nine channelled leaves. Each flowering stem bears one yellow, star-shaped flower with green-backed tepals and an inconspicuous bract. It grows in wet rock crevices on sandstone or granite. The account also contrasts it with broad-leaved P. ovata, whose flowers are white with reddish backs; these are regional diagnostic observations.
