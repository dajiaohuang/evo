## Diplodocid names follow a specimen-level revision {#s-5d0ae5b974}
Tschopp and colleagues’ specimen-level matrix and explicit revision criteria determine the operational genus and species taxonomy represented here. The 168–125 Ma interval is a broad stage envelope of that study’s diplodocid sample, not continuous occupancy, exact family boundaries, divergence date, global first or last appearance, or direct ancestry.
