## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-dfd42f5296}
COL26.8 records Brachyurophis campbelli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-3e0d7e171b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cogger 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-425e698cc0}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Wilson and Heinsohn 2007, Wilson et al. 2006b, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009.
