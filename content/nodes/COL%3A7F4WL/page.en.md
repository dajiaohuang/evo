## What range of trap and flower forms is retained? {#s-bb9320e237}
Utricularia rostrata is described with linear leaves and ovoid bladder traps that open at the side. The traps may bear simple or forked appendages on the back or underside. The flowers are recorded in several colors—white, purple, violet or pink—with a bulging yellow-to-orange throat. The spur may be shorter than or equal to the lower lip, and its tip may split into two or four points. These are source alternatives, not one combined form.

## What habitat category does the record give? {#s-ce0eff6a55}
The habitat field lists Utricularia rostrata as terrestrial. No locality or complete range accompanies that label.
