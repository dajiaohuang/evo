## A dense shrublet with narrow winged branches {#s-381016b0da}
This account describes a dense shrublet up to 1 m tall, with angled or narrowly winged branches. Its linear-lanceolate leaves have stipules. The flowers may be white, mauve or blue, and each ovary contains 4–8 ovules. The erect, rough fruits are pod-like and 8–28 mm long. Sandstone slopes are recorded as habitat.
