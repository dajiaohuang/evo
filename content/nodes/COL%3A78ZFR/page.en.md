## Salacia maburensis {#s-93ac49fd80}
## Flowers grow directly from the stem

Salacia maburensis has erect stems with subcylindrical young branches and opposite, leathery elliptic leaves. The lower leaf surface turns brown when dry, with raised secondary veins. Hairless flower clusters grow directly from older stems; their flowers have a wheel-shaped corolla, two-loculed anthers and an unlobed stigma. Each ovary chamber has two ovules. The fruit is ellipsoid, and habitat is labeled terrestrial.
