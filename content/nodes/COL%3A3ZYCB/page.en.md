## Mesembryanthemum bicorne {#s-3a12f61065}
## White flowers rise from long trailing shoots

The Greater Cape flora account describes a perennial with slender, rounded leaves and green, succulent stems that persist at least in part. The plant lies low, with a central cluster of short branches and long, trailing flowering shoots. It bears a few white flowers, and the fruit has five chambers.

The habitat account characterizes it as a pioneer, mostly on disturbed sites.
