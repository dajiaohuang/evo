## What makes its short stems unusual? {#s-fb104e5345}
A regional flora account describes *Restio anomalus* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 25 cm. Its branching culms, or stems, are finely warty, and the leaf sheaths are tightly rolled with pointed tips. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
