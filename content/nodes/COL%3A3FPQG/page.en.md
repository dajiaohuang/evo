## Catalogue placement {#s-53d6892417}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 lists *Geoffroea spinosa* as an accepted species in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-f6545d9048}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrestrial. This is a habitat category only; it gives no locality, range or sampling coverage.
