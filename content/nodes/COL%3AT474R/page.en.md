## Two newly scanned skeletons constrain comparisons {#s-e92a503e68}
The study provides full-body microCT imagery of male holotypeIRSNB4208 and female paratypeIRSNB4210. It reports no unambiguous full-skeleton difference from imawari and upuigmae, despite some proposed cranial diagnostic characters. These two preserved specimens do not describe all ages or population variability. Decalcification and sutures affect how contact or fusion is interpreted, and a visually similar skeleton does not prove identical species identity.

## Stripes and cranial processes belong to a comparison context {#s-367c16b128}
The diagnoses describe white dorsolateral stripes in lathropae, sometimes short, and a longer vomer postchoanal process reaching the neopalatine. Those contrasts help compare the described species but are not independently certified universal characters. A thumb nuptial-excrescence photograph supplies a male anatomical example, not a new mating or care-duration experiment.
