## Flowers on the trunk and a long waxy fruit {#s-b710ed9108}
The Flora of Panama describes *Parmentiera cereifera* as a small tree reaching 7 m. Its opposite leaves have three leaflets. Flowers, with a musky odor, grow singly or in small groups on short shoots of large branches or the trunk—a pattern called cauliflory. The corolla is pure white and tubular, 3.7–6.4 cm long. The elongated fruit reaches 39–54 cm; the account describes it as waxy yellow, with a fleshy portion around the seeds and a fibrous central core. This regional flora description gives no observation date or population frequency.

## Natural habitat and cultivation in the flora account {#s-35abaab058}
The Flora of Panama distribution field says the species is naturally restricted to tropical moist forest on the Atlantic slope of Central Panama, while also widely cultivated as a curiosity throughout the tropics. This is the wording of a historical regional source, not a current global distribution assessment.
