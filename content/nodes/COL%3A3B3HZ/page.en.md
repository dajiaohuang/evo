## *Eriocephalus glandulosus*: a spiny shrub with two kinds of florets {#s-0b9c2b6d1b}
The South African regional account describes a rounded, much-branched shrub about 0.2–0.6 m tall and across, with rigid, spine-tipped branches. Its tiny leaves overlap in four dense rows along the stems. They are glossy green, sometimes tinged red-purple, and bear glands in small surface pits.

Each small flower head has two white female florets at the margin and 10–18 purple central florets that function as male. The account records flowering in July–October in western winter-rainfall areas and February–March in eastern summer-rainfall areas. It lists several veld types, including Kalahari Thornveld and Shrub Bushveld, Orange River Broken Veld, and Arid Karoo and Desert False Grassveld; these are regional habitat records, not a complete range statement.
