## Syagrus loefgrenii: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-fb6fa3fbbf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes stems with remnants of leaf sheaths and lists solitary, clumping, subterranean and very short forms. Leaves are green and pinnate, with persistent, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is erect or slightly arched. Pinnae are leathery, rigid and grouped, inserted at different angles; their tips are asymmetrically acuminate. They are dark green and discolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is pruinose or tomentose; its rachis is sparsely tomentose, and the rachillae are arranged on one side. Fruits are ovoid or elliptic. The epicarp is yellow-green and tomentose; the mesocarp is fibrous and succulent; the endocarp is woody, fusiform or oval; and the endosperm is homogeneous.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
