## *Xyris obscura*: yellow flowers on a compact rhizome {#s-64557d6c75}
This perennial herb reaches 30 cm and often has a slightly bulb-like base with twisted, fibrous coverings. Its narrow leaves cluster on compact rhizomes. The few-flowered spikes have fairly uniform bracts, and the flowers are yellow.

The habitat account lists grassland among boulders, sand and gravel, as well as swamps; it does not describe a single substrate.
