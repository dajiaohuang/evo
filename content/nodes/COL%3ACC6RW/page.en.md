## A species described from two collections {#s-bcd0cbcf48}
COL26.8 accepts *Tatraea griseoturcoisina* C.J.Y. Li & Q. Zhao in Helotiaceae. Index Fungorum record 901179 matches the name and identifier printed in the cited species paper; this exact name link does not settle all broader concept comparisons. The paper describes its apothecia (disc-shaped fungal structures) as scattered or grouped, superficial discs with a short stalk; fresh discs are greyish turquoise in wet habitat and change as they dry. Fresh apothecia measured 2.5–4.0 mm wide (mean 3.1 ± 0.4 mm, n=27); dry ones measured 1.0–2.1 × 0.6–0.8 mm (mean 1.6 ± 0.3 × 0.7 ± 0.1 mm, n=20). Both collections came from decayed oak branches in a managed plantation in Menghai County, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan, China, at 1660 m and 1500 m on 2022-09-08. The authors describe the fungus as saprobic on decayed branches; these two collections are study localities, not a global range. In the combined LSU and ITS analysis, it grouped with T. clepsydriformis (85% maximum-likelihood bootstrap; 0.96 Bayesian posterior probability). A five-locus PHI recombination comparison found no significant recombination between the sampled groups (Φw=0.4185). These results apply to the reported collections and analyses.
