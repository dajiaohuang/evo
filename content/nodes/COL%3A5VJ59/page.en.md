## Form in the flora record {#s-cd8cb611ba}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem, with cataphylls and prophylls entire at the tip and fibrous at the base. Petioles are smooth; the field records flattened or grooved forms, with obtuse or acute margins in the grooved forms. The flat, entire leaf blade is elliptic or ovate, with an apiculate tip and an acute, blunt, or rounded base. Glandular dots are present on both surfaces. The primary veins are blunt on both surfaces or acute above and blunt below; secondary veins are prominent on the upper surface, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is shorter than half the leaf length and has a cylindrical peduncle. The flat, ovate spathe meets the peduncle at a right angle. A stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. The berry is wine-colored at the tip and white or wine-colored at the base.

The regional dataset row cites Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
