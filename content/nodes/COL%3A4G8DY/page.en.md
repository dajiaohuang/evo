## Morphology, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-bbdcc1184e}
The field notes feeding roots and a wine-coloured prophyll. The leaf sheath and petiole are conspicuous; the green petiole is purple near its tip. The blade is entire and sagittate or ovate, with a sinuate margin; “7–11” follows the blade descriptors without an explicit count target in the structured text. The axillary sympodial inflorescence has a short green or wine-coloured peduncle, shorter than the slightly constricted spathe. The spadix is subsessile.

The record lists 2, 3, or 4 stamens and short staminodes. The gynoecium and ovary are ovoid; the ovary has 8, 9, or 10 locules and 4 or 5 ovules, with subbasal placentation. The stigma, berry, and seed characters are marked unknown.
