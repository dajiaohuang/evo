## Accepted name and placement {#s-ef5ece337f}
COL26.8 accepts Amolops dafangensis Li, Liu, Ke, Cheng & Wang, 2024 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Amolops (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A 2025 proposal for the Wumeng population {#s-df7bf2acb8}
The 2025 paper named the Wumeng population Amolops dafangensis wumengmontis as a new subspecies. Its mitochondrial tree placed this population within A. dafangensis, whereas CO1-based ASAP delimitation favoured species separation. The authors chose subspecies rank to avoid excessive splitting. This page records that dated proposal under the pinned species identity; it does not create a separately accepted COL species or independently certify the proposed rank.

## A four-specimen type series and variable coloration {#s-3ec6d5548f}
The proposed subspecies diagnosis gives three adult males with snout–vent length 43.0–45.5 mm and one adult female at 62.1 mm. It describes vomerine teeth, an indistinct tympanum, glandular dorsolateral folds, no male vocal sacs and white-spined nuptial pads on finger I. Living specimens varied in colour. These four types describe this named population, not the size limits of all A. dafangensis or a statistically tested sexual size difference.

## Locality evidence retains collection-date disagreement {#s-100ef62ab8}
The account reports streams above 1500 m in Yongshan and Yiliang counties of the Wumeng Mountains, with type localities at 1915 and 1797 m. Methods say six animals were collected in August 2022 and November 2023, with four retained vouchers and two released after sampling. The type account instead dates the holotype and female paratype to July 2021. These dates are inconsistent in the source and remain unresolved. Named sites are not proof of continuous occupancy or a complete contemporary range.

## July nuptial pads support only a breeding hypothesis {#s-635f248388}
The natural-history paragraph reports nuptial pads in July males but no tadpoles or amplexus. The authors interpreted this as an early breeding stage. Nuptial pads alone do not establish the onset, duration or success of breeding. The same paragraph reports sympatry with Leptobrachium boringii and Odorrana margaretae; co-occurrence does not demonstrate predation, diet or competition.

## Two mitochondrial markers and an untested barrier hypothesis {#s-8b12db3dde}
The combined 16S–CO1 alignment was 1094 base pairs; reported Wumeng versus type-locality distances were 0.5% for 16S and 2.8% for CO1. ASAP used CO1 alone because 16S differences were considered too small for this analysis. The suggestion that the Wumeng Mountains restricted gene flow is a hypothesis, not a direct gene-flow measurement. These marker distances are not universal species boundaries, divergence dates or nuclear-genome evidence.

## Three adult males and two juveniles {#s-54454d4721}
The type series comprises three adult males and two juveniles collected in Dafang County, Guizhou, on 1 June 2023. Adult male snout–vent length was 43.2–46.8 mm. The diagnosis includes a distinct oval tympanum, vomerine teeth and weak glandular dorsolateral lines; male vocal sacs were absent. No adult female size was reported for this series.

## A restricted field sample {#s-5a7dcfda4e}
Five surveys during 2020–2023 found these five specimens only in June 2023, within approximately 100 m of a stream. Frogs were observed on vegetation beside a mountain stream. The natural-history account gives approximately 1600 m elevation, whereas the holotype locality gives 1300 m; that internal discrepancy is retained as unresolved. The observations do not measure total population size or establish the complete range.

## Evidence still missing {#s-b085b4bb47}
Males had distinct nuptial pads, leading the authors to suggest breeding in early June. They found no eggs, tadpoles or females and recorded no advertisement calls. Early June is therefore a hypothesis based on the survey, not a demonstrated breeding season.
