## Socratea exorrhiza {#s-e47174b2e4}
Socratea exorrhiza is described with a solitary stem 10–20 m tall, tubular leaf sheaths 1–1.5 m long and leaves divided into multiple segments per leaflet. Each side has 15–25 leaflets; the branched inflorescence bears 12–17 smaller branches. Habitat is recorded as terrestrial.
