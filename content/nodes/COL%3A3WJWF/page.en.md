## Blunt or rounded leaves and a pale outer pappus {#s-66919fd345}
The morphology record notes fibrous roots and leaves along the stem, with veins that curve and connect near the margins. Leaf tips may be blunt, rounded or mucronate. Flower clusters contain three or four florets, sometimes five or more; the fruits have a pale, scale-like outer pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
