## Flowers and an uncertain species boundary {#s-68cd588ab5}
A SANBI regional description calls M. flanaganii a dwarf epiphyte, a plant that grows attached to another plant for support. Its pale-green flowers are up to 10 mm across, with slender 20–30 mm spurs (narrow projections from the flower). Another cited account says it cannot be separated satisfactorily from M. pusillum: it uses flowering season to distinguish the forms, but calls that split superficial and in need of careful study.

## A cool, moist forest record {#s-763918a7b3}
A regional SANBI habitat entry places the species in cool, moist forests. This is a habitat description, not a precise collection locality or a measure of the species’ full range.
