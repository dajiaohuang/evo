## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-81fb6b3da9}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field describes hairless upper branches. Leaves are obovate to oblanceolate, with a narrowly wedge-shaped base and an acute or rounded tip; they are leathery or nearly leathery, with pinnate loops of secondary veins. The field allows sparse hairs or a nearly hairless surface, records small hair tufts in some vein axils beneath the leaves, and says glands are absent. Axillary spikes are elongated and bear bisexual flowers. The lower floral cup is hairy and the upper part is cup-shaped; the calyx lobes are inconspicuous. There are ten stamens in two whorls, and the style is hairless. The fruit is recorded without wings. The dataset does not provide a global description or a sampling frame.
