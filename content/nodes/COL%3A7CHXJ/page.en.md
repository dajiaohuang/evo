## Tontelea laxiflora {#s-83dcc05b62}
## Loose axillary clusters and a three-lobed stigma

Tontelea laxiflora is recorded with erect or climbing stems, opposite elliptic leaves and triangular stipules. Loose axillary inflorescences have smooth stalks and opposite branches; the branch cross-sections vary from flattened to nearly cylindrical or grooved. Each flower has three stigma lobes, each split or notched at the tip, and two ovules per chamber. The cylindrical fruit is three-lobed when immature and develops a crusty outer layer. Habitat is labeled terrestrial.
