## Accepted name and classification {#s-ab6193a267}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Amolops tonkinensis* (Ahl, 1927) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Amolops (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## Traits in the published group comparison {#s-76ab280d35}
The 2023 description of *Amolops ailao* places *A. tonkinensis* in its *ricketti* species-group comparison. Earlier descriptions it reviews report a groove on the first-finger disc, no dorsolateral glandular folds (raised folds along the back’s sides), and conical or papilla-shaped nuptial spines (spines associated with breeding). These are literature-derived contrasts, not new measurements of *A. tonkinensis*; historical group placement alone does not settle current species limits.
