## A small annual in stony cracks {#s-154a259178}
The account describes a soft, sparsely hairy annual up to 29 cm tall, with narrow lance-shaped leaves 10–40 mm long. Small flowers are white, drying cream-colored; the petals have basal appendages. The inflated, round fruits are 2–3 mm across and contain two ovules. It grows in stony soil and rock cracks on south-facing slopes.
