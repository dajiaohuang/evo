## Two branches in a sampled cynodont tree {#s-4541b8d9d5}
A revised cynodont matrix recovers Probainognathia as one of the two principal sampled eucynodont branches. The analysis also reports lower early rates than in Cynognathia, offering a comparison within that matrix rather than a direct account of mammal ancestry.

## The range is matrix coverage {#s-7a5f2e29e4}
The displayed 237–174.7 million-year interval describes the sampled matrix coverage. It does not mark a clade origin or extinction, and the tree does not establish a direct lineage from Probainognathia to mammals.
