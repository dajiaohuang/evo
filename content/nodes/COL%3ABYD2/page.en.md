## Allophylus dioicus {#s-0fe71903e2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes one-leaflet leaves, an unbranched inflorescence and a four-lobed concentric nectar disc; the dried pericarp colour is explicitly unknown.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-4cf4a51bb6}
Leaves are unifoliolate (with one leaflet); lateral leaflets are recorded as absent, and the central leaflet is sessile with a symmetrical, leathery blade. The blades are concolorous, do not dry black, and have entire margins. Their lower surface is glabrous to nearly glabrous, and upper-surface venation is described as prominent and brochidodromous, with curved secondary veins; domatia are absent. The inflorescence is unbranched. The androgynophore is absent; the nectar disc is four-lobed and concentric. Sepal undersides are recorded as sparsely to densely pubescent. The colour of the pericarp when dry is unknown in this source field.

## Habitat field {#s-c8b93085d2}
The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality, survey date, global range or sampling-completeness information.
