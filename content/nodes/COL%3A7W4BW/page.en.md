## Ochotona (Pika) coreana: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Ochotona (Pika) coreana* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Allen & Andrews, 1913.
