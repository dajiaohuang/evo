## The source concept {#s-00486b3777}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Anaxyrus terrestris* as row Anf0414, in the genus Anaxyrus, family Bufonidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-34e5db5d69}
The row records fossorial (Fos = 1) and terrestrial (Ter = 1) as broad foraging categories. Fossorial means associated with burrowing. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Seasonal activity {#s-acf988b0db}
The row records wet and warm (Wet_warm = 1) as a seasonal activity category. AmphiBIO relates activity conditions to the local annual climate, so these labels do not correspond to fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-183f666640}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 33.25 g and maximum adult body length of 116.3 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass or microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not been checked against that revision.

## Compiled life history {#s-384f3e0e5d}
The row records 4000 as the minimum and 4000 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch. The row records minimum and maximum offspring or egg sizes of 1.2 and 1.4 mm. The maximum lifespan listed is 10 years. The maximum reproductive output listed is 1 event per year. These are literature-compiled extrema or counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-d1ad07c4ef}
The row records larval stage (Lar = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
