## Ecological evidence {#s-4bde4cdf0f}
A short post-release study at Mehao Wildlife Sanctuary in Arunachal Pradesh, India, recorded feeding by three Eastern hoolock gibbon family groups. Fruits and flowers were observed in the two Sally Lake groups; leaves and animal matter were also recorded for the 20 Kilo group. Each group was monitored for about two months. These observations concern rescued and released groups, not the species’ usual or range-wide diet.

## Evidence limits {#s-aca36f0e12}
Source-linked introduction;. Evidence is limited to three post-release groups at one sanctuary and a short observation period, not wild-born groups or a general species diet.
