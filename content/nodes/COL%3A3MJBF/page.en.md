## Feeding in three post-release family groups {#s-4bde4cdf0f}
A short post-release study at Mehao Wildlife Sanctuary, Arunachal Pradesh, India, recorded feeding by 3 Eastern hoolock gibbon family groups from February to May 2012. Fruits and flowers were observed in the 2 Sally Lake groups; leaves and animal matter were also recorded for the 20 Kilo group. Each group was monitored for about 2 months. These observations describe rescued and released groups during a preliminary survey, not the species' usual or range-wide diet.

## Evidence limits {#s-aca36f0e12}
Evidence is limited to three post-release groups at one sanctuary and a short observation period, not wild-born groups or a general species diet.
