## What leaf and fruit traits mark Duguetia dicholepidota? {#s-37739a7768}
The source records elliptic to egg-shaped leaves. Scale-like hairs on the upper surface vary from absent to dense; the underside also ranges from sparsely to densely scaled. Yellow or greenish flowers occur in clusters. The green, rounded fruit has a basal collar and dense star-shaped or scale-like hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-5fa6847dc5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field categorizes it as terrestrial; no site is named.
