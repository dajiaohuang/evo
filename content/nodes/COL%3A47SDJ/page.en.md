## What growth form and stamen traits does the flora record? {#s-82a25cdd86}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes an erect, unbranched stem with hairs and a basal rosette of leaves. The floral cup is hairy. Flowers have five stamens and staminodes, sterile stamens alongside them. The anther tips are blunt. This is a regional database description and gives no sampling frame or broader variation.
