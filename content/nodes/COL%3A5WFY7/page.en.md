## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the flora description {#s-b43075ab19}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records unarmed branches. The leaf blade has an entire edge and a somewhat leathery texture (subcoriaceous), a heart-shaped base and a rounded tip. Its upper surface is described as becoming hairless or hairless, with sunken veins; the lower surface has coarse reddish hairs and prominent secondary and tertiary veins. The flower-bud field includes the numeral 5 and describes a narrow wing, a club-shaped form and wavy wings; it does not clarify what the number counts. The floral cup (hypanthium) is urn-shaped; the calyx splits into two to five lobes as the flower opens. Petals are linear. The source records ten anthers, a staminal column ranging from bearded to hairy, and a stalked ovary. The fruit is a legume recorded as opening elastically. This is one regional dataset description and does not assess variation or distribution across the species.
