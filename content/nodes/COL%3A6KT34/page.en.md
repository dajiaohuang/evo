## Grindelia pulchella {#s-5447e9073b}
## Yellow rays on solitary flower heads

The Brazilian Flora description records a simple axial root and a woody, branched stem with glandular hairs. Sessile leaves are linear or elliptic with toothed margins. Solitary radiate heads have yellow, three-lobed female ray florets and five-toothed bisexual tubular disc florets. The compressed cypselae are glabrous. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
