## Trattinnickia mensalis {#s-b157c51557}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records “Terrícola” as the habitat category for *Trattinnickia mensalis*.

## A habitat category, not a locality {#s-937d1ea891}
The source field places the species in the terrestrial category. It does not supply a collection locality or survey date, and it cannot establish a complete distribution.
