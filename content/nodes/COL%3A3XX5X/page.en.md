## What are the flowers and leaves like? {#s-79e79c26f7}
Manulea gariepina is described as an upright annual 7–36 cm tall. Its basal leaves are oblanceolate to elliptic and bear glandular hairs. Flowers grow in racemes or loose, branched clusters. The corolla tube is 5–7 mm long and often blackish violet; the narrow lobes range from cream and yellow to brown or reddish brown, with their margins strongly rolled back.

## What substrate is reported? {#s-cbd9416a47}
The habitat field places it in sand, sometimes over calcrete, a calcium-rich hardpan. No collection site is specified.
