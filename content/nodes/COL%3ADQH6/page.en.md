## Andira ormosioides {#s-965f6e6ee7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Andira ormosioides* records 3–5 leaflet pairs, purple-to-pink petals, hairy ovary, brown fibrous fruits. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
