## Trichilia lecointei {#s-dfc022732e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes heteromorphic basal leaflets and contrasts free or united petals with a fully united staminal column.

## Heteromorphic leaflets and united staminal filaments {#s-6f12f12b3a}
The stem-bud field records no cataphylls and simple hairs. Leaves may be odd- or even-pinnate, with an unwinged rachis and alternate or opposite leaflets; basal leaflets are heteromorphic. Their bases are asymmetric, acute, obtuse or truncate, and the lower surface may be hairy or glabrous. Petals are free or united and valvate in bud. Staminal filaments are fully united; the nectary is absent. The ovary is pubescent, three-locular and has two collateral ovules per locule. Fruits are oblong or obovoid and silky. A separate habitat category is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), with no locality or complete-range information.
