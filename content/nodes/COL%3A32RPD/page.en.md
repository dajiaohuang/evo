## Cybianthus cuyabensis Mez {#s-93eedb054a}
## Obovate leaves and four-parted flowers

Cybianthus cuyabensis has cylindrical terminal shoots and obovate, leathery leaves with acuminate tips and acute bases. Curved secondary veins are visible beneath. The inflorescence is a raceme, though the source does not resolve its position; leaf arrangement is also unknown. The corolla has four parts and a wheel-shaped form, with pointed lobes. Staminate flowers are stalked and have threadlike filaments; pistillate flowers are unknown. The immature fruit wall is also unrecorded.
