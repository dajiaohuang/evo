## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Hypericum ternum? {#s-df887382d8}
Side branches occur near the stem tip. Opposite leaf pairs cross at right angles; the blades may be rolled inward or flat, with unthickened edges and mainly lengthwise veins. Terminal cymose clusters and either cymose or solitary lateral flowers are recorded. Flowers exceed 7 mm in diameter. Sepals range from unequal to nearly equal; petals are asymmetrical. The stamens form a continuous ring, with no elongated connective, and there are three styles. The capsule is spherical. Brown seeds have ladder-like ridges on the coat.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-90d20059df}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
