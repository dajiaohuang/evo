## The source concept {#s-4e4797a56c}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Duttaphrynus brevirostris* in row Anf0576 under Duttaphrynus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.
