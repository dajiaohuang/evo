## What leaf and flower-cluster traits are recorded? {#s-e6555471d0}
The branches lie along the ground. Petiolate leaves have linear, sickle-shaped stipules and triangular blades divided into three to five lobes. The blades are uniformly colored, heart-shaped at the base, pointed at the tip and scalloped at the edge; simple hairs occur on the upper surface. Paired axillary scorpioid cymes bear the flowers. The calyx cup is under 1 cm long and bristly. More than 15 stigmas are recorded, and the fruit segments have no awns.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-efb542c4c1}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
