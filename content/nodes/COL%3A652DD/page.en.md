## *Adenostemma fosbergii*: erect stems and ovate leaves {#s-c310b7004a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records erect branches and ovate leaves with either smooth or scalloped margins. The corolla is described as having a short limb covered in long, coarse hairs; the style branches are cylindrical near their tips. Its substrate entry is the broad category “terrestrial,” with no locality or fuller habitat description.
