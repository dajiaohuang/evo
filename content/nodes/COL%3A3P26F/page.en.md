## Leaves, flowers, and season {#s-6e7beceefb}
Singh's 2019 regional account describes a dwarf perennial geophyte, similar to *H. angustifolia*. It has 3–5 leaves. Usually there is 1 flower per stalk, seldom 2; flowers may be yellow or white. The account gives a flowering period from November to April.

These observations come from a regional species account published in 2019; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-59afb479d6}
The account records the species on grassy slopes and rocky outcrops, and names the Drakensberg Grassland and Sub-Escarpment Grassland.
