## What does the account say about Manulea paucibarbata? {#s-5395534e5d}
This glandular-hairy annual can reach 40 cm. Its toothed leaves accompany loose racemes of flowers with a narrow funnel-shaped corolla tube. The corolla ranges from white to lilac, with a yellow tube.

## Which traits separate it from related species? {#s-f7f485a3ff}
Compared with M. annua, the source notes sparse, shorter hairs around the corolla mouth, looser flower clusters, usually longer stalks, larger flowers, and hairless filaments. It cautions that the type material has a short 4.75 mm tube, but those flowers were young or reduced. Compared with M. arabidea, its flowers are less crowded and larger, and the flower cluster axis is more flexuous.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-2b9061f94e}
The habitat field records sandy or stony slopes, without naming a specific site.
