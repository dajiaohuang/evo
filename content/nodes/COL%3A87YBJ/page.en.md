## Begonia capanemae {#s-c07e69f5d9}
## Scaly hairs cover the divided leaves

Begonia capanemae has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its strongly asymmetric, transversely ovate leaves are lobed or divided, with toothed edges and scale-shaped hairs on both surfaces and the petioles. The male and female flower segments also bear scales; female margins are toothed or ciliate, and bracteoles are present. The capsule wings are strongly unequal, while the seed tip is rounded. Habitat is labeled terrestrial.
