## What does the description record for Annona hayesii? {#s-311f34e66a}
This high shrub or small tree has densely rust-colored, silky-hairy young branches. Its thin leaves are obovate or elliptic, abruptly tapering at the tip. Flowers occur singly or in small groups, opposite leaves or along the stem between them; the rusty flower stalks are densely hairy. The fruit is smooth, nearly spherical, and at least 5 cm long.

## What distribution does the source report? {#s-64907303ad}
The source says Annona hayesii was found only in Panama. This is the distribution statement in the cited account.
