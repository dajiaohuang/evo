## Recorded parent of Ankylosaurinae {#s-ff80b300d9}
Ankylosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Ankylosauridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
