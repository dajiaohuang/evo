## overview {#s-d3ed198232}
COL26.8 records Acrocodia Goldman, 1913 at genus rank, with Tapiridae Gray, 1821 as its direct parent.

This catalogue places the Malayan tapir in this genus. The biological account uses Tapirus indicus. The adult black-and-white body pattern characterizes this species rather than all tapirs.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-1431519f73}
Source-linked introductory account;. Quantitative traits, complete ranges, divergence dates and current conservation assessments require separate evidence. Catalogue counts are not counts of living wild biological species.
