## What ranges are recorded for its leaves and flowers? {#s-47e0b36865}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records pinnatisect leaves—deeply cut along the midrib—with rough, bristly surfaces and width over 5 mm. Length is listed as either up to 13 mm or greater than 14 mm, so the entry preserves alternatives rather than one fixed size. Flower stalks reach 4 mm, the corolla tube up to 14 mm, and the calyx lobes exceed 3 mm; the corolla is yellow.

## One habitat label: aquatic {#s-d39bc62a57}
One Brazilian Flora 2020 field categorizes Agalinis glandulosa as aquatic. It gives no water body or collection locality.

## Another label: hemiparasitic {#s-7de62f7fd9}
A separate field records the species as hemiparasitic. No host plant is named in this category entry.

## A third label: terrestrial {#s-4d298c5763}
The dataset also lists a terrestrial category. The three source labels are retained separately; no site-specific explanation accompanies them.
