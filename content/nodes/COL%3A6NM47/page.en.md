## Kniphofia linearifolia {#s-70821dca04}
Kniphofia linearifolia is a tall perennial with a flower stalk that may reach 1.5 m and a large, oblong to rhomboid cluster. Its long flowers are yellow to greenish yellow; buds range from pink-red to green-tinged dull red. The regional account notes intermediate plants where ranges overlap with close relatives. Mountain grassland, marshes and stream banks are recorded habitats.
