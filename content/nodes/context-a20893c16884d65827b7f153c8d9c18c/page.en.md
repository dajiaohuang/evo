## The source concept {#s-2df4d624ce}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Ansonia echinata* in row Anf0420 under Ansonia, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-7f8f2eb1ee}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 21.3 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
