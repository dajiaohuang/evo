## A brown dorsum and male vocal sac {#s-34e9889ee6}
Microkayla huayna is a direct-developing frog described from Bolivia's Cordillera Real. The authors identify M. teqta as its close relative and distinguish it by brown dorsal skin and a large dark-brown male vocal sac; they characterize its known range as small.
