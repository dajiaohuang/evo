This age belongs to the fossil-bearing unit, not a direct radiometric date on the skull or a genus-wide range. ## Limits of the selected occurrence route {#s-cf189dc94c}
The PBDB path is a navigation classification. Its selected occurrence cannot establish a global first or last appearance, a complete genus range or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence rows and collections are database record units, not counts of specimens or populations. Keep this path distinct from COL classification and study phylogenies.
