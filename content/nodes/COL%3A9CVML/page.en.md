## Ancistrotropis firmula {#s-f3c35f9041}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Ancistrotropis firmula* records upright growth, leathery hairless leaflets with notched tips, crowded inflorescence axis, flowers under 2 cm. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
