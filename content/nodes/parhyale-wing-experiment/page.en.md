## Parhyale experiments test leg-patterning genes {#s-519644ef8a}
This record concerns living *Parhyale hawaiensis*: CRISPR knockouts and comparative expression tests examine leg-patterning genes, including proximal leg and body-wall structures, alongside published insect phenotypes. Its 0 Ma marker denotes modern experimental animals, not a fossil, an insect-wing origin date or the species’ full temporal range.
