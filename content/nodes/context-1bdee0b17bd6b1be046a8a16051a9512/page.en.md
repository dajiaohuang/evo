## The source concept {#s-487caa7620}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Nanorana chayuensis as row Anf1740. Its source classification is genus Nanorana, family Dicroglossidae and order Anura. Its species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this archived record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2946a286c5}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 80 mm. For Anura, length is snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, total length is used. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
