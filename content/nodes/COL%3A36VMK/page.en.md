## A coastal mat that roots at its nodes {#s-d23d9b413f}
The record describes a mat-forming perennial that roots at its nodes. Its smooth, dark-green leaves are three-angled, 25–35 mm long, and dotted with translucent marks. One to three terminal flowers are white to rose-red and about 40 mm across. The five-chambered fruits are pale yellow and spongy. It grows on coastal rocks just above the high-tide mark.
