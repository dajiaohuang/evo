## A tuberous plant with crowded, divided leaves {#s-da0aa0b425}
This account describes a short-stemmed perennial that grows from a turnip-shaped tuber, with felted hairs near the root tip. Its leaves crowd around the short stem and have wedge-shaped, lyrate blades that narrow into a stalk-like base; the edges are coarsely toothed or wavy and cut. Yellow radiate heads grow on branched stalks up to 20 cm long. The fruit is hairy and bears a pappus. It grows on stony flats.
