## Maranta sobolifera {#s-edd636eccf}
## A hairy ovary and persistent sepals

The recorded leaf blades are ovate or oblong, and the pulvinus has sparse hairs on its upper side. The bracts are loosely arranged. Its straight corolla tube is shorter than the sepals; the ovary is hairy but has a smooth surface. One staminode callus is noted. The fruit is ellipsoid and the sepals persist upright; the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
