## Form described in the flora record {#s-110144d662}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem; cataphyll and prophyll characters are not specified. Petioles (leaf stalks) are cylindrical and smooth. Leaf blades are entire (undivided, with unbroken margins), flat, and ovate, with an acute tip and a heart-shaped base; glandular dots are absent. No primary-vein character is recorded. Secondary veins are prominent on the upper side, and a collecting vein (running along the leaf margin) is present along the full upper edge.

The field does not characterize inflorescence size or the peduncle (inflorescence stalk). The spathe (floral bract) is flat and elliptic, but its angle to the peduncle is not specified. The stipe (supporting stalk) is not characterized. Berry color is not specified.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
