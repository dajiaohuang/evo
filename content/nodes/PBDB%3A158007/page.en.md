## Limits of the selected occurrence {#s-bccd675f25}
This guide follows a selected occurrence record, not a complete genus catalogue. One occurrence from one collection cannot establish a genus-wide age range or population size. The PBDB parent chain is a classification path and does not show that Junggarsuchus was a direct ancestor of crocodilians.
