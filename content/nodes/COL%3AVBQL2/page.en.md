## Evidence limits {#s-c4449f5c74}
The PanTHERIA and life-history routes are selected historical entries, not complete descendant lists; the latter also retains source averages, longest-record fields, and possible inherited data. EltonTraits records are literature-based categories: food weights are coarse relative estimates, and ABC fields can include a related species rather than direct observations of the named species. Names aligned through older taxonomies do not prove that historical and COL26.8 species concepts are identical.

This smaller PanTHERIA set includes entries with at least 1 focal field and a same-name, same-family match in the historical crosswalk. It is still a partial record set; source conditions differ, and it does not establish current species concepts, distribution, or conservation status.

## Distinguish averages from records {#s-f01d17b075}
Gestation appears in 13 records, female age at first reproduction in 12, and maximum lifespan in 8. First reproduction is the age when conception is possible; it is distinct from first birth and sexual maturity. Maximum lifespan is a record, not a typical value.

## Limits of these historical data {#s-0275122ba8}
The Ernest (2003) pages use historical same-name and same-family links supported by the MSW93/MSW05 table; equivalence to present COL26.8 concepts remains unestablished. Most fields average cited values equally, regardless of sample size, while maximum lifespan keeps the longest record. Inputs can be averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and may reuse underlying literature. Adult mass is not interpreted here, and conditions or locality are not uniform.
