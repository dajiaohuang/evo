## Large slate-blue flowers {#s-f721d21237}
Moraea ardesiaca is the only blue-flowered member of its subgenus recorded in southern Africa. Its large flowers are slate-blue to blue-mauve, with outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 65–75 mm long; the cited account distinguishes them from the smaller, pale-pink flowers of M. carnea.

## Streams and drainage lines {#s-64be53303d}
The regional account records streams and drainage lines, generally at lower elevations than the related M. carnea.
