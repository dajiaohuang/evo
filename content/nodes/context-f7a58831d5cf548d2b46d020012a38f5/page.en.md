## The source concept {#s-0ca8eef2a1}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0068 records Anomaloglossus guanayensis in genus Anomaloglossus, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=23.5 mm {#s-04de8efaa2}
Body_size_mm=23.5 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
