## What did one cultivation trial produce? {#s-e2e0b321a3}
At the National Mushroom Development Institute in Savar, Bangladesh, a managed bag-cultivation experiment tested a substrate made of 50% waste tea leaves and 50% sawdust. Across two harvest flushes, the reported total biological yield was 189.50 ± 3.31 g per packet, and the study’s biological-efficiency measure was 79%. The table reports means from three replicates. These values describe this setup only; they do not establish wild ecology or performance on other substrates and under other conditions.
