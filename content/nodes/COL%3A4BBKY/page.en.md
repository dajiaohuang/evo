## Flower colour alternatives {#s-c4011b3ae5}
The description records a slender geophyte up to 10 cm tall, with terminal three-part leaves and cuneate-obovate leaflets that are shortly ciliate and hairy below. Flowers may be sessile and are listed as yellow, white or pale lilac, always with a yellow tube; the habitat note is clay flats at about 250 m.
