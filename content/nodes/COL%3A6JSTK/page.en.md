## How the cereal-crop isolates were sampled {#s-0066ad6bb3}
Kelly and Ward’s 2018 population-genomic panel comprised 60 *Fusarium graminearum* isolates from cereal crops in the northern United States and southern Canada: 20 NA1/15ADON, 20 NA2/3ADON, and 20 NX-2. This describes the study sample, not prevalence across the species’ range or across host plants. Exact collection dates and exhaustive site coverage are not claimed here.

## Population structure within the study panel {#s-b63ba26a9a}
Bayesian clustering and phylogenomic analyses of the 60 sampled isolates supported three North American populations, NA1, NA2, and NA3; eight NX-2 isolates had admixed ancestry across the groups. These are population assignments in the authors’ 2018 study panel, not a species-wide phylogeny or global evolutionary history. The evidence is from archived crop-associated isolates and does not resolve wild-host populations or current population status.
