## A rootless floating frond with tiny flowers {#s-df819eb797}
The accepted species name follows Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage 5C2XG and matched World Flora Online usage wfo-0000334452. The biological details below come from a Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record.

COL26.8 accepted species ID 5C2XG and exact name/authorship map through exactly one accepted WFO usage wfo-0000334452; the Brazilian Flora source record carries the same WFO ID.

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Wolffia brasiliensis through its small floating fronds, a two-flower inflorescence, and a spherical fruit. One printed length range is given in descending order, so it is preserved as recorded.

## Fronds and structure {#s-c1d76deb07}
The source describes rootless fronds floating freely at the water surface. One or two may be joined; their shapes are recorded as conical, boat-shaped, or flattened ovoid. A small papilla (raised bump) lies along the upper midline of sterile fronds. The field gives frond length as “1.4 to 0.3 mm” in that order, width 1 mm, and height 0.45 mm; the descending endpoints are retained without reordering. Fronds are dark green, with dark-spotted epidermal cells noted in dried material, and a convex lower surface. An adult frond has one basal opening described as conical and funicular; young fronds form there, with or without an epidermal stomatal cavity.

The inflorescence has two flowers, one male and one female, without a prophyll; they sit in a cavity along the upper midline of the frond. The male flower has a single-chambered anther that opens at its tip and measures 0.37 mm. The globular female flower has one orthotropous (straight) ovule, a short style, and a concave stigma with a brown spot. The fruit is spherical and retains its stigma.
