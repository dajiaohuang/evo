## Five Pennsylvanian fossils sample early eumetabolan branches {#s-74cb4960bf}
Five insect fossils dated from the Moscovian to the Gzhelian represent a stem coleopterid, a holometabolous larva, a stem hymenopterid, early Hemiptera and early Psocodea. They broaden the sampled Pennsylvanian record before better-known post-Permian crown radiations. The fossils do not show that these branches originated at the same time or fix the first appearance of any living crown order.
