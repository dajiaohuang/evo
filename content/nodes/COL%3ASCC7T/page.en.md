## Vegetation encounters over several heights {#s-0e5192955b}
The revision reported western Andean Azuay sites at 1800–2900 m, in primary and secondary forest. Afternoon and nighttime specimens occurred on low vegetation up to 1.5 m high and inside bromeliads up to 8 m high; some were collected on banana plants. Those encounter heights are not physiological limits or proof of breeding in bromeliads.

## January and August reproductive observations {#s-e34e27c523}
Gravid females and calling males were collected in January and August; males were heard at 16:00 and 19:00. These records support reproductive activity in the sampled months and times, without establishing a complete breeding calendar, clutch sizes or absence of activity in other months.
