## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a399ba90ed}
COL26.8 records Oligodon woodmasoni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-baba789b21}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 650 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.
