## Overview {#s-d41c99e15d}
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID 4F78F. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name East African Potto and notes its former treatment as a subspecies of P. potto. The page keeps the pinned COL identity while preserving older combinations when reading the literature.

## Research observations {#s-a4d68f0855}
Luhrs et al.’s ten-site comparison reported 28 P. ibeanus, alongside 51 central pottos, using an established behavioural ethogram. Across both species the study covered 21 postures and 42 of 50 behaviours, and reported bark chewing and previously undescribed vocalisations; the abstract does not assign those novel records to one species alone.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-30b6092df6}
The 28 animals are study observations, not a population estimate or a range-representative sample. The behaviour inventory is pooled across two species; the source does not establish behaviours unique to the eastern potto, range-wide diet or population trend.

## Evidence limits {#s-58d7ba1be2}
This page centers on one comparative behavioural study. Observation counts are not abundance estimates, and the abstract does not partition every behavioural result by species. This page does not summarize range-wide distribution, a species-specific diet, current conservation status or long-term trend. The Chinese name is editorial.
