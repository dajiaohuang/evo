## Hairy leaf undersides and many-flowered heads {#s-ab491b45e1}
This account notes a swollen underground stem and few-branched shoots bearing leaves along their length. The thin leaves range from lance-shaped to inversely lance-shaped; their upper surfaces are smooth and the lower surfaces pubescent, with finely toothed edges. Flower heads may be stalkless or stalked, with bell-shaped or hemispherical involucres and about 40–50 florets. The cylindrical fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
