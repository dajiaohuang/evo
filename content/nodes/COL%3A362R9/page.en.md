## Forking flower stems and pale flowers {#s-4a0d2a0941}
Dietes bicolor is a 0.7–1.0 m perennial herb. Its flowering stem branches dichotomously (repeatedly dividing into paired branches), and its pale-yellow to white flowers usually have dark-brown nectar guides at the bases of the outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals). The cited treatment also identifies it as the genus’s only polyploid (having more than two sets of chromosomes) species and reports a closer relationship to Lord Howe Island’s D. robinsoniana than to the other African species.

## Streams, vleis and surrounding vegetation {#s-7f3a05a93d}
The regional account records plants along streams and vlei (a seasonally wet depression or wetland), and lists Drakensberg Grassland, the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt and Albany Thicket. These habitat notes do not define the species’ full distribution.
