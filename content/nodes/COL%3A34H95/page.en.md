## Delosperma fredericii {#s-fbf8e333f1}
## Pale brick-coloured flowers darken as they fade

The Greater Cape flora account describes a shrublet exceeding 10 cm in height and gives a species description of a plant up to 25 cm tall. Its narrow, pointed leaves are three-angled and 10–15 mm long. Many-flowered clusters grow on 6–13 mm stalks; the pale brick-coloured flowers darken as they fade. Fruits are unknown, and the habitat entry is uncertain.
