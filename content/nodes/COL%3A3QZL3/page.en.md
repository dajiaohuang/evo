## Historical rainy-season aggregations with recent-record gaps {#s-931751f0b3}
The 2016 account described K. picta as distributed throughout the Philippines and formerly often encountered in dense rainy-season aggregations in rice fields and temporary water bodies. It also emphasized a lack of recent field observations confirming the presumed range. Historical commonness is therefore not used as a present-day population estimate, and these habitat accounts do not provide a complete range census or evidence of continuous occupancy.

## A proposed information-gap category and untested invasive threats {#s-e702103c76}
The authors proposed Data Deficient despite the cited 2016 Least Concern listing, because recent occurrence confirmation and possible invasive-species effects were insufficiently known. They suggested competition or predation involving introduced K. pulchra and Hoplobatrachus rugulosus as unevaluated threats, not measured impacts. They also cited a 2013 genetic study as supporting uniformity across the Philippines. The proposed category, threats and cited taxonomic interpretation remain dated and attributed; none is treated as a current official assessment or a new independent genetic analysis.
