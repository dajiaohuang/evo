## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Carpodacus sibiricus (Pallas, 1773) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Гнездование урагуса Uragus sibiricus в окрестностях Ирбита {#study-account}
Bachurin's 2005 reprint reports three nests found near Irbit in 1998 under the name Uragus sibiricus. Nests were in shrubs or birch growth about 1.0-1.4 m above ground; clutches had four or five eggs. The author describes loose grass-and-fibre nests with plant down. The historical source name is preserved without equating it to the current COL26.8 record.
