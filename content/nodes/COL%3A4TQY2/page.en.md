## Ruschia fourcadei {#s-3f3ef2f079}
## Recurved leaf tips and grey five-chambered fruits

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes an erect, stiffly branched shrublet up to 15 cm tall. Its leaves are three-angled in cross-section and end in a small point that curves backward. The pink flowers may be solitary or occur in groups of three, and reach 26 mm across.

The grey fruits have five chambers and a closing body. The habitat account records loam in karroid scrub.
