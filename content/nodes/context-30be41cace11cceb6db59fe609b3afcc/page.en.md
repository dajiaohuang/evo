## Source classification and concept status {#s-14f6701a32}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3039 records Hylodes perere under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-6085ed1745}
The compilation records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Activity periods (daytime and night) {#s-8f60d39597}

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-1d2e0b79f6}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 28.4 mm for this row. Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
