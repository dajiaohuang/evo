## *Mikania stylosa*: hairy leaves and branched flower clusters {#s-1b9932395f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, papery, egg-shaped leaves with toothed edges. The upper surface is hairy and the lower densely woolly; three main veins and two additional veins above the base are listed. Branches may twine or be hairy. Loose panicles or corymb-like clusters carry stalked heads. The surrounding bracts may be woolly, and the bell-shaped corolla limb is longer than its tube, with pointed, glandular lobes. The fruitlets are sparsely hairy.
