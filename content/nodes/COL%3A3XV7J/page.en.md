## What traits does the record list for Manihot peltata? {#s-ec51f9d4ba}
The roots are non-tuberous, with a rigid, shapeless xylopodium. Stems may be 0.5–0.95 m or 1–2.9 m tall, smooth and hairless, with yellow latex. Simple, petiolate leaves may be lobed; triangular stipules are recorded. Terminal unisexual racemes have basal female flowers. The involucral bracts are hairless, oval or oval-lanceolate, and 0.2–0.4 cm wide. Male buds are ovoid and hairless; female buds are hairless. The capsule is round, smooth, hairless, and unribbed. Seeds are elliptic and smooth, with a caruncle.
