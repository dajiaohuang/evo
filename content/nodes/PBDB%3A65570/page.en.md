## Recorded parent of Merycodontinae {#s-d0d51ea3e1}
Merycodontinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Antilocapridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
