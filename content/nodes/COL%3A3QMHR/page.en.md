## What does the source describe about its sprawling branches and fragrant flowers? {#s-666958c97e}
Jasminum grandiflorum is described as a sprawling shrub with slender branches that may extend several metres. Its leaves are divided into usually five to seven leaflets. The few-flowered clusters are terminal, and the fragrant flowers have a white, salver-shaped corolla whose tube is 15–20 mm long; it dries yellowish. The source specifically says fruits were not seen, so it provides no fruit description.

## What native region did the historical flora report? {#s-48aa576d7d}
The 2013 Flora of Panama field describes Jasminum grandiflorum as native to subtropical parts of Southwest Asia. This is the historical regional statement in the source version retrieved on 8 September 2026, not a current range survey or global inventory.
