## White flowers with a dark purple centre {#s-b239ec76f3}
Ixia leipoldtii is a small corm-bearing plant (its corm is a swollen underground storage stem), 11–25 cm tall. Its large, mostly white flowers have a dark-purple central blotch and pale-greenish throat; translucent brown bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) and filaments enclosed within the flower tube are additional diagnostic features.
