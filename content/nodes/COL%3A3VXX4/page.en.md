## ## Racemes with purple flowers {#s-19dff7e282}
The *Flora of Panama* account describes *Lonchocarpus oliganthus* as a shrub or slender tree about 10 m tall. Its leaves bear five to seven oblong or elliptic leaflets, 6–15 cm long and 2.5–7 cm wide. The thin, papery leaflets are rounded or tapering at the base and long-tapered at the tip; young ones are densely bristly, while mature leaflets are hairless or have sparse, minute bristles beneath. Axillary racemes are slender, loosely flowered and 6–10 cm long. The small cup-shaped calyx has mostly indistinct teeth, and the petals are purple; the account gives detailed form and surface hairs for the standard and wing petals. Legumes were unknown to this account. It is a historical regional description, not a complete account of variation or range.
