## A small fish defined from Rüdersdorf material {#s-8a82f92ac8}
Pseudopholidoctenus germanicus is a named stem teleosteomorph known from the Rüdersdorf type sample: holotype MB.f.18641 and paratypes MB.f.19904–19906. Its diagnosis combines a fused skull roof, distinctive scales and tail-fin characters; a described individual is about 55 mm long.

## Diet is unresolved {#s-cdf6987bff}
The cited description does not establish this species' diet or prey.

## A restricted marine-basin setting {#s-39e00e936e}
The type material comes from a restricted marine setting in the Central European (Germanic) Basin; a broader habitat preference is not established.

## Locality and time limits {#s-83689fa5dc}
The named type specimens and their diagnosis support the stem-teleosteomorph identification. Their lower Anisian Rüdersdorf horizon is about 244 Ma; it is one locality sample, not a global first appearance or a date for the origin of teleosts.

## What the type sample preserves {#s-f7045efa7a}
Holotype MB.f.18641 and paratypes MB.f.19904–19906 establish the species. The diagnosis describes a fused skull roof with a short triangular nasal region, large near-square scutes before the upper tail lobe, fewer than 18 principal tail rays and no lower-side procurrent rays. One described specimen is about 55 mm in total length and 36 mm in standard length.

## One locality sets the scope {#s-9507e30e80}
The type material was collected at Rüdersdorf in Germany's Central European (Germanic) Basin, from the lower Anisian middle Muschelkalk, dated in the study to about 244 Ma. The paper describes a restricted marine-basin setting. This locality does not establish a global distribution, diet, or swimming performance.
