## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-b1e9717118}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves have tapered bases, linear to elliptic or oval blades, and flat margins. The inflorescence is leafy, with flower stalks 7–20 mm long and bracteoles present. Its floral tube is 13–30 mm long and either hairless or covered with short, fine hairs. A helmet-shaped spur bends downward. The field marks the flowers as lacking petals, while also listing white as a petal color; these entries are not reconciled in the source row. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube, no vesicle is recorded, and the nectar gland lies horizontally at the ovary base. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin acute. This is a regional database field, not a global survey.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-640cf2f932}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no collection locality or date.
