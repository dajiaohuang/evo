## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-2e42cb5bd3}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Pseudis limellum* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Pseudis (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.
