## Syagrus caerulescens {#s-83fbfa2bb9}
Syagrus caerulescens is recorded with a solitary, very short or underground stem and glaucous pinnate leaves. The stiff leaflets are grouped at differing angles; the inflorescence is spike-like or branched. Ovoid yellow-green fruits have a hairy surface and fibrous, juicy mesocarp. Habitat is listed as terrestrial.
