## Felicia mossamedensis {#s-e5c1430215}
## Yellow rays and three rows of bracts

The source describes a perennial herb up to 300 mm tall, with overlapping involucral bracts arranged in three rows. Both the ray and disc florets are yellow. A taxonomic note calls yellow rays unusual outside section Longistylus and says the species can resemble young, lightly woody F. clavipilosa subsp. transvaalensis; it also flags the distribution record as problematic. Habitat is labelled grassland.
