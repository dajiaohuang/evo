## Limits of the historical life-history and ecology records {#s-efbd021c7d}
Life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 archive. Except for maximum longevity, summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespan or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names and families and same-name rows support the association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed.

EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical guide, not a complete species monograph; matching names do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Diet is coded D1 and withheld because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized literature weights, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite claims. Underlying works have not been reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other evidence.
