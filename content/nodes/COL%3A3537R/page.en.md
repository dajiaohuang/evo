## Leaves, flowers and segmented fruit {#s-943a97178b}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium, a woody underground structure, as present. The stem is erect, cylindrical and velvety. Leaves are alternate and each has three leaflets; the leaf base bears free, ear-shaped stipules that partly clasp the stem and later fall. Leaflets are ovate, rhombic or elliptic, with secondary veins that curve toward the edge and join before reaching it. Panicles carry lilac flowers; their stamens are described as pseudo-monadelphous, with the filaments appearing joined as one group. The fruit is a loment, a segmented pod: its elliptic sections are alike, separated by narrow off-centre joints, not twisted, and covered with hooked hairs. These traits come from one regional dataset entry, not a description of all variation or a global account.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-ad4bbf8e80}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
