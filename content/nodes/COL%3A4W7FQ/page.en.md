## Five-lobed yellow flowers {#s-37c8e2b7a8}
The SANBI account describes *Sebaea pentandra* as an erect annual 50–300 mm tall, with orbicular-ovate to elliptic leaves. Its flowers have five lobes and are yellow; the calyx is described as keeled or winged, with sides that are approximately opaque. The account records a short-stalked round gland at each anther tip and two smaller short-stalked glands at the base. It lists the style swelling in the lower half as either present or absent.

## Seasonal watercourse habitat {#s-ecc7622110}
The same SANBI record lists seasonal watercourses as habitat. It names no particular watercourse or observation locality.
