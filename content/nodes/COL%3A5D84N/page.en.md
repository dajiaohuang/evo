## Growth form, two leaflets and spiny loments {#s-672b4f6bca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a woody root without a xylopodium. Stems are listed as decumbent, erect or prostrate. Leaves are bifoliolate, the inflorescence spike like, and flowers papilionaceous. The loment articles are recorded with prickles; seeds are brown. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields retain alternatives and do not establish variation or a complete range.
