## Ecological evidence {#s-44a51753c9}
At Taï Forest in Côte d'Ivoire, a 13-month comparison followed two habituated groups of each of three guenon species. C. petaurista ate more foliage than the other two study species and used the middle canopy strata, about 5–20 m above ground. The contrast is useful for reading local niche partitioning alongside C. campbelli and C. diana; it describes six sampled groups at one forest, not all populations of the current COL species.
