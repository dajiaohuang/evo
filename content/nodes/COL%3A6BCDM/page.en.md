## The name in COL26.8 {#s-ae959ec992}
COL26.8 accepts *Craugastor epochthidius* (McCranie & Wilson, 1997) as a species in its 20 August 2026 release. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Craugastoridae (family) → Craugastor (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## How to read this profile {#s-b57b3778c2}

## Two broad historical foraging categories {#s-2fc987648a}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf1261 marks Ter=1 (terrestrial) and Aqu=1 (aquatic) as broad foraging categories under the compilation's Frost 2011 species concept. It does not specify season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat categories but does not say whether Anf1261 was affected. Keep the flags as historical values, not observations of current ecology; the revision notice alone does not show this row is wrong.

## AmphiBIO's compiled maximum length {#s-4cbc2a442f}
For its Frost 2011 source concept, AmphiBIO lists maximum adult body length as 36.7 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length. It is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual, and the historical concept has not been matched to the current COL26.8 record.

## One annual-output value in the compilation {#s-c18d5c502a}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for its Frost 2011 source concept. The row does not tie this value to a particular sex, population, date or wild/captive setting. Other offspring-size fields in the dataset may refer to offspring or eggs; they do not supply a confirmed size for this species here.

## A developmental category, not a breeding account {#s-fe1f2164a2}
