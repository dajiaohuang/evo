## Lessingianthus grearii {#s-9bc0172c8d}
## Grey felt covers the underside of narrow leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a rhizome and stems with leaves along their length, unbranched or sparsely branched. Linear, leathery leaves have rolled-under, entire margins. The upper face is hairless; the underside has a grey-white felt of hairs. Both faces are the same colour.

Stalked, solitary heads have bell-shaped involucres with 5–7 rows of bracts and 25 florets. The cylindrical fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
