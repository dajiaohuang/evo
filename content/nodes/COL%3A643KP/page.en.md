## What the records describe {#s-ab2a015d60}
The records linked from Rukia cover different questions. AVONET v7 provides a measured profile for Rukia longirostra, while EltonTraits 1.0 has foraging-position entries for R. longirostra and R. ruki. The pages follow the COL26.8 genus classification, but each biological record keeps the name and scope used by its historical source.

## One mass mean is not a genus range {#s-48ae492666}
The AVONET account for Rukia longirostra gives a compiled mean mass of 17.9 g, with the source category Updated_literature. It is the only measured species profile represented in this genus route, so this value is not a lower or upper limit for Rukia. That same account records Forest as habitat and Omnivore as trophic niche; those categories describe the linked record, not every member of the genus.

## Foraging positions in two historical entries {#s-88120752fb}
EltonTraits has two Rukia entries with foraging-stratum weights: Rukia longirostra assigns 80% to the canopy or just above it and 20% to trees or tall shrubs below the canopy; Rukia ruki assigns 70% and 30% to those positions. No usable food-category weights or activity-period values are available here, so the comparison is limited to feeding position. These literature-based weights estimate relative use, not time budgets, nest sites, flight height or habitat area. The linked records identify Pratt, Bruner and Berrett’s 1987 field guide as the source label.

## Evidence limits {#s-557e4987d6}
These are source-specific records, not a complete account of the genus. A historical name match and a COL26.8 placement do not establish that the older and current species concepts are identical. The EltonTraits percentages are standardized estimates from literature descriptions, not direct behavioral measurements; the AVONET mass is a compiled mean from a separate source. Neither value establishes the range of variation within a species or a trait shared by all Rukia.

## A separate ecology record for Rukia ruki {#s-047fba7ddf}
The AVONET ecology entry linked here is for [Rukia ruki](Rukia_ruki/page.en.md). It records a closed-canopy, tall-tree habitat category and a trophic niche spanning multiple food categories. These are broad source classifications; they do not supply a local habitat description or a list of foods.

## Reading the ecology labels {#s-4032fbd707}
The single Rukia ruki ecology entry supplies one value each for habitat, trophic niche, primary lifestyle and migration. It labels the lifestyle Insessorial, meaning chiefly perching on vegetation, rocks or other substrates above the ground, and migration Sedentary. These source categories do not describe every population, season or individual. The page also gives historical breeding and resident-map latitude endpoints, not a complete account of seasonal movement.

## Keep source and taxonomic scope in view {#s-6bed34851c}
The two one-to-one name crosswalks and COL26.8 placements help locate these records, but they do not resolve later species splits or synonymies. EltonTraits describes foraging positions with coarse relative weights; AVONET supplies broad ecological categories and a historical map summary. None of these fields alone establishes current occupancy, conservation status or a complete species-level natural history.
