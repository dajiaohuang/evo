## *Xyris amorimii*: ridged stalks and ovoid flower heads {#s-0aefbe955b}
The leaves are arranged in two ranks and have flat, rough blades. Its flower stalk, or peduncle, is 20–70 cm long and irregularly ridged. The head is ovoid or top-shaped and enclosed by several layers of bracts; the account records 10–25 flowers. It is listed as terrestrial.
