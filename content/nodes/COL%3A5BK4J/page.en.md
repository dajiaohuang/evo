## What does the record say about Virola decorticans? {#s-87e7759a8b}
The field describes leathery, oblong to obovate leaves with more than 40 pairs of side veins. Their undersides bear star-shaped or branched star-shaped hairs. Panicles carry flowers whose perianth is split to the base; the male flowers have three anthers. The oval fruit is velvety and contains an ellipsoid to egg-shaped seed with a red aril.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-5529359735}
The dataset records it as terrestrial, without naming a specific site.
