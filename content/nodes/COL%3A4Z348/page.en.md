## White flowers on long stalks {#s-9077e64364}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records square, hairless stems with internodes shorter than the leaf blades. Leaves are nearly sessile, elliptic, and hairless, with attenuate bases and acute tips. Long peduncles carry more than four flowers. The linear sepals are hairless; the funnel-shaped corolla is white or white marked with pink or purple. The ovary is hairless and the stamens remain within the corolla. The fruit is hairless, and the seeds are globose.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-f033703984}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Spigelia beyrichiana* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
