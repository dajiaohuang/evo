## *Eryngium ombrophilum*: a low flower cluster and stalked fruit {#s-492b4f3747}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description records stalked leaves with central veins listed as parallel, pinnate or net-like. Its flower cluster lies low, and individual flowers have stalks. The fruit is stalked and does not split open; each mericarp, or fruit segment, has a blister-like back.

The substrate field is rupicolous, meaning rock-associated, but gives no named locality or date. These details are a regional morphology record.
