## Ditassa retusa {#s-e5bdab881c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ditassa retusa* as an accepted species of *Ditassa*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 habitat entries include both terrestrial and rupicolous (rock-substrate) categories. They do not specify where the plant was recorded or establish a complete range, and the source record has no morphology description.
