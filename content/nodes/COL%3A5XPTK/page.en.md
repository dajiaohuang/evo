## Thirty to sixty leaflet pairs {#s-305ebc0696}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field records 30–60 pairs of leaflets and bulbous, star-shaped glandular hairs. Racemes are listed at both shoot tips and leaf axils; the yellow flowers have 10 stamens. Mature pod valves twist, and seeds are described as depressed obovoid. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. These are regional database traits, with no sampling frame, sex, life stage, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status supplied.
