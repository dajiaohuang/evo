## What does the field record for Oxandra macrophylla? {#s-baeb0a085a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery leaves ranging from egg-shaped to inversely egg-shaped, with pointed or heart-shaped bases and tapering tips. Flower buds are ellipsoid; the fruit segments and seeds are also recorded as ellipsoid.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-5d9fa62b36}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
