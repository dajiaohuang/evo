## Senna martiana {#s-fe036ccf7b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna martiana* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry lists 11–15, 16–20, or more than 20 pairs of leaflets and no extra-floral glands. Its flowers have six fertile stamens; the erect, flat pods are dehiscent (splitting open at maturity), with seeds in one row. The same regional record labels the plant terrestrial.
