## Genomes place gar and bowfin together {#s-945ac332b1}
A matrix of 243 one-to-one proteins from 25 vertebrates, bowfin transcriptomes, and conserved gene order support living gars plus bowfin as Holostei, sister to teleosts. The spotted-gar genome represents a lineage that split before the teleost genome duplication. This is evidence from sampled living genomes, not a fossil date or evidence that gars have remained morphologically unchanged; it also does not show that genome duplication alone caused teleost diversity.
