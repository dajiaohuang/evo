## Lepidaploa almasensis {#s-a3a7cc43db}
## A basal rosette sends up a scape with pink florets

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright stem that gradually loses its hairs and has no branch glands. Leaves form a basal rosette and also occur on the stem. The sessile blades are oblong to oblanceolate, pointed, narrowed at the base and hairy on both faces; leaf-surface glands are absent.

A flowering scape carries a bell-shaped involucre with non-overlapping bracts; the inner bracts are pointed to long-pointed. Each head has 17–20 pink florets. The pappus is white, and glands are recorded on the fruit. The source habitat field classifies the plant as rupicolous, growing on rock.
