## An aquatic plant with three-lobed base {#s-114229a64c}
The morphology record describes an aquatic plant with a rounded, three-lobed stem base and upright, linear leaves. At the leaf bases sit pale-brown, elliptic spore cases; their covering is rudimentary or absent. The record also distinguishes larger spores with a knobbed surface from smaller spores with a spiny outer layer.
