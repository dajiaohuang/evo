## Chamaecrista bifoliola {#s-dd66bd5395}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista bifoliola* records one pair of leathery leaflets 4–8 by 1.4–3 cm, racemes with 10–24 flowers, legumes 3.3–4 cm long. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
