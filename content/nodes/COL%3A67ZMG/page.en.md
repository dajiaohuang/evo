## Bactris pliniana {#s-c4332181ee}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes Bactris pliniana with stems to 2 m and brown or yellow spines up to 5 cm. Its 15–21 pairs of sickle-shaped leaflets are irregularly grouped; orange obovoid fruits have a prickly surface. The habitat label is “Saprófita” (saprophyte), with no locality or explanatory context.
