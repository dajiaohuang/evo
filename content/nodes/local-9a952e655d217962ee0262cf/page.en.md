## The deposited caecilian record {#s-ff2ff92a86}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis kohtaoensis in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurement material {#s-cfd3d5c699}
The compilation lists 11 measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of 11 distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-4689798f25}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” The accompanying species note describes adults in loose soil along small mountain streams, with abundant plant cover, and on moist shores beside pools or rice fields. These source descriptions do not show how often each setting is used or define a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-31e2ed7634}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-43a5c8a80c}
The species-level record gives 339 for dorsal annuli (body rings), 4.125 for post-vent annuli, 3.75 annuli interrupted by the vent, and 114.555555555556 vertebrae. These compiled values may summarize records rather than describe one animal; they do not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic counts.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-ed4ff96261}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.

## Compiled clutch counts {#s-c3c02a9191}
The source species record lists a minimum clutch size of 32 and a maximum of 58. These counts come from cited reproductive accounts and describe the compiled source material, not universal limits or annual reproductive output. The associated reproductive metadata remains part of their interpretation.
