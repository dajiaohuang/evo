## Name and this data account {#s-0ce86da1f3}
Kinosternon herrerai is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Kinosternidae. This account uses its ReptTraits v1-2 record, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and trait-specific sources. The dataset consolidates information into one row per species; this describes the cited record rather than a uniform state of all populations.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-ef319ae4e9}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 130 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding head, neck and tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Microhabitat, diet and activity {#s-9cd5b2f886}
The entry records Arboreal (tree-associated), Saxicolous (rock-dwelling) and Terrestrial (ground-associated) microhabitats, Omnivorous diet, Cathemeral activity, and foraging mode ACT (active). These labels are not an exhaustive habitat list or fixed schedule; populations, seasons and life stages may differ.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-b549dc1ef4}
The reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 3; lowest reported clutch/litter mean 2; highest reported clutch/litter mean 4; consolidated litters/clutches per year 4. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-b49f92ba04}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
