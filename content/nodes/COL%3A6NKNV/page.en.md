The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The diet field classifies the entry as plant- and animal-eating (Omnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Carr and Roderic 1988. The active-time field classifies activity as active across day and night (Cathemeral); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Carr and Roderic 1988.
