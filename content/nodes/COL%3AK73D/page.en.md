## Baccharis breviseta {#s-48814bdcfb}
## Serrated leaves and two kinds of florets

The Brazilian Flora fields record no xylopodium and describe stems that are unwinged and either smooth or striated. Mature leaves are sessile, lanceolate and serrated, with three main veins. The flower heads are grouped in panicles and are heterogamous: threadlike pistillate florets line the margin, while tubular staminate florets occupy the centre. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
