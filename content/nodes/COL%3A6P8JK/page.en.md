## Overview {#s-8a1a39145e}
Hershkovitz originally described this taxon from a 1945 illustration by Cruz Lima; the animals were said to come from the upper Purus, but the specimen was lost and its precise provenance was unknown. Sampaio et al. (2015) redescribed its morphology and proposed species status and a neotype using morphology, mitochondrial evidence and pelage.

## Research observations {#s-05bc7e604d}
In 2011, three 5-km transects at three sites in Brazil’s Purus National Forest covered 120 km and recorded 13 groups and 73 animals; groups were also seen at nearby localities in 2012–2013. The paper reports about 6.08 animals per 10 km, but this transect count is not a detection-corrected population density, and range limits remain unclear.

## Classification {#s-2986ff94c1}
The neotype anchors the formerly imprecise illustration-based name near Cujuri on the left bank of Rio Inauini within the forest reserve. This page follows the COL26.8 authorship year and ID; MDD v2.5 lists the shared epithet as *Saguinus cruzlimai*, but its crosswalk is unmatched, so the epithet alone is not used to relabel identity.

## Evidence limits {#s-4182d864c4}
Transect counts are not density estimates. The original name lacked a verifiable type specimen; the 2015 neotype anchors a locality but does not establish complete range limits or multi-site ecology.
