## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-4d104c431e}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 7158.29 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 2.17 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-e214e6a95e}
The archive’s nonzero food weights are other plant material (the source category also includes fungi) 80%; fruit 20%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. ABC cannot be rewritten as direct observations of this species. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

The entry is assigned Ar: arboreal; code A records compiler confidence in source and assignment. Metadata collectively attributes mammalian stratum assignments to Nowak (1999). This is a category assignment, not a percentage of time among strata, and does not mean the species acts only there.

## Evidence limits {#s-91855c8af1}
All abundance and occupancy figures are tied to the survey’s coverage and date; they do not replace a later census.
The 2015 archive compiles historical species- and subspecies-level life-history records: non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians, while maximum_longevity_y is the largest compiled record. Historical taxonomic inheritance means a same-name match does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept; these records do not yield a uniform wild-only or captive-only estimate. EltonTraits 1.0 converts literature descriptions into diet weights and categorical activity/foraging fields; mammalian ABC fields may draw on congeners, so they are not direct species-level observations in every field. Neither archive establishes current distribution or conservation status.
