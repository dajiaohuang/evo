## Roella goodiana {#s-a81fea064f}
## Pale leaves and a small white corolla distinguish the species

Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 Cape flora account describes this species as softer than R. recurvata, with upper bract teeth that do not bend back and smaller flowers. Adamson’s 1951 revision separately distinguishes its longer, more spreading, pale-green leaves, which are not recurved; regularly three-toothed calyx lobes; and small white corolla. The habitat record is sandy flats.
