## What growth and flower features are recorded? {#s-c900e5162e}
This compact, flexible shrublet grows to 75 cm, with ascending, narrowly ovate to lance-shaped leaves. Flowers range from cream to dark pink; the outside of the flower tube (hypanthium) is hairless, and the stigma is head-shaped with papillae.

## How does the monograph distinguish it from related species? {#s-5bbf40aaf6}
A 2001 monograph groups it with L. pudens, L. leipoldtii and L. pusilla, whose upper flower tubes are indented at the base. L. axillaris differs from L. pudens by having a hairless outer tube. Its upper tube is cup-shaped, and its stigma is head-shaped with papillae. In L. leipoldtii the upper tube is narrowly inverted-cone-shaped and the stigma conical; in L. pusilla the stigma is narrowly conical. Both comparison species also have papillose stigmas.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-492b3ffca2}
The regional account reports sandy flats. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
