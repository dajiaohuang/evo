## Mesembryanthemum pellitum {#s-9f118c0fd7}
## Broad basal leaves accompany large white flowers

The Extra Cape flora describes a succulent annual or biennial that grows upright or leaning, then lies flat when flowering. It has no basal vegetative rosette; the main stem reaches 500 mm and ends in a flower. The stems are round in cross-section. Flat leaves are largest at the base, up to 150 × 80 mm, with wavy margins.

The flowers are white and can reach 50 mm across, with numerous petals, sterile stamens, and fertile stamens. The fruit has five chambers. The habitat record is open plains and disturbed ground.
