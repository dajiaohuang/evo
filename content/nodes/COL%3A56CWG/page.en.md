## Violet flowers on a narrow-leaved plant {#s-780329eb48}
Thereianthus intermedius is a 15–25 cm corm-bearing plant (its corm is a swollen underground storage stem) with linear, midribbed leaves. Its violet flowers have dark markings on the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals); flowering is reported for December.

## Sandstone seeps in the Cold Bokkeveld {#s-4d3f9f571f}
The regional flora records seeps on sandstone slopes and gives the Cold Bokkeveld as the regional range. This is not a complete global range assessment.
