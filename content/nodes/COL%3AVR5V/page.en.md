## Clara ophiopogonoides: rosette leaves and a simple raceme {#s-bf1e4993f4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records threadlike roots and a rhizome. Leaves are linear, arranged in a rosette, and described as papery to leathery. The inflorescence is a simple raceme. The flower bud is greenish yellow, the tepals are yellow, the filaments are threadlike and the anthers oblong. The field describes the capsule as spheroidal and records no wings or thickened margins.

The dataset records the habitat category “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.
