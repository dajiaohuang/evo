## Toothed leaves with rounded or tapered bases {#s-c454ea29dc}
The morphology record notes a swollen underground stem and little branching, with lance-shaped leaves along the stem. The thin, papery blades have pointed tips, rounded or tapering bases and finely toothed edges; either surface may be smooth or minutely hairy. Flower heads may be stalkless or stalked and are arranged singly or in branched clusters. The bell-shaped heads contain about 70–80 florets; the cylindrical fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
