## Source classification and concept status {#s-ac94d1922b}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3331 records Pleurodema cordobae under the source classification Pleurodema (Leiuperidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

The compilation records terrestrial and aquatic as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. ## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-d94621e8e3}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 42.9 mm for this row. AmphiBIO's Body_size_mm field records maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
