## What underground, leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Camarea elongata? {#s-dd11a2ec0f}
The plant has an underground rhizome. Its oval leaves grow in whorls and have pointed tips, blunt or rounded bases, silky to velvety hairs and edges rolled under; they lack petioles. The inflorescence is a cincinnus, a coiled, one-sided flower cluster. The flowers have three hairy carpels and flattened stigmas, and their sepals are hairy. The fruit bears wings and has a spiny seed-bearing center.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b6818c51c0}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
