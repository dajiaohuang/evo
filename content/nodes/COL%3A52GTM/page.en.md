## *Stevia cruziana*: dense axillary clusters and awned fruits {#s-26f0e4c415}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records opposite leaves with stalks and dense flower-head clusters in the leaf axils. The clusters are corymb-like, and the heads have stalks. Its fruitlets bear bristly or glandular hairs, and the pappus has awns. These morphology fields accompany only the broad substrate label “terrestrial.”
