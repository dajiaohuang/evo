## Begonia subvillosa {#s-263d3cb475}
## Hairy leaves with ciliate or crenulate edges

Begonia subvillosa has erect stems and persistent stipules. Simple hairs occur on the petioles and both surfaces of its strongly asymmetric leaves. The transversely elliptic blades may be entire or lobed, with ciliate or crenulate edges. Male and female flower segments are also hairy; female margins may be entire or ciliate. Bracteoles are present, capsule wings unequal, and seed tips blunt. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings.
