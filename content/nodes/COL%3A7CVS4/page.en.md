## Life-history evidence limits {#s-c96d0b73c0}
The life-history values come from a historical 2015 archive. They are reported as compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching names, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. EltonTraits 1.0 food and stratum percentages are standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty; mammalian ABC diet or activity fields may refer to a congener considered ecologically similar by compilers. The records do not establish direct observation of this species for every field. Individual, seasonal and local variation, diet and behavior beyond these categories, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
