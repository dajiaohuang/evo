The two selected guides cover three occurrence rows from two collections; Tiaojishan is the listed stratum. Read each collection's own age bracket and identification before comparing records. Three rows do not represent three animals or establish ancient abundance.

## Evidence limits {#s-64673ec8dc}
These pages summarize selected PBDB occurrences, not every member of Scaphognathidae or a shared ecological profile. Database rows and collections are not estimates of specimens, populations, or sampling intensity. Their age brackets do not directly date biological origin or extinction; the recorded parent route is distinct from phylogenetic treatments.
