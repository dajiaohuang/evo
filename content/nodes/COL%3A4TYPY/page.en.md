## Morphology recorded in the flora dataset {#s-208568dd73}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes adventitious roots and a cylindrical corm, a swollen underground stem. It records two leaf forms: submerged, sessile, threadlike leaves, and floating, petiolate leaves that are elliptic, ovate, or arrowhead-shaped. The floating leaves have campylodromous venation: their main veins curve back toward the base before turning toward and converging near the tip. Petioles are three-angled.

The inflorescence is a raceme, with flowers arranged along an axis that may lie down or float. Lower whorls bear female flowers and upper whorls bear staminate (male) flowers; the whorls are described as unconstricted. The field describes the staminate and female flowers as unisexual, each with a whorl of sterile carpels.

Sepals are appressed, membranous, and ovate. Petals are ovate, white with yellow and purple spots. Stamens are numerous and hairless; carpels are numerous. The fruit is an aggregate of achenes, with sepals described as reflexed or appressed. Each achene is flattened, winged, obovate (wider toward the tip), keeled, and muricate, with short rough projections. Seeds are yellowish-brown.

The regional Brazilian Flora 2020 row reports Matias's Alismataceae treatment as the dataset citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description. It does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.

## Habitat label {#s-7d481bf9df}
The dataset labels the habitat *Aquática*, its aquatic category.

This is a habitat category, not a collection locality; the field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.
