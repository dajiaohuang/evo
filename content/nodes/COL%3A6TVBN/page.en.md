## Pauridia ovata {#s-d9de6dd084}
Pauridia ovata bears one star-shaped flower above a corm covered by tangled roots. Its leaves are ovate to lanceolate and clasp the base; the white tepals have wine-red backs. The regional account distinguishes it from P. gracilipes by those flower colours and the more erect upper leaf, which may clasp much of the scape and pedicel. It records seasonally wet clay banks and depressions in renosterveld as habitat. This comparison and habitat record reflect the cited regional treatment.
