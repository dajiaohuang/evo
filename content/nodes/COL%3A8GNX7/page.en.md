## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-83ab9af38b}
COL26.8 records Ahaetulla farnsworthi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-316542a967}
The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2010.
