## Baccharis dracunculifolia {#s-780aa1fc54}
## Narrow leaves and varied head arrangements

The source records no xylopodium, a woody underground base; branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated. Developed leaves have one main vein, a very short petiole, and lance-shaped or oblong blades with margins ranging from entire to toothed or serrated. The flower heads may be racemose, spike-like or solitary in the leaf axils. The head-floret fields list either all tubular male florets or all threadlike female florets. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
