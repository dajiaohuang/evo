## What leaf and flower measurements appear in the regional field? {#s-ec646b5592}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes hairy leaves that blacken when dry. Petioles are 0–2.5 mm long; blades are more than 5 mm wide, with acute, tapered or blunt bases and slightly to strongly rolled-under margins. Secondary veins may curve toward the edge before fading or converge toward the leaf tip; at least two occur on each side, and small hair tufts are present in the vein axils. A small bract below the flower reaches from halfway up the floral tube to its tip. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. Epicalyx segments, an extra sepal-like ring below the calyx, are either 0–1.5 mm or 2–11 mm long, shorter than the sepals, and bend downward or spread outward. The corolla is listed at either 1–3 cm or 3.5–7 cm across, with 12 or 15 stamens. These are alternatives in a regional database field, without specimen-level frequencies.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-8c0f3fc3ce}
The dataset lists a terrestrial habitat; it gives no locality or collection date.
