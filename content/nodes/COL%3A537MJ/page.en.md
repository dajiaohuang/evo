## Variable gland counts and pale flowers in the entry {#s-94415fa688}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists 1, 2 or 3 warty petiolar nectaries and 2, 3, 4 or 5 conical rachis nectaries. Leaves have 9–17 pairs of pinnae. Hair tufts on the lower leaflet surfaces are recorded as present on one side or absent. The inflorescence is a simple thyrse, white or yellow. The campanulate calyx is sparsely pubescent or pubescent; the campanulate corolla is yellow or cream and is also listed as sparsely pubescent or pubescent. Filaments are white, a dorsal fold on the anther is conspicuous, and the ovary is glabrous or pulverulent. The fruit is a legume with glabrescent or pulverulent indumentum and a subcoriaceous texture. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
