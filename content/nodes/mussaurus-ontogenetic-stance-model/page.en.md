## Mussaurus models test how stance changed during growth {#s-ad338aa775}
Three-dimensional models combined hatchling material, the yearling specimen PVL 4587, and adult Mussaurus specimens. In the models, the estimated centre of mass shifts forward with growth, supporting a bipedal-to-quadrupedal stance change. Because the reconstruction combines different individuals and depends on body-volume and segmentation choices, it is a model of posture, not a preserved gait or a rule for all sauropodomorphs.
