## Paramachaerium gruberi {#s-032c61ad22}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Paramachaerium gruberi* as an accepted species of *Paramachaerium* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama fields in a WFO MBG archive retrieved 2026-09-08 describe a tree up to 30 m tall, with 9–13 leaflets per leaf and red sap. The record details small flowers and a knife-shaped fruit wing about 7.5 cm long and up to 4.5 cm wide; the fruit has 4–5 seeds. Its distribution field says it is known only from Panama.
