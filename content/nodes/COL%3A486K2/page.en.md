## A sampled biogeographic hypothesis {#s-83a40723f6}
The Discussion reports sampled pluvialis/posadae as allopatric sister taxa. The authors propose Andean linearity anddry transverse valleys as possible isolation mechanisms, but explicitly note no studies designed to test those valleys’ diversification effects. This selected mitochondrial/geographic association is not direct gene-flow measurement, a new divergence clock or a settled relationship for all unsampled populations.
