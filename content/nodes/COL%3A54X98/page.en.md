## Tarenaya siliculifera {#s-c90ab75c7e}
## Ciliate leaf margins accompany rounded petals

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records 3–5 leaflets on each leaf. The petioles and blades lack spines, and the entire leaf margins are fringed with hairs. Bract stalks have no basal spines.

The sepals are oblong to inversely lance-shaped, and the petals are round. A disc-shaped nectary sits near a nearly round to inversely egg-shaped ovary. The style is about 1 mm long and the stigma does not expand. The fruit is elliptic to inversely egg-shaped.
