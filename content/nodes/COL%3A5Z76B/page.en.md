## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-ad4622cc6f}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Cinnyris chloropygius (Jardine, 1842) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Sample scope for beak, wing and tail {#s-60508051f7}
Morphology covers 12 birds: 2 female, 9 male and 1 unsexed; 11 birds have complete measurement sets. Nostril-to-tip beak length 15.2 mm, tarsus 14.7 mm, unflattened wing 47.5 mm, tail 31.8 mm and hand-wing index 14.6 are historical row means. Inference=NO does not establish 12 independent records in every field; complete sets do not supply each field’s denominator. Beak length alone does not establish adaptation to particular flower species, tongue structure or pollination effects. Hand-wing index is Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, not flight speed.

## Keep the compiled-mass denominator {#s-d3fe188ec0}
Mass=6.2 g and Mass.Source=Dunning denote a compiled species average; the 12 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book and individual weights were not read, leaving sex, season and locality minimum–maximum bounds unsupported.

## Omnivore is retained within a sunbird genus {#s-bea0dad253}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Omnivore and Trophic.Level=Omnivore. Omnivore niche means using multiple food niches in relatively equal proportions; Nectarivore in other genus rows does not replace this record. Omnivore level means obtaining resources from multiple trophic levels in roughly equal proportions, separately from the niche definition. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-a11b73bb58}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Shrubland; density code 2 means semi-open vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=2 is partial migration, which may include a minority travelling long distances or most birds making short-distance, nomadic or distinct altitudinal movements; this row does not distinguish these alternatives. These historical categories provide neither current range-wide occupancy nor individual movement radii or routes; historical map fields are not interpreted in this batch.

## Evidence limits {#s-de1a84830a}
This page uses selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two crosswalks, relevant Metadata definitions and local COL ci/ne shards. Crosswalk names and older synonyms do not guarantee equivalence between historical concepts and COL26.8; source spellings such as preussi, neergardi and coccinigaster are retained. Morphology birds, complete sets and separately compiled mass keep different denominators. Individual specimens, Dunning’s book, the whole workbook and a new full methods-paper reading were not examined. Current maps, conservation, plumage, calls, breeding, tongue structure, pollination effects, flower or prey species, observed diets and movement routes are unsupported here.
