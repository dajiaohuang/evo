## Polygala peduncularis {#s-d1302d76cf}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala peduncularis* as an accepted species. Its regional account describes a sprawling shrub to 1 m, often velvety on young growth. The linear-oblong leaves have rolled-under margins and may be hairy. Large purple flowers occur in small numbers on stout axillary racemes; the side petals are divided into nearly equal lobes with a small tooth between them. The stated habitat is rocky slopes, not a complete range.
