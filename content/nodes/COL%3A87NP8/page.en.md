## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d577ba462f}
COL26.8 records Agama picticauda as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Agamidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-83420b7a92}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/rock-dwelling (Saxicolous) [source label: Arboreal/Saxicolous]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bars-Closel et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-aa39b95b27}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-5a22f9ce9c}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
