The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as concealed (Cryptic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-a8a92fdc75}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
