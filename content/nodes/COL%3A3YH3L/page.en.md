## A name in a historical dataset {#s-b2e31ca4d0}
COL26.8 accepts Maxomys whiteheadi in the family Muridae. PanTHERIA 1.0 links this record through the MSW05 name in its August 2008 table; MSW05 is a historical mammal classification. Its biological data gathering ended in June 2004. This name association does not resolve later taxonomic changes.

## Consolidated body-size values {#s-9c620c59cf}
The table reports adult body mass of 54.33 g and adult head–body length of 167.5 mm. The values were adjusted for sex and then summarized by medians. The mass field can include adults or animals of unspecified age but excludes pregnant females; head–body length excludes the tail. These are historical summaries, not measurements of one specimen or the limits of a species-wide range.

## A historical litter-size entry {#s-5b6570b6c9}
PanTHERIA reports three offspring per litter. This is a historical table entry, not a complete account of reproduction or development. The value does not provide a litter-size range or establish how the records were distributed across individuals.

## A historical range-map estimate {#s-27ff0b3f46}
PanTHERIA reports 1,330,551.32 km² for area derived from historical range maps and latitude endpoints from −5.94° to 7.98°. This map area is not an individual home range and does not show that every point inside it was occupied. The endpoints are not a locality list, nor do these historical values establish current distribution or population size.

## An older record of feeding and activity {#s-1e2e30aba6}
EltonTraits 1.0 has a historical same-name, same-family entry with fields for diet, foraging position and activity. Its taxonomy follows an earlier scheme; the name match does not settle later splits or synonym disputes.

## Weights assigned to food descriptions {#s-6cbd069a83}
EltonTraits assigns 30% to fruit, 30% to other plant material (including fungi in the source category), 20% to seeds, nuts and spores, 10% to invertebrates and 10% to unspecified vertebrates. These weights standardize written descriptions; they are not measured shares of food intake. The ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and an estimate may use a related species in the same genus judged ecologically similar. The weights therefore are not all direct observations of Maxomys whiteheadi. A zero weight would not prove that a food is never eaten.

## A broad foraging-position category {#s-bb7ae2e54d}
The entry assigns the species to G, the ground category; the source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Archive metadata attributes mammalian stratum labels collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a broad category, not a measure of time spent there, and it does not mean the animal uses no other position.

## An activity label, not a daily schedule {#s-6b74f25ceb}
EltonTraits marks nocturnal activity as 1. Several activity fields can be 1 together, so this code is not a share of a 24-hour budget or a schedule guaranteed across days, seasons and sites. Its ABC confidence code can include a related species in the same genus; no genus- or family-level typical value is used to fill this entry.

## Following the EltonTraits source labels {#s-dcf12039a6}
The fields carry the labels Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, position and activity can come from different material, and a Ref number in the bird table does not necessarily identify the same work as that number in the mammal table. Original labels are retained by file, row and field group. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so these labels are trace information, not additional observations.

## Evidence limits {#s-edb9d2b7bf}
This page brings together three historical sources with different scopes. PanTHERIA 1.0 summarizes its WR05/2008 entry: continuous traits are generally medians, some adjusted for sex or method with a generalized linear model, and missing *_EXT extrapolations are not used. The August 2015 life-history archive reports compilation summaries and may inherit data across historical species and subspecies. EltonTraits 1.0 turns literature descriptions into coarse food weights and categories; its ABC codes can include material from a related species in the same genus. The underlying publications have not been individually reviewed here. Matching historical names do not establish equivalence with COL26.8, and these records do not provide a complete account of current ecology, range, population or conservation.
