## A terrestrial category in regional data {#s-a1d9cac6e1}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Funaria calvescens Schwägr. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

It does not give a collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

This habitat category is the only biological information taken from the regional record; it is not a complete species account.
