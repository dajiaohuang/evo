## The deposited caecilian record {#s-d315eef0fd}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis daribokensis in the genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) species concept.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-33a01c96a5}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” Its accompanying species note says the animal occurs in forests beside rivers and has been found under leaf litter, dry leaves, mud, soil and debris. These source descriptions do not show how often each setting is used or establish a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-0d5ccdc4d0}
The compilation classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. The accompanying metadata describes this as presumed, probable or expected, so it remains a qualified compilation statement rather than an unqualified observation.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-a127cfac1e}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
