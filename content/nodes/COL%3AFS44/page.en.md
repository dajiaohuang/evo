## Apodolirion macowanii: bulb and flower characters {#s-57ed766f51}
The regional SANBI account describes a globose bulb 1 in in diameter, with a neck 1–1.5 in long. The leaves are linear, firm in texture and markedly crisped. The spathe and peduncle are concealed within the bulb neck. The perianth tube is 3–4 in long; its white limb is 1–1.5 in long, with oblong-lanceolate segments 0.25–0.33 in broad. The anthers are 0.167 in long and arranged in two superposed rows.
