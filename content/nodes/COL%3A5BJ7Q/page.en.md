## How to read reproductive records {#s-6547c16696}
The reproductive-mode field uses the original label viviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 10. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.
