## ## Finely divided leaves and racemose flowers {#s-cc23506a6b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character fields describe bipinnate leaves with 3–10 pairs of pinnae and 11–21 leaflets on each pinna. Leaflet dimensions are recorded as 2–10 by 1–7, but the field supplies no unit. The inflorescence is racemose, with brown puberulous hairs and lanceolate bracts 2–5 mm long. The flower stalk has an articulation 2–2.5 mm below the floral cup (hypanthium), and the lateral petals are 12–13 mm long. This translated regional character list leaves the leaflet units unspecified.
