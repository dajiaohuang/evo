## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton claussenianus? {#s-1bc47a7ed0}
Young branches have scale-like or star-shaped scale-like hairs. The short-stalked leaves are oval-lanceolate or lance-shaped, with scale-like star hairs above and scale-like hairs beneath; their side veins loop toward the margin. Margins are entire, nectar glands are absent, and young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are shortly stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are also shortly stalked, with separate sepals and a two-lobed style. Calyx lobes may be alike or unequal, and female petals are reduced to glands. The bud arrangement is valvate or reduplicate-valvate. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
