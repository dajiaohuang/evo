## Long silky hairs at the base of the fruit {#s-3a8890e29d}
This account describes a perennial herb up to 60 cm tall, with flower heads surrounded by several rows of bracts. The fruits have pappus scales longer than the fruit itself and a conspicuous tuft of long, silky hairs at the base. The ray florets are pale yellow and the disk florets yellow. It grows in grassland.
