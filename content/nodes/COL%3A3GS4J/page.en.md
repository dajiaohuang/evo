## Gomesa barbata {#s-98edbe8725}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records Gomesa barbata as tufted, with smooth, four-angled pseudobulbs and oblong-ovate leaves. Its upright, branched racemes bear yellow flowers with rounded marks; the lip has conspicuous side lobes and a smooth, five-lobed callus. The habitat field lists it as epiphytic.
