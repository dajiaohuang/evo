## What leaflet and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-f9559cdcf6}
The central leaflets are elliptic or elliptic-ovate, with blunt tips. The fruit tip may be depressed, flat or slightly pointed. Its stalk is 15–40 mm long; the outer surface of each fruit segment is smooth or has only a faint keel.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-85be2d7eec}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
