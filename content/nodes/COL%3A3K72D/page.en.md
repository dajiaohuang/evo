## Helichrysum nudifolium {#s-190134c486}
## A highly variable grassland herb

Hilliard's *Flora of Southern Africa* account describes a perennial herb up to 1.5 m tall. Leaves vary widely in shape, width and hair covering, without a clear geographic pattern in the account. Basal leaves may be linear-lanceolate, elliptic or ovate, up to 600 by 130 mm, with petioles; stem leaves are similar but smaller and stalkless. Both surfaces range from rough to cobwebby or white-woolly. Heads are narrowly bell-shaped and numerous in large corymb-like panicles; bracts are pale or lemon-yellow, sometimes with pale-brown outer bracts.

The account notes different leaf and flower-cluster forms in the KwaZulu-Natal Drakensberg, and tentatively suggests local ecological races. It says a full field study is needed, some specimens are difficult to place, and crossing with *H. miconiifolium* is possible. Grassland is the recorded habitat; these observations do not establish geographic divisions.
