## Babiana torta {#s-d7fe36aa2a}
Babiana torta is a small geophyte whose leaves coil while young and broaden as they mature. Its few-flowered spike appears in late autumn, mainly from May to mid-June, and the pale blue to mauve flowers usually carry white or pale-yellow markings on the lower tepals. The account notes occasional white or greenish-mauve flowers and variation in leaf and bract hairiness. It is recorded from granite outcrops, often in rock cracks; these are regional account observations, not a full-range survey.
