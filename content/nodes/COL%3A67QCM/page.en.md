## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-4b4c4c1b05}
A. patagonicus is grouped with early Last Glacial Period expansion and late decline, while emperor A. forsteri declines and then recovers. Large-bodied congeners can differ in historical response; shared candidate diving or size-related genes do not require identical ecology or demographic change. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
