## Three veins and dense hairs below {#s-79e28ab2e6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes tree-like hairs on young stems. Leaves are elliptic, with an acuminate tip, a base that extends down the stalk or is wedge-shaped, and entire margins without cilia. Three main veins begin above the leaf base; the underside is densely covered by sessile star-shaped or tree-like hairs. Flowers are grouped in compact clusters. The bell-shaped hypanthium is paired with a calyx that falls away and has flat-ended lobes; each flower has five petals. The anther connective may or may not extend, and its appendage is absent or a single tooth on the dorsal side. White anthers have one medium-sized pore, while the ovary is hairless.
