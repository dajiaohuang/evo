## Xyris bialata {#s-74b42a6185}
## Two wings on a long flower stalk

The flora record describes flattened, ridged leaf blades and a long to very long flowering stalk, at least 50 cm in the recorded length categories. Two lateral wings run along the stalk. Its oval or cylindrical spike bears 10–25 sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers; the habitat label is terrestrial.
