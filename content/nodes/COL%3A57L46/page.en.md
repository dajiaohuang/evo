## What leaf, flower, and fruit traits are noted for Tovomita mangle? {#s-b61abcc259}
The record describes slight stilt roots and clear exudate. Its stalked leaves are oblong or obovate, with arched side veins; they may be green or dark purple when dry. Male and female plants have dichasial flower clusters. Buds are oblong with pointed tips. Flowers have two sepals and four petals, with white, cylindrical stamens or staminodes. The female structures have four chambers and four styles. Fruits may be spherical or pear-shaped, with four valves, a smooth unribbed outer wall, and red inner flesh. Seeds bear an orange, vein-marked aril.
