## Disa uniflora {#s-130ec7f361}
The South African description gives Disa uniflora a flexible to upright, stolon-forming, tuberous growth habit, with one to a few carmine-red to orange flowers. It notes substantial geographic variation in plant size, floral-part shape and colour, including occasional yellow forms and a reported white albino. Wet cliffs, stream sides and seeps are recorded habitats.
