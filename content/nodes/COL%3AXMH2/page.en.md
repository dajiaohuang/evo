## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-40f2a97bdd}
COL26.8 records Coniophanes taylori as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-b4b0f6c351}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021.
