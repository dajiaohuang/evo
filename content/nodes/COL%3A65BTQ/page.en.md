## A record of stems, flowers and fruit {#s-555a7cae47}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row describes erect or tufted, bristly stems and pinnate leaves with oblong leaflets. It records crowded, many-flowered racemes in the leaf axils, yellow flowers, and curved, jointed fruits with kidney-shaped, glossy brown or black seeds. The row lists alternative states for several traits, so it should be read as a regional description rather than a survey of variation across the species.
