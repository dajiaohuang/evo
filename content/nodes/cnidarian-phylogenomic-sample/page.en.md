## A 41-taxon test of cnidarian relationships {#s-c452aa3a29}
The matrix combines 15 new transcriptomes with 26 public genomes or transcriptomes to compare living cnidarian branches. It supports several named groups, but Hexacorallia is weakly supported because Ceriantharia’s position is equivocal. These results describe sampled living taxa; they do not date fossils or settle the placement of extinct forms.
