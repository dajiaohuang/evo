## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-bfc4e60446}
The archive marks these foraging-stratum estimates as species-level: 60% in understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m; 20% on the ground; and 20% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a full-day time budget. The source-defined strata are not elevation bands and cannot give flight heights or habitat area.
