## *Dendropanax fluminensis*: suprabasal veins and racemose umbels {#s-9c55386f9f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character field lists elliptic to ovate leaves, with entire or finely toothed margins near the tip and visible speckling underneath. A pair of veins rises above the leaf base. Umbrella-shaped flower clusters are arranged along a raceme; the field lists two to five secondary branches and up to 30 flowers per umbel.

Flower stalks reach 7 mm, and the ovary is described as having three or four chambers. Fruits are shorter than their stalks; the styles may be free or joined into a column up to about 1 mm. The listed alternatives are regional character states, not population frequencies.
