## *Sorocea bonplandii* {#s-d21210c594}
The field records deciduous stipules 4.5–9 mm long. Leaf blades are acuminate, with an acute or blunt, asymmetric base; the lower surface ranges from hairless to minutely hairy, and the margin has small spine-like teeth. Blades are 1–4.2 cm wide and petioles 1–6 mm long. Male and female inflorescences are racemes or spikes, with different recorded lengths and stalks; male flower stalks are 0.3–3.5 mm, and stamens bend outward at flowering. Female flower stalks are 1–4 mm; a sterile pistil may be present. These are source-field ranges and alternatives.
