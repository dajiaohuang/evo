## What flower structures are recorded for Sandwithia guyanensis? {#s-41172814b7}
Male flowers grow in panicles. Their anther connective has a flattened tip, the style is undivided, the anthers are nearly round, and the sepals are joined. Stamens are 5–10 mm long.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d8a9271b8b}
The dataset records it as terrestrial, without a named locality.
