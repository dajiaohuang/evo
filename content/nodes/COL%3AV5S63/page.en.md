## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-06d1ad2564}
The 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release accepts Boulenophrys shuichengensis (Tian & Sun, 1995) as a species. Its recorded placement is Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). This identifies the catalogue name and classification; biological observations below retain the scope of their own sources.

## Adults in forest litter near Zhaxi {#s-b902d41e6f}
The 2026 account recorded adults in mountains around Zhaxi, Weixin County, Yunnan, at about 1400 m. Although nearby streams were 1.5–2 m wide, most specimens were collected in thick litter beneath fir forest about 150 m from a stream, with little understory vegetation. The stream description included several surrounding forest types and a possible groundwater source; neither proximity nor that suggested source establishes an obligatory aquatic adult habitat.

## No field breeding behavior during June visits {#s-e6d98305fc}
During mid- to late-June 2024 surveys at the Yunnan site, the authors observed no courtship, amplexus (the mating clasp in frogs) or egg laying and heard no field vocalizations. Captured adults produced faint rapid sounds while vibrating their throats when held. Handling-associated sounds are not assigned as advertisement calls, and nondetection during these visits does not establish absence of breeding in June.

## Night tadpole observations at the Guizhou type locality {#s-2a1e7cec5a}
Tadpoles were collected at about 1900 m near Fenghuang, Liupanshui, Guizhou, in a slow-flowing stream 2–4 m wide with muddy or sandy margins and flat limestone rocks. At night they moved slowly along the water surface and hid beneath nearby rocks and sediment when illuminated. Repeated surveys there during 2023–2025 found no adults. These larval observations belong to the Guizhou locality, separately from the adult records in Yunnan; the reported regional mean annual temperature of 15 °C is not a measured larval-water requirement.

## The restricted locality account published in 2023 {#s-3fbf674598}
The 2023 monograph listed female holotype (the name-bearing specimen designated in the original description) LTHC 944001 from Fenghuang, Liupanshui, Guizhou, at 1850 m, noting the change from historical Shuicheng County to Zhongshan District. It then recognized the type-locality area at 1800–1870 m and considered a northern Guizhou Suiyang record suspect because associated voucher numbers were absent. This is explicitly the 2023 assessment; it does not replace the separately authored 2026 account of Yunnan adults and Guizhou larvae or establish absence at other sites.

## Cited adult sizes and contradictory vocal-sac descriptions {#s-d3b4201119}
The 2023 diagnosis synthesized earlier descriptions, reporting eight males at 102.0–118.3 mm snout–vent length (measured from the tip of the snout to the vent) and seven females at 99.8–115.6 mm. These are cited diagnostic samples rather than newly measured adults in that treatment. Anatomy included vomerine ridges (ridges on bones in the roof of the mouth) without teeth and one-third webbing of the fourth toe with wide lateral fringes. The diagnosis stated that collected males lacked nuptial pads or spines and subgular vocal sac (a throat pouch used when calling)s; the remarks retained the conflict between an earlier report of a vocal sac and a later revision to absence. That historical conflict is not resolved by assigning the 2026 handling-associated sounds as advertisement calls.

## Unresolved type-series counts in the 2023 review {#s-607008ae35}
The remarks compared an original designation of seven male, three female and thirty-four tadpole paratype (an additional specimen in the original type series)s with a measurement table listing a male allotype and only five male paratypes. The monograph regarded the allotype designation as invalid and kept the male paratype count uncertain because voucher numbers were unavailable and later re-designation did not settle it. It also described the 2000 use of the same holotype for another new-species description as creating an objective synonym. These are attributed nomenclatural interpretations; missing voucher identities and counts are not reconstructed.

## The evidence boundary of the 2023 group placement {#s-772e6e8a66}
The 2023 remarks stated that available molecular data were lacking and the phylogenetic placement was uncertain. Placement in Boulenophrys relied on morphological similarity, while its assignment to the boettgeri group followed two anonymous reviewers’ unpublished phylogenetic suggestions of affinity with B. caudoprocta. This records the basis of that dated decision; unpublished reviewer information is not presented as a publicly reproducible sequence analysis, and the 2023 lack-of-data statement is not asserted as a complete assessment of later research.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-560f90f877}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys shuichengensis as larger in body size than the gutu sample. That statement belongs to the paper’s comparison; it is not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. This single comparison does not show how often the character occurs across the species or provide a complete diagnosis, diet or developmental account.
