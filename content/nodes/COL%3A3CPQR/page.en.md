## *Euphorbia melanohydrata* {#s-f5e136766f}
Compared with *Euphorbia caput-medusae*, this account describes several erect, thicker stems bearing small tuberculate branches. Persistent woody flower stalks are often divided; each cyathial gland has three mostly simple, finger-like projections along its outer edge. This is a comparative diagnosis, not a full species description.

The source records sandy to gravelly plains as habitat; it does not specify a locality or sampling date.
