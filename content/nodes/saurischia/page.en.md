## Tawa anchors a source-bounded interval {#tawa-sample}
The displayed 215–213 Ma interval follows the Late Triassic Tawa-bearing sample in the cited study. A sampled character matrix places Tawa within Saurischia, but this does not make it a direct ancestor or establish the clade’s global first appearance. The interval describes the sampled material, not a complete Saurischia range.
