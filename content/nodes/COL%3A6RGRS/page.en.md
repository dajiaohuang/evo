## What leaf, flower-cluster and anther traits are recorded? {#s-a2edd4f4fc}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands on the stem and on petiolate leaves that have the same color on both faces and one vein. The inflorescence is a dichasium reduced to one or two flowers. Its bell-shaped floral cup bears spherical glands; oblong or lance-shaped sepals are glandular and shorter than the cup. Petals may be yellow, purple or two-colored. Anthers match one another in color and have four pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. These are alternatives in a regional dataset field.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-121d01b2f8}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without locality or collection date.
