## Cannomois anfracta {#s-61bfd4efc7}
## A comparison with Cannomois parviflora

The South African regional account compares Cannomois anfracta with C. parviflora: C. anfracta has no rhizomes (underground stems), and the tips of its male bracts (leaf-like structures beside flowers) range from blunt to pointed. The account lists well-drained sandy slopes and rocky plateaus as habitat. Both the comparison and habitat description belong to this regional flora treatment.
