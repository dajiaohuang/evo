## Hypochaeris glabra {#s-e0b517379d}
## A taprooted annual with yellow ligulate heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a taprooted annual herb with basal leaves whose blades are spatula-shaped. Its flower heads have yellow strap-shaped florets. The dry fruits are spindle-shaped; the beak is present only on fruits from the disc florets. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
