## Pithecellobium oblongum {#s-61cf55e26d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Pithecellobium oblongum* as an accepted species of *Pithecellobium* in Fabaceae. The Flora of Panama archive record retrieved by WFO MBG on 2026-09-08 describes a small tree with twice-divided compound leaves (leaflets arranged along a central stalk), glands on the leaf stalks, and small thorns beside them. White flowers are followed by coiled, reddish pods up to 10 cm long; the distribution field says Mexico to Panama.
