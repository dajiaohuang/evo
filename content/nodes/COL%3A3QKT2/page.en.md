## What stands out about Jamesbrittenia pedunculosa flowers? {#s-5f351d2bff}
This spreading annual reaches 4.5–40 cm and has coarse-lobed, toothed leaves. Yellow flowers grow in the leaf axils on unusually long, slender stalks. The upper lip is larger and slightly hooded; a dark patch marks the back of the swollen corolla tube.

## What else does the source say about the flower? {#s-9e2d2a3ecb}
The second account notes a tuft of long hairs inside the lower lip and sometimes two small dark projections beside the main patch. It says the long flower stalks suggest pollination by small bees; this is presented as an inference from flower form. The flowers are compared with some Hemimeris species from similar habitats.
