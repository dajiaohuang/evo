## Cupania schizoneura {#s-adbc870cb0}
In COL26.8, *Cupania schizoneura* Radlk. is retained as an accepted species in *Cupania*. The associated Brazilian Flora 2020 record contributes one habitat category: “Terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## What a terrestrial label does not locate {#s-5d6669c00e}
The flora row is linked to WFO usage wfo-0000629606. Its “Terrícola” entry identifies a habitat or substrate category, but names no collection site or survey date and does not establish the species’ full range. The field also leaves wild versus cultivated status unspecified.
