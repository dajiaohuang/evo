## Aloe simii: U-shaped leaves and pink flowers {#s-9a793a631f}
A South African regional account describes a stemless succulent herb up to 400 mm tall. Its pale-green leaves have a deep U-shaped channel in cross-section, and their sap is yellow. The bracts are as long as or longer than the pedicels and have 3–5 veins. The cylindrical racemes bear strawberry-pink flowers.

These characters and measurements come from a regional South African flora account cited to a 1997 publication. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish variation across the full range. It does not independently classify the material as native, naturalised, cultivated or domesticated.

The account records open woodland with a lush grass understorey as habitat.
