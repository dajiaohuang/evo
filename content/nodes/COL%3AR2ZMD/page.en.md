## Night-time use of mossy karst forest {#s-28422b8f6e}
The description knew the species only from its northern Guangdong type locality, at 200–300 m. It reported nocturnal frogs on mossy rocks and damp forest floor in evergreen broadleaf and secondary forest. These records describe the sampled karst site and do not establish exclusive habitat requirements or a complete range.

## Seasonal congregation and juvenile records {#s-88bd7dccf2}
The account associated March–June breeding activity with small, steep moss-covered waterfalls emerging from karst caves and reported juveniles in June. Mid-July surveys found no individuals. These are dated seasonal observations, without proving annual absence after June or establishing all egg and larval habitats.
