## Distribution and habitat {#s-547b9dfca8}
The 2020 assessment compiles sparse, dated encounters rather than a continuous modern survey: eight records came from Maiko National Park in 2005 after 680 km of effort across two sectors; three red-colobus vocalizations were heard near the Ituri River in 2007, but observers could not determine which bank they came from; and one animal was seen in Usala after more than 200 km of reconnaissance. The assessment also notes conflicting use of semlikiensis and ellioti for neighboring, variable populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-f9675343b1}
The cited encounters are historical and sparse; the bank-level identity of the Ituri calls was unresolved, and nearby taxonomic boundaries remain fluid.
