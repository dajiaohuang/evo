## Stream substrates and pitfall captures {#s-bce0fa1d61}
At Sekayu, the account reports frogs on mossy granite and low plants beside steep stream edges, as well as pitfall captures. Two Peres River specimens came from a small stream and a drift-fenced pitfall; a Bubu River specimen was collected at the earlier report’s locality. The captures and observed resting surfaces are different kinds of evidence and do not establish habitat preference.
