## Anf1643 and its source comment {#s-298e311434}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1643 is the entry for Fejervarya granosa, placed by the source in Fejervarya (Dicroglossidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

The AmphiBIO OBS field preserves the source comment “Placed in the genus Zakerana by Howlader 2011”. It is presented as a historical note, not as an independently verified current taxonomic or conservation conclusion.
