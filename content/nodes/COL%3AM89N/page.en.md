## Bobartia gracilis {#s-4d9a28a4b3}
Bobartia gracilis is a rhizomatous perennial with flexible, round to nearly round leaves and an upright, rigid spathe that continues the line of the stem. Its yellow flowers are arranged in several cymes, and the account gives a July-to-April flowering period. Smooth, nearly spherical capsules, 5–8 mm long, help distinguish it from the superficially similar B. aphylla. The source describes it from grassy hills and Albany Thicket and notes that its limits against forms of B. orientalis remain difficult to interpret.
