## A broad ridge and comparison-specific tail shape {#s-4184d9a048}
In comparison with sheni, the paper attributed to broadoridgus a broad thick middorsal ridge with indistinct nodules, robust tail musculature, a thin high dorsal fin fold and a rounded tail tip. It reported TH/TW = 1.25 for eight comparison specimens, meaning tail height relative to tail-base width; this is a dimensionless reported value, not an independently certified mean or range. Its toe order was III > IV > II > V > I. Tail shape and the reported ratio are sample-scoped comparative characters rather than universal cutoffs, locomotor performance or evidence of an aquatic-only life history.
