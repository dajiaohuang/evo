## What stem and flower structures are listed? {#s-420001455b}
The entry records distinct or dimorphic stem forms. Stipules are symmetrical, and the petiole is distinct. Flowers have an androphore—a stalk supporting the male organs—with two stamens; the stigma is simple. The fruit surface is ribbed.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-695df813dd}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as aquatic and gives no locality.
