## A name in a historical dataset {#s-0a846937f7}
COL26.8 accepts Mastomys coucha in the family Muridae. The PanTHERIA 1.0 entry is linked through the MSW05 name in its August 2008 table; its biological data gathering ended in June 2004. This is a historical name match, not a resolution of later taxonomic changes.

## One consolidated body-mass value {#s-4520195cb4}
PanTHERIA reports adult body mass as 53.81 g. The value was adjusted for sex and then summarized by a median. The source definition can include adults or animals of unspecified age but excludes pregnant females. It is a consolidated historical estimate, not one animal’s measurement or the limits of a species-wide size range.

## Litter size and time between litters {#s-abd6efd53f}
The historical table reports 7.96 offspring per litter and an interbirth interval of 35.74 days. A fractional litter size is a summary across source records, not a literal litter. The interval is also a consolidated historical value; the archive does not establish that every source record came from a captive study.

## A historical range-map estimate {#s-6d3c4fbb8e}
PanTHERIA reports 636,382.99 km² for area derived from historical range maps and latitude endpoints from −34.21° to −17.94°. This mapped area is not an individual home range and does not show that every point inside it was occupied. The endpoints are not a list of localities, nor do these historical values establish current distribution or population size.

## A second historical life-history compilation {#s-762f5e99cc}
The August 2015 amniote life-history archive has a same-name, same-family entry for Mastomys coucha. Four focal fields have cited rows at species level. ## Adult and newborn mass describe different stages {#s-ab0f06ed8f}
The archive reports an adult body-mass summary of 53.81 g and a birth-mass summary of 2.12 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth mass describes a different life stage. These values do not follow the same individuals through growth and are not a range of individual masses.

## A compiled litter-size value {#s-d3301fd2cc}
The archive’s litter-or-clutch field reports 7.96 offspring or eggs. It combines counts of offspring or eggs, and the fractional summary does not mean one event produced a fraction of an individual. No missing reproductive or developmental duration is inferred here.

## The longest record is not a typical lifespan {#s-e15cb18f0f}
The archive reports 2.91 years for maximum_longevity_y, using the largest record in its collected material. This is not a typical lifespan, a mean wild lifespan, a survival limit for every individual or evidence that the same record is currently reproducible.

## An older record of feeding and activity {#s-fbf55c49ce}
EltonTraits 1.0 contains a historical same-name, same-family entry with fields for diet, foraging position and activity. Its taxonomy follows an earlier scheme; matching the current name and family does not resolve later splits or synonym disputes.

## Relative weights assigned to food descriptions {#s-3104eba308}
EltonTraits assigns 40% to invertebrates, 30% to seeds, nuts and spores, and 30% to other plant material; the last category also includes fungi. These are weights standardized from descriptions, not measured shares of food intake. The ABC confidence code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and a field may draw on a congener judged ecologically similar. Thus the weights are not all direct observations of this species. A zero weight would not prove that a food is never eaten.

## A broad foraging-position category {#s-74baee2270}
The entry assigns the species to G, the ground category; the source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Archive metadata attributes mammalian stratum labels collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a broad category, not a measure of time spent there, and it does not mean the animal uses no other position.

## An activity label, not a daily schedule {#s-39fa6c9a4c}
EltonTraits marks nocturnal activity as 1. Several activity fields can be 1 at the same time, so this code is not a share of a 24-hour budget or a schedule guaranteed across days, seasons and sites. The ABC confidence code can include an ecologically similar congener; no genus- or family-level typical value is used to fill this entry.

## Following the EltonTraits source labels {#s-5b49d13d79}
The fields carry the labels Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, position and activity can come from different material, and a Ref number in the bird table does not necessarily identify the same source as that number in the mammal table. Original labels are retained by file, row and field group. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so the labels are trace information, not additional observations.

## Evidence limits {#s-8613f7dea2}
This page brings together three historical sources with different scopes. PanTHERIA 1.0 reports consolidated values from its WR05/2008 entry: continuous traits are generally medians, with some adjusted for sex or method using a generalized linear model (GLM). Missing fields are not filled from *_EXT extrapolations, and source conditions vary by variable. The August 2015 life-history compilation can inherit data across historical species and subspecies; values are compilation summaries, not individual measurements or current population surveys. EltonTraits 1.0 converts literature descriptions into coarse food weights and categorical fields; its ABC codes can include material from a congener considered ecologically similar. The underlying publications have not been individually reviewed here. None of the matching historical names establishes that older taxonomic concepts are equivalent to COL26.8. Current distribution, population, conservation, and unreported traits require other sources.
