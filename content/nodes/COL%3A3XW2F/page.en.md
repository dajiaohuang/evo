## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0088641843}
COL26.8 records Manouria impressa as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Testudinidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-319b7b3183}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 350 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Günther 1882.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-ce25cef956}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as plant-eating (Herbivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Günther 1882. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Günther 1882.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1ca6d38b0b}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 13.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-c4562c1a7b}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
