## What the Eocursor holotype shows about early ornithischians {#s-298b23bfbe}
Holotype SAM-PK-K8025 preserves a partial skull, vertebrae and limb bones from South Africa’s Lower Elliot Formation. A matrix of anatomical characters places Eocursor among early ornithischians, but that placement is an analytical result, not direct evidence that this individual was an ancestor or the group’s first appearance. The broad age interval follows formation-level correlations rather than a direct date on the specimen.
