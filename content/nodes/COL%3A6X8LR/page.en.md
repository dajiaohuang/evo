## Two basal leaves and cream flowers {#s-b688e0185b}
Romulea leipoldtii usually has two basal leaves and large white to cream flowers with tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) mostly over 5 mm wide. Yellow colour extends from the cup onto the lower half of the tepals; the inner bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) are firm-textured.

## Damp sandy sites {#s-e90b22ded1}
The regional account records damp sandy sites as habitat.
