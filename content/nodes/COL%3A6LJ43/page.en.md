## How does its upright flower cluster differ in the record? {#s-4be6f9c15b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes *Heliconia tenebrosa* with a short, thick rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) and an upright flower cluster. Its petiolate leaves are listed as elliptical or oblong. The bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) may be arranged in two ranks and have straight tips; other bract and flower details are recorded as alternatives.
