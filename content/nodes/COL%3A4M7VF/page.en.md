## Pouteria ucuqui Pires & R.E.Schult. {#s-312c4e4b53}
## Smooth yellow fruit with a broad seed scar

Pouteria ucuqui has young branchlets ranging from smooth to hairy, with no lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have cylindrical petioles and eucamptodromous veins. Axillary or branch-borne clusters carry five-parted flowers with a cup-shaped, five-lobed corolla. The ovary has two chambers. The smooth, hairless fruit is yellow and contains one or two smooth seeds with broad scars.
