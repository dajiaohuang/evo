## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Sauvagesia vellozii? {#s-da807cd4cc}
The woody stem does not branch from the base. Alternate leaves are 10–20 mm long, oblanceolate, with scalloped margins and stalkless, pointed glands; secondary veins are conspicuous. Terminal pyramidal panicles bear long side racemes. Flowers have upright pedicels; the sepals lack marginal glands, and the white star-shaped petals spread without overlapping. Outer staminodes are absent. Inner staminodes are fused and pink with white toward the tip. The single anther is pointed.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1e8979faaf}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
