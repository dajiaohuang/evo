## Stem surface {#s-a4f41b4351}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as glabrous, with hairs and bristles absent. It also records sparse prickles that point backward (retrorse).

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-9302742054}
Stipules are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries as absent and projections between pairs of pinnae as present. The inflorescence is globose, and its floral bracts are smaller than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-cbba8cf9ff}
The perianth is recorded as having four parts. The calyx is membranous, with triangular lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and no hairs. The androecium is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are white.

## Habitat category {#s-6a07dcf2e3}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not identify a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
