## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5de3112f8d}
COL26.8 records Macrochelys suwanniensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelydridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-5f727fa33f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Thomas et al. 2014.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0002c5ab8c}
The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
