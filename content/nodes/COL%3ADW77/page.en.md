## A regional subspecies distinction {#s-22c81a913a}
The regional account describes A. dregeanum as a decumbent perennial: some stems lie along the ground. It places the southern African plants in subsp. dregeanum. The account distinguishes subsp. mossambicense, recorded from northern Mozambique, by generally narrower leaves on shorter stalks and looser flower clusters. “Subsp.” means subspecies; these are the account’s taxonomic and diagnostic statements.

## Moist habitats, with a qualified grassland note {#s-eaf88ebb55}
The habitat account lists moist or moderately moist settings, most often forests and also bush. It marks grassland as a rare, possibly derived habitat, retaining the question mark in the source rather than presenting that interpretation as settled.
