## Accepted name and placement {#s-69966e6fa6}
COL26.8 lists Brachycephalus ferruginus Alves, Ribeiro, Haddad & Reis, 2006 at species rank within Brachycephalus, in family Brachycephalidae and order Anura. This records the catalogue placement in the release dated 20 August 2026; it does not establish that other checklists use the same species concept.

## Sex-specific measurements retain variable-specific sample counts {#s-699b27bfe3}
In the 2025 comparison, males measured 11.6–12.5 mm snout–vent length (N = 9) and females 13.0–14.5 mm (N = 4). Dorsal and lateral skin were both recorded as densely rough (n = 14 for each trait). Each denominator belongs to its own variable; the observed ranges do not establish species-wide extremes, growth or statistically demonstrated sexual dimorphism.

## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-0d96d259b8}
The authors describe live animals as orange, sometimes with brown dorsal spots or a midline mark. In preserved specimens, bright areas appeared cream with darker markings. These are comparative observations, not standardized colour measurements; preserved appearance cannot substitute for live colour, and colour alone is not a complete identification rule.

## Advertisement-call comparisons use distinct acoustic units {#s-d65b7f32f2}
For analyzed calls, note groups and attenuated notes were recorded as present (N = 7 animals for each trait). The maximum was two notes per group and three pulses per note, again N = 7. A note, a group of notes and a pulse are different acoustic units. These sample-based maxima are not fixed limits for every population.

## The 2025 assessment separates records, mapped habitat and risk {#s-6acd0771e2}
The study reports observed elevations of 965–1,537 m above sea level, extent of occurrence (EOO) of 100 km², and area of occupancy (AOO) bounds of 4–100 km²; it identifies one threat-defined location. EOO, grid-based AOO, mapped habitat and specimen records measure different things, and unavailable estimates are not zero. The authors proposed Least Concern (LC) in that assessment; this is not a current official-list check or a population census.
