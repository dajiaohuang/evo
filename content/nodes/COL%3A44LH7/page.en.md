## Muraltia stokoei {#s-26551b75f3}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia stokoei* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a densely leafy subshrub to 20 cm, with soft hairs on the young parts. Its clustered, lance-shaped leaves have bristly margins and lower midribs and a small pointed tip; pink, sessile flowers sit in the leaf axils. Hairy, horned capsules are also recorded. The stated habitat is rocky sandstone slopes.
