## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-575c10df51}
Changchengopterus pani is the accepted species name in the selected PBDB records, with Changchengopterus as its recorded parent. Two occurrences come from two collections and two reference attributions; these are database record units, not specimen or individual counts.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-0658c15925}
Mutoudeng (Hebei) and Daxishan, Linglongta (Liaoning) are both recorded in China, in the Tiaojishan strata. Each collection has a 168.2–152.21 Ma bracket, spanning Late Bathonian to Early Kimmeridgian endpoints. The two collection records do not form a complete species distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-24950a06e3}
The selected records have an outer envelope of 168.2–152.21 Ma. This shared database bracket does not mean continuous occurrence through that interval or establish a species origin, extinction or lifespan.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-c3743fa944}
Mutoudeng is labelled “lacustrine indet.” with preservation mode “body”; Daxishan, Linglongta is also labelled “lacustrine indet.” and “body, adpression, soft parts.” These are collection-level descriptions, not the species’ living habitat or the condition of every specimen.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-0e01298b89}
The database attributes Mutoudeng to Lü (2009), ref:30706, and Daxishan, Linglongta to Zhou and Schoch (2011), ref:35658. These brief source attributions do not necessarily identify the original species descriptions.

## Evidence limits {#s-03f0abc4ac}
This account follows two selected PBDB collections recorded on 2026-10-02. Their common stratum and age bracket do not establish continuous presence or a complete range; collection environment and preservation labels do not determine ecology.
