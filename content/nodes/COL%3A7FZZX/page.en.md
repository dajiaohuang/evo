## *Xyris lanulobractea*: fringed edges on the bracts {#s-e9187523e0}
The leaf blades are cylindrical to angular, and a visible ligule sits where each blade joins its sheath. The flower stalk reaches 20–70 cm. Its egg-shaped or inversely egg-shaped head bears 10–25 bracts and flowers; the bract edges are fringed or woolly.

The source lists it as terrestrial.
