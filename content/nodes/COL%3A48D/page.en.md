## Tinamous provide flighted comparison lineages {#s-80516bc855}
What can genomes from living tinamous tell us about flight loss elsewhere among paleognaths? Two papers published in 2019 analyze four tinamous: Crypturellus cinnamomeus, Eudromia elegans, Nothoprocta perdicaria and Tinamus guttatus. The first three were assembled for those studies; the last was an existing genome. The analyses inform the proposed moa–tinamou relationship and flight-loss history in other lineages, but they do not sample every tinamou species. A candidate regulatory sequence from E. elegans was also tested in a chicken-embryo reporter assay. Activity in that experimental host does not show how tinamous fly in nature.
