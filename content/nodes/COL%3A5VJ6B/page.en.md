## Anthurium organense: a regional morphology record {#s-47cc5c103a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and a fibrous base or stem. Petioles are marked smooth, but their compound shape field combines several forms and edge states, so it is not reduced to one shape. Leaf blades are entire and flat, linear or ovate, with acuminate, apiculate, acute or obtuse tips and obtuse or truncate bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is obtuse on both surfaces; secondary veins are coded as obscure or impressed above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a boat-shaped, linear or ovate spathe at an acute angle to it. The entry gives conflicting states for the spathe stipe (present and shorter than the spadix, or absent). The fruit is a berry, with no color character supplied.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Compound petiole and stipe states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
