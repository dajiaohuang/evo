## Syagrus coronata {#s-26826fe45b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes Syagrus coronata with a solitary aerial stem bearing persistent leaf-sheath remnants and conspicuous scars. Glaucous pinnate leaves have stiff, grouped leaflets at differing angles. The branched inflorescence is between the leaves; ovoid to ellipsoid fruit has a yellow-green hairy skin and fibrous, juicy mesocarp. Habitat is terrestrial.
