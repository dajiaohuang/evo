## What range of leaf, flower and fruit traits is recorded for Banisteriopsis schizoptera? {#s-82970c5c0c}
Leaves are opposite or in threes, with elliptic blades and stalked glands on the side veins. The upper surface may be silky-haired, appressed-haired or becoming hairless; the lower is silky-haired. Axillary or terminal clusters have four flowers. Bracts fall in the bud stage, and the stalkless pedicels are velvety. Calyx oil glands may be green or brown; petals are white or pale pink. Anther chambers have sparse hairs, and the styles are equal. The fruit bears lateral projections, and its seed-bearing center has cross-braced ridges.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3911f0df39}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
