## Large flowers and partly enclosed filaments {#s-e0fa2f9c0c}
Ixia dieramoides has narrow, leathery leaves and larger pale-blue, mauve or watery-pink flowers than I. capillaris. Its tube is 13–18 mm long, with the filaments and often the anther bases inside; plant height varies from about 250 mm in poor, dry ground to 600 mm in good growth.
