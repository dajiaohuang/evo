## Rhodospatha arborescens: measured leaf and inflorescence traits {#s-fd9148c61b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes the stem as non-climbing. Petioles measure 16–65 cm, with a petiole-to-blade length ratio of 0.7–1.7. The petiole sheath measures 15–59 cm; its length is 0.25–0.90 of total petiole length when the pulvinus is included. The sheath does not extend to the pulvinus, and its texture ranges from decomposed to a persistent fibrous remnant. The apical pulvinus state is unknown.

Blades are described as elliptic, narrowly oblong or oblong, 21–25 cm long and 11–15.5 cm wide; their length-to-width ratio is 1.6–1.9. The field lists the base as acuminate at the very base, acute, obtuse or rounded. Spadices without a stipe measure 5.5–15.9 cm; the stipe measures 0.3–1.5 cm. The peduncle-to-combined-spadix-and-stipe length ratio is 0.9–2.2, and peduncles measure 12–38 cm. Basal flowers are described as dense and regular in shape and size.

These measurements and descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.
