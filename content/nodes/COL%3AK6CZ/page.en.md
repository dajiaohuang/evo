## Babiana blanda {#s-b3de99f59b}
Babiana blanda has large, radially symmetrical rosy-pink flowers. Its regional account notes that wild plants were already seriously threatened by loss of seasonally wet habitat, alien Acacia encroachment and pasturage, and describes possible hybrid populations with B. villosula near Klipheuwel and Joostenberg. The recorded habitat is sandy flats. These are statements in the cited account; they are not a current conservation assessment or a survey of all populations.
