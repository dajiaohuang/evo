## Selaginella vestiens {#s-8874dae013}
## Upright stems with ventral rhizophores

The flora description records upright, smooth, greenish or straw-coloured stems without joints and with ventral rhizophores. The small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are ovate-lanceolate or ovate-deltoid, with heart-shaped bases and acute or acuminate tips; distinctive idioblast cells are present. Dorsal leaves have several ovate forms and variable bases, but no idioblasts are recorded there. Axillary leaves are lanceolate or ovate-lanceolate, with rounded or slightly heart-shaped bases and acute tips.
