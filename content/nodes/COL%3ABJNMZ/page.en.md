## A concave, channelled leaf {#s-f3f969a3c8}
The Flora of North America account describes stem leaves as channelled and concave, 1.1–1.8 mm long. Their apex is sometimes incurved, and the costa—the central leaf rib—is broadly channelled. These are characters in the regional taxonomic description.
