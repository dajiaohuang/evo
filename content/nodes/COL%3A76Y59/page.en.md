## A sagittate blade and a light-green spadix {#s-38491af3ba}
The accepted name follows Catalogue of Life COL26.8 usage 76Y59 and a matched World Flora Online usage wfo-0000269083. The biological details below come from the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional morphology field.

COL26.8 accepted species ID 76Y59 and exact name/authorship map through exactly one accepted WFO usage wfo-0000269083; the Brazilian Flora source record carries the same WFO ID.

A regional morphology field describes Philodendron vargealtense with a sagittate blade, an axillary inflorescence, and a light-green or cream spadix. The recorded ovary-chamber and ovule counts remain alternatives.

## Morphology and flowers {#s-a5d8bae733}
The field records clasping roots and short internodes; its stem description also lists “bright gray / dark green” without clearly assigning the two colours to separate structures. The sheath is inconspicuous, while the petiole is conspicuous and smooth. The entire blade is sagittate with a sagittate base, and primary lateral veins are recorded on both sides. The axillary, sympodial inflorescence is one unit. Its short green peduncle is shorter than the slightly constricted spathe. The tube is light green outside, and the outer and inner spathe surfaces are recorded as light green; the inner blade is green. The subsessile spadix is light green or cream.

The flowers have short stamens and staminodes. The gynoecium and ovary are ovoid. The record gives 8 or 9 locules, 5 or 6 ovules, subbasal placentation, and a discoid stigma. Berry and seed traits are unknown.
