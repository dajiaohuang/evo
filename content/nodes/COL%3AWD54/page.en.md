## What leaf and flower traits identify Clusia grammadenioides? {#s-40aa61da38}
Its stalked leaves range from obovate to elliptic or oblong, and from leathery to slightly leathery; secretory channels are conspicuous. The flower clusters may stand upright or hang down. Flowers have five sepals and five petals, white or cream corollas, and 16–30 stamens that are separate or joined only at the base. In male flowers, resin is absent and the pistillode is absent or greatly reduced. The ovary has five chambers. Sepals and staminodes may remain on the mature fruit, while petals fall; seeds bear an aril.
