## Two leaflet pairs and a twisting mature pod {#s-8b66fce83b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field records two pairs of obovate leaflets, with rounded tips that are notched and end in a short point. It describes the plant's covering as hispid, meaning furnished with stiff hairs. The flowers are yellow with 10 stamens; mature pod valves twist, and the seed shape is recorded as flattened, from quadrangular to rhomboid. A separate habitat field labels the species terrestrial. The regional entry gives no sampling frame, locality, date, or wild/cultivated status.
