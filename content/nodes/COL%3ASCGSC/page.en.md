## Accepted name and placement {#s-8227d0ace6}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis resistencia Ortega, Brito-M. & Ron, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Small sex samples and a sometimes inconspicuous fold {#s-4c76cdc0dd}
The diagnosis reported one female at SVL 24.59 mm and a male value 18.90 mm associated with three males, without an explicit range in that sentence. The latter is retained as the reported value rather than assigned an invented spread. Resistencia had a tympanic membrane and annulus, basal toe webbing, broad digital fringes and no vocal slits or nuptial pads. Its discoidal fold could be invisible in some individuals, so failure to see it is not automatically anatomical absence. These small samples do not establish population-wide sexual size limits.

## Ankaku cloud-forest nighttime perches {#s-f061d9ff6c}
Resistencia was known around Ankaku Community in Llanganates National Park at reported elevations 2,271–2,451 m. Frogs were collected at night on leaves or branches 60–300 cm above ground in epiphyte-rich cloud forest; the holotype and one paratype were collected during a rainy night. These are encounter conditions, not a rainfall-trigger experiment, a complete breeding season or evidence that eggs are laid at those perch heights. No generalization to the full ecosystem elevation band or all nearby forests is warranted by these local records.

## Nearby deforestation and a dated recommendation {#s-1528abc140}
The authors recommended Data Deficient because population size and distribution were poorly known. A 2018 map showed deforested areas 7.2 km from collection localities and prompted concern over advancing anthropogenic land use. That mapped distance is not a measured resistance threshold, a population decline rate or direct evidence of mortality at the sampled sites. The recommendation is source-dated rather than a verified present category, and the account does not establish current occupancy or effective protection across the full range.
