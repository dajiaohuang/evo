## What Pojetaia shell sections show about early bivalves {#s-59fb02e92a}
Sections of Pojetaia and Fordilla shells show a layered inner fabric that the study compares with foliated aragonite in living monoplacophorans. Preservation can alter shell minerals, and structural resemblance alone does not establish shared origin: the study does not identify undisputed Cambrian nacre, or mother-of-pearl.
