## Leaves and flowers in a regional record {#s-ec749e8f51}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset row records no xylopodium, a woody underground structure. The stem is pubescent, meaning covered with fine hairs; the leaf entry records no petiolar nectaries and 9–21 leaflets. Flower stalks (peduncles) are longer than 5 mm, with 8–13 flowers per inflorescence and sterile flowers also present. The source describes the bisexual flowers' petals as green with wine-coloured tips. These traits come from one regional row and do not establish the species' full variation or global form.

A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
