## Distinguish averages from records {#s-8e9fc89f96}
Gestation and female age at first reproduction are reported in 2 records each; a maximum-lifespan record is available in 1. First reproduction means age when conception is possible, not age at first birth or sexual maturity. The longest recorded lifespan is not an average.
