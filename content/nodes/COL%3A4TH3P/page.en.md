## *Roupala meisneri* {#s-fde7d3ece1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records adult leaves as simple, with a distinct petiole. Hairs on the lower blade surface partly shed; the blade margin is fully serrated, and the midrib does not fork below the tip. Hairs persist on the inflorescence axis; flower buds and ovary are hairy, hypogynous glands are free, and fruits are curved. These are selected source characters.
