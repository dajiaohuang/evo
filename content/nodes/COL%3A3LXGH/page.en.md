## White-rayed heads along forest edges {#s-fc35c16819}
This record describes a scrambling, much-branched shrub 0.5–2 m tall, with alternate, deeply lobed leaves covered in hairs and glands. Its flower heads may stand alone or form loose clusters: white ray florets, sometimes pink-tinged, surround yellow central florets. The species is recorded from forest edges, streambanks and sandy riverbeds at 500–2,000 m.
