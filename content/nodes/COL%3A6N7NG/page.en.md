## ## Triangular stipules and a hairy fruit {#s-cf3f74cf4b}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with lenticels and surfaces ranging from glabrous to woolly. The triangular stipules fall away; each leaf has two pairs of leaflets and stalked, cylindrical nectaries. Racemes bear stalked flowers with bell-shaped calyces. The flat fruit has woody valves and a villous surface. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
