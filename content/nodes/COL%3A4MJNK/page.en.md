## The reported relationship keeps its comparison scope {#s-a0c29e8f85}

Its earlier group placement relied on external resemblance; the authors interpret discordance as consistent with convergence and propose euphronides-group placement. That interpretation is a comparative hypothesis, not an experimental demonstration of the selective process producing the resemblance.
