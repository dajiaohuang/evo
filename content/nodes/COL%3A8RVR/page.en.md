## How does the record describe Acalypha digynostachya? {#s-59b9ff924d}
Its woody stems lie along the ground, with simple, non-glandular hairs pressed close to the surface. Leaves have pinnate veins, entire to wavy margins, and no stipels. Axillary and terminal male flower clusters and subterminal female clusters are recorded. Female flowers are sessile or nearly so, with three calyx parts and upright, deeply divided bracts. Their separate styles have regular narrow divisions. The fruit is smooth and hairless; seeds lack a caruncle and have a pitted surface.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5919511c01}
It is categorized as terrestrial, without a site-specific description.
