## Relative weights of food categories {#s-0861c1ca3c}
The listed food weights are invertebrates 40%, mammals and birds 30%, and reptiles and amphibians 30%. Source code A marks high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize literature descriptions; they are not direct measurements of diet, and unlisted foods are not proven absent.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-7c2d989993}
Species-level foraging weights are ground 80% and understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m 20%. These are relative weights for feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a daily time budget. The archive's categories have distinct definitions; they are not elevation bands and do not give flight heights or habitat area.

## Trace each field group to its source labels {#s-2f83824ce8}
The selected field groups carry the label Ref_5. Diet, foraging strata and activity may draw on different materials, and Ref_5 in a bird table need not identify the same source as Ref_5 in a mammal table. The archive preserves original labels by file, row, field group and reference table. The underlying works have not been individually read; labels do not add independent observations.

## Limits of the evidence {#s-44521af748}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical data guide, not a complete species account. Food and foraging percentages are weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of diet, intake or feeding time. Source labels have not been individually traced to the underlying works, and some mammal entries may refer to a congener. Local and seasonal variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
