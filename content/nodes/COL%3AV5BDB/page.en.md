Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). ## Heishiding type-locality records {#s-f6408c9d25}
Male holotype SYS a002275 was recorded from Heishiding Nature Reserve, Fengkai, Guangdong, at 399.2 m. Citing Li and colleagues (2014), the monograph knew the species only from that type locality at 399–435 m. This is the locality knowledge reported by the treatment, not proof of absence elsewhere or a physiological elevation limit.

## Measurements retain the uneven sex sample {#s-42f7c1fc34}
The examined type material consisted of one adult male and six adult females. The male measured 35.6 mm snout–vent length; females measured 37.5–41.2 mm. The single male value is not a male size range, and this small, uneven sample does not establish a population-wide distribution of body size or a statistical estimate of sexual dimorphism.

## Diagnostic limbs, skin and oral characters {#s-818612941d}
The diagnosis reported vomerine ridges without vomerine teeth and a smooth dorsum with scattered granules, tubercles and an interrupted X-shaped ridge. Hindlimbs were robust; the heels did not meet when the hindlimbs were folded. Toes had rudimentary basal webbing without lateral fringes. The collected male had a subgular vocal sac and lacked nuptial pads or spines. These are anatomical descriptions, not evidence of locomotor performance or breeding behaviour.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-6214edd68a}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys obesa as with heels not meeting when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
