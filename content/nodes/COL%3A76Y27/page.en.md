## Philodendron billietiae: leaf and inflorescence descriptors {#s-68fba1667b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field pairs clasping and feeding-root descriptors. Stems are listed as flagelliform or erect, with rough internodes. The sheath is inconspicuous; the petiole is conspicuous and yellow, flattened above and rounded below. Leaf blades are triangular or oblong, with a cuspidate tip and sagittate base. Primary lateral veins are listed as sparse or 3–5, with 2 or 3 acroscopic veins, no basioscopic veins and a long sinus; margins are entire or sinuate.

The inflorescence count per sympodium is listed as 4 or 5. Peduncles are striate and green or yellowish. The spathe is constricted, with a light-green outer tube and a pink inner surface with reddish resin; its outer blade is light green. The spadix is listed as subsessile or strongly decurrent. Its female zone is recorded as cylindrical, light green or cream; the sterile and fertile male zones are white.

Floral descriptors include 3, 4 or 5 free, short stamens and staminodes narrow at the base. The gynoecium and ovary are ovoid, and the gynoecium is yellow. Locule counts are listed as 8 or 10; the field gives 12 ovules described as hemianatropous, with axial placentation and a discoid stigma. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field joins alternative states and counts, which remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
