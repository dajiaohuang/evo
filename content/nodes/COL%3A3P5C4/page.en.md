## Which leaf and flower-head traits stand out? {#s-fbfe958a7d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records no xylopodium. Its leathery, flat leaves have crenate margins and may be petiolate or sessile. Mature flower heads are hemispherical and stalked; a stylopodium is present, while flattened appendages on the calyx lobes are absent. These are source-description traits, not a survey of population variation.

## What habitat category accompanies those traits? {#s-b001f36596}
The regional habitat field classifies Hyptis saxatilis as terrestrial. It names no collection locality or complete range.
