## The source concept {#s-959ed9bad5}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria flavescens as row Anf2820 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-8bab163627}
The row lists terrestrial and arboreal foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-5a78cb73d0}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 46.6 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.
