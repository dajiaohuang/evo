## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2e19c38b3d}
COL26.8 records Bothrops insularis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-8b712d55ed}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1180 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-fe915a8cd9}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-069b07d744}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Hiatt 1998, Waye and Shewchuk 2002, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-eeb474a222}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
