## Mikania luetzelburgii {#s-745a802d60}
## Five basal veins and a velvety stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright, velvety branches; their pith is recorded as hollow or solid. Opposite, ovate leaves have a heart-shaped base, an acute or rounded tip, and margins listed as entire or toothed. Both surfaces are hairy, with longer woolly hairs below. Five main veins radiate from the base; the blades are somewhat leathery.

The thyrsoid flowering cluster bears stalked heads. Bracts sit at the base of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around a head are fringed and blunt-tipped. The corolla limb is longer than its tube and has tapered lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. The habitat field says terrestrial; the regional entry does not establish the plant's full range.
