## Which fossil records this name represents {#s-0be2f789eb}
Cacibupteryx caribensis is the accepted species name in the selected PBDB records, with Cacibupteryx as the recorded parent. The guide includes two occurrences from two collections and two reference attributions; these database units are not specimen or individual counts.

## Begin with named collections and strata {#s-b38e8c8e13}
One collection is Mogote Jagua Vieja (Pinar del Rio), region code CU, stratum Jagua, bracketed at 159–154.8 Ma (Middle to Late Oxfordian). The other is Jagua Vieja (Pinar del Río), also CU, bracketed at 161.5–143.1 Ma (Late Jurassic). These are collection-specific ranges, not a complete species distribution.

## Age bounds of these records {#s-aef56ae445}
The selected collections have an outer age envelope of 161.5–143.1 Ma. It combines the oldest recorded early bound and youngest late bound; it is not a continuous record or a measured origin, extinction or lifespan.

## Read preservation setting separately from living habitat {#s-ec91242fce}
Mogote Jagua Vieja is labelled “carbonate indet.” with preservation mode “body”; Jagua Vieja is labelled “marine indet.” with “body, cast.” These describe collection settings and assemblages, not the animal’s living habitat or every specimen’s condition.

## Follow collection records back to references {#s-7606a8b30f}
Both collection records cite Gasparini et al. (2004), under identifiers ref:30567 and ref:90002. These are database attributions and do not by themselves establish that either citation is the original species description.

## Evidence limits {#s-eb5de83db7}
This account follows two selected PBDB collections recorded on 2026-10-02. Their accepted name, localities, age brackets and collection-level environment labels do not resolve every identification, provide a complete range or establish species ecology.
