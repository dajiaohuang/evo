## Short-tubed flowers on branched stems {#s-316482cbfe}
Ixia rigida reaches 600 mm and has two or three upright branches close to the stem axis. Its silvery translucent bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) surround flowers with a very short tube, up to 2.5 mm; leathery leaves have thickened margins and veins. The source notes variation between escarpment and inland populations, whose significance remains uncertain.

## Dolerite outcrops and heavy red clay {#s-5bf13c6384}
The account restricts the species to dolerite outcrops in rocky heavy red clay. It reports overgrazing at some sites and tentatively suggests near-threatened status, while also saying no imminent threat is known; this is the source’s assessment, not a new status determination.
