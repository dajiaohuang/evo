## What leaf and flower forms are noted for Couepia excelsa? {#s-6613722162}
The leaves are elliptic, oblong, or lance-shaped, with cobweb-like hairs. Flowers may be arranged in panicles or racemes. The receptacle is bell-shaped and bears 25–45 separate stamens. The outer fruit wall is smooth and hairless.
