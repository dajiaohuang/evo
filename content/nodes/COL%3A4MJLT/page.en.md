## Placement in the proposed Cryptomantis framework {#s-895e383958}
The 2025 study includes Pristimantis prolatus in the prolatus species group of its proposed subgenus Cryptomantis. The sampled major clade had bootstrap support 100 and its two group clades 99. Morphological synapomorphies were explicitly unknown. The dated placement does not certify a current catalogue subgenus, a complete range for this species or identity of every historical broadly identified voucher. ## Characters in the paganus comparison {#s-a9ea1bc2e5}
The paper distinguishes the new paganus population from Pristimantis prolatus using an H-shaped dorsal fold pattern, an acuminate snout and one conical upper-eyelid tubercle. These are comparative taxonomic characters, not a newly sampled population-wide trait-frequency analysis. Sex and body-region wording are retained.
