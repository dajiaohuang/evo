## Calea angusta {#s-cae314d1a1}
## Long stalks lift yellow heads above smooth, hairless stems

Flora do Brasil 2020 leaves the root type unknown. Stems are decumbent and hairless. Opposite leaves are sessile or nearly so, with elliptic to narrowly elliptic blades. Tips range from acute to blunt; margins are entire to finely toothed, and the base extends down along the stem. Leaves are hairless or become so with age.

Radiate flower heads form corymb-like clusters on long stalks, 8–23 cm. The bell-shaped involucre has one bract form; the conical receptacle bears narrow, linear paleae that fall away. Each head has 10 yellow ray florets, with three-lobed ligule tips, and 35–42 yellow disc florets.

The dry fruits are stiff-haired and slightly stalked; the attachment base extends along their ribs. The pappus consists of separate, lance-shaped scales 1.2–1.5 mm long. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
