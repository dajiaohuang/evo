## Pink bowl-shaped flowers {#s-dc3faddccb}
Tritonia squalida grows 25–40 cm tall and has nearly symmetrical, bowl-shaped flowers in pale to deep or dusty pink and mauve. The tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are more or less spoon-shaped, with broad translucent margins along the claws.

## Limestone and calcareous sands {#s-d26914b7b5}
The regional source records limestone outcrops and calcareous sands.
