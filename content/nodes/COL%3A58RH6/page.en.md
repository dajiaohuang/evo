## What leaf, flower and capsule traits are recorded? {#s-f92ea0b9e5}
The stems are decumbent, lying along the ground. Opposite elliptic or ovate leaves have persistent, divided stipules; the blades lack glands and are green beneath. The inflorescence may be a raceme or a panicle, and its bracts and sepals are not glandular. The flower has six stamens and three staminodes (sterile stamens). One petal type is elongated and swollen but hairless outside; another is spatula-shaped and hairy. Two globose glands have smooth lobes, and the ovary has three chambers. The elliptic capsule has a small pointed tip and opens lengthwise from tip to base around a persistent central framework (replum). Both the outer and inner fruit walls are hairy. The valves split at the tip, remain attached to the framework at the base and are angular along the back. Seeds have long, curled hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-6e6144dbad}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
