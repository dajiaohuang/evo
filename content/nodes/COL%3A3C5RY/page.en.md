## Alternative midrib descriptions and a one-seed record {#s-4ac69359b4}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves with wedge-shaped bases and acute or tapering tips. The leaves are papery, with flat margins and no hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is described as grooved or flat, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles; the source lists bracts as persistent or unknown. Smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup's condition is marked unknown, although the calyx is open in bud and each flower has four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo.

## A terrestrial label without range information {#s-34082b9d12}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This category adds no distribution statement.
