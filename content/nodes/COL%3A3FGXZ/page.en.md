## Large deep-pink flowers {#s-f618eac0e5}
Geissorhiza grandiflora grows 16–35 cm tall and has some of the largest flowers in its genus. The flowers are deep pink and bilaterally symmetrical, with the stamens and style arranged together on one side. Unequal stamens and branched stems are further diagnostic features.

## Rocky sandstone slopes {#s-17ca5eadaa}
The regional source records rocky sandstone slopes. One provisionally assigned Grootwinterhoek collection has smaller-than-usual flowers and uncertain colour, so that record remains tentative.
