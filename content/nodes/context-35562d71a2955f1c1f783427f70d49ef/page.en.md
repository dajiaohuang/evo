## The source concept {#s-6323328806}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Scinax hiemalis as row Anf2656 under genus Scinax, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-b9a9e73506}
The row lists terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-ae4b1367d1}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 34.4 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-2c41373dc5}
The row gives 270 offspring or eggs per clutch and up to one reproductive event per year. These are literature-derived counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions.
