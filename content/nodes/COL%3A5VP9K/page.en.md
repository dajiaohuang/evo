## Leaves and floral parts {#s-e6711225e7}
A regional species account describes this aquatic plant as having a rhizome (a stem that grows sideways), with floating leaves that are lanceolate to linear. Its flowers are arranged in two ranks, white to lilac, and have 1–3 tepals—the similar-looking floral parts not separated into petals and sepals; the account says the stamens are usually six.

## Ponds, marshes, and rivers {#s-8da19e7436}
The account lists ponds, marshes, and rivers as habitats. It is a regional flora description and gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency, so these settings are not a complete distribution map.
