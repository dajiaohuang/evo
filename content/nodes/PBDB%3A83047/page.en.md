## Recorded parent of Scelidotheriinae {#s-ddcd4fde24}
Scelidotheriinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Mylodontidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
