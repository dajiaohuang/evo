## Polygala rhinostigma {#s-b926564b9d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala rhinostigma* as an accepted species. One regional account describes an annual herb to 0.2 m with tufted, non-spiny stems and terminal racemes; it gives October–February as the flowering period and records pale-magenta petals. A second account describes lax racemes 5–15 cm long. Both source records give pink floral wings (expanded side parts of the flower) 6.5–7.0 × 3.5–4.0 mm. The habitat notes place it in mesic grassland, including grassland along watercourses; these are regional account statements.
