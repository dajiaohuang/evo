## Mimosa longistipula {#s-7a623f3544}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa longistipula* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records a woolly stem with forward-pressed, star-like branched bristles, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and spherical flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), 1–3-veined petal lobes with short hairs, and pink stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers). This regional description is not a complete account of worldwide variation.
