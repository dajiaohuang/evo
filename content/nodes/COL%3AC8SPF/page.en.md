## The original description compares cap and spores {#s-6d5e01165d}
The 2023 species account describes the type basidioma and microscopic characters, and compares its spores and pileus with Amanita orientifulva and A. suborientifulva.

## Fruiting records come from described forest settings {#s-e55434f924}
The authors report solitary-to-scattered fruiting on soil in subtropical forests dominated by Fagaceae, sometimes mixed with Pinus. This description comes from the paper’s field collections and herbarium records; it does not by itself establish resource dependence, seasonal patterns or population-level ecology.

## Direct collections cover several regions of China {#s-1028634cc8}
The paper reports Amanita sinofulva from eastern, central and southwestern China and lists dated collections from Anhui and Yunnan. The type material was collected in Nanjian, Dali, Yunnan, on 27 June 2015; additional Anhui and Yunnan collections date to 2017. Tibet and Hunan appear separately in an ITS-tree inference and are not treated here as direct collection records. The account is limited to the study, not an exhaustive global range survey.

## The close relationship is bounded by the study’s samples and loci {#s-04d1ee4db9}
The authors’ multilocus analysis places A. sinofulva close to A. orientifulva and A. suborientifulva. Their ITS analysis also includes additional provisionally identified or previously misidentified collections. This relationship is a result within the study’s sampling and analyses; it is not a broader phylogenetic synthesis and supplies no divergence dates.
