## What does the record say about Guatteria aberrans? {#s-1ba28a9911}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes leathery, narrow elliptic leaves with sparse hairs on both surfaces. Flowers may occur in leaf axils or on leafless stems, singly or in small groups. Their stalks are densely hairy; the free sepals are triangular to broadly triangular. The fruit segments are ellipsoid, and the seeds may be pitted or smooth.

## What broad habitat is recorded? {#s-bde1141d01}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a particular site.
