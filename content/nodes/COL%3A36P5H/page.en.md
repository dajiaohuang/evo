## Hairs, flowers and samara-like fruit {#s-59fef1f858}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional row records rust-coloured woolly or shaggy hairs with wavy strands on the branches, leaves, inflorescences and calyx. The leaflets are leathery; their upper surfaces range from densely hairy to sparsely hairy. Flower clusters are compact panicles, shorter than the adjacent leaves, and the flowers are up to 15 mm long. The fruit is samara-like: compressed from side to side, with the seed near its centre, and rigidly leathery. It exceeds 7 cm in length. These traits come from one regional entry and do not establish the species' full variation or global form.
