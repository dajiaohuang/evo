## Very large succulent stems and pink flowers {#s-e35b515141}
This account describes a large succulent biennial herb up to 1.5 m tall, with stems that may recline or stand upright. The stems are usually four-angled and winged. Broad, flat leaves are largest near the base, where they may reach 40 by 25 cm; their edges are wavy. Pink to pale-pink flowers are 4–6 cm across and have many petals, staminodes and stamens. The fruit has five chambers. Sandy plains are listed as habitat.
