## Limits attached to these records {#s-fe8af863d9}
These PanTHERIA and life-history routes cover selected historical records rather than all current descendants. The life-history compilation averages or takes medians from older sources, can inherit data across historical taxa, and keeps maximum lifespan as a largest record. EltonTraits categories reflect literature coding; relative weights are not measured intake and ABC fields may draw on a related species. The older name matches do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts.
