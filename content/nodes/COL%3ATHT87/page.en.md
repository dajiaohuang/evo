## Accepted name and placement {#s-8d86c4fb46}
COL26.8 accepts Synapturanus sacratus Osorno-Muñoz, Gutiérrez-Lamus, Lynch, Keefe, Caicedo-Portilla, Chan, Tonini & de Sá, 2023 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Synapturanus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A pale stripe distinguishes Synapturanus sacratus {#s-d47b591af2}
Synapturanus sacratus was described from Jirijirimo in Amazonas, Colombia, within Yaigojé Apaporis National Natural Park. The account identifies it as known only from the type locality and records afternoon collection among leaf litter in non-flooded terra firme forest. These are records from the described study, not a complete account of the species’ range or activity.

The diagnosis describes a dull cream stripe from the snout over the eye that becomes discontinuous along the flank; it also notes the absence of spotting on the posterior legs seen in many compared species. The paper’s type and referred series comprises the adult female holotype, four adult paratopotypes, and two referred juveniles. Appendix 2 reports measurements for four adults: females ICN 56890, 16.60 mm SVL, and ICN 56889, 17.30 mm; males ICN 56895, 15.10 mm, and ICN 56892, 14.00 mm. The comparison text on p. 162 instead prints female values 16.9 and 17.3 mm and male values 14.0 and 15.0 mm. We retain the specimen-labelled Appendix 2 values and leave that internal discrepancy unresolved. These measurements describe four specimens, not a population estimate.

## Limits of the measurements {#s-87d349bcdb}
The locality, diagnosis, and measurements here come from one species account. Appendix 2 labels four adult measurements; the full type and referred series also includes two juveniles. The discrepancy between Appendix 2 and the comparison prose is left unresolved, and these specimen measurements are not a population estimate.

## A pale stripe across the eye {#card}
The diagnosis of Synapturanus sacratus describes a dull cream stripe from the snout over the eye, becoming broken along the flank, and no spotting on the hind legs. The paper’s Appendix 2 gives labelled measurements for four adults; those specimen values describe four frogs, not a population estimate.
