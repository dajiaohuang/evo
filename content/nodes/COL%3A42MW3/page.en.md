## Basal veins and a branching flower cluster {#s-125c9421d5}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes star-shaped hairs on young stems. The leaf blade may be elliptic or oblong, with an acute tip, a rounded or blunt base and entire margins without cilia. Three or five main veins arise at the base. The underside has sparse to moderate sessile star-shaped hairs. Flowers are arranged in a dichasial cyme, a cluster with branches in pairs. The hypanthium is bell-shaped; the calyx persists with distinct lobes, and each flower has five petals. The anther connective extends and bears one dorsal tooth. White anthers have a single small pore; the ovary is described as hairless or hairy. A separate field labels the habitat “terrícola,” or terrestrial. The source lists alternatives without showing how they relate to individuals or populations.
