## Lessingianthus cristalinae {#s-2b73ec8cc2}
## Brown wool covers the lower leaf face

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and sparsely branched or unbranched stems with leaves along their length. Subleathery leaves range from elliptic and obovate to oblanceolate, with acute or blunt tips and tapered bases. The upper face may be hairless or silky; the lower is brown-woolly. Margins are entire or finely scalloped and do not roll under.

Stalked heads occur singly or in cymose clusters. Their bell-shaped involucres have 4–6 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 30, 35, 40, 45 or 50 florets. Top-shaped dry fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
