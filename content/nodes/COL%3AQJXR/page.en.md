## Hairy leaves and long pods {#s-c3763a0313}
The Flora of Panama describes Canavalia villosa as a vine twining on shrubs and trees. Each leaf has three large leaflets, usually 10 to 12 cm long; hairs cover both surfaces, especially the underside. The flowers are blue, purple or pink. Its pods are usually 14 to 17 cm long, with ridges along the seams and a covering of soft hairs as they mature.

## A source range with occasional records farther south {#s-1f5a5592d9}
The Flora of Panama field gives a range from northern Mexico to Panama and says the plant has been collected occasionally in South America. That wording distinguishes the stated range from those occasional records and does not establish a complete current distribution.
