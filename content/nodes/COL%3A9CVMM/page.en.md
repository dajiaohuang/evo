## ## A twining stem and compact inflorescence {#s-39137ad036}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a twining growth form. On the same plant, the leaflets are similar in shape, membranous to papery, pointed to tapering at the tip, and becoming nearly glabrous. Its inflorescence is congested, with a rachis shorter than 3.5 cm; flowers are under 2 cm long. The habitat category is “terrestrial.” This is a regional description and does not establish the species’ full variation or geographic range.
