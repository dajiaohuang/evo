## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Hevea guianensis? {#s-e2a7cfea90}
The stem is pubescent. The ascending leaflets are discolorous and may be pubescent or hairless; their tips are unknown. Terminal inflorescences bear male flowers with blunt buds. Their calyx is described as long, narrow, twisted, and hairy outside but hairless inside; the male-flower nectary is absent. The anthers occur in one or two incomplete whorls. The ovary is hairy, the female-flower nectary is absent, and the fruit opens explosively.
