## Stem surface {#s-c228c5753e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as glabrous, while separate hair fields list simple hairs and capitate glandular hairs; trichomes are described as incurved. These fields are retained as recorded rather than reconciled. Branched and unbranched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are listed as absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-2a64a9d985}
Stipules are deciduous, and the record marks extrafloral nectaries as absent. Projections between pairs of pinnae are listed as absent or present. The inflorescence is globose; floral bracts are shorter than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-1ec64a587b}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous; its lobes are listed as absent or triangular. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are glabrous. The androecium is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-bd2785be89}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
