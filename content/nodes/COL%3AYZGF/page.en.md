## What flower traits are recorded for Couepia morii? {#s-f1d0acf429}
The oblong leaves are described as woolly to pubescent, bearing dense or fine hairs. Flowers grow in panicles; the receptacle is cylindrical and carries 14–30 separate stamens. The fruit’s outer wall is not described in this record.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-aabf8944db}
The dataset classifies it as terrestrial; it does not name a specific site.
