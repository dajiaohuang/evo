## The source concept {#s-ebb545b913}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0100 records Mannophryne collaris in genus Mannophryne, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-d5c53a27ad}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The fields Fos=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this does not show that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=27 mm {#s-b6ed346b9d}
Body_size_mm=27 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
