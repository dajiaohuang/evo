## What features describe Lantana montevidensis? {#s-0112cd4c6a}
The record describes a low, partly woody plant with unarmed, trailing branches bearing simple and glandular hairs. Its opposite oval leaves have blunt or squared-off bases. Two unequal, persistent green bracts surround each flower cluster, which bears pink or lilac corollas. The fruit is vinous-colored and fleshy.
