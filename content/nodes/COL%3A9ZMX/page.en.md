## An acoustically supported first Pará record {#s-2ff90d03a2}
The 2025 study reported a specimen from Conceição do Araguaia as the first A. saci record in Pará, about 104 km west of the nearest previously reported locality at Colinas do Tocantins. Observed call parameters agreed with the cited description. The authors distinguished tonal notes of A. saci from pulsed notes of the externally similar A. martinezi. This is evidence supporting the local identification and geographic addition, not proof of continuous occupancy or a recent westward dispersal event.
