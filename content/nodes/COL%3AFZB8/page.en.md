## Aptychopsis subpungifolia: four recorded habitat labels {#s-7a7334a61f}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists “corticícola,” the bark-associated category, for *Aptychopsis subpungifolia*.

A second entry uses “epífita,” the epiphytic category.

A third records “epixila,” the wood-associated category.

The fourth uses “saxícola,” a rock-associated category.

These are source-labelled habitat categories in a regional dataset; they do not show how common each setting is. No collection locality, survey date or complete range is given.
