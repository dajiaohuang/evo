## *Selaginella simplex*: axillary rhizophores on a suberect stem {#s-88ecf073e0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description records a pale, hairless, partly upright stem without joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise in the leaf axils. Small leaves differ by position: lateral leaves are ovate or ovate-elliptic with rounded bases, while upper leaves are ovate-elliptic or lance-shaped with rounded to slightly wedge-shaped bases and tapering tips. Axillary leaves are ovate-elliptic, with rounded or shallowly heart-shaped bases.

The habitat field labels it terrestrial but gives no locality or survey date; the morphology is a regional character record.
