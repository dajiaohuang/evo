## Accepted name and classification {#s-4f40a67c2e}
COL26.8 accepts *Alytes cisternasii* Boscá, 1879 as a species. It places the frog in *Alytes*, within Alytidae and the order Anura. This name and placement follow the release dated 20 August 2026; another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity evidence.

This page brings together older diet, activity and life-history compilations, one morphology record and two thermal-test estimates. They come from separate sources and samples. A name match connects a record to a source; it does not show that every source used the same species concept as COL26.8.

For anurans, the dataset defines length as snout–vent length; for salamanders and caecilians it uses total length. ## Life-history values from the literature {#s-9dafbc83ee}
The Frost 2011 AmphiBIO entry reports sexual maturity at 2 years and 27–36 mm, maximum lifespan of 6 years, 60–180 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 4 reproductive events per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 2.6–3.5 mm. These are literature-derived extrema and counts, not measurements from one population. The row does not give sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value; the size field may refer to an offspring or an egg.

## Scope of these records {#s-3612d19466}
AmphiBIO uses a historical Frost 2011 classification; a later revision reports corrections to some older body-mass and microhabitat entries. The morphology and thermal records come from separate compilations and assays. Together, these selected values and categories do not form a complete or fully comparable account of the species.
