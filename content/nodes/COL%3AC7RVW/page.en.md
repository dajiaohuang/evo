## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3af7871aef}
COL26.8 records Micrurus carvalhoi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d5addbdfea}
The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
