## Anf2115 and the recorded concept {#s-6825fc9727}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf2115 is the entry for Hyla warreni, placed by the source in Hyla (Hylidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-456d928911}
For Hyla warreni, row Anf2115 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Activity category — twilight (dawn or dusk) {#s-1336d9791a}
The activity coding in row Anf2115 includes twilight (dawn or dusk). These are broad periods reported for the source entry, not a continuous behavioural time series; unlisted periods remain unrecorded rather than being treated as absent.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-954163f93f}
Its maximum adult body length is 42.1 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-5de32a0ee4}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
