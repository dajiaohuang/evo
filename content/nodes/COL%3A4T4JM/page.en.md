## Riocreuxia aberrans {#s-6d6e241ad7}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Riocreuxia aberrans* as an accepted species. A 1997 regional account describes a twining perennial herb with cream, bell-shaped flowers 18–20 mm long, whose tube is shorter than the lobes. A separate 2005 diagnosis identifies an erect habit with bell-shaped flowers. The habitat source records exposed quartzite ridges. Because the cited descriptions differ on habit, both are retained as source-specific statements rather than reconciled into one form.
