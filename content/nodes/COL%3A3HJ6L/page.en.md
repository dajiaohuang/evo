## Guapira noxia {#s-80b0866663}
## Shiny leaves vary in shape and underside hairs

The morphology record notes that no xylopodium is present. Leafy branch tips may be hairless or finely rust-haired. The glossy upper leaf surface contrasts with a lower surface that ranges from hairless to rust-pubescent; blade shapes vary from elliptic-lanceolate to oblong or nearly round, with rounded or wedge-shaped bases and acute or blunt tips. Tertiary veins form a network of small enclosed areas. Loose inflorescences range from rust-hairy to hairless and branch in opposite or nearly opposite pairs. Female flowers are tubular, male flowers bell-shaped; the male flowers are nearly sessile or sessile. The fruit tip has an open crown. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
