## Rhinocerotoidea in the PBDB record path {#s-da513c66da}
PBDB records Rhinocerotoidea at superfamily rank and this selected route links to 254 species occurrence guides. The parent chain organizes the database selection; it is not a phylogeny or a measure of evolutionary importance. These are selected non-extant fossil records, not a complete account of the group.

## Occurrences across 1,079 collections {#s-e56457ecf7}
Selected pages include Toxotherium hunteri, Siamolophus krabiense, Proeggysodon qiui, Eggysodon cadibonense, and E. osborni. The route contains 1,461 occurrences from 1,079 collections; Chambers Tuff, Cypress Hills, White River, and Chadron are example strata. Each occurrence keeps its own age bracket and identification qualifications, so compare records with their sources rather than treating the totals as animal or population counts.

## Limits of the PBDB occurrence selection {#s-12eabdd88d}
This route covers selected fossil occurrence records, not every descendant or a shared ecological profile. Counts do not measure specimens, individuals, populations, or sampling effort; outer age bounds do not date group origin or extinction. The PBDB parent path organizes records and remains distinct from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.

## Placement in the sampled analyses {#s-7a1e501a9b}
New early Eocene material and a broad ceratomorph matrix place several sampled taxa within or near Rhinocerotoidea, while parsimony and Bayesian trees differ at basal nodes; the result does not make any one specimen the group’s ancestor or global first occurrence.
