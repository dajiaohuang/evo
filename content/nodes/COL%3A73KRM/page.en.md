## *Mikania micrantha*: heart-shaped leaves on slender vines {#s-63ad91ae3b}
The Flora of Panama account, retrieved on 8 September 2026, describes a slender, sparsely branched vine with opposite, egg-shaped leaves. The leaves reach 3–13 cm long, have heart-shaped bases, three strong basal veins, and pale glandular dots. Small white flower heads, often in groups of three, form branching clusters. The account’s historical distribution statement lists Mexico, Central America, the West Indies, and northern South America. This is a regional historical statement, not a current range inventory.
