## Campylocentrum densiflorum {#s-db7f952fa8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records smooth cylindrical roots, a developed stem and two-ranked oblong leaves folded along the midline, each with a bilobed tip. Its three-lobed lip has a triangular middle lobe, and the spur is club-shaped and bent inward. The fruit is smooth and ellipsoid; the habitat field lists the species as epiphytic.
