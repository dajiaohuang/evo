## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-7739e1e528}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical branches with hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) persist and are described as lance-shaped or linear. Both the leaf stalk (petiole) and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; each leaf has two or three leaflet pairs. Stalked nectar glands (nectaries) are cup-shaped. The inflorescence is spike-like, the outer floral cup (calyx) is funnel-shaped, and the flowers are nearly stalkless. The pod walls (valves) are recorded as leathery or papery; the fruit is flat and hairless. This single regional dataset field does not establish variation or how widely these traits occur.

## Habitat category {#s-39fffd1623}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset calls the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is a category in one dataset row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
