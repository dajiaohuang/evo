## Zephyranthes mesochloa: flower and fruit characters {#s-7240a5a2d8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes erect, linear or nearly flat leaves up to 2 mm wide. The spathe bracts are joined for more than half their length, and the inflorescence bears one flower. The perianth is 2.1–3.0 cm long, with a nectar tube up to 2.0 mm. The main flower colour is white, with reddish tips on the tepals and white or green at their bases. Tepals are elliptic, obovate or lanceolate. There are six stamens, erect or arranged in two series. The style is longer than the stamens, and the stigma lobes are 1.1–2.0 mm long. The mature fruit is recorded as wider than long.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
