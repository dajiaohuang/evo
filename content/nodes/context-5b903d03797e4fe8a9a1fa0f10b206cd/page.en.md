## The source concept {#s-aec611c8e1}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis crusculum as row Anf0126 within Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. Its source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); equivalence with a current species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-b8cdcf27dd}
The row flags both terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. These are broad categories, not detailed accounts of habitat or seasonal use. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; its deposited values are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong. NA remains missing data.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-16a1810dcd}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 20 mm. For anurans, length is measured as snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a population range.

## Compiled life history {#s-31f7b2d3ee}
The row reports up to one reproductive event per year. No clutch-size, maturity, or offspring-size measurements are supplied here.

## Developmental strategy {#s-919c282cab}
The entry flags direct development; larval-stage and viviparity categories are not flagged. This classification does not provide details about breeding sites or parental care.
