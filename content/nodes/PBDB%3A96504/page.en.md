## Nyctosauridae: PBDB classification route {#s-bb16171e24}
The PBDB route records Nyctosauridae as an unranked clade and links two species guides: Muzquizopteryx coahuilensis and Barbaridactylus grandis. This is the database's recorded hierarchy, not a complete catalogue or a phylogenetic test. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a group-wide account of shared anatomy or ecology.

## Member material linked to Nyctosauridae {#s-bc5045ec3f}
The two selected species guides cover two occurrence rows from two collections; Couche III is one of the listed strata. Compare each collection's own locality, age bracket, and identification before drawing wider conclusions. The record totals do not measure ancient animal numbers.

## Limits of the selected Nyctosauridae records {#s-4f92527afc}
These pages summarize selected PBDB occurrences, not every member of the group or a shared ecological profile. Database rows and collections are not specimen, population, or sampling-intensity estimates; their age bounds do not directly date biological origin or extinction. The recorded parent route is distinct from phylogenetic treatments.
