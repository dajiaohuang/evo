## Tapered tips and axillary hairs {#s-d071da32ac}
The Flora of North America account describes the stems as slender and rigid, with stem and branch tips tapering into long points. It also records axillary hairs—hairlike structures at leaf axils—measuring 250–300 µm and 5–8 cells. This is a regional taxonomic description; the excerpt gives no sampling frame for those measurements.
