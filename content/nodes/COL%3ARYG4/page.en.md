## Cayaponia granatensis Cogn. {#s-bd659f2cf1}
## Leaf form changes with age; orange fruit holds three seeds

Cayaponia granatensis is a dioecious vine with smooth stems and tendrils that branch near the base into three arms. Juvenile leaves are simple and elliptic to narrowly ovate, 10–20 cm long; mature leaves are either three-lobed or divided into three leaflets and measure 14–25 cm in both length and width. Their tips are long-pointed or tail-like, and the surfaces are hairless, sometimes with a few small glands near the petiole. The foliage ranges from papery to leathery. Staminate flowers are white and borne in short clusters; mature pistillate flowers were not seen in the cited description. Fruits are reddish orange, globose and about 1.5 cm across, with a faintly pitted surface. Each fruit has three large, light-tan seeds with a smooth or nearly smooth coat. The cited range includes Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela, Peru and Panama.
