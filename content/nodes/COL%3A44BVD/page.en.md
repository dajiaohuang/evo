## What traits distinguish Moquilea longipetala in the record? {#s-c329d18472}
Its elliptic to oblong-elliptic leaves are hairless, and glands are present on the leaf stalks. Flowers grow in panicles or racemes; they are sessile to nearly sessile and have bell-shaped receptacles. The 14 stamens extend beyond the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is described as smooth and hairless.
