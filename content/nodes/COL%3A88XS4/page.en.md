## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e298fa546c}
COL26.8 records Phrynosoma ornatissimum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Phrynosomatidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-09e0651db4}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0f57fe8fd9}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-d4d3ad4577}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
