## Elegia galpinii {#s-7a20e49fa3}
## Almost-black sheaths on dry sandstone

Elegia galpinii is recorded as a rhizomatous perennial reaching 2 m, with culms clustered along the rhizomes. Its sheaths and female spathes are deciduous and almost black. The regional account associates it with dry sandstone slopes below 500 m elevation.
