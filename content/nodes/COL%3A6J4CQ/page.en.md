## Three substrate labels in a regional entry {#s-4f7aee6d73}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens radicans Mont. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat entries are categories, not named collection sites.

One category is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A second is “Epixila,” the source's wood-associated substrate category.

The third is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling. The rows give no collection localities, survey dates or complete range.

These fields do not supply locality, date, range or sampling-coverage details.

The three substrate categories are the biological content included from this regional record; they do not amount to a complete species account.
