## Branches and leaflets {#s-cb8f4776fc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes armed branches with cylindrical cross-sections and spiny stipules. Leaflets are oblong-elliptic, with blunt or shallowly notched tips and rounded bases. Their venation is cladodromous: side veins branch freely near the edge and fade before reaching it. The upper surface is glabrescent (becoming hairless); the lower surface is recorded as either glabrescent or hairy only along the primary veins.

## Inflorescences {#s-ac3c33ea5c}
The inflorescences are terminal and described as either racemes, with flowers along one axis, or panicles, which are branched clusters. Their bracts are leaf-like.

## Flowers {#s-c0acad7a6d}
The calyx is bell-shaped. Petal color is listed as pink, lilac or purple. Hairs are present on the standard, or banner petal, and the stamens are diadelphous, arranged in two groups.

## Fruit {#s-eeaacf86f0}
The fruit is recorded as a nucule, a small dry fruit. Its seed-bearing core is described as warty.
