## What do its seed capsule and filaments look like? {#s-a470dbac56}
One Flora of Panama description notes slender filaments that end in hair tufts or, more rarely, rudimentary anthers. The ovary and style are hairless, while the stigma is dotted and slightly set into the style tissue. The capsule is black and ovoid, about 10 × 7–8 mm, and opens along its chambers; numerous straw-colored, pitted, net-patterned seeds are about 1.5 mm long. The entry gives no population sample.

## How tall and colorful can the plant be? {#s-39b21b5c38}
A second description presents Lamourouxia gutierrezii as an erect perennial herb or shrub, possibly hemiparasitic, growing up to 2 m. Its leaves may be opposite or whorled, and its red or orange tubular corolla is 2.5–4 cm long. The source is a historical regional flora description retrieved from the 2013 Flora of Panama record; it does not define a population sample or current global range.

## What range did the Panama flora report? {#s-88f2279ec6}
The 2013 Flora of Panama statement reports Lamourouxia gutierrezii only from uplands of Panama and Costa Rica. This is a historical regional account, not a current global range inventory.
