## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-01ac224a04}
COL26.8 records Leptosiaphos vigintiserierum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework.
