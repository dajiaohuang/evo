## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-e95ece92bf}
The source records clustered stems up to 7 m tall, with black or yellow spines up to 4 cm long. Leaves are described as pinnate or entire. In the pinnate form, there are 12–33 pairs of lanceolate or S-shaped leaflets, grouped irregularly in sets of two to five at differing angles. The branched inflorescence grows between the leaves; its stalk bract is covered with fine hairs. Fruit shape is listed as flattened, obovoid, or beaked, with a black or purple colour and small spines on the outer layer.

The field records clustered stems up to 7 m tall with black or yellow spines up to 4 cm; pinnate or entire leaves, with 12–33 pairs of lanceolate or sigmoid leaflets in the pinnate form, irregularly grouped two to five at differing angles; a branched inflorescence between the leaves with a finely hairy stalk bract; and flattened, obovoid, or beaked fruit, black or purple, with small spines on the outer layer.
These traits come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; the field does not establish global morphology, population variation, sex, or life stage.
The field does not say whether the described plants were wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material.
