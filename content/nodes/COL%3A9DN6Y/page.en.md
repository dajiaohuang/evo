## Stylosanthes seabrana {#s-4076370ddf}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Stylosanthes seabrana* as an accepted species of *Stylosanthes* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists two inner bracteoles, a rudimentary axis, and a two-segment fruit whose beak is hooked and shorter than 3.5 mm. The seeds are yellow; the habitat field identifies a terrestrial setting.
