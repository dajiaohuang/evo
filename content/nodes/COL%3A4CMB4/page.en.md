## Traits used in the 2018 comparison {#s-0d3d50b33d}
In a 2018 revision of Parabembras, P. curta was compared with P. multisquamata by a combination of spine and scale counts and fin reach. The authors report 8–9 first dorsal-fin spines, 6–8 supraocular spines, 34–39 pored lateral-line scales, and a pectoral fin that does not reach the anus. These are diagnostic ranges in the revision, not estimates of variation within one population.

## The reported Pacific range {#s-f04f04617f}
The 2018 revision summarizes P. curta as known from the northwestern Pacific, from southern Japan to the South China Sea. This is a regional summary of records cited in that paper; it is not a complete locality list and does not establish present-day occupancy or abundance.
