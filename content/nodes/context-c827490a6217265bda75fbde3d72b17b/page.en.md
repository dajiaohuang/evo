## AmphiBIO species record {#s-2f026cb9b9}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Odorrana livida? Row Anf4945 places it in genus Odorrana, family Ranidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Seasonal categories in the compilation {#s-358b9b32a9}
The AmphiBIO row lists these seasonal categories: dry and warm. They compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they are not fixed calendar months that apply worldwide.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-f169343f7e}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 100.4 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-ff4008ba85}
For this source concept, the literature compilation records these life-history values: minimum number of offspring or eggs per clutch: 600 individuals/eggs; maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch: 3724 individuals/eggs; minimum offspring or egg size: 1.9 mm; maximum offspring or egg size: 2.4 mm. These are literature-reported minima, maxima and counts; the row does not tie each value to a sex, population, date or wild/captive setting. The source notes that offspring-size values may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental category in the record {#s-83bb844fc0}
The AmphiBIO record assigns this source concept the developmental category “a larval stage”. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
