## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-eb69d07431}
COL26.8 records Pedioplanis laticeps as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Lacertidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-c5ff157cae}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The foraging-mode field records AMB (ambush or sit-and-wait foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-009cb8132e}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 7.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
