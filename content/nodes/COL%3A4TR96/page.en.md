## Smooth leaves and magenta flowers in clusters {#s-462fc3c483}
This shrub has branches about 60–120 mm long and smooth, rounded leaves 20–40 by 4–7 mm, ending abruptly in a sharp tip. Cymes carry three to five magenta flowers with petals about 2 mm wide; filament-like staminodes are absent. The five-chambered fruit has a funnel- to bell-shaped base, large closing rodlets on the covering membranes and a narrow oval closing body. The record lists several soil types.
