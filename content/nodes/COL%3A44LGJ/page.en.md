## Muraltia satureioides {#s-18c48d3fa8}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia satureioides* as an accepted species. A regional account describes a sprawling shrub to 60 cm with widely branching stems. Its clustered linear to oblong leaves have short hairs along the margins and midrib and end in spines; pink or white flowers grow on short stalks in the leaf axils. The hairy capsules have prominent horns. The cited habitat is coastal calcareous sand.
