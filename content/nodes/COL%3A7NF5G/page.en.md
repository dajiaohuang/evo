## Allonautilus anatomy does not bound Nautiloidea {#s-81a07bee7f}
Anatomy of living *Allonautilus* supports recognizing a distinct genus within Nautilida and informs comparisons with selected fossils. It cannot resolve or time-bound the broader historical Nautiloidea grouping.
