## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f59d75f2c6}
COL26.8 records Emys trinacris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Emydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-547027a1fa}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 145 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2010.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-51de911e60}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-505d3b0de3}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
