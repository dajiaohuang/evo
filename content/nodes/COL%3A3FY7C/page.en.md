## Gethyllis setosa: leaf hairs and flower {#s-042c0d25b8}
A 2013 regional account describes a solitary bulbous geophyte about 70 mm tall. It records 4–8 leaves, dry at flowering, spreading to erect and not coiled. The leaf surfaces bear firm hairs attached near their centres and grouped lengthwise in clusters of five or more; the clusters join the blade by scale-like attachments. Elsewhere the hairs are somewhat spreading, while those at the leaf tips are dense, interlocking and straw-coloured. The flower is salver-shaped and white to pink.
