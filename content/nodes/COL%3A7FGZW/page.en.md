## Velvety flower heads above woolly leaves {#s-df2ea69a47}
This account describes stems with bristly or velvety hairs. Stalked leaves range from lance-shaped to ovate, with blunt bases and pointed tips; the upper surface is bristly and the lower woolly. Velvety, head-like flower clusters form at stem tips. The flowers have a bell-shaped calyx with pointed lobes and a funnel-shaped corolla over 2 cm long. The smooth fruit is ovoid. The habitat field says terrestrial.
