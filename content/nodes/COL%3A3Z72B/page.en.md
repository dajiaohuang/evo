## The name and this data account {#s-f1669266e9}
AVONET v7 records *Melanerpes chrysauchen* under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The sample has 5 measured individuals, 4 in a complete morphological set; individual trait means may use different sample sizes.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-329fa6ecdc}
Mean beak length is 17.5 mm from the anterior edge of the nostril to the tip. At that landmark, mean width is 6.2 mm and depth 6.5 mm. These standardized measurements are not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-79d1fc14d3}
Mean unflattened wing length (carpal joint to longest primary) is 110.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 18.5 mm. AVONET lists separate mean mass of 52.48 g in the Dunning category; it is not a measurement assigned to each morphological specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-909898a2d1}
AVONET assigns the record the habitat category Forest, trophic niche Invertivore and lifestyle Insessorial (perching-associated). These are broad comparison labels, not claims of exclusive habitat or food use.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-8b0a3881d6}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span 8.03°–9.76° latitude. This is not a current global range or a full account of non-breeding movement.

## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-af92e560e2}
The 2015 life-history archive has a same-name, same-family entry. Four focal fields are supported by cited species-level rows matching the name and family. This historical association does not remove possible inherited data.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-8c54fcb9af}
The archive reports adult body mass of 57.55 g. Adult summaries can combine male, female and sex-unspecified records; this is a compiled value, not an individual range. AVONET's 52.48 g mass mean comes from a separate source record.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-57f870dcb5}
Other listed summaries are 3.5 eggs per clutch, 11 days from laying to hatching, and 35.25 days to flight capability. Some source records describe the last field as fledging or leaving the nest. The fractional clutch value summarizes records; no single clutch contains a fraction of an egg.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-3a7f93e475}
Same-name source labels include Dunning (1992), Boback and Guyer (2003), Bennett (1986), Pereira, Daily and Roughgarden (2004), and others. The batch archive preserves the underlying row locators, values and complete reference explanations. Repeated labels are not independent replication.

## Read this historical record through food and foraging position {#s-2a0bfc501f}
EltonTraits 1.0 contains a historical record matching this name and family. This page uses its diet and foraging-stratum groups; the historical name match does not resolve later taxonomic changes.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-79e228b336}
EltonTraits assigns 60% to invertebrates and 40% to fruit among the non-zero diet weights; confidence code A denotes high compiler confidence. These percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than measure diet composition.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-f65850bc62}
Species-level foraging weights are trees or high bushes from 2 m to below the canopy 50%, and in or just above the canopy 50%. They describe feeding positions, not nesting, all habitat or daily activity time. Source strata are not elevations or flight heights.

## Evidence limits {#s-b0153e08d9}
This page combines historical AVONET, 2015 life-history and EltonTraits records. AVONET measurements are sample-based species averages; mass is separately compiled. Its habitat and migration fields are broad codes, and mapped latitudes describe historical breeding/resident geography. Life-history values are archive summaries rather than independent measurements of individuals; the matching historical name and family do not prove current taxonomic equivalence or eliminate inheritance. EltonTraits percentages standardize literature descriptions rather than report direct measurements. The underlying works have not all been reviewed; the page does not establish current range, population, conservation status or complete biology.
