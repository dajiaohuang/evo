## Source classification and concept status {#s-d1368ebf42}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2960 records *Agalychnis granulosa* under the source classification Agalychnis (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-55eb0934c9}
