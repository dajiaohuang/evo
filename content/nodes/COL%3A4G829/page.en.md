The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists feeding roots and a light-green prophyll. The sheath is inconspicuous; the petiole is slightly flattened on the upper side and rounded below. The inflorescence is axillary and sympodial. The peduncle is short and shorter than the spathe; the spathe is slightly constricted, and the spadix is subsessile. Stamen, staminode, gynoecium, ovary, locule, ovule, stigma, berry, and seed characters are marked unknown. The field lists axial, basal, subbasal, or biseriate placentation.
