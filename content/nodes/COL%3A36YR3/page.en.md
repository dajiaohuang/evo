## Ditassa mucronata {#s-8873573fe6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ditassa mucronata* as an accepted species of *Ditassa*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records two habitat categories: terrestrial and rupicolous, the latter indicating association with rock substrates. These regional labels do not identify collection localities or define the species’ full distribution; no morphology claim is included.
