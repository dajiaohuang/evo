## Northeastern survey {#s-036ecfca7e}
Schüßler and colleagues searched 109 nocturnal transects totaling 303.73 km across northeastern Madagascar during three periods in 2019, 2021 and 2022. They reported 23 direct observation occasions and compiled 31 locality records from earlier literature. These are sightings and mapped occurrence records, not counts of unique animals.

## Habitat observations {#s-423ecc91fb}
The survey recorded sightings in undisturbed, selectively logged and heavily logged primary forest, as well as in woody fallow and vanilla agroforestry; none was recorded in grass- or shrub-dominated fallows. The authors noted the small sample and did not statistically test the visually different vegetation structure. The records show where this survey found the lemur, not a general tolerance threshold for habitat disturbance.

## Home ranges at Analamazaotra {#s-5830defbc9}
Biebouw radiotracked animals at Analamazaotra Special Reserve in 2007. One complete sleeping group of four adults was followed for eight months and one partial group of two females for three months. Their group ranges were 35.5 and 16.0 ha by 100% minimum-convex-polygon estimates, or 15.2 and 7.1 ha by the 95% kernel method. These small-sample, site-specific estimates should be read with the method and group coverage attached.

## Model limits {#s-4dffda5be1}
