## Adesmia vallsii {#s-ddcf6f9039}
In COL26.8, *Adesmia vallsii* is accepted as a species in *Adesmia*, within Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records its habitat category as “Terrícola” (“terrestrial”). This is the category used in that dataset row: it gives no collection locality, survey date, global range, or sampling coverage, and does not specify whether the record concerns a wild, cultivated, or managed plant.
