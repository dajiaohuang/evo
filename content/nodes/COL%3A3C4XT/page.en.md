## Heart-shaped leaf bases and alternative flower clusters {#s-61f1e25b15}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes lance-shaped or oblong, leathery leaves with heart-shaped bases and tips that taper to a point. Their margins roll under or are wavy, without a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved; one or two veins run near the leaf edge. Flowers may grow in compact clusters called fascicles or singly. Bract status is unknown, while smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round or ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded; embryo status is unknown. These are source alternatives, without a quantified account of their distribution.
