## *Sebaea zeyheri* {#s-6e9bdfa77a}
## The stigma and style meet in a swelling

The SANBI/WFO morphology field describes an annual herb to 25 cm tall, with ovate leaves and yellow flowers in five lobes. The calyx lobes are winged, and the stamens attach between the corolla lobes. Here the style swelling is described as continuous with the stigma, a different arrangement from the swelling below the midpoint recorded for *S. scabra*. The short account is attributed to Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 *Plants of the Greater Cape Floristic Region*.
