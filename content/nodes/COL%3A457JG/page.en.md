## Ovate leaves and variable pyramidal clusters {#s-9f7d94b128}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and no hairs. Leaves are ovate, with an upper-surface midrib listed as raised or fully flattened; the underside is glabrous. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below leaf nodes are absent. Inflorescence branchlets may be compressed or rounded, with rounded bracteoles below the flowers. One to three inflorescences are listed per axillary shoot, in axillary, terminal, or mixed subterminal and terminal positions; the pyramidal arrangement may be alternate or opposite. These alternatives are retained from one regional dataset field, which gives no frequencies.

## Fused sepals and a fruiting calyx that stays attached {#s-d6747b50ff}
The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is obconic, or inversely cone-shaped, with silky hairs (sericeous). Sepals are fully fused, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens by tearing. On the globose fruit, its lobes wither but remain attached (marcescent). The field does not report frequencies for its alternative states.
