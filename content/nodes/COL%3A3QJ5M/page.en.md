## Jacaratia spinosa {#s-2bdd605ef4}
## Spiny tall stems carry large compound leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a spiny plant usually 12–20 m tall or taller, without a tuberous root. Its hairless compound leaves have 5, 7, 9, or more elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaflets, with smooth margins.

The flowers occur in panicles. The calyx is hairless with a toothed edge and is white to greenish; the inner surface of the petals has fine hairs. The ovoid to oblong fruit has five chambers and five angular faces. The seeds have ridges or small tubercles. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
