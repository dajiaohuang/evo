## Form described in the flora record {#s-d7a8d60c30}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem with fibers at the base and entire cataphylls (scale leaves) and prophylls (the first leaves on a shoot) at the stem tip. Petioles (leaf stalks) are cylindrical and smooth. Leaf blades are entire (undivided, with unbroken margins), flat, and ovate, with an acute tip and a hastate (spearhead-shaped) base; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is recorded as acute on the adaxial (upper) side and obtuse on the abaxial (lower) side. Secondary veins are prominent above, and a collecting vein (running along the leaf margin) extends over the full upper edge.

The inflorescence is larger than half the leaf size, with a cylindrical peduncle (inflorescence stalk). The spathe (floral bract) is flat and oblong; its angle to the peduncle is recorded as obtuse or acute. A stipe (supporting stalk) is recorded as shorter than the spadix (fleshy flower-bearing axis) or absent. The berries are green, with a wine-colored apex and green base.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
