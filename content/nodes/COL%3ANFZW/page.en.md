## Brunsvigia litoralis: diagnostic comparisons and habitat {#s-72fbb21d03}
A 2012 regional account compares this species with *Brunsvigia josephinae*. It describes the bulb as subterranean and gives about 18 leaves, narrower than those of *B. josephinae* (up to 7.5 cm wide versus 20 cm), and approximately upright. The excerpt reports flowers as “mostly to 20(-40)” but gives no unit for that figure. It gives a perianth tube length of 15–25 mm, compared with up to 15 mm for *B. josephinae*.

These characters come from a regional species account citing a 2012 publication. The excerpt provides no dated sampling frame or population frequency; the comparisons do not establish variation across the full species range.

A separate 1951 account notes that the species was previously classified under *B. josephinae*. It lists differences involving the underground bulb, perianth proportions, shorter anthers, a generally smaller growth form and leaves with a half twist near the tip. The account says the flowers are very similar in overall appearance and explains the name *litoralis* by contrasting coastal habitat with the inland habitat of *B. josephinae*.
