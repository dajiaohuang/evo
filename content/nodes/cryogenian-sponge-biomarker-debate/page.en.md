## Did the ancient steranes come from sponges? {#s-ab84750dac}
In a dated succession from Oman's South Oman Salt Basin, Love et al. measured 24-isopropylcholestanes, a type of sterane molecule, in Cryogenian-to-Cambrian rocks. Detecting the compound is a chemical observation; it does not identify the organism that made it. A later study found that sampled Rhizaria can synthesize the relevant sterol precursors, so the rocks do not uniquely demonstrate that demosponges were present. Rock age, compound identity and biological source are distinct questions.
