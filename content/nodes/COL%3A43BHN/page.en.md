## *Mikania pernambucensis*: triangular leaves and spikelike branches {#s-3e261b3a4c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, thin triangular leaves with entire margins and five strong veins from the base. Their bases are arrow- or spear-shaped, and the leaf surfaces are hairless. Branches may twine, and the stem pith is recorded as hollow or solid. Panicles have spikelike branches bearing sessile or nearly stalkless heads. The funnel-shaped corollas and fruitlets are hairless.
