## Overview {#s-6b90a4a4a9}
This page follows COL26.8 accepted-species ID 782LM; the exact MDD name record 1000885 uses the English label Ornate Titi. The Chinese name is editorial. The account focuses on published behavior and diet evidence from a two-group study in the Colombian Llanos.

## Research observations {#s-ea5360e0e9}
Quintero-Tapia and Carretero-Pinzón (2024) used scan sampling to compare two groups living in landscapes with different degrees of fragmentation and recorded 35 food-plant species. Their activity budgets differed, especially in foraging and moving time; diet composition was similar, with fruit predominant at both sites. The authors linked the behavioral differences to landscape context, but the comparison covers only two groups.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-b64f39d467}
The study is useful for comparing activity budgets and feeding records at two sites. It cannot establish a species-wide causal effect of fragmentation or represent the full range, long-term population trend or current conservation status.
