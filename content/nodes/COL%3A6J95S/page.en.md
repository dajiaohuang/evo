## *Fleischmannia remotifolia*: dense clusters and lilac or white corollas {#s-c4946573d5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records open-spreading branches and ovate to narrowly ovate leaves. The leaf hairs may be glandular or non-glandular. Dense, corymb-shaped clusters have flower heads on hairy stalks. The corollas are listed as lilac or white, and the fruit-tips bear pappus bristles with cylindrical bases. The field also gives only the broad substrate category “terrestrial.”
