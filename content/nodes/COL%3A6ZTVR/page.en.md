## Stilpnopappus cearensis {#s-fc454c0902}
## A hollow woolly stem carries a spike of heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, hollow stem with a woolly covering. Sessile, leathery leaves do not form a rosette. Their blades range from elliptic or obovate to lance-shaped or nearly round, with blunt or rounded tips and tapered bases. The upper face is hairless, the underside woolly, and glands occur on the surfaces.

Heads crowd along a spike-like inflorescence, each on a short stalk. Their involucral bracts are villous, and the receptacle is flat. A head may have up to 35 florets; the corollas have sparse glands. The slightly furrowed dry fruits have an outer row of flattened paleae and an inner row of scale-like pappus parts. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
