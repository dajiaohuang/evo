## Evidence limits {#s-6160c93420}
This page interprets historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages for the listed sample, not diagnoses or individual-variation ranges. Mass has a separate literature or weighing source, so the morphology count cannot be assigned to it. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography covers historical breeding and resident ranges. Historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name support the association, not equivalence after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation need other sources.

The August 2015 life-history archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. These are not direct measurements of one animal, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current surveys. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and same-name source rows support the association but do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications were not individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields need other sources.

EltonTraits 1.0 is a reading guide to a historical same-name, same-family foraging record, not a complete monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical species concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse weights standardized from literature descriptions, not measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportion uncertainty; mammalian ABC codes may use a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not support definite conclusions. The underlying books and papers were not reread individually. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
