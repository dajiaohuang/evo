## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded? {#s-9ec9af0c87}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes branches with silky or closely pressed hairs. Elliptic leaves have tapering or tiny-pointed tips and three veins. Terminal flowers have triangular sepals and entirely white petals. Filaments in the outer (antisepalous) stamen whorl are 6–9 mm long; those in the inner (antipetalous) whorl are 5–9 mm, and bear hairs. The ovary and fruit have four chambers; glandular hairs occur at the ovary tip. Seeds are slightly coiled. This is the regional dataset's morphology field, not a global account.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-e09bbff340}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; no locality or collection date is supplied.
