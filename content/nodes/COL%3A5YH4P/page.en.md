## What leaf and flower-cluster traits are recorded for Christianella surinamensis? {#s-436b458b30}
The elliptic leaves are gray and woolly-haired, with short-pointed tips and net-like tertiary veins. The bracts and bracteoles are 2–3 mm long. Bracteoles bear a tiny, 0.2 mm gland that is sessile or nearly so. The thyrse, a branched flower cluster, reaches 5 cm long.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-eddabd7d57}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
