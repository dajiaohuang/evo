## Overview {#s-bf72410380}
The evidence here centres on a Silurian–Devonian Phacopidae sample: researchers compare morphology and growth stages to delimit species, alongside a broader review of trilobite systematics. That family-level sample cannot resolve every phacopid lineage or set global endpoints for Phacopida.

## Diet {#s-707203e256}

## Habitat {#s-079167d39c}

## Evidence and scope {#s-cc7dfb0609}
Crônier and colleagues document a bounded Phacopidae morphology and ontogeny sample, while Fortey reviews broader trilobite systematics. These sources do not establish order-wide first and last occurrences, a complete geographic range or a uniform ecology.

## Limits of this account {#s-339041f793}
The evidence is taxonomic and morphological, not a survey of all Phacopida. It leaves diet, habitat use, locomotion, body-size variation, global range and order-wide temporal endpoints unassigned.
