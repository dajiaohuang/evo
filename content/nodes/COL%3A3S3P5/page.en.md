## Lampranthus dulcis {#s-5c2f9c35ba}
## Glaucous pointed leaves and soft-pink flower clusters

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 50 cm tall. Its ascending leaves are triangular in cross-section, pointed or tapering, and glaucous, with a pale surface bloom; they reach 13 mm long and about 3 mm wide.

A few flowers grow together in small clusters. The flowers range from pink to pale pink and reach 40 mm across; the fruits are about 9 mm in diameter. The habitat account records sandy ground, often among restios.
