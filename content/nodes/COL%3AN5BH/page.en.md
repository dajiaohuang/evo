## Distinguish the measured length {#s-efb1ddd38a}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 86 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent and differs from total length including the tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-ea111bd7e6}
the reproductive-mode field uses the original label viviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 12; lowest reported clutch/litter mean 7; highest reported clutch/litter mean 17. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.
