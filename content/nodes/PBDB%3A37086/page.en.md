## PBDB’s recorded fossil classification {#s-4033e4ef01}
PBDB’s 2 October 2026 snapshot records Benthosuchidae Efremov 1940 at family rank under Capitosauroidea. This page follows that supplementary fossil classification; it does not establish that a similarly named COL entry represents the same taxon.

## What the captured fossil records show {#s-a207adb993}
In the captured PBDB hierarchy, 9 accepted fossil-species concepts are placed under Benthosuchidae. The occurrence snapshot contains 23 records with exact taxon-ID matches; 0 of the 9 concepts have no matching record in this capture. Across these 23 dated collection intervals, the outer age bounds run from 251.902 to 247 million years ago. These pooled collection bounds do not date the taxon’s origin or extinction and do not show continuous presence through the full span. PBDB’s placement is supplementary and does not establish equivalence with a similarly named COL group.
