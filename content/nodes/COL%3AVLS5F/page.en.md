## Narrow leaves and blue flowers {#s-1b10cd6194}
The Flora of Panama describes Centrosema pascuorum as either a delicate vine or an erect herb. Each leaf has three very narrow leaflets, usually 4 to 10 cm long but only 4 to 7 mm wide. Blue flowers grow from the leaf axils, and the compressed pods are slightly curved, usually 4 to 8 cm long, with a sharp beak up to 10 mm. The description is historical and gives no population sampling frame or observation date.

## Panama, Ecuador and Brazil in the source record {#s-18260fae61}
The Flora of Panama distribution field gives Panama, Ecuador and Brazil, and notes collections near the Pacific coast in Panama. These are the places stated in that historical regional record, not a current complete range.
