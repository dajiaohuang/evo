## How are the leaves, flowers and winged fruit described? {#s-14ee40df55}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field describes elliptic leaves with pointed to long-tapering tips and tapered bases. They are papery, with pinnate loops of secondary veins; the surface may be silky-haired or become nearly hairless. Hair tufts, pockets, or no domatia (small structures in vein axils) are reported, and glands are absent. Axillary or terminal, head-shaped spikes bear bisexual flowers. The lower floral cup is spindle-shaped and the upper part bell-shaped; the calyx lobes are conspicuous. Ten stamens occur in two whorls, with rounded anthers and long threadlike filaments; the style is hairy. The fruit has two triangular wings. This is a regional database description, not a global or population-wide account.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c0784bca9d}
The same Brazilian Flora dataset row classifies the habitat as terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality or survey date.
