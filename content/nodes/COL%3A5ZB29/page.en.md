## A woody twiner with three leaflets {#s-87163ea26a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes a woody twiner with leaves divided into three leaflets. Flowering clusters occur at leaf axils and branch tips; the source calls their arrangement a pseudo-raceme, a flower group organized along an axis. Closed flowers are recorded as absent, while petal color and fruit and seed traits are marked unknown. The stamens are described as diadelphous or pseudomonadelphous, joined into two or apparently one group. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
