## What leaves and flowers are described? {#s-04444f6f63}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands on the stem and sessile leaves with the same color on both faces, spherical glands and a single vein. The inflorescence is a two-branched cluster (a dichasium). The bell-shaped floral cup bears spherical glands; the sepals are linear to narrowly triangular, glandular and shorter than the cup. The corolla is yellow. Anthers match one another in color and contain multiple pollen sacs, and the ovary has three chambers. These are regional database entries without a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-0ed471a397}
The Brazilian Flora field classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
