## Uneven encounter records across Mindanao {#s-9aaaea7a32}
The 2016 account described local commonness in central, southern and western Mindanao but few northeastern records, while adding specimens from Bunawan. It reported no records on Bohol, Leyte, Dinagat, Siargao or Samar. Those historical nondetections and apparent gaps do not establish permanent absence or a complete current range; qualitative abundance where found is not a standardized island-wide population estimate.
