## *Baccharis hirta*: serrated leaves and head panicles {#s-9fce8edf34}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records leaves with three main veins or pinnate veins, nearly stalkless, and lance-shaped, inversely lance-shaped, or elliptic. Their margins are serrated. Flower heads are arranged in panicles, branched clusters. The field records heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered; it does not join these as one flower-head type. Rock-associated and terrestrial substrate labels are listed without a locality.
