## An elliptic leaf with two faint marginal veins {#s-e8d01df370}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists young-branch hairs as absent or star-shaped. Leaves are elliptic, with tips that taper to a long point or tail-like tip and wedge-shaped bases. Their margins are entire and not fringed with hairs. Veins arise at the base; three lengthwise veins and two faint veins just inside the edge are recorded. On the underside, the hair covering is absent or sparse; the hairs are absent or sessile (without stalks) and star-shaped. These alternatives are not quantified in the field.

## A closed bud and a medium-sized pollen pore {#s-d2e01a1fa9}
Flowers grow in dichasial clusters, a branching arrangement. The flower cup (hypanthium) is bell-shaped. The calyx falls away; its lobes are listed as truncate or irregular, and the calyx is closed in bud. Each flower has five petals. The connective, the central tissue in an anther, is extended, with an appendage of two lower (ventral) lobes and one upper (dorsal) tooth. Anthers are white, with one medium-sized pollen opening; the ovary is hairless.

## A terrestrial category without a locality {#s-4552b7f5f1}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date, or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material. The category does not describe a geographic range.
