## What does the record describe for Guatteria duodecima? {#s-af6affa85d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery, broadly elliptic to narrow oblong-elliptic leaves with a warty surface and variable sparse hairs. One or two flowers grow in the leaf axils; their stalks are densely hairy. The sepals are free, and the petals are oblong-elliptic. The fruit segments are ellipsoid, and the seeds have a wrinkled surface.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-d28cd0fa2a}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
