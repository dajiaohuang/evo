## Red latex and a furrowed fruit {#s-440f06dfc4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record notes red latex in the leaf entry and describes the branches as cylindrical, with slightly thickened bark. Leaves are alternate and loosely spaced; the inflorescences are terminal, corymb-like cymes. The same field records nearly equal sepals and a hairless ovary. The fruit has distinct ribs, a stalk, inconspicuous lenticels, and a furrowed surface. Corolla-lobe length is listed as either longer than or equal to the tube; the field does not distinguish those alternatives. This is one source-labelled description, not an account of variation across the species.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-925b1260d0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial. That category does not identify a collection locality or a more specific substrate, so it cannot by itself describe where across the species' range the record applies.
