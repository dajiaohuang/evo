## Eriocephalus africanus {#s-e2afe33fdf}
## Silvery leaves and purple ray florets

The SANBI entry describes a twiggy, silky-silver shrub to 1 m tall. Its narrow leaves are linear or divided into three parts and grow in tufts. The radiate flower heads sit in small, umbel-like clusters at branch tips; the conspicuous ray florets are purple with white rays. Habitat is recorded mainly on clay or granite hillsides.
