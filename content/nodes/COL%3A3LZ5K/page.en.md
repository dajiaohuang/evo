## Hippeastrum blossfeldiae {#s-197da02584}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes Hippeastrum blossfeldiae with a subterranean bulb, strap-shaped leaves and funnel-shaped flowers whose tepals have orange tips and pale yellow-green bases. It lists inflorescences bearing either two to four or five to eight flowers, no corona, and numerous flattened, disc-shaped seeds. The habitat field is terrestrial; the entry does not give locality or sampling information.
