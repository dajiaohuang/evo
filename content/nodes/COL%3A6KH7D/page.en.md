## Four-grooved leaves and tubular red flowers {#s-9644742aca}
Gladiolus watsonius grows 30–50 cm tall. Its narrow leaves have strongly thickened margins and midribs, creating four longitudinal grooves; the flowers are tubular and red to orange.
