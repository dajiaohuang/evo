## Muraltia arachnoidea {#s-f1395de5ec}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia arachnoidea* as an accepted species. A regional species account describes a sprawling shrub to 50 cm, with young stems hairy, sessile leaves (attached without a stalk) ending in a spine, and pink or white axillary flowers. Its capsules are hairy and horned; the account places it on lower sandstone slopes. That habitat note is not a complete range survey.
