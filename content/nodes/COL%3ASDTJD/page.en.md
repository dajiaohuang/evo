## The published wart-ridge comparison {#s-53ca3a9412}
The source describes salvatori as lacking an ocular-dorsal wart ridge, in contrast to the korekore diagnosis. A separate paragraph on korekore variation reports incomplete ridges in some individuals. The comparison therefore does not establish an invariant character in every focal specimen or population.
