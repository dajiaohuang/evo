## Delosperma aliwalense {#s-cf78163ac1}
## Leaf shapes vary along the flowering branches

Bolus’s 1938 description begins with a plant initially compact, then sending out spreading or ascending flowering branches. Its leaves differ by position: lower leaves are narrow and inversely egg-shaped or elliptic, middle leaves lengthen toward the tip, and upper leaves are linear. The green leaves have only faint papillae.

Flowers are solitary on long stalks. The petals are purple-pink; the outer row reaches about 1.1 cm long, with narrower inner petals. Pink sterile stamens curve backward, while pale filaments form a cone around pale-yellow anthers and pollen. Five narrow, papillate stigmas reach nearly 3 mm.

The 1938 account describes stony sites in karroid scrub. A 2019 Eastern Cape flora separately lists Dry Highveld Grassland, Mesic Highveld Grassland, and Drakensberg Grassland.
