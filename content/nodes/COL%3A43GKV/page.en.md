## Mimosa niederleinii {#s-f0a4dddf6f}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa niederleinii* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes backward-pressed short bristles on the stem, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and ellipsoid flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts); their petal lobes are silky-haired and the stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers) pink. The plant is listed as terrestrial.
