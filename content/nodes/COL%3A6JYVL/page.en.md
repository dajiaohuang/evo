## Gaylussacia angustifolia Cham. {#s-b5f59dbce8}
## Low, clustered growth and variable leaf hairs

Gaylussacia angustifolia is described with several possible growth forms, including clustered or corymb-like shoots and a prostrate base. Its papery, non-overlapping leaves are obovate to spoon-shaped; the margins may be flat or slightly rolled under. The surfaces may be smooth or hairy only along the midvein, and the lower surface may bear small dark, yellow or reddish glands. Axillary racemes or panicles carry the flowers. The round, nut-like fruit is described as smooth or ribbed and hairless. These alternatives remain unresolved in the source record.
