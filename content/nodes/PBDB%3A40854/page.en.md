## Recorded parent of Catarrhini {#s-a466c2e4f2}
Catarrhini is listed at rank infraorder in the PBDB classification route, with Anthropoidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
