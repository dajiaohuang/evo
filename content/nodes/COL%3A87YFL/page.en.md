## Begonia densifolia {#s-a0496573e4}
## Lance-shaped leaves and nearly equal capsule wings

Begonia densifolia has erect stems, persistent stipules and hairless petioles. Its slightly asymmetric basal leaves are lance-shaped or elliptic, with toothed margins; both surfaces are usually smooth, though the underside may have inconspicuous glandular hairs. Male and female flower segments are hairless, and bracteoles are present. The female segment margin was not observed. Capsule wings are nearly equal and the seed tip is rounded. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
