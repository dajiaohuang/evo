## ## Two pairs of leaflets and bulbous hairs {#s-34c047ecae}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium and two pairs of obovate to oblong or elliptic leaflets. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stem, leaves, inflorescences and outer sepal surfaces. The terminal racemes bear yellow flowers with ten stamens and petals of different forms. The fruit valves twist at maturity and carry bristly or villous hairs. Seed shape is listed as unknown; the dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
