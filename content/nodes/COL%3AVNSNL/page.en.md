## Accepted name and placement {#s-6d098dd02d}
COL26.8 accepts Kaloula laosensis Poyarkov, Orlov, Gorin & Milto, 2024 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Kaloula (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Known limestone forest localities {#s-641395f602}
Named in a revision combining morphology, DNA and natural history. It is recorded from limestone evergreen forest in Khammouane, Laos, at roughly 100-300 m; few localities are known.
