## ## Hooked hairs and triangular fruit segments {#s-6d749b5334}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a climbing plant with cylindrical, hooked-hairy branches and no xylopodium. Its alternate leaves have three ovate, lanceolate or elliptic leaflets with looping side veins. Pseudoracemes bear lilac flowers, and the androecium is pseudo-monadelphous, with the stamens partly joined. The fruit is a loment, a segmented legume; its triangular articles have a central constriction, do not twist and bear hooked hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
