## What details are recorded for Angelonia salicariifolia? {#s-f4629939ca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes softly hairy stems and opposite leaves with broad bases, shaped from lance-like to elliptic-lanceolate. Flowers grow singly in the leaf axils. The field notes a short floral appendage with a split tip, without describing its function.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-c558896629}
The dataset categorizes the species as terrestrial, without naming a particular site.
