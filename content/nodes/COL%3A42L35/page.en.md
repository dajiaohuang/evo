## Contrasting leaf pairs on a compact cushion {#s-94b1700364}
The freely branched succulent shrub forms a cushion up to 300 mm across, with internodes 2–3 mm in diameter. Its two leaf pairs differ in shape: one reaches 12 mm long, the other 35 mm, and the fused portion reaches 4 mm across. Petals may be magenta, rose with white bases, or plain white. The fruit is 4–10 mm across, has four, five or seven chambers, and may sit on a stalk up to 45 mm long. Loam, often with quartz pebbles, is recorded.
