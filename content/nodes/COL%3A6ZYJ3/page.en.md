## Axillary clusters and a subwoody fruit wall {#s-1253a3abcb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records tendrils as present or solitary and spines as absent. Petioles are 3–12 mm long; leaves have three to five veins, and the underside axils of the basal veins are sometimes bearded. The inflorescences are axillary thyrsoid structures described as short-racemose. The corolla is tubular or salver-shaped, with a tube longer than its lobes; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 3–4 cm in diameter, with a subwoody fruit wall 1–2 mm thick. The field lists five to ten or more than ten seeds, with a crust-like coat.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-1a70ab8de3}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Strychnos mitscherlichii* as terrestrial but does not name a collection site.
