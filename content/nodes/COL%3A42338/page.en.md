## Distinguish the measured length {#s-d4246f98df}
The recorded maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 270 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding head, neck and tail. This is a compiled maximum, not a typical size or absolute limit.

## Where and how it is active {#s-5d6486aa59}
The row records aquatic and terrestrial microhabitats and diurnal activity. These are broad categories, not a complete habitat description or fixed schedule; the single-row summary cannot show variation among places, seasons, or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-c542848448}
Reproductive mode is listed as oviparous, with a compiled mean of four offspring or eggs per litter or clutch. Eggs are recorded as 35 mm long and 28 mm wide. The count summarizes source data, not every clutch; the label does not establish embryo nutrition.
