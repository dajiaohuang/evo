## Inferred measurements and a broader community analysis {#s-bb591d8a8e}
Within the study-linked subset, one head-width entry and one tibia-length entry are explicitly marked as inferred. Other lengths are not independently certified as direct measurements; mixed means and maxima, missing header units, and inconsistent sex or stage labels also prevent a uniform size comparison. The article models 766 communities and reports that functional diversity, used as a proxy, explains 36% of variation in direct-development proportions. This is a community-level association; it does not establish competition, predation, or a physiological threshold for this species.
