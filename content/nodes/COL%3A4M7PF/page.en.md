## Pouteria oblanceolata Pires {#s-9804e0bf62}
## Pointed corolla lobes and a single-seeded fruit

Pouteria oblanceolata has young branchlets with a range of recorded hair states, from smooth to woolly, and lenticels are present. Its alternate, spiral leaves have cylindrical petioles and veins that curve near the base, then loop near the margin toward the tip. Axillary clusters bear five-parted flowers with a cup-shaped corolla and pointed, entire lobes. The ovary has five chambers. The smooth, hairless fruit is yellow and contains one seed with a wrinkled coat and narrow scar.
