## Name and classification {#s-f9ad8ace0f}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 lists Craugastor sabrinus (Campbell & Savage, 2000) as a species in Anura, family Craugastoridae, genus Craugastor. This identifies the name used in that catalogue; it does not by itself show that every historical record under the same name belongs to the same biological species.
