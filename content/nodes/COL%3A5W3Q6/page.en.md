## Arctium minus {#s-90777b5e00}
## Heart-shaped leaves and nearly stalkless heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record is brief: leaves are heart-shaped to almost heart-shaped, with smooth to scalloped edges. Raceme-like inflorescences bear sessile or nearly sessile flower heads; their involucres range from almost spherical to egg-shaped. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
