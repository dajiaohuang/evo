## ## Notched leaflets and hairless pods {#s-d13d2e5f2d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two pairs of obovate to elliptic leaflets with notched tips. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stem, leaves, inflorescences and outer sepal surfaces. The terminal racemes bear yellow flowers with ten stamens and petals of different forms. The oblong pod valves twist at maturity but are glabrous; the seed is depressed-obovoid. The xylopodium is listed as unknown, and the dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
