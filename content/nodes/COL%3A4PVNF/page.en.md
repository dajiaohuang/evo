## Winged stems and woolly leaf undersides {#s-e0c809acf6}
This description records an upright herb up to 1 m tall, arising from a thick, woody rootstock. Leaf bases run down the stem to form prominent wings. The lance-shaped to elliptic leaves have a buff, woolly underside and a nearly hairless upper surface. Flower heads sit singly or in clusters along a terminal spike; the fruits are orange-brown and hairy, with a white to straw-colored pappus. The account calls the species rare in Panama and a sporadic weed in the Caribbean.
