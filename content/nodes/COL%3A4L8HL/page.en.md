## Polygala uncinata {#s-29adbfd53d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Polygala uncinata* as an accepted species. The regional account describes leaves with rounded tips that bend back into hooks, and terminal racemes bearing purple or blue flowers. The flower’s wing-like side parts are recorded as 4.0–6.5 mm long. Its habitat field says grassland. These source statements are regional and do not establish the species’ complete range or variation.
