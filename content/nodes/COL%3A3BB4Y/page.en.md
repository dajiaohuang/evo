## *Eryngium smithii*: bare-backed fruit and an upright flower cluster {#s-f123710317}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes stalked leaves whose veins are parallel or form a net near the margins. The flower cluster is upright, and flowers and fruits attach directly without individual stalks. The fruit opens at maturity; the back of each fruit segment is recorded as bare.

The substrate fields include rock and soil, without a named locality or date. They are broad categories in a regional character record, not a complete range description.
