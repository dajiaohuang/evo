## *Palhinhaea cernua*: upright or arching central stems {#s-6f1d5b2864}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description records unequal branching and a central stem that may lie prostrate, stand upright or arch. The vegetative leaves spread outward and are described as rigid and leathery or delicate and papery. Leaves bearing sporangia differ from this foliage.

Its defined strobili—the cone-like structures that carry sporangia—are not arched, and the spores have a rough surface. The habitat field says terrestrial only, without a named locality or survey date; the character list is not a range-wide sample.
