## Tiny leaves and a solitary many-flowered head {#s-24ba824fc4}
The morphology record describes leaves under 1 cm long and 5 mm wide, with obovate blades and a softly hairy upper surface. A single flower head contains more than 14 florets. Its fruits are smooth or nearly smooth along the ribs, and the pappus has two rows of unequal lengths. The habitat field says terrestrial.
