## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-673bb694ac}
COL26.8 records Micrurus mosquitensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-7ad89a9b08}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 750 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-bcb5770249}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Fossorial/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Leenders 2019.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a48ead0ef5}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-cd0c6fd689}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
