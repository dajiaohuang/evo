## Short summary {#summary}
The published abstract diagnoses this French Rhaetian mammaliaform from molars and a reconstructed postcanine row.

## Upper and lower molars define the reported genus {#structures}
The 2017 systematic review of the Rhaetian quarry at Saint-Nicolas-de-Port, France, named Fluctuodon necmergor as a new kuehneotheriid genus and species. Its accessible abstract says the study described upper and lower molars and proposed the first reconstructions of the postcanine tooth row. That makes tooth shape and row reconstruction the supported starting point for this mammaliaform.

## Comparison stays at the locality assemblage level {#study}
The review compares the Saint-Nicolas-de-Port material with Kuehneotherium from Syren, Emborough and Jameson Land. Its abstract suggests two Upper Triassic kuehneotheriid assemblages on opposite sides of the London-Brabant Massif, but does not assign that regional pattern to Fluctuodon alone. The genus is therefore best read through the diagnosed molars and the broader comparison, without converting it into an unsupported habitat or lifestyle story.

## A narrow but useful evidence base {#limits}
Only the article abstract and institutional bibliographic record were accessible in this pass. They support the taxonomic act, the molar material and the comparison scope stated above, but do not support additional claims about body proportions, locomotion, diet or detailed relationships.
