## *Thamnochortus acuminatus*: sheaths that partly hide the stems {#s-2efda86ede}
This tufted perennial reaches 50 cm. Its stems have a faint velvety covering, while the leaf sheaths often overlap and hide much of the stem. The spathes are thin and translucent, with reddish keels.

The account records it on dry sandstone slopes above 1,300 m. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.
