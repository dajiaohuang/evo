## The name in COL26.8 {#s-0c1cbcd74b}
COL26.8 lists *Ischnocnema verrucosa* (Reinhardt & Lütken, 1862) as an accepted species in the 20 August 2026 release. Its recorded path is Anura (order) → Brachycephalidae (family) → Ischnocnema (genus). A name in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity check.

## What the direct-development code means {#s-296b08ee20}
The 2023 Atlantic Forest study's S3 trait table records direct development and terrestrial oviposition for *I. verrucosa*. The authors define direct development as hatching without a free-living larval stage: the hatchling is a froglet rather than a tadpole. This is a literature-coded species entry, not a new observation or experiment on this frog. The paper compiled traits for 464 species and analyzed 766 Atlantic Forest communities, so its community results do not describe this species' population by themselves. Direct development still includes embryonic development and does not mean eggs develop without moisture.

## Egg-site and juvenile-habitat categories {#s-5a6f7839b9}
The S3 row also codes juvenile habitat as terrestrial and selects “eggs on ground, rocks or in burrows” from seven oviposition-site categories. This broad category does not identify a nest structure, exact substrate or oviposition height. The juvenile code does not establish an adult habitat preference, and the row adds no species-level evidence about clutch size, egg dimensions, parental care, breeding season or offspring survival.

## Measurements in a compiled trait table {#s-8d775381d5}
The table lists body length 24, head width 9.16 and tibia length 12.63; its headers do not state measurement units. The article uses literature means when available and maxima otherwise. It marks this species' head width and tibia length as estimates from a phylogenetic maximum-likelihood procedure; across the 464-species trait set, it inferred 139 head widths and 146 tibia lengths. The row does not consistently identify sex or stage, and the cited literature measurements were not independently rechecked.
