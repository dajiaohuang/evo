## Urokodia’s proposed stem placement is contested {#s-e84abf8766}
This Chelicerata route is linked here to the micro-CT character sample of *Urokodia aequalis* and its morphology-matrix placement. The proposed upper-stem position is disputed; a stem placement is outside the living crown group and does not identify a direct ancestor. The 519–0 Ma envelope joins that Chengjiang sample to living total-group chelicerates, not to a crown origin, global first appearance or complete lineage duration.
