Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Hydrobates microsoma (Coues, 1864) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Separate total birds from complete sets {#s-d60b9182cd}
Morphology comprises 5 birds: 2 female, 3 male and 0 unsexed; 4 complete sets. Mass=20.5 g with Mass.Source=Dunning is a compiled species-average body mass incorporating both sexes; the 5 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book, weighed individuals, localities and dates were not read, leaving sex, seasonal and regional mass ranges unsupported. Mass and beak length do not establish feeding mechanics.

## Separate aquatic-prey niche from trophic level {#s-78403e83f6}
Trophic.Niche=Aquatic predator is defined by at least 60% of food resources from vertebrate and invertebrate animals in aquatic systems. Fish, crustaceans and molluscs are examples in the definition, not a measured prey list for this species. Trophic.Level=Carnivore means at least 70% of resources from live invertebrate or vertebrate animals. The fields describe resource sets and trophic level separately, supplying neither seasonal diets nor a specific prey percentage. Aquatic food does not establish diving behavior, dive depth or particular surface-feeding actions.

## Separate marine habitat from aerial lifestyle {#s-425cac8656}
Habitat=Marine includes pelagic waters, sea near coasts and associated near-sea breeding environments in the definition, without locating this row’s measured birds or breeding island. Habitat.Density=3 denotes open habitat, including open water, not bird abundance. Primary.Lifestyle=Aerial means much time in flight and hunting or foraging predominantly on the wing; aquatic food does not turn this lifestyle field into Aquatic. Migration=2 is partially migratory, which can include a minority moving long distances, most birds moving short distances, nomadism or distinct altitudinal migration; the row does not specify which. Categories supply no departure dates, distances, tracked birds or routes; hand-wing index also does not replace measured flight speed. Historical map fields are uninterpreted here.
