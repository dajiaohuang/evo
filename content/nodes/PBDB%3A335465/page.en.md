## Recorded parent of Bonapartheriiformes {#s-ef56d718af}
Bonapartheriiformes is listed at rank suborder in the PBDB classification route, with Polydolopimorphia as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
