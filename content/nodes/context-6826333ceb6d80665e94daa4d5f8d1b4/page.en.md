## Anf1347 and the recorded concept {#s-67faacb902}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf1347 is the entry for Alsodes australis, placed by the source in Alsodes (Cycloramphidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and aquatic {#s-082fd10de7}
For Alsodes australis, row Anf1347 carries both the terrestrial (land) and aquatic (water) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-c7d2d4981b}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
