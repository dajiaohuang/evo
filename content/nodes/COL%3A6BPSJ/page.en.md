## Cullumia aculeata {#s-098ee85f42}
## Pale yellow heads above a prickly, leafy shrub

The account describes an erect, prickly shrub up to 1 m tall, densely leafy and sometimes covered in cobweb-like hairs. Ascending, oblanceolate leaves run down onto the stem. Their margins roll slightly under and bear two unequal rows of bristles. Small flower heads are radiate and pale yellow. The cited habitat field records dry sandstone slopes.
