## Stems, leaves, and fruit {#s-272c46fc4d}
The field describes clustered stems up to 2 m tall, with yellow-brown spines that are black at the base and tip and may reach 9 cm. Pinnate leaves have 15–21 pairs of linear or lanceolate leaflets, regularly arranged at the same angle. The branched inflorescence grows between the leaves and has a spiny stalk bract. Fruit is obovoid or beaked, purple, with small spines on the outer layer.
