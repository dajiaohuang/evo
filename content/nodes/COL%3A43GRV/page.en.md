## Mimosa scaberrima {#s-7d534c5aa0}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa scaberrima* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records rough stem hairs, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and projections between pairs of leaf divisions. Its flower clusters are globose; flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairless petal lobes. The plant is listed as terrestrial.
