## A name restored within the Sydney funnel-web complex {#s-fa4bcfe30f}
In its taxonomic history, Atrax montanus was included within the variation of the Sydney funnel-web. A 2025 analysis combining morphological and molecular evidence for the regional complex restored the name first published in 1914. The Australian Museum notes that A. montanus resembles A. robustus and that their recorded ranges overlap; appearance and an older naming alone therefore do not settle an identification.

## Records in New South Wales {#s-1e86e472c8}
The Australian Museum places A. montanus in New South Wales from the Watagans south to Bowral and west to the upper Blue Mountains. Its more southerly and westerly records overlap those of the Sydney funnel-web. This is a regional outline from New South Wales, not a complete range for Australia.

## What this account can establish {#s-3b0ffbe0ad}
The geographic outline follows the Australian Museum's New South Wales records and a study of the regional complex. It does not establish a global distribution, a population trend, or a formal conservation assessment.
