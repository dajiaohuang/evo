## Siolmatra brasiliensis (Cogn.) Baill. {#s-717f1bbff6}
## Three-part leaves and unusually long seeds

Siolmatra brasiliensis has trifoliate leaves. The lateral leaflets are oblique, broadly wedge-shaped to heart-shaped, and divided; their short leaflet stalks are not jointed to the main leaf stalk. Staminate flowers have notched, heart-shaped petals. Fruits measure 6–8 cm long, and the seeds are reported as 4.2–5.5 cm long.
