## What leaves, flower clusters and ovary traits are recorded? {#s-532122e840}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands on the stem and sessile leaves with the same color on both faces, spherical glands and a single vein. Flowers occur in a two-branched cluster (a dichasium) or alone when that cluster is reduced. The bell-shaped floral cup may bear spherical glands or long-stalked glandular hairs. Sepals are triangular to lance-shaped, bear either type of glandular hair, and are shorter than or equal to the cup. The corolla is pink; the anthers are recorded as matching one another in color and having multiple pollen sacs. The ovary has three chambers. These regional entries do not specify how common each alternative is.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-31cafc5cfc}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat; no locality or collection date is included.
