## Fleshy leaves and pale flowers {#s-1e799c2204}
The regional diagnosis describes S. pallens as a stout, tuberous geophyte up to 50 cm tall. It has two prostrate, fleshy oval leaves, and a dense raceme (flowers arranged along a central stalk) with a few to many white or cream flowers. The sepals are 10–15 mm long; the spurs are 19–22 mm.

## Dry sandstone slopes and Karoo-type scrub {#s-9446ab7129}
The regional account records dry, rocky sandstone slopes and karroid scrub (Karoo-type scrub). These are habitat descriptions, not mapped collection localities.
