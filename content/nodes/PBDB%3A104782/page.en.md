## Recorded parent of Parasaurolophini {#s-faab2c6f50}
Parasaurolophini is listed at rank tribe in the PBDB classification route, with Lambeosaurinae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
