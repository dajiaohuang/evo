## Bulbinella gracilis: smooth leaves and a broad raceme {#s-d48b049099}
A South African regional account describes a rhizomatous geophyte up to 300 mm tall, with a short basal neck of one or two membranous, fibre-free sheaths. There are 4–8 linear, succulent, smooth leaves. Yellow flowers grow in a broadly conical raceme.

A diagnostic account distinguishes *B. gracilis* from other South African *Bulbinella* by the absence of dead leaf bases forming a fibrous sheath around the stem and leaf bases. The spreading pedicels at the fruiting stage are described as unique to *B. gracilis* and *B. nana*. Its nearly cylindrical leaves are more succulent than those of other *Bulbinella*. The small, inconspicuous, boat-shaped bracts resemble but are smaller than those of the autumn-flowering *B. trinervis* and *B. divaginata*.
