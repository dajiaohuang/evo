## Doubtful affinity {#s-23b5292f28}
The source describes a densely branched shrublet to 40 cm, with rough, linear-lanceolate leaves 3–6 mm long and closely revolute margins. Small flattened flower heads occur in panicles and are densely white-woolly outside. Its nearest affinity is stated to be doubtful, though it resembles *P. ericoides*; habitat is high rocky slopes.
