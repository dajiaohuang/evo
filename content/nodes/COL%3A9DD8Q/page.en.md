## Mimosa pycnocoma {#s-a4d52382f7}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa pycnocoma* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes a bristly stem and globose flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), densely woolly petal lobes, and two rings of stamens; the habitat field calls the plant terrestrial.
