## Accepted name and placement {#s-6573a9f0af}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys liboensis (Zhang, Li, Xiao, Li, Pan, Wang, Zhang & Zhou, 2017) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Libo type-locality knowledge and the collection abbreviation {#s-d6622cbb97}
The monograph listed male holotype GZNU 20160408008 from Libo County, Guizhou, at 634 m. Its cited distribution summary knew the species only from that locality at approximately 630 m. The remarks explained that the original institution abbreviation GNUG had been changed to GZNU. This preserves the voucher context; the locality summary is not a present range survey or evidence of absence elsewhere.

## Measurements attributed to the original description {#s-0bf19027a0}
Drawing on the original description rather than newly examined material in this entry, the diagnosis reported four adult males at 60.5–67.7 mm snout–vent length and eight females at 60.8–70.6 mm. These are sex-specific measurements of the cited sample, not an independently remeasured dataset or population-wide size limits.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-16b1321da6}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys liboensis as larger in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
