## Orange flowers and hooked styles {#s-cdf47a9149}
The regional descriptions call B. uniflora a cormous geophyte up to 25 cm tall; a corm is a short, swollen underground stem. Its channelled, lance-shaped leaves are arranged in a spiral, and the flower styles are separate and hooked. One diagnosis calls the flowers orange; another describes them as brilliant orange or sulphur-yellow with a dark eye.

## Rocky sandstone and granite slopes {#s-557a3ceb02}
The regional habitat entry records mainly rocky sandstone and granite slopes. It gives a broad setting rather than a precise locality.
