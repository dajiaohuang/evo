## Magenta flowers among short cylindrical leaves {#s-62290cc43d}
The account describes a succulent shrub up to 25 cm tall and 30 cm across, with stiff, upright thread-like main branches and many short shoots. Its cylindrical leaves spread widely and are covered with papillae. Magenta flowers are about 22 mm across; filament-like staminodes form a cone around the stamens. The fruit base is short and bell-shaped, with crescent-like to nearly rectangular valve wings. It grows on gravelly flats.
