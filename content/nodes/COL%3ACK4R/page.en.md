## *Amauropelta saxicola* {#s-3544c0d0c4}
The field records a creeping or upright rhizome with hairy scales. Fronds are once-pinnate and pinnatifid, with the blade base gradually reduced; buds are absent, while aerophores may be absent or present. Veins are simple, and hooked hairs occur on the lower surface of the pinnae. Sori are median and linear; the indusium and hairs on the sporangia are absent. These are selected database characters.
