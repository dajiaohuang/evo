## Griffinia espiritensis {#s-9fe98a704b}
Griffinia espiritensis is recorded with a subterranean bulb and petiolate, elliptic-to-lanceolate leaves. Its unscented, bell-shaped flowers are lilac with a white base, and the hypanthium tube is short. Six stamens and ellipsoid to ovoid fruit are noted. The dataset labels the habitat terrestrial; it gives no locality or survey frame.
