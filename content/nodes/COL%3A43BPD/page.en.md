## *Mikania triangularis*: hairless triangular leaves {#s-8d86d49d2e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, thin triangular leaves with entire margins, a squared-off base, and a short pointed tip. Both surfaces are hairless, with three strong veins from the base. Branches may twine, climb, or be hairless, and the stem pith is solid. Loose panicles carry stalked heads with blunt-tipped bracts. The corolla limb is tubular and longer than its tube; the fruitlets are glandular.
