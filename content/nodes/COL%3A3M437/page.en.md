## Short bristles on leaves and flower bracts {#s-8d19ebe619}
This account describes a perennial herb 5–40 cm tall, with stems that may lie along the ground or stand upright. Stalkless, linear leaves are entire or deeply divided, with short bristles and longer ones along the edges and tips. Solitary radiate heads have glandular, hairy bracts with short spine-like tips; the ray florets are yellow with brown backs, and the disk florets yellow. It grows on sandy soils.
