## Senecio westermanii {#s-4211d174f1}
## Threadlike outer florets mark the disciform heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, herbaceous perennial with sessile lance-shaped leaves along the stem. Their bases have small ear-like lobes and their edges are toothed. The upper surface is woolly; grey woolly hairs cover the underside.

Heads grow in panicle-like clusters and are described as disciform. Each has 5–15 involucral bracts. The outer florets are threadlike and female rather than broad, strap-shaped rays; the tubular florets in the centre are bisexual. Style branches end bluntly with a spreading crown of hairs. The cylindrical, ribbed dry fruits are hairless and have a persistent white pappus (bristle crown). The source habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
