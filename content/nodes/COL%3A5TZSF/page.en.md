## How to trace these values to sources {#s-95168f685d}
The matching source rows include labels Dunning, 1992; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; Schuck-Paima, C., Alonsob, W.J., and E.B. Ottonia, 2008; and Wasser, D.E. and P.W. Sherman, 2010, among others. The archive keeps each field's row number, value and label with the reference table's full explanation. A label may point to a paper, book or database. Row counts are not numbers of animals measured, and repeated citations are not independent experiments.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-4dbf4ccd57}
These records come from the 2015 life-history archive under the same historical name. Values are compilation medians or, when the record count is even, averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. They are not measurements of one animal, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The archive can share information across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family plus same-name source rows support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 concepts or remove inherited material from every summary. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently known, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed one by one. Other sources are needed for diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields.
