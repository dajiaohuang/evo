## Anf2347 and the recorded concept {#s-e26ef426cc}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf2347 names Hyloscirtus estevesi and records Hyloscirtus, Hylidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

## Foraging categories — terrestrial and arboreal {#s-6aacf2d7db}
For Hyloscirtus estevesi, row Anf2347 carries both the terrestrial (land) and arboreal (tree-associated) foraging categories. The compilation gives broad strata rather than a seasonal or developmental account.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-81c04f13fe}
Its maximum adult body length is 22 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
