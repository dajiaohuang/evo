## Name in COL26.8 {#s-6f80756137}

## What the sources cover {#s-e37a27148f}
The 2023 Atlantic Forest study analysed trait records across 766 frog communities; its row for Brachycephalus garbeana came from a supplementary workbook, not a new field observation of that species. AmphiBIO v1 supplies a separate historical row for B. garbeanus. Neither source record has been certified as equivalent to the current COL26.8 species concept.

## Development and egg-laying in the workbook {#s-0e0d5cbc0d}
Supplementary workbook row 86 records Brachycephalus garbeana as direct-developing and terrestrial-ovipositing. In the article, direct development means that there is no free-living larval stage and hatchlings resemble miniature adults; it does not mean that embryos do not develop or that moisture is irrelevant. These are compiled trait categories, not a new breeding experiment on this frog. They remain observations attached to the workbook’s source name, not newly verified traits of every record assigned to B. garbeanus.

## Juvenile habitat and the egg-site code {#s-10b688b1a2}
For the workbook’s B. garbeana row, juvenile habitat is coded terrestrial. Its S5 egg-site flag is positive; the article defines S5 as eggs placed on the ground, rocks or in burrows. This code describes the source category, not a newly observed nest. Juvenile habitat is not an adult preference, and egg placement is separate from whether development is direct. The categories do not establish clutch size, breeding season, parental attendance, nest dimensions or offspring survival. The workbook cites earlier sources for these traits, but not all of those original studies were independently read for this account.

## What the workbook’s measurements can tell us {#s-8d63afa683}
Workbook row 86 lists raw values of 18.6 for body length, 6.43 for head width and 6.03 for tibia length; it has no head-width or tibia-length inference flag. The article says it used published means where available and maxima otherwise. Length columns have no stated units, and sex or developmental stage is not specified consistently. An absent inference flag does not certify how an upstream measurement was made. These entries are not comparable millimetre dimensions, diagnostic ranges or measurements of population variation; linking the workbook’s name to B. garbeanus does not remove those limits.

## Why the two names share a catalogue entry {#s-2e0e412c24}
COL26.8 dataset 316115 records the exact name Brachycephalus garbeana as usage 68PXZ and explicitly links it to the accepted name B. garbeanus, ID 5X29Z. This archived link establishes a naming relation, not that the article’s source concept or specimens match every record under the accepted name. Misidentifications, later splits and specimen assignments require original evidence. The AmphiBIO row and workbook row remain separate source records; a shared catalogue destination does not make them one biological observation.
