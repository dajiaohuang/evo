## Toothed leaves with three main veins {#s-f714f8d74e}
This morphology field records petiolate leaves with three main veins, toothed or serrated edges, and lance-shaped to elliptic blades. Flower heads form panicles or corymbs; separate heads contain either tubular male florets or slender female florets. The habitat fields include both rock-associated and terrestrial substrates.
