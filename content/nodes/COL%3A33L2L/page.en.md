## Bulb, leaves, and flowers {#s-b4ad0aad0d}
The source describes a robust bulbous geophyte reaching 75 cm. It has up to ten broad, twisted leaves, usually still green when flowering. The account records 15–25 long, tubular flowers that nod; their colour ranges from crimson to pink. The stamens broaden at the base and extend just beyond the floral throat, while the style has a truncate (flat-ended) tip.

Bulbous geophyte to 75 cm, robust. Leaves up to 10, usually green at flowering, broad, twisted. Flowers 15-25, nodding, long, tubular, crimson or pink, stamens dilated at base, shortly exserted from throat, style truncate.
