## What bristly leaf and flower traits are listed? {#s-c0cdc8c849}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records bristles on the stem and sessile leaves. The leaves differ in color between their two faces, have bristles and five veins. Flowers are solitary. The bell-shaped floral cup bears bristles or spherical glands; triangular sepals have the same kinds of surface structures and are shorter than the cup. The corolla is pink. Anthers are two-colored and have four pollen sacs, and the ovary has three chambers. This regional record gives no frequency information.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-7e7114385d}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat, with no locality or collection date.
