## Which traits are known for Croton kalkmannii, and what remains unknown? {#s-9bbbd1e43c}
The record gives short-stalked oval to oval-lanceolate leaves with brochidodromous veins, which loop near the edge, and finely toothed margins. A sessile or nearly sessile nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Hair covering on the branches and both leaf surfaces is unknown. Terminal thyrses bear unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked, but their shape is unknown; female flowers are sessile or nearly so, with separate sepals. Style form, calyx-lobe form, and the bud arrangement are also unknown. Female petals are absent. The fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
