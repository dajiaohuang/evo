## Forty-eight bird genomes test Neoaves’ early branches {#s-3367510d45}
Jarvis and colleagues compared whole-genome and reduced datasets for 48 species, representing the bird orders recognized at the time. Across several analyses they recovered major groupings including Passerea and Columbea, testing hypotheses about rapid early branching within Neoaves.

These are relationships inferred among sampled living birds, not a complete species-level tree or a fossil chronology. Some deep branches shift with data and method, where incomplete lineage sorting and convergence complicate the signal.
