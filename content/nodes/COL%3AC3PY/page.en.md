## Aloe humilis {#s-75ebdad80c}
Aloe humilis forms dense clumps of small, stemless rosettes. Its short, pointed leaves have soft, pale prickles scattered irregularly over the surface; the regional account contrasts them with the black, aligned prickles of related aloes. Scarlet flowers are typical, with orange also recorded. It grows on coastal hills in renosterveld or dry thickets.
