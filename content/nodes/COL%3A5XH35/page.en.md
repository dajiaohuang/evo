## Cavendishia martii (Meisn.) A.C.Sm. {#s-35540d5517}
## Gland-tipped hairs beneath the leaves

Cavendishia martii has stiff branches; the young twigs may be smooth or hairy. Its oval to oblong leaves have pointed tips and rounded to slightly heart-shaped bases. The lower surface bears gland-tipped, club-shaped hairs, and five to seven main veins rise from near the base. Panicles carry green or red membranous bracts with glandular fringes. The fleshy corolla is green to yellowish, slightly wider at the base and 8–10 mm long; the stamens are equal in length and have free filaments.
