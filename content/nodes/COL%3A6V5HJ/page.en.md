## Peritassa pruinosa {#s-8d62b29595}
## A woody-fruited climber of Panama and Colombia

Peritassa pruinosa may be a liana or shrub with climbing branches. Its grey, slender branchlets bear many lenticels; the opposite or nearly opposite leaves are leathery and 10–20 cm long. Compact flower clusters carry tiny paired or three-part groups of flowers. One fruit develops per inflorescence; it is ellipsoid to nearly cylindrical, sometimes curved, up to 3 cm long, with a woody pericarp at least 2 mm thick. One or two seeds are recorded. The source lists Panama and northern Colombia.
