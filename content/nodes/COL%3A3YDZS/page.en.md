## Matayba cristae {#s-6b5a8e3744}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description of *Matayba cristae* links a grooved leaf rachis and urn-shaped domatia with capsules whose outer surface is warty. Its habitat field separately records a terrestrial setting.

## Grooved leaf rachises and warty capsules {#s-d7a461a638}
Leaves are recorded along the branches. The rachis is flat or subcylindrical, with two grooves in one listed state; leaflets are described as papery or leathery, flat and concolorous, with entire margins and a lower surface that is hairless to nearly hairless. The account notes brochidodromous venation, inconspicuous veins on the upper leaflet surface, open areoles, straight or curved secondary veins, and urn-shaped domatia. Sepals are joined at the base and pubescent on the lower surface. Capsules are listed as ellipsoid, triangular, or subglobose; the outer surface is warty and sparsely pubescent, while the inner surface is villous. The seed aril is apical. These are states and alternatives in one regional description, not a measure of population variation.

## A terrestrial habitat entry {#s-65ab4660e8}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat category “Terrícola” (terrestrial). This field names a habitat or substrate category; it gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
