## Crown and inflorescence {#s-e147f89733}
The source records a solitary stem. Leaves are arranged spirally in the crown, have a feathery form, and their sheaths bear long, thick black fibers. The inflorescence stalk is 3–5 cm long and has numerous smaller branches called rachillae.

## Fruit and seed tissue {#s-3125c7b89f}
The fruit entry describes ruminate endosperm, a folded or irregularly grooved texture in the seed tissue. It does not provide a fruit shape or size in this field.

The field records a solitary stem; leaves spirally arranged in the crown and plumose in form, with long, thick black fibers on the sheaths; an inflorescence peduncle 3–5 cm long with numerous rachillae; and fruit with ruminate endosperm.
