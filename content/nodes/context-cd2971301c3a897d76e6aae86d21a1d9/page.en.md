## The source concept {#s-1fed0b9d8b}
AmphiBIO v1 records Hyla savignyi in row Anf2328 under Hyla, Hylidae and Anura, using the species concept in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The measurements and categories below are values deposited in that compilation. This row has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept, so read it as a source record rather than a confirmed modern taxon profile.

## Foraging strata {#s-c8b2cc92bb}
AmphiBIO marks aquatic and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. These broad labels do not give seasonal or life-stage detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; the affected row-level comparison has not been captured here. Treat these historical labels as needing review. No replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show that every original value is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-acd1939713}
Arthropods is the adult-food category listed for this row. AmphiBIO combines literature reports based on expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; the category does not describe a complete diet or the proportion contributed by each food type.

## Activity periods {#s-94fed82736}

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-b27b1bf44d}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 49 mm. For Anura, its body-length field means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-e8ff734ce6}
This row reports sexual maturity at 3–4 years, 200–1,000 offspring or eggs per clutch, at most one reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg size of 1.3 mm. AmphiBIO compiles these values from literature; the row does not specify the sex, population, date, or wild versus captive conditions behind each value. The size field may describe an offspring or an egg.
