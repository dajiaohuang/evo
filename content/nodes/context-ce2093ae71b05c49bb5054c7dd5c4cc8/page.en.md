## The source concept {#s-96f64c26c6}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Alsodes vittatus as row Anf1364, classified in Alsodes, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-d88dfd116f}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 37.4 mm for Alsodes vittatus. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental strategy {#s-c5fa756bc9}
The deposited record marks a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
