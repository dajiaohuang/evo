## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Licania bahiensis? {#s-f21b3fd455}
Its oblong-elliptic leaves have woolly hairs on the underside, and the leaf stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in racemes or panicles, with bell-shaped receptacles. The flowers are sessile, or without individual stalks, and have three separate stamens whose filaments remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is not described.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-7d40bce539}
The dataset classifies it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
