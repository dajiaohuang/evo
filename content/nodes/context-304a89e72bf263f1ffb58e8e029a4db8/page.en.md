## AmphiBIO species record {#s-276e9799c6}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Caecilia albiventris? Row Anf6601 places it in genus Caecilia, family Caeciliidae and order Gymnophiona. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
