## What does the source record for Duguetia reticulata? {#s-89bea65731}
The field describes narrow elliptic leaves, smooth above and sparsely covered with scale-like hairs beneath. Pink flowers have free sepals and nearly equal outer and inner petals. The rounded fruit has a basal collar and star-shaped hairs; its color is not specified. The record does not establish the inflorescence form.
