## Mandevilla moricandiana {#s-683d3696d5}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mandevilla moricandiana* as an accepted species of *Mandevilla*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 source row records terrestrial habitat but no collection site or broader range. With no morphology claim in this row, the supported page is limited to that habitat category.
