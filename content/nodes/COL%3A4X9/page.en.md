## PanTHERIA’s Cercopithecoidea records {#s-98c4619c2b}
PanTHERIA 1.0 links 36 usable historical accounts to Cercopithecoidea, including Cercocebus galeritus, Cercopithecus ascanius, C. cephus, C. erythrogaster, and C. erythrotis. The accounts retain their MSW05 names and notes on field-specific conditions and gaps. The catalogue navigation follows COL26.8; these 36 records are source coverage, not the current accepted-species count.

## Adult-mass summaries in this set {#s-a829eef600}
The available sex-adjusted historical adult-mass medians range from 1,248.86 g for Miopithecus talapoin to 15,964.11 g for Theropithecus gelada. They summarize separate source entries rather than individual extremes or the complete range of body sizes in Cercopithecoidea.

## Which life-history fields were recorded {#s-0e5d7fff96}
The historical life-history route contains 110 accounts. Adult mass appears in all 110; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch and maximum-lifespan records appear in 103 each, while 94 include the source’s female-maturity field, defined as age at first conception. Examples include Allenopithecus nigroviridis, Cercocebus agilis, C. atys, C. chrysogaster, and C. galeritus. The counts describe this compilation, not a complete list of living cercopithecoids.

## Scope of the historical comparisons {#s-85fbc7a46e}
These pages summarize separate historical compilations, each with its own name crosswalk, field definitions, selection rules, and source conditions. The records do not cover every COL26.8 descendant, and matching an older name does not establish identity between historical and current species concepts.

Adult-mass and life-history values summarize source entries rather than individual ranges or current population measurements. Maximum lifespan is a record, not an average; an empty field is not evidence that the trait is absent.

EltonTraits food weights standardize literature descriptions, and mammalian ABC entries can include inference from a congener. Feeding strata and activity codes are categorical summaries, not direct measurements of daily time budgets. Current distributions and conservation status require other evidence.

## Different events, different summaries {#s-265a52ee70}
Gestation appears in 20 accounts, female age at first reproduction in 17, and maximum lifespan in 10. First reproduction is not the same event as first birth or sexual maturity; maximum lifespan is a longest-record measure, not an average. Source conditions vary, and missing fields are not filled from relatives.

## How to interpret these historical values {#s-888c57fba3}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 name crosswalk; this does not prove equivalence with current species concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight, regardless of sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies can be reused. Maximum lifespan is the longest record found, not a typical lifespan. Adult mass is not interpreted in this Ernest route, and wild, captive, and locality conditions were not established for every source.
