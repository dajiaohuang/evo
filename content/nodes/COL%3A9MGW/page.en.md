## A bark-associated habitat field {#s-f8bdd21292}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Acrolejeunea emergens (Mitt.) Steph. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

It is not accompanied by a locality, survey date, full range or sampling coverage.

This category is the only biological information included from the regional record; it is not a full species account.
