## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category {#s-c6990c4453}
Insessorial means primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, without implying never descending to the ground. Habitat is Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is sedentary, without establishing lifelong immobility or inability to fly.
