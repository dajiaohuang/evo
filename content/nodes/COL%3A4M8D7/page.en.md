## Pradosia subverticillata Ducke {#s-ec4dba9edd}
## Leathery leaves and greenish flowers

Pradosia subverticillata has no lenticels. Its leaves may be alternate or whorled, leathery and without intersecondary veins; the primary vein is grooved above, and the petiole is channelled. Stipels are absent. Flowers occur on branches or in the leaf axils, and the corolla is green or greenish. The fruit is smooth, with an outer layer that ranges from nearly hairless to minutely hairy.
