## What does the description say about Buddleja nitida? {#s-b3fb81753e}
A regional flora describes Buddleja nitida as a shrub or small tree 3–10 m tall. Its narrow, lance-shaped leaves have closely pressed hairs underneath. Small flowers form branched clusters 2–8 cm long; the flowers are only a few millimetres long, and the narrow capsules measure 4–5.5 mm. These measurements come from the cited description, which gives no sample size.

## What range is quoted? {#s-6927bad844}
The distribution note quotes Norman as calling it a common tree from the mountains of southern Chiapas to Panama. This is a quoted regional statement, not a current range survey.
