## What range of stem, leaf and flower traits is recorded for Linum carneum? {#s-4b72931b20}
The stems may be simple or branched from the base, and are ridged. Leaves may be alternate, opposite, two-ranked or spiral; they range from appressed to spreading. Their blades are lance-shaped or elliptic, with pointed or beaked tips and rolled-under, entire margins. Axillary or terminal flower clusters range from branched scorpioid cymes—curved, coiled clusters—to a single flower. They have lance-shaped bracts without basal glands. Sepals are elliptic and flat, with small glandular teeth and a rolled margin; petals are obovate and white or pink. The flower has five stamens and five sterile stamens, five ovary chambers and five styles. The nearly spherical capsule splits between chambers; ten flat, elliptic seeds are recorded.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1540e7aeec}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
