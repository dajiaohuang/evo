## Bead-like fruits on a low sprawling shrublet {#s-d9cc1d9720}
The account describes a sprawling shrublet with grooved branches up to 25 cm long. Its fleshy, linear leaves have stipules. Mauve flowers have ovaries with 4–9 ovules, and the strongly bead-like fruits are 15–30 mm long. Sandstone slopes are listed as habitat.
