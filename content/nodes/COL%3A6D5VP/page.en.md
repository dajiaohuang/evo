## Mauve flowers with unequal stamens {#s-383f2e2c03}
The regional descriptions record D. ixioides as a rhizomatous geophyte 20–40 cm tall; a rhizome is an underground stem. Its mauve flowers have tepals (flower parts not differentiated into sepals and petals) and strongly unequal stamens, which project beyond them. The account uses those proportions, together with narrow leaves, to distinguish the species.

## Rocky sandstone slopes {#s-f7acba8054}
The regional habitat record lists rocky sandstone slopes. It does not identify a collection locality.
