## Accepted name and placement {#s-e57bc5fcf2}
COL26.8 accepts Oreobates machiguenga Padial, Chaparro, Castroviejo-Fisher, Guayasamin, Lehr, Delgado, Vaira, Teixeira et al., 2012 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Oreobates (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A forest-floor frog from the Vilcabamba slopes {#s-8036a23eb4}
The 2012 account reports Oreobates machiguenga only from its type locality at Maguireni, on the slopes of Cordillera Vilcabamba in the Río Kimbiri Valley of Cusco, Peru. The authors describe humid montane forest and record the frog on the forest floor at night during the rainy season. This is the study’s known locality and one collection context; it does not establish ecological restriction to that site or season.

The adult female holotype has conspicuous granular dorsal skin, a smooth underside, a head slightly longer than wide, and basal webbing between toes III and IV. These are observations of the holotype and features used in the species diagnosis, not measurements of a population. The revision distinguishes O. machiguenga from congeners by a combination of traits.
