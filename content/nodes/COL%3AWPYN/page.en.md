## Form and flowers {#s-c562f77187}
The archived South African description gives plants as 100–200 mm tall with branched stems. It describes radially symmetric, pale-yellow flowers marked purple or brown, with a relatively large perianth.

This is a SANBI e-Flora South Africa description archive (source 18638.0), so it is regional evidence rather than a global assessment.

## Flowering time {#s-e7df152372}
The flora reports flowering from late September to mid-October and does not provide a full life-cycle study.

This is the species account in Goldblatt and Manning, *Strelitzia* 42 (2020), printed pages 165–166; it is regional evidence, not a current global synthesis.

## Habitat and reported range {#s-1711caa6ab}
The SANBI archive says the plant is most often found on shale soils on hills and lower mountain slopes in renosterveld, and sometimes on rocky sandstone slopes.

This habitat account is from the SANBI e-Flora South Africa archive (source 18638.0); it does not establish a global assessment.

The flora reports a Western Cape range from Piekenierskloof Pass to Piketberg and Saron.

This range statement comes from the species account in Goldblatt and Manning, *Strelitzia* 42 (2020), printed pages 165–166; the regional account is not a current global synthesis.
