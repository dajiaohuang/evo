## Light responses varied across 15 isolates {#s-4535e121a1}
A controlled culture study compared 15 Aspergillus fumigatus isolates. Most showed light-associated hyphal pigmentation or conidiation, while at least one tested isolate showed no overt response. The authors report variation among strain backgrounds and culture conditions. The results do not give the frequency of these responses in wild populations and do not establish a uniform species-wide phenotype.

## Culture conditions bound the observation {#s-a58cb160c9}
The study cultured isolates on GMM or RPMI 1640 under constant darkness or white light; reported assays were conducted at 37°C for 48 hours or three days. The panel included commonly used laboratory, clinical and environmental isolates, but the experiments used laboratory cultures rather than observations of wild populations.
