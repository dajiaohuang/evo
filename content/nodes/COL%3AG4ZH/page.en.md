## *Aralia warmingiana*: tripinnate leaves and jointed flower stalks {#s-4196594af4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character record describes tripinnate leaves—divided into leaflets three times—with serrated margins. Umbel stalks, the supports of umbrella-like flower clusters, measure 1–2 mm across. Individual flower stalks are jointed, and the style, the slender part leading to a flower's stigma, is longer than 1.5 mm.

The habitat field records terrestrial substrate only, without a named locality or survey date. This is a regional set of characters rather than a population-level measurement.
