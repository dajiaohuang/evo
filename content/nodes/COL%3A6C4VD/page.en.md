## Cybianthus schwackeanus Mez {#s-0de39cc3fb}
## Expanded leaf nodes and unknown flower structure

Cybianthus schwackeanus has hairy, cylindrical terminal branches more than 3 mm in diameter; its leaf nodes are expanded and bear grouped scars. The leathery, lance-shaped leaves have tapered bases and acuminate tips. The midrib is smooth or prominent above, while curved secondary veins are visible beneath. Leaves may be alternate or whorled, and axillary racemes are reported. The corolla and staminate flowers are unknown in the source; pistillate flowers are sessile. The immature fruit wall is globose and dotted.
