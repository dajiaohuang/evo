## *Schaueria pyramidalis* {#s-71e9ffc7a1}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — hairless; leaf-related structures — elliptic; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal; flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
