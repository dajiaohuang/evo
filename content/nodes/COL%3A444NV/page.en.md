## Monnina cardiocarpa {#s-c50e1f0ea2}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Monnina cardiocarpa* as an accepted species of *Monnina*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes an upright, partly woody plant with glandular hairs and nectar glands near the leaf bases. Its leaves are elliptic to oblong; the source says each fruit contains two seeds. The habitat field labels it terrestrial, but supplies no collection locality or complete range.
