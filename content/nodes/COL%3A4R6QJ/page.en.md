## What size and flower-count ranges are recorded for Quiina macrophylla? {#s-2ae7a17b31}
Two interpetiolar stipules sit between the opposite leaves. Petioles are 1.1–2 cm. The blades may be leathery or papery, elliptic to oblong-elliptic, and longer than 30 cm; they may be hairless or woolly, with 21–30 pairs of raised veins below. The inflorescence has more than 100 male flowers and more than 30 bisexual flowers. Flowers have four sepals, four petals, two styles and a two-chambered ovary. The subglobose-to-ellipsoid fruit is 1–2.5 cm.
