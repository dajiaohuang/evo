## Form and flowers {#s-0e1c1fe9f0}
Herman and Retief's 1997 regional account describes a perennial herb reaching 0.8 m tall. Its roots are red, stout, cylindrical, and about 4 mm in diameter. The leaves are flat and somewhat two-ranked. Pedicels stand upright; the capsule is globose, and the flowers are white.

These traits come from a regional species account published in 1997; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-d794331897}
The account records the species in black turf marshes.

This habitat note comes from a regional species account published in 1997; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
