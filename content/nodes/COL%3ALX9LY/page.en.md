## Three mountain localities in the 2023 treatment {#s-1867aadb46}
The monograph placed male holotype SYS a001964 in Nanling Nature Reserve, Ruyuan, Guangdong, at 1008 m. Its distribution summary, citing Wang and colleagues (2019), included Nanling, Mangshan and Mount Qiyun along the Guangdong–Hunan–Jiangxi border, at 690–1400 m. These are reported localities and elevations; the treatment does not establish continuous occupancy between them.

## Ten male types and diagnostic skin characters {#s-cd19249c3b}
The examined type series comprised ten adult males, with diagnostic snout–vent lengths of 30.5–37.3 mm. The diagnosis described rough dorsal skin, an interrupted X-shaped dorsal ridge, rudimentary webbing at the toe bases and narrow lateral toe fringes. Breeding males were described with a subgular vocal sac but without nuptial pads or spines. These characters and measurements concern the examined series; no female measurement was supplied in this diagnosis.

## Conflicting delimitation evidence kept visible {#s-070eb911f7}
The remarks reported that Liu and colleagues (2018) proposed the Jiangxi and Guangdong populations, labelled M. sp6 and M. sp7, as possible cryptic species, although their ABGD analysis grouped them together. Further morphological examination found no distinguishing characters, and Wang and colleagues (2019) treated them as conspecific. The monograph reports this reasoning; it does not provide an independent demonstration of reproductive compatibility.
