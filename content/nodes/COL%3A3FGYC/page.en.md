## Small cream flowers and hairy leaf edges {#s-7bf5d3c46c}
Geissorhiza inflexa is recognised by small creamy-white flowers, sometimes flushed pink or red outside, and narrow sword-shaped leaves with raised, narrowly winged margins. Hair-like cilia occur along the leaf veins and marginal wings; the flowers usually have tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 10–15 mm long.
