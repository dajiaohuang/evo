## Accepted name and placement {#s-16a60f687b}
COL26.8 accepts Atelopus nahumae Ruiz-Carranza, Ardila-Robayo & Hernández-Camacho, 1994 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Bufonidae (family) → Atelopus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
