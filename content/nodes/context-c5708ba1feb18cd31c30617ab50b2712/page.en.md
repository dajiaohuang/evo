## Foraging strata {#s-cb38ea3e2e}

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2280ebb4fa}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body size of 56 mm for this row. For frogs and toads (Anura), the dataset defines body size as snout–vent length: the distance from the snout tip to the vent. This is a compiled maximum, not a typical measurement for every individual.
