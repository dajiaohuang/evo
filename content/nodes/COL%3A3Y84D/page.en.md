## Marmota (Marmota) kastschenkoi: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Marmota (Marmota) kastschenkoi* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Stroganov & Yudin, 1956.
