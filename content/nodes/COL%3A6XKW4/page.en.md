## What leaf and reproductive traits are recorded for Sapium paucinervium? {#s-ae24951435}
The leaves are spiral and alternate, with persistent stipules and grooved stalks that may be winged near the tip. A stalk gland is described as either reverse-conical or cylindrical and spreading. Blades vary from elliptic to lance-shaped or oblong forms; they have wedge-shaped bases and tail-like tips. Their two surfaces differ in color, and the margins are entire or nearly entire. Secondary veins loop near the edge, while smaller cross-veins are inconspicuous. The solitary inflorescence is terminal. Male flowers have two stamens and partly joined sepals; the female calyx has three lobes. The capsule has three chambers and a pericarp about 0.8 mm thick. Ellipsoid seeds have blunt bases and tips.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-472f39167a}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
