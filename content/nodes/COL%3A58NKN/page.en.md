## A reed-like orchid on trees {#s-229a171411}
The regional description records T. gentilii as an epiphyte—a plant attached to another plant for support—with reed-like stems about 400 mm tall and 7 mm wide. Its narrow leaves reach about 150 × 10 mm, and small, star-shaped white flowers grow below the leaves.

## Trees in riverine bush {#s-8b5a0d6c87}
The regional habitat note records T. gentilii on trees in riverine bush. It names a setting but not a collection locality.
