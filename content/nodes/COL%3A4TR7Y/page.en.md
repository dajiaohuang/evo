## Ruschia testacea {#s-ac67aa27af}
## Sea-green leaves and brick-red flowers on long branches

Buys’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a shrub with branches about 240 mm long and internodes that range from pale red to maroon. The smooth, pale sea-green leaves may be sickle- or crescent-shaped, or nearly round; they measure 35–55 by about 6 mm.

Brick-red flowers grow in both terminal and side clusters. Their petals are about 1 mm wide, while thread-like staminodes may be few or absent. The five-chambered fruit has a funnel-shaped base. The habitat account records granitic soils.
