## Which leaf and flower traits mark Ocimum labiatum? {#s-fbc449b384}
Ocimum labiatum is described as a twiggy shrub 0.6–0.9 m tall, with ascending hairy branches. Its small, broadly ovate to nearly round leaves have hairs on both surfaces; the underside also bears long interwoven hairs and yellowish gland-dots. The mauve or pink corolla has an erect upper lip and a boat-shaped lower lip.

## How does the account distinguish it from related species? {#s-1e43155f6c}
The regional account says O. labiatum is closely related to O. labiatus but has smaller, more densely woolly leaves and smaller flowers. Compared with O. pseudoserratus, it has rounder leaves with scalloped margins, longer petioles and longer corolla lobes. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
