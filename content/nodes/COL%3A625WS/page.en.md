## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-4146855b21}
COL26.8 records Phaethontidae at family rank, with Phaethontiformes as its direct parent.

The AVONET route for Phaethontidae contains three measured species records in this batch: Phaethon aethereus, P. lepturus and P. rubricauda. Pinned COL26.8 supplies the family route; AVONET keeps the species’ historical HBW–BirdLife names and sample scopes.

## Differences between records {#s-f8448f3d80}
Among these records, compiled mean masses range from 328.04 g in P. lepturus to 749.99 g in P. aethereus. This is a span between species means, not the minimum and maximum mass of an individual bird. Marine habitat and Aquatic predator are represented categories, not evidence that every descendant shares one habitat or diet.

## Amniote Life-history archive member records {#s-b8761ee900}

## Limits attached to these records {#s-235bcc4260}
This family page joins only this batch’s three measured records, three historical life-history entries and three EltonTraits entries. Counts describe the routed records, not every accepted descendant or a family-level biological profile. AVONET values are species means under historical names; life-history summaries may retain inherited older taxonomy; EltonTraits weights standardize literature descriptions. A shared category or name does not establish a trait for the entire family or concept equivalence with COL26.8. Individual variation, current distributions, populations, conservation and missing biology require other sources.
