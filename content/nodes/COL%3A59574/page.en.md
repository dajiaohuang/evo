## A long spike of small brown-pink flowers {#s-6329a4cabd}
Tritoniopsis elongata has stiff stems, mostly 2–3 mm thick, bearing many small, dull or brownish-pink flowers along a spike longer than the non-flowering stem. The bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) are conspicuously pointed and hard at flowering.

## Low sandy loam {#s-a7f4df4fc4}
The flora records sandy loam at low elevations; it gives no exact elevation interval.
