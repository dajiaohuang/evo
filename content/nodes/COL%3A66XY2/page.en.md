## Name and placement in COL26.8 {#s-e60d101378}
COL26.8 lists Ansonia torrentis Dring, 1983 as an accepted species. In the release dated 20 August 2026, its recorded path runs through Anura (order), Bufonidae (family) and Ansonia (genus). This placement belongs to that checklist; the same name in another source needs its own identity evidence.

## Traits from a historical compilation {#s-f68f7e9dbc}
The AmphiBIO entries on this page use a historical Frost 2011 taxon concept, whose equivalence to COL26.8 has not been established. They report broad foraging categories, one compiled body-length maximum, a reproductive-frequency maximum and a larval-stage flag. Each is a source record, not a complete description of the species.

## Foraging categories in AmphiBIO {#s-9fa71b5647}
AmphiBIO's Frost 2011 historical classification flags terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal (tree-associated) foraging categories. These are broad labels, not a detailed account of seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. The available record does not show whether these categories were changed, so it supports neither a replacement value nor a claim that each original label is wrong. Equivalence between this historical concept and COL26.8 is unconfirmed.

## Maximum adult body length in the compilation {#s-02e904389d}
Under AmphiBIO's historical Frost 2011 classification, maximum adult body length is recorded as 33 mm. For Anura, body length means snout-to-vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona it means total length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual. Its source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to COL26.8.

## A compiled breeding-frequency value {#s-ee543eb538}
Under AmphiBIO's historical Frost 2011 classification, the maximum recorded reproductive frequency is one event per year. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a calendar observed across every population. The row does not tie the value to sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions. In this compilation, offspring-size fields may refer to offspring or eggs; no such size value is reported in this entry. Equivalence with the current checklist concept is unresolved.

## A larval-stage flag {#s-f6f8be4f35}
The AmphiBIO record flags a larval stage under its historical Frost 2011 taxon concept. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding place or seasonal timing, and the concept has not been shown to match the one in COL26.8.
