## ## A tall tree with long pods and differently shaped anthers {#s-519a139079}
The historical Flora of Panama describes *Cassia moschata* as a tree reaching 12 m, with young branchlets that are softly hairy and longitudinally ridged. Its large leaves may bear up to 20 pairs of oblong leaflets, about 4–5 cm long and up to 1.5 cm wide. The yellow flowers have ten stamens in several forms: three large, four medium and two small fertile stamens, plus one rudimentary stamen; their anthers differ in shape and in whether pores are terminal or basal. The cylindrical, partitioned, hairless pod may reach 50 cm long and 2 cm wide. The same historical flora gives a range from Central America to British Guiana, without a sampling frame or observation date, so this is not a current range inventory.
