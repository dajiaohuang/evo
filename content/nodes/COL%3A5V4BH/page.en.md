## What does the record say about Annona ferruginea? {#s-b6fbb481fe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes fissured bark and an upright trunk. Leaves have entire edges, curved veins, and pointed to blunt tips, with the midrib impressed on the upper surface. Green flowers grow just above the leaf axils and have three petals and free sepals. The fruit is an aggregate with separate carpels; the seeds are brown and rough.

The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
