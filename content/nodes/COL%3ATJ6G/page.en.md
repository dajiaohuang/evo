## Nectaries along the leaf stalk {#s-3fb8092df1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records two to four pairs of oblong to elliptic leaflets and small axillary flower clusters, sometimes with only one flower. It notes extrafloral nectaries on the petiole and leaf axis; these are nectar-producing structures outside the flower. The yellow flowers have ten stamens, and the pod valves are recorded as twisting at maturity. The source also records a xylopodium as present but does not describe its form here.
