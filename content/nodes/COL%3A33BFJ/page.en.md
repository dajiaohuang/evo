## Cypella pusilla {#s-f3016a63bd}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 record, Cypella pusilla is a bulbous terrestrial plant up to 35 cm tall, with keeled leaf blades no wider than 10 mm. Its pseudolateral inflorescence is unbranched and bears a sessile or nearly sessile, one-flowered rhipidium. The flowers are recorded as yellow or white; the entry also specifies ranges for anther length, connective colour and width, and the upright to slightly twisted style crests. The habitat field gives only “terrestrial.”
