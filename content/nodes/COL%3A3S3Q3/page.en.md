## Grey-coated leaves and solitary yellow flowers {#s-7084194cba}
The erect shrublet reaches 20 cm. Its basally fused leaves are rough, three-sided in cross-section and coated with a grey, waxy bloom; they are 15–30 mm long. Stalked flowers are solitary and golden to lemon yellow, rarely cream, and up to 30 mm across. Seasonally wet sandstone soils or sandy clay loams are recorded.
