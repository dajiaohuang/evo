## Pale flowers on a reduced rootstock {#s-1a6450ac1b}
Gladiolus aquamontanus is 40–100 cm tall. The account describes a reduced, rhizomes (horizontal underground stems)-like rootstock with a basal fan of four to six leaves; its pale mauve-pink flowers have a 34–40 mm tube.

## Streams and wet cliffs {#s-ae6e8f3eff}
The SANBI flora records streams and wet cliffs as habitat; the entry does not describe the species’ full range.
