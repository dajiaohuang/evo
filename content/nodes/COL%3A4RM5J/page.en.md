## An EltonTraits record with a historical name {#s-cdd712aa9f}
Rattus macleari is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Muridae. Its three ecological field groups here come from the same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 entry. That archive uses earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals; this name link does not resolve later splits or synonymy.

## Diet weights are standardized estimates {#s-9031af3e9a}
The nonzero weights are 40% seeds, nuts, and spores; 30% invertebrates; 20% fruit; and 10% other plant material, including fungi. They standardize verbal descriptions, not measured diet composition. ABC pools high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and some estimates may borrow from an ecologically similar congener. These are not necessarily direct observations of R. macleari. A zero does not prove never eaten, and Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broad source categories.

## Ground is a broad stratum category {#s-d74fe45b99}
The mammalian record assigns G, the ground category, whose definition includes some aquatic foraging. Code A expresses compiler confidence in source and assignment; metadata attributes mammalian stratum classifications collectively to Nowak (1999). The label is not a time proportion or proof of exclusive ground foraging.

## A night code is not a full activity schedule {#s-dca7cd0d0f}
The archive records night feeding with a value of 1. Multiple activity periods can share that value, so it is not a daily time budget or a schedule guaranteed across seasons and sites. ABC indicates activity certainty; the estimate may rely on a similar congener. Genus/family typical activity values are not used to fill gaps.

## Keep the reference labels with each field group {#s-0b6b46b4d0}
The retained fields carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999 labels. Diet, stratum, and activity may have distinct sources; matching Ref numbers in bird and mammal reference tables do not imply one common publication. The archive records original strings with file, row, field group, and reference-table context. Books and papers have not been individually read, so labels are not additional independent observations.

## Limits on interpretation {#s-86d2b0fb8e}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a historical same-name, same-family guide rather than a full species monograph; its species concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Diet and avian-stratum percentages are coarse literature-derived weights, not direct measures of stomach contents, food intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportion uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity can use an ecologically similar congener, so not every field is direct observation of this species. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata, and split records awaiting review do not support definite species-level conclusions. Underlying references have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation require other sources.
