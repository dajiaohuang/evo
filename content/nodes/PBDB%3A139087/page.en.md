## Idelesaurus — A New Dicynodont from the Upper Permian of Tatarstan {#body}
Kurkin describes the holotype as a skull without a lower jaw from Semin Ovrag, Tatarstan. It is broad and subtriangular in dorsal outline, with a tapering snout and forward-facing orbits. The author compares it with Australobarbarus; these are skull observations and a proposed relationship, not behavior or a resolved family tree.


## In brief {#preview}
A broad, tapering skull anchors the description; the proposed Australobarbarus relationship is a hypothesis, not behavioral evidence.

## Limits of this evidence {#limits}
Single holotype skull and referred material; no body-wide lifestyle inference.
