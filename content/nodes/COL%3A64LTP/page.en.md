## *Acritopappus heterolepis*: two-sided leaf surfaces and awned pappus {#s-645ed2c4db}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a hairless, non-sticky stem. Its stalked, flat leaves are elliptic or egg-shaped, with a long, tapering tip and serrated margins; the lower surface is hairy while the upper is hairless. Flower heads occur in panicles with bell-shaped bract cups and convex receptacles. The flower parts are glandular. The fruit-tip bears a pappus, listed as either crown-shaped with awns or awned. These regional field entries include alternatives.
