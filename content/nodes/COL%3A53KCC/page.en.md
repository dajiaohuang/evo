## Stem, leaves, and inflorescence {#s-5db8352e56}
The field lists solitary, clustered, underground, or prostrate stems with leaf-sheath remnants. Green pinnate leaves have persistent sheaths arranged helically around the stem, with fibrous toothed margins; the petiole margin is unarmed, and the leaf axis is erect. The leaflets are leathery, uniformly arranged at one angle, erect and rigid, with dark-green surfaces of the same colour on both sides. The panicle may be erect; the fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves. Its spadix is grooved and may be hairless or waxy-coated; the main axis may be hairless or sparsely woolly, and the smaller branches spiral around it.

## Fruit {#s-274b986cc9}
Fruit is elliptic, with a green-yellow outer layer that may be lightly hairy, a fibrous succulent middle layer, and a woody fusiform inner layer. The endosperm is homogeneous.
