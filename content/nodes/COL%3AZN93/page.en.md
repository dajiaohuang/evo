## Compact clusters of yellow flowers {#s-34569228bb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes an erect plant up to one metre tall, with hairy stems and three leaflets per leaf. Its compact, many-flowered clusters sit opposite the leaves and carry persistent narrow bracts. The flowers are yellow. The oblong brown pod is pubescent, meaning it bears short hairs; the seeds are yellowish to ochre-brown.
