## Anf2103 and the recorded concept {#s-2a7bc25343}
In AmphiBIO v1, Anf2103 is the entry for Hemisus barotseensis, placed by the source in Hemisus (Hemisotidae, Anura). Its concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), while equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging category — fossorial {#s-d13ce37df8}
Row Anf2103 records the fossorial (underground-associated) foraging category for Hemisus barotseensis. It is a broad feeding-stratum label, not a detailed account of seasonal or life-stage changes.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. The row-level comparison is not captured here, so this historical category needs checking before reuse; no corrected value is supplied, and the notice does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Adult food category — arthropods {#s-510eb57fe8}
The AmphiBIO row for Hemisus barotseensis lists arthropods as adult food categories.

The compilation draws these categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents. A listed category is neither a complete diet nor a measured proportion.

## Seasonal activity — wet and warm {#s-3d405b7e24}
The source row marks wet and warm in its seasonal-activity fields. These labels compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-d1841a9486}
Its maximum adult body length is 55.01 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Compiled clutch counts {#s-b11922a34d}
For reproduction, offspring-or-egg counts per clutch (one reproductive batch) range from 150 to 200.

AmphiBIO compiles extrema and counts from literature; this row does not separately identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-c452f06549}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
