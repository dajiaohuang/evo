## *Baccharis minutiflora*: narrow, serrated leaves and single heads {#s-57551f4400}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records linear leaves without stalks, with one main vein and serrated margins. Flower heads are solitary in the leaf axils. It records heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered; these are alternatives in the dataset, not a combined description. Rock-associated and terrestrial substrate labels are present, with no locality supplied.
