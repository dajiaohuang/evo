## Dieffenbachia seguine: ranges and variation in a regional flora {#s-7d0783e721}
The 2020 Brazilian Flora entry records basal stem branches as absent or few and aerial stems as erect, generally over 1.0 m tall. Petioles are smooth and reach 23 cm or generally exceed 23 cm; they are at least half the leaf-blade length. The sheath is recorded as less than or more than half the petiole length. Petiole color is listed as uniformly green or sometimes densely marked with pale green.

Leaf blades are described as oblanceolate, elliptic, oblong, ovate or lanceolate. Recorded lengths are 20–40 cm or under 60 cm; widths are over 10 cm or under 20 cm, with a length-to-width ratio of 1.7–2. The upper surface is listed as uniform green, dark green, with a lighter midrib, or lighter-variegated and highly variable. Bases are coded as acute, obtuse, rounded, emarginate, subcordate, cordate or asymmetric. The angle of the secondary veins is under 80°.

The number of inflorescences per sympodium is listed as 1, several or fewer than 4. Peduncle lengths are given as generally under 8 cm, 7.5–25 cm or under 30 cm. Spathes are generally over 10 cm long, their length-to-peduncle ratio is over 1.0, and mature spathes range from pale green to green.

These are measurements and descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Its overlapping and alternative categories are retained as reported rather than collapsed into one profile.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
