## Philodendron maculatum: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-883b40970b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a prophyll on the stem. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous petiole is slightly flattened above and rounded below. Leaf blades are entire, cordate, ovate or triangular, with acuminate tips and entire margins.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium. Its short peduncle is shorter than the spathe. The slightly constricted spathe has an outer tube described as cream when young, an outer surface with vinaceous spots, a greenish-cream inner tube and a whitish outer blade. The spadix is stipitate.

The entry lists 3 or 4 stamens; staminode state is unknown. The gynoecium and ovary are subcylindrical. Locules, ovules and stigma are recorded as unknown; placentation is listed as axial, basal, subbasal or biseriate. Fruit and seed are also unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Unknown and alternative source states are retained.
