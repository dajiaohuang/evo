## What does Dischisma clandestinum look like? {#s-3e4e9b3b6e}
The source describes an upright, hairy annual 10–20 cm tall. Its narrow, spreading leaves are 20–30 mm long and 1–2 mm wide. Broad, hairy bracts below the flowers taper into leaf-like tips. Small white flowers, 5–7 mm long, form an elongated spike; their narrow sepals have fine hairs along the edges.
