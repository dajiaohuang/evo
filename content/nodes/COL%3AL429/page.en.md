## Deeply divided leaves {#s-8e4c1ca3dd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records deeply two-lobed leaves with a straight to shallowly heart-shaped base, a pointed tip, a smooth upper surface and a hairy underside. It also describes a flower calyx that splits into two to five lobes as the flower opens, ten anthers, and a pod that opens elastically. The row lists alternative states for some floral features, so it does not describe one fixed combination across the species.
