## Late-winter pink flowers {#s-33f75599f3}
Hesperantha candida reaches 300 mm and bears usually pink to mauve-pink, sometimes white flowers. The tube is 7–12 mm long; flowers open in daylight in late winter or early spring, when leaf tips may be dry or scorched.

## Grassland, bushveld and rocky shelter {#s-ea9ee15f4b}
The flora records grassland, bushveld, rock ledges, and sheltered spaces between boulders. These are recorded settings, not a complete habitat survey.
