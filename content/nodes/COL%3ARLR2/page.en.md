## Paired leaflets on a grooved rachis {#s-5aa187aafe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes alternate leaves arranged in two ranks, with 6 to 12 pairs of oblong-elliptic leaflets. Each leaflet is about 4.5 to 7 cm long and 1.5 to 2.5 cm wide, nearly smooth except along the midvein. Axillary racemes carry yellow petals 1.3 to 2 cm long; the record notes ten stamens. The stems and flower stalks are hairy, and several fine details are explicitly unknown in the source.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-ac3577671c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row classifies the habitat as terrícola, or terrestrial. It gives no collection locality or complete range.
