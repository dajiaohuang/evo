## Distribution records {#s-4902407278}
The study overlaid IUCN range maps on ten major Brazilian Amazon interfluves and listed Mico munduruku in 1 areas: Tapajós. Here, occurrence means a region-level map overlap; it does not confirm animals at every site in those areas and is not a population count or complete range assessment.
