## White flowers with blue lines and no inner tepals {#s-3bf2ad1a04}
Moraea barnardii grows up to 300 mm tall and has white flowers faintly veined blue underneath. The flowers lack inner tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals); dark-blue anthers and red pollen are recorded, along with nectar guides formed by a dark-blue ring and radiating lines.
