## Deeply lobed leaves and orange-red ray flowers {#s-f2d0f2ca9e}
This morphology record describes leaves with deep lobes, a smooth upper surface and a finely hairy underside. One to three radiate heads sit at branch tips, with orange to red ray florets and about 100 tubular disk florets. The cylindrical fruits have ten ribs and a fine hair covering; their white pappus persists. The habitat field says terrestrial.
