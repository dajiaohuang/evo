## Bulbine haworthioides: fringed leaves in a ground-hugging rosette {#s-d722c7ed66}
A South African regional account describes a dwarf geophyte 90–150 mm tall, with a solitary, broadly oblong to rounded tuber. About 14 succulent, boat-shaped leaves, each about 5 mm wide, form a tight rosette pressed to the ground. Their upper surfaces are nearly flat and grey-green, with prominent white lengthwise and crosswise veins separated by large translucent patches. The midvein is often sparsely fringed, while the margins are densely fringed. The leaves wither at flowering. Dull-yellow flowers grow on a wiry raceme. The capsule is not known from the account.
