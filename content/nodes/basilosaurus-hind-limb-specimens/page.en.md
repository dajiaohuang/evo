## Basilosaurus hind limbs: small bones, separate records {#s-c1a798626f}
Egyptian Basilosaurus material preserves a pelvis with articulated hind-limb and foot bones. A separate named specimen, male UM 93231, provides a femur whose compact-bone microstructure was measured. These records establish that the hind limbs were present and tiny; they do not indicate weight-bearing or terrestrial travel. A proposed role as a guide during mating, and claims about effects on whole-body swimming, remain functional interpretations rather than observed behaviour.
