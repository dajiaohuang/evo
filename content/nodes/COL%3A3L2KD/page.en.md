## A woody rootstock and tiny orange anthers {#s-43783bbcd9}
This perennial herb has a thick, woody caudex and prostrate stems up to 7 cm long. Its obovate-elliptic leaves carry short, appressed, curled hairs. Small cymose clusters have three to six flowers; the flowers bear stiff spreading hairs, equal sepals and thread-like petals. The orange, four-sided anthers are about 0.25 mm across, and the style is split into two lobes or branches. Flowering is recorded in March; the fruit is an ellipsoid nutlet. Alpine regions and Drakensberg Grassland are listed.
