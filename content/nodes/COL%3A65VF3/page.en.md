## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-27f685369c}
The source describes adventitious roots and an underground stem. Leaf sheaths are fibrous and hairy on the underside; the petioles are hairy, while the leaf axis is hairless and bears rigid leaflets in groups. The spike-like inflorescence emerges between the leaves; its short axis and stalk are hairy, and its partly woody spathe is fissured outside. Male and female flowers are cream-colored and have parts in threes; female flowers are stalkless. The yellow, egg-shaped fruit has a hard, thin inner wall and fibrous flesh. The embryo is at the seed base, and the endosperm is uniform.
