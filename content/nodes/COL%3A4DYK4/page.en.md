## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Passiflora kikiana? {#s-5c819e2e7e}
The cylindrical stems may be woody or partly woody. Entire leaves have smooth margins and fine short hairs; stalked glands occur on the petiole, and stipules are bristle-like. Flowers may be solitary or paired, with elliptic bracts and a bell-shaped floral cup. Sepals lack awns. The corona has several rows; the operculum ends in threadlike filaments, and the limen is ring-shaped. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless, with elliptic seeds that are pitted.
