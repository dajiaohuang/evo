## Low leaves and pale flowers {#s-5c222d3399}
The regional description portrays S. rupestre as a slender, tuberous geophyte up to 40 cm tall. Two to four spreading, oval leaves grow near the ground. Its rather dense raceme (flowers arranged along a central stalk) bears a few to many white flowers with pale-green tinges; both sepals and spurs are recorded at 2–4 mm.
