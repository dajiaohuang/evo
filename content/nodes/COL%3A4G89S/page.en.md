The Brazilian Flora 2020 field for Philodendron nadruzianum combines a triangular blade with a sinuate margin and an axillary inflorescence whose spadix is described as pale green to cream. Several counts and reproductive states are alternatives in the source record.

## Morphology, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-75fe06e9bb}
The field describes clasping and feeding roots. The stem has short, rugose (rough-surfaced) internodes and a pale-green prophyll. The inconspicuous leaf sheath contrasts with the subcylindrical green petiole. The triangular blade has a sagittate base and sinuate margin; “3–5” follows these blade descriptors, but the structured text does not identify what it counts. The axillary, sympodial inflorescence is listed with 1, 2, or 3 units. Its short peduncle is shorter than the spathe and recorded as green or wine-coloured. The spathe is slightly constricted, with a light-green outer tube; the subsessile spadix is recorded as light green or cream.

The record lists 4 or 5 stamens and long staminodes narrowed at the base. The gynoecium is ovoid and the ovary subcylindrical. It gives 7 or 8 locules, 3 or 4 ovules, and basal or subbasal placentation. The stigma is recorded as cylindrical or capitate (head-shaped); berry and seed traits are unknown.
