## Aromatic annual with dense yellow heads {#s-fc548ca3c8}
The record describes an aromatic, much-branched annual up to 50 cm tall, with finely divided leaves and many small yellow flower heads in dense clusters. It distinguishes the heads from those of O. piluliferus by their smaller size and slightly bent-down bracts. The source also reports occasional intermediate-looking hybrids with O. grandiflorus along the West Coast.
