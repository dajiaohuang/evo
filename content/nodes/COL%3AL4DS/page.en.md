## One description of stems, leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-b09c4380e9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 regional morphology record (row 79743; WFO name crosswalk wfo-0000173949) describes spinescent branches and rudimentary stipules, the small appendages at leaf bases. The leaf blade is bilobed: its lobes range from triangular to blunt, its base from truncate to shallowly heart-shaped, and its tip from acuminate or acute to slightly blunt. The upper surface is described as becoming glabrous or glabrous, while long soft hairs occur along the lower veins. The flower account reports elliptical-to-lanceolate buds, a disk-shaped hypanthium (cup-like tissue around the ovary base), and a bell-shaped calyx with five irregular teeth. Petals are obovate to spatulate, with the adaxial (axis-facing) petal folded and narrower. The field also reports ten anthers, separate stamens that do not unite into a staminal column, a stalked ovary, and a legume that opens late.
