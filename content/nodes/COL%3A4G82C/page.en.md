## Philodendron edmundoi: flower and habitat descriptors {#s-0423afcb21}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records clasping roots and a vinaceous prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous petiole is smooth and cylindrical, dark green with a purple tip. Leaf blades are entire, elliptic or oblong, with a cuneate base and entire margins.

One axillary inflorescence is listed per sympodium. Its cylindrical peduncle is green. The constricted spathe has a light-green outer tube, a pink inner tube with reddish resin, vinaceous spots outside and a vinaceous inner blade. The spadix is divided into female, sterile-male and fertile-male zones.

Stamens and staminodes are short; the gynoecium and ovary are ovoid. Locule counts are listed as 6, 7 or 8, and 12 ovules are recorded with axial placentation and a discoid stigma. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative locule counts and source-reported colors are retained.

The dataset gives two habitat labels: “Hemiepífita” (hemiepiphytic) and “Rupícola” (rock-dwelling).

These regional categories give no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
