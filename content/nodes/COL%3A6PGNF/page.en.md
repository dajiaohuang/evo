## Accepted name and classification {#s-6d57700f28}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts Leptodactylus paraensis Heyer, 2005 as a species and places it along Anura (order) → Leptodactylidae (family) → Leptodactylus (genus). This is the name and classification recorded in that version; a similarly named entry in another checklist or fossil database needs its own identity evidence before the records can be linked.

## A nematode host record in a regional literature review {#s-58d17f0ad8}
A 2025 regional review lists Leptodactylus paraensis as a host in its Molineidae survey, associating that name with the nematode Oswaldocruzia lanfrediae. The association is compiled from separate literature reports, not a new examination of frogs or evidence that every individual of this species is infected. The entry calls L. paraensis the parasite's type host: the host named in the parasite's original description, not the amphibian's type specimen. The cited reports and archived specimens have not been reidentified here. For this host, the review gives no denominator of frogs examined, prevalence, infection intensity, pathology, or mortality.
