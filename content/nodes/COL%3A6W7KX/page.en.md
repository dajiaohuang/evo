## Portulaca grandiflora {#s-49e2400190}
## Tuberous roots and cylindrical leaves

The morphology record describes Portulaca grandiflora with tuberous roots and narrow, cylindrical leaves that end in a point. The axillary hairs are inconspicuous and shorter than the leaves; the leaves lack coarse hairs. Pink or magenta petals top the flower, whose sepals have hooded, pointed tips. The fruit has a semi-globose lid, and the seed-coat cells are near-square or wavy, with projections. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings.
