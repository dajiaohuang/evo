## Regional gradients and survey gains do not certify recovery {#s-a14b2359e2}
The reserve description spans 1,150–3,830 m, the sampling Methods report 800–2,600 m, and statistical Methods describe observed amphibians at 800–2,500 m. These scopes are retained rather than assigned as this species’ measured range. Richness peaks in two regional bands but its linear elevation association is nonsignificant (reported R² 0.114, p 0.412). Historical recorded-species increase is also nonsignificant (Mann–Kendall p 0.148). Neither result establishes unchanged populations, increased abundance or a causal benefit of management. Discussion and Conclusions use stronger fragmentation, conservation-success and large-population language than these tests alone support; raw records and historical sampling comparability have not been independently reanalysed.
