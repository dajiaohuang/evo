## Leipoldtia gigantea {#s-6740f71f2f}
## Finger-shaped leaves and broad-winged fruits

Klak’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a succulent shrub 300–500 mm tall and up to 1 m across, with woody stems. The smooth leaves are finger-shaped and round in cross-section. Their bases fuse into a 4–5 mm sheath; the free portions are 45–70 mm long, 6–9 mm wide and 6–9 mm thick.

Three to five flowers, 30–35 mm across, grow together. Their petals are white and more rarely pink; thread-like staminodes are absent. The fruit has nine chambers, broad winged valves and large closing bodies. The habitat account records karroid vegetation.
