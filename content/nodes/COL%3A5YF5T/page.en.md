## Narrow leaves below a purple-edged flower cluster {#s-5ead4e955a}
The morphology record describes alternate, sessile or nearly sessile, narrow leaves with smooth or nearly smooth surfaces. A single compact, rounded cluster of heads terminates the stem; its bracts are purple or green with purple edges. The lilac flowers have a white ring near the top of the tube and at the base of the lobes. The pappus persists on the fruits. The habitat field says terrestrial.
