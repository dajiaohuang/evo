## Philodendron nullinervium: leaf and floral characters {#s-66b110e256}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a prophyll described as deciduous and dark green. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous green petiole is cylindrical, flattened above and rounded below. Leaf blades are entire, hastate, elliptic or oblong, with entire margins; lateral veins are obscure when the blade is green.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium, with a long peduncle. The spathe tube is vinaceous on both surfaces; its outer blade is light green and the inner blade light pink. The spadix is subsessile.

The entry lists 3, 4 or 5 stamens, narrowed at the base, and staminodes narrow at the base. The gynoecium is prismatic; the ovary and stigma are unknown. Locule counts are 7 or 8 and ovule counts 3 or 4, with axial placentation. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Alternative counts and unknown source states are retained.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
