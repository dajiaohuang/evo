## Distinguish the measured length {#s-4c7f800bf8}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum straight carapace length (SCL) 100 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding head, neck and tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Microhabitat and activity time {#s-4e05dc5c2f}
The entry lists microhabitat as Aquatic and active timing as Nocturnal. These recorded categories are not an exhaustive habitat list or fixed schedule; populations, seasons and life stages may differ.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-eabe2e0f70}
The reproductive-mode field uses the original label oviparous; consolidated mean offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 2.73; egg length 32 mm. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-1454f4d425}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 144 g; mass at birth or hatching 3 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-d1f0d2fe0a}
Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 2920 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 2.73 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 106 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-af486782c4}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 60 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.
