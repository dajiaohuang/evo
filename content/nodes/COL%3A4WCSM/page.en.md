## How does Selago atherstonei grow and flower? {#s-4005b158c0}
Selago atherstonei is described as an herb up to 40 cm tall, with hairy stems and narrow leaves. Dense flower heads occur at the ends and upper sides of branches; they are white or brick-pink. The account notes that the heads are not always solitary: young stems may first carry one head, then branch and form looser clusters.

## How is it distinguished from related Selago species? {#s-e9bf5240ef}
The source contrasts its short, hairy leaves and 2–3 mm calyx with S. procera, which has larger, clearly veined leaves and larger branched flower clusters. It says the known area of S. atherstonei lies within that of S. procera. It is also closely allied to S. lydenburgensis and may hybridize with it; the source presents hybridization as possible, not confirmed.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-bfd9d7b837}
The habitat field places it in mountain grassland.
