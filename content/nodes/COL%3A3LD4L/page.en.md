## What range of leaf, flower and fruit traits is recorded for Heteropterys sincorensis? {#s-0925bfd9ff}
Opposite leaves may lie close to the branch. The leathery blades range from elliptic or oval to round, with rounded or heart-shaped bases and rounded or small-pointed tips. Their surfaces may become hairless; glands range from inconspicuous to conspicuous and may be sunken at the base or margin, while the margins roll under. Upright inflorescences have persistent elliptic or oval bracts and stalked pedicels. Sepals may be glandular or only the front sepal glandless, with hairy glands and rusty hairs. Petals range from yellow to orange-red, with margins that may be glandular or toothed; they can reflex, spread or stand upright. Anthers are upright and hairless. Styles are equal, straight and hairless, with pointed or blunt tips. The seed-bearing fruit body has conspicuous side veins.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-245094bbf2}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
