## What distinguishes its flowers and seeds? {#s-eae7f77aa2}
The plant has simple stems and petiolate leaves arranged alternately or in two ranks. The blades range from elliptic to broadly elliptic. Small unisexual flower clusters include male and female flowers, and female flowers may also occur singly. Both sexes are described with five red sepals and an entire nectar disc. Male flowers have two stamens joined together. The fruit is a spherical capsule, and the seeds have striated surfaces.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-9fd119fdf2}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
