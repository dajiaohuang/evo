## Limits of the historical ecological record {#s-998e726fa5}
EltonTraits 1.0 is a guide to historical same-name, same-family records, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized relative weights from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar, so not every field is a direct observation of this species. Genus or family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation need other evidence. Diet carrying source code D1 is withheld because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; its archived value does not support a definite ecology conclusion.
