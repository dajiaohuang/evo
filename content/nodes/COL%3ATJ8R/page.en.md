## Many pairs of rounded leaflets {#s-a47e2c9498}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records four to 21 pairs of nearly round to broadly egg-shaped leaflets on spirally arranged leaves. Terminal flower clusters bear bracts; their yellow petals differ in shape, and ten stamens are listed. Glandular hairs are recorded on the stems. The pod valves twist when mature, while the seed form is recorded as unknown.
