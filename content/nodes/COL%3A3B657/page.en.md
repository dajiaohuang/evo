## Eriosema diffusum {#s-5105fce547}

The Flora of Panama describes an erect herb or subshrub with three leaflets; the leaflet blades measure 2.5–4.7 × 0.8–1.2 cm and are silvery-tomentose beneath. Yellow flowers occur 5–10 per cluster; the fruit is 0.9–1.1 × 0.5–0.7 cm, and black or mottled seeds are 3.0–3.3 × 1.5–2.0 mm. The account gives a range from Mexico to northern Colombia.
