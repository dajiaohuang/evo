## Classification {#s-1ca41e1444}
The 2017 description named the Manado form Tarsius spectrumgurskyae and emphasized duet-call form and specimen provenance in identifying northern Sulawesi tarsiers. Its type locality is Manado; the paper situates the species name within a regional biogeographic argument.

## Communication {#s-729846d291}
A later study analyzed duet recordings collected at Tangkoko National Park in July and August 2018. The authors’ classifier identified female phrases to individuals more accurately than male phrases (80% versus 64%); female timing also varied more in the sampled duet contributions.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-b692afddbd}
The acoustic results apply to the recorded pairs and study site, not all populations. Earlier behavior research published under T. spectrum should be mapped to this accepted name only when the studied population is identifiable.
