## Large fruits on axillary flower clusters {#s-230bbb919f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records tendrils as present, solitary, or in one pair, and spines as absent. Petioles are 5–18 mm long; leaves have three to five veins, and the underside axils of the basal veins are not bearded. Axillary thyrsoid inflorescences may be short- or long-racemose. The corolla is tubular or salver-shaped, with a tube longer than its lobes; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 6–7 cm in diameter, with a woody fruit wall 4–6 mm thick. The source lists five to ten or more than ten seeds, with a crust-like coat.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-dbbe86b320}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field lists *Strychnos peckii* as terrestrial but supplies no collection locality.
