## What the evidence supports {#s-2139cb6c60}
The six specimens come from Hengdaozi in western Liaoning. Their Oxfordian age is bounded at about 160–154 Ma; a 157 ± 3 Ma zircon date comes from overlying rock, not a skeleton. Placement and neoteny remain analysis-dependent interpretations.

## Articulated skeletons preserve a developmental clue {#s-01a76b0662}
The holotype PKUP V0601 and referred specimens PKUP V0602–V0606 preserve articulated salamandroid anatomy, including structures interpreted as larval. The authors discuss these features as possible neoteny, the retention of juvenile traits into a later life stage. That interpretation is not a direct record of age or reproduction, and the specimens do not supply a complete growth series.

## The rock dates the sample, not a skeleton {#s-d920cc99bb}
The specimens come from Hengdaozi in western Liaoning, China, in the Tiaojishan Formation. The profile uses an Oxfordian sample interval of about 160–154 Ma. The reported zircon age of 157 ± 3 Ma is from rock above the fossil-bearing horizon, not from the skeleton itself. The published character matrix places Beiyanerpeton within Salamandroidea, but that result depends on the study’s sampled taxa and characters.
