## A founder egg mass and one infertile captive spawning are different records {#s-aa052a7e83}
Pulcher entered the facility in May 2013 as a 33-egg mass collected on a Pandanus plant near the breeding facility. The study later recorded only one captive spawning event for the maintained species, in the warm rainy season, and that egg mass was infertile and lay on the terrarium’s glass bottom. The founder count is not the size of the later captive mass or a mean clutch size. The one infertile event is not a successful captive generation, proof of sterility across the species or an annual breeding-frequency estimate.

## Artificial oviposition-site use does not replace Pandanus ecology {#s-ad21c38d57}
The authors described pulcher as associated with water-filled Pandanus leaf structures and supplied similar-shaped live and artificial plants in captivity. The observed glass-bottom egg mass led them to question whether suitable laying sites had been provided for the two maintained Guibemantis species. That explanation was tentative. A captive glass-bottom event does not establish a wild terrestrial-breeding mode, habitual avoidance of Pandanus or a measured substrate preference. No successful larval development is inferred from the infertile recorded captive mass, and founder identification remained morphology-based.
