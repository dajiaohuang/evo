## Parioscorpio: how a scorpion claim was reconsidered {#s-ba363b291c}
In 2020, Wendruff and colleagues interpreted Parioscorpio specimens UWGM 2162 and 2163 as scorpions and compared internal structures with a pulmonary-cardiovascular system. A 2021 redescription rejected the scorpion identification and left the animal’s precise arthropod affinity unresolved. The earlier paper remains important as a historical hypothesis, but these fossils do not establish scorpion anatomy, terrestrial physiology, or habitat.
