## Leaf lobes, petals and fruit dehiscence {#s-eea4b27935}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records persistent stipules and bilobed leaf blades on flowering or fruiting branches. Young buds have an unwinged calyx tube and are subglobose or ovoid. At anthesis, the calyx is campanulate and its lobes are recorded as absent. The upper petal is spathulate or obovate, with a strigose outer surface; anthers are oblong to linear and the ovary is sessile. The fruit is indehiscent, and the habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
