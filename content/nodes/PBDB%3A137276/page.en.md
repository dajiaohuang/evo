## Recorded parent of Ornithurae {#s-729cd7bba4}
Ornithurae is listed at rank subclass in the PBDB classification route, with Ornithuromorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
