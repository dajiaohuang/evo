## Two straight, terete leaves and small white flowers {#s-661e6e7a5a}
Moraea teretifolia reaches 200 mm and has two basal, nearly round leaves about as long as the stem. Its small white flowers have broad nectar guides; the capsule (a seed-bearing fruit) are 9–10 mm long and lack a beak.

## Sandy, stony ground {#s-cbe1275423}
