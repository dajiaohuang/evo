## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-b350ef1ef0}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 61 mm for Craugastor laevissimus. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
