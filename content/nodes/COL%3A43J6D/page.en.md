## Minaria acerosa {#s-0e9ec8561e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Minaria acerosa* as an accepted species of *Minaria*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes stems hairy over their surface and leaves arranged in whorls, rarely opposite, with oblong blades and rolled-under margins. The flower clusters are umbel-shaped, and the corolla ranges from bell-shaped to nearly wheel-shaped. The habitat field says terrestrial; these are regional source fields, not a complete species account.
