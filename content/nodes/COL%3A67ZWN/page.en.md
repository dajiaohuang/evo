## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-7b725220e7}
The source describes clustered stems up to 6 m tall, with black spines reaching 15 cm. Pinnate leaves carry 15–21 pairs of narrow, lance-shaped leaflets, grouped irregularly in sets of two to five at different angles. The branched inflorescence emerges between the leaves, and its stalk bract bears spines. The fruit is obovoid or egg-shaped and orange, with a smooth outer surface.
