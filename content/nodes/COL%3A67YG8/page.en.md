## What distinguishes the growth and flowers of Bacopa monnierioides? {#s-ae49383ea2}
The cited flora describes an erect herb up to 45 cm tall; lower stem joints sometimes root where they touch the ground. Opposite leaves are narrow and clasp the stem at their bases. Tiny white to bluish-white flowers appear in small leaf-axil clusters. Each flower has four stamens, and the capsule contains numerous seeds.

## What range does the source give? {#s-c71d313414}
The source reports Bacopa monnierioides from Paraguay north to Guatemala. This is the range stated in that flora account.
