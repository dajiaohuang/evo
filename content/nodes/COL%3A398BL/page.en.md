## Elephantopus palustris {#s-5d7ce520d1}
## Terminal clusters of four florets above lance-shaped leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes basal leaves in a rosette and smaller, alternate leaves along the stem. Blades are linear to lance-shaped, sessile or shortly stalked; the source records hairiness and vein prominence as variable.

Terminal clusters hold four florets. Papillae occur on the corolla lobes and style branches. The spindle-shaped dry fruits have ten ribs and a single row of hairs. Their pappus is a persistent row of pale, scale-like structures. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
