## Restio implicatus {#s-f90f8b8e80}
## A fine wrinkling on slender stems

Restio implicatus is described as a tangled perennial to 60 cm, with slender stems bearing fine wrinkles and sheaths loosely wrapped around them. Its spikelets occur singly or in pairs, and the flowers are hairless. The regional account records damp slopes above 1,400 m.
