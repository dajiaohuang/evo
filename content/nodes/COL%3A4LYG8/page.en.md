## A strong-smelling annual with oily leaf glands {#s-8e7fbd9207}
This account describes a stout, strong-smelling annual herb 15–150 cm tall, with smooth, often bluish-green stems. Its leaves may be opposite or alternate and carry conspicuous oil glands along the margins. Branched clusters bear disk-shaped heads with greenish to purplish flowers. The fruits are dark, hairy and topped by many tawny bristles. The recorded range extends from the southwestern United States and West Indies to northern Argentina.
