## Syagrus harleyi: leaves and fruit {#s-126f5ee7ef}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes stems with remnants of leaf sheaths and lists solitary, clumping and subterranean forms. Leaves are green and pinnate, with persistent, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is erect or slightly arched. Pinnae are leathery, rigid and uniformly arranged at a single angle on the rachis. Their tips are described as pendulous and as acute, rounded or asymmetrically notched; the pinnae are green and slightly discolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate or erect, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved or pruinose; its rachis is glabrous, and the rachillae are arranged helically along it. Fruits are ovoid. The epicarp is described as greenish or yellow-green and tomentose; the mesocarp is fibrous and succulent, the endocarp woody and oval, and the endosperm homogeneous.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
