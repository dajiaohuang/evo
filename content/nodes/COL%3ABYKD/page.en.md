## Allophylus strictus {#s-479237e9f3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes three-leaflet leaves and a unilateral nectar disc, while listing alternative leaflet colours, margins and venation states; dried pericarp colour is blackish or greenish.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-80bb475293}
Leaves are trifoliolate, with lateral leaflets smaller than the central leaflet. The central leaflet is petiolulate or sessile, while lateral leaflets are sessile; the blades are symmetrical and chartaceous. The source lists leaflets as concolorous or discolorous and their margins as entire, serrate from the middle toward the apex, or serrate near the apex. The lower surface is glabrous to nearly glabrous. Venation is listed as brochidodromous or mixed; upper-surface secondary veins are prominent and curved, and domatia are present. The inflorescence is unbranched. The androgynophore is present; the nectar disc is four-lobed and unilateral. Sepal undersides are glabrous to nearly glabrous. The pericarp is recorded as blackish or greenish when dry.

## Habitat field {#s-70862199ca}
The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality, survey date, global range or sampling-completeness information.
