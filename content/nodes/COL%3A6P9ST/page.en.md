## White flower heads and rounded leaf bases {#s-db2996d2b3}
This record describes an upright, softly hairy stem without glands. Alternate leaves in two ranks have stalks, oblong blades and rounded bases; the upper surface is hairy and the lower finely pubescent, with glands on the leaves. Bell-shaped heads have overlapping bracts and 20–30 white florets. The pappus is white, and the habitat field says terrestrial.
