## The source concept {#s-906c745dbc}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Rhinella nattereri* in row Anf0856 under Rhinella, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.
