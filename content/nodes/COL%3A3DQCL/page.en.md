## Exostigma rivulare {#s-aa6f4e769b}
## Narrow leaves and a short pappus

The morphology field describes leaves ranging from narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate or obovate, with slightly attenuate bases. The corollas of both central and marginal florets are recorded as short. The fruit has a short pappus, the bristly crown attached to the fruit.
