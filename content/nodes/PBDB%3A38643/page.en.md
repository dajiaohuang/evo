## Mussaurus in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-b5579b8003}
Mussaurus is listed at genus rank in the PBDB parent hierarchy. This selected page set links one fossil-species entry, Mussaurus patagonicus. That is the scope of this guide, not a complete descendant list; a database parent path records classification, not an evolutionary tree.

## Mussaurus patagonicus occurrence records {#s-57fc6095dd}
The selected Mussaurus patagonicus pages contain two occurrence records linked to two collections; one named stratum is Laguna Colorada. Read each occurrence with its own age interval and identification notes. Occurrence and collection counts describe database records, not the number of specimens or animals.

## What selected Mussaurus records cannot establish {#s-76ddb18028}
These selected records do not give complete fossil endpoints for the genus or an ecological profile. Occurrence and collection counts do not count specimens, individuals or populations; pooled age bounds do not establish the genus’s origin or extinction. The PBDB parent path records classification, not phylogeny.

## Mussaurus in a three-age growth sample {#growth-series}
The ontogenetic study uses hatchling, yearling and adult specimens from a Patagonian Mussaurus sample. These materials define the study’s sampled growth series, not the genus’s complete fossil range or three successive evolutionary forms. Its stance reconstruction is a model and should be read as an interpretation of this sample.
