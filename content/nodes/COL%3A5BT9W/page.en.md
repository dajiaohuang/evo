## Wahlenbergia perrottetii {#s-b9d601284c}
## White bell-shaped flowers top a terminal panicle

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes a densely stemmed plant with alternate lance-shaped to elliptic leaves. Their bases taper, their margins range from entire to scalloped, and both surfaces are hairless. The terminal panicle bears radially symmetrical flowers with linear bracts, a cone-shaped flower base, and linear sepals. The white, bell-shaped corolla is entire; the stamens separate after flowering, and the stigma has two lobes. The fruit is a cone-shaped capsule with ovate seeds. The habitat field records it as terrestrial.
