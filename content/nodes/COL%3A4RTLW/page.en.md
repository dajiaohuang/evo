## What is distinctive about its leaf sheaths? {#s-b25e06e31b}
A regional account describes *Restio singularis* as a tangled, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 50 cm and spreading by stolons, horizontal stems. Its branched culms (stems) are densely covered with tubercles, or small bumps. The leaf sheaths wrap loosely and end in large, translucent shoulder-like margins.
