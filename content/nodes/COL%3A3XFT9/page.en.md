## Maerua brevipetiolata {#s-c284158233}
## Short leaf stalks and an annular flower disc distinguish the plant

The South African flora account describes a shrub up to 3 m tall, with short-stalked leaves divided into three hairless leaflets. The narrow leaflets are 1.2–3.2 cm long and 2–4 mm wide; the middle leaflet is longer than the side ones. A separate diagnostic describes it as a thin-stemmed scrambler, with pale-green leaves arranged in one plane and shorter, broader leaflets than its nearest ally, M. rosmarinoides.

The account describes a few-flowered terminal raceme. The flower’s cup-shaped receptacle holds a ring-shaped, fringed disc; petals are reduced. The ellipsoid-cylindrical fruit is 1.8–2.5 cm long, and the nearly spherical seeds are about 3 mm across. The habitat record is lowveld bush.
