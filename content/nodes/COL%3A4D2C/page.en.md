## Keep the two length measures distinct {#s-97fadc32ea}
Snout–vent length (SVL) ends at the vent; total length (TL) includes the tail. The Epictia albipuncta row reports maximum SVL of 144.5 mm and maximum TL of 355 mm, with both values attributed to Marinus Hoogmoed. They are separate compiled measures, not an average or a genus-wide range; the record does not say whether the two maxima came from the same individual.

## Limits of the combined guide {#s-513ea2658c}
This guide joins one authored four-field row and a separate limited set of 43 entries. Neither count is a census of accepted Epictia species. Reported maxima are compiled records, not individual averages or identification thresholds; TL and SVL measure different lengths. Check the source and aggregation rule for each field.
