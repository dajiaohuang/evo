## The deposited caecilian record {#s-c1da8d066e}
This page summarizes the CaecilianTraits version 4 record for Potomotyphlus kaupii, filed under Potomotyphlus in Typhlonectidae (species row 221). That is the source’s identification; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been checked. The compilation keeps individual, population and species entries distinct.

## Measurement material {#s-8733fdf4fc}
There are 34 measurement rows: 22 individual-level and 12 population-level, with no species-level measurement rows. These are record counts, not a population estimate or a count of unique animals. The rows retain specimen identifiers, sex codes and references. Check whether a body measure is fresh, preserved or summarized before comparing it with another.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-0b4f5105c5}
The compilation lists Freshwater (inland waters) as the broad habitat and Aquatic as the microhabitat. A cited account describes the species as predominantly, perhaps entirely, aquatic, living in rivers, streams, lakes and flooded plains. The account’s qualification matters; the categories and description do not quantify use or define a complete range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-d6890b1c86}
The source category is viviparous, meaning giving birth to live young. This classification alone does not describe parental care, breeding timing or variation among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-0fa543c6c4}
The species-level row gives 89.5 primary folds and 0.2 annuli interrupted by the vent. These fractional values are summaries, not literal counts on one animal, and do not establish fixed counts for every individual.
