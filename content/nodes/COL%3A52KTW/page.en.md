## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Stigmaphyllon bannisterioides? {#s-40a57515d5}
The branches are smooth. Leaf stalks range from 1.1 to 20 mm and have stalkless disk-shaped glands at the tip. The blades are always undivided, with sparse silky hairs below and entire, glandless margins. Solitary umbels carry 3–12 flowers. The petals are yellow throughout with ragged edges; the anthers and styles are hairless. Stamens opposite the rear lateral sepals have two developed pollen sacs, whose connective tissue remains separate. The front style is as long as the others; all style tips are reduced, and the stigmas point toward the flower center. The dorsal fruit wing is reduced to a ridge, and lateral wings are absent.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ad863aa9b1}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
