## Rolled leaf edges and bell-shaped heads {#s-fc6dbc959d}
The morphology record describes a swollen underground stem and leaves along the stem. The leathery leaves range from lance-shaped to inversely lance-shaped, with smooth upper surfaces and greyish undersides; their edges may be rolled under and may be entire or toothed. Flower heads may be solitary or grouped, with 4–6 rows of bracts and about 15–25 florets. The top-shaped fruits may be smooth or silky-haired. The habitat field says terrestrial.
