## Eight examined specimens support a relative shape comparison {#s-cec476687d}
The authors measured eight pleskei specimens and compared size-adjusted morphology with six laojunshanensis types. The first principal component accounted for 48.24% of variance in that particular paired analysis, with differentiation interpreted as narrower internarial distance, narrower upper eyelids and longer limbs in laojunshanensis. These relative differences do not supply a species-wide pleskei size range, body-growth relationship or measured swimming performance.

## Comparison characters are not interchangeable with live observations {#s-1fb1c0724a}
The account contrasts pleskei’s olive, yellowish or dark-green dorsal colour with the grey-brown new species, describes finger II longer than I and vomerine teeth absent or very weak. These are the authors’ taxonomic comparison statements, with additional comparative data taken from earlier descriptions. They do not establish one invariant hue, an independently examined skeleton or a larval diagnosis; absence of prominent teeth is not a feeding mechanism.
