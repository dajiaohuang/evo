## What distinguishes Croton longifolius in the record? {#s-dd88262daa}
Young branches and elliptic to lance-shaped leaves bear outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked leaves have looping side veins and entire margins; nectar glands are absent, while leafy stipules are present at young shoot tips. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are nearly sessile; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female flowers have fused sepals, unequal calyx lobes, a two-lobed style, and threadlike petals. The bud arrangement is valvate. The fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1697924cc5}
It is listed as terrestrial; no specific site is named.
