## ## Hirsute stems and taller pre-opening bracts {#s-568dda163a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records hirsute stems with spreading simple hairs and either absent or capitate glandular hairs. Unbranched thread-like setae are listed; branched setae, prickles and short shoots are absent. The stipules may be shed or persistent, extrafloral nectaries are absent, and projections between pinna pairs are absent. The inflorescences are globose, with floral bracts longer than the flowers before opening. The flowers have four perianth parts, a membranous calyx without lobes, and corolla lobes with one to three veins that may be pubescent or tomentose. The androecium is diplostemonous, with pink filaments. The habitat field says “terrestrial,” without a locality or finer habitat description.
