## A twice-divided leaf and small flower parts {#s-9d1c08549f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records twice-pinnate leaves with one to three pairs of pinnae and three to nine leaflets per pinna. The inflorescence follows a raceme pattern and has small, lance-shaped bracts about 3 mm long. One lateral petal is recorded as 12 to 13 mm long. The source does not supply units for its leaflet dimensions, so they are not converted or repeated here.
