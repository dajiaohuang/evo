## Rhesus macaques in distinct study populations {#s-6bd8c6701a}
COL26.8 accepts Macaca mulatta. The selected studies cover Chinese population genomics, one wild troop near Buxa, managed Cayo Santiago cohorts and an 11-km NH-7 road segment. They address different populations and questions, not one range-wide ecological profile.

## Seasonal diet in one troop {#s-642b3859c5}
At Buxa, fruit accounted for 70.8% of recorded diet in the study-defined May–September non-provisioning period and 28.8% in the October–April provisioning period for one 64-individual troop.

## One troop's home range {#s-1187ca95fe}
The selected Buxa troop home range included patches of natural forest, mixed-species plantations and residential settlements; this is one local study setting, not a full habitat account.

## Locomotion {#s-f6dd783e0a}
The selected studies do not assess locomotion.

## Body size in the cited samples {#s-327c45c299}
The Buxa troop study did not estimate body size. A separate Cayo Santiago study measured a defined cohort of adult males and female relatives.

## Ecological role {#s-07daa666c3}
The selected studies do not assign an ecological guild.

## Heritability in one Cayo Santiago pedigree {#s-9299875e0f}
In one 2015 Cayo Santiago pedigree sample, most measured male morphometric traits were heritable under the study’s pooled models; this cohort result is not species-wide.

## Evidence across five study settings {#s-ee6ebcb970}
The evidence includes 81 macaques at 17 Chinese sites, one 64-member Buxa troop, Cayo Santiago pedigree and mating-season cohorts, and 54 road deaths on an 11-km NH-7 segment in 2009–2010. The road count is local, not a population mortality estimate or current conservation assessment.

## Separate populations, measures and questions {#s-e66808fdcf}
Morphometrics and pedigree records come from managed Cayo Santiago macaques; feeding and travel were observed in a wild Buxa troop; Chinese samples were used for population genomics. Keeping those settings separate is necessary to interpret each result.

## One species, several study populations {#s-7902eeb75a}
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts Macaca mulatta. The studies here sampled wild macaques in China and India, as well as managed free-ranging groups at Cayo Santiago, Puerto Rico. These settings address different questions and do not combine into a range-wide ecological portrait.

## Five lineages in sampled Chinese populations {#s-ebd40d19b2}
A population-genomic study sampled 81 wild rhesus macaques at 17 sites across China, representing five subspecies. It identified five genetic lineages and evidence of recent gene flow. These results describe the sampled Chinese populations and the study's models, not a global species tree or complete range map.

## Seasonal diet and travel in one Buxa troop {#s-5d3d13584b}
Researchers followed one 64-member troop near Buxa from October 2013 to September 2014. In the study-defined May?September non-provisioning period, fruit was 70.8% of recorded diet and mean daily travel was 4.72 km; during October?April provisioning, the values were 28.8% and 2.58 km. The comparison does not isolate every seasonal influence.

## A Cayo Santiago pedigree sample {#s-23522bd1bf}
A 2015 study measured 125 adult males and 21 female relatives at Cayo Santiago. Under pooled pedigree models, several male traits, including body mass, crown?rump length and canine length, met the authors' heritability criterion; testicular volume did not and its estimate was imprecise. This managed cohort is not a morphology survey of the native range.

## Paternity sampled at one year {#s-c6f421a06c}
During the 2013 mating season, researchers followed 21 high-ranking males aged 7–21 across nine provisioned, semi-free-ranging groups. Older males were associated with fewer offspring recorded by the study. Paternity was sampled at one year, so infants that died earlier were not assigned; this is not lifetime reproductive success.

## Road deaths on one NH-7 segment {#s-b3f95a1bc6}
From August 2009 to July 2010, observers recorded 54 rhesus macaques killed along an 11-km National Highway 7 segment near Pench: 27 in summer, 19 in winter and eight during the monsoon. These are local road observations, not a population mortality rate or current threat assessment.

## Limits of the selected rhesus studies {#s-8a5e3f2a0f}
These sources do not establish a current global range or conservation category, a species-wide wild diet and life-history synthesis, or population-wide mortality. Each result remains tied to its sampled population, place, period and method.
