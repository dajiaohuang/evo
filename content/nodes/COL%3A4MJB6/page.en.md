## Accepted name and placement {#s-8a3cf562d7}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis calcaratus (Boulenger, 1908) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
