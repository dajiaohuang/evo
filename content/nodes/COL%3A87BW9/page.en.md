## The accepted classification record {#s-d094c4559c}
Pleurodira is an accepted suborder under Testudines in pinned COL26.8 (COL 87BW9; Cope, 1864). The catalogue record fixes its rank and parent placement for this release. This page treats that as a taxonomy reference, not as an independent review of the group's biology or present-day status.

## Reported carapace-length records {#s-d5887bda75}
The selected records report maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 155 mm for Pseudemydura umbrina and 1070 mm for Podocnemis expansa. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. These are records from different species, not a within-species range.
