## Keep each species and collection distinct {#s-8492a34fde}
The occurrence guide names *Magericyon anceps* and *M. castellanus* and links records to their collections, age brackets and original references. Compare those details record by record. A fossil occurrence or collection is not a count of individuals or a measure of ancient population size.
