## Temnadenia violacea {#s-cc991ef394}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Temnadenia violacea* as an accepted species of *Temnadenia*. The available Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record supplies only a habitat category: terrestrial. It gives no specimen locality or range description, so this label should not be read as a complete distribution.
