## Source classification and concept status {#s-1f31eaf767}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2687 records Scinax proboscideus under the source classification Scinax (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.
