## Trichogonia cinerea {#s-afb1d59b5d}
## Woolly leaves and a pappus-free fruit

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes branched stems with both stalked and sessile glandular hairs, ranging from softly hairy to woolly. Leaves are usually alternate, though lower leaves may be opposite; their short stalks are 0.5–1 cm long. The oblong to elliptic blades have rounded tips, scalloped margins and three main veins. The lower surface is woolly to finely hairy.

Loose corymb-like panicles carry stalked heads. Two rows of woolly bracts surround each head, with outer elliptic and inner linear bracts. Heads contain 60–100 florets. The dry fruits (cypselae) have a ring-shaped attachment area and short stalks; the pappus is absent. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, without giving a locality or complete range.
