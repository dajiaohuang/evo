## A jaw contact in Riograndia, absent in Brasilodon {#s-f4def207f5}
Micro-CT scans across 26 specimens found dentary–squamosal contact in Riograndia; the nine-specimen Brasilodon sample lacked it. This contact between two bones is not the full condyle–glenoid jaw joint seen in mammaliaforms, and it does not by itself make Riograndia a crown mammal.

The uneven distribution favors homoplasy—independent origins of a similar feature—over one smooth sequence of jaw-joint handoffs. The reported 225.42 ± 0.37 Ma is a maximum depositional age for the fossil-bearing assemblage; the broad 205.7 Ma display bound is a navigation limit, not a fossil last-appearance date.
