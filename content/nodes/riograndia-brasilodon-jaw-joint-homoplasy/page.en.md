## The mammalian jaw joint did not follow one ladder {#s-f4def207f5}
Micro-CT scans of 26 specimens found a dentary–squamosal contact in Riograndia but not Brasilodon. This distribution supports repeated, independent acquisition rather than a simple one-way ladder of jaw-joint transformation; the contact alone is not a complete jaw joint or proof of crown-mammal status.
