## Najas guadalupensis {#s-43215ee0a4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records an aquatic species with more than 25 inconspicuous, single-cell teeth along the leaf margins; teeth are absent from stem internodes and the underside of the midrib. The plant is listed as monoecious (with male and female flowers on one plant), with elliptic seeds and polygonal seed-coat cells wider than long. These are regional source fields, not a complete range account.
