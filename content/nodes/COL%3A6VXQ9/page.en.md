## Survey records {#s-ea3f8a9cb8}
Banks and colleagues revisited distribution and abundance because earlier baselines were more than a decade old. Line transects in 2003–2004 covered 359.68 km at Analabe and Ankavana in the Analamera region; the study estimated a mean density of 3.11 individuals per km² and about 915 animals overall. Those are the authors’ historical, survey-based estimates, not a current census.

## Read this historical record through food and foraging position {#s-bf016bbcdd}
Propithecus perrieri is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Indriidae. This page compares the same-name, same-family EltonTraits 1.0 record through 3 ecological field groups with source information. The source follows earlier bird or MSW3 mammal taxonomy; a name match does not resolve modern splits or synonym disputes.
