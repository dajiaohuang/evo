## *Spigelia humboldtiana* {#s-359409801f}
## Spikes with up to 35 flowers

The Flora of Panama excerpt describes an herb or low subshrub 1–6 dm tall, usually with fibrous roots. Slender stems may be simple or branched, hairless or sparsely hairy in strips beside grooves; the lower part often creeps. Near the shoot tip, the leaves may be whorled, imperfectly whorled, or opposite. Blades measure 2–10 cm by 0.8–4.5 cm, and their stalks range from absent to 15 mm. One, occasionally two, terminal spikes are usually simple, 3–13 cm long and bear 6–35 flowers; flowering branches sometimes extend above them.

The flowers are nearly stalkless or have stalks up to 1 mm. Calyx lobes are 2–4 mm long. The slender funnel-shaped corolla is white, often tinged pink: its tube is 9–19 mm long, 1 mm wide at the base and 3–7.5 mm at the throat; the lobes are 3–4 mm long. Anthers measure 1–1.5 mm and remain inside the corolla; the free filament portion is about 2 mm. The style tip reaches or just passes the anthers, and its upper half enlarges and later falls away.

The same account describes a smooth capsule 4–6 mm wide and 2.5–4 mm long. Seeds are brownish, egg-shaped and fleshy, about 1.5 mm long, often netted or tuberculate. These measurements summarize the Flora of Panama description; no additional range estimate is inferred here.
