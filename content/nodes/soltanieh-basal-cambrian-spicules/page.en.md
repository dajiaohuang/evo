## Soltanieh spicules and the basal Cambrian sponge record {#s-730b1619ec}
Which very early fossils can be identified as sponges with confidence? In the Soltanieh Formation’s Protohertzina anabarica Zone, a review describes siliceous spicules and reassesses twenty proposed Precambrian candidates, rejecting some as abiotic and others as too ambiguous. Under those diagnostic criteria and the record then available, these basal-Cambrian spicules are called the oldest reliable sponge remains; isolated spicules do not establish a crown hexactinellid relationship or rule out an older future find.
