## Kutorgina: soft tissues preserved with the shell valves {#s-d9309ee21c}
Kutorgina chengjiangensis shows that an early shell-bearing brachiopod preserved more than its valves. Chengjiang fossils retain paired calcareous shells with an early spirolophe-stage lophophore, oesophagus, stomach, intestine, terminal anus and pedicle. These are fossilized anatomical structures; comparisons about how the animal fed or what they imply about brachiopod ancestry are interpretations. The combination does not make Kutorgina a direct ancestor of living brachiopods.
