## Bulb, leaves, and flower structure {#s-4105da1133}
The account describes a globose-to-ovoid bulb 5–8 cm across, budding freely from its base and narrowing into an 8–12 cm neck covered densely with membranous tunics. Usually four leathery, sickle-shaped leaves accompany the flowers or appear during the flowering period; each is about 25 cm long and 2–3 cm wide, narrowing evenly to its tip.

The flowering stalk is 25–30 cm tall, recurved and somewhat flattened just below the umbel, so the umbel hangs. About four oblong- or linear-lanceolate spathe valves surround it; the largest reach 5 cm by 1.25 cm. Pedicels are about 1 cm long. Each umbel carries 6–10 pendulous red flowers. The perianth is about 6 cm long, widening evenly from its base to a throat about 1 cm across; its tube is about 4 cm, and its more-or-less obovate-oblong lobes about 2 cm by 1.25 cm. The outer three lobes are described as bearing a few-haired apiculus; the archive wording for its modifier is “indexed.”

Stamens are arranged in one row, or in two rows that are barely distinct, and are inserted at the perianth throat. Their filaments are exserted to about 1.25 cm; the bases form a sub-coronal ridge, sometimes with basal lobules. The ovary is oblong, becoming oblong-elliptic. The style is slightly shorter than the perianth and trifid.

Bulb globose to ovoid [as 'avoid'], 5-8 cm in diam., budding freely from the base, contracted into a neck 8-12 cm long, densely covered with membranous tunics. Leaves usually 4, contemporary with flowers or produced during flowering periods, falcate, evenly narrowed to the apex, about 25 cm long, 2-3 cm broad, leathery. Peduncle 25-30 cm tall, recurved and somewhat flattened just below the umbel, making the whole umbel pendulous. Spathe-valves about 4, oblong- or linear-lanceolate, the largest up to 5 cm long and 1.25 cm broad. Pedicels about 1 cm long. Flowers 6-10 in an umbel, pendulous; perianth about 6 cm long, red, evenly dilated from the base to a throat about 1 cm wide; tube 4 cm long; lobes more or less obovate-oblong, about 2 cm long and 1.25 cm broad, the outer three with an indexed apiculus with a few hairs. Stamens uniseriate or very indistinctly biseriate, inserted at the throat of the perianth-tube; filaments exserted, about 1.25 cm long, bases extended into a sub-coronal ridge, sometimes with basal lobules. Ovary oblong, becoming oblong-elliptic. Style slightly shorter than the perianth, trifid.
