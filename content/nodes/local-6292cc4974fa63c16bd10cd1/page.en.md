## The deposited caecilian record {#s-a2b5a1a63d}
This page summarizes the CaecilianTraits version 4 record for Microcaecilia taylori, filed under Microcaecilia in Siphonopidae (species row 201). That is the source’s identification; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been checked. The compilation keeps individual, population and species entries distinct.

## Measurement material {#s-862a4a0abf}
There are five measurement rows, all classified as population-level; the source lists no individual-level or species-level measurement rows. These records do not state the number of animals measured or the size of any population. Their specimen identifiers, sex codes and references remain row-specific; compare body dimensions only after checking whether the measures are fresh, preserved or summarized.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-270922512d}
The compilation assigns the broad habitat category Terrestrial and the microhabitat category Fossorial, meaning associated with burrowing. These categories do not quantify habitat use or define a complete geographic range.
