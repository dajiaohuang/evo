## Trithrinax brasiliensis {#s-fefad30297}
Trithrinax brasiliensis is recorded with a solitary stem and fan-shaped pale-green leaves. The leaf-sheath fibres bear deciduous spines, while the petiole is unarmed. Its interfoliar inflorescence branches through as many as four orders and carries yellow, bisexual flowers. The globose yellow fruits are 15–40 mm wide, with juicy mesocarp and a non-woody, one-seeded endocarp; habitat is listed as terrestrial.
