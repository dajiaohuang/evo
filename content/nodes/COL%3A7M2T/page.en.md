Gbp and Kbp mean billion and thousand base pairs. Contig N50 measures assembly continuity: at least half of the assembled bases lie in contigs this long or longer. These are assembly statistics from the study's NCBI snapshot dated 29 October 2024, not direct cellular DNA measurements or a fixed genome size for the genus. ## Comparative models and proposed mechanisms {#s-37950b31dd}
The authors compared 61 assemblies with phylogenetic models, multiple-testing correction and contig N50 as a covariate. RepeatMasker matches, occupied length and genome proportions are distinct measures. Sequence divergence from a repeat consensus is a proxy for the age or activity of a repeat copy, not a direct observation of current transposition. Similarity to a putatively full-length Ty3 open reading frame (ORF, a DNA sequence that could encode a protein) does not show that the protein is expressed. Developmental constraints are interpretations of across-species associations, not experimental evidence of a causal effect in each genus member. The paper gives conflicting statements about TE proportions, ORF totals and GC-content associations; GC content is the share of DNA bases that are guanine or cytosine.
