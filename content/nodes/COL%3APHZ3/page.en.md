## Calea lantanoides {#s-59cdc067a3}
## Velvety stems support yellow heads without ray florets

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes an erect plant with a simple taproot and velvety stems. The opposite ovate leaves are petiolate. Umbel-like clusters bear discoid flower heads with cylindrical involucres and one bract form; the receptacle carries folded, lance-shaped paleae.

Ray florets are absent; the disc florets are yellow. The dry fruits are velvety, and the pappus consists of separate, lance-shaped scales. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
