## Two fossil records, two different time bounds {#s-71472a9ed6}
The profile brings together a late Sandbian Macurdablastus record and a separately sourced Wuchiapingian last occurrence. They are observations from different studies, not the first and last points of a fully sampled, continuous history. The difference matters: a named fossil record can anchor a local time bound without fixing the class’s global origin or extinction moment.

A second source examines blastoid hydrospires, an internal anatomical feature. That morphology can be discussed without assigning one diet or habitat to the whole class. The selected fossils and structures provide bounded evidence; they do not recover a complete ecology or turn a Permian–Triassic boundary into the exact instant of blastoid extinction.
