## What leaf, flower and seed traits distinguish Passiflora recurva? {#s-c704d16773}
The herb has cylindrical stems and three-lobed, pubescent leaves with glandular, scalloped margins. Petiole glands are sessile, and stipules are linear. Solitary flowers have lance-shaped or oblong bracts and awned sepals. The floral cup is bell-shaped, with a two-row corona; the operculum is membranous at the tip and the limen is ring-shaped. The reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless, with oblong, alveolate seeds.
