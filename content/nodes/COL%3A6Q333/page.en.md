## Smooth narrow leaves and urn-shaped heads {#s-f2b64ef6c5}
The morphology record notes a swollen underground stem and moderately branched shoots. Narrow leaves range from linear to lance-shaped, are smooth on both sides and have entire, flat edges. Flower heads may be stalkless or grouped, with cylindrical to urn-shaped involucres containing about 7–25 florets. The top-shaped fruits are silky or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
