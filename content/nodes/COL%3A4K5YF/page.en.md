## Distribution records {#s-35fe87c4d1}
A review of Paraguayan specimens, literature and reliable field records placed more recent records in Alto Paraguay; occurrence in Boquerón and Presidente Hayes was more marginal and possibly seasonal. The authors associated the titi with the Pantanal, Cerrados del Chaco and wetter parts of the Dry Chaco, especially near watercourses; particularly wet years may allow records farther into the Dry Chaco.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-0dc67797f9}
The review distinguishes examined specimens, unexamined specimens, literature, photographs and reliable sightings; many museum specimens were collected decades ago. This summarizes evidence classes for Paraguay, not the species' complete transnational range or a current census.
