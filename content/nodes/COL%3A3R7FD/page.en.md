## Which leaf and flower traits are recorded for Kielmeyera neglecta? {#s-a7fb084c21}
The record describes upright growth and elliptic, oblong, or oval leaves with slightly to fully leathery texture. Their tips may be sharp or blunt, the base is wedge-shaped, and the margins may be slightly rolled under or flat. Leaf glands are recorded as present, spherical or elongated. The flowers have white, strongly asymmetric petals and unequal green sepals; their clusters contain more than three flowers. The upright anthers have conspicuous apical glands, and the ovary and style lack hairs.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-7dd264b1ca}
The regional dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and supplies no locality or collection date.
