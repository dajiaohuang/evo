## What does Manulea leiostachys look like? {#s-babc1ab09a}
The source describes a perennial herb up to 1.2 m tall, hairy near the base, with toothed leaves. Its flowers form loose, small clusters; the brownish corolla tube is swollen toward the top.

## How is it distinguished from Manulea rubra? {#s-c8f34182fa}
The source says M. leiostachys is closely allied to M. rubra and easily confused with it. Their main distinction is the throat: M. rubra has clear bands of club-shaped hairs, while M. leiostachys is nearly hairless there. Stem and leaf hairs also differ in shape and abundance. The account suggests the species may be separated by altitude, but explicitly says that needs confirmation.

## What terrain is recorded? {#s-365e6acdc0}
The habitat field places it on rocky mountain slopes. It gives no more specific locality.
