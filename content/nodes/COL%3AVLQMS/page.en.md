## Zygia inaequalis {#s-9ff2c0a174}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zygia inaequalis* as an accepted species of *Zygia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records bipinnate leaves with one pair of pinnae and 5 or 7 leaflets on each pinna. Its flowers are sessile, with a funnel-shaped corolla and an exserted staminal tube; the flat legume has hairy valves. The habitat field says terrestrial. This is a regional source description, not a complete survey of the species.
