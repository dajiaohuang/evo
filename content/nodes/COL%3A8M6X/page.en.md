COL26.8 records Abronia gaiophantasma as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Anguidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-4d7e5cd3a7}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
