## The deposited caecilian record {#s-9b907c7a43}
In CaecilianTraits version 4, Caecilia orientalis is listed in the genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. The compilation keeps individual, population and species records separate. It has not yet been established whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept.

## Measurement material {#s-1731fd3c76}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One database row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-e671c6f91b}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and “Freshwater (inland waters),” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are recorded categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-772c484473}
The deposited reproduction field classifies the species as oviparous, meaning that it lays eggs. This category does not tell us about parental care, when reproduction occurs or how it varies among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-fc183bf7f5}
At species level, the compilation gives a primary-fold count of 121. This value may summarize records rather than describe one animal, so it does not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic count.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-fcaf187c80}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as present. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean that adults always live in water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
