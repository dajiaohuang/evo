## What is recorded for Licania coriacea? {#s-3e83e86853}
The elliptic to oblong-ovate leaves have a lower surface described as waxy-powdery or scurfy, and their stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in racemes or panicles; the receptacle is urn-shaped and the flowers are sessile. Each flower has four or five separate stamens that remain inside the floral tube. The outer fruit wall is hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f7bb93703e}
The dataset records it as terrestrial and gives no named site.
