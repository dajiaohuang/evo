## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-92cbab9052}
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Ololygon tripui as follows: A series of eight to 12 short unpulsed notes and a long non-harmonic note with 30–44 fused pulses, emitted alone or together. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.
