## Species-group placement remained unresolved {#s-f0e85d959e}
The study explicitly left Raorchestes echinatus outside its defined species groups because phylogenetic relationships were unresolved and clearly shared diagnostic characters were lacking. Its spinular dorsum, mid-dorsal lines and mottled belly prompted a suggested affinity to the bombayensis group, but the authors did not adopt that placement. Acoustic characters were not studied. Missing calls and superficial resemblance cannot be replaced with relatives’ durations, frequencies or a certified group assignment. This is an unresolved grouping within the accepted genus, not evidence that the species itself is invalid. The authors identified further acoustic, ecological, reproductive and behavioural evidence as potential ways to resolve the uncertainty.
