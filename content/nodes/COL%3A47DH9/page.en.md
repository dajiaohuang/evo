## *Nidorella hottentotica*: a woolly annual with yellow flowers {#s-08662e2ce1}
The regional account describes an annual herb up to 1 m tall, with woolly leaves that range from inversely egg-shaped to narrow and inversely egg-shaped. The leaves may become less hairy with age, but woolly hairs remain at the nodes or in leaf axils. Its yellow flower heads have short outer ray florets, and a plentiful pappus, the bristly crown on the fruit. The account records grassland or woodland, often among rocks on hillsides, and notes that the plant can spread into disturbed places.
