## Campylocentrum itatiaiae {#s-57f8ca1696}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology entry describes smooth cylindrical roots, a developed stem and two-ranked leaves that are oblong to linear and bilobed at the tip. The lip has three lobes, with a triangular middle lobe; the spur is obovoid and straight or slightly curved. Its fruit is spindle-shaped and smooth.
