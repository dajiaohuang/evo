## Piptocarpha macropoda {#s-ef70fddbc6}
## Axillary clusters of cream heads below stellate-hairy leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes cylindrical branches with star-shaped hairs. Alternate, leathery leaves range from elliptic and ovate to lance-shaped, with acute or long-pointed tips and wedge-shaped or blunt bases. Their edges are entire; the underside is covered in star-shaped woolly hairs.

Axillary clusters contain 3–15 sessile heads with top-shaped involucres. Each head has 12–20 cream florets. The angular dry fruits have ribs and glands; their outer pappus ranges from threadlike bristles to scale-like structures. No habitat category is supplied in the cited fields.
