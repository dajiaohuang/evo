## Why primate crown ages shift between clock models {#s-5dabe73aa8}
A phylogenomic analysis of 372 primate species combined a dated tree with a relaxed molecular clock. Under its autocorrelated-clock model and calibration strategy A, the estimated interval for crown Primates is 79.2–70.0 million years ago. “Crown” here means the last common ancestor of living primates and all its descendants.

A different calibration strategy in the same study gives 71.4–63.9 Ma. These are posterior estimates, not fossil occurrences: clock choice, fossil priors and taxon sampling affect deep-node ages. The interval should therefore be read as a result of a specified model, not a date directly measured in a fossil.
