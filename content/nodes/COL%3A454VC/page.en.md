## An older record of feeding and activity {#s-82d9fe058d}
COL26.8 accepts Myomyscus angolensis in the family Muridae. EltonTraits 1.0 has an older same-name, same-family entry with fields for diet, foraging position and activity. Its taxonomy follows an earlier scheme; the name match does not settle later splits or synonym disputes.

## Weights assigned to food descriptions {#s-998f1df126}
EltonTraits assigns 40% to invertebrates, 30% to seeds, nuts and spores, and 30% to other plant material; the last source category also includes fungi. These weights standardize written descriptions; they are not measured shares of food intake. The ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and an estimate may use a related species in the same genus judged ecologically similar. The weights therefore are not all direct observations of Myomyscus angolensis. A zero weight would not prove that a food is never eaten.

## A broad foraging-position category {#s-717020e703}
The entry assigns the species to G, the ground category; the source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Archive metadata attributes mammalian stratum labels collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a broad category, not a measure of time spent there, and it does not mean the animal uses no other position.

## An activity label, not a daily schedule {#s-b5664a4894}
EltonTraits marks nocturnal activity as 1. Several activity fields can be 1 together, so this code is not a share of a 24-hour budget or a schedule guaranteed across days, seasons and sites. Its ABC confidence code can include a related species in the same genus; no genus- or family-level typical value is used to fill this entry.

## Following the EltonTraits source labels {#s-5758e6515d}
The fields carry the labels Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, position and activity can come from different material, and a Ref number in the bird table does not necessarily identify the same work as that number in the mammal table. Original labels are retained by file, row and field group. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so these labels are trace information, not additional observations.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-1977a702c4}
This is a guide to one historical EltonTraits entry, not a complete species account. Its food weights summarize literature descriptions, and ABC diet or activity codes can include material from a related species in the same genus. The fields are not all direct observations of Myomyscus angolensis, and the underlying books and papers have not been individually read. The historical name match does not establish equivalence with COL26.8. Current range, population, ecology and conservation need other sources.
