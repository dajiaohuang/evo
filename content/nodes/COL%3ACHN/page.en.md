## What the evidence supports {#s-b8263685f0}
Articulated faunas, isolated stereom ossicles and Yanjiahella answer different questions. None alone fixes the phylum’s origin, a global centre of origin or one ancestral ecology.

## Do not merge three different fossil signals {#s-920fbb055b}
Isolated stereom ossicles, tiny pieces of diagnostic echinoderm skeletal microstructure, occur earlier than articulated bodies. By about 510 Ma, articulated faunas include at least four differentiated body plans. The Fortunian fossil Yanjiahella has been proposed as a stem echinoderm, but it lacks unambiguous echinoderm synapomorphies and stereom, so its placement remains contested.

## An early record is not an origin date {#s-2618ffc4aa}
The robust endpoint used here is the articulated fauna, not the older microscopic skeletal pieces or the contested Yanjiahella assignment. Highlighted articulated records come from Gondwanan regions, but they do not locate a global origin or map the full Cambrian distribution. Echinoderms also span different body plans and ways of life, so one diet or mode of movement is not assigned to the phylum’s ancestor.
