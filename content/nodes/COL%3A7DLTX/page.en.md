## Accepted name and classification {#s-7b9b9672fc}
COL26.8, released 20 August 2026, accepts Uperoleia russelli (Loveridge, 1933) as a species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Myobatrachidae (family) → Uperoleia (genus).
