## Sample behind the measurements {#s-85a8ff989b}
COL26.8 accepts Pogoniulus chrysoconus in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 uses its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and reports 11 measured birds, six with a complete morphological set. A particular trait mean can use fewer than the full sample.

## Beak dimensions {#s-670f467b70}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 8.6 mm; width and depth there average 5.2 and 5.1 mm. They describe separate axes measured at standard landmarks, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-b80ba0d1d4}
Unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, averages 57.6 mm; tarsus length averages 14.2 mm. A separate species-level compilation gives mean mass as 10.8 g (source category Dunning); these records do not show that the measured birds were weighed.

## Woodland and fruit-feeding categories {#s-e6c00fa2eb}
AVONET assigns the habitat class Woodland, trophic niche Frugivore and lifestyle Insessorial (primarily perched above ground). These labels summarize dominant tendencies and should not be read as exclusive habitat use or diet.

## Movement and map limits {#s-60318efbed}
The migration field calls the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from 28.15° south to 18.24° north. They do not give a complete non-breeding itinerary or current occupancy, and the map is separate from the morphology compilation.

## Limits of the historical profile {#s-0366310d38}
AVONET values are species means from historical samples, not diagnostic limits or individual ranges. Mass comes from a separate source and does not inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad classes; the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges. A name crosswalk links this source record to the current catalogue entry but does not establish that taxonomic concepts remained identical through splits or lumps. Reproduction, present-day range, populations and conservation require other sources.
