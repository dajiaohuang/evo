## Climate and mammal records across the Eocene–Oligocene transition {#s-41e492488d}
Near the beginning of the Oligocene, Pacific marine oxygen-isotope records show ice growth in steps. In the Hampshire Basin, the mammal record instead captures regional turnover, a depositional gap of about 350,000 years and the later appearance of Asian immigrants. Together they show climate and faunal change, but not a single simple chain of cause: immigration, climate and habitat shifts differed by region, and one ecological group did not simply replace another.
