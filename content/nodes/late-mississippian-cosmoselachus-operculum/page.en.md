## A CT view of the Cosmoselachus gill cover {#s-d245deaaf1}
X-ray CT reconstruction of the partial holotype AMNH FF 20509 from the Late Mississippian Fayetteville Shale reveals long rays from the hyoid and branchial arches. The rays fuse into a corrugated operculum covering at least two posterior gill arches. The specimen is damaged and incomplete, so it does not preserve a full body or directly show locomotion, population variation or the complete function of the structure.
