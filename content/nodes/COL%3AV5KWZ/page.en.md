## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-d4b7f48674}

Its recorded placement is Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). ## Baishanzu type-locality records in the monograph {#s-30837af2d2}
The 2023 monograph listed the male holotype (the name-bearing specimen designated in the original description) CIB QY20200726001 from Baishanzu National Park, Qingyuan, Zhejiang, at about 1537 m. Citing the 2020 original description, it summarized the species as known only from the type locality at 1400–1600 m. This is a dated, cited locality account, not a new survey establishing absence elsewhere or physiological elevation limits.

## Male measurements and selected diagnostic features {#s-ef93982077}
The diagnosis, explicitly based on the original description, gave 28.4–32.4 mm snout–vent length (measured from the tip of the snout to the vent) for six adult males. It described a visible tympanum (the visible membrane of the ear), an unnotched tongue, vomerine ridges (ridges on bones in the roof of the mouth) without teeth, rough dorsal skin with an interrupted X-shaped ridge, and unwebbed toes with narrow lateral fringes. These are selected characters of the cited diagnostic material, not a female size range, universal population limits or a sufficient stand-alone identification key.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-7052bb15ad}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys baishanzuensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. That statement belongs to the paper’s comparison; it is not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. This single comparison does not show how often the character occurs across the species or provide a complete diagnosis, diet or developmental account.
