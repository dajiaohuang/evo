## Four historical life-history accounts {#s-e645ab83e3}
The family route links four historical accounts: Callimico goeldii, Callithrix flaviceps, Callithrix jacchus, and Leontopithecus rosalia. In the 2003 compilation, three accounts report gestation, female age at first reproduction, and maximum lifespan. This is a selected set under the pinned COL26.8 family route, not a complete account of Callitrichidae.

## Reproduction and longevity measure different things {#s-5604321bf9}
Gestation describes pregnancy duration; female age at first reproduction is neither age at sexual maturity nor age at first birth. Maximum lifespan is the longest record in the compilation, not a typical lifespan. The traits may come from different source conditions, so compare each entry with its own notes. A missing value is an unfilled field, not evidence that the trait is absent.

## Limits of the historical comparison {#s-41e918a5ae}
The four accounts summarize historical entries linked by family and a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 name table; that association does not establish equivalence with current species concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, fields generally average literature values with equal weight, regardless of sample size. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints when data are sparse, and underlying studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted here, and missing fields are not filled from relatives.
