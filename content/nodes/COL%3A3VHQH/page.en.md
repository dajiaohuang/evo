## Accepted name and classification {#s-da934bb685}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Lithobates neovolcanicus* (Hillis & Frost, 1985) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Lithobates (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## A source-listed native regional occurrence {#s-c95fccc8f0}
The 2025 checklist lists Rana neovolcanica as native in the Sierra Madre Occidental biogeographic province of western Mexico. The authors compile and check state lists, museum-based information, VertNet and GBIF rather than newly surveying every species. Their regional scope crosses parts of Sonora, Chihuahua, Sinaloa, Durango, Zacatecas, Nayarit and Jalisco; it does not imply occurrence throughout all seven states or every habitat in the mountains. The record is attributed to the source taxonomy, not an independently examined voucher, density estimate or complete current range.
