## Scales and feeding {#s-2457fbc942}
Adult moths and butterflies usually have scales covering their bodies and wings. Many drink liquids through a coiled proboscis, while some groups retain chewing mouthparts or have reduced mouthparts.

## From caterpillar to adult {#s-27a1941f24}
Caterpillars commonly chew plant tissue before pupating and emerging as adults. Activity time does not cleanly separate butterflies from moths: some moths are active during the day.

## Variation across the order {#s-6b7bd650bd}
These are broad patterns across Lepidoptera; individual species differ. The traits described here do not provide a complete range, quantitative measurements, evolutionary tree or conservation assessment for every species.
