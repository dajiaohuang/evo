COL26.8 accepts *Zosterops ficedulinus* in Zosteropidae. The EltonTraits 1.0 table has a same-name, same-family record with two ecological field groups. Its bird taxonomy is historical; matching names do not resolve later splits or synonym disputes.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-69c1a2b415}
The nonzero food weights are invertebrates 50% and fruit 50%. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; a zero does not prove that an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and do not imply exclusive use of nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-509c60cf6d}
The species-level foraging-stratum record assigns 60% to the canopy or just above it and 40% to trees or tall bushes from 2 m up to the canopy. These weights estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or time budgets for the whole day. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevation bands and cannot provide flight heights or habitat area.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-f46221e246}
EltonTraits 1.0 provides two historical ecological field groups, not a complete species account. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical species concepts and COL26.8. Food and foraging-stratum percentages are relative weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. The cited books and papers have not been independently re-evaluated here. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation need other sources.
