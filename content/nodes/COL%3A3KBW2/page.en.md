## Fleshy leaves in moist sandstone crevices {#s-4c89461206}
The description records a stiff shrublet with ridged young branches and fleshy, linear-oblanceolate leaves bearing stipules. The flowers range from white to purple, and the sepals are not pouch-like. Lance-shaped fruits are 15–30 mm long and contain 4–8 ovules. It grows in moist sandstone crevices.
