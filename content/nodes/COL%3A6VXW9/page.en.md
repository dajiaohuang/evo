## A named character in the 2021 comparison {#s-e0d8ac0929}
The 2021 comparison describes two well-delimited rows of forearm tubercles in Proceratophrys bagnoi. It contrasts them with one row of unequal-sized tubercles and sparse bordering tubercles in korekore. This is the authors' morphological comparison, not a new examination of every population; one character does not replace the full diagnostic combination.

## Eyelid characters in the published comparison {#s-7dccff5587}
The 2021 description characterizes the eyelid tubercles of Proceratophrys bagnoi as conical and pointed; it contrasts them with the small, pointed, fused warts described for korekore. These are the authors' comparative observations, not a diagnosis established for every age or an independent re-examination of the original type specimens.
