## Scale-like hairs and a broad anther pore {#s-f7793eb01c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes scale-like hairs on young stems and dense scale-like hairs below the leaf. Leaves may be elliptic, oblong or lance-shaped, with pointed tips and narrowed or wedge-shaped bases. Three main veins are listed, with two faint veins just inside the edge. Flowers are arranged along one side of the cluster axis. The bell-shaped flower cup bears a persistent calyx with distinct or flat-ended lobes; each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the pollen sacs is prolonged, and its appendage is described as two lower-side lobes or one back-facing tooth. Anther colour is unspecified; each has one broad pore, and the ovary bears hairs. These are the recorded alternatives, not a quantified description of variation.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-de62907392}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It provides no locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This is one habitat category rather than a full account of where the species lives.
