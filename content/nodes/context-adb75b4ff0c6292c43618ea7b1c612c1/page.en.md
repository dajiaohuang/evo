## Source classification and concept status {#s-ee10f04354}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2980 records *Phrynomedusa marginata* under the source classification Phrynomedusa (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-ff759bc2db}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm) {#s-44bf7b2c38}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-87d2a28a37}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Annual reproductive output {#s-72528c196e}
The row lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO gives no clutch-size or offspring-or-egg-size values for this record; these fields are missing, not zero. The row does not give the population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind the annual value.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-5211402721}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
