## What does the record say about leaves and flowers? {#s-8b3b8c1b9f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records no xylopodium, a woody underground storage organ. Leaf margins are recorded as toothed or entire, and the membranous blades are flat and lack petioles. The mature flower heads are hemispherical and stalked; the stylopodium and flattened appendages on the calyx lobes are absent. The entry reports a source description, without establishing how these traits vary across populations.

## How is its habitat categorized? {#s-5ed257f65d}
The archived Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field calls the species terrestrial. No collection locality or complete range is supplied with that category.
