## ## Umbels and finely hairy fruit {#s-ab41bd447a}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with lenticels and fine, short hairs. The deciduous stipules are lanceolate; the petiole and main leaf axis are cylindrical. Each leaf has two or three pairs of leaflets and stalkless, saucer-shaped nectaries. Umbels—flower stalks radiating from one point—bear flowers on individual stalks, with bell-shaped calyces. The flat fruit may also be constricted or curved; it has leathery valves and fine, short hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
