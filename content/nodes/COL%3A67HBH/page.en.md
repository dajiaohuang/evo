## Anthurium erskinei: morphology and rocky habitat {#s-eeee571a93}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records the stem as erect or creeping, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and a fibrous base. Petioles are marked smooth, though their compound shape description is difficult to parse. Leaf blades are entire and flat, elliptic, ovate or obovate, with rounded tips and bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is coded as obtuse above and acute below, secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as less than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, ovate spathe. The angle of the spathe to the peduncle is uncharacterized; the entry gives conflicting states for its stipe (present and shorter than the spadix, or absent). The berry is described with a green apex and white base.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Ambiguous compound states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
