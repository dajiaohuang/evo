## A low plant with small purplish-pink flowers {#s-9d7f090c75}
The Latin description records a smooth, suberect plant about 12 cm tall, possibly climbing among shrubs. Older branches become woody and rigid, while younger stems are slender. The nearly linear leaves may reach 2–3.5 cm long and are up to 7 mm wide. Only a few small daytime flowers were observed; their petals are purplish pink. The habitat record includes succulent karoo and thicket, with rocky mountain slopes among the named settings.
