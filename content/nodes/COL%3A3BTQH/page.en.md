## A rock-dwelling source category {#s-1921c7cba5}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Eucladium verticillatum (With.) Bruch & Schimp. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. This page uses only its habitat category.

The category is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The field gives no locality, survey date, range boundary or sampling coverage.

This source-labelled habitat category is the only biological content included here; it is not a complete account of the species.
