## The source concept {#s-654be23b4a}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Sphaenorhynchus platycephalus as row Anf2728 under genus Sphaenorhynchus, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-88a2f964be}
The row lists terrestrial, aquatic, and arboreal foraging. These categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. Its row-level comparison is unavailable here, so review these historical categories before reuse; no replacement is inferred, and the revision does not show this entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2279c276ba}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 33 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Developmental strategy {#s-81fe4d8cfb}
The record indicates a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or reproductive timing.
