## The Cajas Massif type locality {#s-0445a2afb7}
The description places the type locality in páramo grassland and shrubs in the Cajas Massif at 3,450–3,500 m. Specimens were mainly collected in Puya hamata bromeliads and Neurolepis villosa grasses near small streams. These are encounter substrates; they do not show that eggs were deposited in bromeliads or streams, nor establish physiological elevation limits.

## When calls and activity were observed {#s-28f1bdcb46}
Vocalizations were heard but not recorded from 08:00–11:00 and 17:00–19:00. Active individuals were seen from dusk until about 21:00, after which activity declined. These are observation windows, not a verified acoustic description or proof that the species is inactive outside the sampled hours.

## Survey non-detections and the dated proposal {#s-2a2f0427fc}
Surveys at 26 suitable Cajas Massif localities in 2014–2017 produced no additional records. The authors used those non-detections to suggest a very restricted distribution and propose Critically Endangered status. The proposal is tied to that study, not a verified current IUCN assessment; the surveyed non-detections also do not prove absence from every unsurveyed site.
