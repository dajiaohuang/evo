## A middle-ear connection that still ran to the lower jaw {#s-a817daf60c}
The near-complete Liaoconodon hui holotype IVPP V16051 preserves an unusual connection between the middle ear and lower jaw. Its ectotympanic and malleus bones are no longer in direct contact with the dentary, yet ossified Meckel's cartilage still links them. This is an observation from one articulated fossil; a proposed stabilizing role for the connection is a functional hypothesis. It does not make Liaoconodon a universal step in mammal evolution or a direct ancestor of living mammals.
