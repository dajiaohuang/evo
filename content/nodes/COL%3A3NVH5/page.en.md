## Hypodiscus rigidus {#s-5111881742}
## Elongate nuts with a seed appendage

The regional flora describes Hypodiscus rigidus as a neatly tufted, dioecious perennial (female and male flowers grow on separate plants) reaching 60 cm, with unbranched stems that have a round cross-section. Its nuts are elongate, 3–5 mm long, concave at the base, and bear an elaiosome (an appendage on the nut). Coastal limestone is listed as habitat.
