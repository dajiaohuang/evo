## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9f3399562c}
COL26.8 records Limnophis bicolor as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-57252aa46f}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-0c782a596f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
