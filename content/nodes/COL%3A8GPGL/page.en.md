## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ce6397d1a1}
COL26.8 records Cnemaspis butewai as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-522d52510a}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
