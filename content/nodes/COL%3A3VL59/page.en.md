## Cloudy green windows on a split leaf pair {#s-fcdaf25fb5}
This dwarf succulent has a double wedge-shaped body and a widely gaping, often uneven leaf pair. The smooth upper surfaces may be flat, convex or humped. Its windows are cloudy and uniformly milky green, rarely reddish. Flowers are yellow with a white centre, usually 25–30 mm across; the stigmas are sometimes saffron red. Quartzite gravel plains are the recorded habitat.
