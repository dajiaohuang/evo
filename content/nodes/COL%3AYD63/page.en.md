## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-061d5aa38f}
COL26.8 records Cordylus machadoi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Cordylidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e3a4e81353}

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a3d09b1ea9}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d8b79651d0}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-fce625b10a}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
