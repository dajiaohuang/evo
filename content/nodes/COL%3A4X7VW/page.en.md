## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-3205d3689b}
The taproot supports erect, hairy stems with simple and star-shaped hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned, linear leaves have symmetrical blades, tapered bases, pointed tips and toothed margins; the upper surface is hairless. Flower clusters are crowded in the axils and at stem tips. Sepals are pointed and the corolla is yellow. The dry fruit splits into more than ten segments (mericarps); each may have an awn no longer than 1 mm and bears simple hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-2fe13b9571}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
