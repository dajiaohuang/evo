## The source concept {#s-cd2a1ef835}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Diasporus diastema* as row Anf1802, in the genus Diasporus, family Eleutherodactylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-21e56aac2a}
The row records arboreal (Arb = 1) as a broad foraging category. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Adult food categories {#s-de8bdd1ca6}
The row records arthropods (Arthro = 1) as an adult food category. AmphiBIO compiles these from literature reports based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; listed categories are not a complete diet or measured food proportions.

## Activity periods {#s-d3c83dad6f}
The row records daytime (Diu = 1) and night (Noc = 1) as activity periods. These are broad categories, not a continuous record of behaviour through the day.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-c2a8b89b1f}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 25 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-bda5519210}
The row records 16 as the minimum and 18 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch. The row records minimum and maximum offspring or egg sizes of 4.3 and 4.3 mm. The maximum reproductive output listed is 1 event per year. These are literature-compiled extrema or counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-c2e04c11ea}
The row records direct development (Dir = 1) as a developmental category. These source fields do not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
