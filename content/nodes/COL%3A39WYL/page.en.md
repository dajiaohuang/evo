## A towering plant with banana-like leaves {#s-adf430c740}
One flora description calls this a large, fleshy plant reaching 12 m, with a crown of banana-like leaves up to 2–5 m long and about 1 m wide; their midribs are rose-pink. Its hanging flower clusters carry large, saucer-shaped maroon bracts, and the flowers are maroon. The same record lists forests, stream margins and wooded ravines as habitats.
