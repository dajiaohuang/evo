## Red flowers on a reclining stem {#s-b10cf3964d}
The morphology record describes reclining stems and alternate, thin leaves ranging from oval to lance-shaped. The leaf bases are heart-shaped, and the edges are toothed or finely toothed. Flowers are solitary, with an obconic floral base and red corollas; the lobes may be red or yellow. The habitat field says terrestrial.
