## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}

Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Dendrocitta cinerascens Sharpe, 1879 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## A field study in context {#meratus-study}

In May 2017, Shakya and colleagues surveyed Mt. Besar in the Meratus Mountains and collected birds for a preliminary phylogeographic study. Their sample table lists two Dendrocitta cinerascens entries from Meratus (LSUMNS B-79092, sequence MG546367, and B-79129, MG546368) among comparisons using the mitochondrial ND2 gene. This is a documented regional sample set, not a complete account of the species or a genome-wide test.
