## Two habitat labels in a regional record {#s-1d9a8039d3}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens yucatanensis Steere as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat labels are source categories rather than localities.

One label is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The other is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The record does not provide collection sites, survey dates or a complete range.

Neither category is tied to a collection locality, survey date or sampling-coverage estimate.

These two source-labelled categories are the only biological content included from this regional record; they do not describe the species in full.
