## Syagrus vermicularis: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-9896fa8a21}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes a smooth, solitary or aerial stem. Leaves are green and pinnate, with deciduous, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is arched. Pinnae are leathery, grouped and inserted at different angles; the source describes them as rigid or flexible, with pendulous, asymmetrically acuminate tips. They are dark green and concolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate or pendulous, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved, glabrous or pruinose; its rachis is glabrous. The rachillae are arranged helically, with worm-like tips bearing no flowers. Fruits are ovoid, with an orange, glabrous epicarp, fibrous and succulent mesocarp, woody and oval endocarp, and homogeneous endosperm.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
