## What range of leaf and flower traits is recorded for Banisteriopsis byssacea? {#s-7737e8242b}
Leaves are opposite or in whorls of three, with elliptic blades and shield-shaped glands along the side veins. The upper surface may be woolly or become nearly hairless; the lower is densely silky-haired. Terminal inflorescences may be clusters or racemes of four-flowered umbels. Bracts persist, and the stalkless flower pedicels are woolly. The calyx bears an oil gland and the petals are white. Anther chambers have sparse hairs; the styles are equal. The fruit lacks lateral wings, and its seed-bearing center is rough.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c29cf30e7e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
