## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Sauvagesia imthurniana? {#s-2f7a94023c}
The woody stem is simple from the base. Leaves are crowded at shoot tips, 5–10 mm long, lance-shaped or obovate, with scalloped margins and stalkless, pointed glands; secondary veins are conspicuous. Terminal flowers may form a cyme or be solitary, with hanging pedicels. The star-shaped corolla has open, non-overlapping petals that are white or pink. Outer staminodes are absent; inner petaloid staminodes are separate and pink with white bases. The single anther is pointed.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-e2ffe3c9e4}
The source classifies it as rupicolous, meaning associated with rocky substrates; it gives no locality.
