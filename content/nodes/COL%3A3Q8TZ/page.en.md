## Isoetes spannagelii {#s-f6a666ea50}
## Reticulate megaspores and filiform leaves

The record describes dichotomously divided roots and a two-lobed globose stem. The leaves are filiform, upright or ascending, and winged only at the base; their cross-section near the tip is circular or semicircular. A partial velum covers the basal sporangia, whose recorded form is ovate and colour hyaline. The megaspores are trilete and reticulate on both surfaces; the microspores are monolete, with a smooth distal perispore.
