## A model-based conservation ranking {#s-0fd5f070ae}
In Table 4 of a 2024 assessment, *E. insularis* has a reported conservation-priority index of 1.02 and falls in the “low” category. The authors assessed 58 selected Chilean amphibians using literature, citizen-science records and government plans; this was not a new census of every population. The index combines distribution, threat, population-trend, taxonomic-uniqueness and body-size scores with a protected-area term. It is a management ranking under that model, not an extinction probability, current IUCN status or population count. “Low” does not mean safe or without conservation need.
