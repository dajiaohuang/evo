## Symplocos trachycarpos Brand {#s-d6e6270946}
## Variable leaf hairs and flower-sex inconsistency

Symplocos trachycarpos is recorded as dioecious, although the flower field calls its flowers bisexual; the source does not resolve this inconsistency. Its papery leaves are elliptic to obovate, with margins that may be flat, crenulate near the tip or serrated. The upper surface ranges from smooth to woolly, while the underside is woolly or shaggy. Flowers occur in fascicles on exposed stalks beneath persistent triangular bracts. The ellipsoid to obovoid drupe retains a calyx that may end level with the floral disk or extend beyond it.
