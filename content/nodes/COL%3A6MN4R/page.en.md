## Accepted name and classification {#s-29c7de0bf6}
The COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026 lists *Hylarana grisea* (Van Kampen, 1913) as an accepted species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Hylarana (genus). This is the treatment in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database does not by itself establish identity.

## Reading the source records {#s-52aea02eda}
COL26.8 identifies the accepted taxon. Research entries below retain the names, samples and methods used by each source, so each finding stays within its stated scope.

## Adult length in the AmphiBIO compilation {#s-001f5e5bfb}
In AmphiBIO v1’s Frost (2011) classification, this entry appears under *Hylarana grisea*. That historical concept has not been reconciled with the current COL26.8 taxon. AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 91.5 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
