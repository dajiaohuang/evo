## A diagnosis from one adult female {#s-84cefe6645}
The description was based on a single adult female with a snout–vent length of 64.9 mm. It described a grey-green patterned dorsum and yellow ventral surfaces with dark markings. One specimen does not establish male appearance, population-wide size or colour variation.

## One high-montane night observation {#s-3e588e96c5}
The holotype was found at 3190 m in Río Negro-Sopladora National Park, Ecuador, active at 20:30 on tree branches about 5 m above ground and 8 m from a stream.
