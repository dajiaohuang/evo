## Vicia nana {#s-083c85dc3d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Vicia nana* as an accepted species of *Vicia*. In Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147, the leaflets may be alternate, opposite, or nearly opposite, while the tendrils can be simple or divided into branches. The source describes stalked racemes with flower counts recorded as 1–5 or 5–16, and calyx teeth of nearly equal length. These are alternatives in the source field, not one fixed count.
