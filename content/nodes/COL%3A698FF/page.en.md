## Evidence limits {#s-3d71bc57c9}
AVONET's morphology means summarize four complete specimens, not the range of individual variation. The 700 g mass comes from separate Updated_literature information; it is not a weight measured on those four birds. Broad habitat, niche and migration codes do not replace field observations, and this row has no latitude endpoints. EltonTraits converts literature descriptions into relative weights, not measurements of intake or time. Historical name matches do not prove that the modern and source taxonomies define the same species. Current range, population, breeding and conservation need evidence beyond these records.
