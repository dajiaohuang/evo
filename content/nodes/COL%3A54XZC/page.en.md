## Classification {#s-9a0555dc7b}
COL26.8 retains Tarsius pelengensis as an accepted species. The cited Banggai survey treated calls and observed animals on Peleng and Banggai as the same taxonomic unit, while explicitly noting that morphological and genetic confirmation was still needed.

## Field ecology {#s-cb7af5c26f}
Across surveys totaling 225 contact hours in 2017–2018, the team recorded tarsiers on Peleng and Banggai, but not on Labobo or Bangkurung. The study's distance-sampling estimate for Peleng was 234 individuals per km² (reported interval 148–371); animals were encountered across several vegetated settings, including secondary forest and village gardens.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-a534ec7b80}
These detections and the Peleng estimate belong to a dated four-island survey with uneven effort and imperfect detection. Non-detection on two islands is not proof of absence, and the reported density is not a current range-wide estimate.
