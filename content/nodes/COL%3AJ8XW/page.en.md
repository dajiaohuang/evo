## Accepted name and placement {#s-1239d33793}
COL26.8 accepts Atelopus chiriquiensis Shreve, 1936 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Bufonidae (family) → Atelopus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Seasonal activity — AmphiBIO source {#s-262467ff88}
The seasonal codes are “wet and warm” and “dry and warm.” They compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate, so they should not be read as universal calendar seasons.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-661a53a800}
The compiled maximum adult snout–vent length is 58 mm. This measurement runs from snout to vent in a frog and represents a reported maximum, not a typical size.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-de037c17c9}
The literature compilation lists 18–365 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to one reproductive event per year, and a reported offspring-or-egg size of 2–2.1 mm. It does not identify the sex, population, date or captive status behind each value; the size field may refer to eggs.
