## What growth form, flower and fruit traits identify Janusia guaranitica? {#s-15338e5b6f}
The plant is a climbing vine with oval or lance-shaped leaves. Its flowers are arranged in corymb-like clusters, with five stamens and yellow petals. The fruit lacks lateral wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b1d23f9126}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
