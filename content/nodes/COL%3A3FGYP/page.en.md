## Violet flowers on a woody-corm plant {#s-34fbdc9d8e}
Geissorhiza lithicola is 15–30 cm tall, with hard woody corm tunics (the fibrous outer layers around the underground corm, a swollen storage stem) and violet to purple flowers. Its tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are 15–20 mm long and the perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts) is 7–8 mm; plants typically have three leaves.

## Lower rocky slopes {#s-b332c49646}
The account records lower rocky slopes at 50–200 m. This is the elevation range supplied for the cited habitat record.
