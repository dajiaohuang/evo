## Tapirira pilosa {#s-04e99fbbe1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset gives one ecological category for this species: terrestrial.

## A terrestrial category without a locality {#s-704cc5d2f7}
The habitat field is “Terrícola,” recorded as a terrestrial setting. It supplies no collection locality, survey date or distribution limit, so it cannot be used as a mapped occurrence or a complete range.
