## Leaflets and bell-shaped flowers {#s-5950dc5659}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records 9 to 13 opposite leaflets, oblong to elliptic, with a slight covering of fine hairs on both surfaces. Their texture is listed as leathery or papery. The flowers have a bell-shaped calyx, blunt or oval hairy sepals, and 14 to 15 stamens with hairy anthers. The row does not report the wood heartwood color, so none is inferred here.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-d58a195795}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 entry labels the species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. The field supplies a substrate category, not a collection locality or a full range.
