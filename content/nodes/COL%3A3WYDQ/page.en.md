## ## Leaflet surfaces and striate samaras {#s-ced67ed862}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical branches that may be armed or unarmed, and leaves with leaf-like stipules. Leaflets are listed as elliptic, ovate or oblong-elliptic, with tips from acute to tapered or cuspidate and bases from acute to rounded, attenuate or blunt. The upper and lower surfaces may be becoming hairless; hairs may also remain beneath or be confined to the primary veins. The entry records brochidodromous venation, racemes, panicles or fascicles in the leaf axils, and leaf-like bracts. Flowers have a bell-shaped calyx, white or greenish petals and a hairy standard; the stamens are united in one bundle. The fruit is a samara with a striate seed-bearing region. These are listed states in a regional flora entry, not a measured account of variation.
