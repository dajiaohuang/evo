## Sample and measurement context {#s-c2af3508a3}
COL26.8 accepts Colaptes auricularis in Picidae. AVONET v7 uses its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Four birds were measured, all with complete morphological sets, though each trait can still have its own usable sample count.

## Beak dimensions at standard landmarks {#s-0b9a4a7597}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 19.6 mm; width and depth there average 6.8 and 6.5 mm. They are distinct dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification limits.

## Wing, tarsus and separate mass source {#s-1c6c395d9d}
The unflattened wing, from carpal joint to longest primary, averages 119.5 mm; the tarsus averages 20.3 mm. A separate compilation gives mean mass as 65.4 g and labels its source Dunning. It does not show that the four measured birds were weighed.

## Forest and broad diet class {#s-c99a1c1b14}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, an Omnivore trophic niche and an Insessorial lifestyle (primarily perched above ground). These are broad comparison categories, not claims of exclusive forest use or a fixed menu.

## Migration and mapped latitude {#s-e0188cebc0}
The migration field calls the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from 15.94° to 28.74° north. They do not cover all non-breeding movements or current occupancy; the map and measurements were compiled separately.

## Limits of the historical profile {#s-1636bf729c}
Morphological values are means from historical AVONET samples, not identification limits or individual ranges. Mass has separate provenance and cannot inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, diet and migration are broad classes; the map summarizes historical breeding and resident ranges. The name crosswalk associates this record with the current catalogue entry but does not prove unchanged species concepts through later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current distribution, populations and conservation require other evidence.
