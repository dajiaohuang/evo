## Comparative results retain their measurement limits {#s-073f638310}
Pairwise comparisons with T. shanjing indicated differences especially in lateral skull view, whereas a cited earlier analysis did not detect morphometric differences. This competing evidence does not alone resolve the debated species boundary. The authors warn that old captive mating reports labelled verrucosus may concern newly distinguished species or shanjing because origin and identity were uncertain. Such reports do not establish a universal wild mating habitat, season or clutch size for the pinned verrucosus concept.
