## Leaf, inflorescence and ovary characters {#s-3e56cc89fa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a trunk that is smooth to fissured. Leaves are simple, alternate, elliptic to ovate, chartaceous and glabrous, with brochidodromous venation, cuneate bases and mucronate tips. Stipules are deciduous or not evident; petioles are channelled and glabrous. Axillary solitary racemes are straight and have 16–20 flowers. Deltoid bracts and bracteoles are sparsely tawny tomentose; bracteoles are inserted on the upper half of the pedicel or at the calyx base. Pedicels are densely tawny tomentose. Floral buds are ovate and asymmetric, with acuminate tips, acute bases and dense tawny tomentum. Flowers are zygomorphic and lack a hypanthium. The calyx is spathaceous and unilobed, chartaceous, glabrous inside at the base and pilose at the apex. There are five pink petals and 10 stamens; filaments are glabrous and anthers subulate and glabrous. The gynoecium is sericeous; the sericeous ovary is narrowly elliptic with eight ovules. The style is curved and glabrous, and the stigma is punctate and glabrous. The fruit is a globose drupe; the entry lists 1–3 seeds. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
