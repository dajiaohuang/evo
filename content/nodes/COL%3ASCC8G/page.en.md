## Night branches at Alto Nangaritza {#s-11deb51f5c}
The revision reported only the Alto Nangaritza Protected Forest type locality in Ecuador at 1809–1843 m, in low-stature foothill forest with bromeliads, orchids, moss and Podocarpus. Individuals were active at night on branches 1–2 m above ground, and calling males were found in April. These observations retain locality and survey scope; poorly explored adjacent areas are not confirmed records, and calling alone does not identify egg sites.
