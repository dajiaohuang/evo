## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-453e7ee7b9}
Tachybaptus tricolor is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Podicipedidae. The 2015 life-history archive lists seven focal fields under this same-name, same-family record, each supported by cited species-level rows. Its summaries may still inherit information across historical taxa; matching names do not remove that influence.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-30271d6d91}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 174 g; mass at birth or hatching 10 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-88c65c20a6}
Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 545.97 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 5 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 20.5 d; age at flight capability (leaving the nest in some source material) 46 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## The longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-27d0cf9c8b}
The life-history archive reports a maximum longevity of 17.5 years, using the largest record found under its method. This is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current record that can be assumed repeatable.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-623c9d5839}
Same-name sparse rows cite Dunning (1992), Szekely, Lislevand and Figuerola (2007), de Magalhaes and Costa (2009), and Bennett (1986), among others. Each field has its own source label; a label may identify a paper, book or database. Row counts are not numbers of measured individuals, and repeated citations do not represent independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-b595d25b58}
The 2015 archive reports medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. These are compiled summaries, not one individual's measurements, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies can share inherited data, so the same name, family and species-level rows do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently established, and the cited publications have not been individually reviewed here. This life-history account does not establish diet, behavior, current range or conservation status; missing fields require other sources.
