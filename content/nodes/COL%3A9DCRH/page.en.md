## Stem surface {#s-e3c30dd286}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a hirsute stem, with hairs directed toward the tip and pressed against the surface. Simple hairs and glandular hairs with a head-shaped tip (capitate) are recorded. Unbranched bristles are absent; branched bristles have tiny barbs (barbellate). Prickles and short shoots (brachyblasts) are also recorded as absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-08cbd952c5}
Stipules are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries and projections between pairs of pinnae as absent. Inflorescences are globose; floral bracts are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-d3b56da9c0}
The floral perianth is recorded with four parts. The calyx is membranous and has no lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are tomentose, densely covered with matted hairs. The androecium has two stamen whorls (diplostemonous), and the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-0db288fe5a}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
