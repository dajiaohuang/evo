## One tooth below the leaf tip {#s-316a676d0b}
This dwarf perennial shrublet forms clumps and reaches 16 cm. Its glaucous, nearly sickle-shaped leaves have a rigid tip and one tooth on the keel below the apex. Solitary pink flowers are recorded in October and November; the capsules have five chambers. Rocky ridges and gentle to steep slopes on shale, granite or norite are listed.
