## Gaylussacia luizae Romão & V.C.Souza {#s-d8a52633ae}
## Rolled leaves with bristly hairs

Gaylussacia luizae has upright, narrowly arranged shoots; early-falling scale leaves are noted. Its non-overlapping, somewhat leathery leaves are obovate to oblanceolate with rolled-under margins. Both surfaces may bear long, soft or bristly hairs, including gland-tipped hairs. Terminal racemes or panicles carry many flowers. The white corolla may be tubular, urn-shaped or cylindrical and is described as woolly. The ovoid fruit is hairy. The source records alternatives for branch, leaf and flower indumentum.
