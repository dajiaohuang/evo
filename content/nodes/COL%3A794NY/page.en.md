## What surrounds its tangled spikelets? {#s-f900eaf49c}
A regional account describes *Restio ejuncidus* as a dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants), stoloniferous perennial reaching 50 cm. Its often tangled, branched culms (stems) bear white tubercles. The spikelets (small flower clusters) have long, sharply pointed, papery bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers).
