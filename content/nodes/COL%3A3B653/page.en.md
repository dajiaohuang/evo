## A small set of recorded traits {#s-fc0e6457ab}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records a woody root with a xylopodium, an erect stem, and trifoliolate leaves (each leaf has three leaflets). The inflorescences are racemes, described as loose or congested, and bear many flowers. The fruit is described as very hairy; seeds are black or brown. The row does not report the fruit's hair type or variation, and it is not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-3a1d72ff7b}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, without a locality, range or sampling coverage.
