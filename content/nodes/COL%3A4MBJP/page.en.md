## Dichasial cymes and variable sepal indumentum {#s-5262f80179}
The source description records inconspicuous intrapetiolar colleters on a glabrous stem. Leaves lack blade colleters and are coriaceous to subcoriaceous and glabrous. Axillary dichasial cymes have glabrescent to pubescent indumentum and inconspicuous scarious bracts. The floral throat ring is evident; anthers are included, the corolla glabrous, and sepal indumentum variably glabrescent. Corolla lobes are present, the throat lobes have exserted tips, and the nectary is divided into five. Follicles are recorded as free and joined at the apex or fully joined. Seeds are truncate; seed indument is unknown. The habitat field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial). This single morphology field does not establish variation or full diagnostic scope; no locality or range evidence was assessed.
