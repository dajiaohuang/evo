## What leaf and floral structure does the regional record describe? {#s-7e3310c36f}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite leaves are oval or elliptic, with blunt bases and flat margins. The inflorescence is leafy. Flower stalks are 0.1–6 mm long, and bracteoles are present. The 13–30 mm floral tube may be pubescent (softly hairy) or hairless; its blunt spur points horizontally or downward. Red petals are absent by the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube; no vesicle is recorded, and the ovary-base nectar gland bends downward. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin blunt. The source row gives these alternatives without a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-89dd484758}
The Brazilian Flora row lists terrestrial habitat and no collection locality or date.
