## Three historical canga assignments revised by mitochondrial evidence {#s-c93fce96a9}
The study sequenced São Geraldo do Araguaia specimens MPEG 43920–43922, formerly assigned to P. canga. Their 16S sequences clustered with cited P. mystacalis sequences, differing by 0.27% from a Babaçulândia comparison and 4% from a Cuiabá comparison. The authors used these three vouchers to include P. mystacalis in their list. The distinct distances are not erased, and the result is not a reidentification of every regional canga specimen or a completed resolution of the entire mystacalis complex.

## Cryptic resemblance and a cited multi-lineage concept {#s-1a823b10b7}
The account summarized earlier evidence of at least ten recognized lineages with divergence up to 5% within the mystacalis complex. It described P. mystacalis and P. canga as externally similar, with important differences in osteological characters, and cited occurrence in standing water across open South American formations. These are cited concept and habitat summaries, separate from the three newly sequenced vouchers. They do not create ten accepted species, establish a universal 5% threshold or certify all historical South American records.
