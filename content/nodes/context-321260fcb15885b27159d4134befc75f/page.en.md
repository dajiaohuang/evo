## The source concept {#s-89fc9b0c20}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria albolabris as row Anf2760 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental strategy {#s-f7f6721768}
The record indicates a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or reproductive timing.
