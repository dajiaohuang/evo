## Reference-marker role in a Japanese ancient-DNA comparison {#s-cd3ae5962e}
The 2025 Ehime study uses previously published Andrias cheni sequence accession entries MH051473 in its short cytb comparison. Table1 records KIZYPX6151 from Huangshan, Anhui. These table entries are reference material, not newly excavated remains of this species or a complete population sample. The fossil assignment to japonicus does not date the origin or expansion of Andrias cheni, and a93 bp marker does not certify modern nuclear ancestry, hybridization history or all species boundaries. Original sequence records and specimen identities have not been independently rechecked.
