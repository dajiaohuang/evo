Yinlong downsi is represented by the nearly complete holotype IVPP V14530 from the upper Shishugou Formation. The specimen preserves a broad skull behind the eyes and a shallow snout; the published matrix places it among basal ceratopsians, but does not establish direct ancestry.

## What the holotype preserves {#s-f2b734262b}
The nearly complete skeleton IVPP V14530 is missing the distal tail. Its skull is broad behind the eye sockets and shallow in front; the diagnosis also records a frontal midline fossa and a trapezoidal quadratojugal. The individual is estimated at about 120 cm long. Short forelimbs and robust hindlimbs support a bipedal interpretation, not an observed gait.

## The sample and the character analysis {#s-19f36cb6e7}
The holotype comes from Wucaiwan in the Junggar Basin, Xinjiang, in the upper Shishugou Formation. The 161.2–155.7 Ma interval is a literature-bounded envelope for this occurrence. A character-matrix analysis recovers Yinlong downsi as a basal ceratopsian; that placement depends on the study's character scoring and sampling and does not make the specimen a direct ancestor or a global first appearance.
