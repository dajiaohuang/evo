## Recorded parent of Amphicynodontinae {#s-8eef062d11}
Amphicynodontinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Ursidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
