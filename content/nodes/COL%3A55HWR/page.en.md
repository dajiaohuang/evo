This is a maximum in the reported material, not a theoretical limit or typical individual size. ## How to read reproductive records {#s-feae555840}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode and a consolidated mean of 4 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch. A summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The value is a dataset summary, not a fixed count for each clutch. These records do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
