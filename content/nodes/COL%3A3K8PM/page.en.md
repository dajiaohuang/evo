## Which leaf and flower forms does the flora record? {#s-1402fa1a1f}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes *Heliconia hirsuta* with a slender, spreading rhizome (a horizontal underground stem) and leaves whose stalks may be present or absent. The blades are listed as oblong or ovate, with several possible base shapes. Its upright flower cluster has boat-shaped bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers); the source field lists multiple bract and floral states rather than settling on one form.
