## What leaf, flower-cluster and stamen variation is recorded? {#s-d3fb28975f}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field allows hairless stems or stems with glandular hairs. Oval leaves have rounded or shallowly heart-shaped bases, pointed tips, seven or nine veins and long stiff hairs. Flowers occur in axillary or terminal clusters reduced to one or two flowers. The bell-shaped floral cup may have glandular hairs or become nearly hairless; triangular sepals may be hairless or bristly and lack a small tip. Petals are purple or magenta. Stamens differ in form and size; the ovary has three chambers and glandular hairs at its tip. These are alternatives in a regional database field.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-61df382289}
The regional dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
