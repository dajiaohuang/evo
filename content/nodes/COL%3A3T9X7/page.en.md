## Lepidaploa salzmannii {#s-c05afedce1}
## Silky and woolly two-ranked leaves carry lilac heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright pubescent stems with glands. Alternate leaves sit in two vertical rows on stalks. Their elliptic, lance-shaped or ovate blades have pointed to tail-like tips and bases that are tapered or heart-shaped. The upper face is silky; the underside is woolly. Glands occur on both leaf faces.

Flower heads grow on leafy stems, not a leafless scape. The bell-shaped involucre has overlapping bracts, with long-pointed inner bracts. Each head has 20–30 lilac florets. The pappus is white, and the fruit has glands. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
