## Leaves, flowers and winged fruit {#s-05de49841d}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry describes bipinnate leaves, divided twice, with no nectary on the leaf stalk. It lists either 3–6 or 7–11 pairs of pinnae; each pinna bears 7–14 pairs of leaflets, which range from hairless to finely hairy. The flowers grow in spike-like clusters. A nectar gland is recorded at the base of the floral cup, and the calyx and petals have fine short hairs. The row lists the plant's sexual system as monoecious or dioecious (male and female flowers on the same plant or on separate plants). It records up to ten stamens, which do not form a tube, and a hairless ovary. The fruit is indehiscent (it does not split open) and narrowly winged. Seeds are elliptic to oblong and lack a pleurogram, a line-like mark on the seed coat. These are traits from one regional dataset row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-0eacf0d713}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It supplies a habitat category only, without a locality, range or sampling coverage.
