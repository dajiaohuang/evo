## Heteropsis oblongifolia: leaf and floral descriptors {#s-8ab5f454b4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field pairs clasping and feeding-root descriptors, but its concatenated modifiers do not clarify their combined meaning. It describes the stem as subquadrangular and striate. Petioles are strongly channelled, with sinuate margins and a geniculate bend. Leaf blades are listed as lanceolate, oblong or oblanceolate, leathery or subleathery, with acuminate tips, acute, obtuse or cuneate bases, and revolute margins. The midrib is described as grooved on the upper surface, alongside an unexplained “1/2” value.

The inflorescence is described as axillary or terminal, with a short or cylindrical peduncle. The spathe is listed as convolute, inflated, oblong, deciduous and yellow, with an acuminate or cuspidate tip and white margin; the ellipsoid spadix is yellowish, with an acuminate or rounded tip. The stipe is short, subcylindrical and light green. Floral descriptors include 4 stamens per flower, ovate anthers, a prismatic gynoecium, an incompletely bilocular ovary, sessile anatropous ovules, subbasal placentation and disc-shaped or oblong stigmas. The fruit is a truncate red berry; seeds are oblong, and the seed coat is unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Some concatenated terms and the “1/2” value remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiparasita,” or hemiparasitic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
