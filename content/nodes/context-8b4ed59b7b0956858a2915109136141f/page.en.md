## The source concept {#s-abaf9395d3}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Leptodactylus rhodomystax in row Anf3424, under the genus Leptodactylus, family Leptodactylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; they do not establish that it matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-e0eb658340}
This row marks terrestrial and aquatic foraging as present categories. These labels are broad and do not describe changes across seasons or life stages. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not yet been checked against those changes. Treat its categories as provisional historical values: no replacement is supplied here, and the revision does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-af3e9d9960}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines adult diet categories reported from expert judgement, observations, or stomach contents. A listed category does not give a complete diet or a measured share of food.

## Activity periods {#s-719eb983d5}

## Seasonal activity {#s-6cac82f47d}
The row records activity in wet, warm conditions. In AmphiBIO, seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months or imply the same season everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a2ba8e099e}
The compiled maximum adult body length is 90 mm. For Anura, AmphiBIO measures body length from the tip of the snout to the vent (snout–vent length); this is a reported maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
