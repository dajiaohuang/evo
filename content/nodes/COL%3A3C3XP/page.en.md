## Two veins near a flat leaf edge {#s-42f7f8e8c8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes papery elliptic or narrowly elliptic leaves with wedge-shaped bases and acute or blunt tips. Their margins are flat and lack a cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib is grooved, and two veins run near the margin. Flowers grow in compact clusters; the supporting bracts fall away, while small, inconspicuous bracteoles remain. The smooth flower cup bears hairs. Its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid with a smooth surface, and its small bracteoles persist. One seed is recorded, while the embryo is unknown. The habitat field separately records “terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## A terrestrial category beside a known fruit form {#s-0b60813e1c}
The habitat field says “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category is separate from the recorded ellipsoid, smooth fruit.
