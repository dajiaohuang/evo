## A volcanic-ridge neotype and twenty measured adults {#s-cd7cc9f42e}
The revision designated male MVZ 138783 as neotype, collected on the ridge between Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl on 26 July 1976 at 3300 m. Nineteen additional specimens comprised nine males and ten females. The diagnosis summarized ten males, mean SVL 35.9 mm and range 33.6–38.9 mm, and ten females, mean 39.0 mm and range 34.9–43.0 mm. Mean tail/SVL was 1.02 in both sexes; mean maxillary teeth were 8.2 in males and 28.8 in females. Adpressed-limb intervals averaged 1.9 and 2.9 costal folds respectively. These sexed sample summaries are not species-wide anatomical limits or a population-level test of sexual dimorphism.

## Live photographs from a different locality {#s-dee88f1bb5}
No live-colour information was available for the neotype or individuals from its locality. The account used photographs from Lagunas de Zempoala: dark grey backgrounds commonly carried reddish-brown, tan or golden-brown dorsal bands, but some animals lacked bands; irises were coppery. Preserved specimens were medium brown above and tan to pale brown below. ## Logs in pine and fir forest across the volcanic belt {#s-68dd4caffc}
The 2020 account placed the species in the central and eastern Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt, listing La Marquesa, Desierto de los Leones, Ajusco, Lagunas de Zempoala, Iztaccíhuatl, Popocatépetl, Río Frío and La Malinche. It described terrestrial encounters in pine and fir forest, typically under log bark or inside rotting logs, at 2500–3500 m. These are reported localities and substrates. They do not establish breeding inside logs, a wood-based diet, universal forest-disturbance tolerance or a verified complete contemporary range.
