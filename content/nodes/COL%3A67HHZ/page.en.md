## Form described in the flora record {#s-728b049f83}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem with fibers at the base and entire cataphylls and prophylls at the stem tip. Petioles are cylindrical and smooth. Leaf blades are palmately compound (divided into leaflets that spread from one point), flat, and elliptic, ovate, or obovate; the tip is acuminate and the base acute. Glandular dots are absent. No primary-vein character is recorded; secondary veins are sunken on the upper side, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is larger than half the leaf size, with a cylindrical peduncle. The spathe is flat and ovate; its angle to the peduncle and the presence or length of a stipe are not characterized. The berries are wine-colored.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
