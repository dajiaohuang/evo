## Five adults in the diagnostic sample {#s-dd71dff918}
The description’s diagnostic sample contained three adult males with snout–vent lengths of 31.9–34.2 mm and two adult females measuring 41.7–43.7 mm. These small-sample ranges describe measured specimens, rather than all males and females throughout the species range.

## Two recorded localities {#s-b2362778bf}
The account reported the type locality in Morona Santiago and another locality in Zamora Chinchipe, Ecuador, approximately 32 km apart, at 882–1183 m elevation. Frogs were associated with ravines and streams in primary and secondary forest. These observations establish the article’s recorded sample without implying continuous occupation between localities.

## A tadpole and a metamorph near streams {#s-b74d726fb3}
The authors recorded a tadpole, QCAZ 67083, in a pool beside a stream in February and a metamorph on a rock island within a stream in March. They inferred stream development of larvae from these observations. This inference does not establish every larval microhabitat, a complete developmental sequence or the annual breeding season.
