## Name, lineage and sample {#s-ddaa81b7d0}
COL26.8 accepts Pogoniulus leucomystax in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 uses its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name: 9 individuals were measured, and 4 have a complete morphological set. Usable samples may vary across traits.

## Beak dimensions {#s-800c27c390}
Mean length from anterior nostril edge to tip is 6.7 mm; width and depth at that edge are both 4.1 mm. The dimensions use standardized landmarks and are not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-96133d869f}
Mean unflattened wing length from carpal joint to longest primary is 54.9 mm; mean tarsus is 13.8 mm. A separate compiled mean mass is 11.8 g (source category Dunning), so morphology sample counts do not supply its denominator.

## Ecological categories {#s-bf08096d1e}
The dataset records Forest habitat, Frugivore trophic niche and Insessorial lifestyle. These broad comparison categories do not mean exclusive use of one habitat or food.

## Migration and mapped range {#s-eacbc7c8f7}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitudes -16.11° to 2.58°. They do not describe every non-breeding movement or current/global occupancy; geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Evidence limits {#s-b923d37123}
AVONET v7 measurements are historical species averages for the listed samples, not individual ranges or identification criteria. The Dunning mass estimate is separately compiled and must not inherit morphology sample counts. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories; mapped latitudes refer to historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one crosswalks and matching names associate records without proving equivalent concepts after taxonomic changes. Reproduction, current populations and conservation need other sources.
