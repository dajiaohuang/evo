## Accepted name and placement {#s-d6278aa475}
COL26.8 accepts Chiropterotriton casasi Parra-Olea, García-Castillo, Rovito, Maisano, Hanken & Wake, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Plethodontidae (family) → Chiropterotriton (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A 1969 holotype and one measured female {#s-a2c36e923e}
Male holotype MVZ 92874 was collected southwest of Tlapacoyan, Veracruz, on 26 December 1969 at an estimated 1450–1550 m; the location had a maximum error distance of 2 km. Four male and one female paratypes came from the same locality. The diagnosis summarized four adult males, mean SVL 37.8 mm and range 34.5–42.0 mm, and one female at 40.9 mm. The holotype itself measured 42.0 mm. The diagnostic male count remains separate from the five listed male types, and a single female value is not a population mean or proof of sexual size dimorphism.

## Tooth counts, webbing and unresolved tail ratios {#s-e2b30a67c8}
Male maxillary teeth averaged 9.0, range 6–13; the single female had 30. The diagnosis described slender digits with subterminal pads, webbing reaching the penultimate phalanx of the third toe and a short first digit contained within the webbing. Male tail/SVL was printed as mean 1.0, range 0.90–1.15, whereas some comparison paragraphs printed 1.04. The only female had a broken tail. These differing printed summaries are retained without silently choosing one, and the incomplete female tail cannot provide a full female ratio.

## Preserved mottling with no recorded live colour {#s-420acc6cc9}
The live-colour entry explicitly stated that no data were available. Preserved specimens were faded brown with paler venters. The holotype lacked an evident dorsal stripe, while female paratype MVZ 92876 had strong mottling and an irregular light stripe on the anterior tail; some paratypes bore a pale bar between the eyes. These preserved differences do not recover live colour, establish a universal stripe pattern or demonstrate a camouflage function.

## A robust scanned skull and different tooth observations {#s-3985131023}
CT examination of the anterior skeleton of holotype MVZ 92874 described a robust skull, complete frontal–parietal articulation without a frontoparietal fontanel and a well-developed braincase. Hand phalangeal formula was 1–2–3–2 on both sides and mesopodial cartilages were not mineralized. The external holotype description counted maxillary teeth 4–4 and premaxillary teeth four, while the osteology reported five maxillary teeth per side and three premaxillary teeth. Both descriptions concern the same voucher; their differing counts remain unresolved rather than being combined into a single definitive count.

## A single known locality and a proposed former forest {#s-21025e5098}
The 2020 account knew the species only from the type locality. Vegetation there was described as secondary forest and thicket, with former cloud forest considered likely. Wider occurrence was suggested as possible, but little intact forest remained nearby. The former habitat and wider range are hypotheses, separate from the known collection site. This paragraph supplies no demonstrated diet, egg site, contemporary population estimate or measured tolerance of habitat disturbance.
