## What does Jamesbrittenia tenuifolia look like? {#s-8cac526326}
The source describes a glandular-hairy shrublet up to 60 cm tall, with leaves bearing a few teeth. Flowers grow in the leaf axils and may form leafy clusters; the upper part of the corolla tube is swollen. The corolla limb is usually purple-blue, rarely white to pink.

## How can it be separated from similar species? {#s-e343894803}
The account highlights elliptic or spoon-shaped leaves, often with a pair of large teeth near the tip, short flower stalks, and a tendency for flowers to form leafy racemes. J. argentea differs in having flowers scattered along branches and a white corolla limb. J. aspalathoides usually has much smaller, narrower, mostly entire leaves and flowers scattered along its branches.
