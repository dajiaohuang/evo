## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields {#s-05c977bb85}
Frugivore is the Metadata category for predominantly fruit resources; trophic level is separately Herbivore. These categories describe different levels of classification rather than individual feeding observations. Category thresholds are not measured diet percentages for a locality or season. ## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category {#s-2b86810c13}
Insessorial means primarily perching above ground on branches or other substrates, without implying never descending to the ground. Habitat is Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per unit area. Migration=1 is sedentary, without establishing lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted here, and categories do not establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.
