## The source concept {#s-d1b4189abc}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Lysapsus laevis in row Anf2468, classified in Lysapsus (genus), Hylidae (family) and Anura (order). Its source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes that deposited record; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been resolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-78f463260d}
AmphiBIO marks fossorial (burrowing) and aquatic foraging strata in this row. These are broad categories, not detailed accounts of feeding across life stages or seasons. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some body-mass and microhabitat entries carried forward from earlier compilations; its affected fields include these categories. A species-level comparison for this row has not been documented, so read the flags as historical values that need review. No replacement values are supplied here.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-60a4d0583a}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body size of 21 mm for this row. For frogs and toads (Anura), the dataset defines body size as snout–vent length: the distance from the snout tip to the vent. This is a compiled maximum, not a typical measurement for every individual.
