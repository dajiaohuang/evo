## A cited leaf-underside calling habit with identification limits {#s-6fd8c29124}
The 2025 account summarized earlier records of night calling from leaf undersides in lower forest strata along forested streams. It described large irregular dorsal iridophore spots as useful even in preserved specimens, but explicitly noted that H. tricolor and H. mesai could not be separated by external morphology. This is cited habitat and diagnostic background, not a new measured activity profile or proof that the spots alone identify every specimen. ## Two reidentified museum vouchers from Serra Norte {#s-e38c11b8c0}
The authors identified MPEG 42347, collected on 1 March 2019 at 475 m, and MPEG 43023, collected on 19 February 2020 at 434 m, from Serra Norte in Carajás National Forest. They interpreted these as the second Pará record and the easternmost records then known, comparing distances of about 742, 784 and 962 km with three prior localities. These are the study’s museum assignments and dated comparisons; they do not measure recent dispersal, establish intervening occupancy or certify the complete present-day range.
