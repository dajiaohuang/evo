## What stem, leaf and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-f306ab2f0a}
The plant has a taproot and decumbent, hairy stems with appressed star-shaped and translucent hairs. Linear to lance-shaped stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned leaves are lance-shaped or ovate, with symmetrical blades, heart-shaped bases, pointed tips and toothed edges. The upper surface is hairy with star-shaped hairs. Flowers are crowded at the stem tips or solitary in leaf axils; the sepals have pointed tips and the corolla is pink. The dry fruit splits into six to ten raised segments (mericarps) covered in gland-tipped or star-shaped hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-cdcea6bc73}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
