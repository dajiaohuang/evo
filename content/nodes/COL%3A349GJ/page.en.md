## Paired curved spines and unarmed leaf tips {#s-e8408904c4}
The record describes reclining branches with paired, curved spines. Alternate, stalked leaves are papery and elliptic to broadly elliptic; their tips are unarmed, and the lower surfaces are smooth. Branched clusters carry bell-shaped heads with brownish or green bracts. The flowers are described as strap-shaped or somewhat two-lipped, with short hairs on the lobes. The habitat field says terrestrial.
