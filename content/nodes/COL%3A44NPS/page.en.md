## Mus (Coelomys) vulcani: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Mus (Coelomys) vulcani* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority (Robinson & Kloss, 1919).
