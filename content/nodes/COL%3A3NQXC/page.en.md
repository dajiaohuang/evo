## Working identification and reference conflicts remain explicit {#s-055a91e0d4}
The twelve share one COI BIN/ABGD group. Previously labelled phantasticus references included one matching glandicolor, another near cinnamomeoventris and two records sharing field number RABI150 despite divergent sequences. Their true identities could not be verified, so the authors retain their own sample name without treating the conflicting database labels as authenticated vouchers.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-82505b60b9}
For Hyperolius phantasticus, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
