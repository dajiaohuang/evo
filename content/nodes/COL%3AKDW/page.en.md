## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-8e96d90e39}
COL26.8 records Surniinae at subfamily rank, with Strigidae as its direct parent.

AVONET v7 has measured records for 28 Surniinae species, including Aegolius acadicus, A. funereus, A. harrisii, A. ridgwayi and Athene brama. The hierarchy follows pinned COL26.8, while the biological values retain the source's historical names and sampling scope.

## Differences between records {#s-1ebfde523c}

These are source-record comparisons, not individual mass ranges or traits shared by all descendants.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-5a98e3a6fc}
The AVONET summary covers 28 measured species with historical one-to-one crosswalks and matching names and families. The 45.7–319.9 g endpoints are compiled species means, not individual minimum and maximum masses. They do not cover every accepted descendant or remove the sampling and taxonomic limits described on species pages.
The life-history summary covers 12 historical same-name, same-family accounts. Its compiled summaries and possible historical taxonomic inheritance are properties of the archive, not direct measurements of current species concepts or every descendant.
The 28-entry feeding subset has both food and foraging-stratum groups; the separate five-entry subset has strata only. Both are historical same-name, same-family records, not a complete descendant catalogue. Percentages standardize literature descriptions and do not establish shared subfamily-wide traits; source taxonomy and per-record interpretation still matter.
