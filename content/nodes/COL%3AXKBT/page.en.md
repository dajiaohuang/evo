## Conchocarpus hamadryadicus {#s-0478e97df3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 account records several blade-tip forms and two, rarely three, fertile stamens in white flowers.

## Variable leaf tips and a rare third stamen {#s-bcbe47e31f}
Leaves are alternate and simple or one-foliolate, with slightly expanded petiole tips. Blade tips are acute, obtuse, retuse or emarginate. Inflorescences are solitary, generally over 7 cm long and positioned between leafy branches; evergreen leaf-like bracts are absent, and branching is absent (pseudoracemose). Sepals persist. The radially symmetric corolla is white; the anthers are coherent and glabrous. Flowers have two fertile stamens, rarely three. The ovary is pubescent and the style connate; the fruit apex is rounded. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete distribution.
