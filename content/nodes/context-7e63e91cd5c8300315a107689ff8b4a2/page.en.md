## Record identity and classification {#s-6ffee73534}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf4990 records Pelophylax saharicus under Pelophylax, Ranidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-9e3eb1111c}
This AmphiBIO row marks aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Adult food categories {#s-4559d4ca96}
The row lists arthropods; vertebrates, including cannibalism in the source definition as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Seasonal activity categories {#s-147bd3770a}
AmphiBIO records wet and cold for seasonal activity in this row. The categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not map to fixed calendar months across regions.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-c5297ceef3}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body mass of 30.6 g and a maximum adult body length of 104.5 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian body-mass entries; this remains the AmphiBIO v1 value, not a revised species-level estimate.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-7e1cdc6a01}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists: minimum age at sexual maturity: 1 years; maximum age at sexual maturity: 2 years; minimum size at sexual maturity: 40 mm; maximum size at sexual maturity: 40 mm; maximum lifespan: 14 years; minimum clutch count (offspring or eggs): 1300; maximum clutch count (offspring or eggs): 1300; maximum reproductive events per year: 2. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental category {#s-70c3402204}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists larval stage as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
