## Three-winged segments and white flowers {#s-ce6f106b49}
The flattened stem segments have three wings; new segments arise terminally, and their tips are crenate. Lateral flowers have glabrous areoles, a wheel-shaped corolla and white filaments. The flower base projects beyond the stem but is shorter than the perianth segments. The turbinate to oblong fruit has no areoles and is white or green. Epiphytic habitat is recorded.
