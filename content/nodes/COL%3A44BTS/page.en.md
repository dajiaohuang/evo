## What is known about Moquilea angustata in this record? {#s-5f7dfa058b}
The leaves are oblong or lance-shaped, hairless, and their stalks lack glands. Flowers may grow in panicles or racemes and have individual stalks. The receptacle ranges from bell-shaped to cup-shaped. The source field lists 17 stamens and describes them as extending beyond the floral tube. Fruit-wall details are not recorded.
