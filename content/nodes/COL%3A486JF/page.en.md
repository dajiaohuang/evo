## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-077a49b158}
Chancas and colomai are compared as having a tan, green or brown dorsum with pale flecks or dots, and a white iris bearing a dark horizontal stripe. Dajomesae is uniformly green and has a tan-to-greyish-tan iris with fine dark reticulations and punctuations. Variation among those source colors is not reduced to one compulsory color in every individual.
