## Overview {#s-14b583cb87}
This reading path follows the three fixed COL26.8 species IDs in this genus: S. alleni, S. gabonensis and S. makandensis. A 2003 study first compared them as regional acoustic forms within the broad Galago alleni group; the 2013 account named the Makandé form. MDD v2.5 records additional historical combinations and a different note on whether cameronensis is distinct.

## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-9d449dc37d}
Read Ambrose’s ten-location call comparison first, keeping its three regional profiles and its prediction of species boundaries distinct. Then read the Makandé species account for the later name and type locality. The older Makokou radio-tracking study is useful for understanding field history under Galago alleni, but its animals were not assigned to one of the later acoustic forms.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-094223db9f}
These sources support a concise bioacoustic and taxonomic guide, not a genus-wide ecological synthesis. Their names, sites and sample descriptions belong to different taxonomic periods; they should not be combined into a single current range, abundance estimate or behavioral profile.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-6f62af5bbe}
This is a source-linked reading path, not a genus review. The central study concerns calls and morphology at ten locations; later naming and earlier ecology use different taxonomic frames. The cited material does not establish current genus-wide distribution, population trends or a complete life history. The Chinese genus label is editorial; this guide has not been. This page is.
