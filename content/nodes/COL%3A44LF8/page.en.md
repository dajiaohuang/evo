## Muraltia montana {#s-4d7b950207}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia montana* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a sprawling shrublet to 20 cm, hairy on young parts, with clustered, rigid leaves that end in spines. Deep-pink flowers grow on short stalks in the leaf axils; the hairy capsules bear narrow horns. The stated habitat is high rocky slopes, a regional account rather than a complete distribution.
