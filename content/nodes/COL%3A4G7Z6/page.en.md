## Philodendron deflexum: leaf and inflorescence descriptors {#s-acd414435c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a prophyll on the stem. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole green. Leaf blades are listed as cordate, sagittate, elliptic or oblong, with entire margins. The anterior and posterior lobes are described as elliptic to ovate, and the sinus as oblong or long. The field also gives “8–9” and “9–10” without identifying which leaf measurement they describe.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium; its peduncle is green, the spathe is recorded as a tube, and the spadix is subsessile. Stamen, staminode, gynoecium, ovary, locule, ovule and stigma states are unknown. Placentation is listed as axial, basal, subbasal or biseriate. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The unassigned “8–9” and “9–10” values and alternative placentation states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
