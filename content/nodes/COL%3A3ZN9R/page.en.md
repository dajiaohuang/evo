## Melothria trilobata {#s-62d30e7a4d}
## Deeply lobed leaves and striped green fruit

Melothria trilobata is a vine with stems ranging from nearly hairless to sparsely crisped-hairy. Its nearly round leaves are 6–10 cm wide and deeply divided into five to seven pointed lobes. Male flowers occur in long-stalked racemes; female flowers are solitary in the same leaf axils. The yellow corollas have hairy outer surfaces. Smooth, hairless fruits are ovoid, 4–5 cm long and striped in two shades of green. The seeds are white and silky. The cited range extends from Mexico south to Colombia and Suriname.
