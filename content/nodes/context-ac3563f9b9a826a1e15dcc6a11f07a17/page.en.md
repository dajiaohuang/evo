## Source classification and concept status {#s-dad95f35dd}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3112 records *Hyperolius argus* under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-c9f662abad}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Adult food category (arthropods) {#s-86a7d9bc4e}
The row lists arthropods as an adult food category. AmphiBIO combines such categories from literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; the listing is not a complete diet or a measured proportion.

## Seasonal activity {#s-e2fea6d31d}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and warm conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-7b06eee77d}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 40 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-bf4427f926}
AmphiBIO reports both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch as 200, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and both minimum and maximum offspring or egg size as 1 mm. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. Its size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-32d7c4abeb}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
