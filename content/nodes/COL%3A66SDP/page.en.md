## What does the source describe for Annona purpurea? {#s-8ac0739f92}
This tree has rusty, felt-haired young branches and large, deciduous leaves. Its solitary flowers are nearly stalkless; two pointed bracts first enclose the bud. The outer petals are thick and rigid, while the inner petals are thinner. The round fruit can reach 20 cm across and is covered with many pyramidal projections and brown felt-like hairs. The source gives seed length as 28–30 cm.
