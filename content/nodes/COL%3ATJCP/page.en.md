## Chamaecrista lavradioides {#s-65cd4279ac}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista lavradioides* records one pair of oblanceolate to spoon-shaped leaflets, yellow flowers with a longer sickle-shaped petal, twisted mature pod valves. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
