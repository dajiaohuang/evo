## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-7d341ad69b}
COL26.8 records Nilssonia nigricans as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trionychidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4a5449cb65}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as plant- and animal-eating (Omnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Das et al. 2014. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Das et al. 2014.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-c0a1c8140f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 20.2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Das 2002. The lowest value among reported litter or clutch means is 10; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to Greer 1997. The highest value among reported litter or clutch means is 38; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to Greer 1997. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-90a8f24f8e}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
