## Restio perplexus {#s-c8a003976d}
## A solitary spikelet on rocky ledges

The account describes Restio perplexus as a tangled, stolon-forming perennial up to 60 cm. Its branched stems are tuberculate, and the spikelets are solitary; the bracts have translucent margins. Rocky slopes and ledges, usually facing south, are the recorded habitat.
