## Pouteria gardneri (Mart. & Miq.) Baehni {#s-4faa5c137e}
## Cup-shaped flowers and variable fruit color

Pouteria gardneri has young branchlets with soft hairs and lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have cylindrical petioles and veins that fade before the margin. Axillary clusters bear five-parted flowers with a cup-shaped corolla and blunt-tipped lobes. The ovary has two or three chambers. The smooth fruit is recorded with alternative surface hair states and colors ranging from yellow to red or vinaceous; it contains one seed with a narrow scar.
