## Vachellia farnesiana {#s-52044cc658}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Vachellia farnesiana* as an accepted species of *Vachellia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records spiny stipules 0.2–1.4 cm long. Its compound leaves have 3–7 pairs of pinnae, the side axes bearing leaflets; each pinna has 12–21 pairs of narrow, hairless leaflets. The description also notes small, head-shaped flower clusters. These are regional source fields, not a complete account of the species.
