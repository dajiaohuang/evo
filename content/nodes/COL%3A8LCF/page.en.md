## How does its single leaf form frame the flower spike? {#s-95a5b5eaed}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records one leaf form in *Abolboda neblinae*: the leaves are linear, with blades described as flattened or three-sided and as striated or veined. A rosette, the basal cluster of leaves, bears one spindle-shaped spike with two to four flowers. The entry lists alternatives for some blade and sepal features rather than resolving a single state.
