## Myosotis graminifolia {#s-84d43b075f}
## Blue flowers fade to white on softly hairy stems

The Greater Cape flora describes a softly hairy annual that trails along the ground and reaches 40 cm. Its long white hairs lie close against the surface. Leaves are lance-shaped or inversely egg-shaped, and may be stalkless or stalked. Loose, coiled flower clusters lengthen as fruits develop. The flowers are light blue with a yellow centre, then fade to white; the calyx opens as the fruit forms. The flattened, egg-shaped nutlets have a rim and a dull-brown surface. The habitat entry is coastal bush.
