## What does Polycarena gracilis look like? {#s-c7c5d38c2a}
This glandular-hairy annual can reach 23 cm and has narrow, coarsely toothed leaves. Loose racemes carry white to cream flowers with an orange patch near the upper lobes. The corolla is salver-shaped: a narrow tube opens into a flat limb. Its tube is hairless and 8–25 mm long.

## How is it separated from related species? {#s-6feb5de526}
Compared with P. gilioides, P. gracilis has a hairless, often longer corolla tube, a broader corolla limb, and looser flower clusters; the source says the two may meet where their ranges overlap. It may be related to P. aemulans, but differs in narrower upper leaves, a longer calyx and corolla, and four stamens. The proposed relationship is presented as uncertain.

## What terrain is listed? {#s-0447aa3b42}
The habitat field places it on sandstone slopes, without naming a locality.
