## Kanahia laniflora {#s-72b2219a69}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Kanahia laniflora* as an accepted species. A regional account describes a 0.9–1.2 m shrub with white flowers and mostly paired, spindle-shaped follicles. The account places it along sandy or rocky riverbeds. This habitat statement is regional and does not provide a complete range or dated sampling frame.
