## Name and classification in the source {#s-366153f735}
Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Litoria eurynastes* under genus *Litoria*, family Pelodryadidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurements in the deposit {#s-8e9d6da96f}
The source table contains 2 measurement rows; 8 trait columns have at least one recorded value. Its Sex field uses F (female) and M (male). These rows are not necessarily unique specimens, and missing cells do not show that a structure is absent.
