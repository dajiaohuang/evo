## Parapolydora fastigiata {#s-ae861a8b45}
## Woolly bracts and two forms of pappus

Herman and Retief describe an annual herb up to 1 m tall, with linear to linear-lanceolate leaves and entire margins. Flower heads form open corymbs. The long-pointed involucral bracts have bristle-like tips and a persistent cobwebby wool.

The flowers are purple. The fruits are densely covered in short, silvery, stiff hairs. Their pappus has an outer row of short, linear, bristle-like scales and an inner row of short barbed bristles. The cited habitat field records dry, sandy places.
