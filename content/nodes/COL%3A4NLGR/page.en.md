## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-09222bc22f}
COL26.8 records Pseudaspis cana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pseudaspididae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-7bf30f6897}

ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016. This row records TL and SVL in separate fields; the captured definitions provide no conversion between them.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-0aa0bca256}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Fossorial/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bates et al. 2014. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d060d48061}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 34. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Eipper 2012, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-a8c53a6179}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
