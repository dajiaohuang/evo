## Terminal nodes sometimes thorn-like {#s-de4fbdf500}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field leaves the tendril state unknown and records spines at terminal nodes as an occasional form. Leaves are three-veined, and the underside axils of the basal veins are bearded. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are corymbiform. The corolla is rotate or short-funnel-shaped, with lobes longer than the tube; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are about 2 cm in diameter, with a subwoody fruit wall 1–2 mm thick. The seeds are recorded as one to three, with a crust-like coat.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-d5a50215df}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Strychnos jacarepiensis* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
