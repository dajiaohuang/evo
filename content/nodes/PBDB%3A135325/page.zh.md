Based on the regional stratigraphy and palynological assemblages, the Ebelsberg Formation has been considered as Upper Oligocene (Hochuli, 1978, Roetzel, 1983, Harzhauser and Mandic, 2002). Recent investigations of the herein described section near Pucking, however, proved an Aquitanian age (zone lower NN2) based on a nannoplankton assemblage with Reticulofenestra minuta, R. pseudoumbilica and R. haqii (Gregorova et al., 2009).
