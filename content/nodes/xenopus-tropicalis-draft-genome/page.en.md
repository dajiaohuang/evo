## A draft genome for the living frog Xenopus tropicalis {#s-c70690078d}
A seventh-generation inbred female Xenopus tropicalis yielded about 7.6-fold shotgun coverage and a 1.51-Gbp draft assembly, accession AAMC00000000. The sequence became a genomic reference for comparisons among living vertebrates. It represents one laboratory animal and an incomplete assembly, not an amphibian ancestor, a date for the group’s origin, or every amphibian genome.
