## Austrobrickellia bakerianum {#s-0916102ea2}
## Right-angled branches and persistent pappus bristles

The record describes a smooth, cylindrical but pubescent stem with branches spreading at right angles. Opposite, petiolate ovate leaves have three veins from the base and entire margins. A branched thyrsoid panicle bears dense, flat-topped flower clusters at the tips. The purplish florets produce five-ribbed prismatic dry fruits (cypselae), each with a distinct basal attachment area (carpopodium) and 30–35 persistent bristles in the pappus. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
