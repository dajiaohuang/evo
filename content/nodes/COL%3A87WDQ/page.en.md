## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-215aef2ea7}
COL26.8 records Chelodina rugosa as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-131d0a52b6}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Kennett et al. 1993. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Kennett et al. 1993. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Kennett et al. 1993.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-8fdfdc75f3}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 12. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Kennett et al. 1993. The recorded egg length is 20 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Kennett et al. 1993. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (XX/XY). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-9891c3d360}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
