## Pouteria exstaminodia Pires & T.D.Penn. {#s-2619840318}
## Five-part flowers and unknown fruit details

Pouteria exstaminodia has young branchlets with soft hairs and no lenticels. Its alternate, spiral leaves have cylindrical petioles and eucamptodromous veins, which fade before reaching the margin. Axillary clusters bear flowers with five sepals and a tubular, five-lobed corolla; the lobes have entire margins and blunt tips. The filaments are smooth, and the ovary has five chambers. Fruit and seed characters are recorded as unknown.
