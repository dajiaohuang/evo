## Pereskia grandifolia {#s-493de9ccac}
## Large leaves and more than ten flowers per cyme

The source records straight spines and leaves that are elliptic, oblong or lanceolate, with pointed to tapering tips. Their lateral veins meet the midrib at about 40–60°. Cyme inflorescences carry more than ten flowers. The pink-to-violet flowers have a wheel-shaped corolla; receptacle areoles lack bristles, and staminal bristles are absent. The fruit is turbinate, and more than five seeds are recorded. Habitat labels are rocky and terrestrial, without a locality.
