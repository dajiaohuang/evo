## An angular lower midrib and a one-chambered ovary {#s-100f1ee3ae}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic, elliptic-oblong or oval-elliptic leaves with acute tips and bases that are acute or narrowed. The upper midrib is grooved; the lower midrib is raised and angular, while the veins are conspicuous. Flower clusters grow on branches, with one or two branches per cluster, two or three internodes per axis and three, four or five or more flowers per axis. Bracts and bracteoles fall as flowers open. The flower stalk is at least 3.1 mm long. Flowers are five-part, with sepals free in bud and blunt or rounded tips. Petals are yellow. Glands sit behind and opposite the anther sacs and are up to 0.6 mm long. The tail-shaped stamens are listed as 1.6 mm or longer, and the ovary has one chamber. The separate habitat field records “terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## A terrestrial label with no locality {#s-c8b4f1fb19}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not say whether the material is wild, cultivated, managed or fossil. This label cannot be used as a distribution map.
