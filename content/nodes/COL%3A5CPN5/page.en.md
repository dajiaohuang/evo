## *Xyris lutescens*: hairs and ridges on the flower stalk {#s-6722a40538}
Its flattened leaves are striated or finely cross-wrinkled, with rough or ciliate edges. The 20–70 cm flower stalk has ridges bearing hairs or small papillae. The recorded head shapes range from egg-shaped and cylindrical to nearly spherical or ellipsoid; heads carry four to six sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers.

The evidence records free sepals and categorizes the plant as terrestrial.
