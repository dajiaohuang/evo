## Growth and flowers {#s-43ffff1633}
The regional account describes a rhizomatous perennial 20–30 cm tall that forms cushions, with rounded stems. Its leaves are narrow, firm, and linear to sword-shaped. The flowers are blue and have an undivided style. The spathes are translucent with closely fringed, rust-brown margins; the capsules are oblong and have three wings.

These traits come from a regional South African account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Diagnostic features {#s-685de6a3d9}
A 2020 diagnostic account highlights the regularly fringed spathes and floral bracts, a minutely notched style, and relatively long, narrowly winged capsules up to 15 mm long.
