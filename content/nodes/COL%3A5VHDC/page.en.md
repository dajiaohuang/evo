## Anthurium gomesianum: morphology and two habitat labels {#s-2c1ea4be2a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and a fibrous base. Petioles are grooved, smooth and rounded on the lower side, but the upper margin is coded as both obtuse and acute in the compound field. Leaf blades are entire and flat, linear or elliptic, with apiculate or acute tips and acute bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary-vein wording also combines alternative states; secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, elliptic spathe at an acute angle to it; the spathe is not stipitate. The berry is described as green.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Mixed vein and petiole states remain unresolved.

The dataset gives two habitat labels: “Rupícola” (rock-dwelling) and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These regional habitat categories give no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
