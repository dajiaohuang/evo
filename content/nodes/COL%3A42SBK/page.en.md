## Overview {#s-93c7ae8264}
The 2007 northern and northwestern revision included Danfoss's mouse lemur among three newly recognized lineages. The authors used regional mitochondrial samples and morphometrics and described the reddish lineages as having small inter-river distributions under their model; this is not a re-survey of current boundaries.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-31b9fdf346}
The original type locality is a forest fragment near Ambarijeby, between Analalava and Antsohihy. It identifies the record behind the name but cannot establish a full range or population size.

## Classification {#s-7d7b9ea30f}
The study compared it with other northern reddish mouse lemurs rather than treating one pelage color as a species-level diagnosis. MDD currently displays Ambarijeby Mouse Lemur; the original study also uses Danfoss's Mouse Lemur.
