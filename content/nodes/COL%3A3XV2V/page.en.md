## What traits does the record list for Manihot carthagenensis? {#s-fbc2a2d0fb}
The matrix gives alternatives rather than one fixed form. Roots may be elongated or round tubers, with no xylopodium. Stems range from 1–2.9 m to over 3 m and may be smooth, hairless, climbing, or prostrate; latex is white. Leaves are simple, petiolate, and may be glabrous or lobed, with oval or obovate lobes; stipules are oval or triangular, persistent, and hairless. Terminal bisexual inflorescences may be racemes or panicles, with female flowers at the base. Their bracts are bristle-like, threadlike, hairless, and about 0.1 cm wide; male buds are oval and hairless, female buds pyramidal and hairless. The capsule is oval or round, smooth, hairless, and unribbed. Seeds are oblong and smooth with a caruncle.
