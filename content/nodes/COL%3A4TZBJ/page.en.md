## Ecological evidence {#s-6de311097a}
A study of the captive Jersey Zoo colony compared fecal microbiomes of pied tamarins with recurrent diarrhea and weight loss against unaffected animals. The 16S analysis included six affected and 13 unaffected animals; some Lactobacillus sequence variants were relatively more abundant in the affected group, while some Lachnospiraceae and Ruminococcaceae variants were higher in controls. The affected sample was smaller, and age and lifetime antibiotic exposure differed, so the study does not show causation or describe wild microbiomes.

## Evidence limits {#s-98e1908cd4}
Source-linked introduction;. Observed differences in a captive sample do not show that microbes caused illness or substitute for field research.
