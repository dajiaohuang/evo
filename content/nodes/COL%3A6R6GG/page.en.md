## Stems, leaves, and fruit {#s-90960bc826}
The field describes a simple or clustered stem 5–11 cm across; plant height is listed as 2.5–6 m. It records six to twenty leaves, regularly divided into narrow segments with few folds. Many trichomes—fine hairlike structures—cover the upper surface of those segments. Fruit is 3.0–4.5 cm across, covered in long pyramidal projections, with a brown outer layer; the field does not give its overall shape.
