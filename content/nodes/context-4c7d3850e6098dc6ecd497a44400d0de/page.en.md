## Anf1519 and the recorded concept {#s-8e7a3eedb9}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf1519 names Dendrobates nubeculosus and records Dendrobates, Dendrobatidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-69ab1081d6}
Its maximum adult body length is 24.5 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## Development category — a larval stage {#s-5d8f28a446}
The row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
