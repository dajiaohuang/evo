## Rosettes on a slender woody rhizome {#s-555798937a}
The description records a perennial herb with several leaf rosettes on a slender woody rhizome. Woolly flowering stems rise 15–30 cm and bear single or clustered heads; the outer bracts are glossy white with brown tips, while the flowers are yellow. The habitat note places it in short turf, often on stony or rocky slopes.
