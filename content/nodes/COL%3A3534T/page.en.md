## ## Lilac flowers and hooked-hair loments {#s-712eca8ac2}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium and erect, cylindrical branches with velvety hairs. The alternate leaves have three oblanceolate to elliptic leaflets and looping side veins. Pseudoracemes bear lilac flowers; the androecium is pseudo-monadelphous, with the stamens partly joined. The fruit is a loment, a segmented legume; its rounded articles have a central constriction and hooked hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
