## Styrax macrophyllus Schott {#s-c0f43a4193}
## Rust-colored scales beneath the leaves

Styrax macrophyllus has branches covered in small scales that range from yellow to rusty in color. Its papery, ovate leaves have entire to slightly rolled margins; the upper surface is smooth and the underside woolly or scaly, with rusty scales. The yellow petals meet edge-to-edge in the bud, and each flower has ten stamens. The obovoid drupe is covered in star-shaped woolly, silky or scaly hairs, usually yellowish.
