## Leaves {#s-3be9aeaaf9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes persistent stipules and an even-pinnate leaf with one pair of leaflets. It also marks the leaf rachis as absent; that sits uneasily with the paired-leaflet classification, so both fields are reported as entered rather than reconciled. Leaflets are listed as slightly to strongly sickle-shaped, elliptic, lanceolate, or oblong-lanceolate. Their bases are asymmetrical and their tips acuminate or caudate-acuminate. The lower surface is recorded as lacking punctations.

## Flowers, fruit and seeds {#s-3da7904584}
The inflorescence is a raceme whose axis bears hairs. The hypanthium is described as hairless or hairy. The calyx is listed with four or five sepals, either free or with a pair joined. Petals are described as clawed, ear-shaped, or obovate. The source records three stamens, hairy filaments, and a hairy ovary. The fruit is a pod; seeds are listed as ovate or rounded. These alternatives are retained as recorded and are not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-6d41781370}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
