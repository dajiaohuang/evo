## Mitrophyllum abbreviatum {#s-ef2c95fbb0}
## Two unlike leaf pairs form a swollen leaf body

Klak’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a densely branched succulent shrub up to 40 cm tall. Its two successive leaf pairs differ in form. The first pair has free, tongue-shaped to triangular parts, 10–30 by 5–8 mm. The second pair is joined along about four-fifths of its length to make a rounded or oval swollen body, thicker than the stem that bears it.

The flowers have white petals suffused with pink. The fruit stalk is not corky at its base. The habitat account places the plant in crevices of quartzitic rock.
