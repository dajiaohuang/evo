## The maximum excludes the tail {#s-6b8cb5ac32}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length of 52 mm and a female maximum of 45.6 mm. Snout–vent length measures from the snout to the vent, leaving out the tail. These compiled maxima are not an average adult size or a growth limit. They may come from different localities or studies, so their difference alone cannot quantify size variation between sexes in a population.

## What these records cannot establish {#s-f5d08ecacb}
One row compresses differences in sex, age, locality, and population, and the workbook’s 2023 name framework may not match later taxonomic concepts. A reported maximum is not the species’ absolute size limit. These fields do not establish diet, clutch size, body mass, juvenile size, venom, conservation status, current distribution, or population trends. The source labels preserve the workbook’s citation chain, not an independent reassessment of every underlying study.
