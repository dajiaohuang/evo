## Rolled leaf edges and two fruit forms {#s-f934c0344e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or lance-shaped, papery leaves with wedge-shaped bases and acute tips. Their margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles. Their supporting bracts and smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles persist. The flower cup is smooth and hairless; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. Fruit is listed as round or ellipsoid and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. Seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial label alongside fruit alternatives {#s-a1c7179b03}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category is separate from the listed fruit alternatives.
