## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-02e75c6edd}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Hyperolius bocagei* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hyperoliidae (family) → Hyperolius (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.

## Where adults were reported foraging {#s-a716afe08c}
The AmphiBIO compilation uses the historical name Hyperolius bocagei under a Frost 2011 classification. That source concept has not been shown to match the current COL26.8 species concept exactly. The compilation records terrestrial; aquatic; arboreal as present categories. These are broad foraging categories; the compilation does not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries copied from earlier compilations. The affected row is not identified in this record, so no replacement value can be assigned. This caveat does not show that every earlier entry is wrong.

## Reported adult size values {#s-deac0f9f7d}
The AmphiBIO compilation uses the historical name Hyperolius bocagei under a Frost 2011 classification. That source concept has not been shown to match the current COL26.8 species concept exactly. AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 27 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
