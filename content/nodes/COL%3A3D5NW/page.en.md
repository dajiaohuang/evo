## A woolly shrublet with long-stalked yellow heads {#s-ce7480911d}
This account describes a slender, white-woolly shrublet up to 10 cm tall, with narrow, loosely twisted leaves. Yellow radiate heads grow singly on long, slender stalks. The source highlights the woolly young branches, narrow involucral bracts and smooth fruits, which retain a small style-base appendage. It grows on mountain slopes along shale bands at 300–600 m.
