## Aedes (Stegomyia) aegypti {#s-2ce4331beb}
## Three colonies in a host-odor assay

The study compared human odor with guinea-pig odor in a two-port laboratory assay. It used three *Aedes aegypti* control colonies: one described as zoophilic (animal-oriented) and two as anthropophilic (human-oriented). Those labels describe the colonies included in this experiment; the study design does not establish a species-wide host preference or field feeding ecology.
