## Evidence limits {#s-7bec3969bf}
This page summarizes one selected PBDB occurrence. The record and collection counts do not measure specimens or populations. Database age brackets do not directly date biological origin or extinction, and the recorded parent path is distinct from phylogenetic hypotheses.
