## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-7227e0a257}

## A broad sample of living conifers {#s-d550146dae}
The dated phylogeny samples about 80% of living conifer species to compare evolutionary dynamics in the Northern and Southern Hemispheres. This is a large living-species sample, not a census of every conifer lineage that has ever existed.

## Modelled dates and the fossil record {#s-1eb3c5e239}
Fossil calibrations help estimate ages for sampled branches, but those node ages are model results rather than direct dates on every split. The separate 300–0 Ma display is a conservative navigation span; it is not a complete, continuously documented fossil range.
