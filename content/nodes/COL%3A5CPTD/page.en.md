## *Xyris sororia*: blackish sheaths and smooth blades {#s-49e8708a01}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, the leaves spiral around the stem and their sheaths have a blackish base. Blades are flat or nearly cylindrical, smooth and hairless, tapering toward the tip. A small ligule is present but inconspicuous; the profile gives these as regional character states, without measuring population-wide variation.

The cylindrical flowering stalk reaches 20–50 cm and bears one or two ridges. Its obovoid-to-ellipsoid spike has no more than 10 flowers; the seeds are described as spindle-shaped. The associated habitat field labels the plant terrestrial but gives no collection locality or date.
