## Klaprothia fasciculata {#s-bbcb1b5772}
## Small petals and a club-shaped capsule

The source excerpt for Klaprothia fasciculata records petals only 1–2 mm long and 0.5–1 mm wide. Flowers have four to twelve stamens, with three staminodes—sterile stamens—in each series. The capsule is club-shaped. No broader plant description or precise locality is supplied in this entry.
