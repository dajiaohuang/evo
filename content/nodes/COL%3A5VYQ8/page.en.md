## Babiana inclinata {#s-6b684ce361}
Babiana inclinata bears an inclined spike of violet flowers whose dorsal tepals and stamens face toward the spike tip. The regional account distinguishes it from the similar B. angustifolia by the more widely cupped, unequal tepals and longer filaments; in both species the flowers are inverted. It is recorded on stony or clay flats and lower renosterveld slopes. These diagnostic differences and habitat records are from the cited regional treatment.
