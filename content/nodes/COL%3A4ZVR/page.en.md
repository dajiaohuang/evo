## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-344b0bc62a}
COL26.8 records Hydrosaurus at genus rank, with Hydrosaurinae as its direct parent.

COL26.8 recognizes Hydrosaurus as an agamid genus. The ReptTraits v1-2 material represented here names H. amboinensis, H. pustulatus, and H. weberi, following the workbook’s 2023 Reptile Database name framework. Two of those species have reported size values below; the three entries are a selected set, not a complete species count for the genus.

## Compare the measurements within their limits {#s-18dbde4f60}
The compiled maximum snout–vent length is 280 mm for H. pustulatus and 350 mm for H. amboinensis. Snout–vent length runs from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. These are reported values for two different species, not a range of body sizes within one species or the smallest and largest animals in the genus. Other traits need their own field-specific evidence before comparison.

## What the historical life-history records contain {#s-53451bdca2}
The historical life-history compilation has entries for H. amboinensis and H. pustulatus. Both include records of offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, time from egg laying to hatching, and the compilation’s longest-lifespan field. These are separate measures, not proof that every Hydrosaurus species shares a reproductive pattern or lifespan. Their units, event definitions, and source histories matter when comparing them.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-98c920772a}
The trait and life-history entries shown are selected records, not a complete Hydrosaurus catalogue. Reported maximum lengths are compiled values, not average sizes or identification thresholds. The life-history source preserves its own summaries, maximum-lifespan records, and historical species or subspecies assignments; those may not align with every current taxonomic concept. A few records cannot establish that all species share a reproductive mode, lifespan, or activity pattern, and they do not describe current conservation status.
