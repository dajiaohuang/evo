## Overview {#s-c8e78e6bdf}
This reading path follows COL26.8 genus ID S7Y, whose accepted species are lhoesti, preussi and solatus. Earlier studies often used Cercopithecus or Chlorocebus combinations; retain the source names and use COL IDs to connect them to this catalogue.

## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-41037fd648}
Begin with Tosi's sex-chromosome marker comparison of representatives of all three taxa, noting the lhoesti–preussi grouping and solatus in those data. Then read the Kalinzu feeding study for lhoesti. Continue with interviews around Ebo for preussi, separating local ecological knowledge from field abundance surveys. Finish by comparing the 1985–1989 solatus survey with the later compilation of post-1999 records to see how the known range changed as records accumulated.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-fbc2d6a15c}
These evidence types answer different questions: marker comparisons describe relationships among sampled sequences, the Kalinzu study measures local feeding time, Ebo interviews record residents' identification and perceptions, and Gabon range papers compile field observations and local information. They do not share a period or sampling frame and cannot be combined into one genus-wide range, ecology or trend.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-d890b99003}
This is a short reading path anchored to the fixed COL26.8 genus ID, not a complete genus review. Evidence comes from local observations, interviews and limited molecular markers with different periods and sampling frames; it cannot establish genus-wide ecology or current conservation status.
