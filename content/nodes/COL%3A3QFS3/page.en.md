## Ribbon-like stolons and varied flower colours {#s-66a492fdb2}
Ixia sobolifera is 15–70 cm tall and usually branched. Its soft, papery corm tunics (the fibrous outer layers around the underground corm, a swollen storage stem) produce prominent ribbon-like stolons (creeping stems that extend from the plant); flowers are pink, slate-blue or white, and three geographic subspecies are recognised with small morphological differences.

## Mostly clay in renosterveld {#s-2933bac4b7}
The regional flora records mostly clay soils in renosterveld (a Cape shrubland vegetation type).
