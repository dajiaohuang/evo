## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-51d0554201}
COL26.8 records Eunectes notaeus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Boidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-fe38ad15b6}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 4000 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-821d9fda4d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as semi-aquatic (Semi-Aquatic). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Leen 1978, Simon 1992, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Leen 1978, Simon 1992.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-3aa4cf6b68}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 25. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Leen 1978, Simon 1992, De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Leen 1978, Simon 1992. The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-61cb222cff}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
