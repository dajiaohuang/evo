## Cannomois aristata {#s-25a0220fb7}
## Branched tips rise from long rhizomes

Cannomois aristata is a dioecious perennial, with male and female flowers on separate plants, reaching about 1 m. The regional description notes long rhizomes (underground stems), simple culms, and leaf sheaths ending in bristle-like awns up to 10 mm long; the bracts are also pointed. Dry sandy slopes are the recorded habitat.
