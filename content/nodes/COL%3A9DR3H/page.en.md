## Zollernia modesta {#s-27fb0481f0}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zollernia modesta* as an accepted species of *Zollernia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records a rough-barked trunk noted as 15 cm in diameter and leathery, alternate leaves. Racemes carry 6–17 flowers; the petals range from pink to lilac, and the globose fruits contain one to three seeds. Its habitat field labels it terrestrial. The entry is a regional description, not a complete account of the species.
