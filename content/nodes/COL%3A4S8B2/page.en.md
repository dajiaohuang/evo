## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-55bafc3257}
COL26.8 records Rhinoclemmys funerea as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Geoemydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-5eaa2e7835}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 375 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a640496da6}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-e5d0d8c4d0}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3.1. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-1e48fa9198}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
