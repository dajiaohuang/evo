## The source concept {#s-d20796de9f}
In AmphiBIO v1, Hyalinobatrachium iaspidiense appears in row Anf1040, classified in the genus Hyalinobatrachium, family Centrolenidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited record; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-0f4fdb16fd}
For Hyalinobatrachium iaspidiense, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 20 mm. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs and toads (Anura), and total length for salamanders (Caudata) and caecilians (Gymnophiona). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
