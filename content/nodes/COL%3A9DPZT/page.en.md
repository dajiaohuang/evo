## Trischidium alternum {#s-dc2a070c24}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Trischidium alternum* as an accepted species of *Trischidium*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description distinguishes young branches, which are hairy, from older ones that become less hairy or smooth. It also records axillary racemes with white to pale-yellow flowers and 12–16 stamens. The regional description is a single source account, not a survey of variation across the species’ full range.
