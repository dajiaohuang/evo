## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-afb3024f4d}
The source field notes feeding roots and short internodes. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous and the petiole green. The entire blade is ovate or elliptic with an obtuse base; the field also gives “6–9” after the blade descriptors without clearly identifying the feature counted. The axillary sympodial inflorescence has a peduncle shorter than the oblong spathe. The spathe tube is cream outside when young and greenish cream inside; the outer blade is whitish. The spadix is subsessile.
