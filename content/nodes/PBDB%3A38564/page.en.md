## Velociraptor in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-591291a5f3}
PBDB records Velociraptor at genus rank. The selected routes lead to Velociraptor osmolskae and V. mongoliensis, each with its own fossil records. This parent hierarchy classifies database entries; it does not resolve phylogeny or make the selection a complete genus list.

## Selected Velociraptor records and named strata {#s-5a735a53ad}
The selected PBDB route includes records for Velociraptor osmolskae and V. mongoliensis; Bayan Mandahu and Djadokhta are named strata in this set. Compare each occurrence’s age and identification with its source. Occurrence rows and collection identifiers are database units, not specimens or populations.

## Feather attachment on the IGM 100/981 ulna {#s-95370ce828}
IGM 100/981 preserves regularly spaced bony papillae on its ulna, interpreted as quill knobs. They support secondary feathers attached to that forearm. They do not establish powered flight or full-body plumage, and one specimen cannot establish feathering across every species in the genus.

## IGM 100/981’s Campanian interval {#specimen-window}
IGM 100/981 is from the Djadokhta Formation in Mongolia and falls within the 75–71 Ma Campanian specimen interval. This window belongs to this fossil, not the full Velociraptor range, a divergence date, continuous occupancy, or a direct-ancestor claim.
