PBDB records Velociraptor at genus rank. This guide links two selected fossil species pages: V. osmolskae and V. mongoliensis. The recorded parent chain is a database classification; it does not establish phylogeny or complete genus membership.

## IGM 100/981: feather evidence and time window {#s-95370ce828}
Specimen IGM 100/981 preserves regularly spaced bony papillae on the ulna, interpreted as quill knobs. They support attached secondary feathers on that forearm, but do not establish powered flight, full-body plumage or feathering in every Velociraptor species. The associated 75–71 Ma interval is this specimen’s sample window, not the genus-wide range.
