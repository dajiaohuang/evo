## Weights assigned to food descriptions {#s-1282624e26}
EltonTraits assigns 40% to other plant material (including fungi in the source category), 20% to invertebrates, 20% to fruit, and 20% to seeds, nuts and spores. These weights standardize written descriptions; they are not measured shares of food intake. The ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and an estimate may use a related species in the same genus judged ecologically similar. The weights therefore are not all direct observations of Niviventer langbianis. A zero weight would not prove that a food is never eaten.

This page combines two historical sources.
