## Launaea nana {#s-74569196ae}
## Cushion-like rosettes on open grassland

Herman and Retief describe *Launaea nana* as a cushion-shaped perennial herb without an above-ground stem. Its leaves form solitary rosettes. Each head contains 20–25 flowers, yellow or pink. The dry fruits are smooth and 3.4–5.5 mm long; their pappus consists only of bristles. The cited habitat field records open grassland.
