## Overview {#s-7c701de8f8}
Hotaling and colleagues proposed Ganzhorn's mouse lemur in a 2016 multilocus study. Mitochondrial lineages, nuclear population structure and Bayesian species-delimitation tests jointly supported separation of the sample; these results do not exhaustively compare every related population.

## Distribution and habitat {#s-05a9af8722}
The paper records Mandena Forest in the Tolanaro District of southeastern Madagascar as the type locality; the type tissue was collected in 1998. The authors separated this lineage from western M. murinus samples; the taxonomic study does not establish a complete modern range.

## Morphology {#s-f50364950d}
The paper explicitly states that phenotypic measurements were unavailable for the holotype, so it provides no body-size value to generalize across the species. This gap is preserved rather than filled with measurements from relatives.

## Classification {#s-0562a6d854}
The current MDD gray mouse lemur entry includes ganzhorni; COL26.8 retains a separate accepted ID.

## Evidence limits {#s-cf90b07b46}
COL26.8 accepts this name, while the local MDD exact-name crosswalk has no matching record; this page presents only the sample-based taxonomy supported by the 2016 study. No current range survey or conservation assessment is included; the common-name label is an editorial translation. The current MDD page records a broader name treatment; this page follows the pinned COL26.8 ID and presents both treatments.
