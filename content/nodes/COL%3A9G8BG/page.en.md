Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Sporophila angolensis (Linnaeus, 1766) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-269e8d7144}
Mass=13 g with Mass.Source=Dunning is a compiled species-average body mass incorporating both sexes; the 31 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book, weighed individuals, localities and dates were not read, leaving sex, seasonal and regional mass ranges unsupported. Mass and beak length do not establish feeding mechanics.

## Read seed niche and trophic level separately {#s-f81d91d8f4}
Trophic.Niche=Granivore is defined as at least 60% of food resources from seeds or nuts. Trophic.Level=Omnivore means resources from multiple trophic levels in roughly equal proportions. These are separate category definitions, not measured proportions for this species. The Granivore/Omnivore combination is retained without inventing a cause or changing its niche to Omnivore. Underlying diet papers were not read; seed species, seasonal diets, shelling actions and beak bite force remain unsupported.
