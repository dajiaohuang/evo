## Asclepias bracteolata {#s-9c3ef65048}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Asclepias bracteolata* as an accepted species of *Asclepias*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records the habitat category as terrestrial. Because the row gives no collection locality, range, or morphology, it supports only this regional habitat label.
