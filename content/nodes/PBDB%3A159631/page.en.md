## A route across separate whale evidence {#route-boundary}
Cetaceamorpha is used here as a navigation route linking raoellid evidence with sampled Cetacea. The cited synthesis reviews the relationships but does not present this route as one observed lineage, a direct ancestor chain or a dated crown group.

## The fossil-to-living window is a sample {#sample-envelope}
The displayed 53–0 Ma span is an editorial navigation envelope across separately sampled fossils and living whales. It is not a global first or last appearance, a divergence date, continuous occupancy or an origin date for one lineage.
