## A historical life-history entry {#s-e2cab5b435}
COL26.8 accepts Melomys fulgens in the family Muridae. The August 2015 life-history archive has a same-name, same-family entry with three cited fields. Because the compilation may inherit data under historical taxonomy, the matching name does not prove that every value describes the species as classified today.

## An adult-mass summary {#s-e5866720b9}
The archive reports adult body mass of 95.16 g. The summary can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; it is not a range of individual masses. No birth or hatching mass is supplied in this entry.

## Litter size and transition from milk {#s-c02035fd67}
The archive reports 2.08 offspring or eggs per litter or clutch, and 20 days for the age associated with dependence on food other than maternal milk. The first field combines offspring and egg counts, so its fractional summary is not a literal fraction of an animal. The second describes a different event from birth or litter size; these values do not define one uniform reproductive cycle.

## Following the life-history source labels {#s-8d9c32ef8c}
The same-name rows carry source labels including Jones, Bielby, Cardillo and colleagues (2009) and Ernest and colleagues (2003). The archive retains a row locator and source label for each field. A label may point to a paper, book or another database; rows are not counts of measured animals, and repeated citations are not independent experiments.

## A broad foraging-position category {#s-0b80fd832e}
The entry assigns the species to G, the ground category; the source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Archive metadata attributes mammalian stratum labels collectively to Nowak (1999). This is a broad category, not a measure of time spent there, and it does not mean the animal uses no other position.

## An activity label, not a daily schedule {#s-0760e8acfc}
EltonTraits marks nocturnal activity as 1. Several activity fields can be 1 at once, so this code is not a share of a 24-hour budget or a schedule guaranteed across days, seasons and sites. Its ABC confidence code can include a related species in the same genus; no genus- or family-level typical value is used to fill this entry.

## Following the EltonTraits source labels {#s-c20862b584}
The foraging and activity fields carry the labels metadata:Nowak1999 and Ref_1. Different field groups may come from different material, and the same Ref number in the bird and mammal tables does not necessarily identify the same work. Original labels are retained by file, row and field group. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so the labels are trace information rather than additional observations.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-b16295cb4a}
This page combines two historical data sources. The August 2015 life-history archive reports compilation summaries that may inherit data across historical species or subspecies; values are not individual measurements, and the source publications have not been individually reviewed. EltonTraits 1.0 converts older literature descriptions into categorical fields, which may include material from related species. Its diet fields are withheld here under code D1 because of uncertainty, interpolation or unresolved source links; they are not used to fill a conclusion. Neither historical name match establishes equivalence with COL26.8 or provides a complete account of current ecology, range, population or conservation.
