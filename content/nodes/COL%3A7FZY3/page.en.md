## What is recorded about its cylindrical leaves? {#s-9fc3f95d47}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with cylindrical or nearly cylindrical blades, a smooth surface and pointed, sometimes awl-shaped tips. The flower stalk is 20–50 cm long, cylindrical or thread-like, and the obovoid or oblong spike has up to ten flowers. The source field lists alternatives for hairiness and several other traits rather than resolving one state.
