## Wukongopterus: PBDB classification route {#s-b89402fa20}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Wukongopterus as a genus. This fixed reading route links to one species page: Wukongopterus lii. The database’s parent path organizes names for browsing; it is not a claim that every study uses the same classification. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a genus-wide account of anatomy or ecology.

## Member material linked to Wukongopterus {#s-a4d279e04e}
The linked species guide records one eligible occurrence from one collection, with Tiaojishan as the named stratum. Its age bracket and reference help describe that database record. One occurrence and one collection are not counts of individual animals or fossils, and this single locality does not map the genus’ full range.

## Limits of the selected Wukongopterus records {#s-46153b37ab}
This route covers one species page and a selected occurrence record, not a complete account of Wukongopterus’ anatomy, ecology or geographic range. Record counts and broad database ages do not measure population size or directly date the genus’ origin and extinction. PBDB parentage is kept as a catalogue path, separate from Catalogue of Life concepts and phylogenetic hypotheses.
