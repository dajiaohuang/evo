## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-88e6cf703a}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset, branches may be unarmed or armed (spiny) and have cylindrical cross-sections. Stipules are recorded as unknown. Leaflets are narrowly oblong, with obtuse or notched (retuse) tips and wedge-shaped (cuneate) or oblique bases. The source calls their venation brochidodromous: side veins join in loops near the margin. Upper leaflet surfaces are glabrous; lower surfaces are described as becoming nearly hairless or bearing hairs only on the primary veins. The inflorescence is a terminal panicle with leaf-like bracts. Flowers have a bell-shaped calyx, white or yellowish petals, a hairless standard petal and stamens joined into one bundle (monadelphous). The fruit is a samara, a dry winged fruit, with a smooth seed-bearing core. These are source-listed descriptions and alternatives, not a complete account of variation.
