## Crotalaria brachycarpa {#s-d024177a0c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Crotalaria brachycarpa* records plant 0.3–0.5 m tall, prostrate to ascending branches, three-part leaves, yellow flowers, velvety brown pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
