## What the Testudines route does not establish {#s-2c71987bba}
This is a release-pinned navigation route supported by selected life-history and ReptTraits records, not a complete Testudines monograph or descendant list. Existing time-range entries for Cryptodira and Pleurodira lack sufficient provenance here and are not interpreted; the route makes no complete claims about living status, distribution, populations or conservation.

Life-history values come from historical compilations: medians, longest-lifespan records, class-scope definitions and inherited historical taxonomy limit direct comparison. A group route does not imply shared reproductive traits. Where a ReptTraits source row has a scope caution, its trait is withheld from definite prose and remains limited to the relevant species account.
