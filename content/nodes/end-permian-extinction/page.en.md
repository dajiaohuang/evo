## Meishan ash beds time a short extinction pulse {#s-f8c9acc4c0}
At Meishan, radiometric ages on ash beds bracket the main end-Permian fossil losses between 251.941 ± 0.037 and 251.880 ± 0.031 Ma—about 60 ± 48 thousand years in this calibrated section. Fossil-range terminations, a carbon-isotope shift, and volcanic and warming proxies place the losses amid environmental change on land and at sea.

That tight local chronology does not make ecological collapse synchronous worldwide. The evidence points to interacting kill mechanisms, while regional collapse and recovery followed different paths.
