## What parts are visible in the description of this annual herb? {#s-10392cdc35}
Buchnera pusilla is described as a slender annual herb up to 30 cm tall, with wiry stems and narrow leaves. The stem leaves reach 4 cm long and 3 mm wide; the terminal flower spike may reach 15 cm. Flowers are mostly white but may also be blue, pink or purple. The source records four stamens and a capsule enclosed within the calyx; its yellowish seeds are about 1 mm long and longitudinally ridged. The entry gives no population sample.

## What range did the historical flora report, and what part is conditional? {#s-f59f1f17a3}
The 2013 Flora of Panama field reports Buchnera pusilla from Mexico to Ecuador and Brazil. It extends the account to the Antilles and south to Argentina only if B. longifolia is accepted as a synonym. This conditional historical range is from the source version retrieved on 8 September 2026, not a current global inventory.
