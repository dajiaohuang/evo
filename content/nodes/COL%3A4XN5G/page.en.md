## How are flowers and fruit arranged in Siparuna ficoides? {#s-9b718f4c82}
This field describes opposite elliptic to obovate leaves, smooth above and finely hairy below. The plant is monoecious, with male and female flowers on the same plant. Hairy flower clusters bear separate cup-shaped male and rounded female receptacles; the small tepals are greatly reduced, and the female styles are fused. The mature fruiting structure is smooth and does not split open; the small fruit segments lack a style-base appendage.

## What broad habitat is recorded? {#s-6669af5a0a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field categorizes it as terrestrial, without further habitat details.
