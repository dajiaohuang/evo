## Aneura pinguis: three habitat labels {#s-5e636fce7a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records three habitat categories for this taxon:

“Epixila” (wood-associated),

“Rupícola” (rock-associated),

and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These are source labels from three regional dataset rows; none supplies a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
