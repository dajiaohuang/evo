## Myriopus maculatus {#s-b58c2d3ed5}
## Loose terminal flower panicles top leaves that shed hairs

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field reports no lenticels on the stems. The simple, alternate leaves lack stipules, have long petioles, and range from ovate to lance-shaped. Their bases are acute to long-pointed, their tips end in a short point or tail, and their margins are entire. Leaf hairs become sparse or are lost.

Loose panicles grow at the stem tips. Their five-part, bisexual flowers are radially symmetrical and hairy. The calyx has a very short fused tube and short lance-shaped lobes; the green corolla also has fused parts. The linear anthers are held together, the style is prominent, and the short, rounded stigma has a distinct terminal ring. The fruit is a hairless drupe: green when immature, then yellow to orange, and faintly four-lobed. The embryo is curved.
