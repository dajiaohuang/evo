## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Quiina blackii? {#s-69d007b95b}
Two pointed interpetiolar stipules occur between opposite leaves. Petioles are 1.1–2 cm long. The papery elliptic or elliptic-obovate blades reach 20 cm, are hairless, taper gradually at the base and have pointed tips; 20 pairs of raised side veins are recorded. The inflorescence bears ten male and ten bisexual flowers. Flowers have four sepals and four petals; the ovary has two chambers and two styles. The ellipsoid-to-obovoid fruit is 2.5–4.5 cm.
