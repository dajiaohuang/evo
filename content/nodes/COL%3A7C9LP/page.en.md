## Thuidium urceolatum: four habitat entries {#s-777efd4380}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records “corticícola” (bark-associated) for *Thuidium urceolatum*.

Its dataset entries also include “epixila,” the wood-associated category.

Another entry is “rupícola,” the rock-associated category.

The fourth is “terrícola,” the terrestrial category.

These are habitat labels in a regional dataset, not a survey of how common each setting is. No collection locality, survey date or complete species range is supplied.
