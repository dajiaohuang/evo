## Tatelt ash contains the studied Protolenus sample {#s-d0d4def1f0}
The studied *Protolenus (Hupeolenus) sp.* specimens come from volcanic ash in Morocco’s Tatelt Formation, Cambrian Series 2, Stage 4, in the Lemdad Syncline. The dossier’s 515–514 Ma formation envelope describes those scanned specimens, not the genus’s global first appearance, origin or full range.
