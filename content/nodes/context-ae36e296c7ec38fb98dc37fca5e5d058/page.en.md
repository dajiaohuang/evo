## Which classification concept does this record use? {#s-6462540809}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates brunneus* in row Anf0018, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. It follows the source concept used by *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011); its correspondence to the current COL26.8 species concept has not been established. The traits below describe this deposited record.

## Recorded foraging settings {#s-dcb7dd6b16}
AmphiBIO lists terrestrial and aquatic foraging settings. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or developmental changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some microhabitat entries propagated from earlier amphibian compilations, but a row-level comparison for this record is unavailable. The original categories remain historical values; no replacements are inferred.

## Time of activity {#s-77d1b33699}
AmphiBIO lists daytime and twilight as activity categories. These broad labels are not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Reported adult maximum length {#s-b6a7422ec3}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult length of 24 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This is a literature-compilation maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Clutch count and annual reproduction {#s-e57f9db22a}
AmphiBIO lists 17 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, and records at most one reproductive event per year. These are compiled literature counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Larval stage {#s-5818a411d0}
AmphiBIO marks this record as having a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
