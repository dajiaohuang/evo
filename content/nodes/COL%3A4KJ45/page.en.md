## What fruit and flower traits are recorded for Plukenetia polyadenia? {#s-6f3fa2a9b5}
The plant has woody, twining stems. Its papery to slightly leathery leaves are elliptic or oval, with broadly blunt or rounded bases, nearly entire margins, three main veins, and small stipels; scattered leaf glands are absent. Unisexual racemes bear narrow oblong to ellipsoid buds. The stamens have distinct, non-dimorphic filaments, and the styles are partly joined and 3–7 mm long. The subglobose fruit exceeds 6 cm across, has fleshy, keeled segments, and does not split open. Seeds are ovate with a rough surface.
