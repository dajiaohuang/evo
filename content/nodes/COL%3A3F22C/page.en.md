## ## A long raceme-like cluster and two stamen bundles {#s-927432144d}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records erect stems and pinnate leaves with three leaflets. The elongated pseudoraceme is a flower cluster with a raceme-like arrangement. Its flowers have two bundles of stamens, and the standard petal is glabrous. The same dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial; this regional character list gives no locality or population coverage.
