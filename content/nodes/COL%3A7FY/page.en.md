## Foraging codes in AmphiBIO {#s-7f6acf7a2b}
AmphiBIO contains 558 records classified under Bufonidae in its Frost 2011 scheme. Positive foraging codes occur in 19 fossorial, 487 terrestrial, 273 aquatic and 57 arboreal records. A single row may carry more than one code, and missing values remain unknown. These counts describe the historical records represented in the compilation, not the share of all living bufonids with each habit.

## Developmental codes in the same records {#s-661b2aeaba}
Among that same historical subset, 24 records code direct development, 415 code larval development and 14 code viviparity. They are literature-compiled records, not independent population samples; an unreported code cannot be treated as an absent trait. The Frost 2011 source classification also does not define the boundaries of every current COL26.8 member.

## What the thermal studies measured {#s-e33e69c3d8}
The curated AmphiTherm release matches the name Bufonidae to 55 deposited source concepts and 885 estimate rows. Its reported study coverage is Tpref (thermal preference): 172 rows across 20 concepts; CTmax (critical upper limit): 511 across 46; CTmin (critical lower limit): 197 across 36; and LT50_hot (a lethal-limit assay): 5 across 2. These endpoints come from different experiments, and overlapping concepts mean the counts do not add to unique species or animals. Recorded life stages are adults, larvae, juveniles and metamorphs. This is coverage in a source classification dated 2018, not a physiological profile of every current family member.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-d31ba6d740}
The catalogue count, AmphiBIO rows and thermal estimates use different classifications and denominators. Their historical samples do not describe every current bufonid or establish a shared family-wide trait profile.
