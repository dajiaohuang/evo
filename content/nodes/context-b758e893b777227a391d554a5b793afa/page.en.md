## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-bc4690043c}
For Ceratophrys ornata, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 112.4 mm. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs and toads (Anura), and total length for salamanders (Caudata) and caecilians (Gymnophiona). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
