## Multiple Medog localities {#s-4ea77f85d8}
Male holotype CIB 201706MT02 came from Beibeng Village, Medog, Xizang, at 870 m. The monograph examined additional males from Xirang, Didong and the Medog urban neighbourhood. Combining its material with the original description, it summarized known Medog localities at 670–1780 m. This is a reported set of localities rather than a measured physiological range or a demonstration that the entire elevation interval is occupied.

## New and historical measurements remain distinct {#s-4bb4a82c6d}
The diagnosis cited Shi and colleagues (2020) for twelve male snout–vent lengths of 23.8–29.1 mm and two females at 27.9–31.3 mm. Its own eight examined males measured 26.2–30.3 mm. These samples remain separate, including the newly examined maximum beyond the earlier male range. The diagnosis described a weak vomerine ridge without teeth, no subarticular tubercles, and rudimentary basal toe webbing with narrow fringes. Breeding males had a subgular vocal sac without nuptial pads or spines. These are diagnostic observations, not population size limits or a breeding calendar.
