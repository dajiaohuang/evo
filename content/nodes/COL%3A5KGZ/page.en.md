## Nests at one managed field site {#s-0b80f50ad6}
At a 16-hectare rice-field site in central Thailand, the study recorded 74 clutches of 3–9 eggs and a local nesting season from early December to early April. Of the 74 nests, 52 were within 5 m of water and 22 were farther away. The abstract and methods give different monitoring dates, so those dates are not combined here.

## A minimum inferred from genetics and nest timing {#s-21ef9281f7}
From 71 clutches and six adult females, the authors found seven mitochondrial cytochrome-b haplotypes. Together with newly formed nests found on the same night, and assuming one clutch per female per night, this supported an estimate of at least ten reproducing females at the site. It is a conditional minimum for one 16-hectare area, not a direct count or a genus-wide pattern.
