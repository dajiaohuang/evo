## Askidiosperma delicatulum {#s-70cbeda7b8}
## One spikelet in the female inflorescence

The regional account describes compact tufts 0.3–0.6 m tall, without rhizomes or stolons, and with unbranched, solid cylindrical stems. Male inflorescences carry 1–5 spikelets; the female inflorescence has one, and its ovary has three chambers. The account distinguishes the species from A. rugosum by spikelet shape. It records deeper valley sands and seeps as habitat, and says fire may kill plants that then re-establish from seed.
