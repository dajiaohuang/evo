## *Costus guanaiensis*: unbranched stems and green bracts {#s-f99e05b8a5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records unbranched stems and ovate to inversely egg-shaped leaves. The leaf-sheath may be hairless or hairy, and the ligule at the blade-sheath junction has a squared-off tip. Leaf-like appendages occur on the flower bracts; their outer surfaces are green and inner surfaces green or red. The bracts are leathery. The corolla and labellum are listed as white or yellow, and the corolla may be hairless or hairy. The substrate category is “terrestrial.”
