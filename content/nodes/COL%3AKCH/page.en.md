## Scope of the historical route summaries {#s-038180c4d3}
The life-history route covers same-name, same-family historical entries with at least three source-supported focal fields; it does not represent every accepted descendant. Compilation medians, maximum-lifespan records and inherited historical taxonomy retain their limits. The route does not imply that descendants share one reproductive mode or life history.

The ecological routes summarize separate historical sets: one has at least two usable field groups, and another has exactly one. Semiquantitative conversion, historical taxonomy and possible congeneric borrowing in ABC fields limit interpretation.
