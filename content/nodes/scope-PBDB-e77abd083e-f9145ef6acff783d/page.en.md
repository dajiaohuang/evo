## H-GSP 18507: bones behind the amphibious model {#s-2253f1a0d6}
Holotype H-GSP 18507 of *Ambulocetus natans* preserves a partial skull and vertebral column, plus substantial fore- and hind-limb material. The limbs could bear weight. Researchers used the combined vertebral and hind-limb anatomy to compare movement on land and in water; no gait or swimming performance was observed.

## Swimming and land movement are reconstructions {#s-980aa0b006}
The study infers amphibious movement from anatomy and formation context. Teeth and skull support an aquatic-predation interpretation, but no prey item is directly documented. ## Limits of the partial holotype {#s-7c464bacfa}
These interpretations rest on the partial holotype from the Kuldana Formation in Pakistan. The cited material does not define a full body-size range, exact swimming style, land performance or a genus-wide feeding pattern.
