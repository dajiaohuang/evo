## A soft-haired, mat-forming perennial {#s-ccf30c36ed}
The regional description records C. pachyrrhiza as a perennial herb that forms mats. The leaves and flowering stems have soft, spreading white hairs, and the narrow linear leaves are about 3 mm wide. Flowers are described as maroon or cerise.

## Quartzite ledges in mountain areas {#s-93e6e2afed}
The habitat field records montane areas and quartzite ledges. It gives no precise collection locality.
