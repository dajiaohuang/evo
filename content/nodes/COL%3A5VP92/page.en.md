## Aponogeton distachyos: a Cape flora account {#s-2fed573aa9}
The 2012 Cape flora account describes this aquatic, rhizomatous plant with floating, oblong leaves. Its white, scented flowers are arranged in two rows and have one tepal and 8–16 stamens.
