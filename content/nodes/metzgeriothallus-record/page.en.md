## A Devonian liverwort body fossil {#s-c0dbccb7d1}
Givetian strata in eastern New York preserve Metzgeriothallus sharonae thalli with a central costa and branching, plus an associated sporophyte capsule. The material is a Middle Devonian liverwort record, not a date for the origin of crown Marchantiophyta.

## What the shale lenses preserve {#s-3315ddf2ee}
Polarized-light study describes bifurcating thalli with a medial costa—a thicker central strand—and an associated sporophyte capsule. A gametophyte is the life-cycle stage that produces gametes; the sporophyte produces spores. The parts are associated in the fossil record, but the study does not show an anatomically connected whole plant.

## An occurrence, not an origin date {#s-882928d717}
The Givetian specimens from eastern New York provide a Middle Devonian body-fossil record assigned to Metzgeriothallus sharonae. They do not date the crown divergence of Marchantiophyta or prove that liverworts were absent from older rocks. The described morphology diagnoses this material and should not be generalized to every liverwort lineage.
