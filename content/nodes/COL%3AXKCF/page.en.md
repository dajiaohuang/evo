## Conchocarpus paniculatus {#s-81e493373f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records acute or notched leaf tips, a laxly branched inflorescence and glabrous anthers.

## A lax inflorescence and cream-white corolla {#s-5d9cbdc59a}
Leaves are alternate and simple or one-foliolate, with slightly expanded petiole tips and acute, retuse or emarginate blade tips. Inflorescences are solitary, generally over 7 cm long and between leafy branches; evergreen leaf-like bracts are absent, and branching is lax. Sepals persist. Flowers have two fertile stamens and a radially symmetric cream-white corolla; anthers are free and glabrous. The ovary is pubescent and the style connate. The fruit apex is rounded. A separate ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
