## ## Head-like flower clusters and woody fruit valves {#s-d0fb47c87a}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with lenticels and fine, short hairs. The deciduous stipules are linear or oblong; the petiole and main leaf axis are cylindrical. Each leaf has two pairs of leaflets and stalkless or stalked nectaries shaped like a saucer or cup. Head-like inflorescences bear stalkless flowers; the calyx is tubular or cup-shaped. The flat or curved fruit has woody valves and fine, short hairs. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
