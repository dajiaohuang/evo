## The holotype and a matrix-dependent placement {#holotype-and-placement}
Bi et al. (2018) described near-complete holotype STM33-5 from the Lujiatun Unit of the Yixian Formation in Liaoning, China, within an approximately 126–125 Ma interval. Their phylogenetic analysis places Ambolestes among eutherians, but that higher placement depends on the character matrix. The specimen interval is not a secure global genus range, and the placement does not make it a direct placental ancestor.
