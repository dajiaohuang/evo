## What traits are recorded for Oxandra sphaerocarpa? {#s-38af57f8ff}
The field describes papery, narrow elliptic to egg-shaped leaves with pointed or tapering tips and pointed or blunt bases. Flower buds are ellipsoid, the fruit segments are globose, and the seeds range from ellipsoid to egg-shaped.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-19807fcf21}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific site.
