## A slender plant with small magenta flowers {#s-9385d09157}
Ixia tenuis has a slender 180–350 mm stem and usually three narrow leaves, 1.5–3.0 mm wide. Its flowers are small, with tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 8–9 × 4–5 mm; the account distinguishes it from I. scillaris by its smaller corm (a swollen underground storage stem), finer fibres and narrower leaves.

## Restricted Piketberg sandstone sites {#s-d21b0a8de0}
The flora reports only a handful of sites in rocky sandstone habitat in the Piketberg Mountains, mostly at 650–800 m. It describes the species as rare and says it merits Vulnerable (VU) status, while noting no imminent threat; that is the cited account’s assessment.
