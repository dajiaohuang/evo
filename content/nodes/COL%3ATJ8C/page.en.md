## Leaflet range and axillary flowers {#s-c1b34f2d56}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile records spirally arranged leaves with two to six pairs of elliptic to narrow elliptic leaflets; seven pairs are rare. Flowers occur in leafy clusters beside the leaves. Their yellow petals differ strongly in shape, and ten stamens are recorded. The oblong pods are 2.5–3 cm long and about 0.8 cm wide; the entry describes them as sticky and finely bristled, darkening as they mature. Seed form is marked unknown.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-c43f5f8946}
The same flora entry labels the habitat terrestrial. This is a habitat category in the record, rather than a collection site or a complete range.
