## Distribution records {#s-79d110806c}
The supplement lists one observation at Paranaíta, Mato Grosso, and one vocalization record at Nova Bandeirantes. These records document occurrence at survey sites but do not provide a complete range or population estimate.

## Evidence limits {#s-fa84ec4606}
The Paranaíta observation and Nova Bandeirantes vocalization are separate site records; they do not show occupancy between sites or population density. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation.
