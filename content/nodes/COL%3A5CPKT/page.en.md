## *Xyris hatschhachii*: dark sheaths and a densely flowered spike {#s-1f05e46185}
Its leaves spiral around the plant; the sheaths are brown-red to nearly black, and the flat blades may be smooth or finely wrinkled. The stalk is 20–50 cm long. The ovoid to ellipsoid spike bears six to nine sterile bracts and at least ten flowers; the seeds range from elliptical to spindle-shaped.
