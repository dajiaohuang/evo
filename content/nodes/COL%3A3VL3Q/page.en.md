## Lithops divergens {#s-7873cec747}
## Wedge-shaped paired leaves with frosted grey-green surfaces

Hammer’s 2013 Greater Cape flora describes a dwarf succulent with a double-wedge shape, usually bearing two to four heads. Its leaf pairs gape widely and are slightly asymmetric. Viewed from above, they are elliptic; the somewhat convex upper surfaces range from opaque to translucent and are pale grey-green, compared in the account to frosted glass or amethyst.

The flower is yellow with a white centre and 15–30 mm across. The habitat account records shale flats covered with quartzite and calcrete.
