## One reported maximum does not define the genus {#s-82bb73ed2f}
The Mantheyus phuwuanensis entry reports a maximum snout–vent length of 90 mm. This measurement runs from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. A single species record cannot show the size range across Mantheyus, the typical size of an individual, or an upper limit for the genus; comparable size support is missing or unresolved for other descendants in this material.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-237148376b}
The available trait material is a selected set of descendants, not a complete Mantheyus species list. Its 90 mm figure is a reported maximum for M. phuwuanensis, not an average or an identification threshold for the genus. The workbook’s one-row summaries and 2023 name framework do not resolve later taxonomic changes or differences among populations. The source record and its field definitions remain important when interpreting each value.
