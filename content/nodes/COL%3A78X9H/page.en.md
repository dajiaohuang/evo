## A creeping rhizome with radiate flower heads {#s-05a9844752}
The record describes prostrate, rhizomatous stems and upright rosettes of narrow, spoon-shaped leaves. The leaves have toothed edges and faint veins. Branched clusters carry radiate heads with about ten white ray florets, sometimes marked purple underneath, and 30–58 white disk florets. The fruits bear a single row of numerous bristles joined at the base. The habitat field says terrestrial.
