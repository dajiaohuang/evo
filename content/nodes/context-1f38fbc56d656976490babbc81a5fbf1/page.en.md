## Record identity and classification {#s-4264880408}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf4938 records Odorrana indeprensa under Odorrana, Ranidae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental category {#s-b1e5d7e0bc}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists larval stage as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
