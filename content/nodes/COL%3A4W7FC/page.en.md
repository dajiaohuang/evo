## *Sebaea minutissima* {#s-4c844fef2d}
## A minute herb with flowers in short clusters

The SANBI/WFO morphology field describes an erect annual only 3–35 mm tall. Its few pairs of leaves are separated by prominent stem gaps (internodes); each blade is rhombic to broadly elliptical or nearly round, 2.5–7.0 × 2.5–5.0 mm. Flowers occur in clusters of 1–3. The filaments attach between the corolla lobes, the anthers have a gland at the tip, and the style may swell below the stigma or around its middle. The yellow corolla tube is 3–4 mm long.
