## Bidens pilosa {#s-a5a4aaf2b4}
## Opposite leaflets and barbed fruit awns

The description records an erect, branched annual herb up to 1.5 m tall. Its opposite leaves usually have three leaflets, sometimes five; the ovate leaflets are toothed and hairy on both surfaces. Several loose flower heads usually form at the tips. The heads may be radiate or discoid, and the white or yellow rays may be absent. The black, linear achenes are 15–22 mm long and bear two or three stiff awns with backward-pointing barbs. The distribution account calls the species native to the Caribbean and widespread as a weed in tropical and warm-temperate regions.
