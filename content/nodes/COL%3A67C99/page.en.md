## Anthochortus graminifolius {#s-3c258c12ff}
## Mat-forming stems and damp seep habitat

The SANBI regional account describes a dioecious perennial—male and female flowers occur on separate plants—that forms mats and produces stolons, or horizontal stems. Its culms (stems) are branched and compressed; the green leaf sheaths often have large, free blades. The same account places it on damp slopes and around seeps. These details belong to that regional account and do not establish variation across the full range.
