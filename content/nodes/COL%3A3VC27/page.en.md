## What does Lippia rehmannii look like? {#s-7c08f0d594}
This shrub reaches 0.5–1.1 m. Adult leaves are elliptic and no longer than 25 mm, with scalloped or toothed edges. The calyx is nearly flat-topped or has two very short rounded lobes; the bracts are under 4 mm. Flowers are white or cream-colored.

## Where is it found? {#s-7616fd39b3}
The habitat account lists rocky hillsides, dry Sandveld, and roadsides. These are the reported settings, not a complete range.
