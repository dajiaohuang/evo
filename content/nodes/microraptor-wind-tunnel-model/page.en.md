## What wind-tunnel tests say about Microraptor’s glide {#s-aaf257ad4a}
A scaled physical model based on published Microraptor proportions was tested with different wing and hindlimb configurations. Measured forces showed stable gliding with high lift and drag, while performance changed little among tested hindlimb arrangements. This is evidence about model aerodynamics, not direct observation of posture, launch or flight path; reconstructed geometry and feather shape remain assumptions.
