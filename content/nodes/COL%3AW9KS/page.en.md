## A woody twiner with three leaflets {#s-c39adc59a1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes a woody twiner whose leaves are divided into three leaflets. Its axillary flowers are arranged in pseudo-racemes, clusters along a shared axis. Closed flowers are recorded as absent; petal color and fruit and seed form are marked unknown. The stamens are described as pseudomonadelphous, appearing joined into one group. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
