## Harrisia tortuosa {#s-d1b477a131}
## A climbing stem with wavy ribs and colored fruit

Brazilian Flora 2020 records a climbing stem with six to ten wavy ribs. The floral tube is described as ribbed or bearing triangular bracts, and the fruit is yellow or red. The habitat field says terrestrial; it does not identify a locality or show how widely these traits were surveyed.
