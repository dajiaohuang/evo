## *Stevia involucrata*: sessile leaves and clustered heads {#s-097ef2a64c}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records opposite leaves without stalks and dense, corymb-like clusters in the leaf axils. The flower heads may be sessile or stalked. Their fruitlets bear bristly hairs, and the pappus has awns. The regional dataset gives only the broad substrate category “terrestrial.”
