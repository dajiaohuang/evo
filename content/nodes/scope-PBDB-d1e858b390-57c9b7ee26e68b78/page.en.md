## Eighty-five specimens and a reinterpreted central column {#s-a155eddc51}
Ou et al. describe 85 additional Chengjiang specimens plus type material and reconstruct Xianguangia as a tentaculate suspension feeder with stem-cnidarian affinity. Zhao et al. reinterpret the column-like feature as tentacle-sheath complexes and place Dinomischidae on the ctenophore stem in their main Bayesian analysis; alternative coding yields an unresolved polytomy. The body's reconstruction and its evolutionary placement remain contested.

## Competing cnidarian-stem and ctenophore-stem placements {#s-32e5e52ebc}
Ou et al. use the expanded sample to support a stem-cnidarian interpretation. Zhao et al. reinterpret the central structure and recover Xianguangia, Daihua, and Dinomischus as Dinomischidae on the ctenophore stem in their main Bayesian analysis; sensitivity analyses produce an unresolved polytomy. Neither placement is definitive, and the Chengjiang sample is not a global first appearance.
