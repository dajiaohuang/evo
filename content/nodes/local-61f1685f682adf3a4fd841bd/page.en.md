## The source tadpole concept {#s-f69318d977}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Mannophryne trujillensis, in the genus Mannophryne and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying work is also Mannophryne trujillensis. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-6609b2119d}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Its stage field is NA, so no developmental stage is supplied. The record count is not a count of unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
