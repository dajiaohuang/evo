## Accepted name and placement {#s-6fd4a06589}
The COL26.8 release of 20 August 2026 accepts *Eleutherodactylus beguei* Díaz & Hedges, 2015 as a species in Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family) and *Eleutherodactylus* (genus). This is the name, rank and placement recorded in the pinned catalogue.

## Two rows in a morphology archive {#s-fd493643d5}
Amphibian morphology v3 lists two measurement rows for this name, Anura.csv rows 1654 and 1655. The source codes label one M (male category) and one F (female category). They record SVL (snout–vent length) 13.1 and 15.2, head length (HL) 4.8 and 5.3, and head width (HW) 4.8 and 5.3, respectively; 15 additional trait fields are populated in each row. The source family field says Brachycephaloidea, whereas COL26.8 places the name in Eleutherodactylidae. Units and mapping to the current COL species concept remain unresolved. Row count does not identify unique frogs, and the sex-coded entries are not a diagnosis or a species-wide range.
