This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-04f9116a75}
For Anf0768, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2ce3e26030}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 38 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
