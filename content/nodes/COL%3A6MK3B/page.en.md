## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-c6b6b0f147}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis melanocephalus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-bf58892d72}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1230 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-9e0b86ca5f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2007.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-3dc88b12da}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 7.25. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Iverson et al. 1993.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-6754f127f9}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
