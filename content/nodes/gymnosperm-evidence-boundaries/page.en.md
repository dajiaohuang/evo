## Six boundaries in gymnosperm deep time {#s-3c7b5329a8}
A Ginkgo-like wood, several fossil-calibrated and biogeographic models, and a tree of living gymnosperms illuminate different boundaries. Read them as separate windows, not as one timeline for the origin of every living lineage.

## One Jurassic wood specimen {#s-261aa00bb8}
The Jurassic specimen PB22285 preserves a combination of Ginkgo-like wood characters in strata dated 165–153 Ma. Wood can identify anatomical resemblance, but an isolated organ cannot reveal the whole plant or establish the first appearance of Ginkgophyta.

## A crown age moves with calibration {#s-0a9734c36e}
A plastome-clock analysis using fossil and tectonic constraints places the crown of living Cycadaceae earlier than a Miocene-only account would suggest. Alternative calibration schemes move the estimate substantially, so the crown age belongs to the model and its assumptions.

## Fossils change the map {#s-b6bbc80cb3}
When fossil leaves are added to a species-level cycad model, inferred past ranges extend farther in latitude and the number of inferred local extirpations rises. Living distributions alone omit those former populations and range shifts.

## A famous young-radiation model {#s-e18de3ec1b}
A 2011 analysis proposed that most sampled living cycad species were younger than about 12 Ma. That result remains an important hypothesis about sampled living species, but later fossil-integrated analyses prevent it from serving as the age of the entire order Cycadales.

## A relative hemispheric pattern {#s-18ab998a3d}
In one time tree of living conifers, sampled Southern Hemisphere lineages have older median nodes than sampled Northern Hemisphere lineages. Those medians describe that tree’s pattern; they are not the origin dates or fossil ranges of the families.

## Topology is not a timeline {#s-5adf69781a}
A broad nuclear dataset groups cycads with Ginkgo and recovers a Gnetales–Pinaceae relationship among living gymnosperm families. Conflicting gene trees and the absence of extinct lineages from a living-only sample limit how far that topology can be extended through deep time.
