## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b2e7ab555d}
COL26.8 records Sphenomorphus rufus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-0c516fe581}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 60 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-eec8baa985}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f5eae4c245}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1.9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
