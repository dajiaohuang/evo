## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-ef356ca6a7}
The stems bear yellowish hairs: a mixture of star-shaped, gland-tipped and longer simple hairs; papillae are absent. The leaves are simple, sometimes weakly three-lobed, not shield-shaped at the petiole attachment, broadly ovate with heart-shaped bases and toothed edges. Upright flowers grow singly or in pairs. The cup-shaped calyx has raised central and side veins but no papillae; its lobes taper to long points. The petals are white. The fruit has 15–16 segments (mericarps), with no tip-awn; the dorsal seams are papillose.
