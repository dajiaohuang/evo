## A sparsely leafy shrub with short purple racemes {#s-d9a0d77135}
The regional account describes a sparsely leafy shrub to 60 cm tall. Its leathery leaves are linear to elliptic and usually shortly ciliate. Short terminal, umbel-like racemes carry purple flowers. The side petals are unequally two-lobed, with a linear, elongate lower lobe; the keel crest is reduced and short. The habitat entry records rocky sandstone and clay slopes, without defining a full range.
