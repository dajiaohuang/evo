## Babiana sinuata {#s-3ff9a856c1}
Babiana sinuata is readily recognized by its twisted, broadly undulate leaves with crisped margins and fine hairs along the upper edges. The regional account describes pale blue-mauve flowers and unusually long, arched stamens whose anthers are joined together, a feature it identifies as unique in the genus. It contrasts this with B. striata, whose anthers remain separate. Rocky shale slopes are the recorded habitat; the comparison is from the cited regional treatment.
