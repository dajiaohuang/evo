## *Noticastrum gnaphalioides*: flower heads in a raceme {#s-0f08c56f54}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records flower heads in a raceme, an elongated cluster with stalked heads arranged along an axis. The involucre, the cup of bracts around each head, is bell-shaped, and the bracts taper toward their tips. The marginal florets are reduced and remain inside the involucre rather than projecting beyond it. These are morphology fields in a regional dataset, not a complete species-wide description.
