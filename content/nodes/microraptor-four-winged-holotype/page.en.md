## Microraptor’s holotype preserves feathers on four limbs {#s-8a96bfe1ce}
The Microraptor holotype IVPP V13352 preserves long, asymmetrical pennaceous feathers along both forelimbs and hindlimbs, as well as the tail. Their attachment documents a four-winged anatomical arrangement. The fossil does not record a flight path or establish how the feathers worked aerodynamically, and this individual is not a demonstrated direct ancestor of birds.
