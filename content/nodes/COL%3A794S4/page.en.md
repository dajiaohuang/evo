## Accepted name and placement {#s-60837fd7e6}
COL26.8 accepts Raorchestes ravii Zachariah, Dinesh, Kunhikrishnan, Das, Raju, Radhakrishnan, Palot & Kalesh, 2011 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Rhacophoridae (family) → Raorchestes (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Topotypic specimens clarify an uncertain historical label {#s-80325c4d2a}
Ravii was originally described from two specimens at Naduvattam in the Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu, reported at 1,890 m elevation. The 2021 authors collected two additional topotypic specimens matching the description and type. They distinguished these from older samples labelled aff. ravii or aff. coonoorensis whose localities were unknown and whose assignments differed between papers. The new samples showed 1–1.5% mitochondrial 16S divergence from the older aff. ravii material and 1–2.4% from chromasynchysi. These comparisons do not certify every historical label as a modern ravii record or establish a synonymy. Unknown localities cannot be converted into additional distribution points.

## Re-examination found structures absent from the original account {#s-e02de214ff}
The 2021 type examination supported placement in the chromasynchysi group through a combination including male snout–vent length 20–23 mm, pointed snout, dorsal horny spinules, ridges between the eyes and along the back, tongue papillae and a golden-brown iris not divided into lighter upper and darker lower halves. The authors specifically reported a ridge and tongue papillae on the holotype even though the original description had stated they were absent. This is a documented disagreement between examinations, not justification to erase either account. Variable brown morphs and dorsal X or H marks in chromasynchysi also make colour-only identification unreliable.

## A qualitative call account is not a measured spectrum {#s-7ec287f2a5}
The taxonomic remarks cited the original description’s comparison of the advertisement call to bell-like notes or falling water drops. The 2021 authors interpreted that account as potentially representing a pulsatile call with widely spaced pulses, consistent with the chromasynchysi group. This interpretation is not a new ravii recording, measured pulse interval or numerical dominant frequency. Group-level call properties and measurements of related species cannot fill those missing observations. The qualitative comparison can explain the authors’ grouping argument while remaining separate from direct acoustic measurements.

## A separate table provides temporal measurements {#s-548480069d}
Beyond the qualitative historical call analogy in the taxonomic remarks, Table 2 gives a ravii pulsatile-call summary: duration 499.2 ms, rise time 483.1 ms, fall time 16.1 ms, three widely spaced pulses and pulse rate 4.2 per second, delivered without call groups. Its dominant-frequency cell is blank, so a numerical frequency is not inferred from other group members. Calling height is listed at 0.5–5 m. These table measurements and the original-description analogy are different evidence accounts; neither resolves every uncertain historical specimen label. The table does not establish the full repertoire, environmental variation or an invariant call for all populations.
