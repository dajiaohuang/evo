## Trifolium incarnatum {#s-df9cd49fd2}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Trifolium incarnatum* as an accepted species of *Trifolium*. In Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147, the recorded plant grows upright and has trifoliolate leaves (each leaf has three leaflets). Its inflorescences bear more than 20 red flowers. The source labels its habitat terrestrial; this regional field is not a locality record or a complete range description.
