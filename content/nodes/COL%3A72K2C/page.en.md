## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-885c76ffb0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes hairy stems and opposite leaves, sometimes arranged in whorls of three. The leathery leaves are obovate or spoon-shaped, with glandular or woolly hairs on both surfaces. Rounded flower heads have spirally arranged, papery bracts. The bisexual flowers have a tubular calyx and a salver-shaped corolla, white, pink, or lilac.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-0eee52e0f8}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
