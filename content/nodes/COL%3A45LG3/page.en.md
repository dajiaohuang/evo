## Namaquanula bruce-bayeri {#s-c6dcbb64c7}
Namaquanula bruce-bayeri is a small bulbous geophyte with two or three firm, fleshy leaves that curve near the tips. Its spreading cluster bears pale-pink, nearly translucent funnel-shaped flowers with projecting stamens. The regional account records alluvial gravel plains as habitat; it does not give a sampling frame.
