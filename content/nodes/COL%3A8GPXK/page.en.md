## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-1e8bfdc130}
COL26.8 records Limaformosa capensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Lamprophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e0c3e992d8}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1750 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-9332203b47}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bates et al. 2014. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-cb5706f73f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-06f2a261fa}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
