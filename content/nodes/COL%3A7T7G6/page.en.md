## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-751d13a124}
Dinopium everetti is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Picidae. This page compares the same-name historical life-history entry using 3 focal fields, each supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows with matching name and family. Reported summaries can still include historical taxonomic inheritance; a matching row name does not remove that influence.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-7bc73c20cd}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 76.78 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-fd152a4b47}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 2.5 offspring/eggs. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Missing reproductive and developmental durations are not supplied here.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-4c6d215a12}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 10.5 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-f63933f0f1}
Same-name sparse rows carry source labels including Dunning, 1992; Boback and Guyer, 2003; Bennett, 1986; Boyer, A.G., Cartron, J-L.E., and J.H. Brown, 2009, among others. This batch’s archive retains field-specific raw row numbers, values and labels, plus the final table’s complete reference explanations. A label may represent a primary paper, book or other database. Row counts are not numbers of measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-aa2d06aa0e}
The reported life-history fields come from historical same-name records in the 2015 archive. Mass and clutch summaries use medians or means of the two middle records, while maximum longevity uses the largest record found. These values are not measurements of one individual, ranges of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation may share or inherit values across historical species and subspecies. A matching name and family, together with same-name sparse rows, supports the association here but does not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove inherited components from the summaries. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and fields absent from this account need other sources.
