## Baccharis ramboi {#s-c89448eaf0}
## Winged branches and oblong leaves

This species has winged branches and developed leaves with a short petiole. The oblong blades have pinnate venation and entire margins. Flower heads are listed in several arrangements: spikes, dense clusters or solitary axillary heads. Their florets are recorded as either all tubular and male or all threadlike and female. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
