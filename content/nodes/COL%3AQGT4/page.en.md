## Campylocentrum ornithorrhynchum {#s-e174d90bd6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records cylindrical roots with a granular surface and narrow, cylindrical leaves arranged in two ranks. Its three-lobed lip has a triangular middle lobe; a straight to slightly curved spur follows the flower. The fruit is smooth and ellipsoid, and the habitat field lists the orchid as epiphytic.
