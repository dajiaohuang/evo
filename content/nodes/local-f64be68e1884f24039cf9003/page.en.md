## The deposited caecilian record {#s-fad0fe9e6d}
In CaecilianTraits version 4, Caecilia tenuissima is listed in the genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. The compilation keeps individual, population and species records separate. It has not yet been established whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept.

## Measurement material {#s-4d52bc058d}
The compilation lists one measurement record for this source concept, at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. One database row is not a population estimate or proof that the record represents a unique specimen. The row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-4e7b8d4446}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are recorded categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-369607e5af}
At species level, the compilation gives a primary-fold count of 186. This value may summarize records rather than describe one animal, so it does not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic count.
