## Life-history entries and their fields {#s-827dd0de57}
Five historical life-history accounts are linked here: Yuhina castaniceps, Y. everetti, Y. flavicollis, Y. humilis and Y. torqueola. Each has entries for adult body mass, offspring per litter or eggs per clutch, and the interval from egg-laying to hatching. The repeated field counts describe the available accounts, not all accepted Yuhina species. Units, event definitions and source history matter before values are compared.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-e829ce3775}
The life-history values are historical compilations, not a uniform set of newly measured individuals; some are medians or maximum-lifespan records. Their shared labels do not imply that every species has the same reproductive mode or life history. The old names and two one-to-one crosswalks help associate records with the current genus, but do not prove that historical and present species concepts are identical.
