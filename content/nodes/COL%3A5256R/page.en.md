## Stenospermation ulei {#s-d269782678}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records a stem up to 200 cm high. Leaf blades are 12–17 cm long and 5–6.2 cm wide; the petiole-to-blade length ratio is 1.3–1.5. The flower-bearing spike is 4.5–5 cm long and nodding at flowering, on a 40–45 cm peduncle. The habitat fields list hemiepiphytic and rock substrates; the source is regional, not a population survey.
