## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-0f17871487}

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=22 mm {#s-99677b1b97}
Body_size_mm=22 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-5817326997}
