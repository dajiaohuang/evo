## Limits of this historical ecology record {#s-9c4511e76d}
EltonTraits 1.0 provides a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete species account; its concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse literature-derived weights, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity fields may rely on an ecologically similar congener, so not every entry is a direct observation of this species. Genus/family typical values, avian strata without species-level support, and split records needing individual review do not establish definite conclusions. Underlying works have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation need other sources.
