## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9ab252d58f}
COL26.8 records Atractus melanogaster as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e4ec3719b5}
