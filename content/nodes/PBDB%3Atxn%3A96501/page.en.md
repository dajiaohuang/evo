## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-470b5f3608}
This page brings together guides to 43 species listed under Ornithocheiroidea (superfamily) in the fixed working list from the Paleobiology Database (PBDB). The parent path organizes names for browsing; it does not, by itself, show how the species are related.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-155308090e}
The guide begins with Cearadactylus atrox, Liaoningopterus gui, Lonchodectes machaerorhynchus, Lonchodectes platystomus, Lonchodectes compressirostris. Its selected PBDB entries contain 59 occurrences across 38 collections; represented strata include Romualdo, Jiufotang, West Melbury Marly Chalk, Gault. Each collection keeps its own age bracket and identification labels; these records are a database sample, not a complete species distribution or a measure of prehistoric population size.

## Evidence limits {#s-1036c8367e}
This route follows selected entries from the fixed 276-name PBDB working list, not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological account. PBDB parent paths organize records but do not substitute for evidence about evolutionary relationships. Occurrence totals do not count specimens or populations, and collection age bounds are not exact origin or extinction dates.
