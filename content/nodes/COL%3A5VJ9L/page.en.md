## Anthurium ribeiroi: morphology and terrestrial habitat {#s-93a581f3e8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem with cataphylls and prophylls recorded as fibrous along it. Petioles are flattened, with a sharp upper margin and rounded lower side, and are marked smooth. Leaf blades are entire and flat, ovate, with apiculate or acute tips and acute or truncate bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary-vein description combines alternative states; secondary veins are obscure or impressed above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical or multiple-keeled peduncle. Its flat spathe is linear or ovate, with an obtuse or acute angle to the peduncle; the spathe is not stipitate. Berries are described as green.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The mixed vein states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
