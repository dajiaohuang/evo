## Dimorphodontidae: PBDB classification route {#s-bb524b3e23}
Dimorphodontidae is recorded as a family in this PBDB hierarchy. This guide links four roster species—Peteinosaurus zambelli, Dimorphodon weintraubi, Dimorphodon macronyx and Caelestiventus hanseni—through database parent paths; the route is a source classification, not a tested family phylogeny. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a group-wide account of shared anatomy or ecology.

## Member material linked to Dimorphodontidae {#s-51e236ca6d}
The selected records total five occurrences from five collections. Recorded strata include Zorzino Limestone, La Boca, Blue Lias and Nugget Sandstone. Each linked species page carries its collection-specific locality, age bracket and reference attribution; occurrence and collection counts do not measure ancient populations.

## Limits of the selected Dimorphodontidae records {#s-7b6bfab76a}
This family route covers selected PBDB occurrences for four roster species, not every dimorphodontid or a shared ecological profile. Database parentage does not settle evolutionary relationships, and selected occurrence counts and outer age bounds do not estimate abundance, origin or extinction.
