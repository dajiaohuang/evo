## Winged stem segments and a two-lipped flower cup {#s-1c9d9a62b8}
The flora record describes an erect plant 2–3 m tall, with broad wing-like edges along the stem segments. Its simple leaves have elliptic to obovate blades and no leaf stalk. Loose flower clusters grow at branch tips or opposite leaves, with persistent threadlike bracts. The yellow flowers have a two-lipped outer floral cup, or calyx. The obovate black pod is hairless, and the seeds are ochre-brown. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
