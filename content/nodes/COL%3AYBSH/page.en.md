## Cordia tarodae {#s-7735684ec0}
## Woolly stems, white flowers, and yellow fruits

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes woolly stem hairs and lance-shaped to ovate leaves with wedge-shaped bases and acute tips. Both leaf surfaces have short, stiff, appressed hairs. The bisexual flowers are white. Their ribbed, tubular calyces are under 1 cm long; the tubular corollas are 1–1.9 cm. The anthers lack glands, the stigma is threadlike, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is yellow, elliptic, and hairless.
