## Name and source {#s-288b043fcb}
COL26.8 places *Bitis peringueyi* in Viperidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 row uses the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and provides separate citations for its length and microhabitat fields. The row compiles reports rather than describing every population uniformly.

## Two length measures {#s-f80b18084a}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 230 mm and a maximum total length (TL) of 330 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent; TL includes the tail. These are compiled maxima, not a growth limit or typical individual size. The two values need not describe the same individual, and sex-specific records from different places or studies do not by themselves measure sexual dimorphism within a population.

## Microhabitat {#s-9067d8c691}
The row records the microhabitat as Terrestrial, or ground-associated. This category is not a complete habitat account, and the field does not describe variation among populations, seasons or life stages.

## Reproductive mode {#s-78c8da5913}
The cited reproductive-mode field is labelled viviparous, or live-bearing. The row gives no litter-size summary, so this label does not establish a typical count, fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-31f5796578}
The row can combine reports that differ by sex, age, locality or population. Its Reptile Database 2023 name framework does not by itself prove equivalence to the COL26.8 concept. Neither length maximum is an absolute species limit, and the two maxima do not necessarily describe one turtle. Mass fields may be estimated from length equations, and juvenile-length definitions differ; neither is presented here as a measurement. This account does not update venom, conservation status, current distribution or population trends. Field citations preserve the source chain but do not mean that every underlying work was independently checked here.
