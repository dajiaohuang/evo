## What stem and flower structures are recorded for Euphorbia sabulicola? {#s-bd1b13cb76}
The cylindrical stems are pubescent. Leaves are opposite, with palm-like veins, interpetiolar leaf-like stipules, and margins ranging from irregularly toothed to toothed. Green bracts surround solitary or paired cyathia at lower and upper nodes. The involucre is smooth and bell-shaped, with four or more transversely elliptic glands; each has a spreading, petal-like appendage of the same size. Female flowers lack a pseudoperianth, the ovary is hairless, and the style is two-lobed. The fruit is hairless, and seeds lack a caruncle.
