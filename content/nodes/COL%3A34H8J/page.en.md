## A thick succulent base with straw-colored flowers {#s-7549919edd}
This account describes an erect succulent shrub up to 30 cm tall, with a thick basal stem that may be 4.5 cm across. The stems are glossy yellowish brown. Bright-green, nearly cylindrical leaves spread from upright positions and are 15–25 mm long. Solitary flowers are up to 25 mm across, with straw-colored petals. Stony ground is listed as habitat.
