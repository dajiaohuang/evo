## Source classification and concept status {#s-85d72f4af1}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2471 records Megastomatohyla mixomaculata under the source classification Megastomatohyla (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-3893c2dbfd}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 36.6 mm for this row. The source field Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
