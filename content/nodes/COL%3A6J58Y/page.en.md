## Two substrate categories in a regional record {#s-98b8249c6b}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens brevipes Besch. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its two habitat terms are source categories.

The first is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

The second is “Epífita,” or epiphytic, recorded as growing on another plant as a support.

Neither field gives a collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

These two source categories are the only biological content included here; they do not make a full account of the species.
