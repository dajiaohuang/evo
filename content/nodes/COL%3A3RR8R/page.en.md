## What stem, flower and seed traits are recorded for Lacistema krukovii? {#s-5f282e910f}
The unarmed cylindrical trunk has smooth or slightly ridged bark; branches are also cylindrical and hairless. Leaves have narrow, hairless stipules and thick, grooved stalks. Their leathery, oblong-elliptic blades are hairless above and pressed-hairy below, with a tapering tip, wedge-shaped base and wavy margins that may roll under slightly. Each leaf axil bears one or two spikes, sometimes three. Flowers have four sepals and one stamen with a hairless filament and round, hairless anther. The cup-shaped floral disc has a margin that may be deeply or slightly wavy. The ovary is round and hairless; its short style divides into three at the tip. The capsule ranges from nearly spherical to three-angled or obovate. Each ovate seed has a thin aril and a hard, crust-like coat.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-776d9244a7}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
