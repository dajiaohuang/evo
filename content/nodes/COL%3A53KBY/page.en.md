## Syagrus santosii {#s-d82b6d2696}
The Syagrus santosii record describes a solitary aerial stem with conspicuous leaf scars. Leaf-sheath and petiole margins are spiny; the pinnate leaflets are rigid and evenly arranged. Its branched inflorescence hangs between the leaves, and the ovoid fruit is yellow-green to yellow with a lightly hairy surface. The regional dataset gives no locality.
