## Chrysolaena propinqua {#s-9a82b0752f}
## Basal rosettes grow from a developed woody base

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a developed xylopodium, a woody underground stem. Basal rosette leaves range from ovate to oblanceolate, with narrowed bases and blunt or rounded tips. They are hairy, from bristly to softly hairy. Stems are sparsely branched near the flower clusters and range from finely pubescent to woolly.

Cymose clusters carry sessile heads with cylindrical to narrowly bell-shaped involucres. Their outer bracts are as long as or longer than the involucre. The corolla, style and anthers bear glandular hairs. The top-shaped fruits are silky and glandular. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
