## Delosperma purpureum {#s-ff647cf74f}
## Dumpling-shaped leaf cells and deep purple petals

Hartmann’s 2009 revision describes a woody taproot up to 12 cm long, with clustered stems arising at its tip. Erect stems reach 9 cm. Linear leaves have smooth, dome-like bladder cells, sometimes with a small central knob; a diagnostic passage likens these cells to low dumplings. The leaves are shiny green but, as the account notes, do not sparkle in sunlight.

Raised flower clusters persist as dry skeletons into the next season. The flowers are deep purple, with 20–25 petals and about 45 stamens. The habitat account describes grassy rocky ground with humus-rich pockets near rocks; for this species it specifically records pockets in conglomerate.
