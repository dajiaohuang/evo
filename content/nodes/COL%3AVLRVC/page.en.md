## Leaf shape and flower structures {#s-dfb41a328b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes the leaflets as mostly oval-lance-shaped with pointed tips, and the inflorescences as crowded. It records three to five stamens, sterile stamens alongside them, and a densely silky ovary. These are the recorded traits in this regional entry; it does not give a broader account of variation or ecology.
