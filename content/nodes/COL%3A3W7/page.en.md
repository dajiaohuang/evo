## Life-history fields need separate definitions {#s-c08ba2b502}
The life-history route links entries for *Aotus azarae*, *A. lemurinus*, *A. trivirgatus*, *A. vociferans* and *Alouatta caraya*. Adult mass, offspring per litter or clutch, maximum lifespan and the interval from conception to birth are distinct fields. Check each event, unit and source before comparing species.

## EltonTraits translates reports into categories {#s-d061556b0d}
The EltonTraits route includes entries such as *Allocebus trichotis*, *Cheirogaleus crossleyi*, *C. major*, *C. medius* and *C. minusculus*. Food weights standardize written descriptions; foraging strata and activity periods use separate categories or codes. These fields summarize literature reports, not a uniform ecology for all primates.

## Historical routes do not define all Primates {#s-22d7acc77e}
PanTHERIA, the 2015 life-history archive and EltonTraits use separate historical name matches, selection rules and variable transformations. Their entries do not establish current species equivalence or a complete order-wide account. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. EltonTraits is semiquantitative, and some mammalian ABC fields may draw on congeners.

The life-history route includes pages for *Callimico goeldii*, *Callithrix flaviceps*, *C. jacchus*, *Leontopithecus rosalia* and *Cebus capucinus*. ## First reproduction and maximum lifespan differ {#s-25b11079aa}
The life-history archive distinguishes gestation, female age at first reproduction and maximum lifespan. First reproduction is not the same event as first birth or sexual maturity; maximum lifespan is a longest collected record, not an average. Source conditions can vary, and a missing field needs other evidence.

## How the historical life-history values were assembled {#s-7bd8b4dd1d}
Species entries use an MSW93/MSW05 name crosswalk, which does not prove equivalence with current concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight; inputs may be published averages, individual values or range midpoints, and the same studies can recur. Adult mass is not interpreted in this route, and gaps are not filled from relatives. Wild, captive and locality conditions were not established for every source.

## Crown-primate clock estimates depend on calibration {#clock-estimate}
A relaxed-clock analysis of 372 sampled species estimates crown Primates at 79.2–70.0 Ma under its selected autocorrelated model and calibration strategy A; another calibration strategy is younger. These are model-sensitive posterior estimates, not fossil occurrences or an exact observation of crown origin.
