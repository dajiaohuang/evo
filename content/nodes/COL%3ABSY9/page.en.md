## Allagoptera brevicalyx {#s-53d04681a3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records adventitious roots and an underground stem covered by persistent leaf sheaths. The sheaths, petioles, and leaf axis are hairy; the leaflets are flexuous or grouped. Cream-colored male and female flowers are three-parted, and the yellow fruit is obovoid with a fibrous middle layer. The habitat field records soil.
