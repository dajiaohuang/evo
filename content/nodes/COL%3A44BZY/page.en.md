## Orange pollen and distinctive inner tepals {#s-3c0c9d2917}
Moraea algoensis has small blue to violet flowers and orange-red pollen. Its inner tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are often dark violet to brown, with a broad, short limb that may be entire or three-lobed.
