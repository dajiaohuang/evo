## The name and this data account {#s-058469a2dd}
COL26.8 accepts Gymnobucco sladeni in Lybiidae. In the HBW–BirdLife 2020 classification used by AVONET v7, the record contains one measured bird and one complete morphological set. The measurements below therefore describe a single source specimen; they cannot show how much individuals vary.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-059212462a}
For that one specimen, AVONET records 14.9 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the bill tip, with width 8.2 mm and depth 10.3 mm measured at the nostril. These standardized dimensions are not a species-wide range or identification threshold.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-fb32b9e7f2}
The recorded unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 87 mm; tarsus length is 20 mm. AVONET lists a mass of 45 g from Updated_literature. That is a separate literature value, not another measurement of the one morphological specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-20f5c803be}
The AVONET fields classify habitat as Forest, trophic niche as Frugivore and lifestyle as Insessorial (the dataset's perching category). These broad labels support comparison across species; they do not mean that every feeding event involves fruit or that the bird uses only forest.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-69d6475859}
AVONET assigns migration code 2, “partially migratory”: its definition covers cases where only part of a population moves long distances, most move shorter distances, or movements are nomadic or altitudinal. The historical breeding/resident map has latitude endpoints -6.83° and 4.99°. Neither the broad movement category nor those endpoints specify an individual route or current occupancy.

## Evidence limits {#s-660c4d3836}
The morphological record rests on one bird, so its measurements provide no estimate of within-species variation. The 45 g mass is separately attributed to Updated_literature. Forest, Frugivore and Insessorial are categorical summaries, and “partially migratory” includes several kinds of movement rather than a documented route. Latitude endpoints summarize historical breeding/resident geography, not a complete present-day range. The historical AVONET name and COL26.8 name are linked here without asserting that their taxonomic concepts are identical; breeding biology, population status and conservation need other sources.
