## Lycophocyon and the early caniform branch {#s-85dc97625e}
The cranium and teeth of holotype UCMP 85202, together with postcranial bones from associated specimens SDSNH 107446 and SDSNH 107447, provide the anatomical sample. A 30-terminal morphology analysis places Lycophocyon near the base of Caniformia, the dog-like branch of carnivorans. Its family assignment remains unsettled, and dietary scores are model-based comparisons rather than evidence of meals or observed hunting.
