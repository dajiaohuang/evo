## Vietnamese records checked with morphology and genetics {#s-46de2451de}
The 2017 assessment confirmed the identity of specimens previously reported from Bu Gia Map and Cat Tien national parks, using morphological and molecular evidence. It included southern Vietnamese records in Dak Lak, Lam Dong, Dong Nai and Binh Phuoc at 200–1500 m. Presence in Dak Nong was anticipated rather than recorded. The article’s earlier Cambodian type-locality information is a cited record, not a new collection by this assessment.

## Two mitochondrial lineages within the study’s species concept {#s-57c70164c5}
The assessment distinguished two mitochondrial subclades with 2.6% sequence divergence. Subclade B included Lam Dong and Dak Lak mountain records and a Dong Nai lowland record; subclade C occurred in Cambodia’s Mondolkiri and neighboring Binh Phuoc, Vietnam, corresponding to O. synoria in the strict sense. These are the article’s mitochondrial groupings, not independently established new species or proof of reproductive isolation.
