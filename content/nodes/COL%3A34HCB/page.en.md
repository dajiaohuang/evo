## Delosperma subincanum {#s-a56e06a480}
## White flowers grow among recurved, three-angled leaves

The Greater Cape flora account describes an erect, branched shrub up to 60 cm tall. Its soft, three-angled leaves are shorter than 25 mm; young leaves have fine papillae and the tips curve backward. Flowers usually grow in groups of three, with white petals. The fruits have five chambers. The habitat record is among karroid shrubs.
