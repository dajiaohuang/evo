## Aloe spicata {#s-22a9cb953f}
Aloe spicata ranges from nearly stemless plants to tree-like forms 1–2 m tall. Its long, channelled leaves support one to five inflorescences; flowers are brownish red in bud and open greenish yellow to nearly white. The regional account records it on varied soils at both high and low elevations, in generally warm areas with variable summer rain.
