## Leaflets, flowers and a single-seeded fruit {#s-0669a69da0}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records no buttress roots. Branches and leaves bear hairs ranging from pubescent to silky; the triangular to narrowly triangular stipules fall off, and the paired stipels are conspicuous and longer than 1.5 mm. Leaflets are oblong, up to 5 cm long, dull above, and have tips recorded as acute, blunt or mucronate (ending in a short point); their bases are rounded or blunt. The panicles are globose. Flowers are 15–20 mm long, with hairs along the ovary margins. The fruit is described as nut-like and hydrochorous (carried by water); its shape is listed as oblong, sickle-shaped or nearly round. Each fruit contains one oblong seed. These are alternatives in one regional row, not a complete or global account.
