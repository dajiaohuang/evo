## Different leaves at different growth stages {#s-8ef9a7eda5}
The regional account describes a perennial aquatic herb up to 1 m tall. It records two leaf forms: submerged leaves disappear early and are absent from mature plants; floating or aerial leaves have smooth, leathery blades that range from elliptic to oblong-ovate, with faint veins and often a discoloured patch where blade meets petiole. Many-flowered spikes are reported from November to March, and the fruits are 4–5 mm long.

## Habitat fields kept distinct {#s-5a58908b4e}
The same regional entry lists “Dry Grassland” and also “Mud banks and deep water, pools, rivers.” These are the source’s recorded habitat phrases; the record does not reconcile them into a single setting.

## Evidence limits {#s-8cb1f49695}
This page follows a regional flora account cited to a 2017 inventory. The November–March flowering period is the account’s statement, not a complete observation series.
