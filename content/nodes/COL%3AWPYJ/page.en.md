## Codonorhiza azurea {#s-c7f8eea720}
Codonorhiza azurea is described in the South African archive as 70–180 mm tall, with a stem bearing five to seven branches and two leaves. Its large, dark-blue to violet flowers are zygomorphic, or bilaterally symmetrical, and marked with darker colour. The account records granite-derived gravel soils and a Western Cape range from Saron to the Cape Peninsula and Stellenbosch. SANBI’s Endangered assessment (2012-11-27; B1ab(i,ii,iii,iv,v)) is a dated national assessment, not a current global status.
