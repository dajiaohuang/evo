## Valeriana ulei Graebn. {#s-30c35236d3}
## Woody stems and variable leaves

Valeriana ulei has woody stems that may be erect or decumbent. Its leaves range from entire to lobed or deeply pinnatifid, and the blades may be elliptic, lance-shaped or oblanceolate. They are sessile or borne on a short pseudopetiole, with a slight color difference between the two surfaces. The inflorescence is described as panicle-like or umbel-like. The species is dioecious and its flowers are hairless. The fruit may be three-angled or winged, with three or five ribs, a rounded or notched tip, and a toothed or ring-shaped calyx; the fruit itself is hairless.
