## Accepted name and placement {#s-8174e1b1d8}
COL26.8 accepts Onychodactylus sillanus Min, Borzée & Poyarkov, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Hynobiidae (family) → Onychodactylus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A non-target amplification result limits eDNA attribution {#s-6bbb2e8f62}
In laboratory specificity tests for the koreanus mitochondrial COI eDNA assay, genomic DNA from sillanus also produced a positive result, with different cycle-threshold values. The authors continued using the assay at Hwanseongul because existing survey and genetic evidence placed sillanus outside that study area; they cited its southeastern Korean distribution. This is assay cross-amplification, not detection of a wild sillanus population in the cave or a demonstration that the two species have identical ecology. A positive signal from this assay at a site where both could occur would require additional identity evidence rather than unqualified assignment to koreanus.
