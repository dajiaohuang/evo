## Hermas lanata {#s-103abf8eec}
## Shaggy leaves and cream-coloured sepals

The SANBI account distinguishes this species by firm, heart-shaped leaves with long, shaggy hairs and cream-coloured sepals. Its involucral bracts, smaller bracts around the flower clusters, and flower stalk are felted; those features are shared with H. intermedia. Another source describes the leaves as shaggy-woolly, often rust-coloured, and the flowers as whitish. The habitat field places it on high sandstone slopes.
