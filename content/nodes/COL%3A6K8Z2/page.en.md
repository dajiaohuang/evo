## *Gamochaeta grazielae*: stem leaves and interrupted flower spikes {#s-0204bcff88}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records stem leaves with two differently coloured surfaces; the upper surface is hairless or nearly so. The blades are spoon-shaped or lance-shaped, with pointed tips and no small hard terminal point. Flower-head clusters form a conspicuously interrupted spike, and the inner bracts have pointed tips. The substrate field gives only the broad category “terrestrial.”
