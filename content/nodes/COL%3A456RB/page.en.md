## A grooved midrib and changing hair cover {#s-648ae06bdc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records smooth cork on the stem. Leaves may be elliptic or oblanceolate (widest above the middle), with acuminate or acute tips and bases that are acuminate, acute, or wedge-shaped. Texture is listed as papery or leathery; the edge is flat. The upper midrib is grooved and the marginal vein inconspicuous. The field describes hairs as pressed flat against the leaf, absent, becoming hairless, or very fine and short. Leaf stalks are grooved. These alternatives belong to one regional field and are not assigned frequencies.

## A closed bud and kidney-shaped or unknown seeds {#s-a720ee5c28}
Inflorescences are listed as paired or solitary; bract status is unknown. The flower cup is described as white and silky or inverted-cone-shaped. Bracteoles are recorded as persistent or round, the sepals as cap-shaped, and the petals as round. Flower buds are closed. Fruit shape and fruit bracteoles are unknown. Seed form is listed as kidney-shaped or unknown.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-1e6a12e8c9}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. No locality, survey date, range coverage, or wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil status is supplied.
