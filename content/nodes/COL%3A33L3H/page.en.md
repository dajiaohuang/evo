## Cyrtanthus montanus: leaves and flowers {#s-4a80ad74c5}
A 2012 regional account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 25 cm and producing bulbils. It records 3–5 strap-shaped leaves that are green at flowering. The 5–10 flowers are erect and red, with a narrow tube that opens into a wide funnel. The stamens project well beyond the flower, and the style is subcapitate.
