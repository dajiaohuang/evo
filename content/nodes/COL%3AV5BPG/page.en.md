## Accepted name and placement {#s-4915fd57ab}
COL26.8 accepts Bufo rubroventromaculatus Orlov, Ananjeva, Ermakov, Lukonina, Ninh & Nguyen, 2024 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Bufonidae (family) → Bufo (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Confirmed and unresolved records {#s-1ae73a68d5}
The 2024 supplementary account confirmed the species from central and northern Vietnam, Wenshan in Yunnan and Baise in Guangxi, China. It explicitly left occurrence in Guizhou uncertain. The confirmed localities and the disputed provincial attribution are kept separate; the article does not establish continuous occupancy between them.
