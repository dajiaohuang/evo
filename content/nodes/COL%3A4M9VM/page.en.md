## Praxelis odontodactyla {#s-6b6a3e8159}
## Whorled leaves and a single long-stalked head

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Upright, grooved stems are smooth or becoming smooth. Whorled, ovate leaves are thin and hairy, with serrated margins and three main veins. The inflorescence is recorded as a single head on a long, nearly smooth stalk; its bell-shaped involucre has overlapping, deciduous bracts and a smooth, conical receptacle.

The lilac florets are bisexual, with five fused anthers and two-lobed styles. The ribbed dry fruits (cypselae) have an asymmetric attachment area and hairs along three or four ribs. A single row of barbed pappus bristles ends in sharp tips. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or complete range is supplied.
