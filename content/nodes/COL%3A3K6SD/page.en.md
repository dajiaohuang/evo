## Large golden heads above woolly rosettes {#s-f4ba5dd494}
The description records an aromatic biennial up to 80 cm tall. Grey-woolly basal leaves contrast with greener stem leaves covered in glandular hairs; large flower heads have glossy bracts, golden inside and pale brown outside. It grows in tall rough grassland and boulder-strewn gullies at about 2,280–2,850 m.
