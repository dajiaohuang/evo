## Pilosocereus flavipulvinatus {#s-bbac9c93a4}
## Curved green flower tubes on an erect branched cactus

The stems branch above the base and carry six to ten ribs; the separate areoles bear conspicuous central spines. Branches are erect and no cephalium is recorded. The flower tube is curved and green. A deep-set tube scar marks the fruit, while the seed coat is rough. The source lists rocky and terrestrial habitats without a named locality or survey coverage.
