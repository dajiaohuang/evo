## The source concept {#s-1c7e1a335f}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Litoria hunti as row Anf2836 under genus Litoria, family Hylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this historical entry has not been matched to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental strategy {#s-e01149926a}
The record indicates a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or reproductive timing.
