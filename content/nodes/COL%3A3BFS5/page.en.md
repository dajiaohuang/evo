## What does the cited account describe for Erythroxylum campestre? {#s-5f75103860}
The account describes a shrub up to 3 m tall, with stout, somewhat flattened young twigs that soon become round and smooth bark. Leaves on short shoots are about 6 cm long and broadly elliptic, stiffly leathery, and up to 6 cm by 4 cm. Their tips have a tiny notch and short point; the midrib is prominent beneath, and there are about seven side veins per side. Stipules are striate, up to 4 mm long, and fall early. Few-flowered clusters bear small flowers; the fruit stalk is about 5 mm long and angled. The account, citing Schulz for the flowers, gives sepals about 1.5 mm long, petals about 4 mm long with ventral appendages, and stamens about 3.5 mm long. The fruit is red, 7–10 mm long, with variable width.
