## Red latex and unknown fruit characters {#s-ddaadbaf17}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records red latex and leaves arranged alternately, one at a time along the branches, and set apart; their bases are flat or slightly rolled backward, and the secondary veins reach the leaf edge. The terminal flower clusters are broad-topped, branching groups described as non-rigid. Sepals, the flower's outer leaf-like parts, are similar in length. The petal lobes, the separated segments at the edge of the fused petals, are shorter than the fused-petal tube, and the ovary, the part that later develops into fruit, is hairless. The source marks all fruit characters as unknown, so this entry does not describe the fruit. It is a regional morphology record without a sampling frame or account of variation.
