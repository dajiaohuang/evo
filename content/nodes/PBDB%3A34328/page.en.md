## A Dunkleosteus model estimates jaw mechanics {#s-0cf4c10360}
A reconstructed *Dunkleosteus terrelli* skull and linkage model yield estimates of jaw movement and bite force. These estimates depend on skull geometry and muscle assumptions; they are not direct observations of behaviour, ecology or a body plan shared by every species in the genus. Two named Upper Devonian species provide a 372.15–358.86 Ma Famennian sample envelope, not an exhaustive global first or last appearance.
