## Syagrus gouveiana {#s-deb8eb49ea}
The Syagrus gouveiana record gives a solitary subterranean or prostrate stem and green pinnate leaves with dark-green, stiff leaflets grouped at different angles. The inflorescence may be spike-like or branched, with smaller branches along one side. Globose fruit has a yellow-green hairy skin and fibrous, juicy mesocarp; habitat is terrestrial.
