## What does the punctuation on its stems look like? {#s-9d623cc96a}
A 2012 regional account describes *Thamnochortus punctatus* as a tufted or rhizomatous (with a horizontal underground stem), dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 1 m. Its simple culms (stems) are finely dotted, and the female spikelets (small flower clusters) reach 20 mm long. The account gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Which sandy settings are listed? {#s-349f6a8f14}
The account lists sandy flats and slopes. This regional habitat note does not establish a complete range.
