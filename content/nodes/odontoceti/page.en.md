## The sampled toothed-whale branch {#sampled-branch}
Odontoceti is treated as the toothed-whale branch of sampled Neoceti. Its early fossil membership and character sequence are reconstructed from separate taxa; this is not one observed lineage or an exact 34 Ma origin event.

## A sampled stem-to-living envelope {#sample-window}
The displayed 34–0 Ma interval summarizes the review’s sampled stem-to-living odontocete evidence. It is not an exact clade origin, global first or last appearance, divergence date, continuous range or direct-ancestor claim.
