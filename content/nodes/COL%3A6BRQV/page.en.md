## Cupania paniculata {#s-d413930f78}
COL26.8 accepts *Cupania paniculata* as a species in *Cupania*. A Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat entry classifies its recorded setting as terrestrial; the field gives no collection site or range.

## A habitat label without a location {#s-39f2d1a180}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 row linked by WFO record wfo-0000629558 records “Terrícola” (terrestrial). This is a habitat or substrate category, not a locality. The entry supplies no collection locality, survey date, or global range.
