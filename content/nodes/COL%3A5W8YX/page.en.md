## Total length {#s-8236af1d9b}
The reported maximum total length (TL) is 750 mm. Total length includes the tail, unlike snout–vent length (SVL). This compiled maximum is the largest value in the cited records, not a growth limit or typical individual size. Sex-specific measurements may come from different places or studies, so they do not by themselves quantify sexual dimorphism within a population.

## Activity and feeding {#s-911cbd6b67}
The row records an Arboreal microhabitat, a Carnivorous diet, Nocturnal activity and ACT foraging mode (active foraging). These are broad categories, not a complete habitat or feeding account or a fixed daily schedule; the diet label does not identify prey. Reports may differ among populations, seasons or life stages.

## Reproductive mode {#s-b2852f453d}
The cited reproductive-mode field is labelled viviparous, or live-bearing. The row does not include a litter-size summary, so the label does not establish a typical count, fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-9e625075be}
The row can combine reports that differ by sex, age, locality or population. Its Reptile Database 2023 name framework does not by itself prove equivalence to the COL26.8 concept. Maximum length is not an absolute species limit. Mass fields may be estimated from length equations, and juvenile-length definitions differ; neither is presented here as a measurement.
