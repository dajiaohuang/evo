## Form in the flora record {#s-59c479728d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem with cataphylls and prophylls fibrous along its length. Petioles are smooth and recorded as cylindrical or flattened, with a rounded upper side. The flat, entire leaf blade is oblong, elliptic, or ovate; its tip is apiculate or blunt, and its base is blunt. Glandular dots are absent. The primary veins are blunt on both surfaces, the secondary veins are indistinct on the upper surface, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is longer than half the leaf length and has a cylindrical peduncle. The flat, ovate spathe forms an obtuse or acute angle with the peduncle. A stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. Berry color is not characterized in the field.

The regional dataset row cites Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
