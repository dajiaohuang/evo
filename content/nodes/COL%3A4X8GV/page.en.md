## What leaves, flowers and fruit are recorded? {#s-6e487b537f}
The erect stems are hairy to woolly, with simple, long, gland-tipped or translucent hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned, symmetrical ovate leaves have subcordate or rounded bases, pointed tips and toothed margins. The upper surface is velvety, with gland-tipped or star-shaped hairs. Crowded flowers grow at stem tips; the sepals are pointed and the corolla cream-colored. The dry fruit splits into five segments (mericarps) without awns; the segments are hairless.
