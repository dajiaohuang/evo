## Accepted name and placement {#s-e7031e7af7}
COL26.8 records Craugastor trachydermus (Campbell, 1994) at species rank within Craugastor, in Craugastoridae, order Brachycephaloidea. The name and placement refer to the release dated 20 August 2026; historical source concepts are described separately below.

## Reading route {#s-ea59089c88}
The current catalogue placement is separate from AmphiBIO's Frost 2011 source taxonomy. The page summarizes only that historical category set.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-52d427111c}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. These broad categories can overlap and do not show how use varies by season or stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat and body-mass data; the affected row has not been identified here.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-b28e4e1ff9}
The historical row records a maximum of one reproductive event per year. This compilation count does not specify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-01d00b2682}
Direct development is flagged in the source row. The category alone does not establish care, breeding site or timing.
