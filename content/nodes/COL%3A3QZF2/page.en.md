## Accepted name and placement {#s-a7fc2dccbb}
COL26.8 accepts Kalophrynus sinensis Peters, 1867 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Kalophrynus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A cited resurrection of the Philippine name {#s-7c9c316893}
The 2016 account attributed to Zug’s 2015 revision the restoration of Kalophrynus sinensis for Philippine populations formerly included in the widespread K. pleurostigma. That revision treated Samar’s acutirostris and Basilan’s stellatus as junior synonyms, while allowing that future genetic evidence could require further name restorations. This is the cited historical taxonomic treatment; it does not independently certify that every island population matches one contemporary species concept.

## Calling while floating in temporary water {#s-f9f6ae8dbf}
The authors described rainy-season encounters with frogs calling while floating in temporary pools or water-filled cavities across differently disturbed habitats. Their specimens occurred in streamside pools, rock depressions and even water in half coconut shells in agricultural areas. These encounter records show use of small water bodies in the surveyed settings; they do not demonstrate egg placement in every container, universal disturbance tolerance or a measured breeding-success rate. The account’s cited Least Concern category is retained as a 2016 statement.
