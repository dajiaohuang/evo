## Spathiphyllum neblinae {#s-c0e4d8804a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field gives petioles under 20 cm and leaf blades more than 3 but less than 6 times as long as wide, with fewer than 9 secondary veins on each face. The peduncle is more than twice the petiole length but under 1.9 times the blade length; the cylindrical spadix (flower-bearing axis) exceeds 1 cm. These regional measurements do not establish population variation.
