## Sampled records preserve source localities and names {#s-b5410b81d2}
A 2017 study reassigned a Congo tissue-only USNM entry from the label *Phrynobatrachus ruthbeateae* to *P. horsti*. Museum records outside the Lower Guinean Rainforest road corridor are not observations from that survey, and a survey non-detection within its bounds does not establish absence across the range.

## Working identification and reference conflicts remain explicit {#s-6fc3de5a55}
The authors placed this tissue entry as sister to sequences KF020537–KF020538 and reported a morphological match to material associated with vouchers MCZ A147865 and MCZ A147891. Because the archived entry is tissue-only, this is the study’s identification comparison, not a re-examination of an intact voucher.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-f71d1224ab}
The identification workflow compared COI and 16S, two mitochondrial DNA markers, with morphology and type-locality context. The authors used neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, while cautioning that it does not recover evolutionary history. Most species were sampled at only one or two localities; GenBank and BOLD searches used a 2016 database snapshot, so reference labels and missing sequences limit the comparison.

## Study counts and identification scope are not population estimates {#s-d35aad1a65}
The paper reports 540 sequenced specimens across its multi-species library, including 524 COI and 528 16S sequences. These are study-wide totals, not counts for *P. horsti*. The methods and tables also differ on several overall totals; those discrepancies remain unresolved and are not population estimates or a census of accepted species.
