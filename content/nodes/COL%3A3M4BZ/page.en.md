## What flower traits distinguish Hirtella glabrata in the record? {#s-6fb8840568}
Young branches are hairy, and the elliptic leaves may be broadly elliptic. The record notes no ant-associated cavities in the leaves. Flowers grow in panicles, with glands on the bracts and stalked glands on the smaller bracteoles. Each flower has six or seven stamens.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-08e623b074}
The dataset records it as terrestrial without a specific locality.
