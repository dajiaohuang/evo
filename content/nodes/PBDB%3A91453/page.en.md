## What the material shows {#s-7bf49c654e}
The single 4.2 cm Famennian holotype AM5750 preserves an oral disc and branchial basket, documenting lamprey-grade anatomy in the total group.

## Feeding {#s-d32c02de07}
The oral disc and teeth do not by themselves establish parasitism; feeding mode is undetermined.

## Setting {#s-31cad67f66}
The fossil comes from the Waterloo Farm depositional setting. The animal’s habitat in life was not directly observed.

## Movement {#s-5c4997b9cc}
Its elongate body supports a swimming interpretation, but locomotor performance was not measured.

## Size {#s-a42138e1db}
The holotype measures 4.2 cm; no broader size range is established.

## Ecological role {#s-88138b133c}
Ecological guild remains unresolved.

## Preserved features {#s-18f3c2f554}
The oral disc is large.

## Preserved features {#s-ad4b932355}
Fourteen teeth ring the mouth.

## Preserved features {#s-6a3fba4e15}
A branchial basket is preserved.

## How to read the evidence {#s-c27167219c}
AM5750 directly records lamprey-grade anatomy, but one fossil does not set the first appearance of crown lampreys or preserve a life cycle or feeding behavior.
