## How large is its rounded flower cluster? {#s-a296819077}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes leaf sheaths 15–24 cm long and leaf blades less than 3 cm wide in *Stegolepis angustata*. Its rounded inflorescence, the full flower cluster, is 3–5 cm across and bears 14–25 spikelets (small flower clusters); the entry notes that 35 is rare. These measurements are from one regional flora record and do not establish variation across populations.

## What habitat label does the record use? {#s-614d5068fd}
The same Brazilian Flora row labels the species as terrestrial. That is a broad habitat category; the field gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
