## *Spigelia spartioides* {#s-ea461b1015}
## A square stem and narrow leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes four-angled, hairless stems whose internodes exceed the leaf-blade length. The nearly stalkless leaves are linear or lance-shaped, with a cut-off base and acute tip; their surface may be hairless or rough, and the venation is recorded as one-veined. Developed flower stalks bear more than 4 flowers. The linear sepals, funnel-shaped white corolla, ovary, and fruit are recorded as hairless; the corolla may have pink or purple spots, and the stamens remain inside it. Seeds are nearly spherical. The field records these alternatives without measuring their frequency.
