## Trichilia schomburgkii {#s-e44278631a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records several leaflet arrangements and base shapes, united staminal filaments, and an ellipsoid or ovoid fruit.

## Variable leaflet arrangement and united filaments {#s-2c6b7f83fb}
The stem-bud field records no cataphylls and simple hairs. Leaves may be odd- or even-pinnate, with an unwinged rachis and alternate, subopposite or opposite leaflets; basal leaflets are heteromorphic. Their bases may be acute, rounded, attenuate or cuneate, and the lower surface hairy or glabrous. Petals are free or united and valvate in bud. Staminal filaments are fully united; the nectary is absent. The ovary may be pilose, pubescent or puberulous, is three-locular, and has two collateral ovules per locule. Fruits are ellipsoid or ovoid, with puberulous or silky indument. The separate ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
