## What unusual structural traits are recorded? {#s-8a61d59734}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records conspicuous rounded or cylindrical stems, with neither rhizomes nor tubers listed. Leaves form a rosette, and each leaf stalk attaches beneath the blade rather than at its edge. Gland-tipped hairs cover the flower cluster and bracts. The flowers are described as resupinate—turned so their apparent upper and lower sides are reversed—and lilac to purple in the tube and lobes.
