## Aedes (Stegomyia) albopictus {#s-7a5a3a34f5}
## Six colonies, one laboratory choice

In a 2023 dual-port olfactometer test—a two-choice device that presents odors through separate ports—each of six F2–F4 *Aedes albopictus* colonies was more likely to choose guinea-pig odor than human odor. The colonies came from Thailand, Cameroon, Florida, New York, Maryland, and Virginia; F2–F4 refers to the second through fourth generations maintained in the laboratory. This result applies to the tested colonies and assay. It does not show what mosquitoes fed on in the field or establish a species-wide host preference.
