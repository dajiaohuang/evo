## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-df846deec5}
S. demersus is grouped with lineages whose effective size increases near the beginning of the Last Glacial Period and declines toward its end, resembling S. humboldti. The authors discuss possible sensitivity of restricted inshore ecology to environmental fluctuations. This ancient reconstruction does not measure present breeding success, food supply or annual abundance trends along African coasts. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
