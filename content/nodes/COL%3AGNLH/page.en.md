## Form and recorded measurements {#s-0403e12cff}
A 1997 regional account describes a perennial herb up to 400 mm tall. It records dark-brown and black capsules 6.0–7.5 mm long, two or more stem leaves, incurved tips on the upper stem leaves, very broadly ovate bracts, and bright-blue flowers.

These traits and measurements come from a regional South African account published in 1997; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Comparison with Aristea torulosa {#s-ce4119be12}
A 2020 diagnostic account considers *A. montana* likely related to *A. torulosa*. Both are described as having dark-green spathes and bracts with broad pale margins that grade irregularly to darker colour and become unevenly fringed with age; the two species can be difficult to distinguish. The account characterizes *A. montana* as a high-mountain species that is relatively short, seldom exceeding 250 mm. It points to the stem in *A. torulosa* as a distinguishing feature: that stem is at most weakly compressed and bears several relatively widely spaced flower clusters, with those at lower nodes sometimes on short branches.

This comparison comes from a regional South African account published in 2020; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
