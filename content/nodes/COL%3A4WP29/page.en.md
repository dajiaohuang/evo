## Leaflet pairs and a head like inflorescence {#s-e1e96aa0e9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical, unarmed branches; prickles were not observed. Lanceolate stipules accompany one sessile, concave petiolar nectary just below the first pair of pinnae; a rachis nectary is present. Leaves have two pairs of pinnae, each with one pair of leaflets. Leaflets are elliptic to falcate, glabrous on both surfaces, with an asymmetric base and obtuse or acuminate tips. The primary vein is central, with hairs at its base. The inflorescence is head like. Flowers are sessile; the tubular calyx and corolla are sericeous, anther tips bear glands and the ovary is glabrous. The fruit is a follicle with orbicular seeds. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
