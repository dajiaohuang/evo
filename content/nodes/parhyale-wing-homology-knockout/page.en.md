## Developmental knockouts test a wing-homology model {#s-eea6466c39}
CRISPR knockouts and gene-expression comparisons in Parhyale hawaiensis and insects test how proximal limb segments correspond with lateral body-wall and wing-related structures. These experiments support a model of serial homology, treating those structures as developmentally corresponding, but they do not preserve the first insect wing or identify one fossil precursor. The result links development in living organisms; it does not by itself reconstruct the ecology or exact historical path of wing origins.
