## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-7672df8fe1}
COL26.8 records Pelusios cupulatta as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pelomedusidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-8c0da2f309}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 214 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-58200aee4a}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2011.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-467ea4162c}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
