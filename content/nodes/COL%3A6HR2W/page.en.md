## An invasive-species concern with limited distribution evidence {#s-ee1ac8a1c5}
The authors proposed Data Deficient rather than the cited 2016 Least Concern category because confirmed distribution and possible effects of introduced Hoplobatrachus rugulosus and Kaloula pulchra were insufficiently known. Their statement that the native frog could be replaced when these species invaded drew on personal observations and cited studies. It is retained as an attributed concern, not a new controlled competition experiment, a quantified decline across the Philippines or a verified current official assessment.
