## The older edge follows a combined-data model {#s-4500e24b8e}
The atlas displays a 58.3–0 Ma Xenarthra navigation route based on O’Leary et al.’s combined phenomic–molecular chronology. Its older edge follows the model’s Riostegotherium entry and ghost-lineage treatment; living xenarthrans extend the route to the present. This is a sampled, model-conditioned bound, not a securely dated global first fossil or crown origin.

## How to read this navigation window {#s-4fb185fd91}
The 58.3 Ma value reflects the cited analysis and its sampling and ghost-lineage assumptions. It does not summarize every fossil occurrence, set an exact crown-group origin, or show continuous occupancy. The rounded atlas value is retained as model context rather than a direct occurrence endpoint.
