## The source concept {#s-11bd55a964}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Ischnocnema lactea in row Anf0296, with genus Ischnocnema, family Brachycephalidae and order Anura. The source says its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a link to a current COL26.8 concept has not been established. The traits below describe this archived row, not a new species assessment.

## Foraging strata {#s-c3f1f7b3a0}
The row marks Ter=1 in its foraging-stratum field. This is a recorded category, not a measure of how often or how long an individual used that stratum; the row does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. Later TetrapodTraits revisions reported corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries, but this row has not been compared field by field. Treat this archived flag as awaiting row-level review.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-0543ea4596}
AmphiBIO reports Body_size_mm=32 mm as a maximum adult body size. For Anura, this field denotes snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a sample distribution or a claim that every adult reaches this size.

## Compiled life history {#s-664ef26a74}
AmphiBIO records Reproductive_output_y=1, interpreted in its literature compilation as a maximum of one reproductive event per year. The row does not identify the population, sex, observation date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind that maximum, so it cannot be read as a schedule for every population.

## Developmental strategy {#s-504ef758cc}
The row marks Dir=1 as its developmental-category field; AmphiBIO presents this as direct development. The flag does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
