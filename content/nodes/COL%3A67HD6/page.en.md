## Leaf shape and venation {#s-d90e682051}
The regional flora lists erect stems, ovate leaf blades with a bullate (blistered) surface, and bases described as obtuse or heart-shaped. Secondary veins are prominent, and the spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) is oblong; the berry is recorded as green. These descriptors are not accompanied by a population sampling frame.

## Soil-associated in the habitat row {#s-0bd03a08f3}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 labels the species “terrícola” (soil-associated). The field provides no collection place or date, so the label should not be read as a complete distribution.
