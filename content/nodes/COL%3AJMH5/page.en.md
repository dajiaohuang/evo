## Attalea racemosa: leaves, flowers and fruit structure {#s-37a1de8701}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists the stem as subterranean or very short, with the note “under 25 cm diameter.” Leaf sheaths are fibrous, and petioles exceed 50 cm. Pinnae are uniformly arranged along the rachis, attached at a single angle, and have rusty indumentum at their tips. The erect leaf rachis is described as vertically positioned on the plant and densely covered with rusty indumentum.

The field lists androgynous or pistillate inflorescences and staminate inflorescences. They have deeply grooved peduncular bracts and numerous rachillae. In the androgynous or pistillate form, rachillae are absent from the adaxial face of the rachis; pistillate flowers are paired with staminate flowers in the basal portion, while only staminate flowers occur distally. In staminate inflorescences, rachillae are arranged along one side of the rachis.

Male flowers are described as surface-attached to or inserted on the rachilla, in pairs or singly on its abaxial surface. Their 3 petals are free, lanceolate, non-coriaceous and transversely flattened; the field lists them as curved at the apex in longitudinal view or straight. Venation is conspicuous, margins tomentose, and petals are longer than the stamens; there are 6 or more stamens. Anthers are straight or rarely curved. Pistillate flowers are absent from staminate plants or occur only at the base of rachillae in androgynous inflorescences. Their petals are cuspidate with irregular margins; the staminal ring is membranous, smooth-edged, not tomentose and slightly denticulate. The stigma has 3 parts.

Fruit has a smooth epicarp and a thick mesocarp. The endocarp has an irregular surface, robust adherent fibres and an irregular base; its locule or operculum is inconspicuous. The field also describes one or more cavities, small dispersed fibres inside, and aborted locules not compressed by fertile locules. The seed count is 1 or more.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Source alternatives and the “under 25 cm diameter” note are retained without further interpretation. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
