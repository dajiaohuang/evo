## Form and flowers {#s-e836297e1e}
The regional account describes a cormous geophyte 10–16 cm tall, with woody, concentric corm tunics. The lance-shaped leaves trail along the ground. Blue flowers with spreading tepals occur one to a few per spike.

## Diagnostic comparison with *Geissorhiza foliosa* {#s-4b0cb1fee4}
The SANBI diagnostic account describes *G. nigromontana* as having blue-purple flowers with a short, 2–3 mm perianth tube and much longer tepals 14–16 mm long; spikes bear only 2 or 3 flowers. Its style branches are unusually long at 4–5 mm, and the corm tunics are softly papery. The stem lies prostrate at the base and becomes abruptly erect above. The account says the species is probably closely related to *G. foliosa*, from the Langeberg foothills, which flowers mainly in October and November; it describes *G. nigromontana* as having the broad, flat leaves typical of *G. foliosa*. By comparison, *G. foliosa* has a 5–6 mm perianth tube, spikes with up to 7 flowers, and style branches up to 3 mm long. *G. nigromontana* also bears cormlets in the lower stem axils, a feature described as rare in the genus.
