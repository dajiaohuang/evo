## ## Lobed leaflets and a hooked keel {#s-7a1d746aeb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a prostrate growth form and trifoliolate leaves with lobed leaflets. It notes that first-order bract fascicles are absent. The corolla is violet; a callus at the base of the standard blade extends toward its center and bears prominent papillae, while the keel beak is hooked. The legume is recorded as 4–5.5 cm long. The habitat field says “terrestrial,” a broad category that supplies no locality or more specific habitat description.
