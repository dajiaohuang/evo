## 1KITE: tracing relationships across insect orders {#s-c571cfe6db}
The 1KITE study compared transcriptomes from 144 insect species across 1,478 protein-coding genes to estimate relationships among insect orders. It also modelled when branches diverged, but those dates are estimates from a calibrated molecular clock, not observed fossil first appearances; both the tree and its timing depend on taxon sampling, sequence models, fossil assignments and clock priors.
