## ## Velvety leaflets and lilac flowers {#s-10cc2f7e99}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a three-leaflet leaf with a visible rachis and a texture from membranous to papery. The oval to lanceolate leaflets are tomentose or velvety. Lilac flowers have a spur, and the straight fruit has pubescent valves. These traits come from a regional flora dataset, whose field does not state population coverage.
