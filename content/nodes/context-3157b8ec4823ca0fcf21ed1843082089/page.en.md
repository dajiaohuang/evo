## Source classification and concept status {#s-614c8bef29}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2900 records *Litoria phyllochroa* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-5e31d86946}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-2f4ea45c6d}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 1.16 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 40 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-087eec0948}
The row lists a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year and offspring-or-egg sizes of 1.2–1.2 mm. These are literature-compiled values. The row does not give the population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions, and the size field may refer to eggs.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-4936188902}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
