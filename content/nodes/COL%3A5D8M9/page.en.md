## Name, source and sample {#s-3cdcb30af3}
COL26.8 accepts Zosterops everetti within Zosteropidae. AVONET v7 uses the name in its HBW–BirdLife 2020 taxonomy: 5 individuals were measured and 4 had a complete morphological set. A trait may have fewer usable measurements than this total.

## Three beak dimensions {#s-6f2f3c7970}
The recorded means are 8 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip, 3.2 mm in width and 3.1 mm in depth at that landmark. They describe length, width and depth separately; they are standardized measurements, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, leg and mass summaries {#s-f62ecd23e4}
Mean unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 53.6 mm; mean tarsus length is 15.4 mm. The separately compiled mean mass is 10 g and its source category is Dunning. That mass summary does not mean the 5 morphological specimens were each weighed.

## Broad ecological categories {#s-6df7921b52}
AVONET labels the habitat Forest, trophic niche Omnivore and primary lifestyle Insessorial. These are broad categories used for comparisons across species. They do not describe every habitat or food used, or the same proportions in every season and place.

## Movement and the mapped range {#s-7b7517c20f}
The migration field is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude 3.82° to 12.58°. The mapped endpoints are not a current location or proof of occupancy throughout that span, and they do not describe the full non-breeding movements. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## A second historical source for feeding ecology {#s-0c90bcad85}
EltonTraits 1.0 also has a same-name, same-family record for Zosterops everetti, with two sourced groups: diet and foraging stratum. Its older bird taxonomy does not settle later splits or synonym disputes, so read it as a historical record associated by name and family.

## Standardized food weights {#s-b9ef0af3d3}
The EltonTraits entry assigns fruit 40%, invertebrates 30%, and nectar, pollen and plant exudates 30%. Code A records high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These weights standardize written descriptions; they are not measured diet shares, and zero would not prove a food is never eaten. Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader source categories than their everyday names.

## Feeding positions in the source {#s-a5005e634a}
The species-level stratum weights are 80% in or just above the canopy and 20% in trees or tall shrubs from 2 m upward but below the canopy. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not all-day time budgets, nest sites or habitat area. Water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial classes have separate source definitions and are not elevation or flight-height bands.

The diet and stratum groups carry labels Ref_13 and Ref_44. Their complete source strings are retained with file, row, group and reference-table details. These are source pointers, not extra observations; the cited books have not been individually reviewed here.

## Limits of these historical summaries {#s-4b30afa03d}
AVONET v7 values describe a small historical sample and species-level summaries, not individual variation or a current population survey. Body mass comes from a separate source and must not inherit the morphology sample size. Its ecology categories and mapped breeding/resident latitudes are coarse and historical. EltonTraits food weights are normalized from literature descriptions rather than measured intake; the compiler’s A confidence code does not make the proportions exact. Name matches, family placement and historical crosswalks support association but do not prove that these records share the present COL26.8 species concept. Underlying species literature has not been individually reviewed; local and seasonal variation, current distribution and conservation need other sources.
