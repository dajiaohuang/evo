## Lobelia fistulosa {#s-4a208c7531}
## Hollow stems and a pink-violet flower tube

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records hollow stems and alternate oblong to lance-shaped leaves. Their bases run down along the stem, their margins are toothed with a translucent edge, and both surfaces are hairless. A terminal raceme bears bilaterally symmetrical flowers with erect, lance-shaped bracts and a bell-shaped flower base.

The tubular corolla has a dorsal split and is pinkish violet. The stamens join after flowering, and the stigma has two lobes. The fruit is an ovoid to rounded capsule with lens-shaped, winged seeds. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
