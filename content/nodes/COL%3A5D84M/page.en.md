## Zornia cryptantha {#s-b87372e961}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zornia cryptantha* as an accepted species of *Zornia*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record contains only a habitat category, marked “terrestrial.” That regional field supplies neither a collection locality nor a complete range, and this record includes no morphology description.
