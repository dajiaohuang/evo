## Record identity and scope {#s-0ee4af734a}
CaecilianTraits v4 lists Caecilia albiventris in species-level row 3, under Caecilia, Caeciliidae. The workbook keeps individual-, population-, and species-level records separate. This source entry does not establish equivalence with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Habitat categories in the compilation {#s-aed8a0e618}
The species-level row lists broad habitat as Terrestrial and microhabitat as Fossorial. The accompanying description is qualified as presumed or possible, so these are source classifications rather than confirmed observations.
