## Delosperma pageanum {#s-9af394720d}
## Magenta flowers top an erect papillate shrublet

The Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 30 cm tall, with spreading papillate leaves. One to three magenta flowers grow in groups on papillate stalks and reach 16 mm across. The fruits are about 4 mm across, with broad valve wings and un-rimmed septa. The habitat record is karroid hills.
