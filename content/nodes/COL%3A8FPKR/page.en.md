## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b42b6d7591}
COL26.8 records Terrapene yucatana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Emydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f2a5d38cf9}
The row reports 3 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-20c23e6e1e}
