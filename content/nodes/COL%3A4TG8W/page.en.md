## *Rotheca louwalbertsii* {#s-0a94d08725}
The cited flora description records an herb, reaching 0.8 m tall, oval-shaped leaves, opposite leaf arrangement, flowers in purple. These are the characters reported in that account; they do not amount to a survey of population variation.
