## A leaf with a single leaflet {#s-8b6d0ab254}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes a single-leaflet leaf, rather than the more familiar three-leaflet arrangement in some related legumes. Its leaflet is oval or heart-shaped and papery, with a hairy to finely hairy surface. The flowers are white and have a spur; the straight fruit has pubescent valves. The row gives alternatives for shape and hairiness, not a full survey of plant variation.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-3039d91aa9}
The habitat field labels the species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. The regional dataset supplies no locality or complete range in this field.
