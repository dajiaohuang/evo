## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-adf5aada1d}
COL26.8 records Micrurus annellatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-fcf2cb712b}
