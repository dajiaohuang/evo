## Coccoloba obovata {#s-8e6db6b2c2}
## Large leathery leaves and dense terminal flower clusters

Coccoloba obovata ranges from a 3 m shrub to a tree 15 m tall. Leaves on ordinary shoots are obovate to nearly round and 9–24 cm long; those on adventitious shoots are larger. The leaves are leathery and may darken to brown or black when dry. Dense flower clusters terminate the branches, with male clusters up to 25 cm and female ones up to 17 cm. The globose fruit is bluntly three-angled, about 6 mm long; its achene is tan to dark brown and three-angled. The cited range is Costa Rica, Panama and Colombia.
