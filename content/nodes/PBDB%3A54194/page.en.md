The PBDB classification path records Rhynchocephalia at the order rank. This route links to occurrence guides for 32 listed species. ## Fossil diversity does not make Sphenodon an unchanged ancestor {#s-cfbb53ff7b}
The sampled Rhynchocephalia radiation was once diverse; *Sphenodon* is its only living lineage. Quantitative disparity analyses do not make the tuatara an unchanged ancestor or a proxy for every fossil member. The time-scaled dataset spans Late Triassic fossils to living *Sphenodon*, giving a rounded 238 Ma–present study window rather than an exact global first appearance.
