## Polycarpon tetraphyllum {#s-39054b07e1}
## Four-leaved whorls above a low, spreading herb

Polycarpon tetraphyllum is described as a prostrate or somewhat upright annual herb up to 200 mm tall. Its green leaves are mostly arranged in whorls of four. The loose inflorescence spreads and branches extensively, bearing green-and-white flowers. The source lists waste places as habitat but gives no locality or sampling coverage.
