## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-b63d7a54a4}
The source records a solitary aerial stem with a smooth surface. Green pinnate leaves have deciduous sheaths arranged helically around the stem, with fibrous margins; the petiole margin is unarmed, and the leaf axis is curved. Leaflets are leathery or membranous, grouped and inserted at differing angles; they may be rigid or flexible, with asymmetric pointed tips and dark-green surfaces of the same colour on both sides. The panicle and fruiting structure are exposed between the leaves. The grooved spadix may be hairless or waxy-coated; its main axis may be hairless or sparsely woolly, and its smaller branches spiral around it.

## Fruit {#s-e66b6dfbb2}
The fruit is pear-shaped. The green or yellow-green outer layer may bear hairs at the tip; the middle layer is fibrous and succulent. The woody inner layer is listed as globose or pear-shaped, with a conical cap. The endosperm is described as irregularly ruminate or as having internal cavities.
