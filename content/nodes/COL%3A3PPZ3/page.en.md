## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-24544a4377}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical branches with stiff bristles and no stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent ovate stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) remain attached. The leaf stalk (petiole) and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; leaves have four or six leaflet pairs. A stalked, cylindrical nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. Flower clusters are spike-like; the calyx (outer floral cup) is tubular, and flowers lack separate stalks. The leathery pod walls surround a fruit described as flat or curved, with a bristly surface. This single regional field preserves the listed alternatives but does not establish variation or how widely the traits occur.

## Habitat category {#s-88705eb910}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
