## Clawed tracks support a contested minimum {#track-evidence}
Early Tournaisian clawed tracks from the Snowy Plains Formation in Victoria, Australia, are the evidence behind this route. Their manus–pes morphology supports the cited study’s preferred primitive-sauropsid interpretation, but the trackmaker could instead lie near the amniote crown. The rounded 358.9 Ma endpoint is therefore a contested inferred minimum, not a body-fossil first appearance or crown-birth date.
