## Lavender heads on an epiphytic shrub {#s-b6b44f5447}
The morphology account describes a small epiphytic shrub 1–2 m tall, with opposite, fleshy elliptic leaves. Loose flower clusters carry lavender heads with purple-tinged bracts. The dry fruits are prism-shaped and have a narrow rim at the base; each head carries about 40 slender bristles.
