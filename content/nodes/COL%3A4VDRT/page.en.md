## What growth form and leaves are described? {#s-f701ad03c3}
The source describes Schlegelia fuscata as a woody vine whose stem can reach at least 2 cm in diameter. The leaves are opposite, simple, very leathery, and about 7.6–12.6 cm long by 4.2–9.5 cm wide. One cited specimen is labeled “epiphyte”; that specimen note does not establish that all individuals grow on other plants. The description also records 5–12 flowers in an axillary cluster.

## What are the flowers and fruit like? {#s-68d6899e5e}
The source’s continuation describes a pale, cup-shaped calyx and a tubular flower about 1 cm long, greenish white outside with pink lobes and a purplish-pink throat. The fruit is a fleshy, pale-purple berry up to 1.2 cm across, with the persistent calyx covering part of it; the angular seeds have a pebbled surface. These are features in the cited description.
