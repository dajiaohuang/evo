## Encyclia alboxanthina {#s-6b77833a5a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology record describes Encyclia alboxanthina with a conical pseudobulb, oblanceolate leaves and a double raceme. Its reflexed petals and rounded lip lobes frame a middle lobe with a wavy edge and notched tip. Habitat labels in the dataset include both rupicolous and terrestrial.
