## What stem, leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-1462a95058}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records a short-lived taproot, an erect stem without stolons, and branches with inconspicuous wings and glandular hairs. Leaves are ovate, with acute to acuminate tips and cordate or wedge-shaped bases. The terminal inflorescence is a thyrse, a branched flower cluster. Petals are white; anthers may be white or purple and have appendages at the base of the connective, the tissue joining the two sides of an anther. The ovary tip bears glands. The fruit is berry-like; its pericarp and placenta are shed when mature. Seeds are laterally coiled. These are regional field entries without a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-780b5840ed}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without a locality or collection date.
