## Mikania sessilifolia {#s-40fa2716e6}
## Trailing stems and a woolly leaf underside

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes decumbent, velvety branches; their pith is recorded as hollow or solid. Opposite, ovate leaves have scalloped margins and heart-shaped bases. The upper surface is covered with stiff hairs, the lower densely woolly; three main veins radiate from the base. The blades are somewhat leathery.

Flowering clusters are recorded as racemes or panicles with spike-like branches. Stalked heads have bracts at the base of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around a head are woolly and blunt-tipped. The corolla limb is longer than its tube, and its lobes taper to a point. The dry fruits (cypselae) are recorded as smooth or glandular. The habitat field says terrestrial; this regional record gives no locality or full range.
