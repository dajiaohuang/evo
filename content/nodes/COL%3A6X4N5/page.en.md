## Renealmia monosperma {#s-7bf4a4d9ce}
## Red-pink bracts and a tubular calyx

The Brazilian Flora record describes striated leaf sheaths and an erect flowering stalk. Its terminal inflorescence grows on a branch without leaves and has membranous bracts recorded as red or pink, each carrying two or more flowers. The calyx is tubular, and the mature capsule is globose. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
