## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton thomasii? {#s-9c90a18cc0}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. Long-stalked oval or elliptic leaves also bear star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and scalloped or toothed margins. A sessile or nearly sessile nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; leafy stipules at young branch tips may be absent or present. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are stalked; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate and alike, the style has many lobes, and petals are absent. The bud arrangement is reduplicate-valvate. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
