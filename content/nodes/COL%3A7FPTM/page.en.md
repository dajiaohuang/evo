## Vernonanthura densiflora {#s-a5118e829f}
## Hairy leaf faces contrast with gland-free flowers

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright stems with woolly to pubescent hairs and no glandular dots. Petiolate leaves are leathery, lance-shaped to oblanceolate, and pointed or minutely tipped. Hairs on both faces range from woolly or pubescent to bristly; leaf glands are present. Secondary veins arch toward the margin and fade before reaching it.

Involucral bracts have acute tips. Each head contains 8–22 florets; the corolla is hairless and lacks glands. The pubescent dry fruits have no glands, and the outer pappus bristles are flattened. No habitat category is supplied in the cited fields.
