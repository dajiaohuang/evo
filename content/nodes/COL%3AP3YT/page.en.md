## Caamembeca oxyphylla {#s-f7c112f6f0}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Caamembeca oxyphylla* as an accepted species of *Caamembeca*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records leaves with small, 2–4 mm petioles and glands outside the flowers. Its flowers are yellow, and the seeds are pear-shaped. The description comes from one regional dataset entry; it does not provide a locality or a complete account of variation.
