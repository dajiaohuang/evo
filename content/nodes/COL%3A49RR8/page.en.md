## What distinguishes its foliage and flower clusters? {#s-d7104aea9f}
Oocephalus crassifolius is recorded with a xylopodium, non-viscid branches, and leathery, stalkless leaves with scalloped edges. Its branched flower cluster is a thyrse built from ovoid cymes; the tubular calyx persists and has awl-shaped lobes. The tubular corolla is white or lilac. The source records three oval nutlets and no mucilage, but gives no population sample.
