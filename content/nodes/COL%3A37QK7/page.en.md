## Magenta flowers and raised fruit rims {#s-3069b8a647}
The account describes a rigid, upright shrublet up to 20 cm tall, with brown branches bearing few papillae. Its nearly cylindrical leaves are blunt-tipped and densely papillate. Magenta flowers are 20–30 mm across. The five-chambered fruits have raised rims and a false partition. Loamy soils are listed as habitat.
