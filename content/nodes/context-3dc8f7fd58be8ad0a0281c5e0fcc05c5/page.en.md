## The source concept {#s-9927ac0cdb}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Engystomops guayaco in row Anf3272, under the genus Engystomops, family Leiuperidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page identifies the source row; it does not establish that the row matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental strategy {#s-69815ffb07}
The deposited row records a larval stage. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or the timing of reproduction.
