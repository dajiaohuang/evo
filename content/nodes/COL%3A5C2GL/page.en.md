## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-50b803fafa}
The erect stems are pubescent with star-shaped, simple and gland-tipped translucent hairs. Stipules are under or about 1 cm long. Green leaves differ slightly between their two surfaces; they are heart-shaped, with pointed, acuminate or cuspidate tips and toothed margins. The upper surface has sparse simple, gland-tipped or star-shaped hairs. A leafy, pyramidal inflorescence bears flowers with sepals divided to about the middle and unspotted yellow petals. The dry fruit splits into four or five hairy segments (mericarps), each with an awn; each segment contains three seeds.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-7a55e69087}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
