## *Turnera tapajoensis* {#s-e5c8d964c9}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — elliptic, egg-shaped, entire, untoothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
