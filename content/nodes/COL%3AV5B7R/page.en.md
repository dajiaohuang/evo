## Two mountain localities in northwestern Hunan {#s-a07d4fda69}
The monograph listed male holotype HNNU 03080902 from Tianzishan Nature Reserve, Sangzhi, Hunan, at 1130 m, and newly examined male SYS a004310 from Tianping Mountain at approximately 1380 m. It summarized known distribution as Tianzishan and Tianping at 1000–1380 m, citing earlier work and its own specimen. This dated locality summary is not a population census or proof of continuous occupancy.

## A newly examined male exceeds the earlier sample range {#s-c0f1a36725}
The diagnosis separately reported earlier male snout–vent lengths of 33.2–40.3 mm for nine individuals, attributed to Fei and Ye (2016), and a 44.0 mm male examined in this study. The earlier female value was 50.5 mm for one individual. The 44.0 mm observation is not removed to fit the earlier range; the samples remain separate rather than being presented as one uniformly measured series or a universal size bound.

## Diagnostic skin characters and collection-name changes {#s-833aa3dd00}
The diagnosis combined previous descriptions and examined material, describing no vomerine ridges or teeth, granular dorsal skin with weak or absent interrupted X-shaped ridges, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. Breeding males were described with black nuptial spines on finger I and II pads and a subgular vocal sac. The remarks changed the collection abbreviation from HNUL to HNNU, attributing this to personal communication, and adjusted the epithet to tuberogranulata. These nomenclatural changes are not new specimens or biological discoveries.
