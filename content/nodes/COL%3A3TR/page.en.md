## Evidence limits {#s-cb646080cf}
This order route reports only the authored entries in this batch, with overlap among AVONET, life-history, EltonTraits and ecology-only routes. Its counts do not give a complete accepted-species denominator. AVONET values are species-level summaries under historical names; life-history data may inherit earlier taxonomic records; EltonTraits percentages standardize literature descriptions; and the limited ecology pages omit biometric interpretation. Two one-to-one historical crosswalks support some links without proving modern concept equivalence. Current range, population, conservation and missing species accounts require separate evidence.
