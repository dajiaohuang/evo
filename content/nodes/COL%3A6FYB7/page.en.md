## Scope of the biological account {#s-3fba4476ac}
African wild asses should be distinguished from domestic donkeys. This biological source concerns Somali wild asses, including striped legs and feeding in arid habitats; subspecies traits are not automatically generalized to all African wild asses.

## Catalogue identity and evidence limits {#s-c72a4238cf}
Quantitative traits, complete ranges, divergence dates and current conservation assessments require separate evidence. Catalogue counts are not counts of living wild biological species.
