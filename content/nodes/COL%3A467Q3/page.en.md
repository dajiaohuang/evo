## What are the flowers and spur like? {#s-3fbff3ae20}
The source describes Nemesia calcarata as an annual herb up to 30 cm tall, with elliptic-lance-shaped leaves and loose flower clusters. Its flower reaches 26 mm long: the upper lip is white or pale yellow and the lower lip yellow to orange-yellow. A glandular-hairy spur about 12 mm long projects downward or forward beyond the lower lip. The broadly egg-shaped capsule is about 9 × 8 mm.
