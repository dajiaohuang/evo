## Baccharis magnifica {#s-2950c24009}
## Broad leaves and racemes of flower heads

This record describes developed leaves with pinnate veins and distinct petioles. The blades range from ovate and obovate to elliptic or oblong, with entire margins. Branches are listed as unwinged and either smooth or striated; no xylopodium, the woody underground base, is recorded. Flower heads form racemes, and the head-floret field lists either tubular male florets only or short ligulate florets only.
