## The source concept {#s-7e8585a8fd}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Amietophrynus blanfordii* in row Anf0356 under Amietophrynus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-67dc4205e5}
For Anf0356, the compilation tags terrestrial as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-21b80e4b52}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 39 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-459c25d2eb}
The row records a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; it does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report.
