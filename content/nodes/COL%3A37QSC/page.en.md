## Drosera indica {#s-6367eb9289}
## Linear leaves line a slender flowering stem

Drosera indica is described as a delicate annual herb with an evident slender stem. Its linear leaves are loosely arranged along the stem, and the inflorescence reaches 150 mm. The flowers are mauve. The cited habitat is grassland and vlei wetlands; this account does not give a specific locality or survey date.
