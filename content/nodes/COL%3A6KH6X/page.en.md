## A separate winter leaf and small pink flowers {#s-6bc058d597}
Gladiolus brevifolius has short, bladeless leaves on the flowering stem and small, usually unscented pink to mauve flowers with an 11–13 mm tube. A solitary, narrow, hairy leaf grows later on a separate shoot; the account says its dried remains can help confirm identification.
