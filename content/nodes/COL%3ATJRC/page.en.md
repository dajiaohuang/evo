## Roots, stems, and leaves {#s-649fcf8083}
The source describes spiny aerial roots and erect stems that are solitary or clustered, 0.5–2 m tall and no more than 1 cm in diameter. It records four to nine leaves; the blades are divided into two, or are rarely pinnate. When pinnate, they have two to ten irregularly arranged pinnae. The leaf sheaths are tubular and striated; the entry gives leaf lengths of 15–30 cm and petioles of 10–50 cm.

## Inflorescence and fruit {#s-b0d2b8516a}
The inflorescence is a spike between the leaves, with three to six bracts and a 20–50 cm stalk. The fruit is ellipsoid and 1.0–1.5 cm long.

The field records spiny aerial roots; erect solitary or clustered stems 0.5–2.0 m tall and no more than 1.0 cm in diameter; four to nine leaves with bifid or rarely pinnate blades, and two to ten irregular pinnae when pinnate; tubular, striated sheaths; leaf lengths of 15–30 cm and petioles of 10–50 cm; an interfoliar spike with three to six bracts and a 20–50 cm peduncle; and ellipsoid fruit 1.0–1.5 cm long.
