## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-7fc372e177}
The source describes clustered stems up to 4 m tall, with black spines up to 6 cm long. Pinnate leaves have 15–21 pairs of S-shaped leaflets, grouped in sets of two to four; their spacing and insertion angle may be regular or irregular, and may be the same or different. The branched inflorescence emerges between the leaves and has a spiny stalk bract. The fruit is spherical, red to magenta, and has a warty outer surface.

The source records clustered stems up to 4 m tall with black spines up to 6 cm; pinnate leaves with 15–21 pairs of sigmoid pinnae, grouped two to four with regular or irregular arrangement at the same or different angles; a branched interfoliar inflorescence with a spiny peduncular bract; and globose red or magenta fruit with a muricate epicarp.
