## ## Grooved leaf axes and coiled fruit {#s-88bb035886}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes cylindrical branches, with their hair covering recorded as absent (glabrous) or long and soft (villous); lenticels are absent. The stipules, small appendages at the leaf base, persist and are linear. Both the leaf stalk (petiole) and central leaf axis (rachis) are grooved, and the field lists one, two or three pairs of leaflets. It records no nectaries (nectar-producing glands). The flower cluster is head-shaped, the calyx (outer floral cup) bell-shaped, and the flowers stalkless or nearly so. The fruit is listed as having papery pod walls (valves), a spiral form and long, soft hairs. A separate habitat field says terrestrial but supplies no locality or survey frame. The record lists alternatives without explaining whether they reflect variation, so it does not establish a species-wide range of traits.
