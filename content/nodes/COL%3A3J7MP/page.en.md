## Leaves and flower head {#s-fb89adf1d8}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte 150–280 mm tall, growing in clumps. It has two leaves that are dry at flowering, erect, lanceolate, smooth, shiny green, and unmarked. The flowers form a compact head; their bracts range from red to pale carmine.
