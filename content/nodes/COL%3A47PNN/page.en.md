## The name and this data account {#s-4b46d33476}
Nonnula ruficapilla is accepted in COL26.8 within Bucconidae. AVONET v7 links the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name to 8 measured individuals, 3 with complete morphological sets. Trait-specific usable samples can differ; the name match is historical and does not prove concept equivalence.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-e69654ee7c}
Mean beak length is 17.6 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width and depth at that edge average 5.8 mm and 6 mm. These are separate standardized measurements, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-8fb349ee89}
Mean unflattened wing length is 59.8 mm from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 14 mm. A separately sourced Dunning compilation gives mean mass of 22 g, not a mass recorded for each morphology specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-7aed77faf7}
AVONET labels this record Forest for habitat, Invertivore for trophic niche and Insessorial for lifestyle. These comparative categories do not claim that the bird uses only one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-1d2bd7e845}
The movement field is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps have latitude endpoints −16.08° and −2.11°; they are not a current occupancy map or a complete account of non-breeding movements. Geography and morphology are separate source products.

Invertebrates carry all of the nonzero diet weight (100%). Code A marks high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. The percentage translates literature descriptions and is not a direct measurement of consumed food.
