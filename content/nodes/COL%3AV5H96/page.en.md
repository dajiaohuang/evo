## Accepted name and placement {#s-5ffa615070}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys wugongensis (J. Wang, Lyu & Y. Y. Wang in J. Wang et al., 2019) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Wugongshan and neighbouring mountain records {#s-1328fc6349}
The male holotype was recorded from Yangshimu Scenic Area, Luxi, Jiangxi, at 550 m. The monograph summarized Wugongshan, neighbouring Yangshimu, Dawei and Mufu mountain records along the Hunan–Jiangxi border at 550–1200 m. These recorded mountain localities do not establish continuous occupancy between them or a physiological elevation range.

## Examined adult measurements and rough dorsal skin {#s-4fd3c708ce}
The diagnosis reported eight adult males at 30.9–34.1 mm snout–vent length and nine adult females at 38.5–42.8 mm. The examined series combined the type material with additional Dawei and Mufu males. It described weak vomerine ridges without teeth, rough densely granular dorsal skin with an interrupted X-shaped ridge, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. These sample measurements and anatomical descriptions are not population-wide size limits or measures of locomotor performance.

## Revision of contradictory nuptial-pad statements {#s-6d61f64806}
The remarks identified contradictory statements in the original description: a comparison referred to male nuptial pads and spines, while its habits discussion said they were not visible and that the breeding season remained unknown. After re-examining types and newly collected specimens, the monograph revised the character to dark nuptial pads with dense spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II in breeding males. Males also had a subgular vocal sac. This attributed anatomical revision does not by itself determine a breeding-season calendar.

## Sex-specific measurements in the revised comparison {#s-5b6c6355d4}
The 2025 comparison cites four adult wugongensis males at 31.0–34.1 mm snout–vent length and nine adult females at 38.5–42.8 mm. It describes clear large pale cloud-like abdominal patches and absent lateral toe fringes, contrasting them with hengshanensis. The reported sister relationship comes from the included phylogenetic references. These comparator samples and traits are not a newly surveyed wugongensis population, a universal size limit or a complete identification key.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-cf8a33bc1e}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys wugongensis as with heels not meeting when the thighs are held at right angles to the body. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
