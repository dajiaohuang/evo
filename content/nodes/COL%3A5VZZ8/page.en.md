## Bactris killipii {#s-6166c26b9f}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records Bactris killipii with a solitary or clustered stem to 2 m and no stem spines noted. Leaves are entire or bifid; the between-leaf inflorescence is spike-like or branched. Globose orange fruits have a smooth outer surface. The row labels the habitat terrestrial and gives no locality or survey frame.
