## *Thalia petersiana*: lance-shaped leaves in aquatic or terrestrial records {#s-7b5fc6123d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character record describes linear, lance-shaped leaves. It records no overlapping outer bracts—the small, modified leaves around a flower cluster; the bracts that are present are oblong. The sepals, the outer leaflike parts of a flower, are oval. The mature capsule is ellipsoid.

Two substrate categories appear in the source fields: aquatic and terrestrial. They are broad habitat labels without a named collection locality or date, so this entry does not locate particular populations or describe a complete range.
