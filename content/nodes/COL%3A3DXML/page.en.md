## Felicia whitehillensis {#s-2f58f356af}
## Silky fruits and variable ray florets

The source describes a dwarf shrub to 400 mm tall with long and short shoots. Its leaves are alternate, linear to lance-shaped, slightly succulent and usually entire; flower heads are solitary, with purple rays sometimes absent and yellow disc florets. Cypselae are strikingly silky-hairy, and the pappus bristles are barbellate. The taxonomic note says ray florets are absent in one collection and rare in another; leaf differences in Calvinia plants grade into other forms, so it does not support separate treatment of that population. Habitat records include sandy flats, rock crevices and shale ridges.
