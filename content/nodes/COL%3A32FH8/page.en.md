## What leaf, floral and seed details does the regional field give? {#s-bbcd5a9b9b}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite oval or elliptic leaves have blunt, tapered or wedge-shaped bases and rolled-under margins. The inflorescence is leafy. Flower stalks are 0.1–6 mm long, with bracteoles present. The floral tube is listed in two length groups, 3–10 mm or 13–30 mm, and bears long or closely pressed stiff hairs. Its blunt spur points horizontally or downward. Petals may be white or pink and are absent by the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube. A vesicle is present; the ovary-base nectar gland may point downward or horizontally. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin acute. These alternatives are not assigned to particular specimens.
