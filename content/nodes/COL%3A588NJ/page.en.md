## Trichilia casarettoi {#s-8c86d5e4af}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record allows either three-foliolate or odd-pinnate leaves and records a glabrous ovary with a three-locular structure.

## Leaf forms and floral alternatives {#s-692eace9d2}
The source records simple hairs and buds without cataphylls. Leaves may be three-foliolate or odd-pinnate, with an unwinged rachis and opposite leaflets; basal leaflets are not heteromorphic, and their bases are acute or attenuate. The lower leaflet surface may be hairy or glabrous. Flowers have free, imbricate petals; the staminal filaments are partly or fully united, and a nectary is present. The ovary is glabrous and three-locular, with two collateral ovules per locule. Fruit is ellipsoid or three-angled and puberulous. The separate ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
