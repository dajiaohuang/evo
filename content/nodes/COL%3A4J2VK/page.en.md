## The name and this data account {#s-a0801fce69}
Pionus chalcopterus is accepted in COL26.8 within Psittacidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 4 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-3553d3d56a}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 24.6 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 14.7 mm in width and 24.7 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-4e0e4e3def}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 189.8 mm; mean tarsus length is 15.9 mm. Compiled mean mass is 210 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-d016d27437}
The dataset assigns habitat Forest, trophic niche Frugivore and lifestyle Insessorial. These are dominant categories for cross-species comparisons; they do not imply exclusive use of one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-f68dcdc565}
The migration field classifies this record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -4.29° to 10.99°. This excludes a complete account of non-breeding movements and is not a current-position or global occupancy estimate. Geography and morphological measurements come from different compilation steps.

## EltonTraits record and taxonomic context {#s-40a49d891f}
The EltonTraits 1.0 record associated with Pionus chalcopterus has two ecological field groups: food weights and foraging strata. Its taxonomy follows an earlier bird classification; a name match does not resolve later splits or synonym disputes.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-ac7c45a295}
The archive’s nonzero food weights are fruit 80%; seeds, nuts and spores included in this category 20%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-e4e13141a8}
Foraging-stratum weights flagged as species-level in the archive are trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy 50%; understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m 30%; in or just above the canopy 20%. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or whole-day time budgets. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-889a860251}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
