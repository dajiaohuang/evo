## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-996b298057}
The source describes an underground or very short stem and petioles longer than 50 cm. The pinnae are evenly arranged along the slightly curved leaf axis; their tips have rusty hairs, and the axis bears dark or whitish hairs. In bisexual inflorescences, female flowers pair with male flowers near the base of each small branch, with only male flowers farther out. The fruit has a thick middle layer and a strongly fibrous inner wall; small fibres are scattered inside it.

The source records an underground or very short stem with a stated diameter below 25 cm; fibrous leaf sheaths; petioles over 50 cm; evenly arranged pinnae with rusty indumentum at their tips on a slightly curved rachis bearing dark or whitish indumentum; bisexual inflorescences with pistillate flowers paired with staminate flowers in basal rachilla portions and only staminate flowers distally; six stamens and three stigmas; and a thick mesocarp with irregular, strongly fibrous adherent endocarp and small fibres dispersed internally.
