## Accepted name and placement {#s-d2383b9300}
In the frozen COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026, Leptodactylus bolivianus Boulenger, 1898 is accepted at species rank. Its recorded placement is Anura (order), Leptodactylidae (family) and Leptodactylus (genus). This is the classification in that release; other checklists or fossil databases require their own evidence for name identity.

## Molineid nematodes in a regional literature checklist {#s-458d95b8f0}
A 2025 review of Molineidae literature lists Leptodactylus bolivianus as a host of the nematode Oswaldocruzia lopesi. This is a host–parasite association compiled from earlier reports, not a new examination or evidence that all frogs of this species are infected. The checklist provides no examined-host denominator, prevalence, infection intensity, pathology or mortality.
