## Magericyon’s feeding loads, tested in finite-element models {#s-e68d996e50}
The complete young-adult skull B-4071 anchors finite-element simulations of canine, fourth premolar (p4), and first molar (m1) bites, plus pull-back and head-shaking loads. Comparative carnivoran mandibles provide context as the models calculate forces and stresses across these scenarios.

These calculations test feeding-mechanics hypotheses; they depend on segmentation, muscle reconstruction, material properties, constraints, and normalization. They model loads on bone, not how Magericyon actually handled prey.
