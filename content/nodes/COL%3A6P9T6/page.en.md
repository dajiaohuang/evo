## Lepidaploa cuiabensis {#s-b6ac3c1f71}
## Pointed oblanceolate leaves above lilac to pink heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright, pubescent stems without branch glands. Alternate leaves sit in two vertical rows on stalks. Their oblanceolate blades have tapered bases and a tiny point or mucro at the tip; the upper face ranges from hairless to rough, while the underside has short fine hairs. Leaf-surface glands are absent.

Flower heads grow on leafy stems, with bell-shaped involucres and non-overlapping bracts. The inner bracts are pointed to long-pointed. Each head has 35–45 lilac to pink florets. The pappus is white, and fruit glands are absent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
