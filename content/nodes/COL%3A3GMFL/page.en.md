## What leaves and flowers are described? {#s-8b45ec7c9c}
This shrublet grows to 60 cm. Its opposite elliptic-obovate leaves, including the leaves around the flower cluster, are densely covered in smooth silky hairs. Six to nine cream-colored flowers grow at branch tips. The flower tube (hypanthium) is densely silky-haired and bears four fleshy, petal-like scales.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-32bff7c6cf}
A regional account reports coastal flats. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
