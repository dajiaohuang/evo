## ## Many leaflets and a veined oblong fruit {#s-4d9643274a}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 13–25 pairs of elliptic leaflets, with a reported upper value of 29; their tips are blunt or acute and their bases blunt. The inflorescence may be racemose or cymose. The oblong fruit has surface veins only over the seed region. The source marks several floral details—including corolla, stamens, filaments and anther dehiscence—as unknown, so those features are not described here. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
