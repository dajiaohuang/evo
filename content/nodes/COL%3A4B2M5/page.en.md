## Fleshy leaves above a woolly-based stem {#s-af8cf8d564}
This record describes a dwarf shrublet with a short, one- to few-branched woody base; its crown is finely woolly and then covered by old, dry leaf remains. Fleshy, entire leaves form tufts. One or a few bare stalks carry yellow radiate heads, whose bracts have dark tips. It grows among gravel and patches of quartz.
