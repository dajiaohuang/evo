## Winged stems and pale axillary flowers {#s-c0e873a1b4}
The source description records a prostrate subshrub to 15 cm tall, with winged stems. Leaves are sessile, linear elliptic and mucronate. Flowers are solitary in the leaf axils and pale mauve or white. The ecology field gives moist sandstone slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
