## Limits across the separate datasets {#s-ae62ef86d8}
PanTHERIA, the Amniote archive, EltonTraits, and Ernest’s compilation use separate historical samples, definitions, and name crosswalks. The records do not cover every COL26.8 descendant, and matching an older name does not establish current species-concept equivalence. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. EltonTraits categories are semi-quantitative, and mammalian ABC values may borrow from a congener. The living-primate tree supports sampled relationships but does not date the family from a fossil first occurrence; numerical range remains withheld.

## Gestation, first reproduction, and longevity {#s-8cb52a8371}
Gestation and female age at first reproduction appear in two accounts each; maximum lifespan appears in one. First reproduction differs from first birth and sexual maturity. Maximum lifespan is the longest record, not an average. Source conditions may vary, and the missing lifespan entry is not filled from a relative.

## Limits of Ernest’s historical values {#s-914f6dc3fb}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 table; equivalence with current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature entries equally, without sample-size weighting. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted in this Ernest route; Original source numbers remain; wild, captive, and locality conditions are not consistently specified across sources.
