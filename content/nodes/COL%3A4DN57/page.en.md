## Parkia lutea {#s-10263a0d06}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Parkia lutea* as an accepted species of *Parkia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records alternate leaves with leaflets that have rounded-notched tips and ear-shaped bases. Its axillary flower clusters are club-shaped on hanging stalks; the fruit is indehiscent (not splitting open at maturity), hairless, and leathery. The record also labels the plant terrestrial.
