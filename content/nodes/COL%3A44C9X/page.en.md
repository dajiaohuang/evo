## Nodding flowers with tightly reflexed tepals {#s-9f0edaf994}
Moraea pendula has several glaucous (with a grey waxy coating) leaves and nodding to pendulous yellow flowers, with tepal limbs (the expanded ends of tepals, flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) tightly reflexed against the stalks. One account records stems up to 900 mm; a diagnostic treatment describes plants up to 750 mm. Orange-red anthers and pollen contrast with the tepals.

## Seasonally wet places {#s-59e25222ac}
The source records seasonally wet places; a diagnostic comparison also says it grows in wetter sites than the related M. miniata.
