## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-8f836497a1}
COL26.8 records Pseudonaja aspidorhyncha as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-97f0d504c0}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1300 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Seymor 1987.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-fc5c56f6d1}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 5.8. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
