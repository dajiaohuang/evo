## What leaf and flower traits are described for Euphorbia glanduligera? {#s-f3300d45c7}
The erect herb reaches 300 mm and branches from the base. Its opposite, glaucous leaves are lance-shaped to linear with entire margins. The cyathia have four glands; the capsule is hairy, and the flowers are yellowish green.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-cd0eb19a9f}
The source lists bushveld.
