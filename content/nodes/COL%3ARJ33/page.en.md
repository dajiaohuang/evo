## What leaf and flower details identify Caryocar glabrum? {#s-5f844b5fe1}
Its leaflets have tapering tips and may have scalloped or smooth edges. The record notes conspicuous domatia on the underside—small structures often found near leaf-vein junctions—and long flower stalks. The inflorescence may be finely hairy or smooth. Flowers have yellow, elliptic petals and lack small bracts on the flower stalks.
