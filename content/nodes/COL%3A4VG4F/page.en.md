## *Schultesia bahiensis* {#s-33e1edcaf3}
## A four-sided stem and a winged calyx

Brazilian Flora 2020 records a four-angled stem and papery leaves. Its venation field uses the specialist label “acrodromous–brochidodromous” for the main veins that run toward the leaf tip and join in loops near the margin. The flowers are yellow, with a winged calyx; its narrow, lance-shaped lobes taper to points. The funnel-shaped corolla has obovate lobes, wider toward their rounded ends. The filaments carry toothed, membranous wings, and the seed coat is pitted (foveolate). This is the morphology field in the regional dataset, not a global survey of the species.
