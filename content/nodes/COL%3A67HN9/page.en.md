## Anthurium cachoeirense: a regional morphology record {#s-19e1d06974}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem with fibrous cataphylls and prophylls along it. Petioles are grooved, with a sharp upper margin and a rounded lower side, and are marked smooth. Leaf blades are entire, flat and elliptic, with acute tips and bases coded as acute or truncate; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is coded as obtuse above and acute below, secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a one-keeled peduncle. Its flat, elliptic or ovate spathe forms an acute angle with the peduncle. The entry gives conflicting states for the spathe stipe (present and shorter than the spadix, or absent), so that character remains unresolved. The fruit is a berry; no color character is supplied.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Conflicting compound states remain unresolved.
