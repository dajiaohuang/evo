## Cyrtanthus angustifolius: leaves and flowers {#s-8f469dfc9f}
A 2012 regional account describes this as a bulbous geophyte reaching 60 cm and often forming clumps. It records 2–4 strap-shaped leaves, which are rarely dry at flowering. There are 4–10 nodding, tubular, scarlet flowers, often orange inside. The stamens project beyond the flower throat, and the style has three branches.
