## A reported distance to velocicantus {#s-00e3b51be1}
The2020 study’s Table2 reports 10.0% uncorrected p-distance and 10.8% Kimura2-parameter distance between the Allobates bacurau label and velocicantus, based on a678-bp16S fragment. These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. The table is a sampled comparison; original accessions, alignments and per-donor TableS1 entries remain independently unreviewed.

## Male throat colour in a diagnostic comparison {#s-9303d11bc6}
The2020 comparison describes male bacurau throats as light to dark gray, in contrast to the white centre/yellow edges reported for velocicantus. Sex and comparison scope matter: this paragraph does not establish female throat colour, a complete preservation-dependent colour series or population-wide variation. Original descriptions cited by the paper remain separately unreviewed.

## Duration and spacing in cited acoustic comparisons {#s-05f505b037}
The2020 paper attributes to previous studies a maximum call duration of 11.1 s, maximum 81 notes and inter-note interval 71–129 ms for bacurau. These cited summaries preserve their units and do not describe the same sampling protocol or temperatures as the eight focal velocicantus males. Original source recordings have not been independently reanalysed, and no new mate-recognition experiment follows from this numerical contrast.
