## Chamaecrista aiarana {#s-ebc7649058}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista aiarana* records two pairs of ovate leaflets 15–20 cm long, extrafloral nectaries on petiole and leaf axis, yellow petals. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
