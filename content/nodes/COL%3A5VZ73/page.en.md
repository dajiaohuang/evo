## Classification {#s-ea17c33948}
The 2006 eastern-woolly-lemur study compared cytogenetic and mitochondrial material collected at eastern and western sites. Its type series for A. peyrierasi comprised tissue and DNA from eleven animals; the named type locality was Mahasoarivo near Ranomafana. The authors diagnosed the taxon against eastern congeners using mitochondrial cytochrome-b differences. These are the original study’s samples and taxonomic argument, not a modern range survey.

## Classification {#s-e4996958a8}
A later genus-level analysis identified two mitochondrial types assigned to A. peyrierasi, including samples around Ranomafana, and called for additional work to resolve their relationships. The labels “type 2” and “type 3” describe that paper’s sampled lineages; they are not extra accepted COL species.

## Evidence limits {#s-930563b690}
The taxonomic and genetic account is limited to the cited collections and analyses; it does not establish current occupancy or a complete range.
