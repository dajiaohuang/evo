## Tracing the historical genus name {#s-95d47094fa}
COL26.8 accepts Psilopogon virens as ID 7WP6Q. The AVONET BirdLife 2020 row 9705 and eBird 2021 row 8672 both use Psilopogon virens with Avibase ID AVIBASE-F5636BF7. BirdTree row 8627 records Megalaima virens and a 1BL to 1BT match; COL older-combination record 7VSWF points to 7WP6Q. These are links between historical name records and the accepted entry, not proof of unchanged species boundaries.

## Measured samples and beak/wing averages {#s-0c0cbc2b8c}
Eight birds contribute to the AVONET sample—four female, four male and none unsexed—with complete measurements for four. The recorded means are 29.6 mm from nostril to bill tip, 14.5 mm beak width, 33.4 mm tarsus and 138.4 mm unflattened wing; hand-wing index is 21.7. Inference=NO does not make every trait's denominator eight. The index is Kipp's distance divided by wing length, ×100; it is not flight speed, and beak width is not a feeding-performance test.

## Compiled mass has a separate denominator {#s-1169cda312}
The mass entry is 202 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning. It is a compiled species average and has a separate basis from the eight-bird morphology sample. Individual weights and Dunning's book were not examined, so the record cannot provide sex-, season- or locality-specific weight limits.

## Fruit niche and trophic level are separate fields {#s-780f6f42fa}
The Metadata niche category is Frugivore, while the separate trophic-level field is Herbivore. One describes a fruit-centered resource niche and the other a broader trophic grouping; neither is a record of individual feeding events or a measured diet percentage. No fruit species, food sizes, digestion or seed-dispersal experiments are supplied, so detailed diet and dispersal effects remain unknown here.

## Perching, vegetation and sedentary category {#s-856317f780}
AVONET uses Insessorial for the perching lifestyle category. The habitat is Forest and density code 1 means dense vegetation, not bird density per area. Migration=1 is the sedentary category; it does not establish that every individual stays in one place throughout life. This page does not interpret the historical map fields, so the categories cannot establish current occupancy, nest sites or daily activity heights.

## Evidence limits {#s-d397ac2f30}
This page draws on the selected AVONET v7 row, two name crosswalks, Metadata definitions and local COL name records. Morphometric summaries and the Dunning mass entry have separate source bases; name matches and an older-combination record do not establish identical concepts across dates. Individual specimens, the complete workbook, Dunning's book and a new full reading of the methods paper were not examined. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation status are outside the evidence used here.
