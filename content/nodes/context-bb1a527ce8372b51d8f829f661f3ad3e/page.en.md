## Source classification and concept status {#s-a97ade640e}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3056 records *Afrixalus aureus* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-7b4a6cba3f}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-92542426c9}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 26 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-e215da3d23}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for this row. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
