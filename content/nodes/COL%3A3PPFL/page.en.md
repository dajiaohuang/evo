## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-596c283b04}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical, hairless branches with stem lenticels (small pores). Persistent stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) are sickle-shaped or elliptic. The leaf stalk (petiole) and the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are cylindrical; each leaf has two or three leaflet pairs. A stalkless, saucer-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is recorded. Inflorescences are spike-like; the outer floral cup (calyx) is tubular, and flowers lack a separate stalk. The pod walls (valves) are woody; the fruit is flat and hairless. This is one regional dataset field, so variation and the frequency of these traits are not established.

## Habitat category {#s-a6b5eb14f7}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). That is the category in one dataset row, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
