## Senna alata {#s-bcfcbcc70a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna alata* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records 6–10 pairs of leaflets and six or seven fertile stamens. Its pods are four-angled, upright, and open late; the seeds are arranged in two rows. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
