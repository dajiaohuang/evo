## Large pale flowers and a long tube {#s-f7ac17024d}
Geissorhiza confusa is 12–30 cm tall, with large creamy-white to beige flowers that may fade pinkish. The flowers have a long tube, mostly 35–50 mm, while the linear to sword-shaped leaves and woody corm tunics (the fibrous outer layers around the underground corm, a swollen storage stem) help identify the plant.

## Fynbos sandstone slopes {#s-87569826e6}
The regional account records rocky sandstone slopes in fynbos (a Cape shrubland vegetation type), at 400–1,200 m. These observations describe the cited account rather than the full species range.
