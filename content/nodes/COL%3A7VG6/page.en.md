Three species in this batch have ReptTraits records assigned to Thecadactylus by the pinned Catalogue of Life COL26.8 ancestry: Thecadactylus oskrobapreinorum, T. rapicauda and T. solimoensis. The pages follow that pinned classification, while the workbook’s trait records retain its Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This is a route through the records available in this batch, not a count of all accepted species in the genus.

## The two length examples are from different species {#s-d30e5999b9}
In the size records here, T. oskrobapreinorum has a reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 99 mm, while T. solimoensis reaches 126 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent; it is not total length including the tail. The two maxima belong to different species, so they show neither the range within one species nor the smallest and largest individuals in the genus. Any comparison of activity, diet or reproduction also needs the definition and source for that particular field.

## Evidence limits {#s-e57a74cef7}
This route covers records selected into this batch, not every accepted descendant of Thecadactylus. The maximum lengths are compiled reports, not individual averages or identification thresholds. When comparing body size, keep different measurements distinct; for example, turtle carapace length is not gecko snout–vent length. Historical one-row records and their aggregation rules do not resolve every taxonomic revision or population difference. The route also does not imply that all species share one reproductive mode or life history, and it does not provide a complete account of biology or current status.
