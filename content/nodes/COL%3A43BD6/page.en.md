## *Mikania leiolaena*: round leaves with dense hairs beneath {#s-b412b76013}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, round, papery leaves with heart-shaped bases and short tapering tips. The upper surface is hairy and the lower surface densely woolly; three strong veins arise from the base. Upright, silky branches bear loose panicles of stalked heads. Their bracts are oblong, fringed, and rounded at the tip. The flower corolla has a bell-shaped limb longer than its tube, with triangular, hairless lobes; the fruitlets are hairless.
