## *Tibouchina nigricans* {#s-d321e7c13c}
The field records unbranched, cylindrical stems with scale-like hairs on the branches. Leaves have five or seven veins; petioles are recorded as either conspicuous and over 5 mm or short, up to 4 mm. Bracteoles are free and oval. Filaments may be hairy or hairless, and a tuft of hairs occurs on the connective. These are the source's alternatives.
