## CGM 60584: trunk and sacral anatomy {#s-6cd91b73f2}
CGM 60584 preserves an elongate posterior trunk, unfused sacrum and reduced limbs used to model tail-driven swimming; no fluke is securely preserved.

## Diet {#s-9402bb7dc3}
Marine predation is inferred from cetacean teeth; prey is not preserved.

## Habitat {#s-72ede8e472}
The known material comes from a marine lower Gehannam depositional setting at Wadi Al Hitan.

## Locomotion {#s-1a6e4fd217}
Mid-body and tail undulation is inferred; a tail fluke is not preserved.

## Body size {#s-66c49ad630}
A large protocetid represented by substantial associated remains; no exact body length is assigned here.

## Ecological role {#s-d2b2789431}
Marine predation is inferred from anatomy rather than observed feeding.
