## Fire-following growth and a two-part flower tube {#s-c9739ba983}
Gladiolus overbergensis is described as an obligate pyrophile, a plant whose flowering depends on fire. Its abrasive leaves have tiny recurved hairs, and the trumpet-shaped flowers have a two-part tube 46–55 mm long; the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are much shorter than the upper ones.

## Sandstone slopes {#s-34bc843e4a}
The flora records sandstone slopes as habitat but does not specify a fire interval or a broader range.
