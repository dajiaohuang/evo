## The source concept {#s-05265e2cef}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Eleutherodactylus cubanus* as row Anf1846, in the genus Eleutherodactylus, family Eleutherodactylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-155ba255c9}

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-dac7185123}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 0.18 g and maximum adult body length of 14 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass or microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not been checked against that revision.
