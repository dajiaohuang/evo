## The name and this data account {#s-0ce5134ce2}
Nestor notabilis is accepted in COL26.8 within Strigopidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 4 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-60a4b93758}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 46.2 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 15.9 mm in width and 28.7 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-aa14925278}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 308.5 mm; mean tarsus length is 45.1 mm. Compiled mean mass is 862.97 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-65ac060372}
The dataset assigns habitat Grassland, trophic niche Omnivore and lifestyle Insessorial. These are dominant categories for cross-species comparisons; they do not imply exclusive use of one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-326bac1159}
The migration field classifies this record as sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -46.26° to -40.78°. This excludes a complete account of non-breeding movements and is not a current-position or global occupancy estimate. Geography and morphological measurements come from different compilation steps.

## Adult mass in the historical account {#s-1a57954d86}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 867.5 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-a904045bcb}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 2.75 offspring/eggs. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Missing reproductive and developmental durations are not supplied here.

## The longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-a837efcf9e}
The life-history archive reports a maximum longevity of 47 years, using the largest record found under its method. This is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current record that can be assumed repeatable.

## Evidence limits {#s-1be4606483}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. One-to-one historical crosswalks and a matching catalogue name provide the association here, not proof of equivalent species concepts after later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status require other sources.
