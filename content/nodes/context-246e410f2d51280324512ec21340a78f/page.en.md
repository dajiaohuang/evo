## Which classification concept does this record use? {#s-01631898bd}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates paleovarzensis* in row Anf0046, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura, following the source concept in *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011). Its correspondence to the current COL26.8 species concept has not been established; the traits below describe this deposited row.

The category remains a historical value, with no replacement inferred and no claim that the original is proven wrong.
