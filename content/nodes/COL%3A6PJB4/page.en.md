## Maximum total length {#s-91420ebb4c}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 220 mm, sourced to Feldman et al. (2016). TL includes the tail, unlike snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled reported maximum, not a typical size or an absolute limit.

## Microhabitat label {#s-c1be3137ec}
The entry records “fossorial” microhabitat, cited to Feldman (2015). The workbook groups this with arboreal, cryptic, saxicolous, semi-aquatic and terrestrial categories, which can also be combined. The label does not describe a specific habitat type or observation locality.

## Reproductive-mode label {#s-448c65cc25}
The row lists reproductive mode as “oviparous,” with Zimin et al. (2022) as its source. It provides no clutch count, annual frequency or egg dimensions, so those details remain unreported.

## What this account leaves open {#s-dce22a8240}
