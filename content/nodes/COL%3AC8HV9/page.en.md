## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d81d73b0a1}
COL26.8 records Terrapene triunguis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Emydidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-e1e38fc4a9}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 180 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman & Meiri 2013.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4a3e62b6e6}
The diet field classifies the entry as plant- and animal-eating (Omnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2011.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-4dcd207e23}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-4858317383}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.

## ReptTraits v1-2 field citations {#s-d2819b0183}
ReptTraits v1-2 records a cited source for each reported trait; the citation beside each value belongs to that field.
