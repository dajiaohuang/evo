## Disa atricapilla {#s-30583b73b7}
The South African account describes Disa atricapilla as a tuberous plant up to 30 cm tall, with linear-lanceolate stem leaves and flowers whose median sepal is white while the lateral sepals are red, black and white. It notes that the species is very close to D. bivalvata and hybridises with it. The recorded habitat is seeps and moist sandstone slopes.
