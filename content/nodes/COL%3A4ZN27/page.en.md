## A broad historical name is restricted by later work {#s-ddcfc6cda8}
The paper explains that summit populations previously combined as satelles include multiple cryptic lineages. It restricts satelles to its Aprada-tepui type locality and describes the Angasima and Upuigma populations as different species. This is a dated source interpretation of historical records; unexamined specimens and neighbouring summit populations still require evidence. Old satelles references cannot all be treated as observations of the currently restricted species.

## Diagnostic comparisons are not new population measurements {#s-ce600af91c}
The new-species comparisons cite satelles as having moderately developed, dorsolaterally projecting frontoparietal crests and a copper iris. The authors also emphasize conserved osteology and extensive colour polymorphism among Stefania. These are source comparison traits, not a sampled frequency distribution, a guaranteed field diagnosis or evidence that crest shape alone resolves every historical satelles specimen.
