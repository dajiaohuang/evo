## Name and placement in COL26.8 {#s-c78c992dfc}
COL26.8 lists Ansonia glandulosa Iskandar & Mumpuni, 2004 as an accepted species. In the release dated 20 August 2026, its recorded path runs through Anura (order), Bufonidae (family) and Ansonia (genus). This placement belongs to that checklist; the same name in another source needs its own identity evidence.

## Maximum adult body length in the compilation {#s-7eefd5447a}
Under AmphiBIO's historical Frost 2011 classification, maximum adult body length is recorded as 39.7 mm. For Anura, body length means snout-to-vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona it means total length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual. Its source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to COL26.8.

## A larval-stage flag {#s-991a25e9bb}
The AmphiBIO record flags a larval stage under its historical Frost 2011 taxon concept. That category alone does not describe parental care, breeding place or seasonal timing, and the concept has not been shown to match the one in COL26.8.
