## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-784b940876}
COL26.8 records Simoselaps littoralis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-3a0cbe0415}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 390 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to How & Shine 1999. The row reports a male maximum SVL of 240 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Greer 1997. The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 390 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016. This row records TL and SVL in separate fields; the captured definitions provide no conversion between them. These overall and sex-specific maxima cite separate studies, but the row gives no matched sample ranges, sample sizes, or localities; they do not establish population-level sexual dimorphism.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a47b5bd19b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cogger 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-52571563c6}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 3.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Slavens and Slavens 1999. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-e9179fb707}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
