## Recorded parent of Pelecanimorphae {#s-879afb5965}
Pelecanimorphae is listed at rank superorder in the PBDB classification route, with Feraequornithes as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
