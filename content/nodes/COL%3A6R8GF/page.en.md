## How are its stems, leaves and floral bracts recorded? {#s-b90559755a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes Medusantha plumosa as having hairy branches and oval leaves with heart-shaped bases. The leaves are toothed along the edge and lack petioles. Its inflorescence bears thread-like bracteoles, the small leaf-like parts beside flowers. The source field does not quantify variation in these traits.
