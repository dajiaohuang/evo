## *Chromolaena epaleacea*: five-veined leaves and bell-shaped heads {#s-2027e0e8ef}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records hairy stems and opposite, egg-shaped leaves with five main veins. Both leaf surfaces are hairy, and the margins are entire or scalloped. Flower heads sit on stalks in corymb-like or forked clusters; their bract cups are bell-shaped. The outer bracts lie close against the cup, while inner bracts may stand upright or lie close. The fruitlets are oblong with short bristles, and the pappus bristles are rough. The substrate field is the broad category “terrestrial.”
