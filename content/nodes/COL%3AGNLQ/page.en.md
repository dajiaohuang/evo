## Aristea palustris {#s-fce5234caf}
Aristea palustris is a robust, branching perennial, commonly about 500 mm tall and reaching 1 m. Its sword-shaped leaves are relatively soft, and its blue flowers sit in translucent spathes with dark keels; the capsules have broad wings. The regional account contrasts its 4–9 mm leaves with the narrower, firm, fibrous leaves of A. oligocephala. It is recorded at wet coastal and lower-slope sites. The comparison and habitat reflect the source’s regional treatment.
