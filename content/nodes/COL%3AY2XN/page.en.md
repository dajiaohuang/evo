## Long leaflets and a hairy ovary {#s-e994e89120}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a single-trunk tree with five to eight or eight to 12 pairs of oblong to lance-shaped leaflets; the entry lists both ranges. The leaflets have long-pointed tips and a leathery texture. Loose branching flower clusters may be shorter than nearby leaves or up to about the same length. The ovary is recorded as hairy. Pods are listed as either 1–2 cm or 2–3.5 cm long, orbicular or oblong, with a yellow seed covering. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
