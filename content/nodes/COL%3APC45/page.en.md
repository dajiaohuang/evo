## Caladium intermedium: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-bdd14e91c3}
The 2020 Brazilian Flora entry records a globose stem. Leaves are peltate, membranous and ovate, with cordate or sagittate bases and posterior lobes; the midrib is green. The field calls the blades variegated but also records them as green without spots. Petioles are cylindrical and striated.

The spathe has a white blade shorter than its green tube. The peduncle is striated and longer than the petiole, and the inflorescence is described as solitary in the sympodium. The female floral zone is yellow; the male zone is recorded as cream or whitish. Fruit is listed as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The leaf-color label and male-zone colors remain ambiguous in the source field.
