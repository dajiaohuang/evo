## A slender basal branching subshrub {#s-ca02027768}
The source description records a slender, glabrescent subshrub to 40 cm tall, branching mainly from the base. Leaves are sessile, fascicled and linear to ovate; they are pilose, becoming scabrid, and apiculate. Flowers are axillary, pink and borne on short pedicels. Capsules are hispid with short, slender horns. The ecology field gives rocky sandstone slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
