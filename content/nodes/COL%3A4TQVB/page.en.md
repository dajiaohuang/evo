## Magenta flowers; fruit and habitat unrecorded {#s-509662c9b0}
The source describes a spreading shrub with decumbent grey to reddish branches, growing from a decumbent to erect habit. Its three-sided leaves reach about 10 × 4–5 × 5–6 mm, and the flowers are magenta in cymes. Fruits are explicitly recorded as unknown, and the habitat entry is marked uncertain.
