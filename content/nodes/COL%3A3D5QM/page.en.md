## Euryops tagetoides {#s-38fcc0847c}
## Finely divided leaves and orange heads in a poorly known species

Manning and Goldblatt's account compares this species with *E. othonnoides*: *E. tagetoides* is taller, up to 1 m, and its leaves are more finely divided, with five to eight lobes on each side. The flower heads are orange and the involucre is cup-shaped. Dry sandstone slopes in arid fynbos are recorded as habitat.

Nordenstam describes the species as rare and imperfectly known. It shares features of habit, indumentum and dry fruits with *E. othonnoides*, but leaf, involucre and ray-floret characters also link it with *E. wageneri*. His treatment places it in section *Euryops* despite an unusual pappus (bristle crown on the fruit) that elongates after flowering. Only scant flowering material had been collected, so whether the rays are always orange remained unresolved.
