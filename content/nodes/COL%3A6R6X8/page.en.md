## Matayba opaca {#s-65c1ff81c4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record for *Matayba opaca* describes leathery, entire leaflets and a closed vein mesh, then gives compact notes on the sepal base, capsule walls, and apical seed aril. A separate habitat field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

## Leathery leaflets and a closed vein mesh {#s-6fb1b7fab1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field places leaves along the branches and describes the leaf rachis as flat or subcylindrical. Leaflets are leathery, flat, and concolorous—that is, the two surfaces are alike in color—with dry specimens recorded as green, yellow, brown, or grey. Their margins are entire, and the lower surface is hairless to nearly hairless. The source calls the venation brochidodromous: secondary veins curve and join toward the leaflet edge. It also lists the upper-surface veins as prominent or inconspicuous, preserving both states as recorded. The vein mesh is closed, secondary veins are arcuate or curved, and domatia are recorded as absent. These are the states in one regional flora field, not a survey of variation.

## Notes on sepals, capsule walls, and seed {#s-103309be0e}
The sepals are joined at the base and sparsely hairy on their lower surface. The capsule-shape entry reads “subglobose/triangular subglobose”; its alternative wording is retained here rather than resolved into a single shape. The outer capsule wall is warty and hairless or nearly so, and the inner wall is hairless. The aril is recorded at the apical end of the seed. The source provides these concise descriptive fields without a broader account of the plant's flowers or fruit development.

## What the habitat field establishes {#s-830ec2dab7}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies a habitat or substrate category, not a collection locality or range. COL26.8 species ID 6R6X8 is crosswalked to one accepted WFO usage, and the Brazilian Flora record carries that WFO ID; the identity match does not establish complete equivalence of taxonomic concepts across versions. The selected record is regional and does not establish a current, global, or complete distribution.
