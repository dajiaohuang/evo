## Accepted name and classification {#s-d13d17a1e4}
COL26.8, released 20 August 2026, accepts Platyplectrum ornatum (Gray, 1842) as a species. Its recorded classification follows Anura (order) → Limnodynastidae (family) → Platyplectrum (genus).

## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-4e0ffb9776}
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Platyplectrum ornatum was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-927d39fec8}
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.

## Conflicting source identifiers prevent a repeat-data assignment {#s-70b344ad5c}
Main Table 1 links Platyplectrum ornatum to GCF_905171775.1, while supplementary Table S1 row 42 labels Platyplectrum ornatum with GCA_016617825.1. That supplementary accession appears under Xenopus borealis in main Table 1. The species-name and accession correspondence therefore cannot be authenticated by simply joining these tables. The supplementary repeat counts and proportions are retained as conflicting source data, without treating them as verified traits of this accepted species. Tables S2–S3 supply 55 clutch and39 larval-period rows by accession without individual provenance or unit labels in their headers; those life-history values are also withheld from species trait assignment pending reconciliation.
