## Ormosia elata {#s-2b112c6310}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ormosia elata* as an accepted species of *Ormosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes leaves with 5–13 leaflets and 9–20 pairs of straight, parallel secondary veins. Flowers are up to 13 mm long; the flattened, dehiscent (splitting open at maturity) pods are usually 2–4 cm wide, and the seeds are red and black, with up to three per fruit. The entry also labels the plant terrestrial.
