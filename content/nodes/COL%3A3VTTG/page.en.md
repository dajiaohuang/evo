## Lobostemon curvifolius {#s-a6891bb143}
## Silvery leaves and pink flowers cover a low shrub

The Greater Cape flora account describes a shrub 30–60 cm tall, with sessile, silvery-hairy leaves that are linear to lance-shaped; their tips often curve backward. Pink flowers grow in cymes and have hairs on the outside. The ridge-like staminal scales, structures associated with the stamens, lack side lobes; the style is hairy.

A 2011 revision notes that this species has often been confused with L. fruticosus and directs readers to that species’ taxonomic note for the differing characters. The habitat account lists sandstone, limestone, or sandy flats.
