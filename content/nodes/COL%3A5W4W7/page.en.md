## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Arremon abeillei R. Lesson, 1844 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## The nest and eggs of Black-capped Sparrow Arremon a. abeillei (Passerellidae) in southwestern Ecuador {#study-account}
Harold F. Greeney documented seven nests of Arremon a. abeillei at two sites in southwestern Ecuador. The article describes enclosed oven-shaped nests hidden among leaf litter; one nest had a confirmed clutch of four eggs, while 13 observed eggs ranged from pale buff to white with sparse dark markings. It also reports a first brood-parasitism record by Molothrus bonariensis. These are bounded nest observations.
