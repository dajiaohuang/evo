## How does the source describe its fringed leaves and spike? {#s-554b0fdc48}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists leaf arrangement as spiral or two-ranked, with flattened, cross-wrinkled blades bearing hairs along their edges. The flower stalk is 20–50 cm long, with one or two ridges; its ellipsoid or oblong spike has more than 25 flowers. The field gives alternative leaf-tip and spike-shape states rather than identifying one form.
