## Three leaflets on a creeping or climbing herb {#s-5d82dba295}
The Flora of Panama describes *Desmodium molliculum* as an herb with stems that creep along the ground or climb from a perennial rootstock. Each leaf has three leaflets; both surfaces are covered with close-lying hairs. The usually upright flower cluster is a raceme, with flowers borne in pairs or threes along its axis. The fruit is a loment, a segmented pod: the account describes up to five curved segments, each with hooked hairs, and seeds about 2 × 1.5 mm. This is a historical regional flora description; it gives no observation date or population sampling frame and does not establish a current or global account.

## Two incomplete Panamanian specimens {#s-797cd2d586}
The Flora of Panama notes that its records from Panama rest on two incomplete specimens, whose leaflets were smaller than usual. The same distribution field gives a broad range from Mexico to South America at rather high altitudes. These statements belong to that historical regional source; they do not provide a current or complete range assessment.
