## The source concept {#s-977ae73c57}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Leptodactylus martinezi in row Anf3404, under the genus Leptodactylus, family Leptodactylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; they do not establish that it matches a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging strata {#s-0e247cbb49}
This row marks fossorial and terrestrial foraging as present categories. These labels are broad and do not describe changes across seasons or life stages. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; this row has not yet been checked against those changes. Treat its categories as provisional historical values: no replacement is supplied here, and the revision does not show that every original entry was wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-e9b4eddf2b}
The row records daytime as an activity category. This summarizes a reported period of activity; it is not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-3d203b08fe}
The compiled maximum adult body length is 25 mm. For Anura, AmphiBIO measures body length from the tip of the snout to the vent (snout–vent length); this is a reported maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-ec1535bd5b}
The row gives 6 as the minimum and 8 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. It reports offspring-or-egg sizes from 2.4 mm to 2.6 mm. AmphiBIO compiles extrema and counts from literature, but this row does not specify the sex, population, date, or wild versus captive conditions behind each value. The size entries may describe eggs or offspring.
