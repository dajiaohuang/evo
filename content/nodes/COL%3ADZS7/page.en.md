## Minute stems and tiny white flowers {#s-75ae7950f5}
Angraecum chamaeanthus is a tiny epiphyte (a plant that grows on another plant for support) with minute stems and three or four narrow leaves up to 16 mm long. Its inflorescence is under 20 mm long and its white flowers are less than 2 mm across.

## Cool, moist forest branches {#s-a722d3a6fc}
The regional account records the plant on twigs and branches in cool, moist forests.
