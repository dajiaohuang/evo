## Aspilia duarteana {#s-7e20b5042b}
## Two-ranked leaves and nearly cylindrical flower heads

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes woody stems with shaggy hairs. The petiolate leaves are opposite in two ranks, papery, and lance-shaped to ovate or ovate-lance-shaped. The flower-head clusters may be corymbs; each radiate head has a subcylindrical involucre of overlapping bracts and a flat receptacle bearing paleae, or narrow chaff scales. Flowers are yellow, with neutral outer ligulate florets and bisexual central tubular florets. Central anthers form a tube; disc florets are fertile, while the female parts of ray florets are rudimentary. Dry fruits have an oblong to oblanceolate attachment base and a pappus without awns.
