## Overlapping body sizes in a syntopic comparison {#s-01a18775e7}
The phyllofolia study reported arathooni in syntopy at Bontomaranu and gave its comparison SVL range 29.47–44.3 mm, average 35.23. This overlaps the upper end of the phyllofolia sample, which reached 30.13 mm, so size alone cannot reliably separate every animal. Multivariate morphological differences supported the study’s comparison but are not a complete field key or a guarantee for unsampled populations. Reduced webbing and a postorbital groove were attributed to both species in cited descriptions and should not be treated as uniquely phyllofolia characters.

## Ten chirping calls from five males {#s-9fe0eec155}
The study analysed ten arathooni advertisement calls from five males, including one non-vouchered animal. Calls contained 2–7 notes, lasted 0.355–3.537 seconds and had pulse rates 0.57–6.13 notes per second. Their high-pitched chirping differed from the sampled phyllofolia clicks, but dominant-frequency ranges overlapped: arathooni was 1,751.40–2,813.67 Hz. These are recording-sample statistics, not species-wide bounds or experimentally tested recognition cues. Ten calls do not represent ten independent males, and the non-vouchered record retains a different identity-evidence level.
