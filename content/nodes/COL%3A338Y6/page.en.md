## A milky-sapped climber with a cup-shaped corona {#s-c0a73e0604}
The regional account describes a climber to 2 m with a woody base and milky sap. Its opposite leaves are fleshy and elliptic-oblong. Flowers occur in extra-axillary clusters and are brown to green; the white corona is cup-shaped and five-crenate, and the gynostegium is shortly stalked. The habitat entry says coastal bush; these account fields do not establish a complete range.
