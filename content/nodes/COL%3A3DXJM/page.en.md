## Felicia ebracteata {#s-a482464d8a}
## Closely packed leaves and long bare flower stalks

This roughly hairy shrublet grows to 40 cm, with oblong to lance-shaped leaves held partly upright; the margins roll under and are spiny. Its radiate flower heads are described as solitary at branch tips, yellow with dark-blue rays. A taxonomic note emphasizes the contrast between the densely overlapping leaves on the stems and the long flower stalks that lack bracts. Habitat is recorded on limestone hills.
