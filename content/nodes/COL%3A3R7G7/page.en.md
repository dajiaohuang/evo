## What sets Kielmeyera trichophora apart? {#s-2a7d2ad7dd}
The record notes a xylopodium, a woody underground root structure, and upright stems. The leathery leaves are oblong or obovate, with tips ranging from short-pointed to rounded and bases from rounded to shallowly heart-shaped. Flower clusters have more than three flowers. The white petals are only slightly asymmetric, and the green inner and outer sepals are alike. Anther tips may be recurved and their pollen-bearing chambers are described as divided; the anther glands lie just below the tip. Pollen is recorded as tetrads or polyads. The ovary and style are hairless.
