## Scarlet flowers above glossy leaves {#s-adae2c3e89}
The source describes a sprawling, ascending herb with somewhat woody stems up to 1 m long. Large, glossy green leaves have sharply toothed edges, sometimes with a second set of smaller teeth. Solitary flowers arise from upper leaf axils; the long corolla is bright red or scarlet, sometimes paler outside. The fruits are white, broadly oval berries. The recorded range includes Costa Rica, Panama and Colombia.
