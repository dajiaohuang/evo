## The species and its compiled row {#s-a350062a21}
COL26.8 accepts Homonota rupicola within Phyllodactylidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 entry follows the workbook’s Reptile Database 2023 name framework and retains sources for each trait. The one-row summary describes compiled reports, not a uniform state across the species.

## A maximum length to the vent {#s-6e343a089d}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 36 mm; the female maximum is 35.4 mm. SVL measures from snout to vent, not including the tail. These are compiled maxima rather than typical sizes or growth limits. Their sex-specific sources may differ in locality or study, so the difference alone does not establish the size contrast in one population.

## Ground use, animal food and night activity {#s-02aa4811ab}
The entry records terrestrial microhabitat, a carnivorous diet and nocturnal activity. These labels describe the cited observations, not a complete habitat map or fixed schedule. The diet category does not identify prey, and the one-row summary leaves variation by locality, season or life stage unresolved.

## Reproductive mode without a clutch count {#s-8a63c90fcc}
The source labels reproduction as oviparous, or egg-laying, but gives no clutch or litter number. The entry therefore does not show how many eggs are laid per event. This mode label does not establish embryo nutrition or prove that reproduction is the same in every population.

## Limits of the species summary {#s-21997ccd6b}
This page paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row under its 2023 naming framework. A single row compresses variation in age, sex, locality and population; a name-and-family match does not prove equivalence with later concepts. The length is a compiled maximum, not a species-wide ceiling, and reproductive mode is not a clutch count. Other mass fields may be estimates from length equations, while juvenile-length definitions vary; neither is treated as a direct measurement here. Conservation, venom, current range and population trends are not updated, and the source chain does not imply that the original studies were independently reread for this page.
