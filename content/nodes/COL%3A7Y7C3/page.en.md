## What rhizome, flowers and ovary traits are recorded? {#s-9173173c6f}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records a rhizome and young stems without pale hairs. The inflorescence is corymb-shaped, with flower stalks reaching a shared level. The floral cup is hairless. Flowers have five or six petals, and the ovary tip bears short bristles; the ovary has four chambers. This regional database field gives no sampling frame or variation details.
