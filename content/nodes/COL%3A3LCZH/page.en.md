## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Heteropterys hypericifolia? {#s-fac3a37e1b}
Opposite leaves are thin and oval, with heart-shaped bases and rounded tips. Both surfaces are hairless; dark, stalkless glands occur at the base, and the margins roll under. Upright inflorescences have persistent, oval bracts without glands and stalked pedicels. Sepals have flat tips and may bear glands except on the front sepal; they are hairless. Yellow clawed petals have a keel and entire margins. Anthers are hairless. Styles are equal, curved or diverging distally, and hairless, with pointed tips. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-41ce51b909}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
