## A whole-plant fossil and the limits of Leefructus {#s-98e9babc0e}
Leefructus mirus from a 125.8–122.6 Ma bed in the Lower Yixian Formation preserves deeply trilobate leaves and an axillary reproductive axis ending in five long, narrow, partly united carpels. The linked leaves and reproductive structure support an Early Cretaceous eudicot assignment. Ranunculaceae was proposed from morphology, but that family affinity remains provisional; one locality does not establish the global first appearance of eudicots.
