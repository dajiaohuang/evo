## Drosanthemum leptum {#s-47b73e5ff9}
## Papillate, triangular leaves surround flowers of uncertain colour

The Greater Cape flora’s species description records an erect shrublet up to 35 cm tall, with scattered, flattened papillae on the branches. Stem bladder cells have rounded tips. Its three-angled leaves curve slightly backward and are densely papillate. Flowers are solitary, five-chambered fruits follow, and the species-level flower colour is left unknown.

The same source first gives a broader statement that petals may be yellow, orange, red, or rarely white and that sterile stamens can be present; its species-specific passage then says they are absent. The habitat record is rocky ravines.
