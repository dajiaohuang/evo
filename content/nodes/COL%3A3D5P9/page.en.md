## Tiny fleshy leaves on a twiggy shrublet {#s-301e461e3b}
This record describes a sparsely leafy, twiggy shrublet up to 60 cm tall. Its small, entire, fleshy leaves are easy to recognize, and solitary yellow radiate heads sit on slender stalks. The fruits are conspicuously woolly. The source treats the genus name Thodaya as a synonym of Euryops and considers the short, stout-stalked Sutherland collection conspecific with plants from Whitehill. Rocky slopes are recorded as habitat.
