## *Duvalia immaculata* {#s-235d78c725}
The cited comparison with *Duvalia caespitosa* notes somewhat rhizome-forming stems and dark reddish-brown, nearly hairless flowers, with a bright yellow corona (the ring of floral structures around the reproductive parts) inside the annulus. This is a comparison, not a full description.

A second account compares plants with *D. modesta*: typical plants form compact mats with four-angled stems and slender leaf rudiments on the tubercles. It also describes differences among plants from named districts, including flower surface, lobe folding, colour, and corona; some Colesberg and Richmond plants are said to correspond closely to *D. gracilis*. These are source-reported comparisons and geographic observations, not a settled range-wide account.

The source lists dry coastal fynbos as habitat. It supplies no locality or collection date here.
