## Name and trait record {#s-854e5c2895}
COL26.8 places *Cerastes gasperettii* in Viperidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 row uses the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and keeps separate sources for its trait fields. The row compiles reports, not a uniform profile of every population.

## Two length measures {#s-ecfaa4bfe5}
The reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) is 547 mm and the maximum total length (TL) is 860 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent; TL includes the tail. These compiled maxima are the largest cited values, not growth limits or typical sizes. They need not describe the same individual, and sex-specific records from different places or studies do not by themselves measure sexual dimorphism within a population.

## Microhabitat and activity {#s-50e2d0f5bb}
The row records a Terrestrial microhabitat and Cathemeral active time, a category for activity across day and night. These are broad labels, not a complete habitat account or fixed daily schedule. Reports may differ among populations, seasons and life stages.

## Reproduction in ReptTraits {#s-cdac41be77}
The ReptTraits row labels reproduction oviparous, or egg-laying, and gives a consolidated mean of 12 eggs per clutch. The dataset may calculate this summary from the midpoint of reported means or, when those are unavailable, from reported endpoints; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The value does not establish a fixed clutch size or uniformity among populations.

## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-d03a920426}
COL26.8 places *Cerastes gasperettii* in Viperidae. A separate 2015 life-history archive supplies three fields from historical species-level rows with the same name and family. Such compilations can carry values between species or subspecies; name matching supports this association but does not prove that every source used the same concept or remove inherited values.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-1d0078749f}
The 2015 archive separately reports 12.5 eggs per clutch and 48 d from oviposition to hatching; d means days. Its fractional clutch value is a compilation summary, not part of an individual clutch. These archive values are distinct from the ReptTraits mean above, and the time field describes one interval rather than a complete reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-5092ee6afb}
The archive's maximum_longevity_y field is 18 years, the largest record found under its method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current record that can be assumed repeatable.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-bc9ee98cb3}
Source labels for the historical rows include Reptile Incubation book; Birchard, G. and D. Marcellini, 1996; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; and Carey, J.R. and D.S. Judge, 2002. The archive associates labels and values with individual fields. A label may refer to a paper, book or database; row counts are not counts of measured animals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replication.

## Evidence limits {#s-3ef9a55eab}
The two sources retain different scopes. The ReptTraits row uses its 2023 name framework and reports selected length, activity and reproductive fields; one row can combine records that vary by sex, age, locality or population. Its maximum lengths are not species limits, and mass fields may be estimates from length equations. The 2015 archive reports medians or averages of two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. Those summaries are not individual measurements, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical data may be shared or inherited among species and subspecies; matching name and family does not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove inherited values. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently identified, and the underlying cited works were not individually checked for this account. Current range, conservation and unreported traits are not established here.
