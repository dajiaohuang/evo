## An urn-shaped flower cup and uneven vein counts {#s-e714e41609}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description lists three kinds of hairs—star-shaped, simple and tree-like—on young stems. Ovate leaves have acute or blunt tips and heart-shaped or rounded bases; their margins lack fringe-like hairs and may be toothed. The field lists five or seven main veins, and also includes a five-plus-two description of faint veins just inside the margin, so it does not settle on one count. The lower surface is hairless. Flowers form dense clusters. The urn-shaped flower cup bears a persistent calyx with distinct lobes; each flower has five petals. The tissue joining the anther's pollen sacs is not prolonged and bears two lower-side lobes. White anthers have one small pore, and the ovary is hairless. The listed alternatives do not establish how the traits vary among plants.
