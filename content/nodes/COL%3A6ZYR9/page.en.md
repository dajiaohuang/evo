## Strumaria luteoloba {#s-5aa9129bce}
Strumaria luteoloba is marked by erect, fan-arranged leaves and pale lemon to cream flowers. Its narrow tepals are deeply channelled and sharply reflexed, with the outer whorl set below the inner, creating a two-tiered appearance. The account records rocky slopes on dolomite or granite; it does not provide population frequencies.
