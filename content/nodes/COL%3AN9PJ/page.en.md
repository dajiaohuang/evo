## Accepted name and placement {#s-46269492c0}
COL26.8 accepts Breviceps poweri Parker, 1934 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brevicipitidae (family) → Breviceps (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
