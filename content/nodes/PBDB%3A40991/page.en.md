## Vulpavus in the PBDB genus path {#s-a7c41124de}
PBDB records Vulpavus at genus rank, and this selected guide links seven fossil-species pages. The database parent chain organizes records; it does not establish phylogeny or a complete list of genus membership.

## Ninety occurrences from seventy-five collections {#s-80fb8c6d60}
The selected pages include Vulpavus canavus, V. ovatus, V. hargeri, V. farsonensis, and V. australis. Across the guide, 90 occurrences come from 75 collections; DeBeque, Willwood, Wasatch, and Wind River are example stratum names. Each occurrence has its own age and identification fields to read with the cited source. These totals count database records, not animals or populations.

## Relationships studied; numerical range withheld {#s-5e5358865b}
A cladistic study samples Vulpavus when examining relationships among early miacids, but leaves broader “Miacidae” relationships poorly resolved. It supports treating Vulpavus as a sampled fossil branch, not a direct carnivoran ancestor. The available evidence does not provide auditable numerical genus endpoints, so no numerical range is shown.
