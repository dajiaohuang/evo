## PBDB classification and seven selected species pages {#s-a7c41124de}
PBDB records Vulpavus at genus rank, and this guide links seven selected fossil species pages. The recorded parent chain is a database classification; it does not establish phylogeny or complete genus membership.

## 90 occurrences across 75 collections {#s-80fb8c6d60}
Five linked pages—Vulpavus canavus, V. ovatus, V. hargeri, V. farsonensis and V. australis—offer a starting set for comparing records and cited literature. The selected guide contains 90 occurrences from 75 collections. DeBeque, Willwood, Wasatch and Wind River appear as example stratum names. Compare each occurrence’s age and identification fields with its source; these counts describe database entries, not animals or populations.

## Relationships studied; numerical range withheld {#s-5e5358865b}
A cladistic study samples Vulpavus when examining relationships among early miacids, but leaves broader “Miacidae” relationships poorly resolved. It supports treating Vulpavus as a sampled fossil branch, not a direct carnivoran ancestor. The available evidence does not provide auditable numerical genus endpoints, so no numerical range is shown.
