## ## Short lobes, smooth leaves and an elastic pod {#s-eb65f50ff5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes leaves divided shallowly into two nearly round lobes, with a heart-shaped base and rounded to almost truncate tips. Both surfaces are recorded as hairless, and the smaller veins are faint or sunken. The five-ridged flower bud has a notched tip; at flowering, the calyx splits into two to five lobes, and ten anthers are listed. The legume opens elastically. These regional descriptors do not establish the species’ full variation.
