## Traits cited in the Deo Ca comparison {#s-30e8db2019}
The 2026 description of L. deocaensis cites strongly tuberculate dorsal skin, a white belly with small grey spots or streaks and rudimentary toe webbing for Leptobrachella tuberosa. These traits come from the literature comparison, not newly measured animals of this species in the Deo Ca survey. The comparison contrasts it with the newly described population and is not a complete identification key. It supplies neither trait frequencies across populations nor new measurements of diet, development or reproductive success.
