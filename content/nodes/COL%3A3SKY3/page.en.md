## ## Narrow leaflets and divided tendrils {#s-4136c53730}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry for *Lathyrus nitens* lists lance-shaped or linear, very narrow leaflets with prominent veins. Its stipules are semi-sagittate, meaning partly arrowhead-shaped. The same field records branching as absent and branches as winged, with thin, flattened edges. Tendrils are described as setaceous (bristle-like) or trifid (split into three tips). The raceme, a flower cluster arranged along a central axis, bears more than eight flowers, colored bluish to purple. Its stigma, the pollen-receiving tip, is divided in two. A separate habitat field says terrestrial, without giving a locality or survey coverage. The regional entry does not establish whether listed alternatives reflect variation or a full range.
