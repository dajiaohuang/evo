## Population evidence {#s-22009761cb}
Ibouroi and colleagues combined surveys in northwestern Madagascar in October and December 2012 with surveys in the Comoros from 2017 to 2019 and habitat-suitability modelling. The paper reports transect-based density estimates of 23.49 animals/km² in the Comoros (89.9 km of transects) and 15.23 animals/km² in Madagascar (65 km), with modelled population sizes of 9,128 and 7,270, respectively. These are estimates conditional on the survey records and model, not current census counts.
