## Population evidence {#s-22009761cb}
Ibouroi and colleagues combined surveys in northwestern Madagascar in October and December 2012 with surveys in the Comoros from 2017 to 2019 and habitat-suitability modelling. The paper reports transect-based density estimates of 23.49 animals/km² in the Comoros (89.9 km of transects) and 15.23 animals/km² in Madagascar (65 km), with modelled population sizes of 9,128 and 7,270, respectively. These are estimates conditional on the survey records and model, not current census counts.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-bf1b23a50f}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 1915.57 g; mass at birth or hatching 55.97 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-eb7d452e9c}
The archive’s nonzero food weights are nectar, pollen and plant exudates 50%; other plant material (the source category also includes fungi) 30%; fruit 20%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. ABC cannot be rewritten as direct observations of this species. ## Evidence limits {#s-174b0be6a7}
The 2012 Madagascar and 2017–2019 Comoros surveys are separate periods and regions; the reported abundance values depend on those transect samples and habitat model, so they are not current counts.
Amniote LifeHistory 2015 reports historical summaries, generally medians (or averages of the two middle records) and maximum longevity as the largest recorded value. Values may be shared across historical species and subspecies; a name match does not establish equivalence to the pinned COL26.8 concept, and wild or captive conditions are not identified consistently.
