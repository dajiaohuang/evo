## Mimosa suburbana {#s-a6f221abf6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa suburbana* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes a short-haired stem, recurved prickles arranged in rows, and a projection between pairs of leaf divisions. Its globose flower clusters bear flowers with four or five perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairless petal lobes; the source labels it terrestrial.
