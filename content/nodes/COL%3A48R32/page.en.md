## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-f2b81dfbd1}
The Atlantic Forest trait compilation codes Odontophrynus cultripes as having larval development and its oviposition as aquatic. A larval-development code does not specify larval duration, all larval habitats or the degree of dependence on standing water; those require additional evidence. These are literature-compiled categories, not newly observed spawning or a developmental experiment for this species. ## Juvenile habitat and egg-site categories have defined scope {#s-800a37b0cd}

The positive category summarizes a broad trait; it does not establish a specific nest architecture, substrate species or oviposition height.
