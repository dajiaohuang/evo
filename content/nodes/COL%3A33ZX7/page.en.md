## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Dalechampia liesneri? {#s-39e21830dc}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium and has simple, oval leaves; no lobed or compound leaves are listed. The surrounding bracts are inconspicuous, and resin glands are absent. Female sepals are lance-shaped, and the style-column tip is cylindrical. The fruit bears silky hairs; seeds are smooth or globose.
