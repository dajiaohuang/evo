## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-9d0f3888d1}
In the Paleobiology Database (PBDB), Campylognathoides is recorded as a genus under Campylognathoididae. This database classification organizes fossil records; it does not by itself propose an ancestor relationship.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-cf8fbdf8dc}
Linked occurrence guides are available for Campylognathoides liasicus and C. zitteli. Their collection names, strata and age brackets belong to the individual records; compare those details before inferring a wider pattern.
