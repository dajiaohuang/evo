## Aloe ammophila {#s-db6ab3b740}
Aloe ammophila is a stemless, freely suckering aloe that forms dense groups. Its spotted, toothed leaves surround a branched flowering stalk; the regional account notes that groups of a hundred or more may occur. It favours sandy soils in flat grassland, sometimes in shrub clearings. Those observations describe the cited regional account, not an abundance survey.
