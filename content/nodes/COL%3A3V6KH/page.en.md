## Which record these facts describe {#s-3d1cad56bf}
The pinned COL26.8 checklist accepts Liolaemus pachecoi in the family Liolaemidae. The traits below come from its ReptTraits v1-2 row, which follows the Reptile Database's 2023 name framework and cites sources by trait. That row describes the compilation, not every population as if it were identical.

## What the length number measures {#s-6941de3c1a}
The row reports a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 93.8 mm. SVL measures from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. This compiled maximum is neither a typical size nor a biological ceiling. No sex-specific maximum is interpreted here, so the record cannot show how lengths differ between sexes.

## Microhabitat, food, and time of activity {#s-e70ccefa1b}
The microhabitat field combines Saxicolous, a rock-associated category, and Terrestrial, a ground-associated one. The row also classifies its diet as omnivorous, or including plant and animal material, and its activity as diurnal, or active by day. These broad labels do not describe a complete habitat or prey list, and the one-row summary does not show how traits vary among places, seasons, or life stages.

## What the reproductive label means {#s-75c8eba3d2}
The row labels the reproductive mode viviparous, or live-bearing, but gives no consolidated litter count or low/high litter values. The mode label alone does not describe litter size or how it varies among populations. Missing counts are not evidence that all litters have the same size.

## Limits of this account {#s-211f83e24f}
This page paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row and keeps its 2023 name framework and trait-specific citations. A single row compresses differences in sex, age, locality, and population; the same name and family do not prove that a historical record matches every later taxonomic concept. The reported maximum is not an absolute size limit. ReptTraits also includes mass estimates derived from length equations; they are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length fields have differing headers and definitions, so they are not used as conclusions. This page does not update conservation status, venom, current distribution, or population trends. The citation labels retain the dataset's source chain; they do not mean this project independently rechecked every original study.
