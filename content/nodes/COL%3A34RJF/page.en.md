## Yellow flower heads on a woody-stemmed plant {#s-b9a8a43011}
The morphology record describes an erect plant with a woody base, or woody stems, and alternate lance-shaped to ovate-lanceolate leaves. Their edges are toothed, and the lower surface is smooth. Branched clusters carry radiate heads with yellow ray and disk florets. The cylindrical fruits are ribbed and smooth, with a persistent white pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
