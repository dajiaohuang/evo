## Minaria polygaloides {#s-ce1bdddb88}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Minaria polygaloides* as an accepted species of *Minaria*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records opposite, narrow lanceolate to linear leaves with flat margins and single-flowered cymes. The bell-shaped flower has hairy or finely papillose lobes and a hairy throat. Its habitat field labels it terrestrial; this is a regional description, not a complete account of variation.
