## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-60a242caa0}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 171 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.
