## What leaf, flower and ovary traits are recorded? {#s-400c269a4b}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands on the stem and on petiolate leaves that have the same color on both faces and one vein. The inflorescence is a dichasium. Its oblong floral cup bears spherical glands; triangular sepals also have glands and are shorter than the cup. Petals are white. Anthers match one another in color and have multiple pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. These are entries in a regional database field, not a global account.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c2ac7be4f3}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial; no locality or date is supplied.
