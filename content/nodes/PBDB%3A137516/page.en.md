## Recorded parent of Rubidgeinae {#s-030c46df36}
Rubidgeinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Gorgonopidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
