## Gymnopentzia bifurcata {#s-fa14419620}
## Forking leaves and bright yellow flower heads

The source describes an upright or decumbent shrub up to 1.2 m tall. Opposite leaves have sheathing bases; their blades are simple or forked dichotomously one to three times into narrow linear lobes. The lobes may be hairless or covered by sparse to dense long hairs. Bright yellow flower heads are grouped at branch tips. Habitat includes open grassland, rocky gullies and rock outcrops.
