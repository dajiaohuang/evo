## Cayaponia racemosa (Mill.) Cogn. {#s-e780bca18c}
## Variable, rough leaves and orange-red fruit

Cayaponia racemosa is a monoecious vine with stems that range from minutely hairy to nearly smooth. Its simple leaves may be entire, three-lobed or more deeply palm-divided; they are papery to somewhat leathery and have rough hairs, pale blister-like bumps on the upper surface, and small glands near the base. The tendrils branch in two. Male flowers grow in axillary racemes or panicles, sometimes alone, and are yellowish white to greenish white. Smooth, hairless fruits are ellipsoid to egg-shaped, 1.3–2 cm long and orange to red. Each contains two or three rough-coated, orange-brown seeds. The cited range spans Mexico, Central America, the West Indies and northern South America.
