## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-83bc681fab}
The species-level foraging-stratum estimate is divided equally: 50% in understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m and 50% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy. It estimates relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a whole-day time budget. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-c042bf78d1}
This is a guide to historical AVONET v7 data. Morphological values are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass uses separately cited literature or weighing sources; morphological sample counts must not be assigned to mass. Habitat, diet and migration are coarse categories; geography concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only.
