## Dacryodes sclerophylla {#s-f03e38691c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record assigns *Dacryodes sclerophylla* the habitat category “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

## What the terrestrial field establishes {#s-f7b57404bd}
“Terrícola” is the habitat or substrate category recorded for this species. The field gives no collection locality, survey date or complete-range information, so it describes a category rather than a mapped occurrence.
