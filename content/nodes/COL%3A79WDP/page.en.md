## Triangular stems up to 1.5 m {#s-7431708381}
The regional flora describes *Schoenoplectus triqueter* as a perennial with triangular stems reaching 1.5 m and brown spikelets.

## Pools near the coast {#s-9afdf798f3}
The SANBI habitat entry records the edges of pools near the coast. It does not give a dated sampling frame or population frequency, so this regional habitat note is not a full-range survey.
