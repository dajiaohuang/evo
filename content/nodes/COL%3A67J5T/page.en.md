## Leaves and fruit {#s-56ccf33dff}
The Brazilian Flora description records erect stems, oblong or obovate leaf blades with tapering tips, and an ovate spathe (a bract around the flower cluster). The blade base is listed in several forms—acute, obtuse, rounded, or truncate—and the berries are described as reddish. These are regional descriptors, not a quantified account of variation.

## Epiphytic and soil-associated entries {#s-6cd0b5dab2}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 has separate habitat categories “epífita” (growing on another plant) and “terrícola” (soil-associated). The rows provide no localities or dates, so they record categories without mapping their occurrences.
