## What leaf, flower and style traits are recorded for Peixotoa catarinensis? {#s-2198f87c98}
Inflorescence stipules are 5–9 mm long and pubescent. Leaf stalks are 6–9 mm; ovate or elliptic blades have a blistered upper surface, stalkless glands and velvety hairs below, with margins rolled under. A solitary umbel carries four flowers. The pedicels are up to 20 mm long. Anther sacs are recorded as always open; the staminode glands are smooth, and the styles are unequal in length.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3b42137bac}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
