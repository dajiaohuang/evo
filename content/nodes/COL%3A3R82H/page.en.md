## Name and this data account {#s-3e74fa4501}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts Kinixys spekii in Testudinidae. This account follows its ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and trait-specific sources. Because the dataset has one row per species, its categories describe that record rather than every population.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-70717bc747}
The recorded maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 200 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. This is a maximum in the reported material, not a theoretical limit or typical individual size. Sex-specific maxima, when available, may come from different localities or studies and cannot alone quantify sexual dimorphism across a population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-068be70387}
The row classifies the microhabitat as Terrestrial, diet as Omnivorous, activity as Diurnal and foraging mode as active (ACT). These are broad categories, not a complete habitat or food inventory or a fixed schedule. They do not identify particular foods or show variation among populations, seasons or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-8991da651c}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, a mean of 3.5 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, and low and high reported means of 2 and 4. A summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The fractional mean is a dataset summary, not a count from one event. The low and high fields are endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed individual-clutch limits. These records do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-05fc097a19}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps. Dataset citation labels preserve the source chain and do not imply that this project independently reread every original work.
