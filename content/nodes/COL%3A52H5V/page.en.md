## *Stevia verticillata*: whorled leaves and terminal panicles {#s-4584f6356f}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records petiolate leaves arranged in whorls. Loose, stalked flower heads form terminal panicles. The fruitlets bear bristly hairs, and the pappus is papery with awns. Its substrate entry is the broad category “terrestrial,” without a locality.
