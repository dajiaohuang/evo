## The name and this data account {#s-701b85ff03}
Aratinga auricapillus is accepted in COL26.8 within Psittacidae. AVONET v7 lists 5 measured birds under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name, 4 with a complete morphological set. Usable counts may differ by trait.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-26cbffc069}
Mean beak length is 23.9 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip. At that edge, mean width is 13.3 mm and depth is 23.3 mm. These are separate measurements at defined landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-14e7cfb747}
Mean unflattened wing length from carpal joint to longest primary is 154.2 mm, and mean tarsus length is 15.9 mm. A separate compilation gives mean mass as 130 g (Dunning category); this does not imply all morphological specimens were weighed.
