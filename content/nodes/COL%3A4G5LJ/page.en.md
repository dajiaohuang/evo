## Identification difficulty and a proposed molecular review {#s-a61d0e2f2a}
The authors emphasized that Philippine Philautus were difficult to identify because of similar size, variable appearance, colour polymorphism and similar crunch-like mating calls. They suggested that future molecular data might reveal multiple species within the widely distributed surdus name. The cited Least Concern category was based on the historical broad concept. This suggestion is not a completed taxonomic split, and the category is not presented as an independently checked current assessment.
