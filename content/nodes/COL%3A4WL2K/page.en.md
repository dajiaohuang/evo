## Toothed leaves with woolly undersides {#s-cc028361ce}
This record describes an upright herb with lance-shaped, toothed leaves whose bases clasp the stem. The upper surfaces are hairy and the lower surfaces woolly. Yellow ray and disk florets form radiate heads in corymb-like clusters. The cylindrical, ribbed fruits are silky or dotted with small projections; the white pappus persists. The habitat field says terrestrial.
