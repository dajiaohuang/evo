## A slender climber with pink to red tubular flowers {#s-bfa210bf18}
The regional description records a slender climber to 1 m or more, with fibrous roots and clear sap. Opposite leaves are narrow and 7–35 mm long. Cylindrical flowers are 5–11 mm long, at least slightly pubescent outside, and pink to red, with lanceolate petals. The habitat entry ranges from stony slopes to sandy flats; it does not define the full distribution.
