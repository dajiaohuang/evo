## What colors are recorded in its flower cluster? {#s-1cac8bba35}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an obovoid inflorescence with green, ovate bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) and leaf-like appendages. The corolla is listed as white or yellow; its petal-like stamens and flower lip are yellow. The corolla and ovary are hairy. The source also records tuberculate roots and a rhizome (a horizontal underground stem), while some stem-joint and leaf-tip states remain alternatives.

## What rocky habitat label is recorded? {#s-064229c1a9}
The regional data row labels the species rupicolous, meaning rock-dwelling. It gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
