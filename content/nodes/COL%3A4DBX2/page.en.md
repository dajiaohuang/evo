## Parasenegalia santosii {#s-643f0ee543}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Parasenegalia santosii* as an accepted species of *Parasenegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records 4–6 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), each with 10–19 pairs of leaflets, and flower heads 8–9 mm across. It notes cup-shaped glands along the leaf stalk, silky calyx (the outer layer formed by sepals) and corolla (the petal layer) surfaces, and a legume fruit. These are the characters in the regional flora entry.
