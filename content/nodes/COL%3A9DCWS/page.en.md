## ## Retrorse prickles and globose inflorescences {#s-fc9dc66359}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records pubescent to pilose stems with simple hairs described as spreading, incurved or recurved. Capitate glandular hairs and serial, retrorse prickles are listed; setae and short shoots are absent. The stipules persist, extrafloral nectaries are absent, and a projection between pinna pairs may be present or absent. The inflorescences are globose, with floral bracts shorter than the flowers before opening. The flowers have four or five perianth parts and a membranous calyx with absent or triangular lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and may be hairless or pubescent; the androecium is diplostemonous, with pink filaments. These listed states are not a quantified account of variation.
