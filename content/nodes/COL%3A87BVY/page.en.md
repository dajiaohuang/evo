## Measuring the brachidium {#s-e5322b104c}
Three-dimensional geometric morphometrics compares the shape of long-looped brachidia in selected living terebratulides. The analysis uses those measurements to test species assignments within this sample.

## A focused sample, not the whole order {#s-37d120363c}
The analysis concerns selected living forms and their species-level assignments. It does not characterize every member of Terebratulida or establish the order’s fossil duration.
