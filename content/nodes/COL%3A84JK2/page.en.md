## Classification {#s-719640cca4}
The original description of Groves's dwarf lemur used material collected in Ranomafana and Andringitra National Parks. Combining genetic and morphometric analyses, the authors distinguished the southeastern Madagascar lineage within the C. crossleyi group and formally described it as a new species. These parks are the paper's sampling localities, not a fully delimited range boundary.

## Evidence limits {#s-788df0078e}
The 2017 description and its two sampled parks define the field scope; later inference or single-locality observations do not establish the complete current range.
