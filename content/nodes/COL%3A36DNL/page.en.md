## Diosma strumosa {#s-2386997bd3}
The regional descriptions distinguish *Diosma strumosa* by its deeply pitted fruit, recurved petals, wine-coloured anthers and sparsely hairy floral parts.

## A deeply pitted fruit and recurved petals {#s-288733ad61}
A 2012 description compares this species with *D. passerinoides*, noting a style and filaments with sparse hairs, purple anthers and a rough, pitted fruit. The 1979 account calls the plant distinctive: it describes a deeply pitted, swollen fruit, long recurved petals, wine-coloured anthers and disc, and minute hairs sparsely covering the style and filaments. That account says no other *Diosma* occupies a similar habitat on Bokkeveld shales; it identifies *D. passerinoides* as the nearest comparison and associates it with silcrete hillocks on the Bokkeveld beds. The name is said to allude to swellings on the fruit. The later habitat field records shale slopes in renosterveld. These are regional descriptions, not a dated survey of the genus or its distribution.
