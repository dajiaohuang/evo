## PBDB’s recorded fossil classification {#s-eda5c0ce2e}
PBDB’s 2 October 2026 snapshot records Plagiosterninae Shishkin 1986 at subfamily rank under Plagiosauridae. This page follows that supplementary fossil classification; it does not establish that a similarly named COL entry represents the same taxon.

## What the captured fossil records show {#s-5c00b67ff1}
In the captured PBDB hierarchy, 6 accepted fossil-species concepts are placed under Plagiosterninae. The occurrence snapshot contains 17 records with exact taxon-ID matches; 0 of the 6 concepts have no matching record in this capture. Across these 17 dated collection intervals, the outer age bounds run from 250.8 to 227.3 million years ago. These pooled collection bounds do not date the taxon’s origin or extinction and do not show continuous presence through the full span. PBDB’s placement is supplementary and does not establish equivalence with a similarly named COL group.
