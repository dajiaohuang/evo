## What traits mark Plantago coronopus? {#s-ee284ba352}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records an unthickened main root and a barely visible stem base, with no aerial stem. Its narrow, linear leaves taper to pointed tips. At maturity the flowering stalk stands upright; its flat, ribbon-like hairs point toward the tip. The flowers have four stamens and a hairy corolla tube (the tube-shaped base of the petals). Each fruit is recorded with four to six smooth, oval seeds, flat on one side.

## Where does the record place it? {#s-2c28f001c3}
The same dataset labels the species terrestrial. That broad category does not identify a specific site or region.
