## Lonchocarpus atropurpureus {#s-fefcf4cab7}

The Flora of Panama describes a shrub or tree 3–15 m tall, with 5–9 leaflets per leaf and dark-purple to pinkish-purple flowers. Its flat legumes are 4–10 cm long, about 1 cm wide, and contain 1–4 seeds. The account lists southern Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras, Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela and Ecuador.
