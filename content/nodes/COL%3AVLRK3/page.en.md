## Leaflets, flowers and fruit {#s-3876ebf394}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records a xylopodium, a woody underground structure, and a ridged stem with gray, rough Malpighian-type hairs. Leaves are odd-pinnate, with opposite pairs of leaflets; these may be linear, elliptic or oblanceolate. Glands are present on the leaflets, while stipels are absent. Crowded racemes bear pink-petalled flowers, and the fruit is a straight legume. This is one regional row, not a complete or global description.
