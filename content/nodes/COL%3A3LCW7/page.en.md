## What range of leaf and flower traits is recorded for Heteropterys alternifolia? {#s-eea07b6719}
The leafy branches may become hairless. Leaves range from alternate to nearly opposite; petioles may be silky-haired and bear glands in their middle third. The papery blades may be elliptic, oval or obovate, with wedge-shaped bases and tips ranging from rounded or blunt to small-pointed. Hairiness, marginal glands and edge rolling vary: some blades are strongly rolled under. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts and stalked pedicels. The sepals may be wholly glandular or have only the front sepal without glands, with flat tips and rusty hairs. Petals are yellow, clawed and thick, with toothed, glandular margins; they may stand upright or reflex. The anthers are upright and hairless. The styles are equal, straight, diverge distally, and have rounded tips. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.
