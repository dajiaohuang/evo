## Mucilaginous stems and lilac fruit {#s-6b56f220ca}
The description records globose to cylindrical stems with a white pith and mucilage inside; the epidermis may be dark green, greyish or bluish. Nine or more spines curve toward the stem. The cephalium is taller than wide and bears conspicuous bristles, with no brown tuft. Flowers are pink, fruits lilac, and the seed coat convex or angular. Both rock-associated and terrestrial habitats are listed.
