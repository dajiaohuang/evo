## Chromolaena myriocephala {#s-a24a8d7f99}
## Three-veined leaves and glandular corolla lobes

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Stems range from glandular to finely hairy or nearly smooth. Opposite, lance-shaped to ovate leaves have scalloped or serrated margins and three main veins. The upper face is smooth; the lower is recorded as gland-dotted, nearly smooth or stiffly hairy.

The head clusters are described as corymb-like or panicle-like. Heads have stalks and cylindrical involucres; the smooth outer and inner bracts lie close against the head. Corolla lobes are papillose or glandular. The dry fruits (cypselae) are linear and become smooth; their pappus bristles are rough. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; no locality or complete range is given.
