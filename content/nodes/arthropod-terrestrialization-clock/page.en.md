## Three arthropod land colonisations in model comparisons {#s-ad9a314d5d}
Fossil-calibrated analyses compare 57 taxa across 246 genes, with a 30-taxon subset used to test alternative trees. Models of ancestral habitat favor marine rather than freshwater routes for separate land colonisations by hexapods, myriapods and arachnids. The routes and their timing are reconstructions conditioned on the tree and calibrations, not observed migrations.
