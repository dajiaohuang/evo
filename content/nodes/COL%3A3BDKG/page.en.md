## Erythrochiton trichanthus {#s-c476471229}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes a cylindrical or angular-based peduncle, a velvety calyx interior and four stamens.

## A velvety calyx interior and four stamens {#s-d6ffb9ec52}
The regional morphology field records an inflorescence peduncle that is cylindrical or angular at its base. The calyx is white or greenish and entire, with a velvety inner surface. The outer corolla surface is pubescent, and the flower has four stamens. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), but gives no locality or complete range.
