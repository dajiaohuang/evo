## Cloud forest and lower secondary-forest encounters {#s-feee7a907d}
At the Mt. Rossel type locality, frogs were described as abundant in dense ridge cloud forest with persistent fog drip. They occurred less commonly in tall lowland secondary forest at 280 m. These qualitative encounter descriptions retain the contrasting sites, without providing standardized density estimates or proving a causal effect of forest condition.

## Calling under wet and drier conditions {#s-467fd581e0}
At the type locality, calling extended from late afternoon through early morning, usually from stems or leaves 1–4 m above ground. Longer advertisement calls occurred under wet conditions; under drier conditions calls were slower and more irregular, often from hidden perches. The account documents calling variation rather than a complete breeding calendar or observed egg-deposition sites.
