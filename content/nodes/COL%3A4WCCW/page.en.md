## *Selaginella revoluta*: a specialized cell in side leaves {#s-d60ed257a6}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 character record, pale, hairless stems lie prostrate and lack joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise in the leaf axils or on the upper side of the stem. The small leaves differ by position: side leaves are ovate-oblong or lance-shaped, while upper leaves are nearly round to broadly elliptic, with rounded or blunt bases and long tapering or short bristle-like tips.

An idioblast, a specialized cell type, is recorded in side leaves but not upper leaves. Axillary leaves are ovate to triangular, with blunt bases and pointed tips. The habitat field says terrestrial but gives no locality or survey date.
