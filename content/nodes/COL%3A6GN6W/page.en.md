## Accepted name and placement {#s-f76227b721}
COL26.8 accepts Ericabatrachus baleensis Largen, 1991 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Ericabatrachidae (family) → Ericabatrachus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## What six gene regions changed about its placement {#bio-r16-1}
Siu-Ting and colleagues' abstract reports fresh E. baleensis samples and sequence data from two mitochondrial and four nuclear genes. It describes the de novo and constrained-tree analyses as strongly supporting a close relationship between Ericabatrachus and Petropedetes, while rejecting earlier proposed close placements with cacosternines or Phrynobatrachus. This is the abstract's summary of the analyses; it gives no per-analysis support values, so it does not show that every tree has strong bootstrap support. The abstract also calls the frog rare and Critically Endangered; the material read here does not provide a full natural-history account.
