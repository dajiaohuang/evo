## Ambolestes and the sampled therian boundary {#s-2202f60ec0}
A near-complete holotype, STM33-5, preserves the ectotympanic (a middle-ear bone), malleus and hyoid anatomy. A morphological matrix, which compares coded anatomical characters across taxa, placed Ambolestes and Sinodelphys within Eutheria and shifted the oldest sampled metatherians to younger North American fossils. The placental–marsupial split is not visible in this skeleton: the reassignment and resulting ghost lineage depend on the characters and taxa included in that analysis.
