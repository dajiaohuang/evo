## Wolffia columbiana: tiny fronds and flowers {#s-25b9e715ee}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes slightly submerged fronds without free-floating roots. It records 1–2 joined, symmetric, ellipsoid to nearly spherical fronds, light green, with large cells and no dark spots. The field reports no papillae and gives frond dimensions of 9–27 mm long and 0.7–2.3 mm wide. An adult frond has one basal, conical funnel-like opening that forms a young frond; the young frond may or may not have an epidermal stomatal cavity.

The inflorescence has 2 flowers, one male and one female, without a prophyll; the flowers sit in cavities along the median line on the upper side of the frond. The male flower has a unilocular anther with apical dehiscence; the field records 0.50 mm. The female flower is globular, with 1 ovule, a short style and a circular stigma. The spherical fruit is 0.5 mm long and 0.3 mm wide.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Aquática,” or aquatic.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
