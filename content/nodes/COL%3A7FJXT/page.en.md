## Vataireopsis iglesiasii {#s-f30ccae6d1}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Vataireopsis iglesiasii* as an accepted species of *Vataireopsis*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 account describes tawny, woolly inflorescences and a bell-shaped calyx. Its fruit is a samara (a winged fruit); the seed-bearing body has a lateral wing extending toward the stalk. This compact set of characters comes from one regional description and does not establish how much the species varies.
