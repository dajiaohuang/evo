## Senna spectabilis {#s-562712d22b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna spectabilis* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 6–10 pairs of leaflets, no extra-floral glands, and seven fertile stamens. Its hanging, cylindrical pods are dehiscent (splitting open at maturity) and have seeds arranged in one row; the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
