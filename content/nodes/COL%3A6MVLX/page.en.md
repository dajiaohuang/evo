## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-0efa8a7c31}
The historical Flora of Panama describes a medium-sized tree with hairless branchlets bearing many stem lenticels (small pores). Leaflet number is presented with alternatives: leaves usually have four leaflets, sometimes six and occasionally two; later, the same account gives two pairs as usual and asks whether three or one pair also occurs. The 1–2 cm leaf stalk (petiole) is hairless and cylindrical except for a lateral angle above. The 2–5 cm central axis (rachis) is hairless, wingless but ridged, and somewhat edged above; it bears a small, unstalked, disc-shaped or shallow cup-shaped gland where leaflet stalks join. Leaflets are mostly oblong or elliptic, 4–12 × 1.5–5.5 cm, usually pointed with a blunt taper at the tip and wedge-shaped, slightly uneven bases; both surfaces are hairless. Linear-oblong stipules (small leaf-base appendages), up to 5–6 mm long, are hairy and may persist briefly or fall. One or two long spikes can grow from each of several leafy or leafless axils. They are about 10 cm long, nearly hairless, with a short stalk of about 2 cm and many flowers scattered along the rest. Flowers are whitish and range from hairless to minutely hairy. The cup-tubular calyx (outer floral cup) is 1–2 mm long with tiny but distinct teeth. The corolla (petal whorl) is usually 4–5 mm long, narrow at the base and expanded at the tip. Stamens (pollen-producing organs) are 12–15 mm long and fused at the base into a tube that extends beyond the corolla. The source adds “Panama?” to its note of strong extension; it also records that the tube may exceed the corolla only slightly. The flattened, strongly compressed pod is 7–15 × 2–3.5 cm, nearly hairless, with thickened margins. This historical description gives no population sampling frame or observation date.

## Historical distribution entry {#s-a68121962a}
The same Flora of Panama source lists “Mexico to Panama; West Indies.” This historical regional statement is not a current or complete global range, and it does not specify whether the records are native, introduced, cultivated, or wild.
