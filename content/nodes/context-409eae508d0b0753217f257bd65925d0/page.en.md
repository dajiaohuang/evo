## The source concept {#s-b7a3195b46}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Alsodes igneus as row Anf1352, classified in Alsodes, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Developmental strategy {#s-fdd595b7e2}
The deposited record marks a larval stage. This category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
