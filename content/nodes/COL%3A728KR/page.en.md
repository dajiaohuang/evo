## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Licania membranacea? {#s-0976919936}
The oblong leaves have woolly or cobweb-like hairs beneath, and the leaf stalks lack glands. Flowers grow in panicles, with bell-shaped receptacles and individual stalks. Each flower has three to five separate stamens whose filaments remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is described as woolly or reddish.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-8a8a1fd695}
The source record categorizes it as terrestrial and gives no locality.
