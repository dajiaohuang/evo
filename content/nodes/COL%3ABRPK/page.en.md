Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — alternate arrangement, lance-shaped, entire, untoothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
