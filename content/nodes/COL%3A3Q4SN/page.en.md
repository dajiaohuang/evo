## A source-coded development mode and oviposition environment {#s-a1858fbbd2}
The Atlantic Forest compilation codes the source row as direct-developing and terrestrial for oviposition. Direct development means a froglet hatches without a free-living tadpole stage; it does not mean embryonic development or moisture is absent. These are literature-derived categories rather than a new observation or experiment for this species, and concept equivalence remains uncertified.
