## Darwinius: one skeleton on two slabs {#s-a6366eec46}
The plates PMO 214.214 and WDC-MG-210 are the counterpart halves of one juvenile Darwinius skeleton from Messel, dated in the cited account to about 47.5 million years ago. Together they preserve an unusually complete tail, hands, right foot, soft-tissue outline, and gut contents. The description also discloses preparation-made features on one plate; interpretations of sex, ecology, and evolutionary placement remain distinct from the preserved anatomy.
