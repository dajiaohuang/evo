## Talisia nervosa {#s-ac254c8086}
The Flora of Panama portrays *Talisia nervosa* as a small shrub or tree with unusually long leaves, broad terminal flower clusters, and brown ellipsoid-to-globose fruits. Its brief range field runs from Costa Rica to Colombia.

## Long leaves near the shoot tips {#s-b935a831ad}
The historical account labels the plants polygamous and describes shrubs or small trees, usually less than 5 m tall. The trunk is up to 6 cm in diameter at breast height and remains unbranched unless damaged. Pinnate leaves cluster near the shoot apex and are often 1 m or more long, without reduced leaflets at the tip. The petiole is about 25 cm long and cylindrical; swollen petiolules are 5–10 mm long. Leaves usually carry five to eight pairs of oblong-elliptic leaflets, 20–45 by 6.5–13 cm, with acute tips and bases. The upper leaflet surface is hairless; the lower surface ranges from hairless to minutely hairy.

## A broad panicle of small flowers {#s-313193343d}
Small thyrses form a widely branched panicle up to 70 cm long, with ribbed main branches. The branches, short pedicels, and calyces bear minute hairs ranging from fine pubescence to a short, dense covering. Pedicels reach 2 mm and are jointed below the calyx. Flowers are white. The bowl-shaped calyx is about 2 mm long, divided halfway into five firm, acute, fringed lobes. Five oblong petals, 3–5 mm long, acute to blunt at the tip and spreading above the calyx; they are hairless. The petal scales exceed the petals, are silky-haired, and form tufts at their slightly spreading tips. The raised floral disc has five angles alternating with the petals. The account lists five or eight stamens, with three often reduced or aborted; the stamens equal the petal scales and reach 5 mm. Filaments are weakly hairy and shorter than the oblong anthers, which are 1–4 mm long; the anther connective ends in a small beak. Staminate flowers have a densely hairy ovary under 2 mm long, no style, and three tiny stigmas hidden by the ovary hairs. In bisexual flowers, the narrowly egg-shaped, silky ovary has a style about as long as itself and a head-shaped stigma held near the anther level.

## Brown fruits and a regional range note {#s-e757618794}
Fruits range from ellipsoid to globose and are brown, sparsely hairy, finely dotted with small lenticels, and sharply pointed at the tip. They are usually 2–3.5 cm long and have a woody pericarp 2–3 mm thick. Ellipsoid seeds, 1.5–2.5 cm long, are embedded in whitish-to-orange mesocarp described as firm or jellylike; if a fruit has more than one seed, the source notes that a seed's side is flat. The Flora of Panama's distribution field says the range extends from Costa Rica to Colombia. This historical regional statement gives no native, introduced, or cultivated status and is not a current or global inventory.
