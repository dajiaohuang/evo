## What flower and fruit structures are recorded? {#s-4ec7281c49}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes alternate leaves arranged in two ranks, sometimes hairy and sometimes smooth. Flower clusters form panicles on woolly stalks. The ovary and style also have woolly hairs. The fruit is a hairy capsule that opens along its internal partitions; the outer fruit layer is not fused to the inner wall.
