## Cryptovaranoides: competing placements from the same fossils {#s-2608db9776}
The same fossils have supported two different positions for Cryptovaranoides: the 2022 description and 2024 response place holotype NHMUK PV R36822 within crown Squamata, while competing analyses place the taxon outside it. The disagreement turns on what some bones are, whether isolated elements belong to this animal, and how anatomical characters are scored. Until those points converge, this fossil is not a secure first appearance of crown squamates or a fixed clock calibration.
