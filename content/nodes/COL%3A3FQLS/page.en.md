## Geonoma leptospadix: leaf, inflorescence and flower traits {#s-13bb32e09f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists a solitary or cespitose stem. The leaf blade, or its basal segments, joins the rachis at an acute angle; the blade is entire or irregularly pinnate, and its veins do not stand out from the blade in cross-section.

The inflorescence is interfoliar or infrafoliar and branches to the first order. Peduncular bracts are narrow, elongate and tubular. The prophyll surface has long, unbranched veins. Rachillae are not filiform; floral pits are arranged spirally, have a distal lip, and lack indumentum on their inner surface.

Staminate flowers fall after anthesis, and their thecae diverge at anthesis. Unfertilized pistillate flowers also fall. Fruits have rounded tips and a smooth, glabrous epicarp; the field records their color as black or purple.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
