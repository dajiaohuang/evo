## The Leigongshan record {#s-10fb2ce5b4}
At publication, the description knew the species only from Leigongshan National Nature Reserve in Leishan, Guizhou, China, at 1600–1800 m. The recorded locality and elevation interval do not establish a complete modern distribution or physiological altitude limits.
