## Hairy leaves beneath a compact flower head {#s-c43a3d6f0a}
The record describes hairy stems and stalked, ovate-lanceolate leaves with blunt bases and pointed tips. The upper surfaces are bristly and the lower surfaces shaggy. Hairy flower clusters form at the stem tips or between leaf nodes. The flowers have a tubular calyx with thread-like lobes and a funnel-shaped corolla under 1 cm long. The obovoid fruit is smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
