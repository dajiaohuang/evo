## Evidence limits {#s-ee81787bdb}
This page combines historical AVONET v7 species means, the 2015 life-history compilation and two EltonTraits 1.0 field groups. Historical name and family matches support these record links, not equivalence with current COL26.8 species concepts. AVONET morphology describes listed samples rather than individual ranges or identification thresholds; mass has a separate source and sample basis. Life-history summaries retain compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, the largest record for maximum longevity, and historical species/subspecies inheritance. Their historical name match does not itself establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. They do not represent one measured individual, wild-lifespan averages or current population surveys; wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed. EltonTraits food and foraging values are semiquantitative translations of literature, not direct measures of intake, energy or time. Underlying works have not been individually reviewed, and reproduction, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
