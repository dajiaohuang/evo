## What leaf, flower and style traits are recorded for Heteropterys lindleyana? {#s-462567bd77}
The leafy branches may become hairless. Opposite petioles are hairless and have glands at the base. The papery elliptic blades have wedge-shaped bases, cuspidate tips, and impressed marginal glands; their margins are flat. The inflorescence hangs downward, with persistent oval bracts lacking glands and stalked pedicels. Sepals have flat tips, non-running glands, and hairs; the front sepal alone may lack glands. Yellow petals are clawed and thick, with a keel and ragged edges; they may reflex. Anthers are hairless. Styles are equal, bent at the base and diverging distally, with hairless hooked tips. The seed-bearing fruit body is smooth.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a289deea99}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
