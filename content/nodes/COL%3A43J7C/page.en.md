## Minasia splettiae {#s-bdbee772a5}
## Narrow leaves and small clustered heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes thin, linear leaves under 10 cm long and no more than 0.8 cm wide. Flower clusters may be one-sided or two-sided cymes (monochasial or dichasial), or glomerules. A cluster has one head or 2–10; individual heads may be sessile or stalked and contain fewer than 18 florets. The dry fruits are setulose across their surface. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
