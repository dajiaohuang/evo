## Syagrus oleracea: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-96aa9f93d9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes a smooth, solitary or aerial stem. Leaves are green and pinnate, with deciduous, fibrous-margined sheaths. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is arched. Pinnae are leathery and rigid, grouped and inserted at different angles; they are dark green and slightly discolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate or pendulous, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved or pruinose; its rachis is glabrous, and the rachillae are arranged helically along it. Fruits are fusiform or elliptic. The epicarp is yellow-green or yellow, with indumentum at the tip; the mesocarp is fibrous and succulent; the endocarp is woody and fusiform; and the endosperm is homogeneous.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
