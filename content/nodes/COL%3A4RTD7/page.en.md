## Restio curviramis {#s-257df5b0bf}
## A seasonally damp mat among sand and rock

Restio curviramis is described as a tangled, stolon-forming perennial, often a mat, reaching 40 cm. Its stems may be smooth or rough and bend along their length; the leaf sheaths include a thick, leathery section with a squared-off end. The account records sandy flats and rocky ledges, usually seasonally damp.
