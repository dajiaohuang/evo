## Vocalization research {#s-7778bef0a2}
A comparison of two Bolivian endemic titis found more frequent calling by groups in more continuous forest; P. olallae gave more territorial calls in the rainy season. Call rates varied with season and habitat, so call surveys cannot be read as population density without those conditions.

## Survey methods {#s-55b601c5fd}
The study notes that calls are used to locate groups, but call rates differ between species and seasons, affecting detection opportunities. This informs survey design; it does not update the species' current range or abundance.

## Evidence limits {#s-268732a768}
The call study covered particular groups and survey periods; more rainy-season calls do not imply an increase in groups or replace current population surveys. The Chinese name is editorial; this guide is.
