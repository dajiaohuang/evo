## How do its hairy leaves continue into the flower stalk? {#s-fc9c8beed3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records nearly two-ranked leaves with dark sheaths that widen at the base. Both the sheaths and flat blades are hairy. The 20–50 cm flower stalk is also hairy and has more than two ridges; the ovoid or obovoid spike bears up to ten flowers. Several tip and spike-shape states are listed as alternatives.
