## Feeding research {#s-ea62366425}
At Ranomafana National Park, Overdorff compared Eulemur rubriventer with a lemur then called E. fulvus rufus, using feeding observations from July 1988 to August 1989. Both were described as highly frugivorous, and foods varied seasonally; food changes were associated with dietary overlap between the two taxa, feeding-bout length and daily feeding time. The paper uses a historical subspecific name, so its observations should not be transferred without qualification to the full range of modern E. rufus.

## Ecological evidence {#s-4316e475ad}
Overdorff later compared activity and habitat use of two lemurs at Ranomafana, again using E. fulvus rufus for the red-brown form. The current MDD v2.5 entry accepts E. rufus and notes that E. rufifrons was formerly included under that name; historical group identity and present boundaries therefore should not be assumed to match exactly. This page retains the paper’s name and treats the observations as historical field evidence with explicit taxonomic context.
