## Small club-shaped leaves and broad-winged capsules {#s-506f2a9a8e}
The Latin description notes twigs often bearing about ten leaves, with internodes barely visible; after the leaves fall, smooth brown sheaths remain. The small club-shaped leaves are about 6.9 mm long, and the five petals are golden inside and orange outside. The capsule expands to about 6 mm across and has broad wings; its pale brown seeds are finely dotted. The record lists flats on Karoo shale and Lower Karoo Albany Thicket.
