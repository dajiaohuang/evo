## Accepted name and placement {#s-42ab7d8608}
COL26.8 accepts Chiropterotriton ceronorum Parra-Olea, García-Castillo, Rovito, Maisano, Hanken & Wake, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Caudata (order) → Plethodontidae (family) → Chiropterotriton (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A dated holotype and separate diagnostic sample sizes {#s-8782dd3950}
Male holotype USNM 224212 was collected near Santa Cruz Texmalaquilla on the southern Pico de Orizaba slope on 3 September 1975 at 3110 m. Twenty paratypes were listed, ten males and ten females. The diagnosis summarized ten adult males with mean snout–vent length 33.9 mm and range 30.6–36.2 mm, and ten adult females with mean 34.9 mm and range 33.3–38.4 mm. The male diagnostic count is not silently equated with the holotype plus ten male paratypes. These are the 2020 description’s material and summaries, not universal adult size limits.

## Sexed tooth counts and a defined limb interval {#s-70d0293bd7}
The diagnosis reported male maxillary teeth averaging 11.0, range 7–18, and females averaging 47.7, range 36–56. Mean tail/SVL ratios were 1.0 in males and 0.97 in females. Adpressed-limb interval was expressed in costal folds, averaging 0.0 in males and 1.5 in females; negative values denote overlap rather than a negative anatomical length. Digits were discrete with basal webbing and subterminal pads. These sampled anatomical differences do not demonstrate a mating function, a universal tooth count or measured climbing performance.

## Variable live bands from photographs and preserved types {#s-0fe79b2fd1}
Live-colour notes used one photographed Xometla specimen and nine near Texmalaquilla. The Xometla animal lacked a dorsal stripe; Texmalaquilla animals usually bore bands varying from reddish or orange-brown to salmon and clay colours. The preserved holotype was uniformly dark tannish-brown dorsally and pale cream below, while paratypes could be striped or unstriped. The photographs are not assumed to show the holotype alive, and neither colour state is promoted to an invariant identification test or demonstrated camouflage.

## Bromeliads and terrestrial cover on southern Orizaba {#s-ad112ee8e5}
The 2020 account reported southern Pico de Orizaba localities in Puebla and Veracruz at 2600 to approximately 3100 m, with specimens in arboreal bromeliads and under terrestrial cover objects. The holotype’s 3110-m entry remains distinct from that approximate summary. These encounters document more than one substrate; they do not establish exclusive arboreality, a full contemporary range, a measured elevation tolerance or the species’ egg site and diet.
