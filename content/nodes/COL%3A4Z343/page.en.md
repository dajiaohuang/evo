## *Spigelia asperifolia* {#s-eea3b1590b}
## Rough leaves and a spotted corolla

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes hairy, four-angled stems with internodes longer than the leaf blades. The nearly stalkless leaves are oblong or elliptic, taper toward the base, have acute tips, rough hairs, and palmate veins. A long flower stalk bears more than 4 flowers. The linear sepals are hairy; the white, tube-shaped corolla may be spotted pink or purple and has a rough hair covering. The ovary is rough, the stamens remain enclosed within the corolla, the fruit is rough, and the seeds are nearly spherical. The field lists alternatives without stating their frequency or variation.
