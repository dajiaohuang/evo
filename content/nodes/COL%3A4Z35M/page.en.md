## Rough stems and pale flowers {#s-7bd5bada5b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records rough stems that are square or winged, with internodes about as long as the leaf blades. Leaves are nearly sessile, ovate or elliptic, rough, and palmately veined; their bases taper and their tips are acute. Developed peduncles carry more than four flowers. The funnel-shaped corolla is white and hairy, while the ovary is pubescent and the stamens remain within the corolla. The fruit is hairless, and the seeds are subglobose.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-60d277d326}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Spigelia insignis* as terrestrial but supplies no collection locality.
