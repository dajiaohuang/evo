## What the *Yicaris* material contributes {#s-d350e17140}
The atlas traces crustacean history from Lower Cambrian *Yicaris* material to living crustaceans. Phosphatized growth stages preserve limb anatomy, while a character analysis places *Yicaris* within Eucrustacea. That placement depends on the analysis, and the rounded 520 Ma start is a fossil-sample anchor rather than an origin date.

Growth stages from the Yu’anshan Formation preserve limbs that were included in a morphological analysis. The study places *Yicaris* among sampled eucrustaceans, but this is an interpretation of coded characters, not a direct observation of its position on an evolutionary tree.

## Reading the displayed range {#s-1c0309cbe5}
The 520–0 Ma envelope runs from the Lower Cambrian sample to living crustaceans. Its older endpoint rounds the fossil’s biostratigraphic context. It does not identify the exact origin of crustaceans or a crown-group first appearance.
