## Eunotosaurus ribs test a model of shell assembly {#s-e86138893b}
Two specimens preserve different parts of the proposed sequence. A section through dorsal rib NHM PV R 4949 shows successive bone deposition extending from the primordial rib; SAM K 1133 preserves an articulated shoulder girdle with the scapula in front of the dorsal ribs. These observations inform models of how the turtle shell developed. Placing Eunotosaurus on the turtle stem and treating its ribs as homologous with shell components are analytical interpretations, not evidence that these individuals were direct ancestors.
