Table 1 compares this with an earlier reported elevation of 594–900 (Campbell 2000). and lists photographic catalogue number IBH-RF 618. The locality is in the Chinantla region of Oaxaca, Mexico. ## Interpret the record within the survey design {#s-38292d8247}
Four observers surveyed pristine and managed cloud-forest sites on eleven five-day trips in 2018–2019, in wet and dry seasons. Three daily windows were 09:00–13:00, 15:00–19:00 and 21:00–01:00; the paper reports 2,640 effective field hours for the whole herpetofaunal inventory. Animals were photographed and released, and GPS locations had a reported 5 m error range. The checklist's Quilticohyla acrochorda row establishes presence in that sampling context. Total effort and the nineteen amphibian species are not species-specific detection probabilities, population estimates or trends. The accumulation curve had not reached an asymptote, so a missing record cannot establish local absence.
