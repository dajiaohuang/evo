## Heart-shaped leaves and no stem hairs {#s-2ffda28023}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes sympodial growth and no stem hairs. Leaves are cordate (heart-shaped), hairless below, with the upper midrib raised along its length. Bracts or cataphylls beneath leaf nodes are absent. Small flowering branches are rounded, and the flower bracteoles fall away. The source lists these as regional field states without frequencies.

## Near-terminal clusters and unknown ovary chambers {#s-1532cde7fe}
The field records one inflorescence per axillary shoot, placed near the shoot tip or mixed with terminal clusters. The arrangement is pyramidal and alternate. The anther pollen sacs and ovary chamber count are unknown. The floral disc is hairless; the flower cup is inverted-cone-shaped, with hair status unknown. Sepals are separate, and the calyx opens without tearing. In fruit, the calyx lobes enlarge; the fruit is globose.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-d2489fd8e3}
The habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date, or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material.
