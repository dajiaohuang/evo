## Barcode-supported identification retains provisional boundaries {#s-501d7f4fb6}

Other variabilis-labelled sequences fall in another clade, including material reportedly checked against variabilis types. The authors therefore apply perreti to USNM558516. This is their revised identification, not an independent modern re-examination of the original type specimens here.

## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-9258a50c37}
For Arthroleptis perreti, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June2012 and March–April2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
