## Long white or yellow flowers above smooth leaves {#s-d8b745ad3e}
The morphology record describes stems that are smooth or nearly so, and elliptic to obovate leaves whose tips range from pointed to rounded. The upper leaf surface may be smooth or bristly, while the lower is hairy. Bisexual white or yellow flowers have a ribbed tubular calyx over 1 cm long and a salver-shaped corolla over 2 cm. The brown, ellipsoid fruit is smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
