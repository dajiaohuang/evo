## What leaf, flower and fruit traits distinguish Hiraea silvicola? {#s-3c086a0561}
The leaves are elliptic or obovate, with wedge-shaped bases and small-pointed tips. Petioles bear glands; leaf margins may be glandular or glandless, and the blades range from becoming hairless to velvety. Axillary corymbs carry flowers with eight glands and ragged-edged petals. The fruit has rounded wings.
