## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-5e1558c80a}
The stems are simple. Leaves have petioles and are alternate or arranged in two ranks; their blades are elliptic, obovate or oblong. The small unisexual flower clusters carry male and female flowers. Male flowers have six cream sepals, a lobed nectar disc and three united stamens. Female flowers also have six cream sepals, with an entire nectar disc. The fruit is a spherical capsule; the seeds are warty.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-fb428392f7}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
