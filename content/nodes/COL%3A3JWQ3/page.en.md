## Haworthia blackburniae {#s-3653a4b151}
Haworthia blackburniae is an acaulescent succulent that forms clumps, with long grey-green leaves that are channelled and may be smooth or lightly toothed. The regional account highlights its fusiform roots and leathery, linear leaves. It is recorded on steep, rocky, south-facing slopes in transitional karroid vegetation.
