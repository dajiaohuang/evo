## Leaflets and hanging flower clusters {#s-dbdaa8ef83}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records non-leaflike stipules positioned between the petiole and stem. Each leaf has 3–4 pairs of sickle-shaped leaflets. The inflorescences are long and pendulous. Sepals are equal in size and lack nectar glands; the floral cup is regular in form. The stamens form a short, regular tube, with fewer than ten fertile stamens. The stigma is blunt to head-shaped. These traits come from one regional row, not a complete or global description.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-005a4f013d}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, with no locality, range or sampling coverage.
