## Senegalia mattogrossensis {#s-1a4ce20c34}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senegalia mattogrossensis* as an accepted species of *Senegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists 9–15 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf) and 40–80 pairs of leaflets. The leaflets are linear and hairless; the flower heads are rounded, and the flowers have hairless calyces and corollas. The source records pods but did not observe seed shape; it also labels the plant terrestrial.
