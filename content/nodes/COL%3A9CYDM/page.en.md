## Three leaflets and wine-red flowers {#s-1c0da9c90f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row records three leaflets with a thin, membranous texture; their shapes range from lance-shaped to elliptic, and their surfaces from nearly smooth to hairy. The flowers are described as vinaceous, a deep wine-red color, and have a spur. The fruit is straight or sickle-shaped, with valves that are nearly smooth. The entry records alternatives rather than one fixed form.
