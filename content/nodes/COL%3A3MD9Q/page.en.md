## *Homalolepis maiana* {#s-d02ab5834c}
The field records a developed, unbranched above-ground stem and a 22.5–36 cm leaf rachis. Leaflets are opposite or nearly opposite, oblong-elliptic to oblong-obovate, subleathery to leathery, with no conspicuous apical gland and margins not rolled under; the lower surface is sparsely minutely hairy. The inflorescence is thyrsoid. Sepals are hairy; petals narrowly oblong and woolly; the staminal appendages end bluntly or squarely. The gynophore and ovary are velvety-haired; the fruit surface is smooth. These are source-field characters.
