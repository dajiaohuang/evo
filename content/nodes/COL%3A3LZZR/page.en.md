## Hippocratea volubilis {#s-18367fd655}
## A long climber with broad leaves and winged seeds

Hippocratea volubilis is a woody climber that may reach 25 m. Young stems and inflorescences are densely covered in minute brown hairs. Its leaves are 4–17 cm long and 2–8 cm wide, with wavy, shallowly toothed or nearly entire margins. The flowers are only 4–8 mm across. The fruit splits into large segments, each containing a seed with a broad, obovate-oblong wing. The cited range extends from southern Florida and central Mexico to northern Argentina.
