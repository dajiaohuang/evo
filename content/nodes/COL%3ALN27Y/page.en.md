## A reference assembly in a comparative genome study {#s-d269a957a0}
The study, published online in December 2025 and in the 2026 volume, lists the Rana clamitans assembly GCA_040206685.1: 6.36 Gbp (billion base pairs) and contig N50 4,403.2 Kbp (kilobase pairs). Contig N50 is the contig length at which contigs this long or longer make up at least half of the assembly. These are assembly statistics, not a direct measure of DNA per cell, a fixed species-wide genome size or a ploidy test. The study selected NCBI reference assemblies on 29 October 2024; these values and the assembly name are reported as published.

## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-f6254a84f5}
The authors used RepeatModeler 2 to build species-specific repeat libraries, RFSB to classify unknown sequences, and vertebrate repeat references before applying RepeatMasker. These are computational annotations: although the article calls matches insertions, a match alone does not establish a complete, expressed or currently mobile transposable element (TE). Superfamily labels can combine distinct families. Element count, total occupied length and genome proportion are different measures, and overlapping hierarchy categories cannot be added as independent fractions. The cited analysis reports no repeat-expression measurement for Aquarana clamitans.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-7488392c76}
The study compared 61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, a previously pruned tree, contig N50 as a covariate and multiple-comparison correction. Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size in that sample, but recent activity is inferred from sequence divergence and is not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Similarity filters for length and an open reading frame (ORF, a DNA sequence that could encode a protein) do not demonstrate protein expression. The authors' developmental-constraint proposal is a comparative hypothesis; it does not show that transposable elements caused this species' larval duration or clutch size.

## Conflicting source identifiers prevent a repeat-data assignment {#s-f8f9404e98}
Main Table 1 links Rana clamitans to GCA_040206685.1, while Supplementary Table S1 row 58 gives GCA_037952925.1 for the same name. Main Table 1 assigns that second accession to Bufotes viridis.
