## Stems, leaflets, and fruit {#s-b62452bcd6}
The source describes clustered stems up to 6 m tall. Their spines are yellow-brown and black at the base and tip, and may reach 6 cm. Pinnate leaves bear 30–57 pairs of linear to lanceolate leaflets; the leaflets may be arranged regularly or irregularly in groups of two to eight and at differing angles. The branched inflorescence grows between the leaves and has a spiny stalk bract. Fruit is flattened or globose, black or purple, with a hairless outer layer.

The field records clustered stems up to 6 m tall, with yellow-brown spines that are black at the base and tip and reach up to 6 cm; pinnate leaves with 30–57 pairs of linear or lanceolate leaflets, arranged regularly or irregularly in groups of two to eight at differing angles; a branched interfoliar inflorescence with a spiny stalk bract; and flattened or globose fruit, black or purple, with a hairless outer layer.
