## Source classification and concept status {#s-56cec130a7}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2996 records *Phyllomedusa hypochondrialis* under the source classification Phyllomedusa (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-88d38ba747}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-1c5b1fd587}
The row is marked for both daytime and night-time activity. These broad categories are not a continuous behavioural record; unlisted periods remain unspecified.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm; dry and warm) {#s-e0e4990af0}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm, and dry and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-31a2e79a7d}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 4.8 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 46 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-b84b8aa8f1}
The row lists 25–300 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 2–2.4 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size fields may refer to eggs.

## Developmental record (a larval stage) {#s-9bb6883a3d}
AmphiBIO v1 records a larval stage for this row. This classification does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
