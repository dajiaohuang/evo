## Early amphicyonids branch outside Canoidea {#s-f3f8f369c2}
Expanded morphology sampling recovers Amphicyonidae as a basal caniform branch outside Canoidea, while exact sister relationships remain unresolved. The 42–1.8 Ma display is the source-bounded family sample, not continuous global occupancy or direct dog–bear ancestry; the family is not a blend of dog and bear ancestors.
