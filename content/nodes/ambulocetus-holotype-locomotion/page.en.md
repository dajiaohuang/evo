## Ambulocetus: limbs between water and land {#s-0dd2f8322c}
The partial holotype H-GSP 18507 from Pakistan’s Kuldana Formation preserves a skull, vertebrae, and substantial fore- and hind-limb material. Their combination supports models of amphibious movement: otter-like up-and-down swimming and sea-lion-like movement on land. These are functional interpretations from bones and living comparisons, not observed behavior; this individual is not a direct whale ancestor.
