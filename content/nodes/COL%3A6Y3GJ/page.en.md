## What stem, leaf and flower traits distinguish Sauvagesia ramosissima? {#s-077603c194}
The woody stem does not branch from the base. Its spaced, alternate leaves are 10–20 mm long, elliptic or lance-shaped, with entire margins and no glands; secondary veins are conspicuous. The terminal, nearly cylindrical panicle has flowers crowded at nodes or on short side racemes. Flowers have upright pedicels and a star-shaped corolla with open, non-overlapping white petals. Outer staminodes are threadlike; inner petaloid staminodes are separate and dark pink. The single anther has a small point.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ade59406a6}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
