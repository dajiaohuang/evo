## Two Llanganates locality records {#s-ea74496a1f}
The description reported two Llanganates National Park localities in Ecuador’s Tungurahua and Pastaza provinces, at 2095–2280 m, separated by 32 km in a straight line. Both were assigned to eastern montane forest. Continuous forest mapped at those sites does not establish continuous species occupancy, and the authors lacked population data and extensive regional amphibian inventories.

## Night vegetation records after logging {#s-44d989cd8f}
The holotype and same-locality paratypes were collected at night on vegetation in recently logged forest. Another paratype was found at night on a roughly 1 cm diameter branch 2 m above ground. These encounters retain the collection context, without establishing tolerance of logging, successful reproduction in disturbed forest or a universal perch-height preference.
