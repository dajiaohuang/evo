## Measurement material {#s-d6f68b65ad}

These counts describe table rows; they are not population estimates or counts of unique specimens. Specimen identifiers, sex codes and references remain attached to their rows. Comparisons of body dimensions need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.
