## One surveyed stream and adjacent grassland {#s-3d5dc4a62a}
During surveys from 2017 to 2021, the authors found the species at one site in Wuyishan National Park, Fujian, China. Study specimens came from a stream and nearby grassland beneath evergreen broadleaf forest. The single recorded site does not prove absence elsewhere or establish exclusive use of one substrate across all life stages.

## Seasonal larval observations and inferred onset {#s-4b678d6a9f}
Six adults and some small early-stage tadpoles were found in late August and early September; larger, middle-stage tadpoles were collected in early November. The authors inferred that breeding might begin in July or early August. That onset remains an inference from staged samples rather than observed spawning dates or a measured development duration.
