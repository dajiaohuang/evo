## Chromolaena tyleri {#s-c1b58d7a64}
## Ovate serrated leaves and clustered heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Stems are finely hairy or stiffly hairy. Opposite, ovate leaves have serrated margins and three main veins; the upper surface is stiffly hairy, and the lower is both gland-dotted and stiffly hairy.

The inflorescence is recorded as a glomerule-like cluster or a paired-branch cyme. Its heads are nearly stalkless, with a cylindrical involucre. Outer bracts lie close to the head and are smooth; inner bracts are smooth and either upright or pressed close. Corolla lobes are papillose or smooth. The dry fruits (cypselae) are oblong, smooth or short-bristled, with rough pappus bristles. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; no locality or complete range is given.
