## Eryngium aristulatum {#s-d7a860895e}
## Parallel-veined leaves and a dehiscent fruit

The morphology field allows leaves to be sessile or borne on petioles and records parallel venation. The inflorescence is described as decumbent, and the flowers are sessile. The fruit is also sessile and dehiscent, meaning it opens at maturity; the back of each mericarp, one half of the split fruit, is vesiculose or bladder-like.
