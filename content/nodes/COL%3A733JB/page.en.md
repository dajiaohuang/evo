## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a58afc4acd}
COL26.8 records Madascincus polleni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-945009b5ee}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 102 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-f552a95854}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as concealed (Cryptic)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Cryptic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-8d6eb63e7c}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-c868d4db5d}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
