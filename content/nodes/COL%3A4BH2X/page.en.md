## Oxypetalum karstenianum {#s-11ac13c849}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum karstenianum* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 provides only a terrestrial habitat label. The regional field does not specify where it was recorded or describe its form, so no broader claim follows from it.
