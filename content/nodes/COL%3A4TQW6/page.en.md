## Magenta flowers and a five-chambered fruit {#s-7225fa6d52}
This shrub reaches about 200 mm and has smooth, three-sided leaves. Its magenta flowers are about 17 mm across; filament-like staminodes are absent, while fertile stamens are magenta at the tips and white below. The five-chambered fruit has an elongated funnel-shaped base and short keels. Granite- or gneiss-derived soils are recorded.
