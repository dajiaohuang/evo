## Apium graveolens {#s-c1b37f5ba0}
## Membranous leaflets and green flowers

The Brazilian Flora entry describes branches that run along the ground or rise upward. Leaflets range from rhomboid and oval to lance-shaped, with a membranous texture. Flowers are recorded as green. The fruit has a bifid carpophore, the forked structure that supports its two fruit halves; the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
