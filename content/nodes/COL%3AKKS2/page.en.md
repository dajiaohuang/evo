## What leaf and flower traits identify Banisteriopsis harleyi? {#s-bf150ec43a}
Opposite elliptic or rounded leaves bear shield-shaped glands along their side veins. The upper surface is becoming hairless or remains softly hairy; the lower is densely silky-haired. Axillary flower clusters have four flowers; their bracts persist and their stalkless pedicels are silky. The calyx bears a green oil gland, the petals are white, and anther chambers have sparse hairs. The styles are equal, and the fruit lacks lateral projections.
