## What leaf, flower-cluster and fruit traits are recorded for Sacoglottis guianensis? {#s-84a806c739}
The leaf edges range from slightly toothed or scalloped to nearly smooth. On the upper surface, veins are sunken or only slightly raised. The inflorescence is stalked, and its bracts persist. The fruit is an oblong drupe, a fleshy fruit with a hard inner layer.
