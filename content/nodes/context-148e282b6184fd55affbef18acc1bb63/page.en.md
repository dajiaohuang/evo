## The source concept {#s-94bda8c70f}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Bombina bombina in row Anf0260, with genus Bombina, family Bombinatorina and order Anura. The source says its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a link to a current COL26.8 concept has not been established. The traits below describe this archived row, not a new species assessment.

## Foraging strata {#s-8f63be4984}
The row marks Fos=1, Ter=1 and Aqu=1 in its foraging fields. ## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-f43fb8e74c}
AmphiBIO reports Body_mass_g=5.9 g and Body_size_mm=50 mm as maximum adult values. For Anura, Body_size_mm denotes snout–vent length. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited body-mass entries; this row has not been compared field by field, so its mass remains an archived value for review. These compiled maxima are not measurements of every adult.

The row also records Reproductive_output_y=1, a compiled maximum of one reproductive event per year, and offspring-size limits from Offspring_size_min_mm=1.2 mm to Offspring_size_max_mm=1.8 mm. The offspring-size fields may refer to offspring or eggs.
