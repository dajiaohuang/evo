## What stands out in its narrow, hairy leaves? {#s-501c76d602}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records leaves arranged in two ranks, with dark-brown, hairy sheaths and narrow, thread-like blades that are ridged and hairy. Its flower stalk is 5–20 cm long and thread-like, with one or two ridges. The ellipsoid spike bears up to ten flowers; the entry lists more than one possible bract (a leaf-like structure around a flower)-edge condition.
