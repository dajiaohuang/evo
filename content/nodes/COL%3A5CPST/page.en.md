## Xyris schizachne {#s-1f63366b1a}
## A wrinkled blade and hairy stalk ridges

The source records flat leaf blades with a crosswise wrinkled surface and a 20–70 cm flowering stalk. One or two ridges run along the stalk, with hairs on those ridges; the egg-shaped to nearly spherical spike carries four sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers. Its habitat label is terrestrial.
