AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3051 records Megaelosia jordanensis under the source classification Megaelosia (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.
