## Leaves and flowers {#s-c69a89efa8}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 25 cm, with two leaves that are dry at flowering, spreading, narrowly strap-shaped, and shallowly channelled. Star-shaped flowers form a widely spreading cluster. Their crisped tepals are white to pale pink with red lines near the throat. The stamens are less than two-thirds as long as the tepals.

Bulbous geophyte to 25 cm. Leaves 2, dry at flowering, spreading, narrowly strap-shaped, shallowly channelled. Flowers star-shaped, in a widely spreading cluster, tepals crisped, white to pale pink, lined red near throat, stamens less than two thirds as long as tepals.
