## Recorded parent of Trionychoidea {#s-f35c76d5c2}
Trionychoidea is listed at rank superfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Cryptodira as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
