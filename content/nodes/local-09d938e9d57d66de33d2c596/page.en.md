## The source tadpole concept {#s-8492f84ce2}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Ingerophrynus celebensis, in the genus Ingerophrynus and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying work prints Bufo celebensis, which the compilation retains as its own source name. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-950588a8a3}
The compilation has eight records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 33, 34, 35, 37, 38, 41, 42 and 43. These are labels attached to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; eight records do not necessarily represent eight unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
