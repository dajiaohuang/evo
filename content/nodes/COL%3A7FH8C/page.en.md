## Varronia curassavica {#s-16427e1efa}
## Small flower spikes grow at stem tips or leaf axils

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records simple hairs; hairs on the stem may become sparse, stiff, or very short. The petiolate leaves range from elliptic and oval to lance-shaped. Their bases are acute or wedge-shaped and the tips acute. The upper surface is stiff-haired or rough; the underside ranges from short-stiff-haired to densely woolly.

Short-stiff hairs also cover the spike-like flower clusters, which grow at the stem tip or in leaf axils. The calyx is bell-shaped with acute lobes, and the tubular corolla is under 1 cm long. The fruit is ovoid and hairless. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
