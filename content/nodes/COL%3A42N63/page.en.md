## Leaf hairs and an irregular calyx {#s-08694b7a49}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field describes tree-like hairs on young stems. Leaves are elliptic or oblong, with acuminate tips, wedge-shaped or blunt bases, and margins described as scalloped, ciliate or finely toothed. The field lists three or five main veins above the base, with two faint intramarginal veins; it also repeats a five-plus-two arrangement, so the entry does not resolve that count. The lower surface has sparse to moderate hairs, described as tree-like or stalked star-shaped (stipitate means carried on a small stalk). Flowers form compact clusters. The bell-shaped hypanthium has a persistent calyx with irregular lobes; the calyx is also described as closed in bud. Each flower has five petals. The anther connective is not prolonged and bears one dorsal tooth; yellow anthers have one small pore, and the ovary is hairy. These source descriptors do not establish how alternatives are distributed among plants or populations.
