## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Vantanea morii? {#s-ba560d481d}
The elliptic leaves are 5–7.5 cm long and 3.2–4.7 cm wide, on stalks 2–5 mm long. The white corolla—the joined petals—is 5 mm long and hairless; the ovary is also hairless. The pericarp, or fruit wall, has no tubercles.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-40d0f861fd}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
