## What does the record say about Duguetia glabriuscula? {#s-7b79276c65}
The field describes elliptic to egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and sparsely scaled below. Few flowers form each cluster; their color may be cream, red, or green. The fruit ranges from green to red and from elliptic to nearly spherical. It has a basal collar and a smooth surface.
