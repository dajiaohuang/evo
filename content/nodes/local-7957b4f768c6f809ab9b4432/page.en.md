## The source tadpole concept {#s-1b10592747}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Leptopelis viridis, in the genus Leptopelis and family Arthroleptidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Leptopelis viridis. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-c83b982d23}
The compilation has two records transcribed from literature and one measured from an image. Supplied stage labels are 30, 40 and 39. These labels belong to source records, not one tadpole’s timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. Three records are not necessarily three unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
