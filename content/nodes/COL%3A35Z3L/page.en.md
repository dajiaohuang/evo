## Didymopanax angustissimus {#s-9f6902130c}
## Narrow leaflets in a terminal whorl

The source describes compound leaves with seven to twelve narrow elliptic or oblong leaflets radiating from a single terminal whorl. Their margins are entire and their tips acute or acuminate; the lower surfaces range from nearly hairless to silky. The inflorescence is pseudolateral, with two to seven primary branches and partial umbels. Petals are yellowish or greenish, and the ovary is recorded as having two to six chambers. When dry, the fruit is laterally flattened or divided into three to six lobes.
