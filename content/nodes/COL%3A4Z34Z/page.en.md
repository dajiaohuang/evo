## Yellow flowers with exserted stamens {#s-4752279241}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records hairless stems whose internodes may be shorter than or equal to the leaf blades. Stems are cylindrical or quadrangular; leaves are lanceolate or elliptic, with attenuate bases and acute or blunt tips. The inflorescence is sessile and bears two to four flowers. Its yellow corolla is recorded as tubular or funnel-shaped; the stamens project beyond it. Sepals and ovary are hairless, and the seeds are subglobose. The field keeps several stem, leaf, and corolla states as alternatives.

## Rock-associated habitat category {#s-3c61086ac9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels *Spigelia flava* rupicolous, a rock-associated category. It gives no collection locality.
