## How small are its female spikelets? {#s-d449bce59a}
A regional account describes *Restio tenuispicatus* as a tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 60 cm, with branched culms (stems). Its leaf sheaths are tightly rolled and carry a straight awn, a bristle-like tip. Female spikelets (small flower clusters) reach 5 mm long.
