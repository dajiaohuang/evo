## The deposited caecilian record {#s-e802f09d8a}
This page summarizes the CaecilianTraits version 4 record for Microcaecilia iyob, filed under Microcaecilia in Siphonopidae (species row 193). That is the source’s identification; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been checked. The compilation keeps individual, population and species entries distinct.

## Measurement material {#s-48ceab6b98}
There is one measurement row, classified as individual-level; the source lists no population-level or species-level measurement rows. The cited 2010 description says the species was known from a single specimen collected more than 70 years earlier and had not been targeted by dedicated surveys; it treated the species as data deficient for conservation purposes. One row is therefore a very limited record, not a population estimate.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-62422abbf0}
The species-level row gives 97 primary folds. It is a value in the deposited compilation, not evidence that every individual has a fixed count.
