## Disa biflora {#s-51120afb9d}
The South African description gives Disa biflora a flexible stem 80–300 mm tall and one to six flowers, usually white and rarely rose-red. The pale-green lip and recurved middle sepal form a hood over the petals; the spur is bifid and 5–10 mm long. The account records it on alluvial or flat sands derived from Table Mountain Sandstone.
