## Tomentose stems and yellow ellipsoid fruit {#s-b348288603}
This record describes woolly stems and elliptic to obovate leaves with blunt bases and pointed tips. Both leaf surfaces have stiff hairs. The bisexual white flowers have smooth, bell-shaped calyces and tubular, bell-shaped corollas under 1 cm long. The ovary is smooth, and the fruit is yellow, ellipsoid and glabrous. The habitat field says terrestrial.
