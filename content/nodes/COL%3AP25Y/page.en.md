## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima pachyphylla? {#s-457d7dcddb}
The lower leaf surface is woolly-haired. Flower clusters carry one or two flowers on stalkless pedicels. Petals are yellow or orange, the anthers are hairless, and the fruit is hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f02daed70e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
