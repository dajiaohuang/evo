## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a66c6eb5f1}
COL26.8 records Heloderma exasperatum as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Helodermatidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-5b31d4823c}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 11.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
