## Domatia and leaflet alternatives in the entry {#s-085aaad141}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records foliaceous stipules. Domatia are listed as present or absent; the location field gives the petiole and the form is triangular. Leaves have up to 10 pairs of leaflets (juga), listed as oblong, elliptic or ovate, with coriaceous texture and a lower surface that is glabrous or sericeous. Flowers are over 10 mm long and bilaterally symmetric, with deciduous bracteoles. The hypanthium is asymmetric and cylindrical. Petals are over 1 mm wide and glabrous or sparsely indumented; stamens are dimorphic, with 10 counted. The fruit is elliptic to oblong. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields retain alternatives and do not establish variation or a complete range.
