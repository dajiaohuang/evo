## A skull-focused research path {#s-ff8e0623b1}
The species page follows Chatterji, Hutchinson and Jones (2021), who described three adult N. depressus skulls and used X-ray micro-CT to examine internal braincase structures. Their Bayesian analysis of morphological characters supported a sister-group relationship with Chelonia mydas. Read this as the authors' hypothesis from sampled morphology; COL's accepted names remain the catalogue authority for this directory.
