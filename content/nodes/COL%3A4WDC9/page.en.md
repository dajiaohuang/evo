## Selenicereus anthonyanus {#s-5577463d79}
## Flattened toothed stems bear crimson-pink flowers

The recorded stem segments are flattened, with toothed edges and tips that are not swollen. Flowers measure 10–13 cm; the outer perianth is crimson-pink. The fruit is spiny. The habitat field labels the plant epiphytic, meaning it grows on another plant's surface, but gives no host or locality.
