## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-792cfa9ec7}
COL26.8 records Chelodina ipudinapi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-e88d4b370c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to McCord & Thomson 2002.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-91438d4144}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
