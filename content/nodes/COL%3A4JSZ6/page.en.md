## Platymiscium floribundum {#s-0126721ff0}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Platymiscium floribundum* as an accepted species of *Platymiscium* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records distal leaflets in size groups of 2–6.5 cm or 4–11 cm, with some reaching 25 cm. Its hanging racemes (flower clusters arranged along a stalk) or panicles (branched flower clusters) may carry 10–35 flowers, occasionally up to 60; the flowers are at least 10 mm long, and the fruit is a samara listed as oblong, elliptic, or oval.
