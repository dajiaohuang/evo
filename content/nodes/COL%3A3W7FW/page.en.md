## Distribution records {#s-68268ee069}
A 2001–2002 survey in Sri Lanka covered 31 sites across five ecological zones. About 766 km of survey effort crossed 17 areas with no loris sightings; another 192 km was walked or motored at 14 sites where 185 sightings were recorded. These route observations describe the survey record, not the number of lorises across the island.

## Ecological evidence {#s-c5199d604b}
Under the paper’s taxonomic usage, 111 sightings were assigned to L. l. nordicus and four to L. l. grandis. The authors reported a sightings-based density range of 0.33–50 animals per kilometre for nordicus. Both counts and estimates belong to that survey and its historical labels; they are not current species totals or densities across the full range.
