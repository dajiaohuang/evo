## White flowers on smooth, cylindrical segments {#s-0120394b5a}
The mature areoles are spineless, and the cylindrical stem segments continue growing only at their tips, which are smooth. Flowers arise at the terminal ends; their areoles are glabrous, the corolla is bell-shaped and the filaments are white. The emergent pericarpel is shorter than the white perianth segments. The ovoid pink to magenta fruit has no areoles. Epiphytic habitat is recorded.
