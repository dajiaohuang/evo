## Long-haired stems and a simple corona in small flowers {#s-c279b5ab47}
The regional description gives stems 20–35 cm tall with long hairs. Leaves on the lower, non-flowering stem are crowded or scattered and irregularly inserted; at flowering nodes they are whorled, with internodes up to 20 mm, and are not reduced in size. Blades are 25–47 × 2–9 mm, linear to triangular with a truncate base, subsessile and long-haired. Flowers form fascicles of 3–9 at distal nodes; pedicels reach 12 mm. Calyx segments are 4–4.5 × 1–1.5 mm, densely hairy dorsally and glabrous or hairy at the apex ventrally. Green corolla lobes are 4–5 × 2–3 mm, convex and reflexed at anthesis; their dorsal surface has very sparse to dense spreading hairs at the apex. The green corona, white-tipped, is 1–1.5 mm high; its simple lobes are somewhat compressed-ovoid, with two shallow ventral concavities, one on either side of the connection with the staminal column. Anther appendages are about 0.8 mm and nearly flat. The corpusculum is 0.33–0.43 mm long, with slight to well-developed flanges; the pollinium is 0.63–0.75 mm. The gynostegium is greenish cream. Mature fruit is about 5 cm long, with bristles about 2 mm long and densely whitish-woolly; seeds are 2 mm long, with a white coma about 20 mm long. A separate diagnostic note states that flowers under 1.5 cm in diameter have simple corona lobes without appendages. The habitat entry gives grassland and stony slopes. This regional account does not establish developmental or population-level variation.
