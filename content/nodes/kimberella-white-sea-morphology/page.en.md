## A White Sea sample of Kimberella bodies {#s-cda0f014ca}
More than 35 Kimberella fossils from the White Sea show a bilaterally symmetrical benthic body, a broad foot-like region and a non-mineralized dorsal cover. The repeated anatomy invites comparison with molluscs, but the sample preserves no radula and does not establish membership in the molluscan crown group.
