## Distinguish the measured length {#s-42a4bb3a6f}
The recorded maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 120 mm. SCL is a straight-line shell measurement that excludes the head, neck and tail. This value is the maximum in the compiled material, not a theoretical growth limit or typical individual size. Sex-specific maxima, when available, may come from different localities or studies and cannot by themselves quantify sexual dimorphism across a population.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-218f703ed0}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, temperature-dependent sex determination (TSD), and a consolidated mean of 4.29 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch. The summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. A fractional value does not mean that one clutch contained a fraction of an individual. The lowest and highest fields mark endpoints of reported means, not guaranteed limits for individual clutches. These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-3a6660c514}
This limited account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 row, retaining its Reptile Database 2023 framework and trait-specific sources. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; a name and family match does not guarantee the same later taxonomic concept.
