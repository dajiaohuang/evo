## Lilac bell-shaped flowers in terminal panicles {#s-33908a3f62}
The morphology record describes dense stems and smooth leaves that may be alternate or opposite within a branch. The leaf blades range from spoon-shaped to lance-shaped or linear, with bases extending down the stem and edges that are entire or wavy. Terminal panicles carry lilac, bell-shaped flowers with three-parted stigmas. The fruit is an obconic capsule with oval seeds.
