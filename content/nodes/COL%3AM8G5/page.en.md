## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-bbaf28cdaa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery leaves ranging from narrow elliptic to lance-shaped, with pointed tips and variable, sometimes asymmetric bases. The leaf surface is not warty. The fruit segments are ellipsoid.

## What broad habitat is given? {#s-98278ce94c}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a locality.
