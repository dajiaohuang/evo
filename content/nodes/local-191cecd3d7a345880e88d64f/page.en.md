## The source tadpole concept {#s-42a664168c}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Atelopus laetissimus, in the genus Atelopus and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Atelopus laetissimus. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-4d6dbff5b6}
The compilation has four records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Their supplied stage labels are 25–28, 29–30, 35–37 and 41–42. These are labels attached to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; four records do not necessarily represent four unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
