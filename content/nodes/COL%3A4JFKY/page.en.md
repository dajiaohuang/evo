## Two substrates in the flora record {#s-ebb3e5df64}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists *Corticícola*, meaning growth on bark.

It also lists *Rupícola*, meaning growth on rock.

These are substrate categories, not collection localities. The field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## Scope of the record {#s-1d9bad2b0e}
The habitat fields do not say whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and they do not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

These substrate labels do not describe life cycle or reproduction; those were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
