## Long white flowers above woolly leaf undersides {#s-c4da0fa30c}
The morphology record describes woolly stems and obovate leaves with wedge-shaped bases and pointed tips. The upper surfaces are bristly and the undersides woolly. White bisexual flowers have a ribbed tubular-bell-shaped calyx over 1 cm long and a funnel-shaped corolla over 2 cm. The ovary is smooth; the fruit is a smooth, white globe. The habitat field says terrestrial.
