## Recorded parent of Protoceratidae {#s-fdfcc45fd3}
Protoceratidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Ruminantiamorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
