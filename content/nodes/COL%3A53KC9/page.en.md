## Leaves and inflorescence {#s-cf1dec16d7}
The source records a solitary aerial stem with persistent leaf-sheath remnants and conspicuous scars. Green pinnate leaves have persistent sheaths arranged helically around the stem, with fibrous, toothed margins. The petiole margins are also fibrous and toothed, and the leaf axis is curved. Leaflets are leathery, grouped, rigid, and inserted at differing angles; their tips are asymmetrically pointed. The panicle hangs down, and the fruiting structure is exposed between the leaves. The spadix is grooved and waxy; its main axis is sparsely woolly, and the smaller branches spiral around it.

## Fruit {#s-613aad8a4a}
Fruit is ovoid or spindle-shaped. The green or yellow-green outer layer is woolly; the middle layer is fibrous and succulent. The inner layer is woody and spindle-shaped or oval, and the endosperm is homogeneous.
