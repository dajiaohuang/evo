## The name and this data account {#s-fa8eb89fc0}
Capito auratus is accepted in COL26.8 within Capitonidae. AVONET v7 reports 10 measured individuals under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name, 4 with a complete morphological set. Usable samples may differ among traits, and the historical name match does not establish concept equivalence.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-4d873573b8}
Mean beak length is 16 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width and depth at that edge average 7 mm and 10.4 mm. These distinct dimensions use standardized landmarks and are not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-5815c68920}
Mean unflattened wing length is 84.3 mm, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 22.6 mm. The compiled mean mass is 57.37 g, with source category Dunning. Mass is a separate species-average source, not a measurement assigned to every morphology specimen.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-105ecd35d9}
AVONET assigns the categories Forest for habitat, Frugivore for trophic niche and Insessorial for lifestyle. They support cross-species comparison but do not mean exclusive forest use or an exclusively fruit-based diet.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-2d800b41f2}
The movement field is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps have latitude endpoints −16.71° and 7.91°; these are not a complete non-breeding movement record or a current range map. Geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Evidence limits {#s-2bd65aa75e}
These AVONET v7 values are compiled species means under the historical HBW–BirdLife name. Morphology comes from ten measured individuals, while mass is separately sourced; neither gives an individual range. Forest, Frugivore, Insessorial and Sedentary are broad categories, and the map summarizes historical breeding and resident limits rather than current occupancy. Matching names do not prove equivalence to COL26.8. Reproduction, current populations and conservation require other evidence.
