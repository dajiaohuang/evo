## Which classification concept does this record use? {#s-0ea701b53c}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates granti* in row Anf0030, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. It follows the source concept used by *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011). Its correspondence to the current COL26.8 species concept has not been established; the sections below describe this deposited row.

## Recorded foraging setting {#s-e480a5d2bb}
AmphiBIO lists terrestrial foraging. This broad category does not detail seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some microhabitat entries propagated from earlier amphibian compilations; a row-level comparison for this entry is not available. The recorded category is retained as historical, without an inferred replacement or a claim that the original is proven wrong.
