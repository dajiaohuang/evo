## One leaf and a three-lobed flower lip {#s-e9488a844d}
The regional account describes P. cultriformis as an epiphyte—a plant attached to another plant for physical support. Its pseudobulbs (swollen, stem-like orchid organs) or stems bear one elliptic leaf. The flower lip, a specialized petal in orchids, has three lobes and a cushion-like feature on the middle lobe; the flowers are described as yellow-green, white, or pink.

## A broad forest habitat label {#s-85664b81fb}
The regional habitat field says only “forests.” It does not name a locality or describe which forest conditions are associated with the species.
