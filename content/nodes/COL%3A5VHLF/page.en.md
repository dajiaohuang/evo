## Leaf veins and the inflorescence {#s-00be97a01e}
The regional description lists erect stems, ovate leaf blades with acute bases, and prominent secondary veins. The spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) is elliptical; the spadix (flower-bearing axis) is described as having a short stipe (supporting stalk), shorter than the spadix itself. The field does not characterize berry color. These are flora descriptors, not a sampled account of variation.

## A soil-associated category {#s-545f7387ee}
A separate Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 habitat row labels the species “terrícola,” or soil-associated. It gives no locality or collection date and does not define the species’ range.
