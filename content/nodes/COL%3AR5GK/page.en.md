## Accepted name and placement {#s-e6108be238}
COL26.8 accepts Cardioglossa nigromaculata Nieden, 1908 as a species and places it in the order Anura, family Arthroleptidae and genus Cardioglossa. This is the placement in the release issued on 20 August 2026; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database is a separate record whose identity needs its own evidence.
