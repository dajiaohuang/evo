## Bipinnate leaves and a swollen legume {#s-e29e417573}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical, unarmed branches and lanceolate stipules. Leaves are bipinnate, with one pair of pinnae and 3 or 5 foliolules per pinna; the leaflet number field is absent. A patelliform leaf nectary is present. The inflorescence is head shaped. Flowers are sessile with a tubular corolla, an exserted staminal tube and a nectary disk. The fruit is a turgid legume with glabrous valves. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
