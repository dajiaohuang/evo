## Lampranthus rupestris {#s-6e2881aaba}
## A creeping shrublet with rough grey leaves and white flowers

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a slender shrublet up to 15 cm tall, with spreading branches that lie prostrate or creep along the ground. The leaves are joined at the base, grey and rough, and grow to 12 mm long.

One to three white flowers, up to 25 mm across, are borne on stalks about 20 mm long. The habitat account records shallow depressions on granite outcrops.
