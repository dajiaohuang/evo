## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-16d6a01ff5}
COL26.8 records Lophozosterops at genus rank, with Zosteropidae as its direct parent.

EltonTraits 1.0 groups two historical entries here: Lophozosterops javanicus and L. pinaiae. The source has diet and foraging fields for both entries, with 0 activity records. These are compiled field counts, not counts of individual birds or proof that the same feeding pattern applies across the genus.

## Three more entries with narrower coverage {#s-fab9b82eaa}
The second EltonTraits group lists Lophozosterops squamiceps, L. superciliaris and L. dohertyi. It contains 1 diet field and foraging fields for 2 entries; activity records again total 0. This set has narrower coverage than the first and is not a complete ecology summary for the genus.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-785f942526}
The two-entry set contains same-name, same-family historical records with at least two usable ecology field groups; the three-entry set has exactly one usable group per record. Neither set is a complete descendant catalogue or evidence of traits shared by the genus. EltonTraits describes semiquantitative translations and interpolation under historical taxonomy; possible congeneric borrowing in ABC fields also limits species-level interpretation. The underlying source literature has not been reviewed here.

## A systematic review of the Indo-Australian Zosteropidae (Part III) {#study-account}
G. F. Mees treated Lophozosterops in a genus account on pages 171-204 of his 1969 review. The diagnosis compares wing and tail proportions and throat and underpart colors with Zosterops. These historical characters are not measurements for every species currently placed here, and the account does not settle later genus boundaries.
