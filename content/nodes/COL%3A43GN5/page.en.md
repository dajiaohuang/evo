## ## Glabrous stems and alternative calyx lobes {#s-e1f1228c99}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records glabrous stems and capitate glandular hairs; setae and short shoots are absent, although simple hairs are also listed in the stem fields. The stipules are shed, extrafloral nectaries are absent, and the projection between pinna pairs is listed as absent or present. The inflorescences are globose, with floral bracts shorter than the flowers before opening. The flowers have four or five perianth parts and a membranous calyx whose lobes are listed as absent, triangular or lance-shaped. Corolla lobes are pubescent and have one to three veins; the androecium is diplostemonous, with pink filaments. The entry preserves these alternatives but does not quantify their occurrence.
