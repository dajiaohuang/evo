## Life-history evidence limits {#s-73233f7a52}
The 2015 life-history archive reports historical same-name summaries. Except for maximum longevity, values are medians or averages of the two middle values, not measurements of one animal, individual ranges, mean wild lifespan, or current population estimates. The compilation may share or inherit records among historical species and subspecies; name and family matches do not remove that possibility or establish equivalence to COL26.8. Wild and captive conditions are not uniform, and underlying publications were not individually reviewed.

The EltonTraits account is a guide to historical same-name, same-family foraging records, not a complete species monograph. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of intake, stomach contents, energy, or time. Avian A/B and mammalian ABC codes retain uncertainty; mammalian ABC fields can draw on a congener. These fields do not establish individual, seasonal, or local variation, present-day range, population, or conservation status.
