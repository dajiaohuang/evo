These are compilation summaries rather than measurements of one animal. Reported non-longevity values are database medians or interpolated medians. The female-maturity field is defined as age at first conception, not first birth. Gestation is the source-defined interval from conception or fertilization to birth. The litter/clutch field can combine offspring and egg counts; a decimal is a compilation summary, not a fractional reproductive event. Maximum longevity is the longest record found, not a mean or typical lifespan.
