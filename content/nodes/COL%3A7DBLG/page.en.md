## A Nepalese comparison with prominent nodules and tail grooves {#s-6823da5d4b}
The kachinorum description characterized its Nepalese himalayanus comparator by sixteen large prominent rib nodules, distinct lateral tail grooves and relatively rougher skin. Light markings were described as absent from the head and rib nodules and confined to ventral surfaces on the limbs, in contrast to kachinorum. These are study-specific comparisons, not a complete modern diagnosis or a verified trait survey of all Himalayan populations. The named sister-species interpretation remains mitochondrial evidence rather than proof of an experimentally measured reproductive barrier.

## Relative nodule size compared with a different species {#s-39f1fffdd0}
The 2026 vietnamirabilis comparison called himalayanus rib nodules small and its tail fin fold well developed, with dark brown parotoids, nodules and vertebral ridge. The 2019 kachinorum comparison called himalayanus nodules large and prominent relative to kachinorum. These relative adjectives have different comparators and do not provide a common measured nodule-size scale. Both source scopes are retained; the newer relative description does not erase the earlier observation or certify all populations.
