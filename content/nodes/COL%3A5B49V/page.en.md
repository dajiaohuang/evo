## Soft leaves with varied hair cover {#s-eb851136f5}
This morphology record describes upright stems with woolly, silky or soft hairs, without glands. The stalked leaves are thin and range from lance-shaped to inversely lance-shaped, oblong or oval; the upper surface may be woolly or finely hairy, while the lower may be nearly smooth or shaggy. The heads contain 8–22 florets with smooth, glandless corollas. The hairy fruits have no glands, and the pappus has flattened outer bristles. The habitat field says terrestrial.
