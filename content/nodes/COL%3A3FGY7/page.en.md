## Form and flowers {#s-3eedb0efd6}
The regional account describes a cormous geophyte 7–25 cm tall, with small corms and woody, concentric tunics. The linear leaves have thickened, sticky margins and midribs. Flowers are small and cream-coloured or white, with spreading tepals.

## Diagnostic comparison with *Geissorhiza humilis* {#s-91f5723a6f}
A separate SANBI account specifies three leaves, with sticky surfaces and thickened margins and main veins. The relatively small, creamy-white flowers have a narrow perianth tube 3–4 mm long and narrowly elliptic tepals 10–15 × 5–8 mm. The account says *G. hispidula* has often been confused with *Geissorhiza humilis*; when the two sometimes grow and flower together, their differences are clear. *G. humilis* has bright-yellow flowers, broader tepals 16–22 × 12–14 mm, and a perianth tube 5–6 mm long.
