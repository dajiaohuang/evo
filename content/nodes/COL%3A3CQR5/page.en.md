## What traits distinguish Euphorbia sessilifolia? {#s-29596343ac}
The cylindrical stems are pubescent. Leaves are alternate, entire, and have lateral glandular stipules. Green bracts surround loose dichasial clusters of cyathia at the upper nodes. The involucre is bell-shaped and hairy, with four or more transversely elliptic glands bearing upright, finger-like appendages. Female flowers lack a pseudoperianth; their ovaries are hairy and styles undivided. The fruit is hairy, and seeds lack a caruncle.
