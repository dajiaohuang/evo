## T-shaped leaves and black-and-white flowers {#s-d3dc0e2578}
Romulea barkerae is 4–6 cm tall and has a single basal leaf with two broad grooves, giving it a T-shaped cross-section. Its white flowers have large black patches outlined in yellow; the inner bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters) are membranous.

## Limestone rocks {#s-199be284ba}
The regional source records limestone rocks as habitat.
