## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-f9bf5b6cde}
COL26.8 records Agamodon anguliceps as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trogonophidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-4ab55581f5}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 200 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-10a64e9054}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bars-Closel et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-46d3b7a469}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
