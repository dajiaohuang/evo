## Philodendron insigne: leaf and floral descriptors {#s-5660757090}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records densely branched roots, short stem internodes and a vinaceous prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous. The dull-green petiole is flattened above and rounded below. Leaf blades are entire, oblanceolate or somewhat spatulate, with cuspidate tips and cuneate bases. Primary lateral veins are listed on both surfaces, with 13–20; secondary and tertiary veins are numerous.

The terminal inflorescence count per sympodium is given as “1/2,” without clarifying whether it represents alternatives or a range. The cylindrical peduncle is vinaceous. The constricted, convolute, oblong spathe has vinaceous tube surfaces and inner blade, with a cream outer blade. The spadix is stipitate: its female zone is oblong, the sterile-male zone cylindrical and the fertile-male zone subcylindrical, with an obtuse tip.

The entry lists 4 or 5 stamens, narrowed at the base, short staminodes, a prismatic gynoecium and an elliptic ovary. Locule counts are 4 or 5; ovule counts are 2 or 6, described as hemianatropous. Placentation is axial or biseriate, and the stigma is discoid. The fruit is subcylindrical and pink; seeds are subglobose.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The “1/2” value and alternative counts remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
