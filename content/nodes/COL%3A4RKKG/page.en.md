## Flower structure in the 1997 flora account {#s-30382364a8}
The 1997 regional flora describes *Raphionacme dyeri* as a perennial herb recorded at 250 mm tall, with flowers ranging from green to purplish. Its corolla lobes carry a bright purple or magenta band shaped like an inverted V. The corona lobes divide into three segments: the middle segment is threadlike, while the two lateral segments are horn-shaped. These diagnostic details come from one regional account.

## Recorded habitat {#s-bc3652a4a3}
The same account lists grassland as habitat. It supplies no observation locality, date, or population frequency, so the entry does not establish the species’ complete range or habitat variation.
