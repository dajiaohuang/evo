## Lasiopogon ponticulus {#s-e4ff4da316}
## A sand-dwelling annual with woolly flower clusters

Herman's 2013 account describes a small annual that lies flat or grows with its stems partly upright; stems reach 80 mm. Its spatula-shaped leaves are gray-white and covered in cobwebby wool.

Small, disc-shaped flower heads form woolly clusters at branch tips. Their surrounding bracts are colorless, whitish or pale straw-colored; the disc is whitish and sometimes red-tipped. The dry fruits are hairless, and the pappus bristles are feathered along their upper half. The account records the species in sand.
