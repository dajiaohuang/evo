## Morphology and field descriptions {#s-9b82416170}
The Mananara-Nord description reported a mean body mass of about 0.99 kg. The head, shoulders, and mid-back were mottled reddish gray, becoming lighter gray-brown farther back. A faint dark midline or inverted-Y pattern crossed the head and back; the underside was pale and the tail tip dark brown to black. These traits describe the study sample.

## Known localities and evidence limits {#s-8b46335f12}
The paper then knew the species only from the Ivontaka-Sud and Verezanantsoro lowland-rainforest parcels in Mananara-Nord Biosphere Reserve. The Fahambahy or Mananara River were possible northern limits; sparse sampling to the south left the southern limit unresolved.
