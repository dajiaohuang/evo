## Habenaria malacophylla {#s-4608dc7340}
The South African account describes Habenaria malacophylla as a terrestrial herb up to 0.7 m tall, with green-and-white flowers. The lip is three-lobed, 6–7 mm long, and its lobes are similar in length; the spur measures 12–15 mm. Forest margins and forest floors are the recorded settings.
