## Chresta curumbensis {#s-659208020f}
## A terminal hemispherical flower-head cluster

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes basal rosettes of obovate leaves with pinnate veins. Leaf stalks are absent or very short. Margins range from smooth to toothed or double-toothed, and the dense covering of hairs may be lost as leaves mature.

A single, terminal, hemispherical syncephalium—a compact head-like cluster—ends its growth as a determinate unit. The surrounding bracts are red. Flowers are red with yellow rings at the top of the corolla tube and at the bases of its lobes. The fruits are alike in form, and their pappus falls away. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
