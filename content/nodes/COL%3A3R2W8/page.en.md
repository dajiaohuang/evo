## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-500014ebd3}
COL26.8 records Karnsophis siantaris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Homalopsidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-4329f7e7b5}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 465 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-fcfb86ba38}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
