## Tinamous provide flighted comparison lineages {#s-e08e7cbb55}
How do comparative genomes inform ideas about paleognath relationships and flight loss? Two papers published in 2019 analyze four tinamous: Crypturellus cinnamomeus, Eudromia elegans, Nothoprocta perdicaria and Tinamus guttatus. The first three were assembled for those studies; the last was an existing genome. Their analyses inform the proposed moa–tinamou relationship and flight-loss history in other lineages, but do not sample every tinamou species. A candidate regulatory sequence from E. elegans was tested in a chicken-embryo reporter assay; activity in the experimental host is not natural tinamou flight performance.
