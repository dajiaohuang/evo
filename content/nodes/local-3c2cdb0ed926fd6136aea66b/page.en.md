## The deposited caecilian record {#s-7175880621}
CaecilianTraits version 4 lists Ichthyophis elongatus in species-level row 120, under genus Ichthyophis and family Ichthyophiidae. Individual, population and species data are kept separate in the compilation. This source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Measurement material {#s-13eefac29e}
This source concept has 2 measurement rows: 2 individual-level rows, 0 population-level rows and 0 species-level rows. These counts describe table rows; they are not population estimates or counts of unique specimens. Specimen identifiers, sex codes and references remain attached to their rows. Comparisons of body dimensions need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-d51e1c9eaf}
For this source concept, CaecilianTraits records broad habitat as Terrestrial|Freshwater (=Inland waters) and microhabitat as Fossorial. These categorical labels summarize the compilation and do not specify the frequency of habitat use or a complete geographic range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-ac04dd7d9b}
The deposited reproduction field lists the category as oviparous. This category does not specify parental care, the timing of reproduction or population variation.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-0196bfb4f7}
The species-level row lists dorsal annulus count: 290; ventral annulus count: 274. These are deposited species-level values, which may be summaries rather than integer measurements of one animal; they do not establish invariant diagnostic counts for every individual.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-a982940858}
In the deposited record, the aquatic-larvae field notes presence of a water-dependent larval stage. This is the compilation category described in the associated data paper, not a statement that adults always inhabit water. The accompanying reproductive metadata and original reference must be consulted for observational scope and qualifications.
