## What leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Manihot irwinii? {#s-acea1edeba}
The roots are non-tuberous, with a rigid, shapeless xylopodium. Stems may reach 1–2.9 m and are described as smooth, erect, and hairless, with yellow latex. Simple, petiolate leaves may be lobed, with oblong or rounded lobes; triangular stipules fall early. Terminal unisexual racemes have basal female flowers. The involucral bracts are leaf-like, over 0.5 cm wide, glabrous, and oval-lanceolate. Male buds are ovoid and female buds pyramidal, both hairless. The capsule is conical, smooth, hairless, and ribbed or slightly ridged. Seeds are elliptic and smooth, with a caruncle that may be prominent.
