## Mostly aquatic egg deposition with one glass-associated mass {#s-45daf56527}
The maintained pyrrhus generally deposited eggs in water, but one egg mass was found partly attached to terrarium glass. The study also identified high within-species variability in egg-mass size. The exception prevents a claim that every captive egg was laid freely in water; it does not demonstrate a separate obligatory natural reproductive mode. ## Captive metamorphosis from 126 to nearly 300 days {#s-4efa289a99}
The fastest reported pyrrhus tadpole completed metamorphosis after 126 days, while some slow individuals required nearly 300 days. The statement describes the study’s captive time-to-metamorphosis observations, not a mean or a fixed species schedule.
