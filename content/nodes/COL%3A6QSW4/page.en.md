## What does the record say about Malmea manausensis? {#s-2e506c96b8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic leaves with tapering tips and pointed or gradually narrowed bases. The fruit is ellipsoid. Flowers grow opposite the leaves or at branch ends.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-45c4ba3d43}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial; no site-specific detail is provided.
