## Large leaves with five pairs of side veins {#s-3d08050095}
The record describes oblong to ovate leaves 18–25 cm long, on 1.2–1.7 cm stalks. Their bases are wedge-shaped or rounded, and the tips are pointed; four or five pairs of secondary veins are recorded. The main flower-stalk axis is 6–10 cm long, sometimes 20 cm, with 1 cm bracts fringed by hairs. The petals are smooth and lack appendages; the sepals are hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
