## Marcgravia nepenthoides Seem. {#s-543a9d52d7}
## Long, dipper-shaped nectaries on a woody vine

Marcgravia nepenthoides is a large woody vine with long, leathery leaves and small glands near their margins. Its umbel-like inflorescences bear 20–35 or more fertile flowers and five or six large nectaries shaped like dippers, each 5–7.5 cm long. Flowers sit at right angles to their stalks; the corolla is conical and about 8–10 mm long, with roughly 25–30 stamens. The globose fruit has a small pointed tip. The cited range is in Atlantic Central American rainforests, from British Honduras to Panama.
