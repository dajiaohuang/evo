## Guarea convergens {#s-e14c618cab}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description links opposite leaflets with axillary inflorescences, strigose flower parts and a ribbed or warty globose fruit.

## Opposite leaflets, axillary flowers and a textured fruit {#s-b208bd9842}
The regional description records a grooved or semi-cylindrical leaf rachis and a terminal bud. Leaflets are opposite, elliptic, oblong or oblanceolate, with acuminate or attenuate tips, eucamptodromous venation and pubescence below. Inflorescences are axillary. The outer calyx and corolla are strigose; corolla parts meet edge-to-edge in bud (valvate). The outer surface of the staminal tube may be glabrous or hairy, the ovary is strigose, and each ovary locule has one ovule. The fruit is globose with a truncate apex, ribbed or warty texture, and hairs. A separate ecology field classifies the recorded setting as “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
