## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-7f2bbd5748}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Ovate stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) may persist or fall. The leaf stalk (petiole) and central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) are winged; each leaf is recorded with four or five leaflet pairs. A stalkless, cup-shaped nectary (nectar-secreting gland) is noted. The inflorescence is listed as spike-like or racemose (flowers arranged along a main axis); the calyx (outer floral cup) may be tubular or funnel-shaped, and flowers are recorded as stalkless or stalked. The dataset marks the pod-wall texture, fruit shape and surface hair covering as unknown. This is one regional field and does not establish variation or how widely the traits occur.
