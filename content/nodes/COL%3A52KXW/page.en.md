## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Stigmaphyllon rotundifolium? {#s-f3bfcf57ed}
The branches are smooth, with 31–60 mm petioles bearing sessile, cup-shaped glands at the tip. Leaf blades may be lobed, with sparse silky hairs below and entire margins carrying sessile glands. Flower clusters form umbels arranged in a dichasium and bear 15 to many flowers. The yellow petals may have red spots or streaks; their edges are toothed. Anthers are hairless, and styles are hairless, with shorter front styles and expanded tips. The fruit has a developed dorsal wing and one pair of lateral winglets.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c65e4a929f}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
