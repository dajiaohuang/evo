## Asteriornis near the crown of the bird tree {#s-e2d5eae814}
The holotype NHMM 2013 008 preserves a three-dimensionally intact skull imaged by CT, with associated postcranial bones from a tightly constrained Maastrichtian horizon in Belgium. Parsimony places Asteriornis beside crown Galloanserae, while tip-dated Bayesian analysis puts it on the galliform stem. These competing results make the fossil a test of early bird relationships, not proof of the globally oldest modern bird.
