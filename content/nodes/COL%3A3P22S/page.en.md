## Source-reported morphology {#s-7c5b46a7cb}
A morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset reports leaves 3.0–15 mm wide, with downward-pointing tips and a herbaceous texture. It also describes mature infructescences as procumbent and the seed coat as black.

This is a verbatim morphology field from the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset (row 90403). It does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, or sex or life stage beyond what the field states.

## Habitat category in the dataset {#s-b312b8cda1}
The Brazilian Flora dataset records the habitat or substrate category as “terrestrial” (*terrícola*).

This category is copied from Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat row 39159; the field supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range, or sampling-completeness information.
