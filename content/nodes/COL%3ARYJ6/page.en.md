## Cayaponia trilobata Cogn. {#s-0f025699c7}
## Few large fruits among raceme-borne flowers

Cayaponia trilobata has non-prostrate branches and tendrils that may be simple or branched. Its alternate leaves are thin or leathery, and their bases do not run down the petiole. Bell-shaped flowers are borne in racemes or alone. Fruits are reported as few, with one or two noted; they are at least 25 mm long, ellipsoid to obovoid, and arranged in racemes. At maturity they range from yellow to red and from becoming hairless to hairless. The fruit stalk is hairless. Each fruit has one to three dark, ovate-oblong to nearly round seeds with a fibrous aril.
