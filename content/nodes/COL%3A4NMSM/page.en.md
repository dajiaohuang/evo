## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d064349af8}
COL26.8 records Pseuderemias brenneri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Lacertidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-226044f00f}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 53 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Dixon 2013. The row reports a female maximum SVL of 48 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. These overall and sex-specific maxima cite separate studies, but the row gives no matched sample ranges, sample sizes, or localities; they do not establish population-level sexual dimorphism.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-2081a7e8ea}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.
