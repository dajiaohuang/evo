## Overview {#s-ed2489cab1}
COL26.8 lists three accepted species in Oedipomidas: O. oedipus, O. geoffroyi and O. leucopus. This reading path adds species pages for all three. Many selected field papers use the earlier combination Saguinus; English common names are taken from research titles, Chinese names are editorial renderings, and taxonomic identity follows the pinned COL26.8 IDs.

## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-63735dae34}
Start with O. oedipus: compare the five-year group study at Colosó with reproductive records from two field sites in 1994–2008, then read the 2005/2012 accessible-forest survey and its extrapolation assumption. Continue with O. geoffroyi, from the multi-site Panama survey in the 1980s to canopy work at two forest sites and the local Cerro Ancón transect. Finish with O. leucopus by separating the twelve-month ecological observations of two Remedios groups from a GPS methods trial on another group. Spatial coverage, sample units and years differ and should not be pooled.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-1bdfbb1576}
This is a reading order connecting three COL-accepted names to selected local studies, not a genus-wide ecological review or population comparison. Saguinus combinations remain in source citations; similar names across databases have not been used to infer record equivalence. Population estimates, density, reproductive observations and home ranges remain tied to their stated dates, sites and methods.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-2bf0ac7d90}
The genus count follows the pinned COL26.8 checklist and does not imply that every checklist uses the same genus-level arrangement.
