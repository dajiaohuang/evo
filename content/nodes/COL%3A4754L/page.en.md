## Nerine ridleyi {#s-bdb44e6d41}
The regional account says little is known about Nerine ridleyi. It resembles N. humilis but has broader leaves, 15–25 mm wide, and flower stalks up to 80 mm long. The account also notes short, thick leaves with blunt tips and records steep, south-facing sandstone ledges as habitat; these limited observations do not establish its full range.
