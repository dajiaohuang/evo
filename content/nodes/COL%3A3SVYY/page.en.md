## Lecosia formicarum {#s-27cdef52c0}
## Papery elliptic leaves and short perianth segments

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology record gives stems 1–5 mm wide. Leaf blades are elliptic or oblong-elliptic with a papery texture, and the inflorescence axis is hairless. Tepals, the perianth segments, measure 4.5–6.5 mm long.

That dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial.
