## Leaves and flowers {#s-66d7c5dcc1}
Bayer's 2012 regional account describes a stemless succulent with smooth, triangular, sharply pointed leaves that range from silver-green to yellowish. The flowers are white.

This is a regional species description published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-0f46d23e28}
The account records the species on shale or sandstone flats in renosterveld.

This habitat note comes from a regional species account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
