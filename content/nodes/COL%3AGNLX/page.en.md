## Form and flowers {#s-6cbfd28e19}
The 2012 regional account describes a rhizomatous perennial up to 20 cm tall, with a flattened, two-winged stem and fairly soft, sword-shaped leaves. The flowers are blue, the spathes have translucent margins, and the capsules are elongated and cylindrical with three angles.

These traits come from a regional South African account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Flower clusters and capsules {#s-1773c8e62f}
A 2020 diagnostic account gives a typical height of 80–120 mm, though some plants are shorter. It describes soft, sword-shaped leaves and a compressed, narrowly two-winged flowering stem 1.5–2.0 mm wide, with one or more leaves along its length. The stem is often unbranched and ends in one flower cluster; particularly robust plants may also have one or two sessile clusters on the sides. Each cluster has at most two flowers, and side clusters often have just one. The flowers are nearly sessile in relatively long green spathes; their elongated ovaries extend above the spathes. The ovary is triangular in cross-section; mature capsules are recorded as (14–)20–25(–30) mm long.

These diagnostic details come from a regional South African account published in 2020; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-acd2da825c}
The regional flora records the species mainly on clay flats and lower slopes in renosterveld.

This habitat description comes from a regional South African account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
