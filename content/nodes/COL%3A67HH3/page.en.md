## Anthurium illepidum: morphology and two habitat labels {#s-b4b92e2830}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and a fibrous base. Petioles are marked smooth, but their shape is uncharacterized. Leaf blades are entire and flat, linear or elliptic, with acute tips and bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is not characterized, secondary veins are impressed on the upper surface, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf. Peduncle shape and the spathe's angle to it are uncharacterized; the flat spathe is oblong or elliptic and not stipitate. The fruit is a berry, with no color character supplied.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The dataset gives two habitat labels: “Rupícola” (rock-dwelling) and “Terrícola” (terrestrial).

These regional habitat categories give no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
