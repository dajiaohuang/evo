## What first description is given for Jamesbrittenia albanensis? {#s-73082b9140}
The source describes a spreading, glandular-hairy shrublet up to 45 cm tall. Its leaves are lobed, and pink to mauve flowers grow in the leaf axils, with an orange throat.

## How does the source compare it with related species? {#s-faaf76d5a1}
A second account highlights blunt leaf lobes, glandular hairs on both leaf surfaces, and mauve-to-magenta flowers. It compares J. albanensis with J. filicaulis, whose flowers have a white corolla limb, and with J. foliolosa, which differs in its leaf-hair pattern. The source says J. foliolosa may hybridize with J. albanensis near Grahamstown; this is presented as a possibility, not a confirmed hybrid record.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-7fde8f3bbf}
The habitat field places it in Karroid scrub, a shrubland vegetation type. No locality is given in this field.
