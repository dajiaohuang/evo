## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-00ae93754c}
COL26.8 records Ctenotus militaris as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-c6d12b18a8}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 65 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-3b89bcd5b2}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-772a41381d}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
