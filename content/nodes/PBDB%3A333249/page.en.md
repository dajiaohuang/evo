## Recorded parent of Proentelodontinae {#s-4de4d79706}
Proentelodontinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Entelodontidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
