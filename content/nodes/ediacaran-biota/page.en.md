## What dated Ediacaran fossils can tell us {#s-5ad198d7a8}
Late Ediacaran marine rocks preserve large body fossils and trace fossils in repeated assemblages across several continents. Many organisms' biological affinities remain debated, and preservation shapes which forms enter the record. In Newfoundland's Avalon succession, zircon dating brackets the Gaskiers glaciation and places fossils above it by about 571 million years ago. That age applies to this regional assemblage; it does not date the origin of animals or a global first appearance.
