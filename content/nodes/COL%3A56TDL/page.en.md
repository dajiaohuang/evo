## Thyrsodium rondonianum {#s-5066b196d8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records coloured hair tufts and floral details, while leaving two colour or sex-related fields unknown.

## Hair tufts, a cup-shaped hypanthium and a globose fruit {#s-9487eb05a7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field leaves the outer bark colour unknown and records the inner bark as reddish brown. It describes tufts of golden, cream or yellow-orange hairs at the lower-surface axils of secondary leaf veins; the upper leaflet surface is dull, and its primary vein is sparsely or densely pubescent. Inflorescence hairs are listed in the same three colour alternatives. Anthers are glabrous; the presence of staminodes in female flowers is unknown; the male-flower hypanthium is deeply cup-shaped. The fruit is globose. A separate ecology field is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or range.
