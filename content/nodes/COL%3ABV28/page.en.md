## Male throat colour in a diagnostic comparison {#s-f8665ff43a}
The2020 comparison describes male nidicola throats as dark gray, in contrast to the white centre/yellow edges reported for velocicantus. Sex and comparison scope matter: this paragraph does not establish female throat colour, a complete preservation-dependent colour series or population-wide variation. ## A single-note arrangement in the reviewed comparison {#s-4ac95be22d}
The2020 description summarizes nidicola calls as a single note emitted continuously or irregularly, contrasting them with velocicantus trills of66–138 pulsed notes. This information is attributed to prior acoustic studies cited there; their original recordings, individual counts and temperature conditions have not been reread here. “Single note” describes the reported call unit, not inability to repeat notes or produce other vocalizations.
