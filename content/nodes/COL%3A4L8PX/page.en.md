## Polygonanthus punctulatus {#s-58eeb42308}
## Dotted leaves with slightly rolled margins

The source describes Polygonanthus punctulatus with grey upper branches and brown lower stems. Its leaves are oblong-lanceolate or oblong-elliptic, with slightly rolled margins, a yellowish underside and visible dots. Bracts are bristly; stamens extend beyond the flower, while the outer calyx lobes and petal margins are smooth. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
