## Stems, leaves and flowers {#s-d752109f39}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records erect stems. Each pinna (a main division of a compound leaf) bears 15–80 pairs of linear, sickle-shaped leaflets, with silky hairs on the underside. The source gives this broad leaflet-count range without explaining its variation. It also describes an elongated raceme (a flower cluster arranged along a central axis) and purple petals. This is a regional dataset field, not a complete account of the species' variation.

## Habitat category {#s-8b357611fd}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
