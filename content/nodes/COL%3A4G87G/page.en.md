## Philodendron loefgrenii: leaf and inflorescence characters {#s-5a9e7b8007}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots, short stem internodes and a light-green prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous. Petioles are described as conspicuous, short, striate and cylindrical. Leaf blades are entire, elliptic, oblong or oblanceolate, with truncate or cuneate bases and entire margins; lateral veins are obscure when the blade is green.

The inflorescence is axillary on the sympodium. Its green peduncle is short and shorter than the spathe. The spathe is non-constricted and oblong; its outer and inner surfaces are recorded as light green or bright green, but the compound color field does not clearly assign each state. The spadix is subsessile.

Stamens and staminodes are short; the gynoecium and ovary are ovoid. There are 5 locules and 4 or 5 ovules, with subbasal placentation and a globose stigma. Fruit and seed are recorded as unknown.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The spathe color states are retained without assigning them to a specific surface.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
