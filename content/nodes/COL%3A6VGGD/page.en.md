## Accepted name and catalogue position {#s-eceae15564}
COL26.8, released on 20 August 2026, records Physalaemus obtectus Bokermann, 1966 as a species in the genus Physalaemus, family Leptodactylidae, and order Anura. This is the classification in that release; a matching name in another checklist or fossil database needs separate evidence before the records can be treated as the same biological concept.

## What the classification can tell us {#s-39e437af4b}
The accepted record places this species within Physalaemus, Leptodactylidae and Anura. That classification gives a route to related catalogue entries, but it does not by itself establish the species' full morphology, life history, distribution, evolutionary history or conservation status.

## A compilation codes larval development and aquatic egg laying {#s-1d4fe8a153}
