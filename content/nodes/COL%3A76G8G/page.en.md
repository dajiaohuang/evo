## A literature-recorded parasite host {#s-58861205e9}
A 2025 review of Molineidae parasites lists Peltophryne fustiger as a host of Oswaldocruzia barusi. The association is compiled from earlier reports, not from newly examined animals or evidence that every member is infected. The review gives no number of frogs examined, prevalence, infection intensity, pathology, or mortality for this host; its host label does not by itself verify each historical identification.

## What the review covers {#s-0567c79247}
The review searched regional Molineidae literature from 1819–2025 in BioOne, ISI, Jstor, PubMed, Scielo, Scopus, Web of Science, and monographs. It excluded incomplete records, unpublished theses and meeting material, and parasites not identified to species; it also sought species-level molecular records in literature and GenBank.
