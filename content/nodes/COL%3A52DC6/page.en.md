## What leaf, flower and follicle traits are recorded? {#s-e18a466ccc}
The simple leaves have three to five lobes, and the stipules fall away. The bell-shaped calyx has appendages on the inner surface of its lobes. The androgynophore—the stalk joining the male and female flower structures—is hairy at least near its base; the flower has 12–15 stamens. The inner surface of the fruit follicles bears star-shaped hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-9a3ab9ec59}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
