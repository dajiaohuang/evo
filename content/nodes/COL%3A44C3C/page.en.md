## A very short tube and afternoon flowers {#s-d795c412c0}
Moraea brevituba is 400–600 mm tall and has yellow flowers with a perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts) only 1–2 mm long. The flowers open in mid-afternoon and last only a few hours; the ovary is often partly outside the spathe (a bract that surrounds or encloses a flower cluster).
