## *Eryngium ciliatum*: sessile flowers and scale-like fruit surfaces {#s-9ac853855d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field describes stalked leaves with veins that run in parallel or join near the margins. The flower cluster stands upright, and its flowers attach directly to the axis without individual stalks. The fruit also lacks a stalk, opens at maturity, and has a back covered in blister-like to scale-like structures.

The substrate field records terrestrial ground only, without a locality or date. These are regional morphological characters, not a population-level survey.
