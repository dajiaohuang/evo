## Cynometra hostmanniana {#s-8b5bdebcb0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Cynometra hostmanniana* records leaflets with slightly asymmetric bases and long tips, stalks over 2 mm, ellipsoid wrinkled fruits. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
