## A basal rosette with large bladder cells {#s-39014e6721}
This account describes a prostrate annual with a small basal rosette of flat, ovate-spoon-shaped leaves. The leaves have large, bladder-like cells on their surfaces. White or pinkish flowers are 15–30 mm across. The fruit has valve wings that bend inward over the valves. Coastal sands are listed as habitat.
