## The source tadpole concept {#s-0e2eec9d75}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Hyalinobatrachium aureoguttatum, in the genus Hyalinobatrachium and family Centrolenidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Hyalinobatrachium aureoguttatum. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-019d1abd5e}
The compilation has 12 records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28 and 35. These are labels attached to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; 12 records do not necessarily represent 12 unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
