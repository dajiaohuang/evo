## The source concept {#s-fa39d2e63b}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Odontophrynus americanus as row Anf1380, classified in Odontophrynus, Cycloramphidae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-b4567f12e1}
The row marks fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic foraging. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage variation in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against those revisions; verify the historical categories before reuse. No replacement values are inferred, and the notice does not imply that every original entry is wrong.

## Activity periods {#s-0b4432fb37}
The row marks daytime activity. These broad categories are not a continuous record of behaviour over time.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-4c5cc0ac12}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 51 mm for Odontophrynus americanus. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-3a79895d44}
The row reports up to one reproductive event per year. These literature-derived extrema and counts do not separately identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions. Size values may refer to offspring or eggs.
