## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b7d9b61270}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Slavens and Slavens 1999. ## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-45bbd721a8}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
