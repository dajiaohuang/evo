## Early perissodactyls spread across northern continents {#s-dbcf21e371}
An analysis of 71 terminals and 102 anatomical characters traces the earliest sampled perissodactyls to the Paleocene–Eocene Thermal Maximum, followed by records across northern continents and varied dental and locomotor forms. The pattern supports rapid dispersal, but sparse sampling and alternative stem placements leave the order’s place of origin unresolved; a first sampled fossil is not an origin date.
