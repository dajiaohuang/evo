## *Mikania grazielae*: triangular leaves with gland-dotted undersides {#s-a291b4d9cb}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, thin triangular leaves with entire margins and three strong basal veins. The base may be heart-shaped or squared off; tips range from sharp to shortly tapered. The upper surface is hairless, while the lower is gland-dotted. Branches may twine or be hairless, and the stem pith is recorded as hollow or solid. Loose panicles carry stalked heads; their bracts are hairless with blunt tips. The field describes the fruitlets as woolly.
