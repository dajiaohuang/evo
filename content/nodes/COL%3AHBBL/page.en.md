## Asparagus angusticladus {#s-0d2d77ffe0}
Asparagus angusticladus is a deciduous climber reaching 3 m, with slender, twining branches and short, narrow cladodes. Its flowers are small and white, with red anthers, and the shiny red berries are about 8 mm across. The regional account records it in bushveld and describes it spreading among upper tree branches.
