## Mitochondrial trees and clocks answer different questions {#s-0f53bc4ef0}
Mitochondrial sequences recover a conditional topology among sampled neopterygians, with uneven support among nodes; the limited living sample cannot delimit the fossil range or direct ancestors of Neopterygii. A separate 2024 clock analysis estimates crown Neopterygii at 331.33–312.76 Ma. That interval depends on its model and calibrations and is not a fossil first appearance or exact lineage duration.
