## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-264244abe1}
COL26.8 records Hemidactylus srikanthani as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-20736e090b}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-e526a37078}
