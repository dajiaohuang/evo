## How far can a joint model take us? {#s-48d369fdbd}
Ichthyostega is a Devonian tetrapod whose reconstructed limb joints let researchers test how much rotation the limbs could achieve. One study compared that model with the mechanics of a typical lateral-sequence walk; the result concerns possible joint motion, not observed behavior.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-0230563465}
Micro-CT reconstruction and comparison with five living analogues constrain which joint movements are mechanically feasible. The result depends on the reconstruction and does not turn feasible motion into observed behavior.

## A feasible gait is not an observed gait {#s-7a61997a14}
Micro-CT joint reconstructions were compared with five living analogues. In the tested model, a typical lateral-sequence walk required no long-axis limb rotation; that result describes what the reconstruction permits, not how the fossil animal was seen to walk.

## The model is tied to East Greenland material {#s-1c9684c75a}
The analysed Ichthyostega specimens come from Famennian Upper Devonian deposits of East Greenland. This is the study's sample context, not a complete geographic or habitat range.

## atlas-profile / readerLimitations / en {#s-342d4e5fdb}
The specimens come from Famennian East Greenland deposits described as aquatic or marginal-water; that setting does not establish preferred habitat.
