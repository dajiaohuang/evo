## A complex branch-site signal is not a demonstrated amino acid change {#s-72e94052ac}
The paper discusses a MEME signal at RH1 site277 on spurrelli and the ancestral branch leading to spurrelli, glyphus, limosus and varius. It reports two synonymous changes between serine codons rather than a recovered amino acid replacement on those branches; an unobserved nonsynonymous intermediate is suggested. For Atelopus varius, this ancestral-branch context does not establish a species-terminal mutation, a measured spectral shift or a proven adaptive mechanism.
