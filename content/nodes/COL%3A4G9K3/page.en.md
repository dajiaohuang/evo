## Philonotis rufiflora: two recorded habitat categories {#s-9af0de7395}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists “rupícola” (rock-associated) as one habitat category for *Philonotis rufiflora*.

A second entry lists “terrícola” (terrestrial).

These are habitat or substrate categories in a regional flora dataset. The entries give no collection locality or survey date and do not establish a complete range.
