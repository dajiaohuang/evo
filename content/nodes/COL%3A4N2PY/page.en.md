## Resprouting shrub and flower heads {#s-9c9d97328e}
The source describes a densely branched, resprouting shrub to 1 m, with linear, channelled, rigid leaves that are rough and softly hairy when young. Flower heads are cup-shaped, 35–40 mm across, with reddish-brown velvety involucral bracts; habitat is sandstone slopes.
