## How does Priva africana look and feel? {#s-7ca435da36}
Priva africana is described as a perennial herb up to 40 cm tall, sticky across the plant. Its leaves have pointed tips and deeply cut teeth; they are stalkless or nearly so. The fruiting calyx swells conspicuously around the fruit. Flowers may be white, pale blue, or purple.

## What habitat and soil are listed? {#s-f08678fa0b}
The habitat field records savanna and limestone or other calcareous soils. It does not name a collection site.
