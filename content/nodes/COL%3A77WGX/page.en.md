## Accepted name and placement {#s-d3dcd9aa5d}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis avicuporum (Duellman & Pramuk, 1999) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A compiled maximum length {#s-5eaacc03f5}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 34.3 mm for its Frost 2011 source concept. In anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the cloaca. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of all adults, and the source concept has not been reconciled with the accepted species name used here.
