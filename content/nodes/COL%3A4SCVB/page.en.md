## What seedling-root, leaf and flower-cluster details are listed? {#s-c86ba74b1e}
The radicle, the young root of the seedling, is reported as 25–65 cm long. The leaf blades lack dots. Inflorescences contain many flowers and branch three to six times. The style divides into two to four branches.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-b743cb0f19}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
