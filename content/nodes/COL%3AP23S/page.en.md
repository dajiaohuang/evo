## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima guilleminiana? {#s-665c8f4ba0}
The lower leaf surface has silky hairs. Flower clusters carry two, three or many flowers on stalkless flower stalks. Petals are yellow or orange. Anthers may be hairless or hairy, and the fruit may be hairless or silky-hairy.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d686f659f2}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
