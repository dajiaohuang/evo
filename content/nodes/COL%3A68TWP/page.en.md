## Calea serrata {#s-cd476fa8cb}
## Congested yellow heads above toothed leaves

Flora do Brasil 2020 leaves the root type unknown. Stems may be erect or decumbent and are shaggy-haired. Opposite, petiolate leaves are papery and differ in colour between surfaces; lance-shaped blades have acute tips, serrated margins and rounded bases. Their surfaces are recorded as felted or bristly.

Radiate flower heads are crowded in umbel-like clusters. The bell-shaped involucre (ring of bracts around each head) has one bract form, and the conical receptacle bears narrowly elliptic paleae (small scales on the receptacle). Each head has two or three, or five, yellow ray florets with three-lobed ligules, and 20–30 disc florets. The dry fruits are pubescent; the pappus (crown of scales attached to the fruit) consists of separate scales described as awned or linear-lance-shaped. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
