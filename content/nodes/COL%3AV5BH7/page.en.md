## Accepted name and placement {#s-2a74f9816b}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys mirabilis (Lyu, Wang & Zhao in Lyu, Li, Zeng et al., 2020) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Huaping type-locality records {#s-3405f310f3}
The monograph listed male holotype SYS a002917 from Huaping Nature Reserve, Lingui, Guangxi, at about 1300 m. Citing the original description, it knew the species only from that locality at 1300–1330 m. This narrow set of known elevations is locality information, not a measured physiological range or proof that the frog is absent elsewhere.

## Two males and two females in the examined series {#s-8faa841e8d}
The examined type material comprised two adult males and two adult females. Diagnostic snout–vent lengths were 55.8–61.4 mm in males and 68.5–74.8 mm in females. These small-sample measurements retain their sex and sample count; they do not establish population-wide size limits or a statistical estimate of sexual size differences.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-b195987e9f}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys mirabilis as larger in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
