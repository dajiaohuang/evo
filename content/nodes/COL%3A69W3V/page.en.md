## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-bad74d289d}
COL26.8 records Chitra vandijki as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Trionychidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-4352c3e05b}
The row reports a maximum straight carapace length (SCL) of 1000 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2010.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-9a1660bc3f}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-9a76581e68}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
