## The Sin Ho records {#s-892ab9bc34}
The original account reported the species from Sin Ho District, Lai Chau Province, Vietnam. This is its published geographic sample and does not prove that the species occurs throughout the province or only within that district.

## Small forest streams around 1630 m {#s-404c0240ef}
Specimens came from small streams around 1630 m elevation in evergreen forest near Sin Ho town and road DT128. Leptobrachella ventripunctata was also reported there. Shared locality does not establish shared reproductive requirements, diets or competitive effects, and the article’s short account leaves these topics unresolved.
