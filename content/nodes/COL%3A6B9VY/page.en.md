## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Couepia bernardii? {#s-cad0b86aa6}
Its elliptic leaves are described as pubescent to woolly, meaning they carry fine or dense soft hairs. Flowers grow in branched panicles. The floral receptacle is cylindrical and bears 14–30 separate stamens. The outer fruit wall is described as smooth and hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-446d862b28}
The dataset records it as terrestrial but provides no specific locality.
