## What traits are recorded for Haploclathra grandiflora? {#s-4a11e15e76}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes opposite, elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves with blunt tips and bases. The petals are hairless on both surfaces.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5cdb5ed9e7}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
