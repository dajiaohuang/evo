## Geonoma poiteauana: leaf, inflorescence and flower traits {#s-ff2b625c5d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists solitary, short or subterranean stems, as well as aerial, elongate stems. The leaf blade, or its basal segments, joins the rachis at an acute angle; blades are entire or irregularly pinnate, with veins that stand out from the blade in cross-section.

The inflorescence is interfoliar or infrafoliar, spike-like and unbranched. Peduncular bracts are narrow, elongate and tubular. The prophyll is described either with long, unbranched veins or as short and asymmetric, with an apiculate tip and a margin curved relative to the stipe. Rachillae are not filiform; floral pits are arranged spirally, have a distal lip, and lack indumentum on their inner surface.

Staminate flowers fall after anthesis, and their thecae diverge at anthesis. Unfertilized pistillate flowers also fall. Fruits have rounded tips and are black or purple; the epicarp is described as smooth and glabrous or as having pores.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Source alternatives are retained without resolving them. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
