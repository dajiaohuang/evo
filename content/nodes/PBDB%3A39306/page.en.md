## Recorded parent of Cuculiformes {#s-40ad4ea71b}
Cuculiformes is listed at rank order in the PBDB classification route, with Cuculimorphae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
