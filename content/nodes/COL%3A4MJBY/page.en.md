## Accepted name and placement {#s-95c877b9bf}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis citriogaster (Duellman, 1992) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-487178be8a}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 record for Pristimantis citriogaster codes terrestrial foraging, night activity, direct development, and maximum adult snout–vent length of 51 mm. Its later name match to the accepted record remains a historical-source link.

## Night activity in a broad category {#s-9660982fec}
AmphiBIO records night activity for the historical source concept. This broad category is not a continuous record over the night or across seasons, and it has not been verified as a pattern of the current species concept.

## Compiled maximum adult length {#s-66d38b2ac6}
The AmphiBIO row gives 51 mm as maximum adult body length under its Frost 2011 source concept. For an anuran this is snout–vent length, from snout to cloaca. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of each adult or a value confirmed for the current species boundary.

## A developmental category {#s-9d06cf5393}
The historical AmphiBIO row is coded as direct development. It does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing; equivalence between the Frost 2011 source concept and the current accepted species remains unresolved.
