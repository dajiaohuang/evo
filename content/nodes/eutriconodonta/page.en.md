## “Triconodont” membership changes with the matrix {#s-449a80b864}
A broad morphology analysis recovers sampled Mesozoic mammals as Eutriconodonta, but membership and relationships among taxa historically called “triconodonts” change across character sets. The atlas label therefore marks a tested hypothesis, not a fixed ancestor branch. Its 201.4–66 Ma display is the matrix’s broad Mesozoic sample coverage, not a stable membership list, exact endpoint pair or continuous global range.
