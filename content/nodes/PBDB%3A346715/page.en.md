"The horizon is uncertain because, like many of Robert Broom’s localities, the type locality, ‘Victoria West and District’ (Kitching, 1977), is not well established [...] " (Reisz & Modesto, 2007 p. 735). Both Formations belong to the Beaufort Group, the former being Wuchiapingian in age, the latter being Capitanian in age, according to Catuneanu et al. (2005, J. Afr. Earth Sci. 43). Modesto & Reisz (2007) prefer assignment to the Abrahamskraal Formation.
