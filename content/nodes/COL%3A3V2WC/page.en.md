## A living brachiopod’s molecular toolkit {#s-834862fbeb}
The genome of living Lingula anatina, staged transcriptomes and a shell proteome document a set of molecules involved in phosphate-shell biomineralization. These data describe sampled living material; they do not show that the species has remained morphologically unchanged through time.

## A present-day collection is not an origin date {#s-090bd4e8b9}
Gravid adults collected at Kasari Bay in July and August provide a present-day reproductive observation. Here 0 Ma marks that living collection only; it does not date the lineage’s origin or fossil duration.
