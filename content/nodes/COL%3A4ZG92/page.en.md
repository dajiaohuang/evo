## What does the source record for Stachytarpheta schottiana? {#s-8288c1c294}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes smooth or nearly smooth stems and opposite, stalked elliptic leaves with scalloped or toothed edges. The leaves are hairless. Terminal spikes bear lilac to purple, salver-shaped flowers; the persistent calyx has four teeth. The dry fruit splits into two segments, recorded as black.
