## Hippeastrum espiritense: recorded habitat categories {#s-46548cae22}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records the habitat as “Rupícola,” meaning associated with rock.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.

A second habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
