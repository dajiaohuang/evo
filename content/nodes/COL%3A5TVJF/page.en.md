## Allium campanulatum: outer bulb coats {#s-82b9dc51cc}
The Flora of North America account describes the outer coats as enclosing one or more bulbs. It characterizes the coats as brown to gray, membranous and cellular-reticulate.

This is a selected morphology excerpt from a regional North American taxonomic account.
