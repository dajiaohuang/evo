## ## A species entry with a terrestrial habitat category {#s-69dc0f8515}
COL26.8 places *Arachis villosulicarpa* in *Arachis*, within Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 dossier records the habitat category “terrestrial,” but gives no linked site or survey date. The label describes the entry’s recorded habitat class; it is not a mapped locality or a complete range.
