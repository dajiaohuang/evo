## Selaginella epirrhizos {#s-8842dea116}
## Root-bearing structures and auriculate dorsal leaves

The source lists prostrate or ascending, smooth straw-coloured stems with joints; rhizophores arise axially or dorsally. The small leaves differ by position. Lateral leaves are oblong-lanceolate with rounded or truncate bases; dorsal leaves are ovate-elliptic, with two-eared (biauriculate) bases and acuminate tips. Axillary leaves are ovate-elliptic or ovate-lanceolate, with rounded or truncate bases. Idioblasts are recorded absent in lateral and dorsal leaves. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
