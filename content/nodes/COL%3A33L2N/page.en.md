## Cyrtanthus collinus: leaves and flowers {#s-f233421ad6}
A 2012 regional account describes this as a bulbous geophyte, 15–35 cm in size, that forms clumps. It records usually two leaves; these are narrowly strap-shaped and greyish, and may be dry at flowering. There are 4–11 nodding, narrowly funnel-shaped flowers, bright red to orange. The stamens project slightly beyond the flower throat, and the style has three branches.
