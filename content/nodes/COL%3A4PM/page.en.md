## First reproduction and maximum lifespan differ {#s-b04f48d721}
The life-history source separates gestation, female age at first reproduction and maximum lifespan. First reproduction is not first birth or sexual maturity; maximum lifespan is a longest collected record, not an average. Source conditions may differ, and missing fields need independent evidence.

## How the life-history values were assembled {#s-8bbf17d776}
Species entries use an MSW93/MSW05 name crosswalk, which does not prove equivalence with current concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight; inputs may be averages, individual values or range midpoints, and studies may recur. Adult mass is not interpreted in this route, gaps are not filled from relatives, and wild, captive or locality conditions were not established for every source.
