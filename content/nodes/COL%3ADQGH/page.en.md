## *Andira fraxinifolia* {#s-961dbf54fc}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal; flowers — pink. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as rock-dwelling (Portuguese: Rupícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
