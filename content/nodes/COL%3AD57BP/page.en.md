## Features used to distinguish it from P. curta {#s-f79fe9d250}
Kai and Fricke's 2018 revision distinguishes P. multisquamata from P. curta by several features. P. multisquamata usually has 10 spines in the first dorsal fin (the front fin along the back), 9–11 spines above the eyes, and 40–44 pored scales along the lateral line (the line of scales along the body side). Its paired pectoral fins (side fins) extend past the anus. These are selected diagnostic characters from examined specimens; the description does not establish every anatomical feature or variation across the species' full range.

## Reported western Pacific records {#s-db2f4de443}
The 2018 revision reports P. multisquamata from Vanuatu, Papua New Guinea and the Philippines. Newly examined specimens in the type series (the specimens associated with the original species description) were collected at depths of 299–444 m. These are reported records, not an exhaustive distribution or a full depth range; current occupancy, abundance and population trend were not inferred.
