## White and mauve flowers with a tail-like sepal {#s-4c724b620f}
This record describes a perennial herb up to 2 m tall, with an unbranched stem and a tuft of leaves at its tip. The white sepals include a lower one whose ridge narrows into a slender tail; the petals are pale mauve. The entry lists forest edges and open, rocky but moist places as habitats.
