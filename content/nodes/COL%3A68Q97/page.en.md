## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-fc3159d586}
Dark dorsal areas contrast with orange head, midline and belly; those orange areas become cream in preservative. These are the authors’ comparative observations rather than a standardized colour-chart measurement. Colour and specimen counts vary by trait; similar colours recur among species, so a single hue is not a complete identification rule. Preserved appearance should not be substituted for live appearance.
