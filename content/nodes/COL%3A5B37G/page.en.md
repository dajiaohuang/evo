## Two-winged fruits on a plant without winged stems {#s-38970db9ae}
The record describes a plant with unwinged stems and alternate, stalked leaves ranging from narrow to broad elliptic. The leaves are papery, with a long-tapering base. Convex clusters carry disk-shaped heads with yellow tubular bisexual flowers and no ray florets. The fruits have two wings and a pappus of two awns; the habitat field says terrestrial.
