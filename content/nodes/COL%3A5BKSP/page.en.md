## What is recorded—and still unknown—about Vismia sessilifolia? {#s-4e2597c105}
The orange sap is recorded, while terminal branches may be hairy or hairless. Leaves have nearly stalkless blades, ranging from oval to lance-shaped, with heart-shaped bases; black glands dot their lower surfaces. Flower clusters may be terminal or in leaf axils. The flowers’ sexual form—homostylous or heterostylous—is unknown. Each flower has five sepals without glands and five petals with linear glands. The woolly staminal column and many stamens per bundle are recorded; bundles fall after flowering. Each ovary chamber has many ovules, but the ovary has no glands. The ovoid fruit lacks black dots or lines; its sepals may point upward or bend back.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1fcc56998a}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
