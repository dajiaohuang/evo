## *Polyarrhena reflexa*: prickly leaves and white-backed rays {#s-65bbed692d}
The regional account describes a tangled, sprawling shrub with softly woody stems, about 30–100 cm long. Its closely spaced, triangular leaves bend back and have prickly teeth. Solitary flower heads have yellow centers and white ray florets that are pink on the reverse; the surrounding bracts are hairless. The account records sandstone slopes in fynbos as habitat.
