## Asparagus retrofractus: tangled growth and pale cladodes {#s-6dc2d4ee5d}
A South African regional account describes a scrambling plant reaching about 3 m, or forming tangled shrubs. Dense bundles of cladodes gather at branch tips. The stems zigzag and are usually whitish to pale grey. The threadlike cladodes are about 10–30 mm long and range from pale to bright yellow-green. The flowers are white.
