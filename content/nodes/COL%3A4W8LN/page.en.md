## Securidaca prancei {#s-69355358a7}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Securidaca prancei* as an accepted species of *Securidaca*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 gives only a habitat category, recorded as terrestrial. This regional field provides no collection locality or complete distribution, and the source record has no morphology description.
