## The name and this data account {#s-9f69a65153}
Pterodroma brevipes is accepted in COL26.8 within Procellariidae. This account summarizes AVONET v7 data under its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. The source records 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set; usable sample sizes may differ among traits.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-c9797561bd}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 18.4 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 5.7 mm in width and 6.8 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-ead6235632}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 212.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 27.6 mm. Compiled mean mass is 147.05 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-99d389469f}
The dataset assigns habitat Marine, trophic niche Aquatic predator and lifestyle Generalist. These are dominant categories for cross-species comparisons; they do not imply exclusive use of one habitat or food type.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-ea21ca0155}
The migration field classifies this record as partially migratory. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -29.63° to 7.32°. This excludes a complete account of non-breeding movements and is not a current-position or global occupancy estimate. Geography and morphological measurements come from different compilation steps.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-2a1d17abc6}
Same-name sparse rows cite Dunning (1992), de Magalhaes and Costa (2009), Bennett (1986), and BC Birds (Tacutu, Craig, Budovsky et al., 2013), among others. Each field has its own source label; a label may identify a paper, book or database. Row counts are not numbers of measured individuals, and repeated citations do not represent independent experimental replication.

## Evidence limits {#s-89a74ef46b}
AVONET v7 measurements are means from the five listed specimens, not identification diagnoses or individual ranges; the 147.05 g mass is a separate Dunning species average and is not based on those specimen counts. Habitat, feeding and movement are broad categories, and the latitude endpoints describe historical breeding and resident maps. The 2015 life-history archive reports compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. These summaries are not one individual's measurements, ranges of variation or mean wild lifespans. Historical taxa can share inherited data, so matching names, family and species-level rows do not prove equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently established, and cited publications have not been individually reviewed here. Current range, population, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
