## Siphocampylus lycioides {#s-7b703ce6ae}
## Red-orange flowers appear singly on erect stems

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes erect stems and alternate, papery leaves that are lance-shaped to linear. Their bases are acute, and their margins toothed. Flowers are solitary, with calyx lobes about 5 mm long and an inverted-cone-shaped flower base. The source records the corolla as red to orange and its lobes as red to yellow. Its habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
