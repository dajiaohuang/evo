## Tiny plants with small magenta flowers {#s-8aeaed15ea}
Hesperantha glabrescens is only 30–50 mm tall. Its spikes usually carry one or two small magenta flowers, with a tube about 3.5 mm long and tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) about 8 mm long; its leaves are sparsely hairy.

## Wet stony clay {#s-c1c7b636bb}
The source records wet, stony clay as habitat; it does not provide an estimate of how often the species occurs there.
