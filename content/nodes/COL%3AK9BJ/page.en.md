## What leaf and flower traits are noted? {#s-0bd2d81c4f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes leaves arranged in whorls around the stem. They are narrow, lance-shaped, toothed at the edges, and lack leaf stalks. Flower stalks are absent or up to 2 mm long. The fruiting outer sepals are recorded with flat tips and rounded bases.

## One habitat label: aquatic {#s-5718295804}
One source field categorizes Bacopa verticillata as aquatic; it does not identify a particular water body.

## A second habitat label: terrestrial {#s-53f9ec0678}
A separate field also categorizes it as terrestrial. Both labels are retained as recorded; neither gives a collection locality.
