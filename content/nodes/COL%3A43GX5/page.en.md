## Mimosa xanthocentra {#s-8bbf51b122}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa xanthocentra* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists several stem-hair states and alternatives for prickles, which may be absent or recurved in different positions. Flower clusters are listed as globose or ellipsoid; flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), pappus-like calyx lobes (the outer floral segments), and hairy petal lobes. This is a regional flora description.
