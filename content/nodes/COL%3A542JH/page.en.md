## Violet flowers with a white throat {#s-6c4a6f32d3}
Syringodea concolor grows 30–250 mm tall, with four to eight channelled leaves and pale to deep-violet flowers with a white throat. The Eastern Cape flora reports flowering from January to May; this is a regional flowering record.

## Seasonal drainage in clay scrub {#s-3dfef7ea09}
The archived flora records washes or seasonal drainage lines on clay flats in karroid scrub and open grassland.
