## Early teleost branching shifts with sampling {#s-c4eec4c6ed}
Genome-scale comparisons test early teleost branching patterns, including the sampled Clupeocephala route. The recovered position changes with species and gene sampling, so the topology is not a fossil first appearance or ancestor sequence. A separate 2024 clock analysis estimates crown Clupeocephala at 210.83–186.01 Ma; this model interval is not a complete fossil or species range.
