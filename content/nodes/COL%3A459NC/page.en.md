## How does Myristica fragrans grow and fruit? {#s-e59d478911}
The source describes aromatic trees up to 20 m tall, with smooth, mottled bark and leathery leaves. The species is dioecious: male and female flowers occur on separate trees. Male flowers grow in small branching clusters; female flowers are usually solitary. The fleshy fruit is egg- to pear-shaped and splits into two valves, exposing one large seed wrapped in a red, fringed aril.

## Where is it native and cultivated? {#s-21895a83c0}
The source lists the Moluccas as its native region and says it is widely cultivated in tropical America. This is the distribution statement in the cited account.
