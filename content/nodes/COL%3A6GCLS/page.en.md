## Eremanthus polycephalus {#s-503bdc22da}
## A hemispherical cluster contains one floret per head

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes grey velvety stems. Lance-shaped leaves are sessile or shortly stalked, green to dark green, and membranous to subleathery.

Small heads unite into a hemispherical syncephalium, a compact head-like cluster. Six to ten or more heads form each glomerule. The cylindrical involucres have purple-tipped bracts, and each small head contains one floret. The corolla tip is hairless. Hairless dry fruits carry a pappus of 3–5 rows of bristles, recorded as persistent or deciduous. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
