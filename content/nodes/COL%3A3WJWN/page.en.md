## Lychnophora uniflora {#s-21dffcee27}
## One-floret heads above varied leaf arrangements

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes roots that are fascicled or form a xylopodium. Leaves may form a rosette or grow along the stem. Venation is recorded as hyphodromous, with no conspicuous side-vein network. Tips range from acute to rounded or mucronate.

Each head has one floret. An outer pappus row is present and consists of pale, scale-like structures. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
