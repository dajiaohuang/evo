## Sheltered nocturnal calling {#s-aa8f203553}
The study described hillside forest with varying human disturbance, and nocturnal observations along ravines beside rivers and streams. Frogs called beneath rocks in streams with little water and in cracks. The account had no records of individuals perched on riparian vegetation; that observational absence does not prove that such perching never occurs.
