## Very large flowers with long anthers {#s-082e5d1964}
Moraea gigandra bears one of the genus’s largest flowers, usually dark blue-purple and rarely white or orange. The broad outer tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) reach 45 mm, and unusually long anthers, 13–15 mm, extend beyond the style branches; the inner tepals have a long central lobe.
