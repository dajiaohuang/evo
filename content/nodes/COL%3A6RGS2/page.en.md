## What bristly flower traits are described? {#s-d271b417f3}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records bristles on the stem and on petiolate leaves that have the same color on both faces and one vein. The flower cluster is a dichasium reduced to one flower. The oblong floral cup and triangular sepals bear bristles; the sepals are as long as the cup. Petals are pink. Anthers match one another in color and have multiple pollen sacs, and the ovary has three chambers. These are regional database traits, with no sampling frame given.
