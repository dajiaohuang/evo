## Lonicera japonica Thunb. {#s-940c36d009}
## Fragrant flowers fade from white to yellow

Lonicera japonica is a twining vine or shrub that may trail along the ground or climb over shrubs and small trees. Its opposite leaves are usually entire, though juvenile leaves can be lobed. Paired, fragrant flowers grow from the leaf axils; the white corollas turn yellow and have a strongly two-lipped shape. The fruit is a shiny black, juicy berry crowned by the persistent calyx. The source describes the species as an almost cosmopolitan weed, spread to many countries through cultivation as an ornamental.
