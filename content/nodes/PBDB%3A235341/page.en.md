## Recorded parent of Metriacanthosauridae {#s-30b311b8f7}
Metriacanthosauridae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Allosauroidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
