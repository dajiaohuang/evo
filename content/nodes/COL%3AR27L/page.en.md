## How does Caraipa richardiana differ in the record? {#s-00eb3d6277}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes smooth, two-ranked leaves and records bulliform cells as absent. Flowers grow singly or in racemes on smooth stalks; the ovary and style are also hairless. The capsule is smooth, with the outer fruit layer joined to the inner wall. The source labels it septifragal but also marks it indehiscent; both descriptors are retained as recorded.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-b2b05680e4}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 row classifies the habitat as terrestrial. It gives no locality, collection date or sampling-completeness information.
