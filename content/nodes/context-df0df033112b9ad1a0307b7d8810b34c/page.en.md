## Source classification and concept status {#s-250e364ac3}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3036 records *Hylodes nasus* under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-0ccc51f72e}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-77853490a8}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; night-time activity is unspecified.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-145cb68e2a}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 38 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-a8117e2860}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for this row. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-863e981133}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. These source categories do not by themselves describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
