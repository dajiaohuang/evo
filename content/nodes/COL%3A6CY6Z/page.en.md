## Two habitat labels in the flora record {#s-ad0a6712b3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes this species as *Corticícola* (growing on bark).

The label is a substrate category, not a collection locality; this field supplies no survey date, range, or measure of sampling completeness.

The same source also records *Epífila*, meaning that it grows on leaves.

This second label is also a habitat category rather than a mapped collection site, and it supplies no survey date, range, or sampling-completeness measure.

## Scope of the available record {#s-decda06238}
No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habit labels are not evidence about life cycle or reproduction; those were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

These regional flora records do not establish a complete distribution.
