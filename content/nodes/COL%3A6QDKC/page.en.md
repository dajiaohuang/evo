## Syntopy and one mitochondrial comparison {#s-b68ec597a9}
The study recorded microtympanum with phyllofolia at Bontomaranu and Bontosiri; arathooni was also collected at Bontomaranu. In a 510-bp mitochondrial 16S comparison it reported minimum pairwise distances of 3.85% between microtympanum and arathooni and 6.9% between microtympanum and phyllofolia. These are marker- and sample-specific minima, not genome-wide distances, divergence dates or universal species cutoffs. Shared collection sites do not establish identical microhabitats, competition or absence of hybridization. The study’s phyllofolia nesting observations are not transferred to microtympanum.
