## A Devonian lamprey mouth preserved in Priscomyzon {#s-2e4dc30693}
The 4.2 cm holotype AM5750 of Priscomyzon riniensis, from Famennian strata dated to about 360 Ma, preserves a large oral disc, fourteen circumoral teeth and a branchial basket. It documents a Devonian member of the lamprey total group, but does not establish a crown-group first appearance, parasitic feeding, migration or an ammocoete lifecycle.
