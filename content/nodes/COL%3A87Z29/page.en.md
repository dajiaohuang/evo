## Begonia parvistipulata {#s-7f06fc9049}
## Lobed leaves carry simple hairs on both surfaces

Begonia parvistipulata has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its strongly asymmetric, transversely elliptic basal leaves are lobed and serrated, with simple hairs on both surfaces and the petioles. Male flower segments also bear simple hairs, while female segments are smooth and toothed along the margins. Bracteoles are present. Capsule wings are nearly equal; the seed tip is blunt. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings.
