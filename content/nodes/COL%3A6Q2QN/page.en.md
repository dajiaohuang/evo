## Lessingianthus psilophyllus {#s-1fbf8a6403}
## Hairless narrow leaves have rolled-under edges

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and stems with leaves along their length, unbranched or sparsely branched. Subleathery, linear to lance-shaped leaves have tapered bases and acute tips. Both faces are hairless. Their margins are entire or finely toothed and roll under.

Stalked heads occur singly or in corymb-shaped clusters. Bell-shaped involucres have 5–7 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 20, 25, 30, 35 or 40 florets. Top-shaped fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
