## Leaves, flowers and fruit in the regional description {#s-a7d020205e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records four to eight pairs of leaflets, with hairs on the lower surface and a hairless upper surface. It places the reddish-brown flower clusters in leaf axils or at branch ends. The flowers have white petals, hairy ovaries and wing petals with lamellae, or thin ridges. In the fruit, the outer layer is green, the middle layer granular, and the inner layer woody and fibrous; the source lists one or two seeds. These are regional dataset observations, not a complete account of variation in the species.

## The habitat field {#s-75f84ad3d2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row labels the habitat as terrestrial (“Terrícola”). It supplies no collecting locality, so this field does not define the species’ distribution.
