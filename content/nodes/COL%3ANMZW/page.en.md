## What traits describe Buddleja cestriflora? {#s-80ef7e282a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records solid, upright stems and narrow lance-shaped leaves with hairs on both surfaces; the leaves have no stalk or only a very short one. About ten orange flowers form each stalked cluster. The corolla tube is longer than 1 cm. The capsule is elliptic to egg-shaped, and the seeds are described as spongy.

## What broad habitat is given? {#s-3cd2d167fd}
The dataset labels it terrestrial but does not name a particular locality.
