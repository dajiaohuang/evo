## Paleofavosites links corallite structure and colony growth {#s-c30c686728}
A study of *Paleofavosites* links internal corallite architecture—the structure of individual coral tubes—with external colony growth in one sampled tabulate genus. That observation does not generalize the same growth pattern to every Tabulata lineage. Oliver’s review bounds the displayed fossil record from the Early Ordovician to the end of the Permian, expressed as 486.85–251.9 Ma; these converted endpoints are not specimen-level first and last appearances or proof of uninterrupted global occupancy.
