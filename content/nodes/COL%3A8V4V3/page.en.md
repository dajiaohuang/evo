## The Chengjiang fossil anchor {#s-66422e5489}
The atlas places its vertebrate route within a rounded Cambrian-to-present envelope, while the direct fossil anchor is younger: approximately 518 Ma Chengjiang material assigned to *Myllokunmingia* and *Haikouichthys*. These records support an early vertebrate sample, not a dated crown node or a global first appearance.

Material from the Chengjiang Lagerstätte in Yunnan includes *Myllokunmingia* and *Haikouichthys* specimens used in a sampled morphological analysis. The study places these fossils in a vertebrate context. Their approximate age, about 518 million years, is distinct from the route’s rounded 520 Ma navigation ceiling.

## Two dates with different roles {#s-73226c585e}
The 520–0 Ma display preserves a rounded Cambrian route to living vertebrates. The approximately 518 Ma material supplies the direct fossil anchor. Neither number establishes the age of crown Vertebrata or a global first appearance.
