## Accepted name and placement {#s-b0adcb2c68}
COL26.8 accepts Adenomera hylaedactyla (Cope, 1868) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Leptodactylidae (family) → Adenomera (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
