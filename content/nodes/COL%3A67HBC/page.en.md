## Form described in the flora record {#s-927e85ed23}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright stem, with cataphylls and prophylls recorded as fibrous along the stem. Petioles are smooth and channelled, with sharp margins above and rounded margins below. Leaf blades are entire, flat, and linear, with an acute tip and a base recorded as acute or obtuse; glandular dots are absent. Primary veins are listed either as obtuse on both sides or as obtuse above and acute below. Secondary veins are obscure above, while a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is smaller than half the leaf size, with a cylindrical peduncle. The spathe is flat and elliptic, with an acute angle to the peduncle; a stipe is absent. Berry color is not specified.

The regional dataset row reports Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment as its citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
