## *Apinagia tenuifolia* {#s-2e4d996e5b}
The field records an upright main stem and entire leaves. Flowers are radially symmetric; the spathe (a bract enclosing the flower) is hairless, and triangular tepals reach the ovary base. Stamens form a complete cycle and number 15 or 22; a gynophore is absent. The capsule has three ridges per valve, extending from base to tip. These are database characters.
