## Begonia biguassuensis {#s-40993f8a8a}
## Peltate leaves with a collar of hairs at the petiole tip

Begonia biguassuensis has erect stems, persistent stipules and hairless petioles, with a collar of hairs at each petiole tip. The lance-shaped leaves are peltate—the stalk joins the blade within its margin—and strongly asymmetric, with serrated edges. Both blade surfaces are hairless. Female flower segments have smooth margins and lack bracteoles. The capsule wings are nearly equal; the seed tip is rounded. Habitat is broadly labeled terrestrial.
