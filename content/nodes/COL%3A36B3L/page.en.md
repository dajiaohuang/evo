## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-8278b8bac8}
COL26.8 accepts *Diomedea dabbenena* in Diomedeidae. The 2015 life-history archive has an older record with the same name and family. Seven fields are shown because cited species-level sparse-table rows support them, though their summaries may still inherit older taxonomic data.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-90ba9fa2e6}
The historical summaries are 7704.25 g for adult body mass and 357.9 g at birth or hatching. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; the other value describes an earlier life stage. They are not a longitudinal series for the same individuals or a range of individual masses across the species.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-a00a7c3fa3}
The compilation reports 3670.76 d to female maturity (defined by the source as age at first conception), 1 offspring or egg per litter/clutch, 78.75 d from oviposition to hatching, and 274.35 d to flight capability (some sources describe leaving the nest); d means days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring and egg counts. Each timing value refers to a different event, not one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-80b01c546b}
The archive's maximum_longevity_y field is 60 years, the largest record found under the source method. It is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, a survival limit for every individual or a current record that can be repeatedly attained.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-86a0a47da6}
The same-name rows cite Dunning (1992); Szekely, Lislevand, and Figuerola (2007); de Magalhaes and Costa (2009); Bennett (1986); and other works. The archive preserves raw row numbers, values and field-specific labels with each reference's full explanation. A label may point to a paper, book or another database. Row counts are not measured individuals, and repeated citations are not independent experimental replications.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-370067b4e0}
This page uses historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family, together with same-name sparse rows, supports the association but does not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove every inherited component. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
