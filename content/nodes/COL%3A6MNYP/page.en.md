## Survey records {#s-a8fbd1b7a6}

They combined triangulation and acoustic spatial capture–recapture to estimate group density, then used a habitat-suitability model to extrapolate a local population estimate of 1,092 gibbons. That number is a model-based estimate for the Dieng landscape and study period, not a current count for Java or the species as a whole.
