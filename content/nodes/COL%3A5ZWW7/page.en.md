## Copaifera luetzelburgii {#s-4059478168}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Copaifera luetzelburgii* records 3–5 leaflet pairs with margins rolled back near the base, legumes 2–3.5 cm long and a white seed covering. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
