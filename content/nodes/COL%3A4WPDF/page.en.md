## Senna rugosa {#s-15ecddae35}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna rugosa* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists two leaflet pairs and extra-floral glands between them. Its flowers have seven fertile stamens; the hanging, cylindrical pods are indehiscent (not splitting open at maturity) or open late, with seeds arranged in two rows. The plant is also recorded as terrestrial.
