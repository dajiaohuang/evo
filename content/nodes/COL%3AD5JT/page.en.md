## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e0c7dc09e3}
COL26.8 records Amphisbaena maranhensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Amphisbaenidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-32107c1d8a}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 125 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
