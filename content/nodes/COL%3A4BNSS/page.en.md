## Felted leaf undersides and darkening fruit {#s-184937eac1}
The account describes a small evergreen tree reaching 9 m. Leaves cluster at branch tips; they are obovate, dark green and hairy above, with matted grey felt-like hairs below. Their lateral veins are strongly ridged and divide before reaching the margin, which is entire or slightly scalloped. Flowers are small and whitish. Fruits measure 5–9 mm across on stalks 6–9 mm long and turn blackish. The stated setting is rocky slopes; these details belong to the cited regional account, not a complete range survey.
