## Cream and greenish flowers with reddish streaks {#s-60da8ca88c}
Gladiolus grandiflorus is 25–50 cm tall and has funnel-shaped cream to greenish flowers. It is separated from the similar G. rudis by its longer tube, usually 27–35 mm and sometimes 50 mm, and reddish median streaks on the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) rather than yellow spade-shaped marks.
