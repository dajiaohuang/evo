## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Actinodura waldeni Godwin-Austen, 1874 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Actinodura waldeni, n. sp. (Plate XII) {#study-account}
In his 1874 account, H. H. Godwin-Austen described one Actinodura waldeni taken near Japvo peak at about 9,000 feet. He noted the crest and streaked rufous-brown plumage, gave measurements and compared it with A. egertoni. This describes one specimen, not population-wide variation or ordinary behavior.
