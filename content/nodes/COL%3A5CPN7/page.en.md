## What is the small ligule at its leaf base? {#s-fc657a0000}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with flat, ridged blades and a conspicuous ligule, a small projection where the blade meets the sheath. The flower stalk is listed in two length classes, 20–50 cm and 50–70 cm, and has one or two ridges. Its spike may be ovoid, globose, or nearly globose, with 10–25 flowers.
