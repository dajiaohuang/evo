## A grooved leaf midrib and lance-shaped leaves {#s-e09b30d6bd}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and simple hairs on the stem. Leaves are lance-shaped, with a fully grooved upper midrib and a hairy underside. Bracts or cataphylls (small scale-like leaves) beneath leaf nodes are absent. These regional field descriptors are not quantified.

## Terminal clusters and a three-chambered ovary {#s-12bd564ca6}
Small flowering branches are rounded, with bracteoles beneath the flowers that fall away. The field lists one or two terminal inflorescences per axillary shoot and describes their arrangement as pyramidal and opposite. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal. The floral disc is hairless; the flower cup is inverted-cone-shaped and densely woolly. Sepals are separate, the ovary has three chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. The field leaves fruit shape and the fruit’s calyx lobes unknown.
