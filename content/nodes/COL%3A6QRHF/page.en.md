## A historical foraging record {#s-631643d7d3}
COL26.8 accepts *Madanga ruficollis* in Zosteropidae. EltonTraits 1.0 has an older record with the same name and family; this page interprets one source-linked group of foraging-position fields. That match does not establish equivalence between the historical and current species concepts or resolve later splits and synonym disputes.

## Feeding position is not a time budget {#s-c1752d912e}
The archive flags these values as species-level foraging strata: understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m, 40%; trees or high bushes from 2 m up to below the canopy, 40%; and the canopy or just above it, 20%. They are relative weights for feeding positions, not nest sites, total habitat use or a full-day time budget. The source defines surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories separately; they do not give elevation ranges, flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-65b326a8cd}
Only one field group is interpreted here; the page does not fill in diet or activity. The foraging weights are standardized from historical literature descriptions, not direct measurements of observation time or all habitat use. The underlying works have not been checked individually, and this record does not describe individual, seasonal or local variation. A same-name, same-family match to EltonTraits does not prove that its historical species concept equals COL26.8.
