## Accepted name and placement {#s-98a4abf4d8}
COL26.8 lists Pelophryne signata (Boulenger, 1895) as a species in Anura, Bufonidae and Pelophryne. The name and placement follow the release dated 20 August 2026; another checklist or fossil database may use a different species concept.
