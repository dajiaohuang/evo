## Cattleya lawrenceana {#s-9e4f2d6482}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 account, Cattleya lawrenceana has clavate pseudobulbs that are flattened from the sides and a single elliptic leaf. Its pink flowers carry a lip with pink and pale areas; the lateral lobes are long and enclose the column. The dataset lists it as epiphytic.
