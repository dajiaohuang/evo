## Phlegmariurus hippurideus {#s-1676d75d53}
## Leaf spacing and spore-bearing leaves like the foliage

The record gives a green-to-brown basal stem that can be upright or prostrate, with a decumbent base. Its small leaves are described as uniform or spaced apart, with blades ranging from linear to needle-shaped or awl-like and recurved near the upper part. The leaf bases are short and green; the entry lists several positions and orientations for how they attach. Spore-bearing leaves are recorded as like the vegetative leaves. The kidney-shaped sporangia have two equal valves, and the spores are pitted. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
