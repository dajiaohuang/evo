## Chresta pycnocephala {#s-e9440a3062}
## Globose heads carry lilac florets with white marks

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes alternate stem leaves on short or absent stalks. Blades are obovate to elliptic, with entire or wavy edges, dense hairs and pinnate veins.

Globose syncephalia (compact head-like clusters) form a corymb and have determinate growth. Their bracts are green with purple margins. The lilac florets lack coloured rings but have white marks at the corolla-tube tip and at the bases of the lobes. Fruits within each head share one form, and the pappus persists. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
