## Leucaena multicapitula {#s-57d73f8efc}

The Flora of Panama describes an unarmed tree or shrub with 3–4 pairs of pinnae and usually four pairs of leaflets per pinna. The dense, nearly spherical flower heads are less than 1 cm across; its flat, hairless pods are about 12 cm long and nearly 3 cm wide. The recorded locality is Panama.
