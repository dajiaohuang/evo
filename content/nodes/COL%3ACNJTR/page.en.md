## Invertebrate niche and carnivorous trophic level {#s-e5289b2e66}
Trophic.Niche=Invertivore is defined as at least 60% of food resources from invertebrates in terrestrial systems; Trophic.Level=Carnivore means at least 70% from live invertebrate or vertebrate animals. These thresholds define categories rather than measured proportions for this species. Underlying diet papers were not read; insect species, other prey, seasonal diet and capture actions remain unsupported. Carnivore does not mean exclusive vertebrate feeding.
