## Pristimera nervosa {#s-73409364f0}
## A climber with conspicuous bracts and tiny flowers

Pristimera nervosa is described as a liana or small tree. Its papery leaves are elliptic to broadly ovate, 7–20 cm long, with wavy or crenate margins. Inflorescences are 2.5–7 cm long, with contracted upper nodes that look jointed and conspicuous bracts. The flowers are small, 1.7–3.5 mm across. Fruit is explicitly unknown in this account. The listed range includes Panama, Suriname, Venezuela, Peru, Bolivia and Acre in Brazil.
