## What changes from the leaf base to the flower stalk? {#s-c62adff813}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records leaves in two ranks, with a markedly widened sheath base that can form ear-like lobes. The blade is listed as flat or thread-like and rough. The flower stalk has two recorded length classes, 5–20 cm and 20–50 cm; the spike is listed as ovoid, ellipsoid, or spindle-shaped, with up to ten flowers. These are alternatives in the source field, not a single resolved form.
