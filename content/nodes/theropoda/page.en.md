## The review covers non-avian theropods {#s-7d81d73d91}
The Theropoda route uses Hendrickx and colleagues’ review for its classification and coverage of non-avian branches; living birds extend the application route separately. The 233.2–66 Ma interval describes the review’s sampled non-avian theropod coverage, not the complete clade, living birds, exact global endpoints or a literal ancestor sequence.
