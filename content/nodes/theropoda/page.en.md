## What the non-avian theropod review covers {#s-7d81d73d91}
Theropoda’s fossil account uses Hendrickx and colleagues’ review for non-avian branches and their classification. The review compares major classification schemes and identifies relationships that remain unresolved, so it does not provide a settled ancestor sequence. Its 233.2–66 Ma window describes the non-avian record it covers; living birds extend the application route independently. Read this as the scope of one review, not the complete clade history.
