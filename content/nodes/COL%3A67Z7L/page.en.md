## Averrhoidium gardnerianum {#s-936681d4d2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records pubescent stems, a hairy leaf rachis and leaflets with serrate margins; the habitat field records terrestrial growth.

## Recorded morphology {#s-81122e2ef6}
The description records pubescence on the stem and a hairy leaf rachis. Leaves have five to ten leaflets, whose margins are serrate. The floral disc is glabrous. The ecology field separately records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or range.
