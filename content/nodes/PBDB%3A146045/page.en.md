## What the material shows {#s-efebbef508}
Across 189 specimens, transverse tooth rows, a broad foot and mantle-groove structures recur. Whether the rows are homologous to a molluscan radula remains an interpretation.

## Feeding {#s-e74e2ae009}
A benthic feeding role is inferred from the tooth rows and body plan; the fossils do not preserve a meal.

## Setting {#s-2d3e6b7162}
The fossils come from the marine Burgess Shale seafloor.

## Movement {#s-d827457db4}
Crawling is inferred from the broad foot, not directly observed.

## Size {#s-ae770ee519}
The soft-bodied animal is centimetre-scale; no more precise size estimate is given here.

## Ecological role {#s-bf7bc82b38}
A mobile benthic feeder is an anatomical inference.
