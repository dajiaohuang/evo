## An arboreal category {#s-e21d925ed0}

## Limits of the supported field {#s-db43966910}
This page interprets one supported stratum field; it does not fill the diet or activity fields. EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete monograph, and its concepts do not automatically match COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized literature weights, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may rely on a congener. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata, and split records requiring review do not establish definite claims. Underlying sources have not been reread. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation need other evidence. Diet and activity code D2 are withheld because of interpolation, certainty, or source-link limits; archived values do not support a definite ecology claim.
