## What leaf, flower and fruit structures are listed? {#s-5cac430b0b}
The leaves are lobed or deeply cut into narrow divisions. The flowers or inflorescences are cymose, with three threadlike bracteoles. The stigmas are capitate and match the number of carpels. In each fruit segment (mericarp), the base has net-like ridges and remains closed, while the smooth tip opens.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-6cb9be7353}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
