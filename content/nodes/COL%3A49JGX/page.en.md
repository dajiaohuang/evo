## *Oncosiphon schlechteri*: divided leaf tips and cobwebby stems {#s-5efd44d39c}
The regional account compares *Oncosiphon schlechteri* with *O. sabulosus*. Its leaves are spoon-shaped to linear and divided into three to five lobes near the tip, though some are undivided. The young stems and flower-head stalks are conspicuously covered with cobweb-like hairs; in the comparison species they are hairless. The bracts enclosing each head extend down along the stalk, and their base is wedge-shaped. The account records coastal dunes as habitat.
