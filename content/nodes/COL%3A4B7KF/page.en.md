## Limits of the life-history and ecological entries {#s-8e7b949a84}
The life-history values come from same-name records in the 2015 archive. Except for maximum longevity, summaries are medians or averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity is the largest record found. They are not measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespan or a current population survey. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching name and family and the same-name rows support the association, but do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed.

Only one EltonTraits field group is interpreted here, for ground stratum. Diet, activity and additional stratum evidence are not supplied by that category. EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family guide, not a complete monograph, and its concepts are not automatically equivalent to COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are standardized literature weights, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time; avian A/B codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity may rely on a congener. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing review do not establish definite claims. Underlying sources have not been reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation need other evidence. Diet and activity fields with source code D1 are withheld because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; their archived values do not support definite ecology conclusions.
