## Accepted name and placement {#s-bf446f83f9}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus barlagnei Lynch, 1965 at species rank in Eleutherodactylus, within Eleutherodactylinae, order Eleutherodactylidae. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026; it is distinct from the historical source concepts below.

## Reading route {#s-0b042ea46c}
The catalogue placement is separate from AmphiBIO's Frost 2011 source classification. Read the entries below as historical compilation values.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-5d1d3a5816}
The AmphiBIO row codes fossorial and aquatic foraging for its historical concept. These broad codes can overlap and do not quantify how often either setting is used. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against the correction notice.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-abbc372347}
The compilation reports a maximum adult length of 33 mm; in frogs, length means snout–vent length. This is a compiled maximum rather than a measurement range.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-d23f01a9e7}
AmphiBIO records at most one reproductive event per year. The literature-derived count is not specified by sex, population, date or wild/captive conditions; offspring-size fields may refer to eggs.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-37f0a39601}
The historical row marks direct development. This code alone does not establish parental care, breeding place or timing.
