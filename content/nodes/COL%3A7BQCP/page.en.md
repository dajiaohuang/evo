## A calling male in a water-filled tree hole {#s-481cdf4169}
The description reported the type locality in the Ere–Campuya headwaters of Peru’s Putumayo basin at 145 m. The holotype called from a narrow water-holding tree hole 1.5 m above ground, with most of its body submerged. Playback elicited faster calling and movement to the entrance. These observations document one male’s calling site and response, without proving egg deposition there or a general territorial mechanism.

## Calling detections with other stages unobserved {#s-56463b2069}
The authors detected at least six individuals by advertisement calls during 18 survey hours near the holotype locality; none of those additional callers was collected. Females, amplectant pairs, clutches and tadpoles were unobserved. These records do not provide a standardized population density or a verified larval habitat.
