## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-4ea943a618}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a whitish lower leaf surface that becomes smoother with age, and winged leaf stalks. Male flowers are longer than 2.5 mm; the female reproductive structure is hairless. The fruit width is given as 1.5–2.5, with no unit specified in this field, and the pericarp (fruit wall) is 1–2 mm thick.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-c6cf107489}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a locality.
