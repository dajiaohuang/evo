## The source concept {#s-ad8c3205f7}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0120 records Arthroleptis affinis in genus Arthroleptis, family Arthroleptina and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-99fe4f1c71}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The fields Fos=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this does not show that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=45 mm {#s-540bdae616}
Body_size_mm=45 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Reproductive_output_y: maximum 1 event/year {#s-74e9b1e0c5}
The row lists Reproductive_output_y=1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Dir=1: direct development recorded {#s-632a783965}
Dir=1 records direct development. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
