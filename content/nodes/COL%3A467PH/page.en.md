## How does Nemesia arenifera look? {#s-65257a4690}
This description presents Nemesia arenifera as an upright annual 4–30 cm tall, with narrow leaves and glandular hairs over most of the plant. Its small flowers are pale to bright yellow, with darker markings on the upper lip. A slender, backward-pointing spur extends from the corolla tube and lies roughly parallel to the flower stalk. The account records flowering and fruiting in August and September.

## Which traits separate it from related species? {#s-fe4d1ecebb}
The source says it does not resemble any known southern African Nemesia species. It highlights its upright habit, small yellow flowers, spur position and sticky hairs. It compares the flower orientation with N. rupicola, but says the two differ in growth form, range, leaves and other floral traits.

## What landscape and climate does the account describe? {#s-f11d362c61}
The account places it in deep, loose windblown sand, from white through yellow to red. It reports the species only from the Namaqualand Sandveld and Sand Fynbos bioregions within the Succulent Karoo and Fynbos biomes. Winter rain falls mainly from May to August, averaging 50–114 mm annually; winter fog adds moisture, while frost is very rare. These details describe the cited source’s known setting.
