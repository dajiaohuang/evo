## Sample behind the measurements {#s-a2b7ae1c5d}
COL26.8 accepts Zosterops flavifrons in Zosteropidae. Its AVONET v7 record uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and includes 6 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set. Sample size may differ among traits.

## Beak dimensions at standardized landmarks {#s-15efb27b35}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 9.5 mm; width and depth at that edge average 3 mm and 3.6 mm. These separate dimensions describe the measured sample, not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-5dff956ba1}
Unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary averages 61.9 mm, and tarsus length averages 20.3 mm. The compiled mean mass is 10.7 g, classed as Dunning. It comes from separate species-average information, not from weighing each bird in the morphological sample.

## Broad habitat and feeding labels {#s-4e8220ec83}
The AVONET categories are Forest for habitat, Omnivore for trophic niche and Insessorial for primary lifestyle, meaning chiefly perching. They allow broad comparisons but do not mean the bird uses only forest or any single food source.

## Migration and historical map {#s-6040f5fb62}
The migration category is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints −20.25° to −13.07°. These endpoints do not describe all non-breeding movements or show current occupancy across the mapped range. Geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Scope of the historical account {#s-272cb9ae2a}
The AVONET measurements are sample averages, not diagnostic values or ranges of individual variation; mass has a separate source. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, and the map concerns historical breeding and resident ranges. EltonTraits converts literature descriptions into relative weights rather than direct measurements of intake, energy or observation time. The foraging-stratum group is not supported at species level in this record. Historical name links associate the sources with COL26.8 but do not establish equivalent species concepts, current range, population or conservation status.
