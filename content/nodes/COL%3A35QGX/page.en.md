## Dicoma fruticosa {#s-541c105444}
## Pink heads top a sticky, twiggy shrublet

Manning and Goldblatt's Greater Cape flora account describes a twiggy shrublet up to 50 cm tall, sticky on its young parts. Its leathery leaves are oblanceolate to spoon-shaped and have a small pointed tip.

Pinkish flower heads grow singly at branch tips and have only a few ray florets. About five disc florets sit within ovate, non-spiny bracts whose thin, membranous edges are torn. The cited habitat field records shale flats.
