## AmphiBIO species record {#s-ecae435c13}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Oedipina collaris? Row Anf6253 places it in genus Oedipina, family Plethodontidae and order Caudata. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging categories in this record {#s-2033b285ce}
The AmphiBIO row lists these broad foraging categories: fossorial (underground), terrestrial (land), and aquatic (water). They do not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat categories carried over from earlier compilations, but does not identify whether this row was affected. These remain the AmphiBIO labels; no replacement category is inferred.

## Activity periods in the compilation {#s-833d92206b}
The AmphiBIO row classifies activity as night. These are broad time-of-day categories, not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-e09530f33d}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 255 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.

## Developmental category in the record {#s-be04cb0b6c}
The AmphiBIO record assigns this source concept the developmental category “direct development”. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
