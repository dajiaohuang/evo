## Rhipsalis grandiflora {#s-57212b12ec}
## White or pink fruit follows lateral flowers

The cylindrical stem segments have smooth tips and mature areoles without spines. Flowers arise laterally from hairless flower-bearing areoles; the corolla is wheel-shaped and its filaments are white. The exposed pericarpel—the flower's ovary-bearing base—is shorter than the white perianth segments. The fruit is globose and recorded as white or pink. The habitat is labeled epiphytic, but no host or locality is named.
