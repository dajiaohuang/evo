## Stem, leaf, and spathe dimensions {#s-b9446dbc2c}
The Brazilian Flora description records an erect aerial stem, usually under 1.0 m, with few or no branches. Leaf stalks (petioles) reach 23 cm; leaf blades are listed at 20–40 cm long and more than 10 but less than 20 cm wide, with a length-to-width ratio of 2–3.5. The entry also records four or more inflorescences per sympodium (branching shoot unit), peduncles 7.5–25 cm long, and mature spathes (bracts around the flower clusters) generally under 10 cm. These are regional descriptors, not population-wide estimates.

## Soil-associated in the habitat field {#s-b779bc98eb}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 classifies this species as “terrícola” (soil-associated). The row gives no locality or survey date and does not establish its complete range.
