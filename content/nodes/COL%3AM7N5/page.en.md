## Blumenbachia amana {#s-bae6ca2fee}
## Axillary flowers above low, spreading stems

Blumenbachia amana has ascending or creeping stems 30–50 cm long. Its broad ovate leaves are either three-lobed or divided into three leaflets. Axillary flowers sit on 2–4 cm stalks; the white or greenish petals are 8–9 mm long. Nectar scales may be red, yellow or green. The ovoid capsule is 10–12 mm long and 6–8 mm wide.
