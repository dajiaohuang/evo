## Alternanthera adscendens {#s-9deeb39a83}
## Prostrate stems and white to cream floral segments

Brazilian Flora 2020 records secondary roots and prostrate stems. The leaves have petioles and elliptic blades with acute tips that bear a small point (mucro); leaf hairs are recorded as absent or present.

The inflorescence is sessile, and its paracladial branches are recorded as unbranched or primary. Flowers have short stalks, five three-veined, papery perianth segments (tepals) and a white-to-cream perianth. The androecium has five stamens and long pseudostaminodes; the stigma is hairy and the style is long.

The fruit wall is smooth, and the seeds are recorded without lateral projections. The dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial.
