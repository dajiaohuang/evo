## Bonatea steudneri {#s-e448cca0d1}
The Bonatea steudneri account notes substantial variation in flower-part and spur lengths; the spur spans 75–230 mm in the treatment. In northern KwaZulu-Natal, it may be confused with B. lamprophylla, but its lip’s lateral lobes are shorter in the cited comparison (35.5–60 mm versus 106–135 mm). The account records grassland, savannah, thicket and forest margins.
