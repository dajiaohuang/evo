## Dieffenbachia cannifolia: measurements in a regional flora {#s-bd19b4ee53}
The 2020 Brazilian Flora entry records basal stem branches as absent or few and the aerial stems as erect. It lists two stature categories—generally under 1.0 m and generally over 1.0 m—without reconciling them. Petioles reach 23 cm, are about one quarter the leaf-blade length, and have sheaths nearly as long as the petioles; their texture is smooth, while color is uncharacterized.

Leaf blades are oblanceolate or obovate. Their recorded length categories are 20–40 cm or less than 60 cm; widths are listed as more than 10 cm or less than 20 cm, and the length-to-width ratio is 3–3.5. The upper-surface color is uncharacterized, and the base is described as gradually narrowing to narrowly acute or acute. Secondary veins meet at an angle under 80°.

Several inflorescences are recorded per sympodium. Peduncles are generally under 8 cm; spathes generally exceed 10 cm, with a spathe-to-peduncle length ratio over 1.0. Mature spathe color is uncharacterized.

These are measurements and descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; the field does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Its alternative size categories are retained as reported.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
