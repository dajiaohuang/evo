## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-2817a6c1eb}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Ditrichum paulense Geh. & Hampe as an accepted species name.

Its Brazilian Flora entry is regional data with one source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

The category has no associated collection locality or survey date and does not define a complete range or sampling coverage.

This habitat field is the only biological content included here from the regional source; it is not a full species account.
