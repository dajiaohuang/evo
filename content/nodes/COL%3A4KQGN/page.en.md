## Hairy stems and a short-beaked fruit {#s-0cedfae7b8}
The description records hairy stems and alternate leaves that cluster loosely near the base. The leaf blades are elliptic to oblanceolate, with a main vein and an additional vein pair arising above the base. Its dry fruit, called a cypsela in the daisy family, has a short beak. The habitat field says terrestrial.
