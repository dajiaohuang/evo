## Rolled leaf edges and a hairy flower cup {#s-e6662c8c00}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes papery elliptic or narrowly elliptic leaves. Their bases are acute, their tips blunt or tapering, and the margins roll under; a cartilage-like edge is absent. The upper midrib is grooved, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a central stalk bearing smaller flower stalks. Bracts and bracteoles fall away, while the smooth flower cup bears hairs; the calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The source leaves fruit shape and surface, fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo status unknown. A separate habitat field labels the setting “terrícola,” or terrestrial.
