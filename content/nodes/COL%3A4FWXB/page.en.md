## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-d9e1483909}
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Phasmahyla cochranae as follows: One harmonic note containing three or four non-fused pulses, sometimes repeated in groups of four to eight. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population.
