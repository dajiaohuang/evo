## Centrosema plumieri {#s-3ca76054a1}
A historical Flora of Panama account describes *Centrosema plumieri* as a robust twining vine with three leaflets and white-outside, purple-inside flowers; flat legumes are 10–14 cm long. It reports the species throughout tropical America and introduced in the Old World, with Panamanian records mainly in moist forests. This is a historical regional summary, not a current range inventory.
