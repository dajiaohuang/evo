## Recorded parent of Apatosaurinae {#s-6a42cb19ea}
Apatosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Diplodocidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
