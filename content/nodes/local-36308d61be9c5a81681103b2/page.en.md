## The source tadpole concept {#s-b422f22f1c}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Allobates magnussoni, in the genus Allobates and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying work is also Allobates magnussoni. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-bdcb9026c1}
The compilation has eight records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Their supplied stage labels are 27, 38, 25 and 36. These are labels attached to source records, not a single tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; eight records also do not mean eight unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
