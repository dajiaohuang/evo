## Leaf tips and secondary veins {#s-ec38b76099}
The Brazilian Flora field lists elliptic blades, prominent secondary veins, and several recorded forms for the blade tip (tapering, small-pointed, or acute) and base (acute, blunt, or rounded). It also describes the berry apex as green and its base as white. These are regional taxonomic descriptors, not a measure of population variation.

## Soil as the recorded habitat category {#s-55234f112b}
The habitat row in Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 is “terrícola,” meaning soil-associated. It contains no collection site or date and cannot map the species’ distribution.
