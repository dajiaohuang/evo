## What leaf, flower, and fruit traits are noted for Hymenopus glabriflorus? {#s-1fa65d6fa0}
The leaves are oblong to oblong-elliptic, with hairless undersides and no glands on the leaf stalks. Flowers grow in panicles on individual stalks, with bell-shaped floral receptacles; the record describes the stamens as equal. The ellipsoid fruit has a surface marked by small lenticels, or pores.
