## Red latex and a grooved fruit {#s-076114f298}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with bark that is thickened or slightly thickened, and red latex. Leaves are arranged alternately, one at a time on alternating sides of the branch, with the record listing them as either spaced or crowded; their bases are flat or slightly rolled backward. The terminal flower clusters are listed as rigid or non-rigid. The sepals, the outer leaf-like parts of a flower, are similar in length; petal lobes, the separated segments at the edge of the fused petals, are longer than the fused-petal tube, and the ovary, the part that later develops into fruit, is hairless. Fruits are grooved, ribbed, and stalked; lenticels, small openings in the surface, are inconspicuous. The source leaves the alternatives unresolved and gives no account of variation.
