## Roella arenaria {#s-3596d205b6}
## Spreading leaves and hooked sepals distinguish one comparison

Manning and Goldblatt’s 2012 Cape flora account describes the leaves as spreading and the sepals as strongly bent back or hooked and always hairy, in comparison with R. prostrata. Adamson’s 1951 revision gives a separate set of distinctions: larger leaves, bracts that spread outward or curve backward, a hairy calyx, and more deeply divided corolla lobes. The habitat record is sandy flats.
