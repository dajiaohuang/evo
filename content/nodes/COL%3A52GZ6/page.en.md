## Stevia organensis {#s-6434e62bf5}
## Sessile leaves and an awned pappus

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes opposite leaves without petioles. Dense, stalked heads form corymb-like clusters in the leaf axils. The dry fruits are smooth, and their pappus consists of scale-like pieces with awn-like tips. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial. These regional fields give no locality or complete range.
