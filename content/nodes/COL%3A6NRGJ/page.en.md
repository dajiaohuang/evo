## Lachenalia pusilla {#s-2924f4adb3}
Lachenalia pusilla is a very small bulbous geophyte with a rosette of three to seven leaves and a dense, ground-level raceme of strongly scented white flowers. The regional account distinguishes it from L. barkeriana by its prostrate lanceolate leaves and longer inner tepals. Sandy flats and slopes are the recorded habitat.
