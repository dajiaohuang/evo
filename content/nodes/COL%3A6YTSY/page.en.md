## *Solandra paraensis* {#s-0f9512ed89}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records hairless leaves and calyx. The calyx is shorter than the tubular part of the corolla; the basal corolla tube is longer than its expanded distal portion. Corolla length is recorded as 29–38 cm, with stamens inserted 15–20 cm from its base. These are source-field values.
