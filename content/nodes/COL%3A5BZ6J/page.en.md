## *Willdenowia bolusii*: a grooved seed appendage {#s-45246b8ddb}
This tufted perennial can reach 1.5 m. Its branched stems are ridged; greenish spathes with red margins partly cover the flowers, and the styles are white. The fruit bears a large, fluted elaiosome, an attached seed appendage.

It is recorded on lower sandstone slopes.
