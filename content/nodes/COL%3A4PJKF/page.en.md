## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-6c69e1b7a7}
Psiloscops flammeolus is accepted in pinned COL26.8 within Strigidae. This page compares the same-name historical life-history entry using 6 focal fields, each supported by cited species-level sparse-table rows with matching name and family. Reported summaries can still include historical taxonomic inheritance; a matching row name does not remove that influence.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-7692771ac7}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 55.75 g; mass at birth or hatching 7.5 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-aed6c1f4bc}
Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 365 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 3 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 22 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-5bcf969275}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 9 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-1a4cf2b7ee}
Same-name sparse rows cite Dunning, 1992; Szekely, Lislevand, and Figuerola, 2007; de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009; Bennett, 1986, among others. Field-specific source rows, values and labels are recorded in the evidence; a label may refer to a paper, book or other database. Row counts are not numbers of measured individuals, and repeated citations do not constitute independent replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-07763de884}
The 2015 life-history archive reports compiled medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity is the largest record found. These summaries are not direct measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies can share or inherit data; matching name and family, together with same-name sparse-table rows, supports the association but does not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and fields not represented here require other sources.
