## Heliophila arenosa {#s-4950235dba}
## Smaller flowers and narrow petals distinguish it from H. arenaria

The Cape flora account compares this species with H. arenaria: its flowers are smaller and the petals are very narrow, about 5–8 mm long and 2–3 mm wide. A separate diagnostic account makes the same comparison. The habitat record is sandstone slopes.
