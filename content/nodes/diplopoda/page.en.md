## Kampecaris keeps millipede age uncertainty visible {#s-3dfa4a5edd}
Reassessed morphology and imaging recover a sampled phylogeny for major millipede lineages, but topology does not establish the fossil range of Diplopoda. A recent age synthesis places *Kampecaris obanensis* at 425.5 ± 4.5 Ma; the uncertainty remains explicit rather than being rounded into a hard 430 Ma boundary. Less secure pre-Pridoli claims were rejected in an earlier review, and living millipedes extend the route to the present.
