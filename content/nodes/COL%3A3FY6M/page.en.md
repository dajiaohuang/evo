## Leaves, flower, and fruit {#s-ef26139268}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte and gives a 15–25 cm measurement without specifying what it measures. The large flower is broadly cup-shaped, white to pink, and has 35–60 anthers arranged in six clusters. The fleshy berry is yellow with red spots.
