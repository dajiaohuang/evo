## Leaf veins, bracts, and flower characters {#s-9c733a6f52}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description notes parallel tertiary veins (third-order veins). It records bracts that are free at the base and nearly equal in size. The calyx is tubular, with unequal lobes. The corolla is salver-shaped; in the bud, its lobes are folded lengthwise and meet edge-to-edge without overlapping. The anthers are inserted near the corolla throat. These descriptors come from one regional field.
