## Garuleum tanacetifolium {#s-9ae623155c}
## Deeply divided leaves and a short flower stalk

This shrub has a simple, leafless lower stem that branches above. Alternate leaves crowd the branch tips; they are glandular-rough, recurved-spreading and divided twice into sharply cut lobes. Solitary radiate heads have short stalks and a bell-shaped involucre. Female ray florets range from dark purple and mauve to pale lilac or white; the functionally male disc florets are yellow. Mature cypselae were not seen in the source. It is recorded along forest edges and mountain ridges, especially on sandstone, including south-facing slopes above 1,600 m and local montane fynbos.
