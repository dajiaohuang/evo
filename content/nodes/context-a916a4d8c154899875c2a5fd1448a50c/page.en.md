## Source classification and concept status {#s-747ae5d183}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3096 records *Heterixalus andrakata* under the source classification Heterixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-1efc5ef89e}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage.
