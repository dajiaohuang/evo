## A curated map, not a complete tree {#s-871412cfac}
This compact Perissodactyla hypothesis groups examples under horse-line Hippomorpha, chalicothere-line Ancylopoda and tapir-and-rhino Ceratomorpha, with Brontotheriidae also shown within the order. The nested names provide a working map of selected groups rather than an exhaustive account of every odd-toed ungulate.

## Branch lengths do not measure time {#s-bf912aa4ee}
The evidence explicitly describes this as a non-exhaustive hypothesis. Its branches organize proposed relationships; their drawn lengths do not encode elapsed time or divergence dates.
