## Accepted name and placement {#s-6d7f44daa4}
COL26.8 accepts Occidozyga laevis (Günther, 1858) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Occidozyga (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## How to read this profile {#s-866d3d254b}
COL26.8 supplies the accepted name and placement below. The other sections summarize a historical trait compilation, one image-based tadpole record and a 2016 Mindanao account. These sources differ in scope and have not all been reconciled to the same species concept.

## Foraging categories in AmphiBIO {#s-29a77ff430}
In AmphiBIO's Frost 2011 classification, Occidozyga laevis is marked as both fossorial (associated with digging or underground activity) and aquatic. These broad categories do not say when either behavior occurs. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries, but the available row-level comparison does not establish whether this entry changed. Treat the labels as historical; their equivalence to the COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Freshwater encounter habitats from lowlands to mountain streams {#s-25839a6905}
In a 2016 account from Mindanao, aquatic frogs assigned to O. laevis were reported from lowland streams, rivers, swamps and flooded rice fields, as well as cascading mountain streams. This list describes reported settings; it gives no numeric elevational range or physiological tolerance test, and does not show that every freshwater body is suitable. The paper lists specimen voucher lots separately, so the historical assignments can be traced without adding unreported coordinates or life-stage details.

## A historical widespread concept and dated status citation {#s-0caccb46a0}
The 2016 paper described O. laevis as widespread and not endemic to the Philippines, and cited a Least Concern assessment. It listed, among other material, voucher lots KU 333097–102 from site AN 10 and KU 333109–37 from site MO 2. These are historical identifications and a dated status citation; they do not establish the present range or current official conservation category under the COL26.8 concept.
