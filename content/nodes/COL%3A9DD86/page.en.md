## Stem surface {#s-2d75880e8d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as hairy, with hairs spreading outward. It also lists simple hairs, fine thread-like unbranched bristles, and capitate glandular hairs with small head-like tips. Branched bristles, prickles, and brachyblasts (short lateral shoots) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-6e40ec23b2}
Stipules, small appendages at leaf bases, persist, and extrafloral nectaries (glands outside flowers) are absent. Projections between pinna pairs are present. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; its floral bracts are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-b9fc6a6b6d}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The calyx is membranous, with triangular lobes. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are pubescent. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Substrate {#s-1a53ae4b14}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, meaning it grows on land. It does not give a more specific habitat or collection locality.
