## Two possible growth patterns and a grooved leaf midrib {#s-2536bdf9d9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists both monopodial growth, with a continuing main axis, and sympodial growth, in which later growth continues from side shoots. Stem hairs are two-armed. Leaves are elliptic or obovate; the upper midrib is channelled toward the stalk and flattens toward the tip. The lower surface is hairless or finely short-haired. Bracts or cataphylls beneath leaf nodes fall away. These alternatives are listed without frequencies.

## Axillary clusters and a globose fruit {#s-d684395b43}
Small flowering branches are compressed, and the bracteoles beneath the flowers fall away. The field lists one, two, three, four, or more than four inflorescences per axillary shoot. Their position is axillary; the arrangement is corymb-like or pyramidal and opposite. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal. The floral disc is hairless; the flower cup is top-shaped and smooth. Sepals are separate, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. Fruit calyx lobes fall away, and the fruit is globose.
