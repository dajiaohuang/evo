## Did trilobites have a long hidden history? {#s-d50c56eae3}
Trilobites appear widely in the early Cambrian body-fossil record. Holmes and Budd reassessed a morphological character matrix for early artiopodans and compared parsimony and Bayesian analyses to ask how much earlier trilobite origins might lie than those fossils.

In the sampled analyses, the inferred origin falls relatively close to the oldest widespread trilobite body fossils, leaving a shorter “cryptic” interval than some accounts propose. This is a topology-and-clock result, not a fossil date or proof that trilobites were absent earlier. Character coding, tree shape, time priors and the incompleteness of the record all affect the estimate.
