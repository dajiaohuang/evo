## The 2021 comparison after separating the Izumo lineage {#s-3380ba266d}
After distinguishing the Izumo lineage as H. kunibiki, the authors reported no yellow ventral tail-edge stripe in sampled H. utsunomiyaorum of either sex. The fifth hind toe was absent in most sampled males, 89.5%, and females, 70.0%, while white lateral spots were common. These frequencies describe assigned comparison samples rather than invariant rules. The discussion said ranges in central and eastern Hiroshima were not resolved in detail and proposed further morphology before any synonymization of H. hidamontanus; this reader does not enact that proposed synonym or certify all older utsunomiyaorum identifications.
