## Taxonomy and lineage {#s-5643283291}
COL26.8 accepts Xanthonycticebus intermedius (BV3HZ); MDD v2.5 uses the English name Northern Pygmy Slow Loris and lists it as Endangered (EN). Blair et al. (2023) used historical museum specimens collected from 1884 to 1980 and mitochondrial sequences to examine loris lineages and restore the taxonomic status of intermedius. That historical-sample study addresses lineage and classification, not the size of living populations.

## Regional field evidence {#s-ca05f7697f}
Yang et al. (2026) report nighttime spotlight surveys across southwestern and southern Guangxi, China, from June to October 2025. They confirmed stable populations in Bobai, Ningming, Longzhou, and Pingxiang and recorded 98 individuals. The authors say these records may represent the largest known population of the species in China. The figure belongs to this Guangxi survey; it is not a census or range-wide estimate across China, Laos, and Vietnam.

## Scope of the evidence {#s-8291fb55e4}
The recent field survey provides direct species-level occurrence and local population evidence for Guangxi, China; it covers four counties and does not replace surveys across the full range. The page places historical lineage work beside a modern local survey without combining them into a range-wide count, trend, or conservation assessment for all regions.

## Evidence limits {#s-120319246d}
The 2025 field records are limited to four counties in Guangxi, China; 98 individuals are not a total for the species, and the survey did not cover Laos or Vietnam. Historical museum DNA addresses lineage and taxonomy, not present-day distribution or abundance. The MDD EN category is a version-specific assessment field and was not independently assessed here.
