## A solitary shoot with elliptic leaves {#s-fc76a984f5}
The morphology record describes an unthickened underground base producing one firm aerial shoot. Alternate, stalked leaves are elliptic to oblong and end in a tiny point. Radiate flower heads have pointed bracts and a single series of ray florets; the narrow obovoid fruits are glabrous, and their awns are joined to the pappus scales.
