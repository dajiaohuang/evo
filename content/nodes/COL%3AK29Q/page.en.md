## Feeding research {#s-605d4cfa9a}
In a one-month eastern Madagascar rainforest study, most observations followed one radio-collared male. The male’s recorded home range was 1.4 ha and appeared to be used by him and one female. All observed feeding during those follows was on leaves, and only leaves were found in fecal samples. This small, single-male study should not be read as a species-wide diet or ranging estimate.

## Survey records {#s-adf35443fc}
A 2026 study surveyed 18 unprotected forests using 97 nocturnal transects totalling 291.8 km, vegetation plots and 208 interviews. The authors detected A. laniger at each study site; the sites were described as representing about one third of the species’ range. This is evidence from the surveyed landscape, not a full-island census.
