## Ruschia intermedia {#s-c37a005212}
## Slightly sickle-shaped leaves and striped purple petals

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 35 cm tall. The leaves are three-angled to almost cylindrical in cross-section and slightly sickle-shaped, measuring 20–30 mm by about 3 mm.

Purplish flowers grow in clusters; their petals have lengthwise stripes and reach 10–12 mm long. The fruit has five chambers. The habitat account records rocky sandstone flats.
