## Orange flowers and glossy nectar guides {#s-6645e2f029}
Moraea tulbaghensis is 25–40 cm tall and usually has bright-orange flowers with navy-blue to glossy green nectar guides. Western populations differ in inner-tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal) form and are diploid (having two sets of chromosomes), whereas the species is tetraploid (having four sets of chromosomes) elsewhere; the source retains them within the species.
