## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Ptilochaeta bahiensis? {#s-1b103c5a8b}
The expanded leaves appear only after flowering. The corymb-shaped inflorescence has a peduncle up to 4 mm long. The anthers and petal blades are oval.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-c2161dc613}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
