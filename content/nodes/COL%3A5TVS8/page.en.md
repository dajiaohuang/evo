## A regional description of the plant {#s-a6e11d3eb6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records alternate, ovate, leathery leaflets with hairless upper and lower surfaces and a rounded leaf rachis. The inflorescences are racemes. In the flower, the banner petal is obovate, while the wings and keel petals are lance-shaped; all three are described as pubescent. The fruit is curved. This is one regional dataset description, not a complete account of variation across the species.

## The habitat field {#s-693f27d259}
The same dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial (“Terrícola”). That category does not give a collecting locality or establish the species’ full distribution.
