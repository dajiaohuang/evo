## Mikania pinnatiloba {#s-d0f92e8fa5}
## Lobed leaves and hairy stems

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright, striate, hairy branches with solid pith. Opposite leaves are triangular and lobed, with narrowing bases and rounded tips. Both surfaces are hairy; three main veins radiate from the base, and the blades are leathery.

Loose panicles carry stalked heads. Long, linear bracts sit at the top of each head stalk; the elliptic bracts around a head are finely hairy and tapered. The bell-shaped corolla has triangular lobes shorter than its tube. The dry fruits (cypselae) are sparsely hairy. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; this regional entry gives no locality or complete range.
