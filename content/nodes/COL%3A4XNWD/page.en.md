## Siphocampylus umbellatus {#s-48e1234f4d}
## Green flowers gather in corymbs

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes erect stems with alternate, thin, oval to oblong leaves. Their bases are acute, and their margins toothed. The flowers are arranged in corymbs, clusters in which the lower stalks are longer. Calyx lobes are at least 1 cm long, and the hemispherical flower base supports a green corolla with green lobes. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
