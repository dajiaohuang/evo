The whole Grès rouge Group exhibits a reversed polarity, meaning that it was deposited before the mid-Wordian Illawarra Reversal. Based on comparisons with Saint-Affrique and Lodève Basin stratigraphic equivalents, Werneburg et al. (2022) estimate the M1 and M2 megasequences as having a latest Artinskian age.

The revised 2023/04 Permian stratigraphic chart and the revised ages of the Saint-Affrique Permian sequence provided by Poujol et al. (2023) both suggest instead an Artinskian-Kungurian age.
