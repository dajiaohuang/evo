## Babiana lobata {#s-9b8de88580}
Babiana lobata is a hairless geophyte with narrowly sword-shaped leaves and blue-mauve flowers marked with yellow. The flowers sit along the upper side of nearly horizontal spikes; their lower lateral tepals have ear-like lobes at the base. The regional account also notes short floral bracts and twisted remains of older leaves around the stem base. Rocky slopes and flats are the recorded habitat. These features help separate it from related Babiana with longer bracts or hairy stems.
