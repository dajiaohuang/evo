Between Banks’ Valley and the summit of Sugar Loaf, within about a quarter of a mile from the sea, there is a barren area in which there is a series of ridges of calcareous sand, loosely cemented together, the ridges being separated by narrow gullies: the sides of the ridges contain many fragments of bird bones.
