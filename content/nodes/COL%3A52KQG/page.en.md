## Stifftia parviflora {#s-a767d568e3}
## Cream pappus tops the ten-ribbed fruit

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes young stems with smooth bark and branches that are leafy near their tips, nearly hairless. Elliptic to oblanceolate leaves are 4–11 cm long, 1–4 cm wide, leathery, pointed and entire-edged, with tapered bases and short stalks.

Panicle-like clusters bear disc-shaped heads on 1.2–2 cm stalks. Each bell-shaped involucre is about 6–8 mm high and 1 cm wide, with roughly 20 smooth, round-tipped bracts in 5–6 rows. About 12 greenish florets have corolla lobes rolled back and divided to the tube base. The finely hairy, ten-ribbed dry fruits have a cream pappus arranged in 3–4 rows.
