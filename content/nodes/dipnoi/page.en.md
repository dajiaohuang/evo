## What the sequence sample tests {#s-7d4b120929}
Lungfish expressed-sequence-tag data provide a phylogenomic test of where living lungfishes sit among jawed vertebrates. A calibrated bony-fish clock places the lineage shared by living lungfishes and tetrapods relative to the coelacanth split at about 409 Ma. The 409–0 Ma route is model-based, not a fossil range for Dipnoi.

The EST sequences address the placement of living lungfishes in a vertebrate phylogeny. They help test relationships among living lineages but do not date the lungfish fossil record or show that one fossil species directly gave rise to another.

## A clock boundary, not a fossil endpoint {#s-bb52059f67}
The calibrated analysis uses 24 calibrations and estimates a boundary near 409 Ma for the lineage containing living lungfishes and tetrapods relative to coelacanths. The displayed interval carries this model result to the present; it is not a complete fossil history or an exact Dipnoi origin date.
