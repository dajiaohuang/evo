## What leaf, flower and seed traits distinguish Turnera stipularis? {#s-d458a94e75}
The branches have simple hairs. Petiolate leaves bear stipules and extrafloral nectaries. Elliptic to slightly rhombic blades have toothed margins, are hairless above and sparsely hairy below. The inflorescence is a raceme, with a peduncle and bracteoles but no pedicels. The yellow corolla lacks a corona; filaments attach along the petal claw to the throat. Styles are heterostylous. The fruit exocarp is smooth; seeds have striate net-like surfaces, papillose outer layers and a prominent chalaza.
