## Samolus parviflorus Raf. {#s-dc618abba1}
## Basal or stem leaves with several blade shapes

The quoted Flora of North America passage describes Samolus parviflorus leaves as basal and stem-borne, or entirely stem-borne. They may be sessile or petiolate, with blades ranging from obovate or broadly spatulate to elliptic and measuring 1.5–15 cm long. This excerpt provides leaf characters only.
