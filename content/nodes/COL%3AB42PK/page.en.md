## White-woolly leaves along the stems {#s-f6b6281f1e}
The record describes an upright perennial herb with ovate, stalked leaves distributed along the stem. The leaf stalks have ear-like lobes, and both leaf surfaces are covered in white woolly hairs. Yellow ray and disk florets form radiate heads in branched clusters. The fruits are silky or softly hairy, with a white pappus that falls away. The habitat field says terrestrial.
