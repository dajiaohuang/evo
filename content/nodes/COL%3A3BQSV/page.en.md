## A bark-associated substrate entry {#s-2870ae0262}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Eucamptodontopsis pilifera (Mitt.) Broth. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; this page uses its source-labelled habitat field.

The field is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling. It names a substrate category rather than a collection locality.

No collection site, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage is supplied.

The habitat category is the only biological content included from this regional entry; it is not a complete species account.
