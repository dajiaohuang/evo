## *Abolboda macrostachya*: spikes with many flowers {#s-b0ae937fda}
The leaves are linear to lance-shaped, with flat or three-angled blades; their veins or ridges are visible, while the edges are not thickened. Several spikes arise from a leaf rosette, each holding more than six flowers. The lateral sepals are longer than the bracts.

The evidence lists both aquatic and terrestrial settings, so the documented habitat spans water and land.
