## Historical name association {#s-04afddedea}
COL26.8 accepts Bostrychia bocagei within Threskiornithidae. AVONET v7 has a historical record with the same name and family. Two one-to-one crosswalks connect the datasets’ names; they do not settle species concepts after later splits or lumps.

## Habitat, feeding niche and activity {#s-788c79278b}
AVONET records tall-tree vegetation with a relatively closed canopy, a terrestrial-invertebrate feeding niche and a ground-oriented primary lifestyle. The source defines a dominant feeding niche using a threshold of at least 60% of food resources. This is a category rule, not an independently measured diet proportion or evidence of exclusive feeding.

## The migration label {#s-6684178e24}
The migration category is sedentary. This broad source label does not list regional seasonal movements or provide current routes, schedules or distances for every population.

## Historical map endpoints {#s-944c34afb4}
Breeding and resident maps extend from latitude 0.1° to 0.24°. The worksheet links these fields to BirdLife/HBW 2019.1 maps accessed on 27 March 2020. Endpoints summarize mapped geography, not a locality list, continuous occupancy or non-breeding movements; they do not establish current distribution or conservation status.

## What remains uninterpreted {#s-ef6b713e4a}
All 4 AVONET ecological categories have readable records, but they do not make a complete ecology account. The source flags inference from relatives for some biometric fields, and mass is inferred from a related species. Morphological dimensions and mass are therefore left uninterpreted here; reproduction, growth and conservation are also incomplete.

## Scope of the historical AVONET record {#s-2f9c858904}
This is a limited account of AVONET1_BirdLife ecological and geographic fields. The dataset uses HBW–BirdLife 5.0 names from December 2020; same-name records and BirdLife/eBird/BirdTree crosswalks support association, not equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Ecological labels are coarse literature-derived categories, and the underlying species literature was not individually reviewed. Some biometric fields involve inference from relatives, including mass from a related species, so morphology and mass are not interpreted as direct measurements of this species. Maps cover historical breeding and resident ranges only; current occupancy, population and conservation need other sources.
