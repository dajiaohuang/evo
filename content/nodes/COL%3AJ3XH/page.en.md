## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-ff18fd295b}
The source describes clustered stems up to 20 m tall and 5–15 leaves. Each side of the leaf axis bears about 165 pinnae, arranged irregularly in different planes. The inflorescence is upright and emerges between the leaves. Two to eight female flowers are recorded at the base of each small flower-bearing branch. Fruits may be ellipsoid, egg-shaped, or nearly spherical; their surface is smooth and they do not split open at maturity.
