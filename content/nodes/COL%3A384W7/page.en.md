## A dwarf, leafless succulent with small cream flowers {#s-f3c844d92e}
The regional account describes a dwarf, mat-forming, leafless succulent with nearly globose stems 1–3 cm long and clear sap. Its cream-coloured, glabrous flowers are compared with those of D. caespitosa and are under 15 mm across; the corona is also cream-coloured. The habitat entry records Karroid scrub. A separate diagnostic excerpt in this evidence record names D. maculata and D. modesta, so it does not add supported characters for D. parviflora.
