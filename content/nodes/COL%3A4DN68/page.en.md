## Parkia ulei {#s-c6144cc53e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Parkia ulei* as an accepted species of *Parkia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes axillary, spherical flower heads on upright stalks and leaflets with rounded tips and ear-shaped bases. The anthers are yellow; the fruit is a hairless, leathery pod that splits open. The habitat field lists the plant as terrestrial.
