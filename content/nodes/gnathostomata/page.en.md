## Qianodus teeth provide a minimum sampled record {#s-ba39ba22af}
Twenty-three *Qianodus* tooth whorls from late Aeronian strata, approximately 439 Ma, anchor the 439–0 Ma Gnathostomata route. These isolated dental elements support a sampled minimum occurrence for jawed vertebrates and a stem-chondrichthyan analysis; they do not date the origin of jaws, crown Gnathostomata or a global first appearance.
