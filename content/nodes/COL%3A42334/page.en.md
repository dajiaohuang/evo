## Reproductive fields and summary values {#s-f54cde8c03}
The row records oviparous reproduction (egg-laying); consolidated mean of offspring or eggs per litter/clutch 5.5. Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. Fractions do not describe non-integer young in a litter. The lowest/highest fields are defined as endpoints of reported means rather than guaranteed smallest/largest individual clutches. Reproductive and sex-determination labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity across all populations.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-93d9810bb1}
Available compilation fields are offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 5.5 offspring/eggs; oviposition to hatching 135 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 13.5 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.
