## What does the field record for Duguetia aripuanae? {#s-c1bd890af0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow elliptic leaves, smooth above and sparsely covered with scale-like hairs below. Flowers occur in small clusters; their buds are oval and greenish, with sepals joined at the base. The outer and inner petals are nearly equal and oval. Fruit color, form, surface hairs, and a basal collar are not specified in this record.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-b925f821d3}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
