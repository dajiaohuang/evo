## Short stature and pink flowers in small spikes {#s-236c27673e}
Geissorhiza altimontana is a small corm-bearing plant (its corm is a swollen underground storage stem), up to 12 cm tall, with five or six short leaves and spikes of one or two pink flowers with greenish throats. Its longer perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts), 7–8 mm, helps distinguish it from the similar G. nigromontana, whose tube is 2–3 mm.

## High rocky sandstone slopes {#s-ac7375974d}
The regional record places it on rocky sandstone slopes at high elevations. The entry does not supply a complete elevation range.
