## Neea clarkii {#s-86aed78d18}
## Opposite leaves and clustered flower heads

Neea clarkii has opposite, petiolate leaves with smooth surfaces. The blades are elliptic or lance-shaped, with entire margins and pointed or tapering tips. Multi-flowered cymes may be upright, drooping or clustered; they arise at branch tips or directly from the trunk. Male flowers have a tubular perianth, while female flowers have a club-shaped tube. The fleshy anthocarp is globose or ellipsoid; the source leaves its cupule lobes unknown.
