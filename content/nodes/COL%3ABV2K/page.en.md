## Selected hand and colour contrasts {#s-f3c9672228}
The2020 author comparison reports opaque-white belly/throat, absent ventrolateral stripe and conspicuous transverse thigh bars for subfolionidificans, contrasting those selected characters with velocicantus. These are attributed comparative descriptions, not new measurements or a complete key. Similar preserved ventral colours and male size can make superficial identification difficult; the paper does not authenticate all historical museum labels through these traits.

## A single-note arrangement in the reviewed comparison {#s-b334475599}
The2020 description summarizes subfolionidificans calls as a single note emitted continuously or irregularly, contrasting them with velocicantus trills of66–138 pulsed notes. This information is attributed to prior acoustic studies cited there; their original recordings, individual counts and temperature conditions have not been reread here. “Single note” describes the reported call unit, not inability to repeat notes or produce other vocalizations.
