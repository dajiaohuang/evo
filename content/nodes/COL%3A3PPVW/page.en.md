## ## Large leaflets and long, showy flowers {#s-ff06d86de7}
The historical Flora of Panama describes *Inga portobellensis* as a mostly glabrous tree with elongated lenticels on young branches. Its leaves have two pairs of very large leaflets: the upper pair is 20–30 cm long and 10–13 cm wide, while the lower pair is about half as long. A winged leaf stalk and axis carry cup-shaped glands where the leaflet pairs attach. Solitary axillary flower heads or very compact spikes bear flowers with a tubular, funnel-shaped calyx about 25 mm long and a tubular corolla nearly 4 cm long. The flat, curved, woody pod is about 16 cm long and 2.5 cm wide. The same historical flora lists Panama and Costa Rica; it gives no sampling frame or observation date, so this is not a current range inventory.
