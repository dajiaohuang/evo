## Prestonia tomentosa {#s-c6d90735bb}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Prestonia tomentosa* as an accepted species of *Prestonia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records pubescent stems and leaves, axillary cymes, and a hairy corolla. The follicles are described as free and spreading; follicles are the dry fruits that open along one side. The habitat field labels it terrestrial. This is a regional description and does not establish complete variation.
