## Record identity and classification {#s-8a31083b83}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6590 records Siren lacertina under Siren, Sirenidae, and Caudata, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-08803061d4}
This AmphiBIO row marks fossorial; terrestrial; aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Adult food categories {#s-cd4fc8acd8}
The row lists arthropods; vertebrates, including cannibalism in the source definition as adult food categories. AmphiBIO compiles these labels from literature based on expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents; the list is not a complete diet or a measure of the share each food contributes.

## Activity categories {#s-924f79e533}
For this row, AmphiBIO marks night as activity periods. These broad labels summarize reported activity times; they are not a continuous record of behaviour.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-2332a29a86}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body mass of 1000 g and a maximum adult body length of 978 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian body-mass entries; this remains the AmphiBIO v1 value, not a revised species-level estimate.
