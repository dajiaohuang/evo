## Name and classification in the source {#s-0034eed257}
Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Odorrana versabilis* under genus *Odorrana*, family Ranidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurements in the deposit {#s-8f8d4d791f}
The source table contains 2 measurement rows; 15 trait columns have at least one recorded value. Its Sex field uses M (male) and F (female). Absolute measurement units are not confirmed, so the numeric cells cannot be interpreted as absolute dimensions. These rows are not necessarily unique specimens, and missing cells do not show that a structure is absent.
