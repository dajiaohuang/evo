## The source concept {#s-508dafddbd}
In AmphiBIO v1, Platymantis mamusiorum appears in row Anf1108, classified in the genus Platymantis, family Ceratobatrachidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited record; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Activity periods {#s-3982aa894c}

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-629ea16bc1}
For Platymantis mamusiorum, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 31 mm. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs and toads (Anura), and total length for salamanders (Caudata) and caecilians (Gymnophiona). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
