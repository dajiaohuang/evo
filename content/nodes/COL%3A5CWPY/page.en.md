## Zanthoxylum rigidum {#s-5fadc03fcd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records deciduous leaves and terminal panicles, while listing alternatives for leaf division, armature, margins, flower-part number and fruit-segment fusion. The habitat field records terrestrial growth.

## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-63ac73a0d0}
The entry describes thin, non-exfoliating periderm on the branchlets. Leaves are deciduous. The source lists prickles as present or absent, leaflet bases as sessile or stalked, margins as entire or crenate/crenulate, lower surfaces as sparsely or densely pubescent, and leaf division as paripinnate or imparipinnate. Domatia are absent. Terminal panicles are pyramidal rather than corymb-shaped; their stalk and axis are neither corky nor reticulate. Flowers have three carpels, rarely two, a disc-shaped stigma and four or five parts; flowers are pedicellate. Fruit segments (mericarps) are described as connate at the base or at the stigma and bear raised glands. The seed hilum is linear.
