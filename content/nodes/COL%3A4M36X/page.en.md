## Visible leaf veins and short flower stalks {#s-3f196a0284}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records obovate or oblanceolate leaf blades with conspicuous secondary veins; their texture is listed as leathery or papery. The flower stalk is described as short. Calyx colour is recorded as yellow, orange, or white, and corolla colour as yellow, white, or cream. The corolla lobes are narrowly elliptic, and pollen is recorded as occurring in monads. The source field retains colour and leaf-texture alternatives rather than resolving them.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-d95573d011}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Potalia amara* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
