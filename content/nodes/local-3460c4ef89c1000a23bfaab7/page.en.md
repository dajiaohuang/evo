## The source tadpole concept {#s-35ac8774aa}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Leptopelis kivuensis, in the genus Leptopelis and family Arthroleptidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Leptopelis kivuensis. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-eab5c50ac1}
The compilation has three records transcribed from literature and one measured from an image. Supplied stage labels are 39, 30–39, 36 and 32; stage 32 is marked “Judging based on the picture.” These labels belong to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves uncertainty and distinguishes picture-based judgements from directly documented stages. Four records do not necessarily represent four unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
