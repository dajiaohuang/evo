## Leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-46f31a0ce7}
The source describes a shrub or tree up to 20 m, usually deciduous, with simple, broadly elliptic leaves to 90 mm; lower branches are often spiny. Flowers occur in umbel-like racemes or corymbs, and the fruit has a fleshy-juicy mesocarp with grit cells. The habitat note lists mesic grassland, roadsides and moist places.
