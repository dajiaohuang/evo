## Three leaflets, a white flower, and a spur {#s-46d8c7d45a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes Centrosema virginianum with three leaflets on a conspicuous leaf axis. Leaf tissue is coded as membranous or papery; the oval to elliptic leaflets may be becoming hairless or pubescent. The flower is white and has a calcar, a projecting floral spur; the pod is straight, with finely hairy valves. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. The record is a regional description without a sampling frame, sex, life stage, locality, date, or wild/cultivated status.
