## *Sebaea bojeri* {#s-0a2fd453b9}
## A small herb with a measured flower

The SANBI/WFO morphology excerpt describes an erect annual herb 5–20 cm tall, simple or branched from the base. Its lower stems may bear glands or tiny gland-tipped teeth. Leaves reach 1 cm long by 3 mm wide; the lower ones are reduced and often scale-like. The loose flower cluster bears one to a few blooms. Each of the five calyx segments is 5.5–9 mm long and 1–2.25 mm wide; the segments join for up to 3 mm and carry a keel-like wing 0.5–1.75 mm wide. The yellow corolla tube is 5–6 mm long, and its lobes are 5.5–8 mm long by 2.25–4 mm wide.

Filaments measure 1.3–2.75 mm and attach between the corolla lobes. Anthers are 1.5–2.5 mm long; each has a tiny gland at its tip without a stalk or appears to lack one. The style and stigma measure 5–7 mm together. The account records either no swelling below the stigma or a large swelling below the middle; the stigma itself is large and two-lobed, with broad lobes. These measurements and alternatives follow the cited account.

## A taxonomic note and a mixed herbarium sheet

The same archived account describes *S. bojeri* as closely related to *S. filiformis*, with a similar distribution, while saying the floral differences appear constant. It also reports that the Wilms 971 sheet at the British Museum of Natural History mixes *S. bojeri* and *S. grandis*. The note is specific to that account and specimen sheet.
