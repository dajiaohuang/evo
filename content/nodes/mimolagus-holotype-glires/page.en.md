## Teeth, ankles and the size of Mimolagus {#s-990e3bc58f}
Mimolagus aurorae is represented by holotype upper third molar IVPP V20115 and referred teeth and tarsals from the Middle Eocene. A 4.5-kilogram body-mass estimate and comparisons with small tapiroids are model-based; the fossils do not directly reveal diet or ancestry.

## A dental type and associated ankle bones {#s-7c2efb23e1}
The holotype is a right upper third molar, IVPP V20115. The study also refers other teeth, an astragalus, calcanei and a cuboid to Mimolagus aurorae. These named materials document a large Middle Eocene mimotonid in the sampled localities; the teeth and ankle bones are not all one articulated skeleton.

## Models are not direct ecology {#s-1f1d264b85}
An astragalar regression produced an estimated mass of about 4.5 kg, and functional comparison suggested convergence on a small tapiroid form. Cursoriality and a tapiroid-like niche are interpretations, not observed movement or feeding. The result does not make Mimolagus a direct lagomorph ancestor, and it cannot establish a species-wide menu from these specimens.
