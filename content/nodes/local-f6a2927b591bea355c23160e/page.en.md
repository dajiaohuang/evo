## The source tadpole concept {#s-2764934042}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Rhinella diptycha, in the genus Rhinella and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying works use three names: Rhinella diptycha, Rhinella schneideri and Rhinella jimi; the compilation retains them separately. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-8a21e395f7}
The compilation has five records transcribed from literature and one measured from an image. Supplied stage labels are 35, 39; 36; 28–29; 37; 36–38; and 30, with stage 30 marked “Judging based on the picture.” These labels belong to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves uncertainty and distinguishes picture-based judgements from directly documented stages. Six records do not necessarily represent six unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
