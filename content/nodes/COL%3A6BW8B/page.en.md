## ## Asymmetrical leaflets and a granular fruit {#s-8f5c69a3f4}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records leaflets with strongly asymmetrical bases, petiolules under 2 mm long, a truncate outer-side base and a long-acuminate tip. The single main vein is off-center and asymmetrical. The fruit is globose with a granular surface. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial; this regional character field does not specify a collection locality or population coverage.
