## How do the fruit and flower traits of Stigmaphyllon cavernosulum differ? {#s-d0506009a6}
Its smooth branches have leaf stalks 10.4–60 mm long, each with a stalkless disk gland at the tip. The undivided blade becomes nearly hairless below; its entire edge bears stalked glands. Solitary umbels carry 15 to many flowers. Yellow petals have toothed edges. The anthers are hairless; rear lateral stamens have two reduced pollen sacs joined by connective tissue. The styles are hairless, with the front ones shorter and smaller at the expanded tips; the stigma points toward a rear petal. The fruit has a developed dorsal wing and one pair of small lateral wings.
