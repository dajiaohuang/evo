## Simple leaves and a two-lipped flower cup {#s-8a816fd24c}
The flora entry describes an erect plant 2–3 m tall with broad wings along the stem joints. Unlike the nearby three-leaflet forms, its leaves are simple, with an obovate to spatula-shaped blade and no leaf stalk. Loose flower clusters grow at branch tips or opposite leaves. The yellow flowers have a two-lipped outer floral cup. Their pods are black, oblong to obovate, and hairless; the seeds are ochre-brown. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
