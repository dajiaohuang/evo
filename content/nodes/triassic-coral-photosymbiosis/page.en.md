## Do Triassic coral skeletons record photosymbiosis? {#s-a51723c242}
In Turkish Late Triassic coral skeletons, researchers compared growth-band regularity, carbon and oxygen isotope patterns, and nitrogen isotopes in intracrystalline organic matter with living taxa. Together, these proxies are consistent with photosymbiosis in the sampled fossils, around 212 million years old. The chemistry supports an ecological interpretation; it does not preserve the symbiont or identify an algal partner.
