## What marks its flattened leaves and spindle-like spike? {#s-eea4c43e3f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records *Xyris riopretensis* with two-ranked leaves, broad sheath bases and smooth, flat blades. A conspicuous ligule, the small projection where blade meets sheath, is also noted. The 20–50 cm stalk bears an ellipsoid or spindle-shaped spike with up to ten flowers. Several bract (a leaf-like structure around a flower) and sepal states remain alternatives in the source field.
