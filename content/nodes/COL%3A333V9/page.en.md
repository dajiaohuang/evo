## A spreading cactus with papery spine sheaths {#s-b9d6e3b3fa}
This spreading shrub can reach 3 m. Its cylindrical stem joints have tubercles; leaves fall early or may be absent. Each areole bears 8–30 light-brown spines covered by papery sheaths. The flowers are red, with purple stamens and style. The ecology entry says it has escaped cultivation and become a weed.
