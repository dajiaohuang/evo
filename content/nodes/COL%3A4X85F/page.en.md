## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-ba273e9b97}
The erect stems bear bristles and simple or gland-tipped hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned, symmetrical ovate leaves have heart-shaped bases, pointed tips and toothed edges. The upper surface is hairy with gland-tipped or star-shaped hairs. Solitary flowers grow in the leaf axils; the sepals are pointed and the corolla yellow. The dry fruit splits into five segments (mericarps) without awns; the segments are hairless.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-aa3d146144}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
