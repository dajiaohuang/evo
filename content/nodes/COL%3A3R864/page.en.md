COL26.8 records Kinosternon creaseri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Kinosternidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Aquatic/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-f774eb9f1f}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to van Dijk et al. 2007. The lowest value among reported litter or clutch means is 1; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to van Dijk et al. 2007. The highest value among reported litter or clutch means is 2; the field does not make it the smallest or largest individual litter. ReptTraits attributes this field to van Dijk et al. 2007. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-c31155575e}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
