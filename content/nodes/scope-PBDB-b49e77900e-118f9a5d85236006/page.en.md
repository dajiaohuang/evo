## A rounded model ceiling and a bounded fossil sample {#s-de8229da49}
The 66–0 Ma Glires route uses a rounded K–Pg model/display ceiling, contextualized by a post-K–Pg placental analysis, and a separate direct Mimolagus fossil sample dated to 47.8–41.2 Ma in China’s Erlian Basin. Living descendants carry the navigation route to the present. The model ceiling is not a fossil first appearance; the Mimolagus interval dates that sample, not crown Glires. Neither source shows continuous fossil occupancy.
