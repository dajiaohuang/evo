## Selaginella cabrerensis {#s-2737bf2a0c}
## Prostrate stems and unequal leaf microforms

Its stems are recorded as prostrate or partly upright, straw-coloured or brown, and smooth, with no joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise from axillary or lateral positions. The small leaves differ by position: lateral leaves are oblong or ovate-oblong with rounded bases, while dorsal leaves are ovate to ovate-lanceolate with truncate or slightly heart-shaped bases. Idioblasts are recorded in lateral leaves but absent from dorsal and axillary leaves. The habitat field says terrestrial.
