## Embryos observed under laboratory conditions {#s-bd5f692776}
In lagoon-resident North Uist clutches incubated at 14 °C, daily observations recorded a visible heartbeat by day 5. By day 7, yolk-sac circulation and developing pectoral fins could be seen. These observations describe selected embryonic stages under the study conditions, not adult diagnostic morphology.

## Hatching times in two ecotypes {#s-3f455d0fd8}
At 14 °C, embryos from six migratory and six lagoon-resident North Uist crosses hatched earlier in the migratory ecotype. Mean first hatch was 11.7 versus 13.2 days after fertilisation, and mean time to 50% of the clutch hatching was 12.2 versus 13.7 days. Hatching success did not differ significantly in this small clutch experiment; these timings are not a species-wide rate.

## One shared collection site {#s-01208537ed}
Breeding migratory and lagoon-resident adults were collected at Loch an Duin, a saltwater lagoon in northeast North Uist, Scotland, from 30 April to 19 May 2023. The study establishes local co-occurrence at this site and time, not a distribution estimate. Wild-bred parents were used; eggs and embryos were then maintained under laboratory conditions.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-3eda008c0b}
This focused study follows two local ecotypes from one North Uist site through selected embryonic stages and hatching at 14 °C. It does not assess adult diagnostic morphology, other life stages, reproduction in natural nests, survival, lifespan, other populations, the species’ full distribution, fossils or conservation status.
