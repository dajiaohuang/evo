## Recorded parent of Teleoceratina {#s-af52111bce}
Teleoceratina is listed at rank subtribe in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotini as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
