## Othonna hederifolia {#s-6f2596c3b8}
## Cuneate leaf bases distinguish this species from O. auriculifolia

Manning and Goldblatt compare *Othonna hederifolia* with *O. auriculifolia*: *O. hederifolia* has wedge-shaped leaf bases and broad teeth on the upper part of the leaf. The account records sandstone pavements as habitat.
