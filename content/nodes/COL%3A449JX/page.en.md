## *Monotagma grallatum*: orange bracts that expand at flowering {#s-ed7a017d02}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 character profile, the leaf blade is symmetric, with a green midrib and a hairless underside. The leaf joint has a ring, and the plant has no root tubers or overlapping sheaths. Its terminal flower cluster rises on a stalk longer than 2.5 cm and bears two to nine nodes.

The orange bracts expand when the flowers open, and the outer staminode—a sterile stamen-like floral part—is obovate. The habitat field records terrestrial substrate only; no locality or date is attached to this regional morphology entry.
