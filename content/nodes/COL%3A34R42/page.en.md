## *Dendropanax arboreus*: large lobed juvenile leaves and purple fruit {#s-66ace68ba5}
A historical Flora of Panama account describes shrubs or trees to 15 m. Young leaves are often three- or four-lobed and can reach 45 cm across; mature blades are ovate to elliptic or oblong, usually about 15 by 7 cm, within a stated range of 6–29 by 3–9 cm. Three or more umbrella-shaped flower clusters form a terminal raceme, with up to 45 greenish-white flowers in each umbel.

The purple fruit is 4–8 mm across and contains five to seven seeds. The historical account places the species from Sinaloa to Tamaulipas and southward, naming Cuba, Puerto Rico and South America; that source-era statement is not a current range inventory.
