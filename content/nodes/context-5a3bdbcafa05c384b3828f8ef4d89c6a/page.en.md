## Record identity and classification {#s-4ddf89355b}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf6610 records Caecilia disossea under Caecilia, Caeciliidae, and Gymnophiona, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Habitat-use categories {#s-5e212d06ea}
This AmphiBIO row marks fossorial; terrestrial; aquatic among its broad habitat and foraging categories. These labels show which categories the compilation recorded, not changes in use by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations; read this as the AmphiBIO v1 label, not as a corrected species-level account.

## Seasonal activity categories {#s-06d258f283}
AmphiBIO records dry and warm for seasonal activity in this row. The categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not map to fixed calendar months across regions.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-762a19cc77}
AmphiBIO lists a maximum adult body length of 1170 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length (from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These are maximum values compiled from literature, not measurements of every individual.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-bf1842b723}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists: maximum reproductive events per year: 1. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
