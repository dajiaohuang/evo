## *Xyris diaphanobracteata*: a ridged stalk and flower-rich head {#s-6c9528bc7f}
The leaves have rough, ciliate margins, and the flower stalk is 20–70 cm long. It may be flattened near its tip and bears several ridges with hairs along them. The ovoid head has four to six sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers. The source lists this species as terrestrial.
