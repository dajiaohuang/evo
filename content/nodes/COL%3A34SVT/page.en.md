## Denekia capensis {#s-c52139a037}
## Small white or blue heads in damp places

SANBI describes a perennial herb up to 0.7 m tall, with oblong or linear-oblong leaves. Upper leaves are sessile; lower leaves may have petioles, and the undersides are often white-felted. Small heads form terminal corymbs and have white or bluish flowers. The account notes that plants growing in water can be robust while plants from seasonally dry sites may be dwarfed, even though their floral characters remain consistent. Habitat is damp ground, often shallow water, marshes or marshy stream edges.
