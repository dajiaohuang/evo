## *Xyris rigidiformis*: threadlike leaves and torn bracts {#s-647e21ea30}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes spirally arranged leaves with narrow, cylindrical to threadlike blades and pointed tips. Their broad-based sheaths may be brown-red or pale and can widen into ear-like lobes; sheath margins may be hairy or become nearly hairless. The blades themselves are recorded as smooth, wrinkled or rough.

The smooth cylindrical flowering stalk reaches 20–50 cm and has no ridge or only an indistinct one. Its egg-shaped to obovoid spike bears four sterile bracts with differently colored, torn edges and 10–25 flowers. The habitat field gives only the broad label “terrestrial,” with no named locality or survey date. The morphology is a regional description; it does not measure how variable these features are across populations.
