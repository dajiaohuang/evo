## The 2015 life-history entry {#s-8ad284097a}
COL26.8 accepts Egretta garzetta within Ardeidae. The August 2015 life-history archive provides 6 focal fields in cited species-level rows with the same name and family. This supports a historical association, but taxonomic inheritance may remain in the summaries.

## Adult mass in the archive {#s-cc55ad26ab}
The compiled adult body mass is 495 g. This field can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; it is not a range of individual masses. No birth or hatching mass is included here.

## Reproductive and developmental timing {#s-6ecccdcee8}
The source-defined female maturity field (age at first conception) is 364.23 d; litter or clutch size is 4 offspring or eggs; oviposition-to-hatching time is 21.5 d; age at flight capability is 42.5 d (some source material defines this as leaving the nest). Here, d means days. These summaries describe different events, not one reproductive cycle.

## The longest recorded lifespan {#s-963c8e44b6}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 22.34 years, selected as the largest record found under its method. It is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, an individual survival ceiling or a current record known to be repeatedly attainable.

## Limits of these compiled sources {#s-15cdbf94da}
This page combines the August 2015 life-history archive with EltonTraits 1.0; it is not a complete species monograph. Life-history values are compiled summaries, not measurements of one individual: most are medians or averages of two middle values, while maximum longevity is the largest record found. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data; same-name rows do not prove equivalence to the current COL26.8 concept. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently known, and original papers were not individually reviewed. EltonTraits weights are literature-derived estimates, not direct measurements. Current diet, range, population and conservation need other sources.
