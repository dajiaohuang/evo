## Entries under the older genus receive different interpretations {#s-1707ccc1d2}
The fixed PBDB directory has entries for Huaxiapterus benxiensis, H. corollatus, and H. jii. A 2023 revision proposed moving corollatus to Huaxiadraco, treating benxiensis as its synonym, and referring jii to Sinopterus dongi. The older directory path remains useful for tracing those names but does not make them a confirmed natural group.

## Read comparable material before older names {#s-c298df0b64}
Compare the [corollatus](Huaxiapterus_corollatus/page.en.md) holotype, wing bones, and D2525 referral with the humeral reassessment and relative-growth discussion for [benxiensis](Huaxiapterus_benxiensis/page.en.md). Then read the juvenile specimen, long first metacarpal, and crests described for [jii](Huaxiapterus_jii/page.en.md), alongside the synonymy and morphotypes proposed for [Sinopterus dongi](../Sinopterus/Sinopterus_dongi/page.en.md). Three occurrence rows do not represent three specimens or prove three separate species.

## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-2c331e10db}
Here, Huaxiapterus is a database index entry that preserves an older name grouping. The 2023 revision offers a different arrangement for the three records; the PBDB route aids traceability but does not decide their phylogenetic relationships or specimen identities.

## Compare collections, strata and age brackets {#s-52c4d84671}
Across the three entries, the selected PBDB set has three occurrence rows from two collections; Jiufotang is among the listed strata. Read each collection's age bracket and identification qualifications before comparing them. Occurrence and collection counts are not measures of ancient population size.

## Evidence limits {#s-7edb866f63}
The 2023 revision's referrals, synonymy, and maturity interpretations are taxonomic and anatomical proposals. The full 2005 description is unavailable here, and the original 2006 and 2007 descriptions, images, physical specimens, additional reported specimens, supplementary measurements, histology, and diet have not all been independently examined. The occurrence rows therefore do not establish three confirmed biological species or a shared ecology.
