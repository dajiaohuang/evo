## Placement in the sampled analyses {#s-e23202e697}
Ornithischia is represented by the taxa and characters sampled in Butler and colleagues’ morphology analysis; internal relationships are a matrix hypothesis and do not encode direct ancestors or exact clade duration.

## The Eocursor interval is a sample window {#s-57d5e00e2b}
The displayed 216.5–201.4 Ma interval summarizes the source-bounded Eocursor sample from South Africa’s uppermost-Triassic Lower Elliot Formation and its ICS stage conversion. It is not Ornithischia’s global first or last appearance, a divergence date, continuous occupation or direct-ancestor claim.
