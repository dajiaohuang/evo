## Limits of these historical data {#s-e600db2594}
These pages summarize historical datasets rather than a current species monograph. AVONET’s morphological averages come from a small sample with trait-specific counts; the separate mass value and the historical breeding/resident map limits have different sources and scopes. The 2015 life-history database reports medians or means of the middle pair, except that longevity is the largest collected record. It may inherit values across historical species and subspecies, and wild or captive conditions are not consistently recorded. EltonTraits converts literature descriptions into relative food and feeding-position weights; the underlying works were not individually reread here. Matching names do not prove that these taxonomic concepts are identical to COL26.8. Present-day movements, population, conservation and local variation need other evidence.
