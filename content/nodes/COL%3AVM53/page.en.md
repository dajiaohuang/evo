## What traits does the record give for Citharexylum solanaceum? {#s-0484e36888}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records bristly stems and opposite, papery leaves. The lower leaf surface has coarse hairs, while the upper surface bears finer ones. Its calyx (the outer floral cup) is 3–6 mm long, with five regular teeth; the corolla tube exceeds 7 mm.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-f068e336ae}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
