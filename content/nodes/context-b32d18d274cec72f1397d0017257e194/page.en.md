## The source concept {#s-59cf8b50c4}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Probreviceps rhodesianus* in row Anf0344 under Probreviceps, Brevicipitidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-00c85882b7}
For Anf0344, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These labels do not say when, how often or at which life stage each applies. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-0211014487}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 48 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-93da5f9f43}
The row records 20 as both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year and 5 mm as both the minimum and maximum offspring or egg size. These are literature-compiled values; it does not identify the sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report. Equal bounds describe this entry, not a guarantee for every clutch, age or offspring.

## Developmental strategy {#s-dff153f2c7}
The deposited row classifies development as direct. That label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
