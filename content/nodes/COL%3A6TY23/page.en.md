## How does Peixotoa magnifica differ in leaves and inflorescences? {#s-e0bf33ebae}
Inflorescence stipules are pubescent and reach 18 mm. Leaf stalks are 10–11 mm; broad elliptic blades lack blistering above and have stalkless glands and velvety hairs below, with margins rolled under. Four-flowered umbels are grouped into a dichasium, a branching flower cluster. Pedicels reach 20 mm. Anther sacs are recorded as always open, staminode glands are smooth, and the styles are equal in length.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1901f4dba7}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
