## Anf1927 and the recorded concept {#s-a0a2ff2396}
The AmphiBIO v1 entry Anf1927 names Eleutherodactylus notidodes and records Eleutherodactylus, Eleutherodactylidae and Anura as its classification. The compilation uses species concepts from Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); the available evidence does not resolve this historical entry against COL26.8.

## Maximum adult length in the compilation {#s-ee1c4b4d28}
Its maximum adult body length is 22 mm. In Anura, this measurement is snout–vent length, the distance from the snout tip to the vent. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
