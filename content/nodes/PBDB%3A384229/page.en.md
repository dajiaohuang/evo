## A whole hagfish preserved in the Cretaceous {#s-310665303c}
The holotype BHI 6445 is a complete soft-bodied hagfish from the Cenomanian Hâdjoula Lagerstätte of Lebanon, in the eastern Tethys. Its feeding apparatus and body characters were included in a published morphology-based phylogenetic analysis. That analysis places Tethymyxine tapirostrum within crown Myxini, the group containing living hagfishes, though the exact branch changes with the matrix. The 100.5–93.9 Ma interval is the sampled fossil envelope for this specimen, not a global origin date.

## A fossil is not a record of modern hagfish behaviour {#s-b3b43e7980}
The marine deposit documents where BHI 6445 was buried, but the specimen does not directly establish diet, scavenging, preferred depth or the ecology of living hagfishes. One locality also cannot define the group's global distribution. The fossil therefore supports a bounded anatomical and phylogenetic comparison, not a complete ecological profile or a continuous history of the crown group.
