## Stems, leaves and flowers in the source description {#s-7327f5d280}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records stems with puberulous or silky indumentum. Leaves are ovate or lanceolate, petiolate and coriaceous. The inflorescence is described as racemose and dense; the flowers are blue or violet and puberulous. The fruit is falcate, and the habitat field records the category terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish character variation or a complete range.
