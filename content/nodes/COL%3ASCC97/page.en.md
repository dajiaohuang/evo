## Daytime refuges and active night perches {#s-339cfde65e}
The revision reported western-Andean Azuay localities at 2943–3662 m in páramo and western montane forest. Daytime specimens sheltered beneath rocks or in arboreal bromeliads up to 2 m high; nighttime individuals were active on low shrub branches up to 80 cm high or inside bromeliads. These encounters retain separate activity contexts rather than establishing a universal perch limit or breeding substrate.

## Gravid females and a January calling record {#s-71edac0a9f}
Gravid females were found in August and January, and calling males in January. The observations support reproductive condition in those samples, without defining the full annual season, observed egg-deposition sites or developmental mode.
