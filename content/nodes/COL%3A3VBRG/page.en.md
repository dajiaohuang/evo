## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-a6a07aa952}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stems with glandular or stiff, appressed hairs. Leaves may be opposite or in groups of three, with no stalk or only a short one; the elliptic blades are papery and hairy, especially underneath. Dense, head-shaped flower clusters have leathery bracts arranged in a spiral. The flowers have a tubular calyx, yellow corolla, and separate male and female flowers.
