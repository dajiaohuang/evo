## Beak-width comparisons do not replace feeding studies {#s-0131049be9}
Compare [Spatula clypeata](Spatula_clypeata/page.en.md), [Spatula rhynchotis](Spatula_rhynchotis/page.en.md) and [Spatula hottentota](Spatula_hottentota/page.en.md): mean beak widths at the nostrils are 18.6, 19.4 and 12.7 mm, from morphology samples of 8, 4 and 5 birds, with 4, 3 and 4 complete measurements. No filtration-efficiency or food-particle experiment is supplied, so width differences cannot directly establish efficiency. All four measured smithii birds are unsexed, leaving sex differences unsupported.

## Similar niches, different movement codes {#s-bdd2efa4e1}
[Spatula puna](Spatula_puna/page.en.md), [Spatula platalea](Spatula_platalea/page.en.md) and [Spatula querquedula](Spatula_querquedula/page.en.md) share a Herbivore aquatic niche, but their movement codes are sedentary, partially migratory and migratory. querquedula has Omnivore trophic level and the other two Herbivore. Feeding niche, trophic level and migration are different classifications. Then compare Aquatic predator/Omnivore in [Spatula clypeata](Spatula_clypeata/page.en.md) with Aquatic predator/Carnivore in [Spatula smithii](Spatula_smithii/page.en.md), retaining the source columns without forcing agreement. Continue with [Spatula cyanoptera](Spatula_cyanoptera/page.en.md), [Spatula versicolor](Spatula_versicolor/page.en.md) and [Spatula discors](Spatula_discors/page.en.md): all three have aquatic-plant niches, but their migration codes are partial, partial and migratory. The versicolor and puna compiled mass rows both give 595.21 g; their pages retain the values and explain that the original book has not been reconciled.

## Evidence limits {#s-c3aaf7697b}
Scope is the selected historical AVONET v7 rows, relevant field definitions, two name crosswalks and local COL accepted/synonym records. The whole workbook, individual raw measurements, Dunning’s book and a new full methods-paper reading are excluded. Morphology counts are not literature-mass denominators, and ecological categories are not seasonal diet surveys.
