## Accepted name and placement {#s-105e5fd80d}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus flavescens Noble, 1923 at species rank in Eleutherodactylus (Eleutherodactylinae, Eleutherodactylidae), in the release dated 20 August 2026.

## Reading route {#s-7423871c41}
The biological entries below come from an AmphiBIO row classified under Frost 2011. Its historical concept has not been reconciled with the current COL26.8 species record.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-f9f58b9761}
The row codes terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal foraging. These are broad settings in a compilation, not measured time budgets or evidence that all three are used equally. A later TetrapodTraits notice reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against that notice.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-f1c4e9f592}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult length of 41 mm. For an anuran, this is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a range for the species.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-2be4dae56c}
The row reports at most one reproductive event per year. The literature compilation does not specify sex, population, date or wild versus captive conditions for this value.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-4241953250}
The historical row marks direct development. The category does not by itself establish parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
