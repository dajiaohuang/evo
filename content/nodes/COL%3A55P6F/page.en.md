## What does Tetrameranthus umbellatus look like? {#s-023b58b2ca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes narrow, elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves, with smooth or sparsely hairy undersides. More than five flowers grow in each cluster. The sepals join at the base, while the fruit cluster comprises two to seven red or orange, oval segments, each with one or two seeds.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-9a70a3fc85}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a locality.
