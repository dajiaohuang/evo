## Accepted name and placement {#s-126d7b5356}
COL26.8 accepts Breviceps gibbosus (Linnaeus, 1758) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Brevicipitidae (family) → Breviceps (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading the older record {#s-c26f8aa8d5}
This page joins the accepted COL26.8 name to an older AmphiBIO entry. That entry follows the Frost 2011 source classification, and its equivalence to the current species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-9f1ffe7bd0}
AmphiBIO records fossorial, terrestrial and aquatic as foraging categories for its Frost 2011 concept of Breviceps gibbosus. These broad labels do not show when or at which life stage each applies. A TetrapodTraits revision (version 3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) reports corrections to selected inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries. This row has not been checked against those changes, so the categories remain historical source values; no replacement is inferred.

## Adult food categories — AmphiBIO source {#s-4b5ec9f199}
The same AmphiBIO row lists arthropods among the adult food categories. The compilation combines literature reports based on expert judgement, observation or stomach contents; this category neither describes a complete diet nor measures the share of each food type.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-29248aef3f}
AmphiBIO gives a compiled maximum adult body length of 80 mm for this source concept. In Anura, the field is snout–vent length, measured from the snout tip to the vent. It is a reported maximum, not a size measured in every individual.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-2ebf4cb9aa}
The row gives 30 as both the minimum and maximum reported offspring-or-egg count per clutch, and records at most 1 reproductive event per year. The compilation does not tie these values to a sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive setting; “offspring” in the size fields may mean young or eggs.

## Developmental strategy — AmphiBIO source {#s-960640706b}
AmphiBIO labels development as direct: the source term refers to development without a free-living larval phase. The record does not describe parental care, breeding site or seasonal timing, and its historical taxon concept remains distinct from the unverified current match.
