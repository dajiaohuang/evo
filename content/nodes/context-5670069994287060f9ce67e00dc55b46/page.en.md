## The source concept {#s-e54309f33a}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Silverstoneia flotator as source row Anf1512, under genus Silverstoneia, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat (small-scale habitat) entries carried forward from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so the listed category remains a historical AmphiBIO value; no replacement is inferred. The notice does not mean every original entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-753cb14234}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 18.3 mm. For Anura, the compilation measures body length as snout–vent length (from snout to vent); it uses total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a maximum compiled from literature, not a measurement for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-b5fabb7b61}
The row records 6 offspring or eggs per clutch (the listed minimum and maximum are equal) and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO compiles these counts and extrema from literature; this row does not identify the sex, population, measurement date or wild-versus-captive context for each value.
