## Leaves, flowers, and fruit {#s-ef653304a6}
The regional description records a solitary stem up to 30 m tall and 8–16 leaves. Each side of the leaf axis can carry as many as 175 pinnae, arranged irregularly in different planes. The inflorescence stands upright between the leaves. At the base of each small flower-bearing branch, the record notes two to five female flowers. The fruit is obovoid, has a smooth outer surface, and does not split open at maturity.

The source records a solitary stem up to 30 m tall; 8–16 leaves; up to 175 pinnae on each side, inserted in different planes and irregularly arranged; an upright interfoliar inflorescence; two to five pistillate flowers at the base of a rachilla; and obovoid, smooth, indehiscent fruit.
