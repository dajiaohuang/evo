## Two substrate categories in a regional entry {#s-2d98bf6979}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Bryopteris diffusa (Sw.) Nees as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its terms are source-labelled habitat categories.

One row is “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

A separate row is “Epixila,” a wood-associated substrate category. Neither entry identifies a collection site.

The source fields give no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

The two categories are the biological content carried from this regional record; they do not form a complete species account.
