## Cordia nodosa {#s-0475fb9f46}
## Long hairs cover the leaves and red fruits

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes long, coarse hairs on the stem and both surfaces of the elliptic to lance-shaped leaves. Leaf bases are rounded, and tips range from acute to long-pointed. The bisexual flowers are yellow, with smooth, bell-shaped calyces and tubular corollas, each under 1 cm long. The anthers lack glands; the stigma is head-shaped, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is red, egg-shaped, and hairy. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
