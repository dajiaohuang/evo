## Delosperma hollandii {#s-efc49152b8}
## White flowers rise above creeping two-angled stems

The Greater Cape flora describes a creeping, succulent perennial with stems to 30 cm long and two angled sides. Its leaves are held somewhat upright and end in a small point. White flowers grow in groups of three on stalks up to 15 mm long and reach 25 mm across. The five-chambered fruits are 5–6 mm across. The habitat entry is uncertain.
