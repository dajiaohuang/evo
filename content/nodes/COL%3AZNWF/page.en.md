## Crotalaria paulina {#s-2740a3abe5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Crotalaria paulina* records erect plant up to 1.5 m tall, simple oblong leaves, yellow flowers, black hairless obovate pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
