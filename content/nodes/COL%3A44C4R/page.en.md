## A long fibrous leaf and pale flowers {#s-e2cd65b32d}
Moraea doleritica has one long, narrow, channelled foliage leaf and pale-yellow flowers with spreading, slightly twisted tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals). The cited account records a low dolerite habitat and notes that its rigid leaf contrasts with the broader stem-clasping leaves of related species.

## Low dolerite outcrops {#s-42ebb6fa77}
The source says the species is evidently restricted to low dolerite outcrops.
