## A rock-associated source category {#s-9b92ea52aa}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Sphagnum molle Sull. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data with a habitat field attributed at dataset level.

That field is labelled “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

It is not accompanied by a collection site, survey date, full range or sampling-coverage information.

The habitat field is the only biological information included from this regional entry; it does not provide a full species account.
