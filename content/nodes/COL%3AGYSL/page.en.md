## Workshop vocabulary and later species naming {#s-053cb120a4}
The workshop drew an opportunity sample of 13 volunteer native isiZulu speakers, ages 18–55, from five Zululand areas. Ten took part on 28 November and three on 1 December 2016 at Tembe Elephant Park. Participants viewed frog photographs and discussed names as a group, settling disagreements through discussion. The six broad names elicited there did not distinguish all 58 species one by one. Species-level names were assigned afterwards with Bongani Mkhize, drawing on that vocabulary, earlier published names and naming guidelines. The group process does not provide individual response frequencies, agreement across isiZulu dialects or a test of how accurately participants recognized Arthroleptis stenodactylus.
