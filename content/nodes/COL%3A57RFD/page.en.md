## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d194a587e0}
COL26.8 records Trachylepis socotrana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-64dda6e65d}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 100 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-41e9ec62f3}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Saxicolous/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
