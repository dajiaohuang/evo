## What traits are recorded for Annona mammifera? {#s-bc82b3607a}
The field describes smooth bark and an upright or climbing trunk. Leaves have entire edges and long-tapering tips, with curved veins. Green flowers grow opposite leaves or at branch ends and have three petals with free sepals. The aggregate fruit is syncarpous, meaning its carpels are fused; seed color and surface are not specified.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-f0c7900771}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
