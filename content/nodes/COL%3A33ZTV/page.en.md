## What floral structures distinguish Dalechampia allemii? {#s-e57d6744d3}
The plant lacks a xylopodium, a woody underground root structure, and has woody stems. Its leaves are compound and divided into three leaflets. The flower cluster is surrounded by deeply three-lobed bracts. Resin glands are present; female flowers have sparsely lobed sepals and a cylindrical tip to the style column. The fruit is covered with stiff, appressed hairs, and the seeds are smooth.
