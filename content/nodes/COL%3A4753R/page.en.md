## Nerine filifolia: flower structure and growing conditions {#s-60dd01c952}
A 1974 regional account describes a small *Nerine* with a scape about 30 cm long. It records 8–10 pedicels and gives their length as “2-5 to 3-8 cm,” a range whose formatting is unclear in the excerpt. The perianth segments are 2.5–3 cm long and 3–4 mm wide. The very slender, thread-like leaves are about 1 mm in diameter. Hairs on the scape and pedicels are conspicuous. Filaments are about as long as the segments or slightly shorter. The segments are twisted and wavy for about a third of their length, are not as strongly recurved as in *N. filamentosa*, and tend to curve sideways and upward. No staminal appendages are recorded.
