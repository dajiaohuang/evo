## Brassia arachnoidea {#s-13df10d279}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 description, the rhizome is usually inconspicuous and the pseudobulbs usually lack sheaths. The lateral sepals are about 1.3–1.5 times the dorsal sepal’s length, while the dorsal sepal is about 1.3–1.5 times as long as a petal; the segments spread outward. The lip is an inverted, spatula-shaped form with a pointed tip. The habitat field says epiphytic.
