## Name, lineage and sample {#s-1fd293794e}
COL26.8 accepts Tricholaema lacrymosa in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 records it under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name: 11 individuals were measured, with complete morphological sets for 4. Trait-level sample counts can differ.

## Beak dimensions {#s-3f5295e55a}
Mean beak length from the anterior nostril edge to the tip is 13.1 mm; width is 7.5 mm and depth at that edge is 9.2 mm. These standardized dimensions are not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-59b9a2c6d3}
Mean unflattened wing length, from carpal joint to longest primary, is 68.8 mm; mean tarsus is 17.3 mm. A separate compilation reports mean mass of 22.9 g (source category Dunning); the morphology sample is not its weighing denominator.

## Ecological categories {#s-cb92890cd6}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, Frugivore trophic niche and Insessorial lifestyle. They are broad comparison labels, not statements of exclusive habitat use or diet.

## Migration and mapped range {#s-9e560e13ad}
The migration entry is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints -10.65° and 5.16°. They exclude a full account of non-breeding movement and do not estimate current position or global occupancy; geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Evidence limits {#s-5f24401c79}
AVONET v7 measurements are historical species averages for the listed samples, not individual ranges or identification criteria. Dunning mass is independently compiled and must not use the morphology sample count. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories; mapped latitudes cover historical breeding and resident ranges only. Crosswalks and names link records but do not prove historical concepts equal COL26.8. Reproduction, current population and conservation need other sources.
