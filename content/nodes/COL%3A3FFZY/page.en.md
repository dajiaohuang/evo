## Gazania linearis {#s-ebf8482a05}
## Tufted leaves carry yellow-felt undersides

Manning and Goldblatt's Greater Cape flora account describes a tufted perennial without an above-ground stem, with a woody rootstock up to 20 cm long. Dead leaf bases decay into a fibrous sheath. Most leaves are deeply divided, though some are linear-oblanceolate; the narrow leaflets range from linear to elliptic. Their undersides are white-felted, the upper faces sometimes rough, and the margins roll under.

Radiate heads are yellow or orange with a dark ring. The inner involucral bracts taper to a point. The cited habitat field records grassy slopes.
