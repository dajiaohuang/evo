## Auroralumina: what two branching tubes can tell us {#s-c7c80f3686}
On a bedding plane from Charnwood Forest, specimen BGS GSM 105875 preserves two branching tubes within a rigid, many-sided organic skeleton, with dense tentacle-like impressions. The dated succession places this Ediacaran fossil at about 562–557 million years ago. A morphology analysis used the preserved combination to test its position among cnidarians.

That placement depends on how the tentacles, periderm-like covering and single fossil surface are interpreted. The specimen does not record a measured feeding event, a direct ancestor, or a permanent global first appearance for cnidarians.
