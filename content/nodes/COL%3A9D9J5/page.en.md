## Leaflets {#s-04819621ee}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record lists 9–11 leaflets, broadly elliptic to ovate, with margins often entire and tips ranging from blunt to pointed. The leaflets and leaf axis are densely hairy, described as tomentose or pubescent. Each leaflet pulvinus (the small joint at its base) is under 6 mm long.

## Flower clusters {#s-f43bbc7341}
The panicles (branched flower clusters) are erect. Their axes and the calyx are described as covered in tawny, silky hairs. The bracteoles (small bracts near each flower) are oval to elliptic.

## Flowers {#s-0687285cb2}
Flowers are listed as larger than 13 mm, although the field does not identify which dimension is measured. The petals are pink. The standard (banner), wings and keel (the lower pair of petals) are all described as spatulate, or spoon-shaped.

## Fruit {#s-93a33fea45}
The fruit record notes no lateral wings on the seed-bearing portion. This describes the field’s specified part of the fruit, not every feature of its outline.

## Habitat category {#s-853c5e6e85}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
