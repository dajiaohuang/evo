## What alternatives are recorded for its leaves and spur? {#s-6778390110}
The source describes Utricularia tridentata with obovate or circular leaves and ovoid bladder traps that open at the base. The traps may have simple, straight appendages on both the back and underside. Its lilac corolla has a three-lobed lower lip and a bulging throat that may be yellow, orange or white; the straight, cone-shaped spur is longer than the lower lip and has a blunt tip. The fruit opens in two valves and the seeds are cylindrical. The listed alternatives are not one fixed form.
