## Anthurium simonii: morphology and terrestrial habitat {#s-75642de2ef}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls and fibers recorded at the stem base or along the stem. Petioles are marked smooth, but the compound shape field combines flattened, keeled and pointed states, so their form remains unresolved. Leaf blades are entire and flat, linear or ovate, with acuminate, apiculate or acute tips and acute bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is acute on both surfaces, secondary veins are impressed above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical or one-keeled peduncle and a flat, linear spathe at an acute angle to it; the spathe is not stipitate. The berry's apex is vinaceous; its base is recorded as white or green.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Compound petiole and berry-color states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
