## Hunan–Guangxi mountain records {#s-145042d5aa}
Male holotype HNNU 16SH02 was recorded from Shunhuang Mountain, Xinning, Hunan, at 882 m. The monograph summarized records from Shunhuang, Nanshan Forest Park and Huangsang in southwestern Hunan, and Huaping and Mao’er Mountain in northeastern Guangxi, at 880–1600 m. These named localities define the treatment’s reported scope rather than a continuous distribution or a tested elevation requirement.

## Guangxi measurements kept separate from the type list {#s-edc6e050e6}
The examined material comprised five adult males and one female from Huaping at about 1300 m, plus ten adult males and two females from Mao’er at about 1600 m. The diagnosis reported male snout–vent lengths of 28.9–37.1 mm (n=15) and female lengths of 38.4–42.5 mm (n=3). These measurements concern that examined Guangxi sample. The separately listed Hunan paratypes included a juvenile, which is not added to the adult measurement denominators.

## Reported dorsal and breeding-male characters {#s-2929c21e90}
The diagnosis described no vomerine ridges or teeth, a mostly smooth dorsum with scattered granules and tubercles, and rudimentary basal toe webbing without lateral fringes. Breeding males had nuptial pads bearing tiny, dense spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. These anatomical observations do not establish reproductive timing or the functional performance of the limbs or spines.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-a0d996a132}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys shunhuangensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
