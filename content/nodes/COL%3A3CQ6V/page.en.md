## How does the source distinguish Euphorbia pentops from E. brakdamensis? {#s-e7f4e6303d}
The account describes E. pentops by comparison with E. brakdamensis: its cyathium glands have a small round green center surrounded by a cream to pink hairy area, from which finger-like processes arise.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-ccbc6fcf3e}
The source records gently sloping or flat karroid areas on loam with scattered quartz gravel.
