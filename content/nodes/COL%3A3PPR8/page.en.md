## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-8ff59ce966}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records cylindrical branches with fine, very short hairs and stem lenticels (small pores). Oblong stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) fall away. The leaf stalk (petiole) is recorded as margined or channelled (with a groove); the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) as channelled or margined. Each leaf has three or four leaflet pairs. A stalkless, cup-shaped nectar gland (nectary) is recorded. Inflorescences are racemes (clusters of individually stalked flowers along a main axis), and flowers have stalks. The calyx (outer floral cup) is recorded as tubular or funnel-shaped. The pod walls (valves) are leathery; the fruit is listed as flat or curved and has a hairless surface. This is one regional dataset field and does not establish variation or how widely the traits occur.

## Habitat category {#s-6f4ee1cb51}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
