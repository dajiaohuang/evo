## Disa nivea {#s-b7f35ac18e}
The account describes Disa nivea as an upright, sub-robust plant 200–400 mm tall, with rigid, closely overlapping leaves. Its white or cream flowers often have red spots at the tips of the lateral sepals, and the horizontal spur is 25–40 mm long. It is recorded on sunny rock ledges or steep slopes of Cave Sandstone.
