## Ormosia stipularis {#s-809c03313f}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ormosia stipularis* as an accepted species of *Ormosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes 5–13 leaflets with dense hairs, lilac flowers longer than 13 mm, and flattened pods usually 2–4 cm wide with woody valves 2–5 mm thick. The seeds are listed as red-and-black or red to orange, up to three per fruit and up to 15 mm wide. The plant is recorded as terrestrial.
