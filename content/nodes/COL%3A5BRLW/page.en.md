## Orange stems and a loose flower cluster {#s-68c7efd06f}
The Flora of Panama describes *Voyria aurantiaca* as an herb to 18 cm tall, with one to several orange to purplish-yellow stems. The erect stems are slender, hairless, and round in cross-section; opposite stem scales are membranous and joined for about one-sixth to one-half their length. Loose, two-branched cymes carry up to seven flowers. Flowers have five parts; the colorless, bell-shaped calyx is 5–6 mm long, while the orange or yellow corolla is 15–20 mm long. The capsule is narrowly oblong, reaches 8 mm, and opens completely; the seeds lack wings.

## Regional distribution in the Panama flora {#s-7ca8f55591}
The Flora of Panama distribution field lists British Honduras, Panama, and Surinam. It gives regional names rather than individual collection localities.
