## Cybianthus nestorii Pipoly {#s-4644385640}
## Prominent veins and variable leaf tips

Cybianthus nestorii has hairy terminal branches more than 3 mm in diameter and leaves with no swollen nodes. Its leathery leaves are elliptic or lance-shaped, with a tip that may be rounded, sharp, blunt or notched. The midrib is prominent above and the secondary veins are visible beneath. Lateral or axillary racemes are reported. The corolla has four parts with pointed lobes, but the male and female flowers and their stamens are recorded as unknown. The immature fruit wall is globose and dotted.
