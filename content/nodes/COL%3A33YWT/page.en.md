## Narrow leaflets and cream-yellow flowers {#s-fa2210b1a9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records 13–25 pairs of narrow-oblong leaflets, sometimes up to 29, with blunt ends. Flowers are cream to yellowish; ten stamens are listed, with their filaments joined into one bundle. The oblong fruit has surface veins concentrated around the seed region. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
