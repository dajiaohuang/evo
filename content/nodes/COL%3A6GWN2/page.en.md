## A hard leaf edge and a fused embryo {#s-b0af33d5b0}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic leaves with wedge-shaped bases and tips that are acute or tapering. Their texture is listed as papery or leathery. The margins roll under and have a cartilage-like edge; the raised midrib on the upper surface is accompanied by one vein near the leaf margin. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles. Their supporting bracts persist, as do the small, inconspicuous bracteoles beside each flower. The flower cup is smooth and hairless; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth, with persistent inconspicuous bracteoles. One seed is recorded, and its embryo has fused cotyledons (the seed leaves).

## A terrestrial label alongside seed details {#s-2c090fd31b}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category is separate from the recorded seed and fruit traits.
