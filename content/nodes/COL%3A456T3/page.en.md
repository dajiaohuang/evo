## A grooved leaf midrib and sparse or absent hairs {#s-ae8f25f939}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes stem cork as smooth or grey. Leaves are elliptic, with acuminate, acute, or gradually narrowed tips and acuminate or wedge-shaped bases. They are leathery, with flat edges and a grooved upper midrib; the marginal vein is inconspicuous. The leaf hair covering is sparse or absent. Leaf stalks are grooved. The field does not quantify the listed alternatives.
