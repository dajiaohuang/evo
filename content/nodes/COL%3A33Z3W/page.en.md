## ## Obovate leaflets and an incompletely known flower {#s-60b5e4aacf}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records usually 5–9 pairs of obovate or oval leaflets, with values ranging from 3 to 13 pairs; the tips are acuminate and the bases wedge-shaped or rounded. Inflorescences may be racemes, panicles or fascicles. The corolla is cream to yellowish, but stamen number and several other floral details are unknown in the source. The oblong to oblong-elliptic fruit has surface veins only over the seed region. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
