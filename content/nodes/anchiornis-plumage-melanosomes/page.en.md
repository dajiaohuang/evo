## What melanosomes reveal about Anchiornis plumage {#s-5189d81421}
In the feathered dinosaur Anchiornis, specimen BMNHC PH828 preserves melanosomes—microscopic pigment-related structures—in its feathers. Comparing their shapes and distributions with measurements from living birds supports a reconstruction with grey, dark, white and rufous regions. The colours are statistical inferences rather than directly visible fossil colours, and preservation and reference samples limit the result; the reconstructed tail pattern came from a different Anchiornis specimen.
