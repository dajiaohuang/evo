## Leaflet pairs and twisting pods {#s-8f8cb997db}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records two to five pairs of elliptic to ovate leaflets on alternate, spirally arranged leaves. The yellow flowers have ten stamens; as the legume matures, its valves twist. Glandular hairs are listed on the stems and other structures. These traits come from one regional dataset entry, not a study of population variation.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-64e7c95c0d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. It gives no collection site or complete range.
