## Three-veined leaves and racemes {#s-421a87f5bb}
The morphology record describes a woody underground base and nearly stalkless leaves with three main veins. Leaf blades range from lance-shaped to elliptic, with margins that may be smooth, scalloped or toothed. Flower heads form racemes; the record includes male-only, female-only and mixed heads. The habitat field says terrestrial.
