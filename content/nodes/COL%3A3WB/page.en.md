The single Ernest account reports gestation, female age at first reproduction, and maximum lifespan. First reproduction is not first birth or sexual maturity. ## How to read the Ernest account {#s-8a5f0da988}
The Ernest record uses a same-name, same-family historical association and a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 mapping; equivalence with the current species concept has not been established. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally give literature inputs equal weight, which may include averages, individual values, or range midpoints; sample size does not determine weight and sources may overlap. The original source number remains on the species page, but underlying papers and wild, captive, and locality conditions were not reviewed consistently here.
