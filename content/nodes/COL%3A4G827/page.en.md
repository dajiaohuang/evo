## Philodendron dyscarpium {#s-b4758e832a}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes entire ovate-to-oblong leaves with an acuminate tip and rounded base. The spathe (bract) is recorded with green outer surfaces marked reddish and a red-purple inner surface; the spadix (flower-bearing axis) sits close to its base. This regional morphology field does not establish population variation or a complete diagnosis.
