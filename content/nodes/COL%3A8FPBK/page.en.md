## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-ac478d7371}
COL26.8 records Stegonotus sutteri as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-58947b13ac}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 8.95. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The row reports 2.5 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015.
