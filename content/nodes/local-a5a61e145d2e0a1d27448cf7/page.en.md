## The source tadpole concept {#s-1289492ce9}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Aromobates alboguttatus, in the genus Aromobates and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying work prints the name Nepbelobates alboguttatus, which the compilation retains separately. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-7d8f43bba7}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images; its supplied stage label is 30. That is a label attached to a source record, not a full developmental timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
