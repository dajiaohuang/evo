## Rhipsalis ormindoi {#s-6ab824daac}
## Pink flowers and ovoid fruit on terminal segments

The cylindrical segments end in smooth tips, and their mature areoles are unarmed. Distal segments remain terminal; flowers appear at the tips on hairless areoles. The corolla is bell-shaped, with pink filaments and pink-to-red perianth segments. The exposed pericarpel is shorter than those segments. Fruits are pink and ovoid, without areoles. The source labels the plant epiphytic but gives no host or collection locality.
