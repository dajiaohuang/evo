## *Selaginella ventricosa*: creeping stems with three leaf forms {#s-2bb42559b0}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, pale, hairless stems creep along the substrate and lack joints. Root-bearing rhizophores arise in the leaf axils. Small leaves differ by position: side leaves are ovate-oblong or broadly ovate with rounded bases; upper leaves are ovate-round or broadly ovate with slanted or blunt bases; axillary leaves are oblong with blunt bases and pointed tips.

An idioblast, a specialized cell type, is recorded in both side and upper leaves. The profile supplies these regional morphology characters but no habitat or locality claim.
