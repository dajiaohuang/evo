## Source classification and concept status {#s-6362084765}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3072 records *Afrixalus nigeriensis* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (aquatic and arboreal) {#s-1cf4d361c1}
AmphiBIO records aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Seasonal activity {#s-5915827ddf}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and cold conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-9e132b74c1}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 35 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-d10fe8d85a}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for this row. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-38038e6ab9}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
