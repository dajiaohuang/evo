## What leaf and flower traits are described? {#s-f2d0ff9bd3}
This procumbent herb has linear to lance-oblong leaves about 45 by 8–10 mm. The underside shows a prominent net of veins; the upper margin is finely toothed, while the lower margin is entire. The leaf base is wedge-shaped. Fine grey star-shaped hairs give the surface a slightly rough feel and a blistered appearance above. Each inflorescence has two or three flowers, which are dark pink.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-d34356248f}
The regional account reports grassland. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
