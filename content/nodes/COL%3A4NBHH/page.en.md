## How to read reproductive records {#s-02850235c2}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode, a consolidated mean of 3.5 offspring or eggs per litter/clutch, and 1 litter or clutch per year. A clutch/litter summary may be the midpoint of reported means or, when means are unavailable, the midpoint of reported extremes; it is not necessarily an arithmetic average of observed clutches. The fractional mean and annual count are dataset summaries, not fixed values for every event or individual. These fields do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
