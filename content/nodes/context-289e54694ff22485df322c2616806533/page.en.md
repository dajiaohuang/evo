## Name and classification in AmphiBIO {#s-90a7bea0c8}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Petropedetes euskircheni as row Anf4460. The compilation assigns it to genus Petropedetes, family Petropedetidae, and order Anura, using Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. These traits describe the deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some body-mass and microhabitat entries from earlier compilations; this row-level comparison is not captured here. Check the historical categories before reuse. No replacement is inferred, and the notice does not make every original entry erroneous.

## Maximum adult body length: 62 mm {#s-7ad5705937}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length of 62 mm. For Anura, this means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, total length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.
