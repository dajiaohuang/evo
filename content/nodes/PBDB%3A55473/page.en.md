## Recorded parent of Aceratheriinae {#s-e252b95868}
Aceratheriinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
