## How are its leaves and bracts arranged? {#s-ff74b51f18}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records basal leaves in two ranks; young leaves are listed as unornamented. The flower stalk arises at the same node as the leaves. Its fertile bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are spiral and not red or orange. Each small flower cluster has two flowers and two membranous bracteoles.
