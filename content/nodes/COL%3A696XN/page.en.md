## Calopogonium velutinum {#s-9f04fa37aa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Calopogonium velutinum* records rust-colored velvety stem hairs, triangular stipules, asymmetric side leaflets, rigid flower-cluster axis. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
