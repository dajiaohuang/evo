## What leaf and flower traits identify Stephanopodium gracile? {#s-2f0d2a52be}
Young branches and stipules become nearly hairless. The leaf stalks exceed 5 mm; blades are elliptic, obovate, or oblanceolate, with tapering tips and smooth upper and lower surfaces. The midrib is flat above and raised beneath. Leaves are thin to slightly papery. Flowers arise at the leaf-stalk tips on stalks under 2 mm long, joined at the base. The calyx lobes are alike and hairy underneath; the corolla is as long as the calyx, with a hairless inner tube. The ovary is hairy.
