## *Anemia pyrenaea* {#s-dbfabb2ff7}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, linear, pinnate (side divisions arranged along a central axis); spores (reproductive cells) — granular. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as rock-dwelling (Portuguese: Rupícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
