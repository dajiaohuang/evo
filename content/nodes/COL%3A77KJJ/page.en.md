## Survey records {#s-cb56825146}
The type specimen was collected at Mabobi on the left bank of the Lualaba. Field notes report that patrol teams began recording red colobus identified as P. parmentieri in and near Lomami National Park in 2016–2017, extending the known local record southward. The 2021 action plan summarizes this as a range extension but also notes that the species was not found in 2007 surveys north of Ubundu and calls for more southern-range surveys. The record history remains patchy.
