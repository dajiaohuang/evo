## Chamaecrista machaeriifolia {#s-4f8fd31ec2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista machaeriifolia* records usually 4–9 leaflet pairs, four broad yellow petals plus one hood-shaped petal, legumes 2.8–5.3 cm long. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
