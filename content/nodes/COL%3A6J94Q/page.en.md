## Fleischmannia laxa {#s-61621c1c52}
## Lilac florets on a broad panicle

The branches spread open and carry broadly ovate or narrowly ovate leaves with glandular or non-glandular hairs. The inflorescence is a broad panicle; its flower heads sit on bristly stalks. The corollas are lilac, and the pappus—the bristly crown on the fruit—has bristles with cylindrical bases. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
