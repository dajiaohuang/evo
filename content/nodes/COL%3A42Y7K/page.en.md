## *Microlicia furnensis* {#s-de85952492}
The field describes stems with bristly hairs and spherical glands. Leaves may be stalked or sessile, have three veins, and carry similar gland-tipped bristles. Flowers are solitary; the hypanthium is oblong to bell-shaped and bristly. Sepals range from oblong to triangular, bear spherical glands, and are at least as long as the hypanthium. The corolla is pink or cream, the anthers match its colour, and the ovary has three chambers. These are recorded alternatives.
