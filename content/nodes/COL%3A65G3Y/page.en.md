## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9bec70e136}
COL26.8 records Afroedura rondavelica as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-82f09f79b4}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 55 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a6d424dfa0}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bars-Closel et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d7ad2f9b45}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
