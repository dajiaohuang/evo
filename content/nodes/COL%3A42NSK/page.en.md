## A tube-shaped flower cup and hairless leaves {#s-7c56aae8ee}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records no hairs on young stems and no hairs on the leaf underside. The leaves are elliptic or lance-shaped, taper to a point, and have wedge-shaped bases. Their margins are entire and lack fringe-like hairs; three main veins arise above the base. Flowers form compact clusters. The flower cup is tubular, with a persistent calyx whose lobes end flat. Each flower has five petals. The tissue connecting the anther's pollen sacs is not prolonged; its appendage is recorded as absent or as one tooth on the back-facing side. White anthers have one small pore, and the ovary bears hairs. The alternatives for the appendage are source entries, not a quantified account of variation.
