## Inga saffordiana {#s-1272841a9a}

The Flora of Panama describes a slender tree with 8 or 10 leaflets per leaf and long-stalked glands on the leaf axis. Its flowers are incompletely known; the densely dark-hairy pod is about 30 cm long, nearly 3 cm wide and described as holding about 18 seeds. The recorded locality is Panama.
