## *Phlegmariurus mollicomus*: needle-like leaves on an epiphyte {#s-1a9b37ba11}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional profile lists this species as epiphytic, meaning its recorded substrate is another plant. Stems are described as hanging, straight or arched. Its small leaves are needle- to awl-shaped, spreading, recurved or pressed to the stem; the leaves bearing sporangia are listed as like the vegetative foliage.

The sporangium, a spore case with two similar valves, is kidney-shaped or globose; spores are pitted. The habitat category is a regional substrate record and does not name a host, locality or date.
