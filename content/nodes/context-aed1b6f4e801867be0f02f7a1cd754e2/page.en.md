## Name and classification in AmphiBIO {#s-5ab9cf5319}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Xenophrys sangzhiensis as row Anf3832. The compilation assigns it to the genus Xenophrys, family Megophryidae, and order Anura, using Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. These traits describe the deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Maximum adult body length: 54.7 mm {#s-fccf78372e}
The compilation lists a maximum adult body length of 54.7 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
