## What do its leaves and flower parts look like? {#s-8f9d4348fa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description gives Leptohyptis macrostachys an oval leaf with a heart-shaped base and a densely woolly underside; its petiole is 1–2 cm long. The blade is recorded as rough-textured and up to 2.0 mm long. Inconspicuous, ovate to oval-lanceolate bracts and bracteoles are 4–5 mm long, and the corolla tube is 6–8 mm. The source does not state the sampling basis for these measurements.
