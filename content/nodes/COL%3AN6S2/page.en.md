## Local ecology and population genetics {#study-account}
A North Adriatic survey sampled 32 sites and used DNA barcoding to confirm Branchiostoma lanceolatum. Adults were consistently present, with reported densities above 300 individuals per square metre at some sites. The authors observed breeding in early spring and possibly late fall in this population; this is a local observation, not a species-wide calendar.

A separate whole-genome study analyzed 36 wild adults from Roscoff and Banyuls in France. It estimated average heterozygosity of 2.73% and reported minimal differentiation between sites. The authors interpret this as consistent with gene flow and long-distance larval dispersal; larval movement itself was not tracked.

## A scoped research note {#study-preview}
Studies report a North Adriatic population survey and a genome-wide comparison of 36 adults from two French sites. These local samples do not define the species’ full range.
