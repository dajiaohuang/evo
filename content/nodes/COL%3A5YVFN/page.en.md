## Cinnamodendron occhionianum {#s-b1e90d6c8d}
## Ciliate wine-red sepals surround the flowers

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records leaves with straight tips and flowers borne singly or in small bundles. The sepals have fine hairs along their edges and are wine-red. The petals are unequal in size; the anthers are equal, and their connective ends bluntly. The stigma is papillose, and the female flower structure has two parts. The fruit is stalked and globose. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
