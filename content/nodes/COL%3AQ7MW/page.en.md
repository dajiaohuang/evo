## *Calyptrion arboreum* {#s-397f4a780b}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — lance-shaped, elliptic, saw-toothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
