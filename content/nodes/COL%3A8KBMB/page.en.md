## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Oreotrochilus cyanolaemus Sornoza-Molina, Freile, Nilsson, Krabbe & Bonaccorso, 2018 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
