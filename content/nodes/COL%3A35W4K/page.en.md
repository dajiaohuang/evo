## Catalogue placement {#s-d5b9c6f95b}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 lists *Dicymbe stipitata* as an accepted species in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-3264f8658a}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrestrial. It gives a habitat category only, without a locality, range or sampling coverage.
