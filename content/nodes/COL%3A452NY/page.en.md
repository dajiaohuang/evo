## Measured beak dimensions {#s-2cbebd7ee0}
Recorded mean beak measurements are 12.9 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip, 7.1 mm in width and 5.2 mm in depth at that edge. These describe separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-6e84be8060}
Mean unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary is 80.4 mm; mean tarsus length is 20.1 mm. Compiled mean mass is 17.7 g, with source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information; this does not mean every morphological specimen was weighed.
