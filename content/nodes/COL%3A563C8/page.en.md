## *Thamnochortus fruticosus*: spreading rhizomes and velvety stems {#s-e9c98051e2}
This rhizomatous perennial often spreads widely and reaches 60 cm. Its simple stems may carry tufts of sterile branches—branches that do not bear flowers—and the plant surface is velvety-hairy.

It is usually recorded on stony slopes below 1,300 m.
