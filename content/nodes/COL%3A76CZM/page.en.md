## Compiled adult body lengths {#s-e1a77f851d}
The 2019 compilation reported mean snout–vent lengths of 47 mm for females (29–59 mm; three populations) and 48 mm for males (29–61 mm; twelve populations). These population counts are not individual sample sizes. Colour and dorsal markings varied; the compiled ranges are not invariant diagnostic limits.
