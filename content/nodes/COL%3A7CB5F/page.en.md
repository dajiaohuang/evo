## Reusing an existing genome rather than adding a field sample {#s-657168fba8}
The 2019 study compares an existing genome of Tinamus guttatus with eleven added palaeognath genomes. Existing material retains its own provenance: vouchers for the ten newly sequenced living birds are not assigned to this species. Sequence counts describe marker coverage, not multiple independent individuals of this species; the two companion papers share the comparison material.

## Flighted tinamous are nested within palaeognaths {#s-bec54f30ea}
This species is one of four tinamous in the 2019 analyses. Sequence analyses recover tinamous as sister to little bush moa, nested among lineages traditionally called ratites. Flightless palaeognaths therefore do not form a natural group excluding tinamous. Their flighted condition informs comparisons of flight-loss history; the genome tree does not measure this species’ flight distance, frequency or field behaviour.
