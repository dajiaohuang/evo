## Soft twisted leaves and nodding flowers {#s-6f9d1335ad}
Ixia nutans has two narrow, soft-textured basal leaves, about 3 mm wide, that twist slightly. Its nodding pale-mauve flowers have a 6–7 mm tube and partly exserted filaments.

## Sandstone outcrops {#s-5be6010955}
The regional source records sandstone outcrops as habitat.
