## Life-history evidence limits {#s-acc1b27f5a}
The life-history values come from historical same-name records in the 2015 archive. They are compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical taxa may share or inherit data; matching names, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. In EltonTraits 1.0, the 100% invertebrate food weight is standardized from literature descriptions, not a direct diet measurement; its ABC confidence code may include estimates borrowed from an ecologically similar congener. Ground category G is a categorical assignment, not a proportion of time among strata. Activity periods are withheld under source code D1 because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; the original values and specific reason remain in the source record and do not establish a definite activity period. Ref_54 and metadata:Nowak1999 identify catalogued sources, not independent observations. Individual and seasonal variation, behavior beyond these fields, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
