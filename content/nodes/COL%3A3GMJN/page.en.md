## What leaves and flowers are described? {#s-df5b8d622e}
This slender, spreading shrublet grows to 80 cm. Its alternate linear-lanceolate leaves are accompanied by similar-looking leaves around the flower cluster. A few to eight pale yellow or yellow-green flowers occur at branch tips. The flower tube is minutely hairy and bears eight membranous petal-like scales.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-6afb6f8da2}
A regional account reports dry slopes. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
