## Hereroa odorata {#s-81bb34543e}
## Yellow flowers with red tips rise on erect shoots

The Greater Cape flora describes a small, sprawling shrublet with erect flowering branches. Its mostly subcylindrical, glaucous leaves spread or lean outward. Three flowers grow on each branch on stalks up to 10 mm long. The flowers are yellow with red tips and reach 22 mm across. The habitat record is shaly flats.
