## Recorded parent of Sinolagomyinae {#s-32b34101ad}
Sinolagomyinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Ochotonidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
