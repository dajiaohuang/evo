## Dapingian spores record plants on emerged land {#s-f8ba2d1ce8}
A palynological sample of fossil spores from one biostratigraphically controlled horizon of Argentina’s Zanjón Formation contains five cryptospore genera. The study reports both single spores (monads) and groups of four (tetrads), either naked or enclosed in envelopes, and dates the assemblage to about 473–471 Ma. This supports the presence of embryophytes on emerged land. The spores do not identify a crown-bryophyte lineage or pinpoint where and when land plants originated.
