## Many small leaflets and a hairy pod {#s-d7186a9575}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records 10–16 pairs of leaflets, each 5–21 mm long and 2.5–16 mm wide, with heart-shaped bases. Yellow flowers have 10 stamens. The pod is 2–3.5 cm long and 0.6–0.7 cm wide; its surface is densely hairy, with short bristles along the seam. The separate habitat field says terrestrial. The regional record supplies no sampling frame, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status.
