## Limits of the selected Kepodactylus records {#s-9eb08a06f0}
This page groups selected PBDB occurrence guides under Kepodactylus. It is not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological account. Counts refer to occurrence and collection records, not fossil individuals, populations, or sampling intensity. An outer age bracket is a database bound, not a measured origin or extinction date. The PBDB path is one database arrangement and should be kept separate from COL concepts and other phylogenetic hypotheses.
