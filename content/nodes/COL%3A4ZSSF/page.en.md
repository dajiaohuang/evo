## What does the regional description say about Stemodia verticillata? {#s-4660d77794}
A Flora of Panama description portrays it as a small herb, erect or sprawling, up to 15 cm tall. Its opposite leaves are broadly egg-shaped with prominent teeth. Blue or purplish flowers with a white center grow singly in the leaf axils; the flower has four stamens arranged in two pairs. The rounded capsule opens along both its chambers and partitions, while the seeds are ridged lengthwise.

## What range does the flora report? {#s-dcf8d5dc4b}
The cited flora reports the species from Mexico to Argentina and the Antilles. This is the range stated in that regional source.
