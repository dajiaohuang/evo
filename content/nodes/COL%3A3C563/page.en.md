## A hard leaf edge and two flower-cluster forms {#s-efa8de00da}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or oblong, leathery leaves with acute or blunt bases and tips that are acute, blunt or tapering to a point. Their margins roll under and have a cartilage-like edge. The grooved midrib, or main leaf vein, on the upper surface is accompanied by one or two veins near the leaf edge. Flowers are listed as solitary or in a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis; the field labels the raceme “auxotélico” without explaining that qualifier. Supporting bracts persist, while the smaller bracteoles beside the flowers fall away. The smooth flower cup bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth, and its bracteoles fall away. Seed number is unknown; the embryo is described with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves).

## A terrestrial label without range coverage {#s-97b99ae46a}
The habitat field says “terrícola,” or terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This label does not provide a range statement.
