## What traits does the record list for Manihot grahamii? {#s-ea04620060}
The record lists non-tuberous roots with a rigid, shapeless xylopodium. Stems may be 1–2.9 m or over 3 m tall, smooth and hairless, with white latex. Simple, stalked leaves may be lobed, with oblong or fiddle-shaped lobes; stipules are threadlike and hairless. Terminal or lateral bisexual panicles have basal female flowers. Involucral bracts are bristle-like, hairless, and inconspicuous, about 0.1 cm wide; male buds are ovate and female buds pyramidal, both hairless. The capsule is round, smooth, hairless, and unribbed. Seeds are oblong and smooth, with a caruncle.
