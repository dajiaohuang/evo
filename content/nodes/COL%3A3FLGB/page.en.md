## What distinguishes the recorded flower and seed forms? {#s-b4c5f4faa3}
The Genlisea flexuosa entry describes a rosette-forming stem without tubers and thin, slightly membranous, spatula-shaped leaves. Its violet corolla is 15–20 mm long excluding the spur, a narrow projection from the flower; the spur is straight and parallel to the flower stalk. The capsule opens lengthwise, and the seeds are rounded or angular-prismatic with a net-like surface pattern. These traits come from a source description that gives no sample size or population comparison.
