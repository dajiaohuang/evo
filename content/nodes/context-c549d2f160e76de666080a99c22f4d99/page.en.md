## The source concept {#s-2818643b6f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0032 records Allobates humilis in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat field: Ter=1 {#s-ed508bd2da}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). The fields Fos=NA, Aqu=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Body_size_mm: 21.8 mm maximum adult body length {#s-416fc10541}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=21.8 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Reproductive_output_y: maximum 1 event/year {#s-e6e800916f}
The row lists Reproductive_output_y=1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-509ccf1305}
Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
