## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-c6af9a772e}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 43 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-2f5050f54b}
AmphiBIO lists 4–37 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to one reproductive event per year, and offspring or egg sizes of 2.3–2.5 mm. These are compiled literature counts and extrema; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value. The size range may refer to offspring or eggs.
