## ## Star-shaped hairs and twisted pods {#s-63fa46b021}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records 3–6 pairs of elliptic to oblanceolate leaflets and bulbous, star-shaped glandular hairs on the stems, leaves and inflorescences. Racemes occur at branch tips and in leaf axils. The source marks the sepals, stamens and petals as unknown, so flower details are not available in this record. The oblong pod valves twist at maturity and bear yellowish, villous and bristly hairs; the seed is depressed-obovoid. The xylopodium is listed as unknown, and the plant is classified as terrestrial.
