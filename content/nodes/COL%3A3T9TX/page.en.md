## Lepidaploa bahiana {#s-c8ee82aaa2}
## Two-ranked leaves and lilac flower heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright stems with silky to bristly hairs and glands. Petiolate leaves sit in two vertical rows; their blades are elliptic to lance-shaped, pointed and narrowed at the base. Both faces bear hairs and glands, with the surfaces becoming less hairy in places.

Flower heads are borne on leafy stems rather than a leafless scape. The bell-shaped involucre has non-overlapping bracts, with pointed inner ones. Each head contains 20–30 lilac florets. The pappus is brown, and the fruit is glandular. The source habitat field classifies the plant as rupicolous, growing on rock.
