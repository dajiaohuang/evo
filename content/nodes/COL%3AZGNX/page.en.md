## Crinum acaule {#s-2636d481e4}
Crinum acaule grows from a 7–12 cm bulb with a short neck and long, narrow, channelled leaves. Its mostly underground flower stalk bears one or two large, funnel-shaped flowers; the white tepals are pink-flushed and often have deep-pink keels. The regional account records sandy grassland as habitat. The long floral tube and nearly ground-level stalk are central features of this description.
