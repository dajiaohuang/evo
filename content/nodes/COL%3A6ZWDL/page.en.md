## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-fad4837b25}
COL26.8 records Storeria victa as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-b0e6a29b3c}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 12.9. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015.
