## Source classification and concept status {#s-349e9a039c}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3040 records *Hylodes perplicatus* under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-04e5762f74}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and aquatic as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-2d4fe184b7}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; night-time activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-cfd6b4ef9d}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and warm conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-94158269b8}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 45.1 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-5e37bbfa4e}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year for this row. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental strategy {#s-62c1043aec}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
