## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-26bffec95f}
COL26.8 records Micrurus putumayensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-ec29dd0843}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 805 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-fff7c62eac}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
