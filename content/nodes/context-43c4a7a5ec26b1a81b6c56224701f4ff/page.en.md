## The source concept {#s-24b096b09f}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Craugastor vocalis as row Anf1340, classified in Craugastor, Craugastoridae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-aeb09ccbe1}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 58 mm for Craugastor vocalis. In the compilation, body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
