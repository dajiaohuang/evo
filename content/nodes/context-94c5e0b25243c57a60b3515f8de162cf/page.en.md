## Source classification and concept status {#s-c3a38070fd}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2976 records *Phasmahyla timbo* under the source classification Phasmahyla (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging category (arboreal) {#s-54def5decc}
AmphiBIO records an arboreal category for this row. It is a broad category, not a fine-scale habitat description. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-1901e05560}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 35.8 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
