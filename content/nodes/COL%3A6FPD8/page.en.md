## What floral structure is recorded for Ephedranthus boliviensis? {#s-af2fcf1a2a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes papery leaves whose shape ranges from narrow elliptic to ovate. A single flower is recorded per inflorescence. Each flower has six leathery petals and three triangular to broadly oval sepals. The fruit segments are ellipsoid.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-5a082a2b4c}
The field categorizes it as terrestrial, without further habitat detail.
