## Leaves and stipules {#s-33a686915c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes compound leaves with three leaflets. The leaflets are oblanceolate, oval or oblong, hairy on both faces, and have entire margins. The free distal portion of the stipules (appendages at the leaf base) is reported as 3.3–56 mm long; the record does not explain this wide span.

## Flower clusters {#s-c6b60f63ac}
The source describes the inflorescence bracts as deciduous and longer than 10 mm.

## Flower color {#s-5e1ed2a6d0}
Flower color is recorded as blue or violet.
