## Scales and fin supports in an early ray-finned fish {#s-306e9e1981}
Eifelian Cheirolepis material from Scotland preserves a combination of internal and external features. The named specimens show peg-and-socket scales, an imperforate propterygium—the leading basal support of the pectoral fin—and an imperforate hyomandibular shaft in the skull’s jaw-supporting region. These details let the study compare the fish’s endoskeleton as well as its outer covering.

The fossils document an early actinopterygian anatomical combination; they do not make Cheirolepis a fixed blueprint for all later ray-finned fishes. The value of the sample lies in the specific structures preserved and the comparisons they permit, not in treating one early genus as a direct ancestor of the whole radiation.
