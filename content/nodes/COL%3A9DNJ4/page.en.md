## *Tachigali vulgaris* {#s-0b698338bf}
The field records vestigial stipules, absent domatia, and up to 10 leaflet pairs. Leaflets range from oblong, elliptic, to ovate; texture ranges from leathery to papery, and lower surfaces may be hairless, softly hairy, or bristly. Flowers are up to 10 mm and radially symmetric, with 10 similar stamens; fruits are elliptic-oblong. Alternatives follow the source field.
