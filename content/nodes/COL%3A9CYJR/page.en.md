## A single leaflet pair and axillary flowers {#s-c40cd174dc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records leaves arranged in two rows, each with one pair of obovate to oblong or elliptic leaflets. Nectar glands sit on the leaf stalk. Yellow flowers appear singly or in small groups beside the leaves; ten stamens are listed. The mature pod sides twist, while seed form is recorded as unknown.
