## *Phlegmariurus hemleri*: fertile leaves like the ordinary foliage {#s-9a8d6057c2}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, stems are upright or lie along the ground at the base. The small linear-lanceolate leaves are attached down the stem, recurved or pressed close to it; the record also allows equal-sized or more widely spaced leaves. The leaves bearing sporangia are described as like the vegetative foliage.

The two valves of a sporangium, the spore case, are similar; the case is listed as kidney-shaped or globose. Spores are pitted or furrowed. These are regional flora characters, and the listed alternatives are not population frequencies.
