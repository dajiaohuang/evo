## Corymbiform or paniculate clusters {#s-21d9dea837}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records tendrils as present or solitary and spines as absent. Petioles are 5–10 mm long; leaves have three to five veins, and the underside axils of the basal veins are not bearded. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are listed as corymbiform or paniculate. The corolla is tubular or salver-shaped, with a tube longer than its lobes; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 2–4 cm in diameter, with a subwoody fruit wall 1–2 mm thick. The source gives the usual seed count as three to five and describes the coat as crust-like.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-6d86c473bc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Strychnos rondeletioides* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
