## What these historical records can tell us {#s-d7ff2e8184}
COL26.8 lists Mallomys gunung as an accepted species in the family Muridae. The EltonTraits 1.0 entry discussed here is an older, same-name record, organized around feeding and foraging position. Its taxonomy follows an earlier scheme; the matching name and family do not settle whether that record represents the species as classified today.

## A description of feeding, not a measured menu {#s-22e8d70b59}
The only nonzero food category is “other plant material,” at 100%; that source category also includes fungi. EltonTraits converts descriptions in the literature into relative weights rather than measuring what an animal ate. Its ABC code combines high, reasonable and partial confidence, and an estimate may draw on a congener judged ecologically similar. It therefore does not show that every item was observed in this species, or that the percentage is a measured share of its diet. A zero category means no weight was recorded there, not that the food is never eaten.

## A broad label for foraging position {#s-db16680aed}
The entry assigns Mallomys gunung to Ar, the arboreal (tree-associated) stratum. The accompanying A code records the compiler’s confidence in the source and assignment. The archive metadata attributes mammalian stratum labels collectively to Nowak (1999). This is one broad category, not a measure of time spent there, and it does not mean the animal uses no other position.

## Following the source labels {#s-af7172857d}
The feeding and stratum fields carry the labels Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. The archive warns that feeding, position and activity may come from different material, and that the same Ref number in its bird and mammal tables does not identify the same work. Original labels are retained by file, row and field group. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so the labels are trace information rather than additional observations.

## Limits of the foraging record {#s-3e7b311fea}
This is a guide to a historical EltonTraits entry, not a complete account of the species. A shared name and family do not prove that its older taxonomic concept matches COL26.8. The food weights summarize literature descriptions; they are not measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. The ABC code can include an ecologically similar congener, so the page does not treat every field as a direct observation of Mallomys gunung. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read. EltonTraits withheld activity periods under source code D1 because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; those values are not used here. Current range, population and conservation status need other sources.

The PanTHERIA 1.0 entry is linked through the MSW05 name in its August 2008 table. Its biological data gathering ended in June 2004. ## Two consolidated body-size values {#s-31521f94e1}
The table reports adult body mass of 2,041.47 g and adult head–body length of 442.03 mm. The values were adjusted for sex and then summarized by medians. The mass field can include adults or animals of unspecified age but excludes pregnant females; head–body length does not include the tail. These are consolidated historical estimates, not measurements of one specimen or the limits of a species-wide size range.

## What the ecological counts represent {#s-03b5693324}
PanTHERIA classifies feeding as herbivorous and records one diet category and one habitat layer. A diet count distinguishes resource classes, not individual food species. Habitat layers are positions below ground, at ground level, in water or above ground; they are not habitat types such as forest or grassland.

## Statistics derived from a historical range map {#s-8d5439a611}
PanTHERIA reports 710.81 km² for area derived from a historical range map and latitude endpoints from −4.09° to −3.69°. This map area is not a measured home range and does not show that the species occupied every point inside it. The endpoints are not a list of collecting localities, nor do these historical statistics establish current distribution or population size.

## How to read the PanTHERIA summary {#s-71f6767f80}
This account paraphrases the historical PanTHERIA 1.0 WR05/2008 entry. Continuous traits are generally consolidated medians; some were adjusted for sex, definitions or methods with a generalized linear model (GLM). Missing values are not filled from fields marked *_EXT, which are extrapolations. Depending on the variable, the input can include wild, captive, provisioned or unspecified conditions and primary, secondary or extrapolated sources. The reported values are not individual ranges, population counts or species diagnoses. Same-name links through MSW93/MSW05 and COL establish an association, not equivalence after later taxonomic splits or lumps.
