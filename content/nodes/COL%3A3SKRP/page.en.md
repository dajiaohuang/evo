## ## Winged branches and many-flowered racemes {#s-24e65107b3}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology row records branching as absent while also describing the branches as winged, with thin, flattened extensions along their edges. The stipules, small structures at the leaf base, are semi-sagittate, or partly arrowhead-shaped. Leaflets are elliptic (oval) or lance-shaped, with prominent veins. The leaves bear tendrils, slender structures for attachment, described as bristle-like or divided into three tips. A raceme, a flower cluster arranged along a central axis, has more than eight flowers, bluish to purple in color. The stigma, the pollen-receiving tip of each flower, is recorded as divided in two.
