## Syagrus mendanhensis {#s-e7ef97e2a2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records Syagrus mendanhensis with a solitary, very short or underground stem and green pinnate leaves. Its erect, stiff leaflets are grouped at different angles; the inflorescence is a panicle, and the ovoid to ellipsoid fruit is yellow-green with hair near its tip. Habitat is listed as terrestrial.
