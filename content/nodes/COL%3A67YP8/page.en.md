## Babiana noctiflora {#s-a03d926ed9}
Babiana noctiflora resembles B. odorata in its broad leaves and pale-yellow flowers, but has a taller, inclined stem and a much longer floral tube, about 33–50 mm rather than 10–14 mm. The regional account places its flowering later, from mid-September to mid-October, and says the scented flowers remain open at night and are adapted for moth pollination. Rocky outcrops in renosterveld are the recorded habitat. Timing and pollination are reported in the cited regional treatment.
