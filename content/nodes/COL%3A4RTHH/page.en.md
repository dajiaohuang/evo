## How do its male and female flower clusters differ? {#s-0543944426}
A regional species account describes *Restio mkambatiae* as a mat-forming plant 0.4–0.6 m tall that spreads by stolons. A male inflorescence has one, occasionally two, spikelets (small flower clusters); a female inflorescence has one. The male spikelet contains three or four flowers, while the female spikelet has three to six. The account records the seed as unknown.

## What does fire do to its wet habitat? {#s-6e4cab77ca}
The account places the species on sandstone beside streams, in marshy grassland, permanently wet ground and seepage zones. It says these habitats burn regularly and that the plants apparently regrow from the base after fire. The source presents the regrowth tentatively.
