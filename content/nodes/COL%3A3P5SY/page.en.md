## Hysterionica matzenbacheri {#s-b6e659264f}
## White rays and two rows of pappus bristles

The Brazilian Flora description records an annual herb 10–22 cm tall, with branched roots and sessile leaves 50–105 mm long. The leaf blades are entire or incised and hairy on both surfaces. Solitary radiate heads sit at branch tips; white pistillate ray florets surround yellow bisexual disc florets. The pappus has two rows: short, flattened outer bristles and longer barbellate inner bristles. The compressed, silky cypselae are 1–1.2 mm long and have two lateral ribs.
