## Name and classification {#s-eb1b91ffc8}
COL26.8 lists Platypelis saikamavo as a species. Its recorded classification path is Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Platypelis (genus). This entry reflects the catalogue release dated 20 August 2026.

## A species described by its yellow flash marks {#s-418cbd3b4f}
The 2024 paper formally describes Platypelis saikamavo sp. nov. from rainforest on the Tsaratanàna Massif and at Bemanevika, across 1,000–1,530 m above sea level. Its abstract reports an isolated phylogenetic position and an uncorrected mitochondrial 16S pairwise distance of at least 4.5% from every other nominal Platypelis species. The abstract describes a body size of 28–31 mm snout–vent length, distinct superciliary tubercles, and bright yellow areas at the forelimb insertion and posterior abdomen that extend as flash marks toward the inguinal region. This account is limited to those abstract statements; it does not add sex-specific measurements, site-specific elevations, calls, or reproductive observations.
