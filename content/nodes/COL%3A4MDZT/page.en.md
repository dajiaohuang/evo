## Printzia polifolia {#s-aa1dcaac96}
## Decurrent leaf bases on a woolly shrub

SANBI describes a stiffly branched, white-woolly shrub 1–2 m tall. The obovate leaves become nearly hairless above, have weak teeth and wavy edges, and run down the stem at their bases. Solitary radiate heads have yellow centres and mauve-blue rays. A taxonomic note identifies the decurrent leaf bases as a key character. The recorded habitat is flats and hills, mostly on clay below 500 m.
