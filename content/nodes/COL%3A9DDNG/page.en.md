## Muellera longiunguiculata {#s-623bb595d9}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muellera longiunguiculata* as an accepted species of *Muellera* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description gives a 2–5 m height and leaves with 5 or 7 leaflets. Its pink or lilac flowers have a pseudo-monadelphous androecium; the fruit is described as a winged legume with papery to leathery texture, and the seeds are kidney-shaped.
