## Evidence limits {#s-8062890569}
This page reads AVONET v7 as a historical dataset. Morphological figures are species averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. The mass value comes from a separately cited literature or weighing source, so the morphology sample counts do not describe its sample. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories; the mapped geography covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. A one-to-one historical crosswalk and matching catalogue name connect the record to COL26.8 but do not prove that the older species concept is equivalent after later splits or lumps. Reproductive biology, current populations and conservation status need other sources.
