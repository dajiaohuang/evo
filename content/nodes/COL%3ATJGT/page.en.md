## ## One leaflet pair and bulbous glandular hairs {#s-031a914859}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records one pair of oblanceolate to narrowly obovate or obovate-elliptic leaflets. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stem, leaves, inflorescence and outer sepal surfaces. Terminal racemes bear yellow flowers with ten stamens and petals of different forms. The fruit valves twist at maturity and have glandular and non-glandular hairs; the seed is depressed-obovoid. The xylopodium is listed as unknown, and the plant is classified as terrestrial.
