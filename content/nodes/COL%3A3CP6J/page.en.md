## What leaf and flower traits are listed for Euphorbia hirsuta? {#s-6aa8b23984}
This erect perennial herb reaches 1 m. The sessile stem leaves are lance-shaped or oblanceolate, finely toothed, and hairy; at the base they are at least half as wide as the blade. Flowers are yellowish green.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-b35c84c9ae}
The source lists disturbed places as its habitat.
