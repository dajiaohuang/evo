## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton fulvus? {#s-fa4f4cfa27}
Young branches and lance-shaped leaves have outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked leaves have brochidodromous veins, which loop near the margin; their edges may be entire or finely toothed. Nectar glands are absent, as are leaf-like stipules at young branch tips. Terminal thyrses bear unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are shortly stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are shortly stalked, with separate sepals, equal calyx lobes, and a two-lobed style. Female petals are threadlike, and the bud arrangement is valvate. The fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
