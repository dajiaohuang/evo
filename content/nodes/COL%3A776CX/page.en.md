## Accepted name and placement {#s-f7d151a973}
COL26.8 accepts Petropedetes newtonii (Barboza du Bocage, 1895) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Petropedetidae (family) → Petropedetes (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Records reconsidered under a revised name {#s-c7239afcca}
The revision recognizes *Petropedetes newtonii* on Bioko and the southern coast of Cameroon, while assigning older mainland records to *P. vulpiae*. Earlier locality labels alone do not establish which mainland frogs belong to the revised species.

## The 2003 Bioko field observations {#s-28c3b0056c}
Field notes from Bioko in late November to early December 2003 describe frogs collected mostly at dusk near shallow streams, on rocks, leaves or branches. Three juveniles were found by day, including one away from immediate water. No calls, mating, eggs or parental care were observed, so these visits do not establish a universal activity or stream-distance pattern.
