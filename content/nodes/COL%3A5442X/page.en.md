## A regional record of bark association {#s-6f719eee5d}
The accepted species usage for Syrrhopodon ligulatus Mont. in COL26.8 maps to one World Flora Online name and authorship record.

Its linked Brazilian Flora entry is a regional dataset record, and its habitat row has dataset-level attribution.

The row records “Corticícola,” a bark-dwelling substrate category.

No locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage accompanies this field.

This single habitat row is the only biological content carried by the regional record; it is not a full species description.
