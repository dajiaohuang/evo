## Melocactus conoideus {#s-43d70950e3}
## A conical stem, bristled cephalium and magenta flowers

The source records a conical stem with white pith, dark-green skin and angular ribs. It has straight spines, nine or more in total; central spines exceed 1 cm, while the lower radial spines are 2–4 cm. The cephalium is longer than wide, with conspicuous bristles and no brown tuft. Flowers are magenta, with up to 23 perianth segments. Fruits are lilac to magenta and no longer than twice their diameter; seeds are at most 1.5 mm with a convex seed coat. Rocky and terrestrial habitat labels give no locality.
