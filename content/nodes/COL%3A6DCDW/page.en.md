## *Disynaphia variolata*: five-flowered heads and smooth fruits {#s-4f9bbb5f78}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a perennial herb with spatula-shaped or linear leaves. Both leaf surfaces are listed as becoming less hairy or bearing glands. Its inner bracts have blunt tips, and each head is recorded with five florets. The fruit is hairless. The dataset also carries both rock-associated and terrestrial substrate categories without giving collection localities.
