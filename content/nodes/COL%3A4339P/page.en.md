## Micropholis guyanensis (A.DC.) Pierre {#s-8b0a15d054}
## Unisexual flowers and a vinaceous fruit

Micropholis guyanensis has elliptic to oblong-elliptic leaves arranged in two ranks or spirally. Its flowers are unisexual, with five sepals and four or five corolla lobes; the corolla is bell-shaped or tubular. Four or five stamens are enclosed within the corolla, and lance-shaped or awl-shaped staminodes and a nectary disk are present. The style extends beyond the corolla. The fruit is vinaceous, with one smooth-coated seed.
