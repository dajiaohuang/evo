## Catalogue placement {#s-5bc0930cef}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 lists *Harleyodendron unifoliolatum* as an accepted species in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-1ca9f43e26}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the species terrestrial. This records a habitat category only; it supplies no locality, range or sampling coverage.
