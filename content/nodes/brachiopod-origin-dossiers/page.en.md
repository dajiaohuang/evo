## The brachiopod-origin dossier links separate tests {#s-1d7f5ce0f6}
This reading guide links character tests of *Micrina* and *Yuganotheca*. It is an editorial reading path, not a taxon, direct-ancestor sequence or global brachiopod first-appearance claim. The former 530–518 Ma envelope is withheld because the component taxa retain their own source-bounded records and the route is not a lineage duration.
