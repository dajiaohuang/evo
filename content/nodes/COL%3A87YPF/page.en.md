## Begonia itatinensis {#s-2f5f4d38a4}
## Lobed leaves and strongly unequal capsule wings

Begonia itatinensis has erect stems and persistent stipules. Its slightly asymmetric basal leaves are lobed, lance-shaped or elliptic, with serrated or ciliate margins. Simple hairs occur on the petiole and may occur on either leaf surface. Male flower segments may be smooth or glandular; female segments are glandular, with entire margins. Bracteoles are present. Capsule wings are strongly unequal and the seed tip is blunt. Habitat is labeled terrestrial.
