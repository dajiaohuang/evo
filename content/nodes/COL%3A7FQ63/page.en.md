## Vernonanthura phaeoneura {#s-003ae38856}
## Woolly lower leaf faces contrast with silky hairs above

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes erect stems covered in velvety to woolly hairs and lacking glandular dots. Petiolate, membranous leaves range from lance-shaped and ovate to oblong, with acute or tail-like tips and tapered or wedge-shaped bases. The underside is finely woolly to villous; the upper face has stiff or short fine hairs. Glands occur on both faces, and the secondary veins arch toward the margin before fading.

Involucral bract tips are blunt or rounded. Each head contains 8–22 florets; the corolla is hairless and lacks glands. The pubescent dry fruits have no glands, and the outer pappus bristles are cylindrical. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
