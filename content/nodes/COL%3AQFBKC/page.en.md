## Reported localities and elevations {#s-e156d2007a}
The original description reports grassy-meadow localities on Ethiopia’s Didda Plateau and in the northern Bale Mountains at 2,429–3,296 m. Named places include areas around Assela, Huruta, Dinsho, Adaba, Dodola, Goba, and Chole. ## Calling across reported seasons {#s-d498cec69d}
Males were heard calling in April and June, described as dry-season months, and in July and August, described as rainy-season months; calls were heard by day and at night. Calling sites included stream banks and side pools, often from ground holes or beneath low vegetation. These observations do not establish year-round breeding or the timing of egg laying and larval development.
