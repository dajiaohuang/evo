## Yellow funnel flowers and basal leaves {#s-d46fca60fc}
Flora do Brasil describes Sisyrinchium brasiliense as having fleshy roots, an inconspicuous rhizomes (horizontal underground stems) and flat basal leaves. Its winged flowering stalk bears yellow funnel-shaped flowers; the fruit is globose to nearly globose.

## Terrestrial growth {#s-f9294b0a03}
The flora classifies the species as terrestrial (growing on land rather than on another plant); the record does not provide a more detailed habitat description.
