## ## Bulbous hairs and compound racemes {#s-359c5df072}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium and usually 5–9 pairs of oblong-elliptic to oval or nearly round leaflets, with the source giving extremes of 4 and 12 pairs. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stem and inflorescences. Compound racemes grow at branch tips and in leaf axils. The yellow flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms. The pod valves twist at maturity and bear glandular hairs; seed characters are listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
