## Measured beak dimensions {#s-83c6e08f18}
Mean beak length is 17.7 mm from the anterior nostril edge to the tip; width at that landmark averages 5.7 mm and depth 5.4 mm. These standardized dimensions are not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-d9a2a1fc79}
Mean unflattened wing length (carpal joint to longest primary) is 125.2 mm; mean tarsus is 19.2 mm. AVONET lists separate mean mass of 52.4 g in the Dunning source category, not as a measurement of every specimen.

AVONET classifies habitat as Shrubland, trophic niche as Frugivore and lifestyle as Insessorial (perching-associated). These broad labels do not imply exclusive use of shrubland or fruit.
