## How does its thread-like flower stalk rise above the leaves? {#s-dfd38cbdaf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes *Xyris hilariana* with two-ranked or nearly two-ranked leaves. Their dark-brown sheaths widen at the base, sometimes into ear-like lobes; the flat blades are ridged. The thread-like flower stalk is 20–50 cm long and has one or two ridges. Its ovoid or obovoid spike bears 10–25 flowers; the source lists alternative states for several traits.
