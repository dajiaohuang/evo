## The source concept {#s-7644d63b66}
In AmphiBIO v1, Platymantis diesmosi appears in row Anf1092, classified in the genus Platymantis, family Ceratobatrachidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited record; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-de175fe72c}
For Platymantis diesmosi, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 52.7 mm. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs and toads (Anura), and total length for salamanders (Caudata) and caecilians (Gymnophiona). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
