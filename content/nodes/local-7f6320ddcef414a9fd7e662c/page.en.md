## Measurement material {#s-0899bb4f23}
The compilation lists four measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of four distinct specimens. The records retain specimen identifiers, sex codes and references. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.
