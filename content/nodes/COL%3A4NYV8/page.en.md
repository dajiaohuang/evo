## What tree, leaf and fruit traits are recorded for Pseudolachnostylis maprouneifolia? {#s-cd84ced051}
This deciduous tree reaches 18 m. Young shoots and leaves are nearly hairless. Simple ovate to almost round leaves are green to bluish green above. Flowers are greenish yellow. The spherical fruit is at least 20 mm across and pale yellow when ripe.

## What habitats are recorded? {#s-b2f374b27a}
The source lists woodland, bushveld and disturbed places.
