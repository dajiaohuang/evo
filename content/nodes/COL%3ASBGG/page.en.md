## One leaflet field and a pink, spurred flower {#s-10f9ae732f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record contains two leaf codings: it calls the leaf pinnate (leaflets arranged along a central axis) but also marks the rachis absent and the blade one-foliolate, with oval or arrow-shaped leaflets. We retain that combination as the source presents it. The field also records pink flowers with a calcar, a projecting floral spur, and straight pods whose valves are hairless to finely hairy. A separate habitat label says terrestrial; this regional row gives no sampling frame or locality.
