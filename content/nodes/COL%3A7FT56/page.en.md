## *Voyria spruceana* {#s-d9d6b30f29}
## Color and flower form in the flora record

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records roots as reddish or yellowish, with sparse scales; stems as simple or branched and yellow, orange, or reddish; and leaves as yellow, orange, or red. Flowers are solitary. The calyx is tubular, with four or five parts, and is recorded as yellow or reddish; the corolla is also yellow or reddish and has four or five parts. Corolla-lobe forms are listed as linear or triangular, and separately as lance-shaped, with tips recorded as acute or rounded/blunt. The stigma is head-shaped. These are the field’s alternative categories, not evidence that every listed color or form occurs on one individual.
