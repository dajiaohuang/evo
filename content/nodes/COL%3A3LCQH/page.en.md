## Heteropsis tenuispadix: leaf and floral descriptors {#s-64b3271280}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field lists clasping, feeding and subcylindrical root forms. Stem descriptors include subcylindrical and striate, with a tortuous form, buds and a pointed apex. Petioles are channelled, with sinuate margins and a geniculate bend, and are dark green. Leaf blades are elliptic or ovate, subleathery or membranous, with acuminate or attenuate tips and cuneate, rounded or sinuate bases. The color field lists light- and dark-green states but does not clearly assign them to the upper and lower surfaces. The midrib is grooved; the field also includes “1” beside its reference to an external marginal collecting vein, without clarifying what the number counts.

The inflorescence is listed as axillary or terminal. Its axis is subcylindrical with buds; small persistent leaves or their absence are both recorded. The peduncle is cylindrical. The deciduous spathe is ovate or elliptic, cream, pale yellow or white, with an acuminate tip. The ellipsoid spadix has an acute tip and is cream or greenish cream; the stipe is cylindrical and yellow. Floral descriptors include 4 free stamens per flower with ovate anthers, a truncate gynoecium, an incompletely bilocular ovary, anatropous subsessile ovules with translucent mucilage, subbasal placentation and a subdiscoid stigma. Berries are subcylindrical or subobovoid and yellow or orange. Seeds are obovoid with orange mucilage and a rugose seed coat.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; it does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Several concatenated alternatives and the “1” value remain unresolved.

The dataset records the category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
