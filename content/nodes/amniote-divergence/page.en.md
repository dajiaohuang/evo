## What separates Synapsida from Sauropsida? {#s-3a5541b0ad}
Start with the skull, not a fossil marking a single moment of division: the cited phylogenetic analysis distinguishes Synapsida from Sauropsida by diagnostic skull openings. It uses the tree to infer reproductive and skeletal changes, then compares those reconstructions with Carboniferous body fossils.

The earliest amniote record is sparse, and several Paleozoic placements shift among analyses. The recorded 320–300 Ma window is a broad context; the divergence date depends on the tree and its calibration.
