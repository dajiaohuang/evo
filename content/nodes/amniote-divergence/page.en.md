## Early amniote skulls mark two major branches {#s-3a5541b0ad}
What distinguishes the early synapsid and sauropsid branches? The cited analysis uses diagnostic skull openings to separate these lineages, while reproductive and skeletal changes are inferred from phylogeny and compared with Carboniferous body fossils. The earliest members are rare, several Paleozoic placements vary among analyses, and the exact divergence dates depend on tree calibration.
