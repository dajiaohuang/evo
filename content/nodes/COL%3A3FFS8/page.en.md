## How does this species differ in its leaves, flowers and fruit? {#s-7a203bc44d}
The stems are densely covered in long simple, star-shaped or glandular hairs. Ovate to narrowly ovate leaves have toothed margins, pointed or rounded tips and nine to eleven veins from the base. The calyx is 10–15 mm long, with a prominent central vein and a long-tailed tip. Dark yellow petals are 20–42 mm long, with a wine-colored basal mark; the staminal tube is 9–10 mm. The fruit is a depressed-spherical schizocarp partly enclosed by the calyx, with 20–35 blunt or rounded segments (mericarps). The internal tongue-like structure and its teeth are absent. The seed surface bears star-shaped hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-60cda756b8}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
