## *Mikania selloi*: gland-dotted leaf undersides and small stipules {#s-dabe14b355}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records climbing branches with short hairs and a hollow pith. Opposite leaves are lance-shaped or egg-shaped, with entire margins, tapered or blunt bases, and short pointed tips. The upper surface is hairless; the lower has glandular dots. A pair of interpetiolar stipules is present but under 1 mm long. Loose panicles have spikelike branches and nearly stalkless heads. The tubular corolla limb is shorter than its tube, and the fruitlets are hairless.
