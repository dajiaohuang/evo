## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-75451eb726}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Kassina mertensi* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hyperoliidae (family) → Kassina (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.
