## Babiana planifolia {#s-e20934bf09}
Babiana planifolia has nearly flat, lanceolate to oblong leaves and mauve flowers with yellow-green nectar guides. The regional account notes that the leaves may be nearly hairless or sparsely long-haired along the veins, while the stem and bracts are usually smooth. It distinguishes the species by its relatively fine corm tunics and cobwebby to woolly lower leaf sheaths. Rocky slopes are the recorded habitat; the description reflects the cited regional material.
