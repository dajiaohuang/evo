## Ternstroemia dehiscens Huber {#s-7e12d03e2f}
## Single axillary flowers and uncertain fruit opening

Ternstroemia dehiscens has alternate, leathery, hairless leaves with an abruptly tapering tip. The lower half of the leaf margin is entire and the upper half crenulate. Each leaf axil bears a single flower; the five sepals persist on the fruit. The source records the fruit as either indehiscent or opening by valves, without resolving the difference. The seed is elliptic and curved at the tip; the stamens were not observed.
