## ## A small-leaflet count and twisted pods {#s-9725e39e94}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium and 2–6 pairs of elliptic to obovate-elliptic leaflets. Bulbous glandular hairs occur on the stems, leaves, inflorescences and outer sepal surfaces. The terminal racemes bear yellow flowers with ten stamens and petals of different forms. The fruit valves twist at maturity and carry glandular hairs; seed characters are listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
