## Philodendron myrmecophilum {#s-b40b6b6f03}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes entire ovate leaves with an acuminate tip, a heart-shaped or arrow-shaped base, and 6–9 lateral veins. Its habitat labels include growth on other plants, hemiepiphytic growth, and soil. The source does not map where each label applies or give a complete range.
