## How are the leaves, flower spikes and fruit described? {#s-977598c317}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes obovate to nearly round leaves with rounded or blunt tips and acute bases. They are papery and hairless, with 7–9 pairs of secondary veins, rarely five; small hair tufts occur in some vein axils, while glands are absent. Elongated spikes arise from leaf axils or branch tips and bear bisexual flowers. The lower floral cup is spindle-shaped and the upper part bell-shaped, with five conspicuous calyx lobes. Ten stamens occur in two whorls; the filaments are long and slender, and the anthers nearly round. The style is hairless. The fruit has two rounded to elliptic wings, rarely four. This is a regional morphology field and gives no sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-ed35fc2256}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial, with no locality or collection date.
