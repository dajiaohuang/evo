## What traits are recorded for Annona calcarata? {#s-e84eadc274}
The field describes an upright trunk with fissured bark and leaves with entire edges, blunt tips, and curved veins. Green flowers grow in the leaf axils or opposite the leaves. Each flower has three petals; the sepals may be free or joined at the base. Fruit aggregation and seed color and surface are left unspecified.
