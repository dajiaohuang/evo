## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5193fde6b2}
COL26.8 records Caledoniscincus notialis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-fdf54471dc}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 57 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-11f0bd3227}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 6.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
