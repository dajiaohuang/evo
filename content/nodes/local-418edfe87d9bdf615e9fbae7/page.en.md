## The deposited caecilian record {#s-be398caf29}
The fourth version of CaecilianTraits lists Gegeneophis pareshi in the genus Gegeneophis and family Grandisoniidae. It keeps individual, population and species records separate. Whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept has not been established.

## Measurement material {#s-fcb9e19576}
The compilation lists 16 measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level and none at population or species level. These row counts are not estimates of population size or proof of 16 distinct specimens. Each record retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-5cdd306fb1}
The compilation labels the broad habitat “Terrestrial” and the microhabitat “Fossorial.” These are source categories: they do not show how often each setting is used or describe the species’ complete geographic range.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-d1db51a45f}
At species level, the compilation gives a primary-fold count of 148. This value may summarize records rather than describe one animal; it does not establish that every individual has the same diagnostic count.

## Water-dependent larval development {#s-b29b7fbf2d}
The compilation marks a water-dependent larval stage as absent. This is the dataset category described in its accompanying data paper; it does not mean adults never use water. The reproductive metadata and original reference are needed to understand the observation’s scope and qualifications.
