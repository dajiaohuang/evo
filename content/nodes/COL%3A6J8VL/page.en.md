## Fleischmannia tysonii {#s-6af6764988}
## A hairy perennial shrub with purple heads

The description records an erect perennial shrub up to 2 m tall. Its opposite, broadly ovate leaves are papery, up to 9 × 7 cm, and densely hairy beneath; three prominent veins arise near the base. A broad, flat-topped panicle carries compact heads with 20–25 florets, and the corollas are lavender to purple. The mature achenes, or dry fruits, are black with black ribs and many bristles, mostly on the ribs; the pappus is a tuft of 22–25 narrow bristles. The distribution field restricts it to Volcan Chiriqui, Panama.
