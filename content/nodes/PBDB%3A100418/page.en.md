## Recorded parent of Victoriapithecidae {#s-b9ffdbe7bd}
Victoriapithecidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Cercopithecoidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
