## Saurauia rubiformis Vatke {#s-86cef1b27e}
## Large leaves and white flowers; fruit not seen

Saurauia rubiformis is a shrub or a tree with several trunks, reaching about 15 m and densely hairy. Its large, papery to nearly leathery leaves are obovate to elliptic, 13–30 cm long, with serrated margins and 15–25 pairs of lateral veins. The underside bears tufts or clusters of hairs, sometimes star-shaped. Large branched inflorescences carry many flowers; each flower has five white petals and 26–41 stamens. The cited account did not see fruit. Its reported range is Guatemala, Costa Rica and Panama.
