## Northern Guangdong and southern Hunan localities {#s-78b0b9d0f8}
Male holotype SYS a004710 came from Shimentai Nature Reserve, Yingde, Guangdong, at approximately 370 m. The examined series also included Nanling material at about 1160 m and a Tianjing female at about 990 m. The distribution summary included Shimentai, Luokeng and Nanling in northern Guangdong and Mangshan in southernmost Hunan at 210–1200 m. The examined specimens and the wider summary are separate evidence sets and do not demonstrate occupancy at every intervening elevation.

## Twenty males and one female {#s-cb9d1a92cf}
The monograph examined twelve male types, eight additional Nanling males and one Tianjing female. The diagnosis reported male snout–vent lengths of 25.8–30.6 mm (n=20) and one female at 34.5 mm. The single female value is retained as one measurement, not a female range or a population estimate of sexual size differences.

## Tiny skin spines and population-marking differences {#s-ff9614f9a4}
The diagnosis described tiny dark spines across the dorsum, flanks, dorsal limbs, cloacal region and rear of the hindlimbs, with rudimentary basal toe webbing and narrow lateral fringes. It distinguished dark upper-eyelid stripes in the Yingde population from an incomplete interorbital triangular marking in the Ruyuan population. Breeding males had weak nuptial pads with densely arranged olive-coloured spines on fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. The reported marking variation is preserved within this treatment; it is not converted into a species-delimitation conclusion.
