## *Neocabreria catharinensis*: glandular fruit tips {#s-ccd5c2da33}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records narrowly elliptic or inversely lance-shaped leaves, with the upper surface hairless or becoming hairless. The inner bracts have blunt tips, and the receptacle beneath the florets is hairy or sparsely hairy. The fruitlets have glands near their tips. These are regional dataset fields; the substrate category is only “terrestrial,” with no collection locality.
