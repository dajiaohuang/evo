## Temperature studies represented in AmphiTherm {#s-46595725a2}
The pinned AmphiTherm dataset uses a 2018 source classification. Under that scheme, Cyclorana matches 3 deposited species concepts and 4 estimate rows: CTmax, 3 rows across 2 source concepts; Tpref, 1 row across 1. CTmax and CTmin are critical temperature-limit measures; LT50_hot and LT50_cold label hot and cold lethal-limit assays; Tpref records thermal preference. These measures come from different experiments and cannot be combined into one tolerance range. Counts overlap and do not represent distinct species or animals. The source life-stage code is adults. These entries describe study coverage under the source classification, not the physiology of every currently accepted species in Cyclorana.
