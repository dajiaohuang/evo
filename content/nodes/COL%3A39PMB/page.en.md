## Encyclia spiritusanctensis {#s-4ddf4821d6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes Encyclia spiritusanctensis with a conical pseudobulb, lanceolate leaves and a double raceme. Its hanging spatulate petals frame a folded, wavy-edged middle lobe of the lip; side lobes may overlap it. The habitat category is rupicolous.
