## Strudiella: one specimen, opposing insect diagnoses {#s-440e9d690c}
The Strud specimen was first described as the nearly complete Late Devonian insect Strudiella devonica. A later primary re-investigation rejected the insect identification, treating it as an arthropod whose placement cannot be secured as an insect. The two studies therefore record a real disagreement over the same fossil. The case is useful for reading how diagnoses change, but the batch keeps both interpretations visible rather than using Strudiella as a secure insect calibration.
