## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Cinnyris usambaricus Grote, 1922 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Systematics and Biogeography of Double-Collared Sunbirds From the Eastern Arc Mountains, Tanzania {#study-account}
Bowie and colleagues compared morphology, plumage and mitochondrial DNA in the Eastern Arc double-collared sunbird complex. Their Nectarinia mediocris usambarica samples came from four named localities. They reported a divergent mitochondrial lineage and proposed species status for N. usambarica. This is one study's test of a taxonomic boundary; its historical name is retained without assuming older database labels identify the same concept.
