## Ebracteola derenbergiana {#s-7ebf3c6214}
## Pink flowers rise from a compact tuft of three-sided leaves

The Extra Cape flora describes a compact, tufted succulent perennial about 100 mm tall, with a thickened taproot. Its stiff leaves are three-sided and compressed laterally, with rounded edges and a dotted surface. Flowers are pink, pale toward the base, and sometimes white. Filament-like sterile stamens and fertile stamens form a white cone around a ring-shaped nectary.

The five-chambered fruit persists in the centre of the plant. It is funnel-shaped, narrows into a stalk, and has broad wings ending in an awn; the closing bodies range from small and round to larger and egg-shaped. The habitat record is finely grained soils.
