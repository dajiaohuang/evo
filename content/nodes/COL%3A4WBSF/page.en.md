## *Selaginella flexuosa*: asymmetrical side leaves and long-awned upper leaves {#s-ccb8da6106}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes prostrate or partly upright green to pale stems, without hairs or joints. Root-bearing rhizophores may occur on the lower or upper side. The side leaves are oblong with asymmetrical bases; upper leaves are elliptic and end in a long bristle-like point. Axillary leaves are lance-shaped to ovate-lanceolate.

The source lists epiphytic, rock and soil substrates, without a named host, locality or date. These are broad categories in a regional record; the listed leaf states do not measure population variation.
