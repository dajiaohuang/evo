## Accepted name and placement {#s-e545d03ed3}
COL26.8 accepts Amolops sangzhiensis Qian, Xiang, Jiang, Yang & Gui, 2023 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Ranidae (family) → Amolops (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
