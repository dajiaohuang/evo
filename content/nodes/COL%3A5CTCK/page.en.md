## Adult mass is a compiled summary {#s-5a55ec6189}
The archive reports an adult body-mass summary of 11.8 g. The adult field can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex, so it is not a range of individual masses. Birth or hatching mass is not included in this value.

## Clutch size and hatching interval {#s-7f25d8ded2}
The archive summarizes offspring per litter or eggs per clutch as 3.5 offspring or eggs, and gives 12 days from oviposition to hatching. The first field combines litter and clutch counts, so its fractional summary does not describe a single brood. The interval refers to one stage and should not be read as a general reproductive cycle.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-de34091206}
These are historical summaries from the 2015 life-history archive. The values shown are reported medians or means of the two middle records, not measurements of one bird or ranges of individual variation. The compilation may share or inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family support the link used here but do not prove that the archival concept equals COL26.8 or remove inherited components from the summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and the underlying publications have not been reviewed individually. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and fields not shown here require other sources.
