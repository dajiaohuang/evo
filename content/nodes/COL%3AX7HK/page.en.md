## Cololobus rupestris {#s-55c4ec63b9}
## Brown hairs persist on a single stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes one stem with persistent brown hairs. Outer involucral bracts turn outward at the tip and sit at the top of the head. Style branches are 2 mm long, and corolla lobes are as long as they are wide. The source habitat field classifies the plant as rupicolous, growing on rock.
