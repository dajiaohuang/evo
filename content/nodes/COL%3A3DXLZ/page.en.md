## Felicia rosulata {#s-90f57b9d66}
## A basal rosette with a single blue head

The source records a perennial herb up to 300 mm tall. Each unbranched stem carries one head; the elliptic to obovate leaves form a hairy basal rosette. The involucral bracts are equal and arranged in three rows, and the achenes are shortly hairy. The flowers are blue; habitat is labelled grassland.
