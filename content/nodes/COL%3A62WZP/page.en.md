## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-bd41245d8d}
COL26.8 records Leptotyphlops at genus rank, with Leptotyphlopinae as its direct parent.

The authored ReptTraits v1-2 route has three same-name, same-family records with field citations: Leptotyphlops conjunctus, L. distanti and L. telloi. The workbook follows The Reptile Database’s 2023 names; COL26.8 supplies the current navigation. A matching name and family do not by themselves establish taxon-concept equivalence.

## Keep length measures distinct {#s-affc47cc6c}
L. distanti is the only one of these three records with a maximum snout–vent length (SVL): 143.5 mm, attributed to Jacobsen (1982). The other two have maximum total lengths (TL): 253 mm for L. conjunctus and 180 mm for L. telloi, both from Feldman et al. (2016). TL includes the tail, so it is not an SVL comparison; none of these compiled records gives a genus-wide range or average.

## Scope of the authored route {#s-e927cad841}
These three authored records are not an exhaustive descendant list or population survey. The maxima are compiled reports, not averages or identification thresholds. Historical workbook names and current COL placement are separate taxonomic views; check each field’s own source and scope.

The two routes provide different kinds of coverage. Compare only fields present on both pages, retaining their measures and citations; a TL value cannot be substituted for SVL. Missing values are not borrowed from relatives, and record counts do not show that traits are complete.
