## ## Hirsute branches and velvety fruit {#s-92a0e24cc4}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records cylindrical branches with long, stiff hairs and no lenticels. The deciduous stipules are lanceolate; the petiole is cylindrical and the main leaf axis is winged. Each leaf has one or two pairs of leaflets and stalked, head-shaped nectaries. Racemes bear flowers that may be stalkless or borne on short stalks; their calyces are bell-shaped. The fruit is flat, with leathery valves and a velvety surface. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
