## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for this tree? {#s-e8e4adba8f}
The record lists no buttress roots and gives a trunk height of 10 m. The 1.1–1.5 cm petioles bear clustered hairs. The membranous leaves are oblong to obovate; domatia are absent or rarely form hairy pockets beside the midrib. Solitary flowers grow on short shoots from branches or trunk. They stand upright on 0.5–0.8 cm stalks. The smooth calyx is 1.9–2.4 cm long and 0.8–0.9 cm wide; petals are about 4.6 by 0.6 cm. The staminal tube is 4.5 cm at the shortest, usually 6–11.5 cm, and bears clustered hairs; its tip is toothed. The nearly sessile anthers are positioned below the tube-lobe tips. The ovary has clustered hairs, the style is undivided, and the fruit is spherical. Seed number is not reported.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-b99e24ddb5}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
