## Aloe umfoloziensis {#s-6260827d17}
Aloe umfoloziensis is a clumping aloe with broad, spotted leaves and a tall, branched panicle of coral-red flowers. The regional account places it chiefly in low-lying subtropical parkland along rivers and watercourses. The described leaf spots vary in visibility between surfaces; the habitat statement is regional, not a full distribution survey.
