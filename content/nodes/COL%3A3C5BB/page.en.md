## Showy flower bracteoles and multiple seed counts {#s-66a2cb91ed}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes obovate leaves with gradually narrowed bases and acute or tapering tips. The leaves are papery; their margins are flat or roll under, without a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis, labeled “auxotélico” by the field without explanation. Supporting bracts persist. The smaller bracteoles beside the flowers are persistent and conspicuous. The smooth flower cup bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth, with persistent but inconspicuous bracteoles. Seed count is listed as one, two, three or four; the embryo has fused cotyledons (the seed leaves).

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-e07b6cbd85}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This category adds no separate distribution claim.
