## Ust’-Ishim 1 DNA and a model of earlier admixture {#s-b0d437c081}
Two direct dates place the Ust’-Ishim 1 femur at 46,880–43,210 calibrated years before present (95.4% probability); its genome contains long Neanderthal-derived segments. A recombination model uses segment lengths to estimate admixture 7,000–13,000 years before this individual lived. That interval does not identify a contact place, the number of events or the history of all modern-human dispersals.
