## Distribution and habitat {#s-7e5b86196f}
MDD currently displays Northern Bamboo Lemur, while 1990s papers often used Hapalemur griseus occidentalis or “western gentle lemur.” Curtis and colleagues reported a Tsiombikibo Forest sighting roughly 200 km south and 200 km north of previously reported populations. They called for further surveys rather than treating the sighting as a range census.

## Ecological evidence {#s-9440c875b9}
A 2008 short report documented the lemur at six locations in the agricultural landscape of Fampotobe on the Masoala Peninsula. The author intermittently followed one group of three at Tsarasoa for 11 days, totaling 45.9 hours. In that report, feeding occupied 37% of observation time; 89% of feeding observations involved young pseudo-petioles of the planted native bamboo Cathariostachys madagascariensis. These are observations of one group in small forest fragments, not species-wide dietary proportions.
