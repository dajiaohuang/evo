## Nerine gracilis {#s-1dcc13c21d}
Nerine gracilis is described as a geophyte up to 25 cm tall, with pink or white flowers that may be flushed with pink. The perianth segments are about 10 mm long with wavy margins, and the filament appendages are two-horned. Grassland, riverbanks and vleis are the recorded habitats in the regional account.
