## Elegia verreauxii {#s-815ee1556a}
## Stems grow in groups along underground stems

The South African regional flora describes Elegia verreauxii as a dioecious perennial (female and male flowers grow on separate plants) reaching 40 cm, with slender, spreading rhizomes (underground stems). Its stems grow in groups along the rhizomes, and its leaf sheaths (the basal parts of leaves around a stem) fall away. Damp sandy flats are listed as habitat.
