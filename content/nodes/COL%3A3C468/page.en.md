## A grooved midrib and a smooth round fruit {#s-d387e793d1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic, papery leaves with an acute base and tips that are acute or taper to a point. The margins are flat and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, with one additional vein near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, with stalked flowers arranged along a main axis; bract status is unknown. Small, inconspicuous bracteoles (the smaller leaf-like parts beside flowers) persist. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx, the outer ring of flower parts, is open in bud, with four sepals (outer parts) and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth. Fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.
