## What the account describes {#s-30e02598da}
Aeschynomene rudis is described in the Flora of Panama as a shrubby herb about 1 to 2 metres tall, with sparse stiff hairs. Its 4 to 10 centimetre leaves carry many small leaflets; the account also notes yellow flowers and segmented fruits. The description is tied to a historical regional flora record, which gives no population sampling frame or observation date.
