## ## Winged leaf stalks and a spike-like cluster {#s-3ba457555c}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records angular branches with lenticels—small corky pores—and surfaces that range from glabrous to hairy. The deciduous stipules are lanceolate or obovate; the petiole and main leaf axis may be cylindrical or winged. Each leaf has three or four pairs of leaflets and stalkless, saucer-shaped nectaries. The spike-like inflorescence bears stalkless flowers with tubular calyces. The flat fruit is glabrous, with leathery valves. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
