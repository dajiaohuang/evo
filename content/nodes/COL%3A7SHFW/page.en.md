## Reading an older ecology record {#s-62765da939}
COL26.8 accepts Colaptes rubiginosus in Picidae. EltonTraits 1.0 contains a same-name, same-family entry under an older taxonomy. The comparison uses its diet and foraging-position groups with their source labels; matching names do not settle later splits or synonymies.

## Diet-weight estimate {#s-bef88a30ba}
The nonzero diet weights are 90% invertebrates and 10% fruit. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet entry. These proportions standardize literature descriptions rather than measure intake; zero weight elsewhere would not prove a food is never eaten, and broad labels do not imply exclusive diets.

## Feeding positions in the vegetation {#s-81bfae650e}
The species-level record assigns 50% to trees or tall bushes from 2 m to below the canopy, 30% to the canopy or just above it, and 20% to understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m. These are relative foraging positions, not nest sites, all habitat use or a full-day activity budget. Water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial classes have different definitions; they do not give elevation or flight height.

## Limits of the historical record {#s-0271358b20}
EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family comparison, not a complete species account. Matching name and family do not prove equivalence with the current COL26.8 concept. Diet and foraging percentages are standardized readings of literature, not direct measures of intake, energy or time. The selected bird fields are marked species-level, but the Handbook was not independently reread here. Local, seasonal and individual variation, current distribution, population and conservation require other evidence.
