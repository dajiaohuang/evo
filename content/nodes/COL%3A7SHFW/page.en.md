## Limits of the historical record {#s-0271358b20}
EltonTraits is a historical same-name, same-family comparison, not a complete species account. Matching name and family do not prove equivalence with the current COL26.8 concept. Diet and foraging percentages are standardized readings of literature, not direct measures of intake, energy or time. The selected bird fields are marked species-level, but the Handbook was not independently reread here. Local, seasonal and individual variation, current distribution, population and conservation require other evidence.
