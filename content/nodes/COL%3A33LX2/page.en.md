## A reported maximum of 60.2 mm SVL {#s-8a3cd8f561}
The maximum snout–vent length is 60.2 mm. SVL runs from the snout to the vent and excludes the tail. This compiled maximum is not a typical adult size or a limit on growth; no separate male and female measurements are established in this page.

## Terrestrial and cathemeral are broad labels {#s-fe5af97f21}
The microhabitat entry is terrestrial. Active time is recorded as cathemeral, meaning activity is reported at intervals across both day and night rather than confined to one daylight or nighttime period. These categories do not specify exact sites, hours or seasonal patterns; diet is not established here.

## The reproductive mode is oviparous {#s-34b7e700b6}
The source labels this species oviparous. It does not provide a supported clutch-size mean here, so the count of eggs and frequency of laying remain unknown in this account. A mode label does not show whether reproduction is uniform among populations.
