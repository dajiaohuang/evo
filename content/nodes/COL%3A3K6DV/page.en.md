## Silvery mats on dry stony ground {#s-e30a62c6eb}
This account describes a prostrate dwarf shrub that forms extensive mats under a close, silvery-white felt. Its yellow flower heads have radiating bracts, lemon-yellow inside and golden-brown outside. The source says it favors dry rock sheets and bare ground, and can become dominant in overgrazed or poorly managed grassland, particularly in the Amatola Mountains.
