## Pterocaulon balansae {#s-5c8522c94c}
## Cream flower heads scattered along a cylindrical spike

Flora do Brasil 2020 records either a fibrous root cluster or a taproot, and erect stems. The leaf underside is covered in dense felted hairs; the upper surface is woolly or becomes nearly hairless.

Flower heads are scattered along a cylindrical spike, and the corollas are cream-coloured. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
