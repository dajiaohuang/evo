## Leaves {#s-65f2f02a27}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record allows stipules to be deciduous or absent. It describes an even-pinnate leaf with one pair of leaflets, while also marking the leaf rachis as absent; these fields do not fit neatly together, so they are reported as entered. Leaflets are listed as slightly to strongly sickle-shaped or elliptic, with asymmetrical bases and acute, blunt, or notched tips. The lower surface is recorded as having punctations present or absent; both alternatives are retained here.

## Flowers, fruit and seeds {#s-29769b5601}
The inflorescence is listed as a raceme with a hairy axis. The hypanthium is hairy; the calyx has four free sepals. Petals are described as clawed or obovate. The record lists three stamens and a hairy ovary. The fruit is a legume, and the seeds are nearly round (orbicular). These are source-listed descriptions and alternatives, not a complete account of variation.

## Habitat category {#s-c81c146595}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not give a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
