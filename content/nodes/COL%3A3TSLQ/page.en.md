## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-762eb89c3d}
The study identified sampled Leptopelis aubryi using COI barcode clustering (BIN and ABGD), comparison of 16S with reference sequences, and morphology and type-locality context. COI and 16S are both mitochondrial markers, so they do not provide independent nuclear confirmation or test reproductive isolation. The authors used neighbor-joining to cluster similar sequences and explicitly limited its use for reconstructing evolutionary history. Most species in the library were represented by only one or two localities, and not every GenBank voucher could be checked morphologically; reference labels and missing sequences also limit the evidence. Fieldwork took place in May–June 2012 and March–April 2013. GenBank/BOLD comparisons refer to the 2016 source-library snapshot, not a current database audit.
