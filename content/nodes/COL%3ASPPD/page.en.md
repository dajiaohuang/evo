## Ceratosanthes multiloba Cogn. {#s-033eecac81}
## Leaves range from entire to lobed

The source describes Ceratosanthes multiloba as having stems with soft hairs, ranging from pubescent to minutely pubescent. Its thin, membranous leaves may be simple or lobed; the margins may be entire or lobed, and the surfaces smooth or rough. These alternatives are not resolved in the record. The habitat entry is only the broad terrestrial label.
