## Ecological evidence {#s-5fccdbb694}
At Tangkoko Reserve in North Sulawesi, a long-term study monitored phenology in 41 tree species and estimated home ranges for four habituated macaque groups. The monthly mean estimate was 2.32 km² (SD 1.12); monthly temperature was the best-supported predictor in the model, with higher temperatures associated with larger monthly ranges. The abstract reports 2,877 ranging days, while the Methods group subtotals sum to 2,577; this discrepancy is unresolved. The estimate is neither species-wide nor evidence of a causal climate effect.

## Distribution records {#s-f2ad16a9c9}
A July–August 2023 survey in North Maluku recorded wild crested macaques or their feces at 18 locations on Bacan, four on Kasiruta and one in southern Halmahera. It separately recorded ten pet macaques at six Bacan locations. The survey did not visit Mandioli; reports of no evidence there came from interviews, not a direct absence survey. These are records from sampled locations, not a complete range map.
