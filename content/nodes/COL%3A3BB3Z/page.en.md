## *Eryngium ramboanum*: sessile flowers and papillose fruit segments {#s-a8d21feee5}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 field, leaves have stalks and veins listed as parallel near the centre or net-like. The flower cluster is upright; individual flowers and fruits attach directly to the axis without stalks. The fruit does not split at maturity, and the back of each segment bears small raised papillae.

The habitat field records terrestrial ground only, without a named locality or date; this is a regional character record.
