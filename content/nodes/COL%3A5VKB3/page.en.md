## Form in the flora record {#s-716728fafd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a prostrate stem, with cataphylls and prophylls entire at the tip and fibers at the stem base. Petioles are smooth and grooved, with obtuse margins and a rounded upper side. The flat, entire leaf blade is ovate, with an apiculate tip and a rounded or truncate base. Glandular dots are absent. Primary-vein and upper-surface secondary-vein characters are not recorded; a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is longer than half the leaf length and has a cylindrical peduncle. The boat-shaped spathe is linear and meets the peduncle at a right angle. A stipe is present and longer than the spadix. The berries are green.

The regional dataset row cites Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
