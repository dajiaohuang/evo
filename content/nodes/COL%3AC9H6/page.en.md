## Pale flowers on stems with variable growth forms {#s-f7b5894851}
The Portuguese record allows erect, climbing or prostrate stems. It lists leaves with or without petioles, ranging from elliptic to oblong or linear, with acute tips and hairs. Flower clusters may be sessile or branched, and flowers have five pale, papery tepals with three veins. Five stamens and long pseudostaminodes are listed; the stigma is papillose and the style long. The fruit may be smooth or textured at the tip, and the seed lacks a lateral projection.
