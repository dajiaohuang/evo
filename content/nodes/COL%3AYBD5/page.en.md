## Cordia fusca {#s-d6e7b34ddf}
## White bell-shaped flowers sit above contrasting leaf surfaces

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stem hairs that become sparse or are lost. Leaves are elliptic to lance-shaped, with wedge-shaped to rounded bases and pointed tips. The upper surface has short, stiff hairs; the underside is densely woolly. The bisexual white flowers have smooth, bell-shaped calyces and bell-shaped corollas, each under 1 cm long. The anthers lack glands, the stigma is club-shaped, and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is a hairless, red, egg-shaped structure. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
