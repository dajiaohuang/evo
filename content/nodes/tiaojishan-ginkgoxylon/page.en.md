## Ginkgo-like wood from the Tiaojishan Formation {#s-cf4a029476}
The isolated wood PB22285 from the 153–165 Ma Tiaojishan Formation of western Liaoning was named Ginkgoxylon liaoningense from its wood-anatomical characters. It is a dated Ginkgo-like wood record, not a whole plant, a first appearance of Ginkgophyta or a secure match to any particular co-occurring leaf species.
