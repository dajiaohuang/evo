## Many leaflets and terminal flowers {#s-4f5456984a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with nine to 16 pairs of oblong-elliptic to elliptic-ovate leaflets; seven pairs can also occur. It describes bulb-tipped glandular hairs and flower clusters at both branch tips and leaf axils. The yellow petals differ in shape, ten stamens are listed, and the mature pod sides twist. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
