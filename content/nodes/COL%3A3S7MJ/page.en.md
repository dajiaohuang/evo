## What features are recorded for Lantana cujabensis? {#s-954c7d46c1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a woody plant with upright or climbing branches, which may bear prickles. Its oval leaves have a wedge-shaped or heart-shaped base. Two unequal, persistent green bracts surround each flower cluster; yellow, red, or orange corollas rise above them. The fruit is vinous-colored and fleshy.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-df53f9b1d5}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
