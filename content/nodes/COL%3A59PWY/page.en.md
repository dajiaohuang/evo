## *Turnera lineata* {#s-b77f7ffeec}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — elliptic, egg-shaped, saw-toothed margins; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis); flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
