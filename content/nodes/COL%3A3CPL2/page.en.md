## What appearance is recorded for Euphorbia louwii? {#s-a1e88c4f2f}
This spiny succulent shrub reaches about 1 m. Its branches have five to seven angles and are blue-gray or green-blue with dense purple mottling. Separate chestnut to purplish spine shields each bear five slender, needle-like spines. The flowers are yellow.

## Which habitats are listed? {#s-f046ddb40e}
The source lists bushveld and hills of sandstone or conglomerate.
