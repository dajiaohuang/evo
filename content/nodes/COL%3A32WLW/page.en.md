## Accepted name and placement {#s-37531fbdd6}
COL26.8 accepts Cyclorana platycephala (Günther, 1873) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Pelodryadidae (family) → Cyclorana (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Two regional lineages in a frog revision {#s-216d23caeb}
A 2016 revision compared eastern and northern Australian populations of Cyclorana platycephala with a western population using molecular, morphological, and call evidence. The authors described the western population as C. occidentalis and retained the sampled eastern and northern populations as C. platycephala. This conclusion applies to the populations examined in that revision; it does not identify older database records carrying other name combinations.

## Eastern and western populations in a frog revision {#card}
A 2016 revision compared sampled eastern and northern Australian populations of Cyclorana platycephala with a western population using molecular, morphological and call evidence. The authors recognized the western population as C. occidentalis while retaining the sampled eastern and northern populations as C. platycephala.
