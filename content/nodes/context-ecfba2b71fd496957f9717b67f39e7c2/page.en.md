## The Anf0158 source record {#s-def1a0948c}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Arthroleptis xenochirus* as row Anf0158, in the genus Arthroleptis, family Arthroleptina, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; its species concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Terrestrial category and its limits {#s-22ae863d1c}
The row marks terrestrial as a broad foraging category (Ter = 1). This category does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so its terrestrial flag remains a historical AmphiBIO value rather than a verified account of present-day ecology; the revision does not establish that every original entry was wrong.

## Reported maximum adult length {#s-ef95b458de}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 24 mm. For anurans, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual or a typical adult length.

## Clutch count and annual breeding output {#s-d64c101809}
The row gives both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch as 9, and the maximum reproductive output as one event per year. These are counts compiled from the literature. The row does not specify the sex, population, observation date, or wild versus captive setting for each value, so it cannot support a fuller account of breeding behaviour.

## Developmental coding {#s-8cd0a944b8}
The source records direct development (Dir = 1). This compiled category does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
