## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-9ce64cb487}
The erect stems are hairy with star-shaped and gland-tipped hairs, ranging from rusty to translucent. Stipules are under 1 cm long. The leaves have a pale underside, lance-shaped to ovate blades, tapering bases, pointed or cuspidate tips and entire margins; the upper surface is hairless. A leafy, pyramidal inflorescence bears flowers with sepals divided to about the middle and unspotted cream petals. The dry fruit splits into four or five hairy segments (mericarps), each with an awn; each segment contains three seeds.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-5c15e1c3f8}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
