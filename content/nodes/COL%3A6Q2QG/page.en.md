## Lessingianthus warmingianus {#s-306a092664}
## Felted leaves and a moderately branched upper stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and a moderately branched stem with leaves concentrated in its upper part. The ovate to oblong subleathery leaves have acute tips and blunt or rounded bases. Grey-white felt covers both faces; their edges are entire and do not roll under.

Heads may be sessile or stalked, solitary or arranged in cymes and panicles. Their involucres range from bell-shaped to cylindrical, with 4–5 rows of bracts. The source lists 10 or 15 florets per head. Top-shaped fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
