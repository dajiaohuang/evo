## Cyrtanthus epiphyticus {#s-eac63b42e9}
The regional account describes Cyrtanthus epiphyticus with scarlet, trumpet-shaped flowers and an elongated bulb neck. The species was observed growing in moss on Podocarpus trees, at heights up to 20 m. The account cautions that epiphytic habit and several distinguishing floral characters vary with environment, so they are not constant separators from C. macowanii.
