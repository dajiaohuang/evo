## Habitat recorded in the flora dataset {#s-db4634f3b7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record uses *Corticícola*, meaning growth on bark.

This is a substrate category rather than a collection locality; the field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## What the record does not describe {#s-152c76303c}
The habitat field does not identify whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat label is not evidence about life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
