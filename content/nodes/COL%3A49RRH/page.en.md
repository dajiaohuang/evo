## How does its recorded foliage differ from a close relative? {#s-b310d501fe}
Oocephalus nubicola shares the reported xylopodium, non-viscid branches and scalloped leaf margins, but its leaves are described as papery or leathery and petiolate. The flower cluster is built from ovoid cymes with oval bracteoles; its tubular calyx persists, and its corolla is tubular, white to lilac. The source records four oval nutlets without mucilage. This account reports the listed characters, not the range of variation.
