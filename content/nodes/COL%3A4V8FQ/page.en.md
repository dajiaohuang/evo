## Schinus engleri {#s-15470c1cd7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records spines, several leaf-margin states and red or pink fruit, alongside a terrestrial ecology category.

## Spines, leaflet edges and red-to-pink fruit {#s-78cb4b9204}
The source describes an aerial stem with spines and a glabrous surface. Leaves are simple and alternate or fasciculate; the cylindrical rachis bears entire, serrate or crenate margins that are revolute. Venation is recorded as cladodromous, with veins prominent on the upper surface. The inflorescence is pseudoracemose and its peduncle is marked absent. Fruit colour is red or pink. A separate habitat field says “Terrícola” (terrestrial), but supplies no locality or range.
