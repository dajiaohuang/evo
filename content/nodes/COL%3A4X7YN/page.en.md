## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-a423d3890a}
The taproot supports erect stems with pubescence and simple or gland-tipped hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. The leaves are uniformly colored and may be ovate or oblong, with symmetrical blades, heart-shaped bases, pointed tips and toothed margins; the upper surface becomes nearly hairless and bears simple hairs. Solitary flowers grow in the leaf axils. The sepals have pointed tips and the corolla is yellow. The dry fruit splits into five segments (mericarps), without awns and with simple hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-92d4fd4b94}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
