## Schnella flexuosa {#s-8fbcf61364}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Schnella flexuosa* as an accepted species of *Schnella* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes deciduous stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) and bilobed leaves. Its flower buds lack wings on the calyx tube (the tube formed by the outer sepals); at flowering the calyx is cup-shaped and the upper petal is spatula-shaped and hairless. The fruit does not split open at maturity; the plant is listed as terrestrial.
