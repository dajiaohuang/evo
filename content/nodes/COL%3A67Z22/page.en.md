## Babiana fragrans {#s-4edb4caaaa}
Babiana fragrans has fragrant, violet to pale-blue flowers with pale markings edged in darker colour, and soft, lightly pleated leaves. The regional account notes variation across its range: plants from the interior southwestern Cape have a more distinctly two-lipped flower than those near the Cape Peninsula.
