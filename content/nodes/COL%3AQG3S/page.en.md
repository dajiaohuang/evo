## ## Three leaflets and a pseudo-raceme {#s-49dd1841c4}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes pinnate leaves with three leaflets and elongated, pseudo-racemose inflorescences. It records a pseudo-monadelphous androecium—the stamens are arranged in a partly joined bundle—and a hairless standard petal. The seeds are described as not laterally compressed. Its habitat field says “terrestrial”; no locality or survey date is supplied.
