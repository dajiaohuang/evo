## The source tadpole concept {#s-7981d67c82}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Ikakogi tayrona. The source classification lists Ikakogi as the genus and Centrolenidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Ikakogi tayrona. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
