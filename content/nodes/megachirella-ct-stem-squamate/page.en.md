## Megachirella in a combined-data squamate analysis {#s-dd346ee95d}
The only known Megachirella skeleton, holotype PZO 628, was scanned with high-resolution micro-CT and its segmented anatomy added to a combined fossil, living-taxon, morphological and molecular analysis. That matrix recovered Megachirella on the squamate stem. The result is a placement within one sampled analysis of a single, compressed specimen; its clock-model divergence estimates are not fossil observations, a date for crown Squamata, or evidence that this animal was a direct ancestor.
