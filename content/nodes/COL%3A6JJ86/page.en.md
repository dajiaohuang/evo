## Forsteronia laurifolia {#s-187178768d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Forsteronia laurifolia* as an accepted species of *Forsteronia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 provides a single habitat label, terrestrial. The regional field gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range, and it offers no morphology description.
