## Anthurium affine: a morphology and rocky-habitat record {#s-a30730328c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with cataphylls and prophylls recorded as fibrous along it. The leaf blades are entire and flat, oval to obovate, with obtuse or rounded tips and bases coded as acute, obtuse or cordate; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is coded as obtuse on the upper surface and acute below, while secondary veins are prominent above and a collecting vein is absent along the margins. The petiole is marked smooth, but its shape description is difficult to parse and is not summarized here.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, elliptic or ovate spathe set at an obtuse angle to it. The entry lists the spathe as stipitate and shorter than the spadix, but also records the stipe as absent, leaving that character unresolved. Berries are described as red.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. Ambiguous compound states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
