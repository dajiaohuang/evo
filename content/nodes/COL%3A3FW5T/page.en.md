## Gerbera linnaei {#s-687c45e53c}
## Yellow felt beneath divided leaves

Manning and Goldblatt's Greater Cape flora account describes an acaulescent perennial up to 40 cm tall. Its leaves grow in tufts; each oblanceolate blade is deeply divided, with rounded, twisted lobes. The underside has yellow felt-like hairs, and the margins roll under.

Scapes carry radiate flower heads that are cream-coloured, sometimes yellow, and maroon on the reverse. The bracts are hairless. The cited habitat field records sandstone slopes after fire.
