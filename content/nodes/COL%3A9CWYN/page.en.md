## ## Veined leaves and spiral calyx lobes {#s-d65d20af42}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes unarmed branches with linear stipules and papery, bilobed leaves. The leaf base is slightly heart-shaped to notched; the upper surface is finely hairy, while the lower surface has prominent secondary and tertiary veins, some with rusty hairs. The flower bud is five-ridged and bristle-tipped, and the calyx opens into five spiral lobes; ten anthers are recorded. The legume opens elastically. This regional description does not assess variation beyond its listed characters.
