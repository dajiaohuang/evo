## Aloe-like leaves with pearl-like dots {#s-b6673172be}
The Latin description calls the plant stemless and aloe-like, with entire, semi-cylindrical leaves marked by pearl-like dots. The leaves are concave above and thicken toward a three-angled tip; the root is spindle-shaped. A comparison with M. tigrinum highlights green, entire leaves with similar dots, especially beneath, and a sessile yellow flower. The habitat note says Burchell collected it at “C.B.S.” and that it thrives in that garden.
