## Swartzia glazioviana {#s-5e00254f49}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Swartzia glazioviana* as an accepted species of *Swartzia* in Fabaceae. COL26.8 accepts *Swartzia glazioviana* as a species of *Swartzia* (Fabaceae).
