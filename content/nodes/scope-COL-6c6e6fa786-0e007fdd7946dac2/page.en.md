## atlas-profile / overview {#s-b35601d075}
Four head capsules and roughly ten further body specimens from the Rhynie Chert in Scotland include an abdomen with a springtail-like furcula, a fork-shaped structure. Together, they support treating this sample as *Rhyniella praecursor* and its affinity with springtails (Collembola); they do not establish a complete species history or global range.

## atlas-profile / evidenceSummary {#s-6fcf08d94f}
The 1981 reassessment reports four head capsules and roughly ten additional specimens from one locality; the furcula supports collembolan affinity. These fossils do not document ecology or distribution beyond the Rhynie Chert. The 411–407 Ma Pragian envelope is a modern display conversion; the paper used older numerical correlations.
