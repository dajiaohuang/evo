## Long pink flowers and a mostly unbranched stem {#s-0df36c70a6}
Watsonia knysnana reaches 1.6 m and has mostly unbranched stems with pink to purple long-tubed flowers. The source distinguishes it from orange-flowered W. pillansii and records extensive hybridisation between the two along parts of the southern Cape coast.

## Sandstone slopes in fynbos {#s-502aece7e6}
The regional account records sandstone slopes in fynbos, a Cape shrubland vegetation type.
