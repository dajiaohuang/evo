## Axillary clusters of small cream-colored heads {#s-439b1d0326}
The record describes angled, scaly branches and alternate elliptic leaves with pointed tips and wedge-shaped bases. The lower leaf surfaces are scaly-woolly and dotted with glands. Axillary, corymb-like clusters carry 20–60 stalked cylindrical heads, each with three cream-colored florets. The three- or four-angled fruits are glandular and bear a two-row pappus, with the outer row ranging from thread-like to scale-like. The habitat field says terrestrial.
