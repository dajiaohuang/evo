Field-row counts are not counts of measured individuals, and repeated citations do not establish independent experimental replication.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-efd5cbba1e}
This page uses one historical same-name entry from the 2015 life-history archive, not a contemporary field study. The archive reports medians (or the average of the two middle values) except for maximum longevity, which uses the largest record found. These are compiled summaries, not measurements of one individual, distributions of individual variation, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The archive may share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name, family and field rows support this association; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 or remove every inherited component. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and the underlying publications were not individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and unavailable fields require other sources.
