## A three-part holotype under the name umbrinus {#s-532bae36f5}
The catalogue places Colobus (Tropicolobus) umbrinus Matschie, 1914 under Piliocolobus oustaleti (Trouessart, 1906). The umbrinus holotype ZMB 17966/17967/17968 (old numbers A 108,14,178–180) represents one male’s skull, skin and skeleton, collected by Escherich on 24–29 December 1913 at Bungi on the Sangha between Wesso (Ouesso) and Ikelemba, in present-day Republic of the Congo. This is the recorded collection interval, not a capture date resolved to one day; it is not the original oustaleti 1906 holotype.

## Three paratypes represented by five part numbers {#s-4d05cc29d9}
The umbrinus paratypes are one female skull and skin ZMB 17969/17970, one male skull and skin 17971/17972, and one female skin 17973: three animals, not five. This small series with differing preservation does not measure species-wide sex ratio, morphological variation or group structure. The catalogue’s placement of the historical name umbrinus remains separate from the pinned COL26.8 page identity, without a fresh audit of the original description or all specimens.

## Survey records {#s-7af3f31bd6}
At Lac Télé Community Reserve, 83 cameras sampled nine seasonally flooded forest clearings from January 2020 to May 2023. The survey reported 169 photographs in 11 events for Oustalet's red colobus across 6,399 camera-days; the species-specific capture rate was 0.17 in the authors' relative-abundance index. These records show use of sampled clearings in one reserve, not density or reserve-wide abundance. A separate 2020 assessment describes the species as highly variable and notes unresolved boundaries with adjacent forms.
