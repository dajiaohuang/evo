## Rhinephyllum pillansii {#s-cf3d12fc7b}
## A compact succulent with chin-shaped leaf projections

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a compact, tufted succulent shrub up to 8 cm tall. The three-angled leaves have a pronounced chin-like projection; leaves within a pair differ in size and are light grey. Their dimensions are recorded as 13–20 × 10 × up to 9 mm.

The solitary flowers are yellow. Fruits are 6–9 mm across and sit on persistent stalks up to 15 mm long. Each fruit has a funnel-shaped base, a flat top, broad valve wings and five chambers. The habitat account records flats with grey shale.
