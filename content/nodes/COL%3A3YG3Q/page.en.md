## Maurocenia frangula {#s-9cfb03927f}
## Opposite leathery leaves and honey-scented flowers

Maurocenia frangula is often a monoecious shrub or spreading tree. Its opposite leaves are broadly elliptic to rounded, stiff and leathery, with margins rolled under. Axillary cymes carry cream-colored, honey-scented flowers, followed by red berries. The source places it in coastal bush or forest but gives no specific locality.
