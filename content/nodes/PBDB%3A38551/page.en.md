## From forelimbs to a composite skeleton {#s-911bcd1fc1}
The Deinocheirus route moves from the giant-forelimb holotype collected in 1965 to two specimens discovered in 2006 and 2009. ## Evidence limits {#s-245b05ccd3}
Mass and behavior remain inferences; the preserved tail feathers do not directly establish complete plumage. The 2014 research classification and PBDB classification remain distinct.
