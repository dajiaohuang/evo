## From forelimbs to a composite skeleton {#s-911bcd1fc1}
The Deinocheirus route moves from the giant-forelimb holotype collected in 1965 to two specimens discovered in 2006 and 2009. The 2014 paper added head, trunk and hindlimb anatomy, revealing a combination unlike the usual cursorial ornithomimosaur form. A complete outline still requires filling gaps across individuals. Referred near-complete material, proportional reconstruction, stomach contents and lifestyle hypotheses play distinct roles; a skeletal image does not erase missing material.
