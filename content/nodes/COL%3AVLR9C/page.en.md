## A spiny fruit and fissured bark {#s-47355882ce}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records fissured bark, lance-shaped stipules up to 5 mm wide, and leaflets that range from nearly smooth to hairy. Persistent bracts accompany the inflorescence; the bell-shaped calyx is part of a flower up to 12 mm long. The fruit can reach 12 cm, with spines up to 2.5 cm.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-02327f83d2}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 record labels the species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. This is a habitat category, not a collection point or complete range.
