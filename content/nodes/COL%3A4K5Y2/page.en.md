## Distribution records {#s-3546a18a95}
The 2021 Mammalian Species account confirmed this titi at 104 localities in Colombia's Cauca and Caquetá departments, at about 190–400 m elevation. The described area includes Amazonian piedmont near the eastern Andes and adjoining lowlands. Confirmed localities are occurrence records, not a complete range survey.

## Group composition and foods {#s-a2be13876a}
Observations summarized in the account describe family groups commonly comprising an adult pair and offspring, usually 3–4 animals. Published diet records emphasize leaves, seeds and fruit, with insects also recorded. Group and diet observations come from particular sites and studies and should not be treated as fixed proportions across the range.

## Classification {#s-7de858b521}
COL26.8 accepts Plecturocebus caquetensis. The 2021 account uses this genus and records the original combination in Callicebus.

## Evidence limits {#s-304880897e}
