## A designated neotype and sexed diagnostic measurements {#s-c094b17a52}
The 2020 revision designated adult male MVZ 85590 as neotype, collected near Huatusco, Veracruz, on 16 January 1969 at an estimated 1400 m. Its location error was printed as a maximum distance of 1.202 miles. Twelve additional specimens comprised eight males and four females. The diagnosis reported eight males with mean SVL 37.5 mm, range 36.1–38.8 mm, and four females with mean 33.5 mm, range 30.7–36.7 mm. Mean tail/SVL was 1.25 in males and 1.19 in females; mean maxillary tooth counts were 12.6 and 48.0. The diagnostic male count remains separate from the neotype plus eight additional males; measurements describe the revision sample, not universal size or tooth limits.

## Seven photographed animals with variable dorsal bands {#s-a70f229dab}
Live-colour notes came from slides of seven Huatusco specimens. Many had bright tan-to-yellow dorsal colours, sometimes with orange on the snout and a band continuing onto the tail; darker specimens could lack an obvious stripe. Preserved animals were pale brown, sometimes with an indistinct broad stripe, and pale tan below. The photograph series is not automatically identified with the live neotype. Variation prevents treating a yellow dorsal band as an invariant standalone identification feature, and preservation colours remain distinct from life colours.

## A dated Veracruz–Oaxaca range summary {#s-2bca60adf2}
The 2020 account placed the species from near Huatusco, Veracruz, south to Sierra de Juárez, Oaxaca, at 1400–2170 m, reporting higher elevations in Oaxaca than Veracruz. It listed C. orculus, C. perotensis, C. ceronorum and C. lavae as geographically associated species. Geographic association is not proof that every species shared each collecting site or partitioned resources experimentally. The dated range summary is not a verified current census, an occupied continuous polygon or a measured elevation tolerance.
