## *Goeppertia lietzei*: spiral fertile bracts and an entire corolla tube {#s-df7a5c69ab}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, leaves spiral around the stem or arise basally; young leaves are recorded as ornamented. All bracts in the flower cluster are fertile, arranged spirally or nearly in two ranks, and not red or orange. Their margins stand upright, and each small cluster contains two flowers.

The corolla tube is described as entire, while the callus at the tip of the outer staminode is not petal-like. These are the stated character fields in a regional record, not a claim about every population.
