## Bactris tefensis {#s-3f8d1558ae}
Bactris tefensis is recorded with a solitary or clustered stem up to 2 m and black spines up to 4 cm. Its 11–13 pairs of sickle-shaped leaflets are irregularly grouped; the branched inflorescence lies between the leaves. The obovoid to beaked fruit is orange to red with a prickly surface; habitat is listed as terrestrial.
