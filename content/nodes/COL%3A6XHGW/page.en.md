## *Sanchezia lispa* {#s-a2a7f067de}
The field describes oblong to elliptic leaf blades with tapered bases and long-pointed tips. The inflorescence branches only near the base, with one or two flowers at a node along one side of the main axis. Outer bracts are ovate, 6–8 mm long and up to 1 cm wide; inner bracts are ovate. The calyx is 12–13 mm long with oblong lobes, and the corolla is red to orange. These are recorded characters.
