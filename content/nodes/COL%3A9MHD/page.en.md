## Acrolejeunea torulosa: two habitat labels {#s-e9d3831d4e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset lists two habitat categories for this species:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Rupícola” (rock-associated).

These labels come from two regional dataset rows; neither specifies a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
