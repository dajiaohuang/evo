## What alternatives are recorded for its bracts and flower? {#s-aa2c5542fd}
Utricularia praelonga is recorded with linear or obovate leaves and globose bladder traps that open at the base. Its bracts have cut or fringed edges, and the bracteoles may be joined to them at the base. The yellow corolla has a bulging throat; its lower lip may be entire or three-lobed. The curved, cone-shaped spur is as long as or longer than the lower lip, with a pointed tip. These are alternatives preserved by the source, not a single fixed form.

## Which habitat category accompanies the description? {#s-a2522beced}
The habitat field classifies Utricularia praelonga as terrestrial. It names no collection locality or complete range.
