## Where does its woolly texture appear? {#s-a4ee279298}
A regional account describes *Restio laniger* as a stoloniferous perennial reaching 40 cm. Its branched culms (stems) are often curved and smooth; woolly scales appear where the leaves join the stems. Stolons are horizontal stems that extend from the plant.
