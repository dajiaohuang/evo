## Which classification concept does this record use? {#s-c2fb333cdd}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Allobates nidicola* in row Anf0042, under the genus *Allobates*, family Aromobatidae and order Anura, following the source concept in *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost, 2011). Its correspondence to the current COL26.8 species concept has not been established; the traits below describe this deposited row.

## Recorded foraging setting {#s-1e9febf126}
AmphiBIO lists terrestrial foraging. This broad category does not detail seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some microhabitat entries propagated from earlier amphibian compilations; a row-level comparison for this record is unavailable. The category remains a historical value, with no replacement inferred and no claim that the original is proven wrong.

## Time of activity {#s-c5268afaf2}
AmphiBIO lists twilight as the activity period. This is a broad category, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Reported adult maximum length {#s-c0195a68f0}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult length of 21.4 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This is a literature-compilation maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Clutch count and egg or offspring size {#s-97507356a5}
This row gives 2–6 offspring or eggs per clutch and records both the minimum and maximum size as 2.4 mm. AmphiBIO compiles literature extrema and counts; the row does not identify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions for each value, and the size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-798ea84ab2}
AmphiBIO marks the row as direct development rather than a larval stage. This source category alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
