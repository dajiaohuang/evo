## A 2017 reassessment of the name {#study}
White, Ebert and Naylor report in their abstract that re-examining the original description and illustration led them to treat *Centrophorus lusitanicus* as a junior synonym of *C. granulosus*. The abstract separately describes the London Natural History Museum specimen then considered a syntype of *C. lusitanicus* as a long-finned species, not conspecific with *C. granulosus*. The authors further state that this specimen should not be considered a syntype and was likely not originally collected off the coast of Portugal. COL26.8 retains this assigned record as accepted. This account reports the abstract only; it does not reconcile the paper's treatment with the pinned COL26.8 concept.
