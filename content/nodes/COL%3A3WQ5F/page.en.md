## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-b38b7efad9}
COL26.8 records Lygodactylus waterbergensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-1d9baa01bb}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 40 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-42c3dc501e}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-353172ae88}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
