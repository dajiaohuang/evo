## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-f7914dc562}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Psilopogon corvinus (Temminck, 1831) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## What the measured birds contribute {#s-d9b9fc2228}
Four birds contribute to the morphology row—two females and two males—and all four have complete measurement sets. Their means are 29.4 mm for nostril-to-tip beak length, 13.3 mm for beak width, 32.8 mm for tarsus, and 121.8 mm for unflattened wing. The hand-wing index is 18.3: Kipp's distance divided by wing length, multiplied by 100. It is a comparative wing proportion, not flight speed. Inference=NO does not guarantee the same sample count for every field, and beak width alone does not test fruit handling or pecking performance.

## The mass value comes from another compilation {#s-362d657901}
The compiled mean mass is 130 g and its source field is Dunning. It has a separate denominator from the four birds measured for morphology, so those specimens cannot be treated as the weighed sample. Individual weights and Dunning's original book were not examined; sex-, season- or locality-specific ranges cannot be supplied.

## Fruit niche does not specify a measured diet {#s-e430dbbbbd}
The Metadata field labels the niche Frugivore, meaning fruit is the predominant resource, while the separate trophic-level field is Herbivore. These categories classify the record; they are not observations of individual meals. Their thresholds are not diet percentages measured at a particular place or season. This evidence gives no fruit species, food sizes, digestion results or seed-dispersal experiments, so those details and effects remain unknown here.

## Activity and habitat classes {#s-11d47291a6}
Insessorial describes a primarily perching way of life on branches or other substrates above ground; it does not mean the bird never descends. The habitat class is Forest, and density code 1 means dense vegetation, not a count of birds per area. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, not evidence of lifelong immobility or inability to fly. Historical map fields are not interpreted in this account, and none of these categories establishes current occupancy, nesting sites or daily activity height.

## What these records leave unresolved {#s-61b8e4b8fe}
This account draws on selected historical AVONET v7 rows, two name crosswalks, the relevant Metadata definitions and local COL name records. Morphological means and compiled mass have separate sample bases, and matching names or catalogue synonyms do not prove that source concepts were identical through time. The specimens, complete workbook, Dunning's book and full methods paper were not examined here. Nest cavities, breeding, calls, plumage identification, current range and conservation status therefore remain outside the evidence.
