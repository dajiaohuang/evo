## Name and placement in COL26.8 {#s-124d33aa49}
COL26.8 lists Atelopus famelicus Rivero & Morales, 1995 as an accepted species. In the release dated 20 August 2026, its recorded path runs through Anura (order), Bufonidae (family) and Atelopus (genus). This placement belongs to that checklist; the same name in another source needs its own identity evidence.

## Maximum adult body length in the compilation {#s-e7d3b576e2}
Under AmphiBIO's historical Frost 2011 classification, maximum adult body length is recorded as 45 mm. For Anura, body length means snout-to-vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona it means total length. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual. Its source concept has not been confirmed as equivalent to COL26.8.
