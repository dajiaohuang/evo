## What flower colors are recorded for Polycarena aurea? {#s-4bbcf16851}
Polycarena aurea is described as a glandular-hairy annual up to 20 cm tall, with narrow, sparsely toothed leaves. Small flower heads are terminal. The flowers are yellow, or have a white upper lip tipped with yellow; the short corolla tube is hairless.

## How does it vary and compare with similar species? {#s-8200649fad}
The source reports wholly yellow flowers in the southern range, while plants around Middelpost may also have white corolla lobes tipped yellow. Compared with P. pubescens, P. aurea has a larger corolla limb, a nearly hairless upper lip, and shorter hairs. It differs from P. formosa in hair length, bract shape and attachment, and usually a shorter corolla tube. Their ranges appear to overlap only near Nieuwoudtville, according to this account.
