## What range of leaf and flower forms is recorded? {#s-2ef60de328}
The papery leaves may be lance-shaped, elliptic, oval or broadly elliptic; their tips range from acute to tapering, and their bases from blunt or rounded to heart-shaped or acute. Margins are toothed. The blades may be hairless but marked by black or amber resin dots or lines. Flowers may be solitary, in shortened axillary racemes or in clusters. The yellowish to greenish-yellow flowers have nearly equal, lance-shaped sepals. The upper petals may be oblong or obovate; the side petals spatula-shaped or oblong and slightly narrowed in the middle. The front petal has a curved or straight spur, and its blade varies in outline. The ovary is hairless and three-carpellate, with 16–20 ovules per placenta arranged in two rows. Fruit valves are hairless and may be broadly oblong, obovate or oblong, with notched or rounded tips and blunt, truncate or rounded bases. Seeds have broad, wavy or nearly entire wings; they are hairless, round and slightly flattened across.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-3ec80fc53b}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
