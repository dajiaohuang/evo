## *Arrojadocharis praxeloides*: narrow leaves and umbel-like clusters {#s-a102d34914}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field lists alternate or spiral leaves, narrow in outline—linear, narrowly oblong, or narrowly lance-shaped. Their bases taper; the margins are entire or finely fringed and may lie flat or roll inward. Flower heads form umbel-like clusters. These are the specific morphology fields recorded, alongside only the broad substrate category “terrestrial.”
