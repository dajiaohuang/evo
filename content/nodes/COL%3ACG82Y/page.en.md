## An erect plant with a single leaflet in the source entry {#s-f8f761ec95}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records an erect stem and classifies the leaves as unifoliolate. It describes the inflorescence as a panicle. Pedicels are categorized as long or short, and the loment is recorded as having few articles. The habitat field gives the category terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
