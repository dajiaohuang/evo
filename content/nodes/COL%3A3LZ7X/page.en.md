## Hippeastrum sanfranciscanum {#s-b1ed887687}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes Hippeastrum sanfranciscanum with a bulb and narrow, semi-cylindrical leaves. One or two funnel-shaped flowers are listed, with dark orange-red tepal tips and a pale-green to yellow base; the tube length is entered as either short or 2.5–6 cm. The habitat field gives terrestrial substrate and no locality.
