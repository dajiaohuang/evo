Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — pinnate (side divisions arranged along a central axis), three-leaflet; flowers — pink; fruits — straight. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
