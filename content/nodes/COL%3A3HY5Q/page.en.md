## Gyptis lanigera {#s-b091e26e19}
## Recorded form, limited in scope

The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field is brief. It records leaves as opposite or alternate, with entire blades; a scape-like flowering axis; linear styles; and dry fruits (cypselae) bearing glandular or nonglandular hairs. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial. These are regional dataset entries: they do not resolve the leaf arrangement or establish a complete description or range.
