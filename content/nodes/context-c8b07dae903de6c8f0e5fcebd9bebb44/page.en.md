## The source concept {#s-70b2777526}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Rhinoderma rufum as source row Anf1448, under genus Rhinoderma, family Cycloramphidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. A row note citing Soto-Azat et al. (2013) says the taxon was considered extinct; this historical note does not establish current conservation status. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-2e9d3ea589}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 33 mm. For Anura, the compilation measures body length as snout–vent length (from snout to vent); it uses total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a maximum compiled from literature, not a measurement for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-d18456f34e}
AmphiBIO records 12–24 offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and an offspring-or-egg size of 2.4 mm (the listed minimum and maximum are equal). These are compiled literature values; the row does not identify the sex, population, measurement date or wild-versus-captive context for each value. The size field may refer to offspring or to eggs.
