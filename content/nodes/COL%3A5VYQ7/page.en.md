## Features noted in the regional account {#s-491012be02}
The 2012 account compares *B. latifolia* with *B. ecklonii*: *B. latifolia* has narrower tepals, leaf tips that are almost blunt, dark-purple flowers with narrow white streaks on the lower tepals, and broad style branches.

This comparison comes from a regional South African account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Distinguishing Babiana ecklonii {#s-c6deb2ce80}
A 2020 diagnostic account distinguishes *B. latifolia* by style branches that are unusually broad and wavy at the tips, purple flowers with narrow white nectar guides on the lower lateral tepals, blue-black anthers, and blunt tepals with a small point. Its style divides opposite the anther tips, and the branches are about 4 mm long. In *B. ecklonii*, the flowers are violet with broad white to pale-yellow nectar guides, the anthers pale violet, and the tepals narrow and pointed. The style division in *B. ecklonii* varies among plants: it may be below the anthers, with branches about 2 mm long, or opposite the middle or near the tips of the anthers, with branches up to 4 mm long. In both positions, its style branches are slender and widen gradually toward the tips.

The two species also differ in their foliage and stems. *B. latifolia* has velvety hairs on leaves and stems, whereas the hairs of *B. ecklonii* are longer and sparser. The stem of *B. ecklonii* is erect and largely enclosed by leaf bases; that of *B. latifolia* arches outward and is free of the leaf bases for at least half its length.

These diagnostic comparisons come from a regional South African account published in 2020; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
