## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Bunchosia pernambucana? {#s-9634a90532}
The smooth leaf blades are flat, with opposite side veins. The recorded inflorescence stalk is sessile, or without a distinct stalk. Flowers sit opposite one another; their styles are separate, and the petal margins bear glands. The orange drupe has three chambers.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-54e6e7267e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
