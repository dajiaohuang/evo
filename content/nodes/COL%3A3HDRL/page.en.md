## Griffinia aracensis: leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-e1b6c6e0cd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field records a subterranean bulb. Leaves have pseudopetioles and elliptic, spotted blades with attenuate bases and acute tips. The solid scape bears fused, spathe-like bracts. The flowers are unscented and campanulate, with lilac perianths that are white at the base and unspotted. The hypanthium tube is short, 0.1–1.0 cm long. There are six stamens; their filaments are as long as, or slightly shorter than, the sepals and petals. The anthers are ash-grey, and placentation is basal. The fruit shape is recorded as unknown.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
