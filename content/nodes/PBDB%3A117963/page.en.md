## A topology from sampled forms {#s-a397f0412c}
A revised morphological matrix proposes relationships among the sampled Heterostraci, while dated Silurian–Early Devonian occurrences define the study’s 443.8–393.3 Ma sample window. This window summarizes material used in the analysis; it is not a global first or last appearance, or a sequence of ancestors.

The study’s revised character matrix provides an explicit phylogenetic hypothesis for its included heterostracans. The result describes the sampled analysis and does not resolve every deep relationship or convert its branching pattern into direct ancestry.

## The study’s time window {#s-69da2a989e}
The dated occurrences used in the matrix span 443.8–393.3 Ma, from the Silurian into the Early Devonian. This is the time window of the study sample, not the exact duration or global endpoints of Heterostraci.
