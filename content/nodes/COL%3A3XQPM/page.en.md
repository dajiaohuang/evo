## What leaf, bract and fruit traits are recorded for Malpighiodes leucanthele? {#s-6b48d6eb8d}
The leaves are woolly-haired. Flat bracteoles are woolly on both surfaces and attach at the tip of the flower stalk. The lateral wings on the fruit segments are fully developed.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-349653e32e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
