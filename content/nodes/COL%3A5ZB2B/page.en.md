## Erect stems and closed flowers {#s-afd8e1faa7}
The flora entry describes an erect plant that may be semi-woody or herbaceous, with leaves divided into three leaflets. Purple flowers grow beside the leaves in pseudo-racemes, flowering clusters arranged along an axis. Closed flowers are recorded as present. The stamens are described as pseudomonadelphous, appearing joined into one group. The oblong fruit has leathery sides, the seeds are globose, and the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
