## Philodendron barrosoanum {#s-87c9c6eca4}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 lists the leaf form as arrow-shaped or three-lobed, with a wavy margin. The same field gives several alternatives for the blade lobes and floral structures, so it does not resolve one form; the habitat category is hemiepiphytic. This regional record is not a population survey.
