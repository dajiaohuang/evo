## *Euphorbia oxystegia* {#s-14c8184cef}
Compared with *Euphorbia loricata*, the source describes fewer, shorter branches and fewer, longer, softer, usually curved structures termed “spines” in the account. The capsule is softly hairy. The comparison is limited to these recorded characters.

The source gives densely bushy, west-facing rocky slopes as habitat. No locality or date is supplied.
