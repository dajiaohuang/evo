## The source concept {#s-e2602e2f68}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0092 records Aromobates mayorgai in genus Aromobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-36977f745d}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The fields Fos=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this does not show that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-68ce5b8ea1}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity). Noc=NA remains missing, not evidence of absence. These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=25.7 mm {#s-9d9130ee52}
Body_size_mm=25.7 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.

## Reproductive_output_y: maximum 1 event/year {#s-974a8844d3}
The row lists Reproductive_output_y=1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
