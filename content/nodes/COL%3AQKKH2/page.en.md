## Accepted name and placement {#s-7bdba5ef3e}
COL26.8 accepts Microhyla daklakensis Hoang, Nguyen, Ninh, Luong, Pham, Nguyen, Orlov, Chen, Wang, Ziegler & Jiang, 2021 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Microhyla (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Nam Ka type-locality records {#s-23cb64f849}
The description recorded the species at Nam Ka Nature Reserve, Dak Lak Province, Vietnam, around 500 m elevation. At publication, it was known only from this type locality. That scope does not establish a complete present-day distribution or physiological elevation tolerance.

## Temporary-pond observations with unknown eggs {#s-14e519009c}
Specimens were collected between 19:00 and 23:00 near temporary ponds formed after heavy rain, at forest edges and beside a recently built road. The authors reported simultaneous reproduction with several other Microhyla at the site but explicitly did not know this species’ eggs or larvae. Shared breeding-site use does not establish identical eggs, larval morphology or development.
