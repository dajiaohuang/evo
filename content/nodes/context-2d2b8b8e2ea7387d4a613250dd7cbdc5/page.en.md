## The source concept {#s-de5db952ec}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Osteopilus septentrionalis as row Anf2508, classified in the genus Osteopilus, family Hylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); whether this record matches a species concept in COL26.8 has not been established. The traits below describe the deposited record.

A listed category does not show the full diet or the proportion of food in each group.

## Seasonal activity {#s-b04f859006}
The row is assigned to the wet-and-warm seasonal activity category. These categories compare activity conditions with the local annual climate, so they do not correspond to the same calendar months everywhere.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-8ab15d559e}
The compilation gives a maximum adult body length of 165 mm. For frogs, this measure is snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-d528420948}
The row reports a minimum and maximum age at sexual maturity of 0.3 and 0.7 years, a maximum lifespan of 12.9 years, 130–1,500 eggs or offspring per clutch, and a maximum of one reproductive event per year. The minimum and maximum offspring-or-egg size are both 3 mm. These are extrema and counts compiled from literature; the row does not specify the sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive setting for each value. The size measure may refer to an egg or offspring.
