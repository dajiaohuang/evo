## Smooth leaves and hairy fruits {#s-64b9ca5da7}
The record describes an upright perennial with woody or partly woody stems and sessile, lance-shaped leaves along the stem. The leaf bases may narrow or clasp the stem; the blades are smooth on both surfaces and toothed along the edges. Yellow radiate heads form corymb-like clusters. The ribbed fruits are bristly and retain a white pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
