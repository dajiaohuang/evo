## Stryphnodendron barbatulum {#s-d4544defeb}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Stryphnodendron barbatulum* as an accepted species of *Stryphnodendron* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records 5–7 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), one elongated wart-shaped gland on the leaf stalk and one wart-shaped gland on the leaf axis. The bell-shaped calyx (the outer layer formed by sepals) is hairless and the corolla (the petal layer) vinaceous; fruit characters remain unspecified. The plant is listed as terrestrial.
