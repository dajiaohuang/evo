## The source concept {#s-c554f1c220}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Amietophrynus chudeaui* in row Anf0360 under Amietophrynus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-dd6e5a7b7d}
For Anf0360, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-5097def5b9}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 13 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
