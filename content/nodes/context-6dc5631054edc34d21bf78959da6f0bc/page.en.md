## Record identity and classification {#s-914340edb5}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf5150 records Philautus truongsonensis under Philautus, Rhacophoridae, and Anura, following the source classification in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to this dataset row; it has not been matched to an equivalent current COL26.8 species concept.

## Life-history values in the compilation {#s-4d3f903e97}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists: maximum reproductive events per year: 1. These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not identify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.

## Developmental category {#s-3c465bafcc}
The AmphiBIO v1 row lists direct development as its developmental category. This label alone does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
