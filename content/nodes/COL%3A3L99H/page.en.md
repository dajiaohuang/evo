## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-15502d6ceb}
COL26.8 records Heterodon simus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-a3a38b4d5e}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 610 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-09024377fe}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Fossorial/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Beane 2015, Slavens and Slavens 1999.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-29e1d50b3d}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 9.6. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Beane 2015, Slavens and Slavens 1999. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-cd2a712b12}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
