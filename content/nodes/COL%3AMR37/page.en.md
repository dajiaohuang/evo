## Bougainvillea praecox {#s-5ca4318619}
## Small clustered leaves and pale bracts

Bougainvillea praecox has short shoots and straight spines. Its small leaves are clustered, with petioles under 1 cm and ovate or elliptic blades under 2 cm; young leaf surfaces may be woolly or become nearly hairless. Simple cymes arise in the leaf axils. Each bract is heart-shaped and whitish green, enclosing one woolly, tubular flower with a flared limb. Five to six stamens are recorded, and the fruit is obovoid.
