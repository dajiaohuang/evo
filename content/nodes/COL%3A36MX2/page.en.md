## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Diplopterys nigrescens? {#s-a9ef29deef}
Leaves persist during flowering. Their elliptic, papery blades have wedge-shaped bases and four pairs of side veins; the lower surface ranges from becoming hairless to silky-haired, with tiny glands near or at the edge. Umbel inflorescences carry ovate bracts held upright or close to the stalk. Flower stalks have golden silky hairs. The sepals overlap in the bud and bear oil glands. Petals have sparse silky hairs below and long-fringed margins; the stamens are joined at the base. The front and rear styles are equal, hairless or rough-haired at the base. Lateral fruit wings are reduced to multiple ridges.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5477a87589}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
