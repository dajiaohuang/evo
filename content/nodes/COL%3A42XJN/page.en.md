## Microlejeunea cystifera: two habitat labels {#s-033ee6f189}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this species:

“Corticícola” (bark-associated)

and “Epífila” (leaf-associated).

These are regional dataset labels, not a mapped range; neither row gives a collection locality or survey date.
