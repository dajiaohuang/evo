## Restio zwartbergensis {#s-f7bdda9ec3}
## Flat-topped tubercles on trailing stems

Restio zwartbergensis is described as a tangled, stolon-forming perennial to 30 cm. Its stems branch repeatedly and carry flat-topped tubercles, while the sheaths loosely wrap around them. The regional record places it on sparse, gravelly slopes up to 1,000 m.
