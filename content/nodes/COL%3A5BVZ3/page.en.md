## Wedelia kirkbridei {#s-f87dd9ca0f}
## Serrated leaves on branches that lose their hairs

Flora do Brasil 2020 records a xylopodium (a woody underground swelling) and tufted or erect branches that become nearly hairless. The cylindrical, ribbed branches bear petiolate leaves; blades range from lance-shaped to obovate, with pointed or blunt tips, serrated margins and wedge-shaped bases. Both surfaces become nearly hairless or retain a fine, short covering of hairs.

Flower heads form paired-branched clusters (dichasia) on finely hairy stalks. Ray florets are present, and the fruits have smooth surfaces. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
