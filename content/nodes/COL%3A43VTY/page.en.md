## What leaf, ovary and seed traits are recorded? {#s-a96b0f87ae}
The ovate leaves have pointed, acuminate or shortly pointed tips and rounded to slightly heart-shaped bases. Scale-like hairs cover both surfaces; the edges are entire, and the lower surface’s tertiary veins are inconspicuous. The ovary is also covered in scale-like hairs. The fruit may be globose or ellipsoid, with a notched tip; it is scale-hairy and often winged. Seeds have wings.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-08994e5d13}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
