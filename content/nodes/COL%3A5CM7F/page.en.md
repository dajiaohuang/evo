## What does the field record for Xylopia sericea? {#s-af61c25f21}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes young branches densely covered with silky or woolly hairs. Narrow elliptic to lance-shaped leaves are usually 4–11 cm long, with 7–10 pairs of side veins and a densely silky underside. Axillary clusters usually bear two to eight flowers. The fruit has two to seven segments, 8–30 mm long, ranging from egg-shaped to curved or club-like; the dry surface may be smooth or slightly wrinkled. Seeds are 7–8 mm long.

The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
