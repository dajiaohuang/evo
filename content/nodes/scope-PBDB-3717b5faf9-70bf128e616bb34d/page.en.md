## What the large chelicerae show {#s-417977d201}
Part and counterpart KUMIP 314091a,b from Utah’s Drumian Wheeler Formation preserve the paired front appendages, or chelicerae, with three segments each. Behind them are five pairs of prosomal limbs with two branches, followed by posterior appendages carrying plate-like lamellae. Read together, these parts make the holotype’s body plan more concrete than the label “Cambrian arthropod.”

The massive chelicerae support a predatory interpretation, but the holotype preserves no prey and does not record an attack. Published matrices place Megachelicerax among stem chelicerates, between sampled habeliid and synziphosurine groups; that is a conditional tree position, not a demonstrated ancestor–descendant sequence.
