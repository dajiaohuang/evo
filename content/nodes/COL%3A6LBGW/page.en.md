## Distribution and habitat {#s-14287883d9}
MDD displays this taxon as the Southern Bamboo Lemur and gives its type locality as the Mandena forestry station, about 10 km north of Tolagnaro (Fort-Dauphin). Eppley and colleagues studied feeding in fragmented southeastern littoral forest at Mandena; that study site is not a complete range.

## Ecological evidence {#s-8bdaad27b1}
An initial feeding-ecology study followed habituated lemurs for three months during the austral winter, combining instantaneous and continuous focal data with nutritional and secondary-compound analyses of foods. In that sample, foods had a low protein-to-fiber ratio, and the lemurs spent most feeding time on terrestrial grasses during the resource-poor winter. A later study made 103 full-day focal follows of three social groups during 2013 to examine factors associated with terrestrial feeding; its inference remains tied to littoral-forest samples.
