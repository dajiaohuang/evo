## Accepted name and source scope {#s-85f2488d22}
COL26.8 lists Cyrtodactylus cucphuongensis as an accepted species in Gekkonidae. Its ReptTraits v1-2 row records a maximum snout–vent length, nocturnal activity and oviparity, with trait-specific citations under the workbook’s Reptile Database 2023 name framework. These entries summarize cited reports; they do not establish a full description or uniformity among populations.

## The recorded maximum is 96 mm SVL {#s-e92dc084c9}
The ReptTraits entry gives a maximum snout–vent length (SVL) of 96 mm. SVL is measured from the snout to the vent, excluding the tail. Because this is a compiled maximum, it should not be read as a typical adult measurement or a limit on how large an individual can grow; separate sex-specific records are not supplied here.

## The activity label is nocturnal {#s-f61ae9741a}
The source classifies active time as nocturnal. That label describes the broad category recorded in the workbook; it does not specify a daily timetable or show whether activity changes with season, place or life stage. No microhabitat or diet value is interpreted here, so the record does not tell us where it forages or what it eats.

## Oviparity is recorded, without a clutch count {#s-dcd10c9fb2}
The reproductive-mode field is labelled oviparous. No supported mean or range for eggs per clutch is presented in this account, so the mode label should not be mistaken for information about how many eggs are laid or how often.
