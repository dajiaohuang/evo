## Read anatomy and name registration separately {#s-2be6949e63}
I. avatar is the type species of Ikrandraco. The 2014 study describes anatomy and proposes feeding hypotheses; a 2020 correction addresses whether the electronic work and ZooBank registration make the name available. The two publications address anatomy and nomenclature, respectively.

## From two flattened specimens to feeding hypotheses {#s-a4f42b43c5}
The [I. avatar guide](Ikrandraco_avatar/page.en.md) describes two specimens, their unknown exact horizons, the mandibular crest and teeth, then considers maturity, phylogeny, a hypothesis of briefly skimming the water surface to feed, and a possible throat pouch. Neither specimen represents a complete population, and skeletal anatomy does not directly show feeding behavior. Other formation-level pterosaurs provide context, not a measured division of niches.

## Organize fossil records by source classification {#s-ae03478674}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) path lists Ikrandraco as a genus and links one species occurrence guide beneath it. That database hierarchy locates the records; it does not, by itself, demonstrate a phylogenetic relationship.

## Evidence limits {#s-472a76979e}
The 2014 study proposes temporary skimming, a horny covering, a throat pouch and air-sac functions from anatomy; these remain hypotheses rather than observed behavior. A specimen number appears as V18046 in a figure caption and V18406 in the text, and that discrepancy remains unresolved. The exact horizons of the specimens are unknown.
