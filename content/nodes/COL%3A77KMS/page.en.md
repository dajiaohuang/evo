## Piptadenia affinis {#s-80a569cdfa}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Piptadenia affinis* as an accepted species of *Piptadenia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records recurved prickles arranged in rows and a climbing growth form. It lists the leaves as microphyllous (a small-leaved form) and the inflorescences as panicles (branched flower clusters), and describes the plant as terrestrial.
