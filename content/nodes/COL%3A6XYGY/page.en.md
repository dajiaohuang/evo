## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-bbab6d4005}
The 20 August 2026 COL26.8 release accepts Scutiger bhutanensis Delorme & Dubois, 2001 as a species. Its recorded placement is Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Scutiger (genus). This identifies the catalogue name and classification; biological observations below retain the scope of their own sources.
