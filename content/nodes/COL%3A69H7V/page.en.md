## The measured sample {#s-0029a1fd27}
COL26.8 accepts Celeus obrieni in Picidae. AVONET v7 records it under the HBW–BirdLife name used in 2020. Five birds were measured and all five had complete morphological sets, although an individual trait can still have its own usable sample count.

## Beak dimensions at standard landmarks {#s-70a49a078b}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 20.7 mm; width and depth at that edge average 8 and 8.2 mm. The measurements describe separate dimensions at standard landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-821ea4469f}
The unflattened wing, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, averages 139 mm; the tarsus averages 19.3 mm. A separate compilation gives mean mass as 114 g and names Dunning as its source category. The mass record does not show that the five measured birds were weighed.

## Forest, prey and perching classes {#s-6ea10b3d96}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, an Invertivore trophic niche and an Insessorial lifestyle (primarily perched above ground). These broad categories do not imply exclusive forest use or an invertebrate-only diet.

## Movement and historical map extent {#s-4a99eddf67}
The migration field labels the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span 17.62° to 4.24° south. They do not describe complete non-breeding movements or present occupancy; the geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Limits of the historical profile {#s-b70132cb4e}
The measurements are species means from historical AVONET samples, not diagnostic limits or estimates of individual variation. Mass has separate provenance and cannot inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map summarizes historical breeding and resident ranges. The name crosswalk links this record to the current catalogue entry but does not prove that concepts remained identical after taxonomic splits or lumps. Reproduction, present range, populations and conservation require other evidence.
