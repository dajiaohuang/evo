## What distinguishes Croton atrorufus in the record? {#s-521481398c}
Young branches have star-shaped or outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked leaves are oval or oblong, with appressed star-shaped hairs on both surfaces; margins are toothed and nectar glands lie along them. Young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are shortly stalked and wheel-shaped; female flowers are sessile, with separate sepals and a two-lobed style. Female petals are absent. In the bud, the calyx parts meet edge to edge in a valvate arrangement, and the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
