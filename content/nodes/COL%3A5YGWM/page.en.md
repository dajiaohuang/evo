## Chromolaena lilacina {#s-efed88b64d}
## Narrow leaves and varied head positions

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record notes no xylopodium. Stems are finely hairy or become smooth. Opposite leaves are linear to lance-shaped, entire and three-veined. The upper surface becomes smooth; the lower is recorded as gland-dotted and either smooth or stiffly hairy.

Corymb-like clusters or paired-branch clusters bear heads on short stalks or nearly stalkless. The bell-shaped involucre has outer bracts that stand upright or curve back and are finely hairy; inner bracts are upright and smooth. Corolla lobes are recorded as glandular or smooth, and the dry fruits (cypselae) as smooth or bristly. The pappus bristles are rough. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, without giving a locality or complete range.
