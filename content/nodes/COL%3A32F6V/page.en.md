## What leaf and flower traits does the regional field list? {#s-fde8be0a01}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Whorled leaves are linear, with blunt or squared-off bases and flat margins. The inflorescence is leafy. Flower stalks are 0.1–6 mm long, with bracteoles present. The 3–10 mm floral tube bears long, stiff or soft hairs outside; its blunt spur points downward. Petals may be purple or pink and are absent by the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube. No vesicle is recorded; the nectar gland at the ovary base points downward. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin blunt. These are the regional dataset's listed states, not a global variation survey.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d6605ce1d1}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial, without a locality or collection date.
