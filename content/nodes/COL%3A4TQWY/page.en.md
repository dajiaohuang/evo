## Spiny terminal clusters above dotted leaves {#s-17b369ea8e}
This spiny shrublet reaches 35 cm and often has thickened, spindle-shaped roots; its branches are whitish beige. The 10–15 mm leaves have small raised dots. Dense, spiny terminal cymes carry pink flowers about 15 mm across. Fruits have narrow valve wings; sandy flats are the recorded habitat.
