## Kleinia cephalophora {#s-fb1d9ab95b}
## Nodding buds open into upright yellow heads

Manning's 2013 account describes a succulent shrublet up to 200 mm tall, leafy while flowering, with stems up to 30 mm in diameter. Lance-shaped leaves are mostly 30–100 by 10–13 mm, fleshy, convex above and concave beneath, with recurved margins and a glaucous, waxy surface.

Solitary disc-shaped heads grow on stout, bract-bearing stalks. They nod in bud and later become erect; each broadly cylindrical head is bright yellow and about 15 mm across, with 6–8 involucral bracts, mostly 15–30 mm long. The pappus bristles (hair-like crown attached to the fruit) are 15–20 mm long. Rocky slopes are recorded as habitat.
