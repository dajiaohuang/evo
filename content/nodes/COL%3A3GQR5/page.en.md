## *Goeppertia allouia*: spiral bracts and two-flowered clusters {#s-313466b485}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character record places leaves in a spiral on stem or basal shoots; a leaf stalk may be absent or present. All flowering bracts are recorded as fertile and spiral, with upright margins, and are not red or orange. Each small flower cluster contains two flowers and one or two membranous bracteoles, the smaller leaf-like structures immediately beneath flowers.

The same field describes an unbroken corolla tube and one outer staminode, a sterile structure derived from a stamen. These are the documented floral and leaf characters in this regional profile; it does not provide a habitat or locality claim.
