## ## White flowers and ribbed pods {#s-00f34fdf49}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes an upright, semi-woody or herbaceous plant with pinnate leaves of three leaflets. Axillary pseudoracemes bear closed cleistogamous flowers, and the petals are white. The androecium is pseudo-monadelphous, with the stamens partly joined. The oblong fruit has ribbed, leathery valves, and the seeds are globose. The same regional dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
