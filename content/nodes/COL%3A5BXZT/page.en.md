## Stem, leaves, and fruit {#s-ceae1aa347}
The source describes a solitary, robust stem 3–19 m tall. The leaflets are hairy on their lower surface. The inflorescence has three to seven rachillae, and the fruits are densely arranged along them. The seed endosperm is homogeneous.
