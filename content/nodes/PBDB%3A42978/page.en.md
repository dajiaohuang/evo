## Recorded parent of Balaenidae {#s-dc69f4cbc2}
Balaenidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Chaeomysticeti as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
