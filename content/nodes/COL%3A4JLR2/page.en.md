## *Plantago napiformis* {#s-37b3ba4718}
The field describes a thickened taproot, inconspicuous caudex, and no above-ground stem. Blades range from oblanceolate to obovate, with tips acute to blunt and texture membranous to papery. The mature inflorescence is upright; stalk hairs spread outward. Flowers are radially symmetric, with four stamens and a hairless corolla tube. The fruit has three smooth, elliptic seeds with a flat inner side. These are selected source characters.
