## One- or two-flowered cymes and linear outer corona lobes {#s-d873cc48d4}
The source morphology field records indumentum over the full surface of the branches. Leaves are opposite, elliptic and flat-margined. Cymes bear one or two flowers. The sepals are hairy on the abaxial surface and glabrous at the margins. The corolla is bell-shaped, with triangular lobes that are finely hairy to hairy on the adaxial surface; the throat is hairy. Outer corona lobes are linear and longer than the gynostegium, which is sessile or subsessile. The oblong retinaculum is larger than the pollinia; caudicles descend obliquely and are not geniculate, and the pollinia are subovoid. The habitat label is “Rupícola” (rupicolous, associated with rock). This single field does not establish variation or full diagnostic scope, and no locality or range was assessed.
