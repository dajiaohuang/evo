## Catalogue placement {#s-1303bf7634}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 lists *Deguelia hatschbachii* as an accepted species in Fabaceae, the legume family.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-721f5ac73e}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset labels the species terrestrial. This field gives a habitat category, not a locality, range or measure of sampling coverage.
