## Accepted name and placement {#s-9171958a9d}
COL26.8 accepts Boulenophrys daiyunensis (Lyu, Wang & Wang in Lyu et al., 2021) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Megophryidae (family) → Boulenophrys (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Daiyun reserve and a lower Tingxi locality {#s-157ed1d15b}
The account examined the type series and six additional males from Tingxi Farm near Xiamen at about 400 m. Its cited range summary distinguished Daiyun Mountain Nature Reserve at 1000–1250 m from Tingxi at about 400 m. The holotype came from Daiyun Village at about 1040 m. These are separate locality records, not a continuous occupied elevational band or evidence of equivalent conditions at both sites.

## Sex-specific measurements from the examined material {#s-58aa08012c}
The diagnosis reported ten adult males at 27.6–30.9 mm snout–vent length and three females at 33.7–35.6 mm. Selected features included an unnotched tongue, distinct tympanum, vomerine ridges and teeth, rough dorsal skin with an interrupted X-shaped ridge, and rudimentary toe webbing with narrow lateral fringes. These are sample measurements and selected diagnostic characters, not universal size bounds or a complete identification key.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-7ff51c55f5}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys daiyunensis as smaller in body size than the gutu sample. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator. The selected character does not establish population-wide frequency, complete diagnosis, diet or developmental biology.
