## Bulbs and leaflets {#s-7baaa7c216}
The source describes an acaulescent geophyte with large, gummy bulbs and three-part leaves; the leaflets may be glabrous or hairy and are often purple beneath. Flowers are yellow. Younger bulbs are always viscous, while their outer layers are only sometimes soft and separable after drying; mature bulbs are hard and usually carry adhering sand.
