## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-729123bc4f}
COL26.8 records Geophis dunni as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.
