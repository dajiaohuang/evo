## Five-millimeter leaves and a four-chambered ovary {#s-41ca960fea}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists two leaf-stalk ranges: 1–3.5 mm and 3.6–5 mm. The leaf blade is elliptic or oval-elliptic, tapering to a point, with an acute base; short fine hairs are recorded along the lower midrib. Flower clusters grow on branches, with one to five branches per fascicle and one to three flowers on each axis. The field does not specify petal colour. It records the stamens as connate, or joined, and the ovary as four-chambered with axile placentation: ovules are attached along the central axis. A separate habitat field labels the setting “terrícola,” or terrestrial. These are the fields' reported details; no additional flower description is supplied.

## A terrestrial label beside limited floral data {#s-85eb76c935}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category does not add a distribution claim to the limited floral data.
