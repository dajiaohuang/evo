## The Devonian tree behind the older bound {#s-8897c011a6}
The atlas uses *Pteridophyta* as a broad historical navigation group. Its 385–0 Ma display begins with a Middle Devonian fern-like tree reconstruction, but that fossil is a cladoxylopsid—not evidence for the first crown ferns or horsetails, and not a known direct ancestor.

At Gilboa–Schoharie, attached crowns and trunks assigned to the Wattieza–Eospermatopteris reconstruction preserve enough connected material to support a fern-like cladoxylopsid tree. The reconstruction comes from the fossil material itself. That specimen does not resolve how cladoxylopsids relate to living fern or horsetail lineages.

## Reading the displayed range {#s-599162c052}
The atlas carries this historical pteridophyte-grade envelope from about 385 million years ago to living fern and horsetail lineages. The older bound is a rounded Middle Devonian sample, not the first appearance of crown ferns or crown horsetails. The fern-like form of the fossil is not a claim that it sits on either living group’s direct ancestral line.
