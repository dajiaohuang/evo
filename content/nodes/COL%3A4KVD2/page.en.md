## The name and this data account {#s-ccd69a345f}
COL26.8 accepts Pogoniulus subsulphureus in Lybiidae. AVONET v7 records it under the HBW–BirdLife 2020 taxonomy: six individuals were measured and four had a complete morphological set. The species-level values below summarize that historical sample; not every trait necessarily uses all six birds.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-fa657c1008}
AVONET's mean culmen length from the front edge of the nostril to the bill tip is 8.8 mm. At the nostril, the recorded width is 5.2 mm and depth is 5 mm. These standardized landmarks keep length, width and depth distinct; the means are not identification cutoffs or individual ranges.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-73ccc7f91b}
The unflattened wing mean is 50.3 mm, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary; mean tarsus length is 13.1 mm. The listed mass is 10.5 g, attributed to Dunning. It is a separately sourced species-level value, not a claim that the measured morphological birds were weighed.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-b08b9a0785}
The AVONET record uses Forest for habitat, Frugivore for trophic niche and Insessorial for its perching-lifestyle category. These broad classifications summarize the source record for comparison; they do not make the diet or habitat exclusive.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-addca1971c}
AVONET codes migration as sedentary. The dataset's historical breeding/resident geography runs from latitude -12.27° to 9.47°. These endpoints describe the mapped record, not an individual's location or a full account of non-breeding movements; the geography is compiled separately from the morphological sample.

## Evidence limits {#s-bba8053537}
These are historical AVONET v7 summaries, based on six measured birds with four complete sets; the values do not show individual variation or serve as diagnosis. Dunning is the separate provenance for the 10.5 g mass. Habitat, niche and migration are broad categories, and the latitude limits summarize historical breeding/resident geography only. The crosswalk connects the AVONET and COL26.8 names without proving that their species concepts are identical.
