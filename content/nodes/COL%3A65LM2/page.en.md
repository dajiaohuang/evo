## Sample and historical name {#s-d1b6768a82}
COL26.8 accepts Agamia agami in Ardeidae. The AVONET v7 record uses the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and includes 5 measured individuals, 4 with a complete morphological set. Usable sample sizes can differ among traits.

## Beak dimensions {#s-19cf85aba8}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 146 mm; width and depth at that landmark average 12.5 mm and 17.4 mm. These are separate dimensions at standardized landmarks, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-e59ac28919}
Unflattened wing length from the carpal joint to the longest primary averages 273.6 mm; tarsus length averages 98.3 mm. The compiled mean mass is 566.99 g, classed as Dunning. It is a separate species-average value, not a weight recorded for every bird in the morphology sample.

## Habitat and feeding categories {#s-45d0727c6d}
AVONET classifies habitat as Forest, trophic niche as Aquatic predator and primary lifestyle as Terrestrial. These are broad comparison labels; they do not mean the bird uses only forest or that every feeding event is aquatic.

## Migration and historical map {#s-8962a77604}
The migration category is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints −18.44° to 19.63°. This range summary is not a complete account of non-breeding movement or evidence of current occupancy throughout the mapped area. Geography and morphology were compiled separately.

## Two EltonTraits field groups {#s-68004c48db}
EltonTraits 1.0 also links a historical same-name, same-family record for Agamia agami, with diet and foraging-position groups. It uses an earlier bird classification. Linking it to the COL26.8 name helps locate the record but does not settle later splits or synonymies.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-d7afb8cb2f}
The diet entry assigns all its relative weight (100%) to fish. Code B indicates reasonable confidence in the source but possible uncertainty in the proportions. This is a standardized estimate from verbal description, not a measured diet composition; the record does not quantify how often fish were eaten.

## Water-surface and ground positions {#s-d20a36dbbf}
The species-level foraging entry assigns 50% to the water surface or just below it and 50% to the ground. These weights estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, a complete habitat description or whole-day time budgets. They do not give water depth, flight height or habitat area.

## Shared source label {#s-514fc96009}
Both EltonTraits field groups carry the Ref_1 source label in the archive. The label connects the diet and foraging summaries to their recorded source trail; it does not turn either estimate into a direct observation.

## Scope of the historical summaries {#s-7f4361e7cb}
The AVONET values are averages from the listed sample, not diagnostic characters or individual-variation ranges; mass comes from another source. Habitat, diet and migration are broad classifications, and the map concerns historical breeding and resident ranges. EltonTraits weights standardize literature descriptions and are not direct measures of intake or time. Historical name links associate the entry with COL26.8 but do not prove the species concepts identical or establish current distribution, populations or conservation status.
