## How short is its flower stalk? {#s-0118f9d9b3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a short, cylindrical flower stalk 5–20 cm long. Its cylindrical leaf blades have blunt tips and a surface listed as transversely wrinkled or papillose; the ellipsoid spike bears up to ten flowers. The field lists alternatives for seed shape and some other traits.
