## Thamnochortus nutans {#s-81d670a2d3}
## Female spikelets on seasonally wet high ground

The regional description gives Thamnochortus nutans a tufted habit and a height of up to 60 cm. Its stems are simple, though sterile stems may be much branched; female spikelets reach 20 mm and have firm, cartilage-like bracts. The recorded habitat is shallow soil above 900 m that becomes wet seasonally.
