## The source concept {#s-bcbb61e9ce}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Cochranella euhystrix* in row Anf0952 under Cochranella, Centrolenidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-5c5d5db7a3}
For Anf0952, the compilation tags terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.
