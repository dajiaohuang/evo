## Micropholis porphyrocarpa (Baehni) Monach. {#s-1e976a586a}
## Margin-reaching veins and darkening fruit

Micropholis porphyrocarpa has elliptic to oblong leaves arranged in two ranks or spirally. Their side veins reach the leaf margins. The flowers are unisexual, with five sepals and five corolla lobes; the tubular corolla encloses five stamens. Awl-shaped staminodes are present, while a nectary disk is absent. The style projects beyond the corolla. The fruit is vinaceous to nearly black and contains one seed with a wrinkled or rough coat.
