## Philodendron rhizomatosum: recorded morphology {#s-b438f0b5fa}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and a pale-green stem prophyll. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous; the conspicuous cylindrical petiole is listed as green or dark green. Blades are entire, heart-shaped or ovate, with entire margins. The field also gives “3 to 5” without making clear what this count describes.

It records one terminal sympodial inflorescence, a cylindrical green peduncle, and a spathe that is not constricted; the outer spathe tube is described as light green or light pink. The spadix is subsessile. Flowers are recorded with 4 or 5 stamens, staminodes narrowed at the base, an ovoid gynoecium and ovary, 6 or 7 locules, 3 or 4 ovules, subbasal placentation and a capitate stigma. Fruit and seed states are unknown in the source.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.
