## A terrestrial source category {#s-f9c288cc1f}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens juruensis Broth. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data, and this page summarizes its habitat row.

The row is labelled “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

The category is not tied to a collection locality or survey date and does not establish a complete range or sampling coverage.

This habitat field is the only biological content included from the regional record; it is not a full account of the species.
