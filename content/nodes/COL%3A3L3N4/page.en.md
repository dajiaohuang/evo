## Herreria cipoana {#s-0ea6f7e4c2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry for Herreria cipoana records angular, hairy, spiny branches and leathery, oblanceolate to narrowly obovate leaves, with a simple raceme. Several flower characters are explicitly marked “not observed,” including the inflorescence axis, pedicels, buds and most floral parts. Habitat is listed as terrestrial; this partial record does not support a fuller floral description.
