## What shapes its tangled, cushion-like growth? {#s-41b41ea5d5}
A 2012 regional account describes *Anthochortus ecklonii* as a dioecious perennial reaching 50 cm, often forming a cushion. Its sparsely branched, ridged culms (stems) are tangled, and it produces stolons, horizontal stems that extend from the plant. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## Which wet habitats does the account name? {#s-7368dea829}
The same regional account lists damp slopes and seeps. This habitat description does not specify a survey date or how frequently the species occurs there.
