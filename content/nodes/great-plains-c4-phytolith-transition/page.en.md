## Grass phytoliths trace a Great Plains C3–C4 shift {#s-3a169e5861}
Fourteen selected palaeosol assemblages from Nebraska and Kansas record an increase in chloridoid-type and other potentially C4 PACMAD grass short cells between 8 and 5.5 Ma. By about 5.5 Ma, these forms made up roughly 50–60% of the grass component in the sampled mixed C3–C4 habitats; the signal is regional, and these morphologies do not all diagnose C4 photosynthesis.
