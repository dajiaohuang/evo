## Midbody scales and the type series {#s-c369b18b51}
Jins, Sampaio, and Gower described Uropeltis bhupathyi from six type specimens collected at Anaikatty. The diagnosis specifies 17 dorsal scale rows at midbody. The holotype has 19 rows behind the head, 17 along most of the body, and 15 before the vent. The six-specimen type series is distinct from three additional referred specimens.

## Surface activity and an unresolved placement {#s-0af9679251}
The authors recorded surface activity in the morning and evening after rain and considered a fossorial life likely, but they did not conduct digging surveys. Their molecular analysis recovered the species as sister to sampled U. cf. beddomii; the vouchers of that comparison were not examined, and patchy sampling limits the placement.
