## Branches, leaflets and flowers {#s-1926698cbe}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional row describes brown branches covered with long, stiff hairs and without glandular hairs. The stipules are about 4 mm long and linear to triangular. Leaves bear 5–7 oblong to elliptic leaflets, each about 25 × 7 mm; the leaflet edge is the same colour as its blade. The inflorescence axis is hairy and carries lance-shaped bracts, while the smaller bracteoles are also lance-shaped. The flag petal (standard) is broad-elliptic to nearly round. The row's fruit wording is ambiguous and does not support a clear fruit description. These are details from one regional entry, not a complete or global account.
