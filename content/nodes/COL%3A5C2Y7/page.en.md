## Tiny ribbon-shaped fronds {#s-4bdc4d8cf0}
The regional account describes this plant as perennial, or perhaps sometimes annual. Its submerged, ribbon-shaped fronds are 2–7 mm long and 0.3–0.8 mm wide, with 2–4 marginal teeth. Two to seven fronds cohere in groups that are often star-like; their bases lie near the water surface and their tips bend downward. Each frond bears one inflorescence. Flowers and fruits are rare, the seeds are unknown, and flowering plants are shorter and wider than vegetative ones.

## Pools and lakes {#s-8f3e29b238}
The habitat field lists pools and lakes. The source further says the plants are usually found mixed with other floating plants or between emergent reeds, and are not very gregarious.

## Evidence limits {#s-d388815ec0}
This is a regional account cited to Cook (2004). It leaves annual status uncertain, records seeds as unknown, and supplies no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
