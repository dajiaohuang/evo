## Anthurium lacerdae: a regional morphology record {#s-4ab961b934}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records an erect stem but does not characterize its cataphylls or prophylls. Petioles are cylindrical and smooth. Leaf blades are entire and flat, oblong or elliptic, with acute tips and bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is coded as obtuse on both surfaces, secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as less than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, oblong spathe forming an obtuse angle with it. The spathe is not stipitate. The fruit is a berry, but no color character is supplied.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The dataset records the habitat category “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
