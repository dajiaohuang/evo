## Ardisia geniculata Lundell {#s-b37cd9fa89}
## Rusty scales on a small tree

Ardisia geniculata is a 4–5 m tree with slender, rusty, scaly branchlets. Its narrow elliptic to oblanceolate leaves are 5.8–11 cm long, membranous and sparsely scaly; 12–16 pairs of lateral veins are conspicuous beneath. Terminal and axillary, twice-branched panicles are under 4 cm long and bear a few white flowers. The flowers have five parts, and the ovary contains about 25 ovules. The species is native to Panama.
