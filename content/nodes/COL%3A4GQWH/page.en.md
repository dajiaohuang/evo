## *Phoradendron northropiae* {#s-74fc3267c6}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: flower clusters (inflorescences) — axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as hemiparasitic (partly dependent on a host plant; Portuguese: Hemiparasita). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
