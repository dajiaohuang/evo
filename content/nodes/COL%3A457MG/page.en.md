## An elliptic leaf with a fine-haired underside {#s-be742f5f88}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field allows either no stem hairs or simple hairs, and records monopodial growth. Leaves are elliptic, with an upper-surface midrib that may be raised or channelled; the underside is puberulent, with fine, short hairs. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below the leaf nodes are absent. Compressed inflorescence branchlets have no bracteoles beneath the flowers. One inflorescence is listed per axillary shoot, in the leaf axil and in an opposite pyramidal arrangement. The field gives alternatives without frequencies.

## A three-chambered ovary and a globose fruit {#s-15474aac22}
The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is top-shaped (turbinate) and puberulent. Sepals are free, the ovary has three chambers, and the calyx is listed as not opening by tearing. Its lobes enlarge on the globose fruit. These details are from one regional dataset field, which reports no frequencies.

## A terrestrial label, without site details {#s-378d459047}
The same regional dataset classifies the species as terrestrial (terrícola). This is a habitat category only; the field gives no locality or more specific habitat description.
