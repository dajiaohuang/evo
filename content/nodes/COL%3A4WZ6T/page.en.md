## Sesbania punicea {#s-64828de665}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Sesbania punicea* as an accepted species of *Sesbania* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records smooth, hairless stems, opposite leaflets, red petals, and oblong fruits that do not split open. The same dataset lists both aquatic and terrestrial habitat states, so its habitat is not reduced here to only one of those settings.
