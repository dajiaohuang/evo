## Branches and leaflets {#s-1f2e81d547}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes armed branches with cylindrical cross-sections and spiny stipules. Leaflets are listed as narrowly oblong, oblong or oblong-obovate, with notched or shallowly notched tips and oblique bases. Their venation is cladodromous: side veins branch freely near the edge and fade before reaching it. The upper surface is glabrescent (becoming hairless); the lower surface is recorded as either glabrescent or hairy only along the primary veins.

## Inflorescences {#s-15e51647f6}
The inflorescences are terminal or axillary and described as either racemes, with flowers along one axis, or panicles, which are branched clusters. Their bracts are leaf-like.

## Flowers {#s-8de0f0167c}
The calyx is urn-shaped. Petals are lilac, hairs are present on the standard or banner petal, and the stamens are monadelphous, united into one bundle.

## Fruit {#s-8dd17f0ba9}
The fruit is described as a samara, or winged dry fruit. Its seed-bearing core is warty.

## Habitat category {#s-5d0649cf45}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
