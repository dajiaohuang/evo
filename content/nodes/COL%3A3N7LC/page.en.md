## One skull in a CT-based comparison {#s-c83b1eacd4}
Martín-Jiménez and Pérez-García (2021) examined skull MNHN.ZA.AC 1870-9. Their reconstruction describes a nearly horizontal dorsal cranial-cavity profile, a long, narrow olfactory nerve and limited lateral expansion of the cerebral hemispheres. These observations belong to that skull, not a survey of species-wide variation.

The skull account is limited to one specimen. Its anatomy cannot establish variation among ages or populations, abundance, current range or conservation status; this page reproduces no figure or table. They are not measurements of one animal, individual ranges, wild-life means or current surveys. Historical records may be shared across older species and subspecies.
