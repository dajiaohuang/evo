## Form and diagnostic notes {#s-62f3ad8611}
Letty's 1973 account describes plants 40–75 cm tall. The deciduous leaves reach 75 cm; petiole length and proportion vary, and the petiole may be green, purple, or purple-striped or mottled at the base, sometimes with soft hairs near its base. Blades may be spotted or unspotted, oblong-hastate to triangular-hastate or nearly round-hastate, reaching 40 cm long and 25 cm wide. The lower lobes range from short and rounded to long triangles with rounded tips, or strap-shaped with blunt or acute tips. The peduncle reaches 75 cm and may be green or purple-stained or mottled, sometimes with soft hairs near the base.

Plants 40–75 cm tall. Leaves deciduous, up to 75 cm long; petiolate; petiole variable in length and proportion to leaf-blade, green or purple or striped and mottled with purple at the base, sometimes with soft hairs towards the base; blade maculate or immaculate, oblong- or triangular- to orbicular hastate, up to 40 cm long and 25 cm broad; lower lobes variable from short and rounded to long triangular with rounded apex, or strap-shaped with a blunt or acute apex. Peduncle up to 75 cm long, green or sometimes stained or mottled with purple, sometimes with soft hairs towards the base. Spathe 2.5–17 cm long, seldom without a dark purple area at the base inside, varying in colour from white through ivory, cream, straw-coloured to pale yellow and, seldom, coral pink, the colour outside greenish at the base, intensifying upwards as the spathe matures; tube sometimes open to the base, mostly variably convolute, broadening towards the apex; limb subtruncate to gradually tapering, apiculate, somewhat recurved. Spadix sessile or stipitate; ovaries not interspersed with staminodes, 3-locular with 3–4 ovules per locule. Fruits few to many, up to 2 cm in diam., green, crowded, 4–8 seeded, upper surface flattened, persistent stigma short and sunk.

The spathe is 2.5–17 cm long. Its inner base usually has a dark-purple area; the outer color ranges from white, ivory, cream, straw, or pale yellow to rarely coral pink. The outside is greenish at the base and deepens upward as it matures. The tube may open to the base, is usually variably convolute, and widens toward the tip; the limb is nearly truncate to gradually tapering, apiculate, and somewhat recurved. The spadix is sessile or stalked. Ovaries are not interspersed with staminodes; they have three locules with three or four ovules per locule. Fruits are few to many, green, crowded, up to 2 cm across, with 4–8 seeds, a flattened upper surface, and a short, sunken persistent stigma.

The account distinguishes this species from Z. aethiopica by its deciduous leaves, absence of staminodes among the female organs, and fruit that remains a firm berry until germination rather than turning orange and pulpy on the peduncle. The account uses a broad species concept that includes forms formerly treated as separate species. It notes considerable variation in leaf shape and maculation and in spathe shape and colour; some variation is linked to geographic distribution, and three subspecies are upheld.

## Habitat in the Eastern Cape account {#s-490500cf1f}
The 2019 regional account records rocky hillsides, forest margins, and streambanks, and places the species in the Drakensberg Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Savanna, and Indian Ocean Coastal Belt vegetation units.
