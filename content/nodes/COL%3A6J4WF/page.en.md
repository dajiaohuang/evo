## Rock and terrestrial labels in a regional entry {#s-69a2d6f1af}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Fissidens acacioides Schrad. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its entries are habitat categories, not collection localities.

One category is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

A separate category is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

These fields give no collection locality, survey date, complete range or sampling coverage.

The two source-labelled categories are the only biological content in this regional entry; they do not describe the species in full.
