## Simple leaves and a dense terminal cluster {#s-69e0ea0b5a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect plant up to 1.5 m tall, with simple leaves: each has one undivided blade rather than separate leaflets. Dense clusters of many yellow flowers grow at the branch tips, with persistent oval-lanceolate bracts. The obovate pod is black or brown and hairless; the seeds are recorded as black or ochre-brown.
