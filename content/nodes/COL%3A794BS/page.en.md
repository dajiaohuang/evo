## *Restio hyalinus*: white stem tubercles and pale sheath tips {#s-ed78efd968}
This tufted, rhizomatous perennial reaches 50 cm. Its branched stems bear white tubercles—small rounded projections—and its spathe tips are hyaline, meaning thin and translucent. Two or three flowers occur in each two-ranked spikelet; the description records three styles, the slender parts above the ovary.

It is recorded on well-drained sandy flats.
