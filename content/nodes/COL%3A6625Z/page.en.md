## Agarista duartei (Sleumer) Judd {#s-95bdcda33b}
## Folded, narrow leaves and a globose capsule

Agarista duartei has upright shoots and rigidly leathery, linear leaves folded lengthwise. The leaf tip narrows gradually, and the margins may be flat or slightly rolled under. The upper surface is hairy; the lower surface ranges from smooth to sparsely hairy, especially along the midvein. Axillary racemes carry tubular to urn-shaped flowers; the source lists several possible corolla colors, from white or cream through green, yellow or red. The globose capsule is described as hairy or woolly. Several indumentum and flower-form alternatives in the source are not resolved.
