## Senecio colpodes {#s-559363f2d3}
## White wool beneath the leaves, radiate heads above

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes an upright, herbaceous perennial. Leaves cluster near the base and become smaller farther up the stem. Their blade is oblanceolate (widest above the middle), with a tapered base and scalloped or lobed edges. The upper surface has glandular hairs; dense white woolly hairs cover the underside.

Radiate flower heads grow in corymb-shaped clusters. Each has 5–15 involucral bracts around its base. Yellow, strap-shaped female florets form the rays, while yellow tubular bisexual florets fill the centre. The style branches end bluntly in a spreading crown of hairs. The dry fruits are cylindrical, ribbed and hairless; their white pappus (bristle crown) persists. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
