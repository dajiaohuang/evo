## Hairy branches and a velvety fruit {#s-1777e79f73}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes the branches as hirsute and the leaves as obovate, with blunt tips and blades longer than 5 cm. The calyx is hairless. The corolla tube and seeds are both recorded as unknown; the fruit is velutinous, with a velvety covering.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-9ecc69ba59}
The linked Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Bonyunia venusta* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
