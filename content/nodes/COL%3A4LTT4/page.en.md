## Distribution records {#s-ec03a7733f}
A historical synthesis estimated the Tapanuli orangutan’s extent of occurrence at about 40,796 km² in the 1890s and 21,313 km² in the 1940s, compared with about 1,023 km² today. The historical figures depend on attributing populations south and southeast of the present range to *Pongo tapanuliensis*. Northern records may instead represent *P. abelii*, and cited specimens outside the current range had not been genetically identified. These are conditional reconstructions, not a verified complete range history.

## Evidence limits {#s-82870aeefb}
Historical range proportions rely on older records with unresolved taxonomic attribution; they are not a complete, genetically verified range history or a substitute for a current population or conservation assessment.
