## Mikania teixeirae {#s-68e1a9c4da}
## Five basal veins on smooth, leathery leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes upright, smooth branches with hollow pith. Opposite, elliptic leaves are leathery, with entire margins, nearly heart-shaped bases and blunt tips. Both surfaces are smooth, and five main veins radiate from the base.

Loose panicles carry stalked heads. Lance-shaped bracts sit near the top of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around a head are blunt-tipped. The funnel-shaped corolla is longer than its tube and has triangular, smooth lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. A separate habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; the regional record gives no locality or complete range.
