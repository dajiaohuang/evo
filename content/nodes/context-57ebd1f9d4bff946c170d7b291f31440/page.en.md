## Source concept and record identity {#s-fc7ecd0e56}
AmphiBIO v1 names Mannophryne speeri in row Anf0111, listing genus Mannophryne, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging-stratum flags: terrestrial and aquatic {#s-5d8c66b1cf}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial); Aqu=1 (aquatic). These are broad source categories: 1 marks a listed category, 0 is the stored code, and NA remains missing data. The fields do not describe seasonal or developmental changes.

A later TetrapodTraits revision notice (3.0.0, 5 August 2026; revision history dated 9 March 2026) covers selected body-mass and microhabitat entries propagated from earlier compilations. This row has not been compared at row level, so its original entries remain and no replacement is inferred; the notice does not establish that every original value was wrong.
