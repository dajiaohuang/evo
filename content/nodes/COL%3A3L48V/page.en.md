## Pale flowers with long tubes and axillary cormlets {#s-7a96499f25}
Hesperantha huttonii is 0.25–0.60 m tall and has pale-pink flowers with an elongate tube, typically 30–39 mm, and large yellow anthers. Cormlets are usually produced in the axils of the lower leaves; flowering is reported from January to June.

## Damp, partly shaded rocky sites {#s-7416057b36}
The regional account records damp, partly shaded rocky sites and lists the Indian Ocean Coastal Belt and Drakensberg Grassland.
