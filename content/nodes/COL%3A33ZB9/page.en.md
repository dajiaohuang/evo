## Dalbergia villosa {#s-59c0600373}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Dalbergia villosa* records 13–25 oblong leaflets, sometimes 29, purple-violet flowers with nine stamens, oblong-elliptic pods. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
