## Restio distractus {#s-0e2541c328}
## Curled branches and woolly bracts

Restio distractus is recorded as a tangled, stolon-forming perennial up to 40 cm tall. Its branched stems curl and carry dense, fine tubercles; the sheaths have a woolly bract that projects beyond them. The regional account places it on high, rocky slopes and plateaus.
