## Mimosa parvipinna {#s-824ca29264}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa parvipinna* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes backward-pressed stem hairs, capitate glandular hairs, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and short shoots. Its flower clusters are globose; flowers have five perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and short-haired petal lobes. The source also records the plant as terrestrial.
