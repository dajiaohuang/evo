## Davilla macrocarpa Eichler {#s-febbf0bd02}
## Smooth leaves and included stamens

Davilla macrocarpa has smooth stems and hairless, leathery leaves. The petiole is crenate, and the secondary veins arch toward the margin. Flowers may be terminal or lateral, with stamens enclosed within the other floral parts. The filaments are cylindrical, petals are split into two lobes, and the inner sepals meet closely. The fruit has two carpels and one or two seeds, each partly covered by an aril.
