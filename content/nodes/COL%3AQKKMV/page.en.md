## Accepted name and placement {#s-83a4048dfb}
COL26.8 accepts Microhyla hmongorum Hoang, Nguyen, Phan, Pham, Ninh, Wang, Jiang, Ziegler & Nguyen, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Microhyla (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## The first reported Chinese records {#s-93054b1519}
The 2023 paper added records from Mengla in Xishuangbanna and Yuanyang in Honghe, Yunnan, China, to its cited earlier range in Vietnam, Laos and Myanmar. These new locality records are retained without independently revalidating every older citation or assuming continuous occupancy between countries.

## Standing water in disturbed areas {#s-004316d110}
The account described frogs in highly disturbed settings including rice paddies, still ponds and rain puddles, and reported breeding mainly during April–September. These are the authors’ habitat and seasonal observations, not experimental disturbance tolerance or a complete reproductive schedule for every population.
