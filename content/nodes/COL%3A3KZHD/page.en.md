## What does the regional account describe about this grey shrub? {#s-57db8d7273}
This twiggy shrub grows to 1.5 m. Its grey, velvety leaves are obovate to oblanceolate, with teeth toward the tip. Small yellow flowers form crowded, one-sided, spreading racemose clusters.

## What additional hair and growth details does the revision report? {#s-42e32766cd}
A 1980 revision notes velvety hairs on young branches, leaves, stipules and inflorescence bracts. It describes terminal cymose panicles on the youngest branchlets, with many small flowers arranged along one side. If protected, the plant can grow into a leafy bush over 1 m tall and 1 m across.

## What soils are reported? {#s-92c014100b}
The regional account reports clay, sandy and limestone soils. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
