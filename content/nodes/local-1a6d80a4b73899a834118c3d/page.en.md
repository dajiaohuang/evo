Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Litoria umbonata* under genus *Litoria*, family Pelodryadidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.
