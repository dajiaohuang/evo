## A 40–50 mm flower tube {#s-6b1c0da91f}
Geissorhiza stenosiphon grows 15–30 cm tall and has narrow, grooved, cylindrical leaves. Its slender perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts) is unusually long at 40–50 mm; the flowers are white with a pink reverse and the style divides at or below the anther bases.

## Rocky sandstone above 400 m {#s-8b7d709ad6}
The cited account records rocky sandstone slopes above 400 m. That threshold describes the source record, not a complete elevational range.
