## The source concept {#s-cd5b386829}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Churamiti maridadi* as row Anf0558, in the genus Churamiti, family Bufonidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-867bd3b4aa}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) and arboreal (Arb = 1) as broad foraging categories. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-e7321376e7}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 53 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-09942db341}
The maximum reproductive output listed is 1 event per year. These are literature-compiled extrema or counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
