## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-71c29274d3}
COL26.8 records Thamnodynastes silvai as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4c9eb1d7e6}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as animal-eating (Carnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Stark et al. 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-cb0556ad78}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
