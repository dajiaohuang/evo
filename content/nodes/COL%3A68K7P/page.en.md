## What leaf, flower and fruit traits does the regional field describe? {#s-5e38bf317e}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field describes an erect above-ground stem with glandular hairs, bristles or long soft hairs. Opposite leaves have a flat upper surface, similar hairs, a heart-shaped or lobed base and toothed or fringed margins; their main veins arise from the base. The inflorescence may be a scorpioid cyme (a curved cluster whose flowers open in sequence) or a dichasium (a branching cluster with a central flower). The floral cup may be cup-shaped, glandular or hairy. The calyx is membranous; its lobes may be single-lobed, glandular, bristly or fringed with hairs. Pink petals spread outward. The ovary has three chambers and the style is hairless. The fruit placentas are smooth or wrinkled, the fruit is obtriangular in outline, and the seed bears a beak-like tip. These alternatives belong to a regional database field.

## What rocky habitat is listed? {#s-af1a399084}
One Brazilian Flora row records rupicolous habitat, meaning growth on rock surfaces. It gives no locality or collection date.

## Is a terrestrial habitat also recorded? {#s-1fb7ec182d}
A second row in the same regional dataset also lists terrestrial habitat. The two rows provide habitat categories but not their locations or relative frequency.
