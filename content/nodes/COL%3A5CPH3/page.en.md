## Xyris dissitifolia {#s-19c3b33c15}
## Threadlike leaves and an elongated spike

The description gives Xyris dissitifolia narrow, threadlike to nearly cylindrical leaves and an elongated, egg-shaped spike. The flowering stalk measures 20–70 cm; the spike carries 6–9 sterile bracts and up to ten flowers. The habitat field is terrestrial.
