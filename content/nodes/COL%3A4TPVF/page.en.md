## Rumex pulcher {#s-82de8b7d6b}
## Toothed fruiting valves on a branched perennial

Rumex pulcher is a perennial herb up to 0.6 m long. Its angular, flexuous stems branch below the middle. Ovate-oblong leaves have heart-shaped bases, acute tips and shallow teeth; branched flower clusters bear greenish flowers in whorls, with most whorls subtended by a leaf. The inner perianth segments are toothed. Pale-brown fruiting valves are leathery, netted with pits and coarsely toothed; each encloses a nut up to 4 mm long. Flowering is recorded from August to April. The source notes pasture and mountain-slope weeds across the Mediterranean region and southwest Asia, as well as Sub-Escarpment Grassland.
