## Pale rays and smooth-edged ray flowers {#s-eb80b0d813}
The record describes a softly hairy annual that spreads along the ground, 5–20 cm tall, with blunt, oblanceolate leaves. Its single flower heads have yellow centers and pale-blue or cream rays; the outer ray flowers lack the bristly pappus found on many daisy-family fruits. The source notes that the species is variable and often confused with F. dubia.
