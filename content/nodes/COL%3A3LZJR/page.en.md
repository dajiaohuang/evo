## Hippobroma longiflora {#s-972efdf552}
## Long white flower tubes rise above toothed leaves

The historical Flora of Panama description records erect or leaning herbs, usually 30–50 cm tall but ranging from 15 to 90 cm. Their mostly sessile, thin leaves are elliptic to oblanceolate, with irregular coarse teeth; smaller, thickened teeth occur between the larger ones. The leaves are usually 10–20 cm long and 2–5 cm wide, with source-reported extremes down to 5 cm long and 1 cm wide.

The flowers have a ribbed, angled base and a long, narrow corolla tube 5–13.5 cm long. The corolla is white, sometimes with pale-green veins, and its spreading lobes are 1.3–2.7 cm long. The hanging capsules are 1.2–2.0 cm long.
