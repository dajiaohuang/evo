## Twenty-seven examined adults with half-webbed toes {#s-03ef2094b6}
The diagnosis reported twenty-four adult males at 48.3–62.5 mm snout–vent length and three females at 58.6–68.2 mm. It described vomerine ridges and teeth, toes with half webbing and wide lateral fringes, and dense black nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II in breeding males, with a subgular vocal sac. The reported measurements concern the examined series. The toe description is not a measurement of locomotor ability, and the finger characters do not supply breeding-season dates.
