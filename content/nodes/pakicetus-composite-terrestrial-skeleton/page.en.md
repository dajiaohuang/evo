## Pakicetus: a terrestrial skeleton assembled from one locality {#s-0c23471a72}
The published Pakicetus reconstruction combines skull H-GSP 96231 with separately catalogued vertebrae and limb bones from one locality, including humerus H-GSP 92042 and calcaneum H-GSP 96420. Their weight-bearing anatomy supports terrestrial locomotion, while the skull retains diagnostic early-cetacean anatomy. This is a locality composite, not one associated skeleton or a direct ancestor; the cited 2001 morphological tree also did not recover Hippopotamidae as the immediate cetacean sister group.
