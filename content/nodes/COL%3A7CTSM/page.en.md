## Three fields with citations {#s-230be165fc}
Pinned COL26.8 accepts Trilepida nicefori in Leptotyphlopidae. Its limited ReptTraits v1-2 row reports maximum total length, microhabitat and reproductive mode, with a separate source for each field. The workbook follows The Reptile Database’s 2023 names; this accepted-name match does not prove the historical and current taxon concepts are identical.

## A reported maximum total length {#s-2716df5f46}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 147 mm, attributed to Feldman and Meiri (2013). TL includes the tail, unlike snout–vent length (SVL). This compiled value is not an average or an absolute limit for every individual.

## Microhabitat field {#s-4a2afff9b9}
The source row classifies microhabitat as “fossorial,” citing Feldman (2015). The workbook also uses arboreal, cryptic, saxicolous, semi-aquatic and terrestrial categories, sometimes in combination. This entry does not describe a particular habitat type or observation site.

## Reproductive mode {#s-0d76fecd2c}
The record labels reproductive mode “oviparous,” with Zimin et al. (2022) as its source. It gives no clutch count, egg dimensions or annual reproductive frequency, so these details remain unreported here.

## Limits of the row {#s-d0b575788c}
One row cannot describe all individuals, localities or population variation. The reported TL is a compiled maximum, and the entry provides no sex-specific lengths. Source labels preserve the workbook’s citation trail; they do not mean this page independently reviewed every underlying study.

## Fields not covered by this row {#s-72c748370c}
The row provides no sourced value here for SVL or sex-specific lengths, offspring counts, egg dimensions, diet, active time, foraging mode or sex determination. These details remain open for this species and cannot be filled from relatives or blank cells.
