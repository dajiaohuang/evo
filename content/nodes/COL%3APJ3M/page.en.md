## Yellow disk heads on hairy upright stems {#s-7f8f403084}
This morphology record describes an upright plant with long, soft hairs on the stem and a simple taproot. Its opposite, stalkless leaves are linear to lance-shaped. Disk-shaped heads with yellow florets form corymb-like or paired branching clusters; ray florets are absent. The fruit is smooth, with a pappus of separate, lance-shaped scales. The habitat field says terrestrial.
