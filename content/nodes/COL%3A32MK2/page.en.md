## Cussonia zuluensis {#s-1fc9ad51ad}
## Large divided leaves and fleshy mauve fruit

The source describes a multi-stemmed tree up to 4 m tall, with smooth grey-green bark that flakes and tuberous roots bearing fleshy spindle-shaped swellings. Its leaves are twice compound, rarely three times, with 8–12 leaflets and a spread up to 30 cm; mature leaflets are thick and leathery, glossy green above and darker below. The terminal inflorescence forms an umbel of 8–26 racemes. Five greenish-white petals spread like a star, and the ripe fruit is a fleshy, mauve, goblet-shaped structure with one or two seeds. The source places it along coastal areas from Durban to Mozambique, in dry scrub, open dry forest, river valleys and thornveld inland to Weenen in Natal.
