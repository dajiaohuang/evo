## 478 loci and a Permian wing fossil {#s-b291fa792f}
A targeted-genomics study compared 136 living species from 46 of 48 odonate families using 478 loci. It updated relationships among sampled dragonflies and damselflies, while several backbone branches remained uncertain. A separate fossil review identifies Saxonagrion minutus at about 268 Ma as the earliest recognized modern odonate; neither result dates the order’s origin or supplies a complete fossil history.

## Living-genome sampling and one fossil locality {#s-343e36492c}
The living gene sample and the Upper Permian wing fossil answer different questions. The sample leaves backbone relationships unresolved; the fossil is a single locality and does not establish a global range or the order’s origin.
