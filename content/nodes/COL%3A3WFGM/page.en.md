## ## Leaflet form and blue flowers {#s-ae48ef207c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records oblanceolate to obovate leaflets and lists unifoliolate, trifoliolate or digitately arranged leaves. The leaflets are described as hairless with smooth margins. The free distal portion of the stipules is recorded as 3.3–56 mm long. Inflorescence bracts are shed and longer than 10 mm, and the flowers are recorded as blue. These dataset fields preserve the listed states; they do not quantify variation among populations.
