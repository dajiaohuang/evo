## Hairless leaf undersides and a falling calyx {#s-7da4c23b2d}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records star-shaped hairs on young stems. Leaves may be elliptic, oblong or lance-shaped, with acuminate or tail-like tips and rounded, wedge-shaped or blunt bases. Their margins are entire and without cilia. Three basal veins are listed; the lower surface is recorded as hairless. Flowers form a dichasial cyme, with paired branching. The bell-shaped hypanthium has a calyx that falls away and distinct lobes; each flower has five petals. The anther connective is prolonged and bears one dorsal tooth. White anthers have one medium-sized pore, and the ovary is hairless. A separate habitat field says “terrícola,” or terrestrial. These source fields describe the recorded traits but do not establish their variation or completeness.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-72200d26a3}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no collection locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not specify whether the material is wild, cultivated, managed or fossil. This is one source category, not a complete ecological account.
