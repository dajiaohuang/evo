## Source classification and concept status {#s-d04f4c511f}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3023 records Hylodes amnicola under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

The compilation records terrestrial and aquatic as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. ## Activity periods (daytime and twilight) {#s-3ed150f862}

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-1015dd4d8e}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
