## An EltonTraits record in historical context {#s-c04cf939da}
Maxomys wattsi is accepted in COL26.8 within Muridae. Its same-name, same-family entry in EltonTraits 1.0 supplies three ecological field groups, but follows earlier bird taxonomy or MSW3 for mammals. The match supports a historical comparison; it does not resolve later splits or synonymy.

## How the food weights are distributed {#s-c1daf9933c}
The record assigns 30% to fruit, 30% to other plant material (including fungi), 20% to seeds, nuts, and spores, 10% to invertebrates, and 10% to unspecified vertebrates. These are literature-derived standardized weights, not measured intake. ABC combines high, reasonable, and partial confidence, and a value may borrow from an ecologically similar congener. A zero category would not prove that the food is never eaten; Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broader source categories than their names suggest.

## Where the source places foraging {#s-17b3b256e0}
The entry is coded G, ground, a category that also covers some aquatic foraging in the source definition. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment; metadata links mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). It is not a time budget or an exclusive location.

## Reading the nocturnal flag {#s-eeb45d85e3}
Night feeding is marked 1. The archive permits several activity fields to be marked at once, so this flag does not give a share of the day or a fixed pattern across seasons and sites. ABC certainty can include an ecologically similar congener; typical genus or family activity values were not used to complete the entry.

## Labels attached to the ecological fields {#s-57b7132a89}
The chosen groups use Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, stratum, and activity may have different source material, and reference numbers are table-specific. Original strings remain archived with their file, row, field group, and reference table. The underlying works have not been individually read; a source label is not a new observation.

## What this historical record does and does not show {#s-940bf50cf6}
EltonTraits 1.0 provides a historical same-name, same-family foraging entry, not a complete species account. It cannot establish that the older concept matches COL26.8 exactly. Food weights summarize literature descriptions and are not measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy, or time spent feeding. Mammalian ABC diet or activity values may include information from an ecologically similar congener, so not every field is a direct observation of M. wattsi. Genus- or family-level typical values and records affected by unresolved taxonomic splits were not used to fill gaps. The source books and papers have not been reread individually. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, present range, population, and conservation need separate evidence.
