## Baccharis multifolia {#s-e69688695d}
## Linear leaves and clustered flower heads

The source records developed, sessile, linear leaves with one main vein and entire margins. Branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated; the woody underground base (xylopodium) is absent. Flower heads are grouped in dense clusters. The head-floret fields give two alternatives: heads with only tubular male florets or only threadlike female florets.
