## Relative weights of food categories {#s-0a615bab9d}
The recorded nonzero food weight is invertebrates 100%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. ABC cannot be rewritten as direct observations of this species. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Limits of the ecological record {#s-4b66b6f0d6}
These EltonTraits 1.0 fields are a historical same-name, same-family association, not a complete species account or proof that the source’s species concept equals COL26.8. Food weights standardize literature descriptions; they are not direct measurements of diet. ABC diet estimates can include values borrowed from a congener judged ecologically similar, so they do not establish direct observation of every field in this species. G is categorical, not a measure of time spent at a stratum. Activity periods are withheld under source code D1 because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; the original values and specific reason remain in the source record. Underlying books and papers have not been independently reviewed here. Individual and seasonal variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
