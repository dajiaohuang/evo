## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-6d6afb1833}
COL26.8 records Tropidophis galacelidus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Tropidophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-6b993ab0e5}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
