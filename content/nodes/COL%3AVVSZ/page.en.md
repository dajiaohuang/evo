## Clavija nutans (Vell.) B.Ståhl {#s-e4cf8ec2df}
## Entire leaves and five-petaled flowers

Clavija nutans has young branches with hairs. Its leaves are entire and obovate to elliptic, on petioles up to 3 cm long. The flowers have five petals and glabrous filaments. The source describes the flowers as bisexual or unisexual and pistillate, without resolving which condition applies.
