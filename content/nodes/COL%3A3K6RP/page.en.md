## Low mats with woolly grey leaves {#s-f8e1d1c64a}
This account describes a well-branched subshrub forming mats up to 25 cm tall and about 1 m across. Its small grey-white woolly leaves overlap along the stems, while many tiny yellow heads form compact, flat-topped clusters. The pappus is absent in the record. It favors bare rock sheets and stony mountain slopes.
