## Which flower colors does the entry leave open? {#s-51e59385cf}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an unbranched stem and elliptical or obovate leaves. Its bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are red on both sides, leathery and lack leafy appendages. The corolla is listed as yellow or pink, and the flower lip as white or yellow; the corolla is hairless. These are alternatives in the source record, not one chosen color combination.
