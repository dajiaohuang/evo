## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-e68ced6a86}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Ammospiza leconteii (Audubon, 1844) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Beak, wing and tail in a small sample {#s-0d1ad37454}
Morphology covers 6 measured birds (3 female, 3 male), of which 6 have complete measurements. Species averages are 7.1 mm from the nostril’s anterior edge to the beak tip, 17.3 mm tarsus, 53 mm unflattened wing and 52 mm tail. These are not individual size bounds. Hand-wing index is 18.8, defined through Kipp’s distance/wing length ×100, rather than flight distance or speed. Dividing rounded species-average lengths cannot replace the reported index.

## Mean body mass comes from a separate compilation {#s-afad56d834}
Body mass is 13 g, with Mass.Source=Dunning, defined as a species average incorporating female and male mass. It is not field weighing of the 6 morphology birds or an annual or geographic minimum–maximum range. Inference=NO means the biometric row was not inferred from a relative; mass still comes from published material, and Dunning’s original book and individual denominator were not read here.

## Compiled habitat and food categories {#s-797ba11451}
The row records Grassland habitat, a Granivore niche, Omnivore trophic level and Generalist lifestyle. Seed-oriented niche and omnivorous trophic level describe different aspects of food use. Habitat-density code 3 means open habitats, not three birds per square metre. These compiled categories provide neither seasonal diet percentages nor independent stomach-content observations.

## What the migration code means {#s-ed3a0bb235}
Migration=3 classifies the species as having long-distance migration in the majority of its population. The code supplies no departure month, specific route or tracked individual, and does not require every bird to migrate. Hand-wing index cannot replace migration observations.

## Evidence limits {#s-001b3364a8}
This page interprets historical species averages and coarse ecology in AVONET v7. Selected rows, field definitions and name crosswalks were read; individual raw measurements, Dunning’s original book, independent field behaviour were not. Morphology counts are not mass-literature sample sizes, and Inference=NO does not establish concept identity with COL26.8. Plumage identification, breeding, lifespan, current range and conservation status remain unsupplied; this is not a complete monograph.
