## Overview {#s-2c4a90b647}
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID 3F2DV. The local exact MDD name match supplies the display label Demidoff's Dwarf Galago; the Chinese name is editorial.

## Research observations {#s-a2adcdffd2}
Ambrose and Perkin conducted 60.5 hours of nocturnal surveys at Moca in southern Bioko from 26 February to 8 March 1997, recording four galago species including this one. They also noted earlier Moca records from 1964–1965. These are local montane-site records, not island-wide abundance or range estimates.

## Morphology {#s-6d608626e4}
A museum-based craniodental study measured 290 Galagoides skulls. Specimens bearing the existing demidoff and thomasi identifications were separated with about 70% reliability, while subspecies labels within demidoff were not distinguished by skull morphology. Morphology is informative but not definitive and does not replace field-call or specimen-provenance checks.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-1eccb08246}
Early Gabonese nocturnal-primate work predates the gradual clarification of dwarf-galago species boundaries. Later Gabon work discusses demidoff and thomasi as sympatric populations; broad names in older sources should not automatically be assigned to every present COL species record.

## Evidence limits {#s-74f946711d}
This is a guide to Moca records on Bioko and craniodental identification, not a complete species account. The available sources do not support a complete range, range-wide diet, population trend or current conservation assessment here; close-relative data are not substituted.
