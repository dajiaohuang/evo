## Stem surface {#s-93e91849da}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a hirsute stem, covered with coarse hairs that spread outward. It lists simple hairs, thread-like unbranched bristles, and capitate glandular hairs whose tips form small heads. Branched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-0c622cf487}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are listed as caducous (falling early) or persistent. Extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) are absent, while projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) are listed as absent or present. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; its floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are larger than the flowers before they open (anthesis). The source does not assign these leaf alternatives to particular specimens.

## Flowers {#s-1d3a2a2646}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, is marked as lacking lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and a tomentose covering of dense, matted hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are white.

## Habitat category {#s-217e415165}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not specify a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
