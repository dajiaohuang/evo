## Ruprechtia glauca {#s-d1d1fecca0}
## Papery fruiting wings surround a smooth nut

Ruprechtia glauca has short shoots and smooth upper branch tips. Its leaves are oval, obovate or elliptic, with entire margins and a lower surface that may be hairless or pubescent. A branched panicle carries unisexual flowers with hairless oval sepals. The papery fruiting envelope has an ovoid tube and smooth, spatulate or oblong wings; the enclosed nut is smooth and ovoid or three-lobed. The habitat label is terrestrial.
