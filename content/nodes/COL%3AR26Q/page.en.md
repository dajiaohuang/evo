## What is recorded for Caraipa iracemensis? {#s-72c2bb8b6d}
The field describes two-ranked, alternate leaves with hair and hair-like scales, and bulliform cells recorded as present. Flowers grow in panicles or clustered groups; the stalks, ovary, and style are woolly. The fruit is a hairy capsule that opens along its internal partitions, and the outer fruit layer is not fused to the inner wall.
