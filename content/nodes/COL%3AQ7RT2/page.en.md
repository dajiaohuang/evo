## Accepted name and placement {#s-232eb24272}
COL26.8 accepts Limnonectes pseudodoriae Yodthong, Rujirawan, Stuart & Aowphol, 2021 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Dicroglossidae (family) → Limnonectes (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Detections across surveyed transects {#s-3e8a406bf1}
On Ko Pha-ngan, Thailand, the study detected this frog mainly in rocky river systems. Detections occurred in one of eight settlement transects, three of 13 disturbed-forest transects and five of 11 national-park forest transects. These survey detections have different category denominators and are not population abundance or proof of a causal disturbance response.

## Eggs on land beside a rocky-stream pool {#s-6877187ca2}
The island survey reported eggs on land at the edge of a standing-water pool in a level part of a rocky stream wash in national-park forest. Adults were encountered in or around rocky streams, including a flooded stream between a house and small pond. This documents one reported egg setting, without supplying clutch size, parental attendance, hatching behavior or a general rule for all breeding sites.
