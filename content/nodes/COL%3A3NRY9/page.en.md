## Hyphessobrycon loweae {#s-d9c9ccf2c0}
## A diagnosis based on fin form and counts

The 2013 comparison describes mature male *Hyphessobrycon loweae* with an elongated, filament-like dorsal fin. In the examined material, six to seven horizontal scale rows ran from the dorsal-fin origin to the lateral line, and the branched supporting rays of the anal fin numbered 17–21, most often 20. The compared *H. peugeoti* material had five such scale rows and 21–24 branched rays, most often 22. These are diagnostic counts from the treatment, not estimates for every population.

## Locality-specific stream and lake reports

The authors report *H. loweae* from clear, slow-flowing streams with abundant submerged vegetation at Córrego Xavante, the upper rio das Mortes, and rio Culuene tributaries near Gaúcha do Norte. The treatment also cites paratypes from the small, densely vegetated Lago do Leo. The habitat notes draw on different sources, including personal communications, an author observation, and an earlier account, so they document reported localities rather than a measured habitat preference.
