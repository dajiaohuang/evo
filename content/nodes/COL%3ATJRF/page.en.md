## Chamaedorea pinnatifrons: stem, leaves and fruit {#s-bae6b45d09}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records orange aerial roots and a solitary stem 0.5–3.0 m high and 1.0–3.0 cm in diameter; its growth is decumbent or erect. There are 4–10 pinnate leaves, with 4–8 sigmoid pinnae. The leaf sheath is partly closed and 15–30 cm long; petioles are 10–45 cm long.

The inflorescence is branched and may be interfoliar or infrafoliar. It has 3–5 bracts and a 20–70 cm peduncle. Fruits are ellipsoid or globose and 0.9–1.5 cm long.

These measurements and descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
