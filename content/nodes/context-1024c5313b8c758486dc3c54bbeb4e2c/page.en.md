## The source concept {#s-b1e1faf278}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis nimbaensis as row Anf0138 within Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011), and its match to a current species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-05d8176fc5}
The row flags terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. The compilation does not say which habitats, seasons, or life stages these categories represent. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the saved values are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-60296586a3}
The compilation reports maximum adult body length of 21.2 mm. For anurans, this is snout–vent length. The value is a compiled maximum, not a range across individuals.

## Developmental strategy {#s-7a7617b04b}
The record flags direct development and does not flag a larval stage or viviparity. The row does not add information about breeding conditions or parental care.
