## Ekebergia capensis {#s-96b99409ae}
The regional flora account records either evergreen or semi-evergreen growth, 9–13 leaflets and a drupe with 2–4 pyrenes; it places the species in forest.

## Many leaflets and a drupe with several pyrenes {#s-06500b2f7b}
A 1997 South African account describes a tree up to 20 m that is evergreen or semi-evergreen. Its leaf rachis is unwinged or very narrowly winged; leaves bear 9–13 lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate leaflets, up to 145 × 60 mm. Flowers are white or pinkish white. The drupe contains 2–4 pyrenes. A separate diagnostic note says its leaves can look superficially like those of *Harpephyllum caffrum*; it records a resemblance, not a conclusion about relationship. The habitat entry is “forest.” This regional account gives no dated sampling frame or population-frequency estimate.
