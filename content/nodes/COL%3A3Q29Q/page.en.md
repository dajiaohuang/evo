## *Irlbachia plantaginifolia* {#s-c54fea5d3e}
## A basal rosette and a white corolla

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an unbranched stem with broad elliptic to ovate leaves crowded near its base, forming a rosette. The blades are membranous or slightly fleshy, with visible secondary veins. The inflorescence bears scale-like bracts; the flower buds have rounded tips, and the corolla is white. The field calls the corolla deciduous, meaning that it falls away. These details come from one regional morphology record; it does not assess variation or whether the observed plants were wild or cultivated.
