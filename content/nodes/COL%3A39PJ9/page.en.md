## Encyclia gonzalezii {#s-c72fe254cc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row records a conical pseudobulb, oblong to lanceolate leaves and a simple raceme. The reflexed, wavy petals accompany separate lip lobes; the rounded middle lobe has a blunt tip. The description also records a three-toothed clinandrium and a stigmatic hook; the habitat field is epiphytic.
