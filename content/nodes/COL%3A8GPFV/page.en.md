## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-11a745b05e}
COL26.8 records Ceratophora ukuwelai as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Agamidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-09ef19c15f}
The active-time field classifies activity as active across day and night (Cathemeral); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Karunarathna et al. 2020.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-437da575a6}
The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (ZW/ZZ). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
