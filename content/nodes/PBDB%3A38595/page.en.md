## Spinosauridae in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-844b343439}
PBDB records Spinosauridae at family rank. The selected species pages are entry points into taxon-specific fossil records, not a complete family list. The database parent path records classification; it is not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## How the spinosaurid revision weighs named and fragmentary fossils {#s-2381a61c2f}
Hone and Holtz revise named and fragmentary spinosaurid material, distinguishing diagnostic fossils from weaker referrals. Their subfamily placements and ecological signals are evidence-weighted hypotheses. The review does not make every member uniformly semiaquatic or establish a complete family range.

Across the named and fragmentary material accepted in the revision, 145–93.9 Ma marks its taxonomically qualified fossil coverage. This is not a precise first or last appearance for Spinosauridae, a divergence date, continuous occupancy, or evidence of a direct ancestor.
