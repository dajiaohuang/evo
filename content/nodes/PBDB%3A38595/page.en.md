## PBDB hierarchy for Spinosauridae {#s-844b343439}
In the PBDB parent hierarchy, Spinosauridae is recorded at family rank. This guide links 15 fossil-species entries from the selected page set. The count describes this guide, not every descendant, and a list of extinct records does not mean that the whole group is extinct. A PBDB parent path is a database classification, not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Collections in this selected guide {#s-5ced9a551c}
The guide begins with Irritator challengeri, Spinosaurus aegyptiacus, Spinosaurus mirabilis, Angaturama limai, Camarillasaurus cirugedae. Across the selected species pages, 30 occurrence records map to 29 collections; named stratum examples include Romualdo, Baharîje, Ifezouane, Gara Samani. Each collection record retains its own age interval and identification qualifications, so comparisons belong at that record level. Occurrences and collections are not counts of specimens or populations.

## What the selected fossil records do not establish {#s-2381a61c2f}
This selection covers a subset of fossil records, not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence and collection totals do not measure specimens, individuals, populations or sampling effort; pooled outer age bounds are not the group origin or extinction. PBDB parent paths organize these records separately from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
