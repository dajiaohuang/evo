## Daytime activity in camera detections {#s-67a4dcf5ff}
Three systematic terrestrial camera-trap surveys in Lomami National Park and its buffer zone covered Okulu (2013), Losekola (2014) and E15 (2015). Across 92 cameras and 5,960 camera-trap days, the study recorded 598 independent lesula events. The authors reported strongly terrestrial locomotion and daytime activity in the photographed sample. Camera events document detections under the survey design; they are not a direct count of animals or a population-density estimate.
