## What leaf and flower details identify Tetrorchidium parvulum? {#s-b3ef07984e}
The branches bear Malpighian hairs. Leaves have tapering or tail-like tips, prominent toothed margins, and a pair of glands at the base. Male flower clusters are 1.5–2.5 cm long, with the small flower groups loosely spaced along the axis. The outer surfaces of the male-flower sepals are hairless.

The source lists it as terrestrial, without naming a site.
