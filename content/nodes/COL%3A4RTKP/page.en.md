## Restio purpurascens {#s-467f211943}
## Purple bracts on the damp upper slopes

Restio purpurascens may grow in tufts or from stolons and reaches 1.8 m. The regional description notes erect, somewhat flattened stems with coarse papillae, and spikelets 15–25 mm long with purplish bracts. Its ovary has two chambers. Damp upper slopes, often near seeps, are the reported habitat.
