## A basal rosette above a small swollen root {#s-2fb9df8f83}
The morphology record notes a small swollen underground stem and a simple, hairy stem. Oval, hairy leaves form a basal rosette, with wedge-shaped bases and blunt or rounded tips. Stalked flower heads form umbel-like clusters; their involucres are hemispherical to broadly bell-shaped, and the bracts are reduced or absent. The silky fruits are top-shaped and glandular. The habitat field says terrestrial.
