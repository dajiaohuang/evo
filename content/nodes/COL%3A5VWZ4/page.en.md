## Distinguish the measured length {#s-a57aba73b7}
The reported maximum total length (TL), including the tail, is 369 mm. Snout–vent length (SVL) stops at the vent and is a different measurement. This compiled maximum describes source records, not typical size or an absolute growth limit. Values for different sexes may come from different places or studies and do not establish size differences within a population.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-966f0a8dd3}
The source labels reproductive mode as viviparous. The compilation’s clutch or litter summaries may be midpoints of reported means, or of reported extremes when means are unavailable; they are not necessarily averages of observed clutches. A fractional summary is not part of an individual. The dataset defines lowest and highest values as endpoints of reported means, not the smallest or largest observed clutches. These labels do not establish fetal nutrition or uniformity among populations.
