## Quercus oocarpa {#s-65f0eff4fc}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Quercus oocarpa* as an accepted species. A historical Flora of Panama account describes trees 6–25 m tall. The cup around the acorn is 3–4 cm wide; the acorns are about 2.5 × 3 cm, while Panama material is given as 4 × 2.5 cm, with one-half to one-quarter of the acorn enclosed. The account’s distribution field says the species ranges north to Mexico; this is a historical regional record, not a current or global range.
