## Malephora latipetala {#s-41922f43a6}
## Golden flowers among blue-green, sprawling branches

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a sprawling shrublet with decumbent branches that do not root; the internodes are brown. Its cylindrical leaves are pointed at the tip, glaucous with a pale surface bloom, and reach 25 mm long by 3–5 mm wide. Five sepals accompany golden-yellow flowers about 34 mm across. The fruits have nine or ten chambers.

The same treatment marks the habitat as uncertain.
