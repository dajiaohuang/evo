## What leaves, flowers and fruit-stage traits are recorded? {#s-8ef366e84d}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records an axial root system and no Malpighian-type hairs on the stem. Opposite elliptic leaves have acute bases and flat margins. The inflorescence is bracted. Flower stalks are 0.1–6 mm long and bracteoles are absent. The floral tube is 3–10 mm long and nearly hairless or has short, fine hairs; its blunt spur points horizontally. Violet petals are absent by the fruit stage. Stamens attach in the upper third of the tube; no vesicle is recorded, and the nectar gland at the ovary base bends downward or horizontally. The fruit is tubular and the seed margin blunt. These are the regional field's listed states, without frequency information.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-ff100617b9}
The dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
