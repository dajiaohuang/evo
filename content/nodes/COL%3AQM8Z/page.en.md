## How does it differ from *Cannomois parviflora*? {#s-003b9c8c74}
The 2012 regional account compares *Cannomois arenicola* with *C. parviflora*. It describes a much more developed rhizome (a horizontal underground stem), plants that grow without clumping and reach 80 cm, and an elliptical nut that is widest in the middle. It also reports fewer female spikelets (small flower clusters) per culm (a stem) than in the comparison species. The excerpt supplies no comparative counts or dated sampling frame.

## What ground does the account describe? {#s-f6c85b9ad4}
The regional account lists well-drained sandy flats. This is a habitat description, not a complete range or a dated survey record.
