## Shell interiors help test early relationships {#s-b9d18c0cea}
Early internal-shell characters are compared to infer relationships near the origin of the sampled Ceratitida. This is a character-based reconstruction of a difficult early part of the ammonoid tree, not an observed ancestor or a complete global first appearance.

## Period bounds converted to ages {#s-fbe6af8b0f}
Shigeta and colleagues describe Ceratitida from the Early Permian through the end of the Triassic. The atlas converts those period boundaries to 298.9–201.4 million years ago using ICS v2026/06. The numbers do not imply direct dates, continuous fossil preservation or ancestry.
