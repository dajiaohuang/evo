## The living perissodactyl tree cannot date fossil Hippomorpha {#s-90946dcf57}
Supertree and supermatrix analyses of living perissodactyls recover Equidae—the living component labelled Hippomorpha in this route—as sister to sampled Ceratomorpha. This extant-only topology does not resolve every fossil hippomorph or date the total group’s origin. The former 56–0 Ma display is withheld because the cited analysis does not audit fossil endpoints.
