## Begonia obdeltata {#s-3e9441b008}
## Kidney-shaped leaves with glandular and star-shaped hairs

Begonia obdeltata has erect stems and deciduous stipules. Its slightly asymmetric, kidney-shaped leaves have toothed or wavy margins; inconspicuous glandular and star-shaped hairs occur on both surfaces and the petioles. The male and female flower segments bear these hair types as well. Female margins are entire and bracteoles are present. Capsule wings are unequal, and the seed tip is rounded.
