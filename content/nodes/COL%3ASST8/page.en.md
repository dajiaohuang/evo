## Population evidence {#s-4242a223cc}
Oguge, Karere and Kirathe censused 73 forest patches along the lower Tana River from January to March 2001. They estimated 2,070 mangabeys in 59 groups; 56% of recorded groups were inside the Tana River Primate National Reserve. The paper also compared its estimate with an earlier 1994 census, but these dated surveys do not give a current population count.

## Feeding research {#s-8f9c9d609e}
A separate 13-year comparison followed one group in a 16.25-ha forest in 1988 and again in 2000–2001. Its diet composition shifted between periods, while only some changes tracked shifts in tree abundance or measured fruit availability. The study presents a local example of dietary flexibility in a dynamic riverine forest, not a fixed species-wide menu.
