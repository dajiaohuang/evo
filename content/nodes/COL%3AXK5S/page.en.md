## What flower traits are recorded for Conceveiba guianensis? {#s-9672468974}
The leaves have inconspicuous stipules, and their undersides are hairy at least along the veins. Male flower clusters include sessile cymules, small flower clusters without stalks; female clusters have two or more flowers. Male flowers have staminodes, sterile stamens. In female flowers, the styles are joined only at the base rather than forming a column, and the ovary has three chambers.
