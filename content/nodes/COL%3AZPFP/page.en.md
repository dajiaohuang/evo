## What is known about Croton abonari in this record? {#s-2f8077633b}
Young branches have appressed, star-shaped hairs. The long-stalked elliptic leaves bear star-shaped or scale-like star hairs above and scale-like star hairs beneath; their side veins loop toward the margin. Edges may be entire or toothed. A sessile or nearly sessile nectar gland occurs near the upper end of the leaf stalk, and leafy stipules are absent at young shoot tips. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and wheel-shaped. Female-flower structures and details are unknown; the fruit columella has only weakly distinct apical lobes.
