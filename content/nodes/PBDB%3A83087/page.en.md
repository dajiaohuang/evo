## What this PBDB guide counts {#s-b50ff8f8d9}
The 2026-10-02 Paleobiology Database (PBDB) snapshot places *Homo neanderthalensis* under *Homo* in the selected mammal path. This guide selects five eligible species-level occurrence records, linked to five collections and five reference identifiers. Occurrence, collection, and reference are distinct database units, not specimen or individual counts.

## Three named collection examples {#s-fbe51f8bb5}
The selected records include Apidima Cave A (Lakonia, region code GR; 0.774–0.0117 Ma), Artenac 'Bed 6 composite list', Commune de Saint-Mary, Charente (Poitou-Charentes, FR; 0.129–0.0117 Ma), and Kalamakia (Peloponnesus, GR; 0.129–0.0117 Ma). The database assigns these broad collection-age brackets and, where present, named intervals. Two of the five selected collection records are not summarized by name here. These examples are not a complete distribution list, and a recorded locality does not establish absence elsewhere.

## The outer age envelope of selected records {#s-3f8011378b}
Across these five PBDB occurrences, the combined outer age envelope is 0.774–0.0117 Ma (Ma means million years ago). In this extract both bounds come from the same broad Apidima Cave A occurrence record, occ:339569; they are not two separate fossils or species endpoints. The other named examples have 0.129–0.0117 Ma collection brackets. These database intervals do not show continuous presence, a global first or last appearance, or the age of an individual. They also do not replace the separate Vindija genome sample record.

## Collection labels are not species ecology {#s-1d002e2d22}
PBDB labels Apidima Cave A as cave; Artenac as cave with collection-level preservation mode anthropogenic; and Kalamakia as cave with modes body, anthropogenic, and bone collector. These describe database collection settings or assemblages, not the habitat, diet, or behavior of every Neanderthal, and not the preservation state of each specimen. Missing source fields remain unknown.

## Three database reference attributions {#s-61bd672d64}
The named records carry these abbreviated PBDB attributions: Apidima Cave A, Harvati et al. (2019), ref:71674; Artenac, Delagnes et al. (1999), ref:11457; and Kalamakia, Harvati et al. (2013), ref:61580. They are database source labels, not completed author lists or necessarily the original species descriptions. The independent archive retains all five occurrence, collection, and reference identifiers. This guide has not re-reviewed the papers or specimens.

## Limits of the occurrence guide {#s-3772929065}
This guide covers selected PBDB occurrence records from the 2026-10-02 snapshot, not a species monograph. The PBDB hierarchy remains separate from COL concepts. Locality, age, environment, and collection-level preservation describe different record fields; they do not establish a complete range, species-wide ecology, abundance, or the condition of each specimen. The Vindija genome sample is separate from this occurrence envelope.

## The separate Vindija genome sample {#vindija-genome}
A separate study reports a high-coverage Neanderthal genome from Vindija 33.19 in Croatia and a radiocarbon result greater than 45.5 ka. The candidate’s approximately 55–45.5 ka interval is a source-bounded specimen envelope (0.055–0.0455 Ma), not the full duration of Homo neanderthalensis. The approximately 52 ka age and demographic relationships are model outputs. This genome context is separate from the five PBDB occurrences above.
