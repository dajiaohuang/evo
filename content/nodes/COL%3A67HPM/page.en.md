## Anthurium croatii: a morphology and growth-form record {#s-1f728deb67}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and a fibrous base. Petioles are marked smooth, but their shape is not characterized. The leaf blades are flat and palmately compound, with elliptic, ovate or obovate outlines and acuminate tips; the bases are acute and glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is left uncharacterized, secondary veins are impressed on the upper surface, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, ovate spathe. Its angle with the peduncle is not characterized, and the spathe is not stipitate. Berries are described as vinaceous (wine-colored).

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The dataset records the growth-form category “Hemiepífita,” or hemiepiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
