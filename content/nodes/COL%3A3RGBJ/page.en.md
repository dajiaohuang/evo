## Habitat recorded in the flora dataset {#s-8b1433bc78}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record uses *Terrícola*, the dataset's terrestrial habitat category.

This category is not a collection locality; the source field gives no survey date, geographic range, or measure of sampling completeness.

## What this record does not describe {#s-d7225e7d75}
The habitat field does not say whether the material was wild, cultivated, or managed, and it does not assess fossil applicability.

No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat category is not evidence about life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

This regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
