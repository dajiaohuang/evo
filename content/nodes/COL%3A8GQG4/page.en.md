## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-89e12e9a55}
COL26.8 records Trimerodytes yapingi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-69f97ad8f2}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman 2015. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Christian et al. 2010.
