## A rare RH1 replacement has method-specific support {#s-53f5b9be6e}
The paper reports glycine at bovine-numbered RH1 site164 in antisana, while 102 sampled species had alanine there. CODEML identified selection at this site; other analyses did not. The discussion uses previous bovine substitutions to explore possible effects but stresses the difficulty of predicting frog pigment sensitivity. Its cited two-nanometre effect belongs to a different bovine substitution, not a measured shift caused by the antisana glycine. No strong support for a role in adaptation to diurnality follows from this result.
