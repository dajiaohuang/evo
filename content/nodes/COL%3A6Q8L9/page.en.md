## What does the record say about Licania lamentanda? {#s-bc4c5e1ef6}
The oblong-elliptic leaves have closely pressed woolly hairs beneath, and the leaf stalks lack glands. Flowers may grow in racemes or panicles; their receptacles are bell-shaped, and the individual flowers are nearly stalkless. Each flower has six or seven separate stamens that remain inside the floral tube. The fruit’s outer wall is recorded as finely woolly or velvety.
