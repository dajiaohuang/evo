## Pouteria cicatricata T.D.Penn. {#s-b489981877}
## Small warty fruit with a nearly full-sized seed scar

Pouteria cicatricata has young branchlets covered in woolly hairs and no lenticels. Alternate, spiral leaves have eucamptodromous veins and cylindrical petioles. Axillary or branch-borne clusters carry four-parted flowers with a tubular corolla; the lobes have entire margins and squared-off tips. The fruit is velvety, finely warty and vinaceous to blackish. It contains one or two wrinkled seeds whose scar covers almost the entire surface.
