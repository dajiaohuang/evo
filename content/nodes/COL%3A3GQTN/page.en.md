## Goeppertia fragilis {#s-786cfa908d}
## Basal leaves and bracts shape the inflorescence

The morphology record describes leaves arranged in two ranks and inserted at the base, with both a sheath and petiole. The inflorescence stalk also emerges basally at the same node as a leaf. Its bracts are all fertile, spirally arranged, with erect margins and colors other than red or orange. The corolla tube is split, and one outer staminode—a sterile stamen—is recorded.

The source leaves the young-leaf form, flower count per cyme, bracteoles and staminode callus unknown.
