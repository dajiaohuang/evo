## Zornia harmsiana {#s-468d111a32}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zornia harmsiana* as an accepted species of *Zornia*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records creeping stems, leaves with four leaflets, and a spike-like inflorescence. Its segmented pod (a loment) lacks prickles, and the seeds are yellow-green with brown spots. The source also labels the habitat terrestrial; these fields are a regional description, not a complete account of variation.
