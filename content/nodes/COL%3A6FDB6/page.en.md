## Leaves, flower clusters and seed pods {#s-7748a99f38}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional entry records a nectary on the leaf stalk and bipinnate leaves with 4–7 pairs of pinnae; each pinna carries 10–20 pairs of leaflets. The inflorescences are dense, head-shaped racemes with similar-looking flowers. The calyx and corolla are hairy, while the ovary is hairless. The fruit is round and twisted, with a smooth outer wall. Seeds are elliptic and have a pleurogram, a line-like mark on the seed coat. This is one regional row, not a complete or global account.
