## Crinum minimum: flowers and habitat {#s-c45a6235be}
A 1997 regional account describes a perennial herb up to 200 mm tall. Open flowers spread horizontally before becoming nodding. The short peduncle is 40–150 mm long, and the umbel bears one flower or, rarely, two. Leaves are narrow and flaccid, 3 mm wide, with smooth margins. The flowers are white with a deep-rose keel.
