## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9b16bde620}
COL26.8 records Platyceps noeli as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-1c4f42cdf3}
