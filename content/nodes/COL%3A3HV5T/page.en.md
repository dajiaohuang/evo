## Gymnostomum aeruginosum: two habitat labels {#s-28456f6716}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this moss:

“Rupícola,” a rock-associated category,

and “Terrícola,” a terrestrial category.

These are labels from two regional dataset rows; neither gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
