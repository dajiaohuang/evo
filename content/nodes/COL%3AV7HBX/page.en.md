## Ten localities within a defined collecting program {#s-268d122ac5}
The 2025 species account summarizes field records from a collecting program spanning 2001–2022. It reports ten localities (numbered 3–12 in the paper) in Croatia’s upper Cetina Basin and southern Livansko Polje in north-west Bosnia and Herzegovina. The reported settings include springs, the Cetina River and interstitial habitats. These are the sites listed in the paper, not a complete range boundary.

## The type locality identifies one specimen {#s-308713e18c}
A type locality is the place where the designated name-bearing specimen was collected. The paper gives the type locality as Kotluša spring at Cviljane, Croatia (43°56′57″N, 16°23′56″E; 397 m). The holotype, NHMW-MO-113902, was collected on 17 March 2017. This locates that specimen; it does not represent the species’ full distribution.

## The COI data do not resolve deeper relationships {#s-b1ca8d71d0}
The study assigns the taxon to mitochondrial operational unit mOTU D and reports COI sequence accessions PX113150–PX113167. The authors caution that COI data do not resolve deeper phylogenetic relationships. Here, mOTU D is the study’s sequence-group label, not a placement in a complete species tree.

## VU D2 is the paper authors’ assessment {#s-f3fc1e9a72}
The treatment assesses the species as Vulnerable (VU D2). This is the authors’ assessment; the local evidence does not identify an independently verified, current IUCN or national Red List entry. Its scope is also limited by the locality information reported in the paper, so it should not be read as a separately verified current formal status.
