## Anthurium minarum: a regional morphology record {#s-8113fbbf34}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes an erect stem with entire cataphylls and prophylls along it. Petioles are grooved, obtuse-edged above and rounded below, and are marked smooth. Leaf blades are entire and flat, oblong, elliptic or obovate, with acute or obtuse tips and obtuse or truncate bases. Glandular dots are absent above and present below. The primary vein is uncharacterized, secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as less than half a leaf; the peduncle is uncharacterized. Its flat, ovate spathe forms an acute angle with the peduncle, and its stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. Berries are described as orange, reddish or vinaceous.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.
