Trophic.Niche=Aquatic predator is defined as at least 60% of food resources from vertebrate and invertebrate animals in aquatic systems; Trophic.Level=Carnivore as at least 70% from live invertebrate or vertebrate animals. These are separate category thresholds, not measured species diet proportions or shares of fish, crustaceans or molluscs. Underlying diet papers were not read; prey lists, seasonal diet, dive depth, capture actions and handling ability remain unsupported.
