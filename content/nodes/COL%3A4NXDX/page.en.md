## Pseudognaphalium gaudichaudianum {#s-2c83e83413}
## Herbaceous stems, yellow heads and oval-oblong fruits

The Flora do Brasil 2020 description notes herbaceous stems. The lower leaf surface is woolly, while the upper surface is finely hairy or glandular. Flower heads are yellow, and the dry fruits (cypselae) are ovate-oblong. The catalogue also classifies the plant as terrestrial.
