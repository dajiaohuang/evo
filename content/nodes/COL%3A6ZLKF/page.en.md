## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-df34d19d24}
COL26.8 records Stenolepis ridleyi as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gymnophthalmidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-07f88fb49d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as burrowing (Fossorial)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Fossorial/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
