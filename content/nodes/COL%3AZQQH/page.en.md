## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton odontadenius? {#s-0114c4d7fe}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. The short-stalked oval-lanceolate, elliptic, or oblong leaves bear outward-radiating star hairs on both surfaces; side veins reach the margin, and the edges are toothed. Nectar glands may be absent or stalked near the upper end of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are shortly stalked and shallowly bell-shaped; female flowers are nearly sessile, with separate sepals, unequal calyx lobes, and a many-lobed style. Female petals are threadlike, the bud arrangement is valvate, and the fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
