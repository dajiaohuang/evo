## Broad rough leaves below violet flower heads {#s-1961cb20ac}
This account describes a perennial herb up to 60 cm tall, with broad, soft leaves that are rough-haired on both surfaces. The upper stem is often nearly leafless, though the source notes this is not invariable. Relatively large heads carry violet flowers. The source distinguishes it from H. flanaganii and H. hirsuta by the leaf hair structure and by having fewer, larger heads. It grows in grassland and thorn scrub, often in warm, dry river valleys.
