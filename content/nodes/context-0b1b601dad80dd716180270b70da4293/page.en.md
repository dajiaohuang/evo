## AmphiBIO species record {#s-23b9d0bf7f}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Microhyla fowleri? Row Anf4289 places it in genus Microhyla, family Microhylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Adult food categories in the compilation {#s-02d07c7e65}
The AmphiBIO row lists these adult-food categories: arthropods (invertebrates with jointed limbs). The compilation draws its categories from literature reports based on expert judgment, observations or stomach contents. A listed category is not a complete diet or a measured share of the diet.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-bfea6cfdba}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 41.5 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.
