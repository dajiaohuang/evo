## The source tadpole concept {#s-8b75110be1}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Leptopelis boulengeri, in the genus Leptopelis and family Arthroleptidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Leptopelis boulengeri. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-f67ad3cea4}
The compilation has 16 records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 36, 40, 38 and 37. These are labels attached to source records, not a single tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; 16 records do not mean 16 unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
