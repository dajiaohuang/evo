## Micropholis casiquiarensis Aubrév. {#s-023b6becbb}
## Five-chambered ovary and a single rough-coated seed

Micropholis casiquiarensis has alternate, two-ranked, oblong-elliptic leaves with brochidodromous veins, whose side veins loop near the margin. Its bisexual flowers have five sepals and petals, a tubular corolla, and five included stamens. Lance-shaped staminodes (sterile stamens) are present, but a nectary disk is absent. The ovary has five chambers and the style remains within the corolla. The fruit is green to yellow and contains one seed with a wrinkled or rough coat.
