## How a molecular matrix rebuilt the living-whale tree {#s-1532a67257}
The 2009 study assembled a 42,335-character molecular matrix for 87 of the 89 cetacean species recognized in its own sampling framework. It combined nuclear and mitochondrial data with transposon evidence to estimate relationships among living whales and dolphins, then used relaxed molecular clocks to model divergence times. These are results for that historical sample, taxonomic framework and calibration set—not coverage of every species recognized today or direct dates from fossils.
