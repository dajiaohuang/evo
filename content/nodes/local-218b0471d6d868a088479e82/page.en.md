Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Adelophryne pachydactyla* under genus *Adelophryne*, family Brachycephaloidea, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.

The source table contains 1 measurement row; 1 trait column has at least one recorded value. Its Sex field uses M (male). These rows are not necessarily unique specimens, and missing cells do not show that a structure is absent.
