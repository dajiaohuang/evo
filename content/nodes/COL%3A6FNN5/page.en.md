## ## Leaf-like stipules and an asymmetrical flower {#s-54a3ee591a}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records lateral, leaf-like stipules and 3–5 pairs of leaflets that are not sickle-shaped. The inflorescences are short and upright. In the flower, the cup-like hypanthium is bilaterally symmetric, the sepals differ in size, and the long stamen tube is also bilaterally symmetric; fewer than ten stamens are fertile. The stigma ranges from blunt to head-shaped. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
