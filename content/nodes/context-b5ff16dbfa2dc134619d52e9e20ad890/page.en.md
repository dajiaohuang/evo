## The source concept {#s-097c4e8492}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0040 records Allobates melanolaemus in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat field: Ter=1 {#s-c8a905ed39}
The row records Ter=1 (terrestrial). The fields Fos=NA, Aqu=NA, Arb=NA remain missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=NA {#s-7ac7ab2a8a}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity). Noc=NA remains missing, not evidence of absence. These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Body_size_mm: 23.6 mm maximum adult body length {#s-f0c40e80c9}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=23.6 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-351fbbc1c0}
Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
