## ecology {#s-36af1bf630}
Why can a fly look like a bee or wasp? Adults in Syrphidae, the hoverfly family, often resemble bees or wasps, but they are flies. The larvae do not all eat the same food: some eat aphids, while others use decaying plant material. The Chinese family name 食蚜蝇科 (“aphid-eating flies”) is not a feeding rule for every larva.
