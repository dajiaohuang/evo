## Goeppertia roseopicta {#s-ff889cea14}
## Spiral leaves and clustered flowers

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes basal, spiral leaves with sheaths and petioles; young leaves are marked as ornamented. Its basal flower stalk is recorded at the same node as the leaves. The inflorescence has apical sterile bracts, two flowers in each small cluster and one club-shaped bracteole. Bract colour is recorded as unknown, so the page does not infer it. The flower has an entire corolla tube, a petaloid callus at the staminode tip, and one outer staminode.
