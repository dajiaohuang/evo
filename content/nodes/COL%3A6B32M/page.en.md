## What flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-19f699720c}
The stems bear simple hairs. Leaves have basal appendages. The flowers have an evident staminal disc, 20–30 stamens, ovate petals and an ovary with three or four chambers. The fruit is a cylindrical capsule with six or eight wings and three or four pointed or forked appendages at the tip. The seeds are disc-shaped.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-50b403d5a8}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
