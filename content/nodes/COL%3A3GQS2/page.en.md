## Goeppertia capitata {#s-7e3b2a1d42}
## Sterile bracts crown a basal stalk

The record describes leaves emerging at the base and a flower stalk arising from the same node. Sterile bracts sit at the top of the inflorescence, while each small flower cluster has two flowers and two membranous bracteoles. The callus on the staminode tip is not petaloid.
