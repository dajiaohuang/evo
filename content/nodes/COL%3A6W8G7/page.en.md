## Accepted name and placement {#s-660a55bcbb}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis sacharuna Reyes-Puig, Reyes-Puig, Pérez-L. & Yánez-Muñoz, 2015 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Placement in the proposed Cryptomantis framework {#s-f7f0ea2974}
The 2025 study includes Pristimantis sacharuna in the bicantus species group of its proposed subgenus Cryptomantis. The sampled major clade had bootstrap support 100 and its two group clades 99. Morphological synapomorphies were explicitly unknown. The dated placement does not certify a current catalogue subgenus, a complete range for this species or identity of every historical broadly identified voucher. ## Characters in the paganus comparison {#s-fcf4e7f5cf}
The paper distinguishes the new paganus population from Pristimantis sacharuna using a W-shaped scapular fold followed by an inverted-V fold, subconical ulnar and eyelid tubercles and a round-to-subconical outer metatarsal tubercle. These are comparative taxonomic characters, not a newly sampled population-wide trait-frequency analysis. Sex and body-region wording are retained. A selected fold or tubercle alone is not a complete identification key, and these comparisons provide no new diet, clutch-size or developmental measurements for this species.
