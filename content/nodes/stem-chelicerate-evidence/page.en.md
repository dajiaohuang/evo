## Appendage anatomy and a sampled tree {#s-52b4799bec}
This navigation group connects appendage evidence from *Mollisonia* with separate Cambrian fossils: *Urokodia* from Chengjiang and *Megachelicerax* from the Wheeler Formation. The 519–500.5 Ma span links those sample dossiers; it is not the duration of one lineage or a complete stem-chelicerate range.

The *Mollisonia plenovenatrix* study describes appendage anatomy and tests its placement in a morphology matrix. That sampled placement is an analytical result. The route does not name a natural grade, establish direct ancestors, or resolve every chelicerate stem relationship.

## Two fossil samples, one browsing route {#s-379d0bba98}
The Chengjiang *Urokodia* sample is dated to 519–518 Ma; the Wheeler Formation *Megachelicerax* sample is 504.5–500.5 Ma. Their combined 519–500.5 Ma envelope links separate specimens and competing analyses. It must not be read as continuous survival of a single group.
