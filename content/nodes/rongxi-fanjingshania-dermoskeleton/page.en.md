## Fanjingshania’s dermoskeleton and shoulder remodelling {#s-b6d6595e1e}
Fanjingshania is known from holotype IVPP V27433.1 and more than a thousand isolated elements, in a record dated to about 439 Ma. Tomography of six referred shoulder elements documents cyclical resorption of bone and dentine. The remains do not form an articulated skeleton or include associated teeth.
