## Quetzalcoatlus: PBDB classification route {#s-332b8a131f}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Quetzalcoatlus as a genus. This route links to two species pages: Quetzalcoatlus northropi and Q. lawsoni. Its database parent path helps readers navigate records; it is not a universal phylogenetic arrangement. The selected occurrence rows provide a route to member records, not a genus-wide account of anatomy or ecology.

## Member material linked to Quetzalcoatlus {#s-4ae6a2bc20}
The two species pages together cover four selected occurrences from four collections. Their recorded strata include Javelina and Black Peaks. Read the age bracket and identification notes for each collection before comparing sites: occurrence, collection and species counts measure different parts of the database, not the size of an ancient population.

## Limits of the selected Quetzalcoatlus records {#s-c79bf8f78c}
This route covers selected occurrence records for two species, not a complete descendant list or an ecological account of Quetzalcoatlus. Database record counts do not measure specimens, animals or population size; broad age bounds do not directly date origin or extinction. PBDB parentage is a navigation path kept separate from Catalogue of Life concepts and phylogenetic hypotheses.
