## What flower structure is described? {#s-05a267ad36}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records axillary flowers. The calyx lacks clear lobes and splits into one or two irregular segments. Flowers have five petals, anthers with one pore, and a stigma divided into five lobes. The row gives no flower dimensions, colors or sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-83aa65139e}
The regional dataset classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
