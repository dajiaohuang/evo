## Poiretia tetraphylla {#s-a3d6dd2324}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Poiretia tetraphylla* as an accepted species of *Poiretia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes an upright stem and four-leaflet leaves. Its inflorescence is listed as a panicle (a branched flower cluster) or raceme (a flower cluster arranged along a stalk), and the fruit as a many-segmented lomento; the habitat field records it as terrestrial.
