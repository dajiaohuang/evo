## Choose a reading path by research question {#s-ea490c812e}

Read the 2005 lower Caquetá–Apaporis transects alongside the 2022 Loreto species description. The 2021 Itaya–Nanay–Tigre survey adds regional context, but it records unidentified Cheracebus sp. and its counts are not assigned to C. aquinoi. Keep the three studies’ places, taxonomic scope and methods distinct; this path is not a genus-wide range, abundance or ecological synthesis.
