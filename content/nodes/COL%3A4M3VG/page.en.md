## Potamogeton nodosus: floating mature leaves {#s-6c002f0762}
A 1997 South African regional account describes Potamogeton nodosus as a perennial herb up to 1 m high. In deep water, its primary submerged leaves soon disintegrate. Mature leaves float and have elliptic to ovate, leathery blades with smooth margins and indistinct venation. The flowers are green.

These traits come from a regional species account cited to 1997. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish the full range of variation.

The account lists “mud banks and deep water, pools, rivers” as habitat settings.
