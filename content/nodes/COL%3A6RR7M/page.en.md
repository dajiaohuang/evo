## Moutabea victoriana {#s-0388072543}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Moutabea victoriana* as an accepted species of *Moutabea*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes leathery, oblanceolate to obovate leaves with a faintly papillate surface. Its tubular flower parts are thick, and the eight stamens are fused into an open sheath. The fruit wall is thick and woody in cross-section, while the seeds are velvety. This source record supplies morphology only, without a habitat claim.
