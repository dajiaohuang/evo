## Philodendron traunii: recorded morphology and habitat {#s-d61b490a2b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records feeding roots and short stem internodes. The leaf sheath is inconspicuous, while the conspicuous petiole is cylindrical. Blades are entire and sagittate or triangular, with an oblong sinus and entire margins. The field also gives “3 to 6” without making clear what is counted.

The axillary sympodial inflorescence has a green peduncle shorter than the spathe. The spathe is constricted; its outer tube is described as light green and its inner tube as greenish cream, while the field also records a bright-green outer surface and a cream inner blade. The spadix is subsessile. Flowers are listed with 4 stamens, an ovoid gynoecium and ovary, and 4 or 5 locules. Staminodes, ovules and stigma are unknown; placentation is listed as axial, basal, subbasal or biseriate. Fruit and seed states are unknown.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Hemiepífita,” meaning hemiepiphytic.

This regional habitat category supplies no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling coverage, and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil applicability.
