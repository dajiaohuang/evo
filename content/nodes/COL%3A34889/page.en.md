## *Dasycondylus platylepis*: white flowers and a persistent bristle crown {#s-26db181d33}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, stalked, oblong leaves with tapered tips, wedge-shaped bases, and serrated edges. The cylindrical stems are ridged and hairy. Short-stalked flower heads form branched, corymb-like clusters, with a conical, pitted receptacle beneath the florets. The corolla is white and funnel-shaped. Each five-ribbed fruitlet has a distinct attachment base and a single persistent row of about 30–40 rough pappus bristles.
