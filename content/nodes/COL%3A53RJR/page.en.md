## Symplocos corymboclados Brand {#s-18c8f39abb}
## Persistent bracts conceal the flower stalks

Symplocos corymboclados is monoecious, with simple hairs on branchlets that may be smooth or hairy. Its papery leaves are elliptic to obovate; the margins may be flat or toothed, and marginal glands may persist at the tooth tips. The lower surface ranges from strigose to nearly smooth. Compact flower clusters have peduncles concealed by persistent, triangular bracts. Bisexual flowers have five or six corolla lobes. The stamens may alternate in groups or have filaments joined into one bundle. The ellipsoid or cylindrical drupe retains a calyx that extends beyond the floral disk.
