## Scaly hairs and flowers on one side {#s-51f3218be4}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes scale-like hairs on young stems. Lance-shaped leaves have acuminate or tail-like tips, narrowed bases and entire margins without cilia. Three main basal veins are listed, with two faint veins just inside the edge; the underside is densely covered by scale-like hairs. The flowers are secund, arranged along one side of the inflorescence axis. Their hypanthium is bell-shaped, the calyx persists with flat-ended lobes, and each flower has five petals. The anther connective is prolonged and bears two lower lobes and one dorsal tooth. White anthers have a single small pore, and the ovary is hairless. The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” or terrestrial. These source descriptions do not establish how traits vary across populations.
