## Mandevilla widgrenii {#s-b672aea425}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mandevilla widgrenii* as an accepted species of *Mandevilla*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 supplies only a terrestrial habitat label. It does not name a locality, establish a complete range, or include a morphology description; the evidence therefore supports no further biological detail here.
