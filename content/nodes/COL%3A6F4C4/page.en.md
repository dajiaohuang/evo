## Accepted name in the catalogue {#s-3b4194681c}
The 20 August 2026 release of Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) accepts Eleutherodactylus glaphycompus Schwartz, 1973 as a species. Its recorded path is Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family), then Eleutherodactylus (genus). This is the name and placement in that release; another checklist or fossil database needs separate identity evidence.

## A compiled maximum adult length {#s-ae7f491d19}
AmphiBIO v1 reports a maximum adult body length of 34 mm for its Frost 2011 concept of E. glaphycompus. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs, but total length for salamanders and caecilians. It is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement for every individual, and the historical concept remains distinct from the current catalogue concept.
