AmphiBIO v1 lists Leptopelis bequaerti in row Anf0212, with genus Leptopelis, family Arthroleptina and order Anura. The source says its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a link to a current COL26.8 concept has not been established. The traits below describe this archived row, not a new species assessment.

## Foraging strata {#s-d03cdae426}
The row marks Ter=1 and Aqu=1 in its foraging-stratum fields. These are recorded categories, not measures of how often or how long an individual used each stratum; this row does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. Later TetrapodTraits revisions reported corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries, but this row has not been compared field by field. Treat these archived flags as awaiting row-level review.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-58fd643931}
AmphiBIO reports Body_size_mm=50 mm as a maximum adult body size. For Anura, this field denotes snout–vent length. It is a compiled maximum, not a sample distribution or a claim that every adult reaches this size.
