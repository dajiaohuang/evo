## Harperocallis paniculata: flowers and fruit {#s-2d3e5dc7c7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes leaf-like peduncle bracts and secondary inflorescence branches bearing many flowers. Bracteoles of the calyculus are ovate-deltoid, with only the midrib conspicuous. Flowers are erect, and the perianth is open at anthesis; it is white to cream internally and yellow externally. Stamens are erect; their filaments are flattened, white and marked with brown, and the anthers are sagittate. The fruit is a capsule, and the style is completely divided into 3.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.
