## Dryadella krenakiana {#s-000b80e4dc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology entry records a short ramicaule (1, 3, 8 or 15 mm) and flat to intermediate leaves that may be elliptic or linear. The inflorescence is crowded; the dorsal and lateral sepals are oval, and the lip is obovate. The column is listed at 2–2.5 mm.
