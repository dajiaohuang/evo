## What traits identify Manihot reptans in the record? {#s-2d5e1557bf}
The roots are not tuberous, and the xylopodium condition is unknown. Stems are under 0.45 m, smooth, hairless, and decumbent, with white latex. Simple, petiolate leaves attach at the base and may be three- or five-lobed; the middle lobe is larger than the elliptic or obovate side lobes. Threadlike stipules are hairless, persistent, and 1–5 mm long. Terminal bisexual racemes have basal female flowers with stalks under 2 cm; male and female buds are ovoid and hairless. Involucral bracts are bristle-like, hairless, and about 0.1 cm wide. The capsule is ovoid, hairless, and unribbed. Seeds are oblong and smooth, with a caruncle.
