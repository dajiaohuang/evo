## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Myzomela irianawidodoae Prawiradilaga, Baveja, Suparno, Ashari, N. S. R. Ng, Gwee, Verbelen & Rheindt, 2017 as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.
