## Verbesina luetzelburgii {#s-c54f81e16d}
## Wingless stems and two- or three-winged fruits

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes stems without wings. The alternate leaves may be petiolate or sessile; their blades are entire, papery, and elliptic to obovate or broadly elliptic, with tapered bases and the same colour on both surfaces.

Radiate flower heads form corymb-like clusters on a convex cluster axis. Disc florets are bisexual, while ray florets are neutral; both are yellow. The dry fruits bear two or three wings, and their pappus has two or three awns. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
