## *Maranta arundinacea*: an oval leaf and loose flower bracts {#s-391391aab8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character record describes oval leaf blades and a sparsely hairy pulvinus, the joint where a leaf stalk meets the blade. The flower cluster has loosely arranged bracts, the leaf-like structures around its flowers. Its corolla tube is straight, and the sepals—the outer floral parts—are longer than that tube.

The ovary, which develops into the fruit, is hairless or sparsely silky; the resulting capsule is recorded as ellipsoid, with sepals remaining upright around it. The habitat field says only “terrestrial,” without a collection locality or date. These characters are regional flora records, not a measured account of population variation.
