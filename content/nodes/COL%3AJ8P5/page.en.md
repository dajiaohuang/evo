## Overview {#s-591bef4529}
This page follows accepted COL26.8 species ID J8P5. MDD v2.5 supplies the display name Brown-headed Spider Monkey. The field study summarized here concerns the Ecuadorian subspecies A. f. fusciceps and a newly documented local population; its results are tied to the sampled landscape.

## Distribution surveys {#s-806619eaeb}
Cervera and Griffith surveyed a 1,500-km² landscape in Manabí, about 100 km southwest of the then-known range. After an initial encounter in 2014, they interviewed local residents and used call playback in six forest fragments between March and July 2015. Groups of one to seven were seen in four fragments, and 16 individuals were visually confirmed. The landscape was reported as 89% deforested, with most fragments under 300 ha.
