## Philodendron propinquum: vegetative and reproductive features {#s-53f4fb6def}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records feeding roots and a flagelliform stem. The leaf sheath is conspicuous; the short petiole is cylindrical and green. Blades are entire, elliptic or oblong, with acuminate tips and entire margins.

The terminal inflorescence is sympodial. Its short green peduncle is shorter than the spathe, which is slightly constricted and cream-colored; the spadix is stipitate. The flowers have short stamens narrowed at the base and staminodes also narrowed at the base. The gynoecium is subcylindrical, the ovary ovoid, and the stigma cylindrical. The entry records 3 locules, 91 ovules, axial placentation, cylindrical berries and numerous seeds.

These are descriptors from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not identify the material as wild, cultivated, managed or fossil.

The dataset labels the habitat “Hemiepífita,” meaning hemiepiphytic.
