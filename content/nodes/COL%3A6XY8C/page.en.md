## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-5d83f1b854}
COL26.8 (Catalogue of Life dataset 316115, released 20 August 2026) records *Scinax tigrinus* as an accepted species. Its recorded hierarchy is Anura (order) → Hylidae (family) → Scinax (genus). This is the classification in that release; the name and placement alone do not describe the organism’s biology.

## Reported adult size values {#s-5fdc2a2d83}
The AmphiBIO compilation uses the historical name Scinax tigrinus under a Frost 2011 classification. That source concept has not been shown to match the current COL26.8 species concept exactly. AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 30.8 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
