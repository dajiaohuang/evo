## Geonoma undata {#s-3278f38be3}
Geonoma undata is recorded with solitary or clustered elongated stems and entire or irregularly pinnate leaves. Its inflorescence may branch through three orders; floral pits are spiral, decussate or grouped, with a distal lip. The black-purple fruit has an irregular surface noted as bearing subepidermal fibres; habitat is terrestrial.
