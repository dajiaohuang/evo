COL26.8 accepts Prosciurillus murinus in Sciuridae. EltonTraits 1.0 provides three ecological field groups for a same-name, same-family record. Its framework uses older bird or MSW3 mammal taxonomy, so the match does not settle later splits or synonymy.

## Standardized diet weights {#s-7d412657ca}
The nonzero weights are other plant material, including fungi in the source category (40%); fruit (30%); and seeds, nuts and spores (30%). ABC combines high, reasonable and partial confidence and may include similar congeners. These standardized literature weights are not measured diet proportions; zero does not establish never eating a category. Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader source categories than their names suggest.

## Scansorial stratum {#s-7d5e154508}
The record assigns S, scansorial, with confidence code A. Metadata groups mammalian stratum values under Nowak (1999). This is a category, not a time proportion or claim of exclusive use.

Daytime activity is marked 1. Multiple periods may each be 1, so they do not form a daily time budget or a fixed schedule across places and seasons. ABC confidence may include similar congeneric evidence; typical genus and family values did not fill gaps.

## Source labels and evidence {#s-896f368d23}
The groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, strata and activity may draw on different material; a Ref number in bird and mammal tables is not necessarily the same source. ## Limits of the foraging summary {#s-0a1424d602}
This is a reading guide to an EltonTraits 1.0 same-name, same-family record, not a complete species account. Historical concepts are not proven equivalent to COL26.8. Food weights standardize literature descriptions, not intake, stomach contents, energy or observation time. ABC fields may include congeneric evidence; not every field is a direct observation of this species. Source works have not been reread. Current variation, distribution, population and conservation need other evidence.
