## A morphology matrix samples Silurian–Devonian genera {#s-6c7eea8514}
Genus-level observations and a morphology matrix test osteostracan relationships, classification and character polarity. The 433.4–358.9 Ma interval is the stratigraphic envelope of the genera represented in that matrix, not an exact global origin or extinction boundary; unsampled occurrences could extend either edge.
