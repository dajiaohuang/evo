## *Felicia tsitsikamae*: recurved leaves on a low shrublet {#s-a49b886bf9}
The regional account describes a low shrublet to about 30 cm, softly covered with long, woolly hairs. Its egg-shaped leaves bend downward and have margins rolled back beneath the blade. Solitary blue flower heads are radiate. A related passage places the plant among the *Felicia amoena* group and describes its growth as distinctly shrubby, with leaves nearly always opposite. The account records rocky slopes at high elevation as habitat.
