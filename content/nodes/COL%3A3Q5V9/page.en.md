## Ischnosiphon petiolatus {#s-f393fced8e}
## An unbranched, upright growth form

The Brazilian Flora entry describes an upright or rosette-forming stem without branches. Its leaves are elliptic and symmetric, and each small flower cluster has two bracteoles. The record gives the petals as pink and the outer staminodes as white or pink; it does not say whether the alternatives reflect variation among flowers or specimens. The habitat field labels the species terrestrial.
