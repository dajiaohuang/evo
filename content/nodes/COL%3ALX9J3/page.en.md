## China–Vietnam border locality account {#s-3231dd9194}
Male holotype KIZ 93088 came from Dawei Mountain, Pingbian, Yunnan, at 1900 m. The monograph recognized Pingbian and Xichou in Yunnan and neighbouring Lao Cai in northern Vietnam, citing previous work and giving an approximate elevation of 1900 m. This approximate locality account is not an exact species-wide elevation range or a demonstration of continuous occupancy across the border.

## Original-description adult sample {#s-84a10b34e1}
Based on Rao and Yang (1997), the diagnosis reported eighteen adult males at 34–37 mm snout–vent length and three females at 40–46 mm. It described vomerine ridges and teeth, smooth dorsal skin with a weak interrupted V-shaped ridge, unwebbed toes, and a subgular vocal sac in males. These cited measurements concern the original sample, not a newly examined series in this monograph. The vocal-sac statement alone does not supply a breeding season or a call measurement.

## A character cited in the gutu comparison {#s-2e9285d93b}
The 2025 gutu description lists Boulenophrys daweimontis as with vomerine teeth. This is an attributed comparator statement within that paper’s taxonomic scope, not a new measurement series for this species. The gutu adult sample spans 34.4–44.7 mm in seven males and 36.2–52.8 mm in eight females; those numbers belong to gutu, not to this comparator.
