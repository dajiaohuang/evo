## Vaccinium puberulum Klotzsch ex Meisn. {#s-953082cada}
## Very leathery leaves and globose berries

Vaccinium puberulum has erect, much-branched shoots. Its alternate leaves are very leathery and loosely spaced rather than overlapping; the blades are elliptic to obovate, taper at the base and have serrated edges. The leaf tip may be blunt, sharp or rounded. Panicles carry urn-shaped to nearly globose flowers with white or pink corollas. The ovary is inferior and five-chambered, and the fruit is a globose berry.
