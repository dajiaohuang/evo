## Leaf pattern and lip lobes distinguish the flowers {#s-9d33181fb8}
Two SANBI regional descriptions characterize H. orthoceras as a terrestrial herb up to 300 mm tall. Its dark-green leaves remain at flowering and show a silvery net pattern. The outer lobes of the lip are longer than the inner ones; the white-netted leaves and longer outer lobes distinguish it from H. macowaniana, while a straight spur and triangular central lip lobes help separate it from H. parviflora. The flowers have green sepals, white petals and purple-veined white lips. These are regional account characters, not a survey of variation across the species.

## A forest-floor setting in one regional account {#s-37bf01d686}
One SANBI regional account records H. orthoceras growing in humus on mossy rocks in forest. That habitat description supplies no locality or sampling frequency.
