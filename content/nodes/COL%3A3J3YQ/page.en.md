## Habenaria kraenzliniana {#s-3e72faafa1}
The South African account describes Habenaria kraenzliniana as a terrestrial herb up to 300 mm tall. Its yellowish-green flowers have a 30–40 mm spur; the lip’s threadlike side lobes are 30–33 mm long, compared with a middle lobe of about 5 mm. It is recorded on stony, grassy hillsides.
