## What single-flowered traits are recorded? {#s-aa52cc8899}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records spherical glands on the stem and on sessile leaves that have the same color on both faces and a single vein. Flowers are solitary. The bell-shaped floral cup and narrow-triangular sepals bear spherical glands; the sepals are shorter than the cup. The corolla is magenta. Anthers match one another in color and have multiple pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. This regional database field gives no locality or sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-aad7379289}
The regional row records terrestrial habitat and supplies no locality or date.
