## Leishan locality account after taxonomic restriction {#s-c495606e02}
Male holotype CIB 24339, formerly CIB 63II0615, came from Getou, Fangxiang, Leishan, Guizhou, at 1100 m. The monograph examined two males from Leigong Mountain at approximately 1620 m and restricted its recognized distribution to Leishan at 800–1800 m. This is the paper’s taxonomically revised locality account, not a demonstration of continuous occupancy or physiological elevation limits.

## New male measurements and unit gaps in cited values {#s-3f33aa4f52}
The two newly examined males measured 49.6–51.6 mm snout–vent length. The diagnosis also cited historical values of 47.2–54.4 for eighteen males and 54.0–55.0 for two females without printing units beside those endpoints; their unit remains uncertified here rather than silently supplied. Anatomical descriptions included spiny dorsal granules, one-third toe webbing with wide fringes, and large sparse black conical nuptial spines on fingers I and II in breeding males, with a subgular vocal sac. Historical samples are not combined with the new two-male series.

## Former broad records divided among unresolved and named lineages {#s-0e90fec713}
The remarks cited molecular evidence that the formerly broad B. spinata populations were paraphyletic. The monograph assigned northern regional records to B. qianbeiensis and northeastern Guizhou/southeastern Chongqing records to B. fanjingmontis, while restricting B. spinata to Leishan using voucher and molecular evidence. The corresponding qianbeiensis and fanjingmontis accounts still required further molecular vouchers for some historical assignments. A separate Guangxi lineage remained taxonomically unresolved because vouchers were unavailable. These reassignment decisions and unresolved cases are retained with their evidence limits, not converted into new species names or verified records.
