## Caladium bicolor {#s-420e1004df}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes a globose stem and shield-like leaves with heart-shaped or arrow-shaped bases. The green blades are recorded with white or wine-red markings; the spathe (the bract around the flower-bearing axis) has a white blade and a green to wine-green tube. Female flowers are pink and male flowers cream-colored. The habitat field says soil.
