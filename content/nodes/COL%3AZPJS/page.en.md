## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton araripensis? {#s-3affa6f931}
Young branches and both leaf surfaces bear lepidote, scale-like hairs. The short-stalked oval leaves have looping side veins and toothed to finely toothed margins. Nectar glands are absent, and young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are shortly stalked; male flowers are bell-shaped. Female sepals are separate and unequal, and the petals are reduced to glands. In the bud, the calyx parts meet edge to edge (a valvate arrangement). The fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
