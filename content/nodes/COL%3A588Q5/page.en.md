## Trichilia euneura {#s-a591a1c81e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records star-shaped hairs, alternate or nearly opposite leaflets and a pubescent ovary.

## Star-shaped hairs and pinnate leaf alternatives {#s-7b9bcb9af1}
The stem-bud field records no cataphylls and star-shaped hairs. Leaves may be odd- or even-pinnate; the unwinged rachis bears alternate or subopposite leaflets whose bases are not heteromorphic. Leaflet bases are acute, attenuate or cuneate, and the lower surface is hairy. Petals are free and imbricate; staminal filaments are partly united, a nectary is present, and the ovary is pubescent and three-locular with two collateral ovules per locule. Fruits are ellipsoid or obovoid and pubescent. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
