## A Devonian transition sample is not an ancestor chain {#s-22c6f93707}
Clack’s synthesis integrates sampled Devonian tetrapodomorph fishes and early digit-bearing tetrapods into a transition framework. Its 385.3–358.86 Ma window applies to the samples included in the review; it does not date the origin or full duration of Tetrapodomorpha, identify one direct ancestor, or establish a complete global fossil range.
