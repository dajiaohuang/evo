## What features are recorded for its flower clusters? {#s-2307322541}
The stipules persist. The inflorescence bears two-flowered scorpioid cymes and at least two extrafloral nectaries; the prophylls are threadlike or deeply divided. Flowers bend at a joint. Their upper petals differ from the lower petals, and their claws are recorded as four to eight times shorter than the calyx. Ten stamens join at the base. The densely hairy capsule is spiraled.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-59c347e10e}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
