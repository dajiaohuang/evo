## How PBDB organizes fossil occurrences {#s-2beb295cfb}
PBDB groups the selected fossil-occurrence records for *Protocetidae* under its recorded classification path and links them to collections, strata, age brackets, and references. This hierarchy organizes database records; it remains distinct from COL taxonomy and is not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Named protocetid occurrences and their strata {#s-c435937943}
The route includes *Phiomicetus anubis*, *Takracetus simus*, *Rodhocetus kasranii* and *R. balochistanensis*. Each occurrence should be read with its own collection, stratum, age bracket and original reference. Those records describe where material was registered, not the number of animals that lived there.

## How to interpret the displayed age brackets {#s-d4863824f6}
These PBDB age brackets bound the registered occurrences shown here. They do not by themselves establish a taxon’s global first or last appearance or a clade’s origin or extinction.

## Protocetidae was a varied middle Eocene assemblage {#s-50687b797d}
Uhen’s synthesis treats Protocetidae as a geographically dispersed middle Eocene assemblage with varied amphibious adaptations. The 47–37.7 Ma interval bounds the reviewed protocetid sample, not uninterrupted global occupancy or exact family endpoints. The group is not reduced here to one ancestor or one habitat.
