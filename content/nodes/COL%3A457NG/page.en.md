## Two-armed stem hairs and deciduous flower bracteoles {#s-96737ab466}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and two-armed stem hairs. Leaves are elliptic or ovate, with a channelled upper-surface midrib and a glabrous underside. Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below leaf nodes are deciduous. Rounded inflorescence branchlets carry bracteoles beneath the flowers that also fall away. One or two inflorescences are listed per axillary shoot, in the leaf axil and in an opposite pyramidal arrangement. The field gives no frequencies for the alternatives.

## A club-shaped flower cup and a globose fruit {#s-40cd8f5ff8}
The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup (hypanthium) is club-shaped (clavate); its covering is listed as fine-haired, densely woolly, or pubescent. Sepals are partly fused, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens by tearing. On the globose fruit, the calyx lobes fall away. These alternatives are recorded in one regional dataset field without frequencies.

## A terrestrial label, without site details {#s-a4b63d3f09}
The same regional dataset classifies the species as terrestrial (terrícola), but gives no locality or more specific habitat description.
