## Keep the compiled-mass denominator {#s-c4549b15ef}
Mass=6.3 g and Mass.Source=Dunning denote a compiled species average; the 4 morphology birds are not a verified independent weighing denominator. The original book and individual weights were not read, leaving sex, season and locality minimum–maximum bounds unsupported.

## Nectarivore is not a flower-visiting record {#s-37f299fc0d}
This row has Trophic.Niche=Nectarivore and Trophic.Level=Herbivore. The source defines Nectarivore as at least 60% of food resources from nectar; that threshold is not a locally measured seasonal nectar percentage here. Herbivore level is defined by at least 70% of food resources coming from plants, a different threshold from nectar niche. These compiled categories contain no flower or insect species, visiting frequency or pollination-success records, leaving detailed menus and ecological effects unsupported.

## Read perching, vegetation and migration separately {#s-f4bac94571}
Primary.Lifestyle=Insessorial means much time is spent perching above ground on branches or other raised substrates, not individual observations of hovering to feed on nectar. Habitat=Forest; density code 1 means dense vegetation, not birds per square metre. Migration=1 is the sedentary category, without implying lifelong immobility. These historical categories provide neither current range-wide occupancy nor individual movement radii or routes; historical map fields are not interpreted in this batch.
