## Accepted name and placement {#s-cb643d5edb}
COL26.8 lists Ceratophrys aurita (Raddi, 1823) as a species in Anura, Ceratophryidae and Ceratophrys. This name and placement come from the release dated 20 August 2026; another checklist or fossil database may use a different species concept.

## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-1b0577acc6}
A 2019 analysis of archived recordings describes each analyzed call as one note containing 80–172 fused pulses, with frequency modulation that may rise or fall. These observations describe the analyzed recordings; the proposed advertisement and aggressive functions discussed for complex calls remain hypotheses.

## Temporal and spectral measurements retain their samples {#s-b759888888}
For the analyzed male, the paper reports call duration of 0.87 ± 0.09 s (range 0.54–0.98; 25 calls), dominant frequency of 1261 ± 69 Hz (1163–1421), and a rate of 27 calls/min. These are study summaries from one male, not species-wide limits. Repeated calls do not increase the number of sampled animals.

## Filtering and energy-defined frequencies {#s-1b172d8df1}
The archive files were standardized to 44.1 kHz and 16-bit resolution and individually normalized to a −0.8 dB peak; the table specifies no filter.
