## A meal preserved inside one trilobite {#s-d2e38a61dc}
Synchrotron X-ray microtomography maps shelly remains through the gut of Bohemolichas incola specimen 8 from the Šárka Formation. The contents are direct evidence from one fossil; feeding rate, food choice and gut chemistry are interpretations of that single preserved meal.

## What the scan shows {#s-87a84f5fc3}
The researchers used synchrotron microtomography—X-ray imaging reconstructed as fine internal slices—to map ostracod valves, hyolith material and stylophoran plates inside specimen 8. The reported voxel size is 11.35 micrometres. The named shelly remains are observations in this individual trilobite, not a survey of its usual diet.

## A fossil meal has limits {#s-e31c44b7dc}
The paper discusses rapid, indiscriminate feeding and a neutral-to-alkaline gut, but these are physiological interpretations built from one shelly meal. Soft foods would not necessarily be preserved, and a single specimen cannot reveal population-level variation. The fossil records what remained and where it lay; it does not preserve feeding behaviour as it happened.
