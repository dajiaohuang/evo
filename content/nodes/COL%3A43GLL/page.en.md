## Mimosa oligophylla {#s-4ee2069fa3}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa oligophylla* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry lists the stem as hairless or covered with rough, appressed short hairs, with forward-pressed hairs; a projection may occur between pairs of leaf divisions. Flower clusters are listed as globose or ellipsoid, and the petal lobes as hairless or short-haired. These alternatives belong to the regional flora record.
