## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima chalcophylla? {#s-e9787b9545}
The lower leaf surface has silky hairs. Each flower cluster bears one flower on a stalkless pedicel. Petals may be white or pink, and the anthers are hairless. The fruit is hairless or silky-hairy.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a12673e574}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
