## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-49fce263a4}
COL26.8 records Rhineura floridana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Rhineuridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-bb546863b1}

ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-30a6db51a4}

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-70b8c0335c}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
