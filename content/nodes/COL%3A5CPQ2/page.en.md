## What is recorded about its broadened leaf sheaths? {#s-417197bbf1}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records leaves in two or nearly two ranks. Their sheaths are reddish-brown or blackish and widen markedly at the base, sometimes forming ear-like lobes. The blades are listed as flat or nearly cylindrical, with rough or wrinkled surfaces. The cylindrical flower stalk is 20–50 cm long, and the ovoid or obovoid spike has 10–25 flowers. Several states remain alternatives in the source field.
