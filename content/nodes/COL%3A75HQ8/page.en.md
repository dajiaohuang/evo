## ## Tuberous roots and constricted pods {#s-0f46049186}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records tuberous roots and twining stems. Leaflet margins are listed as entire or toothed. The fruit valves are strongly constricted between the seeds, and the seeds are kidney-shaped and not flattened. A habitat field says “terrestrial,” but supplies no locality or more specific habitat description. These few source fields do not establish the species’ full morphology, range or variation.
