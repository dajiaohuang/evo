## Accepted name and placement {#s-fb7d52ddc4}

## One land-based encounter was missed by the water assay {#s-7af1058c4c}
Pristimantis variabilis was observed once and was among the species detected only by visual surveys in the Tena study. The Discussion invokes terrestrial habitat use and potentially low transfer of DNA into sampled water as explanations, drawing on earlier natural-history accounts. Those explanations were not tested by measuring DNA shedding, activity cycles or habitat selection in this species. Aquatic nondetection does not overturn the visual record or establish regional absence. The study is not a new description of diurnality or reproduction.
