## Branches, leaves, flowers and fruit {#s-7f08da7ec0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records brown, smooth branches with lenticels and no spines. Leaves bear 21–27 leaflets. Their blades are narrowly lance-shaped, with unequal, wedge-shaped bases and tips described as acute or acuminate. The slender rachis (leaf axis) is hairless; the small leaflet stalks have fine, short hairs. Stipules and stipels are recorded absent. Flowers are arranged in racemes; their stalks have fine hairs, as does the bell-shaped calyx. The corolla field records one yellow, hairless petal, while the male and female floral structures are marked unknown. The fruit is a hairless pod, and the flattened seeds are black. These are details in a regional dataset description; the source does not assess range or variation across the species.
