## What leaf, flower and ovary traits are recorded? {#s-ad106bc216}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records long-stalked glandular hairs on the stem. Petiolate leaves have the same color on both faces, spherical glands and three veins. Flowers are solitary. The oblong floral cup bears spherical glands; triangular sepals also have glands and are shorter than the cup. The corolla is magenta. Anthers match one another in color and have four pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. These are the regional field's listed traits, without a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-d4cd1f691f}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality or collection date.
