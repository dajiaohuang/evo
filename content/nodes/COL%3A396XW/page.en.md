## Elegia acockii {#s-69f3ec2326}
## Leaf coverings that remain on a tufted plant

The South African regional flora describes Elegia acockii as a dioecious perennial (female and male flowers grow on separate plants) that forms tufts and reaches 1 m. It has unbranched stems, with several persistent leaf sheaths (the basal parts of leaves that wrap around a stem) on each stem. Persistent sheaths remain attached as the plant matures. The account lists sandy flats as habitat.
