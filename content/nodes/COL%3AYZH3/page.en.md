## What is known about Couepia reflexa in this record? {#s-24c4cc08bc}
The leaves are oblong-elliptic and hairless. Flowers may be arranged in panicles or racemes, and the cylindrical receptacle bears 25–45 separate stamens. The fruit’s outer wall is not described.
