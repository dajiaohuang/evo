## What calyx and fruit details are recorded? {#s-f789717e79}
The stems bear yellowish woolly hairs made up of tiny star-shaped hairs, with papillae present. The simple leaves are broadly ovate or nearly round, with deeply heart-shaped bases and crenate-toothed edges. Upright flowers grow singly or in pairs. The cup-shaped calyx has strongly raised veins and papillae; its lobes taper to a point. Petal color ranges from pink or lilac to white. The fruit has 16–18 segments (mericarps), whose tips may lack a beak or have one; the dorsal seams are smooth.
