## Stem, leaf, flower and fruit structure {#s-9dc18ef1c8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes cylindrical branches with stem lenticels (small pores); the hair covering is recorded as absent or villous (long and soft). Triangular stipules (small appendages at the leaf base) fall away. The leaf stalk (petiole) is described as cylindrical or margined (with a defined edge), while the central axis carrying leaflets (rachis) is winged. Each leaf has two leaflet pairs. A stalkless, saucer-shaped nectar gland (nectary) is recorded. Flowers are arranged in racemes (clusters of individually stalked flowers along a main axis); the calyx (outer floral cup) is bell-shaped, and flowers have stalks. The dataset marks fruit-wall texture, shape and hair covering as unknown. This is a single regional field and does not establish the full variation of these traits.

## Habitat category {#s-05ce488482}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” or terrestrial (growing on land). This is one dataset-row category, not a locality, survey result, or complete account of the species' distribution.
