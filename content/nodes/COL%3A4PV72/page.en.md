## Ground feeding, habitat and sedentary category {#s-32a35db343}
Habitat is Woodland; density code 1 means dense habitats, not birds per unit area. Omnivore niche means use of multiple resources without one dominant niche. Trophic level is separately recorded as Herbivore; niche and level are different fields whose labels are not substituted for each other. Terrestrial means mainly feeding while walking or hopping on the ground; Migration=1 is sedentary. Neither supplies individual routes, flight capacity or annual diet percentages.
