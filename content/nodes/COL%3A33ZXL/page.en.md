## What traits distinguish Dalechampia micromeria? {#s-e04d06cc68}
The herbaceous plant lacks a xylopodium. It has oval simple leaves and three-leaflet compound leaves, with no lobed simple leaves recorded. The flower cluster is surrounded by three-lobed bracts and has resin glands. Female sepals are deeply pinnately divided; the style-column tip is moderately lobed. The fruit is finely hairy, and the seeds are smooth.
