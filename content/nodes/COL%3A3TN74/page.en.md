## Accepted name and classification {#s-46900ac336}

## Four specimens compared with two mitochondrial references {#s-64ef7a4778}
The study sequenced MPEG 44060–44062 and 44064 from Conceição do Araguaia. Their mitochondrial 16S (the mitochondrial ribosomal RNA gene) ribosomal RNA gene sequences differed by 1.6–1.9% from the cited Cuiabá sequence MW291351.1 and by 0–0.25% from the Estreito sequence KU495350.1. The authors used these comparisons to support assignment to L. podicipinus. The four specimens and two reference localities are not a comprehensive genetic survey; low or high mitochondrial distances are not automatically converted into a universal species threshold or proof of reproductive isolation.

## A first Pará record and the cited broader range {#s-df98997ebe}
The 2025 authors called their material the first Pará record. They cited a broad distribution in open formations of Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil and Paraguay and earlier Amazon records near the Madeira and Guaporé rivers. The account placed the frog in the morphologically challenging melanonotus group. These are dated record and background statements, not independently certified contemporary occupancy across countries or a complete habitat-tolerance assessment.
