## Needle like leaves and prominent capsule horns {#s-972d6d5ef9}
The source description records a sprawling or erect, glabrescent subshrub to 40 cm tall. Leaves are sessile, fascicled, rigid and needle like, with spine tipped apices. Flowers are sessile, axillary and pink. Capsules are scabrid and prominently horned. The ecology field gives sandstone slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
