## Plant and floral structure {#s-3945f08c56}
The SANBI account describes clump-forming plants 1.50–1.65 m tall, with a corm about 30 mm across. The lowest peduncle is 45–90 mm long. Several foliage leaves measure 800–900 × 6–12 mm, with about five reduced sheathing leaves. Spikes are erect: the terminal spike is densely 11–13-flowered, and there are about 10 lateral spikes, each with 5–10 flowers.

Bracts are lance-shaped to elliptic, tapering to a point, and measure 14–20 × 8 mm; they extend for two or more internodes. Their basal one-third to one-half is green, drying brown and often brown-flecked. The remainder is membranous and whitish, very sparsely flecked reddish brown or ochre, with a reddish-brown to ochre midline; numerous veins join into a solid patch below. Flowers are nearly round in outline, pale magenta-pink, with conspicuous red and yellow diamond markings at the base of the tepals. The perianth tube is (5–)7–8 mm long, tepals 18–19 × 7.0–8.5 mm, and anthers 6–7 mm. Stigmas are held 7–9 mm below the top of the perianth.

## Diagnostic comparison {#s-bcfdb755d3}
A diagnostic account also describes *D. erectum* as clump-forming, with stems over 1.5 m tall, leaves 6–12 mm wide, erect spikes, and partly solid bracts. Its nearly round flowers have a distinct red-and-yellow marking at the base of each tepal. The account compares it with *Dierama insigne*, which also has partly solid bracts but is described with solitary stems or a few stems in small tufts, narrower leaves 2–6 mm wide, and pendulous spikes with fewer lateral spikes, mostly 2–4.
