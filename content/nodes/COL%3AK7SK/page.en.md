## Baccharis uncinella {#s-f2a0880528}
## Obovate leaves and single axillary heads

The record describes developed, sessile leaves with entire margins and blades ranging from obovate to oblong. Branches are unwinged and either smooth or striated; no woody underground base (xylopodium) is recorded. Flower heads are solitary in the leaf axils, with the source listing either all tubular male florets or all threadlike female florets. Habitat labels include both rock-dwelling and terrestrial.
