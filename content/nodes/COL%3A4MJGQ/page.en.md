## Accepted name and placement {#s-47390eedec}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis leucorrhinus Boano, Mazzotti & Sindaco, 2008 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). ## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-3352e11b14}
Historical source concept: Pristimantis leucorrhinus, under the compilation's Frost 2011 classification. Equivalence with the current species concept remains unverified. AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 21 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
