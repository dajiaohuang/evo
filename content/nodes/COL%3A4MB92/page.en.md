## *Prepusa connata* {#s-87a72d075f}
## Bracts joined around the stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a green, unbranched stem, with leaves crowded near the base or in a rosette. Their tips are acute or taper to a point. The bracts are fused together for up to half their length. The calyx is red, wine-colored, or pink; its broad, flattened ovate lobes have a smooth outer face. The corolla is recorded as white, cream, yellowish, or pink and has a bell shape. The color alternatives are those in a single regional flora record, not a statement that all occur on one plant.
