## Aristea zeyheri {#s-44d92e4222}
Aristea zeyheri is a slender, often solitary perennial with an unbranched stem and cylindrical leaves. The regional account describes relatively large, nodding blue flowers, an elongate ovary and fringed, broadly lobed stigma branches. Its greenish spathes have translucent margins. It is recorded on damp sandstone slopes. These features distinguish the source’s account of A. zeyheri; the habitat note does not define the species’ full ecological range.
