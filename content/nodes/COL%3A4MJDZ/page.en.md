## Accepted name and placement {#s-6d91e728db}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis fallax (Lynch & Rueda-Almonacid, 1999) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-353c25c015}
AmphiBIO’s Frost 2011 record for Pristimantis fallax codes terrestrial foraging, direct development, and up to 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiTherm separately reports adult CTmax of 34.6 °C and CTmin of 6.1 °C using loss of righting response.
