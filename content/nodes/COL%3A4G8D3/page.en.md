A regional morphology field describes Philodendron rheophyticum with a decumbent stem, an elliptic blade, and a stalked spadix whose female, sterile-male, and fertile-male zones have different recorded colors or shapes. The entry contains no habitat field.

## Morphology, inflorescence, and flowers {#s-af0d68356c}
The field records clasping roots, a decumbent stem, and a deciduous prophyll. The leaf sheath is conspicuous. The short, cylindrical green petiole is slightly channelled above; the entire elliptic blade has an acuminate tip, and its lateral veins are obscure when fresh. The axillary sympodial inflorescence is listed as one unit. Its short cylindrical peduncle is shorter than the nonconstricted spathe, whose tube and blade surfaces are light green. The stalked spadix has a light-green female zone, a cylindrical sterile-male zone, and a white fertile-male zone.

The stamens are short and narrowed at the base; the short staminodes are also narrowed at the base. The gynoecium is cylindrical and the ovary subcylindrical. The record gives 3 or 4 locules, 20 ovules, axial placentation, and a discoid stigma. Berry and seed traits are unknown.
