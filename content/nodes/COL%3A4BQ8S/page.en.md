## Reflexed, spreading corolla lobes and broad basal keels {#s-50fd18effb}
The regional account describes a perennial herb 220–500 mm tall, with flat leaf margins. Flowers are light green or greenish white, often lightly flecked outside with dull purple-brown; the corona is light dull green. Corolla lobes reflex and spread. Corona lobes have a prominent basal pair of keels, always as broad as or broader than high, and their tips extend into linear, appendage-like points. In Natal, Swaziland and the eastern Transvaal, the account distinguishes this species from P. reflectens by its usually ovate, elliptic or oblong leaves (rather than linear-lanceolate to lanceolate or narrowly ovate), reflexed-spreading corolla lobes (rather than straight-back reflexed lobes), corona blades usually with an appendage-like apex, and keels no narrower than their height (P. reflectens has keels higher than broad). The account also cautions that sympatric plants can be difficult to distinguish: some P. appendiculatus have narrow leaves like P. reflectens. The habitat entry records grassland; these regional fields do not establish the full range.
