## Pale-pink flowers with a long tube {#s-3aaa80c74d}
Hesperantha leucantha grows to 200 mm and bears pale-pink flowers. Its tube is usually 12–15 mm long, with tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 9–15 mm; the account reports flowering from January to April.

## Mesic grassland and shaded rock {#s-2c85e96ce9}
The source records mesic grassland, shaded rocks, cliffs in kloof (a steep-sided valley), and damp places. It does not claim these notes cover the full range.
