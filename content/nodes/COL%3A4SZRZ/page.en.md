## A woolly rosette beneath pale disk flowers {#s-dce7e6a41a}
This record describes a low, woody flowering stem rising from a rosette of leathery leaves. The oval to oblong leaves have toothed edges and prominent veins, with rusty velvet-like hairs near their expanded leaf-stalk bases. Branched clusters carry disk-shaped heads with 30–37 white or pink bisexual florets. Each cylindrical fruit bears one row of many bristles joined at the base. The habitat field says terrestrial.
