## Accepted name and placement {#s-599cc98874}
COL26.8 records Eleutherodactylus albolabris (Taylor, 1943) at species rank in Eleutherodactylus, within Eleutherodactylinae, order Eleutherodactylidae. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026, separate from the historical source classification.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-dfd3ae4412}
The Frost 2011 AmphiBIO row codes arboreal foraging. This is a broad source category, not a detailed account of habitat use. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited microhabitat and body-mass entries; the affected row has not been checked, and current-concept equivalence is unresolved.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-7cb7aa994a}
The compilation reports maximum adult body length of 23.8 mm. For this anuran the measure is snout–vent length; it is a compiled maximum, not an individual range.
