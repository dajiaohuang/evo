## What branch and flower traits define Euphorbia stellispina? {#s-c957fcbabd}
This dioecious, much-branched, spiny succulent reaches about 0.7 × 0.7 m. Its club-shaped cylindrical branches are 30–80 mm thick; low tubercles merge into 10–16 shallow angles. Many stout, persistent peduncles, 4–20 mm long, later become spiny, branching near the tip into three to five rigid sterile spines 4–10 mm long. Leaves fall early and are 3–10 mm long. Numerous 3.5–5 mm cyathia form near branch tips on 5–20 mm stalks; each has five smooth-edged green to reddish glands. The sessile capsule is hairless and 5–7 mm across.

## Where is it recorded growing? {#s-b3cedb7b8e}
The source lists Karroid scrub on stony slopes with scattered dolerite rocks.
