## Oval leaves and bell- or cylinder-shaped heads {#s-11c5765a1c}
This record leaves the root form unknown and describes few-branched stems with leaves along them. The blades range from elliptic to ovate or oblong, with smooth or finely hairy surfaces and entire or minutely toothed edges. Sessile heads may be solitary or grouped; their involucres range from bell-shaped to cylindrical and contain about 15 florets. The fruits may be top-shaped or cylindrical, smooth or softly hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial.
