## Haworthiopsis reinwardtii {#s-ebcc6962a9}
Haworthiopsis reinwardtii forms leafy stems or mats, with white tubercles arranged in rows on the leaves. The regional account records it in hilly summer-rainfall country at about 70–100 m, growing in exposed or shaded sites and on stony slopes. The cited source describes firm soils and annual rainfall of roughly 500–700 mm.
