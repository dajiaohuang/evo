## Mikania guilleminii {#s-628ff3e791}
## Long-tapered leaves and three basal veins

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records smooth, striate, twining branches; stem pith was not observed. Opposite, ovate leaves have entire margins, narrowing bases and long-tapered, sometimes tail-like tips. Both surfaces are smooth, and three main veins radiate from the base. The thin blades are described as membranous.

Loose panicles carry stalked heads, with short, linear bracts at the base of each head stalk. The elliptic bracts around a head are smooth and blunt-tipped. The corolla is tubular, with a limb longer than the tube and triangular, tapered lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial; this regional entry does not establish the plant's full range.
