## A notialis mitogenome supplies a voucher-scoped comparison {#s-d045003e59}

Its eleven-mitogenome analysis included ten Hynobius species and one outgroup, placing notialis near unisacculus and quelpaertensis within the sampled Korean group. This is mitochondrial placement from a limited comparison, not a fully sampled genus tree or a direct gene-flow measurement. The thirty-seven annotated mitochondrial genes do not represent the entire nuclear genome, and a single specimen cannot quantify geographic haplotype diversity.
