## *Xyris coutensis*: a very tall flower stalk {#s-ac5683d3d9}
The stalk rises above 70 cm and has one lengthwise ridge, with hairs along it. Its egg-shaped to top-shaped flower head carries more than 25 sterile bracts and more than 25 flowers. The sepals are free, and the seeds are elliptical in outline.

The source categorizes the plant as terrestrial.
