## *Phlegmariurus biformis*: hanging stems with leaves of unequal size {#s-f1d09e3056}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional character record describes hanging or flexuous stems with green to brown bases. The small leaves are linear-lanceolate and may differ in size or sit far apart; the field lists them as attached down the stem, spreading, recurved or pressed close to it. Leaves that bear sporangia differ from the ordinary foliage.

Each sporangium, the spore case, has two similar valves and is kidney-shaped; the spores are described as pitted. These are the characters in a regional morphology field, not a range-wide sample of forms.
