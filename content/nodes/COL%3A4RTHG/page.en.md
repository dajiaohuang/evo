## *Restio miser*: button-like spikelets at wet seeps {#s-387e8a24db}
This tufted perennial reaches 50 cm and has slender, unbranched stems. Its several spikelets are described as button-like and arranged along a simple axis. Male and female flowers occur on separate plants.

The flora records it at seeps that remain wet throughout the year, above 800 m.
