## Name and classification in the source {#s-9ad2b6d447}
Amphibian morphology version 3 indexes *Capensibufo selenophos* under genus *Capensibufo*, family Bufonidae, order Anura. This is the source table’s own name and placement; it does not by itself establish equivalence with the current Catalogue of Life 26.8 (COL26.8) species concept.

## Measurements in the deposit {#s-c0c2faaf91}
The source table contains 1 measurement row; 1 trait column has at least one recorded value. Its Sex field uses M (male). Absolute measurement units are not confirmed, so the numeric cells cannot be interpreted as absolute dimensions. These rows are not necessarily unique specimens, and missing cells do not show that a structure is absent.
