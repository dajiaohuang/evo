## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#s-c823f992b7}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Peucaea aestivalis (M. H. K. Lichtenstein, 1823) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Separate total birds from complete sets {#s-b49e517e7f}

The original book, weighed individuals, localities and dates were not read, leaving sex, seasonal and regional mass ranges unsupported. Mass and beak length do not establish feeding mechanics.

## Read food niche and trophic level separately {#s-f66969ce9e}
Trophic.Niche=Granivore is defined as at least 60% of resources from seeds or nuts; Trophic.Level=Herbivore as at least 70% of resources from plants. These separate fields and definitions are not measured diet proportions for this species; Omnivore has different definitions for niche and level. Retain the recorded combination without inventing a cause or changing one field to agree with the other before reading underlying diet sources. Labels do not supply particular seeds, prey, seasonal diet, foraging strata, handling actions or beak mechanics.

## Diet does not replace primary lifestyle {#s-d87df28653}
Primary.Lifestyle=Terrestrial means most time on the ground, obtaining food while walking or hopping in the source definition. Habitat=Forest; density code 2 denotes semi-open vegetation, not bird abundance. Migration=2 is partially migratory, which can include a minority moving long distances, most birds making short-distance movements, nomadism or distinct altitudinal migration; the row does not specify which applies. A shared food niche does not establish shared foraging strata, social activity or seasonal movements. Historical map fields are uninterpreted here and do not supply current range-wide distributions or movement routes.
