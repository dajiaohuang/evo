## Leaves, flower, and fruit {#s-8a8580574d}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte about 70 mm tall, occasionally growing in clumps. Its five to fifteen leaves are dry at flowering, broadly lance-shaped, and lie flat; their tips sometimes turn upward. The leaves range from limp to somewhat succulent, are green, and are either hairless or sparsely hairy underneath. Their margins have minute papillae. The flower is salver-shaped and white to delicate pink. The succulent fruit is aromatic, yellow, and red-tipped.
