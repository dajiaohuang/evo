## Name and AVONET sample {#s-1bd3629d1d}
COL26.8 accepts Hapaloptila castanea within Bucconidae. AVONET v7 associates its data with the HBW–BirdLife 2020 name. Four individuals were measured and all 4 had a complete morphological set; usable samples may still vary by trait.

## Beak dimensions {#s-0448921673}
Mean beak length is 28.9 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 12.3 mm and depth 11.7 mm at that edge. These are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-4befe37a3b}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 109 mm; mean tarsus length is 19.8 mm. The compiled mean mass is 80.6 g, sourced to Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information, not necessarily the measured specimens.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-c03f10eff9}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, an Invertivore trophic niche and an Insessorial primary lifestyle. These are broad comparison labels, not a complete habitat or diet description.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-5c2ed2b598}
The migration category is sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from latitude −9.87° to 6.82°. These endpoints do not describe non-breeding movement, a current location or occupancy across the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## Where the record places feeding {#s-6f1dd0287e}
Species-level foraging weights are 60% in trees or tall bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy, 20% on the ground and 20% in understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m. They describe relative feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or time budgets.
