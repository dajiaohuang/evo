## Bombus terrestris {#s-46877e0fb5}
## Winter sightings at Kew

At the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, London, researchers made 27 walks along a fixed survey route, or transect, from 1 October 2007 to 28 March 2008. *B. terrestris* queens and workers were seen throughout the winter. Worker sightings rose in early December, peaked in January, and declined in late February or early March; males were recorded only through early November. These observations show winter activity at this site during that season, but do not establish successful winter reproduction or a second generation.
