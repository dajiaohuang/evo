## How does Bacopa axillaris grow and flower? {#s-e8bbe96c17}
A Flora of Panama description presents Bacopa axillaris as an erect herb with stout, soft stems and fibrous roots. Its opposite leaves can reach 5 cm long and 1 cm wide, with blunt teeth along the upper edge. Three to six small white flowers cluster in each leaf axil; the tubular corolla is about 3 mm long and has four stamens. The narrow, cone-shaped capsule is about 3 mm long, with tiny seeds about 0.5 mm long.

## Where does the regional account report it? {#s-1d6f75fdf1}
The Flora of Panama account places it in swamps and other wet lowland sites in central Panama and also reports it from Colombia and Guatemala. This is the cited flora’s regional account, not a current global inventory.
