## Cybianthus lepidotus (Gleason) G.Agostini {#s-597496011d}
## Hairy stems and an incompletely known inflorescence

Cybianthus lepidotus has hairy, cylindrical terminal branches 1–3 mm in diameter. Its elliptic leaves are papery to leathery, with acuminate tips, acute bases and a hairy midrib; the petioles are smooth. The source does not identify the inflorescence type or position. Both staminate and pistillate flowers are stalked; the corolla may have four or five parts, and its lobes are rounded. The anthers are attached on their backs. The immature fruit wall is globose and dotted.
