## Atlantic Forest localities between two rivers {#s-0d20eb3107}
The description reported localities between Brazil’s Doce and Mucuri rivers, including forest reserves and cocoa plantations in Espírito Santo. It reassigned some northern populations previously identified as C. lacrimae or C. capixaba to the new species. Those reassignments concern the populations discussed in the study, without transferring all records under the older names.

## Pitfall captures after heavy rain {#s-5175247b9a}
At Floresta Nacional do Rio Preto, frogs were captured in pitfall traps after heavy rain. Trap lines were near a permanent lagoon and temporary swamp in a landscape of secondary forest, plantations and a few primary Atlantic Forest remnants. This is a trapping context, without direct evidence of egg deposition in either water body or a quantified dependence on heavy rain.
