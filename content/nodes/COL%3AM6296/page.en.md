## Accepted name and placement {#s-6f0151e509}
COL26.8 accepts Blommersia nataliae Vieites, Nieto-Roman, Fernández & Santos-Santos, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Mantellidae (family) → Blommersia (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Eggs above water in cut bamboo {#s-9eb102a22c}
At Mont M’Sapere in November 2012, observers counted three clutches containing 42, 43 and 22 eggs. The eggs were placed on inner bamboo walls above water, and only some appeared fertilized. Eggs and tadpoles were observed during wet periods in November–December 2012 and April 2014. The suggestion that unfertilized eggs provision tadpoles remains a hypothesis for further study. Not hearing calls during these observations does not establish that the species never calls.

## Two surveyed Mayotte mountains {#s-cd91eaf0b9}
The species description records Blommersia nataliae in forest and bamboo at Mont M’Sapere, 235–409 m above sea level, and at one 317 m site on Mont Bénara, Mayotte. These are dated survey records from 2012, 2014 and 2019; unsuccessful searches elsewhere do not establish absence from every other locality.
