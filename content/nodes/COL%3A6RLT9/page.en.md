## ## Velvety stems and three-part flowers {#s-364955460d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records tomentose or velvety stems with incurved or recurved simple hairs and capitate glandular hairs. Setae and short shoots are absent; prickles are listed as absent or spreading below the nodes. The stipules persist, extrafloral nectaries and projections between pinna pairs are absent, and the inflorescences are globose with bracts longer than the flowers before opening. The flowers have three perianth parts, a membranous calyx with triangular lobes and corolla lobes with one to three veins that may be hairless or pilose. The androecium is listed as isostemonous or diplostemonous, with pink filaments. The habitat field says “terrestrial”; the source does not quantify these alternative states.
