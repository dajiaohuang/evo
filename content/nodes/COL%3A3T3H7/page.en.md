## Three habitat categories in the flora record {#s-d3e79790c2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes this species as *Corticícola* (growing on bark).

This source label identifies a substrate category, not a collection locality; it gives no survey date, range, or measure of sampling completeness.

It also lists *Rupícola*, meaning that it grows on rock.

This second label is not a mapped collection site and supplies no survey date, range, or sampling-completeness measure.

A third recorded category is *Terrícola*, or growing on soil.

Like the other labels, this is not a collection locality and does not indicate the geographic range or completeness of sampling.

## What the record leaves open {#s-8ec7d56540}
No separate morphology description is provided in this dossier.

The habitat categories are not evidence about life cycle or reproduction; these were not assessed.

Interactions, habitat variation, locality, and sampling coverage have not been assessed here.

The regional dataset does not establish a complete distribution.
