## *Limadendron hostmannii* {#s-2a40f69bbb}
The recorded petiole is cylindrical and leaflet venation weakly reticulate. Inflorescence length is given as 4.2–8 with no unit specified. Fruits exceed 10 cm, are oblong to oblanceolate, have conspicuous venation, and contain 4–5(–7) seeds; these are source-field values.
