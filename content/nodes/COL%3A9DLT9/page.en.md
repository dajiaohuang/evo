## Schnella maximiliani {#s-0e3caf7622}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Schnella maximiliani* as an accepted species of *Schnella* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description contrasts persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) and entire leaves with species whose leaf blades are divided. Its calyx (the outer layer formed by sepals) is cup-shaped when the flower opens, and the outer petal surface has rough, appressed hairs. The fruit is indehiscent (not splitting open at maturity); the record labels the plant terrestrial.
