## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-79c732b539}
COL26.8 records Tetracheilostoma at genus rank, with Epictinae as its direct parent.

The authored ReptTraits v1-2 set has four cited fields for Tetracheilostoma breuili and T. carlae: maximum SVL, maximum TL, microhabitat and reproductive mode. The records use The Reptile Database’s 2023 names, while navigation follows pinned COL26.8. The name matches do not establish full equivalence between historical and current taxon concepts.

## Compare the two lengths separately {#s-524e06480e}
The reported maximum SVL is 99.9 mm for T. breuili, attributed to Feldman and Meiri (2013), and 93.7 mm for T. carlae, attributed to Hedges (2008). Their corresponding maximum total lengths are 113 mm and 104 mm, respectively; TL includes the tail, so it is not interchangeable with SVL. These are compiled species records, not a range of individual sizes.

## What the two-record view leaves out {#s-4a8205c688}
The two authored records do not form a full descendant list or a population-level size survey. Their maxima are compiled records, not means or identification thresholds. Check the source attached to each field, and do not treat the workbook’s 2023 name framework as proof of taxonomic concept equivalence.
