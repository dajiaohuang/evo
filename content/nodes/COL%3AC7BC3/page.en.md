## What is known about Guatteria stenophylla? {#s-02121d3484}
The field describes narrow, egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and sparsely hairy below. Solitary flowers grow in the leaf axils; their stalks are densely hairy. The free sepals are broad and triangular, and the petals are elliptic to egg-shaped. Fruit and seed form are not specified in this record.
