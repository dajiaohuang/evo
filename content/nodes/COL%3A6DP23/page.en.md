## What marks Duguetia moricandiana? {#s-bb10a94089}
The source describes elliptic leaves with smooth upper surfaces and dense scale-like hairs underneath. Yellow or greenish flowers form small clusters; the stamens are red. The rounded fruit lacks a basal collar and bears star-shaped hairs. Its fruit color is not specified in the record.
