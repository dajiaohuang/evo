## Response under standardized loading {#s-74078232f2}
AMR 106933 is subadult. Its cranial model exhibits high strain in shake and pull loading. The study discusses proportions of bone between the orbits, but this skull does not establish adult Komodo monitor bite force or a maximum prey-processing capacity.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-216ed5c85a}
Recorded dimensions are reported maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 1570 mm; female maximum snout–vent length (SVL) 1083.7 mm. SVL runs from snout to vent and differs from total length including the tail. Compiled maxima are reported material, not theoretical growth limits or typical individual size. Sex-specific values may come from different localities or studies and alone cannot quantify population sexual dimorphism.

## Limits of these trait data {#s-870b44a138}
This account paraphrases the same-name ReptTraits v1-2 entry, retaining its 2023 taxonomic framework and sources for individual traits. One row per species compresses variation in sex, age, locality and populations; matching name and family do not ensure equivalent later concepts. Reported maximum length is not an absolute species limit, and reproductive means are not fixed counts in every clutch. Many mass entries are estimated from length equations and are not presented here as measured mass. Juvenile-length headers and definitions differ and are also omitted from reader conclusions. Conservation, venom, current distribution and population trends are not updated here; unrecorded fields remain gaps.
