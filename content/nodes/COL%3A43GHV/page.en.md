## Mimosa maracayuensis {#s-8a3c905416}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa maracayuensis* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists the stem as hairless or short-haired and the flower cluster as globose. Flowers have four or five perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts), 1–3-veined petal lobes, pink stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers), and two rings of stamens; it also labels the plant terrestrial. These are the states recorded in a regional flora entry.
