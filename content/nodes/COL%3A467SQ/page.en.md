## What does the description record about this annual? {#s-6f132c0b2c}
The historical description presents Nemesia platysepala as a glaucous, upright annual only 4–7 inches tall. Its egg-shaped leaves have toothed edges, and a few flowers sit in terminal clusters. The broad, cone-shaped floral spur is especially prominent, measuring about 1/4–2/7 inch long and 1/5–1/4 inch wide.

## Where is it placed? {#s-d18d1bfa8c}
The source gives only the broad setting “among hills,” without a named site.
