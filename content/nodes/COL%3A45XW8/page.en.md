## Relative weights of food categories {#s-bc8e0604f0}
The recorded nonzero food weight is invertebrates 100%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. ABC cannot be rewritten as direct observations of this species. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Mammalian foraging-stratum assignment {#s-9cf5a1df25}
EltonTraits assigns the entry to G: the ground category, whose source definition also includes some aquatic foraging. Code A records compiler confidence in the source and assignment. Metadata collectively attributes mammalian stratum assignments to Nowak (1999). This is a category, not a percentage of time among strata, and does not mean the species acts only there.

## Activity periods can occur together {#s-247651f8da}
The EltonTraits activity field marks daytime feeding as 1. Several periods can be marked 1 together; they are not proportions adding to a daily time budget or schedules shared by every day, season and site. Activity certainty is ABC and can still draw on ecologically similar congeneric material; genus/family typical activity values do not fill gaps here.

## Limits of this ecological record {#s-2ba912e8ab}
This is a reading guide to historical same-name, same-family foraging records in EltonTraits 1.0, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical species concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized by the authors from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes can also refer to a congener judged ecologically similar by the compilers; this page does not claim direct observations of this species for every field. Genus/family typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing individual review do not fill definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been individually reread. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation still require other sources.
