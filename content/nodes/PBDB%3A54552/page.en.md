## Recorded parent of Dromaeosaurinae {#s-d79ac79315}
Dromaeosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Eudromaeosauria as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
