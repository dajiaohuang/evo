## Leaf, inflorescence and flower structures in the entry {#s-cc0e61bb24}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records a trunk that is smooth to fissured. Leaves are simple and alternate, elliptic, glabrous, cuneate at the base, acuminate at the tip and entire at the margin. Stipules are falcate, glabrous and persistent; petioles are glabrous. Terminal fascicled racemes are curved and have more than 20 flowers. Subulate bracts and bracteoles are hairy at the tip; bracteoles are inserted on the middle half of the pedicel. Pedicels are brown and puberulous. Floral buds are asymmetric and elliptic, with acute tips and bases and puberulous surfaces. Flowers are zygomorphic and lack a hypanthium. The calyx is spathaceous and unilobed, chartaceous to membranous, glabrous inside at the base and pilose at the apex. There are five petals and 10 stamens; filaments are glabrous and anthers sparsely pilose. The gynoecium is tomentose; the ovary is stipitate and tomentose, with eight ovules, and the style is straight and glabrous. The fruit is a globose drupe; seeds are oblong, one to three. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
