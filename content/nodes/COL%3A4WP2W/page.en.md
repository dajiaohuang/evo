## Senegalia pedicellata {#s-ef554273aa}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senegalia pedicellata* as an accepted species of *Senegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 description records only 2–4 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), each with 3–7 pairs of leaflets. Its flowers are stalked and grouped in rounded heads; the seeds are recorded as round. The habitat field lists the plant as terrestrial.
