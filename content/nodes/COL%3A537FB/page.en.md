## Hair at the ovary tip and style base {#s-c3a3ae016d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records no tendrils and spines present. Petioles are about 0.5 mm long; leaves have three to five veins, and the underside axils of the basal veins may be bearded or form membranous pockets. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are corymbiform. The corolla is rotate or short-funnel-shaped, with lobes longer than the tube; the ovary is hairy at its tip and the style at its base. Fruits are 1–2 cm in diameter, with a leathery fruit wall no thicker than 0.5 mm. Seeds are usually one or two, with a fibrous coat.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-22f1af6f53}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field records *Strychnos tarapotensis* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
