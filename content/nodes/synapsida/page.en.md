## The *Echinerpeton* evidence {#s-a1cab34b18}
The synapsid route is anchored by *Echinerpeton* material from the Sydney Mines Formation in Nova Scotia, dated to 308.5–305.5 Ma. A morphological matrix assigns the referred neural spine to Ophiacodontidae. The sample anchors a route to living mammals; it is not the origin of Synapsida or a global first appearance.

The cited material includes a neural spine compared with the type series and tied to the Sydney Mines locality. Its ophiacodontid placement is an analytical result, not something established by the spine alone. The formation-level interval is 308.5–305.5 million years old.

## Reading the route {#s-9a9002ead5}
The atlas displays 308.5–0 Ma, extending from the sampled early synapsid occurrence to living mammals. The older edge follows the formation interval and does not date synapsid origination or identify a global first fossil occurrence.
