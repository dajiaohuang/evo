## Membranous leaves and pink flowers {#s-f55c8a36d7}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 record, the leaves are membranous and not perfoliate; their bases lack auricles, and the secondary veins are inconspicuous. Flower pedicels are also described as inconspicuous. The calyx is longer than the corolla tube, and its lobes are the same length as the calyx tube. The corolla is pink.

## Terrestrial habitat category {#s-e7fe95d0f7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels *Deianira damazioi* terrestrial but gives no collection locality or more specific environment.
