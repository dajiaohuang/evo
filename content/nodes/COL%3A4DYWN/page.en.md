## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Passiflora sclerophylla? {#s-978fc2d5f3}
The woody, cylindrical-to-nearly-cylindrical stem bears entire leaves with glandular, smooth margins and fine short hairs. Sessile glands occur on the petiole; stipules and bracts are unknown. Flowers may be solitary or paired. Sepals lack awns, and the floral cup is cylindrical or bell-shaped. The corona may have two or several rows; the operculum may be fringed or club-tipped. The limen is absent, and the reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. Fruit and seed traits are unknown.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-19cd89b8d5}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
