## How is Hebenstretia fastigiosa described? {#s-86c29e8f6f}
The source describes an annual herb up to 45 cm tall, with branches rising at an angle. Narrow leaves have small teeth. White flowers form long spikes; the calyx is hairless and the anthers lack stalks. The fruit is top-shaped and pointed, with the upper of its two segments larger.
