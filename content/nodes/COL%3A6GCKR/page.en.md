## Eremanthus crotonoides {#s-54faf76f19}
## Grey woolly stems bear clusters of tiny heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes grey tomentose stems. Petiolate leaves range from ovate to oblong, green to dark green, and membranous to subleathery.

Heads occur singly or in compact glomerules, which may contain 6–10 or more heads. Cylindrical involucres have brown-tipped bracts. Each head has three or four florets with hairs at the corolla tips. The pubescent dry fruits have a two-row pappus; the source records it as either persistent or deciduous. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
