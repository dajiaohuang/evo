## What does the type strain look like? {#s-41397a2867}
The published description centers on cultured type strain GH36T, the named reference strain in the study. Its cells were motile and irregularly granule-shaped, 1.0–4.0 micrometres (μm) across; the paper classifies them as Gram-negative by staining. Colonies were red, raised and round, about 0.5 mm wide. These observations are limited to GH36T under the reported conditions and do not establish variation across the species.

## Where was GH36T collected? {#s-5077285636}
The type strain was isolated from a salt lake in China. This single reported source does not establish the species' wider distribution, abundance or ecological role.

## How did the authors name the species? {#s-28d2da73fb}
Genome-based comparisons and taxonomic characterization led the authors to propose Haloarcula montana for strain GH36T. That proposal is the authors' classification of the strain and does not establish variation across populations.
