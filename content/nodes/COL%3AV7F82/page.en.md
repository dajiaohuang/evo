## What does the species description cover? {#s-892a2faf81}
The 2025 species treatment describes the shell and soft-part anatomy of *Radomaniola mislinensis* and provides a diagnosis. This dossier records the scope of that account but does not extract a full character matrix; measurements, sex and developmental-stage coverage, and variation among specimens have not been independently summarized. The observations concern the treatment specimens, not a complete account of variation across the species.

## Two reported springs bound the locality record {#s-7c02ffb5a2}
The treatment gives Mislina spring, in the Neretva River Delta near Metković, Croatia (42°58′56″N, 17°36′24″E; 4 m), as the type locality; holotype NHMW-MO-113906 was collected on 2020-08-04. It also reports spring Studena (locality 13). The authors describe the known area as very small and around the spring that supplies Mislina lake. This account covers only named collection localities and does not identify unsampled sites or establish a complete range.

## What the mitochondrial evidence can resolve {#s-356f35cd1f}
The 2025 study assigns the taxon to mitochondrial operational unit mOTU B and reports COI accessions PX113142–PX113146 for the sampled wild snails. The authors caution that COI data do not resolve deeper phylogenetic relationships; mOTU B is not a species-tree placement.

## The authors’ Endangered assessment {#s-81d6cad484}
The authors of the 2025 treatment assess the species as Endangered (EN), within the locality scope reported in that account. This is their assessment only, not a verified current IUCN or national listing; assessment criteria, date validity, and population trend were not independently checked.
