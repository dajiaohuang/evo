## Steropodon: a tooth-bearing jaw and monotreme affinity {#s-43a2bf9344}
The evidence for Steropodon begins with one fossil: right dentary fragment AM F66763 from the Early Cretaceous Griman Creek Formation, bearing three tribosphenic molars. Researchers compare those teeth with monotreme and other mammalian molar patterns to test the proposed Mesozoic monotreme affinity. The jaw and teeth are observed; their homology and phylogenetic meaning are inferred. One jaw cannot show a complete body plan, prove direct ancestry, or set a universal first appearance for monotremes.
