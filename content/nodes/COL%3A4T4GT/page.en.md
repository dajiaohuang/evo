## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-e149f0d605}
The opposite elliptic leaves have tapering tips, nearly entire margins, symmetrical bases and no domatia. The upper surface is hairless; the lower surface bears appressed golden fine hairs. The inflorescence is a thyrse. Petals have golden bristly hairs; the filaments are fused, the connective is hairless and the ovary has golden woolly hairs. Each carpel has one ovule. The fruit is asymmetric and under 1.2 cm long, with one seed per valve. The seed is globose and hairless.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-6ec49cc40b}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
