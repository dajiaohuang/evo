AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3535 records Boophis miadana under the source classification Boophis (Mantellidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 27 mm for this row. The source field `Body_size_mm` denotes maximum adult body size in millimetres; in Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
