## Varronia truncata {#s-823c584e85}
## Funnel-shaped flowers rise between the sessile leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stems and flower clusters with short, stiff, appressed hairs. The ovate to inversely ovate leaves are wedge-shaped at the base, acute to blunt at the tip, and attached directly to the stem without leaf stalks. The flower heads grow between stem joints, the points where leaves attach.

Each flower has a bell-shaped calyx with long-pointed lobes and a funnel-shaped corolla under 1 cm long. The ovoid fruit is hairless. The habitat field records the plant as terrestrial.
