## *Chromolaena parviceps*: stalked heads and papillary corolla lobes {#s-47e4162e73}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, lance-shaped or egg-shaped leaves with three main veins and toothed margins. Both surfaces are hairy, with the lower surface sometimes gland-dotted. Stalked heads form corymb-like or panicle-like clusters inside cylindrical bract cups. The bracts lie close against the cup and are hairless; the corolla lobes are papillary. The inversely cone-shaped fruitlets are hairless and carry rough pappus bristles. These are the field’s listed states.
