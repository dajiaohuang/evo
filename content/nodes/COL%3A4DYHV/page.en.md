## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora imbeana? {#s-05f016665c}
The herb has cylindrical stems and hairless, three-lobed leaves with smooth margins. Stalked glands occur on the petioles; stipules are kidney-shaped. Solitary flowers have ovate bracts, awned sepals and a bell-shaped floral cup. The corona has several rows, the operculum is membranous at the tip, and the limen is cup-shaped. The reproductive stalk is at most 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless; oval seeds have reticulate surfaces.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-3c266f0e71}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
