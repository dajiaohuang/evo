## Helicolocellus and the hypothesis of an organic sponge skeleton {#s-ccc826ffed}
The holotype NIGP-176531 and referred fossils preserve a stemmed, goblet-shaped body more than 0.4 metres high, with at least three nested orders of quadrate grids. A morphology matrix places Helicolocellus within crown Porifera, near Hexactinellida, supporting a testable hypothesis of an organic skeleton that did not require mineralized spicules. Grid homology and phylogenetic placement depend on character coding and sampled taxa; these fossils do not directly date every split within the sponge crown group.
