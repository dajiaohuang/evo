## Elliptic leaves and overlapping bracts {#s-f79c7d8fd3}
The Brazilian Flora description records elliptic leaf blades and a pulvinus—the joint at the base of the leaf blade—as either hairless or hairy. The flower bracts overlap, and the corolla tube is straight. It also describes a densely silky-haired ovary and an ellipsoid fruit with erect sepals that remain attached.
