## What this evidence can establish {#s-815bd5bd33}
The fossil preserves the branch and its body-wall attachment. Gill function, haemolymph flow and homology with other limb branches are interpretations from morphology and imaging; gas exchange was not measured, and the comparison does not establish ancestry.

## A branch examined in images {#s-cb4e35777e}
The upper limb branch is incompletely preserved. Its attachment to the body wall can be compared with lamellate respiratory branches.

The filament-bearing branch was included in comparisons using environmental scanning electron microscopy (ESEM) and micro-CT.

## A marine deposit is not a full life record {#s-a00dc9e6a1}
The appendage comes from a marine Burgess Shale setting. The cited limb study does not resolve diet, locomotor performance or body size; its respiratory interpretation comes from morphology and imaging rather than a measurement of gas exchange.
