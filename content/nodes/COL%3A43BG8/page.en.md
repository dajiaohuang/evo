## Mikania ochroleuca {#s-15efd60ddc}
## Arrow-shaped leaf bases and a hollow stem

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, hairy branches with hollow pith. Opposite leaves are triangular and thin, with toothed margins, arrow- to heart-shaped bases and acute tips. Both surfaces are smooth, and three main veins radiate from the base.

The branched panicles include spike-like side branches. Their heads are recorded from stalkless to shortly stalked, and the bracts just below each head match the surrounding bracts in form. Those bracts are oblong to lance-shaped and smooth. The tubular corolla and dry fruits (cypselae) are also recorded as smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial; this regional record gives no locality or complete range.
