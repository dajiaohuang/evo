COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis yonke Chávez, Aznaran, Wong, Victoriano-Cigüeñas, García Ayachi, Valencia-Málaga, Ormeño et al., 2025 as a species. ## Diagnosis and sampled close relatives {#s-712763f164}
Pristimantis yonke was described among three terrestrial-breeding frogs from Peru's high Cordillera de Huancabamba. The diagnosis notes its small size, acuminate snout, oval palmar tubercle and lack of vomerine dentigerous processes. In the sampled phylogeny it falls near P. morlaco and P. cf. cajamarcensis; the reported pairwise 16S distances to those closest samples are 5.4–6.2%.
