## Four-part flowers and a warty fruit {#s-500126bcbe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes papery elliptic or lance-shaped leaves, with wedge-shaped bases, tips that taper to a point, and flat margins without a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib is grooved and one vein runs near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters; the supporting bracts fall away, while the small, inconspicuous bracteoles remain. The smooth, hairless flower cup has an open calyx in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid and warty; its bracteoles remain. One seed is recorded, but the embryo is unknown. These are the fields supplied for this species; the separate habitat field only says “terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## A terrestrial label, without a locality {#s-1b66b18559}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no collection locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. It is a habitat category, not a full ecological account.
