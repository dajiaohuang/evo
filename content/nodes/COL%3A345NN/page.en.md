## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-02662e653b}
The branches grow monopodially, with one continuing main axis. Alternate leaves are obovate or oblanceolate, with tapered bases and tips that may be pointed, blunt or rounded. Simple hairs occur on the blades; the upper surface is hairless, while the lower is sparsely hairy or hairless. Axillary male-flower stalks may reach 8 cm. Petal-like scales are absent; staminodes may be absent or number four or eight. The fruit is recorded as white.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-3ab759fedb}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
