## Two leaf forms and the fruit {#s-d6460bbd33}
The source records submerged leaves that are linear to linear-lanceolate, acute at the tip, cuneate at the base, and without petioles. Floating leaves are also present: they range from elliptic and oblong to lanceolate, with acute or obtuse tips and cuneate or truncate bases; their petioles are present. The fruit is elliptic and has a dorsal keel.
