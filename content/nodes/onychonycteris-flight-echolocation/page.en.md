## Onychonycteris: flight and hearing clues in one skeleton {#s-68a28f45b2}
The nearly complete Onychonycteris finneyi holotype ROM 55351A preserves elongated wings and a relatively small cochlea. Wing proportions are compatible with powered flight, while cochlear size was interpreted as evidence against specialized laryngeal echolocation. Both are functional inferences from anatomy, not observed behavior; one fossil also cannot stand in for every early bat or a direct ancestor.
