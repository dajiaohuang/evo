## Accepted name and placement {#s-5f88a66a35}
COL26.8 records Diasporus tinker (Lynch, 2001) at species rank in Diasporus, within Eleutherodactylinae, order Eleutherodactylidae. This is the placement in the release dated 20 August 2026, separate from the historical source classification.

## Reading route {#s-7d7bbeb655}
The catalogue placement and the AmphiBIO historical classification are distinct. The values below belong to the Frost 2011 source concept.

## Foraging strata — AmphiBIO source {#s-92aba0f7c1}
The AmphiBIO row codes terrestrial foraging. It is a broad historical category, not a detailed habitat-use study. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports correcting some inherited amphibian microhabitat and body-mass entries; this specific row has not been checked, and equivalence to the current species concept remains unknown.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-68ec7e79f1}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 21.8 mm for its historical anuran row, meaning snout–vent length for a frog. It is a compiled maximum, not an individual range.

## Compiled life history — AmphiBIO source {#s-15d30b467c}
The row records at most one reproductive event per year. This count is drawn from literature and is not specified by sex, population, date or wild/captive conditions; offspring-size fields may refer to eggs.
