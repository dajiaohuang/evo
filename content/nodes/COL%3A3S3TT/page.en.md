## Recurved leaf tips on an upright or spreading shrublet {#s-710f0cddad}
The shrublet may be decumbent or erect and reaches 35 cm. Its leaves are subcylindrical to three-sided, taper to a point, and have recurved tips; they are 15–25 mm long. One to three magenta flowers reach 35 mm across on short stalks up to 10 mm long. The fruit is about 8 mm across, and shale soils are recorded.
