## Pendulous flower clusters and changing stem colours {#s-13fc30e7b2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records round, hairless stems with striated or exfoliating surfaces. Petiolate leaves are elliptic, oblong-elliptic, or broadly elliptic, with acute to acuminate tips and flat margins; their colour is listed as reddish or greenish. The terminal inflorescence is pendulous, short, and described as spike-like or paniculate, without bracts. Flowers are pedicellate and white. The fruit is recorded as globose or ellipsoid. These are the alternatives given in one regional data field.

## Terrestrial habitat label {#s-a19959fc27}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field classifies *Stachyarrhena harleyi* as terrestrial but gives no collection locality.
