## What stem, leaf and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-5c01c64d9a}
The erect stems bear hairs that may be star-shaped, short-stalked or gland-tipped; the stipules are papery. Alternate leaves spiral around the stem, are papery to membranous and differ in color between their two surfaces. Their bases are heart-shaped to nearly so, their tips taper, and the upper surface is hairy. Long-stalked inflorescences grow in the leaf axils along the branches. Flowers are arranged in two ranks and have narrow lance-shaped or linear bracteoles. The stigmas are long and feathery. The fruit has a flat, cut-off tip, a papery upper third and splits between its chambers.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-9f5fb2d161}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
