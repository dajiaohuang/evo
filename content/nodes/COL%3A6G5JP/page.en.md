## Peters’s original account {#account}
In Peters’s original account, the features used to distinguish *Epigonichthys* from *Branchiostoma* include a high, ray-bearing dorsal fin and a median anal opening. Peters describes the anal and caudal fins as absent and the ventral fin as rudimentary, represented by low rays (pp. 325–326). He reports ten specimens, 13–23 mm long, from Peale Island in Moreton Bay, northeastern Australia (p. 326). The capture-depth statement is omitted because the page prints the internally contradictory wording “15 Kilometern (8 Faden)”; neither value is selected, corrected, or standardized. These statements concern Peters’s described collection and account, not a modern species-wide diagnosis or distribution. The source names *Epigonichthys cultellus* n. sp.; whether Peters’s original circumscription maps to the current COL 6G5JP concept remains unknown.
