## Stenopterygius in the PBDB classification {#s-d1f000895e}
PBDB records Stenopterygius at genus rank, and this guide links nine selected fossil-species pages. The recorded parent path organizes database entries; it is not a phylogenetic hypothesis or a complete genus inventory.

## Reading selected species occurrences {#s-a7f04ecf24}
Five starting pages are Stenopterygius megacephalus, Stenopterygius cuneiceps, Stenopterygius megalorhinus, Stenopterygius quadriscissus and Stenopterygius triscissus. Across the selected species pages, the guide covers 23 occurrence records from 13 collections; recorded stratum names include Posidonia Shale, Beacon Limstone and Opalinuston. Compare each identification, collection, stratum and age with its source. These are database record counts, not animal or population counts.

## Limits of the occurrence records {#s-a4df40886a}
These are selected PBDB occurrence records, not a complete catalogue of the genus or a shared ecological summary. Counts describe database occurrences and collections; they do not measure specimens, animals, populations or sampling effort. The guide’s age bounds do not establish the genus’s actual origin, extinction or full duration. The PBDB parent chain is a classification, not a phylogenetic tree.
