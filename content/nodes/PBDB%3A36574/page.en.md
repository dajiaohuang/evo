## PBDB classification and selected species pages {#s-d1f000895e}
PBDB records Stenopterygius at genus rank, and this guide links nine selected fossil species pages. The recorded parent chain is a database classification; it does not establish phylogeny or complete genus membership.

## Occurrences, collections and recorded strata {#s-a7f04ecf24}
Five linked pages—Stenopterygius megacephalus, S. cuneiceps, S. megalorhinus, S. quadriscissus and S. triscissus—offer a starting set for comparing the records and cited literature. Across the selected species guide, the data include 23 occurrences from 13 collections; Posidonia Shale, Beacon Limstone and Opalinuston appear as example stratum names. Compare each occurrence’s identification and age fields with its source. These counts describe database entries, not animals or populations.

## Limits of the occurrence records {#s-a4df40886a}
These are selected PBDB occurrence records, not a complete catalogue of the genus or a shared ecological summary. Counts describe database occurrences and collections; they do not measure specimens, animals, populations or sampling effort. The guide’s age bounds do not establish the genus’s actual origin, extinction or full duration. The PBDB parent chain is a classification, not a phylogenetic tree.
