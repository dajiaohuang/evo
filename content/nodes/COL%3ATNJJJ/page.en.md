## Head proportions, toe order and lateral nodules {#s-ad612de4d9}
The comparison described lizhenchangi head width as greater than or approximately equal to head length, toe order III > IV > II > V > I, and body-side nodules as separated and distinct. These were contrasted with the Huangyan population’s longer head, different last two toe ranks and indistinct lateral warts. “Subequal” supplies no numerical tolerance, and no specimen allocation is stated in this paragraph. These source-scoped morphological comparisons do not supply a population distribution of ratios, invariant identification rule, habitat preference or developmental sequence.

## A dorsal external-head size comparison has a limited meaning {#s-55fa5ddc66}
In the reported dorsal external-head analysis, Pleurodeles had larger centroid size than lizhenchangi. The result describes a landmark configuration and one view, not a whole-body length or mass comparison. It does not establish a species-wide upper size limit or show that every lizhenchangi is smaller than every ribbed newt. The study supplies comparative cranial evidence without a new ecological or reproductive observation for this species.
