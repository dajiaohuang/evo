## *Phylica fourcadei* {#s-8bd2f26b1d}
Compared with *Phylica gnidioides*, the account records shorter leaves, 5–7 mm long, that are scarcely compressed, and a longer style of about 8 mm.

A second source statement repeats the distinction as smaller, scarcely compressed leaves and a much longer style. The account frames these as comparative characters.

The source lists sandstone slopes as habitat and gives no locality or survey date.
