## *Baccharis itapirocensis*: single axillary flower heads {#s-b6a989e5cc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records a single flower head in a leaf axil. Its leaves have one or three main veins, a developed stalk, and blades listed as inversely lance-shaped, inversely egg-shaped, or elliptic; the margin may be entire or toothed. The field describes heads as either all tubular male-flowered or all threadlike female-flowered. These are alternatives in the dataset field, which supplies no substrate category or collection locality.
