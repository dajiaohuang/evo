## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-2fbf3e6f8a}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis ocellatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-c3e6adce0b}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Mirtschin et al. 2017.
