## What leaf, flower and fruit traits identify Verrucularina piresii? {#s-f347001912}
The leaves are elliptic and somewhat leathery, with stalks lacking glands. The inflorescence is a raceme. Yellow petals are associated with ten elaiophores, oil-producing floral glands. Sepals are flat, and stamens differ in length; their filaments and anthers are hairless. The ovary may be hairless or silky-hairy. The fruit is a hairless three-lobed schizocarp, which separates into three seed-bearing sections.
