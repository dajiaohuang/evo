## Blue flowers with yellow extending onto the tepals {#s-1679e35cb6}
Romulea tabularis has lavender-blue, occasionally white, flowers with a yellow cup that often extends onto the lower half of the tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals). One or two basal leaves are typical, though leaf number and flower colour vary within the account.

## Moist sandy or limestone flats {#s-7af9c91c2c}
The flora records moist sandy or limestone flats as habitat.
