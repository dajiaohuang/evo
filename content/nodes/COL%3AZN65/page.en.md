## Leaves and flower head {#s-e1378decbb}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte up to 45 cm tall. Its four to six leaves lie flat and are dry at flowering; they are broadly strap-shaped, with brownish-red speckling beneath and bristly margins. About 200 small, radially symmetrical flowers form a rounded head. They range from maroon to dusky pink, and the tepals turn back.

Bulbous geophyte to 45 cm. Leaves 4-6, dry at flowering, prostrate, broadly strap-shaped, speckled brownish red beneath, margins bristly. Flowers ± 200, in a round head, small, actinomorphic, maroon to dusky pink, tepals reflexed.
