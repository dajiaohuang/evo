## Aristea rufobracteata {#s-a819f37753}
Aristea rufobracteata is a tall, clumping perennial with a dense flowering axis and many short-branched flower clusters. Its rusty-brown spathes and bracts are thin and somewhat translucent at the edges, distinguishing it in the regional account from the broadly similar A. capitata. The blue flowers are recorded on stony sandstone slopes, especially commonly after fire. The account gives these as regional observations and does not quantify post-fire abundance.
