## Hairy lance-shaped leaves and radiate heads {#s-56d5dfb06a}
The morphology record describes a woody plant with hairy, stalked leaves arranged in opposite pairs at right angles. The leaves range from lance-shaped to oblong-lanceolate and are papery. Radiate heads occur singly or in corymbs; the yellow ray florets are sterile and the central florets bisexual and fertile. The fruit has an obovate attachment base and lacks an awn-like pappus.
