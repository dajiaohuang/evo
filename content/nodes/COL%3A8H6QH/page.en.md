## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-d5c775218a}
COL26.8 records Chelus orinocensis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chelidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-26ba97e90c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Sanchez-Vilag et al. 1995.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-60ab078a76}
The row reports 1 litters or clutches per year. ReptTraits attributes this field to Scharf et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022. The sex-determining-mechanism field records GSD (XX/XY). ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-18af9fecae}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
