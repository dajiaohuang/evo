## Leaf, flower and fruit characters in the source entry {#s-a3508e84e9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral leaves with acute leaflet tips and no auriculate bases; both surfaces are smooth. Its inflorescences are spherical and axillary, with erect peduncles. The pink anthers are dorsifixed and bear stalked glands, and the fertile stamens are recorded as red. The fruit is indehiscent, pubescent and coriaceous; the habitat field gives the category terrestrial. This regional entry does not establish how these characters vary or define the species’ full range.
