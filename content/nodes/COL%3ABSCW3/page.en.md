## Overview {#s-094786d394}
COL26.8 lists this species as *Tamarinus mystax*. Many field studies published as *Saguinus mystax* predate the 2023 taxonomic review of the moustached-tamarin group. Lopes et al. treated mystax, pileatus and the newly described kulina as separate lineages. This page uses only local studies whose sites and taxonomic context can be traced.

## Social behavior and organization {#s-e2d9764e56}
Heymann (2000) compared scent-mark locations within the home ranges of four wild groups. Marks were not concentrated at boundaries or overlap zones, and the study found no territorial function for the behavior. This result describes the sampled groups and study period, not every site or social context.
