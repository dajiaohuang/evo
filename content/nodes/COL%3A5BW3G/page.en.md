## Sessile leaves on tufted stems {#s-ca7c98c0cf}
This record notes a swollen underground stem and tufted, upright shoots with grooved stems. The oval leaves are sessile, with toothed or scalloped edges; their upper surfaces are sparsely bristly and the lower surfaces more consistently hairy. Flowering branches divide in pairs, with hairs on the flower stalks; the fruits are described as smooth.
