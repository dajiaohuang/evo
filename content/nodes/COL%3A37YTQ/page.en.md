## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-0922814e58}
COL26.8 records Duberria shirana as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Pseudoxyrhophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-1f4c313004}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 438 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-123254d9ce}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Bates et al. 2014.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a4d84545da}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
