## A cushion shrublet with paired blue flowers {#s-216f87286c}
Nivenia fruticosa is a cushion-shaped shrublet 9–20 cm tall, with sword-shaped leaves and paired pale-blue flowers. The regional flora reports flowering from November to January.

## Rocky Langeberg slopes {#s-e9eb688f71}
The account records rocky sandstone slopes and gives Langeberg as the regional range; it is not a complete global range assessment.
