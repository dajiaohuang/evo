## A mat-forming plant with solitary white flowers {#s-423f5c5f35}
The description records a smooth, mat-forming succulent with prostrate branches that send up slender flowering shoots. The leaves spread or curve backward and are concave above. Flowers are usually solitary at the tips of short shoots, forming a loose display; petals are snow-white, and rare groups of three are noted. The fruit capsule opens at maturity. It grows on rocky areas of Karoo flats and hills, including places near the sea.
