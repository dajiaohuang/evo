## Distribution records {#s-4465842595}
The study overlaid IUCN range maps on ten major Brazilian Amazon interfluves and listed Mico nigriceps in 1 areas: Rondônia. Here, occurrence means a region-level map overlap; it does not confirm animals at every site in those areas and is not a population count or complete range assessment.
