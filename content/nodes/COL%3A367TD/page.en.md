## Dimorphotheca acutifolia {#s-80acdf8dbd}
## White rays with purple-brown reverses

SANBI describes a spreading, roughly hairy shrublet up to 50 cm tall, with linear-oblanceolate leaves that are usually toothed and sometimes sharply so. Solitary radiate heads sit at branch tips; the rays are white above and purple-brown on the reverse, while the head is described overall as purple to brown. Ray achenes are three-angled and warty, about 6 mm long; the disc florets are sterile. Habitat is sandstone slopes.
