## Recorded parent of Pelecaniformes {#s-cfa877b79d}
Pelecaniformes is listed at rank order in the PBDB classification route, with Pelecanimorphae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
