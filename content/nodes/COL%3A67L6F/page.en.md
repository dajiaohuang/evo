## Study sites {#s-e417d9c470}
At San Juan del Carare in central Colombia, researchers followed one habituated group of five A. griseimembra in seasonally flooded lowland rainforest during parts of 2018–2019. The group occupied one study forest at 90–150 m elevation; this is a local research account, not a range survey.

## Feeding and space use {#s-5d108d6e7e}
In that group, fruits were the principal food and the monkeys spent about 48% of the night resting, similar to the three A. lemurinus groups in the same study. Its estimated home range was 2.7 ha by the 90% kernel method and 3.6 ha by the 100% minimum-convex-polygon method. These estimates describe one group and depend on the method used.

## Evidence limits {#s-5091b653fc}
Behavior, diet and space-use statements come from one habituated group at one lowland site during a bounded study period; they are not species-wide averages or a complete ecological account.
