## Effective-size inference across glacial history {#s-396b786e12}
E. moseleyi is grouped with early Last Glacial Period decline and late recovery, while E. chrysocome declines across roughly the last 250,000 years. Shared rockhopper naming does not imply one demographic history, and ancient recovery does not establish a present increase. These curves infer long-term effective size from genomic heterozygosity and depend on mutation rates and time calibration. They are not individual censuses and cannot reliably recover changes in the most recent years.
