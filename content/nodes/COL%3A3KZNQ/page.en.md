## How is it compared with Hermannia prismatocarpa? {#s-d6c600e40c}
The regional account compares it with H. prismatocarpa and notes its short, coarse hairs, softly velvety lower leaf surfaces, stipules that fall away, and shorter, nearly spherical capsules.

## What branch and leaf pattern does the revision describe? {#s-7075ed0981}
A 1980 revision describes long, smooth-looking, bright green branches that lie along the ground. Their tips bear ascending, leafless inflorescences; ascending side branches also end in inflorescences. The leaves grow on fairly long petioles, vary considerably in shape and differ in color between their two surfaces.

## What habitat is reported? {#s-0118e32c2f}
The regional account reports sandy coastal flats. It gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
