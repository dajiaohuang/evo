## A short tube and raised white mottling {#s-dd6ee53729}
Gladiolus rudis is distinguished by cream-coloured flowers with a short tube, 18–21 mm long, and yellow, spade-shaped marks outlined in red or purple on the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals). Raised white mottling on the protective leaf sheaths is another diagnostic feature.

## Sandstone slopes in fynbos {#s-9973455af3}
The flora records sandstone slopes in fynbos (a Cape shrubland vegetation type).
