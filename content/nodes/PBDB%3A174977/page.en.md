## A sampled classification {#s-d521da7e6a}
The atlas’s 318–259 Ma span records the fossil sample used for Meganisoptera, from Namurian B material through at least the end of the Middle Permian. Named specimens from Hagen-Vorhalle and an explicit character analysis support a sampled classification, but they do not define every lineage or an exact global extinction interval.

The Hagen-Vorhalle material is part of the Namurian evidence used in a character analysis of Meganisoptera. The matrix documents how the sampled forms were classified; it does not settle relationships across all meganisopteran lineages.

## An envelope of known samples {#s-2c006cd933}
The displayed 318–259 Ma interval runs from the reported Namurian B occurrences to records extending at least through the end of the Middle Permian. The younger edge is rounded and does not mark a precisely observed extinction date.
