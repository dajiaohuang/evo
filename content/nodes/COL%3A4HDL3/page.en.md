## *Phymaspermum peglerae*: silvery leaves and solitary yellow heads {#s-63ed47f181}
The regional account describes a densely leafy shrublet, 0.6–0.7 m tall, with narrow, fleshy leaves covered in silvery hairs. Each leaf is about 9–18 mm long and ends in a sharp point. Its flower heads are solitary at branch tips on long, densely woolly stalks. Each head has about 20 yellow florets and an 8–12 mm involucre, the cup of overlapping bracts around the flowers. The account places flowering around January and records plants along the banks of the Mthatha River.
