## *Tachigali acrensis* {#s-475e6b1d97}
The field records leaf-like stipules and cylindrical domatia (small structures associated with leaves) on the rachis. Leaflets are elliptic to ovate, leathery, and softly hairy beneath; there are up to 10 leaflet pairs. Flowers exceed 10 mm, are bilaterally symmetric, and have 10 dimorphic stamens; fruits are elliptic-oblong. These are selected characters from the entry.
