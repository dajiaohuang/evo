## The source concept {#s-bf965c7ebe}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0060 records Allobates zaparo in genus Allobates, family Aromobatidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Adult dimensions: Body_size_mm=31 mm {#s-cf5a24757c}
Body_size_mm=31 mm is the compiled maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
