## Elasmosauridae in the PBDB hierarchy {#s-f13e0fe523}
PBDB records Elasmosauridae at family rank, and this guide links 39 selected fossil-species entries. The count describes this page set, not every descendant. A PBDB parent path organizes occurrence records; it is not a phylogenetic hypothesis. Fossil entries do not establish that the whole family is extinct.

## Selected species and collection contexts {#s-feb399c1c9}
The route begins with Wapuskanectes betsynichollsae, Traskasaura sandrae, Nakonanectes bradti, Styxosaurus snowii and Styxosaurus browni. Across the selected species pages, 64 occurrence records map to 64 collections; recorded stratum names include Clearwater, Haslam, Bearpaw Shale and Pierre Shale. Compare each occurrence with its own age interval and identification qualification. Occurrence and collection counts are not specimen or population counts.

## What the selected fossil records do not establish {#s-f42fa79bf8}
This selection covers a subset of fossil records, not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence and collection totals do not measure specimens, individuals, populations or sampling effort; pooled outer age bounds are not the group origin or extinction. PBDB parent paths organize these records separately from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
