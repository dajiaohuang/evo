## PBDB hierarchy for Elasmosauridae {#s-f13e0fe523}
In the PBDB parent hierarchy, Elasmosauridae is recorded at family rank. This guide links 39 fossil-species entries from the selected page set. The count describes this guide, not every descendant, and a list of extinct records does not mean that the whole group is extinct. A PBDB parent path is a database classification, not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Collections in this selected guide {#s-feb399c1c9}
The guide begins with Wapuskanectes betsynichollsae, Traskasaura sandrae, Nakonanectes bradti, Styxosaurus snowii, Styxosaurus browni. Across the selected species pages, 64 occurrence records map to 64 collections; named stratum examples include Clearwater, Haslam, Bearpaw Shale, Pierre Shale. Each collection record retains its own age interval and identification qualifications, so comparisons belong at that record level. Occurrences and collections are not counts of specimens or populations.

## What the selected fossil records do not establish {#s-f42fa79bf8}
This selection covers a subset of fossil records, not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence and collection totals do not measure specimens, individuals, populations or sampling effort; pooled outer age bounds are not the group origin or extinction. PBDB parent paths organize these records separately from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
