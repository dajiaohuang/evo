## Feeding research {#s-e10a95081e}
In Silent Valley rainforest, direct feeding observations from 1993 to 1996 identified 89 plant species eaten by Nilgiri langurs. Most were characteristic evergreen-forest plants, and the frequency of feeding observations on different foods varied by month and season.

## Classification {#s-eaa671f63f}
The Silent Valley paper used the combination Trachypithecus johnii in its title. This page follows the pinned COL26.8 name Semnopithecus johnii while keeping the paper's published usage visible; it does not settle generic placement.

## Evidence limits {#s-310668bf3b}
The feeding account is based on observations in one rainforest park during 1993–1996; it is not a current range-wide dietary survey. The older paper's generic combination is retained as published.
