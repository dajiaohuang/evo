## Several trunks and rounded leaflets {#s-42288cdc59}
The flora record describes a tree with two or more grouped trunks. Its leathery leaves have two or three pairs of elliptic to oblong leaflets, whose tips may be blunt or notched. Loose branching flower clusters are about as long as nearby leaves. The pod is 2–3.5 cm long and recorded as either rounded or slightly sickle-shaped; its seed covering is orange or red. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
