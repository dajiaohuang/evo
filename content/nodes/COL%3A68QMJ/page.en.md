## Type elevation and examined sites outside the summary {#s-83e8f2bc89}
Male holotype CIB 98010, formerly CIB 890126, came from Xinmin, Wuliang Mountain, Jingdong, Yunnan, at 1200 m. The monograph explicitly corrected the printed longitude minutes from 75 to 45. It examined Huangcaoling material at about 2400 m and Ailao material at about 1980 m, while its wider southwestern Yunnan distribution summary gave 2000–2400 m. Both the 1200 m type elevation and 1980 m examined locality remain separate from that narrower summary; no unexplained correction is invented.

## Nine measured adults with unwebbed toes {#s-98a2dc43c7}
The examined diagnosis reported four adult males at 25.9–28.5 mm snout–vent length and five females at 32.1–38.0 mm. The separately listed historical type series included juveniles and tadpoles, which are not added to these measured adult denominators. Anatomy included no vomerine ridges or teeth, toes without webbing or lateral fringes, and brown nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II in breeding males, with a subgular vocal sac. These observations do not establish population-wide size limits or breeding-season dates.
