## Platycaulos subcompressus {#s-6e7d835105}
## Two chambers in the ovary

This dioecious perennial reaches about 40 cm and grows either from stolons or in tufts. The regional description notes tangled, branched, compressed stems and solitary female spikelets; unlike a one-chambered ovary, its ovary has two chambers. Seeps and stream margins above 750 m are the recorded habitat.
