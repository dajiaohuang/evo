## Neea liesneri {#s-874d2e0642}
## Trunk-borne flower clusters and closed cupule lobes

The leaves of Neea liesneri are opposite, stalked and obovate, with entire margins and pointed to tapering tips. Their upper surfaces are hairless; hairs are recorded along the lower midrib. Loose, many-flowered cymes arise from the trunk. Male flowers have an urn-shaped perianth, while the female perianth is not described. The fleshy, globose-to-ellipsoid anthocarp has closed cupule lobes. The source leaves several flower details unknown.
