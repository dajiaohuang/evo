## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Hiraea bullata? {#s-569c5b7214}
Leaves are elliptic, obovate or oval, with rounded bases and acute or mucronate tips. The petioles and leaf margins bear glands; the blades range from nearly hairless to velvety. Terminal or axillary corymbs carry flowers with eight glands. Petals have toothed or ragged margins. The fruit has kidney-shaped wings.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-92b6eee26c}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
