## What leaf and flower features are recorded for Croton cearensis? {#s-7d639cb492}
Young branches have simple or star-shaped hairs. The long-stalked oval to elliptic leaves bear star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, radiating main veins, and finely toothed edges; a sessile nectar gland occurs at the base. Young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are nearly sessile and bell-shaped; female flowers are nearly sessile, with fused sepals and a four-part style. Female petals are absent, and the calyx opens valvately. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
