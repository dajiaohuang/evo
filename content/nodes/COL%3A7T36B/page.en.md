## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-8c32c8e9d9}
The species-level foraging-stratum weights are 70% in or just above the canopy, 20% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy, and 10% in understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a whole-day time budget. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.
