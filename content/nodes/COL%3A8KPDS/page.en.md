## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-7a93f798be}
COL26.8 records Uroplatus fangorn as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-267ae80b81}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.
