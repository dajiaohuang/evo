## What is recorded for Bronwenia cinerascens, and what remains unspecified? {#s-081a6b6130}
The leaf blades are woolly-haired, and the petioles bear glands. The inflorescence has more flowers than an unstated threshold; the source field gives no number. The styles are parallel. Each fruit segment has a smooth seed-bearing center.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-a75c6a9001}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
