## What range of leaf and flower traits is recorded for Banisteriopsis malifolia? {#s-03688224f8}
Opposite leaves range from narrow elliptic to broad ovate and bear shield-shaped glands at the blade base. The upper surface may be velvety or pubescent, or become nearly hairless; the lower ranges from silky-haired to densely or sparsely woolly. Axillary clusters have four flowers. Their bracts fall in the bud stage, and the flower pedicels are stalkless and hairless. The calyx has a green oil gland; petals are white or pale pink. Anther chambers are hairy, styles are equal, and the seed-bearing center of the fruit has hairs inside.
