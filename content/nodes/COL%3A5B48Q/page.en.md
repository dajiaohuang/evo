## Vernonanthura almedae {#s-5ab2efc9fc}
## Tomentose leaves bear eucamptodromous veins

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes upright stems with woolly to pubescent hairs and glandular dots. Subleathery, stalked leaves are lance-shaped, long-pointed and sharp-based. Both faces are woolly and glandular. Their secondary veins arch toward the edge and fade before reaching it (eucamptodromous venation).

Involucral bracts have acute tips. Each head contains 8–22 florets; the corolla is hairless and lacks glands. The dry fruits are setose and glandular, with cylindrical outer pappus bristles. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
