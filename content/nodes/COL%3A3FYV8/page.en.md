## Velvety grey-green leaves and magenta flowers {#s-d164c18e52}
The record describes a tufted succulent perennial with a well-developed rootstock. Unequal leaves emerge from below ground and spread along the soil; the larger may be 5 cm long, tapering to a point, and are velvety with papillae. Solitary magenta-pink flowers grow between older and newer leaves, with a darker line through the petals. The fruit has six chambers. It grows on rocky sandstone slopes over clayey sand, with or without quartz pebbles.
