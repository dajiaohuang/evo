## Type interpretations and specimen referrals by year {#s-f16577930e}
Published accounts interpret the 2010 type FSAC-KK 26 differently: the original paper treated it as a lower jaw, while a 2020 reanalysis identified it as an upper rostrum. A 2023 study compares the paired projections and single median boss, but these isolated jaw elements do not make a complete skeleton.

## Two anatomical interpretations of one type specimen {#s-0a8cbb3654}
FSAC-KK 26 remains the same type specimen, but its jaw assignment changes between the 2010 and 2020 accounts. That difference affects jaw reconstruction and proportional comparisons. Referred jaws and undetermined cervical bones remain separate; they do not together form one associated skeleton.

## Additional jaw material does not complete the biology {#s-1ee75c4b74}
Toothlessness, straight jaw margins and a palatal boss are anatomical observations, not a complete diet or flight model. Possible synonymy with Xericeps and finer clade placements remain unresolved; indeterminate body material is not treated as confirmed Alanqa material.

## Alanqa: PBDB classification route {#s-d20635dc5d}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) lists Alanqa as a genus and includes one species occurrence guide. Its parent path groups database records; it does not by itself demonstrate evolutionary relationships.
