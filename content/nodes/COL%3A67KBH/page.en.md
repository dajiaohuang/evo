## Anthurium trinervium: morphology and epiphytic habit {#s-40bd3dbfbe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records an erect stem but does not characterize its cataphylls or prophylls. Petiole shape is uncharacterized, although the petiole is marked smooth. Leaf blades are entire and flat, ovate, with obtuse tips and bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is uncharacterized, secondary veins are obscure above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf. Peduncle shape is uncharacterized; the flat, linear spathe forms an obtuse angle with it and is not stipitate. The fruit is a berry, with no color character supplied.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage.

The dataset records the habitat category “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
