## Leaf veins, bracts, and flower characters {#s-82d813d14b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description notes parallel tertiary veins (third-order veins). It records bracts that are free at the base and nearly equal in size. The calyx is cup-shaped, with lobes described as either equal or unequal. The corolla is salver-shaped, with its lobes overlapping in the bud; the anthers are inserted near the corolla throat. These are the descriptors in one regional field, which retains both calyx-lobe alternatives.
