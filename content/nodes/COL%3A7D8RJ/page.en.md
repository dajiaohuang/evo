## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-986df10583}
COL26.8 records Tropidophis haetianus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Tropidophiidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-84a08c70fc}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to HenDerson and Powell 2009, Feldman et al. 2015.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-3171bddb5a}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 8. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to HenDerson and Powell 2009, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-7b2a5ce0d0}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
