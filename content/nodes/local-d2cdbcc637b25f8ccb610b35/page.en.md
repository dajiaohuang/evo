## The source tadpole concept {#s-2e0a684f30}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Ansonia platysoma, in the genus Ansonia and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Ansonia platysoma. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-2b9da488df}
The compilation has five records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Supplied stage labels are 28, 27, 26 and 41. These are labels attached to source records, not a single tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; five records do not necessarily represent five unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
