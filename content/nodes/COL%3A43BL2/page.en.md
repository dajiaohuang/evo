## Mikania rufescens {#s-b2b051fe09}
## Five basal veins and tiny stipules

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, smooth, striate branches with solid pith. Opposite, ovate leaves are thin and papery, with entire margins, narrowing bases and acute tips; both surfaces are smooth. Five main veins radiate from the leaf base. Tiny stipules between the leaf stalks are recorded as absent or shorter than 1 mm.

Loose panicles or corymb-like clusters bear stalked heads. Lance-shaped bracts sit near the top of each head stalk; the elliptic to oblong bracts around a head are finely hairy and acute. The funnel-shaped corolla limb is shorter than its tube; its smooth, lance-shaped lobes are recorded as longer than the limb. The dry fruits (cypselae) are sparsely hairy. The habitat field says terrestrial; no locality or full range is supplied.
