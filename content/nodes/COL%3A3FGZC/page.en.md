## Trailing leaves and a clump-forming habit {#s-f1395d2fbd}
Geissorhiza outeniquensis grows 20–50 cm tall and often has trailing stems and leaves. Its pink to purple flowers have a 10–13 mm tube. Short runners produce new stems and leaf clusters close to the parent, and the account reports that plants do not become dormant in late summer and autumn.

## Streams, falls and shaded wet rock {#s-178c138ccd}
The source records stream banks, waterfalls and wet rocks in shade, consistent with the species’ riverine description. It is not a complete account of its range.
