## *Edmondia pinifolia*: woolly stems and papery flower-head bracts {#s-674322cd89}
The regional account describes a sparsely branched shrublet to 60 cm tall, lightly covered in white woolly hairs. Its narrow leaves ascend along the lower stems. Solitary discoid heads sit on long stalks that bear short, pressed leaves; the uppermost of these leaves become dry and papery. The head bracts are papery and white to pink. The fruitlets are flattened and winged. The account records rocky sandstone slopes as habitat.
