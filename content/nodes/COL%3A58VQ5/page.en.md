## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-53caab494e}
COL26.8 records Trioceros conirostratus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Chamaeleonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-da6fb7553b}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 67 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-c73aee9c0d}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as day-active (Diurnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-117d902e94}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 12. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-f9b135b026}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
