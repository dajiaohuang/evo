## Rock and terrestrial habitat labels {#s-860505c31d}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Calypogeia laxa Gottsche & Lindenb. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its entries are habitat categories rather than collection places.

One entry is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

A separate entry is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. Neither label supplies a collection locality or survey date.

The habitat fields do not provide a full range or sampling coverage.

The two source categories are the only biological content included in the regional record; they are not a complete species account.
