## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-3f85978644}
COL26.8 records Cyrtodactylus consobrinoides as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-612394d65a}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 50 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-08d9fc3616}
The active-time field classifies activity as night-active (Nocturnal); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-d20f1738e2}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.

## Reading this compiled trait record {#s-760a8945ff}
ReptTraits compiles separately cited fields into a species-level row. Its values may come from different source materials, localities, or study samples and should not be treated as observations from one animal.
