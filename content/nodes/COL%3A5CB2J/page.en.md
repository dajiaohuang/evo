## Nanling and Dayao mountain records {#s-8d07415dba}
Female holotype CIB 66000, formerly CIB 75I0689, came from Mangshan Nature Reserve, Yizhang, Hunan, at 1000 m. The examined material included localities in Guangdong and Guangxi, from Shimentai at about 370 m to Dayaoshan at about 1200 m. The broader summary placed the species in Nanling and Dayao mountain localities along Jiangxi, Hunan, Guangdong and Guangxi at 350–1200 m. Although the paper described it as common, it supplied no population census in this treatment; the locality summary is not a quantified abundance estimate.

## Fifteen examined adults with unwebbed toes {#s-5bec4357a7}
The diagnosis reported ten adult males at 60.4–71.6 mm snout–vent length and five females at 62.0–77.9 mm. It described vomerine ridges and teeth, smooth dorsal skin with sparse granules, small black lower-jaw asperities, and toes without webbing or lateral fringes. Breeding males had dense brown nuptial spines on the dorsal bases of fingers I and II and a subgular vocal sac. The measurements concern the examined sample, not every population or the separately listed historical types.
