## Relative weights of food categories {#s-39da8ee787}
The nonzero food weights are invertebrates 70% and fruit 30%. Source code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Foraging strata differ from whole-day activity {#s-685b1bdce3}
The species-level foraging-stratum weights are 50% in trees or high bushes from 2 m upward but below the canopy, 30% in understory, edges or shrubs below 2 m, and 20% on the ground. They estimate relative use of feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or a whole-day time budget. Surface-water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial categories have different source definitions; they are not elevational ranges and cannot yield flight heights or habitat area.

## Evidence limits {#s-893c2b8903}
The EltonTraits 1.0 entry is a historical ecological summary, not a complete species account. Food and avian stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time.
