## Live and preserved colours are separate evidence {#s-d56cc3e842}
The study describes live Brachycephalus leopardus as orange with rounded green spots along the sides and underside. In preservative, these contrasts appear cream with darker spots. This is a comparative description rather than a colour-chart measurement; preserved specimens do not show live colours, and colour alone is not a complete identification rule.
