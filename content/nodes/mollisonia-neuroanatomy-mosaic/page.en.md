## Mollisonia’s nerves and limbs tell competing stories {#s-8a90342fce}
Carbonaceous films in Burgess Shale specimens MCZ 1811 and USNM 305093 have been interpreted as nervous tissue. They preserve optic nerves, a segmental ventral nerve cord and a possible compact cephalic synganglion—a fused nerve centre in the head. These structures offer an unusual view of Mollisonia’s internal anatomy.

The evolutionary signal is not uniform: nervous-system characters and appendage characters support competing placements, while the front of the body is incompletely preserved. The study therefore leaves both ancestral organization and Mollisonia’s position among stem chelicerates contested. A fossil nerve pattern informs a tree analysis; it does not settle the tree by itself.
