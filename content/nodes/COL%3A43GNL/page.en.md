## Mimosa platyphylla {#s-866f107d36}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa platyphylla* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes upright stem hairs, listed as short-haired or bristly, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and globose flower clusters. The flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairy petal lobes; the entry also labels the plant terrestrial.
