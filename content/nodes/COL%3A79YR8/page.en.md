## What leaf and flower traits does the record give? {#s-bdf8d4d7d9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes simple, oval to elliptic leaves and lists their hair covering as glabrous or pubescent. It records five sepals, a blue corolla, and four petals. The field does not resolve the alternative leaf-hair states further.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-7f7ded6a3a}
The dataset labels Scoparia elliptica terrestrial; no locality is supplied.
