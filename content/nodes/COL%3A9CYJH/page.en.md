## *Chamaecrista obolaria* {#s-eed8b9caf9}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — linear; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis), axillary (arising where a leaf joins the stem), terminal; flowers — yellow. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
