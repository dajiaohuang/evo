## When did births and weaning peak at Los Tuxtlas? {#s-249b4ce9b3}
From 2000 to 2011, researchers made 454 monthly visits to ten wild howler groups in Los Tuxtlas, Mexico, studying the subspecies Alouatta palliata mexicana. They inferred that most conceptions leading to births occurred from April to June and that weaning peaked in April–May; conceptions were fewer than expected during the November–March energetic-stress period. These are seasonal patterns in the monitored population, not a fixed cycle for the whole species.

## How did two groups sleep at night? {#s-ffe521ae1a}
On Nicaragua's Ometepe Island, researchers recorded night-time sleep posture in two habituated wild groups.
