## Form and flowers {#s-8615edb18e}
Bayer's 2012 regional account describes a stemless succulent that forms clumps. Its leaves are usually hairless, swollen toward the underside, and yellowish green with a pink flush and mottled, net-like patterning. The flowers are pinkish, with the tips of the buds turned upward.

This is a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-449b0dbc52}
The same account records the species in Karroid broken veld and on rocky slopes.

This habitat note comes from a regional species account published in 2012; it does not establish conditions across the full species range.
