## Upright habit, tuber and leaves {#s-83e264a5b9}
Venter’s 2009 revision describes *Raphionacme villicorona* as an erect perennial herb up to 0.3 m tall. It records a narrowly ovoid tuber (a swollen underground organ) with a tapering base, 0.2 × 0.1 m, and one to a few upright aerial stems, branching laterally, up to about 0.1 m long and 2–3 mm in diameter; the greenish-brown stems range from finely hairy to densely woolly. The narrow, greyish-green leaves are 60–70 × 2–3 mm and woolly on both surfaces, with an acute tip.

## Flowers and fruit in the revision {#s-2dbfa356d1}
The revision describes few-flowered racemes at or near the stem tips. Each flower has a 15–25 mm stalk; its corolla, the petals collectively, has a broad bell-shaped tube 3–4 × 3 mm and narrow lobes 14–15 × 2 mm that bend back. The tube is maroon-violet inside and violet-green outside; the lobes are green with a maroon-violet base. The corona, a separate crown-like floral structure, has green, threadlike lobes 9–12 mm long whose upper halves are hairy. The account records solitary, upright follicles about 100 mm long and lists the seeds as unknown.

## Mountain savanna habitat {#s-9fdb8b844f}
The same regional account places the plant in subtropical dry *Lydenburgia cassinoides–Kirkia wilmsii* savanna on mountains and adjacent hills. It describes full-sun growth in well-drained, stony, dark-brown loam, with tubers wedged tightly between rocks. No observation date, population frequency, or complete distribution is supplied.
