## Cyrtanthus wellandii {#s-e6279198ec}
Cyrtanthus wellandii bears four to seven vermilion-to-scarlet flowers whose darker stripes lead into the throat. Its style remains inside the lower half of the floral tube, a feature the regional account calls unusual in the genus. A small number of plants grown under cover did not set seed without hand pollination, but the account presents those observations as preliminary and says breeding biology remains little known. It records stony slopes in grassy renosterveld.
