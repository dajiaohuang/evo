## The source tadpole concept {#s-873bad7155}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Quasipaa exilispinosa. The source classification lists Quasipaa exilispinosa as the genus and Dicroglossidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Quasipaa exilispinosa. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
