## PBDB classification and 101 selected species pages {#s-cfafbec437}
PBDB lists Ceratopsia at suborder rank and this guide links 101 selected fossil species pages. The recorded parent chain is a database classification; it does not establish phylogeny or complete membership.

## 345 occurrences across 332 collections {#s-bea3e7d057}
Five linked species pages—Stenopelix valdensis, Yinlong downsi, Chaoyangsaurus youngi, Xuanhuaceratops niei and Hualianceratops wucaiwanensis—offer a starting set for comparing the records and cited literature. The selected guide contains 345 occurrences from 332 collections. Bückeberg, Shishugou, Tuchengzi and Kuwajima appear as example stratum names. Compare each occurrence’s age and identification fields with its source; these counts describe database entries, not specimens or populations.

## The Aquilops sample interval and its limits {#s-dc964487a3}
The associated Aquilops evidence uses 113–100.5 Ma as a source-bounded sample interval for Ceratopsia, not the group’s origin or total duration. A morphology matrix places the Aquilops holotype among early neoceratopsians, but that analytical placement and its biogeographic implications do not identify a direct ancestor or a group-wide first appearance.
