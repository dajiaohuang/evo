Each field has its own source label; a label may identify a paper, book or database. ## Life-history evidence limits {#s-8901dc2f7b}
The 2015 life-history archive reports historical same-name records as compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except that maximum longevity uses the largest record found. These are not direct measurements of one individual, individual variation ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. The compilation can share and inherit data across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family and the presence of same-name sparse rows support the association here; they do not establish equivalence to COL26.8 concepts or remove all inherited components from the summaries. Wild or captive conditions are not uniformly confirmed, and underlying publications have not been individually reviewed. Diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields require other sources.
