## Accepted name and placement {#s-3a88313a1f}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis glendae Ortega, Brito-M. & Ron, 2022 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A visible tympanum and male-only size range {#s-a1f67fcdb7}
The glendae diagnosis reported adult male SVL 16.60–19.93 mm and female size unknown. It described a visible tympanic membrane and annulus, no discoidal or dorsolateral folds, no heel tubercles, basal toe webbing, and absent vocal slits and nuptial pads. The reported annulus-to-eye proportion belongs to the described material, not a universal identification cutoff. Female measurements from other anaiae-group species are not glendae data, and absence of a heel tubercle or vocal slit is not evidence of impaired locomotion or measured call production.

## Cloud-forest perches and recorded sympatry {#s-914ebadba7}
The account recorded glendae near the Volcan River on Sangay’s eastern foothills and around Ankaku in Llanganates, with reported elevations 2,191–2,266 m. The primary cloud forest had abundant moss, orchids, ferns, bromeliads, bamboo and araceous plants. Specimens were found at 19:30–21:00 about 1.5 m above ground; kunam and bicantus were reported sympatric. Shared locality does not establish hybridization, competition or the same reproductive mode. The encounter window is not a demonstrated exclusive daily activity period.
