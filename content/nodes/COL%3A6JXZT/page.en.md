## Gasteria excelsa {#s-dd664a7514}
Gasteria excelsa is distinguished by its very large, spreading panicles, which may approach 2 m tall and more than 2 m across. Its dark-green rosettes have sharply serrated leaf margins and light-pink flowers. The regional account records shaded evergreen forest and river valleys from near sea level to about 1,400 m, on several well-drained soil types.
