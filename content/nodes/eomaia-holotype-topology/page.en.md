## Eomaia: one skeleton, two positions in mammal trees {#s-a44aeb64a0}
Holotype CAGS 01-IG-1a,b from China’s Yixian Formation preserves an articulated skeleton, including the skull, teeth, feet and claws. The original description used these characters in a matrix that placed Eomaia near the base of Eutheria and interpreted its limbs as suited to climbing.

Later combined analyses can place Eomaia outside Theria. Both position and “scansorial” locomotion—adapted for climbing—depend on character coding and comparison, rather than direct observation of ancestry or behaviour. The fossil is an unusually informative individual, not a settled marker for one branch or a witnessed way of life.
