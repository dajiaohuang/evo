## Cybianthus fuscus Mart. {#s-fd84cb3e78}
## Hairy midribs and lateral racemes

Cybianthus fuscus has smooth, cylindrical terminal branches. Its lance-shaped, papery leaves are hairy, especially along the midrib; the petiole is channelled and hairy. Lateral racemes carry four-parted flowers with rounded corolla lobes. Both staminate and pistillate flowers are stalked. The anthers are attached at their base, and the immature fruit wall is globose and dotted.
