## A time tree for 7,238 living amphibian species {#s-638cc7a74e}
A set of 10,000 model trees represents 7,238 living amphibian species, but the 15-gene matrix includes molecular data for only 4,061; other species are placed softly within taxonomic constraints. The trees compare estimated relationships and times, while unsampled placements and model dates depend on taxonomy and analysis. Their nodes are not fossils or direct observations of species origins.
