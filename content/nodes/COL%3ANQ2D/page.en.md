## Bulbine margarethae: clustered tubers and ground-level leaves {#s-141329c05b}
A South African regional account describes a tight rosette and roughly net-like leaf markings, as in *B. haworthioides*, but with globose tubers clustered densely. Leaves remain present at flowering, lie against the ground or sometimes spread outward, and are 5 mm wide. They range from bright green to greyish or reddish green, with convex upper surfaces. The margins and midvein are smooth or occasionally ciliate. The ovoid capsule is 4 mm long.

The account records limestone rock crevices as habitat.
