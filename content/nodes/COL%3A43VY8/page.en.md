## What does the record say about Mollinedia heteranthera? {#s-7aaf4c6fb3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes opposite, papery leaves with toothed edges. The upper surface becomes nearly hairless, while the underside has long soft hairs; dried leaves turn yellow. Male flowers grow in three-flowered clusters. Their floral cup is flat, and the inner tepals lack appendages. The rounded fruit segment becomes nearly hairless at maturity; the outer stem bark and fruit surface are not specified in this record.

## What broad habitat is listed? {#s-2210e1051d}
The dataset categorizes it as terrestrial, without a specific locality.
