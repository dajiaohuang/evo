## *Pentagonia williamsii* {#s-b0893622ea}
COL26.8 accepts *Pentagonia williamsii* Standl. as a species of *Pentagonia*, placed in tribe Dialypetalantheae and subfamily Dialypetalanthoideae within Rubiaceae. One Brazilian Flora 2020 row labels its habitat “Terrícola” (terrestrial). This is a habitat category only: the record gives no collection locality or survey date, establishes no complete range, and does not say whether it refers to wild or cultivated plants.
