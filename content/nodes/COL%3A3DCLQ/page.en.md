## Classification and source scope {#s-2db62e3747}
COL26.8 lists *Euterpe precatoria* as an accepted species in *Euterpe*, within the palm family Arecaceae. The description below draws on one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; it does not capture the species' full variation or distribution.

The accepted species name follows COL26.8; the Brazilian Flora description is linked to its matching World Flora Online record.

## Stems, leaves, and inflorescence {#s-4edc2db21f}
The source records a solitary stem 3–20 m tall and 4–23 cm across. It describes 10–20 leaves with closed green sheaths, sometimes striped vertically in yellow; dark fibers are absent at the sheath tip. Each leaf has 43–91 pinnae inserted regularly in the same plane. The inflorescence branches at the first order; its stalk is 10–15 cm long, its main axis 30–95 cm, and it bears 70–200 smaller branches (rachillae).

## Fruit and seed {#s-82e0cf976a}
The fruit is globose and 1–1.3 cm in diameter. The seed is described as having homogeneous endosperm, the nutritive tissue inside a seed.

The field records a solitary stem 3–20 m tall and 4–23 cm in diameter; 10–20 leaves with closed green sheaths, sometimes vertically striped yellow, and no dark fibers at the sheath tip; 43–91 pinnae inserted regularly in the same plane; a first-order-branched inflorescence with a 10–15 cm peduncle, a 30–95 cm rachis, and 70–200 rachillae; globose fruit 1–1.3 cm in diameter; and homogeneous seed endosperm.
These traits come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset; the field does not establish global morphology, population variation, sex, or life stage.
The field does not say whether the described plants were wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material.
