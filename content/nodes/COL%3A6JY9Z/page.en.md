## Gasteria batesiana {#s-91e6eb6193}
Gasteria batesiana forms small to large clumps of firm, brittle leaves, densely marked with white tubercles and bands. Its simple raceme carries large, pinkish flowers. The regional account notes variation in leaf size, colour and shape, and records shallow, humus-rich soils on shaded rocky ridges in hot, dry, frost-free valleys.
