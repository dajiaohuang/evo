## Small turf-forming plants with blue-pink flowers {#s-e85d6fc31f}
The Flora of Panama account describes Sisyrinchium micranthum as a small, tuft-forming herb with slender fibrous roots and linear, sword-shaped leaves 4–9 cm long. Its blue to pinkish-lavender flowers are yellowish at the base, with a perianth (the flower’s non-reproductive outer parts) about 7–8 mm long.

## From southern Mexico to Bolivia {#s-1cd0b63f38}
The cited flora gives a range from southern Mexico to Bolivia and records subalpine meadows and open woods. This account is not a current global occurrence inventory.
