## Disperis oxyglossa {#s-5f9fe9a850}
The account describes Disperis oxyglossa as 160–330 mm tall, with two to four alternate stem leaves and two to five flowers. Pink sepals and cream petals with green spots accompany a pungent scent; the lateral sepals bend sharply after rising. It is recorded in damp grass below slopes, at seeps and along watercourses.
