## Peplonia asteria {#s-ca190a45e2}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Peplonia asteria* as an accepted species of *Peplonia*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records terrestrial as its habitat category, but gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range. The source row includes no morphology description, so the evidence supports only this regional habitat label.
