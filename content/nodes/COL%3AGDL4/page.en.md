## Ardisia perinsignis Lundell {#s-f85ca40fd6}
## Large leathery leaves on a tree to 10 m

Ardisia perinsignis is a tree to about 10 m tall with thick, smooth branchlets. Its hairless, pale leathery leaves may reach 32 cm long; the blade narrows toward the base and extends down along the petiole. Large terminal panicles carry five-parted flowers. The sepals and petals are dotted with dark glands, and the many ovules are arranged in several rows. Fruit details are not included in the cited description. The species is native to Panama.
