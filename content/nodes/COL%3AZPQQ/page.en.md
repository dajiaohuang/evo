## What leaf and flower traits are noted for Croton campanulatus? {#s-264be57089}
Young branches have lepidote, scale-like hairs. Long-stalked oval leaves have the same kind of hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and entire margins. A stalked nectar gland occurs near the upper end of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are stalked and bell-shaped; female flowers are stalked, with fused sepals and a four-part style. Female petals are reduced to glands. Their bud parts have a quincuncial overlap pattern. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
