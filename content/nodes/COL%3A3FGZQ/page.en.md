## Yellow flowers with a dark centre {#s-0170d0837e}
Geissorhiza purpureolutea is up to 15 cm tall, with ribbed leaves and pale to deep-yellow flowers, usually with a dark-brown to purple centre. The account describes the species as relatively rare today; its flower tube is 2–4 mm long and tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are mostly 10–17 mm.

## Wet sandy flats {#s-52cc3dddc2}
The regional account lists wet sandy flats as habitat. It does not provide a current population estimate.
