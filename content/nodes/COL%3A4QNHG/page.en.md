## Overview {#s-9d8fc9751c}
COL26.8 accepts *Pygathrix nigripes*. Its English common name comes from the locally exact-matched MDD v2.5 record; the Chinese rendering is an editorial translation. The main source is a field diet study at Vietnam’s Nui Chua and Phuoc Binh national parks.

## Research observations {#s-5c5851ee18}
From February to August 2005, the researchers observed the animals for 22 days and 144 contact hours across the two parks, recording 903 scans and 1,537 feeding scores from 63 individuals. Black-shanked doucs ate mostly leaves year-round but also used fruit and flowers from many plant species. Plant diversity in the diet increased during the wet season, when fruit made up nearly twice its dry-season proportion of feeding scores.

## Interpreting the evidence {#s-c493660c4e}
The paper recorded foods from 152 plant species but found no small set of heavily preferred species. Sampling covered two parks and a limited observation period; the authors found no evidence of foraging to maximize protein or energy. The study therefore documents diet breadth and seasonal differences at those sites, not a range-wide diet or fixed preference.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-00c55dfbad}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 10104.16 g; mass at birth or hatching 393.5 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex; birth/hatching mass describes a different life stage. These are not a longitudinal series of the same individuals or a species-wide range of individual masses.

Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 1460 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 1 offspring/eggs; conception or fertilization to birth 182.88 d; age of dependence on food other than maternal milk 330 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 26.78 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

The used groups carry labels Ref_51, metadata:Nowak1999, Ref_1. Diet, foraging stratum and activity may draw on different sources. A Ref number belongs to its own bird or mammal reference table; the same number across tables can identify different works.

## Evidence limits {#s-b3c8f8f79d}
Observations were concentrated in two Vietnamese national parks during 22 days in 2005; seasonal conclusions are limited by the observation window and visibility. These observations do not establish range-wide distribution, current abundance or a conservation category.
