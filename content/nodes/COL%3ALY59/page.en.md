## What details are listed for the leaves and fruit? {#s-0bd4e54580}
The leaf base is rounded. Inflorescences are longer than 10 cm. The extrafloral nectary at the tip of the flower stalk is inconspicuous or absent. The fruit is flattened and unarmed, without spines.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-3d43ed2710}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
