## Senna corymbosa {#s-ec1c5478d2}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senna corymbosa* as an accepted species of *Senna* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists 2–4 pairs of leaflets and stalked glands between the basal leaflet pairs. Its flowers are grouped in racemes (flower clusters arranged along a stalk) or corymbs and have seven fertile stamens; the hanging pods are cylindrical or nearly so and do not split open.
