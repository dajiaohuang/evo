## Cyrtanthus inaequalis: flowers and comparison {#s-85c590a5c9}
A 2012 regional account describes a bulbous geophyte reaching 30 cm and producing bulbils. It records 2–7 linear leaves that are green at flowering. There are 4–9 fleshy, coral-red flowers, from nearly upright to spreading, with narrow funnel-shaped tubes. The oblong segments are arranged with three upper ones hooded, two lateral ones spreading, and the lower one bent downward. The flowers become more bilaterally symmetric with age. The style is subcapitate and arches upward.
