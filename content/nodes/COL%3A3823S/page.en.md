## What flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-799d8ba243}
The field describes narrow elliptic to inversely egg-shaped leaves, smooth above and sparsely scaled below. Cream-colored flowers grow in clusters on branches or older stems. Their outer petals are larger than the blunt-tipped inner petals. The green stamens contrast with the flowers. The round fruit is brown or yellow, with scale-like hairs and a basal collar.
