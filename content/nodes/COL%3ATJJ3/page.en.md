## Chamaecrista xinguensis {#s-196bc04336}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista xinguensis* records 6–10 ovate leaflet pairs, nectaries on leaf axis and flower clusters, yellow flowers, lens-shaped seeds. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
