## The source concept {#s-1e88d64543}
AmphiBIO v1 records Arthroleptis phrynoides as row Anf0142 under Arthroleptis, Arthroleptina, and Anura. The source concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); current taxonomic equivalence has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-fb7ec51f18}
The row flags terrestrial and aquatic foraging strata. These categories do not describe specific locations or seasonal patterns. A later TetrapodTraits compilation reports microhabitat corrections for some amphibian entries, but this row has not been checked against them; the deposited categories are retained without treating them as confirmed or necessarily wrong.

## Developmental strategy {#s-173ddec6d7}
The record flags direct development. Larval-stage and viviparity fields are not flagged; the compilation does not describe breeding sites or care in this row.
