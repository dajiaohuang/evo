## *Polygala parkeri* {#s-8d4a6071dd}
In comparison with *Polygala ludwigiana*, the cited account distinguishes this species by oblong, blunt side petals with entire or nearly entire edges, and a broad, unbroken, wavy crest on the keel (the lower, boat-shaped floral part).

The source lists rocky slopes as habitat. It gives no locality, date, or indication that this is the species' full habitat range.
