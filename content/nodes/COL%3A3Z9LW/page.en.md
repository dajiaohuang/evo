## Organization of the recorded vocalization {#s-69c7eb9db2}
The 2019 archive-based analysis describes Melanophryniscus moreirae as follows: A regularly repeated non-harmonic note series and separate non-pulsed harmonic notes; the authors propose advertisement and aggressive roles, respectively. These observations characterize the analyzed vocalizations, rather than every social context or population. The paper's advertisement/aggressive distinctions for complex repertoires are functional hypotheses; playback experiments and behavioral observations would be needed to establish the proposed roles.
