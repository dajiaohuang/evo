## Color, scales, and a species boundary {#s-78413958b3}
Vogel and David revised Macrocalamus from 119 preserved specimens, alongside observations of living and freshly collected animals. In the examined M. lateralis forms, ventral color, ventrolateral stripes, and morphological and scale-count data covaried. The authors treated the yellow-bellied form as distinct from the red-bellied form; the revision also described M. schulzi as new. Their discussion did not resolve relationships across the genus.

## What records show for M. schulzi {#s-af20142f3e}
The species account places M. schulzi in wet Cameron Highlands forest at about 1,000–1,800 m, including damp leaf litter, moss, and terrace fields. The paper records nocturnal encounters and road-killed specimens, while diet information was sparse. These observations concern M. schulzi and do not characterize every Macrocalamus species.
