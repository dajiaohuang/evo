## Nomenclature and description samples {#s-67e7fa886b}
The accepted name Lepilemur sahamalaza was formally established in the 2017 printed correction, which explained that the earlier online name did not meet nomenclatural availability rules and fixed the current spelling. The type series came from four animals on the peninsula, with six additional Ankarafa animals measured. Available measurements suggest it may be smaller than L. tymerlachsonorum, but the sample is limited.

## Known localities and evidence limits {#s-5aa65d3d1a}
The correction paper restricted the known distribution to the type locality on the Sahamalaza Peninsula and proposed the Sambirano River as a possible northern limit; further fieldwork was needed. The Ankarafa morphology sample was about 25 km southeast of the type locality, which does not establish continuous occupancy.
