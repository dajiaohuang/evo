## Lampranthus stenopetalus {#s-05586af5d9}
## Tapered rough leaves and white-to-pink flowers

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes an erect shrublet up to 25 cm tall. Its tapering, subcylindrical leaves are rough to the touch and reach 15 mm long by 1.5 mm wide.

Small clusters carry white-to-pink flowers on short stalks; the flowers reach 25 mm across. The habitat account records sandy flats.
