## New mountain and island records broadened the known range {#s-14087142d1}
The 2016 account reported collections at high elevations on Mts. Balatukan and Lumot and on Dinagat Island, expanding beyond earlier Diwata-range knowledge. The Mt. Kitanglad record was explicitly based on one tentatively identified specimen, whereas broader occurrence across northern and central Mindanao mountains was a prediction. These record types retain different certainty; the prediction is not converted to confirmed occupancy of every mountain or a complete contemporary range.
