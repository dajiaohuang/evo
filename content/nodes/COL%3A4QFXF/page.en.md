## The 2015 life-history entry and this name {#s-2dd3aedeb5}
Puffinus bannermani is an accepted Procellariidae species in pinned COL26.8. This page reads five life-history fields from a historical record with the same name and family; each is backed by cited species-level rows. The archive may have carried information across older taxonomic groupings, so matching row names do not rule out inherited data.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-bd19b18d50}
The historical compilation gives an adult body-mass summary of 194 g. Its adult records may combine males, females and adults whose sex was not specified; the summary is not a range of individual masses. No birth or hatching mass is included here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-15bd04bd97}
The compilation reports 1 offspring or egg per litter/clutch, 51 d from egg laying to hatching, and 72 d to flight capability (some sources record departure from the nest). Here, d means days. Litter and clutch counts are combined in one field, so a fractional summary would not describe a fraction of an offspring in one event. The timing values measure different events, not one interchangeable reproductive cycle.

## A longest record differs from usual lifespan {#s-a251e3b4d7}
The archive records 19 years for maximum_longevity_y, the largest value found using its collection method. This is not a typical or average wild lifespan, a limit for every individual, or confirmation of a current record that can be repeated.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-93ef529b3c}
The matching source rows include labels Dunning, 1992; Bennett, 1986; Dyke and Kaiser, 2010; and Wasser, D.E. and P.W. Sherman, 2010, among others. The archive keeps the original row numbers, field values and labels, with the reference table's full explanations. A label may point to a paper, book or another database. The number of rows is not the number of animals measured, and repeated citations do not count as independent experiments.

## Life-history evidence limits {#s-2e9b79a954}
These are historical same-name records from the 2015 life-history archive. Values are compilation medians or, for an even number of records, averages of the two middle values; maximum longevity instead uses the largest record found. They are not measurements of one animal, ranges of individual variation, average wild lifespans or current population surveys. The source can share or inherit information across historical species and subspecies. Matching name and family plus same-name source rows support the association, but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 concepts or remove inherited material from every summary. The records do not consistently establish wild versus captive conditions, and the original publications have not been reviewed one by one. Other sources are needed for diet, behavior, current range, conservation and missing fields.
