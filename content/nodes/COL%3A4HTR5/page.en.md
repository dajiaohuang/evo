## Classification {#s-15a7c14c0a}
McGraw's 2006 short communication tested four probabilistic methods against the dated sighting and museum-specimen record for the taxon then named Piliocolobus badius waldronae. Each method returned a probability above 0.05 for persistence at the time of the 2000 extinction claim; the author argued that the records were compatible with an extant but extremely rare form. This is an inference from historical records, not a confirmed later sighting. COL26.8 now carries waldronae as a species ID.

## Evidence limits {#s-2b26964258}
The paper analyzes historical records under a subspecies name; its statistical result does not verify a living population, and the exact MDD crosswalk does not match this COL name.
