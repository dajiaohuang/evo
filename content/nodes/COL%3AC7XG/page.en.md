## Accepted name and placement {#s-5a75919613}
COL26.8 accepts Alsodes vittatus (Philippi, 1902) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Alsodidae (family) → Alsodes (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A Chilean priority score and parasite-host records {#s-1899224192}
A 2024 Chilean assessment assigns Alsodes vittatus a priority index of 2.29 in the medium category and codes population-trend information as missing, with the population recorded outside protected areas. A 2025 Molineidae review separately lists the name as a host associated with Oswaldocruzia neghmei at three Chilean localities. The score is a model ranking, and the parasite entries are literature records rather than current prevalence data.
