## Distribution records {#s-62169af753}
The supplement lists three brown titi observations at Candeias do Jamari and Itapuã do Oeste, Rondônia. The records establish occurrence at survey sites, not a complete distribution or census.

## Evidence limits {#s-64fc7e9d28}
This guide summarizes records from selected sites; it is not a complete species account, range survey, population estimate, or conservation assessment. The Chinese common name is an editorial translation.
