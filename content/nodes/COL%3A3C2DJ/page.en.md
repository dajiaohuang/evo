## Name and AVONET sample {#s-c90e4e02b0}
COL26.8 accepts Eudocimus albus within Threskiornithidae. AVONET v7 reports this name under HBW–BirdLife 2020. Five individuals were measured and four had a complete morphological set; usable counts may vary by trait.

## Beak dimensions {#s-a27a4130b4}
Mean beak length is 120.1 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip; width is 12.2 mm and depth 17 mm at that edge. These are separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-7a4360b194}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 280.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 90.4 mm. AVONET’s compiled mean mass is 889.66 g, sourced to Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information, not necessarily the morphology specimens.

## Broad habitat and ecology categories {#s-91fc3180dd}
AVONET assigns Wetland habitat, an Aquatic predator trophic niche and a Terrestrial primary lifestyle. These are broad comparison labels, not a complete habitat or diet description.

## Movement and mapped range {#s-7a58f6665f}
The migration category is partially migratory. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude −3.89° to 35.2°. They do not give a complete account of non-breeding movement, current position or occupancy throughout the span. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## The August 2015 life-history record {#s-e741bb0688}
The 2015 archive provides 7 focal fields in cited same-name, same-family, species-level rows. This supports a historical association, but summaries may retain inherited taxonomy; a matching name does not remove that influence.

## Mass at two life stages {#s-f9bec61616}
The archive reports adult body mass of 900 g and birth or hatching mass of 31.95 g. The adult field may combine sexes and adults of unspecified sex. These values describe different stages, not the same individuals over time or a range of individual masses.

## Maturity, clutch size and timing {#s-621a6e9afe}
The source-defined female maturity field (age at first conception) is 730 d; litter or clutch size is 2.7 offspring or eggs; oviposition-to-hatching time is 22 d; and age at flight capability is 35 d (some sources define it as leaving the nest). Here, d means days. The fractional clutch summary is not a fraction of an individual, and the fields describe different events.

## A maximum record, not typical lifespan {#s-bdfef11f62}
The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 27.6 years, selected as the largest record under its method. It is not a typical or mean wild lifespan, an individual survival ceiling or a current record known to be repeatedly attainable.

## Standardized diet weights {#s-cc0a9a153d}
The archive assigns 60% to fish, 30% to unspecified vertebrates and 10% to invertebrates. Code A indicates high compiler confidence in the source and diet information. These weights translate descriptions, not measured diet shares; zero would not prove a food is never taken. Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader source categories than their everyday names.

## Ground and water-surface feeding positions {#s-25252aa6ff}
Species-level foraging weights are 60% on the ground and 40% on or just below the water surface. They describe relative feeding positions, not nest sites, all habitat or time budgets. Water, understory, mid/high, canopy and aerial classes have separate source definitions; they are not elevation bands or flight heights.

## Limits across these historical sources {#s-1b4d5e885e}
This page combines AVONET v7, the August 2015 life-history archive and EltonTraits 1.0; it is not a complete species account. AVONET values are sample means, mass has separate provenance, ecological labels are broad and maps are historical. The life-history archive reports compiled summaries rather than measurements of one individual and may retain inherited taxonomy. Source conditions are not consistently known, and underlying papers were not individually reviewed. EltonTraits weights come from literature descriptions, not direct measurements. Same-name and same-family associations do not establish equivalence to COL26.8. Current ecology, range, population and conservation require other sources.
