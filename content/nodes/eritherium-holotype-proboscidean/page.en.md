## Eritherium: a Paleocene skull and early proboscidean relationships {#s-60f5030c44}
Eritherium azzouzorum is known from a 15-specimen hypodigm from the lower bone bed at Sidi Chennane, Morocco. Holotype MNHN PM69 preserves the rostrum and upper teeth; fourteen additional specimens extend the sampled upper and lower dentition and skull anatomy. Dental and cranial characters place it on the proboscidean stem in the cited matrix. The often-repeated 3–8 kg body-mass estimate comes from dental allometry, and the precise relationship is comparative—not an observed body mass or ancestor.
