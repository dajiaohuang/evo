## Hispid foliage and an uncertain capsule character {#s-5769be9ed5}
The source description records a densely leafy, hispid subshrub to 20 cm tall, branching mainly from the base. Leaves are sessile, fascicled and linear lanceolate, hispid below and along the margins, with spine tipped apices. Flowers are sessile, axillary, and listed as pink or white and yellow. Capsule indument is marked uncertain as “?villous”; capsules are shortly horned. The ecology field gives sandy and gravel slopes. These source fields do not define a complete range.
