## Pilocarpus pauciflorus {#s-70333c7102}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a winged petiole or rachis, a terminal inflorescence and smooth mericarps up to 15 mm.

## Winged leaf axes and smooth fruit segments {#s-3dbb14a0bb}
The petiole is conspicuous, with wings recorded on the petiole and/or leaf rachis. Leaves are simple; blade tips may be acuminate, obtuse or retuse, the base attenuate, and the lower surface pubescent. Blades are described as bullate or flat. The terminal inflorescence is upright or curved only toward its distal end; its rachis is not corky. Flowers are cream or greenish, with pedicels present and petals without a keel. Fruit mericarps are not rugose and measure up to 15 mm. The ecology field is “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or full range.
