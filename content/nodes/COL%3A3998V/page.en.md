## Accepted name in the catalogue {#s-761daf4ee2}
The 20 August 2026 release of Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) accepts Eleutherodactylus erendirae Grünwald, Reyes-Velasco, Franz-Chávez, Morales-Flores, Ahumada-Carrillo, Jones & Boissinot, 2018 as a species. Its recorded path is Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family), then Eleutherodactylus (genus). This is the name and placement in that release; other checklists and fossil databases need separate identity evidence.
