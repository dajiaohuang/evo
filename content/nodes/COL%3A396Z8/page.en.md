## Elegia juncea {#s-2414e4fa3b}
## Female flowers partly hidden by surrounding structures

The regional flora describes Elegia juncea as a perennial with underground stems that grows in clumps; female and male flowers grow on separate plants. It reaches 1 m, and its leaf sheaths (the basal parts of leaves around a stem) fall away. Its female flowers are largely hidden by spathes (leaf-like bracts around the flower group); small bracts in that group, called spathellae, are absent. The account places it usually on slopes above 600 m.
