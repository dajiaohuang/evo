## Diclidanthera penduliflora {#s-9fa114ddc6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Diclidanthera penduliflora* as an accepted species of *Diclidanthera*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes pendulous branches and simple or clustered racemes with a bristly axis. The flowers have 10 stamens, and the corolla tube is at least twice the length of the calyx. Its habitat field says terrestrial. These characters come from one regional flora record; no broader variation survey is included.
