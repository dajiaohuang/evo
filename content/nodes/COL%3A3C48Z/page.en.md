## Rolled leaf edges and a raceme qualifier {#s-de98e30ffe}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or obovate leaves (widest toward the tip), with wedge-shaped bases and tips described as beak-like or tapering to a point. The leaves are papery; their margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The midrib, the main leaf vein, is grooved on the upper surface, and one vein runs near the edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis; the field labels it “auxotélico” but does not explain that qualifier. Supporting bracts persist, as do the small, inconspicuous bracteoles beside the flowers. The flower cup is smooth but bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is ellipsoid and smooth. Fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial label, not a range record {#s-c66d6a817d}
The habitat field labels the record “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category does not supply the missing range information.
