## Lupinus costaricensis {#s-9cdc4fd495}

The Flora of Panama describes a weak-stemmed, sprawling annual herb 15–30 cm long, with 8–11 palmately arranged leaflets; a second season of growth may leave the lower stem woody. Its pod is 2–3 cm long and contains 7–8 seeds. The account records it only from Volcán Chiriquí in Panama and Costa Rica.
