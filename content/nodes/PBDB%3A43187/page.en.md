## Recorded parent of Rhinocerotidae {#s-6d8fe4190c}
Rhinocerotidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotoidea as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
