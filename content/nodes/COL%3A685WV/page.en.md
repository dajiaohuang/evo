## What do its female spikelets contain? {#s-d4c8dce216}
A 2012 regional account describes *Askidiosperma chartaceum* as a tufted, dioecious perennial reaching 1 m. Each female spikelet, a compact flower cluster, bears many flowers 5–6 mm long. Dioecious means that male and female flowers occur on separate plants. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## What upland wetlands are listed? {#s-61d2b5523b}
The flora account associates the species with marshy mountain slopes and plateaus. It does not give a survey date or a complete distribution.
