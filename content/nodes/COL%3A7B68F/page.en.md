## Syagrus romanzoffiana: leaves and fruit {#s-f9d63b51e2}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes a smooth, solitary or aerial stem. Leaves are green and pinnate, with deciduous, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is arched or strongly recurved. Pinnae are described as leathery or membranous, grouped and flexible, with pendulous, asymmetrically acuminate tips. They are dark green and slightly discolorous.

The inflorescence is paniculate or pendulous, with an exposed, interfoliar infructescence. The spadix is grooved or pruinose; its rachis is sparsely tomentose, and the rachillae are arranged helically along it. Fruits are ovoid, with an orange epicarp bearing indumentum at the tip, fibrous and succulent mesocarp, and woody, oval endocarp. The endosperm is described as slightly irregular and gibbous.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
