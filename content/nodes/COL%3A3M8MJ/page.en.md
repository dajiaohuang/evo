## Holocheilus brasiliensis {#s-ef7d0210d3}
## A few heads form a compact to open corymb

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes leaves ranging from broadly elliptic and obovate to round. Their edges are toothed or scalloped, and the surfaces range from hairless to bearing stiff, appressed hairs.

The inflorescence is a corymb-shaped cluster with only a few heads; the cluster may be compact or open. Small bracteoles occur at its branches. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
