## Accepted name and placement {#s-0cda2344a9}
In the frozen COL26.8 release dated 20 August 2026, Pseudopaludicola javae Silva, Andrade, Painkow, Dantas, Haga & Garda, 2023 is accepted at species rank. Its recorded placement runs through Anura (order), Leptodactylidae (family) and Pseudopaludicola (genus). This is the classification in that catalogue release; another checklist or fossil database needs its own evidence for name identity.

## Twelve encounters at a wet-soil ecotone {#s-2599ad57b1}
Researchers encountered 12 frogs in March 2022 at Conceição do Araguaia, 217 m above sea level, on saturated soil between a forest patch and seasonally flooded open vegetation. Calls helped locate several. The paper’s cited background describes ground activity in the evening and at night, and records from grass-dominated flooded areas and disturbed settings. The 12 encounters are not a population-density estimate or evidence that every such setting supports breeding.

## Three calls from one male retain their sampling units {#s-85c7f975ee}
A male identified as MPEG 44093 was recorded at 16:34 on 17 March 2022. Three calls contained 11–18 notes made of pulses and were produced at 51.3–55.0 notes per minute. Eighteen measured notes lasted 297–339 ms, with 10–12 pulses per note and 32.2–36.5 pulses per second. Dominant frequency was 4.91–5.21 kHz and matched the fundamental frequency. These measurements describe one male’s recording; the 18 notes are not 18 animals.

## Acoustic and genetic specimens supporting the first Pará record {#s-e3336150b0}
The researchers reported that the call matched the original description. A mitochondrial 16S ribosomal RNA gene sequence from MPEG 44094 differed by 1.3% from the cited type-series sequences OR509824–OR509828; this genetic specimen is distinct from the recorded male MPEG 44093. They treated the combined acoustic and sequence evidence as supporting identification. The locality was reported as the first record from Pará and the northernmost then known, about 183 km north of the type locality, Fazenda Escondida in Tocantins. This extends the documented range at the time of publication but does not show continuous occupancy or movement between sites.

## Marker partitions and correlation labels retain their scope {#s-03f91b7569}
The workbook assigns available 16S records to group 36 and COI records to group 38 in its ASAP sequence partitions. ASAP is a sequence-based method that proposes groupings of specimens; these numbers are local labels and cannot be compared across markers. The Correlation16S-COI column reads True for six records, False for none and NA for three. These are worksheet categories, not correlation coefficients. Since 16S and COI are both mitochondrial markers, their agreement is not independent nuclear evidence or proof of reproductive isolation.
