## Mimosa sensitiva {#s-49f40dc188}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa sensitiva* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry includes several stem-hair states and records recurved prickles in rows. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts); the source lists lanceolate or pappus-like calyx lobes (the outer floral segments) and petal lobes ranging from hairless to hairy. Those alternatives remain as reported.
