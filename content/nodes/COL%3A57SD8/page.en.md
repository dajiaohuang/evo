## Distribution and habitat {#s-4040c00de5}
The current MDD page uses the English display name Sunda Silvery Langur. Akbar and colleagues worked in coastal forest at Gunung Padang, West Sumatra, Indonesia, observing one wild group. A single coastal-forest site cannot summarize the species' island-wide range or other habitats.

## Ecological evidence {#s-5ade3eba21}
The team observed the group for 482 hours from August 2018 to July 2019, recording 74 food items from 37 plant species. Young leaves made up 68.3%; ripe and unripe fruits also formed a large share in some months. The authors interpreted both young leaves and ripe fruit as possible preferred and fallback foods. These data describe seasonal diet in one group at one site, not a fixed species-wide menu.
