## Source classification and concept status {#s-ad1ef3e909}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3219 records Hyperolius tanneri under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-f7db11bc09}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 34 mm for this row. AmphiBIO's Body_size_mm field records maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout-to-vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
