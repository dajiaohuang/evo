## Stem surface {#s-c21fb0e93e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a tomentose stem surface, densely covered with matted hairs that point forward and lie close to the surface. Simple hairs and unbranched bristles are absent; branched bristles are listed as feathery or star-shaped. Glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-e93f2fc444}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are persistent. The record marks extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) and projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) as absent. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is cylindrical. Floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are recorded as smaller or larger than the flowers before they open (anthesis); the field does not resolve these alternatives.

## Flowers {#s-0c2c5688f6}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, is marked as lacking lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and a tomentose covering of dense, matted hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is isostemonous, with stamens equal in number to the petals; the filaments are yellow.

## Habitat category {#s-10602257e1}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not state a collection locality or establish the species’ full range.
