## Xyris spathacea {#s-b70934b83c}
## The habitat fields name both water and land

Xyris spathacea is recorded with a short, 5–20 cm stalk bearing an egg-shaped to spindle-shaped spike. The spike has 2–4 sterile bracts and up to ten flowers. Its habitat entries include both aquatic and terrestrial settings, so the record does not resolve a single habitat type.
