## Bulbine striata {#s-050a006e3c}
Bulbine striata has distinctly striped, translucent leaves and bundled roots surrounded by a corky sheath. Its erect raceme bears pale-yellow flowers; the regional account uses the leaf patterning to separate it from other Bulbine. It grows among quartz pebbles and rocks in well-drained sand on the upper and middle south slope of Groot Pellaberg.
