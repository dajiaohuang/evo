## Maranta pluriflora {#s-06abf6e657}
## A curved corolla tube and two calluses

The flora description gives elliptic-to-oblong leaf blades and sparse hairs on the upper side of the pulvinus. Its bracts are loosely arranged. The corolla tube is curved and shorter than the sepals; the ovary is densely silky-haired with a smooth surface. Two calluses are recorded on the staminode. The fruit is ellipsoid, with persistent upright sepals, and the habitat is labelled terrestrial.
