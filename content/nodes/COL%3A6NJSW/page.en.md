## Accepted name and placement {#s-bfd3c9d7a5}
COL26.8 accepts Kalophrynus orangensis Dutta, Ahmed & Das, 2000 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Microhylidae (family) → Kalophrynus (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## From the type series {#s-98bb377c7b}
This species entered the scientific record from Orang National Park in Assam, where the authors found a member of Kalophrynus not previously reported from India. The type-series adults measured 35–38 mm from snout to vent. The diagnosis combines an acute projecting snout, no parotid gland, an inverted V-shaped mark across the back, an inguinal eye-spot, unwebbed fingers and only rudimentary toe webbing. The series was collected at night on 15 September 1998; the paper does not establish a broader range or detailed life history.

## The type-series boundary {#s-d839c704f9}
The reported morphology and collection event concern the original type series from Orang National Park. They do not establish a population-wide diagnosis, a broader distribution or a detailed life history. Classification records remain a separate source of name and placement information.

## What the type series revealed {#card}
Kalophrynus orangensis was described from Orang National Park, Assam, where the authors first recorded the genus in India; adults in the type series measured 35–38 mm snout-to-vent and had an inverted-V back mark and unwebbed fingers. The paper does not establish a broader range or detailed life history.
