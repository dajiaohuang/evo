## Drosanthemum ramosissimum {#s-8aa4300513}
## Solitary pink flowers rise from a cushion-forming shrub

The Extra Cape flora describes a tufted, cushion-forming succulent shrub. Its semi-cylindrical leaves are 6–10 mm long and up to 2 mm thick, with dense, rounded papillae on the surface. Solitary flowers have pink to magenta petals, and the fruit has five chambers. The habitat record is sand.
