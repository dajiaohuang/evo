## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-029f2d40bc}
Flattened cladodes are recorded, and leaves are mostly absent; when present, they are alternate and oval to elliptic. Small unisexual flower clusters and solitary male or female flowers are listed. Male flowers have six cream or greenish sepals, a segmented nectar disc and three stamens joined into a column. Female flowers have six sepals, reported as cream, green or white, and a shallow dish-shaped nectar disc. The fruit is a flattened spherical capsule; the seed surface is described as warty.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-8ad02536ae}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
