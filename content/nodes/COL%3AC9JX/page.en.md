## Alternanthera pungens {#s-89a4610b89}
## Spiny-tipped leaves and white, papery flowers

Brazilian Flora 2020 records secondary roots and prostrate stems. The source lists petioles as absent or present, blades as elliptic or oblate, and tips as acute or blunt with a sharp, spine-like point. Leaf hairs are present.

The inflorescence is recorded as sessile, with an unbranched side axis; individual flowers have short stalks. Each flower has five three-veined, dry papery tepals and a white perianth. The androecium has five stamens and short pseudostaminodes; the stigma is described as papillose or hairy, and the style is short. The fruit wall is smooth, and the seeds lack lateral projections. The dataset labels the habitat as terrestrial.
