## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Hiraea macrophylla? {#s-da206da3f3}
Elliptic leaves have heart-shaped bases and tips with a short point. Petioles and leaf margins bear glands; the blades may become hairless or remain velvety. Axillary corymbs have flowers with either no glands or eight. Petals have toothed or ragged margins, and the fruit bears kidney-shaped wings.
