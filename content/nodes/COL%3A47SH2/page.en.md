## The name and this data account {#s-f5f12c20b4}
COL26.8 accepts Notharchus ordii in Bucconidae. AVONET v7 links the name to a historical HBW–BirdLife taxonomy entry from December 2020. That row includes 6 measured individuals, 5 with a complete measurement set; the usable count can differ by trait. The row is marked Inference=NO.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-163d086739}
AVONET reports a mean beak length of 23.8 mm from the anterior edge of the nostrils to the tip. Width is 11.4 mm and depth 11.8 mm, both measured at that nostril landmark. These are distinct standardized dimensions, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-81bad38cbc}
Mean unflattened wing length, from the carpal joint to the tip of the longest primary, is 89 mm; mean tarsus length is 15.6 mm. The compiled mean mass is 52.8 g and its source label is Dunning. The morphology sample counts are not assigned to that mass estimate.

## Habitat, feeding and lifestyle {#s-9f4c94ce28}
AVONET assigns Forest habitat, an Invertivore trophic niche and an Insessorial primary lifestyle. In its definitions, Forest means tall tree-dominated vegetation with a more or less closed canopy; Invertivore means at least 60% of food comes from terrestrial invertebrates; and Insessorial describes spending much time perched above ground on vegetation or other raised surfaces. These literature-derived classes summarize a pattern, not exclusive habitat or feeding.

## Movement and geographical summary {#s-d2943a8a8b}
Migration code 1 means sedentary in AVONET. The historical breeding/resident geography has latitude endpoints -13.09° and 5.79°; these do not describe non-breeding movements, a current location or a complete current range. Geography and morphology come from separate compilation steps.

## Evidence limits {#s-16ff479660}
AVONET's measurements are compiled species means, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation; the usable sample count can vary by trait. The Dunning mass estimate has its own source and is not assigned the morphology sample counts. Habitat, trophic niche, lifestyle and migration are categorical summaries, while the latitude endpoints describe historical breeding/resident geography only. The name match and historical crosswalk do not prove that the 2020 taxonomy and COL26.8 represent identical species concepts. Reproduction, current range, population and conservation status need other sources.
