## *Goeppertia oblonga*: flower clusters beside or below the leaves {#s-688eded01b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character record describes spiral leaves on stem or basal shoots; young leaves are not ornamented. Flowering stalks may arise from the leaf-bearing node or from the base. All bracts are fertile and spiral, and each small cluster has two flowers with one membranous bracteole, the small leaf-like structure beneath a flower.

The corolla tube is entire, and the flower has one outer staminode with a petal-like callus. This is a regional morphology entry; it supplies characters but no habitat or locality claim.
