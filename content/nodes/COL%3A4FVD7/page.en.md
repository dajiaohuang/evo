## Pharnaceum detonsum {#s-9fc635bfe0}
## Tiny woody tufts carry long ascending leaves

Pharnaceum detonsum forms small tufts with woody, branched, partly upright stems only 1–5 cm long. Its ascending leaves are 1–1.5 cm long, narrow and rolled, with a short bristle at the tip. The loosely sheathing stipules have one, sometimes two, tooth-like side projections. Umbel-like clusters bear small flowers; the shiny seeds are nearly spherical. The account separates it from P. trigonum by its longer leaves and different stipules. It occurs in open sandy or rocky places in drier regions.
