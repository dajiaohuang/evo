## Ditassa itambensis {#s-91181eac16}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ditassa itambensis* as an accepted species of *Ditassa*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records terrestrial habitat, without a collection locality, survey date, or complete range. No morphology is described in the source row, so the supported information is limited to this regional habitat category.
