## Copaifera coriacea {#s-7d1844e30e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Copaifera coriacea* records two or more trunks growing together, 2–3 leaflet pairs, legumes 1–2 cm long with a white seed covering. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
