## Ayna and Anco forest records {#s-5e040755bf}
The description recorded this frog in secondary montane forest of Ayna and Anco, Ayacucho, Peru, at 1200–2000 m. These sampled areas and elevations do not establish a complete contemporary range or a physiological altitude interval.

## Wet-leaf perches after dusk {#s-3a73611a91}
Frogs were found after 18:00, usually on wet leaves 0.5–1.5 m above ground. The account reported rare male calls that could be masked by louder neighbouring frogs, and encounters near abandoned farms, roads and houses. These observations do not measure call frequency across all populations or demonstrate disturbance tolerance experimentally.
