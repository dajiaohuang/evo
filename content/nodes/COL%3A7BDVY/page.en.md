## *Tachia lancisepala* {#s-9fa427bbd0}
## Winged sepals and a broad funnel

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a square-sided stem and leathery ovate leaves. The calyx lobes are joined to about half the calyx length; each is lance-shaped, tapers to a long point, and has a wing on its outer face. The corolla is broadly funnel-shaped with broad ovate lobes. The entry is one regional morphology field, not a survey of the species’ full range or variation.
