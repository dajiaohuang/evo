## Narrow leaves and pale-pink flowers {#s-f3a48448ea}
Watsonia emiliae is 40–60 cm tall, with narrow linear leaves 4–5 mm wide and pale-pink flowers. Its arcuate stamens have yellow anthers 8–9 mm long.

## Rocky sandstone slopes {#s-95177da7a8}
The source records rocky sandstone slopes as habitat.
