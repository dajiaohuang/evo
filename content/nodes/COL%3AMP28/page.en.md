## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-5f373fd3be}
COL26.8 records Bothrops punctatus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Viperidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-98a2c68760}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1400 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-6719eef76c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Gearty et al. 2021.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-1003fda820}
The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
