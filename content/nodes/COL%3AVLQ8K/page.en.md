## *Eperua glabriflora* {#s-55e89cac85}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records laterally placed, non-leaf-like stipules; two pairs of leaflets that are not sickle-shaped; and a short, upright inflorescence. It also records unequal sepals with nectaries, a regular hypanthium, a long bilaterally symmetric staminal tube, 10 fertile stamens, and a stigma ranging from blunt to head-shaped. These are selected database characters.
