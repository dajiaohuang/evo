## What stem and flower structures are recorded for Euphorbia vauthieriana? {#s-a360754caa}
The cylindrical stems are hairless. Opposite leaves have pinnate veins, toothed margins, and leaf-like stipules between the petioles. Green bracts subtend loose dichasial clusters at lower and upper nodes. The involucre is smooth and top-shaped, with four or more transverse-elliptic glands bearing equal, spreading, petal-like appendages. Female flowers lack a pseudoperianth; the ovary is hairless and styles undivided. The fruit is hairless, and seeds lack a caruncle.
