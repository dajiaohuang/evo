## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora urubiciensis? {#s-fd697753e6}
The cylindrical stem may be herbaceous or partly woody. Hairless leaves have three lobes and smooth margins; petioles bear stalked glands, and stipules are oval-lanceolate. Solitary flowers have similar bracts and awned sepals. The floral cup is bell-shaped, with a three-row corona. The operculum is fringed, the limen cup-shaped, and the reproductive stalk at most 1.5 cm. The berry is hairless; oval seeds have net-like surfaces.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-0e4e8f51ec}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
