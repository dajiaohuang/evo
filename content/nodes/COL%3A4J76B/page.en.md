## Small cream heads in axillary umbels {#s-5654814513}
The morphology record describes cylindrical, scaly branches and alternate, papery elliptic leaves with pointed tips and wedge-shaped bases. Their edges are slightly toothed and the undersides have star-shaped woolly hairs. Axillary umbels carry three to ten short-stalked heads, each with five to ten cream florets. The fruits are three-angled and glandular, with a two-row, inconspicuous pappus. The habitat field says terrestrial.
