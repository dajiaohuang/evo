## Cavendishia allenii A.C.Sm. {#s-a364cd7bd4}
## Persistent bracts enclose tightly packed flowers

Cavendishia allenii is a glabrous epiphytic shrub up to 1 m tall. Its elliptic to ovate-elliptic leaves have rounded or slightly heart-shaped bases. Compact racemes bear eight to sixteen flowers and are surrounded at the base by conspicuous, striate bracts in rose, salmon pink or pale green; the bracts persist as the berry develops. The tubular corolla is pale green with a yellow tip. The berry is about 15 mm across. The species is reported as endemic to central Panama.
