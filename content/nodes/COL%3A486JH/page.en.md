## A diagnostic comparison with the El Quimi glassfrog {#s-65e7d3f619}
The diagnosis describes occasional black dorsal flecks, black flecks on dorsal legs, pale-green digits and translucent esophageal/stomach peritonea in cristinae, versus an unmarked green dorsum, yellow digits and white peritonea in dajomesae. The cited adult-male SVL comparison is26.0–31.1mm versus20.8–27.2mm.
