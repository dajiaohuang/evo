## Habenaria dives {#s-102f6f78f0}
The South African account describes Habenaria dives as a terrestrial herb up to 0.6 m tall, with white flowers whose sepals have green veins. Its spur is 8–15 mm long. The account notes variation in the petals and lip lobes, but says the lower petal lobe remains shorter and usually broader than the upper; it distinguishes the species from H. falcicornis by its denser inflorescence, smaller flowers and shorter spurs. Well-drained grassland is recorded habitat.
