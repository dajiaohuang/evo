## Source classification and concept status {#s-ec62b9f2bf}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3076 records *Afrixalus quadrivittatus* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-a92a5edf0d}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-74d30bc1f1}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; daytime activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-3c00105226}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and warm conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-bfed528218}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 32 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-1bad030c42}
AmphiBIO reports both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch as 50, and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. The row does not report an offspring-size value. These counts are literature-compiled; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-a7a9cceb44}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
