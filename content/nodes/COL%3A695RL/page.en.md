## Burmeistera kirkbridei {#s-13e9bec030}
## Dark purple flowers sit among hairless leaves

A historical Flora of Panama account describes a small shrub with round, hairless, purplish stems. Its elliptic leaves are 3.5–7 cm long and 1.5–3.5 cm wide, with acute to long-pointed tips and wedge-shaped bases. Both leaf surfaces and the petioles are hairless; the margins have faint small teeth and about 2–4 callus-like raised points per centimetre.

Flowers grow singly in upper leaf axils on ascending stalks about 3 cm long. Each flower is 1.2–1.5 cm long. Its base is obconic and about 3 mm long; the sepals are ovate to oblong-elliptic and about 3.5 mm long. The dark purplish corolla has dense, very short stiff hairs and is reportedly pink-tipped. The account gives fruits and seeds as unknown.
