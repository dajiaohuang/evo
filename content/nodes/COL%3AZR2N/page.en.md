## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton salutaris? {#s-cad483ef47}
Young branches have scale-like hairs. The long-stalked oval or heart-shaped leaves have sparse or scale-like hairs above and scale-like hairs beneath, looping side veins, and entire margins. A stalked nectar gland occurs near the top of the leaf stalk; young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have bisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are stalked; male flowers are bell-shaped. Female sepals are fused, the style has four lobes, and calyx lobes are alike. Female petals are reduced to glands, with a quincuncial overlap pattern in the bud. The fruit columella has weakly distinct apical lobes.
