## Dasyphyllum fodinarum {#s-f05a6b2ceb}
## Paired curved spines and solitary bell-shaped heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a monoecious plant, with male and female flowers on the same individual. Its branches trail or climb and bear paired, curved spines. Spaced leaves have stalks and papery, elliptic to broadly elliptic blades, with a sharp spine-like tip. The underside is softly hairy, and several main veins rise from the leaf base.

Flower heads grow singly. Each has a bell-shaped involucre, with brown to straw-coloured bracts that end in a short point or awn. The ligule is as long as its corolla tube, and the lobes have long hairs. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
