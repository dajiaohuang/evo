## A mat of papillate branches with solitary flowers {#s-6e41b87bce}
This account describes a prostrate or sprawling, mat-forming shrublet with papillate branches up to 15 cm long. The cylindrical leaves also bear papillae. Magenta flowers grow singly on short shoots and are up to 25 mm across. The five-chambered fruits have flat bases, low rims and false partitions. It grows in open karroid vegetation on gravelly loam.
