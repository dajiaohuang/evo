## *Selaginella parkeri*: jointed stems and upper-side rhizophores {#s-1880a7142d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile describes pale, hairless stems that may lie along the ground or stand upright; unlike several unjointed stem records, this one notes joints. Root-bearing rhizophores are placed on the upper side. The small leaves differ by position: side leaves are oblong-lanceolate with asymmetrical bases, while upper leaves are narrowly elliptic and taper from asymmetrical bases. Axillary leaves are elliptic with blunt bases and pointed tips.

The habitat field says terrestrial, without a named locality or survey date. These characters summarize a regional flora record.
