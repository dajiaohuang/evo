## Couratari macrosperma A.C.Sm. {#s-1a0438e890}
## Spiny yellow hood and bell-shaped fruit

Couratari macrosperma has flaky bark and papery leaves that remain on the plant while it flowers. The leaves are softly hairy, longitudinally striated and serrated or crenate. Racemes bear flowers with a finely woolly hypanthium and white to pale-yellow petals. The yellow androecial hood is spiny. The fruit is bell-shaped with a round cross-section and crusty outer surface; it lacks a stalk, and the lid has a convex tip. The seeds have circular wings.
