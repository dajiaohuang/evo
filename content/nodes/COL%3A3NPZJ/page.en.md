## What stem, leaf and flower traits are recorded for Hypericum piriai? {#s-ba37f41c07}
Side branches arise near the stem base. Leaves are opposite or in whorls of three, with separate blades rolled under at the edges; margins are not thickened. The main veins run lengthwise. Large flowers, over 7 mm wide, occur singly or in cymose clusters at branch tips, and singly along the sides. Their reduced bracts, unequal sepals and asymmetrical petals are recorded. Stamens form three bundles; the anthers lack an elongated connective, and there are three styles. The capsule is ovoid, and the black seeds have ladder-like ridges on the coat.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-2f33c80cb0}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
