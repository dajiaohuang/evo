## Spinose stems and small fruits {#s-47f1d0d518}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records spines and no tendrils. Leaves are sessile or have petioles up to about 1 mm long, with three veins; the underside vein axils are not bearded. Terminal thyrsoid inflorescences are short-racemose. The corolla is rotate or short-funnel-shaped, with lobes longer than the tube; the ovary and style are hairless. Fruits are 2–3 cm in diameter, with a leathery fruit wall 0.5–1 mm thick. The source records one or two seeds with a crust-like coat.
