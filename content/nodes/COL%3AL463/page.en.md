## Lobed leaves and a splitting calyx {#s-b3a416a604}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes lobed, leathery or partly leathery leaves with a heart-shaped base and rounded lobes. The lower surface is recorded as woolly or long-haired, with raised veins and abundant glands. At flowering, the calyx splits into two to five lobes; the petals are linear and the fruit is an elastically opening legume. Several traits appear as alternatives in the source, so the entry should not be read as a single specimen showing every state.
