## Source classification and concept status {#s-742bdaacdd}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3156 records *Hyperolius langi* under the source classification Hyperolius (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal) {#s-105bf53d3f}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial, aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-38120f5b2c}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; daytime activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-89f1000376}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and warm conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-8c06a0b76e}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 31 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-d26b8ff24c}
AmphiBIO reports both minimum and maximum numbers of offspring or eggs per clutch as 56, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and both minimum and maximum offspring or egg size as 1.6 mm. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. Its size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-485a6f0e94}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
