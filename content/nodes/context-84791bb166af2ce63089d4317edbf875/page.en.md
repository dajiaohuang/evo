## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-46cf58dccf}
AmphiBIO gives a maximum adult body length of 29 mm. For Anura, length means snout–vent length. This compiled maximum is not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-5e128b2ecd}
The compilation gives 74–400 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to one reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 1 mm. These are literature-derived counts and extrema. The row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value; size may refer to offspring or eggs.
