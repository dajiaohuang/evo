## Leaves and stipules {#s-c9d3549e2a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes unifoliolate leaves, each with one leaflet. The leaflet is lanceolate, oval or oblong, with an entire margin. Its surface is listed as either glabrous or hairy on both sides, an unresolved alternative in this record. The free distal portion of the stipule is entered as 0 mm; the field does not explain this value.

## Flower clusters {#s-ac892d24ad}
The inflorescence bracts are recorded as deciduous and up to 10 mm long.

## Flower color {#s-dc30014bda}
Flower color is recorded as purple, lilac or blue.

## Habitat category {#s-bfdeb77538}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category is not a collection locality or a statement of the species’ full range.
