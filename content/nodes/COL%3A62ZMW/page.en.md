## Ochthomantis is one studied part of this genus {#s-be6c6ec4eb}
The 2023 revision recognizes eleven species in the studied subgenus Ochthomantis: five previously recognized species, two resurrected names, catalai and poissoni, and four new species, danieli, macrotympanum, olgae and tavaratra. Type examination, morphology and mitochondrial 16S comparisons support that revision; greater-than-3% distances are part of its argument, not a universal species threshold. These eleven species are not the whole Mantidactylus genus. Majori placement remains an unresolved comparison: the paper excludes majori morphologically while recovering it as the molecular sister of macrotympanum and recommending broader sampling. No catalogue hierarchy is replaced by this source hypothesis.

## Water-edge records retain species and sex context {#s-6b4f3ce99c}
The selected species accounts describe different water-edge settings and encounter substrates. Catalai was recorded in holes and rock interstices near water; femoralis included streamside shrubs, with differing male and female heights; mocquardi was described as apparently more diurnal, including degraded-forest records. Macrotympanum observations were entirely male, while tavaratra included both adult night records and juvenile daytime encounters. These source-specific examples do not establish a single nocturnal schedule, perch height or disturbance tolerance for the genus. Encounter sites are observational evidence rather than controlled preference measurements, and forest or plantation occurrence does not demonstrate successful reproduction there.
