## What shape are its leaves and bladder traps? {#s-dad811b5f8}
Utricularia guyanensis is recorded with simple, linear leaves and ovoid bladder traps. The trap opening is lateral and no appendages are recorded. Its yellow corolla has a smooth lower lip and a spur reduced to a short rounded tip. The capsule opens lengthwise and the seeds are ovoid. These are traits in the source description, not a survey of variation.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-9669e8ce38}
The habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial. It gives no collection locality or full distribution.
