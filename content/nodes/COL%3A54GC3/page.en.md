## Tacinga braunii {#s-42170a0233}
## Flattened stem pads reach two metres or more

The recorded plant reaches 1–2 m or more and has flattened cladodes, the pad-like stem segments of this cactus. Its areoles are unarmed and spaced 15–20 mm apart; growth is recorded as indeterminate. The corolla is greenish, with perianth segments bent back. The fruit is green inside. Habitat labels include rocky and terrestrial settings, but no locality is given.
