## Diagnostic features of a cloud forest frog {#s-b5bc7f6ae0}
P. chocolatebari was described from cloud forests of Colombia's northern Central Cordillera. Its diagnosis includes a triangular vomerine dentigerous process, projecting snout, smooth dorsum, vocal slits and sac, and yellow groin and hidden thigh surfaces. The authors caution against using genetic distance alone for species delimitation.
