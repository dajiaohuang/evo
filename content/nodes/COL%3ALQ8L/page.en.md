## What leaf and flower details are recorded for Bia fallax? {#s-3e6785f166}
The leaves are oval or elliptic, with heart-shaped or arrow-heart-shaped bases, a straight sinus between the basal lobes, long tail-like tips, and irregular teeth. Five veins arise from the base, with secondary veins forming a partly looping pattern near the margin. Male flowers have three sepals; their floral disc has six glands arranged in two whorls. Female sepals are lance-shaped or oblanceolate.
