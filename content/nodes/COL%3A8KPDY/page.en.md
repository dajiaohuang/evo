## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-9fd65ac730}
COL26.8 records Boiga melanota as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-d4c12fa292}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cox et al. 2012.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-76cc045a31}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Cox et al. 2012.
