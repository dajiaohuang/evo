## Distinguish the measured length {#s-6c46f42a95}
The compiled entry reports a maximum total length (TL) of 262 mm. TL includes the tail; snout–vent length (SVL) ends at the vent. This is a recorded maximum, not typical size or an absolute limit, and sex-specific values from separate sources do not measure dimorphism within a population.
