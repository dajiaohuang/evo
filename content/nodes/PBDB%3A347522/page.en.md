## An articulated skeleton and recurved teeth {#s-522cc73111}
The holotype ULBRA-PVT280 includes a partial skull, axial skeleton, forelimb and nearly complete left hind limb. Its preserved teeth curve backward and carry serrations. These are observations from the named specimen; the character-based study used the teeth to test a possible animal-eating diet, but the fossil does not record what this individual ate.

The study's morphology matrix recovered Buriolestes among sauropodomorphs, but a position in a sampled tree does not establish direct ancestry.
