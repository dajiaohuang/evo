## Evolutionary evidence {#s-a73133dc66}
A mitochondrial-DNA Bayesian skyline analysis of a tentative Upper Subansiri population inferred expansion after the Last Glacial Maximum, around 15,000 years before present. Ten of twelve old hunting-trophy skin samples were successfully sequenced. This is a model inference for one geographic population, not a census or species-wide history.

## Evolutionary evidence {#s-c0c6ae1adc}
In the same study's approximate Bayesian computation, a decline scenario for the tentative Upper Subansiri population best fit the model, estimating an approximately 30-fold reduction in effective population size around 3,500 years ago. This is a historical model inference based on a small sample and assumptions; unsampled migration could not be excluded, and climate or human causes were not established.
