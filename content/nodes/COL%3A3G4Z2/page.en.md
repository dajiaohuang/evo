## Long tubes with red-streaked tepals {#s-69b2e094b7}
Gladiolus sekukuniensis grows 600–1,100 mm tall and bears white to cream or pale salmon-pink flowers with narrow red streaks. The tube is 25–35 mm long and exceeds the bracts (modified, leaf-like structures associated with flowers or flower clusters); scaly corm (a swollen underground storage stem) suckers distinguish it from G. permeabilis.

## Open woodland on rocky alkaline slopes {#s-32761bd555}
The source records open woodland on rocky dolomitic or alkaline slopes.
