This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-b0ac26d57b}

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-08d98f82f6}

The sex-determining-mechanism field records TSD. ReptTraits attributes this field to Nemesházi & Bókony 2023.
