## Unusual green and turquoise flowers {#s-3a8b670888}
Ixia viridiflora reaches 50–100 cm and bears widely spaced, large flowers, usually green to turquoise with a maroon or nearly black centre. The long anthers are usually 9–13 mm; the source notes that some populations have smaller flowers and paler centres, an unresolved variation.

## Mostly rocky clay slopes {#s-679d2f4c29}
The regional account records mostly rocky clay slopes.
