## Hong Kong genetic structure is one species-specific example {#s-1b4876294c}
A 2025 study of hongkongensis genotyped 310 animals from ten Hong Kong streams sampled in 2013–2014. Seven microsatellite loci remained after two of nine amplified loci were excluded. The principal four-cluster result included admixture, and some alternative nine-cluster support was reported. Genetic differentiation and heterozygosity describe those historical markers and sites, not whole-genome diversity, abundance or a genus-wide species boundary. ## Management proposals do not establish dispersal or urban tolerance {#s-13ffe8e84c}
The hongkongensis paper recommends considering genetic composition in translocation and treating principal clusters as management units. It did not test a relocation protocol, road-barrier effects or a causal impact of urbanization. Earlier breeding-pool fidelity and approximately 0.4-kilometre land movement are cited background, not movements measured from its tail clips. Proposed explanations for unusual cluster assignments include poorly documented releases, without established origins.
