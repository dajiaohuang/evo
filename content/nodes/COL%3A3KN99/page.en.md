## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e00ce45279}
COL26.8 records Hemidactylus citernii as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Gekkonidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-4e369885f7}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 37.8 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-a9a03a88b7}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-56dae349b6}
The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
