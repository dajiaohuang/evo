## The source concept {#s-3ca1be9cf5}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Odontophrynus maisuma* as row Anf1386, in the genus Odontophrynus, family Cycloramphidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The values below belong to this deposited row; equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging strata {#s-2dde9af85d}
The row records terrestrial (Ter = 1) as a broad foraging category. The categories do not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology.

## Seasonal activity {#s-e19393b2d0}
The row records wet and warm (Wet_warm = 1) as a seasonal activity category. AmphiBIO relates activity conditions to the local annual climate, so these labels do not correspond to fixed calendar months.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-6147aef9dd}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length of 43.6 mm. For anurans, body length means snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-efd322f3c4}
The row records 2980 as the minimum and 2980 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch. The row records minimum and maximum offspring or egg sizes of 1.1 and 1.1 mm. These are literature-compiled extrema or counts; the row does not specify sex, population, measurement date, or wild versus captive conditions for each value.
