## Bulb-based glandular hairs on the leaves {#s-360cdfc76f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists 3–4 leaflet pairs, with a parenthetical “(2)” retained in the source entry, and describes the asymmetric leaflets as oval to elliptic. It records bulb-based glandular hairs on the plant and yellow flowers with 10 stamens. Pod and seed form are marked unknown, not absent. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. This regional description gives no sampling frame, locality, survey date, or wild/cultivated status.
