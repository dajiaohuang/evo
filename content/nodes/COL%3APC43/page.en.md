## Form in the flora record {#s-a5b22866cc}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a globose stem. The leaves are peltate, with membranous blades that are ovate or oblong, cordate or sagittate at the base, and green with whitish spots; posterior lobes are present, and the midrib is whitish. Petioles are cylindrical and marked as having neither spots nor stripes.

Several structures are explicitly marked unknown in the field: the spathe blade and tube, peduncle, sympodium, female and male flower zones, and berry.

The regional dataset row cites Camelo and Coelho's 2020 Caladium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
