## What range of leaf, flower and fruit traits is recorded for Heteropterys coriacea? {#s-7c0a9cd1ae}
The opposite leaves are leathery and round to heart-shaped, with tips ranging from pointed to rounded or cuspidate. Both surfaces are hairless. Conspicuous glands lie just inside the rolled-under margin. Upright inflorescences have persistent oval bracts and stalked pedicels. Sepals may curl back, and the front sepal may be the only one without glands; the sepals can bear rusty hairs. Yellow petals are clawed, unscented and thin, with edges ranging from smooth to ragged; they may reflex or spread. Anthers are upright and hairy. Styles are straight, hairless and diverge toward the tip, which may be pointed or hooked. The seed-bearing fruit body has conspicuous side veins.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-f4a1ff5320}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
