## How many flowers sit in each female spikelet? {#s-443b3f3e35}
A 2012 regional account describes *Elegia aggregata* as a robust, tufted, dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants) perennial reaching 2 m; its leaf sheaths fall away. Each female spikelet, a compact flower cluster, bears three or four flowers, each 3.5–5.0 mm long. The account gives no dated sampling frame or frequency for these traits.

## Which high ridges are named? {#s-39f3a009ee}
The flora account lists rocky sandstone ridges at 1,000–1,400 m. This regional habitat note does not establish a complete range.
