## The source tadpole concept {#s-db8da14996}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Leptodactylodon boulengeri, in the genus Leptodactylodon and family Arthroleptidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying works is also Leptodactylodon boulengeri. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-b39dde3c79}
The compilation has two records transcribed from literature and none measured from images. Their supplied developmental-stage labels are 25 and 37. These labels belong to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations; two records are not necessarily two unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
