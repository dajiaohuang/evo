For Oswaldocruzia mazzai, the review replaces an old Bufo sp./Bufo marinus type-host attribution with Rhinella diptycha because the original record is Argentinean and R. marina is not considered present there by the authors. This is the review's inference from geography and the marina-group taxonomy, not an independently demonstrated reidentification of the original host voucher. It must be distinguished from the other named diptycha records and from parasite material later reassigned from O. mazzai to O. proencai in Paraguayan hosts.
