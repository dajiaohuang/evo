## Leaves and flowers {#s-9153e6a6db}
Snijman's 2012 regional account describes a slender, corm-bearing geophyte 25–35 cm tall, with a fibrous neck. It has 4–6 leaves, occasionally 8. They are nearly upright, clasp at the base to form a short false stem, and are linear with ribs on the upper surface. The leaves are as long as or longer than the inflorescence; the midrib and margins are fringed with thick, short, white hairs. There are 4 or 5 yellow flowers, occasionally 7.

These measurements and traits come from a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
