## What does the description say about this olive and its flowers? {#s-7b91dfdb51}
Olea capensis is described as either a shrub or a tree up to 12 m tall, with elliptic-ovate leaves. Its small white flowers grow in terminal panicles, branched clusters at the ends of shoots.

## What habitats are named? {#s-1a61948557}
The habitat entry lists forests and scrub for Olea capensis. It gives no particular collection site or complete range.

## Which close relatives does the account name? {#s-f70a1c277a}
The regional taxonomic account describes Olea capensis as closely related to the tropical African species O. welwitschii, O. hochstetteri and O. guineensis. It does not give a dated sampling frame or population frequency for that comparison.
