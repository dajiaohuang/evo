## Pyrrhobryum spiniforme: four recorded substrate categories {#s-ab29b23d46}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists “corticícola,” a bark-associated substrate category, for *Pyrrhobryum spiniforme*.

Another entry uses “epixila,” the wood-associated category.

A third records “rupícola,” the rock-associated category.

The fourth is “terrícola,” the terrestrial category.

These are separate labels in a regional flora dataset; they do not show how often each substrate is used. The fields give no collection locality or date and do not establish the species’ complete range.
