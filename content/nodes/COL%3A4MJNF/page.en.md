## Accepted name and placement {#s-6c4bdeb473}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis sagittulus (Lehr, Aguilar & Duellman, 2004) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Compiled adult dimensions — AmphiBIO source {#s-e9e6c52dc9}
Historical source concept: Pristimantis sagittulus, under the compilation's Frost 2011 classification. Equivalence with the current species concept remains unverified. AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 29.9 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.

## A dated Junín range-extension record {#s-672d4cf961}
The 2020 study reported a Pui Pui Protected Forest record approximately 65 km southeast of the species’ type locality and described it as the first record for Peru’s Junín region. This is a study-attributed regional extension, without establishing continuous occupancy across that distance, a complete current distribution or biological traits absent from the short account.
