## Distinguish the measured length {#s-c2f0cd7ddc}
The recorded maximum straight carapace length (SCL) is 350 mm. SCL measures the shell in a straight line, excluding the head, neck and tail. This is a maximum in the reported material, not a theoretical limit or typical individual size. Sex-specific maxima, when available, may come from different localities or studies and cannot alone quantify sexual dimorphism across a population.

## Where and how it is active {#s-9dcf335b67}
The row classifies the microhabitat as Aquatic, diet as Carnivorous and foraging mode as active (ACT). These broad categories are not a complete habitat or food inventory or a fixed schedule. The diet category does not identify particular prey, and the one-row summary cannot show differences among populations, seasons or life stages.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-3a3d014fce}
The entry records an oviparous reproductive mode. No clutch or litter number is interpreted here. The mode label does not establish fetal nutrition or show that all populations share the same reproductive pattern.
