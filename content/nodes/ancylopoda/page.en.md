## Ancylopoda in a basal perissodactyl matrix {#ancylopoda-matrix}
A cladistic analysis of basal perissodactyls includes ancylopods among the sampled early branches and tests their placement relative to Tapiromorpha and Hippomorpha. This is a character-matrix hypothesis, not a direct-ancestor claim. The cited analysis does not audit numerical range endpoints, so it does not establish an age span for Ancylopoda.
