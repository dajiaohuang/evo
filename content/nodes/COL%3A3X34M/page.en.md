## *Macrocarpaea neblinae* {#s-258f3515e4}
## Green funnel-shaped flowers

The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a cylindrical stem and papery, elliptic to ovate leaves. Their bases are blunt and symmetric, their tips acute or tapering to a point, and both surfaces are hairless. The bracts are short-stalked, ovate to elliptic, with rounded or blunt symmetric bases and pointed tips. Calyx lobes are ovate or oblong with blunt ends; the calyx surface is recorded as hairless or finely warty. The corolla is green and funnel-shaped. These alternatives are retained as recorded; one regional field does not establish their frequency or population-level variation.
