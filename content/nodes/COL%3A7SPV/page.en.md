## overview {#s-0befa69166}
This catalogue places Baird’s tapir in this genus. The biological account uses Tapirus bairdii. Its proboscis and leaf-feeding observations concern that species; the older genus usage is not imposed on the entire family.

## catalogue-profile / limitations {#s-4c4bc6086e}
Source-linked introductory account;. Quantitative traits, complete ranges, divergence dates and current conservation assessments require separate evidence. Catalogue counts are not counts of living wild biological species.
