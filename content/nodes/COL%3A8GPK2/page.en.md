## Name and linked traits {#s-f10567af51}
COL26.8 accepts Cyrtodactylus lenya in Gekkonidae. ReptTraits v1-2 links this name to overall and female maximum SVL, a saxicolous microhabitat and oviparity, with separate citations under the workbook’s Reptile Database 2023 framework. The row consolidates reports and does not describe every population.

## Two reported maxima, both measured to the vent {#s-34f31e1d86}
The overall maximum is 74 mm SVL and the female maximum is 69.2 mm. Snout–vent length measures from snout to vent and excludes the tail. Compiled maxima are not typical sizes; the sex-specific values may come from different samples, so their difference alone does not establish sexual dimorphism across the species.

## Rock-associated habitat is listed {#s-857bf6b13c}
The microhabitat category is saxicolous, or associated with rocky settings. The label does not identify a particular rock type, locality or frequency of use. Diet and active time are not interpreted here, so no prey or schedule follows from the habitat entry.

## An egg-laying mode without a clutch count {#s-c637123cdd}
The reproductive-mode field says oviparous. No supported mean or range for clutch size is presented in this account, leaving egg number and laying frequency unspecified. The label does not show that every population has the same reproductive pattern.

## What the row can and cannot show {#s-c5946f393b}
The ReptTraits v1-2 record retains its Reptile Database 2023 name framework; matching names do not prove that this older concept is equivalent to pinned COL26.8. Its one-row summary also leaves variation among sexes, ages, sites and populations unresolved. Missing fields do not support claims about mass, juvenile length, venom, conservation, present range or population trends.
