## Long yellow flowers above strandveld openings {#s-5dea1ab14f}
This creeping succulent shrub has internodes 20–30 mm long and erect, nearly cylindrical leaves more than 60 mm long. Its yellow flowers are 40–45 mm across; the fruit has 15 or 16 chambers and small closing bodies. The record places it in open patches among shrubs in northern strandveld.
