## Leaflets, flowers and fruit {#s-d2d172a558}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset row records 13–25 leaflets, sometimes up to 29. They are oval, oblong or elliptic, with blunt tips and rounded bases; the inflorescences are panicles. The flowers are purple, with ten stamens described as diadelphous (their filaments are joined in two bundles). The fruit is oblong to elliptic, and its surface venation is recorded as uniform; anther opening is listed as unknown. This is one regional record, not a global description or a measure of the species’ full variation.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-477f079ccf}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. The field supplies a habitat category, but no locality, distribution, collection date or sampling coverage.
