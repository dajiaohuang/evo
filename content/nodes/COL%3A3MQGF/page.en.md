## Hortia excelsa {#s-93a61f0b70}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records sessile leaves hairy on both surfaces and a four- or five-part perianth.

## Sessile leaves and a variable perianth count {#s-55e197eccb}
Leaves are recorded as smooth-surfaced and hairy on both the upper and lower surfaces; petioles are absent. The perianth is four- or five-part, and petals bear hairs on the ventral surface. The ecology field classifies the setting as “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or complete range.
