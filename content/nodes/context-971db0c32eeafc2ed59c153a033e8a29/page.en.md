## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-641eb28fc8}
For Telmatobius brevipes, AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 74.6 mm. In this compilation, body length means snout–vent length for frogs and toads (Anura), and total length for salamanders (Caudata) and caecilians (Gymnophiona). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
