## The source tadpole concept {#s-01c30e66ef}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files these records under *Ololygon littoralis* in *Ololygon*, Hylidae, following its recorded use of Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). The source spellings are kept separately: Scinax littoralis. This is the dataset’s filing choice; by itself it does not establish that the source concept is identical to a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-eacd60af34}
TadpoleTraits version 4 lists **1 literature row** and **1 image-derived row** for this source concept. The developmental labels are **37; 34/35 (Judging based on the picture)**, kept as supplied, including any ranges or image-based notes. These are data entries, not a count of individual tadpoles; sample-size fields stay with their own rows and are not added across studies. The source readme does not confirm the units for its measurement columns, so this page does not present those numbers as absolute lengths. A stage marked as judged from a picture remains an image-derived inference, not a stage directly documented in the literature.
