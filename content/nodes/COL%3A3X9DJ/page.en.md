## ## Large flowers on head-like racemes {#s-63a3a9dc1b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records glabrous, unarmed cylindrical branches and alternate bipinnate leaves with one to three pairs of pinnae. The proximal and distal leaflets are described as different in form, with two to four per pinna. Axillary, simple, head-like racemes have globose to ellipsoid receptacles. The recorded flowers have a green to whitish-green calyx 19–30 mm long and a white, funnel-shaped corolla 25–35 mm long with distinct veins. Numerous stamens arise in a tube attached to the corolla; their filaments are white, becoming pink toward the tips. The entry also records compressed follicles, usually single and rarely in pairs, and dark-brown, elliptic to oblong seeds. These are regional dataset fields, not a quantified study of variation.
