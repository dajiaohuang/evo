## *Restio bifurcus*: a low, sparsely branched restio {#s-3cf6b49cdb}
This tufted perennial reaches about 40 cm. Its stems branch sparsely and have fine wrinkles; the regional account notes roughly ten flower-bearing spikelets. Male and female flowers grow on separate plants.

The species is recorded on rocky slopes that are often damp, a habitat description limited to these reported sites.
