## The source concept {#s-5222be3645}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Astylosternus schioetzi in row Anf0172, with genus Astylosternus, family Arthroleptina and order Anura. The source says its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); a link to a current COL26.8 concept has not been established. The traits below describe this archived row, not a new species assessment.

## Foraging strata {#s-399db7a01b}
The row marks Ter=1, Aqu=1 and Arb=1 in its foraging-stratum fields. These are recorded categories, not measures of how often or how long an individual used each stratum; this row does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries, but this row has not been compared field by field. Treat these as archived values awaiting row-level review.

## Compiled life history {#s-5018428831}
AmphiBIO records Reproductive_output_y=1, interpreted in its literature compilation as a maximum of one reproductive event per year. The row does not identify the population, sex, observation date or wild-versus-captive conditions behind that maximum, so it cannot be read as a schedule for every population.

## Developmental strategy {#s-461fbc5e79}
The row marks Lar=1. AmphiBIO labels this as a larval stage. This flag does not describe parental care, breeding location or timing.
