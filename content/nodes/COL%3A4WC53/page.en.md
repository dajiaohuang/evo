## *Selaginella monticola*: cream megaspores and orange microspores {#s-47f404a7ac}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes prostrate stems without hairs or joints; root-bearing rhizophores are listed on the lower side, in the leaf axils or on the upper side. The small leaves differ by position: side leaves are ovate to broadly ovate with asymmetrical bases, while upper leaves are round to broadly elliptic. Axillary leaves are ovate, with rounded or heart-shaped bases.

The record gives the megaspores as cream and the microspores as orange. Its alternative leaf states are regional character entries, not a measured account of population variation.
