## Two habitat labels in a regional record {#s-86568b8733}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Mittenothamnium macrodontium (Hornsch.) Cardot as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data. Its habitat labels are source categories, not specific collection locations.

One category is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

A second category is “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. The record gives no collection locality, survey date or complete range.

The first habitat field supplies no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

The second habitat field likewise supplies no collection locality, survey date, range or sampling coverage.

These two source-labelled habitat categories are the only biological content in this regional record; they do not make a complete account of the species.
