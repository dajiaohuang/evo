## Fissidens zollingeri: four habitat entries {#s-5975de544b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records four habitat categories for this moss:

“Corticícola,”

“Epífita,”

“Rupícola,”

and “Terrícola.”

These are source labels from four regional dataset rows; none gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
