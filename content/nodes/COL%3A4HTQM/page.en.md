## The single original specimen of the name kabambarei {#s-6754f15c38}
Under the original name Colobus (Tropicolobus) kabambarei Matschie, 1914, the catalogue records holotype by monotypy ZMB 32735/32736, old number A 48,09,92: an adult female represented by skull and skin, collected by Grauer near Kabambare in present-day Democratic Republic of the Congo in October 1908. The skin was not found according to the catalogue, so its registration is not confirmation of current presence. The catalogue places kabambarei under its valid name Procolobus foai. This is not an account of the original foai 1899 holotype and does not independently settle this page’s Piliocolobus genus treatment.

## Two part numbers do not mean two animals {#s-c54317a47b}
The kabambarei skull and skin numbers belong to one individual. Holotype by monotypy indicates an original description based on a single specimen, not an ecological survey of foai. “Skin not found” is the 2024 catalogue’s collection report, without a fresh live inventory check here. Its collection locality supplies no modern abundance, diet or home-range measurements. Field reading should retain separate evidence for the historical name, its type material and the pinned catalogue species.

## Overview {#s-0152fd9362}
Hart, Ting and Maisels's 2020 assessment treats Foa's red colobus as a Democratic Republic of the Congo montane lineage and reports only two isolated locations in the evidence available to that assessment. The 2021 red-colobus action plan places its boundaries near other Central African forms and calls for monitoring and genetic work. These are dated syntheses of uneven survey evidence, not a new range survey or a present-day population count.
