## *Mesosphaerum caatingense* {#s-efe19a2fb0}
The field describes toothed leaf margins and a flat upper leaf surface. The inflorescence stalk is up to 10 mm long; the cluster is a one-sided coiled cyme, not globose. At flowering the calyx is up to 3 mm and the corolla tube exceeds 2 mm; the fruiting calyx is up to 5.5 mm. These measurements are from the source entry.
