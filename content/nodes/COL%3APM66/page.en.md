## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-49323da3ce}
The stems have rusty, powdery star-shaped hairs and no papillae. Leaves may be simple or weakly three-lobed, broadly to narrowly ovate or elliptic, with heart-shaped, rounded or blunt bases and toothed to scalloped edges. Flowers grow singly or two to three together and stand upright. The cup-shaped calyx has raised veins but no papillae; its lobes taper to pointed tips. Petals are pink or lilac. The fruit bears 10–15 segments (mericarps), without tip-awns; their dorsal seams are smooth or have short papillae.
