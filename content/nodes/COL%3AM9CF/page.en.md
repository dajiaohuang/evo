## Opposite and alternate leaves {#s-3fc8dfaa9b}
The source describes an erect herb up to 1.5 m, with simple or branched stems. Leaves are opposite on the stem but alternate on branches, and may be narrowly lanceolate, ovate-lanceolate or ovate; the stated distribution spans eastern Canada, the eastern and southern United States, Mexico to Panama, the West Indies and southeastern Brazil.
