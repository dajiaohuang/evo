## Stem surface {#s-4093788915}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes the stem as pubescent or hispid (hairy to bristly), but does not specify hair direction. Simple hairs and fine, thread-like unbranched bristles are present. Branched bristles, glandular hairs, prickles, and short lateral shoots called brachyblasts are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-1c41a8e2a7}
Stipules, small appendages at leaf bases, persist. Extrafloral nectaries (glands outside flowers) and projections between pinna pairs are absent. The flower clusters are globose, and their floral bracts are shorter than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-6de169949c}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The calyx is paleaceous, or thin and papery, and its lobes are pappus-like, with a bristle- or scale-like form. Corolla lobes have one to three veins and are pilose (hairy). The androecium (the stamens collectively) is isostemonous, arranged in one stamen whorl; the filaments are pink.

## Substrate {#s-9e22790685}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, meaning it grows on land. The field does not provide a more specific habitat or collection locality.
