## Single-leaflet leaves and segmented fruit {#s-fffb46c459}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records a xylopodium, a woody underground structure, as present. The stems are erect and cylindrical; the branches are described as uncinate, or hooked. Leaves are alternate and unifoliolate (each has one leaflet). That leaflet may be ovate, linear, oblanceolate or elliptic, with secondary veins that curve and join near the edge. Stipules persist, remain free, and are neither ear-shaped nor stem-clasping. Flower clusters are pseudo-racemes, or raceme-like; the flowers are lilac, and their stamens are described as pseudo-monadelphous, with the filaments appearing grouped together. The fruit is a loment, a segmented pod with similarly shaped, obovate sections joined at the edge; it is not twisted and bears hooked hairs. These traits are from one regional dataset entry, not a complete or global account.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-6d58d6eb58}
A separate habitat field in the same dataset labels the species terrestrial. The field supplies a habitat category only, without a locality, range or sampling coverage.
