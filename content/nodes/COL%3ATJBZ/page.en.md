## Chamaecrista itambana {#s-c919847865}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional description of *Chamaecrista itambana* records 3–4 elliptic leaflet pairs, glandular hairs on stems and flower clusters, yellow unequal petals. A separate habitat field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), a broad land-based category without a named locality or finer habitat.
