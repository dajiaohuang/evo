## The source tadpole concept {#s-8b9e2fade5}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Mertensophryne taitana, in the genus Mertensophryne and family Bufonidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The underlying works print both Bufo taitanus and Mertensophryne taitana; the compilation keeps those source names distinct. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept; these tadpole records also do not provide an adult diagnosis or a range-wide description.

## Developmental material {#s-cdb8ad397a}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and one measured from an image. Supplied stage labels are 28 and 30; stage 30 is marked “Judging based on the picture.” These labels belong to source records, not one tadpole’s continuous timeline. The compilation preserves uncertainty and distinguishes picture-based judgements from directly documented stages. Two records do not necessarily represent two unique animals, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
