In CaecilianTraits version 4, Caecilia yaigoje is listed in the genus Caecilia and family Caeciliidae. The compilation keeps individual, population and species records separate. It has not yet been established whether this source concept matches a current Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) concept.

## Measurement material {#s-cac15bff57}
The compilation lists three measurement records for this source concept, all at individual level; it lists none at population or species level. These are database rows, not a population estimate or proof of three distinct specimens. Each row retains its specimen identifier, sex code and reference. Body-size comparisons also need to distinguish fresh, preserved and summary measurements.
