## Hypodiscus alternans {#s-1fc46d3d4d}
## A nut-bearing plant of shale seeps

The account describes Hypodiscus alternans as a tufted perennial up to 55 cm tall, with simple, round culms bearing several nodes. Male spikelets are 5–10 mm long; their bracts are reddish and pointed, and the nut is ornamented. The recorded habitat is a seep on a shale band at 150 m elevation.
