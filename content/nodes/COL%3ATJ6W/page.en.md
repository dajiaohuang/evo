## Yellow flowers on terminal and axillary racemes {#s-92aa01a9ca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes five to nine pairs of oval-lance-shaped leaflets on alternate, spirally arranged leaves. Racemes occur at the shoot tips and in leaf axils; the yellow flowers have ten stamens. The record also notes star-shaped, bulbous glandular hairs and pods whose valves twist as they mature.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-f556a582b1}
The same dataset classifies the habitat as terrícola, meaning terrestrial. No collection site or range is supplied by this field.
