## Zephyranthes amoena: flowers and recorded habitat {#s-1e20f9a9c3}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes erect, linear or thread-like leaves up to 2 mm wide. The spathe bracts are joined for more than half their length, and the inflorescence bears one flower. The perianth is 2.1–3.0 cm long, with a nectar tube up to 2.0 mm. Its main colour is bluish lilac, with green at the tepal bases; the tepals are spatulate. There are six erect stamens. The style is as long as or shorter than the stamens, and the stigma lobes are up to 1.0 mm long. Mature fruit shape is recorded as unknown.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Rupícola,” meaning associated with rock.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.

A second habitat field records “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
