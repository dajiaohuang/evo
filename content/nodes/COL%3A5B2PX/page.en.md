## What range of forms is recorded for Verbena hirta? {#s-f38a1fb6f5}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field records erect or ground-spreading plants with soft hairs. Leaves range from elliptic and egg-shaped to nearly round, with a wedge-shaped base; their edges may be smooth or have teeth mainly near the tip. Dense flower clusters carry leaves and stalks, with side clusters shorter than the terminal one. The calyx has five short triangular teeth, and the corolla tube is under 7 mm. The alternatives in this field describe recorded variation, not necessarily one plant bearing every form.
