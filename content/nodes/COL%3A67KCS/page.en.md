## Anthurium viridivinosum: a regional morphology record {#s-e6ea7a42a7}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records the stem as erect or creeping, with entire cataphylls and prophylls at its tip and fibers at the base. Petioles are flattened with keels along their margins and are marked smooth. Leaf blades are entire and flat, elliptic, with acute tips and bases; glandular dots are absent. The primary-vein description combines alternative states: obtuse on both surfaces, or obtuse above and acute below. Secondary veins are impressed above, and a collecting vein is present along the full margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as larger than half a leaf, with a one-keeled peduncle and a flat, linear spathe at an acute angle to it; the spathe is not stipitate. The berry is described with a green apex and white base.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The alternative primary-vein states remain unresolved.
