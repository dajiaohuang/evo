## Three basal veins and flowers on one side {#s-05acc2d0c8}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes tree-like hairs on young stems. Leaves are elliptic or ovate, with acute or blunt tips and heart-shaped, rounded or blunt bases; their margins are entire and lack cilia. Three main veins and two faint intramarginal veins are listed from the leaf base. The underside is densely covered by tree-like hairs. Flowers are secund, arranged along one side of the axis. The bell-shaped hypanthium has a calyx that falls away and distinct lobes; each flower has five petals. The anther connective is not prolonged and has no appendage. Anther colour is not specified; the pore is single and small, and the ovary is described as hairless or hairy. The listed alternatives are not assigned to individuals or populations in this field.
