## Bulbinella divaginata {#s-e963add74a}
Bulbinella divaginata is an autumn-flowering rhizomatous geophyte with narrow, threadlike leaves and white membranous cataphylls around their bases. It usually appears after the first substantial winter rains, while the leaves are still developing. The regional account records mainly clay soils in renosterveld and notes variation in plant size and flower and leaf counts.
