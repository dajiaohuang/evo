## Anthurium loretense: morphology and epiphytic habit {#s-cead10fd40}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records an erect stem, but does not characterize its cataphylls or prophylls. Petioles are marked smooth; the compound shape field combines flattened, rounded, keeled and grooved states, so it is not reduced to one form. Leaf blades are entire and flat, oblong, elliptic or obovate, with apiculate, acute or obtuse tips and bases coded as acute, rounded, truncate or cordate; glandular dots are absent. The primary vein is obtuse on both surfaces, secondary veins are prominent above, and a collecting vein is absent along the margin.

The inflorescence is recorded as less than half a leaf, with a cylindrical peduncle and a flat, oblong or linear spathe at an acute angle to it; the spathe is not stipitate. Berries are described as reddish or vinaceous.

These are descriptors in one regional Brazilian Flora dataset field; they do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The compound petiole states remain unresolved.

The dataset records the habitat category “Epífita,” meaning epiphytic.

This regional category gives no collection locality, survey date, global range or sampling completeness.
