## Variable hairs and many-flowered clusters {#s-12059b76e0}
This tufted annual has ascending, spreading branches to about 15 cm; their hairs range from dense near the lower nodes to sparse or absent above. Leaves are opposite or near-whorled, often in unequal pairs, and vary from oblanceolate or narrowly obovate to less common elliptic or linear shapes. Many-flowered cymes grow at stem tips or in leaf axils; the central flower is sessile and the side flowers have stalks. Sepals have a green, keeled mid-zone and pale margins; petals are usually five but may number two or three or be absent. The three-valved capsule is slightly shorter than the persistent sepals, and the many seeds are smooth and brown. The source says damp sandy places are common habitat, though the plant is inconspicuous. A nomenclature note refers readers to Milne-Redhead for fuller synonymy.
