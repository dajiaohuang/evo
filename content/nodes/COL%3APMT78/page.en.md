## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-79d722ef2b}
COL26.8 lists Ischnocnema crassa Silva-Soares, Ferreira, Ornellas, Zocca, Caramaschi & Cruz, 2021 as a species in Anura, family Brachycephalidae, genus Ischnocnema. This is the classification in the release dated 20 August 2026; other catalogues require their own name and identity records.

## Hand webbing in a leaf-litter breeder {#s-09545b2182}
The 2021 description places Ischnocnema crassa in montane rainforest at Santa Teresa, Espírito Santo, and reports it as a leaf-litter breeder. The authors describe moderate webbing between the fingers, then unique among described Ischnocnema, and place the species as the third member of the I. parva series. The study also reports a molecular phylogeny and field and laboratory observations, but its abstract does not give specimen-level measurements or detailed results for those observations.
