## Confirmed Langbian Plateau records in the 2017 assessment {#s-829cdeeffb}
The assessment confirmed records from the Dalat environs, Bidoup–Nui Ba National Park and Chu Yang Sin environs on southern Vietnam’s Langbian Plateau, collectively at 700–2000 m.
