## Picris hieracioides {#s-bdc5661bf6}
## Tall rough stems carry bright yellow heads

Hilliard's 1977 *Compositae in Natal* account describes a stout, bristly herb, biennial or perennial, reaching 2 m. Its lower leaves are oblanceolate and narrow into a petiole-like base; upper leaves are lance-shaped, broad at the base and clasp the stem. All have coarse teeth. Stems branch irregularly above into a spreading corymb.

Small heads, under 1 cm across, sit singly at branch tips. Their linear-lanceolate bracts are similar in shape, with the outer bracts shorter and a central line of stout bristles. Flowers are bright yellow. The grooved, transversely wrinkled dry fruits are about 4 mm long; their plumose pappus bristles soon fall away. The cited habitat account records roadsides and other disturbed places. This is a regional source description.
