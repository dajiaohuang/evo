## Montsechia and evidence for a submerged habit {#s-fd0317d249}
More than 1,000 Barremian specimens of *Montsechia vidalii* preserve flexible leafy axes and closed, single-seeded fruits. Their thin cuticle, rare stomata, freshwater-limestone setting and morphology support a submerged freshwater habit. A proposed position near *Ceratophyllum* remains a morphology-based hypothesis, and this extinct plant alone cannot establish the ecology of the angiosperm ancestor. The paper reported deposits at about 130–125 Ma; the event's current Barremian boundaries use a different age scale.
