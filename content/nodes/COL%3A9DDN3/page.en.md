## Muellera denudata {#s-590497c46f}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muellera denudata* as an accepted species of *Muellera* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry gives a height range of 1.5–15 m and leaves with 5, 7, or 9 leaflets. It describes pink or purple petals, a segmented, bead-like legume with papery to leathery texture, and kidney-shaped seeds. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
