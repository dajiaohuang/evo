## Stegolepis celiae {#s-92c46e7dab}
## A compressed flower cluster with measured leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record gives leaf-sheath lengths of 15–24 cm and leaf-blade widths of 4–9 cm. It also describes a compressed inflorescence—a cluster of flowers—3–5 cm across, with 15–50 spikelets, the small units that bear the flowers. The same row labels the habitat “terrestrial”; it gives no locality or complete range.
