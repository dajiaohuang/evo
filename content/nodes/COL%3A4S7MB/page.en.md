## Accepted name and placement {#s-e013145958}
COL26.8 accepts Rhinella arenarum (Hensel, 1867) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Bufonidae (family) → Rhinella (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.
