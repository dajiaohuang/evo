COL26.8 accepts *Piculus chrysochloros* in Picidae. In AVONET v7, the HBW–BirdLife 2020 record includes 14 measured individuals, 7 with a complete morphological set. Trait-specific sample sizes can be smaller.

## Measured beak dimensions {#s-aa48e4e2ae}
Mean beak length from the anterior nostril edge to the tip is 19.4 mm; mean width and depth at that edge are 7.1 mm and 6.5 mm. The source defines these as separate measurements at standardized landmarks, not field-identification cutoffs.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-ee91ae532f}
The mean unflattened wing measurement, from carpal joint to longest primary, is 126.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 19.2 mm. The separately compiled mean mass is 88 g (source category Dunning). It is a species-average value from another source, not a weight assigned to each morphological specimen.

## Evidence limits {#s-1aab639a7c}
AVONET v7 provides historical comparisons, not a complete species account. The morphological figures are averages from the listed samples, not identification diagnoses or ranges of individual variation. Mass comes from separately cited literature or weighing sources, so its sample cannot be inferred from the morphology count. Habitat, diet and migration are broad categories, while the map concerns historical breeding and resident ranges only. A one-to-one historical crosswalk and the catalogue name support this association but do not prove the species concepts stayed equivalent after splits or lumps. Reproduction, current populations and conservation status need other sources.
