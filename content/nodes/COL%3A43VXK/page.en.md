## What does the record say about leaves, flowers, and fruit? {#s-93115ee175}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes fissured bark and opposite, leathery leaves with pointed to long-tapering tips. The leaf surfaces are hairy, though the upper may become smoother; dried leaves turn yellow-brown. Male flowers occur in clusters of three, with hairy parts and a bell-shaped receptacle. The elliptic fruit is a wrinkled drupelet that becomes nearly hairless as it matures.
