## A short cleft in the leaf blade {#s-b25671359e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 row describes a short, two-lobed leaf blade with a heart-shaped to nearly flat base and a blunt tip. Its lower surface may have fine hairs pressed close to the leaf, and the source also records glands there. The calyx is described as opening into five spiral lobes; the legume opens elastically. Alternative states in the row include differences in hair cover and flower structure, so they are not combined here as one certain form.
