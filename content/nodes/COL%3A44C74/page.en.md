## Yellow flowers with guides on all six tepals {#s-9eecb91b9d}
Moraea intermedia is up to 150 mm tall, with pale-yellow flowers whose six tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) each have a nectar guide. Its red anthers and pollen are prominent; the account also records hybrids with the related M. margaretae at the type locality.

## Granitic gravel slopes {#s-c421340cb3}
The cited flora records granitic gravel slopes as habitat.
