## Evidence limits {#s-531d3be58b}
AVONET v7 measurements are historical sample means, not identification diagnoses or individual ranges; mass has a separate source and denominator. Habitat, trophic niche, lifestyle and migration are coarse categories, and the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges only. The life-history values come from the 2015 archive and are reported as medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record. They are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical taxa may share or inherit data; matching names, family and sparse rows support an association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. EltonTraits food and stratum percentages are standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy or time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty; mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may refer to a congener considered ecologically similar. The records do not establish direct observation of this species for every field. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
