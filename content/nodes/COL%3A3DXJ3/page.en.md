## Felicia cana {#s-20ecbefd27}
## A white-woolly shrublet with mauve rays

The SANBI account describes a delicate dwarf shrublet to 15 cm tall, densely covered in white felted hairs. Narrow oblanceolate leaves are blunt-tipped and clasp the stem. Its solitary radiate heads have yellow centres and mauve rays. A taxonomic note separates it from F. hyssopifolia in part by the dense white felt and broadened hair-like structures on the achenes; it also says one collection, SMITH 4268, leans in habit toward that species.
