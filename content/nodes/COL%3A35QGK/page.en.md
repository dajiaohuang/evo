## Pale ray florets on a low annual herb {#s-83124444c7}
This account describes an annual or biennial herb up to 30 cm tall, with narrow leaves whose edges are wavy. The heads contain pale ray florets that are sterile and pale-violet disk florets. The undeveloped ovary of each ray floret bears many fine, fringed pappus hairs. It grows in dry to very dry sandy or limestone soils, sometimes along the edges of shallow pans.
