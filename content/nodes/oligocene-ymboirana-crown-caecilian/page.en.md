## Ymboirana: a provisional Oligocene caecilian assignment {#s-dbe026349d}
The part-and-counterpart holotype DGM 1462-R preserves a skull, mandibles, the atlas (first neck vertebra) and 66 trunk vertebrae. CT-informed comparisons support assigning the roughly 25-million-year-old fossil to Typhlonectidae, a living aquatic caecilian family, but the skeleton is damaged and no unambiguous family-level synapomorphy was identified. Formal phylogenetic testing remains pending. The proposed aquatic affinity is a classification hypothesis, not preserved behaviour.
