## The source tadpole concept {#s-2cb01710e9}
In TadpoleTraits version 4, these records are assigned to Ololygon ranki. The source classification lists Ololygon as the genus and Hylidae as the family, following Amphibian Species of the World version 6.2 (2024). Names printed in the underlying works are retained separately as Scinax ranki. This assignment has not been established as equivalent to a current COL26.8 species concept.
