## A dense leaf underside and several hair forms {#s-72dfa6d546}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field describes young branches with star-shaped, simple, or tree-like (dendroid) hairs. The oblong leaves taper to an acuminate tip (a long point) and have a rounded or blunt base. Their margins are entire or ciliate (fringed with hairs). Veins begin at the base or just above it; the field lists three lengthwise veins and two faint veins just inside the edge. The underside has a dense hair covering, with sessile star-shaped hairs (attached without stalks). The field lists alternatives without frequencies or a full account of variation.

## One-sided flowers and an anther detail {#s-43c1bb40c1}
The flowers are described as secund, arranged along one side of the cluster. The flower cup (hypanthium) is bell-shaped. The calyx, the outer floral whorl, persists and has distinct lobes. Each flower has five petals. The connective, the central tissue in an anther, is extended; its appendage has two lower (ventral) lobes and one upper (dorsal) tooth. Anthers are white with one small pollen opening, and the ovary is hairless.
