## Begonia leopoldinensis {#s-144b341c32}
## Star-haired peltate leaves on creeping stems

Begonia leopoldinensis has prostrate stems and petioles with star-shaped hairs. Its slightly asymmetric leaves are peltate, transversely ovate to round, with entire margins and star-shaped hairs on both surfaces. The male and female flower segments are hairless; the female margins are entire, and bracteoles are absent. Capsule wings are strongly unequal, while the seed tip is rounded. The habitat label is rocky.
