## What traits are recorded for Annona angustifolia? {#s-e3053e1f71}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes an upright trunk with fissured bark. Leaves have entire edges, pointed tips, and curved veins; the central vein is impressed into the upper surface. Yellow or white flowers grow between stem joints and have three petals with free sepals. The record leaves fruit aggregation and seed color and surface unspecified.

## What broad habitat is recorded? {#s-3d8b65a87a}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial, with no specific locality.
