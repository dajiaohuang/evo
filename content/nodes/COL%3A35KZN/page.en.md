## What is recorded for Dichapetalum odoratum? {#s-685bd1245e}
Its stipules fall early. The leaves are oblong to broadly elliptic, with tapering tips and rounded or wedge-shaped bases that may be symmetric or slightly asymmetric. They range from leathery to papery; the upper surface is flat, while the underside is hairless or sparsely hairy. When dry, leaves are light or dark brown. Axillary or leaf-stalk panicles have a finely gray-hairy central axis. The sepals may be free and alike; the petals may be free or split in two. The ovary has two chambers. Hair coverage on the fruit’s outer wall is unknown.
