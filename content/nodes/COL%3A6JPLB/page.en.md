## What leaf and flower traits are recorded? {#s-51402d91af}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records hairless branches. Leaves have petioles and one vein; the blades are oblong-lance-shaped, with blunt bases, and range from thin and membranous to thick. The upper leaf surface is hairless. Flowers are not recorded in an inflorescence. The urn- to oblong-shaped floral cup is hairless outside. Stamens are all alike, with ear-shaped or two-lobed appendages; the connective below the anther is 0.1–0.2 mm long. Anthers are straight and the ovary is hairless. The fruit is a capsule that opens along its chambers, and the seed coat is pitted. The regional row does not provide a sampling frame.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1867b03abf}
The dataset lists terrestrial habitat and no locality or collection date.
