## Which features are settled, and which remain alternatives? {#s-0c67bf0c97}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records two-ranked leaves with flat, ridged blades and a simple cylindrical flower stalk. It lists two stalk-length classes, 5–20 cm and 20–50 cm, while the ovoid spike has up to ten flowers. Hairiness and leaf-tip form are also given as alternatives, not as one settled state.

## What broad habitat label is recorded? {#s-2556116a1a}
The regional data row labels the species terrestrial. It gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
