## Eight digits, without a walking trace {#s-763aeef68a}
The East Greenland limb specimens described for Acanthostega preserve an eight-digit forelimb. That count differs from the five-digit pattern familiar in living tetrapods and shows that digit number varied during Devonian limb evolution. The evidence here is the anatomy of described limb material, compared with other Devonian forms.

A hand with eight digits does not record how often the animal moved on land. The cited study does not directly establish diet, habitat preference or observed gait, so the profile keeps those ecological questions open rather than converting limb anatomy into a lifestyle claim.
