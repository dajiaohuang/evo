## The source concept {#s-0041e42f21}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Anaxyrus boreas* in row Anf0396 under Anaxyrus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-09fff0230d}
For Anf0396, the compilation tags terrestrial as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Adult food categories {#s-6cf8222b16}
The adult food fields list leaves and arthropods. AmphiBIO combines categories reported through expert assessment, observation or stomach contents; these tags do not make a complete diet or give the share of food items.

## Activity periods {#s-bef2323fc0}

## Seasonal activity {#s-7ba9b13b5a}
The seasonal fields tag wet and warm and wet and cold. In this compilation, such labels relate activity to the local annual climate; they do not name fixed calendar months that apply everywhere.
