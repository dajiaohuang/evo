## ## Golden hairs and red seeds in the Panama flora {#s-4da75732fc}
The historical *Flora of Panama* account describes a tree about 15 m tall, with young stems clothed in golden silky hairs that become sparse with age. Leaves bear five to nine leaflets; the oblong-lanceolate leaflets are 10–15 × 3–6 cm, subleathery, long-tapered at the tip and blunt at the base. Their upper surfaces become nearly hairless, while the undersides retain fine golden hairs. Lilac flowers have a densely golden-silky, five-lobed calyx and a corolla about 2 cm long. The flattened, dehiscent fruit is nearly round, about 4 cm across, and not constricted between seeds; it contains one to four dark-red seeds, each about 1.5 cm long. This historical regional description does not establish full variation or global range.
