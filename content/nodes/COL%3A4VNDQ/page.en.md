## Sciurus (Guerlinguetus) pucheranii: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Sciurus (Guerlinguetus) pucheranii* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority (Fitzinger, 1867).
