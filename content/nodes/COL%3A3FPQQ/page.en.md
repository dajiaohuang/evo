## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}

Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Geoffroyus simplex (A. B. Meyer, 1874) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## A field study in context {#archbold-camp-record}

In the account of bird life at the 1,800-meter Camp, Archbold, Rand, and Brass include Geoffroyus simplex in a list of parrots recorded in the forest. This is a historical occurrence tied to that camp; it does not establish the species' full elevational range or behavior.
