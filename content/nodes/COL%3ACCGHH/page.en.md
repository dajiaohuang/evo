## One nest on Dinagat Island {#field-study}
Senarillos and Ibañez report the first nest description for the Mindanao treeshrew from Dinagat Island in the Philippines. Their abstract identifies one active nest, built at ground level inside a cavity of a dead pandan tree identified as Sararanga cf. philippinensis and surrounded by natural vegetation. This is a single observed nest site; the report does not establish whether other populations use the same tree, height, or setting.

The authors present the record as a contribution to a sparse natural-history literature and call for more nest searches and breeding-ecology work in Dinagat and elsewhere in the species’ range. That request follows directly from the narrow evidence: one nest can establish that this site was used, but not how common such nests are or how the species reproduces generally. The accessible abstract gives no measurements of the cavity or nest contents, so those details remain open.
