## ## Name and a single habitat label {#s-487100edcb}
COL26.8 records *Arachis appressipila* as a species of *Arachis* in the bean family, Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 dossier supplies one biological descriptor: the habitat category “terrestrial.” That label is not tied to a locality, survey date or complete range, and no morphology is described in this record.
