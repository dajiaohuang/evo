## Leaves and flowers {#s-be5646aaca}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes underground bulbs and gives plant height as “up to 100 cm or more.” Leaf blades have a keel-like projection and are wider than 10 mm. The terminal synflorescence has more than 10 branches; its stalked rhipidium bears one flower. Flowers are yellow or orange. The field records joined filaments at about 1.4–2.8 mm and, separately, filament length greater than 2.0 mm. Anther length is given as ≤5 mm/>5 mm. The connective is described as vinaceous or purplish to dark brown and wider than 0.5 mm. The adaxial style crest is erect to slightly twisted, with length given as ≤2.0 mm/>2.0 mm. Slash-separated values are retained as recorded; the field does not assign them to specimens or define them as ranges.

This is one morphology field in the Brazilian Flora 2020 regional dataset. It does not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex, or life stage beyond what the field states.

## Recorded growth form {#s-a5a691da06}
The dataset labels the growth form “terrestrial” (Portuguese: *terrícola*).

This is a habitat or substrate category in the Brazilian Flora dataset row; it gives no collection locality, survey date, or complete range.
