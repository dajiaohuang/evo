## A comparison marked by the calyx keel {#s-b5d8bbd46a}
The SANBI account describes *Sebaea schlechteri* by comparison with *S. ambigua*: its flowers are yellow or white, and the calyx lobes are strongly keeled throughout. The passage limits the comparison to these characters and gives no fuller description of *S. ambigua*.
