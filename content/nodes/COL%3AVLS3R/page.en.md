## Rhynchosia corylifolia {#s-c95db8ded3}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Rhynchosia corylifolia* as an accepted species of *Rhynchosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record describes erect stems and three-leaflet leaves. Its flower clusters are flat-topped and longer than the leaves; the calyx lobes (the outer floral segments) are longer than the corolla (the petal layer). The fruit is not constricted between seeds, which are recorded as one color.
