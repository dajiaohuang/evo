## Aloe gerstneri {#s-cb5db6d2c7}
Aloe gerstneri is a solitary rosette aloe with long, glaucous leaves and a tall, densely branched flowering stalk. Its flowers open from orange buds to chrome yellow. The regional description notes that young leaves may bear many prickles that largely disappear with age. Rocky grassland in a cold-winter, relatively wet area is the recorded habitat.
