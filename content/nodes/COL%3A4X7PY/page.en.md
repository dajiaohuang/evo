## What leaf, flower and fruit details are recorded? {#s-753bf7688a}
The record describes a taproot and erect, hairy stems, but the stem-hair type and stipules are unknown. Slightly two-toned leaves are linear to lance-shaped, with symmetrical blades, pointed bases and tips, and toothed margins near the upper middle. The upper surface has sparse simple hairs. Flowers are crowded at the tips or solitary in the axils. The sepals have pointed tips; corolla details are unknown. The fruit is a schizocarp with five segments (mericarps), but segment characters and their hairs are unknown.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-e773d0a9de}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
