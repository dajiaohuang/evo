## Zephyranthes flavissima {#s-813e66b8f9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes Zephyranthes flavissima with narrow, upright leaves and one flower per inflorescence. The yellow perianth is 3.1–4.0 cm long, with a green base; the six stamens are erect and the style is as long as or longer than them. The dataset records both rock-dwelling and terrestrial habitat categories.
