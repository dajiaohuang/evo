## Solitary magenta flowers on a smooth-leaved shrub {#s-40389f8a5d}
A general description calls the species a robust, smooth-leaved shrub 20–60 cm tall. The more specific account describes a loosely branched shrub to 30 cm, with smooth, glaucous, three-sided, somewhat curved leaves 18–25 mm long. Solitary, stalked flowers are magenta to pink and up to 40 mm across; the fruit has five chambers. Coastal rocks and clayey hills are listed as habitat.
