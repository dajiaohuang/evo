## Stem surface {#s-f945dd5f60}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes a tomentose stem surface, densely covered with matted hairs that curve inward. It lists simple hairs, thread-like unbranched bristles, and capitate glandular hairs (with small head-shaped tips). Branched bristles, prickles, and short lateral shoots (brachyblasts) are absent.

## Leaves and inflorescences {#s-c3db792691}
Stipules, small structures at leaf bases, are caducous and fall early. The record marks extrafloral nectaries (nectar-secreting glands outside flowers) and projections between pairs of pinnae (the leaf’s primary side divisions) as absent. The inflorescence, or flower cluster, is globose; its floral bracts (small leaf-like structures) are longer than the flowers before they open (anthesis).

## Flowers {#s-2006f4eb73}
The perianth (calyx and corolla together) is recorded as having four parts. The membranous calyx, the outer sepal whorl, has triangular lobes. Corolla (petal-whorl) lobes have one to three veins and a tomentose covering of dense, matted hairs. The androecium (the stamens collectively) is diplostemonous, with two stamen whorls; the filaments are pink.

## Habitat category {#s-7d35b7a48e}
The regional dataset labels the habitat “Terrícola,” Portuguese for terrestrial. This category does not identify a collection locality or the species’ full range.
