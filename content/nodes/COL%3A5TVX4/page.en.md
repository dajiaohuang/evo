## What leaf, flower, and fruit details are recorded for Alchornea fluviatilis? {#s-9f5c59d9ac}
The branches are hairless. Leaves have pinnate veins and are elliptic to lance-shaped; the upper surface is hairy, and the greenish underside also bears hairs. Male and female flower clusters may be separate or bisexual panicles; the hair covering on female-cluster axes is recorded as present, while their axis structure was not observed. Male flowers lack a central tuft of hairs among the stamens. Female flowers have fused sepals, a rounded hairy ovary, and a threadlike style. Fruits range from elliptic to spherical and may divide into two to five segments. The ovate seeds have a slightly rough, bluntly toothed surface.
