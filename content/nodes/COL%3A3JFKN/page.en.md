## A seagrass with paired leaf shoots {#s-ec0e602724}
The Flora of Panama description calls the plant dioecious (male and female flowers occur on separate plants), with a thin, fragile rhizome. Its lateral shoots carry 2–3 pairs of leaves near the tip. The firm leaf blades range from oblong or ovate to elliptic-lanceolate, 5–22 mm long and 2–8 mm wide; their margins are finely spinulose, and each side of the midrib has 3–8 cross-veins. Each inflorescence contains one flower. The account also describes separate staminate and carpellate flowers and globose fruits 2–3 mm across, with 10–20 seeds.

## A historical regional description {#s-7817932b7f}
This morphology comes from a historical Flora of Panama record cited to 2013. The source version was retrieved in 2026, but the text gives no population sampling frame or observation date.
