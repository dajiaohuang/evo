## Gestation and lifespan are not interchangeable {#s-22005aea87}
The archive distinguishes gestation, female age at first reproduction and maximum lifespan. First reproduction differs from first birth and sexual maturity; maximum lifespan is a longest collected record rather than an average. Source conditions vary, and missing values require other evidence.

## How the life-history values were assembled {#s-2106227be1}
Species entries use an MSW93/MSW05 crosswalk, which does not prove equivalence with current species concepts. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources with equal weight; inputs may be averages, individual values or range midpoints, and studies may recur. Adult mass is not interpreted in this route, gaps are not filled from relatives, and wild, captive or locality conditions were not established for every source.
