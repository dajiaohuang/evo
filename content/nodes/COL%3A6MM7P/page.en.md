## Two incomplete mitochondrial sequences {#s-ef5d8b17d5}
The study generated sequences from Jinxiu, Guangxi and Wencheng, Zhejiang, preliminarily identified by morphology. Both sequences lack part of the control region, although they include thirteen protein-coding genes, two ribosomal genes and twenty-two tRNAs. Reported sequence lengths are 15675 and 15977 bp under those site labels. These are mitochondrial assemblies, not complete nuclear genomes or a population-wide genome-size estimate. Two samples cannot establish all geographic variation.

## Site and accession labels conflict between sections {#s-3910a7b107}

## Distance heterogeneity suggests a question, not new accepted species {#s-36de529d80}
The source reports 4.4% mitochondrial distance excluding the control region between the labelled Jinxiu sequence and the Wencheng or Lishui references, versus 0.2% between Wencheng and Lishui. It suggests possible cryptic diversity. These are sample comparisons, not a species description, universal divergence threshold or direct reproductive-isolation estimate. Nuclear data, broader sampling and voucher reconciliation remain needed before converting the pattern into new accepted taxa.

## Within-name branching order is inconsistent in the text {#s-8d8df1e592}
Results place the Wencheng–Lishui pair with Mingxi before Jinxiu; Discussion instead places the pair with Jinxiu before Mingxi. The shared Wencheng–Lishui relationship is reported in both, while the deeper order remains unresolved from the text alone. A mitochondrial tree is not a dated dispersal map or direct measure of contemporary gene flow.

## Branch-site candidates do not prove cold adaptation {#s-937682d50f}
The branch-site model reports five candidate alignment sites in mitochondrial Cytb, ND3, ND4 and ND5 for the chosen Hyla/Dryophytes foreground, while broader models did not detect the same signal. These are model-dependent candidates, not experimentally measured protein function or temperature tolerance. The authors explicitly call historical cold adaptation a hypothesis requiring further work. The printed positions are not certified here as local residue numbers within individual proteins.
