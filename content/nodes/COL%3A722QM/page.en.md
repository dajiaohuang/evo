## Grey leaf undersides and scalloped margins {#s-3255896e37}
The morphology record describes moderately branched stems bearing narrow, lance-shaped leaves. The upper surfaces are smooth and the lower surfaces greyish; the margins may be scalloped or finely toothed. Sessile heads are solitary or occur in branched clusters, with 5–6 rows of bracts and about 20–30 florets. The cylindrical fruits are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
