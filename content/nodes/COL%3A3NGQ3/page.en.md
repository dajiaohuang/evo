## Two nearby Ecuadorian sites {#s-9fda482aba}
The description recorded two sites 1.7 km apart in Ecuador’s Río Quimi basin, Zamora Chinchipe, at 1991–2134 m. The adult collection site was about 500 m from Peru, prompting the authors to expect occurrence there. That expectation remains a geographic prediction rather than a confirmed Peruvian specimen record.

## Different adult and larval collection settings {#s-fc67f7543c}
Two adults and one juvenile were found on shrubs beside small streams at 2134 m; tadpoles and juveniles were found in ponds along the Río Cristalino at 1991 m. Collections occurred in July 2017 and April 2018. These stage-specific observations retain their sites and dates, without establishing a complete annual breeding season or showing that all stages use identical habitats.
