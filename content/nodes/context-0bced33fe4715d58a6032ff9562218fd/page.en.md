## The Anf1914 source record {#s-39d2eea0ad}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Eleutherodactylus marnockii* as row Anf1914, in the genus Eleutherodactylus, family Eleutherodactylidae, and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this deposited row; its species concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 concept.

## Foraging categories in the source row {#s-922e64d652}
The row marks terrestrial (Ter = 1) and aquatic (Aqu = 1) as broad foraging categories. The compilation does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat entries inherited from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so these flags remain historical AmphiBIO values rather than a verified account of present-day ecology; the revision does not establish that every original entry was wrong.

## The recorded adult food category {#s-80897c0ecf}
The row marks arthropods as an adult food category (Arthro = 1). AmphiBIO aggregates diet categories reported in literature through expert assessment, observation, or stomach contents. This one category is not a complete diet list or a measured share of food.

## Activity categories {#s-4dbd3fd4be}
The row marks night (Noc = 1) and twilight (Crepu = 1). These are broad source categories, not a continuous record of behaviour through the day.

## Seasonal activity category {#s-5934cca9e7}
Wet and warm is the recorded seasonal category (Wet_warm = 1). AmphiBIO compares activity conditions with the local annual climate, so this label does not correspond to fixed calendar months.

## Reported maximum adult length {#s-b775d2c992}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 39.5 mm. For anurans, the compilation defines body length as snout–vent length, measured from the snout to the vent. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual or a typical adult length.

## Maturity, lifespan and reproduction {#s-f5084874a9}
The row records age at sexual maturity as 1 year for both its minimum and maximum, size at maturity as 18–22 mm, maximum lifespan as 3 years, 8–20 offspring or eggs per clutch, and up to 3 reproductive events per year.

These are literature-compiled extrema and counts. The row does not specify the sex, population, observation date, or wild versus captive setting for each value, so the figures should not be read as a complete life-history account.

## Developmental coding {#s-ef16e33c26}
The source records direct development (Dir = 1). This compiled category does not describe parental care, breeding location, or seasonal timing.
