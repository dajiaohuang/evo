## *Stromanthe schottiana*: a loose panicle above a rhizome {#s-0676bebacd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 profile records an underground rhizome and aerial stems, with no basal leaves. Leaf blades are oval to oblong, with pointed tips; their stalks may be absent or reduced to the pulvinus, the joint at the base of the blade. A loose, panicle-shaped flower cluster carries two or three flowering units per node, beneath leaf-like bracts.

The tubular flowers have three free sepals and one fertile stamen; the profile records no outer petaloid staminodes. The brown capsule contains a single smooth, ellipsoid brown seed with a layered aril, a seed attachment. The habitat field says only “terrestrial”; this regional character record gives no locality or date.
