## What features distinguish this small herb? {#s-7c22d55d08}
The regional account describes a herb up to 200 mm tall, with leaves undivided or only shallowly lobed. The epicalyx bracts are much narrower than the sepals. Petals are under 15 mm and usually no more than twice the calyx length. Flowers are white or pale mauve. Each fruit has 9–16 hairless segments (mericarps) with clear net-like ridges on the back; their edges may be slightly raised but are neither toothed nor overlapping.

## Which regional vegetation types are listed? {#s-6d6feece10}
A 2017 regional account lists Dry Grassland, Dwarf Karroid Shrubland and Zastron Moist Grassland. These are vegetation categories in that account; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
