## Lessingianthus niederleinii {#s-9a152d6032}
## Toothed leaves and heads in varied clusters

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes a xylopodium and stems that are unbranched or sparsely branched, with leaves along their length. Leaves are ovate, obovate or lance-shaped, leathery to subleathery, and hairless above with a pubescent underside. Their margins are toothed or spiny but do not roll under.

Heads may be sessile or stalked and occur singly or in cymes and panicles. The bell-shaped involucre has 4–5 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 25, 30 or 35 florets. Top-shaped fruits are silky or pubescent. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
