## *Felicia dregei*: velvety leaves and fragrant heads {#s-20e932368f}
The regional account describes a spreading shrub taller than 1 m, with short, fine hairs that give the leaves a gray-green, velvety appearance. Its flower heads are solitary and radiate: yellow central florets are surrounded by mauve rays, and the heads are fragrant. Leaves are usually toothed around the middle of a shoot, though not consistently. The account notes that hairiness and the presence of glands vary among plants without a geographic pattern, and it records sandy slopes and flats as habitat.
