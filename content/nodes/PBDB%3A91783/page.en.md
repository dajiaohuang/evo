## Recorded parent of Eumyarioninae {#s-697193dbfb}
Eumyarioninae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Cricetidae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
