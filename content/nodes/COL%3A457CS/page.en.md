## Linear leaves and a channelled midrib {#s-3481eb4e3c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records sympodial growth, in which successive growth continues through side shoots, and simple stem hairs. The leaves are linear; their upper-surface midrib is channelled, while the underside is glabrous (without hairs). Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below the leaf nodes are absent. These are states in one regional dataset field, with no frequencies reported.

## A solitary flower or a forked cluster {#s-9349eaa242}
The inflorescence branchlets are rounded, and the bracteoles beneath flowers are linear or oblong. Each axillary shoot bears one inflorescence, positioned in an axil or at the tip; the field describes it as either a simple dichasium (a forked flower cluster) or a single flower. The anther's two pollen sacs are equal, and hairs cover the floral disc. The source leaves the hypanthium's shape and indumentum unknown. Sepals are free, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx is listed as not opening by tearing. The globose fruit bears calyx lobes that enlarge as it develops.
