## Purple flowers and nine stamens {#s-db96241166}
The flora entry describes 13–25 pairs of oblong leaflets, sometimes up to 29, with blunt tips. Flower clusters are branched. The corolla is recorded as purple to violet, and the flower has nine stamens whose filaments join into one bundle. The fruit is oblong; its surface-vein pattern is unknown in this record. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
