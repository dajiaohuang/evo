## Leaves and flowers {#s-f3f56ad503}
The cited description is of a perennial herb up to 0.9 m tall, with leaves more than 50 mm wide at the middle. The perianth segments form a narrow trumpet shape. The flowers are white with a dark-red keel; the anthers are grey or brown. Older flowers dry pink or red. The account also notes many new leaves and fruit without a beak.

The field records a perennial herb up to 0.9 m high; leaves over 50 mm broad at the middle; perianth segments forming a narrow trumpet; grey or brown anthers; older flowers drying pink or red; fruit not beaked; many new leaves; and white flowers with a dark-red keel.
