## Thicker leaves and wider capsules than a close relative {#s-fa0d844a93}
The source compares this species with D. asperulum and describes it as smaller, up to 15 cm tall, with thicker leaves 2–4 mm wide and broader capsules 5–10 mm across. The leaves may be triangular in cross-section or flat; the petals range from pink or magenta to white, and the staminodes are never black. It grows on stony quartz outcrops on clay slopes.
