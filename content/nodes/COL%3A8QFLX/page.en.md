## Adult forearm measurements {#s-8fcfb2a802}
The 2025 study measured 3 adult Molossus melini and reports a mean forearm length of 39.93 mm, which falls in the authors' medium category under their stated length bands. Forearm length is one size indicator; this small adult-only sample does not describe the species' full morphology or variation by sex, age or place. Species-level specimen localities were not abstracted here.

## A weakly supported mitochondrial grouping {#s-794e132ae6}
In the study's tree based on two mitochondrial markers, COI and cytochrome b, M. melini groups with M. molossus and M. paranaensis. The authors report low support for this grouping. It is one study's sampled mitochondrial result, not a complete evolutionary history; nuclear-genome and population-level trees, independent replication and comparisons with other studies were not reviewed here.
