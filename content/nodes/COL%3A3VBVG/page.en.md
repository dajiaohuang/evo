## What marks Lippia hatschbachii? {#s-bfd8427789}
This record describes stems with short, stiff hairs. Opposite pairs of stalkless, egg-shaped leaves grow at right angles to successive pairs; the papery blades carry stiff and glandular hairs on both surfaces. Rounded flower heads have spirally arranged, papery bracts. The bisexual flowers are white, with a salver-shaped corolla and tubular calyx.
