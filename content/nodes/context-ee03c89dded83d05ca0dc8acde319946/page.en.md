## Source classification and concept status {#s-2cedbf15b5}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2851 records Litoria lodesdema under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-e2fa72b9ef}
The compilation records terrestrial and arboreal as foraging categories for this row. The compilation uses broad categories rather than fine-grained habitat observations. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-013d618a4a}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult snout–vent length of 23.8 mm for this row. The source field Body_size_mm is defined as maximum adult body size in millimetres; for Anura, it is reported as snout–vent length (SVL). This is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.
