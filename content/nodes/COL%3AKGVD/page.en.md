## Balfourodendron riedelianum {#s-b6f0238c1a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records a 9–32 m height, leaflet details and fruit wings 2.5–5 cm wide.

## Tall stems and broad fruit wings {#s-e41996e006}
The height field gives 9–32 m. Leaflets have acuminate tips, rarely acute, petiolulate bases and surfaces that become glabrescent; punctations are recorded only on the lower surface. Inflorescence peduncles are 10–20 mm. Petal bases are clawed. Fruit wings are 2.5–5 cm wide. The separate ecology field classifies the setting as “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a locality or full range.
