## The source concept {#s-8a6b09c0e1}
AmphiBIO v1 lists *Didynamipus sjostedti* in row Anf0572 under Didynamipus, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-26cbe1a572}
For Anf0572, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-ffe7be699e}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 20 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-c7f13176e4}
The row records a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not identify sex, population, date or wild versus captive status behind each report.
