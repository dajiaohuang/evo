## The source concept {#s-e0b09a91a7}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf0008 records Discoglossus jeanneae in genus Discoglossus, family Alytidae and order Anura. The compilation's species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). Correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved; the passages below describe the deposited row.

## Recorded habitat fields: Fos=1, Ter=1, Aqu=1 {#s-ca923f8141}
The row records Fos=1 (fossorial), Ter=1 (terrestrial), Aqu=1 (aquatic). The field Arb=NA remains missing; this is not evidence that any category is absent. These broad fields do not describe seasonal or developmental change. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries. Its affected-field scope includes Body_mass_g, Fos, Ter, Aqu, Arb. No row-level comparison is captured here; no corrected value is substituted, and the notice does not say every original entry is wrong.

## Adult diet field: Arthro=1 {#s-3a5005e539}
The row records Arthro=1 (arthropods). Vert=NA remains missing. AmphiBIO compiles adult-diet categories from literature reports, including expert judgement, observations or stomach contents. The listed categories do not describe a complete diet or measured proportions.

## Activity fields: Diu=1, Noc=1 {#s-34a93c91b7}
The row records Diu=1 (daytime activity), Noc=1 (night activity). These are broad categories, not a continuous behavioural time series.

## Body_size_mm: 60 mm maximum adult body length {#s-41938a4895}
AmphiBIO records Body_size_mm=60 mm as maximum adult body length. For Anura, body length is snout–vent length; this is a compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Clutch counts and reproductive output {#s-5efadfc768}
The row lists Litter_size_min_n=1500 as the minimum number of offspring or eggs per clutch; Litter_size_max_n=1500 as the maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch; Reproductive_output_y=1 as the maximum number of reproductive events per year. The dataset compiles these extrema and counts from literature; this row does not specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. When offspring size is reported, fields may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Lar=1: larval stage recorded {#s-b7aeb0f2bd}
Lar=1 records a larval stage. This source flag alone does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
