## A reference assembly in a comparative genome study {#s-8aa427d904}
The study published online in December2025 and in the 2026 volume lists Bombina bombina assembly GCA_029206835.1, with an assembled genome-size summary of 10.02 Gbp and contig N50 3,759.9 Kbp. These describe this listed assembly and its continuity, rather than a direct measurement of DNA per cell, a universally fixed species genome size or a demonstrated ploidy. The source selected NCBI reference assemblies on 29October2024. Original sequence data, assembly quality and specimen identity have not been independently reassessed here.

## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-7f28d35d75}
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Bombina bombina was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-96f5c803b5}
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.
