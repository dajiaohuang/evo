## What leaf and flower traits mark Croton agrophilus? {#s-3b03549944}
Young branches have star-shaped hairs. The short-stalked, oval-lanceolate leaves have star-shaped hairs on both surfaces, looping side veins, and scalloped margins; nectar glands and leafy stipules at young shoot tips are absent. Axillary thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male flowers are nearly sessile and wheel-shaped. Female flowers are also nearly sessile, with separate sepals and a two-lobed style; their petals are unknown. The fruit columella has inflated apical lobes.
