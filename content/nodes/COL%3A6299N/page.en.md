## Recorded classification and direct parent {#s-fe9bc25b84}
COL26.8 records Ammospiza Oberholser, 1905 at genus rank, with Passerellidae as its direct parent.

Compare four historical records through Ammospiza caudacuta, Ammospiza nelsoni, Ammospiza maritima, Ammospiza leconteii. Pinned COL26.8 places all four in Ammospiza; workbook BirdLife and eBird names agree, while BirdTree uses Ammodramus combinations. Both layers are retained, and one-to-one workbook links do not establish complete concept alignment across dates.

## Different sizes, bounded samples {#s-cfff115040}
Morphology sample sizes are 10 caudacuta, 11 nelsoni, 9 maritima and 6 leconteii. Mean nostril-to-tip beak lengths in that order are 9, 8.7, 11.1 and 7.1 mm; unflattened wings average 56.8, 56.4, 60.6 and 53 mm. These sampled species averages can be compared without treating their endpoints as bounds for every individual in the genus. Dunning’s compiled mass averages are 19.19, 15.11, 22.16 and 13 g, with denominators separate from morphology.

## Different categories in open habitats {#s-761cd00e86}
All four rows have open-habitat density code 3; leconteii is Grassland and the other three are Wetland. maritima has partial-migration code 2 and the others code 3; leconteii has a Granivore niche and the others Invertivore. nelsoni and leconteii have Omnivore trophic level, the other two Carnivore. Niche, trophic level, migration and habitat remain separate fields rather than interchangeable labels.

## Limits attached to these records {#s-9ba9af1d31}
This page interprets historical species averages and coarse ecology in AVONET v7. Selected rows, field definitions and name crosswalks were read; individual raw measurements, Dunning’s original book, independent field behaviour were not. Morphology counts are not mass-literature sample sizes, and Inference=NO does not establish concept identity with COL26.8. Plumage identification, breeding, lifespan, current range and conservation status remain unsupplied; this is not a complete monograph.
