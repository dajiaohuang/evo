## A rock-associated habitat entry {#s-fe66470c56}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Sphagnum brasiliense Warnst. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data; its habitat row is attributed at dataset level.

The row is “Rupícola,” meaning rock-dwelling.

The category has no associated collection locality, survey date, full range or sampling coverage.

This habitat category is the only biological information included from the regional record; it is not a full species account.
