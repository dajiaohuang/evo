## Asparagus mariae: clustered spines and flowers {#s-7462b29621}
A South African regional account describes an erect, spiny shrub up to 1 m tall, with pale stems densely covered in short fine hairs. The spines occur in groups of three. Threadlike cladodes grow one to four per bundle. One to three flowers sit on a disc at the shoot tip, with the tepals and filaments spreading outward.
