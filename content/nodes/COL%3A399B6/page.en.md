## Accepted name in the catalogue {#s-d03d239ba7}
The 20 August 2026 release of Catalogue of Life (COL26.8) accepts Eleutherodactylus griphus Crombie, 1986 as a species. Its recorded path is Anura (order), Eleutherodactylidae (family), then Eleutherodactylus (genus). This is the name and placement in that release; another checklist or fossil database needs separate identity evidence.
