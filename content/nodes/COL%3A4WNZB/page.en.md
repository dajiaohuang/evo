## Linear leaflets and spike like inflorescences {#s-1e10605a0f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records angular branches with serially arranged, recurved prickles. Lanceolate stipules accompany one sessile petiolar nectary with a warty oblong form on the lower third of the petiole; a rachis nectary is present. Leaves have 15–24 pairs of pinnae, each with 40–70 pairs of linear leaflets. The leaflets are glabrous on both surfaces, with truncate bases and acute tips. The primary vein is subcentral, with no hairs at its base. The inflorescence is spike like. Flowers are sessile; the tubular calyx is puberulent, the tubular corolla is sericeous, anther tips bear glands and the ovary is villous. The fruit is a legume; seeds are elliptic or orbicular. The habitat field records terrestrial. These regional fields do not establish variation or a complete range.
