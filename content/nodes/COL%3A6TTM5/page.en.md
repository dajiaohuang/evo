## What traits are recorded for the tree and its fruit? {#s-06d5f0721b}
The dataset lists no buttress roots and describes a tree about 30 m tall with whorled branches. Leaves have five main veins and petioles 2.2–4 cm long. A solitary flower grows opposite a leaf; it is erect, with a tubular calyx bearing five toothed lobes. The petals are linear to oblong, and the ovary has five chambers. The fruit is an indehiscent, spherical berry about 10 cm long. Its seeds are densely woolly.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-09d04ec787}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
