## What flower and seed traits are recorded for Tragia uberabana? {#s-a740ade199}
The stem is upright. Its undivided leaves are lance-shaped to broadly triangular, with rounded bases, tapering tips, and toothed edges. Racemes bear glandular or stinging hairs. Male flowers have three ovate sepals and three stamens; a female cluster has one flower with six lance-shaped sepals. The female sepals lack tip glands, and the stigma surface is papillose. The fruit stalk does not lengthen during fruit development; seeds have faint papillae.
