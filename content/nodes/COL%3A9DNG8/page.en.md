## Tachigali candelabrum {#s-3988787c10}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Tachigali candelabrum* as an accepted species of *Tachigali* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records comb-like stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), triangular domatia (small cavities) on the petiole or leaf axis, and more than 10 leaflet pairs. Flowers exceed 10 mm and have dense petal hairs; each flower has 10 stamens. The source labels the plant terrestrial.
