## Ditassa fasciculata {#s-151bc80ef9}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ditassa fasciculata* as an accepted species of *Ditassa*. Its Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 habitat field says rupicolous, a source category for plants associated with rock substrates. It gives no locality or range, and the record contains no morphology description; the habitat label remains regional.
