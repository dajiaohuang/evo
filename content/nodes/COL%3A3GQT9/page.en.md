## Which flower colors are excluded from its bracts? {#s-6bb6a97936}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records spiral, basal leaves; a petiole may be present or absent. The flower stalk arises at the same node as the leaves. Its fertile bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are spiral and recorded as not red or orange. Each small flower cluster has two flowers and one membranous bracteole.
