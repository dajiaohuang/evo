## Accepted name and placement {#s-76fd63b52e}
COL26.8 accepts Allobates conspicuus (Morales, 2002) as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Aromobatidae (family) → Allobates (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## A reported distance to velocicantus {#s-823ff017af}
The2020 study’s Table2 reports 9.5% uncorrected p-distance and 10.3% Kimura2-parameter distance between the Allobates conspicuus label and velocicantus, based on a678-bp16S fragment. These metrics are distinct and do not give divergence time or a whole-species identity certificate. The table is a sampled comparison; original accessions, alignments and per-donor TableS1 entries remain independently unreviewed.

## Selected hand and colour contrasts {#s-a836197932}
The2020 author comparison reports fingerI considerably longer thanII, toe fringes and transverse thigh bars for conspicuus, contrasting those selected characters with velocicantus. These are attributed comparative descriptions, not new measurements or a complete key. Similar preserved ventral colours and male size can make superficial identification difficult; the paper does not authenticate all historical museum labels through these traits.
