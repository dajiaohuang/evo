## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-4f0c7b417c}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as ground-dwelling (Terrestrial). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023. The diet field classifies the entry as plant- and animal-eating (Omnivorous); this category does not identify particular prey or foods. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harrington et al. 2018. The active-time field classifies activity as active across day and night (Cathemeral); it is the dataset's timing category. ReptTraits attributes this field to Harding 200. The foraging-mode field records ACT (active foraging). ReptTraits attributes this field to Rhodin et al. 2011.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-7f41dfead2}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Slavens and Slavens, 1999, Feldman et al. 2015. The reproductive-mode field records egg-laying (oviparous); this is the source's category label.
