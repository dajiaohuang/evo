## Goeppertia zebrina {#s-2af77f4356}
## Leaf and flower-cluster characters in the regional record

The Brazilian Flora record describes basal spiral leaves with sheaths; it marks young leaves as ornamented or not and records the petiole as absent. The flower stalk is basal and at a node different from the leaves. Fertile bracts are arranged spirally, with recurved or wavy margins; each cluster has two flowers and two membranous bracteoles. The source also records an entire corolla tube and one outer staminode.
