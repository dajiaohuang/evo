## A bark-associated source entry {#s-91a9a8bf39}
Catalogue of Life COL26.8 and a pinned World Flora Online crosswalk identify Syrrhopodon elatus Mont. as an accepted species name.

The linked Brazilian Flora record is regional data, with habitat categories attributed at dataset level.

Its habitat field is labelled “Corticícola,” meaning bark-dwelling.

The field gives no collection locality, survey date, full range or sampling coverage.

This substrate label is the only biological information in the regional entry; it does not describe the species in full.
