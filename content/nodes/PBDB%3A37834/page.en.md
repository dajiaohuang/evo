## Recorded parent of Paliguanidae {#s-4ab4067792}
Paliguanidae is listed at rank family in the PBDB classification route, with Lepidosauromorpha as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
