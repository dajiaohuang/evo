## Muraltia calycina {#s-2355394b5b}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Muraltia calycina* as an accepted species. The regional account describes a much-branched shrublet to 50 cm, with clustered, linear-oblong leaves that are hairy beneath and end in a small point. Its sessile, axillary flowers are pink, and the capsules have horns. The account records limestone hills as habitat; it does not establish the species’ full range or population variation.
