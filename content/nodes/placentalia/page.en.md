## Placental clocks yield different timelines {#s-556b77f3d7}
The 100–0 Ma Placentalia route is a rounded model envelope, not a fossil range. One molecular analysis estimates a deep placental split at about 103 Ma, while a combined phenomic analysis places its sampled crown diversification after the K–Pg boundary. The two model results differ; neither is a fossil first appearance or an observation of an ancestor.
