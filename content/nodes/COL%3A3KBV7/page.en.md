## Heliophila namaquensis {#s-d19a1e6dab}
## Purple-tinged leaves and small hanging fruits

The Extra Cape flora describes a purplish annual 150–350 mm tall, with fine hairs on stems and branches. Its pinnately lobed leaves are 30–100 mm long and have 3–9 lobes. The flowers are small and white; the hanging fruits are nearly round to inversely egg-shaped and 8–10 mm across.

A separate diagnostic compares it with T. capensis: it describes stronger purple pigmentation, usually across the whole plant, smaller fruits and seeds, looser fruiting racemes, and smaller flowers in all parts. The habitat source says it is apparently confined to northern Namaqualand.
