## Crocidura kegoensis: accepted name and source boundary {#col-name-record}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 (2026-08-20) records *Crocidura kegoensis* as an accepted species, with the recorded name authority Lunde, Musser & Ziegler, 2004. This node currently contains a classification record but no biological observation. The record therefore does not support a description of the animal’s form, habitat, range, or behavior.
