## What marks Lippia sclerophylla? {#s-b80161cd3f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes stiff-haired stems and leathery leaves arranged in opposite pairs, sometimes in whorls of three. The elliptic to egg-shaped blades are rough or smooth above, and woolly with glands underneath. Cylindrical flower clusters bear spirally arranged papery bracts. The calyx is winged; the bisexual flowers are white with salver-shaped corollas.
