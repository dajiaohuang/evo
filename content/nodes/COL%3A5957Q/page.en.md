## Small, fragrant flowers with a short tube {#s-b0ec4e3412}
Tritoniopsis unguicularis has small pale-yellow to creamy-white flowers, sometimes flushed mauve. The tube is about 3 mm long, tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) 9–13 mm and filaments 3–7 mm; the flowers are fragrant.

## Rocky sandstone slopes {#s-93cfba2f7b}
The cited flora records rocky sandstone slopes.
