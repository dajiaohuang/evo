## *Heliconia subulata*: upright clusters with two-ranked bracts {#s-546159043f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes elliptic to oblong leaves with pointed or blunt bases and tapering tips; a leaf stalk may be present or absent. Its upright flower cluster bears persistent bracts arranged in two ranks. The bracts may be folded lengthwise, while the flower buds are described as exposed and turned over relative to the usual orientation.

The fruit is a rounded or angular drupe. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial without naming a locality or survey date. The Flora description is a regional character record; it does not measure how frequently the listed alternatives occur across populations.
