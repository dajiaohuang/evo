## Ardisia copeyana Standl. {#s-2c3fd6c574}
## Large punctate leaves and pyramidal panicles

Ardisia copeyana is a tree with stout branchlets marked by ridges between the leaf stalks. Its large, leathery leaves are smooth, elliptic to obovate and 10.5–20 cm long; tiny punctate glands dot the blades. Terminal, pyramidal panicles carry five-parted flowers. The petals are joined briefly at the base, and the anthers open through small pores at their tips. The fruit is a densely dotted, globose berry about 5 mm across. The cited range is Costa Rica and Panama.
