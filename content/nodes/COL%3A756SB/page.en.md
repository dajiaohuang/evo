## Ormosia nobilis {#s-6a536c3b46}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Ormosia nobilis* as an accepted species of *Ormosia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists 5–13 leaflets and 9–20 pairs of straight, parallel secondary veins. Flowers are vinaceous and longer than 13 mm. The flattened pods are usually 1–2 cm wide; the source lists more than three seeds, each up to 8 mm long and 9 mm wide, with red-and-black or red-to-orange coloring.
