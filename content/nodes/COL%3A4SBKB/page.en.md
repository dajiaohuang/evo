## Rhipsalis sulcata {#s-3cf0540340}
## Three-angled stems and notched tips

This account describes three-angled stem segments with smooth, unarmed areoles when mature. Segment tips are notched, and the determinate growth ends there. Lateral flowers have a wheel-shaped corolla; their perianth segments are white or yellowish. The exposed pericarpel is shorter than the perianth, and the ovoid fruit is white and lacks areoles. The habitat is labeled epiphytic, without a named host or locality.
