## ## Leaflet hairs and flower-color records {#s-51904dcada}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry describes the leaves of *Lupinus crotalarioides* as one-foliolate, with lance-shaped to oval leaflets that bear hairs on both faces and have smooth margins. It records the free distal portion of the stipules as 3.3–56 mm long. The inflorescence bracts are shed and measure up to 10 mm; flower color is listed as purple, lilac, blue or violet. These are the character states recorded in this regional dataset entry, not a quantified account of variation across the species.
