## What leaf, flower and seed traits are recorded for Passiflora amethystina? {#s-5cbd55ef89}
The herb has cylindrical stems and hairless, three-lobed leaves with smooth, glandular edges. Stalked glands occur on the petioles; stipules are kidney-shaped. A solitary flower has oval-lanceolate or ovate bracts, awned sepals and a bell-shaped floral cup. Around the center are several rows of corona filaments; the operculum, a membrane over the nectar chamber, ends in a membranous or threadlike fringe. The ring-shaped limen borders the chamber, and the reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. The fruit is a hairless berry; oval seeds have pitted surfaces.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-8ffc1d5e4e}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
