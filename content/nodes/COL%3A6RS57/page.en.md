## A tomentose leaf underside and alternate clusters {#s-7de494af60}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and no stem hairs. Leaves are lance-shaped, with a fully grooved upper midrib and a tomentose underside (dense, matted hairs). Bracts or cataphylls below leaf nodes are listed as deciduous or present. Small flowering branches are rounded; bracteoles beneath flowers may fall away or be lance-shaped. The field lists two, three, or four inflorescences per axillary shoot, placed near the shoot tip and in whorls, with pyramidal, alternate arrangement.

## A fused calyx and a globose fruit {#s-7acf2d83a5}
The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal, and the floral disc is hairless. The flower cup is top-shaped and hairless. Sepals are fused together, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens by tearing. On the fruit, the calyx lobes wither but remain attached (marcescent); the fruit is globose.
