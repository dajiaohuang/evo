## *Phlegmariurus taxifolius*: hanging stems and distinct fertile leaves {#s-684d7b1c9c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 character field describes hanging or flexuous stems and small, linear to lance-shaped leaves that may differ in size or be spaced apart. The leaves may descend along the stem, spread outward or be twisted at the base; the source records either state. Leaves that bear sporangia differ from the ordinary foliage.

The sporangium, a spore case with two similar valves, is listed as kidney-shaped or globose. Spores are pitted or grooved. The linked habitat field records epiphytic substrate, but names no host, locality or date.
