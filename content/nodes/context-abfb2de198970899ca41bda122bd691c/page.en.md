## The source concept {#s-5dc83a1b83}
AmphiBIO v1 records Pseudepidalea sicula as row Anf0783, within Pseudepidalea, Bufonidae, Anura. Its species concepts follow Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The following traits describe that deposited source record; correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Seasonal activity {#s-c9fad4c432}
The source flags wet and warm; wet and cold as present categories. Seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they are not fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-dd27f9cd61}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body length: 85.5 mm. Body length means snout–vent length for Anura and total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. These are compiled maximum adult values, not measurements of every individual.
