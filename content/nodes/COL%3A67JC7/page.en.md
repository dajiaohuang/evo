## Form in the flora record {#s-4fe8fc5414}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes a prostrate stem, with cataphylls and prophylls fibrous along its length. The petioles are smooth and grooved, with obtuse margins on the upper side and a rounded lower side. The flat, entire leaf blade is oblong, with an acute tip and a rounded or truncate base; glandular dots are absent. The primary veins are blunt on both surfaces, the secondary veins are sunken on the upper surface, and a collecting vein runs along the full upper leaf margin.

The inflorescence is longer than half the leaf length and has a cylindrical peduncle. The flat spathe is oblong or ovate and meets the peduncle at an acute angle. A stipe is present and shorter than the spadix. The berries are greenish or wine-colored.

The regional dataset row cites Coelho and colleagues' 2020 Anthurium treatment in Flora do Brasil 2020.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description; it does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
