## Guarea zepivae {#s-57299df1ea}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology record describes opposite leaflets, axillary or branch-borne inflorescences and a smooth globose fruit.

## Opposite leaflets and a globose fruit {#s-ce4c2955f2}
The leaf rachis is grooved or semi-cylindrical and a terminal bud is recorded. Opposite leaflets are elliptic or oblanceolate, with acuminate tips, eucamptodromous venation and hairs on the lower surface. Inflorescences are axillary or branch-borne. The outer calyx is pubescent; the corolla aestivation is recorded as shortly imbricate and its outer surface is strigose or pubescent. The staminal tube is externally glabrous, the ovary strigose, and each locule has one ovule. The fruit is globose, smooth, and glabrous or pubescent, with an emarginate or truncate apex. The regional morphology field does not establish variation beyond these recorded alternatives.
