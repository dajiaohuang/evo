## Syagrus evansiana: leaves, inflorescence and fruit {#s-9b5d4a33a6}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology field describes stems with remnants of leaf sheaths and lists solitary, subterranean and very short forms. Leaves are green and pinnate, with persistent, fibrous-margined sheaths arranged helically on the stem. Petiole margins are unarmed, and the leaf rachis is slightly arched. Pinnae are leathery, rigid and grouped, inserted at different angles. Their tips are listed as acute, rounded or asymmetrically notched; they are dark green and concolorous.

The source lists the inflorescence as spike-like or paniculate and describes the exposed infructescence as interfoliar. The spadix is grooved and tomentose, while its rachis is sparsely tomentose; the rachillae are arranged helically along it. Fruits are globose, with a yellowish, tomentose epicarp, fibrous and succulent mesocarp, woody and oval endocarp, and homogeneous endosperm.

These descriptors come from one morphology field in the regional Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset. They do not establish a global description, sampling coverage, population variation, sex or life stage. The field does not state whether observations concern wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material.

The dataset records the habitat as “Terrícola,” meaning terrestrial.

This is a habitat category in the regional dataset, not a collection locality or a complete range. The field gives no survey date or sampling-coverage information.
