## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded? {#s-b29dbbd5e7}
The erect stems are woolly with star-shaped hairs. Linear stipules are under 1 cm long. Slightly two-toned, symmetrical leaves are lance-shaped or ovate, with pointed bases and tips; the upper-middle edge is toothed. The upper surface becomes nearly hairless and has sparse star-shaped hairs. Flowers are crowded in the leaf axils or solitary there. The sepals are pointed and the corolla yellow. The dry fruit splits into nine or ten segments (mericarps), each with an awn no longer than 1 mm and star-shaped hairs.
