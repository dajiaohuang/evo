## An older foraging record under the accepted name {#s-e5e489c34c}
Maxomys inflatus is accepted in COL26.8 within Muridae. EltonTraits 1.0 has a same-name, same-family entry with three ecological field groups. Its taxonomy follows earlier bird classifications or MSW3 for mammals, so the name match supports a historical comparison but cannot resolve later species splits or synonymy.

## Food categories and their estimated weights {#s-8e4a9760e4}
The record assigns 30% to fruit, 30% to other plant material (including fungi), 20% to seeds, nuts, and spores, 10% to invertebrates, and 10% to unspecified vertebrates. These are standardized weights based on literature descriptions. The ABC confidence code combines high, reasonable, and partial levels; compilers may also borrow from an ecologically similar congener. The values are not direct diet measurements, and zero in another category would not mean it is never eaten. Nect, Seed, and PlantO are broad source categories, not literal exclusive diets.

## A categorical foraging-stratum assignment {#s-a265c964a6}
EltonTraits places the record in G, the ground category, which the source definition also uses for some aquatic foraging. Code A marks compiler confidence in the source and assignment; metadata attributes mammalian stratum assignments collectively to Nowak (1999). G is not a percentage of time or a claim that foraging occurs only on the ground.

## Night activity is a flag, not a schedule {#s-03b0f615a3}
Feeding activity at night is coded 1. More than one activity field may be marked, so the value is not a share of the day or a schedule that applies at every season and site. The ABC certainty code can include a congener judged ecologically similar; genus- and family-typical activity was not used to fill this record.

## The source labels behind each group {#s-d25a5e18f5}
The selected groups carry Ref_1 and metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, stratum, and activity may draw on different material, and a Ref number must be read within its own reference table. The archive retains full source strings by file, row, field group, and table. The underlying books and papers have not been individually read, so a citation label is not an additional observation.

## Scope of the EltonTraits record {#s-2a67a39fd7}
This is a reading guide to a historical same-name, same-family entry in EltonTraits 1.0, not a complete species monograph. The older name and classification do not establish equivalence with the COL26.8 concept. Food weights translate literature descriptions into coarse relative estimates; they are not direct measures of stomach contents, intake, energy, or observation time. Mammalian ABC diet and activity fields may include a congener considered ecologically similar, so this page does not claim that every value is a direct observation of M. inflatus. Typical genus- or family-level values and records affected by unresolved splits do not fill gaps here. The source books and papers have not been reread individually. Individual, seasonal, and local variation, current range, population, and conservation require other sources.
