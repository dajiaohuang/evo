## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are recorded for Tetrapterys subaptera? {#s-e43465cca6}
This liana has opposite, leathery lance-shaped to oblong-lance-shaped leaves, 5–13 × 2–5.5 cm, with pointed-to-tapering tips and blunt-to-pointed bases. The upper surface is glossy and hairless; the lower is hairless or finely appressed-hairy, with faint glands and usually one or two pairs of plate-like glands near the midrib base. Petioles are 5–10 mm and lack glands; interpetiolar stipules fall early. Terminal flower clusters bear flowers 1.8–2 cm across. The eight oblong calyx glands are about 3 mm long; yellow clawed petals are hairless, 5.5–8 mm long, and finely toothed. Stamens are slightly unequal and joined at the base; the ovary is bristly and the style hairless, flattened near the tip and recurved. The fruit is a rough, hairy samara with reduced wings: lateral wings are 2–8 mm, and the lower wing is absent.

## What distribution does the source report? {#s-e77dfc4538}
The source reports a range from eastern Panama to Colombia’s Pacific coast. This is the account’s range statement, not a current survey.
