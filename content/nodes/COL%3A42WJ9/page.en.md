## Evidence limits {#s-11138efd19}
This page interprets one supported ecological field group; it does not fill the other diet, activity or stratum fields. The EltonTraits 1.0 guide covers historical same-name, same-family foraging records, not a complete species monograph. Matching names and classification do not establish equivalence between historical concepts and COL26.8. Food and avian-stratum percentages are coarse relative weights standardized from literature descriptions, not direct measurements of stomach contents, intake, energy or observation time. Avian A/B diet codes retain source and proportional uncertainty. Mammalian ABC diet or activity codes may refer to a congener judged ecologically similar by the compilers; not every field is claimed as a direct observation of this species. Genus- or family-typical values, non-species-level avian strata and split records needing individual review do not support definite conclusions. Underlying books and papers have not been reread individually. Individual, seasonal and local variation, current range, population and conservation require other sources.

The page withholds diet (source code D1) and activity periods (source code D1) because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits. The specific reasons and original values remain in the source archive; they are not used here to fill definite ecological conclusions.
