## Source classification and concept status {#s-0cd0a970b2}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf2952 records *Litoria xanthomera* under the source classification Litoria (Hylidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and arboreal) {#s-fa580e3e48}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass and microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement value is supplied.

## Night-time activity {#s-3836d40c51}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record, and unlisted activity periods remain unspecified.

## Seasonal activity (wet and warm) {#s-309a6e4d77}
The row carries the seasonal categories wet and warm. They relate activity to the local annual climate, not to fixed calendar months worldwide.

## Maximum adult body mass and snout–vent length {#s-7509044191}
AmphiBIO reports maximum adult body mass of 5.86 g and maximum adult snout–vent length of 56 mm for this row. Its Body_size_mm field is reported as snout–vent length for Anura. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier body-mass entries; this row has not been checked against those changes.

## Life-history measures (clutch size, annual reproductive output and offspring-or-egg size) {#s-877ed868d3}
The row lists 200–1,526 offspring or eggs per clutch, up to 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring-or-egg sizes of 1.45–1.45 mm. These are literature-compiled values; the row does not give the sex, population, date or wild-versus-captive conditions for each value, and the size field may refer to eggs.
