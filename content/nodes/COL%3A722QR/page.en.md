## Lessingianthus lanatus {#s-b77d4cc5f2}
## Woolly undersides and finely toothed leaves

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record does not identify the root type. Stems carry leaves along their length and are unbranched or sparsely branched. Elliptic, leathery leaves have pointed tips, blunt or rounded bases, and finely toothed, non-rolled margins. The upper face is hairless; the underside is woolly.

Solitary, sessile heads have bell-shaped involucres with 7–8 rows of bracts. The source lists heads with 20, 25 or 30 florets. Top-shaped fruits are hairless. The source habitat field classifies the plant as terrestrial.
