## Schnella rutilans {#s-6d1b7352f3}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Schnella rutilans* as an accepted species of *Schnella* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record lists stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf) that fall away and leaves that are entire or shortly bilobed. At flowering, the calyx (the outer layer formed by sepals) is bell-shaped; the upper petal has a woolly outer surface, and the ovary is stalkless. The fruit is dehiscent (splitting open at maturity).
