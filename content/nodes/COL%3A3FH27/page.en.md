## Large nodding flowers with sticky leaves {#s-e34dfbbea0}
Geissorhiza schinzii is 10–20 cm tall, with large, nodding pink flowers marked by dark veins. The perianth tube (the tubular base of the flower’s outer parts) is 24–32 mm long and the tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals) are 35–40 mm; the linear leaves have sticky, thickened margins and midribs.

## Stony sandstone slopes {#s-6d814a656c}
The flora records stony sandstone slopes. It does not give a dated sampling frame or a full distribution boundary.
