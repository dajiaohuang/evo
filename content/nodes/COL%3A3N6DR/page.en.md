## Morphology recorded in the flora dataset {#s-0b99eb523d}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 field describes adventitious roots and globose rhizomes, with stolons commonly present. Its floating leaves are petiolate, elliptical or orbicular, and have acrodromous venation, in which the main veins curve toward the leaf tip. The leaf base is rounded or heart-shaped; the apex is slightly notched. Petioles are cylindrical and septate (divided by internal partitions), with a sheath at the base.

The inflorescence is a cyme, a determinate flower cluster. Its peduncle is described as decumbent, floating, and septate; the field also records propagation by propagules. Bracts are elliptical with blunt tips and shorter than the individual flower stalks (pedicels).

Flowers are bisexual, with cylindrical pedicels. The green sepals are elliptical or lanceolate, keeled, and have prominent central veins. The ovate petals are yellow with orange spots at the base and longer than the sepals. The field records stamens in one to many series, with the outer series sterile; it gives a count of 12–18 stamens and many staminodes (sterile stamens). Filaments are linear-lanceolate. Carpels are cylindrical and linear-lanceolate, narrowing in the upper section; styles are curved and papillose at the tip.

The fruiting structure is described as globose. Its lanceolate follicles are membranous and lack dorsal striae. Seeds are obovate, pubescent, and bear glandular trichomes over the whole surface.

The regional Brazilian Flora 2020 row reports Matias's Alismataceae treatment as the dataset citation.

This is one regional morphology field, not a global description. It does not establish sampling coverage, population variation, or diagnostic scope.

The field does not say whether the observations concern wild, cultivated, managed, or fossil material; none is inferred.
