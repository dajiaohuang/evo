## Crowded linear leaves and yellow flowers {#s-18418c11aa}
This account describes several stiff, striped stems, usually 5–16 cm tall, densely covered with leaves. The sessile, narrow leaves overlap; their tips are sharp, and some have distant, hardened teeth. A few stalkless flowers sit at the stem tips, with yellow, two-lipped corollas about 12 mm long. The source distinguishes it from M. arenaria, which has a prostrate habit, smaller leaves toothed mainly near the tips, and distinctly stalked flowers. Damp flats are recorded as habitat.
