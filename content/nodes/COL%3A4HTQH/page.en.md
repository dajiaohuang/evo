## Feeding research {#s-c1ba8d2eaf}
At Taï National Park in Côte d’Ivoire, a feeding and oral-processing study followed habituated Western red colobus groups alongside black-and-white colobus. During the sampling period, leaves made up about 75% of the red colobus diet reported in the study. The result is a useful snapshot of feeding in those groups and dates, not a fixed species-wide diet.

## Different stages in the mass fields {#s-80c17420a5}
Historical summary values are adult body mass 8070.5 g. Adult mass can combine males, females and adults of unspecified sex and does not provide a species-wide range of individual masses. Birth/hatching mass is not added to this account here.

## Reproduction and developmental timing {#s-347d9513f0}
Available compilation fields are female maturity field (defined by the source as age at first conception) 1481.67 d; offspring per litter or eggs per clutch 1 offspring/eggs; conception or fertilization to birth 161.38 d; age of dependence on food other than maternal milk 724.93 d; d denotes days. The litter/clutch field combines offspring or egg counts, and a fractional summary does not mean a single event produced a fractional individual. Timing fields refer to different events and cannot be exchanged for one uniform reproductive cycle.

The archive’s maximum_longevity_y value is 3.16 years, taking the largest record in the collected material under the source method. It is not typical lifespan, mean wild lifespan, a survival ceiling for every individual or a current repeatedly attainable record.

## How to trace these values to sources {#s-649a56250c}
Same-name sparse rows carry source labels including Jones, K., Bielby, J., Cardillo, M. et al., 2009; Ernest et al., 2003; Delson, E., Terranova, C.J., Jungers, W.L. et al., 2000; Weigl, R., 2005. These labels identify material used by the compilation. Row counts are not animal counts, and repeated citations need not represent independent studies.

## Relative weights of food categories {#s-2ef935a804}
The archive’s nonzero food weights are other plant material (the source category also includes fungi) 80%; fruit 20%. Code ABC pools high, reasonable and partial confidence; some estimates can borrow from an ecologically similar congener. ABC cannot be rewritten as direct observations of this species. These percentages standardize verbal descriptions rather than measure diet composition; zero does not prove an item is never eaten. Categories such as Nect, Seed and PlantO are broader than their everyday names and cannot imply exclusively nectar, seeds or plants.

## Mammalian foraging-stratum assignment {#s-6e547213f0}
The entry is assigned Ar: arboreal; code A records compiler confidence in source and assignment. Metadata collectively attributes mammalian stratum assignments to Nowak (1999). This is a category assignment, not a percentage of time among strata, and does not mean the species acts only there.

## Activity periods can occur together {#s-80eb679441}
The archive marks feeding activity during daytime as 1. Several fields can be 1 together; they are not proportions adding to a daily time budget or schedules shared by every day, season and site. Activity certainty is ABC and can still use ecologically similar congeneric material; genus/family typical activity values do not fill gaps here.

## Trace each field group to its source labels {#s-2ab89f5dd4}
The used groups carry labels Ref_1, metadata:Nowak1999. Diet, foraging stratum and activity may draw on different sources. A Ref number belongs to its own bird or mammal reference table; the same number across tables can identify different works.
