## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-a6676e711a}
COL26.8 records Hydrophis pacificus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-95cf46bfa7}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 1400 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Feldman et al. 2016.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-8a646f9003}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Marine. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Mirtschin et al. 2017.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-81712650e5}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 17. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to De Magalhaes and Costa 2009, Feldman et al. 2015.
