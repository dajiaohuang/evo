## What flowers are recorded for Manulea virgata? {#s-fb7c72b51f}
Manulea virgata is described as a densely hairy, upright shrublet up to 45 cm tall, with toothed leaves. Its loosely arranged flower clusters carry two-lipped yellow flowers with narrow lobes and an orange patch at the back of the throat; the flowers turn brown as they age.

## How does it differ from similar Manulea species? {#s-74110f5f9b}
The source says it can be mistaken for M. ovatifolia, which is itself confused with M. turritis. M. virgata has shorter hairs, a shorter corolla tube, narrower lobes, more hairs in the throat, and a longer stigma relative to the style than M. ovatifolia. The account also calls it closely allied to M. stellata.

## Where does it grow? {#s-7a9d7fbe5f}
The habitat field places it on rocky slopes in scrub. No locality is supplied.
