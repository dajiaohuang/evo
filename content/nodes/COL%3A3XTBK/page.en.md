## Mandevilla clandestina {#s-72876e734e}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mandevilla clandestina* as an accepted species of *Mandevilla*. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record gives one ecological category, terrestrial, without a locality, survey date, or range. No morphology is described in this source entry.
