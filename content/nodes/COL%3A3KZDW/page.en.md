## What does the regional account describe about this shrublet? {#s-656442d563}
The shrublet grows to 60 cm tall, with conspicuously stalked, nearly round leaves whose margins are scalloped. Its small yellow flowers grow in crowded clusters, and the calyx has conspicuous golden hairs.

## Which hairs and flower clusters are highlighted in the revision? {#s-e7a87448b0}
A 1980 revision highlights long, spreading hairs, especially on young flowers, and dense, head-like inflorescences.

## What soil is reported? {#s-6659991075}
The regional account reports that it grows mainly on clay soils; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.
