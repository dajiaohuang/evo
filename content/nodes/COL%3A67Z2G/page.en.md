## Baccharis hyemalis {#s-a6af24129c}
## Linear leaves and corymbs of flower heads

The Brazilian Flora record notes no xylopodium and branches that are either unwinged and smooth or striated. Its developed leaves are sessile, linear, one-veined, and entire or toothed. Flower heads are arranged in corymbs. The source lists either homogamous heads with only short ligulate florets, or heterogamous heads with marginal female or neutral ligulate florets around central tubular male florets. The habitat label is rock-dwelling.
