## Purple florets above glandular leaves {#s-b5a573d448}
The record describes an upright, hairy stem with glands. Alternate leaves are arranged in two ranks, have stalks and oval blades with tiny pointed tips and a shallowly heart-shaped base; both surfaces bear silky hairs and glands. Bell-shaped heads carry 35–45 purple florets. The habitat fields list both rock-growing and terrestrial settings.
