## Traits cited in the Deo Ca comparison {#s-e05126d782}
The 2026 description of L. deocaensis cites coarsely shagreened dorsal skin, a dark reddish-brown belly with faint white speckling, black flank spots, distinct dorsolateral markings and an iris copper above and gold below for Leptobrachella pallida. These traits come from the literature comparison, not newly measured animals of this species in the Deo Ca survey. The comparison contrasts it with the newly described population and is not a complete identification key. It supplies neither trait frequencies across populations nor new measurements of diet, development or reproductive success.

## Conflicting comparator descriptions remain unresolved {#s-122e99d86f}
The 2025 kungfu description cites absence of black spots on the flanks for Leptobrachella pallida. However, the 2026 deocaensis comparison explicitly describes black flank spots for pallida. These two attributed comparator statements conflict; neither a corrected universal state nor a variation explanation is certified here.
