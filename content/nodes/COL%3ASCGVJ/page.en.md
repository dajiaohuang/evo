## Accepted name and placement {#s-8b6d2d2dd1}
COL26.8 accepts Pristimantis romeroae Ron, Carrión, Caminer, Sagredo, Navarrete, Ortega, Varela-Jaramillo, Vidal-Maldonado & Terán, 2020 as a species. Its recorded classification places it along Anura (order) → Strabomantidae (family) → Pristimantis (genus). The name and placement refer to the release issued on 20 August 2026; names in another checklist or fossil database require their own identity evidence.

## Reading route {#s-802ad95d95}
The description records Pristimantis romeroae at one locality on Sumaco volcano’s south-southeastern slope, Napo, Ecuador, at 1,602 m. Reported specimens were found by day in bromeliads, mostly a low spiny species; the authors suggested inaccessible bromeliads may help explain sparse records.

## The Sumaco type-locality record {#s-509272d25d}
The account knew the species from one locality on the south-southeastern slope of Sumaco volcano, Napo, Ecuador, at 1602 m. This record describes the original sampled locality, without establishing the entire range or physiological elevation limits.

## Daytime bromeliad encounters {#s-f5581c098e}
All reported specimens were found by day in bromeliads; most were in a low spiny bromeliad, while one came from a recently fallen tree. The authors suggested that inaccessible bromeliads might contribute to scarce records. That detectability explanation remains a hypothesis, not proof of low abundance or exclusive high-canopy occupancy.
