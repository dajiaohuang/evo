## What range of flower and fruit traits is recorded for Byrsonima schunkei? {#s-76354d7729}
The lower leaf surface is velvety. Flower clusters vary from one to two, three or many flowers. Pedicels are stalked, petals are yellow, and the anthers and fruit bear silky hairs.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-44763d6c8d}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
