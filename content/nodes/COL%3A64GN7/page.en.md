## Achyrocline marchiorii {#s-5590a1912e}
## Woolly stems and yellow clusters of heads

The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records woody or partly woody, usually much-branched roots. Stems are aromatic; only the outermost branches become woody, and dense wool hides glandular hairs beneath. Alternate leaves are densely hairy on both sides, lance-shaped to elliptic or obovate, with entire margins that may be eroded or rolled under. Their bases narrow, and tips range from acute to blunt or small-pointed.

Numerous yellow heads form compact clusters. The narrow involucre is cylindrical to spindle-shaped, with translucent, acute bracts. Disk and marginal florets differ in corolla and style form; both are described as glandular-hairy. The white pappus has blunt tips. Dry fruits are oblong to elliptic, slightly compressed and angled, with a papilla-textured surface. Seeds are curved, oblong or elliptic, and straw-colored or greenish. A habitat field labels the plant terrestrial; no locality or full range is given.
