## Remipede sampling tests the insect-side branches of the tree {#s-df2a0862cb}
A 2019 phylogenomic study added remipedes to taxon-rich genomic matrices. Across its analyses, Remipedia repeatedly grouped as the sampled sister lineage of Hexapoda within Labiocarida. Several deeper pancrustacean branches, including positions involving copepods and cephalocarids, shifted with method and sampling. The tree is a tested relationship hypothesis, not a ladder of known ancestors.
