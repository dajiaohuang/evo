## Twelve comparative animals with coarse skin and pointed digits {#s-0128cba4dd}
The B. muisca description reported twelve B. pandi with mean SVL 44.0 mm, range 35.9–52 mm. It described coarse rather than mostly smooth skin, triangular pointed outlines of the third finger and toe tips, and a tail tapering gradually from broad anteriorly to slender posteriorly. These features are attributed to the comparison in the 2023 paper, not treated as a complete redescription or an invariant identification test for every age and population.
