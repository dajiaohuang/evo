## Inrolled leaves and red-marked white flowers {#s-5b9df14681}
Tritonia lancea grows 10–30 cm tall and has narrow leaves with inrolled margins. Its white, salver-shaped flowers have a tube up to 25 mm long and two short red marks near the bases of the lower tepals (flower parts not differentiated into petals and sepals).

## Sandstone slopes {#s-0ae8364259}
The cited flora records sandstone slopes as habitat.
