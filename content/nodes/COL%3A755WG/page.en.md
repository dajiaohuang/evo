## Sex-coded records in Amphibian Morphology {#s-0f88f37dc9}
Amphibian morphology retains this material under the name Oreophryne cameroni. A shared name does not establish that its source concept matches the COL26.8 entry. The dataset contains 1 source row with 11 trait columns containing values. Recorded sex codes are M. In this dataset, F and M mean the source female and male categories, J means juvenile material, and NA means the category was unspecified. These are compilation rows, not counts of unique specimens. Measurements from different sexes, life stages or publications should not be combined into one diagnostic size. An empty cell does not show that an anatomical structure is absent.

## Different encounter settings in two mountain ranges {#s-2d67ac68f4}
The author collected a subadult (an individual not yet fully adult) male in primary lowland rainforest on a steep Torricelli Mountains slope at 550 m. Two calling animals in the Adelbert Mountains at 850 m perched at night about 2.5–3.5 m above ground; other heard callers were higher. These sampled encounters retain their different stages and sites, without establishing a species-wide preferred elevation or perch height.
