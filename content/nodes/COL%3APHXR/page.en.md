## Calea ferruginea {#s-d0518767a6}
## Loose yellow heads with two kinds of bract

Flora do Brasil 2020 leaves the root type unknown. Stems are erect and pubescent. Opposite, nearly sessile leaves are oval to lance-shaped, with serrated margins, wedge-shaped bases and a hairy surface.

Loose radiate flower heads form corymb-like clusters on 1–3 cm stalks. The bell-shaped involucre has two distinct bract forms; its convex, chaff-bearing receptacle carries folded, lance-shaped paleae. Each head has 10 yellow ray florets with three-lobed ligule tips and either 12–15 or 20–30 yellow disc florets.

The dry fruits are hairless. Their pappus has separate scales, lance-shaped to obovate or oblong; the account gives scale lengths of 0.3–0.5 mm and 2–3 mm. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
