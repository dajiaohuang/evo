## How participants developed frog names {#s-32cbff8393}
The 2016 workshop included 13 volunteer native isiZulu speakers aged 18–55 from five Zululand areas: ten met on 28 November and three on 1 December at Tembe Elephant Park. They viewed frog photographs and discussed names as a group, resolving differences through discussion. The six broad names collected at the workshop did not distinguish all 58 species individually. Afterward, the authors worked with Bongani Mkhize to develop species-level names using that vocabulary, earlier published names and naming guidelines. Group discussion does not provide independent per-person frequencies, agreement across all isiZulu speakers, or a test of recognition accuracy for Leptopelis natalensis.
