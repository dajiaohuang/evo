## Potamogeton illinoensis {#s-fbf759a054}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 records submerged leaves with elliptic, obovate, or oblanceolate forms. It also lists floating leaves as present or absent, and gives alternative petiole and blade states rather than resolving one form. The habitat field says aquatic; this regional source does not establish population variation or a complete range.
