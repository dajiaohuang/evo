## Morphology and field descriptions {#s-790141270d}
The type-series estimate for Petter's sportive lemur was about 0.63 kg, slightly above the paper's 0.54 kg estimate for L. leucopus. The body was gray to gray-brown with a whitish-gray underside and lighter circular patches around the eyes. The authors associated it mainly with deciduous thicket, thorn scrub, and limited gallery forest.

## Known localities and evidence limits {#s-71caade1db}
The type locality and main records were around Beza-Mahafaly. The authors summarized the then-known region as south of the Onilahy River and west of the Linta and Menarandra rivers, while noting that the northern limit needed survey. These are the paper's sampling and range summary, not a complete distribution map.
