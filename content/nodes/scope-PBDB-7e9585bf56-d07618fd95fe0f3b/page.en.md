## What the Patagotitan sample can show {#patagotitan-sample}
The Titanosauria material here is the Patagotitan specimen set and its placement in a sampled sauropod matrix. The study uses that taxon in a body-mass analysis; the specimens and geochronology are direct, while broader body-mass evolution and placement depend on the comparative model and its sampling. This evidence concerns the sampled taxon, not a complete Titanosauria range, origin, or ancestor chain.

## Patagotitan quarry window, not a clade duration {#quarry-window}
The study window is 101.8–101.4 Ma at the directly dated Patagotitan quarry in the Cerro Barcino Formation of Patagonia. It marks this sampled site only; it is not Titanosauria’s first or last appearance, divergence date, continuous presence, or a direct-ancestor record.
