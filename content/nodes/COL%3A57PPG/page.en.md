## Growth and flowering structure {#s-46a39d89f4}
Manning and Goldblatt's 2012 regional account describes a rhizomatous perennial reaching 60 cm tall, with many somewhat fleshy, swollen roots. Its linear leaves are tough and rough to the touch. The flowers are crowded in a hairy, unbranched raceme that is nearly corymb-shaped, on long hairy pedicels; they range from white to grey. The fruits are densely hairy.

These traits come from a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## A diagnostic combination in the source {#s-4b3961385c}
Obermeyer's 1962 account distinguishes the species from *T. ciliata* and *T. hispida* by its rough, woolly covering and its upright pedicels when fruiting.
