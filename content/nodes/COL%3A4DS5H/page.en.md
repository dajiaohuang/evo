COL26.8 records Paroplocephalus atriceps as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Elapidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as tree-dwelling (Arboreal)/ground-dwelling (Terrestrial) [source label: Arboreal/Terrestrial]. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to O'Shea 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0a633770be}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 1. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020. The reproductive-mode field records live-bearing (viviparous); this is the source's category label. ReptTraits attributes this field to Zimin et al. 2022.
