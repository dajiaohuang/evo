## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-1180e27d5c}
COL26.8 records Rhabdops aquaticus as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Colubridae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-8c72c428b7}
The row reports maximum total length (TL) of 600 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Tanaka & Ota 2002.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-47c584360a}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as Aquatic. It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to Deepak et al. 2022.
