## Eriospermum spirale: coiled flower stalk {#s-6c643e70ea}
A South African regional account describes a tuberous geophyte up to 6 cm tall. Its wiry peduncle is coiled, and the leaf is cylindrical in cross-section. Star-shaped flowers are white or yellow and sit on long, persistent pedicels.

These characters and measurements come from a South African regional flora account cited to a 2012 publication. The excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency and does not establish variation across the full range. It does not independently classify the material as native, naturalised, cultivated or domesticated.

The account records sandstone flats and granite outcrops as habitats.
