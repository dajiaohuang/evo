## Sample and measurement scope {#s-efbe117a2d}
COL26.8 accepts Dendrocopos leucopterus in Picidae. AVONET v7 reports the record under the HBW–BirdLife name used in 2020: five birds were measured and four had complete morphological sets. A trait mean may use fewer birds than the overall count.

## Beak dimensions at standard landmarks {#s-6df6d22a93}
Mean beak length from the front edge of the nostril to the tip is 23.3 mm; width and depth at that edge average 7.1 and 7.2 mm. These describe separate axes at standard points, not identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass records {#s-8ce2f82ba6}
The unflattened wing, measured from carpal joint to longest primary, averages 126.4 mm; the tarsus averages 23 mm. A separate compilation reports a mean mass of 67 g (source category Dunning). It does not establish that these measured birds were weighed.

## Woodland and prey categories {#s-a466825192}
AVONET assigns Woodland habitat, an Invertivore trophic niche and an Insessorial lifestyle (primarily perched above ground). These broad classes summarize dominant tendencies, not exclusive use of woodland or an invertebrate-only diet.

## Movement and historical map extent {#s-decbf92a56}
The migration field labels the record sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps extend from 35.9° to 48.46° north. They do not capture all non-breeding movements or establish current occupancy; the map and morphology were compiled separately.

## Limits of this historical profile {#s-f9094e15d1}
AVONET values are means from historical samples, not diagnostic limits or ranges of individual variation. Mass has separate provenance and cannot inherit the morphology sample count. Habitat, trophic niche and migration are broad categories; the map covers historical breeding and resident ranges. The name crosswalk links this record to the current catalogue entry but does not prove that taxonomic concepts remained identical through later splits or lumps. Reproduction, current range, population and conservation require other sources.
