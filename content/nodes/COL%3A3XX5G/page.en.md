## What is recorded for Manulea derustiana? {#s-65225e400e}
This account describes a glandular-hairy annual up to 20 cm tall, with leaves crowded near the base. White flowers form racemes, with a short funnel-shaped corolla tube that is yellow inside.

## How does it compare with Manulea latiloba? {#s-d87369d6b9}
The source distinguishes M. derustiana by the relative width of its calyx lobes, its longer corolla tube, and long balloon-tipped hairs on the calyx. It notes that De Rust lies east of the recorded area of M. latiloba, whose nearest cited locality was the Ladismith road toward Riversdale. This is a comparison of the source’s known localities, not proof of complete separation.

## What habitat is listed? {#s-dcb1e25bed}
The habitat field records deep sand in Karroid scrub. It gives no finer locality.
