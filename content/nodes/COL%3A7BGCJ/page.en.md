## Flattened stem segments and reflexed flower parts {#s-5c520c6912}
The Portuguese record gives a height of 1–2 m or more and describes flattened stem segments with areoles 15–20 mm apart; the areoles may bear spines or be unarmed. Growth is determinate. Flowers have orange to red corollas and reflexed perianth segments, while the fruit is yellow inside.
