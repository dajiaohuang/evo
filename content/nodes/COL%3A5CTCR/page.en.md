## A historical measurement record {#s-3d1e1a1653}
COL26.8 accepts Yuhina nigrimenta in Zosteropidae. The AVONET v7 account uses its HBW–BirdLife 2020 name and records 9 measured individuals, 8 of them with a complete morphological set. Each measurement below is a species summary; the usable sample can vary by trait.

## Three beak dimensions {#s-04c7fab7bc}
The reported means are 6.9 mm from the front edge of the nostril to the tip, 2.8 mm wide and 2.9 mm deep at the nostril edge. Length, width and depth are separate measurements taken at defined landmarks. They describe the compiled sample; they are not field-identification thresholds.

## Wing, tarsus and mass {#s-89753d97ad}
Mean unflattened wing length, measured from the carpal joint to the longest primary, is 55.2 mm; mean tarsus length is 14.9 mm. The compiled mean mass is 9.5 g and carries the source category Dunning. Mass comes from separate species-average information, so it is not a measurement of each of the 9 birds in the morphological sample.

## Habitat and feeding categories {#s-3065c62f3f}
The source categories are Forest for habitat, Invertivore for trophic niche, and Insessorial for primary lifestyle. Insessorial describes a chiefly perching way of life on vegetation or another substrate above ground. These broad categories support cross-species comparison; they do not mean the bird uses only one habitat or food type.

## Migration label and map limits {#s-6292e6b7a7}
The migration category is Sedentary. Historical breeding and resident maps span latitude endpoints 11.02° to 34.47°. Those map limits do not give a complete account of non-breeding movements or show current occupancy throughout the range. The geographic summary and morphological measurements come from separate compilations.

## What these averages do not establish {#s-d360e92adf}
These values are historical species averages, not diagnostic characters or ranges of individual variation. The mass value comes from a separate source and cannot be assigned to the morphological sample count. Habitat, feeding and migration are coarse categories; the map summary covers historical breeding and resident ranges. Name matches and one-to-one historical crosswalks link the record to COL26.8 but do not prove that species concepts remain equivalent after splits or synonymies. Reproduction, current population and conservation status are outside this account.
