## What traits mark Manihot nana in the record? {#s-2f4eda95c1}
The roots are non-tuberous and have a rigid, shapeless xylopodium. The plant is stemless or up to 15 cm tall, with smooth, hairless growth in a rosette and white latex. Basal petioles carry simple, hairless, five-lobed, nearly round leaves with a small tip. Bristle-like stipules are persistent, hairless, and entire. Terminal racemes are loose and unisexual, with basal female flowers on stalks longer than 5 mm but under 2 cm. Their involucral bracts are inconspicuous, oval-lanceolate, hairless, and 0.2–0.4 cm wide; male buds are constricted midway and female buds are pyramidal. The round capsule is smooth, hairless, and unribbed. Seeds are oblong and smooth, with a prominent caruncle.
