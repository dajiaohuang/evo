## The source concept {#s-8e1ec6d65e}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Craugastor saltator as row Anf1324, classified in Craugastor, Craugastoridae and Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). This page describes the deposited source record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.
