## Mitochondrial and specimen scope {#s-28202ce672}
The molecular analysis joins partial mitochondrial 12S and 16S regions. It reports a broad tree of 36 species from 43 source labels and selected Asian subtrees; the nearest placement of Bufoides to the focal group has low support. Results depend on the sampled taxa, and these two mitochondrial regions are not independent nuclear evidence for ancestry or reproductive isolation. Cleared-and-stained and radiographed labels apply only to specimens explicitly so marked. The paper calls its distance measure “uncorrected k2p”; that phrase is the source's terminology, not a universal genus boundary.
