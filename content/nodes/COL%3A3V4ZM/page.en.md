## What features distinguish Linum thesioides in its comparison with L. thunbergii? {#s-16885b3a0d}
The source compares L. thesioides with L. thunbergii and highlights three differences: stipular glands occur only on the lower leaves, the outer sepals have comb-like edges, and the flower stalks bear sparse hairs. This comparison does not establish additional traits beyond those stated.

## What habitat is recorded? {#s-0a1083640c}
