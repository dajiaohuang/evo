## Zapoteca scutellifera {#s-c30a9ecdd6}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Zapoteca scutellifera* as an accepted species of *Zapoteca*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 describes climbing stems and leaves with crater-shaped nectaries (nectar-secreting glands). Its axillary flower clusters range from solitary to grouped, and the fruit valves are membranous. The habitat field says terrestrial. These source fields identify features recorded for this regional flora entry, not the species’ complete variation.
