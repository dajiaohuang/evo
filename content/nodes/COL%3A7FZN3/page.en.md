## Xyris subsetigera {#s-31ab3d5407}
## Four bracts around a small, wrinkled-leaf spike

The record describes flat leaf blades with a crosswise wrinkled surface and a 20–70 cm flowering stalk. One or two raised ridges bear hairs; the egg-shaped to nearly spherical spike has four sterile bracts and up to ten flowers. The habitat field labels it terrestrial.
