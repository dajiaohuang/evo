## The source concept {#s-e1fc00385b}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Adelophryne patamona as row Anf2000. Its source classification is genus Adelophryne, family Eleutherodactylidae and order Anura. Its species concept follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below describe this archived record; its correspondence to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-3a891b65cf}
In this row, the recorded foraging-stratum categories are terrestrial. These broad categories do not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat entries carried over from earlier compilations, but this row has not been checked against those revisions. Treat the archived category as provisional until that check; no replacement value is inferred, and the revision notice does not mean every earlier entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-a7fd7f3974}
The compilation lists maximum adult body length as 23 mm. For Anura, length is snout–vent length; for Caudata and Gymnophiona, total length is used. These are literature-compiled maxima, not measurements of every individual.
