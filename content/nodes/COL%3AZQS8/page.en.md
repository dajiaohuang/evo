## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Croton pannosus? {#s-5265ff5aab}
Young branches have star-shaped or outward-radiating star hairs. The short-stalked oval or elliptic leaves have simple, star-shaped, or porrect star hairs above and star-shaped hairs beneath; their side veins loop toward the margin. Margins are entire, nectar glands are absent, and young branch tips lack leaf-like stipules. Terminal thyrses have unisexual flower clusters near the base. Male and female flowers are shortly stalked; male flowers are wheel-shaped. Female sepals are separate and alike, the style is many-lobed, and petals are absent. The bud arrangement is unknown, as is the fruit columella.
