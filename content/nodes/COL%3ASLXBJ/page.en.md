## A proposed broader synonymy was explicitly deferred {#s-43048876f9}
The article cited earlier work synonymizing similipalensis and terebrans with sanctisilvaticus. Its own comparison then found sanctisilvaticus closely related to bombayensis and discussed possible conspecificity because morphology, calls and mitochondrial distances were difficult to separate. Crucially, the authors explicitly refrained from a new taxonomic action pending comprehensive population sampling across the known ranges. The pinned catalogue retains sanctisilvaticus and bombayensis as separate accepted readers here. The paper’s proposal is therefore recorded as an unresolved source interpretation, not applied as a merge of names, specimens, distributions or species-specific biological evidence.

## Shallow distances and similar calls have interpretation limits {#s-ca7a87c893}
The comparison reported 16S distances of 0.8–1.5% between bombayensis samples from northern Karnataka, Goa and Maharashtra and sanctisilvaticus samples from Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and Andhra Pradesh. Each named set had reported internal distances up to 0.8%, touching the lower end of the between-set range. Calls were described as similar and near 3 kHz. The call-duration sentence has an inconsistent ordering of taxon names and four duration values, so it is not used here to assign a precise duration to sanctisilvaticus. Similarity and shallow distance do not themselves certify contemporary gene flow or remove the need for broader concept reconciliation.
