## *Sinningia valsuganensis* {#s-b743a4c2e9}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — opposite arrangement, egg-shaped, tapering tips. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.
