## Short tubes and red-edged nectar marks {#s-d59ad38fb8}
Gladiolus phoenix is 50–75 cm tall, with branched stems and small, dark-pink flowers. Their short tube is 18–20 mm long; each lower tepal (a flower part not differentiated into a petal and a sepal) bears a white, lozenge-shaped nectar guide outlined in red. Short stolons (creeping stems that extend from the plant) from the corm (a swollen underground storage stem) carry clusters of small cormlets.
