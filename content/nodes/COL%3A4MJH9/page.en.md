## Tympanum, ventral skin and patterned dorsum {#s-24d672d806}
The local diagnosis cites large size, a prominent tympanum, smooth ventral skin, a reddish-brown dorsum, dark canthal and postorbital stripes, dorsal chevrons and transverse limb bars. The paper identifies it as the local member of the conspicillatus group. These cited traits are not new body-size statistics or a molecular verification of all records under the accepted name.

## Western slope and pasturelands near Loja {#s-1e696b89b4}
The revision reports lymani as common in Abra de Zamora and the most common frog species near Loja, encountered on the western slope in evergreen upper montane forest below2600m and pasturelands neighboring the city. These relative descriptions concern the authors’ local encounters. Presence in pastureland does not prove that agricultural conversion benefits population survival or that the species has no conservation threats.
