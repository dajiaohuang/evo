## Name and classification in COL26.8 {#s-d166fb9840}
COL26.8 lists Brachycephalus pulex Napoli, Caramaschi, Cruz & Dias, 2011 as a species in Anura, family Brachycephalidae, genus Brachycephalus. This is the classification in the release dated 20 August 2026; it does not reconcile names in other taxonomic or fossil databases.

## What the linked records represent {#s-af752e7b2e}
COL26.8 supplies the pinned accepted name. AmphiBIO preserves an older row based on the Frost 2011 classification, while Amphibian morphology v3 contains a separate measurement row. Their classifications and purposes differ, so the historical traits are not automatically facts about the current COL26.8 species concept. The linked records show what each source actually stores.

## An older compilation flags terrestrial foraging {#s-01eb1335fb}
AmphiBIO row Anf0280 records Ter=1 under the compilation's Frost 2011 classification. It is a broad foraging-stratum category, not a measure of how often or how long an individual used it; the row does not describe seasonal or life-stage changes. TetrapodTraits v3.0.0, released 5 August 2026 with revision history dated 9 March 2026, reports corrections to some inherited fields, including Ter. This row has not been compared field by field, so its historical flag remains provisional; no corrected value is inferred and the revision does not show that every source entry is wrong.

## The older row lists arthropods as adult food {#s-459cdb96e8}
AmphiBIO row Anf0280 marks Arthro=1, recording arthropods as an adult food category in this Frost 2011-based compilation. AmphiBIO gathered such categories from literature reports using expert judgement, observation or stomach contents. The flag is not a complete diet or a measured share of food intake, and its equivalence with the current species concept is unresolved.

## AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult SVL of 8.4 mm {#s-480ae9f671}
AmphiBIO row Anf0280 reports a maximum adult body length of 8.4 mm. For Anura, this field means snout–vent length (SVL). It is a compiled maximum, not a distribution of measurements or a claim that every adult reaches that size. The row follows the source's Frost 2011 concept; equivalence with the current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## A separate morphology table records coded measurements {#s-bf99b3f5ba}
The morphology v3 table places the name in Anura, family Brachycephaloidea, rather than the Brachycephalidae used by COL26.8; that source classification does not settle concept identity. Its row 857 has one record, with deposited values SVL 8.2, HL 3.3 and HW 3.3, plus values in ED, IOD, IND, UEW, EN, NS, FL, TL and THL. The source reader leaves sex unspecified.
