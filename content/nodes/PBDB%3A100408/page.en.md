## A family route through seven species pages {#s-2675e69acf}
The Paleobiology Database (PBDB) records Anurognathidae as a family. This route links to occurrence guides for seven species in the fixed pterosaur roster. Its database parent path helps readers find records; it does not make that hierarchy a universal phylogenetic arrangement.

## Five starting points, four recorded strata {#s-30cc32d698}
Start with Anurognathus ammoni, Batrachognathus volans, Dendrorhynchoides mutoudengensis, Dendrorhynchoides curvidentatus and Jeholopterus ningchengensis. Across the seven species represented here, the guide selects seven occurrences from seven collections. Their recorded strata include Solnhofener Plattenkalk, Karabastau, Tiaojishan and Yixian. Compare each page’s locality, age bracket and identification notes on their own terms. These record counts are not counts of fossils, animals or ancient populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-881ea36719}
This route covers selected records for seven species; it is not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological account of Anurognathidae. Occurrence and collection counts do not measure specimens, individuals, population size or sampling effort, while broad database age bounds do not directly date the family’s origin or extinction. PBDB parentage is used for navigation and remains separate from Catalogue of Life concepts and phylogenetic hypotheses.
