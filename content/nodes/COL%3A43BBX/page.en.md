## Mikania involucrata {#s-8609ab5423}
## Mucronate leaves and ciliate head bracts

The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes twining, striate branches with solid pith; the branches are listed as smooth or villous. Opposite leaves are triangular to ovate, leathery, and have blunt bases and a tiny sharp tip. Their margins are entire, rolled under, or ciliate. The upper face is nearly smooth with sparse glands, while the lower face is dotted with glands; the veins are pinnate and loop near the edge.

Corymb-like clusters carry stalked heads. Long, linear bracts occur near the top of each head stalk; the oblong bracts around a head are fringed and acute. The funnel-shaped corolla widens beyond its tube and has triangular, smooth lobes. The dry fruits (cypselae) bear glands. The habitat field labels the plant terrestrial, but gives no locality or complete range.
