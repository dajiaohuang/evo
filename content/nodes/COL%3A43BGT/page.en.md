## *Mikania pachychaeta*: three-veined leaves and loose panicles {#s-74efca8d99}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, elliptic leaves with tapered bases, pointed tips, and three strong veins from the base. Both surfaces are hairless, and the leaves are thin. Erect or hairless branches carry loose panicles of stalked heads. Their bracts are finely hairy and blunt-tipped; the corolla limb is bell-shaped and about as long as its tube. The fruitlets are glandular.
