## Asemeia ilheotica {#s-4fa8331e1c}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Asemeia ilheotica* as an accepted species of *Asemeia*. In Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147, the recorded branches are pubescent, and the leaves are ovate with rounded to blunt bases and pointed tips. Its racemes are somewhat loose; the outer sepals have ciliate margins. The seeds bear a horny caruncle, a small appendage at the seed. This regional description does not include a habitat field.
