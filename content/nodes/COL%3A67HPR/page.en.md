## An elliptic blade and ovate spathe {#s-da32d428e0}
The flora field describes erect stems, flat elliptic leaf blades with acute tips and blunt bases, and an ovate spathe (a bract around the flower cluster) set at an acute angle to its stalk. Berry color is left uncharacterized. These descriptors come from a regional taxonomic entry and do not quantify variation.
