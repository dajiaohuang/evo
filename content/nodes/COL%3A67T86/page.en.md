## A name fixed to COL26.8 {#name-card}
Catalogue of Life release COL26.8 records Arremon taciturnus (Hermann, 1783) as the accepted species name (published 2026-08-20). This card preserves that version's identity; the name entry alone does not describe anatomy or ecology.

## Nesting of the Pectoral Sparrow (Arremon taciturnus) in Southeastern Peru {#study-account}
Valdez-Juarez and Londoño monitored nests near Peru's Manu National Park during August-December of 2008 and 2009. They found 15 dome nests, each with two eggs. In observed nests, females incubated while males supplied most nestling food. The authors compare these records with other Arremon nests; the evidence is specific to this site and period.

## Research preview {#study-preview}
A two-season study near Peru's Manu National Park found 15 dome nests and recorded female incubation and male-led provisioning.
