## The deposited caecilian record {#s-e20838aceb}
This page summarizes the CaecilianTraits version 4 record for Siphonops paulensis, filed under Siphonops in Siphonopidae (species row 209). That is the source’s identification; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been checked. The compilation keeps individual, population and species entries distinct.

## Measurement material {#s-ac39f5c901}
There are ten measurement rows, all classified as individual-level; the source lists no population-level or species-level measurement rows. This counts records, not animals in a population or necessarily unique specimens. The rows retain specimen identifiers, sex codes and references. Before comparing body dimensions, check whether each measure came from fresh or preserved material or is a summary.

## Compiled habitat categories {#s-d9e241adcd}
The compilation assigns the broad habitat category Terrestrial and the microhabitat category Fossorial, meaning associated with burrowing. Its habitat field also lists tropical and subtropical moist broadleaf forest, dry broadleaf forest, grassland, savanna and shrubland, and flooded grassland and savanna. These entries do not quantify use or define a complete range.

## Compiled reproduction category {#s-e68efe2845}
The source category is oviparous, meaning egg-laying. This classification alone does not describe parental care, breeding timing or variation among populations.

## Compiled anatomical counts {#s-03b9fae430}
