## Two mitochondrial markers and uneven references limit inference {#s-1c2c40f289}
For Hylarana lepus, the study’s identification workflow combines COI BIN/ABGD clustering,16S reference comparison and morphology/type-locality context. COI and 16S are both mitochondrial and do not constitute independent nuclear confirmation or reproductive-isolation tests. The authors use neighbor joining to cluster similar sequences, explicitly limiting its use for evolutionary-history inference. Most per-species material came from only one or two localities, and not all GenBank vouchers could be morphologically examined. Reference labels and missing sequences therefore remain evidence limits. Fieldwork took place in May–June 2012 and March–April 2013; GenBank/BOLD comparison queries refer to the 2016 source-library snapshot, not a refreshed present-day database audit.
