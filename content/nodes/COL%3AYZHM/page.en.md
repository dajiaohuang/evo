## What leaf and flower traits identify Couepia venosa? {#s-f6d179d745}
The record describes oblong-elliptic leaves with a dense, woolly covering of hairs. Flowers grow in panicles. The receptacle is bell-shaped and bears either 25–45 or 55–60 separate stamens in the source field. The fruit’s outer wall is smooth and hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-1f7cdadb6b}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial. No collection locality or date is supplied.
