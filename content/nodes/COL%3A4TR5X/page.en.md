## Long-lived leaves that feel prickly {#s-90085f60e0}
This perennial succulent herb grows to 15 cm and is explicitly described as not spinescent. Its decumbent or prostrate stems carry leaves that persist for several years and clasp the internodes. The three-sided blades are 25–30 by 4 mm, with a keel tooth, papillae and a prickly feel. Pink petals and staminodes have paler pink filaments. Dry grassland and Dwarf Karroid Shrubland are listed, including rock pans and open spaces.
