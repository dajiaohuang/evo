## AmphiBIO species record {#s-f2dd361107}
What does AmphiBIO v1 record for Scinax canastrensis? Row Anf2629 places it in genus Scinax, family Hylidae and order Anura. The compilation follows Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) for its species concepts. The trait values below belong to this historical source record; its equivalence to a current COL26.8 species concept has not been established.

## Foraging categories in this record {#s-72bc47e71a}
The AmphiBIO row lists these broad foraging categories: terrestrial (land), aquatic (water), and arboreal (trees or other vegetation). They do not describe seasonal or developmental changes in detail. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some microhabitat categories carried over from earlier compilations, but does not identify whether this row was affected. These remain the AmphiBIO labels; no replacement category is inferred.

## Seasonal categories in the compilation {#s-4dd7b683b6}
The AmphiBIO row lists these seasonal categories: wet and warm; dry and warm; dry and cold. They compare activity conditions with the local annual climate; they are not fixed calendar months that apply worldwide.

## Adult measurements in the compilation {#s-79dcb59ac5}
The AmphiBIO literature compilation lists these maximum adult values: maximum adult body length: 38 mm. For Anura, body length means snout–vent length (the distance from the snout tip to the vent); for Caudata and Gymnophiona, it means total length. These literature-based maxima are not measurements of every individual.

## Developmental category in the record {#s-4768b911a6}
The AmphiBIO record assigns this source concept the developmental category “a larval stage”. This field does not describe parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
