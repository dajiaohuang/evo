## Source classification and concept status {#s-58f7417fb2}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3044 records *Hylodes sazimai* under the source classification Hylodes (Hylodidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (terrestrial and aquatic) {#s-23eb960723}
AmphiBIO records terrestrial and aquatic as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-be5947172b}
The row is marked for daytime activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; night-time activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-bda4fbf1c0}
AmphiBIO records activity in both wet and warm, and dry and cold conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-3b6763d0df}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 30.3 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-9578ad69be}
AmphiBIO reports both the minimum and maximum number of offspring or eggs per clutch as 109. The row does not report a maximum number of reproductive events per year or an offspring-size value. These counts are literature-compiled; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions.

## Developmental strategy {#s-11fe676b3a}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
