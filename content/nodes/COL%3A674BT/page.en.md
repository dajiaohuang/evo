## What does the record say about Annona nitida? {#s-eefc8e4877}
The field describes an upright tree with fissured bark and leaves that taper to a pointed tip. Cream-colored flowers grow opposite leaves or at branch ends; each has six petals, and the sepals are slightly joined at their base. The aggregate fruit is syncarpous. Seeds are reddish brown and smooth.
