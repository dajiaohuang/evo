## PBDB hierarchy for Hadrosauridae {#s-cead948adb}
In the PBDB parent hierarchy, Hadrosauridae is recorded at family rank. This guide links 72 fossil-species entries from the selected page set. The count describes this guide, not every descendant, and a list of extinct records does not mean that the whole group is extinct. A PBDB parent path is a database classification, not a phylogenetic hypothesis.

## Collections in this selected guide {#s-91ee90d65f}
The guide begins with Brachylophosaurus canadensis, Hadrosaurus foulkii, Ornatops incantatus, Acristavus gagslarsoni, Maiasaura peeblesorum. Across the selected species pages, 244 occurrence records map to 235 collections; named stratum examples include Judith River, Claggett, Oldman, Woodbury. Each collection record retains its own age interval and identification qualifications, so comparisons belong at that record level. Occurrences and collections are not counts of specimens or populations.

## What the selected fossil records do not establish {#s-3390d9c27d}
This selection covers a subset of fossil records, not a complete descendant catalogue or a shared ecological profile. Occurrence and collection totals do not measure specimens, individuals, populations or sampling effort; pooled outer age bounds are not the group origin or extinction. PBDB parent paths organize these records separately from COL classification and phylogenetic hypotheses.
