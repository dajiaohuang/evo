## ## Petiole glands and nearly straight pods {#s-8d767b46e8}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records no xylopodium and 2–5 pairs of oblong to elliptic-oblong leaflets. Extrafloral nectar glands occur on the petiole. The flowers have ten stamens and petals of different forms, with colors listed as yellow, orange or reddish. The oblong pods are nearly straight rather than twisted at maturity, 2.5–5 cm long, with pubescence along the suture. Seed characters are listed as unknown. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
