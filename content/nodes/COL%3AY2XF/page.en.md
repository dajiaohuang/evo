## ## Grouped trunks and long panicles {#s-833a2ae0e8}
Brazilian Flora 2020 records two or more trunks growing together and leaves with 2–3 pairs of nearly equal-sized, hairless leaflets. The elliptic, oval or oblong leaflets are sub-leathery, with blunt or acute tips and flat margins; surface dots are absent. Loose panicles are two to three times longer than the adjacent leaf. The sepals and ovary are glabrous. The slightly sickle-shaped pod is 2–3.5 cm long, and the seed bears a white aril. The dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial.
