## *Grazielia nummularia*: rounded leaf bases and spindle-shaped fruits {#s-d2840e5cbd}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records stems with sparse long hairs. The egg-shaped leaves have blunt tips and rounded or heart-shaped bases. The fruitlets are spindle-shaped, and the field records a distinct attachment base. Its substrate entry is the broad category “terrestrial,” without a locality or fuller habitat description.
