## What leaf and seed traits are recorded? {#s-2e0e484d6d}
The stems are hairy to nearly hairless. The leaves have linear to lance-shaped stipules and five to seven lobes; the lobes are ovate with abruptly pointed tips and are hairy. The ovary has three to five carpels. Each carpel contains six to eight seeds. The seeds are ovate and bear white to grey hairs.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-52a07972f1}
The Brazilian Flora record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
