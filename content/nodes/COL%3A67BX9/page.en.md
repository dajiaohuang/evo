## Anthochortus crinalis {#s-fed2086b45}
## Bumpy stems and bristles that turn back

The South African regional flora describes this as a perennial that often forms a cushion and reaches 80 cm, with densely branched stems covered in small rounded bumps. Female flowers occur singly or in pairs within spikelets (small flower clusters); the bracts (leaf-like structures beside flowers) are bristle-like and turn back at their tips. The same account lists damp slopes and seeps as habitat, a regional record rather than a summary of the species’ full global range.
