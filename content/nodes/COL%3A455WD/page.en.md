## Blue flowers above rough-haired leaves {#s-aa4f73bd13}
The account describes a smooth stem that dries purplish-black. Elliptic leaves taper into long, sheath-based stalks and have fringed edges; the blade bears stiff bulb-based hairs above and near the tip below, but is otherwise smooth. The blue flowers form near-terminal clusters; their stalks and the outside of the calyx are hairy. The fruits are smooth and lens-shaped. It grows in grassland, along watercourses, among rocks and in shaded bush margins.
