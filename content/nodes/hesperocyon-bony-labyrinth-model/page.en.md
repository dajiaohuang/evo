## Can the inner ear suggest how Hesperocyon hunted? {#s-04d3bdf81c}
Researchers compared micro-CT reconstructions of left bony labyrinths in a 36-specimen sample, including Hesperocyon gregarius, using multivariate shape analyses. The model assigns Hesperocyon to a pounce-hunting category and reports 82.05% overall classification accuracy. That result depends on living-animal categories, body-size scaling, and phylogenetic assumptions; the fossils preserve an inner ear, not a hunt or social behavior.
