## A high-set leaf and a narrow floral cup {#s-131848d817}
Moraea pyrophila is 15–30 cm tall and has a single foliage leaf inserted well above the ground. Its yellow flowers form a narrow cup that completely encloses the stamens; the plant’s short leaf seldom exceeds the stem.
