## Source classification and concept status {#s-f285849dae}
AmphiBIO v1 row Anf3060 records *Afrixalus dorsalis* under the source classification Afrixalus (Hyperoliidae, Anura). The compilation follows *Amphibian Species of the World* (Frost 2011); this source concept has not been reconciled with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Foraging categories (aquatic and arboreal) {#s-d7a510dab1}
AmphiBIO records aquatic and arboreal as broad categories for this row. They do not describe fine-scale habitat use or changes by season or life stage. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some earlier microhabitat entries; this row has not been checked against those changes, so no replacement category is supplied.

## Daytime and night-time activity {#s-e0f35541d8}
The row is marked for night-time activity. This broad category is not a continuous behavioural record; daytime activity is unspecified.

## Seasonal activity {#s-0afdcddce2}
AmphiBIO records activity in wet and cold conditions. These seasonal categories compare conditions during activity with the local annual climate; they do not identify fixed calendar months.

## Maximum adult snout–vent length {#s-729fd114f5}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult snout–vent length of 38 mm for this row. This is a literature-compiled maximum, not a measurement of every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-77c2a7edcd}
AmphiBIO reports 66–100 offspring or eggs per clutch, a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year, and offspring or egg sizes of 1.7–2.2 mm. The dataset compiles extrema and counts from literature; the row does not separately specify sex, population, measurement date or wild versus captive conditions. Its size field may refer to offspring or eggs.

## Developmental strategy {#s-5bddf55b00}
The deposited record flags a larval stage. This field does not specify parental care, breeding location or seasonal timing.
