## Kniphofia caulescens {#s-d2702da24f}
Kniphofia caulescens has short leafy stems topped by dense racemes of greenish-yellow to creamy-yellow flowers. The buds are coral-pink to flame-coloured, and the regional account records flowering mainly from January to March. It grows in high mountain grassland on peaty soils, marshes and seepage areas.
