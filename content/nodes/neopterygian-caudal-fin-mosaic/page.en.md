## A developmental mosaic in the neopterygian tail fin {#s-7a785d9e38}
Adult anatomy and developmental series in sticklebacks, zebrafish, gar, bowfin, paddlefish and bichir reveal a hypural–diastema complex: a gap among the tail-fin bones that support fin rays. Early fin rays also contribute to external symmetry. Because the complex occurs in gars as well as teleosts, it is not an unconditional teleost synapomorphy. Descent with losses, parallel origins and latent parallelism remain alternatives; soft tissues in the complex are rarely visible in fossils.
