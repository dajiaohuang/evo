## *Goeppertia propinqua*: paired flowers with two bracteoles {#s-586d6da6ff}
In the Brazilian Flora 2020 profile, leaves arise basally and spiral around the shoot; their young blades are not ornamented. The flowering stalk emerges from the same node as the leaves. All bracts are fertile and spiral, and each two-flowered cluster has two membranous bracteoles, the small leaf-like structures beneath the flowers.

The corolla tube is cleft, and one outer staminode has a non-petal-like callus at its tip. These details come from a regional morphology record, without a linked habitat or locality field.
