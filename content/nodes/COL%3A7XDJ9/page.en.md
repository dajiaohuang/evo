## Lanceolate leaves with a channelled midrib {#s-7cc5eb3d29}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records monopodial growth and simple hairs on the stem. The leaves are lanceolate, with a channelled midrib on the upper surface; the underside is listed as either glabrous or puberulent (covered with fine, short hairs). Bracts or small scale-like cataphylls below leaf nodes are deciduous. The source presents these alternatives in a regional dataset field without frequencies.

## A top-shaped flower cup and a globose fruit {#s-8abc5178fd}
The rounded inflorescence branchlets carry lanceolate bracteoles. One or two inflorescences are listed per axillary shoot; they may be axillary or terminal, in opposite pyramidal forms. The anther sacs are described as displaced. Hairs on the floral disc are reported at the style base or only on the staminal ring. The flower cup (hypanthium) is top-shaped and pubescent. Sepals are free, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx is listed as not opening by tearing. Its lobes enlarge on the globose fruit. The field does not explain the anther-sac displacement or report frequencies for the listed alternatives.
