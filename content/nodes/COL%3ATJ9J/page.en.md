## Leaflet, petal, and pod size ranges {#s-6716336483}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field lists 2–6 leaflet pairs, rarely 7. Leaflets are broadly obovate to nearly round or elliptic-obovate, 0.8–2.8 cm long and 0.7–2.3 cm wide. Yellow petals are 1.5–2.2 cm by 1.2–1.9 cm; brown pods are 2.8–5.3 cm by 0.5–0.9 cm. It also describes 10 stamens and depressed, obovoid seeds with a shiny, spotted coat. The habitat field says terrestrial; no population frame, locality, date, or wild/cultivated status is given.
