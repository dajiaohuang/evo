## European records after separation from P. vespertinus {#s-2113ec9fc9}
Under its 2019 species treatment, the review described records across western, central and eastern Europe at 0–810 m. It included southern Scandinavian and Baltic coastal records, northern Italian populations particularly in the Po Valley, and isolated populations in central France and western Bulgaria. The eastern contact with P. vespertinus was described from Russia’s Kursk region toward the Black Sea. These boundaries belong to the review’s taxonomic and geographic synthesis; previously pooled conservation assessments and cited declines are not treated as a current species-specific status evaluation.

## Repeat annotations are computational observations {#s-5cc1384193}
The authors built species-specific repeat libraries with RepeatModeler2, classified unknown elements with RFSB and supplemented the libraries with vertebrate repeats before applying RepeatMasker. Its hits are termed insertions in the paper, but a hit is not independently demonstrated to be a complete, expressed or mobile element. Superfamily labels can aggregate distinct families. Element counts, total occupied length and genome proportions are separate quantities; overlapping hierarchy categories cannot safely be added as independent fractions. No new repeat annotation or expression experiment for Pelobates fuscus was performed here.

## Across-species correlations do not prove this species mechanism {#s-9b764e7a47}
The study compared61 assemblies using phylogenetically adjusted models, an earlier pruned tree, contig-N50 covariates and multiple-comparison correction. Recently active Ty3/DIRS1-associated sequences correlate with genome size across that sample, but recent activity is a divergence-based proxy, not direct observation of current transposition in this species. Length and open-reading-frame similarity filters likewise do not demonstrate active protein expression. The authors' suggested developmental constraints are comparative hypotheses; they do not establish a causal TE effect on this species' larval duration or clutch size.
