## What leaf and flower traits distinguish Mabea anadena? {#s-f0a1db1490}
The elliptic leaves have pointed, tapering tips and blunt or wedge-shaped bases. Their upper surfaces are hairless; the lower surfaces bear red hairs. Marginal glands are present, while glands at the leaf base are not larger than those along the edge. Branched flower clusters carry three male flowers in each umbrella-shaped subcluster, without glands on the bracts. Female flowers have six sepals shorter than the ovary. The smooth fruit is over 15 mm long, with a style column under 10 mm.

The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
