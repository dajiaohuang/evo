## Leaves and flowers {#s-d318283b87}
Snijman's 2012 regional account describes a corm-bearing geophyte 10–30 cm tall, with membranous basal sheaths. It has 1–4 lance-shaped leaves, which may be only just emerging at flowering; they are deeply pleated, with bristly ribs. The flowers are yellow. The ovary is subterranean and bears a bristly beak 50–100 mm long.

These measurements and traits come from a regional species account published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## Habitat {#s-6adf54e043}
The account records the species on clay and granite flats and lower slopes.
