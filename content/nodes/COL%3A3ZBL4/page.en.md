Clutch/litter summaries can use midpoints of reported means or, when means are unavailable, extrema; they are not necessarily arithmetic averages of observed clutches. ## Bite force in three adult black caimans {#s-ad3e7189a7}
In Erickson et al.'s 2012 comparison, 3 sexually mature black caimans were tested, with a mean sample body mass of about 59 kg. At the prominent molariform tooth near the back of the upper jaw, the study reported a taxon-representative bite force of 2,696 N; the sampled values ranged from 1,779–4,310 N. Researchers used the highest of 3–5 bites per animal. Across species, body mass was the main predictor of bite force, while rostral proportion explained little additional variation. This is a standardized measurement from a limited institutional sample, not a field-performance constant or population range.
