## Variable flower clusters and four- or five-chambered ovaries {#s-020b5a9439}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic to oblong or oval-elliptic leaves. Their tips may be acute or tapering; bases range from heart-shaped or blunt to rounded. The upper midrib is flat or slightly rounded, the lower one raised and rounded, and the veins are conspicuous. Flower clusters may sit in leaf axils, on branches or on the trunk. Each cluster has one to five or more branches; an axis bears one to three internodes and one to five or more flowers. Bracts and bracteoles fall as flowers open. Flower stalks are listed at 1.1–2 mm, 2.1–3 mm or at least 3.1 mm. Flowers are five-part, with sepals free in bud; their tips may be acute, blunt or rounded. Petals may be yellow, white, pink or lilac. Glands lie behind and opposite the anther sacs, at lengths up to 0.6 mm or 0.7–1.2 mm. Tail-shaped stamens are listed as up to 0.5 mm or 0.6–1.0 mm. The ovary has four or five chambers. The habitat field says “terrícola,” or terrestrial.

## A terrestrial category beside broad floral ranges {#s-6e8bada9a0}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It does not provide a collection locality, survey date or range coverage, or specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The floral ranges and habitat label are separate records and do not establish a complete ecology.
