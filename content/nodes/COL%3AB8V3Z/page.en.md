## What the living sample resolves {#s-2811c6eacd}
Sea-spider relationships among living groups have been sampled across 89 species and every extant family. Their fossil history is less certain: *Cambropycnogon* may belong to the sea-spider total group, but it is not a secure crown first appearance. The atlas therefore presents 500–0 Ma as a contested fossil-to-living envelope.

The phylogenomic analysis integrates 89 sea-spider species across all living families and supports a sampled topology. This result concerns relationships among the included living taxa; it does not date the group’s fossil origin.

## An uncertain Cambrian fossil {#s-af3e6e8c98}
The older edge uses *Cambropycnogon* at about 500 Ma as a possible total-group record. Its assignment remains uncertain, so the value is not a secure crown-group first appearance or a precise origin date. Living pycnogonids extend the display to the present.
