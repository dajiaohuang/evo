## Magenta flowers and grey five-chambered fruits {#s-8bfcaa2092}
The rounded shrublet reaches 25 cm and may have erect or sprawling branches. Its sharply keeled, three-sided leaves are up to 20 by about 5 mm. Magenta flowers grow singly or in cymes, with petals about 8 mm long; the grey fruit has five chambers and a closing body. Weathered shale, sometimes with quartz pebbles, is recorded.
