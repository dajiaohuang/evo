## Leaflet and pod measurements; seeds unobserved {#s-30bb5b44b4}
Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 lists 2–6 pairs of leaflets, rarely one pair; the oblong-to-oval leaflets are 14–29 mm long and 8–22 mm wide. Yellow flowers have 10 stamens. The pod is sometimes curved and recorded at 3–5 cm long by 0.7–0.9 cm wide; the source explicitly marks seed form as not observed. A separate habitat field says terrestrial. These regional fields give no population sampling frame, locality, observation date, or wild/cultivated status.
