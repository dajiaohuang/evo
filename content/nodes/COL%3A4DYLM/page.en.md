## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Passiflora lorenziana? {#s-a7b1bd94ab}
The woody stems are cylindrical. Entire leaves are hairless, with smooth glandular margins and sessile glands on the petiole; stipules are unknown. Solitary flowers have bristle-like bracts and a cylindrical-to-bell-shaped floral cup. Sepals lack awns, the corona has several rows, and the operculum has a club-shaped tip. The limen is absent, and the reproductive stalk exceeds 1.5 cm. Fruit and seed traits are unknown.
