## A flattened midrib and hairless leaf underside {#s-e82da27335}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field records sympodial growth, in which new growth continues from side shoots, and simple stem hairs. Leaves are elliptic or lance-shaped; the upper midrib is flattened along its length, and the underside is hairless. Bracts or cataphylls (small scale-like leaves) below leaf nodes are absent. The field does not describe a full species-wide range of variation.

## Terminal clusters and fruit that keeps its calyx {#s-c8298b1cab}
Small flowering branches are compressed, with the bracteoles beneath flowers falling away. The field lists two or four terminal inflorescences per axillary shoot, arranged pyramidal and opposite. The two pollen sacs in each anther are equal. Hair status on the floral disc is unknown; the flower cup’s shape is unknown, though it is described as silky-haired. Sepals are separate, the ovary has two chambers, and the calyx opens without tearing. The fruit is ellipsoid or cylindrical, with calyx lobes that enlarge as it develops.
