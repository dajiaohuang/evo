## Piptocarpha regnellii {#s-d6cd98e88b}
## Opposite leaves and cream heads in axillary clusters

The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional record describes cylindrical branches with star-shaped hairs. Leathery, elliptic leaves are opposite or nearly opposite, with acute tips, wedge-shaped bases and edges that range from entire to toothed. Star-shaped woolly hairs cover the underside.

Axillary clusters contain 3–10 sessile heads with top-shaped involucres. Each head has 7–9 cream florets. The three- or four-angled fruits have ribs and glands; their pappus has two rows, the outer one scale-like. The source habitat field classifies the species as terrestrial.
