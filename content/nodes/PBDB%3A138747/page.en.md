## Two catalogue entries and one revision {#s-9aa8d23e72}
The catalogue contains entries for S. dongi and S. gui. A 2023 revision treats S. gui as a synonym of S. dongi and names the other morphotype Huaxiadraco corollatus. The database entries and the revision’s interpretation therefore count different things; two catalogue rows do not by themselves settle how many biological species are recognized.

## Skeletal growth, names and flight subjects {#s-9ac4623060}
The S. dongi account follows the immature holotype, its character combination and the study methods; S. gui covers preservation and the proposed synonymy. A small crest in a juvenile does not identify sex, and a cluster of similar measurements is not a family tree. The very young Nemicolopterus specimen cannot yet be assigned confidently to either species.

## Limits of the selected Sinopterus records {#s-fa7e84db78}
This route brings two catalogue entries together with a 2023 taxonomic revision. The paper proposes that S. gui is a synonym of S. dongi and assigns another morphotype to Huaxiadraco corollatus; these are published interpretations. Juvenile crest size does not establish sex, morphological clustering does not show genealogy, and the very young Nemicolopterus remains unassigned. The guide does not assemble every specimen account, the original 2003 descriptions, all anatomical variation or a complete ecological history. The occurrence totals and broad age bounds do not measure populations or exact origin and extinction times; published matrices and specimens are not independently remeasured here.
