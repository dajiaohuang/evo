## Where does its flower stalk arise? {#s-623036b64f}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry lists the leaves as solitary and basal, with leaf sheaths and petioles present. The basal flower stalk arises from a different node than the leaves. Its fertile bracts (leaf-like structures around flowers) are arranged nearly in two ranks and are red or orange; each small flower cluster has two flowers and one membranous bracteole.
