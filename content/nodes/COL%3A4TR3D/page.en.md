## Papillate leaves and a six-chambered capsule {#s-3430590e95}
This shrub reaches about 400 mm, with grey-brown internodes and papillate, yellow-green leaves. Solitary flowers have six sepals and pink to magenta petals in three rows; the gynoecium is densely papillate. Its six-chambered fruit has an elongated funnel- to bell-shaped base, low rims and a small hook-shaped closing body. Shale- to granite-derived soils are listed.
