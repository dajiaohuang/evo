## Many leaflet pairs and a three-seeded fruit {#s-e881255990}
The regional morphology record gives Andira multistipula five to nine pairs of leaflets. Both leaf surfaces are described as smooth, and the secondary veins loop toward the margin; the upper midrib is grooved. The flowers are recorded as pink or purple, with reddish-brown hairs on the calyx and a hairy ovary. The fruit description notes a green outer layer and three seeds. The source lists alternatives for some traits, so these entries should not be read as fixed states in every plant.

## A terrestrial habitat category {#s-3e51ca18b5}
The same Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset labels this species terrícola, meaning terrestrial. That category does not identify a collection site or establish the species range.
