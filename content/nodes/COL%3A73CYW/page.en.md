## Metalasia seriphiifolia {#s-9a32d3371d}
## Fine twisted leaves and pink to white inner bracts

The regional account describes a slender shrublet up to 40 cm tall, sparsely covered in white woolly hairs. Its needle-like leaves are often 2–10 mm long; they may be hairy and are twisted, with small tufts in their axils. Disc-shaped flower heads form terminal clusters. The upright, three-toothed inner bracts are petal-like and range from pink to nearly white. Lower sandstone slopes, up to 100 m, are recorded as habitat.
