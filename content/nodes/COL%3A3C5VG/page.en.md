## A ribbed flower cup and warty fruit {#s-7ae8a8bf09}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or oblong leaves with acute or wedge-shaped bases and acute or blunt tips. The texture is papery or leathery; the margins roll under and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is grooved, with one vein near the edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis, labeled “auxotélico” by the source without explanation; bract status is unknown. Smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is ribbed and hairy, with the calyx open in bud and four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and warty. Fruit bracteole status, seed number and embryo are marked unknown.

## A terrestrial label beside fruit details {#s-55217b0363}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It supplies no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not specify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. The category is separate from the recorded fruit traits.
