## Ambrosia scabra {#s-92ee820ba4}
## Differently coloured leaves and smooth oval fruits

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes the leaf blades as differently coloured on their two surfaces. Its inflorescence entry records no conical projection on female flower heads. The flowers have linear paleae, or narrow chaff scales on the receptacle; the ellipsoid fruits are hairless. The catalogue classifies the plant as terrestrial.
