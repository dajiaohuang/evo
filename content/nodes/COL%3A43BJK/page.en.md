## *Mikania pseudogracilis*: whorled leaves and glandular hairs {#s-7e4ccf70d0}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records heart-shaped leaves in whorls, with scalloped edges, blunt tips, and three strong veins from the base. Their surfaces may have sparse glands or hairs. Upright, ridged or hairy stems carry corymb-like clusters of heads. The bracts may be woolly; the bell-shaped corolla limb is longer than its tube and has pointed, glandular lobes. The fruitlets are glandular. These are alternatives in a regional morphology field.
