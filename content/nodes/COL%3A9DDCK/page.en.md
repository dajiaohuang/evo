## ## Capitate hairs and alternative floral states {#s-f0d9aef3ad}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records pubescent stems with spreading or recurved simple hairs and capitate glandular hairs. Setae and short shoots are absent; prickles are listed as absent or serial and retrorse. The stipules persist, extrafloral nectaries are absent, and projections occur between pinna pairs. The inflorescences are globose, with floral bracts shorter than the flowers before opening. The flowers have four or five perianth parts, a membranous calyx with absent or triangular lobes, and corolla lobes with one to three veins that may be hairless or pubescent. The androecium is diplostemonous, with pink filaments. The habitat field says “terrestrial”; the entry does not quantify its listed alternatives.
