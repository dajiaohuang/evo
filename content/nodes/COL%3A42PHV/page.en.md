## Several leaf and hair states in one field {#s-6a72f7150c}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 morphology field lists young-branch hairs as absent or star-shaped. Leaves may be obovate (widest toward the tip), oblong, or lance-shaped. Their tips are acuminate (tapering to a long point) or caudate (tail-like), and the base narrows gradually or extends down the leaf stalk. Margins are entire (without teeth), not fringed with hairs, or wavy. Veins start at the base or just above it; the field lists three lengthwise veins and two faint veins just inside the edge. On the underside, the hair covering is absent or sparse; the hairs are absent or sessile (without stalks) and star-shaped. The field does not report how often each alternative occurs or the full variation.

## A branching flower cluster and its parts {#s-34a494e810}
Flowers are in dichasial clusters, a branching arrangement. The flower cup (hypanthium) is tubular. The calyx, the outer floral whorl, falls away; its lobes are distinct, and the flower has five petals. The connective, the central tissue in each anther, is extended but has no appendage. Anthers are yellow with one small pollen opening; the ovary is hairless.
