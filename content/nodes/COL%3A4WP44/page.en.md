## Senegalia serra {#s-e078abec0d}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Senegalia serra* as an accepted species of *Senegalia* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes 17–32 pairs of pinnae (the primary divisions of a compound leaf), each with 30–64 pairs of narrow leaflets. The inflorescences are rounded heads; flowers have tubular, nearly hairless calyces and corollas, while the ovary is hairy. Seed shape was not observed, and the record labels the plant terrestrial.
