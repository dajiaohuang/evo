## Dendropanax gonatopodus {#s-81d69d885c}
## Large leaves and a Central American range record

The description records trees up to 16 m tall with stout, round branchlets. Leaves are thin-leathery, ovate to elliptic, 10–30 cm long, with acute to acuminate tips and entire or wavy margins. Flower clusters contain 4–16 umbels, each with 15–50 five-parted flowers. The nearly spherical fruit is 3–4 mm across and contains five seeds. The distribution field lists Costa Rica and Panama; it gives no further habitat detail.
