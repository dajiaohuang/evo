## Rainy-season specimens in one temporary pond {#s-c650e3081e}
The description found all its specimens around 14:30 under leaf litter and among arrowroot stems in a small temporary pond surrounded by hill evergreen forest. The clear pond had a litter-covered bottom and measured approximately 5.2 m by 2.7 m, with a maximum depth of 17 cm. Fish were not observed during this visit.
