## Holomitrium arboreum: two habitat entries {#s-2dbbad74f9}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 dataset records two habitat categories for this moss:

“Corticícola”

and “Epixila.”

These are source labels from two regional dataset rows; neither gives a collection locality, survey date or complete range coverage.
