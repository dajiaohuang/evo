## Hairs, veins and flower parts {#s-7cb8a5d8f7}
A Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry describes the stem as shaggy, with upright and glandular hairs. It lists oval leaves with five veins and scalloped edges; their recorded orientation is upward or sideways, and their surfaces are flat with a membranous texture. The flower cluster bears oval or oblong bracteoles, small leaf-like structures near the flowers, with a hairless underside. Flowers are recorded as five-parted, with floral parts in groups of five. Their floral cup (hypanthium) has lengthwise ridges; the upper face of each sepal is hairless, the anthers have flat-cut tips, and glandular hairs occur on the ovary. These are descriptors from one regional data field, not an account of variation across populations or a complete diagnostic description.

## A single habitat label {#s-8c49ecfd82}
The same dataset classifies the recorded habitat as “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It provides no collection locality, survey date or range coverage, and does not specify whether the entry concerns wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This one label cannot stand for the species’ full ecology.
