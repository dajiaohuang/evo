## Leaves and flowers {#s-f3bde8bf2e}
The account describes a bulbous geophyte up to 40 cm tall. It has two to five greyish, linear leaves that are green at flowering. Two to six flowers spread horizontally. Their tube is narrow and curved below, then abruptly widens into an urn shape; the flowers are vermilion. The stamens project slightly, and the style has three branches.
