## Grey leaf undersides and grouped flower heads {#s-25f1e0d0c1}
This record notes a swollen underground stem and few branches, with leaves distributed along the stems. The leathery blades range from ovate to lance-shaped, have smooth upper surfaces and greyish lower surfaces, and may have a heart-shaped or tapering base. Flower heads may be stalkless or stalked and solitary or grouped, with bell- to cylinder-shaped involucres. The top-shaped fruits are smooth. The habitat field says terrestrial.
