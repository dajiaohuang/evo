## Many pink flowers on a sea-green shrub {#s-a372801bb6}
The shrub reaches about 300 mm and has sea-green leaves ranging from three-sided to rounded. Numerous flowers grow in cymes. Pink petals are about 1.5 mm wide; a few filament-like staminodes and the filaments are pink above and white below, while anthers and pollen are brownish yellow. The fruit has five chambers; the record lists hills as habitat.
