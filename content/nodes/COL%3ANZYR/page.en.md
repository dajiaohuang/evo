## What flower and fruit traits are recorded for Byrsonima alvimii? {#s-20d1bae61d}
The lower leaf surface is hairless. Each flower cluster has one to three units, and the flower stalk is pedunculate. The petals are pink, while the anthers bear silky or coarse hairs. The fruit is hairless.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-5ace355c9b}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
