## What leaf, flower and fruit traits are described for Ouratea flexipedicellata? {#s-ff05bf0acb}
The source tentatively describes shrubs with smooth, round branches. Stout petioles reach 8 mm. Narrow oblong leaves reach 25 × 6.5 cm; they are glossy, hairless and thinly leathery, with an obtuse base, a long tapering tip and about ten curved side veins. Small teeth are spaced 4–7 mm apart along the thickened margin. Axillary or terminal narrow flower clusters are 8–30 cm long, with 3–5 flowers per subcluster; the threadlike, finely hairy pedicels bend conspicuously backward. The one bud observed was narrow-ovate and up to 9 mm long. The fruits described were immature: their receptacle was oblong and up to 8 mm, and the elliptic-rounded drupes were 4 mm long.

## What is known about its locality? {#s-c03e1153c2}
The cited source says the species is known only from its type locality. The account does not identify a broader range.
