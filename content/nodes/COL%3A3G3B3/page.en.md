## Three inferred refugia and later introductions {#s-05cfa5bdf7}
A genome-resequencing study sampled 545 trees from 51 populations in nine countries. From those sampled trees, the authors inferred three refugia (areas where populations persisted through unfavorable periods) in China and multiple human-assisted introductions from eastern China to other continents. These are population-history inferences from the study’s sample, not a census; the source does not resolve whether every sampled tree was wild or planted.
