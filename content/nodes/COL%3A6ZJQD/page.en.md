## Stenachaenium adenanthum {#s-95c1f8e58a}
## A short plant with pale-hairy leaves and clustered heads

Flora do Brasil 2020 describes plants under 35 cm tall. The basal leaves are obovate or oblanceolate and bear white to yellowish hairs. Stem leaves are triangular and scale-like, with sparse shaggy hairs.

Flower heads form corymb-like clusters, either with many heads or with two. Flowers are purplish or cream-coloured. The fruits are pubescent and lack a beak.
