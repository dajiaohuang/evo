## The source tadpole concept {#s-393dd6c316}
TadpoleTraits version 4 files this material under Allobates niputidea, in the genus Allobates and family Aromobatidae, following Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 (2024). The name printed in the underlying work is also Allobates niputidea. This cataloguing choice does not confirm equivalence with a current COL26.8 species concept.

## Developmental material {#s-b53230c1cf}
The compilation has one record transcribed from literature and none measured from images; its supplied stage label is 25. That is a label attached to a source record, not a full developmental timeline. The compilation preserves any reported uncertainty and does not treat picture-based estimates as direct observations. One record is not necessarily one unique animal, and sample sizes from separate studies are not added together.
