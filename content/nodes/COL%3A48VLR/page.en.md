## Small leaves and solitary flower heads {#s-90564add48}
The account says this species resembles O. multipunctata but has smaller leaves; its flower heads are nearly stalkless and solitary. It also describes gland-dotted leaves and slender involucres. Head size varies: the involucre is usually narrow and holds few florets, but can be broader with more florets. The account notes greatest variation in the Cedarberg and an unusual specimen with both scales and bristles in the pappus. Rocky sandstone slopes are listed as habitat.
