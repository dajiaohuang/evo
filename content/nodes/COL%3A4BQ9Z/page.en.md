## Narrow leaves and corona keels taller than broad {#s-6e69a5f2ec}
The regional description gives slender to stout stems 270–540 mm tall, simple or branched at the base and sparsely to densely pubescent, often with hairs in two rows. Spreading leaves have 3–12 mm petioles and blades 44–75 × 12–28 mm, from linear-lanceolate to narrowly ovate or narrowly oblong. The upper surface ranges from nearly glabrous to pubescent, the lower from finely hairy to pubescent; the midvein and margins are rough. Inflorescences bear 2–4 flowers, are sessile or pedunculate at the lowest position, and occur at nodes and terminally; pubescent peduncles reach 30 mm. Deciduous, narrowly linear bracts are 3.4–8.8 mm long, acute or acuminate and hairy below. Pedicels are 15–30 mm and pubescent or woolly. Sepals are 9–15 × 3.5–5.5 mm, lanceolate to narrowly ovate, narrowly elliptic or ovate-oblong, acute, glabrous above and pubescent below.

The corolla is 14–23 mm long and lobed nearly to the base. Its elliptic lobes are 12–19 × 8–13 mm, usually straight-back reflexed, rarely reflexed-spreading, with obtuse, notched tips and usually rolled margins. They are described as apparently pale green or greenish white with purple spots; the upper surface is glabrous or has minute, soft, velvet-like hairs at the base, and the lower is glabrous or finely hairy. Corona lobes are 7–15 mm long, with a narrow, claw-like base spreading horizontally, 2–6 × 1–2 mm, often expanded at the extreme base. A pair of contiguous, erect keels measures 2.2–3.6 × 1.2–3.0 mm and is always taller than broad; the keels are subdeltoid, oblong or subfalcate, with obtuse or truncate tips. Beyond them, the claw-like base expands into an orbicular, broadly ovate or broadly elliptic blade, 3.5–8.5 × 5.7–8.5 mm, with an obtuse or obtusely apiculate tip and often undulate margins. The blade stands vertically or is slightly inflexed over the keels; it is often transversely oblong and sharply cuspidate, with a linear, obtuse tip 0.5–4.1 mm long. Corona lobes are described as apparently brownish green. Anther wings are 3–6 mm long, somewhat oblique and slightly concave, with basal angles diverging and curving outward. Appendages are 2.0–3.4 mm, broadly ovate or ovate-oblong and obtuse; they begin erect, then curve over to well above the style apex, with sides slightly reflexed. The account says this species is often confused with P. natalensis and refers to that account for distinguishing characters. It also notes difficulty separating it from P. appendiculatus, especially where the two occur together, and refers to that account for discussion. The habitat entry records occasional occurrence in coastal and midland grassland at 200–1,400 m, in Sub-Escarpment Grassland, Drakensberg Grassland, Sub-Escarpment Savanna and Albany Thicket. These regional account statements do not define the full range or population-level variation.
