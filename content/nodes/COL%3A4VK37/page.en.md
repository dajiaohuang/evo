## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-e9a68d312b}
COL26.8 records Scincella macrotis as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Scincidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Length values in the archived records {#s-3b83b6f16e}
The row reports a maximum SVL of 24 mm. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri 2018.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-0d7412eb69}
The compiled mean for offspring per litter or eggs per clutch is 2.5. It is a mean summary, not the count from one litter or clutch. ReptTraits attributes this field to Meiri et al. 2020.
