## What the material shows {#s-bf84b82138}
Holotype NFM F-994 and plastotype OUM ÁT.424/p preserve a quadrate Ediacaran impression with repeated aligned internal structures. These have been interpreted as muscle-like, but later studies dispute orientation and the strength of a staurozoan comparison.

## Feeding {#s-910ebd28a8}
Feeding structures are not directly preserved, so diet remains unresolved.

## Setting {#s-3fb9eeb5ca}
The impression is from a marine Fermeuse Formation setting; its life position is uncertain.

## Movement {#s-b50a627fb0}
A sessile or attached life position has been proposed, but movement was not observed.

## Size {#s-367d3a0882}
The holotype impression is centimetre-scale; no full-body measurement is available.

## Ecological role {#s-1d1ab6a782}
Both cnidarian affinity and feeding role remain contested.

## Preserved features {#s-18b86ee962}
The body impression is quadrate.

## Preserved features {#s-2fb62c5460}
Repeated aligned structures appear inside the impression.

## Preserved features {#s-33e3149535}
A muscle-like interpretation has been tested against comparisons with staurozoans.

## How to read the evidence {#s-0defb0fb13}
The holotype and locality are documented. Tissue identity, orientation and precise cnidarian placement remain contested across the original description, critical reassessment and later re-examination.
