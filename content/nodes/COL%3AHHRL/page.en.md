## Whorled leaves and axillary flowers {#s-ec0677711a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records colorless latex, cylindrical branches with no thickened bark, and spaced leaves arranged in whorls, with several leaves arising at one level of a stem. Their secondary veins reach the leaf edge. Broad-topped, branching flower clusters grow in the leaf axils, the angles between leaf stalks and stems. The sepals, the flower's outer leaf-like parts, are similar in length; petal lobes, the separated segments at the edge of the fused petals, are as long as or longer than the fused-petal tube, and the ovary, which later develops into fruit, is hairless. The fruit is grooved and ribbed. The entry lists its stalk as present or absent and its lenticels, small surface openings, as conspicuous or inconspicuous; it does not resolve either alternative.
