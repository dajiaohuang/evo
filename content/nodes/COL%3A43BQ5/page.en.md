## *Mikania vauthieriana*: lance-shaped leaves and an uncertain fruit {#s-5368d42f27}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 regional field records opposite, thin lance-shaped leaves with entire margins and hairless surfaces. Their bases are wedge-shaped or rounded, and pinnate veins loop toward the leaf edge. Branches may twine; the stem pith is hollow. Loose panicles carry stalked heads with short bracts at the base of each stalk. The bell-shaped corolla limb is about as long as its tube, with rolled-back, rounded lobes. The field marks the fruit as unknown.
