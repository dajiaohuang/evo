## Cleistes bella {#s-d3a9f82e6b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 morphology row describes Cleistes bella with four or more conspicuous, broadly ovate leaves that spread away from the stem. Its magenta flower has a strongly three-lobed lip; the narrow side lobes end in points, and the callus changes from smooth proximally to scaly distally. The habitat category is terrestrial.
