## How much flower structure fits on a 15–25 cm plant? {#s-1dae52f973}
A regional account describes *Restio pumilus* as a tufted plant 15–25 cm tall, with clustered stem bases. Its solid, round stems branch and carry dense, irregular bumps; they reach only 0.7 mm in diameter. Male inflorescences (the whole flower clusters) bear 2–7 loose spikelets (small flower clusters), each 3–4 mm long with two or three flowers. Female spikelets contain one or two flowers. The account also describes a capsule with a 1.2 mm seed.
