## ## Two large leaflets and one petal {#s-21454c201e}
The historical *Flora of Panama* account describes *Macrolobium pittieri* as a small tree with two nearly sessile, broadly oblanceolate leaflets, 30–35 cm long and sometimes about 10 cm wide. Lateral inflorescences arise on older branches; their flowers have hairless 4–6 mm pedicels and a hairless sheath formed from fused bracteoles. The calyx is hairless, with overlapping lobes that reach about 2 cm at flowering. Each flower has one thin, elliptic petal about 4 cm long. The ovary is lightly hairy along its margins, while the style is hairless. The account describes a glabrous legume whose valves twist as it ages, and a flattened, wrinkled seed about 2 cm long. This historical regional description does not establish full variation or global range.
