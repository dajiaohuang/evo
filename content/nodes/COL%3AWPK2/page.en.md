## *Codonanthe venosa* {#s-5398aaf272}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: leaf-related structures — elliptic, egg-shaped, entire, untoothed margins; flowers — white. This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as epiphytic (growing on another plant; Portuguese: Epífita). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
