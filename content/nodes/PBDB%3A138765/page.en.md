## Read anatomy and names from an immature holotype {#s-dee39a87a5}
In the 2023 revision, the holotype IVPP V 13363 is identified as a juvenile Sinopterus dongi. The authors place several names used in earlier catalogues within this species while retaining Huaxiadraco corollatus separately. Those historical names are not, by themselves, proof of separate biological species.

## Diagnosis combines skull, neck and limb features {#s-247d9e1c69}
The diagnosis is a combination: a gently downturned snout, an elongate opening ahead of the orbit, a roughly quadrangular orbit, a near-vertical descending nasal process, a fourth neck vertebra longer than the fifth, and an elongate first metacarpal. The authors also note a pteroid shorter than half the ulna and a first metatarsal at least as long as the second. Displacement in the holotype affects some reconstructed proportions, so the combination is more useful than treating one angle as fixed.

## Fusion supplies a relative developmental sequence {#s-d2c9920b54}
Several bones in IVPP V 13363 remain unfused, including the scapula and coracoid, humeral end, carpals, a tendon process on the first wing phalanx and the tibiotarsus. Compared with more mature specimens, the revision treats the holotype as juvenile. Its estimated wingspan is about 1.2 m: a size estimate for this specimen, not an adult maximum. Humeral length serves as a size proxy, and fusion helps order relative growth stages without giving exact ages.

## Separate growth signals before comparing morphotypes {#s-dbd80de10f}
The authors tested 21 measurements for size correlation and allometry, using humeral length as a size proxy. They then clustered and ran principal-component analyses on proportions judged less affected by growth. Missing values were filled by mean and iterative imputation. Incomplete BPV-077 and the very young specimen named Nemicolopterus were excluded. These choices bound the analysis; a nonsignificant correlation does not prove that growth has no effect.

## Two morphotypes support the authors’ two-species interpretation {#s-558cbfe596}
The quantitative and anatomical comparisons separate two morphotypes, which the authors identify as S. dongi and Huaxiadraco corollatus. Their 2023 treatment places S. gui, Huaxiapterus jii, S. lingyuanensis and H. atavismus in synonymy with S. dongi. Clusters describe similarity, while a separate phylogenetic analysis informs genus placement. This summary retains those published acts without combining them with the database occurrence rows.

## Crest presence, size and shape answer different questions {#s-134b468a6f}
The smallest juveniles lack a premaxillary crest, which the authors link to growth. Crest size also varies among larger specimens within a morphotype and may reflect individual or sexual variation. In the sample, the crest of S. dongi has a raised profile, while that of Huaxiadraco corollatus is more trapezoidal. These patterns do not determine an individual’s sex or set a universal crest shape for pterosaurs.

## Stratigraphic overlap does not establish shared localities {#s-f8048ecb40}
The revision notes overlapping age ranges and considers the two forms possibly contemporaneous, but neither is known from the exact same locality as the other. Some specimens are located only to the Chaoyang area, with weak control of their precise beds. Better locality and sediment data would be needed to test niche separation; the current record does not demonstrate different diets, social behaviour or a shared habitat.

## Read juvenile-model identities with their study date {#s-8ac2774157}
A 2021 flight study treated IVPP V-14377, the Nemicolopterus holotype, as a near-hatching S. dongi. The 2023 revision considers it too young for a reliable assignment to either S. dongi or Huaxiadraco corollatus and retains it as indeterminate Sinopterinae. Flight models using the 2021 identification therefore do not establish uncontested, species-specific hatchling performance. Compare preservation and synonymy in S. gui, then the separate model inputs for Pterodaustro guinazui.
