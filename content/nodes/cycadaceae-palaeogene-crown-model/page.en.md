## How old is the crown of living Cycadaceae? {#s-f320d0aa89}
A whole-plastome analysis combined fossil and tectonic calibrations to estimate the crown age of living Cycadaceae—the group descending from the most recent common ancestor of living members. Under different tree priors, the reported mean ages are 69.31 Ma and 42.88 Ma. The estimates vary with the model and calibration scheme; neither is a directly observed fossil first appearance, and the result concerns Cycadaceae rather than all cycads.
