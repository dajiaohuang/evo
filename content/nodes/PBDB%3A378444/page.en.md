## What this catalogue does and does not show {#s-bca247d79a}
This page summarizes selected pterosaur occurrences in a PBDB snapshot dated 2026-10-02; it is not a complete species monograph. The records use the database accepted name and parent path and include usable species-level occurrences without uncertain-identification qualifiers. A PBDB link to an older name does not settle specimen identification or all taxonomic disputes, and it does not merge the record with a same-named COL concept. Collection locality, stratigraphic interval, depositional setting, and collection-level preservation mode are distinct fields. They do not by themselves establish a complete range, true origin or extinction time, fixed ecology, abundance, or the preservation of every specimen.
