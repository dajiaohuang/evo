## Phoradendron nigricans {#s-fdb8d9b7ca}
A single Brazilian Flora 2020 description records this species as hemiparasitic and describes alternatives in its stems, leaves, inflorescences and fruits.

## Sheathed stems and variable leaf forms {#s-9cee2c47aa}
The description records monopodial stems that are elliptical or rhombic in cross-section and unwinged. One pair of sheath-forming cataphylls occurs on lateral-branch internodes; fertile cataphylls are absent. Leaves are petiolate with expanded blades and palmate venation. Their bases are recorded as acute or obtuse, and their tips as obtuse, rounded or apiculate. These are the alternatives in one source description, not a measure of variation across populations.

## Axillary flowers and two recorded fruit shapes {#s-412b46eaee}
Inflorescences are axillary, with one or two pairs of sterile basal bracts. The field leaves sexuality unknown, records two or three fertile articles, and describes them as cylindrical with flowers in two rows; the position of staminate flowers is also unknown. Fruits are listed as globose or ellipsoid, with erect-spreading petals, a smooth outer skin, and translucent white or green colour.

## A hemiparasitic habit in the regional record {#s-b23ccc658c}
Brazilian Flora 2020 labels the habit “Hemiparasita.” The regional dataset field gives no host, locality or evidence about the species’ full range.
