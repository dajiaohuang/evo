## Male papillae and adpressed-limb comparison {#s-ed269595cc}
The sheni comparison described tongziensis as having strongly overlapping digits when its limbs were adpressed to the trunk, a high dorsal tail fin fold, tail height greater than tail-base width and male cloacal genital papillae. The paragraph supplies no species-specific sample size or numerical ratio for these observations.
