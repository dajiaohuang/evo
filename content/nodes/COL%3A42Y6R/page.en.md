## What leaf and flower colors and structures are recorded? {#s-654f70b31b}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 field records sessile leaves with different colors on the two faces, either bristles or spherical glands, and one or three veins. Flowers are solitary. The bell-shaped floral cup bears bristles or spherical glands. Triangular sepals have the same surface types and are shorter than or equal to the cup. The corolla is magenta. Anthers are two-colored and have four pollen sacs; the ovary has three chambers. The regional row lists alternatives without specifying their frequency.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-dff00b3606}
The Brazilian Flora field classifies the habitat as terrestrial and supplies no locality or collection date.
