## Leaves and flowers {#s-51b36a9373}
Codd's 2005 regional account describes plants growing singly or with a few stems. The soft, dull-green leaves stand upright, measure 400–600 × 15–25 mm, and have a deep keel. Both surfaces are hairy along the veins, and the margins have conspicuous, regular fine teeth. The flowering stalk is as long as or longer than the leaves, at 450–600 mm. The dense raceme is short and rhomboid-cylindrical to ovoid, measuring 80–120 × 40–45 mm. Its buds are orange or dull coral to salmon-pink, tipped with green; the flowers hang down and are dull greenish yellow. The pointed, linear-lance-shaped bracts are 10–14 mm long, with margins that range from minutely toothed to nearly entire. Pedicels are 2–4 mm long. The nearly cylindrical perianth measures 22–27 mm; its ovate, 2.5 mm lobes do not spread. At flowering, the stamens do not extend beyond the perianth or do so only slightly. The nearly spherical fruit is 7–9 mm long.

These measurements and observations come from a regional species account published in 2005; it gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## A diagnostic feature noted by the source {#s-a758ef8167}
Codd's account says *K. hirsuta* resembles *K. ritualis* in raceme, bract, and perianth characters, but distinguishes it by its hairy leaves, described there as unique in the genus. The account says *K. stricta* is the only other species approaching it in this respect, usually with rough papillae on the veins.
