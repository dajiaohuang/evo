## Stylosanthes guianensis {#s-544713ebad}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Stylosanthes guianensis* as an accepted species of *Stylosanthes* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry records no woody swollen root (xylopodium), one inner bracteole, and a one-segment fruit with a rudimentary beak shorter than 3.5 mm. The seeds are yellow; the habitat field labels the plant terrestrial.
