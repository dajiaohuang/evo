## What leaf and flower traits are recorded for Acmanthera parviflora? {#s-813029dba2}
The branches may be hairless or covered with silky hairs. Leaves are elliptic or obovate, tapering at the base and abruptly pointed at the tip; their surfaces have sparse silky hairs. Stipules are hairy, ranging from silky to woolly. Bracts and smaller bracteoles are elliptic or triangular, and the flower stalks are silky-hairy. The sepals are round with broadly rounded tips and remain exposed in the bud. Petals are hairless and white-pink or yellow. The connective appendage on the anther is not glandular.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-69ec530457}
The record classifies the habitat as terrestrial and gives no locality.
