## Symplocos neblinae Maguire & Steyerm. {#s-f844deeca6}
## Five- or six-lobed corollas and elongated fruit

Symplocos neblinae is monoecious, with branch hairs that may be smoothening or stiff and appressed. Its leaves range from papery to leathery and elliptic to nearly round, with margins that may be flat, slightly rolled or entire; marginal glands fall away. Racemes or panicles bear bisexual flowers with five or six corolla lobes. The filaments are smooth and may be arranged in alternating groups or fused into a bundle. The ellipsoid or club-shaped drupe retains a calyx extending beyond the disk.
