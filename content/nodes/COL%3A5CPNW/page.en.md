## *Xyris metallica*: a flattened upper stalk {#s-b38572694f}
The leaves spiral around the stem and have flat blades, smooth or striated. The flower stalk is 20–70 cm long, with one or two ridges and a flattened upper part. Its head is inversely egg-shaped to nearly spherical or ellipsoid, with four to six sterile bracts and 10–25 flowers.

The species is listed as terrestrial.
