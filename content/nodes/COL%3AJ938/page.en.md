## Accepted name and classification {#s-287501ae29}
COL26.8 accepts *Atelopus walkeri* Rivero, 1963 as a species in Anura → Bufonidae → Atelopus. This placement follows the release published on 20 August 2026. Names in other catalogues or fossil databases may reflect different records and require their own identity evidence.

## Maximum adult body length reported by AmphiBIO {#s-4dbec1c040}
AmphiBIO gives 49.3 mm as its maximum adult body length for this historical source concept. In the compilation's convention, body length for frogs and toads (Anura) means snout–vent length; for salamanders and caecilians it means total length. The number is a compiled maximum, not a measurement for every individual or a newly verified measurement of the current concept.
