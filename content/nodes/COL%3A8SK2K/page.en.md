## COL26.8 name and the ReptTraits row {#s-98bddb7343}
COL26.8 records Stoliczkia vanhnuailianai as an accepted species. The ReptTraits v1-2 row gives its Family field as Xenodermidae under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework. This association uses an exact accepted-name match and matching family; it does not establish that historical and current taxonomic concepts are identical.

## Habitat use and activity labels {#s-35244ba403}
The microhabitat field classifies the recorded position as rock-dwelling (Saxicolous). It is a habitat-use category, not a full description of every setting used. ReptTraits attributes this field to IUCN 2023.

## Reproductive fields in ReptTraits {#s-a4ce5716ab}
