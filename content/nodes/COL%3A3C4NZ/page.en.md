## Tail-like leaf tips and a pear-shaped fruit {#s-2e6baa136b}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or oval, leathery leaves with wedge-shaped bases and tips that taper to a point or extend like a tail. Their margins are flat and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is described as grooved or flat; one vein runs near the leaf edge. Flowers form a raceme, a cluster of stalked flowers along a main axis, labeled “auxotélico” by the field; that qualifier is not explained there. Supporting bracts fall away, as do the smaller bracteoles beside flowers. The smooth flower cup bears hairs; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is pear-shaped and smooth, and its bracteoles fall away. One seed is recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo.
