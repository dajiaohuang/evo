## Recorded parent of Giraffomorpha {#s-033d0f13f5}
Giraffomorpha is listed at rank superfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Pecora as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
