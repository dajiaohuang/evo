## Red fruits and minutely bristly leaf undersides {#s-16341ee18a}
This record describes bristly stems and elliptic to lance-shaped leaves with rounded bases and pointed tips. The upper surfaces are smooth and the lower surfaces finely bristly. Small white bisexual flowers have smooth, bell-shaped calyces and tubular, bell-shaped corollas; the anthers bear glands. The ovoid fruit is red and finely bristly. The habitat field says terrestrial.
