AmphiBIO v1 lists *Nectophrynoides minutus* in row Anf0708 under Nectophrynoides, Bufonidae and Anura. Its names follow the compilation’s Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011) concept. This page describes that deposited row; its match to a current COL26.8 species concept remains unresolved.

## Foraging strata {#s-fcdf6fb0b2}
For Anf0708, the compilation tags terrestrial and aquatic as foraging strata. These are broad source categories; the entry does not say when, how often or at which life stage they apply. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some inherited microhabitat entries; this row’s correction status is not recorded here. No replacement is supplied, and the notice does not establish that this row is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-e4235d15f0}
AmphiBIO records maximum adult body length of 22 mm. For Anura, the length field means snout–vent length. These are compiled maxima, not typical values for every individual.
