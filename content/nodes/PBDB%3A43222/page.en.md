## Recorded parent of Rhinoceros {#s-b3831ce54f}
Rhinoceros is listed at rank genus in the PBDB classification route, with Rhinocerotini as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
