## Many pale flowers in dense heads {#s-ad4f7e7633}
This description records a shrub up to 50 cm tall, with smooth, ridged stems that become woody below and branch mostly from the base. Its numerous soft leaves are usually flat and spreading, with entire or occasionally toothed edges. Many-flowered heads grow on the main stem and on long or short side branches. The flowers may be white, pale blue, mauve or pinkish, and the ovary is smooth. It is most often recorded on sandy ground near the coast or in low-altitude grassy places in the eastern districts.
