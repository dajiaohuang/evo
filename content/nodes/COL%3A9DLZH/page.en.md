## Many leaflet pairs and a head like inflorescence {#s-74d01f961e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 entry records cylindrical branches with irregularly arranged, recurved prickles. Triangular stipules accompany two petiolar nectaries, listed as orbicular or oval and positioned at mid-petiole or just below the first pair of pinnae; a rachis nectary is present. Leaves have 16–33 pairs of pinnae, each with 40–70 pairs of linear leaflets. The leaflets are glabrous on both surfaces with obtuse tips; the primary vein is central, with no hairs at its base. The inflorescence is head like. Flowers are sessile; the calyx is campanulate and puberulent, the campanulate corolla is glabrous, anther tips lack glands and the ovary is pubescent. The fruit is a legume; seed shape was not observed. No habitat field is included, so the entry does not establish habitat or range.
