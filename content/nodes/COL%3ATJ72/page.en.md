## ## Star-shaped hairs and twisted mature pods {#s-01c6d3f23e}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 record describes 4–7 pairs of elliptic to obovate-elliptic leaflets and panicles, with bulbous, star-shaped glandular hairs on stems, leaves and inflorescences. The petals are yellow, and the fruit valves twist at maturity. The presence or absence of a xylopodium is recorded as unknown. The same dataset classifies the plant as terrestrial; these regional character fields are not a global account.
