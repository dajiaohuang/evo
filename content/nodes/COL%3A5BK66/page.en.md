## What does the record say about Virola subsessilis? {#s-7fad864bc1}
The field describes elliptic to oblong-elliptic leaves with a tiny, inconspicuous stalk. The underside may be finely hairy or become smoother, with star-shaped scales. Flowers form clusters or racemes. Three anthers are fused, and the floral column is longer than the anthers; the perianth is split about one-third of its length. The ellipsoid fruit has fine hairs, and the ellipsoid seed bears a red aril.

## What habitat category is recorded? {#s-12bf9fa0ac}
The source categorizes it as terrestrial; no site is specified.
