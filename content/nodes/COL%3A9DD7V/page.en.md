## Mimosa porrecta {#s-02472a572a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa porrecta* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 entry lists hairy or bristly stems, capitate glandular hairs, and recurved prickles arranged in rows. Its spherical flower clusters bear flowers with four or five perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts); the record describes pink stamen filaments (the slender threads that support anthers) and two rings of stamens. These details come from a regional flora entry.
