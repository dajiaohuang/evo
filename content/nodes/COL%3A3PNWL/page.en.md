## Name and this data account {#s-674784c884}
COL26.8 places Indotyphlops malcolmi in Typhlopidae. Its ReptTraits v1–2 row follows the Reptile Database 2023 name framework and retains field sources; it is a compiled account of selected traits.

## Distinguish the measured length {#s-8665f2686e}
The recorded maximum total length (TL) is 107 mm. TL includes the tail, whereas snout–vent length (SVL) ends at the vent. This maximum is not typical size or an absolute limit.

## Where and how it is active {#s-911c88ff30}
The microhabitat field is Fossorial, indicating an underground or burrowing association; it does not provide a full habitat description.

## How to read reproductive records {#s-54abfb6a53}
The source classifies reproduction as oviparous (egg-laying). It gives no clutch size and does not establish the same pattern in all populations.

## Evidence limits {#s-4a7754bbb2}
This page summarizes selected fields from a ReptTraits v1–2 row under the Reptile Database 2023 name framework; it is not a complete species account. A row may combine records from different sexes, ages, places, or populations, and a shared name does not establish identical later taxonomic concepts. A reported maximum is a compiled measurement, not an absolute size limit. Reproductive labels do not give clutch size; workbook averages summarize source records rather than every clutch. Estimated mass and differing juvenile-length definitions are not used here as direct observations. Conservation, venom, current distribution, and population trends are not updated. Citation labels preserve the workbook's source chain but do not mean every original study was independently reviewed here.

## Traits not interpreted here {#s-8dce16a760}
This account covers maximum total length, microhabitat, and reproductive mode. It does not interpret sex-specific size, clutch or litter counts and frequency, egg dimensions, diet, activity time, foraging mode, or sex determination where the record lacks species-specific support.
