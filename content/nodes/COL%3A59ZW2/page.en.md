## Evidence limits {#s-fcbc38f72c}
The life-history values come from a historical 2015 archive. They are reported as compilation medians or averages of the two middle values, except maximum longevity, which uses the largest collected record; they are not measurements of one individual, individual ranges, mean wild lifespans or current population surveys. Historical species and subspecies may share or inherit data. Matching name, family and sparse rows support the association but do not establish equivalence with COL26.8 or remove all inherited components. Wild versus captive conditions are not consistently confirmed, and underlying publications have not been independently reviewed. The EltonTraits entry supplies one supported foraging-stratum field; it does not fill diet or activity. Its diet is withheld under source code D1 because of interpolation, certainty or source-link limits; the original value and specific reason remain in the source record and do not support a definite diet conclusion. EltonTraits categories and weights are standardized from literature, not direct measurements of food, energy or time. Mammalian ABC fields elsewhere may draw on a congener considered ecologically similar, and the records do not establish direct observation of this species for every field. Individual, seasonal and local variation, behavior beyond the recorded field, current range, conservation and other missing information require additional sources.
