## Cynorkis compacta {#s-78be41fa3a}
The South African account describes Cynorkis compacta as a plant only about 80 mm tall, with one or two basal leaves and a dense raceme of up to 15 flowers. Its small white flowers have a pink-spotted lip divided into three lobes and a short, 3–4 mm spur. It is recorded from rock crevices.
