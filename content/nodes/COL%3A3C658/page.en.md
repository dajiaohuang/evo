## A wavy leaf edge and one or two seeds {#s-963fca033a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 field describes elliptic or oval, papery leaves with blunt bases and acute or blunt tips. Their margins are wavy and lack a hard, cartilage-like edge. The upper midrib, or main leaf vein, is flat, with one vein near the leaf edge. Flowers grow in compact clusters called fascicles; bract status is unknown. Smaller, inconspicuous bracteoles beside flowers persist. The flower cup is smooth and hairless; its calyx is open in bud, with four sepals and four petals. The fruit is round and smooth. One or two seeds are recorded, with fused cotyledons (the seed leaves) in the embryo; fruit bracteole status is unknown.

## A terrestrial habitat label {#s-fb019eb95a}
The habitat field records “terrícola,” meaning terrestrial. It gives no locality, survey date or range coverage and does not identify wild, cultivated, managed or fossil material. This is a habitat category rather than a distribution account.
