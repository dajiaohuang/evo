## The source concept {#s-a331415385}
AmphiBIO v1 lists Phyllobates lugubris as source row Anf1536, under genus Phyllobates, family Dendrobatidae and order Anura. The compilation follows the species concepts in Amphibian Species of the World (Frost 2011). The traits below belong to that deposited row; the reviewed evidence does not establish that its species concept matches the current COL26.8 concept. Here, NA means missing data, not zero, and categories not listed on this page are not inferred to be absent. Per-trait primary citations, modern taxonomic reconciliation and a full seven-topic scientific review remain incomplete.

## Foraging strata {#s-f0c41049c2}
The source row marks terrestrial (on land) and aquatic (in water) as broad foraging settings. These labels do not pinpoint feeding sites or show seasonal or life-stage changes. A later TetrapodTraits revision reports corrections to some amphibian body-mass and microhabitat (small-scale habitat) entries carried forward from earlier compilations. This row has not been checked against that revision, so the listed categories remain historical AmphiBIO values; no replacements are inferred. The notice does not mean every original entry is wrong.

## Compiled adult dimensions {#s-7505d474f3}
AmphiBIO reports a maximum adult body length of 24 mm. For Anura, the compilation measures body length as snout–vent length (from snout to vent); it uses total length for Caudata and Gymnophiona. This is a maximum compiled from literature, not a measurement for every individual.

## Compiled life history {#s-8eeab53727}
The row gives minimum and maximum ages at sexual maturity of 1 year, 5–30 offspring or eggs per clutch, and a maximum of 1 reproductive event per year. AmphiBIO compiles these values from literature; the row does not identify the sex, population, measurement date or wild-versus-captive context for each value.
