## Slender fruits on a small annual {#s-c59e7af1ad}
This account describes an annual up to 30 cm tall, with smooth or hairy stems and linear to thread-like leaves; stipules may be present or absent. The flowers are white to blue and the ovaries contain 10–24 ovules. The fruits are narrow and linear, 15–37 mm long. Sandy soils are listed as habitat.
