## Overview {#s-4ace03491c}
This page follows COL26.8 accepted species ID BVHG. The exact MDD v2.5 name record supplies the display label L'Hoest's Monkey; the Chinese name is editorial.

## Research observations {#s-43ebbeca87}
Tashiro studied L'Hoest's and blue monkeys in Uganda's Kalinzu Forest. In the observations, L'Hoest's monkeys spent up to 66% of feeding time on invertebrates and mainly foraged near the ground. This is a behavioral time budget from one forest, not a year-round diet or range-wide proportion; the paper uses Cercopithecus lhoesti.

## Phylogeography {#s-e2df5a4f3d}
Tosi compared representatives of the three taxa then called the lhoesti group using two Y-chromosomal genes and one X-chromosomal intergenic region. In maximum-likelihood trees from these sequences, lhoesti grouped with preussi to the exclusion of solatus. This depends on a limited marker set and representative samples, not a checklist identity or genome-wide relationship.

## Evidence limits {#s-9b376cac36}
This page guides readers through local Kalinzu feeding and limited-marker phylogenetic studies; it is not a complete species account. The sources do not support a complete range, year-round diet, population trend or current conservation assessment. The Chinese name is editorial.
