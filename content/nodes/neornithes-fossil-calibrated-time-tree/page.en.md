## A molecular-clock estimate near 95 Ma for Neornithes {#s-c2f206769f}
This fossil-calibrated Bayesian study used two molecular samples: clock-like exons from 48 species and a broader RAG dataset from 230 species across all bird orders. It estimated the Neornithes common ancestor near 95 Ma and inferred rapid diversification around the K–Pg boundary.

The dates and reconstructed geographic histories are model outputs conditioned on topology, fossil calibrations, priors, and geographic coding. They are not dates measured from a particular ancestral fossil.
