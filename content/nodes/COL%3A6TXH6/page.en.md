## Mat-forming stems and bristly leaf edges {#s-26d07fd839}
This account describes a much-branched annual herb that lies along the ground or rises slightly, often forming mats. Narrow leaves have marginal bristles and tiny glands on their paler undersides. Small radiate heads bear bright yellow ray florets and yellow disk florets; at maturity, the enclosed fruits fall together with the head covering. The source says the species is native from the southern United States to northern South America and apparently was introduced to Panama, where it was first collected in 1969.
