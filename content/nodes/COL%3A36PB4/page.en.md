## How does the regional field describe the leaves and flowers? {#s-cd358277c4}
The Flora do Brasil 2020 regional field records hairless leaves that do not turn black when dry. Petioles are 0–2.5 mm long, and blade width is listed as either 0.5–4 mm or more than 5 mm. Secondary veins fade before the margin; the record gives either 0–1 or at least two on each side, without hair tufts in the vein axils. The floral prophyll may extend halfway up the tube, to its tip, or beyond it. Flower stalks are 0–6.5 mm long. The epicalyx segments, an extra sepal-like ring below the calyx, are 2–11 mm long, equal to or shorter than the sepals, and erect. The corolla is 3.5–7 cm across and has 12 stamens. This regional database field lists alternatives but not their frequency.

## What habitat category is listed? {#s-374779fe19}
The Brazilian Flora row classifies the habitat as terrestrial; no locality or collection date is supplied.
