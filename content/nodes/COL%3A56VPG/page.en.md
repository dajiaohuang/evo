## Ticorea foetida {#s-5eb65ba97a}
The Brazilian Flora 2020 description records leaf domatia, densely pubescent corollas and minutely puberulous fruit.

## Domatia and a long corolla tube {#s-2c75419001}
Domatia are present on the lower leaf surface. Flower buds are rounded or obtuse, and the corolla is 17–22 mm long and densely pubescent. Filaments are free; the corolla tube is recorded as 0.75–2.3 times the length of its lobes. Fruit has a puberulous surface. The ecology field records “Terrícola” (terrestrial), without a collection locality or complete range.
