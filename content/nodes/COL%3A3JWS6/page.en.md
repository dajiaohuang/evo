## Form and flowers {#s-df64279f63}
Bayer's 2012 regional account describes *H. monticola* as a stemless succulent that forms clumps. Its leaves are small and slender, dark green to reddish, with white teeth; their tips range from upright to incurved. The flowers are white.

This is a regional species description published in 2012; the excerpt gives no dated sampling frame or population frequency.

## A comparison in the source {#s-391f0a92d4}
Scott's 1985 treatment suggests *H. chloracantha* as its closest affinity. It distinguishes *H. monticola* by its longer marginal teeth and incurved leaf tips, and notes differences in flowering time and floral characters without specifying them in the excerpt.
