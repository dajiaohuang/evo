## *Plinia nana* {#s-26c411c759}
Brazilian Flora 2020 lists these morphology descriptors: stems — fissured; leaf-related structures — egg-shaped, tapering tips; flower clusters (inflorescences) — racemes (flower stalks arranged along a central axis). This regional field does not establish a global description or population-wide variation, and reports no sampling coverage.

Brazilian Flora 2020 records the habitat or substrate category as terrestrial (Portuguese: Terrícola). This is the category in that regional data row; it gives no collection locality or survey date and does not establish the species' complete or global range.
