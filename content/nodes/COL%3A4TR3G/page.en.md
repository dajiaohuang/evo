## Ruschia misera {#s-4b680e42e7}
## Velvety papillae cover its fused, sickle-shaped leaves

Klak’s 2012 Greater Cape flora describes a stiffly branched shrublet up to 40 cm tall that may lie down or grow upright. Its slightly sickle-shaped leaves are fused below, with a sheath about 2 mm long, and measure about 17 by 2.5 mm. Fine, velvety papillae cover them.

Many flowers grow together in clusters. The petal-less flowers have white filaments; the account gives their diameter as about 7 mm. The fruits have five chambers. The habitat account records coastal flats.
