## Neocabreria malachophylla {#s-80237b91df}
## Lance-shaped leaves and a hairy receptacle

The record describes lance-shaped leaves with short hairs on the upper surface. Inner involucral bracts have blunt tips, and the receptacle beneath the florets is hirsute. Hairs are also recorded at the tips of the cypselae. Habitat is labelled terrestrial.
