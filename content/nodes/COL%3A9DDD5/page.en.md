## Mimosa thomista {#s-47a3590e12}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Mimosa thomista* as an accepted species of *Mimosa* in Fabaceae. The Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 record notes backward-pressed stem hairs, persistent stipules (small leaf-like structures at the base of a leaf), and globose flower clusters. Flowers have four perianth parts (the flower’s outer parts) and hairless petal lobes; the regional entry also describes the plant as terrestrial.
