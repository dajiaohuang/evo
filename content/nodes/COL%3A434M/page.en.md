The 2017 leucophyllatus-complex revision shows why a familiar genus and species name do not guarantee a stable biological identity. A lost leucophyllatus type was replaced with a neotype and the name restricted to one studied clade; material previously called triangulum was separated using type comparisons, morphology, sequences and calls, with reticulatus restored as a distinct name. Read historical names with their specimen and lineage scope before comparing ranges, colours or acoustic measurements across papers.
