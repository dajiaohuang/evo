## Overview {#s-d8a87fa34a}
COL26.8 accepts *Tamarinus inustus*; earlier ecological papers use *Saguinus inustus*. A 2022 multilocus taxonomic study placed the inustus and mystax groups in its proposed genus Tamarinus. Two site-specific field records under the older combination still do not define a complete range or current population status.

## Ecological evidence {#s-c1b2904736}
Palacios et al. (2004) followed one group of three to six animals near the lower Caquetá River in Colombia. Its observed range was about 35 ha, but full-day follows were completed on only five days. The study recorded fruits of 23 plant species and animal prey in lower forest layers. Valsecchi et al. (2010) recorded 11 localities during two 40-day seasonal expeditions at Amanã, including the first confirmed flooded-forest records. Both datasets are local and time-bounded.

## Evidence limits {#s-51a171ef0a}
