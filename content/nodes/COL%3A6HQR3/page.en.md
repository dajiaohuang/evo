## Historical coastal and brackish-water records {#s-36e712434b}
The 2016 account described frogs assigned to F. moodiei in northeastern Mindanao coastal areas, brackish swamps, mangroves and river-mouth estuaries. It said the coastal CAS specimens had been collected about four decades earlier and recommended reassessing those heavily populated areas. These historical encounters do not establish current abundance or occupancy, a measured salinity tolerance or successful reproduction at every estuary. The listed voucher identifiers are retained as the paper records them rather than treated as newly checked museum identifications.

## An invasive-species concern attributed to the survey authors {#s-3ce9edf696}
The paper attributed separation from F. cancrivora to earlier genetic studies and cited Data Deficient in 2016. Its authors raised concern about introduced Hoplobatrachus rugulosus and Kaloula pulchra and proposed competition or predation as possible mechanisms behind their observed replacement of native frogs. They recommended field-based conservation surveys. These are cited taxonomic evidence and attributed concerns, not a new controlled mechanism test, a measured national decline or a verified current official assessment.
