## Limits across the separate datasets {#s-8e08e3969f}
PanTHERIA, the Amniote archive, EltonTraits, and Ernest’s compilation are separate historical sources with different name crosswalks, definitions, and selection rules. Their records do not cover every COL26.8 descendant or establish current species-concept equivalence. For the 2015 Amniote archive, non-longevity values are medians or interpolated medians; maximum longevity is the longest recorded value, not an average. EltonTraits values translate literature categories, and mammalian ABC entries may borrow from a congener.

## Limits of Ernest’s historical values {#s-db5dbeda3f}
The species links use matching names and families plus a one-to-one MSW93/MSW05 table; equivalence with current species concepts remains unproven. Except for maximum lifespan, values generally average literature sources equally, without sample-size weighting. Inputs may be published averages, individual values, or range midpoints, and underlying studies may overlap. Adult mass is not interpreted in this Ernest route; Original reference numbers remain; wild, captive, and locality conditions are not consistently specified across sources.
