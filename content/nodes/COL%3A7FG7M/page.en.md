## Skull samples and models {#s-f3096ee849}
McCurry et al. (2015) built skull models from CT geometry of Varanus gouldii: AMR 142826. Table 1 records dorsal cranial lengths of AMR 142826, 4.0 cm. These are sample measurements, not species size ranges or dimensions of volume-standardized models.

## Response under standardized loading {#s-bab2b03e29}
AMR 142826 is subadult. Its cranium exhibits high strain during shaking and its mandible during pulling. The authors discuss juvenile–adult proportional differences as a possible influence, so the specimen cannot directly represent adults.

## Model assumptions and specimen age {#s-c9afaef50d}
The finite element models standardize volume and bite loading and use homogeneous isotropic bone properties with shared muscle-attachment assumptions to compare shape. Model strain is not measured live bite force. Single-skull samples, age differences and model restraints limit generalization; they do not establish whole-species rankings, conservation status or complete life histories.
