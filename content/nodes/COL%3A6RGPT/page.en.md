## Microglanis lundbergi {#s-5974ba0e07}
## Diagnostic markings and a collection-label habitat

A 2013 taxonomic treatment diagnoses *M. lundbergi* by a deeply notched forked tail fin and two adjacent pale elliptical spots on the supraoccipital region of the skull roof. It also reports an adipose fin—a small fleshy fin behind the dorsal fin—with a base measuring 13.1–16.6% of standard length (SL, the distance from snout to tail-fin base). The description reports orange-brown coloration in preserved specimens, including the pale spots. These are characters from one treatment, not a survey of variation across populations. Its morphometric table reports nine specimens, while the listed type series totals ten; the article does not explain the difference.

Collection-lot labels place the reported fish in the main channel of the rio Amazonas, in white water over a bottom rich in organic matter and pieces of wood. This habitat note belongs to those collected lots; it does not establish a species-wide habitat preference or a complete range.
