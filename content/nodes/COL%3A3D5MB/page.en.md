## Short, rigid lobes distinguish its leaves {#s-09ea5cc5ca}
The account describes a stout shrub rarely taller than 1 m, with rigid leaves divided into short lobes. It says the species is closer to E. speciosissimus and E. wageneri than to E. serra, and is best recognized by the short, stiff, spreading lobes, which are longest near the middle of each leaf. Sandstone slopes are listed as habitat.
