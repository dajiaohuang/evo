## Recorded parent of Albertosaurinae {#s-27eab56a5a}
Albertosaurinae is listed at rank subfamily in the PBDB classification route, with Tyrannosauridae as its recorded parent. This source relation does not assert direct evolutionary ancestry or equivalence with another authority.
