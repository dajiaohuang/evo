## Oxypetalum pardense {#s-ec3013418a}
COL26.8 (released 2026-08-20) lists *Oxypetalum pardense* as an accepted species of *Oxypetalum*. Brazilian Flora 2020 v393.147 gives terrestrial as its habitat field but no locality or range. Since the record contains no morphology, this category is the only source-supported biological detail here.
