# Node content and presentation

When authoring node prose or implementing its page, card, map bubble, reading
window or local tree, follow [the node production standard](docs/node-presentation-standard.md)
and [the reusable drafting template](docs/templates/node-presentation.md).
Keep these requirements in the linked documents; do not duplicate them here.

Author scientific content in its existing canonical files, following
[content storage](docs/content-storage.md). Preserve source bindings and identity;
do not hand-edit generated projections or invent missing biology or locations.
