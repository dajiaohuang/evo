# Data licenses and provenance

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to software only. It does not relicense third-party scientific data, publications, names, institutional pages or media.

The ITIS Bigyra exact-root sidecar (`data/catalogue-of-life/releases/2026-08-20/resource-packs/protists-chromists/itis-bigyra-*`) is derived from the official ITIS `itisSqlite082626` export dated 2026-08-26 (DOI `10.5066/F7KH0KBK`, CC0 1.0) and the COL26.8 hierarchy (DOI `10.48580/dgywk`, CC BY 4.0). It retains only COL nomenclatural rows and exact-root audit metadata: the selected exact ITIS Bigyra root has no accepted species, so no mapping is inferred. Preserve both attributions, release dates, checksums and the boundary statement in `docs/itis-bigyra-authority.md`.

The ITIS Amoebozoa boundary sidecar (`data/catalogue-of-life/releases/2026-08-20/resource-packs/protists-chromists/itis-amoebozoa-*`) is derived from the official ITIS `itisSqlite082626` export dated 2026-08-26 (DOI `10.5066/F7KH0KBK`, CC0 1.0) and the COL26.8 hierarchy (DOI `10.48580/dgywk`, CC BY 4.0). It retains only COL nomenclatural rows and exact-root audit metadata: ITIS has no exact Amoebozoa root in this export, so no ITIS mapping is inferred. Preserve both attributions, release dates, checksums and the boundary statement in `docs/itis-amoebozoa-authority.md`.

The Cryptophyta authority boundary sidecar (`data/catalogue-of-life/releases/2026-08-20/resource-packs/protists-chromists/itis-cryptophyta-*`) is derived from the official ITIS `itisSqlite082626` export dated 2026-08-26 (DOI `10.5066/F7KH0KBK`, CC0 1.0) and the COL26.8 hierarchy (DOI `10.48580/dgywk`, CC BY 4.0). Preserve both attributions, release dates, checksums and the boundary statement in `docs/itis-cryptophyta-authority.md`.
