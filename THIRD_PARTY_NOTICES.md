- The Viruses nomenclatural resource pack includes the CC BY 4.0 ICTV Master Species List `MSL41.v1` and corrected Virus Metadata Resource `VMR_MSL41.v1.20260729`. It retains all 17,554 current ICTV species, 17,554 exemplar-virus rows and 1,731 additional-isolate rows. Exactly 17,552 current species map to COL26.8 by a case-sensitive unique current name and shared ICTV ID; `Boscovirus hypoboscidae` and `Simiispumavirus macfas` remain explicit ICTV-only records with null COL IDs. Cite ICTV and the pinned DOIs `10.5281/zenodo.19154110` and `10.5281/zenodo.21694279`. GenBank accessions are preserved from VMR and have not been independently revalidated by Evo Atlas.
- Six paleogeographic geometry series and five observational/constraint point datasets derive from the CC BY 4.0 CAO2024 v2.4 dataset (Cao et al. 2024, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.13628813`). Geometry and eligible point coordinates are reconstructed with GPlates/pyGPlates tooling operated or published by EarthByte and AuScope; Evo Atlas does not call GPlates at runtime. CAO2024 layers and bundled PBDB paleocoordinates can use different reconstruction models and are not asserted to be co-registered. Point records wholly outside 0–1,800 Ma remain source-only and are not extrapolated.
- The complete 109-frame palaeotopography/palaeobathymetry series derives from the CC BY 4.0 PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs v2 dataset by Scotese and Wright (2018), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.5460860`. Each 0–540 Ma, 5 Ma nominal frame retains its archive-member bytes/SHA-256, filename age and verbatim internal NetCDF description/parsed age; the nominal 65 Ma member, for example, says 66 Ma internally and neither value is silently corrected. Android and iOS bundle every independent 3601×1801 0.1° lossless integer-metre grid. Web/Pages and browser-offline instead publish every age as an independently checksummed 721×361 0.5° preview created by exact every-fifth-cell sampling, not averaging or interpolation. Runtime rendering dynamically colours one selected grid at a time; no tile pyramid is stored. The surfaces are model outputs, not direct measurements, ground truth, uncertainty bounds, temporal interpolation or evidence of co-registration with CAO2024/PBDB reconstructions.

The sidecars retain exact identifiers, current-name status and limited quality/date fields; they are not presented as an MDD equivalent, a complete expert review of COL concepts or a final classification authority.
